# masonic_dining - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masonic_dining form is used to register Masonic meetings with members attending. It collects information on meeting number, date, time, location, attendees, comments, and dress code.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Masonic_dining form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Meeting Number field with the unique identifier for the meeting.
2. Enter the Date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the Time of the meeting in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
4. Enter the Location where the meeting will take place.
5. List the Brethren attending the meeting in the comments field.
6. Optionally, add any comments related to the meeting.
7. If necessary, specify the Masonic Dress Code for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Number** (Meeting Number, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for this meeting.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
* **Location** (Location, text, required): Enter the venue or location where the meeting will take place.
* **Brethren Attending** (Brethren Attending, text, required): List the names or IDs of the members attending the meeting.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required): Add any additional comments or remarks related to the meeting.
* **Masonic Dress Code** (Masonic Dress Code, text, optional): Specify the dress code for the meeting if necessary.
  Tips:
  - Make sure to fill in the required fields (Meeting Number, Date, Time, Location, and Brethren Attending) accurately and completely.
  - For Date and Time fields, enter in the formats specified above.
  - For Location, provide a clear and descriptive address or venue name.
  - If unsure about any field, contact the meeting organiser or a designated authority for clarification.
