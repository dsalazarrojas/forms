# Arts And Culture Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to submit nominations for the Arts and Culture Award. It gathers information about the nominee, the nominator, and the nomination itself, including the reason for nomination, specific contributions, and impact on the community. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address as the nominator.
2. Provide the nominee's name and email address.
3. Choose the relevant award category for the nominee's work.
4. Explain the reason for nomination and the specific contributions or achievements of the nominee.
5. Describe the impact of the nominee's work on the community or field.
6. If applicable, provide any supporting materials such as a portfolio or press articles.
7. Check if this is the first time you're nominating this person.
8. Agree to the confidentiality of the nomination process.
9. Provide any additional comments or references.
10. Finally, select the correct award ceremony date and confirm your submission by providing the correct nomination deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the nominator of this person.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the nominator, so we can contact you about the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number as the nominator, so we can reach you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Your Relationship to Nominee** (`nominator_relationship`, text, optional): Briefly describe your relationship to the nominee, such as colleague, friend, or supervisor.
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email Address** (`nominee_email`, email, required): Enter the nominee's email address.
* **Nominee Organization or Institution** (`nominee_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the nominee's organization or institution, if applicable.
* **Award Category** (`award_category`, select_one, required): Select the relevant award category for the nominee's work.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`nomination_reason`, text, required): Explain why the nominee deserves this award.
* **Specific Contributions or Achievements** (`contributions_made`, text, required): Describe the specific contributions or achievements of the nominee.
* **Years of Contribution to Arts and Culture** (`years_in_field`, number, optional): Enter the number of years the nominee has contributed to arts and culture.
* **Impact on Community or Field** (`impact_on_community`, text, required): Describe the impact of the nominee's work on the community or field.
* **Previous Awards or Recognition Received** (`awards_recognition`, text, optional): List any previous awards or recognition received by the nominee.
* **Supporting Materials Attached** (`supporting_materials`, text, optional): Attach any supporting materials such as a portfolio or press articles.
* **First Time Nominating This Person** (`nomination_submitted_before`, select_one, optional): Check if this is the first time you're nominating this person.
* **Additional References or Supporters** (`additional_references`, text, optional): Provide any additional references or supporters who can vouch for the nomination.
* **Nomination Deadline** (`nomination_deadline`, date, required): Enter the nomination deadline in the correct format.
* **Expected Award Ceremony Date** (`award_ceremony_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected award ceremony date.
* **I Agree to Confidentiality of Nomination Process** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Check the box to agree to the confidentiality of the nomination process.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the nomination.
