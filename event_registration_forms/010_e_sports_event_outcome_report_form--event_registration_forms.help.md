# E Sports Event Outcome Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The E Sports Event Outcome Report Form is used to gather feedback and information about E Sports events, helping us improve our events and better serve our community. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and identify the relevant sections based on your specific feedback or observations.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field, taking into account the specific event and your assessment.
3. Provide clear and concise comments where necessary.
4. Review your input for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Outcome** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Please select all the outcomes that apply to the event, such as `outcome 1`, `outcome 2`, etc.
* **Event Outcome** (`2`, `text`, required: false): 
  Describe the outcome of the event, providing as much detail as possible in the text field.
* **Player Performance** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose one of the following options: `excellent`, `good`, `average`, or `poor`, to indicate your assessment of player performance.
* **Audience Engagement** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Select all the options that apply to the audience's engagement with the event, such as `excellent`, `good`, `average`, or `poor`.
* **Overall Event Success** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose one of the options: `True` or `False`, to indicate your assessment of the overall success of the event.
* **Comments** (`6`, `note`, required: false): 
  Leave any comments or observations you have about the event. This is a free-text field, so feel free to provide as much detail as you need.
* **Player Comments** (`7`, `note`, required: false): 
  Provide any specific feedback or observations about the players' performances during the event. 
* **Event Comments** (`8`, `note`, required: false): 
  Leave any comments or observations you have about the event as a whole, including suggestions for improvements or next steps. 

## Tips
- Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully and understand the fields before proceeding.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
- The form will guide you through the submission process.
