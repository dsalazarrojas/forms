# Parental Consent And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain parental consent and waiver of liability for an event. It requires information from parents or guardians of minors attending the event, including event details, parental contact information, and the minor's date of birth and consent. The form also includes a waiver agreement and signature sections for both parents and minors to acknowledge their understanding and acceptance of the event's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before filling it out.
2.  Fill out the event registration form with the required information.
3.  Sign and date the waiver agreement sections as indicated.
