# Media Release Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Release Permission Form is used to obtain consent for media-related activities, such as photo, video, or audio recordings. This form will be used to capture essential information from individuals or their parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step guide below:

1. Enter the **Individual Name** in the field labeled "Individual Name" to identify the individual or their parent/guardian.
2. Provide the **Email** address associated with the individual or parent/guardian in the "Email" field.
3. Select the **Date of Media Release** for the event or activity.
4. Choose whether you grant **Permission** for the media release by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
5. Specify the **Consent Type** by choosing "Individual" or "Parent or Guardian".
6. Select the type of **Consent Type** for the media release, if applicable.
7. Choose whether you grant **Photo Release** for the media release by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose whether you grant **Video Release** for the media release by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
9. Select the **Consent Date** for the media release.
10. Enter the name of the **Signed By** person or entity.
11. Capture any **Additional Comments** related to the media release, such as the type of media (Video, Photos, or Other).
12. If applicable, enter the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, **Event Location**, **Event Time**, and **Image Uploads**, **Video Uploads**, and **Audio Uploads** fields to provide context for the media release.
13. Finally, upload any additional **File Uploads** and sign the form by entering your name in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Individual Name**: Enter the name of the individual or their parent/guardian.
* **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the individual or parent/guardian.
* **Date of Media Release**: Select the date of the media release event or activity.
* **Permission**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to grant or deny permission for the media release.
* **Consent Type**: Select "Individual" or "Parent or Guardian" to specify the type of consent.
* **Consent Type 2**: Select "Individual" or "Parent or Guardian" to specify the type of consent for the additional media release.
* **Photo Release**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to grant or deny photo release permission.
* **Video Release**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to grant or deny video release permission.
* **Consent Date**: Select the date of consent for the media release.
* **Signed By**: Enter the name of the person or entity that signed the form.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or details related to the media release.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event or activity.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event or activity.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event or activity.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event or activity.
* **Image Uploads**: Upload any images related to the event or activity.
* **Video Uploads**: Upload any videos related to the event or activity.
* **Audio Uploads**: Upload any audio files related to the event or activity.
* **File Uploads**: Upload any files related to the event or activity.
* **Upload File**: Enter the uploaded file name.
* **Signature**: Enter your name in the signature field to sign the form.
* **Date Signed**: Select the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**: Select the time you signed the form.
* **Additional Comments 2**: Provide any additional comments or details related to the form.
