# esports_arena_time_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event registration at the esports arena. Please use this form to book a time slot for your event participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other general information you'd like to provide in the `user_input` field.
2. Enter the date of your arrival in the `date_of_arrival` field. Please use the format `MM/DD/YY`.
3. Specify your arrival time in the `arrival_time` field. 
4. If you need a gaming station, select "Yes" or "No" in the `gaming_station` field.
5. If you've selected "Yes" for gaming station, select the type of gaming station you'd like in the `gaming_station_type` field.
6. Enter the duration of your visit in the `duration` field. Please note that this will be the length of your visit in minutes.
7. If you have any additional information or preferences, please provide them in the `additional_info` field.
8. Enter your email address in the `email` field to stay updated on event details and schedule changes.
9. Add your phone number in the `phone` field to enable us to contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
10. Confirm your event booking preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the `confirm` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (text, required/optional): Enter any general information you'd like to provide.
* **date_of_arrival** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of your arrival in `MM/DD/YY` format.
* **arrival_time** (time, required/optional): Enter your arrival time.
* **gaming_station** (select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you need a gaming station, "No" otherwise.
* **gaming_station_type** (select_one, required/optional): If you selected "Yes" for gaming station, select the type of gaming station you'd like.
* **duration** (number, required/optional): Enter the duration of your visit in minutes.
* **additional_info** (note, required/optional): If you have any additional information or preferences, please provide them here.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to stay updated on event details and schedule changes.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Add your phone number to enable us to contact you in case of any last-minute changes.
* **confirm** (select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your event booking preferences by selecting "Yes" or "No".
