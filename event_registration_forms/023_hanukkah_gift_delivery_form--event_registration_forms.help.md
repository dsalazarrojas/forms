# Hanukkah Gift Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the Hanukkah Gift Delivery process. It's used to gather details about the recipient, gift preferences, and delivery information. This form is typically used during the Hanukkah celebration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the fields as instructed below.
2.  Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hanukkah Gift Delivery Form** (`hanukkah-gift-delivery-form`, text, not required): Enter a title or description for the gift delivery.
* **Recipient** (`recipient`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the gift.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, not required): Enter any special gift preferences or requests you may have.
* **Gift Message** (`gift-message`, text, not required): Enter any message or notes you'd like to include with the gift.
* **Recipient's Name** (`recipient-name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the recipient.
* **Recipient's Email** (`recipient-email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery-date`, date, not required): Select a date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery-time`, time, not required): Select a time for delivery.
* **Gift Options** (`gift-options`, select_one, not required): Choose an option for the gift (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
* **Gift Amount** (`gift-amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of the gift.
* **Gift Amount Currency** (`gift-amount-currency`, text, not required): Enter the currency of the gift (e.g., "USD", "EUR", etc.).
* **Additional Message** (`additional-message`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about the gift.

## Tips

- Ensure that the recipient's email is entered correctly for delivery confirmation.
- Enter the correct currency for the gift amount.
- Use the gift options to select the desired gift type.
