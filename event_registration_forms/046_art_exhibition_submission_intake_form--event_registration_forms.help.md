# Art Exhibition Submission Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Submission Intake Form is designed to collect necessary information from artists who wish to participate in exhibitions organized by the event registration platform. This form helps to streamline the exhibition process and ensure that all required information is collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Artist Information section with your personal details.
2.  Enter your exhibition call or opportunity and number of pieces submitted.
3.  Provide details about each artwork, including title, medium, dimensions, year created, and selling price.
4.  Write a 100-200 word artist statement and background information.
5.  Confirm that all information is accurate and agree to the exhibition terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Artist Information 
* **Artist Full Name** (`artist_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Artist Website or Portfolio** (`artist_website`): Enter your website or portfolio for further reference.
* **Social Media Handles** (`social_media`): Enter any relevant social media handles for further reference.

### Exhibition Information 
* **Exhibition Call or Opportunity** (`exhibition_call`): Select the exhibition you are applying for.
* **Number of Pieces Submitted** (`number_pieces`): Enter the number of pieces you are submitting.

### Artwork Details 
* **Artwork 1 - Title** (`artwork_1_title`): Enter the title of your first artwork.
* **Medium or Technique** (`artwork_1_medium`): Enter the medium or technique used for your first artwork.
* **Dimensions - Width x Height x Depth** (`artwork_1_dimensions`): Enter the dimensions of your first artwork.
* **Year Created** (`artwork_1_year`): Enter the year your first artwork was created.
* **Selling Price** (`artwork_1_price`): Enter the selling price of your first artwork.
* **Available for Sale or Exhibition Only** (`artwork_1_available`): Select whether your artwork is available for sale or only for exhibition.
* **Artwork 2 - Title** (`artwork_2_title`): Enter the title of your second artwork (if applicable).
* **Medium or Technique** (`artwork_2_medium`): Enter the medium or technique used for your second artwork (if applicable).
* **Dimensions - Width x Height x Depth** (`artwork_2_dimensions`): Enter the dimensions of your second artwork (if applicable).
* **Year Created** (`artwork_2_year`): Enter the year your second artwork was created (if applicable).
* **Selling Price** (`artwork_2_price`): Enter the selling price of your second artwork (if applicable).
* **Available for Sale or Exhibition Only** (`artwork_2_available`): Select whether your second artwork is available for sale or only for exhibition (if applicable).

### Artist Statement and Background 
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`): Write a 100-200 word statement describing your artwork and artistic experience.
* **Years of Artistic Experience** (`artistic_background`): Enter the number of years you have been creating art.
* **Artistic Education or Training** (`education`): Enter any relevant education or training received.

### Submission Agreement 
* **I Confirm All Information is Accurate** (`submission_agreement`): Select that all information is accurate.
* **Permission to Publish Artwork in Promotional Materials** (`publication_rights`): Select whether you permit the exhibition to use your artwork in promotional materials.
* **Agree to Exhibition Terms and Conditions** (`final_confirmation`): Select that you agree to the exhibition terms and conditions.
