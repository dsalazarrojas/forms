# Webinar Attendee Email Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Webinar Attendee Email Validation Form. The form is designed to validate the email address of attendees who are attending a webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the attendee's email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
2. Select the desired validation rule(s) from the "Email Validation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Webinar Attendee Email Validation Form**: This is the title of the form, and is provided for informational purposes only.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the email address of the attendee you wish to validate. This should be a valid email address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Email Validation**: Choose the validation rule(s) that you wish to apply to the attendee's email address. The options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Attendee Info**: This field is not required and is provided for any additional information you may want to add about the attendee. Please note that this field is not used for validation purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct email address of the attendee to ensure proper validation.
* Select the correct validation rule(s) based on your specific needs.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer back to this guide for further clarification.
