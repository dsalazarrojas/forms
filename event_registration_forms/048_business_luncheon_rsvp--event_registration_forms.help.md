# Business Luncheon RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a business luncheon event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Provide your full name in the "Attendee name" field.
3. Enter your company or affiliation in the "Organization" field (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address in the "Contact email" field.
5. Indicate the number of guests attending, including yourself, in the "Number of guests" field.
6. If you have any dietary requirements or allergies, type them in the "Dietary requirements" field.
7. Share any seating preferences or requests in the "Seating requests" field.
8. Choose whether you are attending the event or not by selecting "Attending" or "Not attending" from the "Attend choice" dropdown menu.
9. If you have any special phone number, please enter it in the "Contact phone" field (optional).
10. If you have any special accommodations or accessibility needs, type them in the "Special accommodations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed on the event materials.
* **Organization**: Enter your company or affiliation if you are registering from a company or organization.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for event confirmation and updates.
* **Number of guests**: Include the number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Dietary requirements**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, enter them here.
* **Seating requests**: Share any special seating requests, such as needing wheelchair accessibility.
* **Attend choice**: Choose whether you are attending or not attending the event.
* **Contact phone**: Enter a phone number for optional contact, but not required for confirmation.
* **Special accommodations**: If you have any special needs, such as accessibility or other accommodations, enter them here.
