# RSVP For Performing Arts Webinar - Help Guide

## Purpose
The RSVP for Performing Arts Webinar form is designed to collect RSVPs for attendees of an online Performing Arts Webinar. This form is intended to be completed by users who have been invited to the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Assigned Tool" and your "Name" (though your name is not a required field, filling it in will help the host manage attendees more efficiently).
2. Select the type of Performing Arts that interests you (e.g., Performing Arts, Dance, Music, Visual Arts, Theater, or Other).
3. Optionally, add any comments about your registration.
4. Choose the date and time you wish to attend the webinar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (name, text, Optional): Fill in your name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email** (email, email, Optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted or have access to additional resources.
* **Date** (rsvp_date, date, Optional): Select the date you wish to attend the webinar.
* **Time** (rsvp_time, time, Optional): Select the time you wish to attend the webinar.
* **Performing Arts Webinar Type** (performing_arts_webinar_type, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of Performing Arts that interests you (e.g., Performing Arts, Dance, Music, Visual Arts, Theater, or Other).
* **Comments** (rsvp_comments, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, Required): This is a mandatory field for internal management purposes; please select the assigned tool from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select the correct Performing Arts Webinar Type, as this will help the host manage attendees accordingly.
* You can add any comments or questions you have about the webinar in the Comments field.
* If you have any issues with the Assigned Tool, contact the webinar host for assistance.
