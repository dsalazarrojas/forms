# skill_building_session_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a participant for a skill-building session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Info field with your own information.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Participant Name.
5. Enter the Session Topic.
6. Enter any additional Workshop Details.
7. If you have any additional Participant 1 Info, enter it in the corresponding field.
8. Enter the Email of the participant you are registering (if different from yours).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: Your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Email**: Your email address.
	+ Type: Email input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant you are registering.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Session Topic**: The topic of the session you are registering for.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Workshop Details**: Any additional details about the workshop.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Participant 1 Info**: Additional information about the first participant.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Email 1**: The email address of the first participant.
	+ Type: Email input field.
	+ Required: No.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out for a single participant at a time. If you are registering multiple participants, you will need to fill out this form multiple times.
