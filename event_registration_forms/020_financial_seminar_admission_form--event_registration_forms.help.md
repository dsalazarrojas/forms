# Financial Seminar Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for attendees to register for a financial seminar. It's a three-page form that gathers information for session selection, attendee details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, select the sessions you'd like to attend by checking the boxes next to each session. You can select as many or as few sessions as you like.
2. On the second page, fill in your attendee details. This is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to the seminar.
3. On the third page, provide your payment information if you've selected to attend any sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Selection
* **Session Selection** (`session_selection`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sessions you'd like to attend. You can choose one or multiple sessions by checking the boxes next to each session.

### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, optional): Fill in any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to the seminar. You can provide as much or as little information as you like, and it can be in any format that makes sense to you.

### Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): If you've selected to attend any sessions, you can provide your payment information here. You can enter your payment details in any format that you like, and it's not required unless you've selected to attend a session.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the sessions you'd like to attend on page 1.
* You can leave the "Attendee Details" and "Payment Info" fields blank if you don't have any additional information to provide.
* If you're having trouble with any part of the form, don't hesitate to reach out to the seminar organizers for help.
