# Sponsorship Agreement Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsorship Agreement Verification Form is a tool used to gather information about sponsorship agreements for events. It helps event organizers to accurately record and verify the details of these agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To fill out this form, select the relevant options for each field based on the actual sponsorship agreement.
2. Enter the sponsor's name and title as they appear on the agreement.
3. If the sponsor has a physical address, enter it in the Sponsor Address field.
4. Enter the sponsor's email address as it appears on the agreement.
5. For the Contract Start and End Dates, enter the actual dates when the contract began and ended.
6. Select the correct Contract Status (Active or Inactive) and Contract Value as per the agreement.
7. Enter the Contract Term as described in the agreement.
8. Make sure to enter all the required information accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration**: Choose whether the sponsor is registered for the event (Yes) or not (No).
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor as it appears on the agreement.
* **Sponsor Title**: Enter the title of the sponsor as it appears on the agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date when the sponsorship agreement was signed.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Status**: Select Active if the contract is currently active, and Inactive if it is not.
* **Contract Value**: Enter the monetary value of the contract.
* **Contract Term**: Enter the term of the contract as described in the agreement.
* **Sponsor Address**: Enter the physical address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the email address of the sponsor.
* **Contract Status**: Select Active or Inactive to indicate the current status of the contract.
