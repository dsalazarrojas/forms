# Paymentwall Hotel Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide you through the hotel booking process, ensuring you provide all necessary information for a successful reservation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your desired booking dates in the "Booking Dates" field.
2.  Choose your reservation type (Hotel or Package) in the "Reservation Type" field.
3.  Select the type of room you'd like to book (Yes or No) in the "Room Type" field.
4.  Enter your guest's name, phone number, and email address in the "Guest Name", "Guest Phone", and "Guest Email" fields, respectively.
5.  If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Request" field.
6.  Choose the payment method to be used (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) in the "Payment Method" field.
7.  Enter your reservation ID, room rate, and total cost for the booking in the "Reservation ID", "Room Rate", and "Total Cost" fields, respectively.
8.  Select the currency for the room rate (USD, Euro, Pound) in the "Room Rate Currency" field.
9.  Select the payment status (Pending, Approved, Declined) and booking status (Available, Occupied, Cancelled) in the "Payment Status" and "Booking Status" fields, respectively.
10.  Add any additional notes about your reservation in the "Notes" field.
11.  Check if the booking is assigned to someone (Yes or No) in the "Assigned to" field.
12.  Select the assigned date and completed date for the booking in the "Assigned Date" and "Completed Date" fields, respectively.
13.  Enter your Paymentwall ID in the "Paymentwall ID" field.
14.  Enter your name, email address, phone number, and address in the "Customer Name", "Customer Email", "Customer Phone", and "Customer Address" fields, respectively.
15.  Enter your company name in the "Customer Company" field.
