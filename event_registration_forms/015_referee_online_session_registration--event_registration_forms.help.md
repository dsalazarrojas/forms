# Referee Online Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Online Session Registration form is designed to collect information from users to manage online referee sessions. This form is used to create or update sessions for various events. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Select a session name that accurately reflects the event you are registering.
3. Enter the attendee's name and other relevant details.
4. Add the referee's name and other relevant details.
5. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
6. Input your phone number for further communication and backup.
7. Specify the region where the session is taking place.
8. Choose the date of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Name**: Enter the name of the session you are registering. This could be the name of the event, the date, or the location. 
* **Attendee Name**: This field is for the name of the person attending the session. Please enter their full name.
* **Referee Name**: Enter the name of the referee for this session. This could be a name, title, or other identifying information.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure that you can be contacted in case of last-minute updates or changes.
* **Region**: Choose the location where the session is taking place. This could be a city, state, or region.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you are registering for. This should be the date of the session you are registering for.
