# mental_health_advocacy_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather participant information for a mental health advocacy workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Select relevant options from the dropdown menus as required.
3. Fill out any additional information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **participant_info** (Participant Information)
* **participant_info**: A text field for participant information.

### 2. **name** (Name)
* **name**: A text field for your name.

### 3. **email** (Email)
* **email**: An email address field for verification purposes.

### 4. **phone** (Phone)
* **phone**: A text field for your phone number.

### 5. **date** (Date)
* **date**: A date field for scheduling purposes.

### 6. **time** (Time)
* **time**: A time field for scheduling purposes.

### 7. **select_one** (Select One)
* **select_one**: A dropdown menu for selecting one option from the list.

### 8. **select_multiple** (Select Multiple)
* **select_multiple**: A dropdown menu for selecting multiple options from the list.

### 9. **note** (Note)
* **note**: A text field for any additional comments or notes.

### 10. **submit** (Submit)
* **submit**: A submit button to complete the registration process.

### 11. **email_verification** (Email Verification)
* **email_verification**: An email address field for verification purposes.

### 12. **phone_verification** (Phone Verification)
* **phone_verification**: A text field for verification purposes.

### 13. **date_verification** (Date Verification)
* **date_verification**: A date field for verification purposes.

### 14. **time_verification** (Time Verification)
* **time_verification**: A time field for verification purposes.

### 15. **note_verification** (Note Verification)
* **note_verification**: A text field for verification purposes.
Please note that fields 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, and 15 are not currently required fields and can be filled out as needed for the workshop organizers' reference.
