# Young Engineers Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Young Engineers Event Registration form is designed for participants to register for an event related to engineering, such as students, teachers, or parents. The form collects details about the session, participant, and consents. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Session Details** field with any relevant information about the session you're registering for.
2. In the **Participant Details** field, provide any necessary information about yourself.
3. For **Session Choice**, select one of the available options (Engineering Event, STEM Workshop, Hackathon, or Meet and Greet).
4. In **Participant Type**, choose whether you are a Student, Teacher, or Parent.
5. For **Consents**, select both options to agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
6. If you wish to provide more information, you can do so in the **Participant Info** field.
7. Finally, provide any relevant **Session Info** and **School Info** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details**: A field where you can provide details about the session you're registering for.
- **Participant Details**: A field for you to provide any necessary information about yourself.
- **Session Choice** (type: select_one): Select one option for the session you'd like to attend (Engineering Event, STEM Workshop, Hackathon, or Meet and Greet).
  • This choice might be based on your interests or preferences.
- **Participant Type** (type: select_multiple): Choose whether you are a Student, Teacher, or Parent.
  • This is essential for us to understand your role and provide the appropriate resources.
- **Consents** (type: select_multiple): Select both options to agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
  • This is a requirement for your registration to be complete.
- **Participant Info** (type: text): A field for you to provide any additional information about yourself if needed.
  • This is optional and can be used to provide context or details about your background.
- **Session Info** (type: text): A field for you to provide any extra information about the session.
  • This is optional and can be used to offer further context or insights about the session.
- **School Info** (type: text): A field to provide information about your school.
  • This field is optional and can be used to offer further context about your educational institution.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Choose your session choice based on your actual preferences and interests.
- Select both options for consents to ensure your registration is complete.
- Use the optional fields to provide additional information if needed.
