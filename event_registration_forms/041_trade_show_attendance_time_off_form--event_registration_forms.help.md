# Trade Show Attendance Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Attendance Time Off Form is designed to record an employee's attendance at a trade show. The form gathers essential information to help the employee and their manager keep track of their time off.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information.
3. Submit the form once you've completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Show Time Off Form** (`trade_show_attendance_time_off_form`, text, not required): This field is for a brief description of the purpose of the form. You can fill it in to provide more context, but it's not mandatory.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your current job title.
* **Date of Show** (`date_of_show`, date, not required): Enter the date of the trade show you attended.
* **Time Start** (`time_start`, time, not required): Enter the time you started attending the trade show.
* **Time End** (`time_end`, time, not required): Enter the time you finished attending the trade show.
* **Show Name** (`show_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the trade show you attended.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* Make sure to include all relevant details about your attendance.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.
