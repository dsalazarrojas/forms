# Control Number Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Control Number Registration Form is used to register for events and provide essential details about the registrant and the event. This form helps event organizers collect relevant data for event management and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your registrant information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your organization's name (if applicable).
3. Enter the event name, date, and location.
4. Select your registration type (e.g., Standard, VIP, Student, Group, Virtual, or Hybrid).
5. Enter your payment method and status.
6. Select your preferred sessions.
7. Provide any special dietary requirements.
8. Specify any accessibility needs.
9. Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.
10. Enter the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registrant Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.

* This field is required to identify you as a registrant.

### 2. **Email Address** (`registrant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.

* This field is required for communication purposes.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`registrant_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.

* This field is required for contact purposes.

### 4. **Organization Name** (`organization`, text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter the name of your company or institution.

* This field is optional and allows you to specify your organization's name.

### 5. **Event Information** (`section_event_info`, note, optional): This section is for additional event details.

* This field is optional and does not require a specific entry.

### 6. **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.

* This field is required to identify the event you are registering for.

### 7. **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.

* This field is required to track the event schedule.

### 8. **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.

* This field is required to provide logistical information.

### 9. **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of registration (e.g., Standard, VIP, Student, Group, Virtual, or Hybrid).

* This field is required to categorize your registration type.

### 10. **Participant Details** (`section_participant_details`, note, optional): This section is for additional participant details.

* This field is optional and does not require a specific entry.

### 11. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.

* This field is optional and allows you to specify your job title.

### 12. **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, optional): Select your industry sector (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, Government, Manufacturing, Retail, or Other).

* This field is optional and helps categorize your industry.

### 13. **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, optional): Select your years of experience in your field (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years).

* This field is optional and helps assess your level of experience.

### 14. **Preferences** (`section_preferences`, note, optional): This section is for session preferences.

* This field is optional and does not require a specific entry.

### 15. **Preferred Sessions** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sessions you prefer to attend.

* This field is optional and allows you to specify your session preferences.

### 16. **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, optional): Select any special dietary needs (e.g., None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, Halal, Other).

* This field is optional and helps cater to your dietary needs.

### 17. **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs or accommodations required.

* This field is optional and allows you to specify any accessibility needs.

### 18. **Payment Information** (`section_payment`, note, optional): This section is for payment details.

* This field is optional and does not require a specific entry.

### 19. **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Invoice, Bank Transfer, Check, Waived).

* This field is required to track payment information.

### 20. **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current payment status (e.g., Paid, Pending, To Be Invoiced, Waived).

* This field is required to track payment status.

### 21. **Additional Information** (`section_additional`, note, optional): This section is for any additional information.

* This field is optional and does not require a specific entry.

### 22. **How did you hear about this event?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about this event (e.g., Email, Website, Social Media, Colleague, Advertisement, Other).

* This field is optional and helps track marketing efforts.

### 23. **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements.

* This field is optional and allows you to specify any special requests.

### 24. **I accept the terms and conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.

* This field is required to ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.

### 25. **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of registration.

* This field is required to track the registration date.

### 26. **Control Number** (`control_number`, text, optional): This field is auto-generated and not required for entry.

* This field is not required and is auto-generated for internal purposes.
