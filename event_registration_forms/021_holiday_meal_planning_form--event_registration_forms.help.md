# holiday_meal_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday meal planning form is designed to gather information from users on their meal preferences, dietary restrictions, number of guests, and date of event. This information will be used to help plan and prepare the perfect holiday meal for the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "holiday_meal_planning_form" link to access the form.
2. Select one of the meal preferences options: "Yes" or "No".
3. Check if you have any dietary restrictions, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each restriction.
4. Enter the number of guests attending the event.
5. Choose the date of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of meal are you interested in** (`meal_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in a specific type of meal, and "No" if you have no preference.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, and "No" if you do not.
* **Number of guests attending** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Date of event** (`date_of_event`, date, required): Choose the date of the event.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when selecting your meal preferences and dietary restrictions.
* If you are unsure of the number of guests attending, you can estimate or leave it blank.
* Make sure to choose the correct date of the event to ensure accurate planning.
