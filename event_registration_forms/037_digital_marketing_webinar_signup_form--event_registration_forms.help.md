# Digital Marketing Webinar Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Marketing Webinar Signup Form is designed to collect registration information for a digital marketing webinar. This form helps in tracking attendee registrations and confirmation status, making it easier for the webinar organizers to manage the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Select the required options from the available options.
3. Submit the form after reviewing your details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration** (`id: 1`): Enter the name of the event registration. (Text Field)
* **Attendee Interests** (`id: 2`): Select one or more interests for the attendee from the available options. (Select Multiple Field)
* **Confirmation** (`id: 3`): Confirm the registration for the event. (Time Field)
* **Attendee Information** (`id: 4`): Enter the attendee's information. (Text Field)
* **Attendee Email** (`id: 5`): Enter the attendee's email address. (Email Field)
* **Event Date** (`id: 6`): Select the date of the event. (Date Field)
* **Event Time** (`id: 7`): Select the time of the event. (Time Field)
* **Webinar Title** (`id: 8`): Enter the title of the webinar. (Text Field)
* **Event Description** (`id: 9`): Enter a brief description of the event. (Text Field)
* **Event Location** (`id: 10`): Enter the location of the event. (Text Field)
* **Attendee Name** (`id: 11`): Enter the attendee's name. (Text Field)
* **Attendee Phone** (`id: 12`): Enter the attendee's phone number. (Text Field)
* **Attendee Notes** (`id: 13`): Add any additional notes for the attendee. (Note Field)
* **Event ID** (`id: 14`): Enter the event ID. (Text Field)
* **Event Status** (`id: 15`): Select the status of the event from the available options. (Select One Field)
* **Form Submitted** (`id: 16`): If the form has been submitted, select True. Otherwise, select False. (Select Multiple Field)
* **Confirmation Status** (`id: 17`): Select the confirmation status of the event. (Select One Field)
* **Confirmation Method** (`id: 18`): Select the method of confirmation. (Select One Field)
* **Confirmation Time** (`id: 19`): Select the time of confirmation. (Time Field)
* **Confirmation Date** (`id: 20`): Select the date of confirmation. (Date Field)
* **Method** (`id: 21`): Add any additional notes for confirmation. (Note Field)
* **Notes** (`id: 22`): Add any additional notes for the event. (Note Field)
* **Email** (`id: 23`): Enter the email address for confirmation. (Email Field)
* **Phone** (`id: 24`): Enter the phone number for confirmation. (Text Field)
* **Status** (`id: 25`): Select the status of the event confirmation. (Select One Field)

Note: The fields marked as required will be highlighted in the actual form for better clarity.
