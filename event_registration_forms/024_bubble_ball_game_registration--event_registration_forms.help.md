# Bubble Ball Game Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from players registering for the Bubble Ball Game. It covers various fields that will help us prepare for the event, including contact details, health and medical information, game preferences, and more.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your Player Full Name, Date of Birth, Age, Player Phone Number, Player Email Address, and Emergency Contact Name and Phone.
2. Choose your Bubble Ball Experience Level and preferred Game Date and Time.
3. If you are under 18, enter the Parent or Guardian Name and their contact information (Phone and Email).
4. Provide information on any Medical Conditions or Injuries, known Allergies, and Current Medications (if applicable).
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the health and waiver form.
6. Enter the Registration Fee Paid (amount).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name** (player_name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (player_date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1999-02-15).
* **Age** (player_age, number, optional): Enter your age in years.
* **Player Phone Number** (player_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Player Email Address** (player_email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (parent_name, text, optional): If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (parent_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (parent_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Bubble Ball Experience Level** (experience_level, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience in the Bubble Ball Game:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Do you have a team preference** (team_preference, text, optional): If you have a team preference, enter it here.
* **Preferred Game Date and Time** (game_date_preference, select_one, required): Choose your preferred game date and time:
	+ Saturday Morning
	+ Saturday Afternoon
	+ Saturday Evening
	+ Sunday Morning
	+ Sunday Afternoon
* **Any Medical Conditions or Injuries** (medical_conditions, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please provide details for our safety considerations.
* **Known Allergies** (allergies, text, optional): If you have any known allergies, please list them.
* **Current Medications** (medications, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, list them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required): Enter the name of someone other than your parent or guardian that we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I agree to the health and waiver form** (health_waiver, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the health and waiver form:
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ False
* **Registration Fee Paid** (registration_fee, number, required): Enter the amount you have paid as the registration fee.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in ensuring the smooth execution of the Bubble Ball Game.
