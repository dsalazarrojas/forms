# HR Modernization Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the HR Modernization Seminar. It is an optional form and does not require authentication. Please ensure that you are not registered for a different seminar before filling this form.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, click on the "Register" button to begin.
- Fill in your details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, etc.
- Ensure that all the required fields are filled in as indicated by the required label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<name>, text, required/optional):** <plain explanation>
  Enter your first name here.

* **Last Name (<name>, text, required/optional):** Enter your last name here.
* **Email (<name>, email, required/optional):** Enter your email address here.
* **Role (<name>, text, required/optional):** Enter your role here.
* **Department (<name>, text, required/optional):** Enter your department here.
* **Company (<name>, text, required/optional):** Enter your company name here.
* **Phone (<name>, text, required/optional):** Enter your phone number here.
* **Date (<name>, date, required/optional):** Enter the date of the seminar you are registering for. (Format: YYYY-MM-DD)
* **Time (<name>, time, required/optional):** Enter the time of the seminar you are registering for. (Format: HH:MM)
* **Note (<name>, note, required/optional):** Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for the organizers here.
