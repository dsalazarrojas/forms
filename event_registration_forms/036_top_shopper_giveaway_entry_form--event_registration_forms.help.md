# Top Shopper Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers or participants to enter a giveaway contest hosted in a shopping environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Add your participant details in the "participant details" field.
4. Select your shop name from the "shop name" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Select a date and time for the giveaway participation in the "date and time" field.
8. Select a time for the giveaway participation in the "time" field.
9. Add any additional notes about your participation in the "note" field.
10. Enter your participant ID in the "participant ID" field.
11. Provide any additional details about yourself as a shopper in the "shopper details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Add any details about your participation in the giveaway.
* **Shop name** (`shop_name`, select_one, required: false): Select your shop name from the options. Only one participant per shop name can be registered.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date and time** (`date_time`, date, required: false): Select a date and time for the giveaway participation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select a time for the giveaway participation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your participation.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required: false): Enter your participant ID.
* **Shopper details** (`shopper_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about yourself as a shopper.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your entry is complete and valid.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Only one participant per shop name can be registered to avoid duplication.
