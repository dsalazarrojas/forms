# Workforce Development Week Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Workforce Development Week event. It collects information about each attendee, including their personal details, event participation, and registration method. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the "Event Name" field.
* Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
* Enter your email in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
* Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Enter your department name in the "Department" field.
* Select your role in the "Role" field.
* Select the event start date in the "Event Start Date" field.
* Select the event end date in the "Event End Date" field.
* Select your attendee type in the "Attendee Type" field.
* Select your registration method in the "Registration Method" field.
* Enter any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
* Enter your company address in the "Company Address" field.
* Enter your department address in the "Department Address" field.
* Enter your event organizer in the "Event Organizer" field.
* Enter your company email in the "Company Email" field.
* Enter your department email in the "Department Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Enter your event location in the "Event Location" field.
* Enter any notes in the "Notes" field.
* Select your registration date in the "Registration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as the attendee.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Role**: Select your role in the event (e.g., Attendee, Speaker, Both).
* **Event Start Date**: Select the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date**: Select the end date of the event.
* **Attendee Type**: Select your type as an attendee (e.g., Attendee, Speaker, Both).
* **Registration Method**: Select the method of your registration (e.g., In Person, Online, Hybrid).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your registration.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company address.
* **Department Address**: Enter your department address.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Company Email**: Enter your company email.
* **Department Email**: Enter your department email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Notes**: Enter any notes about your registration.
* **Registration Date**: Select the date of your registration.

Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. 

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact the event organizers.
