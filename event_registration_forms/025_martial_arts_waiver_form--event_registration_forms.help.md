# Martial Arts Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Martial Arts Waiver Form is a document that participants must complete before engaging in martial arts training or events. It helps ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the risks associated with martial arts training and provide necessary emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. You will need to provide basic demographic information (name, date of birth, age), medical history (injury history, medical condition, medications), and emergency contact information (emergency contact name, phone, relationship). You will also be asked to confirm your martial arts experience and sign the form to acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with martial arts training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This field asks for your basic demographic information, including your name, date of birth, and age.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in case of an accident or injury.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): This field is for providing any medical history, including injury history, medical conditions, and medications you are currently taking.
* **Parent or Guardian Information** (`parent_or_guardian`): If you are under 18, please provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Medical Certification** (`emergency_medical_certification`): This field is not currently used and is reserved for future expansion.
* **Participant Statement** (`participant_statement`): Please use this field to confirm that you understand the risks associated with martial arts training and are aware of the emergency procedures in place.
* **Parent or Guardian Permission** (`parent_or_guardian_permission`): If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian sign this section to confirm their permission for you to participate.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`): This field is not currently used and is reserved for future expansion.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the martial arts waiver.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`): Please enter your age.
* **Martial Arts Experience** (`martial_arts_experience`): Please describe your level of martial arts experience, including any relevant training or competition history.
* **Injury History** (`injury_history`): Please list any previous injuries or medical conditions that may affect your participation in martial arts training.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in martial arts training, please list them here.
* **Medications** (`medications`): Please list any medications you are currently taking that may affect your participation in martial arts training.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): Please list any allergies you have that may impact your participation in martial arts training.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): If you listed an emergency contact person, please describe their relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Please enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.

## Tips
Please fill in all fields accurately and honestly. If you are unsure about any of the information requested, please ask your instructor or the event organizer for clarification.
