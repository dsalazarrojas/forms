# Halloween Haunt Party Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from partygoers for the Halloween Haunt party event. It gathers basic details about each attendee, including their preferences and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your Partygoer Information in the first field. Please provide your name, as this information is required to process your submission.
2.  If you'd like to specify any special event preferences, you can do so in the second field (Event Details).
3.  Provide any additional party information you'd like to share in the third field (Party Info).
4.  If you're making a payment, enter your payment details in the fourth field (Payment).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Partygoer Information (Partygoer Information)**: Enter your name, as this field is necessary for form processing.
*   **Event Details (Event Details)**: If you have any specific event preferences or requests, type them here. This information will help us better organize the event.
*   **Party Info (Party Info)**: Share any additional information about your party preferences or requests with us.
*   **Payment (Payment)**: Enter your payment details if you're making a payment for the event.

## Tips
*   Be sure to fill out all required fields, marked with \*.
*   Please use your real name in the Partygoer Information field for accurate processing.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
