# Research Symposium Theme Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather input from registered participants on potential themes for the research symposium. Your suggestions will be reviewed to inform the symposium's theme.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available themes listed and select the ones you are most interested in or would like to suggest.
2. Choose all relevant themes that apply.
3. Once you've selected all relevant themes, click the "Submit" button to submit your suggestions.
