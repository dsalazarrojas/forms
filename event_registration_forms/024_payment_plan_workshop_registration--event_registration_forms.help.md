# Payment Plan Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payment Plan Workshop Registration form is a tool for customers to provide information for a payment plan workshop. This form is used to capture the necessary details for workshop administration and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **User Details** section with your name and contact information.
2. Choose the **Payment Plan** that you are interested in by selecting one of the available options.
3. Provide the **Event Details** in the space provided.
4. Enter the **Fees** and **Total Fees** for the event, if applicable.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter your name and contact information to be reached for workshop administration and follow-up.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_plan`, select_one, required/optional): 
    Select the payment plan you wish to enroll in for the workshop.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): 
    Provide any additional information relevant to the event, such as date or location.
* **Fees** (`fees`, number, required/optional): 
    Enter the fees associated with the workshop, if applicable.
* **Total Fees** (`total_fees`, number, required/optional): 
    Enter the total fees for the workshop, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- Review the available payment plans and choose the one that best suits your needs.
- Enter accurate fees and total fees, if applicable.
