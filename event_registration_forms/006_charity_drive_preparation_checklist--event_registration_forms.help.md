# Charity Drive Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for charity drive organizers to gather and organize essential information before and during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Drive Title** field with a brief title for your charity drive event.
2. Enter the name of the **Lead Coordinator**, who will be responsible for overseeing the event.
3. Select a **Planned Start Date** for the drive.
4. Describe the **Fundraising / Collection Goal** for the event, e.g., how much money you aim to collect.
5. Check off the **Venue or Location Secured** status, indicating whether the physical space is confirmed.
6. Select the status of **Necessary Permits Obtained**, indicating whether you've secured the required permits.
7. Choose whether **Volunteer Team Recruited** is in place or not.
8. Check if **Marketing Materials Created**, such as flyers, social media posts.
9. Select the status of **Budget Status**, whether you're **Under Budget**, **On Budget**, or **Over Budget**.
10. Optionally, enter any **Potential Risks or Obstacles** you've identified.
11. Update the **Last Updated Date** to reflect when the information was last revised.
12. Add any **Coordinator Additional Notes**, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Drive Title** (id: 2, required: true): A brief title for your charity drive event.
* **Lead Coordinator** (id: 3, required: true): The person responsible for overseeing the event.
* **Planned Start Date** (id: 4, required: true): The date you plan to start the event.
* **Fundraising / Collection Goal** (id: 5, required: true): A description of the main objective for the event.
* **Venue or Location Secured** (id: 7, required: true): Check if the physical space is confirmed.
* **Necessary Permits Obtained** (id: 8, required: true): Indicate if the required permits are obtained.
* **Volunteer Team Recruited** (id: 9, required: true): Select if the volunteer team is in place.
* **Marketing Materials Created** (id: 10, required: true): Check if marketing materials are created.
* **Budget Status** (id: 13, required: true): Select the status of your budget.
* **Potential Risks or Obstacles** (id: 14, optional): Describe any potential risks or obstacles you've identified.
* **Last Updated Date** (id: 15, required: true): Update the date when the information was last revised.
* **Coordinator Additional Notes** (id: 16, optional): Add any additional notes from the coordinator.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the **Venue or Location Secured** status to avoid last-minute changes.
* Keep the **Fundraising / Collection Goal** concise and achievable.
* Be honest about the **Budget Status** to track your expenses accurately.
* Use the **Potential Risks or Obstacles** section to identify and address any issues before the event.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. If you have any specific questions or need further clarification, please contact your event coordinator or refer to the official event guidelines.
