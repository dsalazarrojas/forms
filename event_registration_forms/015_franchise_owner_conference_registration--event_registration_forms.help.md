# franchise_owner_conference_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for franchise owners to register their details for our conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Select your preferred options for accommodation, language, and diet.
3. Choose the type of event you are attending.
4. Select the size of the event you are organizing.
5. Enter the dates and times of your event.
6. Provide the venue details of your event.
7. Add any special comments or requests.
8. Review and submit the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Details**: Fill in the general details about your conference.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your registered email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your primary address.
* **Event Details**: Enter the details of your event, such as the topic or agenda.
* **Accommodation Preferences**: Specify your preferred accommodation preferences for the conference.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication and event details.
* **Preferred Location**: Select one or more locations where you would like to hold your event.
* **Preferred Diet**: Choose your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian or non-vegetarian.
* **Special Requests**: Add any special requirements or requests you have for your event, such as accessibility needs or equipment requirements.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are organizing, such as a meeting or conference.
* **Event Size**: Choose the estimated number of attendees for your event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your event.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your event.
* **Venue**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about your event.
