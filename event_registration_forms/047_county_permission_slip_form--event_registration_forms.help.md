# county_permission_slip_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from schools to allow students to participate in events outside of the school, and to verify the student's identity and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant event registration forms that the student is registered for.
2. Enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
3. Enter the student's school name as it appears on their school's records.
4. Enter the school contact name as it appears on the school's records.
5. Enter the school's address as it appears on their records.
6. Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents.
7. Enter the student's grade level as it appears on their records.
8. Enter the school's grade level as it appears on their records.
9. Enter the student's phone number as it appears on their records.
10. Enter the school's phone number as it appears on their records.
11. Enter the student's email address as it appears on their records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Registration Forms**: 
    * Select the event registration forms that the student is registered for. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.
- **County Permission Slip**:
    * Enter the name and contact information of the student, as it appears on their school's records.
- **Students Name**:
    * Enter the name of the student as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Students School Name**:
    * Enter the name of the student's school as it appears on their records.
- **School Contact Name**:
    * Enter the name of the contact person at the student's school.
- **School Address**:
    * Enter the address of the student's school as it appears on their records.
- **Student Name**:
    * Enter the student's name again, for verification purposes.
- **Student Grade Level**:
    * Enter the grade level of the student as it appears on their records.
- **School Grade Level**:
    * Enter the grade level of the student's school as it appears on their records.
- **Student Phone**:
    * Enter the student's phone number as it appears on their records.
- **School Phone**:
    * Enter the school's phone number as it appears on their records.
- **Student Email**:
    * Enter the student's email address as it appears on their records.
