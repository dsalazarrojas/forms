# volunteer_shift_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps you request a volunteer shift for an event. Complete it accurately to ensure your shift preferences and availability are considered.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the event details in the `Event Details` field.
2.  Indicate if you are a volunteer or not in the `Volunteer Details` field.
3.  Choose your shift preferences in the `Shift Preferences` field.
4.  Enter the event date in the `Event Date` field.
5.  Enter the event time in the `Event Time` field.
6.  Enter the shift start time in the `Shift Start Time` field.
7.  Enter the shift end time in the `Shift End Time` field.
8.  Enter the shift duration in the `Shift Duration` field (if necessary).
9.  Enter your availability in the `Availability` field (if necessary).
10. Add any notes about your request in the `Notes` field (if necessary).
11. Enter the event location in the `Event Location` field (if necessary).
12. Enter your role in the event in the `Volunteer Role` field (if necessary).
13. Enter the shift start date in the `Shift Start Date` field (if necessary).
14. Enter the shift end date in the `Shift End Date` field (if necessary).
15. Enter the shift location in the `Shift Location` field (if necessary).
16. Enter your name in the `Volunteer Name` field.
17. Enter your email in the `Volunteer Email` field.
18. Enter your phone number in the `Volunteer Phone` field.
19. Choose if you'll be volunteering on your shift in the `Volunteer Shift` field.
20. Choose the type of shift you'll be volunteering on in the `Volunteer Shift Type` field.
21. Enter your shift start time in the `Volunteer Shift Start` field (if necessary).
22. Enter your shift end time in the `Volunteer Shift End` field (if necessary).
23. Enter the duration of your volunteer shift in the `Volunteer Shift Duration` field (if necessary).
24. Choose your preferences for the shift you'll be volunteering on in the `Volunteer Shift Preferences` field.
25. Finally, enter your availability in the `Volunteer Availability` field (if necessary).
