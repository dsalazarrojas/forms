# Regional Product Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from exhibitors and event organizers for a regional product showcase event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For non-required fields, enter any relevant information you'd like to share.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Regional Product Showcase Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of your product showcase.
* **Event Details** (`2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant details about the event, such as dates, times, and locations.
* **Exhibitor Info** (`3`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your company name, booth number, or any other relevant exhibitor information.
* **Product Info** (`4`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the product you're showcasing.
* **Terms Agreement** (`5`, `note`, optional):
	+ Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Event Organizer** (`6`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the type of event you're organizing:
		- Event Organizer
		- Local Expos
		- Trade Fairs
* **Contact Details** (`7`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Make sure to review the terms of agreement carefully before agreeing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
