# Fire Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Check In Form is designed to gather crucial information from employees during emergencies or events. This form helps ensure that everyone's presence is accounted for and that emergency responders can quickly locate and evacuate all employees as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Ensure you fill in all required fields to help emergency responders locate and assist you effectively.
2. **Select all applicable employees** in the "Employees" field, as their presence will be tracked for safety reasons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Main Form** (page 1)
* **Main Form** (`text`, optional): This field is a catch-all for any other important details or comments you may have regarding your location or status.
### **Fire Safety Check In Form** (page 2)
* **Fire Safety Check In Form** (`text`, optional): This field is a duplicate of the field above and can be used for further details or notes.
### **Employees** (page 3)
* **Employees** (`select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the employees present, including yourself. This list includes options for Employee A, Employee B, and Employee C. Please select all applicable employees for proper tracking and emergency response.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out the form completely and accurately.
* If there are any additional concerns or questions, use the "Main Form" field for further details.
* If you are unsure about which employees are present, refer back to your supervisor or team lead for assistance.
* Please complete the form as quickly and efficiently as possible to aid emergency response and ensure everyone's safety.
