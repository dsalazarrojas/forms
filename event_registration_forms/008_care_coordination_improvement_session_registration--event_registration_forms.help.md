# Care Coordination Improvement Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
<thinking>
This form is intended for healthcare professionals to register for a care coordination improvement session. The form collects information about the registrant, their role, experience, and interests in care coordination. The session is designed to improve care coordination practices and facilitate collaboration among healthcare professionals. 
The form is not meant to be used for non-healthcare purposes. If you are unsure about your eligibility, please contact the session organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: title, job title, organization name, email address, phone number, years of experience, role in care coordination, preferred session date, attendance format, topics of interest, current challenges, learning objectives, and how you heard about this session.
2. Select your preferred attendance format (in-person or virtual).
3. Indicate if you need continuing education credits and specify the type.
4. If you have a team attending with you, select the number of additional attendees.
5. If applicable, specify any dietary restrictions and accessibility needs.
6. If you need continuing education credits, select the type.
7. Specify how you heard about this session.
8. If you have any special requests, please include them.
9. Finally, confirm your agreement to attend the selected session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title within your organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization, such as hospital, clinic, or agency.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of organization you represent, such as hospital, outpatient clinic, etc.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for confirmation and session details.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional/optional): Enter the department you work in, if applicable.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your years of experience in healthcare.
* **Role in Care Coordination** (`role_in_care_coordination`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all roles that apply to your position in care coordination.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_preference`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your preferred session date.
* **Attendance Format** (`attendance_format`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your preferred attendance format, such as in-person or virtual.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all topics that interest you, such as interdisciplinary team communication, patient engagement strategies, etc.
* **Current Challenges** (`current_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your top 3 current care coordination challenges.
* **What Do You Hope to Learn** (`learning_objectives`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your primary learning objective for the session.
* **Team Attendees** (`team_attendees`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if you will be attending with others from your organization.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_one`, optional/optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please select them.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional/optional): Specify any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Continuing Education Credits** (`continuing_education`, `select_one`, required/optional): If you need continuing education credits, select the type.
* **How Did You Hear About This Session** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how you heard about this session.
* **Special Requests or Questions** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional/optional): Enter any special requests or questions you may have.
* **Agree to Attend the Selected Session** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm your agreement to attend the selected session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you select your preferred session date and attendance format.
* You may select all that apply for your role in care coordination and topics of interest.
* If you need continuing education credits, please select the type.
* If you are bringing a team, please select the correct number of attendees.
* If you have any accessibility needs or dietary restrictions, please specify them.
* Finally, confirm your agreement to attend the selected session.
