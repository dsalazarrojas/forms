# Academic Seminar Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Seminar Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for an academic seminar. This form is used to gather essential details for logistical planning and to ensure the accurate registration of volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form sections, each covering a specific aspect of your profile and commitment.
2. Fill out the required fields, denoted by a red asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Volunteer Name, text, required): Please enter your full name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Volunteer Email, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Volunteer Phone, text, required): Provide your mobile phone number, as it will be used for scheduling and communication.
* **Current Status** (Status, select_one, required): Select your current role within the institution: Student, Faculty, Staff, or External.
* **Roles you are interested in** (Roles Interested, select_multiple, required): Choose all the roles you are interested in volunteering for, such as Registration Desk, Technical Support, Speaker Liaison, etc.
* **Select your availability** (Availability Shifts, select_multiple, required): Choose the shifts you are available to work, such as Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Evening Reception, etc.
* **T-shirt Size for Volunteer Gear** (T-shirt Size, select_one, required): Select your t-shirt size for volunteer gear, considering unisex sizing (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
* **Have you volunteered for academic events before?** (Previous Volunteer Experience, select_one, required): Answer whether you have any prior experience volunteering for academic events.
* **Are you proficient in a second language for international guests?** (Language Proficiency, select_one, required): Indicate your proficiency in a second language, which will be useful for supporting international guests.
* **List any technical or organizational skills** (Specific Skills, text, optional): Briefly describe any technical or organizational skills you possess, such as Excel, AV equipment, or Crowd control.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **I commit to attending the orientation session and my assigned shifts** (Volunteer Commitment, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to attend the orientation session and your assigned shifts.
