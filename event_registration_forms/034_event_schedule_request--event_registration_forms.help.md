# Event Schedule Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Schedule Request form is designed to collect information from event organizers or planners about their event requests. It helps in scheduling events and arranging necessary facilities and arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a brief description of your event in the "request_event" field. This will help the event coordinator understand the scope and nature of your event.
2.  Provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field, so that the event coordinator can reach out to you for further clarification or questions.
3.  Enter details about the event in the "event_info" field, including the type of event and any specific requirements.
4.  In the "facility_setup" field, describe the facility arrangements you need for your event, such as equipment or venue requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **request_event** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your event, such as a wedding, conference, or workshop.
*   **contact_info** (text, required/optional): Provide your name, email, and phone number so that the event coordinator can contact you for further clarification or questions.
*   **event_info** (text, required/optional): Enter details about the event, including the type, date, time, and number of attendees.
*   **facility_setup** (text, required/optional): Describe the facility arrangements you need, such as equipment, seating capacity, or catering options.
