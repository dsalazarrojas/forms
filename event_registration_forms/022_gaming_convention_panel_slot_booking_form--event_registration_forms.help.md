In the Gaming Convention Panel Slot Booking Form, you'll find the following fields:

*   Slot Start Time: A field for selecting the start time of your panel slot in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   Slot Duration: A field for selecting the duration of your panel slot in hours.
*   Slot Days: A field for selecting the day of the week your panel slot will take place.
*   Panel ID: A field for entering a unique identifier for your panel.
*   Panel Name: A field for entering the name of your panel.
*   Description: A field for entering a brief description of your panel.
*   Start Date: A field for selecting the start date of your panel slot in the format MM/DD/YY.
*   End Date: A field for selecting the end date of your panel slot in the format MM/DD/YY.

Note that some fields may not be applicable to specific panels or scenarios, so use your best judgment when completing the form.
