# Trick Or Treat Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in a Trick or Treat event. Fill out this form to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Street** fields. These are the basic contact details that will help us get in touch with you.
2. If you have an **Address 2**, please fill it in. This could be a unit or apartment number.
3. Enter your **City**, **Region**, and **Postal Code**. These will help us understand your location.
4. Fill in your **Country**. This will help us determine the event's location and accessibility.
5. Enter your **Email** address. This is where we'll send you updates and important information about the event.
6. If you have a **Phone** number, please fill it in. This will help us contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
7. Fill in your **Date of Birth**. Please note that this field is currently optional, but its purpose is unclear. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
8. Click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Street**: Enter your street address.
* **Address 2**: Enter your unit or apartment number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Region**: Enter the region or state where you reside.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth**: (Optional) Enter your date of birth if required by law or event regulations.
* **Note**: If you're unsure about any of the fields, please leave them blank and we'll contact you to clarify.
