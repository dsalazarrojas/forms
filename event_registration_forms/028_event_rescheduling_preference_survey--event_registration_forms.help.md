# Event Rescheduling Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect preferences from participants regarding rescheduling an event. Its purpose is to gather information on the ideal date, time, and duration of the event to accommodate the needs and preferences of all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form title, "Event Rescheduling Preference Survey," to understand its purpose.
2. Answer each question in the designated field, providing your preferences for the event date, time, and duration.
3. Ensure all required fields (none are marked as required in the provided form schema) are completed to the best of your ability.
4. If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Rescheduling Preference Survey** (1) (`event_rescheduling_preference_survey`, `text`, required: false): This is the form title. You can't fill out this field.
* **Which date** (2) (`event_rescheduling_preference_survey_1`, `date`, required: false): This field is for selecting an ideal date for the event. Choose a date when you would be available to attend.
* **Which time** (3) (`event_rescheduling_preference_survey_2`, `time`, required: false): Select a preferred time for the event. Keep in mind that this will help organizers to schedule the event at a time that suits most people.
* **How long** (4) (`event_rescheduling_preference_survey_3`, `text`, required: false): Describe how long you think the event should last. This will help organizers to plan the event duration accordingly.
* **How long (hours)** (5) (`event_rescheduling_preference_survey_4`, `number`, required: false): Provide a specific number of hours you would like the event to last. Please enter a whole number without decimal points. 

# Tips
Remember, this form is for expressing your preferences. Please answer honestly, and feel free to share any additional comments or suggestions that may be helpful to the event organizers.
