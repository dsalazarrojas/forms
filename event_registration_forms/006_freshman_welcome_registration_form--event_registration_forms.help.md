# Freshman Welcome Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freshman Welcome Registration Form is used to collect information from new students to help the institution understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Enter your Phone Number.
6. Enter your Student ID Number.
7. Select your Gender.
8. Select your Academic Program (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Other).
9. Select your Student Status (Freshman, Transfer, Graduate, or Other).
10. Select your Student Level (Undergraduate or Postgraduate).
11. Indicate whether or not you will attend events.
12. Provide the Name of your Emergency Contact.
13. Provide the Phone Number of your Emergency Contact.
14. Enter a brief description of your Parent or Guardian (if applicable).
15. Enter the Phone Number of your Parent or Guardian (if applicable).
16. Select your Diet (Vegan, Non-Vegan, Vegetarian, Omnivore, or Other).
17. Enter any Medical Conditions you may have.
18. Enter any Medical Notes or requirements.
19. Enter any Disability information.
20. Enter any Disability Notes or requirements.
21. Enter any Allergy information.
22. Enter any Allergy Notes or requirements.
23. Enter a brief description of your Student Interests.
24. Enter any Emergency Notes.
25. Enter any Special Requests or requests for accommodations.
