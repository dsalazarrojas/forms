# Annual Ride Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Ride Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors for the annual ride event. This form helps us understand your company's interests and needs as a sponsor and ensures we can tailor our event to meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company or organization name in the "Company or Organization Name" field.
2. Provide your primary contact person's name and email address in the "Primary Contact Person" and "Email Address" fields.
3. Specify your preferred payment method in the "Preferred Payment Method" field.
4. Choose your sponsorship level and amount in the "Sponsorship Level Requested" and "Sponsorship Amount" fields.
5. If you have previously sponsored this event, select your sponsorship history in the "Prior sponsorship history" field.
6. If you are an existing sponsor, provide the year and level of your previous sponsorship in the "If previous sponsor" field.
7. Choose whether you are interested in providing in-kind donations and specify the details of such donations in the "In-kind donation offered" field.
8. Provide the date and location of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Location or Route" fields.
9. Specify the approximate number of participants expected in the "Expected Participants" field.
10. Choose whether you are interested in using your logo and whether you need permission for its usage in the "Logo usage rights requested" field.
11. If you are interested in using your logo, provide the high-resolution logo file in the "Logo file attached" field.
12. Specify whether you need booth or vendor space and the size of the space you need in the "Booth or vendor space requested" and "Booth size needed" fields.
13. Choose how many of your company's participants will be participating in the event in the "Company participants in the ride" field.
14. Specify the number of volunteers you will provide in the "Volunteer support" field.
15. If you have any special recognition requests, specify them in the "Special recognition requests" field.
16. If you are providing promotional materials, describe them in the "Promotional materials provided" field.
17. Provide your company's social media handles for promotional purposes in the "Social media handles" field.
18. Finally, add any additional comments or requests in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company or Organization Name**: Enter your company or organization name that will be sponsoring this event.
* **Primary Contact Person**: Name and title of the person responsible for coordinating this sponsorship.
* **Email Address**: Contact email of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your sponsorship (e.g. Credit card, bank transfer, check, etc.).
* **Prior sponsorship history**: Select how many times you have previously sponsored this event.
* **If previous sponsor - year and level**: If you are a previous sponsor, enter the year and level of your previous sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Level Requested**: Choose the level of sponsorship you are interested in (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze, Friend, In-kind).
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount of money you are willing to sponsor.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for your sponsorship.
* **In-kind donation offered**: Choose whether you are willing to provide in-kind donations.
* **Details of in-kind donation**: If you are providing in-kind donations, describe the details of such donations.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location or Route**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Expected Participants**: Enter the approximate number of participants expected.
* **Logo usage rights requested**: Choose whether you would like to use your logo for promotional purposes.
* **Logo file attached**: If you are using your logo, attach a high-resolution logo file.
* **Booth or vendor space requested**: Choose whether you would like to have a booth or vendor space.
* **Booth size needed**: If you need a booth or vendor space, choose the size you need.
* **Company participants in the ride**: Choose how many of your company's participants will be riding.
* **Volunteer support**: Enter the number of volunteers you are willing to provide.
* **Special recognition requests**: Enter any special recognition requests.
* **Promotional materials provided**: If you are providing promotional materials, describe them.
* **Social media handles**: Enter your company's social media handles for promotional purposes.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or requests.
