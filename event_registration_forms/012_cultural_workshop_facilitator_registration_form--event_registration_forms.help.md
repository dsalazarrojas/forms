# cultural_workshop_facilitator_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register as a cultural workshop facilitator. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Select your phone number from the list in the "phone" field.
4. Choose the type of event you will be facilitating from the list in the "event_type" field.
5. Enter the name of the event in the "event_name" field.
6. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
7. Enter your last name in "last_name" field.
8. Enter your facilitator phone number in the "facilitator_phone" field.
9. Enter your facilitator email address in the "facilitator_email" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note" field (optional).
11. Review the form for any errors or missing information before clicking the "submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **registration** (`cultural_workshop_facilitator_registration`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter a brief description of your facilitation experience and background.
* **facilitator_info** (`facilitator_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about your facilitation style, approach, or experience.
* **event_details** (`event_details`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the event you will be facilitating, including the date, time, and location.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
* **email** (`email`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select your email address from the list.
* **phone** (`phone`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select your phone number from the list.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **event_name** (`event_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the event.
* **event_type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of event from the list (workshop, conference, or meeting).
* **event_location** (`event_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location of the event.
* **facilitator_experience** (`facilitator_experience`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of years of experience you have as a facilitator.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional):
	+ Click this button to submit the form.
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your last name.
* **facilitator_phone** (`facilitator_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your facilitator phone number.
* **facilitator_email** (`facilitator_email`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your facilitator email address.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before clicking the "submit" button.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
