# data_visualization_training_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for data visualization training sessions. It collects information about the participant, including their name, email, role, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields carefully, as they are essential for creating the participant list.
2. If you are registering multiple participants, use the "Attachments" field to attach a single CSV or Excel file containing all the registrations.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form, as any errors might lead to incorrect participant details in the participant list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (id: 1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID, business card, or other official documents.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Role (id: 5)**: Select your role, such as Data Scientist, Data Analyst, or Other.
* **Comments (id: 6)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Date (id: 7)**: Select the date of the training session you are registering for.
* **Time (id: 8)**: Select the time of the training session you are registering for.
* **Training Date (id: 9)**: Select the date of the training session you are registering for.
* **Session Duration (id: 10)**: Select the duration of the training session you are registering for.
* **Location (id: 11)**: Enter the location of the training session you are registering for.
* **Session Type (id: 12)**: Select the type of session you are registering for, such as Online, On-site, or Hybrid.
* **Training Length (id: 13)**: Select the length of the training session you are registering for, such as Half-day, Full-day, or Multi-day.
* **Training Format (id: 14)**: Select the format of the training session you are registering for, such as In-person, Online, or Hybrid.
* **Training Level (id: 15)**: Select your level of training, such as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Session Language (id: 16)**: Select the language of the training session you are registering for, such as English, Spanish, or Mandarin.
* **Session Time (id: 17)**: Select the time of the training session you are registering for.
* **Session Start (id: 18)**: Select the start date and time of the training session.
* **Session End (id: 19)**: Select the end date and time of the training session.
* **Instructor (id: 20)**: Select the instructor leading the training session.
* **Course (id: 21)**: Select the course you are registering for, if applicable.
* **Notes (id: 22)**: Enter any additional notes or information about your registration.
* **Session Notes (id: 23)**: Enter any notes or information specific to the session.
* **Comments (id: 24)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Attachments (id: 25)**: Attach a single CSV or Excel file containing all the registrations, if applicable.
