# DJ Services Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DJ Services Billing Form is a critical tool for processing client payments and managing event details. It is designed to gather essential information from clients about their events and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select Your Service Details**: Choose the service you require from the provided options or describe your service needs in the text field.
2. **Enter Billing Address**: Provide your billing address for accurate payment processing.
3. **Choose Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer, such as Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer. Note that Other is an option for custom payment arrangements.
4. **Enter Service Price**: Provide the exact price for the services rendered, including any additional fees or taxes.
5. **Enter Event Date**: Select the date of the event or service.
6. **Enter Event Time**: Select the time of the event or service (optional, but recommended).
7. **Enter Client Name**: Provide the client's full name for accurate billing and communication.
8. **Enter Client Email**: Enter the client's email address for communication and payment receipt.
9. **Enter Client Phone**: Provide the client's phone number for quick contact and confirmation.
10. **Add Event Note (Optional)**: Provide any additional event details or notes, if necessary.
11. **Add Billing Note (Optional)**: Provide any additional billing details or notes, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the services you require, if you don't see your service listed.
- **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where the payment will be sent to.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, required/true): Choose how you want to pay for your services (Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
- **Service Price** (`service_price`, number, required/true): Enter the exact price for the services rendered.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/true): Select the date of the event or service.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/false): Choose the time of the event or service (if applicable).
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/true): Enter the client's full name for accurate billing and communication.
- **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required/true): Enter the client's email address for communication and payment receipt.
- **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/true): Enter the client's phone number for quick contact and confirmation.
- **Event Note** (`event_note`, note, required/false): Enter any additional event details or notes.
- **Billing Note** (`billing_note`, note, required/false): Enter any additional billing details or notes.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure you have the client's permission to share their contact information.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
