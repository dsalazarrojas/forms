# Trade Season Pass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Season Pass Registration form is used to register a trade season pass for a specific trade show or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a field is optional, fill it out if it applies to your situation.
4. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trade_season_pass_registration_form** (id: 1, text, required/optional: false): This is the main registration title.
* **business_details** (id: 2, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name and any other relevant details about your business.
* **pass_type** (id: 3, select_one, required/optional: false): Select the type of pass you are registering for (e.g., "Pass 1", "Pass 2", etc.).
* **start_date** (id: 4, date, required/optional: false): Enter the start date of the trade season pass.
* **end_date** (id: 5, date, required/optional: false): Enter the end date of the trade season pass.
* **venue** (id: 6, text, required/optional: false): Enter the venue name where the trade season pass is being used.
* **contact_name** (id: 7, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the person or business representative who is filling out the form.
* **contact_phone** (id: 8, text, required/optional: false): Enter the phone number of the person or business representative who is filling out the form.
* **contact_email** (id: 9, email, required/optional: false): Enter the email address of the person or business representative who is filling out the form.
* **pass_type_note** (id: 10, note, required/optional: false): Add any additional notes about the pass type.
* **membership_program** (id: 11, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the membership program associated with the trade season pass.
* **membership_program_url** (id: 12, text, required/optional: false): Enter the URL of the membership program associated with the trade season pass.
* **trade_show_organizer** (id: 13, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the trade show organizer.
* **trade_show_organizer_url** (id: 14, text, required/optional: false): Enter the URL of the trade show organizer.
* **pass_price** (id: 15, number, required/optional: false): Enter the price of the trade season pass.
* **pass_notes** (id: 16, text, required/optional: false): Add any additional notes about the trade season pass.
* **trade_show_name** (id: 17, text, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the trade show.
* **trade_show_url** (id: 18, text, required/optional: false): Enter the URL of the trade show.
* **season** (id: 19, text, required/optional: false): Enter the season of the trade season pass.
* **season_notes** (id: 20, text, required/optional: false): Add any additional notes about the season.
* **season_start_date** (id: 21, date, required/optional: false): Enter the start date of the season.
* **season_end_date** (id: 22, date, required/optional: false): Enter the end date of the season.
* **Trade show dates** (id: 23, select_multiple, required/optional: false): Select the trade show dates for the pass.
* **season_start_date_note** (id: 24, note, required/optional: false): Add any notes about the season start date.
* **season_end_date_note** (id: 25, note, required/optional: false): Add any notes about the season end date.

## Tips
1. Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
2. If you are unsure about a particular field, refer to the provided hints.
3. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form. 

This will ensure that your trade season pass registration is processed correctly and efficiently.
