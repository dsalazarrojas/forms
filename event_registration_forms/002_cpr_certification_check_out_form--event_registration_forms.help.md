# CPR Certification Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for CPR certification check out purposes. It's essential to understand that this form is for check out purposes only, and it's not intended to be a registration form for new participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** accurately.
2. Select the **CPR Course Type** that corresponds to your certification needs.
3. Choose your **Preferred Training Date** from the available options.
4. Select the **Training Location** that suits your needs.
5. Choose a **Payment Method** that is convenient for you.
6. If applicable, enter a **Discount or Referral Code** to receive any available discounts.
7. Read and agree to the **Terms and Agreement** by selecting the correct option.
8. If necessary, provide any **Special Requirements or Comments** to help us assist you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for digital certificate delivery.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **CPR Course Type**: Choose the CPR certification level that you're seeking (e.g., Adult CPR/AED, Pediatric CPR/AED, etc.).
* **Preferred Training Date**: Select the date you'd like to attend the training session.
* **Training Location**: Choose the training location that suits your needs (Main Campus, Downtown Center, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method that's most convenient for you (Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Discount or Referral Code**: If you have a valid discount or referral code, enter it here to receive any available discounts.
* **Terms Agreement**: Please review and agree to the cancellation and refund policy.
* **Special Requirements or Comments**: If you have any special requirements or comments, such as accessibility needs or dietary restrictions, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Please ensure that you've reviewed and agreed to the cancellation and refund policy before submitting the form.
