# Wedding DJ And Lighting Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your wedding event and your contact details, so our team can provide a quote for our wedding DJ and lighting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Event Details**: Provide information about the wedding event, including event date, start and end time, venue address, and event name.
2. **Wedding DJ Details**: Describe the type of DJ services required for the event.
3. **Wedding Lighting Details**: Describe the lighting requirements for the event.
4. **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details, including phone number, email, contact name, title, company, and department.
5. **Additional Information**: If there are any special requests or notes you would like to share with us, please provide them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**:
  * **Event Date**: The date of the wedding event.
  * **Start Time**: The start time of the event.
  * **End Time**: The end time of the event.
  * **Venue Address**: The address of the event venue.
  * **Event Name**: The name of the event.
- **Wedding DJ Details**:
  * **Wedding DJ Services**: Describe the type of DJ services required, such as playlist selection, lighting, or special effects.
- **Wedding Lighting Details**:
  * **Wedding Lighting Details**: Describe the lighting requirements for the event, such as number of lights, color scheme, or special effects.
- **Contact Information**:
  * **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
  * **Email**: Your email address.
  * **Contact Name**: Your name.
  * **Contact Title**: Your job title.
  * **Contact Company**: Your company name.
  * **Contact Department**: Your department name.
  * **Contact Notes**: Any additional notes you would like to share with us.
- **Additional Information**:
  * **Event Date 2**: The date of the second event (if applicable).
  * **Start Time 2**: The start time of the second event (if applicable).
  * **End Time 2**: The end time of the second event (if applicable).
  * **Venue Address 2**: The address of the second event venue (if applicable).
  * **Event Name 2**: The name of the second event (if applicable).
  * **Notes**: Any additional notes you would like to share with us.
