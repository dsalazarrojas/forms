# press_interview_slot_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a press interview slot for an event. It helps the event organizers collect information from journalists who are interested in conducting interviews at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the event, including the event ID (if provided).
2. Fill in the event title to help identify the specific event you are registering for.
3. Enter your journalist ID, if you have one.
4. Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a journalist.
5. Enter the name of your media outlet.
6. Enter the website of your media outlet (if available).
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
9. Select the time slot you are interested in for the interview.
9. Indicate whether you would like to conduct an interview ('Yes') or not ('No').
10. Enter the expected length of your interview (if you have a specific time in mind).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_id** (number, optional): Enter the ID of the event you are registering for.
* **event_title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the event you are registering for, which can help identify the event.
* **journalist_id** (number, optional): Enter your journalist ID, if you have one.
* **journalist_name** (text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a journalist.
* **media_outlet** (text, optional): Enter the name of your media outlet.
* **media_outlet_website** (text, optional): Enter the website of your media outlet, if available.
* **journalist_phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **journalist_email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **requested_time** (time, optional): Select the time slot you are interested in for the interview.
* **interview_type** (select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would like to conduct an interview ('Yes') or not ('No').
* **interview_length** (text, optional): Enter the expected length of your interview, if you have a specific time in mind.

## Tips

* Please ensure the accuracy of the information provided, as this will help ensure that the event organizers can coordinate the interviews effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers for further assistance.
