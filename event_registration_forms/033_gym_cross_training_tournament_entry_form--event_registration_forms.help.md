# Gym Cross Training Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Cross Training Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect information about participants and teams for the Cross Training Tournament. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament.
   - **Event ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies the event.
   - **Event URL**: Enter the URL associated with the event.
   - **Event Type ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies the type of event.
   - **Event Format ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies the format of the event.
   - **Entry Fee ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies the entry fee of the event.
   - **Team Size ID**: Enter a unique number that identifies the team size of the event.

   Note: These fields are required to be completed by the user.

2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
   - **Division**: Choose the division for which you are registering (Athlete, Coach, or Team).
   - **Athlete Name**: Enter your name as an athlete.
   - **Athlete Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
   - **Division Name**: Enter the name of the division you are registering for.
   - **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event.
   - **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event.
   - **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
   - **Checkin Time**: Enter the time to check-in for the event.
   - **Checkin Date**: Choose the date to check-in for the event.
   - **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event (Scheduled, Canceled, or In Progress).
   - **Division Status**: Choose the status of the division (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).
   - **Athlete Status**: Choose the status of the athlete (Active, Inactive, or Retired).
   - **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Team or Solo).
   - **Event Format**: Choose the format of the event (Round Robin or Single Elimination).
   - **Entry Fee**: Choose whether the entry fee is Included or Excluded.
   - **Team Size**: Choose the team size (1-2, 3-4, or 5-6).
   - **Team Division**: Choose the division of the team (Men, Women, or Mixed).
   - **Team Status**: Choose the status of the team (Active, Inactive, or Canceled).
