# Grip Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grip Application Form is designed to help applicants register their experience, job preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Select your experience level in the "Experience" field.
3. Choose the equipment you are familiar with in the "Equipment" field.
4. Indicate your job type in the "Job Type" field.
5. Select your job location in the "Job Location" field.
6. Enter your job start and end dates in the "Job Start Date" and "Job End Date" fields respectively.
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Describe your job description, requirements, and job status in the "Job Description", "Job Requirements", and "Job Status" fields respectively.
9. Select your language proficiency level in the "Job English" field.
10. Enter your job supervisor's name in the "Job Supervisors" field.
11. Add any additional job notes in the "Job Notes" field.
12. Enter your job address in the "Address Line 1" and "Address Line 2" fields.
13. Enter your city and phone number in the "City" and "Phone" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Experience (Experience, select_one, required)**: Select your level of experience, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Equipment (Equipment, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the equipment you are familiar with from the dropdown list.
* **Job Type (Job Type, select_one, required)**: Indicate if you are looking for a job type, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Location (Job Location, select_one, required)**: Select your job location, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Start Date (Job Start Date, date, required)**: Enter your start date in the job.
* **Job End Date (Job End Date, date, required)**: Enter your end date in the job.
* **Job Title (Job Title, text, required)**: Enter your job title.
* **Job Description (Job Description, text, required)**: Describe your job description.
* **Job Requirements (Job Requirements, text, required)**: List the requirements you are looking for in a job.
* **Job Status (Job Status, select_one, required)**: Indicate the status of your job, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Job Skills (Job Skills, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the skills you have from the dropdown list.
* **Job Experience (Job Experience, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the experience you have from the dropdown list.
* **Job References (Job References, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the references you have from the dropdown list.
* **Job Supervisors (Job Supervisors, select_multiple, required)**: Enter the names of your job supervisors.
* **Job English (Job English, text, required)**: Describe your level of English proficiency.
* **Job Notes (Job Notes, text, required)**: Enter any additional job notes.
* **Address Line 1 (Address Line 1, text, required)**: Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2 (Address Line 2, text, required)**: Enter your address line 2.
* **City (City, text, required)**: Enter your city.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submit (Submit, text, required)**: Click this button to submit your application.
* **Cancel (Cancel, text, required)**: Click this button to cancel your application.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field label for clarification.
