# parachuting_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document parachuting incidents, providing essential details for the safety team to investigate and analyze incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief summary of the incident in the **Incident Summary** field.
2. Enter the **Incident Location** where the incident occurred.
3. Select the **Incident Date** of the incident.
4. Provide the **Jump Location** where the incident took place.
5. Enter the **Number of Participants** involved in the incident.
6. Describe the **Equipment Used** during the incident.
7. Write a detailed **Incident Description** of what happened.
8. Select the **Incident Outcome** of the incident.
9. Choose the **Investigation Status** of the incident (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
10. Provide a **Conclusion** about the investigation findings.
11. List **Safety Risk Factors** that contributed to the incident.
12. Specify the **Incident Cause** of the incident.
13. Enter the name of the person who **Reported the Incident**.
14. Select the **Time** when the incident was reported.
15. Choose the person or team to whom the incident was **Reported To**.
16. Select the **Investigation Responsible** team or person.
17. Add any **Investigation Comments** (optional).
18. Enter the **Skydiver Name** involved in the incident.
19. Select the **Skydiver License** level (A, B, C, or D).
20. Enter the **Skydiver License Expiration** date.
21. Enter the **Jump Attendant** name.
22. Select the **Skydiver Rating** (A, B, C, or D).
23. Check if the **Skydiver License Valid**.
24. Enter the **Equipment Inspection** notes.
25. Add any **Additional Comments** about the incident (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: Select the date of the incident.
* **Jump Location**: Enter the location where the jump took place.
* **Number of Participants**: Enter the number of people involved in the incident.
* **Equipment Used**: Describe the equipment used during the incident.
* **Incident Description**: Write a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Outcome**: Select the outcome of the incident.
* **Investigation Status**: Choose the status of the investigation (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Investigation Conclusion**: Provide a conclusion about the investigation findings.
* **Safety Risk Factors**: List the factors that contributed to the incident.
* **Incident Cause**: Specify the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Reported By**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported At**: Select the time when the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reported To**: Choose the person or team to whom the incident was reported.
* **Investigation Responsible**: Select the team or person responsible for the investigation.
* **Investigation Comments**: Add any comments about the investigation (optional).
* **Skydiver Name**: Enter the name of the skydiver involved in the incident.
* **Skydiver License**: Select the skydiver's license level (A, B, C, or D).
* **Skydiver License Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the skydiver's license.
* **Jump Attendant**: Enter the name of the jump attendant.
* **Skydiver Rating**: Select the skydiver's rating (A, B, C, or D).
* **Skydiver License Valid**: Check if the skydiver's license is valid.
* **Equipment Inspection**: Enter any inspection notes about the equipment used.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about the incident (optional).
