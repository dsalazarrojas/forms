# Computer Science Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from attendees for the Computer Science event.

### Step 1: Registration ID
* **Registration ID** (`registration_id`, text, required): Enter your unique registration identifier.

### Step 2: Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Select the date of your registration.

### Step 3: Attendee Information
* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current position.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Step 4: Event Preferences
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your industry from the options provided.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, select_one, required): Select the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of registration you are applying for.

### Step 5: Event Details
* **Technical Tracks of Interest** (`technical_tracks`, select_multiple, required): Select the technical tracks you are interested in attending.
* **Networking Interests** (`networking_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas you are interested in networking about.

### Step 6: Additional Information
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, optional): Select any special dietary needs.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your T-shirt size for event swag.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility accommodations required.
* **How Did You Hear About Event** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select the source that led you to the event.
* **First Time Attendee** (`first_time_attendee`, select_one, required): Indicate if this is your first time attending the event.

### Step 7: Payment and Preferences
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Marketing Communications** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, required): Allow or decline future event updates.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the event.

### Step 8: Registration Status
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required): Select your current registration status.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you experience any issues during the registration process, please contact event staff for assistance.
* Registration will be confirmed upon successful completion of the form and payment processing.
