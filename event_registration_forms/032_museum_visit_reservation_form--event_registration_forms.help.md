# Museum Visit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Visit Reservation Form is used to collect information from visitors to better prepare for their visit and provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the visit date for the visitor.
2. Choose the reservation time for the visit.
3. Enter the visitor's name.
4. Provide the visitor's email address.
5. Enter the visitor's phone number.
6. Add any additional notes about the visitor.
7. Specify the expected visit duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Date** (date): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Reservation Time** (time): Choose the time of the reservation.
* **Visitor Name** (text): Enter the visitor's name.
* **Visitor E-mail** (email): Enter the visitor's email address to help us contact them if needed.
* **Visitor Phone** (text): Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Visitor Notes** (note): Add any additional information about the visitor, such as special requests or accessibility needs.
* **Visit Duration** (text): Estimate the expected duration of the visit.
