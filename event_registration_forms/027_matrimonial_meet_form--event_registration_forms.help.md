# Matrimonial Meet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Matrimonial Meet Form is a document designed to collect information about individuals who wish to meet with a matrimonial counselor. This form helps to gather essential details, such as contact information, availability, and preferences, to facilitate a smooth and efficient meeting experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select your current marital status from the dropdown menu in the "marital_status" field.
7. Choose a preferred date for the meeting in the "preferred_meet_date" field.
8. Select your availability for the meeting by checking the relevant boxes in the "availability" field.
9. Indicate your preferences for the meeting by selecting the relevant options in the "event_preferences" field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current marital status from the available options: Active or Inactive.
* **Preferred Meet Date** (`preferred_meet_date`, date, required: false): Choose a preferred date for the meeting.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the boxes for the dates and times you are available.
* **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferences for the meeting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the matrimonial counselor for assistance.
