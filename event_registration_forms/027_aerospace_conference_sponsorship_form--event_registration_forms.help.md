# Aerospace Conference Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors for the Aerospace Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the sponsor's company name in the "Company Name" field.
2.  Enter the sponsor's primary contact person in the "Primary Contact Person" field.
3.  Enter the sponsor's email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Enter the sponsor's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  If applicable, enter the sponsor's website in the "Company Website" field.
6.  Select the desired sponsorship level from the "Sponsorship Level" dropdown menu.
7.  If applicable, select any additional opportunities from the "Additional Opportunities" dropdown menu.
8.  Upload or link the high-resolution logo in the "Link to High-Res Logo (Vector/PNG)" field.
9.  Enter the company description in the "Company Description (100 words)" field.
10. Select the "Payment Terms Agreement" option to agree to the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Company Name**: As it should appear in marketing materials.
*   **Primary Contact Person**: The person to contact for sponsorship related inquiries.
*   **Email Address**: The email address of the contact person.
*   **Phone Number**: The phone number of the contact person.
*   **Company Website**: The website of the sponsor's company.
*   **Sponsorship Level**: Choose from Platinum ($25,000), Gold ($15,000), Silver ($10,000), Bronze ($5,000), or Exhibitor Only ($2,500) sponsorship levels.
*   **Additional Opportunities**: Select any extras, such as the Gala Dinner Sponsor, Lanyard Branding, Coffee Break Sponsor, Workshop Host, or Bag Insert.
*   **Link to High-Res Logo (Vector/PNG)**: Upload or link a high-resolution logo in Vector or PNG format.
*   **Company Description (100 words)**: A brief description of the company, to be included in the conference program.
*   **Payment Terms Agreement**: Select "I Agree" to confirm payment within 30 days.

## Tips
*   Ensure that the company name, primary contact person, email address, phone number, and company description are accurate and complete.
*   Choose the correct sponsorship level that suits the company's needs and budget.
*   Ensure that the logo is in high resolution and in the correct format (Vector or PNG).
*   Review the prospectus for benefits and requirements for each sponsorship level.
*   Agree to the payment terms to confirm that the company will pay the agreed-upon amount within 30 days.
