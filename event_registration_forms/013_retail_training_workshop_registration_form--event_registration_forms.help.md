# Retail Training Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants who are interested in attending a retail training workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, one at a time.
2. Each page has a unique label and contains relevant fields for you to fill out.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Below, we explain each field in the form, one by one:

- **Workshop Information** (Page 1)
  - **Title**: The title of the form, which is "Retail Training Workshop Registration Form". This is the name of the form, not a field to fill out.
  
- **Workshop Information** (Page 2)
  - **Page 2 Label: Workshop Information**: This is the label for the page, not a field to fill out. You can fill out the fields on this page.
    - **Page 2 Hint**: This page is about providing information about the workshop.
  - **Your Name**: <name> (Text field): Provide your name as it is spelled and formatted.
  - **Email Address**: <email> (Text field): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
  - **Phone Number**: <phone> (Text field): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
  - **Company/Organization**: <organization> (Text field): If you are registering as a representative of a company or organization, please enter your company name.
  - **Job Title/Position**: <job_title> (Text field): Enter your job title or position within your organization.

- **Personal Information** (Page 3)
  - **Page 3 Label: Personal Information**: This is the label for the page, not a field to fill out. You can fill out the fields on this page.
  - **Page 3 Hint**: This page is about providing personal details.
  - **Name**: <name> (Text field): Enter your name as it is spelled and formatted.
  - **Email Address**: <email> (Text field): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
  - **Phone Number**: <phone> (Text field): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

  - **Session Type**: <session_type> (Text field): Choose the type of session you are interested in attending.
