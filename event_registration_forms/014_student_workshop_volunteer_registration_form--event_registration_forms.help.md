# Student Workshop Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who want to volunteer for a workshop event. Fill out this form to express your interest in volunteering for one or more events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form and ensure all fields have been filled out accurately.
- Fill out each field according to its label, starting with "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
- Input your email address in the "Email" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
- Select your available date to volunteer in the "Date" field.
- Choose your available time to volunteer in the "Time" field.
- Select one or more events you are interested in volunteering for in the "Event" field.
- If you have any additional comments or information to provide, enter them in the "Note" field.
- Finally, enter your name as the submitter in the "Submitted By" field.
