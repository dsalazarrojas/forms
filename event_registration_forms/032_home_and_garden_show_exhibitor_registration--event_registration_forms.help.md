# Home And Garden Show Exhibitor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home And Garden Show Exhibitor Registration form is designed to collect information from exhibitors for the Home And Garden Show. This form will help us better understand your needs and requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields, making sure to provide accurate and complete information. Please note that this form is not a binding contract, and submitting it does not guarantee your participation in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section will ask for your general information, including your name, email, and contact phone number. While this information is not required, providing it will help us contact you with any important updates or reminders.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section will ask for your vendor's name, address, and website. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section will ask for the contact person's name, email, and phone number. This information will help us get in touch with someone who can respond to any questions or concerns.
* **Booth Plan** (`booth_plan`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, please specify the type and requirements of your booth setup. For example, you can enter "8x8" or "10x10" for the size of your booth.
* **Payment** (`payment`, `text`, required/optional): This section will ask for your payment method and any additional comments. Please enter the method of payment you will be using (e.g. credit card, cash) and any extra details you may need to share with us.
* **Shipping** (`shipping`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, please let us know if you need shipping arrangements made or if you will be handling it yourself. If you need shipping, please provide more details about your requirements.
