# Meeting Scheduling Form With Instructor - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect meeting scheduling information from users. Please use this form to provide details about upcoming meetings, allowing the instructor to prepare and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required to complete it accurately.
2.  Select "Yes" or "No" for "Meeting Details" to indicate whether a meeting is scheduled.
3.  Enter the "Instructor Name" in the text field provided.
4.  Choose your preferred "Meeting Slots" from the available options. You can select multiple slots if needed.
5.  Provide the "Meeting Date" in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6.  Enter the "Meeting Time" in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
7.  If required, enter "Meeting Detailed Info" to provide any additional details about the meeting.
8.  Finally, include any "Additional Info" relevant to the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This is the header section of the form where you will start your meeting scheduling information.
* **Meeting Details**: Choose "Yes" if a meeting is scheduled and "No" otherwise.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor for the meeting.
* **Meeting Slots**: Select one or more preferred meeting slots.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Detailed Info**: Provide any additional information about the meeting that is relevant.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any extra details or information that is not covered in the above fields.

## Tips
* Always check the form carefully to ensure you have provided accurate information.
* Review the "Meeting Details" field to ensure you have selected the correct option.
* Use the "Meeting Slots" field to select all relevant slots for the meeting.
* Enter the "Meeting Date" and "Meeting Time" accurately to avoid scheduling conflicts.
* Use the "Meeting Detailed Info" field to provide any important details about the meeting.
* In the "Additional Info" field, include any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.
