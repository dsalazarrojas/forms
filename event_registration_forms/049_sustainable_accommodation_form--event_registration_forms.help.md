# Sustainable Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Accommodation Form is designed to gather information about accommodations available for booking. It is used by accommodation owners or managers to input details about their properties and provide a platform for potential customers to book or find more information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information about your accommodation.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all the fields are correctly filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`1`, text, optional): This field is a general text input field for users to input any relevant information or notes about their booking.
* **Accommodation Name** (`2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your accommodation.
* **Sustainable Practices** (`3`, select multiple, optional): Check yes or no to indicate if your accommodation has sustainable practices such as solar panels or a recycling program.
* **Accommodation Address** (`4`, text, optional): Enter the address of your accommodation.
* **Start Date** (`5`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your booking period.
* **End Date** (`6`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your booking period.
* **Start Time** (`7`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your booking period.
* **End Time** (`8`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your booking period.
* **Accommodation Description** (`9`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your accommodation.
* **Capacity** (`10`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of people that can stay at your accommodation.
* **Price Range** (`11`, text, optional): Enter the price range of your accommodation.
* **Booking Window** (`12`, date, optional): Enter the date by which you need to book your accommodation.
* **User Email** (`13`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **User Phone** (`14`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`15`, select one, optional): Check yes or no to indicate if you accept online payments or not.
* **Payment Details** (`16`, text, optional): Provide more information about your payment method if needed.
* **Booking Terms** (`17`, text, optional): Enter your booking terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`18`, text, optional): Enter your cancellation policy.
* **Refund Policy** (`19`, text, optional): Enter your refund policy.
* **Accommodation URL** (`20`, text, optional): Enter a URL for more information about your accommodation.
* **Accommodation Phone** (`21`, text, optional): Enter your accommodation phone number.
* **Accommodation Email** (`22`, email, optional): Enter your accommodation email address.
* **Accommodation Longitude** (`23`, text, optional): Enter the longitude of your accommodation location.
* **Accommodation Latitude** (`24`, text, optional): Enter the latitude of your accommodation location.

## Tips
- Double check that all the required fields are filled out accurately.
- Ensure that you have permission to fill out the form for your accommodation.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.
