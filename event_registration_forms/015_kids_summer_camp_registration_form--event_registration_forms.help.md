# Kids Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from parents or guardians for their child to attend a summer camp. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and successful experience for your child at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and carefully, taking care to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field to ensure you understand what information is required and what is optional.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the camp staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camp Registration Form** (`camp_registration_form`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide your child's registration information.
	+ This field is not required, but it's a good idea to fill it out if you want to include some details about your child's registration.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): 
	+ This field is for providing contact information about yourself or the parent/guardian.
	+ Please fill in your contact details so we can reach you for any updates or concerns.
* **Child's Background** (`child_background`, text, optional): 
	+ This field is for providing information about your child's background, including any relevant medical history or allergies.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible to ensure we can provide the best care for your child.

## Tips

* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the camp staff.
