# Faculty Strategy Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to register for the Faculty Strategy Workshop. It's a simple way to collect your preferences for the workshop and share any additional information that might be helpful for the organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the session(s) you'd like to attend.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you with important updates.
3. If you'd like, include your phone number for added contact options.
4. Share any additional notes or details you think might be helpful for us to know.
5. Confirm your participation consent (agree to attend the workshop).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, not required): This is an optional field where you can write any additional details or comments about the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the session(s) you'd like to attend from the list.
	+ Select all the sessions you're interested in, or leave this field blank for now. You can always add more sessions later.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted about the workshop, provide your email address. This will help us reach out to you with updates and important information.
* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, select_one, not required): Confirm that you'll attend the workshop by selecting 'True'. If you have any conflicts or need to cancel, let us know ASAP.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about any workshop updates or changes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted via phone as well, add your phone number here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Share any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about your workshop participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all the relevant sessions you're interested in attending.
* Double-check your email and phone numbers for accuracy.
* If you have any conflicts or need to cancel, respond to the email or phone call we'll send to you.
