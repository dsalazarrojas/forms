# Craft Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Workshop Waiver Form is designed to collect participant information and obtain their waiver signature for the workshop. It helps ensure that participants are aware of the workshop rules and guidelines and provides a safe and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Fill in your participant information on **page 1: Participant Information**.
* Provide your contact information on **page 2: Contact Information**.
* Read and agree to the **Waiver** on **page 3: Waiver**. This is a crucial part of the form, so make sure to read it carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (page 1)**: This is where you provide basic information about yourself, such as your name, email, and phone number. Please fill in your real name, email, and phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**: This section is for providing your address and any other relevant contact details. Please fill in your address and any other contact info that you'd like us to have in case of emergency or workshop updates.
* **Waiver (page 3)**: This is the most important part of the form. Please read the waiver carefully and check the box if you agree to its terms. It's a contract between you and the workshop organizers, and it's essential to ensure you understand and accept the rules and guidelines of the workshop.
