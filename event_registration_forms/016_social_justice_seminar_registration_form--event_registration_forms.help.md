# Social Justice Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Justice Seminar Registration Form is designed to gather details from registrants to prepare for the event and ensure a smooth experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your home or work Address.
4. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
5. Enter your Date of birth for identity verification.
6. Choose the date you plan to attend the seminar.
7. Select the Time of the event you plan to attend.
8. Enter the location where the seminar will take place.
9. If necessary, mention any Dietary restrictions.
10. If required, specify any Accessibility needs.
11. If you have any special requests, please mention them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (Text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Address** (Text, required/optional): Enter your home or work address.
* **Phone number** (Text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of birth** (Date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for identity verification.
* **Event date** (Date, required/optional): Choose the date you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Time of event** (Time, required/optional): Select the time of the event you plan to attend.
* **Event location** (Text, required/optional): Enter the location where the seminar will take place.
* **Dietary restrictions** (Text, required/optional): If necessary, mention any dietary restrictions.
* **Accessibility needs** (Text, required/optional): If required, specify any accessibility needs.
* **Special requests** (Text, required/optional): If you have any special requests, mention them here.
