# Team Collaboration Skills Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees who will be participating in the Team Collaboration Skills Seminar. It will help the event organizers to efficiently track attendee details and manage the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Choose the desired seminar type from the list.
3. Provide details about your team size and members.
4. Select the team leader from the list.
5. Confirm the seminar fees and payment term.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (id: 1, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of why you are registering for the seminar.
* **Name** (id: 2, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (id: 3, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (id: 4, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Seminar Date** (id: 5, `date`, required: false): Please select the date you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Time** (id: 6, `time`, required: false): Please select the time you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Seminar Type** (id: 7, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please choose the type of seminar you are interested in attending.
	+ Team Collaboration Seminar
	+ Leadership Seminar
	+ Communication Skills Seminar
	+ Time Management Seminar
	+ Project Management Seminar
* **Team Size** (id: 8, `number`, required: false): Please enter the number of team members who will be attending the seminar.
* **Team Members** (id: 9, `text`, required: false): Please provide a list of team members who will be attending the seminar.
* **Team Leads** (id: 10, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the team leader from the list.
	+ John Doe
	+ Jane Doe
	+ Jim Doe
	+ Joe Doe
* **Seminar Fees** (id: 11, `number`, required: false): Please confirm the seminar fees you will pay.
* **Payment Term** (id: 12, `text`, required: false): Please provide the payment term (e.g. "Pay on receipt of invoice" or "Pay online").
* **Seminar Fees Paid** (id: 13, `number`, required: false): Please confirm if you have paid the seminar fees.
