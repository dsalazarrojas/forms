# Pool Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Party Registration Form collects essential information from individuals attending a pool party event. This data is vital for event management purposes, including accurate headcount, attendee validation, age and contact information validation, and participant preferences identification.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Pool Party Registration Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name in the first field.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the third field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
5.  Enter your Date of Birth in the fifth field in the format (mm/dd/yyyy).
6.  Enter your Time of Arrival in the sixth field in the format (hh:mm).
7.  The Age field is automatically calculated from your Date of Birth.
8.  In the "Select One" field, choose one option from the dropdown menu.
9.  In the "Select Multiple" field, choose one or more options from the dropdown menu.
10.  In the "Note" field, enter any optional notes about your attendance.
11.  In the "Event Type" field, choose the type of event from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Time of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your arrival in the format (hh:mm).
*   **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): This field is calculated automatically from the Date of Birth.
*   **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
*   **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more options from the dropdown menu.
*   **Note** (`note`, `text`, optional): Enter any optional notes about your attendance.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required): Choose the type of event from the dropdown menu.
