# HR Process Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Process Innovation Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information for attendees interested in participating in the HR Process Innovation Workshop. This includes session preferences, attendee details, and workshop requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and understand what information is required for each.
2. For each field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below to ensure you accurately provide the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Preferences** (Select One):
	+ This field allows you to choose your session preferences for the workshop. Please select 'Yes' if you would like to attend a session that has specific requirements or interests.
	+ **Type**: Select One (Single choice)
	+ **Required**: Optional
* **Attendee Details** (Text Input):
	+ This field is for providing your details as an attendee. Please enter your name and any relevant contact information.
	+ **Type**: Text Input
	+ **Required**: No
* **Session Requirements** (Text Input):
	+ This field is for providing any additional session requirements you may have, such as specific needs or concerns.
	+ **Type**: Text Input
	+ **Required**: No
* **Workshop Details** (Text Input):
	+ This field is for providing any additional details about your interests or goals related to the workshop.
	+ **Type**: Text Input
	+ **Required**: No

## Tips
- Ensure you accurately provide your session preferences and attendee details to ensure a smooth registration process.
- Review the workshop details to ensure you understand the session requirements and are able to provide accurate information.
- If you are unsure about any field or section, please reach out to the workshop organizers for clarification.
