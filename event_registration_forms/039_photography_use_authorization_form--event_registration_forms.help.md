# Photography Use Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Use Authorization Form is used to clarify the terms of use and any restrictions or permissions granted to the photographer for specific events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields. Fields marked as required must be filled out to continue.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`photographer_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization you are working with.
* **Client Contact** (`client_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the client.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, select_multiple, required): Select the permissions you wish to grant or restrict to the client. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about the event or usage.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
