# Cane Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cane Giveaway Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking a cane for mobility assistance. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand your needs and provide you with the correct cane for your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully, making sure to fill out all required fields.
2. For select-one questions, choose one option that best describes your situation.
3. If you need help with any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Participant's Name)** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): This is the participant's given name.
* **Last Name (Participant's Last Name)** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): This is the participant's family name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): We will use this to confirm your registration and keep you updated on your cane delivery.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): This is your contact number for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Please provide your birth date for proper cane sizing.
* **Street Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): This is your home address for home delivery.
* **City** (`participant_city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`participant_state`, text, required): The state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`participant_zip`, text, required): The postal code of your city or town.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Please select the age category you belong to for proper cane sizing.
* **Do You Currently Use a Mobility Aid?** (`mobility_aid_current`, select_one, required): If you are already using a mobility aid, please select the type of aid you are using.
* **Type of Cane Needed** (`cane_type_needed`, select_one, required): Please select the type of cane you think you need for your mobility assistance.
* **Cane Color Preference** (`cane_color_preference`, select_one, optional): Please select your preferred cane color, if you have a specific color preference.
* **Your Height Range** (`height_range`, select_one, required): Please select your height range for proper cane sizing.
* **Dominant Hand** (`dominant_hand`, select_one, required): Please select the hand you use the cane with.
* **Reason for Needing a Cane** (`medical_need`, select_one, required): Please select the reason why you need a cane for your mobility assistance.
* **Medical Need Details** (`medical_need_details`, text, optional): If you have specific medical needs or requirements, please provide additional information in this field.
* **Is This Physician Recommended?** (`physician_recommendation`, select_one, optional): If you have a doctor's recommendation, please select 'True'.
* **Do You Have Insurance Coverage?** (`insurance_coverage`, select_one, optional): Please select your insurance status.
* **Financial Need Level** (`financial_need`, select_one, required): Please select your financial need level for our consideration.
* **How Did You Hear About This Giveaway?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Please select how you heard about this cane giveaway.
* **Will You Attend the Distribution Event?** (`event_attendance`, select_one, required): Please select whether you can attend the distribution event.
* **Do You Need Delivery?** (`delivery_needed`, select_one, optional): If you require home delivery, please select 'Yes - home delivery'.
* **Accessibility Needs at Event** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): If you have specific accessibility needs, please select all that apply.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please provide an emergency contact person's name and number.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please provide the emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes - I agree' to accept our terms and conditions.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): This is the date of your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions carefully and honestly.
* If you require assistance with your cane, please let us know and we will be happy to help.
* Our goal is to provide you with the correct cane for your needs, so please help us by providing accurate information.
