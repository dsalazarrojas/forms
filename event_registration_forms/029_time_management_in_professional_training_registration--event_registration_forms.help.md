# Time Management In Professional Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for registrations to our professional training sessions on time management topics. Please fill out this form accurately and only once per participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are registering for the correct time management topic.
2. Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the training session you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name or the name of the participant registering.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address or the email address of the participant registering.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the participant registering.
* **Time Management Topic** (`time_management_topic`, select_one, required: false): Select the time management topic you want to register for (Time Management or Time Management with Excel).
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false): Select the duration of the training session you are registering for (format: HH:MM or HH:MM:SS).
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required: false): Enter the number of participants attending the training session.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are registering as an Individual or as part of a Group.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are registering for the training session.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Select the payment method you plan to use (Cash, Check, or Card).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required: false): If you have any additional information to provide, enter it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to register only once per participant.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
