# Customer Service Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Training Attendance Form is a tool used to record and track attendance at customer service training sessions. This form helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured and stored accurately, making it easier to evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the session you are recording attendance for.
2. Choose the start time of the session.
3. Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
4. Indicate whether or not each attendee was present at the session. You can select multiple attendees if necessary.
5. Optionally, provide any comments or feedback about the session in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the session took place.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the session began.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select multiple, optional): Check whether or not each attendee was present at the session. You can select multiple attendees by checking all relevant boxes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have about the session.
