# industrial_data_cloud_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial data cloud program registration form is designed to collect information about participants, their contact details, project locations, and manager details for tracking and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of participant (Company or Employee) in the "Participant" field.
2. Provide your contact details, including your name in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Enter the location of the project in the "Location" field.
4. Enter your project title and description in the "Project" field.
5. Enter your manager's details, including their name and title in the "Manager" field.
6. Enter your manager's email address and phone number in the "Manager Email" and "Manager Phone" fields, respectively.
7. Enter any additional notes about your project in the "Notes" field.
8. Review and submit your form.
