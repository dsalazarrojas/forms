# Betting Pool Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees for a betting pool event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your registration is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of several sections, each containing one or more fields. Please complete each field as instructed:
*   Make sure to enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   Enter a valid email address that can be used for contact purposes.
*   If you are outside the local region, make sure to include the country code in your phone number.
*   Select the date that best applies to your situation.
*   Choose the option that best describes your role at the event (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
*   If applicable, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
*   Enter any additional relevant information about your registration.
*   Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the event.
*   If necessary, select any of the options provided for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your legal full name.
*   **Email address** (`email`, `email`, `true`): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, `false`): Include the country code if outside the local region.
*   **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, `true`): Select the relevant date for your registration.
*   **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose the option that best describes your role at the event.
*   **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, `false`): Provide a whole number if applicable.
*   **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional relevant information.
*   **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
*   **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, `false`): Enter the estimated value in decimal format if applicable.
*   **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, `false`): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
*   **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, `false`): Enter any final comments or notes.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
*   The event organizers will use the information you provide to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.
