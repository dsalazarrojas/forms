# Corporate Optimization Planning Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help attendees register for the Corporate Optimization Planning Session. It gathers information about the attendee's name, contact details, and event preferences.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the fields with the required information.
2. Start by providing your name and contact details on **Page 1: Attendee Information**.
3. On **Page 2: Registration Information**, submit your registration details, such as any special requests or accommodations you may need.
4. Finally, on **Page 3: Event Information**, provide your event preferences, such as your preferred session date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Information**:
  - **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on our records.
  - **Contact Information** (`contactInformation`, text, required): Please provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you with further instructions or updates.
  - **Company/Organization** (`company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company or organization, please provide the name of your organization.

- **Registration Information**:
  - **Session Preferences** (`sessionPreferences`, text, required): Tell us about your interests and expectations from the event.
  - **Special Requests** (`specialRequests`, text, optional): If there are any special requirements or requests you may have, please let us know.

- **Event Information**:
  - **Event Date** (`eventDate`, text, required): Please select the date you would like to attend the event.
  - **Event Time** (`eventTime`, text, required): Please select the time you would like to attend the event.
  - **Session Time Zone** (`sessionTimeZone`, text, required): Please select the time zone you prefer for the event.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly and accurately to ensure your registration is processed smoothly. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
