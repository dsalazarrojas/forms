# Art Exhibition Visitor Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Visitor Information Form is a crucial tool for the art exhibition team to gather essential information from visitors. This form ensures that visitors provide accurate and complete information, allowing for effective organization and communication during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), which include:
	* Visitor name
	* Visit date
	* Contact email
2. If visiting as a group, enter the number of people in your group in the "Group size" field.
3. If you have any special needs or requirements, such as accessibility needs, provide them in the "Special needs" field.
4. Select whether you would like a guided tour using the dropdown options.
5. If you have additional requests or requirements, such as extra arrangements, you can provide them in the "Additional requests" field.
6. If you have a contact phone number, provide it for further communication.
7. Review and submit your form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor name (text, required)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Organization or school (text, optional)**: If you are visiting with a group, enter the name of your organization or school.
* **Group size (number, required)**: Enter the total number of people in your group.
* **Visit date (date, required)**: Enter the scheduled visit date.
* **Arrival time (time, optional)**: Enter your expected arrival time.
* **Tour requested (select_one, required)**: Choose whether you would like a guided tour.
* **Special needs (text, optional)**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, provide them here.
* **Contact email (email, required)**: Enter a valid email address for confirmations and updates.
* **Contact phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for additional communication.
* **Additional requests (text, optional)**: If you need any extra arrangements, provide them here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to enable efficient communication and planning.
* If you have any special needs or requests, provide them in the respective fields for the exhibition team to cater to your requirements.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the exhibition team for assistance.
