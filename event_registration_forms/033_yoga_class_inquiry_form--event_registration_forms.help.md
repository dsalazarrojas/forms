# yoga_class_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential students about their interests and preferences for a yoga class. It allows you to register interest in a class and provide your contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name in the "student name" field.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted in the "contact method" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email address" field if you have one.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field if you have one.
5. Check the box next to the days you are interested in attending class in the "preferred days" field.
6. Select the time of day you are available to attend class in the "available time" field.
7. Choose the instructor you prefer to work with (Male, Female, or Both) in the "instructor preference" field.
8. Enter any additional message or comments in the "message" field if you would like.
9. Enter the name of the class you are interested in in the "class name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student name`, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Method** (`contact method`, required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text, or Online).
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): If you have an email address, please enter it.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Class Interest** (`class_interest`, required: false): Check the boxes next to the classes you are interested in (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, required: false): Check the boxes next to the days you are available to attend class (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, required: false): Select the time of day you are available to attend class (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Instructor Preference** (`instructor_preference`, required: false): Choose the instructor you prefer to work with (Male, Female, Both).
* **Message** (`message`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, required: false): If you know the name of the class you are interested in, please enter it.
