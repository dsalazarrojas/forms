# Public Relations Event Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting approval for public relations events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event. For example, "Marketing Conference" or "Company Gala".
* **Location (2)** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event, such as "New York City" or "Chicago".
* **Event Date (3)** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time (4)** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM or HH:MM.
* **Event Status (5)** (select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the event, either **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Event Details (6)** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the event. For example, "This is a company conference."
* **Contact Name (7)** (text, required/optional): Enter your name.
