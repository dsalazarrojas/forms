# Match Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participant attendance for events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to include all required fields.
2. Provide accurate information for each field.
3. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Team Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the team you are representing.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Attendance Status (Select One, Required)**: Choose your attendance status:
	+ Confirmed: You will be attending the event.
	+ Absent: You will not be able to attend the event.
	+ Late: You will be late to the event.
* **Comments (Note, Optional)**: Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
