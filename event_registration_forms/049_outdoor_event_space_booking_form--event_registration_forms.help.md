# Outdoor Event Space Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your outdoor event and allow you to book the available space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the following details:
	* Event space: Enter the name of the event space where your event will take place.
	* Event Date: Enter the date of your event. Use the date picker to choose the correct date.
	* Event Start Time: Enter the start time of your event. Use the time picker to choose the correct time.
	* Event End Time: Enter the end time of your event. Use the time picker to choose the correct time.
	* Event Venue: Enter the name of the venue where your event will be held.
	* Event Capacity: Enter the number of attendees expected.
	* Contact Name: Enter your name and title (if applicable).
	* Contact Email: Enter your email address for future correspondence.
2.  Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Space** (Event Space): This field is for selecting the name of the outdoor event space where your event will be held.
* **Event Date** (Event Date): This field is for selecting the date of your event. Please use the date picker to choose the correct date.
* **Event Start Time** (Event Start Time): This field is for selecting the start time of your event. Use the time picker to choose the correct time.
* **Event End Time** (Event End Time): This field is for selecting the end time of your event. Use the time picker to choose the correct time.
* **Event Venue** (Event Venue): This field is for specifying the name of the event venue where your event will be held.
* **Event Capacity** (Event Capacity): This field is for entering the expected number of attendees for your event.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name): This field is for specifying your name and title (if applicable) for communication purposes.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email): This field is for entering your email address for future correspondence.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information required to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
