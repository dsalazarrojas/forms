# win_your_dream_week_contest_entry - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect entry information for the "Win Your Dream Week" contest. It's meant to be filled out by individuals who wish to participate in the contest, and it will help the contest organizers to get to know their contestants better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the main page with your general information.
2. Select your country and state of residence.
3. Choose your zip code.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Add a message (if you have any comments or questions).
6. Confirm your email address.
7. Confirm your phone number.
8. Choose whether you consent to participate in the contest.
9. Confirm your consent.
10. Upload a file (if required).
11. Confirm the file you've uploaded.
12. Confirm the file size.
13. Confirm the file type.
14. Confirm the file you've uploaded.
15. Confirm the uploaded file name.
16. Confirm the uploaded file type.
