# Authorization And Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a required document for all participants to confirm their understanding of the event, their role, and their commitment to the sponsoring organization. It's a crucial step in the event registration process to ensure a smooth and successful experience for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing each section carefully and provide accurate information about your participation.
2. Fill out the form in a single session, as duplicate or incomplete forms may cause issues with your involvement.
3. Ensure that you answer all required questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date**: <name>: form_date (Date of this authorization) - Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Participant Full Name**: <name>: participant_name (Your legal name) - Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address**: <name>: participant_email (Your email contact) - Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: <name>: participant_phone (Your contact phone) - Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event or Program Name**: <name>: event_name (Name of the event you are committing to) - Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Dates**: <name>: event_dates (When is the event) - Enter the dates of the event.
* **Sponsoring Organization**: <name>: organization_name (Organization running the event) - Enter the name of the organization running the event.
* **Your Role or Position**: <name>: participant_role (What is your role in this event) - Select your role in the event from the provided options.
* **Duration of Commitment**: <name>: commitment_duration (How long will you be involved) - Select the duration of your commitment from the provided options.
* **Hours Per Week Commitment**: <name>: time_commitment_hours (Approximate hours per week) - Enter the approximate hours you will be involved per week.
* **Do you Understand Your Responsibilities**: <name>: responsibilities_listed (Do you understand what is expected) - Select your level of understanding of your responsibilities.
* **Specific Duties or Responsibilities**: <name>: specific_duties (List what you will be doing) - Enter a list of specific duties or responsibilities you will be involved with.
* **Start Date of Commitment**: <name>: start_date (When will you start) - Enter the start date of your commitment.
* **End Date of Commitment**: <name>: end_date (When will you finish) - Enter the end date of your commitment. (Note: This field is optional and only required for commitments with an end date.)
* **Attendance Expectation Understood**: <name>: attendance_expectation (Do you understand attendance requirements) - Select your level of understanding of attendance expectations.
* **Expected Attendance Percentage**: <name>: attendance_percentage (Required attendance percentage) - Select the expected attendance percentage from the provided options.
* **Cancellation and Absence Protocol**: <name>: absence_protocol (How to request time off) - Enter your protocol for requesting time off (this field is optional and only required if you are not 100% committed to perfect attendance).
* **Training or Orientation Required**: <name>: training_required (Will you need training) - Select if you require training or orientation for your role.
* **Conduct and Behavior Expectations**: <name>: conduct_expectations (Do you agree to code of conduct) - Select your level of agreement to the code of conduct.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: <name>: confidentiality_agreement (Do you commit to confidentiality) - Select if you commit to maintaining confidentiality.
* **Conflict Resolution Process**: <name>: conflict_resolution (Will you follow organization conflict process) - Select if you agree to follow the conflict resolution process.
* **Safety and Risk Acknowledgment**: <name>: safety_commitment (Understand potential risks) - Select if you understand potential risks and safety measures.
* **Termination Procedures**: <name>: early_termination (How can you end the commitment) - Enter the procedures for terminating your commitment (this field is optional and only required if you are not 100% committed to your role).
* **Legal Authorization**: <name>: legal_authorization (Are you authorized to make this commitment) - Select if you are authorized to make this commitment.

## Tips

* Please double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the event organizers or sponsors before submitting the form.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
