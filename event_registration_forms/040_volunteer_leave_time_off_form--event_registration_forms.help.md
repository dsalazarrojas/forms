# Volunteer Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request leave time off for volunteer work. Please fill out this form to submit your leave request, providing necessary details such as the date and reason for your leave. This form is typically used by volunteers who need to take a break from their volunteer work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Provide a brief explanation of your leave request in the "reason_for_leave" field.
3. Choose the "status" field to indicate the current status of your leave request.
4. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Leave Time Off Form** (`1`): This is the title of the form.
* **Volunteer Leavers** (`2`): Please enter the volunteer's name(s) that are requesting leave.
* **Date of Leave** (`3`): Enter the date you would like to start your leave. This should be in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Time of Leave** (`4`): Enter the time you would like to start your leave. This should be in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Reason for Leave** (`5`): Please provide a brief explanation of your leave request. This will help the team understand why you need to take time off.
* **Start Date** (`6`): Enter the date you would like to start your leave. This should be in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **End Date** (`7`): Enter the date you would like to end your leave. This should be in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
* **Leave Duration** (`8`): Enter the number of days you would like to take off.
* **Status** (`9`): Please choose the current status of your leave request:
	+ Pending: Your leave request is waiting for approval.
	+ Approved: Your leave request has been approved.
	+ Denied: Your leave request has been denied.
* **Notes** (`10`): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request.
* **Volunteer Name** (`11`): Please enter the name of the volunteer requesting leave.
* **Email** (`12`): Enter the email address of the volunteer requesting leave.
* **Phone** (`13`): Enter the phone number of the volunteer requesting leave.
* **Assigned User** (`14`): Please select the person who will be handling your leave request. Options include:
	+ Jim
	+ Sarah

This form is designed to be completed by volunteers who need to request time off for their work. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please feel free to contact the assigned user.
