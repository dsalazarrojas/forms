# Event Attendance Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Attendance Sign In 1** (`event_attendance_sign_in_1`, text, optional): This field is a text field for any additional information about your attendance.
- **Attendee Information** (`event_attendance_sign_in_2`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about you as an attendee.
- **Attendee Name** (`event_attendance_sign_in_3`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which is likely used for identification and event management.
- **Attendee Email** (`event_attendance_sign_in_4`, email, optional): This field is for your email address, which is often used for event updates and communications.
- **Event Attendance Sign In 5** (`event_attendance_sign_in_5`, select_one, optional): This field is a dropdown menu with three options. Select one that best describes your preference or situation.
- **Attendee Phone** (`event_attendance_sign_in_6`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number, which might be used for event communication or contact purposes.
- **Event Attendance Sign In 7** (`event_attendance_sign_in_7`, time, optional): This field is for a specific time, likely related to your event schedule or preference.
- **Event Attendance Sign In 8** (`event_attendance_sign_in_8`, select_multiple, optional): This field is a multiple-choice dropdown menu. Select all that apply from the options provided.
- **Event Attendance Sign In 9** (`event_attendance_sign_in_9`, date, optional): This field is for a specific date, likely related to your event schedule or preference.
- **Event Attendance Sign In 10** (`event_attendance_sign_in_10`, note, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments about your attendance or event experience.

Please note that this form is not required to be completed in its entirety. Only fill out the fields that are relevant to your situation and needs.
