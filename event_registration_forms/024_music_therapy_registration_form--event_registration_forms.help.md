# Music Therapy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Therapy Registration Form is used to collect information from registrants for music therapy sessions. Completing this form will help the music therapy team to provide an efficient and effective music therapy experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your registrant information, including your name and any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number (if available).
3. Specify your session preferences, including the start date and time, duration, and music therapy type.
4. Choose the frequency and level of music therapy you are interested in.
5. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about your music therapy experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registrant Info
* **Registrant Info**: Enter your name and any relevant details about your music therapy needs.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your email and phone number (if available) to contact you for any updates or further information.
* **Session Details**: Specify the start date and time, duration, and music therapy type for your session.

### Session Details
* **Date**: Select the date for your music therapy session. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time for your music therapy session.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of your music therapy session in minutes.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about your music therapy session.

### Music Therapy Options
* **Music Therapy Type**: Choose the type of music therapy you are interested in (Yes/No).
* **Music Therapy Frequency**: Select the frequency of music therapy you are interested in (Yes/No).
* **Music Therapy Level**: Choose the level of music therapy you are interested in (High/Low).
* **Music Therapy Status**: Select the status of your music therapy (Active/Inactive).

**Tips**
