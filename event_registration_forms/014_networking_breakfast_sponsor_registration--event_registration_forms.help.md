# Networking Breakfast Sponsor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register sponsors for the Networking Breakfast event. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth event execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose your package choice by selecting the desired options from the list.
2. Enter your sponsor details in the text field.
3. Provide the name and contact information of the person representing the sponsor.
4. Enter the sponsor's email address.
5. Provide the sponsor's phone number.
6. Confirm if the sponsor will be attending the event.
7. Confirm if the email address provided is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Package Choice** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Choose from the options provided to select the sponsor's package preference.
* **Sponsor Details** (`text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the sponsor's company or organization.
* **Contact Person** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person representing the sponsor.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Confirm Sponsor** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Confirm if the sponsor will be attending the event.
* **Confirm Email** (`Yes`, `No`, required: false): Confirm if the email address provided is correct.
