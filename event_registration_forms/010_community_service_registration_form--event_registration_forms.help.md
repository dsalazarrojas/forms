# Community Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in community service programs. The form will be used to confirm their registration and ensure they can join the activities they've chosen.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name in the "Registrant full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Registrant email" field. This will be used for confirmations and updates.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Registrant phone number" field. This is optional.
4. Choose the program or activity you wish to join from the "Program or activity" dropdown list.
5. Provide the name and phone number of a contact person for emergency situations.
6. Choose whether you consent to the use of media, such as photos or video, for promotion.
7. If applicable, describe any accessibility needs you have.
8. If necessary, provide any special instructions for the event coordinators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant full name**: This field is for your full name.
* **Registrant email**: This field is for your email address, which will be used for confirmations and updates.
* **Registrant phone number**: This is an optional field for your phone number, which can be useful for coordinators.
* **Program or activity**: Choose the program or activity that interests you. Select one from the dropdown list.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Provide the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to use media**: Choose 'True' if you agree to the use of photos or video for promotion.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you need any accommodations, describe them here.
* **Special instructions for coordinators**: If you have any important notes for the event staff, provide them here.
