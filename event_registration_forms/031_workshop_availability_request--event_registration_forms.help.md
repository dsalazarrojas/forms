# Workshop Availability Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture your availability for a workshop or event. Please provide your details below to schedule the workshop efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Specify your preferred date for the workshop in the "Preferred Date" field.
5. Select the size of your group in the "Group Size" field.
6. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details**: A field to enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
  - **Email**: A field to enter your email address. Please ensure this is correct to ensure smooth communication.
  - **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number. This can be a landline or mobile number.
  - **Preferred Date**: A field to specify your preferred date for the workshop. Select a date that works for you.
  - **Group Size**: A field to select the size of your group. Enter a number that represents the number of attendees.
  - **Comments**: A field to add any additional comments or information about your requirements.

## Tips
- Ensure your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
- Be specific about your preferred date to help us schedule the workshop efficiently.
- If you have any special requirements, please mention them in the "Comments" field.
