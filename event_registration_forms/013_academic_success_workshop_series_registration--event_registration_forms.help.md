# Academic Success Workshop Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Academic Success Workshop Series Registration" form is designed to collect information from students who wish to register for workshops aimed at promoting academic success. This form is used to gather relevant details about the registrant, such as name, email, ID number, year of study, preferred workshop format, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your Full Name as per your university records.
2.  Enter your University Email Address.
3.  Enter your unique Student ID Number.
4.  Select your current Year of Study (First Year, Second Year, Third Year, etc.).
5.  Choose the workshops you wish to attend from the options provided.
6.  Select your preferred workshop format (In-person, Online or Zoom, or Hybrid).
7.  Confirm that you intend to attend all selected workshops (Yes - Confirmed, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name (`student_name`):** Enter your full name as per your university records.
*   **University Email Address (`student_email`):** Enter your official .edu email address. This is a required field.
*   **Student ID Number (`student_id`):** Enter your unique campus ID number. This is a required field.
*   **Year of Study (`academic_year`):** Select your current year of study (First Year, Second Year, Third Year, etc.). This is a required field.
*   **Select the workshops you wish to attend (`workshops_selected`):** You may choose multiple sessions.
*   **Preferred workshop format (`preferred_format`):** Choose your preferred mode of learning (In-person, Online or Zoom, or Hybrid). This is a required field.
*   **What do you hope to achieve by attending these workshops? (`specific_goals`):** Provide your personal learning objectives.
*   **Do you require any special accommodations? (`special_accommodations`):** Enter your needs if any, such as ASL, screen readers, etc.
*   **Dietary restrictions (`dietary_restrictions`):** Select any dietary restrictions you may have for sessions including snacks or meals.
*   **Attendance commitment (`attendance_commitment`):** Confirm your intention to attend all selected sessions.

Note: Ensure to review each field carefully and fill them in accordingly to ensure accurate and valid data for program evaluation and planning purposes.
