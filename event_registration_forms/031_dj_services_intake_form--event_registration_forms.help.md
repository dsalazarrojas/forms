# DJ Services Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients who are interested in hiring a DJ for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the designated field.
2. Provide the **Event Date** for the event you are interested in.
3. If applicable, enter the **Event Time** for the event.
4. Enter your **Client Email** address so we can get in touch with you.
5. Enter your **Client Phone** number, if you have one.
6. In the **Event Details** field, describe your event in as much detail as possible.
7. Enter the **DJ Name** you are interested in hiring.
8. Share your **DJ Experience** with us, including any relevant experience with the DJ.
9. Enter the **DJ Rate** you are willing to pay for the services.
10. Select any **Event Services** you require (e.g., lighting, sound system, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the client.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Describe your event in as much detail as possible.
* **DJ Name** (`dj_name`, text, required): Enter the DJ's name you are interested in hiring.
* **DJ Experience** (`dj_experience`, text, required): Share your experience with the DJ, including any relevant experience.
* **DJ Rate** (`dj_rate`, text, required): Enter the rate you are willing to pay for the services.
* **Event Services** (`event_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any services you require from the options provided (e.g., lighting, sound system, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed accurately.
* If you have any specific requirements or questions, you can include them in the **Event Details** field.
* We will review your submission and be in touch with you to discuss the details further.
