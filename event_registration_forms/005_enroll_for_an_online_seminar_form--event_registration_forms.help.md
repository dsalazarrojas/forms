# enroll_for_an_online_seminar_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register an individual for an online seminar. It requires personal details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, date of birth, and time of birth. The form also includes a confirmation of email and phone number fields to ensure accuracy and prevent errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your last name.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your date of birth.
6. Choose your time of birth.
7. Verify your email address by re-entering it in the "Confirm email" field.
8. Confirm your phone number by re-entering it in the "Confirm phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: A field to input your first name. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Email**: A field to input your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Last name**: A field to input your last name. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Phone**: A field to input your phone number. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Date of birth**: A field to select your date of birth. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Time of birth**: A field to select your time of birth. Please ensure it is accurate.
* **Confirm email**: A field to re-enter your email address for verification purposes. Please ensure it matches the email address entered in the "Email" field.
* **Confirm phone**: A field to re-enter your phone number for verification purposes. Please ensure it matches the phone number entered in the "Phone" field.
