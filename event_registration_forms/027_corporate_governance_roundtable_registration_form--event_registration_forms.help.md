# Corporate Governance Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Governance Roundtable Registration Form is a tool used to collect participant information for the Corporate Governance Roundtable. The form is designed to gather details about the participant's title, company, job function, and registration information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information available before starting the form.
2. Fill in your participant name, title, and company name in the respective fields.
3. Select your job function from the options provided.
4. Choose your category of participation (e.g., Board of Directors, CEO/CFO, etc.).
5. Select your company size.
6. Enter your email address and phone number.
7. Choose your registration type (in-person or virtual).
8. Set your registration date and time.
9. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Select the person you would like to be assigned to from the options provided.
11. If applicable, list your participant interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (1)
* Participant Name (Text): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
### Title (2)
* Title (Text): Enter your official title or job position.
### Company Name (3)
* Company Name (Text): Enter the name of your company as it appears on official documents.
### Job Title (4)
* Job Title (Text): Enter your job title or position within the company.
### Email (5)
* Email (Email): Enter your valid email address that can be used for communication.
### Phone (6)
* Phone (Text): Enter your valid phone number that can be used for communication.
### Category (7)
* Category (Select One): Choose your category of participation from the options provided.
### Company Size (8)
* Company Size (Select Multiple): Choose the size of your company from the options provided.
### Job Function (9)
* Job Function (Text): Enter your job function or department within the company.
### Company Type (10)
* Company Type (Text): Enter the type of your company (e.g., publicly traded, privately held, etc.).
### Company Sector (11)
* Company Sector (Text): Enter the sector your company operates in (e.g., finance, technology, healthcare, etc.).
### Registration Type (12)
* Registration Type (Select One): Choose your registration type (in-person or virtual).
### Registration Date (13)
* Registration Date (Date): Enter the date you want to be registered.
### Registration Time (14)
* Registration Time (Time): Enter the time you want to be registered.
### Note (15)
* Note (Note): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
### Assigned To (16)
* Assigned To (Select Multiple): Choose the person you would like to be assigned to from the options provided.
### Participant Interests (17)
* Participant Interests (Text): List any participant interests or areas of expertise.
