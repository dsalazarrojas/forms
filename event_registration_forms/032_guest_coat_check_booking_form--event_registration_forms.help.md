# Guest Coat Check Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Coat Check Booking Form is used to gather information from guests who need to check their coats during an event, ensuring a smooth and efficient coat-checking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the event registration option that applies to you.
2. Fill in your guest information, including your name and contact details.
3. Provide coat information, such as the type of coat you have.
4. Confirm your email address if you have provided it.
5. Select the submission option that suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information (event_reg_info)**: Select the event registration option that applies to you. This is used to determine which event you are registering for.
* **Guest Information (guest_info)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact details. This will help us keep you informed about any coat-related updates or notifications.
* **Coat Information (coat_info)**: Provide details about the coat you are checking, including its type. This helps us identify and keep track of your coat.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive coat-related updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any urgent coat-related matters.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address you provided is correct. This is used to ensure accurate communication with you.
* **Submit**: Select the submission option that suits your needs. This is used to finalize your coat check booking.
* **Submit 1, Submit 2, Submit 3**: You can select one or more submission options, depending on your needs. This is used to confirm your coat check booking.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any issues or concerns during the coat check process, please contact us for assistance.
* Please be prepared to provide additional information if required by our team.
* We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in ensuring a smooth coat-checking experience for everyone.
