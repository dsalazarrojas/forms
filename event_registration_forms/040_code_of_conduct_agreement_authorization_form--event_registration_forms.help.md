# Code Of Conduct Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Code Of Conduct Agreement Authorization Form is a vital document that outlines the expectations and responsibilities of event participants. By completing this form, you will be affirming your commitment to treating all participants with respect and dignity, adhering to safety and security guidelines, and upholding the code of conduct principles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully, ensuring you understand each principle and section.
2. Answer each question truthfully, selecting the correct response for each field.
3. Sign the declaration, certifying that you have read and agreed to the code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please specify it here (if applicable).
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select_one, required): Select your role as an attendee, volunteer, staff, speaker, or other.
* **Principle 1: I will treat all participants with respect and dignity** (`principle_1`, select_one, required): You must agree to treat all participants with respect and dignity.
* **Principle 2: I will not engage in harassment or discrimination** (`principle_2`, select_one, required): You must agree not to engage in harassment or discrimination.
* **Principle 3: I will follow all safety and security guidelines** (`principle_3`, select_one, required): You must agree to follow all safety and security guidelines.
* **Principle 4: I will not use substances that impair judgment** (`principle_4`, select_one, required): You must agree not to use substances that impair your judgment.
* **Principle 5: I will respect intellectual property and confidentiality** (`principle_5`, select_one, required): You must agree to respect intellectual property and confidentiality.
* **Understand Prohibited Behaviors** (`understand_prohibited`, select_one, required): You must understand what behaviors are prohibited, including illegal activities, violence, harassment, and theft.
* **Understanding of Consequences** (`understand_consequences`, select_one, required): You must understand that violations of the code of conduct may result in removal or legal action.
* **I Agree to Report Incidents** (`will_report_incidents`, select_one, required): You must agree to report any conduct violations to the organizers.
* **Confidentiality Protection** (`confidentiality_protection`, select_one, required): You must understand that reports will be treated confidentially.
* **Declaration and Authorization** (`signature_declaration`, select_one, required): You must declare that you have read and agreed to the code of conduct.
* **Truthfulness** (`truthfulness`, select_one, required): You must certify that all information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, required): If you are under 18, you must have parental consent to participate.
* **Date of Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you sign this declaration.

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer each question truthfully, as your responses will be used to ensure a safe and respectful environment for all participants.
* If you are unsure about any of the principles or sections, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
* By completing this form, you are committing to uphold the code of conduct and respect all participants.
