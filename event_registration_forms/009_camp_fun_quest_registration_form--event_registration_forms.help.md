# Camp Fun Quest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Fun Quest Registration Form is used to collect vital information from campers and their parents or guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable camp experience. Please complete this form to provide the necessary details for our staff to prepare and plan activities tailored to your camper's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Camper and Parent/Guardian Information**: Please provide the basic information of the camper and their parent or guardian, including name, date of birth, address, and contact details.
2. **Health and Emergency Contact**: Provide any necessary health information, emergency contact names, and relationships.
3. **Session Selection**: Choose the camp session that best suits your camper's schedule.
4. **Transportation and Before/After Care**: Indicate if transportation is required and whether before and after camp care is needed.
5. **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer.
6. **T-Shirt Size**: Choose the size of the camp t-shirt for your camper.
7. **Photo Release**: Decide if you grant permission for photos of your camper to be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper First Name**: Enter your camper's first name.
* **Camper Last Name**: Enter your camper's last name.
* **Camper Nickname**: Enter your camper's nickname, if any (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your camper's date of birth in the required date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Age at Start of Camp**: Enter your camper's age in years.
* **Gender**: Select your camper's gender.
* **School Grade**: Select your camper's current school grade.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your camper's school (optional).
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Home Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city or town where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Relationship**: Describe your relationship to your emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Name** (optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact 2.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone** (optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact 2.
* **Who Is Authorized to Pick Up Your Camper**: List all authorized individuals to pick up your camper.
* **Medical Conditions or Health Concerns**: List any medical conditions or health concerns your camper may have.
* **Allergies**: List any food, insect, or medication allergies your camper may have.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications your camper is taking with dosages.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of your camper's primary physician.
* **Physician Phone**: Enter the phone number of your camper's primary physician.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your camper's insurance provider (optional).
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your camper's insurance policy number (optional).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select all dietary restrictions that apply to your camper.
* **Camp Session Selection**: Choose the camp session that suits your camper's schedule.
* **Transportation Needed**: Indicate if transportation is required and specify the frequency.
* **Before Camp Care Needed**: Indicate if before camp care is needed and specify the frequency.
* **After Camp Care Needed**: Indicate if after camp care is needed and specify the frequency.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you prefer.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose the size of the camp t-shirt for your camper.
* **Photo Release Permission**: Grant permission for photos of your camper to be used.
* **How Did You Hear About Camp Fun Quest**: Select all sources you heard about the camp.
