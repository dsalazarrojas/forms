# Community Theater Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your commitment to participating in our community theater activities. Please fill out this form to provide us with your personal and contact information, availability, and consent to participate in our productions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. If you are under 18, provide the name and phone number of your parent or guardian.
4. Select all the types of participation you are interested in (acting, behind the scenes, etc.).
5. Choose your availability for participating in rehearsals and performances.
6. Acknowledge your understanding and commitment to participating in rehearsals for 8-12 weeks.
7. Confirm your availability to attend all scheduled performances.
8. Read and agree to our liability and safety policies.
9. Provide any medical conditions or special skills you have that might impact your participation.
10. Select your level of theater experience.
11. If applicable, mention any special skills you have.
12. Sign the electronic signature field with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address** (2): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (if under 18)** (5): If you are under 18, provide the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone (if under 18)** (6): If you are under 18, provide the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (7): The full name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (8): The phone number of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **What type of participation are you consenting to?** (9): Select all the types of participation you are interested in (e.g., acting, behind the scenes, etc.).
* **Which production(s) are you interested in?** (10): Select the productions you are interested in participating in.
* **What is your general availability?** (11): Choose when you are available to participate (weekdays, weekends, etc.).
* **I understand rehearsals typically require 8-12 weeks commitment** (12): Acknowledge your understanding of the time commitment required for participating in rehearsals.
* **I understand I must be available for all scheduled performances** (13): Confirm your availability to attend all scheduled performances.
* **Liability and Safety** (14): Read and agree to our liability and safety policies.
* **I understand that theater activities involve some physical risk** (15): Acknowledge that you understand the physical risks involved in participating in theater activities.
* **I agree to follow all safety rules and instructions** (16): Agree to follow all safety protocols during rehearsals and performances.
* **I agree to immediately report any injuries or unsafe conditions** (17): Agree to report any injuries or unsafe conditions.
* **Media and Publicity** (18): This section explains what we will use your image and likeness for.
* **I consent to being photographed during rehearsals and performances** (19): Consent to being photographed during rehearsals and performances.
* **I consent to being video recorded during rehearsals and performances** (20): Consent to being video recorded during rehearsals and performances.
* **I consent to my name being used in programs and publicity materials** (21): Consent to your name being used in programs and publicity materials.
* **Code of Conduct** (22): This section explains our behavioral expectations.
* **I agree to treat all participants with respect and professionalism** (23): Agree to treat all participants with respect and professionalism.
* **I agree to refrain from any form of harassment or discrimination** (24): Agree to refrain from any form of harassment or discrimination.
* **I agree to maintain good attendance at rehearsals** (25): Agree to maintain good attendance at rehearsals.
* **Are there any medical conditions we should know about?** (26): If applicable, provide any medical conditions or special skills you have that might impact your participation.
* **What is your theater experience level?** (27): Select your level of theater experience (no experience, some experience, etc.).
* **Do you have any special skills?** (28): If applicable, mention any special skills you have.
* **Date of Consent** (29): This field will be auto-filled with today's date.
* **Electronic Signature** (30): Type your full name to confirm your consent.
