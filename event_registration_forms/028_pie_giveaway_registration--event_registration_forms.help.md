# Pie Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pie Giveaway Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a pie giveaway event. To complete this form, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with your relevant information.
2. Review and confirm your eligibility status.
3. Enter any additional details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Name** (`entry_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Eligibility** (`eligibility`, select_multiple, required: false): Check whether you are eligible for the giveaway by selecting "True" if you are, or "False" if you are not.
* **Field 3** (`field_3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information you would like to provide, if necessary.
* **Field 4** (`field_4`, text, required: false): Enter any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about your eligibility status, contact the event organizer.
* You can enter any additional information in Field 3 and Field 4 if you feel it is relevant to your participation.
