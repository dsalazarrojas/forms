# Smart Material Tracking Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Smart Material Tracking Workshop. It is used to collect information about attendees, including their personal details and workshop-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your Contact Phone number, if applicable.
4. Enter your Company, Department, and Job Title, if applicable.
5. Select the correct Workshop Date and Time from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the Workshop Location.
7. Choose how you will be attending the workshop (In-person or Online).
8. If applicable, enter your Emergency Contact Name, Relationship, Phone number, and Email address.
9. Indicate if you will Attend or Did Not Attend the workshop.
10. Optionally, provide Additional Comments about your attendance.
11. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions of the workshop.
12. Confirm your agreement with the Privacy Pledge.
13. Finally, submit your registration.
