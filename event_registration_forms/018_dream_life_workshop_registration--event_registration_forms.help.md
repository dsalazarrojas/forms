# Dream Life Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Dream Life Workshop Registration form is a tool for users to express their interest in attending the Dream Life Workshop. By filling out this form, users will be able to provide their contact details and workshop preferences, which will help us better understand their needs and provide relevant information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
3. Make sure to select the correct workshop preferences that align with your interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of who you are and what makes you tick.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, text, optional):
	+ A short description of the workshop you are interested in, including any specific topics or themes you are looking for.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, text, optional):
	+ Any additional comments or details about your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact details for accuracy.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please let us know in the "Registration Information" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the workshop, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
