# Athlete Participation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athlete Participation Leave Form is a form used for athletes to request absence from training or competition. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your athlete name and team name in the respective fields.
2. Select the category of leave from the dropdown menu. This will help the administrators understand the reason for your absence.
3. Provide the start and end dates of your absence.
4. Enter the total number of training sessions and games or competitions missed.
5. If applicable, provide details about the event and location.
6. Indicate if this absence is school-related and if school authorities have been notified.
7. Check if your primary coach has been notified.
8. Add any additional comments or details if necessary.
9. Sign the form with your electronic signature.
10. Finally, select the date of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Team or Squad Name** (`athlete_team`, Text, Required): Enter your team or squad name as it is recognized in our system.
* **Category of Leave** (`leave_category`, Select One, Required): Choose the reason for your absence from the options provided.
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the first day of your absence.
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the last day of your absence.
* **Total Number of Training Sessions Missed** (`training_sessions_missed`, Number, Required): Enter the total number of training sessions you will miss.
* **Total Number of Games or Competitions Missed** (`games_missed`, Number, Required): Enter the total number of games or competitions you will miss.
* **Event Name and Description** (`event_description`, Text, Required): Enter the event name and description as relevant.
* **Location of Event** (`event_location`, Text, Required): Enter the city and country of the event.
* **Is this a school-related absence?** (`school_absence`, Select One, Required): Check if your absence is related to school.
* **Have school authorities been notified?** (`school_notification`, Select One, Required): Indicate if school authorities have been notified.
* **Has your primary coach been notified?** (`coach_notification`, Select One, Required): Check if your primary coach has been notified.
* **Additional Comments or Details** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your absence.
* **Athlete or Guardian Electronic Signature** (`athlete_signature`, Text, Required): Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Date of Request** (`request_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are requesting this leave.
