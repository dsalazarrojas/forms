# School Talent Quest Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the School Talent Quest Tournament, where students can showcase their talents to a panel of judges. Fill out this form to register for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, as all fields are required for registration.
2. Enter your student name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose your grade level from the dropdown menu.
4. Select your performance type from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the type of talent you will be showcasing.
5. Provide a brief description of your performance in the performance details field.
6. Enter your school name and contact information (email and phone number) in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your school records.
* **Student Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Performance Type**: Choose the type of talent you will be showcasing (e.g., Ballet, Dance, Music, Theater, or Other).
* **Performance Details**: Briefly describe your performance, including any relevant information about your talent.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school as it appears on your school records.
* **School Email**: Enter a valid email address of your school that can be used to contact your school.
* **School Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for your school.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tournament organizers.
* Double-check your email address and school contact information for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a teacher or counselor for help.
