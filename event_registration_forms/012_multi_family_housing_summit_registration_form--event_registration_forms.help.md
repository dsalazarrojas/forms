# Multi Family Housing Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to the Multi Family Housing Summit. It collects necessary information to ensure a smooth and efficient experience for all participants. Please fill in the required fields accurately to ensure a successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if available).
4. If you are representing a sponsor, please fill in their name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Name**: This field is where you will be listed as a registered attendee. Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear in the summit materials and networking lists.
* **Email**: This field collects your official email address for contact and communication purposes. Please use the email address associated with your work or organization.
* **Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number. If you don't have a phone, you can leave this field blank.
* **Sponsor Name (if applicable)**: If you are representing a sponsor, please fill in their name for proper recognition and credit during the summit.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (though none are currently marked as required).
* If you have any issues or concerns during the registration process, feel free to reach out for support.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
