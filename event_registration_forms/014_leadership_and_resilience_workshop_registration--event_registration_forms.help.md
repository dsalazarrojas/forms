# Leadership And Resilience Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Leadership and Resilience Workshop. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to register for the workshop.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to register for the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive communication about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for any workshop updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to verify your eligibility for the workshop.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, optional): Select the time you expect to complete the workshop.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, select_one, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Participant Interest** (`participant_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the interests that align with your goals and objectives for attending the workshop.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Confirm your submission and completion of the form.
