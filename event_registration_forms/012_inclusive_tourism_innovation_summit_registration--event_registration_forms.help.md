# Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from potential attendees of the Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit. The summit aims to promote inclusive tourism practices and bring together experts and industry professionals to share knowledge and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the form carefully.
2. For each page, you will be presented with fields that ask for specific information.
3. Fill in the required information for each field.
4. Make sure to review the terms and conditions before submitting your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Registration Form
* **Event Registration Form** (`Event Registration Form`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending the event.
* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of yourself and your interests in attending the event.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`Accessibility Needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the accessibility requirements that apply to you, such as wheelchair accessible accommodation or sign language interpretation.
* **Session Interests** (`Session Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the topics you are interested in attending during the summit.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`Dietary Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending sessions.
* **Registration Form Completion** (`Registration Form Completion`, `text`, required): Fill in the details of your registration, such as name and contact information.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`Accessibility Requirements`, `text`, required): Specify any additional accessibility requirements you may have.
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, `text`, required): If you have any special requests, please specify them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`Emergency Relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have a relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have a relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, `email`, required): Provide the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, required): Provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`Emergency Contact Notes`, `text`, required): Provide any additional notes for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Agency** (`Emergency Contact Agency`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have an emergency contact agency.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`Registration Confirmation`, `note`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the summit.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `note`, required): Confirm that you have provided accurate contact information.
* **Submit To** (`Submit To`, `text`, required): Specify where you would like to submit your registration.
* **Registration Status** (`Registration Status`, `text`, required): Confirm your registration status.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required): Provide any additional notes or comments.
