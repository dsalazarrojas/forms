# Senior Manager Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from senior managers attending a networking event. It includes fields for basic contact details, job role, preferences, and event attendance details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. Please use the standard email format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
6. Select your job role from the options provided in the "Role" field.
7. Choose your attending date from the options provided in the "Attending Date" field. This is the date you will be attending the event.
8. Select the start time from the options provided in the "Start Time" field. This is the time you will arrive at the event.
9. Finally, select any applicable preferences in the "Preferences" field. This is where you can specify if you have any special requirements or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., [name]@domain.com).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Role**: Select your job role from the options provided:
	+ Senior Manager
	+ Director
	+ Team Lead
	+ Other
* **Preferences**: Select any applicable preferences:
	+ Senior Manager
	+ Director
	+ Team Lead
	+ Other
* **Attending Date**: Select the date you will be attending the event from the options provided.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time you will arrive at the event from the options provided.
* **Preferences**: This field allows you to select any specific preferences or interests related to the event.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review your form submission before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.

Note: The field "Other" in the "Role" and "Preferences" fields allows for additional options that are not explicitly listed, but might still be relevant in certain contexts.
