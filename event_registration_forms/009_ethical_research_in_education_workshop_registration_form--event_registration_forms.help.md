# Ethical Research In Education Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the registration of attendees for the Ethical Research In Education Workshop. It gathers details about the workshop, the attendee, and the contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required and optional fields as per your interest.
2. Enter the details of the workshop you are interested in.
3. Select the contact information and preferences accordingly.
4. Select the date, time, and location of the workshop you are attending.
5. Enter the attendee details such as their name, position, institution, department, phone number, and email address.
6. Add any additional notes about the attendee and the workshop.
7. Select the payment method and status of the workshop.
8. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details**:
  - **Workshop Details** (`Workshop Details`, `text`, optional)
    Enter a brief description of the workshop you are interested in. This field is useful for adding context about the workshop.

* **Contact Information**:
  - **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional)
    Enter your contact information, which will be used for further communication purposes with the workshop organizers.

* **Preferences**:
  - **Preferences** (`Preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending the workshop, "No" if you are not, and "Other" if you have other preferences.

* **Consent**:
  - **Consent** (`Consent`, `note`, optional)
    This field is not applicable for further explanation.

* **Email**:
  - **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional)
    Enter your email address, which will be used for further communication purposes with the workshop organizers.

* **Phone**:
  - **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional)
    Enter your phone number, which will be used for further communication purposes with the workshop organizers.

* **Date**:
  - **Date** (`Date`, `date`, optional)
    Select the date of the workshop you are interested in attending.

* **Time**:
  - **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional)
    Select the time of the workshop you are interested in attending.

* **Attendee Details**:
  - **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, `text`, optional)
    Enter any additional details about the attendee, such as any specific accommodations they may require.

* **Attendee Name**:
  - **Name** (`Attendee Name`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the name of the attendee, who will be attending the workshop.

* **Attendee Position**:
  - **Position** (`Attendee Position`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the position of the attendee at their institution.

* **Attendee Institution**:
  - **Institution** (`Attendee Institution`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the institution the attendee is from.

* **Attendee Department**:
  - **Department** (`Attendee Department`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the department the attendee is from.

* **Attendee Phone**:
  - **Attendee Phone** (`Attendee Phone`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the phone number of the attendee.

* **Attendee Email**:
  - **Email** (`Attendee Email`, `email`, optional)
    Enter the email address of the attendee.

* **Attendee Notes**:
  - **Notes** (`Attendee Notes`, `note`, optional)
    Add any additional notes about the attendee.

* **Workshop Notes**:
  - **Workshop Notes** (`Workshop Notes`, `note`, optional)
    Add any additional notes about the workshop.

* **Start Date**:
  - **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, optional)
    Select the start date of the workshop.

* **End Date**:
  - **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, optional)
    Select the end date of the workshop.

* **Workshop Time**:
  - **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, `time`, optional)
    Select the time of the workshop.

* **Location**:
  - **Location** (`Location`, `text`, optional)
    Enter the location of the workshop.

* **Description**:
  - **Description** (`Description`, `text`, optional)
    Enter a description of the workshop.

* **Attendee Attendance**:
  - **Attendee Attendance** (`Attendee Attendance`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Select whether the attendee will be attending the workshop.

* **Payment Method**:
  - **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Select the payment method for the workshop.

* **Payment Status**:
  - **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_multiple`, optional)
    Select the payment status for the workshop.

## Tips
1. Be accurate in filling out the form.
2. Check for completeness before submitting the form.
3. Be clear about the workshop and attendee details.
4. Review the form before submission.
5. Be considerate when adding notes about the attendee and the workshop.
