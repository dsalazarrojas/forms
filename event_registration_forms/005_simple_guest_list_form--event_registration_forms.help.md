# Simple Guest List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Guest List Form is a tool to help event organizers keep track of guests attending an event. It collects essential information such as name, contact details, and event-specific information to ensure a smooth and organized guest experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information for each guest.
2. Ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) are completed.
3. Review the form to ensure all fields are filled in correctly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Form**:
This is the main section of the form where you will enter the event-specific information. Enter the name of the event you are registering your guest for.
* **Guest Name** (Required): Enter the full name of the guest.
* **Contact Number** (Required): Enter the primary contact number for the guest. This could be their phone number or other contact method.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Arrival Time** (Required): Enter the expected time of arrival for the guest.
* **Departure Time** (Required, if applicable): Enter the expected time of departure for the guest.
* **Date of Event** (Required): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Name** (Required): Enter the name of the event the guest is attending.

Tips:
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field to ensure a smooth and organized event experience.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's best to consult with the event organizer or relevant authority.
