# Tournament Participation Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end users about their participation preferences for a tournament. It will help event organizers or administrators understand the preferences of participants, making it easier to organize and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the "Tournament Preferences" field. This field is compulsory and should be filled in accurately.
2. If you have a team participating in the tournament, provide the team details in the "Team Details" field.
3. If there's any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers, you can provide it in the "Additional Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Tournament Preferences (Required)**
*   This field is for stating your preferences for the tournament. Please provide a short description of what you're looking for in the tournament.
*   Make sure to fill this field accurately as it is compulsory.

### **Team Details (Optional)**
*   If you're participating in the tournament as a team, provide the name and details of your team here.
*   Team details can include team name, captain's name, and any other relevant information.

### **Additional Details (Optional)**
*   If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers, please provide it here.
*   You can use this field to ask specific questions or provide any other relevant details.
*   Please keep in mind that this field is not compulsory and can be left blank if not necessary.

## Tips
*   Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure that your preferences are considered correctly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
*   Make sure to review your submission before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
