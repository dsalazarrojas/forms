# Innovation Award Nominee Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to recommend an innovation award nominee. It is designed to gather information about the nominee, including their name, email, phone number, and title. The form also asks for a brief recommendation text, which should describe why the nominee is worthy of the innovation award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and your name, email, phone number, and title.
2. Select the nominee's title (individual, team, or organization).
3. Enter the nominee's name, email, and phone number.
4. Write a brief description of the nominee's innovation.
5. Select your own title and department (optional).
6. Select your own location (optional).
7. Enter a brief description of why you recommend the nominee for the innovation award.
8. Fill in your own name, email, and phone number.
9. Select your own title (optional).
10. Enter your own department (optional).
11. Enter your own location (optional).
12. Enter a brief description of why you are submitting this nomination.
13. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or team being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the nominee.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the nominee.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the nominee (if not already specified).
* **Recommendation Text** (`recommendation_text`, text, required): Provide a brief description of why the nominee is worthy of the innovation award.
* **Your Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your own name.
* **Your Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Your Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Your Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter your own title.
* **Your Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter your own department.
* **Your Location** (`submitter_location`, text, optional): Enter your own location.
* **Your Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your own job title.
* **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Select Nominee** (`select_one_nominator`, select_one, required): Select whether the nomination is for a single individual or multiple individuals.
* **Select Nominees** (`select_multiple_nominators`, select_multiple, optional): Select the nominees (if multiple individuals).
* **Date of Nomination** (`date_of_nomination`, date, required): Enter the date the nomination was submitted.
* **Nomination Type** (`nomination_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the nomination is for an individual, team, or organization.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit the form.
