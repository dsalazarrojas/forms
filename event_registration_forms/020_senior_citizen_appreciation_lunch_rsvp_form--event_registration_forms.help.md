# senior_citizen_appreciation_lunch_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Senior Citizen Appreciation Lunch event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)** (`name`, text, required)
    Enter your full name, as it appears on your official ID or driver's license.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required)
    Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates about the event.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional)
    Enter your phone number (optional) to provide an additional contact method, in case we need to reach you about the event.
