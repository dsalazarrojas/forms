# Sports Competition Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about sports competitions, including participant and event details. It's used to ensure accurate participant registration and event organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **League Name** in the corresponding field. This is the league or category the participant is participating in.
2. Enter the **School Name** where you are representing.
3. Enter the **Competition Name**, which is the specific competition you are participating in.
4. Enter your **Participant Name**, which is your name.
5. Enter your **Participant Email**, which will be used for communication purposes.
6. Enter your **Participant Phone** number, for contact purposes.
7. Enter your **Participant Date of Birth**, in the format DD/MM/YY.
8. Select your **Participant Gender** from the options (Male, Female, Other).
9. Enter your **Participant Age**.
10. Enter your **Participant Address**, which includes your street address.
11. Enter your **Participant Postal Code**, which is the postal code of your address.
12. Enter your **Participant City**, which is the city of your address.
13. Enter your **Participant Country**, which is the country of your address.
14. Enter your **Participant Province**, which is the province of your address.
15. Enter your **Participant Licence**, which is your sports licence number.
16. Enter your **Participant ID**, which is your participant ID.
17. Enter the **Event Name**, which is the name of the event.
18. Enter the **Event Date**, in the format DD/MM/YY.
19. Enter the **Event Time**, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
20. Enter the **Event Location**, which is the venue of the event.
21. Enter the **Event City**, which is the city of the event location.
22. Enter the **Event Province**, which is the province of the event location.
23. Enter the **Event Country**, which is the country of the event location.
24. Select all **Participant Registrations**, which are the events you want to participate in.
25. (Optional) Add any **Note** you'd like to add to your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **League Name (1)**: The name of the league or category you are participating in.
* **School Name (2)**: The name of the school you are representing.
* **Competition Name (3)**: The name of the specific competition you are participating in.
* **Participant Name (4)**: Your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email (5)**: A valid email address that will be used for communication.
* **Participant Phone (6)**: A valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Date of Birth (7)**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Participant Gender (8)**: Select your gender from the options (Male, Female, Other).
* **Participant Age (9)**: Enter your age.
* **Participant Address (10)**: Enter your street address.
* **Participant Postal Code (11)**: Enter your postal code.
* **Participant City (12)**: Enter the city of your address.
* **Participant Country (13)**: Enter the country of your address.
* **Participant Province (14)**: Enter the province of your address.
* **Participant Licence (15)**: Enter your sports licence number.
* **Participant ID (16)**: Enter your participant ID.
* **Event Name (17)**: The name of the event.
* **Event Date (18)**: Enter the date of the event in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Event Time (19)**: Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Location (20)**: Enter the venue of the event.
* **Event City (21)**: Enter the city of the event location.
* **Event Province (22)**: Enter the province of the event location.
* **Event Country (23)**: Enter the country of the event location.
* **Participant Registrations (24)**: Select all events you want to participate in.
* **Note (25)**: Add any additional notes about your participation.
