# Pride Event Participation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to record their participation in the Pride event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Employee ID number.
2. Provide a reason for your leave, if applicable.
3. Specify the dates of your absence.
4. Mention the duration of your leave.
5. Choose your participation status (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
6. If necessary, add manager comments.
7. Enter your name.
8. Provide your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Select the person you want your leave to be assigned to (either chatjimmy or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (1, number, required: false):
    Your employee ID number is required to identify you as a participant.
* **Reason For Leave** (2, text, required: false):
    If you are taking leave for a specific reason, please provide it here.
* **Dates Of Absence** (3, date, required: false):
    Specify the exact dates when you will be absent from work.
* **Duration Of Leave** (4, number, required: false):
    Mention how many days you will be absent.
* **Status** (5, select_one, required: false):
    Choose whether your leave is pending, approved, or denied.
* **Manager Comments** (6, note, required: false):
    If your manager needs to provide any comments, please add them here.
* **Employee Name** (7, text, required: false):
    Enter your full name so the organizers know who is participating.
* **Email** (8, email, required: false):
    Provide your email address so the organizers can contact you.
* **Phone** (9, text, required: false):
    Enter your phone number so the organizers can contact you.
* **Assigned To** (10, select_one, required: false):
    Select either chatjimmy or Other to indicate who you want to be assigned to.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organizers.
