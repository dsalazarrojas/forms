# Professional Development Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to register for a professional development workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of workshop you are interested in.
2. Choose the location of the workshop.
3. Select the facilitator for the workshop.
4. Select the professional development areas you are interested in.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select the date and time of the event.
8. Enter any additional notes if necessary.
9. Enter your name.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter your workplace.
13. Enter your department.
14. Enter your job title.
15. Enter your position.
16. Enter your workshop experience.
17. Enter your education.
18. Select your level of English proficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Type** (Select One): Select the type of workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Location** (Select Multiple): Choose the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (Select One): Select the facilitator for the workshop.
* **Professional Development Areas** (Select Multiple): Select the professional development areas you are interested in.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (Date): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time): Select the time of the event.
* **Registration Date** (Date): Select the date you registered for the workshop.
* **Registration Time** (Time): Select the time you registered for the workshop.
* **Registration Facilitator** (Select One): Select the facilitator you registered with.
* **Participant Details** (Text): Enter any additional notes if necessary.
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your name.
* **Participant E-mail** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Workplace** (Text): Enter your workplace.
* **Participant Department** (Text): Enter your department.
* **Participant Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Position** (Text): Enter your position.
* **Participant Workshop Experience** (Text): Enter your workshop experience.
* **Participant Education** (Text): Enter your education.
* **Participant English Level** (Select One): Select your level of English proficiency.
* **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes if necessary.
