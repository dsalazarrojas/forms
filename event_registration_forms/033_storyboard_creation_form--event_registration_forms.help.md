# Storyboard Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about event attendees, speakers, and organizers to help improve the event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page as directed.
2. Answer all required questions (marked as "true" in the form).
3. Use the provided options for select questions.
4. Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This page is a placeholder page and does not contain any questions.
* **Page 2: What's your name?** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
* **Page 3: What's your email?** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with event updates.
* **Page 4: When is the event?** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Page 5: What's your role?** (`select_one`, `Attendee`, `Speaker`, `Organizer`, required: true): Choose how you are involved in the event.
* **Page 6: Are you attending?** (`select_one`, `Yes, I am attending`, `No, I am not attending`, required: true): Confirm your attendance.
* **Page 7: Why are you attending?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share why you are attending the event.
* **Page 8: What do you hope to get out of this event?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share your expectations for the event.
* **Page 9: Do you agree?** (`select_one`, `I agree`, `I do not agree`, required: true): Confirm your agreement to the event terms.
* **Page 10: Additional comments?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the event.
* **Page 11: Do you need help?** (`select_one`, `Yes, I need help`, `No, I do not need help`, required: true): Confirm if you need assistance.
* **Page 12: How can we help you?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): If you need help, describe how we can assist you.
* **Page 13: Do you have any other comments?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share any other comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Page 14: Are there any questions you'd like to ask?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Ask any questions you may have about the event.
* **Page 15: Have you already registered for this event?** (`select_one`, `Yes, I have already registered`, `No, I have not registered`, required: true): Confirm your registration status.
* **Page 16: Do you have a special request?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share any special requests or needs you may have.
* **Page 17: Is there anything else?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share any other thoughts or comments about the event.
* **Page 18: Do you have any further comments?** (`text`, `text`, required: false): Share any further comments or feedback about the event.
* **Page 19: Is this the end?** (`checkbox`, `Option A`, `Option B`, required: false): Choose how you'd like to conclude the survey.

This help guide is meant to be a helpful resource for users to understand the purpose and use of the Storyboard Creation Form.
