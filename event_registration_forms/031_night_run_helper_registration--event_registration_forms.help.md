# Night Run Helper Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information about individuals who are interested in volunteering at our events. Please complete this form to register as a helper for our upcoming event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked as *optional*.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Ensure you've provided accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, *optional*): This field is used to record the name of the event you're registering for.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, *optional*): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, *optional*): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, *optional*): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, *optional*): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, *optional*): Enter your date of birth for record purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, *optional*): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, *optional*): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Select whether the emergency contact person is an Emergency Contact or Not.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, *optional*): If you selected "Not Emergency Contact", please provide additional information about your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Shift** (`shift`, required): Select your preferred shift (Morning, Afternoon, All).
* **Shift Time** (`shift_time`, *optional*): If you select "All", please provide the time you are available to work.
* **Availability** (`availability`, *optional*): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, required): Select the volunteer position you're interested in (Event Staff, Medical Support, Volunteer).
* **Additional Volunteer Position** (`additional_volunteer_position`, *optional*): If you're interested in additional volunteer positions, select "Yes".
* **Medical Certification** (`medical_certification`, required): Select your medical certification level (Basic First Aid, Advanced First Aid, Both).
* **Experience Hours** (`experience_hours`, *optional*): If applicable, enter your experience hours.
* **Experience Date** (`experience_date`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the date of your experience.
* **Experience Time** (`experience_time`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the time of your experience.
* **Medical Certification Details** (`medical_certification_details`, *optional*): Enter details about your medical certification.
* **First Aid Course Hours** (`first_aid_course_hours`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the hours of your first aid course.
* **First Aid Course Date** (`first_aid_course_date`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the date of your first aid course.
* **First Aid Course Time** (`first_aid_course_time`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the time of your first aid course.
* **First Aid Certification Date** (`first_aid_certification_date`, *optional*): If applicable, enter the date of your first aid certification.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, *optional*): Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.

Please review your submission before submitting it for accuracy and completeness. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
