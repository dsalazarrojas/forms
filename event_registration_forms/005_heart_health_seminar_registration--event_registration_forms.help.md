# Heart Health Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heart Health Seminar Registration form is used to collect information from attendees for the "Heart Health Seminar". This includes event title, event category, attendee name, email address, phone number, date of birth, event date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Heart Health Seminar Title** in the text field.
2. Select the **Event Category** from the list.
3. Provide your **Attendee name**.
4. Enter your **Email Address** in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
5. Enter your **Phone Number**.
6. Fill in your **Date of Birth** in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
7. Enter the **Event Date** in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Select your **Consent** by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Choose your **Preferred Language** from the list.
10. Enter any **Additional Comments** you have about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Heart Health Seminar Title
* **event_title** (text, required/optional): A brief description of the seminar.

### Event Category
* **event_category** (select multiple, true/): Select one or more categories to which this event belongs (e.g., Clinic, Nonprofit, Wellness Program).

### Attendee Name
* **attendee_name** (text, required/optional): Your name as it appears on your ID or government-issued document.

### Email Address
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about the seminar.

### Phone Number
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Your phone number where we can reach you.

### Date of Birth
* **date_of_birth** (date, required/optional): Your date of birth in the standard format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Event Date
* **event_date** (date, required/optional): The date of the event.

### Consent
* **consent** (select one, true/): Please give your consent for us to contact you about the seminar.

### Preferred Language
* **preference** (select one, required/): Choose your preferred language for the seminar (e.g., English, Spanish, French).

### Additional Comments
* **additional_comments** (text, required/optional): Any additional comments or details about the seminar you'd like to share.
