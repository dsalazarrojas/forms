# Attendance Management Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Attendance Management Access Form is designed to collect attendance information from employees for various events, ensuring that employees have the necessary access level and permissions to attend.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee information, including your name, ID, department, position, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Enter the event name, date, time, and location.
3. Select the access level you require for the event (General Access, VIP Access, Speaker/Presenter, Staff/Volunteer, Admin, or Other).
4. Indicate if you need a badge or ID for the event.
5. If you need parking, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
6. If you plan to bring guests, select 'Yes', and fill in the number of guests.
7. If you have any dietary restrictions, select the appropriate options from the list.
8. If you require any special accommodations, fill in the relevant information in the 'Accessibility Needs' field.
9. Confirm your attendance by selecting 'Yes, Confirmed', 'No', or 'Maybe - Uncertain'.
10. Finally, ensure that your manager has approved your attendance by selecting 'Approved', 'Pending Approval', or 'Not Approved'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Select your department from the list.
* **Position** (Position/Title, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your corporate email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your business phone number (if applicable).
* **Direct Manager Name** (Direct Manager Name, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required): Enter the name of the event or activity.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (Event Location, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Access Level Requested** (Access Level Requested, select_one, required): Select the access level you require for the event (General Access, VIP Access, Speaker/Presenter, Staff/Volunteer, Admin, or Other).
* **Badge ID Needed** (Badge ID Needed, select_one, required): Indicate if you need a badge or ID for the event.
* **Parking Required** (Parking Required, select_one, required): If you need parking, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Bringing Guests/Family** (Bringing Guests/Family, select_one, required): If you plan to bring guests, select 'Yes', and enter the number of guests.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Nut Allergy, or Other).
* **Accessibility Needs** (Accessibility Needs, text, optional): Fill in any accessibility needs you require.
* **Will You Attend This Event?** (Will You Attend This Event?, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance by selecting 'Yes, Confirmed', 'No', or 'Maybe - Uncertain'.
* **Manager Approval/Authorization** (Manager Approval/Authorization, select_one, required): Ensure your manager has approved your attendance by selecting 'Approved', 'Pending Approval', or 'Not Approved'.
