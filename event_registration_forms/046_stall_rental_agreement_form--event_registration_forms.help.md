# Stall Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stall Rental Agreement Form is used to collect rental details from event organizers or vendors who are renting out their stalls at an event. This form helps ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of the rental terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the stall rental details, including the rental term and rate.
* Provide event information, including any relevant details about the event.
* Specify payment terms, including payment status and date.
* Include any necessary vendor information and signature.
* Include any event organizer information and signature.
* Specify rental duration and agreement status.
* Specify cancellation reason and cancellation notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stall Details**: Provide a description of the stall, including any necessary details about the location and availability.
* **Rental Term**: Select the rental term that applies to the stall (e.g. Month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months).
* **Rental Rate**: Enter the rental rate for the selected term.
* **Event Information**: Provide any necessary details about the event, including date, time, and location.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify payment status (e.g. Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid) and payment date.
* **Vendor Information**: Provide any necessary vendor information, including name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Vendor Signature**: Sign the form as the vendor to indicate agreement to the rental terms.
* **Event Organizer Information**: Provide any necessary event organizer information, including name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Event Organizer Signature**: Sign the form as the event organizer to indicate agreement to the rental terms.
* **Rental Duration**: Specify the rental duration (e.g. hour, day, week).
* **Agreement Status**: Specify the agreement status (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Cancellation Reason**: Specify the reason for cancellation (e.g. Insufficient Demand, Unplanned Event Cancellation, Unexpected Event Cancellation).
* **Cancellation Notes**: Provide any additional notes about the cancellation.
* **Payment Confirmation**: Confirm payment details.
* **Agreement Status**: Confirm the agreement status.
* **Vendor Notes**: Add any additional notes from the vendor.
* **Vendor Signature 2**: Sign the form as the vendor to confirm agreement.
