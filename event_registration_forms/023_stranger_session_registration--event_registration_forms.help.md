# Stranger Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for registering participants for a stranger session. It is designed to collect necessary information for the session's organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter session details on page 1.
2. Choose the scheduling preferences for the session.
3. Provide participant details, including confirming their participation.
4. Confirm agreement with terms and conditions.
5. Confirm payment terms and conditions.
6. Confirm session details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`session_details`): Enter the session title and other relevant details.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`): Choose how you would like to schedule the session (e.g., "Pay on arrival" or "Pay online").
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Provide your name, contact information, and other relevant participant details.
* **Confirm Participation** (`confirm_participant`): Confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions and that you are participating in the session.
