# Electric Vehicle Expo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide is designed to assist attendees in completing the Electric Vehicle Expo Registration Form. The form is used to register vehicles for various events, such as expos and conferences. It is essential to complete the form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Prophets Name** (name) in the text field. This is a required field, as it is essential for registration purposes.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the email field. This is also a required field, as it is necessary for communication during the event.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number** (phone) in the text field. This is a required field, as it is needed for communication during the event.
4.  Select your **Company Name** (company_name) from the dropdown list (Tesla, Tesla Motors, Toyota). If you do not have a company affiliation, you can leave this field blank.
5.  Select your **Vehicle Make** (vehicle_make) from the dropdown list (Tesla, Toyota, Hyundai). This field is required.
6.  Select your **Vehicle Model** (vehicle_model) from the dropdown list (Model 3, Prius, Kona Electric). This field is also required.
7.  Enter the **Event Date** (event_date) in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD). This field is required.
8.  Enter the **Event Time** (event_time) in the 24-hour format (HH:MM). This field is required.
9.  Enter the **Registration Fee** (registration_fee) as a numerical value. This field is required for accurate fee calculations.
10.  If you have additional comments or information, you can enter it in the **Notes** field, which is optional.
11.  Select your **Role** (role) in the event from the dropdown list (Owner, Driver, Other). This field is required.
12.  Enter the **Vehicle Year** (vehicle_year) as a numerical value. This field is required.
13.  Enter the **Vehicle Mileage** (vehicle_mileage) as a numerical value. This field is required.
14.  Select your **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type) from the dropdown list (Electric, Gas, Hybrid). This field is required.
15.  Enter the **Vehicle Battery Capacity** (vehicle_battery_capacity) as a numerical value. This field is required.
16.  Select your **Event Type** (event_type) from the dropdown list (Expo, Show, Conference). This field is required.
17.  Select your **Vehicle Length** (vehicle_length) from the dropdown list (Short, Long). This field is required.
18.  Select your **Registration Status** (registration_status) from the dropdown list (Pending, Confirmed). This field is required.
19.  Enter the **Event Location** (event_location) as a text value. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Prophets Name** (name): A text field for the attendee's name.
*  **Email**: A text field for the attendee's email address.
*  **Phone Number** (phone): A text field for the attendee's phone number.
*  **Company Name** (company_name): A dropdown list of companies (Tesla, Tesla Motors, Toyota).
*  **Vehicle Make** (vehicle_make): A dropdown list of vehicle makes (Tesla, Toyota, Hyundai).
*  **Vehicle Model** (vehicle_model): A dropdown list of vehicle models (Model 3, Prius, Kona Electric).
*  **Event Date** (event_date): A date field for the event date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*  **Event Time** (event_time): A time field for the event time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*  **Registration Fee** (registration_fee): A numerical value for the registration fee.
*  **Notes** (notes): An optional text field for additional comments or information.
*  **Company** (company): An optional text field for the attendee's company affiliation.
*  **Role** (role): A dropdown list of roles (Owner, Driver, Other).
*  **Vehicle Year** (vehicle_year): A numerical value for the vehicle's year.
*  **Vehicle Mileage** (vehicle_mileage): A numerical value for the vehicle's mileage.
*  **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type): A dropdown list of vehicle types (Electric, Gas, Hybrid).
*  **Vehicle Battery Capacity** (vehicle_battery_capacity): A numerical value for the vehicle's battery capacity.
*  **Event Type** (event_type): A dropdown list of event types (Expo, Show, Conference).
*  **Vehicle Length** (vehicle_length): A dropdown list of vehicle lengths (Short, Long).
*  **Registration Status** (registration_status): A dropdown list of registration statuses (Pending, Confirmed).
*  **Event Location** (event_location): A text field for the event location.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter all required fields accurately to avoid delays or issues during the registration process.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
*   Double-check your form submission before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
