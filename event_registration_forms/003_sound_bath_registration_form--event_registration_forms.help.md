# Sound Bath Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sound Bath Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who want to register for a sound bath session. This form helps us understand your preferences and needs to create a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the "User Information" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. If you have any health concerns or needs, please specify in the "Attendee Health" field.
4. Choose the session time that best suits you in the "Session Time" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
6. If you want to receive email or phone communication from us, please provide your email and phone number in the respective fields.
7. Choose the sound frequency you prefer in the "Sound Frequency" field.
8. Lastly, choose the session type you're interested in: "Sound Bath" or "Reiki".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your address, phone number, or any other contact details you'd like us to have.
* **Attendee Health** (`attendee_health`, text, optional): If you have any health concerns or allergies, please let us know so we can accommodate you properly.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, text, optional): Choose the session time that works best for you. You can also select multiple times if you're interested in attending more than one session.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Please enter your payment method and any other payment details if required.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose between "Sound Bath" or "Reiki".
* **Sound Frequency** (`sound_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple sound frequencies you'd like to experience during the session.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method: "PayPal" or "Stripe".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you're interested in scheduling multiple sessions, please enter your date of birth so we can tailor the sessions to your needs.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of your preferred session.

## Tips
- Please ensure you've filled out all required fields to submit the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We'll respond to you within 24 hours regarding your session details.
