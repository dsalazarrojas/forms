# Financial Risk Modeling Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users for a financial risk modeling session. It is used to gather information about the user, the session details, and other related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
4. Enter the date of the session in the "Session Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the session in the "Session Time" field.
6. Mention the location of the session in the "Location" field.
7. Enter the date of registration in the "Registration Date" field.
8. If you are a participant, enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
9. If you are a participant, enter your participant number in the "Participant Number" field.
10. Choose your participant type from the "Participant Type" field.
11. Select the type of session you want to attend from the "Session Type" field.
12. Enter the topic of the session in the "Session Topic" field.
13. Enter the capacity of the session in the "Session Capacity" field.
14. Enter the start time of the session in the "Session Start Time" field.
15. Enter the end time of the session in the "Session End Time" field.
16. Enter the duration of the session in the "Session Duration" field.
17. Enter the registration code for the session in the "Registration Code" field.
18. Enter the location of the session in the "Session Location" field.
19. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
20. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
21. Enter the email addresses of the participants in the "Participant E-mail List" field.
22. Enter the phone numbers of the participants in the "Participant Phone List" field.
23. Enter the names of the participants in the "Participant Name List" field.
24. Enter the participant numbers of the participants in the "Participant Number List" field.
25. Finally, enter the tool assigned to the session in the "Assigned Tool" field.
