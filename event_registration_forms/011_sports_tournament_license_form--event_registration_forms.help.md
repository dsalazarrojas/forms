# Sports Tournament License Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Tournament License Form is used to collect information from both the tournament organizers and participants. This form helps to ensure that all necessary data is collected for a smooth and successful sports tournament, including organizer and participant details, registration details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Organizer Information** section with your name, email, and contact number.
2. Provide the **Participant Information** section with the participant's name, email, and contact number.
3. Enter the **Registration Details** section with the participant's team or player name, and any other relevant registration information.
4. Fill in the **Emergency Contact** section with the participant's emergency contact name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Information (page 1)**:
	+ **Organizer Name** (`title`, text): Enter your name as the tournament organizer.
	+ **Organizer Email** (`title`, text): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
	+ **Organizer Contact Number** (`title`, text): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Participant Information (page 2)**:
	+ **Participant Name** (`name`, text): Enter the name of the participant (player or team).
	+ **Participant Email** (`name`, text): Enter the participant's email address.
	+ **Participant Contact Number** (`name`, text): Enter the participant's contact number.
* **Registration Details (page 3)**:
	+ **Team or Player Name** (`title`, text): Enter the team or player name (if applicable).
	+ **Registration Status** (`title`, text): Indicate whether you are registering as a team or player.
* **Emergency Contact (page 4)**:
	+ **Emergency Contact Name** (`name`, text): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
	+ **Emergency Contact Email** (`name`, text): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
	+ **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`name`, text): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
- This form is designed to be easy to complete and submit, so take your time and double-check your information before submission.
