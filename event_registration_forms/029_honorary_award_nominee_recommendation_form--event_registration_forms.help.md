# Honorary Award Nominee Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for an honorary award nomination. Please use it to provide detailed and accurate information to help us evaluate the nominee's work.

## How To Complete This Form
* Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address for the nominator and award nominator.
* Select the correct award nomination category from the options provided.
* Enter a brief description of the award and a reason for the nomination.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`): This is a placeholder field for any additional information related to the event.
* **Nominator** (`nominator`): The person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`): The title of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Organization** (`nominator_organization`): The organization or department submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`): A valid email address of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`): A valid phone number of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee** (`nominee`): The person being nominated.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`): The title of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Organization** (`nominee_organization`): The organization or department of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Email** (`nominee_email`): A valid email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone** (`nominee_phone`): A valid phone number of the person being nominated.
* **Award Nomination Category** (`award_nomination_category`): Choose a category from the options provided.
* **Award Nomination Category Other** (`award_nomination_category_other`): If you don't see a category, enter the relevant information here.
* **Award Description** (`award_description`): A brief description of the award being nominated for.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`): A brief explanation for the nomination.
* **Nominee Signature** (`nominator_signature`): Signature of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Name** (`nominator_name`): The name of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Job Title** (`nominator_job_title`): The job title of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Department** (`nominator_department`): The department of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Position** (`nominator_position`): The position of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Job Title 2** (`nominator_job_title_2`): Another job title of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Nominee Phone 2** (`nominator_phone_2`): Another phone number of the person submitting this nomination.
* **Award Nominator** (`award_nominator`): The person nominating the award.
* **Award Nominator Email** (`award_nominator_email`): A valid email address of the person nominating the award.
