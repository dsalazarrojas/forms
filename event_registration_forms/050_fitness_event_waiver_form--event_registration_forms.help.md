# Fitness Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This waiver form is a required document to be completed by all participants before attending the fitness event. It helps us ensure that each participant has been informed about the potential risks involved in the event and provides essential information about their medical conditions and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Particpant Information**: Please fill out this section with your basic information, such as name, address, and contact number.
2. **Has Medical Conditions**: Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your ability to participate in the event. Please answer 'True' if you have any medical conditions.
3. **Acknowledges Risks**: Acknowledge that you understand the potential risks involved in the event. Please type 'True' to confirm.
4. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact, in case of an accident.
5. **Contact Details**: Include any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their relationship to you and their contact number.
6. **Participant Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver.
7. **Emergency Contact Signature**: If you have identified an emergency contact, please have them sign this section to confirm that they are aware of their role and responsibilities.
8. **Medical Authorization**: Indicate if you authorize medical treatment in case of an accident. Please answer 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. **Medical Authorization Details**: Provide any additional information about your medical conditions or treatment authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This field is for capturing your personal details such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Has Medical Conditions**: This field helps us identify participants with medical conditions that may affect their ability to participate in the event. If you answer 'True', we will take necessary precautions to ensure your safety.
* **Acknowledges Risks**: This field is a confirmation that you understand the potential risks involved in the event. Please acknowledge by typing 'True'.
* **Emergency Contact**: This field is for providing the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Details**: This field is for including any additional information about your emergency contact, such as their relationship to you and their contact number.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is for you to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver. Sign it to complete the form.
* **Emergency Contact Signature**: If you have identified an emergency contact, please have them sign this section to confirm that they are aware of their role and responsibilities.
* **Medical Authorization**: This field is for indicating if you authorize medical treatment in case of an accident. Answer 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Medical Authorization Details**: This field is for providing any additional information about your medical conditions or treatment authorization.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask our staff.
* Make sure to complete this form carefully and thoroughly before submitting it.
