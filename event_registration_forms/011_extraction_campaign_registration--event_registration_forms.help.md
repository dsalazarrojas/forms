# extraction_campaign_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering an event extraction campaign. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the campaign, including the participant's details, campaign type, and preferred dates. It also requires the participant's consent and assigned tool for the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the campaign type from the "Campaign Type" field.
5. Choose the start and end dates of the campaign from the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
6. Fill in any additional participant details in the "Participant Details" field (this is optional).
7. Select your preferred dates from the "Preferred Dates" field (this is optional).
8. Agree to the consent statement in the "Consent" field.
9. Choose the assigned tool for the campaign from the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Select the category of the campaign from the "Category" field.
11. Choose the output file type from the "Output File" field.
12. Fill in a brief description of the campaign in the "Description" field (this is optional).
13. Fill in a description of the category of the campaign in the "Category Description" field (this is optional).
14. Fill in the form ID in the "Form ID" field (this is optional).
15. Fill in a description of the form in the "Form Description" field (this is optional).
16. Fill in the title of the form in the "Form Title" field (this is optional).
17. Fill in the category of the form in the "Form Category" field (this is optional).
18. Fill in a description of the category of the form in the "Form Category Description" field (this is optional).
19. Fill in tags for the category of the form in "Form Category Tags" field (this is optional).
20. Fill in any additional tags for the form in "Form Tags" field (this is optional).
21. Fill in any comments for the form in "Form Comments" field (this is optional).
22. Fill in comments specific to the form in "Form Comments Text" field (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Campaign Type** (select one, optional): Select the type of campaign you are registering for.
* **Start Date** (date, required): Choose the start date of the campaign.
* **End Date** (date, required): Choose the end date of the campaign.
* **Participant Details** (note, optional): Fill in any additional participant details.
* **Preferred Dates** (select multiple, optional): Select your preferred dates.
* **Consent** (select one, required): Agree to the consent statement.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required): Choose the assigned tool for the campaign.
* **Category** (select one, required): Select the category of the campaign.
* **Output File** (select one, required): Choose the output file type.
* **Description** (text, optional): Fill in a brief description of the campaign.
* **Category Description** (text, optional): Fill in a description of the category of the campaign.
* **Form ID** (text, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Form Description** (text, optional): Enter a description of the form.
* **Form Title** (text, optional): Enter the title of the form.
* **Form Category** (text, optional): Enter the category of the form.
* **Form Category Description** (text, optional): Enter a description of the category of the form.
* **Form Category Tags** (text, optional): Enter tags for the category of the form.
* **Form Tags** (text, optional): Enter any additional tags for the form.
* **Form Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments for the form.
* **Form Comments Text** (text, optional): Enter comments specific to the form.

Note that not all fields are required, and some fields are optional. Please fill in the required fields first before proceeding to the next step.
