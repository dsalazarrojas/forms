# Gaming Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gaming Tournament Registration Form is a digital form designed to collect relevant information from teams and players participating in a gaming tournament. This form helps us to better understand your team's or player's preferences, skills, and needs, enabling us to provide a more enjoyable and competitive experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team's name and any additional team members who will be participating in the tournament.
2. Provide your Discord ID or a valid Discord server link.
3. Indicate if you have a Discord server.
4. Provide the tournament name and date.
5. Enter the game name, version, and mode you will be playing.
6. Specify the game server IP or URL.
7. Enter the number of players on your team.
8. Choose the type of team you are registering (e.g., casual, competitive).
9. Enter your player's rank and experience level.
10. Specify your player's position in the team.
11. Enter any additional notes about the player.
12. Enter any additional notes about the team.
13. Provide your team's email address.
14. Enter your team's phone number.
15. Enter your team's address.
16. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of your team, including any additional team members who will be participating in the tournament.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): Select "Yes" if you have any team members who will be participating in the tournament.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`): Enter your player's name.
* **Discord ID** (`discord_id`): Enter a valid Discord ID for your team or player (e.g., 123456).
* **Discord Server** (`discord_server`): If you have a Discord server, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Discord Channel** (`discord_channel`): If you have a Discord channel, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`): Enter the name of the tournament (e.g., "Tournament of Champions").
* **Tournament Date** (`tournament_date`): Enter the date of the tournament (e.g., 2024-03-16).
* **Tournament Time** (`tournament_time`): Enter the time of the tournament.
* **Tournament Duration** (`tournament_duration`): Enter the length of the tournament (e.g., 2 hours).
* **Game Name** (`game_name`): Enter the name of the game you will be playing.
* **Game Version** (`game_version`): Enter the version of the game (e.g., 1.0.1).
* **Game Mode** (`game_mode`): Enter the mode of the game (e.g., casual, competitive).
* **Game Server** (`game_server`): Enter the game server IP or URL.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`): Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`): Choose the type of team you are registering (e.g., casual, competitive).
* **Player Rank** (`player_rank`): Enter your player's rank.
* **Player Experience** (`player_experience`): Enter your player's experience level.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`): Enter your player's position in the team.
* **Player Notes** (`player_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the player.
* **Team Notes** (`team_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the team.
* **Team Email** (`team_email`): Enter your team's email address.
* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`): Enter your team's phone number.
* **Team Address** (`team_address`): Enter your team's address.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
