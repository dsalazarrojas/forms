# Treasury Workforce Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Treasury Workforce Summit Registration" form is designed to collect information from attendees, hosts, and speakers for the Treasury Workforce Summit. This form helps the event organizers to prepare for the event and ensure that all attendees have a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields with your full name.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to contact you regarding the event.
3. Select your **Role** from the options provided (Participant, Host, Speaker, or Sponsor).
4. Choose your **Participation Type** from the options provided (Full, One day pass, or Two day pass).
5. In the **Agreement** field, confirm that you agree to participate in the summit.
6. You can provide any additional information in the **Additional Info** field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you regarding the event.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: true): Select your role in the event (Participant, Host, Speaker, or Sponsor).
	+ Participant: You will be attending the event as a participant.
	+ Host: You will be hosting a session or event within the summit.
	+ Speaker: You will be presenting a session within the summit.
	+ Sponsor: You will be sponsoring the event or providing support.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose your participation type (Full, One day pass, or Two day pass).
	+ Full: You will be attending the entire summit.
	+ One day pass: You will be attending only on one specific day of the summit.
	+ Two day pass: You will be attending only on two specific days of the summit.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, note, required: false): Write any preferences or special requests you may have regarding the event.
* **Participation Date** (`participation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date(s) you plan to participate in the event.
* **Participation Time** (`participation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time(s) you plan to participate in the event.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any contact purposes.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required: true): Confirm that you agree to participate in the event.
	+ True: You agree to participate in the event.
	+ False: You do not agree to participate in the event.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure if you can participate in the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Write any notes or additional information you may have.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers.
