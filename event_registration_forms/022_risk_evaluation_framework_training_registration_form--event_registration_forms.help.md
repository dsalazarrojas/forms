# risk_evaluation_framework_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to register participants for a risk evaluation training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address for the participant.
3. Check the box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the participant agreement.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the participant, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the participant (if applicable).
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, text, required): Enter the organization of the participant.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, required): Select the specific training session that the participant is registering for.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session that the participant is registering for.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, required): Check the box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the participant agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizer for clarification.
* Do not register the same participant multiple times.
