# St. Patrick's Day Parade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The St. Patrick's Day Parade Entry Form is a crucial part of the parade's organization process. It allows participants to provide necessary details about their entry, ensuring a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Entry Information section with your entry's details.
2. Provide contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Enter your address and city details.
4. Select your payment method and entry type.
5. Choose your entry subtype (if applicable).
6. Add a brief description of your entry.
7. Upload an entry photo (optional but recommended).
8. Select your entry time.
9. Confirm your payment fee and frequency.
10. Indicate if you have performed here before or not.
11. Provide any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Information (1)** (Entry Information, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your entry.
* **Entry Details (2)** (Entry Details, text, optional): Add any additional details about your entry.
* **Contact Information (3)** (Contact Information, text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Logistics (4)** (Logistics, text, optional): Enter any logistics information related to your entry.
* **Payment Information (5)** (Payment Information, text, optional): Enter the entry fee and payment method.
* **Entry Fee (6)** (Entry Fee, number, optional): Enter the entry fee for your participant.
* **Entry Fee Frequency (7)** (Entry Fee Frequency, select_one, optional): Choose how often you'd like to participate with this entry.
* **Entry Type (8)** (Entry Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of entry you're submitting (e.g., Float, Drum, Dance, etc.).
* **Entry Subtype (9)** (Entry Subtype, select_multiple, optional): Select additional subtypes for your entry (e.g., if it's a band, select "Band" as one of the options).
* **Entry Description (10)** (Entry Description, text, optional): Add a brief description of your entry.
* **Entry Photo (11)** (Entry Photo, date, optional): Upload a photo of your entry.
* **Entry Time (12)** (Entry Time, time, optional): Enter the start and end time of your entry.
* **Email (13)** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (14)** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address (15)** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **City (16)** (City, text, optional): Enter the city where you're located.
* **State (17)** (State, text, optional): Enter the state where you're located.
* **Zip Code (18)** (Zip Code, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Payment Method (19)** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to pay (e.g., Online, Cash, Check).
* **Sponsored? (20)** (Sponsored?, select_one, optional): Indicate if your entry is sponsored.
* **Sponsor (21)** (Sponsor, text, optional): Enter the name of your sponsor if applicable.
* **Additional Info (22)** (Additional Info, text, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Have you performed here before? (23)** (Have you performed here before?, select_one, optional): Confirm if you've performed here before.
* **Have you performed here in previous years? (24)** (Have you performed here in previous years?, select_one, optional): Confirm if you've performed here in previous years.
* **Entry Photo (25)** (Entry Photo, text, optional): Upload a photo of your entry.
