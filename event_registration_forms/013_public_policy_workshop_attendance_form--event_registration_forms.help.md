# public_policy_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used by organizers or administrators to collect information from attendees for a public policy workshop. The form will help in tracking the workshop participation and attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, optional)
Enter a brief description of the workshop, including the topic, purpose, and expected outcomes.

### 2. **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `text`, optional)
Provide details about the attendee, including their name, title, and affiliation.

### 3. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, optional)
Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can reach out to you about any further questions or updates.

### 4. **Event Info** (`event_info`, `text`, optional)
Enter any additional information about the event, such as the event hashtag, social media handle, or any other relevant details.

### 5. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional)
Add any additional comments or notes about the workshop or your participation.

### 6. **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `text`, optional)
Enter the physical location of the workshop.

### 7. **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of the workshop.

### 8. **Workshop Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, `time`, optional)
Enter the start time of the workshop.

### 9. **Workshop End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, `time`, optional)
Enter the end time of the workshop.

### 10. **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the start date of the event. 

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information about the workshop and the event.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the organizers or administrators for clarification.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
