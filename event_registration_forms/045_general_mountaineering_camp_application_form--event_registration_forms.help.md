# General Mountaineering Camp Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for collecting information from general mountaineering camp attendees. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. 
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. 
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. 
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. 
5. Select your camp preferences from the "Camp Preferences" field. 
6. Choose your date of arrival using the "Date" field. 
7. Choose your time of arrival using the "Time" field. 
8. If you have any additional information to provide, use the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name. 
  • Please enter your first name in this field. 

- **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name. 
  • Please enter your last name in this field. 

- **Email**: An email field to enter your email address. 
  • Please enter a valid email address that you regularly check. 

- **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number. 
  • Please enter your phone number in this format: `XXX-XXX-XXXX`. 

- **Camp Preferences**: A select_one field to choose your camp preference. 
  • Please select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. 

- **Date**: A date field to choose your date of arrival. 
  • Please choose a valid date for your arrival. 

- **Time**: A time field to choose your time of arrival. 
  • Please choose a valid time for your arrival. 

- **Notes**: A note field for any additional information. 
  • You can use this field to provide any additional comments or requests. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete. 
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
