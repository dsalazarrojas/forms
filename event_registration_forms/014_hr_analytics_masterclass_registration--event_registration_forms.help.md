# HR Analytics Masterclass Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your preferences and experience level for the HR Analytics Masterclass. Please complete the form to help tailor your experience and get the most out of the masterclass.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select all that apply for Session Preferences
* Enter your Name
* Choose your Experience Level
* Select your Session Preferences

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (Select multiple, optional): Please select all the session preferences that apply to you. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs.

### Name
* **Name** (Text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the masterclass.

### Experience Level
* **Experience Level** (Select one, optional): Choose your level of experience with HR Analytics. This will help us understand your needs and tailor the experience accordingly.

### Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (Select one, optional): Please select your session preferences from the options provided. This will help us understand what you would like to get out of the masterclass.
