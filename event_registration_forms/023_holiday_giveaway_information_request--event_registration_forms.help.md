# Holiday Giveaway Information Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Giveaway Information Request form is used to collect information about a holiday event you are hosting or participating in. This form is essential for our giveaway program to verify the event's legitimacy and ensure it meets our program's criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Participant Information" page to provide your personal details.
2. Select the "Holiday Information" page to enter information about the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
3. Fill in the required fields with the correct information, making sure to include your first and last name, email, phone number, holiday name, date, time, location, address, and a brief description.
4. Review your form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is listed on your ID or official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is listed on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a working email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Information** (`holiday_giveaway_first_page`, `text`, required): This is a placeholder page to collect your participant information. Please fill out your details here.

### Holiday Information
* **Holiday Name** (`holiday_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
* **Holiday Date** (`holiday_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
* **Holiday Time** (`holiday_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
* **Holiday Location** (`holiday_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
* **Holiday Address** (`holiday_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
* **Holiday Description** (`holiday_description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the holiday event you are hosting or participating in.
