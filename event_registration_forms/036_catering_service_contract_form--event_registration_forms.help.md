# Catering Service Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Catering Service Contract Form is designed for clients to register their event catering service contract with the caterer. It collects essential information about the client, their event, and the terms of the contract. This form is required to be filled out to ensure a successful and enjoyable event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to provide all required information.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Your complete address.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, please provide the name of the company.
* **Catering Company Name**: The name of the catering company providing the services.
* **Caterer Representative**: The person representing the catering company.
* **Contract Date**: The date of the contract.
* **Event Name**: The name of the event.
* **Event Type**: The type of event (e.g., wedding, corporate event, birthday party).
* **Event Date**: The date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: The start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: The end time of the event.
* **Venue Name**: The location name where the event will be held.
* **Venue Address**: The complete address of the venue.
* **Guaranteed Guest Count**: The final number of guests attending the event.
* **Menu Package**: The selected menu package (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, Deluxe, Custom).
* **Menu Details**: Specific menu items or requests.
* **Beverage Package**: The selected beverage package (e.g., Non-Alcoholic, Beer and Wine, Full Bar, Coffee and Tea).
* **Additional Services**: Optional services such as setup, cleanup, tables and chairs, linens, china and silverware, serving staff, bartenders, decorations.
* **Total Contract Cost**: The total amount of the contract.
* **Deposit Amount**: The required deposit amount.
* **Deposit Due Date**: The date the deposit is due.
* **Balance Due Date**: The date the final payment is due.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment (e.g., Credit Card, Check, Bank Transfer, Cash).
* **Cancellation Policy Accepted**: Acceptance of the cancellation policy.
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted**: Acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Client Signature Date**: The date of the client's signature.
* **Caterer Signature Date**: The date of the caterer's signature (optional).
* **Special Provisions**: Any additional terms or provisions.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submission.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the caterer's team.
* The caterer's team will review and process your submission promptly.
