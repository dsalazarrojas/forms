# Material Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects attendee information for the Material Innovation Workshop. Please fill it out to register and provide us with essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Material Innovation Workshop, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "E-mail" field.
4. For the "Topic Interests" field, select all the topics you are interested in.
5. If you have any special needs, type them in the "Special Needs" field.
6. Provide a brief note about yourself and your interests in the "Attendee Details" field.
7. Select your topic preferences in the "Topic Preferences" field.
8. Indicate if you have any dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **E-mail** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all topics that interest you, such as materials science, innovation, or sustainability.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special needs or requirements, type them here, for example, wheelchair accessibility or dietary restrictions.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `note`, required: false): Enter a brief note about yourself and your interests. This will help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
* **Topic Preferences** (`topic_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all topics that you are interested in or prefer to attend.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_one`, required: false): If you have any dietary restrictions, select "Yes" to indicate that you need special accommodation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific and honest when selecting your topic interests and preferences.
* If you have any special needs, be sure to mention them so we can accommodate you.
* If you have dietary restrictions, please let us know so we can provide the necessary accommodations.
