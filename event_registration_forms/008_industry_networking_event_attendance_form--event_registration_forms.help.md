# industry-networking-event-attendance-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register their attendance and provide information about their relationship with the industry at an industry networking event. It collects details such as participant name, contact information, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your relationship with the industry from the "Relationship with Industry" dropdown menu.
5. If you have any questions about the event, type them in the "Do you have any questions about the event?" field.
6. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Do you have any special requests?" field.
7. Confirm your participation by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Participation" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Finally, confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information**: This field is not used in this form. You can skip it.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Relationship with Industry**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Attendee: You are attending the event as a participant.
	+ Speaker: You are speaking at the event.
	+ Exhibitor: You are exhibiting at the event.
	+ Vendor: You are a vendor or supplier.
	+ Media: You are a member of the media.
	+ Organizer: You are helping to organize the event.
* **Do you have any questions about the event?**: If you have any questions, please type them here.
* **Do you have any special requests?**: If you have any special requests, please type them here.
* **Confirm Participation**: Confirm that you are able to participate in the event.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information you would like to share.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that your email address is correct.
