# Wedding Planner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Planner Referral Form is designed to allow wedding planners to refer potential clients or vendors to our services. This form helps us understand who is referring and who is being referred, enabling us to follow up and provide more information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the name of your business in the "Business name" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the referral type from the options provided in the "Referral type" field.
7. Enter the name of the client being referred in the "Client name" field, if applicable.
8. Enter the name of the vendor being referred in the "Vendor name" field, if applicable.
9. Enter any additional message or information in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name. This will be used to address you in our follow-up communication.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Last name**: Enter your last name. This will help us understand your full name.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Email**: Enter your email address. This is where we will send information and follow up on your referral.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
- **Business name**: Enter the name of your business. This will help us understand your affiliation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This is where we can contact you for more information.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Referral date**: Enter the date of the referral. This will help us track the timing of the referral.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no
- **Referral type**: Select the type of referral. You can choose from "client", "vendor", or "both".
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: no
  * Options: 
    + client
    + vendor
    + both
- **Client name**: Enter the name of the client being referred. This is optional.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Vendor name**: Enter the name of the vendor being referred. This is optional.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
- **Message**: Enter any additional information or message you'd like to provide. This is optional.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no
