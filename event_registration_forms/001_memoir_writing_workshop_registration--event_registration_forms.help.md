# Memoir Writing Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Memoir Writing Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from participants who are interested in attending a workshop on memoir writing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your session preferences (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Indicate whether you have any class requirements (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
4. Select the workshop date and time you would like to attend.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the registration process.
