# Donation Counting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track and count donations received, including gift cards and cash donations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the donor's name and phone number (fields 1 and 2).
2. Enter the gift card value and any other relevant details (fields 3 and 5).
3. Record the cash donation amount (field 4).
4. Enter the gift card number and issuer details (fields 7 and 13).
5. Enter the donor's email address and address (fields 6 and 14).
6. Check the status of the gift card and the donor's status (fields 8 and 10).
7. Enter the date and time of the donation (fields 9 and 11).
8. Provide any additional notes about the donor (field 12).
9. Select the gift card type (field 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (1, text, Required): Enter the full name of the donor.
* **Donor Phone** (2, text, Optional): Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Gift Card Value** (3, number, Optional): Enter the value of the gift card.
* **Cash Donation** (4, number, Optional): Enter the cash donation amount.
* **Gift Card Number** (5, text, Optional): Enter the gift card number.
* **Donor Email** (6, email, Optional): Enter the donor's email address.
* **Gift Card Status** (7, select_multiple, Optional): Select the status of the gift card (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Donor Status** (8, select_one, Optional): Select the donor's status (Active, Inactive, or Other).
* **Donation Date** (9, date, Optional): Enter the date of the donation.
* **Donation Time** (10, time, Optional): Enter the time of the donation.
* **Donor Notes** (11, note, Optional): Provide any additional notes about the donor.
* **Gift Card Type** (12, select_multiple, Optional): Select the type of gift card.
* **Gift Card Issuer** (13, text, Optional): Enter the issuer of the gift card.
* **Donor Address** (14, text, Optional): Enter the donor's address.
* **Gift Card Number 2** (15, text, Optional): Enter the second gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 3** (16, text, Optional): Enter the third gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 4** (17, text, Optional): Enter the fourth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 5** (18, text, Optional): Enter the fifth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 6** (19, text, Optional): Enter the sixth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 7** (20, text, Optional): Enter the seventh gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 8** (21, text, Optional): Enter the eighth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 9** (22, text, Optional): Enter the ninth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 10** (23, text, Optional): Enter the tenth gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 11** (24, text, Optional): Enter the eleventh gift card number.
* **Gift Card Number 12** (25, text, Optional): Enter the twelfth gift card number.

Note: All fields with a label that ends with a number (e.g., "Gift Card Number") are optional and should be filled out only if relevant.
