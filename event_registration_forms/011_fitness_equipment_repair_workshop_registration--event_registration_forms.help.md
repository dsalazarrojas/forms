# Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This help guide is designed to guide users through the process of filling out the Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop Registration form with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
- Select your session preferences from the dropdown menu.
- Provide details about any equipment issues you are experiencing.
- Enter your attendee details accurately.
- Choose your preferred workshop date and time from the calendar.
- If applicable, specify the equipment model.
- Select the workshop type that suits your needs.
- Choose the workshop location that is most convenient for you.
- Optionally, provide any additional comments or notes.
- If applicable, fill out the workshop facilitator's details, including their phone number, email, address, and additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your session preferences.
* **Equipment Issues** (`equipment_issues`, text, required): Describe any equipment issues you are experiencing.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (`preferred_workshop_date`, date, required): Select a date for the workshop from the calendar.
* **Preferred Workshop Time** (`preferred_workshop_time`, time, required): Select a time slot for the workshop from the calendar.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the model of the equipment you are bringing.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your workshop type preference.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your workshop location preference.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Notes** (`notes`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any additional notes.
* **Workshop Facilitator** (`workshop_facilitator`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the workshop facilitator's name.
* **Workshop Facilitator Phone** (`workshop_facilitator_phone`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the workshop facilitator's phone number.
* **Workshop Facilitator Email** (`workshop_facilitator_email`, email, optional): If applicable, provide the workshop facilitator's email address.
* **Workshop Facilitator Address** (`workshop_facilitator_address`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the workshop facilitator's address.
* **Workshop Facilitator Notes** (`workshop_facilitator_notes`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional notes from the facilitator.
* **Workshop Facilitator Notes Type** (`workshop_facilitator_notes_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of notes.
* **Workshop Facilitator Additional Notes Venue** (`workshop_facilitator_notes_venue`, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional notes from the facilitator.
* **Workshop Facilitator Additional Notes** (`workshop_facilitator_additional_notes`, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional notes from the facilitator.
* **Workshop Facilitator Notes 3** (`workshop_facilitator_notes_3`, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional notes from the facilitator.
