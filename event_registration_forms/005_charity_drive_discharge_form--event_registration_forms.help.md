# Charity Drive Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and information from participants who have completed their duties in a charity drive. The information collected through this form will help the organization to improve future drives and ensure that participants' experiences are positive and productive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Participant Full Name, which is your name.
2. Select your Role in Charity Drive from the available options: Volunteer, Staff Member, Sponsor, or Coordinator.
3. Enter the Discharge Date, which is the date you concluded your duties.
4. Rate your Overall Satisfaction with the Drive using a scale from 1 to 5 (Poor to Excellent).
5. Choose how well the event was organized: Well Organized, Moderately Organized, or Poorly Organized.
6. If you wish, provide the Key Successes of this drive.
7. If you wish, describe any challenges you faced during the drive.
8. Answer if the resources provided were adequate or not.
9. Indicate if you are interested in participating in future drives.
10. Rate your likelihood of recommending this organization to others: Very Likely, Likely, or Unlikely.
11. Provide any Suggestions for Improvement for future drives.
12. Enter any Final Checkout Notes, such as items returned or remaining tasks.
13. Confirm that you have completed all required discharge steps by selecting "Confirmed".
