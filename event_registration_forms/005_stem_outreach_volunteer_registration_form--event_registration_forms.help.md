**STEM Outreach Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteers who want to participate in STEM outreach activities. This information will help us better understand who is interested in volunteering and what they are interested in doing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the information requested in each field. You can save and edit as you go, and you will be able to preview your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, required: false): Please fill in the details of the event you are interested in participating in. This could be a specific activity, event name, or description.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false): We would like to know how to contact you. This could be your name, email, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **Volunteer Details** (`Volunteer Details`, text, required: false): Please tell us about your skills, interests, or availability to volunteer. This will help us better understand your fit for our program.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required: false): If you know the date of the event you are interested in, please fill it in. If not, leave it blank.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required: false): If you know the time of the event, please fill it in. If not, leave it blank.
* **Volunteer Time** (`Volunteer Time`, time, required: false): If you know the time you are available to volunteer, please fill it in. If not, leave it blank.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing information.
* If you are interested in multiple events, you can fill out this form multiple times.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's recommended that you fill them out if you have information available.
