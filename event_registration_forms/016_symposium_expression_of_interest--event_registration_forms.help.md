# symposium_expression_of_interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event organizers gauge interest in participating in an event and collect contact information and preferences from potential participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the event details to ensure you are expressing interest in the correct event.
2. Answer the questions on each page, selecting the relevant options for each field.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number as prompted.
4. Provide any additional participant details in the "Participant Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is this a good fit for our event?** (`early_interest`, select_multiple): This field allows you to select one or more of the provided options to indicate your level of interest in the event.
* **What topics would you be interested in?** (`topics`, text): In this field, you can enter the topics that you are interested in, separated by commas or on new lines if you prefer.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed or used by the event organizers.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached about the event.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note): Provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation in the event.
