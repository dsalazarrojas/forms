# Fundraiser Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraiser Check In Form is used to collect information from event attendees, organizers, and fundraisers for the purpose of tracking event participation and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the event.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the attendee or organizer.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the attendee or organizer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the attendee or organizer.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the attendee or organizer.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select whether this is a yes or no event.
* **Fundraiser Name** (`fundraiser_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the fundraiser.
* **Fundraiser Title** (`fundraiser_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the fundraiser.
* **Fundraiser Organization** (`fundraiser_organization`, text, required): Enter the organization of the fundraiser.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Enter the goals of the event.
* **Goal Amount** (`goal_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the goal.
* **Fundraiser Goal Type** (`fundraiser_goal_type`, select_multiple, required): Select whether this is a yes or no goal.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Title** (`event_organizer_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Organization** (`event_organizer_organization`, text, required): Enter the organization of the event organizer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **None** (`none`, text, optional): Enter any other information not covered in the above fields.
