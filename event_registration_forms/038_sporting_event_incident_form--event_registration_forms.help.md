# Sporting Event Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur during sporting events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of the incident in the `Incident Date` field.
2. Enter the event ID number in the `Event ID` field.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the `Description` field.
4. Choose the type of incident from the `Incident Type` dropdown menu.
5. Select the sport being played in the `Sport` dropdown menu.
6. Enter the location of the incident in the `Location` field.
7. If applicable, choose the type of injury from the `Injury Type` dropdown menu.
8. Enter any relevant details about the incident in the `Incident Details` field.
9. If medical treatment was provided, select all applicable options in the `Medical Treatment` field.
10. Enter the name and contact information of the person reporting the incident in the `Reporting Person` field.
11. Enter the contact number of the person reporting the incident in the `Contact Number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (Date)
	+ The date of the incident.
* **Event ID** (Number)
	+ The unique ID of the event.
* **Description** (Text)
	+ A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Type** (Select One)
	+ The type of incident, choose from: Incident, Injury, Other.
* **Sport** (Select One)
	+ The sport being played at the time of the incident, choose from: Football, Basketball, Baseball, Hockey, Golf.
* **Location** (Text)
	+ The location where the incident occurred.
* **Injury Type** (Select One)
	+ The type of injury involved, choose from: Head, Neck, Back, Joint, Other.
* **Incident Details** (Text)
	+ Additional details about the incident.
* **Medical Treatment** (Select Multiple)
	+ The type of medical treatment provided, choose from: Emergency medical treatment, First aid, Both.
* **Reporting Person** (Text)
	+ The name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Number** (Text)
	+ The contact information of the person reporting the incident.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident and any injuries involved.
* If an athlete received medical treatment, make sure to select all applicable options.
* If the reporting person is not the athlete, enter their name and contact information.
