# Congress Presenters Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an application for a presentation spot in a congress. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your presentation proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the presenter's information section (Primary Presenter Full Name, Professional Title/Position, Organization or Institution Affiliation, Email Address, and Brief Professional Biography).
2. In the presentation proposal section, provide a title for your presentation, choose the preferred presentation format, select the relevant topic category, and submit a brief abstract and three key learning objectives.
3. If necessary, list your technical requirements, including any audiovisual or equipment needs.
4. Confirm that you agree to the presenter terms and conditions and indicate whether you require travel funding support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Presenter Full Name** (Text, Required): Fill out the full name of the primary presenter.
* **Professional Title/Position** (Text, Required): Enter the primary presenter's job title or position.
* **Organization or Institution Affiliation** (Text, Required): Provide the name of the organization or institution the presenter is affiliated with.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the primary presenter's email address.
* **Brief Professional Biography** (Text, Required): Write a brief bio of the primary presenter, up to 200 words.
* **Title of Presentation** (Text, Required): Enter a title for your presentation.
* **Preferred Presentation Format** (Select One, Required): Choose the format for your presentation (Oral, Workshop, Panel Discussion, or Poster Session).
* **Relevant Topic Category** (Select One, Required): Select the category that best describes your presentation (Scientific Research, Policy and Governance, Educational Innovation, Technology and Society, or Environmental Sustainability).
* **Presentation Abstract** (Text, Required): Summarize your key points and objectives in 300-500 words.
* **Three Key Learning Objectives** (Text, Required): Provide three key learning objectives for your presentation.
* **Audiovisual and Equipment Needs** (Select Multiple, Optional): List any audiovisual or equipment needs you have for your presentation (Projector and Screen, Audio/Speakers, Handheld Microphone, Laptop Hookup (HDMI), or Whiteboard/Flipchart).
* **I agree to the presenter terms and conditions** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you agree to the presenter terms and conditions.
* **Do you require travel funding support?** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether you require travel funding support or not.
