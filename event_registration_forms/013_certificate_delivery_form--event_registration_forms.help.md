# Certificate Delivery Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Certificate Delivery Form is a critical component of our certificate delivery process. This form ensures that we receive all necessary information to deliver certificates to your recipients efficiently and accurately. Please use this form for all certificate delivery requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form below with all required information to ensure prompt and accurate delivery of certificates.
2. Ensure that you have provided all necessary details for the recipient, event, and certificate type.
3. Review and double-check all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date**: Date of delivery request (<type:date>): The date when you need the certificate to be delivered. Please use the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Recipient Name**: Full name of certificate recipient (<type:text>): Enter the full name of the person or organization receiving the certificate.
* **Email Address**: Email for delivery notifications (<type:email>): Enter the email address for delivery notifications and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone for delivery issues (<type:text>): Enter the phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Event Name**: Name of the event or program (<type:text>): Enter the name of the event or program related to the certificate.
* **Event Date**: Date when the event occurred (<type:date>): The date when the event took place.
* **Certificate Type**: Type of certificate to be delivered (<type:select_one>): Select one of the available certificate types (e.g., Attendance, Completion, etc.).
* **Delivery Method**: How should the certificate be delivered (<type:select_one>): Choose the delivery method (e.g., Email, Postal mail, etc.).
* **Mailing Address**: Full address for postal delivery (<type:text>): Enter the full mailing address if you choose postal delivery.
* **Delivery Urgency**: How quickly do you need the certificate delivered (<type:select_one>): Select the delivery urgency (e.g., Standard, Expedited, etc.).
* **Number of Certificates**: How many certificates are needed (<type:number>): Enter the number of certificates required.
* **List of Recipient Names**: Names as they should appear on certificates (<type:text>): Enter the names as they should be printed on the certificates.
* **Special Formatting Requests**: Any special design or formatting requirements (<type:text>): Enter any special design or formatting requests.
* **Envelope Preference**: Type of envelope for physical certificates (<type:select_one>): Choose the envelope preference (e.g., Standard, Premium, etc.).
* **Include Official Seal**: Should an official seal be included (<type:select_one>): Select whether or not to include the official seal.
* **Include Authorized Signature**: Should the certificate include signatures (<type:select_one>): Select whether or not to include signatures.
* **Delivery Notification Preference**: How should you be notified of delivery (<type:select_one>): Choose notification method (e.g., Email, SMS, etc.).
* **Tracking Required**: Do you require delivery tracking (<type:select_one>): Select whether or not tracking is required.
* **Signature Required**: Is signature required upon delivery (<type:select_one>): Select whether or not a signature is required.
* **Special Delivery Instructions**: Any specific delivery instructions (<type:text>): Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Method**: Method of payment for delivery fees (<type:select_one>): Select the method of payment (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
* **Requester Signature**: Type your full name as signature (<type:text>): Enter your full name as a signature.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have included all necessary information for delivery.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email].
