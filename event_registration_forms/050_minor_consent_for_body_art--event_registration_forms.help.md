# Minor Consent For Body Art - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the minor's and parent's consent for body art services. It captures essential information, including minor's name, date of birth, parent's name, contact information, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Parent's name: Enter the parent's name as it appears on their ID or official documents.
	* Minor's name: Enter the minor's name as it appears on their ID or official documents.
	* Minor's date of birth: Enter the minor's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
	* Date: Enter the date you are completing this form.
	* Time: Enter the time you are completing this form.
2. Answer the required questions:
	* Parent's contact information: Enter a valid contact method for the parent or guardian (e.g., phone number, email).
	* Parent's signature: Sign as the parent or guardian.
	* Minor's signature: Sign as the minor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Consent
* **Parent Consent** (`Parent Consent`, `select_one`, required): Please sign this consent form to give your permission for your child to receive body art services.
* **Studio Information** (`Studio Information`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about the studio, such as address or contact number.
* **Minor Information** (`Minor Information`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional information about the minor, such as medical conditions or allergies.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's name as it appears on their ID or official documents.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`Parent Contact Info`, `text`, required): Enter a valid contact method for the parent or guardian (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Minor Name** (`Minor Name`, `text`, required): Enter the minor's name as it appears on their ID or official documents.
* **Minor DOB** (`Minor DOB`, `date`, required): Enter the minor's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Minor Signature** (`Minor Signature`, `note`, required): Sign as the minor.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`Parent/Guardian Signature`, `text`, required): Sign as the parent or guardian.
* **Tattoo Designer** (`Tattoo Designer`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the tattoo designer.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are completing this form.

## Tips
* Ensure that both parent and minor sign this form to give proper consent.
* Enter accurate information for both parent and minor.
* Ensure that the minor's date of birth is entered in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
