# Financial Seminar Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Seminar Referral Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in attending one of our financial seminars. This form allows us to collect details about the seminar, attendees, and next steps. Please fill out this form to refer someone to our seminar.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, first select the seminar type from the dropdown list. Then, fill in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Seminar Date) and any additional details as needed.
2.  Make sure to enter a valid email address for the attendee.
3.  If you're referring someone from your company, provide the company name in the Company field.
4.  Enter the job title of the attendee.
5.  Provide any additional invitation details in the Invitation Details field.
6.  Finally, specify the next steps for the attendee in the Next Steps field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Seminar Referral Form**: This is the form title.
*   **Seminar Details**: This field is not explicitly labeled, but it's implied that it's about selecting the seminar type. The options are:
    \- Seminar A
    \- Seminar B
    \- Seminar C
*   **First Name**: Enter the attendee's first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter the attendee's last name.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for the attendee.
*   **Phone Number**: This field is for additional phone number details, not explicitly required.
*   **Company**: If the attendee is from your company, enter the company name.
*   **Job Title**: Enter the attendee's job title.
*   **Seminar Type**: Select the seminar type (Seminar A, Seminar B, or Seminar C) that the attendee is interested in.
*   **Seminar Date**: Enter the date of the seminar.
*   **Invitation Details**: Provide any additional details about the invitation.
*   **Next Steps**: Specify the next steps for the attendee.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can follow up with the attendee correctly.
*   If you're unsure about any information, contact us for assistance.
