# Surprise Birthday Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a surprise birthday invitation, including host and party details, guest list, and RSVPs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the host's information, including phone number and email.
2.  Enter the birthday information, including the date and time of the celebration.
3.  Indicate if you are bringing a guest or not.
4.  Respond to the RSVP invitation with a yes or no.
5.  Leave a personal message for the guest, if desired.
6.  Enter any other relevant information or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Host Information** (`host_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the host's phone number and email for contact information.
*   **Birthday Information** (`birthday_info`, `note`, optional): Enter the date and time of the birthday party.
*   **Guest List** (`guest_list`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you are bringing a guest or not.
*   **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `select_one`, optional): Respond to the invitation with a yes or no.
*   **Secret Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Leave a personal message for the guest, if desired.
*   **Host Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the host's phone number for contact.
*   **Host Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the host's email for contact.
*   **Birthday** (`birthday`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the birthday party.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the birthday party.
*   **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the birthday party.
*   **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests attending the party.
*   **Message from Guest** (`message_from_guest`, `text`, optional): Enter a message from each guest, if desired.
*   **Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, `text`, optional): List any gift preferences for the birthday person.
*   **Other Information** (`other_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the party.
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the birthday party.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help ensure a successful and enjoyable birthday celebration.
