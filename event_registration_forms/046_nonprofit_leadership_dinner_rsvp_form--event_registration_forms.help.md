# nonprofit_leadership_dinner_rsvp_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs for a nonprofit leadership dinner event. It's meant to gather information about each guest's attendance, dietary preferences, and any additional message or comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to save your responses.
2. Fill in the Guest Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
3. Select your attendance status in the "Will you be attending?" section.
4. Choose your dietary preferences in the "Dietary Preferences" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or messages, you can type them in the "Message or Comments" section (this is optional).
6. Finally, confirm your RSVP in the "RSVP" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details (Guest Details)** (`name` `text`, **Required**): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Will you be attending? (Will you be attending?)** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): Select one or more options to indicate if you'll be attending the event, with a partner, or not at all.
	+ Options: Yes, with partner, Yes, solo, False
* **Dietary Preferences (Dietary Preferences)** (`select_multiple`, **Required**): Choose your dietary preferences from the list of options.
	+ Options: I don't have any dietary restrictions, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free
* **Message or Comments (Message or Comments)** (`text`, **Optional**): Type any additional comments or messages you may have about the event (this is optional).
* **RSVP (RSVP)** (`select_one`, **Required**): Select "True" to confirm your attendance or "False" to decline.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the Guest Details section accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please specify them in the Message or Comments section.
* Confirm your attendance status in the RSVP section.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, feel free to ask your event organizer for clarification.
