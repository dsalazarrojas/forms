# Pilgrimage Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for pilgrims to register their interest in participating in a trip. It captures essential information needed for trip planning and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing your first name, last name, email, phone number, age, date of birth, and gender.
2. Select your country of residence and the languages you can speak.
3. Choose whether you will be participating in a trip planning process ('Yes' or 'No').
4. Provide any additional comments, if needed.
5. Assign a user to work with for the trip.
6. Assign a tool to be used for the trip.
7. Assign a team to work with for the trip.
8. Choose the type of trip plan you are interested in ('Online', 'In-person', or 'Hybrid').
9. Provide any comments about the trip plan type.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Age** (5, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (7, select_one, required): Choose your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Country** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all countries you reside in.
* **Languages Spoken** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select all languages you can speak.
* **Trip Planning** (11, select_one, optional): Choose whether you will participate in trip planning ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Note** (12, note, optional): Provide any additional comments.
* **Assigned User** (13, email, required): Assign a user to work with for the trip.
* **Assigned Tool** (14, text, required): Assign a tool to be used for the trip.
* **Assigned Team** (15, text, required): Assign a team to work with for the trip.
* **Comments - Trip** (16, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip.
* **Comments - Assigned User** (17, note, optional): Provide any comments about the assigned user.
* **Comments - Assigned Tool** (18, note, optional): Provide any comments about the assigned tool.
* **Comments - Assigned Team** (19, note, optional): Provide any comments about the assigned team.
* **Comments - Trip Plan** (20, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip plan.
* **Trip Plan Type** (21, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of trip plan (Online, In-person, or Hybrid).
* **Comments - Trip Plan Type** (22, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip plan type.
* **Comments - Trip Plan Type - Online** (23, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip plan type (Online).
* **Comments - Trip Plan Type - In-person** (24, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip plan type (In-person).
* **Comments - Trip Plan Type - Hybrid** (25, note, optional): Provide any comments about the trip plan type (Hybrid).

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have selected all required fields.
* Use the correct format for date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
