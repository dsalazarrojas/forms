# DJ Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DJ Service Renewal Form is designed to collect information for the renewal of DJ services. This form is used to gather details about the client, service, and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
2. **Contract Date**: Enter the date of the contract.
3. **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided.
4. **Renewal Date**: Enter the date of the renewal.
5. **Renewal Term**: Enter the term of the renewal.
6. **Contract Value**: Enter the value of the contract.
7. **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
8. **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
9. **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
10. **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the client.
11. **Service Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the service.
12. **Service Address**: Enter the address of the service.
13. **Event Type**: Select the type of event.
14. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
15. **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
16. **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
17. **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
18. **Contract Status**: Select the status of the contract.
19. **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions.
20. **Billing Terms**: Enter any billing terms or conditions.
21. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes.
22. **Service Provider**: Enter the name of the service provider.
23. **Client Agreement**: Enter the client's agreement or terms.
24. **Contract Signed**: Select whether the contract has been signed.
25. **Signature**: Enter the signature of the client or representative.
