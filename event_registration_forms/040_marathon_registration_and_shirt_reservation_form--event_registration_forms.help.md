# Marathon Registration and Shirt Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marathon registration and shirt reservation form is a simple form designed to collect participant information and t-shirt preferences for the upcoming marathon event. Please read through this guide to understand how to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields on page 1.
2. Enter your email address in the email field on page 1.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field on page 1.
4. For the participant's name, please enter the name of the participant who will be attending the marathon.
5. Select your preferred t-shirt size on page 2.
6. Choose your gender on page 2.
7. Select your preferred t-shirt color on page 2.
8. Enter your age on page 3.
9. Select your t-shirt size on page 3.
10. Choose your t-shirt color on page 3.
11. Select your t-shirt design preference on page 3.
12. Enter the name of the participant's emergency contact on page 4.
13. Select the participant's diet preferences on page 4.
14. Enter the participant's occupation on page 4.
15. Confirm your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant who will be attending the marathon.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred t-shirt size.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose your gender.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred t-shirt color.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred t-shirt size.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred t-shirt color.
* **T-Shirt Design** (`t_shirt_design`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your preferred t-shirt design.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant who will be attending the marathon.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required: false): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, required: false): Choose the gender of the participant.
* **Shirt Type** (`shirt_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of shirt you prefer.
* **Participant Diet** (`participant_diet`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the participant's diet preferences.
* **Participant Occupation** (`participant_occupation`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's occupation.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant's emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your selections and preferences.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the event organizers for assistance.
