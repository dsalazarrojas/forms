# Cultural Festival Vendor Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Vendor Intake Form is designed to gather information from vendors who want to participate in the cultural festival. This form aims to provide festival organizers with the necessary details to promote the vendors and their products effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: vendor information, product description, price, and contact information.
2. Add any additional product features that you want to showcase at the festival.
3. Provide any social media links where your audience can find you online.
4. Add any additional details about your products and yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Information** (required)
- **Vendor Info** (text): Please enter your name and any other information about yourself that you'd like to share with the festival organizers and audience.
### 2. **Product Information** (required)
- **Product-Info** (text): Describe your products in 2-3 sentences, including their type and any unique features.
### 3. **Contact Information** (required)
- **Contact Info** (text): Enter a valid email address and a 10-digit US phone number.
### 4. **Product Features** (optional)
- **Product Features** (select multiple): Choose any of the following options that describe your products: Cultural Food, Traditional Artisanal Products, Music, or Cultural Performers.
### 5. **Social Media Links** (optional)
- **Social Media Links** (select one): Choose one of the following options: Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube.
### 6. **Image** (optional)
- **Image** (text): You can add a brief description of your image or product.
### 7. **Website** (optional)
- **Website** (text): Enter your website URL.
### 8. **Email** (required)
- **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address.
### 9. **Phone** (required)
- **Phone** (text): Enter a 10-digit US phone number.
### 10. **Address** (optional)
- **Address** (text): Enter your business address.
### 11. **Product Description** (optional)
- **Product Description** (text): Describe your products in more detail, including their features and benefits.
### 12. **Price** (optional)
- **Price** (number): Enter the price of your product.
### 13. **Additional Tips**
- **Please make sure to fill in the required fields to ensure your vendor information is complete.**
- **You can add any additional details that you think would be helpful for the festival organizers and audience to know.**
