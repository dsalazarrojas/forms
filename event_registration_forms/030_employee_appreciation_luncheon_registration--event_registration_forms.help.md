# Employee Appreciation Luncheon Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Employee Appreciation Luncheon. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending, your dietary restrictions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your current Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the "Email" field.
4. Indicate whether you will be attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Will Attend" field.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, select the corresponding option in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
6. If you have any special preferences or requests, type them in the "Special Preferences" field.
7. Provide any additional dietary notes or comments in the "Dietary Notes" field.
8. Indicate whether you will dress formally ("Yes") or informally ("No") in response to "Will Dress" field.
9. Specify your expected arrival time by selecting "Early", "Late", or "Unknown" in the "Will Arrive" field.
10. If applicable, provide more details about your collar preference in the "Collar" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name** (text, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your ID card or official documentation.

### 2. **Job Title** (text, required: false)
Enter your current job title or role within the company.

### 3. **Email** (email, required: false)
Enter your official company email address for communication purposes.

### 4. **Company** (text, required: false)
Enter your current company name or department.

### 5. **Will Attend** (select_multiple, required: false)
Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, "No" otherwise.

### 6. **RSVP Status** (select_one, required: false)
Select "Yes" if you have already RSVP'd, "Maybe" if you are still unsure, or "No" if you won't be attending.

### 7. **Dietary Restrictions** (select_multiple, required: false)
Select any dietary restrictions you may have, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, Pescatarian, or Kosher.

### 8. **Special Preferences** (text, required: false)
If you have any special requests or preferences, please type them here.

### 9. **Dietary Notes** (note, required: false)
Provide any additional dietary notes or comments, if applicable.

### 10. **Will Dress** (select_one, required: false)
Select "Yes" if you plan to dress formally, "No" otherwise.

### 11. **Collar** (text, required: false)
Enter your collar preference, if applicable.

### 12. **Will Arrive** (select_multiple, required: false)
Select "Early", "Late", or "Unknown" to indicate your expected arrival time.

### 13. **Arrive Time** (time, required: false)
If applicable, select your expected arrival time.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling them out will help the event organizers better plan and accommodate your needs.
