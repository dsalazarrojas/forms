# Access Badge Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate a smooth and secure visit experience. It collects essential information from visitors, including their personal details, visit purpose, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full name**, which is required to identify you.
2. Enter your **Email address**, which is also required and helps us contact you for any visit-related updates or follow-ups.
3. Provide your **Phone number**, which is optional but recommended in case we need to contact you during your visit.
4. Specify your **Company or organization**, which is required to understand your affiliation.
5. Choose your **Type of visitor**, which categorizes your visit purpose.
6. Select the **Check-in date** and **Check-in time**, which are both required to track your visit.
7. Enter the **Name of the person or department you are visiting**, which is required to ensure you are visiting the correct personnel.
8. Specify the **Purpose of visit**, which helps us understand your visit goals.
9. Select the **Areas you need access to**, which is required to ensure you have the necessary clearance.
10. Choose your **Expected duration of visit**, which helps us plan for your visit.
11. Specify the **Type of identification presented**, which is required for security purposes.
12. Indicate if you have a **Vehicle on the premises**, which is required to ensure we are aware of any vehicle-related risks.
13. If applicable, enter your **License plate number** for vehicle tracking.
14. Confirm that you agree to **follow all site safety and security rules during your visit**, which is a required checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone number**: Provide your phone number for emergency contact or follow-up.
* **Company or organization**: Specify your company or organization to understand your affiliation.
* **Type of visitor**: Choose your visitor category to classify your visit.
* **Check-in date**: Enter the date you arrive at the facility.
* **Check-in time**: Enter the time you arrive at the facility.
* **Name of the person or department you are visiting**: Identify the personnel you will be visiting.
* **Purpose of visit**: Choose the reason for your visit to help us plan.
* **Areas you need access to**: Specify the areas you will be accessing.
* **Expected duration of visit**: Choose your visit duration to help us plan.
* **Type of identification presented**: Specify the type of ID you are presenting.
* **Vehicle on premises**: Indicate if you have a vehicle on the premises.
* **License plate number**: If you have a vehicle, enter its license plate number.
* **I agree to follow all site safety and security rules during my visit**: Confirm your agreement to abide by site rules.
