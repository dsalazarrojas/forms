# Geography BEE Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geography BEE Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect relevant information from students participating in a geography-themed event or competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your grade level in the "Grade Level" field.
3. Choose your parent's permission for participation in the "Parent Permission" field.
4. Enter your parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
5. Enter your parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
6. Enter your school's name in the "School Name" field.
7. Enter your school's address in the "School Address" field.
8. Enter your student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
9. Select the date for the event in the "Event Date" field.
10. Select the time for the event in the "Event Time" field.
11. Enter your school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
12. Enter your school's email address in the "School Email" field.
13. Enter your school's address again in the "School Address 2" field (this might be a redundant field, but follow the instructions for consistency).
14. Add any additional comments or remarks in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's name as it appears on their identification or official documents.
- **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, required/optional): Select your student's grade level (e.g., 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, etc.).
- **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your parent's permission for the student's participation (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your parent's email address.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your parent's phone number.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's name.
- **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's address.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required/optional): Enter your student's grade level (note: same field as Grade Level, use for consistency).
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time for the event.
- **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's phone number.
- **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your school's email address.
- **School Address 2** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's address again (this might be redundant, but follow the instructions for consistency).
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
