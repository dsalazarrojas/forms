# grappling_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grappling event registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants for the sake of event management. It is used to manage registrations for the grappling event, ensuring that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your Participant Information on the first page.
2.  Indicate whether you have participated in grappling events before.
3.  Choose your experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
4.  Check if you have read and accepted the waiver.
5.  Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
*   **Participant Info**: This is where you enter your name and any other identifying details as requested.
    *   Required Field: Yes
    *   Hint: This information is used solely for the purpose of registering you for the event. Ensure it's accurate and up to date.
*   **Participant Name**: Enter your name, as this will help the event management team identify you properly.
*   **Additional Info**: Any other participant details you want to share with the event management team may be entered here.

### Divisions
*   **Divisions**: Select 'Yes' if you are participating in divisions, 'No' otherwise.
    *   Required Field: No
    *   Hint: This field helps us understand if you will be participating in divisions or not. Please answer truthfully.
*   **Select 'Yes' if you will be participating in divisions.**

### Experience Level
*   **Experience Level**: Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced based on your grappling experience.
    *   Required Field: No
    *   Hint: Be honest about your grappling experience to ensure an appropriate match-up for you during the event.
*   **Beginner**: Select this if you have little grappling experience.
*   **Intermediate**: Choose this if your grappling experience falls between beginner and advanced levels.
*   **Advanced**: Select this if you have considerable grappling experience.

### Waiver
*   **Waiver**: Check 'I accept' if you have read and accepted the waiver.
    *   Required Field: No
    *   Hint: Please ensure you have read and understood the waiver before checking this box.
*   **I accept**: This confirms that you have read and accepted the waiver.
*   **Decline**: Choose this if you haven't read or accepted the waiver yet.

### Submit
*   **Submit**: Click this to complete your registration.
    *   Required Field: Yes
    *   Hint: Clicking this will finalize your registration, ensuring you are enrolled in the grappling event.
*   **Complete Registration**: Clicking this button will finalize your registration for the grappling event.
