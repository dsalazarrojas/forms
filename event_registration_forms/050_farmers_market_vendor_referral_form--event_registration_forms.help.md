# farmers_market_vendor_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from farmers market vendors who are being referred by existing customers or partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Each field marked with an asterisk (\*) is required. Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is accurate and complete.
2. **Provide accurate contact information**: Ensure that the email addresses and phone numbers you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Select the correct category**: Choose the category that best describes the vendor's products or services.
4. **Be detailed with the referral information**: Provide as much information as possible about the referrer, including their name, organization, title, and phone number.
5. **Describe the relationship**: Select the correct relationship between the vendor and the referrer from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Vendor Information**
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): 
	+ Enter a brief description of the vendor, such as their name and a brief description of their products or services.

### 2. **Vendor Name**
* **vendor_name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the vendor, such as John Doe or Jane Smith.

### 3. **Vendor Email**
* **vendor_email** (`vendor_email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of the vendor, such as `john.doe@example.com`.

### 4. **Vendor Phone**
* **vendor_phone** (`vendor_phone`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of the vendor.

### 5. **Vendor Address**
* **vendor_address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the address of the vendor, such as 123 Main St.

### 6. **Vendor Category**
* **vendor_category** (`vendor_category`, `select_one`, required: true):
	+ Select the category that best describes the vendor's products or services, such as Farm Fresh Produce or Prepared Foods.

### 7. **Referral Information**
* **Referral Information** (`referral_info`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the referral, such as their name or contact information.

### 8. **Referral Name**
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the referrer, such as Jane Doe.

### 9. **Referral Organization**
* **Referral Organization** (`referral_organization`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the organization or company of the referrer, such as John Doe Inc.

### 10. **Referral Title**
* **Referral Title** (`referral_title`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the title of the referrer, such as Manager or CEO.

### 11. **Referral Phone**
* **Referral Phone** (`referral_phone`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of the referrer.

### 12. **Referral Email**
* **Referral Email** (`referral_email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter the email address of the referrer, such as jane.doe@example.com.

### 13. **Additional Referral Information**
* **Additional Referral Information** (`additional_referral_info`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional information about the referral, such as their contact information or any other relevant details.

### 14. **Relationship Between Vendor and Referral**
* **Relationship Between Vendor and Referral** (`vendor_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select the relationship between the vendor and the referrer from the options provided, such as Primary Contact or Second Contact.

### 15. **Additional Information**
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional information about the vendor or the referral, such as their relationship history or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure accurate and complete submission.
* Double-check the email addresses and phone numbers you provide for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
