# Theater Performance Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Theater Performance Attendance Form" is used to record attendance information for theater performances. This form helps track the number of people who attended a specific show and provides additional details such as the date of the performance, the theater company involved, and any notes or remarks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct performance from the "performance_id" dropdown menu.
2. If required, enter the date of the performance into the "attendance_date" field.
3. If required, enter the number of people who attended the performance into the "number_of_people" field.
4. Select all applicable theater companies that participated in the performance from the "theater_company" multiple-choice field.
5. If desired, record any additional comments or remarks in the "notes" field.
6. If required, enter your email address into the "email" field.
7. If required, enter your contact phone number into the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### attendance_date
* Label: attendance_date
* Type: date
* Required: false
* Explanation: Record the date of the performance.

### number_of_people
* Label: number_of_people
* Type: number
* Required: false
* Explanation: Record the number of people who attended the performance.

### performance_id
* Label: performance_id
* Type: select_one
* Required: false
* Explanation: Select the correct performance from the dropdown menu.

### theater_company
* Label: theater_company
* Type: select_multiple
* Required: false
* Explanation: Select all applicable theater companies that participated in the performance.

### notes
* Label: notes
* Type: note
* Required: false
* Explanation: Record any additional comments or remarks.

### email
* Label: email
* Type: email
* Required: false
* Explanation: Record your email address.

### phone
* Label: phone
* Type: text
* Required: false
* Explanation: Record your contact phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure you have the correct performance ID selected in the "performance_id" field.
* Double-check the "attendance_date" field for accuracy.
* Be thorough when selecting the "theater_company" field to ensure all participating companies are listed.
