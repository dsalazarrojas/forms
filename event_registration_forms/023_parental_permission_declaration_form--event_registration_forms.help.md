# Parental Permission Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians to declare their permission for an event. This includes their personal details, information about their child, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 13 fields, each with its own specific purpose. Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Provide your first name.
2. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
3. **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
4. **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. **Time of event** (`time_of_event`, time, optional): Note the time of the event.
6. **Child's name** (`name_of_child`, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
7. **Relationship to child** (`relationship_to_child`, text, optional): Describe your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
8. **Contact preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, SMS).
9. **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
10. **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, other).
11. **Emergency contact information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact person's details.
12. **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Choose to grant or deny consent for the event.
13. **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your child's participation in the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, even if you have opted out of other fields.
- Use the correct date and time format for your date of birth and time of event fields.
- Select all relevant contact preferences to ensure you receive important updates about the event.
- Fill in the emergency contact person's details carefully to ensure they can be reached in case of an emergency.
