# hiring_event_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event registration information from potential attendees for a hiring event. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant data about the event, company profile, workforce, and business plans from interested individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter event data in the "Event Data" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your company's profile in the "Company Profile" field.
3. List your workforce in the "Workforce" field.
4. Enter your business plans in the "Business Plans" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Data** (`event_data`, text, not required): Please enter a brief description of the event you are registering for, including the name and date of the event.
* **Company Profile** (`company_profile`, text, not required): Please provide a brief description of your company, including its name, location, and a short history.
* **Workforce** (`workforce`, text, not required): Please list your company's workforce, including the number of employees and their job titles.
* **Business Plans** (`business_plans`, text, not required): Please enter a brief description of your business plans, including your company's goals and objectives for the next quarter or year.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* The information collected through this form will be used to facilitate the hiring event and provide attendees with relevant information about participating companies.
