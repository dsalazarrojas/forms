# Mastermind Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mastermind Event Registration form is used to collect information from participants for events organized by Mastermind. This form helps Mastermind track participant registrations and manage event data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select the type of event you are registering for in the "Event Type" field.
7. Select your participant type (Attendee, Speaker, or Staff) in the "Participant Type" field.
8. If applicable, select your consent options (yes or no) in the "Do you consent" field.
9. If applicable, select your agreement options (yes or no) in the "Agree and Term and Condition" field.
10. If applicable, select your agreement options (yes or no) in the "Agree to Terms" field.
11. In the "Terms and Conditions" field, provide any additional comments or information.
12. In the "Participant Data" fields, provide any additional information about your participant data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Type (6)**: Select the type of event you are registering for (Mastermind Session, Workshop, or Community Event).
* **Participant Type (7)**: Select your participant type (Attendee, Speaker, or Staff).
* **Do you consent (8)**: If you are 18 or older, select "Yes" if you consent to the collection and use of your data.
* **Agree and Term and Condition (9)**: If you are 18 or older, select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Terms (10)**: If you are 18 or older, select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions (11)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your agreement.
* **Participant Data (12)**, **Participant Data Confidential (13)**, **Participant Data Public (14)**, **Participant Data Private (15)**, **Participant Data Sensitive (16)**, **Participant Data Unsure (17)**: Enter any additional information about your participant data.
* **Participant Data Consent (18)**: If you are 18 or older, select "Yes" if you consent to the collection and use of your participant data.
* **Participant Data Confidential Agreed (19)**, **Participant Data Public Agreed (20)**, **Participant Data Private Agreed (21)**, **Participant Data Sensitive Agreed (22)**, **Participant Data Unsure Agreed (23)**: Select "Yes" if you agree to the collection and use of your participant data.
* **Participant Data Confidential Public (24)**, **Participant Data Private Public (25)**: Enter any additional information about your participant data that is public or private.
