# open_mic_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "open_mic_registration_form" is used to collect information about upcoming performances for an event. This includes details such as the event date, start and end times, performance length, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details in the "event_details" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number in the "email" and "phone" fields respectively.
3. Indicate your availability to perform in the "availability" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Confirm your event details in the "event_details_2" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.
6. Select the category of the event in the "category" field.
7. Provide the event date in the "event_date" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
8. Enter the start and end times of the performance in the "start_time" and "end_time" fields respectively.
9. Enter the length of the performance in the "performance_length" field.
10. Enter any additional contact details in the "contact_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_details** (text): Enter the details of the event.
* **performer_details** (text): Enter your performer's details.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address. (required)
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number. (required)
* **availability** (select multiple): Indicate your availability to perform. (required)
* **event_details_2** (select one): Confirm your event details. (required)
* **notes** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments. (optional)
* **category** (select one): Select the category of the event. (required)
* **event_date** (date): Enter the event date in MM/DD/YYYY format. (required)
* **start_time** (time): Enter the start time of the performance. (required)
* **end_time** (time): Enter the end time of the performance. (required)
* **performance_length** (number): Enter the length of the performance. (required)
* **contact_details** (text): Enter any additional contact details. (optional)
