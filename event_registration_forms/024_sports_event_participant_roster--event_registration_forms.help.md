# Sports Event Participant Roster - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Event Participant Roster form is used to record participant details for an event, including participant status, contact information, and other relevant details. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, completion process, and each field's explanation to help you accurately complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the event details section to understand event specifics.
2. Enter participant details in the designated sections.
3. Select the participant roster status in the participant roster section.
4. Provide event, participant, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event to be registered.
* **Event Date** (`event_details_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_details_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the participant's date of birth.
### Participant Roster
* **Participant Roster** (`participant_roster`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether the participant is part of the event's roster.

## Tips
- Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate records.
- Use the participant roster status to indicate whether the participant is participating in the event.
- Double-check the event details to ensure accurate information is provided.
