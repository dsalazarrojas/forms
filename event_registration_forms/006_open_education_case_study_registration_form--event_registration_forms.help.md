# open_education_case_study_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The open_education_case_study_registration_form is used to collect information from participants in an open education case study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Participant Address" field.
5. Specify your city in the "Participant City" field.
6. Enter your state in the "Participant State" field.
7. Enter your country in the "Participant Country" field.
8. Answer whether you have any education in the "Participant Education" field.
9. Select your area of interest for the case study in the "Participant Interest Areas" field.
10. List the instruments you will use for the case study in the "Participant Instrument" field.
11. Choose the type of event this case study is for in the "Event Type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address where the researchers can contact you.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where the researchers can contact you.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current address for the researchers to send information to you.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you live.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where you live.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter the country where you live.
* **Participant Education** (`participant_education`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you have any formal education, otherwise "No".
* **Participant Interest Areas** (`participant_interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the areas of interest for the case study (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Participant Instrument** (`participant_instrument`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the instrument(s) you will use for the case study.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the event type for this case study (e.g., "Yes", "No").
