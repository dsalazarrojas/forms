# Community Dialogue Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information about you to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the Community Dialogue Series events. Please take a few minutes to complete this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details such as your first name, last name, and email address.
2.  Select the dialogue series you are interested in participating in.
3.  Choose your preferred session time.
4.  Indicate how you plan to attend the event.
5.  Share your previous dialogue experience.
6.  Specify the topics of interest to you.
7.  If necessary, provide any accessibility needs or dietary restrictions.
8.  Indicate whether you need childcare or transportation assistance.
9.  Let us know how you heard about the series.
10.  Finally, specify your goals and expectations from attending the dialogue series and express your interest in facilitating future dialogues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Participant First Name</u> (`participant_first_name`, text, required)
• Your legal first name

### <u>Last Name</u> (`participant_last_name`, text, required)
• Your legal last name

### <u>Email Address</u> (`email_address`, email, required)
• For event communications

### <u>Phone Number</u> (`phone_number`, text, optional)
• Primary contact number

### <u>Organization or Affiliation</u> (`organization`, text, optional)
• Any organization you represent

### <u>Dialogue Series Selection</u> (`dialogue_series`, select_one, required)
• Which series are you registering for? (Select one: Racial Equity Dialogue, Community Policing Discussion, Youth Engagement Series, Housing and Development, Environmental Justice, Economic Opportunity, Full Series Pass)

### <u>Session Time Preference</u> (`session_preference`, select_one, required)
• When do you prefer to attend? (Select one: Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons, Weekday Evenings, Weekend Mornings, Weekend Afternoons)

### <u>Attendance Type</u> (`attendance_type`, select_one, required)
• How will you attend? (Select one: In Person, Virtual, Hybrid)

### <u>Previous Dialogue Experience</u> (`dialogue_experience`, select_one, required)
• Have you participated in dialogues before? (Select one: First Time, Some Experience, Regular Participant, Facilitator)

### <u>Topics of Interest</u> (`topics_of_interest`, select_multiple, required)
• What topics matter most to you? (Select multiple: Social Justice, Community Building, Policy Reform, Grassroots Organizing, Conflict Resolution, Youth Issues, Senior Issues)

### <u>Accessibility Needs</u> (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional)
• Do you require any accommodations?

### <u>Dietary Restrictions</u> (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional)
• If refreshments are provided

### <u>Childcare Needed</u> (`childcare_needed`, select_one, required)
• Do you need childcare services? (Select one: True, False)

### <u>Transportation Assistance Needed</u> (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required)
• Do you need help getting to the event? (Select one: True, False)

### <u>How Did You Hear About This Series</u> (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional)
• Where did you learn about us? (Select one: Website, Social Media, Email Newsletter, Friend or Colleague, Community Organization, Flyer or Poster, Other)

### <u>Goals and Expectations</u> (`goals_expectations`, text, optional)
• What do you hope to gain?

### <u>Interested in Facilitating</u> (`facilitator_interest`, select_one, required)
• Would you like to be a facilitator? (Select one: True, False, Maybe in Future)

## Tips

*   Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
*   If you need help with any part of the form, feel free to ask.
*   You can reach out to us with any questions or concerns.
*   Thank you for participating in the Community Dialogue Series!
