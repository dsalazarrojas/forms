# Music Festival Vendor Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for music festival vendors to update their registration and contact information. If you have already been registered as a vendor at the music festival, use this form to renew your registration and make any necessary updates to your contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to check the required fields (none are marked as required in the form schema, but please ensure you complete all fields for best results).
3. Double-check your information for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **music festival vendor renewal form** (`1`, `text`, not required): This is the title of the form, which is not required to be filled out but can be used for reference.
* **vendor name** (`2`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your business or entity.
* **vendor email** (`3`, `email`, not required): Enter your business or entity's email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **vendor phone** (`4`, `text`, not required): Enter your business or entity's phone number.
* **vendor address** (`5`, `text`, not required): Enter your business or entity's address.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our team for assistance.
* If you need help with renewing your registration, please contact us to inquire about our renewal process.
