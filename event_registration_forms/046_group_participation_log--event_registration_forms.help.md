# Group Participation Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Participation Log form is designed to collect information about group events, including event registrations, participants, and event details. This form is meant to be filled out by group organizers or administrators to record and track event participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Registrations Form (page 1) with the required information about the event, including the event title and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the Participants Form (page 2) and enter the participant's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. On the Attendance page (page 3), record the participant's attendance status.
4. On the Date and Time pages (pages 4 and 5), select the event date and time.
5. On the Event Date and Event Time pages (pages 6 and 7), select the event date and time again (note: this is a duplication of pages 5, but for completeness).
6. Finally, on the Additional Information pages (pages 8 and 9), enter the participant's email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Registrations Form (Page 1)** (`event_registrations_form`, text, optional)
Enter the event title and any other relevant details in this form.
### 2. **Participants Form (Page 2)** (`participants_form`, text, optional)
Enter the participant's details here.
### 3. **Participant Details (Page 3)** (`participant_details`, text, optional)
Enter participant's details here.
### 4. **Attendance (Page 4)** (`attendance_form`, text, optional)
Record the participant's attendance status.
### 5. **Date (Page 5)** (`date`, date, optional)
Select the event date.
### 6. **Time (Page 6)** (`time`, time, optional)
Select the event time.
### 7. **Event Date (Page 7)** (`event_date`, date, optional)
Select the event date again (note: this is a duplication of Page 5).
### 8. **Event Time (Page 8)** (`event_time`, time, optional)
Select the event time again (note: this is a duplication of Page 6).
### 9. **Email (Page 9)** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter the participant's email address.
### 10. **Phone (Page 10)** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your group administrator.

Note: The form seems to have some unnecessary duplications (pages 7 and 8, and pages 5 and 6), which can be simplified or removed for a better user experience.
