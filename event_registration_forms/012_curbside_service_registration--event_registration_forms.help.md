# Curbside Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curbside Service Registration form is used to register customers for curbside service, where a representative will visit a customer's home or business to provide assistance. This service is ideal for customers who are unable to visit a physical location or need support with a specific issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Confirm your Email address in the Email Confirmation field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Confirm your Phone number in the Phone Confirmation field.
5. Select the Event Date and Start and End times for the curbside service visit.
6. Enter the Address where you would like the curbside service representative to visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### Last name
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your valid Email address.

### Email Confirmation
* **Confirm email** (email, required: false): Confirm your Email address for verification purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your valid Phone number.

### Phone Confirmation
* **Confirm phone** (text, required: false): Confirm your Phone number for verification purposes.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (date, required: false): Select the date for the curbside service visit.

### Start time
* **Start time** (time, required: false): Select the start time for the curbside service visit.

### End time
* **End time** (time, required: false): Select the end time for the curbside service visit.

### Address
* **Address** (text, required: false): Enter the address where you would like the curbside service representative to visit.

## Tips
