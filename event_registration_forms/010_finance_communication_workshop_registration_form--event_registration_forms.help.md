# finance_communication_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The finance_communication_workshop_registration_form is designed to collect user registration information for the finance communication workshop. This form is intended to help organizers and administrators gather essential details from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Name.
2. Provide your Email (if you want to be contacted about the event).
3. Enter your Phone (if you want to be contacted by phone).
4. Choose whether you will attend the Workshop (Yes/No).
5. Select your Session Time (if applicable).
6. Choose the Session Date (if applicable).
7. Provide your Registration Date.
8. Add any Comments (if you have any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes. This will be used if the organizers need to contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. This will be used if the organizers need to contact you by phone.
* **Workshop**: Choose "Yes" if you plan to attend the workshop, or "No" if you cannot.
* **Session Time**: Choose your preferred session time if it's applicable. If not, leave it blank.
* **Session Date**: Choose your preferred session date if it's applicable. If not, leave it blank.
* **Registration Date**: This field is used to record the date you registered for the workshop. Please enter the current date.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about the workshop or event. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
- If you have any doubts or questions about the form or the workshop, contact the organizers directly.
- Registration information will be reviewed and managed by the event team. They will contact you about any issues or further instructions.
