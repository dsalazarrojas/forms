# Hunting Lodge Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hunting Lodge Accommodation Form is designed to help users book and manage their accommodation requests for the hunting lodge. This form collects essential information about the user's stay, including their name, dates of stay, guest count, room preferences, payment details, and any special requests or instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Select your Checkin Date and Checkout Date using the date picker.
3. Enter the number of guests in your party in the Guest Count field.
4. Choose your preferred Room Type from the options provided (Double Room, Single Room, or Suite).
5. Select the duration of your stay in the Accommodation Length field, or enter a custom length in the Accommodation Length Other field if necessary.
6. If you have any special requests or instructions, type them in the Message field.
7. Choose your preferred payment frequency (Every Week, Every Two Weeks, or Every Month) and payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other) in their respective fields.
8. Enter any additional payment details, such as the deposit amount or total cost, as required.
9. Submit the form to complete your accommodation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Checkin Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Select your checkin date using the date picker.
* **Checkout Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Select your checkout date using the date picker.
* **Guest Count** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Room Type** (`6`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred room type (Double Room, Single Room, or Suite).
* **Accommodation Length** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the duration of your stay (1, 2, or 3 nights).
* **Accommodation Length Other** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter a custom accommodation length if necessary.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`11`, `note`, optional): Type any special requests or instructions here.
* **Accommodation Request** (`12`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any special accommodation requests (Room Service, Food and Drink, or Outdoor Activities).
* **Accommodation Request Other** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional accommodation requests.
* **Payment Method** (`14`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Payment Method Other** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Total Cost** (`16`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of your stay.
* **First Payment Date** (`17`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your first payment.
* **Second Payment Date** (`18`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your second payment.
* **Third Payment Date** (`19`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your third payment.
* **Fourth Payment Date** (`20`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your fourth payment.
* **Payment Frequency** (`21`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your preferred payment frequency (Every Week, Every Two Weeks, or Every Month).
* **Payment Frequency Other** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment frequency details.
* **Deposit Amount** (`24`, `number`, optional): Enter the deposit amount.
* **Total Cost Other** (`25`, `number`, optional): Enter any additional total cost details.

Note that some fields are marked as optional, but it's essential to fill out all required fields (marked as required) to ensure your accommodation request is processed correctly.
