# photography_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a photography workshop. It gathers essential details such as name, email, phone number, and payment method to manage workshop attendance and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Name and Email Address to enable us to contact you efficiently.
2. Provide your Participant Phone Number so that we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
3. Specify the Workshop Date and Time for the workshop you are attending.
4. Choose your preferred Payment Method (Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Cash).
5. Enter the Payment Amount you are willing to pay for the workshop.
6. Optionally, include any Participant Notes if you have any specific requirements or requests.
7. Select whether you can attend the workshop (Yes/No).
8. Provide any additional Workshop Comments if you have any thoughts or suggestions.
9. Rate the workshop (1-5 stars) to help us improve our future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Workshop Details** (`photography_workshop_registration_form`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of the workshop you are interested in attending. This could be a few sentences summarizing the event's main focus or a specific skill you are looking to learn.
### 2. **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, optional)
Please enter your name as it should appear on the workshop materials and communications.
### 3. **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address so that we can keep you updated on any workshop changes, announcements, or follow-up information.
### 4. **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, optional)
Provide your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
### 5. **Workshop Date** (`Workshop Date`, date, optional)
Select the date of the workshop you plan to attend.
### 6. **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, time, optional)
Choose the time of the workshop you are attending.
### 7. **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional)
Select how you plan to pay for the workshop. This could be Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Cash.
### 8. **Payment Amount** (`Payment Amount`, number, optional)
Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the workshop.
### 9. **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, note, optional)
Provide any additional information regarding your participation or requirements.
### 10. **Workshop Attendance** (`Workshop Attendance`, select_multiple, optional)
Indicate your ability to attend the workshop by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
### 11. **Workshop Comments** (`Workshop Comments`, text, optional)
Share any thoughts, suggestions, or recommendations regarding the workshop.
### 12. **Workshop Rating** (`Workshop Rating`, number, optional)
Rate the workshop's quality (1-5 stars) to help us improve our future events.
