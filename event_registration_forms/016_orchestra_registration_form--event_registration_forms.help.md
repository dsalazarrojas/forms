# Orchestra Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orchestra Registration Form is designed to collect information from users about their musical skills, experience, and practice habits in order to facilitate better event organization and participant matching.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself.
2. Select your instrument from the available options.
3. Choose your skill level, whether you are a beginner, intermediate, or advanced player.
4. Indicate your practice experience, including the duration and frequency of your practice sessions.
5. Choose your experience with orchestras, from never to professional.
6. Select the specific areas you have experience with in the orchestra (e.g., conductor, musician, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, select_one, required/optional): Select the instrument you play from the available options.
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your skill level, whether you are a beginner, intermediate, or advanced player.
* **Practice Experience** (`practice_experience`, time, required/optional): Indicate the length of time you have practiced your instrument for.
* **Practice Frequency** (`practice_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you practice your instrument, from rarely to frequently.
* **Practice Duration** (`practice_duration`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate how long you usually practice for each session, from less than an hour to several hours.
* **Orchestra Experience** (`orchestra_experience`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your experience with orchestras, from never to professional.
