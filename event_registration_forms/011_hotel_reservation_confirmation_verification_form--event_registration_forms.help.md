# hotel_reservation_confirmation_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests for hotel reservation confirmation and verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, carefully fill in all required fields and ensure that all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Confirmation** (1, text, optional): Enter the confirmation of your reservation.
* **Guest Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your guest's full name (First name and last name).
* **Reservation ID** (3, text, optional): Enter your reservation ID number.
* **Checkin Date** (4, date, optional): Enter your checkin date.
* **Checkout Date** (5, date, optional): Enter your checkout date.
* **Guest Contact Info** (6, select multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Guest phone or Guest email).
* **Hotel Information** (7, text, optional): Enter your hotel address or website.
* **Payment Method** (8, select one, optional): Choose your preferred payment method (Bank, PayPal, or Credit card).
* **Room Service** (9, text, optional): Enter your room number and bed type.
* **Notes** (10, note, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share with the hotel.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the hotel directly.
