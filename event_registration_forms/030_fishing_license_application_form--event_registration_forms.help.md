# Fishing License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for a fishing license. To complete this form, please provide the necessary details for your license application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form fields with your personal details.
2. Select the correct license type (Commercial, Recreational, or Other).
3. If you are applying for a new license, select "New" from the Application Type field. If you are renewing or replacing an existing license, select "Renewal" or "Replacement".
4. Enter your address, phone number, and emergency contact information (if applicable).
5. Confirm your application by selecting "Yes" from the Confirm field and providing additional details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **License Type**: Select the type of license you are applying for (Commercial, Recreational, or Other).
* **Fishing Experience**: Enter your level of fishing experience (not applicable for this form).
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been fishing (not applicable for this form).
* **Fish License Expiration**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Application Type**: Select the type of application you are making (New, Renewal, or Replacement).
* **Boat Registration**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Boat License Number**: Not applicable for this form.
* **License Number**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Fishing Location**: Enter the location where you plan to fish.
* **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional information about your application.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Confirm**: Select "Yes" to confirm your application details.
* **Confirm Type**: Select the type of confirmation you are making ("Yes" to confirm the application details).
* **Confirm Date**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Confirm Location**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Confirm Address**: Not applicable for this form.
* **Confirm Phone**: Not applicable for this form.

This guide should help end-users complete the fishing license application form accurately and efficiently.
