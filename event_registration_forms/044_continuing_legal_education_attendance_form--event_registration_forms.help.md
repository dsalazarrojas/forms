# Continuing Legal Education Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for attorneys to register their attendance to Continuing Legal Education (CLE) sessions. It captures essential information to verify the authenticity of the CLE credits earned.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the primary state for CLE credit from the provided options.
3. Enter the details of the CLE session, including the title, date, and provider/Organization name.
4. Enter the total hours and hours for ethics and bias elimination credits.
5. Enter the verification code provided by the instructor during the session.
6. Select "I Certify My Attendance" to attest your attendance.
7. Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attorney Full Name** (`attorney_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your attorney license.
* **State Bar Number** (`state_bar_number`, text, required): Include the state prefix (e.g., NY-123456).
* **Primary State for CLE Credit** (`registration_state`, select_one, required): Choose the state for which you are claiming CLE credit.
* **Title of CLE Session** (`session_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the CLE session as provided by the provider.
* **Date of Session** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the CLE session.
* **Provider/Organization Name** (`session_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the provider or organization that offered the CLE session.
* **Total CLE Hours** (`total_cle_hours`, number, required): Enter the total hours of the CLE session.
* **Ethics Credit Hours (Included in Total)** (`ethics_hours`, number, required): Enter the hours of ethics credit earned during the session.
* **Elimination of Bias Hours (Included in Total)** (`bias_elimination_hours`, number, required): Enter the hours of bias elimination credit earned during the session.
* **Verification Code** (`session_verification_code`, text, required): Enter the verification code provided by the instructor during the session.
* **I Certify My Attendance** (`attorney_attestation`, select_one, required): Select "I Certify My Attendance" to attest your attendance.
* **Date of Attestation** (`attendance_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are attesting your attendance.
* **Attorney Digital Signature** (`digital_signature_attorney`, text, required): Enter your full name to sign the form.

This form is designed to be completed by attorneys to register their attendance to CLE sessions. Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
