# empowerment_movement_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Empowerment Movement Registration Form is used to register participants in empowerment movements. This form gathers information from participants, including their contact details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section, which includes their name, company, and title.
2. Enter their email addresses, phone numbers, and preferred dates and times.
3. Select the workshop topics that interest you.
4. Add any additional information as necessary.
5. Select the options as instructed in the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
6. Add any notes as required.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date the participant is attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time the participant is attending the workshop.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the participant.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, `text`, not required): Enter the company of the participant.
* **Workshop Topic** (`workshop_topic`, `text`, not required): Select the workshop topic that interests you.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, not required): Add any additional information as necessary.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, not required): Select one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any notes as necessary.
