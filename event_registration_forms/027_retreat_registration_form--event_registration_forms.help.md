# Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a retreat. Please fill out the required fields to provide your information and preferences for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on "Submit" at the end of the page. The form is divided into different sections, each with a clear label and a clear explanation of what the field is for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (text)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you. (Optional)
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Time of Birth (text)**: Enter the time of your birth in 24 hour format (e.g., 12:00). 
*   **How did you hear about this retreat? (select one)**: Choose how you heard about this retreat.
  + Facebook
  + Instagram
  + Twitter
  + Word of mouth
  + Online Search
* **What are your interests? (select multiple)**: Choose the activities you are interested in. 
  + Hiking
  + Biking
  + Camping
