# Invoice Processing Live Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Invoice Processing Live Demo Registration form is designed to collect registration information from users to participate in a live demo for invoice processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your Role from the "Role" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your Department in the "Department" field.
7. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Enter your Job Function in the "Job Function" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Optionally, enter a Location (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, **Required**: False): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Role (5)** (`select_one`, **Required**: True): Select your role from the dropdown menu:
	+ Admin
	+ Manager
* **Company (6)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your company name.
* **Department (7)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your department.
* **Job Title (8)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function (9)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Enter your job function.
* **Location (10)** (`text`, **Required**: False): Optionally, enter your location.
