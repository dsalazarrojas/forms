# Election Night Watch Party Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register and manage their attendance at an Election Night Watch Party. It is meant to be a simple and convenient way to provide party information and RSVPs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the Party Information field with your party details (if required).
3. Enter the Expected Number of Guests if you are attending with a group.
4. Select your RSVP response (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party Information** (`party_info`, text, optional): Enter your party details such as your name and any special requests.
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, number, optional): If you are attending with a group, enter the number of guests you will bring.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate you will attend the watch party, or "No" to decline.
