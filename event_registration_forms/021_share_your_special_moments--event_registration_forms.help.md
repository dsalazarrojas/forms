# share_your_special_moments - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sharing personal stories, experiences, and testimonials about a specific event or occasion. Please fill in the required fields to help create a meaningful and engaging story.

## How To Complete This Form
To share your special moments, simply fill out the form below. Each field is explained in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)**: Enter the name of the event or occasion you're sharing about.
* **Event Date (2)**: Enter the date of the event or occasion.
* **Event Time (3)**: Enter the time of the event or occasion.
* **Event Location (4)**: Enter the location of the event or occasion.
* **Event Details (5)**: Briefly describe the event or occasion.
* **Attendees (6)**: Indicate if others attended the event or occasion. Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Share Your Story (7)**: Share your personal story about the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Photo (8)**: If applicable, upload a photo related to the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Video (9)**: If applicable, share a video related to the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Audio (10)**: If applicable, share an audio recording related to the event or occasion.
* **Upload File (11)**: If applicable, upload a supporting file related to the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Link (12)**: Share a link related to the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Email (13)**: Share your email address.
* **Share Your Phone (14)**: Share your phone number.
* **Share Your Note (15)**: Add a note about the event or occasion.
* **Share Your Comment (16)**: Add a comment about the event or occasion.
* **Report (17)**: Select a report type (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **Feedback (18)**: Provide feedback about your experience. Choose from options 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
* **Rating (19)**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Score (20)**: Enter a score related to your experience.
* **Date (21)**: Enter the date you're sharing about.
* **Time (22)**: Enter the time you're sharing about.
* **Location (23)**: Enter the location you're sharing about.
* **Experience (24)**: Describe your experience.
* **Testimony (25)**: Share your testimony about the event or occasion.
