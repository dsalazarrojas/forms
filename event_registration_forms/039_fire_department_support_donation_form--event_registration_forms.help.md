# fire_department_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire_department_support_donation_form is a form designed to collect information from individuals who want to support their local fire department. This form allows users to input their personal details, including their name, contact information, and a message for the fire department. The form also includes a field to select the department they wish to support (Fire Department, Police Department, or Hospital).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. If you have any additional comments or remarks, you can type them in the "Comment" field.
7. Select the department you wish to support from the "Fire Department" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Donation Amount** (number, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the fire department.
* **Comment** (text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or remarks you have for the fire department.
* **Fire Department** (select_one, required/optional): Select the department you wish to support:
	+ Fire Department
	+ Police Department
	+ Hospital
* **Submit** (text, required/optional): Click this button to complete and submit your form.
* **Confirm** (note, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form and should be ignored.
 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
* If you need to support multiple departments, you will need to complete the form multiple times.
* The "Donation Amount" field expects numeric values, so be sure to enter a number.
* You can add comments in the "Comment" field, but they are not required.
