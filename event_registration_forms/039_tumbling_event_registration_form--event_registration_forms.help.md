# Tumbling Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering athletes for an event. Please fill out the form with your information and the event details to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required fields.
2. Make sure to review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for emergency purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Date of Birth** (`7`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Name** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`9`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`10`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event End Time** (`11`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Athlete Details** (`12`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as an athlete.
* **Event Location** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Athlete Name** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Athlete Event ID** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your athlete ID or registration number.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required" but this does not necessarily mean all fields are required. Please refer to the field description for specific requirements.
