# School Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from teachers and parents/guardians for school field trips. It's an important tool for school administrators or staff members to collect data efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Teacher Info**: Fill out the "Teacher Info" section with your name, which will serve as the primary contact person for this field trip.
2. **Student Info**: For each student participating in the field trip, fill out the "Student Info" section with the student's name, grade level, birthdate, and other relevant details.
3. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the emergency contact information for each student, including name, phone number, and address.
4. **Payment Details**: Enter the payment details for each student, including payment amount, payment method, and payment date.
5. **Parent Info**: For each parent/guardian, fill out the "Parent Info" section with their name, phone number, and address.
6. **Additional Student and Parent Information**: If needed, add any additional students and their corresponding parent/guardian information.
