# City Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to book a city tour, where you can select the type of tour, date, time, and other relevant details. The form will guide you through the required and optional fields to ensure you provide accurate and complete information for your tour booking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of tour that interests you (e.g., Walking, Bus, Bike, Food, Historical, Art and Culture, Nightlife).
3. Choose your preferred tour duration (e.g., 2 hours, 4 hours, 6 hours, or Full day - 8 hours).
4. Select your preferred tour date and time.
5. Enter the number of participants (including yourself).
6. Choose your preferred tour language and any accessibility or special needs you may have.
7. If applicable, select any special interests you have (e.g., Photography, Architecture, Local Cuisine).
8. Review the estimated price per person based on your tour selection.
9. Enter any special requests or comments you may have.
10. Finally, confirm that you accept the tour cancellation and liability terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address to receive the tour confirmation and any updates.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for tour coordination.
- **Nationality** (Nationality, text, optional): Enter your nationality if it's not already listed as an option.
- **Tour Type** (Tour Type, select_one, required): Select the type of tour that interests you.
- **Tour Duration** (Preferred Tour Duration, select_one, required): Choose the duration of your tour.
- **Preferred Tour Date** (Preferred Tour Date, date, required): Select the date of your tour.
- **Preferred Tour Time** (Preferred Tour Time, time, required): Choose the time of your tour.
- **Number of Participants** (Number of Participants, number, required): Enter the number of participants (including yourself).
- **Preferred Tour Language** (Preferred Tour Language, select_one, required): Select the language you'd like the tour to be conducted in.
- **Accessibility or Special Needs** (Accessibility or Special Needs, text, optional): Describe any accessibility or special needs you may have.
- **Special Interests** (Special Interests, select_multiple, optional): Select any special interests you have (e.g., Photography, Architecture, Local Cuisine).
- **Price per Person** (Price per Person, number, optional): Review the estimated price per person based on your tour selection.
- **Total Estimated Price** (Total Estimated Price, number, optional): Review the total estimated price for your tour.
- **Special Requests or Comments** (Special Requests or Comments, text, optional): Enter any special requests or comments.
- **I accept the tour cancellation and liability terms** (I accept the tour cancellation and liability terms, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept the tour cancellation and liability terms.

Note: Some fields may appear in a different order or have additional options not listed here due to the structure of the form YAML.
