# Local Business Networking Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Local Business Networking Confirmation" form is used to gather information from attendees to confirm their participation in a local business networking event. This form helps event organizers ensure that attendees are registered and have the necessary details to facilitate successful event coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the name of your business in the "Business Name" field (if applicable).
5. If you have a job title, enter it in the "Job Title" field (optional).
6. If you are affiliated with a company, enter it in the "Company" field (optional).
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
8. Select "Yes" if you are attending the event or "No" otherwise in the "Are you attending the event?" field.
9. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
10. If you are willing to have follow-up conversations after the meetups, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Are you willing to have followup conversations after the meetups?" field (optional).
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name to help us know who you are.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name to help us know who you are.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for further communication.
- **Business Name** (Text, Optional): If you represent a business, provide the name of the business.
- **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
- **Company** (Text, Optional): If you are affiliated with a company, enter its name.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Are you attending the event?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select "Yes" if you plan to attend or "No" otherwise.
- **Event Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the event.
- **Are you willing to have followup conversations after the meetups?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" if you are willing to follow up, "No" otherwise.
- **Additional Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes related to the event.
