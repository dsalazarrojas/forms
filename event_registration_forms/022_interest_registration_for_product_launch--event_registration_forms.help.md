The form "Interest Registration For Product Launch" is designed to capture the interest and contact information of individuals who wish to register for a product launch event. This form is intended for internal use and will help the event organizers to gauge interest in the event. 

The following is a guide to help users complete the form effectively.

# Interest Registration For Product Launch - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register interest in a product launch event. It is intended for internal use to gather information from potential attendees about their interest and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and ensure the information provided is accurate.
2. Please select your interest in the event using the dropdown options.
3. Fill in your contact information, including name, email address, and any other relevant details.
4. Choose a specific date and time that works for you to attend the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (Select "Yes" or "No"): 
	+ Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending the event. 
	+ Select "No" if you are not interested in attending the event.
* **Contact Info Form**:
	+ Enter your name in this field. This will help us know who is registering for the event.
	+ Enter your email address in this field. This will be used to contact you about event updates and details.
* **Date and Time**:
	+ Choose a specific date and time that you are available to attend the event. 

Note: Please be as specific as possible while selecting date and time. 

## Tips
* Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you experience any issues while filling up the form, please do not hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* Registration is open until [insert date and time]. Please register early to secure your spot!
