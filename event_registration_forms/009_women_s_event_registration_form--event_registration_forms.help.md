# Women's Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Event Registration Form is a tool used to gather information from attendees before, during, or after an event. This form will help event organizers collect necessary information from attendees, such as contact details, event preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide information about the event you are registering for (Event Information).
3. Specify the type of event you are attending (Event Type).
4. Indicate who you are bringing with you (Attendees).
5. Add any additional information about the event you'd like to share (Comments).
6. Choose a payment method (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
7. Fill out the Contact Information and Date sections if necessary.
8. Finally, add any additional comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (1): Provide your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Event Information** (2): Enter a brief description of the event you're attending or the event's name.
* **Event Details** (3): Add any additional details about the event you'd like to share.
* **Event Type** (4): Choose the type of event you are attending (Conference, Workshop, or Seminar).
* **Attendees** (5): Indicate who you are bringing with you (Friend, Family Member, or Both).
* **Attendee Information** (6): Provide any additional information about your attendee(s).
* **Contact Information** (7): Enter your email, phone number, or other contact details.
* **Payment Method** (8): Choose how you'd like to pay for the event (Online Payment or Offline Payment).
* **Comments** (9): Add any additional comments or remarks about the event or your registration.
* **Date** (10): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (11): Enter the time of the event.
* **Email** (12): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (13): Enter your phone number.
* **Zip** (14): Enter your zip code.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you provide all necessary information to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the event organizer for clarification.
