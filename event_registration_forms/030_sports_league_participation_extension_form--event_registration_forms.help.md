## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a sports league. It collects information about the participant, such as their name, email, phone number, and participation status. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your name and other relevant details.
2. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your **Email** address for communication with the league.
4. Select your **League Id** to associate yourself with the correct league.
5. Choose whether you are currently participating in **Event Id**.
6. Enter the **Event Name** to associate yourself with the specific event.
7. Select the **Participation Date** for which you will be participating.
8. Enter the **Participation Time** of your session.
9. Specify the **Participation Duration** of your session (if applicable).
10. Add any additional **Notes** about your participation (if any).
11. Select your **Participant Role** from the available options (if applicable).
12. Specify your **Participant Status** as Active or Inactive.
13. Provide a reason for your **Participant Status** (if inactive).
14. Enter the date of the last status change in **Participant Status Change Date** (if applicable).
15. Provide a reason for the last status change in **Participant Status Change Reason** (if applicable).
