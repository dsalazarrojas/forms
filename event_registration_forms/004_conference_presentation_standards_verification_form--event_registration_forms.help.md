# Conference Presentation Standards Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify that a presentation meets the standards for a conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by reviewing the presentation standards and ensuring that your presentation meets all the requirements.
*   Enter your presenter full name as it is listed in the program materials.
*   Enter the exact title of your presentation as it will appear on the schedule.
*   Confirm that your slides follow the guidelines (16:9 aspect ratio and non-animated).
*   Confirm that your speaker bio is within the 150-word limit.
*   Confirm that your content is appropriate for the audience (no explicit or discriminatory material).
*   If required, list any additional audio-visual (AV) requirements (e.g., projector, microphone, laptop adapter needs).
*   If required, enter the date for slide submission to meet deadlines.
*   Finally, type your full name to confirm that you have read and agree to the presentation standards.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Presenter full name**: Enter your full name as it is listed in the program materials.
*   **Presentation title**: Enter the exact title of your presentation as it will appear on the schedule.
*   **Slide format**: Confirm that your slides follow the guidelines (16:9 aspect ratio and non-animated).
*   **Speaker bio length**: Confirm that your speaker bio is within the 150-word limit.
*   **Content appropriateness**: Confirm that your content is appropriate for the audience (no explicit or discriminatory material).
*   **AV requirements**: If required, list any additional audio-visual (AV) requirements (e.g., projector, microphone, laptop adapter needs).
*   **Slide submission date**: If required, enter the date for slide submission to meet deadlines.
*   **Confirmation of compliance**: Type your full name to confirm that you have read and agree to the presentation standards.
