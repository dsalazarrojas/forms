# Church Event Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Event Planning Form is designed to help you plan and manage various events within the church. This form guides you through the process of planning and submitting event details, including event title, date, time, and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select a title for your event and provide a brief description of its purpose.
2.  Choose the lead organizer of the event.
3.  Select the sponsoring ministry.
4.  Enter the preferred date for the event.
5.  Choose the start and end time for the event, including setup and cleanup.
6.  Estimate the total attendance for the event.
7.  Select the facility rooms needed for the event.
8.  Choose the tech and AV needs for the event.
9.  Enter an estimated total budget for the event.
10. Select the funding source for the event.
11. Decide on a promotional plan to announce the event.
12. Choose whether child care will be provided.
13. Decide on food provision plans.
14. Enter the date the event plan is submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Title** (`proposed_event_title`): Enter a brief title for the event.
*   **Lead Organizer** (`lead_organizer`): Enter the name of the person leading the event.
*   **Sponsoring Ministry** (`sponsoring_ministry`): Choose the ministry responsible for the event.
*   **Event Purpose and Description** (`event_description_purpose`): Briefly explain the "why" and "what" of the event.
*   **Target Audience** (`target_audience`): Specify the target audience for the event.
*   **Preferred Event Date** (`preferred_event_date`): Choose the date for the event.
*   **Start Time (Including Setup)** (`start_time_including_setup`): Enter the start time of the event, including setup time.
*   **End Time (Including Cleanup)** (`end_time_including_cleanup`): Enter the end time of the event, including cleanup time.
*   **Estimated Total Attendance** (`estimated_attendance_planning`): Estimate the total attendance for the event.
*   **Facility Rooms Needed** (`facility_rooms_needed`): Select all the facility rooms needed for the event.
*   **Tech and AV Needs** (`tech_needs_planning`): Choose the tech and AV needs for the event.
*   **Estimated Total Budget ($)** (`budget_estimate`): Enter an estimated total budget for the event.
*   **Funding Source** (`funding_source`): Select the funding source for the event.
*   **Promotional Plan** (`promotion_plan`): Decide on a promotional plan to announce the event.
*   **Will Child Care be Provided?** (`child_care_provision`): Decide whether child care will be provided for the event.
*   **Will Food be Served?** (`food_provision_plan`): Decide on a plan for food provision for the event.
*   **Date Plan Submitted** (`submission_date_planning`): Enter the date the event plan is submitted.
