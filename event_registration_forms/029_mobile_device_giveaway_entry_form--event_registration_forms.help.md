# Mobile Device Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who wish to participate in a mobile device giveaway. The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information and preference details from participants, ensuring that the winner receives notifications and that the prize is shipped to the correct location.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1.  First, enter your **First Name** in the text field provided. This is not a required field.
2.  Next, enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided. This is not a required field.
3.  Enter your **Email** address in the email field. This is a required field.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number in the text field. This is not a required field.
5.  Enter your **Address** in the text field. This is not a required field.
6.  Enter your **City** in the text field. This is not a required field.
7.  Enter your **State** in the text field. This is not a required field.
8.  Enter your **Zip Code** in the text field. This is a required field.
9.  Select your **Country** from the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
10. Select how you **Found out** about the giveaway through one or multiple options (e.g., Online Ad, Social Media, Email, Friend). This is a required field.
11. Select your **Preferred Device** from the dropdown menu. This is a required field.
12. Select the type of prize you would like to **Win** (Device or Gift Card). This is a required field.
13. Enter any additional **Comments** you have in the text field. This is not a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your last name here.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your home or office address.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your city.
*   **State** (`state`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of your state or province.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
*   **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country from the dropdown menu.
*   **How did you find out?** (`how_did_you_find_out`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you found out about the giveaway.
*   **Preferred device** (`preferred_device`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred device.
*   **Would you like to win a** (`win_device`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the prize you would like to win.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional comments about the giveaway.
