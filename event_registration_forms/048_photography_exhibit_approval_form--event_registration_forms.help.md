# Photography Exhibit Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Exhibit Approval Form is a tool used to collect information about photography exhibits, enabling the event organizer to review and approve the exhibit's inclusion in the event. This form is typically completed by the artist who submitted their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the artist who created the photography exhibit.
2. Provide your email address to ensure the event organizer can contact you if needed.
3. Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
4. Share your personal website (if applicable).
5. Describe your image with a title and a brief description.
6. Specify the date the image was created.
7. Select the category (type) of your image (Photograph, Print, Painting, Drawing, Sculpture, or Other).
8. Provide the title of the exhibition.
9. Fill in all required fields, as some fields are marked as "optional" and can be left blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the artist who created the photography exhibit.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, Email, Optional): Provide your email address to allow the event organizer to contact you for any questions or updates.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for additional communication.
* **Artist Website** (`artist_website`, Text, Optional): Share your personal or professional website for further reference.
* **Image Title** (`image_title`, Text, Optional): Add a title for your photography exhibit.
* **Image Description** (`image_description`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe your photography exhibit.
* **Image Date** (`image_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the image was created (optional but recommended for authenticity).
* **Image Type** (`image_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the category of your image from the provided options (Photograph, Print, Painting, Drawing, Sculpture, or Other).
* **Image File** (`image_file`, Text, Optional): Share the image file URL or a brief description of it.
* **Exhibition Title** (`exhibition_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the title of the exhibition where your work will be showcased.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for the form to be complete.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the event organizer for guidance.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly, so don't hesitate to ask for help if you need it.
