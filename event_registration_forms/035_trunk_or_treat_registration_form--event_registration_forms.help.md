# trunk_or_treat_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Trunk or Treat event. 
The form is used to capture essential details about the event, including event name, participant information, and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Name** in the text field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone** number.
5. Select the **Event Date** from the calendar picker.
6. Choose the **Event Time** from the time dropdown.
7. Enter the **Assigned Tool** (if applicable).
8. Provide a brief **Description** of the event.
9. Select the **Category** of the event from the dropdown menu.
10. (Optional) Add any additional **Note** if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Trunk or Treat Halloween Party".
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar picker.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event from the time dropdown.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the event (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe the event in a few sentences.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category of the event from the dropdown menu.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event if needed.
* **Trunk or Treat Registration Form Comments** (`trunk_or_treat_registration_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the event registration.
