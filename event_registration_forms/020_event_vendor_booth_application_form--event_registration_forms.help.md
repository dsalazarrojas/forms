# Event Vendor Booth Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from event vendors to facilitate their participation in an event. It includes fields for vendor information, product offers, contact details, event details, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Read and understand the form's purpose and requirements.** Review the form's fields carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting the application process.
2.  **Enter your vendor information.** Fill in the required fields with your accurate and up-to-date information:
	*   **Vendor Name**: Your company or business name.
	*   **Vendor Email**: Your primary contact email address.
	*   **Vendor Phone**: Your primary contact phone number.
3.  **Enter event information.** Fill in the required fields with the accurate information about the event:
	*   **Event Name**: The name of the event you are participating in.
	*   **Event Date**: The date of the event.
	*   **Event Time**: The time of the event.
	*   **Event Location**: The location of the event.
4.  **Provide additional event information (optional).** If required, provide more event information:
	*   **Product Offers**: Briefly describe the products or services you will be offering at the event.
	*   **Application Terms**: If you have any questions or concerns, read and understand these terms carefully.
5.  **Upload required documents and media (optional).** If required, upload or provide links to:
	*   **Product Images**: High-quality images showcasing your products or services.
	*   **Product Videos**: A brief, high-quality video demonstrating your products or services.
	*   **Product Demo**: A detailed demonstration of your products or services.
6.  **Sign the application.** Carefully review and sign the form, ensuring all information is accurate and complete.

### Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review and understand the application terms and conditions before submitting the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, text, required): Your company or business name.
*   **Vendor Email** (`Vendor Email`, email, required): Your primary contact email address.
*   **Vendor Phone** (`Vendor Phone`, text, required): Your primary contact phone number.
*   **Event Name** (`Event Name`, text, required): The name of the event you are participating in.
*   **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): The date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): The time of the event.
*   **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text, required): The location of the event.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, text, optional): Read and understand these terms carefully.
*   **Vendor Signature** (`Vendor Signature`, text, required): Sign the form, ensuring all information is accurate and complete.
*   **Product Offers** (`Product Offers`, text, optional): Briefly describe the products or services you will be offering at the event.
*   **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): Provide additional contact information for your team.
*   **Application Terms** (`Application Terms`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, read and understand these terms carefully.
*   **Vendor Agreement** (`Vendor Agreement`, text, optional): Review and understand these terms carefully.
*   **Vendor Certification** (`Vendor Certification`, text, optional): Provide information about your certifications.
*   **Product Images** (`Product Images`, text, optional): Upload high-quality images showcasing your products or services.
*   **Product Videos** (`Product Videos`, text, optional): Upload high-quality videos demonstrating your products or services.
*   **Product Demo** (`Product Demo`, text, optional): Provide a detailed demonstration of your products or services.
*   **Vendor Experience** (`Vendor Experience`, text, optional): Provide information about your experience with similar events.
*   **Vendor References** (`Vendor References`, text, optional): Provide references from satisfied customers or partners.
*   **Product Description** (`Product Description`, text, optional): Describe your products or services.
*   **Vendor Requirements** (`Vendor Requirements`, text, optional): List your requirements for participating in the event.
