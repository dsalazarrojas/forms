# Curbside Halloween Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendance to a Curbside Halloween event. Please provide information about yourself and any additional participants in your party.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your own information, including your neighborhood address, phone number, email address, and any other required fields.
2. If you have additional family members or friends attending, repeat the form for each of them, using different names (e.g., Name 2, Name 3, etc.).
3. Select the correct options for each field as prompted.
4. Enter any additional comments or messages in the designated text areas.
5. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neighborhood Address**: Enter the address of your neighborhood or area where you will be trick-or-treating.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you with event updates or reminders.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for event updates or reminders.
* **Will Participate**: Select which options you and your party members will participate in during the event (e.g., trick-or-treating, etc.).
* **Name**: Enter your name or the name of the individual participating.
* **Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages about your participation.
* **Confirm Participation**: Confirm that you and your party members will participate in the event.
* **Will Bring Food**: Select if you will bring food for the event (e.g., for a potluck).
* **Name of Food**: If you bring food, enter the type or name of the dish you will bring.
* **Date of Food**: If you bring food, enter the date it will be brought or ready for consumption.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the Curbside Halloween event.
* **Time of Event**: Enter the time of the event.

* **Name 2**: Enter the name of another participant in your party.
* **Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of another participant.
* **Email 2**: Enter the email address of another participant.
