# Luxury Train Journey Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a luxury train journey for customers. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name in the text box.
2. Enter your Last Name in the text box.
3. Type in your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written in your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written in your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Type in your email address where we can contact you about your booking.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your booking.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately for a smooth booking process.
* Make sure to use a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at your email address or phone number.
