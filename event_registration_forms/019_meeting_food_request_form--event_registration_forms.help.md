# Meeting Food Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps meeting organizers collect important information from attendees about their food requests and other meeting details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and select any dietary restrictions you have.
2. Provide any special requests or notes about your food preferences.
3. Select the date and time of the meeting.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. Specify the topic of the meeting.
6. Provide the agenda for the meeting.
7. List the attendees attending the meeting.
8. Add any comments or notes about the meeting.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. If assigned a tool, provide its name.
12. Upload any relevant output files.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Food Request Form Page 1**:
	+ **Meeting Food Request** (`meeting_food_request_form_page_1`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any requests or preferences regarding food.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Enter any special requests or notes about your food preferences.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the meeting.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the meeting.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required: false): Specify the topic of the meeting.
* **Meeting Agenda** (`meeting_agenda`, text, required: false): Provide the agenda for the meeting.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): List the attendees attending the meeting, using the options provided.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If assigned a tool, please enter its name.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): If applicable, upload any relevant output files.
