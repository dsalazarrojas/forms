# Sponsor Product Display Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event registration with the purpose of gathering information from sponsors about their products and booths. Sponsors can register for one or multiple events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the title of the event you are registering for.
2. Choose the registration details you wish to provide (Product Display or Sponsor Details).
3. Fill in the registration title, title, description, and image for each product you are registering.
4. Choose the products you are registering for this event.
5. Fill in the details for the sponsor and their product information.
6. Select the booth information and location.
7. Provide additional details for the submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Product Display Registration Form (form_title)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Registration Details (page_1_1)**:
	+ Select the type of registration you wish to provide (Product Display or Sponsor Details).
* **Registration Title (page_1_1_1)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the title of the product.
* **Title (page_1_1_2)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the title of the product.
* **Registration Description (page_1_1_3)**:
	+ A text field where you can provide a description of the product.
* **Sponsor Details (page_2_1)**:
	+ Select the sponsor information you wish to provide (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Sponsor Name (page_2_1_1)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Company (page_2_1_2)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the company of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email (page_2_1_3)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the email of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone (page_2_1_4)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the phone number of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Address (page_2_1_5)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Country (page_2_1_6)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the country of the sponsor.
* **Product Details (page_3_1)**:
	+ Select the products you are registering for this event.
* **Product 1 (page_3_1_1)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the details of the first product.
* **Product 2 (page_3_1_2)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the details of the second product.
* **Product 3 (page_3_1_3)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the details of the third product.
* **Product Category (page_3_1_4)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the category of the product.
* **Product Price (page_3_1_5)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the price of the product.
* **Product Description (page_3_1_6)**:
	+ A text field where you can provide a description of the product.
* **Product Image (page_3_1_7)**:
	+ A field for uploading an image of the product.
* **Booth Details (page_4_1)**:
	+ Select the booth information you wish to provide (Booth A, Booth B, Booth C).
* **Booth Number (page_4_1_1)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the number of the booth.
* **Booth Location (page_4_1_2)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the location of the booth.
* **Booth Description (page_4_1_3)**:
	+ A text field where you can fill in the description of the booth.
* **Submission Status (page_4_1_4)**:
	+ A field for indicating the submission status.
