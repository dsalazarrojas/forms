# Diversity and Inclusion Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants attending the Diversity and Inclusion Workshop. This form will help the workshop organizers understand who is attending, their background and preferences, and any special accommodations they may need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2.  Fill out the form with your participant information.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
6.  Enter the name of your company in the "Participant Company" field (if applicable).
7.  Select your diversity group (if applicable) in the "Diversity Group" field.
8.  Choose the date you will be attending the workshop in the "Workshop Date" field.
9.  Choose the time you will be attending the workshop in the "Workshop Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**:
	+ (Participant Information, text, required): This section is where you will provide some basic information about yourself as a participant.
	+ **Email**:
		- Email, email, optional): This is where you enter your email address. This will help us keep in touch with you.
	+ **Phone**:
		- Phone, text, optional): This is where you enter your phone number. This can be useful if we need to contact you with important updates or reminders.
	+ **Participant Name**:
		- Participant Name, text, optional): This is where you enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Participant Company**:
		- Participant Company, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company here.
	+ **Diversity Group**:
		- Diversity Group, text, optional): If you identify with a diversity group, select the group that best fits your identity.
	+ **Workshop Date**:
		- Workshop Date, date, optional): Choose the date you will be attending the workshop.
	+ **Workshop Time**:
		- Workshop Time, time, optional): Choose the time you will be attending the workshop.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
* You can always reach out to us if you need any special accommodations during the workshop.
