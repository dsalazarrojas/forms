# Most Engaging Online Community Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather nominations for online communities that have made a significant impact.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your E-mail address to allow us to contact you with any questions or to confirm your nomination.
3. Select the Nominee's Community from the dropdown list.
4. Choose a Nomination Category that best describes the nominee's achievements.
5. Provide a brief description of the nominee's achievements in the Nomination Description field.
6. (Optional) Explain why you like the community and how long you've been a part of it in the Why Do You Like and How Long fields.
7. Enter your Contact Name and E-mail address so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **E-mail**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with any questions or to confirm your nomination.
* **Nominee's Community**: Select the online community you are nominating from the list:
	+ Community A
	+ Community B
	+ Community C
* **Nomination Category**: Choose a category that best describes the nominee's achievements:
	+ Category A
	+ Category B
	+ Category C
* **Nomination Description**: Please provide a brief description of the nominee's achievements.
* **Why Do You Like**: (Optional) Explain why you like the community and what makes it special.
* **How Long**: (Optional) Enter how long you've been part of the community.
* **Contact Name**: Please enter your name so we can follow up with you.
* **Contact E-mail**: Please enter a valid email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed the form, click submit to submit your nomination.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the nominee's achievements.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask.
