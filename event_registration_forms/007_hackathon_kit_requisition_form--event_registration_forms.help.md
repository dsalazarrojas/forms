# Hackathon Kit Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hackathon Kit Requisition Form is used to collect information from participants to manage kit requisition and distribution for the hackathon event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Event** that you are interested in attending from the list of available options.
2. Enter your **Participant Name** and **Participant Email** to ensure that we can get in touch with you about your kit requisition status.
3. Enter your **Participant Phone** (optional) to provide an additional contact method.
4. Select the **Kit Requests** that you require for the event.
5. Choose the **Kit Delivery** option that suits your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event** (`event_registrations`, select_one): Select the event that you are interested in attending.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email): Enter your email address that we can use to get in touch with you about your kit requisition status.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes (optional).
* **Kit Requests** (`kit_requests`, select_multiple): Select the kits that you require for the event. You can select multiple kits by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) and clicking on multiple options.
* **Kit Delivery** (`kit_delivery`, select_one): Choose the kit delivery option that suits your needs, such as "Deliver to me" or "Collect from office".
