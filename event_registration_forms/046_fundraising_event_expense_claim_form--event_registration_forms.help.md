# Fundraising Event Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Event Expense Claim Form is designed for event organizers or volunteers to document and submit their expenses related to fundraising events. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to facilitate a smooth review and processing process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure you accurately represent your expenses incurred during the event.
3. Double-check your submissions for any errors or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, optional): Briefly describe the event you participated in, including the event title, date, and location.
* **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the expense you are claiming.
* **Expense Item** (`expense_item`, text, optional): Describe the expense you are claiming, e.g., food, transportation, or equipment rental.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, optional): Enter the exact amount of the expense, including any receipts or supporting documents.
* **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the expense, including any relevant details.
* **Total** (`expense_total`, text, optional): Enter the total amount of expenses claimed.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Select the correct submission option (e.g., "Submit" or "Save as Draft").

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Attach any supporting documents or receipts to back up your claims.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant personnel for assistance.

Please note that some fields may be optional, but it's essential to complete all fields for a smooth review and processing process.
