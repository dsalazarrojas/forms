# Newcomer Family Retreat Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from families attending a retreat. It will help the retreat organizers understand the family's registration details, family information, emergency contacts, special needs, and guideline consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields as per the instructions provided for each section.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate information.
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Information** (`newcomer_family_retreat_agreement`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can enter your family's registration details. Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant registration details.
*   **Family Information** (`family_info`, text, required/optional): In this field, please provide information about the members of your family, including their names, ages, and relationships to you.
*   **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required/optional): List any emergency contacts that you would like the retreat team to have access to in case of an emergency.
*   **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required/optional): If you or any member of your family has any special needs, please list them here so that the retreat team can be prepared to accommodate them.
*   **Guideline Consent** (`guideline_consent`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide your consent to the retreat guidelines. Please review the guidelines carefully before submitting your consent.
