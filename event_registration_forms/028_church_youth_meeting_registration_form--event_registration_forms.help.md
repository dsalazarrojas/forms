# Church Youth Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Youth Meeting Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from youth participants to ensure a well-planned and executed event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
3. Ensure you sign and date the form when required.
4. Submit the form to ensure your registration is processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth's Full Name** (`youth_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the participant, first and last.
* **Preferred Name or Nickname** (`youth_nickname`, text, **optional**): If the participant has a preferred name or nickname they would like to be called, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Provide the participant's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Current Age** (`age`, number, **required**): Enter the participant's current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, **required**): Select the participant's gender from the available options.
* **Current Grade Level** (`grade`, select_one, **required**): Choose the participant's current grade level from the available options.
* **School Name** (`school`, text, **required**): Enter the participant's school name.
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`parent_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, **required**): Enter the participant's home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, **required**): Enter your name and phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.
* **Which Youth Meeting Are You Registering For** (`meeting_name`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of meeting you are registering for from the available options.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Is This Your First Youth Meeting** (`first_time`, select_one, **required**): Indicate if this is the participant's first youth meeting.
* **Are You a Church Member** (`church_member`, select_one, **required**): Check if you are a church member.
* **Any Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, **optional**): If the participant has any medical conditions or allergies, enter them here.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, **optional**): If the participant is taking any medications, list them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, **optional**): If food will be served and the participant has any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Do You Need Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, **required**): Indicate if you need transportation or can provide it.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, **optional**): If a T-shirt will be provided, select your size from the available options.
* **Photo Release Permission** (`photo_release`, select_one, **required**): Grant permission for photos to be taken for church use.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, **required**): Sign with your full name as a parent or guardian.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, **required**): Date and sign the form.
