# Travel Permission For Minors Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain travel permission for minors attending events. It is intended for parents or guardians of minors to provide necessary contact information and emergency contact details for their child. This form is required for event organizers to ensure that all minor attendees have proper supervision and care during events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the event in the "Event Name" field.
2. Fill in the event details in the "Event Location" and "Event Start Date" fields.
3. Enter the minor's name and date of birth in the "Minor Name" and "Minor DOB" fields.
4. Enter the minor's contact information in the "Parent Name", "Contact Number", "Email", and "Minor School" fields.
5. Provide an emergency contact person and their relationship to the minor in the "Emergency Contact Person" and "Emergency Contact Relationship" fields.
6. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Number" field.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Permission For Minors Form**: This is the form title and is pre-filled for your convenience.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Minor Name** (`minor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the minor attending.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the minor's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter an email for the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe the relationship between the minor and the emergency contact person.
* **Minor DOB** (`minor_dob`, date, optional): Enter the minor's date of birth.
* **Minor School** (`minor_school`, text, optional): Enter the minor's school or educational institution (if applicable).
* **Travel Group** (`travel_group`, text, optional): Enter any information about the travel group, such as the group's name or purpose.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
- Ensure that you have permission from a parent or guardian before submitting this form.
