# Cultural Event Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about participants in cultural events for communication and organization purposes. It aims to collect relevant details about participants, including their type, contact information, and feedback. Completing this form will help you effectively communicate with and manage participants in a smooth and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling in the Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, select one):
Select the type of participant that best describes yourself (Participant Type or Participant Type 2).
2. **Enter your contact information** (`Phone`, `Email`, `Address`, select multiple):
Choose one or more ways you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Address).
3. **Provide your feedback** (`Participant Feedback`, text area):
Share any comments, suggestions, or additional information about your participation in the cultural event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, select one):
This field asks you to choose the category that best describes you as a participant. This helps us understand your role in the event and tailor communication accordingly.
* **Contact Details** (`Phone`, `Email`, `Address`, select multiple):
Select one or more ways you prefer to be contacted. This will help us reach out to you effectively and efficiently.
* **Participant Feedback** (`Participant Feedback`, text area):
Share any additional comments, suggestions, or information about your participation. This will help us improve future events and better understand your needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Participant Type, Contact Details).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, don't hesitate to ask the event organizers.
