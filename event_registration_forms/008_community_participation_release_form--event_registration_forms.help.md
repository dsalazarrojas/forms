# Community Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be completed by participants in community events. It gathers essential information, including personal details, consent for medical treatment and media usage, and agreement to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the waiver section carefully and ensure you understand the terms.
2.  Fill in your Participant Full Name.
3.  Enter your Date of Birth.
4.  Select your gender.
5.  Enter the Event Title and Event Date.
6.  Check "Yes - I agree" for the Release of Liability and Medical Treatment sections.
7.  Check "Yes - I consent" for the Photo and Video Release section.
8.  Enter the Emergency Contact Full Name and Emergency Contact Phone Number.
9.  If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them under Medical Concerns (optional).
10. Check "Yes - I agree" for the General Participation Agreement section.
11. Type your full name under Digital Signature to complete the form.
12. Click "Submit" to complete the form and submit your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (month/day/year).
*   **Participant Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
*   **Event Title**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are participating in.
*   **Release of Liability**: Check "Yes - I agree" to release the organization from any claims or liability.
*   **Consent for Medical Treatment**: Check "Yes - I consent" to receive medical treatment if needed.
*   **Photo and Video Release**: Check "Yes - I consent" to allow the organization to use images or video of you for promotional purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Concerns**: List any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of (optional).
*   **General Participation Agreement**: Check "Yes - I agree" to follow all instructions and rules of the event.
*   **Digital Signature**: Type your full name here to complete the form. 

Please ensure you have carefully read and understood all sections of this form before submitting it.
