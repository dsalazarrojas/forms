# Airbnb Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from guests for their stay at your accommodation. Please fill out this form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both you and your guest.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
- Review each field to understand its purpose and ensure you're providing accurate information.
- If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to ask the host for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest name**: Please enter the name of the guest or the main person responsible for the reservation.
  - This is a required field, as it helps the host verify the guest's identity.
  - Use the name as it appears on the guest's identification or passport.
- **Email address**: Enter the guest's email address for reservation confirmation and any urgent communications.
  - Ensure the email address is accurate and active to receive important updates.
  - This is a required field for efficient communication.
- **Check in date**: Select the date the guest will arrive at the accommodation.
  - This field is required to schedule check-in and ensure the room is prepared.
  - Use the standard date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
- **Check out date**: Choose the date the guest will depart from the accommodation.
  - Like the check-in date, this is required for room preparation and scheduling.
  - Enter the date in the standard format (yyyy-mm-dd).
- **Number of guests**: Indicate the total number of guests staying at the accommodation.
  - This helps the host prepare for the correct number of rooms and amenities.
  - Enter a whole number (e.g., 2 or 4).
- **Special requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements the guest may have.
  - This is an optional field for guests to communicate any unique needs.
  - Use plain text to provide detailed information.
- **Payment method**: Select the preferred method of payment for the reservation.
  - This is a required field for payment arrangements and billing.
  - Choose from available options (e.g., Credit card, Bank transfer, or Other).
- **Emergency contact name and phone**: Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact for the guest.
  - This field is optional but recommended for guests to provide an alternate contact method.
  - Use the format "Name (Phone number)" (e.g., John Doe (123-456-7890)).
- **House rules acknowledgement**: Please review and agree to the property rules and conduct.
  - This is a required field for guests to accept the terms of the accommodation.
  - Read and understand the rules before selecting "Agree".

## Tips
- Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the host for clarification.
- Ensure accurate information to avoid any issues during your stay.
