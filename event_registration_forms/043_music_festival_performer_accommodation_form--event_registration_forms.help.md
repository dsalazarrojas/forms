# Music Festival Performer Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from musicians and performers attending the music festival, including their arrival details, accommodation preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each section to ensure you provide all required information.
- Fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please specify them clearly in the special requests field.
- Ensure that you provide your preferred mode of arrival and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Information** (performer_info, text, optional)
	+ This field is for you to provide your name or band name.
* **Arrival Date** (arrival_date, date, optional)
	+ Choose the date of your expected arrival at the festival location.
* **Arrival Time** (arrival_time, time, optional)
	+ Please select the time of your expected arrival at the festival location.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (accommodation_preferences, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select your preferred type of accommodation (e.g., Accommodation 1, Accommodation 2, etc.).
* **Special Requests** (special_requests, text, optional)
	+ If you have any specific needs or requirements, please specify them here.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you for further communication.
* **Arrival Method** (arrival_method, select_one, optional)
	+ Please select how you plan to arrive at the festival location (e.g., Car, Train, etc.).
* **Arrival Details** (arrival_details, text, optional)
	+ Provide any additional information about your arrival, such as flight numbers or other relevant details.
