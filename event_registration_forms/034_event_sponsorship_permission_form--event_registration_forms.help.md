# Event Sponsorship Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sponsorship Permission Form is a template for obtaining sponsorships for an event. It is used by event organizers to gather information from potential sponsors and ensure they understand the terms and conditions of their involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and company (optional)
2. Enter your email address
3. Provide your phone number (optional)
4. Check the box if you agree to share information about you or your company
5. Check the box to indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions
6. Sign the form to confirm your agreement
7. Select the relevant options for event details, payment terms, and payment method
8. Enter the payment amount (if applicable)
9. Choose your agreement status
10. Enter the event ID (a unique identifier for the event)
11. Check the box to confirm you have accepted the terms

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsorship Permission Form** (`event_sponsorship_permission_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the sponsorship materials.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Permission to Share Information** (`permission_to_share_information`): Check this box to agree to share information about you or your company.
* **Agreement to Terms** (`agreement_to_terms`): Check this box to indicate you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`): Enter the date you agreed to the terms.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Select the event details that apply to you or your company (yes/no options).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`): Select the payment terms that apply to you or your company (yes/no options).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method (yes/no option).
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`): Choose your agreement status (yes/no option).
* **Event ID** (`event_id`): Enter the event ID (a unique identifier for the event).
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`): Check the box to confirm you have accepted the terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* Ensure you enter the correct event ID.
