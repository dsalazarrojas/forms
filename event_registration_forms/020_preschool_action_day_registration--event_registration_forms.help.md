# Preschool Action Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents to register their child for the Preschool Action Day event. This event is a fun and educational activity for children to learn and engage in various activities. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide essential details about your child's participation, including their availability, event preferences, and any medical conditions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event type (Yes/No) that best describes your child's participation.
2. Enter your child's name.
3. Provide your parent's email address.
4. Enter your parent's phone number.
5. Choose a date for the event.
6. Select the time for the event.
7. Enter the event location.
8. Provide any additional event details.
9. Add any notes or comments about your child's attendance.
10. Confirm the event time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" if your child plans to attend the event.
	+ Choose "No" if your child will not attend the event.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your parent's email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your parent's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Choose a date for your child's event attendance.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Select a time for your child's event attendance.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional information about your child's event attendance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Add any notes or comments about your child's attendance.
* **Event Time 1** (`event_time_1`, time, required: false):
	+ Confirm the time for your child's event attendance.
