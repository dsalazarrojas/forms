# pay-what-you-can-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow attendees to register for an event and optionally contribute a payment amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button when you have finished filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the "attendee information" field on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Information** (`attendee_information`, text, required): Enter the details of the attendee, such as their name, email, and any other relevant information.
- **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to pay for the event.
- **Payment Amount Hint** (`payment_amount_hint`, note, optional): A note providing additional information about the payment amount field.
- **Payment Amount Hint 2** (`payment_amount_hint_2`, note, optional): Another note providing additional information about the payment amount field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the "attendee information" field completely.
- You can leave the "payment amount" field blank if you do not wish to pay anything.
- The "payment amount hint" fields are optional and provide additional context about the payment amount field.
