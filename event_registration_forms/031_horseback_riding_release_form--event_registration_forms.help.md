# Horseback Riding Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from participants before they engage in horseback riding activities. It includes their participant information, legal consent, facility information, risk awareness, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name and contact details.
2. Indicate your legal consent to participate in horseback riding activities.
3. Provide facility information, such as the location and date of the activity.
4. Answer the risk awareness question truthfully.
5. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information):
	+ This is where you provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Legal Consent** (Legal Consent):
	+ Please select "Yes" if you agree to participate in horseback riding activities and "No" if you do not.
* **Facility Information** (Facility Information):
	+ Provide the location and date of the horseback riding activity you are participating in.
* **Risk Awareness** (Risk Awareness):
	+ Answer this question truthfully, indicating that you are aware of the risks involved in horseback riding activities.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details):
	+ Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
