# Traffic Safety Campaign Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential sponsors for the Traffic Safety Campaign. This includes road safety initiatives, contact details, and sponsorship specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the relevant road safety initiatives you are interested in sponsoring.
2. Fill in your organization's details, including name and contact person.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Enter a valid phone number.
5. Provide your street address, city, and zip code.
6. Enter the name of the sponsor, if applicable.
7. Select the type of sponsor (if applicable).
8. Enter the sponsorship amount (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Road Safety Initiatives** (`road_safety_initiatives`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more road safety initiatives you are interested in sponsoring. This will help us understand your area of interest and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization, which will be used to identify you as a sponsor.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name and title of the person to contact for further information or to discuss sponsorship details.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for follow-up questions or to confirm sponsorship details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's street address, which will help us to send sponsorship materials and information.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of your organization's address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code of your organization's address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of your organization's address.
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the sponsor, if applicable.
* **Sponsor Type** (`sponsor_type`, text, required: false): Select or enter the type of sponsor, if applicable.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the sponsorship amount, which should be a valid number.
