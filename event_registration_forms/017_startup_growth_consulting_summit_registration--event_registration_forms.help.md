# Startup Growth Consulting Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for the Startup Growth Consulting Summit, a one-day conference for professionals in the startup world.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field, which is optional but helps us get to know you.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field and will be used to communicate with you regarding the summit.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your interests in the "Interests" field. This is a required field and helps us tailor the content of the summit to your needs. You can select multiple interests that apply to you.
5. (Optional) In the "Consent" field, you can add any additional information or requests you may have.
6. (Optional) In the "Time" and "Date" fields, you can select your available time and date, respectively. These fields are not currently required but are provided for future use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional)
    Enter your name in this field. This will help us get to know you better.
### Email (2)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
    Enter your email address here. This is the primary contact method we will use to communicate with you.
### Phone (3)
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
    If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will help us reach out to you if needed.
### Interests (4)
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, required)
    Select all the interests that apply to you. This will help us tailor the content of the summit to your needs.
### Consent (5)
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, optional)
    Use this field to add any additional information or requests you may have. You can write a note here if needed.
### Time (6)
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional)
    Select your available time here. This field is not currently required but will be used in the future.
### Date (7)
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional)
    Select your available date here. This field is not currently required but will be used in the future.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure we can accommodate your needs properly.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please select all the fields that apply to you in the "Interests" section.
* The "Time" and "Date" fields are not currently required but are for future reference.
* If you need to make any changes, please contact us so we can update your registration.
