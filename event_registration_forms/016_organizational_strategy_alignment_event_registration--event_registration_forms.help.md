# Organizational Strategy Alignment Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to an organizational strategy alignment event. It is meant to gather crucial information for both attendees and event organizers to ensure a well-organized event that meets everyone's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form accurately, start at the top and work your way down.
2. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with "select" options, choose one or multiple options depending on your preference.
4. Enter any additional information in the "Notes" field if necessary.
5. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (`id: 1`): This is where you will enter your general information for the event. It is an open text field for you to provide your details.
* **Event Details** (`id: 2`): Provide any additional information about the event you find relevant.
* **Attendee Details** (`id: 3`): Enter any additional attendee information if this field is required for you or the event organizers' needs.
* **Preferences** (`id: 4`): Indicate the preferences you have for the event, such as the format or type of session.
* **Logistics** (`id: 5`): Enter any details about logistics related to the event.
* **Notes** (`id: 6`): Leave any additional comments or information here if necessary.
* **Email** (`id: 7`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 8`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`id: 9`): Enter your address.
* **Name** (`id: 10`): Enter your name.
* **Company** (`id: 11`): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`id: 12`): Enter your role within your company or organization.
* **Strategy** (`id: 13`): Enter your strategy related to the event.
* **Strategy Priority** (`id: 14`): Indicate the priority level of your strategy.
* **Strategy Status** (`id: 15`): Enter the status of your strategy.
* **Follow Up** (`id: 16`): Enter any follow-up information if necessary.
* **Date** (`id: 17`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`id: 18`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Select One** (`id: 19`): Choose whether you will be attending the event or not.
* **Select Multiple** (`id: 20`): If available, select multiple options that apply to you or your organization.
* **Note** (`id: 21`): Leave any additional notes or comments here.
* **Email 2** (`id: 22`): Enter a second email address if necessary.
* **Phone 2** (`id: 23`): Enter a second phone number if necessary.
* **Date 2** (`id: 24`): Enter a second date if necessary.
* **Time 2** (`id: 25`): Enter a second time if necessary.

## Tips

* Double-check for accuracy before submitting your form.
* If you have any questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in for successful submission.
