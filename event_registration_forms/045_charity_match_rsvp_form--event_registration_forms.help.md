# Charity Match RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Match RSVP Form is designed to help individuals register for a charity match event, providing their personal details and expressing their interest in attending, donating, or volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Select the **Event Name** field from the provided options, as it relates to the event you're interested in attending.
6. Choose a date for the **Event Date** field, when the event will be held.
7. Select your response to **Will You Attend**, indicating whether you'll be attending the event.
8. Enter the number of guests you'll be bringing in the **Number of Guests** field.
9. If applicable, list the names of your guests in the **Guest Names** field.
10. Choose your table preference in the **Seating Preference** field (optional).
11. Select your dietary restrictions or allergies in the **Dietary Requirements** field (optional).
12. Indicate your interest in making a donation in the **Interested in Making a Donation** field (optional).
13. If donating, choose the desired **Donation Amount** from the provided options (optional).
14. Select your interest in volunteering in the **Interested in Volunteering** field (optional).
15. If necessary, enter any accessibility requirements you have in the **Accessibility Needs** field (optional).
16. Choose how you heard about the event from the provided options in the **How Did You Hear About This Event** field (optional).
17. Confirm that you understand the **RSVP Deadline** in the provided options (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact.
* **Organization**: Enter your company or group name (if applicable).
* **Event Name**: Select the event you're interested in attending.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event you'll be attending.
* **Will You Attend**: Indicate whether you'll be attending the event.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you'll be bringing.
* **Guest Names**: List the names of your guests (if any).
* **Seating Preference**: Enter any specific table preferences you have (if applicable).
* **Dietary Requirements**: Choose any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Interested in Making a Donation**: Indicate your interest in making a donation.
* **Donation Amount**: Choose the amount you'd like to donate (if making a donation).
* **Interested in Volunteering**: Choose your interest in volunteering at the event.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any accessibility requirements you have, such as wheelchair accessibility.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Choose how you learned about the event, such as through social media.
* **RSVP Deadline**: Confirm that you understand the deadline for responding to this registration.

By following these steps and reading the explanations for each field, you'll be able to successfully complete the Charity Match RSVP Form and register for this charity event.
