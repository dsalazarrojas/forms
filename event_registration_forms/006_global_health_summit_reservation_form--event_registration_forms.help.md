# global_health_summit_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global health summit reservation form is used to collect event details and contact information from event organizers and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to enter all required fields.
* Review your information for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer_details`): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter a brief description of the event, including its purpose and scope.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`): Enter the name and contact information of the attendee, including their email and phone number.
* **Reservation Details** (`reservation_details`): Select 'Yes' if you have a reservation for this event, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the event in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the event in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the event in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the event in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): Select the status of the event as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to the event. 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the event organizer or event administrator for assistance.
