# Best Hotel Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Hotel Nomination Form is designed to help identify outstanding hotels that excel in providing exceptional guest experiences. This form is used to collect nominations for hotels that demonstrate leadership, innovation, or remarkable service. The information collected will be reviewed to select top-notch hotels that deserve recognition and accolades within the hospitality industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Best Hotel Nomination Form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the nominator (yourself), including your full name and email address.
2. Enter the details of the nominee hotel, including its official name and location.
3. Select a category for the nominee hotel from the provided options (Luxury and Ultra-Luxury, Boutique and Lifestyle, Business and Corporate, Family-Friendly Resort, Sustainable and Eco-Friendly, Budget and Economy, or Other).
4. Rate the nominee hotel on various performance categories, including Service Quality and Staff Conduct, Room Quality and Comfort, Food and Beverage Quality, Facilities and Amenities, and Location and Accessibility.
5. Provide a detailed justification for why the nominee hotel deserves recognition, including specific moments or services that impressed you.
6. Describe a unique experience you had at the hotel.
7. Explain how the nominee hotel has contributed to the hospitality industry, including innovations or leadership.
8. Confirm that you have stayed at the hotel in the last 12 months or have visited/worked in the industry.
9. Provide the date of your most recent stay or visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Organization or Agency** (`organization_agency_name`, `text`, optional): If you are not an individual nominator, enter the name of your company or travel agency.
* **Your Contact Email** (`nominator_email_addr`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for further questions or verification.
* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_legal_name`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the hotel being nominated.
* **Hotel Location** (`hotel_location_city_country`, `text`, required): Enter the city and country of the hotel's location.
* **Hotel Brand or Chain** (`hotel_brand_chain`, `text`, optional): If the hotel belongs to a chain or brand, enter the name here.
* **Category for Award** (`award_category_select`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary category for this hotel.
* **Service Quality and Staff Conduct** (`service_quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the hotel's service quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Room Quality and Comfort** (`room_comfort_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the hotel's room quality and comfort from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Food and Beverage Quality** (`food_beverage_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the hotel's food and beverage quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Facilities and Amenities** (`facilities_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the hotel's facilities and amenities from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Location and Accessibility** (`accessibility_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the hotel's location and accessibility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Why does this hotel deserve to be recognized** (`recognition_reason_desc`, `text`, required): Describe the key factors that make this hotel stand out.
* **Describe a unique experience at this hotel** (`unique_experience_desc`, `text`, required): Share a specific moment or service that impressed you.
* **How has this hotel contributed to the hospitality industry** (`industry_contribution_desc`, `text`, required): Explain the hotel's innovations or leadership within the industry.
* **Have you stayed at this hotel in the last 12 months** (`recent_stay_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your recent stay or association with the hotel.
* **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_final_date_stamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your most recent stay or visit.
