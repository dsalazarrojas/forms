# Trade Show Display Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for trade show display booth orders. Please fill it out carefully to ensure your booth requirements are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Trade Show**: Please enter the name of the trade show you're attending.
2. **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you're participating in.
3. **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
4. **Event End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
5. **Booth Size**: Select the size of your booth from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large).
6. **Booth Type**: Please enter any specific details about your booth type.
7. **Number of Attendees**: Enter the number of attendees you expect.
8. **Booth Materials**: Select the materials you'll be using for your booth (Red, Blue, Green).
9. **Order Deadline**: Enter the date by which you need to complete your order.
10. **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or additional requirements you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Show**: A text field for you to enter the name of the trade show you're attending.
* **Event Name**: A text field for you to enter the name of the event you're participating in.
* **Event Start Date**: A date field for you to enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date**: A date field for you to enter the end date of the event.
* **Booth Size**: A select field to choose the size of your booth:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **Booth Type**: A text field for you to enter any specific details about your booth type.
* **Number of Attendees**: A number field for you to enter the number of attendees you expect.
* **Booth Materials**: A select multiple field to choose the materials you'll be using for your booth:
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
* **Order Deadline**: A date field for you to enter the date by which you need to complete your order.
* **Special Requests**: A text field for you to enter any special requests or additional requirements you have.
