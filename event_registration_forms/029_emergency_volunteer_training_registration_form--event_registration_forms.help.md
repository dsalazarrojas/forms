# emergency_volunteer_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from volunteers who have registered for emergency training sessions. This form helps to keep track of the training session details, volunteer names, and email addresses. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your registration is complete and you receive the necessary information about the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Organization Name field with the name of the organization you are representing or affiliated with.
2. Select the Training Session Date for which you are registering.
3. Enter your Volunteer Name.
4. Provide your Volunteer Email address to receive updates and notifications about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are representing or affiliated with.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the training session for which you are registering.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the training session.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the training session, contact the organization or training administrators directly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid missing any required fields.
