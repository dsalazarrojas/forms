# Nonprofit Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Event Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from registrants and donors for nonprofit events. This form helps event organizers to collect and record details about events, attendees, and donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event title, date, address, and other relevant details in the respective fields.
2. Provide your first and last name.
3. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
5. For payment method, select one of the options provided (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
6. If you are registering as a donor, enter the amount you are donating.
7. For donation frequency, select how often you would like to make donations (e.g., One time, Recurring, Monthly, Quarterly).
8. Enter your non-profit number (if applicable).
9. Provide any additional notes or comments (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration**
	+ This is where you enter the title of the event.
* **Non Profit Organization**
	+ Enter the name of the nonprofit organization hosting the event.
* **First Name**
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Email**
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**
	+ Enter your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Event Date**
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Address**
	+ Enter the address of the event location.
* **Event City**
	+ Enter the city of the event location.
* **Event State**
	+ Enter the state of the event location.
* **Event Zip**
	+ Enter the zip code of the event location.
* **Event Description**
	+ Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Payment Method**
	+ Select how you would like to make payment (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Attendee Count**
	+ Enter the number of attendees for the event.
* **Attendee Name**
	+ Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email**
	+ Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone**
	+ Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Donor Amount**
	+ Enter the amount you are donating.
* **Donation Frequency**
	+ Select how often you would like to make donations (e.g., One time, Recurring, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Non Profit Number**
	+ Enter your non-profit number (if applicable).
* **Payment Status**
	+ Select the status of your payment (e.g., Paid, Pending, Refunded, Declined).
* **Notes**
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submission Status**
	+ Select the status of your submission (e.g., Submitted, Draft, Published).
* **Submission Date**
	+ Enter the date of your submission.
