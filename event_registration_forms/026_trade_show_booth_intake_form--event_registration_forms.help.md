# Trade Show Booth Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vital details about trade show booths, allowing event organizers to effectively manage booth registrations and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact person's name and information in the "Contact Person" field.
2. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Email" field.
3. Provide the phone number of the contact person in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
5. Choose the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
6. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
7. Select the type of booth you will be setting up in the "Booth Type" field. You can choose from multiple options.
8. Enter the name of your company in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (`trade_show_booth_intake_form`, text, required): This field is for the person who will be the point of contact for the booth. Enter the name and any other relevant information for the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Booth Type** (`booth_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of booth you will be setting up. Multiple options can be chosen.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* This form is used for multiple trade shows or events, so be clear and specific with your information.
