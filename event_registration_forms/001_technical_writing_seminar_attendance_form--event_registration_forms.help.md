# technical_writing_seminar_attendance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The technical writing seminar attendance form is designed to collect details about participants attending the technical writing seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the seminar title field with the title of the seminar you are attending.
2. Enter your participant name in the participant name field.
3. Select your role from the dropdown menu (e.g. attendee, speaker, organizer).
4. Enter your date of attendance in the date field.
5. Select the time of your attendance from the time options.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the note field.
9. If you are filling out the form on behalf of someone else, enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
10. Finally, enter the date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Title (Text)**: Enter the title of the seminar you are attending.
* **Participant Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role (Select One)**: Select your role at the seminar from the options provided.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Time (Time)**: Select the time of your attendance.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address associated with you.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **Submitted By (Text)**: If you are filling out the form on behalf of someone else, enter their name.
* **Submitted On (Date)**: Enter the date you submitted the form.
