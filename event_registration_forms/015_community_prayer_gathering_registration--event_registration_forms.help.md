# Community Prayer Gathering Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Community Prayer Gathering Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and any guest information you'd like to register.
2. Check the box if you'd like to share a prayer request.
3. Indicate your interest in volunteering for the event.
4. Select your volunteer roles (if applicable).
5. Provide any specific accessibility needs or dietary restrictions you have.
6. Sign up for our weekly mailing list (if you'd like to receive updates on future events).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can reach out with updates.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Total Number Attending** (`attendee_total_count`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of attendees, including yourself and any guests.
* **Names of Your Guests** (`guest_names_list`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of any guests you plan to bring (if applicable).
* **Would you like to share a prayer request?** (`prayer_request_intent_check`, `select_one`, required): Check this box if you'd like to share a prayer request.
* **Prayer Request Description** (`prayer_request_description_text`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the prayer request you'd like to share (if applicable).
* **May we share this request during the gathering?** (`prayer_request_sharing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select how you'd like to handle your prayer request sharing (publicly or privately).
* **Are you interested in volunteering for this event?** (`volunteer_interest_check`, `select_one`, required): Check this box if you're interested in volunteering for the event.
* **Volunteer Roles** (`volunteer_roles_selection`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any volunteer roles you'd like to take on (if applicable).
* **Any Specific Accessibility Needs?** (`accessibility_needs_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific accessibility needs, please let us know so we can arrange for accommodations.
* **Dietary Restrictions for Refreshments** (`dietary_restrictions_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please let us know so we can provide alternatives.
* **Join Our Weekly Mailing List?** (`mailing_list_join_check`, `select_one`, required): Check this box if you'd like to join our weekly mailing list to receive updates on future events.
* **Today's Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Select the current date for your registration.
