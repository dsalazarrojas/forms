# Workplace Modernization Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a Workplace Modernization Lab training session. Please complete the form to provide your details and register for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant details in the "participant_details" field.
2. Select your interests from the "interests" field.
3. Choose your preferred session time in the "preferred_session_time" field.
4. Provide your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
5. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions in the "terms_agreement" field.
6. Specify your participant interests in the "participant_interests" field.
7. Choose the session time and date in the "session_time" and "session_date" fields respectively.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
10. Enter any additional agreement or comments in the "agreement" field.
10. Finally, select the session duration in the "session_duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing your participant details. Please enter your name and any other relevant information as requested.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select your interests related to the training session. This will help us tailor the session to your needs.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_session_time`, select_one, optional): Choose the time slot you prefer for the session.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact details. Please enter your address, phone number, and any other relevant information.
* **Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, text, optional): Specify your participant interests in the training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Choose the session time that suits you best.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Choose the session date that suits you best.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, optional): Enter any additional agreement or comments.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, optional): Select the session duration that suits you best.
