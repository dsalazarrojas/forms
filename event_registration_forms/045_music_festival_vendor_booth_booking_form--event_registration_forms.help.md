# Music Festival Vendor Booth Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for vendor registration for the music festival. Fill out the form to provide your booth information, preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields as marked.
* Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple fields.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vendor Info
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of your business or individual vendor.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter your business or individual address.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

### Booth Preferences
* This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about your booth preferences.

### Booth Location
* **Booth Location**: Select the location where you want to set up your booth from the options: Main Stage, Side Stage, Tent, Outdoor Area, or Vendor Lounge. This is a required field.

### Vendor Name
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of your business or individual vendor. This is a required field.

### Vendor Address
* **Vendor Address**: Enter your business or individual address. This is a required field.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Person**: Enter your contact person's name. (Optional)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact person's phone number. (Optional)
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact person's email address. (Optional)
* **Contact Notes**: Add any additional contact information. (Optional)

### Payment and Terms
* **Booth Type**: Select the type of booth you want to set up from the options: Standard, Premium, or Custom. This is a required field.
* **Booth Size**: Select the size of your booth from the options: Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large. This is a required field.
* **Vendor Services**: Select the services you will be providing from the options: Food, Drink, Merchandise, Music Equipment, or Other. This is a required field.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will be using from the options: Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer. This is a required field.
* **Tax ID**: Enter your tax ID number. This is a required field.
* **Vendor License**: Enter your vendor license number. This is a required field.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms from the options: Net 30, Net 60, Net 90, or On Invoice. This is a required field.
* **Terms of Agreement**: Select whether you agree to the terms of agreement. This is a required field.
* **Terms of Service**: Select whether you agree to the terms of service. This is a required field.
* **Terms of Payment**: Select whether you agree to the terms of payment. This is a required field.
* **Review Policy**: Select whether you agree to the review policy. This is a required field.

### Special Requests
* **Special Requests**: Add any additional requests or notes. (Optional)

### Additional Documents
* **Vendor License Number**: Enter your vendor license number. (Optional)
* **Booth Notes**: Add any additional notes about your booth. (Optional)
