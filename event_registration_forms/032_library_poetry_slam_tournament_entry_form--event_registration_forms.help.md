# Library Poetry Slam Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Poetry Slam Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a poetry slam tournament held at a library. The form helps to gather essential details from performers, including their name, poem, event date, and performance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your details**: Fill in the required fields with your name and the name of your poem.
2. **Provide your poem**: Enter your poem in the designated field.
3. **Select your event date**: Choose the date of the event you wish to participate in.
4. **Submit your entry**: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the submit button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### library_poetry_slam_tournament_entry_form (Required: false)
* **library_poetry_slam_tournament_entry_form**: This field is the main entry section for the library poetry slam tournament. Please enter your details as instructed above.

### performer (Required: false)
* **Performer**: This field is for you to enter your name, which will be associated with your poem.

### name (Required: false)
* **Name**: This field is for you to enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the performer of the poem.

### poem (Required: false)
* **Poem**: This field is where you will enter the text of your poem.

### event_date (Required: false)
* **Event Date**: This field is for you to select the date of the event you wish to participate in.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly to ensure your entry is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
