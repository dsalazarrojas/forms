# Christening Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christening Invitation Form is a tool used to collect essential information for the christening ceremony and reception of a child. This form will be used by parents or guardians to provide details about the child, ceremony, and other important aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the child's information, including their name, date of birth, and gender.
2. Enter the parents' information, including their names and contact details.
3. Specify the ceremony details, such as date, time, and location.
4. Choose the reception details, including whether there will be a reception, its location, and the preferred RSVP method.
5. Confirm your RSVP information and provide any additional notes or special requests.
