# Dance Workshop Schedule Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Workshop Schedule Booking Form is used to book a dance workshop by providing personal details and schedule preferences. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Choose your preferred schedule for the dance workshop.
3. Indicate your booking status.
4. Provide contact information for yourself and the instructor (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Workshop Schedule Booking Form** (`dance_workshop_schedule_booking_form`, text, required/optional: false): This is the form title and is not a field to fill out, but it's there to identify the form.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/true): Fill out your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/true): Enter your valid email address to enable communication with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/false): This is your contact phone number. You can leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/true): Choose the date you'd like to attend the dance workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/false): If you want to attend at a specific time, please select it from the provided options.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select_one, required/true): Choose 'Yes' if you'd like to attend the workshop at your chosen date and time. If you're unsure or need to change your schedule, choose 'No'.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`, select_multiple, required/false): Indicate if you'd like to be actively involved in the workshop or not by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required/false): Fill out the name of the instructor if you have one assigned to you.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, email, required/false): Enter the email address of the assigned instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, text, required/false): Leave this blank if no instructor is assigned.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and completeness of the form to avoid any issues.
- Double-check your personal details before submitting.
- Choose your schedule preferences correctly to ensure a smooth booking process.
