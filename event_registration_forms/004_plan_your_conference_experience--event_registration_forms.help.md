# Plan Your Conference Experience - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help conference organizers plan and tailor their event experience according to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill in the required fields with the conference title, start time, and end time.
2. Select the breakout sessions you'd like to have during the conference.
3. Choose your preferred session times (morning, afternoon, or evening) for each session.
4. Indicate your preferences for each session type (e.g., yes or no).
5. Add any additional session preferences or workshop preferences as needed.
6. Provide any additional notes about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Title** (`conference_experience`, text): Enter a brief title for your conference.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text): Enter the name of the person or team organizing the event.
* **Breakout Sessions** (`breakout_sessions`, select_multiple): Select the sessions you want to include in your conference.
* **Conference Days** (`conference_days`, number): Specify the number of days your conference will take place.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of your conference.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time): Enter the end time of your conference.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple): Select the sessions you'd prefer to have during your conference.
* **Session Preferences Options** (`session_preferences_options`, select_one): Select specific session preferences from the available options.
* **Session Preferences Options 1-11** (`session_preferences_options_1-11`, select_one): Select specific session preferences from the available options for each session type.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple): Select the workshops you'd like to include in your conference.
* **Workshop Preferences Options** (`workshop_preferences_options`, select_one): Select specific workshop preferences.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Leave any additional notes about your conference experience.
