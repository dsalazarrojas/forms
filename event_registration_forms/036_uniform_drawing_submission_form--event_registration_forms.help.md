# uniform_drawing_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The uniform_drawing_submission_form is a form for artists to submit their work for a specific event. Please use this form to submit your unique artwork for review and consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your artist information in the **Artist Info** field. This is not mandatory.
2. Enter the details about your **Drawing Info** in the field below. Again, this is not required.
3. Upload your drawing file in the **Upload** field. Please note that file size and type limitations may apply.
4. Fill out the **Event Info** field with any relevant information about the event.
5. Select your preferred options for the **Review** field. You can choose one or more options from the list.
6. Choose the relevant **Competition** option for your drawing.
7. Enter the **Drawing ID** of your artwork, if applicable.
8. Select the date of the event from the **Event Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist Info
* **Artist Info (Artist Info)**: Enter your artist name, email, and any other relevant information.

### Drawing Info
* **Drawing Info (Drawing Info)**: Describe your drawing in a few words. This could include its name, size, and any notable features.

### Upload
* **Upload (Upload)**: Upload your drawing file. Please ensure it is in a supported format (e.g., JPEG, PNG).

### Event Info
* **Event Info (Event Info)**: Provide any relevant details about the event, such as the theme, location, or date.

### Review
* **Review (Review)**: Select one or more options to describe your drawing (e.g., "My artwork is a unique combination of color and texture", "My artwork is inspired by nature", etc.).

### Competition
* **Competition (Competition)**: Choose the competition category for your drawing (e.g., "Best of show", "People's choice", etc.).

### Drawing ID
* **Drawing ID (Drawing ID)**: Enter the unique ID of your drawing, if you have one.

### Event Date
* **Event Date (Event Date)**: Select the date of the event where your artwork will be displayed.
