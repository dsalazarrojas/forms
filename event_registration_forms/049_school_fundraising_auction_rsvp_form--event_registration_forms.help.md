# School Fundraising Auction RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect attendee information and RSVP status for a school fundraising auction event. It will help event organizers track attendee contact info, event details, and attendee participation.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out all required fields: event title, contact info, and rsvp status.
- For optional fields, fill out as much information as you feel necessary.
- Use the select multiple field for the attending status, you may select multiple options.
- For the will pay for field, select True if you will pay for all of your tickets, False if you will not, and Partial if you will pay for part of your tickets.
- Use the number of guests field to input the total number of guests you will be attending with.
- In the event details field, you can input any additional information about your participation in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: The date of the fundraising auction event. Please input the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: The time of the fundraising auction event. Please input the time of the event.
* **Event Title**: The title of the fundraising auction event. This is a required field.
* **Name**: Your name. This is a required field.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the person to contact for your RSVP.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address. This is where we will contact you for event updates.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person we will contact for your RSVP.
* **RSVP Message**: Any additional information about your RSVP.
* **Number of Guests**: The total number of guests you will be attending with. Please input the number of guests.
* **Will Pay for**: This field indicates whether you will pay for all of your tickets or not. Select True, False, or Partial.
* **Attending Status**: Your status for the event. Select True, Maybe, or False to indicate whether you will attend, might attend, or will not attend.
* **Event Details**: Any additional information about your participation in the event.
