# sustainability_in_leasing_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sustainability In Leasing Seminar Registration Form" is a non-mandatory form designed for attendees to provide their details for event management and follow-up purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field. This is the name that will be displayed on the event materials and communications.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This will be used for event updates and follow-up communications.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This is an optional field that will allow us to contact you in case of last-minute updates or changes.
4. Select your desired **Date** for the event. Please note that the event date and time are pre-defined and cannot be changed.
5. Choose your preferred **Time** for the event. Please note that the event time is pre-defined and cannot be changed.
6. If you have any **Note** or special requests, please enter them in the "Note" field. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
7. Enter your company name in the "Company" field. This will help us to provide you with relevant information and updates during the event.
8. Enter your job **Position** in the "Position" field. This will help us to provide you with relevant information and updates during the event.
9. Enter your role within the organization in the "Role" field. This will help us to provide you with relevant information and updates during the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on event materials and communications.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for event updates and follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of last-minute updates or changes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you would like to attend the event. Please note that the event date and time are pre-defined and cannot be changed.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you would like to attend the event. Please note that the event time is pre-defined and cannot be changed.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any note or special requests you have for the event. This is an optional field and can be left blank if not applicable.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name to receive relevant information and updates during the event.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job position to receive relevant information and updates during the event.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): Enter your role within the organization to receive relevant information and updates during the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our event organizers for assistance.
- The event organizers will use the provided information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.
