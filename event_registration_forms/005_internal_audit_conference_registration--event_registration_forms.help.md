# internal audit conference registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Internal Audit Conference Registration form allows conference attendees to provide their preferences and requirements for each session during the conference. This form is used by conference organizers to plan and prepare for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your preferences and requirements are recorded correctly.
2. Start by entering your **Event Name** in the first field.
3. Enter your **Name** in the second field.
4. Enter your **Email** in the third field.
5. Select your **Session Preferences** for each session by choosing the options that apply.
6. For each session, enter any specific **Session Preferences** or **Session Sessions Required** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: A field for you to enter the name of the event.
* **Name**: A field for you to enter your name.
* **Email**: A field for you to enter your email address.
* **Session Preferences**: A field where you can select the sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Session 1**: A field where you can select whether or not you are interested in Session 1.
* **Session 1 Preference**: A field for you to enter any specific preference you have for Session 1.
* **Session 2**: A field where you can select whether or not you are interested in Session 2.
* **Session 2 Preference**: A field for you to enter any specific preference you have for Session 2.
* **Session 3**: A field where you can select whether or not you are interested in Session 3.
* **Session 3 Preference**: A field for you to enter any specific preference you have for Session 3.
* **Session 4**: A field where you can select whether or not you are interested in Session 4.
* **Session 4 Preference**: A field for you to enter any specific preference you have for Session 4.
* **Session 5**: A field where you can select whether or not you are interested in Session 5.
* **Session 5 Preference**: A field for you to enter any specific preference you have for Session 5.
* **Session 1 Sessions Required**: A field for you to enter any specific sessions you require for Session 1.
* **Session 2 Sessions Required**: A field for you to enter any specific sessions you require for Session 2.
* **Session 3 Sessions Required**: A field for you to enter any specific sessions you require for Session 3.
* **Session 4 Sessions Required**: A field for you to enter any specific sessions you require for Session 4.
* **Session 5 Sessions Required**: A field for you to enter any specific sessions you require for Session 5.
* **Submitted By**: A field for you to enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure that your preferences and requirements are recorded correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact conference organizers.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that everything is correct.
