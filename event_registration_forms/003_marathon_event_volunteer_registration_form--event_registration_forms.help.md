# marathon_event_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marathon_event_volunteer_registration_form is used to collect information from potential event volunteers. Please complete the form accurately and completely to ensure that your volunteer information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure you complete the form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your volunteer role and the events you are registered for.
3. Enter your availability start and end dates if applicable.
4. Provide your availability in hours for each event.
5. Fill in your special skills, if any.
6. Enter contact information for your emergency contact, including phone number, email, and relationship.
7. Sign the volunteer agreement form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information (id: 1)**: This field is used to collect general information about you as a volunteer. It is not a required field.
* **name (id: 2)**: Enter your name accurately and completely.
* **email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address accurately and completely.
* **phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number accurately and completely. This field is not required.
* **volunteer_role (id: 5)**: Select your volunteer role from the provided options.
* **event_name (id: 6)**: Select all the events you are registered for from the provided options.
* **start_date (id: 7)**: Enter your start date, if applicable.
* **end_date (id: 8)**: Enter your end date, if applicable. This field is not required.
* **volunteer_hours (id: 9)**: Enter your availability in hours for each event.
* **special_skills (id: 10)**: List any special skills you have, if applicable. This field is not required.
* **emergency_contact (id: 11)**: Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_relationship (id: 12)**: Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **emergency_phone (id: 13)**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_email (id: 14)**: Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **volunteer_agreement (id: 15)**: Review and sign the volunteer agreement form.
* **volunteer_agreement_date (id: 16)**: Enter the date you signed the volunteer agreement. This field is not required.
* **volunteer_agreement_signed (id: 17)**: Sign that you have signed the agreement.
* **submitters_name (id: 18)**: Enter your name if you are registering someone else.
* **submitters_email (id: 19)**: Enter your email if you are registering someone else.
* **submitters_phone (id: 20)**: Enter your phone number if you are registering someone else.
* **submitters_agreement (id: 21)**: Review and sign the agreement for the person you are registering.
* **submitters_agreement_date (id: 22)**: Enter the date you signed the agreement for the person you are registering. This field is not required.
* **submitters_agreement_signed (id: 23)**: Sign that you have signed the agreement for the person you are registering.
* **submitters_ip (id: 24)**: Enter your IP address if you are registering someone else. This field is not required.
* **submitters_country (id: 25)**: Enter the country of the person you are registering, if applicable. This field is not required.

By following these steps and field explanations, you should be able to complete the form correctly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
