# recreational_activity_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recreational activity check-out form is used to collect and verify participant and staff information for recreational activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information, including their name and email address.
2. Select the type of equipment used or not used during the activity.
3. Record any equipment serial numbers if applicable.
4. Provide your signature as a participant to verify your identity.
5. Select and enter your name and signature as staff.
6. Record any contact information for staff as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of the activity.
* **Check-out Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of check-out.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether equipment was used or not.
* **Equipment Serial Numbers** (`equipment_serial_numbers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any serial numbers for equipment used.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as a participant to verify your identity.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as staff.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as staff to verify your identity.
* **Staff Contact** (`staff_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter any contact information for staff as needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Verify that participant and staff signatures are genuine.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
