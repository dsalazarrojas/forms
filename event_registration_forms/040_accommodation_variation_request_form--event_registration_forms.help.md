# Accommodation Variation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests requesting variations to their booking. Please use this form to provide accurate and relevant information about your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form instructions and fields.
2. Enter your full name as per your identification.
3. Provide your booking reference number for accurate tracking.
4. Enter your email address for confirmation and communication.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Enter the original check-in and check-out dates of your booking.
7. Select the type of variation you are requesting (e.g., date change, room type change, etc.).
8. If applicable, select new check-in and check-out dates for your variation.
9. If applicable, select a new room type for your variation.
10. If applicable, provide the number of additional nights you are requesting.
11. Provide a clear explanation for your variation request.
12. If applicable, mention any special requirements or accessibility needs related to your variation.
13. Select your smoking preference for the new accommodation.
14. If applicable, choose to change or add your meal plan.
15. Indicate by what date you expect a response to your variation request.
16. Provide any additional comments about your request.
17. Declare that the information provided is accurate and understand that variations are subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (`guest_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as per your identification.
* **Booking Reference Number** (`booking_reference_number`, text, required): Enter your booking reference number for accurate tracking.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and communication.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Original Check In Date** (`original_check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the original check-in date of your booking.
* **Original Check Out Date** (`original_check_out_date`, date, required): Enter the original check-out date of your booking.
* **Original Room Type** (`original_room_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of room originally booked.
* **Variation Request Type** (`variation_request_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of variation you are requesting.
* **Requested New Check In Date** (`requested_new_check_in_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the new check-in date.
* **Requested New Check Out Date** (`requested_new_check_out_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the new check-out date.
* **Requested Room Type** (`requested_room_type`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the new room type.
* **Number of Additional Nights** (`number_of_additional_nights`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of additional nights you are requesting.
* **Reason for Variation** (`reason_for_variation`, text, required): Explain the reason for your variation request.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any special requirements related to your variation.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select any accessibility needs related to your variation.
* **Smoking Preference** (`smoking_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your smoking preference for the new accommodation.
* **Meal Plan Change** (`meal_plan_change`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose to change or add your meal plan.
* **Expected Response Date** (`expected_response_date`, date, required): Indicate by what date you expect a response to your variation request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your request.
* **Guest Declaration** (`guest_declaration`, select_one, required): Declare that the information provided is accurate and understand that variations are subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

## Tips
- Please ensure accuracy and completeness of information provided.
- Be as detailed as possible when explaining your reason for variation.
- If you have any special requirements or accessibility needs, please mention them for proper accommodation.
- Responses may take some time, so please allow the necessary time frame for processing.
