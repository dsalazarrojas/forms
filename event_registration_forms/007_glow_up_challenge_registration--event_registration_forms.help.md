# Glow Up Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Glow Up Challenge Registration" form is designed to collect participant information for the Glow Up Challenge event. Please complete this form to register yourself and/or your team (if applicable) for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "participant_info" field, which is the primary identifier for this form. If you're a team leader, enter your name to represent your team.
2. In the "challenge_info" field, provide a brief description about the challenge you'll be participating in (e.g., which challenge level, date, or specific details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_info** (participant_info, text, required): Enter your name as the primary identifier for this registration.
* **challenge_info** (challenge_info, text, required): Provide a brief description about the challenge you'll be participating in.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out both fields completely and accurately to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* If you're registering as a team, make sure to enter your team name or a unique identifier so we can track your team's progress.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out this form, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
