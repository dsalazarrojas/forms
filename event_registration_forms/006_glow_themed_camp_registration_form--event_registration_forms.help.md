# Glow Themed Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for the Glow Themed Camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Ensure you've reviewed the form purpose and instructions.
* Fill out each field with the required and optional information as specified.
* Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Info (page 1)
* **Participant Info** (`participant info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on camp materials and communication.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or special requests that we should know before your arrival.

### Participant Preferences (page 2)
* **Participant Preferences** (`preferences`, text, required: false): Share any information about yourself that would help us personalize your experience, e.g., dietary restrictions or favorite activities.
* **Participant Preferences Options** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **Yes**: I'd like to participate in activities that include physical exertion.
	+ **No**: I'd rather avoid activities that include physical exertion.

### Preferred Activities (page 3)
* **Preferred Activities** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ **Yes**: I'd like to participate in this activity.
	+ **No**: I'd rather avoid this activity.

## Tips
* Please fill out as much information as possible to help us provide a safe and enjoyable experience.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the camp organizers for assistance.
* We'll review and update your information after submission.
