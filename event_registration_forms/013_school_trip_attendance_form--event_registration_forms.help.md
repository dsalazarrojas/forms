# school_trip_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school trip attendance form is used to collect and track information for school events, including student attendance and permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event ID** (a unique identifier for the event) in the designated field.
2. Enter the **Event Name** of the trip in the corresponding field.
3. Choose the **Trip Date** for the event from the calendar picker.
4. Enter the **Student ID** of the student participating in the trip.
5. Enter the **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** of the student.
6. Select the **Parent Permission Prophets** for the student.
7. Select the **Student Permission Prophets** for the student.
8. Enter the **Student Permission Notes** for any additional information.
9. Choose the **Student Permission Status** (Allowed or Not Allowed).
10. Enter the **Student Permission Expiration Date** if applicable.
11. Choose the **Event Status** (Active or Inactive).
12. Enter any additional **Notes** about the event or student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID** (Required: True): A unique identifier for the event.
* **Event Name** (Required: True): The name of the event.
* **Trip Date** (Required: True): The date of the trip.
* **Student ID** (Required: True): The student's unique identifier.
* **Name** (Required: True): The student's name.
* **Email** (Required: True): The student's email address.
* **Phone** (Required: True): The student's phone number.
* **Parent Permission Prophets** (Required: True): Select one or more options for parental permission.
* **Student Permission Prophets** (Required: True): Select one or more options for student permission.
* **Student Permission Notes** (Required: True): Additional notes for student permission.
* **Student Permission Status** (Required: True): Choose whether the student is allowed or not allowed to attend.
* **Student Permission Expiration Date** (Required: False): The date when student permission expires.
* **Student Permission Status Expiration Date** (Required: False): The date when student permission status expires.
* **Student Permission Remarks** (Required: False): Additional comments for student permission.
* **Student Permission Presentation** (Required: False): The time of the student permission presentation.
* **Student Permission Status Presentation** (Required: False): The time of the student permission status presentation.
* **Student Permission Status Presentation Date** (Required: False): The date of the student permission status presentation.
* **Student Permission Status Presentation Time** (Required: False): The time of the student permission status presentation.
* **Notes** (Required: True): Additional notes about the event or student.
* **Event Status** (Required: True): Choose whether the event is active or inactive.
* **Event Status Reason** (Required: False): Additional reason for event status.
* **Student Status** (Required: True): Choose whether the student is active or inactive.
* **Student Status Notes** (Required: False): Additional notes for student status.
* **Student Status Reason** (Required: False): Additional reason for student status.
* **Notes Notes** (Required: False): Additional notes about the student status.
