# literature_festival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The literature festival registration form is designed to collect information from participants about their literary works and personal details for the purpose of registering them for the literature festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure you attach any supporting documents as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (Required): Please fill in your name in this field.
* **Participant Email** (Required): Enter a valid email ID that can be used to contact you.
* **Participant Phone** (Optional): Fill in your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Participant Address** (Optional): Provide your address for our records.
* **Participant City** (Optional): Enter your city name.
* **Participant State** (Optional): Select your state.
* **Participant Postal Code** (Optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Participant Gender** (Required): Choose your gender.
* **Participant Age** (Required): Enter your age.
* **Participant Country** (Required): Enter your country.
* **Event Name** (Required): Fill in the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Start Date** (Required): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (Required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (Required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Literature Category** (Required): Fill in the category of your literary work.
* **Event Organizer** (Required): Fill in the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email** (Required): Enter the email ID of the event organizer.
* **Event Description** (Required): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Literature Genre** (Optional): Fill in the genre of your literary work.
* **Literature Type** (Optional): Fill in the type of your literary work.
* **Literature Language** (Optional): Fill in the language of your literary work.
* **Participant Notes** (Optional): Add any additional notes about yourself.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form. 

This guide is for end users who will be using the form to register for the literature festival. 

Please let me know if you need any further clarification or modifications.
