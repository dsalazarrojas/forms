# yoga_class_signup_schedule_preference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information and preferences for signing up for a yoga class. Users can specify their class preference, schedule, instructor preference, and payment information to ensure a smooth and organized class management process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info field with your personal details.
2. Select your preferred class time from the Class Preference field.
3. Choose your preferred schedule date using the Schedule Preference field.
4. Select your preferred instructor from the Instructor Preference field.
5. Enter your payment information in the Payment Info field.
6. Add any additional information you would like to provide in the Additional Info field (this is optional).
7. Confirm your submission by checking the Confirm field.
8. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting I Agree from the Agree field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`User Info`, `text`, required): Fill out this field with your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information you would like to provide.
* **Class Preference** (`Class Preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred class time from the provided options (6:00 AM, 7:00 AM, 8:00 AM).
* **Schedule Preference** (`Schedule Preference`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred schedule date.
* **Instructor Preference** (`Instructor Preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred instructor from the provided options (John, Jane, Bob).
* **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, `text`, required): Enter your payment information, including any relevant details about your payment method and any applicable discounts or promotions.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information you would like to provide about your class preference or payment information, enter it here.
* **Confirm** (`Confirm`, `note`, required): Confirm your submission by checking this field.
* **Agree** (`Agree`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting I Agree.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately select your class preference and schedule date to ensure that you are registered for the correct class.
* Double-check your payment information to avoid any processing errors.
* Be aware that your instructor preference may be subject to change based on availability and other factors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at contact@yogaclass.com.
* By submitting this form, you are agreeing to our terms and conditions, which can be found on our website.
