# Prayer And Fasting Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a prayer and fasting event. It collects information about the fasting initiative, participants, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Fasting Initiative Name field with a brief description of the fasting initiative you are participating in.
2. Enter the name(s) of the participant(s) participating in the fasting initiative in the Participant Name field.
3. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of the fasting initiative in the Fasting Start Date and Fasting End Date fields, respectively.
4. Specify the duration of the fasting initiative in the Fasting Duration field.
5. Enter the time of the prayer session in the Prayer Time field.
6. Indicate the frequency of the fasting initiative in the Fasting Frequency field.
7. Enter the email address of the participant in the Participant Email field.
8. If necessary, enter the name of the contact person responsible for the fasting initiative in the Contact Person field.
9. Specify the fasting group in the Fasting Group field.
10. Select the type of fasting being observed in the Fasting Type field.
11. Enter the location of the fasting event in the Fasting Location field.
12. Specify the diet observed during the fasting initiative in the Fasting Diet field.
13. Enter any additional notes about the fasting initiative in the Fasting Notes field.
14. If applicable, select the date of the event from the Event Date field.
15. If applicable, select the time of the event from the Event Time field.
16. If applicable, enter the location of the event in the Event Location field.
17. Select the participants involved in the Fasting Participants field.
18. Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the fasting initiative in the Fasting Organizer field.
19. Select the coordinators responsible for the fasting initiative in the Fasting Coordinators field.
20. Select the participation terms for the fasting initiative in the Fasting Participation Terms field.
21. Enter any comments about the participants in the Fasting Participants Comment field.
22. Enter the agreement of the participants in the Fasting Participants Agreement field.
23. Enter the agreement of the coordinators in the Fasting Coordinators Agreement field.
24. Enter the agreement of the organizer in the Fasting Organizer Agreement field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fasting Initiative Name** (`fasting_initiative_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the fasting initiative you are participating in.
* **Participants Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) of the participant(s) participating in the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Start Date** (`fasting_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the fasting initiative if applicable.
* **Fasting End Date** (`fasting_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the fasting initiative if applicable.
* **Fasting Duration** (`fasting_duration`, number, optional): Specify the duration of the fasting initiative.
* **Prayer Time** (`prayer_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the prayer session.
* **Fasting Frequency** (`fasting_frequency`, text, optional): Indicate the frequency of the fasting initiative.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the fasting initiative if necessary.
* **Fasting Group** (`fasting_group`, text, optional): Specify the fasting group.
* **Fasting Type** (`fasting_type`, text, optional): Select the type of fasting being observed.
* **Fasting Location** (`fasting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the fasting event.
* **Fasting Diet** (`fasting_diet`, text, optional): Specify the diet observed during the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Notes** (`fasting_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the fasting initiative.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If applicable, select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If applicable, select the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the location of the event.
* **Fasting Participants** (`fasting_participants`, select_multiple, optional): Select the participants involved in the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Organizer** (`fasting_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Coordinators** (`fasting_coordinators`, select_multiple, optional): Select the coordinators responsible for the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Participation Terms** (`fasting_participation_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Select the participation terms for the fasting initiative.
* **Fasting Participants Comment** (`fasting_participants_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the participants.
* **Fasting Participants Agreement** (`fasting_participants_agreement`, note, optional): Enter the agreement of the participants.
* **Fasting Coordinators Agreement** (`fasting_coordinators_agreement`, note, optional): Enter the agreement of the coordinators.
* **Fasting Organizer Agreement** (`fasting_organizer_agreement`, note, optional): Enter the agreement of the organizer.
