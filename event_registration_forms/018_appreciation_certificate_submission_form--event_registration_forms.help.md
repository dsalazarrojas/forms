# Appreciation Certificate Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appreciation Certificate Submission Form is used to gather information from the submitter for the purpose of issuing an appreciation certificate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill out the **Your Name** field with your name and surname.
2.  Fill out the **Email Address** field with your valid email address.
3.  If you want, fill out the **Phone Number** field with your phone number.
4.  If you are a member of an organization or company, fill out the **Organization or Company** field.
5.  You can add any additional information in the **Certificate Details** field.
6.  Fill out the **Certificate Recipient Name** field with the name of the person receiving the certificate.
7.  Select the **Award or Appreciation Category** from the drop-down menu that matches the reason for the award field.
8.  Fill out the **Award Date** field with the date the award was given.
9.  Fill out the **Reason for Certificate** field with a brief description of why the certificate is being issued.
10. You can add additional details about the certificate in the **Key Achievements or Accomplishments** field.
11. Select the **Preferred Certificate Design Style** from the drop-down menu, if desired.
12. Add any additional comments or details in the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name** (`submitter_name`): This is your name and surname.
*   **Email Address** (`submitter_email`): This is your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`): This is your phone number, if you want to provide it.
*   **Organization or Company** (`organization`): This is the name of your organization or company, if you are a member.
*   **Certificate Details** (`certificate_details`): This is an optional field where you can add any additional information about the certificate.
*   **Certificate Recipient Name** (`recipient_name`): This is the name of the person receiving the certificate.
*   **Award or Appreciation Category** (`award_category`): This is a drop-down menu that matches the reason for the award field.
*   **Award Date** (`award_date`): This is the date the award was given.
*   **Reason for Certificate** (`reason_for_award`): This is a brief description of why the certificate is being issued.
*   **Key Achievements or Accomplishments** (`achievement_highlights`): This is an optional field where you can add any additional details about the certificate.
*   **Preferred Certificate Design Style** (`certificate_design`): This is a drop-down menu to choose the style of the certificate.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): This is an optional field where you can add any additional comments or details.
