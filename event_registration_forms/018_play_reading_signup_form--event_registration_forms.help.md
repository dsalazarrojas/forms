# Play Reading Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Play Reading Signup Form is designed to collect information about participants' availability and roles for a play reading event. This form helps us determine the best fit for each role and ensures that we have an accurate headcount for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first and last name.
2. Select your availability for the event.
3. Choose the roles you are interested in participating in.
4. If you have any participant details, please provide them in the text field.
5. Enter your contact email and phone number (optional).
6. If you are representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization.
7. Add a brief description of your role (if applicable).
8. Select your availability for any additional roles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Role** (select_one, required): Choose whether you are available for this role or not.
* **Availability** (select_multiple, required): Select the days and times you are available for the event.
* **Participant Details** (text, required): Provide any additional details about your participation in the event.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter your contact email (if different from your work email).
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Role Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role (if applicable).
* **Role Additional Info** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your role.
* **Availability 1-5** (date/time, optional): Select the days and times you are available for each role.
* **Role 1-10** (select_multiple, optional): Choose which roles you are interested in participating in for each availability slot.
