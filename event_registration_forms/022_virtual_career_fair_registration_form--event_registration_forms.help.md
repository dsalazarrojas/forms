# Virtual Career Fair Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for companies to register for the Virtual Career Fair. It allows you to provide information about your company, such as company name, event name, and contact details, to facilitate a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your company.
2. Make sure to answer all the required questions truthfully.
3. Preview your submission before submitting it to ensure that all details are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (`company_name`, text, Required)
Enter your company's name as it should appear on the fair's promotional materials.

### Company Logo (`company_logo`, text, Optional)
If you have a logo for your company, you can provide it here. This will be used for branding purposes.

### Event Name (`event_name`, text, Required)
Enter the name of the event you are participating in.

### Start Date (`start_date`, date, Required)
Enter the start date of the event.

### End Date (`end_date`, date, Required)
Enter the end date of the event.

### Booth Type (`booth_type`, select_multiple, Required)
Select whether your company will be participating as a booth, sponsor, or neither.

### Booth Size (`booth_size`, select_one, Optional)
Select the size of your booth. Please note that booth size affects the pricing for the event.

### Booth Layout (`booth_layout`, select_one, Optional)
Select the layout of your booth. This will affect the placement of your booth during the event.

### Event Description (`event_description`, text, Optional)
Provide a brief description of the event.

### Company URL (`company_url`, text, Optional)
Provide the URL of your company's website.

### Company E-mail (`company_email`, email, Required)
Enter your company's email address.

### Company Phone (`company_phone`, text, Required)
Enter your company's phone number.

### Primary Contact Name (`primary_contact_name`, text, Required)
Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.

### Primary Contact Title (`primary_contact_title`, text, Optional)
Enter the title of the primary contact person.

### Primary Contact E-mail (`primary_contact_email`, email, Required)
Enter the email address of the primary contact person.

### Primary Contact Phone (`primary_contact_phone`, text, Optional)
Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.

### Booth Questions (`booth_questions`, text, Optional)
Provide any questions or requests for your booth.

### Company Type (`company_type`, select_one, Required)
Select whether your company is a job seeker, service provider, or other.

### Booth Questions 1 (`booth_questions_1`, select_multiple, Required)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Booth Questions 2 (`booth_questions_2`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Booth Questions 3 (`booth_questions_3`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Booth Questions 4 (`booth_questions_4`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Booth Questions 5 (`booth_questions_5`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Booth Questions 6 (`booth_questions_6`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select the services you provide from the options.

### Submit (`submit`, text, Required)
Click the submit button to complete the form.

Please note that all required fields must be filled out to successfully submit the form.
