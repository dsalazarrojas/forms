# Dance Rehearsal Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication between students and instructors during dance rehearsals. It's essential to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure a smooth rehearsal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Schedule" field and select the date and time of your scheduled rehearsal.
2. Enter the name of your instructor in the "Instructor" field.
3. Indicate your attendance at the rehearsal in the "Attendance" field. Select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your presence.
4. If you have any comments or feedback about the rehearsal, type them in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure that any important notifications are sent to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, `date`, required: false):
  This field is for selecting the date and time of your scheduled rehearsal. Please choose the correct date and time to help your instructor prepare accordingly.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, `text`, required: false):
  Enter the name of your instructor for this rehearsal. This will help them know who to expect and communicate with effectively.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  Indicate your attendance at the rehearsal by selecting "Yes" or "No". This helps the instructor keep track of who's attending and make necessary adjustments.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false):
  If you have any comments or feedback about the rehearsal, type them here. This is an opportunity for you to share your thoughts and suggestions with your instructor.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false):
  Enter your email address to receive important notifications about the rehearsal, such as schedule changes or updates.

## Tips
* Be accurate when selecting your schedule to ensure you don't miss the rehearsal.
* Double-check your attendance to avoid any last-minute confusion.
* Keep your comments concise and clear to help your instructor understand your thoughts effectively.
* Make sure to enter your correct email address to receive important notifications.
