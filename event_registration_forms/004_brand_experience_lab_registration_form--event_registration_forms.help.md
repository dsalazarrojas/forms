## Brand Experience Lab Registration Form - Help Guide

This form is for attendees of the Brand Experience Lab event to register for the event. It is used to collect demographic information about the attendees and their preferences to better understand the audience and tailor the event experience to their needs. 

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
2. Provide your email address where we can send event details.
3. Enter your phone number for event updates.
4. Enter your current job title.
5. Enter your company name.
6. Select your company size.
7. Choose your industry.
8. Select the session you would like to attend.
9. Choose your preferred event date.
10. Select any dietary restrictions or allergies.
11. If applicable, select any accessibility needs.
12. Rate your experience level.
13. (Optional) Enter what you hope to learn at this event.
14. Indicate your interest level in networking opportunities.
15. (Optional) Specify where you heard about this event.
16. Agree or decline to receive marketing communications from us.
17. Confirm your agreement to the event terms and conditions.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your badge.
* **Email Address**: We will send event details to this email.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for event updates.
* **Job Title**: Your current job title.
* **Company Name**: Name of your organization.
* **Company Size**: Select your company size.
* **Industry**: Choose your industry.
* **Preferred Session**: Select which session you would like to attend.
* **Event Date**: Choose your preferred date.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select if you require any accessibility accommodations.
* **Brand Experience Level**: Rate your experience level.
* **Learning Goals**: (Optional) Enter what you hope to learn at this event.
* **Networking Interest**: Indicate your interest level in networking opportunities.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: (Optional) Specify where you heard about this event.
* **Marketing Communications**: Agree or decline to receive marketing communications from us.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement to the event terms and conditions.
* **Referral Source**: (Optional) If applicable, select where you heard about this event.
* **Other**: This field is currently not included in the form but is available for additional information or other purposes not specified.
