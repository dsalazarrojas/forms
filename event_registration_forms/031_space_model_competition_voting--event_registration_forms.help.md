# space_model_competition_voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to cast their vote and provide feedback on other participants' work in a space modeling competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the participant's work and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2.  Select the participant you are voting on.
3.  Fill out the form with your vote and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **event_id** (`event_id`, `number`, required: false)
The event_id is used to identify the competition event. Please enter the number associated with the competition.

### **participant_name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the participant you are voting on.

### **judge_score** (`judge_score`, `number`, required: false)
Please rate the participant based on their work. A score of 1-10 is required.

### **vote** (`vote`, `select_one`, required: true)
Select "True" if you believe the participant's work is worthy of recognition.

### **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
Please provide any comments or feedback on the participant's work.

### **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter the participant's email address.

### **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the participant's phone number.

### **scorecard** (`scorecard`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select all the criteria that the participant's work met.
