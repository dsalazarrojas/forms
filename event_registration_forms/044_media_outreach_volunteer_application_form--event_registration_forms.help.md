# media_outreach_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in media outreach efforts. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand your interests and availability, which will enable us to match you with the right opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing your contact information, availability, skills, and preferred communication methods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false):
  This field is used to capture your name and any other relevant personal details. Please enter your name and any other information you think is essential for us to know.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false):
  This field is used to capture your contact information, such as email and phone number. Please enter your email and phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Media Skills** (`media_skills`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  This field allows you to select which media skills you possess. Please choose all the skills that apply to you.
  * `Yes` - You have the skills mentioned
  * `No` - You do not have the skills mentioned
* **Availability** (`availability`, `number`, required: false):
  This field asks for your availability in hours per week. Please enter a number that indicates how many hours per week you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required: false):
  This field is used to track the number of volunteer hours you commit to per month. Please enter the number of hours you are available to volunteer per month.
* **Preferred Media** (`preferred_media`, `select_one`, required: false):
  This field asks which media you have experience with. Please choose the media type that is most relevant to you.
  * `Yes` - You have experience with the mentioned media
  * `No` - You do not have experience with the mentioned media
* **Media Outreach Initiative** (`media_outreach_initiative`, `select_one`, required: false):
  This field asks if you are interested in being part of a media outreach initiative. Please choose whether you are interested in being part of such an initiative.
  * `Yes` - You are interested in being part of a media outreach initiative
  * `No` - You are not interested in being part of a media outreach initiative
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, `text`, required: false):
  This field is used to capture any special skills you may have. Please enter any skills you think are relevant and useful for media outreach efforts.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
  This field allows you to select which types of volunteer experience you have. Please choose all the experiences that apply to you.
  * `Yes` - You have the experience mentioned
  * `No` - You do not have the experience mentioned
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false):
  This field asks how often you volunteer. Please choose the frequency that best describes your volunteer habits.
  * `Yes` - You volunteer frequently
  * `No` - You do not volunteer frequently
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`preferred_communication_method`, `select_one`, required: false):
  This field asks how you would like to be contacted. Please choose your preferred communication method.
  * `Yes` - You prefer the mentioned communication method
  * `No` - You do not prefer the mentioned communication method
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required: false):
  This field asks which language you prefer to communicate in. Please choose the language that you are most comfortable with.
  * `Yes` - You prefer the mentioned language
  * `No` - You do not prefer the mentioned language

## Tips
Please take your time to fill out this form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
