# Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freshers’ Fest Play Submission Form is a one-time submission form for new students to submit their plays for an event. Please fill out this form to provide necessary information about your play and your details for submission.

## How To Complete This Form

### To submit a play for the event:

1.  Select "Yes" for "Submitting A Play" to confirm you have a play to submit.
2.  Enter the "Play Title" of your play, a short title should be sufficient.
3.  Write a brief "Play Description" of your play.
4.  Select the genre of your play.
5.  Enter the "Play Length In Minutes" of your play.
6.  Select the "Language" of your play.
7.  Enter a "Rating" for your play.
8.  Enter the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" of your play.
9.  Enter your "Email", "Phone", "Address", "Name", "Year Of Graduation", "Roll Number", "Batch", "Programme", "Department", "Section", "Roll", "Year Of Admission", and "Academic Year" information.
10. Select whether you have confirmed or not for "Confirmation" (this seems to be a confirmation of submission).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitting A Play**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you are submitting a play. This is a required field.
* **Play Title**: A short title of the play to be submitted.
* **Play Description**: A brief description of the play.
* **Genre**: The genre of your play.
* **Play Length In Minutes**: The length of your play in minutes.
* **Language**: The language of your play.
* **Rating**: A rating for your play.
* **Submission Date**: The date you submitted your play.
* **Submission Time**: The time you submitted your play.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Address**: Your address.
* **Name**: Your name.
* **Year Of Graduation**: The year you graduated.
* **Roll Number**: Your roll number.
* **Batch**: Your batch.
* **Programme**: The programme you are enrolled in.
* **Department**: The department you are enrolled in.
* **Section**: The section you are enrolled in.
* **Roll**: Your roll number.
* **Year Of Admission**: The year you were admitted.
* **Academic Year**: The academic year you are in.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm whether you have submitted your play or not. 

### Tips

* Please fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure successful submission.
* You can select "No" for "Submitting A Play" if you are not submitting a play.
* You can select "No" for "Confirmation" if you have not confirmed your submission. 

Please note that some fields seem to be optional or not clearly defined, so you may need to use your best judgment when filling out this form.
