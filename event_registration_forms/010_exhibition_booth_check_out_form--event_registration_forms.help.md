# Exhibition Booth Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from event attendees about their experience at the exhibition booth. It helps us understand their needs, preferences, and any additional services they may require. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select all relevant options for each field.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes if necessary.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Number**: Enter the number of your exhibition booth.
* **Booth Name**: Enter the name of your exhibition booth.
* **Check Out Date**: Select the date you checked out the exhibition.
* **Check Out Time**: Select the time you checked out the exhibition.
* **Services Selected**: Select one or more services that you used or would like to use. Examples: Service A, Service B, Service C.
* **Services Used**: Select one or more services that you used. Examples: Service A, Service B, Service C.
* **Services Ordered**: Select one or more services that you would like to order in future events. Examples: Service D, Service E, Service F.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Contact**: Provide any additional contact information if necessary.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Booth Selections**: Select one or more booth selections you would like to make.
* **Staff**: Enter the number of staff members attending from 1 to 10.
* **Parking**: Select the parking spot number or provide a description of where you parked.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Additional Services**: Select one or more additional services you would like to use in future events. Examples: Service D, Service E, Service F.
* **Special Request**: Provide any special requests or requirements.
* **Services Wanted**: Select one or more services you would like to have available in future events. Examples: Service D, Service E, Service F.
* **Staff Email**: Enter your staff email address so we can contact them if needed.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter your staff phone number for quick contact.
* **Staff Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your staff experience.
* **Event Staff**: Select the event staff you will be interacting with.
* **Staff Type**: Select the type of staff you will be interacting with. Examples: Type A, Type B, Type C.
* **Staff Availability**: Select the staff availability.
* **Staff Availability**: Select the staff availability for future events.
