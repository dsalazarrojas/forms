# Social Enterprise Seminar Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record attendance information for a social enterprise seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your seminar registration information.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
3. Indicate whether you will attend the event.
4. Select the date and time of attendance.
5. Add any additional comments you want to share with the organizers.
6. Confirm your submission by selecting "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Seminar Registration
* **Seminar Registration**: Enter your seminar registration number or ID. This is an optional field and helps the organizers to track your registration.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. This is an optional field and helps the organizers to contact you.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field and helps the organizers to contact you.

### Attendees
* **Attendees**: Indicate whether you will attend the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Date of Attendance
* **Date of Attendance**: Select the date you plan to attend the event.

### Time of Attendance
* **Time of Attendance**: Select the time you plan to attend the event.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you want to share with the organizers.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes" or "No".

### Event Date
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you want to attend. This field is pre-set to default date and is not editable.

### Event Time
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you want to attend. This field is pre-set to default time and is not editable.

Tips: Please ensure that you have registered for the seminar before completing this form. If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers.
