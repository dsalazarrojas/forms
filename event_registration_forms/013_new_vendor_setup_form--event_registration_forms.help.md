# New Vendor Setup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Vendor Setup Form is used to register a new vendor for the event. It is used to collect information about the vendor and their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields. The fields with a star (\*) are mandatory.
2. Ensure that the contact email and contact phone are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Select the correct vendor type from the available options.
4. Enter a list of products that you will be selling.
5. If you have multiple operating hours, you can enter multiple values separated by commas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, text, not required): This is a unique code used to identify your company's event registration. It is used to link your company's event registrations to your vendor information.
* **Vender Name** (`vendor_name`, text, not required): Enter your company's name that will be displayed to event attendees.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address associated with your company. This will be used to contact you about event-related matters.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your company's contact phone number. This is used to contact you about event-related matters.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of vendor you are. Choose from the available options: Event, Food, Other.
* **Products** (`vendor_products`, text, not required): Enter a list of products or services that you will be offering at the event. Use commas to separate multiple products.
* **Hours** (`vendor_hours`, time, not required): Enter your operating hours in 24-hour format (HH:MM). If you have multiple operating hours, separate them with commas.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with event registration or payments.
* If you are experiencing difficulties filling out this form, contact the event organizers for assistance.
