# Solo Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solo Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for a one-time event. This form is used to gather essential details such as personal contact information, event-specific information, and registration data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal information in the "User Info" field.
2. In the "Solo Event Info" field, provide details about the event you are registering for.
3. Enter the date of the event in the "Date" field.
4. Fill in the time of the event in the "Time" field.
5. Add your email address in the "Email" field to allow us to contact you about the event.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (text)**:
	+ This field is for your personal contact information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
* **Solo Event Info (text)**:
	+ This field is to provide details about the event you are registering for, such as the event name, description, or other relevant information.
* **Date (date)**:
	+ Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time (time)**:
	+ Fill in the time of the event in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM or 24-hour format (HH:MM)).
* **Email (email)**:
	+ Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about the event.
* **Phone (text)**:
	+ If you have a phone number, please fill it in to allow us to contact you about the event.
