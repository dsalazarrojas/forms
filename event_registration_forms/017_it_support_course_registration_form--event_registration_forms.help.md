# IT Support Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the IT Support Course. It collects information about the participant, including their details, enrollment status, and program information. Please ensure you are registering for the correct course and institution before submitting this form. If you are unsure, please contact your course coordinator or instructor for guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. If you need to provide additional comments or notes, use the text boxes accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): This is your name, as you would like to be addressed.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, text, required: false): Enter your institution or affiliation.
* **Participant Course ID** (`participant_course_id`, text, required: false): Enter your course ID (if applicable).

### Enrollment Information
* **Participant Enrollment Status** (`participant_enrollment_status`, select_one, required: true): Select your current enrollment status. Options: active, inactive, pending.
* **Participant Enrollment Type** (`participant_enrollment_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your enrollment type. Options: full-time, part-time, self-funded.
* **Participant Enrollment Level** (`participant_enrollment_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your current enrollment level. Options: beginner, intermediate, advanced.
* **Participant Enrollment Start Date** (`participant_enrollment_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter your enrollment start date.
* **Participant Enrollment End Date** (`participant_enrollment_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter your expected enrollment end date.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required: false): Leave any additional comments or remarks here.

### Additional Information
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If a tool or equipment has been assigned to you, please enter that here.
* **Participant Institution Type** (`participant_institution_type`, text, required: false): Enter your institution or affiliation type.
* **Participant Program Notes** (`participant_program_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your program.
* **Participant Enrollment Type Notes** (`participant_enrollment_type_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your enrollment type.
* **Participant Date of Birth Notes** (`participant_dob_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your date of birth.
* **Participant Enrollment Status Notes** (`participant_enrollment_status_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your enrollment status.
* **Participant Enrollment Level Notes** (`participant_enrollment_level_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your enrollment level.
* **Participant Enrollment Program Notes** (`participant_program_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or remarks about your enrollment program.
