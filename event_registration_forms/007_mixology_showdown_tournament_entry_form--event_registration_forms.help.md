# Mixology Showdown Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mixology Showdown Tournament Entry Form is designed for teams to register for the Mixology Showdown competition. This form gathers information about the team, its members, and the team leader's contact details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the required information for your team.
2.  Select your team members from the available options.
3.  Enter your team name and description.
4.  Provide your team leader's name and contact information (phone and email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, text): This field is where you enter the name of your team, which will be displayed on the competition board.
*  **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple): Choose the members of your team from the list of options. You can select up to 10 team members.
*  **Team Name** (`team_name`, text): Enter a brief description of your team, including any relevant details about your team's identity and goals for the competition.
*  **Team Description** (`team_description`, text): This field is for a more detailed description of your team, including your team's background, experience, and any notable achievements.
*  **Bartender Name** (`bartender_name`, text): Enter the name of the team leader or the mixologist responsible for the team's entry.
*  **Bartender Phone** (`bartender_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the team leader or the mixologist responsible for the team's entry.
*  **Bartender Email** (`bartender_email`, email, text): Enter the email address of the team leader or the mixologist responsible for the team's entry.
