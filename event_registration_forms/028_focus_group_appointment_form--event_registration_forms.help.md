# focus_group_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and preferences for a focus group appointment. It is intended to be completed by users who wish to participate in a meeting or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your information in the fields provided, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select your preferred time slot for the appointment. You can choose from the available options provided.
3. Provide any feedback or comments you have regarding the appointment.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Input** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
- **Time Slot** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select a time slot from the options provided that suits your schedule and preference. This will be used to schedule your appointment.
- **Feedback** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any feedback or comments you have regarding the appointment. This will help us understand your needs and expectations.
- **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you regarding the appointment.
- **Phone Number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
- **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your appointment.
- **Selected Time Slot** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple time slots that suit your needs. Please note that this is a duplicate field, and the selected time slot should match the one selected in the Time Slot field.

Note: Please be aware that some fields are marked as optional, but providing information in these fields will help us better understand your needs and preferences.
