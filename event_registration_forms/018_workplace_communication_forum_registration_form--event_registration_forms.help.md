# Workplace Communication Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the workplace communication forum. Please fill in your details accurately to ensure you receive updates and information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
4. Select your role within the organization:
	* Employee
	* Manager
	* Guest
5. Choose your department:
	* HR
	* Marketing
	* IT
	* Sales
	* Development
6. Select the date and time you are attending the event.
7. Provide the location where the event will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **E-mail**: Enter a valid email address associated with your organizational account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number (if you have one).
* **Role**: Choose your position within the organization.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to for work-related purposes.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date of the event you are attending.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Location**: Enter a note about the location where the event takes place, if necessary.

## Tips
* Be accurate and up-to-date with your information to receive relevant updates and notifications about the event.
* Review your submissions before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or the event organizer for guidance.
