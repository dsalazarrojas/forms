# Science Fair Participation Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the science fair event. It is a one-time submission per participant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's name, participant's school, and participant's grade level.
2. Select the project category.
3. Enter a brief description of the project.
4. Enter the participant's T-Shirt size.
5. Add any additional notes about the participant.
6. Enter the participant's contact information (email, phone, etc.).
7. Enter the parent or guardian's contact information (name, email, phone, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Science Fair Project** (`science_fair_project`, `text`, required: false)
    This is a text field where you can enter a detailed description of your science fair project.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: true)
    Please enter the participant's name in this field.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, `text`, required: false)
    This is where you can enter the participant's school name.
* **Participant Grade** (`participant_grade`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Select the participant's grade level from the drop-down menu.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false)
    Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select the project category from the drop-down menu.
* **Participant T-Shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required: false)
    Select the participant's T-Shirt size.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required: false)
    Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required: false)
    Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, `email`, required: false)
    Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select the project type from the drop-down menu.
* **Project Supervising Teacher** (`project_supervising_teacher`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the project supervising teacher's name.
* **Project Supervising Teacher Phone** (`project_supervising_teacher_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the project supervising teacher's phone number.
* **Project Supervising Teacher Email** (`project_supervising_teacher_email`, `email`, required: false)
    Enter the project supervising teacher's email address.
* **Project School Name** (`project_school_name`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the project's school name.
* **Project School Email** (`project_school_email`, `email`, required: false)
    Enter the project's school email address.
* **Project School Phone** (`project_school_phone`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter the project's school phone number.

Note: This is an exhaustive help guide for the form.
