# Zoom Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees on their experience at the "Zoom Event Feedback Survey" event. It helps the event organizers understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing them to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Provide your Email Address, which will be used to send you a copy of your feedback.
3. Select the correct Event Type (Conference, Meeting, or Webinar) that you attended.
4. Choose the person you would like to assign your feedback to from the options below.
5. Optionally, provide additional feedback about the event in the Feedback field.
6. If applicable, provide your rating and comments for the event.
7. Finally, submit your completed form to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly used in informal settings.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly used in informal settings.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you attended.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you want to provide the date of the event, enter it in the correct format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you want to provide the time of the event, enter it in the correct format.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the event.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for future events.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Choose the person you would like to assign your feedback to.

Note: Fields marked as required must be filled out before submitting the form.
