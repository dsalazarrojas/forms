# Used Electronics Auction Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for bidders to register for a used electronics auction. By filling out this form, you will be able to participate in the auction and potentially purchase a used electronics item.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Used Electronics Auction Registration form:

1. Enter your Bidder Details, including any relevant information about yourself.
2. Select your interests from the available options. This helps us understand what you're looking for in the auction.
3. Accept the terms and conditions for the auction. This ensures you're aware of and agree to the rules of the auction.
4. Enter your contact information, including a phone number and email address. This is how we will contact you with any updates or further information.
5. Add any additional contact notes, if needed.
6. Finally, confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions by selecting the "Agree" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Used Electronics Auction Registration** (Text): Enter a brief description of the item you're interested in.
* **Bidder Details** (Text): Enter your details, such as your name and address.
* **Interests** (Select Multiple): Select one or more of the available options that match your interests.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Select One): Choose "Agree" to indicate you've read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Contact Email** (Email): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Contact Note** (Note): Add any additional contact information or notes, if needed.
* **Accept Terms** (Select One): Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
* **Terms Agreement** (Text): This field appears to be a duplicate of the "Accept Terms" field, and it's likely not necessary. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all fields accurately to ensure a smooth experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Make sure to carefully review the terms and conditions before accepting them.
