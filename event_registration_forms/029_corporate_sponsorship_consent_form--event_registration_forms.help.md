# corporate_sponsorship_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate sponsorship consent form is a mandatory document that requires corporate sponsors to confirm their agreement and sign a date to secure a spot at your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of your event in the `event_title` field.
2.  Select the date your event will take place in the `event_date` field.
3.  Enter the location of your event in the `event_location` field.
4.  Fill in your company's name in the `sponsor_name` field.
5.  Enter your company's email address in the `sponsor_email` field.
6.  Fill in your company's phone number in the `sponsor_phone` field.
7.  Select the type of sponsorship agreement you'd like to make from the options provided in the `agreement_type` field.
8.  (Optional) If you'd like to add any notes regarding the agreement, you can do so in the `agreement_terms` field.
9.  Sign and date your agreement in the `sponsor_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your event.
*   **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date your event will take place.
*   **event_location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your event.
*   **sponsor_name** (`sponsor_name`, text, optional): Fill in your company's name.
*   **sponsor_email** (`sponsor_email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address.
*   **sponsor_phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Fill in your company's phone number.
*   **agreement_type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of sponsorship agreement you'd like to make.
*   **agreement_terms** (`agreement_terms`, note, optional): (Optional) Enter any notes you'd like to add regarding the agreement.
*   **sponsor_signature** (`sponsor_signature`, date, optional): Sign and date your agreement. 

Note: This form is used for corporate sponsors, therefore, the fields are designed to accommodate their needs. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organiser.
