# Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants of the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp, a hands-on training program for veterinarians and animal health professionals. The form is used to collect participant details, motivations, and needs. The information collected will help us understand the participants' backgrounds, goals, and expectations, which will be used to tailor the bootcamp experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Provide a brief motivation statement explaining why you are interested in the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp.
4. Describe your needs and expectations from the bootcamp.
5. Scroll down to the submission button and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Page 1, Required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Motivations and Needs** (Page 2, Required): In 2-3 sentences, explain why you want to attend the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp and what specific skills or knowledge you hope to acquire.
* **Special Needs** (Page 3, Required): If you have any special needs or requirements, such as accessibility needs, please describe them here.
* **Participant Details 2** to **Participant Details 25** (Pages 4-25, Optional): Enter any additional details about yourself, such as your experience, skills, or goals.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Keep your answers concise and focused.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We will review your application and respond promptly to let you know if you have been accepted to the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp.
