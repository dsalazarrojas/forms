# women_s_self_defense_seminar_fournier_s_karate--event_registration_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Women's Self-Defense Seminar - Fournier's Karate event. It is used to gather information from potential attendees, including their name, contact details, and date of birth. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the fifth field using the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for event updates and information.
* **Phone** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for event updates and information.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling this form, feel free to contact us.
