# Liability Waiver For Bowling Activities - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and obtain their waiver acknowledgement before participating in bowling activities. It's essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and safe experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the Participant Details section.
2. Provide your parent or guardian's information in the Parent Details section.
3. Enter your emergency contact's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
4. List any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in the activity.
5. Select your emergency contact's relationship with you.
6. Acknowledge your understanding of the risks involved in bowling activities.
7. Confirm your emergency contact's consent to be contacted in case of an emergency.
8. Confirm your own participation consent.
9. Enter your contact email address.
10. Provide your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter your name, age, and other personal details as required.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`): If you're under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address. Please ensure they agree to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your 9-digit phone number for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter your emergency contact's 9-digit phone number.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`): Enter your age (5-18).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Friend, Other).
* **Waiver Acknowledgement** (`waiver_acknowledgement`): Select one of the three options indicating your understanding of the risks involved in bowling activities.
* **Emergency Contact Consent** (`emergency_contact_consent`): Confirm your emergency contact's consent to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`): Confirm your own participation consent.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the activity.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement 2** (`waiver_acknowledgement_2`): Select one of the three options indicating your understanding of the risks involved in bowling activities.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): Enter your address for contact purposes.
