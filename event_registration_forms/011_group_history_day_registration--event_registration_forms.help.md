# Group History Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group History Day Registration form is used to collect and manage group information for events. This form helps event organizers gather necessary details about the groups attending their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the title of your group (if applicable).
2. Enter the relevant organizer's contact information (name, email, phone number, and address).
3. Specify the sessions your group is interested in attending.
4. If necessary, add any special requirements or notes for your group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Group Information
* **Group Name (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter the name of your group if you have one. This field is not mandatory.
* **Contact Name (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Contact Email (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address for your contact person.
* **Contact Phone (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter the contact phone number of your group.
* **Contact Address (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Enter your group's contact address.
* **Organizer's Details (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: This section is intended to provide more detailed information about the organizers of your group.

### Requirements and Sessions
* **Requirements (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: Select from the available sessions your group is interested in attending.
* **Special Requirements (<name>, <type>, required: false)**: If your group has special requirements, you can specify them here.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out the contact information to ensure proper communication and follow-up.
* Make sure to specify the correct sessions for your group, as this will help with event organization.
* If you have special requirements, please be as detailed as possible to ensure the organizers can accommodate your needs.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate.
