# Corporate Inclusion Strategy Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Inclusion Strategy Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who wish to attend the Corporate Inclusion Strategy Workshop. Please complete this form to provide your details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, job title, and email address.
2. Enter your company name.
3. Choose your role within your company.
4. Select which workshop(s) you would like to attend.
5. Add any additional comments or information.
6. Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about yourself.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format of `example@domain.com`.
* **company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within your company.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to attend the workshop in the format of `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to attend the workshop in the format of `HH:MM`.
* **What is your role in the company?** (`select_one`, select one, optional): Choose your role within your company from the options: I am the CEO, I am a manager, I am an employee.
* **Which of the following workshops would you like to attend?** (`select_multiple`, select multiple, optional): Choose which workshop(s) you would like to attend from the options: Workshop 1, Workshop 2, Workshop 3.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your registration.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **email_repeat** (`email_repeat`, email, optional): Please enter your email address again for verification purposes.
