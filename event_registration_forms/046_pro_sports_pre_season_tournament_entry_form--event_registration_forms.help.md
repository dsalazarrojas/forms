# Pro Sports Pre Season Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pro Sports Pre Season Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect registration information from teams and athletes for a pre-season tournament. This form helps the tournament organizers and management team to have a smooth and organized registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next one.
3. Make sure to check and re-check all the details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Team Info
* **Team Info** (`team_info`, text, required/required): This is the title or name of your team. Enter your team's name in this field.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/true): This is the name of your team. Enter your team's name in this field.
* **Athlete Info** (`athlete_info`, text, required/required): This is the information about your team or athlete. Enter any relevant details about your team or athlete.
* **Division** (`division`, select_multiple, required/required): Select your team's division.
* **Division Info** (`division_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team's division.
* **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one, required/required): Select your team type.
* **Team Type Info** (`team_type_info`, select_one, required/required): Select details about your team type.
* **Team League** (`team_league`, text, required/required): This is your team's league. Enter your team's league name in this field.
* **Team League Info** (`team_league_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team's league.
* **Team Level** (`team_level`, select_multiple, required/required): Select your team level.
* **Team Level Info** (`team_level_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team level.
* **Team Status** (`team_status`, text, required/required): This is your team's status. Enter your team's status in this field.
* **Team Status Info** (`team_status_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team's status.
* **Team Manager** (`team_manager`, text, required/required): This is your team's manager. Enter your team's manager in this field.
* **Team Manager Info** (`team_manager_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team's manager.
* **Team Tourney** (`team_tourney`, text, required/required): This is your team's tourney. Enter your team's tourney in this field.
* **Team Tourney Info** (`team_tourney_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team's tourney.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required/required): This is your athlete's name. Enter your athlete's name in this field.
* **Athlete Info** (`athlete_info`, text, required/required): This is your athlete's information. Enter your athlete's details in this field.
* **Athlete Tourney** (`athlete_tourney`, text, required/required): This is your athlete's tourney. Enter your athlete's tourney in this field.
* **Athlete Tourney Info** (`athlete_tourney_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your athlete's tourney.
* **Athlete Level** (`athlete_level`, select_multiple, required/required): Select your athlete's level.
* **Athlete Level Info** (`athlete_level_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your athlete's level.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/required): This is your phone number. Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/required): This is your email address. Enter your email address in this field.
* **Division Details** (`division_details`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your division.
* **Team Manager Info** (`team_manager_info`, select_multiple, required/required): Select details about your team manager.

### Additional Tips

* Please note that some sections have varying numbers of options, and ensure you provide the correct selection for each field.
* If unsure about any option or field, contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
* Double-check all the fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter your team and athlete details accurately and thoroughly.

By following this guide and filling in the form carefully, you will be able to complete the Pro Sports Pre Season Tournament Entry Form efficiently and accurately.
