# Yoga Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Registration form is designed to collect participant information for a yoga event. This form will help us track and organize registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Yoga Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. Review the form to ensure all fields are accurate.
3. Submit the form to register for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it will appear on the event schedule and communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for event communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact methods.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are registering for.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event you are registering for.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the event you are registering for.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the payment method used for the event (e.g. PayPal, Stripe, Venmo).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper event organization and communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
