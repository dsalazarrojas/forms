# trade_show_vendor_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trade_show_vendor_information_collection_form is designed to collect essential information from vendors participating in a trade show or event. This form helps event organizers gather details about vendors, such as their name, contact person, and event dates, which are crucial for event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's name in the "Vendors Name" field.
2. Provide the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
4. Fill in the booth number in the "Booth Number" field.
5. Choose the product categories relevant to the vendor from the "Product Categories" dropdown list.
6. Enter a brief description of the vendor's business or products in the "Description" field.
7. Provide the vendor's contact email address in the "Contact E-mail" field.
8. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
9. If applicable, provide the vendor's website in the "Website" field.
10. Upload a logo for the vendor in the "Logo" field.
11. Enter the event date in the "Event Date" field.
12. Enter the event start and end times in the "Event Start Time" and "Event End Time" fields, respectively.
13. Select the booth size from the "Booth Size" dropdown list.
14. Choose the vendor type from the "Vendor Type" dropdown list.
15. Select the vendor status from the "Status" dropdown list.
16. Provide a brief description of the vendor's status in the "Status Description" field.
17. Choose the payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
18. Enter any additional payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
19. Enter any additional notes about the vendor in the "Additional Notes" field.
20. Enter any booth layout details in the "Booth Layout" field.
