# Screenwriting Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Screenwriting Workshop Admission Form is designed to gather essential information from aspiring screenwriters to participate in our workshop sessions. The form aims to collect data that will help the workshop organizers schedule and tailor the sessions to the needs of each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your first and last name in the "First and Last Name" field.
*   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
*   Upload your writing sample in the "Writing Sample" field.
*   Enter the title of your writing sample in the "Writing Sample Title" field.
*   Choose your preferred workshop dates in the "Workshop Date" field.
*   Write a brief bio statement in the "Bio Statement" field.
*   Enter a title for your bio statement in the "Bio Statement Title" field.
*   Select the type of workshop that interests you (Film and TV, Theatre, or Web Series) in the "Workshop Type" field.
*   Choose the location of your preferred workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
*   Specify the workshop duration in the "Workshop Duration" field.
*   Enter the desired start and end dates of the workshop in the "Workshop Start Date" and "Workshop End Date" fields, respectively.
*   Provide the start and end times of the workshop in the "Workshop Start Time" and "Workshop End Time" fields, respectively.
*   Enter the workshop venue in the "Workshop Venue" field.
*   Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First and Last Name** (Text): Enter your first and last name.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Number** (Text): Provide your contact number.
*   **Writing Sample** (Text): Upload your writing sample.
*   **Writing Sample Title** (Text): Enter the title of your writing sample.
*   **Workshop Date** (Date): Choose your preferred workshop date.
*   **Bio Statement** (Text): Write a brief statement about yourself.
*   **Bio Statement Title** (Text): Enter a title for your bio statement.
*   **Workshop Type** (Select One): Select the type of workshop that interests you (Film and TV, Theatre, or Web Series).
*   **Workshop Location** (Select Multiple): Choose the location of your preferred workshop location (New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Paris, or London).
*   **Submission Date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to submit your work.
*   **Submission Time** (Time): Enter the time you plan to submit your work.
*   **Workshop Duration** (Number): Specify the duration of the workshop.
*   **Workshop Start Date** (Date): Enter the desired start date of the workshop.
*   **Workshop Start Time** (Time): Enter the start time of the workshop.
*   **Workshop End Date** (Date): Enter the desired end date of the workshop.
*   **Workshop End Time** (Time): Enter the end time of the workshop.
*   **Workshop Venue** (Text): Enter the location of the workshop.
*   **Notes** (Text): Add any additional notes about your application.

Note: This form is not intended to collect all possible data, but rather to gather the necessary information to evaluate your application. If you have any doubts, please reach out to us for clarification.
