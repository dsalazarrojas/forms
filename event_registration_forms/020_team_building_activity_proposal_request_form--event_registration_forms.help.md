# Team Building Activity Proposal Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about team building activity proposals from team members, including the type, location, date, time, duration, budget, and leader of the activity. It aims to streamline the process of submitting and managing team building activity proposals in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct team building activity type from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the team building location in the text field.
4. Enter the team building date in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter the team building time in the time format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
6. Enter the team building duration in the text field.
7. Enter the team building budget in the number format (e.g., 100.00).
8. Enter the team building leader's name in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Building Activity Proposal** (`team_building_activity_proposal`): Enter a brief description of the team building activity proposal, including the details and objectives of the activity.
* **Team Building Activity Type** (`team_building_activity_type`): Choose the type of team building activity you are proposing (e.g., Team Outing, Team Lunch, Team Building, etc.).
* **Team Building Location** (`team_building_location`): Enter the location of the team building activity.
* **Team Building Date** (`team_building_date`): Enter the date of the team building activity in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Team Building Time** (`team_building_time`): Enter the time of the team building activity in the 10:00 AM format.
* **Team Building Duration** (`team_building_duration`): Enter the duration of the team building activity (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Team Building Budget** (`team_building_budget`): Enter the budget for the team building activity (e.g., $100.00).
* **Team Building Leader** (`team_building_leader`): Enter the name of the team building activity leader.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your team lead or manager.
