<figure># Cultural Diplomacy Event Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a cultural diplomacy event sponsorship, ensuring a clear understanding of event details and sponsorship terms. Please complete this form if you are interested in sponsoring a cultural diplomacy event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event for which you are interested in sponsoring.
2. Fill in your name and title as the event sponsor.
3. Choose the sponsorship type (option 1, option 2, option 3, or option 4) that best describes your sponsorship interest.
4. Indicate your sponsorship commitment and sponsorship level (level 1, level 2, level 3, or level 4).
5. Confirm the currency and payment terms for your sponsorship.
6. Sign and date the form, confirming your sponsorship agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer**: Please fill in the name of the event organizer.
* **Sponsor Name**: Fill in your name as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Organization**: If you represent an organization, fill in its name (optional, but recommended).
* **Event Title**: Enter a clear and concise title of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Sponsor Contact**: Enter your contact details for any follow-up communication.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Venue**: Enter the event venue address (if applicable).
* **Sponsorship Type**: Choose the type of sponsorship that best fits your interest (option 1, option 2, option 3, or option 4).
* **Sponsor Level**: Indicate the level of sponsorship (level 1, level 2, level 3, or level 4).
* **Sponsorship Commitment**: Enter your commitment to the sponsorship terms.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your sponsorship interest.
* **Sponsorship Agreement**: Select the sponsorship agreement option that applies to your sponsorship terms.
* **Additional Terms**: Select any additional terms that are specific to your sponsorship interest (if applicable).
* **Sponsor Address**: Enter your address as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Zip**: Enter your zip code (if applicable).
* **Sponsorship Currency**: Choose the currency for your sponsorship payment (currency 1, currency 2, or currency 3).
* **Payment Terms**: Confirm the payment terms for your sponsorship interest (term 1, term 2, term 3).
* **Sponsor Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes about your sponsorship interest.
* **Sponsor Signature**: Sign your name as the sponsor, confirming your sponsorship agreement.
* **Submit**: Click submit to send the form, confirming your sponsorship interest.
* **Cancel**: Click cancel to cancel your sponsorship interest without sending the form.

Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
