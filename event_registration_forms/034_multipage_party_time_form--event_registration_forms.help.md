# Multipage Party Time Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a party. It is used to gather details about the party's host, date, start and end times, type, location, guest list, and description. The form is divided into multiple pages for easier completion. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page. The form is divided into 10 sections, each with its own page.
2. Fill out each section with the required information:
	* Host Name: Enter the name of the person hosting the party.
	* Date: Enter the date of the party (MM/DD/YY).
	* Start Time: Enter the start time of the party (HH:MM AM/PM).
	* End Time: Enter the end time of the party (HH:MM AM/PM).
3. Click on "Save" to save your progress after completing each page, or "Continue" to move on to the next page.
4. On the final page, complete the remaining fields:
	* Party Type: Select the type of party (Day Party, Night Party, or Event Party).
	* Party Location: Enter the location of the party (optional).
	* Guest List: Select the guests attending the party (optional).
	* Party Description: Enter a brief description of the party (optional).
	* Party Address: Enter the address of the party (optional).
5. Click on "Submit" to finalize and submit the form.
