# welcome_message_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "welcome_message_form" is designed to collect essential information from users who are registering for an event. This form is meant to be a one-time entry point for users to provide their details for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your Name in the "Enter Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Enter Email" field.
4. Provide your Phone number in the "Enter Phone" field.
5. Once you have filled in all the required fields, click on the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Enter Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that is associated with your account.
* **Enter Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Ensure that the email address you enter is valid and associated with your account.
* Use your actual phone number where you can be reached.
* Fill the form carefully to avoid any errors.
