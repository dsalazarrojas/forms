# Trade Show Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers who want to participate in a trade show event. The form helps us understand your availability and preferences as a volunteer, so we can best fit you into the event schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your trade show name if you are representing a trade show.
2. Enter the name of the event you want to volunteer for.
3. Select your volunteer start date.
4. Choose a time for your volunteer start time.
5. Select your volunteer end date.
6. Enter the number of hours you can volunteer for.
7. Choose your volunteer role (e.g., General Volunteer, Event Organizer, Exhibition Staff).
8. Enter the location of the event.
9. Enter the email of the event organizers.
10. Enter the phone number of the event organizers.
11. Select your volunteer shift.
12. Select the duration of your volunteer shift.
13. Choose your start time for the shift.
14. Choose whether you are available or not for the shift.
15. Enter any additional information about your availability.
16. Confirm your availability for the shift.
17. Confirm your volunteer role.
18. Confirm the location of the event.
19. Confirm the email of the event organizers.
20. Confirm the phone number of the event organizers.
21. Confirm your shift details.
22. Confirm the duration of your shift.
23. Confirm your start time for the shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Show Name** (`trade_show_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trade show you are representing.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you want to volunteer for.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Start Time** (`volunteer_start_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you want to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to end volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer for.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, optional): Choose your volunteer role (e.g., General Volunteer, Event Organizer, Exhibition Staff).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Organizers Email** (`event_organizers_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the event organizers.
* **Event Organizers Phone** (`event_organizers_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the event organizers.
* **Volunteer Shift** (`volunteer_shift`, time, optional): Select your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer Shift Duration** (`volunteer_shift_duration`, number, optional): Select the duration of your volunteer shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, time, optional): Choose your start time for the shift.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you are available or not for the shift (e.g., Available, Not Available, Maybe Available).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your availability.
* **Confirm Availability** (`confirm_availability`, text, optional): Confirm your availability for the shift.
* **Confirm Volunteer Role** (`confirm_volunteer_role`, text, optional): Confirm your volunteer role.
* **Confirm Event Location** (`confirm_event_location`, text, optional): Confirm the location of the event.
* **Confirm Event Organizers Email** (`confirm_event_organizers_email`, text, optional): Confirm the email of the event organizers.
* **Confirm Event Organizers Phone** (`confirm_event_organizers_phone`, text, optional): Confirm the phone number of the event organizers.
* **Confirm Shift** (`confirm_shift`, text, optional): Confirm your shift details.
* **Confirm Shift Duration** (`confirm_shift_duration`, text, optional): Confirm the duration of your shift.
* **Confirm Shift Start Time** (`confirm_shift_start_time`, text, optional): Confirm your start time for the shift.
