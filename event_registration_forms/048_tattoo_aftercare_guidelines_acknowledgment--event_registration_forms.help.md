# Tattoo Aftercare Guidelines Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by individuals who have received a tattoo. It is a required form that acknowledges the individual's understanding and agreement to follow the provided tattoo aftercare guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided tattoo aftercare guidelines carefully.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully.
3. Fill in all fields as directed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **i_agree_with_the_above_guidelines**:
	+ A text field where you must agree with the provided guidelines by typing "Yes" or similar affirmation.
* **contact_information**:
	+ A field to enter your email address or contact phone number.
* **phone_number**:
	+ A field to enter your phone number.
* **signature**:
	+ A field for your digital signature, which should be a unique identifier for your device.
* **i_agree_to_provide_contact_info**:
	+ A select field where you must choose "Yes" to indicate that you agree to provide your contact information.
	+ Please note that this is not a required field, but it is recommended for follow-up and aftercare instructions.
* **agree_to_tattoo_artist_using**:
	+ A select field where you must choose "Yes" to indicate that you agree for the tattoo artist to use your contact information.
* **tattoo_artist**:
	+ A field to enter the name of the tattoo artist who performed your tattoo.
* **i_agree_with_the_above**:
	+ A text field for you to reiterate your agreement with the guidelines.
* **i_understand_and_agree_to_the_above**:
	+ A text field for you to express your understanding and agreement with the guidelines.
* **date_of_completion**:
	+ A field for the date you completed the form.
* **time_of_completion**:
	+ A field for the time you completed the form.
* **tattoo_completion_time**:
	+ A field for the duration in minutes that you spent completing the form.
* **i_understand_and_agree_to_the_above**:
	+ A text field for you to express your understanding and agreement with the guidelines.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and completely.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your tattoo artist for guidance.
