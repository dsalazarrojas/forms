# Community Choral Festival Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from choirs and ensembles that want to participate in the Community Choral Festival. This form is used to manage the festival entries, schedule performances, and ensure a smooth organization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information. If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choir Name** (1): Enter the official name of your choir or ensemble.
* **Choir Director Name** (2): Enter the full name of the director.
* **Director Email** (3): Enter the primary contact email of the director.
* **Director Phone** (4): Enter the primary contact phone number of the director.
* **Organization Name** (5): Enter the name of the school, church, or organization affiliation.
* **Organization Type** (6): Select the type of organization (e.g., School Choir, Church Choir, Community Choir, etc.).
* **Number of Choir Members** (7): Enter the total number of singers in your group.
* **Age Category** (8): Select the primary age group of the choir members (e.g., Youth, Young Adult, Adult, etc.).
* **Performance Category** (9): Select the type of music you'll be performing (e.g., Sacred Music, Secular Music, Folk Music, etc.).
* **Preferred Performance Date** (10): Enter your first choice for performance date.
* **Alternate Performance Date** (11): Enter your second choice for performance date (optional).
* **Repertoire List** (12): List the pieces you plan to perform.
* **Estimated Performance Duration** (13): Enter the total time in minutes for your performance.
* **Accompaniment Type** (14): Select the type of accompaniment you'll use (e.g., Piano, Organ, Full Orchestra, etc.).
* **Technical Requirements** (15): List any special technical needs for your performance.
* **Do You Need Risers** (16): Indicate if you need choir risers for your performance.
* **Dressing Rooms Needed** (17): Indicate if you need changing facilities.
* **Grant Recording Permission** (18): Allow the festival to record your performance.
* **T-Shirt Sizes for Festival Merchandise** (19): List sizes and quantities needed for festival merchandise (optional).
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (20): List any accessibility or other needs (optional).
* **Entry Fee Payment Method** (21): Select the method for paying the entry fee (e.g., Credit Card, Check, etc.).
* **Accept Terms and Conditions** (22): Confirm that you've read and agree to the festival rules.

Note: Please make sure to review the form carefully and complete it accurately to ensure a smooth festival organization process. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
