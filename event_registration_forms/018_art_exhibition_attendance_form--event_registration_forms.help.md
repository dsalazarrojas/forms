# Art Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Attendance Form is designed to collect information from attendees for the Art Exhibition. This form helps the organizers track expected visitor numbers, plan events, and cater to attendees' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the required fields for visitor information: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Exhibition Name.
2. Choose the correct time slot for your visit.
3. Specify the number of additional guests you will be bringing.
4. Indicate if you will be attending the Opening Reception.
5. If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in the "Accessibility Requests" field.
6. Choose whether you'd like to receive updates on future exhibitions and events.
7. Select how you heard about the exhibition.
8. Finally, submit the form by filling out the Registration Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, **text**, **required**): Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, **email**, **required**): Enter your official email address to stay updated on the exhibition.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, **text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Professional Affiliation** (`affiliation`, **text, **optional**): Specify your relation to the art world, e.g., Artist, Collector, Critic, Student, or General Public.
* **Name of the Exhibition** (`exhibition_name`, **select_one, **required**): Choose the correct exhibition you're attending from the provided options.
* **Planned Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, **date, **required**): Enter the date you plan to visit the exhibition.
* **Preferred Time Slot** (`time_of_visit`, **select_one, **required**): Select your preferred time slot for the visit: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Number of Additional Guests** (`number_of_guests`, **number, **required**): Indicate the number of additional guests you'll be bringing.
* **Will you be attending the Opening Reception?** (`opening_reception_rsvp`, **select_one, **required**): Choose 'True' if you'll attend, 'False' otherwise.
* **Accessibility Requests** (`accessibility_requests`, **text, **optional**): Describe any specific accessibility needs, e.g., Wheelchair access, Sign language interpretation.
* **Will you like to receive updates on future exhibitions and gallery events?** (`newsletter_opt_in`, **select_one, **required**): Choose 'Yes' to subscribe or 'No' to decline.
* **How did you hear about this exhibition?** (`discovery_source`, **select_one, **optional**): Specify how you learned about the exhibition.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, **date, **required**): Enter the date you're submitting this form.

By following these steps, you'll complete the Art Exhibition Attendance Form accurately, ensuring that your visit is planned and organized effectively.
