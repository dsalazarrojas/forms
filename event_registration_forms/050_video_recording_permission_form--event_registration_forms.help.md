# Video Recording Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from individuals involved in video recordings for events. It ensures that they grant permission for their video footage to be captured, used, and disseminated by the event organizers or sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form

*   First, make sure to review the event details and event ID to ensure you are providing accurate information for the correct event.
*   Fill out the form with the individual's information, including name, address, phone number, and email address.
*   Select the correct options for the individual's consent, event date, event time, recording duration, video type, and event signature.
*   Fill in the individual's signature and date for confirmation of consent.
*   Provide any additional notes or comments about the individual or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Id**: This is the unique identifier for the event. Please enter the correct event ID to reference the correct event.
    *   Enter the event ID as provided by the event organizers.
*   **Individual Name**: This is the name of the individual involved in the event.
    *   Enter the name of the individual, e.g., John Doe.
*   **Individual Address**: This is the address of the individual involved in the event.
    *   Enter the individual's street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Individual Phone**: This is the phone number of the individual involved in the event.
    *   Enter the individual's phone number.
*   **Individual Email**: This is the email address of the individual involved in the event.
    *   Enter the individual's email address.
*   **Individual Consent**: This is the individual's consent for their video footage to be captured, used, and disseminated.
    *   Select 'Yes' to indicate consent.
*   **Event Date**: This is the date of the event.
    *   Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Event Time**: This is the time of the event.
    *   Enter the time of the event in the format hh:mm.
*   **Recording Duration**: This is the duration of the video recording.
    *   Enter the duration of the video recording in minutes.
*   **Individual Agreement**: This is the individual's agreement to the terms and conditions of the video recording.
    *   Select 'Yes' to indicate agreement.
*   **Video Type**: This is the type of video recording.
    *   Select the type of video recording, e.g., interview or presentation.
*   **Event Signature**: This is the event signature.
    *   Select 'Yes' to indicate event signature.
*   **Individual Signature**: This is the individual's signature.
    *   Select 'Yes' to indicate signature.
*   **Individual Date**: This is the date of the individual's signature.
    *   Enter the date of the individual's signature in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Individual Time**: This is the time of the individual's signature.
    *   Enter the time of the individual's signature in the format hh:mm.
*   **Reporting Date**: This is the date for reporting.
    *   Enter the date for reporting in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Reporting Time**: This is the time for reporting.
    *   Enter the time for reporting in the format hh:mm.
*   **Reporting Note**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about the event.
    *   Enter any additional comments or notes about the event.

## Tips

*   Please review the event details and event ID before filling out this form.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Select the correct options for the individual's consent, event date, event time, recording duration, video type, and event signature.
*   Fill out this form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers for clarification.
