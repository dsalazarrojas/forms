# Sports Team Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from sports team volunteers who want to participate in events or activities. It is meant to be completed by team members, players, coaches, or referees who want to offer their support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details: This includes your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your role: Select your position within the team, which can be Team Captain, Coach, Player, Referee, or Volunteer.
3. Select your availability: Choose the days you are available to work.
4. Provide your preferred time slot: If applicable, select a specific time you are available to work.
5. Add any comments or notes (optional): This is an open-ended field for any additional information you want to share.
6. Enter your volunteer experience: If you have experience volunteering in the past, please share it with us.
7. Emergency contacts: Fill in the emergency contact's details, including their name, relationship, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it should be displayed on your volunteer ID or name tags.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname as it should be displayed on your volunteer ID or name tags.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with event updates or information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, optional): If you are part of a team, please enter your team name.
* **Position** (`position`, `select_one`, optional): Select your role within the team, which can be Team Captain, Coach, Player, Referee, or Volunteer.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple roles you can perform, which can be Team Captain, Coach, Player, Referee, or Volunteer.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): If the event has a specific date, please enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Activity** (`activity`, `time`, optional): If the event has a specific time, please enter it in the format HH:MM.
* **Volunteer Since** (`volunteer_since`, `number`, optional): If you have previous experience volunteering, please enter the number of years since you started.
* **Volunteer Until** (`volunteer_until`, `number`, optional): If you have previous experience volunteering, please enter the number of years until you plan to stop.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your volunteer frequency, e.g., regular, occasional.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred time to work, please enter it in the format HH:MM.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your availability or experience.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe your previous volunteer experience if any.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Name** (`emergency_contact_1_name`, `text`, optional): If you have a second emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Relationship** (`emergency_contact_1_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship with the second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone** (`emergency_contact_1_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email** (`emergency_contact_1_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your second emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Address** (`emergency_contact_1_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your second emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you fill in all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
* Be honest about your availability and experience to help us match you with suitable events or activities.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for volunteering and helping us make our events a success!
