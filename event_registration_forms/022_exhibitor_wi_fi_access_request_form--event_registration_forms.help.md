# exhibitor-wi-fi-access-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibitor Wi-Fi Access Request Form is used to gather information from exhibitors to provide them with secure and reliable Wi-Fi access during an event. This form ensures that all necessary details are captured to facilitate smooth communication with the event organizers and the exhibitors.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your event name in the Event Name field.
2. Enter your booth number for the event in the booth-number field.
3. Provide your name in the contact-person field, which will be your primary contact for communication.
4. Enter your contact information by filling in your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field (optional).
6. Enter your company name in the company-name field.
7. Enter your company's website in the company-url field.
8. Specify your internet requirements in the internet-requirements field.
9. Select your power requirements from the drop-down list in the power-requirement field.
10. Enter your equipment weight (if applicable) in the equipment-weight field.
11. Provide your shipping address in the shipping-address field.
12. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field (optional).
13. If you have any special requests, please provide them in the special-requests field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of the event you are participating in.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, number, required): Your booth number at the event.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): The person who will be your primary contact for communication during the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number that we will use to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address (if you would like us to contact you via email).
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): The name of your company.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, text, required): The URL of your company's website.
* **Internet Requirements** (`internet_requirements`, text, required): This is where you can specify your internet requirements.
* **Power Requirement** (`power_requirement`, select_one, required): Please select your power requirement from the list.
* **Equipment Weight** (`equipment_weight`, number, optional): If you have special equipment that requires a specific weight rating, please specify here.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): The address where we can ship any necessary equipment or materials to you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Additional notes or comments about your needs.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, please specify them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* We will use the information you provide to facilitate your Wi-Fi access during the event.
