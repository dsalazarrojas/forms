# Standby Participant Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Standby Participant Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in participating in an event. This form will help us gather necessary details from potential participants and add them to a waitlist for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name in the first field.
2. Add your email address in the second field.
3. Enter your phone number, if you have one, in the third field.
4. If you have been assigned a waitlist ID, enter it in the fourth field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Waitlist ID** (`waitlist_id`, text, required: false): If you have been assigned a waitlist ID, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number for us to contact you efficiently.
* If you are unable to access the form online, contact us for assistance.
* We will be in touch with you via the email address you provided once we review and process your registration. 

This form helps us ensure that we have a complete and accurate representation of your interest in the event. Thank you for your participation!
