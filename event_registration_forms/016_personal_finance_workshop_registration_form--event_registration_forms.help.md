# Personal Finance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Personal Finance Workshop. Please fill it out to provide your details and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out each field with your relevant information.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If a field is optional, feel free to provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Information**: This is the title of the workshop you're registering for. It's a required field but doesn't necessarily need any specific details.
- **Name**: This is the name of the person registering for the workshop. 
- **Email**: This is the email address that we'll use to communicate with you regarding the workshop. Please enter a valid email address.
- **Phone**: This is the phone number we can contact you with any workshop updates or concerns. Please enter a valid phone number.
- **Address**: This is the address where you can be reached with any workshop updates or concerns.
- **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth. 
- **Workshop Date**: This is the date of the workshop you're registering for.
- **Workshop Time**: This is the time of the workshop you're registering for.
- **Registration Type**: You can select how you'd like to register for the workshop, whether it's in-person or online.
- **Registration Date**: This is the date you're submitting your registration for the workshop.
- **Payment Method**: Please select how you'd like to pay for the workshop. 
- **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please use this field to share with us.
- **Email Opt-in**: Please select whether you'd like to be contacted via email regarding the workshop.
- **Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Please review and agree to the workshop's terms and conditions.
- **Payment Method**: Please review our payment options and terms.
 
Please note that some fields are not required and are optional, please only fill them out if you have relevant information to provide.
