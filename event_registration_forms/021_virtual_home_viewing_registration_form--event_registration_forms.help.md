To ensure accurate and relevant information is collected, please review the form fields below.

# Virtual Home Viewing Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from potential buyers for virtual home viewing sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Registration Form section (Section 1) with your details.
2. In the Buyer Details section (Section 2), select 'Yes' if you are a buyer or 'No' if you are a non-buyer.
3. Select your preferred Viewing Request in Section 3.
4. Choose a preferred date and time for the virtual home viewing session in Section 4.
5. Select your preferred Viewing Method in Section 5 (e.g. Home Tour, Virtual Tour, In-person Tour).
6. In the Agent Details section (Section 6), select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted by an agent or 'No' if you prefer not to be contacted.
7. Enter your Email and Phone number in Sections 8 and 9, respectively.
8. Add any additional Note or Message in Section 10.
9. Finally, confirm your form information in Section 12.
10. Review and confirm your Viewing, Agent and Contact information in Sections 13, 14 and 15, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`id: 1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your registration information in this field.
* **Buyer Details** (`id: 2`, `text`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a buyer or 'No' if you are a non-buyer.
* **Viewing Request** (`id: 3`, `text`, required: false): Describe your viewing request in this field.
* **Preferred Date** (`id: 4`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for the virtual home viewing session.
* **Viewing Time** (`id: 5`, `time`, required: false): Select a time for the virtual home viewing session.
* **Viewing Method** (`id: 6`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred viewing method (e.g. Home Tour, Virtual Tour, In-person Tour).
* **Agent Details** (`id: 7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be contacted by an agent or 'No' if you prefer not to be contacted.
* **Email** (`id: 8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`id: 11`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional note or message.
* **Form Confirmation** (`id: 12`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your form information.
* **Viewing Confirmation** (`id: 13`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your virtual home viewing information.
* **Contact Confirmation** (`id: 14`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your contact information.
* **Virtual Tour Confirmation** (`id: 15`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your virtual tour information.
* **Home Details** (`id: 16`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select a home for viewing.
