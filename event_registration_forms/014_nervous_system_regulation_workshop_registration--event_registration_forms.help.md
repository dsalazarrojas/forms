# Nervous System Regulation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants for the Nervous System Regulation Workshop. This information will be used to tailor the workshop experience to the attendees' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each section to fill in the relevant information.
2. Ensure that you complete all the required fields.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date of your workshop registration.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are an attendee or not.
* **Attendee Preferences** (`attendee_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any preferences you have for the workshop, such as dietary restrictions or accommodation needs.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Please indicate your consent for the workshop organizers to contact you and store your information.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, please enter it here.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any workshop preferences, such as topics or time slots.
* **Accommodation Request** (`accommodation_request`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you require any special accommodation, please select the options that apply.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please indicate any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the venue of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the location of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Workshop Organizer** (`workshop_organizer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop organizer.
* **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any workshop topics you are interested in.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you have any special requests, please select the options that apply.
