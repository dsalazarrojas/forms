# Mental Health Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a mental health workshop. The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants, including their personal details, preferences, and feedback after attending the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form.
2. Choose your preferred language from the options provided.
3. Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions.
4. Provide any additional comments or information if needed.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (*): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location where the workshop will take place.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional information or notes you would like to share with the event organizer.
* **Workshop Preferences**: Choose your preferred time of the workshop (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Participant Preferred Language**: Choose the language you would like to use during the workshop (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Participant Agreed Terms**: Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Participant Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information you would like to share with the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Workshop Capacity**: Enter the total capacity of the workshop.
* **Workshop Details**: Add any additional details about the workshop.
* **Participant Attended Workshop**: Check the box to indicate if you attended the workshop.
* **Participant Attended Workshop Date**: Select the date you attended the workshop.
* **Participant Attended Workshop Time**: Choose the time you attended the workshop.
* **Participant Attended Workshop Location**: Enter the location where you attended the workshop.
* **Participant Attended Workshop Notes**: Add any notes about your experience at the workshop.
* **Participant Survey Response**: Add your response to the survey questions.
* **Participant Survey Response Date**: Select the date you responded to the survey.
* **Participant Survey Response Time**: Choose the time you responded to the survey.
* **Participant Additional Info**: Add any additional information you would like to share with the event organizer.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the workshop.
