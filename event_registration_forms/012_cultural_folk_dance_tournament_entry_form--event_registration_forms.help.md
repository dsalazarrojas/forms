# Cultural Folk Dance Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a team for a cultural folk dance tournament. It is essential to fill out all required fields to ensure that your team's registration is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Team Name field with your team's name.
2. Select the category of your team from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the team leader's name, phone number, and email address in the respective fields.
4. Specify the number of participants in your team.
5. Choose the dance style of your team from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the date and time of your entry.
7. Provide a description of your team.
8. Confirm your registration status and the status of your team's confirmation.
9. Enter the number of entries your team will be performing.
10. Select the team dance styles that your team will be performing.
11. Upload your team's dance form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): This field is for entering your team's name.
* **Team Category** (`team_category`, select_multiple, required): This field is for selecting the category of your team (Indian, Folk, Classical, Modern, Other).
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, text, required): This field is for entering the team leader's name.
* **Team Leader's Phone** (`team_leader_phone`, text, required): This field is for entering the team leader's phone number.
* **Team Leader's Email** (`team_leader_email`, email, required): This field is for entering the team leader's email address.
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required): This field is for specifying the number of participants in your team.
* **Team Dance Style** (`team_dance_style`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the dance style of your team (Indian, Folk, Classical, Modern, Other).
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required): This field is for entering the date of your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, required): This field is for entering the time of your entry.
* **Team Description** (`team_description`, text, required): This field is for providing a description of your team.
* **Registration Status** (`is_registered`, select_one, required): This field is for confirming your registration status (True, False).
* **Confirmation Status** (`is_confirmed`, select_one, required): This field is for confirming the status of your team's confirmation (True, False).
* **Number of Entries** (`number_of_entries`, number, required): This field is for specifying the number of entries your team will be performing.
* **Team Dance Styles** (`team_dance_styles`, select_multiple, required): This field is for selecting the team dance styles (Indian, Folk, Classical, Modern, Other).
* **Team Dance Form** (`team_dance_form`, text, required): This field is for uploading your team's dance form.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out for a complete registration.
