# VIP Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and capture information about a VIP experience, such as events, appointments, or other special occasions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select whether the experience is a VIP event or not.
2. If the experience is a VIP event, provide your contact information, including any yes or no responses to any other relevant fields that apply.
3. Enter the date of the event if required.
4. Enter the time of the event if required.
5. Specify the number of VIP guests.
6. Enter any notes or additional information about the event.
7. Enter the VIP email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **VIP Experience** (`vip_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the experience is a VIP event or not.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If the experience is a VIP event, provide your contact information, including any yes or no responses to any other relevant fields that apply.
* **VIP Experience Date** (`vip_experience_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event if required.
* **VIP Experience Time** (`vip_experience_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event if required.
* **VIP Guests** (`vip_guests`, `number`, required: false): Specify the number of VIP guests.
* **VIP Experience Notes** (`vip_experience_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes or additional information about the event.
* **VIP Email** (`vip_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the VIP email.
* **VIP Phone** (`vip_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the VIP phone number.
* **VIP Experience Title** (`vip_experience_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter a title for the event or experience.
* **VIP Experience Type** (`vip_experience_type`, `text`, required: false): Enter a type for the event or experience.
