# Couples Activity Challenge Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Couples Activity Challenge Registration form is used for registering information about couples participating in an event. This form captures details such as participant names, contact information, relationship, and event specifics. The form helps to collect accurate and comprehensive data about the participants and their event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the participants and event details.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete data.
3. Select the correct options for participant relationship and event type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Relationship** (`participant_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the relationship type of the participant, such as Married, Partner, Friend, or Family.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the participant.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Describe the event details.
* **Submit Information** (`submit_info`, select_one, required): Choose whether to submit or save as draft.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`submit_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have registered for the event.
* **Registration Comments** (`submit_comments`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the registration terms.
* **Event Details Confirmation** (`confirm_event_details`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have reviewed the event details.
* **Confirmation Message** (`confirmation_message`, text, required): Enter a confirmation message for the participant.
