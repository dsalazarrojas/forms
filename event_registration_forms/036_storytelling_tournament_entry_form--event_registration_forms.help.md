# Storytelling Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather participant information and submission details for the storytelling competition.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, click on the "Submit" button once you have filled all the required fields.
- Review each field below for more information on how to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Story Form** (`story_form`, `text`, optional): This is the main form where participants can enter their story. Please provide a brief or detailed story of your choice.
- **File Uploads** (`uploads`, `text`, optional): Upload your story files in 5MB size limit per file.
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information and other details.
- **Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address as it will be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number for future reference.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it will appear in the competition.
- **Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your gender from the options provided.
- **Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age.
- **City** (`participant_city`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your city from the options provided.
- **State** (`participant_state`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your state from the options provided.
- **Country** (`participant_country`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your country from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have uploaded all required story files and completed all required fields before submitting.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, feel free to contact us.
