# Innovative Design Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovative Design Training Registration Form is used to collect information from participants for the innovative design training session. This form covers various aspects of the participant's registration, including participant information, contact details, payment information, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the participant information section. This includes providing your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the contact information section, select the preferred communication channels for the participant (phone, email, or both).
3. In the payment information section, select the payment method (online or offline) and enter the payment amount.
4. Add any additional comments or preferences in the participant comments and participant comments 2 sections.
5. Finally, confirm your payment and participant details in the participant confirmation and participant confirmation details sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This field is for providing your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Information**: Select one or more preferred communication channels (Phone, Email, or both) for the participant.
* **Preferred Date**: Enter the date you would like to attend the training session.
* **Preferred Time**: Select the preferred time slot for the training session.
* **Design Experience**: This field is for providing any relevant design experience or background information.
* **Payment Information**: Enter the payment amount for the training session.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (online or offline).
* **Payment Method Details**: Add any additional payment details.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Participant Comments**: Enter any participant comments or feedback.
* **Participant Comments 2**: Enter any further participant comments or feedback.
* **Participant Preferences**: Select one or more participant preferences (Option 1, Option 2, etc.).
* **Participant Preferences Details**: Add any additional participant preferences details.
* **Participant Info**: Enter any additional participant information.
* **Participant Info Details**: Add any additional participant info details.
* **Training Session Details**: Enter any additional training session details.
* **Training Session Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Session Time**: Select the time slot for the training session.
* **Training Session Length**: Enter the length of the training session.
* **Training Session Info**: Add any additional training session information.
* **Participant Info**: Enter any additional participant information.
* **Participant Info Details**: Add any additional participant info details.
* **Payment Confirmation**: Confirm the payment details.
* **Participant Confirmation**: Confirm the participant details.
* **Participant Confirmation Details**: Confirm the participant confirmation details.
* **Training Session Info**: Add any additional training session information.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and should be completed in the above-mentioned steps. If you have any doubts or require further clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us.
