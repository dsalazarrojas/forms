# Cultural Event Vendor Booth Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for cultural event vendors to register their participation in the event and provide necessary details for booth setup and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Provide event details in the "Event Details" section.
2. Select your booth type in the "Select Booth Type" section.
3. Indicate your booth requirements in the "Booth Requirements" section.
4. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
5. Provide your vendor details in the "Vendor Details" section.
6. Choose your booth size in the "Booth Size" section.
7. If you have any special requests, please specify them in the "Special Requests" section.
8. Confirm who will be submitting the form in the "Submitter" section.
9. Select the type of submittor in the "Submitter Type" section.
10. Enter the submitter's email address in the "Submitter Email" section.
11. Enter the submitter's phone number in the "Submitter Phone" section.
12. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Please enter the name and description of the event you are participating in.
* **Select Booth Type**: Choose the type of booth you will be setting up, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Booth Requirements**: Select any booth requirements you need, either "Yes" or "No". This may include amenities such as electricity, internet access, or special equipment.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, company name, address, and phone number.
* **Vendor Details**: Enter your company name, product/service name, and product/service description.
* **Booth Size**: Choose the size of your booth, either 3x3, 3x6, or other (please specify).
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests for your booth setup, such as power outlets or internet access, please specify them here.
* **Submitter**: Confirm who will be submitting this form, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitter Type**: Select the type of submittor, either "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the submittor.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the submittor.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any errors in the submission process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the event organizers or your event representative.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
