# easter_event_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The easter_event_vendor_registration_form is a form designed for vendors to register for the Easter event. This form allows vendors to provide their contact information and other details necessary for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a vendor.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your business name (if applicable).
5. Enter your address.
6. Enter your website (if applicable).
7. Enter a brief description of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vendor Name
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the name of your business or personal name. This will help us identify you as a vendor.
### EMail
* **EMail** (`EMail`, `email`, required: false):
Enter your email address. This will help us contact you for further information.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you for further information.
### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your business name if you have one. This will help us identify your business.
### Address
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your physical address. This will help us contact you for further information.
### Website
* **Website** (`Website`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your business website (if you have one). This will help us learn more about your business.
### Description
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter a brief description of your business. This will help us understand what you sell or offer.
