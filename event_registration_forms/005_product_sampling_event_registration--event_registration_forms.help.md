# Product Sampling Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees for a product sampling event. The collected data will be used to tailor the event experience according to the attendees' preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill in your contact details and any other relevant information.
3.  Specify your sample preferences to ensure you receive the right samples.
4.  Mention any dietary restrictions, if applicable.
5.  Select your event preferences to help us provide a tailored experience.
6.  Double-check the form for any errors before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Sampling Event Registration** (Page: 1)
	+ This is the main event title.
* **Contact Details** (Page: 2)
	+ A text field for providing your contact details, including name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Sample Preferences** (Page: 3)
	+ A text field where you can specify your preferred sample types or any special requests.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Page: 4)
	+ A text field to list any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Event Preferences** (Page: 5)
	+ A text field to specify any event preferences, such as language or other special requests.

## Tips
