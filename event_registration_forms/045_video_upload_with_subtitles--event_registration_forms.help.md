# video_upload_with_subtitles - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to upload videos with subtitles for various purposes. It helps users to provide accurate and formatted subtitles for their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Title" field with a brief description of the video.
2.  Enter a description of the video in the "Description" field.
3.  Select the subtitle language from the "Subtitle Language" dropdown menu.
4.  Choose the subtitle style from the "Subtitle Style" dropdown menu.
5.  Select the video resolution from the "Video Resolution" dropdown menu.
6.  Select the audio format for the video from the "Audio Format" dropdown menu.
7.  Select the audio language from the "Audio Language" dropdown menu.
8.  Enter the video file in the "Upload" field.
9.  Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Text)**: Enter a brief description of the video.
* **Description (Text)**: Enter a detailed description of the video.
* **Subtitle Language (Select One)**: Select the language of the subtitles from the provided options.
* **Subtitle Style (Select Multiple)**: Choose the style of the subtitles, such as Font, Color, Size, Bold, Italic, or Underline.
* **Video Resolution (Select One)**: Select the resolution of the video, such as 1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p, or 240p.
* **Audio Format (Select Multiple)**: Choose the format of the audio, such as Stereo, Mono, or No Audio.
* **Audio Language (Select One)**: Select the language of the audio.
* **Upload (Text)**: Enter the video file.
* **Submit (Text)**: Click to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the video and subtitles are correctly formatted and displayed.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our support team.
