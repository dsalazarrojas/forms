# Annual General Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual General Meeting Registration Form is designed for attendees to register for the meeting and provide their information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** (if you want to be contacted by phone).
2. Choose your **Role in Organization** (e.g., Board Member, Committee Member, etc.).
3. Select your **Attendance Status** (Yes, I will attend in-person, Yes, I will attend virtually, No, I cannot attend, or Still deciding).
4. If you are not attending in-person, select the person you will be voting by **Proxy**.
5. Choose whether you will be attending the **meal or reception**.
6. If you have any **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**, please specify them.
7. If you require any **Accessibility Accommodations**, please let us know.
8. Choose your **Contact Preference** (Email, Phone, Text, Mail, or No preference).
9. Select the topics you are interested in from the **Agenda Topics** section.
10. If you have any **Questions or Comments**, please provide them.
