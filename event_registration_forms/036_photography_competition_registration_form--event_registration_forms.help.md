# photography_competition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for a photography competition. It collects information about the photographer and their photo submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your basic information as a photographer.
2. Describe your submitted photograph.
3. Share your contact information.
4. Upload your photo submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **photographer_info** (`photographer_info`, text, **optional**): This is where you can enter your name and any other details about yourself as a photographer.
* **photo_info** (`photo_info`, text, **optional**): Describe your submitted photograph in a brief and concise manner.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, **optional**): Share your email address, phone number, or any other contact information you would like to have on record.
* **photo_submission** (`photo_submission`, text, **optional**): Upload the image of your submitted photograph. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and rights to share this content.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all provided information is accurate and up-to-date.
* You can attach supporting documents or files to your photo submission if necessary.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the competition organizers for further guidance.
