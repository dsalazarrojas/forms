# Halloween Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween Potluck Invitation Form is designed for individuals to RSVP to the Halloween potluck event, providing their name, contact email, and RSVP status. This form helps us gauge interest in the event and gather essential information for planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided. This is a text field where you can enter your name as it appears on your official records.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided. This is a text field where you can enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** in the field. This is the email address we will use to reach out to you with event updates and details.
4. Choose your **RSVP** status:
	* 'True' if you plan to attend the event
	* 'False' if you do not plan to attend
5. If you've selected 'True' for RSVP, enter the number of people attending in the **If Yes, how many are coming?** field. This helps us estimate the headcount for food and drinks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter the email address we will use to reach out to you with event updates and details.
* **RSVP** (RSVP, select_one, optional): Choose 'True' if you plan to attend the event, or 'False' if you do not plan to attend.
* **If Yes, how many are coming?** (If Yes, how many are coming?, number, optional): If you've selected 'True' for RSVP, enter the number of people attending the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure to enter accurate information to help us plan the event successfully.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact us separately.
* You will receive event updates and reminders via the email address provided.
