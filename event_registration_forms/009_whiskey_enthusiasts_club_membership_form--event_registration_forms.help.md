# Whiskey Enthusiasts Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register or update your membership with the Whiskey Enthusiasts Club. Please fill out the required fields to provide accurate information about your membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form fields as instructed**:
	* Enter your name, email address, and phone number in their respective text fields.
	* Select your preferred membership type (Monthly, Annual, or Lifetime) in the Membership Type field.
	* Choose your whiskey taste preferences (Fruity, Spicy, Floral, Vanilla, or Woody) from the dropdown menu.
	* Select your membership preferences (Regular Newsletter, Special Offers, Early Access, or All of the above) from the dropdown menu.
	* Provide any additional notes about your membership in the Notes field.
	* Leave the Membership Number and Expiration Date fields blank as they appear to be automatically populated fields.
	* You can leave the Last Updated Date and Last Updated By fields blank as they are system-generated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Form (1)**: A text field to enter your basic information about your membership.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our membership list.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address so we can stay in touch with you.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Membership Type (5)**: Choose how often you want to be charged and what benefits you want to receive from your membership.
	+ Monthly: Receive regular updates and news about our events and promotions.
	+ Annual: Receive less frequent updates and news about our events and promotions.
	+ Lifetime: Get all the benefits with no recurring charges.
* **Whiskey Taste Preferences (6)**: Choose the types of whiskey you like to taste.
	+ Fruity: Whiskey with fruity flavors.
	+ Spicy: Whiskey with spicy flavors.
	+ Floral: Whiskey with floral flavors.
	+ Vanilla: Whiskey with vanilla flavors.
	+ Woody: Whiskey with woody flavors.
* **Membership Preferences (7)**: Choose how you want to receive updates and news from us.
	+ Regular Newsletter: Receive regular updates and news about our events and promotions.
	+ Special Offers: Receive exclusive offers and discounts on our events and merchandise.
	+ Early Access: Get early access to our events and promotions.
	+ All of the above: Receive all the above preferences.
* **Notes (8)**: Enter any additional information about your membership that you'd like to share.
* **Submit (9)**: Click this button to submit your membership application.
* **Membership Number (10)**: This field will be automatically populated with your membership number.
* **Expiration Date (11)**: This field will be automatically populated with your expiration date.
* **Last Updated Date (12)**: This field is system-generated and will show the last time your information was updated.
* **Last Updated By (13)**: This field is system-generated and will show the last person to update your information.
