# Asset Risk Evaluation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Asset Risk Evaluation Workshop Registration form is designed to gather information from participants about their personal and professional background, workshop preferences, and other relevant details. This form is intended for professionals who are interested in attending the workshop to enhance their skills and knowledge in asset risk evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Company Name**: Enter your personal details, such as your first name, last name, primary email address, phone number, and the name of your company or organization.
2. **Job Title and Industry Sector**: Select your job title and the industry sector you work in to help us tailor the workshop content to your needs.
3. **Experience Level with Asset Risk**: Choose your level of experience with asset risk to help us understand your background and provide relevant content.
4. **Primary Interest Areas**: Select all the areas of interest that apply to you, such as property risk assessment, equipment valuation, risk mitigation strategies, and more.
5. **Preferred Workshop Session and Date**: Choose your preferred workshop session and date to ensure we can accommodate your schedule.
6. **Dietary Restrictions and Accessibility Needs**: If you have any dietary restrictions or special accessibility needs, please let us know so we can provide suitable arrangements.
7. **Number of Team Members Attending**: If you're attending with a team, please indicate the number of members.
8. **Prior Workshop Attendance**: If you've attended one of our workshops before, please let us know.
9. **Referring Organization**: Please specify how you heard about the workshop.
10. **Registration Type**: Choose your registration type, whether individual, group rate, corporate, or non-profit.
11. **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method, such as credit card, bank transfer, or check.
12. **Additional Comments or Questions**: If you have any extra information or questions, please share them with us.
13. **Terms Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
14. **Registration Date**: Finally, please confirm the date of your registration.
