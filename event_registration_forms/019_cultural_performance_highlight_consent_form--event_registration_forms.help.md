# Cultural Performance Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants in cultural performance events to provide their demographic and consent information, as well as permission for the event organizers to release certain elements of their performance, such as images, videos, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant information:
	* **Participant name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Participant email**: Enter your official email address.
	* **Contact name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted for additional information.
	* **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted.
	* **Contact email**: Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted.
2. Provide consent for release of materials:
	* **Permission**: Grant permission for us to release your name and image.
	* **Image release**: Choose whether you would like to allow us to release your images.
	* **Performance release**: Choose whether you would like to allow us to release your performance.
	* **Video release**: Choose whether you would like to allow us to release your video.
3. Provide additional information:
	* **Image description**: Describe any specific images you would like us to release.
	* **Performance description**: Describe any specific performances you would like us to release.
	* **Video description**: Describe any specific videos you would like us to release.
	* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.
