# restaurant_management_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The restaurant management workshop registration form is designed to collect participant data, workshop preferences, and other relevant information for the workshop organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant data in the first page.
2. Select the workshop(s) you would like to attend on the second page.
3. Enter the date and time of the workshop you selected on the third page.
4. Choose your preferred registration type (in-person, online, or other) on the fourth page.
5. Provide any additional information about your registration on the fifth page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Data** (`participant_data`, text, required): This is the section where you can enter your basic details, such as name and email address.
* **Select a Workshop** (`workshop_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose which workshop(s) you would like to attend from the options provided.
* **Date of Workshop** (`date_workshop`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you selected.
* **Time of Workshop** (`time_workshop`, time, required): Enter the time of the workshop you selected.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to register (in-person, online, or other).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any extra information about your registration, such as special requests or accommodations.
