# innovation_in_preservation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering an event at the Innovation in Preservation Forum. The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to facilitate the registration process. Please ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Submit the form once you have entered all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Page 1** (`page_1`, `note`, not required)
Page 1 is a general information page. Please note that this field is not required and is for general purposes only.

### 2. **Email** (`page_2`, `email`, not required)
Enter your email address. This will be used for contact purposes.

### 3. **Name** (`page_3`, `text`, not required)
Enter your full name. This will be used for identification purposes.

### 4. **Additional Comments** (`page_4`, `text`, not required)
Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.

### 5. **Interested in Participating** (`page_5`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select all that apply:

* Yes
* No

### 6. **Additional Information** (`page_6`, `text`, not required)
Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
