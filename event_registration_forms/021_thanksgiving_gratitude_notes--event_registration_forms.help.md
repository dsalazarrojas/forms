# thanksgiving_gratitude_notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving Gratitude Notes" form is designed to allow users to express their gratitude and share their thoughts on thanksgiving. This form is meant to be a simple and easy-to-use platform for users to share their appreciation and thanks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your **First Name**, which should be a brief introduction of yourself.
3. Type your **Message** in the text field provided, describing your thoughts and feelings on thanksgiving.
4. If you have any additional information to share, you can add it in the **Note** field.
5. If you'd like, you can also provide your **Email** address, which will allow us to contact you if needed.
6. Once you've filled out all the fields, click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A brief introduction of yourself.
* **Your Message**: A detailed message expressing your thoughts and feelings on thanksgiving.
* **Email**: An optional contact information for you to be reached if needed.
* **Note**: An optional additional note or comment to share your thoughts.

## Tips
- Please be concise and clear in your message.
- Make sure to provide a valid email address if you choose to include it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
