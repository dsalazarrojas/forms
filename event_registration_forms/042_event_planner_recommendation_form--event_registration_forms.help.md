# Event Planner Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to suggest a candidate for an event or opportunity, providing an in-depth profile of the recommended individual.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic contact information (First Name, Last Name, and Email).
2. Provide your phone number, job title, department, job function, and years of service for context.
3. Choose your gender.
4. Add any additional details about the candidate's job title, department, and job function.
5. Enter any other company details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the recommended candidate's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the recommended candidate's last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's email address, if available.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's phone number.
* **Company** (text, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's company name.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's job title.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's department.
* **Job Function** (text, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's job function.
* **Years of Service** (number, optional): Enter the number of years the recommended candidate has been working.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the recommended candidate's date of birth.
* **Gender** (select_one, required): Choose the recommended candidate's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Job Title 2** (text, optional): Enter additional job title information, if needed.
* **Department 2** (text, optional): Enter additional department information, if needed.
* **Company 2** (text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate's current company.
* **Job Function 2** (text, optional): Enter additional job function information, if needed.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing information to create a comprehensive profile.
* Ensure accuracy and consistency across all fields.
* If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
* Review and submit the form to recommend the candidate.
