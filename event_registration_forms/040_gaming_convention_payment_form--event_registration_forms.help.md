# gaming_convention_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registrants to provide payment details and additional information for attending the gaming convention. It includes fields for payment, attendee information, and preferences for t-shirts and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the "Amount Paid" field with the amount you paid for the convention.
2.  Enter your "Attendee Name" in the provided field.
3.  Enter your "Email" address to ensure we can contact you.
4.  Choose your "T-shirt Size" preference from the dropdown menu.
5.  Select your preferred "Payment Method" for making the payment.
6.  If you want a t-shirt, choose your preferred "T-shirt Color".
7.  If you have any special dietary requests, provide them in the "Special Dietary Requests" field.
8.  If you want a custom t-shirt design, select "Yes" for "T-shirt Design" and choose your preferred "T-shirt Design Size" and "T-shirt Design Type".
9.  If you have any preferences for the t-shirt design color, select "Yes" for "T-shirt Design Color" and choose your preferred color.
10. Finally, review your selections and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Amount Paid** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you paid for the convention.
*   **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the convention materials and badges.
*   **Email** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with important convention information.
*   **T-shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred t-shirt size from the dropdown menu.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
*   **T-shirt Color** (`tshirt_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferred t-shirt color from the available options.
*   **Special Dietary Requests** (`diet_request`, note, required/optional): If you have any special dietary requests, provide them in this field.
*   **T-shirt Design** (`tshirt_design`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you want a custom t-shirt design, select "Yes" and choose your preferred design size and type.
*   **T-shirt Design Color** (`tshirt_design_color`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have a color preference for the t-shirt design, select "Yes" and choose your preferred color.
*   **T-shirt Design Size** (`tshirt_design_size`, select_one, required/optional): If you want a custom t-shirt design, choose your preferred size.
*   **T-shirt Design Type** (`tshirt_design_type`, select_one, required/optional): If you want a custom t-shirt design, choose your preferred type.
*   **T-shirt Design Color Preference** (`tshirt_design_color_preference`, select_one, required/optional): If you have a color preference for the t-shirt design, choose your preferred color.

## Tips
Remember to review your selections carefully before submitting the form, and don't hesitate to contact us if you need any assistance.
