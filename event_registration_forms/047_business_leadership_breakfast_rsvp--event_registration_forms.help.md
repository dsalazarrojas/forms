# Business Leadership Breakfast RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Business Leadership Breakfast RSVP" form is designed to collect essential information from attendees of a specific event. Please ensure you are registered to attend the event before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Attendee Name** (field 1) in the required "Attendee Name" field, which is the name of the person registering for the event.
2. Provide your **Job Title** (field 2) for the same attendee.
3. Select the name of your **Company/Organization** (field 3) where you work or represent.
4. Enter your **Email Address** (field 4), which will be used for correspondence.
5. Optionally, provide your **Phone Number** (field 6) if you'd like to be contacted directly.
6. Select your **RSVP Status** (field 8) from the options Attending, Not Attending, or Tentative/Maybe.
7. Enter the **Number of Guests** (field 9) you will be bringing, including yourself.
8. If applicable, enter **Guest Names and Titles** (field 10) for any companions.
9. In the **Dietary Requirements** (field 12) section, select any dietary needs you or your guests may have. You can choose from the options None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Nut-free, Halal/Kosher.
10. Provide any **Special Access or Mobility Needs** (field 13) you may have if necessary.
11. Opt-in or out of the **Leadership Circle** (field 14) mailing list for monthly event updates.
12. Lastly, select the **Date of Submission** (field 15), which is the date you are completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (field 1, required): Enter the name of the person registering for the event.
* **Job Title** (field 2, required): Provide the job title of the person registering for the event.
* **Company/Organization** (field 3, required): Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
* **Email Address** (field 4, required): Enter a valid email address for the attendee.
* **Phone Number** (field 6, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **RSVP Status** (field 8, required): Select one of the options Attending, Not Attending, or Tentative/Maybe.
* **Number of Guests** (field 9, required): Enter the number of attendees, including yourself.
* **Guest Names and Titles** (field 10, optional): Enter any additional guests' names and titles for name tags.
* **Dietary Requirements** (field 12, optional): Select any dietary needs for the event attendees.
* **Special Access or Mobility Needs** (field 13, optional): Describe any special needs you may have.
* **Leadership Circle** (field 14, required): Opt-in or out of the mailing list for event updates.
* **Date of Submission** (field 15, required): Select the date you are completing this form. 

Please fill out this form carefully, ensuring all fields are completed as required. If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to reach out to the event organizer for guidance.
