# Tennis Tournament Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Tennis Tournament Release Form is a document that participants and their parents/guardians must complete to participate in the tournament. This form ensures that participants are aware of the potential risks and consequences of participating in the tournament and provides a clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Tournament Name**: This field is used to identify the name of the tournament.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
3. **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
4. **Date Of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
5. **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for the participant.
6. **Medical History**: This field is used to record any medical history or conditions that may be relevant to the participant's participation in the tournament.
7. **Medical Condition**: Enter any known medical conditions that the participant has.
8. **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
9. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact.
10. **Relationship To Participant**: Describe the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
11. **Medical Authorization**: Select the level of medical authorization required.
12. **Emergency Medical Services**: Select the level of emergency medical services required.
13. **Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether the participant has medical insurance.
14. **Emergency Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether the participant has emergency medical insurance.
15. **Participant Acknowledgement**: Select whether the participant acknowledges the risks and consequences of participating in the tournament.
16. **Participant Agreement**: Select whether the participant agrees to participate in the tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament as it appears on the tournament sign.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification card or government-issued ID.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached during the tournament.
* **Medical History**: This field is used to record any medical history or conditions that may be relevant to your participation in the tournament.
* **Medical Condition**: Enter any known medical conditions that you have.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship To Participant**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).
* **Medical Authorization**: Select the level of medical authorization required (e.g., emergency medical services, non-emergency medical services).
* **Emergency Medical Services**: Select the level of emergency medical services required (e.g., first aid, medical doctor, ambulance).
* **Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether you have medical insurance.
* **Emergency Medical Insurance**: Indicate whether you have emergency medical insurance.
* **Participant Acknowledgement**: Select whether you acknowledge the risks and consequences of participating in the tournament.
* **Participant Agreement**: Select whether you agree to participate in the tournament.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have completed all fields accurately and comprehensively.
* If you are unsure about any information, ask for clarification or assistance from the tournament organizers.
* Review the form carefully before signing it to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
