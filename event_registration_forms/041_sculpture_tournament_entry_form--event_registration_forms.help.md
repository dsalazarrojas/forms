# sculpture_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register yourself or your work in the sculpture tournament. This form helps the tournament organizers collect necessary information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields with your details.
2. Make sure to input your contact number and email for easy contact.
3. Provide an image of your artwork, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist submitting the work.
* **Participant** (`participant`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the artist submitting the work.
* **Artist Address** (`artist_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the artist submitting the work.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your or the artist's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your or the artist's contact number.
* **Artwork Image** (`artwork_image`, text, optional): Provide a URL or a description of the artwork image.

## Tips
* Make sure to input accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're providing a URL for the artwork image, ensure it is accessible and publicly available.
* If you're describing the artwork image, provide sufficient details for it to be identified correctly.
