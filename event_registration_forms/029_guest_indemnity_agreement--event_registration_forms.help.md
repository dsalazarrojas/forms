# Guest Indemnity Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record an event registration information and obtain a guest's acknowledgment and signature, acknowledging the risks associated with attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with the following information:
  * Event name
  * Event date
  * Event time
  * Event location
  * Acknowledge the risks (select one of the provided options)
  * Provide any additional risk details (if applicable)
  * Guest name
  * Guest email
  * Guest phone number

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **event name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event.
- **event date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event.
- **event time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event.
- **event location** (text, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **acknowledge risks** (select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to acknowledge the risks associated with attending the event.
  * Option 1: I acknowledge and understand the risks associated with the event.
  * Option 2: I do not acknowledge the risks associated with the event.
  * Option 3: I am unsure about the risks associated with the event.
- **risk details** (text, optional): If you selected Option 2 or 3, provide a brief explanation of your concerns about the risks associated with the event.
- **guest name** (text, required): Enter the name of the guest.
- **guest email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the guest.
- **guest phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the guest.
- **signature** (note, required): Sign this document to acknowledge that you have read and understand the risks associated with the event and to confirm your attendance.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the event details carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the risk options, please select Option 3 and provide a brief explanation in the risk details field.
