## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information (name, address, email, phone number).
2. Enter your dish name and cooking method.
3. List the ingredients used in your dish.
4. Specify the cooking time and unit.
5. Pay the entry fee (the exact amount and currency will be specified below).
6. Enter the name of the event you are submitting to.
7. Specify the date and location of the event.
8. Choose the tool you were assigned (if applicable).
9. Indicate who submitted the entry (chef or home cook).
10. Review and submit your entry.
