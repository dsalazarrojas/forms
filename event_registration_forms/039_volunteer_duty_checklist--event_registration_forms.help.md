# Volunteer Duty Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Duty Checklist" form is used to register volunteers for events. Fill out the form to indicate your interest in volunteering, provide contact information, and specify your availability and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the event type** that you are interested in volunteering for.
2. **Indicate your availability** by selecting the time you usually start and finish your day.
3. **Provide your contact information** by entering your email address and selecting if you have a landline phone number.
4. **Answer the question about your availability** to volunteer for this event.
5. **Choose how you would like to be contacted** through email, SMS, or phone.
6. **Select the event date** you would like to volunteer for.
7. **Add any additional event details** that you would like to include.
8. **Specify the type of volunteer role** you are interested in (e.g., Role 1, Role 2, Role 3).
9. **Let us know if you want to receive email updates** about the event.
10. **Choose if you want to receive SMS updates** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What time do you usually start and finish your day?** (`volunteer_details`, time, required): This field is where you can select the time you usually start and finish your day to help us understand your availability.
* **Are you available to volunteer for this event?** (`event_status`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you are available to volunteer, or "No" if you are not interested in volunteering for this event.
* **Please enter your email address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about event updates and details.
* **Do you have a landline phone number?** (`contact_phone`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you have a landline phone number, or "No" if you do not.
* **What type of event is this?** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event you are interested in volunteering for from the available options.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to be contacted for event updates (Email, SMS, or Phone).
* **Event start date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to volunteer for the event.
* **What details do you want to include about the event?** (`event_details`, text, required): Add any additional details about the event you would like to include.
* **What type of volunteer role are you interested in?** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of volunteer role you are interested in (e.g., Role 1, Role 2, Role 3).
* **Do you want to receive email updates?** (`communication_updates`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you want to receive email updates about the event, or "False" if you do not.
* **Do you want to receive SMS updates?** (`communication_sms`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you want to receive SMS updates about the event, or "False" if you do not.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be specific about your availability and volunteer preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your preferred contact method.
