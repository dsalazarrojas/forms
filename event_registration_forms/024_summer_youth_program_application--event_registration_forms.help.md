# Summer Youth Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Youth Program Application is a form designed to collect personal details, contact information, and written responses from applicants interested in participating in the Summer Youth Program. The program is a chance for young people to develop their skills, engage in community activities, and grow as individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Details** page with your name, age, and any other relevant personal information.
2. Provide your **Contact Details**, including your phone number and email address.
3. Answer the two essay questions on the **Essay Question** page, explaining your experience with leadership and how you plan to apply your leadership skills in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details (Personal Details, Text, Optional)**: This section is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think would be relevant to the application.
- **Contact Details (Contact Details, Text, Optional)**: Please enter your phone number and email address so that we can contact you regarding your application.
- **What is your experience with leadership? (Essay Question, Text, Optional)**: Describe your experience with leadership, including any relevant stories or examples.
- **How do you think you will be able to apply your leadership skills in this program? (Essay Question, Text, Optional)**: Explain how you plan to use your leadership skills in the program, and how you will contribute to the activities and events.
