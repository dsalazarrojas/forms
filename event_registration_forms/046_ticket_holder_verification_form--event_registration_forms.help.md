# Ticket Holder Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ticket Holder Verification Form is used to gather information about the ticket holder, including their name, contact details, and document verification details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for "Ticket Holder Information" to indicate whether the ticket holder is the person being verified or someone else.
2. Enter the ticket holder's first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter the ticket holder's ticket number and ticket code.
4. Check the "Ticket Holder Verification" option to confirm whether the ticket holder is verified or not.
5. Enter the ticket holder's date of birth, place of birth, country of origin, document type, document number, expiration date, and place of issue.
6. Enter the issue dates for any additional documents.
7. Add any additional notes for the ticket holder verification.
