# Faith And Wonders Tour Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faith And Wonders Tour Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable tour experience. Please read through the form carefully to understand what each field is asking for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" field.
2. Choose your preferred tour date in the "Preferred Tour Date" field.
3. Select your preferred tour time in the "Preferred Tour Time" field.
4. Provide emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Consent" field.
6. Indicate your relationship with the tour guide in the "Relationship" field.
7. Select your preferred communication method in the "Communication" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
9. Review and agree to the tour agreement in the "Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Required, text): Please enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (Optional, date): Choose a date for the tour that suits you best. If you have no preference, select "No Preference".
* **Preferred Tour Time** (Optional, time): Select a time for the tour that suits you best. If you have no preference, select "No Preference".
* **Emergency Contacts** (Optional, text): Please enter emergency contact names, phone numbers, and relationship with them.
* **Consent** (Required, select_one): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Relationship** (Optional, select_multiple): Please indicate your relationship with the tour guide (e.g. Spouse, Parent, Family member, Friend, Other).
* **Communication** (Optional, select_multiple): Please select how you would like to be contacted for tour updates (e.g. Email, Phone, Text Message, Other).
* **Comments** (Optional, note): Please add any additional comments or notes about your tour experience.
* **Agreement** (Optional, text): Please review and agree to the tour terms and conditions.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
