# Crowd Control Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Crowd Control Incident Form" is a tool to report incidents related to crowd control, allowing for a thorough understanding of what happened and its consequences. Please use this form to provide detailed and accurate information about the incident, including its circumstances, outcomes, and any relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page to navigate through the form.
2. Review the fields on each page carefully and provide accurate information in each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_details (page 1)**: Describe the event or situation that led to the incident.
* **crowd_control_info (page 2)**: Provide information about the crowd control measures that were in place at the time of the incident.
* **incident_report (page 3)**: Give a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
* **safety_measures (page 4)**: Describe the safety measures that were or should have been taken before, during, or after the incident.
* **witness_info (page 5)**: Provide information about any witnesses, including their names, contact details, and statements (if possible).
* **incident_details (page 6)**: Offer any additional details about the incident, including dates, times, locations, and any other relevant information.
* **incident_outcome (page 7)**: Describe the outcome of the incident, including any injuries, property damage, or other consequences.
* **incident_reporter_info (page 8)**: Provide your contact information, so we can follow up with you about the incident.
* **incident_review (page 9)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the incident.
* **incident_reporter (page 10)**: Enter your name and title/position (if applicable), so we can identify you as the person submitting the report.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, contact us for clarification.
* Do not submit incomplete or inaccurate information, as this can hinder incident management and analysis.
