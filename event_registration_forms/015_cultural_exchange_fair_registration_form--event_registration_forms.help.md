# Cultural Exchange Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals and organizations attending the Cultural Exchange Fair. It includes personal details, participant type, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
    a. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
    b. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
    c. Enter your Email Address in the "Email" field (format: username@domain.com).
    d. Provide your Phone Number in the "Phone" field (no special formatting required).
    e. Select your participant type from the "Participant Type" field (choose one: Cultural Exchange Participant, Guest, Student, or Staff).
    f. If you're representing an organization, enter the name in the "Organization" field.
    g. Provide any cultural details you'd like to share in the "Cultural Details" field.
    h. Choose your Registration Date (format: YYYY-MM-DD) for the "Registration Date" field.
    i. Enter the Time you'd like to arrive or depart from the "Time" field (format: HH:MM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter your first name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter your last name, e.g., Smith.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your contact email address in the format username@domain.com (e.g., john.smith@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number without special formatting (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose one: Cultural Exchange Participant, Guest, Student, or Staff.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Cultural Details** (`cultural_details`, Text, Optional): Share any cultural details you'd like to share with the community.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, Date, Optional): Choose your registration date in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you'd like to arrive or depart in the format HH:MM (e.g., 09:00).
