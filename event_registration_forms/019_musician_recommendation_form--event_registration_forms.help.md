# Musician Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Musician Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about musicians and their music-related activities. Event organizers and administrators will use this form to gather data on musicians participating in or recommended for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your musician details, including your name and other identifying information. 
2.  Next, add event details, such as the event name, location, and date. 
3.  Then, recommend a musician, including their name and any relevant music-related activities. 
4.  Provide submission details, such as how you know the musician and why you're recommending them. 
5.  Finally, review the musician agreement terms and submit your response. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Musician Details (musician_details)**: Enter your name and any other identifying information as required. This field is not required.
*  **Event Details (event_details)**: Add event details such as event name, location, and date. This field is not required.
*  **Musician Recommendation (musician_recommendation)**: Recommend a musician, including their name and any relevant music-related activities. This field is not required.
*  **Musician Submission Details (musician_submission_details)**: Provide submission details, such as how you know the musician and why you're recommending them. This field is not required.
*  **Musician Agreement Terms (musician_agreement_terms)**: Review and agree to the terms of the form before submitting. This field is not required.
*  **Submitter Details (submitter_details)**: Add your name and any other identifying information as required. This field is not required.

## Tips
*  Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form to ensure valid data is collected. 
*  Review each field carefully to ensure you complete it correctly. 
*  If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
