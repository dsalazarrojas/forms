# performance_improvement_initiative_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register a performance improvement initiative. Please fill out this form to provide information about a performance improvement initiative, including first name, last name, email, phone number, performance improvement initiative, date of last attendance, time of last attendance, assigned tool, and output file.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Make sure to select the correct performance improvement initiative from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date and time of your last attendance in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM:SS respectively).
4. If you have an assigned tool, enter its name.
5. Upload your output file if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your official phone number.
* **Performance Improvement Initiative** (`performance_improvement_initiative`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one of the available options: Improved Processes, Improved Products, Improved Systems, Improved Skills.
* **Date of Last Attendance** (`date_of_last_attendance`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last attendance in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-02-15).
* **Time of Last Attendance** (`time_of_last_attendance`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your last attendance in the format HH:MM:SS (e.g., 14:30:00).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): Upload your output file if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your administrator for clarification.
* Double-check your input for formatting issues before submitting the form.
