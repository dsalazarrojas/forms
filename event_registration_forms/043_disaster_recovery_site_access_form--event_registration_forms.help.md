# Disaster Recovery Site Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Site Access Form is used to register users for access to a disaster recovery site during a time of crisis. This form is used to collect information from users who need to access the site in order to perform critical operations or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your User Name, which is your identifier for the site.
2. Enter your User Email, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Specify the Site Name, which is the location of the site you are requesting access to.
4. Choose the Access Date and Access Time for which you need to access the site.
5. Describe the purpose of your visit, which will help us understand the reason for your access request.
6. Enter your Contact Number and Emergency Contact Number, which will be used to contact you in case of an emergency.
7. Select the Authorized Personnel who will be accompanying you, from the list of available options.
8. Add any additional Comments about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (Text, Optional)**: This is your identifier for the site. Please enter your first and last name.
* **User Email (Email, Optional)**: This is the email address that we will use to contact you.
* **Site Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the site you are requesting access to.
* **Access Date (Date, Optional)**: Choose the date you need to access the site.
* **Access Time (Time, Optional)**: Choose the time you need to access the site.
* **Purpose (Text, Optional)**: Describe the purpose of your visit, which will help us understand your request.
* **Contact Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Number (Text, Optional)**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Authorized Personnel (Select One, Required)**: Select the authorized personnel who will be accompanying you.
* **Comments (Text, Optional)**: Add any additional comments about your visit.
