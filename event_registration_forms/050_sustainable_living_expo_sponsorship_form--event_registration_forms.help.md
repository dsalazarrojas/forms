# Sustainable Living Expo Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors for the Sustainable Living Expo.

## How To Complete This Form

* Read through the form carefully before starting to complete it.
* Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by asterisk *).
* Use clear and concise language to fill out the form.
* Double-check your submissions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide a brief description of the event you are interested in sponsoring. Please keep it concise and within the limit of 255 characters.
* **Sponsor Info** (`sponsor_info`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide information about your organization or individual. Please include your name, address, and any other relevant details about your sponsorship.
* **Package Info** (`package_info`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide information about the products or services you are offering as a sponsor. Please be as detailed as possible and include any relevant specifications or features.
* **Branding Info** (`branding_info`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide information about your organization's branding and logo. Please include any relevant details such as your company name, logo, and any other visual identity elements.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide contact information for further communication. Please include your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, optional): 
  This field is used to provide any additional details or comments you would like to share with the expo organizers. Please keep it brief and within the limit of 255 characters.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy and clarity.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your interest in sponsoring the Sustainable Living Expo and look forward to reviewing your submission.
