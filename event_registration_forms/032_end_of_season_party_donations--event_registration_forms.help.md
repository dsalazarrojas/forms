# end_of_season_party_donations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect end-of-season party donations from individuals or organizations. Fill out this form to provide financial support for a specific cause or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the amount you wish to donate in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Select the currency you would like to use for the donation from the "Donation Currency" options.
3. Choose the payment method you would like to use from the "Payment Method" options.
4. If you are using a credit card, enter your name as it appears on the card in the "Name on Card" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you would like to receive a confirmation email.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you would like to receive a follow-up call or text.
7. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code in the "Address", "City", "State", and "Zip Code" fields, if you would like to receive a physical donation receipt or have your donation tax-deductible.
8. Select your country in the "Country" field.
9. Enter any additional comments or information about your donation in the "Comment" field.
10. Enter any relevant information about the donor in the "Donor Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`title`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the charity.
* **Donation Currency** (`donation_currency`, `select_one`, required): Select the currency you would like to use for the donation from the available options.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the payment method you would like to use from the available options.
* **Name on Card** (`name_on_card`, `text`, optional): If you are using a credit card, enter your name as it appears on the card.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive a confirmation email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive a follow-up call or text.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address, if you would like to receive a physical donation receipt or have your donation tax-deductible.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city, if you would like to receive a physical donation receipt or have your donation tax-deductible.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state, if you would like to receive a physical donation receipt or have your donation tax-deductible.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code, if you would like to receive a physical donation receipt or have your donation tax-deductible.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Select your country of origin.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your donation.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the donor.
