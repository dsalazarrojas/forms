# Global Education Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants, teachers, and schools for various events. It collects participant information, contact details, event specifics, and agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section with your name, contact email, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact phone number.
3. Enter the participant school's name and address.
4. Select your grade level and that of your school.
5. Enter any other relevant details about the participant and the school.
6. If you are a teacher, provide your name and contact email.
7. Enter any other relevant details about the teacher.
8. Select your participant type (yes/no) and enter any other details if necessary.
9. Enter the event name and date.
10. Provide the event start time.
11. Select your participant type (yes/no) and enter any other details if necessary.
12. Sign and agree to the participant agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Exchange Details** (`exchange_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any other relevant details about the participant.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your school's name.
* **Participant Grade Level** (`participant_grade_level`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select your grade level.
* **Teacher Details** (`teacher_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any other relevant details about the teacher.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Contact Email** (`teacher_contact_email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter the teacher's contact email.
* **Teacher Contact Phone** (`teacher_contact_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the teacher's contact phone number.
* **School Details** (`school_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any other relevant details about the school.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the school's address.
* **School Grade Level** (`school_grade_level`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the school's grade level.
