# marketing_meeting_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event registration for marketing meetings. It helps event organizers to track attendees' information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you with meeting updates.
2. Choose your role in the meeting (yes or no).
3. If you have a company, please provide it in the text field.
4. If you have a department, please provide it in the text field.
5. Select the date and time for the meeting.
6. Provide the location for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill in your name to help us know who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Fill in your email address so we can contact you.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your role in the meeting (yes or no).
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a company, please provide it here.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a department, please provide it here.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date for the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the location for the meeting.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate event planning.
* If you have any special requests or preferences, feel free to include them in the "Company" and "Department" fields.
* If you are unable to select the meeting date or time, please contact us separately to discuss alternatives.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
