# yay_it_s_turkey_day - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a guest for the "yay it's turkey day" event. It is a private form for internal use and will not be publicly accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your guest name in the "guest_name" field.
2. Answer the question "what_will_guest_bring" with a "Yes" or "No" to indicate what you will be bringing.
3. If you are bringing others, select all the people you will be bringing in the "coming_with_guest" field.
4. Choose the date of your visit from the calendar in the "when_is_your_visit" field.
5. Enter the time of your visit in the "what_time" field.
6. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the "note" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **guest_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as a guest.
* **what_will_guest_bring** (select_one, required/optional): Answer "Yes" if you will be bringing something, "No" otherwise.
* **coming_with_guest** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the guests coming with you, if any.
* **when_is_your_visit** (date, required/optional): Choose the date of your visit from the calendar.
* **what_time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your visit.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that your registration is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer for assistance.
* This form will only be used for internal purposes and will not be publicly accessible.
