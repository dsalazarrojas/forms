# Restaurant Host/Hostess Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect application information from individuals interested in becoming a host or hostess at a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
2. Select your availability in the "Availability" section.
3. Indicate your experience level in the "Experience Level" section.
4. Enter your availability date and time in the "Availability Date" and "Availability Time" sections.
5. Choose your preferred shift in the "Preferred Shift" section.
6. Select the languages you speak in the "Languages Spoken" section.
7. Provide a brief description of your work experience in the "Work Experience" section.
8. List your available frequency in the "Available Frequency" section.
9. Enter your availability ending date in the "Availability Ending" section.
10. Attach a copy of your resume in the "Resume" section.
11. (Optional) Provide emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): This section allows you to provide your contact information.
	+ Please enter your name and email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): This section is for your contact person.
	+ Please enter your contact person's name and email address.
* **Availability** (`availability`): This section determines your availability to work.
	+ Select the days you are available to work.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`): This section indicates your level of experience.
	+ Choose your experience level as Host/Hostess, Server, Chef, Manager, or Owner.
* **Availability Date** (`availability_date`): This section is for your availability date.
	+ Enter the date you are available to work.
* **Availability Time** (`availability_time`): This section is for your availability time.
	+ Enter the time you are available to work.
* **Preferred Shift** (`preferred_shift`): This section determines your preferred shift.
	+ Choose your preferred shift as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages`): This section is for languages you can speak.
	+ Select the languages you are fluent in.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): This section provides a brief description of your work experience.
	+ Enter a brief description of your work experience.
* **Available For** (`available_for`): This section determines how often you are available to work.
	+ Choose how often you are available to work as Full-time, Part-time, Seasonal, or Casual.
* **Available Frequency** (`available_frequency`): This section determines how often you are available to work.
	+ Choose how often you are available to work as Daily, Several times a week, Occasionally, or Rarely.
* **Availability Ending** (`availability_ending`): This section is for your availability ending date.
	+ Enter the date you are available to work until.
* **Resume** (`resume`): This section is for your resume.
	+ Attach a copy of your resume.
* **References** (`references`): This section provides emergency contact information.
	+ (Optional) Please list your emergency contact information if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): This section is for emergency contact information.
	+ (Optional) Please list your emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): This section is for emergency phone number.
	+ (Optional) Please enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`): This section determines the relationship of emergency contact.
	+ Choose the relationship of your emergency contact as Spouse, Parent, Friend, or Other.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`): This section is for emergency contact relationship.
	+ (Optional) Please list the relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`): This section provides additional notes about your availability.
	+ (Optional) Please enter any additional notes about your availability.
* **Work Experience Details** (`work_experience_details`): This section provides additional details about your work experience.
	+ (Optional) Please enter any additional details about your work experience.
* **Previous Employer** (`previous_employer`): This section provides information about your previous employer.
	+ (Optional) Please enter your previous employer's name.
* **Availability Availability** (`availability_availability`): This section determines your availability availability.
	+ (Optional) Please enter any additional notes about your availability.
* **Availability Availability Frequency** (`availability_availability_frequency`): This section determines your availability frequency availability.
	+ (Optional) Please enter any additional notes about your availability frequency.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`): This section is for any additional agreements or terms.
	+ (Optional) Please enter any additional agreements or terms.

Note: The form fields with the `(optional)` label are not required fields, but providing them can be helpful for your application. Please be sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
