# Food Festival Vendor Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from food vendors about their products and payment details for the Food Festival. The information collected will be used to verify the vendor's details and ensure a smooth experience for both the vendors and festival attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to answer all the required questions.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_1`): Please provide information about the event where you will be selling your food products.
* **Vendor Information** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_2`): Please enter your vendor information, including your name and contact details.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_3`): Please indicate if your products have any dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Product Information** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_4`): Please enter a brief description of your product.
* **Product Name** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_5`): Please enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_6`): Please enter a detailed description of your product.
* **Product Price** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_7`): Please enter the price of your product.
* **Product Image** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_8`): Please upload an image of your product.
* **Product Video** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_9`): Please upload a video of your product.
* **Food Allergy Warning** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_10`): Please warn festival attendees about any food allergies associated with your product.
* **Food Label Warning** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_11`): Please warn festival attendees about any food labels associated with your product.
* **Product Certification** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_12`): Please enter any certification or awards associated with your product.
* **Certificate File** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_13`): Please upload a certification or award associated with your product.
* **Product Weight** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_14`): Please enter the weight of your product.
* **Packaging Material** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_15`): Please indicate if your product packaging is biodegradable.
* **Packaging Material Other** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_16`): Please specify any other packaging material used for your product.
* **Shipping Details** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_17`): Please enter any shipping details, such as packaging and delivery options.
* **Shipping Information 1** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_18`): Please enter any additional shipping information.
* **Shipping Information 2** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_19`): Please enter any further shipping information.
* **Shipping Information 3** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_20`): Please enter any additional shipping information.
* **Shipping Information 4** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_21`): Please enter any further shipping information.
* **Product Availability** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_22`): Please enter the availability of your product, including the date it will be available for sale.
* **Product Availability 1** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_23`): Please enter the date your product will be available for sale.
* **Product Availability 2** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_24`): Please enter the date your product will be available for sale.
* **Payment Information** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_25`): Please enter any payment information, such as credit card details.
* **Payment Method** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_26`): Please indicate if you accept any payment methods.
* **Payment Details 1** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_27`): Please enter any payment details.
* **Payment Details 2** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_28`): Please enter any further payment details.
* **Payment Details 3** (`food_festival_vendor_intake_form_29`): Please enter any additional payment details.
