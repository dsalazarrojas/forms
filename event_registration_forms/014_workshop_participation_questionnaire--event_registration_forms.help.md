# Workshop Participation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from workshop participants after the event. Please answer the questions to the best of your ability to help us evaluate the effectiveness of our workshops and provide valuable insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below and submit it. If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to ask. Each field has a brief explanation below to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you attended the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you attended the workshop.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address to enable us to contact you with updates and information about future workshops.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you about any further information.
* **What rate this workshop** (`rating`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose how much you enjoyed the workshop from 1 (not enjoyable) to 5 (very enjoyable).
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID number given to you by the workshop staff.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): If a tool was assigned to you, please enter its name or description.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required/optional): If the workshop is related to a specific category, please enter it here.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a short description of the workshop you attended, including its topic and main ideas.
* **Rating Options**: Please select one of the ratings 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 to indicate how much you enjoyed the workshop.
* **Phone Number and Email are optional**: If you do not feel comfortable providing your phone number and email, you can skip them. However, please make sure to provide a valid email address if you choose to enter any contact information. 

Please note that all fields are optional, but the more information you provide, the more valuable feedback we can collect to improve our workshops.
