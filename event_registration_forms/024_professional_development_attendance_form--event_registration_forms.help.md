# Professional Development Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from professionals who have attended a workshop or session. It helps us understand their attendance and gather feedback on the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Review each field carefully and fill in the information accurately.
3. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name* (Text):** Enter the name of the workshop or session you attended.
* **List of Attendees (Select Multiple):** Check the box next to each attendee's name if they were present during the workshop.
* **Session Date (Date):** Enter the date the workshop or session was held.
* **Session Time (Time):** Enter the time the workshop or session started.
* **Trainer Name (Text):** Enter the name of the trainer or instructor who led the workshop.
* **Organization (Text):** Enter the name of the organization you represent (if applicable).
* **Email (Email):** Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Number (Text):** Enter your contact number (if applicable).
* **Notes (Text):** Enter any additional comments or feedback about the workshop or session.
* **Attended (Select One):** Select 'Yes' if you attended the workshop or session.
* **Attended Date (Date):** Enter the date you attended the workshop or session.
* **Attended Time (Time):** Enter the time you attended the workshop or session.
* **Attended Session Date (Date):** Enter the date you attended the workshop or session (this is likely redundant with the above field).
* **Attended Session Time (Time):** Enter the time you attended the workshop or session (this is likely redundant with the above field).
* **Session ID (Text):** Enter the session or workshop ID (if provided).
