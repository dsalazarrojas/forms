# Farm Stay Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help farm administrators or staff collect necessary information from customers or guests who wish to book a stay at a farm. It provides a convenient and organized way to gather details such as dates, stay preferences, contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the start date for your stay from the calendar.
2. Choose the type of stay you prefer (Farm Stay or Glamping).
3. Enter the checkin and checkout dates for your stay.
4. Indicate the number of guests coming with you.
5. Enter the names of your guests.
6. Provide your phone number and email address.
7. Select the duration of your stay from the available options.
8. Enter any message you have for the farm staff.
9. Select the payment type (Cash or Online).
10. Choose the payment method (PayPal or Stripe).
11. Enter the payment amount for your stay.
12. Finally, enter the name of the farm you wish to stay at.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to start your stay at the farm.
* **Stay Type** (Select One): Choose between a Farm Stay and Glamping option.
* **Checkin** (Date): Enter the date you plan to check in to the farm.
* **Checkout** (Date): Enter the date you plan to check out of the farm.
* **Guest Count** (Number): Indicate how many guests are coming with you.
* **Guest Names** (Text): Enter the names of all guests in your group.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Stay Duration** (Select Multiple): Choose the duration of your stay from the available options.
* **Message** (Note): Enter any additional message or request for your stay.
* **Payment Type** (Select One): Select between Cash and Online payment options.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose between PayPal and Stripe payment methods.
* **Payment Amount** (Number): Enter the total payment amount for your stay.
* **Farm Name** (Text): Enter the name of the farm you wish to stay at.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to select the correct payment type and method.
- Enter the correct payment amount for your stay.
- Provide clear and complete information for the farm staff to ensure they can process your booking efficiently.
