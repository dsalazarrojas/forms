# Focus Group Participation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect participant's information, consent, and contact details for a focus group participation. It's a straightforward form to gather the necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** page with your details.
2. Check or uncheck the boxes to indicate your participation status in the **Participant Consent** section.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please type them in the **Do you have any questions about the focus group** field.
4. In the **Contact Information** field, you can add any additional details you'd like to be contacted about.
5. Finally, enter your **Participant Email** and **Participant Phone** (optional) to ensure we can reach you after the event.
