# Focus Group Participation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect participant's information, consent, and contact details for a focus group participation. It's a straightforward form to gather the necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** page with your details.
2. Check or uncheck the boxes to indicate your participation status in the **Participant Consent** section.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please type them in the **Do you have any questions about the focus group** field.
4. In the **Contact Information** field, you can add any additional details you'd like to be contacted about.
5. Finally, enter your **Participant Email** and **Participant Phone** (optional) to ensure we can reach you after the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Participant Information
	+ **Explanation:** Please provide your basic contact details here, including your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Participant Consent** (Page 2, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Participant Consent
	+ **Explanation:** This section allows you to indicate your willingness to participate in the focus group. Check the box if you're willing, or uncheck if you're not.
* **Do you have any questions about the focus group** (Page 3, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Do you have any questions about the focus group
	+ **Explanation:** If you have any questions or concerns about the focus group, type them in here. We'll do our best to address them before the event.
* **Contact Information** (Page 4, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Contact Information
	+ **Explanation:** If you'd like to provide any additional contact information, please enter it here.
* **Participant Email** (Page 5, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Participant Email
	+ **Explanation:** Enter your email address here so we can send you updates or reminders about the event.
* **Participant Phone** (Page 6, Required: No):
	+ **Label:** Participant Phone
	+ **Explanation:** If you'd like to provide your phone number for follow-up, please enter it here (optional).
