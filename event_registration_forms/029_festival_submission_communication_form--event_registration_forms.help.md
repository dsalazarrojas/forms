# Festival Submission Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Submission Communication Form is a tool used by artists and vendors to submit their proposals and information to the festival organizers. This form helps the organizers to evaluate and process the submissions efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Application <span class="required">*required*</span>**
	+ This is the section where you can provide information about your art, including any relevant details about your art form, style, or technique.
* **Vendor Proposal <span class="optional">*(optional)*</span>**
	+ If you are a vendor, please provide a brief proposal outlining your products or services you can offer to the festival.
* **Performer Info <span class="optional">*(optional)*</span>**
	+ Enter any relevant details about yourself as a performer, including your name, contact information, and any special requirements.
* **Artist Media <span class="optional">*(optional)*</span>**
	+ If you have any media files (e.g. videos, images, or documents) that support your proposal, please upload them here.
* **Contact Info <span class="optional">*(optional)*</span>**
	+ Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, so that we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your submission is complete and processed efficiently.
* Upload relevant media files to support your proposal.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
