# Tax Optimization Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering events related to tax optimization, such as workshops, seminars, or meetings. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of event that best describes the purpose of your event (e.g., Tax Strategy Event, Tax Planning Meeting, or Financial Planning Seminar).
2. Enter the name of your event.
3. Enter the start date of your event.
4. Enter the end date of your event.
5. Enter the location of your event.
6. Enter the name of the event organizer.
7. Enter the name of the attendee.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Select the interests related to your event (e.g., Federal Tax Planning, International Tax Planning, State Tax Planning).
11. If you have any other interests not listed, please enter them in the "other interests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event that best describes the purpose of your event.
  * Options: Tax Strategy Event, Tax Planning Meeting, Financial Planning Seminar
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of your event.
* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're registering for the event.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Please indicate your consent to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the interests related to your event (e.g., Federal Tax Planning, International Tax Planning, State Tax Planning).
* **Other Interests** (`interests_other`, text, optional): If you have any other interests not listed, please enter them here.
