# NGO Seminar Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Seminar Check In Form is designed to gather information from attendees for event management and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter the date you attended the seminar.
3. Select the session you attended by choosing "Yes" from the drop-down menu.
4. If you attended additional sessions, select "Yes" from the drop-down menu.
5. If applicable, enter the tools or resources you used during the seminar.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about your attendance in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Date Attended** (Date, date, required): Enter the date you attended the seminar.
* **Time In** (Time, time, required): Enter the time you arrived at the seminar.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Session Attended** (Session Attended, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you attended the session.
* **Additional Sessions** (Additional Sessions, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you attended additional sessions.
* **Tool Used** (Tool Used, text, optional): Enter the tools or resources you used during the seminar.
* **Tool Used 2** (Tool Used 2, text, optional): Enter any additional tools or resources you used during the seminar.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are accurately filled out to avoid data duplication and misclassification.
* If you have any additional tools or resources used during the seminar, please enter them in the "Tool Used" or "Tool Used 2" fields.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
* If you are having trouble accessing the form or have questions, please contact the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
