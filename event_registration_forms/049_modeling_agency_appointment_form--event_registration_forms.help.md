# Modeling Agency Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from agencies for event registrations. It ensures that both the agency and the user have a clear understanding of the appointment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for your agency, including your name, email, phone number, and preferred date and time for the appointment.
2. Confirm your appointment details, including date, time, and purpose.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments for the agency.
4. Select your agency from the dropdown list.
5. Select the names of individuals who will be attending the event from the dropdown list.
6. Confirm your agency and individual details by repeating the same fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency** (`agency`): Enter your agency name.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number. This is a required field.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`): Select the date you prefer for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`): Select the time you prefer for the appointment.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`): Enter the purpose of your appointment.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Confirm Agency** (`confirm_agency`): Select your agency from the dropdown list.
* **Confirm Agency Phone** (`confirm_agency_phone`): Enter your agency's phone number.
* **Confirm Agency Email** (`confirm_agency_email`): Enter your agency's email address.
* **Confirm Agency Notes** (`confirm_agency_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments for your agency.
* **Confirm Agency Date** (`confirm_agency_date`): Select the date you confirm for your agency.
* **Confirm Agency Time** (`confirm_agency_time`): Select the time you confirm for your agency.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Click to submit your form.
* **Agency 2** (`agency_2`): Select another agency from the dropdown list.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`): Select individuals from the dropdown list.
* **Purpose 2** (`purpose_2`): Select the purpose of the appointment for the agency.
* **Date** (`date`): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Time** (`time`): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Agency Phone** (`agency_phone`): Enter the agency's phone number.
* **Agency Email** (`agency_email`): Enter the agency's email address.
* **Agency Notes** (`agency_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments for the agency.
* **Confirm Agency** (`confirm_agency_2`): Select your agency from the dropdown list.
* **Confirm Agency Phone** (`confirm_agency_phone`): Enter your agency's phone number.
* **Confirm Agency Email** (`confirm_agency_email`): Enter your agency's email address.
* **Confirm Agency Notes** (`confirm_agency_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments for your agency.
* **Confirm Agency Date** (`confirm_agency_date`): Select the date you confirm for your agency.
* **Confirm Agency Time** (`confirm_agency_time`): Select the time you confirm for your agency.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2`): Click to submit your form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your administrator.
* Please note that this form is for event registrations, so ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
