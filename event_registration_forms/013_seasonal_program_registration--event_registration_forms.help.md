# Seasonal Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from seasonal program organizers to facilitate the registration of their programs. It is used to gather essential details about the program's type, duration, location, and contact information, ensuring accurate and efficient registration processing.

## How to Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form in sections**: The form is divided into multiple pages, each with distinct fields. Complete one page at a time to help maintain clarity and accuracy.
2. **Select a program type**: Choose one of the listed options to indicate the nature of your program (Yes/No) or enter "Other" to specify a custom type in the text field that follows.
3. **Specify session duration**: Indicate whether your program is of a certain duration (Yes/No) or enter "Other" to describe it in the text field.
4. **Provide start and end dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your program using the provided date fields.
5. **Confirm program location**: Select all applicable locations where your program will be hosted (Yes/No) or enter "Other" to specify a custom location in the text field that follows.
6. **Enter program details**: Provide a brief description of your program in the text field.
7. **Enter contact information**: Fill in your name, email, phone number, and any additional comments about your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **program_type**: Please indicate whether your program is a recurring event or a one-time activity. If not listed, please select "Other" to provide a custom description.
* **session_length**: Specify the duration of your program (Yes/No) or describe it in the text field for more details.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your program to help us schedule it accordingly.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your program to help us schedule it accordingly.
* **program_location**: Confirm the location(s) where your program will take place. If not listed, please enter "Other" to specify a custom location in the text field that follows.
* **program_location_other**: Enter the custom location of your program if not listed in the provided options.
* **program_name**: Provide a brief name for your program to help us identify it.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name as the contact person for the program.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about your program.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you about any program inquiries.
* **submitter_name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the program.
* **submitter_email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the program.
* **submitter_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the program.
* **submitter_comments**: Use this text area to include any additional comments or questions you may have about the form or the program submission process.

This help guide is designed to walk users through the Seasonal Program Registration form, addressing each field and its significance for efficient and accurate registration processing. By following this guide, users should be able to complete the form in a clear and concise manner, ensuring the best outcome for their program.
