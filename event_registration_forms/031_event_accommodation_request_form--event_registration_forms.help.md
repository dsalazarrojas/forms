# Event Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Accommodation Request Form is used to collect information about an event's accommodation needs. This form helps event organizers or administrators to better understand the accommodation requirements for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **event name** field with the name of the event.
2. Enter the **event date** and **event time** fields with the date and time of the event.
3. Enter the **number of attendees** field with the number of attendees expected.
4. Provide any **special requests**, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, in the **special requests** field.
5. Enter the **contact person** and **contact number** fields with the contact information of the person responsible for the event.
6. Enter the **email** field with the email address of the contact person.
7. If necessary, enter any **special diet requests** and **accommodation preferences** in the corresponding fields.
8. Select the **accommodation type** and **accommodation location** from the available options.
9. Enter the **accommodation start date** and **accommodation end date** fields with the dates of the accommodation.
10. Enter the **accommodation end time** field with the end time of the accommodation.
11. Enter the **accommodation address**, **accommodation post code**, **accommodation city**, and **accommodation state** fields with the address and location of the accommodation.
12. Enter the **accommodation country** and **accommodation latitude** and **accommodation longitude** fields with the geographical location of the accommodation.
13. Enter any additional **accommodation notes** in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **event_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time** (time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **attendees** (text, optional): Enter the number of attendees expected.
* **accommodation_request_details** (text, optional): Provide any additional details about the accommodation request.
* **special_requests** (text, optional): Enter any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
* **contact_person** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_number** (text, optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **special_diet_requests** (text, optional): Enter any special diet requests.
* **accommodation_preferences** (text, optional): Enter any accommodation preferences.
* **accommodation_type** (select one, optional): Select the type of accommodation required.
* **accommodation_location** (select multiple, optional): Select the location of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the end date of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_end_time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_postal_code** (text, optional): Enter the post code of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_city** (text, optional): Enter the city of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_state** (text, optional): Enter the state of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_country** (text, optional): Enter the country of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_latitude** (text, optional): Enter the latitude of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_longitude** (text, optional): Enter the longitude of the accommodation.
* **accommodation_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the accommodation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Provide as much information as possible to ensure that the accommodation request can be fulfilled accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer or administrator.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are filled in correctly.
