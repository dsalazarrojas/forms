# Online Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online event registration form is used to gather information about an event and its attendees. This includes event details, attendee contact information, and payment data. Completing this form helps event organizers manage their events efficiently and ensures that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the event title and details in the "Event Details" section.
2. Select the event date and time in the "Event Dates" section.
3. Enter the event location and any additional event notes in the "Event Location" and "Event Notes" sections.
4. Provide attendee information, including name, email, and phone number in the "Attendee Information" section.
5. Specify the event status, type, and category in the "Event Status", "Event Type", and "Event Category" sections.
6. Choose the payment method and status in the "Payment Method" and "Payment Status" sections.
7. Enter the event cost and payment amount in the "Event Cost" and "Payment Amount" sections.
8. Specify the event cost currency and notes in the "Event Cost Currency" and "Event Cost Notes" sections.
9. Select the payment date in the "Payment Date" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event, e.g., "Conference on AI and Machine Learning."
* **Event Dates**: Select the date and time of the event. You can choose from the options provided in the dropdown menu.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event, e.g., "San Francisco, CA."
* **Attendee Information**: Provide the attendee's name, email, and phone number. This information will be used for communication and event updates.
* **Name**: Enter the attendee's name.
* **Email**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Event Status**: Specify the status of the event as "Confirmed" or "Not Confirmed."
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event, e.g., "Virtual" or "In-person."
* **Event Category**: Choose the category of the event, e.g., "Public" or "Private."
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer or host.
* **Event URL**: Enter the URL of the event, e.g., the registration webpage or social media page.
* **Attendee List**: Select whether the attendee list is "Yes" or "No."
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method, e.g., "Cash" or "Bank Transfer."
* **Payment Status**: Specify the payment status as "Paid" or "Not Paid."
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Event Cost**: Enter the event cost.
* **Event Cost Currency**: Choose the currency of the event cost, e.g., "USD" or "EUR."
* **Event Cost Amount**: Enter the event cost amount.
* **Event Cost Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the event cost.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the payment date.
