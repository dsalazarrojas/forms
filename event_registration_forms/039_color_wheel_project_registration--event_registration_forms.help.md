# Color Wheel Project Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for participants to register for the Color Wheel Project, providing their contact details and project information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name, email address, and project title.
2. Select the age group that best represents you.
3. Optionally, list any materials you need for the project and your preferred session time.
4. If applicable, provide your emergency contact's name and phone number and any special needs or accessibility you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address, used for event communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, used for event organization.
* **Project title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a short title for your project.
* **Age group** (`age_group`, select_one, optional): Choose the age group that best represents you: Child, Teen, Adult, or Senior.
* **Materials needed** (`materials_needed`, text, optional): List any specific materials you may need for your project.
* **Preferred session time** (`preferred_session`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred session time: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Special needs or accessibility** (`special_needs`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or accessibility requirements, please specify.
