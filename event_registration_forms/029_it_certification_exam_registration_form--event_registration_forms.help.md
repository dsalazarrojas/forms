# it_certification_exam_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "it_certification_exam_registration_form" is a form designed to collect information from individuals who wish to take an IT certification exam. The form will be reviewed internally to ensure that the necessary requirements are met before proceeding with the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure that you can be contacted for further instructions.
3. Provide your Phone number for any additional contact.
4. Select your Organization (if applicable).
5. Choose your payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
6. Select the payment date and time.
7. Choose the exam date and time (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or All days).
8. Select the exam time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All times).
9. Enter the Exam Fee in dollars, if required.
10. Select the exam currency (USD, EUR, or other currencies).
11. Indicate the payment status (Paid, Not paid, Partially paid).
12. Choose the exam status (Completed, Not completed, Partially completed).
13. Confirm if you are certified (True or False).
14. Select the level of certification (Basic, Advanced, Expert).
15. Enter the certification number, if required.
16. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
17. Click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure that you can be contacted for further instructions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your phone number for any additional contact.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required/optional): Select your organization, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more payment methods (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the payment date.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the payment time.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the exam date (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or All days).
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the exam time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All times).
* **Exam Fee (Dollars)** (`exam_dollars`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the exam fee in dollars, if required.
* **Exam Currency** (`exam_currency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the exam currency (USD, EUR, or other currencies).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate your payment status (Paid, Not paid, Partially paid).
* **Exam Status** (`exam_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the exam status (Completed, Not completed, Partially completed).
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm if you are certified (True or False).
* **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of certification (Basic, Advanced, Expert).
* **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the certification number, if required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes, if necessary.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required/optional): Click "Submit" to submit your registration.
* **Back** (`back`, `text`, required/optional): Click "Back" to go back to a previous page.
* **Payment details** (`payment_details`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment details (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, All methods).
* **Currency** (`currency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the currency (USD, EUR, CAD, AUD, or All currencies).
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the exam location (Virtual, Offline, or All locations).
* **Exam duration** (`exam_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the exam duration (1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, or All durations).
* **Exam type** (`exam_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the exam type (Online, Offline, or All types).
