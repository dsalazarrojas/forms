# Compliance Networking Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees to plan and organize the Compliance Networking Event. This includes your name, job title, and contact information to facilitate networking and communication during the event. By providing accurate and complete information, you will help us create a successful and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Attendee Full Name** in the first field, as it should appear on your badge.
2. Select your **Job Title** as it appears in your organization.
3. Enter the name of your **Organization** (Company or institution you represent).
4. Select your **Industry Sector** from the provided list.
5. Enter your **Business Email Address** (example@company.com).
6. If you wish, you can provide your **Business Phone Number**.
7. For the **Event Participation** field, please help us plan the sessions and networking opportunities by providing any additional comments or questions.
8. Select your preferred **Attendance Type** (In-person or Virtual).
9. Choose up to 3 **Breakout Session Interests** that you are interested in attending.
10. Select your preferred **Networking Experience Level** (New Professional - Looking for Mentor, Established Professional - General Networking, or Expert - Available for Mentorship).
11. If attending in-person, please select your **Dietary Requirements** (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Halal, Kosher, or Other).
12. If you selected "Other" as your dietary requirement, please specify your other dietary needs or allergies.
13. If you require any specific assistance due to accessibility needs, please provide details.
14. Please confirm your **Photography and Media Consent** (I Consent or I Do Not Consent).
15. Confirm your **Data Privacy Acknowledgement** (I Agree or Other).
16. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments or Questions** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on your badge. Please ensure accuracy for proper identification during the event.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title as it appears in your organization.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Industry Sector**: Choose the sector in which you primarily work.
* **Business Email Address**: Enter your work email address (example@company.com).
* **Business Phone Number**: If you wish to provide your business phone number.
* **Event Participation**: Help us plan the sessions and networking opportunities by providing any comments or questions.
* **Attendance Type**: Choose whether you will attend in-person or virtually.
* **Breakout Session Interests**: Select up to 3 sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Networking Experience Level**: Choose your preferred networking approach.
* **Dietary Requirements**: If attending in-person, select your dietary needs.
* **Other Dietary Needs**: If you selected "Other" as your dietary requirement, specify your needs or allergies.
* **Accessibility Accommodations**: If you require specific assistance, please let us know.
* **Photography and Media Consent**: Confirm your consent to being photographed or recorded.
* **Data Privacy Acknowledgement**: Confirm your acknowledgement of data use.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional information or questions you may have.
