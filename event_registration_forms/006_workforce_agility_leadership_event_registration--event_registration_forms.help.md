# Workforce Agility Leadership Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending an event organized by Workforce Agility Leadership. It is intended for event organizers to gather relevant information on attendees' preferences, interests, and attendance plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select whether you will attend the event ("Yes" or "No") and if you will bring attendees ("Yes" or "No") on the "Event Registration Form" page.
2. Select your session interests and preferences on the "Session Interests" and "Session Preferences" pages.
3. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and date of birth on the "Personal Details" page.
4. Provide note if you have any additional comments or questions on the "Note" page.
5. Fill in session details, including session name, date, time, location, and event name, date, time, and location on the "Session Details" page.
