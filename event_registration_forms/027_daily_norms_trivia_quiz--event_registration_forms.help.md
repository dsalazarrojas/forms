# daily_norms_trivia_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users for a daily trivia quiz. You will be asked to enter your first and last name and answer two questions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please make sure to enter your first and last name correctly.
2. Answer the two questions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **First question** (`first_question`, `text`, required: false): Answer the first question.
- **Last question** (`last_question`, `text`, required: false): Answer the last question.

## Tips
* This form is for personal participation in a daily trivia quiz. Please answer honestly.
* If you are having trouble answering a question, try to answer based on your best knowledge.
