# Startup Demo Brunch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP information from attendees for a Startup Demo Brunch event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form carefully, ensuring that all fields are accurate and complete.
2. Review each page of the form to ensure that you have filled out all required and optional fields.
3. Once you have completed all pages, submit your form to reserve your spot at the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: This page is the first step in the RSVP process. Please fill out your name and email address to begin.
* **Page 2**: On this page, you will be asked to select the event date you are interested in attending.
* **Page 3**: Here, you will be asked to provide your company name and role within the company.
* **Page 4**: On this page, you will be asked to choose the number of attendees from your company who plan to join.
* **Page 5**: Please select the type of demo you are interested in attending (e.g., pitch, showcase, or demo).
* **Page 6**: This page is for additional comments or special requests.
* **Page 7**: On this page, you will be asked to provide your preferred seat arrangement (e.g., solo or team).
* **Page 8**: Here, you will be asked to confirm your availability and RSVP details.
* **Page 9**: Please review your form carefully and ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* **Page 10**: On this page, you will have the opportunity to make any final comments or requests.
* **Page 11**: Here, you will be asked to confirm your RSVP status.
* **Page 12**: This page is for any additional information or questions.
* **Page 13**: On this page, you will be asked to confirm your registration.
* **Page 14**: Here, you will be asked to provide any dietary restrictions or special needs.
* **Page 15**: Please confirm your preferred time of arrival and departure.
* **Page 16**: On this page, you will be asked to provide any additional information about your company or team.
* **Page 17**: Here, you will be asked to confirm your RSVP details again.
* **Page 18**: This page is for any final comments or questions.
* **Page 19**: On this page, you will be asked to review your form carefully.
* **Page 20**: Here, you will be able to submit your completed form to reserve your spot at the event.

Note: This form is designed to guide you through the RSVP process, ensuring that you complete all necessary fields and have a smooth experience. Please fill out each page carefully and accurately to ensure that your RSVP is complete and submitted successfully.
