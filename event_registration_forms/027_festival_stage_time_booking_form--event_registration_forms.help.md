# festival_stage_time_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event organizers to book time slots on a festival stage. It will help event organizers provide details about their event, ensuring efficient stage utilization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization behind the event.
- **Event Name**: Enter a brief description of the event, including the type and nature of the event.
- **Start Time**: Select the start time of your event. 
- **End Time**: Select the end time of your event. 
- **Stage**: Choose which stage you would like to book. Options include Stage A, Stage B, Stage C, and Stage D. 
- **Date**: Enter the date of your event. 
- **Booking Type**: Select how you would like your event to be managed. Options include Semi-Live, Live, DJ, and Other. This field is required.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information about your event, such as specific requirements or requests. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and clear to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
- Be mindful of the stage booking schedule to avoid conflicting bookings.
- If you need more information or have questions, contact your festival organizer.
