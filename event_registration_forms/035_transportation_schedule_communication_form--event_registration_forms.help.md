# Transportation Schedule Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to communicate transportation schedules to users. It is designed to provide a convenient way to share transportation information with colleagues, customers, or other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Transportation Schedule** in the first field. This is a description of the transportation schedule you want to communicate, e.g. "Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM".
2. Enter the **Event ID** in the second field. This is a unique identifier for the event or activity being communicated.
3. Select the **Schedule Type** in the third field. This indicates whether the schedule is for a single event, multiple events, or a continuous schedule.
4. Enter the **Start Time** in the fourth field. This is the start time of the schedule.
5. Enter the **End Time** in the fifth field. This is the end time of the schedule.
6. Choose the **Vehicle Type** in the sixth field. This indicates the type of vehicle used for the transportation, e.g. Car, Bus, or Train.
7. Enter the **Location** in the seventh field. This is the location where the transportation will take place or where the vehicle will start/stop.
8. Enter a brief **Description** in the eighth field. This is an optional field for providing additional information about the transportation schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transportation Schedule**: The description of the transportation schedule. This could be a daily schedule, a one-time event, or a recurring schedule.
* **Event ID**: A unique identifier for the event or activity being communicated. This helps identify the purpose of the transportation schedule.
* **Schedule Type**: The type of schedule being communicated. This could be a single event, multiple events, or a continuous schedule.
* **Start Time**: The start time of the transportation schedule.
* **End Time**: The end time of the transportation schedule.
* **Vehicle Type**: The type of vehicle used for the transportation. This could be a car, bus, train, etc.
* **Location**: The location where the transportation will take place or where the vehicle will start/stop.
* **Description**: An optional field for providing additional information about the transportation schedule.
