# Conference Registration Form With Payment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Conference Registration Form With Payment is designed to gather necessary information from attendees for event registration. It includes fields for personal details, contact information, payment preferences, and other essential requirements for a successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields, including attendee name, email, phone number, organization, job title, mailing address, city, state, postal code, country, registration package, receipt email address, and terms of acceptance.
2.  Select your preferred payment method from the given options: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Company Invoice.
3.  For attendees who choose to pay using a credit or debit card, fill in your card number, cardholder name, expiry date, and CVV (Security Code) details. Alternatively, if you're paying through bank transfer, specify your billing address.
4.  If you'd like to add any optional items to your registration package, select them from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name`, required, text): Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, required, email): This field is used for event-related communications and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, required, text): Provide your contact number for further communication.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, required, text): Indicate your company or institution name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, required, text): Fill in your current position.
*   **Mailing Address** (`address`, required, text): Enter your full address for the event.
*   **City** (`city`, required, text): Type in your city name.
*   **State/Province** (`state`, required, text): Enter your state or province.
*   **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, required, text): Specify your postal code (ZIP code).
*   **Country** (`country`, required, select_one): Choose your country of origin.
*   **Registration Package** (`registration_package`, required, select_one): Select your preferred package from the available options.
*   **Additional Options** (`add_ons`, optional, select_multiple): Choose any optional items you'd like to add to your package.
*   **Promotional Code** (`promo_code`, optional, text): If you have a promotional code, enter it here.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required, select_one): Select your preferred payment method.
*   **Card Number** (`card_number`, optional, text): Enter your credit or debit card number.
*   **Cardholder Name** (`card_holder`, optional, text): Fill in the name on your card.
*   **Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, optional, text): Enter your card's expiry date (MM/YY).
*   **CVV** (`cvv`, optional, text): Type in your CVV (Security Code) for the card.
*   **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, optional, text): If your billing address is different from your mailing address, enter it here.
*   **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, required, select_one): Choose your preferred meal option for the event.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional, text): If you have any dietary restrictions, specify them here.
*   **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional, text): If you require any special accommodations, mention them here.
*   **Receipt Email Address** (`receipt_email`, required, email): Enter the email address where you'd like to receive the receipt.
*   **I Accept Terms and Refund Policy** (`terms_accepted`, required, select_one): Choose whether you accept the terms and refund policy of the event.

## Tips

- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- Use the promotional code or coupon if you're eligible for a discount.
- Choose your preferred payment method and fill in the corresponding details carefully.
- If you're paying through a company invoice, be sure to provide the required information for the company to complete the payment.
