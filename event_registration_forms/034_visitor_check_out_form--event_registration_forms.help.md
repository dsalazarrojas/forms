# Visitor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visitor Check Out Form is a tool to collect visitor information and preferences for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the primary identifier for you as a visitor.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is your electronic address for communication.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field. This is your contact number.
5. Select a **Date** on which you attended the event in the "Date" field. Use the date format: mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Choose a **Time** for your attendance in the "Time" field. Select the time you arrived at the event in 24-hour format: hh:mm.
7. Write any **Feedback** you may have in the "Feedback" field. This is an open text area for comments.
8. Answer the question **How likely are you to return?** by selecting one or more options in the "How likely are you to return?" field.
9. Answer the question **How likely do you feel about returning to this event location?** by selecting one option in the "How likely do you feel about returning to this event location?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is where you enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: This is where you enter your surname.
* **Email**: This is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: This is where you enter your contact number.
* **Date**: This is where you select the date you attended the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: This is where you select the time you arrived at the event in 24-hour format: hh:mm.
* **Feedback**: This is an open area for writing any comments or feedback you may have.
* **How likely are you to return?**: This is a field to select how likely you are to return to this event.
* **How likely do you feel about returning to this event location?**: This is a field to select how likely you feel about returning to this event location. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Only submit one entry per visitor.
