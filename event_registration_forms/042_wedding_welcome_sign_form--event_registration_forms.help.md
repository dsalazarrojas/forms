# Wedding Welcome Sign Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Welcome Sign Form is used to collect RSVP and contact information from guests attending a wedding. This information will help the wedding hosts plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate answers for each of the required fields and provide any additional information that is relevant to your attendance at the wedding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (text, optional): Please provide a brief message to introduce yourself and let the wedding hosts know that you're attending the wedding. This can be as simple as writing your name and "I'm coming!".
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): If you'd like to share your contact information with the wedding hosts, please enter it here. This will help them reach out to you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **RSVP** (select_one, optional): Let us know if you'll be able to attend the wedding by selecting "Attending", "Not Attending", or "Maybe Attending".
* **Email** (email, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address to the wedding hosts, enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number to the wedding hosts, enter it here.
* **Date of Birthday** (date, optional): If you'd like to share your birthday date with the wedding hosts, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to complete this form with accurate and up-to-date information, so that we can plan for your attendance.
* If you're unable to attend the wedding, kindly let us know in advance so we can update our arrangements accordingly.
