# Paintball And Airsoft Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for the paintball and airsoft event. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and safe experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure that you fill out all the required fields, marked as "required".
2. **Select your scheduling preferences**: Choose whether you can attend the event or not.
3. **Acknowledge your safety**: Agree to abide by the event safety rules and guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Provide your name and any other identifying information you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, select multiple, required): Check "Yes" if you can attend the event, and "No" if you cannot. This will help us plan the event schedule accordingly.
* **Safety Acknowledgement** (`safety_acknowledgement`, text, required): Read and agree to the event safety guidelines to ensure your own safety and the safety of others.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates and information.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, select one, required): Choose where you can be reached during the event (e.g., None, Home, Work, Mobile, Both).
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, date, required): Choose a date for the event.
* **Schedule End Date** (`schedule_end_date`, date, required): Choose an end date for the event.
* **Schedule Start Time** (`schedule_start_time`, time, required): Choose a start time for the event.
* **Schedule End Time** (`schedule_end_time`, time, required): Choose an end time for the event.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, select one, required): Check if you will be attending an event at a specific location.
* **Event Organiser** (`event_organiser`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organiser Phone** (`event_organiser_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organiser Email** (`event_organiser_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organiser Address** (`event_organiser_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the event organizer.

## Tips

* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers at [insert email or contact info].
* Be sure to agree to the event safety guidelines to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
