# Street Food Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Street Food Experience Registration" form is designed to collect information for event organizers and attendees for street food experience events. It helps to gather crucial data for event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Event Type**: Select the type of event from the provided options.
2.  **Name**: Enter your name (optional).
3.  **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
4.  **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have from the provided options.
5.  **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
6.  **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
7.  **Street Food Experience Type**: Select the type of street food experience from the provided options.
8.  **Language**: Select the language for the event.
9.  **Event Location**: Enter the event location (optional).
10. **Event Location Note**: Enter any additional notes about the event location (optional).
11.  **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event (optional).
12.  **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer (optional).
13.  **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email of the event organizer (optional).
14.  **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer (optional).
15.  **Attendee Notes**: Enter any notes about the attendees (optional).
16.  **Consent Given**: Confirm that you have given your consent for the event.
17.  **Consent Note**: Enter any additional notes about your consent (optional).
18.  **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
19.  **Privacy Policy**: Confirm that you have agreed to the privacy policy of the event.
20.  **Street Food Experience Terms**: Confirm that you have agreed to the street food experience terms.
21.  **Food Allergy**: Select any food allergies you have from the provided options.
22.  **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions you have from the provided options.
23.  **Accessibility**: Select the accessibility requirements for the event.
24.  **Attendee Count**: Enter the number of attendees attending the event.
25.  **Event Date and Time**: Confirm the event date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event, such as 'Yes', to indicate that the event is happening.
* **Name**: This field is optional but is used to identify you for the event.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about any updates or changes to the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select from the options below to indicate any dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event to ensure accurate scheduling.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event to ensure accurate scheduling.
* **Street Food Experience Type**: Select the type of street food experience from the options provided.
* **Language**: Choose the language of the event so we can tailor the experience accordingly.
* **Event Location**: If the event is held outdoors, provide the exact address or coordinates of the event location. If the location is an event center, enter the name of the venue.
*  **Event Location Note**: Use this field to mention anything specific about the event location that may be of note.
*  **Event Description**: Write a brief description of the event that will help us understand what to expect.
*  **Event Organizer**: This field is optional but can be used to list all the event organizers.
*  **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email of the event organizer so we can contact them.
*  **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer so we can contact them.
*  **Attendee Notes**: If you have any notes that may be helpful for the event organizers, such as special requests or preferences, enter them here.
*  **Consent Given**: Confirm that you have given your consent for the event to proceed.
*  **Consent Note**: Enter any additional notes about your consent (optional).
*  **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
*  **Privacy Policy**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the privacy policy of the event.
*  **Street Food Experience Terms**: Confirm that you have agreed to the street food experience terms.
*  **Food Allergy**: Select any food allergies you have from the options provided, and we will accommodate your needs.
*  **Medical Condition**: Select any medical conditions you have from the options provided, and we will take necessary precautions.
*  **Accessibility**: Choose the accessibility requirements for the event to ensure a smooth experience for all attendees.
*  **Attendee Count**: Enter the number of attendees you expect.
*  **Event Date and Time**: Confirm the event date and time to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and comprehensively.
- If you're an event organizer, ensure to provide clear information for your attendees.
- If you're an attendee, please provide clear and concise information about your dietary restrictions, medical conditions, and accessibility requirements.
- Make sure to review the terms and conditions and privacy policy before confirming your consent.
- If you have any questions or need help with filling in the form, feel free to contact us.
