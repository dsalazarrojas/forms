# Event Coverage Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Coverage Registration form is used to collect information from event participants to document their media coverage. This form is typically used during events to verify that media representatives have registered for coverage and understand their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your event coverage information in the "event_coverage_form" field (page 1).
2. Indicate whether you will be requesting media credentials in the "media_credential_request" field (page 2).
3. Provide any additional details about your coverage in the "coverage_details" field (page 3).
4. Confirm your consent by entering "Yes" or "No" in the "confirm_consent" field (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Coverage Form** (`event_coverage_form`, text, required): This is the main field where you enter your event coverage information. Please provide a clear and concise description of your coverage, including the name of the event, the type of coverage you will be providing, and any other relevant details.
* **Media Credential Request** (`media_credential_request`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you will be requesting media credentials, such as a press pass or a badge. If you are not requesting media credentials, select "No".
* **Coverage Details** (`coverage_details`, text, required): This field is for providing additional details about your coverage, such as the scope of your coverage, your coverage goals, and any other relevant information.
* **Confirm Consent** (`confirm_consent`, text, required): Please enter "Yes" if you confirm that you understand and will comply with the event's media coverage policies and guidelines. Enter "No" if you do not wish to confirm. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in the "event_coverage_form" field to ensure that your coverage is properly recorded.
* If you are unsure about what type of media credentials you need, consult with the event organizers or check the event's website for more information.
* Always confirm your consent in the "confirm_consent" field before submitting the form.
