# Biotech Conference Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotech Conference Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from attendees for a biotech conference. This form helps the conference organizers to understand the attendees' preferences, interests, and needs, allowing them to tailor the conference experience to suit their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and job title.
2. Enter your organization's name and type, as well as its size.
3. Choose your registration type and preferred conference date.
4. Select all sessions of interest from the provided tracks.
5. Choose your keynote session preferences.
6. Specify your networking interests.
7. If applicable, enter your dietary restrictions and accessibility needs.
8. Indicate whether you need hotel or transportation assistance.
9. Select your t-shirt size for conference merchandise.
10. Choose your preferred communication method.
11. Decide whether you want to receive information about future biotech events.
12. Provide any special requests or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your professional title.
* **Company or Organization**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent.
* **Organization Size**: Select the size of your organization.
* **Registration Type**: Choose your registration level.
* **Preferred Conference Date**: Select the date you prefer to attend.
* **Interested Tracks**: Select all sessions of interest.
* **Keynote Session Preferences**: Choose the keynote topics that interest you.
* **Networking Interests**: Select all networking interests.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Specify any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Enter any special accessibility needs.
* **Hotel Assistance**: Indicate whether you need hotel or transportation assistance.
* **Transportation Needs**: Specify your transportation needs.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your t-shirt size for conference merchandise.
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose your preferred communication method.
* **Marketing Consent**: Decide whether you want to receive information about future biotech events.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or comments.
