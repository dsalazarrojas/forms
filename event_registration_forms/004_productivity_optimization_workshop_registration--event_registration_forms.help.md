# Productivity Optimization Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Productivity Optimization Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from potential attendees about their session preferences and contact details for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct options.
2. Select the sessions you wish to attend from the Session Selections dropdown list.
3. Choose your attendee needs from the Attendee Needs dropdown list.
4. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info and Email fields.
5. Verify your Phone number is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details (Workshop Details)** (`workshop_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your interests or goals for attending the workshop.
* **Session Selections (Session Selections)** (`session_selections`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sessions you wish to attend.
	+ Select all sessions you wish to attend. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Attendee Needs (Attendee Needs)** (`attendee_needs`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of support you may need during the workshop.
	+ Select Breakfast, Lunch, or Snacks to indicate your dietary needs.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other contact information you wish to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Workshop Details field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the workshop organizers directly.
