# Leadership Retreat Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help leaders register for or provide information about their participation in a leadership retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your participant information in the `participant_info` field.
2.  Provide details about the event in the `event_info` field.
3.  Enter the event date in the `event_date` field.
4.  Specify the event start time in the `event_start_time` field.
5.  Enter the event end time in the `event_end_time` field.
6.  Provide the location of the event in the `event_location` field.
7.  Enter your participant name in the `participant_name` field.
8.  Enter your participant email address in the `participant_email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **participant_info**: This is the field where you'll provide information about yourself as a participant. This could include your bio, background, or any other relevant details about your leadership role or experience.
*   **event_info**: Please enter a brief description of the event you're enrolling in or would like to enroll in.
*   **event_date**: This is where you'll enter the date of the event. The format for this field is `MM/DD/YYYY` (month/day/year).
*   **event_start_time**: Enter the start time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 8:00 AM or 1:00 PM).
*   **event_end_time**: Enter the end time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 8:00 AM or 1:00 PM).
*   **event_location**: Provide the physical location of the event.
*   **participant_name**: This is the field for your name.
*   **participant_email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.

## Tips
