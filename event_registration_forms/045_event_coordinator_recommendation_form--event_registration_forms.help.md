# Event Coordinator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and recommendations on event coordinators. It helps event organizers to evaluate the performance and satisfaction of their coordinators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the User Input section.
2. Describe the event details in the Event Details section.
3. Rate the coordinator's performance in the Coordinator Rating section.
4. Select the recommended coordinator(s) in the Recommended Coordinator section.
5. Rate the event's overall satisfaction in the Overall Satisfaction section.
6. Choose the coordinator's availability in the Coordinator Availability section.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Comments section.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you would like to review the event.
9. Select "Good" or "Fair" to rate the event's overall rating.
10. Choose a date for the review in the Review Date section.
11. Choose a time for the review in the Review Time section.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback and recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This section collects your contact information.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Describe the event details.
* **Coordinator Information** (`coordinator_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the coordinator.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the event.
* **Coordinator Rating** (`coordinator_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the coordinator's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommended Coordinator** (`recommended_coordinator`, select_multiple, optional): Select the recommended coordinator(s).
* **Event Recommendation** (`event_recommendation`, select_one, optional): Rate the event's overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Coordinator Availability** (`coordinator_availability`, select_one, optional): Choose the coordinator's availability.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback and recommendations.
* **Review** (`review`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you would like to review the event.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the event's overall satisfaction as "Good" or "Fair".
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the review.
