# Piano Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piano Festival Registration Form is used to collect essential information from attendees. This form helps the festival organizers to manage participant data and provide a better experience for all festival-goers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Add your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select your favorite instrument from the list in the "Instrument" field.
7. Choose a valid date for your festival registration in the "Registration Date" field.
8. If you have a specific tool or equipment you need for the festival, describe it in the "Assigned Tool" field.
9. Select a category relevant to your participation in the "Category" field.
10. Provide a brief description of your role or interest in the festival in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Provide a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Add your phone number so we can contact you in case of any updates or changes.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address to help us plan logistics and arrangements.
* **Instrument** (`Instrument`, select_one, required/optional): Select your primary instrument or skill that you would like to showcase at the festival.
* **Registration Date** (`Registration Date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your festival registration. Please note that this date is not necessarily your arrival or departure date from the festival.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required/optional): If you have any specific tool or equipment that you need for the festival, describe it here.
* **Category** (`Category`, text, required/optional): Select a category that best describes your interest or participation in the festival.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required/optional): Give a brief description of your role or interest in the festival, so we can better understand your needs or expectations.
