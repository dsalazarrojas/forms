# Easter Drive Thru Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for the Easter Drive Thru event, which includes participant details and their planned activities during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Indicate if you'll be participating in any Easter activities.
4. Choose your preferred time and date for participating in the event.
5. Let us know if you'll be showing up or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### participant_details (Text)
* **participant_details**: A text field for providing any additional details about yourself or your group.

### participant_name (Text)
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as you want to be addressed.

### participant_phone (Text)
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### participant_email (Email)
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.

### participant_notes (Text)
* **participant_notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your event participation.

### participant_easter_activities (Select Multiple)
* **participant_easter_activities**: Choose one or more activities you plan to participate in during the event.
  - Easter Egg Decorating
  - Easter Egg Hunt
  - Easter Photo Booth

### participant_time (Time)
* **participant_time**: Choose your preferred time for participating in the event.

### participant_date (Date)
* **participant_date**: Select the date you plan to participate in the event.

### participant_dont_show_up (Select One)
* **participant_dont_show_up**: Check Yes or No to indicate if you'll be attending.
