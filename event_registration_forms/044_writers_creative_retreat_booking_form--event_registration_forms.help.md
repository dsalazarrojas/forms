# Writers Creative Retreat Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a spot in the Writers Creative Retreat. Please fill out the form with your booking details and participant information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the booking details section with your desired dates and time for the retreat.
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields respectively.
3. Confirm your booking by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Booking" field.
4. If you are bringing any participants, enter their information in the "Participant" section.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): Enter your desired dates and time for the retreat.
* **Name** (id: 2, type: text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (id: 3, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Dates** (id: 5, type: date, required: false): Enter the dates you would like to book.
* **Time** (id: 6, type: time, required: false): Select the time you would like to attend.
* **Participant Information** (id: 7, type: note, required: false): This field is for any additional participant information you would like to add.
* **Confirm Booking** (id: 8, type: select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have read and agree with the retreat's terms and conditions.
* **Message** (id: 9, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional message you would like to include with your booking.
* **Confirm Arrival** (id: 10, type: select_one, required: false): Select how you plan to arrive at the retreat, either Early, Late, or Flexible.
* **Arrival Details** (id: 11, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional arrival details.
* **Participant Name** (id: 12, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (id: 13, type: email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (id: 14, type: text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Dates** (id: 15, type: date, required: false): Enter the participant's desired dates.
* **Participant Time** (id: 16, type: time, required: false): Select the participant's desired time.
* **Participant Message** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional message for the participant.
* **Participant Arrival** (id: 18, type: select_one, required: false): Select how the participant plans to arrive at the retreat, either Early, Late, or Flexible.
* **Participant Arrival Details** (id: 19, type: text, required: false): Enter any additional participant arrival details.
* **Participant Arrival Time** (id: 20, type: time, required: false): Select the participant's desired arrival time.
* **Participant Arrival Date** (id: 21, type: date, required: false): Enter the participant's desired arrival date.
* **Participant Notes** (id: 22, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional participant notes.
* **Booking Status** (id: 23, type: select_multiple, required: false): This field is for internal use by the retreat organizers to track the booking status.

## Tips
* Please review and submit the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the retreat organizers.
* The form is meant to be completed by writers interested in attending the retreat.
