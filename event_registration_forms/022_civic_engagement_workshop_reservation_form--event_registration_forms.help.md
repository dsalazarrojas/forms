# Civic Engagement Workshop Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Civic Engagement Workshop and collect relevant details for the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee name** in the first field. This is your full name as it should appear on the workshop materials.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the second field. This will be used for confirmation and to send you materials related to the workshop.
3. If you are representing an organization or school, please provide the name in the third field. This is optional.
4. Select your preferred **Session choice** from the options provided. You can choose between Morning, Afternoon, or Evening session.
5. If you have any dietary requirements, specify them in the fourth field.
6. Indicate any **Accessibility needs** you may have in the fifth field.
7. If applicable, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the sixth field.
8. Choose whether you **Consent for photography**. This will allow event photos to be taken.
9. If you have made a payment for the workshop, select the **Payment status** accordingly.
10. Finally, enter the **Submission date**, which will automatically be filled with the current date.
