# Event Innovation Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Innovation Showcase Registration Form is a mandatory form that collects essential details from event planners, attendees, and potential presenters for the Event Innovation Showcase event. This form is crucial for creating a seamless and engaging event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your title**: Fill out your title in the text field provided.
2. **Select your session interests**: Choose your session interests from the available options.
3. **Identify your potential presenter status**: Choose whether you can present at the event.
4. **Provide attendee details**: Enter your attendee information, including your email address and phone number.
5. **Enter event date and time**: Fill out the date and time of the event.
6. **Submit your form**: Once you've filled out all required fields, click the submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Planners**
* **Event Planners**: (`event_planners`, `text`, required): This field is for event planners to provide their title.

### 2. **Session Interests**
* **Session Interests**: (`session_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple session interests from the provided options.

### 3. **Potential Presenters**
* **Potential Presenters**: (`potential_presenters`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you can present at the event by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 4. **Attendee Details**
* **Attendee Details**: (`attendee_details`, `note`, required): Enter your email address and phone number in this field.

### 5. **Date**
* **Date**: (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.

### 6. **Time**
* **Time**: (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the event.

### 7. **Email**
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.

### 8. **Phone**
* **Phone**: (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely. This form is a mandatory component of the event planning process, and incomplete or inaccurate submissions may lead to delays or issues with event preparation. If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, please reach out to the event planners for support.
