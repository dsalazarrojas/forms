# Community Podcast Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for community members interested in appearing on our podcast as a guest. Please fill it out thoroughly to increase your chances of being selected.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and honestly**. Make sure to provide all required information.
2. **Read each field explanation below** to understand what each section is for.
3. **Submit your application** once you're done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Community Podcast Guest Application --**: This is an introduction to our community and the podcast. We are always looking for voices that reflect our vibrant community!
* **Guest Full Name**: Your complete name as it appears on official documents or in your public identity.
* **Organization or Professional Title**: Your official title at your workplace or the one you hold in your field.
* **Contact Email Address**: Your email address for communication and follow-up.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Your phone number for scheduling and coordination.
* **Proposed Podcast Topic**: Briefly describe the topic you'd like to discuss on our podcast. This will help us understand your expertise and relevance to our community.
* **Brief Summary of Your Background**: This is your opportunity to showcase your expertise and experience in the field you're proposing to talk about. 
* **Have you appeared on a podcast before?**: Answer 'True' if you have, otherwise answer 'False'. This helps us understand your podcasting experience.
* **Link to a Previous Episode**: If you've answered 'True', provide the URL to one of your previous episodes.
* **What unique perspective can you bring to the community?**: This is your chance to showcase why your thoughts and opinions are valuable to our community.
* **Availability for Recording**: Select all the days when you're available to record. Please be realistic about your schedule.
* **Do you have your own high-quality recording equipment?**: If you do, answer 'True', otherwise answer 'False'. We may need to know about your setup.
* **Recording Format Preference**: Choose how you'd like to be recorded - in-person at our studio or remote.
* **Website or Social Media Links**: This is for your online presence. Share links to your social media or website.
* **-- Release and Recording Policy --**: By submitting this form, you agree to our standard media release and podcast distribution policy. Make sure you understand and agree to our terms.
* **Today's Date**: This is a timestamp to keep track of when you submitted your application.
