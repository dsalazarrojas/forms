# Class Attendance Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to report their attendance and provide necessary details to the class instructor or staff.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure to provide all required fields (marked as "true" in the required field).
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant support team or instructor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): This is your official name as a student. Please type it clearly and accurately.
* **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, text, optional): If you have a student ID number, please provide it. This is not required.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): If you have an email address associated with your student account, you can provide it. This is not required.
* **Class Name or Section** (`Class Name or Section`, text, required): This is the name of your class or section. Please provide the correct name.
* **Class Date** (`Class Date`, date, required): This is the date of the class meeting. Please ensure to provide the correct date.
* **Sign-in Time** (`Sign-in Time`, time, required): This is the time of your arrival to the class. Please type it accurately.
* **Attendance Status** (`Attendance Status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Present
	+ Late arrival
	+ Excused absence
	+ Unexcused absence
	+ Partial attendance
* **If late, reason for lateness** (`If late, reason for lateness`, text, optional): If you arrived late, please provide the reason for your lateness. This is optional, but it helps to keep track of your attendance accurately.
* **Missing any required materials** (`Missing any required materials`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
	+ Not applicable
* **Details of missing materials** (`Details of missing materials`, text, optional): If you are missing any required materials, please provide a brief description of what you are missing.
* **Health and Wellness Status** (`Health and Wellness Status`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Tired or fatigued
	+ Not feeling well
	+ Injured
	+ Other health concern
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Any health concerns to report** (`Any health concerns to report`, text, optional): If you have any health concerns, please provide a brief description.
* **Need to update contact information** (`Need to update contact information`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **New contact information** (`New contact information`, text, optional): If you need to update your contact information, please provide the new details.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`Additional Notes or Comments`, text, optional): If you have any special circumstances or concerns, please provide a brief description.
* **I confirm this information is accurate** (`I confirm this information is accurate`, select_one, required): Please choose "Yes, confirmed" to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
