# Blogger Symposium Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Blogger Symposium Signup Form" is a registration form designed for bloggers to sign up for the symposium. It collects necessary information to tailor the event experience and provide a better experience for attendees and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Blogger Symposium Signup Form" link.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter the correct information for each field.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name \***: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name \***: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email Address \***: Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone Number \***: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Your Blog Name**: Enter your blog's name as it appears on the web.
* **Blog URL**: Enter the URL of your blog (if you have one).
* **Your Blog Niche**: Select the area of blogging that your blog focuses on (e.g., Lifestyle, Fashion, etc.).
* **Blogging Experience Level**: Indicate your level of experience in blogging (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.).
* **Estimated Monthly Blog Traffic**: Select the estimated number of visitors to your blog each month.
* **Which sessions are you interested in?**: Select all the sessions that interest you (e.g., Monetization Strategies, SEO & Traffic Growth, etc.).
* **Interest in networking with other bloggers**: Indicate your level of interest in networking with other bloggers (Very interested, Interested, Somewhat interested, Not interested).
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration you would like to apply for (Full Day Pass, Half Day Pass, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Special Requests or Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special requests or needs, enter them here.
* **How did you hear about this symposium?**: Indicate how you heard about the symposium (Social Media, Website, Email, etc.).
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select the size of T-shirt you would like to wear during the event.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date and time of your registration.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Please ensure you complete all required fields to complete the registration process correctly.
