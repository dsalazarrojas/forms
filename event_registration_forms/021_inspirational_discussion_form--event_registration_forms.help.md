# inspirational_discussion_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about inspirational discussions, conversations, and topics that participants have found impactful or thought-provoking. The form helps us understand the depth and breadth of these inspirational topics, which can be used to foster further growth and understanding among individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information at hand.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with <required> next to them.
3. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspirational Topics (1)** (`text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of the inspirational topic that resonated with you. This can be a quote, a story, or a personal experience.
* **Inspirational Leaders (2)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose a leader or figure from the list who has inspired you or had a significant impact on your thoughts or actions.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Inspirational Conversation (3)** (`text`, optional): Describe a conversation that you had which was inspirational or impactful for you.
* **Inspirational Leadership (4)** (`text`, optional): Please list the leadership skills or traits that you admire or strive for in yourself or others.
* **Inspirational Participants (5)** (`text`, optional): Name one or more individuals who have participated in or supported you during an inspirational conversation or experience.
* **Inspirational Outcomes (6)** (`text`, optional): Describe the positive outcomes or results that came from an inspirational experience or conversation.
* **Inspirational Topics 2 (7)** (`text`, optional): Enter another inspirational topic that resonated with you or is relevant to your growth and understanding.
