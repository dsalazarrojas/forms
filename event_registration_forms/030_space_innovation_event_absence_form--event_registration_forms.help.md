# Space Innovation Event Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participant absences from an event. It helps to track and understand why participants are unable to attend an event, and to gather relevant details for further action or follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine your response to the "event absences form" question. Select "Yes" if you are unable to attend the event.
2. If you select "Yes", provide a brief reason for your absence in the "event absences reason" field.
3. Fill in the "contact name" field with your name.
4. Enter your phone number in the "contact phone" field.
5. Fill in the "participant name" field with the name of the participant who is absent.
6. Enter the participant's email address in the "participant email" field.
7. Select the date of the participant's absence in the "participant absence date" field.
8. Enter the time of the participant's absence in the "participant absence time" field.
9. Enter the ID of the event in the "event id" field.
10. Enter the date of the event in the "event start date" field.
11. If necessary, provide further details about the participant's absence in the "participant absence reason" field.
12. Add any additional comments or notes in the "participant absence note" field.
13. You can also add a message for the form submission in the "form submission" field.
14. Finally, fill in the "submitted by" field with your name.
15. Enter the date and time of form submission in the "submitted at" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Absences Form (event_absence_form)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are unable to attend the event.
* **Event Absences Reason (event_absence_reason)**: Provide a brief reason for your absence.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter your name as contact.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: Enter the name of the participant who is absent.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Absence Date (participant_absence_date)**: Select the date of the participant's absence.
* **Participant Absence Time (participant_absence_time)**: Select the time of the participant's absence.
* **Event ID (event_id)**: Enter the ID of the event.
* **Event Start Date (event_start_date)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Participant Absence Reason (participant_absence_reason)**: Provide further details about the participant's absence.
* **Participant Absence Note (participant_absence_note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the participant's absence.
* **Form Submission (form_submission)**: You can add a message for the form submission.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by)**: Fill in your name in this field.
* **Submitted At (submitted_at)**: Enter the date and time of form submission.
