# Grand Reopening Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you complete the Grand Reopening Party RSVP Form. Follow the steps below to register yourself and any guests you are attending with.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for yourself and any other guests you are attending with.
2. Choose your preferred options for food and drinks.
3. Enter any additional comments or special requests.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Guest Information

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you will use to receive updates and information about the party.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **RSVP Message**: Enter any message or comment you would like to share with the party host.

### Party Details

* **Event Date**: Select the date of the party you would like to attend.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the party you would like to attend.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date by which you would like to respond to this form.
* **Party Attendees**: Choose who will be attending with you (me and my family, just me, me and my partner, or my friends).
* **RSVP Type**: Select whether you are attending, declined, or unsure about attending the party.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share with the party host.

### Food and Drinks

* **Food Preferences**: Choose your preferred options for food (Beef, Chicken, Fish, Veggie, None).
* **Drink Preferences**: Choose your preferred option for drink (Soda, Juice, Water, Beer, None).
* **Other Notes**: Enter any additional information about your preferences.

### Special Requests

* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event (Music, Dance, Food, Movie Night, Game Night).
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you have.
* **Special Needs**: Enter any special needs or accommodations you require.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Choose your dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, None).
* **Dietary Restrictions 2**: Choose your dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten Free, None).

Note: If you have any questions or need help with this form, please contact the party host.
