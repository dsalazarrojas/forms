# Event Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to grant access to attendees, staff, and vendors during an event. It helps event organizers manage who has access to sensitive areas or information related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Select the relevant access level for each category (attendee, staff, and vendor).
3. Enter the email addresses of the individuals you want to grant access to (if required).
4. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **attendee_access** (`attendee access`, `Number`, **Optional**): Select this option to grant access to attendees. This field is not required.
* **staff_access** (`staff access`, `Select One`, **Optional**): Choose from the options to grant access to staff members: Admin, Super Admin, or User. This field is not required.
* **vendor_access** (`vendor access`, `Select One`, **Optional**): Select the access level for vendors: Restricted, General, or Public. This field is not required.
* **attendee_email** (`attendee email`, `Email`, **Optional**): Enter the email address of the attendee(s) you want to grant access to. This field is not required.
* **staff_email** (`staff email`, `Email`, **Optional**): Enter the email address of the staff member(s) you want to grant access to. This field is not required.
* **vendor_email** (`vendor email`, `Email`, **Optional**): Enter the email address of the vendor(s) you want to grant access to. This field is not required.

## Tips
- Be sure to select the correct access level for each category.
- Double-check the email addresses you enter are accurate and valid.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your event coordinator for assistance.
