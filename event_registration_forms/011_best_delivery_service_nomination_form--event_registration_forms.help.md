# Best Delivery Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information about a delivery service nomination for recognition and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information in the following sections:
	* **Your Information**: Provide your name and email address.
	* **Nominee Service Details**: Enter the name of the delivery service being nominated and its website link (if available).
	* **Service Quality Assessment**: Assess the service's speed, reliability, and overall package condition upon arrival.
	* **Customer Support Quality**: Rate the service's customer support quality from 1 to 5.
	* **Nomination Justification**: Describe what makes this service stand out from competitors.
	* **Specific Positive Experience**: Share a brief story of a time they exceeded your expectations.
	* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant details about the service.
	* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to send your nomination.
