# Best Smart Grid Project Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about smart grid projects that demonstrate innovation and excellence in the field. The purpose of this form is to help the nomination committee evaluate and select the best smart grid project for the year. Please ensure that you are submitting a new project each time you fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, title, organization, and contact email.
2. Provide details about the project, including its name, location, category, and completion year.
3. Rate the project's innovation level, scalability, environmental impact, economic viability, reliability improvement, and nomination justification.
4. Describe the technical innovations, problem solved, measurable impact, and award justification of the project.
5. Provide a professional reference contact and the date of nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (2, text, required): This is the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Your Title** (3, text, required): This is the title or position of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Your Organization** (4, text, required): This is the organization or entity associated with the project.
* **Your Contact Email** (5, email, required): This is the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Project Name** (7, text, required): This is the official name of the smart grid project.
* **Lead Organization** (8, text, required): This is the entity primarily responsible for the project.
* **Project Location** (9, text, required): This is the country and/or specific region of the project's location.
* **Project Category** (10, select_one, required): This selects the primary focus area of the project from the options provided.
* **Year of Completion or Launch** (11, number, required): This is the year the project became operational.
* **Innovation Level** (13, select_one, required): This rates the project's innovation level from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Scalability and Replicability** (14, select_one, required): This rates the project's scalability and replicability from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Environmental Impact and Sustainability** (15, select_one, required): This rates the project's environmental impact and sustainability from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Economic Viability** (16, select_one, required): This rates the project's economic viability from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Reliability and Resilience Improvement** (17, select_one, required): This rates the project's reliability and resilience improvement from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Describe the Technical Innovations** (19, text, required): This describes the key technical innovations of the project.
* **Describe the Problem Solved** (20, text, required): This describes the primary problem the project solved.
* **Measurable Impact** (21, text, required): This shares data points or results of the project's impact on grid efficiency or customer experience.
* **Award Justification** (22, text, required): This summarizes why the project should win the Best Smart Grid Project Award.
* **Professional Reference** (24, text, required): This is the name and email of someone familiar with the project details.
* **Date of Nomination** (25, date, required): This is the date the nomination was submitted.
