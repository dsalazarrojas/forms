# Equipment Efficiency Testing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about equipment efficiency testing. It helps track and manage equipment testing processes for organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Organization" field with your organization's name.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the equipment.
3. Select the type of equipment being tested (e.g., Machine, System, Hand Tool).
4. Enter the equipment model if applicable.
5. Choose the date and start time of the test.
6. Choose the end time of the test.
7. Enter the duration of the test.
8. Select the test results (e.g., Pass, Fail, In Progress).
9. Add any comments related to the test.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of equipment being tested (e.g., Machine, System, Hand Tool).
* **Equipment Model** (`Equipment Model`, text, optional): Enter the equipment model if applicable.
* **Test Date** (`Test Date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the test.
* **Test Start Time** (`Test Start Time`, time, optional): Choose the start time of the test.
* **Test End Time** (`Test End Time`, time, optional): Choose the end time of the test.
* **Test Duration** (`Test Duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the test.
* **Test Results** (`Test Results`, select_multiple, optional): Select the test results (e.g., Pass, Fail, In Progress).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any comments related to the test.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will ensure efficient processing of your equipment testing registration.
