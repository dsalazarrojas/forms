# Academic Transformation Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Transformation Retreat Registration Form is used to gather essential information from attendees for planning and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Full Name as it should appear on your badge.
2. Enter your Institution or Organization, which is your primary affiliation.
3. Provide your Email Address for retreat updates and materials.
4. Choose one or more tracks of interest from the available options: Leadership and Change, Digital Transformation, Inclusive Excellence, and Strategic Planning.
5. Select your Attendance Mode: Full Retreat (3 days), Single Day Pass, or Virtual Access.
6. If you require on-site accommodation, please select True.
7. If you have dietary requirements or allergies, please specify in the text field.
8. If you will need shuttle service from the airport, please select True.
9. Provide your Estimated Arrival Time or Flight Number for shuttle coordination.
10. Agree to the retreat's cancellation and refund policy by selecting I Agree or Other (if you have any other agreement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Attendee Name): As it should appear on your badge.
* **Institution or Organization**: Your primary affiliation.
* **Email Address**: For retreat updates and materials.
* **Select tracks of interest** (Leadership and Change, Digital Transformation, Inclusive Excellence, Strategic Planning): Choose one or more tracks that interest you.
* **Attendance Mode** (Full Retreat (3 days), Single Day Pass, Virtual Access): Select your participation level.
* **Accommodation Needed**: If you require on-site accommodation, please select True.
* **Room Preference** (Single Room, Shared Room, Not Applicable): Select your room preference (if applicable).
* **Dietary Requirements or Allergies**: Specify any dietary requirements or allergies.
* **Shuttle Service** (Will you need shuttle service from the airport?): If you require shuttle service, please select True.
* **Arrival Details**: Provide your Estimated Arrival Time or Flight Number for shuttle coordination.
* **Retreat Cancellation and Refund Policy**: Agree to the retreat's cancellation and refund policy by selecting I Agree or Other (if you have any other agreement).
