# Baby Wishes Collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect wishes, messages, and well-wishes for new parents and their baby.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, leaving no fields blank.
2. Select the type of message you want to send (e.g., General Well Wishes, Advice for Parents, etc.).
3. Write your heartfelt message in the "Your Wish or Message" field.
4. If you want to include a photo, select "Include a Photo" and add a description of the photo in the "Photo Description" field.
5. Decide whether you want to keep the message private or share it publicly.
6. If you're willing to help the family after the baby arrives, select your level of availability.
7. You can also recommend a book for the parents or child.
8. Finally, confirm the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`wellwisher_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person sharing the wishes.
* **Email Address** (`wellwisher_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Relationship to Parents** (`relationship_to_parents`, select_one, required): Select how you know the family (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Co-worker, Neighbor, or Other).
* **Baby Name** (`baby_name`, text, optional): If you know the baby's name, enter it here.
* **Baby Gender** (`baby_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the baby's gender (or leave it as Surprise for unknown).
* **Type of Wish** (`wish_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of message you want to send (e.g., General Well Wishes, Advice for Parents, etc.).
* **Your Wish or Message** (`wish_message`, text, required): Write your heartfelt message for the new family.
* **Advice for New Parents** (`advice_for_parents`, text, optional): Share your best parenting tip with the new parents.
* **Wish for Baby** (`wish_for_baby`, text, optional): Express your hopes and wishes for the baby's future.
* **Favorite Quote or Saying** (`favorite_quote`, text, optional): Include a meaningful quote or saying for the new family.
* **Favorite Memory with Parents** (`memory_with_parents`, text, optional): Share a special memory with the parents.
* **Prediction for Baby** (`prediction_for_baby`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on what the baby will be like in the future.
* **Book Recommendation** (`book_recommendation`, text, optional): Suggest a book for the baby or parents.
* **Include a Photo** (`include_photo`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to include a photo with your message.
* **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional): Describe the photo you're including.
* **Keep Message Private** (`keep_message_private`, select_one, required): Decide whether to keep the message private or share it publicly.
* **Contact for Questions** (`contact_for_questions`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the parents can contact you with questions.
* **Willing to Help** (`willing_to_help`, select_one, optional): Express your willingness to help the family after the baby arrives.
* **Type of Help You Can Offer** (`help_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of help you can provide (e.g., Meal delivery, Babysitting, etc.).
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Form** (submit, not visible): This is not a field, but a button to submit the form.
