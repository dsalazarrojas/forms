# Conference Speaker Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Speaker Request Form is used to request a speaker for a conference or event. This form provides essential information to the speaker and the event organizers, ensuring a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address as the person responsible for speaker arrangements.
2. Provide the name of your organization or event.
3. Enter the date of the event for which you are requesting the speaker.
4. Select the preferred topics for the session (multiple selection allowed).
5. Estimate the audience size for the event.
6. Indicate whether an honorarium is offered to the speaker.
7. Confirm whether travel expenses are covered.
8. Choose whether lodging is provided for the speaker.
9. Select the preferred session delivery format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
10. Specify the deadline for any materials or presentations the speaker needs to provide.
11. (Optional) Provide the name and phone number of the onsite contact person.
12. Add any additional instructions or details for the speaker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Speaker Request --**: This is the header for the form, providing a brief introduction to the purpose of the form.
* **Requester name**: Enter your name and title as the person responsible for speaker arrangements.
* **Requester email**: Enter your email address as the contact for scheduling and logistics.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization or event.
* **Event date**: Enter the date of the event for which you are requesting the speaker.
* **Preferred topics**: Select all relevant topics for the session (multiple selection allowed).
	+ Select "Artificial intelligence"
	+ Select "Leadership and management"
	+ Select "Sustainability"
	+ Select "Diversity and inclusion"
	+ Select "Product and design"
* **Estimated audience size**: Estimate the approximate number of attendees for the event.
* **Honorarium offered**: Enter the amount offered in local currency (optional).
* **Travel expenses covered**: Confirm whether travel expenses are covered.
* **Lodging provided**: Confirm whether lodging is provided for the speaker.
* **Session format**: Choose the preferred session delivery format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Materials deadline**: Enter the date by which slides or materials are due (optional).
* **Onsite contact person**: Provide the name and phone number of the onsite contact person (optional).
* **Additional instructions**: Add any other details or instructions for the speaker.

Note: Please be as detailed and realistic as possible in your answers to ensure that the speaker and event organizers can make informed decisions.
