# Holiday Open House RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your attendance and preferences for the holiday open house event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help the event organizers plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Select the name of the person or team you are attending with from the "Event Attendee" field.
3. Choose the correct number of guests attending from the "Guests Attending" field.
4. Fill in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields with the correct date and time of the event.
5. Enter your contact information in the "RSVP By" field, which is the date by when you need to RSVP.
6. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can write them in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, required): Enter your name as a guest.
* **Event Attendee** (`Event Attendee`, required): Select the name of the person or team you are attending with.
* **Guests Attending** (`Guests Attending`, required): Choose how many guests will be attending with you.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, required): Enter the date of the event in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, required): Enter the time of the event in 12-hour format (e.g., 2:00 PM).
* **RSVP By** (`RSVP By`, required): Enter the date by which you need to RSVP in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Message** (`Message`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Email** (`Email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, required): Select "True" if you are attending, "False" if not, and "Undecided" if you're not sure.
* **RSVP Message** (`RSVP Message`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Company** (`Company`, optional): If you're representing a company, enter the name here.
* **Address** (`Address`, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Host Name** (`Host Name`, optional): Enter the name of the host or organization.
* **Host Email** (`Host Email`, optional): Enter the email address of the host or organization.
* **Host Phone** (`Host Phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the host or organization.
* **Host Company** (`Host Company`, optional): If the host is representing a company, enter the name here.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Event Notes** (`Event Notes`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions about the event, enter them here.
* **Venue** (`Venue`, optional): If you're attending an event at a specific venue, enter the name here.
* **Location** (`Location`, optional): If you're attending an event at a specific location, enter the name here.
* **Event Date Time** (`Event Date Time`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions about the event date and time, enter them here.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, required): Click this button to complete your RSVP.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Check the "RSVP Status" field to ensure you're accurately representing your attendance status.
* Use the "RSVP Message" field to provide any additional comments or questions.
* If you're representing a company, ensure you've filled out the "Company" field accurately.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure everything is correct.
