# Audience Misconduct Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Audience Misconduct Incident Form is designed to collect information regarding incidents or misbehaviors exhibited by audience members that need immediate attention or response from event staff. This form ensures that staff members can report such incidents in a clear and concise manner, providing essential details for prompt action to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the **Report Information** section, which includes the **Staff Member Reporting** field. Enter your full name in this field to identify yourself as the staff member reporting the incident.
2.  In the **Staff Role** field, select the role you were playing at the time of the incident (e.g., Usher, Security, Manager).
3.  Enter the **Date of Incident** field with the date the incident occurred.
4.  Enter the **Time of Incident** field with the time the incident occurred.
5.  In the **Location in Venue** field, specify where the incident took place within the venue (e.g., Section A, Row 5, Lobby, Restroom).
6.  Select the **Type of Misconduct** from the dropdown menu that best describes the incident (e.g., Verbal Abuse, Physical Altercation, Intoxication, etc.).
7.  In the **Detailed Description of Incident** field, provide a clear and objective description of what happened, including specific actions and words used.
8.  If known, list the **Involved Audience Members** in the field provided.
9.  In the **Immediate Action Taken** field, specify the action taken by staff in response to the incident (e.g., Verbal warning, removal from venue).
10. If the police were not notified, select **False** in the **Police Notified** field.
11.  In the **Witnesses** field, list any staff members or other audience members who witnessed the incident.
12.  Finally, enter the **Name of Supervisor Notified** field with the name of the supervisor who was informed about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Staff Member Reporting** (`staff_member_reporting`, text, required): Enter your full name in this field to identify yourself as the staff member reporting the incident.
*   **Staff Role** (`staff_role`, text, required): Select the role you were playing at the time of the incident.
*   **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Location in Venue** (`location_in_venue`, text, required): Specify where the incident took place within the venue.
*   **Type of Misconduct** (`misconduct_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of misconduct that occurred from the dropdown menu.
*   **Detailed Description of Incident** (`detailed_description`, text, required): Provide a clear and objective description of what happened, including specific actions and words used.
*   **Involved Audience Members** (`involved_parties`, text, optional): If known, list the names or seat numbers of audience members involved in the incident.
*   **Immediate Action Taken** (`immediate_response`, text, optional): Specify the action taken by staff in response to the incident.
*   **Police Notified** (`police_notified`, select_one, required): If the police were notified, select **True**.
*   **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, text, optional): List any staff members or other audience members who witnessed the incident.
*   **Name of Supervisor Notified** (`supervisor_notified`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor who was informed about the incident.

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form accurately and in a clear, objective manner.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
*   Consult with a staff member or supervisor if you have any questions or concerns.
*   Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
