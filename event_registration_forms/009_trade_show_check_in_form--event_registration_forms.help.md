# Trade Show Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Check In Form is a tool used to collect information from attendees during a trade show event. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that event organizers have the necessary details to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for you and other attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the information requested in the "Trade Show Check In Form" field.
2. Provide your details in the "Check In Details" field.
3. Enter the emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Show Check In Form** (Trade Show Check In Form, Text, Optional): 
Please enter your name or the name of your company/organization.
* **Check In Details** (Check In Details, Text, Optional): 
Please provide any additional details you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, Text, Required): 
Please enter the name and contact information (phone number and email) of your emergency contact. This is the person the event organizers will contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need to correct any errors, please contact the event organizers directly.
