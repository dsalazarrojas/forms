# Game Hunting Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Hunting Permission Form is used to obtain permission from landowners for game hunting on their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Enter your information in the "Huntng Name" field if you are the hunter requesting the permission.
3. Enter the landowner's contact information in the "Landowner Name", "Landowner Phone", and "Landowner Signature" fields.
4. Fill out the "Start Date", "End Date", "Permission Start Date", and "Permission End Date" fields with the relevant dates for the hunt.
5. Enter your contact information in the "Hunter Signature" and "Hunter Phone" fields.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Once you have completed the form, review and sign it with the landowner's signature in the "Landowner Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Huntng Name** (`hunter_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the hunter requesting permission.
* **Landowner Name** (`landowner_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the landowner's name.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property where the hunt will take place.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the hunt.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the hunt.
* **Permission Start Date** (`permission_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the landowner's permission.
* **Permission End Date** (`permission_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the landowner's permission.
* **Landowner Signature** (`landowner_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign with the landowner's signature to confirm permission.
* **Hunter Signature** (`hunter_signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign with your signature to confirm you are the hunter.
* **Landowner Phone** (`landowner_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the landowner's phone number.
* **Hunter Phone** (`hunter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments related to the hunt.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
