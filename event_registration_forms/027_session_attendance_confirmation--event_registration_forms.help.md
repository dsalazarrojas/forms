# Session Attendance Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Session Attendance Confirmation form is used to record and confirm attendees' attendance to a session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the session you attended from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date you attended the session in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
3. Enter any additional comments or notes about the session in the "Notes" field.
4. Select "Confirmed" if you are confirmed, or "Not Confirmed" if you are not confirmed.
5. If you want to provide additional contact information, you can enter your email address in the "Email" field and/or your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter any additional information about your session attendance in the "Session Attendance Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Attended** (`Session 1`, `Session 2`): Select which session you attended from the dropdown menu.
* **Date Attended**: Enter the date you attended the session in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Confirmed** (True, False): Select "Confirmed" if you are confirmed, or "Not Confirmed" if you are not confirmed.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email if you choose to provide one.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number if you choose to provide one.
* **Session Attendance Confirmation**: Enter any additional information about your session attendance.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct session from the dropdown menu.
* Double-check the date of attendance to ensure it is accurate.
* Be concise in your notes and session attendance confirmation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your session administrator or supervisor for guidance.
