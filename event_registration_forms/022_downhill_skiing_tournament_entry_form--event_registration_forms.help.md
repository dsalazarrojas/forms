# Downhill Skiing Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Downhill Skiing Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect registration information for the Downhill Skiing Tournament. This form is intended to gather necessary details about the skier, event information, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input page with your general information.
2. On the Skier Details page, provide your age, height, and weight.
3. On the Contact Info page, enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. On the Event Info page, enter the event date and start time.
5. On the Skier Experience page, select your ski length and type.
6. On the Racing License page, enter your racing license number (if applicable).
7. On the Emergency Contact page, enter the name, phone, and email of your emergency contact.
8. On the Division and Age Group page, select your division and age group.
9. On the Note page, enter any additional notes or comments.
10. Review and submit the form once complete.
