# Speech Contest Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from speakers and their schools for a speech contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the speaker's name in the "Speaker Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the school or institution the speaker is from in the "School Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the email address of the school or institution in the "School Email" field.
4. Enter the phone number of the school or institution in the "School Phone" field.
5. Select the date of the contest in the "Contest Date" field.
6. Select the time of the contest in the "Contest Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Name**: Enter the name of the speaker who will be participating in the contest.
* **School Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person at the school who will be responsible for the speaker.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school or institution that the speaker represents.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school or institution that the speaker represents.
* **Contest Date**: Select the date of the contest, including the month and year.
* **Contest Time**: Select the time of the contest, including the hour and minute.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your registration is complete.
* If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
