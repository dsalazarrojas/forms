Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose

This guide explains the purpose and fields of the Summer Camp Enrollment Form. It is intended to be used by parents or guardians to enroll a child in the summer camp program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

* Enter your child's name in the **Child Name** field.
* Enter your child's birthday in the **Child Birthday** field. Format the date as mm/dd/yyyy.
* Enter your name in the **Parent(s) Name** field.
* Enter your phone number in the **Parent(s) Phone** field.
* Enter your email address in the **Parent(s) Email** field.
* If applicable, enter any medical record or relevant information in the **Medical Record** field.
* Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
* Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
* Enter the email address of the emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.
* Enter any medical conditions or allergies the child may have in the **Medical Condition** field.
* Enter any medical allergies the child may have in the **Medical Allergies** field.
* Indicate if you have medical insurance in the **Medical Insurance** field.
* Select the payment method (yes or no) in the **Payment Method** field.
* Enter any additional payment details in the **Payment Details** field.
* Enter the payment date in the **Payment Date** field. Format the date as mm/dd/yyyy.
* Enter the payment time in the **Payment Time** field. Format the time as hh:mm AM/PM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Enrollment Form Title**: This is the title of the form.
* **Child Name**: This field is where you enter your child's name.
* **Child Birthday**: This field is where you enter your child's birthday, formatted as mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Parent(s) Name**: This field is where you enter your name and any other parent's name.
* **Parent(s) Phone**: This field is where you enter your phone number.
* **Parent(s) Email**: This field is where you enter your email address.
* **Medical Record**: This field is for any medical information related to your child.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This field is for the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This field is for the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: This field is for the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition**: This field is for any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Medical Allergies**: This field is for any medical allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Insurance**: This field is to indicate if you have medical insurance.
* **Payment Method**: This field is to select if you have paid for the summer camp.
* **Payment Details**: This field is for any additional payment information.
* **Payment Date**: This field is for the payment date, formatted as mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Payment Time**: This field is for the payment time, formatted as hh:mm AM/PM.
