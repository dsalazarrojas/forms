# Retreat Deposit Commitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retreat Deposit Commitment Form is designed to register participants for an event and gather necessary information to facilitate the event's smooth execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the attendee's name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the attendee's date of birth.
3. Select the payment method for the event deposit.
4. Enter the event details, including date, start and end times, location, and organizer.
5. Confirm your commitment and event payment details.
