# Spa Guest Event RSVP Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Guest Event RSVP Registration Form is used to collect information from guests attending a spa event. This information is used to manage the guest list and ensure that each guest's event details are accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your RSVP response to the event: Select "Yes" if you are attending the event, "No" if you are not attending.
2. Enter the number of guests attending the event.
3. Enter your first name.
4. Enter your last name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. If you have any special requests, please provide them in the text field.
7. Enter the date of the event.
8. Enter the time of the event.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Confirm your event details: Select all that apply to confirm that you are attending the event and that you have reviewed the event details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event RSVP Form**: This is the main RSVP response field. Select "Yes" to confirm your attendance, or "No" to indicate that you will not be attending. 
- **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests that will be attending the event with you.
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests for the event, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please enter them here.
- **Date**: Enter the date of the event. 
- **Time**: Enter the time of the event. 
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Confirm Event Details**: Select all that apply to confirm your event details. This includes confirming your attendance, reviewing the event date and time, and reviewing the event details.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your correct email address so that you can be contacted with event updates. 
- Be accurate when entering the guest count, date, and time to ensure that we can plan for your arrival. 
- If you have any special requests, please be detailed in your description to ensure that we can accommodate your needs. 
- Confirming your event details is a critical step in the registration process, as it helps us ensure that you are aware of the event details and are ready to attend.
