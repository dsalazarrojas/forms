# cultural_society_costume_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural society costume tournament entry form is a general-purpose form designed to register users for various events hosted by the cultural society. This form is used to collect basic information from users to facilitate event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Tournament ID (if applicable).
2. Enter your User ID (if applicable).
3. Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
4. Enter a URL of a photo related to your entry.
5. Enter the name of the category your entry belongs to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tournament ID (1)**: Enter the ID of the tournament you are registering for. If you don't have a tournament ID, leave this field blank.
* **User ID (2)**: Enter your ID if you have been assigned one by the cultural society. If you don't have a user ID, leave this field blank.
* **Event Name (3)**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for. This field is required for the system to identify the event and process your entry.
* **Photo URL (4)**: Enter a URL of a photo related to your entry. This can be a photo of your costume, yourself, or anything else relevant to your entry.
* **Category Name (5)**: Enter the name of the category your entry belongs to. This helps the judges and other participants understand your entry during the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful event registration.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the cultural society staff for clarification.
