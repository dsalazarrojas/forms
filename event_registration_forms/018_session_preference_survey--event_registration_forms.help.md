# Session Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to collect user feedback and preferences for sessions they'd like to attend. Please fill it out to help us understand your needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once you've filled out all the required fields.
2. Fill out each field according to the guidelines below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name** (`session_preference_survey_page_1`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you'd like to be referred to in our communication.
* **Which session would you like to attend** (`session_preference_survey_page_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the sessions you're interested in attending. You can select multiple options.
* **Preferred date and time** (`session_preference_survey_page_3`, date, required: false): Select your preferred date and time for the session. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for date and `HH:MM` for time.
* **Preferred date and time** (`session_preference_survey_page_4`, time, required: false): This field is similar to the previous one, where you can select your preferred time for the session. Please use the format `HH:MM`.
* **What is your email** (`session_preference_survey_page_5`, email, required: false): Enter your email address as we may contact you with updates about the session.
* **What is your phone** (`session_preference_survey_page_6`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, even though they're not marked as "required" in this form.
* If you select multiple options in the "Which session would you like to attend" field, we'll take note of all of them.
