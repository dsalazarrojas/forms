# local-host-digital-training-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "local-host-digital-training-registration" form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in participating in local digital training sessions. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the session you are interested in by choosing from the available options in the "Session Choice" field.
2.  Indicate your experience level in the "Experience Levels" field by selecting all that apply.
3.  Provide your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" field. This field is optional.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This field is optional.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form** (Page 1)
*   **Form** (`Form`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the form. Please do not interact with this field; it is not required for registration.

### **Session Choice** (Page 2)
*   **Session Choice** (`Session Choice`, `select_one`, optional): Select all sessions you are interested in by choosing from the available options. If a session is not available, please do not select it.

### **Experience Levels** (Page 3)
*   **Experience Levels** (`Experience Levels`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your experience level in digital training by selecting all that apply. If you do not have any experience, please do not select anything.

### **Attendee Details** (Page 4)
*   **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about yourself as an attendee. This field is optional and helps us understand your needs better.

### **Email Address** (Page 5)
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to contact you regarding further information about the training sessions.

### **Phone Number** (Page 6)
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to contact you regarding further information about the training sessions.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you are not registering under a duplicate account.
*   Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
*   We will contact you regarding further information about the training sessions.
