# Webinar Seat Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to attend a webinar and reserve a seat for the event. It is essential to provide accurate personal details and seating preferences to efficiently manage the event.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Full Name in the `Full Name` field.
2. Enter your Email Address in the `email` field.
3. Provide your Phone Number in the `Phone` field.
4. Select a Date for the event you wish to attend in the `Date` field.
5. Choose a Time for the event in the `Time` field.
6. If you have any additional information about your attendance or preferences, add notes in the `Additional Notes` field.
7. Specify the number of seats you wish to reserve in the `Seat Number` field.
8. Confirm the Ticket Price you are willing to pay in the `Ticket Price` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide a valid phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date on which you wish to attend the webinar.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time slot for the event you prefer.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests for the event, use this field to provide details.
* **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, number, optional): Specify the number of seats you wish to reserve for yourself.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, number, required): Confirm the amount you are willing to pay as the ticket price.
