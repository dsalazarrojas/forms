# Seasonal Tour Package Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a seasonal tour package. Please fill out the form with your details to help us process your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as required) and provide as much information as possible to help us process your registration efficiently.
2. Review your details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Package ID** (`package_id`, number, required: false): Enter your package ID, if you have one. This is not a required field, but it can help us process your registration more efficiently.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the tour.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you with tour updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with tour updates and information.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date): Choose the date you plan to travel.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the destination.
* **Package ID** (`package_id`, number, required: false): Enter your package ID, if you have one. This is not a required field, but it can help us process your registration more efficiently.

Note: Please be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that your tour is processed correctly.
