# Online Hitting Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online hitting program registration form is designed to collect information from participants who want to enroll in a program related to hitting, such as a golf or baseball program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Information on the first page.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email on the first page.
3. Provide your Phone number on the first page.
4. Choose a Date of Birth on the first page.
5. Select your Program Start Date and Program End Date on the second page.
6. Choose a Payment Method on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (Text, not required): This is the first step in the registration process. Enter your basic information, including your name and contact details.

### First Name
* **First Name** (Text, not required): Enter your first name here.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (Text, not required): Enter your last name here.

### Email
* **Email** (Email, not required): Enter your email address here. Please note that we will not allow duplicate registrations using the same email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Text, not required): Enter your phone number here.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (Date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Program Start Date
* **Program Start Date** (Date, not required): Select the start date of the program you want to join.

### Program End Date
* **Program End Date** (Date, not required): Select the end date of the program you want to join.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (Select One, not required): Choose how you want to pay for the program:
  * **Cash**: Pay in cash
  * **Credit/Debit**: Pay using a credit or debit card
  * **Check**: Pay by check
  * **Online Payment**: Pay online through our website

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact our support team.
* Please review our terms and conditions before registering for the program.
