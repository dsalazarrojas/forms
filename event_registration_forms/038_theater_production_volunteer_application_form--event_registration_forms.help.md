# Theater Production Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Production Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect contact information and volunteer preferences from interested individuals who want to participate in our theater productions. This form helps us gather the necessary details to place them in the right position in our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form pages and ensure you have all the required information.
2.  Complete each page with the requested information.
3.  Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Volunteer Application Form**
*   **Volunteer Application Form**: This is the form name, which is not a field in the form. It's just the title of the form.
### 2. **Contact Information**
*   **Contact Name**: Please enter your name as it should be spelled and formatted as per your ID or passport.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
*   **Contact Address**: Please enter your address to help us send you important documents and information related to your volunteer position.
### 3. **Volunteer Skills**
*   **Volunteer Skills**: This field is meant to be used for skills you have that are related to the volunteer position. This can be anything from acting, directing, designing, or anything else that can be useful to the production.
### 4. **Availability**
*   **Availability**: Please state your availability to volunteer for the production. This includes the dates and days you are available to work and for how many days you can work.
### 5. **Volunteer Position Preference**
*   **Volunteer Position Preference**: Please select one of the options: Front of House, Stage Crew, or Production. Front of House refers to the front desk or welcome staff, Stage Crew refers to technical support and setting up stage, and Production refers to being involved in production planning and execution. This will help us place you in the right position based on your skills and availability.
### 6. **Volunteer Shifts**
*   **Volunteer Shifts**: Select a date and time you are willing to work.
### 7. **Volunteer Duration**
*   **Volunteer Duration**: Enter the number of days you are willing to volunteer for the production.
### 8. **Additional Information**
*   **Additional Information**: This field is optional, but if you have any additional comments or information that might be useful for us, feel free to write them here.
### 9. **Front of House**
*   **Front of House**: Please select this if you are comfortable with being at the front desk or welcome staff during the production.
### 10. **Stage Crew**
*   **Stage Crew**: Please select this if you are comfortable with technical support and setting up stage for the production.
### 11. **Production**
*   **Production**: Please select this if you are comfortable with being involved in production planning and execution.
### 12. **Additional Information**
*   **Additional Information**: Please add any other information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
*   Please double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
*   We appreciate your contribution to our theater production and look forward to reviewing your application.
