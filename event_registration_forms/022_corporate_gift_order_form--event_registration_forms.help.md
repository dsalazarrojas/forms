# corporate_gift_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate gift order form is designed to collect information for corporate gifts and other items. It is used to select a gift, specify recipient details, and provide additional notes or messages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select a gift from the list of options provided in the "Select a Gift" field.
2. In the "Recipient Name" field, enter the name of the person to whom the gift is to be given.
3. In the "Delivery Details" field, provide any relevant delivery information, such as the date and time of delivery.
4. If applicable, enter the recipient's title in the "Title" field.
5. Enter the recipient's department in the "Department" field.
6. In the "Message" field, enter any additional notes or messages you'd like to include with the gift.
7. Finally, add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a Gift (1)**: Choose the gift you wish to purchase.
* **Recipient Name (2)**: Enter the name of the person to whom the gift is to be given.
* **Delivery Details (3)**: Provide any necessary details for the delivery, such as date and time.
* **Title (4)**: Enter the recipient's title, if applicable.
* **Department (5)**: Enter the recipient's department.
* **Message (6)**: Enter any additional notes or messages you'd like to include with the gift.
* **Note (8)**: Add any additional notes, if necessary.
* **Submit (7)**: Click to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accurate and complete information is entered.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form is for corporate gift ordering only. If you're using this form for other purposes, please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals.
* Please do not use this form for any unauthorized or malicious purposes.
