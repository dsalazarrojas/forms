# Trade Delegation Welcome Party RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees and sponsors of the Trade Delegation Welcome Party event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are an authorized representative of your company or event.
2. If you are not sure if you are authorized, contact the event organizer for verification.
3. Fill in your details in the corresponding fields:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
	* Company: Enter your company name.
	* Job Title: Enter your job title.
	* Role: Enter your role.
	* Attending: Select 'yes' if you are attending the event.
	* Dietary Requirements: Select your dietary requirements (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, etc.).
	* Message: Enter any additional message or comments.
	* Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
	* Event Start Time: Enter the start time of the event.
	* Event Location: Enter the location of the event.
	* Event Name: Enter the name of the event.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number.
	* Is Registered: Select 'yes' if you are already registered for the event.
	* Is Sponsor: Select 'yes' if you are a sponsor.
	* Sponsor Company: Enter your company name (if you are a sponsor).
	* Sponsor Job Title: Enter your job title (if you are a sponsor).
	* Sponsor Role: Enter your role (if you are a sponsor).
	* Sponsor Message: Enter any additional message or comments (if you are a sponsor).
	* Sponsor Attends: Select 'yes' if your sponsor is attending the event.
	* Sponsor Dietary Requirements: Select your sponsor's dietary requirements (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, etc.).
	* Sponsor Message: Enter any additional message or comments (if your sponsor is not attending).
