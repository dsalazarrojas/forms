# Job Readiness Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Readiness Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from participants who want to attend the Job Readiness Workshop. This form will help us better understand their preferences and availability for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (Name, Email, and one of the available dates and time slots).
2. Choose a suitable date and time slot for the workshop.
3. Select the days you are available for the workshop.
4. Add any additional notes if you have any specific requests.
5. Click the submit button to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Readiness Workshop Registration Form
* Job Readiness Workshop Registration Form: This is the main form header, please fill in your name and any other information that is required.

### Name
* Name: This is your name that will be used to identify you.

### Email
* Email: This is your email address that will be used for communication related to the workshop.

### Phone
* Phone: Your phone number for any additional contact.

### Date Of Birth
* Date Of Birth: Your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY) that will help us identify you.

### Time Of Workshop
* Time Of Workshop: This is the start time of the workshop, not your personal time of birth.

### Job Readiness Workshop Date
* Job Readiness Workshop Date: This is the date of the workshop. Please select one of the available dates.

### Job Readiness Workshop Time
* Job Readiness Workshop Time: This is the start time of the workshop on the selected date.

### Which of the following dates works for you?
* Which of the following dates works for you? This is a dropdown menu where you can select one of the available dates for the workshop.

### Which of the following time slots works for you?
* Which of the following time slots works for you? This is a dropdown menu where you can select one of the available time slots for the workshop.

### Which of the following days?
* Which of the following days? Select the days you are available for the workshop.

### Notes
* Notes: This is an optional field where you can add any additional information or requests you have for the workshop.

### Job Readiness Workshop Location
* Job Readiness Workshop Location: This is the location of the workshop. Please note that this field is not required.
