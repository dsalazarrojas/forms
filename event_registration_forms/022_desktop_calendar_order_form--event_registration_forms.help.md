# Desktop Calendar Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to process their event registrations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the **Event Date** for which you are registering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use to communicate with us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event for which you are registering.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have already submitted this form, please do not submit it again.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
