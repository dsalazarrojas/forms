## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have provided all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2. Fill in your `First Name`, `Last Name`, and `Email` fields with your correct contact information.
3. If applicable, enter your `Phone Number` for easy contact.
4. Choose your `Role` from the options provided (Admin, Member, or Guest).
5. Optionally, provide a `Note` if you have any specific requirements or concerns.
6. Confirm your `Email` and `Phone Number` by selecting `Yes` or `No` from the options provided.
