# festival_experience_feedback_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who attended a festival. It's meant to gather information about their experience, which will help improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the event information**:
	* Enter the name of the festival you attended.
	* You can add any other comments or remarks about the event.
2. **Rate your experience**:
	* Choose your overall rating for the festival.
3. **Add any comments**:
	* Type in any other comments or suggestions about your experience.
4. **Provide contact details**:
	* Enter your email address (will not be used for any other purpose than to follow up with your feedback).
	* Add any other contact information you'd like to share.
5. **Select what you experienced**:
	* If you've seen or experienced any of the provided pages, mark the options that apply.
6. **Submit your feedback**:
	* Choose a submit option to indicate that you've completed the form and are ready to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the festival you attended.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.
* **How did you feel?** (`how_did_you_feel`, select_one, optional): Choose an option to describe your feelings about the festival.
* **Any other comments** (`feedback`, text, optional): Type in any other comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Add any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, optional): Enter the date of the festival.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, optional): Enter the time of the festival.
* **What pages did you see?** (`which_page`, select_multiple, optional): Mark the options that apply to the pages you saw or experienced.
* **Other Page** (`other_page`, select_multiple, optional): If you saw or experienced any other page, mark the options that apply.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, optional): Choose to submit your feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to help us improve future festivals.
* You can skip any section you don't want to fill out.
* Make sure to enter a valid email address so we can follow up with your feedback.
