# Drive In Halloween Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drive In Halloween Registration Form is a tool to register participants for a one-time event. The form collects the first name, last name, date, and time of the participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Fill in the date you plan to attend the event in the "Date" field (if applicable).
4. Fill in the time you plan to arrive at the event in the "Time" field. Please note that this field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1): This field collects your first name. Please fill this field with your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2): This field collects your last name. Please fill this field with your last name.
* **Date** (id: 3): This field is optional and is meant to capture the date you plan to attend the event. If you don't plan to attend on a specific date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time** (id: 4): This field is required and collects the time you plan to arrive at the event. Please fill this field with the correct time of arrival.
