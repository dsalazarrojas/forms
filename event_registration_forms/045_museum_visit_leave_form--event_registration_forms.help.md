# Museum Visit Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Museum Visit Leave Form is a standardized way for employees to request a museum visit on behalf of their company or organization. This form captures essential details about the employee's visit, including start and end dates, employee and manager information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Request A Museum Visit" field to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the start date of your museum visit.
3. Enter the end date of your museum visit.
4. Enter your employee ID.
5. Provide a brief reason for your visit.
6. Select your approval status (Active or Inactive).
7. Provide any additional comments about your visit.
8. Enter your employee email address.
9. Enter your employee phone number.
10. Enter your manager email address.
11. Enter your manager phone number.
12. Enter your manager ID.
13. Select your manager's approval status (Yes or No).
14. Select the department(s) you belong to (Yes or No).
15. Enter your department ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request A Museum Visit**: 
    - This field is a heading to begin filling out the form.
* **Start Date**: 
    - Enter the date you plan to visit the museum.
* **End Date**: 
    - Enter the date you plan to leave the museum.
* **Employee ID**: 
    - Enter your employee ID number.
* **Reason**: 
    - Provide a brief reason for your museum visit.
* **Approval Status**: 
    - Select "Active" if you are approved for the visit, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Comments**: 
    - Provide any additional comments about your visit.
* **Employee Email**: 
    - Enter your employee email address.
* **Employee Phone**: 
    - Enter your employee phone number.
* **Manager Email**: 
    - Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: 
    - Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager ID**: 
    - Enter your manager's ID number.
* **Manager Approval**: 
    - Select "Yes" if your manager approves your visit, "No" otherwise.
* **Department**: 
    - Select "Yes" if you belong to this department, "No" otherwise.
* **Department ID**: 
    - Enter your department ID number.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Ensure you have your manager's approval before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the museum administration.
