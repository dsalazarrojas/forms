# Community League Tournament Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community League Tournament Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential details from teams registering for a Community League Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team name in the "Team name" field (Field 2).
2. Enter the name and contact information of the coach or manager in the "Coach or manager name" field (Field 3).
3. Provide the team's contact email in the "Contact email" field (Field 4).
4. Select the division or age group of your team from the dropdown list in the "Division or age group" field (Field 5).
5. Enter the number of registered players in the "Roster size" field (Field 6).
6. Confirm if payment for the entry fee has been made in the "Entry fee payment received" field (Field 7).
7. If necessary, provide any special requests or notes in the "Special requests or notes" field (Field 8).
8. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field (Field 9).
9. Select "Agree" to confirm that your team representative has reviewed and agrees to the tournament rules in the "Agree to tournament rules" field (Field 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team name** (Field 2, text, required): Enter the official name of your team.
* **Coach or manager name** (Field 3, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your team's primary contact person.
* **Contact email** (Field 4, email, required): Enter the email address to be used for scheduling and updates.
* **Division or age group** (Field 5, select_one, required): Select the division or age group that your team belongs to.
* **Roster size** (Field 6, number, required): Enter the number of registered players for your team.
* **Entry fee payment received** (Field 7, select_one, required): Confirm if payment for the entry fee has been made.
* **Special requests or notes** (Field 8, text, optional): If necessary, provide any special requests or notes for your team.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (Field 9, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Agree to tournament rules** (Field 10, select_one, required): Select "Agree" to confirm that your team representative has reviewed and agrees to the tournament rules.

## Tips
* Double-check the team name and contact information to ensure accuracy.
* Verify the division or age group selection to ensure it matches your team's requirements.
* Confirm payment for the entry fee to avoid any issues with registration.
* Provide clear and concise special requests or notes if necessary.
* Ensure that the emergency contact person is aware of their role and responsibilities.
* Carefully review the tournament rules to understand your team's commitments and responsibilities.
