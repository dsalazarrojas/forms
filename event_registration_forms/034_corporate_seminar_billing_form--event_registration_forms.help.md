# Corporate Seminar Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for corporate seminars. Event organizers use this form to gather information about the participants, including payment details, event date, and participant status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the name of the event organizer in the **Event Organizers** field.
2. Provide the participant's details in the **Participant Details** field.
3. Enter the payment information in the **Payment** field.
4. Provide additional payment details in the **Payment Details** field.
5. Calculate the **Total** amount due for the participant's registration.
6. Enter the **Tax** applied to the total amount.
7. Select the current **Payment Status** of the participant (Paid, Pending, or Cancelled).
8. Choose the **Participant Status** of the participant (Registered, Waiting List, or No Show).
9. Enter the **Event Date** of the seminar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizers** (Text): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Participant Details** (Text): Enter the details of the participant, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Payment** (Text): Enter the payment information, such as amount paid and payment method.
* **Payment Details** (Text): Enter any additional payment details, such as payment references or notes.
* **Total** (Number): Calculate the total amount due for the participant's registration.
* **Tax** (Number): Enter the tax applied to the total amount.
* **Payment Status** (Select One): Select the current status of the participant's payment (Paid, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Participant Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the participant (Registered, Waiting List, or No Show).
* **Event Date** (Date): Enter the date of the seminar.
