# Weekend Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekend Trip Registration Form is a form designed to collect registration information for a weekend trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the main form on the "Weekend Trip Registration Form" page, which contains fields for participant details and preferences.
2. Once you have filled out the main form, click "Next" to move to the "Participant Details" page.
3. On this page, provide specific details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
4. Click "Submit" to move to the "Preferences" page.
5. On this page, specify your preferences, such as food allergies and any special requests.
6. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

* **Weekend Trip Registration Form** (`<name>: Weekend Trip Registration Form`, `type: text`, `required: false>`): This is the main form where you can provide your details and preferences.
* **Participant Details** (`<name>: participant_details`, `type: text`, `required: false>`): This page is meant to collect specific details about you, such as your name and contact information.
* **Preferences** (`<name>: preferences`, `type: text`, `required: false>`): This page is meant to collect your preferences, such as food allergies and any special requests.
