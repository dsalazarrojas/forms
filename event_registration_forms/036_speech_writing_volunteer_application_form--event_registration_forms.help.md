# Speech Writing Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers who want to contribute to speech writing projects. It helps us to determine their suitability and gather details that are essential for the volunteer role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Volunteer Application" section with a brief introduction about yourself.
2. In the "Volunteer Details" section, provide any additional information about your background and experience.
3. In the "Contact Info" section, provide your contact information, including your email address.
4. In the "Speech Preferences" section, specify your preferred types of speeches you would like to work on.
5. In the "Availability" section, indicate your available time slots for volunteering.
6. In the "Speech Writing Experience" section, describe your experience with speech writing, including the number of speeches you have written.
7. In the "Available Languages" section, select the languages you are proficient in writing in.
8. In the "Special Skills" section, list any special skills you have that are relevant to speech writing.
9. In the "Available Dates" section, select the dates you are available to volunteer.
10. In the "Volunteer Shifts" section, select the shifts you are available to work on.
11. In the "Volunteer Hours" section, indicate the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering per week.
12. In the "Volunteer Rate" section, specify the rate you charge for your speech writing services.
13. In the "Volunteer Start Date" and "Volunteer End Date" sections, indicate your available start and end dates for volunteering.
14. In the "Volunteer Reference" section, provide a reference from a previous employer or client.
15. In the "Volunteer Email" section, provide your email address for communication.
16. In the "Volunteer Additional Info" section, provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the volunteer role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Application** (1) (Text, Optional): 
  A brief introduction about yourself, including your background and experience.

* **Volunteer Details** (2) (Text, Optional):
  Provide any additional information about your background and experience.

* **Contact Info** (3) (Text, Optional): 
  Your contact information, including your email address.

* **Speech Preferences** (4) (Text, Optional): 
  Specify your preferred types of speeches you would like to work on.

* **Availability** (5) (Text, Optional): 
  Indicate your available time slots for volunteering.

* **Speech Writing Experience** (6) (Number, Optional): 
  Describe your experience with speech writing, including the number of speeches you have written.

* **Available Languages** (7) (Select Multiple, Required): 
  Select the languages you are proficient in writing in.

* **Special Skills** (8) (Text, Optional): 
  List any special skills you have that are relevant to speech writing.

* **Available Dates** (9) (Text, Optional): 
  Select the dates you are available to volunteer.

* **Volunteer Shifts** (10) (Select One, Optional): 
  Select the shifts you are available to work on.

* **Volunteer Hours** (11) (Number, Optional): 
  Indicate the number of hours you can dedicate to volunteering per week.

* **Volunteer Rate** (12) (Number, Optional): 
  Specify the rate you charge for your speech writing services.

* **Volunteer Start Date** (13) (Date, Optional): 
  Indicate your available start date for volunteering.

* **Volunteer End Date** (14) (Date, Optional): 
  Indicate your available end date for volunteering.

* **Volunteer Reference** (15) (Text, Optional): 
  Provide a reference from a previous employer or client.

* **Volunteer Email** (16) (Email, Optional): 
  Provide your email address for communication.

* **Volunteer Additional Info** (17) (Text, Optional): 
  Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the volunteer role.
