# Book Fair Author Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from authors who wish to participate in the Book Fair event. It will help the event organizers to gather the necessary details to plan and execute the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Author name** (legal or pen name) in the **Author name** field.
2. Enter your **Email address** for event coordination purposes.
3. Enter the **Book title** you wish to promote at the fair.
4. Choose your preferred **Booth size** (Table only, Table and chair, or Booth with signage).
5. If applicable, provide any **Travel needs** or **Additional requirements** for the event.
6. Indicate if you are available to give **Readings or panels** and specify the **Speaking availability** time.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether **Book sales** will occur during the event.
8. Finally, if there are any **Additional requirements** you'd like to mention, do so in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author name** (`author_name`, **text**, required): Enter your legal or pen name as it should be displayed on event promotions.
* **Email address** (`email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you with event details and updates.
* **Book title** (`book_title`, **text**, required): Enter the title of your book to be promoted at the event.
* **Preferred booth size** (`preferred_booth_size`, **select_one**, required): Choose the size of the booth you need: Table only, Table and chair, or Booth with signage.
* **Travel needs** (`travel_needs`, **text**, optional): If you have any special travel or accommodation requirements, mention them here.
* **Speaking availability** (`speaking_availability`, **text**, optional): If you are available to participate in readings or panels, specify the time you are available.
* **Will book sales occur** (`sales_will_occur`, **select_one**, required): Indicate if you will be selling books at the event (Yes or No).
* **Additional requirements** (`additional_requirements`, **text**, optional): If there are any other event needs you'd like to mention, do so here.
