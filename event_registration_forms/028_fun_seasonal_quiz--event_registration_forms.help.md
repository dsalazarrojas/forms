# Fun Seasonal Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fun Seasonal Quiz" form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users on their interest in participating in seasonal activities. The form will help us gauge user interest and preferences for upcoming seasonal events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the questions and select your answers for each question.
2. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form**: Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in seasonal activities, or "No" if you are not.
* **Question 1**: Please provide a brief answer to the first question.
* **Question 2**: Please provide a brief answer to the second question.
* **Question 3**: Please provide a brief answer to the third question.
* **Question 4**: Please provide a brief answer to the fourth question.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your responses.
