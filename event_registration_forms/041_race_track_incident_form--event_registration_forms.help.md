## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What happened?** (race_track_incident, text, Required): A brief description of the incident, including any relevant details about what occurred.
* **Time of incident** (time_of_incident, time, Required): The time the incident occurred.
* **Date of incident** (date_of_incident, date, Required): The date the incident occurred.
* **Location of incident** (location_of_incident, text, Required): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Involved participants** (involved_participants, text, Required): A list of participants involved in the incident.
* **Description of incident** (description_of_incident, text, Required): A detailed description of the incident.
* **Cause of incident** (cause_of_incident, select_one, Required): Whether the incident was caused by human error, equipment failure, or external factors.
* **Race track injuries** (race_track_injuries, select_multiple, Required): A list of any injuries sustained during the incident.
* **Race track damages** (race_track_damages, text, Required): A description of any damages sustained during the incident.
* **Race track violations** (race_track_violations, select_multiple, Required): A list of any violations or rule infractions that occurred during the incident.
* **Race track comments** (race_track_comments, text, Optional): Any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Event ID** (event_id, number, Required): A unique identifier for the event.
* **Race Track Event ID** (race_track_event_id, number, Required): A unique identifier for the race track event.
* **Race Track Name** (race_track_name, text, Required): The name of the race track.
* **Race Track Date** (race_track_date, date, Required): The date of the race track event.
* **Race Track Time** (race_track_time, time, Required): The time of the race track event.
* **Race Track Location** (race_track_location, text, Required): The location of the race track event.
* **Race Track Status** (race_track_status, select_one, Required): The status of the race track (Active or Inactive).
* **Race Track Employees Involved** (race_track_employees_involved, select_multiple, Required): A list of employees involved in the incident.
* **Race Track Equipment Status** (race_track_equipment_status, select_multiple, Required): A list of equipment involved in the incident (Active or Inactive).
* **Race Track Weather Conditions** (race_track_weather_conditions, text, Optional): Any weather conditions that may have contributed to the incident.
* **Race Track Temperature** (race_track_temperature, text, Optional): The temperature at the time of the incident.
* **Race Track Humidity** (race_track_humidity, text, Optional): The humidity at the time of the incident.
* **Race Track Wind Speed** (race_track_wind_speed, text, Optional): The wind speed at the time of the incident.
* **Race Track Comments** (race_track_comments, text, Optional): Any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete all required fields carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for guidance.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
