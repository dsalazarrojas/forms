# Financial Modeling Advisory Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants who want to attend the Financial Modeling Advisory Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as prompted. You can type in your information in the text boxes provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This field is used to collect basic event information, such as the name of the event.
* **participant information**: This field is used to collect information about the participant, including their name and any relevant details.
* **contact info**: This field is used to collect the participant's contact information, such as their email and phone number.
* **Workshop Details**: This field is used to collect any additional information related to the workshop, such as the participant's experience level or interests.
* **Payment Info**: This field is used to collect payment information, such as the payment method and amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your registration is successful.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
