# Tasting Experience Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants and their associated guests for a "Tasting Experience" event. It collects essential contact information and preferences for event planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate your participant type (Participant, Organizer, or Guest).
3. Choose your taster experience level (None, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
4. If applicable, enter the number of guests you are registering.
5. Repeat steps 1-4 for each guest you are registering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information

* **Name**: Enter your participant's name.
* **Email**: Enter your participant's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your participant's phone number.
* **Participant Type**: Indicate your participant type (Participant, Organizer, or Guest).
* **Taster Experience**: Choose your taster experience level (None, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Participant Count**: Enter the number of participants in your party.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share about your registration.

### Participant Guest Information (Repeat for each guest)

* **Name**: Enter each guest's name.
* **Phone**: Enter each guest's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter each guest's email address.
* **Participant Type**: Indicate each guest's participant type (Participant, Organizer, or Guest).
* **Taster Experience**: Choose each guest's taster experience level (None, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Participant Count**: Enter the number of guests in each party.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share about each registration.

Note: The fields for participant information repeat for each guest, allowing you to register multiple participants and their associated guests.
