# Tournament Food Vendor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and experience information from food vendors participating in a tournament. It's intended to gather insights on the quality of the food provided, the cost, and the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are a food vendor.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you had a food vendor experience at the tournament.
3. If you had a food vendor experience, select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to rate the quality of the food.
4. Indicate if food was provided for free or at a cost.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vendor provided additional services.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the overall experience was good or not.
7. Enter the name of the food vendor who provided the experience.
8. Enter the email address of the vendor (if applicable).
9. Enter the phone number of the vendor (if applicable).
10. Enter the address of the vendor (if applicable).
11. Select "Good" or "Poor" to rate the quality of the food.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the cost was acceptable.
13. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the services provided were useful.
14. Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
15. Enter any additional suggestions you may have for improving the experience.
16. Enter any comments you may have.
17. Enter any additional comments.
18. Enter any final comments.
