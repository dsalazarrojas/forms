# Leather Craft Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leather Craft Workshop Booking Form is used to collect participant's information for the Leather Craft Workshop event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a text field where you can type in your first name.
* Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a text field where you can type in your last name.
* Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is an email field where you can type in your email address.
* Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field. This is a text field where you can type in your phone number.
* Select your preferred **Date** for the workshop from the date picker or type it manually in the "Date" field.
* Choose the **Time** of the workshop that suits you best from the time picker or type it manually in the "Time" field.
* Select your preferred **Workshop Date** from the date picker or type it manually in the "Workshop Date" field.
* Choose the **Workshop Time** from the time picker or type it manually in the "Workshop Time" field.
* If you want to be listed with your participant's name, enter their name in the "Participants Name" field.
* Select your participant type from the dropdown menu:
	+ Leather Craft Beginner: For participants who are new to leather crafting.
	+ Leather Craft Intermediate: For participants with some experience in leather crafting.
	+ Leather Craft Advanced: For experienced participants in leather crafting.
* In the "Notes" field, you can add any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your participant's first name. 
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your participant's last name. 
* **Email (email)**: Enter your participant email. 
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your participant phone number. 
* **Date (date)**: Select the date for the workshop. 
* **Time (time)**: Choose the time for the workshop. 
* **Workshop Date (date)**: Select the date for the workshop. 
* **Workshop Time (time)**: Choose the time for the workshop. 
* **Participants Name (text)**: If you are participating with others, enter their names here. 
* **Participant Type (select_multiple)**: Select the level of expertise you have in leather crafting. 
	+ **Leather Craft Beginner**: You have no prior experience in leather crafting. 
	+ **Leather Craft Intermediate**: You have some experience in leather crafting. 
	+ **Leather Craft Advanced**: You have extensive experience in leather crafting. 
* **Notes (note)**: Enter any additional information about your participation in the workshop.
