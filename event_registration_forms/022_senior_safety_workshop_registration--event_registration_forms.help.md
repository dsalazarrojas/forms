# Senior Safety Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Senior Safety Workshop, a training event designed to educate seniors on safety procedures and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Indicate if you wish to opt-in to receive email updates or communications from the workshop organizers.
6. If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and select their relationship with you (e.g., Emergency Contact, Family Member, Friend, or Other).
7. Select the date and time you plan to attend the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email Opt-in** (Text, Optional): Check this box to receive email updates and communications from the workshop organizers.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person or family member.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g., Family Member, Friend, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship options:**
	+ **Emergency Contact**: Select this if the person is your emergency contact.
	+ **Family Member**: Select this if the person is a family member.
	+ **Friend**: Select this if the person is a friend.
	+ **Other**: Select this if the person is not listed above.
* **Workshop Date** (Date, Optional): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.
