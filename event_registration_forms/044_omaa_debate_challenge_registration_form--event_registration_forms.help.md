# omaa_debate_challenge_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for participants to register for a debate challenge, a school event that tests students' debate skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that each field is accurate and relevant.
2. Make sure you've reviewed the required fields (Participant Name, School Name, Participant Email, Participant School Phone) to avoid duplication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the debate challenge event. This will help us identify the event on our database.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Please type the name of your school in the field.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name in this field.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your school address.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, required/optional): Enter your school city name.
* **School State** (`school_state`, text, required/optional): Enter your school state name.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, number, required/optional): Enter your school zip code.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your school phone number.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, select_one, required/optional): If you're a participant, choose 'Yes', if you're a school, choose 'No'.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your team name here.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you have a team participating in the debate challenge by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Team Coaches** (`team_coaches`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you have team coaches by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Team Coach Phone** (`team_coach_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a team coach, enter their phone number here.
* **Team Coach Email** (`team_coach_email`, email, required/optional): If you have a team coach, enter their email here.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your role in the team from the options provided.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event you're participating in from the options provided.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the event.
* **Participant Grade** (`participant_grade`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you're a participant, indicate if you've passed Grade 9 or not.
* **Participant Grade 9** (`participant_grade_9`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you're a participant, indicate if you've passed Grade 9 or not.
* **Participant Subjects** (`participant_subjects`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate which subjects you have expertise in.
* **Team Subjects** (`team_subjects`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have a team, indicate which subjects your team has expertise in.
* **Participant School Class** (`participant_school_class`, select_one, required/optional): If you're a participant, choose 'Yes' if you're in a school class.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your native language.

## Tips
* Please double-check that all fields are accurate and correct before submitting the form.
* Make sure to save your progress in case you need to refer back to it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
