# Student Organization Event Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for students to submit proposals for events sponsored by their student organizations. This form helps to gather essential information about the event, including the title, date and time, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Event Title**: Enter a brief title of the event that clearly describes what it's about.
2. **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the event in a standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy hh:mm AM/PM).
3. **Location**: Enter the physical address of the event venue.
4. **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event, including its purpose and objectives.
5. **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
6. **Student Organization**: Enter the name of the student organization sponsoring the event.
7. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the event.
8. **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the event.
9. **Event Venue**: Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
10. **Event Venue Address**: Enter the physical address of the event venue.
11. **Event Venue Zip**: Enter the zip code of the event venue.
12. **Event Description**: Provide a detailed description of the event, including its purpose, objectives, and any other relevant information.
13. **Event Sponsor**: Enter the name of the sponsor or organization supporting the event.
14. **Event Sponsor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sponsor or organization supporting the event.
15. **Event Sponsor Email**: Enter the email address of the sponsor or organization supporting the event.
16. **Event Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor or organization representative.
17. **Event Sponsor Title**: Enter the title of the sponsor or organization representative.
18. **Event Sponsor Organization**: Enter the name of the sponsor or organization.
19. **Student Name**: Enter your name as the student submitting the proposal.
20. **Student Email**: Enter your email address as the student submitting the proposal.
