# field_trip_consent_and_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from students and parents for a field trip, ensuring a safe and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the field trip consent questions by selecting "Yes" or "No" as prompted.
2. Fill out the student information section, providing accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Provide emergency contact information in case of an emergency.
4. Report any medical information and special needs that may impact the student's participation in the field trip.
5. Disclose any allergies or medications that may be relevant to the student's health.
6. Enter any additional information that may be necessary for the field trip.
7. Sign and date the form as a parent or staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Field Trip Consent** (`field_trip_consent`, Text, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if the student is eligible for the field trip and agrees to participate.

### **Student Information** (`student_information`, Select One, Required)
Indicate whether you are the student's parent or guardian.

### **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if you are giving permission for the student to participate in the field trip. If you are not the parent/guardian, please sign below.

### **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional)
Enter the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and address in case of an emergency.

### **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if the student has any medical information that may impact their participation in the field trip.

### **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if the student has any special needs that may require accommodations during the field trip.

### **Student Conduct** (`student_conduct`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if you are aware of any behavioral issues that may impact the student's participation in the field trip.

### **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, Select One, Optional)
Please select "Yes" to grant permission for medical treatment in case of an emergency.

### **Allergies** (`allergies`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if the student has any allergies that may impact their participation in the field trip.

### **Medications** (`medications`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Please select "Yes" if the student takes any medications that may impact their participation in the field trip.

### **Student Information Other** (`student_information_other`, Text, Optional)
Enter any additional student information that may be necessary for the field trip.

### **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, Text, Optional)
Enter any additional information that may be necessary for the field trip.

### **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, Text, Optional)
The parent/guardian signs below to confirm their understanding and consent for the student's participation in the field trip.

### **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, Text, Optional)
The student signs below to confirm their understanding and commitment to participate in the field trip.

### **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, Text, Optional)
The staff member responsible for the field trip signs below to confirm their understanding and commitment to ensuring the student's safety and well-being.
