# robotics_camp_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Robotics Camp. Please fill out the required fields to ensure that you're signed up for the correct session and receive important information about the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name, last name, and contact information (email and phone number).
2. Select the desired session you'd like to attend.
3. Choose the duration of your stay at the camp.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your stay.
5. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address for contact purposes. (Required)
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any further questions or updates. (Required)
* **Address**: Enter your residential address for logistical purposes. (Optional)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes. (Optional)
* **Camp Name**: Enter the name of the robotics camp you'd like to attend. (Optional)
* **Parent Email**: Enter your parent's email address if you're under 18 years old. (Optional)
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your parent's phone number for emergency contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Session**: Select the desired session for your robotics camp stay. (Required)
* **Dates**: Select the dates you'd like to attend the camp. (Optional)
* **Time**: Select the time slot for your camp stay. (Optional)
* **Duration**: Choose the duration of your stay at the camp. (Optional)
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your stay. (Optional)
* **Submit**: Once you've completed all the fields, click the submit button to finalize your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any difficulties or questions while completing the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please review your registration carefully before submitting to ensure you've entered all required fields correctly.
