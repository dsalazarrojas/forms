# Company Retreat Location Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their preferences for the company retreat location. The goal is to gather input from all employees to ensure that everyone's needs and interests are considered when selecting the retreat location.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, provide your honest and genuine input.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the designated retreat committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (employee_full_name): Your full name as it appears on your company ID or employee record.
* **Email Address** (employee_email): Your company email address.
* **Department** (department): The department you belong to for work purposes.
* **Office Location** (office_location): Where you work, e.g., office building, floor, or room number.
* **Proposed Retreat Dates** (retreat_dates): Optional field for suggesting specific dates for the retreat. If left blank, the committee will consider the general availability of the team.
* **Location Options** (-- Location Options --): This section allows you to browse and select your preferred locations for the retreat. Browse the options carefully and choose the one that suits your needs best.
* **First Choice Location** (first_choice): Choose your top preference for the retreat location from the listed options.
* **Why Did You Choose This Location?** (first_choice_reason): Provide a brief explanation for why you chose your first choice location.
* **Second Choice Location** (second_choice): Select your backup preference for the retreat location, in case your first choice is not available.
* **Third Choice Location** (third_choice): Choose your third preference for the retreat location, in case both first and second choices are not available.
* **Location Preferences** (-- Preferences --): This section allows you to browse and select your preferred locations for the retreat. Browse the options carefully and choose the one that suits your needs best.
* **Accommodation Preference** (accommodation_preference): Choose your accommodation preference from the listed options: Single room, Shared room (2 people), Shared room (4 people), No accommodation needed, or No preference.
* **Dietary Requirements** (dietary_requirements): Check any dietary restrictions or requirements you have, e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, etc.
* **Dietary Details** (dietary_details): Provide more details about your dietary requirements, e.g., if you have a specific allergy.
* **Which Activities Interest You?** (activity_interest): Select all activities that interest you, e.g., Team building exercises, Workshops and training, Outdoor adventures, etc.
* **Which Activities Are You Not Interested In?** (activity_not_interested): Select all activities that do not interest you, e.g., Team building exercises, Workshops and training, Outdoor adventures, etc.
* **Travel Distance** (travel_distance): Choose how far you are willing to travel for the retreat.
* **Preferred Transportation** (transportation_preference): Choose how you would prefer to travel to the retreat, e.g., Company bus, Personal vehicle, Carpool, etc.
* **Accessibility Needs** (accessibility_needs): If you have any specific accessibility needs, please provide details.
* **Attendance Likelihood** (attendance_likelihood): Choose how likely you are to attend the retreat.
* **Attendence Barriers** (attendance_barriers): If not attending, provide details on the barriers preventing you from attending.
* **Plus One** (plus_one): If you are planning to bring a guest, choose 'True', otherwise select 'False', or 'Maybe', or 'Not applicable'.
* **Budget Preference** (-- Budget Considerations --): This section allows you to set your budget priority for the retreat, e.g., Lowest cost option, Moderate cost, etc.
* **Will You Contribute** (willing_to_contribute): If you are willing to contribute to room upgrades, choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Location Suggestions** (-- Suggestions --): If you have any location suggestions or ideas, please provide them in this section.
* **Activity Suggestions** (-- Activities --): If you have any activity ideas, please provide them in this section.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
* **Vote Date** (vote_date): This will automatically be filled in with today's date. You can use this field to keep track of your submission date.
