# Stem Summer Camp Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Stem Summer Camp Enrollment Form" is a required form for parents or guardians to register their child(ren) for the summer camp program. This form collects necessary information about the camper, including contact and address details, medical information, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our staff for assistance.
3. Save your progress as you complete the form and come back later if needed.
4. Make sure to review each field's label for any specific instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, required): Enter the name and any other identifying information of the camper(s) registering for the summer camp.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required): Provide the contact details of the person submitting the form and the campers' parent/guardian's email and phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter the address where the camper(s) will be staying during the camp.
* **Medical Information** (Medical Information, text, required): List any known medical conditions or allergies of the camper(s) and provide any relevant medical information.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required): Identify a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Guardian Information** (Guardian Information, text, required): Provide the contact and address details of the camper's parent or guardian.
* **Camper Information** (Camper Information, text, required): Enter any additional details about the camper(s) registering for the summer camp.
* **Goals and Objectives** (Goals and Objectives, text, required): Describe the camper's personal goals and objectives for attending the summer camp.
* **Session Dates** (Session Dates, select_one, required): Select the session(s) the camper(s) will attend.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method for the camper's registration.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required): Agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Billing Information** (Billing Information, text, required): Provide payment and billing details.
* **Special Dietary Needs** (Special Dietary Needs, select_multiple, optional): Select any special dietary needs of the camper(s).
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_multiple, optional): Identify any medical conditions that may affect the camper's participation in the summer camp.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (Medical Conditions 2, select_multiple, optional): List any additional medical conditions that may affect the camper's participation in the summer camp.
* **Camp Location** (Camp Location, select_one, optional): Select the camp location where the camper(s) will be staying during the camp.
* **Session Dates 2** (Session Dates 2, select_one, optional): Select any additional session dates the camper(s) will attend.
* **Camper Fee** (Camper Fee, number, optional): Enter any additional fees for the camper(s).
* **Discount** (Discount, number, optional): Enter any applicable discounts.
* **Payment Terms 2** (Payment Terms 2, text, optional): Agree to any additional payment terms and conditions.
* **Medical Info 2** (Medical Info 2, text, optional): Provide any additional medical information.
* **Camper Medical Info** (Camper Medical Info, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information.
* **Camper Medical** (Camper Medical, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information for the camper(s).
* **Camper Fee 2** (Camper Fee 2, number, optional): Enter any additional fees for the camper(s).
* **Payment Method 2** (Payment Method 2, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional payment methods for the camper(s).
