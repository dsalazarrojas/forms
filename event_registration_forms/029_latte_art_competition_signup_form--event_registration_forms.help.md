# Latte Art Competition Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a latte art competition. It collects essential information about the participant, event details, and terms of agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** (if applicable) to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3. List your **Participant Skills**, such as latte art or latte painting, to help us evaluate your qualifications.
4. Upload your **Participant Image** for identification purposes.
5. If you're part of a team, fill in the **Team Name**, **Team Leader Email**, **Team Leader Phone**, and **Team Image** (if applicable).
6. Provide the **Event Name**, **Event Date**, and **Event Location** to let us know about your event.
7. Enter the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** to ensure we're aware of the event schedule.
8. Read and agree to the **Entry Rules**, including any specific rules or guidelines for the competition.
9. Pay the **Entry Fee** to secure your spot in the competition.
10. Select your preferred **Payment Method** (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
11. Check the **Participant Agreement** and **Event Agreement** boxes to confirm you've read and understand the terms.
12. Optionally, choose **Participant Information** or **Other** to provide additional context.
13. Select the **Entry Status** as **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected** to update your entry status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email**: Provide a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Participant Skills**: List any relevant skills you have, such as latte art or latte painting.
* **Participant Image**: Upload a clear and recognizable image of yourself.
* **Team Name**: If you're part of a team, enter your team's name.
* **Team Leader Email**: Enter your team leader's email address.
* **Team Leader Phone**: Enter your team leader's phone number (if applicable).
* **Team Image**: Upload a clear and recognizable image of your team (if applicable).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of your event.
* **Entry Rules**: Read and agree to the rules governing the competition.
* **Entry Fee**: Pay the registration fee to secure your spot.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Participant Agreement**: Check the box to confirm you've read and agree to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Terms**: Check the box to confirm you've read and agree to the terms.
* **Event Agreement**: Check the box to confirm you've read and agree to the event agreement.
* **Participant Information**: Optionally provide additional context about yourself.
* **Entry Status**: Update your entry status as **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure you've read and agreed to all terms and conditions.
- Pay the entry fee to secure your spot in the competition.
- Upload clear and recognizable images as required.
