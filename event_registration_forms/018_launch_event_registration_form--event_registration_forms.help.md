# Launch Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event registration details for a launch event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Choose the correct event type from the dropdown menu.
4. If you are bringing attendees, list their names, emails, and phone numbers in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (`text`, required): Enter your name and any additional details about your registration.
* **Launch Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the launch event.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the launch event.
* **Venue** (`text`, optional): Enter the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of event from the dropdown menu (Conference, Trade Show, or Meetup).
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Attendee Names** (`text`, optional): List the names of any attendees you are bringing with you.
* **Attendee Emails** (`text`, optional): List the email addresses of any attendees you are bringing with you.
* **Attendee Phones** (`text`, optional): List the phone numbers of any attendees you are bringing with you.
* **Comments** (`note`, optional): This is for any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the tool that will be used for the event (ChatJimmy or Other).
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): This is for any additional notes about your registration.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, feel free to ask.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.
