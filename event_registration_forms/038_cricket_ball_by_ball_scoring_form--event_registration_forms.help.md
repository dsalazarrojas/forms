# Cricket Ball By Ball Scoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a detailed record of the events in a cricket match, with each field inputting relevant information about the game. This includes details about the match, the batsmen, bowling, and other essential statistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Introduction section to provide a brief summary of the match.
2. Enter the Game Details in the Game Details section.
3. Provide information about the batsman in the Batsman Info section.
4. Record the Overs and Runs data in the Overs and Runs section.
5. Input the Balls and Wickets details in the Balls and Wickets section.
6. Describe the Scoring and Analysis in the Scoring and Analysis section.
7. Add any Additional Info in the Additional Info section.
8. Enter information about the Team Details in the Team Details section.
9. Provide information about the Player Info in the Player Info section.
10. Record the Player Performance in the Player Performance section.
11. Enter the Team Performance in the Team Performance section.
12. Summarize the Game in the Game Summary section.
13. Describe the Wickets Details in the Wickets Details section.
14. Input the Run Rate, Over Rate, and Economy Rate in the Run Rate, Over Rate, and Economy Rate sections.
15. Input the Average Run Rate in the Average Run Rate section.
16. Finally, enter the Final Score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is a brief summary of the match, providing an overview of what happened.
* **Game Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about the game, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Batsman Info** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about the batsman, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Overs and Runs** (`4`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of overs and runs scored by the batsman.
* **Balls and Wickets** (`5`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of balls and wickets taken by the bowler.
* **Scoring and Analysis** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the scoring and analysis of the game, including any notable moments or events.
* **Additional Info** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the game that doesn't fit in the other fields.
* **Team Details** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about the team, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Player Info** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Enter information about the player, including their name and any other relevant details.
* **Player Performance** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the player's performance in the game, including their scores and any notable moments.
* **Team Performance** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the team's performance in the game, including their scores and any notable moments.
* **Game Summary** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Summarize the game in a few sentences.
* **Wickets Details** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the wickets taken during the game.
* **Run Rate** (`14`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the run rate of the game.
* **Over Rate** (`15`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the over rate of the game.
* **Economy Rate** (`16`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the economy rate of the game.
* **Average Run Rate** (`17`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the average run rate of the game.
* **Final Score** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the final score of the game.
