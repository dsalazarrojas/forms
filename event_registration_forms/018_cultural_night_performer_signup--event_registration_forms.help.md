# Cultural Night Performer Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from performers and their contacts for the Cultural Night event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the Performer Information section.
2. Provide a brief description of your performance, including the type and name of your performance.
3. Choose the school or venue where your performance will take place.
4. Enter the contact information for your school or venue organizer.
5. Select the event date and time.
6. Add any additional notes if necessary.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a performer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Performance Name** (`performance_name`, text, required): Describe your performance briefly.
* **Performance Type** (`performance_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of performance (Dance, Music, Theater).
* **Performance Details** (`performance_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your performance.
* **School or Venue** (`school_venue`, select_multiple, required): Choose the school or venue where your performance will take place.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the contact person.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact School** (`contact_school`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or venue.
* **Contact School Type** (`contact_school_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of school or venue (High School, Middle School, Community Center).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **School Organizer** (`school_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or venue organizer.
* **School Organizer Email** (`school_organizer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the school or venue organizer.
* **School Organizer Phone** (`school_organizer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the school or venue organizer.
* **School Organizer School** (`school_organizer_school`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or venue.
* **School Organizer School Type** (`school_organizer_school_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of school or venue (High School, Community Center).
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue.
* **Venue Type** (`venue_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type of venue (Theater, School, Community Center).
* **Venue Capacity** (`venue_capacity`, text, optional): Enter the capacity of the venue.
* **Performance Length** (`performance_length`, text, optional): Enter the length of the performance.
