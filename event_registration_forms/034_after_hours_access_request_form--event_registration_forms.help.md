# After Hours Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The After Hours Access Request Form is a form used for requesting access to the facility outside of regular working hours. This form is required for all requests for after-hours access, ensuring that all relevant information is provided and that the request is properly evaluated and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requester's information, including name, email, phone number, and department or affiliation.
2. Specify the requested access date, start time, and end time.
3. Choose the facility or building you need access to and select specific rooms or areas within it.
4. Explain why you need after-hours access (emergency, urgent project deadline, etc.).
5. Provide details on any potential risks associated with your access and explain how you plan to mitigate them.
6. Specify what equipment or materials you will bring into or remove from the facility.
7. Confirm that your supervisor's approval is required for this request (if applicable).
8. If your supervisor's approval is required, provide their name and email address.
9. Agree to follow all facility rules and safety protocols during your access period.
10. Acknowledge your liability for any damages or incidents during your access.
11. Sign the form to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Employee ID or Student ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): Your employee or student identification number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you can receive access confirmation and communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Your phone number for access coordination.
* **Department or Affiliation** (`department_affiliation`, `text`, required): Your department, faculty, or organizational affiliation.
* **Access Request Details** (`access_request_details_section`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about your access request.
* **Requested Access Date** (`requested_access_date`, `date`, required): The date when you need after-hours access.
* **Access Start Time** (`access_start_time`, `time`, required): The time you need access to begin.
* **Access End Time** (`access_end_time`, `time`, required): The time when you will finish and return access.
* **Facility or Building Requested** (`facility_building_requested`, `select_one`, required): Select the facility or building you need access to.
* **Specific Rooms or Areas** (`specific_rooms_areas`, `text`, required): Specify which rooms or areas within the facility you need access to.
* **Reason for Access Request** (`reason_for_access_request`, `note`, optional): Explain why you need access outside regular hours (e.g., emergency, urgent project deadline).
* **Reason for After-Hours Access** (`reason_for_after_hours_access`, `select_one`, required): Choose the reason for your after-hours access.
* **Detailed Purpose of Access** (`detailed_access_purpose`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed explanation of why you need after-hours access.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, `number`, required): How many people will be accessing the facility.
* **Safety and Security Considerations** (`safety_security_section`, `note`, optional): Provide any safety and security concerns related to your access.
* **Safety Officer Notification** (`safety_officer_notification`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether your activity requires notification of the safety officer.
* **Security Assessment Required** (`security_assessment_required`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether your activity requires a security assessment.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required): Provide contact information for emergency contacts during your access period.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether there are any potential risks associated with your after-hours access.
* **Details of Potential Risks** (`risk_details`, `text`, optional): If yes, describe the potential risks and how you plan to mitigate them.
* **Equipment to Bring In** (`equipment_bringing`, `text`, optional): Specify what equipment or materials you will bring into the facility.
* **Equipment to Remove** (`equipment_removing`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you will be removing any equipment or materials from the facility.
* **Details of Equipment to Remove** (`equipment_details`, `text`, optional): If yes, describe the equipment or materials to be removed.
* **Supervisor Approval Required** (`supervisor_approval_required`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether your supervisor's approval is required for this request.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, optional): If your supervisor's approval is required, enter their name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, optional): If your supervisor's approval is required, enter their email address.
* **Authorization Agreement** (`authorization_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to follow all facility rules and safety protocols during your access period.
* **Liability Acknowledgment** (`liability_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that you are responsible for any damages or incidents during your after-hours access.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign to submit your access request.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy.
- Attach any supporting documentation (e.g., supervisor's approval, risk assessment report) if required.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the facility management for assistance.
