# Winter Bazaar Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Bazaar Vendor Registration Form is a crucial document that collects essential information from vendors participating in the Winter Bazaar event. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you submit your registration accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Vendor Name** in the provided text field. This is a required field.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the email field. Although not required, this field is useful for communication purposes.
3.  Enter your **Phone** number in the text field. This is a required field for contact purposes.
4.  Select your **Product Category** from the available options. You can choose one or multiple categories from the provided list.
5.  Provide a brief **Description** of your products or services in the text area. This field is required.
6.  Choose your **Location** from the dropdown options. You can select from Main Street, Side Street, Parking Lot, or Other.
7.  Select your **Vendor Type** from the dropdown options. You can choose Individual or Group.
8.  Choose your **Contact Person** from the available options. You can select from Vendor Manager, Sales Manager, Marketing Manager, or Other.
9.  Enter any additional information in the text field titled **Field 9**. Note that this field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter your business or organization's name as it should be displayed during the Winter Bazaar.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, optional): A field for your official email address.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
  - **Product Category** (`category`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more categories related to your products or services:

  - **Description** (`description`, text, required): Describe your products or services in a few sentences. This will help us understand your offerings better.
  - **Location** (`location`, select one, optional): Select your location from Main Street, Side Street, Parking Lot, or Other.
  - **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select one, optional): Choose between Individual or Group.
  - **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select one, optional): Select your main point of contact from Vendor Manager, Sales Manager, Marketing Manager, or Other.
  - **Field 9** (`field_9`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your products or services.
