# Simple Seminar Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals interested in attending or registering for a seminar. It includes fields for general contact details such as first and last names, email address, phone number, and a message. Additionally, it includes fields specific to seminar events, such as event name, date, time, and venue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the designated field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the designated field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the format of `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXXX`.
5. Enter a brief **Message** in the text area provided.
6. Provide the **Event Name** of the seminar you are interested in.
7. Enter the **Event Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
8. Enter the **Event Time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` or `HH:MM 24-hour format`.
9. Enter the **Event Venue** in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` or `XXX XXX XXXX`.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information you would like to provide about your seminar registration.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the seminar you are interested in registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the seminar in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the seminar in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` or `HH:MM 24-hour format`.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the seminar.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number in the correct formats.
* If you are registering for a seminar, please ensure that the event name, date, and time are accurate.
* If you have any additional information about your seminar registration, please enter it in the message field.
