# Educational Expo Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for sponsors of the Educational Expo event to provide their information for communication and partnership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your sponsor name and title.
2. Enter your email address and phone number.
3. Share your website link (if applicable).
4. Fill in your school/institution information.
5. Enter the event name, date, time, and location.
6. Provide details about your team, including team name, email, phone number, and website (if applicable).
7. Specify whether you are able to accept payment terms.
8. Leave any additional comments or messages in the "Comments" field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Information** (Sponsor Name): This is the name of the individual or organization representing the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title** (Select One): Choose the title of the sponsor from the options provided.
* **Sponsor Email**: Enter the email address of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Website**: Enter the website of the sponsor (if applicable).
* **School Information**: Provide a brief description of the school or institution associated with the sponsor.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or institution.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school or institution.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school or institution.
* **School Website**: Enter the website of the school or institution (if applicable).
* **Event Information**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Team Information**: Provide a brief description of the team associated with the event.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team.
* **Team Email**: Enter the email address of the team.
* **Team Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team.
* **Team Website**: Enter the website of the team (if applicable).
* **Team Description**: Enter a brief description of the team.
* **Payment Terms** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you are able to accept payment terms or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or messages for the event organizers.
