# Marketing Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users registering for the Marketing Conference. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Select your job title and attendee type correctly.
4. Select your registration type correctly.
5. Provide any special requests if required.
6. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is the title field where you can enter your name as it appears on the conference schedule.
* **Attendee Information** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This field is for your attendee information, please provide a brief description about you and your team.
* **Name** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your full name, please enter it as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your official email address for communication purposes, please make sure it is correct.
* **Contact Number** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your contact number for any follow-up questions or communication.
* **Company** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your job title or position at your company.
* **Company Address** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ This is your company address for any physical delivery purposes.
* **Attendee Type** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Please select the correct attendee type from:
		- Marketing Team
		- Sales Team
		- Other
* **Registration Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Please select your registration type from:
		- Attendee
		- Speaker
		- Exhibitor
		- Organizer
		- Sponsor
* **Special Request** (`11`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ If you have any special requests, please provide them here.
