# Summer Camp Detailed Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Summer Camp. It collects essential information about the participant and their parent/guardian, including name, contact details, and camp-specific information such as camp name, location, and dates/times of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Participant Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2. Enter your parent/guardian's name and contact information in the "Parent Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
3. Select the correct "Camp Name" from the list.
4. Enter the "Location" of the camp.
5. Choose the dates you will be attending the camp using the "Dates Attending" field.
6. Select the times you will be attending using the "Times" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on any camp materials (e.g., name tags, certificates).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted regarding camp information and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **Parent Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your care during the camp (e.g., parent, guardian, or authorized caregiver).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your parent/guardian's email address where they can be contacted regarding camp information and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your parent/guardian's phone number where they can be reached in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **Camp Name** (`camp_name`, `text`, required): Select the name of the summer camp you are registered for.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the camp (e.g., address, city, or state).
* **Dates Attending** (`dates_attending`, `date`, required): Select the dates you will be attending the camp using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Times** (`times`, `time`, required): Select the times you will be attending the camp using the format HH:MM AM/PM.
