# Achievement Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Achievement Certification Form is a tool used to collect relevant details for issuing achievement certificates. It captures the recipient's information, achievement title, date of award, issuing body, and other essential details required to create a comprehensive certificate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields as instructed by each field's hint.
*   Provide accurate and complete information in each field to ensure correct certification creation.
*   Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Recipient name** (`recipient_name`, text, required): Enter the full name as it should appear on the certificate.
*  **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization or institution issuing the certificate.
*  **Achievement title** (`achievement_title`, text, required): Provide the title or description of the achievement.
*  **Date awarded** (`date_awarded`, date, required): Enter the date to be displayed on the certificate.
*  **Certificate ID number** (`cert_id_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the internal tracking number.
*  **Awarding body** (`awarding_body`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or body issuing the certificate.
*  **Certificate delivery method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Choose how the recipient will receive the certificate (Email PDF, Postal mail, or Collect in person).
*  **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): If postal delivery is chosen, enter the recipient's mailing address.
*  **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address for delivery or queries.
*  **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any special wording or details for the certificate.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   Use clear and concise language in all fields for best results.
*   If you encounter any issues, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
