# ski_equipment_rental_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a simple order form for ski equipment rental. The form allows customers to provide information about their rental needs, such as the duration of the rental, the equipment they wish to rent, and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. On "Ski Equipment Rental Form" page, fill in the required fields:
   - **Customer Name** (Customer Name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
   - **Customer Email** (Customer Email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
   - **Rental Duration** (Rental Duration): Select the duration of your rental.
   - **Total Price** (Total Price): Enter the total price for your rental.
   - **Payment Method** (Payment Method): Select your preferred payment method.
   - **Rental Items** (Rental Items): Select the equipment you wish to rent.

2. On "Ski Shops" page, select the ski shop(s) you wish to rent from:
   - **Ski Shops** (Ski Shops): Select one or multiple ski shops from the list.

3. If you need to provide any additional comments, please use the field "Additional Comments" (Rental Comments):
   - **Rental Comments** (Rental Comments): Enter any additional comments or information about your rental request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ski Equipment Rental Form** (Ski Equipment Rental Form, text, required): This is the main page of the form where customers can input their information.
* **Ski Shops** (Ski Shops, select multiple, required): Select one or multiple ski shops from the list.
* **Rental Duration** (Rental Duration, select one, required): Select the duration of your rental. Please note that this field is required.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the date from which you wish to rent the equipment.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required): Enter the date until which you wish to rent the equipment.
* **Start Time** (Start Time, time, required): Enter the time from which you wish to rent the equipment.
* **End Time** (End Time, time, required): Enter the time until which you wish to rent the equipment.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Rental Items** (Rental Items, select multiple, required): Select the equipment you wish to rent.
* **Total Price** (Total Price, number, required): Enter the total price for your rental.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select one, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, required): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your rental request.
