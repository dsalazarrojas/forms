# Commentator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commentator Registration Form is designed to collect information about your experience, skills, and availability to determine your suitability for commentating gigs. This form is a critical step in our talent registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
2. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering questions.
3. Ensure that you provide a working link for the demo reel and portfolio website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (`applicant_name`, text, required):** Enter your legal name for registration purposes.
* **Stage Name or On-Air Name (`stage_name`, text, optional):** Enter the name you'd like to be known by during broadcasts.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required):** Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, text, required):** Enter your contact phone number for event coordination.
* **Location (`location`, text, required):** Enter the city and state of your residence.
* **Years of Commentating Experience (`years_experience`, select_one, required):** Select the range of years you've been commentating.
* **Sport or Event Type (`sport_or_event_type`, select_multiple, required):** Choose the sports or events you specialize in.
* **Previous Events Covered (`previous_events`, text, required):** List notable events you've commentated for.
* **Commentary Style (`commentary_style`, select_one, required):** Describe your commentary style.
* **Languages Spoken (`languages_spoken`, select_multiple, required):** Select the languages you can commentate in.
* **Technical Skills (`technical_skills`, select_multiple, required):** Choose your broadcasting experience (e.g., studio, remote, radio, etc.).
* **Availability (`availability`, select_multiple, required):** Select the times or periods you're available to work.
* **Willingness to Travel (`travel_willingness`, select_one, required):** Indicate if you're willing to travel for events.
* **Demo Reel URL (`demo_reel`, text, required):** Provide a working link to your demo reel.
* **Portfolio Website (`portfolio_website`, text, optional):** Enter your professional website.
* **Social Media Profiles (`social_media`, text, optional):** List your professional social media profiles.
* **Professional References (`references`, text, required):** Enter names and contact information for professional references.
* **Fee Expectation (`fee_expectation`, number, required):** Enter your expected fee per event.
* **Is Fee Negotiable (`fee_negotiable`, select_one, required):** Indicate if you're open to negotiation.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional):** Share anything else you'd like to discuss.

Tips:

* Be honest and accurate when answering questions.
* Ensure that your demo reel link and portfolio website are up-to-date and working.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact email or support contact].
