# Referee Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Waiver Form is designed to collect necessary information from referees to verify their qualifications and status. This information will be used to ensure that referees are adequately prepared for their matches.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly** - Make sure to double-check your answers to ensure everything is correct.
2. **Enter your personal contact information** - Enter your First Name, Last Name, Address, Email, and Phone.
3. **Provide medical information** - Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions regarding any medical conditions, history, and certifications.
4. **Confirm your referee qualifications** - Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions about your experience, level, license, certification, training, education, and certifications.
5. **Confirm your equipment and services availability** - Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to questions about your available equipment and emergency services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Your first name as recorded on official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
  
- **Last Name**: Your last name as recorded on official documents.
  
- **Address**: Your current address where you can be contacted for work or emergencies.
  
- **Email**: Your email address for communication and updates.
  
- **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
  
- **Date of Birth**: Date of birth as recorded on official documents.
  
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of emergency contact person.
  
- **Emergency Phone**: Phone number of emergency contact person.
  
- **Medical Conditions**: Do you have any medical conditions that may affect your refereeing abilities?
  
- **Medical History**: Do you have any medical history that may affect your refereeing abilities?
  
- **Emergency Notes**: Any additional information for emergency contact person.
  
- **Referee Experience**: Are you experienced in refereeing?
  
- **Referee Level**: What level of refereeing experience do you have? (e.g., High, Low)
  
- **Referee License**: Are you licensed to referee?
  
- **Referee Certification**: Are you certified to referee?
  
- **Referee Training**: Are you trained in refereeing?
  
- **First Aid Certification**: Are you certified in first aid?
  
- **Certification Date**: Date of certification.
  
- **Referee Education**: Are you educated in refereeing?
  
- **Referee Education Level**: What level of education do you have in refereeing? (e.g., High, Low)
  
- **Referee Education Institution**: Institution of your referee education.
  
- **English Proficiency**: Are you proficient in English?
  
- **Referee Languages**: What languages do you speak fluently?
  
- **Referee Equipment**: Which refereeing equipment do you have access to?
  
- **Referee Emergency Services**: What emergency services are you certified in? (e.g., CPR, AED, etc.)
  
- **Referee Availability**: Are you available for refereeing at the requested dates?
  
- **Referee Availability Notes**: Any additional information about your availability.

This guide is designed to be a comprehensive resource to help you fill out the Referee Waiver Form accurately and efficiently.
