# ประก Medical Humanities Conference Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from attendees and presenters for the Thai Medical Humanities Conference. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Session Title
2. Provide your Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone number
5. Select your Role (attendee/presenter)
6. Choose your session preference (Yes/No)
7. Confirm your session preference
8. Enter your Address

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Title** (`session_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the session you are participating in or presenting.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Choose your role as an attendee or presenter.
* **Session Choice** (`session_choices`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether you will be attending or presenting a session.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required): Confirm your session preference.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your home or office address.

## Tips
* Please double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure your phone number is in international format (e.g., +66 123 456 7890).
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please note them in your address field or in the comment box (not included in this form).
* Review all fields carefully before submitting to ensure accurate and complete information.
