# interactive_exhibit_sponsorship_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The interactive exhibit sponsorship form is a registration tool for organizations and individuals to express their interest in sponsoring an event. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary information to be considered for sponsorship and support the event's goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you provide all required information.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name (Organization Name, text, required)**: Please enter the name of your organization.
* **Event Title (Event Title, text, required)**: Enter the title of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Event Date (Event Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, required)**: Enter the start date of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **End Date (End Date, date, required)**: Enter the end date of the event you are interested in sponsoring.
* **Contribution (Contribution, select_one, required)**: Please choose the type of contribution you would like to make for this event:
	+ **Event Donation**: Make a donation to support the event.
	+ **Event Sponsor**: Sponsor the event itself.
	+ **General**: Make a general contribution (not specified).
* **Sponsor (Sponsor, select_multiple, optional)**: If you are interested in being a sponsor, please select from the following options:
	+ **Event Patron**: Become an event patron.
	+ **Event Sponsor**: Become an event sponsor.
* **Contact Name (Contact Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who will be the contact for this registration.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, optional)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Message (Message, note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* Review the terms and conditions of the event before submitting this form.
