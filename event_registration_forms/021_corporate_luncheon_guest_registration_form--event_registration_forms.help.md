# Corporate Luncheon Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Luncheon Guest Registration Form is used to gather information from corporate guests attending a corporate luncheon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "Required". These fields are:
	* Company Name
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Confirmation (Note field)
2. Optional fields are:
	* Role
	* Title
	* Department
	* Phone
	* Company Address
	* Job Title
	* Contact Name
	* Contact Phone
	* Contact Email
	* Company Website
	* Company Fax
	* Contact Fax
	* Company Size
	* Company Type
	* Event Attendee
	* Guest Count
	* Additional Guest Info
	* Dietary Restrictions
	* Special Requests
	* Corporate Luncheon Guest

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of your company.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
- **Role** (`role`, text, Optional): Enter your role in the company.
- **Title** (`title`, text, Optional): Enter your job title.
- **Department** (`department`, text, Optional): Enter your department.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, Optional): Enter your company's address.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): Enter your job title.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Enter the contact name.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the contact email address.
- **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, Optional): Enter the company website.
- **Company Fax** (`company_fax`, text, Optional): Enter the company fax number.
- **Contact Fax** (`contact_fax`, text, Optional): Enter the contact fax number.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, Optional): Enter the size of your company.
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, text, Optional): Enter the type of company.
- **Event Attendee** (`event_attendee`, text, Optional): Check the box if you will be attending the event.
- **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, text, Optional): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
- **Additional Guest Info** (`additional_guest_info`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your guests.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, Optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
- **Corporate Luncheon Guest** (`corporate_luncheon_guest`, text, Optional): Check the box if you are a corporate guest.
- **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, note, Required): Enter your confirmation that you will be attending the event.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any special requests or dietary restrictions, please enter them in the corresponding fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
* If you are a corporate guest, please check the box "Corporate Luncheon Guest" to indicate this.
