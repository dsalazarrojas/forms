# e_sports_tournament_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_sports_tournament_payment_form is used to collect participant information, registration details, and payment details for an e-sports tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant_details field with the accurate and up-to-date information about the participant.
2. Enter the registration_details in the registration_details field.
3. Provide payment information in the payment_details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (`Participant Details`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter the participant's name, email, or any other relevant details required for the tournament.
* **registration_details** (`Registration Details`, text, required/optional): 
    Describe the registration process and any relevant information about the participant's registration.
* **payment_details** (`Payment Details`, text, required/optional): 
    Enter the payment method, amount, or any other payment-related information for the participant.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information you provide in the participant_details field.
- Enter clear and concise registration information in the registration_details field.
- For payment_details, provide the payment method and amount to ensure smooth processing.
