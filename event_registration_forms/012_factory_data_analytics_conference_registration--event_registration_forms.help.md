# Factory Data Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the Factory Data Analytics Conference. It collects information about each attendee to provide a smooth experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Check the "Consent" field to agree to the terms and conditions of the conference.
5. Select your preferences for sessions and topics in the "Preferences" field.
6. Add any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Conference Title
* **Conference Title**: This is the title of the conference that you are registering for.
*   This field is not a required field.

### 2. Attendee Name
* **Attendee Name**: This is the name of the attendee who is registering for the conference.
*   This field is required.

### 3. Email
* **Email**: This is the email address of the attendee who is registering for the conference.
*   This field is required.

### 4. Phone
* **Phone**: This is the phone number of the attendee who is registering for the conference.
*   This field is not a required field.

### 5. Consent
* **Consent**: This field is for attendees to agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the conference.
*   This field is optional, but it is recommended that attendees agree to the terms and conditions.

### 6. Preferences
* **Preferences**: This field allows attendees to select their preferences for sessions and topics.
*   This field is optional.

### 7. Sessions
* **Sessions**: This field is for attendees to select the sessions they would like to attend.
*   This field is optional and currently, there are no options provided.

### 8. Sign up details
* **Sign up details**: This is a note field for attendees to add any additional information.
*   This field is optional.

### 9. Additional Info
* **Additional Info**: This field is for attendees to add any additional information.
*   This field is optional.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Check the "Consent" field to agree or disagree to the terms and conditions.
- Use the "Preferences" field to select your sessions and topics.
- Be specific when filling in the "Additional Info" field to provide useful information.
- If you have any questions, please contact us.
