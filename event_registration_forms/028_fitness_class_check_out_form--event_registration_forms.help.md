# Fitness Class Check minimalist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a fitness class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Enter the name of the class you are registering for in the "Class Name" field.
6. Choose the time of the class you are interested in by selecting the corresponding time in the "Class Time" field.
7. Select the date you will attend the class by choosing the desired date.
8. Choose the type of class that suits you best by selecting from the options (Yoga, Pilates, Zumba, or Cardio).
9. Enter the duration of the class (in minutes) in the "Class Duration" field.
9. If you have registered previously, do not re-register. You are encouraged to provide your details only once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cheque).
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class you are registering for.
* **Class Time**: Select the time you would like to attend the class.
* **Class Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the class.
* **Class Type**: Choose the type of class that suits you best (Yoga, Pilates, Zumba, or Cardio).
* **Class Duration**: Enter the duration of the class in minutes.
* **Registration Date**: This field is auto-generated and cannot be edited by the user.

Please note that this form is for one-time use. If you have registered previously, do not re-register.
