# Target Shooting Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for a target shooting tournament. It collects information necessary for participant verification and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your tournament details, including the name and other relevant information.
2. Enter your participant details, such as name and other required fields.
3. Fill out the entry details section as instructed.
4. List the participant organizers.
5. Confirm you've signed the participant waiver (a separate form or process should be established for waiver submission).
6. Provide your medical information for any necessary medical considerations.
7. Specify your equipment usage for the tournament (though this may not be applicable).
8. Enter the entry fee amount.
9. Choose your payment method (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Tournament Details**
* **Tournament Details**: Enter the name and other relevant information about the tournament you're registering for.
### Participant Details**
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name and any other required fields as prompted.
### Entry Details**
* **Entry Details**: Fill out this section as instructed by the tournament organizers.
### Participant Organizers**
* **Participant Organizers**: List any relevant information about the participant organizers who will be attending with you.
### Participant Waivers**
* **Participant Waivers**: Please note that you need to sign a separate participant waiver form; this field is not for waiver submission.
### Participant Medical Info**
* **Participant Medical Info**: Provide any necessary medical information for the tournament organizers' consideration.
### Participant Equipment**
* **Participant Equipment**: Specify your equipment usage for the tournament (though this may not be applicable).
### Entry Fee
* **Entry Fee**: Enter the amount of the entry fee.
### Payment Method**
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method, such as credit card, cash, or other accepted methods.
