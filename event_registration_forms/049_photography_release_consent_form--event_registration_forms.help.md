# Photography Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
<thinking>Before you begin, we want to confirm that this form is for obtaining client consent for photography services. It ensures that you're aware of and agree to our policies regarding photo releases. Please review the form carefully and answer all the fields to ensure a smooth and secure process.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Select the "Client Consent" option: Choose the correct answer for the client's consent to our photography services.
2. Enter event details: Describe the event in the space provided.
3. Provide your photographer's information: Fill out your name, email, and contact number as a photographer.
4. Add contact information: Provide a valid phone number and email address for contact purposes.
5. Choose photo usage rights: Select the platforms where you consent to our use of photos (Print, Web, Social Media, Email, or Other).
6. Specify photo credit line: Include your name and role as a photographer.
7. Enter your contact number: A valid phone number for contact purposes.
8. Enter your email address: A valid email address for contact purposes.
9. Specify the event date and time: Enter the date and time of the event in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH-MM).
10. Provide event location: Enter the venue name and address where the event took place.
11. Describe the event: Give a brief description of the event.
12. Select the date photo was taken: Enter the date when the photo was taken in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
13. Select the time photo was taken: Enter the time of day when the photo was taken in the format HH-MM.
14. Add additional photographer information: Provide any additional details that might be relevant.
15. Specify the photo credit line 2: Include your name and role as a photographer.
16. Enter your contact number: A valid phone number for contact purposes.
17. Enter your email address: A valid email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent**: Select 'Yes' if you agree to our photography release policy.
* **Event Details**: Briefly describe the event in this field.
* **Photographer's Information**: Enter your name, email, and contact number as a photographer.
* **Contact Information**: Provide a valid phone number and email address for contact purposes.
* **Photo Release Consent**: Select 'Yes' if you consent to our photography release policy.
* **Photo Usage Rights**: Choose the platforms where you consent to our use of photos (Print, Web, Social Media, Email, or Other).
* **Photo Credit Line**: Include your name and role as a photographer.
* **Photographer's Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in the format HH-MM.
* **Event Location**: Enter the venue name and address where the event took place.
* **Event Description**: Briefly describe the event in this field.
* **Date Taken**: Enter the date when the photo was taken in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Taken**: Enter the time when the photo was taken in the format HH-MM.
* **Photographer's Information (Additional)**: Provide any additional details that might be relevant.
* **Photo Credit Line 2**: Include your name and role as a photographer.
* **Photographer's Contact**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.

Note: Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your photo release consent. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
