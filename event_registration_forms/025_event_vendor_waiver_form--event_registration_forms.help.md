# Event Vendor Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for event vendors to sign a waiver to protect themselves and the event organizers from potential liability. It's a legal requirement for event vendors to complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Provide the required information (indicated as "required" in the form).
* Review the waiver text carefully and agree to its terms.
* Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Vendor Waiver Form
* **Event Vendor Waiver Form**: This is the title of the form, which is provided for your reference.
### 2. Vendor Name
* **Vendor Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 3. Vendor E-mail
* **Vendor E-mail**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
### 4. Vendor Phone
* **Vendor Phone**: Provide your phone number where we can reach you in case of an emergency.
### 5. Vendor Address
* **Vendor Address**: Enter your business address where we can send any relevant documents.
### 6. Waiver Text
* **Waiver Text**: This is the waiver text to be signed. Please read and agree to its terms carefully.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the waiver text carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* This form is a requirement for event vendors to participate in our events.
