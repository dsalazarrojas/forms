# Summer Camp Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians of children attending summer camp. It helps the camp staff to identify any special needs, medical conditions, or emergency contacts that may be relevant during the child's stay. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the best experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Enter your child's First Name**: Type the first name of your child.
2.  **Enter your child's Last Name**: Type the last name of your child.
3.  **Enter your child's Email**: Enter a valid email address for your child. This is an optional field.
4.  **Enter your child's Phone Number**: Type your child's phone number.
5.  **Enter your child's Emergency Contact Name**: Type the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
6.  **Enter your child's Relationship to You**: Select the relationship between your child and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, Other).
7.  **Enter your child's Emergency Contact's Phone Number**: Type the phone number of your child's emergency contact.
8.  **Enter the Camp Location**: Type the location where your child will be attending summer camp.
9.  **Enter the Arrival Time**: Type the time you plan for your child to arrive at summer camp.
10. **Enter the Departure Time**: Type the time you plan for your child to leave summer camp.
11.  **Special Needs**: Select any special needs or requirements your child may have (e.g., Food allergies or restrictions, Medical equipment needed, Physical disability).
12. **Special Needs Description**: If you selected any special needs, provide a brief description.
13.  **Medical Conditions**: If your child has any medical conditions, type them in this field.
14.  **Medical Notes**: If you would like to provide any additional medical information, type it here.
