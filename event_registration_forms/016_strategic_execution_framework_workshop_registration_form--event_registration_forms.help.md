# Strategic Execution Framework Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Strategic Execution Framework Workshop Registration Form is designed for attendees to register for the workshop. This form will help you provide the necessary information to facilitate your participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to receive updates and notifications about the workshop.
3. Provide your Job Title and if applicable, your Company.
4. Select the workshop location.
5. Choose the workshop date and time.
6. Select one of the options from the "Select One" field.
7. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
8. Write any additional information you'd like to provide in the "Description" field.
9. Write any comments you'd like to provide in the "Comments" field.
10. Confirm your Email and Phone number to help us reach out to you with any last-minute updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the workshop. This will also be used as a contact method.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your role.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are attending from a company, please enter the name of your company.
- **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): Select the location of the workshop you are attending.
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the workshop you are attending.
- **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the workshop you are attending.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a contact method.
- **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address again to confirm your contact method.
- **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number again as a confirmation of your contact method.
- **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your background and expertise to help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the workshop.
- **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): This field is not necessary but serves as a confirmation of your email address.
- **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): This field is not necessary but serves as a confirmation of your phone number.

Note: Please ensure your email and phone number are accurate to ensure that we can contact you with updates and reminders about the workshop.
