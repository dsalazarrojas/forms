# Vision And Goal Setting Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant information and details related to the Vision and Goal Setting Training event. It aims to gather contact information, event-related details, and comments from both participants and facilitators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the participant information section, which includes fields such as name, contact information, and any other personal details you'd like to share.
2. If applicable, provide your email address and phone number.
3. Enter your address if you have a preferred address for receiving updates or communication.
4. In the Goal Setting section, indicate whether you have set your goals for the training event.
5. Add any comments you'd like to share with us about your participation or any suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: 
  * **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Information**: 
  * **Email**: Enter your email address for us to send updates and information.
  * **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your address if you have a preferred address for receiving updates or communication.
* **Goal Setting**: 
  * **Goal Setting**: Select 'Yes' if you have set your goals for the training event, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Training**: Enter any additional training-related details you'd like to share.
* **Other**: Enter any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Goals Objectives**: 
  * **Goals Objectives**: Select 'Yes' if you have set your goals, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Participant Comments**: Add any comments or suggestions you'd like to share about your participation.
* **Facilitator Comments**: Add any comments or suggestions you'd like to share about the training facilitators.
