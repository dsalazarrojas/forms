# Veterans Day Celebration RSVP Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals RSVP for the Veterans Day Celebration event. The event organizers need to know how many people will be attending, their contact information, and any special requests. By filling out this form, you will be providing the necessary information for a successful and enjoyable event for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields. This is where you'll enter your name so that we can address you personally.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used to contact you if we need to.
3. Check the **Are You Attending** field, where you can select "True" to confirm you're attending, "False" to confirm you won't be attending, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.
4. If you're attending, enter your **Date Of Arrival**, so we know when you'll be arriving.
5. If you're attending, enter your **Time Of Arrival**, so we can plan for your arrival.
6. If you have any special requests, type them in the **Special Request** field.
7. Finally, confirm your attendance by selecting "Confirmed" in the **RSVP** field, "Declined" if you won't be attending, or "Maybe" if you're unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name for personal contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name for personal contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address we'll use to contact you if needed.
* **Are You Attending** (`attending`, select_one, optional): Confirm or deny your attendance. Select "True" to attend, "False" to not attend, or "Maybe" for uncertainty.
* **Date Of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, optional): Enter the date you will arrive at the event.
* **Time Of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, time, optional): Enter the time you will arrive at the event.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Any special requests you have, such as dietary restrictions or accommodations.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, optional): Confirm your attendance. Select "Confirmed" to attend, "Declined" to not attend, or "Maybe" for uncertainty.
