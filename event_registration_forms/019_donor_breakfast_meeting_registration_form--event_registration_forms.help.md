# Donor Breakfast Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Breakfast Meeting Registration Form is designed to collect information from donors who are interested in attending a breakfast meeting event. This form helps us to plan and prepare for the event, ensuring that we can accommodate your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to help us contact you with event updates and reminders.
3. Provide your Phone number to enable us to reach you in case of any last-minute changes.
4. Select the type of event you wish to attend (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).
5. If you have any dietary restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free), select all that apply.
6. Choose the date and time of your attendance.
7. If you have any comments or additional information to share, please use the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1, name: first_name, label: First Name, type: text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your first name as it appears on your official records.

### Last Name (id: 2, name: last_name, label: Last Name, type: text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your last name as it appears on your official records.

### Email (id: 3, name: email, label: Email, type: email, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your official email address for communication purposes.

### Phone (id: 4, name: phone, label: Phone, type: text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.

### Event Type (id: 5, name: event_type, label: Event Type, type: select_one, required: false)
* Please select the type of event you wish to attend (Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner).

### Dietary Restrictions (id: 6, name: dietary_restrictions, label: Dietary Restrictions, type: select_multiple, required: false)
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please select all that apply:
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* Gluten-free
	* Dairy-free

### Attendance Date (id: 7, name: attendance_date, label: Attendance Date, type: date, required: false)
* Please choose the date you plan to attend the event.

### Attendance Time (id: 8, name: attendance_time, label: Attendance Time, type: time, required: false)
* Please choose the time you plan to arrive at the event.

### Comments (id: 9, name: comments, label: Comments, type: note, required: false)
* If you have any comments or additional information to share, please enter them in this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate to avoid any errors or discrepancies in event planning.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
