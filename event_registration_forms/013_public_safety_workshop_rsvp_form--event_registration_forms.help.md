# Public Safety Workshop RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Safety Workshop RSVP Form is a crucial tool for workshop organizers to gather essential information from participants and emergency contacts. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are collected and processed in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name** (`participant_name`): Please fill in your name as it will appear on the participant list.
2. **Enter your email address** (`participant_email`): Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
3. **Enter your phone number** (`phone_number`): Include your phone number for easy contact in case of emergencies.
4. **Select your workshop attendance** (`attending_workshop`): Choose which workshop you plan to attend from the available options.
5. **Provide emergency contact information** (`emergency_contact_name`, `emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
6. **Add any additional information** (`additional_info`): If you have any specific needs or requirements, please provide them in this section.
7. **Confirm your attendance** (`confirmed`): Indicate whether you are attending the workshop (`True`) or not (`False`).
8. **Add participant notes** (`participant_notes`): Write any additional notes or comments you'd like to share about your participation.
9. **Sign and date** (`participant_signature`): Please sign and date this form to confirm your participation.
10. **Enter the number of participants** (`workshop_participants`): If you're bringing guests, indicate how many are attending.
11. **Specify your emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (`participant_name`, text, optional)**: Enter your name as it will appear on the participant list.
* **Email (`participant_email`, email, optional)**: Provide your email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Participant Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, optional)**: Include your phone number for easy contact in case of emergencies.
* **Attending Workshop (`attending_workshop`, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose which workshop you plan to attend from the available options.
* **Emergency Contact Name (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Additional Info (`additional_info`, text, optional)**: If you have any specific needs or requirements, please provide them in this section.
* **Confirmed (`confirmed`, select_one, optional)**: Indicate whether you are attending the workshop (`True`) or not (`False`).
* **Participant Notes (`participant_notes`, text, optional)**: Write any additional notes or comments you'd like to share about your participation.
* **Participant Signature (`participant_signature`, text, optional)**: Please sign and date this form to confirm your participation.
* **Number of Participants (`workshop_participants`, number, optional)**: If you're bringing guests, indicate how many are attending.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional)**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
