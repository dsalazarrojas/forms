# Bachelorette Weekend Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bachelorette Weekend Registration form is designed to collect essential information from registrants for the Bachelorette Weekend event. This form helps us ensure that you have all the necessary details to provide a wonderful and safe experience for you and your group.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your details in the form, making sure to enter the required information.
2. Choose the selected weekend package that suits your needs.
3. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
4. Confirm your emergency contact information.
5. Update your payment status.
6. If necessary, specify your accommodation needs and dietary requirements.
7. Enter any special requests or needs you may have.
8. Finally, submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you correctly.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for us to contact you for updates and confirmations.
* **Contact phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your mobile phone number for easy contact.
* **Weekend package selected** (`weekend_package`, select_one, required): Choose from Standard, Premium, or VIP packages for your weekend experience.
* **Accommodation needed** (`accommodation_needed`, text, optional): If you need accommodation, specify the number of nights and room type you require.
* **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): Inform us about any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact for us to reach out in case of an urgent matter.
* **Payment status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Update your payment status as Paid, Pending, or Deposit only.
* **Special requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests or needs, please let us know.
* **Registration date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
