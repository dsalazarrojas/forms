# Wedding Planning Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information related to wedding planning services. It will help you to track payments, communicate with service providers, and keep records of wedding-related transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Wedding Planning Payment Form" form from the available forms.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate information is provided.
3. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Payment Details** (`payment details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the payment, including the payment method and amount.
- **Couple Name** (`couple name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the couple involved in the wedding planning process.
- **Payment Method** (`payment method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the payment method used for this transaction, such as Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
- **Amount** (`amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount paid or to be paid for the wedding planning services.
- **Event Date** (`event date`, date, required: false): Specify the date of the event, if relevant.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event, if applicable.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide the email address of the contact person or couple.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person or couple.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional information or comments related to the payment or event.
- **Service Name** (`service name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the service provided.
- **Provider** (`provider`, text, required: false): Specify the name of the service provider.
- **Vendor** (`vendor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the vendor involved in the event.
- **Vendor Service** (`vendor service`, text, required: false): Specify the service provided by the vendor.
- **Invoice Number** (`invoice number`, number, required: false): Enter the invoice number, if applicable.
- **Event Location** (`event location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of the event.
- **Event Details** (`event details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the event.
- **Total Amount** (`total amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount paid or to be paid for the services.
- **Payment Status** (`payment status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the status of the payment (Paid, Pending).
- **Provider Notes** (`provider notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes from the service provider.
- **Provider Service** (`provider service`, text, required: false): Specify the service provided by the provider.
- **Event Notes** (`event notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the event.
- **Service Provider** (`service provider`, text, required: false): Specify the name of the service provider.
- **Provider Service Notes** (`provider service notes`, text, required: false): Enter any notes about the service provided.
- **Additional Notes** (`additional notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the payment or event.
