# needlecraft_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a handmade tournament in a competition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant information section (page 1) with your details as a participant.
2. Fill in the entry details section (page 2) with your entry information.
3. Choose your category from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category** (`Category`, Select One, Required): Choose the category in which you are participating, such as Handmade Jewelry, Handwoven Bags, Handmade Pottery, or Handmade Woodwork.
* **entry_details** (`Entry Details`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description or details about your entry.
* **participant_details** (`Participant Details`, Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant information about yourself as a participant, such as your name, email, or contact number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, including the category.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
