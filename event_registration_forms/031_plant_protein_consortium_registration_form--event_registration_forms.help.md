# Plant Protein Consortium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration tool for potential members of the Plant Protein Consortium. It will collect your name, contact information, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in your User Name, which should match the name you are known by or registered with.
2.  Enter your Email Address, which will be used for communication and updates.
3.  Fill in your Phone Number, if applicable.
4.  Provide your Organization Name and Name of the Contact Person, if applicable.
5.  Choose your role within the consortium (Member, Non-Member, or Sponsor).
6.  Write a brief Bio, if you like, but it is optional.
7.  Check the Data Protection Policy checkbox to indicate your agreement.
8.  Choose your agreement status with the Terms and Conditions (Agree, Decline, or Not Provided).
9.  For the Assigned Tool field, please leave it blank for now. This field is not required for registration.
10. Finally, select your agreement status with the submission (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Name**: Fill in your name as it is known by or registered with.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
*   **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, fill it in. If not, leave it blank.
*   **Organization Name**: If you are affiliated with an organization, provide its name.
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person in your organization.
*   **Role**: Choose your role within the consortium (Member, Non-Member, or Sponsor).
*   **Bio**: Write a brief description of yourself, if you'd like.
*   **Data Protection Policy**: Check to agree to the data protection policy.
*   **Terms and Conditions**: Select your agreement status with the terms and conditions (Agree, Decline, or Not Provided).
*   **Assigned Tool**: This field is not required for registration; you can leave it blank.
*   **Submit**: Select your agreement status with the submission (Yes or No).

## Tips
*   Be truthful and accurate when filling out the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the consortium management team.
*   Ensure that you are not already registered as a member of the consortium before completing this form.
