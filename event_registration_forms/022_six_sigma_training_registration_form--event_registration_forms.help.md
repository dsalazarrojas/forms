## Six Sigma Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Six Sigma Training Registration Form is used to collect information from participants in a Six Sigma training program. This form will be used to gather the necessary details about the participants to facilitate the training process and manage their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as per your identification.
2.  Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
3.  If you have a phone number, enter it to allow us to contact you in case of any emergency.
4.  Enter your company name.
5.  Enter your job title or position in the company.
6.  Select the training program(s) you are interested in attending.
7.  Choose the date you would like to attend the training.
8.  Select the time you would like to attend the training.
9.  Enter the location where you would like to attend the training.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes you may have about your participation in the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is to be used on the training materials and for communication purposes.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the training.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to allow us to contact you in case of any emergency.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_multiple, required): Select the training program(s) you are interested in attending. You can select one or multiple programs as per your preferences.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to attend the training. Please note that the training dates may not be available on all the selected dates.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to attend the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_date`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to attend the training.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you may have about your participation in the training.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct email address as it will be used for communication purposes.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, please mention them in the participant notes section.
- Please note that the training dates and time may be subject to change. We will keep you updated about any changes.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- Please select the correct training program as per your preferences to ensure that you receive the right materials and information.
- Make sure to enter the correct location where you would like to attend the training.
- We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to improve our training programs.
