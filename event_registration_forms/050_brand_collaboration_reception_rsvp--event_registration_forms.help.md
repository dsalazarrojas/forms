# Brand Collaboration Reception RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register your attendance and gather information about your preferences and requirements for the Brand Collaboration Reception. Your input will help us better understand your needs and ensure a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Company or Brand Name, Job Title, Reception Date, Will You Attend, and I confirm my RSVP for the reception.
2. Provide optional information about your contact details (Phone Number), professional details (Department), and preferences (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies, Accessibility or Accommodation Needs, Special Requests or Comments).
3. If applicable, provide information about your plus one (Plus One Name, Total Number of Guests).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your contact email address for reception updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Company or Brand Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, required): Enter your position.
* **Department** (`department`, optional): Enter your department or division.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Reception Start Time** (`reception_time`, optional): Enter the time you will arrive.
* **Will You Attend** (`rsvp_status`, required): Confirm your attendance by choosing one of the options.
	+ **Yes, I will attend**
	+ **Yes, with plus one**
	+ **Maybe**
	+ **No, I cannot attend**
	+ **Undecided**
* **Plus One Name (If Applicable)** (`plus_one_name`, optional): If you're bringing a guest, enter their name.
* **Total Number of Guests** (`total_guests`, required): Enter the total number of guests, including yourself.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): List any food restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): List any special requirements you may have.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (`special_requests`, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **I confirm my RSVP for the reception** (`rsvp_confirmation`, required): Confirm your attendance by choosing one of the options.
	+ **I confirm my attendance**
	+ **I need to cancel my RSVP**
	+ **I am still deciding**
