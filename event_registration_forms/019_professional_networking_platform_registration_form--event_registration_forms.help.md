# professional_networking_platform_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration purposes on a professional networking platform. You can fill in your details to create a profile or sign up for events and activities on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your **User Details** section.
2. Proceed to the **Contact Details** section.
3. Finally, provide your **Professional Details**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): This section collects your personal information, such as your name, job title, and company. Please fill in your name, and any other relevant details you'd like to share. 
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This section is for your contact information, including your email address and phone number. You can also add any other contact method you prefer. 
* **Professional Details** (`professional_details`, text, optional): This section is for your professional background and experience. Describe your work or education background to help others get to know you better.
