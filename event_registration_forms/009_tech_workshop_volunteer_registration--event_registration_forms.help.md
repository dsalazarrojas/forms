# tech_workshop_volunteer_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for potential volunteers to register for a tech workshop event. Please fill out the form below to provide us with your contact information, availability, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you are interested in volunteering for in the **event_name** field.
2. Choose your role for the event from the **event_role** dropdown menu.
3. Select a date and time you are available to volunteer in the **available_time** field.
4. Enter your contact information in the **contact_information** field.
5. Select the terms of service you agree to by checking the boxes in the **terms_of_service** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name**: Enter the name of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **event_role**: Choose your role for the event from the following options:
	+ event coordinator
	+ event helper
	+ event technical support
* **available_time**: Select a date and time you are available to volunteer.
* **contact_information**: Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **terms_of_service**: Check the boxes to indicate your consent to the following terms:
	+ I am over 18 years old.
	+ I have the right to carry (this field is likely intended to refer to having the necessary permits or licenses to carry or handle certain equipment).
	+ I will not share this information with others.
