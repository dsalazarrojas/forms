# Paddleboard Lesson Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paddleboard Lesson Registration Form is a tool designed to help you book lessons with our instructor. By filling out this form, you will be able to specify your details, choose a time slot that suits you, and receive information about your selected lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Provide Your Details**: Fill out the "User Details" page with your name and any other relevant information.
2. **Book Your Lesson**: On the "Booking Details" page, select a date and time for your lesson that works for you.
3. **Get Lesson Notes**: Please provide any specific notes or requests about what you hope to achieve or focus on during your lesson on the "Lesson Notes" page.
4. **Check Instructor Availability**: On the "Instructor Availability" page, you can view the instructor's schedule. If you need to book a lesson, select an available time slot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (User Details)**: 
  * (Text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details to help the instructor provide a better experience for you.
* **Booking Details (Booking Details)**: 
  * (Text, optional): Choose a date and time for your lesson, and the instructor will get back to you with available options.
* **Lesson Notes (Lesson Notes)**: 
  * (Text, required): Share any specific notes or requests you have about the lesson, such as areas you'd like to focus on or any goals you're working towards.
* **Instructor Availability (Instructor Availability)**: 
  * (Text, optional): Check the instructor's schedule to see when they are available to teach you. Select a time that works for you, or if you don't see an available time, contact the instructor directly.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information in the "User Details" and "Lesson Notes" pages, as these will help the instructor tailor the lesson to your needs.
* If you have any specific requests or requirements, such as equipment needs or special conditions, be sure to mention them in the "Lesson Notes" page.
* Don't worry if you don't see an available time with your preferred instructor; just let us know, and we'll do our best to accommodate your request or find an alternative instructor.
