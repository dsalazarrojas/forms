# Sustainability Summit Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Summit Lead Generation Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in attending or participating in the Sustainability Summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information in the "Event Organizer", "Contact Person", and "Email" fields.
2. Select your role in the event in the "Role" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Provide the location of the event in the "Location" field.
6. Enter the date and start and end time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Start Time" fields.
7. Enter a brief description of the event in the "Event Description" field.
8. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Organizer**: Enter your name or the name of the organization hosting the event.
- **Contact Person**: Enter your name or the name of the person to contact for further information.
- **Role**: Select your role in the event, such as Event Organizer, Team Member, Speaker, or Sponsor.
- **Email**: Enter your email address or the email address of the contact person.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
- **Company**: Enter the name of the company or organization you represent.
- **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the organization.
- **Location**: Enter the location of the event, such as a city or venue name.
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event to give context.
- **Role**: Select your role in the event from the options provided.
