# Counseling Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and contact information from individuals seeking counseling services. It is essential that you submit accurate and complete information to ensure efficient and effective service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed before submitting.
2. Be accurate and truthful in the information you provide.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`name_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Street** (`address_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`address_3`, text, required/optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`address_4`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip or postal code.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submission.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
