# School Field Trip Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for school field trips, including the school's name, number of students, accommodation type, start and end dates, accommodation address, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your needs and provide a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the name of the school for which you are requesting accommodations.
3. Enter the number of students attending the field trip.
4. Select all applicable accommodation options for the trip.
5. Choose the start and end dates of the field trip accurately.
6. Enter the address where the accommodations are needed.
7. Provide any additional details about the accommodations if needed.
8. If necessary, indicate any special arrangements for equipment or tools needed.
9. Confirm your contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Name** (`School Name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the school requesting accommodations.
- **Number of Students** (`student_count`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of students participating in the field trip.
  - This will help us understand the scale of the trip and allocate accommodations accordingly.
- **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose all applicable accommodation types from the provided options.
  - Select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" as needed.
- **Start Date** (`accommodation_start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the start date of the field trip accurately.
- **End Date** (`accommodation_end_date`, date, **required**): Enter the end date of the field trip accurately.
- **Accommodation Address** (`accommodation_address`, text, **required**): Enter the address where accommodations are needed.
  - Be as specific as possible to ensure accurate delivery of services.
- **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional details about the accommodations if needed.
- **Other Accommodation** (`accommodation_other`, text, **optional**): If there are any other special accommodations not listed, provide details here.
- **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your contact email for follow-up and communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up and communication.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**): Select "chatjimmy" as the assigned tool for the field trip.

This guide will help ensure that all necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently for your school field trip accommodations.
