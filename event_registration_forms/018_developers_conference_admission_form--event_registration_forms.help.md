# Developers Conference Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees, speakers, sponsors, and exhibitors for the Developers Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to required fields.
2. If you're registering as an attendee, fill out the attendee information (pages 1-5).
3. If you're a speaker, fill out the speaker information (pages 7-9).
4. If you're a sponsor or exhibitor, fill out the relevant fields for your company's representative (pages 6-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (1)** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your badge.
* **Email (2)** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Company (3)** (Text, Optional): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title (4)** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Registration Date (5)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the conference.
* **T-shirt Size (6)** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the available t-shirt sizes.
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ X-Large
* **Food Preferences (7)** (Select One, Optional): Choose your food preference.
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Omnivore
* **Meal Choice (8)** (Select One, Optional): Choose your meal preference.
	+ Breakfast
	+ Lunch
	+ Dinner
	+ None
* **Dietary Restrictions (9)** (Text, Optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name (10)** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (11)** (Text, Optional): Enter your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (12)** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email (13)** (Email, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address (14)** (Text, Optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Note (15)** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other (16)** (Text, Optional): If your emergency contact relationship is not listed, please specify.
* **Attendee Type (17)** (Select One, Optional): Choose your role at the conference.
	+ Attendee
	+ Speaker
	+ Sponsor
	+ Exhibitor
* **Session Presentation (18)** (Select One, Optional): Check if you will be presenting a session at the conference.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Registration Date 2 (19)** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you registered for the conference (not to be confused with the date you plan to attend).
* **T-shirt Designer (20)** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred t-shirt designer, enter their name.
* **Food Cost (21)** (Select One, Optional): Choose if you will be paying for food or not.
	+ Free
	+ Paid
* **Food Cost Note (22)** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your food cost.
* **Meal Preference (23)** (Select One, Optional): Choose your meal preference.
	+ Breakfast
	+ Lunch
	+ Dinner
	+ None
* **Dietary Restriction (24)** (Text, Optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Session Speaker (25)** (Text, Optional): If you'll be speaking at the conference, enter your session information (topic, time, etc.).
