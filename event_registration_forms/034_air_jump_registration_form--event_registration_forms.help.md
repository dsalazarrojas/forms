# Air Jump Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Jump Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for the Air Jump event. This includes demographic details, medical conditions, and consent for photos and video.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the **Participant name** field.
2. Select your age in the **Age** field.
3. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the **Emergency contact** field.
4. If you have any medical conditions or restrictions, enter them in the **Medical conditions or restrictions** field. This will help us ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.
5. Select a date for your preferred jump session in the **Preferred session date** field.
6. Read and agree to the waiver terms in the **Waiver acknowledgement** field.
7. Indicate your experience level with jumps by selecting one of the options in the **Experience level with jumps** field.
8. If you need any equipment, select **True** in the **Equipment required** field.
9. Grant permission to use your photo and video by selecting **True** in the **Consent for photos and video** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Age**: Select your age for eligibility purposes. Please ensure this is accurate.
* **Emergency contact**: Provide the name and phone number of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical conditions or restrictions**: Inform us of any conditions or restrictions that may affect your participation.
* **Preferred session date**: Select a date for your preferred jump session.
* **Waiver acknowledgement**: Please read and agree to the waiver terms to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.
* **Experience level with jumps**: Indicate your experience level with jumps to help us tailor the experience.
* **Equipment required**: If you need any equipment, select **True**. This will help us prepare for your needs.
* **Consent for photos and video**: Grant permission to use your photo and video for promotional purposes.
