# Artisanal Brand Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artisanal Brand Showcase Registration form is designed to collect detailed information from artisanal brands that want to participate in the event. This form helps the event organizers to understand the brand's products, services, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Provide Business Information

* **Business or Brand Name** (`business_name`): Enter the official name of your artisanal business.
* **Owner or Contact Person Name** (`business_owner_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your business.
* **Business Email Address** (`business_email`): Enter your official business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`business_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Social Media** (`business_website`): Enter the URL of your website or main social media platform.

### Step 2: Provide Business Details

* **Brief Description of Business** (`business_description`): Describe your artisanal brand in a concise manner.
* **Type of Craft** (`craft_type`): Select the type of craft your business specializes in (e.g., Textiles and Fashion, Pottery and Ceramics, etc.).
* **Year Business Founded** (`founding_year`): Enter the year your business was founded (if applicable).
* **Team Size** (`team_size`): Select the number of people who work in your business (if applicable).

### Step 3: Provide Product Information

* **Primary Products or Specialties** (`product_description`): Describe the products or specialties your business offers.
* **Production Method** (`production_method`): Select how your products are made (e.g., Fully Handmade, Mostly Handmade with Some Tools, etc.).
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`): Describe any eco-friendly or sustainable practices your business uses.

### Step 4: Showcase Details

* **Type of Showcase Participation** (`showcase_interest`): Select the level of participation you're interested in (e.g., Vendor Booth, Premium Booth, etc.).
* **Booth Space Needed** (`booth_space_needed`): Select the estimated space requirements for your booth.
* **Promotional Materials** (`promotional_materials`): Select if you have prepared promotional materials (e.g., Flyers, Brochures, etc.).
* **Have You Prepared Promotional Materials?** (`promotional_materials`): Describe your prepared materials.

### Step 5: Visual Assets

* **Do You Have Product Photos Available?** (`product_photos`): Select if you have high-quality product photos available.

### Step 6: Agreement and Confirmation

* **I Accept the Showcase Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`): Confirm that you accept all showcase rules.
* **Additional Information or Special Requests** (`additional_notes`): Enter any special requirements or notes.
* **Best Way to Contact You for Follow-up** (`follow_up_contact`): Select your preferred contact method for follow-up.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* Make sure to follow the showcase rules and terms.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email or phone number].
