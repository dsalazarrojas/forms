# Business Networking Event Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees to the Business Networking Event. By filling out this form, you will be able to choose which event you want to attend, provide your personal and company information, specify your meal preference, and indicate your networking goals. You will also be able to sign up for our newsletter to receive updates about future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Event**: Choose the event you wish to attend from the dropdown menu.
2. **Provide Personal Details**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. **Enter Company Information**: Provide your company name and select the number of employees in your company.
4. **Specify Reservation Preferences**: Choose your meal preference and indicate the number of guests you plan to bring.
5. **Optional Fields**: You can provide names of additional guests, specify any special accommodations, and select your networking goals.
6. **Newsletter Signup**: Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Event**: Choose the event you wish to attend from the dropdown menu.
	+ This field is required to ensure that you are registering for the correct event.
* **Event Date**: This field will automatically be filled in with the date of the selected event.
	+ Please note that this field is required and will be automatically populated based on your event selection.
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name.
	+ This field is required and will be used to address you at the event.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
	+ This field is required and will be used for confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is required and will be used for communication purposes.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
	+ This field is required and will be used to identify your organization.
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand the size of your organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your position in the company.
	+ This field is required and will help us understand your role in the company.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring.
	+ This field is required and will help us prepare for the event.
* **Guest Names**: Enter the names of your additional guests.
	+ This field is optional and will help us seat your guests.
* **Meal Preference**: Choose your dietary preference.
	+ This field is optional and will influence our catering selection.
* **Special Accommodations**: Enter any special needs or accommodations you may require.
	+ This field is optional and will be kept confidential.
* **Networking Goals**: Select any networking goals you hope to achieve.
	+ This field is optional and will help us tailor the event experience for you.
* **Newsletter Signup**: Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter.
	+ This field is optional and will allow you to stay up-to-date on future events.
