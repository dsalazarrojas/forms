# Election Volunteer Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to record information about election volunteers, including their role, hours worked, and contact information. It helps event organizers keep track of volunteer records accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant information for each field from the provided options.
2.  Enter the required information accurately and truthfully.
3.  Save the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Volunteer Information (<id: 1>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Information
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter your name and any other relevant information about the volunteer.

*   **Contact Information (<id: 2>):**
    *   Label: Contact Information
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter your name, email, phone number, and other contact information.

*   **Volunteer Role (<id: 3>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Role
    *   Type: select\_one
    *   Purpose: Select the volunteer's role (Volunteer or Organizer).

*   **Start Date (<id: 4>):**
    *   Label: Start Date
    *   Type: date
    *   Purpose: Enter the start date of the volunteer's service.

*   **End Date (<id: 5>):**
    *   Label: End Date
    *   Type: date
    *   Purpose: Enter the end date of the volunteer's service.

*   **Volunteer Status (<id: 6>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Status
    *   Type: select\_one
    *   Purpose: Select the volunteer's status (Active or Inactive).

*   **Volunteer Type (<id: 7>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Type
    *   Type: select\_one
    *   Purpose: Select the type of volunteer (Primary or Secondary).

*   **Volunteer Hours (<id: 8>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Hours
    *   Type: number
    *   Purpose: Enter the number of hours the volunteer worked.

*   **Volunteer Comments (<id: 9>):**
    *   Label: Volunteer Comments
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter any comments or feedback about the volunteer.

*   **Contact Name (<id: 10>):**
    *   Label: Contact Name
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter your name for contact purposes.

*   **Contact Email (<id: 11>):**
    *   Label: Contact Email
    *   Type: email
    *   Purpose: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

*   **Contact Phone (<id: 12>):**
    *   Label: Contact Phone
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

*   **Relationship (<id: 13>):**
    *   Label: Relationship
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter the nature of your relationship with the volunteer.

*   **Contact Address (<id: 14>):**
    *   Label: Contact Address
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter your address for contact purposes.

*   **Contact City (<id: 15>):**
    *   Label: Contact City
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter the city of your address.

*   **Contact State (<id: 16>):**
    *   Label: Contact State
    *   Type: text
    *   Purpose: Enter the state of your address.

*   **Contact Zip (<id: 17>):**
    *   Label: Contact Zip

*   **Contact Country (<id: 18>):**
    *   Label: Contact Country

*   **Other Assigned Tool (<id: 21>):**
    *   Label: Other Assigned Tool
