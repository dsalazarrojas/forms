# Workplace Efficiency Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about events, allowing event organizers to showcase their sessions and connect with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your event details, such as event name, date, location, and start and end times.
2. Select the relevant session interests and topics you are interested in.
3. Enter your attendee details, including name, email, phone number, and any additional notes.
4. Verify your information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you are interested in attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Session Interest** (`session_interest`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which sessions interest you, selecting from the provided options.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the physical location of the event.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as an attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as an attendee.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share about the event or yourself as an attendee.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of session you are interested in (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Enter the topic of the session you are interested in.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the session you are interested in.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the session you are interested in.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the session.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the event (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Add any additional details about the event you'd like to share.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Company** (`event_organizer_company`, text, required): Enter the company name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Position** (`event_organizer_position`, text, required): Enter the position title of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Address** (`event_organizer_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Notes** (`event_organizer_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event organizer.

Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid errors. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the event organizers or support team.
