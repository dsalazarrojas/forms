# Educational Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Program Volunteer Registration Form is designed for volunteers to register for educational programs and events. This form helps event organizers collect relevant information from volunteers, including contact details, availability, and role within the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Program Details** section with the title of the educational program you are registering for.
2. Provide your **Volunteer Information**, including any relevant background or qualifications that are relevant to the program.
3. Fill in your **Contact Information**, including your email and phone number.
4. Select your **Availability** for the program, if applicable.
5. Choose your **Volunteer Shifts**, if applicable.
6. Fill in the **Event Details**, if applicable.
7. Specify your **Educational Institution**, if applicable.
8. Select your **Volunteer Role**, this will determine what tasks you are assigned to.
9. Fill in your **Volunteer Position**, if you have one.
10. Enter your **Email**, which is a required field.
11. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is a required field.
12. Fill in your **Date**, which is a required field.
13. Select your **Time**, which is a required field.
14. If necessary, add any additional **Note** about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Details**: Enter the title of the educational program you are registering for.
* **Volunteer Information**: This is where you can provide any relevant background or qualifications that are relevant to the program.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email and phone number to ensure that we can contact you about the program.
* **Availability**: Select your availability for the program, if applicable.
* **Volunteer Shifts**: Choose the shifts that you are available to work on, if applicable.
* **Event Details**: Fill in any additional event-specific details.
* **Educational Institution**: If you are affiliated with an educational institution, enter it here.
* **Volunteer Role**: Select your role within the program, this will determine what tasks you are assigned to.
* **Volunteer Position**: Enter your volunteer position, if you have one.
* **Email**: A required field, please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A required field, please enter your phone number.
* **Date**: A required field, please enter your date of availability.
* **Time**: A required field, please select your time of availability.
* **Note**: If you have any additional information, you can add it here.
