# Stage Production Crew Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for stage production crew registration. It is meant to collect information from crew members who will be working on a stage production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Registration Form" section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your "Production Company" name.
3. Provide your "Name of Crew Member" and any other relevant details.
4. Select your "Role" within the production (e.g., Crew Lead, Stage Manager, etc.).
5. Enter your "Email" address (if applicable).
6. Enter your "Phone" number (if applicable).
7. Enter the "Production Start Date" and "Production End Date" of the production.
8. Provide any additional "Production Notes" that are relevant to the production.
9. If you have a specific "Assigned Tool" for the production, select it from the options provided. If not, you can choose "other" and enter the name of the tool in the "Other Tool" field.
10. If you have another tool assigned, select it from the options provided. If not, you can choose "other" and enter the name of the tool in the "Other Tool 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`stage_production_crew_registration_form`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the production company.
* **Name of Crew Member** (`name_of_crew_member`, text, required): Enter your name as a crew member.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role within the production (e.g., Crew Lead, Stage Manager, etc.).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Production Start Date** (`production_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the production.
* **Production End Date** (`production_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the production.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional production notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select your assigned tool for the production from the options provided.
* **Other Tool** (`other_tool`, text, optional): If you choose "other" in the "Assigned Tool" field, enter the name of your tool in this field.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool2`, select_multiple, optional): Select another assigned tool for the production from the options provided (if applicable).
* **Other Tool 2** (`other_tool2`, text, optional): If you choose "other" in the "Assigned Tool 2" field, enter the name of your tool in this field.
