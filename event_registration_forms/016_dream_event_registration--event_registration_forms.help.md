# Dream Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for events, such as conferences, workshops, or meetups. It's a basic registration form to gather necessary details from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. The form has three pages: "Event Details", "Participant Info", and "Participant Data". You can fill out one page at a time, and the form will automatically move to the next page when you complete each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (required/optional): Fill in the title and other relevant details about the event. This could include date, time, venue, and event type.
* **Participant Info** (required/optional): Enter your name and other contact information as a participant, such as email and phone number. This will help the event organizers reach you with updates or important details.
* **Participant Data** (required/optional): This is where you can add any additional information about yourself as a participant, such as your role or interests related to the event. You can also add any other relevant details you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
