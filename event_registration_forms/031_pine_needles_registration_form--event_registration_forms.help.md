# Pine Needles Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pine Needles Registration Form is used for participants to register for events and for submitters to record event information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review your entries for correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be listed on the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date**: Enter the date you plan to participate in the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time you plan to participate in the event.
* **Event Information**: Enter any additional details about the event you'd like to share.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you're participating in.
* **Collection Date**: Enter the date the event was collected.
* **Collection Time**: Enter the time the event was collected.
* **Pine Needle Information**: Enter any additional details about the pine needle you're submitting.
* **Pine Needle Type**: Select whether the pine needle is a 'Yes' or 'No' (for event purposes).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments about the event or participant.

### Submitter Information (Page 2)
* **Assigned To**: Select who the event is assigned to (if applicable).
* **Status**: Select whether the submitter status is 'Active' or 'Inactive' (required for tracking).
* **Submitter**: Enter the submitter's name.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time the submitter is submitting the form.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date the submitter is submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Status**: Select whether the submitter status is 'Active' or 'Inactive' (required for tracking).
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Email Comments**: Enter any email comments from the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone Comments**: Enter any phone comments from the submitter.

Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to help us process your registration efficiently. If you have any questions, contact our support team for assistance.
