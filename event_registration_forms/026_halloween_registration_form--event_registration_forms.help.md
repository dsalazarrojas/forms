# halloween_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering and organizing Halloween events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your email address for communication and updates.
4. (Optional) Enter your contact number for additional contact information.
5. Choose the date and start and end times of the event.
6. Select the type of event you are organizing (e.g., Halloween party, trick-or-treat, costume contest).
7. Choose the specific event types (e.g., Halloween party, trick-or-treat, costume contest) that apply to your event.
8. Enter a list of attendees separated by commas in the "Attendees" field.
9. Provide any comments or additional information about the event in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, `text`, required): Enter your first name here. Please make sure to fill this out.
* **Last Name** (2, `text`, required): Enter your last name here. Please make sure to fill this out.
* **Address** (3, `text`, required): Enter your contact address here for us to reach you.
* **Email** (4, `email`, optional): Enter your email address here for us to contact you about event updates.
* **Contact Number** (5, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number here for additional contact information.
* **Event Date** (6, `date`, optional): Select the date of your Halloween event.
* **Start Time** (7, `time`, optional): Select the start time of your Halloween event.
* **End Time** (8, `time`, optional): Select the end time of your Halloween event.
* **Event Type** (9, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of Halloween event you are organizing (e.g., Halloween party, trick-or-treat, costume contest).
* **Event Type Options** (10, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the specific event types that apply to your event (e.g., Halloween party, trick-or-treat, costume contest).
* **Attendees** (11, `note`, required): Enter a list of attendees separated by commas here.
* **Comments** (12, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your event here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate event registration.
- For the "Attendees" field, separate attendees with commas.
- For the "Event Type" and "Event Type Options" fields, select the relevant event types that apply to your event.
