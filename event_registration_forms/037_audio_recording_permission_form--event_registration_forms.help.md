# Audio Recording Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from presenters to record their sessions at events. It ensures that their rights are respected and that they have control over how their content is used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and affiliation or company as the presenter.
2.  Enter the name of the event and the date it takes place.
3.  Enter the title of your presentation or session.
4.  Choose whether you grant permission to record your session, excluding Q&A if desired.
5.  Indicate whether you grant permission to share the recording with event attendees.
6.  Choose whether you grant permission to use snippets of your recording for future event marketing.
7.  If applicable, add any specific restrictions or conditions for the recording.
8.  Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
9.  Sign the form with your name as the presenter.
10. Date the signed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required)
This field is where you enter your name as the presenter or registrant.
###  **Affiliation or Company** (`affiliation_company`, text, optional)
This field is where you enter your affiliation or company name if applicable.
###  **Name of the Event** (`event_name`, text, required)
This field is where you enter the name of the event where your presentation or session takes place.
###  **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the event where your presentation or session takes place.
###  **Title of Presentation or Session** (`session_title`, text, required)
Enter the title of your presentation or session.
###  **Do you grant permission to record your session?** (`permission_to_record`, select_one, required)
Choose whether you grant permission to record your session. You can choose to record your full session or record everything except the Q&A portion.
###  **Permission to share the recording with event attendees** (`permission_to_share`, select_one, required)
Indicate whether you grant permission to share the recording with event attendees.
###  **Permission to use highlights for future event marketing** (`permission_marketing`, select_one, required)
Choose whether you grant permission to use snippets of your recording for future event marketing.
###  **Any specific restrictions or conditions for the recording?** (`specific_restrictions`, text, optional)
If you have any specific restrictions or conditions for the recording, enter them here.
###  **Email Address for Confirmation** (`contact_email`, email, required)
Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
###  **Presenter Signature (Type Name)** (`digital_signature`, text, required)
Sign the form with your name as the presenter.
###  **Date Signed** (`agreement_date`, date, required)
Date the form after signing.
