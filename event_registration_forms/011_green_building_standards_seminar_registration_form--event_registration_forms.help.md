# Green Building Standards Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Green Building Standards Seminar. The purpose of this form is to help us prepare for the seminar by gathering essential details about the registrants, such as their name, email, phone number, and participant information. This information will be used to communicate with registrants and tailor the seminar experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the registration information on the first page.
3. Review and submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Registration Info
* **Registration Info** (Text): This field is used to provide a brief description of your registration purpose or any special requests you may have for the seminar.

### 2. Name
* **Name** (Text): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on any seminar materials or correspondence.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address where you would like to receive seminar updates, reminders, and other relevant communications.

### 4. Phone
* **Phone** (Text): Provide your phone number so we can contact you with important updates or last-minute seminar information.

### 5. Participant Info
* **Participant Info** (Text): This field is optional and should be used to provide any additional information about your participation in the seminar, such as your role or any specific goals or objectives you hope to achieve.

### Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We look forward to seeing you at the seminar!
