# veterinary_pharmaceutical_demo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The veterinary pharmaceutical demo registration form is a crucial tool for ensuring a smooth and productive demo session for veterinary pharmaceuticals. By providing accurate and complete information, you will receive relevant and timely updates about the demo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2.  Enter your address in the "address" field (if available).
3.  Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (if available).
5.  Select your preferred demo options from the "demo_preferences" field.
6.  Choose a demo time that suits you best in the "demo_time" field.
7.  Add any additional notes in the "note" field (if necessary).
8.  Confirm your email address in the "confirm_email" field.
9.  Confirm your phone number in the "confirm_phone" field.
10.  Select or enter the assigned tool for the demo session in the "assigned_tool" field.
11.  If you have chosen "Other" in the "assigned_tool" field, please provide more details in the "assigned_tool_other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): This field is not used in the form, it seems to be a placeholder for the form name.
*   **name** (`name`, text, true): Please enter your name to identify yourself during the demo session.
*   **address** (`address`, text, false): This field is optional, but if you provide your address, we may use it to contact you for further information.
*   **email** (`email`, email, true): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the demo session.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, false): This field is optional, but if you provide your phone number, we may use it to contact you for further information.
*   **demo_preferences** (`demo_preferences`, select multiple, true): Choose your preferred demo options to customize your experience.
*   **demo_time** (`demo_time`, time, true): Select a demo time that suits you best to avoid conflicts.
*   **note** (`note`, note, false): Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
*   **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, email, true): Confirm your email address to ensure accurate record-keeping.
*   **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, true): Confirm your phone number to avoid errors.
*   **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select one, true): Select a tool to be used during the demo session.
*   **assigned_tool_other** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, false): If you have chosen "Other" in the "assigned_tool" field, please provide more details about the tool you have in mind.

## Tips
*   Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to avoid confusion and miscommunication during the demo session.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to having you at the demo session.
