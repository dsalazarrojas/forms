# Cultural Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Program Subscription Form is designed to collect information from users about their subscription to a cultural program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact the program administrators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your personal email address.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address (if different from your personal email).
* **Subscription Date** (`subscription_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to start your subscription to the cultural program.
* **Subscription Time** (`subscription_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time frame of your subscription to the cultural program.
* **Subscription Choice 1** (`user_subscription_choice_1`, select one, required/optional): Select your first choice from the available options.
* **Subscription Choice 2** (`user_subscription_choice_2`, select one, required/optional): Select your second choice from the available options.
* **Subscription Ongoing** (`subscription_ongoing`, select multiple, required/optional): Select any ongoing subscriptions or options you would like to have.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share.
* **Subscription Frequency Other** (`subscription_ongoing_other`, text, required/optional): If your preferred subscription frequency is not listed, please specify here.
* **Event Frequency** (`event_frequency`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the frequency of events you would like to attend.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share.

## Tips
- Please be as accurate as possible when filling out each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the program administrators for assistance.
- If you are filling out the form for multiple people, please make sure to submit each form separately.
- If you are unsure about which fields to fill out or have any other questions, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
