### Business Partners Appreciation Day RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Partners Appreciation Day RSVP form is designed to register business partners' attendance and provide information for event organizers to plan the event accordingly. The form collects details such as RSVP dates, attendee contact information, and preferences for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have all the required information before starting the form**.
2. Select the correct RSVP date for the event you are attending.
3. Fill in your name, company name, email address, and phone number accurately.
4. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes, I Will Attend" or "No, I Cannot Attend" from the options.
5. If attending, select the number of guests you will bring.
6. You may also add the names and titles of your guests if you are bringing any.
7. Select your meal preference and any dietary restrictions if applicable.
8. If necessary, specify any special accommodations needed.
9. Choose the number of years you have been in partnership and what you appreciate most about our partnership.
10. Select any activities that interest you during the event.
11. If attending with a plus-one, provide their name and email address.
12. Finally, mention any additional requests or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date you are planning to attend the event.
* **Your Name**: Fill in your full name as a representative of the company.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Email Address**: Provide your primary email contact information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title**: If you wish to disclose your job title (optional).
* **Event Date**: Confirm the event date for which you are RSVPing.
* **Will You Attend?**: Select "Yes, I Will Attend" or "No, I Cannot Attend" to confirm your attendance.
* **Number of Guests**: If attending, specify the number of guests you plan to bring.
* **Guest Details**: Provide the names and titles of any guests you are bringing (optional).
* **Meal Preference**: Select your preferred meal type (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Meat-based, Seafood).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Mention any dietary restrictions you have (optional).
* **Special Accommodations**: If you need any special accommodations, specify them here (optional).
* **Partnership Years**: Select the duration of your partnership with us (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, Over 10 years).
* **What You Appreciate Most**: Share what you appreciate most about our partnership (optional).
* **Activity Interests**: Select the activities that interest you during the event (Networking, Food and Drinks, Entertainment, Raffles/Prizes, Business Updates, Awards Recognition).
* **Plus-One Name**: Provide the name of any additional guest you are bringing (optional).
* **Plus-One Email**: Enter the email address of your plus-one guest (optional).
* **Additional Requests**: Mention any special requests or comments you may have.

Please note that some fields are optional and only required if applicable to your situation. Ensure you fill in all required fields to guarantee a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.
