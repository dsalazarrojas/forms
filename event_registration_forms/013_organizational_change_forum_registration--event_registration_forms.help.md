# Organizational Change Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual's interest and availability for a specific organizational change event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Select your role in the event (Yes/No)
5. Select your interests in the session (Yes/No)
6. Enter your Organization
7. Enter your Job Title
8. Enter your Company
9. Enter your Phone number
10. (Optional) Enter a note if you have any comments or questions.
11. Select the tool that is assigned to you (chatjimmy/Other)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): This is the first name of the person completing the form. Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): This is the last name of the person completing the form. Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, text, optional): This is the email address associated with the person completing the form. Enter your email address as it is registered in the system.
* **Role** (Role, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are attending or participating in the event, otherwise select "No".
* **Session Interests** (Session Interests, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending or participating in any part of the session, otherwise select "No".
* **Organization** (Organization, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are a part of. This can be your current or former employer.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your job title as it appears on your identification.
* **Company** (Company, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or former company.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Enter any comments or questions you have about the event.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): Select the tool that is assigned to you, either "chatjimmy" or "Other".
