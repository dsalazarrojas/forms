# Youth Leadership Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registering as a volunteer with the Youth Leadership Program. It collects essential information about you, including your contact details, volunteer schedule, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your details, making sure to provide accurate information where required.
2. Review each field to ensure you've completed it correctly.
3. Submit the form once you've completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (ID: 1)
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, as you'd like it to be displayed in the program's records.

### Last Name (ID: 2)
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, as you'd like it to be displayed in the program's records.

### Email (ID: 3)
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone (ID: 4)
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.

### Date of Birth (ID: 5)
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, which helps us understand your age and ensure program suitability.

### Address Line 1 (ID: 6)
* **Address Line 1** (`Address Line 1`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address for delivery or other contact purposes.

### Address Line 2 (ID: 7)
* **Address Line 2** (`Address Line 2`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address's secondary line.

### City (ID: 8)
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside or work.

### State (ID: 9)
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the state or province where you reside or work.

### Zip Code (ID: 10)
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, optional): Enter your postal code for mailing purposes.

### Volunteer Hours (ID: 11)
* **Volunteer Hours** (`Volunteer Hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of volunteer hours you can commit per week.

### Program Name (ID: 12)
* **Program Name** (`Program Name`, text, optional): Enter the specific program you'd like to volunteer for (if applicable).

### Role (ID: 13)
* **Role** (`Role`, text, optional): Describe your role or position within the program (if applicable).

### Availability (ID: 14)
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select multiple, optional): Select the times of day you're available to volunteer (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).

### Volunteer Start Date (ID: 15)
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`Volunteer Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to start volunteering.

### Volunteer End Date (ID: 16)
* **Volunteer End Date** (`Volunteer End Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to end your volunteer commitment.

### Volunteer Status (ID: 17)
* **Volunteer Status** (`Volunteer Status`, select one, optional): Select your current volunteer status (Active, Pending, Inactive).

### Emergency Contact Name (ID: 18)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone (ID: 19)
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Email (ID: 20)
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.

### Relationship (ID: 21)
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.

### Volunteer Reference (ID: 22)
* **Volunteer Reference** (`Volunteer Reference`, text, optional): Enter any relevant volunteer experience or references.

### Reference Provider (ID: 23)
* **Reference Provider** (`Reference Provider`, text, optional): Describe where you obtained your volunteer reference.

### Medical Condition (ID: 24)
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions we should be aware of for your safe participation in the program.

### Additional Comments (ID: 25)
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your application.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you've filled out all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program's administration for assistance.
