# Annual General Meeting Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Annual General Meeting Reservation Form. The form is designed to gather essential information from guests, including their contact details, attendance preferences, and special requirements. By filling out this form, you will be able to confirm your attendance and provide valuable input to help us cater to your needs during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your guest information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Confirm your attendance by selecting the correct option from the "Will you attend the AGM" dropdown menu.
3. Specify the size of your group, if applicable.
4. If you're bringing additional guests, list their names in the designated field.
5. Provide your preferred seating arrangement, if any.
6. Choose your meal service preference, including meal options and dietary restrictions.
7. Select any beverages you'd like to have available during the event.
8. If you have any special needs or accommodations, please let us know in the designated field.
9. Finally, add any additional notes or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, **required**): Enter your email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, **required**): Provide your contact number for any last-minute communication.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`Organization Affiliation`, text, optional): If you're representing an organization, please enter your affiliation or role.
* **Member Status** (`Member Status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current membership status:
	+ Active Member
	+ Associate Member
	+ Emeritus Member
	+ Prospective Member
	+ Guest of Member
	+ Other
* **Will you attend the AGM** (`Will you attend the AGM`, select_one, **required**): Confirm your attendance:
	+ Yes, I will attend
	+ No, I cannot attend
	+ Maybe - Still deciding
* **Party Size** (`Party Size`, number, **required**): Specify the number of people in your group.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (`Names of Additional Guests`, text, optional): If bringing others, list their names.
* **Meeting Date** (`Meeting Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`Meeting Time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`Meeting Location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Seating Preference** (`Seating Preference`, text, optional): If you have a specific seating preference, please let us know.
* **Accessibility or Special Accommodations** (`Accessibility or Special Accommodations`, text, optional): If you have any special needs, please provide details.
* **Meals Service** (`Meals Service`, select_one, **required**): Choose your meal preference:
	+ Yes, including meal
	+ No, no meal
	+ Just appetizers
	+ Unsure
* **Entrée Selection** (`Entrée Selection`, select_one, optional): Choose your main course preference:
	+ Chicken Dinner
	+ Beef Dinner
	+ Fish Dinner
	+ Vegetarian Option
	+ Vegan Option
	+ Other
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`Dietary Restrictions or Allergies`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please let us know.
* **Beverages** (`Beverages`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred beverages:
	+ Red Wine
	+ White Wine
	+ Beer
	+ Soft Drinks
	+ Non-Alcoholic Only
	+ Coffee or Tea
* **Additional Notes or Requests** (`Additional Notes or Requests`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or requests, please provide them here.
