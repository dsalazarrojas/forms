# Military Warrior Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Warrior Registration form is designed for military personnel to provide their personal details, service history, and emergency contact information for administrative and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including first name, last name, email, phone, date of birth, and military ID.
2. Select your service branch and the number of years you were active in the military.
3. Enter the date you enlisted in the military.
4. If applicable, provide a brief service history.
5. For emergency contact details, select the relationship (spouse, parent, child, sibling, or other) and provide their phone number, email, address, and relationship details (if necessary).
6. If required, provide additional service branch, years active, date of enlistment, and service history information.
7. Enter any medical history details.
8. Confirm your emergency contact information for each relationship.
