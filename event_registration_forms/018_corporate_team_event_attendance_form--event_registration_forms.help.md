# Corporate Team Event Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Corporate Team Event Attendance Form is used to register attendees for corporate events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please navigate through the pages of the form, each page will ask for specific information.
2. For each page, review the provided fields and enter the required information.
3. Please select the correct event from the provided options.
4. Enter the required information for each field, making sure to enter accurate data.
5. Before submitting the form, review your responses for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the company you represent.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Event** (`Event`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the event you are attending.
* **Attendees Count** (`Attendees Count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of attendees from your company.
* **Attendees Names** (`Attendees Names`, `note`, required/optional): Enter the names of the attendees.
* **Attendees Company** (`Attendees Company`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the companies of the attendees.
* **Attendees Title** (`Attendees Title`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the title for each attendee (Mr, Mrs, Ms).
* **Attendees Department** (`Attendees Department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of the attendees.
* **Attendees Job Title** (`Attendees Job Title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the attendees.
* **RSVP by** (`RSVP by`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date by which you are RSVPing.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVP Time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time by which you are RSVPing.
* **Message** (`Message`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your attendance.

## Tips

* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to enter the correct event and select the correct options.
* Review your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
