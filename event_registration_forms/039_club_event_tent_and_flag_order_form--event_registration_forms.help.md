# Club Event Tent And Flag Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Event Tent And Flag Order Form is designed for clubs or organizations to request tent and flag services for their events. This form collects essential information about the event, the club or organization, and the requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact person's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the name of your club or organization and the name of the event.
3. Enter the date and time of the event.
4. Specify the expected number of attendees.
5. Decide on tent requirements, including whether you need a tent, the number of tents, preferred size, and type.
6. Choose the type and color of flags you need.
7. Indicate whether you need delivery and setup service for the tent and flags.
8. Confirm if you require any extra protection measures, such as wind bracing or sandbags.
