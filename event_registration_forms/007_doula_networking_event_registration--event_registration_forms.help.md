# Doula Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Networking Event Registration form is designed for registrants to provide their personal information and demographic details to facilitate networking opportunities at doulas-related events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field next to **Last Name**.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the correct format.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field provided.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth**, which should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Please provide a valid email address for communication and event updates.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date you were born in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/12/1990).
