# Milestone Celebration RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering your attendance to a Milestone Celebration event. It will collect your details and preferences to help us prepare for the celebration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **attendee_name** (required if you're registering someone else).
2. Enter your **email** address (this will be used to contact you with updates).
3. Provide your **phone** number (optional, but recommended for easy contact).
4. Choose a **time** that suits you best for the celebration (if applicable).
5. Select a **date** for the celebration (if applicable).
6. Indicate if you will be **attending** the celebration.
7. Add any **comments** you'd like to share with the organiser.
8. If you have any **special events** you'd like to celebrate (e.g. anniversary, birthday, holiday), select them from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **attendee_name** (text, required/optional): Please fill in your name or the name of the person you're registering for.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about the celebration.
* **phone** (text, optional/optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **time** (time, optional/optional): Select a time that suits you best for the celebration (if applicable).
* **date** (date, optional/optional): Choose a date for the celebration (if applicable).
* **attending** (select_one, optional/optional): Indicate if you will be attending the celebration.
* **comments** (note, optional/optional): Share any comments or messages with the organiser.
* **special_events** (select_multiple, optional/optional): Select any special events you'd like to celebrate (e.g. anniversary, birthday, holiday).
