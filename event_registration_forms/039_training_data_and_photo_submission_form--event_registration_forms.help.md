# Training Data and Photo Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Data and Photo Submission Form is designed to collect metadata and photos from participants in various training programs. This form is used to record and track training data, such as participant information, training status, photo and video metadata, and program details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the corresponding information.
2. Upload photos and videos (if applicable) and fill in the associated metadata.
3. For fields with options, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Data** (ID: 1, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the training data.
* **Photo URL** (ID: 2, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the photo.
* **Photo Description** (ID: 3, Text, Optional): Enter a description of the photo.
* **Training Program** (ID: 4, Select One, Optional): Select the relevant training program.
* **Training Date** (ID: 5, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (ID: 6, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Training Duration** (ID: 7, Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the training session in minutes.
* **Training Status** (ID: 8, Select One, Optional): Select the current status of the training (Complete, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Participant Name** (ID: 9, Text, Optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (ID: 10, Email, Optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (ID: 11, Text, Optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (ID: 12, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
* **Training Location** (ID: 13, Text, Optional): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Venue** (ID: 14, Text, Optional): Enter the venue of the training session.
* **Training Type** (ID: 15, Text, Optional): Enter the type of training (e.g., classroom, online).
* **Training Level** (ID: 16, Number, Optional): Enter the level of the training (e.g., beginner, advanced).
* **Training Status Date** (ID: 17, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the training status update.
* **Training Status Time** (ID: 18, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the training status update.
* **Photo Tags** (ID: 19, Text, Optional): Enter tags for the photo.
* **Training Program Notes** (ID: 20, Text, Optional): Enter notes about the training program.
* **Participant Photo Notes** (ID: 21, Text, Optional): Enter notes about the participant's photo.
* **Participant Photo Tags** (ID: 22, Text, Optional): Enter tags for the participant's photo.
* **Participant Photo Description** (ID: 23, Text, Optional): Enter a description of the participant's photo.
* **Participant Video URL** (ID: 24, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the participant's video.
* **Participant Video Description** (ID: 25, Text, Optional): Enter a description of the participant's video.
