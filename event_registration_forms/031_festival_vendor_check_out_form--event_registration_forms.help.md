# Festival Vendor Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by festival vendors to confirm and update their details, including payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your vendor details in the "Vendor Details" field.
3. Select your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
4. Enter the payment date and time in the "Payment Date" and "Payment Time" fields.
5. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
7. Select the category of the form in the "Category" field.
8. Choose the festival vendor details you want to include in the "Festival Vendor Details" field.
9. Upload any supporting output file in the "Output File" field.
10. Enter any additional information in the "Description", "Title", "Form ID", "Form Title", "Form Description", "Form Category", and "Notes 1" fields.
11. Confirm your vendor signature in the "Festival Vendor Signature" field.
12. Select the payment method 1 and 2 in the "Payment Method 1" and "Payment Method 2" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details** (Vendor Details, text, required): Enter your festival vendor details.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, required): Enter your payment information.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Select the payment method you used for this transaction.
* **Payment Date** (Payment Date, date, required): Enter the payment date for this transaction.
* **Payment Time** (Payment Time, time, required): Enter the payment time for this transaction.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, required): Enter the payment amount for this transaction.
* **Vendor Signature** (Vendor Signature, note, required): Sign this form to confirm your details.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required): Enter the assigned tool for this transaction.
* **Output File** (Output File, text, required): Upload any supporting output file for this transaction.
* **Category** (Category, select_one, required): Select the category for this transaction.
* **Festival Vendor Details** (Festival Vendor Details, select_multiple, required): Choose which festival vendor details you want to include.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes for this transaction.
* **Description** (Description, text, required): Enter a brief description of this transaction.
* **Title** (Title, text, required): Enter a title for this transaction.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, text, required): Enter the form ID for this transaction.
* **Form Title** (Form Title, text, required): Enter the form title for this transaction.
* **Form Description** (Form Description, text, required): Enter the form description for this transaction.
* **Form Category** (Form Category, select_one, required): Select the category for this transaction.
* **Output File** (Output File, text, required): Upload any supporting output file for this transaction.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required): Enter the assigned tool for this transaction.
* **Notes 1** (Notes 1, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes for this transaction.
* **Form ID 2** (Form ID 2, text, required): Enter the form ID 2 for this transaction.
* **Festival Vendor Signature** (Festival Vendor Signature, note, required): Sign this form to confirm your details.
* **Payment Method 1** (Payment Method 1, select_one, required): Select the payment method 1 for this transaction.
* **Payment Method 2** (Payment Method 2, select_one, required): Select the payment method 2 for this transaction.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled in correctly.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you need help or clarification, contact your festival vendor support.
