# Restaurant Reservation Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from users to reserve a table in a restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") completely and accurately.
2. For optional fields, enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to your reservation.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it to the restaurant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
* **First Name**: This field is used to collect your first name.
* Type: `text`
* Required: No

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name**: This field is used to collect your last name.
* Type: `text`
* Required: No

### Email (3)
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address.
* Type: `email`
* Required: No

### Phone (4)
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number.
* Type: `text`
* Required: No

### Date (5)
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date of your reservation.
* Type: `date`
* Required: No

### Time (6)
* **Time**: This field is used to select the time of your reservation.
* Type: `time`
* Required: No

### Reservation Time (7)
* **Reservation Time**: This field is used to select the time you would like to reserve.
* Type: `time`
* Required: No

### Reservation Date (8)
* **Reservation Date**: This field is used to select the date of your reservation.
* Type: `date`
* Required: No

### Number of Guests (9)
* **Number of Guests**: This field is used to let the restaurant know how many people will be joining you for the reservation.
* Type: `number`
* Required: No

### Message (10)
* **Message**: This field is used to provide additional information or special requests to the restaurant.
* Type: `note`
* Required: No

## Tips
- Please ensure that the date and time selected are not past the current date and time.
- If you have any special requests or need any additional assistance, use the message field to let the restaurant know.
