# Sales Strategies Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants in the Sales Strategies Training program. It gathers information about the participant, their company, and payment details. The form is not meant for internal purposes only and will be used to track and manage participant information for the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Details** section:
	* Enter your participant name
	* Enter your email address
	* Enter your phone number
	* Enter your company
2. Select your **Sales Strategies Training** level from the options provided:
	* Basic Training
	* Advanced Training
	* Master Training
3. Answer the **Participant Agreement** questions:
	* Select the options that apply to you
4. Choose your **Payment Method**:
	* Bank Transfer
	* Credit Card
	* Cash
5. Fill in the **Payment Amount** field if required
6. Select your **Participant Status**:
	* Active
	* Inactive
7. Enter your **Participant Start Date** and **Participant End Date** if applicable
8. Fill in any additional **Participant Notes**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your work email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title.
* **Sales Manager** (`sales_manager`): Indicate if you have a sales manager.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): Fill in any additional comments or remarks about your registration.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Fill in the payment amount if required.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`): Select your current status in the program.
* **Participant Start Date** (`participant_start_date`): Enter your start date in the program.
* **Participant End Date** (`participant_end_date`): Enter your end date in the program.
* **Participant Created At** (`participant_created_at`): This field will be automatically populated upon registration.
* **Participant Updated At** (`participant_updated_at`): This field will be automatically populated upon each update.
