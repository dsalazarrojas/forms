# tech_demo_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "tech_demo_appointment_form" form is designed for users to register for a technical demonstration or meeting, providing organizers with essential information to prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Specify your Date and Time preferences.
3. Choose your preferred Demo Interests (demo1, demo2, demo3).
4. Select your Preferred Language (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
5. Choose your Preferred Cuisine (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan).
6. Choose the type of Demo (Online, In-Person, Hybrid).
7. Enter Additional Info if needed.
8. Specify the Demo Date and Time.
9. Enter Meeting Location, Meeting Room, and Meeting Duration.
10. Enter Demo Location.
11. Enter Company Name, Company URL, Contact Number, and Contact Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date**: Select a date for the demo or meeting.
* **Time**: Select a time for the demo or meeting.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select your preferred time slots (Yes, No).
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Role**: Enter your role.
* **Demo Interests**: Select your demo interests (demo1, demo2, demo3).
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, French, Chinese).
* **Preferred Cuisine**: Select your preferred cuisine (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan).
* **Demo Type**: Select the type of demo (Online, In-Person, Hybrid).
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information for the demo or meeting.
* **Demo Date**: Select a date for the demo.
* **Demo Time**: Select a time for the demo.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the meeting location.
* **Meeting Room**: Enter the meeting room.
* **Meeting Duration**: Enter the meeting duration.
* **Demo Location**: Enter the demo location.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Company URL**: Enter your company URL.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.

## Tips

- Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your preferred date and time selections.
- Choose your preferred language and cuisine for a better demo or meeting experience.
- Enter any additional information that will help the demo or meeting organizer understand your needs.
