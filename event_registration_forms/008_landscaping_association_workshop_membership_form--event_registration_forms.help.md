# Landscaping Association Workshop Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for individuals who wish to join the Landscaping Association Workshop. The information collected will be used for internal tracking and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the "Membership Application" page.
2. On the next page, select your workshop sign-ups.
3. Enter your contact information, including Name, Email, Phone, and Address.
4. Provide details about your membership type, member since, and member until dates.
5. If you are planning to attend a workshop, provide the workshop date and time, location, and whether or not you will be attending.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your workshop attendance.
7. Finally, review and accept the terms and conditions by selecting "True" under "Accept Terms" and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Application**: Provide a short description of why you are interested in joining the workshop.
* **Workshop Sign-ups**: Select one or multiple workshops you are interested in attending.
	+ This field is used to track which workshops you have signed up for.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Enter your date of membership.
* **Time**: Enter your time of membership.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your membership application.
* **Membership Type**: Select your membership type (Membership, Associate, Regular, or Honorary).
* **Member Since**: Enter the date you became a member.
* **Member Until**: Enter the date you plan to be a member until.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country.
* **Workshop Attendance**: Select whether or not you will be attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Comments**: Add any additional comments about your workshop attendance.
* **Signature**: Add your signature.
* **Accept Terms**: Select "True" to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Submit the form to complete your membership application.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your membership application.
