# Wedding Planner Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an appointment with a wedding planner for a wedding or special event. It is designed to gather all the necessary information to properly plan and execute the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the main form with your initial information, including details about the couple and the event.
2. Review and confirm the details you entered in the main form.
3. Submit the form to finalize the appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Planning Form** (1): This is the main form where you can input your initial information about the couple and the event.
- **Couple Details** (2): Enter details about the couple, such as their names and other relevant information.
- **Appointment Date** (3): Choose the date for the appointment.
- **Appointment Time** (4): Choose the time for the appointment.
- **Event Type** (5): Choose the type of event as Wedding, Other, or Event.
- **Wedding Details** (6): Select multiple options from the dropdown list to specify the details of the event, such as Ceremony and Reception.
- **Notes** (7): Input any additional notes or comments about the event.
- **Contact Info** (8): Enter contact information for the couple.
- **Email** (9): Enter the email address of the couple.
- **Phone** (10): Enter the phone number of the couple.
- **Submit Form** (11): Choose to submit the form or cancel.
- **Confirm Appointment** (12): Confirm whether you want to book the appointment.
- **Confirm Appointment Date** (13): Confirm the date of the appointment.
- **Confirm Appointment Time** (14): Confirm the time of the appointment.
- **Confirm Event Type** (15): Confirm the type of event.
- **Confirm Event Description** (16): Confirm the description of the event, such as Ceremony and Reception.
- **Confirm Couple Details** (17): Confirm the details about the couple.
- **Confirm Couple Name** (18): Confirm the name of the couple.
- **Confirm Event Location** (19): Confirm the location of the event.
- **Confirm Event Date** (20): Confirm the date of the event.
- **Confirm Event Time** (21): Confirm the time of the event.
- **Confirm Notes** (22): Confirm any additional notes or comments about the event.
- **Confirm Submit Form** (23): Choose to confirm and submit the form.

Note: This form is designed to ensure that all necessary information is captured to properly plan and execute the event.
