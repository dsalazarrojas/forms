# win-event-tickets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about event tickets from users, helping event organizers to gather essential details about attendee preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your user information (name and contact details) in the "User Info" section.
2. Enter the event-related information in the "Event Info" section.
3. Share your contact details in the "Contact Info" section.
4. Choose the type of ticket(s) you'd like to purchase (Single, Double, or Couple ticket).
5. Select the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" sections, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (1)**:
	+ **Name** (`<name>`, Text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
	+ **Contact Info** (`<contact_info>`, Text, required: false): Provide your contact information to ensure we can reach you about your ticket purchase.
* **Event Info (2)**:
	+ **event-info** (`<event_info>`, Text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event you'd like to attend.
* **Contact Info (3)**:
	+ **Contact Info** (`<contact_info>`, Text, required: false): Provide your contact information to ensure we can reach you about your ticket purchase.
* **Ticket Type (4)**:
	+ **Select a ticket type** (`<ticket_type>`, Select Multiple, required: false): Choose the type of ticket you'd like to purchase:
		- Single ticket
		- Double ticket
		- Couple ticket
* **Event Date (5)**:
	+ **Event Date** (`<event_date>`, Date, required: false): Select the date of the event you'd like to attend.
* **Event Time (6)**:
	+ **Event Time** (`<event_time>`, Time, required: false): Choose the time of the event you'd like to attend.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
