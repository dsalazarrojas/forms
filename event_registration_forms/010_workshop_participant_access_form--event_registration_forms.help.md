# Workshop Participant Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Participant Access Form is designed for workshop participants to provide their details and access the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Details on page 1 of the form.
2. Provide your Workshop Details, which will help us understand your involvement in the workshop.
3. Enter your Email address, which we will use to contact you about the workshop.
4. Enter your Phone number, which we will use for further contact.
5. Add any additional Notes about your involvement or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (participant_details): This is the main section for you to input your name and other identifying details.
  - **Workshop Details** (workshop_details): This section is for you to provide any relevant details about your involvement in the workshop.
  - **Email** (email): Please enter your valid email address so that we can contact you about the workshop.
  - **Phone** (phone): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion.
  - **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or requirements you have about your participation in the workshop.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you have any issues or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
- We will review and process your submission shortly after receiving it.
- You will receive further instructions or confirmation via email about your workshop involvement.
