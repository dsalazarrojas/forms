# Giveaway_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect registrant information and event details for a giveaway event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your registrant name in the "Registrant Name" field. This is the name you'd like to be recognized as in the event.

2.  Enter a valid email address in the "Prophet's Email" field. This is the email address we will contact you with regarding the giveaway event.

3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will allow us to reach out to you if any further questions or details regarding the event.

4.  Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field. This is the date when the event will be held.

5.  Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field. This is the time when the event will be held.

6.  Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field. This will be the place where the event will take place.

7.  In the "Registrant Details" field, you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation in the giveaway event.

8.  In the "Further Information" field, you can provide any additional details that you feel are important for us to know for the giveaway event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Name** (text, required): Enter your name that you would like to be recognized as in the event.
* **Prophet's Email** (email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with details regarding the event.
* **Phone** (text, required): This is your phone number where we will contact you to get further information regarding the giveaway event.
*   **Event Date** (date, required):  Enter the date of the giveaway event.
*   **Event Time** (time, required):  Enter the time of the giveaway event.
*   **Event Location** (text, required):  Enter the place where the event will be held.
*   **Registrant Details** (text, optional):  Enter any additional relevant details about yourself.
*   **Further Information** (text, optional): Enter any other relevant information regarding your participation in the event.
