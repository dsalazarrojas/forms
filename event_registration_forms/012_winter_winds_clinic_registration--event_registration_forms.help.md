# winter_winds_clinic_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The winter_winds_clinic_registration form is designed to collect essential information from students interested in registering for the Winter Winds Clinic program. This guide will walk you through each field, making it easier to provide accurate and complete responses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through each field explanation below to ensure you understand what information is needed.
* Fill out the form with the required details.
* Review your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for clinic updates and communications.
* **Instrument (instrument)**: Choose the instrument you want to focus on during the Winter Winds Clinic. You can select from:
	+ Alto Saxophone
	+ Clarinet
	+ Flute
	+ French Horn
	+ Oboe
* **School (school)**: Enter the name of your school or institution where you are currently enrolled or affiliated with.
* **Grade Level (grade_level)**: If applicable, select your current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **Parent Name (parent_name)**: If you are under 18, please enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Consent**: This is a confirmation that you have read and understand the terms of the Winter Winds Clinic program. Check this box to indicate your consent.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form, especially with your email address and school/instrument information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities.

Please review your responses carefully before submitting the form. If you need further assistance, feel free to reach out to the relevant authorities.
