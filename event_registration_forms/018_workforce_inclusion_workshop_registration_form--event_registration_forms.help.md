# Workforce Inclusion Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Inclusion Workshop Registration Form is designed to gather registration information from individuals attending the workforce inclusion workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the **Registration Form** section (page 1).
2. In the **Registration Form** section, you will need to fill out the following fields:
	* **Name**: Enter your name.
	* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Role**: Select your role from the available options (Participant, Facilitator, Organizer).
3. Move on to the **Workshop Details** section (page 2).
4. In the **Workshop Details** section, you will need to fill out the following fields:
	* **Workshop Date**: Select a date for the workshop.
	* **Workshop Time**: Select a time for the workshop.
	* **Event Time**: This field is not applicable for this form.
