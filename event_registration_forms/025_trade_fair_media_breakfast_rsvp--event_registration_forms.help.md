# Trade Fair Media Breakfast RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to register attendees and guests for the Trade Fair Media Breakfast event. It is used to capture essential information from attendees and guests to ensure that they are registered and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Indicate whether you are attending the event by selecting "True" from the "Are you attending?" field.
3. If you are attending, please indicate the number of guests you are bringing and enter their name(s) in the "Guest's Name" field(s).
4. Choose the media type(s) you represent (Print, Digital, Social Media).
5. Select your contact method (Phone, Email, None).
6. If you are representing an organization, enter the organization name and job title.
7. If you have a social media presence, enter the URL.
8. If you have a photo release, indicate whether you grant permission for your photo to be taken.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes.
10. Finally, confirm whether you are registered for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
* **Are you attending?** (`True/False`): Select "True" if you are attending the event.
* **Guest's Name**: Enter the name(s) of your guest(s).
* **Guest's attending** (`True/False`): Select "True" for each guest you are bringing.
* **Dietary**: Choose the dietary options that you require (Gluten-free, Vegan, Vegetarian, None).
* **Special dietary needs**: Enter any special dietary needs you have.
* **Number of guests**: Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Expected arrival time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive.
* **Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, None).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Prophets Name**: This field is likely meant to be a typo and is not relevant to the form.
* **Media Type**: Choose the media type(s) you represent (Print, Digital, Social Media).
* **Social Media**: Enter your social media URL.

* **Photo URL**: This field is likely a typo and is not relevant to the form.
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Photo Release**: Indicate whether you grant permission for your photo to be taken.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes you have.

Note: The fields with typos (e.g., "Prophets Name", "Social Media URL", "Photo URL", "Assigned Tool", "Company", "Job Title", "Contact Method") will not be included in the final form, as they are not relevant to the event registration process.
