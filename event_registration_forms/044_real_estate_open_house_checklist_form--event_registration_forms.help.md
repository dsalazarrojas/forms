# real_estate_open_house_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about real estate open houses.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the form to start filling it out.
- Provide the required information for each field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter a descriptive name for the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the address or location of the open house.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the open house.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the open house.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the open house.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the open house.
* **Address 1** (`address_1`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the open house or property.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, required: false): Enter a secondary address for the open house or property. (This could be an alternate address for parking, etc.)
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for the event.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact number of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the event.
* **Visitor List** (`visitor_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the event has a visitor list, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if the event is a real estate open house, or 'No' otherwise.
