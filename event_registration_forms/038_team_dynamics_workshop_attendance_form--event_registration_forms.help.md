# team_dynamics_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register their attendance to a team dynamics workshop. It gathers information about the participant, such as their name, email, and phone number, as well as details about the workshop, like date, time, and location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information on the first page.
2. Select the workshop date from the calendar.
3. Choose the workshop time from the available options.
4. Enter the workshop location.
5. Specify the number of workshop leaders.
6. Enter the participant's name.
7. Enter the participant's email address.
8. Enter the participant's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter the participant's details. Please fill in the participant's name, email, and phone number.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop from the calendar. Choose a date that works best for you.
* **Workshop Time**: Choose the time of the workshop from the available options. Select the time that suits you best.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop where it will take place.
* **Workshop Leaders**: Specify the number of workshop leaders who will be leading the workshop.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant as it appears on their identity document.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address. This is the email address we will use to contact you about the workshop.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number. This is the phone number we will use to contact you about the workshop.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the contact information provided.
