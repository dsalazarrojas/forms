# Guess The Jar Game Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guess The Jar Game Entry Form is designed to collect entry information from participants in the Guess The Jar game. This includes competition details, entry options, and contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Guess The Jar Game Entry Form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Entry Details" section.
2. Enter your entry details as requested. Note that this field is not mandatory.
3. Click on the "Competition Details" section.
4. Enter your competition details as requested. Note that this field is not mandatory.
5. Click on the "Entry Options" section.
6. Enter your entry options as requested. Note that this field is not mandatory.
7. Click on the "Contact Details" section.
8. Enter your contact details as requested. Note that this field is not mandatory.
9. Review your entries before submitting the form.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Entry Details** (Entry Details, text, optional): This field is where you'll enter a brief description of your entry.
- **Competition Details** (Competition Details, text, optional): This field is where you'll enter your competition details, if applicable.
- **Entry Options** (Entry Options, text, optional): This field is where you'll enter your entry options, if applicable.
- **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional): This field is where you'll enter your contact details, including your name and email address. Please note that entering your contact details is essential to receive updates and notifications from the game administrators.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required information.
- Review your entries before submitting the form.
- Ensure that your contact details are up-to-date and accurate.
