# Mentors Mentorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Mentors Mentorship Registration Form, which is used to collect information about potential mentors interested in participating in a mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Determine whether you are interested in participating in a mentorship program and select the correct option in the "Mentorship Program" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name Of Mentor" field.
3. Enter the name of the person you are looking to mentor, if applicable, in the "Name Of Mentee" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
6. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
7. Select the dates you are available to mentor in the "Available Dates" field.
8. Select the time slot you are available to mentor in the "Available Time" field.
9. Choose the duration of the mentorship in the "Mentorship Duration" field.
10. Select the frequency of the mentorship sessions in the "Mentorship Frequency" field.
11. For each question in the "Mentorship Topics" fieldset, select the topics in which you are interested in mentoring.
12. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mentorship Program**: This field is used to determine whether you are interested in participating in a mentorship program. Select "Yes" if you are interested, and "No" if you are not.
* **Name Of Mentor**: This field is used to enter your name as the mentor.
* **Name Of Mentee**: This field is used to enter the name of the person you are looking to mentor.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address, which will be used to contact you about the mentorship program.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to enter your phone number, which is optional.
* **Date Of Birth**: This field is used to enter your date of birth.
* **Available Dates**: This field is used to select the dates you are available to mentor.
* **Available Time**: This field is used to select the time slot you are available to mentor.
* **Mentorship Duration**: This field is used to select the duration of the mentorship.
* **Mentorship Frequency**: This field is used to select the frequency of the mentorship sessions.
* **Mentorship Topics**: This fieldset is used to select the topics in which you are interested in mentoring. Please select "Yes" or "No" for each topic.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you have any conflicts or questions, please contact us at [insert email or contact info].
* Once you have submitted your form, you will receive a confirmation email with more information about the mentorship program.
