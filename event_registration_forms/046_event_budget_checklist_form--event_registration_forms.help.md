# Event Budget Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event planners estimate and manage the financial aspects of an event. It's intended to collect information on various costs associated with the event, such as venue, catering, and marketing costs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each page as needed, following the instructions for each field. You can complete the form in any order, as the fields are independent sections of the overall budget estimate. When you're finished, the collected data should provide a comprehensive picture of the event's total costs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue Details (Text)**: Please enter a description of the venue where the event will take place. This can include information such as the name, address, and any other relevant details about the venue.
* **Venue Cost (Number)**: Enter the estimated cost of renting or booking the venue. This should include any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Venue Capacity (Number)**: Enter the maximum capacity of the venue, including any limitations or restrictions.
* **Catering Details (Text)**: Describe the catering services you plan to provide or arrange for the event. This can include information about food options, beverage services, and any other relevant details.
* **Catering Cost (Number)**: Enter the estimated cost of catering services, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Entertainment Details (Text)**: Describe the entertainment or activities planned for the event, such as music, speakers, or other attractions.
* **Entertainment Cost (Number)**: Enter the estimated cost of entertainment or activities, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Marketing Details (Text)**: Describe the marketing efforts or promotions planned for the event, such as social media, advertising, or publicity events.
* **Marketing Cost (Number)**: Enter the estimated cost of marketing efforts, including any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Total Cost (Number)**: This field will automatically calculate the total estimated cost of the event, based on the information entered in the previous fields.
