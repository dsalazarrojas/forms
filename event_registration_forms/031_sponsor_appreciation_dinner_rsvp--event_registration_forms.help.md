# Sponsor Appreciation Dinner RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from sponsors to RSVP to a sponsor appreciation dinner event. This information will be used to plan and organize the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field. This will allow us to get back in touch with you if needed.
4. Indicate the number of guests you are bringing in the "Guest Count" field. If you are attending solo, enter "1".
5. Enter the names of your guests in the "Guest Names" field. Use commas to separate multiple names.
6. If any of your guests have dietary restrictions, list them in the "Guest Dietary Restrictions" field.
7. Choose "Yes" or "No" in the "RSVP" field to indicate whether you will be attending the event.
8. If you have any additional message or comments, enter them in the "Message" field.
9. Enter the date you plan to RSVP in the "RSVP Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
10. Enter the time you plan to RSVP in the "RSVP Time" field in the format HH - MM AM/PM.
11. Enter any final message or comments in the "RSVP Message" field.
12. If you want to provide a separate date for the event, enter it in the "Date" field. This is optional.
13. If you want to provide a separate time for the event, enter it in the "Time" field. This is optional.
14. Finally, indicate the status of your RSVP (e.g., "Yes" or "No") in the "RSVP Status" field. Note that this field is not required and is primarily used for internal purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email here. This will allow us to get back in touch with you if needed.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required): Indicate the number of guests you are bringing. If you are attending solo, enter "1".
* **Guest Names** (`guest_names`, text, required): Enter the names of your guests here, separated by commas.
* **Guest Dietary Restrictions** (`guest_dietary_restrictions`, text, required): If any of your guests have dietary restrictions, list them here.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you are attending the event, "No" otherwise.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message or comments here.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to RSVP in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to RSVP in the format HH - MM AM/PM.
* **RSVP Message** (`rsvp_message`, text, required): Enter any final message or comments here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter any separate date for the event if needed. This is optional.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter any separate time for the event if needed. This is optional.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of your RSVP (e.g., "Yes" or "No"). This is not required and is primarily used for internal purposes.
