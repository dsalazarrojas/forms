# Charity Concert Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Concert Registration Form is designed to gather information from attendees and volunteers for the charity concert event. It helps with registration, ticketing, and event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Click on the "Register" button to start the form.
2. Fill in your name and email address for ticket delivery and updates.
3. Select your ticket type, including General Admission, VIP, or Backstage options.
4. Choose the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
5. Confirm your payment status, either as Paid, Pending, or Reserved.
6. Indicate your interest in volunteering at the event (optional).
7. Provide any special accommodation requests (optional).
8. Add an optional additional donation amount.
9. Read and agree to the event terms of service (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Name
 **attendee_name** (Text, Required): Your full name as it appears on your ticket.

### Email
 **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address to receive ticket delivery and event updates.

### Ticket Type
 **ticket_type** (Select, Required): Choose the type of ticket you prefer: General Admission, VIP, or Backstage.

### Ticket Quantity
 **ticket_quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.

### Payment Status
 **payment_status** (Select, Required): Confirm your payment status: Paid, Pending, or Reserved.

### Volunteer Interest
 **volunteer_interest** (Select, Optional): If you're interested in volunteering, select True; otherwise, choose False or Maybe.

### Special Accommodations
 **special_accommodations** (Text, Optional): If you need any accessibility or seating requests, provide details here.

### Donation Addon
 **donation_addon** (Number, Optional): You can make an optional additional donation to the charity.

### Consent Terms
 **consent_terms** (Select, Required): Read and agree to the event policies and code of conduct.
