# Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from participants of an event, including their contact details, emergency contact information, and check-in times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. If you have an emergency contact person, fill in their name and contact information in the "emergency_contact" and "emergency_contact_name" fields.
4. Check in and check out times for the event can be entered in the "check_in_time" and "check_out_time" fields.
5. If you have a contact person who will be available on-site, fill in their name and contact information in the "on_site_contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional: no): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional: no): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional: no): Enter your email address in the format of `example@example.com`.
* **on_site_contact** (`on_site_contact`, text, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the contact person who will be available on-site.
* **check_in_time** (`check_in_time`, time, required/optional: no): Enter your check-in time for the event.
* **check_out_time** (`check_out_time`, time, required/optional: no): Enter your check-out time for the event.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **on_site_contact** (`on_site_contact`, text, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the contact person who will be available on-site.
* **on_site_contact** (`on_site_contact`, text, required/optional: no): This field is redundant, please ignore.
 
Note: The form will automatically save your progress, so you can complete it at your own pace. If you need to revisit or edit any information, you can do so at any time.
