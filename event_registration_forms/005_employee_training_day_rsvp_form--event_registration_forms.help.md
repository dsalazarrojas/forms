# Employee Training Day RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and gather information from employees who are interested in attending a training session. It's a simple text-based form that should be completed by employees, not managers or other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Employee Training Day RSVP Form" page to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field sequentially, from top to bottom.
3. Make sure to provide as much information as possible, especially for fields that are marked as required.
4. Once you've completed the form, click on the "Submit" button to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Training Day RSVP Form** (`employee_training_day_rsvp_form_page_1`, text, optional): This is the first field where you can type in your name or any other information you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): In this field, please provide any details you'd like about yourself, such as your title, department, or any other relevant information.
* **Training Session Details** (`training_session_details`, text, optional): Here, you can provide any details about the training session you're interested in attending, such as which topics you're interested in or any specific questions you have.
* **RSVP Details** (`rsvp_details`, text, optional): In this field, you can provide any other information you'd like to share about your attendance, such as your dietary restrictions or any other specific needs.
