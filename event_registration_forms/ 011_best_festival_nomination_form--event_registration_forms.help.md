# Best Festival Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users to nominate a festival for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your full name in the **Full Name** field. This is your legal name as it appears on official documents.
2. Next, enter your email address in the **Email Address** field. This will be used for contact and communication purposes.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **Phone Number** field. Include your country code if you are outside your local region.
4. Select the relevant **Date** for the festival you are nominating.
5. Choose the **Primary Selection** type that best applies to your nomination.
6. If necessary, provide a whole number for the **Quantity or Rating** field.
7. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the **Additional Details** field.
8. Confirm your agreement by selecting from the options in the **Please Confirm Agreement** field.
9. If you have estimated value, enter it in the **Estimated Value** field in decimal format.
10. If scheduling is required, enter your **Preferred Time** in the format of `HH:MM am/pm`.
11. Finally, enter any **Final Comments or Notes** you have in the **Final Comments or Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including country code if outside your local region.
* **Date**: Select the relevant date for the festival you are nominating.
* **Primary Selection**: Choose the option that best applies to your nomination (Attendee, Exhibitor, Speaker).
* **Quantity or Rating**: Provide a whole number if necessary.
* **Additional Details**: Enter any additional information about your nomination.
* **Please Confirm Agreement**: Select all that apply.
* **Estimated Value**: Enter the estimated value in decimal format if applicable.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter a time in the format of `HH:MM am/pm` if scheduling is required.
* **Final Comments or Notes**: Enter any final comments or notes about your nomination.

## Tips

* Only nominate a festival that you are familiar with.
* Be aware that duplicate nominations may be rejected or removed.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth submission process.
