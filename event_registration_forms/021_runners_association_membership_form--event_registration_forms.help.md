# Runners Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for potential members of the Runners Association to submit their application and provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Application Form** field with a brief description of your interest in joining the association.
2. Fill out the **First Name** field with your first name.
3. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
4. Provide a valid **Email** address that we can use to contact you.
5. Enter your **Phone** number for added contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`Application Form`, text, optional): A text box for a brief description of your interest in joining the association.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): A field for your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): A field for your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): An email field for your contact email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): A text box for your phone number.
