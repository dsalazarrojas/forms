# Facility Rules And Regulations Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to acknowledge the facility rules and regulations. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you have a clear understanding of the rules and regulations of the facility.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the facility rules and regulations provided on the form.
2. Check the box next to "Acknowledge Rules" if you have read and understand the rules.
3. If applicable, provide your signature, date, and time of submission.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Add any comments or remarks, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Rules And Regulations**: This is a note field where you can view the facility rules and regulations. Please review the rules carefully before proceeding.
* **Acknowledge Rules**: Check this box if you have read and understand the rules. This is a required field.
* **Sign**: Provide your signature to acknowledge that you have read and understood the rules.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or remarks, if needed.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to review the facility rules and regulations before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the facility administration.
* This form should only be filled out by authorized personnel or individuals who have been granted access to the facility.
