# Tournament Volunteer Shirt Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from tournament volunteers to order their shirts after participating in the event. It's not meant for registration, but rather for shirt ordering purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your volunteer name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* Select the desired shirt size and quantity you need.
* If you participated in any events, select the event(s) you want to order a shirt for.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, you can add them in the Additional Comments field.
* Choose the order date and time you prefer for your order.
* Leave any fields blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name** (volunteer_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email** (volunteer_email, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Shirt Size** (shirt_size, select_one, optional): Choose the size of shirt you need, from Large to Extra Small.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, optional): Enter the number of shirts you need.
* **Event Name** (event_name, select_multiple, optional): Select the event(s) you participated in to order a shirt for. This will help us determine which event's logo to print on the shirt.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, optional): Choose the date when you'd like to receive your order.
* **Order Time** (order_time, time, optional): Choose the time of day when you prefer to receive your order.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, note, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, add them here.
 
## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we get your order right.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
