# Hunting Permit Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from hunters and their parents/guardians to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants in hunting events. It gathers details about the hunter's personal information, medical conditions, and experience with hunting, as well as waiver and consent from parents/guardians and the hunter themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for the type of permit you are seeking (Hunting permit, Waiver and consent, or Acknowledgement of risks).
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Answer the questions truthfully and carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hunting Permit Waiver Form** (Select One):
	+ Select the type of permit you are seeking. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Hunter Details** (Text):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Hunter Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Hunter Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Hunter Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Parent Guardian Consent** (Select One):
	+ Select whether you are 18 or older, or if you are a minor and need parental consent.
* **Acknowledgement of Risks** (Select Multiple):
	+ Acknowledge that you understand and accept the risks involved with hunting.
* **Event Details** (Text):
	+ Enter the date and location of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date** (Date):
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time):
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (Text):
	+ Enter the location of the event.
* **Organization Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the organization hosting the event.
* **Organization Address** (Text):
	+ Enter the address of the organization hosting the event.
* **Hunting Equipment** (Select Multiple):
	+ Select the type of hunting equipment you will be using.
* **Hunting License** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether you hold a valid hunting license.
* **Hunting Experience** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether you have experience with hunting.
* **Hunting Certification** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether you hold a hunting certification.
* **Medical Conditions** (Select Multiple):
	+ List any medical conditions you may have that could affect your ability to hunt.
* **Medical Conditions 2** (Select Multiple):
	+ List any additional medical conditions you may have that could affect your ability to hunt.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text):
	+ Enter the name and phone number of someone who can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (Text):
	+ Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Relationship** (Text):
	+ Explain your relationship to the child's parent/guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Permission** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether you have the parent/guardian's permission to participate in the event.
* **Waiver Acceptance** (Select One):
	+ Indicate whether you have accepted the waiver and consent.
* **Event Host Name** (Text):
	+ Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.

Note: This is not a real legal document and should not be used for actual hunting permits or waivers.
