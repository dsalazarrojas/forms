# Crew Accommodation Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for crew members to book their accommodation for travel-related events. It captures essential details such as contact information, event details, and accommodation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as **true**) with accurate information.
* For fields with options, select the relevant choice from the dropdown list.
* If you need to add any additional comments or information, use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and contact information (phone and email).
* **Contact Information**: Provide more detailed contact information, if necessary.
* **Contact Type**: Indicate if you are available to be contacted.
* **Name**: Enter your name for reference.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Date of Arrival**: Select the date you arrive at your destination.
* **Date of Departure**: Select the date you depart from your destination.
* **Travel Agency**: Select the travel agency managing your trip.
* **Event Details**: Provide any additional event-related information, if needed.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or requests for accommodation arrangements.
* **Travel Agency**: Select the travel agency managing your trip (required).
* **Crew Member Details**: Provide more information about the crew member, if necessary.
* **Crew Number**: Enter the number of crew members attending the event.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the accommodation type you require (e.g., hotel, hostel, etc.).
* **Accommodation Location**: Enter the location of your desired accommodation.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date you arrive at your accommodation.
* **Arrival Time**: Select the time you arrive at your accommodation.
* **Arrival Time AM/PM**: Indicate if the arrival time is AM or PM.
* **Accommodation Duration**: Enter the duration of your stay.
* **Accommodation Comments**: Add any additional comments about your accommodation preferences.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the accommodation type you require (required).
* **Submit**: Once you've filled in all the required fields, click "Submit" to finalize your accommodation booking.
