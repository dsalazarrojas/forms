# t_shirt_request_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for custom t-shirt requests for events. It ensures that each event participant's preferences are accurately recorded for order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
1. Enter a brief description of the event in the "Event Info" field.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be printed on your t-shirt.
3. Select your preferred t-shirt size: Large, Medium, Small, X-Large, or X-Small.
4. Choose the t-shirt color: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, or Black.
5. Select the type of design you would like on your t-shirt: Logo, Text, or a combination of the two (False/True).
6. If desired, enter your name as it should be printed on the t-shirt.
7. Enter your student ID (if applicable).
8. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info**: This field is where you describe the event you are participating in.
* **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be printed on your t-shirt. This will be used as the text for your name on the custom t-shirt.
* **shirt_size**: Select your preferred t-shirt size from the list.
* **shirt_color**: Choose your preferred t-shirt color.
* **shirt_design**: Select the type of design you would like on your t-shirt. Options include Logo, Text, or a combination of the two.
* **name_on_shirt**: If you have a preferred name to be printed on the t-shirt, enter it here. If left blank, the name you entered in the "name" field will be used.
* **student_id**: Enter your student ID if you are a student participating in the event. This is not required.
