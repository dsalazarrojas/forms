The Easter Egg Hunt Registration Form is a simple form designed to collect details from participants of the Easter Egg Hunt event. It is divided into several fields that will help us understand your registration requirements.

## Purpose
The form helps us verify participant information to ensure a smooth and safe event. We need your details to manage participant capacity and provide essential information on the day of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Easter Egg Hunt details, including the event name.
2. Enter your participant name.
3. Enter your participant email address.
4. Enter your participant phone number.
5. Enter your participant's date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Easter Egg Hunt Details** (`easter_egg_hunt_details`, text, required: false): This field is the main description of the event you're registering for. Please enter the title of the event to help us identify your registration.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to specify the event name associated with your registration. This will help our team verify your event details.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it should be displayed during the event.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address you'd like to use for event communication.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number to contact you on the day of the event.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): This field is used to verify the participant's age eligibility. Please enter your date of birth to help us validate your registration.

## Tips
* Ensure all details are accurate to avoid any confusion or delay in processing your registration.
* If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact our event organizers for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us manage participant numbers and event logistics.
