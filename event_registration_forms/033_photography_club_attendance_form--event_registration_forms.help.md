# Photography Club Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Club Attendance Form is designed to track the attendance of members at club meetings. It helps the club administrators to keep a record of who attended a meeting, which is useful for organizing events, sending out reminders, and getting feedback from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting date from the calendar.
2. Choose the meeting time from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the attendee's name as it appears on their membership card or ID.
4. Enter the attendee's email address.
5. Enter the attendee's phone number.
6. Add any additional notes about the attendee, if necessary.
7. Indicate whether the attendee has brought other attendees with them.
8. If the attendee has brought other attendees, list their names separated by commas.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date**: Select the date of the meeting from the calendar. This will help us keep track of which meeting this attendance record is for.
* **Meeting Time**: Choose the time of the meeting from the dropdown list. This will help us know when the meeting occurred.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter the attendee's name as it appears on their membership card or ID. This will help us identify who the attendee is.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the attendee's email address. This will help us contact them for further information or send them important updates.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number. This will help us contact them in case of last-minute changes or important updates.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the attendee, if necessary. This can include any relevant information about their attendance or any special requests they may have.
* **Attendees**: Indicate whether the attendee has brought other attendees with them. If you select 'Yes', please list their names separated by commas.
* **Other Attendees**: If the attendee has brought other attendees, list their names separated by commas. This will help us track who else is coming to the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Double-check the meeting date and time to ensure you're entering the correct information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask a club administrator for clarification.
