# Parade Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parade Entry Form is designed to collect information from parade participants to ensure accurate and complete registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details 
* **Personal Details** (Text): Enter your name, nickname, or any other identifier you'd like to be known by at the parade.

### Entry Details 
* **Entry Details** (Text): Provide a brief description or name of your entry (e.g., a float, a group, or a performance).

### Contact Details 
* **Contact Details** (Text): Enter your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, or address) so we can reach you about your entry.

## Tips
- Please ensure the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the provided contact information.
- This form is for parade entries only; do not use it for other purposes.
