## Workforce Transformation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workforce Transformation Roundtable Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for the "Workforce Transformation Roundtable" event. Providing accurate information will facilitate effective networking and discussions during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps mentioned earlier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name should be entered in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name should be entered in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address in the format `name@domain.com`.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title to help us understand your role and responsibilities.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name or name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more roles you'd like to participate in during the roundtable discussion. You can choose from Operations, Technology, or Other.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization name or name of your department.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, select_one, optional): Select your job function from Sales, IT, Finance, or HR.
* **Shift** (`shift`, time, optional): If applicable, enter your shift details in the format `HH:MM - HH:MM`.
* **Work Shift** (`work_shift`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your work shift details.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter your team name.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address in the format `name@domain.com`.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about your job function or role, select the one that best represents your responsibilities.
* You can enter your first name and last name in any format (e.g., John Smith or J Smith).
* If you're having trouble selecting your role, feel free to contact the event organizers for guidance.
