# Destination Information Night RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Destination Information Night RSVP Form is used to collect information from event attendees about their travel plans, accommodations, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the correct options for the event organizer and attendee fields.
3. Choose your preferred travel mode and accommodation type.
4. Enter the date and time of your departure and return.
5. Specify the cost of your travel and accommodation.
6. Add any additional comments or information about your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Organizer**
* *event_organizer*: Enter the name of the person organizing the event (Optional)

### 2. **Attendee**
* *attendee*: Enter your name or the name of the person attending the event (Optional)

### 3. **Destination**
* *destination*: Enter the name of the destination for the event

### 4. **Departure Date**
* *departure_date*: Enter the date you will be departing for the event

### 5. **Return Date**
* *return_date*: Enter the date you will be returning after the event

### 6. **Travel Mode**
* *travel_mode*: Select the mode of transportation you will be using to travel to the event:
	+ *flight*
	+ *train*
	+ *driving*
	+ *bus*
	+ *other*
	+ *none*

### 7. **Arrival Time**
* *arrival_time*: Enter the time you expect to arrive at the event

### 8. **Travel Cost**
* *travel_cost*: Enter the estimated cost of your travel to the event

### 9. **Accommodation Type**
* *accommodation_type*: Select the type of accommodation you will be using:
	+ *hotel*
	+ *airbnb*
	+ *hostel*
	+ *home*
	+ *other*
	+ *none*

### 10. **Accommodation Address**
* *accommodation_address*: Enter the address of your accommodation

### 11. **Accommodation Cost**
* *accommodation_cost*: Enter the estimated cost of your accommodation

### 12. **Comments**
* *comments*: Add any additional comments or information about your travel plans

### Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your RSVP is processed correctly.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and completeness.
