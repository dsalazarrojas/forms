## In Person Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Person Training Registration Form is used to collect information from participants who will be attending an in-person training session. This form helps us to track the details of the attendees and their preferences for the training session, such as participating in the session, providing their name, contact details, and any notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please select the type of training session you will be attending by answering "Yes" or "No" to the "Training Session" question.
2. Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose the training location from the options provided.
6. Select the date you will be attending the training session by using the calendar or entering the date manually.
7. Enter the time you will be attending the training session by using the time picker or entering the time manually.
8. You can add any additional notes about your participation in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks if you will be attending the training session. Please answer "Yes" if you will be attending, and "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is your email address. Please enter your email address to stay updated about the training session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you about the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks for the location of the training session. Please choose the location from the provided options.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required/optional): This field is for the date you will be attending the training session. You can use the calendar to select the date or enter it manually.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required/optional): This field is for the time you will be attending the training session. You can use the time picker to select the time or enter it manually.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional notes you may have about your participation in the training session.
