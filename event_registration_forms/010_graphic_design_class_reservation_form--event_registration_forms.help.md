# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for event registration, allowing users to provide their personal details and preferences for a specific event. The form is used to collect information for event planning and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your title in the "title" field.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email format (e.g., `[username]@example.com`).
4.  Choose a date for the event.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6.  Select your class type from the provided options (e.g., Graphic Design, Web Design, Print Design).
7.  Choose your level of experience from the provided options (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
8.  Select whether you want to be considered "Reserved" or not from the provided options (e.g., Yes, No).
9.  If you have any additional information or comments, you can enter them in the "Note" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or title to be displayed for the event.
*   **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format `[username]@example.com`.
*   **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the event.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Class Type** (`Class Type`, select one, required/optional): Select your preferred class type (Graphic Design, Web Design, Print Design).
*   **Level** (`Level`, select one, required/optional): Choose your level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
*   **Reserved** (`Reserved`, select multiple, required/optional): Select whether you want to be considered "Reserved" or not (Yes, No).
*   **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your registration.
*   **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
