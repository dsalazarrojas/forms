# Couples Retreat Meal Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Couples Retreat Meal Preferences" form is designed to collect information about the dietary needs and meal preferences of participating couples in a retreat. This data will be used to tailor meal options to suit their individual and joint needs, ensuring a comfortable and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the form, preferably a descriptive one that identifies the couple's name or a unique identifier for easier tracking.
2. **Meal Preferences**: Select all the meal options that you and your partner are willing to eat. You can select one or multiple options.
3. **Couples' Diet**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences that aren't listed, enter a brief description in the text box.
4. **Couples' Meal Choice**: Select the meal option that best suits your needs.
5. **Additional Notes**: If you have any specific requests or questions, please enter them in the text box.
6. **Email**: Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
7. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
8. **Date**: Select the date of your participation in the retreat (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief title for the form that identifies you and your partner.
* **Meal Preferences**: This field allows you to select all the meal options that you and your partner are willing to eat. You can select one or multiple options (e.g., Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Non-Hunter).
	+ **Vegetarian**: Suitable for those who follow a vegetarian diet.
	+ **Non-Vegetarian**: Suitable for those who consume meat and fish.
	+ **Non-Hunter**: Not specified, please enter a description if you have any related dietary needs.
* **Couples' Diet**: A text box for entering any dietary restrictions or preferences not listed in the previous option.
* **Couples' Meal Choice**: Select the meal option that best suits your needs.
	+ **Option A**: Not specified, please review the meal options carefully before selecting.
	+ **Option B**: Not specified, please review the meal options carefully before selecting.
	+ **Option C**: Not specified, please review the meal options carefully before selecting.
* **Additional Notes**: A text box for entering any specific requests or questions you may have.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date**: Select the date of your participation in the retreat.
