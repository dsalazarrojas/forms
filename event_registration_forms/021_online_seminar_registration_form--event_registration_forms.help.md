# Online Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for an online seminar. Please fill it out carefully to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the date of the seminar in the "Seminar Date" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Select the time of the seminar in the "Seminar Time" field in HH:MM AM/PM format.
6. Enter the room number of the seminar in the "Seminar Room" field.
7. Enter the topic of the seminar in the "Seminar Topic" field.
8. Check the registration type(s) you are interested in, if any, in the "Registration Type" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Registrant Phone" field.
10. Enter your company in the "Registrant Company" field.
11. Enter your job title in the "Registrant Job Title" field.
12. Enter your department in the "Registrant Department" field.
13. Enter your country in the "Registrant Country" field.
14. Enter your city in the "Registrant City" field.
15. Select your state in the "Registrant State" field.
16. Enter your zip/postal code in the "Registrant Zip/Postal Code" field.
17. Agree to our terms and conditions in the "Agree" field.
18. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field, if necessary.
19. Finally, review your information and click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address here.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the seminar in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the seminar in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Seminar Room** (`seminar_room`, `text`, required: false): Enter the room number of the seminar.
* **Seminar Topic** (`seminar_topic`, `text`, required: false): Enter the topic of the seminar.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the registration type(s) you are interested in, if any.
* **Registrant Phone** (`registrant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
* **Registrant Company** (`registrant_company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company here.
* **Registrant Job Title** (`registrant_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title here.
* **Registrant Department** (`registrant_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department here.
* **Registrant Country** (`registrant_country`, `text`, required: false): Enter your country here.
* **Registrant City** (`registrant_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter your city here.
* **Registrant State** (`registrant_state`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your state here.
* **Registrant Zip/Postal Code** (`registrant_zip_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip/postal code here.
* **Agree** (`registrant_agree`, `select_one`, required: false): Agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Comments** (`registrant_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments here, if necessary.
* **Submit** (`registrant_submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Review your information and click "Submit" to submit your registration.
