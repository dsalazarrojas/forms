# Cultural Festival Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their interest in attending a cultural festival. It helps the festival organizers to get an overview of who is interested in attending and what they are interested in, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event(s) you are interested in attending by selecting the corresponding option(s) in the "Event Registration Form 3" field.
2. Provide your participant information, including your name, age, and any other relevant details in the "Participant Information" field.
3. Select your event category, location, and capacity preferences from the respective fields.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number in the "Contact Information" field.
5. Specify your availability for the event by selecting the start date and time in the "Start Date" and "Start Time" fields.
6. Enter the number of guests you are planning to bring along with you.
7. Specify your reservation details, including the reservation date, start date, reservation duration, and reservation status.
