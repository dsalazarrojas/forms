# Event Sponsorship Agreement Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Sponsorship Agreement Verification Form is designed to collect and confirm event sponsorship details, ensuring a clear understanding of the agreement between the event organizer and the sponsor. This form should be used to finalize sponsorship agreements and prevent potential disputes.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the relevant information about the event, such as its name, date, location, and description.
2. Provide the sponsor's contact information in the **Sponsor Info** field.
3. Select the type of sponsorship agreement the sponsor is providing (e.g., **Sponsorship Type**).
4. In the **Agreement Details** section, describe the terms of the sponsorship, including any specific requirements or conditions.
5. Enter the sponsor's name in the **Sponsor Name** field.
6. Enter the sponsor's email address in the **Sponsor Email** field.
7. Enter the sponsor's phone number in the **Sponsor Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Page 1)**:
  - **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event, including its name, date, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Sponsor Info (Page 1)**:
  - **Sponsor Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Type (Page 2)**:
  - **Sponsorship Type**: Select the type of sponsorship agreement (e.g., **Sponsorship Type** or **Co Sponsorship**).
* **Agreement Details (Page 2)**:
  - **Agreement Details**: Describe the terms of the sponsorship, including any specific requirements or conditions.
* **Sponsor Name (Page 3)**:
  - **Sponsor Name**: Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Email (Page 3)**:
  - **Sponsor Email**: Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Sponsor Phone (Page 3)**:
  - **Sponsor Phone**: Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Event Details 2 (Page 3)**:
  - **Event Details 2**: Enter any additional details about the event.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check all information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Review the sponsorship terms carefully to avoid any misunderstandings.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer or the sponsor directly.
- This form is a required step in the sponsorship verification process, so please complete it thoroughly and accurately.
- Keep a record of the completed form for reference and future reference.
