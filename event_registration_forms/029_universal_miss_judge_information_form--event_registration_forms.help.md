# Universal Miss Judge Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from judges for pageant events, including personal details and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your personal details:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone Number
	* Country
	* Province
	* City
	* Region
	* Zip Code
	* Street
2. Confirm your title, organization, position, and start date for the event.
3. Enter the end date and start/end time for the event.
4. Confirm the duration of the event.
5. Provide any additional info or notes as needed.
6. Review all the filled-out fields for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`pageant_judge_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`pageant_judge_last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`pageant_judge_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`pageant_judge_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Country** (`pageant_judge_country`, select_one, required): Select your country.
* **Province** (`pageant_judge_province`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable provinces.
* **City** (`pageant_judge_city`, text, required): Please enter your city.
* **Region** (`pageant_judge_region`, text, required): Please enter your region.
* **Zip** (`pageant_judge_zip`, number, required): Please enter your Zip code.
* **Street** (`pageant_judge_street`, text, required): Please enter your street address.
* **Title** (`pageant_judge_title`, select_one, required): Select your title.
* **Organization** (`pageant_judge_organization`, text, required): Please enter your organization.
* **Position** (`pageant_judge_position`, text, required): Please enter your position.
* **Start Date** (`pageant_judge_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (`pageant_judge_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the end date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`pageant_judge_start_time`, time, required): Please enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`pageant_judge_end_time`, time, required): Please enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Start Date** (`pageant_judge_event_start_date`, date, required): Please enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`pageant_judge_event_end_date`, date, required): Please enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`pageant_judge_event_start_time`, time, required): Please enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`pageant_judge_event_end_time`, time, required): Please enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`pageant_judge_event_duration`, number, required): Please enter the duration of the event in hours.
* **Additional Info** (`pageant_judge_additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or notes as needed.
* **Additional Notes** (`pageant_judge_additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments as needed.
