# Esports Event Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esports Event Licensing Application Form is designed for event organizers to provide information about their esports events to obtain a licence. The form is used to collect event details, organizer information, and other relevant data for the licensing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in each field with the relevant information, using the labels as a guide.
3. Once you have filled in all the necessary information, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Info** (`event_organizer_info`): Enter your company or organization name and any relevant contact information.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Describe the event, including the date, time, and location.
* **Event Schedule** (`event_schedule`): Provide a detailed schedule of the event, including start and end times for each session.
* **Event Format Info** (`event_format_info`): Explain the event format, including any unique features or formats.
* **Tournament Details** (`tournament_details`): If the event is a tournament, provide information about the type of tournament and its rules.
* **Competition Details** (`competition_details`): Describe the competitions or games being held at the event.
* **Team Participants** (`team_participants`): List the teams or players participating in the event.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Event Licensing Agreement** (`event_licensing_agreement`): Read and agree to the event licensing agreement terms.
* **Upload Event Documents** (`upload_event_documents`): Upload any supporting documents required for the event, such as event rules or participant contracts.
* **Confirm Event Details** (`confirm_event_details`): Double-check the accuracy of your event information.
* **Submit Event** (`submit_event`): Click to submit your event application for review.
* **Review Event** (`review_event`): Review your event application for accuracy and completeness.
* **Esports Event Licence** (`esports_event_licence`): Receive your event licence once it is approved.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): Track the status of your event application.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`): Add any additional notes or comments about your event.
* **Esports Licensing Terms** (`esports_licensing_terms`): Review the licensing terms and conditions.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`): Check the status of your event review.
* **Event Reviewers** (`event_reviewers`): List the reviewers assigned to your event.
* **Event Status History** (`event_status_history`): View the history of your event status updates.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in each field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
