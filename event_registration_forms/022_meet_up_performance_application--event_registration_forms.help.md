**Meet Up Performance Application - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
---------------

This form is used to collect information from meet-up performers to manage their applications.

**How To Complete This Form**
---------------------------

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your performance.
2. Select the correct status of your application.
3. Submit your application.
