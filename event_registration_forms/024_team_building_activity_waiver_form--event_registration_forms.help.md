# Team Building Activity Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a team building activity, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and risk acknowledgements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Provide your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and emergency contact information.
* Indicate your risk level for the activity and your relationship with the emergency contact.
* Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risk acknowledgement and emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Please enter your name and date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide details about your relationship with the emergency contact and risk level.
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: Sign and date to acknowledge that you have read and understood the risk level.
* **Consent**: Sign and date to indicate that you have given your consent to participate in the activity.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Choose the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact.
* **Team Leaders**: Enter the name of the team leaders.
* **Team Leaders Relationship**: Enter your relationship with the team leaders.
* **Emergency Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign if you are a parent or guardian of the participant.
* **Emergency Signature**: Sign if you are the emergency contact.
* **Participant Agreement**: Enter any agreements or notes related to your participation.
* **Participant Agreement Date**: Date of the agreement.
* **Emergency Agreement**: Enter any agreements or notes related to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Agreement Date**: Date of the agreement.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and honestly to ensure participant safety and emergency contact details are up to date.
