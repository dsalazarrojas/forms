# Employee Wellbeing Strategy Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for events related to employee wellbeing strategy. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure accurate registration and receipt of event information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the `Full Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your company name in the `Company` field.
4. Enter your position in the `Position` field.
5. Enter your department in the `Department` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
7. Select the date of the event in the `Event Date` field.
8. Select the time of the event in the `Event Time` field.
9. Enter the event location in the `Event Location` field.
10. Select the date of registration in the `Registration Date` field.
11. Select the time of registration in the `Registration Time` field.
12. Enter the location of registration in the `Registration Location` field.
13. Add any comments about your registration in the `Comments` field (optional).
14. Select the option to be assigned to from the list of options in the `Assigned To` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it is spelled in your identification documents.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your work email address.
- **Company** (Company, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
- **Position** (Position, text, required): Enter your position in the company.
- **Department** (Department, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
- **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
- **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required): Select the time of the event.
- **Event Location** (Event Location, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required): Select the date of registration.
- **Registration Time** (Registration Time, time, required): Select the time of registration.
- **Registration Location** (Registration Location, text, required): Enter the location of registration.
- **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your registration.
- **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, optional): Select the option to be assigned to from the list of options.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
- Registration details will be confirmed after submitting this form.
