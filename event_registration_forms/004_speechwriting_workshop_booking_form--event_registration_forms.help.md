# Speechwriting Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speechwriting Workshop Booking Form is used to register for a workshop on speechwriting. It is required to be filled out by end-users to provide essential information for workshop organization and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about the workshop.
3. Provide your Phone number for any additional contact or support.
4. Select your preferred language for the workshop.
5. Enter the Workshop ID provided to you.
6. Choose the Workshop Date and Time you wish to attend.
7. Select the Workshop Duration you wish to attend.
8. If you have any comments or additional requests, please provide them in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact or support.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (DD/MM/YY).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, required): Select your preferred language for the workshop (English, Spanish, French).
* **Workshop ID** (`workshop_id`, number, required): Enter the workshop ID provided to you.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Select the time you wish to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required): Select the duration of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Check your preferred language selection to ensure it matches your needs.
- Review your workshop details carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact us for support.
