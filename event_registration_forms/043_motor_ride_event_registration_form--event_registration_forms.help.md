# Motor Ride Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register or plan a motor ride event. It collects information about the event, including participant details, event details, date and time, venue, and the event organiser's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
3. For the fields that are not required, you can choose to fill them in or leave them blank.
4. Review your form before submitting it to ensure all the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Text, Not Required):** This field is for entering the title of the event.
* **Participant Info (Text, Not Required):** Enter the name and contact information of the participants, including any additional details they might need to know.
* **Event Details (Text, Not Required):** Enter a brief description of the event, including any necessary details about the motor ride.
* **Event Date (Date, Not Required):** Enter the date of the event in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Event Time (Time, Not Required):** Enter the time of the event in the standard time format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Venue (Select Multiple, Not Required):** Choose the venue where the event will take place. You can select multiple options, but only if they are available.
* **Event Organiser (Email, Not Required):** Enter the email address of the event organiser or the person planning the event.
