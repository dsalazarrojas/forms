# Martial Arts Testing Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to provide necessary information for event registration and payment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Event Registration Form** section with the necessary details.
2. Select the **Test Type** that best describes the testing requirements.
3. Enter your **Test Date** in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Choose your preferred **Test Time** in 24-hour format.
5. Select the **Payment Method** for your payment preference.
6. Enter your **Student Email** address for communication purposes.
7. Provide your **Student Phone** number for contact information.
8. Enter your **Student Address** for record-keeping purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form**: This section is where you provide the basic details for your event registration.
* **Student Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Test Type**: Select the type of test you are registering for (1 hour testing, 2 hour testing, or Special testing).
* **Test Date**: Enter the date of your test (MM/DD/YY).
* **Test Time**: Select your preferred test time in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 12:00, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method preference (Online payment or Offline payment).
* **Student Email**: Enter your email address for future correspondence and updates.
* **Student Phone**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student Address**: Enter your address for record-keeping and delivery purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* Ensure test dates and times are correctly formatted.
