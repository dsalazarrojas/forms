# customer_service_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for workshop attendees to register for a customer service workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
*   If a field is not applicable to you, please leave it blank.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your workshop organiser.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Name** (workshop\_name, text, required: false):
    Enter the name of the workshop you are attending.
*   **Email** (email, required: false):
    Enter your email address.
    This is the email address we will use to send you reminders and updates about the workshop.
*   **Participant Name** (participant\_name, text, required: false):
    Enter your name. This is the name we will use to address you during the workshop.
*   **Phone** (phone, text, required: false):
    If you have a phone number, enter it here.
    This is optional and will only be used in case of emergencies.
*   **Address** (address, text, required: false):
    Enter your address if you need it to be taken into account for workshop arrangements.
*   **Date** (date, required: false):
    Enter the date of the workshop you will be attending.
*   **Time** (time, required: false):
    Enter the time of the workshop you will be attending.
*   **Event Details** (event\_details, text, required: false):
    If there is anything specific you need us to know about your attendance or any special requirements you may have, please enter them here.
