# HR Tech Conference Participation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Tech Conference Participation Application form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to attend or participate in the HR Tech Conference. This form is for HR professionals, conference organizers, and other relevant parties to gather data for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
3. Provide your Contact info, which may include additional details like a phone number or other contact methods.
4. Select your participation type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Choose if you will attend the HR Tech Conference ('True' or 'False').
6. Fill in your desired Date and Time of attendance.
7. If you have any additional Notes, please fill in the Notes field.
8. Select your participation type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3) for the Participation types field.
9. Fill in any additional Contact info 1, 2, and 3.
10. Select your preferred Contact method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
11. Choose your preferred Contact method 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last name** (2): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact info** (4): Provide your contact information for conference planning purposes.
* **Participation type** (5): Select your preferred participation type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Requirements** (6): Please fill in any additional requirements you have.
* **Will you attend HR Tech Conference?** (7): Choose 'True' if you plan to attend, otherwise 'False'.
* **Date** (8): Fill in your desired Date of attendance.
* **Time** (9): Fill in your desired Time of attendance.
* **Note** (10): If you have any additional Notes, please fill them in here.
* **Participation types** (11): Select your preferred participation type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact info 1** (12), **Contact info 2** (13), and **Contact info 3** (14): Provide any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Phone** (15): Please fill in your phone number.
* **Email (2)** (16): If you have a preferred email, please enter it here.
* **Contact method** (17): Select your preferred contact method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact method 1** (18), **Contact method 2** (19), **Contact method 3** (20), **Contact method 4** (21), **Contact method 5** (22), **Contact method 6** (23), and **Contact method 7** (24): Select your preferred contact methods (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields and submit the form accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
