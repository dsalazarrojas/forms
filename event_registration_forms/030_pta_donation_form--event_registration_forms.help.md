# PTA Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals willing to make a donation to the PTA. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Type in your **Email** address in the field labeled "Email".
4. If you have a **Phone number**, enter it in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Indicate your desired **Donation Amount** by typing a number in the field labeled "Donation Amount".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Text, Optional)
* Enter your personal name here.

### Last Name (Text, Optional)
* Enter your last name here.

### Email (Email, Optional)
* Type in a valid email address here. This field is required to contact you.

### Phone (Text, Optional)
* If you have a phone number, enter it here.

### Donation Amount (Number, Optional)
* Type a numerical value here, such as a whole number or decimal.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields you wish to complete, even if they are optional.
* Double-check that your email address is accurate for any communication.
