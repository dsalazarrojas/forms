# networking_mixer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The networking mixer registration form is designed to collect essential details from attendees for a networking mixer event. This information helps the organizers manage event registrations efficiently and ensures that attendees receive accurate updates and communications related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are registering for a networking mixer event that is part of our official calendar.
2. Enter your event name in the "Event Name" field.
3. Fill out the "Attendee Name" field with your name as it appears on your business card or official ID.
4. Provide your "Attendee Email" address to enable you to receive notifications and updates about the event.
5. Enter the name of your company in the "Company Name" field.
6. Enter your position or role in your company in the "Position" field.
7. If you work for a different company, enter its name in the "Company" field.
8. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
9. If you represent a business, enter its name in the "Business Name" field.
10. Enter your personal or business address in the "Address" field.
11. If you have a phone number related to your registration, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
12. Enter your company's address in the "Company Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for. Please ensure it matches the actual event name from the company calendar.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or official ID.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, not required): Provide your email address to receive notifications and updates about the event.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Position** (`position`, text, not required): Enter your position or role in your company.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): If you work for a different company, enter its name here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): If you represent a business, enter its name here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your personal or business address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number related to your registration (if required).
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, not required): Enter your company's address (if applicable).
