# Media Sponsorship Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for media representatives to communicate with event organizers and teams about potential sponsorship opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you're providing the correct information.
3. Double-check your contact information to ensure that it's correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Information** (`media_sponsorship_communication_form`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the general information about the event. Please provide a brief description of the event, including any relevant details.

### 2. **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the title of the event. Please enter the exact title of the event as it appears on marketing materials.

### 3. **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the location of the event. Please enter the exact location where the event will take place.

### 4. **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false)
* This field is for the date of the event. Please enter the date of the event in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### 5. **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false)
* This field is for the time of the event. Please enter the time of the event in the correct format (HH:MM).

### 6. **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the name of the sponsor. Please enter the exact name of the sponsor as it appears on marketing materials.

### 7. **Sponsor Title** (`sponsor_title`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the title of the sponsor. Please enter the exact title of the sponsor as it appears on marketing materials.

### 8. **Sponsor Organization** (`sponsor_organization`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the organization of the sponsor. Please enter the exact organization of the sponsor as it appears on marketing materials.

### 9. **Sponsor Contact Name** (`sponsor_contact_name`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the name of the sponsor's contact person. Please enter the exact name of the sponsor's contact person.

### 10. **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the sponsor's phone number. Please enter the exact phone number of the sponsor.

### 11. **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required: false)
* This field is for the sponsor's email address. Please enter the exact email address of the sponsor.

### 12. **Sponsor Message** (`sponsor_message`, text, required: false)
* This field is for any additional information or message from the sponsor. Please enter any relevant details about the sponsorship opportunity.

### 13. **Sponsor Media Outlet** (`sponsor_media_outlet`, text, required: false)
* This field is for the sponsor's media outlet. Please enter the exact media outlet where the sponsor's materials will be published.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information to ensure that it's correct.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you're providing the correct information.
* If you have any questions or need help completing this form, please contact the event team.
