# music_venue_support_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps music venues collect information from potential donors and understand their supporters better.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Venue Info" section with details about the music venue.
2.  In the "Donor Info" section, provide information about the donor, including their name and contact details.
3.  In the "Donation Details" section, specify the type of donation you want to make and any additional details about the donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Venue Info** (`venue_info`, text, optional): This section is for providing details about the music venue, such as its name, address, and contact information.
*   **Donor Info** (`donor_info`, text, optional): This section is for providing information about the donor, including their name, email, and phone number.
*   **Donation Details** (`donation_details`, text, optional): This section is for specifying the type of donation you want to make, such as monetary or in-kind, and any additional details about the donation.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
*   If you need help, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, you can also reach out to the music venue directly.
*   Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and support your favorite music venue!
