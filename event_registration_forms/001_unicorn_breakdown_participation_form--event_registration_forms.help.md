# Unicorn Breakdown Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for event registration and breakdown participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are registering for the correct event.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, number, required): Please enter the unique ID of the event you are registering for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on event materials and correspondence.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select, required): Select the type of event this is (e.g., Whimsical, Themed).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event (e.g., venue name, city).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Breakdown Time** (`breakdown_time`, number, required): Enter the time you will be available for the breakdown event.
* **Breakdown Locations** (`breakdown_locations`, select multiple, required): Select all locations where you will be available for the breakdown event.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Please provide a brief statement indicating you understand and agree to participate in the breakdown event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of your information.
* Contact the event organizer if you have any questions or concerns.
