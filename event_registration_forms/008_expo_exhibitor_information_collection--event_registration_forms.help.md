# Expo Exhibitor Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from exhibitors participating in an expo. It helps us to gather details about the exhibitor, their company, and their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibitor Information** section with the details about the exhibitor participating in the event.
2. Provide information about your company in the **Company Details** section.
3. Enter the contact details of the person who will be representing your company at the event in the **Contact Details** section.
4. Fill in any additional information about the event in the **Show Info** section.
5. If you have any booths at the event, provide their details in the **Booth Info** section.
6. If you have any products that you will be showcasing at the event, provide information about them in the **Product Info** section.
7. Enter your shipping details in the **Shipping Info** section.
8. Review and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** and **Agreement and Waiver** sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Information**: This section is for you to enter the details about the exhibitor participating in the event. Please fill in the exhibitor's name, job title, and other relevant information.
* **Company Details**: This is where you should enter information about your company, such as the company name, address, and contact numbers.
* **Contact Details**: Enter the name, job title, and contact details of the person who will be representing your company at the event.
* **Show Info**: This section is for you to enter any additional information about the event that you think would be relevant.
* **Booth Info**: If you have any booths at the event, enter their details here, including the booth number and location.
* **Product Info**: Enter information about the products you will be showcasing at the event, such as their name, description, and price.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping details, including the shipping method and any relevant shipping addresses.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to our terms and conditions for the event.
* **Agreement and Waiver**: Review and agree to our waiver and agreement for the event.
* **Company Representative**: This section is for you to enter the name and contact details of the person representing your company at the event.
* **Shipping Method**: Enter the shipping method you will be using for shipping your products or materials to the event.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that you think would be relevant to your participation in the event.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide as much information as possible in each section to ensure a smooth and successful participation in the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in filling out this form accurately.
