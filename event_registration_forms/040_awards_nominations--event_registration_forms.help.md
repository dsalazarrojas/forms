# Awards Nominations Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Awards Nominations Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for an awards ceremony. The form helps us understand your registration details, guests and dietary preferences, and payment information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full name of the primary attendee.
2. Provide the email address of the primary attendee, which will be used for event ticketing and confirmation.
3. Enter the phone number of the primary attendee as a contact number for event updates.
4. Select the type of registration (Individual, Group/Team, VIP Corporate, or Student/Non-Profit).
5. If registering for more than one person, provide the number of additional guests and their names in a comma-separated list.
6. Select any applicable dietary restrictions for the guests.
7. Choose the primary meal preference for the primary attendee.
8. Select a preferred seating location for the primary attendee.
9. If necessary, provide special assistance requirements.
10. Select the preferred payment method (Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, or Request Invoice/PO).
11. Enter any applicable discount or promo code.
12. Review the total amount due, which is calculated based on the registration type and guests.
13. Agree to the event terms and cancellation policy.
14. Provide a digital signature with the attendee's full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Primary Attendee**: Enter the full name (First and Last) of the primary attendee.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive event ticketing and confirmation details.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number where the primary attendee can be contacted for event updates.
* **Organization or Business Name**: If the primary attendee represents an organization or business, provide the name here.
* **Registration Type**: Choose the type of registration that best suits your group size and needs.
* **Number of Additional Guests**: If registering for more than one person, enter the number of guests.
* **Additional Guest Names**: List the names of additional guests in a comma-separated format.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions that apply to your guests (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Nut Allergy, or Dairy-Free).
* **Primary Meal Preference**: Choose the main course for the primary attendee.
* **Preferred Seating Location**: Select a preferred seating location (Front Row/Near Stage, Center/Main Hall, or Back/Quiet Area).
* **Special Assistance Needed**: If any guest has special assistance requirements, describe them briefly.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose the payment method that suits your preference (Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, or Request Invoice/PO).
* **Discount or Promo Code**: If applicable, enter any discount or promo code.
* **Total Amount Due**: Review the calculated total amount due for the registration.
* **Event Terms Agreement**: Agree to the event terms and cancellation policy.
* **Attendee Digital Signature**: Type the full name as a digital signature to confirm registration.
