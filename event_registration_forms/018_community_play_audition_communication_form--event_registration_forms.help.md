# Community Play Audition Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for actors to provide their information and availability for an audition. You will be asked for personal details, audition preferences, and scheduling information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details: **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
2. Select your **Age Range You Can Portray**, and choose your **Gender** (if applicable).
3. Choose the **Play Title** you are auditioning for and the **Audition Date** and **Audition Time Slot**.
4. If you have any **Character Preferences**, list them separately.
5. Indicate if you have a **Monologue Prepared**, and if so, provide the **Monologue Title and Author**.
6. Select your **Rehearsal Availability** and **Availability** for the performance dates.
7. Confirm that you are **Available for All Performance Dates**.
8. Choose if you **Have Reliable Transportation**.
9. Confirm if you **Have Submitted Headshot and Resume**.
10. Tell us how you heard about the audition.
11. Add any **Additional Comments** you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for audition updates.
* **Age Range You Can Portray**: Choose the age group you fit into.
* **Gender**: Select your gender identity.
* **Play Title**: Enter the title of the play you are auditioning for.
* **Audition Date**: Enter the date of your audition.
* **Audition Time Slot**: Enter the time slot for your audition.
* **Character Preferences**: List the characters you are interested in.
* **Monologue Prepared**: Indicate if you have a monologue prepared.
* **Monologue Title and Author**: If you have a monologue, provide the title and author.
* **Rehearsal Availability**: Select your availability for rehearsals.
* **Availability**: Provide any scheduling constraints.
* **Have Reliable Transportation**: Confirm if you have reliable transportation.
* **Have Submitted Headshot and Resume**: Confirm if you have submitted your headshot and resume.
* **How Did You Hear About This Audition**: Choose how you heard about the audition.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information you would like to share.

# Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* Make sure to review and submit any requested attachments.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the production team.
