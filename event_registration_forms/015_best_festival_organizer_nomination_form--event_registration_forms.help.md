# Best Festival Organizer Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Festival Organizer Nomination Form is designed to recognize and reward individuals who have demonstrated outstanding leadership and dedication to their work in festival organization. This form is only intended for nominations of individuals with a minimum of 5 years of experience in festival organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Nominator Information section with your details.
2. Enter the nominated organizer's information in the Organizer Details section.
3. Rate the nominee's performance and leadership skills in each of the categories listed.
4. Provide a detailed description of a specific challenge the nominee successfully overcame and an example of innovation they brought to the industry.
5. Summarize why the nominee is a leader in their field.
6. Enter the professional reference's name and contact information.
7. Finally, enter the date of nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the nominator.
* **Your Email Address** (`nominator_email_addr`, email, required): Please enter your email address to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **The individual being nominated** (`organizer_name_text`, text, required): Please enter the full name of the individual being nominated.
* **Organization or Company Name** (`company_org_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the organization or company associated with the nominated individual.
* **Main Festival or Festivals Organized** (`main_festivals_list`, text, required): Please list the primary festivals managed by this individual.
* **Years in Festival Organization** (`years_experience_range`, select_one, required): Select the number of years the nominated individual has been in festival organization (1-5, 6-10, 11-20, Over 20).
* **Safety and Security Management** (`safety_management_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the nominated individual's safety and security management on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Vendor and Logistics Coordination** (`vendor_coordination_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the nominated individual's vendor and logistics coordination on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Marketing and Promotion Strategy** (`marketing_strategy_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the nominated individual's marketing and promotion strategy on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Financial Management and Budgeting** (`financial_management_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the nominated individual's financial management and budgeting on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Staff and Volunteer Leadership** (`leadership_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the nominated individual's staff and volunteer leadership on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Describe a specific challenge** (`challenge_overcome_desc`, text, required): Please describe a specific challenge this individual successfully overcame.
* **What innovation has this organizer brought to the industry?** (`innovation_impact_desc`, text, required): Please provide an example of innovation this individual has brought to the industry (new technologies, processes, or concepts).
* **Why is this organizer a leader in their field?** (`leadership_justification`, text, required): Summarize why the nominated individual is a leader in their field.
* **Professional Reference Name** (`reference_name_text`, text, required): Please enter the name of the professional reference who can confirm the nominee's achievements.
* **Reference Contact Number** (`reference_contact_number`, text, required): Please enter the contact number of the professional reference.
* **Date of Nomination** (`submission_final_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of nomination.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the nominee has a minimum of 5 years of experience in festival organization.
* Only one nomination per individual is accepted.
* Please fill out the form truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at [your email address].
