# Prop Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about equipment rental bookings for events. It helps event organizers and attendees provide necessary details for successful event execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal information and event details.
2. Select the equipment required for your event from the list.
3. Specify the rental duration, booking date, and rental end date.
4. Provide a brief description of your event.
5. Choose your preferred payment method and specify the payment status.
6. Optionally, add any special requests or notes.
7. Select the event category, type, and duration.
8. Enter the contact person's name, phone number, email, and organization.
9. Review the terms and conditions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Booking Details (2)**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Select Equipment (3)**: Choose the equipment you need for your event from the list.
* **Rental Duration (4)**: Specify the length of time you need the equipment for.
* **Booking Date (5)**: Enter the date you need to book the equipment.
* **Rental Time (6)**: Enter the time you need to rent the equipment for.
* **Event Name (7)**: Enter a name for your event.
* **Event Description (8)**: Briefly describe the purpose and objectives of your event.
* **Rental Start (9)**: Specify the start date and time of the rental period.
* **Rental End (10)**: Specify the end date and time of the rental period.
* **Equipment List (11)**: List the specific equipment you need for your event.
* **Contact Info (12)**: Enter your contact information for future correspondence.
* **Special Request (13)**: Optionally, add any special requests or notes.
* **Payment Method (14)**: Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status (15)**: Specify the payment status (Paid or Pending).
* **Notes (16)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Terms and Conditions (17)**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental.
* **Event Category (18)**: Select the category of your event (Party, Meeting, Event, etc.).
* **Event Location (19)**: Enter the location of your event.
* **Event Type (20)**: Select the type of your event (Live, Recorded, Hybrid).
* **Event Duration (21)**: Choose the duration of your event (Half Day, Full Day, All Day).
* **Contact Person (22)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (23)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email (24)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Organization (25)**: Enter the name of the organization or company hosting the event.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact information for errors.
* Review the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact the specified contact person.
