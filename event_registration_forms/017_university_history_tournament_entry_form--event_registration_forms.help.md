# University History Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University History Tournament Entry Form is designed to collect relevant information about a university history entry. This form helps to gather accurate and consistent data, ensuring that each entry is properly recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Entry Name field with a descriptive title for your entry.
2. Provide a brief description of your entry in the Entry Description field.
3. Enter the University Name of the institution associated with your entry.
4. Select the Entry Date and Entry Time for your entry.
5. Choose the Entry Date Time Format for your entry (either 12-hour or 24-hour format).
6. If applicable, enter the Team Name and Team Name Abbreviation associated with your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Entry Name (`entry_name`, text, optional)
* A descriptive title for your entry.

### Entry Description (`entry_description`, text, optional)
* A brief description of your entry.

### University Name (`university_name`, text, optional)
* The name of the university associated with your entry.

### Entry Date (`entry_date`, date, optional)
* The date of your entry (in the selected format).

### Entry Time (`entry_time`, time, optional)
* The time of your entry (in the selected format).

### Entry Date Time (`entry_date_time`, date, optional)
* The date and time of your entry (in the selected format).

### Entry Date Time Format (`entry_date_time_format`, select_one, required)
* The format of the entry date and time (12-hour or 24-hour).

### Team Name (`entry_team_name`, text, optional)
* The name of the team associated with your entry.

### Team Name Abbreviation (`entry_team_name_abbreviation`, text, optional)
* The abbreviation of the team name (if applicable).

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid any errors or issues during the submission process.
- Please note that this form is designed to collect unique entries. Do not submit duplicate entries.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
