# Education Data Governance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for the Education Data Governance Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Registration Information.
2. Enter your Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. (Optional) Enter your Phone number.
5. Select the Event you wish to register for (Education Data Governance Workshop or Other).
6. Choose the Date and Time of the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or any other official document.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: yes
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use for communication and sending workshop material.
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: yes
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number. This is optional but helps us communicate with you better.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Event**: Select the event you wish to attend. Please choose one of the two options: Education Data Governance Workshop or Other.
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: yes
* **Date**: Choose the date of the workshop from the available options.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: yes
* **Time**: Select the time of the workshop from the available options.
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: yes
* **Note**: Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us.
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: no
