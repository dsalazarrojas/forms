# transportation_summit_block_confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for confirming the details of a transportation summit block, a type of event registration form. It is meant to be filled out by event organizers to provide essential information about the block they are managing. The form ensures that all necessary details are captured for efficient communication and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the event organizer in the `Event Organizer` field.
2.  Choose the details of the block from the `Block Details` dropdown menu.
3.  Indicate if attendees will be present (`Yes` or `No`) in the `Attendees Details` field.
4.  Specify the contact details of the person managing the block (`Yes` or `No`) in the `Contact Details` field.
5.  Confirm if transportation details are required or not (`Yes` or `No`) in the `Transportation Details` field.
6.  Choose which group(s) will be participating in the block (`Yes` or `No`) in the `Group Participation` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Organizer** (`Event Organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization managing the event.
*   **Block Details** (`Block Details`, select_one, required): Select the details of the block that are being confirmed, such as Block 1, 2, or 3.
*   **Attendees Details** (`Attendees Details`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if attendees will be present at the block.
*   **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, select_one, optional): Specify if the contact details for the block will be available or not.
*   **Transportation Details** (`Transportation Details`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if transportation details are required for the block.
*   **Group Participation** (`Group Participation`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the group(s) participating in the block, or specify that no group will be participating.
