# Historical Site Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Site Incident Form is used to collect information about historical site incidents, including dates, locations, types, and resolution details. This form helps site managers and security teams to track and respond to incidents in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Incident Date** field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the **Incident Location** where the incident occurred.
3. Select the **Incident Type** from the available options (Historical Preservation, Cultural Heritage, Archaeological, or Other).
4. Provide a brief description of the incident in the **Incident Description** field.
5. If applicable, enter the number of **Incident Injuries** and **Incident Property Damage** sustained.
6. Specify the **Incident Reported By** person or entity.
7. Enter the date the incident was reported in the **Incident Reported On** field.
8. Select the individuals or teams that received the incident report (Site Manager, Security Manager, Site Supervisor, or Other).
9. Choose the **Incident Status** (Open, Closed, Pending, or In Progress).
10. Enter the date the incident was resolved, if applicable.
11. Provide a brief explanation of the incident resolution in the **Incident Resolution** field.
12. If attachments were uploaded, select them from the list.
13. Enter the total number of attachments in the **Incident Attachment Count** field.
14. Specify the total size of the attachments in the **Incident Attachment Size** field.
15. Mark the incident as resolved with or without an attachment.
16. Enter any **Incident Follow-up Actions** taken.
17. Enter the number of follow-up actions taken.
18. Choose the status of the incident investigation (In Progress, Complete, or Pending).
19. Provide any comments about the investigation status.
20. Enter any additional notes about the incident in the **Incident Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Briefly describe what happened.
* **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident (Historical Preservation, Cultural Heritage, Archaeological, or Other).
* **Incident Injuries**: Enter the number of injuries sustained, if applicable.
* **Incident Property Damage**: Enter the amount of property damage sustained, if applicable.
* **Incident Reported By**: Enter the person or entity that reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported On**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reported To**: Select the individuals or teams that received the report (Site Manager, Security Manager, Site Supervisor, or Other).
* **Incident Status**: Choose the status of the incident (Open, Closed, Pending, or In Progress).
* **Incident Resolution Date**: Enter the date the incident was resolved, if applicable.
* **Incident Resolution**: Briefly describe the resolution of the incident.
* **Incident Resolution Attachment**: Specify if an attachment was used to resolve the incident.
* **Incident Attachment Count**: Enter the total number of attachments.
* **Incident Attachment Size**: Enter the total size of the attachments.
* **Incident Follow-up Actions**: Enter any actions taken after the incident.
* **Incident Follow-up Actions Count**: Enter the number of actions taken.
* **Incident Investigation Status**: Choose the status of the incident investigation (In Progress, Complete, or Pending).
* **Incident Investigation Status Comment**: Enter any comments about the investigation status.
* **Incident Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
* **Incident Attachments**: Select the attachments uploaded.
* **Incident Resolution Attachment**: Specify if an attachment was used to resolve the incident.
* **Incident Attachment Size**: Enter the size of the attachments.
* **Incident Follow-up Actions Count**: Enter the number of follow-up actions taken.
