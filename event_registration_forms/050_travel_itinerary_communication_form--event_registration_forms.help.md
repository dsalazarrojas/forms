# Travel Itinerary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and communicate travel-related information to travel agencies or other relevant stakeholders. It captures details about a trip, including travel dates, mode, status, cost, and agency-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
* Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting it.
