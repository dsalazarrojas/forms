# Muay Thai Awards Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals or entities for the Muay Thai Awards. It collects information about the nominee, the category they are being nominated for, and the reason for the nomination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2.  Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3.  Enter your email address in the Email field.
4.  Select Yes or No in the Category field, depending on whether the nominee fits the category.
5.  Enter the name of the person being nominated in the Nominee field.
6.  Enter the title of the award the nominee is being nominated for in the Title Nominated For field.
7.  Enter a brief reason for the nomination in the Reason for Nomination field.
8.  Provide any additional supporting information in the Supporting Information field.
9.  Attach any supporting documents relevant to the nomination in the Supporting Documents field.
10.  Select the Nomination Agreement from the options provided.
11.  Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required)**: Enter the first name of the person being nominated.
* **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter the last name of the person being nominated.
* **Email (email, required)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Category (select_one, optional)**: Select Yes if the nominee fits the category, and No otherwise.
* **Nominee (text, required)**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Title Nominated For (text, required)**: Enter the title of the award the nominee is being nominated for.
* **Reason for Nomination (text, optional)**: Provide a brief reason for the nomination.
* **Supporting Information (text, optional)**: Provide any additional information to support the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents (text, optional)**: Attach any documents that are relevant to the nomination.
* **Nomination Agreement (select_one, required)**: Select the nomination agreement that best fits the nomination.
* **Additional Comments (text, optional)**: Add any additional comments about the nomination.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to attach any required supporting documents.
* Review the nomination agreement carefully before selecting an option.
* Double-check the email address to ensure it is correct.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the award administrators for assistance.
