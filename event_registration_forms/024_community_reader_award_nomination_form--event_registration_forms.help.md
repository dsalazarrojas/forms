# Community Reader Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Reader Award Nomination Form is designed to recognize individuals who have made a significant impact on local literacy and reading. This form helps to gather information about the nominee and their contributions to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Nominator's Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Provide the Nominee's Full Name.
3. Select the Nominee's role in the community (e.g., Teacher, Librarian, Student, Volunteer, Parent, or Other).
4. Describe the Nominee's role in the community.
5. Explain how the Nominee has promoted literacy in the community.
6. List any specific reading initiatives or programs led by the Nominee.
7. Describe the Nominee's passion for reading and literacy.
8. Provide an example of a time the Nominee went above and beyond for a reader.
9. Confirm if you are willing to provide a video testimonial.
10. Check if you are nominating yourself.
11. Select all the age groups the Nominee primarily works with.
12. Confirm your submission and agree to the nomination deadline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nominator Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person nominating the community reader.
* **Nominator Email Address** (`nominator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person nominating the community reader.
* **Nominator Phone Number** (`nominator_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person nominating the community reader.
* **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee - s Role in the Community** (`nominee_role_selection`, select_one, required): Select the role of the nominee in the community (e.g., Teacher, Librarian, Student, Volunteer, Parent, or Other).
* **Nominee - s School or Organization** (`nominee_school_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the school or organization the nominee is associated with.
* **How has the nominee promoted literacy in our community?** (`literacy_promotion_impact`, text, required): Describe the nominee's contributions to promoting literacy in the community.
* **List any specific reading initiatives or programs led by the nominee** (`reading_initiatives_list`, text, required): List any specific reading initiatives or programs led by the nominee.
* **Describe the nominee - s passion for reading and literacy** (`nominee_passion_desc`, text, required): Describe the nominee's passion for reading and literacy.
* **Provide an example of a time the nominee went above and beyond for a reader** (`exemplary_moment_example`, text, required): Provide an example of a time the nominee demonstrated exceptional service to a reader.
* **Would you be willing to provide a video testimonial?** (`video_testimonial_interest`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are willing to provide a video testimonial.
* **Are you nominating yourself?** (`self_nomination_check`, select_one, required): Check if you are nominating yourself.
* **What age group does the nominee primarily work with?** (`primary_age_group_target`, select_multiple, required): Select all the age groups the nominee primarily works with (e.g., Pre-K, Elementary School, Middle School, High School, Adults).
* **-- Nomination Submission Deadline --** (`nomination_deadline_info_note`, note, required): Confirm your submission and agree to the nomination deadline.
