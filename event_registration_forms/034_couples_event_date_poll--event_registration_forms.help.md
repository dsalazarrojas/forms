# couples_event_date_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from couples about their event dates and preferences. It's used for surveying or polling purposes to gather insights on events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event date using the date field.
2. Choose the event time using the time field.
3. Enter the event time duration using the time duration field.
4. For both partners, fill out their name, response, email, phone number, and message fields.
5. Select their status from the status field.
6. Enter any additional event details in the event details field.
7. For each partner, select their status and follow-up options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Time Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Partner 1 Name**: Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner 1 Response**: Provide your response or feedback about the event.
* **Partner 1 Email**: Enter your partner's email.
* **Partner 1 Phone**: Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Partner 1 Message**: Enter any additional message for your partner.
* **Partner 1 Status**: Select your status regarding the event.
* **Partner 1 Note**: Enter any notes related to the event for your partner.
* **Partner 1 Status Message**: Enter a status message for your partner.
* **Partner 1 Followup**: Choose a follow-up option for your partner.
* **Partner 2 Name**: Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner 2 Response**: Provide your partner's response or feedback about the event.
* **Partner 2 Email**: Enter your partner's email.
* **Partner 2 Phone**: Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Partner 2 Message**: Enter any additional message for your partner.
* **Partner 2 Status**: Select your partner's status regarding the event.
* **Partner 2 Note**: Enter any notes related to the event for your partner.
* **Partner 2 Status Message**: Enter a status message for your partner.
* **Partner 2 Followup**: Choose a follow-up option for your partner.
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event.
* **Event Status 2**: Select a secondary status for the event.
* **Event Status 3**: Select a tertiary status for the event.
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Partner 1 Status**: Select your partner's status.
* **Partner 2 Status**: Select your partner's status.
* **Partner 1 Followup**: Select a follow-up option for your partner.
* **Partner 2 Followup**: Select a follow-up option for your partner.
* **Event Message**: Enter a message about the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the event date and time to ensure accuracy.
* Be thorough when filling out the status and follow-up fields.
* Proofread your entries before submitting the form.
