# Retail Payment Tech Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for retail payment technology events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Attendee Name**. This is the name as it appears on your ID. 
2. Enter your **Email**. This is the email address we will use to contact you about the event.
3. You can optionally enter your **Phone Number**. We will use this if you have any questions or to confirm your event details.
4. Select your **Payment Choice**. You can choose one payment method for the event.
5. Select your **Payment Method**. This is optional, but if you want to be notified about available payment methods, select this field.
6. Enter the **Event Date** for the event.
7. Enter the **Event Time** for the event.
8. Add any **Notes** about the event, if you would like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for event notifications.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
- **Payment Choice** (select_one, required): Select one payment method for the event.
- **Payment Method** (select_multiple, optional): Select additional payment methods for the event, if applicable.
- **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the event.
- **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments about the event.
