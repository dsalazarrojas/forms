# Seaport Winter Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Seaport Winter Event Registration form, explaining each field in detail to ensure you complete it accurately and quickly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Seaport Winter Event Registration form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and their corresponding explanations below.
2. Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Submit your completed form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **base-info** (`base_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for your general information or comments. Please provide any relevant details about yourself.
* **base-preference** (`base_preference`, text, required/optional): This field is for any personal preferences or special requests you may have. Feel free to mention anything that might help us provide you with the best experience.
* **base-date-and-time** (`base_date_and_time`, text, required/optional): Please enter the date and time you plan to attend the event. This will help us plan for your visit.
* **base-note** (`base_note`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about your visit, feel free to share them here.
* **base-submit** (`base_submit`, text, required/optional): Please confirm your intention to attend the event by typing 'YES' or 'No' in this field.
* **base-payment-info** (`base_payment_info`, text, required/optional): This is for any payment details or information you'd like to provide.
* **base-submit-2** (`base_submit_2`, text, required/optional): This field is likely a duplicate of the previous 'base-submit' field, but please confirm your intention to attend again if you're certain.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and honest information to help us better organize the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the provided contact information.
* We appreciate your cooperation and look forward to seeing you at the Seaport Winter Event!
