# Developer Meetup Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Developer Meetup Signup Form" is a registration form designed for developers to share their details, preferences, and event information with the meetup organizers. It's a crucial tool for the organizers to get a clear picture of the attendees and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Meetup Details" section with your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the organizers.
2. Select a "Meetup Date" from the calendar provided.
3. Provide your "Organizer Info" in the text field, including any specific contact methods you'd like to share with the organizers.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Attendees" to indicate whether you'll be bringing any attendees with you.
5. Fill in your "Contact Details" in the text field, including your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
6. Enter your "Developer Info" in the text field, including any relevant details about your expertise or experience.
7. Provide "Event Details" in the text field, including any specific information about the event you'd like to share with the organizers.
8. If you have any food allergies, select "Yes" or "No" for "Food Allergies" and provide any relevant information in the "Food Allergies" text field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Dietary Preferences" and provide any relevant information in the text field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Registration Type" and choose your registration type accordingly.
11. Select "Yes" or "Inactive" for "Registration Status" and choose your status accordingly.
12. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" for "Event Type" and choose your event type accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meetup Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the organizers. (Text field)
* **Meetup Date**: Select a date for the meetup from the calendar. (Date field)
* **Organizer Info**: Provide your contact information with the organizers. (Text field)
* **Attendees**: Select "Yes" if you'll be bringing attendees with you, "No" otherwise. (Select Multiple)
* **Contact Details**: Enter your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to share with the organizers. (Text field)
* **Developer Info**: Enter your expertise, experience, or any other relevant details about yourself as a developer. (Text field)
* **Event Details**: Provide any specific information about the event you'd like to share with the organizers. (Text field)
* **Food Allergies**: Select "Yes" if you have any food allergies, "No" otherwise. (Select Multiple)
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select "Yes" if you have any dietary preferences, "No" otherwise. (Select Multiple)
* **Registration Type**: Select your registration type, "Yes" for registering, "No" for not registering. (Select One)
* **Registration Status**: Select your registration status, "Active" for registered, "Inactive" for not registered. (Select One)
* **Event Type**: Select your event type, "Yes" for event-related, "No" otherwise. (Select One)
