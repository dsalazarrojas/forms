# Streaming Trivia Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Trivia Quiz form is designed for participants to answer trivia questions related to streaming trivia events. The form is divided into several sections, each asking a different question or collecting information from the participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the text fields (forms 1, 3, 4, 7, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25) with your answers or information.
2. Select the correct answers for the select multiple or select one fields (forms 2, 8) and date, time, and number fields (forms 5, 9, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25).
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1:** Streaming Trivia Quiz (text): Enter the name or title of the streaming trivia quiz.
* **Form 3:** Trivia Quiz Form (text): Enter your answer to the first trivia question.
* **Form 4:** Trivia Quiz (select one): Select 'Yes' if you are participating in a trivia quiz.
* **Form 5:** Trivia Quiz Questions (date): Select the date of the trivia quiz (if applicable).
* **Form 6:** (Not applicable)
* **Form 7:** Trivia Quiz Form (text): Enter your answer to the second trivia question.
* **Form 8:** Participant Form (select multiple): Select the participant(s) who will be participating in the trivia quiz.
* **Form 9:** (Not applicable)
* **Form 10:** Trivia Quiz Form (text): Enter your answer to the third trivia question.
* **Form 11:** (Not applicable)
* **Form 12:** Trivia Quiz (text): Enter your answer to the fourth trivia question.
* **Form 13:** Trivia Quiz Form Questions (text): Enter your answer to the fifth trivia question.
* **Form 14:** Streaming Trivia Form (text): Enter your answer to the sixth trivia question.
* **Form 15:** Trivia Quiz Form (text): Enter your answer to the seventh trivia question.
* **Form 16:** Streaming Trivia Quiz Questions (text): Enter your answer to the eighth trivia question.
* **Form 17:** Participant Form 1 (number): Enter the number of participants.
* **Form 18:** Participant Form 2 (number): Enter the number of participants (if different from previous field).
* **Form 19:** (Not applicable)
* **Form 20:** Participant Form 3 (number): Enter the number of participants (if different from previous field).
* **Form 21:** Participant Form 4 (number): Enter the number of participants (if different from previous field).
