# Winter Fitness Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Fitness Challenge Registration form is used to collect user information for the Winter Fitness Challenge. Please provide accurate and relevant information to ensure successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields on the single page.
2. Be sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form (page 1, id: 1)
* **Registration Form**: This is the main registration form page. Please read the instructions carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
### Name (page 1, id: 2)
* **Name**: Please provide your name in this field.
### Email (page 1, id: 3)
* **Email**: Please provide your email address in this format: `user@domain.com`.
### Phone (page 1, id: 4)
* **Phone**: Please provide your phone number in this field.
### Select a Challenge (page 1, id: 5)
* **Select a Challenge**: Choose one or more challenges related to the winter fitness theme.
### Date (page 1, id: 6)
* **Date**: Please provide a date in the expected format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
### Challenge Details (page 1, id: 7)
* **Challenge Details**: Provide any additional information about the challenge you are registering for.
### Confirm Email (page 1, id: 8)
* **Confirm Email**: Please re-enter your email address to confirm it.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address for accuracy before proceeding.
- Be honest and accurate with the information you provide, as it will be used to process your registration.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out for assistance.
