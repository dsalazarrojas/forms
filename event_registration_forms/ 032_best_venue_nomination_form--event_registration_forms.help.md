# Best Venue Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Venue Nomination Form is designed to collect information from users to nominate a venue for an event. This form will help event planners manage nominations and ensure that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the personal information section, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select the relevant date for the nomination.
3. Choose the primary selection type (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker) that best applies to your nomination.
4. If necessary, provide a whole number for quantity or rating, and an estimated value in decimal format.
5. Enter any additional details or comments about the nomination.
6. Confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.
7. If applicable, select a time for your preferred scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local region. This field is optional but may be required in some cases.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the relevant date for the nomination.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to your nomination (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating if applicable.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional relevant information about the nomination.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any that apply to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated value in decimal format if applicable.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any final comments or notes about the nomination.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
- If you have any doubts about the form or its fields, please contact us for assistance.
- The form is designed to be completed once per submission, so please avoid duplicate nominations.
