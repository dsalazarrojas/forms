# green_materials_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register participants for the Green Materials Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and any other relevant participant information in the "participant" field.
2. Select the workshop you would like to attend from the list provided in the "workshop" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "notes" field.
6. If you are assigned a tool, enter its name in the "assigned_tool" field.
7. Provide your company name in the "participant_company" field.
8. Enter your job title in the "participant_jobtitle" field.
9. Enter your department in the "participant_department" field.
10. Enter your position in the "participant_position" field.
11. Select the date of the workshop you would like to attend in the "workshop_date" field.
12. Select the time of the workshop you would like to attend in the "workshop_time" field.
13. Confirm your email address in the "participant_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Workshop**: Select the workshop you would like to attend from the list provided.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you are assigned a tool, enter its name here.
* **Participant Company**: Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Participant Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Participant Department**: Enter your department.
* **Participant Position**: Enter your position.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Workshop Time**: Select the time of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Participant Email**: Confirm your email address.
