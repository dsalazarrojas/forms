# Charity Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a Charity Event Waiver Form, used to collect participant information and gather their consent for liability and medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
3. Make sure to check the "I understand" waiver statement at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Name (1)
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government ID.

### Email Address (2)
* **Email Address** (participant_email, email, required): Enter your contact email address.

### Phone Number (3)
* **Phone Number** (participant_phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

### Date of Birth (4)
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.

### Mailing Address (5)
* **Mailing Address** (mailing_address, text, required): Enter your full mailing address.

### Event Name (6)
* **Event Name** (event_name, text, required): Enter the name of the event you're participating in.

### Event Date (7)
* **Event Date** (event_date, date, required): Enter the date of the event.

### Participant Type (8)
* **Participant Type** (participant_type, select_one, required): Choose your participant type:
	+ Attendee
	+ Runner or walker
	+ Volunteer
	+ Performer
	+ Vendor
	+ Other

### Emergency Contact Name (9)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Phone (10)
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the emergency contact phone number.

### Medical Conditions or Allergies (11)
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (medical_conditions, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, describe them here.

### Health Insurance Provider (12)
* **Health Insurance Provider** (insurance_provider, text, optional): If you have health insurance, enter the name of your provider.

### Waiver Acknowledgment (13)
* **Waiver Acknowledgment** (waiver_statement, text, required): Read and agree to the waiver terms:
	+ I understand that participation involves physical activity and potential risks.
	+ I voluntarily assume all risks associated with this event.
	+ I release the organization from liability for injuries or damages.
	+ I agree to follow all event rules and guidelines.

### Release of Liability (14)
* **Release of Liability** (liability_release, select_one, required): Do you release the organization from liability:
	+ Yes I release
	+ No I do not release

### Consent to Medical Treatment (15)
* **Consent to Medical Treatment** (medical_treatment_consent, select_one, required): Do you consent to medical treatment in case of an emergency:
	+ Yes I consent
	+ No I do not consent

### Photo and Video Release (16)
* **Photo and Video Release** (photo_release, select_one, required): Do you permit the use of your photo and video for promotional purposes:
	+ Yes you may use my image
	+ No do not use my image

### Is Participant a Minor (17)
* **Is participant a minor** (minor_participant, select_one, required): Is the participant under 18 years old:
	+ True
	+ False

### Parent or Guardian Name (18)
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (parent_guardian_name, text, optional): If the participant is a minor, enter the name of the parent or guardian.

### Parent or Guardian Agreement (19)
* **Parent or Guardian Agreement** (parent_guardian_signature, select_one, optional): If the participant is a minor, sign this agreement:
	+ Yes I agree
	+ No I do not agree
	+ Not applicable

### Date of Signature (20)
* **Date of Signature** (signature_date, date, required): Enter the date of signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask event staff for clarification.
* If you're participating with a minor, make sure to fill out the parent or guardian information.
* Sign the form with a clear signature.
