# Branding Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Branding Workshop Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to tailor their experience and ensure a smooth registration process for the branding workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
- Submit the form once complete to proceed with your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Provide your valid email address for workshop communications.
- **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any necessary follow-up communication.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Provide your current job title.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company or organization name.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of employees your organization has.
- **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Choose the industry you work in.
- **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, required): Choose which workshop you wish to enroll in.
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select your preferred workshop date.
- **Branding Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current branding experience level.
- **Learning Objectives** (`learning_objectives`, text, required): Specify what you hope to learn from the workshop.
- **Current Branding Challenges** (`current_branding_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any current branding challenges you're facing.
- **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_multiple, required): Indicate any dietary restrictions you have.
- **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): If you need any accessibility accommodations.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the workshop.
- **Payment Responsibility** (`payment_responsibility`, select_one, required): Indicate who is paying for the workshop.
- **Marketing Communications** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, required): Choose if you'd like to receive future workshop updates.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Where did you find out about the workshop.
- **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): Share any questions or concerns you have before the workshop.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you've read and accept the terms and conditions.
