# Protest Activity Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals participating in protest activities, including marches, rallies, and other forms of demonstrations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Provide your name and contact information.
3. Select your level of participation in the protest, if applicable.
4. If you have any emergency contact information, please provide it.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of participating in the protest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant** (`participant`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name and any other names of participants if necessary.
* **Other Participants** (`other_participants`, `text`, required: false): List any other individuals participating in the protest, if applicable.
* **Demonstrations** (`demonstrations`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ March
	+ Rally
	+ Rally and March
	+ Sit-in
	+ Picket
	+ Picket and Sit-in
* **Other Demonstrations** (`other_demonstrations`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other demonstrations not listed above, please specify.
* **Participation** (`participation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Active Participation
	+ Passive Participation
	+ Observer
* **Other Participation** (`other_participation`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other level of participation not listed above, please specify.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): If you have an emergency contact person to be reached in case of an emergency, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, `note`, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of participating in the protest.
* **Other Terms** (`other_terms`, `text`, required: false): If you have any other terms or conditions that you would like to add, please specify.
