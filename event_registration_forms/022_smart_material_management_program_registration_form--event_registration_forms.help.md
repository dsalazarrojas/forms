This is a form to register individuals in a Smart Material Management Program. Before completing this form, please ensure that you are an authorized individual and that you are not a duplicate submission. This form is intended to collect basic contact information and other relevant details from individuals who wish to be part of the program. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

# Smart Material Management Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Material Management Program Registration Form is designed to collect contact information and other details from individuals who wish to be part of the program. This information will be used to provide program updates and materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for the program types you are interested in.
3. Enter your contact information and any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main form field where you can enter your basic information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your contact information, including any phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as it should be displayed in program communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address for program updates and notifications.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your address, including street number and postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional):
	+ Enter your time of birth, not required for most programs.
* **Select One Program Type** (`select_one_program_type`, select one, optional):
	+ Choose one of the program types you are interested in.
* **Select Multiple Program Types** (`select_multiple_program_types`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose multiple program types you are interested in.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
