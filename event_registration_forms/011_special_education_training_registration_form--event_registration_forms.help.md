# Special Education Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for special education training programs. It collects information from users to process their registrations and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select your desired training program from the options provided in the first section.
- Enter your contact information in the second section, including your name and contact number.
- In the third section, enter your email address to ensure proper communication.
- In the fourth section, provide your phone number for further contact.
- In the fifth section, select your payment method from the options provided.
- In the final section, include any additional information you want to share about your registration.
- Confirm your payment information in the last section by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one): Select the special education training program you are interested in from the provided options.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Enter your name and contact number for us to reach out to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Provide your phone number for further contact.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple): Choose the payment method you'd like to use for your registration.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note): Add any additional information about your registration.
* **Confirm Payment Info** (`confirm_payment`, select_one): Confirm your selected payment method to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Please make sure to select the correct training program and payment method to ensure smooth processing of your registration.
- Enter accurate contact information to avoid any miscommunication.
- If you have any additional information about your registration, please add it in the "Additional Info" field.
- Confirmation of your payment information is crucial for further steps.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
