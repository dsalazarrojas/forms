# store_layout_optimization_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for store layout optimization events. It requires minimal information from participants, such as their name, email, phone number, and location. This form is essential for event organization and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4. Enter your location in the "Participant Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name (text, required: false)**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Email (email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for future communication and event updates.
* **Participant Phone (text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Participant Location (text, required: false)**: Enter your location for geographical tracking and event organization purposes.

## Tips

Note: This form is designed for general use. If you have any special requirements or requests, please contact the event organizers.
