# Lighting And Camera Setup Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist for photographers to provide information about their lighting and camera setup for a shoot.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of camera you will be using (DSLR, Film, or Mirrorless).
2. Choose the type of lens you will be using (Prime, Zoom, or Macro).
3. Specify the lighting setup for the shoot (Natural Light, Artificial Light, or Mixed Light).
4. List the equipment you will be using.
5. Select the date and time of the shoot.
6. Provide the location of the shoot.
7. (Optional) Specify the crew members involved in the shoot.
8. (Optional) Provide contact information for emergency contact.
