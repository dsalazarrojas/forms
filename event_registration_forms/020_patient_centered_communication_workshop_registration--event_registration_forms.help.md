# Patient Centered Communication Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist users in completing the Patient Centered Communication Workshop Registration form, which is used to collect necessary information about attendees for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully and determine which fields are mandatory (indicated by "required") and which ones are optional.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Session Choices" and "Workshop Attendance" fields as needed.
3. Enter your preferred date in the "Session Date" and "Training Date" fields in MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Enter your name, provider name, and organization details in the text fields.
5. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the "Special Needs" and "Payment Method" fields as needed.
6. Enter your email address, phone number, and payment amount in the respective fields.
7. Enter any special instructions or workshop notes in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your session preference.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your preferred session date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, note, required/optional): This field is a free-text field for providing any necessary information about the attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in 123-456-7890 format.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for any special needs.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Provider** (`provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the training provider.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the training date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the workshop location.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the workshop time.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Workshop Attendance** (`workshop_attendance`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your workshop attendance.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for the payment method you will use.
* **Payment Method Other** (`payment_method_other`, text, required/optional): Enter any other payment method if "Yes" is selected.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, text, required/optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, note, required/optional): This field is a free-text field for providing any notes about the workshop.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required/optional): Enter any special instructions for the workshop.
