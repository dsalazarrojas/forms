# industry-conference-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is an industry conference registration form designed for easy and hassle-free registration and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your **Full Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Company** in the third field.
4. Enter your **Job Title** in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Department** in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Role** in the sixth field.
7. Enter your **Phone** in the seventh field.
8. Select your **Job Role** from the options (Yes/No) in the eighth field.
9. Choose your **Payment Method** from the options (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash) in the ninth field.
10. Enter your **Payment Date** in the tenth field.
11. Choose your **Payment Time** in the eleventh field.
12. Enter your **Payment Total** in the twelfth field.
13. Select the attendees you will be attending with (**Attendee 1**, **Attendee 2**, **Attendee 3**) in the thirteenth field.
14. Enter any additional comments or notes in the fourteenth field (Payment Confirmation).
15. Enter the **Start Date** of the event in the fifteenth field.
16. Enter the **Start Time** of the event in the sixteenth field.
17. Enter the **End Date** of the event in the seventeenth field.
18. Enter the **End Time** of the event in the eighteenth field.
19. Enter a brief **Description** of the event in the nineteenth field.
20. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** by selecting "Yes" in the twentieth field.
21. Enter the **Accept Terms and Conditions URL** in the twenty-first field.
22. Choose your **Payment Status** from the options (Pending, Paid) in the twenty-second field.
23. Enter the number of **Attendee Quantity** you will be bringing in the twenty-third field.
24. Choose your **Payment Tender** from the options (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash) in the twenty-fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (2, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Company** (3, Text, Optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (4, Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (5, Text, Optional): Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Role** (6, Text, Optional): Enter your role or position in your company.
* **Phone** (7, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Role** (8, Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" if you will be attending with a job role or "No" if not.
* **Payment Method** (9, Select One, Optional): Choose your payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash).
* **Payment Date** (10, Date, Optional): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Time** (11, Time, Optional): Choose your payment time.
* **Payment Total** (12, Number, Optional): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Attendee** (13, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the attendees you will be attending with.
* **Payment Confirmation** (14, Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about payment.
* **Start Date** (15, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Start Time** (16, Time, Optional): Choose the start time of the event.
* **End Date** (17, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the event.
* **End Time** (18, Time, Optional): Choose the end time of the event.
* **Description** (19, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Terms and Conditions** (20, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Accept Terms and Conditions URL** (21, Text, Optional): Enter the URL of the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Status** (22, Select One, Optional): Choose your payment status (Pending, Paid).
* **Attendee Quantity** (23, Number, Optional): Enter the number of attendees you will be bringing.
* **Payment Tender** (24, Select One, Optional): Choose your payment tender (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash).
