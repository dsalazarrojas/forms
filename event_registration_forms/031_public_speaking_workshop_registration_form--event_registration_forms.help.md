# Public Speaking Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Public Speaking Workshop. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read and understand the instructions.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
3. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
4. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Time of Birth
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the correct format (HH:MM).

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.

### Country
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your residence.

### Additional Tips
* Please ensure all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
