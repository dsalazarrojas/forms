# DIY Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register their attendance for a DIY Workshop event. You will need to provide your personal and workshop preferences to help us better organize and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form instructions carefully and make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Fill in your Participant Name and Email (if you have one) in the first section.
- Choose your preferred Workshop Session and Location in the second section.
- Fill in your Workshop Date and Time in the third section.
- Provide any additional comments or notes about the event in the fourth section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (required): This is your name that will be used for communication purposes. Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email (optional)**: If you have a valid email, please provide it so we can contact you about workshop details and updates.
* **Phone** (optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for any last-minute changes or updates.
* **Workshop Date** (required): Select the date of the workshop you plan to attend from the calendar below.
* **Workshop Time** (required): Choose the time of the workshop you would like to attend from the list below.
* **Workshop Session** (required): Select one of the available workshop session times from the list below.
* **Workshop Location** (required): Choose one or more locations where the workshop will take place.
* **Workshop Notes** (optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about the workshop, please enter them here.
* **Participant Comments** (optional): If you have any specific requests or concerns, please write them in the text area below.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your registration successfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
