# Volunteer Coordinator Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Coordinator Training Form is a tool used to register volunteer coordinators for training programs. It helps in collecting contact information and training preferences from volunteer coordinators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your **Event Name** in the field "Event Name".
*   Select your **Volunteer Role** from the options provided.
*   Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
*   Choose your **Training Preferences** (Online or In-Person).
*   Select the **Training Status** (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
*   Provide any **Comments** regarding your training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
*   **Volunteer Role**: Select your role as a volunteer (Volunteer or Organizer).
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are registering for.
*   **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Training Preferences**: Choose whether you prefer training online or in-person.
*   **Training Dates**: Enter the date of the training you are interested in.
*   **Training Time**: Enter the time of the training you are interested in.
*   **Training Location**: Enter the location of the training you are interested in.
*   **Training Status**: Select the status of your training (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
*   **Comments**: Add any comments about your training.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you provide accurate information to ensure smooth coordination of the training sessions.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your training coordinator.
