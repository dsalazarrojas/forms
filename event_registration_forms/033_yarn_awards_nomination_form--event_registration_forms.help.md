# yarn_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yarn Awards nomination form is designed to collect information for recognizing individuals or organizations that have made outstanding contributions to the fiber industry. The information collected will be used to evaluate and consider nominees for the Yarn Awards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the nomination form, starting with Page 2: Nominator Details.
2. Select the correct Nomination Category (Page 3) that best fits the nominee's contribution.
3. Enter the Nominee's Name (Page 4).
4. Provide a brief Nomination Title (Page 5) that summarizes the nominee's contribution.
5. Choose a Nomination Date (Page 6) when the contribution was made.
6. Fill out the Nominator's Contact Information (Page 7).
7. Enter the Nominee's Address (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominator Details** (`nominator_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of nominator you are representing the nominee. Choose from the following options: Yarn Guild, Fiber Artist, Community Champion, or Other.
* **Nomination Category** (`nomination_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best fits the nominee's contribution. Options include:
	+ Outstanding Contribution to Fiber Arts
	+ Outstanding Contribution to Yarn Community Building
	+ Outstanding Contribution to Fiber Arts Education
* **Nominee Name** (`nominee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Nomination Title** (`nomination_title`, text, optional): Provide a brief title that summarizes the nominee's contribution.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the contribution was made.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Nominator Affiliation** (`nominator_affiliation`, text, optional): Enter your affiliation or organization (if applicable).
* **Nominee Address** (`nominee_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person or organization being nominated.
* **Nominator Message** (`nominator_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message to support the nomination.
