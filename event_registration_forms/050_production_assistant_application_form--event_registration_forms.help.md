# Production Assistant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in working as production assistants on various production sets. It is intended for production company staff to gather details about applicants' availability, experience, and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill out your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (if applicable).
5. Choose your preferred start date in the "start date" field.
6. Choose your preferred end date in the "end date" field.
7. Select your preferred shift in the "shift" field (morning, afternoon, or evening).
8. Select all the shifts you are available to work in the "available shifts" field.
9. Select all the days you are available to work in the "available days" field.
10. Choose your preferred start time in the "start time" field.
11. Choose your preferred end time in the "end time" field.
12. If you have any additional comments or information, you can enter them in the "note" field (optional).
13. Enter the name of the department you are interested in working with in the "department" field.
14. Enter the name of the production company you are applying to in the "production company" field.
15. Enter a brief description of your experience in the "experience" field.
16. Enter your job title in the "job title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you do not have a phone number, you can leave this field blank).
* **Start date**: Choose the date you are available to start working on the production set.
* **End date**: Choose the date you are available to end working on the production set.
* **Shift**: Select one of the following shifts: morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Available shifts**: Select all the shifts you are available to work (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Available days**: Select all the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
* **Start time**: Choose the time you are available to start working on each shift.
* **End time**: Choose the time you are available to end working on each shift.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about your application (optional).
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department you are interested in working with.
* **Production company**: Enter the name of the production company you are applying to.
* **Experience**: Enter a brief description of your experience in the production assistant field.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out.
