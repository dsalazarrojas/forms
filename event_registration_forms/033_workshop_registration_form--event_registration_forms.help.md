# Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you sign up for various workshops offered by our organization. Please fill it out carefully to ensure you receive the necessary updates and information about the workshops you've selected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the registration form in the following pages. Each page is designed to collect specific information about your preferences and details.
2. Ensure you provide accurate information, especially for your name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your preferred workshop type from the options provided.
4. Choose the correct date and time for the workshop you want to attend.
5. If you have any specific comments or requests, please fill out the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`workshop_registration_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form and serves as a header.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please fill out your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address so we can keep you updated about the workshop details and any changes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of workshop you are interested in attending. You can choose one from the options provided.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the workshop you want to attend. Please ensure you select a date that fits your schedule.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the workshop you want to attend. Please ensure you select a time that fits your schedule.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any specific comments or requests, please fill out this field. This will help us understand your requirements better.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all the required fields (name, email, phone number).
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us, and we will be happy to assist you.
