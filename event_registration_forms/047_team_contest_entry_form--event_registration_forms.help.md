# Team Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Contest Entry Form is a registration form for teams to participate in a contest. It collects information about the team, its participants, and the team captain's contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. List the team participants in the "Team Participants" field.
3. Enter the team captain's email address in the "Team Captain E-mail" field.
4. Enter the team captain's phone number in the "Team Captain Phone" field.
5. Enter the team captain's name in the "Team Captain" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team. This is the name that will be displayed on the contest leaderboard.
* **Team Participants** (`team_participants`, text, required: false): List all the members of your team, separated by commas (e.g., John, Mary, Bob, etc.).
* **Team Captain E-mail** (`team_captain_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your team captain. This will be used to contact the team about contest updates and notifications.
* **Team Captain Phone** (`team_captain_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your team captain. This will be used to contact the team about contest updates and notifications.
* **Team Captain** (`team_captain`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team captain. This is the person who will be responsible for leading your team during the contest.
