# Lifetime Achievement Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect nominations for individuals who have made significant contributions to a field or community over the course of their lifetime. It helps us recognize and award those who deserve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields (see "Field-by-Field Explanation" for details).
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Name (<name>nomination_name</name>, <type>text</type>, required>false)
Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.

### Nominee Name (<name>nominee_name</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Enter the name of the individual you are nominating.

### Nomination Category (<name>nomination_category</name>, <type>text</type>, required>false)
Enter the category in which the individual is being nominated. This will help us understand their area of contribution.

### Nominee Category (<name>nominee_category</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Enter the category in which the individual belongs.

### Nomination Date (<name>nomination_date</name>, <type>date</type>, required>false)
Enter the date of the nomination. This will help us track the nomination timeline.

### Nomination By (<name>nomination_nominator</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required.false)
Indicate whether the nomination was made by the individual themselves or by someone else.

### Reason For Nomination (<name>reason_nomination</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Explain the reason why you are nominating this individual. Please provide details about their achievements and contributions.

### Nominee Details (<name>nominee_details</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Enter any additional details about the individual you are nominating.

### Additional Info (<name>additional_info</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to the nomination.

### Confirm Nomination (<name>confirm_nomination</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required>false)
Confirm that you are sure about the nomination. If unsure, please provide a reason for your uncertainty.

### Notes (<name>notes</name>, <type>text</type>, required.false)
Enter any additional notes about the nomination. This will help us understand your perspective.

Note to the reviewer: this guide is intended to be concise and practical. Please review it before submitting the form to ensure it meets your needs.
