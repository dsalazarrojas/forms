## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who want to enter the Mid Autumn Festival Giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please make sure you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. This form has multiple pages with different fields, each with its own purpose. Please fill out the required fields, which are marked as 'true' in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter the name of the festival you are interested in.
* **Email** (`email`, email, true): This field is to collect your email address. Please enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your phone number. We may contact you via phone if needed.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): This field is to collect your age. Please enter your age in numbers.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_one, true): You can choose how you want to enter the festival. Please select 'Online', 'Offline' or 'Both'.
* **Submission** (`submission`, note, optional): This field is a note field for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): You can choose which category you are interested in. Please select 'Dance', 'Music', 'Food', or 'Games'.
* **Prize** (`prize`, select_multiple, optional): You can choose which prize you are interested in. Please select 'Prize 1', 'Prize 2', or 'Prize 3'.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): This field is for any other information you would like to provide.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, true): This field is to collect the time when you made your submission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, true): This field is to collect the date when you made your submission.
* **Submission Date Time** (`submission_date_time`, text, optional): This field is a text field for any additional information about your submission time.
* **Prize Time** (`prize_time`, time, optional): This field is to collect the time when you received your prize.
* **Prize Date** (`prize_date`, date, optional): This field is to collect the date when you received your prize.
* **Prize Date Time** (`prize_date_time`, text, optional): This field is a text field for any additional information about your prize time.
* **Submission Location** (`submission_location`, text, optional): This field is to collect the location where you made your submission.
* **Prize Location** (`prize_location`, text, optional): This field is to collect the location where you received your prize.
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your entry.
* **Terms & Condition** (`terms_condition`, note, true): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting this form.
* **Submission Form** (`submission_form`, note, optional): This field is a note field for any additional comments or information about your submission.
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, note, optional): This field is a note field for any information about your privacy.
* **Entry Number** (`entry_number`, number, true): This field is to collect a unique number for your entry.
* **Prize Type** (`prize_type`, select_multiple, true): You can choose which type of prize you are interested in. Please select 'Prize Type 1', 'Prize Type 2', or 'Prize Type 3'.
* **Prize Value** (`prize_value`, number, true): This field is to collect the value of the prize you are interested in.
* **Prize Status** (`prize_status`, select_one, true): You can choose the status of your prize. Please select 'Unclaimed', 'Claimed', or 'Void'.

Please note that all required fields are marked as 'true' in the form. Please fill out all required fields to complete this form.
