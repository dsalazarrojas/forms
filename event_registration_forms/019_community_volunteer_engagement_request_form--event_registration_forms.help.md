# Community Volunteer Engagement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests, availability, and qualifications for volunteer opportunities. It will help us match you with the right volunteer engagement that aligns with your goals and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirement.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the options that best fit your interests, availability, and qualifications.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (\*)**: Please enter your first and last name as you would like to be known by our team.
* **Email Address \* (\*)**: Enter your primary contact email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number \* (\*)**: Provide a reliable phone number for communication and coordination.
* **Mailing Address \* (\*)**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth \* (\*)**: Enter your date of birth for verification and eligibility purposes.
* **What type of volunteer engagement are you seeking? (\*)**: Select one of the options:
	+ One-time Event: A single-day volunteer opportunity.
	+ Short-term Project (1-3 months): A volunteer engagement that lasts several months.
	+ Long-term Commitment (6+ months): A volunteer engagement that requires a 6-month or longer commitment.
	+ Ongoing Regular Volunteering: A recurring volunteer opportunity.
	+ Flexible/As Needed: Volunteer when and how you can.
	+ Virtual Volunteering: A volunteer opportunity that can be done remotely.
* **What causes interest you most? (\*)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Education and Youth
	+ Health and Wellness
	+ Environment
	+ Animal Welfare
	+ Arts and Culture
	+ Homelessness and Housing
	+ Hunger and Food Security
	+ Senior Services
	+ Disability Services
	+ Veterans Services
	+ Community Development
	+ Disaster Relief
	+ Other
* **Do you have a specific program in mind?**: If you know which program or project you're interested in, please enter its name.
* **What skills can you offer? (\*)**: Select all that apply:
	+ Teaching/Tutoring
	+ Mentoring
	+ Administrative Support
	+ Event Planning
	+ Marketing/Social Media
	+ Graphic Design
	+ Photography/Videography
	+ Web Development
	+ Writing/Editing
	+ Fundraising
	+ Grant Writing
	+ Counseling
	+ Legal Services
	+ Medical/Healthcare
	+ Construction/Handyman
	+ Gardening/Landscaping
	+ Cooking/Food Prep
	+ Driving/Delivery
	+ Translation/Interpretation
	+ Other
* **Please describe your skills in detail**: This is your chance to explain your skills and qualifications in more detail.
* **Weekday Availability \* (\*)**: Select the days and times you are available to volunteer.
* **Weekend Availability \* (\*)**: Select the days and times you are available to volunteer on weekends.
* **How many hours per week can you volunteer? (\*)**: Select the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
* **When can you start volunteering? \* (\*)**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Do you have prior volunteer experience? \* (\*)**: Select one of the options:
	+ No, first time
	+ Yes, 1-2 organizations
	+ Yes, 3-5 organizations
	+ Yes, 6+ organizations
* **Please describe your volunteer experience**: This is your chance to explain your previous volunteer experience and roles.
* **Are you interested in group volunteering?**: Select one of the options:
	+ No, individual only
	+ Yes, with family
	+ Yes, with friends
	+ Yes, with coworkers
	+ Yes, with school group
	+ Yes, interested in both
* **If yes, approximately how many people?**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Do you have reliable transportation?**: Select one of the options:
	+ Yes, personal vehicle
	+ Yes, public transportation
	+ No, need assistance
* **I consent to a background check if required**: Select one of the options:
	+ Yes, I Consent
	+ No, I Do Not Consent
* **Are you available for required training sessions?**: Select one of the options:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, with advance notice
	+ Depends on schedule
* **Are there any physical activities you cannot perform?**: Please enter any physical limitations or restrictions.
* **Emergency Contact Name \* (\*)**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone \* (\*)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **How did you hear about volunteer opportunities?**: Select one of the options:
	+ Organization Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Friend or Family
	+ Community Event
	+ School/University
	+ Workplace
	+ Volunteer Matching Site
	+ Newspaper/Magazine
	+ Other
* **Why do you want to volunteer with us?**: Enter your motivation and goals.
* **Any additional comments or questions?**: Enter any additional information or questions you may have.
* **Request Date \* (\*)**: This field is automatically populated with today's date.
