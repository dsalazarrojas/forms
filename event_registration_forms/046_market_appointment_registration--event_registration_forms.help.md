# Market Appointment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule a market appointment between a market visitor and a market vendor. It is essential to provide accurate contact information and scheduling details to facilitate a successful meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you are a market vendor or a market visitor.
2. Select the schedule for your visit, either during or outside of event hours.
3. Choose a date and time for your visit.
4. Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
5. Add any additional information about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name.
* **Are you a** (visitor_or_vendor, select_one, optional): Please choose whether you are a market vendor or a market visitor.
* **Would you like to schedule a visit?** (schedule_visit, select_one, optional): Select the schedule for your visit, either during or outside of event hours.
* **Visit Date** (visit_date, date, optional): Choose a date for your visit.
* **Visit Time** (visit_time, time, optional): Choose a time for your visit.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your visit.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (company, text, optional): Enter your company name.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a successful meeting.
* If you are a market vendor, be prepared to respond to visit requests from market visitors.
* If you are a market visitor, be prepared to schedule visits with market vendors.
* This form should not be used for general contact or sales purposes.
