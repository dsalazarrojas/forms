# Family Rental Sweepstakes Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal and demographic information from participants in the "Family Rental Sweepstakes" event. It is not a requirement for entry, but rather an optional way to provide more information for the organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your sweepstakes entry date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Provide your name as you would like it to appear on any promotional materials.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
4. Enter your phone number for additional communication.
5. Enter your home or work address for prize delivery or additional communication.
6. If you are entering with your company, enter the name of your company.
7. Choose the method through which you entered the sweepstakes: Sweepstakes, Referral, or Online.
8. Select any social media platforms you have a presence on.
9. Enter your age.
10. Enter the number of people entering with you.
11. Enter the number of children you have.
12. If you have any additional comments or requests, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Date**: The date you are entering the sweepstakes. Format: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Name**: Your name as you would like it to appear on any promotional materials.
* **Email**: Your email address for us to contact you with updates.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for additional communication or prize delivery.
* **Address**: Your home or work address for prize delivery or additional communication.
* **Company Name**: If you are entering with your company, enter the name here.
* **Entry Method**: How you entered the sweepstakes: Sweepstakes, Referral, or Online.
* **Social Media**: Select any social media platforms you have a presence on (e.g. Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
* **Age**: Your age.
* **Number of People**: The number of people entering with you.
* **Number of Children**: The number of children you have.
* **Prize**: Any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional information you would like to provide.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your entry is valid and eligible for the sweepstakes. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
