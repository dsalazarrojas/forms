# cultural_event_vendor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by event organizers to collect payment details and event information from vendors after an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Vendor Name**, which should be the name of the vendor you are checking out.
2. Provide the **Vendor Email**, which is the contact email for the vendor.
3. Enter the **Phone**, which is the contact phone number for the vendor.
4. Select the **Payment Terms** that apply to the vendor, which can be 30 days, 60 days, or Net 30/60.
5. Choose the **Payment Method(s)** used by the vendor (e.g., Bank Draft, Certified Check, Credit Card, PayPal).
6. Enter the **Event Date**, which is the date of the event.
7. Enter the **Amount Due**, which is the total amount the vendor needs to pay.
8. Select the **Payment Status** of the vendor (e.g., Not Paid, Paid, Partially Paid).
9. Add any additional **Notes** about the vendor or event.
10. Enter the **Vendor Address**, which is the address of the vendor.
11. Enter the **Event Name**, which is the name of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the vendor.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the vendor.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, optional): Select the payment terms that apply to the vendor (e.g., 30 days, 60 days, Net 30/60).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment method used by the vendor (e.g., Bank Draft, Certified Check, Credit Card, PayPal).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Amount Due** (`amount_due`, number, required): Enter the total amount due from the vendor.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status of the vendor (e.g., Not Paid, Paid, Partially Paid).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the vendor or event.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
