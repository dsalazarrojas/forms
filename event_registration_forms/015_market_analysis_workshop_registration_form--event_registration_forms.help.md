# market_analysis_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and register participants for a market analysis workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the participant's name in the **Participants Name** field.
* Enter the participant's company in the **Company** field.
* Enter the participant's role in the **Role** field.
* Enter the participant's job title in the **Job Title** field.
* Enter the participant's email address in the **Email** field.
* Enter the participant's phone number in the **Phone** field.
* Select the date of the event in the **Date of Event** field.
* Select the time of the event in the **Time of Event** field.
* Enter the duration of the workshop in the **Workshop Duration** field.
* Select the start date of the workshop in the **Workshop Start Date** field.
* Select the end date of the workshop in the **Workshop End Date** field.
* Enter the venue of the workshop in the **Venue** field.
* Enter the location of the workshop in the **Location** field.
* Add any additional notes about the participant in the **Notes** field.
* Select the payment method used to register the participant in the **Payment Method** field.
* Select the payment status of the participant in the **Payment Status** field.
* Select the participant's status in the **Participant Status** field.
* Select the type of participant in the **Participant Type** field.
* Select the status of the workshop in the **Workshop Status** field.
* Enter the ID of the event in the **Event ID** field.
* Enter the name of the person who created this entry in the **Created By** field.
* Enter the name of the person who updated this entry in the **Updated By** field.
* Enter the date this entry was created in the **Created At** field.
* Enter the date this entry was updated in the **Updated At** field.
* Select the tool assigned to this participant in the **Assigned Tool** field.
