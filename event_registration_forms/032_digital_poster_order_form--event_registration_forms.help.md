# digital_poster_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to order digital posters with specific specifications, delivery details, and instructions. It's used by the business to gather essential information for fulfilling orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the digital poster order form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out each field carefully, as it's essential for the business to deliver the digital poster correctly.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Specifications**: This field is for specifying the details of the digital poster, such as the design, size, or content requirements. Please provide a clear and concise description of your order requirements.
* **Quantity**: If you need multiple digital posters, enter the total number of copies you require. This will help the business prepare accordingly.
* **Delivery Details**: Enter a brief description of the delivery method you prefer (Standard or Express). This will influence the delivery process.
* **Delivery Method**: Select the delivery method you prefer for your digital poster order:
	+ **Standard**: Default delivery method, which may take longer than Express.
	+ **Express**: Faster delivery option, ideal for urgent orders.
* **Delivery Address**: Choose the delivery address from the available options: 123 Main St, 456 Oak St, or 789 Maple Rd. If your address is not listed, contact the business for assistance.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date you require the digital poster to be delivered. Please use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Delivery Time**: Select the preferred time of day for the delivery.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Provide any special instructions for the delivery, such as signature required or specific delivery location.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you need to make changes to your order, please contact the business directly.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the business for assistance.
