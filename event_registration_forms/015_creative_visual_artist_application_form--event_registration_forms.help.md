# Creative Visual Artist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Visual Artist Application Form is designed to collect essential information from creative visual artists who are interested in joining or participating in our community, platform, or organization. This form is used to gather details such as name, contact information, and other relevant data that will help us understand their artistic profile and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the form**: Begin by entering your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. **Enter your last name**: In the "Last Name" field, enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Provide your email address**: In the "Email" field, enter your official email address that you use to receive important updates and communications.
4. **Add your phone number**: In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, text, required):** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, text, required):** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (`email`, email, required):** Enter your official email address that you use to receive important updates and communications.
* **Phone (`phone`, text, optional):** Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* Double-check your input before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
