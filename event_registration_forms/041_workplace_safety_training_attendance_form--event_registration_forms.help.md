# workplace_safety_training_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The workplace safety training attendance form is used to register an attendee's attendance to a workplace safety training session. This form captures essential information about the training session and the attendee's attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have attended the relevant training session.
2. Select the training session for which you are registering your attendance.
3. Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
5. Select the status of your attendance in the "Training Attendance Status" field.
6. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
7. Optionally, enter the date and time of your attendance in the "Training Attendance Date" and "Time of Attendance" fields.
8. Enter the number of times you have attended the training session in the "Session Attendance Count" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Trainer Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the trainer who led the training session.
- **Attendee Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification card.
- **Attendee Email** (email, required): Enter your official email address.
- **Date of Attendance** (date, not required): Enter the date of the training session you attended.
- **Time of Attendance** (time, not required): Enter the time of the training session you attended.
- **Training Attendance Status** (select multiple, required): Select the status of your attendance, which can be "Completed", "Not Completed", or "No Attendance".
- **Additional Comments** (text, not required): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
- **Session Attendance Count** (number, not required): Enter the number of times you have attended the training session.
- **Training Attendance Date** (date, not required): Enter the date you attended the training session.

## Tips

* Ensure you have attended the relevant training session before registering.
* Enter accurate information in all fields.
* Select the correct training attendance status.
* Enter any additional comments to provide feedback about the training session.
