# governance_review_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a governance review workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  On the first page, fill in your participant information.
2.  On the second page, select "Yes" if you are interested in attending the workshop.
3.  On the third page, enter the date of the workshop you are interested in.
4.  On the fourth page, enter your name as it should be displayed for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter your participant information. This will help us contact you if necessary.
*   **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to indicate your interest in attending the workshop.
*   **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop you are interested in. Format should be in the YYYY-MM-DD format (YYYY: 4-digit year, MM: 2-digit month, DD: 2-digit day).
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed for the workshop.

## Tips
Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
