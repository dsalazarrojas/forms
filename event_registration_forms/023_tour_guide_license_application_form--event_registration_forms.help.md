# Tour Guide License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Guide License Application Form is used to collect information from potential tour guides who are interested in obtaining a tour guide license. This form is designed to gather personal and professional details that will be used to evaluate their eligibility for a tour guide license.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each page of the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of tour guide license you are applying for (Tour Guide, Tour Guide (International), Tour Guide (Specialty)).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the number of years you have been in the tour guiding profession (1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Educational Level** (`education_level`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your educational level (Primary, Secondary, Higher, Doctoral).
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the languages you speak (English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Other).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the license application.
* **Agreement 2** (`agreement_2`, select_one, required/optional): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the license application.
* **Agreement 3** (`agreement_3`, select_one, required/optional): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the license application.
* **License Status** (`license_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the current status of your license (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your license expires, if applicable.
