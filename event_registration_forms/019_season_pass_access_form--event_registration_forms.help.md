> # Season Pass Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information from individuals or teams seeking access to a season pass.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you've included all necessary information.
2. Enter the Application Form with your details and attach any required attachments (Application Form Attachment 1-4).
3. Choose your season pass type and select 'Yes' for Team Pass if you're applying on behalf of a team.
4. Enter your contact details and confirm them.
5. Choose your payment method and enter the payment details.
6. Attach payment receipts as needed.
7. Review and agree to any applicable agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (`application_form`, `text`, required: false): Enter your application form with your details.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm your contact details.
* **Season Pass Type** (`season_pass_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your season pass type (Individual Season Pass or Team Pass).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Season Pass Number** (`season_pass_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your season pass number.
* **Season Pass Type Other** (`season_pass_type_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other season pass type if not already listed.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter team details if applicable.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm team members.
* **Team Pass Number** (`team_pass_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter your team pass number.
* **Team Pass Type Other** (`team_pass_type_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other team pass type if not already listed.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm payment method.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your payment details.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the payment date.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment confirmation details.
* **Application Form Attachment 1** (`application_form_attachment_1`, `text`, required: false): Attach the application form attachment 1.
* **Application Form Attachment 2** (`application_form_attachment_2`, `text`, required: false): Attach the application form attachment 2.
* **Payment Receipt Attachment** (`payment_receipt_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach the payment receipt attachment.
* **Application Form Attachment 3** (`application_form_attachment_3`, `text`, required: false): Attach the application form attachment 3.
* **Payment Receipt Attachment 2** (`payment_receipt_attachment_2`, `text`, required: false): Attach the payment receipt attachment 2.
* **Application Form Attachment 4** (`application_form_attachment_4`, `text`, required: false): Attach the application form attachment 4.
* **Agreement Form Attachment** (`agreement_form_attachment`, `text`, required: false): Attach the agreement form attachment.

## Tips

* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
