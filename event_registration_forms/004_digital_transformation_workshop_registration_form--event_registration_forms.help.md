# Digital Transformation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to register participants for the Digital Transformation Workshop. It collects essential information about each attendee, including registration details, preferences, payment details, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as `required: true`).
2. For optional fields (marked as `required: false`), you can choose to provide additional information or leave them blank.
3. Double-check your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Registration Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: true`)
Registration details, such as name, title, and contact information, are requested on this page.

### **Preferences** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: true`)
Please provide your preferences for the workshop, including any dietary requirements or special requests.

### **Payment Details** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: true`)
Enter your payment details, such as method and amount.

### **Notes** (`id: 4`, `type: note`, `required: false`)
Leave any additional comments or notes for the organizers in this field. This is an optional field.

### **Submission** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, `required: false`)
This field is for any additional information you'd like to submit. This is also an optional field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and comprehensive registration.
* Double-check your submission before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
