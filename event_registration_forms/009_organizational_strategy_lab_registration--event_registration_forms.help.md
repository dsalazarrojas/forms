# Organizational Strategy Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participation in the Organizational Strategy Lab event. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure proper registration and event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name.
2. Provide your User Email.
3. Enter your Organization.
4. Specify your Role.
5. Enter your Session Preferences.
6. Enter your Session Preferences Goals.
7. Enter your Session Preferences Preferences.
8. Choose the Event from the list of options.
9. Select the Date and Time of the event.
10. Add any additional Notes.
11. Enter your Phone Number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the event materials.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to enable communication and updates.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name or affiliation.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Specify your role within your organization or area of expertise.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any preferences or requirements you have for your session.
* **Session Preferences Goals** (`session_preferences_goals`, text, optional): Enter your goals for attending this session.
* **Session Preferences Preferences** (`session_preferences_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any additional preferences or requirements for your session.
* **Event** (`event_id`, text, optional): Select the event you wish to register for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the event you wish to attend.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the event you wish to attend.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.
