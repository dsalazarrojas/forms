# Music Streaming Service License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for artists or content creators to license their music for our music streaming service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to submit the form once you have all the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submit Form
* **Submit Form** (`form_submission`, `number`, required: false): This is the first page of the form. You need to confirm that you've read and understood the instructions before proceeding. Click "Next" to move on to the next page.

### Artist
* **Artist** (`artist`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can enter your artist name. Please enter the name as you would like it to appear on our platform.

### Song Title
* **Song Title** (`song_title`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can enter the title of the song you're submitting for licensing. Please make sure it's the correct title as it will appear on our platform.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it's accurate.
* If you've already submitted a song to our service, please don't resubmit it. You can check the status of your previous submissions in your account settings.
* Make sure to provide all required information for a better chance of getting your music on our platform.
