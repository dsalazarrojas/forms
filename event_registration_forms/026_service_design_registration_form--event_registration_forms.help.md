# Service Design Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect registration information from service designers who want to participate in a service design event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name field with your first name.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Enter your company information in the Company fields, including Company, Company City, Company State, Company Zip, and Company Country.
5. If you are a service designer, fill out the Service Designer fields, including Service Designer, Service Designer Email, Service Designer Phone, Service Designer Job Title, Service Designer Role, and Service Designer Note.
6. If you are an event organizer, fill out the Event Organizer field.
7. Enter event details in the Service Details and Event Details fields.
8. Select the assigned tool from the Assigned Tool field.
9. Choose your role from the Role field.
10. Enter the date of the event in the Date field.
11. Choose the time of the event in the Time field.
12. Enter any additional notes in the Note field.
