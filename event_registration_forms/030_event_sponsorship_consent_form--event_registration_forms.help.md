# Event Sponsorship Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect sponsorship information and consent for events. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that you can participate in the event as a sponsor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details and sponsorship information.
2. Review each field to ensure you have provided the required information.
3. Once you have filled out the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsorship Title** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_title`): A title to briefly describe the event. This field is required.
* **First Name** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_first_name`): Please enter your first name. This field is required.
* **Last Name** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_last_name`): Please enter your last name. This field is required.
* **Email** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_email`): A valid email address is required. This field is required.
* **Phone** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_phone`): Please enter your phone number. This field is optional but recommended.
* **Address** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_address`): Your address where you can be reached. This field is optional but recommended.
* **City** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_city`): The city where you reside. This field is optional but recommended.
* **State** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_state`): Your state of residence. This field is optional but recommended.
* **ZIP** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_zip`): Your ZIP code. This field is optional but recommended.
* **Company** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_company`): Your company name (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **Job Title** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_job_title`): Your job title (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **Sponsor Type** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_sponsor_type`): Please select the type of sponsorship you are providing. This field is required.
* **Agreement** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_agreement`): Please read and agree to the terms of the sponsorship. This field is required.
* **Sponsorship Terms** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_sponsorship_terms`): A description of the sponsorship terms. This field is required.
* **Sponsorship Commitment** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_sponsorship_commitment`): Please select the commitment level for the sponsorship. This field is required.
* **Event Name** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_name`): The name of the event. This field is required.
* **Event Date** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_date`): The date of the event. This field is required.
* **Event Time** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_time`): The time of the event. This field is required.
* **Event Location** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_location`): The location of the event. This field is required.
* **Event Type** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_type`): The type of event. This field is optional.
* **Sponsorship Commitment Duration** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_sponsorship_commitment_duration`): The duration of the sponsorship commitment. This field is required.
* **Sponsorship Value** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_sponsorship_value`): The value of the sponsorship (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **Event Details** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_event_details`): Additional event details (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **Notes** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_notes`): Any additional notes about the event or sponsorship. This field is optional.
* **Signature** (`event_sponsorship_consent_form_signature`): Please sign this form to confirm your sponsorship. This field is required.
