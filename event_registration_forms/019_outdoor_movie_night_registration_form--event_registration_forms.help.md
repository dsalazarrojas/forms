# outdoor_movie_night_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outdoor Movie Night Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for an outdoor movie night event. This form helps event organizers manage participant registrations, ensuring that everyone has the correct information and can be properly contacted and accommodated for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, start by filling in the participant information page.
2. Enter your **Participant Name**, which is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email Address**, which is required for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is required for emergency contact purposes.
5. Move on to the event details page.
6. Select the correct **Event Date** for your registration.
7. Choose the correct **Event Time** for your registration.
8. Enter the name of the **Venue Name** where the event will take place.
9. On the participant details page, you can add any additional comments or notes that you'd like to share with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in event materials and communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and schedule updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time of the event you are registering for.
* **Venue Name** (`venue_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes that you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Participant Contribution** (`participant_contribution`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of contribution you are willing to make to the event (e.g., volunteering, participating in activities).
* **Participant Dietary Requirements** (`participant_dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary requirements or needs you have.
* **Participant Hall of Fame Status** (`participant_hall_of_fame_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your status regarding event-specific achievements or milestones.

## Tips

- Be sure to double-check your entered information for accuracy.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers.
- Review the participant contribution options carefully to ensure you choose the correct option.
- If you have any dietary requirements, be sure to select them in the participant dietary requirements field.
