# Vendor Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about vendor meetings, including meeting details, vendor contact information, and other relevant details. By using this form, you can easily schedule and manage meetings with vendors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting date in the `Meeting Date` field.
2. Enter the meeting time in the `Meeting Time` field.
3. Enter the vendor's name in the `Vendor Name` field.
4. Enter the purpose of the meeting in the `Meeting Purpose` field.
5. Enter the location of the meeting in the `Event Location` field.
6. Enter a brief description of the meeting in the `Event Description` field.
7. Enter the vendor's contact email in the `Contact Email` field.
8. Enter the vendor's contact phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date (meeting_date)**: Enter the date of the meeting, in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 02/16/2024).
* **Meeting Time (meeting_time)**: Enter the time of the meeting, in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Vendor Name (vendor_name)**: Enter the name of the vendor you are meeting with.
* **Meeting Purpose (meeting_purpose)**: Enter a brief description of the meeting purpose.
* **Event Location (event_location)**: Enter the location of the meeting (e.g. conference room).
* **Event Description (event_description)**: Enter a brief description of the meeting (e.g. introduction, discussion, etc.).
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter the email of the person you will be contacting for further information.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person you will be contacting for further information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your meeting is scheduled correctly.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
