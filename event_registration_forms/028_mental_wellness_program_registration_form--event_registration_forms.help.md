# Mental Wellness Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Wellness Program Registration Form is designed to collect information for registration into a mental wellness program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the mental wellness program you wish to register for by choosing one of the two options provided.
2. Enter the name of the mental health professional providing the service.
3. Enter the location of the event.
4. Choose a date and time for the event.
5. Enter your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
6. Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mental Wellness Programs** (`mental_wellness_programs`): Select the mental wellness program you wish to register for by choosing one of the two options provided.
* **Mental Health Professional** (`mental_health_professionals`): Enter the name of the mental health professional providing the service.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the event where you will receive the mental wellness program services.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Choose a date for the event. This should be the date you wish to receive the mental wellness program services.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Choose the time for the event.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled and that your information is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or the registration process, please contact the mental health professional listed below for guidance.
* If you are registering someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to do so and that their information is accurate.
