# Cultural Festival Media Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Media Upload Form is designed to collect media content from festival participants, allowing them to provide their information, photo, video, audio, and consent for media usage. This form ensures that participants understand what media will be used and how it will be distributed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Information**, including your name, country, and any additional details about your participation.
2. Upload your **Participant Photo**, a clear and recent image.
3. Upload your **Participant Video**, a file type that can be played back on most media players.
4. Upload your **Participant Audio**, a file type that can be played back through audio equipment.
5. Provide **Event Information**, a brief description of your festival experience.
6. Choose your **Media Consent**, specifying what types of media you consent to share (e.g. Photo, Video, Audio).
7. Enter your **Participant Name** and **Participant Email** to ensure we can contact you.
8. Enter your **Participant Phone**, your primary contact number.
9. Select your **Participant Country**, the country you're participating from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This field is used to collect any additional details about your participation, such as your role in the festival or any notable achievements.
* **Participant Photo**: Upload a clear and recent image of yourself, ideally taken in a plain background.
* **Participant Video**: Upload a video file that represents you or your work. Ensure it's a reasonable file size to upload and play back.
* **Participant Audio**: Upload an audio file that represents you or your work. Ensure it's a reasonable file size to upload and play back.
* **Event Information**: This field is used to collect a brief description of your festival experience, helping us better understand your perspective.
* **Media Consent**: Please select which media types you consent to share: Photo, Video, or Audio. This ensures we respect your boundaries and only use media you're comfortable with.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your real name as it should be used for official purposes.
* **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your primary phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Country**: Enter the country you're participating from, which helps us understand your context.

## Tips
* Please ensure all media uploads are clear and of reasonable quality to represent you accurately.
* If you're not comfortable sharing certain media types, please select "No" for **Media Consent**.
* If you have any doubts or concerns about media usage, reach out to us.
