# Parental Consent Waiver For Local Trips - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental consent and gather necessary information for local trips your child is participating in. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your child's safety and successful trip experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the trip details, including the date and time of the trip.
2. Enter your contact information so we can reach you if needed.
3. Enter your child's guardian's details, including their name and contact information.
4. Read and agree to the waiver text.
5. Sign the guardian signature field to confirm consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details**: Enter the date and time of the trip, including the start and end dates and times.
* **Trip Dates**: Enter the date(s) of the trip.
* **Trip Times**: Enter the start and end times of the trip.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name and contact information so we can reach you if needed.
* **Guardian Details**: Enter your name and contact information, as you are the guardian or parent.
* **Waiver Text**: Read and agree to the waiver text, which is a contract that you are agreeing to by signing the form.
* **Guardian Signature**: Check the box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver text. This is a required field, but you can choose not to sign if you do not wish to participate in the trip.
