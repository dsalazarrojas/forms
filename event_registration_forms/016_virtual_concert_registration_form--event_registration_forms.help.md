# virtual-concert-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Virtual Concert Registration Form" is designed to gather information from users interested in attending a virtual concert. The form collects contact information, concert preferences, and event details to ensure the user is properly informed and registered for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Choose your preferred concert style from the select_one field.
3. Select your preferred concert styles from the select_multiple field.
4. Add any additional notes about your preferences.
5. Enter your email address to be contacted for further communication.
6. Enter your phone number for additional contact options.
7. Fill in the event details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Concert Details
* **Concert Details** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the concert you are interested in attending.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further communication.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact options.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth, this information will help us tailor our concert experience to your interests.

### Concert Preference
* **Concert Preference** (select_one, required): Choose the type of concert you are most interested in attending: Classical, Jazz, Pop, or Rock.

### Concert Preferences
* **Concert Preferences** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple concert styles you are interested in attending.

### Note
* **Note** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your concert preferences.

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the event you would like to be informed about.

### Event Description
* **Event Description** (note, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event you are interested in attending.

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event you are interested in attending.

### Event Location
* **Event Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.

### User Email
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter the email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### User Phone
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or need further clarification, please contact us directly.
