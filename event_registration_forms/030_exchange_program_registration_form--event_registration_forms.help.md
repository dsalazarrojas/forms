# Exchange Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exchange Program Registration Form is used to collect participant information, program details, and other essential data from individuals interested in enrolling in the exchange program. Fill out the form to register for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as per the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: no): Enter your name and any other relevant participant details.
* **Program Information** (`program_info`, text, required: no): Provide a brief description of the program you are interested in.
* **Dates** (`dates`, date, required: no): Select the dates you plan to participate in the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: no): Choose the time slot you prefer for the program.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: no): Enter the location of the program.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: no): Enter any contact information you wish to share with the program staff.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: no): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: no): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required: no): Click to submit your registration form.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any errors or delays in processing your registration.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the program staff for assistance.
