# DIY Ornament Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The DIY Ornament Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from participants about their personal details and workshop preferences. It is intended for users who wish to participate in the workshop and is essential for the organizers to run the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the date of the event in the Event Date field.
6. Select the time of the event in the Event Time field.
7. Enter the duration of the workshop in the Workshop Duration field.
8. Enter the number of participants in the Number of Participants field.
9. Enter the name of each participant (if there are multiple participants) in the Participant Name field.
10. Enter the email address of each participant in the Participant Email field.
11. Enter the phone number of each participant in the Participant Phone field.
12. Select the type of workshop you wish to attend from the Workshop Type dropdown menu.
13. Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend in the Workshop Date field.
14. Select the time of the workshop you wish to attend in the Workshop Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event you wish to attend.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the event you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop in hours.
* **Number of Participants** (`num_participants`, number, required): Enter the number of participants attending the workshop with you.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name(s) of each participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of each participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of each participant.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the workshop you wish to attend.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct date and time of the event you wish to attend.
* If you are attending with multiple participants, enter their details in the Participant Name, Email, and Phone fields.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please contact us via email.
* Please review your information before submitting the form to ensure it is accurate.
