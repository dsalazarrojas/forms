# Congratulatory Message Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Congratulatory Message Submission form is designed to allow you to submit a congratulatory message for an event or occasion. This form is intended for individuals who wish to send a personalized message to the honoree(s) of the event. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide a heartfelt message to be displayed and shared with the honoree and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Event Name or Occasion** (required) to specify the event or occasion you are congratulating the honoree for.
2.  Enter the **Name of the Honoree(s)** (required) to identify who the message is intended for.
3.  In the **Your Full Name** field (required), enter your name as you would like it to be displayed with your message.
4.  Provide your **Email Address** (required) for confirmation purposes only.
5.  Choose how you are related to the honoree (required) from the provided options.
6.  In the **Your Congratulatory Message** field (required), write a heartfelt message to the honoree.
7.  Optionally, you can provide a **Message Headline** (optional) to give your message a title.
8.  For the **How should we display your name?** field (required), select how you would like your name to be displayed with your message.
9.  Choose whether you would like your message to be included in a **physical memory book** (required).
10. Select your **Preferred way for the honoree to see your message** (optional) from the provided options.
11. Finally, indicate your **consent to share your message with the honoree and organizers** (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Event Name or Occasion**: Enter the name of the event or occasion for which you are sending a congratulatory message.
	+ This field helps us understand the context of the event, so we can display the message accordingly.
*  **Name of the Honoree(s)**: Enter the name(s) of the honoree(s) you are congratulating.
	+ This field helps us identify who the message is intended for.
*  **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed with your message.
	+ This field is used to display your name with your message.
*  **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes only.
	+ This field is used for internal confirmation purposes and will not be displayed to the honoree.
*  **Your Relationship to the Honoree**: Choose how you are related to the honoree from the provided options.
	+ This field helps us understand your connection to the honoree.
*  **Your Congratulatory Message**: Write a heartfelt message to the honoree.
	+ This is the main message you would like to share with the honoree.
*  **Message Headline**: Optionally provide a title for your message.
	+ This title will be displayed above your message.
*  **How should we display your name?**: Select how you would like your name to be displayed with your message.
	+ This field determines how your name will be displayed with your message.
*  **Inclusion in Memory Book**: Choose whether you would like your message to be included in a physical memory book.
	+ This field determines whether your message will be included in a printed memory book.
*  **Preferred way for the honoree to see your message**: Select the preferred way for the honoree to receive your message.
	+ This field determines how the honoree will receive your message.
*  **I consent to share my message with the honoree and organizers**: Indicate your consent to share your message with the honoree and organizers.
	+ This field is required for the form submission to be valid.
