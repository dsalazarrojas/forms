# Panelist Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panelist Dinner RSVP Form is used to collect information from panelists regarding their attendance and dietary needs for panelist dinner events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your attendance from the options provided. You can choose 'True' if you will attend, 'False' if you will not attend, or 'Maybe' if your attendance is uncertain.
2. If you have any dietary needs, select the options that apply from the list of dietary needs.
3. If you are bringing a guest, enter their details in the 'Guest Details' field.
4. If your guest will be attending, select their attendance from the options provided.
5. If your guest has any dietary needs, select the options that apply from the list of dietary needs.
6. If you have any special requests, you can enter them in the 'Special Requests' field.
7. If you would like to provide any comments, you can enter them in the 'Comments' field.
8. If you would like to provide your contact information, you can enter it in the 'Contact Information' field.
9. Finally, you can enter your email address in the 'Email' field, if you would like to be contacted with further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Attendance** (`panelist_attendance`):
*Select your attendance from the options provided.* You can choose 'True' if you will attend, 'False' if you will not attend, or 'Maybe' if your attendance is uncertain.*

### **Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`):
*Select your dietary needs from the options provided. If you select any options, please be prepared to discuss your choices with us.*

### **Guest Details** (`guest_details`):
*Enter your guest's details here, if you are bringing a guest to the panelist dinner event.*

### **Guest Attendance** (`guest_attendance`):
*Select your guest's attendance from the options provided. If they will be attending, select 'True', if they will not be attending, select 'False', or if their attendance is uncertain, select 'Maybe'.*

### **Guest Dietary Needs** (`guest_dietary_needs`):
*Select your guest's dietary needs from the options provided. If they have any dietary needs, select the options that apply.*

### **Special Requests** (`special_requests`):
*If you have any special requests, you can enter them here. This could be anything from special meal requests to other accommodations.*

### **Comments** (`comments`):
*If you would like to provide any comments or feedback, you can enter them here.*

### **Contact Information** (`contact_information`):
*If you would like to provide your contact information, you can enter it here. This will allow us to get in touch with you with further details.*

### **Email** (`email`):
*If you would like to be contacted with further details, you can enter your email address here.*
