# Church Event RSVP Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Church Event RSVP Lead Form, which is designed to gather necessary information from attendees for a well-organized and enjoyable event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes - I'll be there" or "No - Cannot attend" in the "Will you be attending?" field.
2. If you're attending, provide your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address for event updates and reminders.
4. If you're attending with a group, enter the total number of people in your party.
5. List the names and ages of others attending in your party (if any).
6. Share any dietary restrictions or allergies you or your party might have.
7. Indicate if you or anyone in your party has mobility concerns.
8. Let us know how you heard about the event.
9. If you have any questions for the event organizers, type them in.
10. Select a date to RSVP on.
10. Decide if you want to be contacted about future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Will you be attending?** (rsvp_confirmation_status):
	+ Select "Yes - I'll be there" if you plan to attend the event.
* **Your Full Name** (attendee_name_lead):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Email** (contact_email_lead):
	+ Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive event updates.
* **Phone Number (Optional)** (phone_number_lead):
	+ If you'd like to provide a phone number, enter it here.
* **Total number of people in your party** (total_guests_in_party):
	+ Enter the total number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Names of others in your party** (guest_names_and_ages):
	+ List the names and ages of others attending in your party.
* **Any dietary restrictions or allergies in your party?** (dietary_needs_rsvp):
	+ Share any dietary restrictions or allergies you or your party might have.
* **Do you or anyone in your party have mobility concerns?** (mobility_concerns):
	+ Indicate if you or anyone in your party has mobility concerns.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (hear_about_event_rsvp):
	+ Select how you heard about the event.
* **Do you have a question for the organizers?** (question_for_organizers):
	+ Type any questions you have for the event organizers.
* **Date of RSVP** (rsvp_submission_timestamp):
	+ Select a date to confirm your RSVP.
* **Keep me informed of future church events?** (future_outreach_consent):
	+ Decide if you want to be contacted about future events.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any special requests, such as mobility concerns, let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to type them in and we'll get back to you.
