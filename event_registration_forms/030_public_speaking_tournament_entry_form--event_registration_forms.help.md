# Public Speaking Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the public speaking tournament. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and relevant information.
2.  Review each field to understand the type of information required.
3.  Ensure you have a genuine interest in participating in the public speaking tournament.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you are interested in participating in. This will help us determine your suitability for the event.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Entry Fee** (`entry_fee`, number, optional): Enter the entry fee you are willing to pay.
* **Speaker Type** (`speaker_type`, select_one, required): Select your type as a speaker (e.g., professional, amateur, or other).
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, optional): Briefly describe your background and experience as a speaker.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter it here.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Describe your role within the organization (e.g., CEO, Marketing Manager, etc.).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your personal or professional website (if applicable).
* **Image** (`image`, text, optional): If you have a preferred image for your profile, enter the URL here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation in the event.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your participation in the event.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your submission (e.g., pending, approved, or rejected).

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
