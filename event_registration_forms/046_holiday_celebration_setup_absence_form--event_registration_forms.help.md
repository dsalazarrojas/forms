# Holiday Celebration Setup Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Holiday Celebration Setup Absence Form" is used to request time off for a holiday celebration. This form is meant to gather necessary information for your absence, including dates, reason, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "User Input" section with your personal details.
2. Enter your "Start Date" and "End Date" for the holiday celebration.
3. Provide the "Absence Start Date" and "Absence End Date" for your absence period.
4. Specify the number of "Absence Days" you will be taking off.
5. Enter a brief "Reason" for your absence.
6. Add your "Contact" information (email address) and "Phone" number.
7. Enter any additional "Notes" you have regarding your absence.
8. Select the "Submit" option that best describes your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): This field allows you to enter any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your absence.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the start date of your holiday celebration.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the end date of your holiday celebration.
* **Absence Start Date** (Absence Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date you will be starting your absence.
* **Absence End Date** (Absence End Date, date, optional): Enter the date you will be ending your absence.
* **Absence Days** (Absence Days, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will be taking off for your absence.
* **Reason** (Reason, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your absence.
* **Contact** (Contact, email, optional): Enter your email address so that your team can contact you.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that your team can contact you.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or information regarding your absence.
* **Submit** (Submit, select one, optional): Select the reason for your submission (Label 1, Label 2, or Label 3).

## Tips
Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to submit this form before filling it out. Make sure to enter accurate dates and details to avoid any confusion. If you are unsure about any field, you can consult your supervisor or HR representative.
