# Performance Cancellation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Cancellation Claim Form is used to document the cancellation of an event, providing necessary details for tracking and verification.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required information.
2. Select the correct reason for cancellation from the available options.
3. Enter the date and time of cancellation.
4. Provide additional reasons for cancellation if necessary.
5. Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancellation Reason** (`performance_cancellation_claim_form`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly explain why the event was cancelled.
* **Prophet/Artist Cancellation Reason** (`prophet_artist_cancellation_reason`, `select_one`, required/optional): If the event was cancelled by the artist or prophet, select the reason for cancellation.
* **Artist/Prophet Name** (`artist/prophet_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the artist or prophet responsible for the cancellation.
* **Cancelled Date** (`cancelled_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the event was cancelled.
* **Cancelled Time** (`cancelled_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the event was cancelled.
* **Cancellation Date Time** (`cancellation_date_time`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the event was cancelled.
* **Additional Reason** (`additional_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the cancellation.
* **Cancelled By** (`cancelled_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who was responsible for the cancellation.
* **Other Reason** (`other_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any other reason for cancellation.
* **Performance ID** (`performance_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the cancelled event.
* **Claim ID** (`claim_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a unique ID for the cancellation claim.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe the cancellation.
* **Cancelled At** (`cancelled_at`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the event was cancelled.
* **Claimed At** (`claimed_at`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the cancellation claim was made.
* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the event that was cancelled.
* **Cancelled** (`cancelled`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the event was cancelled.
* **Claimant Email** (`claimant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Claimant Phone** (`claimant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Claimant Address** (`claimant_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Claimant City** (`claimant_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Claimant State** (`claimant_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Claimant Zip** (`claimant_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the person making the cancellation claim.
* **Cancelled By Email** (`cancelled_by_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the cancellation.
* **Cancelled By Phone** (`cancelled_by_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the cancellation.

## Tips

* Please fill out this form as accurately and completely as possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizer for clarification.
