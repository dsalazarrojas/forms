# Theater Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur in a theater setting. This form will help us understand the circumstances surrounding the incident, including the type of incident, the time and date it occurred, and the involvement of theater employees or attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select the relevant information about the incident, such as the type of incident that occurred.
* Enter the date and time the incident occurred.
* Provide a brief description of what happened.
* If applicable, indicate whether any theater employees or attendees were involved.
* If necessary, specify the status of the incident and how it was resolved.
* Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself or your role in the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Reported**: This field is not required, but if you are reporting an incident that you witnessed or were involved in, please indicate that here.
* **Incident Type**: Please select the type of incident that occurred (Theater Incident, Stage Incident, or Other).
* **Incident Date**: This field is for entering the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: This field is for entering the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Location**: If the incident occurred in a specific location within the theater, please enter that information here.
* **Incident Safety**: Please note any safety concerns or issues that arose during the incident.
* **Incident Satisfaction**: Indicate how satisfied you are with the response or outcome of the incident (Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Not Applicable).
* **Incident Status**: Select the status of the incident (Resolved, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Incident Reporter**: Please enter your name and title or role within the theater.
* **Incident Involvement of Theater Employees**: Check if any theater employees were involved in the incident, or if not applicable.
* **Incident Involvement of Theater Attendees**: Check if any theater attendees were involved in the incident, or if not applicable.

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information about the incident.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* If you have any concerns or questions about the incident, please direct them to the relevant authority within the theater.
* This form will help us learn from incidents and improve the theater environment for all attendees.
