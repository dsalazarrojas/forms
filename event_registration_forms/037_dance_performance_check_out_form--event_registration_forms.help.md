# dance_performance_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check out a dance performance participant after the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the dance performance name in the "dance performance name" field.
2. Indicate if the participant performed a "dance performance type" (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Enter the check-out time in the "check out time" field.
4. Enter the participant's name in the "participant name" field.
5. Enter the participant's contact number in the "contact number" field.
6. Enter the participant's emergency contact in the "emergency contact" field (if necessary).
7. Enter the participant's email address in the "participant email" field (if necessary).
8. Indicate the check-out status in the "check out status" field (e.g., "Active" or "Inactive").
9. (Optional) Enter a reason for the check-out status in the "check out status reason" field.
10. Enter any special requests in the "special requests" field (if necessary).
11. Provide any comments or feedback in the "comments" field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **dance performance name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the dance performance, e.g., "Spring Fling" or "Summer Show".
* **dance performance type** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the participant performed in the dance performance (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **check out time** (`3`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the participant checked out.
* **participant name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **participant name** (`4`, `text`, required): 
* **participant name** (`4`, `text`, required): This is the participant's name.
* **contact number** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's contact number.
* **emergency contact** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's emergency contact.
* **participant email** (`8`, `email`, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **check out status** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the participant is checked out ("Active" or "Inactive").
* **check out status reason** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the check-out status.
* **special requests** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests you have.
* **comments** (`11`, `text`, optional): Leave any comments or feedback about the check-out process.
* **emergency contact** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's name and contact information.
