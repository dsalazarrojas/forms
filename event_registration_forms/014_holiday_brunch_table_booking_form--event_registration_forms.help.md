# Holiday Brunch Table Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Brunch Table Booking Form is designed to collect information for the Holiday Brunch event registration. This form will help you provide your guest's details, table reservations, and payment information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form page by page.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information 
* **title** (`Guest Information`, `text`, required/optional): This is where you can enter any relevant information about the guest that will be attending the event. 

### Table Details 
* **title** (`Table Details`, `text`, required/true): Enter the details of the table you are booking, including any special requests or notes. This is a required field.

### Special Requests 
* **Special Requests** (`Special Requests`, `select_multiple`, required/false): Check the boxes for any special requests you have for your table reservation.

### Booking 
* **title** (`Booking`, `text`, required/true): Enter the details of your booking, including your table size and the number of guests. This is a required field.

### Contact Information 
* **title** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/false): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number. This is an optional field.

### Additional Information 
* **title** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/false): Enter any additional information you think would be helpful for the event staff to know.

### Payment Information 
* **title** (`Payment Information`, `text`, required/false): Enter your payment details for the table reservation. This is an optional field.

### Confirm Booking 
* **title** (`Confirm Booking`, `select_one`, required/false): Confirm that your booking is correct.

### Cancel Booking 
* **title** (`Cancel Booking`, `select_one`, required/false): Cancel your booking.

### Payment Method 
* **title** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your preferred payment method for the table reservation.

### Date 
* **title** (`Date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of your booking.

### time 
* **title** (`time`, `time`, required/true): Enter the time of your booking.

### guest_count 
* **title** (`guest_count`, `number`, required/true): Enter the number of guests in your booking.

### table_size 
* **title** (`table_size`, `number`, required/true): Enter the size of the table you are booking.

### holiday_brunch_date 
* **title** (`holiday_brunch_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date of the Holiday Brunch event.

### holiday_brunch_time 
* **title** (`holiday_brunch_time`, `time`, required/true): Enter the time of the Holiday Brunch event.

### payment_method 
* **title** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your preferred payment method for the table reservation.

Please note that some fields have been renamed for better clarity and readability in the guide above.
