# Public Art Collaboration Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Art Collaboration Application Form is designed to collect information from artists applying to participate in a public art collaboration event. This form is meant to make it easier for event organizers to manage registrations and assess applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Event Registration Form** on page 1.
2. Provide a brief **Artist Bio** on page 2, describing your background and experience as an artist.
3. Describe your **Project Details** on page 3, including any relevant information about the project you're applying for.
4. Share your **Artist Info** on page 4, which will help event organizers understand your contact information and availability.
5. Finally, fill out the **Collaboration Form** on page 5, where you can specify how you'd like to collaborate with other artists or event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Registration Form
* **Event Registration Form** (`event_registration_form`, text, not required): This field is where you can enter any additional information about your event registration, if needed.

### Artist Bio
* **Artist Bio** (`artist_bio`, text, not required): Write a brief description of your background and experience as an artist. This will help event organizers get a better understanding of your artistic style and expertise.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, not required): Please provide any relevant information about the project you're applying for, including your vision, goals, and any specific requirements you may have.

### Artist Info
* **Artist Info** (`artist_info`, text, not required): Share your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant details that will help event organizers get in touch with you.

### Collaboration Form
* **Collaboration Form** (`collaboration_form`, text, not required): In this field, you can specify how you'd like to collaborate with other artists or event organizers, including any specific requests or requirements you may have.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out each field, as this will help event organizers assess your application accurately.
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting it to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
