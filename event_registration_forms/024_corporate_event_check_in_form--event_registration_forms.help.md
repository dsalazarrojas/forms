# Corporate Event Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering events, gathering information about corporate events, such as event name, company name, attendee count, date and time of the event, location, and details about the event. The form is used to check in and track corporate events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company hosting the event.
- **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees expected at the event.
- **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
- **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the event.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **Event Status** (`event_status`, text, required): Enter the status of the event (e.g. "confirmed", "cancelled", etc.).
- **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, required): Enter the name of the company representative responsible for the event.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact information for the company representative.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, contact the event coordinators.
