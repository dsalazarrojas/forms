# Marathon Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Sponsorship Donation Form is designed for users who want to sponsor the marathon and provide their contact details, donation amount, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your category from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of your donation, if desired.
3. Fill in your donor name and email address.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
5. Select your donation amount.
6. Choose your payment method (if applicable).
7. If you have chosen to pay via credit card, select your card type, enter your card number, and enter your card's CVC (Card Verification Code).
8. Select your payment expiration date and year.
9. Enter your payment gateway ID (if applicable).
10. Select the "Submit" option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Id**: The unique identifier for the form. (Number)
* **Category**: Select the category for your donation. (Select multiple options)
* **Description**: A brief description of your donation. (Text)
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name. (Text)
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address. (Email)
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable). (Text)
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. (Number)
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method. (Select one option)
* **Accept Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions. (Select one option)
* **Payment Gateway**: Choose your payment gateway. (Select one option)
* **Payment Card Type**: Select your credit card type. (Select one option)
* **Payment Card Number**: Enter your credit card number. (Text)
* **Payment Card CVC**: Enter your credit card's CVC (Card Verification Code). (Text)
* **Payment Expiration Date**: Enter your credit card's expiration date. (Date)
* **Payment Expiration Month**: Select the expiration month of your credit card. (Select one option)
* **Payment Expiration Year**: Select the expiration year of your credit card. (Select one option)
* **Payment Gateway Id**: Enter your payment gateway ID. (Text)
* **Submit**: Select to complete and submit the form. (Select one option)

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understand the form instructions and guidelines before proceeding.
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are experiencing any issues with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
