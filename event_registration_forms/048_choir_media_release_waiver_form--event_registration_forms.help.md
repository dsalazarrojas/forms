# Choir Media Release Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to grant permission for the media release of photographs, video recordings, and audio recordings of an individual's participation in events or activities associated with the choir. This includes but is not limited to promotional materials, news publications, YouTube, streaming performances, and other media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your participant name and date of birth.
2.  Provide your email address.
3.  If under 18, obtain the parent/guardian's consent.
4.  Select your authorization for the use of media (photography, video, audio).
5.  Indicate permitted usage purposes of the media.
6.  Confirm the duration of release for the media.
7.  Confirm understanding of your revocation rights.
8.  Confirm no compensation is provided for media use.
9.  Confirm understanding of the terms of this release.
10. Voluntarily grant this release.
11.  Finalize with a signature confirming your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear in media publications.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your active email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your active phone number.
*   **Media Release Authorization** (`media_release_section`, note, required): Confirm your authorization for media use.
*   **I authorize the use of my photograph** (`photography_release`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you authorize the use of your photograph. If 'No', you do not wish to appear in photographs.
*   **I authorize the use of video recordings** (`video_release`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you authorize the use of video recordings. If 'No', you do not wish to appear in video recordings.
*   **I authorize the use of audio recordings** (`audio_release`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you authorize the use of audio recordings. If 'No', you do not wish to appear in audio recordings.
*   **I authorize the use of my name and likeness** (`name_and_likeness`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you authorize the use of your name and likeness. If 'No', you do not wish for your name and likeness to be used.
*   **Permitted Usage Purposes** (`permitted_uses`, select_multiple, required): Select all the media usage purposes you authorize.
*   **I authorize commercial use of media** (`commercial_use`, select_one, required): Confirm if you authorize commercial use of media.
*   **Duration of Release** (`release_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration for which you are authorizing the release.
*   **Revocation Rights** (`revocation_rights`, note, required): Confirm your understanding of your right to revoke this consent.
*   **I understand I can revoke this consent in writing** (`understand_revocation`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of revocation rights.
*   **I understand no compensation is provided for media use** (`compensation_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of no compensation provided for media use.
*   **I have read and understand these terms** (`understand_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of the terms of this release.
*   **I voluntarily grant this release** (`voluntary_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you voluntarily grant this release.
*   **If Under 18, Parent Consent Needed** (`parental_consent_needed`, select_one, required): Confirm the parent/guardian's consent if applicable.
*   **I consent to this media release** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your consent to this media release.
*   **Perpetual** (`perpetual`, select_one, required): Select the duration for which you are authorizing the release.
