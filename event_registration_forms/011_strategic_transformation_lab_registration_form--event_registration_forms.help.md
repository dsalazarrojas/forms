## Strategic Transformation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather participant information for the Strategic Transformation Lab event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your goals and preferences in the "Participant Details" section.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
4.  Add any additional participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.
5.  Select the assigned tool for the participant from the list.
6.  Choose the participant's acceptance date.
7.  Select the participant's start time.
8.  Indicate the participant's status as Active or Inactive.
9.  Review and confirm the participant's acceptance terms.
10.  Select the event start date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details**: Enter your goals and preferences.
*   **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes about the participant.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the participant.
*   **Participant Acceptance**: Select the date you accepted the participant.
*   **Participant Time**: Select the participant's start time.
*   **Participant Status**: Choose Active or Inactive status.
*   **Participant Acceptance Terms**: Review and confirm acceptance of terms.
*   **Event Start Date**: Select the start date of the event.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
*   Review your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you need any help with the form, contact the event organizers.
*   Double-check the participant's acceptance date.
*   Confirm the participant's status and acceptance terms.
