The form "Vendor Sponsorship Options" is intended for vendors to register as a sponsor for an event. This form is part of the event registration process and allows vendors to provide necessary information about their participation. The form is used to collect information from various fields to determine the vendor's sponsorship level, type, and contact details. It is essential to have accurate and complete information to facilitate effective communication and collaboration with vendors. Please note that this form is designed for internal use and not for external vendors; if you are an external vendor, please contact the event organizers for more information.

# Vendor Sponsorship Options - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Sponsorship Options form is used to register and manage vendor sponsorships for an event. This form helps event organizers to collect essential information from vendors, which enables them to provide tailored support and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Sponsor Name field with the name of the vendor company.
2. Select the Sponsorship Level you wish to register for (Gold, Silver, or Bronze).
3. Choose the Sponsorship Type(s) you wish to participate in (Booth, Event Program, or Both).
4. Provide your Contact Name.
5. Enter your Email address.
6. Enter your Phone number (optional).
7. Enter your Address.
8. Fill in the Sponsorship Details field with any additional comments or information about your sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of your company as a vendor sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Level (select_one, optional)**: Choose one of the sponsorship levels: Gold, Silver, or Bronze.
	+ Gold: Typically reserved for the highest-level sponsorships.
	+ Silver: Suitable for medium-sized sponsorships.
	+ Bronze: For smaller sponsorships.
* **Sponsorship Type (select_multiple, optional)**: Select the sponsorship type(s) you wish to participate in:
	+ Booth: Reserve space for a physical booth at the event.
	+ Event Program: Showcase your company in the event program materials.
	+ Both: Participate in both booth and event program.
* **Contact Name (text, required)**: Enter your name to represent your company.
* **Email (email, required)**: Provide a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address (text, optional)**: Enter your company address.
* **Sponsorship Details (text, optional)**: Add any extra comments or information about your sponsorship.
