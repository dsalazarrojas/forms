# Digital Business Card Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Business Card Registration form is designed to collect essential information about a business card, making it a valuable tool for networking, professional outreach, and contact management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form fields and ensure you have the necessary information ready.
* Fill in the required and optional fields as necessary.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company
* Label: Company
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your company name to associate it with your digital business card.

### Job Title
* Label: Job Title
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your current job title or role in the company.

### Email
* Label: Email
* Type: Email
* Description: Enter your professional email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number
* Label: Phone Number
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your work or personal phone number for easy contact.

### Website
* Label: Website
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your company's website or personal website for professional reference.

### LinkedIn
* Label: LinkedIn
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL or username for easy networking.

### Location
* Label: Location
* Type: Text
* Description: Enter your current location or city for geographical reference.

### Time Zone
* Label: Time Zone
* Type: Select One
* Description: Select your current time zone for accurate scheduling and coordination.

### Time Zone (Multiple)
* Label: Time Zone
* Type: Select Multiple
* Description: Select multiple time zones you are available in for more flexibility in scheduling.

Note: The Time Zone fields are redundant and may be confusing. To avoid any confusion, select only one time zone for your primary location and use the Select Multiple field only if you are available in multiple time zones.
