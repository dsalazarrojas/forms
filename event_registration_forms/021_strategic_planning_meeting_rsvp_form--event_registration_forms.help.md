# Strategic Planning Meeting RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the strategic planning meeting. Please fill out this form to let us know you are attending and provide your details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Attendee Name** in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your **Role at Organization** from the dropdown menu. You can choose from "Attendee", "Presenter", or "Other".
5. If applicable, enter your **Organization** name in the "Organization" field.
6. Provide any **Additional Comments** you have in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Name
* **Attendee Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it is written on your business card or ID badge.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the meeting.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you urgently.

### Role at Organization
* **Role at Organization** (`role`, select_one, optional): Indicate your role in the organization, such as Attendee, Presenter, or Other.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your attendance or any specific needs you have.
