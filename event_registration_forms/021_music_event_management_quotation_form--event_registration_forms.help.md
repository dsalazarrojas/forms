# Music Event Management Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help music event management companies or service providers collect essential information for their services. It includes fields for event details, client information, quotation specifics, and other relevant data. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Details** in the text field.
2. Provide **Client Details** in the text field.
3. Enter **Quotation Details** in the text field.
4. (Optional) Enter any **Quotation Terms** if applicable.
5. (Optional) Enter the current **Event Status** if applicable.
6. Enter **Payment Details** in the text field, which is a required field.
7. (Optional) Upload or mention **Event Files** if necessary.
8. (Optional) Add any **Additional Info** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (page 1)**:
	+ **Event Details**: This field is for entering the event name, date, location, and any other relevant information about the event.
* **Client Details (page 1)**:
	+ **Client Details**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Quotation Details (page 2)**:
	+ **Quotation Details**: This field is for outlining the quotation, including services offered, pricing, and any other specific details.
* **Quotation Terms (page 4)**:
	+ (Optional) **Quotation Terms**: If you have any terms or conditions attached to the quotation, enter them here.
* **Event Status (page 4)**:
	+ (Optional) **Event Status**: Enter the current status of the event if it's already scheduled or cancelled.
* **Payment Details (page 5)**:
	+ **Payment Details**: Enter the payment method and amount.
* **Event Files (page 6)**:
	+ (Optional) **Event Files**: Upload or mention any relevant files related to the event.
* **Additional Info (page 7)**:
	+ (Optional) **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that might be helpful for the music event management company or service provider. 

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to maximize the effectiveness of the form's purpose.
