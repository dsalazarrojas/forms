# public_event_participation_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event participants for their safety and well-being during events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Details** field with the relevant information about the event you are participating in.
2. Provide your **Participant Name** and **Participant E-mail** for contact purposes.
3. Share any **Medical Info**, **Medical Conditions**, **Medications**, and **Equipment Used** for the organizers to be aware of any potential risks.
4. Specify the **Event Date**, **Start Time**, and **End Time** of the event.
5. Enter the **Venue** where the event is taking place.
6. Provide the **Address** of the venue for easy navigation.
7. If applicable, provide the **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Phone**, and **Emergency Contact Relationship** for emergency situations.
8. In the **Waiver** section, make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions, event rules, and permission for data sharing.
9. Select your response to each waiver question carefully, making sure to agree or disagree according to your comfort level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details**: Enter the event title, date, and time you are participating in.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is where you enter the event details to help the organizers know which event you are participating in.
- **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to during the event.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to enter their name, which will be used for address and communication purposes.
- **Participant E-mail**: Enter your contact email address for easy communication with the event organizers.
  * Required: false
  * Type: email
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to enter their email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
- **Medical Info**: Enter any medical information or conditions that the organizers need to know for your safety.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to report any medical information or conditions that may affect their event participation.
- **Medical Conditions**: List any ongoing or chronic medical conditions that may be relevant to event participation.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to report any ongoing or chronic medical conditions that may impact their participation in the event.
- **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking that may be relevant to event participation.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to report any medications they are currently taking that may impact their participation in the event.
- **Equipment Used**: List any equipment or assistive devices you may be using for your participation in the event.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to report any equipment or assistive devices they may be using for event participation.
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are participating in.
  * Required: false
  * Type: date
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to enter the date of the event, which will help the organizers schedule and prepare accordingly.
- **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
  * Required: false
  * Type: time
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to enter the start time of the event to ensure they are on time.
- **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
  * Required: false
  * Type: time
  * Explanation: This field is for the participant to enter the end time of the event for planning purposes.
- **Venue**: Enter the venue where the event is taking place.
  * Required: false
  * Type: text

  * Required: false

  * Required: false
  * Type: text

  * Required: false

  * Required: false

## Tips
