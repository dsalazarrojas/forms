# Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form is used to register your interest in attending the Art Gallery Expansion launch event. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Enter your name in the "Guest name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
4. Select your affiliation or organization in the "Affiliation" field, if applicable.
5. Choose your ticket type (General, VIP, or Sponsor) in the "Ticket type" field.
6. Enter the number of guests you will be bringing in the "Number of guests" field.
7. If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them in the "Catering needs" field.
8. If you have any accessibility requirements, please specify them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
9. Choose your RSVP date in the "RSVP date" field.
10. Select whether you consent to event photography in the "Photo consent" field.
11. If you have any additional comments or information for the organizers, please enter them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name**: *Enter your full name.*
* **Email address**: *Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.*
* **Affiliation**: *If you're representing an organization, please enter your affiliation.*
* **Ticket type**: *Choose General, VIP, or Sponsor.*
* **Number of guests**: *Enter the number of people you will be bringing with you.*
* **Catering needs**: *Specify any dietary requirements you have.*
* **Accessibility needs**: *Specify any accessibility requirements you have.*
* **RSVP date**: *Choose the date you will be able to attend the event.*
* **Photo consent**: *Choose whether you consent to event photography.*
* **Additional comments**: *Enter any additional information for the organizers.*
