# Fire Safety Awareness Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a participant's completion of a Fire Safety Awareness Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select whether you **Acknowledge and agree to the Fire Safety Awareness Program**. This is an optional field, but it is recommended that you confirm your understanding of the program's requirements.
3. In the "Fire Safety Protocols" field, please enter any relevant information related to fire safety protocols that were discussed or implemented during the program.
4. Enter the **Date of Discharge**, which should be the date of completion of the Fire Safety Awareness Program.
5. Enter the **Time of Discharge**, which should be the time of completion of the Fire Safety Awareness Program.
6. In the "Fire Safety Awareness Program" field, enter a brief description of the program or any relevant notes.
7. Select the **Discharge Status**, indicating whether the participant has been discharged from the program or not.
8. Enter the **Assigned Inspector**, who is responsible for overseeing or reviewing the participant's completion of the program.
9. Sign the **Inspector Signature**, confirming that the participant has completed the program.
10. Sign the **Participant Signature**, confirming that the participant has received and understood the program's requirements.
11. Enter the **Discharge Date**, which is the date of completion of the program.
12. Enter the **Discharge Time**, which is the time of completion of the program.
13. Provide **Notes** on any additional comments or information regarding the participant's completion of the program.
14. If necessary, enter **Form Comments**, which can be used to store any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name (`Participant Name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the participant completing the Fire Safety Awareness Program.

### Acknowledge and agree to the Fire Safety Awareness Program (`Acknowledge and agree to the Fire Safety Awareness Program`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply: 

* Yes
* No

### Fire Safety Protocols (`Fire Safety Protocols`, text, required)
Enter any relevant information related to fire safety protocols that were discussed or implemented during the program.

### Date of Discharge (`Date of Discharge`, date, required)
Enter the date of completion of the Fire Safety Awareness Program.

### Time of Discharge (`Time of Discharge`, time, required)
Enter the time of completion of the Fire Safety Awareness Program.

### Fire Safety Awareness Program (`Fire Safety Awareness Program`, text, required)
Enter a brief description of the program or any relevant notes.

### Discharge Status (`Discharge Status`, select_one, optional)
Select: 

* Discharged
* Not Discharged

### Assigned Inspector (`Assigned Inspector`, text, required)
Enter the name of the inspector responsible for overseeing or reviewing the participant's completion of the program.

### Inspector Signature (`Inspector Signature`, text, required)
Sign to confirm that the participant has completed the program.

### Participant Signature (`Participant Signature`, text, required)
Sign to confirm that the participant has received and understood the program's requirements.

### Discharge Date (`Discharge Date`, date, required)
Enter the date of completion of the program.

### Discharge Time (`Discharge Time`, time, required)
Enter the time of completion of the program.

### Notes (`Notes`, note, optional)
Enter any additional comments or information regarding the participant's completion of the program.

### Form Comments (`Form Comments`, text, optional)
Enter any additional comments or information that is not otherwise captured by the form.
