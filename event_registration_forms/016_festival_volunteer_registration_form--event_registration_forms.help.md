# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers who want to participate in a festival event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct volunteer slots and areas you are available to work.
3. Provide a brief short bio.
4. Confirm your contact information.
5. Agree to the festival's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Slots** (4): Select all the time slots you are available to work.
* **Skills** (6): Select all the skills you have that are relevant to the festival event.
* **Volunteer Area** (7): Select the area where you would like to work.
* **Special Talents** (8): Select all special talents you have.
* **Contact Method** (9): Select how you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (10): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Email** (11): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (12): Enter your mailing address.
* **Zip** (13): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (14): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (15): Select the state where you live.
* **Country** (16): Select the country where you live.
* **Short Bio** (17): Write a brief short bio about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact** (18): Provide the name of a friend or family member to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (19): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (20): Provide the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (21): Provide the name of another friend or family member to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone** (22): Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email** (23): Provide the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Confirm Contact Number** (24): Confirm that the contact information you provided is accurate.
* **Confirm Email** (25): Confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (26): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the festival's terms and conditions.

Note: All fields with a number are required unless otherwise specified.
