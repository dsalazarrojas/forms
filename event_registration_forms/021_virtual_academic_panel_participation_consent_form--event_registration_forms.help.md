# Virtual Academic Panel Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and consent for virtual academic panel participation. It is a crucial step in ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event. The form helps to establish clear communication channels and capture any additional comments or questions participants may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the "participant_details" field. This is a text field where you can enter your name, affiliation, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your event details in the "event_details" field. This is another text field where you can enter any additional event-specific information you may need to share.
3. Read through the "terms and conditions" field, which will be displayed as a note field. This is essential to understand the event's rules and guidelines.
4. Select your participation consent by choosing "I consent to participate" or "I do not consent to participate" in the "participant_consent" field.
5. If you have a preferred method of contact, select it in the "contact_preference" field. You can choose between "email" and "phone".
6. If you have other contact information that doesn't fit the provided options, you can enter it in the "other_contact", "other_contact_phone", and "other_contact_email" fields.
7. Use the "participant_comments" fields to provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, **required**): This field is where you can enter your name, affiliation, and any other relevant information.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, **required**): This field is for providing any additional event-specific information you may need to share.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, **required**): Please read through this field carefully to understand the event's rules and guidelines.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select multiple, **required**): Choose "I consent to participate" to confirm your participation, or "I do not consent to participate" to decline.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select one, **optional**): Select your preferred method of contact: "email" or "phone".
* **Other Contact Information** (`other_contact`, `other_contact_phone`, `other_contact_email`, **optional**): If you have other contact information not listed, you can enter it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`participant_comments`, `participant_comments_2` to `participant_comments_15`, text, **optional**): Use these fields to provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
