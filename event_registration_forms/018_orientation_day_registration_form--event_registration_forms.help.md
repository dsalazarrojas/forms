# Orientation Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants and their parents or guardians for an orientation day event. It gathers demographic data, including names, contact information, and student grade.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the First Name and Last Name fields with your personal details.
2.  Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
3.  If you're a parent or guardian, provide your contact information.
4.  Select the grade of the student you are registering for the orientation day.
5.  Choose the Event Date and Event Time for the orientation day.
6.  Ensure you have filled in all the required fields correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you would like to receive communication about the event.
* **Parent Contact First Name** (`parent_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Parent Contact Last Name** (`parent_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): If you are a parent or guardian, enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Parent Contact Email** (`parent_contact_email`, email, required: false): If you are a parent or guardian, enter a valid email address where you would like to receive communication about the event.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade of the student you are registering for the orientation day.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date for the orientation day event. Note: This field does not seem to be required, so you can select any date if you are unsure or if the event date is not fixed.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Select the time for the orientation day event. Note: Like the Event Date, this field is not required, suggesting flexibility in event scheduling.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official documents.

## Tips
Please ensure you have filled in all the required fields correctly before submitting the form. If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
