# Community Outreach Program Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations interested in hosting a Community Outreach Program. It will help us understand your organization's details, program specifics, and any additional requirements. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a successful partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, simply go through each section and provide the requested information. Make sure to follow the instructions for each field and use the options provided. Don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or need further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required, text): Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, required, text): Enter the name of the person responsible for the outreach program.
* **Contact Person Title** (`contact_title`, required, text): Enter the title or role of the person responsible for the outreach program.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required, email): Enter the email address you would like us to use for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required, text): Enter a phone number for us to contact you.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, required, text): Enter your organization's full mailing address.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, required, select_one): Select the type of program you are interested in hosting, such as Health Screening, Educational Workshop, etc.
* **Program Description** (`program_description`, required, text): Provide a detailed description of the activities and events of your program.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, required, text): Specify who will participate in your program.
* **Expected Number of Participants** (`expected_participants`, required, number): Enter the estimated number of participants for your program.
* **Proposed Program Date** (`program_date`, required, date): Enter the date you would like to hold your program.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, required, select_one): Choose the duration of your program, such as Half Day (4 hours), Full Day (8 hours), etc.
* **Program Location** (`location`, required, text): Enter the venue or address where you plan to hold your program.
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`, required, select_multiple): Check all the services your program requires, such as staffing, equipment rental, etc.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, optional, text): If you have any accessibility or dietary concerns, please specify.
* **Estimated Budget Range** (`budget_range`, required, select_one): Select the estimated budget range for your program.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, optional, text): Specify how your program is funded.
* **Quotation Needed By** (`quotation_deadline`, required, date): Enter the date by which you would like to receive a quotation.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, optional, text): If you have any further information or comments, please include them here.
