# professional_association_meeting_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report the absence of a professional association meeting. This form will help track attendance and absence for meetings within the organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the `meeting_absence` field with the reason of your absence (e.g., personal reasons, medical reasons, etc.).
2. Select the date of the meeting you will be absent from in the `meeting_date` field.
3. Choose your expected start time of absence in the `start_time` field.
4. Choose your expected end time of absence in the `end_time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Absence** (`meeting_absence`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for your absence from the meeting, e.g., personal reasons, medical reasons, or other relevant explanations.
  - Provide a brief and specific reason for your absence.
  
- **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the meeting you will be absent from.
  - Choose the correct date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  
- **Reason for Absence** (`reason_absence`, `text`, optional): This field is not explicitly defined, so it will be ignored. Please fill in the `Meeting Absence` field instead.
 
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter your expected start time of absence in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00 or 12:00.
  - Choose your expected start time of absence from the meeting.
 
- **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter your expected end time of absence in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00 or 12:00.
  - Choose your expected end time of absence from the meeting.
 
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, optional): This field is not used in the form and can be ignored.
 
Please note that all fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. However, providing accurate information will help the organization track attendance and absence effectively.
