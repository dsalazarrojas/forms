# Romantic Getaway Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Romantic Getaway Reservation Form is designed for guests to provide essential details for a romantic getaway reservation. It gathers information about the guests, their travel dates, preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in order, one page at a time.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
*   If a field is required (indicated by a red asterisk \*), it must be filled out.
*   If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest Details** (guest_details): Enter your name or the name of the person you're booking for. This is the primary contact information for the reservation.
*   **Travel Dates** (travel_dates): Choose the dates you want to travel. The format is MM/DD/YY.
*   **Arrival Time** (arrival_time): Select the time you plan to arrive at the destination.
*   **Special Requests** (special_requests): If you have any special requests, such as a specific room or meal preferences, enter them here.
*   **Preferred Room Type** (preferred_room): Choose the type of room you prefer from the available options.
*   **Room Request** (room_request): If you have a specific room request, enter it here.
*   **Guest Preferences** (guest_preferences): Select the preferences you have, such as any medical conditions or special needs.
*   **Contact Information** (contact_info): Enter your contact information, including a phone number and email address.
*   **Email Address** (email_address): Enter your email address.
*   **Additional Notes** (note): If you have any additional information to share about your reservation, enter it here.
*   **Confirmation of Booking** (confirmation): Confirm that you have made this booking.
*   **Special Instructions** (special_instructions): If you have any specific instructions for the staff, enter them here.
*   **Payment Method** (payment_method): Select the method you will use to pay for the reservation (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
*   **Credit Card Details** (credit_card_details): If paying by credit card, enter the credit card details here.
*   **Payment Confirmation** (payment_confirmation): Confirm that your payment has been processed.
*   **Cancellation** (cancellation): Confirm or deny any cancellation of the booking.

This form is designed to be clear and user-friendly, and it should take about 10-15 minutes to complete. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
