# Karaoke Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Karaoke Registration Form is designed to collect information from registrants for a Karaoke event. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the event organizers have the necessary details for event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Karaoke Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "registrant name" field.
2. Enter the name of the song you would like to perform in the "song name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field. Please note that this field is required.
4. If you have a phone number, you can optionally enter it in the "phone" field.
5. Select the date of the event you are registering for.
6. Choose the time slot you would like to perform at.
7. Provide any additional notes or information about your performance in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (registrant name)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
* **Song Name (song_name)**: Enter the title of the song you would like to perform, e.g., "Happy Birthday" or "Yesterday".
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you about the event details.
* **Phone (phone)**: If you have a phone number, enter it to enable the event organizers to contact you about any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Date**: Select the date of the Karaoke event you are registering for.
* **Time**: Choose the time slot you would like to perform at.
* **Note (note)**: Provide any additional information about your performance, such as your performance type (e.g., solo, duo, or group) or any special requests.
