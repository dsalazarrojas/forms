# Event Coordination Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather participant information and session preferences for Event Coordination Training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Name in the first field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Select the sessions you would like to attend by checking the boxes next to the session titles.
