# prototyping_workshop_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to handle absences from a prototyping workshop. It is designed to collect information from participants who cannot attend a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the date of the event you plan to miss from the calendar in the **Event Date** field.
2. Provide a reason for your absence in the **reason_for_absence** field, if you feel it's necessary.
3. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field, if you need to be contacted for any reason.
4. If you have an email address, enter it in the **Email** field.
5. If you have any additional comments or information, use the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event you plan to miss from the calendar.
* **reason_for_absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): Provide a reason for your absence, if you feel it's necessary.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number, if you need to be contacted for any reason.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information.
