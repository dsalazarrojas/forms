# Government Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Summit Registration Form is a form designed for participants to register for the Government Summit. The form collects information about the participant, their submission, and their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the required fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field. This will be used to confirm your registration.
3. Select your Participant Type from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your Organization and Job Title (if applicable).
5. Select your Role(s) at your organization.
6. Enter a brief Bio (if desired).
7. Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
8. Accept or decline the Terms and Conditions.
9. Confirm your submission status.
10. Select your submission type and status.
11. Enter your submission date and time.
12. Enter your submission location (if applicable).
13. Add any submission notes (if desired).
14. Enter the details of the person submitting on your behalf (if applicable).
15. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Participant Type (Required)**: Select the type of participant you are (e.g., Senior Official, Delegate, Representative, Guest).
* **Organization (Required)**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title (Optional)**: Enter your job title at your organization.
* **Role (Optional)**: Select your role at your organization.
* **Bio (Optional)**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Confirm Email (Required)**: Confirm that the email address you entered is correct.
* **Terms and Conditions (Required)**: Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the summit.
* **Agreement (Required)**: Confirm that you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Submission Confirmation (Required)**: Confirm that you are submitting the form.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submitter (Optional)**: Enter the name of the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submitter Info (Optional)**: Enter any additional information about the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submission Date (Required)**: Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time (Required)**: Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Location (Optional)**: Enter the location of your submission.
* **Submission Notes (Optional)**: Add any additional notes about your submission.
* **Submission Type (Required)**: Select the type of submission you are making (e.g., In-person, Virtual).
* **Submission Status (Required)**: Select the status of your submission (e.g., Draft, Published, In progress).
* **Submission Author (Optional)**: Enter the name of the person submitting on your behalf.
* **Submission Date Time (Required)**: Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Submitter Email (Optional)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting on your behalf.
