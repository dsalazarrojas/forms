# Gift Card Raffle Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Card Raffle Entry Form is designed to collect participant information and their preferences for a gift card raffle. The form's primary purpose is to gather data on participants, including their name, email, phone number, and date of birth, as well as their preferred gift card and agreement to the raffle terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Gift Card Raffle Entry Form Title** in the first text field.
2. Provide your **Participant Name** in the second text field.
3. Enter your **Participant Email** in the third field, in the format of MMDD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).
4. In the fourth field, enter your **Participant Phone Number**.
5. In the **Agreement** field, confirm that you are participating in this raffle voluntarily and are eligible to win a gift card.
6. Select one or more gift cards you would like to enter the draw for from the **Select Gift Card** dropdown menu.
7. Choose whether you **Accept** or **Refuse** the terms of the raffle in the **Select Agreement** dropdown menu.
8. Finally, enter your **Participant Date of Birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gift Card Raffle Entry Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your entry.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format MMDD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Agreement** (`form_agreement`, note, required: false): Confirm that you are participating voluntarily and are eligible to win a gift card.
* **Select Gift Card** (`select_gift_card`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more gift cards you would like to enter the draw for.
* **Select Agreement** (`select_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you **Accept** or **Refuse** the terms of the raffle.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MMDD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2024).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Choose one or more gift cards that you are interested in.
- Carefully review the terms of the raffle before agreeing to them.
