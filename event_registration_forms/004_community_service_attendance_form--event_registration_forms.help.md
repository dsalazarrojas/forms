# Community Service Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendance at community programs or events. It captures information about the program name, date of attendance, attendee's name and email (if provided), institution or group affiliation, attendance status, and hours attended. This form is essential for keeping records and ensuring accurate attendance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Program name**, which is the title of the community program or event you are attending.
2. Select the **Event date**, which is the date you attended the program or event.
3. Provide your **Attendee full name**, which includes your first and last name.
4. Enter your **Attendee email**, if you have one. This is optional and will help with confirmations and follow-up.
5. If applicable, enter your **Institution or group affiliation**, which is your school, company, or organization if you are representing one.
6. Choose your **Attendance status**, which indicates your status at the event (Present, Absent, or Excused).
7. Enter the **Total hours attended**, which is the number of hours you were present at the event. This field is optional.
8. Confirm your **Electronic signature**, by typing your full name. This is optional and is used for electronic confirmation of attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program name**: The title of the community program or event you are attending.
*   *Event date*: The date you attended the program or event.
* **Attendee full name**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
*   *Attendee email*: Optional. Enter your email address for confirmations and follow-up.
*   *Institution or group affiliation*: If you are representing a school, company, or organization, enter your affiliation here.
*   *Attendance status*: Choose your status at the event (Present, Absent, or Excused).
*   *Hours attended*: The number of hours you were present at the event. This field is optional.
*   *Electronic signature*: Confirm your attendance by typing your full name. This field is optional.
