# Party Favor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect party favor requests from users, including the type of favor they would like and any special messages or details they'd like to include.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you would like to request a party favor.
2. Choose the type of party favor you would like to request.
3. Optionally, enter a special message or details about your event.
4. Enter your phone number, email address, date of birth, and time of the event (if applicable).
5. Provide the name, date, and description of the event.
6. Select the category and description of the form for categorization and searching purposes.
7. Choose the output format (PDF or CSV).
8. Enter the name of the user who will be assigned to work on this form.
9. Optionally, enter a description of the form for further clarification.
10. Enter the ID of the event and user.
