# Voting For Finalists - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect votes for finalists in an event. It has three fields to cast votes for three different finalists and a submit button to submit the votes. The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their preferences for each finalist. It's recommended to fill out the form only once for each event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each option carefully and make your decision on whether or not to vote for each finalist.
2. Select your preferred option for each finalist by clicking on the corresponding radio button.
3. Once you've made your decisions, click on the "Submit" button to send in your votes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vote For Finalists 1** (`vote_for_finalist_1`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to vote for Finalist 1 by selecting one of the options.
* **Finalist 1** (`finalist_1`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide additional information or feedback about Finalist 1. It's optional and not required to fill out.
* **Vote For Finalists 2** (`vote_for_finalist_2`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to vote for Finalist 2 by selecting one of the options.
* **Finalist 2** (`finalist_2`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide additional information or feedback about Finalist 2. It's optional and not required to fill out.
* **Vote For Finalists 3** (`vote_for_finalist_3`, select_one, required): Choose whether or not to vote for Finalist 3 by selecting one of the options.
* **Finalist 3** (`finalist_3`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide additional information or feedback about Finalist 3. It's optional and not required to fill out.
* **Submit** (`submit_button_1`, note, optional): This is the submit button to send in your votes. It's not required to fill out.

Note: The options for each select_one field are "Yes" and "No", allowing you to choose whether or not to vote for each finalist.
