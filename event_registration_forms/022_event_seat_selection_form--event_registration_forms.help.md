# Event Seat Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Seat Selection Form is used to select seats for an event. This form is required when attending events that are ticketed and have a seat selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the event details (Event Details)**: This section is optional, but if filled out it will help identify the event. You can enter any relevant information about the event.
2. **Select your preferred seats (Seat Preferences)**: This section is required and includes a list of available seats. Select one seat for each section.
3. **Enter additional notes (Additional Information)**: This section is optional, you can enter any notes related to the event.
4. **Enter attendee information (Attendee Information)**: This section is required and includes fields for:
	* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the ticket or in any communication related to the event.
	* **Surname**: Enter your surname as you would like it to appear on the ticket or in any communication related to the event.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Title**: Enter your title (optional).
	* **Job Title**: Enter your job title (optional).
