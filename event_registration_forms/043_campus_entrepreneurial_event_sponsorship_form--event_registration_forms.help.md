# Campus Entrepreneurial Event Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a company or individual as a sponsor for a campus entrepreneurial event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the correct Industry Type that best describes your company.
3. Choose the benefits you are interested in receiving from this sponsorship.
4. Select the method of payment you would like to use for this sponsorship.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, \*): Your company name should be the name of the business that will be sponsoring this event.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, \*): A brief overview of your company.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, select_one, \*): The industry type that best describes your company. The options are:
	+ Technology
	+ Finance
	+ Healthcare
	+ Education
	+ Retail
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Services
	+ Other
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, \*): The first name of the contact person.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, \*): The last name of the contact person.
* **Job Title** (`contact_title`, text, \*): The job title of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, \*): The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, \*): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Street Address** (`contact_address`, text, \*): The address of the contact person.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, \*): The name of the event being sponsored.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, \*): The date of the event.
* **Sponsorship Package Level** (`sponsorship_package`, select_one, \*): The level of sponsorship you are interested in. The options are:
	+ Gold Sponsor - $5000
	+ Silver Sponsor - $3000
	+ Bronze Sponsor - $1500
	+ Partner Sponsor - $500
	+ In-Kind Sponsor
* **Sponsorship Amount Committed** (`commitment_amount`, number, \*): The amount you have committed to sponsoring this event.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, \*): The method of payment you would like to use for this sponsorship. The options are:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Invoice
* **Interested Benefits** (`sponsorship_benefits`, select_multiple): The benefits you are interested in receiving from this sponsorship. The options are:
	+ Logo Recognition
	+ Booth Space
	+ Speaking Opportunity
	+ Media Coverage
	+ Networking Opportunity
	+ Student Access
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, false): If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
