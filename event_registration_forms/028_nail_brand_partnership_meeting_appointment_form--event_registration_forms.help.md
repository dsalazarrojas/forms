# nail_brand_partnership_meeting_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register partnerships with external partners for nail brand meetings, appointments, and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, as some fields are required.
* Enter the partner's information in the **Partner Information** section.
* Choose a meeting **Meeting Time**, **Meeting Date**, and **Meeting Location**.
* Select the meeting **Meeting Type** and **Meeting Status**.
* Indicate if you need to follow up with the partner after the meeting.
* Provide any additional meeting details or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, `text`, required): Enter the partner's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `date`, required): Select the desired meeting time for the partnership meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): Choose a date for the partnership meeting.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, `text`, required): Enter the partner's name.
* **Partner Contact** (`partner_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the partner's contact information.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, `number`, required): Select the duration of the meeting in minutes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the meeting.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of meeting that will take place (In-Store Partnership Meeting, Phone Call, Email, Video Call, Event).
* **Meeting Status** (`meeting_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the meeting (Pending, Rescheduled, Confirmed, Cancelled, Postponed).
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you need to follow up with the partner after the meeting.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the appointment time for the partner.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, optional): Choose a date for the partner's appointment.
* **Meeting Address** (`meeting_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting address.
* **Partner Notes** (`partner_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional partner notes or comments.
* **Meeting Location ID** (`meeting_location_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting location ID.
* **Meeting Type ID** (`meeting_type_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting type ID.
* **Meeting Status ID** (`meeting_status_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting status ID.
* **Meeting Details ID** (`meeting_details_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting details ID.
* **Follow Up ID** (`follow_up_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the follow up ID.
* **Appointment Time ID** (`appointment_time_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the appointment time ID.
* **Appointment Date ID** (`appointment_date_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the appointment date ID.
* **Meeting Address ID** (`meeting_address_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the meeting address ID.
* **Partner Notes ID** (`partner_notes_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the partner notes ID.
