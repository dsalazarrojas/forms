# ADR Networking Event Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ADR Networking Event Application form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to ensure a seamless and enjoyable experience. By submitting this form, you'll be able to connect with fellow attendees, participate in sessions of interest, and receive important event updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Applicant full name as it should appear on your badge.
2. Provide your Organization or firm name (if applicable).
3. Enter your contact email address for event communications.
4. If you have a contact phone number, please enter it for urgent updates.
5. Specify your Role or title within your organization.
6. Describe your Networking goals for the event.
7. Select the sessions you plan to attend from the available options.
8. List any food allergies or dietary restrictions.
9. Specify any accessibility accommodation needs.
10. Choose how you heard about this event (optional).
11. Confirm that you agree to the event terms and code of conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your badge during the event.
* **Organization or firm**: This is an optional field for those affiliated with an organization or firm. If you're an individual, just leave this field blank.
* **Contact email address**: Enter a valid email address for event communications. This will be used for updates, reminders, and any urgent messages.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter your phone number for urgent updates or notifications.
* **Role or title**: Specify your job title or role within your organization, if applicable.
* **Networking goals**: Briefly describe what you hope to achieve by attending the event.
* **Sessions of interest**: Select the sessions you plan to attend. You can choose multiple sessions.
* **Dietary restrictions**: List any food allergies or preferences, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Accessibility accommodation needs**: Specify any special accommodations you require to ensure equal access to the event.
* **How did you hear about this event**: Choose how you learned about this event (colleague referral, organization newsletter, social media, or other).
* **Agreement to event terms and code of conduct**: Confirm that you agree to the event terms and code of conduct. This is a mandatory field.
* **Additional comments or questions**: Provide any additional information or questions you have about the event.
