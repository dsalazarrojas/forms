# sports_event_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect participant information, event details, and necessary signatures for sports events. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and safe experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Information**: Fill in the participant's basic information, including date of birth, contact phone, email, and address.
2. Enter **Event Details**: Provide the event name and date.
3. **Risk Acknowledgment**: Carefully read the risk statement and acknowledge the potential hazards involved in the activity.
4. **Emergency Contacts**: Provide the participant's emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and address.
5. **Medical History**: Disclose any medical conditions that may impact the participant's participation.
6. **Medical Authorization**: Get medical authorization from a parent/guardian (if applicable).
7. **Signatures**: Ensure the participant and the parent/guardian sign and date the form as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Date of Birth**: Please enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the participant's address.

### Event Details
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.

### Risk Acknowledgment
* **Risk Statement**: Carefully read and acknowledge the potential risks involved in the activity.

### Emergency Contact
* **Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: Disclose any medical conditions that may impact the participant's participation.

### Signatures
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date the form as the participant.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Sign and date the form as the parent/guardian (if applicable).
* **Date**: Date the form as the participant signs.
* **Time**: Date and time of the signature (if applicable).
* **IP**: Enter the signature's IP address (if applicable).
* **Signature Notes**: Any additional comments or notes for the signature.
