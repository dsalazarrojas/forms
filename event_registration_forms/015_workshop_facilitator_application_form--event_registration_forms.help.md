# Workshop Facilitator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for workshop facilitators to apply for hosting a workshop. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of your workshop's details before filling out this form. This form will be reviewed internally to ensure that your workshop meets our guidelines and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information (Workshop Name, Contact Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Enter your workshop details (Workshop Date, Workshop Time, Workshop Duration, Workshop Location, and Venue Preference).
3. Share a brief description of your workshop and your experience with it.
4. Attach a photo of yourself or your workshop (if applicable).
5. Select your workshop's level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
6. Choose the topics you'll be covering in your workshop.
7. Specify the maximum capacity of your workshop.
8. List any specialized equipment or precautions you'll need for your workshop.
9. Enter any additional venue details or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (Text, required: False): Enter the name of your workshop.
* **Contact Name** (Text, required: False): Enter your name as the contact person for the workshop.
* **Email** (Email, required: False): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Text, required: False): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Workshop Date** (Date, required: False): Enter the date of your workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (Time, required: False): Enter the time of your workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (Number, required: False): Enter the length of your workshop in hours.
* **Workshop Location** (Text, required: False): Enter the location of your workshop.
* **Workshop Description** (Note, required: False): Write a brief description of your workshop.
* **Bio** (Text, required: False): Share a little bit about yourself and your experience with the workshop.
* **Photo URL** (Text, required: False): Attach a photo of yourself or your workshop (if applicable).
* **Workshop Experience** (Select One, required: False): Select your workshop's level of experience (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Workshop Topics** (Select Multiple, required: False): Choose the topics you'll be covering in your workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity** (Number, required: False): Enter the maximum capacity of your workshop.
* **Maximum Capacity** (Number, required: False): Enter the maximum capacity of your workshop.
* **Workshop Equipment** (Text, required: False): List any specialized equipment you'll need for your workshop.
* **Other Equipment (if any)** (Text, required: False): List any additional equipment you'll need for your workshop.
* **Safety Precautions** (Text, required: False): Enter any safety precautions you'll need to take during your workshop.
* **Other Safety Precautions (if any)** (Text, required: False): Enter any additional safety precautions you'll need to take during your workshop.
* **Venue Preference** (Select One, required: False): Choose the venue for your workshop (Venue 1, Venue 2, or Venue 3).
* **Other Venue (if any)** (Text, required: False): Enter any other venue you'll use for your workshop.
* **Venue Details (if any)** (Text, required: False): Enter any additional details about your venue.
* **Venue Notes** (Note, required: False): Enter any notes about your venue.
* **Workshop Notes** (Note, required: False): Enter any additional notes about your workshop.
* **Submission Date** (Date, required: False): This is not editable by the user.
