# Team Building Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Team Building Accommodation Form, a form used for teams to register and provide information about their team-building activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are interested in participating in (Event Type).
2. Choose whether you will be attending the event (Event Dates).
3. Enter your team's name (Team Name).
4. Enter the size of your team (Team Size).
5. Choose the type of accommodation you require (Accommodation Type).
6. Provide any additional accommodation details (Accommodation Details).
7. Enter the name of the contact person for your team (Contact Name).
8. Enter the email address of the contact person (Contact Email).
9. Enter the phone number of the contact person (Contact Phone).
10. Provide any additional information about your team (Additional Info).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type**: Select whether this event is a team-building activity.
* **Event Dates**: Choose whether you will be attending the event. If you select "Yes", provide the event dates.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Size**: Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you require.
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide any additional details about your accommodation requirements.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for your team.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your team.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your event coordinator for clarification.
- You can add any additional information about your team in the Additional Info field.
