# Art Exhibition License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for an art exhibition license. It is a crucial document that helps the event organizers and regulatory bodies ensure the event's compliance with local regulations and safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details such as name, contact information, and legal status.
2.  Provide your exhibition details, including title, venue, dates, and expected attendance.
3.  Answer questions related to compliance and safety, such as insurance coverage and fire safety plans.
4.  Confirm that the information provided is accurate and agree to abide by local regulations.
5.  Sign the form digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name or Organization** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of your organization. This field is required and will be used to identify you as the applicant.
* **Legal Status of Applicant** (`legal_status`, select_one, required): Select your legal status from the options provided. This field is required to ensure that the event is compliant with local regulations.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization or yourself. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you with any inquiries or updates.
* **Business or Residential Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Enter your business or residential address. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you at the correct address.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you via email.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This field is required to ensure that we can contact you via phone.
* **Proposed Exhibition Title** (`proposed_exhibition_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your exhibition. This field is required to ensure that we can identify the event.
* **Exhibition Venue Name** (`exhibition_venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue where the exhibition will take place. This field is required to ensure that we can identify the event location.
* **Venue Full Address** (`venue_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the venue where the exhibition will take place. This field is required to ensure that we can identify the event location.
* **Exhibition Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the exhibition. This field is required to ensure that we can identify the event schedule.
* **Exhibition End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the exhibition. This field is required to ensure that we can identify the event schedule.
* **Anticipated Total Attendance** (`anticipated_attendance`, number, required): Enter the anticipated total attendance for the exhibition. This field is required to ensure that we can plan for the event's capacity.
* **Do you have public liability insurance for the event?** (`insurance_coverage_confirmed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have public liability insurance for the event. If yes, attach the policy document.
* **Is a fire safety and evacuation plan in place for the venue?** (`fire_safety_plan_ready`, select_one, required): Indicate if a fire safety and evacuation plan is in place for the venue. This field is required to ensure that we can ensure the event's safety.
* **Will alcohol be served or sold during the exhibition?** (`alcohol_license_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if alcohol will be served or sold during the exhibition. If yes, a separate liquor license may be required.
* **I declare that the information provided is true and I agree to abide by all local exhibition regulations** (`declaration_of_accuracy`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm that the information provided is accurate and that you will abide by local regulations.
* **Application Submission Date** (`application_submission_date`, date, required): Select the date on which you are submitting this application. This field is required to ensure that we can track the application's timeline.
* **Digital Signature (Type Name)** (`signature_type_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your digital signature. This field is required to ensure that we can verify your identity.
