# stadium_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stadium_check_in_form is used to collect guest information for events held at a stadium. It is a straightforward form designed to gather essential details from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to the stadium_check_in_form on your device.
2.  Review the form content and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
3.  Fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
4.  Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Main Page**: This field is the first page of the form. It serves as an introductory section.
*   **Guest Details**: Enter the guest's name, address, and any other relevant personal information as requested.
*   **Event Info**: Provide any additional event-related details as prompted. This could include event dates, times, or specific event information.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your information is processed correctly.
*   If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, contact the event organizers or stadium management for assistance.
