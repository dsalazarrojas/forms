# Event Speaker Content Approval Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Event Speaker Content Approval Registration Form is designed to collect and manage speaker information and session details for events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the speaker's information, including their bio and email address.
2. Enter session details, such as date, time, and title.
3. Upload any relevant material files or provide a URL for online access.
4. Select the approval status of the speaker's content.
5. Add any additional notes or comments for further reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information** (`speaker_info`):
  * Enter the speaker's name, bio, and any other relevant details.
  * **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`):
    * Briefly describe the speaker's background and expertise.
  * **Email** (`email`):
    * Enter the speaker's email address for communication purposes.
  * **Phone** (`phone`):
    * Enter the speaker's phone number for contact.
  * **Session Information** (`session_info`):
    * Describe the session topic, format, and other relevant details.
  * **Session Title** (`session_title`):
    * Enter the session title as it will be displayed on the event schedule.
  * **Session Date** (`session_date`):
    * Enter the date of the session in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  * **Session Time** (`session_time`):
    * Enter the time of the session in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
  * **Session Materials** (`session_materials`):
    * Describe any materials or resources required for the session.
  * **Material Title** (`material_title`):
    * Enter the title of the material.
  * **Material File** (`material_file`):
    * Upload the material file or provide a URL for online access.
  * **Material URL** (`material_url`):
    * Enter the URL where the material can be accessed online.
  * **Approval Status** (`approval_status`):
    * Select the approval status of the speaker's content (Approved, Rejected, or On Hold).
  * **Notes** (`notes`):
    * Add any additional comments or notes for further reference.
