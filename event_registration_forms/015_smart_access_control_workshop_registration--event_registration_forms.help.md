# Smart Access Control Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from attendees for the Smart Access Control Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Select your role(s) from the "Role" field.
3. Select your topic interest(s) from the "Topic Interest" field.
4. Choose your preferred start date from the calendar.
5. Choose your preferred end date from the calendar.
6. Select the tool you have been assigned to use from the "Assigned Tool" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form**: This is the main registration form for the workshop.
* **Attendee Name**: This is your name as the attendee.
* **Role**: Select one or more roles that apply to you (Admin, IT, Manager, or Other).
* **Topic Interest**: Select one or more topics that interest you (Cloud, Cyber Security, Data Protection, or Other).
* **Preferred Start Date**: Choose your preferred date to start the workshop.
* **Preferred End Date**: Choose your preferred date to end the workshop.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool you have been assigned to use.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
