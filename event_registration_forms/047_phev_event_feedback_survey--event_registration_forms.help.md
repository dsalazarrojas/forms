## PHEV Event Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants and attendees about events that have been conducted under the PHEV project. This form will help us understand their experiences and feedback on the event. Please note that this form is for event feedback and is only applicable for events held under the PHEV project.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the questions carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and relevance.
- Fill in the form with accurate information about the event you attended.
- Make sure to answer all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
- For fields that are not required, feel free to provide any additional comments or details you think might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required: false): This is a space to briefly describe the event, providing any context or information that will help us understand the specifics of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date on which the event took place.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time at which the event occurred.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the boxes to indicate if there were attendees at the event. Choose 'Yes' if there were participants who attended the event, or 'No' if there were no attendees.
* **Event Attendee Name** (`event_attendee_name`, text, required: false): If attendees were present, enter the name(s) of the person(s) who attended the event. If there were no attendees, you can leave this field blank.
