**video_on_demand_access_form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for requesting access to video content on demand. It is meant for event registrants to provide necessary details to access the video recording of an event. The form is split into two parts:

1. Event details (fields 1-3: event_title, start_date, end_date)
2. Registrant details (fields 4-10: request_reason, contact_email, contact_phone, event_location, select_option_1, select_option_2, note)
3. Technical preferences (fields 11-13: time_zone, note_2, note_3)

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Details**: Fill out the **Event Title**, **Start Date**, and **End Date** fields with the requested information.
2. **Registrant Details**: Provide a **Reason for Requesting Access**, your **Contact Email**, **Contact Phone**, the **Event Location**, and select the relevant options for **Select Option 1**, **Select Option 2**, and **Select Option 3**.
3. **Technical Preferences**: Add any additional notes or comments in the **Note** fields if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the event you are requesting access to.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Video Title** (`video_title`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the video title.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for requesting access to the event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the event took place.
* **Select Option 1** (`select_option_1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option for your selection.
* **Select Option 2** (`select_option_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options for your selection.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the event.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `time`, optional): Enter your time zone.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter a specific time for your event.
* **Note 3** (`note_3`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Video Format** (`video_format`, `text`, optional): Enter any information related to the video format.
* **Select Option 3** (`select_option_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options for your selection.
* **Video Language** (`video_language`, `text`, optional): Enter the language of the video.
* **Select Option 4** (`select_option_4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose multiple options for your selection.
* **Note 4** (`note_4`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Time 2** (`time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter a specific time for your event.
* **Select Option 5** (`select_option_5`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option for your selection.
* **Note 5** (`note_5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter a specific date for your event.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
