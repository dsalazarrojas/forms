# Global Education Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Global Education Conference. The conference is a platform where attendees can learn about new trends, technologies, and best practices in education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information, making sure to provide accurate and complete details.
2. Select the relevant fields according to your needs.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Institution** (text, required: false): Enter the name of your institution or organization.
* **Role** (select_one, required: false): Select your role in the institution (Admin, Faculty, Student, Staff, Alumni).
* **Event Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the event.
* **Start Date** (date, required: false): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (date, required: false): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Start Time** (time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Session Length** (text, required: false): Enter the duration of the session.
* **Session Capacity** (text, required: false): Enter the capacity of the session.
* **Session Type** (select_one, required: false): Select the type of session (Plenary, Keynote, Workshops, Panel, Roundtable, Breakout).
* **Session Topic** (text, required: false): Enter the topic of the session.
* **Room** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the room.
* **Room Type** (select_one, required: true): Select the type of room (Auditorium, Classroom, Theater, Lecture Hall, Conference Room, Other).
* **Registration Type** (select_one, required: true): Select if you are registered (Yes, No).
* **Registration Status** (select_one, required: true): Select your registration status (Active, Inactive).
* **Notes** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
* **Additional Notes** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes.
* **File** (text, required: false): Upload any relevant file.
* **Uploaded File** (text, required: false): Upload any file.
* **Email Sent** (text, required: false): Enter if email was sent.
* **Email Sent (2)** (text, required: false): Enter if email was sent.
* **Confirm Registration** (text, required: false): Confirm your registration.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out to complete the form correctly.
