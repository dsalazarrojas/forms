# HR Technology Adoption Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to the HR Technology Adoption Roundtable event. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name**, which is the name of the event you wish to attend.
2. Fill in your **Name** fields: **First Name** and **Last Name**. These are required fields to ensure we have the correct contact information.
3. Enter your **Email**, which is used for communication purposes. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Select your **Interests**, which will help us tailor the event content to your needs. Choose all that apply.
5. Confirm your **Participation Consent**, which is required for event attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: The name of the event you wish to attend.
* **Organization**: Your company or affiliated organization's name (optional).
* **Title**: Your title or position within your organization (optional).
* **First Name**: Your first name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name**: Your last name (e.g., Doe).
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Interests**: Select all the topics you're interested in:
	+ HR Tech Strategy
	+ Digital Transformation
	+ Artificial Intelligence
	+ Machine Learning
	+ Data Analytics
	+ Other
* **Participation Consent**: Confirm your willingness to participate in the event.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your attendance (optional).
* **Company**: Your company or affiliated organization's name (optional).
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position within your organization (optional).
* **Department**: Your department within your organization (optional).
* **Location**: The location of your organization (optional).
* **Phone**: Your work phone number (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure you're registering as an attendee only once per event.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the **Notes** field.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact your HR representative.
* Review the event description for further details on what to expect.
* This form is for HR representatives to use in preparing for the event. Your registration will help us tailor the content to your needs.
