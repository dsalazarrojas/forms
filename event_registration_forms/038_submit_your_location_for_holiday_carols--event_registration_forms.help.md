# Submit Your Location For Holiday Carols - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or groups that want to participate in holiday carol singing. The purpose of this form is to gather personal and event details in order to schedule and coordinate carol singing events across various locations. Please ensure you submit your location once, and do not duplicate your request if you have already submitted before.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Personal Information (page 1).
2. Provide your preferred time for the event (page 2).
3. Enter any accessibility information that you believe might be relevant for event organizers (page 3).
4. Specify the location where you would like to host the event.
5. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information**: Provide your name and contact information for event organizers to contact you.
* **Preferred Time**: Indicate a date and time that works best for you. Please note that this is a preferred time and event organizers might choose a different time.
* **Accessibility Information**: Share any details about how you or your event might require any special arrangements for accessibility reasons. This could include mobility issues, hearing or visual impairments, etc.
* **Location**: Enter the exact address or coordinates (e.g. latitude and longitude) of the location where you would like to host the event.
* **Notes**: Add any other information that you think might be helpful for event organizers to know.
* **Notes (2)**: This is an optional field for adding any further details that might not fit in the previous "Notes" field.
