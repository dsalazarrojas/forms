# Innovation Lab Crawl Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Lab Crawl Registration form is designed to help event organizers register their event, providing necessary details such as event name, date, time, and attendee information. This form ensures that the event information is accurately collected and stored in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the event name in the "Event Name" field.
2. Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
3. Choose the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
4. Enter the number of attendees in the "Attendee Count" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the event in the "Event Description" field.
6. If you wish to include any additional attendee information, type it in the "Attendee Information" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. If you have any additional notes about the event, type it in the "Note" field.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**:
	* A field to enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Name**:
	* A field to enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**:
	* A field to select the date of the event. Please enter in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time**:
	* A field to select the time of the event. Please enter in the format of 24-hour clock (HH:MM).
* **Attendee Count**:
	* A field to enter the number of attendees expected at the event.
* **Event Description**:
	* A field to provide a brief description of the event.
* **Attendee Information**:
	* A field to provide any additional information about the attendees, if needed.
* **Email**:
	* A field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	* A field to enter your phone number.
* **Note**:
	* A field for any additional notes about the event. 

Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely for successful event registration.
