# Crop Innovation Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Crop Innovation Day Registration Form. Please read through the guide carefully to ensure you provide all the necessary information for a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select the relevant options for each field. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): This field is a free text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your role in the event.
* **Select Sessions** (`Select Sessions`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more sessions that interest you from the list of options. You can select multiple sessions by holding down the Ctrl key and clicking on multiple options.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, select one, optional): If you have any special requirements or needs, please select the relevant option from the list.
* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional details about yourself or your role in the event.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): This field is for providing your contact information, which will be used by event organizers.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Please enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Your work phone number.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): Your work organization.
* **Role** (`Role`, text, optional): Your role in the event.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, optional): Your department in the organization.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, optional): Your job title in the organization.
* **Bio** (`Bio`, note, optional): Please enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Session 1 -** (`Session 1 -`, select one, optional): Select one option for the session.

Note: If you have any issues with this form, please contact us.
