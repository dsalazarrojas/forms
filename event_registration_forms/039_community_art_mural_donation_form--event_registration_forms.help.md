# Community Art Mural Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Art Mural Donation Form is a crucial document that captures essential details about your donation to a mural project in the community. It helps the administration track and manage donations, ensuring accurate records and efficient communication with donors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your donation is processed correctly.
- Provide a valid donation amount in USD.
- Select the correct donation frequency (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
- Choose the purpose of your donation (general mural fund, specific mural project, artist compensation, materials and supplies, maintenance fund, or where needed most).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donation Information**: This field is a note field where you can provide any additional details about your donation.
  * Type: note
  * Required: No

- **Donation Date**: The date of your donation.
  * Type: date
  * Required: Yes

- **Donation ID**: A unique reference number for your donation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes

- **Donor Information**: Your contact details for communication.
  * Type: note
  * Required: No

- **Donor Name**: The full name of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes

- **Donor Type**: The type of donor (Individual, Family, Business, Organization, Foundation, or Anonymous).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Donor Email**: The contact email address of the donor.
  * Type: email
  * Required: Yes

- **Donor Phone**: The contact phone number of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Donor Address**: The street address of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Donor City**: The city of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Donor State**: The state or province of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Donor ZIP Code**: The ZIP or postal code of the donor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Mural Project**: Information about the mural project.
  * Type: note
  * Required: No

- **Project Name**: The name of the mural project.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes

- **Project Location**: Where the mural will be located.
  * Type: text
  * Required: Yes

- **Donation Details**: A note field for any additional details about your donation.
  * Type: note
  * Required: No

- **Donation Amount**: The amount you wish to donate in USD.
  * Type: number
  * Required: Yes

- **Donation Frequency**: Whether the donation is one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Donation Purpose**: The purpose of your donation (general mural fund, specific mural project, artist compensation, materials and supplies, maintenance fund, or where needed most).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Specific Mural**: If you are supporting a specific mural, select it from the list.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Payment Method**: The method of payment you prefer (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, check, PayPal, or cash).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Tribute Donation**: Whether the donation is in honor or memory of someone.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: No

- **Tribute Name**: The name of the person being honored or remembered.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Recognition Preference**: How you would like to be recognized (public recognition on plaque, public recognition on website, social media mention, private recognition only, or anonymous).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Tax Receipt**: Whether you need a tax receipt (email or mail).
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes

- **Newsletter Signup**: Whether you would like to receive project updates.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: No

- **Volunteer Interest**: Whether you are interested in volunteering for the project.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: No

- **Additional Comments**: Any message or dedication you would like to include with your donation.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

- **Donation Agreement**: You agree that this donation is final and non-refundable.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: Yes
