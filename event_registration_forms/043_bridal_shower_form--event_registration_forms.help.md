> # Bridal Shower Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bridal Shower Form is designed to collect information from guests attending the bridal shower for the bride-to-be. This form will help the event organizers plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your relationship to the bride from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your availability to attend the shower by selecting "Yes, I will attend" or "No, I cannot attend".
4. If you are bringing a guest, select "Yes, bringing one guest" or "Yes, bringing two guests" from the dropdown menu.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, select the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
6. If you have any special dietary information or requests, type it in the "Detailed Dietary Information" field.
7. If you have any mobility or accessibility needs, type them in the "Mobility or Accessibility Needs" field.
8. Select how you can help with the event from the dropdown menu.
9. If you have any specific skills or areas you can assist with, select them from the dropdown menu.
10. If you plan to bring a gift, select the type of gift you intend to bring.
11. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the "Comments or Questions" field.
12. Finally, rate your confidence level in attending the event by selecting from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name. Required.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address. Required.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. Required.
* **Relationship to the Bride**: Select how you know the bride: Close family, Extended family, Best friend, Close friend, Friend, Coworker, or Other. Required.
* **Bridal Shower Date**: Enter the date of the shower. Required.
* **Shower Time**: Enter the time of the shower. Optional.
* **Shower Location**: Enter the location of the shower. Optional.
* **RSVP Status**: Select "Yes, I will attend" or "No, I cannot attend". Required.
* **Will You Bring a Guest**: Select "No, attending alone", "Yes, bringing one guest", or "Yes, bringing two guests". Optional.
* **Names of Additional Guests**: If you are bringing guests, enter their names. Optional.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have. Optional.
* **Detailed Dietary Information**: If you have any specific dietary requests or information, type it here. Optional.
* **Mobility or Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special needs, type them here. Optional.
* **Can You Help With the Event**: Select "Yes, I can help", "Maybe", or "No, just attending". Optional.
* **What Can You Help With**: Select any areas you can assist with. Optional.
* **Gift Intention**: Select the type of gift you intend to bring. Optional.
* **Registry Item You May Purchase**: If you are bringing a gift from the registry, enter the item you plan to bring. Optional.
* **Comments or Questions**: Any additional comments or questions you have. Optional.
* **Confidence Level in Attendance**: Rate your likelihood of attending the event. Optional.
* **Would You Like to Share a Toast or Reading**: Select "Yes, I would like to" or "No, not interested". Optional.

Note: The optional fields are not required, but they help the event organizers tailor the event to your needs and preferences.
