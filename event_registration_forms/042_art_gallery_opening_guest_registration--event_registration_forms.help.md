# Art Gallery Opening Guest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register guests for an art gallery opening event. Please complete this form to provide essential information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form from start to finish. You can save your progress and resume later if needed.
2. Fill out the fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email address** (Required): Enter your email address for confirmation and to receive any event-related updates.
* **Affiliation** (Optional): If you are representing an organization or company, please enter the name of the organization.
* **Number of guests** (Required): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Access needs** (Optional): If you require special accessibility accommodations, please describe your needs.
* **RSVP date** (Required): Choose the date of your RSVP submission.
* **VIP request** (Optional): If you require VIP support, please select yes.
* **Photo permission** (Required): Indicate if you consent to being photographed at the event.
* **Comments** (Optional): Provide any other notes or information for the event organizers.
* **Contact phone** (Optional): Enter an optional phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

* Please be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure a smooth experience.
* If you require assistance or have questions, feel free to contact the event organizers.
* The form will be reviewed and processed before the event, so please submit it early to guarantee availability and services.
