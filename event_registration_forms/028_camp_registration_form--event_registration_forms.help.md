# Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from parents or guardians to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for their child(ren) at the camp event. Please fill out this form to provide accurate and complete information for the camp organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to fill out the form:

1. Fill in the Participant Full Name field with the child's legal name as it should appear on records.
2. Enter the Date of Birth for the participant.
3. Provide the guardian's contact information in the Guardian Contact Info field.
4. Select the session or dates the participant will attend in the Session Choice field.
5. If applicable, provide any medical information in the Medical Information field.
6. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number in the Emergency Contact Name and Phone field.
7. Fill in the Payment Amount field with the registration fee amount.
8. Select the payment method for the registration fee (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
9. Confirm that the parent or guardian has signed the consent form (True or False).
10. If necessary, provide any special requests or accommodations for the staff in the Special Requests field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Enter the child's legal name as it should appear on records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Guardian Contact Info**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Enter the guardian's phone or email for urgent communication.
* **Session Choice**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Select the session or dates the participant will attend.
* **Medical Information**: <span class="badge badge-info"></span> If necessary, provide any allergies, medications, or special needs of the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Payment Amount**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Fill in the registration fee amount.
* **Payment Method**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Select the method for payment (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Consent Signed**: <span class="badge badge-info">Required</span> Confirm that the parent or guardian has signed the consent form (True or False).
* **Special Requests**: <span class="badge badge-info"></span> If necessary, provide any accommodations or notes for the staff.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to help us provide the best experience for your child(ren) at the camp event. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
