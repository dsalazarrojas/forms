# Concert Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concert Check In Form is used to collect attendee information and verify their identity for concerts. This helps ensure a smooth check-in process, provides access to VIP amenities if applicable, and facilitates emergency contact in case of an unexpected event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your unique **Check In ID** number
2. Enter your **Check In Date**
3. Select your **Check In Time**
4. Provide your **Full Name**
5. Enter your **Email Address**
6. Provide your **Contact Phone Number**
7. Enter your **Date of Birth** for age verification
8. Enter the **Name of the Concert Event**
9. Enter the **Date of the Concert**
10. Enter your **Ticket Number**
11. Select your **Ticket Type** (e.g., General Admission, VIP, etc.)
12. Choose how you **Purchased Your Ticket** (e.g., Online, Box Office, etc.)
13. Indicate if your **ID has been Verified**
14. Select if you are **Age Verified** (18 or older, 21 or older, etc.)
15. Enter the number of people in your **Party Size**
16. If applicable, select if you have access to **VIP Amenities**
17. If you have pre-ordered merchandise, select if you have merchandise to pick up
18. If needed, provide any **Special Accommodations**
19. Enter the name and contact information of your **Emergency Contact**
20. Indicate if you **Accept the Terms and Conditions** of the venue
21. Consent to being photographed or recorded
22. Select your current **Check-in Status** (Checked In, Pending, Denied Entry, etc.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In ID**: Unique identifier for check-in purposes.
* **Check In Date**: Date of check-in.
* **Check In Time**: Time of check-in.
* **Attendee Name**: Provide your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Date of birth for age verification.
* **Concert Name**: Name of the concert event.
* **Concert Date**: Date of the concert.
* **Ticket Number**: Enter your ticket number.
* **Ticket Type**: Select the type of ticket you have.
* **Purchase Method**: How you purchased your ticket.
* **ID Verified**: Indicate if your ID has been verified.
* **Age Verified**: Indicate if you are age verified.
* **Party Size**: Number of people in your group.
* **VIP Amenities Access**: If applicable, select if you have access to VIP amenities.
* **Merchandise Pre-Order**: If you have pre-ordered merchandise, select yes.
* **Special Accommodations**: If needed, provide any special accommodations.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Accept or decline the venue's rules and policies.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Consent to being photographed or recorded.
* **Check In Status**: Current check-in status (Checked In, Pending, Denied Entry, etc.)
* **Staff Initials**: Checking staff member initials.
* **ID Verified**: Checking staff initials (required for ID verification).
* **Age Verified**: Checking staff initials (required for age verification).
* **Staff Initials**: Checking staff member initials (for special accommodations and other purposes).
* **Acceptance of Terms and Conditions**: Accept or decline venue rules and policies.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: Consent to being photographed or recorded.
* **Check In Status**: Current check-in status.
 
Note: This guide is designed to help you understand each field's purpose and requirement. Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with check-in.
