# Guest Speaker Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the event management process, ensuring that all guest speakers have the necessary agreements and information in place before they can be confirmed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the conference details in the **Conference Details** field.
2. Provide your speaker information in the **Speaker Info** field.
3. If applicable, enter your workshop information in the **Workshop Info** field.
4. Select the agreement that best suits your needs in the **Agreement** field.
5. Enter your contact details in the **Contact Details** field.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email Details** field.
7. Choose the speaker agreement options that apply to you in the **Speaker Agreement** field.
8. Finally, select the workshop speaker option that applies to you in the **Workshop Speaker** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Details (Required)**: Enter the details about the conference where you would like to speak.
* **Speaker Info (Required)**: Provide your name, bio, and any relevant information about yourself as a speaker.
* **Workshop Info (Optional)**: If you are presenting a workshop, please provide details about it, such as the title, description, and duration.
* **Agreement (Required)**: Choose the agreement that best suits your needs. This will ensure that you are aware of and agree to all the necessary terms.
* **Contact Details (Required)**: Enter your contact information, including your phone number and address.
* **Email Details (Required)**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you about the conference and your speaking engagement.
* **Speaker Agreement (Required)**: Select all the agreements that apply to you as a speaker.
* **Workshop Speaker (Required)**: Select the workshop speaker option that applies to you.
