# Webinar Host Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from existing webinar hosts who wish to refer a new host or hosts for our upcoming webinars.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as requested in the first field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field to ensure we can contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in the third field to help us reach you.
4. If available, provide the Webinar URL in the fourth field for quick access.
5. Enter the referral code in the fifth field to help us track your referral.
6. Select how you heard about us in the sixth field from the options provided.
7. In the last field, add any additional comments or information that you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name** (1, Text, Required): Please enter your full name to help us identify you.
- **Email** (2, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
- **Phone number** (3, Text, Required): Enter your phone number to ensure we can reach you efficiently.
- **Webinar URL** (4, Text, Optional): If available, provide the URL of the webinar you're referring for quick access.
- **Referral code** (5, Text, Required): Enter the referral code to help us track your referral.
- **How did you hear about us** (6, Select One, Required): Choose how you heard about us from the options provided.
- **Additional Comments** (7, Note, Optional): Use this field to add any extra information you'd like to share with us.
