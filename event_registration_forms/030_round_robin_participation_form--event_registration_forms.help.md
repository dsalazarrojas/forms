# Round Robin Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for a Round Robin Participation event. It is intended to be used by organizers and participants to gather necessary details for the event's management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the Participant Information section with your personal details.
3. Provide Session Information, including any relevant session-specific details.
4. Enter your Time Information, including your availability and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information, text, required/optional)**: This section collects your name, title, and other personal details. Please ensure to fill in your actual name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Session Information (Session Information, text, required/optional)**: This section is for providing details about the session you are participating in or interested in. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Time Information (Time Information, text, required/optional)**: This section is for sharing your time availability and schedule. Please list your work hours, breaks, and any other relevant time commitments.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you are having trouble with a field, please consult the form designer or the event organizer.
- The more detailed and accurate information you provide, the better we can plan and coordinate the event.
