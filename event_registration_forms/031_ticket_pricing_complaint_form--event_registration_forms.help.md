# Ticket Pricing Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ticket Pricing Complaint Form is used to collect information from event attendees who have experienced issues with ticket pricing. This form is a crucial tool for event organizers to understand attendees' concerns and investigate these issues efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of complaint you are experiencing.
2. Enter the date of the ticket pricing event.
3. Enter the name of the event where you purchased the ticket.
4. Enter your name as the attendee.
5. Provide your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions.
6. Describe the complaint in detail.
7. Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of complaint you are experiencing. Please choose one of the options provided.
* **Ticket price** (`ticket_price`, number, optional): Enter the ticket price you paid or believe you should have paid. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Ticket pricing date** (`ticket_pricing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the ticket pricing event. This will help us understand the timing of your complaint.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event where you purchased the ticket.
* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the attendee who experienced the complaint.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for follow-up questions.
* **Complaint description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): Describe the complaint you are experiencing in detail.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Submitter information** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): If you'd like to include any additional information about yourself, please enter it here.
