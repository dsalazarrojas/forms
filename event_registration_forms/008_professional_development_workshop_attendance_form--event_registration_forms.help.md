# Professional Development Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your plans to attend a workshop. Your feedback is crucial in helping us tailor the event to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Choose whether you will attend one of the offered workshops or decline.
5. Select the date you registered for the workshop.
6. Optionally, specify the time you registered.
7. If you have any additional participant information or need to add any extra details, you can write it in the "Additional info" field.
8. If necessary, select an assigned tool for your participation in the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Will you attend the workshop** (Select One, required: false):
	+ Select one of the offered workshops: Workshop A, Workshop B, Workshop C, or Workshop D.
	+ Select "Do not attend" if you are not planning to attend.
* **Date registered** (Date, required: false): Select the date you registered for the workshop.
* **Time registered** (Time, required: false): Optionally, select the time you registered for the workshop.
* **Participant information** (Note, required: false): Provide any additional participant information you have.
* **Additional info** (Note, required: false): Add any extra details you need to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Assigned tool** (Text, required: false): If necessary, select an assigned tool for your participation in the workshop.
