# Professional Conference Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a professional conference. It helps organizers understand what attendees liked or disliked about the event, what they thought of the content, speakers, and facilities, and what they would like to see improved in future conferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each page of the form carefully before filling out the fields.
2. Answer each question honestly, and only fill out the sections that apply to your experience.
3. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional feedback" section.
4. If you are interested in future events of this type, please select "Yes" under the "Are you interested in this type of event?" section.
5. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How can we improve our conference?** (`introduction`, `note`, `required: false`): This is a note field where you can provide any suggestions or comments about how the conference can be improved.
* **Speaker Performance** (`speaker_performance`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the performance of the speakers you heard during the conference. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose how the conference was organized. Choose one of the options: Event, Organized by, External, Other.
* **Session quality** (`session_quality`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the quality of the sessions you attended. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Content** (`content`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the content of the sessions you attended. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Speaker attendance** (`speaker_attendance`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the speaker attendance. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, `number`, `required: false`): Indicate how many sessions you attended.
* **Networking** (`networking`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the networking opportunities. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, `required: false`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
* **Conference facilities** (`conference_facilities`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate the conference facilities. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Rate your overall experience. Choose all that apply from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Recommendations for future improvements** (`recommendations`, `note`, `required: false`): If you have any suggestions for future conferences, please include them here.
* **Additional suggestions** (`suggestions`, `note`, `required: false`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required: false`): If you would like to be contacted for future events, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, `required: false`): If you would like to be contacted for future events, please enter your phone number here.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, `required: false`): This field is not currently used but may be used in the future to track when this form was filled out.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `required: false`): This field is not currently used but may be used in the future to track when this form was filled out.
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose where the conference was held. Choose one of the options: Event venue, Virtual, Other, Online.
* **How many people attended?** (`attendees_count`, `number`, `required: false`): Indicate approximately how many people attended the conference.
* **Expected attendees in the next 6 months** (`attendee_diet`, `number`, `required: false`): Indicate approximately how many people you would expect to attend in the next 6 months.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose your expected budget for future conferences. Choose one of the options: Low, Medium, High, Very High.
* **Event type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Choose the type of event this conference was. Choose one of the options: Conference, Seminar, Meeting, Webinar.
* **Are you interested in this type of event?** (`attendees_interest`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If you would attend future conferences of this type, choose all that apply from the options: True, False.
* **Would you come back?** (`repeat_attendance`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): If you would attend future conferences of this type, choose all that apply from the options: True, False.
* **Next event** (`next_event`, `date`, `required: false`): This field is not currently used but may be used in the future to track when the next conference will be.
