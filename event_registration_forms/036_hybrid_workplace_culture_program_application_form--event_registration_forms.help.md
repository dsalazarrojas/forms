# Hybrid Workplace Culture Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Workplace Culture Program Application Form is designed to collect information from employees interested in participating in the program. This form will help us understand employee preferences and tailor future events to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Make sure to enter information in all fields marked as "Required".
2. Ensure you only apply for events you have not already registered for.
3. Be truthful and accurate when providing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Email**: Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are interested in (Team-building, Training, or Other).
* **Program Date**: Select the date of the event you are interested in.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the event or activity you are interested in (optional).
* **Program Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Attendees**: Select who you would like to bring with you (Self, Colleague, Supervisor, or Other).
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you would like to be assigned to use during the event (Chatjimmy or Tony).

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your HR department.
