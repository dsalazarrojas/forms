# Health Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Conference Registration Form is designed for individuals to register for a health conference. It collects personal data and medical information to provide attendees with updates on the latest events, promotions, and services related to the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Info section with your details.
2. Select your medical profession from the Medical Info dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional information in the Informative Content section.
4. Consent to receive updates on conference events, promotions, and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Info

* **Personal Info (text)**: Enter your personal details, including your full name and any other relevant information.
* **Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
* **Surname (text)**: Enter your surname.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.

### Medical Info

* **Medical Info (select_one)**: Select your medical profession from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	+ Cardiovascular Specialist
	+ Dentist
	+ General Practitioner
	+ Internist
	+ Pediatrician
	+ Psychologist
	+ Surgeon
	+ Urologist

### Additional Information

* **Informative Content (text)**: Add any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Content 2 (text)**: Add any further information you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure you receive relevant updates and notifications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* The form will only collect personal data for the purpose of providing you with updates on conference events, promotions, and services.
