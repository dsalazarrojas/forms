# Corporate Retreat Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Retreat Waiver Form is designed to collect basic information and risk acknowledgements from participants in corporate retreats. This form is meant to be filled out by each attendee to confirm their participation and acknowledge any potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the first page, provide your participant information in the text field.
2. On the second page, acknowledge any risks involved by typing your response in the text field.
3. On the third page, sign your name to confirm your submission.
4. On the last page, confirm your acknowledgement by typing your response in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (text):
	+ This field is for providing your name and contact information as a participant in the corporate retreat.
	+ Please enter your full name, company, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (text):
	+ This field is for acknowledging any potential risks involved in the corporate retreat.
	+ Please type in your response to confirm you have read and understood the risks.
* **Participant Signature** (text):
	+ This field is for signing your name to confirm your submission.
	+ Please sign your name to indicate your participation.
* **Acknowledge** (text):
	+ This field is for confirming your acknowledgement of the risks involved.
	+ Please type in your response to confirm you have read and understood the risks.

## Tips
- Make sure to read the risk acknowledgement carefully before submitting the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your information is up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
