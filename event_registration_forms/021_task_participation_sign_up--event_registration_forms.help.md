# Task Participation Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Task Participation Sign Up" form is designed for participants to sign up for a task, providing essential details about themselves and their task preferences. Please fill out this form to register your interest in participating in the task and to provide the required information for task assignment and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure you can be contacted for further instructions and updates.
3. Provide your Phone number for any additional contact.
4. Choose your task preferences by selecting the relevant options from the dropdown menu.
5. Select your availability by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the corresponding field.
6. If you have already signed up for the task, please fill in the Task ID field with the provided ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. Please ensure to spell it correctly to avoid any confusion.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further instructions and updates.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any additional contact.
* **Task Preferences**: Select the options that apply to your preferences for the task.
* **Availability**: Choose "Yes" if you are available to participate, and "No" otherwise.
* **Task ID**: If you have already signed up for the task, please fill in the provided Task ID.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information and task preferences to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the task administrator for guidance.
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any delays or confusion.
