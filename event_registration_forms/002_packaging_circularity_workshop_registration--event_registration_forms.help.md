# Packaging Circularity Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Circularity Workshop Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees for the Packaging Circularity Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Details, including your name and email.
2. Enter your Workshop Details, such as workshop session date and time, and location.
3. Specify your preferences, including your preferred workshop schedule and any special requests.
4. Review all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Details
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Any other relevant information** (`other_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that you think will be useful for the workshop organizers.

### 2. Workshop Details
* **Workshop Session** (`workshop_details`, `text`, required): Enter the workshop session date and time, and other relevant information.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the workshop location details.
* **Other Workshop Details** (`other_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any other workshop details that are relevant.

### 3. Preferences
* **Preferred Workshop Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`, `text`, optional): Specify your preferred workshop schedule.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requests or requirements.

### 4. Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Confirm your workshop registration by clicking this button.
