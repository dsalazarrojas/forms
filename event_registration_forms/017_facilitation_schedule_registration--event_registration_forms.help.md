# Facilitation Schedule Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facilitation Schedule Registration form is a tool to collect essential details from participants attending events like workshops, conferences, or seminars. It helps event organizers gather the required information for smooth event management and ensures accurate participant details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the workshop you will be attending in the **Workshop Name** field.
2. Provide the start date of the workshop in the **Start Date** field.
3. Enter the end date of the workshop in the **End Date** field.
4. Fill in your name in the **Participant Name** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Participant Email** field.
6. Fill in your phone number in the **Participant Phone** field.
7. If necessary, include any additional notes about your participation or any special requests in the **Participant Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Workshop Name** (1, text, required/optional: required)
Enter the name of the workshop you are attending. This is a required field to ensure the correct workshop is registered.
### **Start Date** (2, date, required/optional: required)
Enter the date when the workshop starts. This helps event organizers keep track of the schedule.
### **End Date** (3, date, required/optional: required)
Enter the date when the workshop ends. This ensures accurate scheduling and planning.
### **Participant Name** (4, text, required/optional: required)
Enter your name as a participant. This is essential for registration and identification.
### **Participant Email** (5, email, required/optional: required)
Enter your email address for communication and follow-ups.
### **Participant Phone** (6, text, required/optional: required)
Enter your contact phone number in case of last-minute updates or requests.
### **Participant Notes** (7, note, required/optional: required)
Add any specific notes or requests related to your participation. This can be helpful for event organizers to accommodate your needs.
### **Tips**
