## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method used for the registration: Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or Discover.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount to be paid for the event.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the order, which should be equal to the payment amount.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event being registered for.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of event: Tabletop, Boardgame, Card Game, Role Playing, or Miniatures Game.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the registration.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment status: Paid, Partial, or Pending.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the registration status: Registered, Pending, or Declined.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment gateway: Stripe or PayPal.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method: Online Payment or Offline Payment.
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information about the attendee.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Payment Refund** (`payment_refund`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment refund status: True or False.
* **Refund Reason** (`payment_refund_reason`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for payment refund if payment refund status is True.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct payment method and event details to avoid errors.
* Double-check the payment status and registration status before submitting the form.
* If you need to refund a payment, select True for payment refund status and provide a reason in refund reason field.
* If you have any questions or issues with form submission, contact the event organizers or support team.
