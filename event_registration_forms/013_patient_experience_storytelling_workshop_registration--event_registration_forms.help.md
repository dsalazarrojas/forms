# Patient Experience Storytelling Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Experience Storytelling Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from patients, their team members, or caregivers about their experience, preferences, and contact information for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Role in the form.
2. Choose your Workshop Preferences (morning, afternoon, or both).
3. Consent to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
4. If you wish, provide your Contact Number and Additional Comments.
5. Choose your Sign-up Preferences (in-person, online, or both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter a valid email address.
* **Role** (5): Select your role as a Patient, Team, Caregiver, or Other.
* **Workshop Preferences** (6): Choose the time of the workshop you prefer (morning, afternoon, or both).
* **Consent** (7): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Contact Number** (8): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (9): Share any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Sign-up Preferences** (10): Choose how you would like to sign up for the workshop (in-person, online, or both).
* **Workshop Date** (11): Enter the date of the workshop you are interested in (optional).
* **Workshop Time** (12): Enter the time of the workshop you are interested in (optional).

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Choose your preferences carefully to ensure you receive the information you need.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us!
