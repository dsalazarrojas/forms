# Hunting Expedition Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants and guides about their experiences, equipment, and vehicles for a hunting expedition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Fill out as much information as possible to provide a comprehensive overview of your capabilities and experiences.
3. Be honest about your experience level, equipment, and vehicle capabilities.
4. Select the correct options for your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required): Fill out this field with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Has Permission Form?** (`permission_form_participant`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have completed the permission form or not.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, `text`, optional): Share your relevant experience with hunting, wilderness survival, or other skills that are essential for this expedition.
* **Participant License** (`participant_license`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of licenses you hold, such as firearms or driver's licenses.
* **Participant Vehicle** (`participant_vehicle`, `text`, optional): Provide details about your vehicle, including make, year, and type.
* **Participant Equipment** (`participant_equipment`, `text`, optional): Describe the equipment you plan to bring, such as binoculars or cameras.
* **Guide Permission** (`guide_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are a licensed guide or not.
* **Guide Experience** (`guide_experience`, `text`, optional): Share your experience as a guide, including relevant certifications or training.
* **Guide Equipment** (`guide_equipment`, `text`, optional): Describe the equipment you plan to bring as a guide.
* **Guide Vehicle** (`guide_vehicle`, `text`, optional): Provide details about your vehicle as a guide.
* **Guide License** (`guide_license`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of licenses you hold as a guide, such as firearms or driver's licenses.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the expedition.
* **Guide Agreement** (`guide_agreement`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the expedition as a guide.
* **Participant Vehicle Details** (`participant_vehicle_details`, `text`, optional): Provide more details about your vehicle, including any modifications or special features.
* **Guide Vehicle Details** (`guide_vehicle_details`, `text`, optional): Provide more details about your vehicle as a guide.
* **Participant Equipment Details** (`participant_equipment_details`, `text`, optional): Describe any specialized equipment you plan to bring.
* **Guide Equipment Details** (`guide_equipment_details`, `text`, optional): Describe any specialized equipment you plan to bring.
* **Participant Agreed to Terms** (`participant_agreed_to_terms`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the expedition.
* **Guide Agreed to Terms** (`guide_agreed_to_terms`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the expedition as a guide.
* **Participant Vehicle Type** (`participant_vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of vehicle you plan to bring, such as 2-Wheel or All-Terrain.
* **Guide Vehicle Type** (`guide_vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of vehicle you plan to bring as a guide.
* **Participant Vehicle Make** (`participant_vehicle_make`, `text`, optional): Provide the make of your vehicle.
* **Guide Vehicle Make** (`guide_vehicle_make`, `text`, optional): Provide the make of your vehicle as a guide.
* **Participant Vehicle Year** (`participant_vehicle_year`, `number`, optional): Provide the year of your vehicle.
* **Guide Vehicle Year** (`guide_vehicle_year`, `number`, optional): Provide the year of your vehicle as a guide.
