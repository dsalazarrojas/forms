# eco-efficiency-symposium-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from attendees interested in attending the eco-efficiency symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form sections in order, from top to bottom.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Details
• **Attendee Details** (text, required): Please provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself as the attendee.

### Session Interests
• **Session Interests** (select_multiple, required): Select all the sessions that interest you, using the options: Energy Efficiency, Sustainable Materials, Green Buildings, Renewable Energy, and Water Conservation.

### Special Requirements
• **Special Requirements** (note, optional): If you need special assistance or accommodations, please provide details here. This could include, but is not limited to, wheelchair accessibility, sign language interpretation, or specific dietary needs.

### Session Preferences
• **Session Preferences** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred session time: Morning, Afternoon, Both, or TBA (To Be Announced).

### Submission Agreement
• **Submission Agreement** (text, required): Please indicate your acceptance of the submission agreement terms. This is a critical part of the registration process.

### Dietary Preferences
• **Dietary Preferences** (select_multiple, optional): Select your dietary preferences, using the options: Vegetarian, Vegan, Omnivore.

### Accessibility Questions
• **Accessibility Questions** (text, optional): If you have any accessibility questions or concerns, please provide them here.

### Comments
• **Comments** (text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when providing your details.
* Choose your session interests and preferences carefully.
* Review your responses before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
