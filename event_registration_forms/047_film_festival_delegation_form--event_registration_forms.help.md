# Film Festival Delegation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the registration process for participants in a film festival. It collects information about each participant, their project, category, and submit status to streamline the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your project details, including the title of your project.
2. Select the category that best describes your project (e.g., Action, Drama, Comedy).
3. Indicate whether you are submitting your project for consideration (True or False).
4. Provide any additional comments about your project.
5. Enter your organisation's name and any other relevant information.
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project**: Please enter the title of your project.
	+ This field is used to collect the title of your project.
	+ It is a required field.
* **Category**: Please select the category that best describes your project from the dropdown list.
	+ This field is used to determine the category of your project.
	+ You can select one or multiple categories (Action, Drama, Comedy).
* **Submit**: Please indicate whether you are submitting your project for consideration (True or False).
	+ This field is used to indicate whether you are submitting your project for review.
	+ Select True if you are submitting your project, False otherwise.
* **Comments**: Please provide any additional comments about your project.
	+ This field is used to collect any additional information about your project.
	+ You can enter any relevant details that are not covered in other fields.
* **Organisation**: Please enter your organisation's name.
	+ This field is used to collect your organisation's name.
	+ You can enter any relevant information about your organisation.
* **Submit**: Please review and submit your form after filling out all required fields.
	+ This field is a submit button, do not enter any text in this field.
