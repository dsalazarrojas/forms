# Storytelling Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about attendees who want to participate in a Storytelling Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, start by filling out all required fields and select options for the workshop preferences (if applicable). 
- Make sure to enter accurate contact details including full name, email, and desired phone number.
- For the workshop date and times, choose your preferred dates and times for the workshop.
- If you have any special requests or preferences, please note them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Story Form Data**: This is the main section of the form where you will share your story ideas and preferences. Please enter a brief description of the story you would like to share, including any relevant details or themes.
- **Workshop Preferences**: This field allows you to indicate if you would like to participate in the workshop as an observer or as a presenter. Please select 'Yes' if you would like to participate as a presenter, or 'No' if you would like to observe only.
- **Contact Details**: This field is for collecting your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number. Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
- **Full Name**: This field is for your full name as it will be used for workshop materials and communication.
- **Email**: This field is for collecting your email address, which will be used for sending workshop updates and information.
- **Phone**: This field is for collecting your phone number, which will be used for any follow-up communication regarding the workshop.
- **Desired Workshop Date**: This is the date you would like to attend the workshop.
- **Start Time**: This is the start time you would like to attend the workshop.
- **End Time**: This is the end time you would like to attend the workshop.
- **Tips**:
  - Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
  - If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us.
  - You can contact us if you need more information or have any questions.
