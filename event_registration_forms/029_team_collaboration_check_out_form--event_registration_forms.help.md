# Team Collaboration Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to facilitate team collaboration checkout, allowing team members to check out team members and resources for a project. It helps the organization to keep track of project-related information and ensure that all necessary resources are checked out and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Project Title**: Enter the title of the project you are working on in the "Project Title" field.
2. **Select Team Members**: Indicate whether team members are required for the project by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Team Members" field.
3. **Specify Equipment Needed**: Enter any equipment required for the project in the "Equipment Needed" field.
4. **List Resources Required**: Enter any resources required for the project in the "Resources Required" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Project Title (project_title)
* A short description of the project you are working on, e.g. "Marketing Campaign: Social Media"
* Please ensure that this field is filled in accurately to help the organization keep track of project titles.

### Team Members (team_members)
* Indicate whether team members are required for the project.
* Select "Yes" if you need to work with team members on the project.
* Select "No" if you can work independently on the project.

### Equipment Needed (equipment_needed)
* A list of equipment required for the project, e.g. "laptops, projectors, etc."
* Please be specific and accurate in listing the equipment needed.

### Resources Required (resources_required)
* A list of resources required for the project, e.g. "facilities, personnel, etc."
* Please be specific and accurate in listing the resources needed.

## Tips
* Ensure that you only fill out this form for authorized projects and team activities.
* Double-check your answers to ensure that they are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your team lead or project manager for clarification.
