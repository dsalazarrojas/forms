# music_festival_volunteer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music festival volunteer application form is used to collect contact information and details about your volunteer availability and experience for the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you have a valid email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact**: Provide your name and any additional contact information you'd like us to have.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on festival materials.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for us to contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Indicate if you are available to volunteer for the festival. If you are available, select 'Yes' to be considered for roles.
*   *   Yes
    *   No
*   **Role**: Select a role that you'd be interested in, or leave blank if you are open to being assigned a role by the festival team.
*   *   event manager
    *   festival staff
    *   event staff
    *   stage support
    *   merchandise team
    *   security team
    *   stage hands
    *   parking team
    *   volunteer coordinator
*   **Volunteer Experience**: Share any relevant volunteer experience you've had in the past.
*   **Availability**:
*   *   morning
*   *   afternoon
*   *   evening
*   *   all days
*   **Availability Date**: Select the date you are available to volunteer.
*   **Availability Time**: Select the specific time you are available to volunteer.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being assigned a role.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct role and availability options.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [festival email or phone number].
