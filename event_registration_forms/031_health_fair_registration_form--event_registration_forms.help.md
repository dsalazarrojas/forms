# Health Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant registration data for health fairs, which are events that provide health-related services and resources to community members. The form helps us better organize and prepare for the event, ensuring that we have accurate information about participants and their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including any relevant details that will help us serve you better during the event.
2. Add your contact information, including your name, phone number, and other relevant details.
3. Share event details, such as the event date and location.
4. Indicate your participant requirements, such as any special needs or requests.
5. Share your participant preferences, such as any services or resources you are interested in.
6. Provide any additional information that will help us serve you better.
7. Choose whether a participant signature is required or optional.
8. If a participant signature is required, sign the form with the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)**: Enter your name, email, or any other relevant details to identify you as a participant.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)**: Enter your phone number and any other contact information that will help us reach you.
* **Event Details (Event Details)**: Enter the event date, location, and any other relevant details about the event.
* **Participant Requirements (Participant Requirements)**: Indicate any special needs or requests you have, such as wheelchair accessibility or language support.
* **Participant Preferences (Participant Preferences)**: Indicate any services or resources you are interested in, such as health screenings or workshops.
* **Participant Additional Information (Participant Additional Information)**: Enter any other information that will help us serve you better, such as medical conditions or previous medical procedures.
* **Participant Signature (Participant Signature)**: Choose whether a participant signature is required or optional. If required, sign the form with the current date and time.
* **Signature Date (Signature Date)**: Enter the date of your signature if it is required.
* **Signature Time (Signature Time)**: Enter the time of your signature if it is required.
* **Participant Info Verification (Participant Info Verification)**: This field is for participant verification purposes only.
* **Participant Photo ID (Participant Photo ID)**: Enter your participant photo ID number if it is required.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth if it is required.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number if it is required.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
