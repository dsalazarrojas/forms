# Game Development Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us track attendance for the Game Development Workshop. Please ensure you have permission to attend and follow the guidelines carefully. If you have already submitted this form, you should not submit it again.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant options for each field, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Enter your Name, Phone, Company, and select your Participant Role and Teams (if applicable).
3. Confirm your Email address by entering it in the Email field and confirming it in the Email Confirmation field.
4. Review the submission form by checking the Confirm Submission field.
5. Click the submit button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop you want to attend.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required: false): Enter the same email address you entered in the Email field to confirm.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Choose the role that best describes you as a participant:
	+ Game Developer
	+ Student
	+ Guest
* **Team** (`team`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all teams you are affiliated with (if applicable):
	+ Front End
	+ Back End
	+ Game Development
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments you have about the workshop.
* **Confirm Submission** (`submit_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that the information entered is correct and you're ready for submission:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
