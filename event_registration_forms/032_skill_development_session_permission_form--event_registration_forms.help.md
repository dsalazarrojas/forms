# Skill Development Session Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Development Session Permission Form is designed to help us verify your identity and contact information to grant you permission to attend, present, and take notes at our skill development sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select all the permissions you are seeking (Permission to Attend, Permission to Attend and Present, Permission to Attend and Present and Take Notes).
3. Enter your participant information (Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone Number).
4. Choose a valid date for the permission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Information (Session Information, Text, Not Required)**: Here you can enter a brief description of the session you are interested in.
* **Participant Name (Participant Name, Text, Not Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Permission Type (Permission Type, Select Multiple, Not Required)**: Select all the permissions you are seeking.
  - Permission to Attend: Grant permission to simply attend the session.
  - Permission to Attend and Present: Grant permission to attend and present at the session.
  - Permission to Attend and Present and Take Notes: Grant permission to attend, present, and take notes at the session.
* **Participant Date of Birth (Participant Date of Birth, Date, Not Required)**: Enter your date of birth to verify your age and identity.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email, Email, Not Required)**: Enter your official email address.
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone, Text, Not Required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Permission Date (Permission Date, Date, Not Required)**: Enter a valid date for the permission.

Note: If you have any issues with the form or need further assistance, please contact your local support team.
