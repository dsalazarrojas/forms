# Team Building Retreat Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record leave requests for team members to attend team building activities, and to gather information about the event details and attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Team Member Name: Enter the name of the team member requesting leave.
	* Leave Start Date: Enter the start date of the leave.
	* Leave End Date: Enter the end date of the leave.
	* Manager Name: Enter the name of the team member's manager.
	* Manager Email: Enter the email address of the team member's manager.
	* Manager Phone: Enter the phone number of the team member's manager.
	* Team Lead Name: Enter the name of the team lead responsible for the event.
	* Team Lead Email: Enter the email address of the team lead responsible for the event.
	* Team Lead Phone: Enter the phone number of the team lead responsible for the event.
	* Team Building Notes: Enter any additional notes about the team building activity.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* Team Building Activity: Check 'Yes' if the team member is attending a team building activity.
	* Team Building Location: Enter the location of the team building activity.
	* Team Building Number of Participants: Enter the number of participants in the team building activity.
	* Team Building Duration: Enter the duration of the team building activity.
	* Team Building Additional Info: Enter any additional information about the team building activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Building Retreat Leave**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Team Member Name**: Enter the name of the team member requesting leave.
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the start date of the leave, in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Leave End Date**: Enter the end date of the leave, in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Team Building Activity**: Check 'Yes' if the team member is attending a team building activity.
* **Team Building Location**: Enter the location of the team building activity.
* **Team Building Number of Participants**: Enter the number of participants in the team building activity.
* **Team Building Duration**: Enter the duration of the team building activity.
* **Team Building Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about the team building activity.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the team member's manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the team member's manager.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team member's manager.
* **Team Lead Name**: Enter the name of the team lead responsible for the event.
* **Team Lead Email**: Enter the email address of the team lead responsible for the event.
* **Team Lead Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team lead responsible for the event.
* **Team Building Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the team building activity.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that the form can be processed correctly.
- Use the date format `mm/dd/yyyy` for the leave start and end dates.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact your manager or team lead for clarification.
