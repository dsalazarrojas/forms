# Independent Artist Tour Support Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to walk you through the completion of the Independent Artist Tour Support Application Form. The form is divided into several sections, each asking for specific information about the artist and their tour. Please refer to the field explanations below for more information on each section.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2.  Ensure that all fields are correctly formatted according to their respective types (e.g., email for email fields).
3.  Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure that it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about your application.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your home or studio address.
*   **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself as an artist.
*   **Tour Budget** (`tour_budget`, `number`, optional): Enter the total budget you are requesting for your tour.
*   **Tour Dates** (`tour_dates`, `date`, optional): Enter the dates you plan for your tour.
*   **Project Goals** (`project_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your tour's goals and objectives.
*   **Funding Requested** (`funding_requested`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount of funding you are requesting for your tour.
*   **Financial History** (`financial_history`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief history of your past financial dealings.
*   **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your tax ID number for tax purposes.
*   **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter your website URL.
*   **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, `text`, optional): Enter your social media links (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, Twitter).
*   **YouTube Video ID** (`youtube_video_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of your YouTube video.
*   **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, `text`, optional): Enter a statement about your artistic goals and vision for your tour.
*   **Promotional Materials** (`promotional_materials`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your promotional materials.
*   **Promotional Media** (`promotional_media`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of your promotional media (e.g., images, videos, audio files).
*   **Promotional Media Type** (`promotional_media_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of promotional media you are providing (e.g., image, audio, video).
*   **Promotional Media** (`promotional_media`, `text`, optional): Upload your promotional media file(s).
