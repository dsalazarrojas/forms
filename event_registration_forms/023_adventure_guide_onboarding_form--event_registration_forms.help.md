# Adventure Guide Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information about you as an adventure guide, ensuring we can verify your identity, assess your qualifications, and confirm your availability for guiding activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form carefully, answering all required fields.
2. Ensure you provide valid and accurate information, as we use this data to assess your qualifications and eligibility for guiding activities.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name): Provide your legal full name as it appears on your documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/01/1990).
* **Address** (Address): Enter your current residential address, including street address and postal code.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address): Enter a valid email address for communication and verification purposes.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Guiding Experience Years** (Guiding Experience Years): Report the number of years you have experience as an adventure guide.
* **Certifications Held** (Certifications Held): List any relevant certifications or qualifications you hold.
* **Specializations** (Specializations): Select all relevant adventure activities you specialize in (e.g., Rock climbing, Mountaineering, etc.).
* **Fitness Level** (Fitness Level): Rate your current physical fitness level (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions): Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect your work as a guide.
* **Reference Contact** (Reference Contact): Provide name and contact information of a professional reference.
* **Availability** (Availability): Specify when you are available to work as a guide.

Note: Please answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
