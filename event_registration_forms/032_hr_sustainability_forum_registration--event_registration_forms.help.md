This form is for registering an HR Sustainability Forum. The form will collect information about the attendees of the event and help the organizers plan the event accordingly. This form should only be filled out once per person. Please make sure to fill out the form correctly and completely before submitting it.

# HR Sustainability Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you fill out the HR Sustainability Forum registration form. Please read through the guide carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "company" field.
4. Select your role in the "role" field (if applicable).
5. Select your department (if applicable) in the "department" field.
6. Choose whether you are interested in joining a session in the "session_interest" field.
7. Enter the date of the event in the "event_date" field.
8. Select the time of the event in the "event_time" field.
9. Enter the location of the event in the "event_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name here. This is required information.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address here. This is required information.
* **Company**: Enter your company name here. This is required information.
* **Role**: If you are an employee or representative of your company, select your role here (e.g. CEO, Manager, Employee).
* **Department**: If you are an employee or representative of your company, select your department here (e.g. Marketing, Sales, IT).
* **Team**: If you are part of a team, enter your team name here.
* **Session Interest**: Choose "Yes" if you are interested in joining a session. Choose "No" otherwise.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the "MM/DD/YYYY" format (e.g., 02/22/2024).
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event where you will be attending.
