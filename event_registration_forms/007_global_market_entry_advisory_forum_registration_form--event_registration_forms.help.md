# Global Market Entry Advisory Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Market Entry Advisory Forum Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from participants for an event. Please ensure you only register if you plan to attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field. This is the name that will be displayed on any event communication.
2. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field. This will be used to communicate event updates and information.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field. This will be used for emergency contact in case of event changes or cancellations.
4. Choose your preferred method of communication in the "Participant Preferences" field. Select "Yes" if you prefer email, phone, or both, or "No" if you prefer not to be contacted.
5. Indicate your interest in specific topics in the "Topics of Interest" field. Select "Yes" for topics you are interested in, or "No" if not.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration. This will confirm your registration and send a confirmation email to the provided email address.
7. Enter the date of registration in the "Event Registration Date" field (optional). This is the date when you registered for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select, required/optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (email, phone, or both).
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, select, required/optional): Select topics you are interested in (yes or no).
* **Event Registration Date** (`event_registration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you registered for the event.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information before submitting the form.
* Ensure your preferred contact method is selected accurately.
* Be honest about your interest in specific topics to help event organizers tailor the event to your needs.
