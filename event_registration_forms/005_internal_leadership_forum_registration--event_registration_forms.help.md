# Internal Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for internal leadership forum registration. It is intended for attendees to provide their session preferences, details, and contact information so that organizers can better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your session preferences by choosing one of the options under "Session Preferences".
2. Provide your attendee details, including the number of attendees.
3. Choose your session selections.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
5. If necessary, provide additional information about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options to indicate whether you will attend the session.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, number, not required): Enter the number of attendees you will be bringing.
* **Session Selections** (`session_selections`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, not required): Enter your email address so that organizers can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Logistics Info** (`logistics_info`, number, not required): This field is intended for additional logistics information, such as transportation or accommodation requirements.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, not required): Provide any additional information about your attendance or requirements.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, date, not required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the event. 

Note: If a field is marked as "not required", it means that the field is optional and can be skipped if not applicable.
