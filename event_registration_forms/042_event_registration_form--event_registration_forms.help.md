# Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for events. Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully to ensure accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out each field according to the guidelines below.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration (text)** (`event_registration_form`, required: no): This is the title of the event you are registering for.
* **Attendees (select multiple)** (`attendees`, required: no): Select "Yes" if you are attending the event with other people.
* **Name (text)** (`name`, required: no): Please fill out your name as it should be on your registration record.
* **Date of Birth (date)** (`date_of_birth`, required: no): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Destination (text)** (`destination`, required: no): Enter the destination for this event.
* **Address (text)** (`address`, required: no): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required: no): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates about the event.
* **Phone Number (text)** (`phone_number`, required: no): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Confirm Attendance (select one)** (`confirm_attendance`, required: no): Select "Yes" to confirm that you will attend the event.
* **Comments (note)** (`comments`, required: no): Add any additional comments about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate.
* If you are attending the event with others, select "Yes" for the "Attendees" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer.
