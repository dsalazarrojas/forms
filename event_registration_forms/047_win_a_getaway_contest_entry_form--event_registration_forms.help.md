# Win A Getaway Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and business information for the Win A Getaway Contest. It is intended for participants who wish to enter the contest and provide their contact details, while businesses can use it to provide their information and any additional messages.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant or individual entering the contest in this field.
2. **Business Name**: If you're representing a business, enter your company name here.
3. **Contact Email**: Enter the email address associated with the participant or business. This will be used for any communication related to the contest.
4. **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact phone number for the participant or business.
5. **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant (individual person) who will be using this account.
6. **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
7. **Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of the business.
8. **Entry Date**: Select the date you're submitting your entry for the contest.
9. **Participant Address**: Enter the address of the participant, including street, number, and postal code.
10. **Business Address**: Enter the address of the business, including street, number, and postal code.
11. **Participant City**, **Participant State**, **Participant Postal Code**, and **Participant Country**: If applicable, enter the city, state, postal code, and country of the participant's address.
12. **Business City**, **Business State**, **Business Postal Code**, and **Business Country**: If applicable, enter the city, state, postal code, and country of the business address.
13. **Message** (for participant or business): Enter any additional message or information you wish to share with the contest organizers.
14. **Entry ID**: Enter a unique identifier (ID number) for your entry.
15. **Entry Timestamp**: Enter the date and time you're submitting this entry. 

**Tips:**

* Please double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* Make sure to include all required fields.
* If you're experiencing issues, reach out to us for assistance.

Note: The "Message" fields are optional and intended for any additional information or comments you might want to include with your entry.
