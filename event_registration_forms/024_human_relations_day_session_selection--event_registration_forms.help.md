# Human Relations Day Session Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Human Relations Day Session Selection form successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form sections and select the session you wish to attend.
2. If you have a preferred session date, enter it in the "Preferred Session Date" field.
3. If you have a preferred session time, enter it in the "Preferred Session Time" field.
4. Select your preferred session(s) using the "Session Selection" field.
5. Review the form for any errors and submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Registration**: This field is for your name and contact information. Please enter your name and other required details.
* **Session Selection 1**: Select your preferred session from the list of options.
* **Session Selection 2**: Select your preferred session from the list of options.
* **Session Selection 3**: Select your preferred session from the list of options.
* **...**: This pattern continues up to **Session Selection 200**.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your session preferences.
* **Event Details**: This field is not required and can be used to provide any additional event details.
* **Session Date 1**: Enter the date of your preferred session, if applicable.
* **Session Selection**: Select your preferred session from the list of options.
* **Preferred Session Date**: Enter the date of your preferred session, if applicable.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Enter the time of your preferred session, if applicable.
