# Sustainability In Business Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from users who wish to register for a sustainability workshop. It will help us understand your preferences and interests to provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the workshop(s) you are interested in (Workshop Selection, id: 1).
2. Provide your full name (Full Name, id: 2).
3. Enter your company name (Company, id: 3).
4. Enter your email address (Email, id: 4).
5. Enter your contact number (Contact Number, id: 5).
6. Select the workshop date (Workshop Date, id: 6).
7. Choose your preferred workshop time (Time, id: 7).
8. Enter the date of your registration (Registration Date, id: 8).
9. Select your preferred workshop time (Registration Time, id: 9).
10. Choose your payment method (Payment Method, id: 10).
11. Provide workshop details (Workshop Details, id: 11).
12. Indicate if you are a participant type (Participant Type, id: 12).
13. Select your preferred workshop location (Workshop Location, id: 13).
14. Choose the type of workshop you are interested in (Workshop Type, id: 14).
15. Enter any additional participant details (Participant Details, id: 15).
16. Enter your participant's address (Participant Address, id: 16).
17. Enter the workshop fees (Workshop Fees, id: 17).
18. Indicate if participants will be attending (Participant Attendee, id: 18).
19. Choose your payment status (Payment Status, id: 19).
20. Enter your participant's name (Participant Name, id: 20).
21. Enter your organization's name (Organization, id: 21).
22. Enter your role within the organization (Role, id: 22).
23. Enter your company's name (Company, id: 23).
24. Enter the name of the contact person (Contact Person, id: 24).
25. Indicate if the contact person will be attending (Workshop Attendee, id: 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Selection (id: 1)**: Select the workshop(s) you are interested in attending from the available options.
* **Full Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed during the workshop.
* **Company (id: 3)**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Contact Number (id: 5)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Date (id: 6)**: Select the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Time (id: 7)**: Choose your preferred time for the workshop.
* **Registration Date (id: 8)**: Enter the date you are registering for the workshop.
* **Registration Time (id: 9)**: Choose the time you are registering for the workshop.
* **Payment Method (id: 10)**: Indicate your preferred method of payment (Yes/No).
* **Workshop Details (id: 11)**: Enter any additional details about the workshop you wish to participate in.
* **Participant Type (id: 12)**: Indicate the type of participant you are (Yes/No).
* **Workshop Location (id: 13)**: Enter the preferred location for the workshop (optional).
* **Workshop Type (id: 14)**: Choose the type of workshop you are interested in (Yes/No).
* **Participant Details (id: 15)**: Enter any additional details about the participant (optional).
* **Participant Address (id: 16)**: Enter the participant's address.
* **Workshop Fees (id: 17)**: Enter the workshop fees.
* **Participant Attendee (id: 18)**: Indicate if participants will be attending (Yes/No).
* **Payment Status (id: 19)**: Choose your payment status (Active/Inactive).
* **Participant Name (id: 20)**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Organization (id: 21)**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role (id: 22)**: Enter your role within the organization.
* **Company (id: 23)**: Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person (id: 24)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Workshop Attendee (id: 25)**: Indicate if the contact person will be attending (Yes/No).

Tips:
* Please ensure all required fields are completed to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
