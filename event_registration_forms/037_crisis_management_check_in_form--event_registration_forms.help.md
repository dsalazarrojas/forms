# crisis_management_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crisis management check-in form is used to gather information about incidents that require attention from a team or organization. This form helps to document and categorize incidents, ensuring that necessary steps are taken to mitigate the crisis and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the **Incident Details** field.
2. Enter your contact information in the **Contact Information** field.
3. If necessary, provide information about the team involved in the incident in the **Team Information** field.
4. Select the **Location** where the incident occurred or is currently occurring (On-site or Off-site).
5. Choose the **Severity** of the incident (Critical, High, or Low).
6. Enter any additional comments or updates in the **Status** field.
7. Add any relevant **Notes** about the incident.
8. Provide the emergency contact information in the **Emergency Contact** field.
9. Select the **Reporting Unit** (On-site or Off-site) that was affected by the incident.
10. Enter the location of the **Reporting Unit Location** where the incident occurred.
11. Specify the **Reporting Unit Type** involved in the incident.
12. Enter the size of the **Reporting Unit Size** affected by the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: A brief description of the incident, including the circumstances and any relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Team Information**: If necessary, provide information about the team involved in the incident, including their name and contact information.
* **Location**: Select where the incident occurred (On-site or Off-site).
* **Severity**: Choose the level of severity of the incident (Critical, High, or Low).
* **Status**: Enter any additional comments or updates about the incident.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes about the incident.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Reporting Unit**: Select the location of the reporting unit (On-site or Off-site).
* **Reporting Unit Location**: Enter the location of the reporting unit where the incident occurred.
* **Reporting Unit Type**: Specify the type of reporting unit involved in the incident.
* **Reporting Unit Size**: Enter the size of the reporting unit affected by the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each field accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or team leader.
* If you need help filling out this form, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
