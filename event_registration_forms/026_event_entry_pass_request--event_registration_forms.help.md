# Event Entry Pass Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Entry Pass Request form is a simple, easy-to-use application designed for event attendees and organizers to submit their entry pass requests. This form gathers crucial information to facilitate smooth event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the start and finish dates of the event you wish to attend.
2. Provide your attendee details (e.g., your name, email, and phone number).
3. Specify your access level (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address for verification purposes.
5. Enter your phone number for communication.
6. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_dates** (`event_dates`, text, required/optional):
  Enter the dates of the event you wish to attend. This field is a crucial piece of information for event management, so make sure to enter the correct dates.
* **event_start_date** (`event_start_date`, date, required/optional):
  Enter the start date of the event. This date will help event organizers keep track of event schedules.
* **event_finish_date** (`event_finish_date`, date, required/optional):
  Enter the finish date of the event. This date is essential for event organizers to manage event duration.
* **attendee_details** (`attendee_details`, text, required/optional):
  Provide your name, contact information, and any additional details about your attendance.
* **access_level** (`access_level`, select_one, required/optional):
  If you require special access during the event, select the appropriate level from the provided options.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional):
  Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from event organizers.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional):
  Enter your phone number for easy communication during the event.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear when entering event dates.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact event organizers for clarification.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are up-to-date and accurate.
* Review your application carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to event organizers for assistance.
