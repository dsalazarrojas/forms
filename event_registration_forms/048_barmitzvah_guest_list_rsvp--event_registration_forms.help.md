# Bar Mitzvah Guest List RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bar Mitzvah Guest List RSVP form is designed to collect information from guests who have been invited to celebrate the occasion. This form helps the organizers track who is attending, their contact details, meal preferences, and logistics such as transportation and lodging requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your contact information under **Primary Guest Full Name**, **Contact Email Address**, and choose how many **Adults Attending** and **Children Attending**.
2. Select your **Attendance Status** as either **Joyfully Accept** or **Regretfully Decline**.
3. If you will not be attending, you can leave the **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** field empty.
4. If you will be attending, select your **Adult Entree Selection** from the options provided.
5. Indicate if you will be staying at the **Group Hotel** and if you will need **Shuttle Service**.
6. Enter any **Message to the Bar Mitzvah and Family** if you'd like to send a personal message.
7. Finally, click the **Submit Date** field to complete your RSVP by selecting a date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Guest Full Name**: Enter the name of the guest who is RSVPing.
* **Will you be attending?**: Select either **Joyfully Accept** or **Regretfully Decline** to indicate your attendance status.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address where you can be contacted for event updates and reminders.
* **Number of Adults Attending**: Enter the number of adults attending the event.
* **Number of Children Attending**: Enter the number of children attending the event (ages 12 and under).
* **Names of Additional Guests in Your Party**: List the names of any additional guests attending with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: List any dietary restrictions or allergies you or your party may have (e.g., Nut Allergy, Gluten-Free, Vegan).
* **Do you require a strictly Kosher meal?**: Select **True** if you require a strictly Kosher meal for your party.
* **Adult Entree Selection**: Select up to 3 entree options for the adults in your party.
* **Are you staying at the group hotel?**: Select **Yes** if you've booked a room at the hotel, **Yes - Send info** if you've reserved but not paid for a room, or **No** if you'll be staying elsewhere.
* **Will you require shuttle service to the synagogue?**: Select **Yes** if you'll need transportation from the hotel to the synagogue.
* **Message to the Bar Mitzvah and Family**: Write a message to be sent to the Bar Mitzvah and their family.
* **Date Submitted**: Select the date you're submitting your RSVP.
