# Workforce Digital Literacy Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees for the Workforce Digital Literacy Workshop. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out the form in English.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   You can save the form and come back to it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name_snippet`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for. This could be a specific workshop title or a series of workshop sessions.
*   **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name_snippet`, `text`, `required`): Provide your name as it should be displayed on the workshop materials and communication with you.
*   **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email_snippet`, `email`, `optional`): If you provide your email address, it will be used for communication about the workshop, including any updates or reminders about the event.
*   **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone_snippet`, `text`, `optional`): If you provide your phone number, it will be used for any last-minute communication about the workshop.
*   **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date_snippet`, `date`, `required`): Choose the date of the workshop you are registering for.
*   **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time_snippet`, `time`, `required`): Select the time slot of the workshop you are registering for.
*   **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration_snippet`, `number`, `required`): Choose the duration of the workshop you are registering for.
*   **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location_snippet`, `text`, `required`): Select the location of the workshop you are registering for.
*   **Workshop Time Slot** (`workshop_time_slot_snippet`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose a specific time slot within the selected workshop time.
*   **Attendee Message** (`attendee_message_snippet`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any special requests or comments, please include them here.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields before submitting to ensure accuracy.
*   If you need to register multiple attendees, please fill out the form for each one separately.
*   If you have any issues while filling out the form, contact your workshop administrator for assistance.
