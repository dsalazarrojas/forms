# Field Trip Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Authorization Form is used to gather necessary information and obtain authorization for a field trip, ensuring that students are properly cleared for off-campus excursions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.
* Select the correct student grade level from the options provided, if applicable.
* Enter the student's name and signature on the Student Signature field.
* Enter the parent's name and signature on the Parent Signature field.
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required): Enter the school ID number assigned to your school.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip, if known.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text, required): Describe the trip in as much detail as possible.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, optional): Choose the student's grade level from the provided options.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the trip.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of the trip, in hours or days.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, required): Sign this field to confirm permission for the trip.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Sign this field to confirm permission for the trip.
