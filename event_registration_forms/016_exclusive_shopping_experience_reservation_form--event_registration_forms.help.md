# Exclusive Shopping Experience Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is created for customers to reserve a unique shopping experience. Please fill out the form to ensure a successful and enjoyable appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in the information requested in each field.
* Make sure to submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. customer (customer, text, required: false)
The customer field is where you can enter your name to identify yourself.

### 2. appointment_time (appointment_time, time, required: false)
Enter the time you would like to make an appointment.

### 3. appointment_date (appointment_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date you would like to make an appointment.

### 4. phone (phone, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number for future contact.

### 5. email (email, email, required: false)
Enter your email address for future contact.

### 6. notes (notes, note, required: false)
Leave any additional comments or notes you'd like to be shared with our team.

### 7. reservation_time (reservation_time, time, required: false)
Enter the time you would like to make a reservation.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure a successful appointment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.
