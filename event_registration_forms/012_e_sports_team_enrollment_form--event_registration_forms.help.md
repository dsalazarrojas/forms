# E Sports Team Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Sports Team Enrollment Form is a form designed to collect information about individuals interested in joining an e-sports team. This form helps the team administrators to get to know the players' details, gaming experience, and preferences, and to determine the best fit for the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details in the "User Details" section.
2. Share your gaming experience in the "Gaming Experience" section.
3. Select your preferred team in the "Team Preferences" section.
4. Provide additional game details in the "Game Details" section (if necessary).
5. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.
6. Enter your team name and its description in the "Team Name" and "Team Description" sections.
7. Share your team's experience in the "Team Experience" section.
8. Select your team's formation in the "Team Formation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details (1)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your name, username, or any other relevant details about yourself as a player.
* **Gaming Experience (2)** (`number`, `required: false`): Share your level of experience in gaming, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Team Preferences (3)** (`select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the team(s) you are interested in joining (Team A, Team B, Team C).
* **Game Details (4)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter any additional information about your gaming experience or skills that might be relevant to the team.
* **Email (6)** (`email`, `required: false`): Enter a valid email address that the team administrators can use to contact you.
* **Phone (7)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter your phone number that the team administrators can use to contact you.
* **Team Name (8)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter a name for your team that is distinct and easy to remember.
* **Team Description (9)** (`text`, `required: false`): Enter a brief description of your team, including its strengths and weaknesses.
* **Team Experience (10)** (`number`, `required: false`): Share your team's level of experience in gaming, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Team Formation (11)** (`select_one`, `required: false`): Select your team's formation (Team A, Team B, Team C).
