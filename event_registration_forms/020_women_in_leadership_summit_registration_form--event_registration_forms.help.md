# Women in Leadership Summit Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Women in Leadership Summit. It collects contact and bio information from participants to facilitate communication and event experience customization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This will be used to send you updates and notifications about the event.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
4. Enter your organization name in the **Organization** field.
5. Select your role in the **Role** field. This will help us understand your perspective and experience in the field.
6. Enter your department in the **Department** field.
7. Enter your job function in the **Job Function** field.
8. Enter a brief description of yourself in the **About Me** field.
9. Enter a bio in the **Bio** field, if you would like to share more about yourself.
10. If you have a photo, enter the URL of your photo in the **Photo** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents. This is required.
* **Email**: Enter the email address you would like to use for event communications. This is not required but recommended.
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your current or affiliated organization.
* **Role**: Select your role from the options: Senior Manager, Manager, Employee, or Leader. This will help us understand your perspective and experience in the field.
* **Department**: Enter your department name or title.
* **Job Function**: Enter your job function or title.
* **About Me**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Bio**: Enter a more detailed bio, if you would like to share more about yourself.
* **Photo**: If you have a public photo URL, enter it here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact us separately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us at [support email].
