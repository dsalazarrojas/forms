# Cotc Nursing Information Meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees of a nursing meeting or event. The collected data helps event organizers and healthcare institutions to better understand the composition and needs of the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your meeting information, if applicable.
2. Enter your name as per your preference.
3. Provide your email address if you want to be contacted.
4. Add your phone number if you want to receive updates or reminders.
5. Indicate if you are a healthcare professional (nurse, healthcare professional, or physician).
6. If not a healthcare professional, specify your vocation.
7. Enter the meeting date and time if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Information**:
	+ A field for any additional information about the meeting.
	+ Please type your notes about the meeting.
* **Name**:
	+ Enter your full name, as you would like to be referred to.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Email**:
	+ If you want to receive email updates, reminders, or notifications, enter your email address here.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Phone**:
	+ If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Address**:
	+ Indicate if you will be attending from this location.
	+ Select 'Yes' if this is the case, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Vocation**:
	+ If you are a healthcare professional, select one of the following options:
		- Nurse
		- Healthcare Professional
		- Physician
	+ If none of the above apply, enter your vocation in the 'Other' field.
* **Other**:
	+ If you selected 'Other' in the vocation field, please provide more information about your vocation.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Meeting Date**:
	+ Enter the date of the meeting, if applicable.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Meeting Time**:
	+ Enter the time of the meeting, if applicable.
	+ This field is optional.
