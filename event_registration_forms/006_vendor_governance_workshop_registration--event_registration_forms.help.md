# vendor_governance_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor governance workshop registration form is for vendors to register for a training or educational event on governance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked as "required" in the form fields.
2. Make sure to select the correct session attendance from the provided options.
3. Ensure that you enter your date and time correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Data** (`_id: 1`, `type: text`, required): Fill out this field with your name, as required.
* **Name** (`_id: 2`, `type: text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`_id: 3`, `type: email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate with you about the event.
* **Phone** (`_id: 4`, `type: text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Session Attendance** (`_id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, required): Select the session(s) you plan to attend from the provided options.
* **Date** (`_id: 6`, `type: date`, required): Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (`_id: 7`, `type: time`, required): Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.

## Tips

* Please double-check your session attendance to ensure you select the correct options.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please contact the event organizers in advance.
* Ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to plan event logistics.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* If you are unable to attend, please cancel your registration by contacting the event organizers at least 24 hours prior to the event start date.
