# Art Class Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students to renew their art classes. Please answer the questions accurately and completely to ensure a smooth transition into your new class.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill out each field completely.
- Start with the student information section.
- Provide your current student ID.
- Enter your current email address to contact you.
- Enter your current phone number if you'd like us to reach you.
- Skip to the renewal date section.
- Enter the current date you would like to start your new class term.
- Continue with the current class information section.
- Enter the name of your current class.
- Select the current term you are in.
- Skip to the renewal preferences section.
- Select the art class you would like to renew.
- Enter the term or session you would like to start in.
- Determine if this is a continuation of your current class.
- Select your satisfaction level with your current class.
- Rate the quality of your instructor.
- Determine the progress you have made in your current class.
- Skip to the preferences section.
- Enter your goals for the next term.
- If you have any new areas you'd like to explore in art, enter them.
- Skip to the logistics section.
- Select your preferred payment method for the renewal.
- Confirm your renewal enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Please enter your student name accurately and completely.
* **Student ID**: Please enter your current student ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your current phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Renewal Date**: Please enter the current date you would like to start your new class term.
* **Current Class Name**: Enter the name of your current class.
* **Current Term**: Select the current term you are in.
* **Art Class to Renew**: Select the art class you would like to renew.
* **Renewal Term or Session**: Enter the term or session you would like to start in.
* **Is this a continuation of your current class**: Determine if this is a continuation of your current class.
* **Satisfaction with Current Class**: Select your satisfaction level with your current class.
* **Instructor Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of your instructor.
* **Progress Made in Class**: Determine the progress you have made in your current class.
* **Goals for Next Term**: Enter your goals for the next term.
* **Change in Artistic Interests**: If you have any new areas you'd like to explore, enter them.
* **Payment Method for Renewal**: Select your preferred payment method for the renewal.
* **Confirm Renewal Enrollment**: Confirm your renewal enrollment.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by students who are renewing their art classes. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
