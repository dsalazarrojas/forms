# Massage Retreat Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a Massage Retreat event. Please complete the form with your personal and massage preferences information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, and Phone).
2. Select your massage preferences (Massage 1, Massage 2, Massage 3).
3. Check the consent box to confirm that you have read and agree to the Massage Retreat Participation Terms and Conditions.
4. Scroll down to the Terms and Conditions section to read and agree to the specific terms and conditions for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Massage Preferences**: Select the types of massages you would like to receive during the retreat. You can choose multiple options.
* **Consent**: Check this box to confirm that you have read and agree to the Massage Retreat Participation Terms and Conditions.
* **Massage Retreat Participation Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions for the event.

Please note that the fields marked as "Terms and Conditions" are not actual fields to fill out, but rather informational fields to inform you about the terms and conditions of the event. They are here to ensure that you are aware of the rules and regulations before participating in the event.

Remember to review all the fields carefully and ask questions if you're unsure about any of the information. This will ensure that you have a complete understanding of what is being asked of you and can register confidently.

If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the event organizers for assistance.

Best of luck with your registration!
