# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Registration Form is used to collect information from guests when they check-in to the hotel. This information helps us to verify the guest's identity and ensure they have all the necessary details for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the **Reservation Date** of your stay.
2. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the text fields.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the email field.
4. Fill in your **Phone** number.
5. Select your preferred **Room Type**.
6. Enter your **Arrival Time** (if applicable).
7. Choose the **Room Number** of your stay (if available).
8. Fill in any **Special Requests** you have.
9. Select the **Arrival Date** of your stay.
10. Choose the **Guest Type** you are (Individual or Group).
11. Enter the **Number of Guests** in your party.
12. Enter the **Stay Length** of your stay.
13. Fill in your **Check In** and **Check Out** dates.
14. Fill in the **Reservation Note** (if you have any specific requests).
15. Choose your **Payment Method** (Credit Card or Cash).
16. If you are paying by credit card, enter your **Card Number**.
17. If paying by credit card, fill in the **Expiration Month** and **Expiration Year**.
18. If paying by credit card, enter the **CVV** (Card Verification Value).
19. Fill in your **Address**, **City**, and **State**.
20. Enter your **Zip** code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: This field is used to select the date of your stay.
* **First Name**: This field is used to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to enter your last name.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Room Type**: This field is used to select the type of room you prefer.
*   *One Bed* - a room with one bed
*   *Two Beds* - a room with two beds
*   *Suite* - a luxurious room with all amenities
*   *Studio* - a compact room with kitchen and living area
* **Arrival Time**: This field is used to enter your estimated arrival time (if applicable).
* **Room Number**: This field is used to enter the number of the room you are staying in.
* **Special Requests**: This field is used to enter any special requests you have.
* **Arrival Date**: This field is used to select the date you arrive at the hotel.
* **Guest Type**: This field is used to select if you are an **Individual** or a **Group**.
* **Number of Guests**: This field is used to enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Stay Length**: This field is used to enter the number of days you will be staying.
* **Check In**: This field is used to select the date you check-in to the hotel.
* **Check Out**: This field is used to select the date you check-out of the hotel.
* **Reservation Note**: This field is used to enter any specific requests or notes about your stay.
* **Payment Method**: This field is used to select if you are paying by **Credit Card** or **Cash**.
*   *Credit Card* - you will be asked to enter your card details later.
*   *Cash* - you will be paying in cash upon check-in.
* **Card Number**: This field is used to enter your credit card number (if paying by credit card).
* **Expiration Month**: This field is used to enter the expiration month of your credit card (if paying by credit card).
* **Expiration Year**: This field is used to enter the expiration year of your credit card (if paying by credit card).
* **CVV**: This field is used to enter the Card Verification Value of your credit card (if paying by credit card).
* **Address**: This field is used to enter your address.
* **City**: This field is used to enter the city where you are staying.
* **State**: This field is used to enter the state where you are staying.
* **Zip**: This field is used to enter your zip code.

Please note that some fields are optional, but are required for us to process your booking.
