# encuentro_de_las_artes_worship_culture_2013 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information for the "Encuentro de las Artes Worship Culture 2013" event registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event ID (Evento ID) in the first field.
2. Provide your full name (Nombre) in the next field.
3. Enter your surname (Apellido) in the following field.
4. Enter your birthdate (Fecha nacimiento) in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Select whether you are wearing a t-shirt (Talla de Camiseta) - yes or no.
6. Enter your email (Contacto) and phone (Teléfono) if available.
7. Select your gender (Género) from the dropdown options.
8. Add any additional comments or remarks (Comentarios).
9. If you have any event staff or assistants, enter their email (Asistente 1-10) and name (Asistente).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evento ID** (`evento_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID for this event registration.
* **Nombre** (`nombre`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Apellido** (`apellido`, `text`, required): Enter your surname.
* **Fecha nacimiento** (`fecha_nacimiento`, `date`, required): Enter your birthdate in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Talla de Camiseta** (`talla_camiseta`, `number`, required): Select whether you are wearing a t-shirt (yes or no).
* **Contacto** (`contacto`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Teléfono** (`telefono`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Género** (`genero`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender from the dropdown options.
* **Comentarios** (`comentarios`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Asistente 1** (`asistente_1`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of your first assistant.
* **Asistente 2** (`asistente_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your second assistant.
* **Asistente 3** (`asistente_3`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your third assistant.
* **Asistente 4** (`asistente_4`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your fourth assistant.
* **Asistente 5** (`asistente_5`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your fifth assistant.
* **Asistente 6** (`asistente_6`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your sixth assistant.
* **Asistente 7** (`asistente_7`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your seventh assistant.
* **Asistente 8** (`asistente_8`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your eighth assistant.
* **Asistente 9** (`asistente_9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your ninth assistant.
* **Asistente 10** (`asistente_10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your tenth assistant.
* **Asistente 11** (`asistente_11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your eleventh assistant.
