# Library Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Volunteer Registration Form is used to register volunteers for various shifts at the library. This form collects personal and schedule-related information from volunteers to ensure accurate scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address to ensure we can contact you about your schedule.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field; this is optional but helps us reach you in case of emergencies.
4. Enter the date you are available to volunteer in the "Date Available" field.
5. Choose the time slots you are available to volunteer in the "Time Available" field. Please note that time slots are 8:00-09:00, 09:00-10:00, and 10:00-11:00.
6. Select the number of hours you are able to volunteer in the "Shift Length" field, which can be 2 hours, 4 hours, or 6 hours.
7. Choose the days you are available to volunteer in the "Shift Days" field, selecting multiple days as needed.
8. Select your volunteer type from the options "Regular", "Substitute", or "Special Event".
9. Finally, enter your availability in the "Availability" field, which is the date you are available to work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your schedule.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which is optional but helps us reach you in case of emergencies.
* **Date Available**: Enter the date you are available to volunteer.
* **Time Available**: Choose the time slots you are available to volunteer.
* **Shift Hours**: Select the number of hours you are able to volunteer, which can be 2 hours, 4 hours, or 6 hours.
* **Shift Days**: Choose the days you are available to volunteer, selecting multiple days as needed.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select your volunteer type from the options "Regular", "Substitute", or "Special Event".
* **Availability**: Enter the date you are available to work.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before proceeding.
* Double-check your availability before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any conflicts or changes in your schedule, please contact us directly.
* We appreciate your help and look forward to scheduling you for a volunteer shift!
