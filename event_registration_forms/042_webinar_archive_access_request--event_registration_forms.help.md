# Webinar Archive Access Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting access to archived webinars for your organization or personal use. If you have attended or are interested in accessing a webinar that is no longer available, this form is where you should start.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details:
   1. **Requester Details** (Optional)
   2. **Intended Use** (Select the reason for your request)
   3. **Consent** (Select your intended use for the archive)
   4. **Recording Details** (Enter a brief description of the webinar or the URL of the original recording)
   5. **Name** (Your full name)
   6. **Organization** (Your organization or company name)
   7. **Role** (Your role in your organization)
   8. **Email** (Your email address)
   9. **Phone** (Your phone number)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Details**: Enter your name and any other identifying details you'd like to share.
* **Intended Use**: Select the reason you want to access this webinar archive.
	+ Option 1: Training and Professional Development
	+ Option 2: Research and Analysis
	+ Option 3: Other (Please specify in the Recording Details)
* **Consent**: Select how you plan to use the archive.
	+ Option 1: Share with colleagues and staff
	+ Option 2: Use for personal learning and development
	+ Option 3: Other (Please specify in the Recording Details)
* **Recording Details**: If you selected "Other" for Intended Use, please enter a brief description of the webinar or the URL of the original recording.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization or company name.
* **Role**: Select your role in your organization.
	+ Option 1: Employee
	+ Option 2: Contractor
	+ Option 3: Student
	+ Option 4: Other (Please specify)
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
