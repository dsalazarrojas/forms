# Creative Producer Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for creative producers who wish to apply for a fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide concrete examples for fields like **Artistic Vision** and **Work Samples**.
3. If a field is marked as optional, provide information only if it's relevant to your application.
4. Do not duplicate any information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Info
* **Applicant Info**: A brief introduction to yourself as a creative producer.
* **Contact Info**: Your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Artistic Vision**: A short statement of your artistic vision and creative goals.
* **Background**: A brief description of your background and experience in the industry.
* **Work Samples**: Attach or describe any relevant work samples that showcase your skills.
* **Experience**: A list of relevant experience in the industry.
* **Education**: Information about your educational background and any relevant training.
* **Fellowships**: Choose any relevant fellowships you have been part of, such as National Arts Foundation or Sundance Institute.

### Tips
* Make sure to proofread your application before submitting.
* Use proper formatting and spelling for your answers.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses to make your application stand out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
