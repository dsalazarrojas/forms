# medicaid-support-rally-sign-up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a Medicaid support rally. It collects basic information about the participants, including their event registration status, participation preferences, logistics needs, and contact details. This form is designed to be completed by individuals who want to join the rally and participate in Medicaid-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your participation preferences for the event by choosing "True" or "False" for the "Rally Participation" field.
4. If you have any special logistics needs, provide a detailed note in the "Logistics Needs" field.
5. Finally, review your information for accuracy and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Form (1)**: A form that gathers basic information about the participant's event registration status.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Participation Preferences (2)**: A field to indicate your interest in participating in the rally event.
	+ Select "True" if you want to participate, "False" otherwise.
* **Logistics Needs (3)**: A field to note any special logistical needs you may have.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your needs in this field.
* **Name (4)**: A field to input your name.
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email (5)**: A field to input your email address.
	+ Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone (6)**: A field to input your phone number.
	+ Enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Organization (7)**: A field to input your organization (if applicable).
	+ If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
