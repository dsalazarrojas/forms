# Global Conversation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Conversation Registration form is used to collect information from participants, including their details, time zone, preferred language, participant status, and any additional information they may want to provide. This form helps us understand the participant's preferences and needs for the event.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your **Participant Details** (Field 1) with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your **Participant Language** (Field 3) from the drop-down list (Japanese, English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Arabic, or Portuguese).
3. Provide your **Participant Topics** (Field 4) of interest.
4. Enter your **Participant Time In** (Field 5) and **Participant Time Out** (Field 6) times if applicable.
5. Select your **Time Zone** (Field 7) from the drop-down list (UTC, EST, CST, MST, PST).
6. Choose your **Participant Status** (Field 8) from the drop-down list (Confirmed, Interested, Declined, Waiting).
7. Finally, you can provide any additional **Participant Additional Info** (Field 9) you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`title`): This is the first field where you can provide your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Participant Language** (`participant_language`): Please select the language you prefer to communicate with us. This will help us better understand and communicate with you throughout the event.
* **Participant Topics** (`participant_details_topic`): List any topics or areas of interest you have for the conversation.
* **Participant Time In** (`participant_time_in`): If applicable, please provide the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Participant Time Out** (`participant_time_out`): If applicable, please provide the time you plan to leave the event.
* **Time Zone** (`participant_time_zone`): Select the time zone you are in. This will help us better schedule and coordinate with you.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`): Choose your status from the options provided (Confirmed, Interested, Declined, Waiting). This will help us understand your commitment and follow up on your interest in the event.
* **Additional Participant Info** (`participant_additional_info`): You may provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself or your participation in the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (indicated by *) to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Please use the correct format when providing time (HH:MM) and date (YYYY-MM-DD) for Fields 5 and 6.
* You can save your progress and return to the form later, and it will be automatically saved.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us at [contact email].
