# Event Sponsorship Budget Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and seek approval for event sponsorships. It provides event organizers with a structured approach to submitting their event details, including budget information and sponsorship proposals. This ensures that all necessary information is gathered and reviewed in a thorough and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Sponsorship Budget Form section with a brief overview of your event.
2. In the Event Sponsorship Budget Details section, provide detailed information about your event's budget.
3. Enter the Budget Amount to specify the total cost associated with the sponsorship.
4. Specify the Sponsor Organization and Event Name to facilitate communication and record-keeping.
5. In the Sponsorship Proposal section, provide a clear and detailed proposal outlining the benefits and value provided by the sponsor to your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsorship Budget Form** (`event_sponsorship_budget_form`, text, Optional): Enter a brief overview of your event, including a description of the event, its purpose, and any relevant context.
* **Event Sponsorship Budget Details** (`event_sponsorship_budget_details`, text, Optional): Provide detailed information about your event's budget, including costs and expenses associated with the sponsorship.
* **Budget Amount** (`budget_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the total cost associated with the sponsorship. Please note that this field should be filled out carefully, as it affects the review and approval process.
* **Sponsor Organization** (`sponsor_organization`, text, Optional): Specify the name and contact information of the sponsor organization, to facilitate communication and record-keeping.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the event being sponsored, to help identify and track the event's sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Proposal** (`sponsorship_proposal`, text, Optional): A clear and detailed proposal outlining the benefits and value provided by the sponsor to your event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all sections are filled out thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth review and approval process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your event manager or administrator for guidance.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to avoid errors and ensure that all necessary information is provided.
