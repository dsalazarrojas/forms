# dance_festival_performer_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from performers to RSVP to a dance festival event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Provide accurate information for your arrival and departure dates and times, performance details, and contact information.
* Select all the required production needs for your performance.
* Read and agree to the agreement terms.
* Enter your contact information and phone number.
* Provide any additional notes or comments that you would like to share with the festival organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to confirm whether you will be attending the festival or not.
* **Arrival Date** (`Arrival Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the festival.
* **Arrival Time** (`Arrival Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the festival.
* **Performance Length** (`Performance Length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of your performance.
* **Production Needs List** (`Production Needs List`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the production needs that you require for your performance.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, `text`, optional): Enter any special requirements that you have for your performance.
* **Agreement Terms** (`Agreement Terms`, `text`, optional): Read and agree to the terms of the festival agreement.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **RSVP Note** (`RSVP Note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments that you would like to share with the festival organizers.
* **RSVP Date** (`RSVP Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to RSVP to the festival.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVP Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to RSVP to the festival.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Performance Time** (`Performance Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your performance.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **RSVP Status Note** (`RSVP Status Note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information related to your RSVP status.
* **Production Team** (`Production Team`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the production team.
* **RSVP Confirmed** (`RSVP Confirmed`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your RSVP has been confirmed or not.
* **Confirmation Note** (`Confirmation Note`, `text`, required): Enter a confirmation note to the festival organizers.
