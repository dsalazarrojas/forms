# Commencement Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commencement Program Enrollment Form is a critical document required for the university's commencement program. This form ensures accurate and complete information about the student's academic details, ceremony preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Commencement Program Enrollment Form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your student information: Fill out the required fields with your name, student ID number, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your program type: Choose the type of commencement program you are participating in (e.g., undergraduate, graduate, doctoral).
3. Choose your ceremony date and time: Select your preferred ceremony date and time.
4. Provide academic details: Enter your college, department, degree type, and major/minor (if applicable).
5. Make ceremony preferences: Choose your preferred ceremony date, time, and regalia size.
6. Add guest information (if applicable): Provide details about your expected number of guests, VIP guests, and accessibility needs.
7. Set contact preferences: Decide on receiving email and SMS notifications.
8. Add additional information: Confirm your honors cord eligibility, special recognition, and dietary restrictions (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Your legal name for program records.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Your university-issued student ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact number.
* **Program Selection** (`program_type`, select_one, required): Select your commencement program (e.g., Undergraduate Commencement, Graduate Commencement, etc.).
* **Enrollment Term** (`enrollment_term`, select_one, required): Choose the term you are completing (e.g., Spring 2026, Summer 2026, Fall 2026, etc.).
* **College/School** (`college_school`, select_one, required): Select your academic college.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Your academic department.
* **Degree Type** (`degree_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of degree (e.g., Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, Master of Arts, etc.).
* **Major** (`major`, text, required): Your primary field of study.
* **Minor** (`minor`, text, required): Your secondary field of study (if applicable).
* **Current GPA** (`gpa`, number, required): Your current GPA.
* **Ceremony Date Preference** (`ceremony_date_preference`, date, required): Choose your preferred ceremony date.
* **Ceremony Time Preference** (`ceremony_time_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred ceremony time (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, no preference).
* **Regalia Size** (`regalia_size`, select_one, required): Choose your cap and gown size.
* **Expected Number of Guests** (`expected_guests`, number, required): Enter the number of guests you expect to attend.
* **VIP Guests** (`vip_guests`, text, required): Identify any special guests requiring accommodations.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, required): Provide any accessibility needs for you or your guests.
* **Email Notifications** (`email_notifications`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to receive ceremony updates via email.
* **SMS Notifications** (`sms_notifications`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to receive text message updates.
* **Honors Cord Eligibility** (`honors_cord_eligibility`, select_one, required): Confirm your eligibility for honors cords.
* **Special Recognition** (`special_recognition`, text, required): Note any awards or recognition to be acknowledged.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): If applicable, select any dietary restrictions for the reception (e.g., vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Diploma Type** (`diploma_type`, select_one, required): This field is currently empty in the provided YAML, so it's not included in the list.
