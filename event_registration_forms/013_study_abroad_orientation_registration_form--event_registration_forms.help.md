# Study Abroad Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect necessary information from participants for the Study Abroad Orientation Registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in your basic contact information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
2. Select your program:
	* Choose the program you are interested in from the options provided.
3. Enter your academic details:
	* **Semester**: Enter the semester you expect to study abroad.
	* **Date**: Select the date you want to register for the orientation.
4. Set up your time management:
	* **Time**: Schedule the time you plan to attend the orientation.
5. Add any additional details:
	* **Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments.
6. Select your assigned tool:
	* Choose the tool you have been assigned from the options provided.
7. Select your category:
	* Choose the category you fall under from the options provided.
8. Complete the final details:
	* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your registration.
	* **Output File**: Enter the file name for your output.
	* **Description**: Briefly describe your purpose of filling this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Your email address used for communication.
* **Phone**: Your phone number used for contact.
* **Program**: Select the program you want to register for.
* **Semester**: Enter the semester you expect to study abroad.
* **Date**: Choose the date you want to register for the orientation.
* **Time**: Schedule the time you plan to attend the orientation.
* **Note**: Add any relevant notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool you have been assigned from the options provided.
* **Category**: Choose the category you fall under from the options provided.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your registration.
* **Output File**: Enter the file name for your output.
* **Description**: Briefly describe your purpose of filling this form.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in accurate and up-to-date information for a smooth registration process.
* If you need assistance, don't hesitate to contact the support team.
* Review your completed form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
