# Music Festival Demo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists or musicians to submit their music demo files for a music festival demo upload process. The purpose of the form is to collect information about the artist, their performance, and their contact details for promotional and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your artist name in the "Enter Artist Name" field.
2. Enter your band name in the "Enter Band Name" field.
3. Provide the title of your song in the "Enter Song Title" field.
4. Share the URL of your music demo in the "Demo URL" field.
5. Attach a file related to your music demo in the "File" field.
6. If required, attach an additional file in the "Select File" field.
7. Enter your artist email address in the "Artist Email" field.
8. Enter your artist phone number in the "Artist Phone" field.
9. If required, enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Artist Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Enter Band Name**: Enter your band or group name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Enter Song Title**: Enter the title of the song you want to upload.
* **Demo URL**: Enter the URL where your music demo can be found.
* **File**: Attach a file related to your music demo.
* **Select File**: If required, attach an additional file related to your music demo.
* **Artist Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Artist Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Event Name**: If required, enter the name of the event you are participating in.
