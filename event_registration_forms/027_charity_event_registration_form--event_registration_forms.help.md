# Charity Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to register for charity events. It is essential to fill in all required fields to ensure proper registration and event arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name in the first section.
2. Enter your Email Address and Phone Number in the next section for contact information.
3. Select your Organization Name if applicable (if not, you can skip this field).
4. Choose the Event Name and Event Date for the event you're attending.
5. Select your Registration Type, specifying how many Guests you're bringing, and list the Guest Names if you're bringing any.
6. Indicate any Dietary Requirements or Accessibility Needs you might have, if any.
7. If applicable, choose a T-Shirt Size for any event-related activities.
8. Select a Payment Method for your registration and any additional Donation if you wish.
9. Finally, indicate how you heard about the event and if you're interested in volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Choose the name of the event you're attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the event.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required): Select how you wish to register (General admission, VIP, Student, Senior, Group rate, Virtual attendee).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Enter the number of attendees (including yourself).
* **Guest Names** (`guest_names`, `text`, optional): If you have guests attending, list their names separated by commas.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose any dietary restrictions you have (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut allergy, Halal, Kosher, Other).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you require any accessibility accommodations, describe them.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, choose the size of T-shirt you prefer (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, Not applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose a payment method (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, Bank transfer, Check, Invoice requested).
* **Additional Donation** (`donation_amount`, `select_one`, optional): If you wish, indicate any additional donation amount ($10, $25, $50, $100, Other amount).
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you heard about the event (Social media, Email, Website, Friend or family, Print media, Radio or TV, Other).
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): If you're interested, choose 'True', otherwise select 'False' or 'Maybe'.
* **Do you agree to terms and conditions** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes I agree' to confirm you've read and understand the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* Be specific and clear in your answers to provide the best possible experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
