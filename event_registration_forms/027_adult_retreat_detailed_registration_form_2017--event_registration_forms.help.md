# Adult Retreat Detailed Registration Form 2017 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Retreat Detailed Registration Form 2017 is a form designed to collect detailed information from adult registrants for the purpose of organizing and planning a successful retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the registrant information section, which includes the following fields:
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as it should appear on the registration documents.
	* Gender: Select your gender to help with room assignments.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* Mailing Address: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
	* Cell Phone: Enter your cell phone number.
2. If you have any specific preferences for the retreat, please select them from the options below:
	* Room Preference: Choose your preferred type of room (Single Room, Shared Room, or No Preference).
	* Roommate Request Name: If you have a specific person in mind, enter their name.
3. If you have any dietary restrictions or other medical concerns, please select them from the options below:
	* Dietary Restrictions: Select all dietary restrictions that apply (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy).
	* Other Medical Concerns: List any health issues we should know about.
4. In case of an emergency, please provide the following information:
	* Emergency Contact Name: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
	* Relationship: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
	* Emergency Phone: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
5. Finally, confirm your registration by selecting the payment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (id: 2)** (`Full Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it should appear on the registration documents. This is the primary identifier for your registration.
* **Gender (id: 3)** (`Gender`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your gender to help with room assignments. Please choose from Male, Female, or Other.
* **Date of Birth (id: 4)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This helps us keep track of your age and any age-related requirements.
* **Mailing Address (id: 5)** (`Mailing Address`, `text`, **required**): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code. This is the address we'll use for mail and communication.
* **Cell Phone (id: 6)** (`Cell Phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your cell phone number. This will help us contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Room Preference (id: 8)** (`Room Preference`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose your preferred type of room (Single Room, Shared Room, or No Preference). This will help us assign you to a suitable room.
* **Roommate Request Name (id: 9)** (`Roommate Request Name`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a specific person in mind to share a room with, enter their name. This is not required, but it will help us consider your request.
* **Dietary Restrictions (id: 10)** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all dietary restrictions that apply (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy). This will help us plan for your dietary needs.
* **Other Medical Concerns (id: 11)** (`Other Medical Concerns`, `text`, **optional**): List any health issues we should know about. This is an opportunity for you to share any medical concerns that may affect your retreat experience.
* **Emergency Contact Name (id: 13)** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the emergency contact person. This is someone who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship (id: 14)** (`Relationship`, `text`, **required**): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Emergency Phone (id: 15)** (`Emergency Phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Registration Deposit Status (id: 17)** (`Registration Deposit Status`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your payment status (Paid, Pending). This will help us track your registration status.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information to ensure a successful retreat.
