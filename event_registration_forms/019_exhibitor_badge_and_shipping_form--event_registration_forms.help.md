# Exhibitor Badge And Shipping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibitor Badge And Shipping Form is designed to collect information from exhibitors for their badge and shipping details. This form is meant to be filled out by exhibitors who need to provide shipping information for their products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your exhibitor information. Make sure to select the correct country and shipping country from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Badge Details**: This is the main section for exhibitor information. Enter your exhibitor name, company name, email, phone number, and country of origin.
* **Shipping Details**: This is the main section for shipping information. Enter the country where the products will be shipped to, shipping address line 1, shipping address line 2, shipping zip code, shipping city, and shipping state.
* **Shipping Country**: Select the country where the products will be shipped to from the options provided.
* **Name**: Enter your name as the exhibitor.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Country**: Select your country of origin from the options provided.
* **Shipping Zip Code**: Enter the zip code for shipping.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city for shipping.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state for shipping.
* **Shipping Country Code**: You do not need to fill this out as the shipping country is already selected in the "shipping_country" field.
* **Shipping Address Line 1**: Enter the shipping address line 1.
* **Shipping Address Line 2**: Enter the shipping address line 2.
