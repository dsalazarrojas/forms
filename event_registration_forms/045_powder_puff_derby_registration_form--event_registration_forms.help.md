# Powder Puff Derby Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powder Puff Derby Registration Form is used to collect information from participants and their emergency contacts. This form is required for all participants to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information:
   * Enter your name and car number.
   * Select your class (Junior, Senior, or Master Powder Puff).
   * Enter your age.
   * Choose your T-shirt size.
2. If applicable, fill in emergency contact information:
   * Enter your emergency contact's name.
   * Choose the relationship with your emergency contact.
   * Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
   * Enter your emergency contact's email.
3. Enter your team information (if you are part of a team):
   * Select your team (Team A, Team B, or Team C).
   * Enter your team manager's name.
   * Enter your team manager's phone number.
   * Enter your team manager's email.
   * Enter your team manager's confirmation code.
   * Enter notes for your team participant.
4. Enter your registration date and confirmation code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1): 
    *   **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license or ID card.
    *   **Driver Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be driving the vehicle.
    *   **Car Number**: Enter the vehicle's number (e.g., 123, ABC-123).
* **Team** (Page 2): 
    *   **Team**: Choose the team you will be part of (Team A, Team B, or Team C).
*   **Class**: Choose your class (Junior, Senior, or Master Powder Puff).
*   **Age**: Enter your age.
*   **T-Shirt Size**: Choose the size of the T-shirt you would like to order.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 3): 
    *   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
    *   **Relationship**: Choose the relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, etc.).
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
    *   **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Team Information** (Page 4): 
    *   **Team Manager Name**: Enter the name of the person managing your team.
    *   **Team Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of your team manager.
    *   **Team Manager Email**: Enter the email of your team manager.
    *   **Team Manager Confirmation Code**: Enter the confirmation code provided by your team manager.
    *   **Team Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes for your team participant.
*   **Registration** (Page 5): 
    *   **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the event.
    *   **Confirmation Code**: Enter the confirmation code you received upon registration.
