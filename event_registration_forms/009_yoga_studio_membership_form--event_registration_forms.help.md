# Yoga Studio Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new members at the Yoga Studio. The goal is to gather essential information from potential members to create a membership account and provide a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your membership plan by selecting one of the three options (Basic, Premium, or Deluxe).
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your home address.
7. Select your membership start date.
8. Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
9. Provide any additional payment information (e.g., card number, bank transfer details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose Membership Plan**: Select one of the three options to determine the level of access and benefits you want to enjoy as a member. 
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where we can send updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Address**: Enter your home address for billing and payment purposes.
* **Membership Start Date**: Select the date you wish to start your membership. Please note that this will not affect your account creation date.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you prefer to pay for your membership (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash). 
* **Payment Information**: Enter any necessary details for your chosen payment method (e.g., card number for Credit Card).
