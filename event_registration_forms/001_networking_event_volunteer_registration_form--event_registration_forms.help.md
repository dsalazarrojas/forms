# networking_event_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a volunteer for a networking event. It helps us understand your interests and availability to best match you with event opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and complete information.
2. Answer each field truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Submit the form once you've finished filling out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`title`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the event or the type of event you'd like to volunteer for. This will help us get a better understanding of the event's context and relevance to your interests. 
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
### Volunteer Info
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience, skills, or any other information relevant to your ability to volunteer. 

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your answers to help us best match you with volunteer opportunities.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact us directly.
- You're welcome to update your information if your interests or availability change.
