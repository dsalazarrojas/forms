# Sports Analytics Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for sports analytics training sessions. It helps collect essential information about each participant, including their name, contact details, and training specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field. This is the name of the person registering for the training session.
2. Enter your **Organization Name** in the "Organization Name" field. This is the name of the organization or team you are representing.
3. Provide your **Participant Email** in the "Participant Email" field. This should be a valid email address associated with your organization or personal account.
4. Enter your **Participant Phone** number in the "Participant Phone" field. This will help us contact you for any further information or updates.
5. Choose your **Training Date** by selecting the date you plan to attend the sports analytics training session.
6. Select your preferred **Training Time** slot from the provided options.
7. Enter the location where you'll be attending the training session in the "Training Location" field.
8. If you have any additional information or notes about the participant, you can add them in the "Participant Notes" field.
9. If your organization has any notes about the participant, you can add them in the "Organization Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: A required field to identify the person registering for the training session. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization Name**: An optional field to specify the organization or team you are representing. This helps us understand the context and provide better support.
* **Participant Email**: A required field for us to contact you and send updates about the training session. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Participant Phone**: An optional field to provide an additional contact method. This will help us reach out to you if needed.
* **Training Date**: A required field to schedule the training session accordingly. Choose a date that works best for you.
* **Training Time**: A required field to accommodate your schedule. Select a time slot that suits you.
* **Training Location**: An optional field to specify the location where you'll be attending the training session. Enter the exact address or venue name.
* **Participant Notes**: An optional field for any additional information or comments about the participant.
* **Organization Notes**: An optional field for any additional information or comments from your organization.
* **Participant Phone**: A field to add any phone number associated with the participant. This will be used for internal contact and follow-up purposes only.
