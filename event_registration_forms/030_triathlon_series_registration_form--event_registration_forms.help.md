# Triathlon Series Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register athletes for the Triathlon Series event. Please fill in the required fields to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
Before you start, make sure to have all the required information ready. The form has several sections:

* Athlete Details: This section collects your personal information.
* Event Choices: Select one or more events you are interested in participating in.
* Waivers: Sign the waivers to agree to our terms and conditions.
* Athlete Info: Enter your athlete info.
* Emergency Contact: Enter your emergency contact person's information.
* Event Info: Enter event information.
* Athlete Info (2): Enter your athlete info.
* Event Distance: Enter the distance of the event.
* Athlete Name: Enter your name.
* Athlete Notes: Add any additional notes about yourself.
* Waivers (2): Sign the additional waivers to agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Details** (`athlete_details`, text, required): This section collects your personal information, including your height, weight, and other relevant details.
* **Event Choices** (`event_choices`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more events you are interested in participating in. The options are Triathlon Sprint, Triathlon Half, and Triathlon Full.
* **Waivers** (`waivers`, text, required): Sign the waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Athlete Info** (`athlete_info`, text, required): Enter your athlete info.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's information.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Height** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight.
* **Height Unit** (`height_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of your height (e.g., meters, feet, etc.).
* **Weight Unit** (`weight_unit`, text, required): Select the unit of your weight (e.g., kilograms, pounds, etc.).
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Waiver 1** (`waiver1`, text, required): Sign the waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 2** (`waiver2`, text, required): Sign the additional waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 3** (`waiver3`, text, required): Sign the waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 4** (`waiver4`, text, required): Sign the additional waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Event Distance** (`event_distance`, text, required): Enter the distance of the event.
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Athlete Notes** (`athlete_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Waiver 5** (`waiver5`, text, optional): Sign the waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 6** (`waiver6`, text, optional): Sign the waiver to agree to our terms and conditions.

Please note that some fields are required while others are optional, so make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
