# Worship Event Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store information about a worship event. It is intended to be used by internal staff or organizers to manage event details and attendee data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event title and description on the "Welcome Page" section.
2. Provide the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" sections, respectively.
3. Enter the speaker's information in the "Speaker Info" section.
4. Enter the contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" sections.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes or feedback in the "Notes and Feedback" section.
6. Select "I agree to terms and conditions" or "I do not agree to terms and conditions" from the "Submit" dropdown menu.
7. Optionally, add any comments about your experience using the form in the "Submit Comment" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Page** (`welcome_page`, text, required: false): Enter a brief welcome message or description for the event.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start and end time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Speaker Info** (`speaker_info`, text, required: false): Enter the speaker's name, title, and bio.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email address for the event.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number for the event.
* **Notes and Feedback** (`notes_and_feedback`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or feedback about the event.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you agree or disagree with the terms and conditions.
* **Submit Comment** (`submit_comment`, select_multiple, required: false): Optionally, provide feedback about your experience using the form.

Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
