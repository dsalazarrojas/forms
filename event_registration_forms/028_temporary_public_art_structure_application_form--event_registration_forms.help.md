# Temporary Public Art Structure Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information for the creation and execution of temporary public art structures. It includes fields for artist and organization details, event specifics, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the desired date for the application.
2.  Enter the artist's name and email address.
3.  Provide any additional contact information for the artist.
4.  Enter a statement from the artist explaining their work and intentions.
5.  If applicable, enter the name and any additional information for any organization involved in the event.
6.  Enter any additional statement or information about the artist or event.
7.  Identify the person responsible for contact and their phone number.
8.  Enter the date and location for the event.
9.  Enter the names of any additional artists involved in the event.
