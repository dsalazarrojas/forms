# Smoky Mountains Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide their information to receive updates and relevant details about the Smoky Mountains giveaway event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information by filling out the User Information page.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Address, Address 2 (if applicable), City, State, Zip, and Country in the corresponding fields.
6. Select your Country of residence from the Country dropdown menu.
7. Enter the Date and Time you are available for the giveaway event.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
9. Confirm your Phone number in the Confirm Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you'd like to share with the organization.
* **First Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`name_last`, text, not required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your street address here.
* **Address 2** (`address_2`, text, not required): Enter your apartment or suite number, or any additional address information here (if applicable).
* **City** (`city`, text, not required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, not required): Select your state of residence from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, not required): Enter your zip code here.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, not required): Select your country of residence from the dropdown menu.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date you are available for the giveaway event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time you are available for the giveaway event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments here.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, not required): Re-enter your phone number to confirm.
