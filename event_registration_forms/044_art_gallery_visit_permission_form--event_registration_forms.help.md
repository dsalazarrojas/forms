# Art Gallery Visit Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who will be visiting the art gallery. The information collected will be used for planning and coordination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the student's full name and the guardian's name.
2. Provide the student's emergency contact number.
3. Choose a visit date for the art gallery visit.
4. Indicate whether or not the student has given consent for medical treatment in case of an emergency.
5. List any known allergies or medical conditions for the student.
6. Specify the pickup arrangements for the student after the visit.
7. Enter the name of the student's school.
8. Provide the teacher's name and contact number.
9. Finally, type the guardian's full name to signify consent for the student's visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Guardian name** (`guardian_name`, required): Enter the student's parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the student's emergency contact number.
* **Visit date** (`visit_date`, required): Choose a date for the art gallery visit.
* **Consent for medical treatment** (`consent_medical`, required): Check if the student has given consent for medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **Allergies or medical conditions** (`allergies`, optional): List any known allergies or medical conditions for the student.
* **Pickup arrangements** (`pickup_arrangements`, required): Specify who will pick up the student after the visit.
* **School name** (`school_name`, required): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Teacher contact** (`teacher_contact`, required): Enter the teacher's name and contact number.
* **Guardian signature** (`signature`, required): Type the guardian's full name to signify consent for the student's visit.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration or the teacher in charge of the visit.
