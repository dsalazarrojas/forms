# Adventure Tour Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Tour Subscription Form is designed to collect information from potential subscribers who are interested in staying updated on adventure tour opportunities. By filling out this form, you will be able to choose your preferred adventure types, experience level, trip duration, budget range, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your basic contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred adventure types, such as mountain climbing or water sports.
3. Select your experience level, preferred trip duration, and budget range.
4. Specify who you typically travel with and how often you would like to receive updates.
5. Check the subscription agreement box to agree to receive updates and offers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for updates and offers.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number for any urgent communication.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, required): Select the age range that best describes you.
* **Location** (`location`, required): Enter your city, state or country where you are located.
* **Preferred Adventure Types** (`preferred_adventure_types`, required): Choose the types of adventures that interest you most, such as mountain climbing or cultural tours.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, required): Select your experience level with adventure activities, from beginner to expert.
* **Preferred Trip Duration** (`preferred_trip_duration`, required): Choose the length of your preferred adventure trips, from weekend getaways to long expeditions.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, required): Select your typical budget range for adventure tours.
* **Travel Companions** (`travel_companions`, required): Specify who you typically travel with, such as solo or with a partner.
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, required): Choose how often you would like to receive updates, from weekly to quarterly.
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (`preferred_communication_channel`, required): Select how you would like to receive updates, either by email, SMS, or both.
* **Special Interests** (`special_interests`, required): Choose any special interests you have for your adventures, such as photography or wildlife observation.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): Mention any dietary restrictions you have, if any.
* **Medical Considerations** (`medical_considerations`, optional): Mention any medical considerations you have for adventure travel.
* **Subscription Agreement** (`subscription_agreement`, required): Agree to receive updates and offers from adventure tour organizers.
