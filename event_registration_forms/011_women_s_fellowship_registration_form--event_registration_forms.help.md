# Women's Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from female participants who want to be part of a women's fellowship program. The program provides a supportive community for women to network, learn, and grow together.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the required fields to ensure you are able to join the fellowship program.
2. Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
3. Choose the correct role that best describes your position or status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Data** (`registration_form_data`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect any additional registration information or comments. You can type up to 255 characters.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as you would like to be known in the fellowship.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is your email address that will be used for official communication and updates. Please make sure to use a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This is your phone number that will be used for communication and event reminders. Please format your number as a 10-digit number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of the organization.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose the role that best describes your position or status:
	+ Option 1: Community Leader
	+ Option 2: Member
	+ Option 3: Guest
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): This is the date you would like to register for the fellowship. Please use the date format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check all your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification.
* The program directors will review and process your registration information in a timely manner.
