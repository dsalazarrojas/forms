# Workshop Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for a workshop, including name, contact details, and event preferences. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field (format: example@example.com).
3. Provide your Phone Number in the Phone Number field (format: XXX-XXXX).
4. Select your preferred Event from the Event Preference dropdown menu.
5. In the Participant Information field, enter any additional details relevant to your workshop participation.
6. Indicate whether you have Event Details (Yes/No) and Participant Details (Yes/No) as required.
7. Provide any Additional Info in the Additional Info field.
8. Confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form correctly by selecting 'Yes' for the Confirm field.
9. Finally, submit the form by selecting 'Yes' for the Submit field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text, required)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Last Name (text, required)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
* **Email (email, required)**: Format your Email address as example@example.com.
* **Phone Number (text, required)**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXXX.
* **Event Preference (select_one, required)**: Select the event you wish to attend.
* **Participant Information (text, required)**: Enter any additional details relevant to your workshop participation.
* **Event Details (select_multiple, required)**: Indicate whether you have event details or not.
* **Participant Details (select_multiple, required)**: Indicate whether you have participant details or not.
* **Additional Info (text, required)**: Enter any further relevant details.
* **Confirm (select_one, required)**: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have reviewed and submitted the form correctly.
* **Submit (select_one, required)**: Select 'Yes' to submit the form for processing.
* **Error Message (text, optional)**: This field will display any error messages if the form is not completed correctly.
* **Submit (select_one, required)**: Select 'Yes' to submit the form for processing.
