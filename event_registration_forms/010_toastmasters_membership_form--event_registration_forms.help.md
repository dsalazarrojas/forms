# Toastmasters Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Toastmasters Membership Form is a tool used to register for events or membership within the Toastmasters organization. This form is intended for existing or potential members to provide necessary information for event registration or membership management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
3. Provide your phone number for additional contact and event communication.
4. Select your preferred club (you can choose only one club).
5. Indicate your interests within the selected club.
6. Provide the date of the event you are registering for (in the format of MM/DD/YYYY).
7. Select the time of the event you are registering for.
8. Add any additional notes or comments related to your event registration.
9. If you are already a member, enter the name of the club you are currently a part of.
10. If you are a member, provide the year and month you have been a member of Toastmasters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for additional contact and event communication.
* **Club preferences**: Select only one club that you are interested in joining.
*   • **Club a**: Select this option if you prefer club a.
*   • **Club b**: Select this option if you prefer club b.
*   • **Club c**: Select this option if you prefer club c.
* **Club interests**: Select all interests that apply within the selected club.
*   • **Club d**: Select this option if you are interested in club d.
*   • **Club e**: Select this option if you are interested in club e.
*   • **Club f**: Select this option if you are interested in club f.
* **Event date**: Enter the date of the event you are registering for (in the format of MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event time**: Select the time of the event you are registering for.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments related to your event registration.
* **Club name**: If you are already a member, enter the name of the club you are currently a part of.
* **Member since**: If you are a member, enter the year and month you have been a member of Toastmasters.
	+ **Member since year**: Enter the year you have been a member of Toastmasters.
	+ **Member since month**: Enter the month you have been a member of Toastmasters.
* **Event start date**: This field is not relevant and should be left blank.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Ensure that your email address and phone number are up-to-date.
* If you are registering for an event, ensure that you have the correct event date and time.
* If you are a member, ensure that you have the correct member since year and month.
