# Mental Health Awareness Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Awareness Program Admission Form is a registration form used by individuals or their representatives to enroll in the program. This form is necessary for participants to gain access to program resources and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you meet the eligibility criteria for the Mental Health Awareness Program.
2. Fill in your First Name on the first page.
3. Fill in your Last Name on the second page.
4. Provide your Email Address on the third page.
5. Enter your Phone Number on the fourth page.
6. Select the correct response for the Consent Form.
7. Fill in the First Name of your Emergency Contact on the fifth page.
8. Fill in the Last Name of your Emergency Contact on the sixth page.
9. Choose the relationship type for your Emergency Contact from the options provided on the seventh page.
10. Enter the phone number of your Emergency Contact on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Please fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Provide a valid email address that can be used for communication related to the program.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Consent Form (select_one)**: Please choose the option that accurately reflects your understanding of the program terms. If you have not read and do not understand the terms, please do not select this option.
* **Emergency Contact First Name (text)**: If you have an emergency contact, please fill in their first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name (text)**: Fill in the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (select_multiple)**: Select the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Friend, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the terms of the program before selecting the Consent Form option.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for support.
* This form should only be completed once; if you have already registered, please do not attempt to register again.
