# Youth Volunteering Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students for the Youth Volunteering Fair event, where they can explore volunteering opportunities, learn new skills, and interact with like-minded peers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form accurately and honestly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter your school name where you attend or represent.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **School ID** (`student_school_id`, number, optional): Enter your school ID number if you have one.
* **Grade Level** (`student_school_class`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level. Please choose from:
	+ 9
	+ 10
	+ 11
	+ 12
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Interests** (`student_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the interests that apply to you:
	+ Interest in arts
	+ Interest in sports
	+ Interest in technology
* **Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your home address for organizational purposes.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* In case of an error or issue, please contact the internal event administrator.
* If you have any questions, you can reach out to us at [contact email].

Note: This form will be reviewed and approved internally to ensure the accuracy and relevance of the submitted data. Please be assured that your information is collected only for the purpose of facilitating the Youth Volunteering Fair event.
