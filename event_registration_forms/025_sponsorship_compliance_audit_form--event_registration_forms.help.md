# Sponsorship Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sponsorship Compliance Audit Form is used to track and verify the compliance status of sponsorships for events and meetings. This form helps to identify potential issues and areas for improvement to ensure that events are conducted in accordance with company policies and regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the event registration forms data.
2. Provide details about the event registration forms.
3. Select whether or not there are sponsorships involved in the event.
4. If sponsorships are involved, select the specific details about the sponsorships.
5. Enter any additional event details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Forms Data**: This field is used to collect information about the event registration forms. Please enter the relevant details about these forms, such as the name, date, and location of the event.
* **Event Registration Forms Details**: This field is used to gather more specific information about the event registration forms. Please provide any relevant details about the forms, such as how they were distributed or collected.
* **Sponsorships**: This field allows you to indicate whether or not there are sponsorships involved in the event. Select "Yes" if sponsorships are present, and "No" if they are not.
* **Sponsorship Details**: If you selected "Yes" for sponsorships, this field will ask you to specify the details about the sponsorships. Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not the sponsorships comply with company policies and regulations.
* **Event Details**: This field is used to provide any additional information about the event that may be relevant to the compliance audit. Please enter any details that are not captured in the previous fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or the compliance team for assistance.
* Complete the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to ensure a comprehensive compliance audit.
