# HR Collaboration Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from attendees for an HR Collaboration Networking Event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
* Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a *).
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Details

* **Event Details**: This field is a text field where you can enter a brief description of the event, including its title, date, time, and location.

### Attendee Details

* **Attendee Details**: This field is a text field where you can enter your details, such as your name, email address, and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Interests and Consents

* **Interests and Consents**: This field is a text field where you can indicate your interests and consents related to the event. Please be specific and clear about your interests and any consents you wish to grant or decline.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the event organizers for clarification.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
