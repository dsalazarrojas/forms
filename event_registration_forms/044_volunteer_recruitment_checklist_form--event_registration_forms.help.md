# Volunteer Recruitment Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers to facilitate the volunteer recruitment and management process. The form is not a substitute for a comprehensive employment application form, and the organization's decision to accept a volunteer will still be based on the individual's suitability for the volunteer role and the organization's needs at the time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter your current address in the "Address" field.
* Select any relevant skills from the list provided in the "Skills" field.
* Provide any relevant references in the "References" field.
* Confirm your consent to undergo a background check by checking the "Consent to background check" field.
* Confirm your consent to volunteer work by checking the "Consent to volunteer work" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address, including street number and postal code.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant skills you have, such as leadership, communication, or teamwork.
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide any relevant references, such as previous volunteer work or professional experience.
* **Consent to background check** (`consent_to_background_check`, text, optional): Check this box if you agree to undergo a background check as part of the volunteer application process.
* **Consent to volunteer work** (`consent_to_volunteer_work`, text, optional): Check this box if you agree to volunteer with the organization and understand the responsibilities and expectations associated with volunteering.
 
## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and efficient volunteer recruitment and management process.
