# Property Investment Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the Property Investment Summit. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your attendance is properly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Select the type of real estate interest you are looking to invest in (e.g., Apartment, Land, Industrial, Office, Retail, or Other).
6. Choose your investment focus (e.g., Cash, Equity, Hybrid, or Other).
7. Specify your preferred investment amount (in numeric value).
8. Select the date you would like to attend the summit (in date format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A required field to collect your first name.
* **Last Name**: A required field to collect your last name.
* **Email**: A field to collect your email address, which will be used to contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: An optional field to collect your phone number.
* **What type of real estate interest are you looking to invest in?**: Select one or more interest areas from the available options (e.g., Apartment, Land, Industrial, Office, Retail, or Other).
* **What is your investment focus?**: Choose one option that best describes your investment focus (e.g., Cash, Equity, Hybrid, or Other).
* **How much is your preferred investment amount?**: Enter a numeric value indicating your preferred investment amount.
* **When would you like to attend?**: Select a date for your preferred attendance date.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid incomplete submissions.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
