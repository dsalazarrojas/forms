# Ethical Sourcing Awareness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Sourcing Awareness Forum Registration Form is designed to collect personal and professional contact information from interested parties. This data will be used to manage forum registrations and may also be used for marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
3. **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your professional contact information.
4. **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
5. **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it is known in the market.
6. **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it is listed on your official identification documents or your company's website.
7. **Job Function**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your job function relates to sourcing or procurement.
8. **Resources**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your company has resources related to sourcing or procurement.
9. **Date**: (Optional) Enter the date you plan to participate in the forum.
10. **Time**: (Optional) Enter the time you plan to participate in the forum.
11. **Location**: Enter your company's location or the location where you will be participating in the forum.
12. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
13. **Goals**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are interested in the forum's goals.
14. **Company Website**: Enter your company's website.
15. **Company Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of company you represent.
16. **Company Industry**: Enter your company's industry.
17. **Company Location**: Enter your company's location.
18. **Company Size**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your company's size.
19. **Company Sector**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the sector your company operates in.
20. **Job Function**: Enter your job function.
21. **Company Website**: Enter your company's website.
22. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
