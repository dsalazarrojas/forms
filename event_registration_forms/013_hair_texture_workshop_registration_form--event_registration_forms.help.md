# Hair Texture Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for salon owners and managers to register their participants for the Hair Texture Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information of the participant.
2. Make sure to select the correct workshop scheduling (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
3. Enter the salon name, address, phone number and website.
4. Provide a participant agreement and workshop agreement.
5. Specify the workshop duration, max participants, price and workshop end date.
6. Make sure to provide participant email address, contact number, and additional info.
7. Optionally, add any participant notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your salon where the workshop will be held.
* **Participant Name** (2, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, required): Enter the participant's contact number.
* **Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Time** (6, time, required): Select the time of the workshop scheduling.
* **Participant Email** (7, email, required): Enter the participant's email address for communication purposes.
* **Consent** (8, select_multiple, required): Select whether or not you agree to the terms.
* **Salon Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of your salon where the workshop will be held.
* **Salon Phone** (12, text, required): Enter the contact number of your salon.
* **Salon Website** (13, text, optional): Enter the website of your salon.
* **Participant Agreement** (14, text, required): Enter an agreement for the participant.
* **Workshop Agreement** (15, text, required): Enter an agreement for the workshop.
* **Workshop Scheduling** (16, select_one, required): Select the workshop scheduling (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Salon Notes** (17, note, optional): Add any notes about your salon.
* **Workshop Notes** (18, note, optional): Add any notes about the workshop.
* **Participant Additional Info** (19, text, required): Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Workshop Start Date** (20, date, optional): Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (21, date, required): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (22, number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Max Participants** (23, number, required): Enter the maximum number of participants allowed.
* **Price** (24, number, required): Enter the price of the workshop.
* **Notes** (25, note, optional): Add any notes about the workshop.
