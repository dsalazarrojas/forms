# Sweet 16 Celebration Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sweet 16 Celebration Survey is designed to gather information from guests about their preferences and needs for a special event. This includes their guest preferences (dancing, food, music, games, etc.), dietary information, attendance details, and contact information for the host.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with a * (asterisk). These include:
	- Event Guest Info
	- Party Preferences
	- Guest Diet
	- Email
	- Phone Number
	- Event Date
	- Event Time
	- Guest Attendance
	- Host Name
	- Host Relation
	- Host Phone
	- Host Email
	- Event Info
	- Comments 2
	- Submit
2. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.
3. Once you've completed all required fields, you can leave the non-required fields (marked as **repeat** in the Field-by-Field Explanation section) empty or fill them out if you want.
4. After filling out the form, click on the "Submit" button to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Guest Info**: This field is required to know who you are attending the event with.
* **Party Preferences**: This field allows you to select up to 2 preferences for the event, e.g., "Dance", "Food", etc.
* **Guest Diet**: Select one of the options to let us know about your dietary requirements.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as a backup contact method.
* **Event Date**: This field is used to determine which date you will be attending the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time you will be attending the event.
* **Guest Attendance**: Select "Attend" if you will attend, "Not Attend" if you won't.
* **Guest Attendance Notes**: Leave any additional comments about your attendance.
* **Host Name**: Enter the name of the person hosting the event.
* **Host Relation**: Describe your relation with the host.
* **Host Phone**: Enter the host's phone number.
* **Host Email**: Enter the host's email.
* **Event Info**: Provide any additional information about the event.
* **Comments 2**: Add any additional comments about the event.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed all required fields, click this button to submit your survey.
