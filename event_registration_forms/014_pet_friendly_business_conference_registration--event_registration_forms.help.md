# Pet Friendly Business Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Friendly Business Conference Registration form is meant to gather information from business owners and professionals interested in attending the conference. This form helps us to know more about our attendees' preferences and needs, and tailor the conference experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, simply navigate through the form pages.
2. Enter your desired session interests in the "Session Interests" field on page 2.
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a successful submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title of the Pet Friendly Business Conference Registration** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title or name for your registration. While this field is optional, it's helpful to identify your application.
* **Pet Info** (`pet_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about your pet, such as their species, age, or size. If you have more than one pet, you can mention them too.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, `text`, required/optional): List the topics or sessions you're interested in attending. This will help us assign you to relevant sessions and ensure you get the most out of the conference.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your submission.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
