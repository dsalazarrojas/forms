# Back To School Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for Back To School Night. Please fill out this form to provide information about yourself and your family to help us prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields** before submitting the form. Required fields are marked with a star (*) symbol.
2. Please provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at the email address provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent name (1)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Student name (2)**: Please enter your child's full name.
* **Attending status (3)**: Choose whether you will attend Back To School Night. Options are:
	+ True: Yes, I will attend.
	+ False: No, I will not attend.
	+ Maybe: Maybe, I am unsure.
* **Number of attendees (4)**: Enter the number of adults attending with your student.
* **Sessions of interest (5)**: Select the sessions you plan to attend. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Childcare needed (6)**: Indicate if you need childcare during the event.
	+ True: Yes, I need childcare.
	+ False: No, I do not need childcare.
* **Accessibility needs (7)**: If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Contact email (8)**: Enter a valid email address for event updates.
* **RSVP date (9)**: Enter the date of your response.
* **Additional comments (10)**: If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them.

## Tips

* Please note that we will send reminders and updates to the email address provided.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask us before submitting the form.
* If you need to make any changes to your registration, please contact us at the email address provided.
