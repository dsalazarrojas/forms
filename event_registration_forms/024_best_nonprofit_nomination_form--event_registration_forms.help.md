# Best Nonprofit Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Nonprofit Nomination Form is a tool designed to facilitate a fair and comprehensive evaluation of nonprofit organizations for a Best Nonprofit Award. The form collects essential information about the nonprofit's profile, goals, achievements, and impact, allowing the award committee to assess eligibility and potential.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Provide accurate and relevant information about the nominator's profile.
2.  Enter the nonprofit's details, including name, website, and founding year.
3.  Rate the nonprofit's performance in specific areas (transparency, effectiveness, community impact, volunteer engagement, and others).
4.  Describe recent achievements and their impact on the community.
5.  Provide a professional reference contact and date of nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`nominator_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information to ensure you can be reached for further information or questions.
*   **Your Organization or Affiliation** (`nominator_organization`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with the nonprofit, provide your organization or affiliation name.
*   **Your Contact Email** (`nominator_email_addr`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure the award committee can reach you for further information or questions.
*   **Nonprofit Name** (`nonprofit_name_text`, `text`, required): Enter the official name of the nonprofit organization.
*   **Nonprofit Website** (`nonprofit_website_url`, `text`, required): Provide the URL of the nonprofit's official website for more information.
*   **Mission Statement** (`nonprofit_mission_statement`, `text`, required): Describe the brief purpose and core values of the nonprofit organization.
*   **Primary Sector** (`nonprofit_sector_choice`, `select_one`, required): Choose the main area of focus or sector that the nonprofit primarily operates in.
*   **Year Established** (`year_nonprofit_established`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the nonprofit was founded.
*   **Performance Evaluation** (`evaluation_metrics_header`, `note`, optional): This section is a placeholder for rating areas of performance.
*   **Transparency and Accountability** (`transparency_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the nonprofit's level of transparency and accountability from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Effectiveness of Programs** (`program_effectiveness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the nonprofit's effectiveness of programs from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Community Impact** (`community_impact_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the nonprofit's community impact from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Volunteer Engagement** (`volunteer_engagement_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of volunteer participation and support from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
*   **Describe a specific project or achievement from the last year** (`recent_achievement_desc`, `text`, required): Share details about a major milestone or achievement from the last year.
*   **How has this nonprofit changed lives or the community?** (`change_impact_narrative`, `text`, required): Provide a brief story or statement about the nonprofit's impact on the community.
*   **Why should this nonprofit win the Best Nonprofit Award?** (`award_justification_text`, `text`, required): Summarize your nomination and reasoning for selecting this nonprofit.
*   **Professional Reference** (`nonprofit_reference_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and email of someone familiar with the nonprofit's work.
*   **Date of Nomination** (`nomination_submission_timestamp`, `date`, required): Enter the date of nomination for record-keeping purposes.
