# Digital Team Building Challenge Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Team Building Challenge Registration form is designed to help event organizers register their challenges and provide essential information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Organizer" field with your name and contact information.
2. Select the type of team members participating in the challenge (Team or Other).
3. Choose the type of challenge (Virtual Escape Room, Virtual Scavenger Hunt, or Virtual Game Show).
4. Select a preferred date for the challenge (if applicable).
5. Choose a start and end time for the challenge (if applicable).
6. Select a challenge goal (Teamwork, Communication, or Time Management).
7. Enter the size of your team.
8. Enter the name of the team lead who will be in charge of the challenge.
9. Enter the phone number of the team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): The name and contact information of the event organizer.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, false): The type of team members participating in the challenge (Team or Other).
* **Challenge Type** (`challenge_type`, select_one, false): The type of digital challenge (Virtual Escape Room, Virtual Scavenger Hunt, or Virtual Game Show).
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, false): The preferred date for the challenge (if applicable).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, false): The start time for the challenge (if applicable).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, false): The end time for the challenge (if applicable).
* **Challenge Goal** (`challenge_goal`, select_one, false): The goal of the challenge (Teamwork, Communication, or Time Management).
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, false): The size of the team.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, true): The name of the team lead who will be in charge of the challenge.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, false): The phone number of the team lead.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Event Organizer and Team Lead).
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your event coordinator.
* Double-check your team size to ensure it's accurate.
* Ensure the team lead's phone number is entered correctly.
