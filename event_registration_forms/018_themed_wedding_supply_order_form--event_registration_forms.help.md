# Themed Wedding Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Themed Wedding Supply Order Form is designed to collect information about the wedding supplies needed for a specific wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of wedding supplies you need (e.g., Wedding Decor, Wedding Favors, etc.).
2. Enter the wedding date (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
4. Estimated budget for the supplies.
5. Specify the wedding location.
6. Provide any special instructions for the supplies.
7. Choose the contact person's name and role (e.g., Bride, Groom, etc.).
8. Enter the contact person's email.
9. Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding supplies**: Choose the type of wedding supplies you need to order (e.g., Wedding Decor, Wedding Favors, etc.).
* **Wedding date**: Enter the date of the wedding (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Estimated guests**: Estimate the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Budget**: Enter your estimated budget for the supplies.
* **Wedding location**: Specify the location of the wedding.
* **Special instructions**: Provide any special instructions or requests for the supplies.
* **Contact name**: Choose the person responsible for ordering the supplies (e.g., Bride, Groom, etc.).
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
