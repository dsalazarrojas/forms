# employee-training-program-attendance-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the attendees of an employee training program. This includes details about the employee who is attending the program, the training session they are attending, and the leader of the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Training Program Attendant** (`employee_training_program_attendee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who is attending the program.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session the employee is attending.
* **Training Session Time** (`training_session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session the employee is attending.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, text, required): Enter the topic of the training session.
* **Employee Attendee Name** (`employee_attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your own name as the attendee.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_attendee_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the attendee.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the attendee.
* **Training Session Leader** (`training_session_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training session leader.
* **Training Session Leader** (`training_session_leader_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the training session leader.
* **Training Session Leader** (`training_session_leader_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the training session leader.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the format specified for each field (e.g., date in dd/mm/yyyy format for training session date).
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
