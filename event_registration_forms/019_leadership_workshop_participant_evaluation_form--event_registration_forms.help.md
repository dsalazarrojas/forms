# Leadership Workshop Participant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from participants who have attended the leadership workshop. Your input will help us improve future workshops and make sure we're meeting your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Select the date you attended the workshop in the "evaluation_form" field.
6. Share any feedback or comments you have about the workshop in the "feedback" field.
7. Rate your overall experience with the workshop by selecting one of the options in the "rating" field.
8. Indicate whether you attended the workshop by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "workshop_attendance" field.
9. If you'd like to add any additional comments, do so in the "additional_comments" field.
10. Finally, enter your name as the participant in the "participant_name" field.
11. If applicable, enter your organization in the "participant_organization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **last_name** (text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **email** (email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **evaluation_form** (date, Optional): Select the date you attended the workshop.
* **feedback** (text, Optional): Share any comments or suggestions about the workshop.
* **rating** (select one, Optional): Rate your overall experience (Good or Fair).
* **workshop_attendance** (select multiple, Optional): Indicate whether you attended the workshop (Yes or No).
* **additional_comments** (note, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the workshop.
* **participant_name** (text, Optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **participant_organization** (text, Optional): Enter your organization, if applicable.
