# Charity Gala Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register for the charity gala event. It will ask for your contact information, preferences for the gala, and other relevant details to ensure a seamless experience for you and your guests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Enter your details accurately, as they will be used to process your registration and any further interactions.
2. **Review each field**: Make sure you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out all the fields, click "Submit" to send your registration.
