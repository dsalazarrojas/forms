# Networking Session Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for internal staff to register and apply for a networking session. It is used to collect necessary information from the applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Email, Job Title, Company, Location, and Phone.
2. Select your preferred session time.
3. Provide any additional comments or details you'd like to share with the session organizers.
4. If you have any specific session category or subcategory preferences, select them from the provided options.
5. If you'd like to assign this session to a specific person, select them from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to input your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to input your last name.
* **Email**: An email field to input your contact email.
* **Job Title**: A text field to input your job title.
* **Company**: A text field to input your company name.
* **Location**: A text field to input your location.
* **Session Date**: A date field to input the date of the session you'd like to attend.
* **Session Time**: A time field to input the time you'd like to attend the session.
* **Preferred Session Time**: A select-one field with options for your preferred session time.
* **Phone**: A text field to input your phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: A note field for you to add any additional comments or details.
* **Session ID**: A text field for internal use only.
* **Category**: A text field for you to input your preferred category.
* **Subcategory**: A text field for you to input your preferred subcategory.
* **Status**: A select-multiple field to select multiple status options.
* **Created By**: A text field for internal use only.
* **Created At**: A date field for internal use only.
* **Updated By**: A text field for internal use only.
* **Updated At**: A date field for internal use only.
* **Deleted At**: A date field for internal use only.
* **Deleted By**: A text field for internal use only.
* **Notes**: A note field for internal use only.
* **Comments**: A note field for internal use only.
* **Created By User**: A text field for internal use only.
* **Assigned To**: A select-one field for internal use only.
