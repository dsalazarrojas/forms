# Environmental Awareness Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Awareness Workshop Registration Form is a form used to collect information from users interested in attending an environmental awareness workshop. This form is designed to be completed by individuals who wish to attend the workshop and provide their contact and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "user_info" field.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select your desired date of attendance in the "date" field.
6. Choose your preferred time of attendance in the "time" field.
7. Provide any additional event information in the "event_info" field.
8. Finally, click the "submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info**: This field is used to capture your user information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details you would like to share with us.
* **contact_info**: This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as address or any other relevant details.
* **email**: This field requires a valid email address to ensure we can contact you with important workshop details.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **date**: Choose a date for the workshop you would like to attend. This field is used to determine the date of your workshop attendance.
* **time**: Select a time for the workshop you would like to attend. This field is used in conjunction with the date field to confirm your attendance.
* **event_info**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional event information, such as any specific interests or needs you may have.
* **submit**: Click this button to complete and submit your registration form.
