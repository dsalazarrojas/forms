# Barrel Racing Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from riders to register for the Barrel Racing Event. It collects personal and horse-related details, as well as preferences for event divisions, racing options, and other essential information to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including Rider Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Mailing Address.
2. Provide information about your horse, such as Horse Name, Breed, and Registration Number (if applicable).
3. Choose the correct Age Division based on your age.
4. Select the Number of Race Slots you want to participate in.
5. Indicate your Event Division and Payment Method.
6. Confirm your association membership (if applicable) and provide Proof of Insurance.
7. Enter your Date of Birth, Coggins Test Date, and Veterinary Health Certificate (if applicable).
8. Choose whether you need a stall or camping accommodations.
9. Indicate your willingness to volunteer and sign the Liability Waiver and Medical Authorization forms.
10. Provide emergency contact information, including name and phone number.
11. Make any additional comments or remarks as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for event communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your complete street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Rider Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age Division**: Select the correct division based on your age: 
  • Youth - under 12
  • Junior - 12 to 17
  • Adult - 18 to 39
  • Senior - 40 and over
* **Horse Name**: Enter your horse's registered name.
* **Horse Breed**: Enter your horse's breed.
* **Horse Registration Number**: Enter your horse's registration number (if registered).
* **Horse Color**: Select your horse's primary color:
  • Bay
  • Chestnut
  • Black
  • Brown
  • Palomino
  • Buckskin
  • Gray
  • Roan
  • Paint
  • Other (please specify)
* **Event Division**: Select which division you want to participate in:
  • Open
  • Amateur
  • Youth
  • Senior
  • Rookie
* **Number of Race Slots**: Select the number of racing slots you want:
  • 1 slot
  • 2 slots
  • 3 slots
  • Unlimited - all day
* **Association Membership Number**: Enter your association membership number (if applicable).
* **Proof of Insurance**: Indicate if you have current equine insurance:
  • Yes, attached
  • Yes, on file
  • False
* **Coggins Test Date**: Enter the date of your horse's Coggins test in the MM/DD/YYYY format, within 12 months of the event date.
* **Veterinary Health Certificate**: Indicate if you have a current veterinary health certificate:
  • True
  • False
* **Do You Need a Stall**: Indicate if you need stall accommodations:
  • Yes, 1 stall
  • Yes, 2 stalls
  • No, trailering
* **Camping Accommodations**: Indicate if you need camping accommodations:
  • Yes, with hookups
  • Yes, no hookups
  • False
* **Willing to Volunteer**: Indicate if you're willing to help with event operations:
  • True
  • False
* **Liability Waiver**: Sign to acknowledge understanding of the event risks:
  • I agree and sign
  • I do not agree
* **Medical Authorization**: Sign to authorize emergency medical treatment:
  • I authorize
  • I do not authorize
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'll pay entry fees:
  • Credit card
  • Debit card
  • Check
  • Cash at event
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or remarks about your registration.

Note: This guide assumes the reader has access to the form's fields and options. Ensure the form is filled out accurately and completely to avoid any potential issues during the registration process.
