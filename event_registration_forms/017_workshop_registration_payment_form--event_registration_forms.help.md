# Workshop Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration and payment information for workshops. Please fill out the form with your details to register for a workshop and complete the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the amount you would like to pay in the "Amount" field.
6. Choose the date and time of the workshop you would like to attend in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Note" field.
9. Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "Accept" field.
10. Read and agree to the terms and conditions, payment terms, terms of service, and privacy policy by entering the text in the respective fields.
11. Finally, enter any promo code, coupon, or referral information if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Select how you would like to pay for the workshop.
	+ Card
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Amount** (Number, Required): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Choose the time of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them here.
* **Accept** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms and Conditions** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Terms of Service** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Privacy Policy** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Terms of Use** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the terms of use.
* **Referral** (Text, Optional): If you have any referral information, you can enter it here.
* **Promo Code** (Text, Optional): If you have a promo code, you can enter it here.
* **Coupon** (Text, Optional): If you have a coupon, you can enter it here.
* **Refund Policy** (Text, Optional): Read and review the refund policy.
* **Payment Terms and Conditions** (Text, Required): Read and agree to the payment terms and conditions.
* **Venue** (Text, Optional): Enter the venue of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Instructor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the workshop instructor (if applicable).
* **Event Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Event Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Accept** (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions, payment terms, terms of service, and privacy policy.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled out before submitting the form.
