# Ocean Series Competitor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants to participate in an event that is part of the Ocean Series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: The name of the event the participant is registering for.
  * This field is required and must be a unique name.
  * Example: Ocean Series Event 1
* **Team Name**: The name of the team the participant is a part of.
  * This field is required and must be a unique name.
  * Example: Team A
* **Event Date**: The date of the event the participant is registering for.
  * This field is required and must be a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  * Example: 01/01/2023
* **Participant Name**: The name of the person registering.
  * This field is required and must be a valid name.
  * Example: John Doe
* **Participant Email**: The contact email address of the participant.
  * This field is required and must be a valid email address.
  * Example: johndoe@example.com
* **Participant Phone**: The contact phone number of the participant.
  * This field is optional and must be a valid phone number.
  * Example: 123-456-7890
* **Waiver Acceptance**: A legal agreement that the participant must read and agree to before participating in the event.
  * This field is required and must be selected as "I have read and agree to our waiver" to proceed.
  * Example: I have read and agree to our waiver
* **Participant Address**: The street address of the participant.
  * This field is required and must be a valid address.
  * Example: 123 Main St.
* **Participant City**: The city the participant is located in.
  * This field is required and must be a valid city name.
  * Example: Anytown, USA
* **Participant State**: The state the participant is located in.
  * This field is required and must be a valid state name.
  * Example: CA
* **Participant Zip**: The zip code of the participant's address.
  * This field is required and must be a valid zip code.
  * Example: 12345
