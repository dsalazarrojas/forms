# marketingConferenceFeedbackSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from conference attendees to help us improve future marketing conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your experience at the conference.
2. For each session, select the quality rating that best describes your experience.
3. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions about the conference.
4. Enter your email address for us to contact you, if needed.
5. Enter your phone number and time of feedback, if you want to be contacted.
6. Add any comments about your experience at the conference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **conferenceFeedback** (Note): This is a text note field where you can write any comments or feedback about the conference.
* **SessionQuality** (Select Multiple): Choose the session(s) that best described your experience:
	+ Session 1
	+ Session 2
	+ Session 3
* **speakerQuality** (Select One): Rate the quality of each speaker:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
* **feedback** (Text): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions about the conference.
* **suggestion** (Text): Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in future conferences.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address for us to contact you, if needed.
* **phoneNumber** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **date** (Date): Enter the date you attended the conference.
* **time** (Time): Enter the time you attended the conference.
* **comments** (Text): Add any additional comments about your experience at the conference.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the conference.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate analysis.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
