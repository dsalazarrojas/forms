# Population Health Data Science Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the Population Health Data Science Workshop. It is essential to provide accurate and unique data for each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Zip Code
	* Workshop Date
	* Time
	* Category
	* Confirm Email
	* Confirm Phone
	* Confirm Zip Code
	* Confirm Workshop Date
	* Confirm Time
	* Confirm Category
	* Confirm Select Multiple
	* Confirm Note
	* Confirm Additional Comments
	* First Name 2 (if you want to register another attendee)
	* Last Name 2 (if you want to register another attendee)
	* Email 2 (if you want to register another attendee)
	* Phone 2 (if you want to register another attendee)
2. Select the correct options for:
	* Category
	* Select Multiple
	* Confirm Category
	* Confirm Select Multiple
3. Enter any additional comments in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (not required).
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date of the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the workshop.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select one of the available categories.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options (not required).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`note_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, text, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmed_2`, select_one, required): Confirm your email address by selecting one of the options.
* **Phone Confirmed** (`phone_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm your phone number by selecting one of the options.
* **Zip Code Confirmed** (`zip_code_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm your zip code by selecting one of the options.
* **Workshop Date Confirmed** (`workshop_date_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm the workshop date by selecting one of the options.
* **Time Confirmed** (`time_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm the workshop time by selecting one of the options.
* **Category Confirmed** (`category_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm the category by selecting one of the options.
* **Select Multiple Confirmed** (`select_multiple_confirmed`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the selected multiple options.
* **Note Confirmed** (`note_confirmed`, select_one, required): Confirm the note by selecting one of the options.
* **Additional Comments Confirmed** (`note_confirmed_2`, select_one, required): Confirm the additional comments by selecting one of the options.
* **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, text, required): Enter the first name of the second attendee (if registering multiple attendees).
* **Last Name 2** (`last_name_2`, text, required): Enter the last name of the second attendee (if registering multiple attendees).
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required): Enter the email address of the second attendee (if registering multiple attendees).
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the second attendee (if registering multiple attendees).

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you are registering multiple attendees, fill in the additional fields for each attendee accordingly.
- Confirm each field to ensure accuracy and prevent errors.
