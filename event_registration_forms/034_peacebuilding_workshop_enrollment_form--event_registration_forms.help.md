# peacebuilding-workshop-enrollment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the peacebuilding workshop enrollment form. The form is designed to gather information from participants to facilitate their enrollment in the workshop. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the workshop type field, selecting one or more workshops that interest you.
2. Enter your participant name in the participant name field.
3. Enter your participant email address in the participant email field.
4. Enter your participant phone number in the participant phone field.
5. If applicable, enter your date of birth and time of birth in the date of birth and time of birth fields.
6. Provide your address in the address line 1 and 2 fields.
7. Enter your zip code.
8. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Enrollment Form** (`workshop_enrollment_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your personal email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more workshops that interest you. Options include:
	+ Workshop 1
	+ Workshop 2
	+ Workshop 3
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the workshop organizers.
* If you are enrolling on behalf of someone else, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
