# Plant Safety Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Plant Safety Technology Fair, an event designed for professionals and companies to showcase and learn about the latest advancements in plant safety technology. This form collects information from attendees to facilitate their registration and participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read the instructions carefully**: Before starting to fill out the form, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. **Fill out the form**: Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. **Submit your registration**: Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to register for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide a brief registration message or any additional information you would like to share with the event organizers.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name, title, company, and any other relevant information about yourself as an attendee.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Specify any specific preferences or requests you have for the event, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility requirements.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional):
	+ Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
