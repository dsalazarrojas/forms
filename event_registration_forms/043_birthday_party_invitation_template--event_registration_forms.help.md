# Birthday Party Invitation Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help hosts create a comprehensive birthday party invitation template to gather information from guests about the party details, their preferences, and RSVP responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction Section**: Start by filling out the basic party details, such as the birthday person's name, the date and time of the party, and the location.
2. **Party Details Section**: Provide more details about the party, including the party theme, party duration, and any special requests or instructions.
3. **Guest Information Section**: Share your name, email address, phone number, and the names of any additional guests you're bringing.
4. **RSVP Section**: Choose your RSVP response and indicate if you'll be attending or not. If you're attending, specify the number of guests in your party.
5. **Dietary Needs and Preferences Section**: Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have, and provide more details about your specific requirements.
6. **Transportation and Mobility Section**: Indicate if you need transportation help or have any mobility or accessibility needs.
7. **Activity Preferences Section**: Choose any activities you're interested in or would like to participate in.
8. **Gift Information Section**: Provide any gift registry information or the birthday person's gift preferences.
9. **Additional Notes Section**: Add any special requests or questions you have for the host.
10. **Confirmation Section**: Choose if you'd like a confirmation email sent to you after completing the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when providing details about your preferences and needs.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests or questions, make sure to include them in the special requests section.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the host for clarification.
