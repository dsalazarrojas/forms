# Volunteer Experience Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Experience Booking Form is designed for volunteers to book their experience for an event. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your contact information and availability for the event, which will help event organizers understand your interest in volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first_name field with your first name.
2. Fill out the last_name field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the email field. This is the email address that event organizers will use to contact you.
4. Select a date and time that you are available to volunteer on the date field.
5. Choose a time slot that you are available to volunteer on the time field.
6. Select your relevant skills from the skills field. This is a checkbox list of available skills, and selecting any of the options indicates that you have experience with that skill.
7. Indicate your availability for the event on the availability field. This is also a checkbox list, and selecting an option indicates that you are available at that time.
8. If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<name>: text, required: false)**: Your first name.
* **Last Name (<name>: text, required: false)**: Your last name.
* **Email (<name>: email, required: false)**: The email address that event organizers will use to contact you.
* **Date (<name>: date, required: false)**: The date that you are available to volunteer.
* **Time (<name>: time, required: false)**: The time that you are available to volunteer.
* **Skills (<name>: select_multiple, required: false)**: Select any of the available skills that you have experience with.
* **Availability (<name>: select_multiple, required: false)**: Indicate your availability for the event. Select "Yes" for any time slot you are available.
* **Message (<name>: note, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in all fields to ensure that event organizers can contact you successfully.
- Only fill out this form if you are interested in volunteering for the event.
- Event organizers will use the information provided in this form to match you with available volunteer opportunities.
