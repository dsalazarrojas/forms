# yoga_instructor_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to walk through the process of completing the yoga instructor application form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** fields with your real name.
2. Enter a valid **Email** address that you have access to.
3. Provide your **Contact Number** for easy communication.
4. Enter the name of the **Studio Name** where you are interested in working.
5. If applicable, mention your **Experience** in years.
6. List your **Qualifications** such as certifications or specializations.
7. Select your **Availability** in 24-hour format (e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
8. Choose your **Preferred Shift** such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
9. Select your preferred **Preferred Days** of work (Yes or No).
10. Provide the name of your **Emergency Contact Name**.
11. Select the relation of your emergency contact such as mother, father, sister, brother, or other.
12. Enter your **Emergency Contact Phone** number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Studio Name** (`studio_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the studio where you are interested in working.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): If applicable, mention your experience in years.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, required): List your qualifications such as certifications or specializations.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, required): Select your availability in 24-hour format.
* **Preferred Shift** (`preferred_shift`, text, required): Choose your preferred shift such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Preferred Days** (`preferred_days`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred days of work (Yes or No).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, select_one, required): Select the relation of your emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
