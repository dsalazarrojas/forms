# School Trip Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Authorization Form is a document used to collect information from school administrators to facilitate the process of authorizing student trips. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are gathered correctly to avoid confusion or duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are an authorized school administrator.
2. Review the form to understand the required fields.
3. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
4. Save the form for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is a basic description of the trip, including the student name, date, and class/year.
- **Student Info** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number for easy tracking.
- **Guardian Info** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for the student's guardian, including their name, phone number, and email.
- **Emergency Contact** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact information, including the contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the student.
- **Medical Info** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Record any medical conditions or allergies relevant to the student's health.
- **Medical Notes** (`6`, `text`, required: false): If the student has any special medical needs, describe the necessary care or equipment required.
- **Student Conduct** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Rate the student's conduct level, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Student Participation** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all that apply for the activities the student will be participating in.
- **Trip Details** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Describe the trip's purpose, destination, and duration.
- **Teacher Info** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Provide the teacher's name, contact information, and any additional notes.
- **School Info** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Record the school name and any other relevant details.
- **Parent Consent** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Obtain parental consent for the trip by recording the parent's name and signature.
