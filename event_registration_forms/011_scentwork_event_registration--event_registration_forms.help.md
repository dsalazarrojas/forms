## Scentwork Event Registration - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To register for a Scentwork event, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the correct **Registration Type**: Choose whether you're registering for an Event Trial, Class Trial, or Trial Series.
2. Fill in your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, and **Participant Phone** for contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Trial Date**, **Trial Start Date**, and **Trial End Date** for event details.
4. Select the **Trial Type**, which includes options like Agility, Jumping Trial, Tracking Trial, and Scent Work.
5. Choose the **Class Level** you belong to, which may be Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Novice, or Expert.
6. If your class level is "Other," provide a brief explanation in the **Class Level Other** field.
7. Confirm your **Entry Fee Paid** status and apply any applicable **Entry Fee Discount**.
8. Select the **Registration Status** as Pending, Approved, or Denied.
