# feature_artist_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from artists who wish to feature their work in a showcase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Artist Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Artist Email** and **Artist Phone** for contact purposes.
3. Add a brief **Artist Description** that describes your work and art style.
4. Share your **Artist Experience**, highlighting any relevant experience or training you have in your craft.
5. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields (Artist Name, Artist Email, and Artist Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents, such as ID cards or passports.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Artist Description** (`artist_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your art style, medium, or any other relevant information about your work.
* **Artist Experience** (`artist_experience`, text, optional): Share your past experience, training, or certifications related to your art, such as workshops, classes, or exhibitions.
