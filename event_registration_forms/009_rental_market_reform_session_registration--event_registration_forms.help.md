# Rental Market Reform Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help session attendees register their details and provide feedback on their attendance. It's a simple and straightforward way to collect information from attendees and ensure a smooth experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill out the form completely, as incomplete submissions may cause issues with session organization.
3. Be sure to submit only one form per attendee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`name`, text, required/optional): This field asks for the name of the attendee. Please provide your full name as it appears on your registration or identification documents.
* **Session Schedule** (`schedule`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide any schedules or time slots you'd like to attend during the session. Please be as specific as possible with your availability.
* **Registries** (`registry`, text, required/optional): In this field, you can list any pre-existing registries you may have that are relevant to the session topic. This will help the session organizers to better understand your interests and provide a more personalized experience.
