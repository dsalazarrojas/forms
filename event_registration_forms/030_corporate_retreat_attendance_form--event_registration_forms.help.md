# corporate_retreat_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for corporate retreat attendance registration, which is a crucial part of corporate events planning. The form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees, such as contact and travel details, corporate information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your basic user information on page 1, including any required fields.
2. On page 2, provide your contact information, including your corporate email, corporate phone, and corporate fax (if applicable).
3. On page 3, fill out your travel details, including your travel method, flight number, and arrival date.
4. On page 4, provide your accommodation details, including your accommodation type and meeting room number.
5. On page 5, fill out any additional comments or questions you may have.
6. Finally, review your entire form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Method** (Select One):
  + Choose your method of travel (Airplane, Train, Bus).
* **Flight Number** (`number`):
  + Enter your flight number (required).
* **Meeting Room** (`number`):
  + Enter the number of the meeting room you will be staying in (required).
* **Comments** (`text`):
  + Provide any additional comments or questions you may have (required).
* **Corporate Email** (`email`):
  + Enter the corporate email address associated with your company (required).
* **Corporate Phone** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate phone number (required).
* **Corporate Fax** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate fax number (if applicable).
* **Corporate Website** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate website (if applicable).
* **Corporate Representative** (`text`):
  + Enter the name of the corporate representative (if applicable).
* **Corporate Address** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate address (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`text`):
  + Provide any additional notes or information you may have (if applicable).
* **Corporate ID** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate ID (if applicable).
* **Corporate Name** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate name (if applicable).
* **Corporate Phone Number** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate phone number (if applicable).
* **Corporate Department** (`text`):
  + Enter the corporate department (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your travel details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant department or person responsible for your corporate events.
