# RFA VendorExhibitor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFA VendorExhibitor Application Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect crucial information from vendors and exhibitors for our events. This form will help us understand your business, products, and services, and ensure a smooth and successful event experience for both you and our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Sign up Form** (Page 1).
2. Enter your **Vendor Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number**, and **Address** (Page 1).
3. Provide a **Description** of your business, products, or services (Page 2).
4. Select the **Event Date** and **Event Time** (Page 2).
5. Choose the **Event Venue** and **Event Type** (Page 3).
6. Indicate the **Event Status** (Page 3).
7. Choose your **Vendor Category** and **Event Venue Type** (Page 4).
8. Select your **Vendor Type** and **Vendor Status** (Page 4).
9. Select your proficiency in the **Vendor English Language**, **Vendor Other Language(s)**, and **Vendor Cultural Background** (Page 5).
10. Choose the **Vendor Services** and **Vendor Products** offered (Page 5).
11. Enter the **Vendor Pricing** and **Vendor Presentation** information (Page 5).
12. Upload a **Vendor Image** or provide a URL (Page 6).
13. Enter any other information you deem relevant in the **Vendor Other** field (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign up Form** (`sign_up_form`, Text, Optional): This field is not currently used, so you can skip it.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter your business or company name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Not Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Not Required): Enter your business or physical address.
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Not Required): Briefly describe your business, products, or services.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Not Required): Select the date of the event you are applying for.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, Not Required): Select the time of the event you are applying for.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name or location of the event venue.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, Text, Not Required): Select the type of event you are applying for.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, Select One, Not Required): Select the status of the event you are applying for (Active or Inactive).
* **Vendor Category** (`vendor_category`, Text, Not Required): Choose a category that best describes your business or products.
* **Event Venue Type** (`event_venue_type`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select whether you have an event venue or not.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, Select One, Not Required): Choose whether you are a vendor or a service provider.
* **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, Select One, Not Required): Select the status of your business (Active or Inactive).
* **Vendor English Language** (`vendor_english_language`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select the languages you are proficient in.
* **Vendor Other Language(s)** (`vendor_other_language`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select any other languages you can communicate in.
* **Vendor Cultural Background** (`vendor_cultural_background`, Text, Not Required): Briefly describe your cultural background.
* **Vendor Services** (`vendor_services`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the services you offer.
* **Vendor Products** (`vendor_products`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the products you offer.
* **Vendor Pricing** (`vendor_pricing`, Number, Not Required): Enter your pricing information.
* **Vendor Presentation** (`vendor_presentation`, Text, Not Required): Enter your presentation information.
* **Vendor Image** (`vendor_image`, Text, Not Required): Upload or provide a URL for a suitable image.
* **Vendor Other** (`vendor_other`, Text, Not Required): Enter any other information you think is relevant.
