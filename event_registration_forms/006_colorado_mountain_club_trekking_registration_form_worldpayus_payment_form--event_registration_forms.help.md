# Colorado Mountain Club Trekking Registration Form WorldPayUS Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for the Colorado Mountain Club Trekking event participant's registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and complete mailing address.
2. Provide your emergency contact's information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
3. Select your preferred trekking event and date.
4. Specify your hiking experience level.
5. List any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
6. If necessary, select the equipment you need to rent for the trek.
7. Review and agree to the liability waiver and medical release terms.
8. Select your payment method and enter the required card number.
9. Provide any special accommodations you may need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name (id: 1)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (id: 2)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Email Address (id: 3)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for registration and update notifications.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Mailing Address (id: 5)** (`address`, text, required): Enter your complete street address.
* **Emergency Contact Information (id: 6)** (`section_header_emergency_contact`, note, required): This is a header for the emergency contact section.
* **Emergency Contact Name (id: 7)** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (id: 8)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship to Participant (id: 9)** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how the emergency contact is related to you.
* **Trek Choice (id: 10)** (`trek_choice`, select_one, required): Select your preferred trekking event.
* **Preferred Trek Date (id: 11)** (`trek_date`, date, required): Select your preferred trek date.
* **Hiking Experience Level (id: 12)** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your hiking experience level.
* **Medical Conditions (id: 13)** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Equipment Rental Needed (id: 14)** (`equipment_rental`, select_one, required): Select if you need to rent trekking equipment.
* **-- Emergency Contact Information -- (id: 15)** (`section_header_emergency_contact`, note, required): This is a header for the emergency contact section.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement (id: 16)** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Review and agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Medical Release Authorization (id: 17)** (`medical_release`, select_one, required): Authorize emergency medical treatment if needed.
* **Registration Fee (id: 18)** (`registration_fee`, number, required): The fee amount for the selected trek.
* **Payment Method (id: 19)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method via WorldPay US.
* **Card Number (id: 20)** (`card_number`, text, required): Enter your card number for payment.
* **Special Accommodations (id: 21)** (`special_accommodations`, text, required): Enter any special needs or accommodations.

Note: Please review the form carefully and fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Colorado Mountain Club Trekking team.
