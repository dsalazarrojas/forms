# launch_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The launch_registration form is designed to collect basic information from users regarding an event registration for internal purposes, such as event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the "Start Date" field.
3. Specify the time of the event in the "Start Time" field.
4. Enter the location of the event in the "Location" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Add any additional information or comments in the "Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your full name.
* **Start Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location of the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Any additional information or comments about the event registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the date is in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* Please enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* This form is intended for internal use only, so please do not share your registration information with anyone outside the organization.
