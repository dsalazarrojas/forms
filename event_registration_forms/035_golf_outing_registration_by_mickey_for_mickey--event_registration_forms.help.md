# Golf Outing Registration By Mickey For Mickey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a golf outing event. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Participant Name" and "Participant Email" fields.
2. If you are part of a team, select the correct team size and enter the names and contact information for each team member in the "Team Size", "Team Members", "Team Member Names", "Team Member Emails", and "Team Member Phones" fields.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant or team in the "Participant Notes" field.
4. If the participant is not part of a team, please select "No" in the "Team Size" field.
5. If the participant is part of a team, please enter "Yes" in the "Team Size" field.
6. Fill in the event date, start and finish times, location, and organizer information in the "Event Date", "Event Start Time", "Event Finish Time", "Event Location", and "Event Organizer" fields.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant in the "Participant Notes 2" field.
