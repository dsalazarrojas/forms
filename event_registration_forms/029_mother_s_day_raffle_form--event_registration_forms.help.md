# Mother's Day Raffle Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Mother's Day Raffle event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Enter your contact phone number, if you have one.
3. Provide your address for reference.
4. Select the raffle items you would like to participate in.
5. Enter your zip code.
6. Provide your Date of Birth (month, day, and year).
7. Indicate your participant status.
8. Enter any additional information about your participant status reason.
9. List your occupation.
10. Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
11. Add any notes about yourself.
12. If you submitted this form, enter the name of the person who submitted it.
13. Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
14. Enter the date and time you submitted this form (again, for timestamping purposes).
15. Enter your IP address (for tracking purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participants Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Participants Address** (text, optional): Provide your address for reference.
* **Raffle Items** (select_multiple, optional): Select the raffle items you would like to participate in.
* **Participants Zip** (number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Participants Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participants DOB Month** (select_one, optional): Select the month of your birth.
* **Participants DOB Day** (select_one, optional): Select the day of your birth.
* **Participants DOB Year** (select_one, optional): Select the year of your birth.
* **Participants Loyalty** (number, optional): Enter a number indicating your loyalty to the event or organization.
* **Participants Status** (select_one, optional): Select your participant status.
* **Participants Status Reason** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your participant status reason.
* **Participants Occupation** (text, optional): Enter your occupation.
* **Participants Company** (text, optional): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Participants Notes** (text, optional): Add any notes about yourself.
* **Participants Form Submitted By** (text, optional): If you submitted this form, enter the name of the person who submitted it.
* **Participants Form Submitted Date** (date, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Participants Form Submitted Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Participants Form Submitted At** (date, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this form (again, for timestamping purposes).
* **Participants Form Submitted By IP** (text, optional): Enter your IP address (for tracking purposes).
