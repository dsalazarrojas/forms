# Stage Hand Crew Addition Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Hand Crew Addition Request Form is designed to help event organizers and stage managers add crew members to their team for a specific event. This form collects essential information about the crew members, such as their name, contact details, and role, to facilitate communication and organization of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the event organizer, if applicable.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Select the role that best describes your position within the crew.
4. Enter the size of the crew you are planning to bring to the event, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, not required): This field is used to enter the name of the event organizer, if you are representing someone else. If you are an event organizer, you can leave this field blank.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as it appears on official documents or your business cards.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the event details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding the event details.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose the role that best describes your position within the crew. This will help us understand your specific responsibilities and expectations for the event.
	+ Stagehand: This role is for individuals who are directly responsible for stage operations, such as stagehands, stagehands assistants, or stage managers.
	+ Production Assistant: This role is for individuals who assist with production tasks, such as set dressing, props, or rigging.
	+ Sound Technician: This role is for individuals who are responsible for sound equipment and operations.
	+ Stage Manager: This role is for individuals who oversee the stage crew and coordinate tasks.
	+ Event Coordinator: This role is for individuals who are responsible for planning and coordinating event logistics.
	+ Volunteer: This role is for individuals who are contributing their time to help with the event without compensation.
* **Crew Size** (`crew_size`, number, not required): If you are planning to bring a group of crew members, please enter the number of individuals in your crew.
