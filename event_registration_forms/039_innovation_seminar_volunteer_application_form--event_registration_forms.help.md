# Innovation Seminar Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Seminar Volunteer Application Form is designed to allow individuals to register themselves as volunteers for an upcoming Innovation Seminar. This form helps us understand your interests, availability, and role preferences for the seminar, ensuring we can best match your skills and needs with available opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the first name, last name, and email fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
3. In the Date of Birth section, select the day, month, and year you were born using the drop-down menus.
4. Describe your interest in the seminar in the What is your interest in the seminar? field.
5. Choose your preferred volunteer role from the Volunteer Role field. You can also provide more details in the Other field if needed.
6. Select your availability for the seminar in the Availability field. You can also provide more details in the Other field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your birth day, month, and year using the drop-down menus.
* **What is your interest in the seminar?**: Describe your interest in the seminar, e.g., why you want to attend, what you hope to achieve, or what you expect from the event.
* **Volunteer Role**: Choose a role that matches your skills and preferences:
	+ **Volunteer**: Assist with general event tasks, such as registration, setup, and cleanup.
	+ **Speaker**: Present on a specific topic related to the seminar.
	+ **Both**: A combination of Volunteer and Speaker roles.
* **Volunteer Role (Other)**: Provide more details about your chosen role or suggest an alternative role.
* **Availability**: Select the times of the day when you are available to volunteer:
	+ **Morning**: 08:00-12:00.
	+ **Afternoon**: 12:00-18:00.
	+ **Evening**: 18:00-20:00.
* **Availability (Other)**: Provide more details about your availability or suggest an alternative schedule.
* **Availability (Other Details)**: Add any additional information about your availability, such as your work hours or other commitments.
