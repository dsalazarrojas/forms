# Garden Club Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Garden Club Meeting Attendance Form is used to register attendees for various events organized by the Garden Club. This form helps to collect information about the attendees' details and their meeting attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first name field with your first name.
2. Fill out the last name field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Indicate your meeting attendance status in the Meeting Attendance field.
6. Select the events you attended from the list of options.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
