# Kayaking Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to register for a kayaking event. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a safe and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the event name where you will be participating.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
4. Declare any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
5. Disclose any medical devices you use (e.g., pacemaker, glasses, etc.).
6. Provide instructions for your emergency contact to follow in case of an accident.
7. Sign to confirm you agree to participate in the event.
8. If the participant is a minor, sign to confirm the parent's consent.
9. Declare your age and date of birth.
10. Provide your address and zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, required): Enter the name of the event where you will be participating.
  - This information helps us identify the event you are registering for.
  - Make sure to spell it correctly for accurate tracking.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, required): Enter your name.
  - This is your personal identifier for the event.
  - Use the name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
  - This is your emergency contact number in case of an accident.
- **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, required for minors): If you are a minor, sign to confirm your parent's consent.
  - This ensures the parent's approval for your participation.
- **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, required): Enter your age.
  - This is necessary for risk assessment and emergency procedures.
- **Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth.
  - This is essential for age verification and medical records.
- **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, optional): Declare your gender.
  - This information can be helpful for emergency procedures.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
  - This is the person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
  - This is the number to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, optional): Declare any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
  - This information helps us understand any potential risks.
- **Medical Devices** (`participant_medical_devices`, optional): Disclose any medical devices you use.
  - This information helps us prepare for emergencies.
- **Participant Emergency Instructions** (`participant_emergency_instructions`, required): Provide instructions for your emergency contact to follow in case of an accident.
  - This ensures they know what to do in case of an emergency.
- **Participant Agree** (`participant_agree`, required): Sign to confirm you agree to participate in the event.
  - This is a necessary step to ensure you understand the risks involved.
- **Event Agree** (`event_agree`, required): Sign to confirm you agree to the event rules and guidelines.
  - This is a necessary step to ensure you understand the event's expectations.
- **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
  - This is for organizational purposes only.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, required): Sign this form to confirm your participation.
  - This is a necessary step to ensure we have your consent.
- **Participant Date** (`participant_date`, required): Enter the current date.
  - This helps us track your participation.
- **Participant Time** (`participant_time`, required): Enter the current time.
  - This helps us track your participation.
- **Participant Annotate** (`participant_annotate`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
  - This is for your personal records.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, required): Enter the event time.
  - This helps us track the event schedule.
- **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, required): Enter your address.
  - This is your physical location.
- **Participant City** (`participant_city`, required): Enter your city.
  - This helps us understand your location.
- **Participant State** (`participant_state`, required): Enter your state.
  - This helps us understand your location.
- **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, required): Enter your zip code.
  - This helps us track your location.
- **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, optional): Enter your email address.
  - This is for future communication purposes.

Please complete the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for all participants. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
