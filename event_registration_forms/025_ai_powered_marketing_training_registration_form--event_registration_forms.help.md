# AI Powered Marketing Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for an AI-powered marketing training program. It collects essential information to provide a personalized experience during the training event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Full Name**, which should include both your first and last name.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** for confirmation and communication purposes.
3.  Specify your **Job Role**, which helps us understand your background and interests.
4.  Choose your preferred **Session** from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, or Full day intensive).
5.  If you have any **Dietary Requirements**, please mention them in the provided field.
6.  If you require any **Accessibility Needs**, describe your requirements in this field.
7.  Choose your **Payment Preference** by selecting one of the provided options (Invoice, Payment link, or No payment required).
8.  Provide any **Additional Comments or Requests**, if you have any specific requirements or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (id: 2)
*   **Full Name**: A field to input your first and last name. This information helps us tailor the training experience to your background and needs.
### Organization (id: 3)
*   **Organization**: Enter your company or affiliation name to help us understand your professional context.
### Email Address (id: 4)
*   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for receiving confirmation and updates about the training event.
### Job Role (id: 5)
*   **Job Role**: Specify your marketing-related job role (Marketing, product, analyst, etc.) to better understand your training needs.
### Preferred Session (id: 6)
*   **Preferred Session**: Choose your preferred session type from the provided options (Morning, Afternoon, or Full-day Intensive). This helps us schedule your training session efficiently.
### Dietary Requirements (id: 7)
*   **Dietary Requirements**: Mention any dietary restrictions or requirements you may have.
### Accessibility Needs (id: 8)
*   **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accommodations or requirements, describe them here.
### Will You Require an Invoice or Payment Link (id: 9)
*   **Will You Require an Invoice or Payment Link**: Choose how you would like to receive payment information (Invoice, Payment Link, or No payment required).
### Additional Comments or Requests (id: 10)
*   **Additional Comments or Requests**: Provide any additional information or special requests you may have for the training event.
