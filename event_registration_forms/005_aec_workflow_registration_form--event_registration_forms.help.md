# AEC Workflow Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registering participants in events related to AEC workflow. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure your registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form carefully before filling it out to ensure you have all required information.
*   Fill out the form in one session for the best experience.
*   Use the provided hints for guidance on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant full name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it will be displayed in event records and on badges.
*   **Firm or company name** (`firm_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your firm or organization.
*   **Contact email address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used to confirm your registration.
*   **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide a phone number for event notifications, if desired.
*   **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Indicate your role within your firm, if applicable.
*   **Licensure state** (`licensure_state`, select_one, optional): If you hold professional licenses, select the states where they're valid. If not applicable, select "Not applicable."
*   **Areas of interest** (`areas_of_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Choose topics you'd like to cover during the event.
*   **Types of projects** (`project_types`, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of projects you work on.
*   **Attendance type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred attendance mode (in-person or virtual).
*   **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide a name and phone number for emergency contact information.
*   **Special requirements or accommodations** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Share any accessibility or dietary needs that we should be aware of.

## Tips

*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints for guidance.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   If you need help or have questions, contact us for assistance.
