# Talent Search Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Search Registration Form is used to collect information about artists and performers for a talent search process. This form helps talent agents and other users to gather details about potential candidates, including their name, contact information, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the text field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the text field.
5. Choose a valid **Date** for your availability.
6. Select a valid **Time** for your availability.
7. Enter a **Note** about your experience and skills.
8. Select one or more **Sining Fields** you are skilled in, such as Vocal, Instrumental, Dance, Acting, or Visual Arts.
9. Choose the type of **Event** you are interested in, such as Vocal, Instrumental, Dance, Acting, or Visual Arts. Note that this field is required.
10. Enter a brief **Bio** about yourself and your experience.
11. If applicable, upload your **Photo** to demonstrate your visual appearance.
12. Select **Submit** to complete and submit your registration.
13. Select **Cancel** to start over or abandon your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date**: Choose a date when you are available to perform.
* **Time**: Choose a time when you are available to perform.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information about your experience and skills.
* **Sining Fields**: Select one or more fields of interest, such as Vocal, Instrumental, Dance, Acting, or Visual Arts.
* **Event**: Choose the type of event you are interested in, such as Vocal, Instrumental, Dance, Acting, or Visual Arts. (Required)
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description about yourself and your experience.
* **Photo**: If applicable, upload a photo to demonstrate your visual appearance.
* **Submit**: Complete and submit your registration.
* **Cancel**: Start over or abandon your registration.
