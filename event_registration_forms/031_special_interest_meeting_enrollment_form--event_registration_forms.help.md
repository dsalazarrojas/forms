# Special Interest Meeting Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Interest Meeting Enrollment Form is used to gather information from participants for special interest meetings. This form helps meeting organizers collect the necessary details to facilitate a smooth and productive meeting experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Meeting Details** field with your desired meeting details.
2.  Enter your **Participant Name** in the corresponding field.
3.  Provide your **Participant Email** to ensure you receive updates and notifications.
4.  Select your **Topic Of Interest** from the drop-down list, which includes options like Meeting Topic, Club Meeting, Association Meeting, Nonprofit Meeting, and Workplace Meeting.
5.  Choose your **Participant Preferred Meetings** from the available options, which include Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. You can select one or more days that suit your preferences.
6.  Enter any additional **Comments** in the text area, if needed.
7.  Specify the **Meeting Location** where the meeting will take place.
8.  Choose the **Meeting Start Time** using the time picker.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (Required): This field is used to fill in the meeting's details. Please provide a brief description of the meeting, such as its purpose, expected attendees, and any other relevant information.
* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID, drivers license, or other government-issued ID.
* **Participant Email** (Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Topic Of Interest** (Optional): Select the topic that interests you the most from the provided options.
* **Participant Preferred Meetings** (Optional): Choose one or more days that you prefer to attend meetings. You can select from Monday to Sunday.
* **Comments** (Required): Enter any additional comments or details about your meeting preferences or needs.
* **Meeting Location** (Required): Specify the location where the meeting will take place.
* **Meeting Start Time** (Required): Choose the start time of the meeting using the time picker.

Note: Fields marked "Required" are mandatory and must be filled in for the form submission to be valid.
