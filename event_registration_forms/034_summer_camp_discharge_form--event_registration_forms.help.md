# Summer Camp Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Discharge Form is used to track the details of a camper's discharge from the summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the camper's information.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
3. Sign and date the form with the camper's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Camper Info
* **Camper Name** (`camper_name`, text, not required): This field is for the camper's name.
* **Camper Email** (`camper_email`, email, not required): This is the camper's email address.
* **Camper Phone** (`camper_phone`, text, not required): This is the camper's phone number.
* **Date of Arrival** (`camper_date_of_arrival`, date, not required): This is the date the camper arrived at the camp.
* **Date of Discharge** (`camper_date_of_discharge`, date, not required): This is the date the camper is discharged from the camp.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, text, not required): This field is for stating the reason why the camper is being discharged.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): This field is for providing any additional comments or information about the camper.
* **Camper Signature** (`camper_signature`, note, not required): This is for the camper to sign and date their signature. 

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
