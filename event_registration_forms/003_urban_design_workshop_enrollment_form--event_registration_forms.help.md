# urban_design_workshop_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll in an urban design workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields with accurate and correct information.
*   Ensure that you are enrolling in a real workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Workshop Name (Workshop Name, text, required)**: This is the name of the workshop you are enrolling in. Please enter the name of the workshop as it appears on the workshop's promotional materials.
*   **Workshop Dates (Workshop Dates, date, optional)**: This field is for the dates of the workshop. If you know the dates of the workshop, please enter them. If not, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Participant Name (Participant Name, text, required)**: This is your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents.
*   **Participant Email (Participant Email, email, required)**: This is your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
*   **Participant Phone (Participant Phone, text, optional)**: This is your phone number. If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
*   **Workshop Organizer (Workshop Organizer, text, optional)**: This is the name of the person or organization hosting the workshop. If you know their name, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
*   Ensure that you are enrolling in a real workshop.
*   Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any issues with enrolling in the workshop or need assistance, please contact the workshop organizer.
