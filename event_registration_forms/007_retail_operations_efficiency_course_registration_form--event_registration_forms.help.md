# Retail Operations Efficiency Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Operations Efficiency Course Registration Form is used for retail employees to register for the Retail Operations Efficiency Course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To register for the course, fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to complete all the fields marked as "required."
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Name
* **user_name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
### 2. Email
* **email** (email, required): Enter your company email address.
### 3. Phone
* **phone** (text, not required): Enter your business phone number for contact purposes.
### 4. Date
* **date** (date, not required): Enter the date you wish to attend the course in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
### 5. Time
* **time** (time, not required): Enter the time of day you wish to attend the course in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00:00.
### 6. Note
* **note** (note, not required): Add any additional information or comments about your course preferences.
### 7. Manager Name
* **manager_name** (text, not required): Enter your direct supervisor's name for team registration purposes.
### 8. Company
* **company** (text, not required): Enter your company name for course attendance records.
### 9. Job Title
* **job_title** (text, not required): Enter your job title for course attendance records.

## Tips
* Review all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or course administrator.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, include them in the "Note" field.
