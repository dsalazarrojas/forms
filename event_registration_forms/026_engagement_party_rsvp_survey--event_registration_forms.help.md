# Engagement Party RSVP Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to RSVP to the Engagement Party, indicating their availability and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Indicate whether or not you will be attending the party.
3. If you are attending, choose the date you will be arriving.
4. If you are attending, select your arrival time.
5. Indicate whether or not you will dine at the party, and if so, choose any dietary restrictions you have.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
7. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Will Attend** (`will_attend`, select_one, optional): Check yes or no to indicate whether you will attend the party.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the party, if you are planning to attend.
* **Will Arrive** (`will_arrive`, select_one, optional): Check yes or no to indicate whether you plan to arrive at the party.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter your expected arrival time, if you are planning to attend.
* **Will Dine** (`will_dine`, select_multiple, optional): If you plan to dine at the party, choose your dietary restrictions from the options. If you don't plan to dine, select 'No'.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
