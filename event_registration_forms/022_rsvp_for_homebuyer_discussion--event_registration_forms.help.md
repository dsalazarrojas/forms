## rsvp_for_homebuyer_discussion - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for RSVP'ing to a homebuyer discussion. The purpose of this form is to help us track attendees and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the First name field with your first name.
2. Fill out the Last name field with your last name.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone number field.
5. Choose a Date to indicate when you plan to attend the homebuyer discussion.
6. Double-check that all details are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for future correspondence.
* **Phone number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you regarding the event.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Select a date that you plan to attend the homebuyer discussion.

## Tips
* Double-check that all details are correct before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an entry under your name.
* If you are unsure about anything, contact the organiser directly.
