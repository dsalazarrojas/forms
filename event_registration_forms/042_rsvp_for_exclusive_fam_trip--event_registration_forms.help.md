# RSVP For Exclusive FAM Trip - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs from individuals who have received an invitation to an exclusive FAM trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you about the trip.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the trip location.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to depart from the trip location.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more of the available preferences options that apply to you.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance by selecting the "True" option.
