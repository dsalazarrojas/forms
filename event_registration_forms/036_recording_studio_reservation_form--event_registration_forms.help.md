# Recording Studio Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a recording session in a studio, allowing clients to reserve a time slot and book a recording studio. It is designed for both live events (e.g., concerts, meetings) and pre-recorded events (e.g., voiceovers, audio podcasts).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Studio Name with the name of the studio where you'd like to book your session.
2. Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you about your session.
3. Select a start time for your session. Note that the start time should be in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 for 1:00 PM).
4. Provide a contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
5. Add any additional contact notes or information about your session in the Contact Note field.
6. Enter the Event Name for your session (e.g., "Band Concert" or "Podcast Recording").
7. Enter the date for your event.
8. Choose a start date and end date for your session.
9. Select the type of recording you'd like to do (Live Recording, Pre-recorded, or Live & Pre-recorded).
10. Select the equipment you need for your session (Microphone, Monitor, Console, or Other).
11. Choose the level of studio equipment you'd like to use (Basic, Deluxe, or Custom).
12. Select the type of event (Live Event, Pre-recorded Event, or Meeting).
13. Choose your payment method (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Cash).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Name (1)**: Enter the name of the recording studio where you'd like to book your session. (Required)
* **Contact Email (2)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your session. (Required)
* **Start Time (3)**: Select a start time for your session in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 for 1:00 PM). (Required)
* **Contact Phone (4)**: Provide a contact phone number in case we need to reach you. (Optional)
* **Contact Note (5)**: Add any additional notes or information about your session. (Optional)
* **Event Name (6)**: Enter the name of your event or session (e.g., "Band Concert" or "Podcast Recording"). (Optional)
* **Event Date (7)**: Enter the date for your event. (Optional)
* **Start Date (8)**: Choose the start date for your session. (Optional)
* **End Date (9)**: Select the end date for your session. (Optional)
* **Recording Type (11)**: Select the type of recording you'd like to do (Live Recording, Pre-recorded, or Live & Pre-recorded). (Optional)
* **Equipment Needed (12)**: Choose the equipment you need for your session (Microphone, Monitor, Console, or Other). (Optional)
* **Studio Equipment (13)**: Select the level of studio equipment you'd like to use (Basic, Deluxe, or Custom). (Optional)
* **Event Type (14)**: Select the type of event (Live Event, Pre-recorded Event, or Meeting). (Optional)
* **Payment Method (15)**: Choose your payment method (Online Payment, Bank Transfer, or Cash). (Optional)

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your start and end dates to ensure they align with your actual event schedule.
* If you need assistance or have questions, feel free to contact us with your contact information.
