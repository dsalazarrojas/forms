# Dance Competition Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Time Off Form is a tool used to request time off for dancers during a dance competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all details are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Competition Time Off Form** (1, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form and is not editable.
* **Dance Competition Time-Off Request** (2, text, optional): Please specify your request for time off in this field.
* **Competition Date** (3, date, optional): Enter the date of the competition where you wish to take time off.
* **Start Time** (4, time, optional): Enter the start time of the competition where you wish to take time off.
* **Dance Competition Name** (5, text, optional): Enter the name of the dance competition where you wish to take time off.
* **Rehearsal Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the date of the rehearsal where you wish to take time off.
* **Rehearsal Time** (7, time, optional): Enter the start time of the rehearsal where you wish to take time off.
* **Rehearsal Start Time** (8, time, optional): Enter the start time of the rehearsal where you wish to take time off.
* **Studio** (9, text, optional): Enter the name of the studio where you train.
* **Dancer's Name** (10, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Dance Style** (11, select_one, optional): Select the type of dance you will be performing (e.g. hip hop, tap, jazz, ballet, lyrical, ballroom).
* **Competition Level** (12, select_one, optional): Select your competition level (e.g. junior, senior).
* **Time-Off Request** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the reasons for your time off request (e.g. rehearsal, competition, practice).
* **email** (14, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (15, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Dancer's Contact** (16, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for you or your dancer.
* **Event Name** (17, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Dancer's Email** (18, text, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Dancer's Phone** (19, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Studio Email** (20, text, optional): Enter your studio's email address.
* **Studio Phone** (21, text, optional): Enter your studio's phone number.
* **Dancer's Address** (22, text, optional): Enter your address.

Note: Please review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are filled in accurately before submitting it.
