# Santa Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Santa Visit Registration Form is a tool used by parents to register their child for a visit with Santa Claus. This form helps the Santa's team to gather essential information about the child and their parent or guardian, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your child's **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Parent's Name** in the "Parent's Name" field.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Select a **Visit Date** from the calendar provided in the "Visit Date" field.
7. Choose a specific **Time** for the visit in the time picker.
8. Add any special **Note** or instructions in the "Note" field.
9. If applicable, assign a specific **Assigned Tool** for the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child who will be visiting Santa.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the child who will be visiting Santa.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian who will be contacting the Santa's team.
* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who will be accompanying the child.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter a contact number for the parent or guardian.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Select a date for the visit using the calendar provided.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a specific time for the visit using the time picker.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any special instructions or notes for the Santa's team.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): If applicable, assign a specific tool for the visit.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the visit.
* If you need to make any changes or updates to the form, please contact the Santa's team directly.
* Only authorized personnel will have access to the form's contents.
