# Art Restoration Project Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Restoration Project Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from art owners to ensure that the restoration process meets their needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your full name as it appears on your artwork's registration document.
*   Provide your email address for us to contact you.
*   Enter the title of the artwork you are seeking restoration for.
*   Provide the original artist's name.
*   Describe the materials and medium used in creating the artwork.
*   Describe the current condition of the artwork, including any visible damage or aging.
*   Specify your desired outcomes for the restoration process.
*   Check all the restoration procedures that apply to your artwork.
*   Acknowledge the risks inherent in art restoration.
*   Sign and date this form to indicate your consent to proceed with the restoration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Artwork Owner Full Name (Required)**: This is the full name of the owner of the artwork.
*   **Contact Email Address (Required)**: This is the email address we will use to contact you for any updates or questions.
*   **Title of the Artwork (Required)**: Please enter the title of the artwork, as it appears on the registration document.
*   **Original Artist Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the artist who created the artwork.
*   **Medium and Materials (Required)**: Describe the materials and medium used in creating the artwork, e.g., oil on canvas.
*   **Description of Current Condition (Required)**: Describe the condition of the artwork, including any visible damage or aging.
*   **Desired Restoration Outcomes (Optional)**: Specify your goals and expectations for the restoration process.
*   **Consent to Restoration Procedures (Required)**: Check all the procedures that apply to your artwork, such as surface cleaning or varnish removal.
*   **Risk Acknowledgment (Required)**: Acknowledge that you understand the risks inherent in art restoration.
*   **Date of Consent (Required)**: Sign and date this form to indicate your consent to proceed with the restoration process.

Note: This form should be filled out carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate information is captured, and to avoid duplication of efforts.
