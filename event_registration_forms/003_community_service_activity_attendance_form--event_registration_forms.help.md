# Community Service Activity Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document community service activity attendance for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event name** in the "Event name" field. This is the title of the service activity.
2. Enter the **Event date** in the "Event date" field. This is the date when the service activity took place.
3. Enter your **Participant full name** in the "Participant full name" field. This is your name as a volunteer or attendee.
4. Enter your **email address** in the "Participant email address" field. This is for follow-up and confirmation purposes (optional).
5. Select your **Activity role or assignment** from the dropdown menu in the "Activity role or assignment" field. This indicates your position or role in the activity.
6. Enter the **Whole number of hours volunteered** in the "Hours contributed" field.
7. If you have a **Supervisor or coordinator name**, enter it in the "Supervisor or coordinator name" field. This person is overseeing the activity (optional).
8. Optionally, add any **Comments or observations** about your attendance or performance in the "Comments or observations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event name** (`event_name`, required): The title of the service activity.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, required): The date when the service activity took place.
* **Participant full name** (`participant_name`, required): Your name as a volunteer or attendee.
* **Participant email address** (`participant_email`, optional): For follow-up and confirmations. You can leave this blank if not applicable.
* **Arrival time** (`arrival_time`, optional): Record your arrival time (not applicable for most activities).
* **Departure time** (`departure_time`, optional): Record your departure time (not applicable for most activities).
* **Activity role or assignment** (`activity_roles`, required): Select your position or role in the activity.
* **Hours contributed** (`hours_contributed`, required): Enter the whole number of hours volunteered.
* **Supervisor or coordinator name** (`supervisor_name`, optional): Enter the person overseeing the activity (only required if you're a volunteer).
* **Comments or observations** (`comments`, optional): Any notes about attendance or performance.

## Tips
* Double-check your event name and date for accuracy.
* Make sure to choose the correct activity role or assignment.
* If you're unsure about your arrival or departure time, you can leave these fields blank.
* Use the comments field to provide any additional information about your attendance or performance.
