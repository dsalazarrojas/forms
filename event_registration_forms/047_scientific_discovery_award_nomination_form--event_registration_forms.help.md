# Scientific Discovery Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for scientific discovery award nominations. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured way to submit nominations for individuals or organizations that have made significant contributions to scientific progress and discovery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields, including **nominating_category** and **nominating_type**, which are essential for categorizing and understanding the nature of the nomination.
*   Enter the **nominator information**, including their contact details such as **nominator_name**, **nominator_phone**, and **nominator_email**.
*   Provide details about the **nomination_title** and **nominating_year** of the discovery.
*   You may also add additional comments or notes about the nomination in the **nominator_comment** field.
*   If you have a supporting URL or file, you can enter it in the **nominating_url** and **nominator_file** fields respectively.
*   Review and submit your nomination by clicking the **nominator_submit** button.
*   If you want to save your draft and continue later, you can click the **nominator_save_draft** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **nominating_category** (Required): Choose the category that best describes the nomination, such as Research, Technology, or Innovation.
*   **nominating_type** (Required): Select all relevant types that best describe the nomination, such as Research, Technology, or Innovation.
*   **nominator_name** (Optional): Enter the name of the person or organization making the nomination.
*   **nominator_phone** (Optional): Enter the phone number of the nominator.
*   **nominator_email** (Optional): Enter the email address of the nominator.
*   **nominator_title** (Optional): Enter a brief title describing the nomination.
*   **nominator_institution** (Optional): Enter the institution or organization related to the nomination.
*   **nominator_country** (Optional): Enter the country where the nomination was made.
*   **nominating_date** (Optional): Enter the date of the nomination.
*   **nominating_time** (Optional): Enter the time of the nomination.
*   **nominating_year** (Optional): Enter the year of the nomination.
*   **nominator_comment** (Optional): Add any additional comments about the nomination.
*   **nominator_url** (Optional): Enter a URL that supports the nomination.
*   **nominator_file** (Optional): Attach a file that supports the nomination.
*   **nominator_submit** (Required): Click to submit the nomination.
*   **nominator_decline** (Optional): Click to decline the nomination.
*   **nominator_save_draft** (Optional): Click to save the draft and continue later.
*   **nominator_delete** (Optional): Click to delete the draft.
*   **nominator_back** (Optional): Click to go back to the previous page.
*   **nominator_next** (Optional): Click to go to the next page.
*   **nominator_reset** (Optional): Click to reset the form.
*   **nominator_reset** (Optional) and **nominator_delete** (Optional) do not have any direct functionality in this context.
