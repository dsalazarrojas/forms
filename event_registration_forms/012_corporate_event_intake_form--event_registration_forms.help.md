# Corporate Event Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Event Intake Form is a tool used to collect detailed information about corporate events, conferences, and meetings from event planners and organizers. This form helps to gather essential details such as event title, date, time, location, and attendees to ensure that the event runs smoothly and efficiently. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Event Details" section to provide the event title, date, and other essential information.
2. In the "Event Planning Details" section, describe the event concept, objective, and key deliverables.
3. In the "Additional Details" section, add any other relevant information that may impact the event planning process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the corporate event. This will help us understand the main theme and subject of the event.
* **Event Date** (no label, date, optional): Enter the date of the corporate event. Please use the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Event Time** (no label, time, optional): Enter the time of the event. Please use 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (no label, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Concept** (no label, text, optional): Describe the event concept or purpose.
* **Objective** (no label, text, optional): Describe the objective or desired outcome of the event.
* **Key Deliverables** (no label, text, optional): List any key deliverables or expected outcomes.
* **Additional Information** (no label, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details about the event that may impact the planning process.
