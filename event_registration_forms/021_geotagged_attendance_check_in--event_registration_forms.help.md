# Geotagged Attendance Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Geotagged Attendance Check In" form is designed to record and track the attendance of participants at events. It captures essential information such as event ID, date, time, location, attendee name, role, and additional details like team leader name and team members. The form helps event organizers and administrators to monitor attendance, roles, and notes, making it easier to manage events efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Event ID**, which identifies the event you are checking in for.
2. Select the **Date** of the event from the calendar.
3. Choose the **Time** of the event from the available options.
4. Provide the **Location** of the event, where you will be attending or participating.
5. Enter your **Attendee Name**, which must be your actual name as it will be used to identify you as a participant.
6. Select your **Role** from the options (Participant, Organizer, or Observer).
7. If your role is Participant, you can optionally enter your team leader's name and team members you are associated with.
8. Add any **Notes** you would like to include about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Id (Required):** Enter the unique identifier for the event, as provided by the event organizer or as seen on the event invitation.
* **Date (Required):** Select the date on which you plan to participate in or attend the event. Choose from the calendar provided.
* **Time (Required):** Choose the time slot when you will be participating in or attending the event. Ensure it matches the event schedule.
* **Location (Required):** Enter the exact location where the event will take place. This helps the organizers and attendees to find each other.
* **Attendee Name (Required):** Type your actual name as you want it to be recorded for the event. This is your identifier for the event's attendance records.
* **Role (Required):** Select your role from the options provided:
	+ **Participant:** If you are attending or participating in the event.
	+ **Organizer:** If you are leading or coordinating the event.
	+ **Observer:** If you are observing or attending the event without participating.
* **Team Leader Name (Optional):** If you are a team leader, enter your name here. This is only required if you select Participant as your role.
* **Team Id (Optional):** Enter the ID of the team you are leading or participating with. This is only required if you select Participant as your role.
* **Team Members (Optional):** Select team members you are associated with from the provided options. This is only required if you select Participant as your role.
* **Notes (Optional):** Add any additional details or comments about your attendance or participation.
