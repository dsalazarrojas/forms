# sustainable_food_systems_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sustainable food systems workshop registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending the workshop. This form helps us understand your interests and needs, which enables us to provide a better experience for you and other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name.
2. Enter a valid email address.
3. If you'd like to be contacted, provide your phone number.
4. Select which workshop you are interested in attending.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, please select them from the options.
6. You can add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional note" field.
7. Fill in any additional information in the "Registration Form 2" to "Registration Form 20" fields as needed.
