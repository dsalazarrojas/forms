# Conference Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for conference attendees, ensuring accurate enrollment and proper badge pick-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name in the "Registrant name" field.
2. If you are representing an organization or affiliation, provide it in the "Organization or affiliation" field. This is optional.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used for enrollment confirmation.
4. Choose your registration type from the options provided in the "Registration type" field.
5. If applicable, select your meal preferences from the options in the "Meal option" field.
6. Confirm your payment status in the "Payment status" field.
7. If you have any accessibility needs, provide them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
8. If necessary, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact" field.
9. If you have any special requests or requirements, type them in the "Special requests" field.
10. Finally, read the "Enrollment confirmation note" to ensure you understand the next steps and badge pick-up procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant name** (`Registrant name`, text, required): This is your full name.
* **Organization or affiliation** (`Organization or affiliation`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization or affiliation, provide it here.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for enrollment confirmation.
* **Registration type** (`Registration type`, select_one, required): Choose your registration type (pass or ticket type).
* **Meal option** (`Meal option`, select_one, optional): Select your meal preferences for on-site meals.
* **Payment status** (`Payment status`, select_one, required): Confirm your payment status (paid or pending).
* **Accessibility needs** (`Accessibility needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, provide them here.
* **Emergency contact** (`Emergency contact`, text, optional): If necessary, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Special requests** (`Special requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, type them in here.
* **Enrollment confirmation note** (`Enrollment confirmation note`, note, optional): Read this note for next steps and badge pick-up procedures.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields (marked with *) accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the conference organizers or check the help documentation for further clarification.
