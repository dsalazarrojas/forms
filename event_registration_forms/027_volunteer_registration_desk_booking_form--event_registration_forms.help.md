# volunteer_registration_desk_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "volunteer_registration_desk_booking_form" is a form designed to collect information from volunteers and staff members for event registration and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Volunteer Details," "Event Details," "Contact Details," "Registration Details," and "Notes" fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct "Volunteer Type" from the drop-down menu.
3. Ensure you enter the correct "Event ID" and "Event Name" if provided.
4. Enter the "Start Date," "End Date," and "Time" for the event.
5. Choose the correct "Status" for the event and "Volunteer Status" from the drop-down menus.
6. Provide the "Contact Person," "Contact Number," and "Email" for the event organizer or contact person.
7. Fill out the "Notes" field with any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a volunteer.
* **Event Details**: Provide the event ID and name if provided.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact person, contact number, and email for easy communication.
* **Registration Details**: Enter any registration details specific to the event.
* **Type**: Select your role as a volunteer (Volunteer, Staff, or Volunteer-Staff).
* **Status**: Choose the status of the event (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Volunteer Status**: Choose the status of your own volunteer status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Event ID**: Enter the event ID if provided.
* **Event Name**: Enter the event name if provided.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Volunteer Status**: Choose the status of your volunteer status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's number.
* **Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the event or yourself.
* **Registered By**: Enter the person who registered you for the event.
* **Registered At**: Enter the date and time of registration.
* **Updated By**: Enter the person who updated the event information.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time of the last update.
* **Registered IP**: Enter the IP address from which you registered.
* **Submitted**: Enter the date and time of form submission.
