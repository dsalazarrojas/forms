# Conference Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Check In Form is designed to collect attendee information for a smooth conference experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the required fields filled before submitting.
2.  Start by providing your **Conference Name** in the text field provided.
3.  Enter your **Check-In Date** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD). This helps us track your arrival.
4.  If you're willing, select your **Check-In Time** from the available options.
5.  In the **Attendee Lookup** field, type or scan your registration ID for quick verification.
6.  Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** for accurate record-keeping.
7.  Enter your **Email Address** for communication purposes.
8.  If you have a **Registration ID**, type it in for quick reference.
9.  For the **Organization**, type the name of your company or institution.
10.  In the **Badge Information** field, type any specific details about your badge preferences.
11.  Provide the **Name for Badge** as you would like it to appear on your badge.
12.  Enter your **Title for Badge** as per your job title or role.
13.  Type the **Organization for Badge** as your company name.
14.  Select your preferred **Primary Track** from the list provided (if any).
15.  Choose the workshops you would like to attend by selecting **Workshop Selection**.
16.  If you'd like to receive conference materials, select **Conference Bag**.
17.  Choose your **Name Tag Lanyard** preference from the options provided.
18.  Indicate your level of interest in networking opportunities.
19.  If you're okay with being photographed, select **Photo Consent**.
20.  Finally, confirm your **Check-In Complete** to finish the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Conference Name**: Enter the name of the conference you're attending.
*   **Check-In Date**: Enter the current date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Check-In Time**: Select your arrival time from the provided options.
*   **Attendee Lookup**: Type or scan your registration ID for quick verification.
*   **First Name**: Enter your given name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
*   **Registration ID**: Type your registration number for reference.
*   **Organization**: Enter the name of your company or institution.
*   **Badge Information**: Type specific details about your badge preferences.
*   **Name for Badge**: Enter the name you want on your badge.
*   **Title for Badge**: Enter your job title or role.
*   **Organization for Badge**: Enter your company name.
*   **Primary Track**: Select your main area of interest.
*   **Workshop Selection**: Choose the workshops you'd like to attend.
*   **Conference Bag**: Select if you'd like to receive conference materials.
*   **Name Tag Lanyard**: Choose your lanyard preference.
*   **Networking Preferences**: Indicate your level of interest in networking opportunities.
*   **Interested in Networking**: Express your level of interest in meeting other attendees.
*   **Social Media Handle**: Enter your Twitter or LinkedIn handle.
*   **Photo Consent**: Select if it's okay to be photographed.
*   **Check-In Complete**: Confirm that you're ready to proceed.

## Tips

*   Ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting.
*   Be as accurate as possible to avoid confusion.
*   Review the form for any errors before submitting.
*   Don't hesitate to ask for assistance if you need help with any field.
