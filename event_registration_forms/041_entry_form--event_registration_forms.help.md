# Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entry Form is designed to collect information from event attendees for entry registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete details for each field.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure they are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (`title`, text, required: false): This field is not a required field. You can leave it blank if you don't want to provide a title.
* **entry_details** (`label`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your entry.
* **event_details** (`label`, text, required: false): Enter the event details you would like to share.
* **additional_info** (`label`, text, required: false): Add any additional information that you think would be helpful for the event organizer to know.
* **entry_form_submission** (`label`, text, required: false): This field is not required, but if you need to provide more information, you can do so here.
* **event_registration** (`label`, text, required: false): Provide your event registration details, such as your name and ID number.
* **payment_gateway** (`label`, text, required: false): If you have a payment gateway account, enter your account details here.
* **submission_confirmation** (`label`, text, required: false): This is where you'll receive confirmation of your submission.
* **contact_info** (`label`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **entry_status** (`label`, text, required: false): This field is not required, but you can use it to track your entry status.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
