# livestock_welfare_workshop_recording_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture participant consent for recording during the Livestock Welfare Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name, Contact Number, and Email.
2. Choose your response for Participant Agreement, Recording Agreement, and Recording Consent.
3. Optionally, provide a brief reason for recording purposes.
4. Sign and date your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your response for being recorded:
	+ I agree to be recorded
	+ I do not agree to be recorded
	+ I need more information on the recording process
* **Recording Agreement** (`recording_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your response for being recorded:
	+ I agree to be recorded
	+ I do not agree to be recorded
	+ I need more information on the recording process
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your recording preference.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, text, optional): Optionally, provide a brief reason for recording purposes.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date your signature to confirm your consent.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the organizers for clarification.
- Remember to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
