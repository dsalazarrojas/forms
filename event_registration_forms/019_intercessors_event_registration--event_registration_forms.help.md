# intercessors_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees for events, including session selections, volunteer interests, and personal details. It aims to efficiently manage event registrations and capture relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your session choices from the options provided.
2. Indicate your volunteer interests by selecting the relevant options.
3. Enter your name, email, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
4. Add any additional notes if needed.
5. Confirm your attendance and agreement to terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Selection
* **Session Selection**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending. You can choose multiple sessions.
### Volunteer Interest
* **Volunteer Interest**: Indicate your interest in volunteering for the event. You can select multiple options.
### Attendee Name
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded for the event.
### Attendee Email
* **Attendee Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
### Attendee Phone
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
### Attendee Address
* **Attendee Address**: Enter your address for logistical purposes.
### Attendee Notes
* **Attendee Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
### Sessions Selected
* **Sessions Selected**: This field is likely not used and can be ignored.
### Session 1, Session 2, Session 3
* **Session 1**, **Session 2**, **Session 3**: Select the sessions you are interested in, similar to Session Selection.
### Interest 1, Interest 2, Interest 3, Interest 4, Interest 5
* **Interest 1, **Interest 2, **Interest 3, **Interest 4, **Interest 5**: These are similar to Volunteer Interest and can be ignored unless specified otherwise.
### Attendee Interest
* **Attendee Interest**: Indicate your interests similar to Volunteer Interest.
### Attendee Attendance
* **Attendee Attendance**: Select your attendance status.
### Attendee Message
* **Attendee Message**: Add any additional comments or messages.
### Attendee Tos Agreement
* **Attendee Tos Agreement**: Agree to the terms of service.
### Tos Agree 1, Tos Agree 2, Tos Agree 3
* **Tos Agree 1, **Tos Agree 2, **Tos Agree 3**: These are similar to Attendee Tos Agreement and can be ignored unless specified otherwise.
### Event Notes
* **Event Notes**: This field is likely not used and can be ignored.
