# Food Festival Vendor Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a vendor space at the Food Festival.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the event you would like to participate in.
2. **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the event.
3. **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the event.
4. **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, Optional): Enter the start time of the event.
5. **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, Optional): Enter your business name.
6. **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, Optional): Enter your business email.
7. **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your business phone number.
8. **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, Optional): Enter your business address.
9. **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of vendor you are (Food Truck, Food Cart, Food Stall).
10. **Number of Days** (`number_of_days`, number, Optional): Enter the number of days you will be participating in the event.
11. **Booth Dimensions** (`booth_dimensions`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the size of the booth you will be using (10x10ft, 10x20ft, 20x20ft, 20x30ft, 20x40ft).
12. **Vendor License Number** (`vendor_license_number`, text, Optional): Enter your business license number.
13. **Vendor License Type** (`vendor_license_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of license you hold (Food Service, Health Permit, Business License).
14. **Vendor Description** (`vendor_description`, text, Optional): Enter a short description of your business.
15. **Vendor Image(s)** (`vendor_image`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the image(s) you would like to associate with your business.
16. **Vendor Requirements** (`vendor_requirements`, text, Optional): Enter any specific requirements you have for your business.
17. **Vendor Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, select_multiple, Optional): Agree to the terms of service.
18. **Additional Info** (`vendor_additional_info`, text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your business.
19. **Payment Status** (`vendor_payment_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose your payment status (Fully paid, Partially paid, Unpaid).
20. **Vendor Status** (`vendor_status`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose your status (Active, Inactive, Removed).
21. **Vendor Note** (`vendor_note`, text, Optional): Enter any notes about your business.
22. **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, Optional): Enter the start time of the event.
23. **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, Optional): Enter the end time of the event.
24. **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, text, Optional): Enter any notes about your business.
25. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, Optional): Choose the tool you are assigned to use.
26. **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose the user assigned to handle your account.
