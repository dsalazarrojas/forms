# Art Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Art Festival Registration Form, which collects essential information from participants to facilitate their registration and participation in the festival.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Art Festival Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal details, including your full name and email address. Please note that these fields are required for registration.
2. If you are a performance artist, select the relevant participation type (Exhibiting Artist (Individual), Performance Artist - Group, Workshop Instructor, Vendor (Food - Merchandise), or Volunteer).
3. Provide a brief description of your artwork or performance, highlighting what you will be showcasing at the festival.
4. Specify any space or equipment requirements you may need for your participation.
5. If applicable, indicate the number of items you will be displaying.
6. Choose your preferred participation dates from the available options.
7. If you have any dietary requirements for participant catering, please specify them.
8. Confirm whether you have public liability insurance for your participation.
9. Agree to the Art Festival terms and conditions and safety guidelines.
10. Complete the registration by providing your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (3)**: Provide your official email address, as this will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Mailing Address (5)**: Specify your mailing address, including street address and any relevant details.
* **How will you be participating in the festival? (7)**: Select one of the options provided:
	+ Exhibiting Artist (Individual)
	+ Performance Artist - Group
	+ Workshop Instructor
	+ Vendor (Food - Merchandise)
	+ Volunteer
* **Description of Artwork or Performance (9)**: Provide a brief description of what you will be showcasing at the festival. Please keep it concise.
* **Space or Equipment Requirements (10)**: List any space or equipment you may need for your participation.
* **Number of items to be displayed (11)**: If applicable, specify the number of items you will be displaying.
* **Preferred Participation Dates (13)**: Select all applicable dates for your participation:
	+ Friday Opening Night
	+ Saturday Full Day
	+ Sunday Full Day
	+ Closing Awards Ceremony
* **Dietary Requirements (14)**: If you have any dietary requirements, please specify them for participant catering.
* **Public Liability Insurance (15)**: Confirm whether you have public liability insurance for your participation.
* **Terms and Conditions (16)**: Review and agree to the Art Festival terms and conditions and safety guidelines by selecting 'I Agree'.
* **Digital Signature (19)**: Type your full name for digital signature purposes.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Art Festival Registration Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.
