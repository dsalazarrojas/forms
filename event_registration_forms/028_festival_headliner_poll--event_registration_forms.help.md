# Festival Headliner Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Headliner Poll is a form used to collect information about users' favorite festival headliners. It is designed to help event organizers understand which headliners are the most popular among festival attendees and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Festival Headliner Poll form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Your Favorite Headliners**: Choose the headliners you enjoy the most from the list of available options.
2. **Select One Headliner (where applicable)**: For fields labeled "Headliner," select only one of the provided options.
3. **Provide Date and Time**: Enter the date and time for the event for which you are submitting your poll (if applicable).
4. **Provide Additional Information (optional)**: Some fields are optional and allow you to provide additional information or comments about your favorite headliners.
