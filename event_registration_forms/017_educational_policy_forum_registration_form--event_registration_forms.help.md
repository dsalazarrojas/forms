# educational_policy_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants, presenters, and organizers for educational events related to educational policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. If applicable, fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your department from the "Department" field.
6. Choose your role in the event from the "Role" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter the time of registration in the "Time of Registration" field.
9. If necessary, add additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
10. Select your educational institution from the "Educational Institution" field.
11. Fill in the event title in the "Event Title" field.
12. Select the date of the event from the "Event Date" field.
13. Choose the time of the event from the "Event Time" field.
14. Select the event location from the "Event Location" field.
15. If applicable, add a description of the event in the "Event Description" field.
16. Add any additional information about the participant in the "Participant Info" field.
17. Add any additional information about the presenter in the "Presenter Info" field.
18. Add any additional information about attendees in the "Attendee Info" field.
19. Enter the presenter's email address in the "Presenter Email" field.
20. If applicable, fill in the presenter's phone number in the "Presenter Phone" field.
21. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
22. If applicable, fill in the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
23. Enter the participant's title in the "Participant Title" field.
24. Select the participant's department from the "Participant Department" field.
25. Choose the participant's role in the event from the "Participant Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you want to be reached by phone, fill in your phone number.
* **Department** (Select One, Required): Select your department.
* **Role** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose your role in the event.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Registration** (Time, Required): Enter the time you registered.
* **Note** (Note, Optional): If necessary, add additional comments or notes.
* **Educational Institution** (Select One, Required): Select your educational institution.
* **Event Title** (Text, Required): Fill in the title of the event.
* **Event Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (Time, Required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (Select One, Required): Select the event location.
* **Event Description** (Note, Optional): Add a description of the event.
* **Participant Info** (Note, Optional): Add additional information about the participant.
* **Presenter Info** (Note, Optional): Add additional information about the presenter.
* **Attendee Info** (Note, Optional): Add additional information about attendees.
* **Presenter Email** (Email, Required): Enter the presenter's email address.
* **Presenter Phone** (Text, Optional): If applicable, fill in the presenter's phone number.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, Optional): If applicable, fill in the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Title** (Text, Required): Fill in the participant's title.
* **Participant Department** (Select One, Required): Select the participant's department.
* **Participant Role** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the participant's role in the event.
