# Press Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Press Release Authorization Form" is a document used to authorize press releases from various sources. It helps to verify the authenticity and approval of press releases, ensuring they are accurate and legitimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Individual or Business Name** (Field 2) with the full name of the individual or business authorizing the press release.
2. Enter the **Press Release Date** (Field 3) if applicable.
3. Select the **Release Type** (Field 5) from the provided options (Press Release, Media Alert, or Media Advisory).
4. If you have a related **Press Release Image** (Field 4), enter the URL or description.
5. Provide your **Signed By** (Field 7) name, who is signing this form on behalf of the individual or business.
6. Add any additional **Release Details** (Field 8) if necessary.
7. Finally, answer **Image Release** (Field 9) to confirm whether the image associated with the press release will be released or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Press Release Authorization** (Field 1, Press Release Authorization Form): Enter a brief description of the press release.
* **Individual or Business Name** (Field 2): Enter the full name of the individual or business authorizing the press release.
* **Press Release Date** (Field 3): Enter the date of the press release, if applicable.
* **Press Release Image** (Field 4): Enter the URL or description of the press release image, if applicable.
* **Release Type** (Field 5): Select one or more of the provided options (Press Release, Media Alert, or Media Advisory).
* **Authorization Date** (Field 6): Enter the date of authorization, if applicable.
* **Signed By** (Field 7): Enter the name of the individual signing this form on behalf of the individual or business.
* **Release Details** (Field 8): Enter any additional details about the press release.
* **Image Release** (Field 9): Select whether the image associated with the press release will be released or not.
