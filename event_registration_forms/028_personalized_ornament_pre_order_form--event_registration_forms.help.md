# Personalized Ornament Pre Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow customers to pre-order a customized ornament with their personal preferences, and to facilitate the order process for both the customer and the seller.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer information fields, including any relevant details.
2. Describe the ornament details you would like to order.
3. Provide order details, including any specific requests or instructions.
4. Enter shipping information to ensure timely and secure delivery.
5. Verify your customer payment information to ensure a smooth payment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Info, Text, Required): Enter your name and any relevant details about yourself.
* **Ornament Information** (Ornament Info, Text, Required): Describe the ornament you would like to order, including any design or customization requests.
* **Order Details** (Order Details, Text, Required): Provide any specific requests or instructions for your order.
* **Shipping Information** (Shipping Info, Text, Required): Enter your shipping address, including any special instructions for delivery.
* **Customer Payment Information** (Customer Payment Info, Text, Required): Enter your payment method and details to complete the order process.
* **Form Submission** (Form Submit, Text, Required): Double-check all entered information and submit the form to complete your order.
