# Harvest Basket Contest Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your entry for the Harvest Basket Contest. It is essential to provide accurate information about your entry, as it will be evaluated based on your descriptions and photos.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Make sure to upload a clear and high-quality photo of your entry.
3. Select the correct type of entry from the options provided.
4. Choose the correct contact person or business from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Details** (`entry_details`, text, required: false): 
	+ Please provide a detailed description of your entry, including its name, ingredients, and any unique features.
* **Entry Photo** (`entry_photo`, text, required: false): 
	+ Upload a clear and high-quality photo of your entry that shows its appearance and any relevant details.
* **Contact** (`entry_contact`, select_one, required: false): 
	+ Choose whether your entry is a person or a business.
* **Type** (`entry_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the type of entry you are submitting. You can choose one or multiple options based on the category that best fits your entry.
