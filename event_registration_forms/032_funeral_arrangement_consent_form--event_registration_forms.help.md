# Funeral Arrangement Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist you in understanding the purpose of the Funeral Arrangement Consent Form and to walk you through how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and familiarize yourself with the fields and their descriptions.
2. Fill out the "Event Registration Form Data" field by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' to register for a service or event related to the funeral.
3. Write down any specific funeral wishes you have in the "funeral_wishes" field.
4. Indicate whether next of kin has been contacted by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the "contact_details" field.
5. Check the "funeral_arrangements" field to see if arrangements have been made or not.
6. Provide contact information for other family members who will be involved in the funeral in the "family_involvement" field.
7. Enter any specific details about the funeral home or other funeral service providers in the "funeral_home_details" field.
8. Make any special requests in the "special_requests" field.
9. Enter contact information for the next of kin in the "next_of_kin_contact" field.
10. Indicate whether other family members will be involved by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the "other_family_members" field.
11. Provide contact information for the funeral home or other funeral service providers in the "funeral_home_contact" field.
12. Enter payment information for funeral services in the "payment_info" field.
13. Sign the form by typing your name in the "signed_by" field.
14. Record the date and time you sign the form in the "date_signed" and "time_signed" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Registration Form Data** (`event_registration_form_data`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are registering for a service or event related to the funeral.
* **funeral_wishes** (`funeral_wishes`, text, required): Write down any specific wishes you have for your funeral arrangements.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether next of kin has been contacted or not.
* **funeral_arrangements** (`funeral_arrangements`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether funeral arrangements have been made or not.
* **family_involvement** (`family_involvement`, text, required): Provide contact information for other family members who will be involved in the funeral.
* **funeral_home_details** (`funeral_home_details`, text, required): Enter any specific details about the funeral home or other funeral service providers.
* **special_requests** (`special_requests`, text, required): Make any special requests for your funeral arrangements.
* **next_of_kin_contact** (`next_of_kin_contact`, text, required): Enter contact information for the next of kin.
* **other_family_members** (`other_family_members`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether other family members will be involved in the funeral.
* **funeral_home_contact** (`funeral_home_contact`, text, required): Enter contact information for the funeral home or other funeral service providers.
* **payment_info** (`payment_info`, text, required): Enter payment information for funeral services.
* **signed_by** (`signed_by`, text, required): Sign the form by typing your name.
* **date_signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Record the date you sign the form.
* **time_signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Record the time you sign the form.
