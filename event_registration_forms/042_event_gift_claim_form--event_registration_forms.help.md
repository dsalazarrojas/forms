# Event Gift Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help attendees submit claims for event-related gifts. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate response for each field based on your situation.
2. Enter the event information if applicable.
3. Provide any additional notes if needed.
4. Enter your contact information (Email and Phone).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Gift Claim** (`Event Gift Claim`, select_one): Choose "Yes" if you are claiming event gifts.
- **Attendee Info** (`Event Gift Claim Form Attendee Info`, select_multiple): Indicate if you are an attendee of the event, this is usually a yes or no response.
- **Event Info** (`Event Gift Claim Form Event Info`, select_multiple): Select the relevant information about the event.
- **Claim Status** (`Event Gift Claim Form Claim Status`, select_one): Choose the status of your claim, either "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Claim Date** (`Event Gift Claim Form Claim Date`, date): If you are claiming gifts, enter the date you claim them.
- **Claimed By** (`Event Gift Claim Form Claimed By`, select_one): Indicate if the claim was made by you.
- **Claimed To** (`Event Gift Claim Form Claimed To`, select_one): Indicate if the claim was made for someone else.
- **Claim Note** (`Event Gift Claim Form Claim Note`, note): Provide any additional comments or notes about your claim.
- **Email** (`Event Gift Claim Form Email`, email): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`Event Gift Claim Form Phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
- **Event** (`Event Gift Claim Form Event`, select_multiple): Select any related events.
