# Annual Flower Show Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from exhibitors for the Annual Flower Show Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above and fill out each section accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Name**: Please enter your full name. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. (Required)
* **Garden Club or Organization**: Enter your affiliated garden club or organization (if applicable). (Optional)
* **Primary Entry Category**: Select your main category (e.g., Roses, Perennials, Annuals, etc.). (Required)
* **Additional Entry Categories**: Select any other categories you'd like to enter. (Optional)
* **Flower or Plant Variety Name**: Enter the specific variety name of the flowers or plants you're entering. (Required)
* **Color or Type**: Enter the color or specific type of the flowers or plants. (Optional)
* **Cultivar or Hybrid Information**: Enter any additional information about the cultivar or hybrid. (Optional)
* **Number of Specimens Entering**: Enter the number of plants or arrangements you're entering. (Required)
* **Arrangement Size or Scale**: Select the size of your arrangement (e.g., Small, Medium, Large). (Optional)
* **Special Features or Achievements**: Enter any special features or achievements of your entries. (Optional)
* **Delivery or Self-Display**: Choose how you plan to deliver or display your entries (e.g., Delivery provided, Will deliver myself, Pickup available). (Required)
* **Preferred Delivery or Setup Time**: Enter the time you plan to deliver or display your entries. (Optional)
* **Special Setup or Display Requirements**: Enter any special requirements for displaying your entries. (Optional)
* **Entry Fee Amount**: Enter the fee per entry. (Optional)
* **Entry Fee Paid**: Confirm that you've paid the entry fee. (Required)
* **Acknowledgment of Rules and Judging Criteria**: Confirm that you agree to the show rules and judging criteria. (Required)
