# Community Pancake Breakfast Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Pancake Breakfast Registration form is used to collect information from attendees and their families for the event. This guide is designed to walk you through each field and provide guidance on completing it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1.  Select the "Pancake Breakfast Details" note section to read important information about the event, including price points for adults and children.
2.  Fill in the required contact information: Family or Primary Contact Name, Contact Phone Number, and Contact Email Address.
3.  Select your preferred payment method.
4.  Indicate if you'd like to volunteer for the event and choose your preferred volunteer shift.
5.  Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
6.  Enter the total number of tickets you plan to purchase.
7.  Optionally, provide special requests for seating or other accommodations.
8.  Finally, review the payment instructions and confirm your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Pancake Breakfast Details --**: This field provides additional information about the event details and rules for attendance.
*   **Family or Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the individual or family member responsible for this registration.
*   **Number of Adults Attending**: Enter the total number of adults attending the event.
*   **Number of Children Attending**: Enter the total number of children under 12 years old attending the event.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Select all applicable dietary restrictions or allergies you or your family members may have.
*   **Are you interested in volunteering for the event?**: Indicate if you or a family member is interested in volunteering for the event.
*   **Preferred Volunteer Shift**: If you've selected to volunteer, choose your preferred shift to help with event setup, serving, or cleanup.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number for the contact person.
*   **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address for the contact person.
*   **How will you be paying for tickets?**: Select your preferred payment method: online, at the door with cash, or at the door with credit card.
*   **Total Tickets to Purchase**: Enter the total number of tickets required for your attendance.
*   **-- Payment Instructions --**: Please review the payment instructions provided and confirm your payment details.
*   **Any special requests?**: If you have any special requests, such as seating or other accommodations, please let us know.
*   **-- See You There! --**: This field serves as a thank-you note for your participation and support of the community event.
