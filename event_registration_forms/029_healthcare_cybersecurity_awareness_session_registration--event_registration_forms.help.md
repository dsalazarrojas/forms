# Healthcare Cybersecurity Awareness Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for a healthcare cybersecurity awareness session. The goal is to collect relevant information from participants, such as session details, date, and training type, to ensure an efficient and informative experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1:** Begin by selecting the training type that suits your needs from the "Training Type" dropdown menu.
- **Step 2:** Enter the session date in the "Session Date" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Step 3:** Enter your participant information: name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
- **Step 4:** Write any additional comments or notes about your participation in the "Participant Notes" field.
- **Step 5:** Review the form carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Details**: Please provide a brief description of the session you are registering for.
- **Session Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the session. Format is YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Training Type**: Select one of the available options: Cybersecurity Fundamentals, Advanced Threat Protection, or Compliance and Risk Management.
- **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
- **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as the participant.
- **Participant Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
- **Participant Notes**: Write any additional comments or notes about your participation, if needed.
- **Submit**: This is the final step to complete your registration. A click on this button will submit your registration for review.
  - **Back**: This button allows you to move back to the previous page, which might be redundant in this form as there's no multiple pages.
