# End Of Season Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and provide details about end of season events. It helps event organizers and administrators to collect necessary information about the event, making it easier to manage and track the event's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and email address, which are required fields.
2. Select the correct event type for your event.
3. Enter the date and time of the event.
4. Specify the number of participants expected.
5. Choose a payment gateway for your event.
6. Enter the total amount of the event fees.
7. Select the payment method for your event.
8. Provide any additional event details.
9. Select any additional registration form options.
10. Choose the payment status for your event.
11. Select any participant details.
12. Read and agree to the terms and conditions and privacy policy.
13. Enter any additional event description and payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact** (4, text, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Event Type** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of event this is (e.g., End of Season Event).
* **Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (7, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (8, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Participants** (9, number, required): Enter the number of participants expected.
* **Payment Gateway** (10, select_one, required): Select the payment gateway to use for this event.
* **Amount** (11, number, required): Enter the total amount for this event.
* **Payment Method** (12, select_one, required): Select the payment method for this event.
* **Event Details** (13, text, required): Enter any additional event details.
* **Registration Form** (14, select_multiple, required): Select any additional registration form options.
* **Payment Status** (15, select_one, required): Choose the payment status for your event.
* **Participant Details** (16, select_multiple, required): Select any participant details.
* **Terms and Conditions** (17, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy** (18, note, required): Read and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Help Text** (19, note, required): Read this for any help or additional information.
* **Terms and Conditions Text** (20, text, required): Enter any additional terms and conditions text.
* **Privacy Policy Text** (21, text, required): Enter any additional privacy policy text.
* **Event Description** (22, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Details Text** (23, text, required): Enter any additional event details text.
* **Payment Terms** (24, text, required): Enter any additional payment terms text.
* **Payment Terms Text** (25, text, required): Enter any additional payment terms text.
