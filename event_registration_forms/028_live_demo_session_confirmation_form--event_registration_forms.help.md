# Live Demo Session Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm participants' participation in a live demo session. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section with relevant information about the session, such as session title and description.
2. Provide your **Participant Details** in the corresponding section, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. Select one of the options in the **Confirmation** section to confirm your participation.
4. If necessary, provide additional comments or information about your participation in the **Additional Comments** field.
5. In the **Follow-up Information** section, select the follow-up method and provide any additional details as requested.
