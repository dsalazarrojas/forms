# Club Leadership Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Leadership Voting Form is designed to collect votes for club leadership positions. It allows users to select their preferred candidates for president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, and other positions. The form also includes fields for voter identification, verification, and submission confirmation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the **Your Name** and **Email Address** fields, respectively. These are required fields for verification purposes.
2. Enter your Student ID Number to verify your membership.
3. Confirm your membership by selecting **Yes, I am a current club member**.
4. Select the **Election Date** and **Academic Year - Election Term** for the current election.
5. Choose your preferred candidates for the leadership positions:
	* **President**: Select one of the candidates or choose **Abstain - no preference** if you don't want to vote.
	* **Vice President**: Select one of the candidates or choose **Abstain - no preference** if you don't want to vote.
	* **Secretary**: Select one of the candidates or choose **Abstain - no preference** if you don't want to vote.
	* **Treasurer**: Select one of the candidates or choose **Abstain - no preference** if you don't want to vote.
	* **Board Members - At Large**: Select up to three candidates from the list.
6. If applicable, choose your preferred candidates for **Events Director** and **Communications Officer**.
7. Confirm that your votes are accurate and complete by selecting **Yes, votes are accurate**.
8. Confirm your submission by selecting **Yes, submit my ballot**.
9. Optionally, request to vote anonymously by selecting **Yes, vote anonymously**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name**: This is the name of the person submitting the vote. (Required)
- **Email Address**: This is the email address associated with your account. (Required)
- **Email Address (For verification purposes)**: This is an explanation of the purpose of the field.
- **Student ID Number**: This is used to verify membership. (Required)
- **I am a current club member**: Confirm your membership. (Required)
- **Election Date**: The date of the current election. (Required)
- **Academic Year - Election Term**: The academic year of the election. (Required)
- **Vote for President**: Select your preferred candidate for president. (Required)
- **Vote for Vice President**: Select your preferred candidate for vice president. (Required)
- **Vote for Secretary**: Select your preferred candidate for secretary. (Required)
- **Vote for Treasurer**: Select your preferred candidate for treasurer. (Required)
- **Vote for Board Members - At Large**: Select up to three candidates for board members. (Optional)
- **Vote for Events Director**: Select your preferred candidate for events director. (Optional)
- **Vote for Communications Officer**: Select your preferred candidate for communications officer. (Optional)
- **My votes are accurate and complete**: Confirm your votes. (Required)
- **I confirm submission of this ballot**: Confirm your submission. (Required)
- **Time of Vote Submission**: This field is currently not used and can be ignored. (Not Required)
- **Request anonymous voting - do not attach to name**: Select to vote anonymously. (Required)
- **Yes, more positions follow**: Only visible if other positions are available to vote on, in which case, a list of additional positions appears, and you can select **No, voting complete** to stop.
