# Zoom Link Request For Info Session - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a Zoom link for an information session. It helps us collect essential details from attendees about their session preferences, ensuring that the session runs smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out your information, including session date, session time, attendee name, session details, and other relevant details.
2. Choose the session type and attendees.
3. Provide your email address and phone number for us to contact you.
4. Confirm your attendance and whether you'd like to receive the Zoom link.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your desired session. Please ensure that this date is correctly reflected in the form to avoid conflicts or miscommunications.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of your session.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for our records.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required: false): Add any extra details about your session, such as its purpose or objectives.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you will be attending with others or solo.
* **Zoom Link** (`zoom_link`, select_one, required: false): Let us know if you'd like to receive the Zoom link.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Add your phone number for our contact.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): If you're attending on behalf of a school, enter the name here.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, text, required: false): Specify the type of session you're requesting, such as a meeting or presentation.
* **Other** (`session_type_other`, text, required: false): If your session type doesn't match the options above, describe it here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your session details are accurate and clear.
- If you're requesting multiple sessions, fill out this form multiple times with different session dates and times.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
