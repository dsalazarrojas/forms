# Production Optimization Software Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Production Optimization Software Fair. It is used to gather details about event organizers and attendees, including their names, emails, phone numbers, interests, and event details such as dates, times, and venues. The form also tracks registration status and event status for each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields for event organizers, including event organizer name, email, phone number, and interests.
2. Enter attendee information, including attendee name, email, phone number, and interests.
3. Provide event details such as date, time, venue, and description.
4. Select the registration status and event status for each attendee.
5. Enter the registration date for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Interests** (`event_organizer_interests`, `text`, required: false): Enter the interests of the event organizer.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the attendee.
* **Attendee Interests** (`attendee_interests`, `text`, required: false): Enter the interests of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the attendee.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, `text`, required: false): Enter the venue of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of registration.
* **Attendee Registration Status** (`attendee_registration_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the registration status of the attendee (Registered, Not Registered, or Pending).
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the event (Live, Scheduled, Canceled, or Ended).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
* Double-check the attendee registration status and event status to ensure accurate representation of event information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizer or the event management team for clarification.
