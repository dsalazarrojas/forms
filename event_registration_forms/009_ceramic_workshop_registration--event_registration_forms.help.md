# Ceramic Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceramic Workshop Registration form is designed to gather essential information from attendees interested in participating in our ceramic workshops. This information helps us prepare for the workshop and ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to attend the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Provide your email address for workshop updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age category.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred workshop from the available options.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date you prefer for your workshop session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred time slot for the session.
* **Ceramics Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Describe your level of experience with ceramics.
* **Previous Workshops** (`previous_workshops`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have attended our workshops before.
* **Apron Size** (`apron_size`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred apron size for workshop use.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Let us know about any accessibility requirements you may have.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method for the workshop fee.
* **Workshop Fee Acknowledgment** (`workshop_fee`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to pay the workshop fee at registration.
* **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Review and agree to our cancellation policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept our liability waiver.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to photos being taken during the workshop.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you became aware of our workshop.
* **Special Requests or Dietary Needs** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Share any special requests or dietary needs we should be aware of.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask or contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your attention to detail and accuracy in filling out this form.
