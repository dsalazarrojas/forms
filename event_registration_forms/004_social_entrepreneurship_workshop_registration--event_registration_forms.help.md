# Social Entrepreneurship Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Social Entrepreneurship Workshop. It is intended for workshop participants to register and provide details about themselves. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the 'Submit' button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the participant details form with your information.
3. Select your motivations and accessibility needs.
4. Enter your contact information.
5. Review and confirm your submission before clicking 'Submit'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): This section is where you will enter your basic information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please fill out this section completely and accurately.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, select_multiple, required/true): This is where you will select the reasons why you want to participate in the workshop. Choose all that apply to your situation.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required/true): Here, you will select if you have any accessibility needs we should be aware of so that we can accommodate them.
* **Participant Details 2**, **Participant Details 3**, **Participant Details 4**, **Participant Details 5** (`participant_details_2`, `participant_details_3`, `participant_details_4`, `participant_details_5`, text, optional/true): These sections are where you will provide additional details about yourself. You can choose to fill them out if you have more information to share.
* **Name 2**, **Name 3** (`name_2`, `name_3`, text, optional/true): If you have a secondary name that you would like to provide, you can do so in these sections.
* **Email 2**, **Email 3** (`email_2`, `email_3`, email, optional/true): If you have a secondary email address that you would like to provide, you can do so in these sections.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional/true): If you have a secondary phone number that you would like to provide, you can do so in this section.
* **University 2, **University 3** (`university_2`, `university_3`, text, optional/true): If you are associated with a university, you can provide that information here.
* **Incubator 2, **Incubator 3** (`incubator_2`, `incubator_3`, text, optional/true): If you are associated with an incubator, you can provide that information here.
* **Non Governmental Organization 2, **Non Governmental Organization 3** (`non_governmental_organization_2`, `non_governmental_organization_3`, text, optional/true): If you are associated with a non-governmental organization, you can provide that information here.
* **Community Group 2, **Community Group 3** (`community_group_2`, `community_group_3`, text, optional/true): If you are associated with a community group, you can provide that information here.
