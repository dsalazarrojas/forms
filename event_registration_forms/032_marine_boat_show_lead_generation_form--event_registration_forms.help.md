# Marine Boat Show Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential attendees of the Marine Boat Show, enabling us to provide them with more information about the event and follow-up communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Marine Boat Show Lead Generation** (`event_registration_form_1`): A description of the Marine Boat Show, which should be filled out in order to understand what the form is about.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Please enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Please enter your name as the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`): Please enter the address of your company or organization.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Please select the date of the Marine Boat Show you are interested in attending.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Please select the time of the Marine Boat Show you are interested in attending.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Please enter the location of the Marine Boat Show you are interested in attending.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`): Please enter the name of the event organizer or the person in charge of the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us.
* This form is intended for one-time use, so please do not submit it multiple times.
