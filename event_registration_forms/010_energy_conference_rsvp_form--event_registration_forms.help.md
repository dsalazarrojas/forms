# Energy Conference RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee information and preferences for the Energy Conference. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that you receive all relevant updates and information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the first two fields. These fields are required, so make sure to provide your full name.
2. Enter your email address in the third field. This will help us contact you about the conference and any updates.
3. Enter your phone number in the fourth field. This will help us contact you in case of any last-minute changes or updates.
4. Indicate your interest in attending specific sessions by selecting your choices in the fifth field. You can select multiple sessions.
5. If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please let us know by selecting your options in the sixth field.
6. If you have any dietary needs or requirements, please select your options in the seventh field.
7. Finally, let us know if you plan to attend the conference by selecting your response in the eighth field.
