# Science Fair Access Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Science Fair Access Subscription Form allows educators and administrators to register events for science fairs, track participant information, and manage updates and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event(s) you wish to register using the `Event Registration Forms` field.
2. Enter your educator or administrator email address in the `Educator E-mail` field.
3. Provide the start and end dates for the event in the `Start Date` and `End Date` fields, respectively.
4. Enter a brief title for the event in the `Event Title` field.
5. Select the science topic related to the event using the `Science Topic` field.
6. Provide a description of the event in the `Event Description` field.
7. Fill in the participant's name in the `Participant Name` field.
8. Enter the participant's email address in the `Participant E-mail` field.
9. Enter the participant's phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
10. Provide the participant's school name in the `Participant School` field.
11. Enter the date of the science fair in the `Science Fair Date` field.
12. Select the time of the science fair in the `Science Fair Time` field.
13. Enter any notes or comments about the participant in the `Science Fair Notes` field.
14. Select the tool assigned to the participant in the `Assigned Tool` field.
15. Enter the name of the teacher assigned to the participant in the `Assigned Teacher` field.
16. Specify the number of students participating in the event in the `Student Group Size` field.
17. Select the participant's status in the `Participant Status` field.
18. Add any comments or feedback about the participant in the `Participant Comments` field.
19. Select the frequency of science fair updates in the `Science Fair Update Frequency` field.
20. Enter any update message or notes for the science fair in the `Science Fair Update Message` and `Science Fair Update Note` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Forms**: Select all events you wish to register using this field. You can select multiple events.
* **Educator E-mail**: Enter your educator or administrator email address.
* **Start Date**: Provide the start date of the event.
* **End Date**: Provide the end date of the event.
* **Event Title**: Enter a brief title for the event.
* **Science Topic**: Select the science topic related to the event.
* **Event Description**: Provide a description of the event.
* **Participant Name**: Fill in the participant's name.
* **Participant E-mail**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant School**: Enter the participant's school name.
* **Science Fair Date**: Enter the date of the science fair.
* **Science Fair Time**: Select the time of the science fair.
* **Science Fair Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the science fair.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the participant.
* **Assigned Teacher**: Enter the name of the teacher assigned to the participant.
* **Student Group Size**: Specify the number of students participating in the event.
* **Participant Status**: Select the participant's status.
* **Participant Comments**: Add any comments or feedback about the participant.
* **Science Fair Update Frequency**: Select the frequency of science fair updates.
* **Science Fair Update Message**: Enter any update message or notes for the science fair.
* **Science Fair Update Note**: Enter any update note for the science fair.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have authorization to access and manage science fair events before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
* Double-check your selections for `Event Registration Forms` and `Science Fair Update Frequency` to avoid mistakes.
* Enter valid email addresses in the `Educator E-mail`, `Participant E-mail`, and `Participant Phone` fields.
* Enter a brief title for the `Event Title` field to help with organization and identification.
* Select the correct `Science Topic` related to the event to help with sorting and categorization.
* Use the `Science Fair Notes` field to record any important information or reminders.
* Be clear and concise in the `Science Fair Update Message` and `Science Fair Update Note` fields to ensure effective communication.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
