# entertainment industry gala rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs from individuals in the entertainment industry for an upcoming gala event. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending and make any necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the required fields. You can navigate between pages using the buttons at the top or bottom of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Details**
(Event Details, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the event, the date, and any other relevant details about the event you'd like to share.

### **Guest Details**
(Guest Details, text, required/optional): If you're attending with someone, please provide their name and any other relevant details about them.

### **Headcount**
(Headcount, number, required/optional): Enter the number of people in your party attending the event.

### **Preferences**
(Preferences, select multiple, required): Indicate whether you will attend, whether you regret to inform us that you won't be able to attend, or if you're unsure and need further information.

### **Comments**
(Comments, note, required/optional): If you have any comments or special requests for the event, please feel free to add them here.

### **Company**
(Company, text, required/optional): Please enter your company name if applicable.

### **Job Title**
(Job Title, text, required/optional): Please enter your job title if applicable.

### **Industry**
(Industry, select one, required/optional): Select the industry you work in from the options provided.

### **Attending with**
(Attending with, select multiple, required/optional): Specify who you are attending with, if anyone. Choose from the options provided.

### **Notes**
(Notes, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about your attendance.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate headcount and event arrangements.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, please use the comments field to let us know.
* If you're unsure about any of the options or have further questions, feel free to contact the event organizers directly.
