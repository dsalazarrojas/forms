# improv_festival_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Improv Festival Sponsorship Form is designed for individuals or companies that want to participate as sponsors in the Improv Festival event. The form is used to gather necessary information from sponsors to facilitate the event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the sponsorship level you are interested in (Sponsor Level).
2. Provide your primary product category and subcategory (Primary Product Category and Primary Product Sub Category).
3. Enter your branding details (Branding Details).
4. Upload your branding image (Branding Image).
5. Provide your signed approval (Signed Approval).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsorship Selections (<type> Select Multiple, <required> False):** Select one or more sponsorship levels that best represent your organization or product. This will help us understand your level of commitment and involvement in the festival.
* **Branding Details (<type> Text, <required> False):** Please provide a short description of your brand, including your name, logo, and any relevant information about your products or services.
* **Branding Image (<type> Text, <required> False):** Upload your company logo or any other image that represents your brand.
* **Signed Approval (<type> Text, <required> False):** Please provide a signed approval from your authorized representative, confirming that you have the permission to use this form. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or errors.
- If you are not sure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
- The form will be reviewed before approval, so please be patient and allow some time for processing.
