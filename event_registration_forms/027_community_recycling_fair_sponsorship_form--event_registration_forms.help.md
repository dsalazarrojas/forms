# Community Recycling Fair Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential sponsors of the Community Recycling Fair. It will help us understand your sponsorship level, contact information, and promotional items you will provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each field carefully and follow the instructions below:

* Fill out all required fields (indicated by \*\*Required\*\*).
* Choose your preferred sponsorship level and payment method.
* If you answered "Yes" to having a promotional booth, provide a description of any special requirements.
* If you are providing promotional items for attendee swag bags, describe them in the text field.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Sponsoring Organization** (`sponsoring_organization_name`, *text*, **Required**): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Primary Contact Full Name** (`primary_contact_full_name`, *text*, **Required**): Enter the full name of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Email Address** (`sponsor_contact_email`, *email*, **Required**): Enter the email address of your primary contact person.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`sponsor_contact_phone`, *text*, **Required**): Enter the phone number of your primary contact person.
* **Select Your Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level_selection`, *select_one*, **Required**):
	+ Choose from:
		- Platinum - $2500
		- Gold - $1000
		- Silver - $500
		- Bronze - $250
		- Friend of Fair - $100
* **Is this a recurring annual sponsorship?** (`recurring_sponsorship_check`, *select_one*, **Required**):
	+ Choose from:
		- True
		- False
* **Company Website Address** (`company_website_url`, *text*, Not Required): Enter the URL of your website (if applicable).
* **Short Company Description** (`company_description_text`, *text*, Not Required): Enter a short description of your organization for promotional purposes.
* **Would you like a promotional booth at the fair?** (`booth_request_check`, *select_one*, **Required**):
	+ Choose from:
		- Yes - I want a booth
		- No - I don't need a booth
* **Special Booth Requirements** (`booth_requirements_info`, *text*, Not Required): If you answered "Yes" to having a promotional booth, enter any special requirements here.
* **Will you be providing items for attendee swag bags?** (`promotional_items_check`, *select_one*, **Required**):
	+ Choose from:
		- True
		- False
* **Description of Promotional Items to be Provided** (`promo_items_description`, *text*, Not Required): If you are providing promotional items, describe them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_selection`, *select_one*, **Required**):
	+ Choose from:
		- Credit Card Online
		- Mail a Check
		- Request an Invoice
* **Sponsorship Benefits Summary** (`sponsorship_benefits_note`, *note*, Not Required): A note summarizing the benefits of sponsoring the Community Recycling Fair.
* **Today's Date** (`sponsorship_submission_date`, *date*, **Required**): Enter the current date in MM/DD/YYYY format.

Note: The fields marked as "Not Required" are optional and only need to be filled out if the corresponding option was chosen earlier in the form.
