# gala-day-participation-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gala-day-participation-survey form is used to gather feedback and information from participants after an event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the Introduction page, which provides context for the survey.
2. On the second page, provide the name of the event you attended.
3. On the third page, select the date and time of the event you attended.
4. On the fourth page, answer the feedback question in the text field.
5. On the fifth page, provide your feedback response in the text field.
6. On the sixth page, rate your overall experience (rating-1, rating-2, or rating-3) by selecting one of the options.
7. On the seventh page, type any additional comments you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This page provides context for the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Event Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter the name of the event you attended. This will help us to better understand the specific event you participated in.
* **Event Date** (`3`, `date`, required: `false`): Select the date on which the event took place. This will help us to understand the timing of your participation.
* **Event Time** (`4`, `time`, required: `false`): Select the time at which the event occurred. This will help us to better understand the schedule of the event.
* **Feedback Question** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Please answer the question in the text field provided. This will help us to gather more information about your experience.
* **Feedback Response** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide your response to the feedback question in the text field. This will help us to better understand your thoughts and feelings about the event.
* **Rating** (`7`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Rate your overall experience by selecting one of the options (rating-1, rating-2, or rating-3).
* **Comments** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the text field.
