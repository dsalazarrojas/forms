# watercolor_course_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a student for a watercolor course. It collects personal details and course selection information from the student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Student Details" page to begin.
2. Fill out the required fields as prompted.
3. Select your course choice on the next page.
4. Enter your start and end dates on the following pages.
5. Enter your instructor name if you have selected a course with a specific instructor.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Details (Student Details)**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter your personal details. This includes any information required for your course enrollment.
* **Course Choice (course_choice)**: This is the second page of the form where you will select the course you want to enroll in. Please choose between the options 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you want to enroll in the course.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: This is the third page of the form where you will enter the start date of your course. Please select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date (end_date)**: This is the fourth page of the form where you will enter the end date of your course. Please select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Instructor Name (instructor_name)**: This is the fifth page of the form where you will enter the name of your instructor if you have selected a course with a specific instructor. Please enter the full name of your instructor.
* **Submit**: Click on this button to complete and submit your form. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* Check your course selection carefully before submitting the form.
