# Cultural Festival Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Attendance Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees of a cultural festival. This form helps us understand your preferences, medical conditions, and emergency contact details, ensuring that we can provide an enjoyable and safe experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in its entirety, starting with the first page.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled out. Fields marked as "required" must be completed.
*   Provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields, as this will help us better understand your needs.
*   Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**: Enter your name, email address, and other contact details in this field.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Contact Details**: Enter the contact information of the person accompanying you or responsible for your care in case of an emergency.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Event Attendance**: Confirm your attendance at the festival and any plans to attend other events in the venue.
    *   This field is required.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the contact information of the person responsible for your care in case of an emergency.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Medical Information**: Select any medical conditions you may have or that may affect your ability to participate in the festival.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Emergency Medical Information**: If applicable, provide any emergency medical information related to your medical conditions.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Event Preferences**: Select any preferences or restrictions you may have for the festival experience.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your festival experience.
    *   This field is optional.
*   **Submit**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click "Submit" to complete your form.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   We are committed to ensuring your comfort and safety during the festival.
