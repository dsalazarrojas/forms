# startups_management_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The startups management training form is a registration form used by participants to register for a startups management training event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the training name in the **training_name** field.
* Enter the training date in the **training_date** field (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
* Enter the training time in the **training_time** field (in the format `HH:MM:SS`).
* Enter your name in the **participant_name** field.
* Enter your email address in the **participant_email** field.
* Enter your phone number in the **participant_phone** field.
* Provide any additional notes or information in the **participant_note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **training_name**: Enter the name of the training you are registering for. (text, required: false)
* **training_date**: Enter the date of the training in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. (date, required: false)
* **training_time**: Enter the time of the training in the format `HH:MM:SS`. (time, required: false)
* **participant_name**: Enter your name to help us identify you as a participant. (text, required: false)
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you. (email, required: false)
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number to help us reach you. (text, required: false)
* **participant_note**: Enter any additional notes or information about yourself. (note, required: false)
