# theater_preservation_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about donations made to the theater preservation organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's pages to ensure all required and optional sections are visible.
2. Enter any relevant information on the first page, "Form Information".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Information (theater_preservation_donation_form_page1)
* **Form Information** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your donation, including the purpose or specific cause you're supporting.

### Donation Amount (donation_amount)
* **Donation Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount you are willing to donate. Please note that this is not a payment field, it's for tracking purposes only.

### Donation Frequency (donation_frequency)
* **Donation Frequency** (select multiple, optional): Select the frequency at which you'd like to make your donation. This can be Daily, Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly. You can select one or multiple options.

### Donation Date (donation_date)
* **Donation Date** (date, optional): Select the specific date you'd like to make your donation. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled before submitting the form.
* Review your entries for any errors before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our team for help.
