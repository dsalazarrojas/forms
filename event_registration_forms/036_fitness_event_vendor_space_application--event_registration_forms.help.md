# Fitness Event Vendor Space Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for vendors who want to rent a space for a fitness event. We will use the information collected to review and process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide all required information.
3. Ask us if you have any questions or need help filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details (Vendor Details)**:
	+ **Vendor Details**: Enter your company's name, contact person, and any other relevant details.
* **Vendor Space Size (Vendor Space Size)**:
	+ Select your vendor space size from the options provided (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Insurance Status (Insurance Status)**:
	+ Check 'True' if you have insurance, 'False' otherwise.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests)**:
	+ Select any special requests you may have from the options provided.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)**:
	+ Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**:
	+ Enter your company's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**:
	+ Enter your company's contact phone number.
* **Company Name (Company Name)**:
	+ Enter your company's name.
* **Company Address (Company Address)**:
	+ Enter your company's address.
* **Company Website (Company Website)**:
	+ Enter your company's website.
* **Special Requests Details (Special Requests Details)**:
	+ Enter any details related to your special requests.
* **Event Details (Event Details)**:
	+ Enter any relevant event details that you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help us process your application efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
