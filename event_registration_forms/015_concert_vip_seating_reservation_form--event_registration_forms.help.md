# Concert VIP Seating Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to reserve a premium VIP experience for the concert event. It will guide you through selecting the best package for your needs and preferences, entering guest information, and finalizing your payment and reservation details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select Your VIP Package**: Choose from our Gold, Platinum, Diamond, or Ultimate VIP packages to customize your experience.
2. **Enter Event Details**: Provide the concert name and date for your event.
3. **Enter Guest Information**: Enter the name, email, and phone number of the primary guest in your party.
4. **Choose Your Experience**: Select from various experiences, including early entry, backstage access, meet-and-greet, complimentary drinks, gourmet catering, reserved parking, and merchandise credit.
5. **Enter Additional Details**: Enter any special occasion, dietary requirements, and payment method details.
6. **Confirm and Complete**: Review your selections and confirm your payment and reservation details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. VIP Seating Reservation**: This is a not required text field where you can add any information regarding your reservation.
* **2. VIP Package Selection**: Choose from our Gold, Platinum, Diamond, or Ultimate VIP packages.
	+ Gold VIP Package
	+ Platinum VIP Package
	+ Diamond VIP Experience
	+ Ultimate VIP All Access
* **3. Event Name**: Enter the name of the concert event.
* **4. Event Date**: Enter the performance date of the concert.
* **5. Number of VIP Guests**: Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **6. VIP Benefits Included**: This is a not required text field where you can add any information regarding the benefits of your package.
* **7. Premium Seating Location**: Choose the best seats in the house for your party.
	+ Front Row Center
	+ Front Row Side
	+ VIP Box Suite
	+ Stage Side Premium
* **8. Early Entry Access**: Choose to enter the venue early before general admission.
	+ Yes Include Early Entry
	+ No Standard Entry
* **9. Backstage Access**: Choose to tour the backstage areas.
	+ Yes Add Backstage Tour
	+ No Thanks
* **10. Meet and Greet**: Choose to meet the artists.
	+ Yes Include Meet and Greet
	+ No Thanks
* **11. Complimentary Drinks**: Choose from Standard Bar Package, Premium Bar Package, or Unlimited VIP Bar.
* **12. Gourmet Catering**: Choose from Light Refreshments, Full Dinner Service, or Premium Buffet.
* **13. Reserved Parking**: Choose to reserve a parking spot.
	+ Yes Include Parking
	+ No Parking Needed
* **14. Merchandise Credit**: Choose from $50, $100, or $200 credit.
* **15. Guest Details**: This is a not required text field where you can add any information regarding your guests.
* **16. Primary Guest Name**: Enter the full name of the primary guest in your party.
* **17. Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary guest for confirmation purposes.
* **18. Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the primary guest.
* **19. Special Occasion**: Choose the reason for your special celebration.
	+ Birthday
	+ Anniversary
	+ Corporate Event
	+ Just a Special Night
	+ Other
* **20. Dietary Requirements**: Enter any food allergies or preferences.
* **21. Payment Details**: This is a not required text field where you can add any payment details.
* **22. Package Price**: This field will display the total price of your selected package.
* **23. Deposit Amount**: Enter the required deposit amount for your package.
* **24. Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Corporate Account
* **25. Reservation Terms**: Confirm that you accept the VIP terms.
	+ I Accept VIP Terms
	+ I Do Not Accept
