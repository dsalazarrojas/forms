# Best Mentorship Program Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Mentorship Program Nomination Form is designed to collect relevant information about a mentorship program that you would like to nominate for the award. Please provide detailed answers to help the evaluation committee assess the program's qualities and impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are nominating a program that meets the award criteria and is not being re-nominated for the same award in the same year.
2. Fill in the nominator's information (name, organization, and contact email).
3. Provide detailed information about the mentorship program, including its name, organizing body, type, and year founded.
4. Rate the program's structure and organization, mentor training, matching process, resources, and support.
5. Provide information about the program's outcomes and impact.
6. Summarize why the nominated program should win the award.
7. Provide contact information for a professional reference who can verify the program's results.
8. Finally, submit the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Your Organization (3)**: Type the name of your organization or institution.
* **Your Contact Email (4)**: Enter your professional email address that the evaluation committee can use to contact you.
* **Mentorship Program Name (6)**: Enter the official name of the program being nominated.
* **Organizing Body or Company (7)**: Type the name of the organization or company that runs the program.
* **Program Type (8)**: Select the primary category for this mentorship program:
	+ Internal Corporate Program
	+ University-based Mentorship
	+ Industry-wide Association Program
	+ Nonprofit Initiative
	+ Peer-to-Peer Network
	+ Specialized Bootcamp or Accelerator
	+ Other
* **Year the Program was Founded (9)**: Enter the approximate year the program was established.
* **Program Structure and Organization (10)**: Rate the program's structure and organization from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Mentor Training Quality (11)**: Rate the quality of mentor training from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Matching Process Effectiveness (12)**: Rate the effectiveness of the matching process from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Resources and Support for Participants (13)**: Rate the resources and support provided to participants from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Overall Participant Satisfaction (14)**: Rate the overall satisfaction of participants from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Measurable Impact on Careers or Education (15)**: Provide detailed information about the program's measurable impact.
* **Diversity and Inclusion in the Program (16)**: Describe how the program promotes diversity and inclusion.
* **Why this Program Should Win the Award (17)**: Summarize the key reasons for the nomination.
* **Professional Reference Contact (18)**: Provide the name and contact information of a professional reference who can verify the program's results.
* **Date of Nomination (19)**: Enter the date of nomination.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in before submitting the form.
