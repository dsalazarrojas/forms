# CBT Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for the CBT Innovation Workshop. The workshop is a professional development opportunity for individuals interested in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is where you will receive confirmation and any additional information about the workshop.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Include the area code.
4. Enter the name of your organization or institution in the "Organization Name" field.
5. Provide your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. If applicable, enter your professional license number in the "Professional License Number" field. This is required for Continuing Education Units (CEU) credits.
7. Select your preferred workshop session from the "Select Workshop Session" field.
8. Choose your level of CBT experience from the "CBT Experience Level" field.
9. If required, select your dietary requirements in the "Dietary Requirements" field.
10. If required, provide any accessibility needs in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
11. Check if you require Continuing Education Units (CEU) credits by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Require CEU Credits" field.
12. Select your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
13. The standard registration fee is noted in the "Registration Fee" field. If you need further information, please contact us.
14. Finally, confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes I Agree" or "No I Do Not Agree" in the "I Agree to the Terms and Conditions" field.
15. Add any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): We will send confirmation and additional information to this email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Include the area code.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Your workplace or institution.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Your current position.
* **Professional License Number** (`professional_license`, `text`, optional): If applicable for CEU credits.
* **Select Workshop Session** (`workshop_session`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred workshop session.
* **CBT Experience Level** (`cbt_experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of familiarity with Cognitive Behavioral Therapy.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your dietary requirements for catering purposes.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Let us know any accessibility needs we can accommodate.
* **Require CEU Credits** (`ceu_credits_required`, `select_one`, required): Check if you require Continuing Education Units (CEU) credits.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, `note`, optional): Note: The standard registration fee is $299 per attendee.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.
