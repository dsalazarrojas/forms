# Book Club Reading Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for members to submit book suggestions for future reads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Full Name" field with your name.
2. Enter your "Email Address" and ensure it's correct.
3. If you're a member, enter your "Membership ID" to receive special perks.
4. Select the "Selection Period" of the book you're suggesting (e.g., March 2026).
5. Enter the "Suggested Book Title" of the book you're recommending.
6. Specify the "Book Author" of the suggested book.
7. Choose the "Book Genre" that best matches the book's category.
8. Enter the "Publication Year" of the book (if known).
9. Provide a 2-3 sentence "Brief Book Description" of the book.
10. Explain "Why Should We Read This Book" in 1-2 sentences.
11. Choose the "Approximate Book Length" of the book (e.g., Under 200 pages).
12. Select the "Reading Difficulty Level" of the book (e.g., Easy Read).
13. Choose any "Content Warnings" that apply to the book.
14. Select where the book is available (e.g., Available at Library).
15. Express your "Vote Preference" for the book (e.g., Strongly Want to Read).
16. If applicable, provide "Alternative Book Suggestions" for other books you like.
17. Choose if you'd like to help prepare "Discussion Questions" for the book.
18. Finally, enter the "Submission Date" of your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email for communication.
* **Membership ID**: Enter your unique member ID if you're a registered member. (This is optional.)
* **Selection Period**: Choose the time period for the book selection (e.g., March 2026).
* **Suggested Book Title**: Enter the title of the book you're suggesting.
* **Book Author**: Enter the author of the book.
* **Book Genre**: Select the genre of the book (e.g., Fiction, Romance).
* **Publication Year**: Enter the year of publication (if known).
* **Brief Book Description**: Provide a 2-3 sentence summary of the book.
* **Why Should We Read This Book**: Explain why you think the book is worth reading.
* **Approximate Book Length**: Choose the book's length (e.g., Under 200 pages).
* **Reading Difficulty Level**: Select the difficulty level of the book (e.g., Easy Read).
* **Content Warnings**: Select any sensitive content that applies to the book.
* **Book Availability**: Choose where the book can be obtained (e.g., Available at Library).
* **Vote Preference**: Express your enthusiasm for the book (e.g., Strongly Want to Read).
* **Alternative Book Suggestions**: Suggest other books you like.
* **Help Prepare Discussion Questions**: Choose if you'd like to help create discussion questions for the book.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of your book suggestion.
