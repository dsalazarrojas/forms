# Space Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for parents to provide accommodation details for their child attending Space Camp. The information collected will be used to ensure their child's stay is comfortable and enjoyable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your parent and child's basic information:
	* Enter the Parent First Name in the "Parent First Name" field.
	* Enter the Parent Last Name in the "Parent Last Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field.
2. Provide your child's basic information:
	* Enter the Child First Name in the "Child First Name" field.
	* Enter the Child Last Name in the "Child Last Name" field.
3. Choose the camp dates your child will attend:
	* Select one or more dates from the "Camp Dates" field.
4. Select an accommodation type for your child:
	* Choose between "Host Family", "Self-Managed", or "On Site" in the "Accommodation Type" field.
5. Provide accommodation details:
	* Enter the address in the "Address" field.
	* Enter the zip code in the "Zip" field.
	* Enter the city in the "City" field.
	* Enter the state in the "State" field.
	* Enter the country in the "Country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent First Name (1)**: Please enter your first name.
* **Parent Last Name (2)**: Please enter your last name.
* **Parent Email (3)**: Please enter your email address.
* **Child First Name (4)**: Please enter your child's first name.
* **Child Last Name (5)**: Please enter your child's last name.
* **Camp Dates (6)**: Select one or more dates your child will attend the camp.
*   *   Please note that you can select one or more dates.
* **Accommodation Type (7)**: Choose between "Host Family", "Self-Managed", or "On Site" to determine your child's accommodation.
*   *   "Host Family" indicates your child will stay with a host family.
*   *   "Self-Managed" indicates your child will stay with you or another designated caregiver.
*   *   "On Site" indicates your child will stay in a camp-provided accommodation.
* **Address (8)**: Enter the address of the accommodation.
* **Zip (9)**: Enter the zip code of the accommodation.
* **City (10)**: Enter the city of the accommodation.
* **State (11)**: Enter the state of the accommodation.
* **Country (12)**: Enter the country of the accommodation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at [support email].
* We'll get back to you as soon as possible.
