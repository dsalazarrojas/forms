# Indoor Sports Award Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Indoor Sports Award Nomination Form is used to submit nominations for outstanding indoor sports teams or athletes. This form helps the sports organization or governing body to recognize and reward the achievements of deserving nominees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the nominee, including their name, contact details, and award nomination details.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors and duplication of efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the first name of the nominee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the last name of the nominee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the nominee or the person submitting the nomination.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the nominee or the person submitting the nomination.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the team being nominated.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required/optional): Select the sport being played by the nominee (Yes/No).
* **Award Type** (`award_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of award being nominated (Yes/No).
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the category or categories the nominee is being nominated for (Yes/No).
* **Year** (`year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of the achievement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the achievement.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the achievement.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations for the nomination.
