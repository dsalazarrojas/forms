# Wedding rsvp form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for guests to respond to the wedding invitation by providing their RSVP information. It includes various fields to capture their details, preferences, and confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required and optional.
2. Fill in the guest details field with your name and any other relevant information.
3. Select your RSVP status, choosing either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you can attend the wedding.
4. If you're choosing "Yes", fill in the date and time you plan to arrive.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number, to ensure we can reach you.
6. Confirm your RSVP status for each guest in your party.
7. Enter any additional RSVP messages or comments.
8. Review your form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **RSVP**: Select "Yes" if you plan to attend the wedding, or "No" if you cannot attend.
* **Date**: If you're choosing "Yes", enter the date you plan to arrive.
* **Time**: If you're choosing "Yes", enter the time you plan to arrive.
* **RSVP Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your RSVP status.
* **Guest Count**: Enter the number of guests you're bringing.
* **RSVP Type**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for each guest in your party.
* **RSVP Status**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the status of each guest's RSVP.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date for each guest's RSVP.
* **Guest Details 2**: Enter additional guest details (name, email, phone number, etc.).
* **Guest Count 2**: Enter the number of guests for this person.
* **RSVP Type 2**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for this guest.
* **RSVP Status 2**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for this guest.
* **RSVP Message 2**: Enter any additional comments or messages for this guest.
* **Email 2**: Enter this guest's email address.
* **Phone 2**: Enter this guest's phone number.
* **Confirm 2**: Confirm this guest's RSVP status.
* **RSVP Date 2**: Enter the date for this guest's RSVP.
* **RSVP Time 2**: Enter the time for this guest's RSVP.
* **RSVP Type 3**: Choose "Yes" or "No" for this guest.
* **RSVP Status 3**: Choose "Active" or "Inactive" for this guest.
* **RSVP Message 3**: Enter any additional comments or messages for this guest.
* **Email 3**: Enter this guest's email address.
* **Phone 3**: Enter this guest's phone number.
* **Confirm 3**: Confirm this guest's RSVP status.

Note that some of the fields may be optional or have specific instructions that are not explicitly stated here, so be sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
