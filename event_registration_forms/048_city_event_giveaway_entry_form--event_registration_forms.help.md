# City Event Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "City Event Giveaway Entry Form" is designed to collect information from potential attendees for a chance to win tickets to various events hosted by the city. This form gathers essential details to determine eligibility and preferences for these events.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** in the "Your Full Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** where you can be contacted if you win.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** for additional confirmation.
4. Select which **Event Interested You in This Giveaway** by choosing one of the provided options (Concert, Theater, Sports, Comedy, etc.).
5. If you have a **Preferred Event Date**, select one of the options (Any date, Weekend, Weekday, Specific date preference).
6. Select how you **Hear About This Giveaway** (Social media, Email newsletter, Website, etc.).
7. Choose your **Likelihood to Attend if You Win** (Definitely will attend, Very likely to attend, etc.).
8. If you wish, provide your **Age Group**.
9. Enter your **ZIP or Postal Code** for local analysis.
10. Indicate how many **Tickets Needed** (1, 2, 3, 4, etc.).
11. Choose who you would attend with (Going alone, With family, With friends, etc.).
12. Indicate how often you **Attend City Events** (Frequently, Several times per year, etc.).
13. Select your **Favorite Event Types** (Music and concerts, Theater and arts, etc., select all that apply).
14. Choose whether you are interested in **Following Our Social Media** for updates on future giveaways and events.
15. Select if you are willing to **Share This Giveaway** with others.
16. Finally, confirm that you **Agree to Giveaway Rules and Terms** by selecting the correct option.
17. Confirm that you give **Permission to Contact You if You Win** by selecting the correct option.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use to contact you if you win. Please ensure it's accurate and checkable.
* **Phone Number**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you for additional information or to confirm your win.
* **Event Interested You in This Giveaway**: Select one of the options to indicate which type of event interests you (Concert, Theater, etc.). This will help us determine the event you are likely to attend.
* **Preferred Event Date**: Select a preferred date for the event. Please note that we cannot guarantee the specific date for the event.
* **How Did You Hear About This Giveaway**: Indicate how you heard about the giveaway to help us evaluate our marketing efforts.
* **How Likely Are You to Attend if You Win**: Select how likely you are to attend the event if you win.
* **Age Group**: Choose your age group for demographic analysis.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code for local analysis.
* **Number of Tickets Needed**: Indicate how many tickets you would like to attend with.
* **Who Would You Attend With**: Choose who you would attend with if you win (Going alone, With family, etc.).
* **How Often Do You Attend City Events**: Select how often you attend city events.
* **Your Favorite Event Types**: Select all types of events that interest you (Music, Theater, etc.).
* **Follow Our Social Media**: Choose if you are willing to follow our social media for updates on future giveaways.
* **Willing to Share This Giveaway**: Select if you are willing to share the giveaway with others.
* **I Agree to Giveaway Rules and Terms**: Confirm that you agree to our rules and terms.
* **Permission to Contact You if You Win**: Confirm that you give permission to contact you if you win.
