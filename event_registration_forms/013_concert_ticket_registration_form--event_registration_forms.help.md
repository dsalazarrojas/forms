# Concert Ticket Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows users to register for a concert event, providing their event preference, personal details, and other relevant information to facilitate the event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the concert event you would like to attend from the "Select Event" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your preferred concert date and time from the available options.
3. Enter your name, email address, and phone number to provide contact information.
4. Select your seating preference from the available options.
5. Check the "Group Discount Eligibility" option if you are registering as part of a group of 10 or more.
6. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes I Will Attend" from the "RSVP Response" dropdown menu.
7. If you have any special requests or requirements, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
8. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number (if applicable).
9. Agree to the event terms and conditions by selecting "I Accept All Terms" from the "Terms Acceptance" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Event**: Choose which concert event you would like to attend. Options include Rock Festival 2026, Classical Evening, Jazz Showcase, Pop Concert Series, and Country Music Night.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the concert you selected. This is a required field.
* **Session Preference**: Select your preferred concert session (Matinee, Evening, or Late Night).
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of attendees.
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for emergencies or event updates.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes (optional).
* **Additional Attendee Details**: Provide information about other attendees (optional).
* **Guest Names**: Enter the names of other attendees (optional).
* **Group Discount Eligibility**: Check if you are registering as part of a group of 10 or more.
* **Seating Preference**: Select where you would like to sit during the concert.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Check if you have any special needs, such as wheelchair access, companion seating, or hearing/visual assistance.
* **RSVP Response**: Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes I Will Attend" or other options.
* **Meal Preference**: Select a meal option if catering is included.
* **Merchandise Interest**: Indicate if you are interested in concert merchandise.
* **Newsletter Signup**: Check if you would like to receive event updates.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you became aware of the event.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional requests or requirements.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact number.
* **Terms Acceptance**: Agree to the event terms and conditions.

Note: This form is for concert registration purposes only. Please ensure you provide accurate and honest information to facilitate a smooth event experience.
