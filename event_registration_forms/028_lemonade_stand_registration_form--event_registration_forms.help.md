# Lemonade Stand Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lemonade Stand Registration Form is a tool for participants to register for a lemonade stand event. This form collects important information from participants, including their details and those of their guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section, including your name and any necessary contact information.
2. If you have a guardian, fill out the guardian details section, including their name and contact information.
3. In the event details section, include any necessary information about the event you are registering for.
4. In the participant notes section, include any additional information about the participant that you think might be helpful for the event organizers.
5. Select your participant's permission status for the event.
6. If you have any further comments about the participant's permission status, include it in the participant permission note section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (text, required/optional):
This field is for you to fill out your name, which will be displayed on the registration form.
* **Participant Guardian Details** (text, required/optional):
This field is for you to fill out your guardian's name and contact information if you have a guardian.
* **Event Details** (text, required/optional):
This field is for you to include any important information about the event you are registering for.
* **Participant Notes** (note, required/optional):
This field is for you to include any additional information about the participant that you think might be helpful for the event organizers.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required/optional):
This is your contact number and will be used to reach out to you with important information.
* **Participant Email** (email, required/optional):
This is your email address and will be used to reach out to you with important information.
* **Guardian Name** (text, required/optional):
This field is for you to fill out your guardian's name.
* **Guardian Relationship** (select_one, required/optional):
This field is for you to select your relationship with your guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (text, required/optional):
This is your guardian's contact number and will be used to reach out to them with important information.
* **Guardian Email** (email, required/optional):
This is your guardian's email address and will be used to reach out to them with important information.
* **Participant Permission** (select_multiple, required/optional):
This field is for you to select the participant's permission status for the event.
* **Participant Permission Note** (note, required/optional):
This field is for you to include any further comments about the participant's permission status.
