# Ingredient Transparency Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from individuals interested in attending the Ingredient Transparency Summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1.  Select a session that interests you by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Select a session" field.
2.  Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
5.  Read and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the "Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a session** (session_preferences, select_one, required): Choose the session that interests you by selecting "Yes" or "No". This field is required and helps us understand your preferences for the event.
* **Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This field is required and helps us identify you.
* **Email** (email, text, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you. This field is required and helps us keep in touch with you.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This field is optional and helps us reach you if needed.
* **Consent** (consent, note, required): Agree to the terms and conditions by checking this field. This field is required to proceed with your registration.
