# global_diversity_summit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The global_diversity_summit_registration_form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the global diversity summit. The form helps to identify relevant attendees and their needs, ensuring a successful and meaningful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information has been provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your personal identifier. Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please provide it for communication purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): This is your company's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This is your job title within your company.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): This field is for your job position or role.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): This field is for your role within the company.
* **Role Description** (`role_description`, text, optional): This is a brief description of your role.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): This is your department within the company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): This field is for your industry or field of work.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): This is your job function or job type.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Revenue** (`revenue`, number, optional): This field is for your company's revenue.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Public or Private.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Part-time or Full-time.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): This field is for your work location.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Entry, Mid-level, or Senior.
* **Education** (`education`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: High School, College, or University.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: Less than 1 year, 1-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional): This is your current salary.
* **Expected Salary** (`expected_salary`, number, optional): This field is for your expected salary.
* **Available for Travel** (`available_for_travel`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: True or False.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This is where you can provide any additional comments or notes.

Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information has been provided accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
