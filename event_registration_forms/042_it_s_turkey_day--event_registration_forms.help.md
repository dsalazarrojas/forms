# Its_Turkey_Day - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event registration information for events related to Turkey Day. It's likely used for event planning, organization, or tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form title, "Its_Turkey_Day", to understand the purpose of the form.
2. Select the correct event registration form from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your name and date if applicable.
4. Provide your email address (not required but recommended for contact purposes).
5. Enter your phone number (not required but recommended for contact purposes).
6. Add any additional notes about the event or activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Registration Forms** (`event_registration_forms`, select_one, required): Select the type of event or activity you are registering for (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for registration purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event or activity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the event or activity.
