# Seminar Registration Form INAFOR - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Registration Form INAFOR is used to register for seminars such as workshops, conferences, events, meetings, and webinars. The form helps gather essential information to facilitate seminar planning and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required information is provided.
2. Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name (required).
* **Last name**: Enter your last name (required).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (required).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your address (optional).
* **Academic Institution**: Enter your academic institution (optional).
* **Department**: Enter your department (optional).
* **Role**: Enter your role (optional).
* **Academic Title**: Enter your academic title (optional).
* **Organization**: Enter your organization (optional).
* **Supervisor**: Enter your supervisor's name (optional).
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter your supervisor's phone number (optional).
* **Seminar Title**: Enter the seminar title (optional).
* **Seminar Start Date**: Enter the start date of the seminar (optional).
* **Seminar End Date**: Enter the end date of the seminar (optional).
* **Seminar Location**: Enter the seminar location (optional).
* **Seminar Description**: Enter a brief description of the seminar (optional).
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the seminar (required).
* **Registration Time**: Enter the time you registered for the seminar (optional).
* **Seminar Type**: Select the type of seminar you are attending (optional): Seminar, Workshop, Conference, Event, Meeting, Webinar.
* **Seminar Level**: Select the level of the seminar you are attending (optional): Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, All.
* **Attendees**: Select the number of attendees for the seminar (optional): 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, More.
* **Registration Fee**: Select the registration fee for the seminar (optional): 0, 100, 200.
* **Registration Method**: Select the method of registration (optional): Online, In Person, Both.
* **Submitted By**: Select whether the registration was submitted by you (required).
