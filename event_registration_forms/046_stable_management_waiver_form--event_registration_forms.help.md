# Stable Management Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stable Management Waiver Form is a document used to obtain consent from riders for stable management activities. Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal information: <br>
   * **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification. (Label: first_name, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification. (Label: last_name, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. (Label: date_of_birth, Type: date, Required: true)
   * **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached during emergency situations. (Label: address, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Email**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached for emergencies. (Label: email, Type: email, Required: true)
   * **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during emergencies. (Label: phone, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact. (Label: emergency_contact_first_name, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact. (Label: emergency_contact_last_name, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with your emergency contact, e.g., "Mother", "Father", "Other". (Label: emergency_contact_relationship, Type: select_one, Required: true)
   * **Emergency Contact Other Relationship**: If your emergency contact relationship is not listed above, please describe it. (Label: emergency_contact_relationship_others, Type: text, Required: false)
   * **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact. (Label: emergency_contact_phone, Type: text, Required: true)
   * **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact. (Label: emergency_contact_email, Type: email, Required: true)
   * **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in stable management activities. (Label: medical_conditions, Type: text, Required: false)
   * **Allergies**: List any allergies you have. (Label: allergies, Type: text, Required: false)
   * **Rider Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understand the stable management activities and their associated risks. (Label: rider_signature, Type: text, Required: true)

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them to help us provide a safe and enjoyable experience.
* If your emergency contact relationship is not listed, please describe it in the "Emergency Contact Other Relationship" field.
* Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understand the stable management activities and their associated risks.
