# Business Conference Keynote Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Conference Keynote Sponsorship Form is designed for businesses to register as keynote sponsors for a conference. This form is used to collect necessary information about your company and presentation details to facilitate your sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled out before submitting the form.
2. Review the form carefully and enter accurate information.
3. Use the provided examples to help guide your input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`title`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Position** (`contact_position`, text, required): Enter the title or position of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company's contact number.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If you have a website, enter the URL.
* **Company Description** (`company_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your company.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference.
* **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the conference.
* **Conference Location** (`conference_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the conference.
* **Expected Attendees** (`expected_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees your company expects.
* **Sponsorship Package** (`sponsorship_package`, note, required): Select the package that best suits your needs.
* **Package Selection** (`package_selection`, select_one, required): Choose which sponsorship level you want to be at (Platinum Keynote Sponsor, Gold Keynote Sponsor, Silver Keynote Sponsor, Bronze Keynote Sponsor, or Custom Package).
* **Keynote Slot Preference** (`keynote_slot_preference`, select_one, required): Choose the slot you want for your keynote presentation (Opening Keynote - Day 1, Closing Keynote - Day 1, Opening Keynote - Day 2, Closing Keynote - Day 2, or Any Slot).
* **Speaker Information** (`speaker_info`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your speaker.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the speaker.
* **Speaker Title** (`speaker_title`, text, required): Enter the speaker's title or position.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio`, text, required): Enter a brief biography of the speaker.
* **Keynote Topic** (`keynote_topic`, text, required): Enter the proposed topic or title of your presentation.
* **Keynote Description** (`keynote_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your presentation.
* **AV Requirements** (`av_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any technical requirements for your presentation, enter them here.
* **Sponsorship Benefits** (`sponsorship_benefits`, select_multiple, required): Choose which benefits you want to receive as a sponsor (Logo on Conference Materials, Booth Space, Attendee List Access, Social Media Promotion, Press Release Mention, VIP Networking Access, Workshop Session, or Panel Participation).
* **Marketing Materials** (`marketing_materials`, select_one, required): Choose if you will provide marketing materials (Yes - Ready, Yes - In Progress, Need Assistance, or False).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will make the payment (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Invoice).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out before submission.
