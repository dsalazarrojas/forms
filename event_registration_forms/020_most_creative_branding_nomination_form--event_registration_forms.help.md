> **# Most Creative Branding Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Most Creative Branding" award is given to companies or organizations that have demonstrated exceptional creativity in their branding efforts. This form allows users to nominate their own company or organization for this award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
3. Review and edit your submission as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, required: false)
	+ This is the text area where you can describe your company's branding efforts.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the company or organization you are nominating.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination or the company's contact email.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the company or organization being nominated.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`, text, required: false)
	+ Explain why you are nominating this company for the "Most Creative Branding" award. Describe their branding efforts and what makes them stand out from others.
