# Corporate Culture Hackathon Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting hackathon ideas to the corporate culture hackathon. Please fill it out if you have a great idea to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the required information for your hackathon idea.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your hackathon idea.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): The name of the person submitting the idea.
* **Describe Your Hackathon Idea** (`idea_description`, text, optional): A detailed description of your hackathon idea.
* **Hackathon Title** (`hackathon_title`, text, optional): The title of your hackathon idea.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Are there team members involved in this hackathon idea? (Yes/No)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address of the person submitting the idea.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the person submitting the idea.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): The company associated with this hackathon idea.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department associated with this hackathon idea.
* **Idea Type** (`select_idea_type`, select_one, optional): What type of idea is this? (Assuming "Yes" means it is a hack, and "No" means it's not)
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): The date you're submitting this hackathon idea.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): The time you're submitting this hackathon idea.
* **Submission Date Range** (`submission_date_range`, date, optional): The range of dates for this hackathon idea submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the relevant fields with accurate and detailed information.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, please reach out to us.
* Please note that this form is not a formal application, and the submission date range is for tracking purposes only.
