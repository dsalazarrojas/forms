# Employee Life Balance Awareness Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Life Balance Awareness Program Registration Form is designed to collect registration information for the Employee Life Balance Awareness Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each page of the form and follow these steps:
1. On **Registration Form**, enter your name and any other relevant contact information. 
2. On **Employee Info**, enter your employee ID, department, and other relevant information about yourself.
3. On **Wellness Initiatives**, select the wellness initiatives that interest you from the list.
4. On **Availability**, enter the number of hours you are available for the program.
5. On **Date**, select a date for the program you are interested in.
6. On **Time**, select a time for the program you are interested in.
7. On **Note**, enter any additional comments or questions you have.
8. On **Email**, enter your work email address.
9. On **Phone**, enter your work phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**:
	+ **Registration Form**: A brief description of yourself, including your name and contact information.
	+ **Registration Form** is not a required field.
* **Employee Info**:
	+ **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for identification.
	+ **Department**: Enter the department you work in.
	+ Other relevant information about yourself.
* **Wellness Initiatives**:
	+ Select the wellness initiatives that interest you, such as fitness classes or stress management workshops.
* **Availability**:
	+ Enter the number of hours you are available for the program.
* **Date**:
	+ Select a date for the program you are interested in.
* **Time**:
	+ Select a time for the program you are interested in.
* **Note**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the program.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your work phone number.
