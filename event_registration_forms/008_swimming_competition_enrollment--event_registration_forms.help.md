# Swimming Competition Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for a swimming competition. It is intended for swimmers to provide their basic information, event type, and event details to ensure a smooth and organized competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the enrollment process.
2. Fill out the swimmer information page with your details.
3. Choose your event type from the options provided.
4. Select the number of swimmers on your team.
5. Enter your event date and time.
6. Provide your event location.
7. If needed, include any additional notes about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swimmer Information** (<type> text): This page is for swimmers to input their basic details.
* **Swimmer Name** (<type> text, required): Enter your name as it is to be displayed on your competition materials.
* **Swimmer Email** (<type> text, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Swimmer Phone** (<type> text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Swimmer Age** (<type> number, required): Enter your age for competition purposes.
* **Event Type** (<type> select_one, required): Choose the type of competition you are registering for (Swim Meet, Water Polo, Diving).
* **Team Size** (<type> select_multiple, required): Select the number of swimmers on your team.
* **Event Date** (<type> date, required): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Time** (<type> time, required): Enter the start time of your event.
* **Event Location** (<type> text, optional): Provide the location of your event.
* **Event Note** (<type> note, optional): Include any additional notes about your event.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* If you need to update your information after submitting your initial enrollment, contact the competition organizers for assistance.
* If you are registering for multiple events, repeat this form for each event.
