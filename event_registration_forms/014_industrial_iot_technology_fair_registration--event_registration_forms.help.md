# Industrial IoT Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Industrial IoT Technology Fair Registration form. The form is designed to collect your basic contact information and specific interests in IoT technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide the name of your Company (if any).
4. Specify your role or position.
5. Select your interests from the list of options provided (at least one is required).
6. (Optional) Fill in your Phone number.
7. (Optional) Select a date and time for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)

Enter your first name here.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)

Enter your last name here.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional)

Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.

### Company
**Company** (`company`, text, optional)

If you are affiliated with a company, enter its name here.

### Role
**Role** (`role`, text, optional)

Describe your role or position.

### Interests
**Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required)

Select at least one interest from the list:

* AIoT
* Industrial Automation
* IoT Platform
* Data Analytics
* Machine Learning
* Robotics

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)

Enter your phone number (optional).

### Date
**Date** (`date`, date, optional)

Select a date for the event.

### Time
**Time** (`time`, time, optional)

Select a time for the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to select at least one interest from the list.
* If you want to be contacted about the event, make sure to fill in your email and phone number.
* If you want to attend the event on a specific date and time, make sure to fill in the respective fields.
