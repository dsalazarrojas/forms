# Celebration Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Celebration Event Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number accurately.
2. Select the correct event date and time.
3. Choose the correct event location.
4. Specify the number of guests attending (including yourself).
5. If applicable, list the names of other guests.
6. Choose your registration type (Individual, Group, Family, VIP, or Volunteer).
7. Select your dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut-free, or No Restrictions).
8. Choose your meal selection (Standard Meal, Vegetarian Meal, Vegan Meal, Special Diet Meal, or No Meal).
9. If needed, mention any special seating requests.
10. Choose your interests among the offered activities.
11. If interested, volunteer for any activities.
12. Provide consent for photography and videography.
13. Agree to the event terms and conditions.
14. Select your preferred contact method for event updates (Email, Phone, Text Message, or No Contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for receiving event updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication with the event organizers.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date**: Select the correct date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Select the correct time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the full address of the event location.
* **Number of Guests Attending**: Enter the number of people attending the event, including yourself.
* **Names of Other Guests**: If applicable, list the names of other guests attending the event.
* **Registration Type**: Choose your registration type (Individual, Group, Family, VIP, or Volunteer).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select your dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut-free, or No Restrictions).
* **Meal Selection**: Choose your preferred meal selection (Standard Meal, Vegetarian Meal, Vegan Meal, Special Diet Meal, or No Meal).
* **Special Seating Requests**: If needed, specify any special seating requests.
* **Interested Activities**: Select any activities you are interested in (Dinner Service, Entertainment, Dancing, Games, Photo Booth, Raffle, Silent Auction).
* **Interested in Volunteering**: If interested, select 'Yes' for volunteering opportunities.
* **I consent to photography and videography**: Choose 'Yes' to allow event organizers to take pictures and videos during the event.
* **I agree to the event terms and conditions**: Choose 'Yes' to accept the event terms and conditions.
* **Preferred Contact Method for Event Updates**: Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text Message, or No Contact).
