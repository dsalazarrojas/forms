# Digital Competency Mapping Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Digital Competency Mapping Workshop Registration Form. It will walk you through the steps and fields required to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select a workshop from the available options.
2. Provide your name and email address.
3. Choose a time zone for your workshop.
4. Confirm your participant type (e.g., Yes/No).
5. Optionally, provide additional comments or information about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Workshop** (`workshop_selection`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose which workshop you wish to attend. Please select "Yes" next to the workshop that interests you the most.

* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is reserved for workshop dates but is currently not required due to its status. You can provide it if needed.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as the participant in this field.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in this field for contact purposes.

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable easy communication regarding the workshop.

* **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, required: false): This field allows you to add any additional details about your participation or requirements you may need during the workshop.

* **Workshop Category** (`workshop_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you're a workshop participant by selecting "Yes".

* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `time`, required: false): Select your time zone to help with scheduling and planning.

* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your participant type by choosing "Yes".

* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your registration time for this workshop.

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your registration date for this workshop.

* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Reserve this field for workshop start dates but is not currently required.

* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the workshop if needed.

* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address again for reference.

* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact.

* **Additional Information** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any extra details or preferences in this field.

* **Workshop Notes** (`workshop_notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter notes about your workshop participation.

* **Workshop Capacity** (`workshop_capacity`, `text`, required: false): This field is not currently required but may be used to track capacity.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name again as the participant.

* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your registration status by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".

* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `time`, required: false): Select your time zone for this workshop.

* **Participant Category** (`participant_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your participant category by choosing "Yes".

* **Workshop Participant Type** (`workshop_participant_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your workshop participant type by choosing "Yes".

* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Enter any preferences or requirements you may have for the workshop.

* **Comments** (`participant_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about your participation in the workshop.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.
