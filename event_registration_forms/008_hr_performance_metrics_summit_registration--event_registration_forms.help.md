# HR Performance Metrics Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Performance Metrics Summit Registration form collects information for attendees to register for the HR Performance Metrics Summit event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for the summit details section.
2. Choose the correct attendance type for the summit.
3. Enter the attendee's name, company name, job title, email, and phone number.
4. Optionally, provide any special needs information in the special needs field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Information
* **Summit Details** (`summit_details`, `text`, required: false): This section requires a brief description of the summit. However, since it's not required, you can skip it.
### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
### Attendance Type
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you will be attending the summit:
	+ Label1
	+ Label2
	+ Label3
### Special Needs
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `note`, required: false): If you have any special needs, please describe them here.
### Attendee Name
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information provided is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
