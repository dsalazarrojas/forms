# Community Murals Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Murals Payment Form is designed to collect payment information from clients or customers for mural projects. It ensures accuracy and efficiency in capturing required payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing the form** to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill in the **Payer Name** field with your full name as it will appear on receipts.
3. Enter your **Contact Email** where payment confirmations and updates will be sent.
4. Provide the **Project Title** for the specific mural project being paid for.
5. Enter the exact **Amount Due** in local currency.
6. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the provided options.
7. If applicable, input your **Billing Address** for deliveries and invoice purposes.
8. Indicate if you need an invoice by choosing **Invoice Needed**.
9. Optionally, enter a **Payment Reference** for transaction tracking.
10. If needed, include any **Additional Notes** for further payment instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Murals Payment --**: This page serves as an introduction or header for the form, providing a clear indication of what the form is for.
* **Payer name**: Please enter your full name as it will appear on receipts. (Required)
* **Contact email**: Enter the email address where you will receive payment confirmation and updates. (Required)
* **Mural project title**: Provide the name or brief title of the project for which you are making the payment. (Required)
* **Amount due**: Enter the exact payment amount in local currency. (Required)
* **Payment method**: Select your preferred payment method: 
  - **Credit card**: Used for digital payments.
  - **Bank transfer**: Used for transfer payments.
  - **Cash**: Used for direct payment.
  - **Check**: Used for payment by check.
* **Billing address**: If you need to provide an address for deliveries or invoice purposes, enter it here. (Optional)
* **Do you need an invoice?**: Choose 'True' if you require an invoice for the payment. (Optional)
* **Payment reference**: Enter a transaction ID or notes for tracking purposes. (Optional)
* **Additional notes**: Provide any further payment instructions or details. (Optional)
