# Jazz Festival Beverage Sponsorship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect sponsorship information from potential beverage sponsors for the jazz festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the necessary information in the corresponding fields.
2. Make sure to enter the company name, contact person's details, and sponsorship details accurately.
3. Select the correct sponsorship type and indicate if you have signed a contract.
4. Choose whether or not you have agreed to the terms.
5. If applicable, enter the date the contract was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This field is for a brief introduction or message about your company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will be the primary contact for this sponsorship.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details`, text, required): Describe the nature of your sponsorship, including any specific details or conditions.
* **Sponsorship Type** (`sponsorship_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your sponsorship is a financial or product sponsorship, 'No' if it's a non-monetary or in-kind sponsorship.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the sponsorship by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contract Signed** (`contract_signed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether a contract has been signed with the festival by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, required): Enter the date the contract was signed, if applicable.
