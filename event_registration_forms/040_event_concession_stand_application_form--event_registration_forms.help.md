# Event Concession Stand Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from event vendors who want to set up a concession stand at an event. This information will be used to verify your identity, determine your booth location, and collect necessary payment and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your vendor details in the "Vendor Details" section.
2. Choose your preferred booth location in the "Booth Preferences" section.
3. Upload any necessary documents in the "Documents" section.
4. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
5. Enter your phone number, email, address, and website in the "Contact Details" sections.
6. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" section.
7. Add any additional notes or payment information in the "Payment Info Notes" section.
8. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, required):
  Provide a brief description of your concession stand and the goods/services you will be selling.
- **Booth Preferences** (`booth_preferences`, select_one, required):
  Choose the type of booth you prefer:
  - Kiosk: A self-service area where customers can browse and purchase goods.
  - Food: A booth selling food and drinks.
  - Beverages: A booth selling drinks.
- **Documents** (`documents`, text, required):
  Upload any necessary documents, such as proof of insurance or certifications, to support your application.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required):
  Provide your company name and a brief description of your business.
- **Contact Details Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, number, required):
  Enter your phone number.
- **Contact Details Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, required):
  Enter your email address.
- **Contact Details Address** (`contact_details_address`, text, required):
  Enter your business address.
- **Contact Details Website** (`contact_details_website`, text, required):
  Enter your website URL.
- **Payment Info Prophets** (`payment_info_prophets`, number, required):
  Enter your payment information, such as your credit card number or bank account number.
- **Payment Info Notes** (`payment_info_notes`, note, required):
  Add any additional payment information or notes.
