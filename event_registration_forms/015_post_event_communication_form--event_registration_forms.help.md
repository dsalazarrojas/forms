# Post Event Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and feedback after an event or meeting. It helps us understand the event's details and participant experiences, which will be used to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Details** (`event_registration_forms_form_1`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing any relevant details about the event you attended. Please share any information you think is important for us to know.
- **Feedback** (`event_feedback_forms_form_2`, `text`, optional): Share your feedback and thoughts about the event. This will help us understand what we can improve for future events.
- **Event Details** (`event_details_form_3`, `text`, optional): This section is another opportunity to provide any additional details about the event.
- **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience_form_4`, `time`, optional): Estimate how much time you spent engaging with our event content (e.g., watching a video, browsing a website, or attending a live session). Your estimate will help us understand how to better engage our audience in the future.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any question, please contact our support team for clarification.
- Please submit the completed form to ensure your feedback is included in our records.
- If you've already submitted this form for the same event, please do not complete it again to avoid duplication.
