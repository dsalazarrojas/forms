# Sponsorship Agreement Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and update sponsorship agreements. It captures essential details about the agreement, including contact information, contract dates, and agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, but note that most fields are optional.
2. Enter the contact person's information, including their title and email address.
3. Provide the organization's name and any additional details about the agreement.
4. Enter the sponsorship amount for the agreement.
5. Choose the current status of the contract from the options provided.
6. Indicate whether the agreement has been signed or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the individual who has signed the agreement.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization involved in the sponsorship.
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the date the agreement began.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the date the agreement ended, if applicable.
* **Agreement Details**: Add any additional information about the agreement.
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount of sponsorship provided.
* **Contact Person Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contract Status**: Choose the current status of the agreement from the provided options: active, inactive, or expired.
* **Agreement Signed**: Indicate whether the agreement has been signed or not.
