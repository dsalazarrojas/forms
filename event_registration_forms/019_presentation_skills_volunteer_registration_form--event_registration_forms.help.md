# presentation_skills_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Presentation Skills Volunteer Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering their presentation skills for workshops. This form aims to gather data on the volunteers' availability, skills level, interests, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the form on the first page.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select your interest in the "Workshop Interest" field by choosing one or more options (Presentation, Workshop, or Other).
6. Choose your skills level from the "Skills Level" dropdown menu (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
7. Select your availability in the "Availability" field by choosing one or more options (Weekends, Weekdays, or Flexible).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
### 2. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a valid contact number where you can be reached.
### 3. **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
### 4. **Workshop Interest** (`workshop_interest`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more interests (Presentation, Workshop, or Other) that you have in relation to volunteering.
### 5. **Skills Level** (`skills_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your presentation skills level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) to indicate your proficiency.
### 6. **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your availability to volunteer (Weekends, Weekdays, or Flexible), indicating when you can commit to helping out.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be as specific as possible when entering your name, contact number, email, and interests.
- If you selected multiple interests, make sure to separate them with a comma.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
