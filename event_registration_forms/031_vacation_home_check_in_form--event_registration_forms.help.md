# vacation_home_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vacation_home_check_in_form" is a form used to collect essential information from guests arriving at a vacation home or rental property. This form helps property managers or administrators gather details about the guests and their vehicles to ensure a smooth check-in experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Guest Information** (`guest_info`): Please fill out this section with your basic contact details, such as your name and phone number.
2. **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`): If you're arriving with a vehicle, please provide its make, model, color, and license plate number. You can also fill out additional vehicle information fields if required.
3. **Check-In Information** (`check_in_info`): Enter your estimated arrival time and any special requests or information about your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information (guest_info)**: Enter your name and contact details to help us assist you better.
* **Vehicle Information 2 (vehicle_info_2)**: This field is likely used to collect additional details about your vehicle, such as its size or type.
* **Vehicle Information 4 (vehicle_info_4)**: This field might be used to collect specific information about your vehicle's condition or any special requirements.
* **Vehicle Information 3 (vehicle_info_3)**: This field is probably used to collect further details about your vehicle, such as its mileage or fuel level.
* **Vehicle Information 5 (vehicle_info_5)**: This field might be used to collect information about your vehicle's accessories or features.
* **Vehicle Information 6 (vehicle_info_6)**: This field is likely used to collect any other relevant details about your vehicle.
* **Check-In Information (check_in_info)**: Enter your estimated arrival time and any special requests or information about your stay to help us prepare for your arrival.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are filled out accurately to avoid any issues during the check-in process.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Check-In Information" section.
* If you're arriving with multiple vehicles, please fill out each vehicle information field accordingly.
