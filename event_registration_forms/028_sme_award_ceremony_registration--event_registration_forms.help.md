# SME Award Ceremony Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SME Award Ceremony Registration form is used to collect information from SMEs who will be awarded during the ceremony. This information will be used to recognize and celebrate their achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter your date of birth (optional).
3. Select your award category from the available options.
4. Enter your company name.
5. Select your role and job title (if applicable).
6. Choose your location.
7. Select your job function (if applicable).
8. Choose the type of your company.
9. Select your industry (if applicable).
10. Choose the size of your company.
11. Indicate whether you are an SME.
12. Select your SME status.
13. Select your SME type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (if you choose to provide it).
* **Award Category** (5, select_one, required): Select the category under which you will be receiving the award.
* **Company Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (7, text, optional): Enter your role within your company (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (8, text, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department** (9, text, optional): Enter your department within your company (if applicable).
* **Location** (10, text, optional): Enter your location (if applicable).
* **Job Function** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select your job function(s) within your company.
* **Company Type** (12, select_one, required): Select the type of your company.
* **Industry** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select your industry.
* **Company Size** (14, select_one, required): Select the size of your company.
* **SME Sector** (15, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you are an SME.
* **SME Status** (16, select_one, required): Select your SME status.
* **SME Type** (17, select_one, required): Select your SME type.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you receive the recognition you deserve.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the ceremony organizers for assistance.
* Ensure that you have provided all required fields before submitting the form.
