# Stage Play Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a stage play competition. It is intended for teams and individuals to submit their team and play information, including team name, description, and original play name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your team in the "Team Description" field.
3. If you are registering a team, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Team Members" field to indicate if your entry is a team or individual.
4. Enter the original name of the play you are presenting in the "Original Name of the Play" field.
5. Upload your play's script in the "Upload Your Script" field.
6. If you know the date of the competition, enter it in the "Competition Date" field. If not, leave this field blank.
7. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (text): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Description** (text): Briefly describe your team, including a brief introduction and any notable achievements.
* **Team Members** (select_multiple): Indicate if your entry is a team (yes) or individual (no).
* **Team Description** (note): Provide a more in-depth description of your team and its composition.
* **Original Name of the Play** (text): Enter the original name of the play you are presenting.
* **Upload Your Script** (text): Upload your play's script in this field.
* **Competition Date** (date): If you know the date of the competition, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are uploading a large script, consider compressing it before uploading.
* If you are registering as a team, be sure to list all team members in your description.
* If you are uploading your script, make sure it is in a compatible format (e.g., PDF, docx, etc.).
