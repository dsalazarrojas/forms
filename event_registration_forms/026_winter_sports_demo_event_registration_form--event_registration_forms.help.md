# Winter Sports Demo Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Sports Demo Event Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from participants for the Winter Sports Demo Event. Please fill out this form to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the format of `yourname@example.com` in the "Email" field.
4. If you want to be contacted by phone, enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
5. Select the Event Date from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
6. Select the Event Time in the "Event Time" field.
7. Choose the Event Type by selecting one of the options (Skiing, Snowboarding, or Snowshoeing) in the "Event Type" field.
8. If you want to pay via a specific method, select your preferred Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer) in the "Payment Method" field. (Note: This field is not required, as you can also pay on-site or other arrangements.)
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Notes" field. (Note: This field is optional.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it is known by your friends and family.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it is known by your friends and family.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in the format of `yourname@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (time, required): Select the time of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of event you are interested in (Skiing, Snowboarding, or Snowshoeing).
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, optional): Select the method of payment you prefer (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer). (Note: You can also pay on-site or other arrangements.)
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you have about your registration.
