# 1:1 Meeting Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request a 1:1 meeting that meets your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Your Contact Information" section.
2. Describe the meeting purpose and desired outcome in the "Meeting Details" section.
3. Choose your preferred meeting format, duration, and virtual platform (if applicable).
4. Select your available dates and times for the meeting.
5. Indicate the urgency of your meeting request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: Your complete name as it appears on official documents or communication. (Required)
* **Your Email Address**: The email address you'd like to use for meeting communication and confirmation. (Required)
* **Phone Number (Optional)**: Your phone number, including country code if outside the local region. (Optional)
* **Organization / Company (Optional)**: If you represent an organization, provide its name. (Optional)
* **Meeting Purpose or Topic**: Briefly describe the meeting's purpose or topic. (Required)
* **Desired Meeting Outcome**: Mention what you hope to achieve from this meeting. (Optional)
* **Meeting Format**: Choose how you'd like the meeting to take place: Virtual Call, In-person Meeting, or Phone Call. (Required)
* **Virtual Platform Preference (if applicable)**: Select all platforms you're comfortable using for virtual meetings. (Optional)
* **Estimated Meeting Duration**: Choose the duration you anticipate the meeting will take. (Required)
* **First Preferred Date**: Select the date you prefer for the meeting. (Required)
* **First Preferred Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the meeting. (Required)
* **Second Preferred Date (Optional)**: Provide an alternative date if your first choice is unavailable. (Optional)
* **Second Preferred Time (Optional)**: Select an alternative time slot for your second preferred date. (Optional)
* **Third Preferred Date (Optional)**: A third option for the meeting date. (Optional)
* **Third Preferred Time (Optional)**: A third option for the meeting time. (Optional)
* **General Days of Week Available**: Choose all days you're generally available for meetings. (Optional)
* **General Time of Day Preference**: Indicate if you're more available in the morning or afternoon. (Optional)
* **Meeting Urgency**: Select how soon you need this meeting to happen. (Required)
* **Note: Please be prepared to share any relevant documents or materials during the meeting...**: A note to ensure you're prepared to share meeting materials.
* **Any other comments or special requests?**: Provide any further context or specific requirements for the meeting. (Optional)

Note: Required fields are marked as "(Required)" in the form and in the explanation above.
