# Global Networking Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Networking Webinar Registration form is a tool used by the organization to collect information from participants for events organized by the organization. This form helps to ensure accurate and relevant information is collected from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form to ensure all required fields are completed before submission.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Title** (ID: 1, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the title of the event.

*   Enter a brief description of the event.

### 2. **Speaker Name** (ID: 2, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the name of the speaker.

*   Enter the name of the speaker or presenter for the event.

### 3. **Speaker Email** (ID: 3, Email, Optional)
This field is for entering the email address of the speaker.

*   Enter the email address of the speaker.

### 4. **Speaker Phone** (ID: 4, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the phone number of the speaker.

*   Enter the phone number of the speaker.

### 5. **Participant Name** (ID: 5, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the name of the participant.

*   Enter your name as a participant.

### 6. **Participant Email** (ID: 6, Email, Optional)
This field is for entering the email address of the participant.

*   Enter your email address.

### 7. **Participant Phone** (ID: 7, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the phone number of the participant.

*   Enter your phone number.

### 8. **Participant Type** (ID: 8, Select One, Optional)
This field is for selecting the type of participant.

*   Choose the type of participant (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### 9. **Event Date** (ID: 9, Date, Optional)
This field is for entering the date of the event.

*   Enter the date of the event.

### 10. **Event Time** (ID: 10, Time, Optional)
This field is for entering the time of the event.

*   Enter the time of the event.

### 11. **Participant Notes** (ID: 11, Note, Optional)
This field is for entering additional notes about the participant.

*   Enter any additional notes about the participant.

### 12. **Participant Company** (ID: 12, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the company name of the participant.

*   Enter the name of the company that you represent.

### 13. **Participant Job Title** (ID: 13, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering your job title.

*   Enter your job title.

### 14. **Participant Role** (ID: 14, Text, Optional)
This field is for selecting your role.

*   Choose your role (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### 15. **Participant Location** (ID: 15, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering your location.

*   Enter your location.

### 16. **Event Category** (ID: 16, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the category of the event.

*   Enter the category of the event.

### 17. **Event Tags** (ID: 17, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering tags for the event.

*   Enter any relevant tags for the event.

### 18. **Event Description** (ID: 18, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering a brief description of the event.

*   Enter a brief description of the event.

### 19. **Event Address** (ID: 19, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the address of the event.

*   Enter the address of the event location.

### 20. **Event City** (ID: 20, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the city of the event location.

*   Enter the city of the event location.

### 21. **Event Zip** (ID: 21, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering the zip code of the event location.

*   Enter the zip code of the event location.

### 22. **Participant Agreement** (ID: 22, Note, Optional)
This field is for agreeing to terms.

*   Read and agree to terms.

### 23. **Participant Agreed** (ID: 23, Note, Optional)
This field is for indicating agreement.

*   Check this box to indicate agreement.

### 24. **Form Submitted By** (ID: 24, Text, Optional)
This field is for tracking who submitted the form.

*   System-generated field.

### 25. **Participant Signature** (ID: 25, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering a signature.

*   Leave this field blank for now.

## Tips
*   Review the form before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Use accurate and relevant information for each field.
*   Check the box to indicate agreement.
*   Leave the signature field blank for now.
