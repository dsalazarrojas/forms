# Entrepreneurship Workshop Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and workshop details for the entrepreneurship workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Confirm your booking by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Confirm Booking" field.
3. Provide your workshop details, including the date and time of the workshop, and the venue.
4. Optionally, you can provide more details about the workshop, such as the capacity and duration.
5. Finally, add any additional comments or notes about your participation or workshop.
