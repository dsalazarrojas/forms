# financial_efficiency_forum_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for the Financial Efficiency Forum. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive relevant resources and discussions in the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Details" section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Topic Interests" section, select all the topics that you are interested in, such as Financial Planning, Risk Management, and Budgeting.
3. In the "Special Requirements" section, indicate if you have any special requirements, such as True or False.
4. In the "Registration Information" section, provide any additional information about your registration.
5. Review and submit your form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required/optional): Please fill out this field with your user details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your full name.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for your phone number.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the topics you are interested in, such as Financial Planning, Risk Management, and Budgeting.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `select_one`, required/optional): If you have any special requirements, please select "True" or "False."
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, `note`, required/optional): This field is for any additional information about your registration.
 
## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive relevant resources and discussions in the forum.
