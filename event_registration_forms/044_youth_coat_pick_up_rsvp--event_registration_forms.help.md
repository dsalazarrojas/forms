# youth_coat_pick_up_rsvp - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for RSVP registration for youth coat pick up events. It captures essential information to ensure accurate and timely coat collection for participating families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information as accurately as possible.
2. Enter the event details such as event name, event date, event time, and event location.
3. Provide contact information including contact number and contact email.
4. Specify event type and event frequency for the event you are RSVPing to.
5. Optionally, provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
6. Choose your RSVP status and provide any relevant status notes.
7. Set the RSVP date and time for your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Youth Coat Pick Up RSVP** (1, text, required): This is the title of the form and is required to be filled in.
* **Event Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to.
* **Event Date** (3, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (4, time, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Pickup Time** (5, number, required): Enter the pickup time for coat collection.
* **Family Name** (6, text, required): Enter the name of the family for whom the coat is intended.
* **Contact Number** (7, text, required): Provide the contact number of the person registering.
* **Contact Email** (16, email, required): Provide the email address of the person registering.
* **Event Location** (8, text, optional): Enter the location of the event, if known.
* **Number of Children** (9, number, optional): If the coat is for multiple children, specify the number of children.
* **Child Names** (10, text, optional): If the coat is for multiple children, list their names.
* **Notes** (11, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the coat and event.
* **RSVP Status** (12, select_one, optional): Choose how your RSVP status by selecting one of the options:
	+ Confirmed
	+ Not Confirmed
	+ No Response
* **RSVP Status Note** (13, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your RSVP status.
* **RSVP Date** (14, date, optional): Enter the date you RSVPed.
* **RSVP Time** (15, time, optional): Enter the time you RSVPed.
* **Event Type** (18, select_one, optional): Choose the type of event:
	+ School
	+ Community
	+ Other
* **Event Frequency** (19, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of the event:
	+ One Time
	+ Repeating
	+ Ongoing
* **Event Frequency Note** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event frequency.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check the event details for correctness.
* Enter any additional notes or comments to ensure your RSVP is clear and concise.
* Review this help guide for any questions or concerns about the form.
* If you have any issues with coat collection, contact the coat coordinator for assistance.
