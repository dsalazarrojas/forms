# Tournament Vendor Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by vendors to provide detailed information about their signage requirements for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the general information section (fields 1-3) with your company name, contact person, and email address.
* In the vendor information section (fields 4-6), provide your company's name and address, as well as a brief description of your signage.
* In the order information section (fields 7-9), specify the type and quantity of signage you require.
* In the order details section (fields 10-12), provide any additional comments or notes about your order.
* In the vendor signage image section (field 13), upload a photo or image of your signage.
* In the submit section (field 14), click the "Submit" button to complete your order.
* In the order review section (field 15), review your order details before submitting.
* In the signature section (field 16), sign and date your order to confirm.

* **Vendor Signage Image** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Upload a photo or image of your signage.
