# Kink And BDSM Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to register for a Kink and BDSM event. It collects information about the participant and the event they are registering for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Participant Information" section, which asks for your basic contact information.
2.  In the "Event Details" section, provide any additional details about the event you are registering for.
3.  Finally, in the "Participant Preferences" section, indicate your preferences and boundaries for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information (participant_information)**:
    *   This field is optional and asks for your basic contact information.
    *   Please provide your name, email address, and any other contact details you feel comfortable sharing.
*   **Event Details (event_details)**:
    *   Provide any additional details about the event you are registering for.
    *   This field is optional, so only fill it out if you have information you think is relevant to your registration.
*   **Participant Preferences (participant_preferences)**:
    *   In this field, specify your preferences and boundaries for the event.
    *   This is your chance to communicate your comfort levels, interests, and any requirements or restrictions.
    *   Be as specific as possible so that event organizers can tailor the experience to your needs.

## Tips
Remember to fill out the form thoroughly and honestly, as this information will help event organizers provide a better experience for you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
