# Comic Convention Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the form for attendees to sign up for the comic convention, providing the necessary information for registration, badge printing, and accommodation needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all required fields, which are marked with <Required> in the field explanations below. You may leave optional fields blank if you're unsure or do not want to provide information. Make sure to click "Submit" once you've filled out all necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Attendee Full Name** (<type:text>, <required:True>)
Legal name for badge printing.

*   Please enter your full name as it should be printed on your convention badge.

### 2. **Badge Name** (<type:text>, <required:True>)
Name to display on your convention badge.

*   Enter the name you'd like to be displayed on your convention badge.

### 3. **Email Address** (<type:email>, <required:True>)
For registration confirmation and updates.

*   Enter your email address to receive updates about the convention, including registration confirmation and important announcements.

### 4. **Phone Number** (<type:text>, <required:True>)
Contact number for event notifications.

*   Enter your phone number to receive calls or texts from the convention staff with important information, such as schedule changes or last-minute updates.

### 5. **Date of Birth** (<type:date>, <required:True>)
For age verification.

*   Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.

### 6. **Ticket Type** (<type:select_one>, <required:True>)
Select your admission type.

*   Choose the type of ticket that suits your needs: Single Day - Friday, Single Day - Saturday, Single Day - Sunday, Weekend Pass, VIP Pass, or Platinum VIP.

### 7. **Number of Tickets** (<type:number>, <required:True>)
How many tickets do you need.

*   Enter the number of tickets you require for yourself and any guests you're attending with.

### 8. **Attendee Category** (<type:select_one>, <required:True>)
What best describes you.

*   Choose your category: General Attendee, Cosplayer, Vendor/Exhibitor, Press/Media, Industry Professional, Guest.

### 9. **Cosplay Contest Participation** (<type:select_one>, <required:True>)
Will you participate in the cosplay contest.

*   Choose Yes Register Me, No Not Interested, or Maybe.

### 10. **Favorite Genres** (<type:select_multiple>, <required:True>)
Select your interests.

*   Choose one or more of the following: Superheroes, Anime/Manga, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Horror, Gaming, Comics, Movies/TV.

### 11. **Panels of Interest** (<type:select_multiple>, <required:True>)
What type of panels interest you.

*   Choose one or more of the following: Artist Alley, Celebrity Q&A, Industry Panels, Workshops, Screenings, Gaming Tournaments, Cosplay Workshops.

### 12. **Accessibility Needs** (<type:select_one>, <required:True>)
Do you require any accommodations.

*   Choose None, Wheelchair Access, Sign Language Interpreter, Service Animal, or Other.

### 13. **Dietary Restrictions** (<type:select_multiple>, <required:False>)
For VIP events with catering.

*   If you have any dietary restrictions, choose one or more of the following: None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, Other.

### 14. **T-Shirt Size** (<type:select_one>, <required:False>)
For convention swag bag.

*   Choose your T-Shirt size: XS, S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL.

### 15. **Interested in Merchandise** (<type:select_one>, <required:False>)
Will you purchase convention merchandise.

*   Choose Definitely Yes, Probably, Not Sure, Probably Not, or False.

### 16. **Payment Method** (<type:select_one>, <required:True>)
How will you pay.

*   Choose your payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay.

### 17. **Promo Code** (<type:text>, <required:False>)
Enter discount code if available.

*   If you have a promo code, enter it here to receive discounts.

### 18. **How Did You Hear About Us** (<type:select_one>, <required:False>)
Where did you learn about this convention.

*   Choose how you heard about us: Social Media, Friend Referral, Website, Advertisement, Previous Attendance, Other.

### 19. **Additional Comments** (<type:text>, <required:False>)
Any questions or special requests.

*   If you have any questions or special requests, enter them here.
