# public_policy_panel_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "public_policy_panel_sponsorship_form" is designed to collect sponsorship information from individuals or organizations for public policy panels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk *) with accurate information.
2. Select the correct option for the "Event Type" field from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter dates for the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields if applicable.
4. Provide a description for "Sponsorship Details" to explain your sponsorship request.
5. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the public policy panel.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter the address of the organization or individual sponsoring the public policy panel.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Enter the name and title of the contact person for the sponsoring organization or individual.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Event Type** (Event Type, select_one, required): Select the type of event from the available options.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): Enter the start date of the event, if applicable.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the end date of the event, if applicable.
* **Sponsorship Details** (Sponsorship Details, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your sponsorship request.
* **Sponsorship Amount** (Sponsorship Amount, number, required): Enter the amount of sponsorship requested.
* **Terms Accepted** (Terms Accepted, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the available options indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
