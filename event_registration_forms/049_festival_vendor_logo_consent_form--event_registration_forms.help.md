# Festival Vendor Logo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Vendor Logo Consent Form is a document used to collect information from festival vendors regarding their logo usage and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and its corresponding options.
2. Answer the questions according to your preferences and requirements.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Logo Consent** (Vendor Logo Consent, select_one, required/optional): 
    This field asks whether you agree to allow your logo to be displayed at the festival. You can choose to allow or deny this permission.
- **Brand Assets** (Brand Assets, select_multiple, required/optional): 
    Select the types of brand assets you are willing to share with the festival, such as images, videos, or audio files.
- **Vendor Information** (Vendor Information, text, required/optional): 
    Enter your vendor information, such as your name and contact details.
- **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required/optional): 
    Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
- **Event Promotions** (Event Promotions, text, required/optional): 
    Enter any event promotions you'd like to be associated with your brand.
- **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): 
    Enter your email address so we can contact you with important updates and information.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): 
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you with important updates and information.
- **Note** (Note, note, required/optional): 
    Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
- **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): 
    Enter the date you'd like to be considered for logo usage.
- **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): 
    Enter the time you'd like to be considered for logo usage.
- **Festival Information** (Festival Information, select_one, required/optional): 
    Indicate whether you have festival-specific information, such as location or venue.
- **Vendor Assets** (Vendor Assets, text, required/optional): 
    Enter any assets you'd like to share with the festival, such as logos or marketing materials.
- **Event Information** (Event Information, select_multiple, required/optional): 
    Select the event information you'd like to be associated with your brand, such as location or schedule.
- **Logo Assets** (Logo Assets, select_multiple, required/optional): 
    Select the types of logo assets you'd like to share with the festival.
- **Event Details** (Event Details, text, required/optional): 
    Enter any additional details about your event. 

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting your responses.
* Review your submissions before submitting the form.
