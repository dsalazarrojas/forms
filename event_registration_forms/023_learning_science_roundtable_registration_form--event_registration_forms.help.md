# **Learning Science Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide**

## **Purpose**
This form is designed to collect information for the Learning Science Roundtable. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the roundtable event, including its title, date, time, location, number of attendees, topic, facilitator, and contact information.

## **How To Complete This Form**
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Roundtable Details** information, which includes the title of the roundtable.
2. Enter the **Date Of Roundtable**, **Time Of Roundtable**, and **Location Of Roundtable** in the respective fields.
3. Enter the **Number Of Attendees** expected to attend.
4. Enter the **Roundtable Topic** of the discussion.
5. Enter the **Roundtable Facilitator** who will lead the discussion.
6. Enter your **Organization** (if applicable).
7. Enter your **Email** address (required).
8. Enter your **Phone** number (required).
9. You may optionally enter any **Comments** about the roundtable.
10. Review and agree to the **Terms And Conditions**.
11. Click the **Submit Roundtable** button to submit the form.
12. You may click on **Back To Roundtable** to return to the previous page if needed.

## **Field-by-Field Explanation**

* **Roundtable Details**: Enter a brief description of the roundtable event.
* **Date Of Roundtable**: Enter the date of the roundtable event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Roundtable**: Enter the time of the roundtable event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Location Of Roundtable**: Enter the location of the roundtable event.
* **Number Of Attendees**: Enter the expected number of attendees for the roundtable.
* **Roundtable Topic**: Enter the topic of the roundtable discussion.
* **Roundtable Facilitator**: Enter the name of the facilitator leading the discussion.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (required).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (required).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the roundtable.
* **Terms And Conditions**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the roundtable.
* **Submit Roundtable**: Click this button to submit the form.
* **Cancel Roundtable**: Click this button to cancel the form submission.
* **Back To Roundtable**: Click this button to return to the previous page.
* **Next Roundtable**: Click this button to proceed to the next page (if applicable).
* **Previous Roundtable**: Click this button to return to the previous page (if applicable).

Note: The **Roundtable Page 2-10** fields are currently unused and do not require completion.
