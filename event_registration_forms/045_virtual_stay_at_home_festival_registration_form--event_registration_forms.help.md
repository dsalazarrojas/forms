# Virtual Stay At Home Festival Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Virtual Stay At Home Festival, a unique opportunity to experience the magic of the festival from the comfort of your own home. The form collects information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **User ID** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to verify your identity.
3. Choose the type of **Festival Type** that best describes your experience (Online or In Person).
4. Select a **Festival Location**, if applicable.
5. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** of the festival.
6. Provide a brief **Event Description** to give us an idea of what to expect.
7. If applicable, enter the **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** to ensure we can reach you for any updates or questions.
8. Add any additional **Festival Notes** as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`3`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **User ID** (`4`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier given by the festival organizers.
* **Password** (`5`, text, optional): Enter your password if required.
* **Confirm Password** (`6`, text, optional): Confirm the password for security purposes.
* **Festival Type** (`7`, select_one, required): Choose between Online Festival or In Person.
* **Festival Location** (`8`, text, optional): Enter the location of the festival, if applicable.
* **Event Date** (`9`, date, required): Enter the date of the festival event.
* **Event Time** (`10`, time, required): Enter the time of the festival event.
* **Event Description** (`11`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the festival event.
* **Contact Name** (`12`, text, required): Enter your name as a contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`13`, email, required): Enter your email address as a contact.
* **Contact Phone** (`14`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as a contact.
* **Festival Note** (`15`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your festival experience.
* **Festival Note 2** (`16`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your festival experience.
* **Event Date 1** (`17`, date, required): Enter the date of the first festival event.
* **Event Time 1** (`18`, time, required): Enter the time of the first festival event.
* **Festival Type 1** (`19`, select_multiple, required): Choose between Online Festival or In Person for the first festival event.
* **Festival Location 1** (`20`, text, required): Enter the location of the first festival event.
* **Festival Note 1** (`21`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the first festival event.
* **Festival Location 2** (`22`, text, optional): Enter the location of the second festival event.
* **Festival Note 2** (`23`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the second festival event.
* **Festival Type 2** (`24`, select_multiple, required): Choose between Online Festival or In Person for the second festival event.
