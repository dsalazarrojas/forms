# Cardio Fitness Class Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to join our cardio fitness program. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process and to ensure that you're aware of any health-related concerns or waivers that may impact your participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the form carefully and provide accurate information for each field.
2.  Enter your participant's full name, contact email, and contact phone number in the relevant fields.
3.  Indicate your age and select the correct experience level that describes you best.
4.  Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number in case of an emergency.
5.  If you have any known health concerns or conditions, please describe them in the provided field.
6.  Select the agreement to participate and confirm your initials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Contact Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive registration updates.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number where your emergency contact can reach you.
*   **Age** (Number, Required): Please enter your age to ensure you meet the program's eligibility requirements.
*   **Your Cardio Experience Level** (Select One, Required): Choose the one that best describes your current fitness level:
    +   Beginner (New to cardio)
    +   Intermediate (Regularly active)
    +   Advanced (High fitness level)
*   **Any known health conditions or concerns?** (Text, Optional): If you have any known health concerns or conditions, please describe them here.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **I agree to the participation waiver and terms of use** (Select One, Required): Please select "I Agree" to confirm your understanding of our program's terms and conditions.
*   **Participant Initials for confirmation** (Text, Required): Please type your initials for confirmation of your registration.
