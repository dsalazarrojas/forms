# winter_plant_market_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register vendors and attendees for the winter plant market event. It collects personal and contact information for both vendors and attendees, as well as their attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the `first_name` field. This is a text field that requires only your first name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `last_name` field. This is another text field for your last name.
3. Add your **Email** address in the `email` field. This is the email address associated with your account.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the `phone` field. This is for communication purposes.
5. Enter your **Vendor Name** in the `vendor_name` field. This is the name of your business or organization.
6. Add your **Vendor Email** in the `vendor_email` field. This is the email address associated with your business or organization.
7. Enter your **Vendor Phone** number in the `vendor_phone` field. This is for business communication purposes.
8. Select your **Attendance Status** by choosing one of the two options provided:
   - If you will attend the event, select "True".
   - If you will not attend, select "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Add your email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, Email, Optional): Add the email address associated with your business or organization.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number for your business communication purposes.
* **Will You Attend** (`attendance`, Select One, Optional): Select "True" if you will attend the event, "False" otherwise.
