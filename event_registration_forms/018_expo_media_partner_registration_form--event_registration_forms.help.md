# Expo Media Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from media partners for an event. It is used to gather relevant details about the media outlet, including coverage plans, credential needed, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the Event Organizer.
2. Provide your media outlet's name and any additional information about it.
3. Select the coverage plans for the event.
4. Specify the credential needed for the event.
5. Enter your contact person's name and email address.
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Select the coverage date and time.
8. Enter any additional notes about the coverage.
9. Select the type of credential needed (e.g., Press, Media).
10. Specify the partner's status (e.g., Approved, Pending, Declined).
11. Enter any additional notes about the partner's status.
12. Select the type of media (e.g., TV, Radio, Print).
13. If applicable, select the partner's status for the past partnership (e.g., Approved, Pending, Declined).
14. Enter the date of the last contact.
15. Enter any additional notes about the last contact.
16. Enter the name of the person who last contacted the partner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Your name as the person organizing the event.
* **Media Outlet**: The name of your media outlet.
* **Coverage Plans**: Select one or multiple plans for coverage of the event (e.g., press conference, interview, review).
* **Credential Needed**: The type of credential needed for coverage (e.g., press pass, media badge).
* **Contact Person**: Your name and email address.
* **Email**: Your email address (make sure it's up to date).
* **Phone**: Your phone number (optional).
* **Coverage Date**: The date you would like to be covered.
* **Coverage Time**: The time you would like to be covered.
* **Coverage Note**: Any additional notes about the coverage.
* **Credential Note**: Any additional notes about the credential needed.
* **Agreement**: The type of agreement you are looking for (e.g., press release, media release).
* **Agreement Attachment**: An attachment with the agreement (optional).
* **Approved**: The status of your agreement (e.g., approved, not approved, pending).
* **Approved Note**: Any additional notes about the approval.
* **Partner Status**: The status of your partnership with us (e.g., approved, pending, declined).
* **Partner Status Note**: Any additional notes about the partner's status.
* **Media Type**: The type of media you represent (e.g., TV, radio, print).
* **Partner Since**: The duration of your partnership with us (e.g., 1 year, 2 years).
* **Partner Since Note**: Any additional notes about the partnership.
* **Last Contact**: The date of the last contact with the partner.
* **Last Contact Note**: Any additional notes about the last contact.
* **Last Contacted By**: The name of the person who last contacted the partner.
