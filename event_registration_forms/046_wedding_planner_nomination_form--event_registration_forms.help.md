# Wedding Planner Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Planner Nomination Form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to be considered as a wedding planner. The form is designed to gather essential details about their qualifications, experience, and achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you about your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nominate Wedding Planner** (Yes/No): 
  * Please answer whether you wish to be considered as a wedding planner.
* **Planner's Name**: 
  * Enter your name as you wish it to be listed on the form.
* **Planner's Experience**: 
  * Enter a number representing the level of experience you have.
* **Projects Completed** (Yes/No): 
  * Check Yes if you have completed projects.
* **Project Details**: 
  * Provide any additional information about your projects.
* **Planner's Achievements**: 
  * Answer whether you have achieved anything significant in your projects.
* **Project Type** (Yes/No): 
  * Check Yes if you have experience with this project type.
* **Project Location** (Yes/No): 
  * Check Yes if you have experience with projects in this location.
* **Project Start Date**: 
  * Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date**: 
  * Enter the end date of your project.
* **Planner's Contact**: 
  * Enter your contact information so that we can reach out to you.
* **Planner's Email**: 
  * Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
