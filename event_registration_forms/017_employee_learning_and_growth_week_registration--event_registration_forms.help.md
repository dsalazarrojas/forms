# employee_learning_and_growth_week_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees for the Employee Learning and Growth Week event registration process. The form helps gather relevant details about the event, employee preferences, and training evaluations to tailor the event experience to each participant's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field. If a field is marked as required, it is essential to fill it in for the form to be valid.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): Enter your name and any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about the event you'd like to note or discuss.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`, text, optional): Share any additional information about your department, team, or workgroup.
* **Training Sessions** (`training_sessions`, text, optional): Mention any training sessions you've attended or plan to attend in the future.
* **Training Preferences** (`training_preferences`, text, optional): Share your interests or preferences for training topics or sessions.
* **Employee Interests** (`employee_interests`, text, optional): Tell us about your interests or hobbies outside of work.
* **Manager Info** (`manager_info`, text, optional): Provide your manager's name and contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Training Evaluations** (`training_evaluations`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the effectiveness of our training sessions.
* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, optional): Leave any feedback or suggestions for future training events.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be specific when sharing your interests or preferences for training topics.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
