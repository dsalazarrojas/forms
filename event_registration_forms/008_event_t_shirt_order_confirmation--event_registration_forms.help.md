# Event T Shirt Order Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm and validate the participant's t-shirt order for an event. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your order is processed correctly and that you receive the right size and color t-shirt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Fill in the participant's ID if required.
3. Choose the desired shirt size from the options provided.
4. Select the quantity of t-shirts you want to order.
5. Choose the desired t-shirt colors.
6. Enter the participant's name.
7. Enter the participant's email address.
8. Enter the participant's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_id** (`participant_id`, number, required: false): Enter your participant ID to confirm your identity.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): This field is not currently used, so you can leave it blank.
* **shirt_size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required: false): Choose the size of the t-shirt you want to order from the options: Large, Medium, Small, X-Large, X-Small.
* **quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of t-shirts you want to order.
* **t_shirt_color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more colors for your t-shirt from the options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates on your order.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Please double-check the fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* Review your order confirmation carefully to ensure everything is accurate.
* If you need to make any changes, contact us immediately.
