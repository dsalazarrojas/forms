# Workshop Instructor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from workshop instructors about their workshop, including details such as their name, contact information, workshop title, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form to ensure that we can process your workshop information accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the name of your **Workshop** in the "Workshop Name" field.
5. Write a brief **Bio** about yourself in the "Bio" field (optional).
6. Provide a more detailed **Bio Text** about your workshop in the "Bio Text" field (optional).
7. Select the type of **Workshop** you are leading (Workshop, Online, or In-person) in the "Workshop Type" field.
8. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the "Date Of Birth" field (optional).
9. Enter your **Time Of Birth** in the "Time Of Birth" field (optional).
10. Select your **Gender** (Male, Female, or Other) in the "Gender" field (optional).
11. If applicable, provide information about the **Assigned Tool** used for your workshop.
12. If applicable, upload an **Output File** related to your workshop.
13. Enter a brief **Description** about your workshop in the "Description" field (optional).
14. If applicable, provide any additional **Form IDs** related to your workshop.
15. If applicable, provide information about the **Assigned Tool 2** used for your workshop.
16. If applicable, select a **Category 2** for your workshop.
17. If applicable, upload an **Output File 2** related to your workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Your name as the workshop instructor.
* **Email** (Required): Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Required): Your contact phone number.
* **Workshop Name** (Required): The title of your workshop.
* **Bio** (Optional): A brief bio about yourself.
* **Bio Text** (Optional): A more detailed bio about your workshop.
* **Workshop Type** (Required): The type of workshop you are leading (Workshop, Online, or In-person).
* **Date Of Birth** (Optional): Your date of birth.
* **Time Of Birth** (Optional): Your time of birth.
* **Gender** (Optional): Your gender.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): If you have an assigned tool, please provide information about it.
* **Output File** (Optional): If you have an output file, please upload it.
* **Description** (Optional): A brief description about your workshop.
* **Form IDs** (Optional): If applicable, provide any additional form IDs related to your workshop.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (Optional): If you have an assigned tool 2, please provide information about it.
* **Category 2** (Optional): If applicable, select a category 2 for your workshop.
* **Output File 2** (Optional): If you have an output file 2, please upload it.
