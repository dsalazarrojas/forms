# Community Storytelling Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Storytelling Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from potential sponsors to support community storytelling events. This form helps us understand your interests, goals, and preferences for sponsorship opportunities, enabling us to tailor our events to your needs and provide a mutually beneficial partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your sponsorship details, such as your name or organization, contact person, and contact email.
2. Select your organization type and preferred sponsorship level.
3. Choose the event(s) you'd like to sponsor and select the benefits you're interested in.
4. If applicable, upload your logo, provide your marketing goals, and specify your target audience.
5. Let us know if you've previously sponsored community events, your preferred payment method, and any special requests or requirements.
6. Finally, tell us how you heard about this sponsorship opportunity and submit your form by selecting today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Name or Organization** (`sponsor_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name or your organization's name, such as "John Doe" or "ABC Corporation".
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): This is the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact phone number.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your organization type from the following options:
	+ Individual
	+ Small Business
	+ Corporation
	+ Nonprofit Organization
	+ Government Agency
	+ Educational Institution
	+ Community Group
	+ Other
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's mailing address, if different from your billing address.
* **Website URL** (`website_url`, `text`, required): Provide your organization's website URL, if applicable.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired sponsorship tier from the following options:
	+ Bronze Sponsor - $100
	+ Silver Sponsor - $250
	+ Gold Sponsor - $500
	+ Platinum Sponsor - $1,000
	+ Diamond Sponsor - $2,500
	+ Custom Amount
* **Custom Sponsorship Amount** (`custom_sponsorship_amount`, `number`, optional): If you've selected Custom Amount, specify your desired sponsorship amount.
* **Event Preference** (`event_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose which storytelling event you'd like to sponsor:
	+ Spring Storytelling Festival
	+ Summer Nights Stories
	+ Fall Harvest Tales
	+ Winter Stories Gathering
	+ Monthly Story Circle
	+ Any Event (Organizer's Choice)
	+ All Events (Season Sponsor)
* **Sponsorship Benefits** (`sponsorship_benefits`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the sponsorship benefits that interest you:
	+ Logo on event materials
	+ Social media recognition
	+ Verbal acknowledgment during event
	+ Booth or table at event
	+ Program advertisement
	+ VIP seating
	+ Meet the storytellers
	+ Press release mention
* **Logo Submission** (`logo_upload_note`, `note`, optional): You will be contacted to submit your logo file if you've uploaded it.
* **Marketing Goals** (`marketing_goals`, `text`, optional): Describe your marketing goals for this sponsorship.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, optional): Describe your ideal customer or demographic.
* **Previous Sponsorship** (`previous_sponsorship`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you've sponsored community events before:
	+ Yes, multiple times
	+ Yes, once
	+ No, this is my first time
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ PayPal
	+ Invoice (for organizations)
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): If different from your business address, enter your billing address.
* **Tax Information** (`tax_id_note`, `note`, optional): You may be required to provide tax information for an invoice.
* **Tax ID or EIN Number** (`tax_id_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your tax ID or EIN number, if applicable.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Specify any special requests or requirements.
* **How Heard About** (`how_heard_about`, `select_one`, required): Tell us how you heard about this sponsorship opportunity:
	+ Social Media
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Community Bulletin
	+ Word of Mouth
	+ Previous Event
	+ Website
	+ Other
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Select today's date to submit your form.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for guidance.
- Please ensure your form is complete and accurate to avoid delays in processing your sponsorship.
- We appreciate your interest in sponsoring our community storytelling events and look forward to partnering with you!
