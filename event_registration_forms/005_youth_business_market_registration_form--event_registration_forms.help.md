# Youth Business Market Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Business Market Registration Form is a tool for business owners or representatives to provide event registration information for a youth business market event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are registering only one business entity per submission.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
3. For the "Event" field, select the correct answer to indicate if the business will be participating in any of the listed events.
4. For the "Events" field, select all the events the business will be participating in.
5. You can add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<code>first_name</code>)
• **First Name**: This field is required and asks for the first name of the person registering the business.

### Last Name (<code>last_name</code>)
• **Last Name**: This field is required and asks for the last name of the person registering the business.

### Business Name (<code>business_name</code>)
• **Business Name**: This field is required and asks for the name of the business being registered.

### Business Description (<code>business_description</code>)
• **Business Description**: This field is required and asks for a brief description of the business being registered.

### Date (<code>date</code>)
• **Date**: This field is required and asks for the date of the event the business will be participating in.

### Time (<code>time</code>)
• **Time**: This field is required and asks for the time of the event the business will be participating in.

### Email (<code>email</code>)
• **Email**: This field is required and asks for the email address of the person registering the business.

### Phone (<code>phone</code>)
• **Phone**: This field is not required and asks for the phone number of the business or person registering the business.

### Event (<code>select_one_event</code>)
• **Event**: This field is required and asks if the business will be participating in any of the listed events.

### Events (<code>select_multiple_events</code>)
• **Events**: This field is required and asks you to select all the events the business will be participating in.

### Notes (<code>notes</code>)
• **Notes**: This field is not required and asks for any additional comments or notes about the registration.

# Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to complete the registration process.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Only submit one registration per business entity.
