# Popcorn Pickup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Popcorn Pickup Registration form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for the Popcorn Pickup program. This form will help ensure that the correct pickup details are provided for each student, ensuring a smooth and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the School Information section with your child's school details.
2. Select the Pickup Time that best suits your schedule.
3. In the Pre Order section, enter any special requests or notes for the pickup.
4. Choose the Pickup Date for the snack pickup.
5. Enter the Pickup Location where you will be picking up your child's snack.
6. In the Pickup Details section, add any additional information or notes about the pickup.
7. Review and select the Policy Acknowledgement to confirm you understand and agree to the program's policy.
8. Select the Pickup Time Slot that works best for you.
9. Enter the Pickup Contact information (email address) in case we need to contact you.
10. Finally, add any additional Pickup Notes in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Information** (`school_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your child's school name and other relevant school details.
- **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of day you would like to pick up your child's snack.
  - If you have a specific time, please select it.
  - If you are unsure, you can select "As Soon As Possible" to pick up the snack at the earliest time.
- **Pre Order** (`pre_order`, `text`, required: false): If you have any special requests or notes for the pickup, enter them here.
- **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you would like to pick up your child's snack.
- **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where you will be picking up your child's snack.
- **Pickup Details** (`pickup_details`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information about the pickup, such as any allergies or dietary restrictions.
- **Policy Acknowledgement** (`policy_acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required: false): Read and agree to the program's policy.
  - Select "I understand and agree to the policy."
  - Select "I do not understand and do not agree to the policy."
- **Pickup Time Slot** (`pickup_time_slot`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose a convenient time slot for the pickup.
  - Select "Morning" for the earliest time slot.
  - Select "Afternoon" for a later time slot.
  - Select "Evening" for the latest time slot.
- **Pickup Contact** (`pickup_contact`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in case we need to contact you.
- **Pickup Notes** (`pickup_notes`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the pickup.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any issues with the pickup.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
