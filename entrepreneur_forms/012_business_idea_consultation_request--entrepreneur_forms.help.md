# Business Idea Consultation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from entrepreneurs seeking consultation on their business ideas. Fill out this form to request a consultation session with one of our experts, and we will review your submission and schedule a meeting at a time that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact email address to ensure we can reach you for scheduling purposes.
2. Provide a brief description of your business idea in 200 words or less.
3. Select the current stage of development of your business idea (Concept, Prototype, Early revenue, or Scaling).
4. Specify the primary customer segment you are targeting with your business idea.
5. Choose the preferred format for the consultation session (in person, video call, or phone call).
6. Indicate your desired outcome of the consultation (e.g., feedback, guidance, or mentorship).
7. If applicable, mention any specific dates or times when you are available for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Entrepreneur name** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as the entrepreneur requesting the consultation.
- **Contact email** (`name`, `email`, **required**): Provide the best email address for us to contact you for scheduling purposes.
- **Business idea summary** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Write a short description of your business idea, 200 words or less.
- **Target market** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): Enter a brief description of the primary customer segment you are targeting.
- **Stage of development** (`title`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the current stage of development of your business idea (Concept, Prototype, Early revenue, or Scaling).
- **Funding needed** (`title`, `number`, **optional**): Enter the estimated funding requirement for your business idea (if applicable).
- **Desired outcome** (`title`, `text`, **required**): Specify what you hope to achieve from the consultation session (e.g., feedback, guidance, or mentorship).
- **Preferred session format** (`title`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose the preferred format for the consultation session (in person, video call, or phone call).
- **Availability** (`title`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, provide specific dates or times when you are available for the consultation.
