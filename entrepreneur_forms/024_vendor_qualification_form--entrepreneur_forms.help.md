# Vendor Qualification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from potential partners or vendors. It includes questions about business and contact information, vendor type, payment terms, and notes. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each section carefully**, ensuring you have all necessary information before submitting.
2.  Review and update your answers **before** pressing **Submit**.
3. Make sure to save your progress in case you need to stop and resume later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Information (Business Information, text, required: false)**: Please enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact (Contact, text, required: false)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person in charge of handling your business or partnership.
* **Business Address (Business Address, text, required: false)**: Provide your business's physical address, including street number and postal code.
* **Vendor Information (Vendor Information, text, required: false)**: Enter any other relevant information about your business that we should know.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Business Type (Business Type, select_one, required: true)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Retail
	+ Wholesale
	+ Both
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms, text, required: false)**: Specify any payment terms or conditions.
* **Notes (Notes, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit (Submit, text, required: true)**: Press this button to submit your answers.

## Tips

- Double-check your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- Save your progress for reference later.
- If you have any questions, please contact us for assistance.
