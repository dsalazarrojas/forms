# Business Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Business Credit Application Form. The form is used to gather essential information about the business owner and their business to help us assess their creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each section carefully** and ensure you understand the required information before proceeding.
2. **Fill out each field accurately** and thoroughly, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to delays in processing your credit application.
3. **Save your progress** periodically to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **2. Applicant Name**: Your full legal name as registered with your government-issued ID or passport.
* **3. Email Address**: Your business email address or personal email address associated with your business.
* **4. Phone Number**: Your phone number where you can be reached for further verification.
* **5. Personal Address**: Your current residential address, not your business address.
* **6. Last 4 Digits of Social Security Number**: For identity verification purposes, please enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number.
* **7. Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **9. Business Legal Name**: The registered name of your business.
* **10. Business Structure**: Select the structure of your business from the options provided.
* **11. Business Address**: Your business address, including the street address and postal code.
* **12. Date Business Established**: The date your business was established.
* **13. Industry Type**: Select the type of industry your business operates in.
* **14. Number of Employees**: The number of employees in your business, including yourself.
* **15. Annual Revenue**: Your business' most recent annual revenue.
* **17. Amount of Credit Requested**: The total amount of credit you are requesting.
* **18. Type of Credit**: Select the type of credit you are requesting.
* **19. Use of Funds**: Explain how you plan to use the credit.
* **20. Preferred Repayment Period**: Select how long you prefer to repay the credit.
* **22. Average Monthly Revenue**: Your business' recent average monthly revenue.
* **23. Existing Business Debt**: The total outstanding business loans.
* **24. Other Credit Lines or Accounts**: Select whether you have other business credit lines or accounts.
* **25. Bank References**: The name of your primary banking institution.

Note: This guide is meant to provide a general understanding of the form and its fields. Please refer to the original form and its fields for specific requirements and guidelines.
