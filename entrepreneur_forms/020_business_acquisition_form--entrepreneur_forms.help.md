# Business Acquisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Acquisition Form is designed to collect information from buyers and sellers about a business acquisition. It helps to gather details about the buyer and seller entities, the business being acquired, and the terms of the transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the buyer information sections (Buyer Name, Buyer Email, Buyer Phone, Buyer Address, etc.)
2. Fill out the seller information sections (Seller Name, Seller Company Name, Seller Email, Seller Phone, etc.)
3. Provide details about the business (Business Name, Business Type, Industry, Years in Operation, Number of Employees, Financial Information, etc.)
4. Answer questions about the transaction (Asking Price, Annual Revenue, Annual Profit, Assets Included in Sale, Liabilities, Transaction Details, etc.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Full Name** (`buyer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of the buyer entity.
* **Buyer Type** (`buyer_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of buyer entity (Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Investment Group, or Other).
* **Buyer Email** (`buyer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email of the buyer entity.
* **Buyer Phone** (`buyer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the buyer entity.
* **Buyer Address** (`buyer_address`, `text`, required): Enter the mailing address of the buyer entity.
* **Seller Full Name** (`seller_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of the seller entity.
* **Seller Company Name** (`seller_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company being sold.
* **Seller Email** (`seller_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email of the seller entity.
* **Seller Phone** (`seller_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the seller entity.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal name of the business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of business entity (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, S-Corporation, or Other).
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Choose the industry of the business (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Food Service, Professional Services, Real Estate, or Other).
* **Years in Operation** (`years_in_operation`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of years the business has operated (Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, 10-20 years, or More than 20 years).
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of employees of the business (1-5, 6-10, 11-20, 21-50, 51-100, or More than 100).
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most recent annual revenue of the business (Under $100,000, $100,000-$500,000, $500,000-$1,000,000, $1,000,000-$5,000,000, $5,000,000-$10,000,000, or More than $10,000,000).
* **Annual Profit** (`annual_profit`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most recent annual profit of the business (Under $50,000, $50,000-$100,000, $100,000-$500,000, $500,000-$1,000,000, More than $1,000,000, or Not Profitable).
* **Assets Included in Sale** (`assets_included`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the assets included in the sale (Real Estate, Equipment, Inventory, Intellectual Property, Customer Lists, Brand and Trademarks, Vehicles, or Accounts Receivable).
* **Liabilities** (`liabilities`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether there are liabilities to assume (No Liabilities, Minor Liabilities, Moderate Liabilities, Significant Liabilities, or To Be Determined).
* **Asking Price** (`asking_price`, `number`, required): Enter the requested purchase price of the business.
* **Proposed Transaction Structure** (`proposed_structure`, `select_one`, required): Choose the proposed structure of the transaction (Asset Purchase, Stock Purchase, Merger, Earn-Out Agreement, or Other).
* **Financing Method** (`financing_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the financing method of the transaction (All Cash, Bank Financing, Seller Financing, Investor Funding, or Combination).

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as the information provided will be used in the transaction process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification or guidance from the relevant parties.
* Please note that this form is for business-to-business transactions, not personal use.
