# Client Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for clients and potential clients to contact us with inquiries, questions, or feedback. It is used to gather information about the purpose of the contact, the client's relationship with us, and the best way to respond to their message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your primary email address in the Email Address field.
3.  Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
4.  Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code in the Street Address, City, State, and ZIP Code fields (if applicable).
5.  Enter your company name and website (if applicable).
6.  Select your job title.
7.  Select your industry from the list provided.
8.  Choose the type of contact you are (e.g., prospective client, current client, etc.).
9.  Select the purpose of your inquiry from the list provided.
10.  Enter a brief summary of the subject of your inquiry in the Subject field.
11.  Type a detailed message with your question, concern, or feedback in the Message field.
12.  Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone call, text message, etc.).
13.  Select the best time to contact you (if applicable).
14.  Indicate the urgency level of your inquiry (e.g., low, medium, high, etc.).
15.  If you would like to receive our newsletter, select 'True'.
16.  If you know how you heard about us, select the corresponding option (e.g., search engine, social media, website, etc.).
17.  If you have any additional information to share, enter it in the Additional Information field (if applicable).
18.  Finally, click the submit button to send your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Best contact number.
* **Street Address** (5, text, optional): Complete mailing address.
* **City** (6, text, optional): null.
* **State** (7, text, optional): null.
* **ZIP Code** (8, text, optional): null.
* **Company Name** (9, text, optional): Your business name.
* **Company Website** (10, text, optional): Business website URL.
* **Job Title** (11, text, optional): Your position.
* **Industry** (12, select_one, optional): Your business sector.
* **Type of Contact** (13, select_one, required): Your relationship with us (e.g., prospective client, current client, etc.).
* **Inquiry Type** (14, select_one, required): Purpose of contact (e.g., sales question, support request, etc.).
* **Subject** (15, text, required): Brief topic summary.
* **Message** (16, text, required): Your detailed message.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (17, select_one, required): How should we respond to you (e.g., email, phone call, text message, etc.).
* **Best Time to Contact** (18, select_one, optional): When are you available (if applicable).
* **Urgency Level** (19, select_one, required): How urgent is this contact (e.g., low, medium, high, etc.).
* **Would You Like to Receive Our Newsletter?** (20, select_one, optional): Monthly updates.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (21, select_one, optional): Referral source (if applicable).
* **Additional Information** (22, text, optional): Anything else to share.
* **Submission Date** (23, date, required): null.
