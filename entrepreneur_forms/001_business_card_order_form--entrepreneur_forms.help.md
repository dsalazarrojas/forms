# Business Card Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a business card order. It collects contact and shipping information, card design preferences, and payment details to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information section (fields 2-5) with your complete name, email address, phone number, and company name.
2. In the card design preferences section (fields 9-11), select the quantity of cards, finish type, and color scheme you prefer.
3. If you have a custom business card design, you can upload it in field 12.
4. Fill in the shipping information section (fields 14-18) with your shipping address, city, state/province, postal code, and country.
5. Select your preferred shipping method in field 19.
6. Choose your preferred payment method in field 21.
7. Finally, confirm whether your billing address is the same as your shipping address in field 22, and accept the terms and conditions in field 23.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (2): Enter your complete first and last name.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter the email address where you want to receive order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number for order follow-up.
* **Company Name** (5): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Job Title** (6): Enter your position within the company (optional).
* **Company Website** (7): Enter the URL of your business website (optional).
* **Quantity of Cards** (9): Select the number of business cards to order from 250, 500, 1000, 2000, or 5000.
* **Card Finish Type** (10): Choose from Gloss, Matte, Linen, or Glossy Foil finish types.
* **Preferred Color Scheme** (11): Select the primary color scheme for your cards, either Standard Black and White, Full Color, or Custom Colors.
* **Design Upload** (12): If you have a custom design, upload your business card design file (optional).
* **Street Address** (14): Enter your shipping address.
* **City** (15): Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **State/Province** (16): Enter the state or province of your shipping address.
* **Postal Code** (17): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (18): Enter the country of your shipping address.
* **Shipping Method** (19): Select your preferred shipping method, either Standard (5-7 business days), Express (2-3 business days), or Overnight.
* **Payment Method** (21): Choose how you will pay for your order, either Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Is Billing Address Same as Shipping Address?** (22): Confirm whether your billing address is the same as your shipping address.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions** (23): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions to proceed with your order.
