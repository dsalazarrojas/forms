# creator_circle_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for creators to register their projects and onboard with the platform. It collects information about the creators, their review applications, and their programs. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, follow the instructions below.
2.  Start by filling out the "Creator Details" field with your basic contact information.
3.  Next, describe your project in the "Review Application" field.
4.  In the "Onboard Membership" field, provide any relevant details about your membership status.
5.  Finally, list your creator programs in the "Creator Programs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Creator Details** (`creator_details`, text, optional): This field is for your basic contact information. Please fill it out with your name, email, and phone number.
*   **Review Application** (`review_application`, text, optional): Describe your project in this field. Try to be as clear and concise as possible.
*   **Onboard Membership** (`onboard_membership`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about your membership status. If you're unsure, leave it blank.
*   **Creator Programs** (`creator_programs`, text, optional): List your creator programs. These could be courses, events, or other projects. Be as detailed as needed.

## Tips

-   Fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
-   Use clear and concise language in the "Review Application" field.
-   If you're unsure about a field, try your best to answer it.
