# Testimonial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Testimonial Consent Form is designed to gather information and obtain consent from individuals or businesses to use their testimonial in marketing, promotional, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose "Client" or "Employee" as the consent giver's category.
2. Enter the consent giver's email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Giver Category** (`Consent Giver Name`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best describes the person giving consent, either "Client" or "Employee".
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person giving consent.
* **Testimonial** (`Testimonial`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the testimonial or a short statement of approval to use the testimonial.
