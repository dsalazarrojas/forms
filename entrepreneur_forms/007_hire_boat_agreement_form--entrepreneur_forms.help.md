# Hire Boat Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hire Boat Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of hiring a boat. It is used to collect information from users about the boat they are hiring, including its details, duration of hire, and total price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). The form is split into multiple pages, each with a specific section for you to fill.
2. Page 1: Enter the title of the form, which is already pre-filled with the title "Hire Boat Agreement Form".
3. Page 2: Provide the details of the boat you are hiring, including its type (e.g., Sail, Speed, Cruiser, etc.).
4. Page 3: Specify the number of days you will be hiring the boat.
5. Page 4: Enter the date and time you want to hire the boat.
6. Page 5: Enter the date and time you plan to return the boat.
7. Page 6: Enter the total price of your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hire Boat Agreement Form** (Text): This field is already pre-filled with the title of the form.
* **Boat Details** (Text): Enter the details of the boat you are hiring.
* **Boat Type** (Select One):
	+ Sail
	+ Speed
	+ Cruiser
	+ Houseboat
	+ Pontoon
	+ Runabout
	+ Pontoon Boat
	+ Bowrider
	+ Bowrider Boat
	+ Kayak
	+ Canoe
* **Number of Days** (Number): Enter the number of days you will be hiring the boat.
* **Hire Date** (Date): Enter the date you want to hire the boat.
* **Hire Time** (Time): Enter the time you want to hire the boat.
* **Return Date** (Date): Enter the date you plan to return the boat.
* **Return Time** (Time): Enter the time you plan to return the boat.
* **Total Price** (Number): Enter the total amount of your agreement.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form's help guide or ask for assistance.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
