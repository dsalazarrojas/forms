# Summer Apprenticeship Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for applicants who want to apply for the Summer Apprenticeship program. It collects information about the applicant's contact details, skills, availability, and work preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you have attached your resume and provided a valid email address.
3. Select the skills and experiences that are relevant to your application.
4. Specify your available start and end dates for the apprenticeship.
5. Enter your expected salary range and notice period.
6. Choose your preferred work location, language, and work hours.
7. Select your level of education, job type, and preferred work style.
8. Answer questions about your motivation for the apprenticeship and why you are a good fit for the program.
9. Finally, sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (page 1, inputSchema): Fill in the required information about yourself.
* **Applicant Name** (page 2, name): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Applicant Email** (page 2, email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Applicant Resume** (page 3, note): Attach your updated resume to demonstrate your skills and experiences.
* **Contact Number** (page 3, text): Enter a phone number where we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Relevant Skills** (page 4, select_one): Choose the skills that you have acquired or are willing to learn (Data Science, Machine Learning, Web Development, Business, Marketing, Product Management, Project Management, UX/UI).
* **Relevant Experiences** (page 4, select_multiple): Select the experiences that demonstrate your skills and abilities (Internship, Full-time job, Part-time job, Volunteer, Project, Startup, Internship/Project).
* **Available Start Date** (page 5, date): Enter the date you are available to start the apprenticeship.
* **Available End Date** (page 5, date): Enter the date you are available to end the apprenticeship.
* **Expected Salary Range** (page 5, number): Enter your expected salary range per week or month.
* **Notice Period** (page 5, time): Enter the notice period for the apprenticeship.
* **Work Location** (page 6, select_one): Choose where you prefer to work (Remote, Local, Hybrid).
* **Language** (page 6, select_one): Choose the language you are fluent in (English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Arabic).
* **Highest Level of Education** (page 7, select_multiple): Select the highest level of education you have acquired (High School Diploma, Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **Motivation for Apprenticeship** (page 8, text): Explain why you want to join the Summer Apprenticeship program.
* **Why Us** (page 8, text): Describe why you think you would be a good fit for our program.
* **Skills Statement** (page 9, text): Summarize your key skills and qualifications.
* **Preferred Work Style** (page 9, select_one): Choose how you prefer to work (Team work, Collaborative, Independent).
* **Job Type** (page 10, select_one): Choose your preferred job type (Permanent, Part-time, Freelance).
* **Work Hours** (page 10, select_one): Select how many hours you are available to work per week (40 hours, 30-40 hours, 20-30 hours, 20 hours, 15-20 hours, Less than 15 hours).
* **Job Level** (page 11, select_one): Choose your job level (Intern, Trainee, Apprentice).
* **Benefits** (page 11, select_one): Do you have any benefits or do you need help with benefits (Yes, No).
* **Emergency Contact** (page 12, text): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Number** (page 12, text): Enter a phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Applicant Signature** (page 13, note): Sign the form to confirm your application.
