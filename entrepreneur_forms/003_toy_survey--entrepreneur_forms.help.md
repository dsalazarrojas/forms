# toy_spring - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "toy_spring" form is a collection of input fields designed to gather information about toys for an entrepreneurship project. This form is intended to collect detailed characteristics, prices, and user experiences related to toys. Please ensure that the form is being used for its intended purpose and not for other purposes such as collecting information about other products or non-toy related data.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information about the toy being described.
