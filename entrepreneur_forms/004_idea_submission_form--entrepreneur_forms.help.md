# Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Idea Submission Form is a platform for employees to share their ideas and suggestions with the company. It is designed to collect and review employee suggestions for potential implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: Employee Suggestion, Idea Description, and Submitter Email.
2. The Category field allows you to categorize your idea, and the Idea Tags field enables you to add multiple tags to describe your concept.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field, which will be used to contact you about your submission.
4. If you need to, fill out the Phone Number field for your contact information.
5. Finally, submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Suggestion**: A brief summary of your idea. This is a required field.
* **Idea Description**: A detailed description of your idea, including any relevant context or background information. This is a required field.
* **Category**: This field allows you to categorize your idea into one of the following options:
	+ Yes/No
* **Idea Tags**: Add multiple tags to describe your concept. This is an optional field.
* **Email**: A required field for submitting your email address, which will be used to contact you about your submission.
* **Phone Number**: This field allows you to add your phone number for contact purposes. This is an optional field.
* **Submitter Email**: A required field for the email address of the person submitting the idea.
* **Phone Number**: This field allows you to add your phone number for contact purposes. This is an optional field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to submit your idea.
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when describing your idea.
- Use the Category and Idea Tags fields to help categorize and highlight your concept.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
