# Printing Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Printing Order Form is used to gather information about a customer's job or project that requires printing services. It is designed to streamline the printing order process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected in a single, organized format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the business name and address in the "Business Name" and "Business Address" fields, respectively.
3. Select the delivery date for the job in the "Delivery Date" field.
4. Choose the job type from the options provided in the "Job Type" field.
5. Select the status of the job from the options provided in the "Job Status" field.
6. Choose the priority of the job from the options provided in the "Job Priority" field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
9. Add any special instructions or notes about the job in the "Notes" and "Special Instructions" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who ordered the printing job.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business that the printing job is for.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the business where the printing job will be delivered to.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Select the date when the printing job is required to be delivered to the customer.
* **Job Name** (`job_name`, text, required: false): Enter a name for the job or project that requires printing.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of job or project that requires printing from the options provided.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the job from the options provided.
* **Job Priority** (`job_priority`, select_one, required: false): Choose the priority of the job from the options provided.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any notes or comments about the job.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false): Enter any special instructions or requirements for the job.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure that all fields are completed accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the printing services department for clarification.
* This form is intended for internal use within the organization and should not be used for external purposes.
