# Silent Partnership Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a silent partnership agreement between two parties. It outlines the terms of the partnership, including roles, responsibilities, profit shares, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Active Partner section with the name and contact information of the active partner.
2. Fill out the Silent Partner section with the name and contact information of the silent partner.
3. In the Roles and Responsibilities section, select the role(s) that the active partner will be responsible for and the role(s) that the silent partner will be responsible for.
4. In the Contributions section, describe any contributions that the silent partner has made or will make to the partnership.
5. In the Profit Share section, specify the percentage of profit share for both the active and silent partners.
6. In the Confirmations section, confirm whether the silent partner will have any confirmations (e.g., access to financial reports, meetings, etc.).
7. In the Signature section, both partners must sign and date the form to confirm their understanding of the agreement.
8. In the Date and Time sections, fill out the date and time of signing the agreement.
9. In the Email, Phone, and Note sections, provide the contact information for both partners.
10. In the Other Details section, fill out any other relevant details about the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Active Partner** (`active_partner`): Fill out this section with the name and contact information (email, phone, and address) of the partner who will be actively involved in the daily operations of the business.
* **Silent Partner** (`silent_partner`): Fill out this section with the name and contact information (email, phone, and address) of the partner who will not be actively involved in the daily operations of the business.
* **Active Partner Share** (`active_partner_share`): Specify the percentage of profit share for the active partner.
* **Silent Partner Share** (`silent_partner_share`): Specify the percentage of profit share for the silent partner.
* **Roles and Responsibilities** (`roles`): Select the role(s) that the active partner will be responsible for and the role(s) that the silent partner will be responsible for.
* **Contributions** (`contributions`): Describe any contributions that the silent partner has made or will make to the partnership.
* **Profit Share** (`profit_share`): Specify the percentage of profit share for both the active and silent partners.
* **Confirmations** (`confirmations`): Confirm whether the silent partner will have any confirmations (e.g., access to financial reports, meetings, etc.).
* **Signature** (`signature`): Both partners must sign and date this section to confirm their understanding of the agreement.
* **Date** (`date`): Fill out the date of signing the agreement.
* **Time** (`time`): Fill out the time of signing the agreement.
* **Email** (`email`): Fill out the email address of both partners.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Fill out the phone number of both partners.
* **Note** (`note`): Fill out any notes about the partnership.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`): Fill out any other relevant details about the partnership.
* **Other Name** (`other_name`): Fill out the name of the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other Email** (`other_email`): Fill out the email address of the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other Phone** (`other_phone`): Fill out the phone number of the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other Note** (`other_note`): Fill out any notes about the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`): Fill out any other relevant details about the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other Address** (`other_address`): Fill out the address of the other party involved in the partnership.
* **Other City** (`other_city`): Fill out the city of the other party's location.
* **Other State** (`other_state`): Fill out the state of the other party's location.
* **Other Zip** (`other_zip`): Fill out the zip code of the other party's location.
