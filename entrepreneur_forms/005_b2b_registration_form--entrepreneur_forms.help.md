# B2B Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The B2B Registration Form is designed to collect information about your business to help us understand your needs and preferences. This form is a required step for registering with our platform and gaining access to our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (indicated by a red star next to the label)
2. Select the appropriate options for each field from the provided list
3. Make sure to review and agree to the terms of service

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name (1)
• **Business Name**: <em>Legal name of your business</em>
• Required: Yes

### Business Type (2)
• **Business Type**: Select your business structure (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, Non-profit, Other)
• Required: Yes

### Industry (3)
• **Industry**: Select your primary industry sector (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Education, Other)
• Required: Yes

### Business Address (4)
• **Business Address**: Full physical address of your company
• Required: Yes

### City (5)
• **City**: City location of your business
• Required: Yes

### State or Province (6)
• **State or Province**: State or province of your business
• Required: Yes

### ZIP or Postal Code (7)
• **ZIP or Postal Code**: Postal code of your business
• Required: Yes

### Country (8)
• **Country**: Country of operation
• Required: Yes

### Business Website (9)
• **Business Website**: Your company website URL (optional)
• Required: No

### Primary Contact Name (10)
• **Primary Contact Name**: Full name of main contact
• Required: Yes

### Contact Title (11)
• **Contact Title**: Job title or position of main contact
• Required: Yes

### Contact Email (12)
• **Contact Email**: Primary business email
• Required: Yes

### Contact Phone (13)
• **Contact Phone**: Direct phone number of main contact
• Required: Yes

### Number of Employees (14)
• **Number of Employees**: Approximate employee count
• Required: Yes

### Annual Revenue Range (15)
• **Annual Revenue Range**: Select your revenue bracket (e.g., Under 100K, 100K to 500K, 500K to 1M, 1M to 5M, 5M to 10M, Over 10M)
• Required: Yes

### Purpose of Registration (16)
• **Purpose of Registration**: Why are you registering with us (e.g., Access to services, Partnership opportunities, Vendor registration, Platform access, Resource library, Networking, Other)
• Required: Yes

### How Did You Hear About Us (17)
• **How Did You Hear About Us**: Source of referral (e.g., Search engine, Social media, Referral from colleague, Industry event, Advertisement, Other)
• Required: No

### Additional Services Interested In (18)
• **Additional Services Interested In**: Select any additional services (e.g., Consulting, Training, Support services, Premium features, Custom solutions, None at this time)
• Required: No

### Terms and Conditions (19)
• **Terms and Conditions**: I agree to the terms of service
• Required: Yes

### Registration Date (20)
• **Registration Date**: Date of this registration
• Required: Yes
