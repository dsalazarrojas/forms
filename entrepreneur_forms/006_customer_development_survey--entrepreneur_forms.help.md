# Customer Development Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customer Development Survey" is a tool used to gather feedback from customers about our services and products. It helps us understand their opinions, preferences, and areas for improvement, ultimately leading to better services and products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the services you use from the list provided in the "Which of the services do you use?" field.
2. Choose the products you use from the list in the "Which of the products do you use?" field.
3. Select the features you use from the list in the "Which of the features do you use?" field.
4. Rate the quality of the service in the "What is the quality of the service?" field.
5. Rate the value of the service in the "What is the value of the service?" field.
6. Choose how satisfied you are with our service in the "How satisfied are you with our service?" field.
7. If you have any suggestions on areas for improvement, type them in the "Which area would you like us to improve?" field.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which of the services do you use?** (`customer_development_survey_service`): Select all the services you have used or currently use. This will help us understand which services are popular among our customers.
* **Which of the products do you use?** (`customer_development_survey_product`): Choose all the products you use or have used. This will give us insight into which products are preferred by our customers.
* **Which of the features do you use?** (`customer_development_survey_feature`): Select the features that you use or have used. This will help us identify which features are most useful to our customers.
* **What is the quality of the service?** (`customer_development_survey_service_quality`): Rate the quality of our service as High, Medium, or Low. This will help us understand where we excel or need improvement.
* **What is the value of the service?** (`customer_development_survey_service_value`): Rate the value of our service as High, Medium, or Low. This will help us understand how our customers perceive the value we offer.
* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (`customer_development_survey_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with our service as Very Satisfied, Satisfied, or Not Satisfied. This will help us understand areas for improvement to increase customer satisfaction.
* **Which area would you like us to improve?** (`customer_development_survey_improvement_area`): If you have any suggestions on areas where we can improve, type them in this field.
* **Comments** (`customer_development_survey_comments`): Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about our services and products. This is an opportunity to provide more in-depth feedback.
