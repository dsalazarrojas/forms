This form is designed to collect information from business owners who are seeking a loan under the Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) loan program. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the business, including its name, address, contact information, loan amount, and purpose of the loan. 

To ensure consistency and accuracy, we are checking if you have submitted this form before. If you have, you may not need to fill it out again. Please make sure to check your email for a confirmation message from us if this is the case.

If you need to fill this form again, please make sure to provide the same email address as used in your previous application. This will help us track your progress and ensure you receive the correct information about your loan application.

This guide will walk you through each section of the form and explain what information we need from you.

## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the PPP loan application process. Please make sure to carefully review and provide accurate information to avoid any delays in processing your loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:

1. Fill in your first and last name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your business's name as it is registered with the government.
3. Provide your business's address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Enter your business's phone number and email address.
5. Specify the loan amount you are requesting.
6. Select the purpose of your loan (e.g., Loan, Expansion, Working Capital).
7. If applicable, enter the time your business has been in operation.
8. If your business has multiple owners, select 'Multi Owner'.
9. If your business operates in more than one industry, select all relevant options.
10. If you have an SBA number, please enter it.
11. Select the status of your loan (New, Refinance, Renewal).
12. If your business is certified, select 'Certified'.
13. If you have assigned a loan officer, enter their name.
14. Add any additional notes about your application.
15. If you have a loan officer assigned, please select 'Yes'.
16. If you have a tool assigned, enter its name.
17. Enter the date the loan was assigned or updated.
18. Enter the date this form was created or updated.
