# e_commerce_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce registration form is designed to collect essential details from online e-commerce store owners and managers. It is a simple and straightforward form that gathers the necessary information for registering an e-commerce store. The fields included are designed to be easy to fill out, and this guide will explain each field to ensure a smooth and error-free experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form to register your e-commerce store and provide the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing general information about the user registering the e-commerce store. Please enter any relevant details about the store owner or manager.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the store, such as phone numbers, email addresses, or physical addresses.
* **Billing Info** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Provide billing information for the store, including any relevant payment details.
* **E-Commerce Store Info** (`e_commerce_store_info`, text, optional): Enter details about the e-commerce store, such as its name, URL, or other relevant information.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide information about the products sold or services offered by the store.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Enter any agreement or terms of service related to the store.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Provide shipping details for the store, including any relevant information about shipping policies or practices.
