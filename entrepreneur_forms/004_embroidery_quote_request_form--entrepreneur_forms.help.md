# Embroidery Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are requesting a quote for custom embroidery services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Enter your company's **Name** in the `Company Name` field.
3. Provide your **Contact Email** in the `Contact Email` field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the `Phone Number` field.
5. Select the type(s) of **Quote Type** you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the material type for your custom embroidery from the **Material Type** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the **Quantity** of items you would like to have embroidered, if applicable.
8. Describe your custom **Design Description** in the text area.
9. If applicable, enter the **Report Date** for which you would like the quote to be valid.
10. Upload any relevant files in the **File Upload** field.
11. If you have any custom requests, specify them in the **Custom Request** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on the quote.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it should be displayed on the quote.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Quote Type**: Select the type of quote you are interested in, which can be used to determine the scope of the quote.
* **Material Type**: Select the type of material you would like to have embroidered, such as cotton, polyester, etc.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of items you would like to have embroidered, if applicable.
* **Design Description**: Describe your custom design in detail.
* **Report Date**: If you would like the quote to be valid for a specific date range, enter that date range here.
* **File Upload**: Upload any relevant files that can help us better understand your requirements.
* **Custom Request**: If you have any special requests or requirements, specify them here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields, but feel free to provide additional information if you have specific requirements.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the sales team for clarification.
- The sales team will review your submission and follow up with you to gather any additional information needed to complete the quote.
