# stripe_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect user information for meal kit delivery subscription orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Info" section with your details.
2. Provide your "Billing Info" to process payments.
3. Enter your "Subscription Info" to define your subscription preferences.
4. Enter your "Shipping Info" to ensure correct delivery.
5. Fill in "Order Details" to complete the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (1)
* **User Info (1)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please fill in your user name and contact information for reference.

### Billing Info (2)
* **Billing Info (2)** (`billing_info`, text, optional): Enter your billing address and payment information for payment processing.

### Subscription Info (3)
* **Subscription Info (3)** (`subscription_info`, text, optional): This field is intended to provide subscription details. Please specify your subscription preferences here.

### Shipping Info (4)
* **Shipping Info (4)** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure correct delivery of your meal kit.

### Order Details (5)
* **Order Details (5)** (`order_details`, text, optional): Fill in any additional order details to complete the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure the form is used for subscription orders only.
* Use clear and accurate information to avoid any issues with payment or delivery.
