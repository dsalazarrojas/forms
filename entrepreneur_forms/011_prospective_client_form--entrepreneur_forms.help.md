# prospective_client_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The prospective client form is designed to collect information about prospective clients, which will aid in the onboarding process for new business partnerships. This form is intended to be completed by prospective clients and should only be filled out once per client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the first name of the prospective client.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is the last name of the prospective client.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is the email address of the prospective client.
4. Enter your **Phone** in the "Phone" field. This is the phone number of the prospective client.
5. Enter your **Company** in the "Company" field. This is the company name of the prospective client.
6. Enter your **Job Title** in the "Job Title" field. This is the job title of the prospective client.
7. Enter your **Position** in the "Position" field. This is a descriptive field for the prospective client's position.
8. Select your **Role** in the "Role" field. This field requires you to choose one of the three available options: Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, or Other.
9. Enter a brief description of your **Job Description** in the "Job Description" field. This is a descriptive field about the prospective client's job responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the prospective client.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the prospective client.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the prospective client.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the prospective client.
- **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the company name of the prospective client.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title of the prospective client.
- **Position** (`position`, text, required: false): Enter a descriptive field for the prospective client's position.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the three available options: Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, or Other.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the prospective client's job responsibilities.
