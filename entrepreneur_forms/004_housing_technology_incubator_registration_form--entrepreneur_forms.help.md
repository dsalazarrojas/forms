# Housing Technology Incubator Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Housing Technology Incubator Registration Form is designed to collect information from startups and entrepreneurs about their housing technology startups.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the instructions.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
3. Provide detailed and truthful information about your startup.
