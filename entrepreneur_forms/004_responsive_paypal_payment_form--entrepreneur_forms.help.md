# Responsive Paypal Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to process payments and allow customers to select their preferred payment method, choose products, and provide necessary contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your payment method by choosing from the available options (Paypal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
2. Choose the products you would like to purchase by selecting from the available options.
3. Enter your name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Add any additional message or comments in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Select Payment Method** (Select Payment Method, select_one, required: false)
Select your preferred payment method from the options provided. This will determine how you will make the payment.

### 2. **Select Product** (Select Product, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose the product(s) you would like to purchase from the options provided. Select all applicable products to add them to your order.

### 3. **Name** (Name, text, required: false)
Enter your name as it should be displayed for the order.

### 4. **Email** (Email, email, required: false)
Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 5. **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 6. **Address** (Address, text, required: false)
Enter your address for delivery or invoice purposes.

### 7. **City** (City, text, required: false)
Enter the city where you would like your order delivered to or sent from.

### 8. **State** (State, text, required: false)
Enter the state where you would like your order delivered to or sent from.

### 9. **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, required: false)
Enter the zip code where you would like your order delivered to or sent from.

### 10. **Message** (Message, text, required: false)
Add any additional comments or messages about your order in this field.

## Tips
- Make sure to select only the payment method that applies to you.
- Select all relevant products to add them to your order.
- Ensure your name, email, phone number, and address are accurate for order processing.
- Double-check your zip code for correct delivery or invoice purposes.
