# laptop_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your laptop preferences, habits, and needs. Please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible to help us better understand what you expect from a laptop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the answer that best matches your preferences or habits.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter your answer in the text field for open-ended questions.
4. Make sure to fill out all required questions.
