# <string> - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your startup, providing a structured and efficient way to gather essential data for entrepreneurs and businesses. It helps us to understand your company's details, goals, and funding stage, ultimately assisting us in providing tailored support and resources for your business growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about your startup.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your startup's characteristics.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your startup.
4. Set a follow-up time and date for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accelerator Name** (`accelerator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your accelerator or startup.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your startup.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Startup Details** (`startup_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your startup.
* **Consistency Goals** (`consistency_goals`, text, required): Describe your startup's consistency goals.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select one or more industries that your startup belongs to. Choose from:
	+ Artificial Intelligence
	+ Biotechnology
	+ FinTech
	+ HealthTech
	+ Software
* **Startup Goals** (`startup_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more goals for your startup. Choose from:
	+ Expand Business/Revenue
	+ Network/Partnerships
	+ Product/Market Development
	+ Product/Market Research
	+ Product/Service Development
	+ Reduce/Improve Costs
	+ Other
* **Funding Stage** (`funding_stage`, select_one, required): Select the funding stage of your startup. Choose from:
	+ seed
	+ Series A
	+ Series B
	+ Series C
	+ Series D
	+ Series E
	+ Series F
	+ Series G
	+ Series H
	+ Series I
	+ Series J
	+ Series K
	+ Series L
	+ Other
* **Startup Location** (`startup_location`, select_one, required): Select the region of your startup location. Choose from:
	+ Europe
	+ Asia
	+ Americas
	+ Africa
	+ Oceania
	+ Other
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter the URL of your website.
* **LinkedIn** (`linkedin`, text, required): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **LinkedIn ID** (`linkedin_id`, text, required): Enter your LinkedIn ID.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of your startup.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, time, required): Set a follow-up time for further communication.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, date, required): Set a follow-up date.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your startup.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Use the provided select options for fields like Industry, Funding Stage, and Startup Location to ensure correct classification.
- Be clear and concise in your descriptions and answers.
- Set a realistic follow-up time and date for further communication.
