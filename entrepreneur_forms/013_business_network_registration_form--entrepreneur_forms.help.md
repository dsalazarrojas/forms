# Business Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business so we can provide you with the most valuable connections, insights, and opportunities to grow your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** on the first page.
2. On the second page, select your **Business Type** and **Industry Sector**.
3. Provide a brief **Business Description**.
4. Optionally, enter your **Years in Business** and **Number of Employees**.
5. List your **Geographic Service Areas**.
6. Optionally, provide the **URL of your Business Website**.
7. Select your **Networking Interests**.
8. Specify what you can **Offer to the Network**.
9. Optionally, list your **Business Specialties**.
10. Choose your **Preferred Contact Frequency**.
11. Share your **Social Media Profiles**.
12. Indicate how you **Hear About Us**.
13. Finally, confirm your agreement to the network terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name as you would like to be known in the network.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: True
- **Email Address** (2): Provide your primary email for network communications.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: True
- **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact phone number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: True
- **Business Name** (4): Enter your company or business name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: True
- **Business Type** (5): Select your business type (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, etc.).
  - Type: Select_one
  - Required: True
- **Industry Sector** (6): Choose your primary industry focus (e.g., Technology, Finance, etc.).
  - Type: Select_one
  - Required: True
- **Business Description** (7): Briefly describe your business.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: True
- **Years in Business** (8): Enter how long your business has operated (optional).
  - Type: Number
  - Required: False
- **Number of Employees** (9): Enter your current team size (optional).
  - Type: Number
  - Required: False
- **Geographic Service Areas** (10): List where you operate (optional).
  - Type: Text
  - Required: False
- **Business Website** (11): Enter the URL of your business website (optional).
  - Type: Text
  - Required: False
- **Networking Interests** (12): Select what you are looking for in the network (e.g., Business Partnerships, etc.).
  - Type: Select_multiple
  - Required: True
- **What Can You Offer the Network?** (13): Specify what services, skills, or expertise you can share with the network.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: True
- **Business Specialties** (14): List your unique expertise or services (optional).
  - Type: Text
  - Required: False
- **Preferred Contact Frequency** (15): Choose how often you want to be contacted (optional).
  - Type: Select_one
  - Required: False
- **Social Media Profiles** (16): Link your professional profiles (optional).
  - Type: Text
  - Required: False
- **How Did You Hear About Us?** (17): Indicate how you heard about the network (e.g., Existing Member, etc.).
  - Type: Select_one
  - Required: False
- **I Agree to Network Terms and Conditions** (18): Confirm acceptance of network rules.
  - Type: Select_one
  - Required: True
