# Women In Tech Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about women in tech projects, including demographic details, project information, and contact preferences. The data collected will be used to support and promote women in tech initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Introduction: Start by providing a brief introduction to your project or initiative.
2. Demographic Information: Provide your demographic details, including age, location, and contact preferences.
3. Occupation and Project Details: Answer the questions about your occupation, project type, status, and description.
4. Contact Information: Enter your contact email address and preferred contact method.
5. Project Details: If you have a project URL, budget, or tech stack, provide the relevant details.
6. Submission: Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (1)**: A brief introduction to your project or initiative.
* **Demographic Information (2)**: Please provide your age and demographic details. This will help us better understand your background.
* **Occupation (3)**: Please select 'Yes' if you have a tech-related occupation or 'No' if not.
* **Job Title (4)**: If you have a job title, please enter it here.
* **Tech Background (5)**: If you have experience with specific technologies, please enter them here.
* **Education (6)**: If you have a relevant education background, please enter it here.
* **Work Experience (7)**: If you have relevant work experience, please enter it here.
* **Current Employer (8)**: If you are currently employed, please enter your employer's name.
* **Gender (9)**: Please select your gender.
* **Age (10)**: Please enter your age.
* **Location (11)**: Please enter your location.
* **Contact Email (12)**: Enter your contact email address. This is the primary way we will contact you.
* **Phone Number (13)**: If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your number.
* **Preferred Contact Method (14)**: Select all the contact methods you prefer, including email, phone, phone call, or other.
* **How did you hear about this project? (15)**: Please enter the source where you heard about this project.
* **About your Project (16)**: A brief description of your project.
* **Project Type (17)**: Please select the type of project you are working on.
* **Project Status (18)**: Please select the current status of your project.
* **Project Description (19)**: A brief description of your project.
* **Project URL (20)**: If you have a project URL, please enter it here.
* **Tech Stack (21)**: If you have a tech stack, please enter it here.
* **Budget (22)**: If you have a budget, please enter it here.
* **Budget Currency (23)**: Please enter the currency of your budget.
* **Gender (24)**: This field is not used in this form.

Note that fields with a required status are marked as such. Make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form successfully. If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
