# simple_business_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The simple_business_contact_form is a form designed to collect basic contact information from users, including first and last names, company name, email address, phone number, and a message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the simple_business_contact_form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Type your company name in the Company field.
4. Enter your valid email address in the Email field.
5. Input your phone number in the Phone field.
6. In the Message field, type your query or message in detail.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company here.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address here. Please use the correct format of your email address (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, required): Type your message or query in detail here.
