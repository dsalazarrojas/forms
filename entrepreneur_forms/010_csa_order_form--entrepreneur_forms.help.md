# CSA Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSA Order Form is designed to collect data for orders related to a Community-Supported Agriculture program. It gathers information from customers about their order preferences, including name, contact email, share type, pickup location, start date, payment option, dietary restrictions, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer name** in the "Customer name" field.
2. Enter your **Contact email** in the "Contact email" field.
3. Choose your preferred **Share type** from the options provided.
4. Select your preferred **Pickup location** from the available options.
5. Enter the **Start date** for your first delivery or pickup.
6. Choose your **Payment option** from the available options.
7. If applicable, enter any **Dietary restrictions or preferences**.
8. Add any **Additional notes** about your order in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name for order purposes.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email to receive order confirmation.
* **Share type** (`share_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly options for your share frequency.
* **Pickup location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Select your preferred farm pickup site.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the first delivery or pickup date.
* **Payment option** (`payment_option`, select_one, required): Choose from Full payment or Monthly installments options for payment frequency.
* **Dietary restrictions or preferences** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please note them here.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or instructions for your delivery or pickup.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date contact information to receive order confirmation.
* Be mindful of your share type and pickup location selection to ensure timely delivery or pickup.
* If you have dietary restrictions or preferences, please provide them in the "Dietary restrictions or preferences" field.
* Use the "Additional notes" field to provide any additional comments or instructions for your order.
* Review your completed form before submission to ensure accurate and clear information.
