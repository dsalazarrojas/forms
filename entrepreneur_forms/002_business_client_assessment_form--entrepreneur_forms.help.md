# Business Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Client Assessment Form is designed to gather information about your business, challenges, goals, and support needs to help us provide tailored consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and answer honestly to provide accurate information.
- Fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip any fields that don't apply to your business.
- You can use this form to update your previous responses if your circumstances have changed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Client Name** (Text, Required, ID 2):
  Enter your business's or company's full name as it appears on your letterhead or business cards.

- **Email Address** (Email, Required, ID 3):
  Provide your primary contact email address for communication and further assistance.

- **Phone Number** (Text, Required, ID 4):
  Enter your best contact number where we can reach you during business hours.

- **Company Name** (Text, Required, ID 5):
  Enter your business or company name as it appears on your letterhead or business cards.

- **Industry Type** (Select One, Required, ID 6):
  Select the category that best describes your business:

  - **Technology**
  - **Retail**
  - **Manufacturing**
  - **Services**
  - **Healthcare**
  - **Education**
  - **Other**

- **Business Age** (Text, Optional, ID 7):
  Estimate how long your business has been operating. This helps us understand your growth and development stage.

- **Current Challenges** (Text, Required, ID 9):
  Describe the key obstacles or pain points you're facing in your business. This helps us understand your needs and provide relevant assistance.

- **Primary Business Goals** (Text, Required, ID 10):
  Share your short-term and long-term objectives for your business. This helps us tailor our services to your needs.

- **Revenue Range** (Select One, Required, ID 11):
  Choose the revenue bracket that best represents your business's annual revenue:

  - **Less than $100K**
  - **$100K - $500K**
  - **$500K - $1M**
  - **$1M - $5M**
  - **$5M+**

- **Team Size** (Number, Optional, ID 12):
  Estimate the approximate number of employees in your team. This helps us understand your operational capacity.

- **Business Strengths** (Text, Optional, ID 13):
  Identify areas where your business excels or has a competitive advantage.

- **Expansion Interest** (Select One, Required, ID 14):
  Let us know if you're interested in business expansion or growth:

  - **Yes**
  - **No**
  - **Not Sure**

- **Support Needs** (Select Multiple, Required, ID 16):
  Choose the areas where you need support:

  - **Strategy and Planning**
  - **Marketing and Sales**
  - **Operations**
  - **Financial Management**
  - **Technology**
  - **HR and Team Building**

- **Preferred Contact Method** (Select One, Required, ID 17):
  Indicate your preferred contact method:

  - **Email**
  - **Phone**
  - **Video Call**
  - **In-Person**

- **Best Contact Time** (Select One, Optional, ID 18):
  Choose the time slot that suits you best for contact:

  - **Morning (8AM - 12PM)**
  - **Afternoon (12PM - 5PM)**
  - **Evening (5PM - 8PM)**
  - **Flexible**

- **Previous Consulting Experience** (Select One, Optional, ID 19):
  Let us know if you have consulted with consultants before:

  - **Yes**
  - **No**
  - **Not Sure**

- **Budget for Consulting Services** (Select One, Optional, ID 20):
  Estimate the maximum amount you can invest in consulting services:

  - **Less than $2,500**
  - **$2,500 - $5,000**
  - **$5,000 - $10,000**
  - **$10,000 - $25,000**
  - **$25,000+**

- **Additional Notes or Comments** (Text, Optional, ID 21):
  Share any other information that might be helpful for us to understand your business needs.
