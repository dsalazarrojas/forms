# Square Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for potential customers to sign up for a Square Meal Kit subscription service, which promises to deliver pre-arranged meal kits to subscribers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on each page tab to progress through the form.
2. Fill out each page with the required and optional information.
3. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (Page 1)
	+ **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Plan** (Page 2)
	+ **Subscription Plan** (select_one, optional): Choose from Basic, Premium, or Business plans.
* **Meal Plan** (Page 3)
	+ **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more meal plans.
* **Payment Information** (Page 4)
	+ **Payment Information** (text, optional): Enter your payment information.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 5)
	+ **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Delivery Address** (Page 6)
	+ **Delivery Address** (text, optional): Enter your delivery address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 7)
	+ **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Details** (Page 8)
	+ **Subscription Start Date** (date, optional): Enter your subscription start date.
	+ **Subscription End Date** (date, optional): Enter your subscription end date.
* **Payment Method** (Page 9)
	+ **Bank Transfer**, **Credit Card**, **PayPal** (select_one, optional): Choose a payment method.
* **Payment Details** (Page 10)
	+ **Payment Details** (text, optional): Enter your payment details.
* **Billing Address** (Page 11)
	+ **Billing Address** (text, optional): Enter your billing address.
* **Card Information** (Page 12)
	+ **Card Number** (number, optional): Enter your card number.
	+ **Card Expiration Date** (date, optional): Enter your card expiration date.
	+ **Card Security Code** (select_one, optional): Choose from CVV or Security Code.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- You can choose any of the available meal plans.
- You can select any of the available payment methods.
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
