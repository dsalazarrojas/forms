# Business Innovation Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for entrepreneurs and innovators who wish to apply for the Business Innovation Grant. It collects essential information about the applicant, their project, and its goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you are the actual applicant or have permission to submit on behalf of the applicant.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
3. Fill out the "Applicant organization" field with the legal name of your company or organization.
4. Enter a short and descriptive title for your project.
5. Provide a brief summary of your project, highlighting its key aspects.
6. Specify the amount of funding requested, using the local currency.
7. Estimate the duration of your project in months.
8. Identify the primary beneficiaries of your project.
9. (Optional) If applicable, list how you will measure the success of your project.
10. (Optional) If applicable, list any relevant prior funding received.
11. Enter your contact email for correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Organization** (`Applicant Organization`, `text`, `required`): Enter the legal name of your company or organization.
* **Project Title** (`Project Title`, `text`, `required`): A short and descriptive title for your project.
* **Project Summary** (`Project Summary`, `text`, `required`): Provide a brief summary of your project, highlighting its key aspects.
* **Funding Requested** (`Funding Requested`, `number`, `required`): Enter the requested grant amount in local currency.
* **Project Duration Months** (`Project Duration Months`, `number`, `required`): Estimate the project duration in months.
* **Primary Beneficiaries** (`Primary Beneficiaries`, `text`, `required`): Identify who will benefit from your project.
* **Evaluation Metrics** (`Evaluation Metrics`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, describe how you plan to measure the success of your project.
* **Previous Grants Received** (`Previous Grants Received`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, list any relevant prior funding received.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email for correspondence.
