# Trade Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to join a trade association and provide information about yourself and your company for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Provide your company information:
	* Company Name
	* Job Title
3. Select your membership interest:
	* Interested in trade association events
	* Interested in networking opportunities
	* Interested in member benefits
	* Not interested
4. Specify how you found out about us:
	* Select all applicable options
5. If you've chosen "Other", please provide the source:
	* Enter the source of how you found out about us
6. Add any additional comments:
	* Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title at your company.
* **Membership Interest**: Select all the options that apply to your membership interest:
	+ Interested in trade association events
	+ Interested in networking opportunities
	+ Interested in member benefits
	+ Not interested
* **How did you find us**: Select all the sources that apply to how you found out about us:
	+ Social media
	+ Referral
	+ Google search
	+ Other
* **Other source**: If you selected "Other", enter the source of how you found out about us.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
