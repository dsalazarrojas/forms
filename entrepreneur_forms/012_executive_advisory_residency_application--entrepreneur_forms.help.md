# Executive Advisory Residency Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from potential candidates for an executive residency program. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's details, including their name, contact number, and email.
2. Provide a statement of interest, explaining why you are interested in the program.
3. Upload your resume to help us understand your background and qualifications.
4. Select your interest level in the program (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Check the application status (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
6. Add any additional information that you feel is necessary.
7. Enter the submitter's information, including their name, email, department, position, and institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, optional): Enter the candidate's name, contact number, and email.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, optional): Upload a resume to help us understand your background and qualifications.
* **Interest Statement** (`interest_statement`, text, optional): Explain why you are interested in the program.
* **Interest Level** (`interest_level`, select_one, optional): Select your level of interest in the program (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, optional): Check the status of your application (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information that you feel is necessary.
* **Submitter Information**:
	+ **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's name.
	+ **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the submitter's email.
	+ **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's department.
	+ **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's position.
	+ **Submitter Institution** (`submitter_institution`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's institution.
	+ **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
	+ **Submitter Institution Type** (`submitter_institutional_institution_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of the submitter's institution.
	+ **Submitter Institution URL** (`submitter_institutional_url`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's institution URL.
