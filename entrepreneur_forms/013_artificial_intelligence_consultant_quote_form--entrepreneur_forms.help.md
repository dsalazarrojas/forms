# Artificial Intelligence Consultant Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artificial Intelligence Consultant Quote Form is designed to gather essential information from you as a client to better understand your project requirements and expectations. This form will help us provide you with a tailored quote for our AI consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most accurate quote for your project, please complete the following sections:

1. **Client Information**
   * **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter your full name.
   * **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your organization or business name.
   * **Industry** (`industry`): Select the industry you belong to.
2. **Project Overview**
   * **Project Title** (`project_title`): Enter the name or description of your AI project.
   * **Project Description** (`project_description`): Provide a detailed overview of what you need help with.
   * **What Business Problem Are You Solving?** (`business_problem`): Describe the challenge or opportunity driving this project.
3. **AI Requirements**
   * **Type of AI Solutions Needed** (`ai_applications`): Select all applicable AI solutions you require.
   * **Data Availability** (`data_availability`): Indicate how much data you have available for the project.
   * **Current Systems or Infrastructure** (`current_systems`): Describe any existing systems or infrastructure relevant to your project.
4. **Timeline and Budget**
   * **Desired Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`): Choose how quickly you need this completed.
   * **Budget Range** (`budget_range`): Select an approximate budget for this project.
5. **Outcomes and Support**
   * **What Do You Expect to Achieve?** (`expected_outcomes`): Define your goals or success metrics for this project.
   * **What Support Do You Need From the Consultant?** (`support_needed`): Specify the level of support you require from us.
   * **Additional Notes or Questions** (`additional_notes`): Provide any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your full name for our records.
* **Company Name**: Your organization or business name.
* **Industry**: The field you operate in.
* **Project Title**: A concise name or description of your AI project.
* **Project Description**: A detailed overview of the project’s objectives and requirements.
* **What Business Problem Are You Solving?**: A clear explanation of the challenge or opportunity driving the project.
* **Type of AI Solutions Needed**: Select all applicable AI solutions for your project.
* **Data Availability**: Indicate the level of data you have for the project.
* **Current Systems or Infrastructure**: Describe any existing systems or infrastructure relevant to your project.
* **Desired Project Timeline**: Choose how quickly you need this project completed.
* **Budget Range**: Select an approximate budget for the project.
* **What Do You Expect to Achieve?**: Define your project's goals or success metrics.
* **What Support Do You Need From the Consultant?**: Specify the level of support you require from us.
* **Additional Notes or Questions**: Provide any additional information or questions you have.

## Tips

- Please complete the form as accurately as possible to ensure we provide the best possible quote for your project.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask us for clarification or guidance.
- This form is not meant to collect unnecessary information, so only fill in the required fields.
- Once you've submitted the form, we'll review your project details and provide a tailored quote.
