# Ebook Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ebook Order Form is a user-facing tool designed to collect contact information and book order details for processing orders and facilitating customer communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (indicated as "required" in the form) with accurate and valid information.
2.  Enter your first name.
3.  Enter your last name.
4.  Provide your email address.
5.  Enter your phone number.
6.  Enter the title of the eBook you wish to order.
7.  Specify the price of the eBook you are ordering.
8.  Enter the quantity of the eBook you wish to order.
9.  Calculate or enter the total price of your order (this field will likely be populated automatically based on your previous entries).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Ebook order form** (text, required/optional): A text field where you can enter any additional comments or remarks about your order.
- **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name for communication and order processing purposes.
- **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name for communication and order processing purposes.
- **Email** (email, required): A field for entering your email address. This will be used for order confirmations, updates, and any other relevant communication.
- **Phone** (text, required): A field for entering your phone number. This will be used for order updates, status changes, and any other communication that may require a call or call-back.
- **Ebook title** (text, required): Enter the title of the eBook you wish to order.
- **Price** (number, required): Enter the price of the eBook you are ordering.
- **Quantity** (number, required): Specify the number of eBooks you wish to order.
- **Total price** (number, required): This field will be automatically populated based on your previous entries. Please do not alter this field as it is calculated by the system.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling in your contact information and book details to ensure smooth order processing and communication.
- The form is designed for your convenience and ease of use. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
- If you need to order more than one eBook, please fill out the form for each eBook separately.
