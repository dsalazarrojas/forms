# Board Of Directors Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Of Directors Application Form is a form used to collect information about applicants to the Board of Directors of an organization. It is designed to help the selection committee evaluate the applicant's qualifications, experience, and commitment to the organization's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your current occupation and job title.
3. Describe your relevant experience in the board governance or a related sector.
4. Disclose any potential conflicts of interest.
5. Indicate your availability to commit time to the board position.
6. Provide two professional references.
7. Write a statement of interest explaining why you want to serve on the board.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where the selection committee can contact you.
* **Current occupation** (`current_occupation`, `text`, required): Describe your current job title and organization.
* **Relevant experience** (`relevant_experience`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your experience in board governance or a related sector.
* **Conflicts of interest** (`conflicts_of_interest`, `text`, optional): If you have any potential conflicts of interest, please disclose them here.
* **Availability commitment** (`availability_commitment`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate number of hours you can commit per month to board duties.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide the names and contact information of two professional references.
* **Statement of interest** (`statement_of_interest`, `text`, required): Explain why you want to serve on the board and what you can bring to the position.
