# Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship is a program designed to support the next generation of global leaders and innovators. This form is used to collect information from candidates who are interested in participating in the fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information required to complete the form.
* Review each field to understand what information is being requested.
* Fill out each field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate CV (candidate_cv)
* **Candidate CV** (required: false)
	+ This field is used to upload or enter a brief description of your background and qualifications.

### 2. Candidate Experience (candidate_experience)
* **Candidate Experience** (required: false)
	+ Please describe your relevant work experience, including any relevant internships, projects, or roles you have held.

### 3. Motivation Statement (motivation_statement)
* **Motivation Statement** (required: false)
	+ Please explain why you are interested in the Global Strategy Innovation Fellowship and what skills or qualifications you bring to the program.

### 4. University ID (university_id)
* **University ID** (required: false)
	+ Select all universities you have affiliation with.

### 5. Think Tank ID (think_tank_id)
* **Think Tank ID** (required: false)
	+ Select all think tanks you have affiliation with.

### 6. Program ID (program_id)
* **Program ID** (required: false)
	+ Select all programs you have participated in.

### 7. Phone (phone)
* **Phone** (required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 8. Email (email)
* **Email** (required: false)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.

### 9. Date (date)
* **Date** (required: false)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the fellowship administrators.
