# Franchise Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Franchise Application Form is designed to collect information from potential franchisees for an entrepreneurship program. This form is used to gather personal details, business information, and preferences related to franchising.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your business name and type (if applicable).
3. Provide your contact phone number.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Indicate your interest in the franchise opportunity.
6. Specify your franchise status.
7. Enter the location for your potential franchise.
8. If applicable, specify the franchise fee.
9. Choose the date for your potential franchise.
10. Choose your franchise terms of agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: true): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your business email address.
* **Business Name** (text, required: false): Enter your business name (if applicable).
* **Business Type** (text, required: false): Enter your business type (if applicable).
* **Address** (text, required: false): Enter your business address (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Franchise Interest** (text, required: false): Indicate your interest in the franchise opportunity.
* **Franchise Status** (text, required: false): Specify your current franchise status.
* **Franchise Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location for your potential franchise.
* **Franchise Fee** (number, required: false): If applicable, enter the franchise fee.
* **Franchise Date** (date, required: false): Choose the date for your potential franchise.
* **Franchise Terms** (select_one, required: false): Choose your franchise terms of agreement (Yes/No).
* **Franchise Type** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of franchise you are interested in (Yes/No).
