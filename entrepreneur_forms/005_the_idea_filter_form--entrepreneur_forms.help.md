# The Idea Filter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Idea Filter Form is designed to collect and categorize ideas based on various criteria such as category, status, priority, and tags. It provides a simple and user-friendly way to filter and filter ideas for better organization and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Description" field with a brief explanation of your idea.
2. Select a category from the "Category" dropdown menu that best matches your idea.
3. Add relevant tags to further describe and categorize your idea.
4. If your idea has a specific date or time frame, please enter it in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
5. Select the "Idea Status" that best describes the current status of your idea.
6. Choose the "Idea Priority" based on the level of importance or urgency of your idea.
7. Finally, add any additional notes or comments about your idea in the "Notes" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Description** (Description, text, optional): A brief explanation of your idea.
- **Category** (Category, select_one, optional): Select a category that best matches your idea.
- **Tags** (Tags, select_multiple, optional): Add relevant tags to further describe and categorize your idea.
- **Date** (Date, date, optional): If your idea has a specific date or deadline, please enter it here.
- **Time** (Time, time, optional): If your idea has a specific time frame, please enter it here.
- **Idea Status** (Idea Status, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your idea (e.g. "Idea A", "Idea B", "Idea C").
- **Idea Priority** (Idea Priority, select_one, optional): Choose the priority level of your idea (e.g. "Priority A", "Priority B", "Priority C").
- **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your idea.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and concrete as possible when filling out the form.
- Make sure to select the most accurate category and tags for your idea.
- Use the "Notes" fields to provide additional context or explanations about your idea.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
