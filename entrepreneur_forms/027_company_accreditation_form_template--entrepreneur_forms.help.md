# Company Accreditation Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Company Accreditation Form Template is a standard template for collecting information from companies seeking accreditation. The form serves to gather essential details about the company, ensuring a smooth and efficient accreditation process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
- Ensure the company name matches the business registration ID.
- Indicate the primary contact person and their email address.
- Select the level of accreditation being requested.
- If attaching documents, list them in the supporting documents field.
- Finally, type your name in the declaration field to confirm accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the legal registered name of your company.
* **Registration number** (`registration_number`, `text`, required): Enter your company's government or business registration ID.
* **Contact person name** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `text`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person for your company.
* **Accreditation level requested** (`accreditation_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of accreditation your company is applying for.
* **Supporting documents list** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): List any supporting documents attached for review.
* **Declaration of accuracy** (`declaration`, `text`, required): Type your name to declare that the information provided is accurate.
