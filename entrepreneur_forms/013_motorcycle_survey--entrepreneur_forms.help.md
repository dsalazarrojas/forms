# motorcycle_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The motorcycle survey form is designed to collect information about a motorcycle and its owner. This form is used to gather details such as the motorcycle's name, owner's name, and purchase price, among other relevant fields.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields, such as the motorcycle's name and owner's name.
2. Fill in the optional fields with the requested information, including preferred model, color, and type.
3. Enter the purchase price and owned time in the respective fields.
4. Provide feedback about the motorcycle, if any.
5. Enter your email address and phone number, if applicable.
6. Select the category and subcategory of the motorcycle.
7. Fill in the preferred features and distance traveled in the respective fields.
8. Enter the number of hours spent riding per week and the average speed and distance of each ride.
9. Enter the motorcycle's ID and owner's ID, if applicable.
10. Fill in the last maintenance details, including the date, miles, and cost.
