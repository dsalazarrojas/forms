# Stripe Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Payment Form is a versatile form designed to collect payment information from users. It is typically used to request payment methods, collect card numbers, expiration dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your payment method from the options provided.
2. Enter the amount you wish to pay.
3. Enter your card number (if selecting a credit card as the payment method).
4. Enter your expiration date (if selecting a credit card as the payment method).
5. Enter your expiration month and year (if selecting a credit card as the payment method).
6. Enter your CVV (Card Verification Value) for credit card payments.
7. Enter your postal code (optional).
8. Enter your email address (optional).
9. Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the options: credit card, PayPal, or Stripe Connect.
	+ This field determines how you will be making the payment.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to pay.
	+ This is the amount you will be paying, e.g., $10.00.
* **Card Number**: Enter your credit card number.
	+ This is the number on your credit card.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter your expiration date for credit card payments.
	+ This is the date your card will expire, e.g., 12/2024.
* **Expiration Month**: Select your expiration month.
	+ This is the month your card will expire, e.g., 1-12.
* **Expiration Year**: Select your expiration year.
	+ This is the year your card will expire, e.g., 2024 or 2025.
* **CVV**: Enter your CVV for credit card payments.
	+ This is the 3-digit code on the back of your card.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code (optional).
	+ This is your mailing address, for example, 123 Main St, Anytown USA 12345.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
	+ This is your contact email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
	+ This is your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
