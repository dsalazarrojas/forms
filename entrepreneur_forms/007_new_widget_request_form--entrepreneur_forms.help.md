# New Widget Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from customers requesting new widgets. It will help us process your widget request efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields (if any) are filled out.
3. Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, optional): Please fill out this field with your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional): Provide your company's name and any other relevant information.
* **Widget Description** (`widget_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the widget you are requesting. Be as specific as possible about the widget's features and requirements.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for this widget request.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Widget Image** (`widget_image`, text, optional): Provide any relevant image or description of the widget you are requesting.
* **Widget Type** (`widget_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of widget you are requesting (e.g., Widget A, Widget B, or Widget C).
* **Widget Color** (`widget_color`, select one, optional): Choose the color of the widget you are requesting (e.g., red, blue, or green).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Double-check your details for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
* Please allow us some time to process your request. We will respond to you as soon as possible.
