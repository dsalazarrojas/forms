# Organization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organization Registration Form is designed to collect information from organizations, allowing them to register and be listed on the platform. This form is essential for creating a profile for your organization on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the designated field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the designated field.
3. Enter your Organization Name in the designated field.
4. Enter your Position Title in the designated field.
5. Enter your Department in the designated field.
6. Enter your Email address in the designated field. Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
7. Enter your Phone number in the designated field.
8. Enter your Date of Birth in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name in the designated field.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name in the designated field.
* **Organization Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your organization in the designated field.
* **Position Title (Text)**: Enter your position title or role within the organization.
* **Department (Text)**: Enter the department or team you belong to within the organization.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact the platform support team.
