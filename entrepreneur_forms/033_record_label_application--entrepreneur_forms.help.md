# record_label_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for artists to submit their record label applications. Please fill out the form completely to ensure that your submission is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your artist name and other details in the required fields.
2. Select the appropriate record type (e.g., Single, Album, EP, etc.).
3. Check the record label agreement box if you agree with the label's terms.
4. Provide a signature to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Please enter your artist name as it should be displayed on the record label.
* **Label Name** (`label_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the record label you are applying to.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your application.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address you can be contacted at.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_biography`, text, optional): Briefly describe your artistic background and experience.
* **Artist Website** (`artist_website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL for more information.
* **Artist Social Media** (`artist_social_media`, select multiple, optional): Select the social media platforms you are active on.
* **Label Representative** (`label_representative`, text, optional): If you have a representative for the label, enter their name.
* **Artist Manager** (`artist_manager`, text, optional): If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address for mail or package delivery.
* **Record Type** (`record_type`, select one, required): Select the type of record you are submitting (e.g., Single, Album, EP, etc.).
* **Record Label** (`record_label`, text, optional): Enter the name of the record label you are applying to.
* **Artist Statement** (`artist_statement`, text, optional): Briefly describe why you are submitting your record to this label.
* **Label Agreement** (`label_agreement`, select one, required): Check the box to agree with the label's terms.
* **Artist Signature** (`artist_signature`, text, optional): Provide your signature to confirm your submission.
