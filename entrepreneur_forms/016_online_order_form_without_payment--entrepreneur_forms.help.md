# online_order_form_without_payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an online order without payment from customers, allowing them to select a service and provide additional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the **Service** you are interested in from the drop-down menu.
5. Choose the date and time you would like to have the service by filling in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Service** (Service, select_one, required: false): Select the service you are interested in. Please choose one of the available options: Service 1, Service 2, or Service 3.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Choose the date you would like to have the service.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to have the service.
* **Note** (Note, note, required: false): Please provide any additional information or notes you would like to add to your order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
- Use a valid email address that you have access to.
- Select a valid service from the available options.
- If you have any specific requests or questions, please use the "Note" field to provide more details.
