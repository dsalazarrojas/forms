# Business Model Innovation Partner Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential partners about their company, interests, and expectations for a potential collaboration. It helps the Innovation Partner team to assess partnership potential and tailor the engagement process accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about your company.
2. Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure a successful partnership assessment.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can ask for clarification by contacting us at [contact information].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Title/Position** (3, text, optional): Provide your job title or role.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Website** (6, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company website.
* **Industry** (7, select_one, required): Select your primary industry focus from the list.
* **Company Size** (8, select_one, required): Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Areas of Partnership Interest** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the areas where you're interested in contributing.
* **Type of Partnership Sought** (10, select_one, required): Choose the type of partnership that interests you.
* **Target Sectors or Markets** (11, text, required): Describe the industries or markets you serve.
* **Your Unique Value Proposition** (12, text, required): Explain what you bring to the table.
* **Project or Partnership Proposal** (13, text, required): Describe your partnership vision.
* **Expected Outcomes** (14, text, optional): Define what success looks like.
* **Timeline Expectations** (15, select_one, optional): Choose when you envision this partnership starting.
* **Budget or Investment Range** (16, select_one, optional): If applicable, provide your budget scope.
* **Company Credentials or References** (17, text, optional): Provide relevant experience or certifications.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (18, select_one, required): Select how you'd like to be contacted.

Note that fields marked as required must be filled out, otherwise, the form cannot be submitted. If you need help or clarification, please don't hesitate to contact us at [contact information].
