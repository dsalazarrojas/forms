# early_access_list_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The early_access_list_template form is designed to gather information from customers who have requested early access to a product. This form helps to capture the essential details of the customer's early access request, allowing us to efficiently process and follow up on their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Customer Info" page.
2. Provide your name and contact information (if you're an individual) or your company and contact information (if you're a business).
3. Enter the name of the product you're requesting early access to in the "Product Name" field.
4. Enter the date you'd like to receive early access to the product in the "Launch Date" field.
5. Enter the price you're willing to pay for the early access in the "Price" field.
6. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments about your early access request in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you'll enter your contact information, which will help us to get in touch with you about your early access request.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the product you're interested in accessing early.
* **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date when you'd like to receive early access to the product. Please note that this is an optional field, but providing a specific date will help us to prioritize your request.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required: true): Enter the amount you're willing to pay for early access to the product. This will help us to determine the feasibility of your request.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or questions, you can use this field to provide more information about your early access request.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you're submitting a valid email address in the "Customer Info" field to ensure we can contact you about your early access request.
- If you're requesting early access to multiple products, please submit a separate early_access_list_template form for each product.
