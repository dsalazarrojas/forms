# Commission Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commission Agreement Form is used to document and agree upon the commission terms for a salesperson's services. This form is essential for ensuring clear communication and avoiding misunderstandings between the salesperson and the entity engaging them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your information**: Enter your name or the name of your company as the principal or entity engaging the salesperson.
2. **Enter salesperson information**: Provide the name of the individual receiving the commission.
3. **Describe commission structure**: Enter the percentage or flat fee details of the commission structure.
4. **Specify commission rate**: Enter the numeric percent of the commission rate (e.g., 10%).
5. **Choose payment schedule**: Select when the commissions will be paid (e.g., monthly, quarterly, upon closing, or other).
6. **Define territory or product scope**: Describe the scope covered by the agreement (e.g., specific product or territory).
7. **Set effective date**: Enter the start date for the commission terms.
8. **List termination conditions**: Briefly list the grounds for termination.
9. **Confirm acceptance**: Select to confirm acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal name or company** (`principal_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the entity engaging the salesperson (e.g., Company X Inc.).
* **Salesperson name** (`salesperson_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual receiving the commission (e.g., John Doe).
* **Commission structure** (`commission_structure`, text, required): Describe the percentage or flat fee details of the commission structure (e.g., 10% of sales revenue).
* **Commission rate** (`commission_rate`, number, optional): Enter the numeric percent of the commission rate (e.g., 12).
* **Payment schedule** (`payment_schedule`, select_one, required): Select when the commissions will be paid (e.g., monthly).
* **Territory or product scope** (`territory_or_products`, text, required): Describe the scope covered by the agreement (e.g., sales in the North region).
* **Effective date of agreement** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the start date for the commission terms (e.g., January 1, 2023).
* **Termination conditions summary** (`termination_conditions`, text, required): Briefly list the grounds for termination (e.g., sales not meeting targets).
* **Sign off confirmation** (`sign_off`, select_one, required): Select to confirm acceptance of the terms (e.g., I accept the terms).
