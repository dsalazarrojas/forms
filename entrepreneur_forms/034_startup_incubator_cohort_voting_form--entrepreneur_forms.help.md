# Startup Incubator Cohort Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from entrepreneurs who have participated in the startup incubator program. The goal is to understand their experiences, perceptions, and opinions on the helpfulness of the mentors and review committee, and to gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each section and fill out the required information.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the options that best fit your experience and opinions.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incubator Name** (`incubator_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the incubator you participated in.
* **Mentor Name** (`mentor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the mentor you worked with.
* **How helpful were the mentors?** (`helpfulness_vote`, select_one, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
* **Score for mentors' helpfulness** (`score_1`, number, required): Rate the helpfulness of the mentors from 1-5.
* **Score for review committee's helpfulness** (`score_2`, number, required): Rate the helpfulness of the review committee from 1-5.
* **Score for overall incubator experience** (`score_3`, number, required): Rate your overall experience from 1-5.
* **Review Committee Name** (`review_committee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the review committee member(s) you interacted with.
* **Review Committee Email** (`review_committee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the review committee member(s).
* **Review Committee Phone** (`review_committee_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the review committee member(s).
* **Review committee helpfulness score** (`review_committee_helpfulness`, number, required): Rate the helpfulness of the review committee from 1-5.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience from 1-5.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
* **How helpful was the review committee?** (`committee_helpfulness`, select_one, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
* **Incubator ID** (`incubator_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the incubator you participated in.
* **How helpful was the mentor?** (`mentor`, select_one, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
* **Incubator Comments** (`incubator_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the incubator.
* **Committee Name** (`committee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the committee member(s) you interacted with.
* **Committee Email** (`committee_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the committee member(s).
* **Committee Phone** (`committee_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the committee member(s).
* **How helpful was the committee?** (`committee`, select_multiple, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
* **Incubator** (`incubator`, select_multiple, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
* **Mentor** (`mentor_2`, select_multiple, required): Answer on a scale of 1-4 (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful).
