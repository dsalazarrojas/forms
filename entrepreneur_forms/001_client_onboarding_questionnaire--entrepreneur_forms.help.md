# Client Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your business, goals, and expectations from the consultant. This information will help us better understand your needs and tailor our approach to provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Business Name** (Page 1).
2. Provide the **Primary Contact Person** (Page 1).
3. Enter the **Email Address** and **Phone Number** of your primary contact person (Page 1).
4. Select the **Industry Type** that best describes your business (Page 1).
5. Specify the **Company Size** (Page 2).
6. Describe your **Primary Business Goals** (Page 2).
7. If applicable, list any **Current Business Challenges** (Page 3).
8. Choose the **Budget Range for This Engagement** (Page 3).
9. Select the **Project Timeline** (Page 3).
10. List the **Key Decision Makers** involved in approving this engagement (Page 4).
11. Indicate if you have had **Previous Experience with Consultants or Advisors** (Page 4).
12. Specify your **Preferred Method of Communication** (Page 4).
13. Provide any **Additional Notes** about your engagement (Page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter your company or business name (Page 1).
* **Primary Contact Person**: Enter the name of your primary contact person (Page 1).
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of your primary contact person (Page 1).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your primary contact person (Page 1).
* **Industry Type**: Select the industry that best describes your business (Page 1).
* **Company Size**: Choose the approximate number of employees (Page 2).
* **What are your primary business goals?**: Briefly describe the goals you hope to achieve through this engagement (Page 2).
* **What are your current business challenges?**: Describe any pain points or obstacles your business is facing (Page 3).
* (Optional)
* **Budget Range for This Engagement**: Select the budget range for the engagement (Page 3).
* **Project Timeline**: Choose the time frame for completing the project (Page 3).
* **Who are the key decision makers?**: List the names and titles of people involved in approving this engagement (Page 4).
* **Have you worked with consultants or advisors before?**: Indicate if you have previous experience (Page 4).
* **Preferred Communication Method**: Choose how you would like to be communicated with (Page 4).
* **Any additional information we should know?**: Provide any other information relevant to your engagement (Page 5).
