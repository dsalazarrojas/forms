## Business Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Information Request Form is designed to collect essential information from business owners and operators to provide accurate and up-to-date data for making informed decisions and catering to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name in the "Your Company Name" field.
2. Fill in your full name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Choose the category of the business you are inquiring about from the "Business Type" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the name of the business you are inquiring about in the "Business Name to Inquire About" field.
6. Select all relevant information categories you are requesting from the "What Information Are You Requesting" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the purpose of your request from the "Purpose of Request" dropdown menu.
8. If needed, provide additional details in the "Additional Details" field.
9. Choose the date you need the response by in the "Date Information Needed By" field.
10. Confirm that you authorize this information request by selecting "Yes, I authorize this request" from the "I authorize this information request" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Company Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Your Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your business or personal contact number.
* **Business Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the category of your business (e.g., Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Technology, Healthcare, Other).
* **Business Name to Inquire About** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the business you want information on.
* **What Information Are You Requesting** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose all relevant information categories you are requesting (e.g., Contact Information, Financial Records, Business Registration, Product Catalog, Services Offered, Pricing Information).
* **Purpose of Request** (Select One, Required): Choose the reason for your request (e.g., Business Partnership, Market Research, Vendor Evaluation, Competitive Analysis, Networking, Other Purpose).
* **Additional Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any specific information or context related to your request.
* **Date Information Needed By** (Date, Optional): Choose the date you need the response by.
* **I authorize this information request** (Select One, Required): Confirm that you authorize this request.
