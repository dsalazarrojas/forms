# Self Employed Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Self Employed Purchase Order Form is a standardized template used to document and submit purchase orders from self-employed individuals or businesses.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter Client Information**:
	* Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
	* Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
2. **Enter Invoice Details**:
	* Enter the date of the invoice in the "Date" field.
	* Enter the invoice number in the "Invoice Number" field.
	* Describe the work or services provided in the "Description of Work" field.
	* Enter the total hours worked or services rendered in the "Hours" field.
3. **Enter Rates and Total**:
	* Enter the rates or fees charged for the work or services provided in the "Rates" field.
	* Calculate the total amount due by multiplying the "Rates" and "Hours" fields.
4. **Add Additional Information**:
	* Enter any additional comments or notes about the invoice in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. **Enter Payment Terms**:
	* Enter any payment terms or conditions, such as payment methods or due dates, in the "Payment Terms" field.
6. **Enter Billing Information**:
	* Enter the billing address of the individual or business in the "Billing Address" field.
	* Enter the billing city in the "Billing City" field.
	* Enter the billing state in the "Billing State" field.
	* Enter the billing zip code in the "Billing Zip" field.
7. **Sign and Date**:
	* Sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
	* Have the client sign and date the "Client Signature" field to confirm that they have received and agree to the terms of the invoice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client who is being invoiced.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the client.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the client.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the invoice.
* **Invoice Number** (`invoice_number`, `number`, required): Enter the invoice number.
* **Description of Work** (`description_of_work`, `text`, required): Describe the work or services provided.
* **Hours** (`hours`, `number`, required): Enter the total hours worked or services rendered.
* **Rates** (`rates`, `number`, required): Enter the rates or fees charged for the work or services provided.
* **Total** (`total`, `number`, required): Calculate the total amount due by multiplying the "Rates" and "Hours" fields.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the invoice.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing address of the individual or business.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing city.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing state.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter the billing zip code.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the "Signature" field to confirm that the form is complete and accurate.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required): Sign and date the "Client Signature" field to confirm that they have received and agree to the terms of the invoice.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check that all fields are accurate and complete.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that all terms and conditions are met.
