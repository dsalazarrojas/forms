# magazine_order_with_eway_payment_gateway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to process magazine orders with eWAY payment gateway integration. It captures details related to order type, issue number, payment method, shipping address, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required and optional fields as per your preferences. Start by selecting the order type and issue number. Then, choose your preferred payment method and fill in the shipping details. Finally, add any order notes and custom preferences if required.
