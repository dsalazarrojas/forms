# product_warranty_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register products for warranty. It is essential to provide accurate information to ensure proper warranty activation and future support.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the correct "Product Name" option (yes or no).
6. Enter the product number in the "Product Number" field.
7. Select the correct "Product Warranty" option (yes or no).
8. Choose the correct "Customer Type" option (yes or no).
9. Review and submit the form once all fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your address here to help us contact you.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive correspondence.
* **Product Name** (select multiple, required): Select whether the product is subject to warranty.
* **Product Number** (number, optional): Enter the product number if applicable.
* **Product Warranty** (select multiple, required): Select whether the product has warranty.
* **Customer Type** (select multiple, optional): Choose the type of business or industry you belong to.
* **Tips**: Ensure you fill in all required fields to register your product correctly. If you're unsure about any field, contact our support team for assistance.
