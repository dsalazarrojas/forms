# Business Formation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This questionnaire helps us understand your business idea and formation plans to guide our support and resources allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Fill in all required fields (marked as required).
3. You can use the space provided in each section to provide additional information or explanations.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please provide your best estimate or answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Name** (`Founder Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government ID.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Business Experience Level** (`Business Experience Level`, select_one, required):
	+ No experience
	+ Some experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Extensive experience
* **Co-Founders Involved** (`Co-Founders Involved`, select_one, required):
	+ No, I am starting alone
	+ Yes, 1 co-founder
	+ Yes, 2 co-founders
	+ Yes, 3 or more co-founders
* **Business Concept** (`Business Concept`, text, required): Describe your business idea in a few sentences.
* **Target Market** (`Target Market`, text, required): Identify your target audience or customer base.
* **Estimated Market Size** (`Estimated Market Size`, text, optional): Provide an estimated size of your market.
* **Competitive Advantage** (`Competitive Advantage`, text, required): Explain what makes your business unique.
* **Preferred Business Structure** (`Preferred Business Structure`, select_one, required):
	+ Sole Proprietorship
	+ Partnership
	+ LLC
	+ C-Corporation
	+ S-Corporation
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Unsure
* **Why this structure?** (`Why this structure?`, text, optional): Explain why you chose this business structure.
* **Where will the business operate?** (`Where will the business operate?`, text, required): Provide the city or area where your business will operate.
* **Type of Operation** (`Type of Operation`, select_one, required):
	+ Home-based
	+ Physical storefront
	+ Online only
	+ Mixed (online and physical)
	+ Mobile/Service-based
* **Estimated Startup Capital Needed** (`Estimated Startup Capital Needed`, number, required): Provide an estimated investment amount required to start your business.
* **Funding Plan** (`Funding Plan`, text, required): Explain how you plan to fund your business.
* **Do you have financial projections?** (`Do you have financial projections?`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ In progress
* **Planned Launch Timeline** (`Planned Launch Timeline`, select_one, required):
	+ Within 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ Over 1 year
* **First Year Goals** (`First Year Goals`, text, optional): Describe your first-year goals and objectives.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`Support or Resources Needed`, text, optional): Indicate any support or resources you may need to start your business.
