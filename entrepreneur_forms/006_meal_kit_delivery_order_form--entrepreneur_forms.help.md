# Meal Kit Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to purchase meal kits from your business. Please fill out the form with your details and order preferences to complete your meal kit order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** and **Last name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive communication about your order.
3. Provide your **Phone number** in case we need to contact you.
4. Choose a date for your **Order date** (optional).
5. Select the **Meal kit name** and **Meal kit description** that you would like to order.
6. Specify the **Quantity** of the meal kit you would like to order.
7. Choose a **Payment gateway** for your order (e.g., Stripe, Payeez, PayPal, Bank transfer).
8. Enter the **Total** amount for your order (optional).
9. If you have any **Note** or additional information for the order, you can provide it in the **Note** field.
10. Finally, confirm your **Form ID**, which is a unique identifier for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive communication about your order.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Order date**: Choose a date for your order (optional).
* **Meal kit name**: Select the meal kit you would like to order.
* **Meal kit description**: Provide a brief description of the meal kit you are interested in.
* **Quantity**: Specify the number of meal kits you would like to order.
* **Payment gateway**: Choose a payment method for your order (e.g., Stripe, Payeez, PayPal, Bank transfer).
* **Total**: Enter the total amount for your order (optional).
* **Note**: If you have any additional information or notes about your order, provide them here.
* **Assigned tool**: This field is reserved for internal use and should only be filled out by authorized staff. Please do not change this value.
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique identifier for your order, as assigned by our team. Do not change this value. 

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
- We will contact you with further instructions on how to proceed with your order.
