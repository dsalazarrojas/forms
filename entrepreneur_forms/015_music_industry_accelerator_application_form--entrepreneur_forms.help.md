# Music Industry Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Industry Accelerator Application Form is designed to gather information from artists and music professionals to evaluate their suitability for the Music Industry Accelerator program. The form helps us understand your background, music style, and goals, which are essential for the selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your artist name, email, and contact information in the "Artist Name", "Artist Email", and "Artist Phone" fields.
2. Select your primary music genre from the "Music Genre" field.
3. Choose your music style from the "Music Style" field, selecting all applicable options.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your project in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
5. Select the time of day you plan to start your project in the "Start Time" field.
6. Choose the location where you will be performing or producing music from the "Music Location" field, selecting all applicable options.
7. Enter a note about your project in the "Artist Statement" field.
8. Select when you expect to start your music project in the "Music Start" field.
9. Finally, provide an update on the status of your application in the "Application Status" field and any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name**: This is the name of the artist or band applying for the program. Enter your real name or the name of your band/artist.
* **Artist Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted. This will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Artist Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached. This is optional but can aid in communication.
* **Music Genre**: Choose your primary music genre from the options provided. You can select only one option.
* **Music Style**: Select all the music styles that apply to your project.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your project. If your project is ongoing, enter the date it started.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your project. If it's an ongoing project, enter the date you anticipate it ending.
* **Start Time**: Choose the time of day you plan to start your project.
* **Music Location**: Choose the location where you will be performing or producing music. You can select multiple options.
* **Artist Statement**: Enter a short note about your artistic vision and goals for your music project.
* **Music Start**: Choose when you expect to start your music project.
* **Application Status**: Update the status of your application (approved, in progress, rejected).
* **Notes**: Additional comments or notes about your application or project.
* **Website**: Enter your website URL (if applicable).
* **Artist Facebook**: Enter your Facebook profile URL (if applicable).

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Music Industry Accelerator Application Form accurately and effectively, increasing your chances of being selected for the program.
