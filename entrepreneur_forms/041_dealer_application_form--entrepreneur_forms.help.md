# Dealer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Application Form is a tool used to collect information from dealers who wish to partner or collaborate with a business. This form helps to establish partnerships by collecting essential details about the dealer, their business, and their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Complete the Dealer Contact Information page with your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Move on to the Dealer Information page and fill in your dealership information.
3.  On the Business Information page, provide details about your business.
4.  The Contact Information page is for additional contact details.
5.  Fill out your dealer's address on the Dealer Address page.
6.  Specify your payment terms on the Payment Terms page.
7.  Share your dealer experience and qualifications on the Dealer Experience page.
8.  Attach your dealer license on the Dealer License page.
9.  Provide information about your employment on the Dealer Employment page.
10.  List your equipment on the Dealer Equipment page.
11.  Add any additional notes on the Dealer Notes page.
12.  Sign the Dealer Agreement page to confirm your agreement.
13.  Sign the Dealer Application Form Signature page.
14.  Finally, select the date and time you applied on the Application Date and Application Time pages.
