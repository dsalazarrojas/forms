# demo_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demo submission data from entrepreneurs for the demo submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field, using the correct format for the email address (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a valid date in the "Date" field.
6. Choose a time in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the name of your Company in the "Company" field.
8. Type a brief Message in the "Message" field.
9. Select the tool you are assigned to use for the demo in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. (Optional) Select one or more categories that apply to your submission in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Select a valid date.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Choose a valid time.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, required): Type a brief message.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to you for the demo.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required/optional): (Optional) Select one or more categories that apply to your submission.
* **Category Option** (`Category Option`, text, required/optional): (Optional) Enter a specific category option.
