# Shopify Website Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's Shopify website. The purpose of this form is to gather data on the client's business entity, website, and store details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to collect and handle the client's data.**
2. Fill out the form with the client's information, following the instructions and guidelines provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info**: This field is not currently used (id: 1). If a client info is required, please add or remove this field.
* **Business Info** (id: 2): Enter a brief description of the client's business.
* **Website Info** (id: 3): Enter the website URL for the client's Shopify website.
* **Product Info** (id: 4): Enter a brief description of the products or services offered by the client.
* **Shipping Info** (id: 5): Enter the shipping information for the client's online store.
* **Payment Info** (id: 6): Enter the payment information for the client's online store.
* **Business Entity Info** (id: 7): Enter a brief description of the client's business entity.
* **Shopify Domain** (id: 8): Enter the client's Shopify domain name.
* **Store Name** (id: 9, 13): Enter the name of the client's store.
* **Store Address** (id: 10, 20): Enter the address of the client's store.
* **Business Type** (id: 11): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' if the client's business is a physical store or an online store.
* **Business Description** (id: 12): Enter a brief description of the client's business.
* **Store Name** (id: 14): Enter the name of the client's store.
* **Store Domain** (id: 15): Enter the client's store domain name.
* **Store Tag** (id: 16): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' if the client's store is tagged or not.
* **Email** (id: 17): Enter the client's email address.
* **Password** (id: 18): Enter the client's password (this field is not secure and should not be used for password creation).
* **Website URL** (id: 19): Enter the URL of the client's website.
* **Store Address** (id: 21): Enter the address of the client's store.
* **Store Province** (id: 22): Enter the client's store province or state.
* **Store Postal Code** (id: 23): Enter the client's store postal code or zip code.
* **Store Country** (id: 24): Enter the client's store country.
* **Store Phone** (id: 25): Enter the client's store phone number.

Note: Some fields may not be necessary for all clients, so please only fill out what is required.
