# Stripe Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stripe Product Order Form is designed to collect and manage customer orders for products in a sales or inventory management system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Order A Product" option from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
5. Enter the product quantity and price in the "Product Quantity" and "Product Price" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the total cost of the order in the "Total Cost" field.
7. Add any customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
8. Select the order status from the "Order Status" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order A Product** (Select Multiple): Choose the product you want to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Customer Email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (Text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Product Name** (Text): Enter the product name.
* **Product Quantity** (Number): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Product Price** (Number): Enter the price of the product being ordered.
* **Total Cost** (Number): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Customer Notes** (Note): Add any notes or comments for the customer.
* **Order Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the order from the available options.
* **Order Date** (Date): Enter the date the order was made.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct product from the "Order A Product" option.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for the customer's email, phone number, and other fields.
* Double-check the total cost before submitting the form.
* Use customer notes to provide relevant information for the customer and sales team.
* Select the correct order status to ensure accurate tracking and follow-up.
* Enter the correct order date to maintain accurate records.
