# Fashion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from customers about their fashion products. It will help us understand their experiences, preferences, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product ID of the item you are surveying.
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the product, such as how you would reorder, if you would recommend, and why.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fashion Product ID** (`fashion_product_id`, number, required): Enter the product ID of the item you are surveying.
- **Fashion Questions** (`fashion_survey_questions`, text, optional): Answer any questions you have about your experience with the product.
- **Fashion Questions Answer** (`fashion_survey_questions_answer`, text, optional): Respond to the question about your fashion product experience.
- **Fashion Customer ID** (`fashion_customer_id`, number, optional): Enter your customer ID, if applicable.
- **Fashion Product Type** (`fashion_survey_product_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of fashion product you are surveying.
- **Fashion Product Size** (`fashion_survey_product_size`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the size(s) of the fashion product you are surveying.
- **Fashion Color** (`fashion_survey_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the color(s) of the fashion product you are surveying.
- **Fashion Date Purchased** (`fashion_survey_date_purchased`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the fashion product.
- **Fashion Time Purchased** (`fashion_survey_time_purchased`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you purchased the fashion product.
- **Fashion Would Reorder** (`fashion_survey_would_reorder`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would reorder the fashion product.
- **Fashion Would Recommend** (`fashion_survey_would_recommend`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would recommend the fashion product.
- **Fashion Why Reorder** (`fashion_survey_why_would_reorder`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you would reorder the fashion product.
- **Fashion Why Not Reorder** (`fashion_survey_why_would_not_reorder`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you would not reorder the fashion product.
- **Fashion Would Recommend** (`fashion_survey_would_recommend`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you would recommend the fashion product.
- **Fashion Why Not Recommend** (`fashion_survey_why_not_recommend`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you would not recommend the fashion product.
- **Fashion Satisfaction Score** (`fashion_survey_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): Enter a satisfaction score out of 100.
- **Fashion Would Purchase Again** (`fashion_survey_would_purchase_again`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would purchase the fashion product again.
- **Fashion Why Purchase Again** (`fashion_survey_what_would_purchase_again`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you would purchase the fashion product again.
- **Fashion What Did Like** (`fashion_survey_what_did_like`, text, optional): Describe what you liked about the fashion product.
- **Fashion What Did Not Like** (`fashion_survey_what_did_not_like`, text, optional): Describe what you did not like about the fashion product.
- **Fashion Why Did Not Like** (`fashion_survey_why_didnt_like`, text, optional): Provide a reason for why you did not like the fashion product.
- **Fashion What Would Improve** (`fashion_survey_what_would_improve`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for the fashion product.
- **Fashion What Did Improve** (`fashion_survey_what_did_improve`, text, optional): Describe any improvements of the fashion product.
- **Fashion Comments** (`fashion_survey_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the fashion product.

Note: Fields marked "optional" are not required but may provide additional information to help us better understand your experience.
