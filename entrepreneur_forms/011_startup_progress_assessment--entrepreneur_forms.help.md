# Startup Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Startup Progress Assessment" form is designed to help entrepreneurs evaluate and track their startup's progress at different stages. It's essential to fill out this form to gain insights into your startup's current situation, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions for its growth.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the current stage of your startup from the options provided in the "Current Stage" field.
2. Choose the milestones your startup has completed from the options in the "Completed Milestones" field (you can select multiple).
3. Provide a short description of your startup's current stage in the "Description Of Current Stage" field.
4. Describe each milestone your startup has achieved in the "Description Of Completed Milestones" field.
5. Describe any challenges your startup is currently facing in the "Description Of Challenges Faced" field.
6. Set short-term goals for your startup in the "Short-term Goals" field.
7. Describe your short-term goals in detail in the "Description Of Short-term Goals" field.
8. Optionally, provide any feedback or comments about your startup's progress.
9. Enter your startup's name in the "Startup Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Stage** (Current Stage): Select the current stage of your startup from the options provided:
	+ Early Stage: Your startup is still in the early stages of development, with little to no revenue generated.
	+ Late Stage: Your startup is in the late stages of development, with some revenue generated.
	+ Growth Stage: Your startup is expanding and growing rapidly.
	+ Exit Stage: Your startup has exited the market or been sold.
* **Completed Milestones** (Completed Milestones): Select the milestones your startup has achieved:
	+ Alpha Launch: Your startup has launched a minimum viable product (MVP) to test the market.
	+ Beta Launch: Your startup has launched a refined version of the MVP with feedback from users.
	+ Revenue Generation: Your startup is generating some revenue.
	+ Exit: Your startup has exited the market or been sold.
* **Challenges Faced** (Challenges Faced): Provide a text description of any challenges your startup is currently facing.
* **Short-term Goals** (Short-term Goals): Enter your short-term goals for your startup.
* **Description Of Current Stage** (Description Of Current Stage): Describe your startup's current stage in detail.
* **Description Of Completed Milestones** (Description Of Completed Milestones): Describe each milestone your startup has achieved in detail.
* **Description Of Challenges Faced** (Description Of Challenges Faced): Describe the challenges your startup has faced in detail.
* **Description Of Short-term Goals** (Description Of Short-term Goals): Describe your short-term goals in detail.
* **Feedback** (Feedback): Optionally, provide any feedback or comments about your startup's progress.
* **Startup Name** (Startup Name): Enter your startup's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct current stage of your startup.
* Be specific when describing each milestone achieved and challenge faced.
* Use this form regularly to track your startup's progress and make informed decisions.
