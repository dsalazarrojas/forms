# Non Fungible Tokens Marketplace Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Non Fungible Tokens Marketplace Application Form is a tool for artists and NFT marketplaces to provide necessary information in order to be considered for feature inclusion on a specific platform. This form is used to gather information about the artist, their marketplace, and their NFTs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by *.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. If a field does not apply to you, please leave it blank. Do not provide fictional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Info** (`text`, optional): This field is for a brief description of the artist, including any relevant details about their background or expertise.
* **Artist Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the artist who created the NFT.
* **Marketplace Name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the NFT marketplace where your project is hosted.
* **Description** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your NFT marketplace.
* **Website** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of your website.
* **Twitter Handle** (`text`, optional): Enter your Twitter handle.
* **Instagram Handle** (`text`, optional): Enter your Instagram handle.
* **LinkedIn Handle** (`text`, optional): Enter your LinkedIn handle.
* **Contact Name** (`text`, optional): Enter your name as it should be used for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Marketplace URL** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of your NFT marketplace.
* **Profile URL** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of your profile.
* **Portfolio URL** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of your portfolio.
* **Social Links** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all social media platforms you have a presence on.
* **Categories** (`select_one`, optional): Select the category that best describes your NFT.
* **Tags** (`text`, optional): Enter keywords related to your NFT.
* **Keywords** (`text`, optional): Enter keywords related to your NFT.
* **Bio** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Artwork URL** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of the artwork.
* **Artwork Title** (`text`, optional): Enter the title of the artwork.
* **Artwork Description** (`text`, optional): Enter a description of the artwork.
* **Artwork Price** (`number`, optional): Enter the price of the artwork.
* **Image** (`text`, optional): Enter the URL of an image of your artwork.

Note: The following fields are not applicable to this form as per the given YAML: none.
