# Self Employment Records Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Employment Records Form is designed to collect information about an individual's self-employment status and related expenses. This form is used to track and document relevant details for tax reporting, financial analysis, and other business-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation before starting.
2. Review the form's sections and questions carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Fill in your self-employment income and total income values in the respective fields.
4. Indicate whether you are using the form for business purposes or personal use.
5. If applicable, report your business expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Income** (`self_employment_records_form`, number, required): Enter your self-employment income.
* **Total Income** (`total_income`, number, required): Enter your total income.
* **Business Use** (`business_use_of_form`, select_one, optional): Check if this form is used for business purposes.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are using this form for business purposes.
	+ Select 'No' if you are using this form for personal or other purposes.
* **Business Expenses** (`business_expenses`, number, optional): Enter your business expenses if applicable.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your input data before submitting the form.
- Review your organization's policies and guidelines for specific requirements and regulations.
- Ensure you have all necessary documentation and supporting evidence for your reported information.
