# Freelancer Growth Bundle Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register freelancers for the Growth Bundle, a program designed to help freelancers grow their business and achieve their goals. This form captures essential information from freelancers to understand their goals, experience, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the relevant fields.
2. Enter your Phone Number for us to contact you.
3. Describe your Goal for the Growth Bundle program.
4. Select your Experience Level to help us understand your current level of expertise.
5. Enter your Experience Years to share your background in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Goal**: Describe your main goal for the Growth Bundle program. This will help us understand what you're looking to achieve.
* **Experience Level**: Select one or more of the following options to indicate your current level of expertise:
	+ Beginner: You're just starting out or have limited experience.
	+ Intermediate: You have some experience, but are looking to build on it.
	+ Advanced: You have significant experience and want to refine your skills.
* **Experience Years**: Enter the number of years you've been working in your field.
