# Responsive Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to contact or provide information to the organization. It is intended to gather relevant information from the user and provide a clear and concise way to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is required.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This field is not required.
3.  Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This field is required.
4.  Enter a brief **Subject** for your message in the "Subject" field. This field is not required.
5.  Enter your **Message** in the "Message" field. This field is required.
6.  Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This field is not required.
7.  Enter your **Company** name in the "Company" field. This field is not required.
8.  Enter your **Website** in the "Website" field. This field is not required.
9.  Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field. This field is not required.
10. Enter your **Location** in the "Location" field. This field is not required.
11. If you have an **Attachment**, select the file and click "Attach".
12. Enter the **Date** of your contact in the "Date" field. This field is not required.
13. Enter the **Time** of your contact in the "Time" field. This field is not required.
14. Enter any **Comment** in the "Comment" field. This field is not required.
15. Select whether you would like to **Select One** option in the "Select One" field. This field is required.
16. Select whether you would like to **Select Multiple** options in the "Select Multiple" field. This field is not required.
17. Enter the **Date/Time** of your contact in the "Date/Time" field. This field is not required.
18. Enter a **Note** about your contact in the "Note" field. This field is not required.
19. Enter your **Email 2** in the "Email 2" field. This field is required.
20. Enter your **Phone 2** number in the "Phone 2" field. This field is not required.
21. Enter your **Website 2** in the "Website 2" field. This field is not required.
22. Enter your **Location 2** in the "Location 2" field. This field is not required.
23. Select a file to attach in the "File 2" field. This field is not required.
24. Enter any **Comment 2** in the "Comment 2" field. This field is not required.
25. Enter the **Date 2** of your contact in the "Date 2" field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Last Name** (2, Text, Optional): Enter your last name, if applicable.
* **Email** (3, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Subject** (4, Text, Optional): Enter a brief subject for your message.
* **Message** (5, Text, Required): Enter your message.
* **Phone** (6, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (7, Text, Optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Website** (8, Text, Optional): Enter your website, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (9, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Location** (10, Text, Optional): Enter your location.
* **Attachments** (11, Text, Optional): Select a file to attach.
* **Date** (12, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your contact.
* **Time** (13, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your contact.
* **Comment** (14, Text, Optional): Enter any comments.
* **Select One** (15, Select One, Required): Select the option that best describes your contact.
* **Select Multiple** (16, Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple options for your contact.
* **Date/Time** (17, Text, Optional): Enter the date and time of your contact.
* **Note** (18, Note, Optional): Enter any notes about your contact.
* **Email 2** (19, Email, Required): Enter your second email address.
* **Phone 2** (20, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number 2.
* **Website 2** (21, Text, Optional): Enter your second website.
* **Location 2** (22, Text, Optional): Enter your second location.
* **File 2** (23, Text, Optional): Select a second file to attach.
* **Comment 2** (24, Text, Optional): Enter any comment 2.
* **Date 2** (25, Date, Optional): Enter the date 2 of your contact.
