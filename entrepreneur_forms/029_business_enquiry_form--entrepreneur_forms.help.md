# Business Enquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from visitors interested in our business or services. It collects contact details and enquiry specifics to enable us to efficiently respond to their queries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your needs.
3. Make sure to provide a detailed message in the "Detailed Message" field to ensure we understand your enquiry correctly.
4. Review your form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us address you correctly.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your job title to provide context about your role.
* **Industry** (`industry`, text, optional): Enter your industry for our reference.
* **Type of Enquiry** (`enquiry_type`, select_one, required): Choose what type of enquiry you're making (e.g., Product Information, Service Inquiry, etc.).
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Briefly describe the subject of your enquiry.
* **Detailed Message** (`detailed_message`, text, required): Provide detailed information about your enquiry to ensure our understanding.
* **Specific Product or Service of Interest** (`specific_interest`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the product or service you're interested in.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, optional): Indicate when you need the information or service (e.g., Urgent, Within 1 Week, etc.).
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Choose your company's size to help us gauge your needs.
* **Company Stage** (`company_stage`, select_one, optional): Indicate your company's stage (e.g., Pre-Launch, Startup Phase, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Approximate your budget for the information or service.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.).
* **May We Follow Up** (`follow_up_ok`, select_one, required): Indicate if you're okay with follow-up communication from us.
* **Specific Interest** (`specific_interest`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the product or service you're interested in (same as "Specific Product or Service of Interest" above).
* **Business Context** (`business_context`, note, optional): Enter any additional business context that may be relevant.

This form has been designed to guide you through the required fields to ensure we can efficiently respond to your enquiries. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns.
