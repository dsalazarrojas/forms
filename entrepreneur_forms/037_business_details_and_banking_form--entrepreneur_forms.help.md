# Business Details And Banking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential business information and banking details from entrepreneurs, ensuring that all necessary data is accurately recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring that the business is registered and that the details being submitted are accurate and up-to-date.
2. Begin by filling out the "Business Name" and "DBA (Doing Business As) Name" fields with the legal name of the business and any alternate names used.
3. Select the "Business Type" from the dropdown list to indicate the structure of the business (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
4. Enter the "Federal Tax ID (EIN)" number, which is the Employer Identification Number assigned to the business.
5. Input the "Date Business Established" in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Fill out the "Business Address", "City", "State/Province", and "ZIP/Postal Code" fields to provide the business's location.
7. Select the "Industry" from the dropdown list to indicate the primary business sector.
8. Enter a brief description of the business operations in the "Business Description" field.
9. For "Owner Information", provide the full name of the owner, their title, and their Social Security Number for documentation purposes.
10. Enter the owner's contact phone number and email address.
11. Select the "Bank Name" from the dropdown list to specify the primary bank.
12. Choose the "Account Type" from the dropdown list (Checking, Savings, Money Market, etc.).
13. Enter the "Account Number" and "Routing Number" for the business account.
14. Select the current "Account Status" from the dropdown list (Active, Inactive, Closed, or Pending).
15. Finally, input the "Average Monthly Balance" for the business account (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (2)**: Enter the legal name of the business.
* **DBA (Doing Business As) Name (3)**: If the business uses a name different from the legal name, enter it here.
* **Business Type (4)**: Select the structure of the business (Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN) (5)**: Enter the Employer Identification Number assigned to the business.
* **Date Business Established (6)**: Input the date the business started in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Business Address (7)**: Enter the street address of the business.
* **City (8)**: Enter the city location of the business.
* **State/Province (9)**: Enter the state or province where the business is located.
* **ZIP/Postal Code (10)**: Enter the postal code of the business location.
* **Industry (11)**: Select the primary business sector.
* **Business Description (12)**: Enter a brief description of the business operations.
* **Owner Name (14)**: Enter the full name of the owner.
* **Owner Title (15)**: Enter the owner's title.
* **Owner Social Security Number (16)**: Enter the Social Security Number for documentation purposes.
* **Owner Phone Number (17)**: Enter the owner's contact phone number.
* **Owner Email (18)**: Enter the owner's email address.
* **Bank Name (20)**: Select the primary bank from the list.
* **Account Type (21)**: Choose the type of business account (Checking, Savings, Money Market, etc.).
* **Account Number (22)**: Enter the account number for the business.
* **Routing Number (23)**: Enter the bank routing number for the business account.
* **Account Status (24)**: Select the current status of the business account (Active, Inactive, Closed, or Pending).
* **Average Monthly Balance (25)**: Enter the typical account balance for the business (if applicable).

## Tips

* Double-check all fields to ensure accuracy before submission.
* If the business details need to be updated, make sure to update the existing information accordingly.
* This form is designed for business registration, banking, or other financial purposes.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid errors.
* If unsure about any field or section, refer to the provided hints for clarification.
