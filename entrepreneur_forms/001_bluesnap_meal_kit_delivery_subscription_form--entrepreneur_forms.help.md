# BlueSnap Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BlueSnap Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form is designed to collect information for creating a meal kit delivery subscription with BlueSnap. This form is for new subscriptions only. Please ensure that you have read and understand the form's purpose before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the subscription plan type that best fits your household.
2.  Choose the number of people who will be eating.
3.  Select all the dietary preferences that apply to your household.
4.  Choose the delivery frequency and preferred delivery day.
5.  Enter your first and last name.
6.  Enter your email address for order confirmations.
7.  Enter your phone number for delivery-related communications.
8.  Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
9.  Enter your apartment/unit number (if applicable).
10. Select your preferred payment method.
11. Enter your card details.
12. Enter your promo code (if applicable).
13. Read and accept the terms and conditions of the subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Subscription Plan Type**: Select the meal plan that best fits your household. This will determine the frequency and selection of meals.
    *   2 Meals Per Week: Two meals per week for your household.
    *   3 Meals Per Week: Three meals per week for your household.
    *   4 Meals Per Week: Four meals per week for your household.
    *   5 Meals Per Week: Five meals per week for your household.
    *   7 Meals Per Week: Seven meals per week for your household.
*   **Number of People**: Choose the number of people who will be eating.
    *   2 People: Two people will be eating.
    *   3 People: Three people will be eating.
    *   4 People: Four people will be eating.
    *   5+ People: Five or more people will be eating.
*   **Dietary Preferences**: Select all the dietary preferences that apply to your household.
    *   No Preferences: None of the listed dietary preferences apply.
    *   Vegetarian: Your household follows a vegetarian diet.
    *   Vegan: Your household follows a vegan diet.
    *   Gluten-Free: Your household requires gluten-free meals.
    *   Dairy-Free: Your household requires dairy-free meals.
    *   Low-Carb: Your household prefers low-carb meals.
    *   Keto: Your household follows a keto diet.
    *   Paleo: Your household follows a paleo diet.
*   **Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive your meal kits.
    *   Weekly: Receive meal kits weekly.
    *   Bi-Weekly: Receive meal kits bi-weekly.
    *   Monthly: Receive meal kits monthly.
*   **Preferred Delivery Day**: Select your preferred day of the week for delivery.
    *   Monday: Receive meal kits on Mondays.
    *   Tuesday: Receive meal kits on Tuesdays.
    *   Wednesday: Receive meal kits on Wednesdays.
    *   Thursday: Receive meal kits on Thursdays.
    *   Friday: Receive meal kits on Fridays.
    *   Saturday: Receive meal kits on Saturdays.
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for order confirmations.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery-related communications.
*   **Street Address**: Enter your street address for delivery purposes.
*   **Apartment/Unit Number**: Enter your apartment or unit number (if applicable).
*   **City**: Enter your city.
*   **State**: Enter your state.
*   **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
*   **Delivery Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for the delivery driver.
*   **Subscription Start Date**: Enter when you would like to start receiving meal kits.
*   **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method.
    *   Credit Card: Use a credit card for payment.
    *   Debit Card: Use a debit card for payment.
    *   PayPal: Use PayPal for payment.
    *   Apple Pay: Use Apple Pay for payment.
    *   Google Pay: Use Google Pay for payment.
*   **Card Number**: Enter your card details.
*   **Promo Code**: Enter any applicable promo code.
*   **Terms and Conditions Acceptance**: Read and accept the terms and conditions of the subscription.
    *   Yes, I Accept: Accept the terms and conditions.
    *   No, I Do Not Accept: Do not accept the terms and conditions.

Tips:
*   Be accurate when completing the form to ensure a smooth subscription process.
*   Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
*   If you have any issues with the form, contact our customer support team.
*   Ensure you understand the terms and conditions before accepting them.
