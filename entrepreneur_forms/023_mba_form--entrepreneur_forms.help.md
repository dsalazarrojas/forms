# mba_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "mba_form" is a form designed to collect information from applicants for MBA programs. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful answers, as this information will be used to assess your suitability for MBA programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your basic demographic information. Please fill out your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact details, including your email and phone number.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, required/optional): Describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees, certifications, or courses you've completed.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): List your work experience, including your job title, company name, and duration of employment.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required/optional): Attach your resume or CV, which should include your professional experience, skills, and education.
* **Transcripts** (`transcripts`, text, required/optional): Provide scanned copies of your transcripts from previous educational institutions.
* **References** (`references`, text, required/optional): Provide names and contact information for two or three professional references.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your financial information, including your income, assets, and liabilities.
* **MBA Goals** (`mba_goals`, text, required/optional): Describe your goals for pursuing an MBA degree.
* **MBA Programs** (`mba_programs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of MBA program you're interested in (Full-Time, Part-Time, Online).
* **Test Scores** (`test_scores`, text, required/optional): Provide your scores on relevant standardized tests, such as the GMAT, GRE, or TOEFL.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the languages you're proficient in (English, Mandarin, Arabic, Spanish, French, German).
* **Computer Skills** (`computer_skills`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the computer skills you possess (Python, JavaScript, Java, HTML/CSS, C++).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your highest education level achieved (High School, Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the date you plan to start your MBA program.
* **Expected Program Duration** (`expected_program_duration`, number, required/optional): Estimate the duration of your desired MBA program.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the date you took a relevant standardized test.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the date of your MBA interview.
* **Admission Decision** (`admission_decision`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you've been accepted into an MBA program ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, select the date you submitted your application.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your application, confirming that the information provided is accurate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **File** (`file`, text, required/optional): Attach any supporting documents or files, such as a resume or certificates.
