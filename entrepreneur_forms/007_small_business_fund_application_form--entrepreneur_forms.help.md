# Small Business Fund Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Small Business Fund Application Form" is designed to help small businesses apply for funding. This form collects relevant information about the business, including its name, address, contact details, and funding requirements. To be eligible for funding, applicants must provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the **small_business_name** field with the name of your business.
2. Select the appropriate **business_type** from the list provided.
3. Enter the **funding_request** in the space provided.
4. If applicable, provide the **business_address** of your business.
5. Enter your **email_address** (if different from the one above).
6. Enter your **phone_number** (if applicable).
7. Add a brief **description** of your business (if desired).
8. Select the **date** and **time** for the funding request (in YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM format, respectively).
9. Add any additional **note** (if necessary).
10. Select the options provided for **select_one_1**, **select_multiple_1**, and **decimal_1**, **integer_1**, **decimal_2**, **integer_2**, and **select_one_2** (as applicable).
11. Enter the numerical values for **integer_1**, **integer_2, decimal_1, and **decimal_2** (if applicable).
12. Enter the **date_1** and **date_2** in YYYY-MM-DD format (if applicable).
13. Enter the **time_1** and **time_2** in HH:MM format (if applicable).
14. Enter your **email_1** and **email_2** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **small_business_name**: Enter the name of your small business.
* **business_type**: Select the category that best describes your business.
* **funding_request**: Describe the reason for your funding request.
* **business_address**: Enter the address of your business (if applicable).
* **email_address**: Enter your email address (if different from the **email_1** field).
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **description**: Provide a brief description of your business.
* **date**: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time**: Enter the time in HH:MM format.
* **note**: Add any additional notes (if necessary).
* **select_one_1**: Select one of the options provided.
* **select_multiple_1**: Select one or more of the options provided.
* **decimal_1**: Enter a decimal value.
* **integer_1**: Enter an integer value.
* **time_1**: Enter the time in HH:MM format.
* **decimal_2**: Enter a decimal value.
* **integer_2**: Enter an integer value.
* **date_1**: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **date_2**: Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time_2**: Enter the time in HH:MM format.
* **select_one_2**: Select one of the options provided.
* **email_1**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **email_2**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
