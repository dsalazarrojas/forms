# Get In Touch Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Get In Touch Contact Form is a support-oriented form designed to facilitate communication between users and support staff. It is used to gather contact information and the reason for the contact, enabling support staff to respond to inquiries and provide assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Name" field with your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
3. Enter your "Email" address for contact purposes.
4. If you have a "Phone" number you'd like to include, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Message" field, provide a clear and concise description of the reason you're contacting us.
6. For "What would you like to get in touch about?", select the relevant option from the dropdown list.
7. If you'd like to sync this contact with our CRM (Customer Relationship Management system), choose the corresponding option from the dropdown list.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to include, enter it in the "Phone" field.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the reason you're contacting us.
* **What would you like to get in touch about?** (`contact_form_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the relevant option from the dropdown list to indicate the reason for your contact.
* **Sync with CRM?** (`crm_sync`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If you'd like to sync this contact with our CRM, choose the corresponding option from the dropdown list.
