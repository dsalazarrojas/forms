# Stripe Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details from customers to be used for processing book orders through Stripe.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the "Order Details" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (Order Details, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Name (customer_name, text, optional)**: Enter your full name, which will be used to address your order.
* **Customer Email (customer_email, email, optional)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your order details for accuracy.
- Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure we can contact you effectively.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
