# new_client_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new client request form is designed to collect information from clients who require assistance. This form will help us gather essential details about the client, including their name, contact information, and specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your company name in the company field.
4. Type your address in the address field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field. Please use the format "example@email.com".
6. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
7. Choose a date and time for when you need assistance in the date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your physical address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, date, required): Choose a date when you need assistance.
* **Time Needed** (`time_needed`, time, required): Choose a time when you need assistance.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
- We will review your submission and follow up with further instructions if necessary.
