# Product Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by consumers who use a specific product, allowing them to acknowledge and waive any liabilities associated with the product. It ensures that the consumer has read and understood the statements regarding the product's use and potential risks. This form is a critical step in protecting both the consumer and the company offering the product.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the necessary fields, beginning with the product name, description, and type. These fields help identify the specific product being used.
2.  Next, enter your name, date of birth, and address. This information is essential for identifying you as the consumer and ensuring that you are aware of the product's terms and conditions.
3.  After that, sign your name in the designated area. This serves as a formal acknowledgment of your agreement to the terms outlined.
4.  Finally, indicate your understanding of the product's terms by selecting "I have read and understand the above statements" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you are using or intend to use.
*   **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the product you are using or intend to use.
*   **Consumer Name** (`consumer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Consumer Address** (`consumer_address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign your name in the designated area to acknowledge your agreement to the terms.
*   **Product Type** (`product_type`, text, required): Enter the type of product you are using or intend to use.
*   **Consumer Agreement** (`consumer_agreement`, select_one, required): Indicate your understanding of the product's terms by selecting "I have read and understand the above statements."
*   **Company** (`company`, text, optional): This field is for the company providing the product, if applicable.

## Tips

*   Always read the product's terms and conditions carefully before filling out this form.
*   Ensure that your signature is legible and clear.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form or product, contact the company providing the product before signing the waiver.
*   Keep a copy of the completed form for your records, as it serves as proof of your acknowledgment of the product's terms and conditions.
