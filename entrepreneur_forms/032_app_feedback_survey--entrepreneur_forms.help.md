# App Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this survey is to gather user feedback on your experience with our application. Your input will help us understand your needs and preferences, and we will use this information to improve the app's features and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the survey honestly and thoroughly, answering all the questions that apply to your experience with the app.
2. If you have used the app on multiple platforms, please select the platform you use most.
3. For rating questions, please rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
4. If you have any suggestions or comments about the app, please provide them in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (Optional)** (`user_name`, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can enter your name if you wish to identify yourself. However, this is not required and you can leave it blank.
* **Your Email** (`user_email`, email, Optional): Please enter your email address. This is not required, but it will help us contact you if we need further clarification on your feedback.
* **Which app are you providing feedback for?** (`app_name`, text, Required): Please enter the name of the app you are providing feedback about.
* **Which platform do you use most?** (`platform_used`, select_one, Required): Please select the platform where you use the app most. The options are iOS, Android, Web, and Desktop.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, Required): Please rate your overall experience with the app on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
* **Rating for Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, Required): Please rate how you feel about the value for money of the app. The options are Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, and Excellent.
* **Satisfaction with App Design and UI** (`design_satisfaction`, number, Required): Please rate your satisfaction with the app's design and user interface on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
* **Rating for App Speed and Performance** (`performance_rating`, select_one, Required): Please rate the speed and performance of the app. The options are Very Fast, Adequate, Slow, and Unusable.
* **What is your main reason for using this app?** (`usage_purpose`, text, Required): Please enter the main reason why you use the app.
* **What is the best feature of the app?** (`best_feature`, text, Required): Please enter the feature you think is the best about the app.
* **What is the worst feature of the app?** (`worst_feature`, text, Optional): Please enter the feature you think is the worst about the app. (Note: This question is optional)
* **Do you use any competitor apps?** (`competitor_apps`, text, Optional): If yes, please enter the names of the competitor apps you use.
* **Would you be willing to participate in a brief user interview?** (`interview_willingness`, select_one, Required): Please select if you are willing to participate in a brief user interview. The options are True and False.
