# Credit Application For Business Account Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from a business applicant for creating a new business account or updating existing account information. The form will help us assess the applicant's creditworthiness and ensure they meet our lending criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. Make sure to attach any required documents or supporting materials as specified in the form's instructions.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors and delays.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information**: Please enter your company's name and other relevant information as per the guidelines provided.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the person responsible for your company's account, including their name, title and contact information.
* **Business Information**: Provide details about your company, such as its nature of business, products or services, and ownership structure.
* **Contact Method**: Select the preferred method of contact, such as phone, email, or mail, to ensure efficient communication.
* **Business Address**: Enter your company's physical address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Credit Score**: (Optional) Enter your company's credit score, if applicable.
* **Account Terms**: Please indicate your understanding of the terms and conditions of your proposed account.
* **Financials**: Provide information about your company's financials, including revenue, expenses, and assets.
* **Financial Information**: Attach any supporting financial documents as per the guidelines.
* **Financial Type**: Indicate whether your company generates revenue from 'Yes' or 'No' sources.
* **Financial Value**: Enter the value of your company's financials.
* **Tax ID Number**: Enter your company's tax identification number, if applicable.
* **Bank Information**: Provide details about your company's banking information, including bank name, address, and contact.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your company's bank.
* **Bank Address**: Enter the address of your company's bank.
* **Bank Contact**: Enter the contact information of your company's bank representative.
* **Business Terms**: Indicate your understanding of the business terms and conditions.
* **Financial 2**: Indicate whether your company generates revenue from 'Yes' or 'No' sources.
* **Bank 2**: Enter any additional banking information as per the guidelines.
* **Account Number**: Enter your company's account number.
* **Business Phone**: Enter your company's business phone number.
* **Business Email**: Enter your company's business email address.
* **Business Fax**: Enter your company's business fax number.
* **Business Website**: Enter your company's business website URL.
* **Credit Terms**: Understand and agree to the credit terms and conditions.

Please note that some fields may require additional documentation or explanation to support your application. Ensure you review the instructions carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
