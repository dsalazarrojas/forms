# Startup Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from start-ups seeking support, funding, or mentorship from the startup accelerator program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name.
2. Select your team members from the list provided.
3. Provide company information.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter the date you are submitting your application.
7. Enter the time you are submitting your application.
8. Enter your address.
9. Provide information about your business.
10. Describe your team.
11. Select your industry.
12. Select your business model.
13. Enter your team leader's name.
14. Enter the number of team members you have.
15. Select your business stage.
16. Select your funding stage.
17. Select your company size.
18. Select the location of your business.
19. Enter information about your product.
20. Enter your website (if applicable).
21. Enter your social media (if applicable).
22. Select the type of your company.
23. Select the role of your team.
24. Enter your company address (if applicable).
25. Enter any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`user_input`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
- **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required): Select all team members from the provided list.
- **Company Information** (`company_info`, note, optional): Briefly describe your company.
- **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date_input`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting your application.
- **Time** (`time_input`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting your application.
- **Address** (`address_input`, text, optional): Enter your address.
- **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, optional): Provide information about your business.
- **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, optional): Describe your team.
- **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry your business operates in.
- **Business Model** (`business_model`, select_one, required): Select the business model of your start-up.
- **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, text, optional): Enter your team leader's name.
- **Team Members Count** (`team_members_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of team members you have.
- **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, select_one, required): Select the stage of your business.
- **Funding Stage** (`funding_stage`, select_one, required): Select the funding stage of your start-up.
- **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of your company.
- **Business Location** (`business_location`, select_one, required): Select the location of your business.
- **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter information about your product.
- **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website address (if applicable).
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, text, optional): Enter your social media handle (if applicable).
- **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of your company.
- **Team Role** (`team_role`, select_one, required): Select the role of your team.
- **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company address (if applicable).
- **Other Info** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.

**Tips**

* Ensure you answer all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Double-check your information for spelling and grammar errors.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your start-up and team.
* If you have any doubts or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the startup accelerator support team.
