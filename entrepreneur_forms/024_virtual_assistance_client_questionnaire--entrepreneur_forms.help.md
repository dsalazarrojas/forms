# Virtual Assistance Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide information to the virtual assistant about the needs and expectations of their clients. This will help the assistant to better serve their clients and provide a more personalized and tailored service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information about your client, and we'll use this to provide you with a better experience. You will need to select the options that best fit your needs and experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Assisting Client** (`client_questionnaire`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will enter a description of the client's needs.
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that describe the services you require, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Communication** (`communication`, time, optional): This field will help us determine how much time you require for communication with your clients. You can select the option that best fits your needs.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, select_one, optional): This field will help us determine how quickly you want us to respond to your messages. You can select one of the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): This field will help us determine if you require follow-up from your assistant. You can select one of the following options:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): This field will allow you to rate your overall experience with your assistant.
* **Support** (`support`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about the support you require from your assistant.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional comments about the client's needs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional comments about the services you require.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of your client.
* **Client E-mail** (`client_email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter the email address of your client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the phone number of your client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the address of your client.
* **Assistant Name** (`assistant_name`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the name of your assistant.
* **Assistant E-mail** (`assistant_email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter the email address of your assistant.
* **Assistant Phone** (`assistant_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the phone number of your assistant.
* **Assistant Address** (`assistant_address`, text, optional): This field is where you enter the address of your assistant.
* **Assistant Hours** (`assistant_hours`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the hours of operation of your assistant.
