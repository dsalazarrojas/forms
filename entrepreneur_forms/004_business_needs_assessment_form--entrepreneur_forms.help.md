# Business Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Needs Assessment Form is a tool to help us better understand your business needs and provide personalized support to help you overcome operational challenges and achieve growth goals. Please answer the questions honestly, and we'll use this information to tailor our services to meet your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing a brief overview of your business in the **Business Overview** section.
2. Fill in your **Business Name**, which should match your company's official name.
3. Choose the current stage of your business from the **Current Business Stage** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the number of years your business has been operating in the **Years in Operation** field.
5. Provide the total number of employees in your **Current Number of Employees** field.
6. Move on to the **Operational Needs** section, where you'll identify your main business challenges.
7. Select all the operational challenges that apply to your business from the **Main Business Challenges** dropdown menu.
8. If needed, specify the financial management support you require in the **Financial Management Needs** section.
9. If you have specific growth goals for the next 12 months, describe them in the **Growth Goals for Next 12 Months** field.
10. If you're planning to enter new markets, select **True** in the **Planning Market Expansion** field.
11. In the **Resource Assessment** section, identify any technology or software needs you have.
12. Specify any training needs for your employees in the **Training and Development Needs** section.
13. Provide your name or the name of the decision-maker in the **Contact Person Name** field.
14. Enter your best email address in the **Email Address** field.
15. Choose your preferred method of contact in the **Preferred Contact Method** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`text`, required): Enter your company's official name.
* **Current Business Stage** (`select_one`, required): Choose where your business is in its lifecycle: Startup, Early Growth, Established, Scaling, or Mature.
* **Years in Operation** (`number`, required): How long has your business been operating.
* **Current Number of Employees** (`number`, required): The total number of employees in your company.
* **Main Business Challenges** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all operational challenges that apply to your business: Cash Flow Management, Employee Retention, Marketing and Sales, Technology Systems, Supply Chain, Regulatory Compliance, or Customer Service.
* **Financial Management Needs** (`select_multiple`, optional): If needed, specify the financial management support you require: Accounting Services, Tax Planning, Bookkeeping, Financial Forecasting, or Funding or Capital.
* **Growth Goals for Next 12 Months** (`text`, optional): Describe your specific growth goals for the next 12 months.
* **Revenue Target for Next Year** (`number`, optional): Enter your revenue target for the next year (in dollars).
* **Planning Market Expansion** (`select_one`, optional): If you're planning to enter new markets, select True, False, or Unsure.
* **Technology and Software Needs** (`select_multiple`, optional): If needed, specify the technology or software systems you require: CRM System, Project Management, Accounting Software, E-commerce Platform, Communication Tools, or Automation Solutions.
* **Training and Development Needs** (`select_multiple`, optional): If needed, specify the training needs for your employees: Leadership Development, Technical Skills, Sales Training, Customer Service, or Compliance Training.
* **Contact Person Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the person completing this form or the decision-maker.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your best email address for follow-up communication.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`select_one`, optional): Choose how you prefer to be contacted: Email, Phone, Video Call, or In-Person Meeting.
