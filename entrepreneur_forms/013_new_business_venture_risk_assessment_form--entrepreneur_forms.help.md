# New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form is a tool to evaluate and assess the risks associated with a new business venture. It's essential for entrepreneurs to understand the potential risks involved in starting a new business to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and according to your business needs and circumstances.
2. Fill out the form in sections, starting with the first page.
3. Review and update the form as needed, and use it to inform decision-making processes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form (Page 1)
* **New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form** (`new_business_venture_risk_assessment_form_data`, Text, Optional): This is the main form title that serves as an introduction to the assessment.
* **Business Idea** (`business_idea`, Text, Optional): Describe your business idea in a few words.
### Risk Assessment (Page 2)
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, Number, Optional): Assess the overall risk level of your venture on a scale of 1-10.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more risk factors that may impact your venture. Choose from Low, Medium, and High.
### Risk Factors Details (Page 3)
* **Risk Factors Details** (`risk_factors_details`, Text, Optional): Provide more details about the selected risk factors.
* **Venture Risk Level** (`venture_risk_level`, Number, Optional): Assess the overall risk level of your venture on a scale of 1-10.
### Risk Factors (Page 4)
* **Business Risk Factor 1** (`business_risk_factor_1`, Select One, Optional): Select the risk level for the first factor, choose from Low, Medium, and High.
* **Business Risk Factor 2** (`business_risk_factor_2`, Select One, Optional): Select the risk level for the second factor, choose from Low, Medium, and High.
* **Business Risk Factor 3** (`business_risk_factor_3`, Select One, Optional): Select the risk level for the third factor, choose from Low, Medium, and High.
* **Business Risk Factor 4** (`business_risk_factor_4`, Select One, Optional): Select the risk level for the fourth factor, choose from Low, Medium, and High.
* **Go or No-Go** (`go_or_no_go`, Select One, Optional): Decide whether your venture is a go or no-go based on the risk assessment.
### Additional Information (Page 5)
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or information about your venture.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.

Note: The form is divided into pages, but it's not a physical page-turner, it's just a section indicator for the field-by-field explanation.
