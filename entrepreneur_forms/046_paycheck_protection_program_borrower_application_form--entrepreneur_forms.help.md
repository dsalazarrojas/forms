# Paycheck Protection Program Borrower Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) is a loan program designed to support small businesses through the COVID-19 pandemic. This form is for borrowers to provide details about their business and loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the title page (Page 1) with the name of the business.
2. Select the correct business category on Page 2.
3. Fill out the business details on Page 3, including business name, address, city, and phone number.
4. Fill out the owner details on Page 4, including name, title, address, phone number, and website.
5. Fill out the loan details on Page 5, including loan amount, start and end dates, and loan use of funds.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paycheck Protection Program Borrower Application Form**: This is the title of the form. Please fill out the title with the name of your business.
* **Business Category**: Select the category of your business from the options provided. Please choose the most accurate option.
* **Business Name**: This is the name of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct name of your business.
* **Business Address**: This is the address of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct address of your business.
* **Business City**: This is the city of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct city of your business.
* **Business State**: This is the state of your business. Please select the correct state from the options provided.
* **Business Phone**: This is the phone number of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct phone number of your business.
* **Business Website**: This is the website of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct website of your business.
* **Business Email**: This is the email of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct email of your business.
* **Owner Name**: This is the name of the owner of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct name of the owner.
* **Owner Title**: This is the title of the owner of your business. Please fill out this field with the correct title of the owner.
* **Owner Address**: This is the address of the owner. Please fill out this field with the correct address of the owner.
* **Owner Phone**: This is the phone number of the owner. Please fill out this field with the correct phone number of the owner.
* **Owner Website**: This is the website of the owner. Please fill out this field with the correct website of the owner.
* **Owner Email**: This is the email of the owner. Please fill out this field with the correct email of the owner.
* **Loan Amount**: This is the amount of the loan you are applying for. Please fill out this field with the correct loan amount.
* **Loan Start Date**: This is the start date of the loan. Please fill out this field with the correct start date of the loan.
* **Loan End Date**: This is the end date of the loan. Please fill out this field with the correct end date of the loan.
* **Loan Use of Funds**: This field is for you to describe how you plan to use the loan funds. Please fill out this field with the correct description.
* **Submit**: This is the submit button. Please select this option to submit your application.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly and accurately to ensure timely loan processing. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the loan administrator for assistance.
