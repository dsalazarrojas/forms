# paypal_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "paypal_payment_form" is a form designed to collect payment information for tracking and recording purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "paypal_payment_form" successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select a valid date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter the amount to be paid in the "amount" field.
6. Select a payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field. Options include PayPal, Credit Card, and Bank Transfer.
7. If using a method other than the listed options, provide the details in the "payment_method_other" field.
8. Confirm that the payment has been made in the "Confirm Payment" field.
9. If necessary, provide additional details about the payment in the "confirm_payment_details" field.
10. If payment is confirmed, select a date and time for the "Confirm Payment Date" and "Confirm Payment Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your real name here. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here (required).
* **email**: Enter a valid email address here (required).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth here (required, e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **amount**: Enter the amount to be paid here (required, e.g., 10.99).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to make the payment (PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer) (required).
* **payment_method_other**: If you selected "Other", enter your chosen method (optional).
* **Confirm Payment**: Confirm that the payment is made (required).
* **confirm_payment_other**: If you selected "Other", provide additional details about the payment method (optional).
* **Confirm Payment Details**: If payment confirmation is needed, enter additional details about the payment (optional).
* **Confirm Payment Date**: Select the date of payment confirmation (optional, e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Confirm Payment Time**: Select the time of payment confirmation (optional, e.g., 14:00).
* **Confirm Payment**: Confirm that the payment is made (required).
