# Outsourcing Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us collect information from potential freelancers, independent contractors, or external partners who are interested in working with us on specific projects or jobs. Please fill out this form to provide us with your details and we'll review your application accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide a brief description of the **Job Title** and its **Job ID** (if applicable).
4. Select your preferred **Job Category** from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose the **Job Type** that best describes your work style.
6. Enter your availability to work in the **Available to Work** field (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
7. Choose a suitable **Work Schedule** from the available options.
8. Enter your **Email Address** for contact and communication purposes.
9. Provide your phone number for further contact and scheduling.
10. Optionally, provide a link to your **Resume** or upload a copy.
11. In the **Job Application** field, briefly describe why you're interested in working with us on this job.
12. Select the **Skills** you possess that match our job requirements.
13. Select the **Languages** you're proficient in that match our job requirements.
14. Enter your **Location** where you can work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name here, for example, John Doe.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname here, for example, Doe.
* **Job Title**: Briefly describe the job title and position you're applying for.
* **Job Title Description**: Provide a short description of the job title, if applicable.
* **Job Title ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the job title, if required.
* **Job Category**: Choose the category that best describes the job, such as "Web Development" or "Graphic Design".
* **Job Type**: Select the type that best describes your work style, such as "Freelance" or "Part-time".
* **Available to Work**: Enter the date you're available to start working, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Work Schedule**: Choose a schedule that best fits your work needs, such as "Full-time" or "Flexible Hours".
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which we'll use for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which we'll use for further contact and scheduling.
* **Resume Link**: Optionally, provide a link to your resume or upload a copy, if applicable.
* **Job Application**: Briefly describe why you're interested in working with us on this job.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you possess that match our job requirements.
* **Languages**: Select the languages you're proficient in that match our job requirements.
* **Location**: Enter your location where you can work.

Note that this form is designed to collect necessary information, so please ensure to fill it out accurately and completely. We'll review your application and follow up with further instructions or questions.
