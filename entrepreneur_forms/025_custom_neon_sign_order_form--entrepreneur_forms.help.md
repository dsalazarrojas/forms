# Custom Neon Sign Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to purchase a custom neon sign. It will help us gather the necessary information to create their sign.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in your information on the "Customer Info" page.
* Move on to the "Sign Details" page to provide information about the sign you want.
* Finally, enter the "Order Info" details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (Customer Info)** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name and contact information for our team to get in touch with you.
* **Sign Details (Sign Details)** (`sign_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Describe the design and color scheme of the sign you want to create.
* **Order Info (Order Info)** (`order_info`, text, required: false)
	+ Add any additional information about your order, such as the location where the sign will be installed.
