# local_listings_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for entrepreneurs to submit local listings information for a business directory or listings platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the Personal Information section.
   - First Name: Enter your first name as you would like to be displayed on the listing.
   - Last Name: Enter your last name as you would like to be displayed on the listing.
   - Email: Enter your active email address for correspondence.
   - Phone: Enter your business contact phone number.
   - Location: Enter the location of your business.

2. Provide your business details in the Business Details section.
   - Business Name: Enter the name of your business as it should be displayed on the listing.
   - Business Type: Select the type of business from the options provided.
   - Category: Select the category of your business from the options provided.
   - Sub Category: Select the sub category of your business from the options provided.
   - URL: Enter the URL of your business website or social media profile.
   - Image: Enter the URL or path to the image of your business logo.
   - Description: Enter a brief description of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required):
  Enter your first name as you would like to be displayed on the listing.

* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required):
  Enter your last name as you would like to be displayed on the listing.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
  Enter your active email address for correspondence.

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required):
  Enter your business contact phone number.

* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required):
  Enter the location of your business.

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required):
  Enter the name of your business as it should be displayed on the listing.

* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the type of business from the options provided.

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the category of your business from the options provided.

* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the sub category of your business from the options provided.

* **URL** (`url`, `text`, required):
  Enter the URL of your business website or social media profile.

* **Image** (`image`, `text`, optional):
  Enter the URL or path to the image of your business logo. This field is optional.

* **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional):
  Enter a brief description of your business.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address for correspondence.
* Enter accurate business details to avoid errors in the listing.
* Use the correct business category and sub category for your business type.
* The "Image" field can be left blank if you do not have a business logo.
* The "Description" field can be left blank if you do not have a brief description of your business.
