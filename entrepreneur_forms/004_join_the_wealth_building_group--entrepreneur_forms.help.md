# join_the_wealth_building_group - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "join_the_wealth_building_group" form is designed to collect information from potential members of the "Wealth Building Group" for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your member details, including your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe your goals for joining the group.
3. Check the box to express your consent for membership.
4. Optional: Provide your address, city, state, and zip code for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Details** (<member_details>): This field is for your personal contact information.
* **Goals** (<goals>): Describe your reasons for joining the Wealth Building Group.
* **Consent** (<consent>): Check "I agree" to express your consent for membership.
* **Member Details Email** (<member_details_email>): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Member Details Phone** (<member_details_phone>): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Member Details First Name** (<member_details_first_name>): Enter your first name.
* **Member Details Last Name** (<member_details_last_name>): Enter your last name.
* **Member Details Address** (<member_details_address>): Enter your address (optional).
* **Member Details City** (<member_details_city>): Enter your city (optional).
* **Member Details State** (<member_details_state>): Enter your state (optional).
* **Member Details Zip** (<member_details_zip>): Enter your zip code (optional).
* **Goal** (<goals_goal>): Describe your goal for joining the group.
* **Consent Consent** (<consent_consent>): Check "I agree" to express your consent for membership.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by "" icon).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
