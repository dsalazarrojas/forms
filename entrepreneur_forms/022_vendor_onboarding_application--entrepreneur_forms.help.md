# Vendor Onboarding Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Onboarding Application is a form designed to collect essential information from new vendors who are interested in partnering with our company. This form helps to streamline the onboarding process, making it easier for both our team and the vendor to understand each other's requirements and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Click on the "Submit" button**: Begin by navigating to the Vendor Onboarding Application form.
2. Fill out the fields: Enter your vendor information, contact details, shipping information, and other relevant data as prompted.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click "Submit" to save your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Info**: Enter your company name, which will be used to identify your vendor profile.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your name, address, phone number, email, and other contact details as prompted.
* **Shipping Info**: Enter your shipping name, address, and city to facilitate smooth shipping and delivery processes.
* **Payment Terms**: Select your preferred payment terms (e.g., Net 30, Pro, Net 15, or Net 10) to understand your payment expectations.
* **Shipping Terms**: Select your preferred shipping methods (e.g., Ground, Air, or Both) to clarify your shipping requirements.
* **Tax Terms**: Select your tax requirements (e.g., None, True, or Partial) to ensure accurate tax calculations.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about your onboarding process.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter your company name to complete your vendor profile.
* **Address 1/2**: Enter your primary and secondary addresses for billing and shipping purposes.
* **City**: Enter your city for location-specific services.
* **State**: Enter your state for location-specific services.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for accurate address verification.
* **Country**: Enter your country for international shipping and delivery.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title**: Enter your job title or role as the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for email correspondence.
* **Contact Fax**: Enter your fax number for fax-based communication.
* **Contact Phone Extension**: Enter your phone extension (if applicable).
* **Contact Email Extension**: Enter your email extension (if applicable).
* **Ship Name**: Enter your shipper's name for easy reference.
* **Ship Address 1/2**: Enter your primary and secondary shipping addresses.
* **Ship City**: Enter your shipper's city for location-specific services.
* **Ship Country**: Enter your shipper's country for international shipping and delivery.

Note: Please fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
