# Risk Evaluation Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Evaluation Accelerator Application Form is a tool used to assess the risk level of various projects. It is designed to help project teams evaluate the potential risks associated with their projects and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. If a field is optional, provide information if it's relevant to your project.
4. For select fields, choose the most accurate answer from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
1. **Project Details**
	* Please provide project details.
2. **Risk Evaluation**
	* Choose the level of risk associated with your project (High, Medium, or Low).
3. **Project Location**
	* Select the location of your project from the provided options (USA, Canada, Mexico, or specify another location).
	* If you select "Other (please specify)", please provide the location in the text field.
4. **Team Lead Name**
	* Provide the name of the team lead responsible for your project.
5. **Organization**
	* Provide the name of the organization associated with your project.
6. **Contact Email**
	* Enter the contact email address for your project.
7. **Contact Phone**
	* Enter the contact phone number for your project.
8. **Project Description**
	* Provide a brief description of your project.
9. **Innovation Program**
	* Choose the type of innovation program associated with your project (Accelerator/Incubator, Innovation Program, or Other).
10. **Submitted By**
	* Select who submitted the application (Team Lead, Organization, or both).
11. **Date**
	* Enter the date of submission.
12. **Time**
	* Enter the time of submission.
13. **Note**
	* Provide any additional notes or comments about your project.
13. **Tips**
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure the form is processed correctly.
* If you're having trouble with the form, you can contact us for help.
