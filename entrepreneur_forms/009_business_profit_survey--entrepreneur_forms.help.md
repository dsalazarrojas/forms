# Business Profit Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather business profit data from business owners to help them analyze their financial performance. It's used for internal assessment and comparison of profit margins, growth, and trends. Please ensure you're filling this form if you're an actual business owner and not for demonstration or testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and the name of your business.
2. Select the reporting period you're submitting data for (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Year-to-Date).
3. Enter your total revenue for the selected period.
4. Enter your direct costs for the goods sold (optional).
5. Calculate your gross profit by subtracting the cost of goods sold from total revenue.
6. Enter your total operating expenses (salaries, rent, utilities, etc.).
7. Calculate your net profit by subtracting operating expenses from gross profit.
8. Enter your expected profit margin percentage (optional).
9. Evaluate how this period's profit compares to the previous period (Significantly Better, Better, Same, Worse, or Significantly Worse).
10. Describe the revenue growth trend (Strong Growth, Steady Growth, Flat, Declining, or Highly Volatile).
11. List your main revenue sources (products or services).
12. Identify areas for profit improvement (optional).
13. Identify areas for cost reduction (optional).
14. If profits vary significantly by season (optional).
15. Select the expected profit level for the upcoming period (Very Positive, Positive, Neutral, Negative, or Very Negative).
16. Set a profit goal for the next 12 months (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Owner Name (1, business_owner_name)** (Text, Required): Your full name as the owner of the business.
* **Business Name (2, business_name)** (Text, Required): The name of your business.
* **Survey Date (3, survey_date)** (Date, Required): The date this survey is being completed.
* **Reporting Period (4, reporting_period)** (Select One, Required): The period you are reporting on (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or Year-to-Date).
* **Total Revenue (5, total_revenue)** (Number, Required): The total sales or revenue for the period.
* **Cost of Goods Sold (6, cost_of_goods_sold)** (Number, Optional): Direct costs of products sold.
* **Gross Profit (7, gross_profit)** (Number, Optional): Revenue minus cost of goods sold.
* **Total Operating Expenses (8, operating_expenses)** (Number, Required): Salaries, rent, utilities, etc.
* **Net Profit (9, net_profit)** (Number, Required): Final profit after all expenses.
* **Profit Margin Percentage (10, profit_margin_percentage)** (Number, Optional): Net profit divided by revenue.
* **Comparison to Previous Period (11, comparison_previous_period)** (Select One, Required): How this period's profit compares to the previous period.
* **Revenue Growth Trends (12, revenue_growth_trends)** (Select One, Required): What is the growth trajectory.
* **Main Revenue Sources (13, main_revenue_sources)** (Text, Required): What products or services generate most revenue.
* **Profit Improvement Opportunities (14, profit_improvement_opportunities)** (Text, Optional): Where you can increase profits.
* **Cost Reduction Opportunities (15, cost_reduction_opportunities)** (Text, Optional): Where you can cut costs.
* **Seasonal Variations (16, seasonal_variations)** (Select One, Optional): Do profits vary by season.
* **Profitability Outlook for Next Period (17, profitability_outlook)** (Select One, Required): Expected profit for upcoming period.
* **Profit Goals for Next 12 Months (18, profit_goals)** (Number, Optional): What profit level you target for the next 12 months.
