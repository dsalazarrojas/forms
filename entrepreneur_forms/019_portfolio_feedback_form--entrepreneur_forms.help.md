# Portfolio Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with a portfolio of projects or services. This feedback is crucial in helping us improve the quality and relevance of our portfolios.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Select One Question" dropdown menu and select either "Yes" or "No" to confirm that the portfolio in question exists and meets your requirements.
2. Enter your thoughts and comments about the portfolio in the "Feedback" text box.
3. Optionally, provide your email address if you wish to be contacted regarding this feedback.
4. Review and submit the form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Portfolio**: A text field where you can provide a brief description or title of the portfolio you are providing feedback on (required).
* **Select One Question**: A dropdown menu where you can confirm that the portfolio in question exists and meets your requirements (required).
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that the portfolio exists and meets your requirements.
	+ Select "No" to indicate that the portfolio does not exist or does not meet your requirements.
* **Feedback**: A text field where you can provide detailed and specific comments or suggestions about the portfolio (optional).
* **Email Address** (optional): A field where you can provide your email address if you wish to be contacted regarding this feedback.
* **Submit**: A button to submit your feedback form (required).
