# Bike Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bike Booking Form is a tool used to book a bike for a specific date and time. It collects personal information, bike preferences, and booking details to ensure a smooth and efficient experience for both the user and the bike operator.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information, including your full name and email address.
2.  Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3.  Select the type of bike you'd like to book (e.g., Road, Mountain, Hybrid, or Electric).
4.  Choose the date and time you'd like to pick up the bike.
5.  Specify the rental duration in hours.
6.  Enter the pickup location address or venue.
7.  If you have any additional requests or special notes, please include them in the "Additional requests or notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Full name**: (Full name, text, required)
Your full name is your name as it appears on official documents or identification.

### 2. **Email address**: (Email address, email, required)
Enter the email address to which you'll receive booking confirmation.

### 3. **Contact phone**: (Contact phone, text, required)
Provide your phone number for contact purposes, including any updates or changes to your booking.

### 4. **Bike type**: (Bike type, select_one, required)
Choose the type of bike you'd like to book:
	*   Road
	*   Mountain
	*   Hybrid
	*   Electric

### 5. **Booking date**: (Booking date, date, required)
Select the date you'd like to pick up the bike.

### 6. **Pickup time**: (Pickup time, time, required)
Choose the time you'd like to pick up the bike.

### 7. **Rental duration hours**: (Rental duration hours, number, required)
Specify the number of hours you'd like to rent the bike.

### 8. **Pickup location**: (Pickup location, text, required)
Enter the address or venue where you'd like to be picked up.

### 9. **Additional requests or notes**: (Additional requests or notes, text, optional)
If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, please include them here.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your booking. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
