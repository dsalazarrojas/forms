# Square Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Product Order Form is a multi-page form used to collect information about a product order from a customer. The form is designed to gather essential details such as product line, product description, customer name, order date and time, and order amount. The form also includes an option for payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the 'Product Line' field and select "Yes" if the product line is relevant to this order, or "No" if it is not.
2. Enter the product description in the 'Product Description' field. This is a required field.
3. Enter the customer's name in the 'Customer Name' field. This is a required field.
4. For orders placed before the current date, you can enter a specific date in the 'Order Date' field. Note that this field is not required, as orders without a specified date will be considered as having taken place on the current date.
5. If your order has a specific time, you can enter it in the 'Order Time' field.
6. Enter the total amount of the order in the 'Order Amount' field. This is a required field.
7. Finally, select your preferred payment method from the 'Payment Method' dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Line** (Yes/No, select_multiple):
	+ Select "Yes" if this product line is relevant to this order, or "No" if it is not.
* **Product Description** (text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of the product being ordered.
* **Customer Name** (text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the customer placing the order.
* **Order Date** (date, optional):
	+ Enter the date on which the order was placed (or leave blank for current date).
* **Order Time** (time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the order (if applicable).
* **Order Amount** (number, required):
	+ Enter the total amount of the order. This includes any applicable taxes or fees.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, optional):
	+ Select your preferred payment method: Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.

Note: This form is designed to be used in conjunction with other Square tools, such as the Square Point of Sale system, to facilitate seamless product ordering and payment processing.
