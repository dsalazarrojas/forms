# Distributor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distributor Application Form is designed to collect information from potential distributors who are interested in partnering with the company. This form helps the company's team to assess the qualifications, products, and experience of the applicant to determine if they are a good fit for their distributor network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your valid Email Address.
3. Provide your physical Address.
4. Enter your Phone and Fax numbers.
5. Enter your Zip code.
6. Select the Country, State, and Region where you are based.
7. Choose the type of distributor you are applying to be (Retail, Wholesale, or Other).
8. List the products you are interested in distributing.
9. Mention your relevant work experience.
10. If applicable, enter the start and end dates of your previous experience.
11. Provide any additional Notes about your application.
12. Fill in the name of the tool assigned to you (if any).
13. Enter the name of the user who assigned you the tool (if any).
14. Enter the name of the person who submitted this application.
15. Finally, provide the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your company or business.
* **Address**: Provide your physical address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Fax**: Enter your fax number (if available).
* **Zip**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Country**: Select the country where you are based.
* **State**: Select the state where you are based.
* **Region**: Select the region where you are based.
* **Distributor Type**: Choose the type of distributor you are applying to be (Retail, Wholesale, or Other).
* **Products**: List the products you are interested in distributing.
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience.
* **Start Date**: If applicable, enter the start date of your previous experience.
* **End Date**: If applicable, enter the end date of your previous experience.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about your application.
* **Assigned Tool**: If a tool has been assigned to you, enter its name.
* **Assigned User**: If a user has assigned you a tool, enter their name.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted this application.
* **Submit Date**: This is a system-generated field and should not be edited.
* **Created By**: This is a system-generated field and should not be edited.
* **Updated Date**: This is a system-generated field and should not be edited.
