# Billionaire Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Billionaire Quiz is a form designed to gather information about your investment style, risk tolerance, and philanthropic focus areas. It will help the quiz administrator understand your preferences and values.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by scrolling through the form and review each section carefully.
2. Enter your name in the "Full name" field (participant_name).
3. If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email address" field (participant_email). This will help us deliver the results of the quiz to your email.
4. In the "Preferred investment style" field (preferred_investment_style), select the option that best matches your approach to investments.
5. In the "Risk tolerance" field (risk_tolerance), select the option that best describes your risk-taking behavior.
6. For the "Favorite industry to invest in" field (favorite_industry), pick one industry that you are interested in investing in.
7. If you have any causes that you support, select them in the "Philanthropic focus areas" field (philanthropic_focus).
8. In the "Morning routine preference" field (morning_routine), choose the statement that best fits your morning routine.
9. If you'd like to rate your confidence in achieving big goals, enter a number between 1 and 10 in the "Personal confidence score" field (personal_confidence_score).
10. If you have any comments or thoughts about the quiz, enter them in the "Comments about the quiz" field (quiz_comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email address** (participant_email, email, optional): Enter your email address for results delivery.
* **Preferred investment style** (preferred_investment_style, select_one, required): Choose the style that best matches your approach to investments.
	+ Long term value investing: Invest for the long term, focusing on steady returns.
	+ Growth and scaling: Invest for rapid growth and expansion.
	+ Private equity and buyouts: Invest in private equity and buyout deals.
	+ Venture capital and startups: Invest in startups and early-stage companies.
* **Risk tolerance** (risk_tolerance, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your risk-taking behavior.
	+ Low: Take few risks and prefer steady returns.
	+ Moderate: Take calculated risks and balance returns.
	+ High: Take high risks and aim for big returns.
	+ Aggressive: Take high risks and focus on quick returns.
* **Favorite industry to invest in** (favorite_industry, select_one, optional): Pick one industry that you are interested in investing in.
	+ Technology: Invest in tech companies and industries.
	+ Real estate: Invest in real estate development and property.
	+ Healthcare: Invest in healthcare and medical companies.
	+ Consumer goods: Invest in consumer goods and retail companies.
	+ Energy: Invest in energy and utilities companies.
* **Philanthropic focus areas** (philanthropic_focus, select_multiple, optional): Select any causes you support.
	+ Education: Support education and knowledge development.
	+ Health care: Support health care and medical research.
	+ Environment: Support environmental conservation and sustainability.
	+ Economic development: Support economic development and poverty reduction.
	+ Arts and culture: Support arts and culture development.
* **Morning routine preference** (morning_routine, select_one, optional): Choose the statement that best fits your morning routine.
	+ Early start with planning and review: Start the day early, planning and reviewing your schedule.
	+ Focus on exercise and wellbeing: Start the day with exercise and self-care.
	+ Networking and meetings: Start the day with networking and meeting with people.
	+ Creative deep work: Start the day with creative work and focus.
* **Personal confidence score** (personal_confidence_score, number, optional): Rate your confidence in achieving big goals on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Comments about the quiz** (quiz_comments, text, optional): Enter any comments or thoughts about the quiz.
