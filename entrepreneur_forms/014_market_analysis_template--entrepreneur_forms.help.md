# Market Analysis Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to assist in the process of analyzing a market by organizing key information about the market, company, and products/services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the provided fields with the relevant information about the market you are analyzing. You can select one option from the dropdown menus or type your own text in the text fields. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Market Analysis Template** (`market_analysis_template`, text): This field is not required, but feel free to add any relevant notes or comments about your market analysis.
- **Category** (`select_one`, optional): Choose one of the available options to describe the category of your market analysis.
- **Date** (`select_one`, optional): Select the date that is relevant to your market analysis.
- **Time** (`select_one`, optional): Select the time that is relevant to your market analysis.
- **Market Analysis Template 2** (`Market Analysis Template 2`, text): This field is not required, and you can use it to add any additional information or notes.
- **Description** (`description`, text): Provide a brief description of your market analysis, including any relevant details or observations.
- **Company Marketplace** (`company_marketplace`, text): Use this field to add any information about the company or marketplace related to your analysis.
- **Market Analysis Template 2 2** (`Market Analysis Template 2 2`, text): This field is not required, and you can use it to add any additional details or notes.
- **Products/Services** (`products_services`, text): Describe the products or services that are relevant to your market analysis.
- **Analysis** (`analysis`, text): Write a brief summary of your market analysis, including any key findings or conclusions.
