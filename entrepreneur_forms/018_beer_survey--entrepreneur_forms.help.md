# Beer Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beer Survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather insights on beer preferences, consumption habits, and attitudes towards beer. The goal is to understand your relationship with beer, including your favorite types, breweries, and purchasing behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- If a question is not relevant, you can skip it. However, be aware that it may impact the survey's outcome.
- You may leave fields blank if you are unsure or do not wish to answer a particular question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name (Optional - you may remain anonymous).
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes (For follow-up if needed).
* **Age** (3): Enter your age (Must be 21 or older).
* **Age Verification** (4): Confirm you are 21 years or older.
* **Beer Consumption Frequency** (5): Select how often you drink beer.
* **Preferred Beer Types** (6): Select all the types of beer you prefer (Select all that apply).
* **Favorite Brewery** (7): Enter your favorite brewery (Name your favorite brewery).
* **Typical Purchase Location** (8): Select where you usually buy beer.
* **Typical Price Range** (9): Select the price range for a 6-pack of beer.
* **Craft vs Domestic Preference** (10): Select your preference for craft or domestic beer.
* **Brewery Visit Frequency** (11): Select how often you visit breweries.
* **Beer Event Attendance** (12): Select if you attend beer festivals or tastings.
* **Packaging Preference** (13): Select your preferred packaging type.
* **Flavor Preferences** (14): Select flavors you enjoy (Select all that apply).
* **Brand Loyalty** (15): Select your level of loyalty to specific brands.
* **Social Media Following** (16): Select if you follow breweries on social media.
* **Beer Knowledge** (17): Select your level of knowledge about beer.
* **Homebrewing** (18): Select if you brew beer at home.
* **Health Consciousness** (19): Select if you consider health factors when choosing beer.
* **Sustainability Importance** (20): Select the importance of sustainability to you.
* **Favorite Beer Memory** (21): Share a memorable beer experience (Optional).
* **Beer Recommendations** (22): Recommend beers to others (Optional).
* **Additional Comments** (23): Any other feedback about your beer preferences (Optional).
