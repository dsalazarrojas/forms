# Virtual Assistant Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Virtual Assistant Survey is designed to gather information about your business and preferences for virtual assistance services. This form will help us understand your needs and provide you with tailored support to ensure you receive the best service possible.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your official business documents or identification.
2. Enter your client's name as it appears on their official documents or identification.
3. Briefly describe your business in a few sentences.
4. Select your business type by choosing one or multiple options from the list.
5. Select your business location from the list.
6. Choose your preferred contact method.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Select your preferred schedule type by choosing one or multiple options from the list.
10. Select your schedule type options.
11. Enter your schedule preferences (date and time).
12. Enter your availability.
13. Select your business hours.
14. Select the days you work.
15. Select your work hours.
16. Select your availability status.
17. Select your work status.
18. Select your preferred schedule type.
19. Enter any additional schedule details.
20. Enter any notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (virtual_assistant_survey, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official business documents or identification.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required): Enter the name of the client as it appears on their official documents or identification.
* **Business Description** (business_description, text, required): Briefly describe your business in a few sentences.
* **Business Type** (business_type, select_multiple, required): Select your business type by choosing one or multiple options from the list.
* **Business Type Options** (business_type_options, text, required): Enter your business type options selected in the previous field.
* **Business Location** (business_location, select_one, required): Select your business location from the list.
* **Contact Method** (contact_method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred contact method.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Schedule Time** (schedule_time, time, required): Select your preferred schedule time.
* **Schedule Date** (schedule_date, date, required): Select your preferred schedule date.
* **Availability** (availability, time, required): Enter your availability.
* **Business Hours** (business_hours, time, required): Select your business hours.
* **Business Days** (business_days, select_multiple, required): Select the days you work.
* **Work Hours** (work_hours, select_multiple, required): Select your work hours.
* **Availability Status** (availability_status, select_one, required): Select your availability status.
* **Work Status** (work_status, select_one, required): Select your work status.
* **Schedule Type** (schedule_type, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred schedule type.
* **Schedule Type Options** (schedule_type_options, text, required): Enter your schedule type options selected in the previous field.
* **Schedule** (schedule, text, required): Enter any additional schedule details.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Enter any notes or comments.

Note: All fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields. Please fill in all required fields to ensure we receive the necessary information to provide you with the best service possible.
