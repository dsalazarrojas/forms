# Product Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to register their products with us. It will help to improve product management and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your product ID in the "Product ID" field.
6. Enter the date you purchased the product in the "Purchase Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or document.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or document.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that is associated with your account.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for any updates or promotions.
* **Product ID** (text, optional): Enter the product ID number if you know it, or leave it blank if you don't have it.
* **Purchase Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the product.
