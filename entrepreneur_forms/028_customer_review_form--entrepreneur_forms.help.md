# Customer Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our company. The information collected will help us understand what customers think of our products or services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Rate your experience with our service or product in the "customer_rating" field. You can choose a number rating.
3. Type your comment about your experience in the "customer_comment" field.
4. If you are representing a company, enter the company name in the "company_name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will allow us to contact you for follow-up and communication purposes.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Select the date of your review in the "review_date" field.
8. Select the time of your review in the "review_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
* **Rate Us** (`customer_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience with our service or product. 
* **Your Comment** (`customer_comment`, text, required: false): Type your comment about your experience.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up and communication purposes.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your review.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of your review.
