# Retail Brand Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail brand accelerator registration form is designed to collect information from retail businesses interested in participating in the retail accelerator program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the application form with accurate and truthful information about your retail business.
2. Answer the questions on the different pages, providing details about your business.
3. Ensure that you have the authority to complete this form on behalf of the business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Application Form** (`1`, text, optional): This is the initial information about your business. Please provide a brief introduction to your business.
* **Brand Details** (`2`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your brand.
* **Business Stage** (`3`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your business is currently participating in a business accelerator program.
* **Goals** (`4`, text, optional): State your goals for your business.
* **Email** (`5`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used as a contact point for us.
* **Phone** (`6`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Business Type** (`7`, text, optional): Describe your business type.
* **Business Model** (`8`, text, optional): Describe your business model.
* **Retail Programs** (`9`, text, optional): List the retail programs you are currently participating in.
* **Retail Location** (`10`, text, optional): Provide details about your retail location.
* **Retail Type** (`11`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are currently participating in retail programs.
* **Retail Stage** (`12`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are currently in a retail stage.
* **Retail Goals** (`13`, text, optional): State your retail goals.
* **Retail Program Stage** (`14`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are currently participating in a retail program.
* **Retail Programs Participated** (`15`, select_multiple, optional): List the retail programs you have participated in.
* **Retail Partners** (`16`, text, optional): List your retail partners.
* **Retail Programs Participated Details** (`17`, text, optional): Provide details about the retail programs you have participated in.
* **Retail Programs Participated Duration** (`18`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your participation in retail programs.
* **Retail Partners Participated** (`19`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are currently participating in retail partners.
* **Retail Partners Participated Details** (`20`, text, optional): Provide details about your retail partners.
* **Retail Partners Participated Duration** (`21`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your participation in retail partners.
* **Retail Accelerators Participated** (`22`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are currently participating in retail accelerators.
* **Retail Accelerators Participated Details** (`23`, text, optional): Provide details about the retail accelerators you have participated in.
* **Retail Accelerators Participated Duration** (`24`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your participation in retail accelerators.
* **Retail Programs Funds** (`25`, number, optional): Enter the funds received from retail programs.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but providing accurate and truthful information is essential for a thorough evaluation of your business. If you are unsure about any question, please contact us for clarification.
