# Online Dating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback and experiences about online dating. Your responses will help us understand how to better serve you and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
- If you have an answer that is not listed, please use the "Other" option and describe your answer in the "Other reasons" field.
- You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
- Take as much time as you need to complete the survey. It should take about 5-10 minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully to understand what the survey is about.
* **What type of user are you?**: Please select the option that best describes you. If you are a user of both dating apps and dating sites, select both options.
* **How often do you use dating apps?**: Choose how often you usually use dating apps.
* **Why do you use dating apps?**: Select all the reasons that apply to you.
* **Age**: Please enter your age.
* **Income**: Please enter your annual income.
* **Location**: Select your location from the list. If you are not sure, select "Other" and describe your location.
* **Which device do you use for dating apps?**: Select all the devices you use for dating apps.
* **What time of day do you usually use dating apps?**: Choose when you usually use dating apps.
* **How long do you usually use dating apps for?**: Select how long you usually spend on dating apps.
* **Why do you not use dating apps?**: Select all the reasons that apply to you.
* **Other reasons (please specify)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous question, describe your reason here.
* **How would you rate dating apps?**: Rate how satisfied you are with dating apps.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Contact details**: If you want to be contacted for further communication, please enter your email address.
* **How long did you take to fill this survey?**: Please enter how long it took you to complete the survey.
* **How satisfied are you with this survey?**: Rate how satisfied you are with the survey.
