# Business Diagnosis Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Diagnosis Questionnaire is a tool designed to help businesses identify their current state, strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. This form will guide you through questions about your business's financials, operations, and overall health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible. This will help you understand your business's current situation and provide a foundation for future improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, Text, required): Enter your full name as the business owner.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, Email, required): Provide your contact email address for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, Text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, Text, required): Enter your business's name or name as it appears on public records.
* **Years in Operation** (`years_operating`, Number, required): Provide the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, Select One, required): Select the type of business structure you have (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Other).
* **Current Annual Revenue** (`current_revenue`, Number, optional): Enter your approximate current annual revenue.
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, Number, required): Provide the total number of employees in your business.
* **Biggest Problem Facing Business** (`biggest_problem`, Text, required): Describe your main issue or challenge in the business.
* **Problem Areas** (`problem_areas`, Select Multiple, required): Select all the areas that apply to your current problems (e.g., Cash Flow, Sales and Revenue, Customer Retention, etc.).
* **How Long Has This Been an Issue** (`problem_duration`, Select One, required): Choose the duration of your current problem (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1 - 3 months, etc.).
* **Impact of Problems** (`impact_assessment`, Select One, required): Assess the severity of your current problems (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Significant, etc.).
* **Cash Flow Status** (`cash_flow_status`, Select One, required): Describe your current cash flow situation (e.g., Excellent, Good, Adequate, etc.).
* **Profitability Status** (`profitability`, Select One, required): Determine if your business is profitable (e.g., Highly Profitable, Profitable, Breaking Even, etc.).
* **Growth Trend** (`growth_trend`, Select One, required): Assess your business's growth trend (e.g., Strong Growth, Moderate Growth, Flat, etc.).
* **Business Strengths** (`strength_areas`, Select Multiple, optional): List your business's areas of strength (e.g., Product Quality, Customer Service, etc.).
* **Business Weaknesses** (`weakness_areas`, Select Multiple, optional): Identify areas for improvement in your business (e.g., Limited Marketing, Poor Systems, etc.).
* **Solutions Considered** (`solutions_considered`, Text, optional): Describe any solutions or strategies you've attempted to address your problems.
* **Business Health Assessment** (`business_health`, Note, optional): This field is reserved for internal use and will not be displayed to you.
