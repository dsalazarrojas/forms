# Chief Executive Officer (CEO) Job Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help applicants apply for the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) position at our company. It is intended to gather information about the applicant's background, skills, and qualifications for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Use the provided options for select fields as a guide to answer the question accurately.
3. For text fields, provide detailed answers that demonstrate your qualifications and experiences.
4. For numerical fields (e.g., years in current/recent role, years of executive leadership experience), provide accurate numbers without rounding up or exaggerating.
5. If you have no experience in a particular field, please indicate this in your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`applicant_first_name`, `text`, `*`): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last name** (`applicant_last_name`, `text`, `*`): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, `*`): Enter your email address to contact you for further questions or information.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `*`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Current or most recent position** (`current_position`, `text`, `*`): Describe your current or most recent work position, highlighting your role and responsibilities.
* **Current or most recent company** (`current_company`, `text`, `*`): Enter the name of the company where you work or most recently worked.
* **Years in current/recent role** (`years_in_role`, `number`, `*`): Enter the number of years you have been in your current or most recent role.
* **Years of executive leadership experience** (`years_executive_experience`, `number`, `*`): Enter the total number of years you have had executive leadership experience.
* **Previous CEO or similar experience** (`previous_ceo_experience`, `select_one`, `*`): If you have had executive experience, select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Largest company size managed** (`company_size_experience`, `select_one`, `*`): Select the number of employees in the company where you have had the largest management experience.
* **Largest annual revenue managed** (`revenue_managed`, `text`, ``): Enter the largest annual revenue you have managed in your previous roles.
* **Highest education level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, `*`): Select the highest education level you have achieved.
* **Primary field of study** (`field_of_study`, `text`, ``): Enter the field of study that is most relevant to the position.
* **Industries with significant experience** (`industry_expertise`, `select_multiple`, `*`): Select all industries where you have had significant experience.
* **Key business achievements and accomplishments** (`key_achievements`, `text`, `*`): Describe your key business achievements and accomplishments.
* **Experience driving revenue growth** (`revenue_growth_success`, `text`, `*`): Describe your experience driving revenue growth in previous roles.
* **Strategic planning and implementation skills** (`strategic_planning`, `select_one`, `*`): Rate your strategic planning and implementation skills from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Team building and leadership abilities** (`team_building`, `select_one`, `*`): Rate your team building and leadership abilities from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Financial management expertise** (`financial_management`, `select_one`, `*`): Rate your financial management expertise from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Board of Directors experience** (`board_experience`, `select_one`, `*`): If you have experience on a board, select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Your vision for the company's future** (`vision_statement`, `text`, `*`): Describe your vision for the company's future.
* **Salary expectations** (`salary_expectations`, `text`, ``): Enter your salary expectations.
* **References or LinkedIn profile** (`references`, `text`, ``): Provide references or a link to your LinkedIn profile for further evaluation.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers to provide an accurate representation of your qualifications.
* Use specific examples and achievements to demonstrate your skills and experiences.
* Proofread your answers for errors and completeness before submitting.
