# Business Model Innovation Hub Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Model Innovation Hub is a platform designed to support entrepreneurs in developing and refining their business models. This guide will walk you through the registration process and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the registration form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your primary email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your location or city in the "Location/City" field.
5. Enter the name of your business or project in the "Business/Project Name" field.
6. Choose the current stage of your business from the "Business Stage" dropdown menu.
7. Select the industry you are focused on from the "Industry Focus" dropdown menu.
8. Describe the problem you are solving in the "Problem Statement" field.
9. Identify your target market or customer base in the "Target Market" field.
10. Enter the current size of your team in the "Current Team Size" field.
11. Indicate your current funding status from the "Current Funding Status" dropdown menu.
12. Select the goals you hope to achieve through the Innovation Hub program from the "Goals for Innovation Hub Program" multi-select menu.
13. Indicate your experience as an entrepreneur from the "Entrepreneurial Experience" dropdown menu.
14. Choose your key skill areas from the "Key Skill Areas" multi-select menu.
15. Describe any areas where you need guidance or mentorship from the "Mentorship Interests" field.
16. Identify the types of connections you are seeking from the "Networking Interests" multi-select menu.
17. Select your preferred cohort size from the "Preferred Cohort Size" dropdown menu.
18. Indicate your commitment level to the program, selecting either "Part-time", "Full-time", or "Flexible".
19. Confirm that you agree to receive program communications by selecting "True" from the "I agree to program communications" dropdown menu.
20. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your application in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Location/City** (`participant_location`, `text`, required): Indicate your location or city where your business is based.
* **Business/Project Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business or project.
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current stage of your business (Idea Stage, MVP/Prototype, Early Traction, Growth Stage, Established).
* **Industry Focus** (`industry_focus`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry you are focused on (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Cleantech, EdTech, Agritech, Other).
* **Problem Statement** (`problem_statement`, `text`, required): Describe the problem you are solving or the challenge you are trying to address.
* **Target Market** (`target_market`, `text`, required): Identify your target market or customer base.
* **Current Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required): Enter the current size of your team.
* **Current Funding Status** (`funding_status`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your current funding status (Bootstrapped, Seeking Seed Funding, Seed Funded, Series A, Series B+, Self-funded).
* **Goals for Innovation Hub Program** (`program_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the goals you hope to achieve through the program (Develop Business Model, Find Co-founders, Secure Funding, Market Validation, Product Development, Scaling Strategy, Networking).
* **Entrepreneurial Experience** (`previous_experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your experience as an entrepreneur (First-time Entrepreneur, Serial Entrepreneur, Corporate Transition, Academic Spinoff).
* **Key Skill Areas** (`key_skill_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your key areas of expertise (Product Development, Sales and Marketing, Technology/Engineering, Finance and Operations, Strategic Planning, Industry Domain).
* **Mentorship Interests** (`mentorship_interests`, `text`, optional): Describe any areas where you need guidance or mentorship.
* **Networking Interests** (`network_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Identify the types of connections you are seeking (Investors, Potential Customers, Technical Partners, Service Providers, Other Entrepreneurs, Advisors/Mentors).
* **Preferred Cohort Size** (`preferred_cohort_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred cohort size (Small (5-10), Medium (10-20), Large (20+), No Preference).
* **Commitment Level** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your commitment level to the program (Part-time, Full-time, Flexible).
* **I agree to program communications** (`consent_communication`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to receive program communications (True, False).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_interests`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
