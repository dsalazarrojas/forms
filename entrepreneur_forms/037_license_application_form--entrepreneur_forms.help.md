# License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a license application process. It helps businesses or individuals to apply for a license or permit by providing a structured format to gather essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each field and determine the required information you need to provide.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary details. Be as accurate and thorough as possible.
3. Ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **application-form**: This is the main section of the form, where you'll provide an overview of your application.
* **applicant-info**: Enter your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **business-info**: Provide information about your business, such as its name, address, and other relevant details.
* **license-info**: Enter information about the license you're applying for, including its type and status.
* **signature-block**: A signature block where you'll sign your application.
* **address**: Enter your address or your business address.
* **address2**: Enter any additional address information.
* **zip-code**: Enter the zip code associated with your address or business address.
* **phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **business-license-info**: Enter information about your business license, including its number and status.
* **business-license-number**: Enter the exact number of your business license.
* **business-license-expiration-date**: Enter the date your business license expires.
* **business-license-issue-date**: Enter the date your business license was issued.
* **business-license-status**: Select the status of your business license.
* **business-license-status-2**: Select any other status of your business license.
* **signature**: A signature block where you'll sign your application.
* **signature-block-2**: Another signature block.
* **date**: Enter the date you're applying for the license.
* **time**: Enter the time you're applying for the license.
* **license-type**: Select the type of license you're applying for.
* **license-type-2**: Select any other type of license you're applying for.
* **license-type-3**: Select any other type of license you're applying for.
* **signature-block-3**: A third signature block.
* **submit**: Click this button to submit your application.

Note: If you're unsure about any field or requirement, please review the provided instructions or contact our support team for clarification.
