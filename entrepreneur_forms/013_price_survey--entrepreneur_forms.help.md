# Price Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about products' prices. As a user, you will be asked to provide details about the product, its price, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the product information in the "Product" field.
2. Enter the price of the product in the "Price" field, making sure to select the correct currency using the dropdown menu.
3. If applicable, provide tax information in the "Tax" field.
4. If applicable, add any discount information in the "Discount" field.
5. Add any additional comments about the product in the "Comments" field.
6. Specify whether the product is categorized or subcategorized using the "Category" and "Subcategory" fields. Note that these fields are not required, and you can choose "No" if the product does not fit into any category or subcategory.
7. Finally, indicate how many business hours are needed to complete the product-related tasks using the "Business Hours" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product
* **Product** (text, required/optional): Enter the name and description of the product you are providing price information for.

### Price
* **Price** (number, required/optional): Enter the price of the product. Make sure to select the correct currency using the dropdown menu.

### Currency
* **Currency** (select_one, required/optional): Select the currency in which the price is given. Options are USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, INR, and BGN.

### Tax
* **Tax** (number, required/optional): Enter any applicable tax information. Note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if no tax is applicable.

### Discount
* **Discount** (text, required/optional): Add any discount information related to the product. This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if no discount is applicable.

### Comments
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the product. This field is not required, but it's a good place to provide any extra information that might be relevant.

### Category
* **Category** (select_multiple, required/optional): Specify whether the product is categorized or not. Options are "Yes" and "No". Note that this field is not required, and you can choose "No" if the product does not fit into any category.

### Subcategory
* **Subcategory** (select_multiple, required/optional): Similar to the "Category" field, specify whether the product is subcategorized or not. Options are "Yes" and "No". Note that this field is not required, and you can choose "No" if the product does not fit into any subcategory.

### Business Hours
* **Business Hours** (time, required/optional): Indicate how many business hours are needed to complete the product-related tasks. Note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if no business hours are needed.
