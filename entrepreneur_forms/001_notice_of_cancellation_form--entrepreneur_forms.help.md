# Notice Of Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Notice Of Cancellation Form is designed to collect information about business cancellations. It is used to maintain records of cancellations and should only be used for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the "Cancelled Date" field and enter the date of the cancellation in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
2. In the "Reason For Cancellation" field, enter a brief description of the reason for the cancellation.
3. If applicable, provide any relevant agreements between parties in the "Party A Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cancelled Date** (`cancelled_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the cancellation occurred. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Reason For Cancellation** (`cancelled_reason`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the reason for the cancellation. This can be a general description or a specific detail about the reason for the cancellation.
* **Party A Agreement** (`party_a_agreement`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant agreements or details about the cancellation.
