# Business Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the impact of a business or program on various areas such as Economic, Social, Environmental, and Educational.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the organization or business being assessed in the Organization name field.
2. Specify your role or relationship with the organization in the Respondent role field.
3. Choose the area of impact that best describes the effects of the organization (Economic, Social, Environmental, or Educational).
4. Provide a detailed description of the observable outcomes or changes resulting from the organization's activities.
5. Estimate the number of people affected by the initiative (if applicable).
6. Choose the methods used to measure the impact (Select all that apply: Surveys, Administrative data, Interviews, Observation).
7. Select the time horizon of the impact (Short term, Medium term, Long term).
8. (Optional) Provide a summary of the key takeaways from the initiative in the Lessons learned field.
9. Enter your contact email address for follow-up questions or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name**: Enter the name of the business or program being assessed. This field is required.
* **Respondent role**: Specify your role or relationship with the organization. This field is required.
* **Impact area**: Choose the area that best describes the effects of the organization (Economic, Social, Environmental, or Educational). This field is required.
* **Impact description**: Describe the observable outcomes or changes resulting from the organization's activities. This field is required.
* **Number of beneficiaries**: If applicable, estimate the number of people affected by the initiative. (Not required)
* **Measurement methods**: Choose the methods used to measure the impact (Select all that apply: Surveys, Administrative data, Interviews, Observation). (Not required)
* **Time horizon of impact**: Select the time horizon of the impact (Short term, Medium term, Long term). This field is required.
* **Lessons learned**: (Optional) Provide a summary of the key takeaways from the initiative. (Not required)
* **Follow up contact**: Enter your contact email address for follow-up questions or additional information. (Not required)

## Tips

* If the initiative is a long-term project, select Long term for the Time horizon of impact.
* If the initiative affects a large number of people, estimate the number of beneficiaries.
* Use the Measurement methods field to indicate if any secondary data sources were used.
