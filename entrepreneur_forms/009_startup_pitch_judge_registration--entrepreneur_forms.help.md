# Startup Pitch Judge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for startup pitches to be judged by registered judges. By providing the following information, you'll be able to participate in a judging round and receive feedback on your startup pitch.

## How To Complete This Form
Fill out the form in the following order:

1. **User Information**: Enter your name and other basic details.
2. **Judge Details**: Provide any additional details about yourself as a judge.
3. **Expertise**: Select the areas where you have expertise. This will help us match you with the right startups to review.
4. **Availability**: Enter your availability date and time.
5. **Availability Date**: Choose a date for which you're available to review pitches.
6. **Availability Time**: Choose a time slot for which you're available to review pitches.
7. **Availability Note**: If there are any specific notes you'd like to add to your availability.
8. **Email**: Enter your work email address.
9. **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
10. **Note**: Provide any additional notes you have.
11. **Assigned Tool**: Select if this is a tool that is assigned to you.

**Tips:**

* If you're unsure about which areas of expertise to choose, refer to the platform's guidelines or ask a colleague for guidance.
* Make sure to check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* You can provide any additional notes if you'd like to share more information about yourself as a judge.
* Only select the tool that's assigned to you, or if none is assigned, select 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required): Enter your basic details such as name, etc.
* **Judge Details** (`Judge Details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about yourself as a judge.
* **Expertise** (`Expertise`, `select_multiple`, required): Select areas of expertise where you have experience and interest in reviewing. This will help match you with the right startups.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `text`, required): Enter your availability date.
* **Availability Date** (`Availability Date`, `date`, required): Choose a specific date for which you're available to review pitches.
* **Availability Time** (`Availability Time`, `time`, required): Choose a specific time slot for which you're available to review pitches.
* **Availability Note** (`Availability Note`, `text`, required): Add any specific notes about your availability.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required): Provide any additional notes about yourself.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, required): Only select the tool that's assigned to you, or if none is assigned, select 'No'.
