# Vendor Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from vendors to gauge their satisfaction and preferences. It helps our organization understand what we're doing well and where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the vendor that this survey is about (page 2).
2. Choose how you rated your overall satisfaction with the vendor (page 3).
3. Enter your email address (page 6).
4. Enter your phone number (page 7).
5. Rate how likely you are to continue working with the vendor (page 9).
6. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or feedback (page 5).
7. (Optional) Select the reason you wouldn't recommend the vendor (page 10).
