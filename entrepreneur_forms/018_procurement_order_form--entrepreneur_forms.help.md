# procurement_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The procurement_order_form is used to collect information from customers or clients to place an order for a procurement or purchase.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Start by filling out the **procurement_order_form_page1** (procurement_order) field, which is a text field where you can type in the details of your order.
2. Next, move on to the **item_details** (procurement_order_form_page2) field, which is a multiple-select field where you can choose one or more items from the options provided.
3. Then, select the **delivery_details** (procurement_order_form_page3) field, which is a select-one field where you can choose the delivery method.
4. After that, fill out the **delivery_date** (procurement_order_form_page4) field, which is a date field where you can enter the date for delivery.
5. You can also add any additional comments or notes in the **comments** (procurement_order_form_page6) field.
6. Finally, click the **submit** (procurement_order_form_page7) button to submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **procurement_order_form_page1** (procurement_order): Enter the title of your order.
* **item_details** (procurement_order_form_page2): Select one or more items from the provided options.
* **delivery_details** (procurement_order_form_page3): Choose the delivery method for your order.
* **delivery_date** (procurement_order_form_page4): Enter the date for delivery.
* **comments** (procurement_order_form_page6): Add any additional comments or notes about your order.
* **submit** (procurement_order_form_page7): Submit your order.
* **comments_2** (procurement_order_form_page8): This is an additional comments field for any further notes or clarification.
