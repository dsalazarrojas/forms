# eco-franchise-application-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from entrepreneurs who wish to register their new eco-franchise business. It is a step-by-step guide to help you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your main information about the business, including its title.
2.  Describe your business's sustainability practices.
3.  If you have a new location for your business, please provide details about it.
4.  If you are applying on behalf of a franchise partner, please provide the necessary information.
5.  Share your experience relevant to the business.
6.  Enter your contact information, including phone and email.
7.  Optionally, you can upload any supporting documents.
8.  Finally, submit your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`main_information`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your eco-franchise business.
* **Partner Sustainability** (`partner_sustainability`, text, required): Describe the sustainability practices of your business partner, if applicable.
* **New Location** (`new_location`, text, required): If you have a new location for your business, provide details about it.
* **Franchise Partner** (`franchise_partner`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of your franchise partner.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Share any relevant experience related to your business or the eco-franchise industry.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including phone and email.
* **Submit** (`submit_info`, text, required): Click to submit your application for review.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, number, optional): You can optionally upload any supporting documents relevant to your application.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, text, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the registration process.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your franchise partner.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your franchise partner.
