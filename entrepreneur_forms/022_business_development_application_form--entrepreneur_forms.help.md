# Business Development Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help entrepreneurs and business owners assess their company's readiness for growth and expansion. It guides users through a series of questions to understand their business development goals, target markets, potential partnerships, expansion timeline, resources and capabilities, budget, key strengths, and current challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing accurate and detailed information about your business.
2. For select multiple options, choose all that apply.
3. For select one options, choose the most relevant answer.
4. If you need more space to provide additional information, use the 'Additional Information' field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your complete name as it appears on your business records or identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for communication.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business records or identification.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter your job title within your company.
* **Years in Business** (`company_age`, number, required): Enter the number of years your company has been established.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, text, required): Choose the type of industry your business operates in (e.g., tech, finance, healthcare, etc.).
* **Primary Development Goal** (`primary_goal`, select_one, required): Choose one goal from the list:
	+ Expand Market Share
	+ Build Strategic Partnerships
	+ Enter New Markets
	+ Increase Revenue
	+ Develop New Products
	+ Improve Operations
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Target Markets** (`target_markets`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable markets from the list:
	+ Domestic Market
	+ Regional Market
	+ International Market
	+ New Customer Segments
	+ Emerging Markets
* **Partnership Interests** (`partnership_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable partnership types from the list:
	+ Technology Partners
	+ Distribution Partners
	+ Strategic Alliances
	+ Joint Ventures
	+ Channel Partners
	+ Supplier Relationships
* **Expansion Timeline** (`expansion_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the expansion timeline that best suits your needs:
	+ Immediate (0-3 months)
	+ Short-term (3-6 months)
	+ Medium-term (6-12 months)
	+ Long-term (12+ months)
* **Budget Available for Development** (`budget_available`, number, optional): Enter the amount of budget available for business development.
* **Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable strengths from the list:
	+ Strong Product
	+ Brand Recognition
	+ Technical Expertise
	+ Customer Relationships
	+ Financial Resources
	+ Team Talent
* **Current Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any current challenges your business faces.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details about your business.
