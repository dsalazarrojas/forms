# Work From Phone Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants seeking to work from home. It helps us assess your suitability for remote work and establish the terms of your work-from-home arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Select the options that accurately describe your situation.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your work-from-home arrangement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info** (Required): Enter your full name.
* **Work From Home** (Required): Select the option that best describes your work-from-home arrangement (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **Work From Phone** (Required): Select the options that accurately describe your work-from-phone arrangement (e.g., mobile, landline, etc.).
* **Work From Location** (Required): Enter the location you will be working from (e.g., home, office, coffee shop, etc.).
* **Work From Time Zone** (Required): Enter your time zone (e.g., Pacific, Eastern, Central, etc.).
* **Work From Application** (Required): Type a brief description of your work-from-home application (e.g., job, project, etc.).
* **Work From Email** (Optional): Enter your work email address (e.g., example@domain.com).
* **Work From Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number (e.g., 1-800-1234567).
* **Work From Application Date** (Required): Select the date you submitted your application.
* **Work From Start Date** (Required): Select the start date of your work-from-home arrangement.
* **Work From End Date** (Required): Select the end date of your work-from-home arrangement.
* **Work From Hours** (Required): Enter the total number of hours you will work per week.
* **Work From Days** (Required): Enter the total number of days you will work per week.
* **Work From Status** (Required): Select the option that best describes your current work-from-home status (e.g., active, inactive, pending, etc.).
* **Work From Description** (Required): Enter a brief description of your work-from-home arrangement (e.g., job description, responsibilities, etc.).
* **Work From Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your work-from-home arrangement.
* **Work From Employer Info** (Optional): Enter any information about your employer (e.g., company name, contact person, etc.).
* **Work From Reference** (Required): Enter a reference or contact person for your work-from-home arrangement.
* **Work From Agreement** (Required): Select the option that best describes the type of agreement you have with your employer (e.g., contract, non-disclosure, etc.).
* **Work From Agreement Date** (Required): Select the date of your work-from-home agreement.
* **Work From Agreement End Date** (Required): Select the end date of your work-from-home agreement.
* **Work From Agreement Status** (Required): Select the option that best describes the status of your work-from-home agreement (e.g., active, inactive, pending, etc.).
* **Work From Agreement Terms** (Required): Enter the terms of your work-from-home agreement (e.g., job description, responsibilities, etc.).
* **Work From Employer** (Required): Enter the name of your employer (e.g., company name, department, etc.).
* **Work From Signature** (Required): Enter your digital signature.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
