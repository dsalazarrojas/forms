# Scooter Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scooter Rental Application Form is designed for customers to apply for scooter rental services. This form gathers essential information to facilitate the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information:
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
	* Provide your **Email** and **Phone** number for contact purposes.
2. Select your scooter preferences:
	* Choose the type of scooter you need from the **Type Of Scooter** dropdown.
	* Select the color of the scooter you require from the **Color Of Scooter** dropdown.
3. Schedule your rental:
	* Select the **Date Of Rental** you plan to rent the scooter.
	* Choose the **Time Of Rental** that suits you best.
	* Specify the **Duration Of Rental**, if needed.
4. Add any special requests:
	* Enter any **Special Requests** you have, if applicable.
5. Confirm your rental agency relationship:
	* If you are associated with a scooter rental agency, select **Yes**. Otherwise, select **No**.
6. Review and submit your application:
	* Double-check all the information provided and click the **Submit** button to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Type Of Scooter**: Select the type of scooter you need, such as a classic scooter, folding scooter, or e-scooter.
* **Color Of Scooter**: Select the color of the scooter you prefer.
* **Date Of Rental**: Choose the date you plan to rent the scooter.
* **Time Of Rental**: Choose the time you plan to pick up the scooter.
* **Duration Of Rental**: Specify the duration of rental, if applicable.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them here.
* **Scooter Rental Agency**: If you are affiliated with a scooter rental agency, select **Yes**. Otherwise, select **No**.
* **Submit**: Double-check all information and click the submit button to complete your application.
