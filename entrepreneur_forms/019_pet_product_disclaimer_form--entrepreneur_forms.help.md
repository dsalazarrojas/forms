# Pet Product Disclaimer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for business owners or representatives who want to register and manage their pet-related products on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information in each field, making sure to double-check for accuracy.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct type of information.
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Product Disclaimer Form** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form, which should not be changed.
* **Product Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the name of your product, e.g., "Furry Fleece Dog Coat".
* **Product Description** (`id: 3`): Describe your product in detail, including its features and benefits.
* **Product Price** (`id: 4`): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product Status** (`id: 5`): Select the current status of your product from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Product Category** (`id: 6`): Choose the category of your product from the options: Dog, Cat, Fish, or multiple options.
* **Product Image** (`id: 7`): Enter a URL or description of the product image.
* **Product Link** (`id: 8`): Enter a URL of the product page or landing page.
* **Product Video** (`id: 9`): Enter a URL or description of the product video.
* **Business Name** (`id: 10`): Enter the name of your business.
* **Address** (`id: 11`): Enter the physical address of your business.
* **Contact Person** (`id: 12`): Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Email** (`id: 13`): Enter the email of the contact person or business.
* **Phone** (`id: 14`): Enter the phone number of the contact person or business.
* **Product Tags** (`id: 15`): Choose relevant tags for your product from the options: Pet Supplies, Pet Accessories, Pet Food, etc.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`id: 16`): This is a field for internal use, but you can add any relevant terms and conditions related to your business.
* **Disclaimer Agreement** (`id: 17`): This is a field for internal use, where you can agree to the terms and conditions of our platform.

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use proper grammar and spelling in all text fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
