# Join The Solopreneur Waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in joining the Solopreneur program or expressing their interest in it. Please fill it out to help us get in touch with you or to learn more about your goals and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To join the waitlist, select "Yes" next to "Join The Solopreneur Waitlist" if you are interested in the program.
2.  Fill out your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
3.  Optionally, provide your website and category (e.g., creator, freelancer, founder) to help us better understand your profile.
4.  Choose the segment that best describes your current stage in your entrepreneurial journey (prelaunch, launch, postlaunch).

*   **Join The Solopreneur Waitlist**: If you are interested in joining the Solopreneur program, select "Yes" to indicate your interest. This helps us track potential customers or prospects who may be interested in the program.

*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about the program or other updates.
*   **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number to enable us to call you back or discuss any questions you may have.
*   **Website** (Optional): If you have a website or blog related to your entrepreneurial endeavors, share the URL to give us a better understanding of your work.
*   **Category** (Optional): Select the category that best describes your role as an entrepreneur (creator, freelancer, founder).
*   **Segment** (Optional): Choose the segment that corresponds to your current stage in your entrepreneurial journey (prelaunch, launch, postlaunch).
