# Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Registration Form is designed to collect customer contact information and demographic details that can help businesses in understanding their customer base.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name, which are both required fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
3. Fill in your Phone number, which is also optional, but useful for communication and verification.
4. Provide your Password and Repeat Password, which are required for security purposes.
5. Enter your Address, City, State, Zip, and Country, which are all optional fields.
6. If you are registering a business, fill in the Business Name, Business Type, and Business Category.
7. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Create a strong password for security purposes.
* **Repeat Password** (`repeat_password`, text, required): Re-enter your password to confirm it.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and verification purposes.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your residential or business address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you live or your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you live or your business is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for postal purposes.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence or business location.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter your business name if you are registering a business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of business you are registering.
* **Business Category** (`business_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of your business.
