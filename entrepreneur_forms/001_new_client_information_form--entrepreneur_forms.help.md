# New Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Information Form is designed to collect personal and professional details from new clients, which will be used to personalize and tailor our communication with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the First Name field.
2. Fill in your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Type in your phone number in the Phone field.
5. In the Reason for Choosing Our Business field, briefly describe why you are interested in our services.
6. Select your preferred method of contact from the options provided in the How Do You Prefer to be Contacted field.
7. If you have a preferred method of contact that is not listed, type it in the Other field.
8. If you have a company or organization, enter it in the Company field (optional).
9. If you have a job title, enter it in the Job Title field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Reason for Choosing Our Business**: A text field to briefly describe why you are interested in our services.
* **How Do You Prefer to be Contacted**: A select_one field with options for Phone, Email, or Mail.
* **Other**: A text field to specify a preferred method of contact if it's not listed.
* **Company**: A text field to enter your company or organization name (optional).
* **Job Title**: A text field to enter your job title or position (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, don't hesitate to contact us for help.
* Please note that the form is intended to be filled out once, so double-check the information provided for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
