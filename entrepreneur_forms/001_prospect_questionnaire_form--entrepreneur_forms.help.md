# Prospect Questionnaire Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Prospect Questionnaire Form is used to collect information about potential clients or business partners. This form is designed to gather general contact information, professional background, and skills of the individual or company. The purpose of this form is to help our team better understand your business needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and what information is required.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Select relevant options for each field, if applicable.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Info (Page 1)

* **Title**: Enter your name or company name.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Address**: Enter your business address.

### Contact Info (Page 2)

* **Email**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Phone**: (Note: This field is likely a typo and should be removed. Assuming you meant to enter a secondary phone number or other contact information.)

### Address (Page 3)

* **Address**: Enter your business address.

### Education (Page 4)

* **Education**: Select one or more educational qualifications you have achieved (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D.).
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant qualifications.

### Work Experience (Page 5)

* **Work Experience**: Select one or more years of work experience you have.
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant experience.

### Skills (Page 6)

* **Skills**: Select one or more skills you possess (e.g., HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, SQL, C++).
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant skills.

### Languages (Page 7)

* **Languages**: Select one or more languages you speak or are familiar with (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant languages.

### Tools (Page 8)

* **Tools**: Select one or more tools you are familiar with (e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud, Illustrator).
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant tools.

### Social Media (Page 9)

* **Social Media**: Select one or more social media platforms you are active on (e.g., Facebook, Twitter, Instagram).
*   Note: Selecting multiple options is allowed, but please select only relevant social media platforms.
