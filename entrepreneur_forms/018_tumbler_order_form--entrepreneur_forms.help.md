# Tumbler Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tumbler Order Form is designed to collect customer information and order specifications to process and fulfill customer orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your customer name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the **Email** field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the **Phone** field.
4. Provide the desired **Order Date** for your order.
5. If applicable, specify the exact time for your order in the **Order Time** field.
6. Select the desired **Tumbler Type** size from the options provided.
7. Choose the preferred **Tumbler Color** from the available options.
8. Enter the quantity of tumblers you would like to order.
9. If you have any special instructions or notes, you can include them in the **Special Instructions** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Text)**: Enter your full name as it should be written on the order.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address where the order confirmation and further information can be sent.
* **Phone (Text)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for further contact.
* **Order Date (Date)**: Provide the date you would like the order to be fulfilled or processed.
* **Order Time (Time)**: If applicable, specify the exact time for the order to be fulfilled or processed.
* **Tumbler Type (Select One)**: Select the desired size of tumbler: 18oz, 20oz, or 32oz.
* **Tumbler Color (Select One)**: Choose the preferred color of the tumbler: blue, red, green, or purple.
* **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the amount of tumblers you would like to order.
* **Special Instructions (Text)**: If you have any special requests or additional information, please include it here.
