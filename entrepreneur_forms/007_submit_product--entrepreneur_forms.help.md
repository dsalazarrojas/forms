# Submit Product - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a new product with details such as name, category, and description. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a new product, please fill out the required fields below and click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product you wish to submit.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select one or more categories that best describe your product. Please choose from the available options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the product, including its features and benefits.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your product submission.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your product submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select one or more categories that accurately describe your product.
* Enter a clear and concise description of your product.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy if you choose to provide them.
