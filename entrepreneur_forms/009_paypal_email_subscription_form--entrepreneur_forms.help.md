# paypal_email_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information from companies or individuals interested in receiving our newsletters on a regular basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Email address.
2. Select how often you want to receive newsletters.
3. Provide your First Name.
4. Provide your Last Name.
5. Enter your Company (if applicable).
6. Enter your Job Title.
7. Enter a brief description of your Job.
8. Select your Industry.
9. Select your Job Function.
10. Enter your Company Website (if applicable).
11. Enter your LinkedIn Profile (if applicable).
12. Enter your Twitter Profile (if applicable).
13. Enter your Instagram Profile (if applicable).
14. Enter your Company Location.
15. Enter your Address.
16. Enter your Zip Code.
17. Enter your City.
18. Select your State.
19. Select your Country.
20. Enter your Phone Number (if applicable).
21. Select whether your company is 'Yes' or 'No' size.
22. Select whether your company is 'Yes' or 'No' type.
23. Select whether you want to 'Yes' or 'No' unsubscribe from future newsletters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address that we can use to send you newsletters.
* **Subscribe Frequency** (`subscribe_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you want to receive newsletters. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you work for a company, please enter its name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, optional): Select the industry you work in.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, optional): Select the function you perform in your job.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, optional): If you have a company website, please enter the URL.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (`linkedin_profile`, `text`, optional): If you have a LinkedIn profile, please enter the URL.
* **Twitter Profile** (`twitter_profile`, `text`, optional): If you have a Twitter profile, please enter the URL.
* **Instagram Profile** (`instagram_profile`, `text`, optional): If you have an Instagram profile, please enter the URL.
* **Company Location** (`company_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of your company.
* **Address** (`company_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `number`, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of your location.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, optional): Select the state of your location.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Select the country of your location.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether your company is 'Yes' or 'No' in size.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether your company is 'Yes' or 'No' in type.
* **Unsubscribe** (`unsubscribe`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you want to 'Yes' or 'No' unsubscribe from future newsletters.
