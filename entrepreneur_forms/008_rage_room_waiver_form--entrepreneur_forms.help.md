This form is designed to collect information from participants in an activity or event, particularly in a rage room setting. The form aims to capture essential details about the participant, including their emergency contact information, waiver and risk disclosure, liability, and safety considerations. It also includes fields for confirming and signing off on the participant's information.

## Purpose
The "Rage Room Waiver Form" is a crucial document that must be completed by participants before engaging in a rage room activity. The form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the rules and regulations of the activity, as well as understand the risks involved. By completing this form, participants confirm that they are physically and mentally capable of participating in the activity and release the facility from liability in case of any injuries or accidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying details as required.
	* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact.
	* **Waiver**: Check the box to confirm you understand and agree to the waiver.
	* **Risk Disclosure**: Check the box to confirm you understand and agree to the risk disclosure.
	* **Liability**: Check the box to confirm you understand and agree to the facility's liability.
	* **Safety**: Check the box to confirm you understand and agree to the safety precautions.
	* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understand the participant information.
	* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your acceptance of the terms.
	* **Emergency Contact Signature**: Sign the emergency contact's name to confirm their agreement.
	* **Facility Confirmation**: Check the box to confirm you have read and understand the facility's information.
	* **Facility Signature**: Sign the facility's name to confirm their agreement.
2. Choose your age group from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your height (in feet or inches, or meters or centimeters).
4. Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your weight.
6. Select your height unit (feet, inches, centimeters, or meters).
7. Enter your height value.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. If you have a medical condition, enter the details.
