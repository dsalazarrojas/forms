# supplier_diversity_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Diversity Registration Form is used to capture contact information and demographic data about diverse suppliers for supplier diversity programs and initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required", with the necessary information.
2. Enter contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your company name, contact person, email, and phone number.
3. In the "Contact Person" field, enter the name of the contact person within your company.
4. Select the category of diverse group that best describes your company.
5. Select the type of supplier your company is classified as (Service Provider, Manufacturer, Distributor, Agent, or Other).
6. Select the industry your company operates in (Construction, Information Technology, Financial Services, Health Care, or Other).
7. Choose the size of your business (Small, Medium, Large, or Very Large).
8. Review and submit the form for verification and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This section captures general contact information about your company and its representative. Enter your company name, contact person's name, contact email, and phone number.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person within your company who is the primary contact for diversity initiatives.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name to be included in supplier diversity databases.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact and verification purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your business contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Underrepresented Group**: Select the category of diverse group your company belongs to. This will help us better understand and support your diversity initiatives.
* **Supplier Type**: Select the type of supplier your company is classified as (e.g., Service Provider, Manufacturer, etc.).
* **Supplier Industry**: Select the industry your company operates in (e.g., Construction, Information Technology, etc.).
* **Business Size**: Choose the size of your business for accurate classification and reporting.

## Tips
