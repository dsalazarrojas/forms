# Competitor Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help companies gather data on their competitors' performance and market position. It's essential to answer these questions accurately to get a comprehensive understanding of the competitive landscape.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Survey Date**: Please select the date when you last surveyed your competitors.
2. **Respondent Name**: Enter your name and title for reference and identification purposes.
3. **Respondent Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. **Your Company**: Enter your company name and details.
5. **Industry**: Select the industry your company operates in.
6. **Company Size**: Choose the number of employees in your company.
7. **Your Role**: Select your position in the company.
8. **Primary Competitor Name**: Enter the name of your main competitor.
9. **Competitor Market Share**: Estimate the competitor's market share as a percentage.
10. **Competitor Strengths**: Describe what your competitor excels at.
11. **Competitor Weaknesses**: Describe where your competitor struggles.
12. **Competitor Pricing**: Compare your competitor's pricing strategy with yours.
13. **Competitor Quality**: Evaluate the quality of your competitor's products or services.
14. **Competitor Customer Service**: Assess the quality of your competitor's customer service.
15. **Competitor Brand Recognition**: Evaluate your competitor's brand recognition.
16. **Competitor Marketing Effectiveness**: Assess the effectiveness of your competitor's marketing strategy.
17. **Competitor Innovation**: Evaluate your competitor's innovation level.
18. **Lost Deals to Competitor**: Have you lost business deals to this competitor? If yes, select the frequency.
19. **Why Lost Deals**: Choose the main reasons you lost deals to this competitor.
20. **Won Deals Against Competitor**: Have you won deals against this competitor? If yes, select the frequency.
21. **Why Won Deals**: Choose the main reasons you won deals against this competitor.
22. **Competitive Threat Level**: Evaluate the overall threat level of your competitor.
23. **Monitoring Frequency**: Select how frequently you monitor this competitor.
24. **Information Sources**: Select the sources you use to gather information on this competitor.
25. **Strategic Response**: Enter any additional comments or strategies you're using to respond to this competitor.
26. **Additional Comments**: Share any other insights or comments you have about this competitor.
