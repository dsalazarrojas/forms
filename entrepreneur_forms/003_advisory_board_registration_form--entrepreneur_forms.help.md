# Advisory Board Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential members of the advisory board from the organization. The form is used to gather essential details about the individual's professional background, interests, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, professional title, and contact details.
2. Select your areas of expertise and years of relevant experience.
3. Provide details about your previous advisory experience and availability to commit to the board.
4. Choose your preferred meeting format and industries of interest.
5. Share your motivation for joining the advisory board and unique value proposition.
6. Disclose any potential conflicts of interest and provide references.
7. Finally, indicate your compensation expectations and availability for references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Your current job title or position.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, `text`, required): Current employer or affiliated organization.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Professional email for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Professional phone number.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`linkedin_profile`, `text`, optional): Share your LinkedIn profile for reference.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your primary areas of expertise.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years of experience in your field of expertise.
* **Previous Advisory Experience** (`previous_advisory_experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you served on advisory boards before?
* **Details of Previous Advisory Experience** (`advisory_experience_details`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your previous advisory roles.
* **Time Availability Commitment** (`availability_commitment`, `select_one`, required): How much time can you commit monthly to advisory activities?
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (`meeting_format_preference`, `select_one`, required): How do you prefer to participate in advisory meetings?
* **Industries of Interest** (`industries_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Which industries would you like to advise on?
* **Motivation to Join Advisory Board** (`motivation_to_join`, `text`, required): Why are you interested in joining our advisory board?
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, `text`, required): What unique value can you bring to our organization?
* **Compensation Expectations** (`compensation_expectations`, `select_one`, optional): What type of compensation are you seeking?
* **Conflict of Interest Disclosure** (`conflict_disclosure`, `text`, required): Please disclose any potential conflicts of interest.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, `select_one`, required): Are professional references available upon request?
