# Customer Account Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Account Application Form is designed to collect information from customers to create a new customer account. Please use this form to gather necessary data from customers who wish to establish a new relationship with your business.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information for the customer.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you've entered all required information.
3. Submit the form to complete the customer account application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their business card, identification, or other official document.
* **Customer Email** (`Customer Email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address where you would like to communicate with them.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's contact number to facilitate communication.
* **Customer Address** (`Customer Address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's physical address where they can be reached.
* **Account Type** (`Account Type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select either "account-type-business" for a business account
	+ Select "account-type-personal" for a personal account
	+ Select "account-type-organization" for an organization account
* **Account Number** (`Account Number`, number, optional): Enter the customer's account number if they have one.
* **Business Name** (`Business Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the customer's business if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to the customer.
* Double-check the customer's information for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or account manager for guidance.
