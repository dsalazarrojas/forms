# Green Attire Fashion Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Attire Fashion Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information on your attitudes and behaviors related to sustainable fashion. Your participation is greatly appreciated and will help us understand how individuals perceive the fashion industry's environmental impact.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the answer that best fits your response to each question.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose one or multiple options that apply to you.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Green Attire Fashion Survey** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the title of the survey.
* **What type of clothing do you prefer?** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your preference for different types of clothing.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you prefer sustainable clothing.
	+ Choose 'No' if you do not prefer sustainable clothing.
* **How do you currently implement sustainable fashion practices?** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your current practices related to sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Eco-friendly materials' if you prefer clothing made from eco-friendly materials.
	+ Choose 'Second-hand clothing' if you often buy second-hand clothing.
	+ Choose 'Local sourcing' if you prefer to source clothing locally.
* **What sustainable fashion options do you currently use?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your current sustainable fashion options.
	+ Choose 'Eco-friendly clothing' if you prefer clothing made from eco-friendly materials.
	+ Choose 'Recycled materials' if you prefer clothing made from recycled materials.
	+ Choose 'Repurposed materials' if you prefer clothing made from repurposed materials.
* **Do you prefer to source locally?** (`5`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your preference for sourcing clothing locally.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you prefer to source clothing locally.
	+ Choose 'No' if you do not prefer to source clothing locally.
* **How concerned are you about the environment?** (`6`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your level of concern for the environment.
	+ Choose 'Somewhat concerned' if you are somewhat concerned about the environment.
	+ Choose 'Very concerned' if you are very concerned about the environment.
	+ Choose 'Not concerned' if you are not concerned about the environment.
* **What drives your preferences for sustainable fashion?** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about what drives your preference for sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Price' if price is a factor in your preference for sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Sustainability' if sustainability is a factor in your preference for sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Style' if style is a factor in your preference for sustainable fashion.
* **How do you think the fashion industry impacts the environment?** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your perception of the fashion industry's impact on the environment.
	+ Choose 'Positive' if you think the fashion industry has a positive impact on the environment.
	+ Choose 'Negative' if you think the fashion industry has a negative impact on the environment.
	+ Choose 'Neutral' if you think the fashion industry has a neutral impact on the environment.
* **Any additional comments?** (`9`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **What are your next steps in promoting sustainable fashion?** (`10`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your plans for promoting sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Educate friends and family' if you plan to educate others about sustainable fashion.
	+ Choose 'Support sustainable fashion brands' if you plan to support brands that offer sustainable fashion options.
	+ Choose 'Make lifestyle changes' if you plan to make personal changes in your fashion choices.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`11`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
