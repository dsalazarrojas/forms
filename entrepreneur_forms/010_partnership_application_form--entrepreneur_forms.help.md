# Partnership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from entrepreneurs and business owners who are interested in applying for partnership with our company. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Double-check your contact information and email address for accuracy.
3. Select the correct options for the business type and agreement sections.
4. Review the terms and conditions and ensure you have read and agreed to them.
5. Submit the form once you have reviewed and agreed to all the terms and conditions.
