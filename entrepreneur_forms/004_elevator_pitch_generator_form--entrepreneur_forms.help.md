# Elevator Pitch Generator Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your professional background, career, and goals. This data will be used to generate a concise and effective elevator pitch that you can use to promote yourself to potential employers or partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your elevator pitch in the "Enter Your Pitch" field.
2. Select the industry you work in from the "Industry" field.
3. Choose your career level from the "Career Level" field.
4. Enter your job title and years of experience in the "Job Title" and "Years of Experience" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
6. Finally, click "Submit" to generate your elevator pitch.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Your Pitch (text)**: A brief description of your professional background, career, and goals. This field is optional but recommended to be filled out in full.
* **Job Role (text)**: A brief description of your job role or title.
* **Career Level (number)**: Your current career level (e.g., entry-level, mid-level, senior).
* **Industry (select_one)**: The industry you work in (e.g., Software and IT, Finance, Marketing and Advertising).
* **Elevator Pitch (text)**: A brief description of your professional background, career, and goals. This field is optional but recommended to be filled out in full.
* **Company Name (text)**: The name of the company you currently work for or have worked for (if applicable).
* **Job Title (text)**: Your current job title.
* **Years of Experience (number)**: Your years of experience in your current or most recent industry.
