# Export Readiness Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Readiness Program Application Form is a tool for entrepreneurs to apply for the Export Readiness Program. It is used to gather information required for eligibility assessment and program enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form with accurate and honest information.
2. Provide all required fields.
3. Ensure that all contact information is up-to-date and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the export program you are interested in applying for.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your business phone number.
* **Export Location** (`export_location`, `select_one`, required): Please select the location where you export your products or services. Options: Hong Kong, Shenzen, Guangdong.
* **Export Date** (`export_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date you are planning to start exporting. Note: This is for informational purposes only.
* **Export Time** (`export_time`, `time`, required): Please select the time you are planning to start exporting. Note: This is for informational purposes only.
* **Export Frequency** (`export_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the frequency of your export activities. Options: Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Tri-Weekly, Monthly.
* **Contact Position** (`contact_position`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact position in the company (e.g. CEO, Manager, etc.).
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Please enter your company name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `text`, required): Please enter your industry (e.g. Electronics, Textiles, etc.).
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief description of your company and products or services.
* **Export Frequency Note** (`export_frequency_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your export frequency.
* **Export Location Note** (`export_location_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your export location.
* **Contact Name Note** (`contact_name_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your contact name.
* **Export Readiness** (`export_readiness`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you are ready to export. Options: Yes, No.
* **Export Status** (`export_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select the status of your export activities. Options: Active, Not Active.
* **Export Frequency Status** (`export_frequency_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select the status of your export frequency. Options: On-going, Not Ongoing.
* **Export Readiness Note** (`export_readiness_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your export readiness.
* **Export Location Status** (`export_location_status`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your export location status.
* **Contact Email Status** (`contact_email_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select the status of your contact email. Options: Verified, Unverified.
* **Contact Position Status** (`contact_position_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select the status of your contact position. Options: Approved, Not Approved.
* **Export Status Note** (`export_status_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your export status.
* **Organization Note** (`organization_note`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information regarding your organization.
* **Export Exported Date** (`export_exported_date`, `date`, optional): Please select the date you have completed an export activity. Note: This is for informational purposes only.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email].
