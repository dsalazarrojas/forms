# Innovation Strategy Advisory Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about your innovation strategy to the Innovation Strategy Advisory Program. Your application will be reviewed by the program administrators to determine eligibility and potential fit with the program's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, taking care to provide clear and concise information about your innovation strategy.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (if any).
3. Double-check your answers for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (1, text, optional): This field is an optional text field where you can provide any additional information about your innovation strategy that you feel is important.
* **Innovation Description** (2, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your innovation, including its nature, scope, and any relevant background information.
* **Innovation Strategy** (3, text, optional): Describe your innovation strategy, including your vision, goals, and objectives.
* **Innovation Objectives** (4, text, optional): Specify the specific objectives you aim to achieve with your innovation, such as increased efficiency, cost savings, or improved customer satisfaction.
* **Innovation Goals** (5, text, optional): Outline your specific goals for the innovation, such as market penetration, revenue growth, or customer acquisition.
* **Innovation Status** (6, text, optional): Indicate the current status of your innovation, such as planning, development, testing, or implementation.
* **Innovation Budget** (7, number, optional): Provide an estimate of the budget allocated or planned for your innovation.
* **Innovation Partners** (8, text, optional): List any partners, collaborators, or stakeholders involved in your innovation.
* **Innovation Timeline** (9, date, optional): Provide a specific date for the start and end of your innovation project.
* **Innovation Timeline Duration** (10, time, optional): Specify the duration of your innovation project.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when answering each question.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, feel free to contact the program administrators for guidance.
* Make sure to proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors before submitting the form.
