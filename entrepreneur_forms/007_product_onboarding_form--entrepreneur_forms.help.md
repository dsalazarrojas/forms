# Product Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Onboarding Form is used to collect information from new customers to populate our internal database. This form will only be used once per customer, so please only submit it once.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. For fields with multiple options, select only one option.
3. Double check your contact info before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Customer Form (Text)** (`new_customer_form`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, company name, or any other relevant information about yourself or your company.
* **Product Details (Text)** (`product_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the product or service you are interested in.
* **Customer Preferences (Text)** (`customer_preferences`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific preferences you have for the product or service.
* **Billing Info (Text)** (`billing_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your billing information, including address, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Method (Select One)** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following payment methods:
	+ option 1: Credit Card
	+ option 2: PayPal
	+ option 3: Bank Transfer
* **Shipping Info (Text)** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping information, including address and any other relevant details.
* **Review (Note)** (`review`, note, required/optional): Enter your review or feedback about our services.
* **Contact Info (Select Multiple)** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select up to two of the following contact methods:
	+ option 4: Phone
	+ option 5: Email
	+ option 6: Text Message
* **Agreement (Select One)** (`agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following agreements:
	+ option 7: Terms of Service
	+ option 8: Privacy Policy
	+ option 9: Both

## Tips
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy before sending.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* This form will not be used for any purpose other than to onboard your account.
