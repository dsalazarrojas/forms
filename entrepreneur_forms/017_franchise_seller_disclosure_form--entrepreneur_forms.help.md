# Franchise Seller Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your business, which will be used to assist with the sale process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully and answer all the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your responses in the corresponding text fields.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Details** (Franchise Details, text): Enter a brief description of your franchise business.
* **Franchisor Information** (Franchisor Information, text): Enter any relevant information about the franchisor (the seller).
* **Franchisor Business** (Franchisor Business, select_multiple): Choose 'Yes' if the franchisor is actively engaged in business or 'No' if not.
* **Franchisor Terms** (Franchisor Terms, text): Enter any additional information about the franchisor's terms.
* **Franchise Terms** (Franchise Terms, text): Enter any additional information about the franchise terms.
* **Franchisor Address** (Franchisor Address, text): Enter the address of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisor Phone** (Franchisor Phone, text): Enter the phone number of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisor Email** (Franchisor Email, email): Enter the email address of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisee Details** (Franchisee Details, text): Enter a brief description of the franchisee (the buyer).
* **Franchise Agreement** (Franchise Agreement, text): Enter any additional information about the franchise agreement.
* **Franchisor Employees** (Franchisor Employees, text): Enter the number of employees at the franchisor's business.
* **Franchise Fees** (Franchise Fees, text): Enter any fees associated with the franchise business.
* **Franchisor Equipment** (Franchisor Equipment, text): Enter any equipment or assets owned by the franchisor.
* **Franchisor Royalties** (Franchisor Royalties, number): Enter any royalties associated with the franchise business.
* **Franchisor Franchisees** (Franchisor Franchisees, select_one): Choose 'Yes' if the franchisor has existing franchisees or 'No' if not.
* **Franchise Fund** (Franchise Fund, text): Enter any information about the fund associated with the franchise.
* **Franchisor Fund** (Franchisor Fund, text): Enter any information about the franchisor's fund.
* **Franchisor Funds** (Franchisor Funds, select_multiple): Choose 'Yes' if the franchisor has multiple funds or 'No' if not.
* **Franchisor Notes** (Franchisor Notes, text): Enter any additional notes about the franchisor.
* **Franchise Terms 2** (Franchise Terms 2, text): Enter any additional information about the franchise terms.
* **Franchisor Address 2** (Franchisor Address 2, text): Enter the secondary address of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisor Phone 2** (Franchisor Phone 2, text): Enter the secondary phone number of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisor Email 2** (Franchisor Email 2, email): Enter the secondary email address of the franchisor's business.
* **Franchisor Date** (Franchisor Date, date): Enter the date of the last update or revision of the franchise terms.
* **Franchisor Time** (Franchisor Time, time): Enter the time of the last update or revision of the franchise terms.

Note: This guide is for general assistance and not a substitute for professional advice. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
