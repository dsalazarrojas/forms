# Seller Initiation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seller Initiation Form Template is a form used to gather information about a seller's details, product, and experience. This form is intended to provide a structured way to collect data that will help the sales team to better understand the seller and their offerings, and to improve the sales process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Step 1: Enter Seller Information**: Fill in the seller's details, including their title and other relevant information.
2. **Step 2: Enter Seller Details**: Provide your personal details, such as your contact information.
3. **Step 3: Enter Product Information**: Enter information about the product you are selling, such as its description and features.
4. **Step 4: Enter Seller Experience**: Share your experience related to the product or services you are offering.
5. **Step 5: Upload Seller Photos**: Upload photos of yourself or your product to help with the sales process.
6. **Step 6: Enter About Us**: Write a brief description about yourself or your company.
7. **Step 7: Upload Files**: Select and upload any files that are relevant to your seller profile, such as business documents or product manuals.
8. **Step 8: Read and Agree to Terms**: Finally, review and agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Step 1: Seller Initiation Form Template Form
* **Seller Initiation Form Template Page 1 (seller_initiation_form_template_page_1)** (`title`, `text`, required):
Enter your title as it appears on your business card or resume.

* **Seller Details (seller_details)** (`number`, `text`, required):
Enter your personal contact details, including your phone number and email address.
