# Responsive Contact Form Default Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contact form designed for entrepreneurs and business owners to gather contact information and feedback from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information fields with your name, contact info, and email address.
2. Enter a brief message describing your inquiry or feedback.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Chat, Email, or Phone) in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4. Select the category of your form (Entrepreneur Forms or Forms) in the "Form Category" field.
5. Optionally, add a brief description of your form in the "Form Description" field.
6. Click submit to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text, optional): Fill in this field with your name.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Fill in this field with your contact information.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message describing your inquiry or feedback.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Fill in this field with your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Fill in this field with your phone number.
* **Message 2** (`contact_message_2`, text, optional): Enter a brief message describing your inquiry or feedback.
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Fill in this field with your contact information.
* **Subject** (`contact_subject`, text, optional): Enter a brief subject for your email or inquiry.
* **Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Fill in this field with your email address.
* **Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text, optional): Fill in this field with your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Chat, Email, or Phone).
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your form (Entrepreneur Forms or Forms).
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Add a brief description of your form if desired.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Chat, Email, or Phone).
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your form (Entrepreneur Forms or Forms).
