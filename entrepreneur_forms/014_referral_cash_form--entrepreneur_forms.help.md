# Referral Cash Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Cash Form is used to capture and record customer referrals and reward information. It includes fields for customer contact details, business information, and date of referral.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a brief title for the referral in the "Referral Cash Form" field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" from the "Category" dropdown to indicate whether this referral is related to a customer or not.
3. Provide a short description of the referral in the "Description" field.
4. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer name" field.
5. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer email" field. Please ensure this is a valid email format.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer phone" field.
7. Enter the reward amount for the referral in the "Reward amount" field.
8. Enter any other relevant details about the customer in the "Customer referral" field.
9. Enter the name of the business the customer was referred to in the "Business name" field.
10. Enter the phone number of the business the customer was referred to in the "Business phone" field.
11. Enter the email address of the business the customer was referred to in the "Business email" field.
12. Enter the date of the referral in the "Referral date" field.
13. Add any additional comments about the referral in the "Form comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Cash Form** (Text): Enter a brief title for the referral.
* **Category** (Select One): Select "Yes" to indicate this referral is related to a customer, or "No" otherwise.
* **Description** (Text): Provide a short description of the referral.
* **Customer name** (Text): Enter the name of the customer.
* **Customer email** (Email): Enter the customer's email address. Please ensure this is a valid email format.
* **Customer phone** (Text): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Reward amount** (Number): Enter the reward amount for the referral.
* **Customer referral** (Text): Enter any other relevant details about the customer.
* **Business name** (Text): Enter the name of the business the customer was referred to.
* **Business phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the business the customer was referred to.
* **Business email** (Email): Enter the email address of the business the customer was referred to.
* **Referral date** (Date): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Form comments** (Text): Add any additional comments about the referral.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete record-keeping.
* Double-check your entries for formatting and accuracy.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the relevant business guidelines or best practices.
