# Smart Business Analytics Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Business Analytics Accelerator Application Form is designed to collect detailed information about your business, allowing us to assess its potential and provide valuable guidance for its growth. This form is specifically tailored for entrepreneurs and small business owners who are looking to leverage the Smart Business Accelerator's resources to enhance their business performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the **Application Form** (page 1). This section is crucial as it provides background information about your business.
2. Move on to the **Contact Information** (page 2) and fill out your contact details accurately.
3. Describe your **Business Description** (page 3) in a concise and clear manner, highlighting your business's unique value proposition.
4. Provide a detailed **Team Description** (page 4) about the people behind your business, including their roles and qualifications.
5. Add any **Additional Information** (page 5) that you believe is pertinent to your application.
6. In the **Legal Information** (page 6) section, you will need to provide details about your business's legal structure and any relevant permits or licenses.
7. Choose the **Company Location** (page 7) from the provided options that best describes your business's geographical location.
8. Enter the **Number of Employees** (page 8) accurately, as this will help us assess your business's operational capacity.
9. In the **Financial Information** (page 9), provide a brief overview of your business's financial situation, including revenue and growth rate.
10. Select the **Industry** (page 10) that best categorizes your business from the provided options.
11. Enter your **Revenue** (page 11) and **Growth Rate** (page 12) accurately, as these are critical metrics for assessing your business's performance.
12. Choose the **Submit** (page 13) option that best describes your application status.
13. Confirm your **Agreement** (page 14) by selecting the correct option.
14. Finally, add any **Additional Comments** (page 15) that you feel are relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form (page 1)**: This section is where you provide the title of your business, a brief description, and any other relevant background information.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**: Fill in your contact details accurately, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Business Description (page 3)**: Explain your business's mission, goals, and unique selling points.
* **Team Description (page 4)**: List the key team members and their roles and qualifications.
* **Additional Information (page 5)**: Any additional information that you feel is crucial to your application.
* **Legal Information (page 6)**: Provide any relevant permits or licenses your business holds.
* **Company Location (page 7)**: Select the geographical location of your business.
* **Number of Employees (page 8)**: Enter the number of employees you have.
* **Financial Information (page 9)**: Provide a brief financial overview, including revenue and growth rate.
* **Industry (page 10)**: Select the industry your business operates in.
* **Revenue (page 11)**: Enter your business's revenue accurately.
* **Growth Rate (page 12)**: Enter your business's growth rate accurately.
* **Submit (page 13)**: Choose whether you are ready to submit your application or need more time.
* **Confirmation (page 14)**: Confirm that you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments (page 15)**: Add any further comments or information about your application.
* **Company Type (page 16)**: Select the type of your company (e.g. sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation).
* **Business Structure (page 17)**: Select the structure of your business (e.g. family, non-profit, for-profit).
* **Agreement (page 18)**: Confirm that you have read and agree with the terms and conditions.
