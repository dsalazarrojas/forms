# Energy Credit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining energy credits from the government or other organizations that provide incentives to reduce energy consumption and expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of energy credit you are applying for: Credit, Rebate, or Incentive.
2. Provide your energy account details, including account ID and account number.
3. Enter your account address and city.
4. Enter your state and zip code.
5. Specify the authorized user, date, and time for the authorization.
6. Provide the authorized reason for the energy credit.
7. Specify the energy credit amount (if applicable).
8. Enter the start and end dates for the energy credit.
9. Specify the frequency of energy credit (if applicable).
10. Add any additional energy credit notes or comments.
11. Confirm that the form has been submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Energy Account Details** (`energy_account_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the energy account type (Electricity, Gas, or Both).
* **Account ID** (`account_id`, number, required): Enter your account ID.
* **Account Number** (`account_number`, text, optional): Enter your account number.
* **Account Address** (`account_address`, text, optional): Enter your account address.
* **City** (`account_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`account_state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (`account_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Authorized User** (`authorized_user`, text, optional): Enter the authorized user's name.
* **Authorized Date** (`authorized_date`, date, optional): Enter the authorized date.
* **Authorized Time** (`authorized_time`, time, optional): Enter the authorized time.
* **Authorized Reason** (`authorized_reason`, text, optional): Enter the authorized reason.
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, note, optional): Provide a handwritten signature from the authorized user.
* **Energy Credit Details** (`energy_credit_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional energy credit details.
* **Energy Credit Application** (`energy_credit_application`, select_one, required): Select the type of energy credit you are applying for (Credit, Rebate, or Incentive).
* **Energy Credit Amount** (`energy_credit_amount`, number, optional): Enter the energy credit amount.
* **Energy Credit Start Date** (`energy_credit_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date for the energy credit.
* **Energy Credit End Date** (`energy_credit_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date for the energy credit.
* **Energy Credit Frequency** (`energy_credit_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of the energy credit.
* **Energy Credit Notes** (`energy_credit_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional energy credit notes.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, text, optional): Confirm that the form has been submitted.
