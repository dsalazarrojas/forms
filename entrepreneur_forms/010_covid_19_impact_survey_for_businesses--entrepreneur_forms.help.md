# COVID 19 Impact Survey For Businesses - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather data on the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on businesses. It will ask you about your business's details, how the pandemic affected your revenue, operational changes you made, and your recovery outlook.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, as your input will help us understand the pandemic's impact on businesses.
2. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the text fields to provide more information.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please choose the most appropriate answer based on your business's experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (required): Enter your business's name as it is known to the public.
* **Owner or Manager Name** (required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business.
* **Email Address** (required): Enter your business's email address.
* **Phone Number** (required): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Industry or Business Sector** (required): Select the category that best describes your business (e.g., Retail, Food Service, etc.).
* **Business Size** (required): Select the number of employees in your business.
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your revenue?** (required): Choose how COVID-19 has affected your revenue.
* **Approximate percentage change** (optional): If you answered that COVID-19 has impacted your revenue, enter the approximate percentage change.
* **Has your business been forced to close?** (required): Choose if your business has been forced to close due to the pandemic.
* **Duration of closure (if applicable)** (optional): If your business has been closed, enter the duration of the closure.
* **Impact on employees** (required): Select the ways in which COVID-19 has impacted your employees.
* **What operational changes have you made?** (required): Select the operational changes you made due to the pandemic.
* **Have you invested in technology or digital tools?** (optional): Choose if you have invested in technology or digital tools to adapt to the pandemic.
* **How confident are you in recovery?** (required): Choose your confidence level in your business's recovery.
* **Have you received government assistance?** (optional): Check if you received government assistance.
* **What additional support would help most?** (optional): Enter the additional support you think would be most helpful.
* **Survey Date** (required): Enter the date you completed this survey.
