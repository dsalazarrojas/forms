# Recurring Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect recurring payment information from users for subscription-based services. It ensures that users provide accurate and consistent payment details for regular transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "Enter Your Details" field.
2. Provide your preferred pricing plan in the "Pricing Plan" field.
3. Enter your payment details in the "Enter Payment Details" field.
4. Review and acknowledge the information provided in the "Review and Acknowledge" field.
5. Submit your payment details by clicking the "Submit Payment" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Your Details** (`user_details`, Text, optional): Enter your name and any other personal details required for recurring payments.
* **Pricing Plan** (`plan_details`, Text, optional): Select or describe your preferred pricing plan for recurring payments.
* **Enter Payment Details** (`payment_details`, Text, optional): Enter your payment card or bank account information for recurring payments.
* **Review and Acknowledge** (`review`, Text, optional): Double-check your provided information for accuracy and confirm that it is correct.
* **Submit Payment** (`submit`, Text, optional): Review and finalize your recurring payment information.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, optional): Enter your valid email address to receive payment confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number to receive payment reminders and notifications.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, optional): Choose your preferred payment method, which can be credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal.
