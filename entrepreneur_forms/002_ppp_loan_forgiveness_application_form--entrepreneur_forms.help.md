# PPP Loan Forgiveness Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPP Loan Forgiveness Application Form is used to gather information about a small business or organization seeking forgiveness for a Paycheck Protection Program (PPP) loan. This form is intended to help applicants provide the necessary details to support their loan forgiveness request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required loan details, including the loan amount, term, start date, and end date.
2. Indicate the status of your loan (Active, Inactive, or other).
3. Specify the approved status of your loan (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
4. Enter your business details, including the contact information (phone, email, address), website, and title of the employee submitting the form.
5. Enter employee details, including first name, last name, phone number, email, address, website, and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **1. Loan Details**
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of the PPP loan you are seeking forgiveness for. (Required)
* **Loan Term**: Enter the term of the PPP loan, including start and end dates. (Required)
* **Loan Start Date**: Enter the start date of the PPP loan. (Required)
* **Loan End Date**: Enter the end date of the PPP loan. (Required)
* **Loan Interest Rate**: Enter the interest rate of the PPP loan. (Optional)
* **Loan Interest Rate Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the interest rate is applied (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually). (Optional)
* **Loan Frequency**: Check if the loan was used for a specific frequency (Yes, No). (Optional)
* **Loan Status**: Indicate the current status of your loan (Active, Inactive, or other). (Optional)
* **Approved Status**: Indicate the approved status of your loan (Approved, Denied, Pending). (Optional)
### **2. Business Details**
* **Business Information**: Enter the contact information of your business (phone, email, address), including website and business title. (Optional)
* **Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of your business. (Optional)
* **Business Email**: Enter the email address of your business. (Optional)
* **Business Website**: Enter the website of your business. (Optional)
* **Business Address**: Enter the address of your business. (Optional)
### **3. Employee Details**
* **Employee First Name**: Enter the first name of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Last Name**: Enter the last name of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Title**: Enter the title of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Address**: Enter the address of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Website**: Enter the website of the employee submitting the form. (Optional)
* **Employee Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the employee submitting the form. (Optional)

## Tips

* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact a financial advisor or a qualified professional for guidance.
