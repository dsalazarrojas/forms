## Mental Health Startup Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Startup Accelerator Application Form is designed to gather information about your startup, helping us evaluate your project's potential for success in the mental health space. This form should be completed by the startup's founder or representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your startup's information in the respective fields, such as the stage of your startup (Idea, Prototype, Scaling, etc.).
3. Provide a clear and concise mission statement that defines your startup's purpose and goals.
4. Enter your startup's name and other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What stage is your startup?**: Select the stage of your startup from the options provided (Idea, Prototype, Scaling, Post-Market Fit, etc.). This will help us understand your startup's maturity level and potential for growth.
* **Mission Statement**: Write a brief statement that defines your startup's purpose and goals. This should be a clear and concise summary of your project's mission and objectives.
* **Startup Name**: Enter your startup's name, which should be the official name of your organization.
* **Founders Information**: Provide a brief description of your founding team, including their names, roles, and relevant experience.
* **Team Information**: Enter a brief description of your team members, including their names, roles, and relevant experience.
* **Product/Service Info**: Describe your product or service, including its features, benefits, and any relevant details.
* **Market Info**: Provide an overview of your target market, including demographics, size, and growth potential.
* **Financials**: Enter your startup's financial information, including revenue, expenses, and funding needs.
* **Management Team**: Describe your management team, including their names, roles, and relevant experience.
* **Timeline**: Enter the timeline for your project's development and launch, including key milestones and deadlines.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address for further communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for further communication and updates.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, and optional fields are optional.
* Proofread your responses for spelling and grammar mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the accelerator team for assistance.

This guide will help you complete the Mental Health Startup Accelerator Application Form accurately and effectively. If you need any clarification or support, feel free to reach out to the accelerator team for guidance.
