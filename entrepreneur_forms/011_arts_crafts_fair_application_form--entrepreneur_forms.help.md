# Arts and Crafts Fair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who wish to participate in the Arts and Crafts Fair. It is used to register and submit vendor information, product descriptions, and preferences for booth and electricity requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Vendor Information** page with your basic contact information.
2. Select your preferred **Contact Name**.
3. Enter your **Business Name**.
4. Choose the **Craft Category** that best represents your work.
5. Describe your **Products** in the **Description of Products** field.
6. If applicable, provide links to your **Social Media or Website**.
7. Choose your preferred **Booth Type Preference**.
8. Indicate if you **Require Electricity** for your booth.
9. Make sure you have prepared **Photos of Your Work and Booth Setup**.
10. Finally, confirm your agreement to the **Agreement Terms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Vendor Information -- (1)**: This page is used to provide general information about you and your business.
* **Contact Name (2)**: Enter your name as the contact person for your business.
* **Business Name (3)**: Enter your business name as it will be displayed at the fair.
* **Craft Category (4)**: Select the category that best represents your craft or products.
	+ Jewelry
	+ Pottery/Ceramics
	+ Textiles/Fiber Arts
	+ Woodworking
	+ Painting/Illustration
	+ Glassware
	+ Other
* **Description of Products (5)**: Describe the products you will be selling at the fair.
* **Social Media or Website (6)**: If you have a website or social media, provide the link.
* **Booth Type Preference (7)**: Choose your preferred booth size and type.
	+ Standard 10x10
	+ Corner 10x10
	+ Double 10x20
* **Electricity Required (8)**: Indicate if you need electricity for your booth.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Photos of Your Work and Booth Setup (9)**: Please ensure you have prepared photos of your work and booth setup ready for upload.
* **I Agree to the Fair Rules and Regulations (10)**: Confirm your agreement to the rules and regulations of the fair.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure successful submission.
- Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
- Prepare photos of your work and booth setup before submitting the form.
- Read and agree to the fair rules and regulations before completing this form.
