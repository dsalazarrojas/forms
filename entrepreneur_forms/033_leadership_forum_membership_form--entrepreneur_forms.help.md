# Leadership Forum Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in joining the Leadership Forum. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your form details in the "Form Details" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Describe your professional history in the "Professional History" section.
4. Provide your member details in the "Member Details" section.
5. Click on the "Submit Button" to submit your application.
6. Select the type of form you are submitting, and the date and time of your submission.
7. Choose the type of form you are submitting from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details** (`form_details`, text, required): This is the title of your form.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Professional History** (`professional_history`, text, required): Describe your work experience and qualifications.
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required): This is the main section where you can describe your interest in becoming a member of the Leadership Forum.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, text, required): Clicking this button will submit your application.
* **Date Type** (`date_type`, date, required): Choose the type of date for your submission.
* **Time Type** (`time_type`, time, required): Choose the type of time for your submission.
* **Form Type** (`form_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of form you are submitting (text, integer, or decimal).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for your form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required): Enter the version number of your form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the status of your form (draft, published, or archived).
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Choose the category of your form (Entrepreneur Forms, Business Forms, or Organization Forms).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, as this information will be used to create your membership profile.
- If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, please contact the forum administrators for assistance.
- This form is intended to be completed only once. If you have previously submitted an application, do not submit another.
