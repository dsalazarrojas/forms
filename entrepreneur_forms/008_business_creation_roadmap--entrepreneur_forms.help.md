# Business Creation Roadmap - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Creation Roadmap form is designed to guide entrepreneurs through the process of creating a comprehensive business plan, covering essential aspects such as business concept, target market, goals, funding, and launch strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Founder Information** section with your personal details.
2. Move on to the **Business Idea** section, where you will describe your business concept, name, and description.
3. In the **Target Market** section, identify your ideal customers.
4. Define the problem your business addresses in the **Problem you are solving** field.
5. Outline your competitive advantage in the **Competitive Advantage** field.
6. Set clear goals and milestones for the first three, six, and twelve months in the **Goals and Milestones** section.
7. Determine the necessary initial investment and funding sources in the **Funding and Resources** section.
8. Outline the key skills required for success in the **Key Skills Needed** field.
9. Plan your launch strategy and timeline in the **Launch Information** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Founding Information**: This section collects your personal details as a founder.
  - **Founder Name**: Your full name.
  - **Email Address**: Contact email.
  - **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number.
  - **Number of co-founders**: How many people are starting this business together.

- **Business Idea**:
  - **Business Name**: What will you call your business.
  - **Business Description**: Brief description of your product or service.

- **Target Market**:
  - **Target Market**: Who are your ideal customers?

- **Problem you are solving**:
  - This field should be used to describe the problem your business addresses.

- **Competitive Advantage**:
  - This field is meant to highlight what makes you different from competitors.

- **Goals and Milestones**:
  - **3-month milestone**: What do you want to achieve in 3 months?
  - **6-month milestone**: What about in 6 months?
  - **1-year milestone**: Where do you want to be in one year?

- **Funding and Resources**:
  - **Initial investment needed**: How much capital do you need?
  - **Planned funding sources**: Select all that apply (Personal savings, Bank loan, Investors, Grants, Crowdfunding, Friends and family, Other).

- **Key Skills Needed**:
  - This field is meant to list the expertise required for success.

- **Launch Information**:
  - **Planned launch date**: When do you want to launch?
  - **Launch strategy**: How will you introduce your product to market?
  - **Type of support needed**: Select all that apply (Business planning, Financial management, Legal guidance, Marketing strategy, Technology support, Mentoring, Networking).

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each field with clear and concise information.
* Use specific details and examples when describing your business and goals.
* Review and update your answers as your business evolves.
* This form is a living document, and it should be regularly revised as your business grows and changes.
