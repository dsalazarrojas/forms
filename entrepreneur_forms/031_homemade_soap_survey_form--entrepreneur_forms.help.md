# Homemade Soap Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homemade Soap Survey Form is used to collect customer feedback and data on purchases of homemade soap products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. If you have already filled out this form for a specific purchase, do not resubmit it.
3. Make sure to enter your contact information and purchasing details correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Please enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Soap Type** (`soap_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of soap product purchased.
* **Soap Purchasing Date** (`soap_purchasing_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the soap was purchased.
* **Soap Purchasing Time** (`soap_purchasing_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the soap was purchased.
* **Purchased From** (`purchased_from`, text, optional): Enter where the soap was purchased from.
* **Soap Rating** (`soap_rating`, number, optional): Rate the soap product (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the soap product.
* **Purchased From Name** (`soap_purchased_from_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of where the soap was purchased from.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the soap product or purchase.
* **Purchased From What** (`purchased_from_what`, text, optional): Enter where the soap was purchased from.
* **Soap Purchased From What** (`soap_purchased_from_what`, text, optional): Enter where the soap was purchased from.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Purchased Price** (`soap_purchased_price`, text, optional): Enter the price of the soap product.
* **Purchased Price Currency** (`soap_purchased_price_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency of the soap product's price.
* **Purchased Price Amount** (`soap_purchased_price_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the soap product's price.
* **Purchased Price Currency Code** (`soap_purchased_price_currency_code`, text, optional): Enter the currency code of the soap product's price.
* **Purchased Price Currency Symbol** (`soap_purchased_price_currency_symbol`, text, optional): Enter the currency symbol of the soap product's price.
* **Purchased Price Exchange Rate** (`soap_purchased_price_exchange_rate`, number, optional): Enter the exchange rate of the soap product's price.
* **Soap Purchased Price Value** (`soap_purchased_price_value`, number, optional): Enter the value of the soap product's price.
* **Soap Purchased Price Value Currency** (`soap_purchased_price_value_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency of the soap product's value.
* **Soap Purchaser** (`soap_purchaser`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who purchased the soap product.
* **Purchased From Who** (`purchased_from_who`, text, optional): Enter the name of where the soap was purchased from.
