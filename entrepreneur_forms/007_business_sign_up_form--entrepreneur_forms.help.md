# Business Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your business for the purpose of creating an account or signing up for a service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the relevant options for each field from the dropdown lists.
4. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next section of the form.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (\*): Enter your company or business name.
* **Type of Business** (\*): Select the type of business you have (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, Startup, Nonprofit, Other).
* **First Name** (\*): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (\*): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (\*): Enter your business or personal email address.
* **Phone Number** (\*): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Create a Password** (\*): Enter a password with at least 8 characters, including letters and numbers.
* **Confirm Password** (\*): Re-enter your password to ensure accuracy.
* **Industry** (\*): Select the industry your business belongs to (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Retail, Manufacturing, Services, Education, Real Estate, Hospitality, Other).
* **Business Address** (\*): Enter your business street address.
* **City** (\*): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (\*): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (\*): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Country** (\*): Enter your country.
* **Business Website (optional)**: Enter your business website URL if you have one.
* **Number of Employees**: Select the number of people working at your business (Just me, 2-5 employees, 6-10 employees, 11-50 employees, 51-100 employees, 100+ employees).
* **Annual Revenue (optional)**: Select your annual revenue level (Less than $10,000, $10,000 - $50,000, $50,000 - $100,000, $100,000 - $500,000, $500,000 - $1 million, Over $1 million).
* **How did you hear about us?** (\*): Select how you heard about the company (Social media, Search engine, Referral from friend, Advertisement, Industry event, Email marketing, Other).
* **I agree to the Terms and Conditions** (\*): Check the box to accept the terms and conditions.
* **Subscribe to our newsletter**: Select whether you want to subscribe to the company newsletter (Yes, subscribe me, No, do not subscribe).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (\*).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact the company for assistance.
