# Beta Product Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beta Product Feedback Form is a collection of questions designed to gather information about your experience with our beta product. Your input will help us refine and finalize the product to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your name and email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up communication.
2. Select the beta product you are providing feedback for.
3. Answer the questions about your experience with the product, including how long you've used it, your overall impression, and your satisfaction with its usability, performance, and design.
4. List any features you found useful or problematic.
5. Describe any issues you encountered while using the product.
6. Answer questions about your satisfaction with the product's performance, design, and documentation quality.
7. If you've had contact with our customer support, rate your experience.
8. Decide if the product provides value to you or your business.
9. Suggest improvements to the product.
10. Let us know if you would continue to use the product after its finalization.
11. Decide if you would recommend the product to others.
12. Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name to allow us to contact you for follow-up communication.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for follow-up communication.
* **Beta Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Select the beta product you are providing feedback for.
* **How Long Have You Used the Product** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you've used the product from the following options: Less than 1 week, 1 to 2 weeks, 2 to 4 weeks, 1 to 2 months, Over 2 months.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, select_one, required): Rate your overall impression of the product from the following options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Usability Rating** (`usability_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of use of the product from the following options: Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Somewhat Difficult, Very Difficult.
* **Feature Completeness** (`feature_completeness`, select_one, required): Rate the completeness of features from the following options: Has All Features Needed, Has Most Features Needed, Has Some Features Needed, Lacks Most Features, Lacks All Features.
* **What Features Did You Like** (`liked_features`, text, required): List the features you found most useful.
* **What Features Did You Dislike** (`disliked_features`, text, optional): List any features you found problematic.
* **What Issues Did You Encounter** (`issues_encountered`, text, required): Describe any bugs or problems you encountered while using the product.
* **Performance Satisfaction** (`performance_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the product's performance from the following options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Design Satisfaction** (`design_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the product's design from the following options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Documentation Quality** (`documentation_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of help documentation from the following options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not Available.
* **Customer Support Experience** (`customer_support`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with customer support from the following options: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Did Not Contact Support.
* **Does This Product Provide Value** (`value_proposition`, select_one, required): Decide if the product provides value to you or your business from the following options: Extremely Valuable, Very Valuable, Somewhat Valuable, Not Very Valuable, No Value.
* **Improvements Needed** (`improvements_needed`, text, required): Suggest improvements to the product.
* **Would You Continue Using** (`would_continue_using`, select_one, required): Decide if you would continue to use the product after its finalization from the following options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Decide if you would recommend the product to others from the following options: Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
