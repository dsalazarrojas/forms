# value_proposition_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help you understand and communicate your product's value proposition, including its benefits, features, and unique selling points. A well-defined value proposition is essential for creating a compelling message that resonates with your target audience and sets your product apart from competitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. For each field, provide a brief description of your product's value proposition, focusing on its benefits, features, and unique selling points.
2. Be as specific and detailed as possible, using bullet points or short paragraphs to support your answers.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you have covered all relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter your product's name, a brief title that summarizes its core offering.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your product, including its key features and benefits.
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, text, required): Define your product's value proposition, explaining how it addresses customer pain points and offers benefits.
* **Customer Segment** (`customer_segment`, text, required): Identify your target customer segment, including demographics, needs, and preferences.
* **Customer Pain Point** (`customer_pain_point`, text, required): Describe the pain points or challenges your product addresses in the customer's life.
* **Customer Benefit** (`customer_benefit`, text, required): Outline the benefits your product offers to customers, including its unique value proposition.
* **Product Pain Point** (`product_pain_point`, text, required): Describe the pain points or challenges your product addresses in the business or operational process.
* **Product Benefit** (`product_benefit`, text, required): Outline the benefits your product offers to the business or operational process.
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, text, required): Define your business model, including revenue streams and key activities.
* **Key Partners** (`key_partners`, text, required): Identify key partners or collaborators that contribute to your business model.
* **Revenue Model** (`revenue_model`, text, required): Outline your revenue model, including pricing strategies and revenue streams.
* **Key Moment** (`key_moment`, text, required): Identify a key moment or milestone that drives sales and revenue.
* **Key Partners 1** (`key_partners_1`, text, optional): Provide additional information about key partners or collaborators (optional).
* **Key Partners 2** (`value_proposition_survey_key_partners_2`, text, optional): Provide more details about key partners or collaborators (optional).

## Tips

* Use clear and concise language to communicate your product's value proposition.
* Be specific about the benefits and features that make your product unique.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
* Use bullet points or short paragraphs to support your answers where possible.
