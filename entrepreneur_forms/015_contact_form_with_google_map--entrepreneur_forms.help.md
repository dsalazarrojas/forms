# Contact Form With Google Map - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to contact the business or schedule a visit. Please fill out the form to provide necessary information and help us understand your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Type a brief message in the "Message" field describing your request.
4. If you plan to visit the location, select "True" in the "Interested in visiting the location" field.
5. If you have a preferred visit date, select it in the "Preferred visit date" field.
6. Enter your phone number for scheduling purposes (optional).
7. Use the embedded Google Map to confirm your location.
8. Add any additional details in the "Additional details" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, required): Enter your full name for correspondence.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, required): Provide your best email address for us to reach you.
* **Message** (`Message`, required): Briefly describe your request or question.
* **Interested in visiting the location** (`Interested in visiting the location`, optional): Select "True" if you plan to visit us.
* **Preferred visit date** (`Preferred visit date`, optional): Choose a date for your visit.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, optional): Enter your phone number for scheduling purposes.
* **Map location displayed** (`Map location displayed`, required): Use this field to confirm your location on the map.
* **Additional details** (`Additional details`, optional): Add any extra information to help us understand your request.
Tips:
* Make sure to enter your name and email address correctly for proper correspondence.
* Use the map to confirm your location before submitting the form.
* If you plan to visit, select a preferred visit date for scheduling purposes.
