# Owner Operator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Owner Operator Application Form is designed to collect personal details from individuals or businesses that want to be registered as owner operators. This form will help our team verify and validate your identity for our records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your current address in the **address** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **email** field (this will be used for verification).
5. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **last_name**: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **address**: Enter your current address or the address you want to use for registration.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use for verification purposes. This will be used to send you updates and notifications.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any issues or questions, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
- This form is for internal use only and does not collect any sensitive or financial information.
