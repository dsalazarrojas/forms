# Escape Room Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Waiver form is used to collect information from participants, ensuring they are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the escape room experience. This form is essential for their safety and to provide emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Provide your emergency contact information, including your relationship to the participant.
3. List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
4. Enter your emergency contact's contact information.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your waiver of liability and participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waiver** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of your understanding of the escape room experience and your agreement to participate.
* **Participant Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Participation** (3, date, required): Enter the date you plan to participate in the escape room.
* **Time of Participation** (4, time, required): Enter the time you plan to participate in the escape room.
* **Email** (5, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (6, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (7, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (8, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the emergency contact (Parent/Guardian, Spouse, or Emergency Contact).
* **Medical Conditions** (9, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Device** (10, text, optional): List any medical devices you use (e.g., pacemaker, wheelchair).
* **Allergy** (11, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Insurance** (12, text, optional): List your insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance Provider** (13, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (14, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (15, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the emergency contact (Child, Spouse, Parent/Guardian, or Emergency Contact).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (16, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact City** (17, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State** (18, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (19, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's zip code.
* **Participant Signature** (20, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.
* **Participant Date Signed** (21, date, optional): Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Participant Time Signed** (22, time, optional): Enter the time you sign the form.
* **Employee Signature** (23, text, optional): Sign and date the form if you are an employee of the escape room company.
* **Company** (24, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Assigned Form** (25, text, optional): Enter the name of the form assigned to you.
