# product_innovation_incubator_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help potential applicants for the product innovation incubator program provide their information, ideas, and goals. This form gathers essential details to assess their application and determine the suitability of their project for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please proceed as follows:

1. Fill out the **Incubator Information** page with a brief overview of your project.
2. On the **Founder Details** page, provide information about the founders of your project.
3. On the **Idea Description** page, describe your product or innovation idea in detail.
4. On the **Program Goals** page, outline the goals and objectives of your project.
5. On the **Founders' Experience** page, share your relevant work experience and skills that align with the incubator program requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incubator Information** (`incubator_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the first page where you can provide a brief overview of your project, including its name, description, and any relevant details.
* **Founder Details** (`founder_details`, `text`, required: false): This page is for providing information about the founders of your project, including their names, roles, and contact details.
* **Idea Description** (`idea_description`, `text`, required: false): In this section, describe your product or innovation idea in detail, highlighting its key features, benefits, and uniqueness.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, `text`, required: false): Define the goals and objectives of your project, explaining how it aligns with the incubator program's mission and requirements.
* **Founders' Experience** (`founder_details_2`, `text`, required: false): Share your relevant work experience, skills, and achievements that demonstrate your ability to lead a successful project.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your responses to help us better understand your project.
* Use specific examples and details to demonstrate your project's potential.
* If you need more space to elaborate on any of the fields, feel free to attach supporting documents or links to your application.
* Make sure to proofread your responses carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
