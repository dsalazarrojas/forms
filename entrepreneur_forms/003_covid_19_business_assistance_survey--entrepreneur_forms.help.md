# Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about how COVID-19 has impacted your business, including its operational status, revenue, employee count, and financial needs. The data collected will help us understand the effects of the pandemic on your business and provide relevant assistance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your business information in the first section.
2. Answer the questions about the impact of COVID-19 on your business in the second section.
3. Describe your financial needs in the third section.
4. Provide information about your recovery outlook and additional challenges in the final section.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Business Information
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter your business name as it is registered or commonly known.
* **Owner/Manager Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the owner or manager of your business.
* **Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter your business's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business you operate (e.g., Retail, Restaurant - Food Service, etc.).
* **Years Operating** (`years_operating`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.

### Section 2: COVID-19 Impact
* **How has COVID-19 impacted your business revenue?** (`revenue_impact`, select_one, required): Select how COVID-19 has affected your business revenue (e.g., Increased, No significant change, Decreased, etc.).
* **Approximate percentage change in revenue** (`revenue_change_percentage`, number, required): Enter the approximate percentage change in revenue due to COVID-19.
* **Number of employees affected by closures or reduced hours** (`employees_affected`, number, required): Enter the number of employees affected by closures or reduced hours.
* **Current operational status** (`operations_status`, select_one, required): Select your business's current operational status (e.g., Fully open, Partially open, Closed temporarily, etc.).

### Section 3: Financial Needs
* **What type of assistance does your business need?** (`assistance_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select the types of assistance your business requires (e.g., Payroll assistance, Rent or mortgage relief, etc.).
* **Estimated amount of assistance needed** (`estimated_assistance_amount`, number, required): Enter the approximate amount of assistance required.

### Section 4: Recovery and Outlook
* **How long do you expect recovery to take?** (`recovery_timeline`, select_one, required): Select the expected recovery timeline (e.g., 1-3 months, 3-6 months, etc.).
* **What additional challenges are you facing?** (`additional_challenges`, text, required): Describe any additional challenges your business is facing due to COVID-19.
* **What kind of support would help most?** (`support_needed`, text, required): Describe the type of support that would be most helpful for your business recovery.
