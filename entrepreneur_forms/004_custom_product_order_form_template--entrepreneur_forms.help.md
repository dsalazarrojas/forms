# custom_product_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "custom_product_order_form_template" form is designed for customers to provide order information, customer details, delivery details, payment information, shipping information, product options, and review/confirmation for their orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Order Information" section with the necessary details about your order.
2. Provide your "Customer Info" in the next section, including any relevant contact information.
3. In the "Delivery Details" section, enter your delivery information to ensure timely and accurate delivery of your order.
4. In the "Payment Info" section, provide your payment method and details.
5. In the "Shipping Info" section, enter your shipping information to ensure accurate delivery.
6. The "Product Options" section is where you can specify any special requirements or options for your order.
7. Finally, in the "Review" section, provide any feedback or comments about your order.
8. Once you have completed all the sections, you can review your order in the "Order Confirmation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Information
* **Order Information** (`product_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your order, including any relevant details.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other contact information.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your delivery address, including city, state, zip, and any special delivery instructions.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card number, PayPal) and any other payment details.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your shipping address, including city, state, zip, and any special shipping instructions.
* **Product Options** (`product_options`, text, required/optional): Specify any special requirements or options for your order.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required/optional): Enter any feedback or comments about your order.
* **Order Confirmation** (`order_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Review your order summary and confirm or make changes as needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any errors or delays in processing your order.
* If you have any special requirements or options for your order, please specify them in the "Product Options" section.
* If you have any feedback or comments, please enter them in the "Review" section.
