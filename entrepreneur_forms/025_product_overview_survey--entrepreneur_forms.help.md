# Product Overview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for creating a product overview by collecting essential details about the product, such as its title, name, description, price, and other relevant information. This form will help users provide a comprehensive view of the product for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter the product title in the "Survey Title" field.
3. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
5. Enter the product price in the "Product Price" field.
6. Upload a product image in the "Product Image" field.
7. List the product features in the "Product Features" field.
8. Describe the product benefits in the "Product Benefits" field.
9. Enter the product rating in the "Product Rating" field.
10. Provide a review of the product in the "Product Review" field.
11. Specify the product warranty in the "Product Warranty" field.
12. Enter the return policy of the product in the "Product Return Policy" field.
13. Describe the product satisfaction level in the "Product Satisfaction" field.
14. Enter the contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
15. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
16. Enter the product ID in the "Product ID" field.
17. Select the product type from the "Product Type" field.
18. Select the product status from the "Product Status" field.
19. Select the product category from the "Product Category" field.
20. Select the product subcategory from the "Product Subcategory" field.
21. Enter the product tags in the "Product Tags" field.
22. Provide a long description of the product in the "Long Description" field.
23. Enter long tags for the product in the "Long Tags" field.
24. List the product bullet points in the "Product Bullet Points" field.
25. Enter a list of bullet points for the product in the "Product Bullet Points List" field.
26. Enter the product comments in the "Product Comments" field.
27. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
28. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a brief title for the survey to identify the product.
* **Product Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the product.
* **Product Description** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product.
* **Product Price** (`id: 4`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the price of the product.
* **Product Image** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Upload a relevant image of the product.
* **Product Features** (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false): List the essential features of the product.
* **Product Benefits** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Describe the benefits of the product.
* **Product Rating** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the rating of the product.
* **Product Review** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide a review of the product.
* **Product Warranty** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Specify the warranty for the product.
* **Product Return Policy** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the return policy of the product.
* **Product Satisfaction** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): Describe the level of satisfaction with the product.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Product ID** (`id: 15`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the unique ID of the product.
* **Product Type** (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of the product from the options.
* **Product Status** (`id: 17`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the product from the options.
