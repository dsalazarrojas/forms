# small-business-credit-application-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from small business owners to determine their credit request requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and up-to-date information for all the required fields.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that the information provided is relevant to your business needs.
3. If you need help or have any questions, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required): Enter your business's contact information, including your email address. This will help us to get in touch with you regarding your credit request.
* **Credit Request** (`credit_request`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the following credit request options: Credit Type, Credit Amount, or Credit Term.
* **Credit Type** (`credit_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of credit you are requesting (Line of Credit, Term Loan, Revolving Credit, or Working Capital).
* **Credit Amount** (`credit_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of credit you are requesting.
* **Credit Term** (`credit_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the term for your credit (Short-Term, Medium-Term, or Long-Term).
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, `text`, optional): Enter additional information about your business, such as your business's mission and vision.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, `text`, optional): Enter additional information about the owner of your business, such as their name and contact information.
* **Business Financials** (`business_financials`, `text`, optional): Enter information about your business's financials, such as its revenue and expenses.
* **Financial Statements** (`financial_statements`, `text`, optional): Upload or enter information about your business's financial statements, such as balance sheets and income statements.
* **Loan Agreement** (`loan_agreement`, `note`, optional): Read and agree to the loan agreement terms and conditions.
* **Business Assets** (`business_assets`, `text`, optional): List your business's assets, such as equipment and real estate.
* **Collateral** (`collateral`, `text`, optional): List any collateral you are willing to offer for the loan.
* **Business Risks** (`business_risks`, `text`, optional): Identify any risks associated with your business, such as market risks and operational risks.
* **Business Obligations** (`business_obligations`, `text`, optional): List any obligations or responsibilities your business has, such as tax obligations and environmental regulations.
* **Loan Purpose** (`loan_purpose`, `text`, optional): Enter the purpose of the loan, such as purchasing equipment or hiring staff.
* **Loan Reasons** (`loan_reasons`, `text`, optional): Enter the reasons for your loan request, such as expanding your business or paying off debts.
* **Business Goals** (`business_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter your business's goals, such as increasing revenue or reducing expenses.
* **Loan Outcome** (`loan_outcome`, `text`, optional): Enter the expected outcome of the loan, such as increased revenue or improved cash flow.
* **Loan Review** (`loan_review`, `note`, optional): Review this form carefully and ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the following terms and conditions: Term 6 months, Term 12 months, or Term 24 months.
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `note`, optional): Enter the payment schedule for your loan, including the repayment terms and conditions.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, `select_one`, required): Choose the interest rate for your loan (Fixed 5.00%, Floating 5.25%, or Floating 5.50%).
* **Repayment Schedule** (`repayment_schedule`, `date`, required): Enter the date for your repayment schedule.
* **Final Review** (`final_review`, `note`, optional): Review this form carefully and ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. 

Note: This form has 13 required fields. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
