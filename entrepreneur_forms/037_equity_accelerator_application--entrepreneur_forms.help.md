# Equity Accelerator Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from organizations seeking to accelerate their equity in a specific area or program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Program Type**: Enter the type of program or area in which you are seeking to accelerate equity. This is an open-ended text field, so provide as much detail as necessary.
2. **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
3. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact regarding this application.
4. **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
5-8. **Address Lines**: Enter the address information for your organization. You will need to provide up to 4 lines for the address.
9. **City**: Enter the city where your organization is located.
10. **State**: Enter the state where your organization is located.
11. **Zip**: Enter the ZIP code where your organization is located.
12. **Country**: Enter the country where your organization is located.
