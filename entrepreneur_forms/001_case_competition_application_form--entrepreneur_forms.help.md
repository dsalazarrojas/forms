# Case Competition Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Competition Application Form is designed to collect information about your background, interests, and proposed solution for the competition. This information will be used to evaluate your eligibility and potential for participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with a * (star), and provide as much detail as possible for the non-required fields.
2. Enter your contact information (applicant full name, email address, and phone number) to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding updates and next steps.
3. Describe your university or company affiliation and program of study or department (if applicable).
4. If applicable, list the names of team members you are working with.
5. Write a 250-word summary of your proposed case approach.
6. Mention any relevant experience, such as prior competitions, internships, or projects.
7. Provide your availability dates for the competition.
8. Finally, electronically sign by entering your name, confirming you have read and agree to the participation terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Email address**: Provide a contact email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications. This should be a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone number**: Include your local or international phone number, including the country code. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **University or company**: Enter the name of your institution or employer. This could be your university, company, or organization you are working with.
* **Program of study or department**: If you are a student, enter your major or department name. If you are working in a company, enter your department or team name.
* **Team members**: If you are working in a team, list the names of your team members, separated by commas. If you are working solo, leave this field blank.
* **Project summary**: Provide a 250-word summary of your proposed case approach. Be concise and clear in your description.
* **Relevant experience**: Mention any relevant experience, such as prior competitions, internships, or projects that demonstrate your skills and expertise.
* **Availability dates**: Enter the dates you are available to participate in the competition. This will help us schedule meetings and events with you.
* **Electronic consent and signature**: By entering your name, you are confirming that you have read and agree to the participation terms. Please be sure to read and understand the terms before submitting your application.
