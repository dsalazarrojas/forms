# Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide their information for meal kit delivery subscription. It helps us to process their order and deliver meal kits to their desired address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Enter your Email
4. Enter your Phone Number
5. Enter your Address
6. Enter your Address 2 (Additional address details)
7. Enter your Postal Code
8. Enter your City
9. Enter your State
10. Enter your Country
11. Add any additional Notes (if necessary)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (required/optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (required/optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you during the subscription process.
* **Address** (required/optional): Enter your street address where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **Address 2** (required/optional): Enter any additional address details such as apartment or building number.
* **Postal Code** (required/optional): Enter your postal code for postal delivery.
* **City** (required/optional): Enter the city where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **State** (required/optional): Enter the state where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **Country** (required/optional): Enter the country where you would like to receive meal kits.
* **Notes** (required/optional): Add any additional information that we should know about your subscription (e.g., special requests or instructions).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns during the subscription process, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Our team will be happy to help you with any questions or concerns you may have.
