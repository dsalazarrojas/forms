# Digital Creator Sponsorship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Digital Creator Sponsorship Application Form, which is used to gather essential information from digital creators who wish to be sponsored by the company. The form is designed to help us review and determine the suitability of the creator for sponsorship opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your creator details**: On page 1, provide your basic information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. **Provide audience metrics**: On page 2, enter your audience metrics, which will help us understand the reach and engagement of your content.
3. **Share your campaign ideas**: On page 3, describe your campaign ideas and how they align with our company's goals.
4. **Share your contact information**: On page 4, enter your email address and phone number.
5. **Specify your campaign budget**: On page 5, provide an estimate of the budget you would need for the campaign.
6. **Indicate your social media presence**: On page 6, select the social media platforms you are active on.
7. **Submit your application**: On page 7, enter any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Creator Details
* **Creator Details** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a creator.
### Page 2: Audience Metrics
* **Audience Metrics** (`audience_metrics`, `number`, required: false): Enter the metrics that describe your audience, such as the number of followers or views.
### Page 3: Campaign Ideas
* **Campaign Ideas** (`campaign_ideas`, `text`, required: false): Describe your campaign ideas and how they align with our company's goals.
### Page 4: Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number.
### Page 5: Campaign Budget
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the budget you would need for the campaign.
### Page 6: Social Media
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the social media platforms you are active on.
### Page 7: Submission
* **Submission** (`submission`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not previously submitted an application and are applying for a new opportunity.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this information will be used to review and determine the suitability of your application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
