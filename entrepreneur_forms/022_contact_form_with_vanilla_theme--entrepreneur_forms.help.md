# Contact Form With Vanilla Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to quickly contact the administrator with their inquiries, suggestions, or messages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Provide a short subject to describe the purpose of your message.
3. Type a detailed message with the information or question you wish to convey.
4. If you have any additional context or information, you can include it in the "Additional information" field.
5. Optionally, specify a time window for the administrator to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Simple Contact Form --** (`greeting`): A brief introduction to the form, indicating its purpose.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email address** (`email`): Enter your email address so the administrator can respond to you.
* **Subject** (`subject`): Briefly describe the purpose of your message or question.
* **Message** (`message`): Provide a detailed description of your inquiry or question.
* **Preferred contact time** (`contact_time`): If you'd like to specify a time window for the administrator to follow up with you, enter it here.
* **Additional information** (`additional_info`): If you have any extra context or information to share, enter it here.
