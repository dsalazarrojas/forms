# Business Insight Accelerator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Insight Accelerator Application Form is used to gather information from entrepreneurs applying to the Business Insight Accelerator program. It helps our team understand your company's stage, growth, and goals, which is essential for the review and potential acceptance process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling in your company's name, which should be the legal or trading name.
2. Enter the name of the primary founder or CEO.
3. Choose the current stage of your company from the provided options (Idea, Prototype, Revenue, or Scaling).
4. Briefly describe your company's key metrics demonstrating traction.
5. Estimate the approximate total addressable market.
6. Enter the total funds raised to date (if any).
7. Specify the expected outcomes or goals from participating in the program.
8. Enter the number of full-time employees in your company.
9. Provide your email address for program communications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company name (Company name)** (id: 2)
  • Enter the legal or trading name of your company.
  • This field is required to ensure we understand your company's identity.
- **Founder name (Founder name)** (id: 3)
  • Enter the name of the primary founder or CEO.
  • This field helps us understand who is leading your company.
- **Company stage (Company stage)** (id: 4)
  • Select the current stage of your company from the provided options:
    - Idea: Your company is still in the idea phase, with no product or service offered.
    - Prototype: You have a minimum viable product (MVP) or a working prototype.
    - Revenue: Your company is generating revenue.
    - Scaling: Your company is expanding rapidly, often with a large customer base or significant revenue growth.
  • This field is required to help us understand your company's growth stage.
- **Traction summary (Traction summary)** (id: 5)
  • Provide a brief description (a few sentences) of your company's key metrics demonstrating traction.
  • Traction can include factors such as revenue, user acquisition, retention, or other relevant metrics.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand your company's growth and achievements.
- **Market size (Market size)** (id: 6)
  • Estimate the approximate total addressable market.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand your company's potential market reach.
- **Funding to date (Funding to date)** (id: 7)
  • Enter the total funds raised to date (if any).
  • This field is optional but helps us understand your financial situation.
- **Goals for accelerator (Goals for accelerator)** (id: 8)
  • Specify the expected outcomes or goals from participating in the program.
  • This field is required to help us understand your expectations from the program.
- **Team size (Team size)** (id: 9)
  • Enter the number of full-time employees in your company.
  • This field is optional but helps us understand your company's scale and resources.
- **Contact email (Contact email)** (id: 10)
  • Enter your email address for program communications.
  • This field is required for us to contact you about your application and program updates.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Be brief but clear in your answers to help us quickly understand your company and goals.
- If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
