# Contact Form With Wooden Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a contact form designed for small businesses and individuals to connect with us about our services or products. Fill in the form with your details and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form header to start filling out the form.
2. Fill in your name in the "Name" field. This is required so we can get in touch with you.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This is where we'll send our response.
4. If you'd like, provide your phone number in the "Phone number" field for an alternative contact method.
5. If you're representing a business, enter the business name in the "Business name" field.
6. Select the type of inquiry you're making from the "Inquiry type" dropdown list.
7. Write a clear message about your request in the "Message" field. This is crucial for us to understand your needs.
8. If you have any additional information or clarification, add it in the "Additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Contact Us --**:
    This is the form header with a rustic theme. Click on it to start filling out the form.
* **Name**:
    Your full name, required for us to contact you.
* **Email address**:
    Your email where we'll send our response, required for our records.
* **Phone number**:
    Optional contact number for alternative communication.
* **Business name**:
    If you're contacting on behalf of a business, enter its name here.
* **Inquiry type**:
    Choose the nature of your request from the dropdown list.
* **Message**:
    Provide a clear message about your request, this is crucial for us to understand your needs.
* **Additional notes**:
    Any other information that can help us respond to your request.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can get back to you efficiently.
- If you have any attachments or files to send, you can use our "Contact Us" email address or alternative methods for submission.
- If you have any further questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us through other means.
