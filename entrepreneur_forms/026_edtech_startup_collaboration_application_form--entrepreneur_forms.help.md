# edtech_startup_collaboration_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The edtech_startup_collaboration_application_form is a tool for edtech startups and professionals to gather information and collaborate on their projects. It collects data on project title, category, description, team size, start and end dates, assigned tool, collaboration and communication methods, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a short project title in the "title" field.
2. Select the category of your project from the "category" options.
3. Provide a detailed description of your project in the "project_description" field.
4. Enter the number of team members in the "team_size" field.
5. Enter the start date of your project in the "start_date" field.
6. Enter the end date of your project in the "end_date" field.
7. Select the assigned tool for your project from the "assigned_tool" options.
8. Choose how you plan to collaborate with team members in the "collaboration_method" field.
9. Choose how you plan to communicate with team members in the "communication_channel" field.
10. If you have any additional notes, you can add them in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter a short project title.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your project from "Education", "Healthcare", "Finance", "Marketing".
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of team members.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the assigned tool for your project from "Google Workspace", "Microsoft Teams", "Slack".
* **Collaboration Method** (`collaboration_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you plan to collaborate with team members from "Email", "Video calls", "Chat apps".
* **Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you plan to communicate with team members from "Email", "Video calls", "Chat apps".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your project.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Select only the options that apply to your project.
* Enter accurate dates and numbers.
* Use this form for edtech startup collaboration applications only.
