## Purpose
The Virtual Talent Application Form is designed to collect information about a user's virtual talent, including their contact details, educational background, and submission.

## How To Complete This Form

### Complete your contact information:
- Enter your **Name** in the field labeled "Name".
- Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email".
- Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone".

### Provide your educational background:
- Select one option from **Education** that best describes your background.
- Enter the name of your **Education Institution** in the field labeled "Education Institution".

### Describe your work experience and skills:
- Select multiple options from **Work Experience** that describe your work experience.
- Select multiple options from **Skills** that describe your skills.
- Select multiple options from **Languages** that describe the languages you speak.

### Share your portfolio and references:
- Enter your **Portfolio** in the field labeled "Portfolio".
- Select multiple options from **References** that describe your references.

### Finalize your submission:
- Select one option from **Submission** that best describes your submission.
