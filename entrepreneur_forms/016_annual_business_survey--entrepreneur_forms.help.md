# Annual Business Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Business Survey is designed to gather information about your business and its operations. This data will help us better understand your business needs and provide you with relevant insights and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question.
2. Answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. For optional fields, provide information that is relevant to your business.
4. If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): The official name of your business.
* **Contact Person Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): The name of the person completing this survey.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): The email address of your business or the person completing this survey.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): The best phone number to contact your business.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): What type of business is this? (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, Corporation, etc.)
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): What industry does your business operate in? (e.g., Technology, Retail, Manufacturing, etc.)
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `select_one`, required): How long has your business been operating? (e.g., Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, etc.)
* **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`, `number`, required): The total number of full-time and part-time employees.
* **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, required): Approximate annual revenue.
* **Revenue Change from Last Year** (`revenue_change`, `select_one`, required): How did your revenue change?
* **Profit Margin Range** (`profit_margin`, `select_one`, optional): Approximate profit margin.
* **Biggest Business Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, `select_one`, required): What is your primary challenge?
* **Growth Priorities** (`growth_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply (e.g., Increase Sales, Expand Market Reach, etc.)
* **Marketing Channels Used** (`marketing_channels`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply (e.g., Social Media, Email Marketing, Website, etc.)
* **Technology Adoption** (`technology_adoption`, `select_one`, required): How would you describe your tech usage?
* **Customer Satisfaction Level** (`customer_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): How satisfied are your customers?
* **Employee Satisfaction Level** (`employee_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): How satisfied are your employees?
* **Primary Funding Source** (`funding_source`, `select_one`, required): How is your business funded?
* **Expansion Plans** (`expansion_plans`, `select_one`, required): Do you plan to expand?
* **Export Activities** (`export_activities`, `select_one`, required): Do you sell internationally?
* **Sustainability Initiatives** (`sustainability_initiatives`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply (e.g., Recycling Programs, Energy Efficiency, etc.)
* **Business Outlook** (`business_outlook`, `select_one`, required): How optimistic are you about the future?
* **Advice for New Entrepreneurs** (`advice_for_entrepreneurs`, `text`, optional): Share your best advice.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information to share.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Provide enough information to help us understand your business needs.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask for clarification.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
