# testimonial_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The testimonial form is a tool to collect genuine feedback and reviews from customers. It allows customers to provide a public or private testimonial, along with supporting media such as images or videos. This helps the business to understand its customers' opinions and improve its services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Select whether the testimonial is public or private.
- Provide a written testimonial about your experience with the business.
- Optionally, upload an image or video that supports your testimonial.
- Ensure you fill out all required fields (public testimonial, testimonial, and video embed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is it public** (`testimonial_public`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether your testimonial will be visible to everyone or kept private.
* **Is it private** (`testimonial_private`, `select_one`, optional): If you select 'Private', your testimonial will only be visible to authorized personnel.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, `note`, required): Write a genuine and detailed account of your experience with the business.
* **Image upload** (`image_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload a relevant image that supports your testimonial (e.g., a screenshot or a photo).
* **Video upload** (`video_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload a video that supports your testimonial (e.g., a review or a testimonial video).
* **Video Embed** (`testimonial_video_embed`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of a video that supports your testimonial (e.g., a YouTube link).
