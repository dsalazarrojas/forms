# paypal_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Purchase Order Form is used to collect information from users making a payment through PayPal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
* Provide your Shipping Address in the "Shipping Address" field.
* Select your country of residence in the "Country" field.
* Enter your Email Address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter the city of your shipping address in the "Shipping City" field.
* Select the payment method you would like to use (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* Enter the amount you would like to pay in the "Payment Amount" field.
* Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field (this is a free-form text field for any additional information you want to provide).
* Finally, click "Submit" to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Shipping Address (text)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Country (select one)**: Select 'Yes' if you are making a payment through PayPal, 'No' otherwise.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping City (text)**: Enter the city of your shipping address.
* **Payment Method (select one)**: Select the payment method you would like to use.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Payment Details (note)**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **Submit (text)**: Click to complete your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
