# Client Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from clients to understand their business, social media goals, and content needs. This form helps us tailor our content creation services to meet their specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and fill out as much information as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submission.
3. Use the "Select All That Apply" option for fields that have multiple options.
4. Enter dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business or Brand Name** (Business or Brand Name, Text, Required): Enter your business or brand name as it appears on your website, logo, or marketing materials.
* **Business Owner Name** (Business Owner Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your business or brand.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter your business phone number.
* **Industry** (Industry or Niche, Select One, Required): Select the industry your business operates in from the provided list.
* **Target Audience Description** (Target Audience Description, Text, Required): Describe your ideal customer or follower.
* **Social Platforms** (Which Social Media Platforms Do You Need Content For, Select Multiple, Required): Select all social media platforms you need content for.
* **Current Follower Count** (Current Total Follower Count, Text, Optional): Enter an approximate number of followers you have across all platforms.
* **Primary Content Goals** (Primary Content Goals, Select Multiple, Required): Select the primary goals you want to achieve with social media content.
* **Brand Voice and Tone** (Brand Voice and Tone, Select One, Required): Select how you want your brand to sound.
* **Desired Posting Frequency** (Desired Content Posting Frequency, Select One, Required): Select how often you want to post content.
* **Content Types** (Preferred Content Types, Select Multiple, Required): Select the types of content you prefer.
* **Brand Colors** (Brand Colors, Text, Optional): List your brand colors or hex codes.
* **Brand Guidelines Document URL** (Brand Guidelines Document URL, Text, Optional): If you have a brand guidelines document, enter the URL here.
* **Competitor Social Media Accounts** (Competitor Social Media Accounts, Text, Optional): List 2-3 competitor social media accounts for reference.
* **Content Approval Process** (Who Will Approve Content Before Posting, Text, Required): Enter the name and role of who will approve content before posting.
* **Monthly Content Budget Range** (Monthly Content Budget Range, Select One, Required): Select the budget range for your content creation services.
* **Desired Start Date** (Desired Start Date, Date, Required): Select the desired start date for content creation.
* **Additional Requests or Comments** (Additional Requests or Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any additional requests or comments you may have.

## Tips

* Use the available space to provide as much information as possible to ensure a tailored experience with our services.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
* This form is not a commitment to our services, but rather a guide to get started with the onboarding process.
