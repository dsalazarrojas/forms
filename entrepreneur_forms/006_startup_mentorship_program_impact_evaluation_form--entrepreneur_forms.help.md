# Startup Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Mentorship Program Impact Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the progress, outcomes, and quality of the mentorship programs implemented within the organization. This form gathers data on key aspects of the program, including its duration, quality, outcome, and the number of team members involved. It also allows mentors, mentees, and program managers to provide feedback and insights to improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, refer to the following pages and fields:

*   Go through each page of the form (e.g., Form Data, Start Date, End Date, etc.), and ensure you have all required information available before proceeding.
*   Enter or choose the appropriate values for each field, using the formats specified for each field type.
*   For any field that is not required (indicated by "required: false" in the form metadata), you can either skip it or enter a value if you have the information available.
*   Use the "Submit Comments" field to provide any additional feedback or comments on the form, the program, or any other relevant information that might be useful for the evaluation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Data** (`form_data`): This field is for any general information you may want to include about the program, such as notes or comments.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date when the program started. Use the date format specified in the hint or guidance provided.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the date when the program ended. Use the date format specified in the hint or guidance provided.
*   **Mentorship Program Name** (`mentorship_program_name`): Provide the name of the mentorship program.
*   **Mentorship Program Description** (`mentorship_program_description`): Briefly describe the purpose and scope of the mentorship program.
*   **Mentor** (`mentor`): Enter or choose the name of the mentor involved in the program.
*   **Mentee** (`mentee`): Enter or choose the name of the mentee involved in the program.
*   **Team Member Count** (`team_member_count`): Enter the number of team members that participated in the program.
*   **Program Duration** (`program_duration`): Enter the duration of the program in days, weeks, or months, as applicable.
*   **Program Outcome Rating** (`program_outcome_rating`): Rate the outcome of the program on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Program Quality Rating** (`program_quality_rating`): Rate the quality of the program on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Evaluation Notes** (`evaluation_notes`): Provide any additional notes or comments on the program, its progress, or its outcomes.
*   **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`): Enter the date when the form is being completed.
*   **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on the form, the program, or the evaluation process.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have all necessary information before proceeding to fill the form.
*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible in your responses.
*   Use the formats specified for dates and numbers as instructed.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification on any field, refer to the hint or contact your supervisor or program coordinator.
