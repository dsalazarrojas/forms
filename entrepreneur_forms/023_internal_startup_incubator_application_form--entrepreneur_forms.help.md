# Internal Startup Incubator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Internal Startup Incubator Application Form is designed to gather information about you and your startup idea. This form is used to assess your application and determine the suitability of your startup for our internal incubator program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User** (`text`, Optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **User Name** (`text`, Optional): This is your name as you would like to be referred to within the incubator program.
* **User Email** (`email`, Optional): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached for communication from the incubator team.
* **User Phone** (`text`, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone for additional information or support.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is correct and functional.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the incubator team for assistance.
* You can submit your application multiple times, but please ensure that each submission is a new and unique application.
