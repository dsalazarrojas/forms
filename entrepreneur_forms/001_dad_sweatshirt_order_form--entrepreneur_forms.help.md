# Dad Sweatshirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to place an order for a customized "Dad" sweatshirt. Please fill out the form with your information and preferences to ensure a smooth and accurate order placement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: *First Name*, *Last Name*, *Phone Number*, and *Order Date*.
2. Select the *Sweatshirt Color*, *T-Shirt Size*, *Sweatshirt Type*, *Font Style*, *Font Size*, and *Font Color* according to your preferences.
3. Provide any additional information or instructions in the *Message on front* field.
4. Review your order for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on the sweatshirt.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on the sweatshirt.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and details about your order.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates and details about your order.
- **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
- **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
- **Sweatshirt Color** (`sweatshirt_color`, select_one, optional): Select a color for your sweatshirt from the provided options.
- **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select a size for your sweatshirt from the provided options.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of sweatshirts you would like to order.
- **Message on front** (`sweatshirt_message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or instructions for the design of your sweatshirt.
- **Sweatshirt Type** (`sweatshirt_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of sweatshirt you would like to order from the provided options.
- **Font Style** (`font_style`, select_one, optional): Select the font style for the text on your sweatshirt from the provided options.
- **Font Size** (`font_size`, select_one, optional): Select the font size for the text on your sweatshirt from the provided options.
- **Font Color** (`font_color`, select_one, optional): Select the font color for the text on your sweatshirt from the provided options.
- **Additional Information** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments for your order.
- **Message on front** (`sweatshirt_message`, note, optional): Enter any text you would like to appear on the front of the sweatshirt.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have all necessary information and preferences ready before filling out the form. Double-check your order for accuracy and completeness before submitting. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us!
