# Small Business Cyber Security Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through completing the Small Business Cyber Security Checklist, a tool to help assess your business's cyber security posture. The checklist evaluates key security controls, such as employee privacy, access control, and incident response.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the appropriate options from the drop-down menus for each field.
2. Provide text responses where indicated.
3. Answer all questions thoroughly, but feel free to skip fields with no answer or a "No" response.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Privacy** (`employee_privacy`, select_one): Indicate whether your business ensures that employee data is kept private.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business has policies and procedures in place to protect employee data.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not have such policies or procedures.
* **Access Control** (`access_control`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of access control your business has in place.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of access control your business has, such as:
		- Physical access controls
		- Administrative access controls
		- Network access controls
* **Incident Response** (`incident_response`, select_one): Indicate whether your business has an incident response plan in place.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business has a plan to respond to and contain security incidents.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not have such a plan.
* **Data Backup** (`data_backup`, select_one): Indicate whether your business has a data backup system in place.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business regularly backs up data.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not back up data.
* **Network Security** (`network_security`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of network security measures your business has in place.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of network security measure, such as:
		- Firewalls
		- Intrusion detection systems
		- VPNs
* **User Accounts** (`user_accounts`, select_one): Indicate whether your business has a system for managing user accounts.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business has a system for managing user accounts.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not have such a system.
* **Password Security** (`password_security`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of password security measures your business has in place.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of password security measure, such as:
		- Strong password policies
		- Password rotation
* **Physical Access** (`physical_access`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of physical access controls your business has in place.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of physical access control, such as:
		- Secure doors and locks
		- CCTV cameras
* **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, select_one): Indicate whether your business has a system for reporting security incidents.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business has a system for reporting security incidents.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not have such a system.
* **Security Policies** (`security_policies`, text): Provide a brief description of your business's security policies.
* **Network Disaster Recovery** (`network_disaster_recovery`, date): Indicate the date your business last performed a network disaster recovery test.
* **Security Budget** (`security_budget`, number): Indicate your business's annual security budget.
* **Security Standards** (`security_standards`, select_one): Indicate whether your business adheres to industry security standards.
	+ Select "Yes" if your business adheres to industry security standards.
	+ Select "No" if your business does not adhere to such standards.
* **Incident Response Plan** (`incident_response_plan`, text): Provide a brief description of your business's incident response plan.
* **Security Compliance** (`security_compliance`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of security compliance your business has in place.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of security compliance, such as:
		- Regulatory compliance
		- Industry compliance
* **Employee Training** (`employee_training`, select_multiple): Indicate the types of employee training your business provides.
	+ Select "Yes" for each type of employee training, such as:
		- Security awareness training
		- Incident response training
* **Incident Classification** (`incident_classification`, text): Provide a brief description of the types of incidents your business is prepared to respond to.
