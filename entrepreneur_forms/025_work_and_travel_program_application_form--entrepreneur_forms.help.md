# Work And Travel Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work And Travel Program Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a work and travel program. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your background, skills, and preferences so we can better match you with suitable opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, contact information, and work experience.
2. Briefly describe your educational background.
3. List your relevant skills and any relevant training or certifications.
4. Provide references, such as previous employers or mentors.
5. Select the type of work travel program that interests you the most.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)**: Please enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your primary contact information, including email address and phone number.
* **Work Experience (3)**: Describe your previous work experience, including job titles, dates of employment, and a brief summary of your job responsibilities.
* **Education (4)**: List your highest level of education completed, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Skills (5)**: List any relevant skills or certifications you possess, such as language proficiency or programming languages.
* **References (6)**: Provide the names and contact information of 2-3 professional references, such as previous employers or mentors.
* **Work Travel Program (7)**: Select the type of work travel program that interests you the most from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Submit (8)**: Review and submit your application form for review.
