# Product Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather information from customers about a product they have tested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
3. If you have a product image, please attach it to the corresponding field.
4. Use the options provided in the product rating and product testing satisfaction fields.
5. You can add any additional comments in the survey comment field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: This is the main content of the survey. Please provide a brief description of your experience with the product. (text, optional)
* **Overall Rating**: This field asks you to rate your overall experience with the product on a scale of 1-10. (number, optional)
* **Product Rating**: This field asks you to rate the product out of 5. (number, optional)
* **Product Description**: This field is for any additional description or information about the product. (text, optional)
* **Product Image**: If you have a photo of the product, please attach it here. (text, optional)
* **Product Price**: This field is not applicable for this survey, please leave it blank. (number, optional)
* **Recommendation**: This field asks you to rate if you would recommend the product to others. Please select from the options: Strongly Agree, Neutral, Disagree. (select_one, optional)
* **Survey Comment**: This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about the product. (note, optional)
* **Product Testing Satisfaction**: This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the product testing experience. Please select from the options: High, Medium, Low. (select_multiple, optional)
