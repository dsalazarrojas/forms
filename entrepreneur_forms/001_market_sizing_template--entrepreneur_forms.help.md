# Market Sizing Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Sizing Template is a tool to help entrepreneurs estimate the size and potential of a market they aim to enter. This template is designed to provide a structured approach to gathering essential information about a market, including its current size, growth rate, and total potential. By using this template, entrepreneurs can get a better understanding of the market landscape and make informed decisions about their business plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Market Sizing Template, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about your company on the Company Information page.
2. Enter the current market size on the next page.
3. Answer questions about the revenue potential, entry and exit dates, and exit strategy on subsequent pages.
4. Select the entry barriers for this market on the next page.
5. Enter the growth rate and total potential on the following page.
6. Choose the market segment and location on the next page.
7. Select the customer segment and geographic location on the next page.
8. Enter the total revenue and market presence on the subsequent pages.
9. Confirm the market presence and location on the final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Company Information** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of your company, including its name and address.
- **What is the current market size?** (Number, Required): Enter the current size of the market.
- **What is the revenue potential for this market?** (Select One, Required): Choose the revenue potential for this market, either Low, Medium, or High.
- **When will you enter the market?** (Date, Required): Enter the date you plan to enter the market.
- **When will you exit the market?** (Date, Required): Enter the date you plan to exit the market.
- **What is your exit strategy?** (Select One, Required): Choose the exit strategy for this market, either Exit 1, Exit 2, or Exit 3.
- **What are the entry barriers for this market?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the entry barriers for this market, choose from Barrier 1, Barrier 2, and Barrier 3.
- **What is the growth rate for this market?** (Number, Required): Enter the growth rate for this market.
- **What is the total potential for this market?** (Number, Required): Enter the total potential for this market.
- **What market segment does this belong to?** (Select One, Required): Choose the market segment for this market, either Segment 1 or Segment 2.
- **Where is this market located?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the geographic location of this market, choose from Location 1 and Location 2.
- **Which customer segment does this belong to?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the customer segment for this market, choose from True and False.
- **What is the total revenue for this market?** (Text, Required): Enter the total revenue for this market.
- **Is the market present in this location?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select whether the market is present in the location, choose from True and False.
- **Is this location within the market location?** (Select Multiple, Required): Select whether the location is within the market location, choose from True and False.

Note: Ensure you answer all required fields to get an accurate analysis of your market size and potential.
