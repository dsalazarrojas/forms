# event_vendor_agreement_amendment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The event_vendor_agreement_amendment_form is used to document changes made to event vendor agreements. This form helps ensure that any updates or modifications to the original agreement are recorded and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Vendor Id number to associate the amendment with the original event vendor contract.
2. Select the Event Id to specify the event associated with the amendment.
3. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for Vendor Id to indicate whether the change affects the vendor.
4. Provide a clear reason for the amendment in the Amendment Reason field.
5. Describe the updated terms of the amendment in the Updated Terms field.
6. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Agreement Amendment Signed to confirm whether the amendment has been signed.
7. Enter your name in the Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted By field to identify who submitted the form.
8. Enter the name of the person or entity the form was submitted to in the Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted To field.
9. Fill in the date and time the form was submitted in the Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Date Subscribed and Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Time Subscribed fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Vendor Id** (Event Vendor Id, number, required): This field is used to associate the amendment with the original event vendor contract. Enter the contract number to link the amendment to the correct contract.
* **Contract Date** (Contract Date, date, required): The date of the original contract.
* **Event Id** (Event Id, select_one, required): Select the event id to specify which event this amendment applies to.
* **Vendor Id** (Vendor Id, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the change affects the vendor.
* **Amendment Reason** (Amendment Reason, text, required): Provide a clear reason for the amendment. Describe the reason for the changes made to the original contract.
* **Updated Terms** (Updated Terms, text, required): Describe the updated terms of the amendment. Include all changes made to the original contract.
* **Agreement Amendment Signed** (Agreement Amendment Signed, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm whether the amendment has been signed. If 'Yes' is selected, it indicates that the amendment is complete.
* **Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted By** (Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted By, text, required): Enter your name to identify who submitted the form.
* **Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted To** (Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Submitted To, text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity the form was submitted to.
* **Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Date Subscribed** (Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Date Subscribed, date, required): Fill in the date the form was submitted.
* **Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Time Subscribed** (Event Vendor Agreement Amendment Form Time Subscribed, time, required): Fill in the time the form was submitted.

## Tips
* Ensure that any changes made to the original contract are well-documented and approved before submission.
* Use the correct event id to ensure the amendment is associated with the correct event.
* If the Agreement Amendment Signed field is selected as 'Yes', ensure that the amendment is complete and signed by all parties involved.
