# Directory Information Verification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify and update directory information for individuals or organizations. It is intended for internal use within a company or institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Provide the name of your organization (if applicable).
3. Submit the form for review and verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Directory Information Verification** (`form_title`, text, required: false):
	+ This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description or purpose of the form.
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your directory listing.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your directory listing.
* **Organization** (`form_organization`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of your organization (if you are an employee or representative).

## Tips
* Ensure you only fill out this form if you are authorized to do so.
* Double-check that your directory information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the designated contact person listed at the end of this guide.
