# Payroll System Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll System Access Form is an administrative tool used to manage and control access levels within the payroll system. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure users have the correct level of access to specific modules of the system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have all the necessary information and details required for the submission.
*   Enter your User Name in the designated field.
*   Enter your Employee Email in the designated field.
*   Select the System Access Type that matches your role within the payroll system.
*   Select your desired Access Level(s) for the system modules.
*   Enter your Department (if applicable).
*   Enter your Job Function (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Not Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Employee Email** (`user_email`, Email, Not Required): Enter the email address associated with your account in the payroll system.
*   **System Access Type** (`system_access_type`, Select One, Required): Choose one of the available options to select your role within the payroll system (HR, Payroll, Accounting).
*   **Access Level** (`access_level`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the available levels of access (High, Low). If you are unsure, leave this field blank or select the lowest level required.
*   **Department** (`department`, Text, Not Required): Enter your department as it appears in the payroll system.
*   **Job Function** (`job_function`, Text, Not Required): Enter your job function as it appears in the payroll system.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your system administrator or the person in charge of the payroll system.
*   Ensure you have the correct access level before submitting the form to avoid potential errors or delays in access processing.
*   This form is for administrative purposes and should only be filled out by authorized personnel within the payroll system.
