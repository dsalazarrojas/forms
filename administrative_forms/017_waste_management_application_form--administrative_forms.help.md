# Waste Management Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for waste management applications. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information for efficient processing and handling of waste disposal requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your given name.
	* Last Name: Enter your surname.
	* Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	* Address: Enter your address where you would like to receive services.
	* Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
	* Email: Enter your email address.
	* Company Name: If you are representing a company, enter the company name.
2. Select your service requirements:
	* Service Type: Choose whether you are applying for waste management services ('Yes' or 'No').
	* Service Frequency: Choose how often you would like to receive services ('Yes' or 'No').
	* Service Start Date: Enter the date you would like to begin receiving services.
	* Service End Date: Enter the date you would like services to end.
3. Describe your waste management needs:
	* Waste Type: Choose the type of waste you are dealing with ('Yes' or 'No').
	* Waste Description: Describe the type of waste you are dealing with in detail.
	* Notes: Add any additional details about your waste management needs.
4. Review and confirm:
	* Review Status: Choose whether this application is active or inactive.
	* Reviewer Name: Enter the name of the person reviewing your application.
	* Reviewer Comment: Enter any comments from the reviewer.
	* Review Date: Enter the date of the review (optional).
	* Approval Status: Choose whether your application is approved or not.
	* User Comments: Add any comments you have about your application.
	* Service Status: Choose the status of your service (Active or Inactive).
