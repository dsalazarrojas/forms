# Claim Settlement Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process refund requests for customers who have made payments to our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your refund request.
2. If you are representing a company or organization, please provide the name of the entity.
3. Enter the customer ID or account number associated with your payment, if applicable.
4. Provide the original transaction ID or order number.
5. Specify when the original payment was made.
6. Enter the original transaction amount.
7. Indicate the amount you are requesting for refund.
8. Choose the type of refund you are requesting.
9. Select the primary reason for the refund.
10. Enter a detailed explanation of the situation.
11. Confirm whether you have supporting documents (receipts, photos, etc.) and if you will attach them.
12. Select the preferred method of refund (original payment method, direct deposit, etc.).
13. If you are requesting direct deposit, enter your bank name, account type, and routing number.
14. If you are requesting a check refund, enter your mailing address.
15. Indicate the level of urgency for the refund request.
16. Confirm whether you have contacted us before regarding this issue.
17. If you have contacted us before, enter the date of the previous contact.
18. Select the expected resolution timeframe for the refund request.
19. Provide any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for correspondence regarding your refund request.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Company or Organization**: If you are representing a company or organization, enter the name of the entity.
* **Customer ID or Account Number**: Enter the customer ID or account number associated with your payment, if applicable.
* **Original Transaction ID**: Enter the original transaction ID or order number.
* **Original Transaction Date**: Enter the date the original payment was made.
* **Original Transaction Amount**: Enter the original transaction amount.
* **Refund Amount Requested**: Enter the amount you are requesting for refund.
* **Type of Refund**: Select the type of refund you are requesting (full, partial, overpayment, etc.).
* **Reason for Refund**: Select the primary reason for the refund request.
* **Detailed Explanation**: Provide a detailed description of the situation leading to the refund request.
* **Supporting Documents**: Confirm whether you have supporting documents (receipts, photos, etc.) and if you will attach them.
* **Preferred Refund Method**: Select the preferred method of refund (original payment method, direct deposit, etc.).
* **Bank Name**: If you are requesting direct deposit, enter your bank name.
* **Account Type**: If you are requesting direct deposit, select the account type (checking, savings, etc.).
* **Bank Routing Number**: If you are requesting direct deposit, enter your bank routing number.
* **Bank Account Number**: If you are requesting direct deposit, enter your bank account number.
* **Mailing Address**: If you are requesting a check refund, enter your mailing address.
* **Urgency Level**: Select the level of urgency for the refund request (standard, urgent - financial hardship, etc.).
* **Have You Contacted Us Before About This**: Confirm whether you have contacted us before regarding this issue.
* **Date of Previous Contact**: If you have contacted us before, enter the date of the previous contact.
* **Expected Resolution Timeframe**: Select the expected resolution timeframe for the refund request (within 5 business days, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding your refund request.
