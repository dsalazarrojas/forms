# policy_rollout_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The policy rollout checklist is a form used to track the status and progress of policy implementations. It collects information about new policies, their titles, descriptions, types, owners, statuses, due dates, completion dates, communication plans, and review cycles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields, if any.
2. For each field, provide accurate and relevant information about the policy rollout.
3. Double-check the provided information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Policy** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the new policy.
- **Policy Title** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the policy, briefly describing its purpose and scope.
- **Policy Description** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter a detailed description of the policy.
- **Policy Type** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of policy, selecting from options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Policy Owner** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if the policy is owned by 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Policy Status** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the status of the policy from options Draft, In Development, Published, or Expired.
- **Tasks Due Date** (`7`, `date`, optional): Enter the due date for tasks related to the policy in the format YYYY-MM-YY.
- **Tasks Completed Date** (`8`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when tasks related to the policy were completed in the format YYYY-MM-YY.
- **Communication Plan** (`9`, `text`, optional): Describe the plan for communicating with stakeholders.
- **Communication Date** (`10`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when communication with stakeholders took place in the format YYYY-MM-YY.
- **Review Cycle** (`11`, `text`, optional): Provide the review cycle for the policy.

## Tips
* Be accurate and precise when filling out this form to ensure correct tracking and monitoring of policy rollouts.
* Use the provided options for select and select_multiple fields to ensure consistency in data entry.
* Check the form before submission to avoid errors and ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
