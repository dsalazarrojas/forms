# Community Center Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Safety Inspection Form is a comprehensive tool used by facilities administrators to evaluate and report on the safety status of community centers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

### 1. Inspection Date
*   Enter the date you performed the safety inspection.

### 2. Inspector Name
*   Enter your full name as the inspector.

### 3. Inspector Title
*   Enter your job title or position.

### 4. Facility Area Inspected
*   Select the area of the facility being inspected.

### 5. Fire Extinguisher Status
*   Evaluate the status of fire extinguishers in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: Fire extinguishers are present and inspected.
	+ Non-compliant: Fire extinguishers are not present or not inspected.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 6. Fire Extinguisher Notes
*   Enter any additional details about fire extinguishers found during the inspection.

### 7. Emergency Exits Status
*   Evaluate the status of emergency exits in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: Emergency exits are clear and accessible.
	+ Non-compliant: Emergency exits are not clear or accessible.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 8. Emergency Exits Notes
*   Enter any additional details about emergency exits found during the inspection.

### 9. First Aid Kit Status
*   Evaluate the status of first aid kits in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: First aid kits are stocked and accessible.
	+ Non-compliant: First aid kits are not stocked or not accessible.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 10. First Aid Kit Notes
*   Enter any additional details about first aid kits found during the inspection.

### 11. Electrical Safety Status
*   Evaluate the status of electrical systems in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: Electrical systems are in good condition.
	+ Non-compliant: Electrical systems are not in good condition.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 12. Electrical Safety Notes
*   Enter any additional details about electrical safety issues found during the inspection.

### 13. Plumbing Status
*   Evaluate the status of plumbing fixtures in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: Plumbing fixtures are functioning properly.
	+ Non-compliant: Plumbing fixtures are not functioning properly.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 14. Plumbing Notes
*   Enter any additional details about plumbing issues found during the inspection.

### 15. Structural Integrity Status
*   Evaluate the status of floors, walls, and ceilings in the selected area.
	+ Compliant: Structural integrity is good.
	+ Non-compliant: Structural integrity is not good.
	+ Not Applicable: Not applicable to the selected area.

### 16. Structural Notes
*   Enter any additional details about structural issues found during the inspection.

### 17. Accessibility Compliance
*   Evaluate if the facility is ADA compliant.
	+ Compliant: Facility is ADA compliant.
	+ Non-compliant: Facility is not ADA compliant.
	+ Partially Compliant: Facility is partially ADA compliant.

### 18. Accessibility Notes
*   Enter any additional details about accessibility features or issues.

### 19. Overall Safety Assessment
*   Evaluate the overall safety status of the facility.
	+ Excellent: Facility is in excellent condition.
	+ Good: Facility is in good condition.
	+ Fair: Facility is in fair condition.
	+ Poor: Facility is in poor condition.
	+ Critical Issues Found: Facility has critical issues.

### 20. Corrective Actions Required
*   List any corrective actions needed to address issues.

### 21. Priority Level
*   Evaluate the urgency of required actions.
	+ Immediate: Urgent action required.
	+ High: High-priority action required.
	+ Medium: Medium-priority action required.
	+ Low: Low-priority action required.

### 22. Follow-up Inspection Date
*   Enter the date for a follow-up inspection.

### 23. Inspector Signature
*   Sign your full name as the inspector.

## Tips
*   Make sure to carefully evaluate each area before answering the questions.
*   Enter any additional details or notes where applicable.
*   Use the correct evaluation categories to ensure accurate reporting.
*   Be thorough in your assessment and documentation.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
