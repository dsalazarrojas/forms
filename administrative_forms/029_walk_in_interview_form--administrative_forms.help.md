# Walk In Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Walk In Interview Form is designed to collect information about the walk-in interviews conducted with job applicants. This form is meant to help the interviewer document the details of the interview, ensuring that all relevant information is captured in a consistent and standardized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's name, the job they applied for, and the date and time of the interview.
2. Specify the location where the interview took place.
3. Enter the name of the interviewer.
4. Add any relevant notes about the interview.
5. If applicable, update the candidate's status and follow-up status.
6. Specify the next action required for the candidate.
7. Document any follow-up actions taken, including reported status and follow-up notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.
* **Job Applied** (`job_applied`, text, optional): Enter the name of the job applied for by the candidate.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, number, optional): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Walk in Location** (`walk_in_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the interview took place.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the interviewer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any relevant notes about the interview.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted this form.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the candidate.
* **Candidate Status** (`candidate_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the candidate (Active or Inactive).
* **Status Date** (`status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the candidate status was updated.
* **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple status options (Active or Inactive).
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple follow-up status options (Active or Inactive).
* **Follow-up Interviewer Name** (`follow_up_interviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the follow-up interviewer.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the follow-up interview.
* **Follow-up Submitted By** (`follow_up_submitted_by`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who submitted the follow-up form.
* **Follow-up Contact Info** (`follow_up_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the follow-up.
* **Next Action** (`next_action`, select_one, optional): Select whether the follow-up action is needed (Yes or No).
* **Follow-up Reported** (`follow_up_reported`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up action reported.
* **Follow-up Status Date** (`follow_up_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the follow-up status was updated.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple follow-up status options (Active or Inactive).

By following these instructions, you should be able to complete the Walk In Interview Form accurately and effectively.
