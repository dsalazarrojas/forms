# internship_records_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "internship_records_release_form" form is used to collect information related to the release of student records, specifically in regards to internships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the "student_email" field.
3. Check the "release_consent" field to indicate whether the student has given their consent for the release of their records.
4. Select the date of the record release in the "release_date" field.
5. Enter the name of the organization that the student worked with in the "organization_name" field.
6. Enter the student's identifier (e.g. student ID number) in the "student_identifier" field.
7. Enter the contact name of the person responsible for releasing the records in the "contact_name" field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "contact_email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_phone" field.
10. Enter the identifier of the organization responsible for releasing the records in the "organization_identifier" field.
11. Enter the identifier of the contact person in the "contact_identifier" field.
12. Add any additional notes regarding the record release in the "note" field.
13. Select the type of record being released (e.g. Academic, Internship) in the "record_type" field.
14. Select the specific details of the record release (e.g. Academic, Internship, Other) in the "record_details" field.
15. Select the reason for releasing the records (e.g. Academic, Employment, Other) in the "reason_for_release" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (Text): Enter the student's full name.
* **student_email** (Email): Enter the student's email address.
* **release_consent** (Select One: True/False): Check to indicate whether the student has given their consent for the release of their records.
* **release_date** (Date): Select the date of the record release.
* **organization_name** (Text): Enter the name of the organization that the student worked with.
* **student_identifier** (Text): Enter the student's identifier (e.g. student ID number).
* **contact_name** (Text): Enter the name of the person responsible for releasing the records.
* **contact_email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **organization_identifier** (Text): Enter the identifier of the organization responsible for releasing the records.
* **contact_identifier** (Text): Enter the identifier of the contact person.
* **note** (Note): Add any additional notes regarding the record release.
* **record_type** (Select One: Academic/Internship/Other): Select the type of record being released (e.g. Academic, Internship).
* **record_details** (Select Multiple: Academic/Internship/Other): Select the specific details of the record release.
* **reason_for_release** (Select One: Academic/Employment/Other/Unknown): Select the reason for releasing the records.
