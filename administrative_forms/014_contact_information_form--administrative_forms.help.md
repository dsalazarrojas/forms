# Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Information Form is designed to collect vital details for effective communication and record-keeping. It is intended for internal use, and the information collected will be used to facilitate communication and data management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your title using the prefix options provided (e.g., Mr., Ms., Mrs., Dr., Prof.).
2. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your primary email address.
4. Enter your mobile phone number and business phone number (if applicable).
5. Fill in your street address, city, state/province, and postal/zip code.
6. Select your country.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1: Administrative Contact Details
* **Administrative Contact Details (Note)**: This field is a heading and not a required field. However, it is a vital part of the form to indicate the purpose of the subsequent fields.

### First Name
* **First Name (text, Required):** Enter your given name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name (text, Required):** Enter your surname.

### Primary Email
* **Primary Email (email, Required):** Enter your primary email address for communication.

### Mobile Phone
* **Mobile Phone (text, Required):** Enter your mobile phone number for efficient communication.

### Business Phone
* **Business Phone (text, Optional):** Enter your business phone number, if applicable.

### Street Address
* **Street Address (text, Required):** Enter your residential or business address.

### City
* **City (text, Required):** Enter the city where you reside or work.

### State / Province
* **State / Province (text, Required):** Enter the state or province where you reside or work.

### Postal / Zip Code
* **Postal / Zip Code (text, Required):** Enter the postal or zip code for your location.

### Country
* **Country (text, Required):** Select your country of residence or nationality.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes (text, Optional):** Enter any additional comments or details about yourself.
