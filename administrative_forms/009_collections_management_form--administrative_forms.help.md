# Collections Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and analyze data on the collections process for a specific period, providing a comprehensive overview of outstanding accounts, collection efforts, and related metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of the collection cycle in the **Collection Period** field.
2. Select the **Collections Manager** responsible for the collection process.
3. Enter the email address of the collections manager in the **Manager Email** field.
4. Enter the **Total Accounts Receivable** for the current collection period.
5. Enter the number of **Outstanding Accounts**, indicating how many customers owe payment.
6. Enter the **Total Collected This Period**, representing the amount successfully collected.
7. Provide the **Collection Rate** as a percentage of the targeted amount collected.
8. In the **Aging Analysis** field, provide a brief summary of the distribution of outstanding accounts.
9. Enter the amount in the **Current to 30 Days Past Due** category.
10. Enter the amount in the **31-60 Days Past Due** category.
11. Enter the amount in the **61-90 Days Past Due** category.
12. Enter the amount in the **Over 90 Days Past Due** category, indicating high-risk accounts.
13. Enter the total number of **Collection Attempts** made, including phone calls, emails, letters, SMS, and in-person visits.
14. Select all contact methods used in the **Contact Methods Used** field.
15. Enter the total **Promised Payments** from customers.
16. Enter the number of **Accounts on Payment Plan**, indicating customers on arrangement agreements.
17. Enter the number of **Accounts in Dispute**, where invoices are disputed.
18. Provide a summary of **Dispute Details** if applicable.
19. If necessary, enter the amount **Written Off** as uncollectible.
20. Provide a justification for **Write-off Justification**, if applicable.
21. Indicate if a **Legal Action Filed** is pending.
22. If a legal action is filed, enter the number of **Legal Actions**.
23. Enter the expected **Forecast for the Next Period**.
24. In the **Summary and Notes** field, enter your overall assessment and recommendations for the collection process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collection Period** (id: 1, type: date, required): Enter the start date of the collection cycle.
* **Collections Manager** (id: 2, type: text, required): Select the person managing the collection process.
* **Manager Email** (id: 3, type: email, required): Enter the email address of the collections manager.
* **Total Accounts Receivable** (id: 4, type: number, required): Enter the total outstanding invoices.
* **Number of Outstanding Accounts** (id: 5, type: number, required): Enter how many customers owe payment.
* **Total Collected This Period** (id: 6, type: number, required): Enter the amount successfully collected.
* **Collection Rate** (id: 7, type: number, required): Provide the percentage of targeted amount collected.
* **Aging Analysis** (id: 8, type: note, required): Briefly summarize the distribution of outstanding accounts.
* **Current to 30 Days Past Due** (id: 9, type: number, required): Enter the amount in this aging category.
* **31-60 Days Past Due** (id: 10, type: number, required): Enter the amount in this category.
* **61-90 Days Past Due** (id: 11, type: number, required): Enter the amount in this category.
* **Over 90 Days Past Due** (id: 12, type: number, required): Enter the amount in this high-risk category.
* **Collection Attempts Count** (id: 13, type: number, required): Enter the total number of collection attempts made.
* **Contact Methods Used** (id: 14, type: select_multiple, required): Select all contact methods used, such as phone calls, emails, letters, SMS, or in-person visits.
* **Promised Payments** (id: 15, type: number, required): Enter the total amount promised by customers.
* **Accounts on Payment Plan** (id: 16, type: number, required): Enter the number of customers on arrangement agreements.
* **Accounts in Dispute** (id: 17, type: number, required): Enter the number of disputed invoices.
* **Dispute Details** (id: 18, type: text, required): Provide a summary of disputed accounts, if applicable.
* **Amount Written Off** (id: 19, type: number, required): If necessary, enter the amount written off as uncollectible.
* **Write-off Justification** (id: 20, type: text, required): Provide reasons for write-offs, if applicable.
* **Legal Action Filed** (id: 21, type: select_one, required): Indicate if a legal action is pending.
* **Number of Legal Actions** (id: 22, type: number, required): If a legal action is filed, enter the number.
* **Forecast for the Next Period** (id: 23, type: number, required): Enter the expected collection amount for the next period.
* **Summary and Notes** (id: 24, type: text, required): Enter your overall assessment and recommendations for the collection process.

**Tips**

* Ensure you complete this form accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear picture of the collections process.
* Review each field carefully and enter the correct information to avoid errors.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use this form to track progress and make informed decisions for future collection efforts.
