# Expense Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Approval Request Form is used to request approval for expenses incurred by employees. It is an essential tool for managing and tracking expenses within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of expense you are requesting approval for (e.g., Food, Travel, Other).
2. Enter the date the expense was incurred.
3. Provide a brief description of the expense.
4. Enter the amount of the expense.
5. Attach any relevant receipts or documentation to support your request (optional).
6. Select the approver who will review and approve your request.
7. Set the date you would like the expense to be approved.
8. Choose the status of your request (Approved, Declined, Pending).
9. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
10. Enter your name and email address for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Expense: Food** (`expense_amount_type`, required)
This field is used to select the type of expense you are requesting approval for. Choose from the options: Expense: Food, Expense: Travel, or Expense: Other.

### **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, required)
Enter the date on which the expense was incurred.

### **Expense Description** (`expense_description`, required)
Provide a brief description of the expense, including the reason for the expense and any relevant details.

### **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, required)
Enter the total amount of the expense.

### **Expense Receipt** (`expense_receipt`, optional)
If you have a receipt or documentation supporting your expense, you can attach it here. This is not required but recommended for transparency and accountability.

### **Approver** (`approver`, required)
Select the person who will review and approve your request. Choose from the options: Jimmy, Jane, or John.

### **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, required)
Set the date you would like the expense to be approved.

### **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, required)
Choose the status of your request: Approved, Declined, or Pending.

### **Notes** (`notes`, optional)
Add any additional notes or comments to support your request, if needed.

### **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, required)
Enter your name and email address for submission.

### **Submitter** (`submitter`, required)
Select the person submitting the request. Choose from the options: Jane, Jimmy, or John.
