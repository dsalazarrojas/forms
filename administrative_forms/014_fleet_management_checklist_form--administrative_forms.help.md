# Fleet Management Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help fleet administrators collect and track essential information about each vehicle in the fleet. It includes sections for maintenance, inspections, licenses, insurance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the relevant information for each vehicle.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, using the options provided.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed, indicated by the "required" label.
4. Use the "Vehicle Image" field to upload an image of the vehicle, if applicable.
5. Review and update the "Vehicle Notes" field with any additional comments or concerns about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`, note, optional):
	+ This is a note field for recording any maintenance or servicing information for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Inspection** (`vehicle_inspection`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" indicating if the vehicle has been inspected recently.
* **Driver Training** (`driver_training`, note, optional):
	+ This is a note field for recording any training or education provided to the driver.
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the driver has the necessary license to operate the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant vehicle registration information, such as the registration status or renewal date.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle is insured.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Schedule** (`vehicle_maintenance_schedule`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the next scheduled maintenance date for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Repair History** (`vehicle_repair_history`, text, optional):
	+ Record any recent repairs or issues with the vehicle.
* **Driver Experience** (`driver_experience`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of years of driving experience the driver has.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle is a specific make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle has a specific color.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any information about the vehicle's condition, such as worn-out parts or needed repairs.
* **Vehicle Usage** (`vehicle_usage`, text, optional):
	+ Describe how the vehicle is being used, such as personal or commercial use.
* **Vehicle Location** (`vehicle_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the current location of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Image** (`vehicle_image`, text, optional):
	+ Upload an image of the vehicle, if applicable.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the driver assigned to the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Notes** (`vehicle_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or concerns about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the current status of the vehicle, such as active or inactive.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle is a specific type.
* **Vehicle Category** (`vehicle_category`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle belongs to a specific category.
* **Vehicle Subcategory** (`vehicle_subcategory`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle belongs to a specific subcategory.
* **Vehicle Tag** (`vehicle_tag`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the vehicle has specific tags or features.
