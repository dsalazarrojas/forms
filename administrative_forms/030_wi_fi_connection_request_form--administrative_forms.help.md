# Wi Fi Connection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a Wi-Fi connection for official work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information about your department and reason for the request.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wi-Fi Request** (wi_fi_request, text, optional): 
This field is where you can briefly describe your request for a Wi-Fi connection.
* **Department** (department, select_one, optional): 
Select your department from the list to indicate which team you belong to.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): 
Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or employee directory.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): 
Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): 
Enter your office phone number (if you have one).
* **Reason** (reason, text, optional): 
Explain the reason for your Wi-Fi connection request (e.g., for a laptop, for a meeting, etc.). 

## Tips
* Ensure you submit this form for official work-related purposes only.
* If you are requesting Wi-Fi for a meeting or event, please provide the meeting/event details in the "Reason" field.
* If you are an IT department employee, please use this form to report any issues with your current Wi-Fi connection.
