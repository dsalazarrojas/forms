# factory_safety_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the safety conditions and equipment of your factory. It includes sections for checking if safety equipment is in use, personal protective equipment (PPE) usage, and submitting a general report.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by selecting the relevant sections you want to report on. You can choose to fill out sections related to safety equipment, PPE, and submitting a general report.
2. For each section, select the appropriate options that apply to your current safety conditions. Be as accurate as possible when answering these questions.
3. Fill out the required fields, such as the inspection date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Factory Safety Inspection Form** (`section_1`, `note`, not required): This section is a placeholder for any additional information you may need to provide about the factory's safety inspection.
* **General** (`section_2`, `text`, not required): This section is for you to provide any general comments or observations about the factory's safety conditions.
* **Safety Equipment** (`section_3`, `select_one`, not required): Select 'Yes' if you have used the safety equipment during the inspection, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Personal Protective Equipment** (`section_4`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the PPE that you used during the inspection.
* **Factory Safety Inspection Form** (`section_5`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the safety inspection in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Factory Safety Inspection Form** (`section_6`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the safety inspection in the format 'HH:MM AM/PM'.
* **Factory Inspection Report** (`section_7`, `text`, not required): Provide a detailed report of your inspection findings in this section.
* **Inspection Date** (`section_8`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.

## Tips

* Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions in this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the sections or fields, contact your supervisor or factory management for guidance.
* Only fill out this form if you are authorized to do so.
