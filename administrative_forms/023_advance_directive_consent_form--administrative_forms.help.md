# Advance Directive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advance Directive Consent Form is a document that outlines the wishes and preferences of an individual for medical treatment and care in the event of a medical emergency or incapacitation. This form is used to ensure that the patient's rights and autonomy are respected, and to guide healthcare providers in making decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their ID.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Select the type of advance directive that applies to the patient (e.g., Living Will, Durable Power of Attorney for Health Care, Do Not Resuscitate Order, or Other).
4. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the appointed health care agent.
5. Describe the patient's general treatment preferences and any limitations.
6. If required, enter the name of the witness who signed the form, as well as their signature.
7. Enter the date the form was signed.
8. If necessary, indicate if notarization is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient full name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their ID.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Type of advance directive** (`directive_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of advance directive that applies to the patient (e.g., Living Will, Durable Power of Attorney for Health Care, Do Not Resuscitate Order, or Other).
- **Appointed health care agent name** (`appointed_agent`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and contact information of the appointed health care agent.
- **General treatment preferences** (`treatment_preferences`, text, required): Describe the patient's general treatment preferences and any limitations.
- **Witness signature name** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): If required, enter the name of the witness who signed the form, as well as their signature.
- **Date signed** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.
- **Notarization required** (`notarization_required`, select_one, optional): If necessary, indicate if notarization is required.
