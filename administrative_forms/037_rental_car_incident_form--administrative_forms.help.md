# Rental Car Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Car Incident Form is used to document incidents involving rental cars, including accidents, thefts, or other events that require reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the vehicle type that best describes the incident. If the vehicle was involved in an accident, choose "Yes" for "Vehicle Type".
2. Enter the date of the incident.
3. Choose the location where the incident occurred.
4. Select the level of vehicle damage involved in the incident, if applicable.
5. If there were witnesses, list their names and provide any statements they may have provided.
6. Enter the police report number, if applicable.
7. List the driver's license numbers, if applicable.
8. Enter the witness contact information, if applicable.
9. Enter the police officer's name and ID, if applicable.
10. Select the vehicle make, year, model, color, license, and VIN, if applicable.
11. Enter the driver's name, address, license, and contact information, if applicable.
12. Select the insurance company, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (1): Select the type of vehicle that was involved in the incident, such as "Yes" for "Yes" and "No" for "No".
* **Accident Date** (2): Enter the date of the incident in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Location** (3): Choose the location where the incident occurred.
* **Vehicle Damage** (4): Select the level of damage to the vehicle, such as "Yes" for "Yes" and "No" for "No".
* **Incident Description** (5): Describe the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Witness Names** (6): List the names of any witnesses who were present during the incident.
* **Witness Statements** (7): Record any statements provided by witnesses.
* **Police Report Number** (8): Enter the police report number for the incident.
* **Driver License Numbers** (9): List the driver's license numbers, if applicable.
* **Witness Contact Info** (10): Enter the contact information for any witnesses who were present.
* **Police Officer Name** (11): Enter the name of the police officer who responded to the incident.
* **Police Officer ID** (12): Enter the ID of the police officer who responded.
* **Vehicle Make** (13): Select the make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Year** (14): Select the year of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model** (15): Select the model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Color** (16): Select the color of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle License** (17): Select the license of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Registration** (18): Enter the vehicle's registration information.
* **Vehicle VIN** (19): Select the VIN of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Driver Name** (20): Enter the name of the driver involved in the incident.
* **Driver Address** (21): Enter the driver's address.
* **Driver License** (22): Enter the driver's license number.
* **Driver Contact Info** (23): Enter the driver's contact information.
* **Insurance Company** (24): Select the insurance company for the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Insurance Company Name** (25): Enter the name of the insurance company.
