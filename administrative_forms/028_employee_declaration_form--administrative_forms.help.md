# Employee Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Declaration Form is a document that employees use to declare their employment status and provide work-related details. It helps the company to ensure that all employees are aware of and agree to comply with company policies and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID number in the `Employee ID` field.
2. Provide your full name in the `Employee Name` field.
3. Select your department from the `Department` dropdown menu.
4. Enter your job title in the `Job Title` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `Email` field in the format `example@example.com`.
6. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
7. Enter your address in the `Address` field.
8. Read and agree to the `Policy Agreement` by checking the box or selecting the text area to acknowledge your understanding.
9. If you have any consequences of non-compliance, list them in the `Policy Consequences` field.
10. Enter a declaration statement in the `Employee Declare` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (Text): Enter your unique employee ID number assigned to you by the company.
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Department** (Select One): Choose the department you belong to from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your work email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your work address.
* **Policy Agreement** (Note): Read and agree to the company policies and regulations by checking the box or selecting the text area.
* **Policy Consequences** (Text): If you have any consequences of non-compliance, list them in this field.
* **Employee Declare** (Text): Enter a declaration statement, e.g., "I, [Your Name], declare that the information above is true and accurate."
