# Renovation Project Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission for a renovation project from the relevant authorities and stakeholders. It is typically used by property owners, developers, or project managers to provide details about the project, including its start and end dates, address, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project's address in the "Address" field, which is the location where the renovation will take place.
2. Select the start date of the project in the "Start Date" field.
3. Select the end date of the project in the "End Date" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field to confirm your identity.
5. (Optional) Add your contact information in the "Contact Info" field, so that the relevant authorities can reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the physical address where the renovation project will take place.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date when the project will start.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date when the project will end.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name to confirm your identity.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, **optional**): Add your contact information so that the relevant authorities can reach out to you.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your project manager or supervisor for assistance.
* Make sure to complete all required fields marked as **required** to avoid submission errors.
