# non-disclosure-declaration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non-Disclosure Declaration Form is used to declare the sharing of sensitive information with others in the workplace. This form helps maintain transparency and ensures that sensitive information is handled according to company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you understand each section.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. If any section is not applicable, please indicate this clearly in that specific field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Information Shared** (`business_information_shared`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the business information shared with others.
- **Confidential Relationship** (`confidential_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the nature of your relationship with the shared information:
	* I am a business owner
	* I am an employee
	* I am a representative of the company
- **Sensitive Information Shared** (`sensitive_information`, date, optional): Enter the date when the sensitive information was shared.
- **Shared With** (`shared_with`, select_multiple, optional): Select with whom the sensitive information was shared:
	* Client
	* Colleagues
	* Manager
	* Other (Please specify)
- **Purpose of Sharing** (`purpose_of_sharing`, text, optional): Explain the reason or purpose behind sharing the sensitive information.
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`non_disclosure_agreement`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or agreements related to non-disclosure.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for any further questions or discussions.
- **Review of Policy** (`review_policy`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you've reviewed the company's policy on non-disclosure:
	* 'True'
	* 'False'
- **Review Policy Details** (`review_policy_details`, text, optional): If you answered 'True' to the previous question, please provide the details of your review.
- **Sign and Date** (`sign_and_date`, time, optional): Enter the current date and time of submission, and sign your name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any section, please ask your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* This form must be completed with the utmost care and attention to detail.
