# Lease Termination Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for documenting the process of terminating a lease between a landlord and a tenant. It's essential to accurately fill out the form to ensure all necessary information is captured and to maintain a smooth leasing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Lease Termination Release Form** on page 1.
2.  Enter the **Landlord Information** (page 2) with the landlord's details.
3.  Enter the **Tenant Information** (page 3) with the tenant's details.
4.  Select a **Notice Date** (page 4) and a **Termination Date** (page 5) for the lease termination.
5.  Choose the **Reason for Termination** (page 6) from the available options.
6.  If necessary, provide a **Witness Statement** (page 7) to support the termination process.
7.  Sign and date the **Tenant Signature** (page 8) and **Landlord Signature** (page 9) sections to confirm the termination of the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Lease Termination Release Form** (Page 1): This is the main section of the form where you will document the lease termination process.
*   **Landlord Information** (Page 2): Enter the landlord's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Tenant Information** (Page 3): Enter the tenant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **Notice Date** (Page 4): Enter the date the landlord provided the tenant with notice of the lease termination.
*   **Termination Date** (Page 5): Enter the date the lease was officially terminated.
*   **Reason for Termination** (Page 6): Choose one or more reasons for terminating the lease from the provided options.
*   **Witness Statement** (Page 7): If a witness is required, provide their statement to support the termination process.
*   **Tenant Signature** (Page 8): Sign and date this section to confirm the tenant's understanding and agreement with the lease termination.
*   **Landlord Signature** (Page 9): Sign and date this section to confirm the landlord's understanding and agreement with the lease termination.

## Tips
*   Make sure to keep a copy of the form for your records.
*   Ensure that all necessary parties have signed and dated the form.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are complete.
