**Recruitment Plan Approval Form - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
The Recruitment Plan Approval Form is a tool used for management to review and approve or reject recruitment plans submitted by HR teams or other department representatives.

**How To Complete This Form**

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**

*   **User Email**: Enter your user email address.
*   **Department**: Provide the department in which the job is being filled.
*   **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job being created.
*   **Job Type**: Select the type of position, whether it's full-time, part-time, or contract.
*   **Hire Date**: Enter the date you intend to hire for the new position.
*   **Budgeted Position**: Specify the budgeted position in the organization.
*   **Recruitment Plan Details**: Describe the recruitment plan details.
*   **Manager Approval**: Select the approver(s) to review and approve or reject the recruitment plan.
*   **Comments**: Leave comments if necessary for further clarification or feedback.
*   **Submitter**: Select the submitter who created the form.
*   **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
*   **Date Approved**: Enter the date the plan was approved.
*   **Comments Approved**: Enter comments if the plan was approved.
*   **Status**: Choose the status of the plan, whether it's approved, pending, or rejected.
*   **Form ID**: Enter a unique form ID.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned for the recruitment plan.
*   **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
*   **Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the form.
*   **Created At**: Enter the date the form was created.
*   **Updated At**: Enter the date the form was last updated.
*   **Deleted At**: Enter the date the form was deleted.
*   **Comments Count**: Enter the number of comments attached to the form.
*   **Comments**: Leave comments for further feedback.
*   **Comments HTML**: Enter comments in HTML format for further reference.
*   **Comments**: Enter any other comments or feedback.
