# warehouse_inventory_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The warehouse inventory checklist form is used to track and manage individual items in the warehouse, including their descriptions, quantities, values, statuses, locations, and reorder dates. This form helps warehouse staff to accurately record and update item information, ensuring that the inventory is properly managed and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the form and have reviewed the instructions carefully.
2. Select the page with the relevant information for the item you are tracking (e.g., page 1).
3. Fill out each field accordingly, starting with the item description field.
4. Enter the item quantity in the quantity field.
5. Select the item status from the status field (Active or Inactive).
6. Select the item location from the location field (Yes or No).
7. If applicable, select the item reorder date from the date field.
8. Enter any relevant notes or comments about the item in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **item description** (text): Enter a brief description of the item being tracked, including any relevant identifying characteristics or codes.
* **item quantity** (number): Enter the current quantity of the item in stock.
* **item value** (number): Enter the monetary value of the item.
* **item status** (select_one): Select the current status of the item (Active or Inactive).
* **item location** (select_multiple): Select whether the item is located at a specific location (Yes or No).
* **item reorder** (date): Enter the date when the item should be reordered or restocked.
* **item notes** (note): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the item.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to maintain accurate inventory records.
* If you are unsure about the status or location of an item, please research and verify before completing the form.
* If you are entering a reorder date, use the format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure accurate date formatting.
