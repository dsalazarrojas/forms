# Uniform Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Policy Acknowledgment Form is a mandatory document that must be completed by all new employees to acknowledge and agree to the company's onboarding process and policies. This form helps to ensure that new employees are properly informed and aware of their responsibilities and obligations within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you understand the information being collected.
2. Fill in your Employee ID, Job Title, Department, and Manager Name accurately.
3. Select the correct option from the list of policies related to the onboarding process.
4. Enter a clear and concise acknowledgment statement in the Acknowledgment field.
5. Verify your contact information for any corrections or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding process** (`onboarding_process`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to provide a brief description of the onboarding process. Please enter the process in the space provided.
* **What is the first policy you read?** (`policies_list`, select_one, optional):
	+ This field asks you to select the first policy you read from the list of options provided. Please select the correct option from the list.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, text, optional):
	+ This field requires you to enter a clear and concise acknowledgment statement, agreeing to abide by the onboarding process and all policies outlined in this form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, note, optional):
	+ This field provides a note section for you to enter any additional contact information. Please use this space to provide any relevant details.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your unique Employee ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your department.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your manager's name.
* **Contact Information** (`manager_contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter any contact information for your manager.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully and ensure you have entered accurate information.
* Double-check your acknowledgment statement in the Acknowledgment field to ensure it accurately represents your understanding and agreement.
* Make sure to provide any necessary contact information in the Contact Information field.
* This form is not a one-time requirement, and you may need to complete it again if your information changes.
