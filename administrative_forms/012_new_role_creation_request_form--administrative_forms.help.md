# New Role Creation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the creation of a new role within the administrative context. It helps in documenting and tracking the process of role creation, including categorization, description, required skills, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a clear and concise title for the new role in the **New Role Creation Request Form** field.
2. Select the most relevant category for the new role from the options provided in the **Category** field (e.g., Administrative Forms, Human Resources Forms).
3. Write a brief and detailed description of the new role in the **Description** field, highlighting its responsibilities and requirements.
4. Specify the essential skills required for the new role in the **Required Skills** field.
5. Indicate the department responsible for the new role in the **Responsible Department** field.
6. Choose the **Status** of the new role from the given options (e.g., Draft, Pending, Active).
7. Optionally, add any additional comments or information in the **Comment** field.
8. Provide the name of the output file for the new role in the **Output File** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Role Creation Request Form**: 
  - This field is the title of the form itself, providing a summary of the purpose and nature of the new role.

  * **Category**
    - Choose the best category for the new role from the available options.
    - This helps in organizing and searching for roles within the administrative context.
  - *Description**
    - Write a clear and concise description of the new role, including its responsibilities and requirements.
  - *Required Skills*
    - List the essential skills required for the new role, helping to identify potential candidates.
  - *Responsible Department*
    - Specify the department responsible for the new role, ensuring clear ownership and accountability.
  - *Creation Date*
    - Indicate the date the new role will be created.
  - *Requester*
    - Provide your name or contact information for further reference.
  - *Status*
    - Choose the status of the new role from the available options (e.g., Draft, Pending, Active).
  - *Comment*
    - Add any additional information or comments about the new role.
  - *Output File*
    - Specify the name of the output file for the new role.

This form is designed to help you create a new role within the administrative context, providing a structured way to document and track its creation process.
