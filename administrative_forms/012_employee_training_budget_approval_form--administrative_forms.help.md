# Employee Training Budget Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Budget Approval Form is a digital form used to request and approve employee training budgets. It enables employees to submit their training requests and their managers and supervisors to review and approve them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
* Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
* Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
* Enter the manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
* Select the department from the list of options in the "Department" field.
* Enter the training budget in the "Training Budget" field.
* Enter a brief description of the training in the "Training Description" field.
* Select the start and end dates of the training in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
* Select the status of the training request from the options in the "Status" field.
* Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
* Select the submitter's name from the options in the "Submitter Name" field.
* Select the submitter's email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
* Select the submitter's department from the list of options in the "Submitter Department" field.
* Select the approver's name from the options in the "Approver" field.
* Select the department from the list of options in the "Department" field.
* Enter the date when the form was submitted in the "Form Submitted" field.
* Enter the date when the form was approved in the "Form Approved" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee submitting the training request.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
* **Department**: Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Training Budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the training.
* **Training Description**: Provide a brief description of the training.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the training.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the training.
* **Status**: Select the status of the training request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the training request.
* **Submitter Name**: Select the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Select the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Approver**: Select the name of the person approving the form.
* **Department**: Select the department for which the form is being submitted.
* **Form Submitted**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Form Approved**: Enter the date when the form was approved.
