# IT Support Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and structured way for employees to request IT support for various issues or concerns. This form helps the IT team understand the nature of the problem and prioritize their support accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by reviewing the form title**: Verify that you're using the correct form, "IT Support Authorization Form".
2. **Fill out the Employee Information section**: Provide your name, title, and contact information in the employee_info field. This ensures the IT team can get in touch with you efficiently.
3. **Describe your availability and priorities**: In the availability field, let us know your current availability and any constraints or priorities you have. This helps the IT team schedule support accordingly.
4. **List any additional issues**: In the additional_issues field, detail any other concerns or problems you're facing. Be as specific as possible so the IT team can address them effectively.
5. **Set a deadline for resolution**: If you have a specific deadline for the IT support, enter it in the deadline field. This helps the IT team plan and prioritize their support according to your needs.
6. **Specify the expected outcome**: In the expected_outcome field, describe the desired outcome or result you're expecting from the IT support. This ensures the IT team understands your objectives.
7. **Check for authorization**: If required by your company policy, select the correct authorization option to grant permission for the IT team to access your account or system.
8. **Add any additional information or attachments**: In the additional_info field, provide any supplementary details or files that are relevant to your IT support request. This helps the IT team better understand your issue and provide effective support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, `text`, required): This section requires your name, title, and contact information to ensure the IT team can get in touch with you efficiently.
* **Availability and Priorities** (`availability`, `text`, required): Describe your current availability and any constraints or priorities you have. This helps the IT team schedule support accordingly.
* **Additional Issues** (`additional_issues`, `text`, required): List any other concerns or problems you're facing. Be as specific as possible so the IT team can address them effectively.
* **Deadline** (`deadline`, `date`, required): If you have a specific deadline for the IT support, enter it in this field. This helps the IT team plan and prioritize their support according to your needs.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, `text`, required): Describe the desired outcome or result you're expecting from the IT support. This ensures the IT team understands your objectives.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `checkbox`, optional): If required by your company policy, select the correct authorization option to grant permission for the IT team to access your account or system.
* **Additional Information or Attachments** (`additional_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any supplementary details or files that are relevant to your IT support request. This helps the IT team better understand your issue and provide effective support.
