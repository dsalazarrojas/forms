# repatriation_planning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repatriation Planning Form is designed to help individuals gather essential information for a smooth repatriation process. It guides users in setting realistic repatriation objectives, determining their current country of residence, planning repatriation and flight dates, and arranging return trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing your repatriation objectives, if any, in the "Repatriation Objectives" text field.
2.  Next, indicate your current country of residence in the "Current Country of Residence" text field.
3.  Enter the planned repatriation date in the "Repatriation Date" field.
4.  Provide your planned flight arrival date in the "Flight Arrival Date" field.
5.  Finally, select your return trip plan from the options provided in the "Return Trip Planned" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Repatriation Objectives** (`Repatriation Objectives`, `text`, optional): This field is used to specify any specific objectives or goals for your repatriation process.
*   **Current Country of Residence** (`Current Country of Residence`, `text`, optional): This field is used to input the country in which you currently reside.
*   **Repatriation Date** (`Repatriation Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date on which you plan to repatriate.
*   **Flight Arrival Date** (`Flight Arrival Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date on which your flight is expected to arrive in your country of origin.
*   **Return Trip Planned** (`Return Trip Planned`, `select_one`, optional): Select from the options "Return planned in 2 weeks", "Return planned in 6 weeks", or "Other (please specify)" to plan your return trip.
