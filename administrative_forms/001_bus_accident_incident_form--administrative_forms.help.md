# Bus Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bus Accident Incident Form is used to document incidents involving buses, including collisions, passenger injuries, property damage, and other types of incidents. It helps gather information to identify the cause of incidents, assess damage, and take necessary corrective actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Date of Incident**, which is the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the **Time of Incident**, which is the approximate time the incident occurred.
3. Provide the **Driver Name**, which is the full name of the bus driver.
4. Enter the **Driver License Number**, which is the driver's license number for identification purposes.
5. Enter the **Bus Registration Number**, which is the vehicle identification number of the bus involved in the incident.
6. Describe the **Incident Location**, which is the street address or route location where the incident occurred.
7. Select the **Type of Accident**, which categorizes the type of incident that occurred (e.g., collision with a vehicle or object).
8. Choose the **Weather Conditions** at the time of the incident (e.g., clear, rainy, foggy, etc.).
9. If other vehicles were involved, enter the number of **Other Vehicles Involved**.
10. Indicate if **Passenger Injuries** were reported (Yes, Minor Injuries; Yes, Serious Injuries; or No Injuries).
11. Check whether an **Emergency Services** was called (e.g., Ambulance, Police, Both, or None).
12. Enter a **Detailed Description of Incident**, which provides a complete account of what happened.
13. Select any **Contributing Factors** that may have contributed to the incident (e.g., driver error, weather conditions, etc.).
14. If a witness was present, enter their **Witness Name** and **Witness Contact Number**.
15. If applicable, enter the **Police Report Number**.
16. If relevant, enter the **Estimated Property Damage** in currency units.
17. Indicate whether **Photos or Evidence Attached** are included.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Incident**: When did the accident occur?
* **Time of Incident**: Approximate time the accident occurred.
* **Driver Name**: Full name of the bus driver.
* **Driver License Number**: License number for identification.
* **Bus Registration Number**: Vehicle identification number.
* **Incident Location**: Street address or route location.
* **Type of Accident**: Select from: Collision with Vehicle, Collision with Object, Passenger Injury, Property Damage, Near Miss, Other.
* **Weather Conditions**: Select from: Clear, Rainy, Foggy, Snowy, Stormy.
* **Other Vehicles Involved**: If other vehicles were involved, enter the number.
* **Passenger Injuries Reported**: Indicate if passenger injuries were reported (Yes, Minor Injuries; Yes, Serious Injuries; or No Injuries).
* **Emergency Services Called**: Check if emergency services were called (Ambulance, Police, Both, or None).
* **Detailed Description of Incident**: Provide a complete account of what happened.
* **Contributing Factors**: Select from: Driver Error, Weather Conditions, Road Conditions, Vehicle Malfunction, Third Party Action.
* **Witness Name**: If a witness was present, enter their name.
* **Witness Contact Number**: If a witness was present, enter their contact number.
* **Police Report Number**: If applicable, enter the police report number.
* **Estimated Property Damage**: If applicable, enter the estimated property damage in currency units.
* **Photos or Evidence Attached**: Indicate if supporting documents are included.
