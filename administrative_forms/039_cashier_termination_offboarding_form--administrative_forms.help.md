# Cashier Termination Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cashier Termination Offboarding Form is a document used to record the details of an employee's termination, including the reason for termination, final pay and benefits, and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents, such as the employee's ID number, position title, and date of hire.
2. Fill out the Termination Date and Date Notice Given fields with the corresponding dates.
3. Select the reason for termination from the options provided in the Reason for Termination field.
4. Provide a detailed explanation for the termination in the Detailed Explanation field.
5. Check the boxes for Final Paycheck Date, Accrued Vacation Days Unused, Accrued Sick Time or PTO, and any other applicable fields as required.
6. Answer Yes/No for questions regarding equipment return, knowledge transfer, and offboarding tasks.
7. Once complete, submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Termination Date**: Date of termination in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date Notice Given**: Date when the employee was given notice of termination in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Employee Full Name**: Complete name of the employee being terminated.
* **Employee ID Number**: Unique identifier for the employee.
* **Department**: Department where the employee was working.
* **Position Title**: Title of the position held by the employee.
* **Date of Hire**: Date the employee was hired in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Reason for Termination**: Select the reason for termination from the provided options.
* **Detailed Explanation**: Provide a detailed explanation for the termination.
* **Final Paycheck Date**: Date of the final paycheck in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Accrued Vacation Days Unused**: Number of vacation days unused by the employee.
* **Accrued Sick Time or PTO**: Number of sick time or PTO hours accrued by the employee.
* **Register Keys Returned?**: Answer Yes or No for whether the register keys were returned.
* **Store ID Card or Badge Returned?**: Answer Yes or No for whether the store ID card or badge was returned.
* **Other Equipment Returned**: Describe any other equipment returned.
* **Knowledge Transfer Completed?**: Answer Yes, No, or Partial for whether knowledge transfer was completed.
* **All Offboarding Tasks Completed?**: Answer Yes, No, or Partial for whether all offboarding tasks were completed.
