# Network Administrator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Network Administrator Onboarding Form is designed to collect personal and role-specific information from newly hired Network Administrators. This information will help the administration team to understand their needs and provide a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and complete information.
2. For each field, use the available options or provide the information as requested.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please provide your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please provide your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 3. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide a valid email address that you regularly use for official communication.

### 4. Role
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Please select the specific role that you will be performing as a Network Administrator.

### 5. Department
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Please provide the department that you will be working with or reporting to.

### 6. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide a valid phone number that you can be reached at.

### 7. Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please provide the location where you will be working from or based.

### 8. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select the date you expect to start your onboarding process.

### 9. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please select the time you expect to start your onboarding process.

### 10. Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Please use this field for any additional information or comments you'd like to share with the administration team.

Note to the admin team: Please review the submitted form for completeness and accuracy before proceeding with the onboarding process.
