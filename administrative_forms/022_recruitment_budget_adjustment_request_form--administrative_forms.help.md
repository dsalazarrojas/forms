# Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Budget Adjustment Request Form is used by staff members to submit requests for budget adjustments due to unforeseen expenses, changes in project scope, or external factors affecting the project's timeline.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Enter your name and title in the **Requester** field, which will appear in the **User Requester** field.
2.  Choose a valid date for the **Date** field.
3.  In the **Reason for Adjustment** field, provide a detailed explanation for your budget adjustment request.
4.  Enter the adjusted budget amount in the **Budget Amount** field.
5.  Choose a realistic expected completion date for the project in the **Expected Completion Date** field.
6.  If necessary, add any **Comments** related to your request.
7.  Finally, click **Submit** to confirm your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Requester**: Enter your name and title to help management identify you as the person submitting the request.
*   **Date**: Choose a valid date for the budget adjustment request. This should be the date the request is being submitted.
*   **Budget Adjustment**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the budget adjustment.
*   **Reason for Adjustment**: Give a detailed explanation of the reason for the budget adjustment. Be as specific as possible.
*   **Budget Amount**: Enter the adjusted budget amount.
*   **Expected Completion Date**: Choose a realistic expected completion date for the project. This date should be based on your current progress and understanding of the project's timeline.
*   **Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments related to your request, add it here.
*   **Submit**: Confirm your request by selecting "Yes" to submit it for review. Selecting "No" will cancel the request.
