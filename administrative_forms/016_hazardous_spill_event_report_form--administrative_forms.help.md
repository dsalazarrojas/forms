# Hazardous Spill Event Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of hazardous spills that require immediate attention and response.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the date of the spill event in the "Spill Date" field.
2.  Enter the time of the spill event in the "Spill Time" field.
3.  Enter the location of the spill event in the "Spill Location" field.
4.  Describe the contents of the spill in the "Spill Contents" field.
5.  Enter the volume of the spill in the "Spill Volume" field.
6.  Provide a brief description of the spill event in the "Spill Description" field.
7.  Select the response status of the spill event from the options provided in the "Response Status" field.
8.  Enter the incident ID number if available in the "Incident ID" field.
9.  Provide additional response information in the "Response Details" field.
10. Select the regulatory filing status of the spill event from the options provided in the "Regulatory Filing" field.
11. Enter the URL of a photo related to the spill event in the "Spill Photo URL" field.
12. Enter the URL of a video related to the spill event in the "Spill Video URL" field.
13. Enter the URL of a document related to the spill event in the "Spill Document URL" field.
14. Select the spill reporter from the options provided in the "Spill Reporter" field.
15. Enter the contact information of the spill reporter in the "Spill Reporter Contact" field.
16. Select the spill reviewer from the options provided in the "Spill Reviewer" field.
17. Enter the contact information of the spill reviewer in the "Spill Reviewer Contact" field.
18. Select the incident status from the options provided in the "Incident Status" field.
19. Enter the date of the incident status in the "Incident Status Date" field.
20. Enter the time of the incident status in the "Incident Status Time" field.
21. Select the regulatory filing status from the options provided in the "Regulatory Filing Status" field.
22. Enter the name of the person who submitted the report in the "Submitted By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Spill Date** (Date): Enter the date of the spill event.
*   **Spill Time** (Time): Enter the time of the spill event.
*   **Spill Location** (Text): Enter the location of the spill event.
*   **Spill Type** (Text): Describe the type of spill that occurred.
*   **Spill Contents** (Text): Describe the contents of the spill.
*   **Spill Volume** (Text): Enter the volume of the spill.
*   **Spill Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the spill event.
*   **Response Status** (Select Multiple): Select the response status of the spill event.
*   **Incident ID** (Text): Enter the incident ID number if available.
*   **Response Details** (Text): Provide additional response information.
*   **Regulatory Filing** (Select One): Select the regulatory filing status of the spill event.
*   **Spill Photo URL** (Text): Enter the URL of a photo related to the spill event.
*   **Spill Video URL** (Text): Enter the URL of a video related to the spill event.
*   **Spill Document URL** (Text): Enter the URL of a document related to the spill event.
*   **Spill Reporter** (Select One): Select the spill reporter from the options provided.
*   **Spill Reporter Contact** (Text): Enter the contact information of the spill reporter.
*   **Spill Reviewer** (Select One): Select the spill reviewer from the options provided.
*   **Spill Reviewer Contact** (Text): Enter the contact information of the spill reviewer.
*   **Incident Status** (Select One): Select the incident status from the options provided.
*   **Incident Status Date** (Date): Enter the date of the incident status.
*   **Incident Status Time** (Time): Enter the time of the incident status.
*   **Regulatory Filing Status** (Select One): Select the regulatory filing status from the options provided.
*   **Submitted By** (Text): Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the report.
*   Ensure that you have permission to submit reports outside of your area of expertise.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
*   Follow the instructions provided above to ensure that you submit a complete and accurate report.
