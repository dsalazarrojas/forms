# Health and Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health and Safety Incident Form is a tool to collect and document information about incidents that could impact employee health and safety. This form helps to ensure that incidents are reported, investigated, and addressed in a timely and thorough manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the Employee Information section with your name, employee ID, and job title.
2.  Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident in the Incident Details section.
3.  Enter your name and title as the incident reporter in the Incident Reporter section.
4.  If applicable, attach any supporting documents related to the incident in the Supporting Documents section.
5.  Review and confirm the accuracy of the information you provided in the Review and Approval section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information** (employee_information, text, required): Enter your name, employee ID, and job title.
    *   This section is used to identify the employee who experienced the incident.
*   **Incident Details** (incident_details, text, required): Provide a detailed account of what happened during the incident.
    *   This section is used to gather information about the incident, including its circumstances and any injuries or harm suffered.
*   **Incident Reporter** (incident_reporter, text, required): Enter your name and title.
    *   This section is used to identify who reported the incident.
*   **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, text, optional): If applicable, attach any supporting documents related to the incident.
    *   This section is used to collect any evidence or supporting materials that may be relevant to the investigation of the incident.
*   **Review and Approval** (review_and_approval, text, required): Review and confirm the accuracy of the information you provided.
    *   This section is used to confirm the accuracy of the information entered and to ensure that it is complete and thorough.
 
## Tips
*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
*   If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
*   If you have already reported the incident, do not complete this form again.
