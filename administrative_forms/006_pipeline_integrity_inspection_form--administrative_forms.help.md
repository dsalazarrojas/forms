# pipeline_integrity_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pipeline integrity inspection form is designed to assess the status of pipeline integrity in a specific region or area, ensuring that the pipeline is functioning correctly and safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct option for each page according to your knowledge and expertise.
2. Ensure you answer each question based on the specific location or area being inspected.
3. If you're unsure about any question, refer to industry standards and guidelines for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, `note`, not required): This is the first page of the form. It provides a general overview of the pipeline's status. Take notes about any issues or concerns you've observed.
* **Page 2** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, not required): On this page, select all the issues you've encountered in the inspection, such as "Yes" for any problems or concerns you've found.
* **Page 3** (`page_3`, `select_one`, not required): In this final page, choose the most accurate statement that describes your overall assessment of the pipeline's integrity.
