# Nursing Home Resident Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a nursing home resident's check out, including the date and time of departure, any new arrangement, discharge time, reason for leave, and resident name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the check out date for the resident.
2. Provide the new arrangement details, if any (e.g., transfer to another facility, home, etc.).
3. Enter the discharge time for the resident.
4. Specify the reason for the resident's leave.
5. Enter the resident's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Check Out Date (1)
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required/optional): The date the resident is checking out of the nursing home. Please enter the date in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### New Arrangement (2)
* **New Arrangement** (`new_arrangement`, `text`, optional): If the resident is being transferred to another facility or has a new arrangement, provide details in this field. This could include the name and address of the new facility or the contact information of the person or organization responsible for the resident's care.

### Discharge Time (3)
* **Discharge Time** (`discharge_time`, `time`, optional): The time the resident is scheduled to be discharged from the nursing home. Please enter the time in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Reason for Leave (4)
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for the resident's leave. This could include any medical reason, personal reason, or other relevant information.

### Resident Name (5)
* **Resident Name** (`resident_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the full name of the resident checking out.
