# Supply Chain Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Chain Audit Form is designed to gather information about your supply chain operations. The form consists of multiple pages, each with different types of questions. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the information gathered is useful and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and question carefully before answering.
2. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of each question and option before selecting an answer.
3. Fill out all the questions on each page, even if you're unsure about some.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Supply Chain Operations

* **Supply Chain Questions** (`supply_chain_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This question asks about the specific aspects of the supply chain operations. You can select one or more of the provided options, such as "Yes" or "No", depending on whether the operation takes place.
* **Suppliers** (`suppliers`, select_one, required: false): This question asks about the suppliers involved in the supply chain operations. You can select one of the provided options, such as "Yes" or "No", depending on whether a supplier is involved.
* **Date** (`dates`, date, required: false): This question asks you to select a date related to the supply chain operations. You can choose a date from the calendar.

### Page 2: Supply Chain Questions

* **Supply Chain Questions** (`supply_chain_questions`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is identical to the one on Page 1, but you can select different options this time.

### Tips
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for help or clarification.
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the information gathered is useful and reliable.
* Make sure to review each page and question before answering to avoid missing important information.
