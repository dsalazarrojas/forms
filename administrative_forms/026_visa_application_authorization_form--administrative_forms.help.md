# visa_application_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users seeking to apply for a visa. The information collected will be used to process their application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`first_page`, `text`, not required): This is the starting page of the form where you can enter your information.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number that we can contact you with.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unable to complete any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* Please note that incomplete or inaccurate information may delay or reject your visa application.
