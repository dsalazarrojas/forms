# Employee Advocate Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request time off from work. It's meant to be completed by employees to submit their time off requests for manager review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select whether or not you are requesting time off on the first page.
2. Enter your start date and end date of your requested time off on the second and third pages respectively.
3. Enter the number of days you are requesting off on the fourth page.
4. Provide a reason for your leave on the fifth page.
5. Indicate whether your manager has approved or disapproved your request on the sixth page.
6. Enter any comments your manager may have on the seventh page.
7. Enter your name on the eighth page.
8. Enter your manager's name on the ninth page.
9. Enter your department on the tenth page.
10. Select whether to submit your request for manager review and approval on the eleventh page.
11. Enter your email address on the thirteenth page.
12. Enter your manager's email address on the fourteenth page.
13. Enter the date you submitted the form on the fifteenth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **time_off_request** (Select One):
    + This option is used to indicate whether or not you are requesting time off.
* **start_date** (Date):
    + Enter the start date of your time off request.
* **end_date** (Date):
    + Enter the end date of your time off request.
* **time_off_days** (Number):
    + Enter the number of days you are requesting for your time off.
* **reason_for_leave** (Text):
    + Provide a reason for your time off request.
* **manager_approval** (Select One):
    + Indicate whether your manager has approved or disapproved your request.
* **comments** (Note):
    + Enter any comments your manager may have.
* **employee_name** (Text):
    + Enter your name.
* **manager_name** (Text):
    + Enter your manager's name.
* **department** (Text):
    + Enter your department.
* **submit_time_off** (Select One):
    + Select whether to submit your request for manager review and approval.
* **submitter** (Text):
    + Enter your name.
* **submitter_email** (Email):
    + Enter your email address.
* **manager_email** (Email):
    + Enter your manager's email address.
* **date_submitted** (Date):
    + Enter the date you submitted the form.
