# police_department_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about incidents reported to the police department.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General information page with the relevant details about the incident.
2. Select the Location where the incident occurred.
3. Choose the type(s) of incident from the list of options.
4. Enter the Date of the incident.
5. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the Incident details field.
6. If there is a suspect involved, enter their information in the Suspect field.
7. If an officer was involved, enter their information in the Officer involved field.
8. Select the Witness information category to indicate the type of witness.
9. Select the current Incident status.
10. Choose the Incident report status from the list of options.
11. Finally, enter any Officer comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General information** (1, text, required: false): This page is the introduction to the form. It is meant to be left blank.
* **Location** (2, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident type** (3, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of incident from the list: Police, Fire, Other.
* **Date** (4, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident details** (5, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Suspect** (6, text, required: false): Enter the suspect's information.
* **Officer involved** (7, text, required: false): Enter the officer's information if they were involved in the incident.
* **Witness information** (8, select_one, required: false): Select the type of witness: Officer, Witness, Public.
* **Incident status** (9, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the incident: Active, Closed, In Progress.
* **Incident report** (10, select_one, required: true): Choose the status of the incident report: Received, Filed, Reviewing.
* **Officer comments** (11, text, required: false): Enter any comments from the officer about the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* Use the correct incident type and status to ensure proper tracking and reporting.
* If there is a suspect, ensure to enter their information correctly.
* If there is an officer involved, ensure to enter their information correctly.
* Select the correct witness type.
* Choose the correct incident report status.
* Enter any officer comments about the incident.
