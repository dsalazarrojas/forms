# public_event_permit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public event permit application form is used to collect information from applicants for a public event permit. This form is designed to gather all necessary details for the event, including the event details, applicant information, and additional requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the necessary information about your event, such as the event name, date, time, and location.
2. Provide your **Applicant Details** in the next section, including your name, address, and contact information.
3. Add any **Additional Details** that are relevant to your event in the **Additional Details** section.
4. If required, upload any necessary **Documents** in the **Upload Documents** section.
5. Review your submission carefully before clicking the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This is the section where you will enter the details of your event, including the event name, date, time, and location.
* **Applicant Details**: This section is where you will provide information about yourself, the applicant, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Additional Details**: This is an open-ended section where you can provide any additional information that is relevant to your event.
* **Upload Documents**: This section is for uploading any necessary documents that support your application. If no documents are required, this field will be skipped.
* **Submit**: This is the final step where you will review your submission and click the submit button to complete the application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review your submission carefully to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Contact form@gic.mx if you need further assistance.
