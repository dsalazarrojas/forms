# procurement_request_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Procurement Request Communication Form is designed to gather information about procurement requests from internal teams and stakeholders. This form helps streamline the procurement process by providing a clear and structured way to capture details about the request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the procurement request in the **Request Details** field.
2. Select the type of procurement request you are making by choosing one of the options in the **Procurement Type** field.
3. If applicable, specify the procurement value and status in the **Procurement Value** and **Procurement Status** fields.
4. Identify the category of goods or services requested in the **Procurement Category** field.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the individual or team requesting the procurement in the **Procurement Requester** and **Procurement Contact** fields.
6. Enter the email address of the procurement requester in the **Procurement Email** field.
7. Optionally, provide any additional phone contact information in the **Procurement Phone** field.
8. If necessary, include any additional notes about the request in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Details**: A brief description of the procurement request, including what is being requested and any relevant context.
  * This is the main area for describing the request, please keep it concise.
- **Procurement Type**: The type of procurement request, either Goods or Service.
  * This will help us understand what you need, and guide further processing.
- **Procurement Value**: The monetary value or amount of the procurement request.
  * If this is a service request, consider specifying a time-based value or other relevant details.
- **Procurement Status**: The status of the procurement request, either Draft or Approved.
  * This will help us track the progress of the request.
- **Procurement Category**: The category or type of goods or services being requested.
  * This will help us ensure the request goes to the correct department or team.
- **Procurement Requester**: The name of the individual or team making the procurement request.
  * This will help us address any questions or requests for clarification directly.
- **Procurement Contact**: The contact person or contact information for the requester.
  * This will help us establish a connection point for follow-up questions or requests.
- **Procurement Email**: The email address of the requestor.
  * This is the best way for us to reach out with any updates or questions.
- **Procurement Phone**: Any additional phone contact information for the requester.
  * This can be helpful for urgent or follow-up matters.
- **Note**: Any additional notes or comments about the request.
  * This can be useful for clarifying or tracking any issues that may arise.
