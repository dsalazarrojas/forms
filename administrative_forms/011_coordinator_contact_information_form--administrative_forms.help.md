# Coordinator Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and update contact information from coordinators. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Enter your contact information in the fields provided.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`coordinator_details`, `text`, optional): This field is where you should enter any relevant contact details, such as your position or department, that will help us contact you.
* **Name** (`coordinator_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your full name to help us associate you with your contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can reach you digitally.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number that you would like us to have for emergency or urgent matters, please enter it here.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter its name to help us connect you with your institution.

## Tips
* If you have any trouble filling out this form, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* If you have already provided your contact information, please do not fill out this form again.
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
