# it_specialist_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Specialist Onboarding Form is used by new employees to provide their personal and professional details to the IT department for quick onboarding and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your new employee details in the "New Employee Details" field.
2. Select your job title from the "Job Title" dropdown menu.
3. If your job title is not listed, enter the title of your job in the "Other Job Title" field.
4. Choose your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
5. Enter your manager's details, including their name, job title, and department.
6. Provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employee Details**: Enter your personal and professional details, such as your name, job title, and department.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the dropdown menu. If your job title is not listed, choose "Other" and enter your title in the "Other Job Title" field.
* **Other Job Title**: If you selected "Other" as your job title, enter your actual job title here.
* **Department**: Choose your department from the dropdown menu.
* **Manager Details**: Enter your manager's name, job title, and department.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Department**: Choose your manager's department from the dropdown menu.
* **Employee Details**: Enter your personal details, such as your name, job title, and department.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Employee Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Employee Department**: Choose your department from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your HR representative.
* Once you have completed the form, submit it for review and approval.
