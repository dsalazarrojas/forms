# corporate_credit_card_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate credit card request form is designed for authorized personnel to request a corporate credit card for work-related expenses. This form should not be used for personal credit card requests or other non-work-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name as it appears on your official personnel documents.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. Select the department you belong to.
4. Enter your job title as it appears on your official personnel documents.
5. Provide a brief explanation for your reason for requesting the corporate credit card.
6. Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
7. Enter your supervisor's email address.
8. Enter your supervisor's contact phone number.
8. If required, enter the reason your supervisor should be aware of the card benefits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required)
* Enter your full employee name as it appears on your official personnel documents.
### 2. **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required)
* Enter your official company email address.
### 3. **Company** (`company`, `text`, required)
* Enter your department or team name, or the name of your company.
### 4. **Department** (`department`, `text`, required)
* Enter the department or team you belong to.
### 5. **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required)
* Enter your official job title as it appears on your official personnel documents.
### 6. **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required)
* Provide a brief explanation for your request for a corporate credit card.
### 7. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required)
* Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
### 8. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, `email`, required)
* Enter your supervisor's official company email address.
### 9. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional)
* Enter your supervisor's contact phone number (if applicable).
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals or permissions before submitting this form.
* If you are unsure about any information or process, contact your supervisor or HR department.
