# Risk Assessment Officer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Officer Information Form is a crucial document that captures the essential details of Risk Assessment Officers. It helps ensure that the necessary information is gathered correctly and consistently, facilitating effective risk management and assessment within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Select your Job Title from the given options.
3. Choose your department affiliation from the options provided.
4. Identify your Reporting Line.
5. Select your Reporting Manager from the given options.
6. Enter your Contact Phone number.
7. Enter a valid Contact Email address.
8. Select the Onboarding Date.
9. Choose your certification(s) from the provided options (if applicable).
10. Enter the Certification Date for each selected certification.
11. Select the status of your certification (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
12. Provide any additional comments or notes as Risk Assessment Officer Notes or Onboarding Comments.
13. Select any additional comments or notes as Risk Assessment Officer Comments or Onboarding Agreed Comments.
14. Confirm your agreement with the Risk Assessment Officer Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is known to you. (Required)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is known to you. (Required)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your official account. (Required)
* **Job Title**: Select your official job title from the given options. (Required)
* **Department**: Choose your department affiliation from the options provided. (Optional)
* **Reporting Line**: Identify your reporting line, which is typically your direct supervisor or manager. (Required)
* **Reporting Manager**: Select your reporting manager from the given options. (Optional)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for official communications. (Required)
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid contact email address for official communications. (Required)
* **Onboarding Date**: Select the date you underwent onboarding. (Required)
* **Certification**: Select the certification(s) you have obtained, if any. (Optional)
* **Certification Date**: Enter the date you obtained each selected certification. (Required for each certification)
* **Certification Status**: Select the status of your certification (Active, Inactive, or Pending). (Optional)
* **Risk Assessment Officer Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes as a Risk Assessment Officer. (Optional)
* **Onboarding Comments**: Provide any comments or notes related to your onboarding. (Optional)
* **Risk Assessment Officer Comments**: Select any additional comments or notes as a Risk Assessment Officer. (Optional)
* **Other**: Provide any additional information not captured in other fields. (Optional)
* **Risk Assessment Officer Agreement**: Confirm your agreement with the information provided. (Optional)
* **Onboarding Agreed**: Confirm your agreement with the onboarding process. (Optional)
* **Risk Assessment Officer Comments**: Select any additional comments or notes as a Risk Assessment Officer. (Optional)
* **Risk Assessment Officer Agreement Comments**: Select any additional comments or notes as a Risk Assessment Officer Agreement. (Optional)
