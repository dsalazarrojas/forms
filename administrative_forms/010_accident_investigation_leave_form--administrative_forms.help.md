# Accident Investigation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accident Investigation Leave Form is a critical document used to report work-related accidents, injuries, or illnesses that result in leave requests. This form helps our organization's risk management and compliance teams track and manage leave requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely before submission.
2. Review each section and its corresponding fields to understand their purpose and requirements.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your personnel records. 
* **Employee ID**: Your unique identifier as assigned to you by the organization. 
* **Department**: The department you belong to within the organization. 
* **Job Title**: Your role or position within the department. 
* **Supervisor Name**: The name of your supervisor or manager. 
* **Email Address**: Your work email address. 
* **Phone Number**: Your work phone number. 
* **Date of Accident**: The date the accident or injury occurred. 
* **Time of Accident**: The time the accident or injury occurred. 
* **Location of Accident**: A brief description of where the accident or injury occurred within the organization's premises. 
* **Brief Description of the Accident**: A concise summary of the events leading up to the accident or injury. 
* **Type of Injury Sustained**: Choose from the list of options provided to describe the nature of your injury (e.g., Fracture, Sprain or Strain, Laceration, Burn, etc.). 
* **Leave Start Date**: The date you would like to begin your leave period. 
* **Estimated Return Date**: The date you expect to return to work after your leave. 
* **Type of Leave Requested**: Choose from the list of options provided to describe the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Paid Sick Leave, Workers Compensation Leave, Short-term Disability, etc.). 
* **Has Medical Clearance Been Obtained for Leave**: Select whether you have obtained medical clearance for your leave. 
* **Current Investigation Status**: Choose from the list of options provided to describe the current status of the investigation. 
* **Name of Investigating Officer or Manager**: The name of the person responsible for investigating the incident. 
* **Has a Workers Compensation Claim Been Filed**: Select whether a workers compensation claim has been filed. 
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Any additional information or comments you would like to share regarding the incident or your leave request.
