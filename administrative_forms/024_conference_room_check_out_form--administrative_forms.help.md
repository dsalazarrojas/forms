# Conference Room Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that the conference room is left in a satisfactory state after use. It will ask you for information about your experience with the room, the condition of the room, and any issues or concerns you may have.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience with the conference room.
3. Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help maintain the quality of our facilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Full Name**: Your full name is required so we can address any issues or concerns with your check-out. 
* **Email Address**: Your contact email is required so we can reach out to you with any questions or for any follow-up. 
* **Phone Number** (optional): Your contact phone number can be provided for any follow-up questions or concerns. 
* **Department** (required): Please enter your department to help us identify the user of the room. 
* **Room Name**: Select the room from the list that you used. 
* **Check-Out Date** (required): Enter the date you are checking out of the room. 
* **Check-Out Time** (required): Enter the time you are checking out of the room. 
* **Booking Reference Number** (optional): If you have a reference number for your booking, please provide it. 
* **Event Purpose** (optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your meeting or event. 
* **Actual Number of Attendees** (optional): If you had a large or small group, please provide the actual number of people who attended. 
* **Room Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the room. 
* **Any Furniture Damage**: Evaluate if there was any significant damage to the furniture. 
* **Damage Details** (optional): If you have any details about the damage, please provide them. 
* **Equipment Status**: Confirm the status of the room's equipment. 
* **Missing or Damaged Equipment** (optional): If there were any issues with the equipment, please list them. 
* **Trash Disposed Properly**: Confirm if all trash has been removed from the room. 
* **Furniture Rearranged**: Confirm if the furniture has been returned to its original state. 
* **Whiteboard Cleaned**: Confirm if the whiteboard has been cleaned. 
* **Lights Turned Off**: Confirm if all lights have been turned off. 
* **AC Adjusted**: Confirm if the temperature has been adjusted. 
* **Windows and Doors Secured**: Confirm if all windows and doors have been locked. 
* **Key or Access Card Returned**: Confirm if you have returned all access cards or keys. 
* **Any Issues to Report**: If there were any issues with the room, please describe them. 
* **Overall Room Experience**: Evaluate your experience with the room on a scale of 1-5. 
* **Additional Comments** (optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them. 
* **I Confirm Check-Out is Complete**: Confirm that you have completed all tasks and are satisfied with the room's state. 

This form is designed to help us maintain our facilities and ensure that they are always in good working order. Please take a few minutes to complete it thoroughly and accurately. Thank you for your cooperation.
