# Guide For Using Online Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help users understand the process of using online forms. It will walk you through the necessary steps and provide detailed explanations for each field to ensure a smooth and error-free submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the "Guide for Using Online Forms" field, which provides an overview of the process.
2. Select one of the options in the "Administrative Forms" field that best describes your current form use case.
3. Enter the date for when you "Businesses Ensure Effective Utilization" (if this is applicable to your situation).
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Administrative Forms** (`administrative_forms`, `select_one`, required: false): This field gives you the option to select from a list of available administrative forms. Choose the one that best describes your current form use case.

- **Businesses Ensure Effective Utilization** (`businesses_ensure_effective_utilization`, `date`, required: true): This field is used to track the date for when you "Businesses Ensure Effective Utilization". Ensure that you enter a valid date in this field.
