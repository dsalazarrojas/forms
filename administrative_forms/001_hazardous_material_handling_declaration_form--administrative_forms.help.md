# hazardous_material_handling_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the handling and transportation of hazardous materials. It collects relevant information from employees handling the materials, including material details, employee information, and handling protocols. The form is required to be filled out accurately and completely to ensure proper handling and transportation procedures are followed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, paying close attention to all fields marked as required.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and truthfully.
3. Double-check your work before submitting the form to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Type** (`material_type`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all applicable material types that you are handling or transporting.
* **Material Quantity** (`material_quantity`, `number`, `required`): Enter the quantity of the material being handled or transported.
* **Handling Status** (`handling_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Specify the current status of the material handling or transportation.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your name as an employee handling or transporting the material.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your position or role as an employee handling or transporting the material.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your company's address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a contact number for your company or department.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a contact email for your company or department.
* **Material Details** (`material_details`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional details about the material being handled or transported.
* **Handling Protocols** (`handling_protocols`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional protocols or procedures followed while handling or transporting the material.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, `required`): Sign the form with your signature to confirm your information.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Signature of Approving Officer** (`signature_of_approving_officer`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the signature of the approving officer.
* **Title of Authorizing Officer** (`title_of_authorizing_officer`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the title of the authorizing officer.
* **Name of Authorizing Officer** (`name_of_authorizing_officer`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the authorizing officer.
* **Name of Authorizing Officer Signature** (`name_of_authorizing_officer_signature`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the signature of the authorizing officer.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date the form was approved.
* **Time Approved** (`time_approved`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time the form was approved.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the approving officer.
* **Approved Title** (`approved_title`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the title of the approving officer.
* **Approved Signature** (`approved_signature`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the signature of the approving officer.

## Tips

* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out properly.
* If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the relevant authorities for clarification.
