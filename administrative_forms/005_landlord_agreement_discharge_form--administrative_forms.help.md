# Landlord Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Agreement Discharge Form is used to document the termination of a landlord agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form provides a formal record of the agreement's end date, the reason for termination, and any applicable fees or notice periods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorization to complete this form.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to all required fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant whose agreement is being terminated.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord who signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement End Date**: Enter the date the agreement will end.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Enter a brief description of why the agreement is being terminated.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days or weeks the tenant has been notified of the termination.
* **Discharge Fee**: Enter any applicable fees for the termination.
* **Payment Method**: Check if payment was made for the termination.
* **Landlord Signature**: Sign below to confirm the termination of the agreement.
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign below to confirm the termination of the agreement.
### Page 2
* **Landlord Name Label**: Enter a brief description of the landlord's name.
* **Tenant Name Label**: Enter a brief description of the tenant's name.
* **Agreement Date Label**: Enter a brief description of the agreement date.
* **Agreement End Date Label**: Enter a brief description of the agreement end date.
* **Reason For Discharge Label**: Enter a brief description of the reason for termination.
* **Notice Period Label**: Enter a brief description of the notice period.
* **Discharge Fee Label**: Enter a brief description of the discharge fee.
* **Payment Method Label**: Enter a brief description of the payment method.
* **Landlord Signature Label**: Enter a brief description of the landlord's signature.
* **Tenant Signature Label**: Enter a brief description of the tenant's signature.
* **Landlord Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the landlord.
* **Tenant Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the tenant.
* **Discharge Condition**: Enter a brief description of the discharge condition.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date of termination.
* **Discharge Status**: Select whether the agreement is active or inactive.

## Tips
- Ensure that the agreement has been mutually agreed upon by both parties before completing this form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Keep a copy of the completed form for reference purposes.
