## medical_certificate_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The `medical_certificate_request_form` is a form used by healthcare providers to request medical certificates for patients. It is designed to collect relevant patient and provider information to facilitate the certificate request process. Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to ensure a smooth certificate issuance process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting.
2. Enter the patient's information in the **patient_info** field.
3. Provide the necessary details about the medical provider in the **provider_info** field.
4. Select the appropriate **certificate_type** from the provided options.
5. Specify the **reason_for_request**, if applicable.
6. Add your digital signature in the **signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (Text, Optional):
    Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information as required.
* **provider_info** (Text, Optional):
    Enter the medical provider's name, title, and other relevant contact details.
* **certificate_type** (Select Multiple, Required):
    Choose one or more of the available options to indicate the type of medical certificate being requested.
* **reason_for_request** (Text, Optional):
    Provide a brief explanation for the certificate request, if necessary.
* **signature** (Text, Required):
    Add your digital signature to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently to avoid delays in the certificate issuance process.
* Review the form carefully before submission to prevent errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant documentation or contact the medical department for assistance.
* This form is designed for healthcare providers; however, if you are a patient, please consult the relevant medical staff for guidance on how to initiate the certificate request process.
