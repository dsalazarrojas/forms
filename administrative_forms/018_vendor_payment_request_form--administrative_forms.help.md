# Vendor Payment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vendor Payment Request Form" is used for requesting payment from vendors. Fill out this form to provide necessary information to process a payment request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields (if any)
*   Fill in the vendor's details, including their name and payment information
*   Specify the payment amount and payment method
*   Choose the payment terms and conditions
*   Enter the date and time of the request
*   Add any comments or additional information
*   Enter the vendor's email and phone number (if applicable)
*   Obtain signatures from both the vendor and finance personnel

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vendor Details**: Enter the vendor's name and any other relevant details.
    *   Label: `Vendor Details`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Vendor Payment Information**: Enter the payment information for the vendor.
    *   Label: `Vendor Payment Information`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount to be paid to the vendor.
    *   Label: `Payment Amount`
    *   Type: `number`
    *   Required: No
*   **Payment Method**: Choose from the available payment methods. 
    *   Label: `Payment Method`
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: No
*   **Payment Terms**: Select all applicable payment terms and conditions.
    *   Label: `Payment Terms`
    *   Type: `select_multiple`
    *   Required: No
*   **Request Date**: Enter the date of the request.
    *   Label: `Request Date`
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: No
*   **Request Time**: Enter the time of the request.
    *   Label: `Request Time`
    *   Type: `time`
    *   Required: No
*   **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information.
    *   Label: `Comments`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Email**: Enter the vendor's email address (if applicable).
    *   Label: `Email`
    *   Type: `email`
    *   Required: No
*   **Phone**: Enter the vendor's phone number (if applicable).
    *   Label: `Phone`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Vendor Signature**: Obtain a signature from the vendor.
    *   Label: `Vendor Signature`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Finance Signature**: Obtain a signature from finance personnel.
    *   Label: `Finance Signature`
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: No
*   **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
    *   Label: `Date Signed`
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: No
*   **Status**: Select the status of the payment request.
    *   Label: `Status`
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: No
