# Staff Duty Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Duty Checklist form is a crucial tool for staff members to manage their duties and responsibilities. It helps in ensuring that all necessary information is provided for specific duties, making it easier for administrators to schedule and coordinate tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
2.  Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
3.  Select your available tasks for the specified duty in the Select Available Tasks field.
4.  Enter the date of your availability for the specified duty in the Availability Date field.
5.  Enter your availability time for the specified duty in the Availability Time field.
6.  Provide a status update in the Status field, indicating your current status.
7.  List the tasks you have completed for the specified duty in the Completed Tasks field.
8.  Enter the deadline for the specified duty in the Deadline field, if applicable.
9.  Enter your start time for the specified duty in the Start Time field, if applicable.
10.  Enter any additional notes related to the duty in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Email Address** (staff_duty_form_1, email, required): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for work-related matters.
*   **Phone Number** (staff_phone_form_2, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any additional contact information.
*   **Select Available Tasks** (staff_available_form_3, select_multiple, required): Choose the specific tasks you are available to perform for the duty.
*   **Availability Date** (staff_availability_date_form_4, date, required): Enter the date when you will be available for the duty.
*   **Availability Time** (staff_availability_time_form_5, time, required): Enter the time when you will be available for the duty.
*   **Note** (staff_note_form_6, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the duty.
*   **Status** (staff_status_form_7, select_one, required): Indicate your current status for the duty, whether it's 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
*   **Completed Tasks** (staff_completed_tasks_form_8, select_multiple, required): List the tasks you have completed for the duty.
*   **Deadline** (staff_deadline_form_9, date, optional): Enter the deadline for the specified duty, if applicable.
*   **Start Time** (staff_start_time_form_10, time, optional): Enter the start time for the specified duty, if applicable.
*   **Notes** (staff_notes_form_11, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the duty.
*   **Select Multiple Options** (staff_availability_form_12, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options related to your availability.
*   **Select One Option** (staff_availability_form_13, select_one, required): Select one option related to your availability.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Use the correct format for date and time fields to avoid errors.
*   Use clear and concise language for the notes field.
*   Double-check your selections for accuracy.
*   If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to contact the administrator.
