# Client Agreement And Terms Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for collecting information about a client's agreement and terms for services such as legal or consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Enter your client's legal name (individual or entity) in the "Client Legal Name (Individual or Entity)" field.
3. If the client is a business entity, enter the name of the authorized representative in the "Authorized Representative Name" field.
4. Provide the business address in the "Business/Primary Address" field.
5. Enter your email address that will be used for official notices in the "Email for Official Notices" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
7. Describe the services to be provided in the "Description of Services to be Provided" field.
8. Specify the term of the agreement (e.g., 12 months, ongoing, project-based) in the "Term of Agreement" field.
9. Choose the agreed-upon fee structure (e.g., fixed price, hourly rate, monthly retainer) in the "Agreed Fee Structure" field.
10. Select the payment terms (e.g., upon receipt, net 15, net 30, monthly in advance) in the "Payment Due Terms" field.
11. Agree to the terms and conditions, including the 30-day written notice requirement for termination in the "I acknowledge the 30-day written notice requirement for termination" field.
12. Agree to the limitation of liability clause in the "I have read and agree to the limitation of liability clause" field.
13. Agree to the mutual confidentiality and non-disclosure terms in the "I agree to the mutual confidentiality and non-disclosure terms" field.
14. Agree that the agreement is governed by the laws of this state in the "I agree that this agreement is governed by the laws of this state" field.
15. Sign your name and date in the "Digital Signature (Type Full Legal Name)" field and select the date of agreement in the "Date Signed" field.
16. Confirm if you have attached the full detailed terms document in the "Have you attached the full detailed terms document?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Legal Name (Individual or Entity)** (Required): Please enter the full name of the client, either as an individual or a business entity.
* **Authorized Representative Name**: If the client is a business entity, enter the name of the authorized representative.
* **Business/Primary Address** (Required): Enter the client's business or primary address.
* **Email for Official Notices** (Required): Enter the email address that will be used for official notices.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Required): Enter the contact phone number of the client.
* **Description of Services to be Provided** (Required): Briefly summarize the services to be provided to the client.
* **Term of Agreement** (Required): Specify the term of the agreement (e.g., 12 months, ongoing, project-based).
* **Agreed Fee Structure** (Required): Choose the agreed-upon fee structure (e.g., fixed price, hourly rate, monthly retainer).
* **Payment Due Terms** (Required): Select the payment terms (e.g., upon receipt, net 15, net 30, monthly in advance).
* **I acknowledge the 30-day written notice requirement for termination**: Agree to the 30-day written notice requirement for termination.
* **I have read and agree to the limitation of liability clause**: Agree to the limitation of liability clause.
* **I agree to the mutual confidentiality and non-disclosure terms**: Agree to the mutual confidentiality and non-disclosure terms.
* **I agree that this agreement is governed by the laws of this state**: Agree that the agreement is governed by the laws of this state.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Legal Name)** (Required): Sign your name in this field.
* **Date Signed** (Required): Select the date of agreement.
* **Have you attached the full detailed terms document?**: Confirm if you have attached the full detailed terms document.
