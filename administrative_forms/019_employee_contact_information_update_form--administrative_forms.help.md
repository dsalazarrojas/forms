# Employee Contact Information Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow employees to update their contact information, such as their name, email, department, job title, phone number, date of birth, and any additional notes or comments. This form is intended for internal use by HR or management staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately.
2. Review each field to ensure that it is correct and up-to-date.
3. Enter the employee's first name in the **first_name** field.
	* This is the employee's first name.
	* Please enter the employee's first name (e.g., John).
4. Enter the employee's last name in the **last_name** field.
	* This is the employee's last name.
	* Please enter the employee's last name (e.g., Doe).
5. Enter the employee's email address in the **email** field.
	* This is the employee's email address.
	* Please enter a valid email address (e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)).
6. Select the employee's department from the available options in the **department** field.
	* Please select the employee's department (e.g., IT, HR, Sales).
7. Enter the employee's job title in the **job_title** field.
	* This is the employee's job title or position.
	* Please enter the employee's job title (e.g., Software Engineer, Marketing Manager).
8. Enter the employee's phone number in the **phone_number** field.
	* This is the employee's phone number.
	* Please enter a valid phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
9. Enter the employee's date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
	* This is the employee's date of birth.
	* Please enter a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 01/01/1990).
10. Select the employee's time zone in the **time** field.
	* Please select the employee's time zone.
	* This field is currently not being used.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **note** field.
	* This is an optional field for any additional comments or updates.
	* Please enter any relevant notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR or management staff.
* If you are updating an existing record, please ensure that the changes are accurate and relevant.
