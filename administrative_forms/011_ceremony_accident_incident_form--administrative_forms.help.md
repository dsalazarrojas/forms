# Ceremony Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ceremony Accident Incident Form" is designed to collect information about incidents that occur during ceremonies, such as weddings, funerals, and other events. This form helps to ensure that incidents are reported accurately and promptly, allowing for swift and effective action to be taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date and time when the incident occurred (fields 1 & 2).
2. Provide your full name, role, and contact information as the reporter (fields 3-5).
3. Select the type of ceremony where the incident occurred (field 6).
4. Indicate the category of incident (field 8).
5. List the names and contact information of persons involved in the incident (field 9).
6. Describe any injuries sustained (field 10).
7. Indicate if medical attention was provided or required (field 11).
8. Confirm if there were witnesses to the incident (field 12).
9. Provide the names and contact information of witnesses (field 13).
10. Provide a detailed account of what happened (field 14).
11. Select contributing factors that may have led to the incident (field 15).
12. Indicate if there was any property damage (field 16).
13. Describe the property damage (field 17).
14. State the immediate actions taken after the incident (field 18).
15. Confirm if photos or video evidence are available (field 19).
16. Indicate if a police report was filed (field 20).
17. Confirm if this incident requires follow-up action (field 21).
18. Sign and date the report (fields 22 & 23).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`): Enter your full name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Role** (`reporter_role`): Select your role at the ceremony (e.g., Event coordinator, Venue staff, Vendor, Guest, Family member, Security personnel, Other).
* **Reporter Contact Information** (`reporter_contact`): Enter your phone number and email.
* **Ceremony Type** (`ceremony_type`): Select the type of ceremony where the incident occurred (e.g., Wedding ceremony, Funeral service, Memorial service, Graduation ceremony, Religious ceremony, Corporate event, Other).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): Select the category of incident (e.g., Slip and fall, Property damage, Medical emergency, Altercation, Equipment malfunction, Weather-related, Other).
* **Persons Involved** (`persons_involved`): List the names of all individuals involved in the incident.
* **Injuries Sustained** (`injuries_sustained`): Describe any injuries sustained.
* **Medical Attention Required** (`medical_attention`): Indicate if medical attention was provided.
* **Were There Witnesses** (`witnesses_present`): Confirm if there were witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Names and Contact Information** (`witness_names`): Provide the names and contact information of witnesses.
* **Detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`): Select contributing factors that may have led to the incident.
* **Property Damage** (`property_damage`): Indicate if there was any property damage.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`): Describe the property damage.
* **Immediate Actions Taken** (`immediate_actions`): State the actions taken immediately after the incident.
* **Photos Available** (`photos_available`): Confirm if photos or video evidence are available.
* **Police Report Filed** (`police_report_filed`): Indicate if a police report was filed.
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`): Confirm if this incident requires follow-up action.
* **Reporter Signature** (`signature`): Sign your name as the reporter.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Date of report submission.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields and assumes that the form is used to report incidents during ceremonies.
