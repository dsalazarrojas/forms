# Incident Report Closure Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm that the incident has been properly reviewed and closed off.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form when an incident has been properly reviewed and is ready for closure. Please fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Acknowledge Incident Report Closure** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form`, text, optional): This field should be filled with a simple "yes" or acknowledgement that the incident has been properly reviewed.
- **Date Acknowledged** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_2`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the incident was acknowledged.
- **Time Acknowledged** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_3`, time, optional): Enter the time on which the incident was acknowledged.
- **Reason for Closure** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_4`, text, optional): Explain the reason for closing the incident.
- **Review and Acceptance** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_5`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to Review and Accept, Review and Reject, or Review and Escalate the closure.
- **Review and Acceptance Reason** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_6`, text, optional): Provide a reason for the choice made in Review and Acceptance.
- **Acknowledgement Comments** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_7`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the closure of the incident.
- **Signature** (`incident_report_closure_acknowledgement_form_8`, text, optional): Enter your official signature to confirm closure.

## Tips
- Double-check the incident has been properly reviewed and addressed before proceeding.
- Make sure to include a reason for closure to provide transparency.
- This form should only be filled out when the incident is ready for closure.
- If Review and Reject or Review and Escalate is chosen, ensure that the reason for doing so is clearly stated.
