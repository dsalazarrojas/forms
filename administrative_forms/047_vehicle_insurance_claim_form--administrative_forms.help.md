# Vehicle Insurance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Insurance Claim Form is designed to collect essential information for processing vehicle insurance claims. This form is used by policyholders to provide details about the incident, their vehicle, and their own information to facilitate the claim process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Policyholder Information Form** (`policyholder_info_form`, Required):
	*   This section is used to collect basic information about the policyholder.
2.  **Vehicle Information Form** (`vehicle_form`, Required):
	*   Enter your vehicle's details such as the vehicle type, make, and registration number to provide a clear understanding of the vehicle's identity.
3.  **Claim Details Form** (`claim_details_form`, Required):
	*   Provide a clear description of the claim, including the date and time of the incident.
4.  **Claimant Information Form** (`claimant_info_form`, Required):
	*   Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details about the person making the claim.
5.  **Damage Details Form** (`damage_details_form`, Required):
	*   Describe the damage to your vehicle and provide supporting documentation if applicable.
6.  **Vehicle Registration Form** (`vehicle_registration_form`, Required):
	*   Enter your vehicle's registration details for accurate identification.
7.  **Supporting Documents Form** (`supporting_documents_form`, Required):
	*   Upload any supporting documents such as photos, repair estimates, or medical records as relevant to the claim.
8.  **Upload Document Form** (`upload_document_form`, Required):
	*   Upload any additional documents that support your claim.
9.  **Policyholder Signature Form** (`policyholder_signature_form`, Required):
	*   Sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
10. **Claimant Signature Form** (`claimant_signature_form`, Required):
	*   Sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
11. **Claimant Contact Form** (`claimant_contact_form`, Required):
	*   Provide your contact information to ensure we can reach you for further clarification or updates.
12.  **Insurance Information Form** (`insurance_info_form`, Required):
	*   Enter your insurance policy details for accurate processing.
13. **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, Required):
*   Once all necessary information is provided, submit this form to initiate the claim process.
## Tips

*   Before starting, ensure you have all required information and supporting documents ready.
*   Double-check the accuracy of the information provided to avoid delays.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask for clarification.
