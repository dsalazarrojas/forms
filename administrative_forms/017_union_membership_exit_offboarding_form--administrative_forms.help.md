# Union Membership Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to notify the union that you are exiting your membership. Please fill out the form accurately to help the union process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are a union member and that you are actively resigning from the union.
2. Fill in your **Name** in the field provided, making sure to spell it correctly.
3. Enter your **Email** in the format of `username@example.com` or `example.com`.
4. Provide a brief explanation for **Reason for Resigning**. This will help the union understand your reasons for leaving.
5. Enter the **Last Date of Union Membership**, using the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Enter your **Membership Number**, if you have one.
7. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please fill in the **Union Membership Exit Offboarding Form Comments** field.
8. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: The name that you want to be used to identify you as the member exiting the union.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that is associated with your union membership.
* **Reason for Resigning**: Provide a brief explanation for your reason of leaving the union. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Last Date of Union Membership**: The date when your union membership ended.
* **Membership Number**: The number associated with your union membership. If you don't have one, please leave this field blank.
* **Union Name**: The name of the union you are exiting.
* **Union Membership Exit Offboarding Form Comments**: Any additional comments or concerns you may have about the offboarding process.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your submission is accurate and complete to avoid any issues with your union membership.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your union representative for assistance.
* Please note that this form is only for union members who are actively resigning from the union. If you are transferring to another union, please use the union transfer form instead.
