# Work Hours Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate and record work hours for employees, ensuring that they have accurately reported their working hours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Enter the **start time** and **end time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
3. Enter the number of **hours worked**.
4. Enter the **break duration**, if applicable.
5. Enter the **pay rate** for the hours worked, if applicable.
6. Enter any **notes**, if necessary.
7. Enter the employee's **email** address and **phone number**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Hours (page 1)** (required/optional): This is the main section where you can enter the total hours worked by the employee for the given date.
* **Employee ID (page 2)**: Enter the employee ID number associated with the employee whose work hours you are reporting.
* **Date (page 3)**: Enter the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` for which you are reporting the work hours.
* **Start Time (page 4)**: Enter the start time of the work hours in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **End Time (page 5)**: Enter the end time of the work hours in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Hours Worked (page 6)**: Enter the total number of hours worked by the employee for the given date.
* **Break Duration (page 7)**: Enter the length of any break taken during the work hours, if applicable.
* **Pay Rate (page 8)**: Enter the pay rate for the hours worked, if applicable.
* **Notes (page 9)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the reported work hours.
* **Employee Email (page 10)**: Enter the employee's email address for reference.
* **Phone (page 11)**: Enter the employee's phone number for reference.
