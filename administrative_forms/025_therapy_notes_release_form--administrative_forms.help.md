# Therapy Notes Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the release of therapy notes, ensuring that clients understand their options for sharing confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client name**: Enter the name of the client for whom you are releasing the therapy notes.
2. **Provider name**: Enter the name of the provider or therapist who has provided treatment.
3. **Date of signature**: Select the date on which you are signing to release the notes.
4. **Release type**: Specify whether the notes are being released as **Signed** or **Unsigned**.
5. **Client signature**: This field is optional but allows the client to acknowledge their understanding of the release.
6. **Date notarized**: If the notes are being notarized, enter the date of notarization.
7. **Additional notes**: Use this field to provide any additional context or comments about the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release notes**:
	+ **Release notes** (text): Provide a brief description of the therapy notes being released, if desired.
* **Client name**:
	+ **Client name** (text): Enter the name of the client for whom you are releasing the notes.
* **Provider name**:
	+ **Provider name** (text): Enter the name of the provider or therapist who has provided treatment.
* **Date of signature**:
	+ **Date of signature** (date): Select the date on which you are signing to release the notes.
* **Release type**:
	+ **Release type** (select one):
		- **Signed**: Indicates that the notes are being released with the client's signature.
		- **Unsigned**: Indicates that the notes are being released without the client's signature.
* **Client signature**:
	+ **Client signature** (text): This field is optional but allows the client to acknowledge their understanding of the release.
* **Date notarized**:
	+ **Date notarized** (date): If the notes are being notarized, enter the date of notarization.
* **Additional notes**:
	+ **Additional notes** (text): Use this field to provide any additional context or comments about the release.
