# Articles Of Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Articles Of Amendment Form is a document used to update or modify the articles of incorporation of a company. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the amendments are processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name in the **Company Name** field (page 1).
2. Enter the incorporation or registration number in the **Incorporation or Registration Number** field (page 1).
3. Specify the **State or Jurisdiction of Incorporation** where the company was initially incorporated (page 1).
4. Enter the **Effective Date of Amendment** when the amendment will take effect (page 1).
5. Select the **Section of Articles Being Amended** from the options provided (page 1).
6. Enter the **Reason for Amendment** (page 1).
7. Enter the **Original Article Provision** and **Amended Article Provision** that are being changed (page 2).
8. Select whether **Shareholder Approval Was Obtained** (page 2).
9. Enter the **Board of Directors Approval Date** when the board approved the amendment (page 2).
10. Enter the **Authorized Signatory Name** (page 3).
11. Enter the **Signatory Title or Position** (page 3).
12. Enter the **Signatory Email** (page 3).
13. Specify the **State for Filing** where the amendment will be filed (page 3).
14. (Optional) Enter the **Legal Counsel Name** if applicable (page 4).
15. Enter any **Additional Notes or Comments** on page 4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the full name of the company as it appears on its articles of incorporation.
* **Incorporation or Registration Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the company by the state or jurisdiction.
* **State or Jurisdiction of Incorporation**: Specify the state or jurisdiction where the company was initially incorporated.
* **Effective Date of Amendment**: Enter the date when the amendment will take effect.
* **Section of Articles Being Amended**: Select the section of the articles of incorporation being amended from the options provided:
	+ Name of Corporation
	+ Principal Office Address
	+ Registered Agent
	+ Board of Directors
	+ Authorized Stock
	+ Bylaws
	+ Other
* **Reason for Amendment**: Explain the reason for the amendment.
* **Original Article Provision**: Enter the original text of the provision being amended.
* **Amended Article Provision**: Enter the text of the amended provision.
* **Shareholder Approval Was Obtained**: Select whether shareholder approval was obtained for the amendment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not Applicable
* **Board of Directors Approval Date**: Enter the date when the board of directors approved the amendment.
* **Authorized Signatory Name**: Enter the name of the person authorizing the amendment.
* **Signatory Title or Position**: Enter the title or position of the signatory.
* **Signatory Email**: Enter the signatory's email address.
* **State for Filing**: Specify the state where the amendment will be filed.
* **Legal Counsel Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of the legal counsel who helped with the amendment.
* **Additional Notes or Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the amendment.
