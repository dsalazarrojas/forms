# Lease Agreement Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Agreement Consent Form is used to capture consent and agreement details from tenants related to a lease agreement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lease Agreement Consent Form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the link provided to access the form.
*   Fill out each field accordingly, making sure to enter accurate information.
*   For fields where multiple options are available, select the most relevant or accurate option.
*   Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lease Agreement Consent Form (1)**: 
    *   This is the title of the form, which cannot be edited.
*   **tenant name (2)**: 
    *   Enter the name of the tenant who is signing the lease.
*   **start date (3)**: 
    *   Enter the start date of the lease agreement.
*   **end date (4)**: 
    *   Enter the end date of the lease agreement.
*   **lease amount (5)**: 
    *   Enter the total amount of the lease.
*   **tenant consent (6)**: 
    *   Select 'True' if the tenant has reviewed and agreed to the lease terms.
*   **landlord name (7)**: 
    *   Enter the name of the landlord or property manager who is handling the lease.
*   **property manager (8)**: 
    *   Enter the name of the property manager.
*   **lease term (9)**: 
    *   This field is currently unclear in purpose, but it seems to be intended to capture lease term details.
*   **rent (10)**: 
    *   Enter the rent amount for the lease.
*   **payment method (11)**: 
    *   Select the method of payment for the lease (e.g., bank draft, check, online bank transfer).
*   **tenant email (12)**: 
    *   Enter the email address of the tenant.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure everything is complete and correct.
