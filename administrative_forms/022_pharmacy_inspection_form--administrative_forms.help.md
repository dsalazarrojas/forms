# Pharmacy Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pharmacy Inspection Form is used to inspect and evaluate a pharmacy's compliance with safety and regulatory standards. It helps the inspector to assess the pharmacy's overall safety and cleanliness, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide general information about the pharmacy, such as its address and contact details.
2. Answer questions related to safety standards, indicating if the standards are met (Yes/No).
3. Complete the compliance checklist with any necessary details.
4. Enter information about the facility, including its name and address.
5. Describe the current state of equipment maintenance, including any repairs or replacements needed.
6. Provide personnel information, including the number of staff members and their training status.
7. Describe the safety equipment available, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.
8. Document any corrective actions taken to address issues found during the inspection.
9. Rate the overall safety of the pharmacy on a scale of 0-10.
10. Leave any recommendations for the pharmacy to improve their safety and compliance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (text, General Information, required: false)
* General information about the pharmacy, including its address and contact details.

### Safety Standards (select_multiple, Safety Standards, required: false)
* Answer "Yes" if the safety standards are met, "No" if they are not.

### Compliance Checklist (text, Compliance Checklist, required: false)
* Provide any necessary details related to compliance with safety and regulatory standards.

### Facility Information (text, Facility Information, required: false)
* Enter the name and address of the pharmacy.

### Equipment Maintenance (text, Equipment Maintenance, required: false)
* Describe the current state of equipment maintenance, including any repairs or replacements needed.

### Personnel Information (select_one, Personnel Information, required: false)
* Answer "Yes" if staff members are properly trained and equipped, "No" if they are not.

### Safety Equipment (text, Safety Equipment, required: false)
* Describe the safety equipment available, such as fire extinguishers and first aid kits.

### Corrective Action (text, Corrective Action, required: false)
* Document any corrective actions taken to address issues found during the inspection.

### Overall Safety Rating (number, Overall Safety Rating, required: false)
* Rate the overall safety of the pharmacy on a scale of 0-10.

### Recommendations (text, Recommendations, required: false)
* Leave any recommendations for the pharmacy to improve their safety and compliance.
