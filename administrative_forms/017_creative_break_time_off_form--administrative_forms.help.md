# Creative Break Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Break Time Off Form is a form used by employees to request a creative break, which is a scheduled time off to focus on personal projects, creative pursuits, or other non-work-related activities. This form allows employees to submit a request for a specific period of time off, providing necessary details about their project, and reason for the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your desired break time off using the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
3. If applicable, fill in the "Project Title" field with a brief description of your personal project.
4. In the "Project Description" field, provide a brief description of your project.
5. Enter the length of your desired break time off in the "Break Length" field.
6. Optionally, provide additional details about your project in the "Project Details" field.
7. In the "Project Status" field, select the current status of your project.
8. If you have a specific reason for your request, you can provide it in the "Reason for Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Start Date**: The date you would like your creative break to start.
* **End Date**: The date you would like your creative break to end.
* **Project Title**: If you're working on a specific project, provide a brief title for it.
* **Project Description**: A brief description of your project.
* **Break Length**: The amount of time you would like your break to be in days.
* **Project Details**: Any additional details about your project that might be helpful for the management to understand.
* **Project Status**: Select the current status of your project (e.g., "Active", "Completed", "On Hold", etc.).
* **Reason for Request**: If you have a specific reason for your request, please provide it here (e.g., "Personal project", "Family vacation", etc.).
