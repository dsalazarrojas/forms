# Public Record Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Public Record Access Request Form is a tool used to request access to public records for legitimate purposes, such as research or other authorized use.

## How To Complete This Form

Before completing this form, please ensure you have a clear understanding of the information you are requesting and the purpose of your request.

### Steps to Complete the Form:

1. Select the relevant Category from the dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief Description of the records you are requesting access to.
3. Enter your Email address to allow us to contact you.
4. Enter your Phone Number in case we need to contact you.
5. Specify the Date and Time when you need access to the records.
6. Add any additional Note regarding your request (optional).
7. Choose the Assigned Tool from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of public record you are requesting access to. Please choose the relevant option that best describes the record you need access to.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the records you are requesting access to. This will help us understand your request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Specify the date when you need access to the records.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Specify the time when you need access to the records.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional note regarding your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the tool that you would like to use to access the records.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* We will review your request and respond to you regarding the status of your request.
