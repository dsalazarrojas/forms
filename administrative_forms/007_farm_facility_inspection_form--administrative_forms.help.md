# Farm Facility Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about farm facilities, including their address, contact details, and inspection history. It helps us understand the condition and status of the facilities, and identify any necessary improvements or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the Facility Address field with the farm facility's physical address.
2. Enter the Facility Location, which is the geographic area or region where the facility is located.
3. Provide the Facility Contact Name, which is the person responsible for the facility.
4. Enter the Facility Phone number and Facility Email for contact purposes.
5. If applicable, enter the Facility Date of Last Inspection and update the Facility Last Inspection Status to Active or Inactive.
6. If the last inspection was conducted, provide details about the Facility Last Inspection Findings and recommendations for improvement in the Facility Last Inspection Findings and Facility Last Inspection Recommendations fields.
7. Finally, select the Facility Inspection Frequency, which indicates how often the facility is inspected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Address (Text)**: Enter the farm facility's physical address, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Facility Location (Text)**: Enter the geographic area or region where the facility is located.
* **Facility Contact Name (Text)**: Enter the person responsible for the facility, e.g. "Farm Owner/Operator" or "Facility Manager".
* **Facility Phone (Text)**: Enter the primary phone number for the facility.
* **Facility Email (Email)**: Enter the primary email address for the facility.
* **Facility Date of Last Inspection (Date)**: If the facility has been inspected before, enter the date of the last inspection.
* **Facility Last Inspection Status (Select One)**: Select Active or Inactive to indicate the current status of the facility's last inspection.
* **Facility Last Inspection Findings (Text)**: If the facility was inspected, provide details about the findings from the last inspection.
* **Facility Last Inspection Recommendations (Text)**: If the facility was inspected, provide recommendations for improvement based on the last inspection's findings.
* **Facility Inspection Frequency (Select Multiple)**: Select how often the facility is inspected, e.g. "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the facility's contact person or your supervisor for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
