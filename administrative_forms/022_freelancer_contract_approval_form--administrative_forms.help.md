To create an efficient and user-friendly help guide for the Freelancer Contract Approval Form, we must first understand the form's purpose and the information required from the end-users. This form is used for the approval process of freelance contracts, which involves selecting the client, providing project details, and confirming the freelancer's information. The form is designed to capture essential data related to the freelancer's contract, ensuring that all required fields are filled in correctly to ensure accurate and efficient processing.

The form is divided into different sections, each with its own specific fields and instructions.

### Freelancer Contract Approval Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Freelancer Contract Approval Form is a critical document used for the approval process of freelance contracts. It ensures that all necessary information is collected and reviewed before proceeding with the contract.

### How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully to understand what information is required.
- Fill in the client selection correctly, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
- Provide accurate project details in the designated field.
- Enter the freelancer's information correctly.
- Review and Approve the contract by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- Enter the date of completion for the contract.
- Provide the time of completion for the contract.
- Confirm the contract signed date.
- Add any additional notes for the contract.
- Fill in the freelancer's email address.
- Fill in the freelancer's phone number.
- Confirm the freelancer's email address.
- Confirm the freelancer's phone number.
- Add any review and approve notes.
- Fill in the name of the person approving the contract.
- Select the review status of the contract, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
- Review and confirm the review result.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Selection** (`client_selection`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if you are choosing this client for the contract, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project you are approving.
* **Freelancer Information** (`freelancer_info`, text, required): Enter the freelancer's name and other required information as requested.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required): Enter any terms and conditions that apply to the contract.
* **Review and Approve** (`review_and_approve`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you approve the contract.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, required): Enter the date the contract is expected to be completed.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, required): Enter the time the contract is expected to be completed.
* **Contract Signed On** (`contract_signed_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes regarding the contract.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the freelancer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the freelancer's phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Confirm the freelancer's email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required): Confirm the freelancer's phone number.
* **Review and Approve Notes** (`review_and_approve_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional review and approve notes.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person approving the contract.
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, required): Enter the date the contract was approved.
* **Approved Time** (`approved_time`, time, required): Enter the time the contract was approved.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional review notes.
* **Review By** (`review_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the contract.
* **Reviewed Date** (`reviewed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract was reviewed.
* **Reviewed Time** (`reviewed_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the contract was reviewed.
* **Review Result** (`review_result`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the review is successful, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the review, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Submit the form after filling in all required fields.
* **Review Note** (`review_note`, note, optional): Add any additional review notes.

### Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information before submitting the form.
- If you need to add any additional notes, use the review and approve notes field.
- Review the form again before approval to ensure all information is accurate.
