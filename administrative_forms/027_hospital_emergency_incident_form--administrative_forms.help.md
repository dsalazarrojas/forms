# Hospital Emergency Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hospital Emergency Incident Form is designed to collect details about a patient's emergency incident in a hospital setting. This form helps gather essential information that aids in the investigation, treatment, and care of the patient. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly ensures that medical staff receive the necessary data to provide efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by entering the **Incident Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Enter the **Patient Name** in the format as they are known to the healthcare provider.
3. For the **Patient Date of Birth (DOB)**, use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Provide the **Patient Address** in the format as it appears on official documents, e.g., street address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter a detailed description of the **Incident Description**.
6. Select the **Incident Time** in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
7. Enter the **Patient Contact** information as available.
8. Enter the **Incident Location** where the incident occurred.
9. If applicable, select the **Assigned Provider** responsible for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date (1)**: The date on which the incident occurred, formatted as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Name (2)**: The name of the patient involved in the incident, as it is known to the healthcare provider.
* **Patient Date of Birth (3)**: The date of birth of the patient, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Address (4)**: The address of the patient, as found on official documents, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Incident Description (5)**: A detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Incident Time (6)**: The time of the incident, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Patient Contact (7)**: Any relevant contact information for the patient or their representatives.
* **Incident Location (8)**: The location where the incident took place.
* **Assigned Provider (9)**: If applicable, select the healthcare provider responsible for treating the patient during the incident.

## Tips

* Use the format guidelines for dates and times as mentioned above to ensure consistency and ease of processing.
* Ensure that you complete all fields marked as required to avoid incomplete submissions.
* Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact your hospital administration or support team for assistance.
