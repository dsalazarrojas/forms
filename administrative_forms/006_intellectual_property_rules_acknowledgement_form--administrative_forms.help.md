# Intellectual Property Rules Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge employee awareness and consent regarding intellectual property rules within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in your details as follows:
* Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
* Enter your Company Name in the "Company Name" field.
* Enter your IP Address in the "IP Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official employee records.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the company you work for.
* **IP Address** (text, required): Enter your IP address, which is the address assigned to your device by your internet service provider.
