# Beverage Inventory Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beverage Inventory Assessment form is designed to gather information about the stock levels of various beverages, supplies, and equipment in a bar, restaurant, or other establishment. This form helps to ensure that inventory levels are accurate and up-to-date, enabling informed decisions about ordering, stocking, and managing supplies.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Basic Information

*   -- Location Information --
    *   **Location or Business Name**: Enter the name of your business, such as "The Fancy Bar" or "The Wine Room".
    *   **Manager or Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the inventory.
    *   **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address for follow-up communications.
    *   **Inventory Assessment Date**: Select the date when you are conducting the inventory assessment.
    *   **Assessment Time**: (Optional) Enter the time when you are conducting the assessment.

### Step 2: Spirits & Liquor Stock

*   -- Spirits & Liquor Stock --
    *   **Vodka - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count the number of full and partial bottles.
    *   **Gin - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count the number of full and partial bottles.
    *   **Rum - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count the number of full and partial bottles.
    *   **Whiskey - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count the number of full and partial bottles.
    *   **Tequila - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count the number of full and partial bottles.

### Step 3: Wine & Beer Stock

*   -- Wine & Beer Stock --
    *   **Red Wine - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count all red wine varieties.
    *   **White Wine - Number of Bottles in Stock**: Count all white wine varieties.
    *   **Beer - Number of Cases/Units in Stock**: Count by case or 6-pack.

### Step 4: Mixers & Non-Alcoholic Beverages

*   -- Mixers & Non-Alcoholic Beverages --
    *   **Soda & Soft Drinks - Stock Level**: Estimate percentage of capacity or count units.
    *   **Juices - Stock Level**: Estimate percentage of capacity or count units.
    *   **Bottled Water - Units in Stock**: Count bottles or cases.
    *   **Energy Drinks - Units in Stock**: Count bottles or cans.

### Step 5: Bar Supplies & Equipment

*   -- Bar Supplies & Equipment --
    *   **Ice Supply Status**: Estimate quantity or percentage full.
    *   **Glassware Condition**: Select the condition of your glassware (Fully stocked and good condition, Well stocked with minor wear, Adequate but some items missing, Low stock, or Critical - Major shortage).

### Step 6: Inventory Concerns & Notes

*   -- Inventory Concerns & Notes --
    *   **Items Approaching Expiration**: List any items nearing expiration date.
    *   **Damaged or Unusable Items**: Describe items that need to be discarded.
    *   **Items That Need Reordering**: List products running low.
    *   **Additional Notes or Observations**: Any other inventory concerns?

## Tips

*   Be as accurate as possible when counting stock levels.
*   Ensure you have the latest information before submitting your assessment.
*   Use this form to inform ordering and stocking decisions.
*   Regularly review and update your inventory to maintain accurate levels.
