# return_to_work_notification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify your manager of your return to work after a leave. Please fill out this form to provide your manager with the necessary information to update your work status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one of the following options:
	* If you are returning to work, select "Yes" for the "Employee Returning" field.
	* If you are not returning to work, select "No" for the "Employee Returning" field.
2. Enter the date you plan to return to work in the "Return Date" field.
3. Provide any comments or information you'd like to share with your manager in the "Manager Comments" and "Employee Comments" fields.
4. Sign and date the "Manager Signature" and "Employee Signature" fields if applicable.
5. If you are returning to work due to a specific reason, select the corresponding option for the "Return Reason" field.
6. If applicable, enter the date you left work and the date you plan to return in the "Leave Start Date" and "Leave End Date" fields.
7. Select "Yes" for the "Manager Approval" field to confirm that you have your manager's approval to return to work.
8. Enter the date you received confirmation of your return in the "Return Date Confirmation" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Form Submitted" field to indicate whether this is a new or updated return to work notification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Returning** (`Employee Returning`): This field confirms whether you are returning to work or not. Please select "Yes" if you are returning to work.
* **Return Date** (`Return Date`): Enter the date you plan to return to work. Please format the date in the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`): Enter any comments or information you'd like to share with your manager about your return to work.
* **Employee Comments** (`Employee Comments`): Enter any comments or information you'd like to share with your manager about your return to work.
* **Manager Signature** (`Manager Signature`): Sign and date this field to confirm that you have your manager's approval to return to work.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`): Sign and date this field to confirm that you are returning to work.
* **Return Reason** (`Return Reason`): Select the reason for your return to work from the options provided. If you are returning to work due to a specific reason, please select the corresponding option.
* **Leave Start Date** (`Leave Start Date`): Enter the date you left work. If you are unsure, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
* **Leave End Date** (`Leave End Date`): Enter the date you plan to return to work. If you are unsure, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
* **Manager Approval** (`Manager Approval`): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have your manager's approval to return to work.
* **Return Date Confirmation** (`Return Date Confirmation`): Enter the date you received confirmation of your return to work from your manager.
* **Form Submitted** (`Form Submitted`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether this is a new or updated return to work notification.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand the fields before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
* Please be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
