# Employee Reimbursement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reimbursement Agreement form is used to record and verify employee expenses and reimbursement requests. This form helps in maintaining an accurate record of employee claims and facilitates the reimbursement process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in your name as an employee.
* Provide your email address.
* Enter your phone number.
* Select the date you incurred the expense.
* Enter the amount of the expense.
* (Optional) Attach a receipt or note regarding the expense.
* Enter the amount you are requesting for reimbursement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as an employee.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address for verification and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Expense Date** (expense_date, date, required): Enter the date when you incurred the expense.
* **Expense Amount** (expense_amount, number, required): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Receipt** (receipt, note, optional): If applicable, attach a receipt or a note regarding the expense.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (reimbursement_amount, number, required): Enter the amount you are requesting for reimbursement.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
* If you need any assistance, contact your supervisor or HR department.
* If you have any doubts or questions, refer back to this help guide.
