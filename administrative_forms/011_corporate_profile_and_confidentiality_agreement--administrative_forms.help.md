# Corporate Profile And Confidentiality Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting company information and ensuring users agree to maintain confidentiality. Please fill out this form carefully and truthfully to create a secure partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer all questions.
2. Fill out the form fields according to your company's information and policies.
3. Review the terms and conditions, and if you agree, check the box.
4. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the company's general information, such as name, address, and contact details. Be sure to enter your company's accurate and up-to-date information.
  - **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `text`, required: false): In this section, please confirm that you understand and agree to maintain confidentiality. This is a critical aspect of our partnership.
  - **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide the relevant contact details for the company's representative or responsible person.
  - **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required: false): Carefully review and agree to the terms and conditions outlined below. This is essential for our partnership to be successful.
  - **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your form and complete the agreement process.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- Review all information before submitting to ensure it's correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
