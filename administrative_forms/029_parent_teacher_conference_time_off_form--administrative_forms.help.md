# Parent Teacher Conference Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and prepare for parent-teacher conferences, allowing teachers and staff to gather essential information from parents and plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of conference you are requesting (Parent-Teacher, Student-Teacher, or Family).
2. Enter the start and end dates for the conference.
3. Enter the start and end times for the conference.
4. Specify the location of the conference.
5. Add any additional comments or details about the conference.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Request Conference**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the conference (e.g., "Meet with parent to discuss math grades").
- **Conference Type**: Select the type of conference you are requesting (Parent-Teacher, Student-Teacher, or Family).
- **Start Date**: Enter the date of the conference (mm/dd/yyyy format, e.g., 02/12/2022).
- **End Date**: Enter the date of the conference (mm/dd/yyyy format, e.g., 02/12/2022).
- **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the conference (HH:MM format, e.g., 08:00 AM).
- **End Time**: Enter the end time of the conference (HH:MM format, e.g., 09:00 AM).
- **Location**: Enter the location of the conference (e.g., Classroom A, School Office, or at home).
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about the conference.
- **Conference Details**: Add any additional information about the conference that may be relevant to the teacher or staff preparing for it.
