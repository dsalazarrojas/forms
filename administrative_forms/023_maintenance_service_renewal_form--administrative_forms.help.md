# Maintenance Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew maintenance services. It collects relevant information from the service provider, client, and internal details for efficient processing and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form sections as instructed.
*   Review each field carefully before proceeding.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the name or ID of the service provider.
*   **Maintenance Service Renewal Date** (`maintenance_service_renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the maintenance service renewal.
*   **Client First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's first name.
*   **Client Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's last name.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on the payment method for the service renewal.
*   **Service Contract Start Date** (`service_contract_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the service contract.
*   **Service Contract End Date** (`service_contract_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the service contract.
*   **Client Notes** (`client_note`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the client.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to the client or service.
*   **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `text`, required): Enter the user assigned to the client or service.
*   **Service Renewal Status** (`service_renewal_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on the service renewal status.
*   **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm or reject the form submission.
*   **Form Submission Confirmation** (`form_submission_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm or reject the form submission.

Note: This help guide is designed to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field in the Maintenance Service Renewal Form. Ensure you review each field carefully before submitting the form.
