# Workplace Complaint Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Complaint Verification Form is a tool to help you report a workplace complaint to your superiors. This form will guide you through the process of submitting your complaint and ensure that it is handled efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form as accurately and truthfully as possible.
2. Enter your employee name in the **Employee Name** field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the complaint in the **Complaint Description** field.
4. Indicate whether a **Reporting Officer** was involved in the complaint.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
7. Select the date of the complaint in the **Date of Complaint** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or in our records.
- **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, note, required): Describe the complaint in detail, including any supporting facts and evidence.
- **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if a reporting officer was involved, 'No' otherwise.
- **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer_label`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reporting officer if checked 'Yes' above.
- **Reporting Officer Value** (`reporting_officer_value`, select_multiple, optional): Select all reporting officers involved if checked 'Yes' above.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your work contact number.
- **Date of Complaint** (`date_of_complaint`, date, optional): Select the date of the complaint.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Avoid using abbreviations and acronyms.
- Provide as much detail as possible when describing the complaint.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
