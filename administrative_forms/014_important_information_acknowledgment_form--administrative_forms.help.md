# Important Information Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Important Information Acknowledgment Form is a document used to collect and track acknowledgment information from users. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "acknowledgment_date" field and enter the date you are providing acknowledgment for.
2. Enter the acknowledgment time in the "acknowledgment_time" field.
3. In the "acknowledgment_information" field, provide a brief description or details about the acknowledgment.
4. Select the appropriate acknowledgment type from the options in the "acknowledgment_acknowledgment" field.
5. Enter any additional information about the acknowledgment reason in the "acknowledgment_acknowledgment_reason" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
8. Select your contact name from the options in the "contact_name" field.
9. Enter your contact position in the "contact_position" field.
10. Select your contact department from the options in the "contact_department" field.
11. Select your contact job function from the options in the "contact_job_function" field.
12. Enter your contact work email in the "contact_work_email" field.
13. Enter your contact work phone number in the "contact_work_phone" field.
14. Enter your contact external phone number in the "contact_ext_phone" field.
