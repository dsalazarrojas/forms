# AI Ethics Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about AI systems for licensing purposes. It guides the applicant through the necessary fields to provide a comprehensive overview of their AI system, including its purpose, deployment environment, data types processed, ethical framework applied, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Applicant Full Name** field with your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Applicant Email** in the provided field to ensure we can contact you regarding your license.
3. If the AI system is being used by your company or institution, enter the **Organization Name**.
4. Provide the **Name of AI System** and a brief description of its **Purpose**.
5. Choose the **Deployment Environment** where the AI system will be used (Cloud, On-Premises, Hybrid, Edge Computing, Mobile, or Other).
6. Select the **Types of Data Processed** that your AI system handles (Personal Identifiable Information, Financial Data, Health Records, Educational Records, Behavioral Data, Biometric Data, or Other).
7. Indicate the **Ethical Framework Applied** to govern your AI system (ACM Code of Ethics, IEEE Ethically Aligned Design, Partnership on AI Principles, Montreal Declaration, EU Ethics Guidelines, or Custom Framework).
8. Describe the **Bias Detection Methods** used to monitor and address algorithmic bias.
9. Explain how the **Transparency Measures** will be taken to explain AI decisions to users.
10. Outline the **Human Oversight Protocol** for monitoring and intervening in AI decisions.
11. Specify the **Audit Schedule** for regular ethical compliance reviews (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, or As Needed).
12. Describe the **Incident Response Plan** for addressing ethical violations.
13. Choose the **License Duration** for which you're requesting a license (1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, 5 Years, or Permanent).
14. Review all fields for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: <strong>Legal name of the person applying for license</strong>
* **Applicant Email**: <strong>Professional email for licensing communications</strong>
* **Organization Name**: <strong>Company or institution requesting the license</strong>
* **Name of AI System**: <strong>Official name of the AI system being licensed</strong>
* **Purpose of AI System**: <strong>Primary function and intended use of the system</strong>
* **Deployment Environment**: <strong>Where the AI system will be deployed (Cloud, On-Premises, Hybrid, Edge Computing, Mobile, or Other)</strong>
* **Types of Data Processed**: <strong>Categories of data the AI system will handle (Personal Identifiable Information, Financial Data, Health Records, Educational Records, Behavioral Data, Biometric Data, or Other)</strong>
* **Ethical Framework Applied**: <strong>Which ethical guidelines govern this AI system (ACM Code of Ethics, IEEE Ethically Aligned Design, Partnership on AI Principles, Montreal Declaration, EU Ethics Guidelines, or Custom Framework)</strong>
* **Bias Detection Methods**: <strong>How algorithmic bias will be monitored and addressed</strong>
* **Transparency Measures**: <strong>How the AI system's decisions will be explained to users</strong>
* **Human Oversight Protocol**: <strong>How humans will monitor and intervene in AI decisions</strong>
* **Audit Schedule**: <strong>Frequency of ethical compliance reviews (Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually, or As Needed)</strong>
* **Incident Response Plan**: <strong>How ethical violations will be reported and addressed</strong>
* **License Duration**: <strong>Length for which you're requesting a license (1 Year, 2 Years, 3 Years, 5 Years, or Permanent)</strong>

Note: Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
