# internship_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Monitoring Form is used to track and monitor the progress of an intern's work. This form is meant to be filled out by the intern and their supervisor to provide an update on the intern's performance, progress, and completion status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections for **Intern Info** (page 1), **Supervisor Info** (page 2), and **Intern Progress** (page 3). These sections are used to gather basic information about the intern, including name, supervisor's name, and progress update.
2. On **Performance Evaluation** (page 4), select one of the two options: 'Yes' or 'No'. This indicates whether the intern's performance has met or exceeded expectations.
3. Select one of the two options for both **Intern Evaluations** (page 5), **Supervisor Evaluations** (page 6), and **Form Completion** (page 9). These fields indicate whether the intern's evaluations are positive or negative.
4. For **Internship Completion Date** (page 8), select the date the internship has completed.
5. On **Form Submission** (page 9), select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the form has been submitted.
6. Finally, fill out the **Form Completion** (page 10) field with any additional comments or information about the form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intern Info (1)**: Enter the intern's name and any additional information about their work.
* **Supervisor Info (2)**: Enter your name and any additional information about the intern's supervision.
* **Intern Progress (3)**: Provide an update about the intern's progress, including any challenges or accomplishments.
* **Performance Evaluation (4)**: Select 'Yes' if the intern's performance has met or exceeded expectations, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Intern Evaluations (5)**: Select 'Yes' if the intern's evaluations are positive, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Supervisor Evaluations (6)**: Select 'Yes' if the intern's evaluations are positive, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Internship Completion Date (8)**: Select the date the internship has completed.
* **Form Submission (9)**: Select 'Yes' if the form has been submitted, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Form Completion (10)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the form submission.
