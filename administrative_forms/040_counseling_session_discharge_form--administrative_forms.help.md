# Counseling Session Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Counseling Session Discharge Form is a document used to record the progress and plans made during a counseling session, ensuring that the client's needs are properly discharged and documented at the end of each session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the client's progress and make sure to update the "Client Progress" field with any relevant information.
2. In the "Session Notes" field, describe the key points discussed and decided upon during the session.
3. For "Discharge Plans", select the relevant plans and tasks assigned to the client.
4. Choose the follow-up schedule that best suits the client's needs.
5. If applicable, schedule the next appointment in the "Next Appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Progress** (`client_progress`, `note`, required: false): Briefly describe the client's progress or any notable changes observed during the session.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `text`, required: false): Provide detailed notes on the main topics discussed and decisions made during the session.
* **Discharge Plans** (`discharge_plans`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all plans and tasks assigned to the client for future progress and follow-up.
	+ Options: "option a", "option b", "option c"
* **Followup Schedules** (`followup_schedules`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the follow-up schedule that best suits the client's needs.
	+ Options: "option 1", "option 2"
* **Next Appointment** (`next_appointment`, `date`, required: false): Schedule the next appointment if one has been scheduled or planned.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the client's status or progress.
* Use the form to track and document the client's progress towards meeting their goals.
* Be thorough and accurate in filling out the form to ensure that the client is properly discharged and set up for future progress. 

Note: As the form fields are relatively simple and straightforward, no further guidance or examples are provided for each field. If you need more clarification, consult with your supervisor or the form's author. 

Please note that the form fields may change as your organization's policies and procedures evolve. Always review and update the form according to your organization's guidelines and best practices.
