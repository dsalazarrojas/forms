# Supply Chain Audit Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather accurate and complete information about a company or business for auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information, and if necessary, upload any required documents or media.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Name (Required)** (`business_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the company or business being audited.
- **Contact Person (Required)** (`contact_person`, `text`, Required): Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the audit.
- **Email (Required)** (`email`, `email`, Required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone (Optional)** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter a phone number to contact the auditor or representative.
- **Audit Date (Optional)** (`audit_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of the audit or examination.
- **Auditor Name (Optional)** (`auditor_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the person or team conducting the audit.
- **Business Type (Required)** (`business_type`, `select_one`, Required): Select one of the provided options for the type of business or company being audited.
- **Industry (Optional)** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select multiple options that apply to the industry of the company or business being audited.
- **Location (Required)** (`location`, `text`, Required): Enter the location or address of the company or business.
- **Description (Optional)** (`description`, `note`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the company or business.
- **Upload Docs (Optional)** (`uploaded_docs`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select and upload relevant documents for the audit.
- **Comments Section (Optional)** (`comments_section`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the audit.
- **Upload Excel File (Optional)** (`uploaded_excel_file`, `text`, Optional): Upload an Excel file related to the audit.
- **Upload Word File (Optional)** (`uploaded_word_file`, `text`, Optional): Upload a Word file related to the audit.
- **Signature (Required)** (`signature`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of signature for the auditor or representative.
- **Certification Date (Optional)** (`certification_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of certification or completion of the audit.
- **Certified By (Optional)** (`certified_by`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the person or team certifying the audit.
- **Certified Date Format (Optional)** (`certified_date_format`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the format of the certified date.
- **Upload File (Optional)** (`upload_file`, `text`, Optional): Upload a file related to the audit.
- **Upload Video (Optional)** (`upload_video`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select and upload a video related to the audit.
- **Upload Images (Optional)** (`uploaded_images`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select and upload images related to the audit.
- **Upload Documents (Optional)** (`uploaded_documents`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select and upload documents related to the audit.
- **Upload Audio (Optional)** (`upload_audio`, `text`, Optional): Upload an audio file related to the audit.
- **Upload PDFs (Optional)** (`upload_pdfs`, `text`, Optional): Upload a PDF file related to the audit.

Note: The optional fields are meant to provide additional information or supporting documents for the audit, but are not required for completion.
