# Workplace Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document workplace incidents, such as accidents, injuries, or near-misses. It helps to gather information about the incident, including details about the employee involved, the incident itself, and any subsequent investigation and follow-up actions. This form is used to ensure that incidents are reported and documented consistently, and that necessary follow-up actions are taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Incident Details**: Fill in the details of the incident, including the date and time it occurred, the location, and a brief description of what happened.
2. **Employee Involvement**: Indicate if an employee was involved in the incident.
3. **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. accident, injury, near-miss).
4. **Description**: Enter a detailed description of what happened.
5. **Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. **Date Of Occurrence**: Enter the date of the incident.
7. **Time Of Occurrence**: Enter the time of the incident.
8. **Witnesses**: Indicate if anyone witnessed the incident.
9. **Investigation Status**: Select the current status of the investigation (e.g. Active, Inactive).
10. **Incident Reported**: Enter the date the incident was reported.
11. **Incident Confirmed**: Enter the date the incident was confirmed.
12. **Incident Documented**: Enter the date the incident was documented.
13. **Incident Closed**: Enter the date the incident was closed.
14. **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the incident.
15. **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact for further information.
16. **Report Type**: Indicate if this is a new report or an update to an existing report.
17. **Report Date**: Enter the date the report was submitted.
18. **Follow Up Frequency**: Enter the frequency of follow-up actions (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).
19. **Follow Up Status**: Select the status of the follow-up actions (e.g. Active, Inactive).
20. **Next Follow Up Date**: Enter the date of the next follow-up action.
21. **Reporting Officer**: Enter the name of the officer responsible for reporting the incident.
22. **Follow Up Notes**: Enter any additional notes about follow-up actions.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the completeness of the report.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is for reporting workplace incidents, so make sure to report all incidents that occurred in the workplace. 
* Follow-up actions are necessary to ensure that the incident is fully resolved, so make sure to enter accurate follow-up dates and frequencies. 

Note: The form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to different types of incidents, so if you are unsure about any field, use your best judgment and contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
