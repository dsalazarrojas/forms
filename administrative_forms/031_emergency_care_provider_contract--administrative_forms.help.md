# Emergency Care Provider Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for emergency care providers to contract with patients. It outlines the terms and conditions of care, including emergency care provisions, contract term, and patient consent. The form ensures that providers and patients understand their responsibilities and expectations for emergency care services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the provider information section with your details.
2. Select the emergency care provisions that apply to the patient (Critical care, Non-critical care, or Other).
3. Choose a contract term and contract term expiration date.
4. Provide a contract signature date.
5. Enter the contract version number.
6. Enter any additional provider notes.
7. Enter the hospital information (if applicable).
8. Enter the patient contact information (if applicable).
9. Provide the emergency contact information (if applicable).
10. Enter the emergency care agreement (if applicable).
11. Choose whether the patient consents to emergency care.
12. Sign and date the patient signature section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Information (text)**: This section is for the provider to enter their contact information, such as name, title, and contact number.
* **Emergency Care Provisions (select multiple)**: Select the type of emergency care the provider will be offering (Critical care, Non-critical care, or Other).
* **Contract Term (date)**: Enter the start date of the contract term.
* **Contract Term Expiration (date)**: Enter the end date of the contract term.
* **Contract Signature Date (date)**: Enter the date the provider signed the contract.
* **Contract Version (text)**: This field is for the provider to track the version of the contract.
* **Provider Notes (text)**: Enter any additional information that the provider wants to share with the patient.
* **Hospital Information (text)**: Enter the hospital's contact information if applicable.
* **Patient Contact Information (text)**: Enter the patient's contact information if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: Enter the emergency contact information for the patient.
* **Emergency Care Agreement (note)**: This field is for the provider to enter any additional agreements or instructions related to emergency care.
* **Patient Consent (select one)**: Choose whether the patient consents to emergency care.
* **Patient Signature Date (date)**: Enter the date the patient signed the contract.
* **Emergency Care Provider Contract**: This section is for the provider and patient to review and sign the contract.
