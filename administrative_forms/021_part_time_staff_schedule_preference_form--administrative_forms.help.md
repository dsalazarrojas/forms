# part_time_staff_schedule_preference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a part-time staff member's schedule preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information ready before starting.
2. Start by filling out the "Schedule Preference" field, which requires you to specify your availability.
3. Next, select your preferred days and hours of work in the "Preferred Days" and "Preferred Hours" fields, respectively.
4. Determine your schedule type in the "Schedule Type" field.
5. Decide on the frequency of your schedule in the "Schedule Frequency" field.
6. Specify the length of your schedule in the "Schedule Length" field.
7. Enter your schedule's start time in the "Schedule Start Time" field.
8. Enter your schedule's end time in the "Schedule End Time" field.
9. Select a date for your schedule in the "Schedule Day" field.
10. Select an end date for your schedule in the "Schedule End Date" field.
11. If necessary, add notes about your schedule in the "Schedule Note" fields.
12. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
13. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
