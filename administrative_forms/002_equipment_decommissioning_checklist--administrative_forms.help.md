# Equipment Decommissioning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Decommissioning Checklist is a form used to track and verify the decommissioning process of company assets. This form helps to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to properly decommission and dispose of equipment, reducing the risk of data loss, damage, or unauthorized access. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Select the category of the equipment being decommissioned from the dropdown list.
3. Provide a brief description of the equipment being decommissioned.
4. Choose the type of equipment from the list.
5. Enter the location of the equipment.
6. Enter the asset tag number if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Equipment Decommissioning Checklist (Note)
*   **Equipment Decommissioning Checklist**: This is the main section of the form, providing space for your notes and comments about the decommissioning process.

### Category (Select One)
*   **Category**: Select the category of equipment being decommissioned from the options provided. This will help in tracking and categorizing the decommissioning activities.

### Description (Text)
*   **Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment being decommissioned, including its purpose and usage.

### Equipment Type (Select One)
*   **Equipment Type**: Choose the type of equipment being decommissioned from the options provided, such as Computers, Other, Printers, Monitors, or Phones.

### Location (Text)
*   **Location**: Enter the location where the equipment is currently situated or was last used.

### Asset Tag (Text)
*   **Asset Tag**: If the equipment has an asset tag, please enter it here. This will help in tracking and identifying the equipment. 

## Tips
*   Make sure to select the correct category and equipment type to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
*   Provide a detailed description of the equipment to help with future reference.
*   Enter the correct location to ensure that the equipment is properly removed from the inventory.
*   If the equipment has an asset tag, please ensure it is accurately recorded.
*   This form is not a replacement for the official asset management process. It is a supplementary tool to aid in the decommissioning process.
