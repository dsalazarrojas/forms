# river_overflow_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and report incidents within an organization or team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Incident Date field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the Incident Time field with the time the incident took place.
3. In the Incident Location field, provide a text description of where the incident happened.
4. Write a detailed description of what happened in the Incident Description field.
5. Choose the Incident Severity level: High, Medium, or Low, to indicate the level of impact or effect.
6. Select the Incident Status: Active, Inactive, or Resolved, to show the current state of the incident.
7. Choose the Incident Priority level: High, Medium, or Low, to determine the urgency of the incident.
8. Enter any additional information about who is assigned to handle the incident in the Incident Assignee field (optional).
9. Finally, add any notes or comments about the incident in the Incident Note field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Required): Enter the date the incident occurred. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure accurate and consistent formatting.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, Required): Enter the time the incident happened. Use 24-hour time format (HH:MM).
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, Required): Describe where the incident took place in text form.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, Optional): Enter any information about the incident's cause or root cause (if applicable).
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, Required): Select the level of impact or effect the incident had: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, Required): Choose the current status of the incident: Active, Inactive, or Resolved.
* **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`, Required): Select the urgency of the incident: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Incident Assignee** (`incident_assignee`, Optional): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to handle the incident (if applicable).
* **Incident Note** (`incident_note`, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the incident (if needed).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate incident reporting.
* Choose the correct Severity and Status levels to reflect the current state of the incident.
* If no assignee is specified, the incident is likely handled by default or by the submitter.
