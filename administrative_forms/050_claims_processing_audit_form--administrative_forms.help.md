# Claims Processing Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for internal use by auditors to document and track claims processing activities, identify areas of improvement, and implement corrective actions to increase efficiency and reduce non-compliance. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "Auditor name" field.
2. Identify the department undergoing the audit in the "Department audited" field.
3. Record the date of the audit activity in the "Audit date" field.
4. Enter the number of claims reviewed in the "Sample size reviewed" field.
5. Summarize any compliance findings in the "Compliance findings summary" field (if applicable).
6. Note any process inefficiencies identified during the audit in the "Process inefficiencies identified" field (if applicable).
7. Provide recommendations for corrective actions in the "Corrective actions recommended" field.
8. Indicate the level of risk assessed in the "Risk assessment" field (Low, Medium, or High).
9. Confirm whether follow-up is required in the "Follow up required" field.
10. Enter the date of report submission in the "Report date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor name (1)**: Enter your name and title.
* **Department audited (2)**: Identify the department or team being audited.
* **Audit date (3)**: Enter the date of the audit activity. 
* **Sample size reviewed (4)**: Enter the number of claims reviewed.
* **Compliance findings summary (5)**: Summarize any non-compliance issues found.
* **Process inefficiencies identified (6)**: List any process bottlenecks or causes.
* **Corrective actions recommended (7)**: Suggest owners and timelines for corrective actions.
* **Risk assessment (8)**: Choose one of the options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Follow up required (9)**: Indicate if follow-up is needed.
* **Report date (10)**: Enter the date you submit this report.
