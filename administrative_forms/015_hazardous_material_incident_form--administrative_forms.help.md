# Hazardous Material Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and report incidents involving hazardous materials that pose a risk to employees or the organization as a whole.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Go to the "Pages" section and select the page with the "Incident Date" field.
2.  Select the date of the incident from the calendar or date picker.
3.  Proceed to the next page, "Employee Affected", and select the employee(s) who were involved in the incident.
4.  On the "Location" page, enter the location where the incident occurred.
5.  On the "Incident Description" page, provide a brief description of the incident.
6.  On the "Material Hazards" page, select all hazardous materials that were involved in the incident.
7.  On the "Material Quantity" page, estimate the quantity of hazardous materials used or involved in the incident.
8.  On the "Employee Injured" page, select the number of employees injured during the incident.
9.  On the "Employee Injured Severity" page, assess the severity of the injury (if any).
10. Proceed to the next page, "Action Taken", and describe any actions taken to address the incident.
11. On the "Follow up Needed" page, indicate whether follow-up actions are required.
12. On the "Reporting Officer" page, enter your name and title.
13. On the "Supervisor Approval" page, select whether the supervisor has approved the report.
14. On the "Notes" page, enter any additional comments or notes related to the incident.
15. On the "Signature Date" and "Signature Time" page, date and time-stamp the report with your signature.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the reporting officer or supervisor.
* This form is for reporting incidents involving hazardous materials, if you are unsure about what constitutes a hazardous material, please refer to the organization's safety guidelines.
* After completing the form, make sure to submit it to the reporting officer or supervisor for review and follow-up actions.
