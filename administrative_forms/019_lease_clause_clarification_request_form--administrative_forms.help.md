# Lease Clause Clarification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Clause Clarification Request Form is used to submit requests for clarification or additional information about a specific clause in your lease. This form helps to ensure that your requests are handled efficiently and effectively, reducing misunderstandings and miscommunications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field is asking you to provide.
2. Fill in the fields as prompted, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. If you need to submit multiple requests, please fill out the form multiple times, ensuring each request is submitted as a separate form.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name and contact information so we can get back to you about your request.
- **Lease Terms** (`lease_terms`, text, optional): Clearly describe the lease clause you are seeking clarification on, including any relevant documentation or context.
- **Request** (`request`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the clarification you are seeking.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to submit your request, and "No" to cancel it.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you about your request.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Use this field for any additional comments or notes you may want to add to your request.
- **Additional Requests** (`submit_request`, select_multiple, optional): If you have multiple requests, choose "Yes" for each one.
- **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, please provide the lease end date for the specific clause you are seeking clarification on.

## Tips
* Please be as clear and concise as possible when describing your lease clause and request.
* If you are unsure about what information to provide, contact us and we will be happy to help.
* Only one request can be submitted at a time. If you have multiple requests, fill out the form multiple times.
