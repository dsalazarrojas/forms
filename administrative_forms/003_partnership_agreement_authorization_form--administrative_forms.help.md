# Partnership Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Agreement Authorization Form is used to capture essential information about a business partner or client, including their name, address, contact details, and other relevant details. This information helps the company to verify the partnership status and ensure that all parties involved are aligned on the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required details about your business.
2. Enter your business name in the **business_name** field.
3. Provide a brief description of your business in the **business_description** field.
4. Enter your business address in the **business_address** field.
5. Enter your contact name in the **contact_name** field.
6. Select your contact title from the options provided in the **contact_title** field.
7. Enter the date of the partnership agreement in the **date** field.
8. Enter the time of the partnership agreement in the **time** field.
9. Enter your business email address in the **email** field.
10. Enter your business phone number in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partnership Agreement Authorization Form** (`partnership_agreement_authorization_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the form.
* **business_name** (`partnership_agreement_business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your business.
* **business_description** (`partnership_agreement_business_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **business_address** (`partnership_agreement_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your business address.
* **contact_name** (`partnership_agreement_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the partnership agreement.
* **contact_title** (`partnership_agreement_contact_title`, select_one, required/optional): Select the title of the contact person from the options provided.
* **date** (`partnership_agreement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the partnership agreement.
* **time** (`partnership_agreement_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the partnership agreement.
* **email** (`partnership_agreement_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your business email address.
* **phone** (`partnership_agreement_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your business phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or contact us for clarification.
* This form is a critical document and should be completed carefully to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
