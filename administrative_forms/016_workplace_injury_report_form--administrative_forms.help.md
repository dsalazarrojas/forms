# Workplace Injury Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Injury Report Form is a tool used to report and track workplace injuries, allowing for prompt action to be taken to address and resolve the issue. By providing detailed information about the incident, supervisors and HR representatives can effectively manage the situation and support the injured employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee's **First Name** and **Last Name**.
2. Provide your **Email** address to help us contact you with updates and information.
3. Enter the **Date Injured**, which should be the date the incident occurred.
4. In the **Description** field, describe the injury as thoroughly as possible, including any details about what happened, where, and when.
5. If applicable, provide **Supervisor Comments** about the incident.
6. If there were witnesses, select **Witness Names** from the provided options or enter additional names if they are not listed.
7. Choose the current **Status** of the incident from the options provided.
8. Enter the **Date Reported**, which is the date the report is submitted.
9. Enter the **Time Reported**, which is the time the report is submitted.
10. If relevant, enter the **Location** where the injury occurred.
11. If applicable, enter your **Job Title** and **Employee ID** for reference.
12. If the incident has been resolved, enter the **Date Completed** and **Time Completed**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your organizational email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Date Injured**: Enter the date the injury occurred, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the injury, including what happened, where, and when.
* **Supervisor Comments**: If you have spoken with your supervisor about the incident, provide their comments or feedback.
* **Witness Names**: Select from the provided options or enter additional names if not listed. This field is not limited to the options provided.
* **Status**: Choose the current status of the incident from the options:
	+ Active: The incident is still being investigated or addressed.
	+ Completed: The incident has been resolved.
	+ In Progress: The incident is being addressed but not yet resolved.
	+ Pending: The incident is waiting for further action or information.
	+ Closed: The incident is no longer being actively addressed.
* **Date Reported**: Enter the date the report is submitted, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time Reported**: Enter the time the report is submitted.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the injury occurred, if relevant.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title, if relevant.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID, if relevant.
* **Date Completed**: Enter the date the incident was resolved, if applicable.
* **Time Completed**: Enter the time the incident was resolved, if applicable.
* **Witness**: Select from the provided options or enter additional names if not listed.
