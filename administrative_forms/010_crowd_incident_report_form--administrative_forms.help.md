This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur within a community or organization. It is meant to be used by community members, staff, or other stakeholders to report incidents that require attention or resolution. The form should only be used for reporting incidents that have not been reported previously.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field.
3. Select the relevant options from the drop-down menus (e.g. incident_type, incident_status, incident_outcome).

When filling out the form, keep the following in mind:

* The "incident_date" field is the date the incident occurred.
* The "location" field is the location where the incident took place.
* The "event_name" field is the name of the incident.
* The "incident_type" field is the type of incident (e.g. Emergency, Security, Medical).
* The "description" field is a brief description of the incident.
* The "incident_reporter" field is the name of the person reporting the incident.
* The "reporter_contact_info" field is the contact information of the person reporting the incident.
* The "reported_by" field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* The "incident_details" field is a more detailed description of the incident.
* The "incident_status" field is the current status of the incident (e.g. Open, In Progress, Closed).
* The "incident_resolution" field is the resolution of the incident.
* The "follow_up" field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* The "incident_outcome" field is the outcome of the incident (e.g. Resolved, Unresolved, In Progress).

# Crowd Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about incidents that occur within a community or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): The date the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): The location where the incident took place.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): The type of incident (e.g. Emergency, Security, Medical).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): A brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required): The name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Contact Info** (`reporter_contact_info`, text, required): The contact information of the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): The current status of the incident (e.g. Open, In Progress, Closed).
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required): The outcome of the incident (e.g. Resolved, Unresolved, In Progress).

This form is designed to be easy to use and provide a clear and concise way to report incidents. If you have any questions or concerns about filling out this form, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
