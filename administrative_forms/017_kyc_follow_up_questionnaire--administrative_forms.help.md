# kyc_follow_up_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The KYC follow-up questionnaire is used to update a business's information in our system. It helps us ensure that the information we have on file is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary documents and information required for the updates.
* Enter the requested information in the corresponding fields.
* Review and submit the form to update your business's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Date**: This field is where you enter the date of your initial onboarding with us.
* **Business Name**: Enter the current name of your business.
* **Business Address**: Enter the current address of your business.
* **Business Phone**: Enter the current phone number of your business.
* **Updated Business Name**: If your business name has changed, enter the new name here.
* **Updated Business Address**: If your business address has changed, enter the new address here.
* **Updated Business Phone**: If your business phone number has changed, enter the new number here.
* **Updated Onboarding Date**: If your initial onboarding date was incorrect, enter the correct date here.
* **Updated Business Status**: Select the current status of your business (Active, Suspended, or Terminated).
* **Reason for Change**: If you are making changes to your business's information, select the reason for the change (Updated business address, Updated business name, or Updated business phone number).
* **Updated Reason for Change**: If the reason for change has changed, select the new reason here.
* **Updated Business Email**: Enter the current email address of your business.
* **Updated Business Website**: Enter the current website of your business.
* **Updated Business Fiscal Account**: Enter the fiscal account number of your business (if applicable).
* **Updated Business IBAN**: Enter the IBAN number of your business (if applicable).
* **Updated Business Phone**: Enter the updated phone number of your business (if changed).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid delays in processing your updates.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- We appreciate your prompt completion and submission of this form to ensure our records are up-to-date and accurate.
