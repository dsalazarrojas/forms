# onsite_dynamic_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onsite dynamic risk assessment form is used to identify and assess risks that are present on-site. This form helps users to document and manage risks by providing a structured format for capturing risk information. The form is designed to be dynamic, allowing users to add or remove fields as needed to accommodate different types of risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the risk type from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time of the risk assessment.
3. Describe the risk and its associated actions to prevent it from happening again.
4. Select the frequency at which you see this type of risk.
5. Determine the risk level impact and urgency.
6. Set the risk priority.
7. Enter any additional risk description if necessary.
8. List any additional actions to prevent the risk from happening again.
9. Enter the risk review date and time if applicable.
10. Enter the risk reviewer and responder information if applicable.
11. Select the risk status (Active, Inactive, or Resolved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What type of risk** (`risk_type`): Please choose one of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Date of Risk Assessment** (`date_of_risk_assessment`): Enter the date of the risk assessment in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time of Risk Assessment** (`time_of_risk_assessment`): Enter the time of the risk assessment in hh -mm AM/PM format.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`): Provide a detailed description of the risk.
* **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`): Choose one of the following options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Risk Level Impact** (`risk_level_impact`): Select one of the following options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Risk Urgency** (`risk_urgency`): Choose one of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Risk Priority** (`risk_priority`): Select one of the following options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Risk Description 2** (`risk_description_2`): Provide an additional detailed description of the risk if necessary.
* **Preventative Actions** (`risk_preventative_actions`): List the actions that can be taken to prevent this risk from happening again.
* **Risk Responder** (`risk_responder`): Enter the name of the person who will take action to prevent the risk.
* **Risk Status** (`risk_status`): Choose one of the following options: Active, Inactive, or Resolved.
* **Risk Note** (`risk_note`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the risk.

Note: Fields with hints will guide you through the input process.
