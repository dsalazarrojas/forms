# Team Building Retreat Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Retreat Leave Form is used to request a team building retreat, providing essential details for planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Building Start Date** (team_building_start_date, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the team building retreat.
* **Team Building End Date** (team_building_end_date, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the team building retreat.
* **Team Building Location** (team_building_location, text, required/optional): Provide the location of the team building retreat. Please be specific and include the address or venue name.
* **Team Building Number Of Attendees** (team_building_number_of_attendees, number, required/optional): Enter the number of team members attending the retreat.
