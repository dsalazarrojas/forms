> # Teacher Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Complaint Form is a tool designed to collect and document complaints or issues experienced by teachers. This form helps to identify areas of concern and facilitate a resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the relevant complaint type from the options provided.
3. Provide a clear description of the complaint.
4. Enter the reported teacher's name if applicable.
5. Enter the date and time of the incident, if possible.
6. Add any additional comments or information that may help with the resolution process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Type** (select_one): 
  This field allows you to specify the type of complaint you are reporting. Please select the option that best describes your experience.

  * Harassment: This refers to behavior or actions that are intended to disturb or intimidate a teacher.
  * Bullying: This refers to repeated and persistent behavior or actions that are intended to cause distress or fear.
  * Unprofessional Conduct: This refers to behavior or actions that are not in line with professional standards or expectations.
  * Other: If none of the above options apply, please select this option and provide a detailed description.

* **Complaint Description** (text):
  This field is for you to provide a clear and concise description of the incident or issue you are reporting.

* **Reported Teacher** (text):
  If you are reporting a complaint about a specific teacher, enter their name here.

* **Complaint Date** (date):
  If possible, enter the date when the incident occurred.

* **Complaint Time** (time):
  If possible, enter the time when the incident occurred.

* **Student Details** (text):
  If relevant, provide any information about the student(s) involved in the incident, such as their name(s), grade level, or class name.

* **Additional Comments** (text):
  Use this field to add any additional information that may help with the resolution process.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing details about the incident.
* Ensure that you are reporting genuine complaints, not duplicates or unfounded ones.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for assistance.
* The data collected will be reviewed by authorized personnel to ensure a fair and thorough resolution process.
