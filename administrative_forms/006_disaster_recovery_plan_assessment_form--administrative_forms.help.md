# Disaster Recovery Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of your organization's disaster recovery plan, helping you identify potential weaknesses and improve your preparedness for disaster situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review your organization's disaster recovery plan and familiarize yourself with the questions and fields.
2. Answer each question based on your organization's current disaster recovery plan.
3. Use the options provided to select the most accurate responses.
4. Provide detailed explanations and estimates where necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Continuity** (Text): Describe your organization's business continuity plan and how it will be implemented in case of a disaster.
* **Impact** (Select One):
	+ Critical: The disaster will have a significant impact on your organization.
	+ High: The disaster will have a moderate impact on your organization.
	+ Low: The disaster will have a minimal impact on your organization.
* **Disaster Plan** (Select Multiple):
	+ Critical: A comprehensive disaster plan is in place.
	+ High: A disaster plan is partially in place.
	+ Low: No disaster plan is in place.
* **Business Process Disruption** (Text): List all critical business processes that may be affected by a disaster.
* **Impact on Employees** (Select One):
	+ Minimal: The impact on employees will be minimal.
	+ Significant: The impact on employees will be moderate.
	+ Critical: The impact on employees will be critical.
* **Emergency Response and Recovery** (Text): Describe your emergency response and recovery process.
* **Communication Planning** (Select One):
	+ Critical: Critical communication planning is in place.
	+ High: High-level communication planning is in place.
	+ Low: No communication planning is in place.
* **Data Loss** (Select Multiple):
	+ Critical: Potential data loss is critical.
	+ High: Potential data loss is moderate.
	+ Low: Potential data loss is minimal.
* **Incident Report** (Text): Describe your incident reporting process.
* **Incident Reporting** (Select One):
	+ Critical: Critical incident reporting is in place.
	+ High: High-level incident reporting is in place.
	+ Low: No incident reporting is in place.
* **Incident Response** (Select One):
	+ Critical: Critical incident response is in place.
	+ High: High-level incident response is in place.
	+ Low: No incident response is in place.
* **Recovery Plan** (Select One):
	+ Critical: A comprehensive recovery plan is in place.
	+ High: A recovery plan is partially in place.
	+ Low: No recovery plan is in place.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Use specific examples and scenarios to illustrate your responses.
* Review and revise your answers as needed.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with your disaster recovery team or relevant stakeholders.
