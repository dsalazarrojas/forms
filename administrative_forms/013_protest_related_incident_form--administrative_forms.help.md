# protest_related_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The protest_related_incident_form is used to document incidents that may be related to protests. This form helps in collecting information about the incident, including its date, time, location, description, and any related persons. It also captures details about the protest, such as its date, time, location, description, and type. The form is used to gather information that will help in understanding the incidents and protests in a comprehensive manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the incident date and time.
2. Enter the location of the incident.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident.
4. Choose the type of incident, if applicable.
5. Select the related persons if any.
6. Fill in the protest date and time.
7. Enter the location of the protest.
8. Provide a brief description of the protest.
9. Choose the type of protest.
10. Select the protestor attitude.
11. Choose the incident outcome.
12. Enter any other related persons.
13. Enter any other incident details.
14. Enter any other protest details.
15. Enter the protestor attitude for other protest.
16. Enter the incident outcome details.
17. Enter the related outcome details.
18. Enter the incident outcome other details.
