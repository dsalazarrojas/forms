# internship_authorization_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Internship Authorization Form is used to collect information about an internship placement, including user information, employer details, and internship specifics. This form is essential for tracking and managing internship placements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your internship, such as the start and end dates, hours worked, job title, and internship site.
3. Enter your employer's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
4. Select the status of your internship (Active or Inactive).
5. Enter any additional details, such as supervisor information and student ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Employer Information** (`employer_info`, text, required): Enter your employer's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Placement Details** (`placement_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your internship.
* **Start Date** (`internship_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your internship.
* **End Date** (`internship_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your internship.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total hours worked during your internship.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Internship Site** (`internship_site`, text, required): Enter the site where you interned.
* **Supervisor Information** (`supervisor_info`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name and contact information.
* **Intern Status** (`intern_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your internship (Active or Inactive).
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter your employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, required): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, email, required): Enter your employer's email.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID.
* **Employer ID** (`employer_id`, text, required): Enter your employer's ID.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's ID.
