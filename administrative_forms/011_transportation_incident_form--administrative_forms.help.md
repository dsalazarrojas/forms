# Transportation Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Transportation Incident Form is a tool for reporting and tracking transportation incidents. It provides a comprehensive framework for capturing essential details of an incident, making it easier to document, review, and respond.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure all required fields (marked as **required**) are filled in accurately and completely.
2. Fill out the **Incident Details** section, providing specific information on the transportation incident.
3. Select the correct **Incident Cause** and **Incident Outcome** based on the options provided.
4. Identify the individual who **Reported the Incident** and their corresponding contact information.
5. Specify the **Department** and **Team** associated with the incident.
6. Choose the individual responsible for **Reviewing the Incident** and their corresponding department.
7. Select the date of **Incident Review**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transportation Incident**: A brief overview of the incident.
* **Incident Details**: A text field for providing specific information about the incident.
* **Incident Location**: The location where the incident occurred. (Optional)
* **Incident Time**: The time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: The date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: A detailed explanation of the incident.
* **Incident Cause**: The reason or explanation for the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Outcome**: The result or impact of the incident (Select multiple options).
* **Incident Reported By**: The individual who reported the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Reporter Contact**: The contact information of the individual who reported the incident (Select multiple options).
* **Incident Reporter Email**: The email of the individual who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Phone**: The phone number of the individual who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Job Title**: The job title of the individual who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By Department**: The department associated with the individual who reported the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Reported By Team**: The team associated with the individual who reported the incident (Select multiple options).
* **Incident Resolution**: The final outcome or solution of the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Resolution Notes**: Additional comments or notes related to the incident resolution.
* **Incident Reviewed By**: The individual who reviewed the incident (Select multiple options).
* **Incident Reviewed By Department**: The department associated with the individual who reviewed the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Reviewed By Comments**: Additional comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Incident Reviewer**: The individual responsible for reviewing the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Reviewer Comments**: Additional comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Incident Review Date**: The date when the incident was reviewed.
* **Incident Reviewer Department**: The department associated with the individual who reviewed the incident (Select one option).
* **Incident Reviewer Job Title**: The job title of the individual who reviewed the incident.
