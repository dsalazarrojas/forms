# internship_completion_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for interns to provide feedback and confirm the completion of their internship experience. It helps program administrators or supervisors collect data on their internship program's effectiveness and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the required fields, marked as *required*.
2. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, *required*): This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback about your internship experience. Please be as specific and honest as possible so that the program can improve for future interns.
* **Confirm Completion** (`confirm_completion`, text, *optional*): If you've already confirmed your completion, please enter the confirmation in this field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, *optional*): Enter any relevant information about submitting your completed form, like your confirmation number or any other relevant details.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, *optional*): If there is a specific person who submitted this form on your behalf, please enter their name or title.
* **Submitting Time** (`submitting_time`, time, *required*): Choose the date and time when you submitted your completed form, including the exact time.
* **Confirm Completed Date** (`confirm_completed_date`, date, *required*): Enter the date when you completed your internship, as accurately as possible.
* **Confirm Completed Location** (`confirm_completed_location`, text, *optional*): If you completed your internship in a specific location, enter that here.
* **Confirm Completed Supervisor** (`confirm_completed_supervisor`, text, *optional*): Enter the name of your supervisor or the person who oversaw your internship.
