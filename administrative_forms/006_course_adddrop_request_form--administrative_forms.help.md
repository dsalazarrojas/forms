# Course AddDrop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course AddDrop Request Form is used to submit a request to add or drop a course from your program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the requestor.
2. Select your program from the options provided.
3. Choose the courses you wish to add or drop.
4. Confirm your selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name (text)** (`student_name`, **required:** False): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **email (text)** (`student_email`, **required:** False): Enter your university email address for verification purposes.
* **Student ID (text)** (`student_id`, **required:** False): Enter your student ID number.
* **Program (select)** (`program`, **required:** False): Select your program of study from the options.
* **Add Course (select)** (`add_course`, **required:** False): Select the course you wish to add to your program. You can select multiple courses if needed.
* **Drop Course (select)** (`drop_course`, **required:** False): Select the course you wish to drop from your program. You can select multiple courses if needed.
* **Submit (note)** (`submit`, **required:** False): Click this button to submit your request.
* **Confirm (note)** (`confirm`, **required:** False): Double-check your selection before confirming.
