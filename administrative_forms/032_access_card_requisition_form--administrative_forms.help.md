# Access Card Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request a new access card for their needs. It captures the required information for processing a new card request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Requisitioner Information, including your Full Name, Department or Unit, Staff ID Number, and Work Email.
2. Select the reason for your request from the options provided.
3. Choose the access zones you need access to.
4. Select the type of card you require (Standard Proximity Card, Smart Card (HID), or Key Fob).
5. Confirm if a printed photo is required for your card.
6. Acknowledge the lost card fee if applicable.
7. Confirm if your supervisor has approved this request.
8. Enter your supervisor's name.
9. Enter today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Employee** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your payroll records.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`): Specify your primary work group.
* **Staff ID Number** (`staff_id_number`): Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`): Enter your official company email.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`): Select one of the following reasons: New Employee, Lost or Stolen Card, Damaged Card, Name Change, Access Level Update.
* **Access Zones Requested** (`access_zones_requested`): Select all areas you need access to.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`): Choose the type of card you require: Standard Proximity Card, Smart Card (HID), or Key Fob.
* **Does the card require a printed photo?** (`include_photo`): Confirm if a printed photo is required for your card.
* **Has your supervisor approved this request?** (`supervisor_approval_status`): Confirm if your supervisor has approved this request.
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Date of Request** (`requisition_date`): Enter today's date.
