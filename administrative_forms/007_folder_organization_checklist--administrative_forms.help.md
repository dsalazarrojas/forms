# Folder Organization Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Folder Organization Checklist is a form designed to guide users in organizing and managing their files effectively. This form helps in ensuring that files are properly organized, named, and categorized, reducing the risk of data loss or mismanagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page, "Organizing Files".
2. Move on to the "Naming Files" page and fill out the required fields.
3. On the next page, "Categorizing Files", select one of the provided options for categorizing your files.
4. Proceed to the "Tagging Files" page and select multiple tags for your files.
5. Fill out the "File Backups" page with the required date information.
6. Move on to the "Archiving Files" page and fill out the required time information.
7. On the next page, "Security Measures", fill out the text field with the relevant information.
8. Continue to the "Data Retention" page and fill out the text field.
9. Fill out the "Shredding Files" page with one of the provided options.
10. On the next page, "Versioning Files", fill out the text field.
11. Move on to the "Checklist Items" page and fill out the text field.
12. Fill out the "File Review" page with the text field.
13. Continue to the "Classification" page and select multiple options.
14. Fill out the "File Search" page with the text field.
15. Move on to the "Disposal" page and fill out the text field.
16. On the next page, "Retirement", select one of the provided options.
17. Fill out the "Disposal Certificate" page with the text field.
18. Continue to the "File Audit" page and fill out the text field.
19. On the next page, "Audit Certificate", fill out the text field.
20. Fill out the "Review Certificate" page with the text field.
21. Move on to the "Classification Certificate" page and fill out the text field.
22. Continue to the "Search Certificate" page and fill out the text field.
23. On the next page, "Retirement Certificate", fill out the text field.
24. Fill out the "Disposal Certificate" page with the text field.
25. Finally, fill out the "Audit Certificate" page with the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizing Files** (`file_organizing`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, where you can briefly describe how you currently organize your files.
* **Naming Files** (`file_naming`, text, optional): On this page, provide a clear and concise naming convention for your files.
* **Categorizing Files** (`file_categorization`, select_one, optional): Select a category that best describes the type of files you are organizing.
* **Tagging Files** (`file_tagging`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple tags that accurately describe the content of your files.
* **File Backups** (`file_backups`, date, optional): Enter the date you last performed a file backup.
* **Archiving Files** (`file_archiving`, time, optional): Enter the time you last performed a file archival.
* **Security Measures** (`file_security`, text, optional): Describe the security measures you take to protect your files.
* **Data Retention** (`file_retention`, text, optional): Provide information on your data retention policies.
* **Shredding Files** (`file_shredding`, select_one, optional): Select the method you use to shred files (e.g., physical, digital).
* **Versioning Files** (`file_versioning`, text, optional): Describe how you version your files.
* **Checklist Items** (`file_checklist`, text, optional): List any additional items related to file organization.
* **File Review** (`file_review`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your file review process.
* **Classification** (`file_classification`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple classifications for your files.
* **File Search** (`file_search`, text, optional): Describe how you search for files.
* **Disposal** (`file_disposal`, text, optional): Describe your file disposal process.
* **Retirement** (`file_retirement`, select_one, optional): Select the type of file retirement (e.g., physical, digital).
* **Disposal Certificate** (`file_disposal_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a disposal certificate for your files.
* **File Audit** (`file_audit`, text, optional): Conduct a file audit to ensure compliance.
* **Audit Certificate** (`file_audit_cert`, text, optional): Obtain an audit certificate for your files.
* **Review Certificate** (`file_review_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a review certificate for your files.
* **Classification Certificate** (`file_classification_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a classification certificate for your files.
* **Search Certificate** (`file_search_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a search certificate for your files.
* **Retirement Certificate** (`file_retirement_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a retirement certificate for your files.
* **Disposal Certificate** (`file_disposal_cert`, text, optional): Obtain a disposal certificate for your files.
* **Audit Certificate** (`file_audit_cert`, text, optional): Obtain an audit certificate for your files.

## Tips

* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to review each page carefully before moving on to the next.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the provided guidelines or seek assistance from a supervisor or colleague.
* Remember to follow all applicable policies and procedures when organizing and managing your files.
