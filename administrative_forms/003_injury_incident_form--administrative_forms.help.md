# Injury Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document incidents of injuries, including details about the incident, the injured person, and any medical treatment received.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window to ensure that you don't lose any work.
2. Enter the details of the incident, including the location, date, and time it occurred.
3. Select the type of injury and whether it occurred on or off duty.
4. Provide information about the injured person, including their name, job title, and relationship to the reporting person.
5. If medical attention was received, provide details about the treatment and treatment provider.
6. Indicate if the injured person was hospitalized or required medical treatment.
7. Enter any details about medical expenses or work status after the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details**: Provide a brief description of the incident, including where and when it occurred.
	+ **Incident Type**: Select the type of injury that occurred (Musculoskeletal, Traumatic, Exposure, Other).
* **Injury Type**: Select the specific injury that occurred.
	+ **Date Occurred**: Enter the date the injury occurred.
	+ **Time Occurred**: Enter the time the injury occurred.
* **Location of Injury**: Indicate whether the injury occurred on or off duty.
	+ **Body Part Involved**: Indicate which body part was affected.
* **Description of Injury**: Provide a detailed description of the injury.
* **Cause of Injury**: Enter the cause of the injury.
* **Reporting Person Name**: Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Reporting Person Job Title**: (Optional) Enter the job title of the person completing the form.
* **Reporting Person Relationship to Injured**: Enter the relationship between the person completing the form and the injured person.
* **Witness**: (Optional) Enter the name of any witness to the incident.
* **Medical Attention Required**: Indicate whether medical attention is required.
* **Medical Treatment Given**: (Optional) Enter details about any medical treatment received.
* **Medical Treatment Provider**: (Optional) Enter the name of the medical treatment provider.
* **Medical Treatment Cost**: (Optional) Enter the cost of medical treatment.
* **Medical Treatment Date**: (Optional) Enter the date medical treatment was received.
* **Medical Treatment Provider**: (Optional) Enter the name of the medical treatment provider.
* **Days Absent**: (Optional) Enter the number of days missed from work due to the injury.
* **Work Status**: Select the current work status of the injured person.
* **Injury Cause Code**: (Optional) Enter a code for the cause of the injury.
* **Injury Severity Code**: Select the severity of the injury (Minor, Moderate, Major, Unknown).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor or person in charge.
* **Supervisor Relationship to Injured**: Enter the relationship between the supervisor and the injured person.
