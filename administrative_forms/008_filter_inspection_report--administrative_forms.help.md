# filter_inspection_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Filter Inspection Report" form is designed to collect and document the results of inspections performed on filters used to purify or clean water or air. This form helps to standardize the recording of inspection conditions and results, ensuring that all critical information is captured and reported accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the inspection result from the list provided.
2. Enter the maintenance schedule date.
3. Add any additional remarks or comments about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Filter Inspection Conditions 
* **Filter Inspection Conditions** (text, optional): This field allows you to describe the conditions under which the inspection was conducted. Provide any relevant context or details that may have affected the inspection.

### 2. Inspection Results 
* **Inspection Results** (select one, required): Select the result of the inspection from the following options:
	+ Air Quality
	+ Water Quality
	+ Filter Performance

### 3. Maintenance Schedule 
* **Maintenance Schedule** (date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled maintenance.

### 4. Remarks 
* **Remarks** (note, required): Add any additional comments or observations about the inspection. This field is for recording any notes or remarks that you feel are important for future reference. 

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct inspection result from the list.
* Enter the correct maintenance schedule date.
* Use this form to document all inspections and keep track of filter conditions and results.
