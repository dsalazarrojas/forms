# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to emergency incidents involving the team. This form is used to gather details about the incident, such as the team's status, incident description, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Title** of the incident, which should be a brief description of what happened.
2. Provide **Emergency Details** about the incident, which should include any relevant information about the situation.
3. Choose whether the **Team Lead** is involved in the incident.
4. Select all **Team Members** who were involved in the incident.
5. Enter the **Contact Information** for the team lead or relevant personnel.
6. Add a **Location** note about where the incident occurred.
7. Describe the **Incident Description** in as much detail as possible.
8. Enter the **Incident Time** and **Incident Date** of the incident.
9. Choose the **Emergency Status** of the incident.
10. Enter a **Status Update** about the incident's resolution.
11. Add a **Team Response** note about the team's response to the incident.
12. Select whether the incident is an **Incident Type**.
13. Choose the **Team Status** of the team affected by the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (title)**: A brief description of the incident. This field is required.
* **Emergency Details (emergency_details)**: Provide any relevant information about the incident. This field is optional.
* **Team Lead (team_lead)**: Choose whether the team lead is involved in the incident. This field is optional.
* **Team Members (team_members)**: Select all team members involved in the incident. This field is optional.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter the contact information for the team lead or relevant personnel. This field is optional.
* **Location (location)**: Add a note about where the incident occurred. This field is optional.
* **Incident Description (incident_description)**: Describe the incident in as much detail as possible. This field is optional.
* **Incident Time (incident_time)**: Enter the time of the incident. This field is optional.
* **Incident Date (incident_date)**: Enter the date of the incident. This field is optional.
* **Emergency Status (emergency_status)**: Choose the status of the incident. This field is optional.
* **Status Update (status_update)**: Enter a status update about the incident's resolution. This field is optional.
* **Team Response (team_response)**: Add a note about the team's response to the incident. This field is optional.
* **Incident Type (incident_type)**: Select whether the incident is an incident type. This field is optional.
* **Team Status (team_status)**: Choose the status of the team affected by the incident. This field is optional.
