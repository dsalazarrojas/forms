# Worker's Compensation Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from employees who have experienced injuries while working. The goal is to gather details about the injury, treatment, work status, and compensation claims.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out each page of the form according to the instructions below.
2. Be as thorough as possible, providing as much detail as you can about your experience.
3. If you're unsure about any section, feel free to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_info**:
	+ **(ID 1) Employee Info** (Text): This is the space for you to enter your name, job title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **injury_info**:
	+ **(ID 2) Injury Info** (Text): Describe your injury, including when and where it happened.
* **injury_details**:
	+ **(ID 3) Injury Details** (Text): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the injury.
* **treatment_info**:
	+ **(ID 4) Treatment Info** (Text): Describe any medical treatment you received as a result of the injury.
* **medical_info**:
	+ **(ID 5) Medical Info** (Text): Enter any medical information related to the injury, such as diagnoses or procedures performed.
* **work_status**:
	+ **(ID 6) Work Status** (Text): Indicate whether you're currently working or not due to the injury.
* **compensation**:
	+ **(ID 7) Compensation** (Text): Provide information about any compensation you've received or are seeking.
* **medical_provider**:
	+ **(ID 8) Medical Provider** (Text): Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider.
* **medical_report**:
	+ **(ID 9) Medical Report** (Text): Attach any relevant medical reports or documents related to your injury.
* **employer_info**:
	+ **(ID 10) Employer Info** (Text): Enter your employer's name and contact information.
* **claim_status**:
	+ **(ID 11) Claim Status** (Text): Indicate the status of your claim, if you're seeking or received compensation.
* **witness_info**:
	+ **(ID 12) Witness Info** (Text): Describe any witnesses to the injury.
* **witness_info_2**:
	+ **(ID 13) Witness Info 2** (Text): Another space for witness information.
* **witness_info_3**:
	+ **(ID 14) Witness Info 3** (Text): Additional witness information.
* **claimant_info**:
	+ **(ID 15) Claimant Info** (Text): Provide any information about you as the claimant.
* **claimant_info_2**:

* **claimant_info_3**:

* **claimant_info_4**:

* **witness_info_4**:

* **witness_info_5**:
	+ **(ID 20) Witness Info 5** (Text): Witness information.
* **claimant_info_5**:
	+ **(ID 21) Claimant Info 5** (Text): More claimant information.
* **claimant_info_6**:

* **witness_info_6**:

* **witness_info_7**:

* **witness_info_8**:
