# Chemical Spill Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chemical Spill Report Form is a document used to collect and document information about chemical spill incidents in facilities that handle hazardous materials. It will help ensure prompt response and effective cleanup of spills, ensuring the safety and security of the environment and personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **-- Chemical Spill Report Form --** (spill_report_intro): This is the introductory page of the form.
2. **Report number** (report_number): This is an auto-generated identifier for the report. It's not required to be filled in as it will be automatically generated.
3. **Report submission date** (report_date): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
4. **Reference to original incident** (incident_reference): If you are reporting a continuation of an ongoing incident, enter the reference number of the original report.
5. **Facility name** (facility_name): Enter the name of the facility where the spill occurred.
6. **Facility location** (facility_location): Enter the location of the facility where the spill occurred, including any relevant floor or building information.
7. **Reporting party name** (reporting_party): Enter your name or the name of the department/individual reporting the incident.
8. **Title or position** (reporting_party_title): Enter your title or position in the facility.
9. **Contact information** (reporting_party_contact): Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
10. **Date spill occurred** (spill_date): Enter the date of the spill, using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
11. **Time spill occurred** (spill_time): Enter the time of the spill, using 24-hour format HH:MM.
12. **Specific location of spill** (spill_location): Enter a detailed description of the location where the spill occurred.
13. **Chemical involved** (chemical_involved): Enter the name of the chemical involved in the spill.
14. **Quantity released** (chemical_quantity): Enter the amount of the chemical released, including units (e.g., Milliliters, Grams).
15. **Unit of measurement** (quantity_unit): Select the unit of measurement for the quantity released (e.g., Milliliters, Liters, Grams).
16. **Origin of spill** (spill_origin): Select the origin of the spill (e.g., Storage container, Equipment, Transportation).
17. **Visible environmental impact** (visible_impact): Select the level of visible environmental impact (e.g., No visible impact, Minimal impact, Severe impact).
18. **Impact to water sources** (water_impact): Select the impact on water sources (e.g., No water impact, Surface water affected, Groundwater affected).
19. **Impact to air quality** (air_quality_impact): Select the impact on air quality (e.g., No impact, Minor odor, Visible emissions, Significant emissions).
20. **When was response initiated?** (response_initiated): Enter the time response was initiated.
21. **Containment method used** (containment_method): Enter the method used to contain the spill.
22. **Cleanup status** (cleanup_status): Select the current status of the cleanup (e.g., Not started, In progress, Substantially complete, Complete).
23. **Notifications made to** (notifications_made): Select all parties that have been notified (e.g., Environmental agency, Health department, Fire department, etc.).
24. **Follow-up actions planned** (follow_up_actions): Enter any follow-up actions planned to address the spill.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all relevant information is included.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the environmental department for guidance.
