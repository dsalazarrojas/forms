# police_misconduct_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track police misconduct incidents within the police department. It helps to maintain accurate and detailed records of incidents, ensuring prompt action can be taken in cases of misconduct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Officer involved, Other agency personnel, Public, or Unknown).
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
3. Enter the date of the incident.
4. Enter the time of the incident.
5. Specify the location of the incident.
6. Indicate if witnesses were present.
7. Enter the officer's name and badge number.
8. Enter any incident details.
9. Select the action taken (e.g., Arrested, Cited, Warned, or Other).
10. Specify the police department involved.
11. Report how the incident was initially reported (e.g., Online, In-person, Phone, or Other).
12. Choose the contact method used to report the incident.
13. Indicate if follow-up is required.
14. Provide any follow-up details.
15. Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
16. Enter the date the report was submitted.
17. Specify the department that submitted the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, required): Select the type of incident that occurred, such as Officer involved, Other agency personnel, Public, or Unknown.
* **Description** (`description`, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
* **Date of the Incident** (`date`, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of the Incident** (`time`, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of the Incident** (`location`, required): Specify the location where the incident took place.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`, required): Indicate if witnesses were present.
* **Officer's Name (Badge Number)** (`officer_name`, required): Enter the officer's name and badge number.
* **Officer ID** (`officer_id`, required): Enter the officer's ID number.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, required): Enter any additional details about the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, required): Select the action taken in response to the incident, such as Arrested, Cited, Warned, or Other.
* **Police Department** (`police_department`, required): Specify the police department involved in the incident.
* **Incident Reported** (`incident_reported`, required): Report how the incident was initially reported, such as Online, In-person, Phone, or Other.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, required): Choose the method used to contact the person who reported the incident, such as Email, Phone, or In-person.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, required): Indicate if follow-up is required.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, required): Enter any follow-up details.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the report.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, required): Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Submitted By Department** (`submitted_by_department`, required): Specify the department that submitted the report.
