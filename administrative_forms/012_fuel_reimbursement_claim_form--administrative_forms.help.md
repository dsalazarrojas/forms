# Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form is used to claim reimbursement for fuel expenses incurred while using a company vehicle for work-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary supporting documentation, such as fuel receipts and a clear explanation of the work-related purpose of the fuel use.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Enter your name and other required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Claim Employer Fuel Expense
* **Claim Employer Fuel Expense**: If you are an employee, please enter 1. If you are an independent contractor, this field is not applicable.

### Page 2: Claim Fuel Expense Date
* **Claim Fuel Expense Date**: Enter the date on which the fuel was purchased or used for work-related purposes.

### Page 3: Claim Miles Driven
* **Claim Miles Driven**: Enter the total number of miles driven for work-related purposes.

### Page 4: Claim Fuel Expense Type
* **Claim Fuel Expense Type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Business: Select this option if the fuel expense was incurred for company business purposes.
	+ Personal: Select this option if the fuel expense was incurred for personal purposes and has been approved by your manager.
	+ Other: Select this option if the fuel expense was for a non-standard business purpose.

### Page 5: Claim Fuel Expense Amount
* **Claim Fuel Expense Amount**: Enter the total cost of the fuel purchased or used for work-related purposes.

### Page 6: Claim Fuel Expense Mileage
* **Claim Fuel Expense Mileage**: Enter the total mileage associated with the fuel expense.

### Page 7: Claim Fuel Expense Rate
* **Claim Fuel Expense Rate**: Enter the rate per mile (if applicable).

### Page 8: Claim Reimbursement Amount
* **Claim Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the total reimbursement amount claimed.

### Page 9: Claim Additional Expense
* **Claim Additional Expense**: Enter any additional expenses related to the fuel expense, such as taxes or fees.
