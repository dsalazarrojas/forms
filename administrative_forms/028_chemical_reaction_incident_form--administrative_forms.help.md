# Chemical Reaction Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to report chemical reaction incidents that occurred in a laboratory setting. It helps to gather information about the incident, identify potential safety risks, and take corrective actions to prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Reporter Role" to indicate your position in the organization.
2. Enter the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" to describe when the incident occurred.
3. Choose the "Lab Location" where the incident took place.
4. Select the "Incident Type" to specify the type of chemical reaction incident that occurred.
5. List the "Chemicals Involved" and approximate "Quantities Involved".
6. Choose the "Type of Reaction" that was being performed.
7. Describe the "Expected Outcome" and "Actual Outcome".
8. Explain the "Cause of Deviation" from the expected outcome.
9. Answer "Were There Any Injuries?" and provide details if necessary.
10. Choose the "Medical Treatment Required" for any injuries sustained.
11. Indicate "Property Damage" and describe any damage if necessary.
12. Answer "Was Evacuation Required?" and describe any emergency response measures taken.
13. Choose the "Emergency Response" measures taken, such as using a fire extinguisher or deploying a spill kit.
14. Provide the names of any "Witnesses".
15. Describe the "Contributing Factors" that led to the incident.
16. Explain the "Preventive Measures" that could have prevented the incident.
17. Describe the "Corrective Actions Taken" after the incident.
18. Confirm if your "Supervisor" and "Safety Officer" were notified.
19. Choose the "Incident Severity" based on the impact of the incident.
20. Finally, confirm that this report is accurate by selecting "Reporter Signature".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter Name**: This field is required to identify the person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Role**: This field is required to indicate your position in the organization. Choose from:
	+ Laboratory technician
	+ Researcher
	+ Student
	+ Supervisor
	+ Safety officer
	+ Other
* **Incident Date**: This field is required to indicate the date when the incident occurred. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Incident Time**: This field is required to indicate the time when the incident occurred. Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Laboratory Location**: This field is required to indicate where the incident took place.
* **Incident Type**: This field is required to specify the type of chemical reaction incident that occurred. Choose from:
	+ Uncontrolled reaction
	+ Chemical spill
	+ Chemical release
	+ Fire or explosion
	+ Equipment failure
	+ Exposure incident
	+ Near miss
	+ Other
* **Chemicals Involved**: This field is required to list all the chemicals involved in the incident.
* **Quantities Involved**: This field is required to indicate the approximate amounts of chemicals involved.
* **Type of Reaction**: This field is required to specify the type of reaction being performed. Choose from:
	+ Synthesis
	+ Decomposition
	+ Oxidation-reduction
	+ Acid-base
	+ Precipitation
	+ Combustion
	+ Other
* **Expected Outcome**: This field is required to describe the expected result of the reaction.
* **Actual Outcome**: This field is required to describe what actually happened during the incident.
* **Cause of Deviation**: This field is required to explain why the reaction deviated from the expected outcome.
* **Were There Any Injuries?**: This field is required to indicate if anyone got injured. Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Injury Details**: This field is optional to describe any injuries sustained.
* **Medical Treatment Required**: This field is required to indicate if medical treatment was needed. Choose from:
	+ Yes, first aid only
	+ Yes, medical attention
	+ Yes, hospitalization
	+ No treatment needed
	+ Not applicable
* **Property Damage**: This field is required to indicate if any property damage occurred. Choose from:
	+ None
	+ Minor
	+ Moderate
	+ Severe
	+ Total loss
* **Damage Details**: This field is optional to describe any property damage.
* **Was Evacuation Required?**: This field is required to indicate if anyone needed to evacuate. Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Emergency Response**: This field is required to describe the emergency response measures taken. Choose from:
	+ Fire extinguisher used
	+ Emergency shower
	+ Eyewash station
	+ Spill kit deployed
	+ Emergency services called
	+ Area evacuated
	+ Ventilation increased
	+ Other
* **Witnesses**: This field is optional to provide the names of any witnesses.
* **Contributing Factors**: This field is required to describe the factors that contributed to the incident.
* **Preventive Measures**: This field is required to explain the measures that could have prevented the incident.
* **Corrective Actions Taken**: This field is required to describe the actions taken after the incident.
* **Was Supervisor Notified?**: This field is required to confirm if your supervisor was informed. Choose from:
	+ Yes, immediately
	+ Yes, within 1 hour
	+ Yes, delayed
	+ False
* **Was Safety Officer Notified?**: This field is required to confirm if the safety officer was informed. Choose from:
	+ Yes, immediately
	+ Yes, within 1 hour
	+ Yes, delayed
	+ False
* **Incident Severity**: This field is required to indicate the overall severity of the incident. Choose from:
	+ Minor - No injuries, minimal impact
	+ Moderate - Minor injuries, some damage
	+ Serious - Significant injuries or damage
	+ Critical - Major injuries or extensive damage
* **Reporter Signature**: This field is required to confirm that this report is accurate. Choose from:
	+ Yes, I confirm
	+ False

Note: The "Reporter Signature" field is the last field on the form and should be completed by the person reporting the incident to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
