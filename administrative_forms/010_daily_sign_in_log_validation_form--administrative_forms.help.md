# Daily Sign In Log Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Sign In Log Validation Form is designed to track employee attendance and validate their sign-in and sign-out times, along with any reason for absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the date of your sign-in using the calendar in the `date` field.
3. Enter your sign-in time in the `time_in` field.
4. Enter your sign-out time in the `time_out` field.
5. If you are absent from work, describe your reason for absence in the `reason_for_absence` field.
6. Have your supervisor verify your information by signing in the `supervisor_signature` field.
7. Sign with your name in the `employee_signature` field, confirming that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daily Sign In Log Validation Form** (`form_title`): This is the title of the form, which should already be filled in for you.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel record.
* **date** (`date`): Select the date of your sign-in using the calendar provided.
* **time_in** (`time_in`): Enter the time you started work or sign-in.
* **time_out** (`time_out`): Enter the time you finished work or sign-out.
* **reason_for_absence** (`reason_for_absence`): If you were absent from work, provide a brief explanation for your absence.
* **supervisor_signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Have your supervisor verify your information by signing in this field.
* **employee_signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign with your name in the box below, confirming that the information provided is accurate.

## Tips

* Always enter accurate information to avoid any discrepancies or issues.
* Double-check your supervisor's signature to ensure they have verified your information.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
* Submit the form as soon as possible to ensure timely validation and tracking of your activities.
