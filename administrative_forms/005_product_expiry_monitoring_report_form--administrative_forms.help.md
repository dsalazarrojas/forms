# Product Expiry Monitoring Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Expiry Monitoring Report Form is used to monitor and report products that are approaching or have exceeded their expiry dates. This form helps administrators keep track of products that require attention before they go bad.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the product from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a brief description of the product and why it's approaching or exceeded its expiry date.
3. Select the form number(s) of the product(s) being reported.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, required/optional): Select the category that the product falls under.
	+ For example, if the product is a food item, select "Administrative Forms".
* **Description** (`description`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the product and why it's approaching or exceeded its expiry date.
	+ Keep it short and sweet, only including the essential information.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, required/optional): Select the form number(s) of the product(s) being reported.
	+ If multiple products are being reported, separate each form number with a comma.
