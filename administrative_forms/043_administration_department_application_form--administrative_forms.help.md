**Administration Department Application Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect application information from potential candidates for administrative roles within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your full name and contact information in the "Applicant details" section.
2.  Enter your full name as it appears on your resume or ID.
3.  Enter your email address that you check regularly for communication regarding your application.
4.  Specify the position you are applying for in the "Position applied for" field.
5.  Describe your relevant qualifications, education, and experience in the "Qualifications" field.
6.  Indicate your earliest available start date in the "Availability to start" field.
7.  Provide two professional references in the "References" field.
8.  Write a brief summary of your interest in the position and why you are a good fit in the "Cover letter summary" field.
9.  Confirm that a resume or CV has been attached by selecting "True" in the "Resume attached" field.
10.  Add any additional information that you believe is relevant to your application in the "Additional information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant details**: This section provides a brief overview of the applicant's contact information and is used for internal purposes.
* **Full name**: This field is used to collect the applicant's full legal name as it appears on their resume or ID.
* **Email address**: This field is used to collect the applicant's email address that they check regularly for communication regarding their application.
* **Position applied for**: This field is used to collect the specific position the applicant is applying for within the administration department.
* **Qualifications**: This field is used to collect the applicant’s relevant qualifications, education, and experience.
* **Availability to start**: This field is used to collect the earliest date the applicant is available to start in the position.
* **References**: This field is used to collect two professional references for the applicant.
* **Cover letter summary**: This field is used to collect a brief summary of the applicant's interest in the position and why they are a good fit.
* **Resume attached**: This field is used to confirm whether a resume or CV has been attached with the application.
* **Additional information**: This field is used to collect any other relevant information that the applicant believes is important for their application.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Attach a resume or CV to support the application.
- Use proper email address and phone number format.
- Provide clear and concise information in the "Cover letter summary" field.
- Proofread the application before submitting it to ensure accuracy and clarity.
- If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the administration department.
