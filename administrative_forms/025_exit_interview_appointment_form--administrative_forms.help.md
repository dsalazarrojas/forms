# exit_interview_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exit interview form is a crucial tool for collecting information from individuals who are leaving the company or organization. It helps to gather essential details about the reasons for departure, the circumstances surrounding the exit, and any suggestions or feedback from the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Interviewee Details section with the individual's name, job title, and any other relevant information.
2. Select the date of the interview or the date of the individual's exit.
3. Choose the time of the interview or the individual's last working hours.
4. Enter the duration of the interview or the individual's notice period.
5. Provide a reason for the individual's departure in the Prophets field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Interviewee Details**: Enter the individual's name, job title, and any other relevant details about the interviewee (e.g., department or team).
* **Date**: Select the date of the interview or the date of the individual's exit from the company.
* **Time**: Choose the time of the interview or the individual's last working hours.
* **Duration**: Enter the duration of the interview or the individual's notice period in hours or days.
* **Prophets**: Provide a reason for the individual's departure. This could be a short paragraph explaining their decision to leave the company.
