# Student Affairs Office Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Affairs Office Admission Form is used to collect information from students for admission purposes. This form is intended for students who want to be admitted into the Student Affairs Office's programs and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title at the top of the page.
2. Provide your student name.
3. Enter your student email address.
4. Enter your student phone number (or any other contact number you prefer to be contacted on).
5. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Select the program of interest from the dropdown menu.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Affairs Office Admission Form** (`form_title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which should be filled out by the student. It's not required but it's a good idea to provide a descriptive title.
- **Student Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your student name.
- **Student Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your student email address.
- **Student Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your student phone number (or any other contact number you prefer to be contacted on).
- **Student Date of Birth** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Program of Interest** (`program`, select_one, optional): Select the program that you are interested in from the dropdown menu. Please select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Review your information carefully before submitting the form. Make sure all fields are filled out correctly before submitting.
