# Trailer Breakdown Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trailer Breakdown Process Checklist is a form designed to guide users through the process of documenting and tracking trailer breakdowns. It helps to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to address any issues that have arisen with the trailer, and promotes efficiency in maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Introduction" page with any relevant details about the trailer breakdown.
2. On the "Trailer Damage" page, describe any damages or issues found with the trailer.
3. On the "Safety Protocols" page, list any safety protocols that were followed or failed to be followed during the breakdown process.
4. On the "Repairs Documentation" page, record any repairs or maintenance activities performed on the trailer.
5. On the "Maintenance Activities" page, select all maintenance activities that were performed or needed to be performed on the trailer.
6. On the "Additional Comments" page, enter any additional comments or observations about the breakdown process.
7. On the "Assigned User" page, select the user assigned to address the trailer breakdown.
8. On the "Completion Status" page, select the status of the trailer breakdown process completion (Not Started, In Progress, Complete).
9. On the "Completion Date" page, enter the date the trailer breakdown process was completed.
10. On the "Completion Time" page, enter the time the trailer breakdown process was completed.
11. On the "Duration" page, enter the total duration of the trailer breakdown process.
12. On the "Start Time" page, enter the start time of the trailer breakdown process.
13. On the "End Time" page, enter the end time of the trailer breakdown process.
14. On the "Total Time" page, enter the total time spent on the trailer breakdown process.
15. On the "Trailer Number" page, enter the trailer number.
