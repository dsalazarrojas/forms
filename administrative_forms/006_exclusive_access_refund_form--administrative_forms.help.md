# Exclusive Access Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exclusive Access Refund Form is used to request a refund for an exclusive access granted to a user. This form helps us understand the reason for the refund and the amount to be refunded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Make sure you are submitting a refund request for a valid reason. If you're unsure or have questions, contact us first.
2. Enter your User Id, which is usually provided to you by our support team.
3. Provide a clear and concise Reason For Refund, explaining why you're requesting the refund.
4. Enter the Refund Amount, which should be the full amount of the exclusive access granted.
5. Select the Date Of Purchase from the calendar, indicating when the exclusive access was granted.
6. Select the Time Of Purchase from the time picker, indicating when the exclusive access was granted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Id** (`user_id`, text, required): Enter your User Id provided by our support team.
* **Reason For Refund** (`reason_for_refund`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation for the refund request. Please be specific and concise.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the full amount of the exclusive access granted for refund.
* **Date Of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, optional): Select the date when the exclusive access was granted.
* **Time Of Purchase** (`time_of_purchase`, time, optional): Select the time when the exclusive access was granted.
