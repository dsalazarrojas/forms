# Audit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audit Authorization Form is used to request permission to conduct an audit within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of audit you are conducting (e.g., Financial Audit, Compliance Audit, etc.).
2.  Enter the audit ID reference.
3.  Enter the lead auditor's name.
4.  Select the department to be audited.
5.  Enter a summary of the audit scope, explaining which processes or areas are covered.
6.  Select the access permissions granted to the auditor (e.g., Full Physical Access to Premises, Access to Digital Records and Systems, etc.).
7.  Enter the expected start and end dates of the audit.
8.  Select the level of authorization granted to the auditor (e.g., Full Unrestricted Access, Limited Access, etc.).
9.  Enter the name and title of the authorized official.
10.  Enter the date of authorization.
11.  Enter the official's digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Audit ID Reference** (`audit_id_reference`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the audit.
*   **Type of Audit** (`audit_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of audit you are conducting (e.g., Financial Audit, Compliance Audit, etc.).
*   **Lead Auditor Name** (`lead_auditor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the lead auditor.
*   **Department to be Audited** (`authorized_department`, `text`, required): Select the department to be audited.
*   **Summary of Audit Scope** (`audit_scope_summary`, `text`, required): Enter a summary of the audit scope, explaining which processes or areas are covered.
*   **Access Permissions Granted** (`access_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the access permissions granted to the auditor (e.g., Full Physical Access to Premises, Access to Digital Records and Systems, etc.).
*   **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected start date of the audit.
*   **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the expected end date of the audit.
*   **Level of Authorization Granted** (`authorization_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of authorization granted to the auditor (e.g., Full Unrestricted Access, Limited Access, etc.).
*   **Authorized Official Name** (`authorized_official_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the authorized official.
*   **Official Title** (`official_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the authorized official.
*   **Date of Authorization** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of authorization.
*   **Official Digital Signature** (`digital_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the official's digital signature.
