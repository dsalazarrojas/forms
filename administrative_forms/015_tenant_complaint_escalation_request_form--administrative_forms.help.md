# Tenant Complaint Escalation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Complaint Escalation Request Form is a tool for tenants to report issues that cannot be resolved through standard communication channels. It helps to escalate these complaints to the right person or department for further action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "Escalate Complaint" button to begin the form.
* Enter a detailed description of the issue in the "Escalation Reason" field.
* Select who you would like to escalate the complaint to from the "Escalate To" dropdown menu.
* Provide any supporting documents related to the issue, such as copies of agreements or rent rolls.
* Enter your preferred method of contact in the "Preferred Contact" field.
* Provide your contact information in the "Tenant Contact Information" field.
* If you have any additional information or attachments that may help with the complaint, you can include them in the "Property Manager Contact Information" field.
* Review your submission to ensure that it's complete and accurate before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Complaint Escalation Request Form** (`tenant_complaint_escalation_request_form_page_1`): This is the title of the form and the page it appears on.
* **Escalate Complaint** (`tenant_complaint_escalation_request_form_page_1`): This button begins the form, allowing you to start the escalation process.
* **Escalation Reason** (`escalation_reason`): This field is for a detailed description of the issue you're reporting. Please provide as much information as possible so that the person or department handling your complaint can understand the issue.
* **Escalate To** (`escalate_to`): Select one of the options to indicate who you would like to escalate the complaint to. This will help direct the issue to the correct department or person.
* **Description of Issue** (`description`): This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about the issue. While not required, it can help with the resolution process.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): If you have any relevant documents that may help with the complaint, such as agreements or rent rolls, you can attach them here.
* **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`): Select your preferred method of contact for follow-up and updates on the status of your complaint.
* **Tenant Contact Information** (`tenant_contact_information`): This field is for providing your contact information, which will help the person or department handling your complaint reach out to you as needed.
* **Property Manager Contact Information** (`property_manager_contact_information`): If you have any additional information or attachments that may be relevant to the complaint, you can include them here. While not required, it can be helpful for the property manager to have this information.
