# Workplace Hazard Identification Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees identify, assess, and address potential hazards in the workplace environment. It is intended to be used as part of a comprehensive occupational health and safety training program to ensure a safe and healthy work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the provided text fields and answer each question thoroughly.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Environment Assessment** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for assessing the overall safety and health of your work environment. Please describe the conditions, hazards, and risks present in your workplace.
* **Hazards Identification** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Identify any potential hazards in your workplace, such as chemical, physical, or biological risks.
* **Incident Reporting** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have experienced an incident, report it here.
* **Action Plan Development** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe the actions you plan to take to mitigate hazards and prevent future incidents.
* **Follow Up** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have followed up on any previous actions, describe the results.
* **Evaluation** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Evaluate the effectiveness of your actions and identify areas for improvement.
* **Review** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Summarize your overall review of the workplace hazards.
* **Follow Up 2** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any additional follow-up information or comments.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Remember to review and update this form regularly to ensure ongoing safety and compliance.
