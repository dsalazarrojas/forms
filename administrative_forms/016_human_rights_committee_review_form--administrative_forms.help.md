# Human Rights Committee Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Rights Committee Review Form is a comprehensive form designed to review and track the progress of cases related to human rights issues. It allows users to provide detailed information, follow up on case statuses, and record review dates and follow-up notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input field with any relevant information about the case.
2. Enter the unique Case Number assigned to each case.
3. Provide the Complainant's Name.
4. Enter the Organization related to the case.
5. Select the Case Type from the provided options (Civil, Criminal, Immigration, or Other).
6. Briefly describe the Case Issue.
7. Set the Follow-up Date for the case.
8. Update the Case Status (Open, Closed) and Review Date.
9. Enter the Next Follow-up Date and Follow-up Status (No follow-up, Completed, Ongoing).
10. Provide any Comments related to the case.
11. Identify who is Assigned To for follow-up actions.
12. Enter Review Notes and Follow-up Notes.
13. Update the Review Status (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
14. Enter Follow-up Details.
15. Provide User Input 2 (optional).
16. Enter Case Notes.
17. Enter Review Details.
18. Set the Next Follow-up Reminder.
19. Record the Follow-up Reminder Sent, Follow-up Reminder Email Sent, Follow-up Reminder SMS Sent, and Follow-up Reminder Call Sent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the case.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique number for each case.
* **Complainant Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Provide the Complainant's Name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): Enter the Organization related to the case.
* **Case Type** (`case_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the Case Type (Civil, Criminal, Immigration, or Other).
* **Case Issue** (`issue`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the Case Issue.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Set the Follow-up Date for the case.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Update the Case Status (Open, Closed).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the Review Date.
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the Next Follow-up Date.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_one`, optional): Set the Follow-up Status (No follow-up, Completed, Ongoing).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any Comments related to the case.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, optional): Identify who is Assigned To for follow-up actions.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter Review Notes.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter Follow-up Notes.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, optional): Update the Review Status (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, `text`, optional): Enter Follow-up Details.
* **User Input 2** (`user_input_2`, `text`, optional): Provide User Input 2 (optional).
* **Case Notes** (`case_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter Case Notes.
* **Review Details** (`review_details`, `text`, optional): Enter Review Details.
* **Next Follow-up Reminder** (`next_follow_up_reminder`, `time`, optional): Set the Next Follow-up Reminder.
* **Follow-up Reminder Sent** (`follow_up_reminder_sent`, `text`, optional): Record the Follow-up Reminder Sent.
* **Follow-up Reminder Email Sent** (`follow_up_reminder_email_sent`, `text`, optional): Record the Follow-up Reminder Email Sent.
* **Follow-up Reminder SMS Sent** (`follow_up_reminder_sms_sent`, `text`, optional): Record the Follow-up Reminder SMS Sent.
* **Follow-up Reminder Call Sent** (`follow_up_reminder_call_sent`, `text`, optional): Record the Follow-up Reminder Call Sent.
