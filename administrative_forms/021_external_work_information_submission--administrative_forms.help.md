## External Work Information Submission - Help Guide

### Purpose
The External Work Information Submission form is designed to collect information about an employee's external work activities, such as client meetings, projects, and other external work engagements. This form is used to keep track of external work hours, rates, expenses, and comments related to these activities.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the External Work Activity type from the list of options provided. You can choose from Client Meeting, Project Development, or Other.
2. Enter a brief description of the project or activity in the Project Description field.
3. Fill in the start and end dates of the external work period in the Work Start Date and Work End Date fields, respectively.
4. Enter the location of the external work in the Work Location field.
5. If you're entering an "Other" external work activity, please provide additional details in the Other Work Location field.
6. Enter the name and contact information of your supervisor in the Supervisor Name, Supervisor Email, and Supervisor Phone fields.
7. Enter your own contact information, including your name, email, and phone number in the Employee Name, Employee Email, and Employee Phone fields.
8. Enter your job title in the Employee Job Title field.
9. Enter the external work hours, rate, and expense amounts in the External Work Hours, External Work Rate, and External Work Expense fields.
10. Finally, add any additional comments about the external work in the External Work Comments field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **External Work Activity** (`external_work_activity`, `select_one`): Select the type of external work activity you are submitting.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`): Enter a brief description of the project or activity.
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, `date`): Enter the start date of the external work period.
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, `date`): Enter the end date of the external work period.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`): Enter the location of the external work.
* **Other Work Location** (`work_location_other`, `text`): Enter additional details about the location if you chose "Other" as the external work activity type.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `text`): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`): Enter your name.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `text`): Enter your email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, `text`): Enter your job title.
* **External Work Hours** (`external_work_hours`, `number`): Enter the external work hours.
* **External Work Rate** (`external_work_rate`, `number`): Enter the external work rate.
* **External Work Expense** (`external_work_expense`, `number`): Enter the external work expense.
* **External Work Comments** (`external_work_comments`, `note`): Add any additional comments about the external work.
