# Maritime Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maritime Incident Report Form is a tool for reporting incidents that occur at or near the maritime domain. It allows users to record and track incidents, which will be used for investigations and follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the form

*   Ensure you submit only one form per incident.
*   Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use the available space for any additional comments or notes you feel are necessary.

### Step 2: Submit the form

*   Once you have completed all the required fields, click submit to submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Date, required: false)
    This field is not required, but it is strongly recommended that you complete it for accurate tracking and follow-up.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required: false)
    Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false)
    Provide a thorough description of the incident, including any relevant details.
*   **Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false)
    Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., spill, collision, grounding, etc.).
*   **Status** (`incident_status`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select the status of the incident (e.g., ongoing, resolved, under investigation, etc.).
*   **Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required: false)
    Enter your name and contact information for follow-up.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, note, required: false)
    Enter any relevant contact information for follow-up or investigation purposes.
*   **Incident Image** (`incident_image`, text, required: false)
    Upload any relevant images or evidence related to the incident.
*   **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, note, required: false)
    Enter any relevant notes or observations for the investigation.
*   **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the status of the investigation (e.g., ongoing, resolved, under review, etc.).
*   **Investigation Closure** (`investigation_closure`, date, required: false)
    Enter the date the investigation was closed.
*   **Incident Closure** (`incident_closure`, date, required: false)
    Enter the date the incident was closed.
*   **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, text, required: false)
    Enter any relevant details on the resolution of the incident.
*   **Followup** (`incident_followup`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select any follow-up actions taken or planned for the incident.
*   **Reams** (`incident_rems`, text, required: false)
    Enter any additional comments or observations.
