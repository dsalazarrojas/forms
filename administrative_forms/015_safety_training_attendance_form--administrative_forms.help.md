# Safety Training Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about attendees and track their attendance status for safety training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the session details, including date, time, and location.
2. Provide the attendee's name and email address.
3. Enter the attendee's phone number (optional).
4. Select the training date for the session.
5. Choose the start and end times for the training session.
6. Enter the duration of the training session.
7. Provide information about the session location and topic.
8. Identify the facilitator or instructor for the session.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments about the session (optional).
10. Indicate whether the attendee attended the session or not.
11. Select the attendance status for the session (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, or Completed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details**: Enter the date, start time, and end time of the training session.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter the name of the attendee (e.g., John Doe).
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the email address of the attendee (e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)).
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the attendee (optional).
* **Training Date**: Select the date of the training session (e.g., 2022-02-01).
* **Training Session Start Time**: Select the start time of the training session (e.g., 09:00 AM).
* **Training Session End Time**: Select the end time of the training session (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Training Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the training session (e.g., 1 hour).
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the training session (e.g., Conference Room).
* **Session Topic**: Enter the topic of the training session (e.g., Basic First Aid).
* **Session Facilitator**: Enter the name of the facilitator or instructor for the session (e.g., Jane Doe).
* **Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the session (optional).
* **Session Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the session (optional).
* **Training Attendance**: Select whether the attendee attended the session or not (Attended or Did Not Attend).
* **Attendance Status**: Select the status of the attendee's attendance (e.g., Pending, Confirmed, In Progress, or Completed).
