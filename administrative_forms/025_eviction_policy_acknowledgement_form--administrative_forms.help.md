# eviction_policy_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eviction Policy Acknowledgement Form is a document used to record the eviction process and gather acknowledgment and agreement from tenants. 

## How To Complete This Form
This form consists of 7 fields, each with a specific purpose. Please review the following instructions carefully:

1. **Tenant Acknowledgment** (Text Field):
This field is used to record the tenant's acknowledgment of the eviction process. Please write a brief statement explaining the reason for the eviction.

2. **Policy Agreement** (Text Field):
This field is used to gather the tenant's agreement to the policy. Please have the tenant sign this field to acknowledge they understand the policy.

3. **Communication Method** (Select One):
This field is used to determine how the tenant would like to be contacted. Please select one of the options provided: Email, Phone, or In Person.

4. **Agreement Date** (Date Field):
This field is used to record the date of the agreement. Please select a date after the date of the acknowledgment.

5. **Signature Date** (Date Field):
This field is used to record the date of the signature. Please ensure that this date is after the agreement date.

6. **Landlord Signature** (Text Field):
This field is used for the landlord's or property manager's signature. Please have them sign this field to confirm the agreement.

7. **Tenant Signature** (Text Field):
This field is used for the tenant's signature. Please have them sign this field to confirm the agreement.

8. **Reason for Eviction** (Text Field):
This field is used to record the reason for the eviction. Please be specific and provide details.

9. **Additional Comments** (Text Field):
This field is used for any additional comments or information. Please provide any relevant details.

## Tips
- Always ensure that the form is filled out by a landlord or property manager.
- Review local and national laws regarding evictions before using this form.
- Use this form in a controlled environment.
- Be clear and concise when asking the tenant for acknowledgment and agreement.
- Always have the tenant sign the agreement and acknowledgment fields. 
- Review the reason for eviction carefully and ensure it is accurate. 
- Use this form as a template and customize it according to your needs.
