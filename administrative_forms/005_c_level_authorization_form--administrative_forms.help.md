# C Level Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The C Level Authorization Form is used to request high-level approval from executive teams for various organizational matters that require their attention. It's a critical tool for ensuring that all necessary information is collected and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Requester Name" field with the person initiating the request.
2. Select the "Requester Role" field with the job title or role within the organization.
3. Choose the department responsible for the request in the "Department" field.
4. Enter a short title for the authorization in the "Project or Request Title" field.
5. Select the type of approval needed from the "Authorization Type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the financial amount or qualitative impact description in the "Amount or Impact" field (if applicable).
7. Provide a clear justification for the authorization request in the "Justification" field.
8. Summarize the principal risks and mitigations in the "Risk Summary" field (if applicable).
9. Choose the date when the authorization should take effect in the "Requested Effective Date" field (if applicable).
10. List the roles or names required to sign in the "Required Signatures" field.
11. Finally, update the "Approval Status" field to track the outcome of the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Name**: Enter the name of the person initiating the request.
- **Requester Role**: Enter the job title or role within the organization.
- **Department**: Choose the department responsible for the request.
- **Project or Request Title**: Enter a short title for the authorization.
- **Authorization Type**: Select the type of approval needed from the available options.
  - **Budget Approval**: Approval for financial transactions.
  - **Policy Exception**: Approval to bypass or modify organizational policies.
  - **Contract Approval**: Approval for contractual agreements or modifications.
  - **Staffing Change**: Approval for changes to staffing levels or structure.
  - **Other**: Choose this option if the type of approval doesn't fit into the other categories.
- **Amount or Impact**: Enter the financial amount or qualitative impact description, if applicable.
- **Justification**: Provide a clear reason for the authorization request.
- **Risk Summary**: Briefly summarize the principal risks and mitigations, if applicable.
- **Requested Effective Date**: Choose the date when the authorization should take effect, if applicable.
- **Required Signatures**: List the roles or names required to sign in this field.
- **Approval Status**: Update the field to track the outcome of the request, which can be:
  - **Pending**: The request is waiting for approval or review.
  - **Approved**: The request has been approved.
  - **Denied**: The request has been denied.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Use clear and concise language to ensure that the request is understood effectively.
- Proofread the form before submitting it to avoid errors.
- Keep the "Risk Summary" and "Amount or Impact" fields up-to-date if the situation changes.
- Monitor the "Approval Status" field for updates on the request's outcome.
