# Course Withdrawal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Withdrawal Form is used to notify the administrative staff of a student's decision to withdraw from a course. This form is intended for students to inform the staff about their withdrawal status and the reason for it.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your enrollment information, including your student ID and name.
2. Select your current status as a student (Active or Inactive).
3. Indicate whether you are withdrawing from the course (Yes or No).
4. Choose the academic term you are currently in.
5. Select whether you are withdrawing from the session (Yes or No).
6. Enter the date of your withdrawal.
7. Enter the time of your withdrawal.
8. Confirm that you are submitting this form to withdraw from the course (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Info** (`enrollment_info`, text, optional): Enter your student ID and name to help us identify you in our records.
* **Student Data** (`student_data`, text, optional): This field is not used in this form, but please enter your name and student ID here if you want to be identified.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current status as a student (Active or Inactive).
* **Withdrawal Info** (`withdrawal_info`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you are withdrawing from the course (Yes or No).
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your withdrawal.
* **Academic Term** (`academic_term`, select_one, optional): Choose the academic term you are currently in.
* **Session** (`session`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are withdrawing from the session (Yes or No).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your withdrawal.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your withdrawal.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you are submitting this form to withdraw from the course (Yes or No).
