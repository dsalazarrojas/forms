# Coach Compliance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your compliance with regulatory requirements and standards for coaches. It's essential to complete this form accurately to demonstrate your commitment to upholding industry standards and our organization's policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question carefully and accurately**. For fields with options, select the one that best applies to your situation.
2. **Fill out all required fields**. These are marked as "required" in the form.
3. **Use the provided hints for guidance**. If you're unsure about a field, review the hint for clarification.
4. **Submit the form when complete**. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Full Name** (`1`: `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`2`: `email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`3`: `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Organization** (`4`: `text`, `optional`): If you're affiliated with a company or organization, enter its name.
* **Coach ID Number** (`5`: `text`, `optional`): If you have a unique internal ID, enter it here.
* **Assessment Date** (`6`: `date`, `required`): Enter the date you're completing this form.
* **Coaching Certification Status** (`7`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your coaching certification.
* **Certification Body** (`8`: `text`, `optional`): If you've received certification, enter the organization that issued it.
* **Certification Expiry Date** (`9`: `date`, `optional`): If your certification has an expiry date, enter it here.
* **Liability Insurance Status** (`10`: `select_one`, `required`): Select your status regarding liability insurance.
* **Insurance Provider** (`11`: `text`, `optional`): If you have liability insurance, enter your insurance company's name.
* **Policy Number** (`12`: `text`, `optional`): If you have liability insurance, enter your policy number.
* **Background Check Status** (`13`: `select_one`, `required`): Select your background check status.
* **Continuing Education Hours** (`14`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the number of hours you've completed for continuing education.
* **Code of Ethics Training Completed** (`15`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your code of ethics training.
* **Confidentiality Agreement Signed** (`16`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your confidentiality agreement.
* **Safety Protocols Training** (`17`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your safety protocols training.
* **Emergency Procedures Known** (`18`: `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you're aware of emergency procedures.
* **Client Records Compliance** (`19`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your client records.
* **Data Protection Training** (`20`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your data protection training.
* **Scope of Practice Understood** (`21`: `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you understand your scope of practice.
* **Mandatory Reporting Training** (`22`: `select_one`, `optional`): Select the status of your mandatory reporting training.
* **Conflicts of Interest** (`23`: `text`, `optional`): If you have conflicts of interest, describe them here.
* **Disciplinary History** (`24`: `select_one`, `required`): Select the status of your disciplinary history.
* **Disciplinary Details** (`25`: `text`, `optional`): If applicable, describe your disciplinary history.
* **Overall Compliance Self-Rating** (`26`: `select_one`, `required`): Rate your overall compliance level.
* **Areas Needing Improvement** (`27`: `text`, `optional`): If you identify areas for improvement, describe them here.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`28`: `text`, `optional`): If you need support or resources, describe them here.
* **I Attest Information Is Accurate** (`29`: `select_one`, `required`): Confirm that all information is accurate.
* **Date of Assessment** (`30`: `date`, `required`): Enter the date of assessment.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about a field, review the hint for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all required fields filled out.
* If you need help or support, contact our organization's support team.
