# Legal Obligation Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Obligation Absence Form is used to report absences from work due to legal obligations, such as court appearances, jury duty, or other legal commitments. This form helps the management to process employee absences and ensure compliance with company policies and applicable laws.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the employee's request for absence in the "Employee Request" field.
2. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of absence in the "Reason for Absence" field.
3. Attach any relevant documents or evidence that support the employee's request, if applicable.
4. Indicate whether the employee has any supporting documents by checking "Supporting Documents".
5. Confirm whether the employee has any documents to support their request by checking "Employee Signature".
6. Choose whether the management should approve or deny the absence request by selecting "Manager Approval".
7. Enter the date of absence.
8. Enter the time of absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Request** (`employee_request`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the legal obligation that requires your absence from work.
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, required/optional): Provide a short explanation for the reason of absence.
* **Employee Documents** (`employee_documents`, text, required/optional): Attach any relevant documents or evidence that support your request, such as court summons or jury duty notice.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required/optional): Confirm whether you have any supporting documents for your request.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you have any documents to support your request. Please select "True" if you have documents and "False" if you do not.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the management should approve or deny your absence request.
* **Date Absence** (`date_absence`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of absence.
* **Time Absence** (`time_absence`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of absence.

## Tips

* Please complete this form as soon as possible after becoming aware of the commitment.
* Make sure to attach any relevant documents or evidence that support your request.
* If you are unsure about the purpose of this form or have any questions, please consult with your supervisor or HR department.
