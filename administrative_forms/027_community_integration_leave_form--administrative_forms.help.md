# Community Integration Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Integration Leave Form is used to submit and manage leave requests from community members. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected and that manager approval is obtained before granting leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Employee Name**: Enter the full legal name of the employee requesting leave.
2. **Enter Employee ID (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the internal identifier for the employee.
3. **Department or Team**: Enter the department or team to which the employee belongs.
4. **Leave Start Date**: Enter the first day of the leave.
5. **Leave End Date**: Enter the last day of the leave.
6. **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief justification or description for the leave request.
7. **Contact Method During Leave**: If available, provide an email or phone number for contact during the leave.
8. **Manager Approval**: Select one of the following options:
	* Approved
	* Denied
	* Pending
9. **Supporting Documents Provided**: List any documents submitted with the leave request.
