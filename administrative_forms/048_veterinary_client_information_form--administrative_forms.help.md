# Veterinary Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Client Information Form is a tool used to collect essential information from veterinary clients. It serves as a comprehensive document to gather details about the client and their pet, including contact information, pet details, and any additional relevant information. This form is designed to be completed by veterinary clients or their representatives to provide accurate and up-to-date information to the veterinary clinic or practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your contact information, including your name, address, and contact details.
2. Proceed to the "Pet Information" section, where you will provide details about your pet, such as its name, species, breed, age, and health status.
3. In the "Contact Information" section, provide contact details for how you can be reached, including your phone number and email address.
4. If there is any additional information you would like to provide, you can do so in the "Additional Information" section.
5. Review your completed form to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. <a href="#page_1" class="anchor" id="page_1">Client Information</a>
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Address**: Provide your residential or business address where you can be contacted or receive mail.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or mail).

### 2. <a href="#page_2" class="anchor" id="page_2">Pet Information</a>
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's full name.
* **Species**: Select the type of animal (e.g., dog, cat, bird, etc.).
* **Breed**: Enter your pet's breed or type (e.g., Labrador, Persian, etc.).
* **Age**: Enter your pet's age.
* **Health Status**: Select your pet's health status.

### 3. <a href="#page_3" class="anchor" id="page_3">Contact Information</a>
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for your pet.
* **Address**: Provide the address of the primary contact person or your pet's location.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the primary contact email address.

### 4. <a href="#page_4" class="anchor" id="page_4">Additional Information</a>
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any extra details you would like to share with the veterinary clinic or practice.
