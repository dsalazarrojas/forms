# Conference Room Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and manage conference room equipment for events and meetings. It's a one-stop-shop to submit your equipment needs and coordinate logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information: 
	* Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the event details: 
	* Choose the purpose of the event (e.g., Meeting, Presentation, Training, etc.).
	* Enter the expected number of attendees.
3. Select the required equipment: 
	* Choose the necessary audio, video, display, computer, connectivity, lighting, and furniture equipment for your event.
4. Provide delivery and logistics information: 
	* Specify the room or location where the equipment is needed.
	* Select the date and time the equipment is needed and will be returned.
	* Indicate if technical support is required.
5. Add any special instructions or notes: 
	* Provide any additional setup requirements or information that may be necessary for the equipment setup.
6. Finalize your submission: 
	* Review and verify all the information provided.
	* Agree to the equipment usage terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Provide your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (4, text, required): Indicate your department or team.
* **Employee ID** (5, text, optional): Enter your staff number (if applicable).
* **Room or Location** (6, text, required): Specify the room or location where the equipment is needed.
* **Date of Request** (7, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Date Equipment Needed** (8, date, required): Enter the date the equipment is needed.
* **Date Equipment Will Be Returned** (9, date, required): Enter the date the equipment will be returned.
* **Event Type** (10, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of the event (e.g., Meeting, Presentation, etc.).
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (11, number, required): Enter the number of expected attendees.
* **Audio Equipment** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary audio equipment (e.g., Wireless Microphone, Lavalier Microphone, etc.).
* **Video Equipment** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary video equipment (e.g., LCD Projector, Large Screen TV, etc.).
* **Display Equipment** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary display equipment (e.g., Whiteboard, Easel, etc.).
* **Computer Equipment** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary computer equipment (e.g., Desktop Computer, Laptop, etc.).
* **Connectivity Equipment** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary connectivity equipment (e.g., HDMI Cables, etc.).
* **Lighting Equipment** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select the necessary lighting equipment (e.g., Stage Lighting, Spotlights, etc.).
* **Furniture Needed** (18, select_multiple, optional): Select the additional furniture required (e.g., Additional Chairs, Tables, etc.).
* **Technical Support Required** (19, select_one, required): Choose if technical support is required.
* **Setup Instructions** (20, text, optional): Provide any special setup requirements or instructions.
* **Delivery Location Details** (21, text, required): Specify the room number and floor where the equipment is to be delivered.
* **On-Site Contact Person** (22, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for receiving the equipment.
* **Budget Code or Cost Center** (23, text, optional): Enter the budget code or cost center for billing purposes.
* **Supervisor Name** (24, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor or manager approving the request.
* **Special Notes** (25, text, optional): Provide any other information that may be necessary.
* **I Accept Equipment Usage Terms** (26, select_one, required): Agree to accept the equipment usage terms.

**Tips**

* Ensure all information is accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* Review and edit as necessary to avoid errors and ensure a smooth delivery process.
* If unsure about any field, contact your department or team for guidance.
* Be mindful of deadlines for equipment pickup and return to avoid late fees or penalties.
