# voter_registration_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The voter registration records release form is used to request the release of voter registration records from our office. This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations requesting access to these records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you are an individual or an organization.
2. Fill in your name.
3. Enter your address.
4. Choose your zip code from the provided options.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. (Optional) Enter your fax number.
9. (Optional) Add any additional comments about your request.
10. Click the "submit" button to complete and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual or Organization** (Individual or Organization, select_one, required):
This field asks you to select whether you are an individual or an organization requesting access to the voter registration records. Select "Individual" if you are a citizen requesting access to your own records, or "Organization" if you are representing a group or business seeking records.
* **Name** (name, text, required):
This field asks you to enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address** (address, text, required):
This field asks you to enter your current address as it appears on your identification.
* **Zip Code** (zip_code, select_multiple, required):
This field asks you to select your zip code from the provided options. Make sure to choose the correct zip code that corresponds to your address.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required):
This field asks you to enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (email, email, required):
This field asks you to enter your email address where you can be contacted about your request.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required):
This field asks you to enter your phone number where you can be reached about your request.
* **Fax** (fax, text, optional):
This field is for entering your fax number if you have one.
* **Comments** (comments, note, optional):
This field is for adding any additional comments or context to your request.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required):
This field is for submitting your request after filling out all other fields.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
* Be accurate when entering your information to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you are representing an organization, you may need to provide additional documentation to support your request.
