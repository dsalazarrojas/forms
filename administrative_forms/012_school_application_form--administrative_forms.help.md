# School Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and process applications from students or their representatives for enrollment, admission, or other administrative purposes in a school or educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the requested information, making sure to include all required fields (if any).
- Review and ensure the accuracy of the provided details before submission.
- If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the school or institution for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (id: 1, type: text, required: false): A field for the user to input their name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (id: 2, type: select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select "Student", "Parent", or "Guardian" to indicate your relationship with the applicant.
* **Academic History** (id: 3, type: text, required: false): A field for the applicant's academic history, including any relevant education or academic achievements.
* **School Name** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): The name of the school or institution the applicant is applying to.
* **Application Date** (id: 5, type: date, required: false): The date the application was submitted.
* **School Level** (id: 6, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the level of the school the applicant is applying to (Elementary, Middle School, High School).
* **Student ID** (id: 7, type: number, required: false): A unique identifier for the student or applicant.
* **Address** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): The address where the student can be contacted.
* **Email** (id: 9, type: email, required: false): The email address of the student or representative.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 10, type: text, required: false): The phone number of the student or representative.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of all provided details before submission.
- If you need to update any information after submission, contact the school or institution directly.
