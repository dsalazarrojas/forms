# factory accident incident form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The factory accident incident form is used to report incidents that occurred in a factory setting, such as injuries or illnesses sustained by employees. This form is essential for ensuring that proper measures are taken to prevent similar incidents in the future and to provide support to affected employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   For fields with options, select the relevant option that best describes the incident.
*   If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accident Details** (`Accident Details`, text, required): Describe the incident in detail, including what happened, where it occurred, and any other relevant information.
* **Employee Injured** (`Employee Injured`, select\_one, required): Indicate if an employee was injured during the incident. Options: Yes, No.
* **Incident Description** (`Incident Description`, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant circumstances or conditions.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`Incident Location`, text, required): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Reported By** (`Reported By`, select\_multiple, required): Indicate who reported the incident. Options: Yes, No.
* **Incident Cause** (`Incident Cause`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the reason or cause of the incident.
* **Injuries** (`Injuries`, text, optional): Describe any injuries sustained during the incident.
* **Illnesses** (`Illnesses`, text, optional): Describe any illnesses sustained during the incident.
* **Damage** (`Damage`, text, optional): Describe any property damage caused by the incident.
* **Property Damage** (`Property Damage`, text, optional): Describe any property damage caused by the incident.
* **Investigation** (`Investigation`, select\_one, required): Indicate if an investigation has been conducted into the incident. Options: Yes, No.
* **Outcome** (`Outcome`, select\_one, required): Indicate the outcome of the incident. Options: Yes, No.
* **Follow-up** (`Follow-up`, select\_multiple, required): Indicate what follow-up action is required for the incident. Options: Yes, No.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Attachments** (`Attachments`, select\_multiple, optional): Attach any relevant documents or evidence to the incident report.
* **Other Details** (`Other Details`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details about the incident.
* **Reported By Email** (`Reported By Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Phone** (`Reported By Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Other** (`Reported By Other`, text, optional): Enter any other information about the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Other Other** (`Reported By Other Other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Other Details** (`Reported By Other Details`, text, optional): Enter any other details about the person who reported the incident.
* **Reported By Phone Number** (`Reported By Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who reported the incident.
* **Illnesses Injured** (`Illnesses Injured`, text, optional): Describe any illnesses sustained during the incident.
