# Travel Agent Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agent Offboarding Form is used to document the details of a travel agent's departure from the company. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the offboarding process is completed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the correct permissions to access this form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including the contact information and departure date.
3. Select the reason for leaving and any applicable asset details.
4. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information (`contact_information`, text, optional)
* Enter the travel agent's contact information, including their name and any other relevant details.

### 2. Departure Date (`departure_date`, date, required)
* Enter the date the travel agent will be departing the company.

### 3. Reason for Leaving (`reason_for_leaving`, select_one, required)
* Select the reason the travel agent is leaving from the provided options.

### 4. Notice Period (`notice_period`, number, optional)
* Enter the notice period provided to the travel agent (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 5. Notice Start Date (`notice_start_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the start date of the notice period (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 6. Notice End Date (`notice_end_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the end date of the notice period (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 7. Company Termination Date (`company_termination_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date the travel agent's employment with the company officially ended (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 8. Company Payout Date (`company_payout_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date the travel agent received their payout (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 9. Asset Return (`asset_return`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the travel agent has returned all company assets (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 10. Asset Status (`asset_status`, select_multiple, required)
* Select the current status of the asset, e.g., Active or Inactive.

### 11. Asset Condition (`asset_condition`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check the condition of the asset, e.g., working or not working.

### 12. Asset Value (`asset_value`, number, optional)
* Enter the current value of the asset (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 13. Asset Serial Number (`asset_serial_number`, text, required)
* Enter the serial number of the asset.

### 14. Asset Brand Model (`asset_brand_model`, text, optional)
* Enter the brand and model of the asset (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 15. Asset Purchase Date (`asset_purchase_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the date the asset was purchased (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 16. Asset Purchased From (`asset_purchased_from`, text, optional)
* Enter the vendor or supplier where the asset was purchased from (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 17. Asset Purchase Price (`asset_purchase_price`, number, optional)
* Enter the original purchase price of the asset (optional, but recommended for accurate records).

### 18. Asset Warranty (`asset_warranty`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset still has a valid warranty.

### 19. Asset Maintenance Schedule (`asset_maintenance_schedule`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset is on an active maintenance schedule.

### 20. Asset Maintenance Performed (`asset_maintenance_performed`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset has undergone any maintenance since its purchase.

### 21. Asset Rental or Purchase (`asset_rental_or_purchase`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset was rented or purchased from the company.

### 22. Last Maintenance (`asset_last_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset has undergone its last maintenance.

### 23. Next Maintenance (`asset_next_maintenance`, select_multiple, optional)
* Check if the asset is due for its next maintenance.
