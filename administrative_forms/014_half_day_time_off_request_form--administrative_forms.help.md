# Half Day Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a request for a half day time off.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
2. Select your job title.
3. Choose the date you would like to take a half day off.
4. Enter the start and end times for your half day off.
5. Provide a brief reason for your request.
6. (Optional) Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Check the status of your request, which may be Approved, Declined, or Pending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Job Title** (text): Enter your job title or position.
* **Date Request** (date): Select the date you would like to take a half day off.
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time for your half day off.
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time for your half day off.
* **Reason** (text): Provide a brief explanation for your request.
* **Approval Status** (select_one): This field displays the status of your request. It may be Approved, Declined, or Pending.
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to submit your request accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or supervisor.
* This form is for internal use only; do not share it with external parties.
