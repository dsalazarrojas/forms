# Physical Therapy Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Leave Form is used to document and track physical therapy-related leaves, allowing healthcare professionals to keep track of patient absences and schedule adjustments. This form is typically used for planning purposes only, and its data is not intended for long-term storage. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that patients' needs are met and that their absence is properly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **Name** field. This field is required.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This field is required.
3.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This field is not required but is useful for communication.
4.  Choose the **Date of Absence** when you will be absent. This field is required.
5.  Choose the **Start Date** and **End Date** when your absence will begin and end, respectively. This field is required.
6.  Provide a **Reason for Absence** that will help the healthcare team understand your needs. This field is not required but is recommended.
7.  Add any **Comments** that might be helpful for the healthcare team to know about your absence. This field is not required but can be useful.
8.  Select **Yes/No** to confirm that your information is correct and you're ready to submit your leave request. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name, as it will appear on the form and help the healthcare team address you correctly.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so the healthcare team can contact you if needed.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case the healthcare team needs to reach you quickly.
- **Date of Absence** (`Date of Absence`, date, required): Choose the date you will be absent.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Choose the start date of your absence.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Choose the end date of your absence.
- **Reason for Absence** (`Reason for Absence`, text, optional): Give a brief explanation for your absence.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any extra information you think would be helpful for the healthcare team to know.
- **Submit** (`Submit`, select_multiple, required): Confirm that your information is correct and you're ready to submit your leave request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Check the form for any errors before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare team for assistance.
- This form is for planning purposes only; its data will not be stored for long-term use.
