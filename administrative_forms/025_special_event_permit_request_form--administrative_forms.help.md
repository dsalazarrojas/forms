# Special Event Permit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Event Permit Request Form is used to request a permit for a special event. This form helps us understand the details of your event so we can process your permit request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out all required fields**: All fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required. Please make sure to fill out all of them to ensure we can process your permit request.
2. **Select the correct event type**: Choose the type of event you are holding from the list of options. This will help us understand the nature of your event.
3. **Enter event details**: Fill out the event name, date, start and end times, location, event capacity, and event contact information.
4. **Specify the permit type**: Choose the type of permit you are requesting from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of your event. This will help us identify your event and keep track of it.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): The type of event you are holding. This will help us understand the nature of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): The date of your event. Please format it as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS+TZ.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): The start time of your event. Please format it as HH:MM:SS.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): The end time of your event. Please format it as HH:MM:SS.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): The location of your event. This could be a physical address or a specific venue.
* **Event Capacity** (`event_capacity`, number, required): The number of people that will be attending your event.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): The person in charge of your event. Please include their name.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, email, required): The email address of your event organizer. We will use this to contact them with any questions.
* **Event Contact** (`event_contact`, text, required): The person we can contact for questions or concerns.
* **Event Contact Number** (`event_contact_number`, text, required): The number we can contact for questions or concerns.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, note, required): A brief description of your event. This will help us understand the context.
* **Permit Type** (`permit_type`, select_one, required): The type of permit you are requesting. This will determine what requirements you need to fulfill.
* **Permit Requirements** (`permit_requirements`, text, optional): Any additional information you need to include about your event.
