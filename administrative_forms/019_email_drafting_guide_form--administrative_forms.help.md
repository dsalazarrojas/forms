# email_drafting_guide_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you craft well-structured and comprehensive emails for various recipients and purposes. It guides you through the process of collecting all necessary information to create a professional and polished email.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
2. Start by inputting a clear and concise email subject in the "email subject" field.
3. In the "email body" field, write a detailed and professional email content.
4. Enter the recipient's email address in the "recipient email" field.
5. Provide your own email address in the "sender email" field.
6. If necessary, attach a file by selecting one of the available options (e.g. file, video, audio, image).
7. Choose one or multiple priority levels for the email.
8. Enter the due date for the email in the "due date" field.
9. If applicable, indicate the time available for the email in the "time available" field.
10. Optionally, fill out the sender's name, recipient's name, sender's company, recipient's company, sender's department, recipient's department, sender's location, and recipient's location fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **email subject** (required): Enter a brief and descriptive subject that will grab the recipient's attention.
* **email body** (required): Write a detailed and professional email content that includes all relevant information.
* **recipient email** (required): Enter the recipient's email address to whom the email will be sent.
* **sender email** (required): Enter your own email address that will be associated with the email.
* **attachment** (optional): Attach a file, video, audio, or image to the email if necessary.
* **priority** (optional): Select one or multiple priority levels (low, medium, high) for the email.
* **due date** (required): Enter the date by which the email needs to be sent or received.
* **time available** (optional): Indicate the time available for the email.
* **sender name** (optional): Enter your name that will be associated with the email.
* **recipient name** (optional): Enter the recipient's name to whom the email is being sent.
* **sender company** (optional): Enter your company that will be associated with the email.
* **recipient company** (optional): Enter the recipient's company to whom the email is being sent.
* **sender department** (optional): Enter your department that will be associated with the email.
* **recipient department** (optional): Enter the recipient's department to whom the email is being sent.
* **sender location** (optional): Enter your location that will be associated with the email.
* **recipient location** (optional): Enter the recipient's location to whom the email is being sent.
* **sender phone** (optional): Enter your phone number that will be associated with the email.
* **recipient phone** (optional): Enter the recipient's phone number to whom the email is being sent.
