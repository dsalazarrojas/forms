# Safety Training Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Training Discharge Form is used to formally document an employee's completion of required safety training. This form helps to verify that the employee has completed the necessary training programs, ensuring compliance with regulatory and company standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Enter the supervisor's name in the `supervisor_name` field.
3. Select the date on which the employee was discharged from training in the `date_discharged` field.
4. In the `training_topics_completed` field, select all the safety training topics that the employee has completed. These topics include Fire Safety, First Aid, and Hazardous Materials.
5. Enter the date on which the employee completed the training in the `training_date_completed` field.
6. Enter the safety training certificate number, if applicable, in the `certificate_number` field.
7. If applicable, enter the name of the person or department that the training was assigned to in the `assigned_to` field.
8. Enter the date on which the training certificate was received in the `date_received` field.
9. Finally, enter the date on which the form was reviewed and verified in the `date_reviewed` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the employee's name who has completed the safety training.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the supervisor who is signing off on the employee's training completion.
* **Date Discharged** (`date_discharged`, date, required/optional): Select the date on which the employee was discharged from training.
* **Training Topics Completed** (`training_topics_completed`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the safety training topics that the employee has completed. Options include Fire Safety, First Aid, and Hazardous Materials.
* **Training Date Completed** (`training_date_completed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the employee completed the training.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the safety training certificate number, if applicable.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required/optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person or department that the training was assigned to.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the training certificate was received.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which this form was reviewed and verified.

## Tips

* Ensure that the employee has actually completed the required safety training before filling out this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is entered.
