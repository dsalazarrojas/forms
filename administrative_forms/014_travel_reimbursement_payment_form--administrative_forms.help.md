# travel_reimbursement_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The travel_reimbursement_payment_form is used for submitting travel requests and associated expenses. This form helps employees and managers keep track of travel-related expenses, ensuring accurate and efficient reimbursement processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide accurate details about your travel request and associated expenses.
2.  Review your submission before submitting it, ensuring that all fields are completed correctly.
3.  Submit the form to the travel department or designated approver for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **travel_request** (`travel_request`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your travel request.
*   **employee_requesting** (`employee_requesting`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee requesting travel reimbursement.
*   **employee_id** (`employee_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
*   **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of your travel.
*   **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your travel.
*   **destination** (`destination`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the destination of your travel.
*   **expense_description** (`expense_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of your expense.
*   **total_expense_amount** (`total_expense_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total amount of your expense.
*   **expense_submitted_on** (`expense_submitted_on`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your expense was submitted.
*   **expense_approved_on** (`expense_approved_on`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your expense was approved.
*   **travel_agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the travel agency you used.
*   **travel_agency_contact** (`travel_agency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name at the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_phone** (`travel_agency_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_email** (`travel_agency_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_address** (`travel_agency_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_notes** (`travel_agency_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the travel agency.
*   **expense_receipts** (`expense_receipts`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the receipts associated with your expense.
*   **expense_reimbursed** (`expense_reimbursed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether your expense has been reimbursed.
*   **travel_reimbursement_status** (`travel_reimbursement_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the status of your travel reimbursement.
*   **travel_request_id** (`travel_request_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID number of your travel request.
*   **expense_type** (`expense_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of your expense (Travel or Other).
*   **travel_agency_fax** (`travel_agency_fax`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_website** (`travel_agency_website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of the travel agency.
*   **expense_description2** (`expense_description2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of your expense.
*   **notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your travel request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to attach receipts for your expenses and keep them up-to-date.
*   Review and update your submission as needed before finalizing it.
