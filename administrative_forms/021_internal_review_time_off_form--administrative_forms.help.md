# Internal Review Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Internal Review Time Off Form" is used by employees to submit a formal request to take time off from work. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name.
2. Enter your Job Title.
3. Select your Department.
4. Choose the Request Date you would like to take time off.
5. Choose the Start Date of your time off.
6. Choose the End Date of your time off.
7. Enter the Reason for your time off.
8. Select the Duration of your time off (in days).
9. Select who will Approve your time off request (e.g. John Doe, Jane Doe, or Bob Smith).
10. If desired, enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to take time off.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of your time off.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of your time off.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief description of why you are requesting time off.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days you would like to take off.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one, required): Select who will approve your time off request.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or context for your time off request, enter it here.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any potential issues with payroll or HR.
- Be respectful of your colleagues and make sure to request time off during non-critical periods.
- Submitting this form will not guarantee approval of your time off request; your supervisor or HR representative will review and process your request accordingly.
