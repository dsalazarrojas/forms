# Systems Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Systems Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to collect information about the evaluation of systems. It is intended to gather feedback and scores from various stakeholders, including project managers and IT teams, about the performance and quality of systems. This form is used to assess the systems' strengths and weaknesses, providing a comprehensive overview for decision-making and improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant pages (e.g., Project Managers, IT Teams) and provide their names in the text fields provided.
2. Choose a date within the last 30 days for the evaluation date.
3. Select a time for the evaluation.
4. Provide a score between 1 and 10 for each evaluation score field.
5. Enter your evaluation comments or feedback in the "Evaluation Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Managers (1)**: Enter the names of all project managers involved in the system evaluation. This could include individuals responsible for overseeing the project or team leaders.
* **IT Teams (2)**: Enter the names of all IT teams involved in the system evaluation, such as teams responsible for implementing or maintaining the system.
* **Evaluation Date (3)**: Select a date within the last 30 days that the system was evaluated. This helps track when the evaluation occurred and provides a clear timeline for reference.
* **Evaluation Time (4)**: Choose the time of day when the evaluation took place. This helps identify any time-sensitive or time-dependent factors.
* **Evaluation Score (5)**: Provide a score between 1 and 10 to indicate your evaluation of the system. The higher the score, the better the system performed. 
* **Evaluation Comments (6)**: Enter any additional feedback or comments about the system's performance. This is a free-text field for any observations or suggestions you may have.
* **Evaluation Score 2 (7)**: Provide a second score between 1 and 10, similar to the first evaluation score, to further quantify your evaluation.
