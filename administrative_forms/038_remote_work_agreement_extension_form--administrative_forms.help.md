# remote_work_agreement_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "remote_work_agreement_extension_form" is used to request an extension to a remote work agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as needed, and provide a valid reason for the extension request.
2. Select the remote work arrangement that you would like to extend from the options provided.
3. Choose the approval status of your request from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Work Agreement** (`remote_work_agreement`, text, required): This field is where you can provide a valid reason for your extension request.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, number, required): Enter the term of the original agreement.
* **Extension Request** (`extension_request`, text, required): This field is where you can provide a valid reason for the extension request.
* **Remote Work Arrangement** (`remote_work_arrangement`, select_multiple, required): Select the remote work arrangement that you would like to extend from the provided options.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the original agreement.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the review date of the original agreement.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, required): This field is for the manager to provide comments on your extension request.
* **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, required): This field is for you to provide any additional comments on your extension request.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Choose the approval status of your request from the provided options.
