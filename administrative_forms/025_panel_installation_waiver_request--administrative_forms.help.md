# Panel Installation Waiver Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panel Installation Waiver Request form is used to obtain a waiver for a panel installation, which includes the terms and conditions that the client and panel inspector must agree upon before the installation begins.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name and contact information.
2. Select the type of panel being installed (Panel, Other, or None).
3. Acknowledge the risks involved with the installation.
4. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the waiver.
5. Provide the date and time of the installation.
6. Enter the contact information of the client and panel inspector.
7. Sign and date the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Name (`client_name`)

*   This field is for the client's name and should be filled out accurately.

### Panel Type (`panel_type`)

*   Select the type of panel being installed (Panel, Other, or None) as per the options provided.

### Risk Acknowledgement (`risk_acknowledgement`)

*   Read and acknowledge the risks involved with the installation.

### Terms and Conditions (`terms_and_conditions`)

*   This field is for the client and panel inspector to read and agree to the terms and conditions of the waiver.

### Date Installed (`date_installed`)

*   Enter the date of the panel installation.

### Time Installed (`time_installed`)

*   Enter the time of the panel installation.

### Contact Information (`contact_information`)

*   This field is optional and may be used to provide additional contact information for the client.

### Signature (`signature`)

*   Sign the waiver to confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.

### Email (`email`)

*   Enter the client's email address.

### Phone (`phone`)

*   Enter the client's phone number.

### Client Address (`client_address`)

*   Enter the client's address.

### Waiver Terms (`waiver_terms`)

*   This field is for the client to read and agree to the waiver terms.

### Acknowledgement (`acknowledgement`)

*   This field is optional, but it may be used for additional acknowledgement from the client.

### Panel Inspector (`panel_inspector`)

*   Enter the panel inspector's name.

### Panel Inspector Signature (`panel_inspector_signature`)

*   Sign as the panel inspector to confirm agreement to the terms and conditions.

### Waiver Terms Acknowledgement (`waiver_terms_acknowledgement`)

*   This field is optional, but it may be used for additional acknowledgement from the client.

### Date Waiver Signed (`date_waiver_signed`)

*   Enter the date the client signed the waiver.

### Time Waiver Signed (`time_waiver_signed`)

*   Enter the time the client signed the waiver.

### Panel Inspector Date (`panel_inspector_date`)

*   Enter the date the panel inspector signed the waiver.

### Panel Inspector Time (`panel_inspector_time`)

*   Enter the time the panel inspector signed the waiver.

### Client Inspector Date (`client_inspector_date`)

*   Enter the date the panel inspector signed the waiver.

### Client Inspector Time (`client_inspector_time`)

*   Enter the time the panel inspector signed the waiver.

### Waiver Terms Agreement (`waiver_terms_agreement`)

*   This field is for the client and panel inspector to read and agree to the waiver terms.

### Date Agreed (`date_agreed`)

*   Enter the date the client and panel inspector agreed to the waiver terms.

### Additional Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to avoid any disputes or issues related to the waiver.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the panel inspector or the client for clarification.
*   The panel inspector will review and sign the waiver after verifying the installation details.
