**Maintenance Cost Report Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about maintenance costs for a given period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate to the form and click on the "Maintenance Cost Report" page.
2. Enter or select the necessary information in each field according to your needs.
3. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Maintenance Cost Report (Page 1)
* **Maintenance Cost Report** (`maintenance_cost_page`, text, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you can enter a brief description of the maintenance cost report.
### Date (Page 2)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date for which you are submitting the maintenance cost report.
### Cost Amount (Page 3)
* **Cost Amount** (`cost_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the total cost amount for the maintenance.
### Cost Frequency (Page 4)
* **Cost Frequency** (`cost_frequency`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the frequency at which the cost occurred (e.g., one-time, recurring).
* Options: 'Yes', 'No'
### Notes (Page 5)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments about the maintenance cost report.

## Tips
* Please make sure to include as much detail as possible in the "Maintenance Cost Report" field.
* Use the "Cost Frequency" field to accurately report the frequency of the maintenance cost.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
