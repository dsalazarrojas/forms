# Travel Expense Pre-Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Expense Pre-Approval Request Form is used to request pre-approval for travel expenses related to work. This form helps to ensure that all necessary steps are taken before incurring travel costs, and it serves as a record of the approved expenses for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form by providing the required information for each field.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.
3.  Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 (Page 1)
*   **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter a brief title to describe the purpose of the pre-approval request.

### Business Trip Information (Page 2)
*   **Business Trip Information** (`business_trip_information`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the trip, including the purpose, destination, and dates.

### Trip Details (Page 3)
*   **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional trip-related information that is relevant to the pre-approval request.

## Tips
*   Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
*   Attach supporting documents as necessary to support the pre-approval request.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required information is included.
