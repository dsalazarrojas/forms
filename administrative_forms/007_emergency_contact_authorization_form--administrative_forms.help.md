# Emergency Contact Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Contact Authorization Form is designed to collect information about the owner's emergency contacts, allowing them to provide essential contact details in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Contact Name" field with the full name of the contact person.
2. Select the "Relationship to Owner" field to indicate the nature of the relationship between the contact person and the owner.
3. Provide the contact person's address on the "Contact Address 1", "Contact City", "Contact State", and "Contact Zip" fields.
4. Enter the contact person's phone number on the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter the contact person's email address on the "Contact Email" field.
6. Select the type of relationship between the contact person and the owner in the "Relationship Type" field.
7. Repeat the process for any additional emergency contacts by scrolling down to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the contact person.
* **Relationship to Owner** (`relationship_to_owner`, select_one, optional): Select the nature of the relationship between the contact person and the owner, such as Primary Contact or Secondary Contact.
* **Contact Address 1** (`contact_address_line1`, text, optional): Enter the first line of the contact person's address.
* **Contact Address 2** (`contact_address_line2`, text, optional): Enter the second line of the contact person's address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the contact person's address.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the contact person's address.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the contact person's address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Relationship Type** (`contact_relationship_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of relationship between the contact person and the owner, such as Friend, Family, or Colleague.
* **Owner Relationship Type** (`owner_relationship_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of relationship between the contact person and the owner, such as Friend, Family, or Colleague.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If the contact person has a relationship with the owner that is not listed, select "Other" and describe the relationship in the field provided.
* If you're filling out this form for someone else, please ask them for their consent and ensure they are aware of the information being collected.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help from the relevant authorities or the owner themselves.
