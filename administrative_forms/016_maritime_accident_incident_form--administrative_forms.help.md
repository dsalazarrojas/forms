# Maritime Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about maritime accidents or incidents that occur on or near the water. Its purpose is to gather details about the incident, allowing for efficient reporting and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing as much detail as possible about the incident.
3. Save your progress if needed, as the form can be completed in stages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: This section captures the main incident details. Please enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant information about what happened.
* **Reporting Party**: Select the party responsible for reporting the incident. This helps track the source of the report.
	+ Option 1: This party is typically the person who witnessed the incident or has first-hand knowledge.
	+ Option 2: This party is often a crew member or shore-based personnel who has witnessed the incident.
	+ Option 3: This party may be a representative from another organization or authority.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the exact address or nearest location where the incident occurred. Be as precise as possible.
* **Reporting Date**: Select the date when the incident occurred.
* **Reporting Time**: Select the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: This section requires a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Type of Incident**: Select the type of incident that occurred. This helps categorize and analyze the report.
	+ Option A: This is often used for minor incidents.
	+ Option B: This is typically used for moderate incidents.
	+ Option C: This is usually used for severe or catastrophic incidents.
* **Damage Type**: Select the type of damage caused by the incident. This helps assess the severity of the incident.
	+ Option Minor: This is often used for minor damage.
	+ Option Major: This is typically used for significant damage.
	+ Option Catastrophic: This is usually used for extreme damage.
* **Damage Description**: This section requires a detailed description of the damage caused by the incident.
* **Damage Amount**: Enter the estimated damage amount, if applicable. This helps with financial analysis and assessment.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the exact address or nearest location where the incident occurred. Be as precise as possible.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing information about the incident.
* Save your progress if needed, as the form can be completed in stages.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your incident report guidelines or consult with a supervisor/ authority figure.
