# Fire Alarm Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about fire alarm systems in a facility. The form is divided into several sections, each focused on specific aspects of the fire alarm system, such as location, fire alarm system type, and inspector's comments. By completing this form, you will be able to document and record important information about the fire alarm system, which will help to identify any potential issues or problems with the system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of fire alarm system**:
   Fill out the "Fire Alarm System" field by selecting the correct type of system from the available options.
2. **Enter the system location**:
   Type in the exact location of the fire alarm system, including room and suite numbers.
3. **Choose locations that the fire alarm system serves**:
   Select all locations that the fire alarm system serves, such as floors, wings, or areas of the building.
4. **Enter inspector's comments**:
   Write a brief description of any issues or problems you found during the inspection.
5. **Enter fire officer details**:
   Fill out the "Fire Officer" field with the name and title of the fire officer in charge of the area.
6. **Enter your name and signature**:
   Fill out your name and signature in the "Inspector Name" field.
7. **Enter date and time of the inspection**:
   Enter the date and time the inspection was completed in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. **Enter time zone**:
   Enter the time zone where the facility is located.
9. **Enter your contact information**:
   Fill out your name, address, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Fire Alarm System (Fire Alarm System)

*   **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`, Select One, Required): Select the type of fire alarm system from the available options.

### System Location (System Location)

*   **System Location** (`system_location`, Text, Required): Enter the exact location of the fire alarm system, including room and suite numbers.

### Locations Served (Location)

*   **Locations Served** (`location`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all locations that the fire alarm system serves, such as floors, wings, or areas of the building.

### Inspector's Comments (Inspectors Comments)

*   **Inspector's Comments** (`inspectors_comments`, Note, Required): Write a brief description of any issues or problems you found during the inspection.

### Fire Officer (Fire Officer)

*   **Fire Officer** (`fire_officer`, Text, Required): Fill out the "Fire Officer" field with the name and title of the fire officer in charge of the area.

### Inspector Name (Inspector Name)

*   **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, Text, Required): Fill out your name in the "Inspector Name" field and sign above your name.

### Date of Inspection (Date)

*   **Date of Inspection** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the inspection was completed.

### Time of Inspection (Time)

*   **Time of Inspection** (`time`, Time, Required): Enter the time the inspection was completed.

### Time Zone (Time Zone)

*   **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, Text, Required): Enter the time zone where the facility is located.

### Inspector Signature (Inspector Signature)

*   **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, Text, Required): Fill out your name and sign above your name.

### Company Name (Company Name)

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Required): Enter your company name.

### Address (Address)

*   **Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your company address.

### City (City)

*   **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter your city.

### State (State)

*   **State** (`state`, Text, Optional): Enter your state.

### Zip (Zip)

*   **Zip** (`zip`, Text, Required): Enter your zip code.

### Contact Information (Phone)

*   **Contact Information** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.

### Contact Information (Email)

*   **Contact Information** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
