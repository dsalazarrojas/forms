# Evacuation Alert Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evacuation Alert Communication Form is used to report and communicate emergencies, drills, or tests that require evacuation of people from a specific location. This form helps you to quickly provide essential information to the relevant authorities in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of emergency (Emergency, Drill, or Test) from the options provided.
2. Enter a brief description of the incident.
3. Provide the location of the incident.
4. Enter the number of people involved in the incident.
5. Enter the number of people evacuated.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for contact (this will help in case further communication is required).
7. Optionally, enter any important evacuation messages if needed.
8. Finally, provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Emergency Type** (`emergency_type`, select, required: false)
Choose the type of emergency that occurred. Select from the options: Emergency, Drill, or Test.

### 2. **incident_description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of what happened.

### 3. **Location** (`location`, text, required: false)
Enter the address or location where the emergency occurred.

### 4. **number_of_people_involved** (`number_of_people_involved`, number, required: false)
Enter the number of people involved in the incident.

### 5. **number_of_people_evacuated** (`number_of_people_evacuated`, number, required: false)
Enter the number of people evacuated from the location.

### 6. **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person responsible for contact.

### 7. **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of the person responsible for contact.

### 8. **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter the email of the person responsible for contact.

### 9. **evacuation_message** (`evacuation_message`, text, required: false)
Enter any important message or instructions related to the evacuation.

### 10. **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false)
Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
