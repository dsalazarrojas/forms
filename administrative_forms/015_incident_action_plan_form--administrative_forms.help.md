# Incident Action Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Action Plan Form is a document that helps in documenting and reporting incidents within the organization. It is used to gather essential information about the incident, including the category, description, date and time of the incident, location, and the incident's title.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the category of the incident from the provided options: Administrative Forms, Other.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
3. Enter the date and time when the incident began (in the YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM:SS format respectively).
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred.
5. Enter a unique number for the incident, if available.
6. Add a title for the incident.
7. Click on the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category (Administrative Forms / Other)** (`category`, select_one, required/false): Select the category of the incident from the available options. If the incident is not related to administrative tasks, select "Other".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/false): Provide a detailed description of the incident. Be as accurate and thorough as possible.
* **Date Incident Began** (`date_incident_began`, date, required/false): Enter the date when the incident began in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time Incident Started** (`time_incident_started`, time, required/false): Enter the time when the incident began in the HH:MM:SS format.
* **Location Incident Occurred** (`location_incident_occurred`, text, required/false): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Number** (`incident_number`, text, required/false): Enter a unique number for the incident, if available.
* **Incident Title** (`incident_title`, text, required/false): Add a title for the incident.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the provided information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's better to ask for clarification or advice from a supervisor or colleague before submitting the form.
* Make sure to save or print the form for future reference, especially if follow-up actions are necessary.
