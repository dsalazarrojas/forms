# Construction Project Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from requestors who want to access construction project records. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide the necessary details to access the records you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and company name if you are requesting records on behalf of a company.
2. Enter the project reference number or identifier.
3. Select the types of records you need access to (e.g. Contracts, Drawings, Inspection reports, Invoices, or Correspondence).
4. Specify the purpose of your request and why you need to access the records.
5. Choose how you would like to receive the records (if applicable).
6. Sign your name to authorize the release of the records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (Requestor name, text, required): This is your name as the requestor. Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Company Name** (Company name, text, optional): If you are requesting records on behalf of a company, please enter the company name.
* **Project Reference** (Project reference, text, required): This is the identifier for the project records you are requesting. Please enter the project reference number or name.
* **Records Requested** (Select multiple, text, required): Select all the types of records you need access to:
	+ Contracts
	+ Drawings
	+ Inspection reports
	+ Invoices
	+ Correspondence
* **Purpose of Release** (Text, optional): This is the reason why you need to access the records. Be concise and clear about your purpose.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (Select one, text, optional): If you have a preferred way to receive the records, please select from:
	+ Digital download
	+ Email
	+ Physical copies
* **Authorization Signature Name** (Authorization signature name, text, required): This is the name of the person authorizing the release of the records. Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
