# Temporary Staffing Authorization Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request temporary staffing for an employee to work on a specific job title during a given period. The form should only be used for requests that are not part of the regular workflow or staffing plan. Requests for temporary staffing should be made in good faith, and the form must be completed with accurate and relevant information to ensure proper approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by entering the job title of the position for which you are requesting temporary staffing.
2. Enter the duration of the job, in days or weeks, for which you are requesting temporary staffing.
3. Provide the start date of the temporary staffing assignment.
4. Enter the end date of the temporary staffing assignment.
5. List the required skills or qualifications for the temporary assignment.
6. Select the type of job you are requesting: Temporary Staffing, Contract, or Permanent.
7. Finally, enter the name of the person making the staffing request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the job title for which you are requesting temporary staffing. This should match the title of the position found in the company's organizational structure.
* **Job Duration** (`job_duration`): Enter the number of days or weeks for which you are requesting temporary staffing. If the job duration is not a whole number, please enter a decimal value (e.g., 1.5 weeks).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date the temporary staffing assignment will begin. Use the format: Month-Day-Year (e.g., 01-15-2023).
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the date the temporary staffing assignment will end. Use the format: Month-Day-Year (e.g., 01-31-2024).
* **Required Skills** (`required_skills`): List the required skills or qualifications for the temporary assignment. Please separate each skill with a comma.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Select the type of job you are requesting: Temporary Staffing, Contract, or Permanent.
* **Staffing Requestor** (`staffing_requestor`): Enter the name of the person making the staffing request.
