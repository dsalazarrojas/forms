# Branded Merchandise Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Branded Merchandise Requisition Form is used for requesting branded merchandise for internal and external events, campaigns, and other purposes. This form helps to gather necessary information from the requester, ensuring that the requested items are properly coordinated and delivered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and department in the respective fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the items you are requesting, including size and color.
3. Specify the quantity of items required.
4. Optional: indicate the purpose for the merchandise (e.g., event or campaign).
5. Set the required delivery deadline.
6. Indicate the current budget approval status.
7. Enter the shipping address where the merchandise should be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name (Requester name)** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further clarification or questions.
* **Department (Department)** (`department`, text, required): Select your department that will be receiving the merchandise.
* **Item description (Item description)** (`item_description`, text, required): Describe the product or merchandise you are requesting, including size and color.
* **Quantity requested (Quantity requested)** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of units you require.
* **Event or campaign (Event or campaign)** (`event_or_campaign`, text, optional): If the merchandise is for an event or campaign, provide more information about its purpose.
* **Delivery deadline (Delivery deadline)** (`delivery_deadline`, date, optional): Specify the date when you need to receive the merchandise.
* **Budget approval status (Budget approval status)** (`budget_approval`, select_one, required): Choose whether budget has been approved, pending, or denied.
* **Shipping address (Shipping address)** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter the address where the merchandise should be delivered.
