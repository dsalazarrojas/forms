# seasonal-travel-time-off-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "seasonal-travel-time-off-form" is a form used by employees to request travel time off from work during the seasonal period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely, providing all necessary information.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_general** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-general`, text, required: false): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your travel plans.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_destination** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-destination`, text, required: false): Enter the destination of your travel.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_date_from** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-date-from`, date, required: false): Select the starting date of your travel.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_date_to** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-date-to`, date, required: false): Select the ending date of your travel.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_duration** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-duration`, number, required: false): If your travel duration is more than 5 days, please indicate the exact duration. Otherwise, you can select "Less than 5 days."
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_approvals** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-approvals`, select_one, required: false): Select all necessary approvals for your travel.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_manager_approvals** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-manager-approvals`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all managers that need to approve your travel.
*   **seasonal_travel_time_off_form_employees** (`seasonal-travel-time-off-form-employees`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all employees that are traveling with you.
 
## Tips
*   Be sure to fill out all fields completely and accurately.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR or manager for clarification.
