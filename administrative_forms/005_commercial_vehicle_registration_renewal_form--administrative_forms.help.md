# Commercial Vehicle Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update the registration of a commercial vehicle, ensuring it remains active and compliant with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your registrant name and title.
2. If your vehicle is registered under a business, enter the business name.
3. Provide the current registration number of the vehicle.
4. Enter the make and model of the vehicle, including the year if possible.
5. Enter the full Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) without any spaces.
6. Select the renewal term from 6 months, 12 months, or 24 months.
7. Choose your preferred payment method for the renewal.
8. Confirm whether the vehicle has active commercial insurance.
9. Enter your contact email address where you want to receive renewal confirmation and any follow-up communication.
10. Provide any additional notes or special instructions if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant name** (`registrant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the registrant or company that owns the vehicle.
* **Business name** (`business_name`, `text`, optional): If the vehicle is registered under a business, enter the business name here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Current registration number** (`current_registration_number`, `text`, required): Enter the exact registration number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle make and model** (`vehicle_make_model`, `text`, required): Enter the make and model of the vehicle, including the year if possible.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vehicle_vin`, `text`, required): Enter the full Vehicle Identification Number without spaces.
* **Renewal term** (`renewal_term`, `select_one`, required): Select the renewal term in 6, 12, or 24 months.
* **Preferred payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to pay for the renewal: Credit card, Bank transfer, Check, or Online payment portal.
* **Insurance on file confirmed?** (`insurance_confirmed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether the vehicle has active commercial insurance.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address where you will receive renewal confirmation and any follow-up communication.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional notes or special instructions to assist with the renewal process.

## Tips
* Fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your contact for further assistance.
* Double-check your preferred payment method and insurance confirmation before submitting the form.
