# Academic Transcript Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Transcript Request Form is used to request your academic transcript. This form requires you to provide personal details, contact information, and preferences for transcript delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name while enrolled, student ID number, and date of birth.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the type of transcript you require (Official or Unofficial).
4. Specify the number of copies you need.
5. Choose your enrollment status.
6. Enter the year of graduation or last attendance.
7. Select your preferred delivery method.
8. If applicable, provide the recipient's address if you are not picking up the transcript in person.
9. Confirm your authorization to release your transcript to the specified recipient.
10. Sign the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### -- Personal Details --

* **Full Name while Enrolled**: Enter your full name as it was at the time of enrollment.
* **Student ID Number**: Your unique university identifier.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for identity verification purposes.

### -- Contact Information --

* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can send updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for questions and updates.

### -- Transcript Details --

* **Type of Transcript**: Select between Official (Sealed) and Unofficial (Student Copy).
* **Number of Copies**: Enter the number of transcripts you require.
* **Enrollment Status**: Choose your current status with the institution.
* **Year of Graduation or Last Attendance**: Enter the year of graduation or last attendance.

### -- Delivery Options --

* **Delivery Method**: Choose between Pick-up in Person, Standard Mail, Express Mail, and Electronic (Secure PDF).
* **Recipient Address**: If you're not picking up the transcript in person, provide the full address including postal code.

### -- Authorization --

* **I Authorize the Release of My Academic Transcript**: Confirm your authorization to release your transcript to the specified recipient.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full legal name for electronic signature purposes.

Please review and fill out this form carefully. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
