# intern-information-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "intern-information-form" is an administrative form used to collect information about an intern's details for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your Phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
5. Select your Start Date and End Date using the calendar input field.
6. Indicate your Department(s) selection in the field labeled "Department".
7. Indicate your Role in the field labeled "Role".
8. Enter your Job Title in the field labeled "Job Title".
9. Enter your Location in the field labeled "Location".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format "123-456-7890".
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Select your start date using the calendar input field.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Select your end date using the calendar input field.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all departments that apply to you, using the multiple selection field.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your role using the single selection field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter your location.
* **Job Title Alt** (`job_title_alt`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional job title information.
