# library_study_room_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The library study room reservation form is designed for library staff to manage study room reservations. This form allows staff to collect and store information about users' study room reservations, ensuring accurate and efficient management of study room bookings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name
2. Select the start and end dates of your reservation
3. Choose the start and end times of your reservation
4. Select the study room(s) you wish to reserve
5. Optional: Enter any additional details about your reservation (if needed)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **proctors** (text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or library card.
- **name** (text, optional): Enter your full name.
- **start_date** (date, optional): Select the start date of your reservation.
- **end_date** (date, optional): Select the end date of your reservation.
- **study_room** (select multiple, optional): Choose the study room(s) you wish to reserve (Room 1, Room 2, Room 3).
- **end_time** (time, optional): Select the end time of your reservation.
- **start_time** (time, optional): Select the start time of your reservation.
- **room_booking_duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of your booking (in hours).
- **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional details about your reservation (if needed).
- **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes (if needed).

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact library staff for assistance.
- If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, please contact library staff directly.
- This form is intended for internal use, so please do not share it with external users.
