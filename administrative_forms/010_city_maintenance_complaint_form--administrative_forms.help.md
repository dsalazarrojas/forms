# City Maintenance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The City Maintenance Complaint Form is designed to provide a convenient way for the public to report any issues or concerns about city maintenance. The goal is to inform city maintenance teams about areas that need attention and ensure that necessary repairs are made in a timely and efficient manner.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your reporter name and contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
2. Provide a location description where the issue is occurring.
3. Select the type of maintenance issue you are reporting.
4. Write a detailed description of the issue and its urgency level.
5. Choose your preferred contact method for updates on your issue.
6. If necessary, attach any photos or additional information to support your report.
7. Specify if you would like to receive follow-up updates on your issue.
8. Finally, confirm the date of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reporter name**: Enter your name or a pseudonym you'd like to be known by for follow-up purposes.
- **Contact phone**: Provide your phone number for maintenance team members to contact you if needed.
- **Location of issue**: Describe the exact location where the issue is happening, such as street address or a known landmark.
- **Type of maintenance issue**: Select one of the options listed, such as pothole, streetlight outage, or graffiti.
- **Description of issue**: Write a detailed description of the issue, including the urgency level on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
- **Preferred contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted for updates, either via email, phone, or no contact at all.
- **Photo upload instructions**: Attach photos or additional information directly to this form or via email to support your report.
- **Urgency level**: Indicate the level of urgency for your issue, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
- **Request for follow up**: Check yes if you would like to receive updates on the status of your issue.
- **Submission date**: The form will automatically display the current date. Please verify that it is correct before submitting the form.
