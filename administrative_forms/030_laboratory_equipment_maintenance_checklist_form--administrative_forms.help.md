# laboratory_equipment_maintenance_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The laboratory equipment maintenance checklist form is used to track and report on the condition and functionality of various lab equipment. This includes checking for wear and tear, damage, and needed repairs or replacements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review each section and field carefully.
2. For each field, select or enter the correct information as instructed.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1, Field 1 (form_section1_field1)**: Enter any observations or comments about the equipment condition in this field. This is a text entry field where you can report any visual or operational issues with the equipment.
* **Section 1, Field 2 (form_section1_field2)**: Enter any other relevant comments or information about the equipment in this field. Similar to the previous field, this is also a text entry field.
* **Section 2, Field 1 (form_section2_field1)**: Check the boxes next to each option to indicate whether the equipment is functioning properly. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. This field helps you track which equipment needs maintenance or repair.
* **Section 2, Field 2 (section2_field2)**: Enter any notes or comments about the equipment condition in this field. This field is a text entry field for any additional information.
* **Section 2, Field 3 (section2_field3)**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if the equipment needs immediate attention or replacement. This field helps you prioritize which equipment needs the most urgent maintenance.
* **Section 3 (form_section3)**: Enter any overall comments or observations about the equipment or the maintenance process. This field is a text entry field for any final notes or feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out fields.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in a field, contact your supervisor or a colleague for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
