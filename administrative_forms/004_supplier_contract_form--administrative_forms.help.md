# Supplier Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Contract Form is used to collect and manage information about supplier contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form data section with any relevant information about the contract.
2. Select the suppliers involved in the contract.
3. Specify the contract terms and conditions.
4. Choose the contract duration.
5. Select the type of contract.
6. Enter the contract price.
7. Select the suppliers list.
8. Enter the contract date.
9. Add any additional notes about the contract.
10. Enter the supplier's email address.
11. Enter the supplier's phone number.
12. Enter the supplier's address.
13. Choose the contract status (active or inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Data (1)**: A text input field where you can enter any relevant information about the contract.
- **Suppliers (2)**: A multi-select field to choose which suppliers are involved in the contract.
- **Contract Terms (3)**: A text input field to specify the contract terms and conditions.
- **Contract Duration (4)**: A date picker field to select the contract duration.
- **Contract Type (5)**: A single-select field to choose the contract type.
- **Contract Price (6)**: A numerical field to enter the contract price.
- **Suppliers List (7)**: A multi-select field to select which suppliers are listed in the contract.
- **Contract Date (8)**: A date picker field to select the contract date.
- **Notes (9)**: A text area field to add any additional notes about the contract.
- **Email (10)**: An email field to enter the supplier's email address.
- **Phone (11)**: A text input field to enter the supplier's phone number.
- **Address (12)**: A text input field to enter the supplier's address.
- **Contract Status (13)**: A single-select field to choose the contract status (active or inactive).

## Tips
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form data field.
* Ensure you have the correct suppliers selected.
* Be specific and detailed when entering contract terms and conditions.
* Double-check your selections for contract type and status.
* Enter the supplier's email address and phone number correctly.
