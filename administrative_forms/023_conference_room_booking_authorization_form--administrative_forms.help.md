# Conference Room Booking Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for a conference room booking. It is essential for users to complete the form accurately and thoroughly to prevent duplication of requests and ensure efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the correct information for your request.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`): Enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department name for reference.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your staff identification number (if applicable).
* **Room Requested** (`room_requested`): Select the desired room for your event.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`): Enter the date of your event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of your event.
* **Setup Time Required** (`setup_time`): Enter the time required for setup before your event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select the purpose of your booking.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`): Enter the title of your event.
* **Expected Number of Attendees** (`expected_attendees`): Enter the expected number of attendees.
* **Room Setup Preference** (`room_setup`): Select the desired layout style for your event.
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`): Select any required equipment for your event.
* **Catering Required** (`catering_required`): Confirm if food and beverage are required.
* **Catering Details** (`catering_details`): Provide any specific catering requirements.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`): Enter any additional needs for your event.
* **Parking Required** (`parking_needed`): Enter the number of parking spaces required.
* **Access Requirements** (`access_requirements`): Enter any security or badge needs.
* **Budget Code or Cost Center** (`budget_code`): Enter your budget code or cost center for billing purposes.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your approving manager.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter the email of your approving manager.
* **Authorization Status** (`authorization_status`): Confirm the approval status of your request.
* **I Accept Room Booking Terms** (`terms_accepted`): Confirm that you accept the room booking policies.
