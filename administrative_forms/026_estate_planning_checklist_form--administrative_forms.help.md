# Estate Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive tool to help individuals and families plan their estates, consider their assets, and make informed decisions about their future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. For sections with multiple options, select all applicable choices that apply to your situation.
3. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers to the questions.
4. Review and update your answers regularly to reflect any changes in your circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Planning Checklist** (Page 1):
	+ This section serves as an introduction to the form and a reminder of the importance of estate planning.
* **Wills and Trusts** (Page 2):
	+ Select all applicable options for your wills and trusts, such as:
		- Will (e.g., last will and testament)
		- Living trust
		- Joint wills/trusts with spouse/partner
* **Beneficiaries** (Page 3):
	+ Select one or more individuals you wish to benefit from your estate, such as:
		- Spouse/partner
		- Children
		- Grandchildren
		- Other relatives or friends
* **Assets** (Page 4):
	+ List your assets, including:
		- Real estate (e.g., home, investment properties)
		- Financial assets (e.g., cash, stocks, bonds, retirement accounts)
		- Tangible personal property (e.g., artwork, jewelry, collectibles)
* **Liabilities** (Page 5):
	+ List any outstanding debts or financial obligations, including:
		- Mortgages
		- Loans
		- Credit card debt
* **Insurance** (Page 6):
	+ Select all applicable insurance policies, such as:
		- Life insurance
		- Health insurance
		- Long-term care insurance
		- Disability insurance
* **Legal Documents** (Page 7):
	+ Select one or more legal documents, including:
		- Will (e.g., last will and testament)
		- Living will
		- Power of attorney
* **Tax Planning** (Page 8):
	+ Provide information on your tax planning goals and strategies, such as:
		- Minimizing tax liabilities
		- Maximizing tax benefits
		- Tax-deferred income sources (e.g., retirement accounts)
* **Estate Plan** (Page 9):
	+ Describe your estate plan, including:
		- Distribution of assets after your death
		- Guardianship of minor children
		- Business succession planning
* **Review and Update** (Page 10):
	+ Review your answers and update as needed to reflect any changes in your circumstances.
