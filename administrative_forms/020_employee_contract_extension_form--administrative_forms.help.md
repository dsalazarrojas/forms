# Employee Contract Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Contract Extension Form is used to submit a request to extend an employee's contract due to extenuating circumstances. This form should only be completed by employees who need to make changes to their contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the reason for the contract extension from the options provided.
2. Enter your job title in the Job Title field.
3. Enter the new contract start date and end date using the date picker.
4. Review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Option 1
* **Employee Contract Extension Form**: Select the reason for your contract extension from the provided options. This will help us understand the nature of your request.

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title to help us locate your contract in our records.

### Contract Start Date
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the new start date for your contract. This will be the date from which your contract will be extended.

### Contract End Date
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the new end date for your contract. This will be the date until which your contract will be extended.
Tips:
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
