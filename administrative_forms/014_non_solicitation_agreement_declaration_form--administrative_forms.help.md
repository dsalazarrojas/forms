# Non Solicitation Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Solicitation Agreement Declaration Form is a document that allows employees to declare that they are not soliciting business or engaging in any activities that may be deemed as solicitation. This form is intended to prevent any potential conflicts of interest and ensure that employees remain impartial in their work. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter your name and title**:
	* In the `employee_name` field, enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
	* In the `employee_title` field, enter your job title as it is currently assigned to you.
2. **Enter company name and date**:
	* In the `company_name` field, enter the official name of your company.
	* In the `date_of_declaration` and `date_of_declaration_date` fields, enter the date of the declaration.
3. **Add your signature**:
	* In the `signature_of_employee` field, sign your name in the provided space.
	* Make sure to date the signature as well, using the same date entered in the `date_of_declaration_date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **solicitation_agreement**: A statement declaring that you are not soliciting business or engaging in any activities that may be deemed as solicitation.
* **employee-name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **company-name**: Enter the official name of your company.
* **date-of-declaration**: The date on which you are making the declaration.
* **date-of-declaration-date**: Enter the same date as in the previous field to ensure consistency.
* **signature-of-employee**: Sign your name in the provided space, using the date as entered in the `date_of_declaration_date` field.
* **employee-title**: Enter your current job title as assigned to you.
* **company**: This field is not explicitly defined in the original schema, likely due to a mistake.
