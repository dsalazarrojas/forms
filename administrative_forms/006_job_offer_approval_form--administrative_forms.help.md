## job_offer_approval_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The job_offer_approval_form is used to collect necessary information to approve job offers within the organization. It helps in ensuring that job offers are processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "approval level" for the job offer from the provided options.
2. Choose the relevant department for the job offer, selecting "Yes" or "No" from the multiple-choice options.
3. Enter the date of the job offer.
4. Provide the job title for the job offer.
5. Enter the job offer amount.
6. Select the type of job from the provided options.
7. Enter the name of the individual who submitted the job offer (the prophet's name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **approval level** (`approval_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of approval you want to assign to this job offer from the given options. This field helps you choose the correct approval level for the job offer.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the department to which this job offer belongs. This field ensures that job offers are properly distributed within the organization.
* **Job Offer Date** (`job_offer_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the job offer. This date will help in tracking the job offer's timeline.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the job title for the job offer. This field is helpful for easy identification of the job offer.
* **Job Offer Amount** (`job_offer_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the job offer amount, which is a critical piece of information for any job offer.
* **Job type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job from the provided options. This field helps in categorizing the job offer correctly.
* **Prophet's Name** (`prophets_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the individual who submitted the job offer. This field ensures accountability for the job offer submission.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have the necessary approvals and permissions before submitting this form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant department.
