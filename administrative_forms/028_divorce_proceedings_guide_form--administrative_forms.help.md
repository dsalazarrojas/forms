# Divorce Proceedings Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Proceedings Guide Form is a document used to gather essential information from both spouses to facilitate a smooth and efficient divorce process. It is crucial to fill out the form accurately and honestly to avoid delays or complications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the first page with your name and contact information.
2. Proceed to the second page and provide the required information for both spouses, including their contact details.
3. On the third page, provide the contact information for both spouses.
4. The fourth page is for listing the grounds for divorce, if applicable.
5. On the fifth page, upload any relevant divorce documents, such as court papers or agreements.
6. The sixth page is for describing the divorce process, including any previous attempts at mediation or counseling.
7. Finally, list any divorce fees associated with the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Divorce Proceedings Form** (`divorce_proceedings_form`): This is the main form title.
* **Step 1** (`step_1`): A brief description of the divorce process, which is not required but can be used for reference.
* **Spouse 1 Contact Information** (`spouse_1_contact_info`): Provide the contact details for the first spouse, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Spouse 2 Contact Information** (`spouse_2_contact_info`): Provide the contact details for the second spouse, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Divorce Grounds** (`divorce_grounds`): If applicable, list the grounds for divorce, such as irreconcilable differences or infidelity.
* **Divorce Documents** (`divorce_documents`): Upload any relevant divorce documents, such as court papers or agreements.
* **Divorce Process** (`divorce_process`): Describe the divorce process, including any previous attempts at mediation or counseling.
* **Divorce Fees** (`divorce_fees`): List any associated fees with the divorce process.
