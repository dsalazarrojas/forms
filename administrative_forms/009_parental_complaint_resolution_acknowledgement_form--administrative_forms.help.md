# parental_complaint_resolution_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Complaint Resolution Acknowledgement Form is used to collect and document information about complaints submitted by parents. This form is a crucial tool in the resolution process, ensuring that all relevant details are recorded and addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the complaint from the options provided.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the complaint.
3. Select the resolution status of the complaint.
4. Record the date of the complaint and the expected follow-up date.
5. Enter the name of the school related to the complaint.
6. Provide the email address of the school.
7. Enter the phone number of the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of complaint from the options provided, such as 'Complaint' or 'Other'.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the complaint was raised.
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the current resolution status of the complaint, which can be 'Resolved' or 'Pending'.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false): Record the expected date for a follow-up on the complaint.
* **Resolution Date** (`resolution_date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the date when the resolution was achieved.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school related to the complaint.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required: false): Provide the email address of the school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the school.
