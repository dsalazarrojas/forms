# Manufacturing Process Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manufacturing Process Records Release Form is used to record and release information about the manufacturing process. This form is used to ensure that all relevant details about the production process are documented and easily accessible for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with any relevant details about the manufacturing process.
2. Provide the Release Details, including the release date and time.
3. Choose the type of records to be released using the dropdown menus.
4. Add any comments or release notes as needed.
5. Enter the release person's name and department.
6. List the team members involved in the manufacturing process.
7. Add any additional comments or attachments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing any additional information about the manufacturing process.
* **Release Details** (`2`, `text`, optional): Enter the details of the release, such as the release date and time.
* **Release Date** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the release.
* **Release Time** (`4`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the release.
* **Records** (`5`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of records to be released.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or notes about the release.
* **Release Type** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of release from the dropdown menu.
* **Release Status** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the release from the dropdown menu.
* **Release Notes** (`9`, `text`, optional): Add any additional release notes.
* **Release By** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person releasing the records.
* **Released To** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or team receiving the records.
* **Notes** (`12`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the release.
* **Email** (`13`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person releasing the records.
* **Phone** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person releasing the records.
* **Date** (`15`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the release.
* **Time** (`16`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the release.
* **Manufacturing Process** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the details of the manufacturing process.
* **Release Person** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person releasing the records.
* **Department** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the department responsible for the manufacturing process.
* **Manager** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the manager responsible for the process.
* **Team** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter the team involved in the manufacturing process.
* **Team Members** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of team members involved in the process.
* **Comments** (`23`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Attachments** (`24`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant files or documents.
* **Other Comments** (`25`, `text`, optional): Add any other comments about the release.

Note: This form is not required, and some fields are optional. Please fill out only the necessary fields according to your needs.
