# Mortgage Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Complaint Form is designed to allow customers to report any issues or problems they have experienced with their mortgage. This form can be used to submit complaints regarding payment processing, interest rate errors, and other issues related to their mortgage account.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of issue you are experiencing by selecting an option from the "Issue Type" field.
2. Describe the issue in detail in the "Description" field.
3. Provide any necessary contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
4. If applicable, upload any relevant supporting documents or proof in the "Upload Document" and "Upload Proof" fields.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to help us contact you regarding your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Issue Type**: This field allows you to select the type of issue you are experiencing with your mortgage account. Please choose the option that best describes your problem.
* **Description**: In this field, please provide a clear and concise description of the issue you are experiencing. This will help us understand your problem and resolve it as quickly as possible.
* **Contact Information**: This field is where you can provide any contact information that we may need to contact you regarding your complaint. This can include your address, phone number, or any other relevant details.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your complaint. We will only use this email address to contact you about your complaint.
* **Upload Document/Upload Proof**: If you have any relevant documents or proof that support your complaint, you can upload them here. This can include scans or photos of documents, or any other relevant evidence.
* **Upload Proof**: This field is similar to "Upload Document", and is used to upload any additional proof that supports your complaint.
* **Note**: Please do not use this field for submitting complaints. This field is used internally for notes and comments from our team.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that you have attached any necessary documents or proof to support your complaint before submitting it. Also, please be as detailed and clear as possible in your description of the issue you are experiencing. This will help us resolve your complaint as quickly as possible.
