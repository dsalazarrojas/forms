# Workplace Incident Investigation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Incident Investigation Request Form is used to document and track incidents that have occurred in the workplace. This includes details about the incident, its location, involved parties, and the investigation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident date and time.
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Provide a list of witnesses involved in the incident.
4. Choose the status of the investigation.
5. Describe the incident in detail.
6. Determine the cause of the incident.
7. Add any notes about the investigation.
8. Select the investigator assigned to the case.
9. Select who submitted the form.
10. Select who the form was submitted to.
11. Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the incident.

### Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (time, required): Enter the time of the incident.

### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.

### Witness List
* **Witness List** (select multiple, optional): List the witnesses involved in the incident. Options: Yes/No

### Investigation Status
* **Investigation Status** (select one, optional): Select the status of the investigation. Options: Completed/In Progress/Not Started

### Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (text, optional): Describe the incident in detail.

### Incident Cause
* **Incident Cause** (text, optional): Determine the cause of the incident.

### Investigation Notes
* **Investigation Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes about the investigation.

### Assigned Investigator
* **Assigned Investigator** (select one, optional): Select the investigator assigned to the case. Options: John/Jane/Joe

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (select one, optional): Select who submitted the form. Options: John/Jane/Joe

### Submitted To
* **Submitted To** (select one, optional): Select who the form was submitted to. Options: Joe/Jane/Joe

### Submit Date
* **Submit Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Enter the tool used for the investigation.

### Comments
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments about the form.

Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields correctly.
* If a witness was involved, list them in the witness list field.
* If the investigation status is not complete, choose the correct status from the investigation status field.
* The form is only visible to authorized personnel.
