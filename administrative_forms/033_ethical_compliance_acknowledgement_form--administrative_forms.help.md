# Ethical Compliance Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Compliance Acknowledgement Form is a mandatory document that ensures all employees are aware of and acknowledge the company's ethical code of conduct.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Employee Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Job Title.
3. Enter your Company Address (if applicable).
4. Enter your Department (if applicable).
5. Enter your Date of Birth (if applicable).
6. Enter your Email (if applicable).
7. Enter your Phone Number.
8. Read and understand the Employee Code of Conduct and select "Fully Understand" if you do so.
9. Enter any comments you may have.
10. Confirm that you have signed the form by selecting "Signed" and entering the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, **Required**): Enter your job title as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, Text, **Optional**): If you are an employee with a work-from-home agreement, or if you are a remote worker, please enter your work address here. Otherwise, please leave blank.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, **Optional**): If you wish to specify a department, please enter it here. Otherwise, please leave blank.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, **Optional**): If you wish to provide your date of birth, please enter it here. This field is not required for most employees.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Optional**): If you wish to provide your work email, please enter it here. This field is not required for most employees.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your work phone number.
* **Employee Code of Conduct** (`employee_code_of_conduct`, Text, **Optional**): Read through the code of conduct and enter any comments or questions you may have.
* **Acknowledgement Status** (`acknowledgement_status`, Select One, **Required**): Please select "Fully Understand" if you have read and understand the code of conduct.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, **Optional**): Please enter any comments or questions you may have about the code of conduct.
* **Signed** (`signed_at`, Time, **Required**): Confirm that you have signed the form by selecting the current date and time.
* **Signed by** (`signed_by`, Text, **Required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. 

Please note that this form is a requirement for all employees to acknowledge the company's code of conduct and ensure they understand their responsibilities. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
