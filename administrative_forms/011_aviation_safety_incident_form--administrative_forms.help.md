# Aviation Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about aviation safety incidents, helping to identify areas for improvement and reduce the risk of future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the primary category of the incident from the provided options.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Fill out the "Departure Point" and "Destination" fields with the airport information.
4. Enter the number of crew members and passengers on board.
5. Select the number of injuries and fatalities.
6. Choose the extent of damage to the aircraft.
7. Select the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
8. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any immediate actions taken.
9. If there were witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
10. If there were contributing factors, describe them in detail.
11. Suggest any preventive measures or safety recommendations.
12. Classify the report as confidential, internal use only, safety report, or regulatory report required.
13. Check if regulatory notification is required.
14. Determine if follow-up is needed, and if so, by whom.
15. Certify that the report is accurate and complete by checking the box.
16. Date and time the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`): Select the primary category of the incident from the provided options (e.g. Aircraft accident, Near miss/mid-air proximity).
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the local time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the airport or location where the incident occurred.
* **Phase of Flight** (`flight_phase`): Select the phase of flight when the incident occurred (e.g. Pre-flight, Taxi, Takeoff).
* **Reporter Full Name** (`reporter_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`reporter_title`): Enter your job title or position.
* **Employee ID** (`reporter_employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number (optional).
* **Department** (`department`): Select your department or division (e.g. Flight operations, Maintenance).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Aircraft Registration** (`aircraft_registration`): Enter the tail number or registration of the aircraft.
* **Aircraft Type** (`aircraft_type`): Enter the make and model of the aircraft.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`): Enter the flight identifier (optional).
* **Departure Point** (`departure_point`): Enter the airport where the flight departed.
* **Destination** (`destination`): Enter the intended destination airport.
* **Number of Crew Members** (`crew_members_aboard`): Enter the total number of crew members on board.
* **Number of Passengers** (`passengers_aboard`): Enter the total number of passengers on board.
* **Injuries and Fatalities** (`injuries_fatalities`): Select the number of injuries and fatalities (e.g. None, Minor injuries, Serious injuries, Fatalities).
* **Aircraft Damage** (`aircraft_damage`): Select the extent of damage to the aircraft (e.g. None, Minor damage, Substantial damage, Destroyed).
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`): Select the conditions at the time of the incident (e.g. VFR - clear, VFR - scattered clouds, MVFR, IFR, Low visibility, Severe weather).
* **Detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Immediate Actions Taken** (`immediate_actions`): Describe any immediate actions taken after the incident.
* **Witness Information** (`witnesses`): If there were witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`): If there were contributing factors, describe them in detail.
* **Safety Recommendations** (`safety_recommendations`): Suggest any preventive measures or safety recommendations.
* **Report Classification** (`report_classification`): Classify the report as confidential, internal use only, safety report, or regulatory report required.
* **Regulatory Notification Required** (`notification_required`): Check if regulatory notification is required.
* **Follow-up Required** (`follow_up_required`): Determine if follow-up is needed, and if so, by whom.
* **I Certify This Report Is Accurate** (`reporter_signature`): Check to certify that the report is accurate and complete.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`): Date and time the report was completed.
