# Corporate Retreat Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about companies and their representatives attending a corporate retreat. It is used to streamline the process of organizing and executing the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's information in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter your contact person's name and email address.
3. Provide your contact person's phone number.
4. Indicate whether you would like to book a room with or without accommodation.
5. If you have any specific accommodation preferences, select them in the "Accommodation Preferences" field.
6. Choose the accommodation type you would like to book.
7. You can provide any additional accommodation details in the "Accommodation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required/optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, required/optional): Select whether you would like to book a room with or without accommodation.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, required/optional): Choose the type of accommodation you would like to book.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the accommodation you would like to book.
