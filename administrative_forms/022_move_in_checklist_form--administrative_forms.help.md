# Move In Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Move In Checklist Form" is a crucial document for property administrators and landlords to obtain essential information from tenants upon move-in. Completing this form accurately helps streamline the process and reduces potential issues during tenancy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a date for the "Move in Date" fields (fields 1 and 2) to reflect the actual or anticipated move-in date.
2. Enter the landlord's information in the "Landlord Information" field (field 3), including their full name, contact details, or any other relevant details.
3. Provide the landlord's email address in the "Landlord Email" field (field 4).
4. Enter the tenant's information in the "Tenant Information" field (field 5), including their full name, contact details, or any other relevant details.
5. Enter the tenant's phone number in the "Tenant Phone" field (field 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Move in Date** (1): Enter the date you plan to move in, or the date you have already moved in. 
* **Move In Date (Confirm)** (2): Confirm the move-in date to ensure accuracy.
* **Landlord Information** (3): Provide the full name of the landlord or property manager, along with any additional contact information or details.
* **Landlord Email** (4): Enter the landlord's email address for easy contact and communication.
* **Tenant Information** (5): Enter your full name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Tenant Phone** (6): Enter your phone number for easy contact and communication.
