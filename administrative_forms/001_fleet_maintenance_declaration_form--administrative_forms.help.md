# Fleet Maintenance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Maintenance Declaration Form is used to collect information about individual vehicles in a fleet, including vehicle make, model, last service date, odometer reading, and maintenance due date. This form is essential for tracking maintenance history and ensuring that vehicles are properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for the vehicle.
2. Enter the vehicle make and model in the respective fields.
3. Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
4. Record the date of the last service performed on the vehicle.
5. Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.
6. Enter the date when the next maintenance is due for the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Make (<name>: vehicle_make, <label>: Vehicle Make)
Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g. Toyota, Ford, Honda, etc.

### Vehicle Model (<name>: vehicle_model, <label>: Vehicle Model)
Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g. Corolla, F-150, Civic, etc.

### Vehicle Identification Number (<name>: vin, <label>: Vehicle Identification Number)
Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle. This is a unique 17-digit code that identifies the vehicle.

### Last Service Date (<name>: last_service_date, <label>: Last Service Date)
Enter the date of the last service performed on the vehicle.

### Odometer Reading (<name>: odometer_reading, <label>: Odometer Reading)
Enter the current odometer reading of the vehicle.

### Maintenance Due Date (<name>: maintenance_due_date, <label>: Maintenance Due Date)
Enter the date when the next maintenance is due for the vehicle.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form with accurate information to ensure that vehicles receive proper maintenance.
* Update the form regularly to reflect changes in vehicle maintenance history.
* Use the form in conjunction with other fleet management tools to ensure effective fleet maintenance.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out on an individual vehicle basis, so make sure to complete one form per vehicle.
