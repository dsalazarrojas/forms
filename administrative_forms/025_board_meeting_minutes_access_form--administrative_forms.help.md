# Board Meeting Minutes Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Board Meeting Minutes Access Form is designed for board members or staff to request access to meeting minutes. Fill out this form to request access to the minutes of a specific meeting, and provide necessary information for the approver to verify your request.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and detailed information.
- Ensure you have checked the "Access type" field carefully, as this will determine the level of access you will receive.
- If you are requesting access for a specific purpose, please be as detailed as possible in the "Purpose" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Minutes Access Request --**
  • This is the form header, which serves as a title for the form.
  • It is a note field and does not require any specific input.
- **Requester name**
  • This field is required and should be filled out with your name as a board member or staff member.
  • This is the name by which you will be identified in the access request.
- **Requester email**
  • This field is required and should be filled out with your email address for delivery of minutes.
  • This is how you will receive the minutes once access is granted.
- **Meeting date**
  • This field is required and should be filled out with the date of the meeting for which you are requesting minutes.
  • This ensures the approver knows which meeting the minutes pertain to.
- **Access type**
  • This field is required and should be selected carefully to determine the level of access you need.
  • View only: This will grant you access to view the minutes, but not edit or annotate them.
  • Download: This will grant you access to download the minutes as a PDF or other format.
  • Annotate: This will grant you access to edit the minutes, usually for the purpose of correcting or adding information.
- **Purpose for access**
  • This field is optional, but if you are requesting access for a specific reason, please provide as much detail as possible.
  • This helps the approver understand your request and make an informed decision.
- **Approver name**
  • This field is optional, but if you are requesting access for someone else, please provide their name.
  • This is the name of the person who will be granting you access.
- **Required by date**
  • This field is optional, but if you need the minutes by a specific date, please fill out this field.
  • This ensures the approver knows when the minutes are needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
