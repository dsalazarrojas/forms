# Construction Project Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Project Check In Form is designed to gather essential information about visitors and their activities on the construction site. This form ensures that visitors are properly checked in and that their site access is recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Visitor name** in the "Visitor name" field. This is a required field, so please make sure to input your full name.
2. If you're part of a company or trade, provide your **Company affiliation** in the "Company affiliation" field. This field is not required.
3. Enter your contact **phone number** in the "Contact phone" field. This will help us contact you during your visit if needed.
4. Select the time you arrived on site in the "**Check in time**" field. Please ensure accuracy to maintain a reliable record.
5. Provide the **Purpose of visit** in the "Purpose of visit" field. This is a required field to ensure we understand the reason for your visit.
6. Confirm that you received the **Site safety briefing** by selecting either "True" or "False" in the respective field. This is a required field to ensure your safety is respected.
7. If you've brought any equipment or tools on site, note them down in the "**Equipment checked in note**" field. Although not required, this helps us keep track of items that may be present on site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor name** (Required): Enter your full name as per the name on your ID or badge.
* **Company affiliation** (Optional): If you're representing a company or trade, provide the name or affiliation.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your contact phone number for any communication purposes.
* **Check in time**: Select the time you arrived on site.
* **Purpose of visit** (Required): Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
* **Site safety briefing received** (Required): Confirm if you received the safety briefing or not.
* **Equipment checked in note** (Optional): If you've brought any equipment or tools, note them down for reference.
