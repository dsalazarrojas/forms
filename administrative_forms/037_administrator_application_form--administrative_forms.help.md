# Administrator Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Administrator Application Form is designed for individuals applying for administrative positions within our organization. This form collects necessary information to evaluate your qualifications, experience, and fit for the position you are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form
Before submitting your application, please make sure you have a clear understanding of the position you are applying for and the requirements listed below. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and be prepared to provide any additional documentation or information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Provide a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`applicant_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`applicant_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`applicant_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`applicant_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`applicant_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, text, required): Select the administrative position you are applying for from the list provided.
* **Department Applied For** (`department_applied_for`, select_one, required): Select the department you would like to work in from the list provided.
* **Employment Type Desired** (`employment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of employment you are seeking (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you can start work if selected.
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text, required): Enter your salary expectations (note: this field is not required, but it is recommended to be honest and transparent).
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest education level achieved from the list provided.
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, required): Enter the name of the school, degree earned, and graduation year.
* **Administrative Experience** (`administrative_experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant administrative experience.
* **Management Experience** (`management_experience`, text, required): Describe your management experience (if applicable).
* **Computer Proficiency** (`computer_proficiency`, text, required): List your computer software skills relevant to the position.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, text, required): List all languages you speak fluently.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Enter your availability requirements.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Select if references are available upon request.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select if you consent to a background check.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submitting this application.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Provide an electronic signature to confirm your application.

Please take your time and fill out the form accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
