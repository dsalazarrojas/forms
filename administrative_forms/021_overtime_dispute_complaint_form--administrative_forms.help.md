# OvertimeDisputeComplaintForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OvertimeDisputeComplaintForm is a form designed to help employees report and resolve overtime disputes. This form is used to provide accurate and up-to-date information regarding overtime hours worked and pay discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing all required fields.
2.  Select the correct submission date and submitter from the respective dropdown menus.
3.  Attach any supporting evidence, if applicable.
4.  Review and verify all information before submitting the form.
5.  Submit the form to the designated email address or submit it online through the HR portal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overtime Dispute** (`overtime_dispute`): Enter a brief description of the overtime dispute.
* **Pay Discrepancy** (`pay_discrepancy`): Describe the pay discrepancy related to the overtime hours worked.
* **Description** (`description`): Provide a detailed explanation of the situation that led to the overtime dispute.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`): Enter the date the overtime occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`): Enter the time the overtime occurred.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`): Attach any supporting evidence, such as screenshots, emails, or other relevant documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter your work phone number.
* **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`): Enter your supervisor's ID number (optional).
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`): Enter your supervisor's work email address (optional).
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`): Enter your supervisor's work phone number (optional).
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`): Enter your manager's ID number (optional).
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's work email address (optional).
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter your manager's work phone number (optional).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`): Select the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Select the person submitting this form from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`): Select who this form is being submitted to (optional).
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): Select the time you are submitting this form (optional).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Select who is submitting this form from the dropdown menu (optional).
* **Overtime Rate** (`overtime_rate`): Enter the overtime rate applicable to your role.
* **Overtime Hours** (`overtime_hours`): Enter the number of overtime hours worked.
* **Submitted By Employee** (`submitted_by_employee`): Select who is submitting this form from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitted By Supervisor** (`submitted_by_supervisor`): Select who is submitting this form from the dropdown menu.
