# Administrative Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Administrative Application Form is used to collect information from potential candidates interested in applying for administrative roles within the organization. This form is a crucial part of the application process, and it is essential to fill it out accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Full name** and **Email address** accurately and truthfully, as they will be used for communication purposes.
2. Clearly state the **Position applied for** that you are interested in.
3. Provide a brief description of your **Qualifications and experience**.
4. If applicable, mention your **Availability to start** date.
5. List your **References**.
6. If you have attached a resume, select **Resume attached** to confirm.
7. Write a brief **Cover letter summary** explaining why you fit the position.
8. Optionally, provide any **Additional information** that might be helpful for the hiring team to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant information**: This section is a brief introduction to your application. Please fill it out carefully and accurately.
* **Full name**: This field requires your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: Use your actual email address that you check regularly for communication about your application.
* **Position applied for**: Clearly state the administrative role you are interested in applying for.
* **Qualifications and experience**: Describe your relevant education, skills, and work experience that align with the position applied for.
* **Availability to start**: If you have a specific date or a range of dates when you can start working, provide it here.
* **References**: List two professional references that can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Resume attached**: If you have attached a resume, select this option to confirm that you have done so.
* **Cover letter summary**: Write a brief statement explaining why you fit the position and what makes you a strong candidate.
* **Additional information**: If you have any other details that might be relevant for the hiring team to consider, list them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have attached all required supporting documents, such as a resume and cover letter.
* Be truthful and accurate when filling out the form.
* Use professional language and format for all sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the designated contact person or email address.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is complete and accurate.
