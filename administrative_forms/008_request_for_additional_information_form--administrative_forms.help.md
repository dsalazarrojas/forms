# Request For Additional Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Additional Information Form is used to gather more details from users about a specific topic. This form helps to ensure that all relevant information is collected, and no important questions are left unanswered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Request For Additional Information Form" title to access the form fields.
2. Select the relevant category from the dropdown list to provide context for your request.
3. Provide a clear description of the additional information you need.
4. Optionally, add more details about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Category (Select One)**
* (`Category`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the relevant category for your request. This will help us understand the context of your inquiry.

### **Description** (`form_description`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the additional information you need. Be specific about what you're looking for, and provide as much detail as possible.

### **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, optional): You can add a title to your request, but this field is not required. If you leave it blank, our system will automatically generate a title based on your request.

### **Tips**
* Be specific about what you're looking for to ensure we collect accurate information.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your request.
* Our team will review your request and follow up with more information if needed.
