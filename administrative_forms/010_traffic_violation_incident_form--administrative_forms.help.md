# Traffic Violation Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report traffic incidents that have occurred on our jurisdiction. It helps in gathering information about the incident, including the date, location, vehicle details, and other relevant details. This information is used to identify and address potential risks to traffic safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are reporting a genuine incident and that it has not been reported before.
2. Select the correct date when the incident occurred.
3. Enter the location where the incident happened.
4. Provide a brief description of the incident.
5. Fill in the vehicle registration number, make, model, year, color, and plate number.
6. If applicable, select the type of violation (e.g., yes/no).
7. Enter the driver's name.
8. Fill in any additional information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): The date when the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the exact location where the incident happened.
* **Violation Description** (`violation_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_maked`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Plate Number** (`plate_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's plate number.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the driver's name.
* **Violation Type** (`violation_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the vehicle was involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, optional): Note: This field was not explicitly mentioned in the YAML but was added for extra information.

Note: If you have any further questions or concerns while filling in this form, please do not hesitate to reach out to our support team.
