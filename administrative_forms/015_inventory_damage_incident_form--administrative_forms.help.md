# Inventory Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Damage Incident Form is designed to capture information about an incident that has caused damage to an inventory. This includes details about the incident, such as the date and time it occurred, the location, and the extent of the damage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details about what happened.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident.
3. Enter the location of the incident.
4. Describe the extent of the damage caused by the incident.
5. Identify the cause of the damage.
6. List the affected items, including any details about each item.
7. (Optional) Provide a list of witnesses and any relevant details about them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description**: A detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details about what happened. This field is required and should be filled out thoroughly.
* **Incident Date**: The date on which the incident occurred. This field is required and should be formatted as a standard date (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Incident Time**: The time at which the incident occurred. This field is required and should be formatted as a standard time (e.g., 03:45 PM).
* **Location**: The location where the incident occurred. This field is required and should be a clear and concise description of the location.
* **Damage Description**: A detailed description of the extent of the damage caused by the incident. This field is required and should be filled out thoroughly.
* **Cause**: The reason or explanation for the damage. This field is required and should be clear and concise.
* **Affected Items**: A list of items that were affected by the incident, including any details about each item. This field is required and should be filled out thoroughly.
* **Witness List**: (Optional) A list of people or entities that witnessed the incident. This field is optional and should only be filled out if relevant.
* **Witness Details**: (Optional) Any relevant details about the witnesses, such as their names or contact information. This field is optional and should only be filled out if relevant.

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
