# warehouse_inventory_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The warehouse inventory records release form is used to document the release of items from the warehouse. This form is essential for maintaining accurate inventory records and ensuring that all necessary information is captured for tracking and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a detailed description of the item being released in the text field labeled "Inventory Item".
2. Provide a clear and concise reason for releasing the item in the text field labeled "Reason for Release".
3. Specify the quantity of the item being released.
4. If applicable, enter the date and time of the release in the fields labeled "Release Date" and "Release Time", respectively.
5. Add any additional remarks or notes about the release in the text field labeled "Remarks".
6. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, in the fields labeled "Email" and "Phone".
7. Select the tool to which the item is being assigned in the field labeled "Assigned Tool" (Note: this field is a dropdown list with specific options).
8. Choose the person responsible for releasing the item in the field labeled "Release Person" (Note: this field is a dropdown list with specific options).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Item** (`text`, required): Enter a detailed description of the item being released.
* **Item Description** (`text`, required): Enter a clear and concise description of the item.
* **Reason for Release** (`text`, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for releasing the item.
* **Quantity** (`number`, required): Specify the quantity of the item being released.
* **Release Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the release, if applicable.
* **Release Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the release, if applicable.
* **Remarks** (`note`, optional): Add any additional remarks or notes about the release.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, required): Select the tool to which the item is being assigned from the provided options.
* **Release Person** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose the person responsible for releasing the item from the provided options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out in order to submit the form accurately.
* Double-check the details entered in each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the specific instructions above for clarification.
