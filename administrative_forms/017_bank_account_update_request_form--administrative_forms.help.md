# Bank Account Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request updates to your bank account information. Please use this form to provide any changes you'd like to make to your account details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your current full name** (`Current Full Name on Account`): Enter your current full name as it appears on your account.
2. **Enter your account number** (`Account Number`): Enter the number of your bank account.
3. **Enter your contact phone number** (`Contact Phone Number`): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
4. **Select the updates you'd like to make** (`Information to Update`): Choose all the updates you'd like to make to your account. You can select from:
	* Legal Name Change
	* Primary Mailing Address
	* Contact Phone/Email
	* Tax ID / SSN Correction
	* Add / Remove Joint Account Holder
	* Change Beneficiary
5. **Enter any details for name changes** (`New Legal Name`): If you selected `Legal Name Change`, enter your new legal name.
6. **Enter any details for address updates** (`New Mailing Address`): If you selected `Primary Mailing Address`, enter your new address.
7. **Enter any details for email updates** (`New Primary Email Address`): If you selected `Contact Phone/Email`, enter your new email address.
8. **Add or remove joint account holders** (`Name of Joint Holder to Add/Remove`): If you selected `Add / Remove Joint Account Holder`, enter the name of the person to add or remove.
9. **Choose how you'd like to be verified** (`Preferred Verification Method`): Select how you'd like us to contact you to verify your changes:
	* SMS Code
	* Email Link
	* Phone Call from Representative
	* In-Person Visit
10. **Authorize the updates** (`I authorize the bank to update my records as specified above`): Confirm that you authorize the bank to make the requested updates.
11. **Select a date for the request** (`Date of Request`): Choose the date you'd like to make the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Current Full Name on Account (text)**: Enter your current full name as it appears on your account.
	* Required
	* Type: text
- **Account Number (text)**: Enter the number of your bank account.
	* Required
	* Type: text
- **Contact Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
	* Required
	* Type: text
- **Information to Update (select_multiple)**: Choose all the updates you'd like to make to your account.
	* Required
	* Type: select_multiple
- **New Legal Name (text)**: If you selected `Legal Name Change`, enter your new legal name.
	* Not required
	* Type: text
- **New Mailing Address (text)**: If you selected `Primary Mailing Address`, enter your new address.
	* Not required
	* Type: text
- **New Primary Email Address (email)**: If you selected `Contact Phone/Email`, enter your new email address.
	* Not required
	* Type: email
- **Name of Joint Holder to Add/Remove (text)**: If you selected `Add / Remove Joint Account Holder`, enter the name of the person to add or remove.
	* Not required
	* Type: text
- **Action for Joint Holder (select_one)**: If you selected `Add / Remove Joint Account Holder`, select whether to add or remove the joint holder.
	* Not required
	* Type: select_one
- **Preferred Verification Method (select_one)**: Select how you'd like us to contact you to verify your changes.
	* Required
	* Type: select_one
- **I authorize the bank to update my records as specified above (select_one)**: Confirm that you authorize the bank to make the requested updates.
	* Required
	* Type: select_one
- **Date of Request (date)**: Choose the date you'd like to make the request.
	* Required
	* Type: date

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* If you select a name change, you will be required to provide legal documentation for the change.
* If you select an address change, you will be able to enter your new address in the corresponding field.
* If you select an email update, you will be able to enter your new email address in the corresponding field.
* If you select adding or removing a joint account holder, you will be able to enter the name of the person to add or remove.
* Please select a preferred method of verification to ensure you are contacted in a way that is convenient for you.
* Please confirm that you authorize the bank to make the requested updates.
