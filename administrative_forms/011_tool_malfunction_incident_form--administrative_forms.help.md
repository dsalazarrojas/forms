# Tool Malfunction Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Tool Malfunction Incident Form" is used to report and track tool-related incidents within the organization. This form helps to collect and document information about tool malfunctions, ensuring that incidents are properly recorded and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the tool malfunction.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
3. Select the correct tool and its condition from the provided options.
4. Indicate the severity of the malfunction.
5. Enter the cause of the malfunction, if known.
6. Suggest any recommended actions to be taken.
7. Provide details about any action taken to address the malfunction.
8. Document the resolution date and outcome of the incident.
9. Enter additional notes about the incident, if needed.
10. Finally, provide contact information for the person reporting the incident and the reviewer reviewing the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tool ID** (1): Enter the ID number assigned to the malfunctioning tool.
* **Location** (2): Provide the location where the malfunction occurred.
* **Incident Date** (3): Enter the date when the malfunction was noticed.
* **Description** (4): Describe the malfunction in detail.
* **Assigned Tool** (5): Select the tool that was malfunctioning from the options provided.
* **Tool Condition** (6): Indicate the tool's condition before and after the malfunction.
* **Severity** (7): Choose the severity of the malfunction from the options provided.
* **Cause** (8): If known, enter the reason for the malfunction.
* **Recommended Action** (9): Suggest any recommended actions to be taken to address the malfunction.
* **Action Taken** (10): Document any actions taken to address the malfunction.
* **Resolution Date** (11): Enter the date when the incident was resolved.
* **Outcome** (12): Choose the outcome of the incident from the options provided.
* **Outcome Notes** (13): Enter any notes about the outcome.
* **Additional Notes** (14): Enter any additional information about the incident.
* **Reported By** (15): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Name** (16): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Email** (17): Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Phone** (18): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Job Title** (19): Enter the job title of the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Department** (20): Enter the department of the person reporting the incident.
* **Report Date** (21): Enter the date when the incident was reported.
* **Reviewer Name** (22): Enter the name of the person reviewing the incident.
* **Reviewer Job Title** (23): Enter the job title of the person reviewing the incident.
* **Reviewer Department** (24): Enter the department of the person reviewing the incident.
* **Reviewer Email** (25): Enter the email address of the person reviewing the incident.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it is essential to fill them out whenever possible to ensure accurate record-keeping and proper tracking of the incident.
