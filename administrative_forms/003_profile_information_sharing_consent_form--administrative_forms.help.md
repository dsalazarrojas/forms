# Profile Information Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals about their consent to share their personal data with third parties. This form ensures that individuals are aware of what data will be shared, with whom, and for what purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the profile information sharing consent form in the **Profile Information Sharing Consent Form** field.
2.  Select the relevant category for the data sharing request in the **Category** field. This will help identify the purpose and scope of the data sharing request.
3.  Provide a brief description of why the data sharing request is necessary in the **Description** field.
4.  Enter a description of the data that will be shared in the **Description** field (note that this field has a duplicate label, please be aware of this duplication and ignore it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Profile Information Sharing Consent Form** (`text`, required): This is the title of the profile information sharing consent form. Enter a clear and concise title that reflects the purpose and scope of the data sharing request.
*   **Category** (`select_one`, required): Choose the category that best describes the purpose and scope of the data sharing request. This will help identify the relevant party or parties involved in the data sharing request.
*   **Category** (this field is a duplicate and should not be filled in, use the previous field instead, or simply ignore it) (`text`, required): **DO NOT FILL THIS FIELD**
*   **Description** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of why the data sharing request is necessary.
*   **Description** (this field is a duplicate and should not be filled in, use the previous field instead, or simply ignore it) (`text`, required): **DO NOT FILL THIS FIELD**
