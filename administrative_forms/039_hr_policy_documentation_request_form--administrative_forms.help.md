# HR Policy Documentation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Documentation Request Form is used to request the creation, update, or review of HR policy documents. This form allows employees to submit requests and track the progress of HR policy documentation projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of HR policy document needed.
2. Explain the reason for your request.
3. Attach any relevant supporting documentation.
4. Select the person responsible for creating or updating the document.
5. Track the progress of your request and view the current status of your document.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as an employee of the company.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Document Type**: Select the type of HR policy document you need, such as "HR Policy" or "Handbook".
* **Reason for Request**: Explain the reason for your request in a clear and concise manner.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant supporting documentation, such as files or images.
* **Date of Request**: Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for creating or updating the document.
*   * **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding your request.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the document.
*   * **Updated Date**: Enter the date the document was last updated.
* **Status**: Select the current status of your request, such as "Draft" or "Approved".
