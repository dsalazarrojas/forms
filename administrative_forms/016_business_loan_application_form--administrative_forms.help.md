# Business Loan Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you apply for a business loan to support the growth and development of your business. The form will guide you through the necessary information required for us to evaluate your loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields with options, select the most accurate and relevant choice.
3. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when answering questions that require descriptions, such as the loan purpose description.
4. Ensure that all financial information is up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (\*\): Enter the legal name of your business as registered with the state and federal government.
* **Business Structure** (\*\): Select the structure of your business (e.g., Sole Proprietship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, etc.).
* **Tax ID Number** (\*\): Enter your EIN or SSN as required for tax purposes.
* **Business Address** (\*\): Enter the full physical address of your business.
* **Years in Business** (\*\): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
* **Industry Type** (\*\): Select the primary industry type of your business (e.g., Retail, Manufacturing, Technology, etc.).
* **Number of Employees** (\*\): Enter the total number of full-time and part-time employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (\*\): Enter your business's gross annual revenue.
* **Loan Amount Requested** (\*\): Enter the amount of financing needed for your business.
* **Purpose of Loan** (\*\): Select how you plan to use the loan funds (e.g., working capital, equipment purchase, etc.).
* **Loan Purpose Description** (\*\): Describe in detail how the loan funds will be used.
* **Preferred Repayment Term** (\*\): Select the desired loan term length (e.g., 6 months, 1 year, etc.).
* **Collateral Available** (\*\): Select if you have collateral to secure the loan (e.g., real estate, equipment, etc.).
* **Estimated Collateral Value**: Enter the approximate value of the collateral.
* **Personal Credit Score Range** (\*\): Select your personal credit score range (e.g., Excellent - 750 or above, Good - 700 to 749, etc.).
* **Business Credit Score Range**: Select your business credit score range if available (e.g., Excellent - 80 to 100, Good - 60 to 79, etc.).
* **Total Existing Business Debt** (\*\): Enter the current outstanding business debt.
* **Monthly Debt Payments** (\*\): Enter the current monthly debt obligation payments.
* **Primary Bank Name** (\*\): Enter the name of your primary bank.
* **Years with Current Bank**: Enter the number of years you have banked with your primary bank.
* **Previous Business Loan Applications** (\*\): Select if you have applied for business loans before (e.g., Yes - approved, Yes - denied, No - first application).
* **Financial Statements Available** (\*\): Select all financial documents you can provide (e.g., tax returns, profit and loss statement, etc.).
* **Primary Owner Name** (\*\): Enter the full legal name of the primary owner.
* **Owner Email** (\*\): Enter the email address of the primary owner.
* **Owner Phone** (\*\): Enter the contact phone number of the primary owner.

Please note that all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required. Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth loan application process.
