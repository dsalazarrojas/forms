# Timesheet Submission Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for verifying the completeness and accuracy of timesheet submissions by employees. The goal is to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly and that the submission meets the company's policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, review the instructions provided below for each field to ensure you understand what is required.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Submit the form for review and verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter the name of the employee who submitted the timesheet.
* **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter the employee ID of the employee who submitted the timesheet.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the timesheet submission.
* **Additional Comments (timesheet_submission_form_page_2)**: If you have any additional comments or remarks about the submission, enter them here.
