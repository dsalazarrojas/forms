# Weekly Sales Rota - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Weekly Sales Rota" form is a tool used to assign sales tasks to sales representatives. It helps in planning and organizing sales tasks for team members on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Weekly Sales Rota" form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have access to the form and that you are viewing the correct week's rota.
2.  Select the user ID of the sales representative you would like to assign a task to.
3.  Choose the manager who will oversee the task.
4.  Enter the start and end times of the assigned task.
5.  Select the day(s) of the week for which you are assigning the sales task.
6.  Enter the sales amount expected for the task (if applicable).
7.  Add any comments you have about the task.
8.  Click on the "Create" button to save your assigned task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Id** (`User Id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the sales representative you would like to assign the task to. 
- **Manager** (`Manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the manager who will oversee this task.
- **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of the assigned task.
  Example: 09:00
- **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of the assigned task.
  Example: 17:00
- **Day of Week** (`Day`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the day(s) of the week for which you are assigning the task.
  Example: Monday, Wednesday
- **Sales Amount** (`Sales Amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the expected sales amount for the task.
  Example: 100
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or notes about the assigned task.
- **Created At** (`Created At`, `date`, required: false): This is automatically generated and displays the date and time when the task was created.
