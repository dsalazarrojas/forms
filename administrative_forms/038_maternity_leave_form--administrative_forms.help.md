# Maternity Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Maternity Leave Form is a standardized way for employees to submit their leave requests related to pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured and facilitates an efficient approval process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all required information.
2. Attach any supporting documents, such as doctor's certificates or other relevant medical documents.
3. Submit the form to your manager or designated authority.
4. The manager will review the form and approve or deny the leave request based on the information provided.

* **Start Date** (1): Enter the start date of your leave, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date** (2): Enter the end date of your leave, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Manager Approval** (3): Select the reason for your leave request from the provided options.
* **Employee Name** (4): Enter your full name as it appears on your official employee record.
* **Reason** (5): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Manager Name** (6): Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Email** (7): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (8): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Doctor's Certificate** (9): Attach a copy of a doctor's certificate from your healthcare provider, if applicable.
* **Supporting Documents** (10): Attach any other relevant medical or employment documents, if applicable.
* **Department** (11): Enter the department you work in.
* **Location** (12): Enter the location where you work.
* **Reason for Leave** (13): Provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Manager Comment** (14): Enter any additional comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Employee Comment** (15): Enter any additional comments or feedback from you.
* **Form Submitter** (16): Enter your name as the form submitter.
* **Submit Date** (17): This field is auto-populated and should not be edited.
* **Approval Status** (18): This field is automatically updated based on the manager's decision.
* **Comments** (19): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the approval or denial of your leave request.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before submitting the form.
