# Construction Permit Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of construction permit records related to a specific property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the requesting party's information (1-5).
2. Provide property and permit details (6-10).
3. Specify the records you are requesting (11-12).
4. Confirm your authorization and agreement to maintain confidentiality (13-14).
5. Choose your preferred method of delivery (15).
6. Acknowledge the search and copy fees (16).
7. Provide any additional instructions or notes (17).
8. Date and time of submission (18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Requesting Party** (2): Enter the full name of the individual or business requesting the records.
* **Relationship to the Project** (3): Select the relationship of the requesting party to the project (e.g., property owner, general contractor, etc.).
* **Primary Email Address** (4): Enter the email address where you want to receive the records digitally.
* **Primary Phone Number** (5): Enter the primary phone number of the requesting party.
* **Subject Property Address** (7): Enter the address of the property related to the permit records.
* **Tax Parcel Number (APN)** (8): Enter the tax parcel number associated with the property (optional).
* **Permit Number (If Known)** (9): If you know the permit number, enter it here.
* **Specific Years or Date Range for Search** (10): Specify the years or date range for which you are requesting records.
* **Records to be Released** (12): Check all the records you are requesting (e.g., approved building permits, inspection reports, etc.).
* **Intended Purpose for Records** (13): Enter the purpose for which you are requesting the records (e.g., sale of property, legal review, etc.).
* **I have obtained owner consent for this release** (14): Confirm if you have obtained the owner's consent for this release.
* **Agreement to Maintain Confidentiality** (16): Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality of sensitive information in the records.
* **Preferred Method of Delivery** (17): Choose your preferred method of delivery (digital, physical, in-person).
* **Acknowledgment of Possible Search/Copy Fees** (18): Acknowledge that you understand and agree to pay the search and copy fees.
* **Additional Search Instructions or Notes** (19): Provide any additional instructions or notes about your request.
* **Date of Authorization** (20): Date and time of submission.
