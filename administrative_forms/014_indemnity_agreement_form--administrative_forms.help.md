# Indemnity Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Indemnity Agreement Form is used to record and store agreements between parties, including details such as user names, dates, and contractual terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and date in the designated fields.
2.  Select the correct payment terms from the options provided.
3.  Enter the name and contact information of the responsible party.
4.  Choose the correct contract type and status.
5.  Fill in the contract effective and end dates.
6.  Enter the contract duration and renewal term (if applicable).
7.  Choose the agreement status and version.
8.  Enter any additional contract notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the agreement was made or will be effective.
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, `number`, required): This field is used to record the term of the contract, i.e., the length of time the agreement is in effect.
* **Liability Limit** (`liability_limit`, `number`, required): This field is used to record any liability limits or conditions agreed upon in the contract.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the type of payment terms agreed upon in the contract.
* **Other Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_other`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select any additional payment terms not listed in the previous field.
*   **Description of Other Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_other_description`, `note`, optional): This field is used to describe any additional payment terms selected in the previous field.
*   **Responsible Party** (`responsible_party`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the name of the responsible party for the contract.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the name of the contact person for the responsible party.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the contact number for the responsible party.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to record the email address of the contact person.
*   **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the version of the agreement.
*   **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the status of the agreement (Active, Pending, or Draft).
*   **Agreement Status Date** (`agreement_status_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the agreement status was last updated.
*   **Indemnity Agreement** (`indemnity_agreement`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the indemnity agreement details.
*   **Indemnity Agreement Version** (`indemnity_agreement_version`, `text`, required): This field is used to record the version of the indemnity agreement.
*   **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the type of contract (Option 1 or Option 2).
*   **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the status of the contract (Option 1 or Option 2).
*   **Contract Effective Date** (`contract_effective_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the contract becomes effective.
*   **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the end date of the contract.
*   **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, `number`, required): This field is used to record the duration of the contract.
*   **Renewal Term** (`renewal_term`, `number`, optional): This field is used to record the term for renewal of the contract.
*   **Contract Status Date** (`contract_status_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the contract status was last updated.
*   **Contract Term Duration** (`contract_term_duration`, `number`, optional): This field is used to record the duration of the contract term.
*   **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, `note`, optional): This field is used to record any additional contract notes.
