# Acknowledgment Of Policy Handbook - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm an employee's understanding and acknowledgment of the company's policy handbook. It ensures that employees have reviewed and comprehend the policies outlined in the handbook.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your employee name, department, and handbook version.
2. Choose whether you have read and understand the handbook or if you have read but do not understand.
3. Sign the form electronically by typing your full name.
4. Date the electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as listed in HR.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your internal identifier, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team name.
* **Handbook version** (`handbook_version`, text, required): Enter the version or date of the handbook reviewed.
* **Date reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, required): Enter the date you reviewed the handbook.
* **Understanding confirmation** (`understanding_confirmation`, select_one, required): Declare whether you have read and understand the handbook or if you have read but do not understand.
* **Questions for HR** (`questions_for_hr`, text, optional): Enter any questions or clarifications you may have about the handbook.
* **Electronic signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name to confirm acknowledgment.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date of your electronic signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the policy handbook carefully before completing this form.
- Ensure that you understand the policies outlined in the handbook.
- If you have any questions or concerns, use the "Questions for HR" field to clarify.
- Sign the form electronically and date it to confirm your acknowledgment.
