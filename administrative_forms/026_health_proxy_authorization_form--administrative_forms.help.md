# Health Proxy Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to designate a health proxy for an individual who is unable to make their own medical decisions. A health proxy is an individual authorized to make medical decisions on behalf of another person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
2. Enter the last name of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the individual has a medical condition.
4. If the individual has a medical condition, enter the medical condition.
5. Enter the contact number of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
6. Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
7. Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
8. Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the individual.
9. Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
10. Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the individual (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
* **Relationship**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the individual has a medical condition.
* **Medical Condition**: If the individual has a medical condition, enter the medical condition.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the individual who needs to be designated as their health proxy.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the individual (e.g. parent, spouse, guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Individual)**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact to the individual (e.g. parent, spouse, guardian). 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this will help ensure that the individual's needs are met.

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
