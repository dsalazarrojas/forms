# Educational Support Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Support Records Release Form is used to request access to a student's records for educational purposes. This includes requesting records from a school or institution where the student was enrolled, attended, or worked. This form helps to ensure that the necessary information is provided to the correct person or organization, while also maintaining student confidentiality and protection of personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Student Name, School ID, and Date SIGNED fields.
2. Select the appropriate option for Consent.
3. Provide your signature in the Signature field.
4. If necessary, provide additional information in the Reason for Request field.
5. Select the Institution Name if you are requesting records from a specific institution.
6. Optionally, provide additional contact information in the Department, Contact Number, Email, Address, and Fax fields.
7. Enter any notes or comments in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: This is the name of the student or individual requesting access to their records.
* **School ID**: This is the identification number or code assigned to the school or institution where the student attended.
* **Consent**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you consent to release your records and "No" to indicate that you do not. This ensures that you are aware of and approve of the release of your records.
* **Signature**: This is where you sign to confirm your consent to release your records.
* **Date SIGNED**: This is where you enter the date you sign this form.
* **Reason for Request**: This is an optional field to provide additional context or information about your request.
* **Institution Name**: If you are requesting records from a specific institution, select the name from the drop-down menu.
* **Department**: This is an optional field to provide additional information about the department or organization requesting the records.
* **Contact Number**: This is an optional field to provide contact information for the person or organization requesting the records.
* **Email**: This is an optional field to provide email contact information for the person or organization requesting the records.
* **Address**: This is an optional field to provide address contact information for the person or organization requesting the records.
* **Fax**: This is an optional field to provide fax contact information for the person or organization requesting the records.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field to enter any additional comments or information about your request.

Note: Please be aware that providing false or incomplete information on this form may result in delays or denial of record release.
