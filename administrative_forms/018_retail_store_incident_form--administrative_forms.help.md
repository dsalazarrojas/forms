# Retail Store Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document incidents that have occurred at retail stores, allowing for efficient tracking and investigation of these incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your employee's name and incident date to provide a brief overview of the incident.
2. Enter the store where the incident occurred, as well as a detailed description of what happened.
3. Select the type of incident that best describes what transpired, and list any employees involved.
4. Provide any witness statements or comments related to the incident.
5. Choose the status of the investigation and resolution of the incident.
6. Select the relevant personnel who will be involved in the followup on the incident.
7. Finally, review and verify the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the employee who was involved in or witnessed the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident to help track and date it.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident to help schedule and time it.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the incident, such as a specific area of the store.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select from the options provided to describe the type of incident. This will help the investigators determine the necessary procedures.
* **Employee Involvement** (`employee_involvement`, select_multiple, required): List all employees involved in the incident, as this will help in understanding their roles and responsibilities.
* **Witness List** (`witness_list`, text, optional): Provide a list of employees who witnessed the incident. This will help in gathering evidence and testimonies.
* **Investigation Status** (`investigation_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the investigation, such as whether it is ongoing, resolved, or pending.
* **Incident Image** (`incident_image`, text, optional): Attach any relevant images or videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (`incident_video`, text, optional): Attach any videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Attachment** (`incident_attachment`, text, optional): Attach any other relevant documents or files related to the incident.
* **Incident Note** (`incident_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Followup** (`incident_followup`, date, optional): Enter the date and time for follow-up on the incident.
* **Incident Followup Time** (`incident_followup_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for follow-up on the incident.
* **Incident Closed** (`incident_closed`, select_one, required): Choose whether the incident has been closed or resolved.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the incident, such as active, closed, or pending.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Escalation** (`incident_escalation`, select_one, required): Choose the level of escalation, such as minor or major.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, text, optional): Enter any resolution details related to the incident.
* **Incident Reviewer** (`incident_reviewer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the incident.
* **Incident Comments** (`incident_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the incident.

# Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, reach out to the relevant personnel or management.
- Review your input before submitting to ensure it meets your needs and the needs of the investigation.
- Use the fields provided to ensure that you capture all necessary information about the incident. 

Note: This help guide is designed to be comprehensive, yet concise, to facilitate efficient and accurate use of the retail store incident form.
