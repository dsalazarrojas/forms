# union_leader_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The union leader information form is designed to collect and update contact and demographic information for union leaders. It's essential to complete this form accurately to avoid duplication of records and ensure that your union leader information is up-to-date in our database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and ensure you're accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Enter the union leader's name in the "Union Leader Name" field.
3.  Enter the union leader's email address in the "Union Leader Email" field. Ensure it's in the correct format.
4.  Enter the union leader's phone number in the "Union Leader Phone" field.
5.  Enter the union leader's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6.  Enter the union leader's title in the "Union Leader Title" field.
7.  Enter the name of the union in the "Union" field.
8.  Enter the union ID in the "Union ID" field.
9.  Enter the union leader's address in the "Union Leader Address" field.
10. Enter the union leader's city in the "Union Leader City" field.
11. Enter the union leader's province in the "Union Leader Province" field.
12. Enter the union leader's country in the "Union Leader Country" field.
13. Enter the union leader's postal code in the "Union Leader Postal Code" field.

*   **Union Leader Name** (Union Leader Name, Required): This is the union leader's full name.
*   **Union Leader Email** (Union Leader Email, Required): Enter the union leader's email address in the format [email address].
*   **Union Leader Phone** (Union Leader Phone, Required): Enter the union leader's phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Required): Enter the union leader's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Union Leader Title** (Union Leader Title, Required): This is the union leader's title or position.
*   **Union** (Union, Required): Enter the name of the union the leader is associated with.
*   **Union ID** (Union ID, Required): Enter the union leader's union ID.
*   **Union Leader Address** (Union Leader Address, Required): Enter the union leader's complete address.
*   **Union Leader City** (Union Leader City, Required): Enter the union leader's city or town name.
*   **Union Leader Province** (Union Leader Province, Required): Enter the union leader's province or state name.
*   **Union Leader Country** (Union Leader Country, Required): Enter the union leader's country name.
*   **Union Leader Postal Code** (Union Leader Postal Code, Required): Enter the union leader's postal or zip code.

## Tips
