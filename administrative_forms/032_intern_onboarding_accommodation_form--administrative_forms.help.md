# Intern Onboarding Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the new intern's onboarding process, including their accommodation preferences and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on the designated page.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Select your preferred accommodation type in the "Accommodation Type" field.
5. Choose your preferred location from the list of available options in the "Preferred Location" field.
6. Enter the date you would like to check-in in the "Check-in Date" field.
7. Enter the date you would like to check-out in the "Check-out Date" field.
8. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`surname`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of accommodation you prefer, such as "house", "apartment", or "hotel".
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the location you prefer from the list of available options.
* **Check-in Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to check-in on.
* **Check-out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to check-out on.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number for any last-minute updates or concerns.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have a valid reason for filling out this form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, feel free to ask your HR representative for assistance.
