# Checklist For New Items - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Checklist For New Items" form is used to ensure that all necessary tasks and steps have been completed before launching a new item. This includes reviewing and confirming the project's progress in various aspects such as project management, quality testing, documentation, marketing, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the project name and project manager's name.
2. Fill in the planned launch date.
3. Select the department responsible for the project.
4. Choose the type of item being launched.
5. Confirm the status of requirements documentation, stakeholder approval, budget allocation, resources assignment, quality testing, documentation completion, marketing material preparation, staff training, legal compliance review, risk assessment, supply chain verification, customer support preparation, and full launch checklist review.
6. If there are any outstanding items, list them in the "Outstanding Items" field.
7. Make a final go or no-go decision on launch readiness.
8. Enter the reviewer's name and the review date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Name** (Project Name, text, required): Enter the name of the project or product launch.
  * This field is used to identify the project.
- **Project Manager** (Project Manager, text, required): Enter the name of the project manager.
  * This field is used to track the project's manager.
- **Planned Launch Date** (Planned Launch Date, date, required): Enter the target date for launch or release.
  * This field is used to track the project's timeline.
- **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Select the department responsible for this item.
  * This field is used to track the department responsible for the project.
- **Item Category** (Item Category, select_one, required): Select the type of item being launched.
  * This field is used to categorize the item being launched.
- **Requirements Documented** (Requirements Documented, select_one, required): Confirm whether all requirements have been documented.
  * This field is used to track the requirements documentation status.
- **Stakeholder Approval** (Stakeholder Approval, select_one, required): Confirm whether all stakeholders have approved the item.
  * This field is used to track stakeholder approval status.
- **Budget Allocated** (Budget Allocated, select_one, required): Confirm whether budget has been allocated for this item.
  * This field is used to track budget allocation status.
- **Resources Assigned** (Resources Assigned, select_one, required): Confirm whether necessary resources have been assigned for this item.
  * This field is used to track resource assignment status.
- **Quality Testing Complete** (Quality Testing Complete, select_one, required): Confirm whether quality testing has been completed.
  * This field is used to track quality testing status.
- **Documentation Complete** (Documentation Complete, select_one, required): Confirm whether all documentation is complete.
  * This field is used to track documentation status.
- **Marketing Materials Ready** (Marketing Materials Ready, select_one, required): Confirm whether marketing materials are prepared.
  * This field is used to track marketing material preparation status.
- **Staff Training Completed** (Staff Training Completed, select_one, required): Confirm whether staff has been trained.
  * This field is used to track staff training status.
- **Legal Compliance Review** (Legal Compliance Review, select_one, required): Confirm whether legal compliance has been reviewed.
  * This field is used to track legal compliance review status.
- **Risk Assessment Done** (Risk Assessment Done, select_one, required): Confirm whether a risk assessment has been conducted.
  * This field is used to track risk assessment status.
- **Supply Chain Verified** (Supply Chain Verified, select_one, required): Confirm whether supply chain has been verified.
  * This field is used to track supply chain verification status.
- **Customer Support Ready** (Customer Support Ready, select_one, required): Confirm whether customer support is prepared for launch.
  * This field is used to track customer support preparation status.
- **Launch Checklist Reviewed** (Launch Checklist Reviewed, select_one, required): Confirm whether the full launch checklist has been reviewed.
  * This field is used to track launch checklist review status.
- **Outstanding Items** (Outstanding Items, text, optional): List any items still pending.
  * This field is used to track outstanding items.
- **Go or No-Go Decision** (Go or No-Go Decision, select_one, required): Make a final decision on launch readiness.
  * This field is used to make a final decision on launch readiness.
- **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing this checklist.
  * This field is used to track the reviewer's name.
- **Review Date** (Review Date, date, required): Enter the date of this review.
  * This field is used to track the review date.

## Tips
* Always review the checklist carefully to ensure that all necessary steps have been completed before making a go or no-go decision.
* Make sure to list any outstanding items that still need to be addressed.
