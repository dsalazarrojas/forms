# Donated Goods Delivery Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donated Goods Delivery Tracking Form is a tool used to record the details of donated goods deliveries, ensuring accurate and efficient tracking of these vital resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the delivery date in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD', which is the date when the delivery occurred or is scheduled to occur.
2. Type in the delivery location, which is the place where the delivery took place or is planned to occur.
3. Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for making the delivery.
4. Provide the type of vehicle used for the delivery, such as a car, truck, or other mode of transportation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **delivery_date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the delivery occurred or is scheduled to occur in the 'YYYY-MM-DD' format.
- **delivery_location** (`delivery_location`, text, required: false): Type in the place where the delivery took place or is planned to occur.
- **delivery_person** (`delivery_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for making the delivery.
- **delivery_vehicle** (`delivery_vehicle`, text, required: false): Provide the type of vehicle used for the delivery, such as a car, truck, or other mode of transportation.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information entered in each field to ensure accuracy.
- If a delivery date is scheduled, enter the date when the delivery will occur.
- If no specific vehicle was used, you can enter 'No vehicle used' or similar.
- Save and submit the form once you have completed it to record your delivery details accurately and efficiently.
