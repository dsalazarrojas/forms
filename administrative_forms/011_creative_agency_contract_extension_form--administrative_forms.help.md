# Creative Agency Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Agency Contract Extension Form is a tool designed to facilitate the processing of contract extension requests from clients. This form collects essential information needed to review and approve or reject contract extension requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in the client's **name** in the "Client Name" field. This is the name of the client whose contract you are requesting to extend.
2.  Enter the **contract ID** associated with the client's contract. This is the unique identifier for the contract you are working with.
3.  Select the type of contract from the drop-down menu next to "Contract Type." This indicates the type of contract being extended.
4.  Enter the **start date** of the contract, which is the date the contract was initially supposed to start.
5.  Enter the **end date** of the contract, which is the date the contract is currently set to end.
6.  Provide the client's **email address** in the "Client Email" field. This is their official email address where communication can be directed.
7.  Fill in the client's **phone number** in the "Client Phone" field. This is their direct contact number for any urgent matters.
8.  Enter any **agency terms** that apply to this contract extension request in the "Agency Terms" field. This includes any specific conditions or requirements imposed by the agency on the client.
9.  Enter any **client terms** that the client has agreed upon in the "Client Terms" field. These are the conditions or requirements agreed upon by the client.
10.  Select the current **contract status** from the drop-down menu next to "Contract Status." This indicates whether the contract is active, inactive, or pending.
11.  Enter the **payment terms** that apply to this contract in the "Payment Terms" field. This includes any payment details or conditions associated with the contract.
12.  Select the method of **payment** from the drop-down menu next to "Payment Method." This indicates how the payment will be made, such as by bank transfer or invoice.
13.  Enter the **date of payment** for the payment made or due for the contract.
14.  Add any **additional notes** or comments relevant to the contract in the "Notes" field. This is a free space for any extra information.
15.  Enter the **start time** of the contract, which is the time it was initially supposed to start.
16.  Enter the **end time** of the contract, which is the time it is currently set to end.
17.  Fill in the client's **address** in the "Client Address" field. This is their official address for any official communications or deliveries.
18.  Fill in the agency's **address** in the "Agency Address" field. This is the agency's official address for any official communications or deliveries.
19.  Lastly, select the **payment status** from the drop-down menu next to "Payment Status." This indicates whether the payment is pending or has been made.
