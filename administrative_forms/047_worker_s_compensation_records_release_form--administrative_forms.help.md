# Workers Compensation Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workers Compensation Records Release Form is a request for an employer to release workers' compensation records related to an employee. This form is used to facilitate access to relevant information for the employee's personal records, new employment opportunities, or other valid reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Name field with the name of the person whose records you are requesting.
2. Enter the Employee Job Title field with the job title of the employee whose records you are requesting.
3. Enter the Employee Job Start Date field with the date the employee started their current or previous job.
4. Enter the Employer Name field with the name of the employer whose records you are requesting.
5. Enter the Employer Address field with the address of the employer whose records you are requesting.
6. Enter the Employee Phone field with the phone number of the employee whose records you are requesting.
7. Enter the Employer Phone field with the phone number of the employer whose records you are requesting.
8. Enter the Employee Email field with the email address of the employee whose records you are requesting.
9. Enter the Employer Email field with the email address of the employer whose records you are requesting.
10. Enter the Authorization Date field with the date you are authorizing the release of the records.
11. Enter the Authorized Person field with the name of the person authorized to access the records.
12. Enter the Reason for Request field with a brief description of why you are requesting the records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: The name of the person whose workers' compensation records you are requesting.
* **Employee Job Title**: The job title of the employee whose records you are requesting.
* **Employee Job Start Date**: The date the employee started their current or previous job.
* **Employer Name**: The name of the employer whose records you are requesting.
* **Employer Address**: The address of the employer whose records you are requesting.
* **Employee Phone**: The phone number of the employee whose records you are requesting.
* **Employer Phone**: The phone number of the employer whose records you are requesting.
* **Employee Email**: The email address of the employee whose records you are requesting.
* **Employer Email**: The email address of the employer whose records you are requesting.
* **Authorization Date**: The date you are authorizing the release of the records.
* **Authorized Person**: The name of the person authorized to access the records.
* **Reason for Request**: A brief description of why you are requesting the records.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the records are released for the correct person and reason.
* Double-check that you are authorized to request the records before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble completing the form or have questions, contact your HR representative for assistance.
