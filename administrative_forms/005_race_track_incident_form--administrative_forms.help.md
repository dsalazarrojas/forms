# Race Track Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect incident reports for racing events to ensure accurate and timely reporting of any incidents that may have occurred during events.

## How To Complete This Form

Please follow the steps outlined above and ensure that all required fields are completed. If you are unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or incident report administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Type**: Select the type of incident that occurred during the event.
*   **Event ID**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the event where the incident took place.
*   **Incident Date**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
*   **Incident Location**: Provide a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Narrative**: Write a concise summary of what happened during the incident.
*   **Vehicle Involved Parties**: Indicate whether vehicles were involved in the incident. If yes, select "Yes".
*   **Race Official Involved**: Indicate whether race officials were involved in the incident. If yes, select "Yes".
*   **Medical Treatment**: Indicate the need for medical treatment. If yes, select "Yes".
*   **Injury Type**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm or deny the presence of an injury.
*   **Incident Reported By**: Select whether the incident was reported by phone or not.
*   **Incident Reported By Phone**: If reported by phone, provide the phone number used.
*   **Incident Reported By Email**: If reported by email, provide the email address used.
*   **Race Track Name**: Enter the name of the race track where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Reported By Name**: Provide the name of the person who reported the incident.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the incident report.
*   Ensure that all required fields are completed.
*   If unsure about any field, consult with a supervisor or incident report administrator.
*   Save the completed form for future reference and review.
