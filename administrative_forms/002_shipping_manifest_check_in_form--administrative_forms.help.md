# Shipping Manifest Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Manifest Check In Form is used by warehouse staff to check in shipments from suppliers. It helps ensure that all shipments are properly tracked and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the manifest number of the shipment.
2. Enter the name of the carrier that delivered the shipment.
3. Enter the date and time the shipment was delivered.
4. Enter the location from which the shipment was sent.
5. Enter the location to which the shipment was sent.
6. Add any relevant notes about the shipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Data** (`shipping_manifest_data`, text, required/optional): This field is where you enter any additional information about the shipment that is not listed in the other fields. Be as specific as possible, as this information will be useful for future reference.
* **Manifest Number** (`manifest_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for this shipment, as assigned by the supplier or shipping company.
* **Carrier Name** (`carrier_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the company or organization that delivered the shipment.
* **Shipment Date** (`shipment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the shipment was delivered.
* **Shipment Time** (`shipment_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the shipment was delivered.
* **Ship From Location** (`ship_from_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location from which the shipment was sent.
* **Ship To Location** (`ship_to_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location to which the shipment was sent.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the shipment that may be useful for future reference.

## Tips
* Double-check the manifest number and carrier name to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to enter specific dates and times for the shipment.
* Use clear and concise language in the notes field to provide useful information.
