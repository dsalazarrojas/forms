# Equipment Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a maintenance request for company equipment. It captures key information about the equipment, the type of maintenance needed, and the contact details for communication.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
* Enter the equipment ID number to help us locate the equipment.
* Provide a brief description of the equipment and its location.
* Specify who requested the maintenance, their role, and their contact information.
* Select the date when the maintenance was requested.
* Choose who the request has been assigned to.
* Select the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* Add any comments or notes related to the maintenance request.
* Enter the contact email and phone number for further communication.
* Finally, confirm that the request has been submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request**: Enter a brief description of the maintenance request.
* **Equipment ID**: Enter the unique ID number of the equipment, which helps us locate it quickly.
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment, including its location.
* **Requested By**: Enter your name and title or position, or the name and title of the person or department that requested the maintenance.
* **Requested Date**: Enter the date when the maintenance was originally requested.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person or team responsible for handling the maintenance request.
* **Status**: Choose the status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes related to the maintenance request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person or department that we can contact for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or department that we can contact for further information.
* **Submit**: Confirm that the request has been submitted for processing.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting a request.
* Double-check the details for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the equipment management team.
