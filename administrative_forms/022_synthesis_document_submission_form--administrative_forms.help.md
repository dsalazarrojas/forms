# Synthesis Document Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Synthesis Document Submission Form is used to gather information from submitters regarding a synthesis document submission. This form is used to collect data on the submitter, collaborator, synthesis document type, submission date, submission time, and submitter notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* **Submitter**: Provide your name and any additional information about yourself.
	* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Collaborator Name**: If there are any collaborators involved in the submission, enter their name.
	* **Collaborator Email**: Enter the collaborator's email address.
	* **Collaborator Phone**: Enter the collaborator's phone number.
	* **Synthesis Document**: Provide a brief description of the synthesis document.
	* **Synthesis Document Type**: Select the type of synthesis document being submitted (e.g. report, paper, etc.).
	* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the document.
	* **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the document.
	* **Submitter Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.
	* **Synthesis Document URL**: If the synthesis document is publicly available, provide the URL.
	* **Synthesis Document URL Title**: Enter the title of the document as it appears on the URL.
	* **Synthesis Document URL Description**: Enter a brief description of the document as it appears on the URL.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out. The submitter name, email, and phone number are all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate.
