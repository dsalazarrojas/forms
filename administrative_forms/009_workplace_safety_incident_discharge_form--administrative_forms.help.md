# Workplace Safety Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document information about workplace safety incidents. The goal is to provide a structured format for employees to report and describe incidents that occur within the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the **Incident Date** field.
2. Provide your name in the **Employee Name** field.
3. Describe the incident in detail in the **Incident Description** field.
4. (Optional) Enter the time of the incident if it's relevant to the case.
5. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the **Incident Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Provide your name as the employee who experienced or witnessed the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Describe what happened during the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): (Optional) Enter the time of the incident if it's relevant to the case.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location where the incident occurred.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, it's best to err on the side of caution and include as much detail as you can provide.
* If the incident involves a third party, you should report the incident to your supervisor or HR representative immediately.
* If you need assistance or clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
