# Renters Insurance Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from renters to provide them with insurance coverage. It is a simple and straightforward form that requires basic contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to use a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used to communicate with you about your insurance coverage.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This will help us verify your identity.
