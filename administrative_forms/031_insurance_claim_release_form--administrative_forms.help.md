# Insurance Claim Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals who are releasing their insurance claims. It is a simple, one-page form designed to gather necessary details for claim release purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your claimant information**: Fill in the "Claimant Information" section with your name, address, contact number, and other relevant details.
2. **Provide claim details**: In the "Claim Details" section, describe your insurance claim, including dates, amounts, and any other relevant information.
3. **Identify the authorized party**: In the "Authorized Party" section, provide the name and contact information of the person authorized to release your insurance claim.
4. **Sign and date**: Both you and the authorized party must sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
5. **Submit the form**: Once complete, submit the form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Claimant Information
* **Claimant Information** (`claimant_information`, text, required): This field is for you to enter your name, address, contact number, and other relevant details.
### Claim Details
* **Claim Details** (`claim_details`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your insurance claim, including dates, amounts, and any other relevant information.
### Authorized Party
* **Authorized Party** (`authorized_party`, text, required): In this field, enter the name and contact information of the person authorized to release your insurance claim.
### Claimant Signature
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
### Authorized Party Signature
* **Authorized Party Signature** (`authorized_party_signature`, text, required): Sign and date this field to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
### Insurance Provider Information
* **Insurance Provider Information** (`insurance_provider_information`, text, optional): This field is for the insurance provider's name, address, contact number, and other relevant details (if applicable).

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for official insurance purposes.
