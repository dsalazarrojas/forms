# work_platform_control_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "work_platform_control_checklist" is a checklist used to assess and document the control measures implemented on a work platform to ensure safety and regulatory compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of platform you are inspecting (platform_type).
2. Enter the location of the work platform (work_location).
3. Answer whether personnel are on board the platform (personnel_on_board).
4. List any control measures implemented on the platform (control_measures).
5. Record the date and time of the inspection (inspection_date and inspection_time).
6. Describe any observations or hazards noted during the inspection (observations).
7. List any safety measures implemented on the platform (safety_measures).
8. Describe any follow-up actions needed on the platform (follow_up_actions).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Platform Type** (`platform_type`, select_one): Choose the type of platform you are inspecting (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text): Enter the location of the work platform (e.g., "123 Main Street").
* **Personnel on Board** (`personnel_on_board`, select multiple): Check if personnel are on board the platform.
* **Control Measures** (`control_measures`, note): List any control measures implemented on the platform (e.g., "Regular maintenance schedules", "Warning signs posted").
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date): Record the date of the inspection in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time): Record the time of the inspection in 24-hour format (e.g., "14:30").
* **Observations** (`observations`, text): Describe any observations or hazards noted during the inspection.
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, text): List any safety measures implemented on the platform (e.g., "Regular fire drills").
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text): Describe any follow-up actions needed on the platform (e.g., "Repair damaged equipment").
