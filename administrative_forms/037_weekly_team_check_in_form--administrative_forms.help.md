# Weekly Team Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help team members submit their weekly progress, challenges, and achievements to the team lead. It allows the team lead to track individual progress, identify challenges, and acknowledge achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form thoroughly and accurately.
- Each field should be filled out only once per week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User** (`user`, text, optional): This field is for entering your name or username for identification purposes.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the current date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about your progress, challenges, or achievements.
- **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced this week.
- **Achievements** (`achievements`, select multiple, optional): Select all the achievements you accomplished this week. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Team Members** (`team members`, select multiple, optional): Select all the team members you collaborated with this week. You can choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
