# Volunteer Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your volunteer experience and status. Completing this form helps us maintain accurate records and understand your involvement with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Enter your volunteer role as Program Volunteer, Program Leader, or Program Assistant, as relevant.
3. Select your volunteer status as Active or Inactive, as relevant.
4. Provide a brief note about your experience as a volunteer in the text field.
5. Enter your last volunteer date in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information** (`text`, required/optional): This is a general field for any additional information you'd like to provide about your volunteer experience.
* **What is your volunteer role?** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of volunteer role you held (Program Volunteer, Program Leader, or Program Assistant).
* **What is your volunteer status?** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select your current volunteer status (Active or Inactive).
* **What would you like to say about your experience as a volunteer?** (`note`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your experience as a volunteer.
* **What is your last volunteer date?** (`date`, required/optional): Enter your last volunteer date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Form 2** (`text`, required/optional): This is the second page of the form. Please do not complete this field, as it is not relevant to the current form.

**Tips**
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- If you have any other questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
