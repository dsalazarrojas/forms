# Stakeholder Communication Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stakeholder Communication Checklist is a form used to collect and document communication with stakeholders. It is essential to ensure that all relevant information is collected and that follow-up actions are taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the project status and stakeholder relationship fields are filled out accurately.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked with an *) first.
3. Provide the stakeholder's name and contact information.
4. Choose the meeting type and date from the corresponding dropdown menus.
5. Select the communication channels used for the meeting (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
6. Document the meeting outcomes and note any next steps.
7. Choose the follow-up status and frequency.
8. Provide a brief note on the follow-up action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stakeholder Name** (*required*): The stakeholder's name is the primary contact person for communication.
* **Meeting Date** (*required*): The date of the meeting.
* **Stakeholder Email** (*optional*): The stakeholder's email address for communication.
* **Stakeholder Phone** (*optional*): The stakeholder's phone number for communication.
* **Meeting Note** (*optional*): A brief note on the meeting's discussion.
* **Stakeholder Message** (*optional*): Any additional message to the stakeholder.
* **Meeting Time** (*optional*): The time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (*optional*): The location of the meeting.
* **Follow-up Date** (*optional*): The date to follow-up on the meeting.
* **Next Steps** (*optional*): Any next steps or actions to take based on the meeting.
* **Follow-up Note** (*optional*): A brief note on the follow-up action taken.
* **Follow-up Phone** (*optional*): The phone number to follow up with the stakeholder.
* **Meeting Attendees** (*optional*): A list of meeting attendees.
* **Meeting Outcomes** (*optional*): A brief summary of the meeting's outcomes.
* **Communication Channels** (*required*): The channels used for communication (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **Meeting Type** (*required*): The type of meeting (e.g., regular, ad-hoc, virtual).
* **Project Status** (*required*): The current project status.
* **Stakeholder Relationship** (*required*): The type of relationship with the stakeholder (e.g., partner, customer, employee).
* **Stakeholder Priority** (*required*): The priority level of the stakeholder (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Meeting Duration** (*optional*): The duration of the meeting.
* **Meeting Frequency** (*optional*): The frequency of meetings with the stakeholder.
* **Stakeholder Notes** (*optional*): Any additional notes on the stakeholder.
* **Follow-up Status** (*required*): The status of the follow-up action (e.g., done, pending).
* **Follow-up Frequency** (*required*): The frequency of follow-up actions (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Stakeholder Category** (*required*): The category of the stakeholder (e.g., internal, external, customer, partner).
