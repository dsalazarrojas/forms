# Shift Handover Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Handover Validation Form is designed to document the details of a shift handover, ensuring a smooth transition of responsibilities and tasks between team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the date of the shift in the "Shift Date" field.
3. Enter the start and end times of the shift in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields respectively.
4. Enter the length of the shift in the "Shift Length" field.
5. Enter any necessary "Handover Notes" for the shift.
6. Choose the type of shift in the "Shift Type" field (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night).
7. Select the personnel involved in the handover in the "Handover Personnel" and "Handover Personnel 2" fields.
8. Enter any previous handover notes or next handover notes if applicable.
9. Enter any comments or observations about the handover in the "Handover Comments" field.
10. Sign the "Handover Signature" field to confirm the handover.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee handing over the shift.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the shift.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the shift.
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the shift.
* **Handover Notes** (`handover_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the handover.
* **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of shift (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Handover Personnel** (`handover_personnel`, select_multiple, optional): Select the personnel involved in the handover.
* **Previous Handover Notes** (`previous_handover_notes`, text, optional): Enter any previous handover notes.
* **Next Handover Notes** (`next_handover_notes`, text, optional): Enter any next handover notes.
* **Handover Personnel 2** (`handover_personnel_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the personnel involved in the handover.
* **Next Handover Personnel** (`next_handover_personnel`, select_multiple, optional): Select the next personnel involved in the handover.
* **Handover Comments** (`handover_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or observations about the handover.
* **Handover Signature** (`handover_signature`, text, optional): Sign the handover to confirm its completion.
