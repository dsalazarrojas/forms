# pony_club_incident_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents that have occurred within the pony club. It helps in keeping track of incidents, their details, and any actions taken or assigned to specific individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Club Id" field with the unique ID of the club where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields respectively.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
4. Mention the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
5. List the participants involved in the incident in the "Incident Participants" field.
6. Classify the type of incident as "Yes" or "No" in the "Incident Type" field.
7. Specify the outcome of the incident in the "Incident Outcome" field.
8. Describe the action taken or planned to be taken in the "Action Taken" field.
9. Choose the status of the incident from the options: "Active", "Completed", or "Pending" in the "Incident Status" field.
10. Assign the incident to a specific individual or group in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Club Id** (1): Enter the unique ID of the club where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date** (2): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (3): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Description** (4): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (5): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Participants** (6): List the individuals or groups involved in the incident.
* **Incident Type** (7): Classify the type of incident as "Yes" or "No".
* **Incident Outcome** (8): Specify the outcome of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (9): Describe the action taken or planned to be taken.
* **Incident Status** (10): Choose the status of the incident from "Active", "Completed", or "Pending".
* **Assigned To** (11): Assign the incident to a specific individual or group.
