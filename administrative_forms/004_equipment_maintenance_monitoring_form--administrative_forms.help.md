# equipment_maintenance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Maintenance Monitoring Form is used to track and monitor the maintenance status of various equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the equipment you are maintaining and decide on the relevant fields that apply.
2. Select the maintenance status of the equipment (Active or Inactive).
3. Choose the maintenance schedule (Yes or No).
4. Record the cost of the maintenance.
5. Enter the date of the last maintenance performed.
6. Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
7. Record the maintenance frequency (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Maintenance Frequency**: Enter the frequency at which the equipment requires maintenance. This can be a numerical value to represent the number of times per year, month, or week the equipment requires maintenance.
* **Equipment Maintenance Status**: Select the status of the equipment's maintenance (Active or Inactive). This indicates whether the equipment is currently being maintained or not.
* **Equipment Maintenance Schedule**: Choose whether the equipment has a scheduled maintenance (Yes or No). This indicates whether a maintenance schedule is in place for the equipment.
* **Equipment Maintenance Cost**: Enter the cost associated with maintaining the equipment. This can include labor costs, parts replacement, or other expenses.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment. This helps track the equipment's maintenance history.
* **Next Maintenance Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance for the equipment. This helps plan and schedule future maintenance.
* **Equipment Maintenance Frequency 2**: Enter the frequency at which the equipment requires maintenance (if applicable). This can be a numerical value to represent the number of times per year, month, or week the equipment requires maintenance.
