# water_service_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The water service information form is used to provide essential contact and address information for water service purposes. It's crucial to fill out the form accurately to ensure proper communication and billing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the form for the correct purpose.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "true" in the form.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter your personal or company name for identification purposes.
* **Street Address (id: 2)**: Enter the full address where your water service will be delivered or sent.
* **Postal Code (id: 3)**: Enter the postal code associated with your address for proper delivery.
* **City (id: 4)**: Enter the city where your address is located.
* **Country (id: 5)**: Enter the country where your address is located.
* **Email (id: 6)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (id: 7)**: Enter your contact phone number for quick communication.
* **Contact Person (id: 8)**: Select the person responsible for managing your water service. Choose from the available options: John, Jane, or Bob.

## Tips
- Double-check your address and contact information for accuracy to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant department for assistance.
- If the form is incomplete or requires further clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to the support team for help.
