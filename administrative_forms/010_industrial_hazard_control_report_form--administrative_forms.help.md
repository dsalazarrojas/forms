# industrial_hazard_control_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report industrial hazards that require immediate attention to prevent accidents or injuries. The form is a crucial tool for ensuring that hazards are identified, reported, and addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the company name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter the location where the hazard was identified.
3. Enter your job title, which is your position within the company.
4. Enter the unique identifier for the hazard.
5. Enter the level of the hazard, which indicates its severity.
6. Enter the date when you identified the hazard.
7. Enter the date when the report was submitted.
8. Select the type of hazard that was identified (e.g., Fire, Chemical, Physical, Biological, Electrical).
9. Provide a detailed description of the hazard.
10. Describe the corrective action taken to address the hazard.
11. Choose the method used to prevent recurrence of the hazard (e.g., Regular inspections, Safety training, Equipment maintenance, Engineering controls, Other).
12. If desired, provide any additional preventive measures that were taken.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the hazard.
14. Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
15. Enter the name of the person reviewing the report (if applicable).
16. Enter the name of the person approving the report (if applicable).
17. Enter the date when the report was approved.
18. Enter the date when the report was submitted.
19. Enter the date when the report was completed.
20. Select the current status of the report (e.g., Open, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate, Closed).
21. Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the hazard.
22. If desired, provide any follow-up comments or actions.
23. Enter the date for the follow-up action.
24. Select the status of the follow-up action (e.g., Open, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate, Closed).
25. Enter the name of the person assigned to the follow-up action (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company as it appears on your official documents.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the hazard was identified.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Hazard Id** (`hazard_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the hazard.
* **Hazard Level** (`hazard_level`, number, optional): Enter the level of the hazard, which indicates its severity.
* **Date Identified** (`date_identified`, date, required): Enter the date when you identified the hazard.
* **Date Reported** (`date_reported`, date, required): Enter the date when the report was submitted.
* **Hazard Type** (`hazard_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of hazard that was identified (e.g., Fire, Chemical, Physical, Biological, Electrical).
* **Hazard Description** (`hazard_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the hazard.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, required): Describe the corrective action taken to address the hazard.
* **Prevention Method** (`prevention_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method used to prevent recurrence of the hazard (e.g., Regular inspections, Safety training, Equipment maintenance, Engineering controls, Other).
* **Preventive Measures** (`preventive_measures`, text, optional): If desired, provide any additional preventive measures taken.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the hazard.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reviewing the report (if applicable).
* **Approver** (`approver`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person approving the report (if applicable).
* **Approved Date** (`approved_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the report was approved.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the report was submitted.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the report was completed.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the report (e.g., Open, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate, Closed).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the hazard.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): If desired, provide any follow-up comments or actions.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the follow-up action (e.g., Open, In progress, Resolved, Duplicate, Closed).
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to the follow-up action (if applicable).
