# Insurance Claim Distance Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Claim Distance Declaration form is a mandatory document for employees who have experienced a travel-related incident that may be covered by the company's insurance policy. It is used to gather information about the distance traveled and other relevant details to process the insurance claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of distance that applies to your travel incident.
2. Enter the distance traveled in kilometers or miles.
3. Provide the starting and ending points of your travel.
4. Enter the date of your travel.
5. Enter the duration of your travel (in the format of hours and minutes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Distance Type**: Select the type of distance that applies to your travel incident (Kilometers, Miles, or Other).
  This field is used to determine the unit of measurement for the distance traveled.
  For example, if you choose Kilometers, please enter the distance in kilometers.
- **Distance**: Enter the distance traveled in the chosen unit of measurement (kilometers or miles).
  Please use the numerical value only (e.g. 10 km or 5 miles).
- **Starting Point**: Enter the starting location of your travel (e.g. airport, hotel, etc.).
  Please provide a brief and specific description of the starting point.
- **Ending Point**: Enter the ending location of your travel (e.g. airport, hotel, etc.).
  Please provide a brief and specific description of the ending point.
- **Travel Date**: Enter the date of your travel in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/2022).
  Please use the exact date of your travel.
- **Travel Time**: Enter the duration of your travel in the format of HH:MM (e.g. 01:30).
  Please use the exact duration of your travel.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative or supervisor for assistance.
- This form is for insurance claims only, and any incomplete or fraudulent information may be subject to investigation and/or penalties.
- If you have already completed this form, please do not submit another one for the same event.
