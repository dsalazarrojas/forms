# Vacation Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and approve vacation leave. It is intended for employees to provide information about their leave, and for managers to approve or deny it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the form title with a brief summary of your leave request.
- Select one of two options for the Category field if required.
- Leave the Description field empty if not required.
- Provide the start and end dates for your leave, and format them as YYYY-MM-DD.
- Enter your name and the leave duration if required.
- Select one of two options for Leave Type and Leave Status if required.
- Enter your reason for leave if required.
- Enter the date you plan to return from leave if required.
- Select one of two options for Leave Approved if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vacation Leave Authorization Form**: This is the form title that summarizes your leave request.
* **Category**: Select Yes or No to indicate if your leave request falls within your category. This field is optional.
* **Description**: If you have any additional information or comments about your leave, enter them here. This field is optional.
* **Leave Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Leave End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Manager Signature**: This is a field for your manager to sign and date when approving or denying your leave.
* **Leave Duration**: Enter the duration of your leave, if required.
* **Leave Type**: Select Yes or No to indicate if your leave is a vacation or not. This field is optional.
* **Leave Reason**: Enter a brief explanation of your leave reason. This field is optional.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return from leave, formatted as YYYY-MM-DD. This field is optional.
* **Leave Status**: Select Active or Inactive to indicate if your leave is active or inactive. This field is optional.
* **Approval Date**: This field will be filled in automatically by the system when the manager approves your leave.
* **Approved By**: This field will be filled in automatically by the system when the manager approves your leave. This field is not editable and will only contain the name of the manager who approved your leave.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your employee handbook or consult with your manager.
