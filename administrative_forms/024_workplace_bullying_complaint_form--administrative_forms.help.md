# WorkplaceBullyingComplaintForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Bullying Complaint Form is designed to document and report incidents of workplace bullying within the organization. This form helps ensure that all reported incidents are thoroughly investigated and addressed in a timely and confidential manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.
2. Please note that some fields are optional and are not required for submission.
3. Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and thoroughly.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your reporting manager for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bullying Incident Report** (`bullying_incident`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to briefly describe the bullying incident that occurred.
* **Reporter Details** (`reporter_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee to provide their details for the incident report.
* **Bullies Name** (`bully_name`, `select_one`, required: false): In this field, select the name of the person being bullied. Options include `Select an option` and `Another option`.
* **Description** (`bullying_description`, `note`, required: false): This field is for the employee to provide a brief description of the bullying incident. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report the date of the bullying incident.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, `time`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report the time of the bullying incident.
* **Workplace Bullying Complaint Form** (`workplace_bullying_complaint_form`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required for submission.
* **Bullying Type** (`bullying_type`, `select_one`, required: false): In this field, select the type of bullying that occurred. Options include `Verbal Abuse`, `Physical Assault`, `Sexual Harassment`, and `Other (please specify)`.
* **Witness Information** (`witness_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee to provide any witness information for the incident.
* **Reporting Employee** (`reporting_employee`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the employee who is reporting the bullying incident. Options include `Reporting Employee 1` and `Reporting Employee 2`.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the manager who will be handling the bullying complaint. Options include `Reporting Manager 1` and `Reporting Manager 2`.
* **Reporting Location** (`reporting_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the location where the bullying incident occurred. Options include `Reporting Location 1` and `Reporting Location 2`.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report the date they submitted the form.
* **Reporting Time** (`reporting_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report the time they submitted the form.
* **Reporting Manager Name** (`reporting_manager_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report their reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Phone** (`reporting_manager_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report their reporting manager's phone number.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report their reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Location** (`reporting_manager_location`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report their reporting manager's location.
* **Bullying Complaint Form Submitter** (`bullying_complaint_form_submitter`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the employee who submitted the bullying complaint form. Options include `Reporting Employee 1` and `Reporting Employee 2`.
* **Bullying Complaint Form Review Status** (`bullying_complaint_form_review_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the bullying complaint form. Options include `In Progress`, `Reviewed`, and `Closed`.
* **Bullying Complaint Form Comments** (`bullying_complaint_form_comments`, `note`, required: false): This field is for the employee to provide any comments about the bullying incident.
* **Date of Reported Incident** (`bullying_incident_reported_date`, `date`, required: false): This field is for the employee to report the date the incident was reported.
* **Workplace Bullying Complaint Form Status** (`bullying_complaint_form_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the bullying complaint form. Options include `In Progress`, `Submitted`, and `Closed`.
* **Incident ID** (`bullying_incident_id`, `text`, required: false): This field is not required for submission.
* **Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the submission status of the form. Options include `In Progress`, `Submitted`, and `Closed`.
