# Teachers Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about teachers' experiences, strengths, and areas for growth. The data collected through this form will be used to provide personalized feedback and support to teachers. It is recommended that teachers complete this form to get a thorough assessment of their performance and identify areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label. 
* Enter your title, name, subject, email, and contact number as they appear in official records.
* If you have years of experience in teaching, enter it as a numerical value.
* In the "Areas for Growth" field, write down specific areas where you think you need support or improvement.
* If you have comments or additional feedback, use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: *form_title* (*text*, not required): Enter a brief title to identify your assessment.
* **Teacher Name**: *teacher_name* (*text*, not required): Enter your full name as it appears in official records.
* **Subject**: *teacher_subject* (*text*, not required): Enter the subject you teach.
* **Email**: *email* (*email*, not required): Enter your official email address as it appears in official records.
* **Contact Number**: *teacher_phone* (*text*, not required): Enter your official contact number as it appears in official records.
* **Years of Experience**: *teacher_experience* (*number*, not required): Enter the number of years you have been teaching.
* **Teacher Strengths**: *teacher_strengths* (*text*, not required): List any areas where you excel or have a strong performance in.
* **Teacher Weaknesses**: *teacher_weaknesses* (*text*, not required): Identify areas where you need improvement.
* **Areas for Growth**: *teacher_areas_for_growth* (*text*, not required): List specific areas where you need support or improvement.
* **Comments**: *comments* (*text*, not required): Enter any additional feedback or comments.
