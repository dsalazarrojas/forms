# Expense Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employee expenses for approval and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information about your expense before starting.
* Enter your **Employee Name** in the relevant field.
* Enter the date of the **Expense Date** if applicable.
* Enter the **Expense Amount** if applicable.
* Select the relevant **Expense Category** from the list provided.
* Provide a brief description of the **Expense Reason** if applicable.
* Select the **Expense Approver** from the list provided.
* Select the relevant **Department** from the list provided.
* Select the status of the **Expense Status** from the list provided.
* Add any additional **Expense Note** if necessary.
* Enter your **Expense Email** if applicable.
* Enter your **Expense Phone** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it is listed on your employee record.
* **Expense Date**: Enter the date of the expense, if applicable.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Expense Category**: Select a category for your expense from the list provided.
*   *   Options: Options 1, Options 2, Options 3
* **Expense Reason**: Provide a brief description of the expense if applicable.
* **Expense Approver**: Select the person who will be responsible for approving your expense.
*   *   Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Department**: Select the department that is responsible for your expense.
*   *   Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Expense Status**: Select the current status of your expense.
*   *   Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Expense Note**: Add any additional information about your expense if necessary.
* **Expense Email**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your expense.
* **Expense Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your expense.

Note: The form fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
