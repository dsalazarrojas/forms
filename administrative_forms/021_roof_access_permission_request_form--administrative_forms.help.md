# Roof Access Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roof Access Permission Request Form is used to request access to a roof area by employees. This form helps employees to accurately and formally communicate their request to their manager or admin for access to a specific roof area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your employee name and email address
* Provide your manager's name and email address (if applicable)
* Specify the date and time of your shift
* Specify the purpose or reason for your access request
* Enter the department you are requesting access to (if applicable)
* Specify the floor level and building name (if applicable)
* Enter the shift start and end time
* Enter the date of your shift
* Enter any relevant employee id, building id, department id

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name**:
	+ (employee_name, text, required)
	+ Enter your name as an employee.
* **employee_email**:
	+ (employee_email, email, required)
	+ Enter your official email address.
* **manager_name**:
	+ (manager_name, text, optional)
	+ If you have a manager, enter their name.
* **manager_email**:
	+ (manager_email, email, optional)
	+ If you have a manager, enter their email address.
* **date**:
	+ (date, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of your shift.
* **time**:
	+ (time, time, optional)
	+ Enter the start and end time of your shift.
* **shift_start**:
	+ (shift_start, time, optional)
	+ Enter the start time of your shift.
* **shift_end**:
	+ (shift_end, time, optional)
	+ Enter the end time of your shift.
* **date_shift**:
	+ (date_shift, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of your shift.
* **purpose**:
	+ (purpose, text, optional)
	+ Specify the reason for your access request.
* **department**:
	+ (department, text, optional)
	+ Enter the department you are requesting access to.
* **floor_level**:
	+ (floor_level, text, optional)
	+ Enter the floor level you are requesting access to.
* **building_name**:
	+ (building_name, text, optional)
	+ Enter the building name you are requesting access to.
* **employee_id**:
	+ (employee_id, number, optional)
	+ Enter your employee id (if required).
* **building_id**:
	+ (building_id, number, optional)
	+ Enter the building id (if required).
* **department_id**:
	+ (department_id, number, optional)
	+ Enter the department id (if required).
* **date_approved**:
	+ (date_approved, date, optional)
	+ The date the request was approved (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it for approval.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact your manager or admin for guidance.
* This form will only be used to request access to roof areas and not for other purposes.
