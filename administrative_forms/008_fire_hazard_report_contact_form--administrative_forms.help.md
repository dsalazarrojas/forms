# fire_hazard_report_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire hazard report contact form is designed to collect information about fire hazards and incidents within the organization. This form helps gather essential details about the location, time, and description of the incident, as well as the contact information of the person reporting the incident.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for "Location" to indicate where the incident occurred.
2. Choose the correct time from the dropdown menu for "Time" when the incident occurred.
3. Select all relevant incident types that apply from the "Incident Type" field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
5. Specify the reason for reporting the incident in the "Report Reason" field.
6. Enter the contact email address of the person reporting the incident in the "Contact Email" field.
7. Provide the contact phone number of the person reporting the incident in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Select the correct option for "Contact Phone Extension" to indicate whether the phone number has an extension.
9. Enter the name of the person reporting the incident in the "Contact Name" field.
10. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
11. Select the correct option for "Fire Department" to indicate whether the incident was reported to the fire department.
12. Select the correct option for "Reporting Officer" to indicate whether an officer was involved in reporting the incident.
13. Choose the correct date from the dropdown menu for "Reporting Date" when the incident was reported.
14. Select the correct option for "Incident Reported" to indicate whether the incident was reported.
15. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
16. Specify the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
17. Select the correct option for "Incident Status" to indicate the status of the incident.
18. Select the correct option for "Incident Severity" to indicate the severity of the incident.
19. If necessary, add any additional comments about the incident in the "Additional Comments" field.
20. Review and submit the form to complete the fire hazard report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location where the incident occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant incident types that apply.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Report Reason** (`report_reason`, `select_one`, required): Specify the reason for reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Provide the contact phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Phone Extension** (`contact_ext_phone`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Contact Phone Extension" to indicate whether the phone number has an extension.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_ext_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_ext_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Fire Department** (`fire_department`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Fire Department" to indicate whether the incident was reported to the fire department.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Reporting Officer" to indicate whether an officer was involved in reporting the incident.
* **Reporting Date** (`reporting_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date when the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reported** (`incident_reported`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Incident Reported" to indicate whether the incident was reported.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Specify the location of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Incident Status" to indicate the status of the incident.
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct option for "Incident Severity" to indicate the severity of the incident.
