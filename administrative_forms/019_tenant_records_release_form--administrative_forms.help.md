# Tenant Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release tenant records from the system, providing a secure and controlled way to access and release sensitive data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the requirements and the fields.
2. Enter the required information accurately and completely.
3. Select the authorized party responsible for the release.
4. Provide the reason for the release.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant being released.
- **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the tenant account.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the tenant, in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number associated with the tenant account, if available.
- **Authorized Party** (`authorized_party`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the authorized party responsible for the release, options are:
	* Authorized Person
	* Authorized Organization
	* Authorized Business
- **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description explaining the reason for the release.
- **Sign Date** (`sign_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form is signed.
- **Sign Time** (`sign_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day when the form is signed.
- **Sign IP Address** (`sign_ip_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the IP address of the device used to sign the form.

Note: Some fields are not required to fill out, but completing them will help to ensure accuracy and completeness of the release process.
