# Facility Maintenance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Maintenance Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about the condition and maintenance status of facilities. This form helps in tracking and managing maintenance activities, ensuring that facilities are properly evaluated and maintained to ensure they remain in good condition and meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by evaluating the overall condition of the facility and choose an option from "Excellent", "Fair", and "Poor" for the "facility_condition" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of any maintenance requests you have for the facility in the "maintenance_request" field.
3. Offer any suggestions for maintenance in the "maintenance_suggestion" field.
4. Choose the priority level of the maintenance activity from "Low", "Medium", and "High" options in the "maintenance_priority" field.
5. Select the status of the maintenance activity as "Incomplete" or "Complete" in the "maintenance_status" field.
6. Provide the planned maintenance date and time in the "maintenance_plan" field.
7. Select the frequency of the maintenance activity from "Once", "Daily", "Weekly" options in the "maintenance_frequency" field.
8. Select the person responsible for maintenance from "Facility Manager" and "Maintenance Staff" options in the "maintenance_responsible" field.
9. Provide any comments about the facility in the "facility_comments" field.
10. Select the status of the facility from "Active" and "Inactive" options in the "facility_status" field.
11. Rate the overall condition of the facility on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "facility_rating" field.
12. Rate the maintenance satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "maintenance_rating" field.
13. Rate the overall satisfaction with the maintenance activity on a scale from 1 to 10 in the "maintenance_satisfaction" field.
14. Provide the date of the maintenance request in the "maintenance_request_date" field.
15. You can optionally provide any supporting images or videos related to the facility in the "facility_image" and "facility_video" fields.
16. You can optionally upload any related documents in the "facility_document" field.
17. You can leave any additional comments about the facility in the "facility_note" field.
18. You can add any maintenance notes in the "maintenance_notes" field.
19. You can provide the name and email of the person requesting the maintenance in the "maintenance_requester_name" and "maintenance_requester_email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **facility_maintenance_form**: This is the form title, which cannot be edited by users.
* **facility_condition**: Evaluate the overall condition of the facility as "Excellent", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **maintenance_request**: Describe any maintenance requests you have for the facility.
* **maintenance_suggestion**: Suggest any maintenance activities or improvements you think are necessary.
* **maintenance_priority**: Choose the priority level of the maintenance activity as "Low", "Medium", or "High".
* **maintenance_status**: Select the status of the maintenance activity as "Incomplete" or "Complete".
* **maintenance_plan**: Provide the planned date and time for the maintenance activity.
* **maintenance_scheduled**: Select the frequency of the maintenance activity from "Once", "Daily", or "Weekly".
* **maintenance_responsible**: Choose who is responsible for maintenance as "Facility Manager" or "Maintenance Staff".
* **facility_comments**: Leave any comments about the facility.
* **facility_status**: Select the status of the facility from "Active" or "Inactive".
* **facility_rating**: Rate the overall condition of the facility on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **maintenance_rating**: Rate the maintenance satisfaction on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **maintenance_satisfaction**: Rate the overall satisfaction with the maintenance activity on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **maintenance_request_date**: Provide the date of the maintenance request.
* **facility_image**: Optionally, provide any supporting images related to the facility.
* **facility_video**: Optionally, provide any supporting videos related to the facility.
* **facility_document**: Optionally, upload any related documents.
* **facility_note**: Leave any additional comments about the facility.
* **maintenance_notes**: Add any maintenance notes.
* **maintenance_requester_name**: Provide the name of the person requesting the maintenance.
* **maintenance_requester_email**: Provide the email of the person requesting the maintenance.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure the facility maintenance activity is properly scheduled and executed.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your facility's maintenance manual or seek guidance from the facility manager.
* This form is for facility maintenance purposes only; do not use it for other purposes.
