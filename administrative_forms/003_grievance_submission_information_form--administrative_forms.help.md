# grievance_submission_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to a grievance submission. A grievance is a complaint or expression of discontent about a situation or issue that requires attention and resolution. This form helps to gather necessary details for processing and addressing the grievance in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation related to the grievance before proceeding.
2. Fill in the form with the required details, paying close attention to the fields described in the next section.
3. Ensure you understand the purpose and meaning of each field, as explained below.
4. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **grievance submission information form 1** (`grievance_submission_information_form_1`, text, required: false)
Enter a brief description of the grievance, stating the nature of the issue.

### **category** (`category`, select_one, required: false)
Select the category under which the grievance falls. The options are:
* Option 1
* Option 2

### **description** (`description`, text, required: false)
Enter a detailed explanation of the grievance, describing the situation and relevant details.

### **grievance submission information form 2** (`grievance_submission_information_form_2`, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the grievance submission, if applicable.

### **grievance submission information form 3** (`grievance_submission_information_form_3`, time, required: false)
Enter the time of the grievance submission, if applicable.

### **grievance submission information form 4** (`grievance_submission_information_form_4`, note, required: false)
Enter any additional notes or comments related to the grievance, if needed.

### **grievance submission information form 5** (`grievance_submission_information_form_5`, select_one, required: false)
Select whether the grievance requires immediate attention or not.
* Yes
* No

### **grievance submission information form 6** (`grievance_submission_information_form_6`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select any relevant issues or options related to the grievance.
* Yes
* No

### **grievance submission information form 7** (`grievance_submission_information_form_7`, email, required: false)
Enter the email address associated with the grievance submission, if applicable.

### **grievance submission information form 8** (`grievance_submission_information_form_8`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional information related to the grievance, if necessary.

### **grievance submission information form 9** (`grievance_submission_information_form_9`, text, required: false)
Enter any further details or comments related to the grievance, if required.

### **grievance submission information form 10** (`grievance_submission_information_form_10`, text, required: false)
Enter any other comments or details, if needed.

### **grievance submission information form 11** (`grievance_submission_information_form_11`, text, required: false)
Enter any last thoughts or comments related to the grievance, if necessary.
