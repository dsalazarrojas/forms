# hoa_document_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to propose amendments to existing documents. Fill out the form to suggest changes to a document, providing a reason for the amendment and the specific changes being made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter a proposed amendment in the **Proposed Amendment** field.
2. Provide a reason for the amendment in the **Reason For Amendment** field.
3. Describe the proposed changes in the **Proposed Changes** field.
4. If applicable, enter the title of the revised document in the **Document Title** field.
5. If necessary, provide the original text of the document in the **Old Document Text** field.
6. Enter the revised text of the document in the **New Document Text** field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about the proposed amendment in the **Comments** field.
8. Review your answers and click the **Submit** button to finalize your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Proposed Amendment** (Text, Optional): This field is used to enter the proposed amendment in brief.
- **Reason For Amendment** (Text, Optional): This field is used to provide a reason for the proposed amendment.
- **Proposed Changes** (Text, Optional): This field is used to describe the specific changes being made to the document.
- **Document Title** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the title of the revised document.
- **Old Document Text** (Text, Optional): If necessary, provide the original text of the document.
- **New Document Text** (Text, Optional): Enter the revised text of the document.
- **Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the proposed amendment.
- **Submit** (Date, Optional): This is the button to finalize your submission.
