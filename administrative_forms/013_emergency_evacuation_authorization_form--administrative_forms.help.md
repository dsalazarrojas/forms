# Emergency Evacuation Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the emergency evacuation authorization for an employee. It provides a record of the employee's status in case of an emergency, which can be used for contact and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Evacuation Authorization** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_data`, text, not required): This field is the main reason for the evacuation. Please provide a brief description of the emergency that led to the employee's absence.
* **Name** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_name`, text, not required): The name of the employee who is absent.
* **Job Title** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_job_title`, text, not required): The job title of the employee who is absent.
* **Work Location** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_work_location`, text, not required): The location where the employee works or is usually located.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_emergency_contact_person`, text, not required): The person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_emergency_contact_phone`, text, not required): The phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_emergency_contact_email`, email, not required): The email of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_emergency_contact_relationship`, text, not required): The relationship between the employee and the emergency contact person.
* **Evacuation Status** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_evacuation_status`, select_one, not required): The status of the employee's evacuation. Choose from the options: Available, Away from Office, On Leave, On Travel, Unable to Contact.
* **Evacuation Method** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_evacuation_method`, select_one, not required): Whether the employee is willing to be contacted through a certain method. Choose from the options: 'Yes', 'No'.
* **Note** (`emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_note`, text, not required): Any additional notes or comments that may be helpful in case of an emergency.

Note: Please keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure that the employee's status is correctly recorded.
