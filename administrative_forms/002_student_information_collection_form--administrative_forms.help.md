# Student Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general information about students for internal administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can skip any field that you do not have or prefer not to provide. Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's first name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's last name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's unique ID number, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the student's current address.

Note: If you are unsure about any field, it's best to leave it blank or skip it, as it's not required.
