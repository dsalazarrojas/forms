# Documentation Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and acknowledge an employee's understanding of the company's documentation policy. It collects information about the employee's receipt and review of the policy, as well as their acknowledgment of it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee title in the "Employee Title" field.
3. Enter the date you received the documentation policy in the "Documentation Date Received" field.
4. Enter the date you acknowledged the policy in the "Acknowledgment Date" field.
5. Enter the title of the policy in the "Documentation Policy Title" field.
6. Enter the URL of the policy in the "Documentation URL" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Enter your manager's title in the "Manager Title" field.
9. Check the "Reviewed by Manager" field to indicate that your manager has reviewed and approved your understanding of the policy.
10. Enter the date your manager reviewed and approved your understanding of the policy in the "Reviewed by Manager Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or personnel file.
- **Employee Title** (Text, Required: False): Enter your official title or job function within the company.
- **Documentation Date Received** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you received the documentation policy document.
- **Acknowledgment Date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you acknowledged understanding of the policy.
- **Documentation Policy Title** (Text, Required: False): Enter the title of the policy you are acknowledging.
- **Documentation URL** (Text, Required: False): Enter the URL of the policy document (if applicable).
- **Manager Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their personnel file or ID.
- **Manager Title** (Text, Required: False): Enter your manager's official title or job function within the company.
- **Reviewed by Manager** (Text, Required: False): Check this box to indicate that your manager has reviewed and approved your understanding of the policy.
- **Reviewed by Manager Date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date your manager reviewed and approved your understanding of the policy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Make sure to review and understand the documentation policy before acknowledging it.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the policy, please reach out to your manager or HR department.
- Keep a record of your acknowledgment of the policy for future reference.
