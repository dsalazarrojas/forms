# Weekly Work Shift Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Work Shift Summary form is used to record and track employee shift information for the week. This includes details about the employee's name, shift date, start and end times, break details, shift status, labor cost, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Select the date of the shift in the `shift_date` field.
3. Enter the start time of the shift in the `start_time` field.
4. Enter the end time of the shift in the `end_time` field.
5. Enter the break hours taken by the employee in the `break_hours` field.
6. Enter the break minutes taken by the employee in the `break_minutes` field.
7. Select the type of break taken by the employee from the options provided (e.g. Break, Lunch, Dinner, Meeting).
8. Enter any notes or comments about the shift in the `notes` field.
9. Select the status of the shift from the options provided (e.g. Completed, Incomplete, Pending).
10. Enter the labor cost associated with the shift in the `labor_cost` field.
11. Select the type of shift from the options provided (e.g. Regular, Overtime, OT).
12. Enter any additional shift details in the `shift_details` field.
13. Enter the location of the shift in the `shift_location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee who worked the shift.
* **shift_date** (date, required): Select the date of the shift.
* **start_time** (time, required): Enter the start time of the shift.
* **end_time** (time, required): Enter the end time of the shift.
* **break_hours** (number, optional): Enter the number of break hours taken by the employee.
* **break_minutes** (number, optional): Enter the number of break minutes taken by the employee.
* **break_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of break taken by the employee (e.g. Break, Lunch, Dinner, Meeting).
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about the shift.
* **shift_status** (select_one, required): Select the status of the shift (e.g. Completed, Incomplete, Pending).
* **labor_cost** (number, optional): Enter the labor cost associated with the shift.
* **shift_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of shift (e.g. Regular, Overtime, OT).
* **created_by** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who created the shift.
* **updated_by** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who last updated the shift.
* **created_at** (date, required): The date the shift was created.
* **updated_at** (date, required): The date the shift was last updated.
* **assigned_to** (select_one, optional): Select the employee to whom the shift was assigned.
* **created_at_time** (time, required): The time the shift was created.
* **updated_at_time** (time, required): The time the shift was last updated.
* **shift_notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional shift notes.
* **shift_comments** (note, optional): Enter any shift comments.
* **shift_details** (text, optional): Enter any additional shift details.
* **shift_location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the shift.
* **shift_status_update** (text, optional): Enter any status updates about the shift.
