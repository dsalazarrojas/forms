Paid Time Off Extension Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request a paid time off extension. It is used to gather information from employees to process their time off extension requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing your name as an employee. This will help us identify you and ensure that your request is processed correctly.
*   Enter the start date of your paid time off extension.
*   Enter the end date of your paid time off extension.
*   If you are requesting a paid time off extension, please provide the name of your manager.
*   If you are requesting a paid time off extension, please provide the name of your department.
*   Enter a brief reason for your paid time off extension request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: This field is where you should enter your name as an employee. Please ensure it is spelled correctly.
*   **Start Date**: This field is where you should enter the start date of your paid time off extension. Format should be MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **End Date**: This field is where you should enter the end date of your paid time off extension. Format should be MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Manager Name**: This field is where you should enter the name of your manager if you are requesting a paid time off extension. If you are not requesting a paid time off extension, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Department**: This field is where you should enter the name of your department if you are requesting a paid time off extension. If you are not requesting a paid time off extension, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Reason**: This field is where you should enter a brief reason for your paid time off extension request.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth processing of your request.
