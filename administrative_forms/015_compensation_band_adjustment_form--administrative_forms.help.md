# Compensation Band Adjustment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Compensation Band Adjustment Form" is a tool designed to assist employees or HR representatives in requesting a compensation band adjustment within the organization. The form helps gather necessary information to support the adjustment request, ensuring that the process is efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the guidelines below:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the relevant policies and procedures within your organization before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
3. Be sure to select the most applicable reason for the adjustment request from the provided options.
4. Clearly justify the proposed compensation band adjustment, providing relevant background data or market comparisons as needed.
5. Verify the proposed effective date for the adjustment and ensure it aligns with organizational procedures.
6. Confirm that the proposed adjustment has been discussed with the budget holder and document the status accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name_adj`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the full name of the employee for whom the adjustment is requested.
* **Hint:** Full name as recorded in official personnel records.

### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id_adj`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the unique payroll or HR ID for the employee.
* **Hint:** Ensure this is the correct ID as per the organization's HR system.

### Current Job Title
* **Current Job Title** (`job_title_adj`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current job title of the employee as per official records.
* **Hint:** This should match the employee's current position within the organization.

### Department
* **Department** (`department_adj`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current department or organizational unit where the employee works.
* **Hint:** This helps in allocating the adjustment within the organization's budget and resources.

### Current Compensation Band
* **Current Compensation Band** (`current_comp_band`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the current compensation band of the employee.
* **Hint:** This is the basis for the proposed adjustment, so ensure accuracy.

### Proposed Compensation Band
* **Proposed Compensation Band** (`proposed_comp_band`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the proposed compensation band after the adjustment.
* **Hint:** This is the band that the employee is requesting, based on their performance or position.

### Primary Reason for Adjustment
* **Primary Reason for Adjustment** (`adjustment_reason_primary`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the most applicable reason for the adjustment from the provided options.
* **Hint:** Select the reason that best describes the circumstances for the adjustment request.

### Proposed Effective Date
* **Proposed Effective Date** (`effective_date_adj`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the adjustment should take effect.
* **Hint:** Ensure this date is aligned with the organization's pay cycles and budget planning.

### Detailed Justification
* **Detailed Justification** (`justication_summary`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed explanation or justification for the adjustment, including any relevant background data or market comparisons.
* **Hint:** This helps in supporting the request with concrete evidence.

### Budget Approval Status
* **Budget Approval Status** (`budget_impact_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if the proposed adjustment has been discussed with the budget holder and their response.
* **Hint:** Ensure this is updated accurately to reflect the current status.

### Budget Holder Name
* **Budget Holder Name** (`budget_holder_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the executive or manager responsible for the budget.
* **Hint:** This helps in tracking communication and approval processes.

### HR Representative Name
* **HR Representative Name** (`hr_rep_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the HR representative processing the request.
* **Hint:** Ensure this is accurate to facilitate smooth processing and communication.

### Adjustment Form Submission Date
* **Adjustment Form Submission Date** (`adjustment_form_submission_date`, `date`, required/optional): This will automatically populate with the current date as the form is submitted.
* **Hint:** There is no action required for this field as it updates automatically.

## Tips

- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or discrepancies.
- This form is designed to streamline the compensation band adjustment process within your organization; follow its guidelines for a smooth and efficient process.
