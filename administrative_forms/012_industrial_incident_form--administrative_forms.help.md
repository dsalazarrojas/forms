# Industrial Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Incident Form is a tool for reporting and documenting industrial incidents, such as accidents, injuries, or near-miss events, to help improve safety and incident management practices within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the incident in the "Incident Title" field.
2. Select the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
4. Describe the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened in the "Incident Description" field.
6. Select the cause of the incident from the options provided in the "Incident Cause" field.
7. Select the effect of the incident on personnel or the organization in the "Incident Effect" field.
8. Choose the severity level of the incident from the options provided in the "Incident Severity" field.
9. Identify the individual(s) who reported the incident in the "Incident Reporter" field.
10. Select the status of the investigation in the "Investigation Status" field.
11. Summarize the investigation conclusion in the "Investigation Conclusion" field.
12. Provide a root cause analysis for the incident in the "Root Cause Analysis" field.
13. Outline the prevention plan for similar incidents in the "Prevention Plan" field.
14. Describe any corrective actions taken or to be taken in response to the incident in the "Corrective Actions" field.
15. Summarize the review and verification of the incident in the "Review and Verification" field.
16. Provide follow-up actions and plans for future incidents in the "Follow-up Actions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Title** (`id: 1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief title for the incident, for example, "Equipment malfunction in the manufacturing line."
* **Incident Date** (`id: 2`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`id: 3`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`id: 4`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`id: 5`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (`id: 6`, `text`, required/optional): Select the cause of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Effect** (`id: 7`, `text`, required/optional): Select the effect of the incident on personnel or the organization.
* **Incident Severity** (`id: 8`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the severity level of the incident from the options provided, such as Critical, Major, or Minor.
* **Incident Reporter** (`id: 9`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Identify the individual(s) who reported the incident.
* **Investigation Status** (`id: 10`, `text`, required/optional): Select the status of the investigation.
* **Investigation Conclusion** (`id: 11`, `text`, required/optional): Summarize the investigation conclusion.
* **Root Cause Analysis** (`id: 12`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a root cause analysis for the incident.
* **Prevention Plan** (`id: 13`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the prevention plan for similar incidents.
* **Corrective Actions** (`id: 14`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any corrective actions taken or to be taken in response to the incident.
* **Review and Verification** (`id: 15`, `text`, required/optional): Summarize the review and verification of the incident.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`id: 16`, `text`, required/optional): Provide follow-up actions and plans for future incidents.
