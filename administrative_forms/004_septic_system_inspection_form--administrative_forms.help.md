# Septic System Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a septic system inspection, including details about the septic system, inspector, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, which are indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant choice that best describes the septic system.
3. Enter accurate information for the date and time of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Septic System Details** (`septic_system_details`): A text field where you can describe the septic system in detail, including its size, location, and any notable features.
* **Inspector Details** (`inspector_details`): A text field for the inspector to provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details about their inspection process.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): A text field for the contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Septic System Condition** (`septic_system_condition`): A select multiple field with four options: Good, Fair, Poor, and Very Poor. This field describes the overall condition of the septic system.
* **Inspector Notes** (`inspector_notes`): A text field for the inspector to record any notes or observations about the inspection.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): A date field to record the date of the inspection. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`): A time field to record the time of the inspection. Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Septic System Type** (`septic_system_type`): A select multiple field with seven options: Conventional, Drip-Dispersion, Drip-Irrigation, At-Grade, Cluster, Sand-Dwelling, and Conventional/Alternative. This field describes the type of septic system.
* **Septic System Size** (`septic_system_size`): A select multiple field with four options: Small (1-2 units), Medium (2-5 units), Large (5-10 units), and Extra Large (10+ units). This field describes the size of the septic system.
* **Septic System Capacity** (`septic_system_capacity`): A select multiple field with three options: Residential, Commercial, and Industrial. This field describes the capacity of the septic system.
* **Well Size** (`well_size`): A select multiple field with three options: Small (1-2 units), Medium (2-5 units), and Large (5-10 units). This field describes the size of the well.
* **System Age** (`system_age`): A select multiple field with three options: New, Older (5+ years), and Older (10+ years). This field describes the age of the septic system.
* **Septic System Condition 2** (`septic_system_condition_2`): A select multiple field with four options: Good, Fair, Poor, and Very Poor. This field describes the condition of the septic system.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): A text field for the inspector's name.
* **Inspector Phone** (`inspector_phone`): A text field for the inspector's phone number.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`): An email field for the inspector's email address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): A text field for the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): A text field for the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): An email field for the contact person's email address.
* **Inspector Note** (`inspector_note`): A text field for the inspector to record any notes or observations about the inspection.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`): A text field for the contact person to record any notes or observations.
* **Septic Size** (`septic_size`): A number field to record the septic system size.
* **Number of Units** (`number_of_units`): A number field to record the number of units.
* **Well Depth** (`well_depth`): A number field to record the well depth.

Note: This guide is not intended to be a comprehensive technical guide, but rather a basic introduction to the form fields and their intended use. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please consult the relevant authorities or experts.
