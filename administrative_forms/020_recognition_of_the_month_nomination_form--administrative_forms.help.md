# Recognition Of The Month Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to recognize and appreciate the outstanding work or achievements of a team member. It is a formal process that allows team members to submit nominations for recognition, which will be reviewed and acknowledged by the team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information. 
2. Make sure to provide accurate and descriptive nomination reason.
3. Ensure that you are selecting up to 3 team members to review the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Title**: Enter a brief title that describes the reason for the nomination.
* **Nominator**: Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominee**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee E-mail**: Enter the email address of the person being nominated.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person being nominated.
* **Nomination Reason**: Provide a detailed description of why you are nominating this person.
* **Nomination Date**: Select the date of the nomination.
* **Nominated By**: Select up to 3 team members to review the nomination. 
* **Nomination Date/Time**: Select the date and time of the nomination.
* **Recognition Message**: Enter a message to be displayed during the recognition ceremony.
* **Reviewer Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes for the reviewers.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Enter the signature of the reviewer.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person being nominated.
