# Inventory Rotation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inventory Rotation Checklist" form is designed to facilitate tracking and managing inventory rotation schedules for businesses. It helps gather essential information for inventory items, such as last rotation dates, next due dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the business associated with the inventory rotation.
2. Provide the name of the contact person responsible for the inventory rotation.
3. Specify the location where the inventory is kept.
4. Enter the date of the last inventory rotation.
5. Enter any notes about the last inventory rotation.
6. Select the date by which the next inventory rotation is due.
7. Enter the rotation frequency (e.g., how often the inventory is rotated).
8. Enter the current stock level of the inventory.
9. Enter any notes about the current stock level.
10. Enter the last stock level of the inventory after the previous rotation.
