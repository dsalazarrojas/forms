# Evacuation Drill Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evacuation Drill Report Form is used to document the results of an evacuation drill. It helps to identify what went well, what didn't, and areas that need improvement for future drills. This form is crucial in evaluating the effectiveness of the drill and making necessary adjustments to ensure the safety of individuals during emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling in the `Organization` field with the name of your organization, which could be a school, hospital, or company.
- Select the `Drill Date` when the evacuation drill took place.
- Enter the location of the drill in the `Evacuation Location` field.
- Choose the teams that participated in the drill from the provided options in the `Drill Participants` and `Drill Observers` fields. These fields are designed to help identify who was involved in the drill.
- In the `Drill Procedures`, `Other Drill Procedures`, `Safety Equipment`, `Drill Supervision`, `Drill Observers`, and `Other Drill Observers` fields, provide a brief description of what was observed, learned, or done during the drill. These fields are crucial in detailing the specifics of the drill.
- The `Drill Conclusion` field is a space for any additional comments or observations.
- Enter any `Action Items` that need to be taken in the next drill in the `Action Items` field.
- Finally, enter your name and any additional information in the `Completed By` field to indicate that you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, required): This field is for the name of your organization, which should reflect the entity hosting or participating in the drill. It's essential for identification and reporting purposes.
* **Drill Date** (`drill_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the evacuation drill took place. This helps in scheduling and planning for future drills.
* **Evacuation Location** (`evacuation_location`, text, required): Specify the exact location where the drill was conducted. This could be a floor, building, or even a specific room within a building.
* **Drill Participants** (`drill_participants`, select_multiple, required): Choose the teams or individuals that took part in the drill. These might include emergency response teams, safety teams, or other groups involved.
* **Drill Procedures** (`drill_procedures`, text, required): Describe the procedures that were followed during the drill. This could include protocols for evacuation routes, communication systems, or other critical processes.
* **Other Drill Procedures** (`drill_procedures_other`, text, required): If there were any additional or unique procedures not listed under the main procedures field, provide details here.
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, text, required): List any safety equipment that was used or observed during the drill.
* **Drill Supervision** (`drill_supervision`, text, required): Describe the supervision or observation methods used during the drill. This could be how the drill was supervised or monitored.
* **Drill Observers** (`drill_observers`, select_multiple, required): Choose the teams or individuals who observed the drill. This helps in evaluating the effectiveness of the drill.
* **Other Drill Observers** (`drill_observers_other`, text, required): If there were other observers not listed under the main observers field, provide details here.
* **Drill Conclusion** (`drill_conclusion`, note, required): Enter any final comments or observations about the drill.
* **Drill Recommendations** (`drill_recommendations`, text, required): Suggest any changes or improvements that could be made for future drills.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required): Identify any tasks or actions that need to be taken before the next drill.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, required): Sign your name and provide any additional information about yourself or your role in completing the form.
