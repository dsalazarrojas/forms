# ethics_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ethics compliance declaration form is used to collect information from employees regarding their understanding and compliance with the company's ethics and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **employee name** in the "employee_name" field.
2. Provide your **job title** in the "job_title" field.
3. Enter your **department** in the "department" field.
4. Select a **declaration date** using the date picker in the "declaration_date" field.
5. Choose your **declaration status** from the dropdown menu in the "declaration_status" field.
6. Sign your name in the **employee signature** field.
7. Enter a statement explaining your understanding and commitment to the company's ethics and compliance policies in the "declaration_statement" field.
8. Sign the declaration date in the **declaration_date_signature** field.
9. Confirm your **declaration status** by selecting it from the dropdown menu in the "declaration_status_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **job_title**: Enter your current job title within the company.
* **department**: Enter the department or team you belong to within the company.
* **declaration_date**: Enter the date you are making this declaration.
* **declaration_status**: Choose whether your declaration is **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **employee_signature**: Provide your handwritten signature to confirm your declaration.
* **declaration_statement**: Describe your understanding and commitment to the company's ethics and compliance policies.
* **declaration_date_signature**: Provide your handwritten signature to confirm the date of your declaration.
* **declaration_status_signature**: Confirm your declaration status by signing below the selected status.
* **declaration_status**: Choose whether your declaration status is **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Review your declaration statement to ensure it accurately reflects your understanding and commitment to the company's ethics and compliance policies.
* Sign all fields as required.
