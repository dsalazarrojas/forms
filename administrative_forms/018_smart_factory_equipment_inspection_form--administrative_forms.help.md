# Smart Factory Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Factory Equipment Inspection Form is used to collect information about the condition and maintenance needs of Smart Factory equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure that you have all necessary information and to understand the fields that require input.
2. Fill in the Inspection Date field with the date of the inspection.
3. Describe the current Equipment Condition in the Equipment Condition field.
4. If applicable, specify the Maintenance Needs for the equipment.
5. Add any additional Notes about the inspection.
6. If you need to report additional Maintenance Needs for this equipment, select the appropriate priority level in the Additional Maintenance Needs field.
7. If the equipment has a unique ID, enter it in the Equipment ID field.
8. Finally, rate the Equipment Condition Rating field with a numerical value from 0 to 10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is excellent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): The date of the inspection.
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, text, required/optional): A description of the current condition of the equipment.
* **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs`, text, required/optional): A list of maintenance needs for the equipment, if any.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Any additional notes about the inspection.
* **Additional Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needs_2`, select_one, required/optional): Select the priority level of additional maintenance needs (High, Medium, Low).
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required/optional): A unique identifier for the equipment, if available.
* **Equipment Condition Rating** (`equipment_condition_2`, number, required/optional): Rate the equipment condition from 0 (very poor) to 10 (excellent).

## Tips
* Be accurate and detailed in your descriptions to ensure accurate data collection.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to the equipment manual or consult with a colleague.
* Please ensure that each submission is unique and accurate for the specific equipment being inspected.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to prevent mistakes and ensure completeness.
