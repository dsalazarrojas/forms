# Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Hazard Resolution Report Form is designed to gather information about safety hazards and incidents within your organization. It allows you to report incidents, document their resolution, and track the progress of hazard resolutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the guidelines below.
2. Ensure you have not already reported this incident.
3. Choose the correct incident type and severity from the options provided.
4. Assign the incident to the correct person for resolution.
5. Enter the resolution date and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (2): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Hazard Location** (3): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (4): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Type** (5): Choose the type of incident from the options provided:
	+ **Hazards**: Identify potential hazards or near misses.
	+ **Near Miss**: Incidents that did not cause harm.
	+ **Injuries**: Incidents that caused physical harm.
	+ **Damages**: Incidents that caused damage to equipment or property.
* **Incident Severity** (6): Choose the severity of the incident from the options provided:
	+ **High**: Severe incidents that caused serious harm or significant damage.
	+ **Low**: Minor incidents with minimal impact.
	+ **Medium**: Incidents that caused moderate harm or impact.
* **Assigned To** (7): Select the person responsible for resolving the incident from the options provided.
* **Resolution Date** (8): Enter the date when the incident was resolved.
* **Resolution Status** (9): Choose the status of the resolution from the options provided:
	+ **Not Resolved**: The incident is not yet resolved.
	+ **Partially Resolved**: The incident is partially resolved, but some work remains.
	+ **Fully Resolved**: The incident is completely resolved.
* **Resolution Comment** (10): Add any additional comments or information about the resolution process.
