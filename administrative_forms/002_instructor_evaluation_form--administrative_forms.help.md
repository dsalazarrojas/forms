# instructor_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The instructor evaluation form is designed to facilitate instructors in providing feedback on students' performance. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the teacher in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Select the evaluation date from the calendar by clicking on the date field.
4. Enter the evaluation time in the format "HH:MM AM/PM" in the "Evaluation Time" field.
5. Choose the type of evaluation (e.g., mid-semester, end-of-semester, semester) from the dropdown menu in the "Evaluation Type" field.
6. Write any comments in the "Comments" field.
7. Rate the student's overall performance on a scale of 1-5 in the "Overall Performance" field.
8. Enter any evaluation comments or ratings in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
9. List the areas of the student's strengths in the "Areas of Strengths" field.
10. List the areas of the student's weaknesses in the "Areas of Improvement" field.
11. Choose whether or not you recommend the student for further education in the "Recommendation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name (text)**: Enter the name of the course in which you are evaluating the student.
* **Teacher Name (text)**: Enter the name of the teacher who is providing this evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date (date)**: Select the date of the evaluation from the calendar. 
* **Evaluation Time (time)**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Evaluation Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of evaluation (e.g., mid-semester, end-of-semester, semester).
* **Comments (note)**: Write any comments or observations you have about the student.
* **Overall Performance (number)**: Rate the student's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Comments (text)**: Write any additional comments or ratings about the student.
* **Overall Performance (number)**: Rate the student's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Areas of Strengths (text)**: List the areas where the student excelled or demonstrated exceptional abilities.
* **Areas of Improvement (text)**: List the areas where the student needs improvement or demonstrated weakness.
* **Recommendation (select_multiple)**: Choose whether or not you recommend the student for further education.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* Remember that this form is a tool for providing constructive feedback to help the student grow and improve.
