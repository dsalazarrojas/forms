# HR Compliance Checklist Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request HR compliance checks for employees. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2.  Enter your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
3.  Provide your job title, department, and job location (if applicable).
4.  Enter your date of hire.
5.  Enter your supervisor's name and ID.
6.  Indicate whether you are a prophet or not.
7.  Indicate whether your department manager is available or not.
8.  Indicate your current employee status (prophet or not).
9.  Optionally, enter your manager's name and select your employee type (prophet or not).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card or payroll records.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number as found on your ID card or payroll records.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as assigned by HR.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department as assigned by HR.
- **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter your job location (if applicable).
- **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, optional): Enter the date you were hired.
- **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's full name.
- **Supervisor ID** (`supervisor_id`, number, required): Enter your supervisor's ID number.
- **Job Code** (`job_code`, text, optional): Enter your job code as assigned by HR.
- **Job Group** (`job_group`, text, optional): Enter your job group as assigned by HR.
- **Job Family** (`job_family`, text, optional): Enter your job family as assigned by HR.
- **Employee Category** (`employee_category`, text, optional): Enter your employee category as assigned by HR.
- **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are a prophet.
- **Department Manager** (`department_manager`, select_one, required): Indicate if your department manager is available.
- **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current prophet status.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's full name.
- **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select_one, optional): Indicate your employee type (prophet or not).

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your HR representative for clarification.
