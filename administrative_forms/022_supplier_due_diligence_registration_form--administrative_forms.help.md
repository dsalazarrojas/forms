# Supplier Due Diligence Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Due Diligence Registration Form is designed to gather important information about suppliers who are new to our system or have undergone significant changes to their business. This form is essential for us to verify and validate the supplier's information and ensure they are in good standing with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all requested information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required: false`): This field is used to collect any additional information that doesn't fit into the other categories.
* **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to gather basic information about the supplier, such as their name and description.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect contact information for the supplier, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Address Information** (`address_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to collect the supplier's address, including their street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, required: false): This field is used to gather information about the supplier's business, such as their business name, business type, and business status.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, select_multiple, required: false`): This field is used to indicate whether the supplier has a tax ID or not. Please select 'Yes' if the supplier has a tax ID, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required: false`): This field is used to select the type of business the supplier operates. Please select 'Small Business', 'Medium Business', or 'Large Business' depending on the supplier's size and scope.
* **Supplier Category** (`supplier_category`, select_one, required: false`): This field is used to select the category of the supplier. Please select 'Domestic', 'International', or 'Online' depending on the supplier's operations.
* **Business Status** (`business_status`, select_one, required: false`): This field is used to indicate the status of the supplier's business. Please select 'Active', 'Inactive', or 'Pending' depending on the supplier's status.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure all necessary information is collected.
- If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
