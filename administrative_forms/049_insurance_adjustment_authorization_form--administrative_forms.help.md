# Insurance Adjustment Authorization Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
The Insurance Adjustment Authorization Form is designed to be used by insurance agents and administrators to document and manage insurance policy adjustments. This form is intended for use when a policyholder requests changes to their policy, such as adjustments to the amount of coverage, changes to the policy terms, or other modifications. This form will guide you through the process of gathering necessary information and authorizing these adjustments.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Client Details section with the policyholder's information. This includes their name and contact details.
2. Indicate the type of policy that is being adjusted using the Policy Type dropdown menu.
3. Provide your policy ID and its status.
4. Indicate whether you are authorized to make changes to this policy by answering the Authorization question.
5. Enter your title and name in the Authorized By Title and Authorized By Name fields.
6. Fill in any remaining fields as necessary, such as email address and phone number of the authorized person.
7. Use the Comments field to add any additional information you feel is necessary.
8. Sign the form with your signature.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
* **Client Details**: Enter the policyholder's name and contact information. This is a required field.
* **Authorization**: Check the box indicating you are authorized to make changes to this policy.
* **Date Of Service**: Enter the date of the adjustment. This is typically the date the policyholder's request was made.
* **Insurance Policy**: Enter the policy number or other identifying information.
* **Authorized Amount**: Enter the amount authorized for the policy change.
* **Reason For Change**: Provide a brief explanation of the reason for the policy change.
* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date you authorized the policy change.
* **Authorized By**: Enter your title and name.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the policy adjustment.
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount of the policy change.
* **Insurance Policy Type**: Select the type of policy being adjusted (e.g. Life, Health, etc.).
* **Policy ID**: Enter the policy number or other identifying information.
* **Policy Status**: Select the status of the policy (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name as a verification of your identity.
* **Agent Signature**: Sign your name as a verification of your identity.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact information so the policyholder can reach you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so the policyholder can reach you.
* **Fax Number**: Enter your fax number so the policyholder can reach you.
* **Address**: Enter your address for additional contact information.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional information about the policyholder or policy.
* **Policy Type**: Select whether this policy is active or inactive.
* **Policy Status**: Select the status of the policy.
* **Authorized By Title**: Enter your title or job position.
* **Authorized By Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Fax Number**: Enter your fax number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional information.
