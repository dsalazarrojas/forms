# Data Collection Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data from individuals for administrative purposes, such as collecting their name, contact information, and authorization level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name where it is being collected.
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your date of birth.
7. Enter your address (two lines).
8. Enter your city.
9. Enter your state.
10. Enter your zip code.
11. Select your country of residence (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
12. Choose the level of authorization you are granting (Full or Limited).
13. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the consent statement.
14. Sign the agreement on the date you sign it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the company collecting your data.
* **First Name** (text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address Line 1** (text, not required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (text, not required): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (text, not required): Enter your city.
* **State** (text, not required): Enter your state.
* **Zip** (text, not required): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (select_one, not required): Select your country of residence (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **Authorization** (select_multiple, not required): Choose the level of authorization you are granting (Full or Limited).
* **Consent** (note, not required): Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the consent statement.
* **Agreement Signed** (date, not required): Date you sign the agreement.
* **Signature** (time, not required): Date and time you sign the agreement (optional). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields correctly.
* Choose the correct level of authorization for your data collection.
* Read and understand the consent statement before checking the box.
* Date and time your signature for your record keeping.
