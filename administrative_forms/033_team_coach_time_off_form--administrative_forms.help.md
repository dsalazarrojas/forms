The purpose of the Team Coach Time Off Form is to allow coaches to request time off from the team, providing necessary information for administrative purposes.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and submit this form.
2. Select the "Team Coach Time Off Form" category from the available options.
3. Start by filling in your "Employee ID", a unique identifier for you within the team.
4. Enter your "Name", which will be used to identify you to the team.
5. Input your "Email", which will be used for communication purposes.
6. Provide your "Phone" number for easy contact.
7. Select the date for which you are taking time off.
8. Choose the time of day you will be taking time off.
9. Select the reason for your time off from the options provided. Common options include:
	* Option 1: Personal/Family reasons
	* Start Date: Time off starts on this date
	* End Date: Time off ends on this date
	* Coach Name: For team coach purposes
	* Team: For team-related reasons
	* Coach Position: For coach-related reasons
	* Notes: Add any additional comments or notes
	* Submit: Confirm your submission
	* Confirm: Confirm your submission
9. Once you have filled in all required fields, submit the form to request your time off.

# Team Coach Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Coach Time Off Form is used to request time off from the team for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: A unique identifier for you within the team.
* **Name (2)**: Your name, used to identify you to the team.
* **Email (3)**: A contact email for easy communication.
* **Phone (4)**: A contact phone number for easy contact.
* **Date (5)**: The date for which you are taking time off.
* **Time (6)**: The time of day you will be taking time off.
* **Reason (7)**: Select one of the provided options for the reason of your time off.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the relevant team members or administrators.
* Note that some fields, such as "Reason", have options to choose from. Choose the most appropriate option for your situation.
* Be mindful of your team's scheduling and make sure to request your time off in a way that minimizes impact on the team.
