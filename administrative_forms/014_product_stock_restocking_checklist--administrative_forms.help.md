# Product Stock Restocking Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Stock Restocking Checklist" form is used to track and manage the re-stocking process of products in stock. It helps ensure that stock levels remain optimal and that customers always have access to the products they need.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the product you want to restock from the list of available products.
2.  Enter the current inventory level of the product.
3.  Enter the target inventory level for the product.
4.  Select the next restocking date and time.
5.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the product or restocking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Product Stock Restocking Checklist Page 1**
    *   **Product** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_product`, text): Select the product you want to restock from the list of available products.
    *   **Inventory Level** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_inventory_level`, number): Enter the current inventory level of the product.
    *   **Restocking Threshold** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_restocking_threshold`, number): Set the threshold for when the product should be restocked.
    *   **Current Level** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_current_level`, text): Enter the current stock level of the product.
    *   **Target Level** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_target_inventory_level`, number): Enter the target stock level for the product.
    *   **Next Restock** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_next_restock`, date): Set the date and time for the next restocking.
    *   **Date Next Restock** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_date_next_restock`, time): Enter the date and time for the next restock.
    *   **Note** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_1_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product or restocking process.
*   **Product Stock Restocking Checklist Page 2**
    *   **Restocking Frequency** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_2_restocking_frequency`, select_multiple): Select how often the product should be restocked (e.g., Every, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, Never).
    *   **Frequency Unit** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_2_frequency_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of time for the restocking frequency (e.g., Weeks, Months, Quarters, Years, Other).
    *   **Note** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_2_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product or restocking process.
*   **Product Stock Restocking Checklist Page 3**
    *   **Restocking Frequency** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_3_restocking_frequency`, select_multiple): Select how often the product should be restocked (e.g., Every, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, Never).
    *   **Frequency Unit** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_3_frequency_unit`, select_one): Select the unit of time for the restocking frequency (e.g., Weeks, Months, Quarters, Years, Other).
    *   **Note** (`product_stock_restocking_checklist_page_3_note`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product or restocking process.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select the correct product to restock from the list of available products.
*   Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the current and target inventory levels.
*   Set the correct restocking threshold and next restock date and time.
*   Enter any additional notes or comments about the product or restocking process.
*   Review and update the form regularly to ensure that stock levels remain optimal and that customers have access to the products they need.
