# Gas Leak Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and track gas leaks that have occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the relevant options for each field and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): The date the gas leak incident occurred.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): The location where the gas leak occurred.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the gas leak incident.
* **Gas Leak Type** (`gas_leak_type`, select_one, required/optional): The type of gas leak (Natural Gas, Propane, Oxygen, or Unknown).
* **Gas Leak Size** (`gas_leak_size`, select_multiple, required/optional): The size of the gas leak (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Gas Leak Location** (`gas_leak_location`, select_one, required/optional): The location of the gas leak (Inside, Outside, Both).
* **Gas Leak Duration** (`gas_leak_duration`, time, required/optional): The duration of the gas leak.
* **Gas Leak Odor** (`gas_leak_odor`, select_multiple, required/optional): Was there a detectable odor associated with the gas leak? (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Gas Leak Sound** (`gas_leak_sound`, select_multiple, required/optional): Was there a detectable sound associated with the gas leak? (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Gas Leak Smell** (`gas_leak_smell`, select_multiple, required/optional): Was there a detectable smell associated with the gas leak? (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Emergency Contacted** (`emergency_contacted`, select_one, required/optional): Was emergency contacted during the gas leak? (True, False).
* **Gas Leak Reported** (`gas_leak_reported`, select_one, required/optional): Was the gas leak reported? (True, False).
* **Gas Leak Investigation** (`gas_leak_investigation`, select_one, required/optional): Was a gas leak investigation conducted? (True, False).
* **Gas Leak Report Status** (`gas_leak_report_status`, select_one, required/optional): The status of the gas leak report (Open, Closed).
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, required/optional): The user assigned to handle the gas leak incident (Yes, No).
* **Incident Number** (`incident_number`, number, required): A unique identifier for the gas leak incident.
* **Incident Date and Time** (`incident_date_time`, date, required/optional): The date and time the gas leak incident occurred.
* **Reported By** (`reported_by`, select_one, required): Was the gas leak reported by someone? (Yes, No).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required): The status of the gas leak incident (Active, Inactive).
* **Incident Status Reason** (`incident_status_reason`, text, required): The reason for the incident status (Active, Inactive).
* **Incident Reported** (`incident_reported`, select_one, required): Was the gas leak incident reported? (Yes, No).
* **Incident Responded** (`incident_responded`, select_one, required): Was the gas leak incident responded to? (Yes, No).
* **Incident Closed** (`incident_closed`, select_one, required): Is the gas leak incident closed? (Yes, No).
* **Incident Closed Reason** (`incident_closed_reason`, select_one, required): The reason the gas leak incident is closed (Yes, No).
* **Gas Leak Comments** (`gas_leak_comments`, note, required/optional): Additional comments about the gas leak incident.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* This form is designed to collect information about gas leak incidents. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure efficient handling and follow-up of the incident.
