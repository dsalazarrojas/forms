# Tax Filing Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tax Filing Appointment Form is used to schedule an appointment with a tax specialist for tax filing purposes. This form helps gather essential information from clients, including their contact details, preferred time and date, and tax form requirements. The completed form will guide tax specialists in preparing for the appointment, ensuring a smooth and efficient tax filing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX.XX.XXXX".
3. Select your preferred **Schedule Time** for the appointment.
4. Choose the **Tax Specialist** you would like to work with.
5. Select your preferred **Schedule Date** for the appointment.
6. Choose the relevant **Tax Form** for your tax filing.
7. Select the desired **Appointment Method** (e.g., in-person, phone, or video call).
8. Add any additional information that is relevant to your appointment.
9. Once you have completed all the required fields, submit the form to schedule your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text Field, Optional)
	+ Enter your full name or the name of the person you are representing.
* **Contact Number** (Text Field, Required)
	+ Enter your contact number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX.XX.XXXX" for easy communication.
* **Schedule Time** (Time Field, Required)
	+ Select your preferred time for the appointment in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 or 08:00).
* **Schedule Date** (Date Field, Required)
	+ Choose a date for the appointment. The date should be in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **Tax Specialist** (Select One, Required)
	+ Select one of the tax specialists available to work with.
* **Appointment Time** (Time Field, Required)
	+ Select the time for the appointment in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00 or 08:00).
* **Appointment Date** (Date Field, Required)
	+ Choose the date for the appointment in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g., 2024-03-15).
* **Tax Form** (Select One, Required)
	+ Select the relevant tax form required for your filing (e.g., Form A, B, or C).
* **Appointment Method** (Select Multiple, Required)
	+ Choose the appointment method that suits you best (e.g., in-person, phone, or video call).
* **Additional Info** (Text Field, Optional)
	+ Add any additional information or details that are relevant to your appointment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
