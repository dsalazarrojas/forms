# Animal Control Field Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Control Field Support Request Form is used to report incidents involving animals in the field. This form helps us to identify the issue, gather relevant information, and dispatch personnel to resolve the situation efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the form accurately, please follow these steps:

*   Start by filling out the required fields (marked as `required` in the form).
*   For optional fields, provide as much information as possible to help us better understand the situation.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Your Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Animal Control Field Support Request**: Enter a brief description of the animal-related issue you're reporting.
*   **Date of Request**: Select today's date to help us keep track of the incident's timeline.
*   **Location of Incident**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Type of Location**: Choose where the incident occurred from the options: Residential property, Commercial property, Public park, School grounds, Roadway, Vacant lot, or Other public space.
*   **Type of Issue**: Choose the type of issue from the options: Stray animal, Aggressive animal, Injured animal, Dead animal, Excessive barking, Animal at large, Wildlife conflict, Animal neglect, Bite incident, or Other.
*   **Describe the Animal**: Enter a description of the animal involved, including its species, breed, color, and size.
*   **How Many Animals Are Involved**: Choose the number of animals involved from the options: 1, 2, 3-5, 6-10, or More than 10.
*   **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level from the options: Emergency - immediate danger, High - within 24 hours, Medium - within 48 hours, or Low - routine response.
*   **Is There a Public Safety Risk?**: Indicate if there's a public safety risk involved by choosing: Yes - immediate threat, Yes - potential threat, No - no safety risk, or Unsure.
*   **Describe the Situation in Detail**: Provide a full description of the incident.
*   **When Did This Occur?**: If applicable, select the time of the incident.
*   **Is This an Ongoing Issue?**: Indicate if this is a recurring issue or a first-time occurrence.
*   **Have You Reported This Before?**: If you've reported this issue before, indicate how many times you've reported it.
*   **Is the Animal Owner Known?**: Indicate if you know the animal's owner or if it's a stray animal.
*   **Owner Information (if Known)**: If the animal's owner is known, enter their name and address.
*   **Witnesses (if Any)**: If there are witnesses, provide their information.
*   **Do You Have Photos or Videos?**: If you have documentation, indicate if you'll provide it.
*   **Special Access Instructions**: If there's a specific way to access the location (e.g., gate codes), enter the information here.
*   **Best Time to Contact You**: Choose the best time to contact you from the options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Anytime.
*   **Do You Want to Remain Anonymous?**: Indicate if you want to remain anonymous.
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any other relevant information about the incident.
*   **Requestor Signature**: Sign your full name as the requestor.
*   **Date**: Sign with today's date.

This guide will help you fill out the form accurately, ensuring that we can respond efficiently and effectively to animal-related incidents in the field.
