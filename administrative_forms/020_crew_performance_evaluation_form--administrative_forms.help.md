# Crew Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crew Performance Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and progress of crew members. This form is used by administrators to evaluate the performance of crew members, provide feedback, and identify areas for improvement. It is essential to complete this form regularly to ensure accurate and consistent evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on the crew member's performance and progress.
3. Provide specific and constructive feedback in the fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Instructions** (`form_instructions`, `text`, optional): Please read and understand the form purpose and requirements before proceeding. This field is optional, but it's recommended to ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's goals.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate which crew members this evaluation applies to. You can select multiple team members as needed.
* **Performance Metrics** (`performance_metrics`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific performance metrics that have been evaluated, such as meeting deadlines, quality of work, or communication skills. This field is optional and should be used to provide more context for your evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of the evaluation. This field is optional, but it helps to track progress and trends over time.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the crew member's performance as Excellent, Good, or Needs Improvement. This field is optional, but it helps to provide a quick and concise assessment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide specific and constructive feedback for the crew member, addressing their strengths and weaknesses. This field is optional and should be used to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, `select_multiple`, optional): Identify specific action items for the crew member to improve their performance. You can select multiple action items as needed.
* **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Determine if any follow-up actions are required for the crew member. You can select multiple follow-up actions as needed.
* **Final Evaluation** (`final_evaluation`, `select_one`, optional): Record the final evaluation status, which can be Completed, In Progress, or Not Started. This field is optional and helps to track progress and completion.
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any final comments or feedback for the crew member. This field is optional and should be used to provide closure or further feedback.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `note`, optional): Record any follow-up comments or feedback for the crew member. This field is optional and helps to maintain a record of communication.
* **Performance Review** (`performance_review`, `time`, optional): Record the performance review duration and frequency. This field is optional and helps to schedule regular performance reviews.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Determine if regular evaluations are performed for this crew member. Select 'Yes' if evaluations are performed regularly, or 'No' if they are not.
* **Evaluation Cycle** (`evaluation_cycle`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if this evaluation is part of a regular evaluation cycle. Select 'Yes' if it is, or 'No' if it is not.

Note: The fields marked as 'optional' are not required for form completion, but they provide valuable context and additional information for a more comprehensive evaluation.
