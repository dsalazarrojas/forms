# Tax Policy Amendment Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Policy Amendment Approval Form is used to approve or deny amendments to tax policies within an organization. This form ensures that all stakeholders are informed and on the same page regarding policy changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Describe the tax policy change in the "tax policy change details" field.
2. Enter the organization's name in the "organization name" field.
3. Select the date when the tax policy change takes effect in the "tax policy effect date" field.
4. Enter the date you are approving or denying the policy change in the "approval date" field.
5. List the emails of stakeholders who need to be informed in the "stakeholder emails" field.
6. List the phones of stakeholders who need to be informed in the "stakeholder phones" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the policy change in the "notes" field.
8. Select the status of the policy change (Approved, Denied, or Held for Review) in the "tax policy amendment approval status" field.
9. Choose the tool used to manage the policy change in the "assigned tool" field.
10. Select who submitted the policy change in the "submitted by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tax policy change details (1)**: Briefly describe the tax policy change.
* **organization name (2)**: Enter the name of the organization making the policy change.
* **tax policy effect date (3)**: Select the date when the tax policy change takes effect.
* **approval date (4)**: Enter the date you are approving or denying the policy change.
* **stakeholder emails (5)**: List the emails of stakeholders who need to be informed.
* **stakeholder phones (6)**: List the phones of stakeholders who need to be informed.
* **notes (7)**: Add any additional notes about the policy change.
* **tax policy amendment approval status (8)**: Select the status of the policy change (Approved, Denied, or Held for Review).
* **assigned tool (9)**: Choose the tool used to manage the policy change.
* **submitted by (10)**: Select who submitted the policy change.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Provide clear and concise information in the text fields.
* Ensure that all stakeholders are informed about the policy change.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
