# Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazardous Material Handling Checklist Form is a tool used to ensure that hazardous materials are handled and stored properly, reducing the risk of accidents and injuries. This form helps employees or users to document and track their handling process, ensuring compliance with safety regulations and company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required sections, indicated by a * in the label.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information in each section.
4. Use the options provided in the select fields to select the correct choices.
5. Ensure you have saved your progress and submitted the form before leaving the work area or closing the application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Section 1** (`Section 1`, text, optional): This section is a free-text field for any additional comments or notes related to the handling process.
* **Section 2** (`section-2`, date, optional): Enter the date when the material was last handled or inspected.
* **Section 3** (`Section 3`, time, optional): Record the time when the material was last handled or inspected.
* **Section 4** (`Section 4`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all relevant options related to the material's handling:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Section 5** (`section 5`, select_one, optional): Select the relevant option for the material's status:
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Section 6** (`Section 6`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the material handled.
* **Section 7** (`section 7`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the material stored.
* **Section 8** (`Section 8`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the material.
* **Section 9** (`section 9`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person responsible for handling the material.
* **Section 10** (`section 10`, text, optional): Additional comments or information about the material.
