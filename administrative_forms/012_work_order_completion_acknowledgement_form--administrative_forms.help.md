# Work Order Completion Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order Completion Acknowledgement Form is a simple tool to confirm the completion of a work order by both the client and the business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the appropriate option for the client's confirmation.
3. Provide your business confirmation in the text field.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Confirmation** (select multiple): 
    This field asks the client to confirm their understanding of the work order's completion. The options are:
	+ Client confirms completion of work order
	+ Client requests additional changes
	+ Other (please specify in the text field)
* **Business Confirmation** (text): 
    This field is for the business to provide their confirmation of the work order's completion.
* **Note**: This form is not meant for official documentation or contract purposes, but rather as a tool to facilitate communication between the client and the business.
