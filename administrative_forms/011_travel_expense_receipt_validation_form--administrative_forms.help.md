# Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form is used for employees to document and validate their travel expenses incurred on company time. This form helps ensure that all necessary information, such as travel dates, purpose, and expense amounts, are accurately reported and reviewed by the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a valid date for the "Travel Date" field. This date should reflect the period of travel.
2. Enter the "Traveler's Name" of the employee submitting the form.
3. Enter the email address of the "Traveler" in the "Traveler's Email" field.
4. Describe the "Business Purpose" of the travel in the provided text field.
5. Enter the total "Travel Expense Total" amount.
6. Enter the total "Company Expense Total" amount, if applicable.
7. Select "Submit and Save" to complete and submit the form.
8. Alternatively, select "Reset" to clear all form fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Date** (`1`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of travel in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Traveler's Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee who traveled.
* **Traveler's Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee who traveled.
* **Business Purpose** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason for the travel.
* **Date of Travel** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of travel, if different from the "Travel Date".
* **Travel Expense Total** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of travel expenses incurred.
* **Company Expense Total** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total amount of expenses reimbursed by the company.
* **Submit** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Submit and Save" to complete and submit the form, or "Reset" to clear all form fields.
* **Traveler's Company Name** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the traveler's company, if applicable.
