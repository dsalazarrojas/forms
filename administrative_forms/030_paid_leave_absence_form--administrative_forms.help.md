# Paid Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paid Leave Absence Form is used to record a paid leave absence request, which includes time off due to a vacation, sick leave, or parental leave.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of several fields that help us gather essential information about your request. Follow the instructions for each field to ensure your form is completed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Paid Leave Absence** (`paid_leave_absence_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate you are submitting a paid leave absence request. Select 'No' if this is not a paid leave absence request.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant categories that apply to your paid leave absence (e.g., 'Yes' for vacation, 'Yes' for sick leave, etc.).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your paid leave absence request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have been assigned a tool by your manager or team lead.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are aware of your form ID number.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): This field is not currently used, so please leave it blank.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): This field is a duplicate of the "Category" field above. You may choose additional categories for your paid leave absence. 

Note: Some of the fields have the option to select 'Yes' or 'No', which means you are confirming whether the corresponding condition applies to your request. 

Tips: Make sure to fill in all relevant fields accurately and truthfully, especially the "Category" field where you can select multiple options. If you're unsure about any field, please contact your manager or HR representative for guidance.
