# Employee Illness Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report an illness incident that may impact an employee's work or daily activities. It is essential for HR or management to be informed about any employee illnesses, enabling them to provide necessary support and accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are filling out the form with accurate information.
2. Complete all required fields marked as necessary.
3. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Employee Illness Incident Form
*   **Employee Illness Incident Form** (`employee_illness_incident_form_page_1`, `text`, required: false): This page is the introductory page of the form. It serves as a placeholder and does not contain any fields for input.
*   **Employee Illness Incident Information** (`employee_illness_incident_form_page_2`, `text`, required: false): This page is a free-text field for you to provide any relevant details about your illness. Please feel free to provide as much information as possible.

### Page 3: Incident Details
*   **Incident Details** (`employee_illness_incident_form_page_3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This page allows you to choose multiple details about your illness. You can select all applicable options from the list provided.
    -   You were not vaccinated or immunized.
    -   You have a medical condition or health concern.
    -   You have been diagnosed with an infectious disease or illness.
    -   You have a mental or emotional health issue.
    -   You are pregnant.
    -   Other (Please specify in the next field)

### Page 4: Incident Description
*   **Incident Description** (`employee_illness_incident_form_page_4`, `text`, required: true): This page is for providing a detailed description of your illness or incident. Please be as descriptive and accurate as possible to help facilitate proper support and action.

### Page 5: Incident Resolution
*   **Incident Resolution** (`employee_illness_incident_form_page_5`, `select_one`, required: false): This page allows you to indicate if your incident has been resolved. You can select "Yes" if your incident has been resolved or "No" if it has not.
    -   Yes
    -   No
