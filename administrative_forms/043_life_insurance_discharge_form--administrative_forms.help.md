# Life Insurance Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the process of discharging a life insurance policy. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals from all relevant parties before filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the beneficiary of the life insurance policy.
2. **Relationship To Policyholder** (`relationship_to_policyholder`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable relationships between the policyholder and the beneficiary.
3. **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the claim was made on the life insurance policy.
4. **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the policy number for the life insurance policy.
5. **Reason For Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, select_one, required): Select the reason for discharging the life insurance policy. This is a critical field that must be filled out.
6. **Amount Disbursed** (`amount_disbursed`, number, optional): Enter the amount disbursed for the life insurance policy.
7. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the policyholder or other relevant parties.
8. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email address for the policyholder or other relevant parties.
9. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the life insurance policy.
10. **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of life insurance policy being discharged.
11. **Policy Status** (`policy_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable policy statuses for the life insurance policy.
12. **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the life insurance policy was discharged.
13. **Policyholder Signature** (`policyholder_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the policyholder.
14. **Beneficiary Signature** (`beneficiary_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the beneficiary.
15. **Authorized Officer Signature** (`authorized_officer_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the authorized officer.
16. **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the witness.
17. **Authorized Officer Name** (`authorized_officer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the authorized officer.
18. **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness.
19. **Policyholder Address** (`policyholder_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the policyholder.
20. **Beneficiary Address** (`beneficiary_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the beneficiary.
21. **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the witness.
22. **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person or party.
23. **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship of the contact person or party to the policyholder.
24. **Policyholder DOB** (`policyholder_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the policyholder.
25. **Witness DOB** (`witness_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the witness.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals before filling out this form.
* Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
* This form should not be used to collect information for duplicate discharges or for policies that are not currently active.
* If you are unsure about any part of this form, please consult the relevant authorities or experts.
* Fill out this form in a clear and legible handwriting.
* Keep a record of this form for future reference.
