# mental_health_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health accommodation request form is designed to gather information about a person or organization's mental health accommodation needs. This form should be filled out by the individual or representative of the individual requesting accommodation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include all the necessary information in each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure there are no errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): 
This field is for your personal details. Please provide your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
This field helps us understand which type of accommodation is requested, e.g., "Accommodation for a family member" or "Accommodation for a friend".
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, `text`, required: false): 
Here, you can provide more information about the accommodation request, e.g., specific dates or times.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Choose how you would like to be contacted, e.g., email, phone, or in person.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select your preferred language to ensure that our communication is in your preferred language.
* **Accommodation Request** (`accommodation_request`, `text`, required: false): 
Please describe what kind of accommodation you need, e.g., extra time to complete an assignment or a quiet space.
* **Accommodation Request Details** (`accommodation_request_details`, `text`, required: false): 
Add any additional information that will help us better understand your accommodation request.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): 
Provide the name of the person who will be handling your contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): 
Enter the email address of the person who will be handling your contact information.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your submission before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
- For urgent matters, please contact our support team directly.
