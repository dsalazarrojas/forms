# New Vendor Onboarding Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Vendor Onboarding Request Form is used to collect necessary information from new vendors to be onboarded into our system. This form is a prerequisite for vendors to be approved and integrated into our operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Complete all the required fields. If a field is optional, make sure you have a valid reason for not filling it out.
2. **Verify your email**: Ensure that the email you provide is accurate and active.
3. **Double-check your contact information**: Make sure your phone number and address are up-to-date and valid.
4. **Provide a detailed description**: Describe your company and what services/products you offer.
5. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to send the form to our team for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email**: Enter your primary email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you are located.
* **City**: Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where your address is located.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code where your address is located.
* **Country**: Enter the country where your address is located.
* **Description**: Describe your company and what services/products you offer. This will help us understand your business better.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* Do not fill out the form if you have already been onboarded or are already integrated into our system.
