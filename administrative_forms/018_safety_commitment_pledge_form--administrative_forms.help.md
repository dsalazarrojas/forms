# Safety Commitment Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Commitment Pledge Form is a document that requires employees to pledge their commitment to safety in the workplace. It is used to ensure that employees understand their roles and responsibilities in maintaining a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your work email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select one of the options in the "Safety Commitment" field to indicate your commitment to safety.
4. Enter the date and time you are pledging your safety commitment in the "Safety Commitment Date" and "Safety Commitment Time" fields.
5. Provide the location of your work site in the "Worksite Location" field.
6. If applicable, list the employees affected by your safety commitment in the "Employees Affected" field.
7. Select the "Supervisor Approval" options to indicate the level of approval from your supervisor.
8. Select the "Review" options to indicate if you have reviewed the safety policies and procedures.
9. Select the "Approval Status" to indicate the status of your safety commitment.
10. If you have any comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
11. Finally, select one of the "Pledge" options to confirm your safety commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Safety Commitment**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you are committed to safety in the workplace.
* **Safety Commitment Date**: Enter the date you are making this commitment.
* **Safety Commitment Time**: Enter the time you are making this commitment.
* **Worksite Location**: Enter the location of your work site.
* **Employees Affected**: If you are making this commitment on behalf of others, list their names and job titles.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Select the level of approval from your supervisor.
* **Review**: Select "Yes" if you have reviewed the safety policies and procedures.
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your safety commitment.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback.
* **Pledge**: Select "Yes" to confirm your safety commitment.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person reviewing your safety commitment.
* **Reviewer Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person reviewing your safety commitment.
* **Reviewer Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Status**: Select the status of your safety commitment.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for this form.
* **Assigned User**: Enter the user assigned to this form.
* **Last Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated this form.
* **Last Updated Date**: Enter the date this form was last updated.
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created this form.
* **Created Date**: Enter the date this form was created.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the date this form was last updated.
