# Emergency Contact Information Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to update the emergency contact information for an individual. You will need to provide basic contact information and confirm the emergency contact's relationship to the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and confirm the emergency contact information provided for the individual.
2. Enter the new contact information in the corresponding fields.
3. Confirm the relationship between the individual and the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Form**: This section is where you will enter the emergency contact information for the individual. Please provide as much detail as possible, including any relevant contact methods (e.g. phone numbers, email addresses).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the individual who will serve as the emergency contact.
* **Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: Describe the relationship between the individual and the emergency contact (e.g. spouse, parent, sibling).
* **Confirm Emergency Contact**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date by re-entering the emergency contact's name and relationship.
