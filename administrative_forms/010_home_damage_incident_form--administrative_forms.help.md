# Home Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Damage Incident Form is a document used to report damage or vandalism that occurred in a home environment. 

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:
1. Enter the date of the incident.
2. Provide the location of the incident in your home.
3. Check if an incident occurred and what type of incident it was.
4. Describe what happened in as much detail as possible.
5. If relevant, enter the amount of damage caused by the incident.
6. Attach any images, videos, or audio recordings related to the incident.
7. If necessary, attach supporting documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Location** (2, text, optional): Provide the location within your home where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (3, select_one, optional): Check if an incident occurred and select 'Yes' or 'No'. If yes, select 'Yes' and choose the type of incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Description** (4, text, optional): Describe what happened, including as much detail as possible.
* **Incident Amount** (5, number, optional): If relevant, enter the amount of damage caused by the incident.
* **Incident Image** (6, text, optional): Attach any images related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (7, text, optional): Attach any videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio** (8, text, optional): Attach any audio recordings related to the incident.
* **Incident Document** (9, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents related to the incident.
