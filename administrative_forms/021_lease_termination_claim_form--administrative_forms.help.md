# Lease Termination Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Termination Claim Form is used to gather information from both landlords and tenants regarding the termination of a lease. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are on the same page and that the termination process is completed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For each field, provide accurate and truthful information.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or fill it out if relevant.
4. Review your form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant_name (Text)**: This field is for the tenant's name.
	+ Enter the tenant's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **tenant_date_of_birth (Date)**: This field is for the tenant's date of birth.
	+ Enter the date of birth as it appears on the tenant's identification documents.
* **landlord_name (Text)**: This field is for the landlord's name.
	+ Enter the landlord's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **landlord_address (Text)**: This field is for the landlord's address.
	+ Enter the landlord's address as it appears on their identification documents.
* **reason_for_termination (Text)**: This field is for the reason of termination.
	+ Enter a clear and concise description of why the lease is being terminated.
* **date_of_termination (Date)**: This field is for the date of termination.
	+ Enter the date the lease was terminated.
* **reason_for_termination_details (Text)**: This field is for any additional information regarding the reason for termination.
