# project_documentation_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The project documentation access request form is used to request access to project documentation, which may be sensitive or confidential. This form helps track and manage requests for access to project information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project name, which is the title of the project you are requesting access to.
2. Enter your name, which should be your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
3. Enter your email address, which should be your work email address where you can be contacted.
4. Enter your phone number, which should be your work phone number where you can be reached.
5. Enter the name of the document you are requesting access to.
6. Enter the version of the document you are requesting access to, if known.
7. Enter the date and time you are requesting access to the document, using the date and time format of your region.
8. Enter a brief reason for your request, so the project team can understand your need for access.
9. Finally, select the team member(s) you would like the document to be assigned to, or choose "Other" if you would like to specify someone else.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Project Name** (project_name, text, optional): Enter the project name for which you are requesting access to documentation. This will help the project team understand which project your request is related to.
- **Requestor's Name** (requestor_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card. This is how the project team will contact you regarding your request.
- **Requestor's Email** (requestor_email, email, optional): Enter your work email address where you can be contacted regarding your request. This is the best way for the project team to reach out to you.
- **Requestor's Phone** (requestor_phone, text, optional): Enter your work phone number where you can be reached. This will help the project team contact you if they need more information.
- **Document Name** (document_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the document you are requesting access to.
- **Document Version** (document_version, text, optional): Enter the version of the document you are requesting access to, if known. This will help the project team understand which version you need access to.
- **Request Date and Time** (request_date and request_time, date and time, optional): Enter the date and time you are requesting access to the document, using the format of your region (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy hh:MM AM/PM).
- **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for your request, so the project team can understand your need for access. This will help them prioritize your request.
- **Assigned To** (assigned_to, select_one, optional): Select the team member(s) you would like the document to be assigned to, or choose "Other" if you would like to specify someone else. 

**Tips**

* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to help the project team process your request efficiently.
* Double-check the project name and document name to ensure you are accessing the correct information.
* If you are unsure of any field, you can always ask the project team for clarification.
