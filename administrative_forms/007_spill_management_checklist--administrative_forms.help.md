# spill_management_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and manage spills, including providing incident details, response procedures, and cleanup actions. It is an essential tool for the team to respond effectively and efficiently to spills, ensuring a prompt and proper response.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Incident report" page to provide a brief description of the spill.
2. Fill in the "Spill details" field to provide more information about the spill.
3. Choose the "Response procedure" field to document the actions taken to respond to the spill.
4. Specify the "Incident location" where the spill occurred.
5. Report on the "Spill containment" actions taken to stop the spill from spreading.
6. List the "Cleanup actions" taken to clean up the spill.
7. Identify the "Response team" members who responded to the spill.
8. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
9. Report the volume of the spill in the "Spill volume" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident report**: A brief description of the incident.
* **Spill details**: More information about the spill, such as its location, cause, and severity.
* **Response procedure**: A description of the actions taken to respond to the spill.
* **Incident location**: The exact location where the spill occurred.
* **Spill containment**: Actions taken to prevent the spill from spreading.
* **Cleanup actions**: Steps taken to clean up the spill.
* **Response team**: Members of the team who responded to the spill.
* **Incident Date**: The date the spill occurred.
* **Incident Time**: The time the spill occurred.
* **Spill volume**: The amount of spill material released.
