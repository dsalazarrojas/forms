# property_ownership_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Ownership Declaration Form is used to declare the ownership status of a property. It's essential for administrative and regulatory purposes, such as in real estate transactions, asset management, or tax reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **User Info**: Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2.  **Property Info**: Provide details about the property, such as its address and description.
3.  **Ownership Details**: Enter the ownership status, including any transfer history and ownership percentage.
4.  **Date of Transfer**: Enter the date of the property transfer (if applicable).
5.  **Transferor Info**: Provide information about the person or entity transferring the property (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal details, including your name and contact information.
*   **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the property, such as its address and description.
*   **Ownership Details** (`ownership_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the ownership status, including any transfer history and ownership percentage.
*   **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the property transfer (if applicable).
*   **Transferor Info** (`transferor_info`, text, required/optional): Provide information about the person or entity transferring the property (if applicable).
