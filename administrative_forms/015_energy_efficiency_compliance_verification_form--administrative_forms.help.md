# energy_efficiency_compliance_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The energy efficiency compliance verification form is designed to collect information about various equipment and systems to ensure they meet energy efficiency standards. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and facilitates compliance checks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Review each field's explanation before proceeding to ensure you understand its purpose.
3. Submit the form once you've filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **user_info** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and title, to help us reach out to you if needed.
- **contact_info** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact information, such as phone number and email, for follow-up questions or concerns.
- **usage_info** (Text, Optional): Describe how the equipment is being used, including its intended purpose and usage capacity.
- **equipment_info** (Text, Optional): Enter details about the equipment being verified, including its type and model number.
- **efficiency_standards** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the energy efficiency standards met by the equipment, such as Energy Star or Energee.
- **usage_data** (Number, Optional): Enter numerical data related to the equipment's usage, such as energy consumption or efficiency rates.
- **equipment_efficiency** (Number, Optional): Provide the equipment's efficiency rating, expressed as a percentage or number.
- **equipment_model_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the model number of the equipment for reference.
- **equipment_serial_number** (Text, Optional): Provide the serial number of the equipment for tracking purposes.
- **manufacturer_part_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the part number of the equipment's manufacturer.
- **model_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the model number of the equipment for reference.
- **manufacturer_part** (Text, Optional): Describe the equipment's manufacturer part for clarity.
- **equipment_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of equipment being verified, such as HVAC or Lighting.
- **usage_start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the equipment started being used.
- **usage_end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the equipment stopped being used, if applicable.
- **installation_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the equipment was installed.
- **notes** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
- **energy_efficiency_rating** (Number, Optional): Enter the equipment's energy efficiency rating, if applicable.
- **energy_efficiency_rating_unit** (Text, Optional): Specify the unit of measurement for the energy efficiency rating.
- **usage_rating** (Select One, Optional): Choose a rating for the equipment's usage, such as Excellent, Very Good, or Fair.
- **usage_rating_unit** (Text, Optional): Specify the unit of measurement for the usage rating.
- **notes2** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
- **energy_efficiency_certification** (Text, Optional): Describe the equipment's energy efficiency certification, if applicable.
- **cert_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the equipment received its energy efficiency certification.
- **cert_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the number of the energy efficiency certification.

**Tips**
- Be sure to review and submit each field carefully to avoid errors.
- Use standard date and time formats to ensure accurate record-keeping.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the equipment's documentation or contact the form's administrator.
- This form is intended for internal use; do not share it externally without permission.
