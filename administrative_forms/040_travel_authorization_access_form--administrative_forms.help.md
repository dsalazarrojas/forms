# travel_authorization_access_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The travel authorization access form is a tool for employees to request travel authorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select the travel dates for your trip.
3. Choose the travel location(s) you plan to visit.
4. Indicate the purpose of your travel (business or personal).
5. Enter your employee email address.
6. Provide your employee phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details (1)
* **Employee Details (1)** (`employee_details`, required/optional): Enter your employee's name and any other relevant information.

### Travel Dates (2)
* **Travel Dates (2)** (`travel_dates`, optional): Choose the dates of your trip. Please select one of the provided dates.

### Travel Location (3)
* **Travel Location (3)** (`travel_location`, optional): Choose the location(s) you plan to visit. You can select one or multiple locations.

### Travel Purpose (4)
* **Travel Purpose (4)** (`travel_purpose`, optional): Indicate the purpose of your trip (business or personal).

### Employee Email (5)
* **Employee Email (5)** (`employee_email`, optional): Enter your employee's email address.

### Employee Phone (6)
* **Employee Phone (6)** (`employee_phone`, optional): Enter your employee's phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand the travel policy before completing this form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
