> **Photo Identification Application - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Photo Identification Application form is used to collect information about a photo, its description, capture location, and other relevant details. This form is designed to help users provide accurate and complete information about the photo, which is essential for various administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth (if applicable).
2. Provide a brief description of the photo.
3. Enter the URL where the photo is publicly available (if applicable).
4. Select a date and time when the photo was taken (if applicable).
5. If you have a photo file, upload it to the designated area.
6. Enter the date and time when the photo was captured (if applicable).
7. Provide any additional notes about the photo.
8. Enter the email address associated with the photo (if applicable).
9. Enter the phone number associated with the photo (if applicable).
10. Specify the location where the photo was captured (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD format.
* **Photo Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the photo.
* **Photo URL** (text, optional): If the photo is publicly available, enter the URL where it can be accessed.
* **Photo Date Taken** (date, optional): Enter the date when the photo was taken.
* **Photo Date Taken At** (time, optional): Enter the time when the photo was taken.
* **Photo File** (text, optional): If you have a photo file, select it from the available options.
* **Photo Upload** (text, optional): Select the file from your computer to upload to the photo.
* **Photo Capture Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the photo was captured.
* **Photo Capture Time** (time, optional): Enter the time when the photo was captured.
* **Photo Capture Location** (text, optional): Specify the location where the photo was captured.
* **Photo Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the photo.
* **Photo Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address associated with the photo.
* **Photo Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number associated with the photo.
