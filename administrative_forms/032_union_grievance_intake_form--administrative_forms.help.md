# Union Grievance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Union Grievance Intake Form is used to document and address grievances that union members may have against the union, its leadership, or other employees. This form is intended for union members to submit their concerns or complaints, and the information will be reviewed and addressed by union officials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start Submission" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Union Grievance Intake Form** (`union_grievance_intake_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text area where you can briefly describe the nature of your grievance. Please keep your description concise and clear.
* **Grievance Description** (`grievance_description`, text, optional):
	+ In this field, provide a detailed description of the grievance, including relevant dates, times, and locations. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Grievance Details** (`grievance_details`, text, optional):
	+ Use this field to provide any additional information or context about the grievance.
* **Grievance Date** (`grievance_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the grievance, if applicable.
* **Grievance Time** (`grievance_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the grievance, if applicable.
* **Union Member ID** (`union_member_id`, text, optional):
	+ Please enter your union member ID, which can be found on your union card or in your union member profile.
* **Union Member Name** (`union_member_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your union membership card or in your union member profile.
* **Grievance Type** (`grievance_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more of the following categories that apply to your grievance:
		- Union dues
		- Union benefits
		- Union leadership
		- Union workplace
		- Union safety
		- Union recruitment
		- Union training
		- Union pensions
		- Union health benefits
		- Union employees
		- Union policies
		- Union procedures

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible when describing your grievance.
* Only submit a single grievance per form.
* If you have already submitted a similar grievance or if it is being addressed through an existing channel, do not resubmit.
* You can save your form at any time by clicking on the "Save" button, which will allow you to come back to it later.
* Once submitted, your grievance will be reviewed and addressed by union officials.
