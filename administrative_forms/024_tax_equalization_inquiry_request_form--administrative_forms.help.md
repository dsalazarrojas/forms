# tax_equalization_inquiry_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tax equalization inquiry request form is used by employees to inquire about their tax type and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your tax type (Yes or No)
2. Select your tax status (Active or Inactive)
3. Provide any additional comments (if applicable)
4. Click the Submit Request button to send your inquiry
