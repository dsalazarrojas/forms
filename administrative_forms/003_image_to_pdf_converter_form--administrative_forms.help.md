# image_to_pdf_converter_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for converting an image into a PDF document.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Upload your image file.
2.  Select the image format you want to convert to (PDF, JPEG, or PNG).
3.  Choose the image orientation (Landscape or Portrait).
4.  Select the desired image resolution, size, and quality settings.
5.  Confirm your selections and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **upload_image** (`upload_image`, `number`, required): Upload an image file from your computer to convert into a PDF document.
* **select_format** (`select_format`, `select_one`, required): Choose the format you want to convert the image to: PDF, JPEG, or PNG.
* **upload_file** (`upload_file`, `text`, optional): Provide a file name to save the converted PDF document. If not provided, the default name will be used.
* **select_orientation** (`select_orientation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the desired image orientation: Landscape or Portrait.
* **upload_size** (`upload_size`, `number`, required): Enter the desired image size.
* **upload_scale** (`upload_scale`, `number`, required): Enter the desired image scale.
* **select_color_mode** (`select_color_mode`, `select_one`, required): Choose the color mode: Color or Grayscale.
* **upload_quality** (`upload_quality`, `number`, required): Enter the desired image quality.
* **upload_dpi** (`upload_dpi`, `number`, required): Enter the desired DPI (dots per inch).
* **upload_resolution** (`upload_resolution`, `number`, required): Enter the desired image resolution.
* **upload_margin** (`upload_margin`, `number`, required): Enter the desired image margin.

* **upload_quality_mode** (`upload_quality_mode`, `select_one`, required): Choose the quality mode: Auto or Best.
* **upload_dpi_mode** (`upload_dpi_mode`, `select_one`, required): Choose the DPI mode: Auto or '300'.
* **upload_scale_mode** (`upload_scale_mode`, `select_one`, required): Choose the scale mode: Fit or '100'.
* **upload_margin_mode** (`upload_margin_mode`, `select_one`, required): Choose the margin mode: '0' or Fit.

## Tips

- Make sure to upload a valid image file.

- Confirm your selections before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
