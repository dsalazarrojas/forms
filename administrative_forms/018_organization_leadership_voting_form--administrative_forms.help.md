# organization_leadership_voting_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The organization leadership voting form is used to collect and record leadership votes within the organization. This form is an essential tool for the leadership team to make informed decisions and track changes over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the leadership position you wish to vote on from the "Leadership Position" dropdown list.
2. Enter the name of the candidate for the selected leadership position.
3. Enter the vote weight, which represents the importance or strength of your vote.
4. Add any additional comments or remarks about your vote.
5. Enter the date and time of submission.
6. Optionally, enter the email address and phone number of the person submitting the vote.
7. Enter the name of the person submitting the vote.
8. Enter the date and time of creation (if this is the initial submission).
9. Enter the date and time of last update (if this is an updated submission).
10. Optionally, enter the name of the person updating the submission.
11. Optionally, enter any additional notes about the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Leadership Position** (`Leadership Position`, `select_one`, required): Select the leadership position you wish to vote on, such as CEO, Vice President, Manager, Director, or Officer.
- **Candidate** (`Candidate`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the candidate for the selected leadership position.
- **Vote Weight** (`Vote Weight`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical value representing the importance or strength of your vote.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about your vote.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the vote (if applicable).
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the vote (if applicable).
- **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
- **Time** (`Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of submission (if applicable).
- **Note** (`Note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
- **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the vote.
- **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
- **Submitted Time** (`Submitted Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of submission (if applicable).
- **Submitted Note** (`Submitted Note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
- **Submitted Email** (`Submitted Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the vote (if applicable).
- **Submitted Phone** (`Submitted Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the vote (if applicable).
- **Created By** (`Created By`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person creating the submission (if this is the initial submission).
- **Created Date** (`Created Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of creation.
- **Created Time** (`Created Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of creation (if applicable).
- **Created Note** (`Created Note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
- **Created Email** (`Created Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person creating the submission (if applicable).
- **Created Phone** (`Created Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person creating the submission (if applicable).
- **Updated By** (`Updated By`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person updating the submission.
- **Updated Date** (`Updated Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the last update.
- **Updated Time** (`Updated Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the last update (if applicable).
- **Updated Note** (`Updated Note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission.
- **Updated Email** (`Updated Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the person updating the submission (if applicable).
- **Updated Phone** (`Updated Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person updating the submission (if applicable).

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that the data collected will be reliable for future reference and decision-making. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
