# emergency_response_time_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_response_time_report_form is a tool used to record and report information about emergency responses, including the date and time of the incident, the start and end times of the response, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Incident Date** in the date field.
2. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the response.
3. Enter the **Duration** of the response in minutes.
4. Select the **Response Type** from the options provided (Medical, Fire, or Other).
5. Add any additional **Response Notes** about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the response started.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the response ended.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the response in minutes.
* **Response Type** (`response_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of response (Medical, Fire, or Other).
* **Response Notes** (`response_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the response.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information to ensure accurate reporting.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant authorities or team members.
* This form is used for reporting purposes only, and all information will be reviewed and verified.
