# Subway Safety Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subway Safety Incident Form is used to document and report any incidents that occur during subway operations, ensuring that safety measures are taken to prevent future incidents and provide a safe working environment for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with as much detail as possible.
2. Be as descriptive as you can when answering each question, providing specific dates, times, and locations.
3. Select the correct incident type from the provided options.
4. Ensure you answer all sections thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Safety Incident
* **Safety Incident** (`safety_incident`, text, required): Briefly describe the incident that occurred, including any details about the incident's severity and impact.
### Incident Date
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
### Incident Time
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
### Incident Location
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
### Incident Type
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select from the options to indicate the type of incident that occurred.
### Incident Description
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
### Cause of Incident
* **Cause of Incident** (`cause_of_incident`, text, optional): Try to identify the root cause of the incident, if possible.
### Actions Taken
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, optional): Describe any actions taken to address the incident or mitigate its effects.
### Actions Planned
* **Actions Planned** (`actions_planned`, text, optional): Describe any planned actions to prevent similar incidents in the future.
### Safety Measures Taken
* **Safety Measures Taken** (`safety_measures_taken`, text, optional): Describe any safety measures taken to address the incident or prevent similar incidents.
### Safety Measures Planned
* **Safety Measures Planned** (`safety_measures_planned`, text, optional): Describe any safety measures planned to prevent similar incidents in the future.
### Safety Measures Results
* **Safety Measures Results** (`safety_measures_results`, text, optional): If available, report any results of the safety measures taken or planned.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when reporting incidents to help maintain a safe working environment.
* Review existing incident reports to avoid duplicating efforts.
* Ensure accurate and detailed reporting to provide a clear picture of incidents and their causes.
* Report incidents promptly to allow for timely action and prevention.
