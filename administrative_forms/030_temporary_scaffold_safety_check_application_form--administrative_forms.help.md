# Temporary Scaffold Safety Check Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for temporary scaffold safety checks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate scaffold type(s) relevant to the check.
2. Enter the site location details in the text field.
3. Input the start and finish dates for the safety check.
4. Provide the inspector's name and company details.
5. Enter contact information, including phone number and email address.
6. Select the assigned tool for the safety check.
7. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the check.
8. Finally, obtain a signature from the inspector to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1): Enter any general information about the safety check, such as a description of the work or activity.
* **Scaffold Type** (Page 1): Select multiple scaffold types that were used during the safety check.
* **Site Location** (Page 1): Provide the location where the scaffold was erected.
* **Start Date** (Page 1): Input the date when the safety check was initiated.
* **Finish Date** (Page 1): Input the date when the safety check was completed.
* **Inspector Name** (Page 1): Enter the name of the inspector who conducted the safety check.
* **Company Name** (Page 2): Enter the name of the company responsible for the scaffold.
* **Company Address** (Page 2): Enter the address of the company.
* **Phone Number** (Page 2): Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Page 2): Enter an email address for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Page 2): Select the tool assigned to perform the safety check.
* **Notes** (Page 3): Provide any additional notes or comments regarding the safety check.
* **Signature** (Page 3): Enter your signature to confirm the safety check.
* **Inspector Signature** (Page 3): The inspector should sign here to confirm they have conducted the safety check and verified the information provided.

## Tips
- Please ensure accurate and complete information is entered for each field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.
- The assigned tool should match the inspector's tool and equipment requirements.
- Keep a record of any additional notes or comments for future reference.
