# Employee Transfer Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Transfer Approval Form is a document used to request the approval of a transfer of an employee from one department to another within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the reason for the transfer from the options provided.
2.  Enter the job title of the employee being transferred.
3.  Enter the department the employee is being transferred to.
4.  Identify the reporting manager of the employee being transferred.
5.  Enter the date the transfer is scheduled to take place.
6.  Enter the date the transfer will start.
7.  Provide a brief description of the reason for the transfer.
8.  Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the transfer.
9.  Indicate whether to approve or deny the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Transfer Approval Form** (`employee_transfer_approval_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select the reason for the transfer from the options provided.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the employee being transferred.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department the employee is being transferred to.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, optional): Identify the reporting manager of the employee being transferred.
- **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the transfer is scheduled to take place.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the transfer will start.
- **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the reason for the transfer.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the transfer.
- **Approval** (`approval`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether to approve or deny the transfer.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out before submitting the form.
- This form is intended to be used for official transfers only, so please ensure that the transfer is a formal process before completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns regarding the transfer, please contact the relevant department or manager for assistance.
