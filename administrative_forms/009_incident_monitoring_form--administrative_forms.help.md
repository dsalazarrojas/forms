# incident_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for incident monitoring and management. It helps to track and record details of incidents that have occurred, including their date, time, type, description, location, status, assignee, priority, due date and time, and the person who reported it.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately for each incident.
2.  Use the date field to select the date the incident occurred.
3.  Use the time field to select the time the incident occurred.
4.  Choose the type of incident from the available options (Incident, Injury, Illness, or Other).
5.  Enter a detailed description of the incident.
6.  Enter the location where the incident occurred.
7.  Choose the status of the incident from the available options (Active, Resolved, Pending, or Cancelled).
8.  Select the person responsible for resolving the incident from the available options (John, Jane, Jim, or Other).
9.  Choose the priority of the incident from the available options (High, Medium, or Low).
10.  If necessary, select a due date and time for the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date**: This field is used to select the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time**: This field is used to select the time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Type**: This field allows you to choose the type of incident (Incident, Injury, Illness, or Other).
*   **Incident Description**: Enter a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Status**: Choose the status of the incident (Active, Resolved, Pending, or Cancelled).
*   **Incident Assignee**: Select the person responsible for resolving the incident (John, Jane, Jim, or Other).
*   **Incident Priority**: Choose the priority of the incident (High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Incident Due Date**: If necessary, select a due date for the incident.
*   **Incident Due Time**: If necessary, select a due time for the incident.
*   **Incident Reported By**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
