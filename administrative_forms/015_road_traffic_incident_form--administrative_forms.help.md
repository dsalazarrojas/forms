# Road Traffic Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report road traffic incidents, including details about the incident, location, date and time, and parties involved. It helps in documenting and tracking incidents to improve road safety and prevent future incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Traffic Incident Details" field and enter a brief description of the incident.
2. In the "Location" field, provide the exact location where the incident occurred.
3. In the "Date and Time" field, select the date and time of the incident using the date picker.
4. In the "Driver Details" field, enter any information about the driver involved in the incident.
5. In the "Vehicle Details" field, enter any information about the vehicle involved in the incident.
6. In the "Witness Details" field, enter any information about the witness or witnesses involved in the incident.
7. Select the level of police involvement in the incident from the "Police Involvement" dropdown.
8. Specify the level of damage to the vehicle from the "Damage" dropdown, selecting one or multiple options if applicable.
9. In the "Vehicle Registration" field, enter the vehicle's registration number.
10. In the "Vehicle Make and Model" field, enter the make and model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
11. Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traffic Incident Details** (`traffic_incident_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): This field is used to enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, required): This field is used to select the date and time of the incident using the date picker.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter any information about the driver involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter any information about the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Witness Details** (`witness_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter any information about the witness or witnesses involved in the incident.
* **Police Involvement** (`police_involvement`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the level of police involvement in the incident.
* **Damage** (`damage`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to specify the level of damage to the vehicle, selecting one or multiple options if applicable.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required): This field is used to enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (`vehicle_make_model`, text, required): This field is used to enter the make and model of the vehicle involved in the incident.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only submit new, unique incidents.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* This form is for administrative purposes only, and the information submitted will be used for reporting and tracking incidents.
