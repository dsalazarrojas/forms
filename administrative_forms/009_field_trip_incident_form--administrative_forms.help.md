# Field Trip Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Field Trip Incident Form is a tool used to report incidents that occurred during a field trip. It helps to document and investigate incidents, and can aid in improving future field trip planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the field trip details in the **Field Trip Details** field.
2. Select the **Date** of the incident.
3. Choose the **Time** of the incident.
4. Enter the **Location** of the incident.
5. Select the type of **Incident Type** that occurred (e.g. Incident, Other, Injury, etc.).
6. Provide a detailed description of the **Incident Description**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field Trip Details**: This field is where you can enter information about the field trip, such as trip purpose, destination, or any other relevant details.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the incident, including the address or a description of the area.
* **Incident Type**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Incident: Reportable incidents or near misses.
	+ Other: Choose this option if the incident doesn't fit into the other categories.
	+ Injury: Incidents that resulted in physical harm to personnel or equipment.
	+ Property Damage: Report damage to property, such as vehicles or buildings.
	+ Lost or Stolen Items: Report items that were lost or stolen during the field trip.
	+ Vehicle Damage: Report damage to vehicles.
	+ Other (please describe): Choose this option if the incident type doesn't fit into the other categories.
* **Incident Description**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any supporting details or circumstances.
