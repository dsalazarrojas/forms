### Franchise Agreement Verification Form

#### Franchise Agreement Verification Form
This form is used to verify the franchise agreement details of a particular franchise location.

#### How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Owner Information** (`franchise_owner_information`, text, required): Please enter the name and contact information of the franchise owner.
* **Franchise Store Information** (`franchise_store_information`, text, required): Enter the store name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Franchise Document Upload** (`franchise_document_upload`, text, required): Upload any relevant documents related to the franchise agreement.
* **Franchise Document Upload 2** (`franchise_document_upload_2`, text, optional): Upload any additional documents related to the franchise agreement (if applicable).
* **Franchise Document Upload 3** (`franchise_document_upload_3`, text, optional): Upload any further documents related to the franchise agreement (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Verification Date** (`franchise_agreement_verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date the franchise agreement was verified.
* **Franchise Agreement Status** (`franchise_agreement_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the franchise agreement (Active, Closed, or Pending).
* **Franchise Agreement Expiration Date** (`franchise_agreement_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the franchise agreement is set to expire (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Start Date** (`franchise_agreement_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the franchise agreement.
* **Franchise Agreement End Date** (`franchise_agreement_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the franchise agreement.
* **Franchise Operating Hours** (`franchise_operating_hours`, time, optional): Enter the operating hours of the franchise store.
* **Franchise Store Address** (`franchise_store_address`, text, required): Enter the store address.
* **Franchise Store City** (`franchise_store_city`, text, required): Enter the store city.
* **Franchise Store Zip** (`franchise_store_zip`, text, required): Enter the store zip code.
* **Franchise Store State** (`franchise_store_state`, text, required): Enter the store state.
* **Franchise Store Country** (`franchise_store_country`, text, optional): Enter the store country (if applicable).
* **Franchise Store Phone** (`franchise_store_phone`, text, required): Enter the store phone number.
* **Franchise Agreement Number** (`franchise_agreement_number`, text, optional): Enter the franchise agreement number (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Version** (`franchise_agreement_version`, text, optional): Enter the franchise agreement version (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Revision** (`franchise_agreement_revision`, text, optional): Enter any revisions to the franchise agreement (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Date** (`franchise_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date the franchise agreement was created.
* **Franchise Agreement Status 2** (`franchise_agreement_status_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional status information for the franchise agreement (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Status 3** (`franchise_agreement_status_3`, text, optional): Enter further status information for the franchise agreement (if applicable).
* **Franchise Agreement Status 4** (`franchise_agreement_status_4`, text, optional): Enter additional status information for the franchise agreement (if applicable).
