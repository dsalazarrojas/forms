# Overtime Work Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overtime Work Request Form is used by employees to submit their overtime work requests, which will then be reviewed and approved by the selected managers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your overtime request in the "Request Form" field.
2. Provide additional details about your request in the "Overtime Details" field.
3. Select the managers who will be approving your request from the dropdown list in the "Manager Approval" field.
4. If you have any additional comments or context about your request, enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form** (employee_request, text, required): Enter a brief description of the overtime work you are requesting.
* **Overtime Details** (overtime_details, text, required): Provide any relevant details about your overtime work request, such as the expected duration or any deadlines.
* **Manager Approval** (manager_approval, select_multiple, required): Select the managers who will be approving your request. Choose the managers who are most suitable and available for the approval process.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or context about your request, such as explanations or justifications for your overtime work. This is an optional field, but it can help facilitate a smooth approval process.

## Tips
* Double-check that you are not submitting duplicate requests.
* Make sure to select managers who are available and suitable for the approval process.
* Be concise and clear in your request and comments to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
