# Corporate Governance Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Corporate Governance Checklist Form is designed to collect relevant information about a company's corporate governance structure and practices. The form is meant to be used by internal staff to ensure the company's governance standards are met and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form for each company that requires assessment.
2. Ensure you have access to the necessary information about the company before filling out the form.
3. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
4. Select the correct options for each field.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A text field for any additional comments or information about the company.

### 2. Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company being assessed.

### 3. Board Size
* **Board Size** (`board_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of the company's board.

### 4. Management Structure
* **Management Structure** (`management_structure`, select_one, optional): Select the structure of the company's management:
	+ Small: The company has a small management structure.
	+ Medium: The company has a medium management structure.
	+ Large: The company has a large management structure.

### 5. Governance Standards
* **Governance Standards** (`governance_standards`, select_multiple, optional): Select the governance standards the company adheres to:
	+ Governance Standard 1: The company has a clear policy on conflict of interest.
	+ Governance Standard 2: The company has a whistleblower policy.
	+ Governance Standard 3: The company has a code of conduct.

### 6. Compliance Status
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, optional): Check the company's compliance status:
	+ True: The company is compliant with governance standards.
	+ False: The company is not compliant with governance standards.

### 7. Data Collection
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, date, optional): Enter the date when data was last collected about the company.

### 8. Review Date
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when this form was last reviewed.

Tips:

* Ensure accuracy and truthfulness in your answers.
* Review this form regularly to monitor changes in a company's governance structure and practices.
* This form is meant to be used for internal staff use only.
