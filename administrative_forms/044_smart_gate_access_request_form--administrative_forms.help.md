# Smart Gate Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Smart Gate Access Request Form is used to request access to restricted areas or resources within the organization. This form is used to collect information from users requesting access, and the submitted data is reviewed and approved by designated administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your phone number and department.
3. Specify your role within the organization.
4. Provide a brief explanation for why you need access to restricted areas or resources.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the Additional Comments field.
6. If you have a manager, please provide their name in the Manager Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name is the first part of your name. Please enter your first name, as it is displayed in the system.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name is the second part of your name. Please enter your last name, as it is displayed in the system.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure accurate communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number is used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to, if any.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Specify your role in the organization.
* **Reason for Access** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for why you need access to restricted areas or resources.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your request.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required if applicable): If you have a manager, please provide their name.

Note: This form does not require all fields, but some fields are marked as required to ensure accurate data collection.
