# Classroom Supplies Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for classroom supplies. It helps the purchasing department to process and fulfill your supply needs efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with all required fields.
2. Be as accurate as possible when providing information about the supplies needed.
3. Review and proofread your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Provide your full name as a teacher or staff member.
- **Requester ID Number** (`requester_id`, text, required): Enter your employee or staff ID number.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your school email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department or subject area you belong to.
  - General Education
  - Mathematics
  - Science
  - English Language Arts
  - Social Studies
  - Art
  - Music
  - Physical Education
  - Special Education
  - Administration
  - Other
- **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the grade levels you teach.
  - Kindergarten
  - Grade 1-2
  - Grade 3-5
  - Grade 6-8
  - Grade 9-12
- **Classroom Number** (`classroom_number`, text, optional): Enter the room number or location of your classroom.
- **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this requisition request.
- **Needed By Date** (`needed_by_date`, date, required): Specify when you need these supplies.
- **Supply Category** (`supply_category`, select_one, required): Select the primary category of the supplies you need.
  - Paper Products
  - Writing Instruments
  - Art Supplies
  - Classroom Decorations
  - Technology Accessories
  - Cleaning Supplies
  - Organizational Materials
  - Instructional Materials
  - Other
- **Item 1 Name** (`item_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name or description of the first item.
- **Item 1 Quantity** (`item_1_quantity`, text, required): Enter the quantity needed for the first item.
- **Item 1 Estimated Unit Cost** (`item_1_unit_cost`, text, optional): Provide an approximate cost per item if known.
- **Item 2 Name** (`item_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name or description of the second item.
- **Item 2 Quantity** (`item_2_quantity`, text, optional): Enter the quantity needed for the second item.
- **Item 2 Estimated Unit Cost** (`item_2_unit_cost`, text, optional): Provide an approximate cost per item if known.
- **Item 3 Name** (`item_3_name`, text, optional): Enter the name or description of the third item.
- **Item 3 Quantity** (`item_3_quantity`, text, optional): Enter the quantity needed for the third item.
- **Item 3 Estimated Unit Cost** (`item_3_unit_cost`, text, optional): Provide an approximate cost per item if known.
- **Additional Items** (`additional_items`, text, optional): List any other items needed with their quantities.
- **Total Estimated Cost** (`total_estimated_cost`, select_one, optional): Select the estimated total cost if known.
  - Under $25
  - $25 - $50
  - $51 - $100
  - $101 - $250
  - $251 - $500
  - Over $500
  - Unknown
- **Justification for Request** (`justification`, text, required): Explain why these supplies are needed.
- **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of the request.
  - Critical - Needed immediately
  - High - Needed within one week
  - Medium - Needed within two weeks
  - Low - Needed within one month
- **Budget Code or Account Number** (`budget_code`, text, optional): If known, provide the budget code to charge.
- **Previous Request Reference** (`previous_request_reference`, text, optional): If this item was requested before, provide the reference.
- **Special Instructions or Notes** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for the purchasing department.
- **Supervisor Approval Needed** (`supervisor_approval_needed`, select_one, required): Select whether this request requires supervisor approval.
  - Yes - Approval required
  - No - Within my budget authority
  - Unsure
