# quality_training_module_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for quality training module requests. It helps the system administrator to select the correct training modules that match the user's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **email** in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
4. Enter your **phone number** in the format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
5. Select your **training module** preferences by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
6. Select the **type of training module** you need by choosing from the available options.
7. Choose the **level of training** you require by selecting from the available options.
8. If you have a specific date for the module, you can enter it in the **Module Date** field. If not, leave it blank.
9. If you have a preferred date for the module, you can enter it in the **Preferred Date** field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com)).
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Select Modules** (select multiple, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you want to receive updates on all available modules, or "No" if you only want to receive updates on specific modules.
* **Module Type** (select one, required: false): Choose the type of training module you need.
* **Training Level** (select one, required: false): Choose the level of training you require.
* **Module Date** (date, required: false): If you have a specific date for the module, enter it.
* **Preferred Date** (date, required: false): If you have a preferred date for the module, enter it. This is optional.
 
## Tips

* Please ensure that you only submit this form once and provide the most up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the system administrator for further assistance.
