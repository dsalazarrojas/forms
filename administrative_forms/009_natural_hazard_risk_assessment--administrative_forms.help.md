# natural_hazard_risk_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about natural hazards and their associated risks. It helps in understanding the frequency, impact, and location of these hazards, and provides a platform to capture any additional information or notes that may be relevant to the risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the different sections and questions.
2. Select the natural hazard type that is relevant to your risk assessment (e.g., flood, earthquake, wildfire).
3. Enter the impact scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High) to indicate the potential impact of the hazard.
4. Enter the area affected by the hazard (e.g., city, county, state).
5. If the area affected is not listed, provide a description of the location.
6. Enter the frequency of the hazard (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
7. Enter the impact scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High) for the hazard.
8. Provide any additional notes or information related to the risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **natural_hazard_risk_assessment_form**: This field is where you enter a brief description of the natural hazard risk assessment.
* **natural_hazard_risk_type**: Select all natural hazard types that apply to your risk assessment (e.g., flood, earthquake, wildfire).
* **hazard_impact_scale**: Enter the potential impact of the hazard, with Low, Medium, or High as options.
* **hazard_impact_frequency**: Enter the frequency of the hazard, with Low, Medium, or High as options.
* **natural_hazard_risk_area**: Enter the area affected by the hazard (e.g., city, county, state).
* **natural_hazard_risk_area_other**: If the area affected is not listed, enter a description of the location.
* **natural_hazard_risk_area_other_location**: If you selected natural_hazard_risk_area_other, enter the location of the area affected.
* **natural_hazard_risk_area_other_frequency**: Enter the frequency of the hazard in the area you described.
* **natural_hazard_risk_area_other_impact**: Enter the impact scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High) for the hazard in the area you described.
* **natural_hazard_risk_area_other_impact_scale**: Enter the impact scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High) for the hazard in the area you described.
* **natural_hazard_risk_notes**: Enter any additional notes or information related to the risk assessment.
* **natural_hazard_risk_notes_other**: If you have any additional information or notes, enter them here.
* **natural_hazard_risk_type_other**: If you have any other natural hazard type not listed, enter a description of it.
