# Supplier Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of our internal suppliers. Your feedback will help us improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
2. Provide the supplier's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Rate the supplier's performance, reliability, and overall rating using the number scale in the "Performance Rating", "Reliability Rating", and "Overall Rating" fields.
5. Add any additional comments about the supplier in the "Comments" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the supplier in the "Supplier Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier being evaluated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, optional): Rate the supplier's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Reliability Rating** (`reliability_rating`, number, optional): Rate the supplier's reliability on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the supplier's overall performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the supplier.
* **Supplier Notes** (`supplier_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the supplier.

## Tips
* Please be honest and concrete in your evaluation to help us improve the supplier's performance.
* Please check our internal records to confirm whether you've submitted an evaluation for this supplier before submitting a new one.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
