# Tenant Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Billing Form is designed to collect information related to tenant billing inquiries, payment details, and property address information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the billing period.
2. **End Date**: Enter the end date of the billing period.
3. **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of rent paid.
4. **Frequency**: Choose the payment frequency, whether Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
5. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status as Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
6. **Notes**: Add any additional notes related to the billing.
7. **Tenant Information**:
	* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant.
	* **Tenant Email**: Enter the email of the tenant.
	* **Tenant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the tenant.
8. **Landlord Information**:
	* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord.
	* **Landlord Email**: Enter the email of the landlord.
	* **Landlord Phone**: Enter the phone number of the landlord.
9. **Property Information**:
	* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
	* **Property Number**: Enter the building number of the property.
	* **Unit Number**: Enter the unit number of the property.
10. **Contact Information**:
	* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
	* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Billing Form** (text, not required, 1): Enter a brief description of the billing inquiry.
* **Billing Inquiry** (text, not required, 2): Describe the nature of the billing inquiry.
* **Payment Details** (select_one, not required, 3): Choose the type of payment.
	+ **Rent**: Rent payment.
	+ **Utility**: Utility payment.
	+ **Other**: Other type of payment.
* **Start Date** (date, not required, 4): Enter the start date of the billing period.
* **End Date** (date, not required, 5): Enter the end date of the billing period.
* **Rent Amount** (number, not required, 6): Enter the amount of rent paid.
* **Frequency** (select_one, not required, 7): Choose the payment frequency.
	+ **Weekly**: Weekly payment.
	+ **Bi-Weekly**: Bi-Weekly payment.
	+ **Monthly**: Monthly payment.
* **Due Date** (date, not required, 8): Enter the due date of the payment.
* **Payment Status** (select_one, not required, 9): Select the payment status.
	+ **Paid**: Paid.
	+ **Unpaid**: Unpaid.
	+ **Partially Paid**: Partially paid.
* **Notes** (note, not required, 10): Add any additional notes related to the billing.
* **Email** (email, not required, 11): Enter the email of the tenant or contact person.
* **Phone** (text, not required, 12): Enter the phone number of the tenant or contact person.
* **Contact Person** (text, not required, 13): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (email, not required, 14): Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Phone** (text, not required, 15): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Tenant Name** (text, not required, 16): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email** (email, not required, 17): Enter the email of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone** (text, not required, 18): Enter the phone number of the tenant.
* **Landlord Name** (text, not required, 19): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email** (email, not required, 20): Enter the email of the landlord.
* **Landlord Phone** (text, not required, 21): Enter the phone number of the landlord.
* **Property Address** (text, not required, 22): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Number** (text, not required, 23): Enter the building number of the property.
* **Unit Number** (text, not required, 24): Enter the unit number of the property.
* **Building Number** (text, not required, 25): Enter the building number of the property.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to access and submit this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrative personnel.
