# Collaborative Work Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collaborative Work Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information about project proposals from individuals or organizations interested in collaborating on a project with our organization. It helps us assess the feasibility of the project and determine whether to proceed with it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting the form.
3. Be specific and detailed when providing information about your project proposal.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (inquirer_name): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (inquirer_email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (inquirer_phone): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Organization Name** (organization): Enter the name of your company or institution.
* **Department** (department): Enter your department (optional).
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter your job title.
* **Inquiry Date** (inquiry_date): Enter the date of submission.
* **Type of Collaboration** (collaboration_type): Select the type of collaboration you are proposing (e.g., Joint Project, Research Partnership, etc.).
* **Project Name** (project_name): Enter the name of the proposed project.
* **Project Description** (project_description): Describe the project in detail.
* **Project Goals** (project_goals): Explain what you hope to achieve with this project.
* **Expected Outcomes** (expected_outcomes): Describe the expected results of the project.
* **Proposed Timeline** (timeline): Provide the start and end dates of the project.
* **Expected Duration** (duration): Select the expected duration of the project (e.g., Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Resources Needed** (resources_needed): Select the resources required for the project (e.g., Personnel, Funding, Equipment, etc.).
* **Resources You Will Contribute** (resources_you_contribute): Describe what you bring to the collaboration.
* **Team Size Needed** (team_size_needed): Select the number of people needed from your partner organization.
* **Skills or Expertise Needed** (skills_needed): Describe the skills or expertise required for the project.
* **Budget Estimate** (budget_estimate): Enter an estimated budget for the project (optional).
* **Funding Source** (funding_source): Select the source of funding for the project (e.g., Internal Budget, External Grant, etc.).
* **Have You Collaborated Before** (previous_collaboration): Indicate whether you have collaborated with our organization before.
* **Potential Challenges** (potential_challenges): Describe any potential obstacles you foresee.
* **Success Metrics** (success_metrics): Explain how you will measure the success of the project.
* **Communication Preference** (communication_preference): Select your preferred method of communication (e.g., Email, Video Calls, etc.).
* **Meeting Frequency** (meeting_frequency): Select how often you would like to meet (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.).
* **Intellectual Property Considerations** (intellectual_property): Describe any intellectual property concerns.
* **Confidentiality Agreement Needed** (confidentiality_needed): Indicate whether a confidentiality agreement is required.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Provide any additional information about your project proposal.

Please review the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
