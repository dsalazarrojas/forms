This form is designed to be completed by employees who wish to update their emergency contact information. Please ensure that you only submit this form if you need to make changes to your current emergency contact information. If you've already submitted a previous version of this form, please do not resubmit unless you have updated information.
# employee_emergency_contact_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_emergency_contact_amendment_form is used to update an employee's emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your city, state, and postal code for your address.
3. Fill in your phone and email addresses.
4. Select your Primary Relationship with the emergency contact.
5. If you have a Secondary Relationship, select the options that apply.
6. Enter the name of the emergency contact.
7. Select the relationship type of the emergency contact.
8. Enter the contact method of the emergency contact.
9. Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_initial`, text, optional): Enter your middle name, if applicable.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state of your address.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Primary Relationship** (`primary_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Secondary Relationship** (`secondary_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select any secondary relationships with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select the relationship type of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Label** (`emergency_relationship_label`, text, optional): Enter a label for the emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Street** (`emergency_street`, text, required): Enter the street address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Postal Code** (`emergency_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_relationship_type`, select_one, required): Select the relationship type of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Label** (`emergency_relationship_label`, text, optional): Enter a label for the emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
* **Primary Contact Method** (`primary_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select the primary contact method of the emergency contact.
* **Contact Method Label** (`contact_method_label`, text, optional): Enter a label for the contact method.
