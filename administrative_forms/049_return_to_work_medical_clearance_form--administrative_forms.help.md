# Return To Work Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Return To Work Medical Clearance Form is a document used to clear an employee's return to work after an absence. It is a crucial step in the process, as it ensures that the employee is fit for duty and has the necessary medical clearance to resume their job.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary documentation and information before starting the form.
2. Enter your Employer Name in the first field.
3. Provide your Employee Name in the second field.
4. Select your current Work Status from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the Date of Return in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
6. If applicable, indicate Medical Clearance as "Yes" or "No".
7. Enter any additional Medical Certificate or note in the Medical Certificate field.
8. If required, enter the Doctor's Name and title in the corresponding fields.
9. Enter your signature in the Signature field.
10. Enter the Date of Signature in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (Employer Name, text, required): Enter your company's or organization's name.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your name as an employee.
* **Work Status** (Work Status, select_one, required): Select the current status of your work, such as Active, On Leave, Retired, Resigned, or Deceased.
* **Date of Return** (Date of Return, date, required): Enter the date you plan to return to work in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Medical Clearance** (Medical Clearance, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have obtained medical clearance to return to work, either as "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Certificate** (Medical Certificate, text, optional): Enter any additional medical certificate or documentation required for your return to work.
* **Doctor Name** (Doctor Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Title** (Doctor Title, text, optional): Enter the title of your healthcare provider.
* **Medical Note** (Medical Note, text, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or comments.
* **Signature** (Signature, text, required): Sign and date your declaration of fitness for duty.
* **Date of Signature** (Date of Signature, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- Keep a copy of the form for your records.
- Consult with HR or management if you have any questions or concerns.
