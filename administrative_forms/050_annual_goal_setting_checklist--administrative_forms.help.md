# Annual Goal Setting Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Annual Goal Setting Checklist is a tool to help you set and document your annual goals. It guides you in establishing clear, measurable objectives that are relevant to your role and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the participant information fields: **Name**, **Department or Team**, and **Position or Role**. These fields help us understand who you are and what team or department you belong to.
2. Select the year these goals are for. Choose the correct year to ensure your goals are aligned with the current year's objectives.
3. Set specific goals by filling out the **Goal X Description** fields for each goal. Describe each goal clearly and concisely.
4. For each goal, select the **Priority Level** that best reflects how important it is to you. This will help us understand the level of emphasis you should place on each goal.
5. Indicate how you will measure success for each goal by filling out the **Goal X Metrics** fields.
6. Enter the target completion date for each goal in the **Goal X Target Completion Date** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Your full name.
* **Department or Team** (text, optional): Your department or team.
* **Position or Role** (text, optional): Your job title.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Your work email address.
* **Goal Year** (number, required): The year these goals are for.
* **Goal Review/Completion Date** (date, optional): The date when goals will be evaluated.
* **Goal 1 Description** (text, required): A description of your first goal.
* **Goal 1 Priority Level** (select_one, optional): The level of importance for your first goal.
*   - Critical - Must achieve
*   - High - Very important
*   - Medium - Important
*   - Low - Nice to have
* **Goal 1 Measurable Metrics** (text, optional): How you will measure success for your first goal.
* **Goal 1 Target Completion Date** (date, optional): The date when your first goal should be completed.
* **Goal 2 Description** (text, required): A description of your second goal.
* **Goal 2 Priority Level** (select_one, optional): The level of importance for your second goal.
*   - Critical - Must achieve
*   - High - Very important
*   - Medium - Important
*   - Low - Nice to have
* **Goal 2 Measurable Metrics** (text, optional): How you will measure success for your second goal.
* **Goal 2 Target Completion Date** (date, optional): The date when your second goal should be completed.
* **Goal 3 Description** (text, optional): A description of your third goal.
* **Goal 3 Priority Level** (select_one, optional): The level of importance for your third goal.
*   - Critical - Must achieve
*   - High - Very important
*   - Medium - Important
*   - Low - Nice to have
* **Goal 3 Measurable Metrics** (text, optional): How you will measure success for your third goal.
* **Goal 3 Target Completion Date** (date, optional): The date when your third goal should be completed.
* **Resources Needed to Achieve Goals** (text, optional): List any resources or support you need to complete your goals.

Note: Each field should be filled out based on its corresponding goal.
