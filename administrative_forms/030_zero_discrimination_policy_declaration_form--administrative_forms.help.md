# zero_discrimination_policy_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for employees to declare their commitment to our organization's zero-discrimination policy. This form will be used to gather information about employee's declarations, which will be reviewed and documented by the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Fill in the fields as instructed, using the correct format for each field type (e.g., email, date, time).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Declare your commitment** (`Declare your commitment`, text, required): This field is where you will declare your commitment to our organization's zero-discrimination policy.
* **Organization name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): If you are an employee, please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, please enter it. This will be used to contact you if necessary.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it. This will be used to contact you if necessary.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the declaration, please enter it in the correct format (e.g., 2022-02-20).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for the declaration, please enter it in the correct format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or explanations, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
- Please review your declaration carefully before submitting it to ensure it accurately represents your commitment.
