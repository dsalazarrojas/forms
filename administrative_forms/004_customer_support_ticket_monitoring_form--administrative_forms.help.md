# Customer Support Ticket Monitoring Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and track the progress of customer support tickets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the ticket details, including ticket status, customer name, and ticket date.
2. Select the current status of the ticket (Open, Closed, or Held).
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Choose the priority level of the ticket (Low, Medium, or High).
5. Describe the issue or problem that the customer is facing.
6. Note the resolution or outcome of the ticket.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Ticket Details
* **Ticket Status**: Select the current status of the ticket from the options provided (Open, Closed, or Held). This field is used to indicate the current state of the ticket.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who submitted the ticket.
* **Ticket Date**: Enter the date the ticket was created or last updated.
* **Ticket Description**: Enter a brief description of the issue or problem that the customer is facing.

### 2. Ticket Status
* **Ticket Status**: Select the current status of the ticket from the options provided (Open, Closed, or Held). This field is used to indicate the current state of the ticket.
* **Ticket Priority**: Choose the priority level of the ticket from the options provided (Low, Medium, or High). This field helps to prioritize tickets based on urgency and importance.
* **Ticket Resolution**: Enter a brief description of how the ticket was resolved or closed.
* **Customer Support Agent**: Enter the name of the customer support agent assigned to handle the ticket.

### Tips
* Be sure to keep the ticket status up-to-date as the ticket is being worked on.
* Use the ticket priority field to prioritize tickets based on urgency and importance.
* Keep the ticket resolution notes brief but clear to ensure accurate tracking of the issue.
* Use this form to review and update customer tickets regularly to ensure that they are being handled efficiently and effectively.
