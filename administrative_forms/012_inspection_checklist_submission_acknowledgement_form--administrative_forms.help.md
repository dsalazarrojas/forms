# Inspection Checklist Submission Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the submission of inspection results, acknowledging the date of submission, and collecting additional comments from the submitter.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the submit button to begin the submission process.
2. Fill in your name as the submitter in the designated field.
3. Enter the date of submission using the date field.
4. Provide a brief description of the inspection results in the text area.
5. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes in the text area below.
6. Click on the submit button to submit your acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting this form. This field is not required, but your name will be associated with the submission for tracking purposes.
* **Date of Submission**: Select the date when the inspection results were submitted. This field is required to track the submission date.
* **Inspection Results**: Describe the outcomes of the inspection, including any notable findings or recommendations. This field is optional but recommended for a detailed understanding of the inspection results.
* **Acknowledgement**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection submission. This field is optional and can be used to add context or clarification to the submitted inspection results.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you submit accurate and up-to-date information about the inspection results.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
