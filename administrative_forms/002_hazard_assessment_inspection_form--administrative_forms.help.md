# hazard_assessment_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document information about potential hazards and risks in a workplace environment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure to complete all required fields.
2. Select the correct assessment type (Hazard, Risk, or Other).
3. Enter the assessment date.
4. Complete the Assessor Name, Assessor Phone, and Assessor Email fields with your contact information.
5. Select the workplace locations where the hazard was assessed.
6. Choose the risk category (Electrical, Falling, Fire, Heat/Flame, or Other).
7. Select the risk level (Low, Medium, High).
8. Provide a description of the hazard and risk.
9. Describe any prevention and control measures taken or recommended.
10. Describe any corrective action plans and measures completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A brief summary of the assessment.
  * This field is required.
  * Please enter a brief introduction to the assessment.
- **Assessment Type**: Select the type of assessment (Hazard, Risk, or Other).
  * This field is not required.
  * Choose the type of assessment that best describes the hazard or risk.
- **Assessment Date**: The date of the assessment.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter the date the assessment was conducted.
- **Assessor Name**: The name of the assessor.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter your name.
- **Assessor Phone**: The phone number of the assessor.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter your phone number.
- **Assessor Email**: The email of the assessor.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter your email address.
- **Assessor Title**: The title of the assessor.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter your title or position.
- **Workplace**: Select the workplace locations where the hazard was assessed.
  * This field is not required.
  * Choose the locations where the hazard was evaluated.
  * Options: General Office, Cafeteria, Warehouse, Laboratory, Other.
- **Risk Category**: Select the risk category (Electrical, Falling, Fire, Heat/Flame, Other).
  * This field is not required.
  * Choose the category of risk that best describes the hazard.
- **Risk Level**: Select the risk level (Low, Medium, High).
  * This field is not required.
  * Choose the level of risk that best describes the hazard.
- **Risk Description**: A description of the risk or hazard.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please provide a detailed description of the risk or hazard.
- **Prevention and Control Measures**: Describe any prevention and control measures taken or recommended.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please describe any measures taken or recommended to mitigate or eliminate the hazard.
- **Corrective Action Plan**: Describe any corrective action plans and measures completed.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please describe any actions taken to address the hazard or risk.
- **Prevention and Control Measures Completed**: Describe any prevention and control measures completed.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please describe any measures completed to mitigate or eliminate the hazard.
- **Follow Up Date**: The date of the follow-up assessment.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter the date of the follow-up assessment.
- **Follow-up Action Plan**: Describe any follow-up actions planned.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please describe any actions planned to follow-up on the hazard or risk.
- **Follow Up**: Add any follow-up comments.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please add any additional comments.
- **Assessor Comments**: Add any assessor comments.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please add any additional comments or concerns.
- **Workplace Address**: The address of the workplace.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter the address of the workplace where the assessment was conducted.
- **Workplace Location**: The location of the workplace.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter the location of the workplace where the assessment was conducted.
- **Workplace Notes**: Add any additional notes about the workplace.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please add any additional notes.
- **Assessor Signature**: The signature of the assessor.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please sign to indicate completion of the assessment.
- **Assessor Date**: The date of the assessor signature.
  * This field is not required.
  * Enter the date of the assessor signature.
- **Risk Description 2**: Add any additional comments or descriptions.
  * This field is not required.
