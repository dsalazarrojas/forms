# Occupancy Permit Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupancy Permit Authorization Form is a critical document that allows property owners or their representatives to request an occupancy permit, a crucial step in the property development process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow the steps below:

1.  Provide your property information:
    * Enter the property owner's name and contact details.
    * Select the type of property (Single, Multi-unit, or Commercial).
    * Enter the property size.
2.  Enter your contact and submitter information:
    * Provide the contact person's name and contact details (phone and email).
    * Enter the submitter's name and contact details (phone and email).
    * Enter the submitter's title.
3.  Request an appointment:
    * Choose the request date and time.
4.  Add any additional comments or notes:
    * Enter any additional comments or notes about the property or the request.
5.  Sign off:
    * Place your signature to authorize the permit request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_owner**: 
  *   Enter the name of the property owner.

* **property_type**: 
  *   Choose the type of property (Single, Multi-unit, or Commercial).

* **property_size**: 
  *   Enter the size of the property (in square feet or acres).

* **property_address**: 
  *   Enter the property address where the permit is needed.

* **contact_person**: 
  *   Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the property.

* **contact_phone**: 
  *   Enter the contact person's phone number.

* **contact_email**: 
  *   Enter the contact person's email address.

* **request_date**: 
  *   Choose the date for the permit request (YYYY-MM-DD format).

* **request_time**: 
  *   Choose the time for the permit request (HH:MM format).

* **notes**: 
  *   Enter any additional comments or notes about the property or the request.

* **signature**: 
  *   Place your signature to authorize the permit request.

* **submitter**: 
  *   Enter the name of the person submitting the form (typically the property owner or their representative).

* **submitter_phone**: 
  *   Enter the submitter's phone number.

* **submitter_email**: 
  *   Enter the submitter's email address.

* **submitter_title**: 
  *   Enter the submitter's title or position.

* **submitter_signature**: 
  *   Place your signature as the submitter to confirm the information provided.

**Tips**
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
- This form should only be filled out once for each property.
