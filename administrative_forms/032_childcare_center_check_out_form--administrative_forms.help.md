# Childcare Center Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document information for a child's check-out from the childcare center. It captures details about the child, the person picking up the child, and any notable events that occurred during the child's stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Check-Out Date**: Enter the date of check-out from the date picker.
2. **Check-Out Time**: Select the time of departure from the time picker.
3. **Child First Name**: Enter the child's legal first name.
4. **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's legal last name.
5. **Child Age**: Enter the child's age in years.
6. **Child Room or Class**: Enter the child's assigned room or class name.
7. **Pickup Person First Name**: Enter the pickup person's legal first name.
8. **Pickup Person Last Name**: Enter the pickup person's legal last name.
9. **Pickup Person Phone**: Enter the pickup person's phone number.
10. **Pickup Person ID Type**: Select the type of identification provided by the pickup person from the list.
11. **Pickup Person ID Number**: Enter the pickup person's ID number (if applicable).
12. **Relationship to Child**: Select the relationship between the pickup person and the child.
13. **Authorized for Pickup**: Select whether the pickup person is authorized to pick up the child.
14. **Child Condition at Pickup**: Describe the child's condition at pickup (e.g. happy, tired, upset).
15. **Incidents During the Day**: Select whether there were any incidents during the child's stay.
16. **Incident Details**: Provide a brief description of any incidents that occurred.
17. **Meals Consumed**: Select how much the child ate during their stay.
18. **Nap Quality**: Describe the child's nap quality during their stay.
19. **Bowel Movement or Diaper Change**: Select whether there were any notable bowel movements or diaper changes.
20. **Medications Administered**: Select whether any medications were given to the child.
21. **Items Returning Home**: List any items being sent home with the child.
22. **Staff Notes for Parents**: Provide any important notes from the staff.
23. **Expected Tomorrow**: Select whether the child will attend the next day.
24. **Pickup Person Signature**: Sign your full name as the pickup person.
25. **Staff Initials**: Enter the staff member's initials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-Out Date**: The date when the child is leaving the center.
* **Check-Out Time**: The time when the child is leaving the center.
* **Child First Name**: The child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name**: The child's legal last name.
* **Child Age**: The child's age in years.
* **Child Room or Class**: The child's assigned room or class name.
* **Pickup Person First Name**: The name of the person picking up the child.
* **Pickup Person Last Name**: The last name of the person picking up the child.
* **Pickup Person Phone**: The phone number of the person picking up the child.
* **Pickup Person ID Type**: The type of identification provided by the pickup person (driver's license, state ID, passport, etc.).
* **Pickup Person ID Number**: The ID number of the person picking up the child (if applicable).
* **Relationship to Child**: The relationship between the pickup person and the child (e.g. mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Authorized for Pickup**: Whether the person picking up the child is authorized to do so.
* **Child Condition at Pickup**: The child's condition when being picked up (e.g. happy, tired, upset).
* **Incidents During the Day**: Whether any incidents occurred during the child's stay.
* **Incident Details**: A description of any incidents that occurred.
* **Meals Consumed**: How much the child ate during their stay.
* **Nap Quality**: The quality of the child's nap during their stay.
* **Bowel Movement or Diaper Change**: Whether there were any notable bowel movements or diaper changes.
* **Medications Administered**: Whether any medications were given to the child.
* **Items Returning Home**: Any items being taken home with the child.
* **Staff Notes for Parents**: Notes from the staff that may be useful to the parents.
* **Expected Tomorrow**: Whether the child will attend the next day.
* **Pickup Person Signature**: The electronic signature of the person picking up the child.
* **Staff Initials**: The initials of the staff member releasing the child.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information is accurate and complete.
* If unsure about any information, please ask a staff member for assistance.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact the center's administration.
* Thank you for your cooperation.
