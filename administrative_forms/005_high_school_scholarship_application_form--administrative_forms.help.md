# High School Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from high school students about their personal, academic, and extracurricular achievements to evaluate their eligibility for scholarships.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details page with your name, contact information, and any other required fields.
2. Select the grade level you are currently in from 9th to 12th on the academic achievements page.
3. Choose multiple extracurricular activities you are involved in on the extracurricular activities page.
4. Enter details about any community service hours you have completed on the community service page.
5. List any awards you have received on the awards page.
6. Select the number of hours you have completed in community service on the financial need page.
7. Select a reference type on the references page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (personal_details, text, required): This page asks for some basic information about you, including your name and contact information.
* **Academic Achievements** (academic_achievements, select_one, required): Select the grade level you are currently in from 9th to 12th.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (extracurricular_activities, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple activities you are involved in, such as Band, Debate Team, Football, or Volleyball.
* **Community Service** (community_service, number, optional): Enter details about any community service hours you have completed.
* **Community Service** (community_service, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple community service options you are involved in, such as Volunteer, Tutoring, or Mentoring.
* **Awards** (awards, text, optional): List any awards you have received.
* **Awards** (awards, select_one, optional): Select one of the options (National Honor Society, School President, etc.) as an award if applicable.
* **Financial Need** (financial_need, number, optional): Select the number of hours you have completed in community service.
* **References** (references, select_one, optional): Select a reference type to submit a reference (Parent 1, Parent 2, or Parent 3).
