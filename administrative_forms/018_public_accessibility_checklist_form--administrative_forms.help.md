# public_accessibility_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's knowledge and compliance with public accessibility standards within an organization. It is used for internal purposes, such as training and compliance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, organization, contact phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter your address.
4. Specify the accessibility standards you are familiar with.
5. Indicate your contact email and the accessibility standards you require.
6. Describe the access requirements for your role or position.
7. List the access policies that apply to your work.
8. Indicate your level of adherence to accessibility standards.
9. Provide proof of access policies documents.
10. Specify the frequency of accessibility standards training.
11. Enter the date of your last accessibility standards training.
12. List the people who attended accessibility standards training with you.
13. Specify who reviewed accessibility standards with you.
14. Indicate whether accessibility standards have been reviewed by you.
15. Specify the frequency of accessibility standards reviews.
16. Enter the document that outlines accessibility standards.
17. List the people who reviewed accessibility standards with you.
18. Indicate the date of the last accessibility standards review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Accessibility Standards** (`accessibility_standards`, text, required): Specify the accessibility standards you are familiar with.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Access Requirements** (`access_requirements`, text, required): Describe the access requirements for your role or position.
* **Access Policies** (`access_policies`, text, required): List the access policies that apply to your work.
* **Accessibility Standards Adherence** (`accessibility_standards_adherence`, number, required): Indicate your level of adherence to accessibility standards (e.g., 1-5).
* **Access Policies Document Proofs** (`access_policies_document_proofs`, text, required): Provide proof of access policies documents.
* **Accessibility Standards Training** (`accessibility_standards_training`, text, required): Specify the frequency of accessibility standards training (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Public Accessibility Checklist Form Submitted** (`public_accessibility_checklist_form_submitted`, select one, required): Mark whether you have submitted the form or not.
* **Accessibility Standards Training Frequency** (`accessibility_standards_training_frequency`, select multiple, required): Specify the frequency of accessibility standards training (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Accessibility Standards Compliance** (`accessibility_standards_compliance`, date, required): Enter the date of your last accessibility standards compliance.
* **Accessibility Standards Training Location** (`accessibility_standards_training_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you received accessibility standards training.
* **Accessibility Standards Review Frequency** (`accessibility_standards_review_frequency`, select multiple, required): Specify the frequency of accessibility standards reviews (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Accessibility Standards Document** (`accessibility_standards_document`, text, required): Enter the document that outlines accessibility standards.
* **Accessibility Standards Reviewers** (`accessibility_standards_reviewers`, select multiple, required): List the people who reviewed accessibility standards with you.
* **Accessibility Standards Review Date** (`accessibility_standards_review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the last accessibility standards review.
* **Accessibility Standards Training Attendees** (`accessibility_standards_training_attendees`, select multiple, required): List the people who attended accessibility standards training with you.
* **Accessibility Standards Reviewed By** (`accessibility_standards_reviewed_by`, select one, required): Specify who reviewed accessibility standards with you.
* **Accessibility Standards Reviewed** (`accessibility_standards_reviewed`, select one, required): Indicate whether accessibility standards have been reviewed by you.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your supervisor or training officer.
* This form is intended for internal use within the organization, so please do not share it outside the organization.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact your HR department.
