# Snow Removal Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Removal Service Agreement form is used to document the terms and conditions of a snow removal service. It is intended for businesses that offer snow removal services to residential or commercial properties. This form will guide you through the necessary information to create a comprehensive agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Service Provision**: In this field, provide a brief description of the snow removal service you are offering. This could include the scope of work, frequency of service, and any specific details about the service.
2.  **Payment Info**: In this section, select the payment options that apply to your business. You can choose multiple options to accommodate different payment scenarios.
    *  **Snow Removal Service**: Select if payment is associated with snow removal services.
    *  **Lawn Care**: Select if payment is associated with lawn care services.
    *  **Other**: Select if payment is associated with services not related to snow removal or lawn care.
3.  **Contact Info**: Provide a valid phone number or email address so the service recipient can contact you for inquiries or to request service.
4.  **Signature Date**: In this field, enter the date the service recipient signs the agreement.
5.  **Signature**: This is a note field where the service recipient signs their acceptance of the agreement. You should not enter anything here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Service Provision**: A short description of the snow removal service being offered.
*  **Payment Info**: Options for the payment terms associated with the service.
	+  **Snow Removal Service**: Payment related to snow removal services.
	+  **Lawn Care**: Payment related to lawn care services.
	+  **Other**: Payment related to services not described in the other options.
*  **Contact Info**: A valid phone number or email address for the service recipient to contact you.
*  **Signature Date**: The date the service recipient signs the agreement.
*  **Signature**: A note field for the service recipient to sign their acceptance of the agreement.
