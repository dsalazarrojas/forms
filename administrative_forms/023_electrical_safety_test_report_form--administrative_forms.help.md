# electrical_safety_test_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report the safety test results of electrical systems in an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill in this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the safety test results in the **Safety Test Results** field (Page 1).
2. Choose the **Electrical Systems Safety Status** for each system (Page 1).
3. Describe the **Electrical Systems Description** (Page 1).
4. Enter the **Test Location** where the safety test was conducted (Page 1).
5. List the **Electrical Systems Equipment** used during the test (Page 1).
6. Enter the date and time of the safety test in the **Safety Test Date** and **Safety Test Time** fields (Page 1).
7. Identify the person who conducted the test in the **Test Conducted By** field (Page 1).
8. Enter any comments or notes about the test in the **Comments** field (Page 1).
9. Choose the **Electrical Systems Risk Assessment** level (Page 1).
10. List any **Electrical Systems Hazards** identified during the test (Page 1).
11. Enter the title of the person who conducted the test in the **Test Conducted By Title** field (Page 1).
12. Attach any **Electrical Systems Evidence** collected during the test (Page 1).
13. Provide any references or sources used for the test in the **Electrical Systems References** field (Page 1).
14. Enter any other comments about the test in the **Other Comments** field (Page 1).
15. Choose whether certification is required for the electrical system (Page 1).
16. Choose the test frequency (Page 1).
17. Choose the test method used (Page 1).
18. Choose the test status (Page 1).
19. Enter any notes about the test in the **Electrical Systems Notes** field (Page 1).
20. Enter the date of the test report (Page 2).
21. Identify the person who reported the test in the **Test Reported By** field (Page 2).
22. Identify the person who tested the electrical system in the **Electrical Systems Tester** field (Page 2).
23. Enter the title of the person who tested the electrical system in the **Electrical Systems Tester Title** field (Page 2).
24. Identify the person who reviewed the test in the **Electrical Systems Reviewed By** field (Page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Test Results**: Enter the results of the safety test, including any defects or issues found. (Required)
* **Electrical Systems Safety Status**: Choose the status of the electrical system after the test. (Required)
* **Electrical Systems Description**: Describe the electrical system being tested. (Required)
* **Test Location**: Enter the location where the safety test was conducted. (Required)
* **Electrical Systems Equipment**: List the equipment used during the test. (Required)
* **Safety Test Date**: Enter the date of the safety test. (Required)
* **Safety Test Time**: Enter the time of the safety test. (Required)
* **Test Conducted By**: Identify the person who conducted the test. (Required)
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the test. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Risk Assessment**: Choose the risk assessment level. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Hazards**: List any hazards identified during the test. (Optional)
* **Test Conducted By Title**: Enter the title of the person who conducted the test. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Evidence**: Attach any evidence collected during the test. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems References**: Provide any references or sources used for the test. (Optional)
* **Other Comments**: Enter any other comments about the test. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Certication Required**: Choose whether certification is required for the electrical system. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Test Frequency**: Choose the test frequency. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Test Method**: Choose the test method used. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Test Status**: Choose the test status. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Notes**: Enter any notes about the test. (Optional)
* **Test Report Date**: Enter the date of the test report. (Optional)
* **Test Reported By**: Identify the person who reported the test. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Tester**: Identify the person who tested the electrical system. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Tester Title**: Enter the title of the person who tested the electrical system. (Optional)
* **Electrical Systems Reviewed By**: Identify the person who reviewed the test. (Optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Enter any relevant information to ensure a complete and accurate test report.
* Use the options available for the test status, risk assessment, test method, and test frequency.
* Attach any evidence or references collected during the test.
* Review the test report before submitting it.
