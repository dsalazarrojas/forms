# Unpaid Leave Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and record unpaid leave from work, including the type of leave, start and end dates, reason, and manager's approval and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Details section with your information.
2. Select the type of leave you are requesting in the Leave Type section.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your leave in the Start Date and End Date sections.
4. Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave in the Reason section.
5. If your manager needs to approve your leave, select their approval in the Manager Approval section.
6. If your manager has any comments or feedback, they can enter it in the Manager Comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, ID number, and any other relevant employee information.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., "option 1", "option 2", "option 3").
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want your leave to start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want your leave to end.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Explain your reason for the leave in a clear and concise manner.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Select your manager's approval for your leave.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback your manager wants to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Check with your HR representative or supervisor if you are unsure about any field.
* If you have already submitted a leave request or approval, please do not duplicate the effort.
