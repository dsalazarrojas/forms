# Workplace Incident Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording and documenting workplace incidents. It captures essential information about the incident, including date, location, nature, and resolution. The form helps track and manage incidents, ensuring accurate and thorough documentation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the incident details carefully and ensure you are reporting the incident accurately.
* Fill in the required fields, including incident date, location, nature, and resolution.
* Select the relevant options from the provided drop-down menus for incident nature, investigation, resolution, status, category, and priority.
* Enter any relevant comments or additional information about the incident.
* Sign and date your report to confirm that it has been completed and reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Incident Description (1)
* **Incident Description**: A text field to provide a clear and concise description of the incident.
### Incident Date (2)
* **Incident Date**: A date field to enter the date the incident occurred.
### Incident Time (3)
* **Incident Time**: A time field to enter the time the incident occurred.
### Incident Location (4)
* **Incident Location**: A text field to enter the location where the incident occurred.
### Incident Nature (5)
* **Incident Nature**: A drop-down menu to select the nature of the incident from the provided options.
### Incident Investigation (6)
* **Incident Investigation**: A drop-down menu to select the investigation type from the provided options.
### Incident Report Date (7)
* **Incident Report Date**: A date field to enter the date the incident was reported.
### Incident Resolution (8)
* **Incident Resolution**: A drop-down menu to select the resolution of the incident from the provided options.
### Incident Status (9)
* **Incident Status**: A drop-down menu to select the status of the incident from the provided options.
### Incident Category (10)
* **Incident Category**: A drop-down menu to select the category of the incident from the provided options.
### Incident Priority (11)
* **Incident Priority**: A drop-down menu to select the priority of the incident from the provided options.
### Incident Reporter (12)
* **Incident Reporter**: A text field to enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
### Incident Supervisor (13)
* **Incident Supervisor**: A text field to enter the name of the person supervising the incident.
### Incident Safety Officer (14)
* **Incident Safety Officer**: A text field to enter the name of the person responsible for safety.
### Incident Comments (15)
* **Incident Comments**: A text field to provide any additional comments or information about the incident.
### Additional Comments (16)
* **Additional Comments**: A text field to provide any further comments or information about the incident.
### Reviewed By (17)
* **Reviewed By**: A text field to enter the name of the person reviewing the incident.
### Date Reviewed (18)
* **Date Reviewed**: A date field to enter the date the incident was reviewed.
### Verified By (19)
* **Verified By**: A text field to enter the name of the person verifying the incident.
### Date Verified (20)
* **Date Verified**: A date field to enter the date the incident was verified.
### Reported By (21)
* **Reported By**: A text field to enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
### Report Date (22)
* **Report Date**: A date field to enter the date the incident was reported.
### Review Date (23)
* **Review Date**: A date field to enter the date the incident was reviewed.
### Released By (24)
* **Released By**: A text field to enter the name of the person releasing the incident.
### Release Date (25)
* **Release Date**: A date field to enter the date the incident was released.

Please note that all fields with a required attribute of "false" are optional.
