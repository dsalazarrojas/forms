# Power Of Attorney Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a Power of Attorney declaration, a legal document that grants authority to another person or entity to act on behalf of the grantor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal and contact information, including your name, address, and contact information. This will be the grantor information.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the agent you are granting power to.
3. Select the type of Power of Attorney you are creating (General, Special, or Medical).
4. Select the type of authority you are granting (Grantor, Attorney-In-Fact, or Both).
5. Sign the form as the grantor and the agent.
6. Have a notary public sign and stamp the document, confirming that it has been executed properly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **grantor_name**: Enter your full name as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **grantor_address**: Enter your full address as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **grantor_contact**: Enter your contact information as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **grantor_email**: Enter your email address as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **grantor_dob**: Enter your date of birth as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **grantor_signature**: Sign this field as the individual or entity granting the power of attorney.
* **agent_name**: Enter the name of the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **agent_address**: Enter the address of the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **agent_contact**: Enter the contact information of the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **agent_email**: Enter the email address of the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **agent_dob**: Enter the date of birth of the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **agent_signature**: Sign this field as the individual or entity receiving the power of attorney.
* **notary_public**: Select the title of the notary public who is signing and stamping the document (Notary Public, Notary, or Notary Commission).
* **date_of_declaration**: Enter the date the power of attorney is being declared.
* **witness_name**: Enter the name of the witness who is signing the document (optional).
* **witness_signature**: Sign this field as the witness (optional).
* **notary_public_name**: Enter the name of the notary public who is signing and stamping the document (optional).
* **notary_public_address**: Enter the address of the notary public who is signing and stamping the document (optional).
* **notary_public_signature**: Sign this field as the notary public (optional).
* **date_of_issuance**: Enter the date the power of attorney is being issued.
* **power_of_attorney**: Select the type of Power of Attorney you are creating (General, Special, or Medical).
* **power_of_attorney_type**: Select the type of authority you are granting (Grantor, Attorney-In-Fact, or Both).
* **date_of_revision**: Enter the date the power of attorney is to be revised.
* **date_of_expiration**: Enter the date the power of attorney is to expire (optional).

Please note that some fields may not be required for your specific use case. Consult with a legal professional for specific guidance on the usage of this form.
