# partnership_agreement_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The partnership_agreement_release_form is a document used to formalize the termination of a partnership agreement. It captures key information about the agreement, including the termination date, termination reason, notice period, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete the "partner_names" field with the names of all partners involved in the agreement.
3. Select the correct "agreement_status" from the options provided (active, inactive, or cancelled).
4. Choose the "agreement_type" that best describes the partnership (partnership or non-partnership).
5. Select the "termination_type" that best describes the termination (mutual or unilateral).
6. Enter the "termination_date" and "termination_reason" details.
7. Fill in the "notice_period" fields (number and units) if applicable.
8. Enter the "termination_fees" details if applicable.
9. Provide details about the "exit_proceedings" if necessary.
10. Review the "terms_and_conditions" field for any additional information or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **partner_names** (Select Multiple, Required): List the names of all partners involved in the agreement.
* **termination_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the termination of the partnership agreement.
* **termination_reason** (Text, Required): Briefly describe the reason for the termination.
* **notice_period** (Number, Required): Enter the number of days or weeks/m months/years given for the notice period.
* **notice_period_units** (Select One, Required): Choose the units for the notice period (week, month, or year).
* **agreement_status** (Select One, Required): Select the current status of the agreement (active, inactive, or cancelled).
* **agreement_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of agreement (partnership or non-partnership).
* **termination_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of termination (mutual or unilateral).
* **termination_fees** (Number, Optional): Enter the termination fees if applicable.
* **termination_fees_currency** (Select One, Optional): Choose the currency for the termination fees (usd, eur, or gbp).
* **termination_fees_amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the amount of termination fees.
* **termination_fees_description** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the termination fees.
* **terms_and_conditions** (Text, Optional): Review and provide any additional terms or conditions related to the termination.
* **exit_proceedings** (Note, Optional): Enter any details about the exit proceedings if necessary.
* **exit_proceedings_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the exit proceedings if applicable.
* **exit_proceedings_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the exit proceedings if necessary.
* **signature_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the signature.
* **signature_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the signature.
* **termination_fees_custom** (Text, Optional): Enter any custom termination fees information if necessary.
