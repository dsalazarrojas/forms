# overtime_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "overtime_report_form" is an administrative form used to collect information about employee-reported overtime hours, manager approvals, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Input** (Field 1) with your relevant information.
2. Select the **Report Date** (Field 2) from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the date format.
3. Enter the **Total Hours** (Field 3) you have worked overtime.
4. Choose your **Department** (Field 4) from the options provided.
5. Select whether to **Approve** (Field 5), **Deny** (Field 5), or **Delegate** (Field 5) the overtime claim.
6. Sign your **Employee Signature** (Field 6) to confirm the accuracy of the submitted information.
7. Have your **Manager Signature** (Field 7) sign to approve or deny the overtime claim.
8. Select the **Approval Date** (Field 8) for manager's approval.
9. Choose if the claim is an **Overtime Claim** (Field 9) and select 'Yes' or 'No'.
10. Add any **Comments** (Field 10) you have regarding the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (Field 1): Enter your name or ID to uniquely identify yourself in the report.
* **Report Date** (Field 2): Enter the date when you submitted your overtime claim.
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you have worked overtime.
* **Department** (Field 4): Select the department you belong to for accurate reporting and approval processes.
* **Manager Approval** (Field 5): Choose Approve, Deny, or Delegate to indicate your manager's decision.
* **Employee Signature** (Field 6): Sign and date to confirm the accuracy of your submitted information.
* **Manager Signature** (Field 7): Sign to approve or deny the claim.
* **Approval Date** (Field 8): Enter the date of your manager's approval.
* **Overtime Claim** (Field 9): Check 'Yes' if the hours are claimed as overtime, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Comments** (Field 10): Add any additional comments or explanations related to your claim.
