# Extended Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an extended leave of absence for an employee. An extended leave of absence is a paid or unpaid break in work that lasts for an extended period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Start by selecting the "extended_leave_absence_form_employee" text box to enter the employee's name.
* In the "start_date" field, select the date you plan to start your leave.
* In the "end_date" field, select the date you plan to return to work.
* Select the "leave_type" dropdown to choose the type of leave you are taking (e.g. Leave of absence, Other leave, Bereavement leave).
* In the "reason_for_leave" text box, enter a brief description of why you are taking the leave.
* In the "leave_details" text box, enter any additional information you feel is necessary to document your leave.
* In the "return_to_work_date" field, select the date you plan to return to work.
* In the "employee_id" field, enter your employee ID (this is an internal field for HR purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Extended Leave Absence Form Employee (extended_leave_absence_form_employee)**: Enter the name of the employee taking the leave.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: The date you plan to start your leave.
* **End Date (end_date)**: The date you plan to return to work.
* **Leave Type (leave_type)**: Choose the type of leave you are taking from the dropdown list (e.g. Leave of absence, Other leave, Bereavement leave).
* **Reason for Leave (reason_for_leave)**: Enter a brief description of why you are taking the leave.
* **Leave Details (leave_details)**: Enter any additional information you feel is necessary to document your leave.
* **Return to Work Date (return_to_work_date)**: The date you plan to return to work.
* **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter your employee ID (this is an internal field for HR purposes).
