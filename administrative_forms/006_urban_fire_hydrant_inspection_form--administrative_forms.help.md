# Urban Fire Hydrant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report on the status of fire hydrants in urban areas, ensuring that the fire department can keep their records up to date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, paying attention to the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
3. If a fire hydrant is not present, select "Removed" as its status.
4. Take photos of the fire hydrants, if possible, and select "True" for the "Photos" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Hydrant Location**: Enter the exact location of the fire hydrant, including the building number and street name. 
* **Address**: Enter the address of the fire hydrant.
* **Building Number**: Enter the building number associated with the fire hydrant.
* **Street**: Enter the street name associated with the fire hydrant.
* **City**: Select the city where the fire hydrant is located.
* **State**: Select the state where the fire hydrant is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the fire hydrant location (optional).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection, in 12-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector, who performed the inspection.
* **Inspector Email**: Enter your email address, where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Inspector Phone**: Enter your phone number, where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the fire hydrant, such as its condition or any issues found.
* **Photos**: Select "True" if you took photos of the fire hydrant, otherwise select "False".
* **Signature**: Enter your signature, as proof of the inspection.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Fire Department**: Enter the name of the fire department that you are inspecting on behalf of.
* **District**: Enter the district where the fire hydrant is located (optional).
* **Section**: Enter the section where the fire hydrant is located (optional).
* **Box**: Enter the box number where the fire hydrant is located (optional).
* **Hydrant Status**: Select the status of the fire hydrant, such as Active, Inactive, Removed, or Out of Service.
* **Fire Hydrant**: Select the direction of the fire hydrant, such as North, South, East, or West.
* **Hydrant Status 2**: Select additional status of the fire hydrant (optional).
* **Signature 2**: Enter another signature, for any additional inspectors or signatories.
* **Signature 2 Date**: Enter the date of the second signature, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
