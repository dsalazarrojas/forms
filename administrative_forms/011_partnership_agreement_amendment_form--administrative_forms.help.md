# Partnership Agreement Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Agreement Amendment Form is a tool for updating or modifying the terms of an existing partnership agreement. This form is designed to be used for amending partnership agreements, not for creating new ones. It's crucial to ensure that the form is filled out correctly and with accurate details to maintain the integrity and effectiveness of the partnership amendments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Business Name** (Field 2) with the correct name of the business that needs the partnership amendment.
2. Enter the **Amendment Date** (Field 4) accurately, as this date will be a part of the amendment record.
3. Provide the **Partner Name** (Field 5) and **Partner Role** (Field 6) for the partner involved in the amendment.
4. Review and fill out any additional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (Field 2):
	+ A text field where you enter the correct name of the business that needs the partnership amendment.
* **Amendment Date** (Field 4):
	+ A date field where you enter the date the amendment will take effect.
* **Partner Name** (Field 5):
	+ A text field where you enter the name of the partner involved in the amendment.
* **Partner Role** (Field 6):
	+ A text field where you enter the role or position of the partner in the business.
* **Business Name (again)** (Field 3):
	+ This field is a redundant field, but it is kept here for consistency with the original partnership agreement.

Note: This form is designed for internal use and adaptation. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact form@gic.mx.
