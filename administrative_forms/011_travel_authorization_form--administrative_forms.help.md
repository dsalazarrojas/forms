# Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Authorization Form is used to record and track employee travel activities. This form helps ensure that travel is properly approved and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Enter your travel dates**: In the "travel_dates" field, enter the dates of your travel.
2. **Enter travel reason**: In the "travel_reason" field, enter a brief explanation for your travel.
3. **Enter travel destination**: In the "travel_destination" field, enter the location you will be traveling to.
4. **Enter travel duration**: In the "travel_duration" field, enter the length of your trip in days.
5. **Enter travel budget**: In the "travel_budget" field, enter the estimated cost of your trip.
6. **Enter travel approver**: In the "travel_approver" field, enter the name of the person who will be approving your travel.
7. **Enter travel approver email**: In the "travel_approver_email" field, enter the email of the person who will be approving your travel.
8. **Enter travel manager**: In the "travel_manager" field, enter the name of the person responsible for managing your travel.
9. **Enter travel manager email**: In the "travel_manager_email" field, enter the email of the person responsible for managing your travel.
10. **Enter travel status**: In the "travel_status" field, enter the current status of your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Authorization Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of your travel in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Travel Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for your travel.
* **Travel Destination**: Enter the location you will be traveling to.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the length of your trip in days.
* **Travel Budget**: Enter the estimated cost of your trip.
* **Travel Approver**: Enter the name of the person who will be approving your travel.
* **Travel Approver Email**: Enter the email of the person who will be approving your travel.
* **Travel Manager**: Enter the name of the person responsible for managing your travel.
* **Travel Manager Email**: Enter the email of the person responsible for managing your travel.
* **Travel Status**: Enter the current status of your travel plans.
