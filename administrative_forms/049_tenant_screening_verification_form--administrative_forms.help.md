# Tenant Screening Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the information of a potential tenant, ensuring all necessary details are collected and accurately assessed by the landlord or property manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the tenant's **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields. Make sure to enter the correct information.
2. Enter the tenant's **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
3. Select the tenant's **Date of Birth** from the calendar or enter it manually in the correct format.
4. Enter the tenant's **Social Security Number** for identity verification.
5. Briefly provide your **Rental History**, including any relevant details about your past rental experiences.
6. Select up to 3 **References**, who can vouch for your character and rental history.
7. Choose the necessary **Rental Application Documents** required for your application, such as a lease agreement or pay stub.
8. Select the name of your current or previous **Landlord** from the options provided.
9. Enter the **Landlord Phone Number**, so we can contact them for verification purposes.
10. Enter the **Report Date**, which will help us track the progress of your application.
11. Provide any additional **Comments** you may have about your application or any concerns you'd like to address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email**: Enter your current email address where we can contact you.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format, such as MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number for identity verification.
- **Rental History**: Provide a brief description of your past rental experiences, including any relevant details.
- **References**: Select up to 3 people who can vouch for your character and rental history.
- **Rental Application Documents**: Choose the necessary documents required for your application, such as a lease agreement or pay stub.
- **Landlord**: Select your current or previous landlord from the options provided.
- **Landlord Phone**: Enter your landlord's phone number where we can contact them for verification.
- **Report Date**: Enter the date on which this form is being submitted.
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your application.
