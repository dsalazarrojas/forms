# Food Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Food Truck Inspection Form" is a tool used by food truck operators to gather information about their vehicle's safety and cleanliness status. This form is intended to ensure that food trucks meet the required standards for safe and clean operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Review the options for each field carefully.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Food Truck Inspection Form 1** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the food truck or its identifier.
- **License Number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the food truck's license number.
- **Vehicle Type** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of vehicle (Car, Truck, or Bus).
- **Food Handling Certification** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the status of the food handling certification (Certified or Pending).
- **Safety Equipment Included** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the safety equipment included with the vehicle (Fire Extinguisher, First Aid Kit, or Safety Sign).
- **Cleanliness Level** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Evaluate the level of cleanliness (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
- **Date Last Inspection** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the last inspection.
- **Time Last Inspection** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the last inspection.
- **Notes** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the food truck.
- **Assigned Inspector** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if an inspector has been assigned to the food truck (Yes or No).
- **Inspector Signature** (`select_one`, required/optional): Check if the inspector has signed the form (Signed or Unsigned).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy.
- Submit the form only after reviewing it thoroughly.
