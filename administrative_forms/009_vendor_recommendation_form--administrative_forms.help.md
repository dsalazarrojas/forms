# Vendor Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about vendors and gather user recommendations on their selection. This form is used to provide input on various vendors and help make informed decisions about vendor selection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form page to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor.
	* **Vendor Address**: Enter the address of the vendor.
	* **Vendor Email**: Enter the vendor's email address.
	* **Recommendation**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you recommend the vendor.
3. Optional fields:
	* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the vendor's phone number.
	* **Vendor Website**: Enter the vendor's website URL.
	* **Reason for Recommendation**: Provide a brief reason for recommending the vendor.
	* **Recommendation Rating**: Select a rating (Good or Fair) for the vendor.
	* **Recommendation Comments**: Provide additional comments about the vendor.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Page**: This is the main page of the form where you can input the vendor information.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the vendor you are recommending.
* **Vendor Address**: Enter the address of the vendor's location.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Vendor Website**: Enter the URL of the vendor's website.
* **Recommendation**: Select "Yes" to recommend the vendor or "No" not to.
* **Reason for Recommendation**: Provide a brief reason for recommending the vendor. This can help clarify your selection.
* **Recommendation Rating**: Choose a rating for the vendor. Select "Good" for a strong recommendation or "Fair" if you have some reservations.
* **Recommendation Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the vendor. This is an opportunity to provide more details about your recommendation.
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the vendor.
* Use a strong recommendation rating (Good) for vendors you highly recommend, and a Fair rating for those with some reservations.
* Use the "Reason for Recommendation" field to provide context for your rating.
