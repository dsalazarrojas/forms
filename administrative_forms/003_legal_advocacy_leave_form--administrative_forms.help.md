# Legal Advocacy Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Advocacy Leave Form is a document used to request time off for legal matters. Employees can use this form to notify their manager of their need to take time off for legal proceedings, such as court dates or lawyer appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the employee in the "employee_name" field.
2. Select the reason for your leave in the "legal_advocacy_activity" field.
3. Enter the start date of your leave in the "start_date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your leave in the "end_date" field.
5. Provide the reason for your leave in the "reason_for_leave" field.
6. Select the status of your leave request in the "manager_approval" field (Approved, Declined, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (Text, Required): This field is for the employee's name, please enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **legal_advocacy_activity** (Text, Required): This field is for the reason for your leave. Please select the reason for your legal advocacy activity (e.g. court date, lawyer appointment).
* **start_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you will start your leave from.
* **end_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you will end your leave by.
* **reason_for_leave** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details or context about your leave request, if necessary.
* **manager_approval** (Select Multiple, Optional): This field indicates the status of your leave request. Please select "Approved" if your manager has approved your leave, "Declined" if your manager has declined your leave, or "Pending" if your manager is still reviewing your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (employee_name, legal_advocacy_activity, start_date, end_date).
* Be as specific as possible when providing the reason for your leave to help your manager understand your needs.
* If your manager has declined your leave, they will notify you and provide a reason. If you are not satisfied with their response, you can discuss it with them directly.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
