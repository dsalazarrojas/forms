# Beneficial Ownership Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beneficial Ownership Declaration is used to provide information about a business entity and its beneficial owners for compliance and regulatory purposes. The form is designed to ensure that beneficial owners declare the accuracy of the information provided and certify that they understand the definition of beneficial ownership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the type of entity from the options provided in the "Entity Type" field.
2. Fill in the Business Entity Name and Business Registration Number if applicable.
3. Enter the Principal Place of Business.
4. If the entity has any beneficial owners, please provide the required information for each owner. For each owner, fill in the Full Name, Date of Birth, Nationality, Identification Type, Identification Number, Ownership Percentage, and Residential Address.
5. If there are multiple beneficial owners, click "Add Another Owner" to add more owner fields.
6. Once all required fields are completed, read the declaration and certification statements carefully and select the correct responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Entity Name** (Required, text): Enter the name of the business entity.
* **Entity Type** (Required, select_one): Select the type of entity, such as Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, LLC, Trust, or Other.
* **Business Registration Number** (Optional, text): Enter the business registration number if applicable.
* **Principal Place of Business** (Required, text): Enter the address of the principal place of business.
* **Date of Establishment** (Required, date): Enter the date the business was established.
* **Number of Beneficial Owners** (Required, number): Enter the number of beneficial owners.
* **Owner 1 - Full Name** (Required, text): Enter the full name of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 1 - Date of Birth** (Required, date): Enter the date of birth of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 1 - Nationality** (Required, text): Enter the nationality of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 1 - Identification Type** (Required, select_one): Select the type of identification document (e.g., Passport, National ID, or Driver's License).
* **Owner 1 - Identification Number** (Required, text): Enter the identification number of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 1 - Ownership Percentage** (Required, number): Enter the ownership percentage of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 1 - Residential Address** (Required, text): Enter the residential address of the first beneficial owner.
* **Owner 2 - Full Name** (Optional, text): Enter the full name of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Date of Birth** (Optional, date): Enter the date of birth of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Nationality** (Optional, text): Enter the nationality of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Identification Type** (Optional, select_one): Select the type of identification document (e.g., Passport, National ID, or Driver's License) if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Identification Number** (Optional, text): Enter the identification number of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Ownership Percentage** (Optional, number): Enter the ownership percentage of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **Owner 2 - Residential Address** (Optional, text): Enter the residential address of the second beneficial owner if applicable.
* **I declare that all information provided is accurate** (Required, select_one): Select "I Agree" to declare the accuracy of the information provided.
* **I understand the definition of beneficial owner** (Required, select_one): Select "True" to indicate that you understand the definition of beneficial ownership.
* **I consent to compliance verification** (Required, select_one): Select "I Agree" to consent to compliance verification.
* **Date of Declaration** (Required, date): Enter the date you are submitting this declaration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all required information available before starting the form.
* Review the guidance and examples provided in this help documentation if you have any doubts or uncertainties.
* Double-check that all information is accurate before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or compliance officer.
