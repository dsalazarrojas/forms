# event_planning_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event planning approval form is a necessary tool for event planning within the organization. It serves to gather essential event details and obtain approval from authorized approvers.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Enter Event Details
- **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
- **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (optional).
- **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
- **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event.
- **Budget**: Enter the budget for the event.
### Step 2: Enter Approver Information
- **Submitter's Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Submitter's Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Submitter's Name**: Enter your name.
- **Approver's Name**: Enter the name of the authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver's Email**: Enter the email address of the authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver's Phone**: Enter the phone number of the authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver's Signature**: Obtain the signature of the authorized approver.
### Step 3: Complete and Approve
- **Event Budget Breakdown**: Provide a breakdown of the event budget (optional).
- **Event Budget Notes**: Enter any additional notes regarding the event budget (optional).
- **Approver Comments**: Enter comments from the authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver Notes**: Enter any notes from the authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver Name2**: Enter the name of the secondary authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver Phone2**: Enter the phone number of the secondary authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver Email2**: Enter the email address of the secondary authorized approver (optional).
- **Approver Signature2**: Obtain the signature of the secondary authorized approver (optional).
