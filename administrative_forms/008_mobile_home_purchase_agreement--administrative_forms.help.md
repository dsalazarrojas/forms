# Mobile Home Purchase Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a mobile home purchase agreement, including buyer and seller information, property details, and financial terms of the sale.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information:
	* Buyer Information: Enter the buyer's contact details (phone and email)
	* Seller Information: Enter the seller's contact details (phone and email)
	* Property Address: Enter the property address
	* Financial Information:
		+ Purchase Price: Enter the sale price of the mobile home
		+ Down Payment: Enter the down payment amount
		+ Annual Interest Rate: Enter the annual interest rate as a decimal
		+ Term Length: Enter the term length of the loan
		+ Loan Amount: Enter the loan amount
	* Payment Terms: Select the payment method (Cash, Check, Online Banking, or Other)
	* Payment Schedule: Confirm whether a payment schedule will be used
	* Financing Type: Confirm whether financing will be used
	* Escrow Instructions: Enter any special escrow instructions
	* Signature Confirmation: Confirm that all parties have signed the agreement
	* Signature Date: Enter the date of signature
	* Signature Time: Enter the time of signature
	* Terms Accepted: Confirm that the terms have been accepted
	* Terms Version: Enter the version of the terms accepted
	* Terms Date: Enter the date of terms acceptance
	* Terms Time: Enter the time of terms acceptance
2. Review the form for accuracy and completeness.
