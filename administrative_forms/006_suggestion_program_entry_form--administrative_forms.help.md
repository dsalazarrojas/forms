# Suggestion Program Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suggestion Program Entry Form is designed to collect user suggestions and feedback on a program. This form is used to gather information from users and track progress on suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Name" field with a brief description of your suggestion.
2. Select one or more "Suggestion Type" from the provided options.
3. Provide a detailed "Description" of your suggestion.
4. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I agree" or "I do not agree".
5. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field in the format "example@example.com".
6. Enter a valid phone number in the "Contact Number" field in the format "1234567890".
7. Select the date of your suggestion using the calendar feature.
8. Select the time of your suggestion using the time picker feature.
9. Choose the status of your suggestion from the provided options.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: <program_name> - This field is required to enter a brief description of your suggestion.
* **Suggestion Type**: <suggestion_type> - Select one or more types of suggestions from the provided options.
* **Description**: <description> - Enter a detailed description of your suggestion.
* **I agree**: <consent_1> - Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **I do not agree**: <consent_2> - Select "Yes" or "No" if you do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Email**: <email> - Enter a valid email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Contact Number**: <contact_number> - Enter a valid phone number in the format "1234567890".
* **Date**: <suggestion_date> - Select the date of your suggestion using the calendar feature.
* **Time**: <suggestion_time> - Select the time of your suggestion using the time picker feature.
* **Status**: <suggestion_status> - Choose the status of your suggestion from the provided options.
