# Compliance Audit Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Audit Confirmation Form is used to confirm the details of an upcoming audit, including dates, auditor information, and company details. This form ensures that all parties involved are on the same page and that the audit proceeds as scheduled.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the audit in the **Audit Date** field.
2. Provide the name of the auditor and their credentials in the **Auditor Name** and **Auditor Credentials** fields, respectively.
3. Enter the name of the audit firm and the organization being audited in the **Audit Firm** and **Company Name** fields, respectively.
4. Select the type of audit (e.g., initial, surveillance, recertification, internal, external, or special) in the **Audit Type** field.
5. Select the scope of the audit (e.g., full organization, specific department, specific location, specific process, or multiple locations) in the **Audit Scope** field.
6. Select the standard being audited (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, ISO 45001, SOC 2, HIPAA, or GDPR) in the **Audit Standard** field.
7. Choose the expected duration of the audit in the **Expected Duration** field.
8. Enter the name of the primary contact person and their email and phone contact information in the **Primary Contact**, **Primary Contact Email**, and **Primary Contact Phone** fields, respectively.
9. Select which departments will be involved in the audit in the **Departments Involved** field.
10. Answer whether all required documents are prepared in the **Is Documentation Ready** field.
11. Indicate if the organization has been audited before in the **Previous Audits** field.
12. Provide the status of previous findings in the **Previous Findings Status** field.
13. Mention any known areas of concern in the **Areas of Concern** field.
14. Provide any special requirements needed for the audit in the **Special Requirements** field.
15. Indicate any access requirements for the auditor in the **Access Requirements** field.
16. Specify what equipment the auditor will need in the **Equipment Needed** field.
17. Select how many meeting rooms are required in the **Meeting Rooms Required** field.
18. Enter the date and time of the opening meeting in the **Opening Meeting Date** and **Opening Meeting Time** fields, respectively.
19. Enter the date and time of the closing meeting in the **Closing Meeting Date** and **Closing Meeting Time** fields, respectively.
20. Select the confirmation status of the audit in the **Confirmation Status** field.
21. Indicate any modifications needed in the **Modifications Needed** field.
22. Confirm who will represent the company during the audit in the **Company Representative** field.
23. Type in the name as it will appear as a signature in the **Representative Signature** field.
24. Enter the date of confirmation in the **Signature Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Audit Date
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the audit will take place.
### Auditor Name
* **Auditor Name** (`auditor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the auditor.
### Auditor Credentials
* **Auditor Credentials** (`auditor_credentials`, text, required): Enter the qualifications and certifications of the auditor.
### Audit Firm
* **Audit Firm** (`audit_firm`, text, required): Enter the name of the audit organization.
### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization being audited.
### Company Address
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the location of the audit.
### Audit Type
* **Audit Type** (`audit_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of audit (e.g., initial, surveillance, recertification, internal, external, or special).
### Audit Scope
* **Audit Scope** (`audit_scope`, select_one, required): Select the scope of the audit (e.g., full organization, specific department, specific location, specific process, or multiple locations).
### Audit Standard
* **Audit Standard** (`audit_standard`, select_one, required): Select the standard being audited (e.g., ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, ISO 45001, SOC 2, HIPAA, or GDPR).
### Expected Duration
* **Expected Duration** (`audit_duration`, select_one, required): Select the expected duration of the audit (e.g., 1 day, 2-3 days, 4-5 days, 1-2 weeks, or over 2 weeks).
### Primary Contact
* **Primary Contact** (`primary_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
### Primary Contact Email
* **Primary Contact Email** (`primary_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the primary contact person.
### Primary Contact Phone
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
### Departments Involved
* **Departments Involved** (`departments_involved`, select_multiple, required): Select which departments will be involved in the audit.
### Is Documentation Ready
* **Is Documentation Ready** (`documentation_ready`, select_one, required): Indicate whether all required documents are prepared.
### Previous Audits
* **Previous Audits** (`previous_audits`, select_one, required): Indicate if the organization has been audited before.
### Previous Findings Status
* **Previous Findings Status** (`previous_findings_status`, select_one, false): Indicate the status of previous findings.
### Areas of Concern
* **Areas of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, text, false): Provide any known areas of concern.
### Special Requirements
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, false): Provide any special requirements needed for the audit.
### Access Requirements
* **Access Requirements** (`access_requirements`, text, false): Indicate any access requirements for the auditor.
### Equipment Needed
* **Equipment Needed** (`equipment_needed`, text, false): Specify what equipment the auditor will need.
### Meeting Rooms Required
* **Meeting Rooms Required** (`meeting_rooms_required`, select_one, true): Select how many meeting rooms are required.
### Opening Meeting Date
* **Opening Meeting Date** (`opening_meeting_date`, date, true): Enter the date of the opening meeting.
### Opening Meeting Time
* **Opening Meeting Time** (`opening_meeting_time`, time, true): Enter the time of the opening meeting.
### Closing Meeting Date
* **Closing Meeting Date** (`closing_meeting_date`, date, true): Enter the date of the closing meeting.
### Closing Meeting Time
* **Closing Meeting Time** (`closing_meeting_time`, time, true): Enter the time of the closing meeting.
### Confirmation Status
* **Confirmation Status** (`confirmation_status`, select_one, true): Select the confirmation status of the audit (e.g., confirmed, confirmed with modifications, pending, or cancelled).
### Modifications Needed
* **Modifications Needed** (`modifications_needed`, text, false): Indicate any modifications needed.
### Company Representative
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, true): Confirm who will represent the company during the audit.
### Representative Signature
* **Representative Signature** (`representative_signature`, text, true): Type in the name as it will appear as a signature.
### Signature Date
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, true): Enter the date of confirmation.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the auditor or the organization's representative.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all details are correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization's representative or the auditor.
* The audit schedule and details may change, so it's essential to review and confirm them regularly.
* Make sure to provide all necessary documentation and information to the auditor.
* The auditor will need to have access to the necessary equipment and meeting rooms.
* Be prepared to discuss any areas of concern and modifications needed.
* Confirm the status of previous findings and provide any relevant documentation.
* Ensure that the company representative is aware of their role and responsibilities during the audit.
