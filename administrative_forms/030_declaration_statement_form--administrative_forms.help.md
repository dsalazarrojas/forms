# Declaration Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Declaration Statement Form is a simple, text-based form designed to collect general information about a declaration statement. It can be used for any type of declaration, such as declarations of intent, declarations of rights, etc.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name.
2. Specify the purpose of your declaration.
3. Type your declaration statement.
4. Enter the date and time of your declaration.
5. Choose the type of declaration you are making.
6. Select the status of your declaration.
7. Provide a reference for your declaration, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`declaration_statement_form_1`, text, required/optional): Please provide your full name as it should be displayed in the declaration.
* **Declaration purpose** (`declaration_statement_form_2`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the purpose of your declaration.
* **Declaration statement** (`declaration_statement_form_3`, text, required/optional): Type your declaration statement in the text field.
* **Declaration date** (`declaration_statement_form_4`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which you made the declaration.
* **Declaration time** (`declaration_statement_form_5`, time, required/optional): Enter the time at which you made the declaration.
* **Declaration type** (`declaration_statement_form_6`, text, required/optional): Specify the type of declaration you are making (e.g., declaration of intent, declaration of rights, etc.).
* **Declaration status** (`declaration_statement_form_7`, text, required/optional): Indicate the status of your declaration (e.g., active, inactive, pending, etc.).
* **Declaration reference** (`declaration_statement_form_8`, text, required/optional): If this declaration is related to another declaration, provide a reference to that declaration, otherwise, leave blank.

## Tips
- This form is meant for general use, and the fields are designed to be flexible.
- If you're unsure about a field, please contact us at `form@gic.mx` for assistance.
- The form does not collect sensitive or confidential information; only general information about declarations.
- You can fill out the form as many times as you need to, but please do not submit duplicate declarations with the same title or purpose.
