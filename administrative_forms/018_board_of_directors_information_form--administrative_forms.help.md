# Board Of Directors Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board of Directors Information Form is used to collect and store information about the current members of the board, including their contact details, work information, and board-specific roles and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your middle initial, if applicable, in the **Middle Initial** field.
4. Select your suffix (e.g. Jr, Sr, II, etc.) from the **Suffix** dropdown menu.
5. Enter your primary email address in the **Primary Email** field.
6. Enter an optional secondary email address in the **Secondary Email** field.
7. Enter your mobile phone number in the **Mobile Phone** field.
8. Enter your work phone number, if applicable, in the **Work Phone** field.
9. Enter your street address in the **Street Address** field.
10. Enter your city in the **City** field.
11. Enter your state or province in the **State** field.
12. Enter your ZIP code in the **ZIP Code** field.
13. Select your country from the **Country** dropdown menu.
14. Select your current board position from the **Board Position** dropdown menu.
15. Select your current board status from the **Board Status** dropdown menu.
16. Enter the start date of your term in the **Term Start Date** field.
17. Enter the end date of your term in the **Term End Date** field.
18. Enter the name of your company or employer in the **Company Name** field.
19. Enter your job title in the **Job Title** field.
20. Enter a brief summary of your professional background in the **Professional Background** field.
21. Enter any relevant education and degrees in the **Education** field.
22. Enter any relevant board certifications in the **Board Certifications** field.
23. List any other boards you serve on in the **Other Board Affiliations** field.
24. Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
25. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.

* **Mobile Phone** (7, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Work Phone** (8, text, optional): Enter your work phone number, if applicable.
* **Street Address** (9, text, required): Enter your street address.

* **State** (11, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (12, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Country** (13, select_one, required): Select your country from the dropdown menu.
* **Board Position** (14, select_one, required): Select your current board position from the dropdown menu.
* **Board Status** (15, select_one, required): Select your current board status from the dropdown menu.
* **Term Start Date** (16, date, required): Enter the start date of your term.
* **Term End Date** (17, date, required): Enter the end date of your term.
* **Company Name** (18, text, required): Enter the name of your company or employer.
