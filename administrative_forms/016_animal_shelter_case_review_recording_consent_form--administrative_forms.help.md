# Animal Shelter Case Review Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and obtain consent for recording a case review in the Animal Shelter. The recording will be used to support case documentation and review processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure that you have reviewed the purpose and terms of the form.
2. Complete each field with the required information.
3. Sign the form electronically at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's street address (not required).
* **Case Number or Reference** (`case_number`, text, optional): Enter the case number or reference number (if applicable).
* **Scheduled Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Select the date of the scheduled interview.
* **Type of Case Review** (`interview_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of case review.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the staff member's name conducting the interview.
* **Purpose of Recording** (`recording_purpose`, note, optional): Enter the purpose of the recording (for case documentation and review purposes).
* **Recording Purpose Details** (`purpose_details`, text, optional): Enter additional details about the purpose of the recording.
* **Do You Consent to This Interview Being Recorded** (`consent_to_record`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you consent to the recording.
* **How the Recording Will Be Used** (`recording_usage`, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways the recording will be used (Internal Case Documentation, Staff Training, Quality Assurance Review, Legal Proceedings, Not for Public Use).
* **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention`, select_one, required): Select the data retention period (Until Case Closure, 1 Year, 3 Years, 5 Years, Indefinitely for Legal Protection).
* **Who Will Have Access to the Recording** (`access_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the staff members and teams that will have access to the recording.
* **Do You Want to Review the Recording** (`right_to_review`, select_one, optional): Select to confirm or decline the opportunity to review the recording.
* **Understanding of Revocation Rights** (`right_to_revoke`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you understand the right to revoke consent.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you understand the confidentiality terms.
* **Voluntary Participation Statement** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you understand that participation is voluntary.
* **Alternative Options If You Decline Recording** (`alternative_options`, select_one, optional): Select alternative options if you decline recording (Written Statement Only, In-Person Interview Without Recording, Phone Interview Without Recording, Decline to Participate).
* **Do You Have Any Questions or Concerns** (`questions_or_concerns`, select_one, required): Select to confirm or decline having questions or concerns.
* **Please Describe Your Questions or Concerns** (`concerns_details`, text, optional): Enter details about your questions or concerns.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the witness name (if applicable).
* **Electronic Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required): Select the date of consent.
* **I Have Read and Understand This Consent Form** (`final_confirmation`, select_one, required): Select to confirm that you have read and understand the consent form.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Answer all required fields truthfully.
* Sign the form electronically at the end of the form.
* Review the confidentiality agreement before submitting the form.
* If you have questions or concerns, select the option to express them.
* Ensure that you understand the purpose and terms of the form before submitting it.
