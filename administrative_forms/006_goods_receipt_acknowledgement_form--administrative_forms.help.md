# Goods Receipt Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the receipt of goods or materials by the company or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date the goods were received.
2. Enter the name of the person or department that received the goods.
3. Enter the quantity of goods received.
4. Provide a brief description of the goods.
5. Enter the name of the person or organization that provided the goods.
6. Enter the name of the person or department that received the goods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **goods_received_date**: <i class="fa fa-calendar"></i> Enter the date the goods were received.
* **goods_received_by**: <i class="fa fa-user"></i> Enter the name of the person or department that received the goods.
* **goods_received_quantity**: <i class="fa fa-list"></i> Enter the quantity of goods received.
* **goods_description**: <i class="fa fa-file-text"></i> Provide a brief description of the goods.
* **received_from**: <i class="fa fa-users"></i> Enter the name of the person or organization that provided the goods.
* **received_by**: <i class="fa fa-user"></i> Enter the name of the person or department that received the goods.
* **goods_receipt_number**: <i class="fa fa-file-text"></i> Enter any relevant goods receipt number (if applicable).

Note: The fields marked as required (false) are not mandatory and can be left blank if no information is available or applicable.
