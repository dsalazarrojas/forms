# Severance Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by companies to document the details of an employee's severance agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all relevant fields**: Fill in all the fields as applicable to your situation.
2. **Enter employer information**: Provide the company's name, title, email, and address.
3. **Enter employee information**: Provide the employee's name, email, and address.
4. **Specify terms of severance**: Enter the terms of the severance agreement, including the notice period and payment terms.
5. **Choose payment method**: Select the payment method for the severance package.
6. **Sign and date**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Information (id: 12)**: Enter the name of the company (Employer Name), title, email, and address.
* **Employee Information (id: 16)**: Enter the employee's name, email, and address.
* **Notice Period (id: 4)**: Specify the number of days or weeks the employee needs to provide notice before leaving the company.
* **Effective Date (id: 5)**: Enter the date when the severance agreement will take effect.
* **Severance Package (id: 6)**: Specify the amount of severance package the employee will receive.
* **Payment Terms (id: 7)**: Choose one of the three payment options (50%, 25%, or 100% of the severance package).
* **Payment Method (id: 8)**: Select one or both payment options (Cash or Check).
* **Employee Signature (id: 9)**: Sign to confirm completion of the form.
* **Employer Signature (id: 10)**: Sign to confirm completion of the form.
* **Date (id: 11)**: Enter the date the form is being completed.
* **Payment Terms 2 (id: 19)**: Specify the second payment terms, if applicable.
* **Payment Method 2 (id: 20)**: Select the second payment option, if applicable.
* **Employer Note (id: 21)**: Enter any additional notes from the employer.
* **Employee Note (id: 22)**: Enter any additional notes from the employee.
* **Employer Note 2 (id: 23)**: Enter any additional notes from the employer.
* **Employee Note 2 (id: 24)**: Enter any additional notes from the employee.
* **Date 2 (id: 25)**: Enter the date the second payment is made, if applicable.

Note: This form is a general guide and specific requirements may vary based on the company's policies and procedures. It is recommended to review the company's HR policies and procedures before completing this form.
