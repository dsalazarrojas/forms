# Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm that you have read and will adhere to the Employee Discipline Policy. This is a mandatory acknowledgment that ensures all employees are aware of and committed to upholding our workplace policies and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please review each field carefully and answer according to your understanding of our Employee Discipline Policy.
- Ensure that you complete all required fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Discipline Policy Acknowledgment Form** (`employee_discipline_policy_acknowledgment_form`, note, required): This is the main acknowledgment section where you confirm that you have read and will adhere to the Employee Discipline Policy.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' if you have read and understand the Employee Discipline Policy, or 'No' if you have not or do not understand it. 
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Briefly describe what you have read and understood about the Employee Discipline Policy.
* **Assigned Tool/Resource** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): If there is a specific tool or resource that you have been assigned to help you understand or implement the Employee Discipline Policy, please provide its name.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required): Please provide the name of the output file that contains the acknowledgment of your understanding of the Employee Discipline Policy.

## Tips
- Make sure to save your changes before submitting the form.
- If you've already completed this form previously, please note that you will need to update your response if the policy has changed since your last acknowledgment.
