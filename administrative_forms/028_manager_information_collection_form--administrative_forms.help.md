# Manager Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Manager Information Collection Form is used to gather information about a manager, including their title, contact details, job title, and other relevant information. This form is used by HR or other management teams for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the manager's title in the "Manager Title" field.
2. Fill in the first name, last name, email, and phone number of the manager.
3. Specify the department the manager belongs to.
4. Enter the phone number of the manager.
5. Enter the email address of the manager (this field is optional).
6. Provide information about the manager's job title and job description.
7. Enter the date the manager has been in this position.
8. If necessary, provide additional information about the manager's job title, job phone number, or other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required): Enter the manager's title, e.g., "Director of Marketing" or "Assistant Manager".
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the manager.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the manager.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department the manager belongs to.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the manager (if applicable).
* **Manager Other** (`manager_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the manager (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title of the manager.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the manager's job.
* **Job Phone** (`job_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number associated with the manager's job.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the manager's job title.
* **Manager Since** (`manager_since`, date, required): Enter the date the manager has been in this position.
* **Department Other** (`department_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the manager's department.
* **Job Phone Other** (`job_phone_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the manager's job phone.
