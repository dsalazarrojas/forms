# Company Asset Purchase Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Company Asset Purchase Request Form is a crucial tool for employees to request company assets for work-related purposes. It helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and evaluated to make informed decisions about asset purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your contact information**:
	* Full Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee badge or ID card.
	* Email Address: Enter your company email address.
	* Phone Number: Provide your direct phone number.
2. **Provide Asset Details**:
	* Asset Category: Choose the category of asset you are requesting (e.g., Computer/Laptop, Monitor/Display).
	* Asset Name/Description: Enter the specific name or description of the asset you need.
	* Asset Specifications: Provide technical details about the asset, such as model number or serial number.
3. **Specify Asset and Cost Information**:
	* Quantity Needed: Enter the number of assets you require.
	* Estimated Cost Per Unit: Enter the estimated cost of the asset.
	* Total Estimated Cost: Calculate the total cost by multiplying the quantity by the estimated cost per unit.
4. **Justify the Request**:
	* Business Justification: Explain why you need this asset.
	* Urgency Level: Choose the urgency level of your request (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
	* Required By Date: Enter the date you need the asset by.
5. **Asset Status and Disposal**:
	* Current Equipment: If replacing existing equipment, indicate the condition or reasons for replacement.
	* Disposal Plan: If replacing, select a disposal method (e.g., Return to vendor, IT surplus, Recycle, Donate).
6. **Approval and Review**:
	* Requester Signature: Sign the form as the requester.
	* Manager Approval Status: This field is automatically populated by the manager's approval status.
	* Manager Comments: If the manager provides comments, they will be displayed here.
7. **Attachments and Additional Information**:
	* Attachments: You may be asked to provide quotes or documentation.
	* Additional Comments: Provide any additional information or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee badge or ID card.
* **Email Address**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your direct phone number.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department**: Specify your department.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Request Date**: Enter the current date.
* **Asset Category**: Choose the category of asset you are requesting (e.g., Computer/Laptop, Monitor/Display).
* **Asset Name/Description**: Enter the specific name or description of the asset you need.
* **Asset Specifications**: Provide technical details about the asset, such as model number or serial number.
* **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of assets you require.
* **Estimated Cost Per Unit**: Enter the estimated cost of the asset.
* **Total Estimated Cost**: Calculate the total cost by multiplying the quantity by the estimated cost per unit.
* **Estimated Cost**: Enter the total cost of the asset.
* **Preferred Vendor**: Enter the preferred vendor's name.
* **Vendor Website/Link**: Enter the product URL if available.
* **Business Justification**: Explain why you need this asset.
* **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level of your request (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Required By Date**: Enter the date you need the asset by.
* **Do you have existing equipment to replace?**: If yes, specify the type of equipment (e.g., upgrading current, replacing old).
* **If replacing, describe current equipment**: Provide details about the condition or issues of the current equipment.
* **Impact if not approved**: Explain the impact on your work or business process if the request is not approved.
* **Budget Source**: Specify the department budget source (e.g., Department Budget, Project Budget).
* **Budget Code/Account Number**: If known, enter the budget code or account number.
* **Where will the asset be used?**: Specify the location of the asset (e.g., office, home office).
* **Will this asset be shared with others?**: Choose the sharing scope (e.g., personal use only, team members, department-wide, company-wide).
* **Will training be required?**: Choose the level of training required (e.g., basic, extensive).
* **Are there ongoing maintenance requirements?**: Provide details about ongoing maintenance needs.
* **If replacing, what is the disposal plan for old asset?**: Select a disposal method (e.g., Return to vendor, IT surplus, Recycle, Donate).
* **Any additional comments or information?**: Provide any additional notes or information.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* Use standard business language to explain your request.
* Be sure to check that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your department or supervisor.
