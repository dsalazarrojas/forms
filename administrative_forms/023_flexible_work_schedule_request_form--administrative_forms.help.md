# flexible_work_schedule_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flexible_work_schedule_request_form is used to request changes to your work schedule. Please fill out the form with your details and schedule specifics, and the approval levels required for your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the following fields:
    - **Schedule Request**: A brief description of your schedule request.
    - **Job Title**: Your job title.
    - **Department**: Your department.
    - **Manager Review**: A note from your manager regarding your request.
    - **Employee ID**: Your employee ID.
    - **Date Start**: The date you wish to start your schedule change.
    - **Date End**: The date you wish to end your schedule change.
    - **Reason**: A brief explanation for your schedule request.
    - **Status**: Select the status of your request (e.g., pending, approved, declined).
    - **Approval Level**: Select the level of approval required (e.g., HR, team lead, department lead, director).
    - **Manager Approvals**: Indicate whether your manager has approved or declined your request.
    - **Team Lead Approvals**: Indicate whether your team lead has approved or declined your request.
    - **Department Lead Approvals**: Indicate whether your department lead has approved or declined your request.
    - **Director Approvals**: Indicate whether your director has approved or declined your request.
    - **Employee Email**: Your email address (optional).
    - **Employee Phone**: Your phone number (optional).
    - **Manager Email**: Your manager's email address (optional).
    - **Manager Phone**: Your manager's phone number (optional).
    - **Time Start**: The time you wish to start your schedule change.
    - **Time End**: The time you wish to end your schedule change.
    - **Days Worked**: The number of days you will work during your schedule change.
    - **Time Zone**: The time zone you will be working in.
    - **Notes**: Any additional comments regarding your request.
