# Lighting Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lighting Failure Incident Form is a tool used to collect and document information about lighting failures that have occurred on campus. This form helps the facilities management team to efficiently track and investigate these incidents, and to identify potential solutions to prevent similar issues in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Start Form" button to begin the form.
*   Select the date and time of the lighting failure incident.
*   Enter the location ID and name of where the incident occurred.
*   Provide a brief description of the incident.
*   Select the cause of the failure from the list of options provided.
*   Add any follow-up actions that were taken or planned to address the issue.
*   Click on the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the lighting failure incident. This field is not required but helps us to track the timeline of incidents.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the lighting failure incident. This field is not required but aids in providing a more detailed account of the incident.
*   **Location ID** (`location_id`, number, optional): Enter the location ID where the incident occurred. This field is not required but assists in identifying the specific location affected.
*   **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the location where the incident occurred. This field is optional but helps in providing a more detailed description.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the lighting failure incident. This field is not required but helps in documenting the specifics of the incident.
*   **Cause of Failure** (`cause_of_failure`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following causes of failure:
    -   Light bulb
    -   Switch failure
    -   Circuit failure
*   **Follow-up Actions** (`follow_up_actions`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up actions that were taken or planned to address the issue. This field is optional but helps in tracking progress and future plans.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to provide the facilities management team with as much information as needed to efficiently resolve the issue.
- Ensure that you're filling this form for a specific, unique incident and not duplicating an existing report.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact the facilities management team for assistance.
