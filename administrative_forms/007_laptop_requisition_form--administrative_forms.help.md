# Laptop Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laptop Requisition Form is used to request a laptop for work-related purposes. It captures details such as laptop model, department, priority level, and other relevant information to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct laptop model from the list.
2. Choose the department that the laptop is being requested for.
3. Indicate the priority level for the request (Yes or No).
4. Provide any additional comments about the request.
5. Attach any relevant documents or files.
6. Select the department or position of the user requesting the laptop.
7. Enter the user's ID and email (optional).
8. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the company's laptop policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the laptop being requested.
* **General** (`form_section_general`, `note`, required): A brief description of the request.
* **Requester** (`form_section_requester`, `text`, required): Enter your name and title as the requester.
* **Requested Device** (`form_section_requested_device`, `select_one`, required): Choose the specific laptop model being requested.
* **Department** (`form_section_department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the department that the laptop is being requested for.
* **Request Date** (`form_section_request_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the request.
* **Requesting User** (`form_section_requesting_user`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID as the requesting user.
* **Priority** (`form_section_priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose the priority level for the request.
* **Comments** (`form_section_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about the request.
* **Requested By** (`form_section_requested_by`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the person or department requesting the laptop.
* **Status** (`form_section_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the request.
* **Urgency** (`form_section_urgency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency level for the request.
* **Attachments** (`form_section_attachments`, `note`, required): Attach any relevant documents or files.
* **Requesting User Department** (`form_section_requesting_user_department`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the department of the user requesting the laptop.
* **Requesting User Position** (`form_section_requesting_user_position`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the position of the user requesting the laptop.
* **Review Status** (`form_section_review_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the review.
* **Reviewed By** (`form_section_reviewed_by`, `select_one`, required): Select the person or department reviewing the request.
* **Review Date** (`form_section_review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Comments** (`form_section_review_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Requesting User ID** (`form_section_requesting_user_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID as the requesting user.
* **Requesting User Email** (`form_section_requesting_user_email`, `text`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Review Comments** is actually **Additional Comments** (`form_section_additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about the request.

## Tips

* Please double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Attach relevant documents or files when required.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the company's laptop policy.
* Contact your department manager or IT department if you have any doubts or concerns about the form or its purpose.
