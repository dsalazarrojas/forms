The purpose of the Cargo Loss Incident Form is to document and report incidents of cargo loss or damage. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured and reported consistently, aiding in the swift resolution and prevention of future incidents.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields have been filled out.
2. Complete the **Incident Report Number** field with a unique identifier for this incident.
3. Provide the **Date of Incident** and **Time of Incident**, describing when the incident occurred.
4. Enter the **Location of Incident**, where the cargo loss or damage happened.
5. Identify the **Reported By**, who reported the incident.
6. Fill out the **Reporter's Email** and **Reporter's Phone Number**.
7. Describe the **Cargo Information**, including its details and any relevant facts.
8. Enter the **Shipment Reference Number**.
9. Describe the **Description of Cargo**, providing details about the goods.
10. Enter the **Declared Value of Cargo**, the total value in currency.
11. Select the **Type of Incident** from the provided options.
12. Enter the **Quantity Affected**, the number of items or packages impacted.
13. Provide a detailed **Detailed Description of Incident**, explaining what happened.
14. Identify the **Suspected Cause of Loss**, based on your investigation.
15. Indicate if there were any **Witnesses**, and if so, list their names and contact information.
16. Attach any **Evidence and Documentation**, such as photos or other relevant materials.
17. Confirm if **Photographic evidence** is available.
18. State whether a **Police Report was Filed**, and if so, provide the report number.
19. List the **Immediate Actions Taken**, following the discovery of the incident.
20. Provide any **Claims Information** related to insurance claims.
21. Indicate your intention to file an **Insurance Claim**.
22. Enter the **Estimated Loss Amount**, the total financial impact.
23. Propose **Recommended Corrective Actions**, to prevent similar incidents in the future.
24. Identify the **Supervisor's Name** who will review this incident.
25. Enter the **Review Date**, when the supervisor will review this form.

**Cargo Loss Incident Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and report incidents of cargo loss or damage, ensuring consistent and thorough reporting.
## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps outlined above to ensure you complete all required fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report Number**: A unique identifier for this incident.
* **Date of Incident**: When the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Approximate time of occurrence.
* **Location of Incident**: Where the cargo loss or damage happened.
* **Reported By**: Person reporting the incident.
* **Reporter Email**: Contact email address.
* **Reporter Phone**: Contact phone number.
* **Cargo Information**: Details about the affected cargo.
* **Shipment Reference Number**: Tracking or reference number.
* **Description of Cargo**: Detailed description of goods.
* **Declared Value of Cargo**: Total value in currency.
* **Type of Incident**: Select the incident category.
* **Quantity Affected**: Number of items or packages impacted.
* **Detailed Description of Incident**: Explain what happened in detail.
* **Suspected Cause of Loss**: Based on investigation, select the cause.
* **Witnesses**: Indicate if there were any witnesses.
* **Witness Names and Contact Information**: List witness names and contact information if applicable.
* **Evidence and Documentation**: Attach supportive materials like photos or other relevant evidence.
* **Photographic Evidence**: Confirm if photographic evidence is available.
* **Police Report**: Confirm if a police report was filed, and if so, provide the report number.
* **Immediate Actions Taken**: List steps taken following the incident discovery.
* **Claims Information**: Provide insurance claims details.
* **Will an Insurance Claim be Filed**: Indicate intention to file a claim.
* **Estimated Loss Amount**: Total financial impact.
* **Recommended Corrective Actions**: Prevent future incidents.
* **Supervisor's Name**: Reviewing manager's name.
* **Review Date**: Date of supervisor review.

**Tips**: Review this form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out and the incident is reported accurately. Attach any supporting evidence and documentation to aid in the investigation and resolution of the incident.
