# new_location_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a new location, including its name, address, contact details, and business or residential type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the new location's name in the **Enter New Location Name** field.
2. Provide the address of the new location in the **Enter Address** field.
3. Enter the city of the new location in the **Enter City** field.
4. Enter the postal code of the new location in the **Enter Postal Code** field.
5. Select the country of the new location in the **Enter Country** field.
6. If available, enter the contact phone number of the new location in the **Enter Contact Phone** field (if this field is not required for your submission).
7. If available, enter the email address of the new location in the **Enter Contact E-mail** field (if this field is not required for your submission).
8. Select the type of the new location as **Business**, **Residential**, or **Other** in the **Business Type** field.
9. (Optional) Enter the reason for expansion and relocation in the **Expansion Reason** field.
10. (Optional) Enter the reason for relocation in the **Relocation Reason** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter New Location Name** (`new_location_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the new location.
* **Enter Address** (`location_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the new location.
* **Enter City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of the new location.
* **Enter Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code of the new location.
* **Enter Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Select the country of the new location.
* **Enter Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the new location (if available).
* **Enter Email** (`email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the new location (if available).
* **Enter Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of the new location.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of the new location as **Business**, **Residential**, or **Other**.
* **Expansion Reason** (`expansion_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for expansion (if available).
* **Relocation Reason** (`relocation_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for relocation (if available).
