# internship_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request internships for leave. This form is used to process leave requests from employees or interns.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Select the 'Submit' option to send in your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request (1)**: This is the main section of the form where you will enter your leave request. Please provide a clear and concise description of your leave request.
* **Start Date (2)**: Enter the date you would like your leave to start from.
* **End Date (3)**: Enter the date you would like your leave to end on.
* **Reason (4)**: Enter the reason for your leave. This can be a personal or professional reason.
* **Leave Type (5)**: Select the type of leave you are requesting. This will help us understand the nature of your leave.
* **End Reason (6)**: This field is optional and not required. If you have any other reason for your leave that is not already stated, you can enter it here.
* **Comments (7)**: This field is also optional and not required. If you have any additional comments or information to provide, you can enter them here.
* **Submit (8)**: Select this option to send in your leave request.
