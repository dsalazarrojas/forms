# IT System Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT System Access Request Form is used to request access to IT systems. This form helps to track and process requests for access to these systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your Employee ID
2. Enter your Employee Name
3. Enter your Job Title
4. Enter your Manager ID
5. Enter your Manager Name
6. Select the IT System you wish to access
7. Select the reason for your request
8. Enter the Date of Birth (if required)
9. Enter the Start Date (if required)
10. Enter any additional Notes or Comments
11. Enter your Email
12. Enter your Phone Number
13. Enter the name of the person assigned to process your request
14. Enter the tool assigned to process your request
15. Confirm if your request has been submitted
16. Enter the date your request was submitted
17. Select the status of your request (Active or Pending)
18. Select the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, or High)
19. Enter the Due Date (if required)
20. Enter the date your request was created
21. Enter the date your request was updated
22. Enter any additional Notes or Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel file.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the ID number of your manager.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your manager.
* **IT System** (`it_system_id`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the IT system you wish to access from the list provided.
* **Reason for request** (`reason_for_request`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the reason for your request from the provided options.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth if required.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start date and time of the requested access period if required.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person assigned to process your request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tool assigned to process your request.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if your request has been submitted.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date your request was submitted.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your request (Active or Pending).
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the priority level of your request (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the due date for your request if required.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date your request was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date your request was updated.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **None** (`error`, `text`, required: false): This field should be left blank.
