# travel_expense_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The travel expense claim form is used to document and manage employee travel expenses, capturing essential details about business trips to facilitate the expense claim process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the relevant information for each section as prompted.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Double-check the entered information for accuracy to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Expense Claim** (`travel_expense_claim_form`, text, required/optional): This section serves as the form's title and identifier.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_expense_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of travel.
* **Travel Start Date** (`travel_expense_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of travel.
* **Travel End Date** (`travel_expense_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of travel.
* **Travel Start Time** (`travel_expense_start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of travel.
* **Travel End Time** (`travel_expense_end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of travel.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_expense_destination`, text, required/optional): Enter the destination of travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_expense_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for travel.
* **Travel Expense Claimed By** (`travel_expense_claimed_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person claiming the expense.
* **Travel Expense Approved By** (`travel_expense_approved_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who approved the expense.
* **Travel Expense Approved Status** (`travel_expense_approved_status`, select, required/optional): Select the status of approval from Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Travel Expense Category** (`travel_expense_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant categories of expense from Airfare, Car Rental, Accommodation, Food, or Other.
* **Travel Expense Currency** (`travel_expense_currency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency used for expense from USD, EUR, or JPY.
* **Travel Expense Currency Rate** (`travel_expense_currency_rate`, number, required/optional): Enter the currency rate if applicable.
* **Travel Expense Amount** (`travel_expense_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the expense amount.
* **Travel Expense Reimbursed By** (`travel_expense_reimbursed_by`, select_one, required/optional): Select who reimbursed the expense from Employee, Company, or Other.
* **Travel Expense Reimbursment Method** (`travel_expense_reimbursment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select the reimbursement method from Cash, Bank Transfer, or Other.
* **Travel Expense Additional Notes** (`travel_expense_additional_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the expense.
* **Travel Expense Attachment** (`travel_expense_attachment`, text, required/optional): Enter any attachments related to the expense.
* **Travel Expense Submitter** (`travel_expense_submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who submitted the expense.
* **Travel Expense Approved By Date** (`travel_expense_approved_by_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Travel Expense Approved By Time** (`travel_expense_approved_by_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of approval.
* **Travel Expense Reimbursed By Date** (`travel_expense_reimbursed_by_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of reimbursement.
* **Travel Expense Reimbursed By Time** (`travel_expense_reimbursed_by_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of reimbursement.
* **Travel Expense Submitted By** (`travel_expense_submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the person who submitted the expense.
