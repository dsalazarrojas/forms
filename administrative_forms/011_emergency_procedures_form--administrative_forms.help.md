# Emergency Procedures Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Procedures Form is designed to provide a structured template for reporting and documenting emergency procedures that need to be followed in case of unexpected events or situations. The form helps ensure that procedures are properly recorded, and necessary actions are taken in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Answer the questions truthfully, providing relevant details for each field.
4. Verify your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_procedures_form_1** (`emergency_procedures_form_1`, text, Optional): This field is for free-form comments or notes about the emergency procedures. You can add any relevant information or details that are not included in other fields.
* **emergency_contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, select_multiple, **Required**): Select all applicable emergency contacts who need to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contacts_phone** (`emergency_contacts_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of each emergency contact selected in the previous field.
* **emergency_contacts_email** (`emergency_contacts_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of each emergency contact selected in the previous field. If you don't have an email address for a contact, leave this field blank.
* **emergency_procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, text, **Required**): Describe the emergency procedures to be followed in case of the unexpected event or situation.
* **emergency_procedures_details** (`emergency_procedures_details`, text, Optional): Add any additional details about the emergency procedures, such as locations, resources, or other relevant information.
* **emergency_procedures_form_submitted_by** (`emergency_procedures_form_submitted_by`, select_one, **Required**): Select the team or person responsible for submitting this form (Emergency Response Team, Security Team, or Management).
* **emergency_procedures_form_submitted** (`emergency_procedures_form_submitted`, date, **Required**): Date the form was submitted.
* **emergency_procedures_form_completed** (`emergency_procedures_form_completed`, time, **Required**): Time the form was completed.
* **emergency_procedures_form_last_updated** (`emergency_procedures_form_last_updated`, date, Optional): Date the form was last updated. This field is usually automatically populated when the form is modified.
* **emergency_procedures_form_created_by** (`emergency_procedures_form_created_by`, select_one, Optional): Select the team or person who created the form (John or Jane). This field is usually automatically populated and does not require input.
* **emergency_procedures_form_modified_by** (`emergency_procedures_form_modified_by`, select_one, Optional): Select the team or person who last modified the form (John or Jane). This field is usually automatically populated and does not require input.
* **emergency_procedures_form_created** (`emergency_procedures_form_created`, date, Optional): Date the form was created. This field is usually automatically populated and does not require input.
* **emergency_procedures_form_modified** (`emergency_procedures_form_modified`, date, Optional): Date the form was last modified. This field is usually automatically populated and does not require input.
