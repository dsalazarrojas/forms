# Sidewalk Condition Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sidewalk Condition Inspection Form is used to record the condition of a sidewalk. It helps to gather information about the sidewalk's condition, including its address, condition description, priority rating, photos, inspection date, and time. This form is typically used by municipal or government agencies to monitor and maintain the city's sidewalks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the address of the sidewalk being inspected.
2.  Describe the condition of the sidewalk, including any notable issues or defects.
3.  Rate the condition of the sidewalk on a scale (the form will guide you on how to select the correct rating).
4.  Indicate if photos have been uploaded to document the condition.
5.  Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6.  Enter your name and badge number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the address of the inspection? (General Info, text)**: This field is where you enter the address of the sidewalk being inspected. Please enter the exact address, including the street number and any additional details.
*   **What is the condition of the sidewalk? (Condition Description, text)**: In this field, please describe the condition of the sidewalk, including any issues or defects you observed during the inspection.
*   **What is the condition rating of the sidewalk? (Priority Rating, number)**: This field asks for a rating of the sidewalk's condition, from 1 (very poor) to 10 (excellent). Please choose the rating that best matches the condition of the sidewalk.
*   **Have photos been uploaded to document the condition? (Photos Uploaded, select_one)**: If you have photos of the sidewalk's condition, select 'Yes'. If not, select 'No'.
*   **What is the date of the inspection? (Date Inspected, date)**: Enter the date the inspection was performed.
*   **What is the time of the inspection? (Time Inspected, time)**: Enter the time the inspection was performed.
*   **What is the inspector's name and badge number? (Inspector Info, text)**: If you are the inspector, please enter your name and badge number (if applicable).
