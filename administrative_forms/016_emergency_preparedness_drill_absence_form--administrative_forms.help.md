# Emergency Preparedness Drill Absence Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to track employee absences during an emergency preparedness drill. The form is intended for employees to report their absence during the drill, providing essential information for management and HR to process and account for attendance.

Please note that this form is not designed to be used for regular attendance tracking or other purposes outside of emergency preparedness drills. 

To ensure the form is filled out correctly, please follow the instructions below:

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form has multiple sections, each representing an employee's absence during a specific drill.
2. Fill out each section for each absence, providing the required information.
3. Use the "Contact Name", "Contact Number", and "Contact Email" fields to report any emergency contact information in case further assistance is needed.
4. For "Attendance Status", select "Absent" if the employee was not present during the drill, "Present" if they were present, or "Late" if they arrived late.
5. Enter a brief explanation for the "Attendance Status Note" if necessary.
6. Fill out any additional sections, up to 17, as applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drill Absence 1** (`drill_absence_1`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the date of the drill absence.
* **Drill Absence 2** (`drill_absence_2`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the date of the drill absence.
* **Drill Absence 3** (`drill_absence_3`, `time`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the time of the drill absence.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the contact name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the contact email.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the participant name.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the participant department.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the attendance status, which can be "Absent", "Present", or "Late".
* **Attendance Status Note** (`attendance_status_note`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing a brief explanation for the attendance status.
* **Drill Absence 4** (`drill_absence_4`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for reporting the date of the drill absence.

## Tips

* Fill out each field carefully and accurately, as this information will be used for official purposes.
* If you are unsure of the correct information, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
* This form is intended for emergency preparedness drills only; do not use it for other purposes.
