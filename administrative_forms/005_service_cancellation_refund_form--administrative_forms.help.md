# service_cancellation_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for processing service cancellations and refunds. It captures key information from the customer, including reason for cancellation, refund amount, and refund method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct reason for cancellation from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter a specific reason for cancellation if not listed in the dropdown.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Enter the customer's phone number.
5. Enter the amount of refund.
6. Select the refund method.
7. Optionally, add any customer note for the staff to reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Why are you cancelling** (`reason_for_cancellation`): Select one of the available options or enter a specific reason for cancellation.
* **Reason for cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation_reason`): Enter a specific reason for cancellation if not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Service start date** (`service_start_date`): Enter the start date of the service in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Amount refunded** (`amount_refunded`): Enter the amount to be refunded.
* **Refund method** (`refund_method`): Select one of the available options for refund processing.
* **Customer's Email** (`customer_email`): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer's Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer's note** (`customer_note`): Add any additional note for the staff to reference.
