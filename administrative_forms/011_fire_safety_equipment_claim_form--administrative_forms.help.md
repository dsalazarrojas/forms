# Fire Safety Equipment Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Equipment Claim Form is designed to gather information about incidents involving fire safety equipment that has been damaged or malfunctions. This form is used to report and process such incidents, ensuring that our team can provide accurate assessments and take necessary steps to prevent future occurrences.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form's sections and ensure you are in the correct category (Fire Safety Equipment Claim).
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, following the instructions below.
3. Double-check your responses for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Safety Equipment (FireExtinguisher, FireAlarm)** (id 2):
	+ Select the type of fire safety equipment involved in the incident.
* **Damage Description (damage_description)** (id 3):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the damage or malfunction experienced with the fire safety equipment.
* **Amount Claimed (amount_claimed)** (id 4):
	+ Enter the total amount you are claiming for the damaged or malfunctioning equipment.
* **Claim Status (claim_status)** (id 5):
	+ Choose the current status of your claim: Pending, Closed, or Reopened.
* **Claim Date (claim_date)** (id 6):
	+ Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Contact Number (contact_number)** (id 7):
	+ Enter your contact number so our team can easily get in touch with you.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)** (id 8):
	+ Provide any additional contact details or instructions.
* **Claimant Signature (claimant_signature)** (id 9):
	+ Sign this form to indicate that you have completed and reviewed it accurately.
* **Reviewer Signature (reviewer_signature)** (id 10):
	+ If your claim is being reviewed, sign this field to confirm you have reviewed and approved the form.
* **Date Submitted (date_submitted)** (id 11):
	+ Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Date Reviewed (date_reviewed)** (id 12):
	+ If your claim has been reviewed, enter the date it was reviewed.
* **Comments (fire_safety_equipment_claim_form_comments)** (id 13):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your claim.
* **Attachments (fire_safety_equipment_claim_form_attachments)** (id 14):
	+ Upload any supporting documents related to your claim, such as photos or repair bills.
Note: This section is used to attach supporting documentation that may be necessary for processing your claim. Please ensure that any attachments are relevant and accurate.
