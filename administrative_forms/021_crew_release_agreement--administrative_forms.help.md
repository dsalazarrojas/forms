# Crew Release Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crew Release Agreement is a document that outlines the terms and conditions for a crew's participation in a production or project. It is used to ensure that all crew members are aware of their responsibilities, expectations, and rights during and after the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand all the terms and conditions outlined.
2.  Fill in your production company's information in the "production_company" field.
3.  Enter your name in the "crew_member" field.
4.  Provide your agreement terms in the "release_terms" field.
5.  Enter the date you are signing this agreement in the "agreement_date" field.
6.  Provide the start date of the project in the "start_date" field.
7.  Enter the end date of the project in the "end_date" field.
8.  Sign and date your part of the agreement in the "crew_signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **production_company**: This is the name and contact information of the production company responsible for the project.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: This is the name of the production company you will be working with.
*   **crew_member**: Your name is required.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `true`
    *   Hint: This is your name that will be associated with this agreement.
*   **release_terms**: This is where you can specify any special conditions or expectations for your release.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: This is where you can outline any additional terms or conditions.
*   **agreement_date**: This is the date you sign this agreement.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: This is the date you are committing to the terms and conditions of this agreement.
*   **start_date**: This is the project start date.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: This is the date the project officially begins.
*   **end_date**: This is the project end date.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: This is the date the project officially ends.
*   **crew_signature**: This is where you sign and date your part of the agreement.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
    *   Hint: Please sign and date your part of this agreement in the space provided.

## Tips
*   Always review the form carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any term or condition.
*   Make sure you understand all the terms and conditions before signing this agreement.
*   Keep a copy of this agreement for your records.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your production company representative or project manager.
