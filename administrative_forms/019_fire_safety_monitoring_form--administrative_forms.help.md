# Fire Safety Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to monitor and record fire safety measures in your building or facility. It helps ensure that all necessary precautions are taken to prevent and respond to fires, and that regulatory requirements are met.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and its purpose.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and accurately based on your current knowledge and practices.
3.  Make sure to save your progress and submit the form when it's complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Safety Monitoring Form** (Note, required): This is the title of the form, it does not need to be filled in.
* **Inspections** (Select One, required: false): Check if regular inspections are being performed to identify potential fire hazards. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Equipment** (Select Multiple, required: false): Check if all necessary fire safety equipment is in working order. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Fire Safety Policy** (Text, required: false): Describe the fire safety policy in place in your building or facility.
* **Fire Risk Management** (Time, required: false): Record the last time a fire risk assessment was performed.
* **Fire Drill** (Date, required: false): Record the last fire drill conducted in your building or facility.
* **Fire Safety Training** (Select One, required: false): Check if regular fire safety training is provided to employees. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Fire Safety Policy Revision** (Time, required: false): Record the last time the fire safety policy was revised.
* **Fire Equipment Maintenance** (Select Multiple, required: false): Check if all necessary fire safety equipment is properly maintained. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Fire Safety Monitoring Frequency** (Select One, required: false): Check if fire safety monitoring is performed on a regular basis. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Fire Safety Training Frequency** (Select One, required: false): Check if fire safety training is provided on a regular basis. If yes, select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No'.
* **Fire Safety Policy Review** (Date, required: false): Record the last time the fire safety policy was reviewed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review and update this form regularly to ensure accuracy and up-to-date information.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please consult with a fire safety expert or supervisor before submitting the form.
*   Save your progress and submit the form when it's complete to ensure that your information is recorded and reviewed.
