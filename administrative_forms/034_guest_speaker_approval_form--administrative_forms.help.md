# guest_speaker_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from guest speakers for a workshop or event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Guest Speaker Information section with your name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Enter a brief Bio about yourself.
5. If applicable, upload a Speaker Image.
6. Review and accept the terms in the Accept Terms field.
7. Enter the Workshop Name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Speaker Information** (`guest_speaker_info`, text, optional): This section is meant for you to enter any necessary details about yourself as a guest speaker.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, email, optional): Enter your email address that we can contact you at.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): This is a text area for you to enter a short description about yourself.
* **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image`, text, optional): This field is not actively specified, but it is assumed to be an optional upload or link to a speaker image.
* **Accept Terms** (`accept_terms`, text, optional): Review and accept the terms by entering 'Yes' or a similar affirmation.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop you are associated with, if applicable.

## Tips
- If you are having trouble uploading a speaker image, ensure it is in a supported format and not exceeding the size limits.
