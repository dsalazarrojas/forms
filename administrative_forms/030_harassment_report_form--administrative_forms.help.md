# Harassment Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for reporting incidents of harassment, providing a structured and confidential way for witnesses or victims to share details about the incident.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
*   Start by filling out the **Report Details** field with a brief description of the incident.
*   Enter the **Date of Occurrence** when the incident happened.
*   Enter the **Time of Occurrence** for the same time.
*   In the **Description of Incident** field, provide a detailed account of what happened.
*   In **Perpetrator Info**, enter any identifying information about the individual or group involved.
*   In **Witness Info**, provide any details about the witness that might be necessary for investigation.
*   In **Reporting Officer**, select the officer in charge of this case from the options provided.
*   In **Reporting Officer Email**, enter the email of the reporting officer for communication.
*   In **Reporting Officer Phone**, enter the phone number of the reporting officer.
*   In **Witness Details**, enter any details about the witness that might be helpful.
*   In **Witness Statement**, provide a statement from the witness, including what they saw or experienced.
*   In **Supporting Documents**, attach any documents that support your claim or provide evidence.
*   Finally, in **Submit**, select whether you want to submit the form or save it for later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Report Details (1)**: A brief description of the incident.
*   **Date of Occurrence (2)**: The date the incident happened.
*   **Time of Occurrence (3)**: The time of the incident.
*   **Description of Incident (4)**: A detailed account of what happened.
*   **Perpetrator Info (5)**: Any identifying information about the individual or group involved.
*   **Witness Info (6)**: Any details about the witness that might be necessary for investigation.
*   **Reporting Officer (7)**: The officer in charge of this case.
*   **Reporting Officer Email (8)**: The email of the reporting officer for communication.
*   **Reporting Officer Phone (9)**: The phone number of the reporting officer.
*   **Witness Details (10)**: Any details about the witness that might be helpful.
*   **Witness Statement (11)**: A statement from the witness, including what they saw or experienced.
*   **Supporting Documents (12)**: Attach any documents that support your claim or provide evidence.
*   **Submit (13)**: Select whether to submit or save the form for later.
