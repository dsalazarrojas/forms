# Pump Prestart Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Pump Prestart Checklist is used to gather information about the status of a pump, including its condition, maintenance, and completion of prestart checks. This form helps ensure that all necessary steps are taken before the pump is started, and that any issues or concerns are documented and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the prestart checklist items to indicate whether they have been completed.
2. Enter the pump number and pump service information in the respective fields.
3. Choose the pump type from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the start date and time of the pump prestart.
5. Enter any comments or notes regarding the pump's condition or maintenance.
6. Select the assigned engineer and enter the assigned date and time.
7. Enter the completed by engineer and completed date and time.
8. Sign the form with your name and date.
9. Enter any pump remarks or additional comments.
10. Select the pump status (Active or Inactive).
10. Enter any maintenance information and notes.
11. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pump Prestart Checklist** (`prestart_checklist`, select multiple): Choose "Yes" for each prestart check that has been completed.
* **Pump Number** (`pump_number`, text): Enter the unique identifier for the pump.
* **Pump Service** (`pump_service`, text): Enter the pump service information (e.g. "Main Service", "Backup Service", etc.).
* **Pump Type** (`pump_type`, select multiple): Choose the type of pump (e.g. "Centrifugal", "Positive Displacement", etc.).
* **Start Date/Time** (`start_date_time`, date/time): Enter the date and time the pump prestart is scheduled to start.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time): Enter the start time of the pump prestart.
* **Prestart Checklist Items** (`prestart_checklist_items`, select multiple): Choose "Yes" for each prestart check that has been completed.
* **Pump Condition** (`pump_condition`, note): Enter any notes or comments regarding the pump's condition.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the pump's condition or maintenance.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text): Enter the name of the engineer assigned to complete the pump prestart.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time): Enter the date and time the engineer was assigned to complete the pump prestart.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text): Enter the name of the engineer who completed the pump prestart.
* **Completed Time** (`completed_time`, time): Enter the date and time the pump prestart was completed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date): Sign the form with your name and date.
* **Pump Remarks** (`pump_remarks`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the pump.
* **Pump Status** (`pump_status`, select one): Choose the pump status (Active or Inactive).
* **Pump Maintenance** (`pump_maintenance`, note): Enter any maintenance information or notes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Checklist Completion Date** (`checklist_completion_date`, date): Enter the date the pump prestart was completed.
* **Checklist Completion Time** (`checklist_completion_time`, time): Enter the time the pump prestart was completed.
* **Checklist Completion Status** (`checklist_completion_status`, text): Enter the completion status of the pump prestart.
* **Checklist Completion Comments** (`checklist_completion_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the pump prestart completion.
* **Checklist Revision Date** (`checklist_revision_date`, date): Enter the date the pump prestart checklist was revised.
* **Checklist Revision Comments** (`checklist_revision_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the pump prestart checklist revision.
