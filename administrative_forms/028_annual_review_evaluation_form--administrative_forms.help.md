# Annual Review Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the performance of employees in their current positions. It's meant to provide an objective evaluation of their skills, qualities, and goals, helping to identify areas of improvement and strengths. The form serves as a tool for evaluators to make informed judgments about an employee's overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the Evaluator's name and title.
2.  Enter the Employee's name and ID (if available).
3.  Fill out the sections below according to your assessment of the employee's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Evaluator Name** (`1`, `text`, **Required**): This field is meant to capture the full name of the person conducting the review.
    Enter your full name as the evaluator.
*   **Evaluator Title** (`2`, `text`, **Optional**): This field should include your position or title as the evaluator.
    Enter your job title or position.
*   **Employee Name** (`3`, `text`, **Required**): This field is for the employee's full name.
    Enter the employee's full name.
*   **Employee ID** (`4`, `text`, **Optional**): This field is for the employee's ID number (if applicable).
    Enter the employee's ID number (if available).
*   **Position Title** (`5`, `text`, **Required**): This field is for the job title of the employee.
    Enter the employee's job title.
*   **Department** (`6`, `text`, **Required**): This field is for the department where the employee is working.
    Enter the department the employee belongs to.
*   **Review Period** (`7`, `text`, **Required**): This field should indicate the date range for this evaluation.
    Enter the evaluation period.
*   **Employment Start Date** (`8`, `date`, **Optional**): This field is for the date when the employee started.
    Enter the employee's start date (if known).
*   **Job Knowledge and Technical Skills** (`9`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's proficiency in required skills.
    Choose the level of proficiency from the provided options:
    *   Excellent - exceeds requirements
    *   Good - meets requirements
    *   Satisfactory - basic competency
    *   Needs improvement - deficient
    *   Poor - unable to perform
*   **Quality of Work** (`10`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the accuracy and completeness of the employee's work.
    Choose the level of quality from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Quantity of Work** (`11`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's productivity and output.
    Choose the level of productivity from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Timeliness and Punctuality** (`12`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the employee's ability to meet deadlines and schedules.
    Choose the level of timeliness from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Communication Skills** (`13`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's written and verbal communication skills.
    Choose the level of proficiency from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`14`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the employee's ability to work effectively with others.
    Choose the level of teamwork from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Reliability and Dependability** (`15`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's follow-through and commitment to responsibilities.
    Choose the level of reliability from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Problem-Solving Ability** (`16`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the employee's ability to identify and resolve issues.
    Choose the level of problem-solving from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Adaptability and Flexibility** (`17`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's ability to respond to change and new challenges.
    Choose the level of adaptability from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Initiative and Self-Motivation** (`18`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the employee's ability to take on responsibilities proactively.
    Choose the level of initiative from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Professionalism and Conduct** (`19`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's maintenance of professional standards.
    Choose the level of professionalism from the provided options:
    *   Excellent
    *   Good
    *   Satisfactory
    *   Needs improvement
    *   Poor
*   **Achievement of Performance Goals** (`20`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field assesses the employee's accomplishment of annual objectives.
    Choose the level of goal achievement from the provided options:
    *   Exceeded goals
    *   Achieved all goals
    *   Achieved most goals
    *   Achieved some goals
    *   Did not achieve goals
*   **Employee Strengths** (`21`, `text`, **Required**): This field is for positive attributes and abilities the employee possesses.
    Enter the employee's strengths and abilities.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`22`, `text`, **Required**): This field is for skills or behaviors the employee should develop.
    Enter areas for the employee's improvement.
*   **Overall Performance Rating** (`23`, `select_one`, **Required**): This field rates the employee's overall performance.
    Choose the level of performance from the provided options:
    *   Exceeds expectations
    *   Meets expectations
    *   Meets most expectations
    *   Below expectations
    *   Unsatisfactory
*   **Development Plan for Next Year** (`24`, `text`, **Required**): This field outlines the goals and training recommendations for the employee.
    Enter goals and recommendations for the employee's development.
