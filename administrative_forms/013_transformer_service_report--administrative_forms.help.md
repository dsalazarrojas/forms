# Transformer Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transformer Service Report form is designed to collect information about transformer services, likely for tracking and recording purposes. This form will help users provide detailed reports about their interactions with transformer services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by selecting the correct page (if multiple pages exist).
2. Enter the date of the event or service interaction in the `date` field.
3. Provide the `transformer_id` number, which may be a unique identifier for the transformer service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transformer Service Report** (`transformer_service_report`): Enter a brief description or summary of the transformer service interaction.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the event or service interaction in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Transformer ID** (`transformer_id`): Enter the unique identifier for the transformer service.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate reporting.
* Double-check the date format to ensure it's in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx)
