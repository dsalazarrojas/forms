# Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Infrastructure Maintenance Tracking Form is a tool used to schedule and track maintenance activities on physical assets. It helps you keep track of maintenance progress, assign tasks to specific personnel, and send notifications to relevant stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill in the required fields**: Ensure you complete the required fields, including `maintenance_date`, `asset_id`, `assigned_to`, `priority`, and `due_date`.
2.  **Select relevant options**: Fill in the select fields with the correct options, such as selecting the maintenance status (in progress, done, or pending), priority levels (high, medium, or low), and notification preferences.
3.  **Provide a brief description**: Briefly describe the maintenance task in the `description` field to help track the purpose and scope of the work.
4.  **Add comments (optional)**: Use the `comments` field to add additional information or clarification regarding the maintenance task.
5.  **Set notification preferences**: Choose whether to send email notifications (`True` or `False`) and select recipients for these notifications.
6.  **Submit the form**: Once you've filled in all the necessary fields, click the submit button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1.  Maintenance Date (`maintenance_date`)**: Enter the date the maintenance activity is scheduled or completed.
*   **2.  Asset ID (`asset_id`)**: Enter the unique identifier for the asset being maintained.
*   **3.  Description (`description`)**: Briefly describe the purpose and scope of the maintenance task.
*   **4.  Assigned To (`assigned_to`)**: Select the individual or team responsible for the maintenance task.
*   **5.  Priority (`priority`)**: Select the priority level for the maintenance task (high, medium, or low).
*   **6.  Maintenance Status (`maintenance_status`)**: Select the current state of the maintenance task (in progress, done, or pending).
*   **7.  Comments (`comments`)**: Add any additional information or clarification regarding the maintenance task.
*   **8.  Email Notifications (`email_notifications`)**: Choose whether to send email notifications for the maintenance task.
*   **9.  Email Recipients (`email_recipients`)**: Select who should receive email notifications for the maintenance task.
*   **10. Due Date (`due_date`)**: Enter the date the maintenance task is due or expected to be completed.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the accuracy of your input before submission.
*   If unsure about a particular field or its purpose, refer to this guide or consult with a supervisor or colleague.
*   Regularly check the form for updates and changes to the maintenance status.
