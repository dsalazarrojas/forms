# Chemical Spill Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document chemical spill incidents to improve incident response and prevention measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date and time of the incident.
2. Provide the location or area where the spill occurred.
3. Specify the building and room number where the spill occurred.
4. Enter the person reporting the incident and their department.
5. Provide the email address and phone number of the reporter.
6. Identify the chemical involved in the spill, including its name and CAS number (if applicable).
7. Select the form of the chemical (liquid, solid, powder, vapor, or gas).
8. Estimate the quantity of chemical spilled.
9. Choose the unit of measurement for the estimated quantity.
10. Select the cause of the spill (container drop, container leak, improper handling, equipment failure, overfilling, or other).
11. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the spill.
12. Indicate if any injuries were reported.
13. Describe any injuries that occurred.
14. Evaluate the environmental impact of the spill (none, minor, moderate, or severe).
15. Select all actions taken to contain the spill.
16. Indicate if the hazmat team was called.
17. Indicate if the cleanup was completed.
18. Identify any witnesses to the incident.
19. Suggest recommended prevention measures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Chemical Spill Incident Report --** (incident_intro): This is the introduction section of the form. Please provide a brief description of the spill incident.
* **Date of incident** (incident_date): Enter the date of the spill incident.
* **Time of incident** (incident_time): Enter the time of the spill incident.
* **Location or area of spill** (location_area): Enter the location or area where the spill occurred.
* **Building and room number** (building_room): Enter the building and room number where the spill occurred.
* **Person reporting the incident** (reporter_name): Enter the name of the person reporting the incident.
* **Department** (reporter_department): Enter the department of the person reporting the incident.
* **Email address** (reporter_email): Enter the email address of the person reporting the incident.
* **Phone number** (reporter_phone): Enter the phone number of the person reporting the incident.
* **Chemical name involved** (chemical_name): Enter the name of the chemical involved in the spill.
* **CAS number** (cas_number): Enter the CAS number of the chemical involved in the spill (if applicable).
* **Form of chemical** (chemical_form): Select the form of the chemical (liquid, solid, powder, vapor, or gas).
* **Estimated quantity spilled** (estimated_quantity): Estimate the quantity of chemical spilled.
* **Unit of measurement** (quantity_unit): Select the unit of measurement for the estimated quantity.
* **Cause of spill** (spill_cause): Select the cause of the spill (container drop, container leak, improper handling, equipment failure, overfilling, or other).
* **Detailed description** (spill_cause_details): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the spill.
* **Any injuries reported** (injuries_reported): Indicate if any injuries were reported.
* **Describe injuries** (injury_details): Describe any injuries that occurred (if applicable).
* **Environmental impact** (environmental_impact): Evaluate the environmental impact of the spill (none, minor, moderate, or severe).
* **Actions taken to contain spill** (response_actions): Select all actions taken to contain the spill (evacuated area, contained spill, neutralized chemical, called hazmat, ventilation increased, personal protective equipment used, or other).
* **Was hazmat team called?** (hazmat_called): Indicate if the hazmat team was called.
* **Was cleanup completed?** (cleanup_completed): Indicate if the cleanup was completed.
* **Any witnesses** (witnesses): Identify any witnesses to the incident.
* **Recommended prevention measures** (prevention_measures): Suggest recommended prevention measures.
