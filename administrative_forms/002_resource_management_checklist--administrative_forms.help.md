# Resource Management Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Management Checklist is a tool used to track and record the status of various resources within the organization. It helps teams and individuals stay on top of resource allocation, utilization, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and fields being asked.
2. Answer all fields truthfully and thoroughly, as incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to incorrect resource management.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the required label on each field.
4. Save your progress and revisit the form as needed to update your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Resource Management Checklist** (`title`, text, not required): This is the title of the form, which serves as a header for the entire document.
- **Resource Management Checklist 1** (`resource_management_checklist_page_1`, text, not required): This section is for entering any additional comments or notes about resource management.
- **Resource Management Checklist 2** (`resource_management_checklist_page_2`, date, not required): This field is used to track dates for resource management. Enter a date in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
- **Resource Management Checklist 3** (`resource_management_checklist_page_3`, number, not required): This field is used to track numerical values for resource management. Enter a whole number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
- **Resource Management Checklist 4** (`resource_management_checklist_page_4`, select_one, not required): This field offers a choice between two options: 'Yes' or 'No'. Choose the answer that best represents your response to the question being asked.
- **Resource Management Checklist 5** (`resource_management_checklist_page_5`, select_multiple, not required): This field allows for multiple selections from the provided options. Select all applicable options that best represent your response to the question being asked.
- **Resource Management Checklist 6** (`resource_management_checklist_page_6`, time, not required): This field is used to track times for resource management. Enter a time in the format `hh:mm AM/PM` or `hh:mm` for 24-hour time.
- **Resource Management Checklist 7** (`resource_management_checklist_page_7`, note, not required): This field is for entering any additional comments or notes about resource management.
- **Resource Management Checklist 8** (`resource_management_checklist_page_8`, email, not required): This field is for entering contact information for resources being managed. Enter the resource's email address.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Review each page before proceeding to the next.
- If you're unsure about any section or field, refer back to this guide for more information.
