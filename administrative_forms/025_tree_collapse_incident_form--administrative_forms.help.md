# Tree Collapse Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about tree collapse incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the tree collapse occurred.
* **Tree Height** (`tree_height`, number, required: false): Provide the height of the tree at the time of the incident.
* **Tree Species** (`tree_species`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the species of the tree that collapsed from the options provided.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the incident, including the specific site or area.
* **Tree Condition** (`tree_condition`, select_one, required: false): Report if the tree was in good or bad condition before collapsing. This field will help in understanding the root cause of the incident.
* **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, text, required: false): Record any witness statements related to the incident. This can include statements from people who witnessed the collapse or from individuals who responded to the incident.
* **Witness Statements Attachment** (`witness_statements_attachment`, note, required: false): Attach any relevant witness statements or supporting documents related to the incident. This can include photos, videos, or other evidence.
* **Response Team** (`response_team`, select_multiple, required: false): Report the response team's involvement and their level of participation in the response effort. This can help in understanding the effectiveness of the response team.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the response team took to respond to the incident.
* **Response Date** (`response_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the response team arrived on the scene.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
- Only submit the form once the incident is fully investigated.
- Attach relevant documents or supporting evidence when possible.
