# fire_alarm_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture details of fire alarm incidents for safety and regulatory compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are reporting an actual incident, not a hypothetical or planned event.
2. Select the correct incident type from the "Incident Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide the accurate incident date and time.
4. Enter the incident location in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Enter as much detail as possible about the incident in the "Incident Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): This field allows you to select the type of incident (e.g., Fire Alarm, Other).
	+ Select "Fire Alarm" for incidents involving fire alarms.
	+ Select "Other" for incidents that do not fit into the "Fire Alarm" category.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): This field is for entering the date of the incident.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): This field is for entering the time of the incident.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): This field is for entering the location where the incident occurred.
- **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required): This field is for providing a detailed description of the incident.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when providing incident details.
- If you are unsure about any field, it's better to err on the side of caution and seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant authority.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
