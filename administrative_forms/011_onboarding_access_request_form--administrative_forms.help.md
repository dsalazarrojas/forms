# onboarding_access_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help employees request access to the onboarding process, allowing them to provide necessary information to be integrated into our onboarding process. To request access to the onboarding process, fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out each of the requested fields. You can type in your employee name and ID, select your role and department, enter your date of joining, and provide any other requested information.
2. Ensure that you are requesting access for the correct manager and providing their name and email accurately.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: (`employee_name`, text, optional)
  Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
* **Employee ID**: (`employee_id`, number, optional)
  Enter your employee ID as assigned by the HR department.
* **Role**: (`role`, select_one, optional)
  Select your role in the company, whether Manager, Team Lead, or Employee.
* **Department**: (`department`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select the department(s) you belong to, such as HR, Finance, Marketing, or IT.
* **Date of Joining**: (`date_of_joining`, date, optional)
  Enter your date of joining in the company.
* **Manager Name**: (`manager_name`, text, optional)
  Enter your manager's name as it appears on official records.
* **Manager Email**: (`manager_email`, email, optional)
  Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: (`manager_phone`, text, optional)
  Enter your manager's phone number.

## Tips

Please ensure that you provide accurate information, as it will be used to process your onboarding request. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department.
