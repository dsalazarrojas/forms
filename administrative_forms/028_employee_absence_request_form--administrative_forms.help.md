# Employee Absence Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an absence (vacation, sick leave, or other) for an employee. This form helps the employee to request a specific period of absence and ensures that it is properly documented and communicated with the management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select the start and end dates of your absence.
3. Provide a reason for your absence.
4. Specify the start and end times of your absence.
5. Enter the number of days off.
6. If necessary, add a note for the manager.
7. Select the approval status of your request.
8. If needed, add comments for further context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number to identify your account.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the start date of your absence.
- **end_date** (`end_date`, date, required): Choose the end date of your absence.
- **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your absence reason.
- **start_time** (`start_time`, time, required): Select the start time of your absence.
- **end_time** (`end_time`, time, required): Select the end time of your absence.
- **Days Off** (`days_off`, number, required): Enter the number of days you plan to be off.
- **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes for your manager if needed.
- **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select whether your request is pending, approved, or declined.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or additional context for your absence request.
 
#Tips
* Please ensure that you have checked the company's absence policy before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about the correct dates or times, you can always double-check the company's absence policy or consult with your HR representative.
* After submitting this form, the status of your request will be updated accordingly.
