# Venture Capital Analyst Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from Venture Capital Analysts who are offboarding from the company. It is a crucial document that helps in the transition process, ensuring that their responsibilities and assets are properly transferred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your Analyst Name.
	* Provide your Date of Birth.
	* Enter your Phone Number (optional).
2. Enter your employment details:
	* Enter your Job Title.
	* Enter your Email Address.
	* Enter your Manager's Name.
	* Enter your Manager's Email (optional).
3. Provide information for transition:
	* Enter the Reason for Leaving (optional).
	* Enter the Next Steps for your team and the company.
4. Share your feedback:
	* Enter any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Analyst Name** (`analyst_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your direct manager's name as it appears on their ID or business card.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address (if available).
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your offboarding from the company (if applicable).
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Enter the steps you have planned for your team and the company to continue your work.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments you may have.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of all information provided.
* Ensure you have all necessary approvals and permissions before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR department.
