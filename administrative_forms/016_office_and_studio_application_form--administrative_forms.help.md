# Office And Studio Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants who wish to rent a studio or office space in our building. It will help us assess the application and contact you for further information or to schedule a viewing if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*)
* Double-check the information you have entered to ensure it is accurate
* Click "Submit" to send the form to us
* We will review your application and contact you shortly

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, false): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as an applicant.
* **Office Address** (`office_address`, text, false): Enter your current office or business address, so we can verify your business or identify your office space location. If you don't have a physical office, leave this field blank.
* **Studio Space** (`studio_space`, select multiple, false): Choose whether you need a studio space. Select both 'Yes' if you need a studio space and 'No' if you don't.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your business email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your contact number so we can easily contact you.

Note: If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to assist you throughout the application process.
