# Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form is designed to assess an organization's ability to respond to and recover from disasters. This form collects information about an organization's disaster recovery plan, which will help us evaluate its effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. For each question, select the correct answer or enter the required information.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, as the form requires all fields to be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form** (`title`, `note`, required: false): This form's title serves as a header and introduction to the assessment questions that follow.
* **Organization Information** (`organization_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your organization's name, address, and other relevant details as prompted.
* **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that apply to your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 1** (`question1`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the last disaster recovery plan review or update.
* **Question 2** (`question2`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the last disaster recovery plan review or update.
* **Question 3** (`question3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of people involved in the disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 4** (`question4`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the most suitable option for your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 5** (`question5`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 6** (`question6`, `text`, required: false): Enter a detailed description of your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 7** (`question7`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other relevant information about your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 8** (`question8`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 9** (`question9`, `text`, required: false): Enter any other details about your organization's disaster recovery plan.
* **Question 10** (`question10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last date and time of the disaster recovery plan update.
