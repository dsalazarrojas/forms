# Reception Supervisor Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of a reception supervisor, gathering data on their work quality, efficiency, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the evaluation date (page 2)
2.  Enter the supervisor's name (page 3)
3.  Enter the supervisor's title (page 4)
4.  Select the performance areas where the supervisor excelled or needs improvement (page 5)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **reception_supervisor** (page 1): Enter a brief description of the reception supervisor being evaluated.
* **evaluation_date** (page 2): Enter the date of the evaluation in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **supervisor_name** (page 3): Enter the full name of the supervisor being evaluated.
* **supervisor_title** (page 4): Enter the title of the supervisor being evaluated.
* **performance_areas** (page 5): Select all applicable performance areas where the supervisor has excelled or needs improvement. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

## Tips
- Be specific and objective when evaluating the supervisor's performance.
- Provide constructive feedback for improvement.
- Ensure you have all necessary information and resources before completing this form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
