# Fire Safety Drill Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants and their parents or guardians for a Fire Safety Drill event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information in the "Participant Info" section.
2. If you are a minor, fill out your parent or guardian's information in the "Parent or Guardian Info" section.
3. Read the "Waiver Terms" and sign the agreement if you understand and agree with its content.
4. Provide your emergency contact information, including your name and relationship with the participant.
5. Have a parent or guardian sign the agreement if you are a minor.
6. Sign the "Participant Signature" section.
7. Have the organization or representative involved in the activity sign the "Organization Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info**: Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
  * Required field.
- **Parent or Guardian Info**: If you are a minor, please provide your parent or guardian's name, contact information, and relationship to you.
  * Optional field.
- **Waiver Terms**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the Fire Safety Drill event or activity.
  * Required field, must be signed.
- **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide the name, contact information, and relationship of your emergency contact.
  * Required field.
- **Participant Signature**: Sign this section to indicate your understanding and agreement with the waiver terms.
  * Required field.
- **Parent or Guardian Signature**: If you are a minor, sign this section as a parent or guardian to indicate your understanding and agreement with the waiver terms.
  * Optional field.
- **Organization Signature**: Sign this section to indicate your organization's or representative's understanding and agreement with the waiver terms.
  * Required field.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
  * Required field.
- **Emergency Contact Info**: Provide the emergency contact's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
  * Required field.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
- Review the waiver terms carefully before signing.
- If you are a minor, ensure your parent or guardian signs the agreement.
- Have your parent or guardian sign the agreement if you are a minor.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date emergency contact information.
- Sign the "Organization Signature" section to confirm your organization's understanding and agreement with the waiver terms.
