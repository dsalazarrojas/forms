# Rental Agreement Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Agreement Acknowledgment Form is a document that is filled out by tenants to acknowledge that they have read and understood the terms and conditions of the rental agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your tenant information (if you are a new tenant or have moved into a new rental property)
2. Select your rental agreement (choose the agreement that applies to your rental property)
3. Confirm your understanding of the agreement (choose all that apply)
4. Enter any contact information (in case we need to contact you)
5. Enter any additional rental terms and conditions (if there are any)
6. Enter the date and time you signed the agreement
7. Sign and date the agreement
8. Enter a note (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Tenant Information
* **Tenant Name**: Enter your name as per the rental agreement. 
* **Tenant ID Number**: Enter your tenant ID number (if applicable).
### Page 2: Rental Agreement
* **Rental Agreement**: Select the agreement that applies to your rental property (Option 1 or Option 2)
### Page 3: Confirmation
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that you have read and understood all the terms and conditions of the rental agreement (choose all that apply)
### Page 4: Contact Information
* **Contact Name**: Enter your contact name (if different from your tenant name).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email.
### Page 5: Rental Terms
* **Additional Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions that you agree to.
### Page 6: Rental Terms Date and Time
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the rental agreement.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed the rental agreement.
### Page 7: Signatures
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign with a number (1) to acknowledge that you have read and understood the agreement.
* **Landlord Signature**: Sign with a number (1) to acknowledge that you have read and understood the agreement.
### Page 8: Additional Information
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the rental agreement.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email. 

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the terms and conditions of the rental agreement before filling out this form.
* Sign the agreement with a number (1) to acknowledge that you have read and understood the agreement.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
