# Vendor Documentation Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors about their preferences on sharing documentation. You are kindly requested to provide your input on sharing vendor documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several sections. To complete it, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your name and input in the first text field (`user_input`). This is the primary contact information that we will use to reach out to you if needed.
2. Please describe your documentation requirements or preferences in the second text field (`vendor_documentation`).
3. Indicate if you are willing to share your documentation with others or not (`share_permission`).
4. Provide your contact email address in the email field (`contact_information`). We will use this to get in touch with you.
5. Enter your phone number in the phone number field (`phone_number`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (User Input, text, required): Enter your name as the primary contact person for this vendor documentation.
* **vendor_documentation** (Vendor Documentation, text, optional): Describe your documentation requirements or preferences. This could include information about what you need to see, how often you want to be updated, or any specific requirements you have.
* **share_permission** (Share Permission, select one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you are willing to share your documentation with others or "No" if you are not.
* **contact_information** (Contact Information, email, optional): Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **phone_number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact if needed.
