# Real Estate Mismanagement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Real Estate Mismanagement Claim Form is designed to gather information about issues related to the mismanagement of a rental property. This form is used to document and report concerns such as leaking roofs, poor maintenance, and disrepair that affect your living space.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details as a tenant in the "Tenant Info" section.
2. Provide information about the property where the mismanagement issue is occurring in the "Property Info" section.
3. Select the type of issue you're experiencing from the "Issue Type" dropdown menu in the "Property Info" section.
4. Describe the problem in detail in the "Description" section, including dates and any relevant events.
5. If you need to contact us for further assistance or updates, please provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Info** (`tenant_info`, text, required): This section asks for your personal details as a tenant, including your name and contact information. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date so we can reach out to you for further assistance.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, required): Describe the property where the mismanagement issue is occurring, including the address and any relevant details about the property.
* **Issue Type** (`issue_type`, select_one, required): This menu lists the types of issues that can be reported, including Leaking Roof, Poor Maintenance, and Disrepair. Select the type of issue that best matches your concern.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): This is where you should provide detailed information about the problem, including dates, events, and any other relevant details. Be as specific as possible to help us understand the issue.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you need to be contacted for further assistance or updates, please provide your contact information here.
