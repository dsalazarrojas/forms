# purchase_requisition_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to log purchase requisitions submitted to the system, allowing for accurate tracking and record-keeping of purchase activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **submit_purchase_request**: Enter a brief description of the purchase request.
2.  **choose_category**: Select the category of the purchase (Goods, Services).
3.  **quantity_requested**: Enter the quantity of the item being requested.
4.  **unit_cost**: Enter the unit cost of the item.
5.  **total_cost**: Enter the total cost of the purchase.
6.  **requested_by**: Select the individual who submitted the purchase request (John, Jane).
7.  **approved_by**: Select the individual(s) who approved the purchase request (John, Jane).
8.  **purchase_date**: Enter the date of the purchase (if known).
9.  **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the purchase request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **submit_purchase_request**: A brief description of the purchase request (text field, required).
*   **choose_category**: Select the category of the purchase (select one from Goods, Services) (required).
*   **quantity_requested**: Enter the quantity of the item being requested (number field, optional).
*   **unit_cost**: Enter the unit cost of the item (number field, optional).
*   **total_cost**: Enter the total cost of the purchase (number field, optional).
*   **requested_by**: Select the individual who submitted the purchase request (select multiple, required).
*   **approved_by**: Select the individual(s) who approved the purchase request (select multiple, required).
*   **purchase_date**: Enter the date of the purchase (date field, optional).
*   **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the purchase request (text field, optional).
