# Shareholder Agreement Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Agreement Monitoring Form is designed to collect and record information about shareholder agreements between the company and its shareholders. This form helps the company keep track of its shareholder agreements and ensures compliance with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the contact information section with your name and email address.
* Enter the date of the agreement in the "agreement-date" field.
* Enter the expiration date of the agreement in the "agreement-expiration-date" field.
* Enter the name of the shareholder in the "shareholder-name" field.
* Enter the email address of the shareholder in the "shareholder-email" field.
* Select the agreement version from the dropdown list in the "agreement-version" field.
* Enter the text of the agreement in the "agreement-text" field.
* Upload the agreement document in the "agreement-upload" field.
* If the agreement is to be removed, mark it as "remove-agreement-upload".
* Select the status of the agreement from the dropdown list in the "agreement-status" field.
* Add any additional notes about the agreement in the "agreement-notes" field.
* Add any comments about the agreement in the "agreement-comments" field.
* List any uploads related to the agreement in the "agreement-uploads" field.
* Select the shareholders involved in the agreement from the dropdown list in the "agreement-shareholder-list" field.
* Add any notes about the shareholders in the "agreement-shareholder-notes" field.
* Add any comments about the shareholders in the "agreement-shareholder-comments" field.
* Enter the date the agreement was signed in the "agreement-signing-date" field.
* Select the status of the review from the dropdown list in the "agreement-review-status" field.
* Add any notes about the review in the "agreement-review-notes" field.
* Add any comments about the review in the "agreement-review-comments" field.
* Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement in the "agreement-signing-signer" field.
* Enter the name of the person who reviewed the agreement in the "agreement-review-signer" field.
* Enter the email address of the person who signed the agreement in the "agreement-signing-signer-email" field.
* Enter the email address of the person who reviewed the agreement in the "agreement-review-signer-email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact-name`, text, required/optional): This field is for the contact name of the person who is submitting the form. It is a text field where you can enter your name.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement-date`, date, required/optional): This field is for the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Expiration Date** (`agreement-expiration-date`, date, required/optional): This field is for the expiration date of the agreement.
* **Shareholder Name** (`shareholder-name`, text, required/optional): This field is for the name of the shareholder.
* **Shareholder Email** (`shareholder-email`, email, required/optional): This field is for the email address of the shareholder.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement-version`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting the version of the agreement.
* **Agreement Text** (`agreement-text`, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the text of the agreement.
* **Agreement Upload** (`agreement-upload`, text, required/optional): This field is for uploading the agreement document.
* **Remove Agreement Upload** (`remove-agreement-upload`, text, required/optional): This field is for marking the agreement as to be removed.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement-status`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting the status of the agreement.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement-notes`, note, required/optional): This field is for adding additional notes about the agreement.
* **Agreement Comments** (`agreement-comments`, text, required/optional): This field is for adding comments about the agreement.
* **Agreement Uploads** (`agreement-uploads`, text, required/optional): This field is for listing any uploads related to the agreement.
* **Shareholder List** (`agreement-shareholder-list`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for selecting the shareholders involved in the agreement.
* **Shareholder Notes** (`agreement-shareholder-notes`, note, required/optional): This field is for adding additional notes about the shareholders.
* **Shareholder Comments** (`agreement-shareholder-comments`, text, required/optional): This field is for adding comments about the shareholders.
* **Agreement Signing Date** (`agreement-signing-date`, date, required/optional): This field is for the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Review Status** (`agreement-review-status`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for selecting the status of the review.
* **Agreement Review Notes** (`agreement-review-notes`, note, required/optional): This field is for adding notes about the review.
* **Agreement Review Comments** (`agreement-review-comments`, text, required/optional): This field is for adding comments about the review.
* **Agreement Signing Signer** (`agreement-signing-signer`, text, required/optional): This field is for the name of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Review Signer** (`agreement-review-signer`, text, required/optional): This field is for the name of the person who reviewed the agreement.
* **Agreement Signing Signer Email** (`agreement-signing-signer-email`, email, required/optional): This field is for the email address of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Review Signer Email** (`agreement-review-signer-email`, email, required/optional): This field is for the email address of the person who reviewed the agreement.
