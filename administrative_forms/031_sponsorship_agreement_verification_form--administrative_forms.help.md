# Sponsorship Agreement Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store information about sponsorship agreements for events. It ensures that the agreement is properly verified and documented.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the event in the **Event Title** field.
2.  Enter the name of the sponsor in the **Sponsor Name** field.
3.  Select the date of the agreement in the **Agreement Date** field as a date.
4.  Enter the address of the sponsor in the **Sponsor Address** field.
5.  Describe the event details in the **Event Details** field.
6.  Explain the verification process that was used in the **Verification Process** field.
7.  Enter the terms of the sponsorship in the **Sponsorship Terms** field.
8.  Select the verification result from the options provided in the **Verification Result** field.
9.  (Optional) Add any additional comments in the **Comments** field.
10.  (Optional) Upload any relevant files in the **File Upload** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event for which the sponsorship agreement was made.
*   **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sponsor involved in the agreement.
*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the sponsorship agreement was signed.
*   **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the sponsor.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Describe the event details in this field.
*   **Verification Process** (`verification_process`, text, required): Explain the process used to verify the sponsorship agreement.
*   **Sponsorship Terms** (`sponsorship_terms`, text, required): Enter the terms of the sponsorship.
*   **Verification Result** (`verification_result`, select_one, required): Select the result of the verification process from the options provided:
    +   **Verified**: The sponsorship agreement has been verified.
    +   **Not Verified**: The sponsorship agreement has not been verified.
    +   **Pending**: The verification process is pending.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the sponsorship agreement.
*   **File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files related to the sponsorship agreement.

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are filled out as required.
*   Use the correct date format for the **Agreement Date** field.
*   Clearly describe the **Event Details** and **Verification Process** fields.
*   Choose the correct verification result from the options provided in the **Verification Result** field.
*   Only upload relevant files in the **File Upload** field.
