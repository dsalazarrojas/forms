# Support Ticket Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Ticket Intake Form is used to collect information about a support ticket from the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, click on each page and fill out the fields as prompted. Each field is labeled and explained below. Please note that not all fields are required, but filling out all required fields will help our team to better understand your issue and resolve it more efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
	+ Please choose one of the two options: Administrative Forms or Other.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required: false): Describe the issue you are experiencing.
	+ Please write a brief description of the problem you are facing.
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the customer submitting the ticket.
	+ Please enter the name of the customer as it appears on their ticket.
* **Customer E-mail** (`Customer E-mail`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer submitting the ticket.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`Customer Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer submitting the ticket.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Ticket Subject** (`Ticket Subject`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief subject for the ticket.
	+ Please enter a brief description of the issue.
* **Ticket Priority** (`Ticket Priority`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the priority level of the ticket.
	+ Please choose one of the three options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Ticket Status** (`Ticket Status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of the ticket.
	+ Please choose one or more of the three options: New, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Ticket Details** (`Ticket Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about the issue.
	+ Please enter any additional information about the problem you are facing.
* **Customer Message** (`Customer Message`, `text`, required: false): Enter a message from the customer.
	+ Please enter a message from the customer.
* **Assigned To** (`Assigned To`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select who the ticket will be assigned to.
	+ Please choose one or more of the three options: chatjimmy, jenny, or joe.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select who submitted the ticket.
	+ Please choose one or more of the three options: chatjimmy, jenny, or joe.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, `date`, required: false): The date and time the ticket was created.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Updated At** (`Updated At`, `date`, required: false): The date and time the ticket was last updated.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Due Date** (`Due Date`, `date`, required: false): The date the ticket is due.
	+ Please enter a date if the ticket has a due date.
* **Ticket Updated At** (`Ticket Updated At`, `date`, required: false): The date and time the ticket was last updated.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, `date`, required: false): The date and time the ticket was submitted.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Submitted Time** (`Submitted Time`, `time`, required: false): The time the ticket was submitted.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, `date`, required: false): The date the ticket was submitted.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Ticket Updated Time** (`Ticket Updated Time`, `time`, required: false): The time the ticket was last updated.
	+ This field is automatically populated.
* **Due Time** (`Due Time`, `time`, required: false): The time the ticket is due.
	+ Please enter a time if the ticket has a due time.
* **Created By** (`Created By`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select who created the ticket.
	+ Please choose one or more of the three options: chatjimmy, jenny, or joe.

Please note that not all fields are required, and you can leave them blank if not applicable. If you need help with the form or have any questions, please contact our support team.
