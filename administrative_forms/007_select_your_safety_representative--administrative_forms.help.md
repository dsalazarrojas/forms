# Select Your Safety Representative - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select a safety representative for various purposes within the organization. It requires the user to provide essential information such as department, reason for selection, name, job title, contact number, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the steps below:
  - Select your department from the provided options.
  - Provide the reason for selection.
  - Enter the name of the safety representative.
  - If applicable, select a start date and provide additional reason for selection 2.
  - Enter the job title and job title 2 (if required).
  - Enter a contact number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Department** (`select_one`): Select your department from the provided options.
- **Reason for Selection** (`text`): Provide a brief reason for selecting the safety representative.
- **Safety Representative Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the selected safety representative.
- **Start Date** (`date`): Select a start date if applicable. This is not a required field.
- **Reason For Selection 2** (`text`): Provide additional reason for selection 2.
- **Job Title** (`text`): Enter the job title of the selected safety representative.
- **Reason For Selection 2 2** (`text`): This field is not required and can be left blank.
- **Contact Number** (`text`): Enter the contact number of the selected safety representative.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the selected safety representative.
- **Reason For Selection 3** (`text`): This field is not required and can be left blank.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (\*). 
- If you are unsure about the department, reason for selection, or other fields, please refer to your supervisor or relevant documentation.
