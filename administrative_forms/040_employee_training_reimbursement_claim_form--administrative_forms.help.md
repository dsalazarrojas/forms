# Employee Training Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a claim for reimbursement of expenses related to approved training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Make sure you have all the necessary documents and information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Employee name
	* Training provider
	* Training date
	* Training duration
	* Reimbursement amount
	* Submission date
3. Attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or certificates of completion, to the form.
4. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed and all information is accurate.
5. Submit the form to the relevant department or manager for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Claim**: This field is a text field where you can enter a brief description of your claim.
* **Employee Name**: This field is for your name as the employee submitting the claim.
* **Training Provider**: This field is for the name of the training provider or organization that delivered the training session.
* **Training Date**: This field is for the date on which the training session took place.
* **Training Duration**: This field is for the duration of the training session, in days or hours.
* **Reimbursement Amount**: This field is for the amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
* **Submission Date**: This field is for the date on which you are submitting this claim.
* **Supporting Documents**: This field is for any additional documents that support your claim, such as receipts or certificates of completion.
