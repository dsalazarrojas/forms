# Vendor Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from vendors regarding their declaration of compliance with various regulatory requirements. It is an administrative form used to gather data for internal record-keeping and compliance monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Vendor Name** in the designated field.
2.  Enter the **Declaration Date** in the provided date field.
3.  Select the correct **Declaration Type** from the available options.
4.  Fill in your **Vendor Address**.
5.  If applicable, enter your **Vendor Contact** information.
6.  Provide a detailed description of your declaration in the **Declaration Details** field.
7.  Sign the **Vendor Signature** field with your signature, if required.
8.  Select all relevant **Compliance Certifications** that apply to your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): This field is for the vendor's name, which may be a business or individual name.
- **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the declaration is made.
- **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vendor's location.
- **Declaration Type** (`declaration_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of declaration from the provided options, which are:
  - Compliance Declaration
  - Compliance Review
  - Regulatory Review
- **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, text, optional): This field is for any additional contact information for the vendor.
- **Declaration Details** (`declaration_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the declaration in this field.
- **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, note, optional): This field is for the vendor's signature, which is optional but recommended for authenticity.
- **Compliance Certfication** (`compliance_certification`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable compliance certifications from the options provided.
