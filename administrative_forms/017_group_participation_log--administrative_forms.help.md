# Group Participation Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Participation Log is used to track individual participation in a group event, capturing key details such as participant information and event specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** in the "Participant Name" field, ensuring it matches your actual name.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the "Participant Email" field for contact purposes.
3. Add your **Participant Phone Number** in the "Participant Phone" field for additional contact options.
4. Choose your **Participation Status** by selecting from the options: **attending**, **not attending**, or **declined**.
5. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, type them in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the title of the form and not a field to fill in.
* **Participant Form**: This section is for filling in your participant's details.
	+ **Participant Name** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter your actual name as a participant.
	+ **Participant Email** (`email`, **required**: false): Enter your contact email for updates.
	+ **Participant Phone** (`text`, **required**: false): Add your phone number for extra contact options.
* **Participation Status** (`select_one`, **required**: false): Choose your participation status: **attending**, **not attending**, or **declined**.
* **Event Details**: 
	+ **Event Date** (`date`, **required**: false): Enter the date of the event.
	+ **Event Time** (`time`, **required**: false): Enter the time of the event.
	+ **Event Location** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the event location.
* **Timing Details**:
	+ **Start Time** (`time`, **required**: false): Enter the start time of the event.
	+ **End Time** (`time`, **required**: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Additional Information**:
	+ **Attendee List** (`select_multiple`, **required**: false): Check if this participant will be attending as part of an attendee list.
	+ **Notes** (`text`, **required**: false): Add any notes or comments about the event.
* **Event Version** (`text`, **required**: false): This is for event version tracking and not a field to fill in.
* **Status Comments** (`text`, **required**: false): Add any comments or updates about the participant's status. 

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in for accurate tracking.
- Review and update this form regularly to reflect any changes or updates.
- Use the **Notes** field to capture any specific details or comments. 

Note: This guide is for the participant to fill out the form accurately. The event organizer is encouraged to review and update the form regularly to track changes and updates.
