# elder-care-authorization-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization for elder care services. It collects family information, patient consent, and other relevant details required for providing care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the family member's information in the "Family Information" field.
2. Provide the patient's consent in the "Patient Consent" field.
3. Enter the caregiver's name in the "Caregiver Name" field.
4. Select the caregiver's contact information from the dropdown list in the "Caregiver Contact Info" field. You can select "None" or "Other" to indicate that the information is not applicable or to provide custom information.
5. Choose the authorized caregiver's information from the dropdown list in the "Authorized Caregiver Info" field. Similar to "Caregiver Contact Info", you can select "None" or "Other" for non-applicable or custom information.
6. Choose the authorization period from the dropdown list in the "Authorization Period" field.
7. Select the healthcare provider from the dropdown list in the "Healthcare Provider" field. You can choose "None" or "Other" to indicate that the information is not applicable or to provide custom information.
8. Enter the healthcare provider's name in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Information**: <label> (id: 1) This field is used to gather information about the family member. Enter the family member's name, relationship, and any other relevant details.
* **Patient Consent** (id: 2): This field requires the patient's consent for the elder care services. Enter a brief statement of consent.
* **Caregiver Name** (id: 3): Enter the name of the person providing care for the family member.
* **Caregiver Contact Info** (id: 4): Select the caregiver's contact information. You can choose "None" if not applicable, or select "Other" and provide custom information.
* **Authorized Caregiver Info** (id: 5): Choose the authorized caregiver's information. Similar to "Caregiver Contact Info", you can select "None" or "Other" for non-applicable or custom information.
* **Authorization Period** (id: 6): Choose the authorization period for the elder care services. This is likely the duration of time the care will be provided.
* **Healthcare Provider** (id: 7): Select the healthcare provider that will be providing services to the family member. You can choose "None" if not applicable, or select "Other" and provide custom information.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (id: 8): Enter the name of the healthcare provider. This field is usually the name of the medical institution or organization providing care.
