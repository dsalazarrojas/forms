# Visitor Escort Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information for authorized visitors on-site, ensuring that all visitors are properly escorted and supervised at all times. This information will be used to confirm the presence of authorized personnel accompanying visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete this form by filling out the fields below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Name**: Enter the name of the visitor to be authorized.
* **Escort Name**: Enter the name of the person accompanying the visitor.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the visit (format MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Start Time**: Enter the time when the visitor arrives at the location.
* **End Time**: Enter the time when the visit will end.
* **Purpose**: Briefly describe the purpose of the visit.
* **Escorted By**: Enter the name of the person accompanying the visitor.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person authorizing this visit.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the visitor's visit.

## Tips
* Always fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that the form is processed correctly.
* This form should be used in conjunction with other security protocols to ensure visitor safety.
