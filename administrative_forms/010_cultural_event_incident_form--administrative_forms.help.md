# Cultural Event Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and document cultural event incidents that have occurred within the organization. The purpose of this form is to gather information and provide a structured approach to investigating and resolving these incidents in a consistent manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, please fill in the Event Description field with a detailed account of what happened during the cultural event. This should include any relevant details about the event, such as what was happening, who was involved, and any notable occurrences.
2. Next, enter the Date of the incident in the Date field. This should be the date on which the incident occurred.
3. If applicable, enter the Time of the incident in the Time field. This should be the time at which the incident occurred.
4. Enter the Incident Location in the Incident Location field. This should be the location where the incident took place.
5. Please enter the name of the person who reported the incident in the Incident Reporter field.
6. Finally, enter the contact number of the person involved in the Contact Number field. This can be a phone number or email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Description (Event Description)** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed account of what happened during the cultural event.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the incident occurred, if applicable.
* **Incident Location (Incident Location)** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Reporter (Incident Reporter)** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the person involved, such as a phone number or email address.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is a critical tool for documenting and investigating cultural event incidents. Your input is crucial in ensuring that we can resolve these incidents effectively and efficiently.
