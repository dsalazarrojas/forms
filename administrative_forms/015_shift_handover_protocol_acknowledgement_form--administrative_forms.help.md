# Shift Handover Protocol Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Handover Protocol Acknowledgement Form is a document that confirms the handover of shifts between employees. It is designed to ensure a smooth transition of tasks and responsibilities from one employee to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct acknowledgement option for your previous shift.
2. Enter any notes from your previous shift in the "Notes from Previous Shift" field.
3. Choose the start time and date for your next shift.
4. Enter any notes you have for the next shift in the "Notes for Next Shift" field.
5. Select the correct acknowledgement option for your next shift.
6. Confirm the handover by checking the "Confirmation of Handover" checkbox.
7. Review and sign the form to confirm you have completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Acknowledgement of Previous Shift**: Select one of the options to indicate if you finished your shift on time, were running late, or did not finish your shift. This is a required field.
* **Notes from Previous Shift**: Enter any relevant notes from your previous shift. This field is not required, but it can be useful for passing on important information to your colleague.
* **Next Shift Details**: Choose the start time and date for your next shift. Please ensure you select the correct time and date to ensure a smooth transition.
* **Next Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your next shift. This field is a required date-time field.
* **Next Shift Start Date**: Enter the start date of your next shift. This field is a required date field.
* **Notes for Next Shift**: Enter any notes or reminders you have for your next shift. This field is not required, but it can be useful for passing on information to your colleague.
* **Acknowledgement of Next Shift**: Select one of the options to indicate if you will be on time, late, or not available for your next shift. This is a required field.
* **Confirmation of Handover**: Confirm that you have completed the handover of shifts by checking this checkbox. This is a required field.
* **Review and Sign**: Review your form to ensure you have completed it accurately, and then sign it to confirm.
* **Signature**: Sign your name as a confirmation of your acknowledgement.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are completing this form.

# Tips
* Please ensure you complete this form accurately and carefully to avoid any misunderstandings.
* If you are unable to complete the form, contact your supervisor or manager for assistance.
* This form is not intended for new employee onboarding or shift scheduling purposes.
