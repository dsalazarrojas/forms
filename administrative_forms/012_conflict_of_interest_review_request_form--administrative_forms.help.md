# Conflict Of Interest Review Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Conflict Of Interest Review Request Form is a document used to report potential conflicts of interest that may arise from personal or professional relationships. It is a tool to help ensure that all employees are aware of their responsibilities in disclosing conflicts of interest and to aid in the timely and thorough review of these situations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your employee ID and department.
3. Enter your position title and the name of your direct supervisor.
4. Choose the type of conflict review you are requesting (e.g., Self Disclosure, Manager Referral, etc.).
5. Select the category of the potential conflict (e.g., outside employment, financial interest, etc.).
6. Describe the situation and provide any details about the parties involved.
7. If applicable, provide an estimated value of financial interest.
8. Indicate if this is an ongoing or one-time situation, and if it affects your job duties.
9. Describe how this conflict affects your work duties.
10. If you have previously disclosed this situation, provide details of when and to whom.
11. Check if you have supporting documents available, and if so, describe them.
12. Provide any suggested resolution or mitigation strategies.
13. Indicate the level of urgency for the review.
14. Add any additional information that may be relevant.
15. Finally, sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Date of this review request.
* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Full name of person requesting review.
* **Contact Email Address** (`requester_email`, email, required): Contact email address.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, text, required): Contact phone number.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Your department or division.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Your job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Name of your direct supervisor.
* **Type of Review Request** (`request_type`, select_one, required): What type of conflict review is needed.
* **Category of Potential Conflict** (`conflict_category`, select_one, required): Select the primary category.
* **Detailed Description of Situation** (`conflict_description`, text, required): Provide full details of the potential conflict.
* **Parties Involved** (`parties_involved`, text, required): Names and organizations of all parties.
* **Nature of Relationship** (`relationship_nature`, text, required): Describe your relationship to the situation.
* **Approximate Value of Financial Interest** (`financial_interest_value`, number, optional): If applicable, estimated value.
* **When Did This Conflict Arise** (`when_conflict_arose`, date, required): Date or time period when situation began.
* **Is This an Ongoing or One-Time Situation** (`ongoing_or_one_time`, select_one, required): Duration of the potential conflict.
* **Does This Affect Your Job Duties** (`affects_job_duties`, select_one, required): Will this impact your work responsibilities.
* **How Does This Affect Your Duties** (`how_affects_duties`, text, optional): Describe the impact on your work.
* **Have You Previously Disclosed This** (`prior_disclosure`, select_one, required): Has this been disclosed before.
* **Prior Disclosure Details** (`prior_disclosure_details`, text, optional): When and to whom was it disclosed.
* **Supporting Documents Available** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, required): Do you have documents to support this disclosure.
* **List of Supporting Documents** (`document_list`, text, optional): Describe documents you will provide.
* **Suggested Resolution or Mitigation** (`suggested_resolution`, text, optional): Any ideas you have for managing this conflict.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): How quickly is review needed.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Any other relevant details.
* **Requester Signature** (`requester_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as signature.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): For administrative use.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- Supporting documents should be attached separately and clearly labeled.
- If you have any additional information or questions, please provide them in the "Additional Information" field.
