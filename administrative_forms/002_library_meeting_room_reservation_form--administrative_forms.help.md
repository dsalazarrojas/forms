# Library Meeting Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for library staff to reserve a meeting room for a specific date and time. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired meeting room from the options provided.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the reservation.
3. Enter the start and end times of the reservation.
4. Enter the duration of the reservation in hours.
5. Enter the date and time of the reservation.
6. Select the room size that best suits your needs.
7. Enter the room number (if applicable).
8. Enter your name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Select Room
* **Select Room** (`Select Room`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose a meeting room from the list of available rooms. The available options are: A, B, C, D.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to reserve the room for.
### End Date
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the reservation will end.
### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the reservation.
### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the reservation.
### Duration
* **Duration** (`duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the reservation in hours.
### Reservation Date
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the reservation (same as start date).
### Reservation Time
* **Reservation Time** (`reservation_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the reservation (same as start time).
### Room Size
* **Room Size** (`room_size`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the meeting room that best suits your needs: Small, Medium, or Large.
### Room Number
* **Room Number** (`room_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room number of the meeting room you would like to reserve.
### User Name
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
### User Email
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are reserving a room that is available for the chosen dates and times. 
* Double-check your reservation details before submitting the form to avoid errors. 
* If you need to make any changes to your reservation, contact library staff directly.
