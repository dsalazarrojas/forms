# Maintenance Request Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Request Monitoring Form is used to track and monitor maintenance requests within a facility. This form provides a clear and concise way for maintenance personnel to document and categorize requests, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and that tasks are completed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting the **Facility** field and choose the relevant facility from the dropdown menu.
*   Enter the **Equipment ID** number, if applicable.
*   Choose the **Equipment Status** by selecting the correct status from the dropdown menu.
*   Select the **Status** of the request by choosing Open, Closed, or Pending.
*   Provide a **Description** of the maintenance request, including any necessary details.
*   Choose the **Priority** level of the request from Low, Medium, or High.
*   Select the person assigned to the maintenance request from the dropdown menu.
*   Choose the person who created the maintenance request, if it was created internally.
*   Enter the **Created Date** of the maintenance request.
*   Enter the **Updated Date** of the maintenance request, if applicable.
*   Select whether the maintenance request was created by internal personnel.
*   Select whether the maintenance request is due by a specific date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Maintenance Request (Text Field)** (`maintenance_request`, text, Optional):
    Enter the title of the maintenance request.
*   **Facility (Select One)** (`facility`, select_one, Optional):
    Select the facility where the maintenance request is being made.
*   **Equipment ID (Number Field)** (`equipment_id`, number, Optional):
    Enter the ID of the equipment being serviced or repaired.
*   **Equipment Status (Select Multiple)** (`equipment_status`, select_multiple, Optional):
    Select the current status of the equipment.
*   **Status (Select One)** (`status`, select_one, Required):
    Choose the status of the maintenance request: Open, Closed, or Pending.
*   **Description (Note Field)** (`description`, note, Required):
    Provide a brief description of the maintenance request.
*   **Priority (Select One)** (`priority`, select_one, Required):
    Select the priority level of the maintenance request: Low, Medium, or High.
*   **Assigned To (Select One)** (`assigned_to`, select_one, Optional):
    Choose the person or team responsible for completing the maintenance request.
*   **Created By (Select One)** (`created_by`, select_one, Optional):
    Check if the maintenance request was created internally.
*   **Created Date (Date Field)** (`created_at`, date, Required):
    Enter the date the maintenance request was made.
*   **Updated Date (Date Field)** (`updated_at`, date, Optional):
    Enter the date the maintenance request was last updated.
*   **Updated By (Select One)** (`updated_by`, select_one, Optional):
    Check if the maintenance request was updated internally.
*   **Due Date (Date Field)** (`due_by`, date, Optional):
    Enter the due date for the maintenance request.

## Tips
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
*   If the maintenance request was not created internally, please enter the correct created date. 
*   If the maintenance request was updated, enter the updated date as well. 

This guide is designed to be concise and easy to understand. If you have any doubts or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or colleague.
