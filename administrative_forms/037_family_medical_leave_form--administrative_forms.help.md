# Family Medical Leave Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request family medical leave for yourself or your family members. Family medical leave is an essential benefit that allows you to take care of your family members when they need medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please ensure that you are eligible for family medical leave benefits. You can find more information about this in the company's handbook or benefits policy. 

To fill out this form, simply select the correct options from the drop-down menus for each question. If a question does not apply to your situation, please select the "None of the above" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (select_one)**:
	+ This page has two options: "Option 1" and "Option 2". Please select the option that best describes your family medical leave reason.
	+ Option 1: Select this if you are requesting family medical leave for a family member who is experiencing a serious medical condition or surgery.
	+ Option 2: Select this if you are requesting family medical leave for another reason (please specify in the second page).
* **Second Page (select_multiple)**:
	+ This page allows you to select multiple options that apply to your family medical leave request.
	+ Option 3: Select this if the family member is a minor child.
	+ Option 4: Select this if the family member is an adult.
* **Third Page (text)**:
	+ Please provide a brief explanation for your family medical leave request. This could include the reason for the leave, the expected duration of the leave, and any supporting documentation or information.
* **Fourth Page (number)**:
	+ Please enter the number of days you are requesting for your family medical leave.
* **Fifth Page (number)**:
	+ Please enter the number of weeks you are requesting for your family medical leave.
* **Sixth Page (date)**:
	+ Please enter the start date of your family medical leave. If you are requesting a recurring leave, please enter the start date of the first leave period.
* **Seventh Page (time)**:
	+ Please enter the end date of your family medical leave. If you are requesting a recurring leave, please enter the end date of the last leave period.
* **Note**: Please ensure that you are providing accurate information when filling out this form. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR or benefits representative for guidance.
