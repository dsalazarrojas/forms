# Classroom Replacement Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and manage teacher absences, ensuring that the educational process is not disrupted.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2.  Review the form carefully before submitting it to the administration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Name** (1): Enter your full name.
- **Teacher ID Number** (2): Enter your employee or staff ID number.
- **Email Address** (3): Enter your school email address.
- **Phone Number** (4): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Absence Start Date** (5): Enter the date you will be absent from the classroom.
- **Absence End Date** (6): Enter the expected return date.
- **Type of Absence** (7): Select the reason for your absence (e.g., Illness, Medical Appointment, etc.).
- **Grade Level Taught** (8): Select all grade levels that will be affected by your absence.
- **Subjects Affected** (9): List all subjects that will be affected by your absence.
- **Classroom Location** (10): Enter the room number or location where the classroom is located.
- **Replacement Teacher Needed** (11): Indicate whether a full or partial day of coverage is required.
- **Lesson Plans Provided** (12): Select whether you have prepared lesson plans for the substitute teacher.
- **Special Instructions** (13): Provide any special instructions for the substitute teacher.
- **Student Roster Attached** (14): Indicate whether the current student roster is attached.
- **Ongoing Assignments** (15): Describe any ongoing projects or assignments the substitute teacher should be aware of.
- **Behavior Notes** (16): Provide any important behavior management notes for the substitute teacher.
- **Emergency Contact Available** (17): Indicate whether you can be reached in case of an emergency.
- **Administrator to Notify** (18): Enter the name of the administrator who should be notified about your absence.
