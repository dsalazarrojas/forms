# visitor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The visitor check out form is a tool to collect visitor information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Ensure you have all the required information available.
* Fill in your contact information in the "Visitor Contact Information" section.
* Enter your check-in and check-out times in the "Check In Time" and "Check Out Time" sections respectively.
* Provide feedback if desired in the "Feedback" section.
* Enter the reason for your visit in the "Reason for Visit" section.
* Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, **required: false**): This field is for entering your contact information, such as your name and contact number. You can enter any additional details if you wish.
* **Check In Time (check_in_time)** (`date`, **required: true**): Enter the date and time you arrived at the location.
* **Check Out Time (check_out_time)** (`time`, **required: true**): Enter the date and time you plan to leave the location.
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`note`, **required: false**): If you have any comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Reason for Visit (reason_for_visit)** (`text`, **required: true**): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your visit.
