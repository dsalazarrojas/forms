# Fishing Boat Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report on the status of a fishing boat, including its details, safety equipment, engine, safety inspection, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the boat details in the "Boat Details" field.
2.  Select the safety equipment that is currently on board in the "Safety Equipment" field.
3.  Provide information about the engine in the "Engine Details" field.
4.  Select the areas that passed the safety inspection in the "Safety Inspection" field.
5.  Enter any emergency equipment that is on board in the "Emergency Equipment" field.
6.  List any navigation equipment that is on board in the "Navigation Equipment" field.
7.  Enter information about any radio equipment in the "Radio Equipment" field.
8.  Select the outcome of the safety inspection in the "Inspection Result" field.
9.  Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
13. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
14. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Boat Details** (`boat_details`, text, required): Enter the details about the boat, including its name, type, and registration number.
*  **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_one, optional): Select any safety equipment that is currently on board, such as Life jackets, Flares, First aid kit, Fire extinguisher.
*  **Engine Details** (`engine_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the engine, such as its type and maintenance status.
*  **Safety Inspection** (`safety_inspection`, select_multiple, optional): Select any areas that passed the safety inspection, such as Propeller, Hull, Steering.
*  **Emergency Equipment** (`emergency_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any emergency equipment that is on board, such as emergency beacons, emergency flares, etc.
*  **Navigation Equipment** (`navigation_equipment`, text, optional): List any navigation equipment that is on board, such as GPS, chart plotter, etc.
*  **Radio Equipment** (`radio_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any information about any radio equipment, such as VHF, UHF, etc.
*  **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, select_one, optional): Select the outcome of the safety inspection, Passed or Failed.
*  **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the vessel.
*  **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to complete the form.
*  **Email** (`email_input`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*  **Phone** (`phone_input`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*  **Name** (`name_input`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
*  **Address** (`address_input`, text, optional): Enter your address for identification purposes.
