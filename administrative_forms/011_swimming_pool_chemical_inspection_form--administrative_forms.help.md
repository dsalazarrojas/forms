# Swimming Pool Chemical Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Swimming Pool Chemical Inspection Form is designed for pool maintenance staff to record and report on chemical levels, maintenance actions, and other relevant information for pool inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the pool's chemical records and maintenance schedule.
2. Check the current chemical levels and maintenance status.
3. Fill in the required information accurately and completely.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Pool ID (1)
 * **Pool ID**: This field is used to identify the pool undergoing inspection.
 * **Number**: Enter the pool's unique ID number.

### Chemical Name (2)
 * **Chemical Name**: Enter the name of the chemical being inspected or tested.
 * **Text**: Enter the name of the chemical in the format "Chemical X".

### Chemical Level (3)
 * **Chemical Level**: Enter the current level of the chemical in the pool.
 * **Number**: Enter the measured level of the chemical.

### Maintenance Action (4)
 * **Maintenance Action**: Describe any maintenance actions performed on the pool.
 * **Text**: Enter the action taken (e.g., "Drained and refilled chemical container", "Treated with shock treatment").

### Safety Standard (5)
 * **Safety Standard**: Describe the pool's safety standard compliance.
 * **Text**: Enter "Compliant" or "Non-compliant" and provide any additional comments.

### Record Date (6)
 * **Record Date**: Enter the date of the inspection or maintenance action.
 * **Date**: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Recorded By (7)
 * **Recorded By**: Enter the name of the person performing the inspection or maintenance action.
 * **Text**: Enter the name and any additional title or department (e.g., "John Doe, Pool Inspector", "Pool Maintenance Team").

### Inspector Notes (8)
 * **Inspector Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the pool's condition or maintenance status.
 * **Textarea**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant for future reference.

### Contact Email (9)
 * **Contact Email**: Enter the contact email of the person performing the inspection or maintenance action.
 * **Email**: Enter the email address.

### Contact Phone (10)
 * **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the person performing the inspection or maintenance action.
 * **Text**: Enter the phone number in the format 123-456-7890.

## Tips

* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to complete this form.
* Consult with your supervisor or colleagues if you are unsure about any field or information.
* Keep the form as up-to-date as possible to ensure accurate records and prevent errors.
