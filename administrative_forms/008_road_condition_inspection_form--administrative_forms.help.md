# Road Condition Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Condition Inspection Form is used to collect information about the condition of roads, including their maintenance frequency, condition, location, and other relevant factors. This form is essential for identifying areas that require immediate attention and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on the road's condition and your personal experience.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the field explanations provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **1. road_condition_inspection**
* (select_one): Select the frequency of inspections performed on this road:
	+ Inspection Frequency: how often is the road inspected (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, etc.)
	+ Road Condition: the general state of the road (e.g., good, fair, poor)
	+ Inspection Location: where the road is located (e.g., urban, rural, national park)
	+ Report Type: what type of report this road inspection relates to (e.g., routine, maintenance, emergency)

### **2. road_condition_inspection_2**
* (select_multiple): Select multiple conditions that apply to this road:
	+ A, B, C, D: options vary depending on the context, but generally represent different levels of road condition (e.g., A: excellent, B: good, C: fair, D: poor)

### **3. road_condition_inspection_3**
