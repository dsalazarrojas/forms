# Lease Renewal Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help property managers and leasing professionals streamline the lease renewal process for tenants. The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from tenants to facilitate a smooth and efficient lease renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Tenant Information** page with your name, contact information, and other relevant details as required.
2. Move on to the **Lease Terms** page and provide an update on the current lease terms, including any changes or renewals you wish to make.
3. On the **Review Date** page, select the date you would like to review and update your lease.
4. Finally, on the **Review Time** page, specify the time you would like to schedule the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information**
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Contact Information**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Lease Terms**
	+ **Lease Terms**: Describe any updates or changes you wish to make to your current lease terms, including rent, term length, or any other relevant details.
* **Review Date**
	+ **Review Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the review of your lease renewal. This will help us ensure that we have all necessary information ready for the review.
* **Review Time**
	+ **Review Time**: Specify the time of day you would like to schedule the review. Please note that we will contact you to confirm the review time and date.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time and fill it out thoroughly.
