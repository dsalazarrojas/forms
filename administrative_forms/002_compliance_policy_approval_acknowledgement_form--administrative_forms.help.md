# Compliance Policy Approval Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure employees acknowledge and confirm their understanding of the compliance policy.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your employee details, including your full name, employee ID number, department, job title, and manager's name.
2. Select your current work location from the dropdown list.
3. Confirm which version of the policy you are acknowledging.
4. Check the policy categories you have reviewed.
5. Enter the date you completed the policy training.
6. Confirm that you understand the policy.
7. Select the acknowledgment statement that best represents your situation.
8. If you have any questions or concerns, list them in the text field.
9. Sign the form electronically with your full name.
10. Enter the current date.
11. If required by your department, enter the name of the witness who can vouch for your acknowledgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears in company records.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your unique employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your current department from the list.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current position.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, select_one, required): Select your primary office or work arrangement from the list.
* **Policy Version Acknowledged** (`policy_version`, select_one, required): Confirm the version of the compliance policy you are acknowledging.
* **Policy Categories Reviewed** (`policy_categories_reviewed`, select_multiple, required): Select all policy areas you have reviewed.
* **Training Completion Date** (`training_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the policy training.
* **Understanding Confirmation** (`understanding_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the compliance policy.
* **Acknowledgment Statement** (`acknowledgment_statement`, select_one, required): Select the statement that best represents your situation.
* **Questions or Concerns** (`questions_or_concerns`, text, optional): List any questions or concerns about the policies.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.
* **Witness Name (Optional)** (`witness_name`, text, optional): If required by your department, enter the name of the witness who can vouch for your acknowledgment.
