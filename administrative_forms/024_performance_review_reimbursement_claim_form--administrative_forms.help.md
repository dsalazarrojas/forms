# Performance Review Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Reimbursement Claim Form is designed to collect information for employee reimbursement claims during performance reviews. It helps to ensure accurate and efficient processing of reimbursement requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Select the date of your performance review in the "Performance Review Date" field.
4. Enter the amount of reimbursement you are claiming in the "Reimbursement Amount" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the reimbursable expense in the "Reimbursement Description" field.
6. If applicable, indicate whether you have been approved by your manager for reimbursement by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Approved By" field.
7. If you have any comments or additional information, please include them in the "Comments" field.
8. Finally, select the date you are submitting the reimbursement claim in the "Submit Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Your employee name as it appears on your HR records or ID badge.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Your employee ID number as assigned by the company.
* **Performance Review Date** (`performance_review_date`, date, optional): The date of your most recent performance review.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): The name of your direct manager who conducted the performance review.
* **Reimbursement Amount** (`reimbursement_amount`, number, optional): The amount you are claiming as reimbursement.
* **Reimbursement Description** (`reimbursement_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the reimbursable expense.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have been approved by your manager for reimbursement. Select "Yes" if approved, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information about your reimbursement claim.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): The date you are submitting this reimbursement claim.
