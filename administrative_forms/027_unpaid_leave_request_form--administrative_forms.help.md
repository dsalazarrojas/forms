The Unpaid Leave Request Form is used by employees to submit a formal request for unpaid leave. This form helps to ensure that all required information is provided and is reviewed and approved by the relevant managers before the leave is processed.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Purpose

The Unpaid Leave Request Form is a formal document that allows employees to request time off from work due to unforeseen circumstances.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge.
2.  **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
3.  **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date you want to start your leave on.
4.  **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): The date you want to end your leave on.
5.  **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, required): Provide the reason for your request. This will help your manager understand your needs.
6.  **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of leave you are requesting.
7.  **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required): Select the managers who you need to approve your leave.
8.  **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your manager.
9.  **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
10. **Department Manager Name** (`department_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your department's manager.
11.  **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about your request, please add them here.
12.  **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have any contact information, please add it.

### Tips

- Make sure to check the relevant managers' schedules before selecting them for approval.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your HR department.

You can submit this form after filling out all the required fields. Your HR team will review and process your request according to the company's policies and procedures.
