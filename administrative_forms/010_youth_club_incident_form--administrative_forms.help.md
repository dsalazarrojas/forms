# Youth Club Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Club Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur at the youth club. This includes accidents, injuries, or other incidents that involve members or staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide the date and time the incident occurred.
2. Enter the location where the incident happened.
3. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
4. Identify the staff member who was involved in or witnessed the incident.
5. Provide any witness statements or statements from other people who saw or heard about the incident.
6. Identify the type of incident that occurred (e.g., First Aid, Medical, Injury, Other).
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date and Time (1)**: Please enter the date and time the incident occurred. This will help us keep track of when incidents happen.
* **Incident Location (2)**: Enter the location where the incident took place. This could be a specific room, area, or section of the club.
* **Incident Details (3)**: Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Try to include as much information as possible, including any circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Assigned Staff (4)**: Identify the staff member who was involved in or witnessed the incident. This will help us assign the right person to follow up on the incident.
* **Witness Statement (5)**: If someone witnessed the incident, please have them provide a statement about what they saw or heard.
* **Reporting Staff (6)**: Identify the staff member who is reporting the incident. This will help us track who is following up on the incident.
* **Incident Type (7)**: Please select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., First Aid, Medical, Injury, Other).
* **Incident Description (8)**: Enter any additional details about the incident that are not covered in the Incident Details field.
* **Witness Name (9)**: If someone witnessed the incident, please enter their name.
* **Witness Contact Info (10)**: If you have any contact information for the witness, please enter it here.
* **Witness Signature (11)**: If the witness is willing, please have them sign below. This will help us verify their statement.
* **Staff Signature (12)**: Please sign below to confirm that you are reporting the incident.
* **Incident Image (13)**: If you have any images or photos related to the incident, please upload them here.
* **Notes (14)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help from a staff member.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the youth club.
