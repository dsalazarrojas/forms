# Documentary Scene Archive Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Documentary Scene Archive Consent Form is a crucial document that helps ensure the comfort and consent of participants in a documentary scene. By filling out this form, participants acknowledge their involvement in the scene and give permission for their participation to be recorded and used for the documentary's purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Scene Description" field, providing a brief description of the documentary scene you participated in.
2. Enter your "Participant Name", which is your full name as it should appear in the documentary credits.
3. Add your "Participant Contact" details, including your address and phone number (if available).
4. Enter your "Participant Email" address, which will be used for correspondence and updates.
5. Select a "Scene Date" for the day when the scene was recorded.
6. Provide the "Scene Location" where the scene took place.
7. Choose your "Participant Consent" by selecting "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" from the options provided.
8. Optionally, add a "Note" if you'd like to provide additional information or context about your participation.
9. Finally, fill out "Submitted By" and "Submitted On" fields with your name and the date of submission, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scene Description** (`scene_description`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the documentary scene you participated in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Your full name as it should appear in the documentary credits.
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, required/optional): Your contact details, including address and phone number (if available).
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Scene Date** (`scene_date`, date, required/optional): The date when the scene was recorded.
* **Scene Location** (`scene_location`, text, required/optional): The location where the scene took place.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required): Your choice of "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" to indicate your consent for participation in the documentary.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): An optional field to provide additional information or context about your participation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Your name for credit purposes.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required/optional): The date and time of submission.

## Tips
Remember to carefully fill out all fields truthfully, especially the "Scene Description" and "Participant Name" fields. These will help ensure that your participation is accurately represented in the documentary.
