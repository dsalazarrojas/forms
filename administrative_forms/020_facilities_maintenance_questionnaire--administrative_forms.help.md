# Facilities Maintenance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about maintenance requests for facilities. This form helps facilities management and maintenance teams to identify issues, status, and condition of the facilities and to follow up on maintenance requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
2. If you are unsure about a field, please ask the facilities team for clarification.
3. Make sure to save your progress as you complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facilities address?** (Text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the address of the facility.
* **Do you have a maintenance request?** (Select One, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the options to indicate if you have a maintenance request.
* **What facilities issues do you have?** (Select Multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to the issues you are experiencing with the facility.
* **What is the facilities status?** (Select One, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the options to indicate the status of the facility.
* **What is the facilities condition?** (Select Multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to the condition of the facility.
* **When is the maintenance request date?** (Date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the maintenance request.
* **What time of day is the maintenance request time?** (Time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the maintenance request.
* **Who submitted the maintenance request?** (Select One, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the options to indicate who submitted the maintenance request.
* **What department is submitting the maintenance request?** (Select Multiple, required: false)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to the department submitting the maintenance request.
* **What is the name of the person submitting the request?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the person submitting the maintenance request.
* **What is the phone number of the person submitting the request?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the person submitting the maintenance request.
* **Is there additional information that needs to be considered?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional information that needs to be considered.
* **Do you have any further comments?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter any further comments about the maintenance request.
* **Has it been agreed?** (Select One, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the options to indicate if it has been agreed.
* **Date agreed?** (Date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date it was agreed.
* **Time agreed?** (Time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time it was agreed.
* **Followup?** (Select One, required: false)
	+ Choose one of the options to indicate if followup is required.
* **Followup date?** (Date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the followup.
* **Followup time?** (Time, required: false)
	+ Enter the time of the followup.
* **What is the email of the person submitting the request?** (Email, required: false)
	+ Enter the email of the person submitting the request.
* **Followup comments?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter any followup comments.
* **Agreed comments?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter any agreed comments.
* **Facilities address?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facilities company?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter the company of the facility.
* **Facilities notes?** (Text, required: false)
	+ Enter any notes about the facility.

Note: Please be careful when completing the form, as some fields may not be required, but are still important for accurate reporting.
