# Building Modification Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details for modifications to a building, including project start and end dates, property address, and various aspects of the project, such as structural and electrical work, permitting, and compliance with building codes. By completing this form, you will ensure that all relevant information is accurately recorded and that the necessary permits, approvals, and safety measures are in place.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Project Date" field and enter the date on which modification work begins.
2. Enter the "Property Address" field with the actual address of the building being modified.
3. Provide a detailed description of the modification in the "Description of Modification" field.
4. Enter the "Building Permit Number" field with the official reference number for the permit.
5. Indicate whether the permit has been issued by selecting "True", "False", or "In Progress" in the "Permit Obtained" field.
6. Enter the name of the general contractor in the "Contractor Name" field.
7. Enter the contractor's license number in the "Contractor License Number" field (if required).
8. In the "Scope of Work" field, provide a detailed description of the work to be done.
9. Indicate whether structural changes are involved by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Structural Changes Involved" field.
10. Select whether electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work is required by choosing "True" or "False" in the corresponding fields.
11. Confirm that the code compliance review has been completed by selecting "True", "False", or "In Progress" in the "Code Compliance Review Completed" field.
12. Indicate whether building inspector approval has been obtained by selecting "Approved", "Pending", "Denied", or "Not Required" in the "Building Inspector Approval" field.
13. Select any required safety certifications (such as Fire Safety, Electrical Safety, etc.) in the "Safety Certifications Required" field (if required).
14. Confirm that the contractor's liability insurance has been verified by selecting "True", "False", or "In Progress" in the "Contractor Insurance Verified" field.
15. Indicate whether an asbestos or hazardous material survey has been completed by selecting "True", "False", "Not Applicable", or "In Progress" in the "Asbestos or Hazmat Survey Completed" field.
16. Confirm that a site safety plan is in place by selecting "True", "False", or "In Progress" in the "Site Safety Plan in Place" field.
17. List any required documents submitted, such as blueprints, specifications, or insurance certificates, in the "Required Documents Submitted" field.
18. Enter the expected project start and completion dates in the "Expected Project Timeline" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Date** (1, date, required): Enter the date on which modification work begins.
* **Property Address** (2, text, required): Enter the actual address of the building being modified.
* **Description of Modification** (3, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the modification.
* **Building Permit Number** (4, text, required): Enter the official reference number for the permit.
* **Permit Obtained** (5, select_one, required): Indicate whether the permit has been issued (True), not issued (False), or is still in progress (In Progress).
* **Contractor Name** (6, text, required): Enter the general contractor's name.
* **Contractor License Number** (7, text, required): Enter the contractor's license number (if required).
* **Scope of Work** (8, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the work to be done.
* **Structural Changes Involved** (9, select_one, required): Select whether structural changes are involved (True) or not (False).
* **Electrical Work Required** (10, select_one, required): Select whether electrical work is required (True) or not (False).
* **Plumbing Work Required** (11, select_one, required): Select whether plumbing work is required (True) or not (False).
* **HVAC Modifications Required** (12, select_one, required): Select whether HVAC work is required (True) or not (False).
* **Code Compliance Review Completed** (13, select_one, required): Confirm that the code compliance review has been completed (True), not completed (False), or is still in progress (In Progress).
* **Building Inspector Approval** (14, select_one, required): Indicate whether building inspector approval has been obtained (Approved), is pending (Pending), denied (Denied), or is not required (Not Required).
* **Safety Certifications Required** (15, select_multiple, required): Select any required safety certifications (Fire Safety, Electrical Safety, etc.).
* **Contractor Insurance Verified** (16, select_one, required): Confirm that the contractor's liability insurance has been verified (True), not verified (False), or is still in progress (In Progress).
* **Asbestos or Hazmat Survey Completed** (17, select_one, required): Indicate whether an asbestos or hazardous material survey has been completed (True), not completed (False), or is not applicable (Not Applicable), or still in progress (In Progress).
* **Site Safety Plan in Place** (18, select_one, required): Confirm that a site safety plan is in place (True), not in place (False), or is still in progress (In Progress).
* **Required Documents Submitted** (19, select_multiple, required): List any required documents, such as blueprints, specifications, or insurance certificates.
* **Expected Project Timeline** (20, text, required): Enter the expected project start and completion dates (if required).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be accurate and clear when providing details about the modification work.
* Review the form carefully to avoid missing any essential information.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant section of this help guide for clarification.
