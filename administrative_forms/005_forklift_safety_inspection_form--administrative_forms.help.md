# Forklift Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forklift Safety Inspection Form is a tool used by supervisors and safety managers to monitor and evaluate the safety of forklifts. It ensures that all forklifts are properly inspected and maintained to prevent accidents and injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Forklift Safety Inspection Form, follow these steps:

- **Select the inspection type**: Choose the correct forklift safety inspection type from the dropdown menu: Forklift Safety Inspection, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 1, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 2, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 3, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 4, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 5, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 6, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 7, Forklift Safety Inspection Part 8.

- **Enter inspection details**: Enter the forklift model number and other inspection details in the designated fields.

- **Date and time**: Enter the date and time of the inspection.

- **Forklift License Number**: Enter the forklift's license number (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Forklift Safety Inspection
* **Forklift Safety Inspection** (`forklift_safety_inspection`, select_one, required/false): Select the inspection type. This field determines the scope of the inspection.
### Forklift Model
* **Forklift Model** (`forklift_model`, text, required/false): Enter the forklift model number. This is essential for tracking and identifying the specific forklift being inspected.
### Inspection Date
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/false): Enter the date of the inspection. This ensures that inspections are conducted at a specific point in time.
### Inspection Time
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required/false): Enter the time of the inspection. This helps in tracking the duration and timing of the inspection.
### Forklift License Number
* **Forklift License Number** (`forklift_license_number`, text, required/false): Enter the forklift's license number. This is an optional field for further identification purposes.

## Tips
* Always complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* Ensure that inspections are conducted regularly and according to the selected inspection type.
* Store the form results for future reference and record-keeping purposes.
