# Information Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and authorization for the release of sensitive data related to employees or applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the type of information to be released from the list of options provided.
3. Specify the purpose of the release.
4. Identify the authorized party.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the release of information.
6. Provide your employee ID number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Types of Information**: Select the type of information to be released from the list of options provided. Please select only one option that best describes the type of information to be released.
* **Purpose of Release**: Describe the reason for the release of information.
* **Authorized Party**: Identify the individual or entity authorized to receive the released information. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the release of information.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number (if applicable).
