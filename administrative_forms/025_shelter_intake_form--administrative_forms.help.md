# Shelter Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelter Intake Form is a tool used to collect information from individuals seeking shelter services. It is used to gather demographic details and contact information, as well as information about the reason for seeking shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have a legitimate reason for seeking shelter and that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
*   If you are unable to complete the form online, please contact the shelter for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's first name. Please enter your first name in this field.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's last name. Please enter your last name in this field.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's email address. Please enter a valid email address in this field.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's phone number. Please enter your phone number in this field.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in this format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the individual's address. Please enter your address in this field.
*   **Shelter Type** (`shelter_type`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select one or more types of shelter you are seeking. Please select all applicable options that apply to you.
*   **Reason for Intake** (`reason_for_intake`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your reason for seeking shelter. Please enter a brief explanation in this field.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of your emergency contact. Please enter the name of your emergency contact in this field.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact. Please enter your emergency contact's phone number in this field.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the shelter for assistance.
*   This form is not meant to be used for any other purpose other than what is described in this guide.
*   The information collected from this form will only be used for providing shelter services to you.
