# Background Check Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document the results of individual background checks for candidates. It ensures that all verification steps are properly recorded and that discrepancies found during the process are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of verification performed from the options provided.
2.  Enter the name of the organization/entity contacted.
3.  Enter the name of the contact person at the organization/entity contacted.
4.  Choose the method of verification used.
5.  Record the result of the verification.
6.  Enter any detailed findings and comments about the verification process.
7.  Enter the name of the admin/staff member who performed the verification.
8.  Enter the date the verification was completed.
9.  If further follow-up is required, select 'Yes'.
10.  Enter the internal file reference number for the background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate whose background was verified.
    * This field is required to ensure that the background check is associated with the correct candidate.
- **Type of Verification Performed**: Select the type of verification that was performed, such as Employment History, Educational Degrees/Certifications, Professional Reference, Professional License, or Personal Reference.
    * This field helps to track the type of verification performed for the candidate.
- **Organization/Entity Contacted**: Enter the name of the organization or entity that was contacted during the verification process.
    * This field is used to identify the organization or entity that was involved in the verification process.
- **Name of Contact Person at Entity**: Enter the name of the contact person at the organization/entity contacted.
    * This field is used to document the name of the contact person who was involved in the verification process.
- **Method of Verification**: Choose the method of verification used, such as Phone Call, Email, Online Database, or Written Correspondence.
    * This field helps to track the method used to verify the candidate's background.
- **Verification Result**: Select the result of the verification process, such as Verified (Matches Candidate Statement), Discrepancy Found (Minor), Discrepancy Found (Significant), Unable to Verify, or Adverse Information Discovered.
    * This field documents the outcome of the verification process.
- **Detailed Findings and Comments**: Enter any additional comments or findings related to the verification process.
    * This field provides a space for any additional information that may be relevant to the verification process.
- **Verified By (Admin/Staff Name)**: Enter the name of the admin/staff member who performed the verification.
    * This field documents the name of the person who performed the verification.
- **Date Verification Completed**: Enter the date the verification was completed.
    * This field tracks the date the verification process was completed.
- **Is further follow-up required?**: Select 'Yes' if further follow-up is required for the background check.
    * This field indicates whether further follow-up is required for the background check.
- **Internal File Reference Number**: Enter the internal file reference number for the background check.
    * This field provides a unique identifier for the background check.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* Double-check the results of the verification process to ensure that they match the candidate's statement.
* If discrepancies are found, document them properly in the "Detailed Findings and Comments" field.
* If further follow-up is required, select 'Yes' in the "Is further follow-up required?" field.
* Use the internal file reference number to easily track and reference the background check.
