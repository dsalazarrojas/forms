# Disability Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disability Accommodation Request Form is used to gather information and provide a formal request for employees who require reasonable accommodations due to a disability or medical condition. This form is used to ensure that all necessary information is collected and processed efficiently, while maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of the employee's request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name and ID in the respective fields.
2. Select the Department related to your accommodation request.
3. Describe the type and details of your accommodation request.
4. Specify whether the accommodation you are requesting is still active or inactive.
5. Enter the date you made your accommodation request.
6. Enter your contact information so that we can get back to you.
7. Please provide any additional information or comments about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Your full name as an employee.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee ID number.
* **Department**: The department you belong to.
* **Accommodation Request**: A detailed description of the accommodation you are requesting, including any necessary medical or supporting documentation.
* **Accommodation Type**: Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting an accommodation.
* **Accommodation Description**: Elaborate on the accommodation you are requesting, including any relevant medical documentation.
* **Accommodation Status**: Select 'Active' if the accommodation is currently in place, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
* **Accommodation Request Date**: The date you made your accommodation request.
* **Accommodation Request Deadline**: The deadline for the accommodation.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address, which we will use to contact you about your request.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number, which we will use to contact you about your request.
* **Requestor Information**: Please provide any additional information that may be relevant to your accommodation request.
* **Request Form Submitted By**: This is a system-generated field and should not be filled out by the user.
* **Submit Date**: The date and time you submitted this form.
* **Submit Time**: The time you submitted this form.
* **Submitter**: The name of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Email**: The email address of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Department**: The department of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Position**: The position of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Title**: The title of the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Notes**: Any additional comments or information from the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Comments**: Any additional comments or feedback from the person who submitted the form on your behalf.
* **Submitter Agreed to Terms**: If the person who submitted the form on your behalf has agreed to the terms and conditions of the accommodation request process.
* **Submitter Declined to Terms**: If the person who submitted the form on your behalf has declined to agree to the terms and conditions of the accommodation request process.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will contact you with updates and information about your accommodation request.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out, and all fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
