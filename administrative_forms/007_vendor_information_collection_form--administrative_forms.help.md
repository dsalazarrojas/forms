# vendor_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect vendor information for administrative purposes. It helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date vendor records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Select the relevant contact information for the vendor from the form pages.
2. Fill out the fields with the required information, ensuring that each field is completed accurately.
3. Review and confirm the information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information**: Enter the contact details of the vendor, including their name and email.
  - **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person.
  - **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
  - **Street Address**: Enter the street address of the company.
  - **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code or zip code of the company.
- **Services Offered**: Indicate the services offered by the vendor.
  - **Yes/No**: Select all the services offered by the vendor.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person or a designated email for the company.
- **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person or the company.
- **Business Description**: Describe the nature of the vendor's business.
- **Business Description**: Enter a brief description of the company's activities.
- **Date**: Select the date of the most recent communication or interaction with the vendor.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- Review and confirm information before submission to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
