# Employee Timesheet Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Timesheet Checklist Form is used to track employee attendance and work hours for the current week.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Open the form to the correct page (page 1) and select the days of the week you worked, up to a maximum of 7 options.
   - You can choose multiple days of the week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_work_hours**: 
  (`employee_work_hours`, select_multiple, not required)
  Select the days of the week you worked during this week, one option per day up to 7 days.
* **total_employees**: 
  (`total_employees`, number, not required)
  Enter the total number of employees.
* **employee_attendance**: 
  (`employee_attendance`, select_one, not required)
  Select one of the following options: Absent, Late, Present, or On leave.
* **employee_overtime**: 
  (`employee_overtime`, select_one, not required)
  Select one of the following options: Less than 2 hours, Between 2-10 hours, More than 10 hours.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately, as it will be used for tracking purposes.
* Only fill out the form for the current week.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
