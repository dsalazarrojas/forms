# Jury Duty Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about a juror's check-in status for administrative purposes within the judiciary system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and other required fields.
2. Confirm your attendance and provide additional information if necessary.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Juror Name** (`juror_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
- **Juror Email** (`juror_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as associated with your juror account.
- **Juror Phone** (`juror_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, required): Enter the time you checked in.
- **Juror Attendance** (`juror_attendance`, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **Juror Message** (`juror_message`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or messages if necessary.
- **Additional Info** (`juror_additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your check-in status.
- **Docket Number** (`juror_docket_number`, text, required): Enter the docket number associated with your case.
- **Juror Status** (`juror_status`, select_one, required): Select your current status as "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Juror Status Note** (`juror_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments about your status.
- **Juror Signature** (`juror_signature`, text, optional): Provide your signature if required or necessary.
- **Date Submitted** (`juror_date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
- **Check In By** (`juror_check_in_by`, select_one, required): Confirm who checked you in (e.g., "Yes" for yes, or "No" for no).
- **Check In By Signature** (`juror_check_in_by_signature`, text, optional): Provide the signature of the person who checked you in.
- **Check In By Date** (`juror_check_in_by_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the person who checked you in.
- **Check In By Time** (`juror_check_in_by_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the person who checked you in.
- **Check In By Location** (`juror_check_in_by_location`, select_one, required): Confirm where you were checked in (e.g., "Yes" for yes, or "No" for no).
- **Check In By Additional Info** (`juror_check_in_by_additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about who checked you in.
- **Check In By Status Note** (`juror_check_in_by_status_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments about who checked you in.
- **Check In By Signature 2** (`juror_check_in_by_signature_2`, text, optional): Provide the signature of the person who checked you in (if necessary).
- **Check In By Date 2** (`juror_check_in_by_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date the person who checked you in (if necessary).
- **Check In By Time 2** (`juror_check_in_by_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the time the person who checked you in (if necessary).
- **Check In By Location 2** (`juror_check_in_by_location_2`, select_one, required): Confirm where you were checked in (e.g., "Yes" for yes, or "No" for no).
- **Check In By Additional Info 2** (`juror_check_in_by_additional_info_2`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about who checked you in.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information and signatures before completing the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or personnel for assistance.
