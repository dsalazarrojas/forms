# Document Archiving Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Document Archiving Checklist is a form used to collect information about documents that are being archived for storage, retrieval, and maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out this form for a document that is being archived.
2. Enter the Storage Location of the document in the field labeled "Storage Location".
3. Select the Document Type from the options provided in the field labeled "Document Type".
4. Enter the Archive Date and Archive Time of the document in the "Archive Date" and "Archive Time" fields, respectively.
5. If applicable, enter the Storage Duration and Retention Period for the document.
6. Select the Archiving Status of the document from the options provided in the "Archiving Status" field.
7. Add any additional Notes regarding the document if necessary.
8. Enter the Email and Phone number (if applicable) for contact purposes.
9. Enter the Document Number and Document Description for reference.
10. Enter the Document Version if necessary.
11. Select the Document Status from the options provided in the "Document Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Storage Location** (Select One): Choose the storage location where the document will be kept.
* **Document Type** (Select Multiple): Select the type of document being archived.
* **Archive Date** (Date): Enter the date the document was created or last updated.
* **Archive Time** (Time): Enter the time the document was created or last updated.
* **Storage Duration** (Number): Enter the duration for which the document will be stored.
* **Retention Period** (Number): Enter the period for which the document will be maintained.
* **Archiving Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the document during archiving.
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional notes or comments regarding the document.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the contact email for the document owner or responsible party.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number for the document owner or responsible party.
* **Document Number** (Number): Enter the unique document number.
* **Document Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the document.
* **Document Version** (Number): Enter the version number of the document.
* **Document Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the document after archiving.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use the available options in the "Document Type" and "Archiving Status" fields to ensure accuracy.
* Use the available options in the "Document Status" field to choose the correct status.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper archiving and retrieval of the document.
