# inventory_storage_equipment_requisition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The inventory_storage_equipment_requisition_form is used to submit a request for the acquisition of new storage equipment for the facility. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to submit this form.
2. Select the type of equipment needed (step 2).
3. Specify the employee responsible for requisitioning the equipment.
4. Choose the storage location where the equipment will be stored.
5. Enter the quantity of equipment required.
6. Verify that all fields are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_id** (number): Enter the unique identifier of the storage equipment being requested. If you are unsure, please contact the equipment manager.
* **type** (select one): Select the type of equipment being requested from the list of options. This will help us understand what kind of equipment you need.
* **requested_by** (select one): Choose the employee who is responsible for requesting this equipment. This will help us track who submitted the request.
* **location** (select multiple): Choose the storage location where the equipment will be stored. Select multiple locations if the equipment is needed for multiple locations.
* **quantity** (number): Enter the number of units of the equipment being requested.
