# Hair Stylist Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Stylist Contract Form is designed to collect essential information from both the client and the stylist during the onboarding process. This form helps in maintaining accurate records, ensuring effective communication, and service agreements with employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information as a client.
2. Enter your stylist's name and contact information.
3. Specify the services you would like to offer as a stylist.
4. Choose a start date and end date for your contract.
5. Provide details on payment terms, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, Optional)
Enter your name as a client.

### **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, Optional)
Enter any contact information you have, such as phone number, email, or address.

### **Stylist Name** (`stylist_name`, text, Optional)
Enter the name of your chosen stylist.

### **Stylist Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, Optional)
Enter the contact information for your selected stylist.

### **Services** (`services`, text, Optional)
Specify the services you would like to offer as a stylist, such as hair cutting, color, or styling.

### **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, Optional)
Enter the start date of your contract with the client. If you're unsure, leave it blank for now.

### **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, Optional)
Enter the end date of your contract with the client. If you're unsure, leave it blank for now.

### **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, Optional)
Enter any specific payment terms or conditions, if applicable. This could include details on rates, payment methods, or due dates.

## Tips
- Please ensure you provide all requested information for a smooth onboarding process.
- Enter any additional comments or notes in the `Services` field.
- Review your completed form before submission.
