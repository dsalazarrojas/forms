# Family Vacation Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Vacation Leave Form is used to request and track vacation leave for employees. This form is used to submit a leave request, which can be reviewed and approved by managers and the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Requesting A Leave**: On the first page, select the start date and end date of your desired leave period. Please enter the correct start and end dates for your vacation.
2. **Enter Details**: On the second page, enter the duration of your leave in days and provide a reason for your leave.
3. **Approval Status**: On the third page, select whether your manager and company have approved your leave. You can also include any comments from your manager or company.
4. **Submit Form**: Once you have completed all the fields, click "Submit" to submit your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Requesting A Leave
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your leave to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like your leave to end.
* **Duration Days**: Enter the number of days you would like to take for your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: Enter a brief description of your reason for taking leave.

### Approval Status
* **Has Manager Approved**: Select "Yes" if your manager has approved your leave, "No" otherwise.
* **Has Company Approved**: Select "Yes" if the company has approved your leave, "No" otherwise.
* **Manager Comments**: If your manager has added comments to your leave request, they will be displayed here.
* **Company Comments**: If the company has added comments to your leave request, they will be displayed here.
* **Employee Comments**: Add any comments you want to share with your manager or the company.
* **Has Employee Approved**: Select "Yes" if you have approved your own leave request, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate start and end dates for your leave.
* Ensure that you have manager and company approval before submitting your leave request.
* Review your leave request carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your manager or HR representative.
