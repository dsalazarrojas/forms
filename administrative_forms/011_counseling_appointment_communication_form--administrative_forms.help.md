# counseling_appointment_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The counseling_appointment_communication_form is used for communication between the client and the counselor regarding counseling appointments. It ensures accurate and clear information exchange.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide session-related information in the **session_details** field.
2. Input the **appointment_date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Select the **appointment_time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
4. Enter your **client_name** in the text field.
5. Provide your **client_email** in the format `email@example.com`.
6. Optional: Input your **client_phone** number.
7. Add any additional **notes** for the counselor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **session_details**: Provide session-related information to help the counselor understand the purpose and nature of the session.
* **appointment_date**: Input the date of the appointment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **appointment_time**: Select the appointment time in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **client_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **client_email**: Input your email address in the format `email@example.com`.
* **client_phone**: Optional: Input your phone number.
* **notes**: Add any additional information for the counselor to note during the session.
* **submit**: Click to submit the form and send the information to the counselor.
