# employment_continuation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "employment_continuation_request_form" is used to request employment continuation, which is a process that involves ongoing employment status. This form is likely used by employees to provide essential information for their employment continuation, such as employment details, dates, and reasons for leaving or requesting termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your employment start date and department.
2. Select the reason for leaving, which may be due to relocation, career change, retirement, or other reasons.
3. Provide your contact information and manager's contact information.
4. Select the reason for requesting termination, which may be due to unemployment, redundancy, or other reasons.
5. Sign and date the form, both as the employee and the manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`): Enter your personal details, such as your name and employment start date.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department where you work, such as HR, Sales, Marketing, or IT. This field is required.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter your working location, which is not required but can provide useful context.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your current job title, which is not required but can provide essential information.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`): Select the reason for leaving, which may be due to relocation, career change, retirement, or other reasons. This field is required.
* **Reason for Requesting Termination** (`reason_for_requesting_termination`): Select the reason for requesting termination, which may be due to unemployment, redundancy, or other reasons. This field is required.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`): Provide any supporting documents that may be necessary for your employment continuation, such as references or certificates.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign and date the form as the employee.
* **Manager Details**:
	+ **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name.
	+ **Manager Contact Number** (`manager_contact_number`): Enter your manager's contact number.
	+ **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email address.
	+ **Manager Department** (`manager_department`): Select your manager's department.
	+ **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Date of Last Evaluation** (`date_of_last_evaluation`): Enter the date of your last evaluation, which is not required but can provide useful context.
* **Supporting Documents for Manager** (`supporting_documents`): Provide any supporting documents that may be necessary for your manager's verification, such as references or certificates.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department or manager for assistance.
