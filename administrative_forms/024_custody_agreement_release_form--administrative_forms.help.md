# Custody Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custody Agreement Release Form is used to document the release of a custody agreement between a parent or guardian and a custodian, typically a family member or caregiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the custodian's name in the "Custodian Name" field.
2. Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
3. Select the current custodianship status of the child in the "Custodianship Status" field.
4. Enter the date of the release in the "Release Date" field.
5. Provide a reason for the release in the "Reason for Release" field.
6. Enter any additional details of the agreement in the "Agreement Details" field.
7. Select the type of sign-off required for the release in the "Sign off" field.
8. Enter the custodian's signature in the "Custodian Signature" field.
9. Enter the witness' name in the "Witness Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Custodian Name
* **Custodian Name**: Enter the name of the person releasing the custodianship.
*   *Type: text*

### Child Name
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child being released from the custody agreement.
*   *Type: text*

### Custodianship Status
* **Custodianship Status**: Select the current status of the child's custodianship.
*   *Type: select_multiple*
*   *Options: active, inactive*

### Release Date
* **Release Date**: Enter the date the custody agreement is being released.
*   *Type: date*

### Reason for Release
* **Reason for Release**: Provide a reason for releasing the custodianship.
*   *Type: text*

### Agreement Details
* **Agreement Details**: Enter any additional details of the agreement.
*   *Type: text*

### Sign off
* **Sign off**: Select the type of sign-off required for the release.
*   *Type: select_one*
*   *Options: Parent/Parent, Guardian/Parent, Adult/Adult*

### Custodian Signature
* **Custodian Signature**: Enter the custodian's signature.
*   *Type: text*

### Witness Name
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness who witnessed the signing.
*   *Type: text*

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* Ensure the custodian's signature is legible and complete.
* Have the witness sign off in the "Sign off" field as required.
* Keep a record of the custody agreement for future reference.
