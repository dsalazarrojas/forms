# Finishing Tips And Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect finishing tips and checklist preferences from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form on the first page with your basic contact information (First Page and Second Page fields).
2. Select your preferred finishing tips from the options provided (Finishing Tips field).
3. Choose a single option from the checklist (Checklist field).
4. Enter a specific date (Date field).
5. Choose a time (Time field).
6. Enter your email address (Email field).
7. Enter your phone number (Phone field).
8. Add any additional comments or information (Notes field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (First Page field): 
    Please enter your first name.
* **Second Page** (Second Page field): 
    Please enter your last name.
* **Finishing Tips** (Finishing Tips field): 
    Please select all the finishing tips that you prefer.
* **Checklist** (Checklist field): 
    Please choose a single item from the options provided.
* **Date** (Date field): 
    Enter a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (Time field): 
    Choose a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (Email field): 
    Enter your email address in the format [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com).
* **Phone** (Phone field): 
    Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
* **Notes** (Notes field): 
    Add any additional comments or information that you would like to provide.
