# Noise Disturbance Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about noise disturbances you have experienced or are experiencing, such as excessive noise, construction noise, or party noise. This form will help our team investigate and address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your street address where the noise disturbance occurred or is occurring.
2. Select the type of noise that is causing the disturbance (e.g., Excessive Noise, Construction Noise, Party Noise, or Other).
3. Enter the start date and start time of the noise disturbance.
4. Enter the end date and end time of the noise disturbance.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments about the noise disturbance.
6. Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
7. Enter an email address where you can be reached.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Street Address**: Please enter the street address where the noise disturbance occurred or is occurring.
  - **Type**: The type of noise that is causing the disturbance (e.g., Excessive Noise, Construction Noise, Party Noise, or Other).
  - **Noise Level**: Enter a number to indicate the level of noise disturbance (e.g., 1-10).
  - **Start Date**: Enter the date when the noise disturbance started.
  - **Start Time**: Enter the time when the noise disturbance started.
  - **End Date**: Enter the date when the noise disturbance ended.
  - **End Time**: Enter the time when the noise disturbance ended.
  - **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or information about the noise disturbance.
  - **Contact Phone**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you to follow up on your complaint.
  - **Contact Email**: Enter an email address where we can reach you to follow up on your complaint.
  - **Submit**: Click this button to submit your complaint.
