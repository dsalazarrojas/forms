# visa-application-assistance-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Visa Application Assistance Request Form" is designed to gather information from users who need help with their visa application process. This form helps the support team understand your concerns and provide accurate assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you to discuss your visa application.
2. Select the correct category for your visa application.
3. Specify which country you submitted your visa application from.
4. State the reason for your visa application.
5. Optionally, provide your passport expiration date, expiration year, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Info
* **Applicant Info (id: 1)**: Please enter your full name as per your passport. This is to ensure that we contact you accurately.

* **Category (id: 2)**: Select "visa" if you are seeking assistance with a visa application or "other" if you are seeking help with an alternative purpose (e.g., visa renewal).

* **Visa Submitted From (id: 3)**: Select the country where you submitted your visa application. If it's not listed, please contact the support team for guidance.

* **Visa Reason (id: 4)**: Explain the reason for your visa application. This will help the support team understand your concern.

* **Email (id: 5)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you to discuss your visa application.

* **Phone Number (id: 6)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you to discuss your visa application.

* **Passport Expiration Date (id: 7)**: If your passport is due to expire soon, please provide the date to help us assess the urgency of your request.

* **Passport Expiration Month (id: 8)**: Enter the month of your passport expiration date. This will help us ensure that your passport is valid for travel.

* **Passport Expiration Year (id: 9)**: Enter the year of your passport expiration date. This will help us assess the validity of your passport.

* **Address (id: 10)**: If you are requesting help with a physical address, please provide it here.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the support team for guidance.
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form to ensure that we can provide the best assistance possible.
* If you have any additional documentation or information that may aid in your application, please attach them to your request.
