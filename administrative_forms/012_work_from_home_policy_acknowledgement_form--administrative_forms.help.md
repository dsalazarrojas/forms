# Work From Home Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to acknowledge that you are aware of and understand the remote work policy implemented by our organization. It is meant to ensure that all employees who work from home (WFH) are aware of their responsibilities, benefits, and expectations while working remotely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. You can find the answers to the questions in your employee handbook or by asking your supervisor for clarification if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Acknowledgement of Remote Work Policy** (`work_from_home_policy_acknowledgement_form`, type: note, required: false):
	+ This is a note field where you can acknowledge that you have read and understand the remote work policy. Please type in a brief statement acknowledging your understanding of the policy.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, type: text, required: false):
	+ Enter your full employee name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Department** (`department`, type: text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of your department as it is listed in the company's organizational structure.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, type: text, required: false):
	+ Enter your current job title as it is listed on your personnel record.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your employee handbook for the details of the remote work policy before completing this form.
- If you have questions or concerns about the remote work policy, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Please return the completed form to your supervisor or HR representative to confirm your understanding of the remote work policy.
