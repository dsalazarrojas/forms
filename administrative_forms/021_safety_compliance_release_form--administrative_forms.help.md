# Safety Compliance Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document an employee's release from safety compliance obligations. It captures essential information to ensure a smooth transition process, maintain accurate records, and facilitate future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Employee Information**: Enter the name, ID number, and department/unit where the employee works.
2. **Safety Compliance Information**: Provide details about the employee's safety compliance status, such as any outstanding tasks or procedures they need to follow.
3. **Employee Consent**: Confirm that the employee is aware of and consents to the release.
4. **Safety Procedures**: Document the procedures that have been followed or completed.
5. **Review and Approval**: Record the dates and details of any reviews or approvals related to this release.
6. **Signature**: The employee signs to acknowledge their release from safety compliance obligations.
7. **Completion and Review Dates**: Enter the date the form was completed and the date it was reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (employee_info)**: This field is for the employee's name, ID number, and department/unit where they work.
* **Safety Compliance Information (safety_compliance_info)**: Provide details about the employee's current safety compliance status, such as any outstanding tasks or procedures they need to follow.
* **Employee Consent (employee_consent)**: Confirm that the employee is aware of and consents to the release from safety compliance obligations.
* **Safety Procedures (safety_procedures)**: Document the procedures that the employee has followed or completed.
* **Review and Approval (review_and_approval)**: Record the dates and details of any reviews or approvals related to this release.
* **Signature (signature)**: The employee's signature acknowledges their release from safety compliance obligations.
* **Completion Date (date_completed)**: Enter the date this form was completed.
* **Review Date (date_reviewed)**: Enter the date this form was reviewed.
