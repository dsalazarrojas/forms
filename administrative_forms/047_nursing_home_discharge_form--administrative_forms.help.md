The "Nursing Home Discharge Form" is intended to document the reason for a resident's discharge from a nursing home and collect information about their transfer to a new facility. This form is typically completed by the nursing home staff and signed by the resident and facility administrators to ensure that all necessary information is accurately documented and that the transfer process is properly executed. To ensure accurate and thorough completion of the form, please review the following instructions and explanations of each field.

# Nursing Home Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the reason for a resident's discharge from a nursing home and collect information about their transfer to a new facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the reason for the discharge from the "Discharge Reason" field and enter the corresponding date.
2. Select the facility where the resident will be transferred and enter their current date of birth.
3. Select the status of the discharge from the "Discharge Status" field.
4. Enter the contact phone number of the resident or their representative.
5. Enter the date and time of the transfer.
6. Have the resident and facility administrators sign the form to confirm the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Discharge Reason (1)** (`text`, `required`): This field is used to document the reason for the resident's discharge. Please select the most accurate reason from the provided options.
* **Date Of Discharge (2)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date of the resident's discharge.
* **Resident Information (3)** (`text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter any additional information about the resident that may be relevant to their transfer.
* **Nursing Home (4)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the name of the nursing home where the resident is currently residing.
* **Date Of Birth (5)** (`date`, `optional`): Enter the resident's date of birth.
* **Discharge Status (6)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select the status of the resident's discharge from the provided options.
* **Contact Phone (7)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter the contact phone number of the resident or their representative.
* **Date Of Transfer (8)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date of the resident's transfer.
* **Resident Signature (9)** (`note`, `optional`): Have the resident sign this field to confirm their understanding of the transfer.
* **Facility Signature (10)** (`note`, `optional`): Have the facility administrators sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.
* **Facility License Number (11)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the facility license number.
* **Facility Name (12)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the name of the facility where the resident will be transferred.
* **Facility Address (13)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the address of the facility where the resident will be transferred.
* **Facility City (14)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the city of the facility where the resident will be transferred.
* **Facility State (15)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the state of the facility where the resident will be transferred.
* **Facility Zip (16)** (`text`, `required`): Enter the zip code of the facility where the resident will be transferred.
* **Discharge Note (17)** (`text`, `optional`): If applicable, enter any additional information about the discharge that may be relevant.
* **Discharge Date (18)** (`date`, `required`): Enter the date of the resident's discharge.
* **Discharge Time (19)** (`time`, `required`): Enter the time of the resident's discharge.
* **Discharge Signature (20)** (`note`, `required`): Have the resident sign this field to confirm their understanding of the transfer.
* **Facility Signature 1 (21)** (`note`, `required`): Have the first facility administrator sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.
* **Facility Signature 2 (22)** (`note`, `required`): Have the second facility administrator sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.
* **Facility Signature 3 (23)** (`note`, `required`): Have the third facility administrator sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.
* **Facility Signature 4 (24)** (`note`, `required`): Have the fourth facility administrator sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.
* **Facility Signature 5 (25)** (`note`, `required`): Have the fifth facility administrator sign this field to confirm their agreement with the transfer.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are accurately completed before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* Have the resident and facility administrators sign the relevant fields to confirm their understanding and agreement with the transfer.

Note: The provided instructions assume that the form will be completed in a paper-based or electronic format. If the form is to be completed in a different format, please adjust the instructions accordingly.
