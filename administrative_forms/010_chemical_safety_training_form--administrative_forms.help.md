# Chemical Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chemical Safety Training Form is designed to track and record employee completion of required chemical safety training and assessment. This form helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of employee knowledge and skills on chemical safety procedures and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have completed all required training sessions and assessment.
2. Fill out your personal details such as First name, Last name, Employee ID, and Department.
3. Provide information about the Training date, Training location or method, and Trainer's name.
4. Answer questions related to the duration of the training, topics covered, and your ability to understand and apply different aspects of chemical safety procedures (e.g., Safety Data Sheets, Personal Protective Equipment, Spill response procedures, etc.).
5. Confirm that you can access and interpret Safety Data Sheets, understand required PPE, respond safely to chemical spills, know emergency procedures, and acknowledge that you have completed the training and understand the material.
6. Sign and date the form to acknowledge completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. **First name** (`employee_first_name`, Text, Required)
Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.

### 3. **Last name** (`employee_last_name`, Text, Required)
Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.

### 4. **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Text, Required)
Enter your unique employee ID assigned by your organization.

### 5. **Department** (`department`, Text, Required)
Enter your department within the organization.

### 6. **Training date** (`training_date`, Date, Required)
Enter the date you completed the training.

### 7. **Training location or method** (`training_location`, Select One, Required)
Select the method through which you received the training: In-person training, Online course, Virtual webinar, Refresher training, or Other.

### 8. **Trainer name** (`trainer_name`, Text, Required)
Enter the name of the trainer who delivered the training.

### 9. **Training duration** (`training_duration`, Number, Required)
Enter the duration of the training in hours.

### 10. **Topics covered in training** (`topics_covered`, Select Multiple, Required)
Select all the topics that were covered in the training from the list provided.

### 11. **Employee can access and interpret Safety Data Sheets** (`sds_knowledge`, Select One, Required)
Confirm your ability to access and interpret Safety Data Sheets.

### 12. **Employee understands required PPE** (`ppe_knowledge`, Select One, Required)
Confirm your understanding of required Personal Protective Equipment.

### 13. **Employee can respond to chemical spills safely** (`spill_response`, Select One, Required)
Confirm your ability to respond to chemical spills safely.

### 14. **Employee knows emergency procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, Select One, Required)
Confirm your knowledge of emergency procedures.

### 15. **Did employee pass the assessment?** (`assessment_passed`, Select One, Required)
Indicate whether you passed the assessment.

### 16. **Assessment score** (`assessment_score`, Number, Not Required)
Enter your assessment score if applicable.

### 17. **Certification expiration date** (`certification_expiration`, Date, Required)
Enter the date when your certification expires.

### 18. **Next refresher training due date** (`refresher_due_date`, Date, Required)
Enter the date when your next refresher training is due.

### 19. **Specific chemicals handled** (`chemical_specific_training`, Text, Not Required)
Enter specific chemicals you are trained on, if applicable.

### 20. **Known workplace hazards** (`workplace_hazards`, Text, Not Required)
List known workplace hazards, if any.

### 21. **Any questions or concerns?** (`questions_concerns`, Text, Not Required)
If you have any questions or concerns regarding chemical safety, enter them here.

### 22. **Training acknowledgment** (`training_acknowledgment`, Select One, Required)
Acknowledge that you have completed this training and understand the material.

### 23. **Training completion date** (`signature_date`, Date, Required)
Enter the date you completed this training.
