# teacher_behavior_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reporting incidents of unprofessional behavior, bullying, or harassment by teachers. It helps to maintain a record of such incidents and provides a structured format for addressing these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields: `teacher_name`, `student_name`, and at least one option from `teacher_behavior` and `resolution`.
2.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the `description` field.
3.  Select the relevant options from `teacher_behavior` and `resolution`.
4.  Ensure that you have filled in all required fields and reviewed the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **complainant** (`complainant`, `text`, required: false): This is the person filing the complaint, which could be a student, parent, or school staff. You can fill in your name and contact information if you want to be kept informed about the resolution of the issue.
*   **description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened and why it is a complaint.
*   **date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **teacher_name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the name of the teacher involved in the incident.
*   **teacher_subject** (`teacher_subject`, `text`, required: false): The subject taught by the teacher involved in the incident.
*   **teacher_contact** (`teacher_contact`, `text`, required: false): The contact information of the teacher involved in the incident.
*   **student_name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the name of the student involved in the incident.
*   **student_grade** (`student_grade`, `text`, required: false): The grade level of the student involved in the incident.
*   **description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Another description of the incident, possibly for context or additional information.
*   **teacher_behavior** (`teacher_behavior`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    +   **Unprofessional behavior**: Unacceptable behavior that shows a lack of professionalism in the teacher's conduct.
    +   **Bullying or harassment**: Behavior that is intended to or has the effect of threatening, intimidating, or causing a substantial emotional distress.
    +   **Rude or dismissive**: Treatment or communication that is impolite or discourteous.
    +   **Lack of communication**: Failure to maintain regular, open, and clear communication with students or parents.
*   **complaint** (`complaint`, `text`, required: false): Additional comments or concerns you have regarding the incident.
*   **resolution** (`resolution`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    +   **Meeting with teacher**: Meeting with the teacher to discuss and address the issue.
    +   **Meeting with school official**: Meeting with a school official, like the principal or director.
    +   **Written warning**: A written warning or formal notice to the teacher.
    +   **Other**: Other solutions or outcomes you prefer.

## Tips

*   Please be as detailed and specific as possible when describing the incident.
*   Ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the school administration for assistance.
