# Lease Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Agreement Discharge Form is used to document the end of a lease agreement between a tenant and a landlord. It's a formal record of the lease's termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `tenant_info` field with the tenant's details, including their name and contact information.
2. Enter the `lease_start_date` field with the start date of the lease agreement.
3. Enter the `lease_end_date` field with the end date of the lease agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tenant_info**: This field is for entering the tenant's name and contact information. Please fill out this field with the tenant's details, such as their name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **lease_start_date**: This field is for entering the start date of the lease agreement. Use the calendar tool to select the correct date.
* **lease_end_date**: This field is for entering the end date of the lease agreement. Use the calendar tool to select the correct date.
