# Event Staff Scheduling Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Staff Scheduling Booking Form is designed for event staff to schedule staff members for specific events. It gathers information about staff members, event details, and staff's confirmation of availability and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Staff Name" and "Staff Email" fields respectively.
2. Select the "Event Date" of the event you are booking for.
3. Select the "Event Type" of the event (Day, Night, or Weekend Event).
4. Select the "Event Details" of the event (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
5. Choose your availability for the event (Available, Unavailable).
6. Select your role for the event (Event Coordinator, Event Manager, or Event Assistant).
7. Confirm your availability for the event by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
8. Confirm your role for the event by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
9. Confirm your event type for the event by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
10. Confirm your break-in and break-out times (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional comments or notes if necessary.
12. Finally, confirm your shift by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the person or team that is organizing this event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date on which the event will take place.
* **Staff Name**: Enter your name as a staff member.
* **Staff Email**: Enter your email address for communication with event organizers.
* **Staff Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication with event organizers. 
* **Shift Duration**: Enter the duration of your shift in hours and minutes.
* **Shift Start**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Role**: Select the role you are taking on for this event (Event Coordinator, Event Manager, or Event Assistant).
* **Shift Status**: Select your current shift status (Available, Busy, or Not Available).
* **Availability**: Confirm your availability for this event (Available or Unavailable).
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are booking for (Day, Night, or Weekend Event).
* **Event Details**: Select the event details you are booking for (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Break In**: Select the time you will break in if applicable.
* **Break Out**: Select the time you will break out if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
* **Confirm Shift**: Confirm your shift by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Event Details**: Confirm your event details by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Staff Availability**: Confirm your availability for this event by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Event Type**: Confirm your event type by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Break**: Confirm your break-in and break-out times by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Role**: Confirm your role by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Shift Status**: Confirm your shift status by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
* **Confirm Availability**: Confirm your availability by selecting "Confirm" or "Decline".
