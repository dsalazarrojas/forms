# Visitor Badge Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Visitor Badge Check In Form is used to capture information about visitors arriving at your location. This form helps ensure that visitors are accurately recorded and can be easily located in case of an emergency. Please complete this form for each visitor.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for each field. A form cannot be submitted without completing all required fields.
2. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter the visitor's full name.
* **Date of Visit** (`Date of Visit`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the visitor's visit.
* **Check-in Status** (`Check-in Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the check-in status of the visitor. Options include:
	+ Not checked in yet
	+ Checked in
	+ Checked out
	+ No show
	+ Other
	+ Cancelled
	+ Rescheduled
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the visitor.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the visitor's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the visitor's phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): Enter an emergency contact's name and phone number (if applicable).
* **Emergency Phone** (`Emergency Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`Emergency Email`, `email`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the visitor.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately record the visitor's information for accurate attendance tracking.
* If the visitor's contact information changes, update their record accordingly.
* In case of an emergency, refer to the visitor's emergency contact information.
