# security_breach_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report security breaches or incidents within the organization. It captures essential information about the incident, including details about the incident itself, the reporter's contact information, and any relevant supporting documentation. This form helps ensure that incidents are reported accurately and consistently, facilitating prompt and effective action to mitigate potential risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Incident Details section with information about the incident, including the date, time, location, and type of incident.
2. Select the severity level of the incident from the options provided.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, email, and title.
4. Upload any relevant images or documents related to the incident.
5. Add any additional notes about the incident in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, optional): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Type of Incident** (`type_of_incident`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident (e.g., Unauthorized Access, Data Breach, Phishing, Malware, or Other).
* **Severity of Incident** (`severity_of_incident`, select_one, optional): Select the severity level of the incident (e.g., Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reporter of the incident.
* **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Image of Incident** (`image_of_incident`, text, optional): Upload any relevant images related to the incident.
* **Document of Incident** (`document_of_incident`, text, optional): Upload any relevant documents related to the incident.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Incident Reporter Phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Incident Reporter Email** (`incident_reporter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Incident Reporter Company** (`incident_reporter_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Incident Reporter Title** (`incident_reporter_title`, text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Incident Reporter Department** (`incident_reporter_department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Incident Reporter Job Title** (`incident_reporter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Incident Reporter Location** (`incident_reporter_location`, text, optional): Enter your location.
* **Incident Reporter Phone Extension** (`incident_reporter_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter your phone extension.
* **Incident Reporter Phone Number** (`incident_reporter_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as "required", which means they must be filled out in order to submit the form.
