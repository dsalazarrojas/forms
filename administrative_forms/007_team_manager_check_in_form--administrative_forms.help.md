# team_manager_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team manager check-in form is designed to facilitate team status updates and feedback during team check-ins. It helps team managers stay organized and ensure all team members are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all team members who need updates.
2. Specify the date and time of the update.
3. Provide a brief update note.
4. Check in team members by selecting their status (On Track, Behind, or Unknown).
5. Add any additional feedback or status note.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_update** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select all team members who need updates for this check-in.
* **update_date** (Date, Required: False): Specify the date of the update (e.g., today's date or a future date for a scheduled update).
* **update_time** (Time, Required: False): Specify the time of the update (e.g., morning or afternoon).
* **update_note** (Note, Required: False): Provide a brief note about the update (e.g., what was discussed or accomplished).
* **team_check_in** (Select One, Required: False): Check in team members by selecting their status (Team Lead, Team Member, or Team Admin).
* **check_in_note** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional feedback or status note.
* **feedback** (Text, Required: False): Provide any feedback or comments about the team's progress.
* **team_status** (Select One, Required: True): Select the team's status (On Track, Behind, or Unknown).
* **team_status_note** (Note, Required: False): Add any additional notes about the team's status.

## Tips
* Make sure to select all team members who need updates.
* Be sure to specify a date and time for the update, if applicable.
* Keep update notes brief and focused on the main points.
* Check in team members accurately to ensure everyone is on the same page.
* Use the feedback field for any additional comments or concerns.
