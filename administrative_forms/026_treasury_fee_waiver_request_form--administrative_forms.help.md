# Treasury Fee Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Fee Waiver Request Form is used to gather information for treasury fee waivers. Users can submit their requests to waive fees associated with services or transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your User Name and selecting your Department if applicable.
- In the Reason for Fee Waiver field, provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting a fee waiver.
- If applicable, enter the amount of the fee you are requesting a waiver for in the Amount field.
- Enter the date of your request in the Date of Request field.
- If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the Comments field.
- Finally, confirm who submitted the request in the Submitted By field and your title in the Submitted By Title field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID badge or as known to the financial team.
* **Department (select multiple)**: Select your department from the list to help the financial team process your request.
* **Reason for Fee Waiver (text)**: A brief explanation of why you are requesting a fee waiver.
* **Amount (number)**: The amount of the fee you are requesting a waiver for. This should match the amount associated with your request.
* **Date of Request (date)**: Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Comments (text)**: Any additional information or comments you'd like to provide.
* **Submitted By (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or as known to the financial team.
* **Submitted By Title (text)**: Enter your title as it appears on your ID badge or as known to the financial team.
