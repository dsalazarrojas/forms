# driving_license_renewal_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The driving license renewal verification form is a crucial document used to confirm an individual's driving license status. This form helps authorities and organizations verify the authenticity of a driver's license and gather accurate information about their driving privileges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your driver's license.
2. Ensure that all fields are correctly filled out, especially the license expiration date and issue date.
3. Verify your vehicle's make and model if applicable.
4. Double-check your contact information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (Page 1)** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required/optional): This section asks for personal information about the driver. Please fill out the correct details about yourself, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **License Status (Page 1)** (`license_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the correct status of your driving license from the provided options: Active, Expired, or Cancelled.
* **License Expiration Date (Page 1)** (`license_expiration_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your driving license is set to expire.
* **Vehicle Information (Page 2)** (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required/optional): If you drive a vehicle, please provide the make and model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (Page 2)** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (Page 2)** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **License Number (Page 2)** (`license_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your driver's license number.
* **License Issue Date (Page 3)** (`license_issue_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your license was issued.
* **Address (Page 3)** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current address.
* **Email (Page 3)** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Page 3)** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **License Status Confirmation (Page 4)** (`license_status_confirmation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Confirm your license status by selecting either "I confirm that my license status is as reported above." or "I am unsure about my license status."
