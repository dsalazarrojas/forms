# cybersecurity_measures_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "cybersecurity_measures_checklist_form" is used to assess and document the organization's current cybersecurity measures in place to protect against threats and vulnerabilities. This form helps identify areas of strength and weakness in the organization's cybersecurity posture, allowing for more effective risk management and mitigation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering the name of your organization in the "organization_name" field.
2. In the "security_measures_used" field, select all the cybersecurity measures your organization currently uses, such as encryption, firewalls, and antivirus software.
3. In the "security_controls_used" field, select all the security controls your organization has implemented, such as access controls, intrusion detection systems, and security information and event management systems.
4. In the "access_controls" field, select all the access controls your organization uses, such as multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, and password policies.
5. In the "incident_response" field, select the incident response process your organization follows, such as containment, eradication, and recovery.
6. In the "risk_assessment" field, select the risk assessment methodology your organization uses, such as risk matrix, risk matrix with probability and impact, and threat and vulnerability assessment.
7. In the "monitoring" field, enter any additional information regarding the organization's monitoring practices, such as the type of monitoring tools used and the frequency of monitoring.
8. In the "training_programs" field, select all the training programs your organization offers to employees, such as phishing awareness, security awareness, and compliance training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization_name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **security_measures_used** (`security_measures_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the cybersecurity measures your organization currently uses, such as encryption, firewalls, and antivirus software.
* **security_controls_used** (`security_controls_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the security controls your organization has implemented, such as access controls, intrusion detection systems, and security information and event management systems.
* **security_controls_used** (`access_controls`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the access controls your organization uses, such as multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, and password policies.
* **security_controls_used** (`incident_response`, select_one, optional): Select the incident response process your organization follows, such as containment, eradication, or recovery.
* **security_controls_used** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, optional): Select the risk assessment methodology your organization uses, such as risk matrix, risk matrix with probability and impact, or threat and vulnerability assessment.
* **monitoring** (`monitoring`, text, optional): Enter any additional information regarding the organization's monitoring practices, such as the type of monitoring tools used and the frequency of monitoring.
* **training_programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the training programs your organization offers to employees, such as phishing awareness, security awareness, or compliance training.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question thoroughly and accurately to ensure an accurate assessment of your organization's cybersecurity posture.
* If unsure about any of the options, consult with the relevant teams or departments within your organization.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any important information.
