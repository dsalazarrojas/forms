# Internal Salary Audit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request an internal salary audit. The audit will help review and verify the accuracy of your salary data, ensuring that it aligns with organizational guidelines and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all mandatory fields (marked as required).
2. Enter your employee name as it appears in the company directory.
3. Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
4. Enter your current salary in the format of a number.
5. Enter the start date of your salary audit request in the YYYY-MM-YY format.
6. Enter the date when you would like the audit to be completed in the YYYY-MM-YY format.
7. Describe any salary discrepancies you have encountered.
8. Enter the reason for your salary audit request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization**: Your department or team within the company. Please enter your department or team name as it appears in the company directory.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name as it appears in the company directory.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your immediate supervisor or manager.
* **Salary**: Enter your current salary in a numerical format. Ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like to start the salary audit process in the YYYY-MM-YY format.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you would like the audit to be completed in the YYYY-MM-YY format. If left blank, the default end date will be assumed as one month from the start date.
* **Salary Discrepancies**: Describe any issues or concerns you have about your salary, including any discrepancies you have noticed during your review of your pay stubs or salary documents.
* **Request Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for your salary audit request, including any specific reasons or concerns you have about your salary.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays in the audit process.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or HR department for assistance.
- Please submit your request only once, as duplicate requests may cause confusion and delay the audit process.
