# remote_employee_offboarding_checklist--administrative_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the remote employee offboarding process. The form helps to gather all necessary details for a smooth transition and ensure that all parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the "Remote Employee Offboarding Checklist" form page.
* Fill in the required fields, such as the employee's name, job title, job start and end dates, reason for departure, and next working day.
* Review and update any additional fields as necessary.
* Submit the form once all relevant information has been provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title (4)**: Enter the job title of the remote employee.
