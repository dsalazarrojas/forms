# Workplace Health And Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Health And Safety Inspection Form is used to gather information about the status of health and safety in the workplace. This data will help management and safety experts make informed decisions to improve workplace health and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2.  Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3.  Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
4.  Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workplace_health_and_safety_inspection_form (text, text, optional):** This is the title of the form. It is not a field that you need to fill but rather a title to identify the form.
* **section_one_general (select_multiple, optional):** This section asks you to select all the general hazards that exist in your workplace. You can choose one or more of the options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **section_two_environment (text, optional):** Describe the environmental conditions in your workplace.
* **section_three_equipment (select_one, optional):** Select the type of equipment that exists in your workplace from the options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **section_four_health_safety_practices (date, optional):** This section asks you to provide a date for the most recent health and safety practices that have taken place. You can enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **section_five_review_comments (note, optional):** This is a text area for you to enter any comments or observations about the health and safety conditions in your workplace.
* **section_six_attachment (time, optional):** This is not a field that you need to fill, as it is intended for attachments.
* **section_seven_reviewer_comments (text, optional):** This section asks you to enter any comments or observations from the reviewer who completed this form.
* **section_eight_reviewer_signature (select_one, optional):** Select the signature of the reviewer who completed this form from the options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **section_nine_reviewer_name (text, optional):** This is the field where the reviewer enters their name.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions carefully and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please consult your supervisor or a member of the health and safety team.
* Keep the form confidential and store it securely.
* Attach any relevant documents or evidence that support your answers, if necessary.
