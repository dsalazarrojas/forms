# Service Level Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Level Agreement (SLA) Approval Form is used to document the terms and conditions of service-level agreements between the organization and its customers or partners. This form helps ensure that all parties are aware of the scope of services, performance metrics, and expectations for service fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the service level agreement with the relevant parties to ensure that all expectations are correctly documented.
2. Fill out the form with the relevant details, such as the service level agreement text, number, date signed, and date approved.
3. Indicate whether the service level agreement has been approved or not.
4. Provide any comments or feedback on the service level agreement.
5. Sign off as the approver, indicating approval or disapproval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Level Agreement Approval Form** (`service_level_agreement_approval_form`): This is the main form section where you will document the service level agreement.
* **Service Level Agreement Text** (`agreement_text`): This field is used to enter the actual text of the service level agreement. Please provide a clear and concise description of the services and expectations.
* **Service Level Agreement Number** (`service_level_agreement_number`): Enter the unique identifier for the service level agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Enter the date the service level agreement was signed.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`): Enter the date the service level agreement was approved.
* **Approval Status** (`approved_by`): Indicate whether the service level agreement has been approved or not. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Approval Comments** (`comments`): Provide any comments or feedback on the service level agreement.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`): As the approver, sign off to indicate approval or disapproval. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
