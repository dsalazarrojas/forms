# Food Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Inspection Form is a tool designed for inspecting facilities to ensure they meet the standards and guidelines for food safety and quality control. This form is used to assess the compliance level of facilities with food safety regulations, identify potential hazards, and document observations and corrective actions taken during the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Review the form sections and ensure you have all necessary information and equipment before starting the inspection.
*   Begin by recording the date and time of inspection on the 'Date Of Inspection' field.
*   Enter the facility type (Food Processing, Food Storage, or Food Service) in the 'Facility Type' field.
*   Provide the facility's location in the 'Facility Location' field.
*   Indicate the number of inspectors in the 'Number Of Inspectors' field.
*   Record the facility's capacity in the 'Facility Capacity' field.
*   Record the time when the inspection started in the 'Inspection Start' field.
*   Record the time when the inspection ended in the 'Inspection End' field.
*   Check if the facility is in compliance or not in the 'Facility Status' field.
*   Document any notes or observations in the 'Facility Notes' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Food Safety Inspection** (`1`, text, required/optional): This section is the main content area where you can record any observations, comments, or notes about the facility's food safety status.
*   **Date Of Inspection** (`2`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the inspection to record the date when the inspection took place.
*   **Time Of Inspection** (`3`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the inspection to record the start and end times.
*   **Facility Type** (`4`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of facility being inspected (Food Processing, Food Storage, or Food Service).
*   **Facility Location** (`5`, text, required/optional): Enter the facility's location to help with navigation during the inspection.
*   **Number Of Inspectors** (`6`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of inspectors conducting the inspection.
*   **Facility Capacity** (`7`, number, required/optional): Record the facility's capacity to understand the scope and scale of operations.
*   **Inspection Start** (`8`, time, required/optional): Record the start time of the inspection.
*   **Inspection End** (`9`, time, required/optional): Record the end time of the inspection.
*   **Facility Status** (`10`, select_one, required/optional): Check if the facility is in compliance (In Compliance) or not (Out of Compliance), or if it's Not Applicable.
*   **Facility Notes** (`11`, note, required/optional): Enter any notes or observations about the facility's food safety status.

## Tips

*   This form is designed for food safety inspection purposes, so ensure that all information recorded is accurate and relevant.
*   Review the form carefully before starting the inspection to ensure you have all necessary equipment and information.
*   Be thorough in recording observations and notes, as these will help identify potential hazards and inform corrective actions.
*   If you are unsure about any aspect of the form or the inspection process, consult with a supervisor or expert.
*   Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to facilitate effective food safety inspections.
