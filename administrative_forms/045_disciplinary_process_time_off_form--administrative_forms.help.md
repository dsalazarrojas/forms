# Disciplinary Process Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Process Time Off Form is designed to record disciplinary process time offs, allowing employees to report their absences due to a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your time off reason in the text field.
2. Enter the start date and end date of your time off in the "Start date" and "End date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the start and end time of your time off in the "Start time" and "End time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time off reason** (`Time off reason`, text, optional):
  This field is used to specify the reason for your time off. Please enter a valid reason for your absence.
* **Start date** (`Start date`, date, optional):
  Enter the date when you started your time off.
* **End date** (`End date`, date, optional):
  Enter the date when your time off ended.
* **Start time** (`Start time`, time, optional):
  Enter the time when you started your time off.
* **End time** (`End time`, time, optional):
  Enter the time when your time off ended.

## Tips
Remember to enter accurate and valid dates and times to ensure correct records.
