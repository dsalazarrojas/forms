# Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form is used to record the service agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form when prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Agreement Acknowledgment** (`service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, note, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you will indicate if you have been given the Service Agreement.
* **Have you been given the Service Agreement?** (`service_agreement_acknowledged`, select_one, required: false): Answer this question with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Date received Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form** (`date_received_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, date, required: false): Enter the date you received the Service Agreement.
* **Time received Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form** (`time_received_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, time, required: false): Enter the time you received the Service Agreement.
* **Name of person receiving Service Agreement Acknowledgment** (`name_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person receiving the Service Agreement.
* **Date Signed Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form** (`date_signed_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the Service Agreement.
* **Time Signed Service Agreement Acknowledgment Form** (`time_signed_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, time, required: false): Enter the time you signed the Service Agreement.
* **Name of person signing Service Agreement Acknowledgment** (`name_signed_service_agreement_acknowledgment_form`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person signing the Service Agreement.

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields accurately and truthfully.
