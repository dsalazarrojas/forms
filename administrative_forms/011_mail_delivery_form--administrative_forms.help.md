# Mail Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mail Delivery Form is used to collect and track information related to mail delivery, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of packages and mail items. This guide provides a clear overview of the form fields and their purpose, enabling users to complete the form with precision and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mail Delivery Form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the delivery date, which represents the date on which the mail will be delivered.
2. Enter the sender's name, which should be the name of the person or organization sending the mail.
3. Enter the sender's address, which should be the physical address of the sender.
4. Enter the recipient's name, which is the name of the person or organization receiving the mail.
5. Enter the recipient's email address, which will be used for communication and tracking.
6. Select the package type, which categorizes the type of mail being delivered (e.g., parcel, package, letter).
7. Select the package status, which indicates the current status of the mail delivery (Active or Inactive).
8. Enter the tracking number(s), which is a unique identifier assigned to the mail item.
9. Enter the delivery location, which is the physical address where the mail will be delivered.
10. Enter any additional remarks or comments about the mail delivery.
11. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the mail delivery.
12. Enter the contact person's email address.
13. Enter the contact person's phone number.
14. Enter the contact person's extension number.
15. Enter the contact department.
16. Enter the contact title.
17. Enter the contact company or organization.
18. Enter the package weight.
19. Enter the tracking number 2 (second tracking number, if applicable).
20. Enter the tracking number 3 (third tracking number, if applicable).
21. Enter the package value.
22. Enter a detailed description of the package.
23. Enter additional details about the package.
24. Select the package status (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which the mail will be delivered. This field is optional.
* **Sender Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization sending the mail. This field is optional.
* **Sender Address**: Enter the physical address of the sender. This field is optional.
* **Recipient Name**: Enter the name of the person or organization receiving the mail. This field is optional.
* **Recipient Email**: Enter the email address of the recipient. This field is optional and should be used for communication and tracking.
* **Package ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the package (number only). This field is optional.
* **Package Type**: Select the type of mail being delivered (e.g., parcel, package, letter). This field is optional.
* **Package Status**: Select the current status of the mail delivery (Active or Inactive). This field is optional.
* **Tracking Number**: Enter a unique identifier for the mail item (text only). This field is optional.
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the physical address where the mail will be delivered. This field is optional.
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional comments or remarks about the mail delivery. This field is optional.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the mail delivery. This field is optional.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone Extension**: Enter the extension number of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Department**: Enter the department of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Company**: Enter the company or organization of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Package Weight**: Enter the weight of the package. This field is optional.
* **Tracking Number 2**: Enter the second tracking number for the mail item. This field is optional.
* **Tracking Number 3**: Enter the third tracking number for the mail item. This field is optional.
* **Package Value**: Enter the value of the package. This field is optional.
* **Package Description**: Enter a detailed description of the package. This field is optional.
* **Package Details**: Enter additional details about the package. This field is optional.
* **Package Status 2**: Select the current status of the package (Active or Inactive). This field is optional.
