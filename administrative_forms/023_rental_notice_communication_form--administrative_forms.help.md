# Rental Notice Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Notice Communication Form is used to document rental agreements and related communication between landlords and tenants. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately for reference and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the necessary details about the agreement, including the type of agreement, start and end dates, and length of the lease.
2. Provide any changes made to the agreement, such as updates to terms and conditions.
3. Document any actions taken, such as sending notifications to the tenant or landlord.
4. Record any next steps, such as future meetings or inspections.
5. Confirm the status of the agreement, including whether it has been signed and by whom.
