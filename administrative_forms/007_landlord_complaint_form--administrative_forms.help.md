# landlord_complaint_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by tenants to report any issues or complaints they have with their landlord or the property they are renting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Describe the issue or complaint you are experiencing.
3. Select the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Other").
4. If you need to contact the landlord or property management company, enter your phone number.
5. If you have an e-mail address, enter it so we can contact you.
6. Enter the date you first experienced the issue.
7. Enter the address of the rental property.
8. Enter the name of the landlord or property management company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (tenant_name, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the tenant reporting this issue.
* **Complaint Description** (complaint_description, text, required: false): Please describe the issue or complaint you are experiencing. This should be a clear and concise explanation of what happened.
* **Complaint Type** (complaint_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of complaint you are reporting (e.g. "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Other").
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, required: false): If you need to contact the landlord or property management company, enter your phone number here.
* **E-mail** (email, email, required: false): If you have an e-mail address, enter it so we can contact you.
* **Complaint Date** (complaint_date, date, required: false): Enter the date you first experienced the issue.
* **Address** (address, text, required: false): Enter the address of the rental property.
* **Landlord's Name** (landlord_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the landlord or property management company.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when describing the issue or complaint.
* Make sure to select the correct complaint type.
* If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, attach it to the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the property management company directly.
