# Healthcare Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information related to incidents that occur within a healthcare facility. It's designed to support the investigation and documentation of incidents, ensuring that all relevant details are captured and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you've completed all relevant sections.
2. Enter the facility information (page 1) as accurately as possible.
3. Provide detailed information about the incident (page 2).
4. Complete any follow-up actions or next steps (page 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Information** (Page 1, facility_info):
	+ A brief description of the healthcare facility where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Information** (Page 1, incident_info):
	+ A brief description of the incident, including date, time, and location.
* **Incident Details** (Page 2, incident_details):
	+ A detailed account of what happened, including any injuries or damages.
* **Incident Outcome** (Page 2, incident_outcome):
	+ The outcome of the incident, including any injuries or consequences.
* **Facility Equipment Involved** (Page 2, facility_equipment_involved):
	+ A list of equipment or assets involved in the incident.
* **Incident Investigation** (Page 2, incident_investigation):
	+ A description of the investigation process and any actions taken.
* **Incident Reporting** (Page 2, incident_reporting):
	+ The date and time of the incident report submission.
* **Incident Follow-up** (Page 2, incident_follow_up):
	+ Any follow-up actions or next steps taken after the incident.
* **Incident Cause** (Page 3, incident_cause):
	+ A detailed description of the root cause of the incident.
* **Incident Recommendations** (Page 3, incident_recommendations):
	+ Suggestions for preventing similar incidents in the future.
* **Incident Root Cause** (Page 3, incident_root_cause):
	+ A detailed description of the root cause of the incident.
* **Incident Conclusion** (Page 3, incident_conclusion):
	+ A summary of the incident investigation and findings.
* **Facility Review** (Page 3, facility_review):
	+ A review of the facility's response to the incident.
* **Incident Review** (Page 3, incident_review):
	+ A review of the incident investigation process and outcomes.
* **Follow-up Details** (Page 4, follow_up_details):
	+ Any additional information or details about the follow-up actions.
* **Facility Response** (Page 4, facility_response):
	+ A statement of the facility's response to the incident.
* **Incident Report** (Page 4, incident_report):
	+ A summary of the incident report.
* **Incident Investigating Person** (Page 5, incident_investigating_person):
	+ The person or team responsible for investigating the incident.
* **Incident Investigating Department** (Page 5, incident_investigating_department):
	+ The department responsible for investigating the incident.
* **Incident Investigating Title** (Page 5, incident_investigating_title):
	+ The title or position of the incident investigator.
* **Incident Investigating Initiation Date** (Page 5, incident_investigating_initiation_date):
	+ The date the incident investigation was initiated.
* **Incident Investigating Initiation Time** (Page 5, incident_investigating_initiation_time):
	+ The time the incident investigation was initiated.
* **Incident Investigating Completion Date** (Page 5, incident_investigating_completion_date):
	+ The date the incident investigation was completed.
* **Incident Investigating Completion Time** (Page 5, incident_investigating_completion_time):
	+ The time the incident investigation was completed.
* **Signature** (Page 6, signature):
	+ A digital signature of the person completing the form.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant personnel or the facility's incident management team.
