# disaster_evacuation_plan_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The disaster_evacuation_plan_evaluation_form is designed to collect information related to disaster evacuation plans. This form helps gather details about emergency contact information, incident details, participant information, and other relevant facts related to evacuation procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    1.  Click on the "Emergency Contact Info" section, and provide your emergency contact information.
    2.  Move on to the "Contact Info" section and select the appropriate contact methods (Phone or Email) for the participant(s).
    3.  Fill in the "Incident Info" section with details about the incident.
    4.  Provide information about the participant(s) in the "Participant Info" and "Participant Names" sections.
    5.  Indicate whether the participant(s) has/have a participant phone number(s) (Phone or Other) in the "Participant Phone Numbers" section.
    6.  Provide details about the evacuation information in the "Evacuation Info" section.
    7.  Enter the drill information in the "Drill Info" section.
    8.  Specify the participant location in the "Participant Location" section.
    9.  Enter the location in the "Location" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Emergency Contact Info**:
    *   (id: 1, text, required: false): This section is used to enter emergency contact information.
*   **Contact Info**:
    *   (id: 2, select_multiple, required: false): Select the appropriate contact methods (Phone or Email) for the participant(s).
*   **Incident Info**:
    *   (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter details about the incident.
*   **Participant Info**:
    *   (id: 4, text, required: false): Provide information about the participant(s).
*   **Participant Names**:
    *   (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter the name(s) of the participant(s).
*   **Participant Phone Numbers**:
    *   (id: 6, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the participant(s) has/have a participant phone number(s) (Phone or Other).
*   **Evacuation Info**:
    *   (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter details about the evacuation information.
*   **Drill Info**:
    *   (id: 8, number, required: false): Enter drill information.
*   **Participant Location**:
    *   (id: 9, text, required: false): Specify the participant location.
*   **Location**:
    *   (id: 10, text, required: false): Enter the location.
