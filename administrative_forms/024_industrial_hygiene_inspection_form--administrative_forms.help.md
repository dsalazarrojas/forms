# Industrial Hygiene Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by inspectors to collect data on industrial hygiene inspections within the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Industrial Hygiene Inspection Form Title**: Enter a brief title to identify the purpose and scope of the inspection.
2. **Category**: Choose the category related to the inspection (e.g. general, construction, etc.).
3. **Description**: Provide a brief description of the inspection and its purpose.
4. **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, enter the tool used during the inspection.
5. **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required.
6. **Output File**: Enter the location or file name where the inspection report will be saved.
7. **Selected Category**: Choose the category that best describes the area of the inspection.
8. **Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
9. **Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
10. **Note**: Record any notes or observations made during the inspection.
11. **Email**: If applicable, enter the contact information of the person responsible for the inspection.
12. **Phone**: If applicable, enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the inspection.
13. **Address**: Enter the address of the facility or location being inspected.
14. **City**: Enter the city of the facility or location being inspected.
15. **State**: Enter the state of the facility or location being inspected.
16. **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the facility or location being inspected.
17. **Country**: Enter the country of the facility or location being inspected.
