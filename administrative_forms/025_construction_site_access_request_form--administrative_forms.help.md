# Construction Site Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Site Access Request Form is used to request access to a construction site for inspection, maintenance, or other authorized purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and organization (if applicable).
2.  Provide the date you need to access the site.
3.  Specify the time window during which you plan to access the site.
4.  Explain the reason for your visit.
5.  If necessary, provide the name and contact information of your on-site supervisor or contact person.
6.  Confirm whether PPE (Personal Protective Equipment) is required for your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company or contractor, enter their name.
* **Access date** (`access_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to access the site.
* **Access time window** (`access_time_window`, `text`, optional): Specify the time you plan to be on site.
* **Reason for access** (`reason_for_access`, `text`, required): Explain the purpose of your visit to the site.
* **Supervisor contact name** (`supervisor_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your on-site supervisor or contact person.
* **PPE required** (`ppe_required`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you will be wearing Personal Protective Equipment during your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to clearly state the reason for your visit to ensure that your request is properly evaluated.
* If PPE is required, please confirm that you will adhere to site regulations.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure that your supervisor or site manager can reach you if needed.
