# Emergency Repair Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for submitting emergency repair requests to the administrative team. It is used to report issues that require immediate attention to prevent damage or harm to people or equipment. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the repair request. Choose "Administrative Forms" for repair requests related to administrative offices or other categories for other types of repairs.
2. Briefly describe the issue or request in the Description field.
3. Provide the location of the issue or request in the Location field.
4. (Optional) If you know the date and time of the issue or request, enter it in the Date and Time fields.
5. Enter your name and contact email or phone number so that we can reach out to you for more information.
6. Submit the form to send your request to the administrative team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Administrative Forms, Other): Select the category of the repair request to ensure it is properly routed to the correct team.
* **Description** (Description): Provide a brief description of the issue or request.
* **Location** (Location): Enter the exact location of the issue or request.
* **Date** (Date): (Optional) Enter the date of the issue or request, if known.
* **Time** (Time): (Optional) Enter the time of the issue or request, if known.
* **Requestor Name** (Requestor Name): Enter your name to help us keep you informed about the status of your repair request.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email): Enter your email address so that we can reach out to you for more information.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you for more information, or in case we need to follow up.
