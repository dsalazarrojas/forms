Biometric Entry System Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Biometric Entry System Application Form is used to request biometric access for employees who require access to sensitive areas of the facility. The form captures personal details, access requirements, and consent to biometric data collection and usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked as "required" (bold font). Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant and applicable choice.
3. Double-check your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
4. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your official ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your official ID.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee identification number.
* **Department**: Enter your department or division you belong to.
* **Job Title**: Enter your position or title within the organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Office Location**: Enter your primary work location.
* **Access Requirements**: Briefly describe your access requirements.
* **Access Level Requested**: Select the level of access you need, such as Standard Employee Access or Extended Hours Access.
* **Areas Requiring Access**: Select all areas you need access to, such as Main Entrance or Server Room.
* **Access Justification**: Briefly describe why you need this access.
* **Biometric Preference**: Select your preferred biometric method, such as Fingerprint or Facial Recognition.
* **Medical Restrictions**: If you have any conditions affecting biometric capture, describe them here.
* **Work Schedule**: Briefly describe your typical work hours.
* **Standard Working Hours**: Select your standard working hours, such as 9-5 or 6 AM - 2 PM.
* **Weekend Access Needed**: Indicate if you need access on weekends.
* **Holiday Access Needed**: Indicate if you need access on holidays.
* **Authorization**: If required by your supervisor, get their approval here.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter your supervisor's contact email address.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Indicate if your supervisor has approved this request.
* **Security Clearance Level**: Select your current clearance level, such as Public Trust or Secret.
* **Clearance Expiration Date**: If your clearance expires, enter the date.
* **Background Check Status**: Indicate the status of your background verification.
* **Consent Agreement**: Agree to biometric data collection and usage.
* **Data Usage Understanding**: Confirm you understand how your biometric data will be used.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact number of the emergency contact person.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date of this application.
* **Processing Status**: This field is for administrative use only.
