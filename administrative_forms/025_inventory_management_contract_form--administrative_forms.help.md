# Inventory Management Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Inventory Management Contract Form" is a document used to capture essential information about the management and maintenance of inventory. It includes details about the contact person, contract terms, contact information, contract date and time, and inventory type. This form aims to streamline the process of managing inventory contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the options for each field as required by the form's schema. Note that some fields are required, so ensure you fill them out accordingly.
* For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inventory Management Contract** (`1`, text, required/optional): This field is the title of the contract, which should be a brief description of the agreement.
* **Contact Person** (`2`, select_one, required): Choose one of the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'. This indicates whether the contact person is available for the contract.
* **Contract Terms** (`3`, text, optional): This field is for any additional terms or conditions that need to be included in the contract.
* **Contact Email** (`4`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the inventory management contract.
* **Contact Phone** (`5`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person responsible for the inventory management contract.
* **Contract Date** (`6`, date, optional): Select the date on which the contract was signed or is expected to be signed.
* **Contract Time** (`7`, time, optional): Select the time at which the contract was signed or is expected to be signed.
* **Inventory Type** (`8`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'. This field is for indicating the type of inventory involved in the contract.
* **Inventory Location** (`9`, text, optional): Enter the location where the inventory is stored or managed.
* **Inventory Status** (`10`, text, optional): Enter the current status of the inventory.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Double-check the options selected for fields with multiple options.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the form's schema for clarification.
- Review the form for completeness before submission.
