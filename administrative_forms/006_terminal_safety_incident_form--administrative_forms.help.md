# Terminal Safety Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Terminal Safety Incident Form is used to report and document incidents that occur within the terminal operations. This form helps to gather information about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, its location, and a detailed description. It also includes fields for investigating the cause and type of incident, as well as any corrective actions taken. This information helps in identifying and resolving incidents efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date and time of the incident from the calendars provided.
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant circumstances or events surrounding it.
4. Choose the type and cause of the incident from the provided options.
5. List any corrective actions taken to address the incident.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the incident reporter.
7. Enter the date and time the incident was reported.
8. Select the status of the incident.
9. (Optional) Attach any images, videos, or documents related to the incident.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Incident Date (id: 1)
* **Date of Incident** (`date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.

### Time of Incident (id: 2)
* **Time of Incident** (`time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.

### Location of Incident (id: 3)
* **Location of Incident** (`text`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.

### Description of Incident (id: 4)
* **Description of Incident** (`text`): Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant circumstances or events surrounding it.

### Type of Incident (id: 5)
* **Type of Incident** (`select_one`): Select the type of incident (Slip, trip and fall, Fire, Explosion, or Other).

### Cause of Incident (id: 6)
* **Cause of Incident** (`select_multiple`): Select one or more causes of the incident (Equipment malfunction, Human error, Natural disaster, or Other).

### Investigation Result (id: 7)
* **Investigation Result** (`text`): Describe the result of the investigation.

### Corrective Action Taken (id: 8)
* **Corrective Action Taken** (`text`): List any corrective actions taken to address the incident.

### Incident Reporter (id: 9)
* **Incident Reporter** (`text`): Enter the name and contact information of the incident reporter.

### Date of Reporting (id: 10)
* **Date of Reporting** (`date`): Enter the date the incident was reported.

### Reported By (id: 11)
* **Reported By** (`text`): Enter the name and contact information of the person reporting the incident.

### Terminal ID (id: 12)
* **Terminal ID** (`text`): Enter the terminal ID number.

### Asset Tag (id: 13)
* **Asset Tag** (`text`): Enter the asset tag number.

### Equipment Type (id: 14)
* **Equipment Type** (`select_one`): Select the type of equipment involved (Pumps, Compressors, or Other).

### Location Type (id: 15)
* **Location Type** (`select_one`): Select the location type (Terminal, Yard, Outside of terminal, or Other).

### Terminal Location (id: 16)
* **Terminal Location** (`text`): Enter the terminal location.

### Incident Image (id: 17)
* **Incident Image** (`text`): (Optional) Attach any images related to the incident.

### Incident Video (id: 18)
* **Incident Video** (`text`): (Optional) Attach any videos related to the incident.

### Incident Document (id: 19)
* **Incident Document** (`text`): (Optional) Attach any documents related to the incident.

### Additional Notes (id: 20)
* **Additional Notes** (`text`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.

### Incident Status (id: 21)
* **Incident Status** (`select_one`): Select the status of the incident (Open, Closed).

### Date Closed (id: 22)
* **Date Closed** (`date`): Enter the date the incident was closed.

### Closed By (id: 23)
* **Closed By** (`text`): Enter the name and contact information of the person who closed the incident.

### Comments (id: 24)
* **Comments** (`text`): Enter any comments or observations about the incident.

### Tips

* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* You can attach relevant images, videos, or documents to provide more context to the incident.
* This form is for reporting incidents that occur within the terminal operations.
