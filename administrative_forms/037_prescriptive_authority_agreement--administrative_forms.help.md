# Prescriptive Authority Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescriptive Authority Agreement form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of an agreement between a prescriber and a facility. This form is used to establish a clear understanding of the responsibilities and commitments of both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions outlined in it.
2. Select the correct options for the "Provider Information" field to indicate the prescriber's details.
3. Choose the correct "Prescribing Authority" option to indicate the prescriber's authority.
4. Enter the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields to specify the duration of the agreement.
5. Select the "Sign Officer" option to indicate the person signing on behalf of the facility.
6. Select the relevant "Facility ID" options to specify the facility's details.
7. Enter any additional "Notes" if necessary.
8. Fill in the "Date" and "Time" fields to record the date and time of signing.
9. Enter the "Email", "Phone", "Fax", and "Address" fields to provide contact information.
10. Sign the "Prescriber Signature" and "Facility Signature" fields with the date and time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Information (Option 1/Option 2)**: Select the correct option to indicate the prescriber's provider information.
* **Prescribing Authority (Option 1/Option 2)**: Choose the correct option to indicate the prescriber's authority.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Sign Officer (Option 1/Option 2)**: Select the correct option to indicate the person signing on behalf of the facility.
* **Facility ID (Option 1/Option 2/Option 3)**: Select the relevant options to specify the facility's details.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes if necessary.
* **Date (DD/MM/YY)**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Time (HH:MM:SS)**: Enter the time of signing.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the facility.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the facility.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Prescriber Signature**: Sign with your name, date, and time.
* **Facility Signature**: Sign with your name, date, and time.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the facility's name.
* **Signee Name**: Enter the signee's name.
* **Facility ID (Option 1/Option 2/Option 3)**: Select the relevant options to specify the facility's details.
* **Agreement Signed**: Enter the number of agreements signed.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Signature Time**: Enter the time of signing.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable.
