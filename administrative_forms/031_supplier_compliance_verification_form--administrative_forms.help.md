# Supplier Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Compliance Verification Form is used to collect information from suppliers about their compliance status with regulatory bodies. This form helps businesses verify the legitimacy of their suppliers and ensures they are working with reliable and compliant partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your supplier's name in the **supplier_name** field.
2. Enter your supplier's company name in the **company_name** field.
3. Provide your supplier's email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your supplier's address in the **address** field.
5. Include your supplier's phone number in the **phone** field.
6. Indicate if your supplier is USA-based by checking the **is_usa_based** field.
7. If applicable, list your supplier's certifications in the **has_certifications** field.
8. Specify the regulatory body your supplier is certified by in the **regulatory_body** field.
9. Enter the date of your supplier's certification in the **certification_date** field.
10. Enter the date of your supplier's certification expiration in the **expiration_date** field.
11. Enter the date of the compliance verification in the **compliance_verification_date** field.
12. Select the current **verification_status** of your supplier (verified, pending, or failed).
13. Add any additional comments about your supplier's compliance status in the **comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **supplier_name** (text, required): Enter your supplier's name.
* **company_name** (text, required): Enter your supplier's company name.
* **email** (text, optional): Provide your supplier's email address.
* **address** (text, optional): Enter your supplier's address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Include your supplier's phone number.
* **is_usa_based** (text, optional): Indicate if your supplier is USA-based.
* **has_certifications** (text, optional): List your supplier's certifications.
* **regulatory_body** (text, optional): Specify the regulatory body your supplier is certified by.
* **certification_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your supplier's certification.
* **expiration_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your supplier's certification expiration.
* **compliance_verification_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the compliance verification.
* **verification_status** (select_one, optional): Select the current **verification_status** of your supplier (verified, pending, or failed).
* **comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about your supplier's compliance status.
