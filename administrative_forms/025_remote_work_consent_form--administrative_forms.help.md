# Remote Work Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Remote Work Consent Form is used to collect information from employees who are working remotely to ensure a smooth and secure remote work experience. This form should only be completed by employees who are currently or will be working remotely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your remote worker information, including your job title and employment status.
2. Select the type of remote work arrangement you are entering into (e.g. full-time, part-time, project-based).
3. Enter the work location and schedule you will be working from.
4. Select your remote status (Active or Inactive).
5. Enter the termination date of your remote work arrangement (if applicable).
6. Review and agree to the terms of use.
7. Sign and date the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote worker information** (`employee`, text, required): Please enter your employee ID or name.
* **Remote work arrangement** (`work_arrangement`, text, required): Enter the description of your remote work arrangement.
* **Type of work arrangement** (`work_arrangement_type`, select multiple, required): Select all the types of remote work arrangements you are entering into (e.g. full-time, part-time, project-based).
* **Work location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you will be working from.
* **Work schedule** (`work_schedule`, text, required): Enter your work schedule, including the hours you will be working.
* **Work hours** (`work_hours`, time, required): Enter your work hours in 24-hour format (e.g. 08:00-17:00).
* **Employer information** (`employer`, text, required): Enter your employer's name and contact information.
* **Employment type** (`employment_type`, text, required): Select your employment type (e.g. full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Employment status** (`employment_status`, text, required): Select your employment status (e.g. active, inactive, terminated).
* **Termination date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your remote work termination (if applicable).
* **Remote status** (`remote_status`, select one, required): Select your remote status (Active or Inactive).
* **Review date** (`review_date`, date, required): The date the agreement was last reviewed.
* **Agreement signed** (`agreement_signed`, text, optional): Enter the date and time you signed the agreement.
* **Terms of use** (`terms_of_use`, text, required): Review and agree to the terms of use.
* **Terms of use link** (`terms_of_use_link`, text, required): Enter the URL of the terms of use document.
* **Remote work policies** (`remote_work_policies`, text, required): Enter the remote work policies you will be following.
* **Agreement version** (`agreement_version`, text, required): Enter the version of the agreement.
* **Employee sign date** (`employee_sign_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Employee sign by** (`employee_sign_by`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
* **Employer sign date** (`employer_sign_date`, date, required): Enter the date your employer signed the agreement.
* **Employer sign by** (`employer_sign_by`, text, required): Enter your employer's name and title.
* **Agreement notes** (`agreement_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the agreement.
* **Agreement review date** (`agreement_review_date`, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was last reviewed.
* **Agreement expiration date** (`agreement_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date the agreement expires.
