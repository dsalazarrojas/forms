# Customer Service Specialist Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help customers provide recommendations for a Customer Service Specialist position. It's used to capture essential information about the individual being recommended, their role, and any supporting details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form according to your feedback.
2. On **Page 2 (Candidate Information)**:
   * Select one of the options: **Name**, **Title**, **Position**, or **Department** to indicate which piece of information the recommendation is based on.
3. On **Page 3 (Reference Information)**:
   * Select multiple options to provide additional relevant information about the individual being recommended.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the **Comments** field on **Page 4**.
5. On **Page 5**:
   * Enter the date when you provided the recommendation.
6. On **Page 6**:
   * Enter the time of day when you provided the recommendation.
7. In **Page 7 (Recommendation)**:
   * Write a brief description of why you are recommending the individual.
8. On **Page 8**:
   * Enter the date when you recommended the individual.
9. If you have an email or phone number associated with this recommendation, please enter them on **Page 9** and **Page 10**, respectively.
10. On **Page 11 (Additional Comments)**:
   * Use this field for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1**: No fields on this page, please move to **Page 2**.
* **Page 2 (Candidate Information)**:
  * **Name**, **Title**, **Position**, or **Department**: This selection is used to indicate which piece of information the recommendation is based on.
* **Page 3 (Reference Information)**:
  * **Name**, **Title**, **Position**, or **Department**: This is a multiple-choice selection to provide additional relevant information about the individual being recommended.
* **Page 4 (Comments)**:
  * Enter any additional comments or feedback about the individual being recommended.
* **Page 5 (Date)**:
  * Enter the date when you provided the recommendation.
* **Page 6 (Time)**:
  * Enter the time of day when you provided the recommendation.
* **Page 7 (Recommendation)**:
  * Write a brief description of why you are recommending the individual.
* **Page 8 (Recommendation Date)**:
  * Enter the date when you recommended the individual.
* **Page 9 (Email)**:
  * Enter an email associated with this recommendation, if applicable.
* **Page 10 (Phone)**:
  * Enter a phone number associated with this recommendation, if applicable.
* **Page 11 (Additional Comments)**:
  * Use this field for any additional comments or feedback about the individual being recommended.

**Submit**:
  * Select this option when you have completed all the required fields and are ready to submit your recommendation.
