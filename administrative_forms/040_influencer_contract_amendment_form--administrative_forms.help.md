# Influencer Contract Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Influencer Contract Amendment Form is used to record changes made to an influencer contract, ensuring that all parties are aware of any modifications to the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Form Data** section with the relevant information about the contract amendment.
2. Select the type of amendment being made from the **Type** field.
3. If necessary, select the sub-type of amendment from the **Subtype** field.
4. Specify the **Amended By** field, indicating who made the amendment.
5. Enter the **Date Modified** and **Time Modified** fields to record the date and time the amendment was made.
6. Provide a brief explanation for the **Reason** field, if applicable.
7. Add any relevant **Notes** to support the amendment.
8. Update the **Contract Status** field to reflect any changes to the contract as a result of the amendment.
9. Enter the **Contract ID** field with the unique identifier for the contract.
10. Record the **Amendment Date** and **Amendment Time** fields to capture the date and time the amendment was made.
11. Identify who **Form Submitted by** to record the person who submitted the form.
12. Enter the date and time the **Form was Submitted on**.
13. If the form has been modified, select who **Form Modified by** and enter the date and time the **Form was Modified on**.
14. Update the **Form Version** field with the current version number of the form.
15. Enter the **Form Revision** field with the current revision number of the form.
16. Record the **Form Last Modified** fields to track the last time the form was modified.
17. Enter the **Form Last Modified by** field with the name of the person who last modified the form.
18. Record the **Form Last Modified on** and **Form Last Modified Time** fields to capture the date and time the form was last modified.
19. Finally, enter any **User Notes** to provide additional context or information about the amendment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: Enter the relevant information about the contract amendment in this field.
* **Type**: Select the type of amendment being made to the contract. (1, 2)
* **Subtype**: If necessary, select the sub-type of amendment from the available options. (1, 2)
* **Amended By**: Identify who made the amendment to the contract.
* **Date Modified**: Record the date the amendment was made.
* **Time Modified**: Record the time the amendment was made.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for the amendment, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant notes to support the amendment.
* **Contract Status**: Update the contract status to reflect any changes to the contract as a result of the amendment.
* **Contract ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the contract.
* **Amendment Date**: Record the date the amendment was made.
* **Amendment Time**: Record the time the amendment was made.
* **Form Submitted by**: Identify who submitted the form to record the person who made the amendment.
* **Form Submitted on**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Form Modified by**: If the form has been modified, select who last modified the form.
* **Form Modified on**: Record the date and time the form was last modified.
* **Form Version**: Enter the current version number of the form.
* **Form Revision**: Enter the current revision number of the form.
* **Form Last Modified**: Record the last time the form was modified.
* **Form Last Modified by**: Identify the person who last modified the form.
* **Form Last Modified on**: Record the date and time the form was last modified.
* **User Notes**: Enter any additional context or information about the amendment.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to make amendments to the contract.
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use the **Reason** field to provide a brief explanation for the amendment, if applicable.
