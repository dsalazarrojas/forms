# vendor_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor audit form is a tool used to document and track vendor information and ratings. It is used to gather detailed data about each vendor, including their address, rating, and reasons for rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the vendor's information on page 1, "vendor_info".
2.  Enter the vendor's address on page 2, "vendor_address".
3.  Rate the vendor on page 3, using the "vendor_rating" field.
4.  Select the reasons for the vendor's rating in the "rating_reasons" field on page 4.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about the vendor in the "vendor_notes" field on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **vendor_info** (`vendor_info`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's name, contact person, and any other relevant information.
*   **vendor_address** (`vendor_address`, text, optional): Enter the vendor's street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **vendor_rating** (`vendor_rating`, number, optional): Rate the vendor's performance out of 5, with 5 being excellent and 1 being poor.
*   **rating_reasons** (`rating_reasons`, select_one, required): Select the reasons for the vendor's rating from the list provided.
*   **vendor_notes** (`vendor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vendor.

## Tips
*   Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate and thorough information.
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure easy reference and decision-making.
*   Review and update the form regularly to keep information up-to-date.
