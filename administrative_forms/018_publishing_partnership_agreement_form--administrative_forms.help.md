# publishing_partnership_agreement_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Publishing Partnership Agreement Form is used to document the partnership between authors and publishers for the publication of a book or other work. This form helps ensure that both parties are on the same page and that all necessary information is collected for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information:

*   Start by giving the form a title and filling in the `Author Name`, `Publisher Name`, and `Agreement Type` fields, depending on your role in the partnership.
*   Next, specify the start and end dates of the partnership if applicable.
*   You can add any terms and conditions that you would like to include in the partnership agreement.
*   Select the submitter role from the provided options (Author or Publisher).
*   Add your signature and name in the `Author Signature` and `Publisher Signature` fields respectively.
*   If you're the publisher, fill in the `Publish Date` and `Publish Time` fields with the relevant information.
*   Finally, add any additional contact information such as your email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for the partnership agreement. This is an optional field and can be left blank.
*   **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, optional): The name of the author involved in the partnership.
*   **Publisher Name** (`publisher_name`, text, optional): The name of the publisher involved in the partnership.
*   **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, optional): The type of partnership agreement being made. You can choose from "Publishing" or "Distribution".
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The start date of the partnership, if applicable.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The end date of the partnership, if applicable.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Any additional terms or conditions that you would like to include in the partnership agreement.
*   **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_multiple, optional): The role of the submitter in the partnership. You can select from "Author" or "Publisher".
*   **Author Signature** (`author_signature`, text, optional): The signature of the author, if applicable.
*   **Publisher Signature** (`publisher_signature`, text, optional): The signature of the publisher, if applicable.
*   **Publish Date** (`publish_date`, date, optional): The date of publication, if applicable.
*   **Publish Time** (`publish_time`, time, optional): The time of publication, if applicable.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email of the publisher or author for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): The phone number of the publisher or author for contact purposes.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): The address of the publisher or author for contact purposes.
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): The city of the publisher or author's location.
*   **State** (`state`, text, optional): The state of the publisher or author's location.
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): The zip code of the publisher or author's location.

## Tips
Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely, and double-check your information for any errors before submitting the form.
