# hangar_space_lease_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hangar space lease agreement form is used to document the terms and conditions of hangar space usage by a lessee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the lessee's information in the "lessee_info" field.
2. Select the type of space being leased from the "space_type" field (e.g., Hangar, T-hangar, Parking, Ramp, or Office).
3. Fill in the "space_details" field with a description of the space being leased.
4. Enter the rental terms and conditions in the "rental_terms" field.
5. Specify the rental fees in the "rental_fees" field.
6. Sign and date the lease agreement by entering your signature in the "lessee_signature" and "landlord_signature" fields.
7. Enter the date the lease was signed in the "date_signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **lessee_info**: Enter the name and contact information of the lessee (user or company) who will be using the space.
* **space_details**: Provide a description of the specific hangar space being leased, including its location and size.
* **rental_terms**: Outline the terms and conditions of the rental, including any restrictions or requirements.
* **rental_fees**: Enter the rental fees per unit of time (e.g., per month or per hour).
* **lessee_signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the lessee's agreement to the terms and conditions of the lease.
* **landlord_signature**: Sign and date this field to confirm the landlord's agreement to the terms and conditions of the lease.
* **date_signed**: Enter the date the lease agreement was signed.
* **space_type**: Select from the options provided to specify the type of space being leased (e.g., Hangar, T-hangar, Parking, Ramp, or Office).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any confusion or issues with the lease agreement.
* Review and sign the lease agreement carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the administrator for assistance.
