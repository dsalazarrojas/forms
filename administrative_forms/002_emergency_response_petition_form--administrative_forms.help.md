# emergency_response_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **emergency_response_petition_form** is a standardized form used to collect responses or expressions of support from individuals in response to an emergency situation. This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals, including their contact details and statement of support, which can be used for further actions or follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Sign Petition**: On the first page, simply enter the number of signatures you are providing. This is a required field.
2. **Enter Your Name**: On the next page, enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license. This is a required field.
3. **Enter Your Email**: On the next page, enter your email address that you use to receive messages. This is a required field.
4. **Enter Your Message**: On the next page, type a brief message expressing your support or response to the emergency situation. This is a required field.
5. **Select Your Signature Type**: On the next page, select one or more options from the list to express your level of support or response. This is a required field.
6. **Optional Date**: You can optionally enter a date to provide additional context about your response.
7. **Optional Time**: You can optionally enter the time you are responding to provide additional context about your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Petition** (<number>): Enter the number of signatures you are providing.
* **your_name** (<text>): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **your_email** (<email>): Enter your email address that you use to receive messages.
* **your_message** (<text>): Enter a brief message expressing your support or response to the emergency situation.
* **sign_and_support** (select multiple): Select one or more options from the list to express your level of support or response.
* **date** (<date>): Enter a date to provide additional context about your response (optional).
* **time** (<time>): Enter a time to provide additional context about your response (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your response is complete and accurate.
- Be as detailed as possible when entering your message to provide a clear expression of your support or response.
- If you have any additional comments or information, feel free to include them in your message.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
