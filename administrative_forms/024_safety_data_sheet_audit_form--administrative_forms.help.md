# Safety Data Sheet Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about Safety Data Sheets (SDS) from companies. The purpose of this form is to ensure that SDSs are accurately and correctly completed, and to maintain the safety and compliance standards of the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information in the "Company Information" field.
2. Enter the safety data sheet number in the "Safety Data Sheet Number" field.
3. Select the relevant material information from the list in the "Material Information" field.
4. Enter the date of the safety data sheet in the "Safety Data Sheet Date" field.
5. Enter the version of the safety data sheet in the "Safety Data Sheet Version" field.
6. Enter your auditor's information in the "Auditor Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Information
* **Company Information (company_info)**: Enter your company's information, such as your company's name and address.

### Safety Data Sheet Number
* **Safety Data Sheet Number (safety_data_sheet_number)**: Enter the unique identifier for the safety data sheet.

### Material Information
* **Material Information (material_info)**: Select all the materials that are present in the safety data sheet, such as materials that are hazardous or have other properties of concern.

### Safety Data Sheet Date
* **Safety Data Sheet Date (safety_data_sheet_date)**: Enter the date when the safety data sheet was issued or updated.

### Safety Data Sheet Version
* **Safety Data Sheet Version (safety_data_sheet_version)**: Enter the version number of the safety data sheet.

### Auditor Information
* **Auditor Information (auditor_info)**: Enter your auditor's name and other relevant contact information.

## Tips
