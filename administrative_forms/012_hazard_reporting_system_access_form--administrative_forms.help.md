# Hazard Reporting System Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hazard Reporting System Access Form is used to report potential hazards that could impact business operations or employee safety. To access this system, please fill out the form with your employee information and the hazard information you wish to report.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields.
2.  Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
*   **Employee email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
*   **Employee phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
*   **First page section 1** (`first_page_section_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you wish to include in your hazard report.
*   **First page** (`first_page`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you wish to include in your hazard report.
