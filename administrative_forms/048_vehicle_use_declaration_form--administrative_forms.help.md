# Vehicle Use Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Use Declaration Form is used to declare the vehicle usage information of a vehicle owned by employees for official business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about the vehicle you are declaring.
2. Provide all necessary documentation, such as vehicle registration and insurance information.
3. Review and verify the accuracy of your submitted information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Vehicle Make (2)** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda, etc.
* **Vehicle Year (3)** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Number (4)** (`vehicle_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's license number.
* **Vehicle Registration Date (5)** (`vehicle_registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your vehicle was registered.
* **Vehicle Use Purpose (6)** (`vehicle_use_purpose`, `text`, optional): Describe the purpose for which you use your vehicle for official business purposes (e.g., client visits, deliveries, etc.). 
* **Vehicle Use Frequency (7)** (`vehicle_use_frequency`, `text`, optional): Describe how often you use your vehicle for official business purposes (e.g., daily, weekly, etc.). 
* **Vehicle Odometer Reading (8)** (`vehicle_odometer_reading`, `number`, optional): Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider (9)** (`vehicle_insurance_provider`, `text`, optional): Enter your vehicle's insurance provider's name.
* **Vehicle Insurance Expiration Date (10)** (`vehicle_insurance_expiration_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the expiration date of your vehicle's insurance.
* **Vehicle License Plate Number (11)** (`vehicle_license_plate_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Insurance Status (12)** (`vehicle_insurance_status`, `text`, optional): Describe the status of your vehicle's insurance (e.g., active, inactive, expired, etc.). 
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Date (13)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are declaring your vehicle for official business purposes.
* **Driver License Number (14)** (`driver_license_number`, `text`, required): Enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Status (15)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your vehicle use declaration (Active or Inactive).
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Comment (16)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_comment`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your vehicle use declaration.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Note (17)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your vehicle use declaration.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Email (18)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Phone (19)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Address (20)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration City (21)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration State (22)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Zip (23)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_zip`, `text`, optional): Enter your zip code for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Country (24)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_country`, `text`, optional): Enter your country for contact purposes.
* **Vehicle Use Declaration Comments (25)** (`vehicle_use_declaration_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your vehicle use declaration.
