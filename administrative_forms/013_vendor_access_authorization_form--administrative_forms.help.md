# Vendor Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Access Authorization Form is used to collect information from vendors to ensure that they have the necessary access levels to perform certain tasks or access specific resources on behalf of our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "company" field.
4. Select your role in the "role" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Select the start date of your access authorization in the "start_date" field.
8. Select the end date of your access authorization in the "end_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required/optional): Enter the specific role or position you hold at your company.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date when your access authorization begins.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date when your access authorization expires.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
* Please fill out the form completely to ensure a successful submission.
