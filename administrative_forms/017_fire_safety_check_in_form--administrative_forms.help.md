# Fire Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Check In Form is used to track the status of fire drills, check in employees and visitors, and note any relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, navigate through the different sections and fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. For each field, read the explanation below to understand its purpose and significance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Check in Employee Form
* **Check in Employee Form** (`checkin_employee_form`, text, required: false): This field is used to check in employees during the safety drill or check. Simply type in the name or ID of the employee you are checking in.

### Visitor Check in
* **Visitor Check in** (`visitor_form`, text, required: false): This field is used to check in visitors during the safety drill or check. Type in the name or ID of the visitor you are checking in.

### Employee
* **Employee** (`employee_form`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the details of the employee during the safety drill or check. Type in the employee's name and any other relevant information.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the contact number of the employee or visitor during the safety drill or check. Type in the contact number of the person being checked in.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the date of the safety drill or check. Select the date from the calendar provided.

### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): This field is used to record the time of the safety drill or check. Select the time from the clock provided.

### Location
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the location of the safety drill or check. Type in the location where the drill or check is taking place.

### Note
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): This field is used to record any additional notes or comments about the safety drill or check. Type in any relevant information that is not captured in other fields.

### Fire Drill Status
* **Fire Drill Status** (`fire_drill_status`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to record the status of the fire drill. Select 'On Site' or 'Off Site' to indicate whether the drill is taking place on or off site.

### Check In Form
* **Check In Form** (`checkin_form`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to record the type of check in performed. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether a check in was performed or not.

### Check In Form
* **Check In Form** (`checkin_form`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to record the type of check in performed. Select one of the options provided, such as 'Option 1', 'Option 2', or 'Option 3'.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use the calendar and clock provided to select the date and time of the safety drill or check.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this guide or consult with the relevant personnel.
