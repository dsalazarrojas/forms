# Worker Certification Completion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Certification Completion Report Form is a document used to report the completion of a worker's certification. It's used to track and record certifications held by workers in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Date of Birth
	* Certification Date
	* Certification Provider
	* Certification Number
	* Completion Date
	* Certification Type
	* Completion Status
	* Certification Status
	* Email
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
	* Job Title
	* Supervisor Name
	* Supervision Phone
	* Supervisor Email
	* Training Hours
	* Certification Hours
	* Certification Hours Units
	* Training Programs
	* Notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Last Name** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`<date>`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Job Title** (`<job_title>`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title, if applicable.
* **Supervisor Name** (`<supervisor_name>`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`<email>`, `email`, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`<supervisor_phone>`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`<date>`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Certification Date** (`<date>`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your certification.
* **Certification Provider** (`<certification_provider>`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the certification provider.
* **Certification Number** (`<certification_number>`, `text`, optional): Enter your certification number.
* **Completion Date** (`<date>`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the certification.
* **Certification Type** (`<certification_type>`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of certification you completed.
* **Training Hours** (`<training_hours>`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the number of training hours required or completed.
* **Certification Hours** (`<certification_hours>`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the number of certification hours.
* **Certification Hours Units** (`<certification_hours_units>`, `text`, optional): Enter the units for the certification hours, if applicable.
* **Training Programs** (`<training_programs>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the training programs you've completed, if applicable.
* **Completion Status** (`<completion_status>`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your completion.
* **Certification Status** (`<certification_status>`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of your certification.
* **Notes** (`<notes>`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your certification.
* **Email** (`<email>`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`<phone>`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Certification URL** (`<certification_url>`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of your certification.
* **Supervisor Title** (`<supervisor_title>`, `text`, optional): Enter your supervisor's title.
