# Board Meeting Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm that the necessary tasks are completed before the board meeting. It is a checklist to ensure that the meeting is well-prepared and runs smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title as the person completing the checklist.
2. Select the date of the meeting.
3. Confirm whether the agenda has been distributed to all members.
4. Check if all necessary materials are prepared.
5. Verify that the meeting room has been booked.
6. Confirm that the audio-visual equipment is ready for use.
7. Confirm whether refreshments have been arranged (if applicable).
8. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Preparation Checklist
* **-- Preparation Checklist --** (`prep_header`, `note`, required: false): This is a header section for the preparation checklist. It is not a required field and does not require an input.

### Coordinator name
* **Coordinator name** (`coordinator_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter your name and title as the person completing the checklist.

### Meeting date
* **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: true): Select the date of the meeting from the calendar.

### Agenda distributed
* **Agenda distributed** (`agenda_distributed`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm if the agenda has been sent to all members.

### Materials prepared
* **Materials prepared** (`materials_prepared`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm if all necessary materials are prepared.

### Room booked
* **Room booked** (`room_booked`, `select_one`, required: true): Verify that the meeting room has been booked.

### Audio-Visual equipment checked
* **Audio-Visual equipment checked** (`av_checked`, `select_one`, required: true): Confirm that the audio-visual equipment is ready for use.

### Refreshments arranged
* **Refreshments arranged** (`refreshments_arranged`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if refreshments have been arranged. This field is not required.

### Additional notes
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the meeting preparation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please consult with the meeting coordinator or chairperson.
* This form is meant to be completed before the meeting to help ensure that everything is in order.
