# Safety Incident Report Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report safety incidents that have occurred within the organization. It allows employees to submit reports of incidents that have happened, providing details about the incident, including the date, location, and any relevant details. The form is used to gather information for internal use and is a requirement for reporting safety incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Incident Date** of when the incident occurred.
2. Provide your **Employee Name** and **Employee ID** as required for tracking and reference purposes.
3. In the **Incident Description** field, provide a brief and detailed description of what happened.
4. If the incident occurred at a specific location, enter the **Incident Location** in the corresponding field.
5. Specify the **Incident Time** when the incident occurred.
6. Indicate if there is a **Reporting Officer** assigned to the incident.
7. Select all applicable **Incident Categories** that apply to the incident.
8. Choose the level of **Incident Severity** (e.g., Minor, Major, etc.).
9. Specify the **Incident Status** as either Active or Inactive.
10. Provide any additional **Incident Resolution** details if applicable.
11. In the **Reporting Officer Note** field, enter any relevant comments or notes from the reporting officer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the employee who reported the incident.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your employee ID for tracking and reference purposes.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief and detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if there is a reporting officer assigned to the incident.
* **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable categories that apply to the incident.
* **Incident Severity** (`incident_severity`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the level of incident severity (e.g., minor, major, etc.).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required/optional): Specify the incident status as Active or Inactive.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the resolution of the incident.
* **Reporting Officer Note** (`reporting_officer_note`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes from the reporting officer.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information about the incident to ensure proper reporting and reference.
* If unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR department.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to prevent incomplete submissions.
* Save the form for later review and revision as needed.
