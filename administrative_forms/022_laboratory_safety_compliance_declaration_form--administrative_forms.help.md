# Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Safety Compliance Declaration Form is a mandatory form for laboratory employees to declare their compliance with laboratory safety protocols. It is used to track the safety compliance status of laboratory employees and ensures that all employees follow the required safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each field that applies to you.
2. Fill in your employee name, laboratory title, and supervisor name in their respective fields.
3. Select the safety protocol version that you are currently using.
4. Enter the date of the last review of the safety protocols.
5. Enter the date of the next review of the safety protocols.
6. Select your safety declaration status (Active or Inactive).
7. Fill in any additional comments if necessary.
8. If not required, you can skip this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Laboratory Safety Declaration** (`laboratory_safety_declaration`): Choose whether you have declared your compliance with laboratory safety protocols.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name in this field.
* **Laboratory Title** (`laboratory_title`): Enter your title within the laboratory.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`): Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Safety Protocol Version** (`safety_protocol_version`): Select the safety protocol version that you are currently using.
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`): Enter the date of the last review of the safety protocols.
* **Date of Next Review** (`date_of_next_review`): Enter the date of the next review of the safety protocols.
* **Safety Declaration Status** (`safety_declaration_status`): Select whether your safety declaration is Active or Inactive.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments that you would like to include.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Sign the form to declare your compliance.
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`): If a visitor is present, sign the form to declare their compliance.
* **Visitor Signiture Date** (`visitor_signiture_date`): Enter the date that the visitor signed the form.
* **Employee Signature Date** (`employee_signiture_date`): Enter the date that you signed the form.
