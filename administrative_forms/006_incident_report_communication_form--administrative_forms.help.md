# incident_report_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The incident report communication form is used to collect and document information on incidents that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the incident date and time fields with the relevant information.
- Provide a detailed description of the incident in the incident description field.
- Select the location where the incident occurred from the options provided.
- Choose the status of the incident from the available options.
- Identify the person who reported the incident and the parties affected by it.
- If applicable, provide the name of the person investigating the incident.
- Include any additional notes about the incident in the incident notes field.
- Enter the email and phone number (if available) of the incident reporter or affected party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, Date, Not Required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, Time, Not Required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Text, Not Required): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, Select One, Not Required): Select the location where the incident occurred from the options provided.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, Select One, Not Required): Choose the status of the incident from the available options.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, Text, Not Required): Identify the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Affected Party** (`incident_affected_party`, Text, Not Required): Identify the party affected by the incident.
* **Incident Investigated By** (`incident_investigated_by`, Text, Not Required): If applicable, provide the name of the person investigating the incident.
* **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, Note, Not Required): Include any additional notes about the incident.
* **Incident Email** (`incident_email`, Email, Not Required): Enter the email address of the incident reporter or affected party (if available).
* **Incident Phone** (`incident_phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter the phone number of the incident reporter or affected party (if available).
