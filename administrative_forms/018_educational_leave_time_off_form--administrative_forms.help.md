# Educational Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request time off for educational purposes, such as attending conferences, workshops, or courses. It's a simple way to notify your educational institution or relevant authorities about your leave.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user name in the "User name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter the start date of your leave in the "Start date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your leave in the "End date" field.
5. Describe the reason for your leave in the "Reason for leave" field.
6. If applicable, provide the name and details of your educational institution in the "Educational institution" field.
7. Add any relevant course details in the "Course details" field.
8. If your leave has a specific return date, enter it in the "Return date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User name**: Enter your full name as it is known in the educational institution.
* **email**: Provide your institutional email address for communication purposes.
* **Start date**: Enter the date you want to begin your educational leave.
* **End date**: Enter the date you want to end your educational leave.
* **Reason for leave**: Describe the educational activities you will be attending or participating in during your leave.
* **Educational institution**: If you are a student, enter the name of your institution. If you are a staff member, enter your institutional affiliation.
* **Course details**: Add any relevant course details, such as course name, number, or description.
* **Return date**: If your leave has a set return date, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when providing course details to ensure your leave request is approved.
* If you have any supporting documents, such as a course certificate, be prepared to provide them upon request.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
