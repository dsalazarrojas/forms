# vendor_onboarding_approval_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for the onboarding process of a new vendor. It is intended to be completed by the vendor or their representative and submitted to the internal review team for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the onboarding process.
* Fill out the vendor information section (fields 1-4) with accurate and up-to-date details.
* In the contact info section (field 5), provide the best contact information for the vendor or their representative.
* In the billing info section (field 6), provide billing details for the vendor.
* In the vendor rep and manager section (fields 7-8), provide the name and contact information of the vendor's representative and manager.
* In the email and phone section (fields 9-10), provide the email and phone number of the vendor.
* In the vendor address section (fields 11-12), provide the address of the vendor (including city and state or country and ZIP).
* In the country field (field 13), select the country of operation for the vendor.
* In the state field (field 14), select the state of operation for the vendor.
* In the notes section (field 15), provide any additional information that may be helpful for the review process.
* Finally, review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Info (Field 1)**: This field is used to enter the name and other identifying information of the vendor.
* **Contact Info (Field 2)**: This field is used to enter the contact information of the vendor or their representative.
* **Billing Info (Field 3)**: This field is used to enter the billing information of the vendor.
* **Vendor Representative (Field 4)**: This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the vendor's representative.
* **Vendor Manager (Field 5)**: This field is used to enter the name and contact information of the vendor's manager.
* **Email (Field 6)**: This field is used to enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Phone (Field 7)**: This field is used to enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address (Field 8)**: This field is used to enter the address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Address 2 (Field 9)**: This field is used to enter any additional address information for the vendor.
* **Vendor City (Field 10)**: This field is used to enter the city of operation for the vendor.
* **ZIP (Field 11)**: This field is used to enter the ZIP code of the vendor.
* **Country (Field 12)**: This field is used to select the country of operation for the vendor.
* **State (Field 13)**: This field is used to select the state of operation for the vendor.
* **Notes (Field 14)**: This field is used to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the review process.
* **Submit (Field 15)**: This field is used to submit the form for approval.
