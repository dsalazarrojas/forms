# Workplace Safety Training Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a workplace safety training program, aiming to obtain their waiver and acknowledgments for safety procedures, emergency contact information, and any potential medical conditions that may impact their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the steps below and fill in the required information on each page:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information**: (`participant_information`, `text`, required: false)
    This field is for you to provide any relevant details about yourself that will be used for internal records.
*   **Acknowledgments**: (`waiver_acknowledgments`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
    Select the options that apply to you, acknowledging the waiver and risks associated with participating in the safety training program.
*   **Safety Procedures**: (`safety_procedures`, `text`, required: false)
    Describe any safety procedures that may impact your participation or pose a risk to you or others during the training session.
*   **Emergency Contact**: (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false)
    List the name and contact information for the person or emergency contact that we may reach in case of an emergency.
*   **Medical Condition**: (`medical_condition`, `note`, required: false)
    If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the training session or pose a risk to yourself or others, please note them here.
*   **Signed**: (`signed`, `text`, required: false)
    Indicate that you have read and understand this waiver form and are signing it of your own free will.
*   **Employee Information**: (`employee_information`, `text`, required: false)
    Enter any additional employee information required for internal records.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have carefully read and understood this waiver form before signing it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
*   This form is a mandatory requirement for participation in our safety training program.
*   We value your health and safety and appreciate your cooperation in filling out this form.
