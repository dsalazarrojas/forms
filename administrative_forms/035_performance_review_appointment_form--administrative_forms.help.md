# Performance Review Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Review Appointment Form is used to schedule appointments for performance reviews between employees and their managers. This form is designed to be completed by employees to request a performance review meeting with their manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a date for the performance review meeting in the "Performance Review Date" field.
2. Choose a time for the meeting in the "Review Time" field.
3. Select the manager who will be conducting the review in the "Manager Reviewer" field.
4. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
5. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
6. Select the location where the meeting will take place in the "Meeting Location" field.
7. Select the date of the meeting in the "Meeting Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Appointment Form** (`appointment_form`): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the appointment.
* **Performance Review Date** (`performance_review_date`): Select a date for the performance review meeting. Choose a date that works best for you and your manager.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`): Choose a time for the meeting that suits you and your manager.
* **Manager Reviewer** (`manager_reviewer`): Select the manager who will be conducting the performance review. Please choose one of the available options.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your email address as it appears in our records.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): This field is for internal use only. Please do not fill this field unless you are a manager.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Select the location where the meeting will take place. Choose a location that is convenient for both you and your manager.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`): Select the date of the meeting. This should be the same as the "Performance Review Date" field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select a date and time that works for both you and your manager.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule the meeting, please contact your manager directly.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your appointment is scheduled correctly.
