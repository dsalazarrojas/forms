# work_hours_tracking_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and verify work hours for employees, and is likely used for payroll or time-tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant information into the designated fields.
2. Select the correct options from the select fields.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours worked.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, select multiple, required: false): Select the employees that this form applies to (e.g. Employee 1, Employee 2, Employee 3).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the hours were worked.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the hours were worked.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the hours worked.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, number, required: false): This field is currently not clear in its purpose. It might be used for manager's approval or validation.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, number, required: false): Similar to the manager signature, this field is also not clear in its purpose.
* **Date Signature** (`date_signature`, date, required: false): This field is likely used for date validation or approval.
* **Time Signature** (`time_signature`, time, required: false): This field might be used for time validation or approval.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes from the manager.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes from the employee.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to submit the form for validation or approval, or 'No' to cancel or save draft.
