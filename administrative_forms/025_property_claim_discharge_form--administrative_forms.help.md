# Property Claim Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to discharge or cancel a property claim, providing a clear and concise way to document the claim's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the policyholder's information: Enter the policyholder's name, email, and phone number, as found on their policy contract.
2. Enter the claim number: Type the claim number assigned to the property claim.
3. Update the claim status: Select the current status of the claim from the options provided.
4. Add a description (optional): Provide a brief description of the claim, if necessary.
5. Sign and date the discharge date (optional): Enter the date of the claim's discharge.
6. Obtain signatures: The policyholder, adjuster, and insurance company representative must sign the form to confirm the claim's discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Name** (`policyholder_name`, text, required):
    Enter the policyholder's name as it appears on their policy contract.
* **Policyholder Email** (`policyholder_email`, email, optional): 
    Enter the policyholder's email address (if available).
* **Policyholder Phone** (`policyholder_phone`, text, optional): 
    Enter the policyholder's phone number (if available).
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, text, required): 
    Type the claim number assigned to the property claim.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional):
    Select the current status of the claim from:
    *   Approved
    *   Reopened
    *   Denied
    *   Pending
* **Claim Description** (`claim_description`, text, optional):
    Provide a brief description of the claim, if necessary (e.g., reason for claim cancellation).
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): 
    Enter the date of the claim's discharge.
* **Policyholder Signature** (`policyholder_signature`, note, optional):
    The policyholder must sign this form to confirm their agreement with the claim's discharge status.
* **Adjuster Signature** (`adjuster_signature`, note, optional): 
    The adjuster must sign this form to confirm their agreement with the claim's discharge status.
* **Insurance Company Signature** (`insurance_company_signature`, note, optional):
    The representative of the insurance company must sign this form to confirm their agreement with the claim's discharge status.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
