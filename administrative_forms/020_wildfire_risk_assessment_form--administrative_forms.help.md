# wildfire_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reporting a wildfire risk assessment. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the information is useful for wildfire risk management and mitigation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Select all relevant options for each field.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "notes" field.
5. Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, optional): A brief introduction to the wildfire risk assessment.
* **land_use** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all land use categories that apply to the area being assessed. (e.g., urban, rural, industrial, etc.)
* **vegetation** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of vegetation that is present in the area being assessed. (e.g., trees, grass, brush, etc.)
* **topography** (`4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all topography types that apply to the area being assessed. (e.g., flat, sloping, etc.)
* **wind** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the wind speed category that applies to the area being assessed. (e.g., low, moderate, etc.)
* **weather** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all weather types that apply to the area being assessed. (e.g., dry, wet, etc.)
* **water_source** (`7`, `select_one`, optional): Select the water source category that applies to the area being assessed. (e.g., river, lake, etc.)
* **previous_wildfires** (`8`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all previous wildfire occurrences in the area being assessed. (e.g., yes, no, etc.)
* **fire_protocols** (`9`, `text`, required): Provide any fire protocols or procedures that apply to the area being assessed.
* **mitigation_strategies** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all mitigation strategies that apply to the area being assessed. (e.g., Good, Fair, etc.)
* **contact_info** (`11`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **submit** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the form submission.
* **notes** (`13`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the area being assessed.
* **email** (`14`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **submit_button** (`16`, `text`, optional): This field is not used for form submission.
* **fire_department** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select if a local fire department is involved.
* **fire_department_contact** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information of the local fire department.
* **fire_department_phone** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the local fire department.
* **fire_department_email** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of the local fire department.
* **submit_form** (`21`, `text`, optional): This field is not used for form submission.
* **fire_department_address** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the local fire department.
* **submit_button** (`23`, `text`, optional): This field is not used for form submission.
* **emergency_contact** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact information.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
