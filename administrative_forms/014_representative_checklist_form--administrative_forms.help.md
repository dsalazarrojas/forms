# Representative Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Representative Checklist Form is used to collect information about a representative assigned to perform specific tasks or duties on behalf of an organization. It helps to ensure that the representative is aware of their responsibilities, provides a clear record of their role and involvement, and facilitates communication and accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand the purpose and the information it requires.
2. Complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information about the representative.
3. Verify that all required fields are filled in according to the form's instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Representative Checklist Form** (`representative_checklist_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the representative's role and responsibilities.
* **Representative Checklist** (`representative_checklist`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to the representative's role, e.g., "Yes" if the representative is responsible for onboarding a new employee, "No" if not.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the representative's start or end date (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the representative's start or end time (if applicable).
* **Representative Name** (`representative_name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the representative.
* **Representative's Email** (`representative_email`, email, optional): Enter the representative's email address.
* **Representative's Phone** (`representative_phone`, text, optional): Enter the representative's phone number.
* **Representative's Address** (`representative_address`, text, optional): Enter the representative's address (if applicable).

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If a field is not relevant to the representative's role, you may leave it blank.
* Ensure that the date and time entered are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM AM/PM).
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the relevant department or team.
