# Employee Time Log Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request adjustments to an employee's time log. It is used to document reasons for changes to an employee's time log, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records of work hours.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the employee whose time log you wish to adjust and fill out the form with the necessary information.
2. Provide the correct time log entry that needs adjustment.
3. Enter a brief reason for the adjustment.
4. Enter the date of the adjustment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's name.
* **Time Log (2)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the time log entry that needs adjustment.
* **Reason (3)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief reason for the adjustment.
* **Date (4)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the adjustment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, consult with the relevant department or supervisor.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
