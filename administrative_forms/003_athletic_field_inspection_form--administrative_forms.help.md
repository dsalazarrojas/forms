# Athletic Field Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Field Inspection Form is a tool to evaluate the condition of athletic fields, ensuring they are safe for play and providing recommendations for maintenance and repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date the inspection was conducted and enter it in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter your full name in the "Inspector Name" field.
3. Enter the field's identifier or location in the "Field Name" field.
4. Choose the sport played on this field from the "Field Type" options.
5. Rate the condition of the field surface using the "Playing Surface Condition" options.
6. Evaluate the drainage of the field using the "Drainage Assessment" options.
7. Assess the grass/turf condition using the "Grass/Turf Condition" options.
8. Check the "Surface Hazards Identified" box for any present hazards.
9. Rate the severity of identified hazards using the "Hazard Severity Level" options.
10. Evaluate the condition of equipment using the "Equipment Condition" options.
11. Check the "Safety Violations Found" box for any present safety issues.
12. Assess whether the field is clear of obstructions using the "Field Clearance Status" options.
13. Choose the current weather conditions during the inspection using the "Weather Conditions During Inspection" options.
14. Evaluate the condition of field markings using the "Field Markings Condition" options.
15. Rate the lighting adequacy for evening games using the "Lighting Adequacy" options.
16. Describe any recommended maintenance or repairs in the "Maintenance Recommendations" field.
17. List any urgent repairs required in the "Urgent Repairs Needed" field.
18. Choose whether the field should be closed to use using the "Field Closure Recommendation" options.
19. Check if photographic documents are attached using the "Photos Attached" options.
20. Enter the recommended follow-up inspection date in the "Recommended Follow-up Inspection Date" field.
21. Enter the date of inspector's signature in the "Inspector Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required): Date the field inspection was conducted.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Full name of person conducting inspection.
* **Field Name** (`field_name`, text, required): Field identifier or location.
* **Field Type** (`field_type`, select_one, required): Sport played on this field (e.g. Baseball, Football, Soccer).
* **Playing Surface Condition** (`surface_condition`, select_one, required): Rate the overall condition of the field surface.
* **Drainage Assessment** (`drainage_assessment`, select_one, required): How well does water drain from the field?
* **Grass/Turf Condition** (`grass_condition`, select_one, required): Assess vegetation coverage and health.
* **Surface Hazards Identified** (`surface_hazards`, select_multiple, optional): Check all hazards observed.
* **Hazard Severity Level** (`hazard_severity`, select_one, required): How severe are identified hazards?
* **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, select_one, required): Condition of goals, nets, bleachers, etc.
* **Safety Violations Found** (`safety_violations`, select_multiple, optional): Check any safety issues observed.
* **Field Clearance Status** (`field_clearance`, select_one, required): Is the field clear of obstructions?
* **Weather Conditions During Inspection** (`weather_conditions`, select_one, optional): Current weather at time of inspection.
* **Field Markings Condition** (`markings_condition`, select_one, required): Assess condition of paint, lines, and field markings.
* **Lighting Adequacy** (`lighting_inspection`, select_one, optional): Rate field lighting for evening games.
* **Maintenance Recommendations** (`maintenance_recommendations`, text, optional): Describe recommended repairs or maintenance.
* **Urgent Repairs Needed** (`urgent_repairs`, text, optional): List critical repairs required.
* **Field Closure Recommendation** (`field_closure_recommendation`, select_one, required): Should the field be closed to use?
* **Photos Attached** (`photos_attached`, select_one, optional): Were photographic documents included?
* **Recommended Follow-up Inspection Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): When should the next inspection occur?
* **Inspector Signature Date** (`inspector_signature_date`, date, optional): Date of inspector's signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form thoroughly and honestly.
* Attach any relevant photographs or documents to support your evaluation.
* Ensure the form is signed and dated by the inspector.
* Review and follow up on any recommended maintenance or repairs.
* Schedule follow-up inspections as needed.
* Communicate with maintenance personnel or field administrators to ensure the field is properly maintained.
