# Weekly Team Reflection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Team Reflection form is used to collect information from team members on their goals, availability, and other important details. This form helps the team to stay up-to-date with each other's progress and ensures that the team is working cohesively towards a common goal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select all relevant team members who have reported weekly goals
* Choose whether each team member has reported weekly goals (Yes/No)
* Choose a date for the reflection
* Enter a time for the reflection
* Enter the email of the team member (if applicable)
* Enter the phone number of the team member (if applicable)
* Add any additional note about the team member

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select the team members who have reported weekly goals. You can choose multiple team members from the options provided.
* **Weekly Goals** (`weekly_goals`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to confirm whether each selected team member has reported weekly goals. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' based on your knowledge.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the reflection you are submitting. This field is essential for tracking historical data.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the reflection. This field helps to track the timing of the reflection.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you have an email address for the team member, enter it here. This field is useful for contacting the team member.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number for the team member, enter it here. This field is helpful for emergency contact purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional information about the team member in this field. You can write down important details that may require further action or discussion.

## Tips
* Always make sure to select the correct team members who have reported weekly goals.
* Confirm the correctness of the team member's reported goals.
* Enter accurate dates and times for the reflection.
* Enter accurate email and phone numbers for team members.
* Use the note field to add any essential details that may require further action or discussion.
