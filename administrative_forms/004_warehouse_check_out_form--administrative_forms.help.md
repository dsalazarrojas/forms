# Warehouse Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Warehouse Check Out Form is used to document and track activities related to issuing and returning items from the warehouse. It collects information about the user, item, quantity, date issued and returned, and other relevant details for administrative and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID to identify yourself as the person issuing or returning the item.
2. Enter the Item ID to specify the item being issued or returned.
3. Enter the quantity of the item being issued or returned.
4. Enter the date the item was issued, if applicable.
5. Enter the date the item is expected to be returned, if applicable.
6. Provide your User Name to identify yourself as the person completing the form.
7. Enter any additional Notes regarding the issue or return of the item, if necessary.
8. Enter your Email address to ensure timely communication.
9. Enter your Phone number for any follow-up contact.
10. Select the person the item is assigned to, if applicable, from the options of Manager, Supervisor, or Team Lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (1, number, required: false): Enter your unique User ID to identify yourself as the person issuing or returning the item.
* **Item ID** (2, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the item being issued or returned.
* **Quantity** (3, number, required: false): Enter the number of items being issued or returned.
* **Date Issued** (4, date, required: false): Enter the date the item was issued, if applicable.
* **Return Date** (5, date, required: false): Enter the date the item is expected to be returned, if applicable.
* **User Name** (6, text, required: false): Provide your full name as the person completing the form.
* **Notes** (7, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details regarding the issue or return of the item, if necessary.
* **Email** (8, email, required: false): Enter your Email address for timely communication.
* **Phone** (9, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Assigned To** (10, select_one, required: false): Select the person the item is assigned to from the options of Manager, Supervisor, or Team Lead.
