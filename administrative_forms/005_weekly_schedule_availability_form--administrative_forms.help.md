# weekly_schedule_availability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your weekly schedule availability for meetings, which will help in scheduling meetings effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your meeting availability information by filling in the required fields.
2. Ensure you provide your start time and end time accurately.
3. Specify your meeting duration in hours.
4. Select your meeting frequency from the options provided.
5. Choose the type of meeting you are available for from the options provided.
6. If you have any additional comments or special requests, use the "Meeting Availability Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Availability** (`meeting_availability`, text, optional): Describe your meeting availability in a concise manner, including any specific dates or times you are free for meetings.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your availability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your availability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of your meetings in hours (e.g., 1, 2, etc.).
- **Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of your meetings from the options provided (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly).
- **Other (Specify)** (`meeting_frequency_other`, text, optional): If your meeting frequency is not listed, use this field to specify your frequency (e.g., Quarterly, Bi-Monthly, etc.).
- **Type of Meeting** (`meeting_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of meeting you are available for from the options provided (Meetings, Calls, or Interviews).
- **Meeting Availability Comments** (`meeting_availability_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests you have regarding your meeting availability.
