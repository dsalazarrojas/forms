# Misconduct Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Misconduct Complaint Form is a document used for reporting incidents of misconduct within the organization. It helps gather information and track progress of investigations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Reportee Details accurately, ensuring that you are reporting on yourself or the person affected by the incident.
2. Choose the correct Nature of Complaint that best describes the incident.
3. Provide a detailed Description of the Incident, including relevant details without unnecessary information.
4. If relevant, add any Supporting Evidence to back up your complaint.
5. Mention any Witnesses who may have seen or been involved in the incident.
6. Check the Investigation Status to ensure it's set to 'Active' if it's still ongoing.
7. Finally, choose the Investigation Outcome once it's complete (default is 'No' for now).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reportee Details (required)**: A brief description of the person being reported on (yourself or the person affected).
* **Nature of Complaint (required)**: Choose one or multiple options that best describe the type of complaint (e.g. Harassment, Abuse, etc.).
* **Description of Incident (required)**: A detailed account of what happened, including dates, times, and relevant locations.
* **Supporting Evidence (optional)**: Add any relevant documents, images, or other evidence that supports your complaint.
* **Witnesses (optional)**: If any witnesses were present during the incident, list their names and contact information.
* **Investigation Status (default: Active)**: Set to 'Active' if the investigation is still ongoing, or 'Inactive' once it's complete.
* **Investigation Outcome (default: No)**: Choose 'Yes' if the investigation found evidence supporting your complaint, or 'No' if the outcome was not found in favor of the complainant.
