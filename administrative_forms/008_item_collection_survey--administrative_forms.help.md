# Item Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users collect and record information about items in your collection, including its name, description, quantity, and price.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the main page and enter any general information that doesn't fit into the other fields.
2. For each item, fill in the item name field (1) to provide a brief description of the item.
3. Enter the item's description in the item description field (2).
4. Enter the quantity of the item in the item quantity field (3).
5. Enter the price of the item in the item price field (4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **main-page** (1, text, required: false): The main page is a general information field for any item-specific details that don't fit into the other categories.
* **item name** (2, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the item, such as its common name or model number.
* **item description** (3, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the item, including any notable features or characteristics.
* **item quantity** (4, number, required: false): Enter the number of items you have in your collection.
* **item price** (5, number, required: false): Enter the price of each item in your collection, if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If an item has multiple items with different prices, you can fill in the item price field with the average or highest price.
* If you're unsure about any field, use your best judgment or ask your supervisor/colleague for clarification.
