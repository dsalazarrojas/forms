# third_party_vendor_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from third-party vendors for accessing our system. It is essential for us to have accurate and up-to-date contact details for our vendors so we can manage access to our system effectively. This form is used to create or update vendor records in our system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the correct information.
2. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the third-party vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the third-party vendor.
* **Vendor Phone Number** (`vendor_phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the third-party vendor.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the third-party vendor.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of access you want to grant to the vendor. Options are:
	+ Read-Only
	+ Read-Write
	+ Full Access
* **Access Expiration Date** (`access_expiration_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the access will expire (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Access Expiration Time** (`access_expiration_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when the access will expire.
* **Vendor Representative Name** (`vendor_representative_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the representative for the vendor.
* **Vendor Representative Email** (`vendor_representative_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the representative for the vendor.
* **Vendor Representative Phone Number** (`vendor_representative_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the representative for the vendor.
