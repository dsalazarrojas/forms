# Payroll Overpayment Resolution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Overpayment Resolution Request Form is used to request an overpayment for an employee's past due wages. This form is used to resolve discrepancies or errors in an employee's payroll.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's name as it appears on their pay stub or personnel file.
2. Enter the amount of the overpayment being requested.
3. Provide the date of the overpayment.
4. Select the department responsible for the overpayment from the available options.
5. Enter the reason for the overpayment.
6. Add any additional comments or details about the overpayment.
7. Sign the form digitally to confirm you are submitting the request.
8. The manager will review and sign the form, indicating approval of the overpayment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who is owed the overpayment.
* **Overpayment Amount** (`overpayment_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total amount of the overpayment being requested.
* **Date of Overpayment** (`date_of_overpayment`, date, optional): Enter the date of when the overpayment was made or should have been made.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department responsible for the overpayment from the available options.
* **Reason for Overpayment** (`reason_for_overpayment`, text, optional): Enter a brief explanation of the reason for the overpayment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the overpayment.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form digitally to confirm you are submitting the request.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, email, optional): A manager will review and sign the form, indicating approval of the overpayment.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, time, optional): The time and date the manager approved the overpayment. This field will be automatically populated by the system once approved.
