# Auto Insurance Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Insurance Discharge Form is used to request the cancellation of an insurance policy. This form is designed to collect necessary information to facilitate a smooth and secure cancellation process. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your policyholder ID and details.
2. Enter your policy number for the specific policy you wish to cancel.
3. Provide your contact email and phone number for confirmation purposes.
4. Choose a reason for cancelling your policy.
5. If switching providers, enter the name of your new insurance company.
6. Select the method for refunding any unused premium.
7. Enter the address where you'd like to receive any final documents or refunds.
8. Confirm that you understand the policy cancellation will result in a lapse in coverage.
9. Authorize the cancellation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Policyholder Identification --**
	+ **Policyholder Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your insurance documents.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the unique ID for the policy you wish to cancel.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for confirmation of cancellation.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **-- Discharge Details --**
* **Requested Discharge Date**: Choose the date you want the coverage to end.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Select the reason for cancelling your policy from the list:
	+ Switching Providers
	+ Vehicle Sold
	+ Vehicle Scrapped
	+ Moving Out of State
	+ Policy no longer needed
	+ Other
* **New Insurance Provider (if switching)**: If you're cancelling to switch to a new provider, enter their name.
* **-- Transition Information --**
* **Preferred Refund Method**: Choose how you'd like to receive any unused premium:
	+ Credit to Original Card
	+ Check by Mail
	+ Electronic Transfer (ACH)
* **Forwarding Address for Refund**: Enter the address where you'd like to receive any final documents or refunds.
* **Confirm Coverage Lapse Understanding**: Confirm that you understand the cancellation will result in a lapse in coverage.
* **Authorize Discharge**: Confirm that you're authorizing the cancellation of your policy.

Note: The "New Insurance Provider" field is only required if you're cancelling to switch to a new provider. The "Preferred Refund Method" and "Forwarding Address for Refund" fields are only required if you're requesting a refund.
