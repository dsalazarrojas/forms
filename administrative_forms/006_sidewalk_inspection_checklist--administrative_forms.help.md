# Sidewalk Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct thorough inspections of sidewalks to identify any issues or defects. It is designed to be used in various settings, such as schools, commercial properties, or public areas, to document the condition of sidewalks and ensure their safety and accessibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the questions and fields it contains.
2. Conduct a thorough inspection of the sidewalk area, taking note of any issues or defects.
3. Answer the questions and fill in the corresponding fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
4. Review your answers and ensure that you have completed all the necessary fields.
