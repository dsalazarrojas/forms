# work_authorization_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain work authorization for employees or contractors to work on specific projects or tasks. It's a critical document that ensures the necessary permissions and approvals are in place before commencing work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting this form.
2. Review each field carefully and enter accurate information.
3. Fill in the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Details**: Enter your current employment status, job title, supervisor's name, and any other relevant details about your employment.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date you started working with your current employer.
* **Employment End Date**: Enter the date you ended your employment with your current employer (if applicable).
* **Work Location**: Enter the location where you work or performed tasks for your employer.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Supervisor Supplied Signature**: This field is reserved for the supervisor's signature (not required).
* **Supervisor Date**: Enter the date the supervisor signed the form (if applicable).
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of the supervisor who signed the form (if applicable).
* **Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Employee**: Enter your name (as the employee or contractor).
* **Contractor**: Check this option if you are a contractor, not an employee.
* **Client**: Enter the name of the client (if applicable).
* **Authorized to Work**: Select whether you are authorized to work (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Work Authorization**: Enter any additional comments or details about your work authorization.
* **Authorization Ending Date**: Enter the date when your work authorization will end (if applicable).
* **Authorization Ending Time**: Enter the time when your work authorization will end (if applicable).
* **Work Status Ending**: Select your current work status when the authorization ends from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Reason for Termination**: Select the reason for the termination of your work authorization from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

Remember to submit the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring all necessary information is included.
