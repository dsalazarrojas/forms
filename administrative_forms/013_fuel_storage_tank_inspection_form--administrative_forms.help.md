# Fuel Storage Tank Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fuel Storage Tank Inspection Form is designed to gather essential information about fuel storage tanks. This form helps inspectors document the status of each tank, which aids in maintaining the safety and efficiency of the fuel storage facility.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out the **Form Heading** field, which should be a brief description of the tank or inspection area.
- Next, enter the **Tank ID** and **Tank Capacity** fields with the corresponding numbers to identify and describe the tank.
- In the **Tank Contents** field, describe the current contents of the tank.
- Specify the **Tank Location** in the corresponding field.
- Provide the **Inspection Frequency** and **Maintenance Frequency** in number of days or other time units.
- Choose the current **Tank Status** from the available options.
- If necessary, add comments or notes in the **Comments** field.
- Finally, select the date of form completion in the **Form Completion Date** field.
- Enter your **Inspector Name** for your identification and accountability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Heading** (`Form Heading`, text, required/optional): A brief description of the tank or inspection area.
* **Tank ID** (`tank_id`, number, required/optional): The identification number of the tank.
* **Tank Capacity** (`tank_capacity`, number, required/optional): The total capacity of the tank.
* **Tank Contents** (`tank_contents`, text, required/optional): A description of the current contents of the tank.
* **Tank Location** (`tank_location`, text, required/optional): The exact location of the tank.
* **Inspection Frequency** (`inspection_frequency`, number, required/optional): The frequency of the regular inspections in number of days or other units.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, number, required/optional): The frequency of required maintenance in number of days or other units.
* **Tank Status** (`tank_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the current status of the tank from the available options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Any additional comments or notes about the tank.
* **Form Completion Date** (`form_completion_date`, date, required/optional): The date of form completion.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required/optional): The name of the inspector responsible for the form completion.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely for reliable data.
- Use the available options for the **Tank Status** field for consistency.
- Ensure that the **Form Completion Date** reflects the actual date of form completion.
- Enter your name in the **Inspector Name** field for accountability.
