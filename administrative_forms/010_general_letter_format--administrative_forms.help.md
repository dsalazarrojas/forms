# general_letter_format - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a general letter format, typically used for official or formal communication between individuals or organizations. It is intended to collect information for a letter or a message to be sent to someone, including the sender's and receiver's details, a date, and the content of the letter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the sender's name in the "Sender Name" field.
2. Enter the receiver's name in the "Receiver Name" field.
3. Enter the receiver's address in the "Receiver Address" field.
4. Enter the sender's address in the "Sender Address" field.
5. Choose a date for the letter in the "Date" field.
6. Write the content of the letter in the "Body" field.
7. (Optional) Add a signature in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Greeting** (`greeting`, `text`, required: false): A brief introduction or salutation for the letter.
* **Sender Name** (`sender_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person sending the letter.
* **Receiver Name** (`receiver_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person receiving the letter.
* **Receiver Address** (`receiver_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the person receiving the letter.
* **Sender Address** (`sender_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the person sending the letter.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Choose a date for the letter.
* **Body** (`body`, `note`, required: false): Write the content of the letter.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Add a signature to the letter, if desired.
