# daily-restaurant-operations-report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The daily-restaurant-operations-report form is used to collect and report on daily sales data, employee information, and cash handling details for a specific restaurant location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the sales period you are reporting on.
2. Enter the name of the sales captain responsible for the sales period.
3. Choose the restaurant location being reported on.
4. Enter the sales region, if applicable.
5. Identify the sales cashier, sales shift manager, sales restaurant owner, restaurant manager, restaurant assistant manager, and restaurant superintendent, if applicable.
6. Report on the total hours worked by employees during the sales period.
7. Enter the start and end times of the sales shift.
8. Report on the total number of employees present during the sales shift.
9. Enter the amount of cash in, cash out, and cash remaining during the sales period.
10. Report on the tips and sales shift tips received during the sales period.
11. Enter the gross sales and net profit for the sales period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sales Date** (`sales_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the sales period being reported on.
* **Sales Captain** (`sales_captain`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales captain responsible for the sales period.
* **Restaurant Location** (`restaurant_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the restaurant location being reported on.
* **Restaurant Location** (`restaurant_location`, text, optional): This field is a duplicate of the previous field and is likely a mistake in the form design.
* **Sales Region** (`sales_region`, text, optional): Enter the sales region, if applicable.
* **Sales Cashier** (`sales_cashier`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales cashier responsible for the sales period.
* **Sales Shift Manager** (`sales_shift_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales shift manager.
* **Sales Restaurant Owner** (`sales_restaurant_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales restaurant owner.
* **Restaurant Manager** (`restaurant_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the restaurant manager.
* **Restaurant Assistant Manager** (`restaurant_assistant_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the restaurant assistant manager.
* **Restaurant Superintendent** (`restaurant_superintendent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the restaurant superintendent.
* **Sales Cash Handling** (`sales_cash_handling`, text, optional): Report on any issues or comments regarding cash handling during the sales period.
* **Sales Shift Cashier** (`sales_shift_cashier`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sales shift cashier.
* **Sales Total Shift Hours** (`sales_total_shift_hours`, number, optional): Report on the total hours worked by employees during the sales shift.
* **Sales Shift Start Time** (`sales_shift_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the sales shift.
* **Sales Shift End Time** (`sales_shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the sales shift.
* **Sales Total Employees** (`sales_total_employees`, number, optional): Report on the total number of employees present during the sales shift.
* **Sales Cash In** (`sales_cash_in`, number, optional): Enter the amount of cash in during the sales period.
* **Sales Cash Out** (`sales_cash_out`, number, optional): Enter the amount of cash out during the sales period.
* **Sales Cash Remaining** (`sales_cash_remaining`, number, optional): Enter the amount of cash remaining during the sales period.
* **Sales Tips** (`sales_tips`, number, optional): Report on any tips received during the sales period.
* **Sales Shift Tips** (`sales_shift_tips`, number, optional): Report on any shift tips received during the sales period.
* **Sales Gross Sales** (`sales_gross_sales`, number, optional): Enter the gross sales for the sales period.
* **Sales Net Profit** (`sales_net_profit`, number, optional): Enter the net profit for the sales period.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Make sure to enter the correct sales date and shift start and end times to ensure accurate reporting.
- If you have any issues or concerns about cash handling, report them in the Sales Cash Handling field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance with this form, contact your sales manager or restaurant management.
