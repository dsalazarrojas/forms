# Safety Plan Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to acknowledge and confirm their safety plans, including their completion and review dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Select the dates for Completion and Review.
3. Enter the name of the employee who completed the plan.
4. Enter the name of the supervisor or person who will review the plan.
5. Confirm when you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as driver's license or passport.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents, such as driver's license or passport.
* **Completion Date** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date when you completed the safety plan. If left blank, the current date will be used.
* **Review Date** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date when you reviewed the safety plan. If left blank, the current date will be used.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your employee name and ID number (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor who will review your safety plan.
* **Signed** (`date_signed`, time, optional): Confirm that you have signed the form, but this field is likely intended for tracking purposes only and does not necessarily need to be completed by the employee.

Note: This form is designed to be simple and straightforward. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
