# Fair Work Laws Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fair Work Laws Questionnaire is designed to gather information about employees' awareness and understanding of Fair Work Laws and Regulations and their experiences and perceptions of their workplaces' compliance with these laws.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question based on your actual experiences and perceptions.
2. Select the correct answer from the provided options.
3. For questions that ask for a numerical value, please provide the actual number.
4. If you are unsure or do not have an answer to a question, please select "False" or "Not Applicable" as the answer.
