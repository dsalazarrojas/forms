# Employee Handbook Distribution Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handbook Distribution Authorization Form is used for employees to authorize the distribution of the Employee Handbook, ensuring they receive essential information about company policies, procedures, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the correct option for "Employee Handbook Distributed" (i.e., which year's handbook to distribute).
3. If applicable, provide a reason for not distributing the handbook.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Sign and date the form to acknowledge receipt of the handbook.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Handbook Distribution** (`employee_handbook_distribution_form`): Enter your name or a unique identifier to authorize the distribution of the Employee Handbook.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`): Enter the date you received the Employee Handbook.
* **Employee Handbook Distributed** (`employee_handbook_distributed`): Select the year's handbook you are distributing (e.g., "Employee Handbook", "Employee Handbook 2022", "Employee Handbook 2021").
* **Reason for Not Distributing** (`reason_for_not_distributing`): If you are not distributing the handbook, select a reason from the available options (e.g., "Not Applicable", "No Interest", "Not Required").
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or explanations about your situation.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your work email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Acknowledged by** (`acknowledgement_by`): Enter your name for signature purposes.

Note: This form is for employee use only. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure you receive the necessary information from the Employee Handbook.
