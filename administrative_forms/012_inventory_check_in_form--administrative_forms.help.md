# Inventory Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Check In Form is a tool used to update the quantity of items in stock. This form is designed to be used by authorized personnel to accurately reflect the current state of the inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the item being checked in.
2. Provide a brief description of the item.
3. Enter the quantity of the item being checked in.
4. Choose whether the item belongs to a 'Yes' or 'No' category.
5. Select the subcategory for the item (if applicable).
6. Enter a detailed description of the item being checked in.
7. Enter the quantity of the item being checked in.
8. Click the 'submit' button when all fields are accurately filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text, required): Enter the title of the item being checked in.
* **description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the item.
* **quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the item being checked in.
* **category** (select one, required): Choose whether the item belongs to a 'Yes' or 'No' category.
* **subcategory** (select multiple, required): Select the subcategory for the item (if applicable).
* **item_description** (text, required): Enter a detailed description of the item being checked in.
* **item_quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of the item being checked in.
* **submit** (text, required): Click this button when all fields are accurately filled out.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid discrepancies in the inventory.
- Use the 'Yes' or 'No' category for items that fit the description.
- Select the correct subcategory (if applicable).
- Enter detailed descriptions for items with complex or unusual characteristics.
- Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
