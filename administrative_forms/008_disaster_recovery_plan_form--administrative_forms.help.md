# disaster_recovery_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for disaster recovery planning, including contact details, recovery planning, and services affected by a disaster.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
2. Ensure you have all necessary documents and materials readily available.
3. Complete the form according to your company's policies and procedures.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
5. Submit the form to the designated authority for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, title, department, phone number, and email address to enable communication and facilitate contact in case of an emergency.
* **Recovery Planning** (`recovery_planning`, `text`, required): Describe the steps taken to ensure business continuity and recovery in case of a disaster, including emergency preparedness, response, and recovery procedures.
* **Service 1** (`service1`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the service that best describes your role or department in the organization, from the list: Service A, Service B, Service C.
* **Service 2** (`service2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all services that are affected by the disaster, from the list: Service D, Service E.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations related to the services affected or recovery process.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and title to confirm your identity and authorization to submit the form.
* **Service 1** and **Service 2** are optional fields, as they are not required for the form to be considered complete.
* **Notes** and **Submitter** are also optional fields.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate in providing information.
* Follow company policies and procedures.
* If unsure about any field, ask for clarification or guidance.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid mistakes.
