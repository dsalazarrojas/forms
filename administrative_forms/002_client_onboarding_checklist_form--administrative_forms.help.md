# Client Onboarding Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for the client onboarding process, ensuring that all necessary steps are completed and that clients are properly set up for success. It is an essential tool for project managers and onboarding specialists to gather and organize vital details about clients and their projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all fields marked as "required"** to ensure that the onboarding process is thorough and accurate.
2. **Review each field carefully** to understand the specific information being requested.
3. **Use the correct format for date fields** (date) to input the required dates.
4. **Select the relevant option** for each field from the provided list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Company Name**: Enter the official name of the client's company. (Text field)
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the client. (Text field)
* **Primary Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person. (Email field)
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person. (Text field)
* **Contract Signed and Returned**: Indicate whether the contract has been signed and returned by the client. (Select one)
* **Contract Signature Date**: Enter the date the contract was signed. (Date field)
* **Initial Payment Received**: Indicate whether an initial payment has been received from the client. (Select one)
* **Payment Method on File**: Select the payment method used by the client (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.). (Select one)
* **NDA Executed**: Indicate whether the non-disclosure agreement (NDA) has been signed by the client. (Select one)
* **Client Portal Access Granted**: Indicate whether access to the client portal has been granted. (Select one)
* **Portal Credentials Sent to Client**: Indicate whether credentials have been sent to the client for portal access. (Select one)
* **Kickoff Meeting Scheduled**: Indicate whether a kickoff meeting with the client has been scheduled. (Select one)
* **Kickoff Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the kickoff meeting. (Date field)
* **Project Requirements Documented**: Indicate whether the project requirements have been documented. (Select one)
* **Brand Assets Received**: Indicate whether brand assets (logos, style guides, etc.) have been received from the client. (Select one)
* **Stakeholder Contacts Collected**: Indicate whether all stakeholder contacts have been collected. (Select one)
* **Internal Team Briefed**: Indicate whether the internal team has been briefed on the client. (Select one)
* **Client Communication Preferences**: Select the client's preferred method of communication (e.g., Email, Phone, etc.). (Select one)
* **Preferred Reporting Frequency**: Select the frequency at which the client prefers to receive reports. (Select one)
* **Special Requirements or Accommodations**: Enter any special requirements or accommodations the client may have. (Text field)
* **Assigned Onboarding Specialist**: Enter the name of the assigned onboarding specialist. (Text field)
* **Expected Onboarding Completion Date**: Enter the expected completion date of the onboarding process. (Date field)
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the onboarding process. (Text field)

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to all clients, but they are all included in the form as per the original YAML schema.
