# Termination Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Termination Declaration Form is a document that formally notifies the company of an employee's termination and provides necessary details for the termination process. It must be completed accurately and thoroughly, as it is an official record of the employee's separation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure all dates entered are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Be thorough and honest when providing reasons for termination and additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (<label>:** Employee Name):** Enter the full name of the employee who is being terminated.
* **Employee Id (<label>:** Employee Id):** Enter the unique identifier assigned to the employee who is being terminated.
* **Date Of Termination (<label>:** Date Of Termination):** Enter the date on which the employee's termination will take effect.
* **Reason For Termination (<label>:** Reason For Termination):** Choose a reason for termination from the options provided or provide a free-form explanation.
* **Reason For Termination  (<label>:** Reason For Termination):** This field should be left blank for future use.
* **Last Day Of Work (<label>:** Last Day Of Work):** Enter the last date the employee worked for the company.
* **Final Payment Date (<label>:** Final Payment Date):** Enter the date when the employee will receive their final payment.
* **Acknowledgement (<label>:** Acknowledgement):** Provide a clear and concise acknowledgement of the termination.
* **Manager Acknowledgement (<label>:** Manager Acknowledgement):** Select "Yes" if you have acknowledged the termination, or "No" if you have not acknowledged it.
* **Additional Comments (<label>:** Additional Comments):** Enter any additional comments about the termination.

## Tips

- Double-check all entered information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the HR department or supervisor for guidance.
- Ensure all fields have been completed before submitting the form.
