# Expense Reimbursement Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about employee expenses for reimbursement purposes. It should only be used for legitimate business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for the expense you are submitting.
2. Choose the correct category of the expense from the "Expense Category" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the expense in the "Description" field.
4. Select the type of expense from the "Expense Type" field.
5. Choose the cost center from the "Cost Center" field.
6. Enter the date the expense was incurred in the "Date Expensed" field.
7. Enter the amount of the expense in the "Amount" field.
8. Attach any relevant receipt or documentation for the expense in the "Receipt" field.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the expense in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date Submitted** (Date): Enter the date you are submitting this expense report.
* **Expense Category** (Select One): Choose the category of the expense (Business, Personal, or Both).
* **Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Expense Type** (Select Multiple): Select the type of expense (Travel, Entertainment, Food, or Other).
* **Cost Center** (Select Multiple): Choose the cost center where the expense was incurred (Headquarters, Branch, or Home Office).
* **Date Expensed** (Date): Enter the date the expense was incurred.
* **Amount** (Number): Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Receipt** (Text): Attach any relevant receipts or documentation for the expense.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or notes about the expense.

## Tips

* Ensure that the expense is legitimate and not for personal use.
* Only submit expenses that have not already been reimbursed.
* Attach any relevant receipts or documentation to support the expense.
* Be clear and concise in your description and comments.
