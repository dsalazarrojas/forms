# safety_inspection_license_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The safety inspection license form is designed to collect information about the safety inspection of a facility. This form is used to document the results of a safety inspection, which is crucial to identify any safety concerns and take corrective actions to prevent accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with details about the inspection, including the facility name and address.
2. Enter the inspector's name and contact information.
3. Select the inspection date and time.
4. Provide a safety rating for the facility, using the provided scale.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the inspection.
6. Select the assigned tool or equipment used during the inspection.
7. Choose the facility type (e.g., Office, Residential).
8. Select the inspection frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
9. Enter the facility size.
10. Enter the facility contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
11. Enter the facility address.
12. Enter any facility notes.
13. Select the facility status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
14. Select the inspection status (e.g., Completed, Incomplete).
15. Enter the safety inspector's signature.
16. Enter the facility's signature.
17. Enter the safety inspection license number and date.
