# Conference Room Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book and manage conference room reservations. It collects necessary information from users to ensure a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your complete name and contact information.
2. Choose the conference room and select the date and time for your event.
3. Enter the number of attendees and indicate if external guests will be attending.
4. Select the setup and equipment needed for your event.
5. Choose your preferred catering options and indicate any special requests.
6. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy and confirm your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservist Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address**: Enter your company or department email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
- **Organization**: Enter your company or department name.
- **Room Selection**: Choose the conference room you wish to book.
- **Reservation Date**: Enter the date of your event.
- **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event.
- **End Time**: Enter the end time of your event.
- **Buffer Time Needed**: If you need extra time before and after your event, select the corresponding option.
- **Event Name**: Enter the title of your event.
- **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of your event (optional).
- **Event Organizer**: Enter the person or team organizing the event.
- **Expected Attendee Count**: Enter the number of attendees expected.
- **Will External Guests Attend**: Indicate if external guests will be attending.
- **Number of External Guests**: If applicable, enter the number of external guests.
- **Setup Style**: Choose the room arrangement that suits your event.
- **Setup Notes**: Provide any special arrangements or instructions for the setup.
- **AV Requirements**: Select the equipment needed for your event.
- **Catering Selection**: Choose the catering options that suit your event.
- **Menu Preferences**: Enter any dietary needs or preferences.
- **Signage Required**: Indicate if you require directional signs for your event.
- **Signage Details**: If required, enter what the signs should say.
- **Parking Arrangements**: Indicate if you need reserved parking for your event.
- **Security Requirements**: Enter any special security needs or requirements.
- **Budget Information**: Enter your budget code or account number (if applicable).
- **Special Requests**: Provide any other special requests or needs.
- **I Understand Cancellation Policy**: Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy.
- **I Confirm This Reservation Request**: Confirm that you have submitted this reservation request.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure a smooth and successful event.
- Review the cancellation policy to understand the implications of cancelling your reservation.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your event coordinator or support team.
