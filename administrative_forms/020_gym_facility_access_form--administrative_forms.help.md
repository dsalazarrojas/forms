# Gym Facility Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Facility Access Form is designed to collect information from members and non-members visiting the gym. This form will help manage access permissions and possibly schedule appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have all required information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form to the designated authority for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gym Facility Access Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Member Information**: This field is used to collect information about the member visiting the gym.
* **Member Name**: Enter the name of the member accessing the gym facilities.
* **Member Email**: Enter the email address of the member accessing the gym facilities.
* **Guest Information**: This field is used to collect information about non-members visiting the gym.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest accessing the gym facilities.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the email address of the guest accessing the gym facilities.
* **Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the visit or appointment.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the designated authority for clarification.
