# onboarding_document_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect new hire details, emergency contacts, work authorization, and tax documents for the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the new hire's details accurately.
2. Enter the emergency contacts' information, including names and phone numbers.
3. Provide work authorization details, if applicable.
4. Upload necessary tax documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, optional): Enter the new hire's name, job title, and any other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, optional): List the new hire's emergency contacts, including names and phone numbers.
* **Work Authorization** (`work_authorization`, text, optional): If the new hire requires work authorization, provide the necessary details.
* **Tax Documents** (`tax_documents`, text, optional): Upload any required tax documents, such as a W-4 or SS-5 form.

## Tips
* Double-check the new hire's details for accuracy.
* Ensure the emergency contacts' information is up-to-date.
* Attach clear and complete tax documents, if required.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
