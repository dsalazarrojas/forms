# winch_system_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct a Winch system inspection, which is a series of checks to ensure that the Winch system is functioning correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select any issues or defects that you have found during the inspection from the "Checklist 1" field.
2. Enter any additional notes or comments you have about the inspection in the "Additional notes or comments" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date and time of the inspection" field.
4. Select one item from the "Checklist 4" field.
5. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date of the inspection" field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments you have about the inspection in the "Additional notes or comments" field.
7. Enter a numeric value in the "Numeric value to input" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments you have about the inspection in the "Additional notes or comments" field.
9. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Date of the inspection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_1** (Checklist 1, Select multiple): Select any issues or defects you have found during the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_2** (Additional notes or comments, Text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_3** (Date and time of the inspection, Time): Enter the date and time of the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_4** (Checklist 4, Select one): Select one item from the list.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_5** (Date of the inspection, Date): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_6** (Additional notes or comments, Text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_7** (Numeric value to input, Number): Enter a numeric value.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_8** (Additional notes or comments, Text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **winch_system_inspection_checklist_9** (Date of the inspection, Date): Enter the date of the inspection.
