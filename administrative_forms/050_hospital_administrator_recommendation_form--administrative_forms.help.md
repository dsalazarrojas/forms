# hospital_administrator_recommendation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about hospital administrators and their experiences, skills, and job details for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have access to the required fields and information.
2. Double-check that you have the necessary permissions to fill out the form.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **candidate_information** (`candidate_information`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the full name of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **reporting_manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the reporting manager of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **reporting_director** (`reporting_director`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reporting directors of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **reporting_executive** (`reporting_executive`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reporting executives of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **years_of_service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of service of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **date_joined** (`date_joined`, date, optional): Enter the date the candidate or individual joined the hospital.
* **current_job_title** (`current_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the current job title of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **reporting_supervisor** (`reporting_supervisor`, select_one, optional): Select the reporting supervisor of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **reporting_committee** (`reporting_committee`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reporting committee of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **years_of_service_2** (`years_of_service_2`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of service of the candidate or individual being recommended (second page).
* **date_joined_2** (`date_joined_2`, date, optional): Enter the date the candidate or individual joined the hospital (second page).
* **department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **job_description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job or position of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **job_status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the candidate or individual being recommended (Active or Retired).
* **job_status_2** (`job_status_2`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the second job or position of the candidate or individual being recommended (Active or Retired).
* **work_location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the work location of the candidate or individual being recommended.
* **job_category** (`job_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the job or position of the candidate or individual being recommended (Manager, Supervisor, Coordinator, Specialist, Technician, Clerk).
* **job_category_2** (`job_category_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of the second job or position of the candidate or individual being recommended (Manager, Supervisor, Coordinator, Specialist, Technician, Clerk).
* **shift_worked** (`shift_worked`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts worked by the candidate or individual being recommended (1st shift, 2nd shift, 3rd shift).
* **shift_worked_2** (`shift_worked_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts worked by the candidate or individual being recommended (second page).
* **signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for administrative use only and should not be filled out by the user.
