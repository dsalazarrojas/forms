# FMLA Notice Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit notice for leave requests due to Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). It is used to gather information about the employee's leave request, including reason, dates, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide the reason for your leave in the "Reason For Leave" field.
3. Enter the start date of your leave in the "Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your leave in the "End Date" field.
5. If applicable, enter the expected return date in the "Expected Return Date" field.
6. Enter the number of days off in the "Number Of Days Off" field.
7. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
9. If your manager has approved your leave, choose "Yes" in the "Manager Approval" field.
10. If your manager has declined your leave, choose "No" in the "Manager Approval" field.
11. Enter any comments from your manager in the "Manager Comments" field.
12. Select your current employee status (Active, Inactive, or Resigned) in the "Employee Status" field.
13. Select the type of leave you are requesting (Family Leave, Medical Leave) in the "Leave Type" field.
14. Select the department you belong to (HR, Management, Finance) in the "Department" field.
15. Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
16. Enter your supervisor's phone number in the "Supervisor Phone" field.
17. Enter your supervisor's email address in the "Supervisor Email" field.
18. Sign your leave request with your signature in the "Employee Signature" field.
19. Sign your manager's approval with their signature in the "Manager Signature" field.
20. Date your manager's approval in the "Manager Date" field.
21. Enter any comments from your manager in the "Manager Comments" field.
22. Select the company representative you would like to contact (HR Representative, Finance Representative, Management Representative) in the "Company Representative" field.
23. Enter the company representative's name in the "Company Representative Name" field.
24. Enter the company representative's phone number in the "Company Representative Phone" field.
25. Enter the company representative's email address in the "Company Representative Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Reason For Leave (reason_for_leave)**: Provide a clear and concise reason for your leave request.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date when your leave will start.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date when your leave will end.
* **Expected Return Date (expected_return_date)**: If known, enter the date you expect to return to work.
* **Number Of Days Off (number_of_days_off)**: Enter the number of days you will be off for the leave.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Manager Approval (manager_approval)**: Select "Yes" if your manager has approved your leave, "No" if they have declined.
* **Manager Comments (manager_comments)**: If your manager has comments, enter them here.
* **Employee Status (employee_status)**: Select your current status (Active, Inactive, or Resigned).
* **Leave Type (leave_type)**: Select the type of leave you are requesting (Family Leave, Medical Leave).
* **Department (department)**: Select the department you belong to (HR, Management, Finance).
* **Supervisor Name (supervisor_name)**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone (supervisor_phone)**: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Email (supervisor_email)**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Employee Signature (employee_signature)**: Sign your leave request with your signature.
* **Manager Signature (manager_signature)**: Sign your manager's approval with their signature.
* **Manager Date (manager_date)**: Date your manager's approval.
* **Manager Comments (manager_comments)**: Enter any comments from your manager.
* **Company Representative (company_representative)**: Select the representative you would like to contact (HR Representative, Finance Representative, Management Representative).
* **Company Representative Name (company_rep_name)**: Enter the company representative's name.
* **Company Representative Phone (company_rep_phone)**: Enter the company representative's phone number.
* **Company Representative Email (company_rep_email)**: Enter the company representative's email address.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* Remember that this form is used for new or updated leave requests, so ensure that your submission is not duplicated.
