# Address Change Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Address Change Declaration Form is used to notify the relevant authorities of a change in address. This form is to be completed by the individual making the address change and should be submitted along with any supporting documentation required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2.  Enter your government-issued ID number.
3.  Enter your current address, including the city and state/province.
4.  Enter the postal code of your current address.
5.  Enter your new address, including the city and state/province.
6.  Enter the postal code of your new address.
7.  Enter the country of your new address.
8.  Enter the date you moved to your new address.
9.  Choose the reason for your address change.
10. Enter your current phone number and email address for contact purposes.
11. If you are using a mail forwarding service, select the relevant option.
12. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
13. Sign the form with your electronic signature.
14. If applicable, provide a witness signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`declarant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Identification Number** (`declarant_id`, text, required): Enter your government-issued ID number.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address before the change.
* **Current City** (`current_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your current address.
* **Current State/Province** (`current_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your current address.
* **Current ZIP/Postal Code** (`current_zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your current address.
* **New Address** (`new_address`, text, required): Enter your new address after the change.
* **New City** (`new_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your new address.
* **New State/Province** (`new_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your new address.
* **New ZIP/Postal Code** (`new_zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your new address.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of your new address.
* **Date of Move** (`move_date`, date, required): Enter the date you moved to your new address.
* **Reason for Moving** (`reason_for_move`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your address change.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your current phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your current email address for notifications.
* **Duration at Previous Address** (`previous_residence_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration you lived at your previous address.
* **Mail Forwarding Service** (`forwarding_service`, select_one, optional): Choose if you will use a mail forwarding service.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submitting this declaration.
* **Declarant Signature** (`declarant_signature`, text, required): Sign this form with your electronic signature.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Sign this form as a witness if applicable.
