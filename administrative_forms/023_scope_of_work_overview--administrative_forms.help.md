# scope_of_work_overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a project's scope, expectations, responsibilities, timelines, and deliverables from project stakeholders, team members, or other relevant parties involved in the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's pages and questions carefully to ensure you understand the information being requested.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the text fields to provide descriptive answers.
4. If a field is marked as optional, you may skip it if you don't have relevant information.
5. Once you've completed the form, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Expectations**: 
    (text, optional)
    Use this field to describe the project's expectations, such as goals, objectives, or key performance indicators (KPIs).
* **Project Responsibilities**: 
    (text, optional)
    Describe the project's responsibilities, including tasks, roles, and duties assigned to team members or stakeholders.
* **Timelines**: 
    (date, optional)
    Enter the project's start and end dates or any significant milestones and deadlines.
* **Project Scope**: 
    (text, optional)
    Define the project's scope, including its goals, objectives, and any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Deliverables**: 
    (text, optional)
    List the project's deliverables, such as reports, documents, or final products that will be produced or provided.
