# Cybersecurity Incident Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cybersecurity Incident Discharge Form is used to document and close cybersecurity incidents, ensuring that they are properly discharged with relevant information and actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the incident and ensure that it has been properly investigated and reviewed.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating effort and only complete the form once.
* Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, using the provided instructions as a guide.
* If you are unsure about any field or section, please consult with the incident reviewer or the IT team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, required: false): Enter the cause of the incident.
* **Incident Effect** (`incident_effect`, text, required: false): Enter the effect of the incident.
* **Incident Recommendation** (`incident_recommendation`, text, required: false): Provide any recommendations for future prevention or mitigation of similar incidents.
* **Incident Resolution** (`incident_resolution`, date, required: false): Enter the date the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Investigation** (`incident_investigation`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the investigation into the incident.
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, required: false): Enter any incident report or notes.
* **Incident Review** (`incident_review`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes from the incident review.
* **Incident Review Comments** (`incident_review_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or notes from the incident review.
* **Incident Approval** (`incident_approval`, select_one, required: false): Select the approval status of the incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Remarks** (`incident_remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any additional remarks or comments.
* **Incident Disposition** (`incident_disposition`, select_one, required: false): Select the disposition of the incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Discharge Date** (`incident_discharge_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the incident was discharged.
* **Incident Discharge Time** (`incident_discharge_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the incident was discharged.
* **Incident Reviewer Name** (`incident_reviewer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the incident reviewer.
* **Incident Reviewer Title** (`incident_reviewer_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the incident reviewer.
* **Incident Reviewer Department** (`incident_reviewer_department`, text, required: false): Enter the department of the incident reviewer.
* **Incident Reviewer Email** (`incident_reviewer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the incident reviewer.
* **Incident Reviewer Phone** (`incident_reviewer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the incident reviewer.
* **Incident Discharge By** (`incident_discharge_by`, select_one, required: false): Select who discharged the incident from the provided options.
* **Incident Comments** (`incident_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.

Please note that some fields may be optional or have specific instructions, please review the form carefully and follow the instructions provided. If you are unsure about any field or section, please consult with the incident reviewer or the IT team.
