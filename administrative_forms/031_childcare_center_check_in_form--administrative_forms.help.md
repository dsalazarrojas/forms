# Childcare Center Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from parents or guardians about their child's stay at the childcare center. It helps the staff provide the best possible care and attention to the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the date of check-in.
2.  Enter the time of arrival.
3.  Fill in the child's first and last name.
4.  Enter the child's age.
5.  Select the child's room or class.
6.  Enter the parent or guardian's first and last name.
7.  Enter the phone number for contact during the day.
8.  Select your relationship to the child.
9.  Indicate the child's mood at drop-off.
10.  Report the child's health status.
11.  If necessary, list any symptoms the child may have.
12.  Indicate if the child has had a fever in the last 24 hours.
13.  If bringing medications, select the type and enter details.
14.  Enter any special instructions for staff.
15.  Enter any nap time instructions.
16.  Enter any feeding instructions.
17.  List any items brought from home.
18.  Enter the expected pickup time.
19.  Enter the name of the authorized pickup person.
20.  Indicate if the emergency contact is available today.
21.  Enter any additional notes for staff.
22.  Sign the form with your full name as a parent or guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-In Date**: Enter the date of check-in in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Check-In Time**: Enter the time of arrival in the format 12:00 (HH:MM am/pm) or 12pm (HH:MM am/pm).
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's first name, which should match their legal first name.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's last name, which should match their legal last name.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's current age in years.
* **Child Room or Class**: Enter the child's assigned room or class name.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter your first name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter your last name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact during the day.
* **Relationship to Child**: Select your relationship to the child (e.g. Mother, Father, Guardian, Grandparent, Other).
* **Child Mood at Drop-Off**: Select how the child is feeling at drop-off.
* **Child Health Status**: Report the child's health status.
* **Symptoms Present**: If the child has any symptoms, list them here.
* **Fever in Last 24 Hours**: Indicate if the child has had a fever in the last 24 hours.
* **Medications Brought**: If the child is on any medications, select the type and enter details.
* **Medication Details**: If you selected yes to medications, enter the names and dosages here.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special instructions for staff.
* **Nap Time Needs**: Enter any special nap time instructions.
* **Feeding Instructions**: Enter any special feeding instructions.
* **Items Brought**: List any items you've brought from home.
* **Expected Pickup Time**: Enter the expected pickup time.
* **Authorized Pickup Person**: Enter the name of the authorized pickup person.
* **Emergency Contact Available**: Indicate if your emergency contact is available today.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional information for staff.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign with your full name as a parent or guardian.
