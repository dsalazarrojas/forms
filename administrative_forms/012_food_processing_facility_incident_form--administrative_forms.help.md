# Food Processing Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur at food processing facilities. Incidents may include but are not limited to, equipment malfunctions, spills, accidents, or other events that may impact operations or food safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred.
2. Select the date of the incident using the date picker.
3. Enter the time of the incident using the time picker.
4. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility where the incident occurred. This is where the incident took place.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the incident happened using the date picker. This should be the date on which the incident occurred.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the incident occurred. This should be the time on which the incident happened.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred. This could be a room, area, or specific location within the facility.
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident. This should include any relevant details about the incident, such as its cause, effects, and any corrective actions taken or recommended.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
- Use the date and time pickers to select the correct dates and times.
- If you're not sure about any of the fields, contact the facility management or administration for guidance.
