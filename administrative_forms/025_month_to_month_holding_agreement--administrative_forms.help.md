# Month To Month Holding Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Month To Month Holding Agreement form is used to establish or update the holding agreements between tenants and landlords on a month-to-month basis. This form helps to collect necessary information for administrative purposes, such as maintaining accurate records, handling rent payments, and ensuring compliance with local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `Tenant Name` field with the name of the tenant.
2. Enter the `Landlord Name` in the corresponding field.
3. Select the `Start Date` and `End Date` as per the agreement duration.
4. Enter the `Monthly Rent` amount as agreed upon in the contract.
5. Enter the `Deposit` amount, if applicable.
6. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the tenant involved in the holding agreement.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the landlord involved in the holding agreement.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, false): Enter the start date of the holding agreement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, false): Enter the end date of the holding agreement.
* **Monthly Rent** (`monthly_rent`, number, false): Enter the monthly rent amount as agreed upon in the contract.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, number, false): Enter the deposit amount, if applicable.
