# Student Emergency Contact Amendment Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable students to update their emergency contact information in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Determine Your Request
- Determine if you need to request an update to your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Student Id** (`student_id`, number, Optional)
This field asks for your student ID number.

### 2. **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, Select One, Optional)
- Please select whether the contact is a family member or someone else.
- Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

### 3. **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, Text, Optional)
- Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
- Example: John

### 4. **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, Text, Optional)
- Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
- Example: Doe

### 5. **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, Text, Optional)
- Enter your last name as a student.
- Example: Smith

### 6. **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional)
- Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- Format: 123-456-7890

### 7. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional)
- Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
- Format: john.doe@example.com

### 8. **Student Email** (`student_email`, Email, Optional)
- Enter your own email address.
- Format: john.smith@example.com

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are not sure about any of the fields, you can contact our support team for assistance.
- The form will be reviewed and updated by our staff after submission.
