# internship_placement_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to internship placements, typically for students and employers. It is likely used in an academic or professional setting to facilitate the authorization and coordination of internships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information on **page 1**.
2. Provide the employer information on **page 2**.
3. Enter the placement details on **page 3**.
4. Sign and date the form on **page 4**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Student Info
* **student_info (Text)**: This is the section where you should enter the student's relevant details.

### Page 2: Employer Info
* **employer_info (Text)**: This is the section where you should enter the employer's relevant details.

### Page 3: Placement Info
* **placement_info (Text)**: This is the section where you should enter the placement details.

### Page 4: Signature
* **signature (Text)**: Sign and date this section to confirm authorization of the internship placement.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
