# residential_leaseterminationoffboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The residential_leaseterminationoffboarding_form is a form used for the administrative process of terminating a lease. This form is required to be completed by a tenant when they wish to terminate their lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields: `leaseenddate`, `noticeperiod`, and `leasebalance` fields are not required, but `tenantname`, `contactnumber`, and `email` fields are highly recommended to be completed.
2. Enter the reason for lease termination in the `reasonfortermination` field.
3. If applicable, enter the number of days for the notice period in the `noticetimerestricted` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tenantname** (text): Enter the name of the tenant who is terminating the lease.
* **contactnumber** (text): Enter the contact number of the tenant.
* **email** (email): Enter the email address of the tenant.
* **leaseenddate** (date): Enter the date when the lease will be terminated.
* **reasonfortermination** (text): Enter the reason why the lease is being terminated.
* **noticetimerestricted** (number): If required, enter the number of days for the notice period.
* **leasebalance** (number): If applicable, enter the amount due on the lease balance.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid any potential issues with the lease termination process.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your landlord or property manager for assistance.
