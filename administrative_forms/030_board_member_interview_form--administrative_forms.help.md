# Board Member Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Member Interview Form is designed to collect detailed information about candidate applicants for board positions. This form serves as a tool for evaluating and assessing potential board members, ensuring that the right individuals are selected for important organizational roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by filling out the candidate's details**: Enter the candidate's name, email address, and a brief professional background in the respective fields.
2. **Select a suitable interview date**: Choose the date the interview was conducted. This will help track the candidate's progress through the evaluation process.
3. **Evaluate the candidate's suitability**: Assess the candidate's overall fit for board responsibilities by selecting a rating from the options provided (Not suitable, Borderline, Suitable, Highly suitable).
4. **Provide next steps**: Suggest any recommended next steps for the candidate, such as proceeding to a second interview or further evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate name**: Enter the full name of the candidate applying for the board position.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Email address**: Enter the candidate's contact email address for follow-up purposes.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: Yes
* **Experience summary**: Briefly describe the candidate's professional background and relevant experience.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Interview date**: Select the date the interview was conducted.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: Yes
* **Interviewer name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
* **Suitability rating**: Assess the candidate's overall fit for board responsibilities.
	+ Type: Select One (Not suitable, Borderline, Suitable, Highly suitable)
	+ Required: Yes
* **Recommended next steps**: Suggest any next steps for the candidate, such as proceeding to a second interview.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
