# Diagnostic Equipment Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diagnostic Equipment Maintenance Checklist is a form designed to help medical staff or equipment managers track the maintenance history and schedule of diagnostic equipment in their institution. This form will help in keeping track of the serial numbers of the equipment, last maintenance date, and next maintenance schedule. It also allows the user to document any safety risks and corrective actions taken during maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out page one, which is a blank page for any introductory comments or notes.
2. Enter the serial number of the diagnostic equipment in the "Diagnostic Equipment Serial Number" field.
3. On page two, record the "Last Maintenance Date" and "Next Maintenance Schedule".
4. If required, fill out the "Safety Risks" and "Corrective Actions" sections to document any potential hazards and actions taken.
5. Finally, enter the "Maintenance Person" responsible for the maintenance and provide any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page One**: This page is for any introductory comments or notes about the equipment or maintenance activity.
* **Diagnostic Equipment**: Enter the make and model of the equipment, e.g., ECG machine, MRI machine, etc.
* **Maintenance Checklist**: This is a text field to record any maintenance tasks performed on the equipment.
* **Safety Risks**: List any potential safety risks associated with the equipment, e.g., electrical shock hazard, radiation exposure, etc.
* **Corrective Actions**: Describe any actions taken to mitigate or address the safety risks.
* **Page Two**: This page is for any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Diagnostic Equipment Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment, as provided by the manufacturer.
* **Last Maintenance Date**: Record the date of the last maintenance activity.
* **Next Maintenance Schedule**: Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **Maintenance Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the maintenance activity.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the maintenance activity.
