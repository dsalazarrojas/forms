# job_role_overview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "job_role_overview_form" form is designed to collect essential details about a job role, including its title, description, responsibilities, expectations, scope, and performance metrics. This form helps you to create a comprehensive overview of a job, which can be useful for administrative tasks, such as job postings, performance evaluations, and talent management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job title in the "Job Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the job role in the "Job Description" field.
3. List the key responsibilities associated with the job role in the "Job Responsibilities" field.
4. Define the key performance expectations for the job role in the "Job Expectations" field.
5. Describe the job's scope in the "Job Scope" field.
6. Provide an example of the job scope in the "Job Scope Example" field.
7. Define the key performance metrics for the job role in the "Job Key Performance Metrics" field.
8. Clearly define the job scope boundary in the "Job Scope Boundary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the official title of the job role.
- **Job Description** (`Job Description`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe the job role in a few sentences.
- **Job Responsibilities** (`Job Responsibilities`, `text`, required/optional): List the key responsibilities associated with the job role.
- **Job Expectations** (`Job Expectations`, `text`, required/optional): Define the key performance expectations for the job role.
- **Job Scope** (`Job Scope`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the job's scope in a few sentences.
- **Job Scope Example** (`Job Scope Example`, `text`, required/optional): Provide an example of the job scope.
- **Job Key Performance Metrics** (`Job Key Performance Metrics`, `text`, required/optional): Define the key performance metrics for the job role.
- **Job Scope Boundary** (`Job Scope Boundary`, `text`, required/optional): Clearly define the job scope boundary.

## Tips
- Be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
- Use specific examples to illustrate job scope and responsibilities.
- Ensure that key performance metrics are measurable and actionable.
- Review and revise the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
