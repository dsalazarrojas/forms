# procurement_planning_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The procurement planning checklist form is designed to guide users through the procurement planning process, helping them collect necessary information and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Answer the questions on each page carefully, making sure to choose the correct options that apply to your procurement planning situation.**
2. **Move to the next page when you have finished with the previous one.**
3. **Submit the form when you have filled out all the necessary fields.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: supplier_selection (ID 1)
* **supplier_selection** (`Supplier Selection`, optional): Select the suppliers you are planning to work with. You can choose multiple suppliers if necessary.

### Page 2: supplier_information (ID 2)
* **supplier_information** (`Supplier Information`, optional): Decide whether you have or do not have the necessary supplier information.

### Page 3: contact_details (ID 3)
* **contact_details** (`Contact Details`, optional): Enter any contact details you have for the suppliers selected on Page 1.

### Page 4: planning_steps (ID 4)
* **planning_steps** (`Planning Steps`, optional): Select the planning steps that apply to your procurement planning.

### Page 5: payment_methods (ID 5)
* **payment_methods** (`Payment Methods`, required): Decide on the payment methods you will use for the procurement. This is a required field.

### Page 6: payment_schedule (ID 6)
* **payment_schedule** (`Payment Schedule`, optional): Select the payment schedule that applies to your procurement planning.
