# Meeting Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about meetings that have taken place. This form helps meeting coordinators, attendees, and team members to record key details, actions, and next steps regarding the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Open the form and start by entering the meeting date using the date field. Choose the correct date from the calendar.
2. Select the time of the meeting from the time field. This helps to identify the start and end time of the meeting.
3. Provide the meeting location in the meeting location field. Be as specific as possible, including the room number or other identifying details.
4. List the attendees of the meeting in the meeting attendees field. Include the names of everyone who participated in the meeting.
5. In the discussion points field, write a brief summary of the key points discussed during the meeting.
6. In the action items field, list any tasks or actions that need to be taken as a result of the meeting.
7. Finally, describe the next steps to be taken in the next steps field. This could include follow-up actions, deadlines, or important reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Summary** (`meeting_summary_form`, text, required: false): This field allows you to enter a brief summary of the meeting, including any key points or takeaways.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting, which can be accessed through the calendar.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): Choose the start time of the meeting, which helps in keeping track of the duration and timing of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required: false): Specify the location where the meeting took place, including room numbers or any other identifying details.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, text, required: false): List the names of all participants who attended the meeting.
* **Discussion Points** (`discussion_points`, text, required: false): Enter a brief summary of the main topics or points discussed during the meeting.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, required: false): List any specific tasks or actions that need to be taken as a result of the meeting.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Describe the next steps or follow-up actions that need to be taken after the meeting.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible while filling out the form.
- Check that all fields are complete before submitting the form.
- Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
