# Holiday Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Leave Absence Form is a formal request for leave of absence submitted to your manager or HR department. This form is used to request leave for personal or work-related reasons, allowing you to take time off without pay or other benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and leave information.
2.  Choose the correct leave type from the options provided.
3.  Select the start and end dates of your leave.
4.  Specify the duration of your leave.
5.  Explain the reason for your leave.
6.  Check the box for your manager's approval status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Details** (Select): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm that you are an employee and that you are eligible for leave.
- **Leave Type** (Select): Choose from options: 'Vacation', 'Holiday', or 'Personal' to describe the reason for your leave.
- **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of your leave in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of your leave in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Duration** (Number): Enter the length of your leave in number of days.
- **Reason for Leave** (Text): Briefly explain why you are requesting leave.
- **Manager Approval** (Select): Select 'Approve', 'Deny', or 'Delegate' for your manager's approval status.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure that you complete the form correctly to avoid any delays or issues with your leave application.
- Double-check your dates and leave type before submitting the form.
- If your manager is not available for approval, you can ask HR for assistance.
- Review your company's leave policies for further information on leave eligibility and procedures.
