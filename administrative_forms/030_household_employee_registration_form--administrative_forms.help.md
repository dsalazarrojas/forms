# Household Employee Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Household Employee Registration Form is a mandatory document used to collect and store employee information for HR record-keeping and insurance compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked as "required" in this guide.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. If applicable, enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter your address information in the "Address Line 1", "Address Line 2", and "City" fields.
6. Select your region from the "Region" dropdown list.
7. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. If applicable, enter your social security number in the "Social Security Number" field.
11. Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Person" and "Relationship" fields.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the "Phone" field.
13. Add any additional notes regarding your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - househould_employee
• **First Name** (Text, Optional): Your name that is commonly used by others in a household.
### Page 1 - last_name
• **Last Name** (Text, Required): Your last name.
### Page 1 - date_of_birth
• **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): The date on which you were born.
### Page 1 - address_line_1
• **Address Line 1** (Text, Optional): Your household's street address or mailing address.
### Page 1 - address_line_2
• **Address Line 2** (Text, Optional): Your household's apartment or suite number.
### Page 1 - city
• **City** (Text, Optional): The city where your household is located.
### Page 1 - region
• **Region** (Select One, Required): The geographic area where your household resides.
### Page 1 - postal_code
• **Postal Code** (Text, Optional): The postal code for your household's address.
### Page 1 - phone
• **Phone** (Text, Optional): Your phone number.
### Page 1 - email
• **Email** (Email, Optional): Your email address.
### Page 1 - social_security_number
• **Social Security Number** (Text, Optional): Your social security number.
### Page 1 - emergency_contact_person
• **Emergency Contact Person** (Text, Optional): The name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
### Page 1 - emergency_contact_relationship
• **Relationship** (Select One, Optional): The relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
### Page 1 - emergency_contact_phone
• **Phone** (Text, Optional): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
### Page 1 - emergency_contact_note
• **Emergency Contact Note** (Note, Optional): Any additional information about your emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Double-check the accuracy of all entered information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative.
