# Life Insurance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Records Release Form is a document used to release life insurance records. It is designed for administrative purposes, such as data anonymization, record transfer, or other tasks that require the release of insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the life insurance form you are releasing in the "Form Name" field.
2. Provide the name of the insurance company associated with the policy in the "Insurance Company" field.
3. Enter the policy number for the policy being released in the "Policy Number" field.
4. If the policy is not held by the form submitter, enter the name of the policy holder in the "Policy Holder" field.
5. Enter the address of the policy holder, if applicable, in the "Address" field.
6. If necessary, enter the effective date and expiration date of the policy in the "Effective Date" and "Expiration Date" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the date on which the form is being signed in the "Signature Date" field.
8. Enter the date on which the form is being released in the "Release Date" field.
9. Enter your name and title in the "Form Submitter" and "Title" fields.
10. Select the authority granting the release, which is either "Administrator", "Agent", or "Policy Holder", in the "Release Authority" field.
11. Add any additional comments or explanations in the "Notes" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the life insurance form being released.
* **Insurance Company** (text, required): Enter the name of the insurance company associated with the policy.
* **Policy Number** (text, required): Enter the policy number for the policy being released.
* **Policy Holder** (text, not required): If the policy is not held by the form submitter, enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Address** (text, not required): Enter the address of the policy holder, if applicable.
* **Effective Date** (date, not required): If necessary, enter the effective date of the policy.
* **Expiration Date** (date, not required): If necessary, enter the expiration date of the policy.
* **Signature Date** (date, not required): Enter the date on which the form is being signed.
* **Release Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which the form is being released.
* **Form Submitter** (text, not required): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Title** (text, required): Enter your title as the person submitting the form.
* **Release Authority** (select multiple, required): Select the authority granting the release, which is either "Administrator", "Agent", or "Policy Holder".
* **Notes** (note, not required): Add any additional comments or explanations, if necessary.
