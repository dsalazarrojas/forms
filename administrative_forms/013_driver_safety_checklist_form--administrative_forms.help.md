# Driver Safety Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Safety Checklist Form is used to collect information related to vehicle safety, driver identification, and vehicle inspection. It helps gather data on vehicle registration numbers, VINs (Vehicle Identification Numbers), vehicle models, and other critical information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle registration number, found on the vehicle's registration card or sticker.
2. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), typically found on the vehicle's dashboard or on the driver's side doorjamb.
3. Select the vehicle model from the provided options, ensuring it accurately represents the vehicle you are checking.
4. Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
5. Enter the vehicle's make.
6. Choose the vehicle's color from the provided options.
7. Enter the vehicle's condition, describing it as 'good', 'fair', or 'poor'.
8. Select the safety features of the vehicle, such as seatbelts, airbags, and anti-lock braking systems.
9. Choose the vehicle inspection status, indicating if it passed with 'good' marks, or if there are some issues indicated by 'fair' or 'poor' marks.
10. Enter the driver's license number.
11. Enter the driver's name.
12. Provide the vehicle's owner's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_registration_number (vehicle registration number)** (`vehicle_registration_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number, found on the vehicle's registration card or sticker.
* **vin (vin)** (`vin`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number, typically found on the vehicle's dashboard or on the driver's side doorjamb.
* **vehicle_model (vehicle model)** (`vehicle_model`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the vehicle model from the provided options to accurately represent the vehicle you are checking.
* **vehicle_year (vehicle year)** (`vehicle_year`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **vehicle_make (vehicle make)** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **vehicle_color (vehicle color)** (`vehicle_color`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the vehicle's color from the provided options.
* **vehicle_condition (vehicle condition)** (`vehicle_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the vehicle's condition as 'good', 'fair', or 'poor'.
* **safety_features (safety features)** (`safety_features`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the safety features of the vehicle, such as seatbelts, airbags, and anti-lock braking systems.
* **vehicle_inspection (vehicle inspection)** (`vehicle_inspection`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the vehicle inspection status, indicating if it passed with 'good' marks, or if there are some issues indicated by 'fair' or 'poor' marks.
* **driver_license_number (driver's license number)** (`driver_license_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the driver's license number.
* **driver_name (driver's name)** (`driver_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the driver's name.
* **vehicle_owner (vehicle owner)** (`vehicle_owner`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's owner's name.
