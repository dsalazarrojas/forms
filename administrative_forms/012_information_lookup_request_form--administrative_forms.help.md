# Information Lookup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Lookup Request Form is used to provide more information about an item or issue that has been reported by a user. This form helps our team to get more context and details to better address the issue and provide the best possible solution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages and fill in the required information as needed. Each page contains a few fields that we need to know in order to look up the information you need. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Start by filling in your **First Name**, which is the first page of the form.
2. If you have a **Last Name**, please fill it in as well. It's not mandatory, but it can help us better identify you in our records.
3. If you have an email address, please fill in your **Email**. This will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
4. If you have a phone number, please fill it in as well. This will help us get in touch with you if we need to discuss your issue further.
5. Finally, in the **Message** page, please provide a detailed description of the information you are looking for. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field to identify you in our records. Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: An optional field to help us better identify you in our records. Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: An optional field for us to get in touch with you if we need more information. Enter a valid email address that you use frequently.
* **Phone**: An optional field for us to get in touch with you if we need to discuss your issue further. Enter a valid phone number that you use frequently.
* **Message**: A required field where you can provide a detailed description of the information you are looking for. Be as specific as possible so we can better understand your question.
