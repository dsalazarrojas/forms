# Mechanical Services Shift Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Services Shift Report form is used to document the mechanical services completed during a shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Shift Date with the date the mechanical services were performed.
* Select whether Team Members were present or not.
* Provide a brief description of the mechanical services performed in the Mechanical Services Performed field.
* If necessary, add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift Date** (Shift Date, date, required): Enter the date the mechanical services were performed.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if team members were present during the mechanical services, 'No' otherwise.
* **Mechanical Services Performed** (Mechanical Services Performed, text, optional): Describe the mechanical services completed during the shift, including any relevant details.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the mechanical services performed.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, optional): Add any additional information or context about the mechanical services or shift.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, number, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required by your organization.
