# Lease Agreement Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lease Agreement Check Out Form is used to document the check-out of a property and record information about its condition and any issues that need to be addressed before returning the security deposit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Check Out Date**, which is the date you are checking out of the property.
2. Enter the **Lease End Date**, which is the date the lease ended.
3. Enter the **Tenant Move In Date**, which is the date the tenant moved in.
4. Enter the **Property Address**, which is the address of the property.
5. Enter the **Property Manager**, which is the person responsible for managing the property.
6. Enter the **Tenant**, which is the name of the person leasing the property.
7. Enter the **Reason for Move Out**, which is the reason the tenant is moving out.
8. Enter the **Check Out Condition**, which describes the property's condition at the time of check-out.
9. Enter any **Comments** about the property's condition.
10. Select the **Property Status**, which indicates the current state of the property (e.g., Clean, Damaged, Dirty, or Junk).
11. Select the **Assigned Tool**, which is the tool used to inspect the property.
12. (Optional) Enter the **Tenant Sign Date**, which is the date the tenant signed the lease agreement.
13. (Optional) Enter the **Property Sign Date**, which is the date the property owner signed the lease agreement.
14. (Optional) Select the **Property Status Update**, which indicates any changes to the property's status.
15. (Optional) Enter **Lease Terms**, which are the terms of the lease agreement.
16. (Optional) Enter the **Move In Condition**, which describes the property's condition at the time of move-in.
17. (Optional) Enter the **Property Condition**, which is a numerical rating of the property's condition.
18. (Optional) Enter the **Property Condition 2**, which is another numerical rating of the property's condition.
19. (Optional) Enter the **Property Condition 3**, which is a third numerical rating of the property's condition.
20. (Optional) Enter the **Tenant Signature**, which is the tenant's signature.
21. (Optional) Enter the **Property Signature**, which is the property owner's signature.
22. (Optional) Enter **Comments 2**, which are additional comments about the property's condition.
23. (Optional) Enter the **Assigned Date**, which is the date the property was assigned.
24. (Optional) Enter the **Property Manager Signature**, which is the property manager's signature.
25. (Optional) Enter **Property Manager Comments**, which are additional comments from the property manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Out Date** (id 1): The date you are checking out of the property. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Lease End Date** (id 2): The date the lease ended. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Tenant Move In Date** (id 3): The date the tenant moved in. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Property Address** (id 4): The address of the property. Enter the full address as it appears on the lease agreement.
* **Property Manager** (id 5): The person responsible for managing the property. Enter the name of the person managing the property.
* **Tenant** (id 6): The name of the person leasing the property. Enter the name as it appears on the lease agreement.
* **Reason for Move Out** (id 7): The reason the tenant is moving out. Choose a reason for moving out.
* **Check Out Condition** (id 8): Describe the property's condition at the time of check-out. Enter a brief description of the property's condition.
* **Comments** (id 9): Additional comments about the property's condition. Enter any additional information about the property's condition.
* **Property Status** (id 10): The current state of the property (e.g., Clean, Damaged, Dirty, or Junk). Choose the current status of the property.
* **Assigned Tool** (id 11): The tool used to inspect the property. Choose the tool used to inspect the property.
* **Tenant Sign Date** (id 12): The date the tenant signed the lease agreement. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Property Sign Date** (id 13): The date the property owner signed the lease agreement. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Property Status Update** (id 14): Any changes to the property's status. Choose any changes to the property's status.
* **Lease Terms** (id 15): The terms of the lease agreement. Enter the terms of the lease agreement.
* **Move In Condition** (id 16): Describe the property's condition at the time of move-in. Enter a brief description of the property's condition.
* **Property Condition** (id 17): A numerical rating of the property's condition. Enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Property Condition 2** (id 18): Another numerical rating of the property's condition. Enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Property Condition 3** (id 19): A third numerical rating of the property's condition. Enter a number between 1 and 5.
* **Tenant Signature** (id 20): The tenant's signature. Enter the tenant's signature.
* **Property Signature** (id 21): The property owner's signature. Enter the property owner's signature.
* **Comments 2** (id 22): Additional comments about the property's condition. Enter any additional information about the property's condition.
* **Assigned Date** (id 23): The date the property was assigned. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Property Manager Signature** (id 24): The property manager's signature. Enter the property manager's signature.
* **Property Manager Comments** (id 25): Additional comments from the property manager. Enter any comments from the property manager.
