# Compensatory Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compensatory Time Off Request Form is used to request compensatory time off from work due to overtime worked. This form will help employees and management track and approve time off requests, ensuring that they follow company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
   * Request Date: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
   * Employee Name: Enter your full name.
   * Employee ID: Enter your employee identification number.
   * Department: Select your department from the list.
   * Job Title: Enter your current position.
   * Manager Name: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
   * Manager Email: Enter your supervisor's email address.

2. Describe your overtime:
   * Date of Overtime Worked: Enter the date when you worked overtime.
   * Reason for Overtime: Select the reason for your overtime from the list.
   * Overtime Details: Describe the work performed during overtime.
   * Overtime Hours Worked: Enter the number of extra hours worked.
   * Overtime Start Time: Enter the start time of overtime work.
   * Overtime End Time: Enter the end time of overtime work.

3. Request time off:
   * Comp Time Requested: Enter the number of hours of compensatory time requested.
   * Comp Time Start Date: Enter the date when you want to take the time off.
   * Comp Time End Date: Enter the date when you return to work.
   * Type of Time Off: Select the type of time off you want to take (full day, half day, hourly increments, or multiple days).

4. Arrange coverage:
   * Is Coverage Arranged: Select whether coverage is arranged.
   * Person Covering: If coverage is arranged, enter the name of the colleague covering your duties.

5. Status of Urgent Tasks:
   * Status of Urgent Tasks: Select the status of urgent tasks from the list.

6. Availability:
   * Availability During Time Off: Select your availability during the time off.

7. Previous Comp Time Used:
   * Previous Comp Time Used This Year: If applicable, enter how much comp time already used this year.

8. Expiration Concern:
   * Is Time Expiring Soon: Select whether you have a use it or lose it concern.

9. Approval:
   * Manager Approval: For manager use only.
   * HR Approval: For HR use only.
