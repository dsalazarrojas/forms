# Performance Review Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review Amendment Form is used to update and revise performance reviews, allowing employees to request changes to their performance ratings and other details after an initial review. This form is meant to ensure that any updates or changes are accurately captured and properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Performance Review Amendment Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee Name field with the ID number of the employee whose review is being amended.
2. Enter the Reviewer Name field with the name of the reviewer who conducted the original review.
3. Enter the Amendment Date field with the date of the amendment.
4. In the Reason for Change field, provide a brief explanation for the amendment.
5. For the Original Review Date and Review Period Start/End fields, enter the original review dates.
6. If necessary, update the Performance Rating, Original Performance Rating, Updated Performance Rating, and Status fields accordingly.
7. Enter the Updated Reason for Amendment and any other fields as necessary.
8. Leave a note in the Notes field with any additional comments.
9. Enter any comments from the reviewer and approver in the Reviewer Comments and Approver Comments fields respectively.
10. Sign and date the approver's signature, title, and email in the Approver Signature, Approver Title, and Approver Email fields.
11. Select the Updated Status field to indicate whether the employee is Active or Inactive.
12. If necessary, enter the Reason for Status Change in the Updated Status Reason field.
13. Leave any additional comments for the approver in the Approver Notes field.
14. Date and sign the approver's date in the Approver Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the ID number of the employee whose review is being amended.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer who conducted the original review.
* **Amendment Date** (`amendment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the amendment.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the amendment.
* **Original Review Date** (`original_review_date`, date, optional): Enter the original review dates.
* **Review Period Start** (`review_period_start`, date, optional): Enter the original review period start date.
* **Review Period End** (`review_period_end`, date, optional): Enter the original review period end date.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, optional): Update the performance rating if necessary.
* **Original Performance Rating** (`original_performance_rating`, number, optional): Enter the original performance rating.
* **Reason for Amendment** (`reason_for_amendment`, text, optional): Enter the updated reason for amendment.
* **Updated Performance Rating** (`updated_performance_rating`, number, optional): Enter the updated performance rating.
* **Updated Reason for Amendment** (`updated_reason_for_amendment`, text, optional): Enter the updated reason for amendment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Leave a note with any additional comments.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the approver.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Approver Signature** (`approver_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the approver's signature.
* **Approver Title** (`approver_title`, text, optional): Enter the approver's title.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Enter the approver's email.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select whether the employee is Active or Inactive.
* **Updated Status** (`updated_status`, select_one, optional): Select the updated status of the employee.
* **Updated Status Reason** (`updated_status_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the updated status.
* **Approver Notes** (`approver_notes`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments for the approver.
* **Approver Date** (`approver_date`, date, optional): Date and sign the approver's date.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank, but please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
