# Safety Equipment Installation Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record safety equipment installation records. You should fill it out when installing safety equipment, such as first aid kits, fire extinguishers, or emergency exit signs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of installation in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` in the "installed_date" field.
2. Enter the name or initials of the person who installed the safety equipment in the "installed_by" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the location where the safety equipment is installed in the "installed_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **installed_date**: The date on which the safety equipment was installed. Use the `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
* **installed_by**: The name or initials of the person who installed the safety equipment.
* **installed_location**: A brief description of the location where the safety equipment is installed (e.g., "Main Office, Room 101").

## Tips
* Be sure to record accurate and complete information to ensure the safety of personnel and visitors.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, consult with your team or supervisor.
* If you need to update or change any information, make sure to fill out a new form with the correct information.
