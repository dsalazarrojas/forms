# Inventory Management Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide information about a customer's inventory management service request to our team. Please fill out this form to initiate the process of managing your inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information in each field as accurately as possible.
2. Select the correct options for "inventory_type" and "service_type" that best describe your request.
3. Enter any additional details in the "inventory_location", "inventory_status", and "inventory_note" fields as needed.
4. Enter the customer's email and phone number for follow-up communication.
5. Click "Submit" to send the form to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_intake_form** (text, optional): Please describe the purpose of your inventory management request.
* **inventory_type** (select_one, required): Select the type of inventory you are requesting service for (e.g. "Inventory Service").
* **service_type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of service you require (e.g. "Inventory Management").
* **inventory_size** (number, optional): If applicable, enter the size of the inventory.
* **inventory_location** (text, optional): Provide the location of the inventory.
* **inventory_status** (select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the inventory (e.g. "Active", "Inactive", "On Order").
* **inventory_note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the inventory.
* **client_email** (email, required): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **client_phone** (text, required): Enter the customer's phone number for follow-up calls.
* **assigned_tool** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the tool or asset that is assigned to the customer.
