# Directional Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Directional Signage Order Form is designed to collect information about directional signage orders for an event or business.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To begin, please fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select the correct options for the Assigned Tool field from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the correct Event Date and Event Time in the respective date and time fields.
4.  Provide the Event Duration if applicable.
5.  Enter the Contact Person, Contact Phone, and Contact Email for the order.
6.  Fill out any additional comments or details as necessary.
7.  Finally, select the Signage Language from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Information**: Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Order Details**: Provide any necessary details about your order.
* **Signage Preferences**: Enter any specific requirements or preferences for the signage.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information to provide, enter it here.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact for this order.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number to contact the person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email to contact the person.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the correct tool from the dropdown menu.
* **Business Location**: Enter the location of your business.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Signage Language**: Select the language for the signage.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the correct tool from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and clear.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the assigned contact person.
