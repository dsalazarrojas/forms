# Equipment Failure Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about equipment failure incidents, allowing for accurate reporting and efficient resolution of such incidents. It captures essential details about the equipment, the incident, and the personnel involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Page 1**: This is the introduction page.
2. **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the failed equipment.
3. **Serial Number**: Provide the serial number of the failed equipment, if available.
4. **Failure Date**: Enter the date the failure occurred.
5. **Failure Time**: Enter the time the failure occurred.
6. **Failure Location**: Describe the location where the equipment failed.
7. **Description**: Write a detailed description of the failure, including any error messages or symptoms.
8. **Equipment Type**: Specify the type of equipment that failed.
9. **Assigned Person**: Identify the person responsible for maintaining or operating the equipment.
10. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the equipment that failed.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, `text`, required): Provide the serial number of the failed equipment, if available.
*   **Failure Date** (`failure_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the failure occurred, in the standard date format (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Failure Time** (`failure_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the failure occurred, in 24-hour format (e.g., HH:MM).
*   **Failure Location** (`failure_location`, `text`, required): Describe the location where the equipment failed.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Write a detailed description of the failure, including any error messages or symptoms.
*   **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `text`, optional): Specify the type of equipment that failed.
*   **Assigned Person** (`assigned_person`, `text`, optional): Identify the person responsible for maintaining or operating the equipment.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Use proper date and time formats for the failure date and time fields.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or the maintenance team for guidance.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
