# Drinking Water Contamination Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents of drinking water contamination. It is used to gather information about the incident, including the type of contamination, the source of the contamination, and the affected population. The purpose of this form is to quickly and accurately report and track incidents of drinking water contamination, allowing for prompt action to be taken to address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
2. Indicate if the water type was contaminated in the "Water Type" field.
3. Specify the source of the contamination in the "Water Source" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Date and Time" field. Use the format "MM/DD/YYYY" for the date and "HH:MM" for the time.
5. Specify the time of the incident in the "Time" field, if necessary.
6. Enter the location of the incident in the "Location" field.
7. Indicate if the affected population is at risk in the "Affected Population" field.
8. Identify if the person reporting the incident is affected themselves in the "Reporting Person" field.
9. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field, so we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Description**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the incident. Please be as detailed as possible.
  - **Water Type**: This field asks whether the water type was contaminated. If yes, please select 'Yes' to indicate that the water was contaminated.
  - **Water Source**: This field is used to specify the source of the contamination. Please enter the specific location or source of the contaminated water.
  - **Date and Time**: This field is used to enter the date and time of the incident. Use the format "MM/DD/YYYY" for the date and "HH:MM" for the time.
  - **Time**: If the incident occurred over a specific time period, use this field to specify the time of the incident.
  - **Location**: This field is used to enter the location of the incident. Please be as specific as possible.
  - **Affected Population**: This field indicates if the affected population is at risk. If yes, select 'Yes' to indicate that the population is affected.
  - **Reporting Person**: This field indicates if the person reporting the incident is affected themselves. If yes, select 'Yes' to indicate that you are affected.
  - **Contact Info**: This field is used to provide your contact information, so we can follow up with you if needed.
