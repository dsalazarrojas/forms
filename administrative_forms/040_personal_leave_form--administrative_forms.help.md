# Personal Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request personal leave, allowing you to provide essential details for processing your leave request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
3. Once you have filled out the form, submit it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leave Request**: 
Please provide a brief description of your leave request in this field.
* **Start Date**:
Select the date you plan to start your personal leave.
* **End Date**:
Select the date you plan to end your personal leave.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out your leave request to ensure it is processed smoothly.
- Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any supporting documents or attachments, you may be able to attach them to your leave request form.
