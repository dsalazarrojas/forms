Dental Appointment Checklist Form - Help Guide
=============================

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about patients and their dental appointments. It is designed to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and consistently. Completing this form will help our dental staff prepare for the patient's visit and provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each section. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about the patient.
3. Review and update the information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the patient's visit.
* **patient name** (`patient_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name as they would like to be addressed.
* **appointment date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **dentist info** (`dentist_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about the dentist or the procedure.
* **dentist name** (`dentist_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the dentist or staff member performing the procedure.
* **appointment time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the appointment.
* **medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical information about the patient.
* **allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): Enter any known allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
* **medical procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the procedure or treatment the patient is undergoing.
* **medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): Enter any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **medical notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient's visit.
* **patient email** (`patient_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **patient phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's phone number (if applicable).
* **patient address** (`patient_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's home address (if applicable).
* **medical insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical insurance information (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact the patient or their representative.
* This form is designed to help our staff provide the best possible care for the patient. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
