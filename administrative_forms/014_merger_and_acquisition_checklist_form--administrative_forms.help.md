# Merger And Acquisition Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a checklist used by administrative staff to gather essential information and track approvals for merger and acquisition processes within the organization. It ensures that all necessary steps are taken before proceeding with a merger or acquisition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the company name related to the merger or acquisition.
2. Select the date of the acquisition.
3. Enter the price of the acquisition.
4. Select whether the merger or acquisition requires regulatory approval. If 'Yes', please provide details in the "Approvals Process" field.
5. Select whether financial approval is required. If 'Yes', please provide details in the "Approvals Process" field.
6. Select whether employee and customer approval is required. If 'Yes', please provide details in the "Approvals Process" field.
7. Enter any relevant details about the approvals process, including any regulatory approval documents, financial approval documents, employee approval documents, or customer approval documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the company related to the merger or acquisition.
- **Acquisition Date** (`acquisition_date`, date, required): Please select the date of the acquisition from the calendar.
- **Acquisition Price** (`acquisition_price`, number, required): Please enter the price of the acquisition.
- **Acquisition Type** (`acquisition_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the acquisition requires regulatory approval, 'No' otherwise.
- **Merger Date** (`merger_date`, date, required): Please select the date of the merger from the calendar.
- **Merger Price** (`merger_price`, number, required): Please enter the price of the merger.
- **Merger Type** (`merger_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the merger requires regulatory approval, 'No' otherwise.
- **Regulatory Approval** (`regulatory_approval`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if regulatory approval is required, 'No' otherwise.
- **Financial Approval** (`financial_approval`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if financial approval is required, 'No' otherwise.
- **Employee Approval** (`employee_approvals`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if employee approval is required, 'No' otherwise.
- **Customer Approval** (`customer_approvals`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if customer approval is required, 'No' otherwise.
- **Approvals Process** (`approvals_process`, text, required): Please enter any relevant details about the approvals process, including approval documents.
