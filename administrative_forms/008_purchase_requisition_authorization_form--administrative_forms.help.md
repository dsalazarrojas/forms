# Purchase Requisition Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to submit a request for a purchase requisition. The form captures necessary details for the purchase requisition, including title, category, description, requested date and time, employee and department ID, amount, and reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title**. This is the title of the purchase requisition, and it should be descriptive and clear.
2. Select the **Form Category**. Choose from the options provided, which are "Administrative Forms" and "Other".
3. Provide a **Description** of the purchase requisition. This should include all relevant details.
4. Enter the **Requested Date** and **Requested Time**. These fields are for the date and time the purchase requisition is required.
5. Enter your **Employee ID**. This is your unique employee ID number.
6. Enter your **Department ID**. This is the ID of your department.
7. Enter the **Amount** of the purchase requisition. This is the total amount of the purchase requisition.
8. Enter a **Reason** for the purchase requisition. This is a brief explanation of why the purchase requisition is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief and descriptive title for the purchase requisition.
* **Form Category**: The category of the purchase requisition, either "Administrative Forms" or "Other".
* **Description**: A detailed explanation of the purchase requisition.
* **Requested Date**: The date when the purchase requisition is required.
* **Requested Time**: The time when the purchase requisition is required.
* **Employee ID**: Your unique employee ID number.
* **Department ID**: The ID of your department.
* **Amount**: The total amount of the purchase requisition.
* **Reason**: A brief explanation of why the purchase requisition is necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Only submit the form once you have received approval from your supervisor or manager.
