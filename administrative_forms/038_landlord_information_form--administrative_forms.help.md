# Landlord Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord Information Form is designed to collect essential information from landlords for administrative purposes. This form ensures that the landlord's identity and status are accurately verified, which is vital for efficient and secure communication and decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your information carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to include your first name, last name, and email address as required fields.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Provide your address for logistical purposes.
5. Enter your date of birth for verification.
6. Select your correct landlord type (Individual or Company).
7. Specify the type of property you own (Apartment, House, or Other).
8. If necessary, add any additional notes about your property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is required and should contain your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is also required and should contain your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as a required field.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address for logistical purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Landlord Type**: Select either Individual or Company to specify your type.
* **Property Type**: Choose the correct type of property you own (Apartment, House, or Other).
* **Property Details**: This field is optional and can be used for additional information about your property.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is also optional and can be used to add any notes or comments about your property.
Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields carefully and accurately to ensure the form is processed correctly.
