# Vendor Approval Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from the vendor to verify their identity and validate their authorization to do business with the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's name in the "vendor_name" field.
2. Provide the vendor's email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter the vendor's phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Provide the vendor's address in the "vendor_address" field.
5. Once you have completed all the fields, review the form for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name (vendor_name)**: Enter the full name of the vendor as it appears on their documentation or business card.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the vendor's primary email address where you can contact them.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter the vendor's primary phone number.
* **Vendor Address (vendor_address)**: Enter the vendor's address in the format "street_address, city, state, zip".

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form for accuracy before submitting.
* If the vendor has multiple email or phone numbers, please provide the primary one.
* If the vendor has multiple addresses, please provide the address where they are registered or based.
