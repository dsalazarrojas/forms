# Donor Acknowledgment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect acknowledgments from donors after a donation has been made.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring that you have all necessary information to complete it.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount** field with the actual amount of the donation made.
3. Enter the **Date of Donation** field with the date the donation was made.
4. If applicable, enter a **Thank You Message** to express appreciation for the donation.
5. Enter the **Donor Name** field with the name of the donor.
6. Enter the **Contact Email** field with the donor's contact email.
7. Enter the **Contact Phone** field with the donor's contact phone number.
8. Select the **Donation Frequency** field with the frequency of the donation (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.).
9. Select the **Additional Donations** field with any additional donations made, if applicable.
10. Enter any **Notes** regarding the donation, including any relevant details or comments.
11. Enter any **Additional Notes** with any further information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: Enter the actual amount of the donation made. (Number)
* **Date of Donation**: Enter the date the donation was made. (Date)
* **Thank You Message**: If applicable, enter a message expressing appreciation for the donation. (Text)
* **Donor Name**: Enter the name of the donor. (Text)
* **Contact Email**: Enter the donor's contact email. (Email)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the donor's contact phone number. (Text)
* **Donation Frequency**: Select the frequency of the donation (e.g., one-time, monthly, etc.). (Select One)
* **Additional Donations**: Select any additional donations made, if applicable. (Select Multiple)
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant details or comments regarding the donation. (Note)
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any further information or comments regarding the donation. (Note)

## Tips

- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure that the form is completed in its entirety to ensure a proper acknowledgment.
- If you have any further questions, please contact our team for assistance.
- This form is intended for internal use only and is not intended for public distribution.
