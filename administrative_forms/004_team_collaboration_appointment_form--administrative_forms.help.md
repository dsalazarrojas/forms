# Team Collaboration Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about upcoming meetings and appointments for team collaboration and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the `Meeting Date` in the date field. This is the date of the meeting or appointment.
2.  Enter the `Start Time` and `End Time` of the meeting in the respective time fields. This will help ensure you're aware of the start and end times of the meeting.
3.  Specify the `Location` of the meeting in the text field.
4.  Choose the `Attendees` for the meeting by selecting the relevant options. This will help notify team members who need to attend.
5.  Provide a brief `Description` of the meeting in the text field.
6.  Choose whether to enable `Reminder` for the meeting.
7.  Select the `Notification` method you'd like to receive (Email, Phone, or Both).
8.  If you've chosen to receive a notification, select the `Notification Time` in the time field.
9.  Choose your preferred `Notification Method` (None, Auto).
10.  Finally, review your entries to ensure they are accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Meeting Date (Page 1)**: Enter the date of the meeting or appointment.
*   **Start Time (Page 1)**: Enter the start time of the meeting.
*   **End Time (Page 1)**: Enter the end time of the meeting.
*   **Location (Page 1)**: Specify the location of the meeting.
*   **Attendees (Page 1)**: Choose the team members who need to attend the meeting.
*   **Description (Page 1)**: Provide a brief description of the meeting.
*   **Reminder (Page 1)**: Choose whether to enable the reminder.
*   **Notification (Page 1)**: Select the method you'd like to receive notifications (Email, Phone, or Both).
*   **Notification Time (Page 1)**: If you've chosen to receive a notification, select the notification time.
*   **Notification Method (Page 1)**: Choose your preferred notification method (None, Auto).
