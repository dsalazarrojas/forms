# Ferris Wheel Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ferris Wheel Safety Inspection Form is a tool used to record and report on safety standards compliance and deficiencies during a Ferris Wheel inspection. It helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded for regulatory compliance and risk assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the "User Input" field.
2. Select your name from the "Inspector Name" dropdown menu.
3. Sign your name in the "Inspector Signature" field.
4. Enter the date and time of the inspection in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
5. Select the safety standards that were complied with in the "Safety Standards Complied" field.
6. Record any safety standards deficiencies in the "Safety Standards Deficiencies" field.
7. Identify the person who identified the safety standards deficiencies in the "Safety Standards Defic Signature" field.
8. Enter the date and time of the safety standards deficiency in the "Date of Safety Standards Defic" and "Time of Safety Standards Defic" fields.
9. Select the level of safety standards deficiency in the "Safety Standards Defic" field.
10. Provide any additional notes about the safety standards deficiency in the "Safety Standards Defic Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for entering your name and title.
* **Inspector Name**: This field is for selecting your name from the dropdown menu. Please ensure that you are listed as an approved inspector.
* **Inspector Signature**: This field is for signing your name as the inspector.
* **Date Completed**: This field is for entering the date of the inspection.
* **Time Completed**: This field is for entering the time of the inspection.
* **Safety Standards Complied**: This field is for selecting the safety standards that were complied with.
* **Safety Standards Deficiencies**: This field is for recording any safety standards deficiencies.
* **Safety Standards Defic Signature**: This field is for identifying the person who identified the safety standards deficiency.
* **Date of Safety Standards Defic**: This field is for entering the date of the safety standards deficiency.
* **Time of Safety Standards Defic**: This field is for entering the time of the safety standards deficiency.
* **Safety Standards Defic**: This field is for selecting the level of safety standards deficiency.
* **Safety Standards Defic Notes**: This field is for providing additional notes about the safety standards deficiency.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you follow the safety standards and procedures outlined by the regulatory bodies.
- Please record all necessary information accurately and thoroughly.
- Please use this form for regular inspections and maintenance schedules.
- Please review and update the form regularly to ensure that all information is up-to-date.
