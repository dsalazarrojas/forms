# Incident Escalation Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Incident Escalation Report form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the incident from the dropdown menu. This will help the team quickly identify the type of incident being reported.
2. Enter a brief description of the incident in the Description field. This will help the team understand the context and severity of the incident.
3. Choose the assigned tool from the dropdown menu. This will indicate which tool was involved in the incident.
4. Select the incident time zone from the dropdown menu. This will help the team understand the time zone in which the incident occurred.
5. Choose the user's role from the dropdown menu. This will indicate which user was involved in the incident.
6. Select the incident type from the dropdown menu. This will help the team quickly identify the type of incident being reported.
7. Choose the incident status from the dropdown menu. This will indicate whether the incident is active, inactive, or closed.
8. Select the incident priority from the dropdown menu. This will help the team understand the urgency of the incident.
9. Choose the incident severity from the dropdown menu. This will help the team understand the severity of the incident.
10. Enter a brief reason for escalation in the Escalation Reason field. This will help the team understand why the incident was escalated.
11. Select the escalation status from the dropdown menu. This will indicate whether the escalation is active, inactive, or closed.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Notes field. This is an optional field for any additional information.
13. Enter any notes from the user in the User Notes field. This is an optional field for any additional information from the user.
14. Enter any notes from the team lead in the Team Lead Notes field. This is an optional field for any additional information from the team lead.
15. Enter any review or comments from the reviewer in the Reviewer Notes field. This is an optional field for any additional information from the reviewer.
16. Enter the reviewer's name in the Reviewer Name field. This is an optional field for the reviewer's name.

* **Incident Time Zone** (`Incident Time Zone`, `select_one`, required): Choose the incident time zone from the dropdown menu.
* **User Role** (`User Role`, `select_one`, required): Choose the user's role from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Type** (`Incident Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the incident type from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Status** (`Incident Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the incident status from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Priority** (`Incident Priority`, `select_one`, required): Choose the incident priority from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Severity** (`Incident Severity`, `select_one`, required): Choose the incident severity from the dropdown menu.
* **Escalation Reason** (`Escalation Reason`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief reason for escalation.
* **Assigned Date** (`Assigned Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the assigned date.
* **Due Date** (`Due Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the due date.
* **Escalation Status** (`Escalation Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the escalation status from the dropdown menu.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **User Notes** (`User Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the user.
* **Team Lead Notes** (`Team Lead Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the team lead.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`Reviewer Notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any review or comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the reviewer's name.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you are filling out this form for a valid incident and that you do not duplicate an existing incident.
* Use the form to document and track incidents that require escalation. 
* Be as detailed and specific as possible in the fields provided to ensure that the team can understand the incident and the escalation process. 
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that the incident is up-to-date and accurate. 
* Communicate with the team and other stakeholders as necessary to ensure that the incident is resolved efficiently.
