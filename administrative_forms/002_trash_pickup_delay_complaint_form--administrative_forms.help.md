# Trash Pickup Delay Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trash Pickup Delay Complaint Form is designed to report a delayed trash pickup event to the relevant authorities. This form helps to capture necessary information for follow-up actions and efficient resolution of the issue.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To report a delayed trash pickup event, please fill out this form with the necessary details.
2.  Fill in the "Resident Information" field with your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
3.  Enter the date of the delayed trash pickup in the "Delay Pickup Date" field if it is known or can be estimated.
4.  Provide a brief description of the issue in the "Description" field.
5.  Enter a phone number in the "Phone Number" field for the relevant contact.
6.  If necessary, select one or more managers from the "Select Manager" field for further investigation.
7.  Any additional comments can be typed in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Information** (text): This field captures the contact details of the resident reporting the issue.
* **Delay Pickup Date** (date): Enter the date of the delayed trash pickup or an estimated date if it is not known.
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the issue, including any relevant details.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for follow-up contacts.
* **Select Manager** (select multiple): If necessary, select one or more managers to involve in the follow-up actions.
* **Comments** (note): Any additional comments can be typed in this field for further reference.

## Tips

* Please provide as much information as possible to help resolve the issue efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank and provide a brief description in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
