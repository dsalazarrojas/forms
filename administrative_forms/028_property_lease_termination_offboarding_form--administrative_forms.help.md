# property_lease_termination_offboarding_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the process of ending a property lease agreement between a landlord and tenant. It helps to track and record important details about the termination, including the termination date, reason for termination, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the landlord and tenant sections.
2. Choose the reason for termination from the select options provided.
3. Enter the termination date, notice period, and other relevant details.
4. If applicable, enter the rent owed, move out date, and security deposit details.
5. Enter the contact preferences and contact details for the tenant.
6. Verify the form for any errors or missing information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Information** (id: 1, required: false): Enter the name and any relevant contact information for the landlord.
* **Tenant Information** (id: 2, required: false): Enter the name and any relevant contact information for the tenant.
* **Lease Details** (id: 3, required: false): Describe the lease agreement being terminated, including its terms and conditions.
* **Termination Date** (id: 4, required: false): Enter the date the lease is being terminated.
* **Reason for Termination** (id: 5, required: false): Choose the reason for termination from the provided options.
* **Notice Period** (id: 6, required: false): Enter the number of days or weeks notice given to the tenant before the termination date.
* **Rent Owed** (id: 7, required: false): If rent is owed, enter the amount.
* **Move Out Date** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the date the tenant moved out of the property.
* **Security Deposit** (id: 9, required: false): If a security deposit was paid, enter the amount.
* **Last Property Inspection** (id: 10, required: false): Enter the date of the last property inspection.
* **Lease End Date** (id: 11, required: false): Enter the date the lease agreement ended.
* **Key Return** (id: 12, required: false): Enter a note about the return of keys or other property.
* **Property Status** (id: 13, required: false): Describe the current status of the property.
* **Contact Preference** (id: 14, required: false): Choose how you would like to contact the tenant (Phone, Email, In-Person).
* **Contact Details** (id: 15, required: false): Enter the tenant's contact details (name, phone, email, etc.).
* **Phone** (id: 16, required: false): Enter the tenant's phone number.
* **Email** (id: 17, required: false): Enter the tenant's email address.
* **Name** (id: 18, required: false): Enter the name of the person handling the offboarding process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, if any.
* Double-check for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* This form is for property lease terminations, so ensure you are using it for the correct purpose.
