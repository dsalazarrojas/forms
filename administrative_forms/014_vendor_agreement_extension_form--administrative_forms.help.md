# Vendor Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to extend the agreement term between the company and a vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Vendor Name field with the name of the vendor whose agreement term you are extending.
2. Enter the Extension Date in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Select the Agreement Term status from the available options (Yes, No, or Pending).
4. If applicable, provide a Reason for Extension to explain the reason for the extension.
5. Enter the name of the Vendor Representative in the space provided.
6. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor whose agreement term is being extended.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the extension in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the agreement term from the options: Yes, No, or Pending.
* **Extension Status** (`extension_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the extension from the options: Pending, Active, or Inactive.
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): If applicable, provide a brief explanation for the extension.
* **Vendor Representative** (`vendor_representative`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor representative responsible for the extension.
