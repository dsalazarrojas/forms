# Adhesion Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Adhesion Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a service provided by a service provider to a client. This form is used to gather information and ensure that both parties understand their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your Client Full Name, Client Company (if applicable), and Client Email.
2. Enter the Description of Service, Service Start Date, and Service End Date (if applicable).
3. Select the correct answers to the terms and conditions questions, ensuring you have read and understood each section.
4. Sign the form digitally using the Electronic Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Adhesion Agreement --** (`note`, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Client Full Name** (`text`, required): Enter the full name of the client.
* **Client Company** (`text`, required/optional): If the client is a company, enter the name of the company (otherwise, leave blank).
* **Client Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication with the client.
* **Client Phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number for contact.
* **Service Provider** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the company providing the service.
* **Description of Service** (`text`, required): Briefly describe the service to be provided.
* **Agreement Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date when this agreement is made.
* **Service Start Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date when the service will begin.
* **Service End Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date when the service will end (if applicable).
* **Terms and Conditions Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Acceptance of Terms** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I accept all terms" to indicate you agree to all terms and conditions.
* **Payment Terms Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the payment terms.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Liability Limitations Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the liability limitations.
* **Confidentiality Terms Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the confidentiality requirements.
* **Dispute Resolution Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the dispute resolution process.
* **Intellectual Property Terms Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the intellectual property provisions.
* **Force Majeure Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the force majeure clause.
* **Amendment Rights Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand how this agreement can be amended.
* **Termination Conditions Acknowledged** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you understand the conditions for termination.
* **Signature Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of signing this agreement.
* **Client Signature** (`text`, required): Enter your electronic signature to confirm your agreement.
