# Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Reimbursement Claim Form is used to submit a request for reimbursement for official business trips taken on company time. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure timely and efficient processing of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your travel information.
- Ensure that you have all necessary documentation and records for your trip before submitting this form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Travel Date** (Travel Date, date, optional): Enter the date of your trip.
  * Example: 2022-01-01
  * Format: YYYY-MM-DD
- **start_location** (Start Location, text, optional): Enter the starting location of your trip.
  * Example: New York
  * Format: text input
- **end_location** (End Location, text, optional): Enter the ending location of your trip.
  * Example: Los Angeles
  * Format: text input
- **Distance** (Distance, number, optional): Enter the total distance traveled.
  * Example: 120
  * Format: numeric input (e.g., 123.45)
- **Odometer Reading** (Odometer Reading, text, optional): Enter the odometer reading of your vehicle before and after the trip.
  * Example: 12345
  * Format: text input
- **Total Distance** (Total Distance, number, optional): Enter the total distance traveled.
  * Example: 120
  * Format: numeric input (e.g., 123.45)
- **Purpose of the Trip** (Purpose of the Trip, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of your trip.
  * Example: Business meeting
  * Format: text input
- **Claim Amount** (Claim Amount, number, optional): Enter the amount you are requesting for reimbursement.
  * Example: 100.00
  * Format: numeric input (e.g., 123.45)
- **Claim Status** (Claim Status, select one, optional): Select the status of your claim.
  * Example: Active
  * Options: Active, Denied, In Progress, Paid, Pending, Resolved
- **Assisted by** (Assisted by, select multiple, optional): Select any team members who assisted you during the trip.
  * Example: chatjimmy, chatjimmy2
  * Options: chatjimmy, chatjimmy2
- **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your trip.
  * Example: This was a business meeting for a new client.
  * Format: free text input
- **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  * Example: john.doe@example.com
  * Format: email input
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  * Example: 123-456-7890
  * Format: text input
- **Submit** (Submit, select multiple, optional): Select how you wish to submit this form.
  * Example: Submit
  * Options: Submit, Other

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- You can submit additional supporting documentation, such as receipts or proof of expenses, with your form.
- If you are unsure about any part of the process, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
