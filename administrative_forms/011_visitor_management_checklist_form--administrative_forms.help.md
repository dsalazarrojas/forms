# Visitor Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information for visitor management purposes. It gathers personal details, visit schedules, and status information to ensure accurate visitor tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone fields with your personal details.
2. For your company, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you belong to a company or not.
3. Enter your Purpose of Visit to help our staff understand the reason for your visit.
4. Provide your Arrival Time and Expected Departure Time to schedule your visit smoothly with our staff.
5. Enter Visitor Name, Company, and Title to identify you and your visiting group.
6. Select the Visitor Status as Active or Inactive, and Visitor Group as 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your status and affiliation.
7. Finally, choose the Employee Name, Title, and Status (Active or Inactive) to indicate the person you are visiting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Date of Birth (Date, required: false)**: Enter your date of birth in the required format.
* **Email (Email, required: false)**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone (Text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address (Text, required: false)**: Enter your office or home address for contact purposes.
* **Company (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you belong to a company, 'No' otherwise.
* **Purpose of Visit (Text, required: false)**: Briefly describe the reason for your visit.
* **Arrival Time (Time, required: false)**: Enter your planned arrival time.
* **Expected Departure Time (Time, required: false)**: Enter your expected departure time.
* **Visitor Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the person visiting.
* **Visitor Company (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Visitor Title (Text, required: false)**: Enter your visiting title.
* **Employee Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the employee you are visiting.
* **Employee Title (Text, required: false)**: Enter the title of the employee you are visiting.
* **Visitor Status (Select One, required: false)**: Choose Active or Inactive status for the visitor.
* **Visitor Group (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you are in a group, 'No' otherwise.
* **Employee Status (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select Active or Inactive status for the employee.
* **Visitor Group (Select Multiple, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you are part of the group you visit, 'No' otherwise. 
* **Employee Name (Text, required: false)**: Enter the name of the person you are visiting.
