# Visitor Management Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information for visitor management purposes. It gathers personal details, visit schedules, and status information to ensure accurate visitor tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Email, and Phone fields with your personal details.
2. For your company, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you belong to a company or not.
3. Enter your Purpose of Visit to help our staff understand the reason for your visit.
4. Provide your Arrival Time and Expected Departure Time to schedule your visit smoothly with our staff.
5. Enter Visitor Name, Company, and Title to identify you and your visiting group.
6. Select the Visitor Status as Active or Inactive, and Visitor Group as 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your status and affiliation.
7. Finally, choose the Employee Name, Title, and Status (Active or Inactive) to indicate the person you are visiting.
