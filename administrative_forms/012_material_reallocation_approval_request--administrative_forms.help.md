# Material Reallocation Approval Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Reallocation Approval Request form is used to request approval for the transfer of materials from one location to another within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct material type (e.g., Metal, Plastic, Wood) from the dropdown list.
2. Enter the quantity of materials to be reallocated.
3. Choose the locations where the materials are currently located (Location From) and where they will be moved to (Location To).
4. Provide a brief reason for the reallocation request.
5. Select the reason for the reallocation request (optional).
6. Choose the approver who will review and approve the request (optional).
7. Enter the date when the reallocation will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Material Type** (`material_type`, Select One): Select the type of material you are reallocating (e.g., Metal, Plastic, Wood).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, Number): Enter the exact amount of materials to be reallocated.
* **Location From** (`location_from`, Select Multiple): Choose the location(s) where the materials are currently located.
* **Location To** (`location_to`, Select Multiple): Choose the location(s) where the materials will be moved to.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text): Provide a brief explanation for the reallocation request.
* **Reason For** (`reason_for`, Text): Enter the reason for the reallocation request (optional).
* **Approval** (`approval`, Select One): Choose the approver who will review and approve the request (optional).
* **Date** (`date`, Date): Enter the date when the reallocation will take place.
Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required to be filled out, but it's recommended to provide as much information as possible to increase the efficiency of the approval process.
