# Conference Agenda Revision Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Conference Agenda Revision Request Form is designed for conference organizers to request changes to the conference agenda. This form collects essential information about the revision request, including the name and email of the requester, the type of change, and the proposed changes. It ensures that all necessary details are captured for efficient processing and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the "Submission Confirmation" field, either "Submit Revision Request" or "Save as Draft".
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Requester Name" and "Email Address" fields.
3. Choose your role at the conference from the "Your Role" dropdown menu.
4. Provide event details in the "Conference Information" field.
5. Enter the conference name and dates in the "Conference Name" and "Conference Dates" fields.
6. Specify the agenda item to be revised in the "Agenda Item to Revise" field.
7. Identify the agenda item with the "Item ID or Name" field.
8. Provide the current schedule in the "Current Schedule" field.
9. Indicate the type of revision in the "Type of Revision" field.
10. Describe the proposed changes in the "Proposed Changes" field.
11. Specify the proposed start and end times, new date, and location in the respective fields.
12. Provide the reason for the revision in the "Reason for Revision" field.
13. Assess the impact of the change on others in the "Impact Assessment" field.
14. Select the urgency level of the change in the "Urgency Level" field.
15. List stakeholders who need to be notified in the "Stakeholders Notified" field.
16. Describe backup plans in the "Alternative Options" field, if applicable.
17. Specify who needs to approve the change in the "Approval Needed From" field.
18. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agenda Revision Request**: Note field for brief requests.
* **Request Date**: Date field to record the date of the request.
* **Requester Name**: Text field for the name of the person requesting the change.
* **Email Address**: Text field for the contact email of the requester.
* **Your Role**: Select-one field to select the role of the requester (Organizer, Speaker, Attendee, Staff, Committee Member, Other).
* **Conference Information**: Note field for additional conference event details.
* **Conference Name**: Text field to enter the conference name.
* **Conference Dates**: Text field to enter the conference dates.
* **Agenda Item to Revise**: Note field for specifying the agenda item to be revised.
* **Item ID or Name**: Text field to identify the agenda item.
* **Current Schedule**: Text field for the current schedule.
* **Type of Revision**: Select-one field to select the type of revision (Time Change, Date Change, Location Change, Speaker Change, Topic Change, Duration Change, Cancellation, New Item Addition).
* **Proposed Changes**: Note field for describing the proposed changes.
* **New Start Time**: Time field to enter the proposed start time.
* **New End Time**: Time field to enter the proposed end time.
* **New Date**: Date field to enter the proposed date.
* **New Location**: Text field for the proposed location.
* **Reason for Revision**: Text field for explaining why the change is needed.
* **Impact Assessment**: Text field to assess the impact on others.
* **Urgency Level**: Select-one field to select the urgency level (Low Can Wait, Medium Within Week, High Within 48 Hours, Critical Immediate).
* **Stakeholders Notified**: Text field to list stakeholders who need to be notified.
* **Alternative Options**: Text field to describe backup plans, if applicable.
* **Approval Needed From**: Text field to specify who needs to approve the change.
* **Additional Comments**: Text field for any additional information.
* **Submission Confirmation**: Select-one field to confirm whether to submit or save as draft.
* **Submit Revision Request**: Confirm that the form has been completed and is ready for review.
* **Save as Draft**: Confirm that the form has been saved as a draft.
