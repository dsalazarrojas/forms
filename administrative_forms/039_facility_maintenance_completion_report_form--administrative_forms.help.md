# Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form is used to report on the completion status of facility maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
2. Fill out the form for each maintenance activity separately.
3. Check the relevant boxes to indicate the completion status of each activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 1
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 1**: This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the form and its purpose.

### 2. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 2
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 2**: This page is for additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity. Enter any relevant details or descriptions of the work performed.

### 3. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 3
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 3**: This page is for recording the number of hours spent on the maintenance activity. Enter the total number of hours worked.

### 4. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 4
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 4**: This page is for recording the number of people involved in the maintenance activity. Enter the total number of personnel involved.

### 5. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 5
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 5**: This page is for recording the duration of the maintenance activity. Enter the start and end times of the activity.

### 6. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 6
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 6**: This page is for recording the date the maintenance activity was completed. Enter the date of completion.

### 7. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 7
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 7**: This page is to confirm that the maintenance activity was completed. Check "Yes" if the activity was completed, or "No" if it was not.

### 8. Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 8
* **Facility Maintenance Completion Report Form Page 8**: This page is for selecting additional activities that were completed as part of the maintenance activity. Check "Yes" for any additional activities that were performed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each page separately for each maintenance activity.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Use this form to report on completed activities only. Do not report on ongoing or future maintenance activities.
