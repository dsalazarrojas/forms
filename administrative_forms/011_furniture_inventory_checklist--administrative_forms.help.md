# furniture_inventory_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "furniture_inventory_checklist" form is designed to collect essential information about items in a furniture inventory. This form helps in tracking and managing the inventory by providing a structured way to input and store details about each item.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by completing the "General Information" section, which includes fields for the items list and any additional notes or comments.
2.  In the "Items List" section, enter a list of items in the inventory, one item per line.
3.  Move on to the "Item Details" section, where you can specify the material and condition of each item.
4.  Enter the "Purchase Date" of the item, which is the date it was purchased.
5.  Specify the quantity and price of the item.
6.  In the "Seller" section, enter the seller's contact information, including their email and phone number (if provided).
7.  Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the item or the overall condition of the items list.
*   **Items List** (`items_list`, text, required): Enter a list of items in the inventory, one item per line.
*   **Item Details** (`item_details`, text, required): Specify the material and condition of each item.
*   **Material and Condition** (`material_and_condition`, select_one, required): Choose the material (new, used, refurbished, damaged, repaired) and condition of the item.
*   **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Enter the date the item was purchased.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the quantity of the item.
*   **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price of the item.
*   **Seller** (`seller`, text, required): Enter the seller's contact information, including their email and phone number (if provided).
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the item.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the seller's contact email (if provided).
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the seller's contact phone number (if provided).

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
*   Use clear and concise language when entering text fields.
*   Ensure that the material and condition are accurately selected for each item.
*   Enter the correct date for the purchase date.
*   Double-check the quantity and price of the item.
*   Enter the correct contact information for the seller (if provided).
*   Use the "Notes" section to add any additional information that may be helpful for future reference.
