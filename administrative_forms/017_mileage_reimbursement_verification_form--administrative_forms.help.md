# Mileage Reimbursement Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for mileage reimbursement claims. It collects information about the user's travel, including the date, locations, distance traveled, and expenses incurred. The form is also used to track the status of the reimbursement request and ensure that it is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. Attach any supporting documents, such as proof of expenses.
4. Review your form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`): A text field for the user to enter any additional information about their claim.
* **Mileage Date** (`mileage_date`): The date of the trip.
* **Starting Location** (`mileage_start_location`): The location where the trip started.
* **End Location** (`mileage_end_location`): The location where the trip ended.
* **Mileage Distance** (`mileage_distance`): The distance traveled during the trip.
* **Odometer Reading** (`mileage_odometer_reading`): The reading of the vehicle's odometer at the start and end of the trip.
* **Rate** (`mileage_rate`): The reimbursement rate for the trip.
* **Currency** (`mileage_currency`): The currency of the reimbursement. Choose from USD, EUR, or CAD.
* **Expenses** (`mileage_expenses`): Select any expenses incurred during the trip, such as Meals, Hotel, Parking, or Fuel.
* **Proof Attachment** (`mileage_proof_attachment`): Attach any supporting documents, such as receipts or invoices.
* **Notes** (`mileage_notes`): Any additional comments or notes about the trip.
* **Email** (`email`): The email address of the user.
* **Phone** (`phone`): The phone number of the user.
* **Confirm** (`user_confirmation`): Confirm your details accurately.
* **Confirm Form Details** (`form_confirmation`): Confirm the form details are correct.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Select whether the form was submitted by the assigned tool or a manual form generator.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): The date the form was submitted.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`): Select who approved the form, either the Manager or Admin.
* **Approved On** (`approved_on`): The date the form was approved.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Any comments about the form.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`): Select the current status of the form, either Open or Closed.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`): A unique ID for the submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select whether a tool was assigned to process the form.
