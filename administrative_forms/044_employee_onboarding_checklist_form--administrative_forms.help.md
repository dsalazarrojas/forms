# Employee Onboarding Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information about new hires and their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your new hire's personal details, such as their name, employee ID, and email.
2. Provide information about your supervisor, including their name and contact information.
3. Fill out the job-specific details, like job title, department, and location.
4. Check the benefits you're eligible for, such as health, dental, and vision insurance.
5. Add any emergency contact information.
6. Finally, review and submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Info (Page 1)**: Enter your new hire's personal details.
	+ **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, required): Enter your employee ID number, which can be found on your ID card or in your offer letter.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Supervisor Info (Page 2)**: Enter your supervisor's information.
	+ **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your supervisor's full name.
	+ **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your supervisor's contact number.
* **Supervisor Contact** (`supervisor_info`, `text`, required): Enter any additional contact information for your supervisor.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter your start date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter your expected end date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your offer letter or ID card.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department as it appears on your offer letter or ID card.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, `text`, required): Enter your job role as it appears on your offer letter or ID card.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your work location.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `text`, required): Enter your work hours as they appear on your offer letter or ID card.
* **Benefits Info (Page 3)**: Check the benefits you're eligible for.
	+ **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have health insurance.
	+ **Dental Insurance** (`dental_insurance`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have dental insurance.
	+ **Vision Insurance** (`vision_insurance`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have vision insurance.

* **Emergency Contact (Page 5)**: Enter your emergency contact information.
	+ **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's full name.
	+ **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required): Enter your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Benefits Info (Page 6)**: Check if you have a retirement plan.
