# Vendor Clerk Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vendor clerk, including their contact details and background information. The form will be used by the company or organization to manage their interactions and relationships with the vendor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the title of the questionnaire.
2. Select the name of the contact person from the list of options.
3. Enter the email address of the contact person.
4. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
5. Enter the cell phone number of the contact person.
6. Enter the fax number of the contact person.
7. Enter the address of the contact person.
8. Enter the apartment or suite number of the contact person.
9. Enter the city where the contact person is located.
10. Select the state where the contact person is located from the list of options.
11. Enter the zip code of the contact person.
12. Select the country of the contact person from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vendor_clerk_interview_questionnaire_title**: This is the title of the questionnaire. It's a text input field where you can enter a brief description of the questionnaire.
* **contact_name**: This field is a dropdown list of contact names. Select the correct name of the contact person from the list.
* **contact_email**: This is the email address of the contact person. Enter the email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **contact_phone**: This is the phone number of the contact person. Enter the 10-digit phone number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.
* **contact_cell_phone**: This is the cell phone number of the contact person. Enter the 10-digit cell phone number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.
* **contact_fax**: This is the fax number of the contact person. Enter the 10-digit fax number in the format `(123) 456-7890`.
* **contact_address_1**: This is the street address of the contact person. Enter the complete address in the format `123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345`.
* **contact_address_2**: This is the apartment or suite number of the contact person. Enter the number if applicable.
* **contact_city**: This is the city where the contact person is located. Enter the city name in the format `Anytown, USA`.
* **contact_state**: This is the state where the contact person is located. Select the state from the list of options.
* **contact_zip**: This is the zip code of the contact person. Enter the 5-digit zip code in the format `12345`.
* **contact_country**: This is the country where the contact person is located. Select the country from the list of options.
* **vendor_clerk_interview_questionnaire_section_1**: This is a note field where you can enter any additional information about the vendor clerk. Enter any relevant background information about the vendor clerk.
