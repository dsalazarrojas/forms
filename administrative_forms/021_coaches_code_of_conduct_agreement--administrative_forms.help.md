# Coaches' Code Of Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that all coaches in [insert organization/league name] uphold high standards of behavior and sportsmanship. By completing this form, coaches agree to abide by the principles of fair play, respect, and professionalism, promoting a positive and safe environment for all participants. This agreement is an essential part of our organization's code of conduct and is expected to be reviewed and signed annually by all coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, answering each question truthfully.
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information (full name, email, and phone number).
- Select the relevant options for your coaching level, years of coaching experience, and other applicable fields.
- Confirm that you understand and agree to all terms of the code of conduct.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
- Finally, a witness's name and signature will be required in some cases (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Coach Full Name**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

- **Team or Organization Name**: If you are part of a team or organization, enter the name.
- **Sport or Activity**: Describe the sport or activity you coach.
- **Coaching Level**: Select the level of coaching you perform:
  - **Youth Recreational**: Recreational coaching for youth players.
  - **Youth Competitive**: Competitive coaching for youth players.
  - **High School**: Coaching in high school settings.
  - **College**: Coaching in college settings.
  - **Professional**: Professional coaching (e.g., national or international teams).
  - **Adult Amateur**: Coaching adult amateur teams or players.
- **Years of Coaching Experience**: Select the number of years you've been coaching:
  - **First Year**: This is your first year of coaching.
  - **1-3 years**: You've been coaching for one to three years.
  - **3-5 years**: You've been coaching for three to five years.
  - **5-10 years**: You've been coaching for five to ten years.
  - **Over 10 years**: You've been coaching for more than ten years.
