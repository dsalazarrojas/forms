# Childcare Assistance Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of childcare assistance records to a third party, such as a new program or a government agency. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the proper handling of the records release process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, following the instructions and hints provided for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Release Date** (`id: 1`): Enter the date on which you are authorizing the release of the records.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`id: 2`): Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian associated with the child.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`id: 3`): Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian associated with the child.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`id: 4`): Enter the primary email address of the parent or guardian for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Address** (`id: 6`): Enter the full street address of the parent or guardian.
* **Child First Name** (`id: 7`): Enter the legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`id: 8`): Enter the legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`id: 9`): Enter the birth date of the child.
* **Childcare Program Name** (`id: 10`): Enter the name of the childcare assistance program associated with the child.
* **Program Case Number** (`id: 11`): Enter the case or file number associated with the program.
* **Type of Records Requested** (`id: 12`): Select the type of records you are requesting to be released, such as Enrollment, Attendance, Payment, or Eligibility determination records.
* **Records Date Range** (`id: 13`): Specify the date range for the records you are requesting.
* **Release Records To** (`id: 14`): Select the organization or individual to whom the records should be released.
* **Recipient Organization** (`id: 15`): Enter the name of the organization or agency receiving the records.
* **Recipient Address** (`id: 16`): Enter the address for delivery of the records.
* **Recipient Email** (`id: 17`): Enter the email address for electronic delivery of the records.
* **Recipient Phone** (`id: 18`): Enter the phone number for the recipient.
* **Delivery Method** (`id: 19`): Select the method of delivery, such as mail, email, fax, or pickup in person.
* **Purpose of Records Release** (`id: 20`): Select the purpose of the records release, such as transfer to a new program or legal proceedings.
* **Purpose Details** (`id: 21`): Provide additional details about the purpose of the records release.
* **Release Authorization Duration** (`id: 22`): Select the duration of the release authorization, such as one-time only or 60 days.
* **Authorization Expiration Date** (`id: 23`): Enter the date on which the release authorization expires.
* **Confidential Information Handling** (`id: 24`): Select how confidential information should be handled, such as release all information or release with redactions.
* **Fee Acknowledgement** (`id: 25`): Confirm that you are aware of any potential fees associated with records copying.
* **Revocation Understanding** (`id: 26`): Confirm that you understand that you can revoke this authorization.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`id: 27`): Type your full name as the electronic signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (`id: 28`): Enter the date of the electronic signature.
* **Witness Name** (`id: 29`): Enter the name of the witness if required (not required for notarized signatures).
* **Notary Required** (`id: 30`): Confirm whether notarization is required for this release.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Ensure that the recipient's email address is valid and able to receive electronic delivery of the records.
* Be aware of the potential fees associated with records copying and confirm your understanding in the Fee Acknowledgement field.
* Understand that you can revoke this authorization at any time before the release expiration date.
* If notarization is required, ensure that the signature is properly notarized.
