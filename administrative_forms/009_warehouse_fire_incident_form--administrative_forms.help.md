# Warehouse Fire Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and document incidents of fire in the warehouse. It captures essential information to aid in the investigation, assessment, and prevention of future fires. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the address of the warehouse where the fire occurred.
2. Specify the location of the fire within the warehouse.
3. Provide the cause of the fire, if known.
4. Indicate the size of the fire.
5. Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether emergency services were called during the incident.
6. Check 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether there were any injuries or fatalities resulting from the fire.
7. Enter a numerical value to estimate the extent of the damage.
8. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the effectiveness of the fire suppression method used.
9. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the fire was extinguished.
10. Enter a detailed description of the damage caused by the fire.
11. Provide any prevention measures that could be taken to prevent future fires.
12. Enter your name and contact information to serve as the incident reporter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address of Warehouse**: Enter the physical address of the warehouse where the fire occurred. This will help in tracing the location and facilitate any necessary investigations.
* **Fire Location**: Describe the exact location within the warehouse where the fire started or was located. This information is crucial for assessing the situation and taking corrective actions.
* **Cause of Fire**: If known, provide a brief description of the cause of the fire. This can help in identifying potential root causes of the incident.
* **Fire Size**: Estimate the size of the fire, using numerical values to gauge its extent. This information can aid in determining the severity of the incident.
* **Emergency Services Called**: Check 'Yes' if emergency services were alerted and responded to the situation. If 'No', provide an explanation for why not.
* **Injuries**: Check 'Yes' if there were any injuries as a result of the fire, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Fatalities**: Check 'Yes' if there were any fatalities as a result of the fire, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Damage**: Enter a numerical value to estimate the extent of the damage caused by the fire.
* **Fire Suppression Method**: Check 'Yes' if the fire suppression method (e.g., fire extinguisher, sprinkler system) was effective in putting out the fire. If 'No', provide an explanation for why not.
* **Fire Extinguished**: Check 'Yes' if the fire was completely extinguished. If 'No', indicate why not.
* **Damage Description**: Enter a detailed description of the damage caused by the fire, including any significant losses or destructions.
* **Prevention Measures**: Suggest any measures that could be taken to prevent similar incidents in the future. This can include improvements to fire safety measures, better maintenance, or employee training.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name and contact information to serve as the incident reporter. This ensures that your details are recorded accurately for any follow-up or investigations.

Tips:

* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information to aid in the investigation and prevention of future incidents.
* Be as detailed as possible, especially when describing the cause, location, and damage of the fire.
* If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, ensure you are authorized to do so and provide their contact information for follow-up.
