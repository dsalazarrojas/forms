# Religious Holiday Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Religious Holiday Time Off Form is used to request time off for employees who need to observe a religious holiday.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee information in the "employee" field.
2.  Enter your employee ID in the "employee_id" field.
3.  Select the date you need to take time off in the "date" field.
4.  Choose the start and end dates of your time off period in the "time_off_start" and "time_off_end" fields.
5.  Select the reason for your time off in the "reason" field.
6.  Choose the holiday(s) you are observing in the "holiday" field.
7.  If applicable, enter any additional comments about your time off request in the "comments" field.
8.  Confirm that you have submitted your request correctly and reviewed it thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **employee** (`employee`, `text`, required): Enter your name and employee information in this field.
*   **employee_id** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID number.
*   **date** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for which you need time off.
*   **time_off_start** (`time_off_start`, `date`, required): Choose the start date of your time off period.
*   **time_off_end** (`time_off_end`, `date`, optional): Choose the end date of your time off period.
*   **reason** (`reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for your time off request (e.g., religious observance).
*   **holiday** (`holiday`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the holiday(s) you are observing.
*   **comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your time off request.
*   **approved** (`approved`, `text`, required): Enter "approved" to confirm that you have submitted your request correctly.
*   **declined** (`declined`, `text`, required): Enter "declined" to confirm that you have not submitted your request correctly.
*   **pending** (`pending`, `text`, required): Enter "pending" to confirm that your request is still being processed.

Note: Please ensure that you've filled out all required fields and reviewed your request carefully before submitting it.
