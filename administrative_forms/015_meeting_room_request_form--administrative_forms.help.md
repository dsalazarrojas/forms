# Meeting Room Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a meeting room for a specific date and time. It is intended for users to provide the required information to book a meeting room, ensuring that the room is available for their scheduled meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Start Date of the meeting in the "Meeting Room Start Date" field.
2. Enter the Start Time of the meeting in the "Meeting Room Start Time" field.
3. Enter the End Time of the meeting in the "Meeting Room End Time" field.
4. Enter the duration of the meeting in the "Meeting Room Duration" field.
5. Enter your name in the "Meeting Room Requester Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Room Start Date (Required: no)**: Enter the date of the meeting, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meeting Room Start Time (Required: no)**: Enter the start time of the meeting, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Meeting Room End Time (Required: no)**: Enter the end time of the meeting, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Meeting Room Duration (Required: no)**: Enter the duration of the meeting, in minutes.
* **Meeting Room Requester Name (Required: no)**: Enter your name as the person requesting the meeting room.
