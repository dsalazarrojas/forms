# Site Visit Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Visit Evaluation Checklist is a form designed to collect feedback on site visit experiences and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each field based on your experience.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. If you're unsure about any field, try to answer based on your best understanding and judgment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logistics** (site_visit_effectiveness, note, required: false): 
    This field is for you to note any issues or concerns about the logistics of the site visit, such as transportation, accessibility, or facility organization.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (stakeholder_engagement, select_multiple, required: false): 
    Check all that apply:
    * Yes: You had a chance to interact and engage with stakeholders, such as staff, volunteers, or other visitors.
    * No: You did not have a chance to interact and engage with stakeholders.
* **Overall Outcomes** (overall_outcomes, select_one, required: false): 
    Check one of the following:
    * Yes: The site visit achieved its intended outcomes.
    * No: The site visit did not achieve its intended outcomes.

## Tips
* Be thorough and honest in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Additional Comments" field (not shown here).
* If you've already completed this form for the same site visit, your input will not be duplicated.
