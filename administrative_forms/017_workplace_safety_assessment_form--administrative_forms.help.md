# Workplace Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Assessment Form is designed to help employees and supervisors assess and document the current state of workplace safety measures and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
2. For each section, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If a section is not relevant, indicate that it is not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Safety Information**: This field is for providing general information about workplace safety. Please enter any relevant details about the workplace, such as emergency procedures, first aid kits, and safety equipment.
	+ Type: `text` (`general_safety_info`)
* **Hazards Identified**: Check all hazards that have been identified in the workplace. This includes hazards such as slippery floors, uneven surfaces, and sharp objects.
	+ Type: `select_multiple` (`hazards_identified`)
* **Safety Protocols**: Describe the safety protocols that are in place at the workplace, such as procedures for emergency situations and regular safety checks.
	+ Type: `text` (`safety_protocols`)
* **Safety Measures**: Describe the safety measures that are currently in place at the workplace, such as guards, warning signs, and safety nets.
	+ Type: `text` (`safety_measures`)
* **Safety Equipment**: Describe the safety equipment available at the workplace, such as fire extinguishers, first aid kits, and hard hats.
	+ Type: `text` (`safety_equipment`)
* **Training Programs**: Describe any training programs in place for employees, such as safety training, emergency response training, and first aid training.
	+ Type: `text` (`training_programs`)
* **Employee Information**: This field is for recording any relevant information about employees, such as their names, job titles, and training status.
	+ Type: `note` (`employee_info`)
* **Supervisor Information**: This field is for recording any relevant information about supervisors, such as their names, job titles, and areas of responsibility.
	+ Type: `note` (`supervisor_info`)
* **Emergency Plan**: Describe the emergency plan in place at the workplace, including procedures for evacuation, emergency contact numbers, and emergency kit locations.
	+ Type: `note` (`emergency_plan`)
* **Incident Information**: Record any incidents that have occurred at the workplace, including dates, times, and descriptions of what happened.
	+ Type: `note` (`incident_info`)
* **Review of Safety Measures**: Summarize the effectiveness of the safety measures in place and provide recommendations for improvement.
	+ Type: `note` (`review_safety_measures`)

## Tips
* Please answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
* If a section is not relevant, indicate that it is not applicable.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [insert contact information].
