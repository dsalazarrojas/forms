# Intent To Vacate Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the employee's intent to vacate their position due to resignation, retirement, or other reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your reason for vacating your position using the options provided.
2. If your reason is "Other", specify the reason in the corresponding text field.
3. If you are moving out of your current location, provide the specific location.
4. Enter the move out date and time (if applicable).
5. Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intent To Vacate**: Enter a brief explanation of your intention to vacate your position. This should be a simple text field for you to type your reason.
	+ Label: `Intent To Vacate`
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false` (though please note that this is an important section to complete accurately)
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you plan to vacate your position.
	+ Label: `Move Out Date`
	+ Type: `date`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Move Out Time**: Enter the time you plan to vacate your position.
	+ Label: `Move Out Time`
	+ Type: `time`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Reason For Vacating**: Select the reason for your vacating from the provided options.
	+ Label: `Reason For Vacating`
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Options: `I have sold my home`, `I am moving to a different location`, `Other`
* **Reason For Vacating Other**: If you selected "Other" as your reason for vacating, please specify the reason in this text field.
	+ Label: `Reason For Vacating Other`
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days or weeks notice you are providing to your supervisor.
	+ Label: `Notice Period`
	+ Type: `number`
	+ Required: `false`
* **Move Out Location**: Select the type of move you are making.
	+ Label: `Move Out Location`
	+ Type: `select_one`
	+ Options: `Other`, `Local Move`, `Move within city`, `Move within state`, `Move out of state`
* **Move Out Location Other**: If you selected "Other" as your move out location, please specify the location in this text field.
	+ Label: `Move Out Location Other`
	+ Type: `text`
	+ Required: `false`
