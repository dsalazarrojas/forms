# Flood Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Flood Incident Form is a tool used by authorized personnel to report and document flooding incidents in the community. It helps in gathering crucial information about the incident, including the incident type, location, and response coordinator. This form is not meant for general public use, and users are expected to have received proper authorization before accessing and submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of incident that occurred from the options provided.
2. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
3. Enter the date of the flood incident.
4. Provide a brief description of the incident.
5. If applicable, report the amount of damage and the number of people affected.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the response coordinator.
7. If you are not the response coordinator, select the time it took for the response team to arrive.
8. Optionally, include any additional notes or comments about the incident.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, text, required: false): Select the type of flood incident that occurred, such as "Flooding", "Storm Surge", "Creek/Stream Overflow", etc.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the address or location where the incident occurred.
* **Flood Date** (`flood_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the flood incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the incident, including any details that may be useful for response and resolution.
* **Damage Amount** (`damage_amount`, number, required: false): If the incident caused damage, report the approximate amount of damage in monetary value.
* **Affected Population** (`affected_population`, number, required: false): Report the approximate number of people affected by the incident, if any.
* **Response Coordinator** (`response_coordinator`, text, required: true): Enter the name and contact information of the response coordinator assigned to the incident.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, time, required: false): If you are not the response coordinator, report the time it took for the response team to arrive on the scene.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
