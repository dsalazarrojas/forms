# Power Outage Survival Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Outage Survival Guide form is designed to collect information from individuals or families affected by a power outage. This form helps responders understand the situation and provide the best support by asking for details about the power outage, including individual or family status, contact information, address, and emergency planning.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select whether you are an individual or a family.
2. Provide your name.
3. Share your contact information.
4. Enter your address.
5. Choose the options that describe your power outage situation.
6. Select the start and end dates of the power outage.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual or Family** (`individual_or_family`, text, required): Please select whether you are an individual or a family member affected by the power outage.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Please provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Please enter your address.
* **Power Outage Information** (`power_outage_info`, select_multiple, required): Please select all that apply from the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Start Date of Power Outage** (`outdate_start`, date, required): Please enter the start date of the power outage.
* **End Date of Power Outage** (`outdate_end`, time, required): Please enter the end date and time of the power outage.
* **Emergency Plan** (`emergency_plan`, text, required): Please provide details about your emergency plan.
* **Backup Power Source** (`backup_power_source`, text, optional): If you have a backup power source, please provide details.
* **Battery Backup** (`battery_backup`, text, optional): If you have a battery backup, please provide details.
* **First Aid and Self Care** (`first_aids`, text, optional): If you have any first aid and self-care needs, please provide details.
* **First Aid and Self Care Description** (`first_aids_description`, text, optional): If you have any additional information about your first aid and self-care needs, please provide details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Alternate Contact** (`alternate_contact`, text, optional): If you have an alternate contact, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, text, optional): If you have accessed any emergency services, please provide details.
* **Emergency Plan Status** (`emergency_plan_status`, select_one, required): Please select the status of your emergency plan:
	+ None
	+ In progress
	+ Complete
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Please provide any additional notes or comments about your situation. 

## Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when providing your contact information and address.
* If you have accessed any emergency services, please provide as many details as possible.
* If you have a backup power source, please be sure to provide details about its capabilities. 
* Please select the correct status of your emergency plan to ensure that responders can provide the best support.
