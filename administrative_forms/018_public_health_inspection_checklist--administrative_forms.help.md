# public_health_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by authorized personnel or designated inspectors to collect information about the public health and safety status of a facility. It's essential for identifying and addressing potential risks and issues that could impact public health.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Facility Information" section and enter the facility's name and address.
2. Choose the type of facility being inspected.
3. Select the inspection date.
4. In the "Regulations" section, select all applicable health and safety regulations that apply to the facility.
5. In the "Deficiencies" section, choose "Observed" if you have seen any issues or non-compliances. If not, select "Not Observed".
6. In the "Corrective Action Needed" section, choose "True" if the facility requires immediate corrective action, otherwise select "False".
7. In the "Facility Status" section, select the current status of the facility. If the facility is not currently open, select "Closed" or "Under Renovation".
8. In the "Additional Comments" section, add any further observations or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name (2)**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Address (3)**: Enter the address of the facility.
* **Inspection Date (4)**: Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Facility Type (7)**: Select the type of facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Area (8)**: This field may be completed by the facility owner.
* **Facility Contact (9)**: Enter the contact information of the facility owner.
* **Facility Status (13)**: Select the status of the facility after the inspection.
* **Regulations (10)**: Select all applicable health and safety regulations that apply to the facility.
* **Observed Deficiencies (11)**: Choose "Observed" if you have seen any issues or non-compliances during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action Needed (12)**: Choose "True" if the facility requires immediate corrective action.
* **Additional Comments (14)**: Record any further observations or concerns during the inspection.
