# Data Privacy Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about their personal data and usage habits related to data privacy. It's intended to help you understand how your personal data is being collected, stored, and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Information" page with your personal details.
2. Move on to the "Data Collection" page and provide information about the data you're willing to share with us.
3. Finally, go to the "Data Retention" page and specify how you'd like your data to be retained.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information (text)** (required): Please enter your full name and any other identifying information about yourself.

### Data Collection
* **Data Collection (text)** (required): Describe the types of data you're willing to share with us, such as personal data, usage habits, or other relevant information.

### Data Retention
* **Data Retention (text)** (required): Specify how you'd like your data to be retained, such as for a specific period, in perpetuity, or in a specific format.
