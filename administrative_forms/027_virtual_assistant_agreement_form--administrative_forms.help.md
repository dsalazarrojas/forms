# Virtual Assistant Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Agreement Form is designed to collect information on the scope of work, payment terms, and other essential aspects of a virtual assistant agreement. It helps establish clear communication between the client and the assistant, ensuring both parties are on the same page before starting work together.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reading through each section and field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form section by section, answering each question or selecting options as prompted.
3. Double-check your answers and selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `text`, optional): A brief overview of the project or task.
### Scope of Work
* **Scope of Work** (`Scope of Work`, `text`, required): A detailed description of the work to be done by the virtual assistant.
### Payment Terms
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `text`, required): The payment schedule and amount for the work to be done.
### Payment Frequency
* **Payment Frequency** (`Payment Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more payment frequencies (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly).
### Client Requirements
* **Client Requirements** (`Client Requirements`, `text`, required): A list of specific requirements or expectations from the client.
### Assistant Requirements
* **Assistant Requirements** (`Assistant Requirements`, `text`, required): A list of specific requirements or expectations from the assistant.
### Termination
* **Termination** (`Termination`, `text`, required): The conditions under which the agreement can be terminated.
### Agreement Sign-off
* **Agreement Sign-off** (`Agreement Sign-off`, `select_one`, required): Select 'yes' to sign off on the agreement.
### Agreement Comments
* **Agreement Comments** (`Agreement Comments`, `text`, optional): Additional comments or notes related to the agreement.
### Assistant Terms
* **Assistant Terms** (`Assistant Terms`, `text`, required): A list of specific requirements or expectations from the assistant.
### Client Signature
* **Client Signature** (`Client Signature`, `text`, optional): The client's signature, if applicable.
### Assistant Signature
* **Assistant Signature** (`Assistant Signature`, `text`, optional): The assistant's signature, if applicable.
### Date of Signing
* **Date of Signing** (`Date of Signing`, `date`, required): The date on which the agreement is signed.
### Date of Revision
* **Date of Revision** (`Date of Revision`, `date`, optional): The date on which the agreement is revised.
### Client Notes
* **Client Notes** (`Client Notes`, `text`, optional): Additional notes or comments from the client.
### Assistant Notes
* **Assistant Notes** (`Assistant Notes`, `text`, optional): Additional notes or comments from the assistant.
### Agreement Version
* **Agreement Version** (`Agreement Version`, `text`, optional): The version of the agreement.
### Assistant Agreement
* **Assistant Agreement** (`Assistant Agreement`, `text`, optional): The assistant's agreement.
### Payment Terms Notes
* **Payment Terms Notes** (`Payment Terms Notes`, `text`, optional): Additional notes or comments on payment terms.
### Agreement Review
* **Agreement Review** (`Agreement Review`, `text`, optional): A review of the agreement.
### Agreement Approval
* **Agreement Approval** (`Agreement Approval`, `text`, optional): An approval of the agreement.
### Client Confirmation
* **Client Confirmation** (`Client Confirmation`, `text`, optional): A confirmation from the client.
### Assistant Confirmation
* **Assistant Confirmation** (`Assistant Confirmation`, `text`, optional): A confirmation from the assistant.
### Agreement Status
* **Agreement Status** (`Agreement Status`, `text`, optional): The status of the agreement.
### Agreement Signed
* **Agreement Signed** (`Agreement Signed`, `select_one`, required): Select 'yes' to indicate that the agreement is signed.
