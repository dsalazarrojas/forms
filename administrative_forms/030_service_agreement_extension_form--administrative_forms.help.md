# Service Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Agreement Extension Form is used to submit an extension for an existing service agreement. This process helps in updating the agreement terms and dates as required by the business and its clients.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Step 1: Choose the correct agreement type**: Select the correct agreement type (Extension, Renewal, or Termination) from the "Agreement Type" dropdown list.
- **Step 2: Enter business ID**: Enter the unique business ID that corresponds to the agreement you want to extend.
- **Step 3: Enter reason for extension (if applicable)**: If you're extending the agreement, provide a brief explanation for the extension.
- **Step 4: Set new terms start date and end date**: Enter the new start and end dates for the extended agreement terms.
- **Step 5: Enter new updated dates**: Enter the updated dates for the agreement, including start and end dates.
- **Step 6: Confirm service agreement status**: Select the correct status of the agreement (Active, Inactive, or Terminated).
- **Step 7: Confirm agreement type**: Select the correct type of agreement (Service Agreement, Service Agreement Extension, or Service Agreement Termination).
- **Step 8: Enter agreement name**: Enter the name of the service agreement being extended.
- **Step 9: Set agreement start and end dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the original agreement.
- **Step 10: Set extension status**: Select the status of the extension (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
- **Step 11: Optional - Enter reason for extension 2 (if applicable)**: If you're extending the agreement for a specific reason, provide a brief explanation.
- **Step 12: Set new terms start and end dates 2 (if applicable)**: If you're extending the agreement for a specific reason, enter the new start and end dates.
- **Step 13: Update last updated date**: Enter the updated date of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required): Select the correct type of agreement: Extension, Renewal, or Termination.
* **Business ID** (`business_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the business that corresponds to the agreement.
* **Reason for Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): If you're extending the agreement, provide a brief explanation for the extension.
* **New Terms Start Date** (`new_terms_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the new terms will begin.
* **New Terms End Date** (`new_terms_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the new terms will end.
* **Client ID** (`client_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the client associated with the agreement.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the user who submitted the extension request.
* **Service ID** (`service_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the service associated with the agreement.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, number, required): Enter the version number of the agreement.
* **Extension Type** (`extension_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of extension: Extension, Renewal, or Termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, optional): If you're terminating the agreement, provide a brief explanation.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required): Enter the period before the agreement is terminated.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, optional): If you're terminating the agreement, provide a brief explanation.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, required): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Service Agreement Status** (`service_agreement_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the agreement: Active, Inactive, or Terminated.
* **Service Agreement Type** (`service_agreement_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of agreement: Service Agreement, Service Agreement Extension, or Service Agreement Termination.
* **Service Agreement Name** (`service_agreement_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the service agreement.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the original agreement.
* **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the original agreement.
* **Extension Status** (`extension_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the extension: Active, Inactive, or Expired.
* **Reason For Extension 2** (`reason_for_extension_2`, text, optional): If you're extending the agreement for a specific reason, provide a brief explanation.
* **New Terms Start Date 2** (`new_terms_start_date_2`, date, required): If you're extending the agreement for a specific reason, enter the new start date of the agreement.
* **New Terms End Date 2** (`new_terms_end_date_2`, date, required): If you're extending the agreement for a specific reason, enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Last Updated 2** (`last_updated_2`, date, required): Enter the date of the last update.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult the business or client for clarification.
