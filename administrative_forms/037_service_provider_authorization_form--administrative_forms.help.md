# Service Provider Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Provider Authorization Form is used to grant access to services for service providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Organization in the field labeled "Organization".
4. Enter your Service Provider ID in the field labeled "Service Provider ID".
5. Enter the date you are authorizing the service in the field labeled "Authorization Date".
6. Select the service type you are authorizing in the field labeled "Service Type".
7. If applicable, select the status of the service you are authorizing in the field labeled "Service Status".
8. Enter any additional notes about the service in the field labeled "Notes".
9. Enter your Email in the field labeled "Email".
10. Enter your Phone in the field labeled "Phone".
11. Select who the service is assigned to in the field labeled "Assigned To".
12. Select which tool is assigned to the service in the field labeled "Assigned Tool".
13. Enter who created the service in the field labeled "Created By".
14. Enter who last updated the service in the field labeled "Updated By".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required: False): Enter your last name.
* **Organization** (Text, Required: False): Enter your organization.
* **Service Provider ID** (Text, Required: False): Enter your service provider ID.
* **Authorization Date** (Date, Required: False): Enter the date you are authorizing the service.
* **Service Type** (Select One, Required: False): Select the service type you are authorizing.
* **Service Status** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select the status of the service you are authorizing.
* **Notes** (Note, Required: False): Enter any additional notes about the service.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): Enter your email.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (Select One, Required: False): Select who the service is assigned to.
* **Assigned Tool** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select which tool is assigned to the service.
* **Created By** (Text, Required: False): Enter who created the service.
* **Updated By** (Text, Required: False): Enter who last updated the service.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your administrator.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share with external users.
