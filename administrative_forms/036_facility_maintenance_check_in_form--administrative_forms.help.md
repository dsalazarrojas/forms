# Facility Maintenance Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage maintenance requests from facility users. It's essential to fill it out clearly and accurately to ensure that maintenance issues are properly addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Use the correct facility name and location to ensure proper assignment of work orders.
3. Describe the issue in detail in the "Issue Description" field.
4. Provide the date and time of the issue to help the maintenance team plan accordingly.
5. Submit the form to the maintenance team for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Maintenance Requester** (`maintenance_requester`, text, optional): The person submitting the maintenance request. This field is optional but helps the maintenance team understand who is submitting the request.
- **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): The name of the facility where the maintenance issue is located. This helps the maintenance team identify the correct location for the work order.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The specific location within the facility where the issue is found. This can be a room number, floor, or building name.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date when the maintenance issue was noticed or occurred. This helps the maintenance team plan and schedule work orders.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of day when the maintenance issue was noticed or occurred. This helps the maintenance team determine the urgency of the issue.
- **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): A detailed description of the maintenance issue. Please describe the problem clearly and concisely.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing the issue.
- Use the correct facility and location to help the maintenance team assign the work order correctly.
- Provide the date and time to help the maintenance team plan and schedule work orders.
- Submit the form completely and accurately to ensure that maintenance issues are addressed efficiently.
