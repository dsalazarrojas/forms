# Power Line Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Line Safety Inspection Form is designed to gather information about power line safety inspections.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of the inspection
2. Fill out your name as the employee conducting the inspection
3. Choose the location of the inspection from the available options
4. Describe any hazards found during the inspection
5. Provide a description of the action plan to address each hazard
6. Add any additional comments or notes from the inspection
7. Select the type of hazards found during the inspection
8. Choose the hazards that have been mitigated
9. Indicate the status of the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date**: A date field where you should enter the date of the inspection.
* **Employee Name**: A field for you to fill out your name as the employee conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Location**: A dropdown field where you can choose the location of the inspection from the options provided.
* **Hazard Report**: A field where you can describe any hazards found during the inspection.
* **Action Plan**: A field where you can provide a description of the action plan for each hazard.
* **Additional Comments**: A field where you can add any additional comments or notes from the inspection.
* **Hazards Found**: A field where you can select the type of hazards found during the inspection.
* **Hazards Mitigated**: A field where you can choose the hazards that have been mitigated.
* **Status**: A field where you can indicate the status of the form.
