# Workplace Safety Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Report Form is a tool used to collect information about workplace safety incidents, near-miss events, or any other workplace safety concerns. This form helps us to identify hazards, document actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and detailed information about the incident.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Hazard Identified" field, depending on whether a hazard was identified during the incident.
3. Describe the incident in the "Description" field, providing as much detail as possible about what happened.
4. Provide your name and title in the "Employee Name" and "Supervisor Name" fields, respectively.
5. Enter "Yes" or "No" for the "Hazard Identified" field, indicating whether a hazard was identified during the incident.
6. Describe the actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents in the "Control Measures" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the actions taken or planned in the "Action Taken" field.
8. Enter a brief description of the next steps planned to prevent similar incidents in the "Next Steps" field.
9. Enter a brief description of the outcome of the incident in the "Outcome" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date Occurred**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time Occurred**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the incident occurred.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Hazard Identified**: Select "Yes" if you identified a hazard during the incident, "No" otherwise.
* **Control Measures**: Describe the actions taken or planned to prevent similar incidents.
* **Action Taken**: Describe the actions taken as a result of the incident.
* **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps planned to prevent similar incidents.
* **Outcome**: Enter a brief description of the outcome of the incident.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the report.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the report was submitted.
* **Reported By**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Report ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the report.
* **Workplace**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Work Location**: Enter the specific location within the workplace where the incident occurred.
* **Job Type**: Enter the type of job or role you held when the incident occurred.
* **Work Shift**: Enter the shift or schedule you were on when the incident occurred.
* **Job Classification**: Enter your job classification or category.
* **Work Group**: Enter your work group or team.
* **Equipment Used**: List any equipment used during the incident.
* **Supervisor Signature**: Enter the signature of your supervisor or manager, confirming that the report has been reviewed and acknowledged.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the form is completed in its entirety, providing accurate and detailed information about the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Do not submit duplicate reports for the same incident.
* Please keep the form for your record-keeping purposes.
