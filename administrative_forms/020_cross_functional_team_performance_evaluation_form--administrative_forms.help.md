# Cross Functional Team Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Functional Team Performance Evaluation Form is a tool to assess team collaboration, productivity, and leadership qualities to provide constructive feedback and improve team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant team member from the dropdown menu.
2. Rate the overall performance using the select options provided.
3. Provide specific feedback and comments as required.
4. Rate the team's project outcome impact.
5. Rate the team's performance on various aspects (project outcome, team collaboration, project outcome, project outcome, etc.).
6. Provide feedback for team leaders.
7. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member 1** (`team_member_input_1`, select_one, optional): Select the team member you want to evaluate.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the team's overall performance (Excellent, Satisfactory, Needs improvement).
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments_1`, note, true): Please provide specific feedback and comments on team performance and collaboration.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, false): Enter the team name.
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, date, false): The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, false): The person submitting the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, false): The current form version.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, select_one, false): Select the team leader.
* **Project Outcome Impact** (`project_outcome_impact`, select_one, false): Rate the project outcome impact (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Team Performance Impact** (`team_performance_impact`, select_multiple, false): Rate the team performance impact (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Project Outcome 1** (`project_outcome_1`, select_one, false): Rate the project outcome 1 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Project Outcome 2** (`project_outcome_2`, select_one, false): Rate the project outcome 2 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Project Outcome 3** (`project_outcome_3`, select_one, false): Rate the project outcome 3 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Team Collaboration 1** (`team_collaboration_1`, select_one, false): Rate the team collaboration 1 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Team Collaboration 2** (`team_collaboration_2`, select_one, false): Rate the team collaboration 2 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Team Collaboration 3** (`team_collaboration_3`, select_one, false): Rate the team collaboration 3 (High, Low, Neutral).
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments_1`, note, true): Please provide any additional comments.
* **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, note, true): Provide feedback for team leaders.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, note, true): Provide feedback for team leaders.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, note, false): Note any leadership qualities.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand the form's purpose and intent.
* Provide specific and constructive feedback for team members and leaders.
* Rate team performance and project outcome impact accurately.
* Use the form to help improve team performance and collaboration.
* Avoid duplication of efforts.
* Fill out the form truthfully and honestly.
