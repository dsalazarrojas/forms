# Spoiled Food Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report incidents of spoiled food in the facility to ensure the food safety and quality of the food served to customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Choose the correct date of the incident.
2.  Select the type of food that was spoiled (e.g., Type A, Type B, Type C).
3.  Enter the storage area where the food was kept.
4.  Provide the estimated quantity of the spoiled food.
5.  Record the date when the spoilage was noticed.
6.  Add any additional investigation notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the incident occurred.
- **Food Type** (`food_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of food involved in the incident (e.g., Type A, Type B, Type C).
- **Storage Area** (`storage_area`, text, required): Enter the storage area where the food was kept.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Provide the estimated quantity of the spoiled food.
- **Spoilage Date** (`spoilage_date`, date, required): Record the date when the spoilage was noticed.
- **Investigation Notes** (`investigation_notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes about the investigation.
