# Teacher Work Expectation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers and school administrators agree on work expectations, ensuring a clear understanding of teacher's responsibilities and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant school administrators from the list provided.
2. Choose your teacher role from the options (Primary Teacher, Assistant Teacher, Substitute Teacher, or Other).
3. List the educational partners involved in your work.
4. Provide contact information for both you and the school.
5. Enter your teaching schedule (date).
6. Specify class time (time).
7. Indicate your office hours (time).
8. Describe the class room conditions.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter the school email address.
11. Choose your availability status (Available, Not Available, or Maybe).
12. Select the school holidays you will observe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Administrators** (Select Multiple, id: 1): Choose one or more school administrators you will be working with.
* **Teacher Role** (Select One, id: 2): Choose your role as a teacher (Primary Teacher, Assistant Teacher, Substitute Teacher, or Other).
* **Educational Partners** (Select Multiple, id: 3): List the educational partners involved in your work.
* **Contact Information** (Text, id: 4): Provide your contact information, including email and phone number (if applicable).
* **Teaching Schedule** (Date, id: 5): Enter the date of your teaching schedule.
* **Class Time** (Time, id: 6): Specify the time of your class sessions.
* **Office Hours** (Time, id: 7): Indicate your available office hours for students and administrators.
* **Class Room Conditions** (Text, id: 8): Describe the conditions of your class room (e.g., availability of equipment, technology, etc.).
* **Teacher Email** (Email, id: 9): Enter your email address for official communication.
* **School Email** (Email, id: 10): Enter the school email address for official communication.
* **Availability** (Select One, id: 11): Choose your current availability status (Available, Not Available, or Maybe).
* **School Holidays** (Checkbox, id: 12): Select the school holidays you will observe.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and clearly.
- Review the form before submitting it to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your school administrator or teacher for clarification.
