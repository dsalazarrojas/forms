# Employee Handover Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handover Form is used to gather details about the handover process, including a description of the handover, the date and time of the handover, and confirmation of asset transfer. This form helps facilitate the process of transferring ownership or responsibilities between employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide a detailed description of the handover in the "Handover Information" field.
2. Enter the date of the handover in the "Handover Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the handover in the "Handover Time" field.
4. Provide a description of the asset transferred in the "Asset Transferred" field.
5. Enter the confirmation of asset transfer by the receiving party in the "Asset Transfer Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Handover Information (Text):** A detailed description of the handover process is required in this field. Please provide as much information as possible about what was transferred and why.
* **Handover Date (Date):** Enter the date of the handover event. This helps track the timeline of the handover process.
* **Handover Time (Time):** Enter the time of the handover event. This helps ensure that both parties are aware of the scheduled handover time.
* **Asset Transferred (Text):** A description of the asset that was transferred is required in this field. Please be specific about the asset and its current status.
* **Asset Transfer Confirmation (Text):** Enter the confirmation of asset transfer by the receiving party. This helps ensure that all parties are aware of the transfer's status.
