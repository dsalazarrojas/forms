# Workplace Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Agreement Renewal Form is used to collect and update essential information related to employee workplace agreements. This form helps ensure that all necessary details are accurately recorded and agreements are properly renewed or terminated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form thoroughly and ensure you understand the information required.
2. Fill in all mandatory fields marked as "required" to prevent form submission issues.
3. Double-check your responses to guarantee accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **General Information**
* **General Information** (Required)
This field is the introductory section of the form, which serves as a placeholder for other fields that follow. Please enter your general information here.
### 2. **Employment Status**
* **Employment Status** (Required)
Select your current employment status from the options provided:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
### 3. **Agreement Start Date**
* **Agreement Start Date** (Required)
Enter the date when your workplace agreement started.
### 4. **Agreement End Date**
* **Agreement End Date** (Required)
Enter the date when your current workplace agreement is due to end or has ended.
### 5. **Renewal Reason**
* **Renewal Reason** (Required)
Provide a valid reason for the renewal of your workplace agreement.
### 6. **Employee ID**
* **Employee ID** (Required)
Enter your unique employee ID number.
### 7. **Employee Name**
* **Employee Name** (Required)
Enter your full name as an employee.
### 8. **Job Title**
* **Job Title** (Required)
Enter your job title as an employee.
### 9. **Department**
* **Department** (Required)
Enter the department you belong to as an employee.
### 10. **Reporting Manager**
* **Reporting Manager** (Required)
Enter the name of your direct reporting manager.
### 11. **Team**
* **Team** (Required)
Enter the name of your team as an employee.
### 12. **Job Function**
* **Job Function** (Required)
Enter a brief description of your job function.
### 13. **Manager Name**
* **Manager Name** (Required)
Enter the name of your manager.
### 14. **Agreement Details**
* **Agreement Details** (Required)
Enter any additional relevant details about your workplace agreement.
### 15. **Review and Signatures**
* **Review and Signatures** (Required)
This field is for review and verification by the employee and relevant parties. Sign and date to confirm submission.

Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and correctly before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or personnel.
