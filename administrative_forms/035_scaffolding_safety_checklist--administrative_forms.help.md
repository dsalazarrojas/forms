# scaffolding_safety_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scaffolding safety checklist is a form designed to evaluate the safety of scaffolding in a work site or construction project. It's a tool for workers or supervisors to ensure that the scaffolding is safe and compliant with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For questions with options, select the answer that best describes your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, discuss it with your supervisor or safety officer.
4. Complete all questions on each page before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Concerns** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_1`, `text`, optional): Describe any safety concerns you have about the scaffolding, such as potential hazards or risks.
* **Hours Worked This Week** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you've worked on the scaffolding this week.
* **Safety Training** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you've received safety training on scaffolding, whether you've completed it, or if it's not applicable.
* **Safety Concerns Description** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_4`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of any safety concerns you have about the scaffolding.
* **Is Scaffold Inspected?** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_5`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the scaffolding has been inspected, whether it hasn't, or if it's not applicable.
* **Number of People Present** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_6`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people present while working on the scaffolding.
* **Weather Checked** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_7`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the weather conditions have been checked for scaffolding safety, whether they haven't, or if it's not applicable.
* **Safety at Heights** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_8`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether safety measures are in place for working at heights, whether they aren’t, or if it's not applicable.
* **Hours Worked This Month** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_9`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of hours you've worked on the scaffolding this month.
* **Report Safety Concerns to Supervisor** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_10`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you've reported any safety concerns to your supervisor, whether you haven't, or if it's not applicable.
* **Safety Orientation** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_11`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you've received safety orientation on scaffolding, whether you've completed it, or if it's not applicable.
* **Equipment Maintenance Needed** (`scaffolding_safety_checklist_page_12`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether equipment maintenance is needed for the scaffolding, whether it isn’t, or if it's not applicable.
