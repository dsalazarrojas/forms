# Service Interruption Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Interruption Incident Form is used to report and track service interruptions. This form is used by team members or users who were directly impacted by the interruption to gather information about the interruption and any subsequent actions taken to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose "Yes" if the incident affected any services.
3. Report the start and end times of the interruption.
4. Select the duration of the interruption.
5. Provide a reason for the interruption.
6. Select which services were impacted.
7. Choose the impact level of the interruption.
8. Report the resolution time.
9. Provide a description of the incident.
10. Identify the root cause of the incident.
11. Provide an action plan to resolve the issue.
12. Select whether the incident was assigned to anyone.
13. Select the status of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident details** (`interruption_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the incident.
* **Affected services** (`affected_services`, select multiple, optional): Choose all the services that were impacted by the incident. 
* **Start time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident started.
* **End time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident ended.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the incident.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for the incident.
* **Services impacted** (`services_impacted`, select one, optional): Choose which services were impacted by the incident.
* **Impact level** (`impact_level`, select one, optional): Choose the impact level of the incident.
* **Resolution time** (`resolution_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident was resolved.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **Root cause** (`root_cause`, text, optional): Enter the root cause of the incident.
* **Action** (`action`, text, optional): Enter a plan to resolve the incident.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select one, optional): Choose whether the incident was assigned to anyone.
* **Status** (`status`, select one, optional): Choose the status of the incident.
