# Educational Facility Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents at educational facilities, such as schools, colleges, or universities. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the incident, allowing for prompt and accurate handling of the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Incident Description" field with a brief description of what happened.
2. Select the "Incident Type" that best describes the incident.
3. Choose who reported the incident from the "Incident Reported By" options.
4. Indicate whether the incident was reported on-site or off-site.
5. Fill out any additional details as needed in the "Notes" field.
6. Provide any relevant images or additional information in the "Incident Image URL" field.
7. Complete any other required fields as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Description**: Enter a brief description of the incident, including as much detail as possible.
* **Occurred At**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the type of incident from the provided options (e.g. Fire, Weather, Injury, etc.).
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: Enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reported By**: Choose who reported the incident from the provided options.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional details or context about the incident.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the person reporting the incident or the person to contact.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address or the email address to contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number or the phone number to contact.
* **Incident Image URL**: Enter a URL to a relevant image or video.
* **Reported On Site**: Indicate whether the incident was reported from the actual site or not.
* **Incident Description Part 2**, **Incident Description Part 3**, and **Incident Description Part 4**: Enter any additional details about the incident.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident.
* If you need to report multiple incidents, you can submit multiple form instances.
* You can attach images or additional information relevant to the incident.
