# Lease Termination Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to confirm that a tenant has agreed to terminate a lease. The form collects essential details for proper lease termination processing.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether the tenant has agreed to terminate the lease.
2. Fill out the remaining fields if the tenant has agreed to terminate the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tenant**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the tenant has agreed to terminate the lease.
* **landlord**: Enter the name of the landlord.
* **termination_date**: Enter the date of the lease termination.
* **reason_for_termination**: Enter the reason for terminating the lease.
* **notice_period**: Enter the notice period for lease termination.
* **rent_amount**: Enter the rent amount for the terminated lease.
* **last_payment**: Enter the last payment made for the terminated lease.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the notice period.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the notice period.
* **reason**: Enter the general reason for the termination of the lease.

## Tips

* Ensure you are filling out this form in a valid and authorized context.
* Enter accurate and complete information for all fields to ensure proper lease termination processing.
* If the tenant has not agreed to terminate the lease, do not access this form.
* Review and verify all information before submitting the form.
