# Conditions Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate and document the condition of a property, including its exterior and interior. It is designed to identify any issues or needed repairs and provide a final assessment of the property's condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure that you are filling out this form for the correct property and assessment date.
2. Enter the assessment date in the "Assessment Date" field.
3. Enter a unique reference number for the property in the "Assessment ID" field.
4. Enter your name and title in the "Assessor Name" and "Assessor Title" fields, respectively.
5. Provide details about the property in the "Property Information" field.
6. Enter the full address of the property in the "Property Address" field.
7. Select the type of property in the "Property Type" field.
8. Enter the square footage of the property in the "Property Size" field.
9. Enter the construction year of the property in the "Year Built" field.
10. Evaluate the condition of the property's exterior in the "Exterior Assessment" field.
11. Evaluate the condition of the roof in the "Roof Condition" field.
12. Evaluate the condition of the exterior walls in the "Siding Condition" field.
13. Evaluate the condition of the foundation in the "Foundation Condition" field.
14. Evaluate the condition of the windows in the "Windows Condition" field.
15. Evaluate the condition of the interior in the "Interior Assessment" field.
16. Evaluate the condition of the floors in the "Flooring Condition" field.
17. Evaluate the condition of the walls in the "Walls Condition" field.
18. Evaluate the condition of the plumbing in the "Plumbing Condition" field.
19. Evaluate the condition of the electrical system in the "Electrical Condition" field.
20. Evaluate the condition of the heating and cooling system in the "HVAC Condition" field.
21. Describe any issues found during the assessment in the "Issues Found" field.
22. Provide any recommended repairs in the "Repair Recommendations" field.
23. Enter an approximate cost for the repairs in the "Estimated Repair Cost" field.
24. Provide a final assessment of the property's condition in the "Overall Assessment" field.
25. Confirm your assessment with your signature in the "Assessor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, required): Enter the date on which the assessment was performed.
* **Assessment ID** (`assessment_id`, required): Enter a unique reference number for the property.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, required): Enter your name as the assessor.
* **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`, optional): Enter your professional title.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, optional): Provide any additional details about the property.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, required): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, required): Select the type of property (e.g. residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`, optional): Enter the square footage of the property.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, optional): Enter the construction year of the property.
* **Exterior Assessment** (`exterior_assessment`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the property's exterior.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, required): Evaluate the condition of the roof (e.g. excellent, good, fair, etc.).
* **Siding Condition** (`siding_condition`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the exterior walls.
* **Foundation Condition** (`foundation_condition`, required): Evaluate the condition of the structural base of the property.
* **Windows Condition** (`windows_condition`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the windows.
* **Interior Assessment** (`interior_assessment`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the interior of the property.
* **Flooring Condition** (`flooring_condition`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the floors.
* **Walls Condition** (`walls_condition`, optional): Evaluate the condition of the walls.
* **Plumbing Condition** (`plumbing_condition`, required): Evaluate the condition of the plumbing system.
* **Electrical Condition** (`electrical_condition`, required): Evaluate the condition of the electrical system.
* **HVAC Condition** (`hvac_condition`, required): Evaluate the condition of the heating and cooling system.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, optional): Describe any issues found during the assessment.
* **Repair Recommendations** (`repair_recommendations`, required): Provide any recommended repairs.
* **Estimated Repair Cost** (`estimated_repair_cost`, optional): Enter an approximate cost for the repairs.
* **Overall Assessment** (`overall_assessment`, required): Provide a final assessment of the property's condition.
* **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, required): Confirm your assessment with your signature.
*   **Please note that this form is to be used as a guide only. Please use the fields as directed and ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly.**
