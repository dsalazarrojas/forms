# Component Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Component Checklist Form is designed to provide a quick and easy way to assess the condition of components in a production environment. It helps maintenance personnel or quality control teams evaluate the physical and functional state of components, identify any maintenance needs, and schedule next inspection dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Checklist date" field with the current date of the inspection.
2. Enter the name of the person conducting the inspection in the "Inspector name" field.
3. Enter the unique identifier or serial number of the component in the "Component id" field.
4. Select the current physical condition of the component from the options: "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
5. Select the result of the functional test from the options: "Pass", "Fail", or "Not tested".
6. If any maintenance actions are required, type them in the "Maintenance required" field.
7. Optionally, enter the recommended next inspection date in the "Next inspection date" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or observations in the "Inspector comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Checklist date
• **Checklist date**: `date` - Date of inspection.

### Inspector name
• **Inspector name**: `text` - Person conducting the check.

### Component id
• **Component id**: `text` - Identifier or serial number.

### Physical condition
• **Physical condition**: `select_one` - State condition of component: Good, Fair, Poor.

### Functional test result
• **Functional test result**: `select_one` - Outcome of functional testing: Pass, Fail, Not tested.

### Maintenance required
• **Maintenance required**: `text` - Any maintenance actions needed.

### Next inspection date
• **Next inspection date**: `date` - Recommended next check date.

### Inspector comments
• **Inspector comments**: `text` - Additional notes or observations.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use the "Next inspection date" field only if you recommend a specific date for the next inspection.
* Provide clear and concise comments or observations in the "Inspector comments" field, if necessary.
