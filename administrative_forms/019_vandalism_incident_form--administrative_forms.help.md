# Vandalism Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report vandalism incidents to the authorities. Reporting an incident helps the authorities to investigate and prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in all required fields and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (id: 1): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (id: 2): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (id: 3): Enter the location where the incident occurred. (e.g., park, school, etc.)
* **Incident Description** (id: 4): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Suspect Details** (id: 5): Enter the suspect's name, age, and contact information.
* **Suspect Photos** (id: 6): Upload photos of the suspect (if available).
* **Damage Description** (id: 7): Describe what was damaged.
* **Suspect Name** (id: 8): Enter the suspect's name.
* **Suspect Age** (id: 9): Enter the suspect's age.
* **Suspect Contact Information** (id: 10): Enter the suspect's phone number and/or address.
* **Suspect Relationship** (id: 11): Select the relationship of the suspect to the victim or property owner.
* **Suspect Address** (id: 12): Enter the suspect's address.
* **Witness Details** (id: 13): Provide any information you have about the witness.
* **Witness Name** (id: 14): Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Relationship** (id: 15): Select the relationship of the witness to the suspect or property owner.
* **Witness Age** (id: 16): Enter the witness's age.
* **Witness Contact Information** (id: 17): Enter the witness's phone number and/or address.
* **Property Owner's Name** (id: 18): Enter the property owner's name.
* **Property Owner Relationship** (id: 19): Select the relationship of the property owner to the suspect.
* **Property Owner Contact Information** (id: 20): Enter the property owner's phone number and/or address.
* **Incident Reporting Agency** (id: 21): Select the agency that reported the incident.
* **Reason for Reporting Incident** (id: 22): Enter a brief explanation for reporting the incident.
* **Incident Reporting Date** (id: 23): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reporting Time** (id: 24): Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **Incident Reporting Venue** (id: 25): Enter the location where the incident was reported.

Please note that each field is required to be filled unless otherwise specified as optional.
