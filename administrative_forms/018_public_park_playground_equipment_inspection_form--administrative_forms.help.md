# public_park_playground_equipment_inspection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help park administrators and maintenance staff inspect and report on the condition of public park playground equipment. By filling out this form, you can identify potential hazards, assess equipment condition, and document any necessary maintenance or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Park Name** field, which should be the name of the park or playground being inspected.
2. Enter the **Inspector Name**, which is your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Park Inspector").
3. Choose the **Inspection Date**, which should be the date of the inspection.
4. For the **Equipment Condition** field, select all the options that apply to the current state of the equipment. These options are:
	* Inspected: The equipment is in good condition and has no issues.
	* Needs Minor Repair: The equipment has minor issues but is still safe to use.
	* Needs Major Repair: The equipment has significant issues and requires immediate attention.
	* Needs Replacement: The equipment is no longer usable and should be replaced.
5. In the **Safety Risks Identified** field, select all the options that apply to the potential hazards present in the playground. These options are:
	* No Risks: The playground is safe with no hazards present.
	* Tripping Hazards: The playground has tripping hazards such as uneven surfaces or obstacles.
	* Sharp Objects: The playground has sharp objects or surfaces that could cause injury.
	* Uneven Surfaces: The playground has uneven or slippery surfaces that could cause falls.
	* Other (Describe): If none of the above options apply, provide a brief description of the hazard.
6. Finally, add any additional **Notes** about the inspection, such as recommendations for improvement or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Park Name**: The name of the park or playground being inspected.
* **Inspector Name**: Your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Park Inspector").
* **Inspection Date**: The date of the inspection.
* **Equipment Condition**: Select one or more of the options to describe the current condition of the playground equipment.
* **Safety Risks Identified**: Select one or more of the options to describe potential safety hazards in the playground.
* **Notes**: Additional comments or recommendations for improvement.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure that the playgrounds under your care are safe for the public to enjoy.
* Take photos or make notes during the inspection to supplement the form and provide additional context.
* Review and update this form regularly to ensure that the information collected is accurate and consistent.
