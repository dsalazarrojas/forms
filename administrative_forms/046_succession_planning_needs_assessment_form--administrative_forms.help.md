## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID number of the employee being assessed.
2. Enter the current Job Title of the employee.
3. Enter the relevant skills the employee possesses for their current role.
4. Enter a brief description of the job the employee is currently in.
5. Enter the employee's career aspirations.
6. Select the future job roles the employee may take on in the future (e.g., CEO, Manager, Director).
7. Rate the employee's skills assessment (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
8. Rate the employee's job performance (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. Rate the employee's career development (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
10. Enter any feedback the employee has about their job or career.
11. Enter any comments from the employee's manager.
12. Rate the employee's job title awareness (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
13. Rate the employee's job performance awareness (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
14. Rate the employee's succession planning (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
15. Rate the employee's career development planning (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID**: This is the unique identifier for the employee being assessed.
* **Job Title**: Enter the current job title of the employee.
* **Skills**: Enter a list of skills the employee possesses that are relevant for their current role.
* **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job the employee is currently in.
* **Career Aspirations**: Enter a brief description of the employee's career aspirations.
* **Future Job Roles**: Select all future job roles the employee may take on in the future (e.g., CEO, Manager, Director).
* **Skills Assess**: Rate the employee's skills assessment (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Job Performance**: Rate the employee's job performance (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Career Development**: Rate the employee's career development (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
* **Employee Feedback**: Enter any feedback the employee has about their job or career.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from the employee's manager.
* **Job Title Awareness**: Rate the employee's awareness of their current job title (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
* **Job Performance Awareness**: Rate the employee's awareness of their current job performance (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
* **Succession Planning**: Rate the employee's succession planning (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
* **Career Development Planning**: Rate the employee's career development planning (e.g., High, Medium, Low, None).
