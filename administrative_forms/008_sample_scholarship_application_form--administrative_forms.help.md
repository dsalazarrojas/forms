# Sample Scholarship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sample Scholarship Application Form is designed for applicants to submit their application for a scholarship. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each section carefully, ensuring you provide all required information.
2. Review the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once, and avoid duplicates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your valid contact phone number.
* **Scholarship Type** (`scholarship_type`, Select One, Required): Select one of the following scholarship types:
	+ Merit: Based on academic merit
	+ Need-Based: Based on financial need
	+ Combination: Combination of both merit and need-based
* **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`city`, Text, Optional): Enter the city of your current address.
* **State** (`state`, Text, Optional): Enter the state of your current address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, Text, Optional): Enter your zip code of your current address.
* **Country** (`country`, Text, Optional): Enter the country of your current address.

## Tips
* Use a valid email address for communication purposes.
* Ensure accuracy and completeness of the provided information.
* Avoid duplicates; submit the form only once.
