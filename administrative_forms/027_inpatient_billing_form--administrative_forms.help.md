# Inpatient Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inpatient Billing Form is a document used for billing purposes when patients are admitted to the hospital. It helps to gather essential information for accurate and efficient billing processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Inpatient Billing Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Patient Name** field with the patient's full name.
2.  Enter the **Hospital Number** field with the corresponding hospital identifier.
3.  Enter the **Billing Date** field with the date of the billing period.
4.  Enter the **Billing Time** field with the time of the billing period.
5.  Enter the **Patient Address** field with the patient's address.
6.  Enter the **Billing Provider** field with the healthcare provider's name.
7.  Enter the **Insurance Info** field with any relevant insurance details.
8.  Enter the **Patient Account** field with the patient's account number or ID.
9.  Enter the **Medical Group** field with any relevant medical group details.
10. Provide a brief note in the **Billing Details** field explaining any relevant medical information.
11. Enter the **Total Charges** field with the total charges amount.
12. Select the **Payment Method** field with the chosen payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Insurance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (1): The patient's full name.
*   **Hospital Number** (2): The hospital's identifier for the patient.
*   **Billing Date** (3): The date of the billing period.
*   **Billing Time** (4): The time of the billing period.
*   **Patient Address** (5): The patient's address.
*   **Billing Provider** (6): The healthcare provider's name.
*   **Insurance Info** (7): Any relevant insurance details.
*   **Patient Account** (8): The patient's account number or ID.
*   **Medical Group** (9): Any relevant medical group details.
*   **Billing Details** (10): A brief note explaining any relevant medical information.
*   **Total Charges** (11): The total charges amount.
*   **Payment Method** (12): The chosen payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or Insurance).
