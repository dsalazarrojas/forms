# RFP Response Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFP Response Communication Form is used to document and communicate project status, stakeholder information, and other relevant details about a Request for Proposal (RFP) response. It helps ensure that all necessary information is accurately and consistently captured, facilitating clear communication with stakeholders and project teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the project status from the drop-down menu to indicate the current state of the RFP.
2. Enter the project name, which should match the name of the RFP.
3. Enter the name of the RFP.
4. Enter the stakeholder's name.
5. Enter the stakeholder's email address.
6. Enter the stakeholder's phone number.
7. Enter the stakeholder's comments, if any.
8. Enter any notes for the stakeholder.
9. Enter your response to the RFP.
10. Upload any supporting files related to the RFP response.
11. Review and update your response as necessary to ensure it is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Project Status (project_status)** (`select_one`, required/optional)
* This field indicates the current status of the RFP, such as Active, Closed, Cancelled, or Pending.

### 2. **Project Name (project_name)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter the name of the RFP project.

### 3. **RFP Name (rfp_name)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter the name of the RFP response.

### 4. **Project Status (project_status)** (`select_one`, required/optional)
* This field is a duplicate of Field 1, indicating the current status of the RFP.

### 5. **RFP Response (rfp_response)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter your response to the RFP.

### 6. **RFP File Upload (rfp_file_upload)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Upload any supporting files related to the RFP response.

### 7. **Stakeholder Name (stakeholder_name)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter the name of the stakeholder associated with this RFP response.

### 8. **Stakeholder Email (stakeholder_email)** (`email`, required/optional)
* Enter the email address of the stakeholder.

### 9. **Stakeholder Phone (stakeholder_phone)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter the phone number of the stakeholder.

### 10. **Stakeholder Comments (stakeholder_comments)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter any comments from the stakeholder.

### 11. **Stakeholder Notes (stakeholder_notes)** (`note`, required/optional)
* Enter any additional notes for the stakeholder.

### 12. **Stakeholder Signature (stakeholder_signature)** (`text`, required/optional)
* Enter any signature or acknowledgement from the stakeholder.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently.
* Review and update your response as necessary to ensure it is complete and accurate.
* Consult with your team or project lead if you're unsure about any field or requirement.
* Use this form for new RFP responses only, to avoid creating duplicate records.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to facilitate clear communication with stakeholders.
