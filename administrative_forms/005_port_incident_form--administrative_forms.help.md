# port_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for reporting new incidents that have occurred on a specific facility or port.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct facility where the incident occurred from the facility dropdown menu.
2. Provide a brief description of what happened.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident.
4. Enter the location of the incident.
5. Choose the priority level of the incident from the options provided.
6. Select the status of the incident from the options provided.
7. Choose the type of incident from the options provided.
8. Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
9. Upload any relevant attachments.
10. If necessary, enter any additional information about the incident or facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility** (`facility`, `select_one`): Select the facility where the incident occurred.
* **Description of Incident** (`incident_description`, `text`): Provide a brief description of what happened.
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, `time`): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, `text`): Enter the location of the incident.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter_id`, `select_one`): Select the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`, `select_one`): Choose the priority level of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, `select_one`): Select the status of the incident from the options provided.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_multiple`): Choose the type of incident from the options provided.
* **Comments** (`incident_comments`, `text`): Enter any comments or notes about the incident.
* **Attachment** (`incident_attachment`, `text`): Upload any relevant attachments.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`): Enter the address of the facility.
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, `text`): Enter the location of the facility.
* **Facility Contacts** (`facility_contacts`, `text`): Enter any contact information for the facility.
* **Incident Responders** (`incident_responders`, `text`): Enter any information about the responders who responded to the incident.
* **Incident Responder ID** (`incident_responder_id`, `select_one`): Select the person who responded to the incident.
* **Reporter Comment** (`incident_reporter_comment`, `text`): Enter any comments from the person who reported the incident.
* **Facility Phone** (`facility_phone`, `text`): Enter the phone number of the facility.
* **Facility Email** (`facility_email`, `text`): Enter the email address of the facility contact.
* **Facility Contact** (`facility_contact`, `text`): Enter any additional contact information for the facility.
* **Facility Other** (`facility_other`, `text`): Enter any other information about the facility.
* **Facility Notes** (`facility_notes`, `text`): Enter any additional notes about the facility.
* **Incident Other** (`incident_other`, `text`): Enter any other information about the incident.
* **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, `text`): Enter any additional notes about the incident.
