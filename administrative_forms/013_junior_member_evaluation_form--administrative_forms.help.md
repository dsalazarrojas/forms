# Junior Member Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior Member Evaluation Form is a tool for managers or evaluators to assess the performance and progress of junior members. It helps to provide constructive feedback and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the junior member's performance and progress during the evaluation period.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate assessment.
3. Provide specific examples or evidence to support your evaluation.
4. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
5. Be thorough and clear in your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Junior Member Name** (`junior_member_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the junior member being evaluated.
* **Junior Member Title** (`junior_member_title`, text, false): Enter the title or position of the junior member.
* **Junior Member Position** (`junior_member_position`, text, false): Enter a brief description of the junior member's role or responsibilities.
* **Evaluation Period Start** (`evaluation_period_start`, date, false): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Period End** (`evaluation_period_end`, date, false): Enter the end date of the evaluation period.
* **Junior Member Performance** (`junior_member_performance`, select_multiple, false): Select all that apply to indicate the junior member's performance during the evaluation period.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Development Areas** (`development_areas`, select_one, false): Select one option to indicate areas where the junior member needs development.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the junior member.
* **Junior Member Signature** (`junior_member_signature`, text, false): This field is intended for the junior member to sign and date their understanding of their evaluation.

Note: The required status of each field is not applicable in this form as all fields are not required.
