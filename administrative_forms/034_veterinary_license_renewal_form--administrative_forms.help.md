# Veterinary License Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by veterinarians to renew their licenses. It requires submission of relevant information such as license numbers, expiration dates, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your veterinary license number in the "License Number" field.
2. Enter the expiration date of your license in the "License Expiration Date" field.
3. Enter your name as a veterinary technician in the "Veterinary Technician Name" field.
4. Enter your name as a veterinarian in the "Veterinarian Name" field.
5. Select your veterinary license type from the dropdown menu in the "Veterinary License Type" field.
6. Enter the date you are submitting your license renewal application in the "Renewal Application Date" field.
7. Enter your signature as a veterinarian in the "Veterinarian Signature Date" field. This should be a date.
8. You have the option to enter additional contact details such as phone, email, and fax in fields "Phone", "Email", and "Fax". This is optional.
9. If you are a practice, enter your practice name in the "Practice Name" field.
10. Enter your practice address in the "Practice Address" field.
11. Enter your city, state, and zip in the "City", "State", and "Zip" fields.
12. Enter your practice license number in the "Practice License Number" field.
13. Enter the expiration date of your practice license in the "Practice License Expiration Date" field.
14. Enter the fee you are paying as a "License Renewal Fee" field. You will be required to pay this fee upon submission of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **License Number**: A unique number assigned to your veterinary license. Enter the full license number here.
- **License Expiration Date**: The date your license expires. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Veterinary Technician Name**: Enter your name as a veterinary technician if applicable.
- **Veterinarian Name**: Enter your name as a veterinarian if applicable.
- **Veterinary License Type**: Select the type of veterinary license you hold.
- **Renewal Application Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
- **Veterinarian Signature Date**: A date indicating when you signed this form.
- **Veterinarian Signature**: Your handwritten or typed signature as a veterinarian.
- **Phone**: Enter your practice phone number if applicable.
- **Email**: Enter your email address if applicable.
- **Fax**: Enter your practice fax number if applicable.
- **Practice Name**: If you are a practice, enter your business name.
- **Practice Address**: Enter your business address if you are a practice.
- **City**: Enter the city where your practice is located.
- **State**: Enter the state where your practice is located.
- **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your practice.
- **Practice License Number**: Enter the license number of your practice.
- **Practice License Expiration Date**: Enter the expiration date of your practice license.
- **License Renewal Fee**: A required fee for license renewal, payable upon form submission.
- **Practice Address 2**: Enter any additional address details for your practice.
- **Practice NPI**: Enter your practice's national provider identifier (NPI) number if applicable.
- **Contact Person**: Enter a contact person for your practice if applicable.
- **County**: Enter the county where your practice is located if applicable.
