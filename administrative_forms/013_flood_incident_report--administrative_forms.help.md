# Flood Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flood Incident Report form is a tool for reporting and documenting flood-related incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the incident, including the location, date, and time.
2. Describe the impact of the flood and the affected population.
3. Attach any relevant photos, videos, or documents to support your report.
4. Answer the questions about the incident's status, category, and priority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flood Incident Report Details**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location**: Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Description**: Describe the incident in more detail.
* **Incident Location Details**: Provide any additional information about the location of the incident.
* **Flood Impact Level**: Select the level of impact of the flood, using the options provided.
* **Flood Impact Description**: Describe the impact of the flood in more detail.
* **Affected Population**: Describe the people or groups affected by the flood.
* **Incident Photo**: Attach any photos related to the incident.
* **Incident Video**: Attach any videos related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio**: Attach any audio files related to the incident.
* **Incident Document**: Attach any documents related to the incident.
* **Incident Map**: Attach a map related to the incident.
* **Incident Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Attachments**: Attach any additional files or documents related to the incident.
* **Incident Status**: Select the status of the incident, using the options provided.
* **Incident Type**: Indicate whether the incident is a yes or no event.
* **Incident Response**: Indicate whether there is a response to the incident, yes or no.
* **Incident Category**: Select all categories that apply to the incident.
* **Incident Priority**: Select the priority of the incident, using the options provided.
* **Incident Tags**: Add any relevant tags or keywords related to the incident.
* **Incident Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Incident Reporter**: Indicate whether you are the reporter of the incident, yes or no.
* **Incident Last Updated**: Enter the date and time the incident was last updated.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Use the options provided to select the correct answers for each field.
* Attach relevant files and documents to support your report.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are complete and accurate.
