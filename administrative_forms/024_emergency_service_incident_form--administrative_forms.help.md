# emergency_service_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency service incident form is designed to capture details about incidents that require some level of attention or follow-up. This form should be used to report incidents that are not necessarily life-threatening or severe.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any) with accurate and detailed information.
3. Choose the correct options for the select_one fields, taking into account the nature of the incident.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture a brief description of the incident, providing essential details about what happened.
- **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options to categorize the type of incident reported.
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred or is still ongoing.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred or is still ongoing.
- **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location where the incident took place.
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident, including any supporting information.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person or team involved in the incident.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the person or team involved in the incident.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the person or team involved in the incident.
- **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, select_one, optional): Choose who reported the incident, from the provided options.
- **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the incident from the provided options.
- **Incident Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the incident from the provided options.
- **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) accurately and in detail.
- Use the select_one fields only when you are certain about the correct option.
- For fields with multiple options, choose the one that best describes the incident.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is accurate and complete.
