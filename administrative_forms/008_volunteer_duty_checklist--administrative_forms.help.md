# Volunteer Duty Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Duty Checklist form is used to record and track the duties assigned to volunteers during their shifts. This helps ensure that volunteers have a clear understanding of their responsibilities and deadlines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the duties assigned to the volunteer.
2. Answer each question truthfully based on the volunteer's duties.
3. Ensure that you have answered all required fields (indicated by a *).
4. If a field is optional, provide the information if it's relevant to the volunteer's duties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Duty Checklists** (`1`, `text`, not required): This field is a page title and is not editable.
* **Duty Checklist** (`2`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" if the volunteer has any duties assigned, and "No" if they do not have any duties. If you select "Yes", you will be prompted for additional information.
* **Volunteer Shift** (`3`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of the volunteer's shift.
* **Volunteer Shift Start Time** (`4`, `time`, not required): Enter the start time of the volunteer's shift.
* **Volunteer Shift End Time** (`5`, `time`, not required): Enter the end time of the volunteer's shift.
* **Volunteer Name** (`6`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the volunteer.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`7`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email** (`8`, `email`, not required): Enter the email address of the volunteer.
* **Duty Assignee** (`9`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate who assigned the duties to the volunteer. You can select multiple options.
* **Assigned Duty** (`10`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate which duties were assigned to the volunteer. You can select multiple options.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your supervisor or administrator for clarification.
* This form should not be used for other purposes, such as tracking volunteer hours or managing volunteer schedules.
