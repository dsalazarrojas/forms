# Adoption Certificate Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for an adoption certificate, which is an official document that confirms an adoption has been finalized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal contact information, including your full name and phone number.
2. Provide your email address for application status updates.
3. Enter the case reference number, which was issued during the adoption process.
4. Enter the final decree date and the court of jurisdiction.
5. Choose how many certified copies you require.
6. Select the delivery method for the certificate.
7. Choose the payment method for the processing fee.
8. If necessary, provide any additional requests or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): This is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide an email address for application status updates.
* **Case Reference Number** (`case_reference_number`, text, required): Enter the case reference number issued during the adoption process.
* **Final Decree Date** (`final_decree_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the court order.
* **Court of Jurisdiction** (`court_of_jurisdiction`, text, required): Enter the court that finalized the adoption.
* **Number of Certified Copies** (`number_of_certified_copies`, select_one, required): Choose the number of certified copies you require.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive the documents (Standard mail, Express courier, or In-person pickup).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of payment for the processing fee (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Money Order).
* **Additional Requests or Instructions** (`additional_requests_or_instructions`, text, optional): Provide any specific requirements or instructions for the certificate.

## Tips
- Please double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary information and supporting documents before completing this form.
- If you have questions or concerns while filling out this form, please reach out to the contact information provided.
