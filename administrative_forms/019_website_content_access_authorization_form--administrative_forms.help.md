# Website Content Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Content Access Authorization Form is used to request access to restricted website content. Users must submit this form to request access to content that is not publicly available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the organization that the content belongs to.
2. Choose the desired access level from the options provided.
3. Select the type(s) of access you require (e.g., Read, Edit, or Delete).
4. Enter the date you wish to start accessing the content.
5. Enter the date you wish to stop accessing the content (if applicable).
6. Add any additional comments about your request.
7. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
9. Select the person this request is assigned to.
10. Enter any comments or additional information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization that the content belongs to.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the level of access you require for the content, which is currently Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more types of access you require, which is currently Read, Edit, or Delete.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to start accessing the content.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you wish to stop accessing the content (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your request.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Select the person this request is assigned to, which is currently John, Jane, or Doe.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your request.
