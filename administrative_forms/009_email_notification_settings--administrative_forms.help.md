# email_notification_settings - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to set up email notification settings for users, allowing them to customize when and in what format they receive emails.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have a clear understanding of each field's purpose.
2. Fill in your email address in the "email_address" field.
3. Choose how often you want to receive emails in the "email_frequency" field.
4. Select the type of email you want to receive in the "notification_type" field.
5. Select the email format in the "email_format" field.
6. Enter the email address to be CC'd in the "cc_email" field (if applicable).
7. Select the recipients of the email in the "send_to" field.
8. Enter the email address of the sender in the "send_from" field (if applicable).
9. Enter the email address of the recipient in the "send_to_email" field.
10. Select the email format to be sent to the recipient in the "email_subject" and "email_body" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **email_address** (text, required): Enter your email address in this field to receive email notifications.
* **How often do you want to receive emails?** (select one, required): Choose how often you want to receive emails, options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Email type** (select one, required): Select the type of email you want to receive, options: Send updates, Send reminders.
* **Email format** (select one, required): Choose the email format, options: Plain text, HTML format.
* **CC email address** (text, optional): Enter the email address to be CC'd in the email.
* **BCC email address** (text, optional): Enter the BCC email address.
* **Email recipient** (select multiple, optional): Select who to send the email to, options: Admin, Staff.
* **Email sender** (text, optional): Enter the email address of the sender.
* **Email recipient's email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Send Cc Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address to be CC'd.
* **Email subject** (text, optional): Enter the subject of the email.
* **Email body** (note, optional): Enter the body of the email.

## Tips
* Be careful when entering email addresses to avoid errors.
* Make sure to select the correct email format to avoid confusion.
* Use the CC and BCC fields with caution, as they can be misinterpreted if not used correctly.
