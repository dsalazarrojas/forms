# Incident Resolution Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Resolution Absence Form is used to record and track the resolution status of incidents that have occurred within the organization. This form helps to ensure that all incidents are properly documented and resolved in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Employee field with the name of the employee involved in the incident.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the Incident Details field.
3. Select the correct resolution status for the incident from the options provided (Active, Closed, or Pending).
4. (Optional) Fill in the Incident Start Date and Incident End Date fields if applicable.
5. (Optional) Enter the duration of the incident in the Incident Duration field if applicable.
6. Click the Save button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee (`employee`)
* The Employee field is used to specify the name of the individual involved in the incident.

### Incident Details (`incident_details`)
* The Incident Details field is used to provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.

### Resolution Status (`resolution_status`)
* The Resolution Status field is used to select the current status of the incident:
	+ Active: The incident is currently being addressed.
	+ Closed: The incident has been resolved.
	+ Pending: The incident is awaiting further action or resolution.

### Incident Start Date (`incident_start_date`)
* The Incident Start Date field is used to specify the date and time when the incident started (if applicable).

### Incident End Date (`incident_end_date`)
* The Incident End Date field is used to specify the date and time when the incident ended (if applicable).

### Incident Duration (`incident_duration`)
* The Incident Duration field is used to specify the length of time the incident lasted (if applicable).
