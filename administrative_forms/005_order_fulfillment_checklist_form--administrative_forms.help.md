# Order Fulfillment Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Order Fulfillment Checklist Form is used to collect and track critical information about orders, ensuring accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Ensure accurate and complete information is entered for each field.
3. Review and update the form as necessary to ensure it accurately represents the order status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Info (Page 1)

• **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their order.

### Order Details (Page 1)

• **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): Describe the order in detail, including any specific products or services involved.

### Shipping Info (Page 1)

• **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, text, optional): Enter the shipping information for the order, including any relevant delivery details.

### Order Status (Page 2)

• **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Complete
	+ Partially Complete
	+ Not Complete

### Payment Method (Page 2)

• **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Cash
	+ Bank Transfer

### Shipping Address (Page 2)

• **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address for the order, including street address, apartment number, and any other relevant details.

### Shipping City (Page 2)

• **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the order will be shipped to.

### Shipping Postal Code (Page 3)

• **Shipping Postal Code** (`shipping_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the postal code (zip code) for the shipping address.

### Order Date (Page 3)

• **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the order was placed.

### Order Time (Page 3)

• **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the order was placed.

### Notes (Page 3)

• **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the order, such as special instructions or concerns.

### Assigned User (Page 4)

• **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to this order.

### Email (Page 4)

• **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.

### Phone (Page 4)

• **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

### Order Total Reimbursed (Page 4)

• **Order Total Reimbursed** (`order_total_reimbursed`, number, optional): Enter the total amount reimbursed for the order.

### Order Items Shipped (Page 5)

• **Order Items Shipped** (`order_items_shippped`, select_multiple, optional): Select all items that have been shipped, separated by commas:
	+ Item 1
	+ Item 2
	+ Item 3
	+ Item 4
	+ Item 5
