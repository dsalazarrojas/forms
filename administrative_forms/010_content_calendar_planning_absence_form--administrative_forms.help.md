# Content Calendar Planning Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by employees to request time off from their work due to various reasons such as vacation, sick leave, family emergencies, or other personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name, Employee ID, and Email Address.
2. Select the Type of Absence (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).
3. Enter the Absence Start Date and End Date.
4. Specify the Total Number of Days of Absence.
5. List your key Content Responsibilities that will be affected during your absence.
6. Confirm if there are any Pending Deadlines during your absence.
7. Describe the Deadline Details, if applicable.
8. Specify the Coverage Arrangements for your responsibilities.
9. Confirm if you have completed Handover with your covering colleague.
10. Confirm if the Content Calendar has been updated for your absence.
11. Specify the Status of Scheduled Social Media Posts.
12. Provide Urgent Contact Information, if applicable.
13. Provide any Access Instructions for your files.
14. Confirm if your Manager has approved this absence request.
15. Enter your Manager's Name.
16. Enter the Submission Date of this form.
17. Add any Additional Notes about your absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)**: Your full name as an employee.
* **Employee ID (2)**: Your employee identification number.
* **Department (3)**: Select your department or team you belong to.
* **Email Address (4)**: Your work email address.
* **Type of Absence (5)**: Select the reason for your absence (e.g. Vacation, Sick Leave, etc.).
* **Absence Start Date (6)**: First day of your absence.
* **Absence End Date (7)**: Last day of your absence.
* **Return Date (8)**: Date you will return to work.
* **Total Days of Absence (9)**: Number of business days you will be absent.
* **Content Responsibilities (10)**: List your key content responsibilities that will be affected during your absence.
* **Pending Deadlines (11)**: Confirm if you have any deadlines during your absence.
* **Deadline Details (12)**: Describe any pending deadlines and how they will be handled.
* **Coverage Arrangements (13)**: Specify who will cover your responsibilities while you are away.
* **Handover Completed (14)**: Confirm if you have completed handover with your covering colleague.
* **Content Calendar Updated (15)**: Confirm if the content calendar has been updated for your absence.
* **Scheduled Posts Status (16)**: Specify the status of scheduled social media posts.
* **Urgent Contacts (17)**: Provide urgent contact information, if applicable.
* **Access Instructions (18)**: Provide any special access instructions for your files.
* **Manager Approval Status (19)**: Confirm if your manager has approved this absence request.
* **Manager Name (20)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Submission Date (21)**: Date this form was submitted.
* **Additional Notes (22)**: Add any other information about your absence.
