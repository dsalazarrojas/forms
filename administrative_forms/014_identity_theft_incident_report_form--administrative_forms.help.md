# Identity Theft Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Identity Theft Incident Report Form is designed to collect information about incidents of identity theft that have occurred within an organization, such as credit card or bank account theft, social media identity theft, or employment identity theft.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the incident, including the type of identity theft, the date and location of the incident, and a brief description of what happened.
2. Enter information about the perpetrator, including their name, phone number, and address (if known).
3. Enter information about the victim, including their name, phone number, and email address (if known).
4. Enter the date the incident was reported and the date it was closed (if applicable).
5. Fill in any additional details or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (ID 1): Enter a brief summary of what happened.
* **Type** (ID 2): Select the type of identity theft that occurred (e.g., Credit card theft, Bank account takeover, etc.).
* **Date** (ID 3): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Location** (ID 4): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Perpetrator Info** (ID 5): Enter any additional information you have about the perpetrator.
* **Name** (ID 6): Enter the name of the perpetrator (if known).
* **Phone** (ID 7): Enter the perpetrator's phone number (if known).
* **Address** (ID 8): Enter the perpetrator's address (if known).
* **Distinguishing Characteristics** (ID 9): Enter any distinguishing features of the perpetrator (e.g., height, hair color, etc.).
* **Victim Info** (ID 10): Enter any additional information you have about the victim.
* **Name** (ID 11): Enter the name of the victim.
* **Phone** (ID 12): Enter the victim's phone number.
* **Address** (ID 13): Enter the victim's address.
* **Email** (ID 14): Enter the victim's email address (if known).
* **Relationship** (ID 15): Enter the relationship between the victim and the perpetrator (if applicable).
* **Description** (ID 16): Enter a detailed description of what happened.
* **Reported** (ID 17): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Reported to** (ID 18): Enter the department or authority the incident was reported to.
* **Reported by** (ID 19): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Date Reported** (ID 20): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Time Reported** (ID 21): Enter the time the incident was reported.
* **Status** (ID 22): Enter the status of the incident (e.g., Open, In progress, etc.).
* **Closed** (ID 23): Enter the date the incident was closed (if applicable).
* **Closed by** (ID 24): Enter the name of the person who closed the incident.
* **Date Closed** (ID 25): Enter the date the incident was closed.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or the person responsible for incident reporting for guidance.
