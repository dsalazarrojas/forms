# License Plate Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information related to vehicle licensing, specifically for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle number, license plate number, and VIN (Vehicle Identification Number).
2. Select the type of your vehicle (vehicle size or class).
3. Choose the state where your vehicle is registered or located.
4. Select all colors that match the vehicle.
5. Enter the date when the vehicle was registered, inspected, or verified.
6. Enter the time when the vehicle was registered, inspected, or verified.
7. Provide the name or contact information of the vehicle owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle number as it appears on the license plate.
* **License Plate Number** (`license_plate_number`, text, optional): Enter the license plate number as it appears on the license plate.
* **VIN** (`vin`, text, optional): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) as it appears on the vehicle documentation.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, optional): Select the size or class of your vehicle.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select the state where your vehicle is registered or located.
* **Color** (`color`, select_multiple, optional): Select all colors that match the vehicle.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the vehicle was registered, inspected, or verified.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the vehicle was registered, inspected, or verified.
* **Vehicle Owner** (`vehicle_owner`, text, optional): Provide the name or contact information of the vehicle owner.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct information for your vehicle.
* Select the correct options for the vehicle type and state.
* Select all colors that match the vehicle.
* Be accurate when entering the date and time.
* Enter the correct contact information for the vehicle owner.
