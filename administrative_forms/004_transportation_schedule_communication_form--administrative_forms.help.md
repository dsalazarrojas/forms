# transportation_schedule_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The transportation schedule communication form is used to schedule and communicate transportation details with your team or organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (transport_type, schedule_start_time, schedule_end_time, schedule_date)
2. Select the transporter details (Active or Inactive) and transport status (Active or Inactive) if required
3. Provide contact details if required
4. Fill in the schedule location if required
5. Review and submit the form 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transport Type** (transport_type, select_one, required): Select whether the transportation is available ('Yes' or 'No') 
* **Schedule Start Time** (schedule_start_time, time, required): Enter the start time for the scheduled transportation
* **Schedule End Time** (schedule_end_time, text, required): Enter the end time for the scheduled transportation
* **Transporter Details** (transporters_details, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the transporter (Active or Inactive)
* **Schedule Date** (schedule_date, date, required): Enter the date for the scheduled transportation
* **Transporters Status** (transporters_status, select_one, required): Select the status of the transporter (Active or Inactive)
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): Enter any additional contact information for the scheduled transportation
* **Schedule Location** (schedule_location, note, required): Enter the location for the scheduled transportation
