# Guest Room Damage Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Room Damage Incident Form is used to report any damage or incidents that occurred during your stay at the hotel. This form helps us to take the necessary steps to address the issue and provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Did the incident occur?"
2. Provide a brief summary of what happened in the text field "Incident Summary".
3. If you wish to contact the hotel for follow-up, please enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Did the incident occur?** (`incident_details`, select_one, required): Please answer "Yes" if the incident occurred, and "No" otherwise.
* **Incident Summary** (`incident_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe what happened during your stay. This will help us to understand the issue and take the necessary steps to resolve it.
* **Contact Information** (`guest_contact_info`, text, optional): If you wish to contact the hotel for follow-up, please enter your name, email, and phone number here. This will ensure that we can get in touch with you to discuss the incident further.
