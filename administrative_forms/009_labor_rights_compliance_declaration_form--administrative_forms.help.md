# Labor Rights Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about an employee's labor rights status. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the accuracy of labor rights documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification card or personnel file.
2.  Choose the correct department for the employee's job title or role.
3.  Enter the date the employee started their employment with the organization.
4.  Enter the date the employee's employment ended (if applicable).
5.  Enter the number of hours the employee worked during their employment.
6.  Enter the employee's rate of pay.
7.  Select the relevant labor practices that apply to the employee's job.
8.  If the employee has any other labor practices not listed, please enter them in the "Other labor practices (if any)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee name** (`employee_name`, text, optional)
Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification card or personnel file.

### 2. **Department** (`department`, text, optional)
Choose the correct department for the employee's job title or role.

### 3. **Employment start date** (`employment_start_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date the employee started their employment with the organization.

### 4. **Employment end date** (`employment_end_date`, date, optional)
Enter the date the employee's employment ended (if applicable).

### 5. **Hours worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional)
Enter the number of hours the employee worked during their employment.

### 6. **Rate of pay** (`rate_of_pay`, number, optional)
Enter the employee's rate of pay.

### 7. **Labor practices** (`labor_practices`, select_one, optional)
Select one or more of the listed labor practices that apply to the employee's job.

### 8. **Other labor practices (if any)** (`labor_practices_other`, text, optional)
If the employee has any labor practices not listed, please enter them in this field.

## Tips
- Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct employee.
- Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
- If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant department or manager.
