# Cybersecurity Incident Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cybersecurity Incident Records Release Form is used to record and release information about cybersecurity incidents that have occurred within the organization. This form helps to gather and document incident details, facilitating accurate tracking and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the incident date, location, and type of the incident. If the incident is a repeat occurrence, mark the incident_type field as 'Yes'.
2. Provide details of the incident in the incident_details field.
3. Classify the incident as either 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your judgment.
4. Select the incident status as either 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on the current situation.
5. If applicable, enter the date of resolution in incident_resolution_date field and the method used in incident_resolution_method field.
6. Add any relevant notes about the resolution in incident_resolution_notes field.
7. Select whether the incident will be reviewed and approved by relevant personnel ('Yes' or 'No').
8. Choose whether to release the incident details ('Yes' or 'No').
9. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes about the incident in incident_comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_date**: Enter the date of the incident in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **incident_location**: Enter the location of the incident, including floor or room numbers if applicable.
* **incident_type**: Mark this field as 'Yes' if the incident is a repeat occurrence.
* **incident_report**: Enter a detailed description of the incident.
* **incident_details**: Provide additional context or information about the incident.
* **incident_classification**: Classify the incident as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **incident_status**: Choose the current status of the incident, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **incident_resolution_date**: Enter the date of incident resolution, if applicable.
* **incident_resolution_method**: Enter the method used to resolve the incident, if applicable.
* **incident_resolution_notes**: Add any additional notes about the resolution.
* **incident_review**: Select 'Yes' if the incident will be reviewed and approved by relevant personnel.
* **incident_approval**: Choose whether to release incident details, marking 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **incident_release**: Select whether to release incident details, marking 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **incident_comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.

## Tips

* Fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the incident is properly recorded and released.
* If the incident status is 'Active', do not mark it as 'Inactive' until it has been resolved.
* Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in incident status.
* Ensure that relevant personnel review and approve the incident before release.
