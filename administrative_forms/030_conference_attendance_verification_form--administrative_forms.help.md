# Conference Attendance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Attendance Verification Form is designed to verify your attendance at a conference. It will help you provide accurate information about your participation, conference details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your information accurately and truthfully.
3. If you have any questions or need help, please contact the conference organizers or event organizers.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
5. Once you submit the form, it will be reviewed and verified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendance Verification** (`verification_header`, note, required: false): Verify your conference attendance in this section.
* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date you are verifying your attendance.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, note, required: false): Enter your details, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required: true): Enter your full name as registered.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: true): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: true): Enter your company or institution name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position.
* **Conference Information** (`conference_info`, note, required: false): Enter event details.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required: true): Enter the conference name.
* **Conference Dates** (`conference_dates`, text, required: true): Enter the conference dates.
* **Conference Location** (`conference_location`, text, required: true): Enter the conference location.
* **Registration Number** (`registration_number`, text, required: false): Enter your registration ID.
* **Attendance Details** (`attendance_details`, note, required: false): Verify your participation.
* **Days Attended** (`days_attended`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all the days you attended.
* **Number of Sessions** (`sessions_attended`, number, required: false): Enter the approximate number of sessions you attended.
* **Keynote Sessions Attended** (`keynote_attended`, select_multiple, required: false): Select which keynotes you attended.
* **Verification Purpose** (`verification_purpose`, select_one, required: true): Select the reason for verification.
* **Credits Needed** (`credits_needed`, number, required: false): Enter the number of credits or hours needed.
* **Certificate Delivery** (`certificate_delivery`, select_one, required: true): Select how to receive your certificate.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required: false): Enter your mailing address for certificate delivery.
* **Employer Verification Required** (`employer_verification`, select_one, required: false): Select if your employer needs confirmation.
* **Employer Email** (`employer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your employer's email for confirmation.
* **Additional Requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, required: false): Enter any special verification needs.
* **Declaration** (`declaration`, select_one, required: true): Confirm the accuracy of your attendance.
