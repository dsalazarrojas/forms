# Food Safety Inspection Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Inspection Records Release Form is a document used to release the results of a food safety inspection, indicating the facility's status and any necessary actions or dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name in the "business_name" field.
2. Enter the inspector's name in the "inspector_name" field.
3. Select the inspection date from the calendar or enter it manually in the "inspection_date" field. This field is optional.
4. Enter the facility's address in the "facility_address" field.
5. Enter the facility's name in the "facility_name" field.
6. Select the inspection result in the "inspection_result" field. Options are:
	* Passed
	* Failed
	* Rescheduled
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field. This field is optional.
8. Select the facility type in the "facility_type" field. Options are:
	* Restaurant
	* Food Processing Plant
	* Food Processing Plant - Retail
	* Food Manufacturing
	* Other (please specify)
9. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact_name" field.
10. Enter the contact person's email in the "contact_email" field. This field is optional.
11. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact_phone" field. This field is optional.
12. Enter any contact notes in the "contact_notes" field.
13. Enter any additional notes in the "additional_notes" field. This field is optional.
14. Enter the release date in the "release_date" field.
15. Enter the name of the person signing the document in the "signed_by" field.
16. Enter a title or description in the "title" field.
17. Enter any inspector comments in the "inspector_comments" field.
18. Select the facility's status in the "facility_status" field. Options are:
	* Active
	* Suspended
	* Permanently Closed
19. Enter the date the facility was suspended or closed in the "facility_status_date" field. This field is optional.
20. Select the facility type status in the "facility_type_status" field. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Unknown
21. Select the facility address status in the "facility_address_status" field. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Unknown
22. Select the facility equipment status in the "facility_equipment_status" field. Options are:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Unknown
23. Enter the date the facility's equipment status was last updated in the "facility_equipment_status_date" field. This field is optional.
24. Select the facility's license status in the "facility_license_status" field. Options are:
	* Active
	* Suspended
	* Expired
25. Enter the date the facility's license status was last updated in the "facility_license_status_date" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business or facility being inspected.
* **Inspector Name**: Enter the name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection or enter it manually. This field is optional.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility being inspected.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility being inspected.
* **Inspection Result**: Select the outcome of the inspection:
	+ Passed
	+ Failed
	+ Rescheduled
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection. This field is optional.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility being inspected:
	+ Restaurant
	+ Food Processing Plant
	+ Food Processing Plant - Retail
	+ Food Manufacturing
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person. This field is optional.
* **Contact Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the contact person.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the facility. This field is optional.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date to release the inspection results.
* **Signed By**: Enter the name of the person signing the document.
* **Title**: Enter a title or description of the document.
* **Inspector Comments**: Enter any comments from the inspector.
* **Facility Status**: Select the status of the facility:
	+ Active
	+ Suspended
	+ Permanently Closed
* **Facility Status Date**: Enter the date the facility was suspended or closed. This field is optional.
* **Facility Type Status**: Select the status of the facility type:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unknown
* **Facility Address Status**: Select the status of the facility address:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unknown
* **Facility Equipment Status**: Select the status of the facility equipment:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Unknown
* **Facility Equipment Status Date**: Enter the date the facility's equipment status was last updated. This field is optional.
* **Facility License Status**: Select the status of the facility's license:
	+ Active
	+ Suspended
	+ Expired
* **Facility License Status Date**: Enter the date the facility's license status was last updated. This field is optional.

## Tips
This form is intended for authorized personnel only. Please ensure that you have permission to complete this form before proceeding.
