# Employee Resource Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Resource Access Request Form is a tool for employees to submit requests for access to resources within the organization. This form will guide you through the necessary fields to complete the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals before submitting the form.
2. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your employee name as it appears on your personnel profile.
3. Select the resource you need access to:
	* **Resource Title**: Enter a brief description of the resource you need.
4. Enter your employee details:
	* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as assigned by the HR department.
	* **Resource ID**: Enter the ID of the resource you are requesting.
	* **Manager ID**: Enter the ID of your direct manager.
5. Specify the dates:
	* **Start Date**: Enter the date you need to start using the resource.
	* **End Date** (if applicable): Enter the date you expect to stop using the resource. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
6. Provide additional information (optional):
	* **Reason**: Enter a brief description of why you need access to the resource.
	* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any additional tool or software you are using to access the resource.
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the resource access.
7. Verify your contact details:
	* **Email**: Enter your official company email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
8. Review and complete the form:
	* Ensure all required fields are filled in and the form is complete.
	* Click on the "Submit" button to send the request.
	* Click on the "Cancel" button if you need to stop the process or if there's an issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: A mandatory field where you should enter your name as it is known in the personnel system.
* **Resource Title**: A brief description of the resource you need.
* **Employee ID**: A mandatory field where you should enter your employee ID as assigned by the HR department.
* **Resource ID**: A mandatory field where you should enter the ID of the resource you are requesting.
* **Manager ID**: A mandatory field where you should enter the ID of your direct manager.
* **Start Date**: A mandatory field where you should enter the date you need to start using the resource.
* **End Date**: A field to enter the date you expect to stop using the resource. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Reason**: An optional field where you can describe why you need access to the resource.
* **Assigned Tool**: An optional field where you can enter any additional tools or software used to access the resource.
* **Notes**: An optional field where you can enter any additional comments or instructions for the resource access.
* **Email**: A mandatory field where you should enter your official company email address.
* **Phone**: A mandatory field where you should enter your work phone number.
* **Submit**: A mandatory field where you should click to send the request.
* **Cancel**: An optional field where you can click to stop the process if necessary.
