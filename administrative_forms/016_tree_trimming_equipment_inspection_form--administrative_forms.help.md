# Tree Trimming Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Trimming Equipment Inspection Form is used to inspect and assess the condition and performance of tree trimming equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information about the equipment being inspected.
2. For each field, select or enter the relevant information according to your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blade** (1, number, required: false): Enter the number of the blade being inspected. This field is optional, but it helps to provide context for the inspection results.
* **Chain Saw Sharpness** (2, text, required: false): Describe the sharpness of the chain saw. Select "Good" if the saw is sharp, "Fair" if it's average, and "Poor" if it's dull.
* **Guard Condition** (3, select_one, required: false): Assess the condition of the guard. Select "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to indicate its state.
* **Chain Saw Condition** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Describe the condition of the chain saw. Select "New", "Used", or "Old" to indicate its age and state.
* **Cutting Head** (5, time, required: false): Record the time spent using the cutting head. This field is optional but helps to track equipment usage.
* **Cutting Head Hose** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Check the condition of the cutting head hose. Select "Attached", "Detached", or "Missing" to report its state.
* **Handle** (7, text, required: false): Describe the condition or any issues with the handle.
* **Motor** (8, text, required: false): Enter any information about the motor, such as its condition or performance issues.
* **Blade Tension** (9, number, required: false): Measure and record the blade tension. This field is optional but helps to provide detailed inspection results.
* **Oil Level** (10, number, required: false): Check and record the oil level. This field is optional but helps to monitor equipment maintenance needs.
* **Bar** (11, text, required: false): Describe any issues or conditions affecting the bar.

## Tips
* Ensure you accurately assess each field and provide clear descriptions.
* Be thorough and methodical in your inspection to identify potential issues.
* If unsure about any field, consult relevant documentation or seek guidance from experts.
