# training_completion_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare or verify your participation in or completion of training sessions. It gathers essential contact information and specific details about the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Go to the relevant page for this form (currently "first_page") and fill out the provided fields.
*   Ensure you provide accurate information in all fields, especially if marked as required (even though none are marked as required in this form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Contact Information (contact_info)**: This field is for providing your contact details.
    *   It's a text field where you can enter your contact information.
*   **Email (email)**: This is your email address.
    *   Enter your email in this format, ensuring it's correct and up-to-date.
*   **Phone (phone)**: This is for your phone number.
    *   Enter your phone number in the format required for your phone type (mobile, landline, etc.).
*   **Date (date)**: This field is for the date of the training completion.
    *   Choose a date from the calendar or enter it in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM-DD, or other format depending on your system settings).
*   **Time (time)**: This is for the time of the training completion.
    *   Choose a time from the provided options or enter it manually in the correct format (HH:MM or other format depending on your system settings).
