# Executive Summary Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Summary Access Form is designed to collect information from executives, managers, and administrators about the type of reports they need to access. This information will help ensure that the right executives receive the correct reports, allowing them to make informed decisions and access relevant information efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Organization Name in the first field.
2. Provide your User Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Email address in the third field.
4. Select your Role from the dropdown menu in the fourth field.
5. Choose the Report Type you need from the dropdown menu in the fifth field.
6. Enter the Date and Time you need access to the report in the seventh and eighth fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are from.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Type your name as it appears on your business cards.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role within the organization:
	+ Executive
	+ Manager
	+ Admin
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Type in the department name if it is different from the organization name.
* **Report Type** (`report_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of report you need:
	+ Sales
	+ Revenue
	+ Expenses
	+ Other (please specify in the notes field)
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you need access to the report.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you need access to the report.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your report needs.

## Tips
* Please be specific about your report needs to ensure that you receive the correct reports.
* If you need multiple reports, fill out this form multiple times with different report types.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
