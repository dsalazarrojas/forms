# corporate_governance_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate governance assessment form is designed to evaluate and assess the effectiveness of an organization's corporate governance practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and provide answers based on your knowledge of the organization's practices.
2. For select fields, choose the most accurate option from the provided list.
3. Be as detailed as possible when responding to text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Governance Practices**: 
    * corporate_governance_practices (text): Briefly describe the organization's corporate governance practices.
* **Board Composition**:
    * board_composition (select_one): Select the type of governance that best describes the board's composition: Governance, Risk, or Compliance.
* **Meeting Minutes**:
    * meeting_minutes (text): Provide an example or description of meeting minutes, if applicable.
* **Reporting**:
    * reporting (select_multiple): Select the frequency of reporting: Quarterly, Monthly, or Annual.
* **Decision Making**:
    * decision_making (text): Explain the decision-making process in the organization.
* **Policy Development**:
    * policy_development (select_multiple): Select the type of policy development that occurs in the organization: Board, Management, or Employees.
* **Risk Management**:
    * risk_management (select_multiple): Select the type of risk management: Internal, External, or Both.
* **Compliance**:
    * compliance (select_multiple): Select the type of compliance: Internal, External, or Both.
* **Audit Trails**:
    * audit_trails (text): Provide an example or description of audit trails, if applicable.
* **Transparency**:
    * transparency (select_one): Select the level of transparency: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Accountability**:
    * accountability (select_multiple): Select the type of accountability: Internal, External, or Both.
* **Communication**:
    * communication (select_multiple): Select the types of communication: Board, Management, or Employees.
* **Decision Making Process**:
    * decision_making_process (text): Describe the decision-making process in the organization.
* **Meeting Frequency**:
    * meeting_frequency (text): Report how often board meetings take place.
* **Reporting Frequency**:
    * reporting_frequency (text): Report the frequency of reporting in the organization.
* **Transparency Level**:
    * transparency_level (text): Describe the level of transparency in the organization.
* **Accountability Level**:
    * accountability_level (text): Describe the level of accountability in the organization.
* **Decision Making Culture**:
    * decision_making_culture (select_multiple): Select the type of decision-making culture: Consensus, Other.
* **Decision Making Process**:
    * decision_making_process (text): Describe the decision-making process in the organization.
* **Board Committees**:
    * board_committees (text): List any existing board committees.
* **Board Committees Meeting Frequency**:
    * board_committees_meeting_frequency (text): Report how often board committee meetings take place.
* **Board Committees Reporting**:
    * board_committees_reporting (text): Report the frequency of reporting from board committees.
* **Board Committees Reporting Frequency**:
    * board_committees_reporting_frequency (text): Report the frequency of reporting from board committees.
* **Board Committees Reporting Transparency**:
    * board_committees_reporting_transparency (select_multiple): Select the level of transparency from board committees: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Board Committees Decision Making Culture**:
    * board_committees_decision_making_culture (text): Describe the decision-making culture of board committees.
