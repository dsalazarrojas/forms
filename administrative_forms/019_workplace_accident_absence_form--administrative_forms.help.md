# Workplace Accident Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Accident Absence Form is used to report incidents that have occurred during work hours. This form helps ensure accurate and consistent reporting of workplace accidents, providing necessary details for investigation and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the incident date when the accident occurred.
2.  Enter the employee's name as they are associated with the incident.
3.  Provide a brief description of the incident, including any details that may be relevant to understanding what happened.
4.  Select the reporting manager who will be handling the incident.
5.  Enter the date and time of reporting, and the location where you're reporting.
6.  Enter the employee's ID for identification purposes.
7.  Provide contact information for the person reporting the incident.
8.  Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Injury, Illness, etc.).
9.  Enter any additional details about the incident.
10.  Choose the reporting method used (e.g., In-Person, Email, etc.).
11.  If witnesses were present, provide their names and contact information.
12.  If a witness was present, select their type (e.g., Manager, Employee, etc.).
13.  Enter the location where the witness(es) was(were) present.
14.  Provide any contact information for the witness(es).
15.  Enter your reporting status, such as whether the incident is being reported or not.
16.  Enter your reason for reporting the incident.
17.  Provide a category for the incident (e.g., Injury, Illness, etc.).
18.  If the category is not listed, enter an "Other" category with further details.
19.  Finally, enter the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date**: The date when the incident occurred.
*   **Employee Name**: The name of the employee who was involved in the incident.
*   **Description**: A brief description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Reporting Manager**: The manager responsible for handling the incident.
*   **Reporting Date**: The date and time when you're reporting the incident.
*   **Reporting Time**: The time when you're reporting the incident.
*   **Reporting Time of Day**: The time of day when you're reporting the incident.
*   **Reporting Location**: The location where you're reporting the incident.
*   **Employee ID**: The employee ID for the employee involved in the incident.
*   **Reporting Contact**: Your contact information for further communication.
*   **Incident Type**: The type of incident that occurred (e.g., Injury, Illness, etc.).
*   **Incident Details**: Any additional details about the incident.
*   **Reporting Method**: The method used to report the incident (e.g., In-Person, Email, etc.).
*   **Witness**: The name of any witness(es) who was/were present.
*   **Witness Details**: Any details about the witness(es).
*   **Witness Type**: The type of witness (e.g., Manager, Employee, etc.).
*   **Witness Location**: The location where the witness(es) was/were present.
*   **Witness Contact**: The contact information for the witness(es).
*   **Reporting Status**: Your reporting status, indicating whether the incident is being reported or not.
*   **Reporting Reason**: The reason for reporting the incident.
*   **Incident Category**: The category of the incident (e.g., Injury, Illness, etc.).
*   **Incident Category Other**: If the category is not listed, enter an "Other" category with further details.
*   **Submission Date**: The date and time when you're submitting the form.
