# requisition_history_overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "requisition_history_overview" form is a summary tool for tracking and reviewing past requisitions, their status, and related details. It can be used by administrators or managers to track and analyze requisition trends, identify patterns, and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Requisition Date" field with the date the requisition was made.
2. In the "Requisitioner" field, enter the name of the person who made the requisition.
3. Enter the "Requisition Number" and "Requisition Type" as relevant details.
4. Select the current "Requisition Status" of the requisition from the options provided.
5. Optionally, enter the "Requisition Amount" if this is a monetary value.
6. Select any relevant "Requisition Type" (Material or Service).
7. Enter any additional details about the requisition in the "Requisition Details" field.
8. If necessary, add notes about the requisition in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the email address and phone number of the person involved in the requisition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requisition Date**: Enter the date the requisition was made.
* **Requisitioner**: Enter the name of the person who made the requisition.
* **Requisition Number**: Enter the unique identifier for the requisition.
* **Requisition Status**: Select the current status of the requisition (Approved, Denied, Reopened, Cancelled, Closed).
* **Requisition Amount**: Enter the monetary value of the requisition, if applicable.
* **Requisition Type**: Select the type of requisition (Material or Service).
* **Approver**: Enter the name of the person who approved the requisition.
* **Requisition Details**: Enter any additional details about the requisition.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the requisition.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the person involved in the requisition.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person involved in the requisition.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary permissions to access and edit this form.
* Verify that you are using the correct format for dates and numbers.
* Double-check the "Requisition Status" to ensure it accurately reflects the current state of the requisition.
* Be thorough in the "Requisition Details" field to provide as much context as possible.
