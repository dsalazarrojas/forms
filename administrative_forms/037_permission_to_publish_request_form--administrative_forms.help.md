# Permission to Publish Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting permission to publish content that may require approval from a reviewer or editor. It collects information from the requestor and allows them to provide details about the content and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide a brief summary of the content you wish to publish.
3. Specify the approval status of the content (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
4. If the content is pending approval, provide the reason for the pending status.
5. If you are a reviewer or editor, specify the review status of the content (Published, Pending, or Draft).
6. If you are a reviewer or editor, enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor** (`Requestor`): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`): Enter your email address and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Content Summary** (`Content Summary`): Provide a brief description of the content you wish to publish.
* **Permission Details** (`Permission Details`): Specify the approval status of the content (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Approval Reason** (`Approval Reason`): If the content is pending approval, provide a reason for the pending status.
* **Approval Date** (`Approval Date`): Enter the date when you expect the content to be approved or published.
* **Publish Date** (`Publish Date`): Enter the date when you expect the content to be published.
* **Review Status** (`Review Status`): If you are a reviewer or editor, specify the review status of the content (Published, Pending, or Draft).
* **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`): If you are a reviewer or editor, enter your name.
* **Submitter** (`Submitter`): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have permission to use this form for your intended purpose.
* This form is a general template and may require customization to fit your organization's specific requirements.
* If you are a reviewer or editor, please only select one review status option (Published, Pending, or Draft).
* If the content is pending approval, please provide a clear and concise reason for the pending status.
