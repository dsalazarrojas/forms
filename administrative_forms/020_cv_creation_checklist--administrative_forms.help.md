# CV Creation Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The CV creation checklist is a form designed to collect information about an individual's professional background, which can be used to create a comprehensive CV or resume.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name as it should appear on your CV.
2. Provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
3. Write a brief professional summary (2-4 sentences) that outlines your experience and qualifications.
4. List your key skills in comma-separated format.
5. List your education, including the institutions you attended and the qualifications you received.
6. Provide a summary of your recent work history, including the roles you've held and the dates of employment.
7. If applicable, list any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
8. If you have references available on request, provide their contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it should appear on your CV.
* **Contact details** (contact_details, text, required): Provide your email address and phone number, making it easy for potential employers or clients to get in touch with you.
* **Professional summary** (professional_summary, text, required): Write a brief overview of your experience and qualifications (2-4 sentences) that highlights your strengths and achievements.
* **Key skills** (key_skills, text, required): List your key skills in comma-separated format, using keywords that are relevant to your profession.
* **Education** (education, text, required): List the institutions you've attended and the qualifications you've received, including any relevant coursework or specializations.
* **Work history summary** (work_history, text, required): Provide a brief summary of your recent work history, including the roles you've held and the dates of employment.
* **Certifications and licenses** (certifications, text, optional): If you have any relevant certifications or licenses, list them here.
* **References available on request** (references, text, optional): If you have references available on request, provide their contact details, but this information is not required.
