# Paid Leave Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a paid leave request to your manager.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* Paid Leave Request: Enter a brief description of your leave request.
	* Date: Enter the date range of your leave.
	* From: Enter the start date of your leave.
	* To: Enter the end date of your leave.
	* Reason for Leave: Enter a brief reason for your leave request.
2. Select the type of leave:
	* Sick Leave
	* Vacation
	* Bereavement
	* Family Leave
3. Enter the number of leave days:
	* This field is not always required, but it's helpful for your manager to know how many days you'll be taking.
4. Enter your contact information:
	* This is optional, but it's a good idea to include a way for your manager to reach out to you.
5. Check with your manager:
	* If you're unsure about your manager's availability, you can check with them before submitting the form.
6. Check the approval status:
	* In Progress: Your manager is still reviewing your request.
	* Approved: Your request has been approved.
	* Denied: Your request has been denied.
7. Add any comments:
	* This is optional, but it's a good idea to include any additional information or context about your request.
8. Submit the form:
	* Click the "Submit" button to send your request to your manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paid Leave Request**: A brief description of your leave request.
* **Date**: Enter the date range of your leave.
* **From**: Enter the start date of your leave.
* **To**: Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason for Leave**: Enter a brief reason for your leave request.
* **Type of Leave**: Select the type of leave you're requesting (Sick Leave, Vacation, Bereavement, Family Leave).
* **Number of Days**: Enter the number of leave days (if applicable).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information (optional).
* **Manager OK**: Confirm that you've checked with your manager before submitting the form.
* **Approval Status**: The status of your leave request (In Progress, Approved, Denied).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or context about your request.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your completed form to your manager.
