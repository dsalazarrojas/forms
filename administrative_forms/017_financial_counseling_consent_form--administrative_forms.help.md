# Financial Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Counseling Consent Form is used to obtain consent from both the client and the financial professional for financial counseling services. It is essential to have a clear understanding of the consent process to ensure that all parties involved are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Provide your client information, including client name, signature date, and signature.
3. If you are the financial professional, indicate that you have informed the client and provide your signature, signature date, and name.
4. Select the financial services you will provide to the client.
5. Check the signature status of the client and financial professional.
6. If necessary, update the signature status after completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Informed**: A note field where the financial professional notes that they have informed the client about the consent process.
*   **Client Signature Date**: The date on which the client signs the consent form. Enter the date in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
*   **Financial Professional Informed**: A note field where the financial professional notes that they have informed the client about the consent process.
*   **Service**: Select the financial service you will provide to the client. Options include Financial Planning, Debt Management, Credit Building, and Budgeting.
*   **Signature Date**: The date on which the financial professional signs the consent form. Enter the date in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
*   **Financial Professional Signature**: Enter the signature of the financial professional in this field.
*   **Financial Professional Name**: The name of the financial professional signing the form.
*   **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client signing the form.
*   **Client Signature**: Enter the signature of the client in this field.
*   **Signature Status**: Select the status of the signatures. Options include Complete, Incomplete, or Not Required.
*   **Financial Professional Title**: Enter the title of the financial professional signing the form. 

Note: The fields are ordered based on their ID in the YAML inputSchema.
