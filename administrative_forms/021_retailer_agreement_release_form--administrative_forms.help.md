# Retailer Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information from retailers about their agreements and contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Retailer" field with the retailer's name.
2. Enter the "Agreement Date" in the "Agreement Date" field.
3. Select the "Prophets" from the list.
4. Fill in the "Retail Address" fields with the retailer's address.
5. Select the "Agreement Terms" from the list.
6. Enter the "Release Date" in the "Release Date" field.
7. Fill in the "Assigned Tool" field with the name of the tool assigned to the retailer.
8. Fill in the "Category" field with the category of the agreement.
9. Fill in the "Description" field with a brief description of the agreement.
10. Fill in the "Output File" field with the name of the output file.
11. Fill in the "Form Id" field with the form ID.
12. Fill in the "Form Version" field with the version of the form.
13. Fill in the "Form Notes" field with any additional notes about the form.
14. Fill in the "Retailer Agreement Version" field with the version of the agreement.
15. Fill in the "Retail Address 2" to "Retail Address 8" fields with additional address information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer** (Required): Enter the name of the retailer.
* **Agreement Date** (Required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Prophets** (Required): Select the name of the prophet from the list.
* **Retail Address** (Required): Enter the address of the retailer.
* **Agreement Terms** (Required): Select the terms of the agreement from the list.
* **Release Date** (Required): Enter the date of release.
* **Agreement Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the agreement.
* **Retailer Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the retailer.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to the retailer.
* **Category** (Required): Enter the category of the agreement.
* **Description** (Required): Enter a brief description of the agreement.
* **Output File** (Required): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Form Id** (Required): Enter the ID of the form.
* **Form Version** (Required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Form Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the form.
* **Retailer Agreement Version** (Optional): Enter the version of the agreement.
* **Retail Address 2** to **Retail Address 8** (Optional): Enter additional address information.
* **Form Ids** (Required): Enter the ID of the form.

Note: The "Retailer" and "Form Id" fields seem to be duplicates, and only one of them is required.
