# Real Estate Deed Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release real estate deeds, which are documents that verify ownership or possession of a property. It is used in administrative contexts for various purposes such as updating records, resolving title disputes, or fulfilling regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the property owner's name in the "Property Owner" field.
2. Provide the property address in the "Property Address" field.
3. Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) from the "Property Type" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of birth of the property owner in the "Date of Birth" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the contact number of the property owner in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter the property owner's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the property in the "Property Description" field.
8. Enter the property value in the "Property Value" field.
9. Select one or more reasons for the removal of the deed from the "Reason for Removal" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the date of the signature in the "Signature Date" field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the property owner as it appears on the deed.
* **Property Address**: Enter the full address of the property, including street number and postal code.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the options provided (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the property owner in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the phone number of the property owner.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the property owner.
* **Property Description**: Provide a brief description of the property, including any relevant details.
* **Property Value**: Enter the value of the property.
* **Reason for Removal**: Select one or more reasons for the removal of the deed from the options provided.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date and time of the signature in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
