# Driver's License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver's License Application Form is a document used to gather essential details about an applicant's driver's license. This form is necessary for the application process to be completed successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your driver's license information, including the license number and expiration date.
3. Enter your address, including your street address and city.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Enter your social security number (if applicable).
6. Enter your driver's license expiration date.
7. Check the box next to "driver's permit info" if you are a permit holder.
8. Choose the status of your vehicle ownership permit.
9. Enter the status of your vehicle ownership.
10. Enter your driver's vehicle permit number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **personal info (personal info, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather your personal details, including your name. Please ensure that this information is accurate and complete.
* **driver license (driver license, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to provide your driver's license number and expiration date. Please enter the correct information to avoid any issues with your application.
* **address (address, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather your address, including your street address and city. Please ensure that this information is accurate and complete.
* **date of birth (date of birth, date, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather your date of birth.
* **social security number (social security number, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather your social security number.
* **expiration date (expiration date, date, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather the expiration date of your driver's license.
* **license number (license number, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather your driver's license number.
* **vehicle info (vehicle info, select multiple, required/optional)**: This field is used to determine if you have a vehicle that requires a permit. Please choose the correct option based on your situation.
* **driver's permit info (driver's permit info, select multiple, required/optional)**: This field is used to determine if you have a driver's permit. Please choose the correct option based on your situation.
* **vehicle ownership permit status (vehicle ownership permit status, select one, required/optional)**: This field is used to determine the status of your vehicle ownership permit.
* **vehicle ownership status (vehicle ownership status, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather the status of your vehicle ownership.
* **driver's vehicle permit number (driver's vehicle permit number, text, required/optional)**: This field is used to gather the permit number of your vehicle.
