# seed_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seed requisition form is a tool used to request seeds from a supplier. It captures essential details about the seed, the seed source, and the user's preferences, ensuring accurate and efficient seed requisitioning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User**: This field is used to identify the person making the seed requisition. 
* **Seed source**: This field is used to identify the source of the seed.
* **Seed type**: Choose the type of seed you are requesting (e.g., 'Yes' for yes, 'No' for no).
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of seeds you are requesting.
* **Unit of measurement**: Select the unit of measurement for the seed (e.g., kg, lbs, g, or oz).
* **Seed description**: This field is used to describe the seed in detail.
* **Seed request date**: Enter the date you are requesting the seed for.
* **Seed request time**: Enter the time you are requesting the seed for.
* **Assigned to**: Check if the seed is to be assigned to a specific person or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the seed requisition.
* **Seed request number**: Enter a unique number for the seed requisition.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the seed requisition.
* **Form submitted**: Check if the form has been submitted.
* **Submission date**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure it is complete and correct.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
