# Worker Equipment Familiarization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from workers to ensure they are familiarized and registered with the equipment they will be using for work. It is crucial for maintaining accurate records, preventing equipment misuse, and ensuring worker safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user's information (page 1).
2. Provide details about the equipment (page 2).
3. Select the relevant safety training for the worker (page 3).
4. Report the usage and maintenance schedule of the equipment (page 4).
5. Complete the training completion form (page 5).
6. Add any notes regarding the equipment (page 6).
7. Enter the user's email address (page 7).
8. Provide the user's phone number (page 8).
9. Assign a tool for the worker to use (page 9).
10. Submit the form by selecting the desired option (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name, ID number, or any other identification number.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment, including its model, serial number, and other relevant specifications.
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_multiple, optional):
  + **Option 1**: Check this box if the worker has received safety training for this equipment.
  + **Option 2**: Check this box if the worker has received safety training, but needs a re-training or refresher.
  + **Option 3**: Check this box if the worker has not received any safety training for this equipment.
* **Equipment Usage** (`equipment_usage`, date, optional): Report the last time the equipment was used or the next scheduled maintenance date.
* **Training Completion** (`training_completion`, time, optional): Record the completion date and time of the safety training.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks about the equipment or the worker.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the user's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the user's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool that has been assigned to this worker.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select the option to submit the form and complete the registration process.
