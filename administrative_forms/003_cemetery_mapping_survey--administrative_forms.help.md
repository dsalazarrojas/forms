# Cemetery Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the condition and status of a cemetery's sections, allowing administrators to track and maintain the cemetery's inventory, infrastructure, and records. This information will aid in making informed decisions on maintenance and resource allocation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct date of the survey completion in the "Survey Date" field.
2. Enter your name and employee or contractor identification number in the "Surveyor Name" and "Surveyor ID Number" fields, respectively.
3. Choose the department or organization conducting the survey from the "Department" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the number of plots in the surveyed section in the "Total Plots in Section" field.
5. Count and enter the number of occupied, vacant, and reserved plots in the "Number of Occupied Plots", "Number of Vacant Plots", and "Number of Reserved Plots" fields.
6. Assess the overall condition of the section by selecting one of the provided options in the "Overall Section Condition" field.
7. Evaluate the condition of the infrastructure (pathways, signage, and utilities) and select the corresponding option in the "Infrastructure Status" field.
8. Indicate the method used to conduct the survey in the "Mapping Method Used" field.
9. (Optional) Enter the number of monuments (headstones, markers, or other markers) documented in the "Number of Monuments Documented" field.
10. (Optional) Select any accessibility features present in the section, such as paved pathways, wheelchair-accessible routes, or seating areas.
11. (Optional) Enter the GPS coordinates of the section's center point in the "GPS Coordinates" field.
12. (Optional) Record any additional survey notes or observations in the "Survey Notes" field.
13. Determine if follow-up is required and select the corresponding option in the "Follow-up Required" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the recommended next survey date for the section in the "Recommended Next Survey Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date**: Enter the date on which the survey was completed.
* **Surveyor Name**: Enter your name as the person conducting the survey.
* **Surveyor ID Number**: Enter your employee or contractor identification number.
* **Department**: Select the department or organization conducting the survey.
* **Total Plots in Section**: Enter the estimated number of burial plots in the surveyed section.
* **Number of Occupied Plots**: Count and enter the number of plots with burials.
* **Number of Vacant Plots**: Count and enter the number of available plots.
* **Number of Reserved Plots**: (Optional) Count and enter the number of pre-purchased but unused plots.
* **Overall Section Condition**: Assess the overall condition of the section, selecting one of the provided options.
* **Infrastructure Status**: Evaluate the condition of the infrastructure and select the corresponding option.
* **Mapping Method Used**: Indicate the method used to conduct the survey.
* **Number of Monuments Documented**: (Optional) Enter the number of monuments (headstones, markers, or other markers) documented.
* **Accessibility Features**: (Optional) Select all accessibility features present in the section.
* **Issues Identified**: (Optional) Select all issues found during the survey.
* **GPS Coordinates**: (Optional) Enter the GPS coordinates of the section's center point.
* **Survey Notes**: (Optional) Record any additional survey notes or observations.
* **Follow-up Required**: Determine if follow-up is required and select the corresponding option.
* **Recommended Next Survey Date**: (Optional) Enter the recommended next survey date for the section.
