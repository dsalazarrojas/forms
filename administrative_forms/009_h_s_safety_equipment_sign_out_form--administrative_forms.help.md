# H₂S Safety Equipment Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and track the signing out of safety equipment within the organization. This includes providing information about the equipment, the user signing it out, and the date and time of sign out.

1. Enter the **equipment ID** (`equipment id`) - This is the unique identifier for the equipment being signed out. Please ensure that you enter the correct ID number.
2. Enter the **User ID** (`user`) - This is the ID number of the user signing out the equipment. Please ensure that you enter the correct ID number.
3. Select the **Sign-out Date** (`sign-out date`) - This is the date the equipment is being signed out. Please use the date format MM/DD/YY.
4. Choose the **Equipment Condition** (`equipment condition`) - This option is used to describe the current state of the equipment. You can select from the options: New, Used, Damaged, or Lost.
5. Provide a brief **Reason for Sign Out** (`reason for sign out`) - This is the description of why the equipment is being signed out. Please keep this field as brief as possible.
6. Enter the **Signature** (`signed by`) - This is the digital signature of the person signing out the equipment.
7. Select the **Sign Out Time** (`sign out by`) - This is the time of day the equipment is being signed out. Please use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
8. Enter the **Equipment Type** (`equipment type`) - This is the type of equipment being signed out (Hand, Electrical, or Other).
9. Enter the **Equipment Model** (`equipment model`) - This is the model number of the equipment being signed out.

20. Enter the **Last Checked Out Time** (`last checked out time`) - This is the time the equipment was last checked out.
21. Enter the **Checked Out Status** (`checked out status`) - This option is used to describe the current status of the equipment (Available, Unavailable, Checked Out, or Lost).
