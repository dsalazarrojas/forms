# Membership Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and complaints from members about their experiences or issues within an organization or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as follows:

1. **User Input**: Enter your name or identifier to be associated with your complaint (this field is not required).
2. **Complaint Details**: Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
3. **Category**: Select one or more categories related to your complaint.
4. **Description**: Enter a brief description of your complaint.
5. **Status**: Choose the status of your complaint as Active, Inactive, or Pending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or identifier to be associated with your complaint.
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your complaint, including as much information as possible.
* **Category** (`complaint_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more categories related to your complaint. These categories might help the organization or community understand the type of issue you're facing.
* **Description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your complaint, which can help clarify the nature of your issue.
* **Status** (`complaint_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your complaint as Active, Inactive, or Pending. This field can help track the progression of your complaint.

## Tips
Remember to be as detailed as possible when filling out **Complaint Details** and **Description**, as this will help the organization or community understand and address your issue more effectively.
