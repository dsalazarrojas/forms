This form is likely to be used by property management or maintenance teams to fulfill homeowner requests.

# Free Home Inspection Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Free Home Inspection Request Form" is a form designed for homeowners to request a free home inspection, which will be performed by the property management or maintenance teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Home Inspection Form**: Type your request and any relevant details in this field. This is where you can enter your request for a free home inspection.
2. **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property that needs inspection. This will help the inspection team to locate the property easily.
3. **Preferred Date**: Choose a date for the inspection, if you have a specific date in mind. If not, you can leave this field blank.
4. **Time of Day**: Select the time of day you would prefer for the inspection.
5. **Inspection Type**: Choose the type of inspection you need. You can select from "Home Inspection", "Maintenance Inspection", or "Other" (if your inspection type is not listed here, please specify in the "Additional Details" field).
6. **Additional Details**: If you have any additional information about your inspection request, please mention it here. This could include any specific areas you would like the inspection team to focus on or any concerns you have.
7. **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so that the inspection team can reach you to schedule the inspection or discuss any questions they may have.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure that the inspection team can fulfill your request efficiently.
