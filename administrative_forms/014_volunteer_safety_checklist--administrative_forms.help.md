# Volunteer Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Safety Checklist is a crucial tool to ensure that volunteers are prepared and equipped to handle emergencies effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Event Details" page and enter the relevant information about the event you are volunteering for.
2. In the "Volunteer Details" page, select the correct contact method for your volunteer information.
3. On the "Safety Protocols" page, ensure you have reviewed and are following the safety protocols outlined.
4. In the "Emergency Procedures" page, fill out the required information, such as providing a first aid kit location.
5. If an incident occurred, report it on the "Incident Report" page, providing details about what happened, the incident's cause, location, and time.
6. On the "First Aid Procedures" page, ensure you are familiar with the first aid procedures and protocols.
7. In the "Emergency Response Plan" page, confirm that you have a response plan in place.
8. On the "Evacuation Plan" page, ensure you are aware of the evacuation plan.
9. If an incident was reported, fill out the relevant information on the "Incident Reporting" page.
10. Confirm that you have emergency equipment available on the "Emergency Equipment" page.
11. On the "Weather Procedures" page, ensure you are aware of the weather procedures.
12. In the "Emergency Contact Info" page, provide contact information for emergency situations.
13. Fill out the "Incident Reported" page if an incident occurred, and select the correct time and date.
14. If an incident occurred, provide a description of what happened on the "Incident Description" page.
15. On the "Incident Cause" page, select the correct cause of the incident.
16. Provide the location of the incident on the "Incident Location" page.
17. If an incident occurred, fill out the time and date on the "Incident Time" and "Incident Date" pages.
18. On the "Incident Reporting Officer" page, provide the name and contact information of the incident reporter.
19. Confirm that safety has been conducted on the "Safety Conducted" page.
20. On the "Training Completed" page, confirm that you have completed the necessary training.
21. Confirm that you have a first aid certificate on the "First Aid Cert" page.
22. Fill out the "Incident Reported" page with the correct time and date.
23. Select the correct safety conducted status on the "Safety Conducted" page.
24. On the "Training Completed" page, confirm that you have completed the necessary training.
