# Local Governance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Governance Feedback Form is a platform for users to provide feedback on local governance matters. This form helps the local administration to understand the issues and concerns of the users and take appropriate actions to address them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the issue you are providing feedback on in the "What issue are you providing feedback on?" field.
2. Provide your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
3. If you want to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. If you want to be contacted about your feedback, provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. You can add any additional comments or information about your feedback in the "Additional comments?" field.
6. Review your feedback before submitting it by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What issue are you providing feedback on?** (Optional): A description of the issue you are providing feedback on, please.
* **Feedback** (Optional): Provide your feedback on the issue you are providing feedback on.
* **Email Address** (Optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your feedback. This will allow the local administration to reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Contact Number** (Optional): Enter your contact number if you want to be contacted about your feedback. This will allow the local administration to reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Additional comments?** (Optional): You can add any additional comments or information about your feedback here.
* **Submit** (Required): Review your feedback and submit it to the local administration for processing.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information about the issue you are providing feedback on.
* Duplicate feedback will not be processed.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact the local administration directly.
