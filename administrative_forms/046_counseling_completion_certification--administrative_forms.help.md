# Counseling Completion Certification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counseling Completion Certification form is used to document the completion of counseling programs or certifications. This form is used to verify the completion of these certifications and to track the progress of students or clients in a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the client or student.
- **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the counselor who supervised or certified the client.
- **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the program or certification completed.
- **Completed Programs** (`completed_programs`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all programs or certifications completed by the client.
- **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the client completed the program.
- **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the client was certified.
- **Certification Number** (`certification_number`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant certification number or ID.
- **Certifying Counselor** (`certifying_counsel`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the certifying counselor.
- **Certification** (`certification`, select_one, required/optional): Select either "Certified" or "Not Certified" to indicate completion status.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the client's certification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Fax** (`fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's fax number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's address.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the certifying counselor's email address.
- **Certifying Counselor Address** (`certifying_counselor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the certifying counselor.
- **Certifying Counselor Phone** (`certifying_counselor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the certifying counselor.
- **Certifying Counselor Email** (`certifying_counselor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the certifying counselor.
- **Certifying Counselor Fax** (`certifying_counselor_fax`, text, required/optional): Enter the fax number of the certifying counselor.
- **Certifying Counselor Address** (`certifying_counselor_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the certifying counselor.
- **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
- **Certifying Counselor Signature** (`certifying_counselor_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the signature of the certifying counselor.
- **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the signature of the client.
- **Certifying Date** (`certifying_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was certified.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.
- Use correct email and phone formatting for email and phone fields.
