# Equipment Readiness Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check the status of equipment in the organization. It's designed to be a simple and efficient way to gather information about the current state of equipment, and to help maintenance staff and equipment managers make informed decisions about maintenance and upkeep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each section, as applicable.
2. For each section, choose the correct status or answer based on the current state of the equipment.
3. Enter any necessary dates or frequencies, if applicable.
4. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checklist Form** (`checklist_form`, text, optional): This is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
* **Checklist Section One** (`checklist_section_one`, select_one, optional): This section is used to check if the equipment is currently available or not. Choose "True" if the equipment is available, or "False" if it's not.
* **Checklist Section Two** (`checklist_section_two`, select_multiple, optional): This section is used to check which maintenance tasks have been completed for the equipment. Choose all that apply.
* **Checklist Section Three** (`checklist_section_three`, date, optional): This section is used to enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Checklist Section Four** (`checklist_section_four`, time, optional): This section is used to enter the time of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, select_one, optional): This section is used to check the status of the equipment. Choose "Available" if it's currently available, or "Not Available" if it's not.
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_multiple, optional): This section is used to check the status of the maintenance tasks. Choose all that apply.
* **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, optional): This section is used to enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Last Maintenance Frequency** (`last_maintenance_frequency`, select_one, optional): This section is used to enter the frequency of the last maintenance performed on the equipment. Choose "Daily", "Weekly", or "Monthly".
