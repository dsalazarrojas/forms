# Disaster Preparadness Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Preparadness Checklist Form is designed to gather information about potential emergency contacts for an individual or group in the case of a disaster or emergency. This form is intended to be used by organizations or individuals who are responsible for emergency preparedness and response, such as emergency management teams or first responders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being collected and why.
2. Identify the emergency contact(s) for the individual or group being assessed.
3. Fill in the emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address.
4. Indicate the relationship between the emergency contact and the individual or group being assessed (e.g., family member, friend, colleague).
5. Select the contact methods that are most likely to be used in case of an emergency (e.g., phone, email, text message).
6. Indicate the frequency of contact with the emergency contact (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly).
7. Identify the location where the emergency contact will be (e.g., home, work, school).
8. Select the contact methods that will be used in case of an emergency (e.g., phone, text message, social media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contact**: This is where you will enter the name of the emergency contact person or group.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person or group.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person or group.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person or group.
* **Insurance**: This field is not currently used in this form.
* **Emergency Plan**: This is a free-form text field where you can provide any additional information about the emergency plan for the individual or group.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This field is not currently used in this form.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the individual or group being assessed.
* **Contact Methods**: Select the contact methods that will be used in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Frequency**: Indicate the frequency of contact with the emergency contact.
* **Contact Location**: Identify the location where the emergency contact will be.
* **Contact Method**: Select the contact methods that will be used in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Method 2-18**: Repeat the same instructions as **Contact Method** for each additional contact method.

Note: The following fields are not currently used in this form and can be skipped: **Insurance**, **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Relationship**.
