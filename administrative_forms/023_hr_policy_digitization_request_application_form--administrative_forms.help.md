# HR Policy Digitization Request Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by HR personnel to collect information for the digitization of HR policies. It is essential to complete this form accurately and submit only one form per policy to ensure the digitization process runs smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and email address as the requestor.
2. Indicate whether the policy is applicable to your department.
3. Select the type of policy (Digital, Non-Digital, or Hybrid).
4. Enter the name of the policy.
5. Choose the start and end dates for the policy.
6. Provide a justification for the digitization request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as the person making the digitization request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check whether the policy is applicable to your department.
* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether the policy is Digital, Non-Digital, or Hybrid.
* **Policy Name** (`policy_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the policy that needs to be digitized.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date for the policy.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date for the policy.
* **Justification** (`justification`, text, required/optional): Provide a clear justification for the digitization request.
