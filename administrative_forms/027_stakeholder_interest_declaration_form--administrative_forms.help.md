# stakeholder_interest_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from stakeholders regarding their interest in participating in [project/initiative], ensuring that all stakeholders have a clear understanding of their interests and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** (`first_name`) - This is your personal name that we will use to address you throughout the project.
2. Enter your **Last Name** (`last_name`) - This is your surname that we will use to identify you.
3. Provide your **Email** (`email`) - This is the email address we will use to contact you for updates and information about the project.
4. Enter your **Phone** (`phone`) - This is the phone number we will use to contact you for urgent matters.
5. In the **Interest Statement** (`interest_statement`) - Describe your interest in participating in the project and what you hope to gain from it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal name that we will use to address you throughout the project.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname that we will use to identify you.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address we will use to contact you for updates and information about the project.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number we will use to contact you for urgent matters.
- **Interest Statement** (`interest_statement`, note, required): Describe your interest in participating in the project and what you hope to gain from it.
