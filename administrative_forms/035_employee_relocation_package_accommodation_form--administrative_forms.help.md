# Employee Relocation Package Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Relocation Package Accommodation Form is used to collect information from employees about their relocation package and accommodation needs. This information will help the company determine the necessary resources and support for the employee's relocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out this form for the correct employee relocation package.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Select the correct travel method from the options provided.
4. Choose only one accommodation method from the options provided.
5. Enter the required dates in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
6. Enter the necessary details about your accommodation.
7. Enter your contact information and the employee's contact information for emergency purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Location
* **Company Location**: Enter the name of the company location you will be working from.
### Job Location
* **Job Location**: Enter the name of the location where you will be working.
### Travel Method
* **Travel Method**: Select all applicable methods of travel you will be using, such as car, bus, or plane.
### Accommodation Method
* **Accommodation Method**: Select only one method of accommodation you will be using, such as airbnb, hotel, or house.
### Travel Date
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date you will be traveling (optional, but recommended if you have a specific travel date).
### Accommodation Date
* **Accmodation Date**: Enter the date you will be accommodated.
### Accommodation Details
* **Accmodation Details**: Enter any additional details about your accommodation.
### Company Phone
* **Company Phone**: Enter the phone number of your company or contact person.
### Employee Phone
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee who will be relocated.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of a contact person for emergency purposes.
### Accommodation Cost
* **Accommodation Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of your accommodation.
### Accommodation Address
* **Accommodation Address**: Enter the address of your accommodation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out this form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact the relevant contact person.
