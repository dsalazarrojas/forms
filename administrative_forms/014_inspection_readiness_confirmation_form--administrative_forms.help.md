# Inspection Readiness Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inspection Readiness Confirmation Form is used to confirm the readiness of a facility for inspection. This form ensures that all necessary safety protocols and procedures are in place, and that the facility is prepared for the upcoming inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name and first and last name of the person completing the form.
2. Select the current facility status (Active or Inactive).
3. Enter the facility address (if applicable).
4. Enter the OSHA 29C 1111 dates and times (if applicable).
5. Enter any comments or notes (if applicable).
6. Sign and date the form.
7. Enter the review date and reviewee's name.
8. Select the facility inspection frequency (Yes or No).
9. Enter the next inspection date.
10. Select the facility inspection type (Yes or No).
11. Select the facility inspection frequency type (Yes or No).
12. Enter the facility inspection safety data.
13. Enter the facility inspection preparedness status (Active or Inactive).
14. Enter any additional comments or notes (if applicable).
15. Sign the form as the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company associated with this facility.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Facility Status** (`facility_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the facility (Active or Inactive).
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, optional): Enter the facility address (if applicable).
* **OSHA 29C 1111** (`osha_29c_1111`, date, optional): Enter the date of the OSHA 29C 1111.
* **OSHA 29C 1111A** (`osha_29c_1111a`, time, optional): Enter the time of the OSHA 29C 1111A.
* **OSHA 29C 1111B** (`osha_29c_1111b`, text, optional): Enter any information related to the OSHA 29C 1111B.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes you have.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your identity.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Facility Inspection Frequency** (`facility_inspection_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the facility is inspected daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Next Inspection Date** (`next_inspection_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the next inspection.
* **Facility Inspection Type** (`facility_inspection_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the facility is inspected daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
* **Facility Inspection Frequency Type** (`facility_inspection_frequency_type`, select_one, required): Select whether the facility is inspected daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
* **Facility Inspection Safety Data** (`facility_inspection_safety_data`, text, required): Enter any safety data related to the facility.
* **Facility Inspection Preparedness Status** (`facility_inspection_preparedness_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the facility inspection preparedness (Active or Inactive).
* **Facility Inspection Preparedness Notes** (`facility_inspection_preparedness_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes (if applicable).
* **Facility Inspection Preparedness Reviewer Signature** (`facility_inspection_preparedness_reviewer_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form as the reviewer.

Note that some fields may require additional information or explanation, so be sure to consult with your facility management or designated representative if unsure.
