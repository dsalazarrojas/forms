# Supplier Delivery Schedule Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Supplier Delivery Schedule Request Form is a tool for suppliers to provide their delivery schedule information. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Supplier Information** (`supplier_info`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's name or company name.
2. **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, date, optional): Select or enter the delivery date.
3. **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, optional): Enter product specifics, such as product name or description.
4. **Notes** (`product_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
5. **Assigned Information** (`assigned_info`, text, optional): Enter any assigned information, such as employee or team name.
6. **Form Options** (`form_options`, text, optional): Enter any custom options or comments.
7. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the supplier's email address.
8. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
9. **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to the task.
10. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool or equipment used.
11. **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the product category.
12. **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
13. **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Specify if you want to output a file for this form submission.
14. **Supplier Info 2** (`supplier_info_2`, text, optional): Enter additional supplier information.
15. **Form Options 2** (`form_options_2`, text, optional): Enter custom options or comments for this supplier.
16. **Product Info 2** (`product_info_2`, text, optional): Enter product specifics for this supplier.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and efficient processing of your delivery schedule request.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that you provide all necessary details for your delivery schedule request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the procurement or supply chain team for assistance.
