# Insurance Claim Review Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reviewing and processing insurance claims. It gathers essential information for making informed decisions regarding claim approval or denial.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have the necessary access and clearance to access and process claims.
2. Fill in the first name and last name fields with the claimant's details.
3. Enter the claimant's date of birth in the date of birth field.
4. Enter the claimant's email address in the email field.
5. Enter the claimant's phone number in the phone field.
6. Enter the claim number in the claim number field.
7. Enter the claim date in the date of the claim.
8. Select the claim status from the drop-down menu.
9. Enter any relevant details about the claim in the claim details field.
10. Enter the policy number if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the claimant's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the claimant's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the claimant's date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email**: Enter the claimant's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **Claim Number**: Enter the number assigned to the claim.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date the claim was submitted.
* **Claim Status**: Select the status of the claim from the drop-down menu:
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
	+ Pending
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the claim (if applicable).
* **Claim Details**: Enter any additional information relevant to the claim in the note field.
