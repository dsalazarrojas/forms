# mining_facility_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mining facility incident form is designed to record and report incidents that occur in mining facilities. It is intended to gather detailed information about the incidents, including location, date and time, reporter, and a detailed description of what happened.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you are filling out the form for an actual incident.
2. Check the facility location where the incident occurred.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident.
4. Identify the person who reported the incident.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Location** (`facility_location`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering the location of the facility where the incident occurred. Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required: false): This field captures the date of the incident. Use the calendar to select the correct date.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is for entering the time of the incident. Ensure that it is accurate and corresponds to the selected date.
* **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, `text`, required: false): This field is for identifying the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing a detailed description of what happened during the incident. Please ensure that the description is accurate and clear.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you provide before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the form is filled out correctly to avoid duplication or incomplete data.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or team leader.
* For any further assistance or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
