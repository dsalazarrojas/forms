# expense_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The expense approval form is used by employees to request reimbursement for approved expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the date of the expense.
2. Enter the name of the employee requesting reimbursement.
3. Select the category of the expense (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
4. Enter the amount of the expense.
5. Provide a description of the expense.
6. Enter the date of the expense (this should match the date of the expense).
7. Select whether the expense has been approved or not.
8. Enter any comments regarding the expense.
9. Choose the current status of the expense (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Expense** (Date): Enter the date when the expense occurred.
* **Employee Name** (Text): Enter the name of the employee requesting reimbursement.
* **Expense Category** (Select One): Select whether the expense is 'Yes' or 'No', but no further explanation is available for this field, as this field seems to be used to indicate if the expense is approved or not.
* **Expense Amount** (Number): Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Expense Description** (Text): Briefly describe the expense.
* **Expense Date** (Date): Enter the date of the expense when it occurred.
* **Approver** (Select One): This field seems to be indicating if the expense is approved or not, but it's unclear without further context. In this context, select 'Yes' if the expense is approved and 'No' otherwise.
* **Approve Date** (Date): Enter the date when the expense was approved.
* **Comments** (Note): Enter any comments or explanations regarding the expense.
* **Approval Status** (Select One): Select the current status of the expense as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
