## Purpose
The "Incident Report Check In Form" is used to report and document incidents that have occurred within the organization. It's a crucial tool for incident management and response, helping to ensure that incidents are properly tracked, documented, and addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form accurately.
2.  Select the incident type from the options provided.
3.  Enter the date and time of the incident.
4.  Fill in the incident location details, including street, city, state, country, and zip.
5.  Provide a detailed description of the incident.
6.  Enter the contact information for the incident reporter.
7.  Review and submit the form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of incident from the options provided.
*   **Incident details** (`incident_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Incident location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Incident location latitude** (`incident_location_latitude`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the latitude of the incident location.
*   **Incident location longitude** (`incident_location_longitude`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the longitude of the incident location.
*   **Incident description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Incident reporter name** (`incident_reporter_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter position** (`incident_reporter_position`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your position as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter phone** (`incident_reporter_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter email** (`incident_reporter_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter department** (`incident_reporter_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter job title** (`incident_reporter_jobtitle`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter work phone** (`incident_reporter_workphone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work phone number as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident reporter work email** (`incident_reporter_work_email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work email as the incident reporter.
*   **Incident location street** (`incident_location_street`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the street of the incident location.
*   **Incident location city** (`incident_location_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the incident location.
*   **Incident location state** (`incident_location_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the incident location.
*   **Incident location country** (`incident_location_country`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country of the incident location.
*   **Incident location zip** (`incident_location_zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the incident location.
*   **Incident location building** (`incident_location_building`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the building of the incident location.
*   **Incident location room** (`incident_location_room`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room of the incident location.

## Tips

*   Before submitting the form, ensure that you have all necessary information.
*   Review and submit the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you're unsure about any information, please ask your supervisor or incident response team for guidance.
*   Do not submit duplicate incident reports.
