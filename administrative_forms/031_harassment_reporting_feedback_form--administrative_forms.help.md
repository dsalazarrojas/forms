# Harassment Reporting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Harassment Reporting Feedback Form is designed to collect information and feedback from users who have experienced or witnessed a harassment incident. This form helps us to better understand the incidents, identify patterns, and take actions to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Incident Date" field with the date the harassment incident occurred.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the name of the reporter in the "Reporter" field.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the person involved in the incident in the "Reported Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date (incident_date)**: Enter the date when the harassment incident took place. This will help us to track the frequency and timing of incidents.
* **Description (description)**: Provide a clear and detailed description of what happened, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Reporter (reporter)**: Please enter your name as the person reporting the incident.
* **Reported Person (reported_person)**: If the incident involved another person, enter their name here.
