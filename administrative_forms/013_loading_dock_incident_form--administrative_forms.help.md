# Loading Dock Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loading Dock Incident Form is a tool used to report and document incidents that occur during the loading and unloading process. This form helps to identify and address any issues that may arise during these critical operations, ensuring a safe and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the "Incident Date" field.
2. Enter the time of the incident in the "Incident Time" field.
3. Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
4. If applicable, select all types of incidents that occurred in the "Incident Type" field.
5. Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident in the "Incident Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): The date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): The time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required/optional): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all types of incidents that occurred, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required/optional): A detailed description of what happened during the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure a thorough investigation.
* If you're unsure of the incident type, refer to the incident description to help identify the correct type.
* If you need to report multiple incidents, fill out a separate form for each incident.
