# Board Meeting Resolution Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document and track board meeting resolutions, gathering the essential information needed for their approval, voting, and implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing a **Resolution Title** that briefly describes the proposed resolution in the text box under "Resolution title". Ensure it is accurate and concise.
2.  Enter the **Full text of proposed resolution** in the "Resolution text" text box, including all necessary details and supporting arguments.
3.  Identify the **Proposer name** by filling in the text box under "Proposer name", indicating the board member who proposed the resolution.
4.  Enter the **Date proposed** in the date format (DD/MM/YYYY) in the field under "Date proposed", marking the date the resolution was proposed.
5.  Cast your **Approval vote** by selecting one of the three options: "Approved", "Rejected", or "Deferred" in the dropdown menu under "Approval vote". Ensure to only select one option.
6.  If desired, add **Approver signatures** in the text box under "Approver signatures", listing the names of board members who approved the resolution.
7.  If applicable, enter the **Effective date** for when the resolution takes effect, using the date format (DD/MM/YYYY) in the field under "Effective date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Resolution Approval --**: This field is a note section where you can include any introductory information related to the resolution, although it's not a mandatory part of the form.
*   **Resolution title**: A text box to enter a short and descriptive title for the proposed resolution. It should be accurate and concise.
*   **Resolution text**: A text box for the full text of the proposed resolution, including all details and supporting arguments. Ensure to provide all necessary information.
*   **Proposer name**: A text box for the name of the board member who proposed the resolution.
*   **Date proposed**: A date field to mark the date the resolution was proposed.
*   **Approval vote**: A dropdown menu with options "Approved", "Rejected", or "Deferred" to record the outcome of the vote.
*   **Approver signatures**: A text box to list the names of board members who approved the resolution.
*   **Effective date**: A date field where you can enter the date when the resolution takes effect, if applicable. 

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly before proceeding.
*   Review and double-check the resolution details before casting your vote or providing your signature.
*   If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, consult with other board members or refer to the organization's governance policies.
*   This form is a template, and any modifications or changes to it should follow standard organizational processes for updates and approval.
