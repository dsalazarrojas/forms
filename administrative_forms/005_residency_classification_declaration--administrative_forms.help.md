# Residency Classification Declaration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to classify the residency status of an individual, likely for the purpose of establishing or renewing their residency or visa application in a specific region or country.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information with you before starting.
2. Fill out the Contact Details page with your current address and contact information.
3. Move on to the Address page and enter your current residential address.
4. On the Purpose of Stay page, select the reason for your stay in the region or country.
5. If required, upload proof of your address on the Proof of Address page.
6. Select your residency status from the options provided on the Residency Status page.
7. Add any additional comments or notes on the Note page if necessary.
8. Finally, enter your email address and phone number on the Email and Phone pages.

* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current residential address.

* **Residency Status** (`residency_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your residency status from the options provided.
