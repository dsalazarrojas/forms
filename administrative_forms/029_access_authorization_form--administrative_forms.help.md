# Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to sensitive areas or systems within the institution. Please fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have permission to access the area or system you are requesting access to.
2. Fill out the form completely, including your personal details and the reason for your request.
3. Check the correct access level and duration for your request.
4. Sign and date the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Staff or Student ID Number** (`staff_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter your institutional ID number to validate your identity.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Provide your official university email address.
* **Department Supervisor or Manager** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the department supervisor or manager who will approve your request.
* **Access Level Requested** (`section_level`, `note`, **required**): Select from the options provided to indicate the access level you need. This field is optional, but please choose a level that accurately reflects your needs.
* **Select areas or systems requiring access** (`access_areas`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Check the areas or systems you need access to. You must choose at least one option.
* **Duration of Access** (`access_duration`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the duration of your requested access. You can choose from the provided options: Permanent (Ongoing Role), Temporary (Project-based), or Visitor (One-day).
* **When Access Should Begin** (`access_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Choose the start date for your requested access period.
* **Expiry Date - if temporary** (`access_expiry_date`, `date`, **required for temporary access**): If you selected Temporary (Project-based) as your access duration, enter the date you expect your access to expire.
* **Reason for requiring access to these sensitive areas or systems** (`justification_for_access`, `text`, **required**): Briefly explain why you need access to the selected areas or systems.
* **Current Security Clearance Level** (`security_clearance_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current security clearance level as per HR records.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`section_confidentiality`, `note`, **required**): This field is optional, but please review and understand the terms of the confidentiality agreement.
* **I agree to maintain confidentiality regarding all data accessed** (`non_disclosure_agreement`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose to acknowledge your agreement with the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Authorized by Signature - Manager or Security Officer** (`authorized_by_signature`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the official authorizing your access request.
* **Date Authorized** (`authorization_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when your access request was approved.

Please submit the form once you have completed all the fields accurately and honestly.
