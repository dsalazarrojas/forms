# Loading And Delivery Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge loading and delivery activities in a warehouse setting. It helps staff keep track of each delivery activity, ensuring accurate and detailed records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Load Date** and **Load Time** if applicable.
2. Enter the **Delivery Date** and **Delivery Time** if applicable.
3. Select the **Delivery Person** responsible for the delivery.
4. Select the appropriate **Goods Document** (if applicable).
5. Add any **Notes** or **Remarks** about the delivery activity.
6. Sign the **Signature** field to confirm the delivery was received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Load Date**: The date when the goods were loaded.
* **Delivery Date**: The date when the goods were delivered.
* **Load Time**: The time when the goods were loaded.
* **Delivery Time**: The time when the goods were delivered.
* **Delivery Person**: The person responsible for delivering the goods.
* **Received By**: The person receiving the goods (if applicable).
* **Goods Document**: A select box to select the relevant goods document (e.g., invoice, receipt).
* **Signature**: A field for the signature of the person acknowledging the delivery.
* **Notes**: Additional notes about the delivery activity.
* **Remarks**: Additional remarks about the delivery activity.
* **Received By**: This field is used to record who received the goods (if the delivery is not direct to the warehouse staff).
* **Delivery Person**: This field is used to record who was responsible for the delivery.
