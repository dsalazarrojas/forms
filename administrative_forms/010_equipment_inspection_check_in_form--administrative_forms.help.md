# equipment_inspection_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment inspection check-in form is used to record the details of an equipment inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of equipment being inspected from the equipment type options.
2. Choose the status of the equipment from the equipment status options.
3. Describe the condition of the equipment.
4. Enter the equipment serial number.
5. Enter the date of purchase.
6. Enter the last maintenance date for the equipment.
7. Enter the next scheduled maintenance date for the equipment.
8. Provide any relevant remarks about the equipment.
9. Select any relevant personnel assigned to the equipment.
10. Select anyone who checked the equipment.
11. Enter the date and time the equipment was checked.
12. Enter the time taken to check the equipment.
13. Enter the location where the equipment was checked.
14. Enter any additional remarks about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **equipment_type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select the type of equipment being inspected.
* **equipment_status** (`equipment_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for choosing the status of the equipment.
* **condition** (`condition`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of the equipment's condition.
* **serial_number** (`serial_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **purchase_date** (`purchase_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
* **last_maintenance_date** (`last_maintenance_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the last scheduled maintenance.
* **next_maintenance_date** (`next_maintenance_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance.
* **remarks** (`remarks`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional remarks about the equipment.
* **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any personnel assigned to the equipment.
* **checked_by** (`checked_by`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the personnel who checked the equipment.
* **checked_date** (`checked_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the equipment was checked.
* **checked_time** (`checked_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the equipment was checked.
* **check_in_time** (`check_in_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the check-in process took.
* **check_out_time** (`check_out_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time the check-out process took.
