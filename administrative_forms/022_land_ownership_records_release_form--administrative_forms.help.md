# Land Ownership Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to release land ownership records for various purposes, such as selling, donating, or transferring land ownership. This form should be completed by the land owner or their authorized representative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the General Information section with your personal details and contact information.
2. Provide authorization for someone else to act on your behalf in the Authorization section, if necessary.
3. Confirm your certification of land ownership in the Certification section, if required.
4. Sign and date the form in the Signature section.
5. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: Enter your name, address, contact phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
  • Required field: Yes
  • Type: Text field with multiple lines
  • Description: Enter your personal details for official communication.
- **Authorization**: If you're not the land owner, enter the name and contact information of the person acting on your behalf.
  • Required field: No (only if not acting on behalf of the owner)
  • Type: Text field with multiple lines
  • Description: Enter the name and contact information of the person authorized to act on your behalf.
- **Certification**: Confirm your certification of land ownership by signing below.
  • Required field: No (only if certification is required)
  • Type: Text field with multiple lines
  • Description: Confirm your certification of land ownership, if required.
- **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the release of land ownership records.
  • Required field: Yes
  • Type: Text field with multiple lines
  • Description: Sign and date to confirm the release of land ownership records.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions for using this form.
  • Required field: Yes
  • Type: Text field with multiple lines
  • Description: Carefully review and agree to the terms and conditions for releasing land ownership records.

## Tips
- Double-check the form before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and understand your responsibilities before completing this form.
- Save a copy of the completed form for your records.
