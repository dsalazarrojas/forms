# Vending Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vending Contract Agreement form is used to collect information for vending machine contracts. This includes details about the contract term length, payment frequency, and other essential contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contract term length from the options provided.
2. Choose the payment frequency from the options listed.
3. Enter the contract term date and start date.
4. Enter the contract end date.
5. Describe the payment terms.
6. Provide a detailed description of the contract responsibilities.
7. Specify the contract duration.
8. Choose the contract locations from the list of options.
9. Enter the name and contact information of the responsible person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contract Term Length (`contract_term_length`, number, required: false)
* This field is used to select the length of the contract term, typically expressed in months or years. 

### Payment Frequency (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required: false)
* This field is used to choose how frequently payments are made, such as weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly.

### Contract Term Date (`contract_term_date`, date, required: false)
* Enter the date when the contract term begins.

### Contract Start Date (`contract_start_date`, date, required: false)
* This field is used to record the date when the contract starts.

### Contract End Date (`contract_end_date`, date, required: false)
* Enter the date when the contract ends.

### Payment Terms (`payment_terms`, text, required: false)
* Describe the payment terms and conditions.

### Payment Terms Description (`payment_terms_description`, text, required: false)
* Provide a detailed description of the payment terms.

### Contract Responsibilities (`contract_responsibilities`, text, required: false)
* List all responsibilities under the contract, including any obligations or tasks.

### Contract Duration (`contract_duration`, text, required: false)
* Enter the length of the contract duration in months.

### Contract Location (`contract_location`, select_multiple, required: false)
* Choose multiple locations where the vending machine will be deployed.

### Contact Person (`contact_person`, text, required: false)
* Enter the name and title of the person responsible for contact and communication.

### Contact Email (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
* Enter the contact person's email address.

### Contact Phone (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
* Enter the contact person's phone number.
