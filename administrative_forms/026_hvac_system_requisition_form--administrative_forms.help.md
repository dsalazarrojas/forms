# HVAC System Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the installation or upgrade of an HVAC system in your building or facility. The information collected in this form will help our team determine the best course of action to fulfill your request.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Requisitioner" field and enter your name and title/ department.
2. Enter the date you are requesting the HVAC system installation or upgrade.
3. Choose the type of heating required for your space.
4. Choose the type of air conditioning required for your space.
5. Enter the total number of rooms where the HVAC system will be installed or upgraded.
6. Enter the square footage per room.
7. Estimate the installation cost of the HVAC system.
8. Estimate the inspection cost of the HVAC system.
9. Estimate the labor cost of the HVAC system installation or upgrade.
10. Estimate any additional material costs associated with the request.
11. Enter any other costs related to the HVAC system installation or upgrade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requisitioner** (1, Number): Enter your name and title/department to ensure that we can contact you directly.
* **Date** (2, Date): Enter the date you are requesting the HVAC system installation or upgrade.
* **Required Heating Type** (3, Select One):
	+ Choose one from the following options:
		- Option 1: Heating is required for all rooms.
		- Option 2: Heating is only required for some rooms.
		- Option 3: No heating is required.
		- Option 4: Other heating requirements.
* **Required Air Conditioning Type** (4, Select One):
	+ Choose one from the following options:
		- Option 1: Air conditioning is required for all rooms.
		- Option 2: Air conditioning is only required for some rooms.
		- Option 3: No air conditioning is required.
		- Option 4: Other air conditioning requirements.
* **Total Rooms** (5, Number): Enter the total number of rooms where the HVAC system will be installed or upgraded.
* **Sqft/Room** (6, Number): Enter the average square footage per room.
* **Installation Cost** (7, Number): Estimate the total installation cost of the HVAC system.
* **Inspection Cost** (8, Number): Estimate the inspection cost associated with the HVAC system installation.
* **Labor Cost** (9, Number): Estimate the labor cost associated with the HVAC system installation or upgrade.
* **Material Cost** (10, Number): Enter any additional material costs associated with the request.
* **Other Costs** (11, Number): Enter any other costs related to the HVAC system installation or upgrade.
