To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Purpose

The Harassment Complaint Absence Form is a tool for reporting and documenting instances of workplace or academic harassment, and for requesting time off for affected employees or students.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Report Incident**: In the first field, provide a brief description of the incident that occurred.
2.  **Request Time Off**: In the second field, select the option that best describes why you are requesting time off.
3.  **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the incident in the third field.
4.  **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the incident in the fourth field.
5.  **Reason**: In the fifth field, select all relevant reasons why the incident occurred.
6.  **Witness List**: In the sixth field, select all witnesses who have knowledge about the incident.
7.  **Description**: In the seventh field, provide a detailed description of what happened.
8.  **Resolution**: In the eighth field, select the current status of the resolution of the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Incident** (`report_incident`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the incident that occurred. Please be as detailed as possible.
*   **Request Time Off** (`request_time_off`, select_one, required/optional): This field allows you to select the reason for your time off request. You can choose from the following options: Incident Date, Incident Time, or Reason.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day when the incident occurred.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, select_multiple, required): This field requires you to select all relevant reasons why the incident occurred. You can choose from: Incident type, Description, or Date.
*   **Witness List** (`witness_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select all witnesses who have knowledge about the incident.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a detailed description of what happened.
*   **Resolution** (`resolution`, select_one, required): This field requires you to select the current status of the resolution of the incident. You can choose from: Completed or Pending.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
*   Ensure that you select all relevant reasons for your time off request and incident description.
*   Make sure to list all witnesses who have knowledge about the incident.
*   Select the correct status of the resolution of the incident.
