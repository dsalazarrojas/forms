# Client Case Progress Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to track the progress of a client's case, providing a structured format for gathering and documenting essential information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information and resources available.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") accurately and thoroughly.
4. For optional fields, only enter information relevant to your case.
5. Save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, required): Enter the client's legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, required): Enter the client's legal last name.
* **Client ID Number** (`client_id_number`, text, required): Enter the internal client identifier.
* **Case Manager Name** (`case_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned case manager.
* **Case Open Date** (`case_open_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the case was opened.
* **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_one, required): Select the category of the case (e.g., Legal Services, Social Services, etc.).
* **Current Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, required): Select the active status of the case (e.g., Open, In Progress, etc.).
* **Last Contact Date** (`last_contact_date`, date, required): Enter the most recent client contact date.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Select the method used to contact the client (e.g., In Person, Phone Call, etc.).
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, required): Enter a summary of the progress since the last review.
* **Goals Achieved This Period** (`goals_achieved`, text, optional): Describe what has been accomplished.
* **Ongoing Goals** (`ongoing_goals`, text, required): Enter the goals still being worked on.
* **Barriers or Challenges** (`barriers_challenges`, text, optional): Identify any obstacles affecting progress.
* **Services Provided** (`services_provided`, select_multiple, required): Select all services provided to the client (e.g., Case Management, Counseling, etc.).
* **Referrals Made** (`referrals_made`, text, optional): Enter any external referrals.
* **Next Appointment Date** (`next_appointment_date`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, required): Enter action items for the next period.
* **Client Engagement Level** (`client_engagement_level`, select_one, required): Assess the client's engagement level (e.g., Very Engaged, Not Engaged, etc.).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required): Evaluate the current risk assessment (e.g., Low Risk, High Risk, etc.).
* **Is Supervisor Review Needed?** (`supervisor_review_needed`, select_one, required): Determine if a supervisor review is required.
* **Tracking Entry Date** (`tracking_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this entry.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by case workers, case managers, and other support staff to track client progress and make informed decisions. Please ensure you have the necessary information and resources available before completing the form.
