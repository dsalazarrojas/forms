# Coach Appointment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach Appointment Authorization Form is a necessary document for clients to provide information about their coaching preferences, authorization for services, and agreement to the coaching business's policies. This form ensures that you, as a client, understand and agree to the terms and conditions of our coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal information, such as your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Specify your coaching preferences, including the type of coaching you need, your preferred appointment date and time, session format, and session duration.
3. Indicate whether this is your first session or if you have had coaching before. If you have had coaching before, please provide the name of your previous coach.
4. Share your reasons for seeking coaching and your primary goals for the sessions.
5. List any relevant medical conditions or medications you are taking.
6. Authorize coaching services, agree to the privacy policy, understand the cancellation policy, and authorize payment for services.
7. Specify how you would like to be reminded about appointments and who to contact in case of an emergency.
8. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of our coaching services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (1): Your legal full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address** (2): Your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (4): For identification purposes only, please enter your date of birth.
* **Coach Name** (5): The name of the coach you would like to see.
* **Coaching Service Type** (6): Specify the type of coaching you need (e.g. Health, Wellness, Nutrition, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (7): Enter your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time** (8): Select your preferred time for the appointment (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend).
* **Session Format** (9): How you would like to meet with your coach (In Person, Video Conference, Phone Call, No Preference).
* **Session Duration** (10): Choose the duration of your coaching session (30, 45, 60, 90 minutes).
* **Is This Your First Session** (11): Yes/No, have you had coaching before.
* **Previous Coach Name** (12): If you have had coaching before, please enter the name of your previous coach.
* **Reason for Seeking Coaching** (13): Why you are seeking coaching services (e.g. personal growth, career development, etc.).
* **Primary Goals** (14): What you want to achieve through coaching.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (15): Any medical conditions your coach should know about.
* **Current Medications** (16): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **I Authorize Coaching Services** (17): Confirm that you understand and agree to the coaching services.
* **Privacy Policy Accepted** (18): Agree to our privacy policy.
* **Cancellation Policy Understood** (19): Understand and agree to our cancellation policy.
* **Payment Method Authorization** (20): Confirm that you authorize payment for services.
* **Appointment Reminder Preference** (21): How you would like to be reminded about appointments (Email Only, Text Only, Email and Text, Phone Call, No Reminders).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (22): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (23): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (24): How you found out about our coaching services (e.g. Online Search, Social Media, Referral, etc.).
* **Date of Authorization** (25): Date and timestamp of your authorization of these services.

This help guide is designed to ensure that you, as a client, understand the purpose and requirements of the Coach Appointment Authorization Form. By following these steps and filling out the form accurately, you will be able to take the first step towards receiving coaching services and achieving your personal goals.
