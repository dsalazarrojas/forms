# hvc_system_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HVC System Inspection Form is used to gather information about a system's condition and performance. It is designed to be completed by a maintenance team to inspect and evaluate a system's functionality during a service visit. This form helps to ensure accuracy and consistency in data collection, making it easier for maintenance teams to identify potential issues and schedule follow-up services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the system's details and select the relevant information.
2. Choose the service type that best describes the purpose of the inspection.
3. Enter the location where the inspection was performed.
4. Select the date of the inspection from the calendar.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the system's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **System Details** (`system_details`, text, optional): This field is where you enter a brief description of the system, including its type and any relevant details about its installation or usage.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select the type of service performed on the system, such as HVAC Service Check, Maintenance Check, or Repair. This helps the maintenance team understand the scope of the inspection.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the address or location where the inspection was performed.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, date, optional): Select the date when the inspection was performed from the calendar.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the system's condition, such as any issues or observations that were noted during the inspection.
