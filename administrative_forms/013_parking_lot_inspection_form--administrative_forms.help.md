# Parking Lot Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help inspectors thoroughly report on the condition and status of a parking lot. It is intended to be used for inspections and reports, and not for general parking lot information or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Inspection Date** for the parking lot inspection in the first field. This is the date the inspection was conducted.
2. In the **Lot Number** field, enter the unique identifier for the parking lot being inspected.
3. In the **Lot Location** field, select the correct option from the dropdown list to indicate the location of the parking lot (e.g., Park A, Park B, Park C).
4. In the **Lot Condition** field, select all that apply from the options to describe the overall condition of the parking lot (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
5. In the **Lot Status** field, select the correct option to indicate the status of the parking lot (e.g., Open, Closed, Restricted).
6. In the **Lot Inspector** field, enter the name and contact information of the inspector performing the inspection.
7. In the **Lot Comments** field, add any notes or observations made during the inspection.
8. Enter the **Lot Parking Capacity** to record the total parking capacity of the lot.
9. Enter the **Lot Parking Spaces** to record the total number of parking spaces available in the lot.
10. Enter the **Lot Parking Spaces Available** to record the number of parking spaces available at the time of inspection.
11. Enter the **Lot Spaces Reserved** to record the number of parking spaces reserved at the time of inspection.
12. Enter the **Lot Spaces Available** to record the number of parking spaces available at the time of inspection.
13. Enter the **Lot Spaces Reserved Percent** to record the percentage of parking spaces reserved.
14. Enter the **Lot Spaces Available Percent** to record the percentage of parking spaces available.
15. Enter the **Lot Spaces Utilization** to record the percentage of parking spaces being used.
16. Enter the **Lot Parking Capacity Percent** to record the percentage of total parking capacity being used.
17. Enter the **Lot Parking Spaces Percent** to record the percentage of total parking spaces being used.
18. In the **Lot Status Comments** field, add any additional comments or notes about the status of the parking lot.
19. In the **Lot Status Notes** field, add any additional notes or comments about the status of the parking lot.
