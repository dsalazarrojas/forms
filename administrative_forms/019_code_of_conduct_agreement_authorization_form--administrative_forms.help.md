# Code Of Conduct Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization and agreement from participants to follow a Code of Conduct for an event or program. It's essential to understand that the Code of Conduct outlines expected behavior and rules for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Participant Information section with your details.
2. In the Code of Conduct Acknowledgement section, select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the Code of Conduct.
3. In the Consequences Understanding section, select "Yes" to indicate that you understand the consequences of not following the Code of Conduct.
4. In the Voluntary Participation section, select "Yes" to indicate that you are signing this agreement voluntarily.
5. If you are under 18, you must have a parent or guardian provide consent. If so, fill out the Parent or Guardian consent section.
6. Finally, select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the Code of Conduct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Fill out your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Organization or Program** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the group or event you are participating in.
* **Role or Position** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role or position (Participant, Volunteer, Staff Member, Leader, Student, or Other).
* **I have read and understand the Code of Conduct** (`read_code`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you have read and understand the Code of Conduct.
* **I agree to treat all persons with respect and dignity** (`agree_respect`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to treat all persons with respect and dignity.
* **I agree not to engage in harassment or discrimination** (`agree_no_harassment`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree not to engage in harassment or discrimination.
* **I agree not to engage in violence or threats** (`agree_no_violence`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree not to engage in violence or threats.
* **I agree to follow all rules and procedures** (`agree_follow_rules`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to follow all rules and procedures.
* **I agree not to use illegal substances** (`agree_no_substance`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree not to use illegal substances.
* **I agree to maintain a safe environment** (`agree_safe_environment`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to maintain a safe environment.
* **I agree to report violations of this Code** (`agree_report`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to agree to report violations of the Code.
* **I understand that violations may result in removal or disciplinary action** (`understand_consequences`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to understand that violations may result in removal or disciplinary action.
* **I am signing this agreement voluntarily** (`voluntary`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are signing this agreement voluntarily.
* **Do you have questions about the Code of Conduct?** (`have_questions`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have questions about the Code of Conduct.
* **Parent or Guardian consent needed** (`parent_guardian_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you are under 18, select "Yes" to indicate that you need parent or guardian consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): If you selected "Yes" to the previous question, fill out the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Consent Method** (`parent_signature_method`, `select_one`, required): If you selected "Yes" to the previous question, select how the parent or guardian will provide consent (Digital Signature, Email Confirmation, Paper Signature, or Phone Call).
* **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand each question before answering.
- If you have questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
- This form is a legal document and is intended to be a record of your agreement to follow the Code of Conduct. Please sign it carefully and accurately.
