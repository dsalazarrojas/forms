# Daily Performance Goals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to set and review their daily performance goals, providing a platform to track progress and reflect on accomplishments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the form and click "Start" to begin.
2. Select "Page 1" and enter your daily performance goals. You can enter text-based goals, such as specific tasks or projects you want to accomplish.
3. Click "Next" to proceed to "Page 2".
4. On "Page 2", you will be asked to enter a numerical value to represent your daily progress. This can be a percentage or a number representing the amount completed.
5. Click "Submit" to save your progress and proceed to "Page 3", where you can add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`page_name_1`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your daily performance goals for the day. These can be specific tasks or projects you want to complete.
* **Page 2** (`page_name_2`, number, required):
	+ Enter a numerical value to represent your daily progress. For example, if you completed 75% of your task, enter 75.
* **Page 3** (`page_name_3`, note, optional):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your progress. This is an open-ended field for you to reflect on your accomplishments.

## Tips
* Be specific and concrete when entering your daily performance goals on "Page 1".
* Ensure you complete and submit "Page 2" to track your progress accurately.
* Use "Page 3" to reflect on your progress and identify areas for improvement.
