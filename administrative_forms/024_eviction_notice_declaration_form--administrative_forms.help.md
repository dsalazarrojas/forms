# eviction_notice_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a tenant's eviction notice, which is a formal document used to inform a tenant that they are being evicted. Please ensure that you have the necessary legal authority and permissions before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as needed. Some fields are optional, so only fill them out if required.
2. Enter the tenant's name in the `Tenant Name` field.
3. Select the reason for the eviction in the `reason_for_eviction` field. If the reason is not listed, select "Other" and provide an explanation.
4. Enter the date of the first notification sent to the tenant in the `date_of_first_notification` field.
5. Enter the date of the last notification sent to the tenant in the `date_of_last_notification` field.
6. If applicable, provide the reason for the non-compliance in the `reason_for_non_compliance` field.
7. Enter the name of the contact person for the tenant in the `tenant_contact_name` field.
8. Enter the name of the contact person for the landlord in the `landlord_contact_name` field.
9. Enter the tenant's phone number in the `tenant_phone` field.
10. Enter the tenant's email address in the `tenant_email` field.
11. Enter the landlord's phone number in the `landlord_phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant being evicted.
* **reason_for_eviction**: Select the reason for the eviction from the list or enter "Other" and provide an explanation.
* **date_of_first_notification**: Enter the date of the first notification sent to the tenant.
* **date_of_last_notification**: Enter the date of the last notification sent to the tenant.
* **reason_for_non_compliance**: Enter the reason the tenant failed to comply with the lease agreement (optional).
* **tenant_contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the tenant.
* **landlord_contact_name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the landlord.
* **tenant_phone**: Enter the tenant's phone number.
* **tenant_email**: Enter the tenant's email address.
* **landlord_phone**: Enter the landlord's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary legal authority and permissions to complete this form.
* Verify that all information entered is accurate and complete.
* This form is intended for administrative use only and not for external communication.
