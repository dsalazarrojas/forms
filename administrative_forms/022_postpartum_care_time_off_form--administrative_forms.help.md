# Postpartum Care Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to request time off for postpartum care, allowing employees to take care of their health and well-being after childbirth. This form is designed to be completed by employees who need to take time off for postpartum care, providing essential information for their manager or HR representative.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Fill out the form completely**: Enter your employee name and dates of absence in the required fields.
2.  **Choose the correct dates**: Select the start and end dates for your postpartum care time off in the corresponding fields.
3.  **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, submit it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee records.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): The date you would like to start your postpartum care time off.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): The date you would like to end your postpartum care time off.
