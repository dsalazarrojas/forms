# emergency_drill_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "emergency_drill_report_form" is a form designed to record information about emergency drill events, including the drill's status, category, location, and outcomes. This form is meant to facilitate the review and evaluation of emergency drills to ensure they are conducted efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (First Name, Last Name) in the first two fields.
2. Answer the question about whether the emergency drill took place (Emergency Drill).
3. Provide any comments or observations about the drill (Comments).
4. Specify the outcome of the drill (Status).
5. Select whether the drill was performed with specific tools or equipment (Assigned Tool).
6. If applicable, specify the category of the drill (Category).
7. Choose the team that was assigned to perform the drill (Assigned Team).
8. If necessary, provide the date of the drill (Date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name to identify yourself as the reporter of this emergency drill report.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name to identify yourself as the reporter of this emergency drill report.
* **Emergency Drill** (select one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the emergency drill took place, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the emergency drill event.
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Add any comments or observations about the drill here.
* **Observations** (text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about the drill.
* **Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date of the emergency drill event, if required.
* **Assigned Tool** (select one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if specific tools were assigned for the drill, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Category** (text, required/optional): Enter the category of the drill.
* **Assigned Team** (select multiple, required/optional): Choose the team assigned to perform the drill.
* **Status** (select one, required/optional): Select the status of the drill (Active or Inactive).
