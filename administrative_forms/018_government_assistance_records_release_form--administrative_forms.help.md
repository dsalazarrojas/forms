# government_assistance_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The government assistance records release form is a mandatory document used to release government assistance records. This form is designed to collect information about the organization seeking release of records and the type of assistance being applied for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are applying for government assistance and not for any other type of application or renewal.
3. Double-check that you are not duplicating any existing records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Government Assistance Records Release Form** (form_title): A brief description of the form.
* **Category** (category): Select the category that best describes the type of assistance you are applying for. (Administrative Forms, Other)
* **Description** (description): A brief description of the records being released. (Not required, but recommended)
* **Form Title 2** (form_title_2): The title of the form being released. (Required)
* **Is this a new or renewal application?** (form_title_3): Select 'New' if this is the first time you are applying for assistance, 'Renewal' if you are reapplying for assistance.
* **What type of assistance is being applied for?** (form_title_4): Select the type of assistance you are applying for. (General Assistance, Other)
* **Government Agency/Office** (form_title_5): Select the government agency or office responsible for the assistance. (Department of Education, Department of Health and Human Services, Other)
* **Date** (form_title_6): The date of the assistance application. (Required)
* **Time** (form_title_7): The time of the assistance application. (Not required)
* **Contact Information** (form_title_8): The contact information of the organization seeking assistance. (Required)
* **Organization's Address** (form_title_9): The address of the organization seeking assistance. (Required)
* **Organization's Contact Person's Name** (form_title_10): The name of the contact person for the organization. (Required)
* **Organization's Contact Person's Email** (form_title_11): The email address of the contact person. (Required)
* **Organization's Contact Person's Phone** (form_title_12): The phone number of the contact person. (Required)
* **Form Title 13** (form_title_13): Another field for email. (Required)
* **Is this a minor's record?** (form_title_14): Check if the record being released is for a minor. ('True', 'False')
* **Parent/Guardian's Name** (form_title_15): The name of the parent or guardian. (Required)
* **Parent/Guardian's Phone** (form_title_16): The phone number of the parent or guardian. (Required)
* **Parent/Guardian's Email** (form_title_17): The email of the parent or guardian. (Required)
* **Date of Birth** (form_title_18): The date of birth of the minor. (Required)
* **Age of Minor** (form_title_19): The age of the minor. (Required)
* **Signature** (form_title_20): A digital signature of the contact person. (Required)
* **Organization's Signature** (form_title_21): A digital signature of the organization. (Required)
* **Form Title 22** (form_title_22): Another field for date. (Not required)
* **Form Title 23** (form_title_23): Another field for time. (Not required)

## Tips

1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Double-check that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant government agency or office.
