This guide is intended for end-users who need to complete the "Lease Deposit Amendment Form" to make changes to a lease agreement. Before proceeding, please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and approvals to initiate this process. This form should not be used in cases where the lease has already been terminated or where the tenant has vacated the premises. 

# Lease Deposit Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lease Deposit Amendment Form" is used to make changes to an existing lease agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form allows for adjustments to be made to the deposit amount, lease terms, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before signing.
4. Obtain the signatures of both the landlord and the tenant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person leasing the property.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Amendment Date** (`amendment_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the amendment is being made.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the change being made to the lease agreement.
* **Change to Deposit Amount** (`change_to_deposit_amount`, number, required): Enter the new deposit amount, if applicable.
* **New Deposit Amount** (`new_deposit_amount`, number, required): Enter the updated deposit amount.
* **Updated Lease Terms** (`updated_lease_terms`, text, required): Describe the new lease terms and conditions.
* **Signed by Landlord** (`signed_by_landlord`, text, required): Obtain the signature of the landlord confirming the changes.
* **Signed by Tenant** (`signed_by_tenant`, text, required): Obtain the signature of the tenant confirming the changes.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Record the date the changes were signed.
 
## Tips
* Always keep a record of the original lease agreement and amendments for future reference.
* Ensure that both parties review and agree to the changes before signing.
* This form should be used for making changes to an existing lease agreement, and not for initial lease signing or termination.
