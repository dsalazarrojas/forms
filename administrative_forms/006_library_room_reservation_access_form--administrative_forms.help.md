# Library Room Reservation Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a library room for a specific period. It allows you to provide details about the date, time, and room preferences for your reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Date" field to choose the start date of your reservation.
2. Select the "End Date" field to choose the end date of your reservation.
3. Choose the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields to select the time range of your reservation.
4. Select the room type from the "Room Type" dropdown menu:
	* "Meeting Room" for meetings or presentations
	* "Study Room" for quiet work or study
	* "Other" for other purposes
5. Enter the room number where you would like to be reserved.
6. Choose your preferred floor from the "Preferred Room" dropdown menu:
	* "1st Floor"
	* "2nd Floor"
	* "3rd Floor"
7. Select the duration of your reservation from the "Reservation Duration" dropdown menu:
	* "Half-day"
	* "Full-day"
	* "Multiple days"
8. Add any additional notes about your reservation in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Date (date)** (required: false): Enter the start date of your reservation.
- **End Date (date)** (required: false): Enter the end date of your reservation.
- **Start Time (time)** (required: false): Choose the start time of your reservation.
- **End Time (time)** (required: false): Choose the end time of your reservation.
- **Room Type (select_one)** (required: false): Select the type of room you need for your reservation.
- **Room Number (number)** (required: false): Enter the room number where you would like to be reserved.
- **Preferred Room (select_multiple)** (required: false): Choose your preferred floor for the room.
- **Reservation Duration (select_multiple)** (required: false): Select the duration of your reservation.
- **Notes (note)** (required: false): Add any additional information about your reservation.
- **Contact Name (text)** (required: false): Enter your name.
- **Contact Email (email)** (required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Phone (text)** (required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Submit (text)** (required: false): Submit your reservation form.
