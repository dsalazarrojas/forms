# Draft Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and validate draft content before it is published. It ensures that all necessary information is included and correct, and tracks the review status of each draft.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the draft content and make sure it is complete and accurate.
2. Fill in the Review Questions field with any questions or concerns that need to be addressed.
3. In the Checklist field, list specific items that need to be reviewed.
4. Enter the date of the draft review in the Date field.
5. Choose the reviewer from the Reviewer field.
6. Select the status of the draft from the Status field.
7. Choose the category of the draft from the Category field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Note field.
