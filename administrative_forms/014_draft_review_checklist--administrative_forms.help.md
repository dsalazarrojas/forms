# Draft Review Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and validate draft content before it is published. It ensures that all necessary information is included and correct, and tracks the review status of each draft.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the draft content and make sure it is complete and accurate.
2. Fill in the Review Questions field with any questions or concerns that need to be addressed.
3. In the Checklist field, list specific items that need to be reviewed.
4. Enter the date of the draft review in the Date field.
5. Choose the reviewer from the Reviewer field.
6. Select the status of the draft from the Status field.
7. Choose the category of the draft from the Category field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Draft Review Checklist (draft_review_checklist_page)
*   **Draft Review Checklist** (text): Enter any comments or feedback on the draft content.

*   **Review Questions** (text): List any questions or concerns that need to be addressed.

*   **Checklist** (text): List specific items that need to be reviewed.

*   **Date** (date): Enter the date of the draft review.

*   **Draft Review Date** (date): Enter the date of the draft review.

*   **Email** (email): Enter the contact information of the reviewer.

*   **Phone** (text): Enter the contact information of the reviewer.

*   **Note** (note): Enter any additional comments or notes from the reviewer.

*   **Reviewer** (select_one): Choose the reviewer from the list of options.

*   **Status** (select_one): Choose the status of the draft (e.g. Draft, Published).

*   **Category** (select_one): Choose the category of the draft (e.g. Administrative Forms, General Forms).

*   **Time** (time): Enter the time of the draft review.

*   **Reviewer** (text): Enter the name of the reviewer.

*   **Status** (text): Enter the status of the draft.

*   **Note** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes from the reviewer.

*   **Page Number** (number): Enter the page number of the draft.

*   **Published** (text): Enter the published status of the draft.
