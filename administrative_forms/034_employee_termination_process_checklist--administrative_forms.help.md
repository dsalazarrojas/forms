# employee_termination_process_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_termination_process_checklist form is used to document and track the employee termination process. It provides a structured way to collect relevant information and status updates throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name and Employee ID in the respective fields.
2. Select the Termination Type (voluntary or involuntary).
3. Enter the Effective Date and End Date of the termination.
4. Provide the Reason for Voluntary Termination, if applicable.
5. Select the Termination Process Status (pending, in progress, or completed).
6. Enter any Comments or additional information, if necessary.
7. Enter the Supervisor Name and Email, if applicable.
8. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being terminated.
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Effective Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the termination takes effect.
* **End Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the termination is finalized.
* **Termination Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of termination (voluntary or involuntary).
* **Reason for Voluntary Termination** (text, optional): Enter the reason for the voluntary termination.
* **Reason for Involuntary Termination** (text, optional): Enter the reason for the involuntary termination (if applicable).
* **Other Reason for Involuntary Termination** (text, optional): Enter any other reason for involuntary termination (if applicable).
* **Other Reason for Involuntary Termination Hint** (text, optional): Enter any additional information or context for the other reason for involuntary termination (if applicable).
* **Termination Process Status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of the termination process (pending, in progress, or completed).
* **Comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the termination.
* **Supervisor Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor involved in the termination process.
* **Supervisor Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the supervisor involved in the termination process.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields, if applicable.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your HR policies or procedures for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for review and approval.
