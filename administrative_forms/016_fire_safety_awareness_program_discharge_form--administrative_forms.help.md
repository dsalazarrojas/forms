# Fire Safety Awareness Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Awareness Program Discharge Form is used to track when an employee or contractor has completed the fire safety awareness program and understands the essential protocols and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have completed the fire safety awareness program.
2. Review the provided fire safety guidelines and protocols.
3. Answer the questions on this form honestly and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fire Safety Awareness Discharge Number** (`program_discharge`, Number, required/optional): Enter the discharge number you received after completing the fire safety awareness program.
- **Participant Acknowledgment** (`participant_acknowledgment`, Text, required/optional): Write a brief acknowledgment that you have attended the program and understand the fire safety guidelines and protocols.
- **Safety Protocols Understood** (`safety_protocols_understood`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Select all safety protocols you have understood during the program. Choose from:
  - Fire Safety Procedures
  - Evacuation Routes
  - Fire Extinguisher Locations
  - Emergency Contact Information
- **No further fields, as this form has no more fields.**

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have completed the fire safety awareness program before filling out this form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns about the fire safety guidelines or protocols, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Double-check that you have selected all the correct safety protocols that you have understood during the program.
