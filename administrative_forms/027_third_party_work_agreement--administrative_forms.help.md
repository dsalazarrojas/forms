# third_party_work_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The third-party work agreement form is used to collect information from third-party workers or vendors about their work agreements. This information is essential for administrative tasks, such as tracking, reporting, and managing third-party relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of work being agreed upon (Option 1 or Option 2).
2. Briefly describe the services being provided in the service description field.
3. Check the terms and conditions that apply to the agreement (Option 1 or Option 2).
4. Specify the payment terms (if applicable).
5. Enter the start and end dates of the agreement (if applicable).
6. Provide your contact information (email address).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Work Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, not required):
	+ This field allows you to choose the type of work being agreed upon. Select the option that best describes the type of work you are providing.
* **Describe Services** (`service_description`, text, not required):
	+ Enter a brief description of the services you will be providing. This should give the administrative team an understanding of what services you will be offering.
* **Accept Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ This field allows you to check the terms and conditions that apply to the agreement. Select all that apply to your services.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, number, not required):
	+ Enter the payment details for your services. If applicable.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the start date of the agreement. If applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the end date of the agreement. If applicable.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
