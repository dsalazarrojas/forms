# Event Budget Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Budget Checklist Form is a tool to help event organizers plan and track their event's budget. It's used to collect estimates and actual expenses for various categories, such as venue, catering, entertainment, and marketing expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct category for each expense type.
2. Enter the estimated cost for each category.
3. Enter the actual cost for each category, if applicable.
4. Use the "Other Expenses" category for any unexpected or miscellaneous expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Venue Details (optional)
**Venue Details** (`venue_details`, text, optional)
Enter the details about the venue you're planning to use for the event.

### 2. Catering Expenses (optional)
**Catering Expenses** (`catering_expenses`, number, optional)
Enter the estimated and actual costs for catering expenses, such as food and beverage services.

### 3. Entertainment Costs (optional)
**Entertainment Costs** (`entertainment_costs`, number, optional)
Enter the estimated and actual costs for entertainment expenses, such as music and decorations.

### 4. Marketing Expenses (optional)
**Marketing Expenses** (`marketing_expenses`, number, optional)
Enter the estimated and actual costs for marketing expenses, such as advertising and promotion.

### 5. Other Expenses (optional)
**Other Expenses** (`other_expenses`, text, optional)
Enter any other expenses not included in the previous categories, such as travel costs or equipment rentals.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about a category, use the "Other Expenses" field for clarity.
- Save your progress as you go, so you can easily retrieve and edit your form later.
