# Vendor Payment Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Payment Monitoring Form is used to track the status of payments made to vendors. It helps the user to quickly and efficiently monitor vendor payments and track any issues that may arise during the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the payment data for the vendor, including payment status, date, and amount.
2. Select the correct payment status from the options provided.
3. Enter the payment date in the required format (yyyy-mm-dd).
4. Enter the amount paid in the required currency.
5. Answer the questions about the vendor's status and address.
6. Enter the vendor's phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the correct payment status for the vendor from the options provided.
* **Payment Date** (`Payment Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date on which payment was made to the vendor.
* **Paid Amount** (`Paid Amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount paid to the vendor in the required currency.
* **Vendor** (`Vendor`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer yes or no to whether or not the vendor has been paid.
* **Vendor Address** (`Vendor Address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (`Vendor Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Vendor Email** (`Vendor Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the vendor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data.
* Double-check the payment status and date to ensure accuracy.
* Enter the amount paid in the correct currency.
* Keep the vendor's information up-to-date and accurate for future reference.
