# Disciplinary Record Affirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Record Affirmation form is used to document and record disciplinary actions or incidents in a workplace setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fields to understand what information is required.
2. Enter the incident date, which is the date the incident occurred.
3. Provide the employee's name and any other relevant information.
4. Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
5. Specify the disciplinary action taken as a result of the incident.
6. List any witnesses to the incident.
7. Select the reporting officer who documented the incident.
8. Record the date of the incident (if different from the incident date).
9. Choose the type of incident (e.g., accident, misconduct, etc.).
10. Choose the status of the incident (e.g., pending, resolved, etc.).
10. Enter the follow-up date, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text): Enter a brief description of the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Disciplinary Action** (`disciplinary_action`, text): Specify the disciplinary action taken as a result of the incident.
* **Witness List** (`witness_list`, select_multiple): List any witnesses to the incident. Select multiple options as necessary.
* **Reporting Officer** (`reporting_officer`, text): Enter the name of the reporting officer who documented the incident.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date): Enter the date the incident occurred (if different from the incident date).
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one): Choose the type of incident (e.g., accident, misconduct, etc.).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_one): Choose the status of the incident (e.g., pending, resolved, etc.).
* **Follow-up Date** (`followup_date`, date): Enter the date of the next follow-up or review.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If an incident has multiple dates, enter the most relevant date.
* For witness lists, select multiple options as necessary to provide a complete picture of the incident.
* This form is a template and should be reviewed and updated regularly to reflect changing workplace policies and procedures.
