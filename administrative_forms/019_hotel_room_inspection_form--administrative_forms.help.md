# Hotel Room Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to aid hotel staff in conducting regular room inspections to identify maintenance issues and ensure that rooms are well-maintained for guests. The form helps to document any problems or concerns and suggests potential maintenance actions to address them.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply answer the questions and fields as thoroughly and accurately as possible. You can start by filling out the **General Information** and **Room Inspection** sections, which cover basic details about the room and any initial findings. Next, move on to the **Maintenance Issues** section to report any problems you've encountered. In the **Maintenance Actions** section, suggest potential solutions or actions to be taken. Finally, include any **Comments** you'd like to add for additional context or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is intended for you to enter general details about the room being inspected, such as its number and location within the hotel.
* **Room Inspection**: Use this field to describe any initial findings or observations about the room's condition.
* **Maintenance Issues**: List any maintenance problems you've detected during the inspection.
* **Maintenance Actions**: Suggest potential maintenance actions or solutions to the issues you've reported.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or context that might be helpful for future reference.
* **General Information 2, Room Inspection 2, Maintenance Issues 2, Maintenance Actions 2, Comments 2**: These fields are duplicates of the above fields, allowing you to report on the same details for a second room or inspection.
