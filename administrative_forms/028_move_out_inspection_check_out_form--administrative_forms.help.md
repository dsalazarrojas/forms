# Move Out Inspection Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Move Out Inspection Check Out Form is a report used to document the condition of a property before a tenant moves out. It is used by property managers to assess the property's condition at the end of the lease term and to track any issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have completed a thorough inspection of the property before filling out this form.
2. Review each section of the form and report any issues you have found.
3. Attach any supporting documentation or photographs to support your claims.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Move Out Details (Tenant)** (tenant_move_out_details, text, optional): This section is for the tenant to provide details about their move out, including the date and time of the move out, and any issues they may have encountered during the process.
* **Property Condition** (property_condition, select_one, optional): Choose the current condition of the property from the options provided. Options are:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Photos Taken** (photos_taken, number, optional): If you took photos of the property during the move out inspection, enter the number of photos you took.
* **Property Manager Comments** (property_manager_comments, note, optional): This section is for the property manager to leave any comments about the property's condition.
* **Landlord Comments** (landlord_comments, note, optional): This section is for the landlord to leave any comments about the property's condition.

## Tips
- Be thorough in your inspection and documentation to ensure accurate reporting.
- Attach any supporting documentation or photographs to support your claims.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
