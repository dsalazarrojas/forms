# Local Policy Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Local Policy Complaint Form is designed to help our users report local policy complaints. Users can submit their grievances and provide necessary information for the organization to address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the User Input page with your relevant information.
2. In the Grievance Description field, provide a detailed description of your complaint.
3. Check the Complaint Status field to indicate whether your complaint has been resolved or not.
4. Enter the Date and Time you submitted this form in the Date Submitted and Time Submitted fields.
5. If you have any contact information, please enter it in the Contact Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This is a text field where you can enter any relevant information about yourself.
* **Grievance Description**: Please provide a detailed description of your complaint to help us understand the issue better.
* **Complaint Status**: Select 'Selected' if your complaint has been addressed, or 'Not Selected' if it's still pending.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you submitted this form for our record-keeping.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the exact time you submitted this form for accurate tracking.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information if you have any, so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* Keep your descriptions concise but detailed enough for us to understand your complaint.
* Use the correct format for dates and times in the respective fields.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

Please note: This form is not intended to be a comprehensive solution to all complaints, but rather a tool to help our organization track and address issues efficiently. If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us at [contact email].
