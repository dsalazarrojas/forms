# Storage Facility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storage Facility Incident Form is a document used to report incidents that occur within the storage facility. This form aims to collect information about accidents, injuries, or other unexpected events that have taken place in the facility. It's crucial to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the necessary information is captured and addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by selecting the correct facility from your storage facility options**.
2. Fill out the incident date and time when the incident occurred.
3. Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant details.
4. Specify the location where the incident took place.
5. Add any additional incident details that might be relevant.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the person you want to be notified about the incident.
7. Select the person or team responsible for handling the incident from the assigned to options.
8. Optionally, provide an alternative name for the assigned to if Jimmy or John are not available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (Facility Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the storage facility where the incident took place.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date, date, required): The date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Incident Time, time, required): The time when the incident occurred.
* **Accident Description** (Accident Description, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of what happened.
* **Location** (Location, text, optional): Enter the place where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Details** (Incident Details, text, optional): Add any additional information that's relevant to the incident.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you want to be notified about the incident.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter the email of the person you want to be notified about the incident.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person you want to be notified about the incident.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_one, optional): Choose the person or team responsible for handling the incident from the list of options.
* **Other (Name)** (Other (Name), text, optional): If Jimmy or John are not available to be assigned to, enter the name of another person or team responsible.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out as much relevant information as possible to ensure the incident is handled correctly.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant contacts for clarification.
