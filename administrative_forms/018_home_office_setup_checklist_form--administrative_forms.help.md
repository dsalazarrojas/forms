# Home Office Setup Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Office Setup Checklist Form is a tool to help you set up your home office space with the necessary equipment, furniture, and supplies to be productive and comfortable while working from home.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully to get a clear overview of the items you need to set up your home office space.
2. Select all the required options for office equipment, furniture, and supplies.
3. Choose the correct option for your office space size and location.
4. Provide a description of your work space if necessary.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Space** (`office_space`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe the space available for your home office. If you have a large or small space, please choose the correct option.
* **Office Equipment Needed** (`office_equipment`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select the office equipment you need, such as Printer, Scanner, and Computer.
* **Office Furniture Needed** (`office_furniture`, select_one, required): Choose the office furniture you need, such as Desk, Chair, and Shelving.
* **Office Supplies Needed** (`office_supplies`, select_one, required): Select the office supplies you need, such as Paper, Pens, and Highlighters.
* **Office Space Size** (`office_space_size`, number, optional): If you have a specific space size in mind, please enter it here.
* **Office Setup Location** (`office_setup_location`, select_one, required): Choose where you will set up your home office, such as Home, Office, or Hotel.
* **Work Space Description** (`work_space_description`, text, optional): If you have any specific requirements or features for your work space, please describe them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Do you have any additional comments or notes about your setup?
