# Main Contract Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and manage contracts between businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Terms and Conditions" page and enter the contract's terms and conditions in the text field provided.
2. Move on to the "Agreement Terms" page and enter the contract's agreement terms in the text field provided.
3. On the "Business Parties" page, select the business parties involved in the contract using the dropdown menu.
4. On the "Contract Signatory" page, select the contract signatory using the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the contract start date and end date in the corresponding date fields on the "Contract Start Date" and "Contract End Date" pages.
6. Enter the contract duration on the "Contract Duration" page.
7. Enter the contract amount on the "Contract Amount" page.
8. On the "Payment Terms" page, enter the payment terms in the text field provided.
9. On the "Payment Frequency" page, select the payment frequency using the dropdown menu.
10. Enter the payment due date on the "Payment Due Date" page.
11. Select the payment method on the "Payment Method" page using the dropdown menu.
12. On the "Cancellation Terms" page, enter the cancellation terms in the text field provided.
13. Enter the contract cancellation date on the "Contract Cancellation Date" page.
14. Enter the cancellation reason on the "Cancellation Reason" page.
15. Select the contract status on the "Contract Status" page using the dropdown menu.
16. Enter the contract version on the "Contract Version" page.
17. Enter the agreement version on the "Agreement Version" page.
18. Enter any additional contract notes on the "Contract Notes" page.
19. Enter any additional agreement notes on the "Agreement Notes" page.
20. On the "Contract Signed By" page, select whether the contract has been signed by a business party using the dropdown menu.
21. On the "Agreement Signed By" page, select whether the agreement has been signed by a business party using the dropdown menu.
22. Enter the contract signed date on the "Contract Signed Date" page.
23. Enter the contract signed time on the "Contract Signed Time" page.
24. On the "Contract Signed By" pages, enter any additional contract signed by notes on the "Contract Signed By Notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contract's terms and conditions in this text field.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contract's agreement terms in this text field.
* **Business Parties** (`business_parties`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the business parties involved in the contract using the dropdown menu.
* **Contract Signatory** (`contract_signatory`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the contract signatory using the dropdown menu.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the contract start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the contract end date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the contract duration in months or years.
* **Contract Amount** (`contract_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the contract amount.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the payment terms in this text field.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment frequency using the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Due Date** (`payment_due_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the payment due date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment method using the dropdown menu.
* **Cancellation Terms** (`cancellation_terms`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the cancellation terms in this text field.
* **Contract Cancellation Date** (`contract_cancellation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the contract cancellation date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`cancellation_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the cancellation reason in this text field.
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the contract status using the dropdown menu.
* **Contract Version** (`contract_version`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the contract version.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the agreement version.
* **Contract Notes** (`contract_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional contract notes in this text field.
* **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional agreement notes in this text field.
* **Contract Signed By** (`contract_signed_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the contract has been signed by a business party using the dropdown menu.
* **Agreement Signed By** (`agreement_signed_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the agreement has been signed by a business party using the dropdown menu.
* **Contract Signed Date** (`contract_signed_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the contract signed date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contract Signed Time** (`contract_signed_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the contract signed time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Contract Signed By Notes** (`contract_signed_by_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional contract signed by notes in this text field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant business parties involved.
