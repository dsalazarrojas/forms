# Discrimination Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to report incidents of discrimination or harassment that you have experienced or witnessed. Please fill out this form to provide as much detail as possible about the incident, and it will be reviewed and addressed by our organization's discrimination reporting team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Report Form** field, providing a brief description of what happened.
2. Enter the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** fields to record the date and time of the incident.
3. In the **Incident Description** field, briefly describe the incident, including any details that might be helpful in investigating or addressing the issue.
4. Select the **Type of Discrimination** that occurred, choosing from the following options:
	* Racial or Ethnic-Based Discrimination
	* Sex-Based Discrimination
	* Disability-Based Discrimination
	* Age-Based Discrimination
	* Sexual Orientation or Gender Identity-Based Discrimination
	* National Origin-Based Discrimination
	* Marital Status-Based Discrimination
5. If you have information about the **Perpetrator** (the person or group responsible for the discrimination), please provide their name or description.
6. If you have any witnesses to the incident, please provide their names or descriptions in the **Witness** field.
7. If you have any supporting information or statements from others, please provide them in the **Support** field.
8. Finally, please enter the name and contact information of the **Reporting Officer**, which will be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on the status of your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Form**: A text field where you can briefly describe the incident.
* **Incident Date**: A date field where you can record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time**: A time field where you can record the time of the incident.
* **Incident Description**: A text field where you can provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Type of Discrimination**: A selection field where you can choose the type of discrimination that occurred.
* **Perpetrator**: A text field where you can provide information about the person or group responsible for the discrimination.
* **Witness**: A text field where you can provide information about witnesses to the incident.
* **Support**: A text field where you can provide any supporting information or statements from others.
* **Reporting Officer**: A text field where you can enter the name and contact information of the person handling your report.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are reporting an incident that occurred in the past, try to provide as much information as you can about the incident, including dates and times.
* If you are reporting an ongoing incident, please try to provide regular updates with any new information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the reporting process, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
