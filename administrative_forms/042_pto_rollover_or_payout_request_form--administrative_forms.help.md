# pto_rollover_or_payout_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees request a paid time off (PTO) rollover or payout. It is used to provide a structured and efficient way to submit requests for PTO.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information in the "Employee Information" field.
2. Fill in your name and employee ID in the respective fields.
3. Enter the start and end dates for your requested PTO rollover or payout period.
4. Provide a clear reason for your request in the "Reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your employee details. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like the PTO rollover or payout to start.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the PTO rollover or payout should end.
- **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Provide a clear reason for your PTO rollover or payout request. This will help your manager understand your request better.
- **Tips**: Before submitting this form, please ensure you have checked your available PTO balance and that the dates you request do not conflict with other company policies or events.
