# Public Advocacy Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Advocacy Program Waiver Form is designed to gather information from participants and ensure that they are aware of the risks and terms involved in the public advocacy program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you will need to provide the following information:

1. General Information: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself and your organization.
2. Participant Consent: Select the option to indicate your consent to participate in the public advocacy program.
3. Risk Acknowledgement: Select the date on which you are acknowledging the risks involved in participating in the public advocacy program.
4. Legal Protections: Select the legal and regulatory protections that apply to the public advocacy program.
5. Participant Agreement: Select the agreements that you are making with the organization.
6. Organization: Enter the name of the organization you are representing.
7. Participant Name: Enter your name.
8. Participant Contact: Enter your contact information.
9. Organization Representative: Enter the name of the representative of the organization.
10. Participant Agree: Select whether you agree to certain terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself and your organization.
* **Participant Consent** (`2`, `text`, required): Select the option to indicate your consent to participate in the public advocacy program.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`3`, `date`, required): Select the date on which you are acknowledging the risks involved in participating in the public advocacy program.
* **Legal Protections** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the legal and regulatory protections that apply to the public advocacy program.
* **Participant Agreement** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the agreements that you are making with the organization.
* **Organization** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization you are representing.
* **Participant Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Participant Contact** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Organization Representative** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the representative of the organization.
* **Participant Agree** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to certain terms.
