# fire_incident_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fire Incident Report Form is used to collect information about fires that have occurred within our organization. This form helps ensure accurate and consistent data collection, allowing for better understanding and response to fires.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click "Submit" to access the form.
2.  Enter the fire incident date in the "Fire Incident Date" field.
3.  Enter the fire incident time in the "Fire Incident Time" field.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
5.  Enter the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
6.  Enter the number of persons involved in the "Number of Persons Involved" field.
7.  Enter the number of firefighters involved in the "Number of Firefighters Involved" field.
8.  Enter the number of civilians involved in the "Number of Civilians Involved" field.
9.  Enter the number of injuries sustained during the incident in the "Number of Injuries" field.
10. Enter the number of fatalities in the "Number of Fatalities" field.
11. For the "Cause of Incident" field, select the option that best describes the reason for the fire.
12. For the "Fire Type" field, select the type of fire that occurred (e.g., electrical, kitchen, etc.).
13. For the "Fire Extinguish Method" field, select all the methods used to extinguish the fire.
14. For the "Incident Disposition" field, select the outcome of the incident (e.g., under control, extinguished, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Fire Incident Date** (`fire_incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date the fire occurred.
*   **Fire Incident Time** (`fire_incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time the fire occurred.
*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Number of Persons Involved** (`number_of_persons_involved`, number, required): Enter the number of persons involved in the incident.
*   **Number of Firefighters Involved** (`number_of_firefighters_involved`, number, required): Enter the number of firefighters involved in responding to the incident.
*   **Number of Civilians Involved** (`number_of_civilians_involved`, number, required): Enter the number of civilians involved in the incident.
*   **Number of Injuries** (`number_of_injuries`, number, required): Enter the number of injuries sustained during the incident.
*   **Number of Fatalities** (`number_of_fatalities`, number, required): Enter the number of fatalities in the incident.
*   **Cause of Incident** (`cause_of_incident`, text, required): Select the option that best describes the reason for the fire.
*   **Fire Type** (`fire_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of fire that occurred (e.g., electrical, kitchen, etc.).
*   **Fire Extinguish Method** (`fire_extinguish_method`, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods used to extinguish the fire.
*   **Incident Disposition** (`incident_disposition`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the incident (e.g., under control, extinguished, etc.).
