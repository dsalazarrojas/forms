# Business Planning Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Planning Leave Form is used to submit a leave request for business planning activities such as meetings with investors, conducting market research, or attending business training. It requires input from employees regarding their leave details and provides fields for manager and HR approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information, including name, ID, and department.
2. Input the name of your direct manager and start and end dates of your leave.
3. Select the total number of business days you are requesting as leave.
4. Choose the reason for your leave from the options provided.
5. Provide a detailed description of the business planning activities you will undertake during your leave.
6. If applicable, specify the expected outcomes of your business planning activities.
7. Identify who will cover your responsibilities while you are away.
8. List any outstanding tasks and their deadlines that need to be covered before or during your leave.
9. Indicate your remote availability during the leave period.
10. Provide your contact information if you will be available remotely.
11. If applicable, specify the status of your manager's approval.
12. If applicable, specify the status of HR's approval.
13. Finally, input the date the leave request was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Full name of the employee)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employee ID (Your employee identification number)**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Department (Your department or team)**: Enter the name of your department or team.
* **Direct Manager Name (Name of your immediate supervisor)**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Leave Start Date (First day of requested leave)**: Enter the date you wish to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date (Last day of requested leave)**: Enter the date you wish to end your leave.
* **Total Days (Number of business days requested)**: Enter the total number of business days you are requesting as leave.
* **Reason for Leave (Select the primary reason)**: Choose the reason for your leave from the options provided.
* **Detailed Purpose (Explain what business planning activities you will undertake)**: Enter a description of the business planning activities you will undertake during your leave.
* **Expected Outcomes (What do you plan to accomplish)**: If applicable, enter the expected outcomes of your business planning activities.
* **Work Coverage Plan (Who will cover your responsibilities)**: Enter who will cover your responsibilities while you are away.
* **Outstanding Tasks Completion (Status of current projects and deadlines)**: If applicable, list any outstanding tasks and their deadlines that need to be covered before or during your leave.
* **Remote Availability (Will you be available remotely)**: Choose whether you will be fully, partially, or not available remotely during your leave.
* **Contact Information (Your contact details while away)**: If you will be available remotely, enter your contact information.
* **Manager Approval Status (Approval from direct manager)**: If applicable, specify the status of your manager's approval.
* **HR Approval Status (Approval status from HR department)**: If applicable, specify the status of HR's approval.
* **Leave Request Submission Date (When this request was submitted)**: Input the date the leave request was submitted.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your leave request aligns with your company's leave policy and departmental guidelines.
- Make sure to include all required information, as incomplete submissions may delay approval.
- Keep a record of your leave request number for future reference.
