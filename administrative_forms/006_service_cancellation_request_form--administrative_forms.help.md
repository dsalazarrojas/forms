# service_cancellation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to request cancellation of a service they have signed up for. Customers can provide their details and reasons for cancellation to ensure a smooth cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Type out your request for cancellation in the "Request from customer" field.
2. Provide the specific reason you are cancelling your service in the "Reason for cancellation" field.
3. If needed, provide additional details about the service you are cancelling in the "Service details" field.
4. Enter the date you are requesting cancellation in the "Cancellation date" field.
5. If applicable, provide the date you expect the cancellation to take effect in the "Expected cancellation date" field.
6. Provide your contact information, including a contact person and phone number, in the "Contact person" and "Contact phone" fields respectively.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
8. If you have an email address, please enter it in the "Email" field.
9. Finally, confirm your cancellation by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Confirm cancellation" field. If you confirm cancellation, provide a reason for cancelling in the "Confirm cancellation reason" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request from customer** (`customer_request`): 
  * Type in a brief description of your request for cancellation.
  * Be concise and clear about your reasons for requesting cancellation.
* **Reason for cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`): 
  * Provide the specific reason you are cancelling your service.
  * Be as detailed as possible.
* **Service details** (`service_details`): 
  * If necessary, provide additional details about the service you are cancelling.
  * This could include account numbers or specific issues encountered.
* **Cancellation date** (`cancellation_date`): 
  * Enter the date you are requesting cancellation.
  * This date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Expected cancellation date** (`expected_cancellation_date`): 
  * If applicable, provide the date you expect the cancellation to take effect.
  * This date should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Contact person** (`contact_person`): 
  * Provide the name of the person making the cancellation request.
  * This could be yourself or a representative.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`): 
  * Enter your phone number for follow-up.
  * Include your area code.
* **Note** (`note`): 
  * Add any additional comments or notes.
  * This could be used for follow-up or reference.
* **Email** (`email`): 
  * If you have an email address, please enter it here.
  * This will help us contact you about your cancellation.
* **Confirm cancellation** (`confirm_cancellation`): 
  * Select "Yes" to confirm your cancellation request.
  * Select "No" if you are not requesting cancellation.
* **Confirm cancellation reason** (`confirm_cancellation_reason`): 
  * If you select "Yes" in the previous field, provide a reason for cancelling.
  * Be as detailed as possible.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed smoothly.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
