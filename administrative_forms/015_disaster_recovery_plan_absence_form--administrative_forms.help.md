# Disaster Recovery Plan Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employees to report their absence from work due to a disaster recovery plan. It helps to document and record employee absences during a disaster recovery period, enabling efficient communication and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Select your job title from the "job_title" field.
3. Enter the start date of your absence in the "start_date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your absence in the "end_date" field.
5. Provide a brief reason for your absence in the "absence_reason" field (optional).
6. Enter your contact name in the "contact_name" field.
7. Enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field (optional).
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your company ID or badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Required): Enter your job title as it appears on your company ID or badge.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, Required): Enter the date from which you are absent from work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, Required): Enter the date until which you are absent from work.
* **Absence Reason** (`absence_reason`, text, Optional): Briefly describe the reason for your absence.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of the contact person who can be reached for further questions or updates.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

**Tips**
- Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure efficient communication and resource allocation during the disaster recovery period.
