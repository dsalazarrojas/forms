# Food Safety Certification Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Safety Certification Intake Form is a document used to gather information from food service providers to determine their eligibility for certification. This form is essential for assessing the service provider's compliance with food safety regulations and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your food service provider information in the "Food Service Provider" field.
2.  Provide your address in the "Address" field.
3.  Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5.  Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6.  Select your food service type in the "Food Service Type" field. Select 'Yes' or 'No' as per your services.
7.  Choose your current certificate status in the "Certificate Status" field. Select "Active" or "Inactive" as per your current certificate status.
8.  Optionally, add any relevant notes about your services in the "Notes" field.
9.  If you have a preferred tool assigned to you, select it from the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Food Service Provider** (`food_service_provider`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your food service provider business.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your business address for contact and communication purposes.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Optional): Enter your primary contact number for communication.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your work email address for official communications.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your Date of Birth to be recorded for certification purposes.
*   **Food Service Type** (`food_service_type`, select_multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you offer food service, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Certificate Status** (`certificate_status`, select_one, Optional): Choose your certificate status as 'Active' or 'Inactive' to reflect your current certification status.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your services that you think are relevant to your certification.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, Optional): Select the tool assigned to you if it is 'chatjimmy', or if not, select 'Other'. 

This form aims to ensure that your food service provider's information is accurately recorded for certification purposes. Please fill out all relevant fields to ensure your certification process is smooth and efficient.
