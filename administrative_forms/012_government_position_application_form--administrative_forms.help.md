# Government Position Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Government Position Application Form is used to collect information from applicants for government positions. This form helps assess candidates' qualifications and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the sections and fields required.
2. Fill out the sections with accurate and honest information.
3. Ensure you have uploaded all necessary documents as required.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, Optional): Enter your name and contact information as instructed on the form.
* **Personal Details** (`Personal Details`, number, Optional): Provide your relevant personal details as requested.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, note, Optional): Describe your work experience, including job roles, job duration, and any relevant achievements.
* **Education** (`Education`, select multiple, Optional): Select all relevant educational institutions and degrees you have completed.
* **Prophets** (`Prophets`, select multiple, Optional): Select all relevant prophetic experiences or qualifications.
* **References** (`References`, select multiple, Optional): Select all relevant professional references.
* **Uploaded Documents** (`Uploaded Documents`, text, Optional): Upload any required or requested documents as instructed.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, select one, Optional): Indicate who submitted the application on your behalf.
* **Submitted Date** (`Submitted Date`, date, Optional): Enter the date the application was submitted.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Upload all necessary documents before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to avoid errors.
* Contact the relevant authorities if you have any questions or concerns.
