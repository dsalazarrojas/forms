# HIPAA Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The HIPAA Compliance Audit Form is used to verify a patient's personal and medical data handling under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) regulations. This form aims to document the patient's consent, auditor's comments, and other relevant details that demonstrate HIPAA compliance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Compliance Audit" page.
2. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Compliance Audit**: This field is for a brief description of the compliance audit.
   - **Organization Name**: Enter the name of your organization.
   - **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the compliance audit.
   - **Auditor's Name**: Enter your name as the auditor.
   - **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
   - **Email**: Enter your email.
   - **Address**: Enter your address.
3. Move to the next page.
4. Fill in the following fields:
   - **Auditor's Signature**: Provide your signature as the auditor.
   - **Auditor's Date**: Enter the date of your signature.
   - **Patient's Signature**: Provide the patient's signature as proof of consent.
   - **Patient's Date**: Enter the date of the patient's signature.
5. If necessary, fill in the following fields:
   - **Auditor's Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the auditor.
   - **Patient Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the patient.
   - **Compliance Officer**: Enter the name of the compliance officer.
   - **Compliance Officer Title**: Enter the title of the compliance officer.
   - **Compliance Officer Contact**: Enter the contact information of the compliance officer.
   - **Compliance Officer Email**: Enter the email of the compliance officer.
   - **Compliance Officer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the compliance officer.
   - **Auditor's Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the auditor.
   - **Patient Consent**: Check if the patient has given consent to handle their medical data.
   - **Is Signed**: Check if the patient and auditor have signed the form.
   - **Signature Block**: This is a field for any additional comments or notes, usually a large text box.
   - **Patient Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the patient.
6. Review and submit the form.
