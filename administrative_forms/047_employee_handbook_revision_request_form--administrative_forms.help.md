# Employee Handbook Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handbook Revision Request Form is a form designed for employees to submit requests for revisions to the company's employee handbook. This form helps to document and track revisions, ensuring that changes are implemented effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: Enter a brief and clear title of your revision request.
2. **Category**: Select one or multiple categories that best describe the area of the handbook that requires revision. Choose from Administrative Forms, Employee Handbook, or Policies.
3. **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the revision request. This section is optional but recommended for clarity and accuracy.
4. **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID, if applicable.
5. **Revision Request Form Type**: Specify the type of revision you're requesting. Choose from Request, Suggestion, or Comment.
6. **Submission Date**: Date and time when you submitted the request.
7. **Submission Time**: Time when you submitted the request.
8. **Submission Status**: Select the current status of your request. Choose from In Progress, Approved, or Rejected.
9. **Submitted By**: Identify who submitted the request. Choose from Manager, Supervisor, or HR.
10. **Submission Type**: Specify the type of submission. Choose from Policy Change, Procedure Change, or Policy/Procedure Change.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: Enter a brief and clear title of your revision request.
* **Category**: Select one or multiple categories that best describe the area of the handbook that requires revision.
*   *Note: Selecting multiple categories is not required, but it helps to ensure that the request is properly routed.*
* **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the revision request. This section is optional but recommended for clarity and accuracy.
*   *This is a text field for you to describe your request.*
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID, if applicable.
*   *This field is for employees who are submitting the request on behalf of another employee.*
* **Revision Request Form Type**: Specify the type of revision you're requesting. Choose from Request, Suggestion, or Comment.
*   *Choose the type that best describes the nature of your request.*
* **Submission Date**: Date and time when you submitted the request.
*   *This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.*
* **Submission Time**: Time when you submitted the request.
*   *This field is automatically populated when you submit the form.*
* **Submission Status**: Select the current status of your request. Choose from In Progress, Approved, or Rejected.
*   *This field is automatically populated based on the submission status.*
* **Submitted By**: Identify who submitted the request. Choose from Manager, Supervisor, or HR.
*   *Choose who submitted the request on your behalf.*
* **Submission Type**: Specify the type of submission. Choose from Policy Change, Procedure Change, or Policy/Procedure Change.
*   *Choose the type that best describes the nature of your request.*

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your request is properly processed.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* This form is designed to facilitate efficient processing of revisions.
