# Hot Work Safety Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hot Work Safety Assessment form is used to assess and report hot work operations on a construction site. It ensures that all necessary safety measures are in place and helps to prevent fires and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Site ID, which identifies the specific site where the hot work operation is taking place.
2. Enter the Permit Number, which is required for all hot work operations.
3. Indicate whether the work is a "Yes" or "No" operation, depending on whether hot work is involved.
4. Enter the Work Location, where the hot work operation is taking place.
5. Enter the Supervisor's Name, who is responsible for overseeing the hot work operation.
6. Enter the Start Date and Finish Date of the hot work operation.
7. Enter the Work Hours, during which the hot work operation will take place.
8. Enter the Fire Watchman's Name, who is responsible for monitoring the area during the hot work operation.
9. Enter the Fire Watchman's Phone and Email, which can be used to contact them in case of an emergency.
10. Enter the Hot Work Equipment, which is required for the operation.
11. Enter the Fire Fighting Equipment, which is required for the operation.
12. Enter the Fire Fighting Training, which should be completed by the Fire Watchman.
13. Enter the Fire Escape Plan, which should be in place for the operation.
14. Enter the Fire Extinguisher, which should be available and functional.
15. Enter the Fire Hydrants, which should be available and functional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hot Work Operations**: This field indicates whether the work is a "Yes" or "No" operation, depending on whether hot work is involved. (Required, select_one)
* **Site ID**: This field is used to identify the specific site where the hot work operation is taking place. (Required, text)
* **Permit Number**: This field is required for all hot work operations, and should be entered accurately. (Required, text)
* **Work Location**: This field is used to enter the location where the hot work operation is taking place. (Required, text)
* **Supervisor Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the supervisor responsible for overseeing the hot work operation. (Required, text)
* **Start Date**: This field is used to enter the start date of the hot work operation. (Required, date)
* **Finish Date**: This field is used to enter the finish date of the hot work operation. (Required, date)
* **Work Hours**: This field is used to enter the hours during which the hot work operation will take place. (Required, time)
* **Fire Watchman Name**: This field is used to enter the name of the fire watchman responsible for monitoring the area during the hot work operation. (Required, text)
* **Fire Watchman Phone**: This field is used to enter the phone number of the fire watchman. (Required, text)
* **Fire Watchman Email**: This field is used to enter the email of the fire watchman. (Required, email)
* **Hot Work Equipment**: This field is used to enter the equipment required for the hot work operation. (Required, text)
* **Fire Fighting Equipment**: This field is used to enter the equipment required for fire fighting. (Required, text)
* **Fire Fighting Training**: This field is used to enter the training completed by the fire watchman. (Required, text)
* **Fire Escape Plan**: This field is used to enter the fire escape plan in place for the operation. (Required, text)
* **Fire Extinguisher**: This field is used to enter the location and status of the fire extinguisher. (Required, text)
* **Fire Hydrants**: This field is used to enter the location and status of the fire hydrants. (Required, text)

## Tips
* Always ensure that all necessary safety measures are in place before starting a hot work operation.
* Double-check that all required permits and equipment are available and functional.
* Keep in touch with the fire watchman during the operation.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it, to ensure that all required fields are filled accurately.
* Store this form for future reference and update it as necessary.
