# Lab Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request new lab equipment or supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the current date from the calendar picker.
3. Enter your name in the "Requested By" field.
4. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
5. Select the type of equipment or supplies you are requesting (e.g., General Lab Equipment, Specialized Lab Equipment, or Consumables).
6. Enter the name of the equipment or supplies you are requesting.
7. If applicable, enter the supplier name and cost of the equipment or supplies.
8. Specify the quantity of the equipment or supplies needed.
9. Add any additional notes about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requisition Date**: Enter the current date when submitting this request. This date is used to track when the request was submitted.
* **Requested By**: Enter your name and title, as this will help us track who submitted the request.
* **Department**: Select the department that will be using the requested equipment or supplies. This will help us route the request to the correct procurement team.
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment or supplies being requested (e.g., General Lab Equipment, Specialized Lab Equipment, or Consumables).
* **Equipment Name**: Enter the name of the specific equipment or supplies being requested.
* **Supplier**: If you know the supplier of the equipment or supplies, please enter their name.
* **Cost**: Enter the cost of the equipment or supplies, or if not known, leave this field blank.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of the equipment or supplies being requested.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or context about the request, such as the specific project or purpose the equipment or supplies will be used for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have checked with your department's procurement officer to see if the equipment or supplies are already on order or if there are any existing plans to acquire them.
* Do not duplicate existing requests, as this can lead to unnecessary duplication of efforts and resources.
* If the equipment or supplies are for a specific project, please provide the project number or title so we can track the request correctly.
