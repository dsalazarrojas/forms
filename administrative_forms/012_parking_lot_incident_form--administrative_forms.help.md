# Parking Lot Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Lot Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents that occur within parking lots or similar areas. This form will help you report and document incidents, allowing for swift and accurate handling of the situation.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. You can complete the form online or offline, as you prefer. If you're filling out the form offline, you can simply type in your answers, and then upload them to the online platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Please provide the date the incident occurred. If you're not sure of the date, you can estimate it to the best of your ability.
- **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred. If you don't know the exact time, you can estimate it or mark it as unknown.
- **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Describe the location of the incident, including the parking lot name, building number, or nearby landmark.
- **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide as much detail as possible about what happened during the incident. Be as descriptive as you can, including the events leading up to the incident and any injuries or damages sustained.
- **Incident Reporter** (`incident_reporter`, text, required: false): If you're reporting the incident, please provide your name and contact information. If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, provide their name and contact information instead.
- **Incident Respondent** (`incident_respondent`, text, required: false): If someone else reported the incident, please provide their name and contact information.
- **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the outcome of the incident, including any injuries, damages, or other notable events.
- **Incident Image or Video** (`incident_image`, text, required: false): If you have any images or videos related to the incident, please upload them here. This can help investigators understand the situation better.
- **Additional Incident Files** (`incident_file`, text, required: false): If you have any other relevant files, such as witness statements or police reports, please upload them here.
- **Incident Additional Info** (`incident_additional_info`, text, required: false): Provide any other relevant information about the incident, including weather conditions, lighting, or other contextual details.

## Tips
- Be as descriptive as possible when providing information about the incident.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can estimate or leave it blank. However, please note that providing some information (e.g., incident date and time) is crucial for proper incident reporting.
- If you're reporting on behalf of someone else, please ensure you have their permission to report the incident.
- If you're providing images or videos, please ensure they are relevant and not excessive in size.
