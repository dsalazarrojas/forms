# Product And Equipment Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product And Equipment Agreement form is used for documenting and managing product and equipment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form carefully and understanding what information is required.
2. Fill out each field with the necessary information, making sure to provide as much detail as possible.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the equipment.
* **Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of equipment from the list provided.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the conditions that apply to the equipment.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the equipment.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment.
* **Serial number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Purchase date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of purchase.
* **Sale date** (`sale_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of sale.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the equipment.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the equipment from the list provided.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the equipment.
* **Image** (`image`, text, optional): Upload an image of the equipment.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the image.
* **Image description** (`image_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the image.
* **Video** (`video`, text, optional): Upload a video about the equipment.
* **Video URL** (`video_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the video.
* **Video description** (`video_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the video.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Upload a signature of the equipment.
* **Signature URL** (`signature_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the signature.
* **Signature description** (`signature_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the signature.
* **File** (`file`, text, optional): Upload a file about the equipment.
* **File URL** (`file_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the file.
* **File description** (`file_description`, text, optional): Provide a description of the file.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the equipment.

Please note that this form is divided into 2 pages, but for the sake of brevity, I have condensed the instructions to focus on the field-by-field explanation.
