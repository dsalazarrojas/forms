# Casual Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Casual Leave Form is a tool used by employees to request leave from their work due to personal reasons such as illness, bereavement, or personal appointments. This form gathers information about the employee's details, leave duration and reason for absence to ensure proper handling and communication within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name and employee ID.
2. Select your department from the provided options.
3. Enter your leave start and end dates.
4. Specify the total number of days you are requesting leave, including half days if applicable.
5. Provide the primary reason for your leave.
6. Enter your contact phone number in case of emergencies.
7. List any work handover notes and the name of your designated back-up colleague.
8. Enter your line manager's name for tracking purposes.
9. Submit the form with the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Employee Name, Text, Required): This is where you enter your full name as it appears on your ID and official documents.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, Text, Required): Enter your employee ID for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Department** (Department, Select One, Required): Choose your department from the provided options to ensure that your leave is recorded correctly.
* **Leave Start Date** (Leave Start Date, Date, Required): Enter the date when you plan to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date** (Leave End Date, Date, Required): Enter the date when you plan to return to work.
* **Total Number of Days Requested** (Total Number of Days, Number, Required): Include half days if applicable.
* **Primary Reason for Leave** (Leave Reason, Text, Required): Enter the main reason for your leave.
* **Contact Phone Number during Leave** (Contact Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter a contact phone number for emergency calls.
* **Work Handover Notes** (Handover Notes, Text, Optional): List tasks or responsibilities to be covered during your absence.
* **Designated Back-up Colleague** (Back-up Colleague, Text, Required): Enter the name of your back-up colleague.
* **Line Manager Name** (Line Manager Name, Text, Required): Enter your line manager's name for tracking purposes.
* **Date of Submission** (Submission Date, Date, Required): Enter the date when you submit this form.
