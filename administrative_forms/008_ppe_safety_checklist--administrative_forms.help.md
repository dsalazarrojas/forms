# PPE Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a site's Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) safety measures. It is designed to ensure that the site's safety protocols are up-to-date and compliant with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. For each question, choose the correct response (e.g., "Yes" or "No", or enter a date/time).
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or site safety officer for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **ppe_safety_checklist_1** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter a brief description of the safety measure.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_2** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the date of the last safety inspection.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_3** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the date the safety measure was implemented.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_4** (Select One, yes/no, **Required**): Has this safety measure been implemented?
* **ppe_safety_checklist_5** (Select One, yes/no, **Required**): Has this safety measure been evaluated?
* **ppe_safety_checklist_6** (Date, date, **Required**): Enter the date of the next evaluation.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_7** (Time, time, **Not Required**): Enter the time of the last inspection.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_8** (Number, number, **Required**): Enter the number of days since the last inspection.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_9** (Number, number, **Not Required**): Enter the number of days since the last evaluation.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_10** (Note, text, **Not Required**): Enter any additional comments or notes about the safety measure.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_11** (Email, email, **Not Required**): Enter the email address of the site safety officer.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_12** (Text, text, **Not Required**): Enter any other relevant information.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_13** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the person responsible for the safety measure.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_14** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the location of the safety measure.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_15** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the type of safety equipment used.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_16** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the condition of the safety equipment.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_17** (Text, text, **Not Required**): Enter any other relevant information.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_18** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the serial number of the safety equipment.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_19** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the manufacturer of the safety equipment.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_20** (Text, text, **Not Required**): Enter any other relevant information.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_21** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the last maintenance date of the safety equipment.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_22** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the next maintenance date of the safety equipment.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_23** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter the location where the safety equipment can be found.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_24** (Text, text, **Required**): Enter any other relevant information.
* **ppe_safety_checklist_25** (Text, text, **Required**)
