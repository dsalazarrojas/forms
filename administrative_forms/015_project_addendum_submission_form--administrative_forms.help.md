# Project Addendum Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Addendum Submission Form is used to submit updates or changes to existing projects. This form is intended for employees to record and communicate project updates, changes, or additions to their projects. The form is used to ensure that all relevant project information is documented and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **form_id**: Enter the unique identifier for this submission.
	* **client_name**: Enter the name of the client associated with this project.
	* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the project manager.
	* **project_number**: Enter the project number.
	* **update_date**: Enter the date of the update.
	* **update_time**: Enter the time of the update.
	* **submission_comments**: Enter any comments or notes related to the update.
2. Enter the submitter's information:
	* **submitter_name**: Enter your name.
	* **submitter_email**: Enter your email address.
	* **submitter_phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **submitter_job_title**: Enter your job title.
	* **submitter_department**: Enter your department.
	* **submitter_extension**: Enter any additional information.
	* **submitter_job_code**: Enter any relevant job codes.
	* **form_version**: Enter the version of the form.
	* **submitter_ip**: Enter the IP address of the device you are using.
	* **submitter_browser**: Enter the browser you are using.
	* **submitter_os**: Enter the operating system of your device.
	* **submitter_device**: Enter the type of device you are using.
	* **submitter_user_agent**: Enter your user agent string.
	* **submitter_referer**: Enter any relevant referer information.
	* **submitter_referal**: Enter any relevant referal information.
	* **submitter_referenceline**: Enter any additional referenceline information.
