# Architectural Plan Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Plan Release Form is a crucial document that facilitates the release of architectural plans to authorized parties. This form ensures that the plans are reviewed and approved before being released, and it helps maintain transparency and accountability throughout the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project information:
	* Fill in the **Project Name**, **Project Number or ID**, and **Project Address** fields with the relevant details.
2. Provide the architectural firm information:
	* Enter the **Architecture Firm Name** and **Firm Address** for the firm responsible for the plans.
3. Confirm plan details:
	* Select the correct **Plan Phase** from the dropdown menu (e.g., Schematic design, Design development, etc.)
	* Enter the **Description of Plans Being Released**, including the type and scope of documents being released.
4. Confirm release and distribution details:
	* Mark the **Design is approved for release** field as True or False.
	* Confirm that you have obtained **Owner or Client approval** and **Building Authority approvals**, if required.
	* Select the distribution method for the plans (e.g., Email, Physical copies, etc.).
5. Provide distribution details:
	* Enter the **Authorized Recipients**, who will receive the plans.
	* Enter the **Date of Release Authorization**, which should be the date the plans are authorized for release.
6. Confirm authorization:
	* Enter the **Authorized Signatory Name and Title**, who is responsible for authorizing the release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project being referred to in the form.
* **Project Number or ID**: Enter the reference code or ID for the project.
* **Project Address**: Enter the location of the project site.
* **Architecture Firm Name**: Enter the name of the firm responsible for the plans.
* **Firm Address**: Enter the address of the firm responsible for the plans.
* **Principal Architect Name**: Enter the name of the principal architect responsible for the plans.
* **Plan Phase**: Select the phase of the plans being released (e.g., Schematic design, Design development, etc.).
* **Description of Plans Being Released**: Enter a brief description of the plans being released.
* **Number of Drawings in Set**: Enter the total number of drawings included in the release set.
* **Design is approved for release**: Mark True if the design is approved for release, False otherwise.
* **Owner or Client approval received**: Mark True if approval has been received, False if not.
* **Building Authority approvals obtained**: Mark True if obtained, False if not.
* **Authorized Recipients**: List the names and titles of those authorized to receive the plans.
* **Distribution Method**: Select the method for distributing the plans (e.g., Email, Physical copies, etc.).
* **Date of Release Authorization**: Enter the date the plans are authorized for release.
* **Authorized Signatory Name and Title**: Enter the name and title of the person authorizing the release.

Note: This guide is meant to be a basic understanding of the form, if you need further clarification or details, please contact your project manager or the relevant personnel.
