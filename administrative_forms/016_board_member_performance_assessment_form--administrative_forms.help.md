# Board Member Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Member Performance Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the performance of board members in various areas, providing an objective and data-driven assessment of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Board Member's Name and Reviewer's Name in the respective fields.
2. Select the assessment period for which you are evaluating the board member's performance (e.g., Q1 2026, Annual 2025).
3. Answer the questions by rating the board member's performance in each category using the provided scales.
4. Provide additional comments or recommendations for the board member's next term.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Board Member Name** (`board_member_name`): Enter the name of the board member being assessed.
* **Assessment Period** (`assessment_period`): Select the time period being evaluated (e.g., Q1 2026, Annual 2025).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter the name of the person completing the assessment.
* **Reviewer Relationship** (`reviewer_relationship`): Choose your relationship to the board member (e.g., Board Chair, Fellow Board Member).
* **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`): Rate the board member's attendance at board meetings (Excellent - Attended all meetings, Good - Attended most meetings, etc.).
* **Meeting Preparation** (`meeting_preparation`): Rate the board member's level of preparation for meetings (Excellent - Always well prepared, Good - Usually prepared, etc.).
* **Participation in Discussions** (`participation_in_discussions`): Rate the board member's quality of participation in discussions (Excellent - Actively contributes, Good - Regularly contributes, etc.).
* **Committee Participation** (`committee_participation`): Rate the board member's involvement in committees (Excellent - Highly active, Good - Regularly participates, etc.).
* **Strategic Thinking** (`strategic_thinking`): Rate the board member's strategic contribution (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Financial Oversight** (`financial_oversight`): Rate the board member's financial stewardship (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Fundraising Contribution** (`fundraising_contribution`): Rate the board member's fundraising efforts (Excellent - Exceeds expectations, Good - Meets expectations, etc.).
* **Community Representation** (`community_representation`): Rate the board member's representation of stakeholders (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Ethical Conduct** (`ethical_conduct`): Rate the board member's adherence to ethics (Excellent - Always demonstrates integrity, Good - Usually demonstrates integrity, etc.).
* **Collaboration with Staff** (`collaboration_with_staff`): Rate the board member's working relationship with staff (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Overall Effectiveness** (`overall_effectiveness`): Rate the board member's overall performance (1 - Unsatisfactory, 2 - Below Expectations, etc.).
* **Key Contributions** (`key_contributions`): List major contributions made by the board member during the assessment period.
* **Areas for Development** (`areas_for_development`): Identify areas where the board member needs improvement.
* **Recommendation for Next Term** (`recommendation`): Provide a recommendation for the board member's next term (Strongly recommend reappointment, Recommend reappointment, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional observations or comments about the board member's performance.
