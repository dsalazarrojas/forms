# Grocery Shopping Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tracking grocery shopping activities, including shopping lists, dates, times, stores, payment methods, notes, and contact names.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shopping list in the Shopping List field.
2. Select the date of your grocery shopping trip in the Date field.
3. Enter the time of day when you went shopping in the Time of Day field.
4. Choose the name of the store where you shopped from the Shopping Store dropdown menu.
5. Select the payment method you used for your purchase (e.g. cash, credit card, etc.).
6. Enter any additional notes or comments about your shopping trip in the Notes field.
7. Enter your contact name if you are a store associate or staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shopping List** (`shopping_list`, text): Enter a list of the items you purchased during your grocery shopping trip.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Select the date of your grocery shopping trip.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time): Enter the time of day when you went shopping.
* **Shopping Store** (`shopping_store`, select_one): Choose the store where you shopped from the dropdown menu.
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple): Select all payment methods you used for your purchase.
  * Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your shopping trip.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your contact name if you are a store associate or staff member.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required information for accurate data collection.
* If you are unsure about a field, ask your supervisor or store manager for clarification.
* The form is designed to be completed for each grocery shopping trip, so please complete it for each trip separately.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your store manager or administrator.
