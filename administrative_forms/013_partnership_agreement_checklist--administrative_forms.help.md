# Partnership Agreement Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a standardized checklist to collect information about a potential partner or collaborator for a business partnership. It is intended to be used during the partnership onboarding process to ensure all necessary details are captured and documented.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Partner Name in the text field provided.
2. Provide the Partner Address in the text field provided.
3. Select the Partnership Start Date using the date selector or type in the date manually.
4. If applicable, enter the Partner Phone number in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the partner or collaborator.
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the partner or collaborator.
* **Partnership Start Date** (`partnership_start_date`, Date, Optional): Select or enter the start date of the partnership. This is the date when the partnership was established.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the phone number of the partner or collaborator.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurately and completely filled out to ensure accurate documentation.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant team members or stakeholders before submitting the form.
* This form is intended to be used for internal purposes only and should not be shared with external parties.
