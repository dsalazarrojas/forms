# Utility Service Termination Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for customers who wish to terminate their utility service. It is essential to understand that by filling out this form, you are providing consent to discontinue your service. Please ensure that you have carefully reviewed your service contract and understand the implications before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Provide your customer details accurately and completely.
3. Select the type of utility service you wish to terminate.
4. Enter the start date of your service.
5. Specify the reason for termination.
6. Enter the date when you wish to terminate the service.
7. Confirm the last payment amount, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required/optional): This is the section where you can enter your customer information.
* **Utility Type** (`utility_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of utility service you wish to terminate from the options provided (Electric Service, Gas Service, or Water Service).
* **Service Start Date** (`service_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your service started.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, required/optional): Specify the reason you are terminating your service (e.g. moving, financial difficulties, etc.).
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you plan to terminate your service.
* **Last Payment Amount** (`last_payment_amount`, number, required/optional): If you have made any payments, enter the amount of the last payment made.

## Tips
- Double-check that all information is accurate before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please contact your service provider.
- This form is for utility service termination purposes only; please do not use for other types of service changes or requests.
