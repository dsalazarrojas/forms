# Refund Request Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Request Verification Form is used to verify and process refund requests from customers. This form helps us to quickly and efficiently process your refund request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the reason for your refund from the "Refund Reason" dropdown menu.
4. Attach any required supporting documents as needed.
5. Enter your order details, including the order number, order date, and order total.
6. Enter the amount you are requesting for refund.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Reason for Refund" field.
8. Select the status of your refund from the "Refund Status" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments for our support team in the "Notes" field.
10. Select who should be assigned to process your refund from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name (customer_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your valid email address associated with your order.
* **Refund Reason (refund_reason)**: Select the reason for your refund from the dropdown menu.
	+ Refund Requested: You are requesting a refund.
	+ Refund Not Received: You have not received your order or refund.
	+ Other: Select this option if your reason for refund does not match the above options.
* **Supporting Documents (supporting_documents)**: Attach any documents that support your refund request, such as proof of delivery or return.
* **Customer Order Number (customer_order_number)**: Enter your order number as it appears on your order confirmation email or receipt.
* **Order Date (order_date)**: Enter the date you placed your order.
* **Order Total (order_total)**: Enter the total amount you paid for your order.
* **Refund Amount (refund_amount)**: Enter the amount you are requesting for refund.
* **Reason for Refund (reason_for_refund)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your refund request.
* **Refund Status (refund_status)**: Select the status of your refund from the dropdown menu.
	+ Approved: Your refund has been approved.
	+ Denied: Your refund has been denied.
* **Notes (notes)**: Enter any additional comments or notes for our support team.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: Select who should be assigned to process your refund from the dropdown menu.
	+ chatjimmy: Assign to chatjimmy.
	+ Other: Select this option if you want to assign someone else.
* **Customer Phone (customer_phone)**: Enter your phone number for our support team to contact you.
* **Customer Address (customer_address)**: Enter your mailing address for our support team to contact you.
* **Supporting Document Description (supporting_document_description)**: Enter a description of the supporting document you attached.
* **Customer Note (customer_note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments for our support team.
* **Order Date Due (order_date_due)**: Enter the date your order was due.
* **Order Total Paid (order_total_paid)**: Enter the total amount you paid for your order.
* **Refund Total (refund_total)**: Enter the total amount of your refund.
* **Reason for Refund Total (reason_for_refund_total)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your refund total.
* **Notes By Customer (notes_by_customer)**: Enter any additional notes or comments for our support team.
* **Customer Order Details (customer_order_details)**: Enter any additional details about your order.
* **Customer Service Notes (customer_service_notes)**: Enter any additional notes or comments for our support team.
* **Order Number (order_number)**: Enter your order number as it appears on your order confirmation email or receipt.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)**: Select who should be assigned to process your refund from the dropdown menu.

Note: Some fields are not required and are optional for you to complete.
