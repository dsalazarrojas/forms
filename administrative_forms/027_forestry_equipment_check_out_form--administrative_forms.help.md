# forestry_equipment_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for checking out forestry equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment being checked out.
2. Enter the equipment size.
3. Enter the quantity of equipment being checked out.
4. Enter the start date and end date of the check out period.
5. Provide a description of the equipment being checked out.
6. Enter the name of the person checking out the equipment.
7. Enter the contact email and phone number of the person checking out the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of forestry equipment being checked out. This could be a chainsaw, a chainsaw sharpener, or any other type of equipment used in forestry activities.
* **Equipment Size**: Enter the size of the equipment being checked out.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units being checked out. For example, if you are checking out 5 chainsaws, enter 5.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when you plan to start using the equipment.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when you plan to return the equipment.
* **Equipment Description**: Provide a brief description of the equipment being checked out, including its condition and any special instructions for use.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person checking out the equipment. This should be the name of the person who is physically taking possession of the equipment.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person checking out the equipment. This could be their work email or personal email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person checking out the equipment. This should be their cell phone or office phone number.
