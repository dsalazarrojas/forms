# Auditor Independence Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify the independence of auditors and auditing firms. It ensures that the auditors are not influenced by any factors that could compromise their impartiality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the independence verification.
2. Provide your full name as an auditor.
3. Enter your professional license number (e.g., CPA).
4. Enter the name of the auditing firm.
5. Enter the firm's registration number (if applicable).
6. Enter the name of the organization being audited.
7. Enter the dates of the audit engagement.
8. Select 'None' for any prior relationships with the client (e.g., if you've never worked with the client before).
9. Select 'No Interest' if you have no financial interest in the client (e.g., you don't own stock or have a financial stake).
10. Select 'None' for any material indirect financial interest.
11. Select 'No' for family members employed by the client (e.g., if no family members work for the client).
12. Select 'No' for ever held a key position at the client.
13. Select 'No' for a contingent fee arrangement (e.g., if your fee is not contingent on the audit results).
14. Enter the total audit fee amount (if applicable).
15. Enter the percentage of the firm's revenue as a fee (if applicable).
16. Select 'None' for gifts or benefits from the client (e.g., if you've received no gifts or benefits).
17. Select 'None' for loans to or from the client (e.g., if you've received no loans).
18. Select 'None' for other business relationships with the client (e.g., if you've never done business with the client before).
19. Select the risk level for advocacy threat (e.g., 'None Identified', 'Minimal Risk', etc.).
20. Select the risk level for familiarity threat (e.g., 'None Identified', 'Minimal Risk', etc.).
21. Enter the steps taken to mitigate threats (if applicable).
22. Select 'Auditor is Independent' to confirm your independence.
23. Enter the name of the person verifying your independence.
24. Enter your title as the verifier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Verification Date** (`verification_date`): Enter the date of the independence verification.
* **Auditor Full Name** (`auditor_name`): Enter your full legal name as an auditor.
* **Professional License Number** (`auditor_license_number`): Enter your professional license number (e.g., CPA).
* **Auditing Firm Name** (`auditing_firm_name`): Enter the name of the auditing firm.
* **Firm Registration Number** (`firm_registration_number`): Enter the firm's registration number (if applicable).
* **Audit Client Name** (`audit_client_name`): Enter the name of the organization being audited.
* **Audit Period** (`audit_period`): Enter the dates of the audit engagement.
* **Prior Relationships with Client** (`prior_relationships`): Select 'None' if you've never worked with the client before.
* **Financial Interest in Client** (`financial_interest`): Select 'No Interest' if you have no financial interest in the client.
* **Material Indirect Financial Interest** (`material_indirect_interest`): Select 'None' for any material indirect financial interest.
* **Family Member Employment at Client** (`family_employment`): Select 'No' if no family members are employed by the client.
* **Ever Held Key Position at Client** (`key_position_held`): Select 'No' if you've never held a key position at the client.
* **Contingent Fee Arrangement** (`contingent_fee_arrangement`): Select 'No - Fixed Fee' if your fee is not contingent on the audit results.
* **Total Audit Fee Amount** (`audit_fee_amount`): Enter the estimated or agreed fee amount (if applicable).
* **Fee as Percentage of Total Firm Revenue** (`fee_as_percentage`): Enter the percentage of the firm's revenue as a fee (if applicable).
* **Gifts or Benefits from Client** (`gift_receipt`): Select 'None' for no gifts or benefits from the client.
* **Loans to or from Client** (`loan_arrangement`): Select 'None' for no loans to or from the client.
* **Other Business Relationship** (`business_relationship`): Select 'None' for no other business relationship with the client.
* **Advocacy Threat Identified** (`advocacy_threat`): Select the risk level for advocacy threat (e.g., 'None Identified', 'Minimal Risk', etc.).
* **Familiarity Threat Risk** (`familiarity_threat`): Select the risk level for familiarity threat (e.g., 'None Identified', 'Minimal Risk', etc.).
* **Safeguards Implemented** (`safeguards_implemented`): Enter the steps taken to mitigate threats (if applicable).
* **Independence Confirmation** (`confirmation_statement`): Select 'Auditor is Independent' to confirm your independence.
* **Verification Completed By** (`verifier_name`): Enter the name of the person verifying your independence.
* **Verifier Title** (`verifier_title`): Enter your title as the verifier.
