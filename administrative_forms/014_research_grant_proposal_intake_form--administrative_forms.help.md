# Research Grant Proposal Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Research Grant Proposal Intake Form is used to collect information from researchers about their grant proposals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the form with the necessary details about your research grant proposal.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for funding sources and funding breakdowns.
4. Enter your collaborators' names, roles, and contact information.
5. Provide details about the institutional review and contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Grant Proposal Intake Form Data**: This field is used to provide a brief description of the research grant proposal. Please provide a clear and concise summary of your project.
* **Research Grant Intake Form Funding Requirements**: Describe the funding requirements for your research grant proposal.
* **Research Grant Intake Form Project Details**: Enter a detailed description of your research project.
* **Research Grant Intake Form Project Budget**: Enter your project budget.
* **Project Budget Breakdown**: Enter a breakdown of your project budget, including costs and funding allocations.
* **Funding Period**: Enter the duration of the funding for your project.
* **Project Funding**: Select the funding sources for your research grant proposal.
* **Funding Breakdown**: Select the breakdown of funding allocations for your research grant proposal.
* **Collaborators**: List the names of your collaborators.
* **Collaborators Name**: Enter the name of your collaborator.
* **Collaborators Role**: Enter the role of your collaborator.
* **Collaborators Email**: Enter the email address of your collaborator.
* **Collaborators Phone**: Enter the phone number of your collaborator.
* **Collaborators Address**: Enter the address of your collaborator.
* **Institutional Review**: Enter a description of the institutional review process.
* **Institutional Review 1**: Enter the name of the institution.
* **Institutional Review 2**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Institutional Review 3**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Institutional Review 4**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Institutional Review 5**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all the required information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Make sure to proofread your form before submission to avoid errors.
