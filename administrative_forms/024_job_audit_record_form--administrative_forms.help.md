# Job Audit Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Audit Record Form is a tool to evaluate and document employee performance. It helps supervisors and other authorized personnel in conducting regular audits and providing feedback to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the employee you are evaluating from the dropdown list on the first page.
* Enter a brief description of the employee's job performance on the "Job Performance" page.
* Provide any feedback or comments on the "Feedback" page.
* Enter the date and time of the visit on the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" pages, if applicable.
* Evaluate the employee's performance level on the "Performance Level" page using the options "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* If applicable, suggest recommended actions to take on the "Recommended Actions" page.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee (page 1)
* **Employee** (`select_one`, required): Select the employee you are evaluating from the list of available options. This is the first step in filling out the form and helps ensure that the correct employee's performance is being evaluated.

### Job Performance (page 2)
* **Job Performance** (`text`, required): Provide a brief description of the employee's job performance. This can include any notable achievements, areas of improvement, and any other relevant information.

### Feedback (page 3)
* **Feedback** (`note`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments on the employee's performance. This can include suggestions for improvement or anything else that might be helpful to the employee.

### Date of Visit (page 4)
* **Date of Visit** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the visit or assessment. This is useful for tracking historical performance data.

### Time of Visit (page 5)
* **Time of Visit** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the visit or assessment. This can be helpful for scheduling follow-up meetings or evaluations.

### Performance Level (page 6)
* **Performance Level** (`select_one`, optional): Evaluate the employee's performance level using the options "High", "Medium", or "Low". This helps identify areas where the employee needs improvement.

### Recommended Actions (page 7)
* **Recommended Actions** (`select_multiple`, optional): Suggest any recommended actions to take on the employee's performance. This can include training, mentorship, or other developmental opportunities.

### Comments (page 8)
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts on the employee's performance. This can be a space for further feedback or suggestions.

### Recommended Actions (page 9)
* **Recommended Actions** (`select_multiple`, optional): Suggest any recommended actions to take on the employee's performance. This can include training, mentorship, or other developmental opportunities. 

Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
