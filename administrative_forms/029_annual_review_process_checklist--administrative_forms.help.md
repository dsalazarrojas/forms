# Annual Review Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the annual review process, ensuring that all necessary steps are taken in a timely and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information for your review cycle.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that you are accurately reflecting the current status of the review process.
3. If a field is marked as "Not started" or "Not required", you can leave it blank or select the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department undergoing the review process.
* **Review Coordinator Name** (`review_coordinator`, text, required): Enter the name of the person managing the review process.
* **Coordinator Email** (`coordinator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the review coordinator.
* **Annual Review Period** (`review_period`, text, required): Enter the dates covered by this review cycle (e.g., January 1 - December 31).
* **Review Process Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the review cycle began.
* **Completion Deadline** (`completion_deadline`, date, required): Enter the target date for all reviews to be complete.
* **Employee notification sent** (`employee_notification_sent`, select_one, required): Select the status of employee notifications (Completed, In progress, Not started, or N/A).
* **Notification Date** (`notification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when employees were notified.
* **Manager training completed** (`manager_training_completed`, select_one, required): Select the status of manager training on the review process (Completed, In progress, Not started, or N/A).
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when manager training occurred.
* **Employee self-evaluations collected** (`self_evaluations_collected`, select_one, required): Select the status of employee self-evaluations (Completed, In progress, Not started, or Not required).
* **Self-evaluation deadline** (`self_eval_submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when employee self-evaluations were due.
* **Peer feedback requested** (`peer_feedback_requested`, select_one, required): Select the status of peer feedback (Completed, In progress, Not started, or Not required).
* **Peer feedback collection date** (`peer_feedback_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when peer feedback was requested.
* **Manager evaluations started** (`manager_evaluations_started`, select_one, required): Select the status of manager evaluations (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **Evaluation start date** (`evaluation_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when managers started evaluations.
* **Direct report feedback collected** (`direct_report_feedback_collected`, select_one, required): Select the status of direct report feedback (Completed, In progress, Not started, or Not required).
* **Feedback deadline** (`feedback_deadline`, date, optional): Enter the date when direct report feedback was due.
* **Calibration sessions held** (`calibration_sessions_held`, select_one, required): Select the status of calibration sessions (Completed, In progress, Not started, or Not planned).
* **Calibration session date** (`calibration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when calibration sessions occurred.
* **Review meetings scheduled** (`review_meetings_scheduled`, select_one, required): Select the status of review meetings (All scheduled, Most scheduled, In progress, or Not started).
* **Review meetings completed** (`review_meetings_completed`, select_one, required): Select the status of review meetings (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **Meetings completion date** (`meeting_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when review meetings were finished.
* **Documentation submitted to HR** (`documentation_submitted`, select_one, required): Select the status of documentation submission (Completed, In progress, Not started).
* **Final approvals obtained** (`final_approval_obtained`, select_one, required): Select the status of final approvals (Completed, In progress, Not started, or Not required).

## Tips
* Ensure that all stakeholders are informed and engaged throughout the review process.

* Review this form regularly to ensure that progress is on track and deadlines are being met.
