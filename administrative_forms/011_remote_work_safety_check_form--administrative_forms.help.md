# Remote Work Safety Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess an employee's safety protocols for remote work and ensure they are following the company's safety guidelines while working outside of the office.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and materials before completing this form.
2. Answer each question carefully, as it will be reviewed by the management team.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required: false): Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **workspace_type** (`workspace_type`, select_one, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, required: false): Select the type of workspace you will be working in (e.g., home, coffee shop, co-working space).
* **Work Safety Risk** (`work_safety_risk`, select_multiple, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, required: false): Check the safety risks you will be exposed to while working remotely (e.g., electrical hazards, falls, sharp objects).
* **Safety Equipment** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, required: false): Select the safety equipment you will be using while working remotely (e.g., first aid kit, fire extinguisher, personal protective equipment).
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_multiple, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, required: false): Check the safety training you have received while working remotely (e.g., basic first aid, electrical safety, confined space safety).
* **Remote Work Location** (`remote_work_location`, select_one, options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, required: false): Select the location where you will be working remotely (e.g., home, public park, coffee shop).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Remote Work Start Time** (`remote_work_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your remote work session.

Note: This form is for your safety and well-being, please answer honestly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor.
