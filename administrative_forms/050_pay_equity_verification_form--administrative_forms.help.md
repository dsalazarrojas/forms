# Pay Equity Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pay Equity Verification Form is used to collect and analyze data on employee compensation to ensure transparency and fairness in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant fields you wish to input information for.
2. Fill in the provided fields with accurate information.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
4. Submit the form for review and analysis.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Job Role** (`role`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the job role of the employee.
* **Base Salary** (`base_salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the base salary of the employee.
* **Bonus** (`bonus`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the bonus paid to the employee.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `note`, required/optional): Describe the benefits provided to the employee.
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of employees in the organization.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the gender of the employee.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of the employee.
* **Salary Frequency** (`salary_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the frequency of salary payment (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the range of the employee's salary (Low, Medium, High, Very High).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of job (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the job (Active, Inactive, Retired).
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the classification of the job.
* **Average Salary** (`average_salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the average salary of employees in the organization.
* **Average Bonus** (`average_bonus`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the average bonus paid to employees in the organization.
* **Average Benefits** (`average_benefits`, `note`, required/optional): Describe the average benefits provided to employees in the organization.
* **Average Employee Count** (`average_employee_count`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the average number of employees in the organization.
* **Average Gender** (`average_gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the average gender of employees in the organization.
* **Average Department** (`average_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the average department of employees in the organization.
* **Average Salary Frequency** (`average_salary_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the average frequency of salary payment (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Average Salary Range** (`average_salary_range`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the range of the average employee's salary (Low, Medium, High, Very High).
* **Average Job Type** (`average_job_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the average type of job (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract).
* **Average Job Status** (`average_job_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the average status of the job (Active, Inactive, Retired).
* **Average Job Classification** (`average_job_classification`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the average classification of the job.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information before filling out the form.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the relevant authorities.
- Submitting the form multiple times with minor changes may lead to unnecessary analysis and confusion.
- If you have already submitted a similar form, please review the existing data to avoid duplication of effort.
