# Career Development Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Career Development Checklist Form is a tool for employees to reflect on their career goals, assess their skills, and identify areas for improvement. It helps employees create a personalized career development plan, set realistic objectives, and seek necessary support and resources to achieve their career aspirations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the sections below, answering questions in each part of the form.
2.  Complete all required fields.
3.  Review and update your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name

*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name.
    *   Required field.
    *   Please enter your official name as per company records.

### 2. Employee ID

*   **Employee ID**: Enter your official company ID.
    *   Required field.
    *   Please enter the ID issued by the company for administrative purposes.

### 3. Current Department

*   **Current Department**: Select the department where you currently work.
    *   Required field.
    *   Choose the department from the list of available options.

### 4. Current Job Title

*   **Current Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
    *   Required field.
    *   Please enter the title assigned to you by the company.

### 5. Section Goal Setting

### 6. What is your primary career goal?

*   **What is your primary career goal?**: Describe your career objective.
    *   Required field.
    *   Be as specific as possible when outlining your aspirations.

### 7. Target role for advancement

*   **Target role for advancement**: Describe your target role.
    *   Required field.
    *   This should be based on your career goal and current job title.

### 8. When do you want to achieve this goal?

*   **When do you want to achieve this goal?**: Choose the time frame for achieving your goal.
    *   Required field.
    *   Select from available options.

### 9. Section Skills Assessment

### 10. Technical skills needed for advancement

*   **Technical skills needed for advancement**: Select skills required for your target role.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Choose all applicable skills from the list.

### 11. Soft skills needed for advancement

*   **Soft skills needed for advancement**: Select the most critical soft skills required.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Choose the skills from the list that are most relevant to your target role.

### 12. Current skills self-rating

*   **Current skills self-rating (1-10)**: Rate your current skills.
    *   Required field.
    *   Rate your overall skill set from 1-10.

### 13. Section Progress Tracking

### 14. Certifications completed this year

*   **Certifications completed this year**: List any professional certifications or courses completed.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Include any relevant certifications or courses completed this year.

### 15. Training hours logged this quarter

*   **Training hours logged this quarter**: Enter the total hours spent in formal training.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Record the hours spent in formal training this quarter.

### 16. Do you have a mentor?

*   **Do you have a mentor?**: Check if you have a mentor.
    *   Required field.
    *   Answer with "True" or "False".

### 17. What resources do you need for career advancement?

*   **What resources do you need for career advancement?**: List the resources needed.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Mention any additional resources you need to achieve your career goals.

### 18. How can your supervisor better support your career development?

*   **How can your supervisor better support your career development?**: Suggest specific ways.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Provide specific suggestions on how your supervisor can support you.

### 19. Planned date for next progress review

*   **Planned date for next progress review**: Enter the planned review date.
    *   Not required field.
    *   Choose a specific date for your next review.

### 20. Section Agreement

### 21. I agree to follow the development plan

*   **I agree to follow the development plan**: Check if you agree.
    *   Required field.
    *   Confirm your agreement to follow the development plan.

## Tips

*   Answer questions honestly and accurately.
*   Provide specific details whenever possible.
*   Review and revise your answers as needed.
*   Discuss your completed form with your supervisor or HR representative.
