# Store Employee Termination Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of an employee termination, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the employee's name**: Type the full name of the employee being terminated in the "Employee Name" field.
2. **Enter the termination date**: Type the date of the employee's termination in the "Termination Date" field.
3. **Enter the reason for termination**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the employee's termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
4. **Enter the employee's ID**: Type the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
5. **Select the notice period**: Choose the notice period for the termination from the options provided in the "Notice Period" field.
6. **Enter the separation package information**: Provide the details of the separation package being offered to the terminated employee in the "Separation Package" field.
7. **Specify outplacement services**: Check the box to indicate whether outplacement services will be provided to the terminated employee in the "Outplacement Services" field.
8. **Select the termination reason**: Choose the reason for the termination from the options provided in the "Termination Reason" field.
9. **Enter the last day worked**: Type the date of the last day the employee worked in the "Last Day Worked" field.
10. **Select the notice end date**: Choose the date when the notice period ends in the "Notice End Date" field.
11. **Enter separation allowances information**: Provide the details of the separation allowances being offered to the terminated employee in the "Separation Allowances" field.
12. **Specify company ownership**: Check the box to indicate whether the company still owns any assets or property associated with the terminated employee in the "Company Ownership" field.
13. **Enter the final pay**: Type the final pay amount for the terminated employee in the "Final Pay" field.
14. **Select the notice period end date 2**: Choose the date when the notice period ends for the second termination in the "Notice Period End Date" field.
15. **Enter the outplacement services provided**: Check the box to indicate whether outplacement services are being provided to the terminated employee in the "Outplacement Services Provided" field.
16. **Enter separation allowances 2 information**: Provide the details of the separation allowances being offered to the terminated employee in the "Separation Allowances 2" field.
17. **Select the notice period 2**: Choose the notice period for the second termination from the options provided in the "Notice Period 2" field.
18. **Enter the termination date 2**: Type the date of the second termination in the "Termination Date 2" field.
19. **Enter the termination reason 2**: Choose the reason for the second termination from the options provided in the "Termination Reason 2" field.
20. **Enter the last day worked 2**: Type the date of the last day the employee worked in the "Last Day Worked 2" field.
21. **Enter the separation package 2 information**: Provide the details of the separation package being offered to the terminated employee in the "Separation Package 2" field.
