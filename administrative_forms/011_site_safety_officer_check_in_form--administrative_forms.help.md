# Site Safety Officer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid site safety officers in checking in on their presence on site, providing essential information for site managers, administrators, or relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information in the fields provided.
2. Select the "Check In Time" field using the provided clock icon to select the correct time of arrival.
3. Choose the "Check In Status" field to indicate your current status (Checked In, Checked Out, or Checked In Late).
4. Enter any relevant comments or safety concerns in the "Comments" field.
5. If applicable, provide any site-specific information or conditions that may be relevant to your presence on site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Check In** (Text, Optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your presence on site.
- **Check In Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your arrival on site.
- **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): If you need to be contacted regarding any issues or concerns, provide your contact information here.
- **Site Location** (Text, Optional): Provide the specific location of your site or workplace.
- **Check In Status** (Select One, Optional): Choose from the options:
	+ Checked In: I am currently on site.
	+ Checked Out: I am leaving the site.
	+ Checked In Late: I am late in arriving on site.
- **Safety Concerns** (Text, Optional): If you encounter any safety concerns or issues, describe them here.
- **Comments** (Text, Optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback that may be helpful to site administrators or relevant authorities.
- **Site Conditions** (Text, Optional): If applicable, provide any site-specific information or conditions that may be relevant to your presence on site.
- **Check In Time** (Time, Optional): Select the exact time of your arrival or departure on site using the clock icon.
- **Check In Status** (Select One, Optional): Please see the "Check In Status" explanation above.
