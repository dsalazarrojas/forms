# loan_underwriter_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new loan underwriters to provide their qualifications and onboarding status. It helps to track and manage the onboarding process of loan underwriters, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the New Loan Underwriter Form.
2. Select your Loan Underwriting Qualifications.
3. Enter your Years of Experience.
4. Choose your Certification status.
5. Select your Underwriting Training status.
6. Choose your Onboarding Status.
7. Confirm if your Onboarding has been Completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Loan Underwriter Form** (`new_loan_underwriter_form`, `note`, required: false): This section is a general information form for new loan underwriters. Please provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your onboarding process.
* **Loan Underwriting Qualifications** (`loan_underwriting_qualifications`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you have loan underwriting qualifications or not.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have loan underwriting qualifications.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have loan underwriting qualifications.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of years of experience you have in loan underwriting.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your certification status.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have been certified in loan underwriting.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not been certified.
* **Underwriting Training** (`underwriting_training`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your underwriting training status.
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have received underwriting training.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not received underwriting training.
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your onboarding status.
	+ Select 'Active' if you are currently onboarding.
	+ Select 'Inactive' if you are not onboarding.
* **Onboarding Completed** (`onboarding_completed`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm if your onboarding is completed.
	+ Select 'Yes' if your onboarding is completed.
	+ Select 'No' if your onboarding is not completed.
