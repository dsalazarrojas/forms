# playground_equipment_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about complaints related to playground equipment. The purpose of this form is to gather details about problems or issues users experience with playground equipment, such as broken or damaged items, and to report them to the relevant authorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "User Information" field.
2. Describe the problem with the equipment in the "Problem with Equipment" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Description of Problem" field.
4. Choose the date you reported the issue in the "Report Date" field.
5. Specify the location where the equipment is currently located in the "Equipment Location" field.
6. Select the type of equipment that is experiencing the problem from the options provided in the "Equipment Type" field.
7. Indicate the current condition of the equipment in the "Equipment Condition" field.
8. Attach any photos or videos that demonstrate the issue in the "Attach Photos" field.
9. Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text)** (Required/Optional): Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you to follow up on your complaint.
* **Problem with Equipment (text)** (Required/Optional): Describe the problem you are experiencing with the equipment, such as a broken or damaged item.
* **Description of Problem (text)** (Required/Optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any relevant details or circumstances surrounding the problem.
* **Report Date (date)** (Required/Optional): Choose the date you reported the issue, as this will help us track the timeline of your complaint.
* **Equipment Location (text)** (Required/Optional): Specify the location where the equipment is currently located, such as a specific playground or park.
* **Equipment Type (text)** (Required/Optional): Select the type of equipment that is experiencing the problem from the options provided.
* **Equipment Condition (select_one)** (Required/Optional): Choose the current condition of the equipment, such as Broken, Damaged, or Intact.
* **Attach Photos (text)** (Required/Optional): Attach any photos or videos that demonstrate the issue, such as photos of the damaged equipment.
* **Submit (text)** (Required/Optional): Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click this button to submit your complaint.

## Tips
* Please be as descriptive as possible when filling out the form, especially in the "Description of Problem" field.
* Attach photos or videos that clearly demonstrate the issue, such as before and after photos of the damage.
* If you are reporting a problem with multiple equipment items, please fill out the form for each item separately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to assist you.
