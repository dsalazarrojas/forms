# policy_violation_incident_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The policy_violation_incident_form is a tool used to gather information and track incidents of policy non-compliance or breaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the date and time of the incident.
2. Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant facts and circumstances.
3. Select the location where the incident occurred.
4. Indicate the type of incident that occurred.
5. Update the status of the incident.
6. Set a priority level for the incident resolution.
7. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
8. Enter the name of the person investigating the incident.
9. Provide any additional information relevant to the incident, if needed.
10. Enter any follow-up activities related to the incident.
11. Enter the date and time of the incident resolution.
12. Select the location where the incident was resolved.
13. Indicate the type of resolution that was applied.
14. Enter the status of the resolution.
15. Set a priority level for the resolution.
16. Enter the name of the person who resolved the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Date** (1, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (2, `time`, required): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (3, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Incident Location** (4, `select_one`, optional): Select the location where the incident took place.
* **Incident Type** (5, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the type of incident that occurred.
* **Incident Status** (6, `select_one`, optional): Update the status of the incident.
* **Incident Priority** (7, `number`, optional): Set a priority level for the incident resolution.
* **Incident Reporter** (8, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Investigator** (9, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person investigating the incident.
* **Incident Resolution** (10, `select_one`, required): Select the resolution type for the incident.
* **Incident Note** (11, `text`, required): Provide any additional information relevant to the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (12, `text`, optional): If relevant, provide a witness statement.
* **Incident Photo** (13, `text`, optional): If applicable, upload a photo related to the incident.
* **Incident Video** (14, `text`, optional): If applicable, upload a video related to the incident.
* **Incident Audio** (15, `text`, optional): If applicable, upload audio related to the incident.
* **Incident Document** (16, `text`, optional): If applicable, upload a document related to the incident.
* **Incident Additional Info** (17, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information related to the incident.
* **Incident Follow-up** (18, `date`, optional): Enter any follow-up activities related to the incident.
* **Incident Resolution Date** (19, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident resolution.
* **Incident Resolution Time** (20, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the incident resolution.
* **Incident Resolution Location** (21, `select_one`, optional): Select the location where the incident was resolved.
* **Incident Resolution Type** (22, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the type of resolution applied.
* **Incident Resolution Status** (23, `select_one`, optional): Enter the status of the resolution.
* **Incident Resolution Priority** (24, `number`, optional): Set a priority level for the resolution.
* **Incident Resolution Reporter** (25, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who resolved the incident.
