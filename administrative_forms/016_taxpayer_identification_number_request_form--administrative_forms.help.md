# Taxpayer Identification Number Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taxpayer Identification Number Request Form is used to collect essential information from taxpayers for identification and verification purposes. This form helps to ensure that the correct tax identification number is associated with the correct individual or entity. It is also used to check for any existing taxpayer identifications in our database to prevent duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **Taxpayer Identification Number**: Enter your Taxpayer Identification Number in this field.
* **Select 1**: Choose one of the options provided for this field.
* **Select 2**: Choose one or more of the options provided for this field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Date of Death**: Enter your date of death in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or comments about your request in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your responses for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation above for clarification.
