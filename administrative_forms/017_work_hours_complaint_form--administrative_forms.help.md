# Work Hours Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about work hours complaints or issues that employees may have. It helps the HR department track and address these concerns, ensuring they are heard and dealt with efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide as much detail as possible about the issue or complaint you're submitting.
2. Enter the date you're reporting the issue.
3. Describe the issue or complaint in the text box.
4. If you're not using your work email, provide your personal contact email.
5. If you're not using your work phone, provide your personal contact phone number.
6. Select the tool you'd like to use to report the issue (e.g., "Work Hours Complaint" or "Other").
7. If you've selected "Other", specify the tool you'd like to use in the text box.
8. Select the category that best describes your issue (e.g., "Work-Life Balance", "Compensation", or "Work Environment").
9. Choose the priority level of your issue (e.g., "Low", "Medium", or "High").
10. Select the status of your issue (e.g., "Open", "Pending", or "Resolved").
11. Choose who submitted the issue (e.g., "Employee", "Manager", or "HR").
12. Choose who the issue was submitted to (e.g., "HR Department" or "Manager").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide as much information as possible about the issue or complaint you're reporting.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you're reporting the issue.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Describe the issue or complaint in detail.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): If you're not using your work email, enter your personal contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you're not using your work phone, enter your personal contact phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool you'd like to use to report the issue (e.g., "Work Hours Complaint" or "Other").
* **Other Tool** (`assigned_tool_other`, text, optional): If you've selected "Other", specify the tool you'd like to use in this text box.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category that best describes your issue (e.g., "Work-Life Balance", "Compensation", or "Work Environment").
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority level of your issue (e.g., "Low", "Medium", or "High").
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your issue (e.g., "Open", "Pending", or "Resolved").
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Choose who submitted the issue (e.g., "Employee", "Manager", or "HR").
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the issue was submitted to (e.g., "HR Department" or "Manager").
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID.
