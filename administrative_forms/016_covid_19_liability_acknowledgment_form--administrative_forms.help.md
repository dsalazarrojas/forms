# Covid 19 Liability Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Liability Acknowledgment Form is a document used to acknowledge and track participants' adherence to safety protocols and risk statements related to COVID-19 activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their Participant Id, Name, Email, Contact Number, and Address.
2. For each of the Safety Protocols 1 to 5 fields, select "Yes" or "No" based on your adherence to the corresponding safety protocol.
3. Enter your Risk Statement in the text field.
4. Sign your name in the Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Id** (`participant_id`, number, required: false): Enter the participant's unique identifier.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's contact number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Safety Protocols 1** (`safety_protocols_1`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you adhere to the safety protocol 1, "No" otherwise.
* **Safety Protocols 2** (`safety_protocols_2`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you adhere to the safety protocol 2, "No" otherwise.
* **Safety Protocols 3** (`safety_protocols_3`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you adhere to the safety protocol 3, "No" otherwise.
* **Safety Protocols 4** (`safety_protocols_4`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you adhere to the safety protocol 4, "No" otherwise.
* **Safety Protocols 5** (`safety_protocols_5`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you adhere to the safety protocol 5, "No" otherwise.
* **Risk Statement** (`risk_statement`, text, required: false): Enter your risk statement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer truthfully and accurately to all the safety protocol and risk statement fields.
* Please sign the form digitally or physically, depending on your preference.
