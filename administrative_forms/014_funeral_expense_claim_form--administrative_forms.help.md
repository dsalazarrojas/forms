# Funeral Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from the claimant about their funeral expenses. It is used by administrative personnel to process claims and ensure that all required information is provided.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:


* Review the form carefully and ensure you have filled in all relevant information.
* Fill in the User Information section with your details.
* Provide your Claimant Info.
* Fill in the Claim Info, including details about the funeral expenses.
* Enter the Funeral Expenses, detailing the costs incurred.
* Choose the Claim Status to indicate the current status of your claim.
* Enter the Claim Amount, if applicable.
* If necessary, upload a supporting document for your claim.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide your personal details. Please fill in your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Claimant Info** (`claimant_info`, text, optional): Enter your details as the person who is claiming the funeral expenses.
* **Claim Info** (`claim_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the claim, including the date of the funeral, the name of the deceased, and any other relevant information about the funeral.
* **Funeral Expenses** (`funeral_expenses`, text, optional): List the funeral expenses you have incurred, including costs for services, goods, and any other related expenses.
* **Claim Status** (`claim_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of your claim from the following options:
	+ Claimed
	+ Pending
	+ Rejected
* **Claim Amount** (`claim_amount`, number, optional): If you have a claim to make, enter the amount you are claiming.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, text, optional): If necessary, upload a document supporting your claim, such as a receipt or a bill.
