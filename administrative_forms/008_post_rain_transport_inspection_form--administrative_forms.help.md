# post_rain_transport_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The post-rain transport inspection form is used to inspect vehicles after a rainfall event to ensure they are roadworthy and safe for use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including user name, vehicle type, vehicle number, vehicle year, and vehicle make.
2. Provide details about the vehicle condition, odometer reading, safety checkpoints, and vehicle mileage.
3. Select the inspection result and add any additional comments if necessary.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Complete the form carefully and accurately to ensure that the vehicle is properly inspected and cleared for use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: <name>, Enter your name as the person conducting the inspection.
* **Vehicle Type**: <select_one>, Choose the type of vehicle being inspected (e.g., car, truck, bike, etc.).
* **Vehicle Number**: <text>, Enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Year**: <number>, Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: <text>, Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Honda, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: <text>, Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Civic, etc.).
* **Vehicle Color**: <text>, Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition**: <select_multiple>, Select the condition of the vehicle (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* **Odometer Reading**: <number>, Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Safety Checkpoints**: <select_multiple>, Select the safety checkpoints that have been checked (e.g., brakes, steering, etc.).
* **Vehicle Mileage**: <number>, Enter the current vehicle mileage.
* **Inspection Date**: <date>, Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Time of Inspection**: <time>, Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspection Result**: <select_one>, Select the result of the inspection (e.g., pass, fail, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: <note>, Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Email**: <email>, Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: <text>, Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Year 2**: <number>, Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle (if different from the original vehicle).
* **Vehicle Type 2**: <select_one>, Choose the type of vehicle being inspected (if different from the original vehicle).
* **Vehicle Make 2**: <text>, Enter the make of the vehicle (if different from the original vehicle).
* **Vehicle Model 2**: <text>, Enter the model of the vehicle (if different from the original vehicle).
* **Vehicle Condition 2**: <select_multiple>, Select the condition of the vehicle (if different from the original vehicle).
* **Vehicle Number 2**: <text>, Enter the vehicle registration number (if different from the original vehicle).
