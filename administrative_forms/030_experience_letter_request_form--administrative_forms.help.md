# experience_letter_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experience Letter Request Form is used to request an experience letter for an employee. This form helps gather necessary information from the employee to facilitate the process of generating an experience letter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to complete the form.
2. Enter your employee ID, which is a unique identifier for each employee.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
4. Enter your date of joining in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. Select your department from the options provided.
6. Enter your job title.
7. Enter the reason for requesting an experience letter.
8. Enter your manager's name.
9. Enter your reporting manager's name.
10. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your employee ID, a unique identifier for each employee.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Date of Joining (3)**: Enter your date of joining in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Department (4)**: Select your department from the options provided.
* **Job Title (5)**: Enter your job title.
* **Reason for Request (6)**: Enter the reason for requesting an experience letter.
* **Manager Name (7)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager (8)**: Enter your reporting manager's name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to complete this form.
* Double-check that you have entered accurate information to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your HR representative or manager for guidance.
