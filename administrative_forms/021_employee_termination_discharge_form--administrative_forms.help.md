# Employee Termination Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to record the details of an employee termination, which is a formal process that occurs when an employee's employment with the organization comes to an end. This form helps to document the reasons for separation, employee details, and the date of termination to maintain accurate records for HR and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the title of the employee's role or position in the "Employee Title" field.
2. Provide the full name of the employee in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Type in the employee's department in the "Employee Department" field.
4. Enter a brief description of the employee's role or position in the "Employee Position" field.
5. Select the date of termination from the calendar for the "Separation Date" field.
6. Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for the employee's separation in the "Reason for Separation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Title** (employee_title, text, optional): Enter the job title or position of the employee being terminated. This could be a job title, a department name, or any other identifier for the employee's role within the organization.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional): Enter the full name of the employee being terminated. This field helps to confirm the identity of the person being terminated.
* **Employee Department** (employee_department, text, optional): Enter the department or team to which the employee belongs. This information is helpful for tracking and managing team dynamics.
* **Employee Position** (employee_position, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the employee's role or position within the organization. This could include their job description, key responsibilities, or any other relevant details.
* **Separation Date** (separation_date, date, optional): Select the date on which the employee's employment ended. This date should match the actual date of termination, as recorded in any relevant HR or personnel records.
* **Reason for Separation** (reason_for_separation, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason why the employee's employment was terminated. This could include details about performance issues, resignation, retirement, or any other reason for departure.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field to maintain accurate records.
* If an employee has multiple positions within the organization, you may need to complete multiple instances of this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant HR or management personnel for clarification.
