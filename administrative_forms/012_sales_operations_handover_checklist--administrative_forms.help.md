# Sales Operations Handover Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the handover process of sales operations between teams. It ensures that all critical information is captured and shared to guarantee a smooth transition of tasks and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into multiple pages with various fields for you to fill out. Please follow these steps to complete it:

1.  **Sales Operations Handover Checklist (Page 1)**: This page is a brief summary of the sales operations handover. Enter a short description of the current state of the sales operations.
2.  **Client Details (Page 2)**: Enter the client's details, which can be a numerical value (e.g., 12345).
3.  **Sales Details (Page 3)**: Provide a brief description of the sales details.
4.  **Deal Details (Page 4)**: Enter any relevant deal details.
5.  **Account Details (Page 5)**: Enter any account details related to the client.
6.  **Sales Notes (Page 6)**: Add any additional sales-related notes.
7.  **Next Steps (Page 7)**: Describe the next steps to be taken by the receiving team.
