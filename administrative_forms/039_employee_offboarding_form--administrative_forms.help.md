# Employee Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Offboarding Form is designed to collect necessary information from both employees and their managers to facilitate a smooth offboarding process. This form will help maintain clear communication, avoid misunderstandings, and ensure that all necessary steps are taken during the offboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Employee**: Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions asked. Answer each section accurately and thoroughly, providing any necessary details.
2. **Manager**: Review the form with your employee and ensure they understand their responsibilities and commitments after offboarding. Answer each section accurately and thoroughly, providing any necessary details.
3. **Jointly**: Both the employee and manager should review and agree on the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input Page
* **Last Working Date** (`date`, optional): The last working date of the employee.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`text`, optional): The reason for the employee's departure from the company.
* **Next Working Date** (`date`, optional): The date the employee is expected to return to work or the last working date if the employee is not returning.
* **Employee's Agreement** (`text`, optional): The employee's confirmation of their agreement to the terms and conditions of offboarding.

### Manager Input Page
* **Manager's Agreement** (`text`, optional): The manager's confirmation of their agreement to the terms and conditions of offboarding.

### Employee Input Page
* **Notice Period** (`text`, optional): The notice period given to the employee before their departure.
* **Return of Company Property** (`text`, optional): A statement from the employee confirming the return of all company property.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`text`, optional): The employee's confirmation of their commitment to keep confidential company information after leaving the company.
* **Final Check-Out Date** (`date`, optional): The final check-out date of the employee.

## Tips
* Ensure you understand all questions before answering.
* Answer accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form with your manager to ensure you are both on the same page.
* Submit the form jointly to maintain clear communication and avoid misunderstandings.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
