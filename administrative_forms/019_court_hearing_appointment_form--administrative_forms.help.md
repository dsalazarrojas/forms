# court_hearing_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The court_hearing_appointment_form is designed for scheduling and managing court hearings. It requires specific information about cases, participants, and hearing status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To submit a new hearing, enter the required details in each field. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Enter the case number, the number assigned to the case by the court system.
3. Select the hearing date, if it is known.
4. Enter the hearing time, if known.
5. Enter the location of the hearing, if known.
6. Enter the name of the judge presiding over the hearing.
7. Enter the name of the lawyer representing the case.
8. Enter the name of the court room where the hearing will take place.
9. Enter the purpose of the hearing.
10. Select the type of case.
11. Enter the name of the court administrator responsible for the case.
12. Enter the name of the court officer attending the hearing.
13. Enter the names of the witnesses participating in the hearing.
14. Select if other parties are involved.
15. Select the status of the hearing.
16. Enter any comments or notes regarding the hearing.
17. Enter a valid email address.
18. Enter a valid phone number.
19. Enter the name of the person submitting the hearing request.
20. Enter the date the request was submitted.
21. Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the hearing.
22. Enter the date the hearing was assigned to.
23. Enter the date the hearing was completed.
24. Enter any additional notes about the hearing.
25. Select the current status of the hearing.
26. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number** (`case_number`, number, required): Enter the unique number assigned to the case by the court system.
* **Hearing Date** (`hearing_date`, date, optional): If known, enter the date of the hearing.
* **Hearing Time** (`hearing_time`, text, optional): If known, enter the time of the hearing.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the hearing, if known.
* **Judge Name** (`judge_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the judge presiding over the hearing.
* **Lawyer Name** (`lawyer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lawyer representing the case.
* **Court Room** (`court_room`, text, optional): Enter the name of the court room where the hearing will take place.
* **Purpose of Hearing** (`purpose_hearing`, text, optional): Enter the reason or objective of the hearing.
* **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of case (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Court Administrator** (`court_administrator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the court administrator responsible for the case.
* **Court Officer** (`court_officer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the court officer attending the hearing.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, optional): Enter the names of the witnesses participating in the hearing.
* **Other Parties** (`other_parties`, select_multiple, optional): Select if other parties are involved in the hearing.
* **Hearing Status** (`hearing_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the hearing (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the hearing.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the hearing request.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, text, optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the hearing.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, text, optional): Enter the date the hearing was assigned to.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, text, optional): Enter the date the hearing was completed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the hearing.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the hearing.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click "Submit" to submit the form.
