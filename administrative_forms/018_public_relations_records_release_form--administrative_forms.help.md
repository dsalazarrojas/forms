# public_relations_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for releasing public relations records, which include information about an organization's or individual's public presence, activities, and releases of public statements. It's used to document and track such events for reference and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the organization's or individual's name.
2. Provide the contact person's information.
3. Enter the phone number of the contact person.
4. Enter the date of birth of the contact person (if required).
5. Enter the contact person's email.
6. Confirm whether the public relations record is complete or not.
7. If the record is partial, select the date the record was updated.
8. Enter the reason for the partial release of public relations records.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments.
10. Select the public relations activities that the record is related to (e.g., social media, news articles, press releases, etc.) or specify other activities if not listed.
11. If "Other" is selected, provide a brief description of the other activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the organization or individual being released.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (Number): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of birth of the contact person, if required.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Release Consent** (Select One): Confirm whether the public relations record is complete (True) or not (False). If it's not complete, select 'Partial' and provide the 'Partial Consent Date'.
* **Partial Consent Date** (Date, Optional): If 'Partial' is selected, enter the date the record was updated.
* **End Consent Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the record will be updated.
* **Reason for Consent** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason for the partial release of public relations records.
* **Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the record.
* **Public Relations Activities** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the public relations activities that the record is related to (e.g., Social Media, News Articles, Press Releases, etc.) or specify 'Other' and describe the activity.
* **Other Public Relation Activities** (Text, Optional): If you selected 'Other' above, enter a brief description of the other activity.
