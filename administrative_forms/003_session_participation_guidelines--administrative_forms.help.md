# Session Participation Guidelines - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for a session, allowing organizers to gather necessary details from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the participant.
2. Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. If you have a phone number, please provide it for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participants Name**: Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address that you would like to be used for communication purposes.
* **Participants Phone Number**: Optional, but if you have a phone number, please provide it for further contact.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
