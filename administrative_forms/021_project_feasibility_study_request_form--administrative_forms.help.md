# Project Feasibility Study Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help gather essential details about a new project and assess its feasibility. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all necessary information to get an informed decision from the evaluation team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the project title to give a brief and descriptive name for your project.
2. Enter a detailed description of the project, including its main goals and objectives.
3. Provide the name of the business or department related to the project.
4. Enter your contact information for further communication and collaboration.
5. Choose the project start and end dates to specify the project duration.
6. Select the current project status from the available options (New, Ongoing, or Completed).
7. If applicable, enter the project location and cost.
8. Choose the members of the project team from the list of available options.
9. Review and submit the form for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title of the project that summarizes its main objective.
* **Project Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the project, including its main goals and objectives.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business or department associated with the project.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department responsible for the project.
* **Business Contact** (`business_contact`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information for further communication.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false): Choose the start date of the project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required: false): Choose the end date of the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required: true): Choose the current status of the project (New, Ongoing, or Completed).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the project will take place, if applicable.
* **Project Cost** (`project_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the estimated cost of the project, if applicable.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, required: false): Enter the project budget.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the members involved in the project from the list of available options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required: false): Review and submit the form for further evaluation.
