# Director Election Ballot - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Director Election Ballot form is used to collect votes for the election of directors within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the candidates you would like to vote for by choosing 'Yes' for each candidate you support.
2. Make sure to review all candidates before submitting your vote.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Your Candidates** (`election_ballot`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the candidates you would like to vote for. You can choose 'Yes' for each candidate.
* **Candidate 1** (`candidate_1`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to vote for Candidate 1.
* **Candidate 2** (`candidate_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to vote for Candidate 2.
* **Candidate 3** (`candidate_3`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to vote for Candidate 3.
* **Candidate 4** (`candidate_4`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you would like to vote for Candidate 4.
* **Cast Vote** (`submit_button`, select_one, required/optional): Once you have selected your candidates, click 'Yes' to cast your vote.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form only once.
* Review all candidates carefully before submitting your vote.
* Only authorized users can fill out this form.
* Duplicate submissions can affect the outcome of the election.
