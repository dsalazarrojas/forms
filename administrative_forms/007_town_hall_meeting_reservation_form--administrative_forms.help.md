# town_hall_meeting_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a town hall meeting reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the meeting details (page 1).
2. Enter the meeting location (page 1).
3. List the meeting attendees (page 1).
4. Select additional attendees to be included in the list (page 1).
5. Choose a reservation date (page 1).
6. Add any additional details about the attendees (page 1).
7. Leave any notes about the meeting (page 1).
8. Confirm the reservation status (page 2).
9. Confirm attendance (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Attendees** (`meeting_attendees`, text, required/optional): List the attendees of the meeting.
* **Attendee List** (`attendee_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the attendees who will be participating in the meeting.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date of the meeting reservation.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required/optional): Add any additional information about the attendees.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Leave any additional notes about the meeting.
* **Confirm Reservation** (`confirm_reservation`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether the meeting reservation has been made.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether the attendees will be attending the meeting.
