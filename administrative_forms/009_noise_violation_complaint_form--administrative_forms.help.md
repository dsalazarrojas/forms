# Noise Violation Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about noise complaints from residents or customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address** (`address_label`): Enter the address where the noise is occurring.
* **City** (`address_city`): Enter the city where the noise is happening.
* **State** (`address_state`): Enter the state where the noise is occurring.
* **Zip Code** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of the address where the noise is happening.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you if necessary.
* **Description** (`description`): Give a detailed description of the noise, including any other relevant details.
* **Noise Type** (`noise_type`): Select the type of noise from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Noise Start Date** (`noise_start_date`): Enter the date when the noise started.
* **Noise Start Time** (`noise_start_time`): Enter the time when the noise started.
* **Noise End Date** (`noise_end_date`): Enter the date when the noise ended.
* **Noise End Time** (`noise_end_time`): Enter the time when the noise ended.
* **Duration** (`noise_duration`): Select the duration of the noise:
	+ Less than 15 minutes
	+ 15-30 minutes
	+ 30-60 minutes
	+ 60-120 minutes
	+ More than 2 hours
* **Noise Violation Repeat** (`noise_violation_repeat`): Check if this is the first, second, third, or more than three times you've reported this incident.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`): If you have a photo of the noise source, provide the URL.
* **Report Repeat** (`report_repeat`): Select how many times you've reported this incident:
	+ First time I am reporting this incident
	+ This is the second time I am reporting this incident
	+ This is the third time I am reporting this incident
	+ More than three times I am reporting this incident
* **Noise Violation ID** (`noise_violation_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the noise violation, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter any additional information about the tool used to investigate the complaint.
