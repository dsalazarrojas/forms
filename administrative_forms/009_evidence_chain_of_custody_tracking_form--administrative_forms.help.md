# Evidence Chain Of Custody Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evidence Chain Of Custody Tracking Form is a tool used to track the chain of custody of physical evidence collected during an investigation or crime scene analysis. This form helps investigators and collectors to document and account for all relevant information related to the evidence, from collection to disposition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Complete each section as you collect and process evidence.
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
3. Make sure to sign and date each section when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Witness Information**:
	+ This section is for collecting basic information about the witness who collected or provided the evidence.
	+ Please provide the name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Evidence Details**:
	+ Describe the evidence collected, including its type, description, and any relevant identifying features.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate identification and tracking.
* **Chain Of Custody**:
	+ Record each event in the chain of custody, including who collected, stored, and transferred the evidence.
	+ Use the provided space to document each transfer, including the date, time, and location.
* **Collection Events**:
	+ Describe each event in the collection process, including where the evidence was collected from and any relevant circumstances.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate tracking of the evidence's origin.
* **Custody Transfer**:
	+ Record each transfer of custody, including who transferred and who received the evidence.
	+ Use the provided space to document each transfer, including the date, time, and location.
* **Storage And Handling**:
	+ Describe how the evidence was stored and handled during processing and transportation.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to ensure the evidence is handled and stored safely.
* **Disposition**:
	+ Record the final disposition of the evidence, including where it is being stored or disposed of.
	+ Be accurate and up-to-date to ensure the evidence is not lost or misplaced.
* **Witness Statements**:
	+ Record any witness statements or statements from the individual who collected or provided the evidence.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to provide context and support for the evidence.
* **Additional Notes**:
	+ Use this space for any additional information or notes that are not included in the other sections.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to provide context and support for the evidence.
* **Investigator Info**:
	+ Record any relevant information about the investigator or collector who completed the form.
	+ Be as detailed as possible to ensure accurate tracking and accountability.

## Tips

* Always use this form to track the chain of custody of physical evidence collected during an investigation or crime scene analysis.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure the evidence is handled and stored safely.
* Use the provided space for any additional information or notes that are not included in the other sections.
* Make sure to sign and date each section when completed.
