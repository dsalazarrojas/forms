# supplier_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from suppliers for check-in purposes, including their contact information, product delivery details, and assigned tool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are on the correct page (Supplier Check In Form).
2. Fill out the required fields (supplier name, product details, assigned tool, notes, and notes 2).
3. Enter your contact email (if you have one).
4. Optionally, enter your contact phone number and delivery date/time if applicable.
5. Enter any additional details about the supplier in the supplier address field (if necessary).
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Name** (`supplier_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the supplier who is checking in.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you have a contact email, enter it here to ensure we can reach you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Delivery Date/Time** (`delivery_date`, `date`, `delivery_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the date and time of delivery, if known.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the product being checked in.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to the supplier from the options provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments about the supplier or their order.
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the supplier or their order.
* **Supplier Address** (`supplier_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the supplier's address if needed.
 

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about a field, you can contact us for assistance.
* Please double-check the information you enter for accuracy to avoid errors.
