The Performance Leave Time Off Form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting time off due to performance-related reasons. This form will likely be used by employees to submit a formal request for time off, and administrators will use it to track and process these requests.

To avoid duplication, please only use this form for performance-related time off requests. Other types of time off requests, such as vacation or sick leave, should be submitted through the appropriate channels. 

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Employee Information section with your employee details.
2.  Select the type of leave you are requesting from the Leave Type dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the start and end dates for your requested leave period.
4.  Provide a brief Reason for Leave, explaining the circumstances surrounding your request.
5.  Enter your Contact Email and/or Phone Number in case the manager or team lead needs to contact you.
6.  If necessary, add a Manager Note for your manager's reference.
7.  If your manager or team lead has a preferred contact method, provide their contact information.
8.  If you're requesting leave from a team lead, provide their name and contact details.

This form is designed to gather necessary information from both the employee and their manager/team lead to process the request efficiently.
