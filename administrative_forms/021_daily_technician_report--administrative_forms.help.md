# daily_technician_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the daily activities and tasks performed by a technician. This includes reporting on maintenance tasks completed, daily activities, and other relevant information such as date and time of completion.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all maintenance tasks completed during the day.
2. Enter a brief description of your daily activities.
3. Enter the date of completion.
4. Enter the time of completion.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Maintenance Tasks**: This field allows you to select all maintenance tasks completed during the day. Select all relevant tasks using the options provided, or add your own custom text if a specific task is not listed.
  * Options: Yes, No
- **Daily Activities**: Enter a brief description of your daily activities.
  * Type: Text field, enter your response in the box
- **Date**: Enter the date of completion.
  * Type: Date field, format: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Time**: Enter the time of completion.
  * Type: Time field, format: HH:MM
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your work.
  * Type: Note field, enter your response in the box
