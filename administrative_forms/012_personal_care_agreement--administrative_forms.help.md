# Personal Care Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate communication and understanding between a caregiver and a care receiver. It is essential to review and understand the agreement carefully before signing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review and sign the form with the caregiver and care receiver.
3. Ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of their roles and responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Care Agreement** (`title`, `note`, required: `false`): This is the title of the form, which is a brief description of what the form is about.
* **Caregiver Name** (`caregiver_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the caregiver who will be providing care services.
* **Care Receiver Name** (`care_receiver_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the care receiver who will be receiving care services.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the start date of the care services.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the end date of the care services.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: `false`): Select the time of the day when the care services will be provided.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the frequency of care services (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Caregiver Shift** (`caregiver_shift`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the type of caregiver shift (Part-time, Full-time, Live-in).
* **Client Needs** (`client_needs`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select the type of client needs (Social, Medical, Emotional, Financial).
* **Caregiver Responsibilities** (`caregiver_responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the responsibilities of the caregiver (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Meal preparation, Medication management, Transportation, Socialization).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional notes or comments about the care services.
* **Caregiver Availability** (`caregiver_availability`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the availability of the caregiver (e.g., available days and hours).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the relationship of the emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend).
* **Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for the care receiver to sign, acknowledging their acceptance of the care agreement.
* **Care Receiver Signature** (`care_receiver_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for the caregiver to sign, acknowledging their acceptance of the care agreement.
* **Caregiver Signature** (`caregiver_signature`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for the caregiver to sign, acknowledging their acceptance of the care agreement.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: `false`): Submit the form to complete the personal care agreement.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, required: `false`): Cancel the form and start over.
* **Caregiver Address** (`caregiver_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the caregiver.
* **Care Receiver Address** (`care_receiver_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the care receiver.
* **Care Receiver Phone** (`care_receiver_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the care receiver.
* **Care Receiver Email** (`care_receiver_email`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the email address of the care receiver.
