# Dependent Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dependent Verification Form is used to verify a dependent's information for administrative purposes. This includes confirming the dependent's identity, date of birth, social security number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the dependent's information in the first field, **Dependent Information** (field 1).
2. Indicate whether the dependent is currently dependent on the primary account holder or not (field 2, **Dependents**).
3. Enter the dependent's date of birth (field 3, **Date of Birth**).
4. Upload dependent documents (field 4, **Dependent Documents**) if required.
5. Enter the dependent's social security number (field 5, **Social Security Number**).
6. Enter the dependent's driver's license number (field 6, **Driver License Number**).
7. Sign the dependent signature field (field 7, **Dependent Signature**).
8. Enter the date the dependent verification form was completed (field 8, **Date**).
9. Provide the status of the dependent verification form (field 9, **Status**).
10. Enter any reason for the verification (field 10, **Reason**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dependent Information** (`dependent_verification_form_info`, text, **optional**): Enter the dependent's name and other identifying details.
* **Dependents** (`dependents`, select_multiple, **optional**): Check yes or no to indicate whether the dependent is currently dependent on the primary account holder.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **optional**): Enter the dependent's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Dependent Documents** (`dependent_verification_form_docs`, text, **optional**): Upload required dependent documents.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, **optional**): Enter the dependent's social security number.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, **optional**): Enter the dependent's driver's license number.
* **Dependent Signature** (`dependent_verification_form_signature`, text, **optional**): Sign the dependent's signature field.
* **Date** (`dependent_verification_form_date`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the dependent verification form was completed.
* **Status** (`dependent_verification_form_status`, text, **optional**): Provide the status of the dependent verification form.
* **Reason** (`dependent_verification_form_reason`, text, **optional**): Enter any reason for the verification.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that the dependent is aware of the information being submitted.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with the primary account holder or relevant authorities.
