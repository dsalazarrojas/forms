# Public Utility Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and track public utility incidents reported by customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information (Name, Email, Phone number) as accurately as possible.
2. Provide the incident date and time.
3. Select the utility company that was affected.
4. Enter a brief description of the incident.
5. Enter the location where the incident occurred.
6. Provide any additional customer message or comments.
7. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required information (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): This is the section where you enter the customer's name, email address, and phone number.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Service Affected** (`service_affected`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the list of available utility services (e.g. power, water, gas, internet) to identify which service was affected.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their account with the utility company.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address associated with their account.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number for communication.
* **Utility Company** (`utility_company`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the list of available utility companies (e.g. Company A, Company B, Company C) to indicate which company's service was affected.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with the utility company.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): If you're an employee of the utility company, enter your name for reference.
