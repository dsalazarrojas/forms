# Payroll Processing Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payroll Processing Checklist Form is used to gather and process payroll information for employees, ensuring accuracy and consistency in the payroll process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options for each field and select the correct information for each employee.
2. Enter the pay process step and employee data in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the pay date and pay frequency for each employee.
4. Choose the pay method and tax rate for each employee.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments as needed.
6. Enter the email and phone number of the employee (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pay Process Step** (`pay_process_step`, text, required: false): This field is used to indicate the current step in the pay process for the selected employee.
* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`, select_one, required: false): Select the employee's name from the options provided.
* **Pay Date** (`pay_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the pay is processed.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, select_one, required: false): Select the tax rate applicable to the employee.
* **Tax Rate Value** (`tax_rate_value`, number, required: false): Enter the tax rate value corresponding to the selected tax rate.
* **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of pay for the employee (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Pay Date 2** (`pay_date_2`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the pay is processed for this employee (if applicable).
* **Pay Frequency 2** (`pay_frequency_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of pay for this employee (if applicable).
* **Pay Method** (`pay_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose the method of pay for the employee (e.g. Direct Deposit, Check).
* **Tax Rate Value 2** (`tax_rate_value_2`, number, required: false): Enter the tax rate value for this employee (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the pay process for this employee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee (if applicable).
