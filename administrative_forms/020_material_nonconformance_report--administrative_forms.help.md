# Material Nonconformance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report nonconformances related to materials used in the production process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in the following order: Category, Description, Date, Time, Material ID, Nonconformance Type, Quantity, Unit Price, Unit of Measure, Location, Nonconformance Code, Reason for Nonconformance, Corrective Action, Notes, and Assigned To.
- Clicking "Next" will move you to the next page of the form. Clicking "Previous" will move you back to the previous page.
- If a field is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Administrative Forms`, `Other`, required: false): Choose the category of the nonconformance, such as "Administrative Forms" or "Other".
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the nonconformance.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the nonconformance.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the nonconformance.
* **Material ID** (`material_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the material ID number associated with the nonconformance.
* **Nonconformance Type** (`nonconformance_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of nonconformance, such as "Material Deficiency", "Material Excess", or "Material Damage".
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the quantity affected by the nonconformance.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unit price of the material affected by the nonconformance.
* **Unit of Measure** (`unit_of_measure`, `text`, required: false): Enter the unit of measure of the material affected by the nonconformance.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the nonconformance occurred.
* **Nonconformance Code** (`nonconformance_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant nonconformance code related to the nonconformance.
* **Reason for Nonconformance** (`reason_for_nonconformance`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reason for the nonconformance.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, `text`, required: false): Enter the corrective action taken to address the nonconformance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information related to the nonconformance.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name or role of the person responsible for addressing the nonconformance.
