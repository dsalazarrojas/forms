# user_sign_out_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "user_sign_out_form" is used for employees to sign out from work. It is an administrative form that collects information for reporting and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before filling out this form.
2. Fill in the required fields (Employee ID, Reason, Confirmation, Department, and Employee Name) completely and accurately.
3. Choose the correct options for Reason, Confirmation, and Department fields.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
5. Ensure the Manager Confirmation field is marked as 'confirmed' to confirm the employee's sign out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, required): Enter the unique ID of the employee signing out.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Select Multiple, required): Choose the reason for signing out (e.g., vacation, sick, meeting).
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, Select One, required): Confirm that you are signing out (choose 'confirmed').
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`, Date, required): Enter the date of the sign out (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, optional): Add any additional comments or details about the sign out.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, Select One, required): Mark as 'confirmed' to confirm the manager's sign out approval.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, required): Choose the department of the employee signing out (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Customer Service).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the manager who approved the sign out (if applicable).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the employee signing out.
* **Job Title** (`employee_title`, Text, required): Enter the job title of the employee signing out.
* **Reason Note** (`reason_note`, Text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the reason for signing out.

Tips:
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant personnel or manager for guidance.
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid errors.
* Double-check the form for completeness and correctness before submitting.
