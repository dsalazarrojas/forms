# Device Access Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and grant access to company devices. It captures necessary information about the user, the device, and the terms of access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the first field.
2. Select your Organization from the options provided.
3. Choose the type of device you are requesting access to from the Device Type dropdown menu.
4. Select your User Position from the options provided (e.g., Management, IT, Admin, Staff).
5. If applicable, enter your Start Date and End Date to specify the duration of access.
6. Select your desired Access Level (High, Medium, Low) to determine your level of access.
7. Enter the Reason for Access Grant to explain your need for access.
8. If you have a Device Description, please enter a brief description of the device you are requesting access to.
9. Check the Terms Accepted checkbox to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
10. Sign and date the Signature field to complete the form.
11. Add any additional Notes or comments about your access request.
12. Enter your Email and Phone Number for contact purposes.
13. If applicable, enter your Employee ID and Department for your department's reference.
14. Enter your Job Title and Manager's Name, Email, and Phone Number for your manager's reference.
15. Finally, enter the Approval Date and Review Date when access is approved or reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as a user requesting access to the company device.
* **Organization**: Select your organization from the options provided (e.g., Company Name, Department, Team).
* **Device Type**: Choose the type of device you are requesting access to (e.g., Mobile Phone, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop).
* **User Position**: Select your position within the organization (e.g., Management, IT, Admin, Staff).
* **Start Date**: If applicable, enter the start date of your access period.
* **End Date**: If applicable, enter the end date of your access period.
* **Access Level**: Determine your level of access (High, Medium, Low).
* **Reason for Access Grant**: Explain your need for access to the device.
* **Device Description**: Briefly describe the device you are requesting access to.
* **Terms Accepted**: Check this box to acknowledge you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature**: Sign and date this section to complete the form.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your access request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Employee ID**: If applicable, enter your employee ID for department's reference.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter your department for reference.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title for reference.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name for reference.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address for reference.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number for reference.
* **Manager Position**: Enter your manager's position for reference.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the approval date when access is approved.
* **Review Date**: Enter the review date when access is reviewed.
* **Review Result**: Select the outcome of the review (Approved, Denied, Pending).
