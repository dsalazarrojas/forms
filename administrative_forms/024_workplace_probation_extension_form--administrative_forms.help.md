# Workplace Probation Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Probation Extension Form is used for requesting a workplace probation extension. It is used by employees to request an extension of their initial probation period, which is the period during which they are being assessed for fit and performance in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee ID number into the field marked "Employee Id". This is your unique employee number.
2. Choose a new extension date from the calendar in the "New Extension Date" field. Please note that only dates in the past or future will be accepted.
3. In the "Reason For Extension" field, please enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting an extension. This will help your manager understand your request.
4. In the "Manager Approval" field, you will select how your manager has approved your request. Please select from the options "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
5. You may add any additional notes in the "Notes" field for your manager's reference.
6. In the "Manager Note" field, your manager can add any additional notes about your request.
7. Enter the reason for the extension in the "Extension Reason" field.
8. Select the manager's approval status in the "Manager Approval Status" field.
9. Enter the date the form was submitted by you in the "Form Submitted By" field.
10. Enter the date the form was submitted by your manager in the "Form Submitted On" field.
11. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes Manager" field.
12. Your manager can enter any additional approval notes in the "Manager Approval Note" field.
13. Finally, enter your current employee status in the "Employee Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number into this field.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, date, required: false): Choose a new extension date from the calendar. Only dates in the past or future will be accepted.
* **Reason For Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting an extension.
* **New Extension Date** (`new_extension_date`, date, required): Choose a new extension date from the calendar. Only dates in the past or future will be accepted.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_multiple, required: false): Select how your manager has approved your request from the options "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes for your manager's reference.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required: false): Your manager can add any additional notes about your request.
* **Extension Reason** (`extension_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the extension.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the manager's approval status from the options "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, number, required): Enter the date the form was submitted by you.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted by your manager.
* **Notes Manager** (`notes_manager`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes in this field.
* **Manager Approval Note** (`manager_approval_note`, note, required: false): Your manager can enter any additional approval notes.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, text, required: false): Enter your current employee status.
