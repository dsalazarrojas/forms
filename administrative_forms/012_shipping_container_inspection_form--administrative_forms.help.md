# Shipping Container Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shipping Container Inspection Form is used to collect information about the condition of shipping containers before they are used for shipping. This form helps inspectors evaluate the container's condition and report any damages or issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title and your inspector name.
2. Enter the form date and time it was completed.
3. Select the inspection items you have checked.
4. Enter any conditions or comments about the container's condition.
5. Enter any damages or notes about the container.
6. Enter your contact information (phone number and email).
7. Choose the container status (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
8. Sign off on the form if you are the inspector.
9. Add any additional comments.
10. Enter the container's location and position.
11. Enter the container's direction.
12. Enter the container's height and width.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the form, which is a short description of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, text, required): Enter the date of the form completion.
* **Container Serial** (`container_serial`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the container, if applicable.
* **Container Number** (`container_number`, text, optional): Enter the container's number, if applicable.
* **Container Size** (`container_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the container, if applicable.
* **Container Type** (`container_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of container, if applicable.
* **Cargo Weight** (`cargo_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the cargo, if applicable.
* **Cargo Volume** (`cargo_volume`, number, optional): Enter the volume of the cargo, if applicable.
* **Inspection Item** (`inspection_item`, select_multiple, optional): Select all items you have checked during the inspection.
* **Conditions** (`conditions`, text, optional): Enter any conditions you have observed during the inspection.
* **Damages** (`damages`, text, optional): Enter any damages you have observed during the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Container Status** (`container_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the container (Good, Bad, or Unknown).
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, select_one, optional): Check if you are the inspector.
* **Inspector Comments** (`inspector_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Form Completed** (`form_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Container Location** (`container_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the container.
* **Container Position** (`container_position`, text, optional): Enter the position of the container.
* **Container Direction** (`container_direction`, text, optional): Enter the direction of the container.
* **Container Height** (`container_height`, number, optional): Enter the height of the container.
* **Container Width** (`container_width`, number, optional): Enter the width of the container.
