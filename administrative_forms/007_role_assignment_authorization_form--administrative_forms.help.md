# Role Assignment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Assignment Authorization Form is used to collect information for assigning roles and responsibilities within an organization. This form helps to ensure that the necessary information is gathered for making informed decisions about role assignments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure that you understand what information is being requested.
2. For each field, enter the required information according to the instructions provided.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by a star (*)).
4. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **main_page_1** (select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the roles you are authorized to perform.
	+ This field allows you to select multiple roles that you are authorized to perform.
	+ Make sure to check only the roles that apply to you.
- **main_page_3** (number, required): Enter your role assignment number.
	+ This field requires you to enter a numerical value.
	+ Make sure to enter the correct role assignment number for your specific role.
- **main_page_9** (number, required): Enter your secondary role assignment number.
	+ This field requires you to enter a numerical value.
	+ Make sure to enter the correct secondary role assignment number for your specific role.
- **main_page_5** (select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are authorized to perform this role.
	+ Make sure to only check "Yes" if you are authorized to perform this role.
- **main_page_8** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your role assignment.
	+ This field requires you to enter a short description of your role assignment.
	+ Make sure to enter a clear and concise description.
- **main_page_14** (text, required): Enter any additional information about your role assignment.
	+ This field allows you to provide any additional information about your role assignment.
	+ Make sure to keep your answer brief and focused.
- **main_page_16** (date, required): Enter the date of your role assignment.
	+ This field requires you to enter a date in the format MM/DD/YY.
	+ Make sure to enter the correct date of your role assignment.
- **main_page_18** (text, required): Enter any relevant details about your role assignment.
	+ This field allows you to provide any relevant details about your role assignment.
	+ Make sure to keep your answer brief and focused.
- **main_page_20** (select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for any additional roles you are authorized to perform.
	+ This field allows you to select multiple additional roles that you are authorized to perform.
	+ Make sure to check only the roles that apply to you.
- **main_page_21** (select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are authorized to perform this role.
	+ Make sure to only check "Yes" if you are authorized to perform this role.
- **main_page_23** (select_one, required): Check "Yes" if you are authorized to perform this role.
