# Food Thawing Temperature Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Thawing Temperature Log is a form used to record the temperature at which food has been thawed. This is an important aspect of food safety and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of food product being thawed (e.g. Cheese, Meat, Seafood).
2. Enter the temperature at which the food was thawed.
3. Select the date and time that the food was thawed.
4. Select the thawing method used (e.g. Running Water, Cold Water, Dry Method).
5. Enter any additional notes about the thawing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Thawing Temperature** (`Thawing Temperature`): This field is used to enter the temperature at which the food was thawed. Enter the temperature in degrees.
* **Date** (`Date`): This field is used to select the date that the food was thawed. Choose the correct date from the calendar.
* **Time** (`Time`): This field is used to select the time that the food was thawed. Choose the correct time from the available options.
* **Food Product** (`Food Product`): This field is used to select the type of food product being thawed (e.g. Cheese, Meat, Seafood).
* **Weight** (`Weight`): This field is used to enter the weight of the food product being thawed.
* **Thawing Method** (`Thawing Method`): This field is used to select the method used to thaw the food (e.g. Running Water, Cold Water, Dry Method).
* **Notes** (`Notes`): This field is used to enter any additional comments or notes about the thawing process. This is an optional field, but it is recommended to enter any relevant information that may be helpful for future reference.
