# CCTV Disclosure and Access Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CCTV Disclosure and Access Log form is used to ensure that requests for access to CCTV footage or other security-related data are properly recorded and tracked to meet organizational policies and external regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), to provide the necessary information for the request.
2. Be as specific as possible when filling in the "Date and Time of Request" and "Date and Time Range of Footage Requested" fields.
3. Select the correct purpose for the access request from the dropdown menu.
4. Indicate the correct method of access provided to the requestor.
5. Sign the "Data Protection Agreement" by selecting "I Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Person Requesting Access**: Enter the name of the person making the request. This is a required field to ensure that the request is attributed to a specific individual.
* **Organization or Department**: Enter the name of the organization or department requesting access. This helps to track which team or entity is requesting access.
* **Date and Time of Request**: Enter the date and time when the request was made. Be as specific as possible.
* **Purpose of Access/Disclosure**: Select one of the provided options for the purpose of the access request, such as "Incident Investigation", "Police Request", or "Insurance Claim".
* **Date and Time Range of Footage Requested**: Enter the specific date and time range for which access to footage is requested.
* **Camera Locations or IDs**: Enter the specific cameras or locations for which access is requested.
* **Access Authorization Status**: Select the status of the request, such as "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending Senior Review".
* **Authorizing Official Name**: Enter the name of the person who approved or denied the request.
* **Justification for Decision**: Enter a brief explanation for the decision made.
* **Method of Access Provided**: Select the method of access provided to the requestor, such as "On-site View Only", "Digital Export (Encrypted)", or "Physical Media (USB/DVD)".
* **I Agree (Data Protection Agreement)**: Select "I Agree" to digitally sign the data protection agreement.
* **Log Entry Date and Time**: This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
* **Related Incident Number (if any)**: Enter any related incident number if applicable.
* **Log Verified by Data Protection Officer**: This field is for internal use only and should be marked as "Verified" when the log has been reviewed and approved by the Data Protection Officer.

Note: All fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in completely and accurately to ensure that the form is properly completed.
