# Roof Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roof Maintenance Checklist Form is a tool for recording and reporting roof conditions, leaks, and damaged materials. It helps users to identify potential issues and schedule necessary maintenance tasks to prevent costly repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as detailed as possible when describing any issues you observe.
4. Check the "roof_leaks" and "damaged_materials" fields for any signs of problems or damage.
5. Select the correct answer for each field based on your observation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Roof Conditions
**Note**: This is a section for any observations or comments about the roof's overall condition. Provide any relevant information about the roof's state.

* **roof_conditions**: (Note) This is the space to describe any issues or concerns you have regarding the roof's condition. Be as detailed as you can about the problems you've observed or concerns you have. 

### 2. Roof Leaks
**Select Multiple**: Check all the options below if you've identified any issues with the roof:
* **No issues**: If you haven't observed any leaks or problems.
* **Small leak**: A minor leak that's not causing significant damage but may lead to bigger issues if not addressed.
* **Large leak**: A noticeable leak that's causing some damage but can still be managed with minor repairs.
* **Severe leak**: A significant leak that's causing considerable damage and requires immediate attention.

### 3. Damaged Materials
**Select One**: Identify the type of material that's damaged or showing signs of wear:
* **Asphalt shingle**: If the asphalt shingle roof is damaged or worn.
* **Clay tile**: If the clay tile roof is damaged or showing signs of wear.
* **Metal**: If the metal roof is damaged or corroded.
* **Slate**: If the slate roof is damaged or cracked.
* **Wood**: If the wooden roof (e.g., wooden shakes or clapboards) is damaged.
* **Other**: If the damaged material is something else (please describe in the "roofs_conditions" note).

**Tips**

* Always report any issues or problems you've observed with your roof.
* Be honest and thorough in your descriptions of any damages or leaks.
* If you're not sure what type of material is damaged, choose "Other" and describe it in the "roofs_conditions" note field.
* If you have no issues or problems, select "No issues" in the "roof_leaks" field.
* Remember that neglecting roof problems can lead to costly repairs.
