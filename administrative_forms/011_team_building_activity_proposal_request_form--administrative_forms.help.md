# Team Building Activity Proposal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request team-building activities within the organization. It's a crucial tool for team managers to submit proposals for team-building events, outings, or projects that promote team cohesion, collaboration, and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your organization's name in the **Organization** field.
2. Indicate whether your team has **Team Leaders** in the **Team Leaders** field. This will help determine the level of involvement and support required for the activity.
3. Select the **Activity Type** that best describes the proposed activity. This will help the organization understand the nature of the event.
4. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** for the activity. This will help plan and schedule the event.
5. Enter the **Time** required for the activity.
6. Enter the venue details in the **Venue**, **Location**, and **Venue 2** fields, if applicable.
7. Provide a clear **Description** of the activity, including its purpose and expected outcomes.
8. Enter the **Budget** for the activity.
9. Indicate whether the activity will be **Assigned User** or not. This will help determine who will be responsible for the activity.
10. Select the team members to be **Assigned Team** or not. This will help determine the involvement of team members.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.
12. Indicate whether attachments are required for the activity by selecting **Attachments**.
13. Select the **Activity Type 2** that best describes the proposed activity.
14. Enter any additional team details in the **Team** field.
15. Enter the name of the person submitting the proposal in the **Submitted By** field.
16. If applicable, enter the venue and location details for the second activity in the **Venue 2**, **Location 2**, and **Description 2** fields.
17. Indicate whether attachments are required for the second activity by selecting **Attachments 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Team Leaders**: Indicate whether your team has leaders.
* **Activity Type**: Select the type of activity that best describes the proposed event.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date when the activity will start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date when the activity will end.
* **Time**: Enter the time required for the activity.
* **Venue**: Enter the venue details for the activity.
* **Location**: Enter the location details for the activity.
* **Description**: Provide a clear description of the activity.
* **Budget**: Enter the budget for the activity.
* **Assigned User**: Indicate whether the activity will be assigned to a user.
* **Assigned Team**: Select the team members to be involved.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Attachments**: Indicate whether attachments are required for the activity.
* **Activity Type 2**: Select the type of activity that best describes the proposed event.
* **Team**: Enter the team details.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name.
* **Venue 2**: Enter the venue details for the second activity.
* **Location 2**: Enter the location details for the second activity.
* **Description 2**: Provide a clear description of the second activity.
* **Attachments 2**: Indicate whether attachments are required for the second activity.
