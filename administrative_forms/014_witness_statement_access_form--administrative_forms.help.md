# Witness Statement Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Witness Statement Access Form is used to collect witness statements for access purposes, ensuring that witnesses have the necessary clearance to access areas or resources.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available options for each field carefully.
2. Select all applicable options for the areas or resources you have access to.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **witness_statement_access_form** (`Yes/No`, Optional): Select all areas or resources you have access to.
* **witness_statement_access_form_1** (`Yes/No`, Optional): Select if you have access to areas or resources that require special clearance.
* **witness_statement_access_form_2** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your access.
* **witness_statement_access_form_3** (Number, Optional): Enter any numerical value related to your access (e.g., time limits).
* **witness_statement_access_form_4** (Time, Optional): Select a time for access (e.g., working hours).
* **witness_statement_access_form_5** (Date, Optional): Select a date for access (e.g., access start date).
* **witness_statement_access_form_6** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about your access.
* **witness_statement_access_form_7** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes or comments about your access.
* **witness_statement_access_form_8** (Text, Optional): Enter any other notes or comments about your access.
* **witness_statement_access_form_9** (Text, Optional): Enter any other notes or comments about your access.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
* If unsure about any field, consult with the relevant authorities.
* Review your answers before submitting the form.
