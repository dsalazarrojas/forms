# Commercial Vehicle Registration Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Vehicle Registration Extension Form is a crucial document used to request an extension of a commercial vehicle registration. This form collects necessary information from the registered owner or business, allowing for the efficient processing of the extension request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the extension request.
3. Attach any supporting documents as required.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number for notifications about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner name** (Owner name, text, required): Enter the name of the registered owner or company.
* **Business name** (Business name if applicable, text, optional): If the vehicle is registered under a business, enter the business name. Leave this field blank if the vehicle is registered under an individual.
* **Vehicle make and model** (Vehicle make and model, text, required): Enter the make and model of the commercial vehicle, including the year if available.
* **Vehicle identification number VIN** (Vehicle identification number VIN, text, required): Enter the full Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) without spaces.
* **Current registration number** (Current registration number, text, required): Enter the current registration number as shown on the registration document.
* **Reason for extension request** (Reason for extension request, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the extension request.
* **Desired extension period in months** (Desired extension period in months, number, required): Enter a whole number of months for the desired extension period.
* **Supporting documents list** (Supporting documents list, note, optional): Attach proof of need or other supporting documents as required.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter the email address to receive notifications about the request.
* **Contact phone number** (Contact phone number, text, optional): Enter the phone number for optional follow-up.
