# Government Employee Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Employee Onboarding Form is a comprehensive tool for HR departments to collect essential information from new employees, ensuring a smooth transition into the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter your Birth Date.
3.  Choose your Hire Date from the calendar.
4.  Select your role and department from the respective fields.
5.  Enter your Supervisor's Name and Manager's Name, if applicable.
6.  Fill in your Employee Number, Department ID, Job Title, and Job Type.
7.  Select your Employment Status and Employment Type.
8.  Enter your Employment Start Date and Employment End Date, if applicable.
9.  Choose your Job Location.
10. Select your Benefits Eligibility and Benefits Start Date and Benefits End Date, if applicable.
11.  Select your emergency contact relationship and phone number, if applicable.
12.  Fill in any additional information for your emergency contact and job location, if necessary.
13.  Provide any other details regarding your job title and job location, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Choose your hire date from the calendar.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select your department.
*   **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text): Enter your supervisor's name.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text): Enter your manager's name.
*   **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number): Enter your employee number.
*   **Department ID** (`department_id`, text): Enter your department ID.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one): Select your job type.
*   **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one): Select your employment status.
*   **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple): Select your employment type.
*   **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date): Choose your employment start date from the calendar.
*   **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date): Choose your employment end date from the calendar.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_multiple): Select your job location.
*   **Benefits Eligibility** (`benefits_eligibility`, select_multiple): Select your benefits eligibility.
*   **Benefits Start Date** (`benefits_start_date`, date): Choose your benefits start date from the calendar.
*   **Benefits End Date** (`benefits_end_date`, date): Choose your benefits end date from the calendar.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple): Select your emergency contact relationship.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, select_multiple): Select your emergency contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Other Info** (`emergency_contact_other_info`, text): Enter any other emergency contact information.
*   **Job Location Other Info** (`job_location_other_info`, text): Enter any other job location information.
*   **Job Title Other Info** (`job_title_other_info`, text): Enter any other job title information.
