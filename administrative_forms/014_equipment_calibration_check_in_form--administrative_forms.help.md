# Equipment Calibration Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Calibration Check In Form is used to document and verify the calibration process of equipment. This form helps ensure that equipment is functioning accurately and is a critical step in various industries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct Equipment ID and calibration date for the equipment being checked.
2. Fill in the technician's details, including their name, phone number, and email.
3. Enter any comments or notes about the calibration check.
4. Select the correct equipment status from the options provided.
5. Sign the form with your technician signature and date.
6. Repeat steps 2-5 for the second piece of equipment, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID** (`1`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier of the equipment being checked.
* **Calibration Date** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the calibration check.
* **Technician Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the technician performing the calibration check.
* **Technician Phone** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Technician Email** (`5`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Technician Comments** (`6`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the calibration check.
* **Equipment Status** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the equipment from the options provided (Certified, Not Certified, In Progress).
* **Notes** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the equipment.
* **Technician Signature** (`9`, `note`, required): Sign the form with your technician signature.
* **Technician Signature Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Reviewer Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer who verified the calibration check.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer for contact purposes.
* **Reviewer Email** (`13`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer for contact purposes.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`14`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`15`, `note`, optional): Sign the form with your reviewer signature.
* **Reviewer Signature Date** (`16`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your reviewer signature.
* **Equipment ID 2** (`17`, `number`, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the second piece of equipment, if applicable.
* **Calibration Date 2** (`18`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the calibration check for the second piece of equipment, if applicable.
* **Technician Name 2** (`19`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as the technician performing the calibration check for the second piece of equipment.
* **Technician Phone 2** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes for the second piece of equipment.
* **Technician Email 2** (`21`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes for the second piece of equipment.
* **Technician Comments 2** (`22`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the calibration check for the second piece of equipment.
* **Equipment Status 2** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the second piece of equipment from the options provided (Certified, Not Certified, In Progress).
* **Notes 2** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the second piece of equipment.
* **Reviewer Name 2** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer who verified the calibration check for the second piece of equipment, if applicable.
* **Reviewer Phone 2** (`not provided`, `text`, optional): Not applicable for this field.
* **Reviewer Email 2** (`not provided`, `email`, optional): Not applicable for this field.
* **Reviewer Comments 2** (`not provided`, `note`, optional): Not applicable for this field.
* **Reviewer Signature 2** (`not provided`, `note`, optional): Not applicable for this field.
* **Reviewer Signature Date 2** (`not provided`, `date`, optional): Not applicable for this field.
