# Performance Evaluation Strategy Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from employees who want to participate in the performance evaluation process. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to provide valuable insights for the evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the "Evaluation Date" in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3. Choose your preferred "Evaluation Strategy" from the list provided.
4. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
5. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Add any additional comments or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required: false): This field is used to collect your name, which will be used to identify you as the person providing feedback on the performance evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (evaluation_date, date, required: false): Select a date for the evaluation. This will help in tracking the progress of the evaluation process.
* **Evaluation Strategy** (evaluation_strategy, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the six strategies provided. This will help in understanding your perspective on how to evaluate the performance.
* **Email Address** (contact_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding any further questions or updates.
* **Phone Number** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding any further questions or updates.
* **Notes** (contact_notes, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or comments regarding the performance evaluation. This will help us in understanding your perspective more thoroughly.
