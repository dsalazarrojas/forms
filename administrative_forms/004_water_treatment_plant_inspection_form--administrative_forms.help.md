# Water Treatment Plant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the status of a water treatment plant, including its location, inspection date, type of inspection, status, maintenance issues, safety and quality standards, compliance issues, and any additional notes or assigned personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the **Plant Location** in the text field on page 1.
2. Enter the **Inspection Date** in the date field on page 1.
3. Select the **Inspection Type** on page 1, which indicates the type of inspection performed.
4. On page 2, select the **Plant Status** from the list, indicating the current status of the plant.
5. In the **Maintenance Issues** field, list any maintenance issues found during the inspection, if applicable.
6. For **Safety Standards**, select all that apply from the list, indicating if the plant meets or does not meet the standards.
7. Similarly, for **Quality Standards**, select all that apply, indicating if the plant meets or does not meet the standards.
8. For **Compliance Issues**, select all that apply, indicating if the plant has any compliance issues.
9. In the **Notes** field, add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
10. Select whether the plant meets the **Assigned to** requirement, indicating if personnel is assigned to the inspection task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Plant Location**: Enter the address or location of the water treatment plant.
* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Inspection Type**: Select the type of inspection performed, such as regular, maintenance, or special.
* **Plant Status**: Select the current status of the plant, such as Normal, Shut Down, or Maintenance.
* **Maintenance Issues**: List any issues found during the inspection that require maintenance or repair.
* **Safety Standards**: Select all that apply, indicating if the plant meets or does not meet the safety standards.
* **Quality Standards**: Select all that apply, indicating if the plant meets or does not meet the quality standards.
* **Compliance Issues**: Select all that apply, indicating if the plant has any compliance issues.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Assigned to**: Select whether personnel is assigned to the inspection task, indicating yes or no.
