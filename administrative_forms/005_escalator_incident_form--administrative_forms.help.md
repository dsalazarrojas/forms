# escalator_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The escalator incident form is used to document and report escalator incidents. It captures essential details such as incident description, status, type, and reported information. This form helps ensure that incidents are accurately recorded and communicated to relevant personnel for prompt action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Incident Description
	* Reported By
	* Incident Status
	* Reported On
	* Incident Type
	* Escalator Model
	* Incident Number
2. Provide additional details in the optional fields, if necessary:
	* Incident Description (optional)
	* Incident Reported (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Describe the incident in as much detail as possible, including any relevant events or circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Reported By** (`Reported By`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`Incident Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ pending
	+ completed
	+ in progress
* **Reported On** (`Reported On`, `date`, required): Enter the date the incident was reported.
* **Incident Type** (`Incident Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options:
	+ mechanical
	+ electrical
	+ software
* **Escalator Model** (`Escalator Model`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable escalator models involved.
* **Incident Number** (`Incident Number`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID number for the incident.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide accurate and detailed information to help facilitate prompt resolution of the incident.
* Review the form thoroughly before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
* If you're unsure about any information, please contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
