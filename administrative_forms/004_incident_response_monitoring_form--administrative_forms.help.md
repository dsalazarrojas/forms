# Incident Response Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather and monitor incident responses in a standardized format, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and tracked efficiently. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to provide a clear and concise picture of incidents that have occurred.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the incident before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, starting with the top field.
3. Enter the incident name in the "Incident Name" field.
4. Select the correct date the incident occurred in the "Incident Date" field.
5. Enter the time the incident occurred in the "Incident Time" field.
6. Provide a brief description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
7. Specify the incident location in the "Incident Location" field.
8. Choose the relevant incident category from the drop-down menu in the "Incident Category" field.
9. Select the status of the incident from the multi-select menu in the "Incident Status" field.
10. If necessary, choose a priority level for the incident in the "Incident Priority" field.
11. Enter the name of the response team handling the incident in the "Response Team" field.
12. Add any additional notes about the incident in the "Incident Notes" field.
13. Enter the email address of the relevant contact person in the "Email Address" field.
14. Enter the phone number of the relevant contact person in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Name** (text, required): Enter a concise name for the incident to help identify it.
* **Incident Date** (date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (text, required): Specify the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Category** (select_one, required): Choose a category for the incident from the drop-down menu.
* **Incident Status** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable statuses for the incident from the multi-select menu.
* **Incident Priority** (select_one, optional): Choose a priority level for the incident from the drop-down menu, if applicable.
* **Response Team** (text, optional): Enter the name of the response team handling the incident.
* **Incident Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the incident.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the relevant contact person.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the relevant contact person.
