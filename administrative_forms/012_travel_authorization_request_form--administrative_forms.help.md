# Travel Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Authorization Request Form is used to request official travel authorization for business or official purposes. It helps to provide necessary information for travel authorization and ensures that all required details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your requested travel dates.
2. Provide the start and end times of your travel, as well as the duration of your trip.
3. Specify the destination and reason for your travel.
4. If you're traveling with others, provide their details.
5. Fill out your return date.
6. Estimate the travel budget in the requested currency (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date**: The date you wish to travel. Please format it according to your system's date format.
* **Travel Start Time**: The start time of your travel. Please enter it in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Travel End Time**: The end time of your travel. Please enter it in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Travel Duration**: The length of your trip in hours.
* **Travel Destination**: The city or location you plan to visit.
* **Travel Reason**: A brief description of the purpose of your travel (e.g., conference, meeting, or training).
* **Return Date**: The date you plan to return.
* **Travel Budget**: The estimated budget for your trip in the selected currency (e.g., USD, EUR, JPY, or CNY).
* **Travel Currency**: If you've selected an option other than the standard ones, please specify the currency in the "Other Travel Currency" field.
* **Submitter**: Select the person submitting this form from the provided options (John, Jim, or Jane).
* **Other Submitter**: If you've selected "Other" in the submitter field, please specify the name of the person submitting this form.
