# Emergency Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect emergency contact information from participants. This information will be used in case of an emergency to reach out to the contact person.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "contact_first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "contact_last_name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Describe your relationship to the participant in the "relationship_to_participant" field.
6. If you would like to add an emergency contact, fill in the "emergency_contact_first_name", "emergency_contact_last_name", and "emergency_contact_phone" fields.
7. Describe the emergency contact's relationship to the participant in the "emergency_contact_relationship_to_participant" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Form**: This field is the title of the form. It's not a field you need to fill in, but it's here to inform you about the purpose of the form.
* **contact_first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **contact_last_name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **contact_email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number in this field. This will be used to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **relationship_to_participant** (Text, Required): Describe your relationship to the participant, such as "family member", "spouse", "colleague", etc.
* **emergency_contact_first_name** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their first name in this field.
* **emergency_contact_last_name** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their last name in this field.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number in this field.
* **emergency_contact_relationship_to_participant** (Text, Optional): Describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant.
