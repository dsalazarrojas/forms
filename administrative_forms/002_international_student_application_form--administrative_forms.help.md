# International Student Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for international students to apply for a program or course at an educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the bio-data section, providing your biographical information.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. If applicable, select your country of origin and passport details.
4. Disclose any medical conditions or medications you are currently taking.
5. List your qualifications, including the degree you have achieved and the university you attended.
6. Motivate your application by explaining why you are interested in this program.
7. Attach supporting documents as required.
8. Select the course you are interested in applying for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Bio-Data**: This section is for providing your biographical information.
	+ **Biographical Data**: Please enter your biographical data as required.
* **Contact Information**: This section is for providing your contact details.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your passport.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address that you regularly use.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Passport Details**: This section is for providing your passport information.
	+ **Country Of Origin**: Select your country of origin.
	+ **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number as it appears on your passport.
	+ **Expirey Date**: Enter the date your passport is due to expire.
* **Medical Data**: This section is for disclosing any medical conditions or medications you are currently taking.
	+ **Physical Condition**: Select any medical conditions you are currently experiencing.
	+ **Medications**: Select any medications you are currently taking.
* **Qualifications**: This section is for listing your educational qualifications.
	+ **Certificate Degree**: Select the degree you have achieved.
	+ **University Attended**: Enter the university where you achieved your degree.
	+ **Major Study Field**: Enter the major study field you are applying for.
* **Motivation For**: This section is for motivating your application.
	+ **Motivation**: Enter your motivation for applying for this program.
	+ **Relation**: Enter the relation between you and your parents or guardians.
* **Supporting Documents**: This section is for attaching required documents.
	+ **Document Type**: Select the type of document you are attaching.
* **Selected Course**: This section is for selecting the course you are applying for.
	+ **Course**: Select the course you are interested in applying for.
