# Monthly Grant Timesheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track work hours, expenses, and activities on grant projects. It helps administrators to monitor and verify the work done on these projects, and ensures compliance with grant reporting requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Grant Project** field with the name of the project you are working on.
2. Enter the **start_date** field with the start date of the project.
3. Enter the total **hours_worked** on the project.
4. List any **expenses** related to the project in the designated field.
5. Describe the activities performed on the project in the **Grant Activities** field.
6. Add any additional **notes** about the project if needed.
7. Enter your **Email** address.
8. Enter your **Phone** number.
9. Select the person assigned to the project from the **Assigned To** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grant Project**: Enter the name of the grant project you are working on. This field is required.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the project. If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Total Hours**: Enter the total number of hours you worked on the project. This field is optional.
* **expenses**: List any expenses related to the project, such as travel costs, equipment rentals, etc. This field is optional.
* **Grant Activities**: Describe the activities you performed on the project, including tasks and tasks completed. This field is optional.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the project if needed. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is optional.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person assigned to the project. This field is optional.
