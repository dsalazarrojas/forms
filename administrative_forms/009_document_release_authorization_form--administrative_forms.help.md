# Document Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Document Release Authorization Form is used to collect information about the release of documents within an organization. This form helps to ensure that documents are released correctly and in accordance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client or individual the document is being released to.
* **Document Type** (document_type, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the document is a 'Yes' or 'No' release. This field helps determine the type of release being made.
* **Document Status** (document_status, select_multiple, required: false): Select the current status of the document. Choose one or more of the following:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
* **Release To** (release_to, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate who or what the release is being made to. Choose one or more of the following:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Release Date** (release_date, date, required: false): Enter the date the document is being released.
* **Release Time** (release_time, time, required: false): Enter the time the document is being released.
* **Reason for Release** (reason_for_release, text, required: false): Provide a clear and concise reason for releasing the document.
* **Authorized By** (authorized_by, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person authorizing the release.
* **Authorized By Date** (authorized_by, date, required: false): Note: This field is not visible in the form but was assumed to be part of the YAML input schema. If you are seeing this field in the form, it is a mistake. Please disregard this field as it is not included in the form. 

Note: This guide assumes that the form is being completed in an environment where the "Authorized By Date" field is not present. If you are seeing this field in your form, please disregard the explanation regarding it. 

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the document is released correctly. If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
