# immigration_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The immigration compliance declaration form is a template for collecting employee information for immigration purposes. This form helps in verifying employee identities and tracking employment details for compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your current employee ID as it appears on your passport or national ID.
2. Select your department from the options provided (HR, Finance, Marketing, Sales, IT, Operations).
3. Choose your employment status (Active, Inactive, Retired).
4. Enter your start date of employment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Provide your section and subsection in the company's organizational structure.
6. Enter your name as it appears on your passport or national ID.
7. Enter your section and subsection for form section 6 (optional).
8. Enter your role in the company.
9. Enter your subsection for form section 7 (optional).
10. Enter your job title.
11. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
12. Enter your nationality.
13. Enter your passport number.
14. Enter your address.
15. Enter your contact email.
16. Enter your contact phone number.
