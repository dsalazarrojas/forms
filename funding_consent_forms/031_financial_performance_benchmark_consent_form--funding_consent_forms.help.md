# Financial Performance Benchmark Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Performance Benchmark Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures all stakeholders, including participants and project leads, are aware and in agreement with the terms of data collection and use. This form is essential for maintaining the transparency and trustworthiness of financial data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before completing this form.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate completion.
3. Answer all required fields marked as "required".
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the project lead or relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as a participant in the project.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Contact Number** (`participant_contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Benchmark Project** (`benchmark_project`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project lead or responsible person.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project Finish Date** (`project_finish_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the finish date of the project.
* **Benchmark Data** (`benchmark_data`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant benchmark data related to the project.
* **Participant Informed Consent** (`participant_informed_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your level of informed consent.
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of data collection and use.
	+ Option 2: I have questions or concerns about the project's terms and conditions.
	+ Option 3: I do not agree to the project's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Informed Consent 2** (`participant_informed_consent_2`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your level of informed consent.
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of data collection and use.
	+ Option 2: I have questions or concerns about the project's terms and conditions.
	+ Option 3: I do not agree to the project's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Informed Consent 3** (`participant_informed_consent_3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your level of informed consent.
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the terms of data collection and use.
	+ Option 2: I have questions or concerns about the project's terms and conditions.
	+ Option 3: I do not agree to the project's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required: false): Enter your signature to confirm your informed consent.
* **Participant Date** (`participant_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Project Signature** (`project_signature`, `text`, required: false): Enter the project lead's or responsible person's signature.
* **Project Date** (`project_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the project lead or responsible person signed this form.
* **Benchmark Financial Data** (`benchmark_financial_data`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant financial benchmark data related to the project.
* **Financial Data** (`financial_data`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant financial data related to the project.
* **Project Financial Data** (`project_financial_data`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant financial data related to the project.
* **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, `text`, required: false): Enter your institution or affiliation.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, required: false): Enter the institution or affiliation of the project lead or responsible person.

## Tips
* Ensure you have read and understood the project's terms and conditions before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the project lead or relevant authorities.
* This form is a legal document, and your informed consent is necessary for the project's continuation.
