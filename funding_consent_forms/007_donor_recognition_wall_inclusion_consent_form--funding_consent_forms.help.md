# Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form is a form used to collect information from donors about their preferences for how they want to be recognized and thanked for their contributions to your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and signature in the provided fields.
2. Select the options that represent how you would like to be recognized and thanked for your donation.
3. Provide your email and phone number, if you would like to be contacted for further information.
4. Review and agree to the terms of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Recognition Wall Inclusion Consent Form** (`form_title`, text, required): This field is the title of the form. Please fill in your name as it should be displayed on the recognition wall.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, optional): This field allows you to specify a category for your recognition, which will help us understand how you want to be acknowledged.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): This field is for providing additional description or context for your recognition preferences.
* **Description Help** (`form_description_help`, text, optional): This field is for providing more context or details about your recognition preferences, if needed.
* **Display Donors** (`donoressure_include`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be recognized and thanked. This includes:
	+ Donors will have a wall of recognition on the main website
	+ Donors will have a listing in the donor newsletter
	+ Donors will have a special thank you letter
	+ Donors will receive a social media shoutout
	+ Donors will receive a call or email from our team to thank them
	+ Donors will be acknowledged in our annual report
	+ Donors will be recognized in an event program
	+ Donors will be acknowledged in a press release
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team for further information, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your phone number.
* **Agreement** (`form_agreement`, text, required): Please review and agree to the terms of the form.
* **Signature** (`form_signature`, text, required): Please sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Date of signature.
