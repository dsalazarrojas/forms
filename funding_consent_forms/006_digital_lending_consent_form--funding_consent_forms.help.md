# digital_lending_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital lending consent form is designed to collect information from borrowers regarding their loan details, employment status, and income verification for internal use within an organization or for loan processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the borrower's details, including loan amount, loan term, loan interest rate, and collateral type.
* Indicate whether the loan purpose is for the borrower's use or not.
* Select the borrower's employment status and income source.
* Choose whether the loan status is active or inactive.
* Verify the borrower's employment income by selecting multiple options.
* Verify the bank statement for the loan by selecting multiple options.
