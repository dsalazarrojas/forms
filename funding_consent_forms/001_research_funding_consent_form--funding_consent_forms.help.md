# Research Funding Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from participants in research studies, ensuring they understand what's involved and what rights they're giving up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's information in the fields below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter the participant's name and contact details for communication purposes.
	+ Label: Participant Information
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Do you consent to take part in this research study**: Check "Yes" if you consent to participate in the research study.
	+ Label: Do you consent to take part in this research study
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Optional
* **Do you want to end participation**: Check "Yes" if you want to end your participation in the research study.
	+ Label: Do you want to end participation
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Optional
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as the participant.
	+ Label: Participant Name
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date you want to end your participation in the research study.
	+ Label: Termination Date
	+ Type: Date field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Participant Contact**: Enter your contact details (email, phone number, etc.) for communication purposes.
	+ Label: Participant Contact
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Label: Participant Email
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
	+ Label: Participant Phone
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher conducting the study.
	+ Label: Researcher Name
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Researcher Affiliation**: Enter the affiliation of the researcher conducting the study.
	+ Label: Researcher Affiliation
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Researcher Phone**: Enter the phone number of the researcher conducting the study.
	+ Label: Researcher Phone
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional
* **Research Study ID**: Enter the ID of the research study.
	+ Label: Research Study ID
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Optional

## Tips
* Make sure to read and understand the terms of the research study before signing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researcher conducting the study.
* This form should be kept confidential and secure.
