# Creative Equipment Funding Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for organizations and projects seeking funding for creative equipment. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to provide the necessary information for the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting.
3. Review the form for any errors or inaccuracies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization applying for funding.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for phone calls.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the project or initiative being funded.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the project or initiative being funded.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter the total budget for the project.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of funding requested.
* **Funding Start Date** (`funding_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the funding period.
* **Funding End Date** (`funding_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the funding period.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors or inaccuracies before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administrator for assistance.
