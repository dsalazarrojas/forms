# local_impact_grant_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "local impact grant application form" is designed for organizations to apply for local impact grants. By filling out this form, applicants can provide detailed information about their project and increase their chances of securing funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully to ensure you understand what information it is intended to collect.
2. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **grant_application_form** (`grant_application_form`, text, required: false)
This field is intended for the applicant to provide a brief overview of their grant application, if desired.

### 2. **grant_category** (`grant_category`, select_one, required: false)
Select "Yes" if your project aligns with the grant's specific focus area or category. This helps the grant reviewers to quickly identify whether your project matches their funding priorities.

### 3. **grant_description** (`grant_description`, text, required: false)
Provide a detailed description of your project, explaining how it will positively impact your local community. Focus on the project's objectives, goals, and outcomes.

### 4. **grant_details** (`grant_details`, text, required: false)
In this field, you can provide any additional information or supporting materials that will help the grant reviewers assess your project. This could include project timelines, budgets, or risk management plans.

### 5. **funding_request_amount** (`funding_request_amount`, number, required: false)
Indicate the amount of funding you are requesting. This will help the grant committee determine the scope and scale of your project.

### 6. **organization_name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your organization or institution that will be implementing or managing the project.

### 7. **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false)
Provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the project or your organization's representative.

### 8. **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
Enter the email address of your contact person for any follow-up or correspondence.

### 9. **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false)
Provide the phone number of your contact person for any urgent or time-sensitive matters.

### 10. **project_location** (`project_location`, text, required: false)
Specify the location where the project will be implemented or carried out.

### 11. **project_start_date** (`project_start_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the expected start date of your project.

### 12. **project_end_date** (`project_end_date`, date, required: false)
Indicate the expected completion or end date of your project.

### 13. **grant_agreement** (`grant_agreement`, text, required: false)
This field is intended for the applicant to provide any agreements or commitments made with other parties related to the grant.

### 14. **grant_agreement_signed** (`grant_agreement_signed`, select_one, required: false)
Select "Yes" if you have already signed a grant agreement or have a commitment to do so.

### 15. **grant_agreement_signed_date** (`grant_agreement_signed_date`, date, required: false)
If you have signed a grant agreement, provide the date it was signed.

## Tips
- Please ensure you answer each question accurately and thoroughly to increase the chances of your grant application's success.
- Review the form before submission to avoid any potential mistakes or omissions.
- If you have any queries or doubts, do not hesitate to contact the relevant authorities or contacts for guidance.
