# Cost Share Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cost Share Program Application form is designed to collect information about your project for funding support. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure a smooth review process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program before proceeding.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide your contact details to enable us to reach out to you regarding your application.
3. **Project Details**: Describe your project in detail, including its objectives and expected outcomes.
4. **Project Location**: Choose the country where your project is located (United States, Canada, Mexico, or Other).
5. **Project Start Date** and **Project End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of your project if applicable.
6. **Funding Request**: Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
7. **Contact Person**: Enter the name and details of the person responsible for your project.
8. **Email** and **Phone**: Provide your contact email and phone number for communication purposes.
9. **Additional Comments**: If you have any further information or comments, please use this section.
10. **Upload Documents**: Attach any relevant documents related to your project.
11. **Upload Documents Comment**: If you have any comments about the attached documents, please use this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Location (id: 4)**: Choose the country where your project is located. This will help us understand the geographical context of your project.
* **Project Start Date (id: 5)** and **Project End Date (id: 6)**: If your project has a specific start and end date, please enter them in the respective fields. If not applicable, leave these fields blank.
* **Funding Request (id: 7)** and **Funding Amount (id: 8)**: Specify the amount of funding you are requesting for your project. Please ensure the amount is accurate and reasonable for your project requirements.
* **Contact Person (id: 9)**: Enter the name and details of the person responsible for your project. This will help us contact you directly regarding your application.
* **Email (id: 10)** and **Phone (id: 11)**: Provide your contact email and phone number for us to communicate with you about your application.
* **Upload Documents (id: 13)** and **Upload Documents Comment (id: 14)**: Attach any relevant documents supporting your project. If you have any comments about these documents, please use the Upload Documents Comment field.
* **Additional Comments (id: 12)**: If you have any further information or comments about your project, please use this section.

Note to the reviewer:
- Please ensure that each field has a clear label and explanation to avoid confusion.
- If any field is required, please mark it as "Required: true" to ensure end users understand its importance.
- If any field is not required, please ensure it has a clear explanation to avoid confusion.
