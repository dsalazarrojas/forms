# eco-housing-innovation-participation-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the eco-housing innovation project. By filling out this form, participants are providing their consent and expertise to contribute to the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, including Participant Name, Participant Email, Participant Expertise, Consent Terms, and Project Participation.
2.  Choose the appropriate options for Participant Communication (Participant updates, Email, SMS, or Phone).
3.  Ensure that all fields are filled correctly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for updates and information about the project.
* **Participant Expertise** (`expertise`, text, required): Briefly describe your relevant skills or experience in the field of eco-housing.
* **Consent Terms** (`consent_terms`, text, required): Read and agree to the project's consent terms to participate in the project.
* **Participant Communication** (`participant_communication`, select_multiple, required): Choose the preferred method for receiving project updates (Participant updates, Email, SMS, or Phone).
* **Project Participation** (`project_participation`, select_one, required): Select the project where you would like to participate (Eco Housing or Other).
