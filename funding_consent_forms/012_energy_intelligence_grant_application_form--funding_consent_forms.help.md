# Energy Intelligence Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Intelligence Grant Application Form is a tool used to gather information from applicants for Energy Intelligence Grants. The purpose of this form is to collect essential details about your project to evaluate its eligibility and suitability for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project title.
2. Enter the applicant information, including a unique ID number.
3. Describe your project in detail, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
4. Enter the location of your project.
5. Enter the grant amount requested.
6. Provide your contact information, including name, phone number, and email.
7. Enter the project budget.
8. Set the start and end dates for your project.
9. Indicate the project status (active, completed, or ongoing).
10. Choose the grant types you are applying for (energy efficiency, renewable energy, energy storage, or energy distribution).
11. Justify the grant amount requested.
12. Enter the implementation and completion dates for your project.
13. Provide information about the grant recipient.
14. Enter the grant agency and program details.
15. Specify the grant funding source.
16. Describe the project location in detail.
17. Choose the status of your grant application (submitted, pending, approved, or declined).
18. Enter any comments from the grant review.
19. Enter the name of the grant reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and concise title of your project.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, number, required): Enter a unique ID number for the applicant.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Describe your project in detail, including its purpose, goals, and objectives.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the location of your project.
* **Grant Amount Requested** (`grant_amount_requested`, number, required): Enter the amount of grant you are requesting.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, text, required): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your project (active, completed, or ongoing).
* **Grant Type** (`grant_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of grants you are applying for (energy efficiency, renewable energy, energy storage, or energy distribution).
* **Grant Amount Justification** (`grant_amount_justification`, text, required): Justify the grant amount requested.
* **Project Implementation Date** (`project_implementation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of project implementation.
* **Project Completion Date** (`project_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of project completion.
* **Grant Recipient** (`grant_recipient`, text, required): Enter the grant recipient.
* **Grant Agency** (`grant_agency`, text, required): Enter the grant agency.
* **Grant Program** (`grant_program`, text, required): Enter the grant program.
* **Grant Funding Source** (`grant_funding_source`, text, required): Enter the grant funding source.
* **Project Location Description** (`project_location_description`, text, required): Describe the location of your project in detail.
* **Grant Application Submission** (`grant_application_submission`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your grant application (submitted, pending, approved, or declined).
* **Grant Review Comments** (`grant_review_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the grant review.
* **Grant Reviewer** (`grant_reviewer`, text, required): Enter the name of the grant reviewer.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out each field completely and accurately. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the grant team.
