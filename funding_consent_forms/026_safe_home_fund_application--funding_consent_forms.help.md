# Safe Home Fund Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safe Home Fund Application form is used to collect information about homeowners' safety improvements. The information collected will be used to identify homes that are in need of safety enhancements and may be eligible for funding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the required field.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the required field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the required field.
4. Select whether the home has a safety improvement need.
5. If yes, provide a brief description of the improvement needed.
6. If necessary, select the status of the improvement (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter any follow-up actions or notes regarding the safety improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as the homeowner.
- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your home's address, if you wish to provide this information.
- **Type of Home** (Type of Home, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if your home has a safety improvement need.
- **Description** (Description, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the improvement you are requesting.
- **Need for Improvement** (Need for Improvement, note, optional): This field is for you to note if your home has any other safety improvement needs.
- **Requested Improvement** (Requested Improvement, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the requested improvement.
- **Improvement Status** (Improvement Status, select_multiple, required): Select 'Active' if the improvement is currently active, or 'Inactive' if it is not.
- **Follow-up Action** (Follow-up Action, note, optional): Enter any follow-up actions or notes about the safety improvement.

## Tips
- Please only submit this form once for each home.
- If you are seeking financial assistance for home improvements, please contact our office for further information.
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information so we can follow up with you.
- If you have any other safety improvement needs, please list them in the 'Need for Improvement' field.
