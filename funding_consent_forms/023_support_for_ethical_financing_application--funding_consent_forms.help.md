# Support For Ethical Financing Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information from applicants seeking support for their projects or research initiatives that align with ethical standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Click on each page to begin filling out the form.
- Fill in the required information accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each field carefully before proceeding to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details** (`Application Details`, text, required/optional): This section collects basic application details. Please provide a brief description of your project or initiative.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach out to you for further details, if needed.
* **Financial Details** (`Financial Details`, text, required/optional): Provide an overview of your project's financial status, including any funding sources or requirements.
* **Funding Sources** (`Funding Sources`, text, required/optional): List any available funding sources for your project, if applicable.
* **Funder Information** (`Funder Information`, text, required/optional): If you have any external funding, provide details about the funder and the funding amount.
* **Applicant Info** (`Applicant Info`, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant information about yourself, the project lead or principal investigator.
* **Supporting Documents** (`Supporting Documents`, text, required/optional): Attach any supporting documents or materials that will help us better understand your application.
* **Application Review** (`Application Review`, text, required/optional): This is the final step where you can provide any additional comments or remarks on your application.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): Review and finalize your application before submitting.
