# Accessibility Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Accessibility Grant Application Form is a tool to gather necessary information from applicants seeking accessibility grants for their projects. This form guides the applicant through the required information and ensures that all the necessary details are captured for grant processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form sections to understand the required information.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, following the guidelines provided.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Individual or Organization Name** (`applicant_org_name`): Enter the legal name of the applicant, whether an individual or an organization.
*   **Type of Applicant** (`applicant_type`): Select the most accurate category that best describes the applicant:
    *   Individual with Disability
    *   Non-profit Organization
    *   Educational Institution
    *   Small Business
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter the primary point of contact's email address.
*   **Grant Project Title** (`project_title`): Enter a descriptive and concise title for the project.
*   **Brief Summary of the Project** (`project_summary`): Provide a 150-word summary of the project.
*   **Primary focus of the project** (`focus_areas`): Select all the areas that apply to the project:
    *   Physical Accessibility
    *   Digital or Web Accessibility
    *   Educational Support
    *   Assistive Technology
    *   Community Outreach
*   **Who will benefit from this grant?** (`targeted_population`): Describe the specific community that will benefit from the project.
*   **Estimated number of people impacted** (`estimated_beneficiaries`): Enter the estimated number of people who will be impacted by the project.
*   **What are the key goals of this project?** (`primary_objectives`): List the three main objectives of the project.
*   **Total Project Budget in USD** (`total_budget`): Enter the total budget for the project, including all costs.
*   **Amount Requested from this Grant** (`grant_amount_requested`): Enter the amount of funding sought from this grant.
*   **Briefly explain how the funds will be used** (`budget_breakdown`): Explain how the funds will be used, covering core expense categories.
*   **Estimated Project Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the estimated start date for the project.
*   **Estimated Project End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the estimated completion date for the project.
*   **I Pledge to use the funds solely for the purposes described** (`accountability_pledge`): Select the option that best describes the applicant's pledge:
    *   I Pledge
    *   Other
*   **Signature of Authorized Official** (`authorized_signature`): Type in the name of the authorized official signing the document.

## Tips

*   Be sure to answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
*   Ensure that you have provided all necessary information before submitting the form.
