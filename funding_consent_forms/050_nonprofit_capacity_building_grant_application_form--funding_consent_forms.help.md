# Nonprofit Capacity Building Grant Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for nonprofit organizations to apply for capacity-building grants, which aim to support the development and growth of nonprofit organizations. The form is used to gather information about the organization, project details, funding requirements, and other relevant information to assess the eligibility and suitability of the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about your organization.
2. Provide detailed information about your project, including its objectives, location, budget, and timeline.
3. Specify your funding requirements and preferences.
4. Select the relevant options for your project's funding status, location, and funding source.
5. Add any additional information that is not covered in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details**: This field is for providing a brief description of your nonprofit organization, including its name, address, and contact information.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Grant History**: Describe your previous grant experiences, including successful and unsuccessful applications.
* **Project Details**: This field is for providing a detailed description of your project, including its objectives, location, and budget.
* **Project Budget**: Enter the estimated budget for your project.
* **Project Duration**: Specify the start and end dates of your project.
* **Funding Requirements**: Specify your funding requirements, including the amount and type of funding you need.
* **Funding Allocation**: Select the option that best represents your funding allocation preference.
* **Project Location**: Enter the location where your project will be implemented.
* **Project Location Map**: Provide a map or a description of your project location.
* **Project Start Date**: Specify the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date**: Specify the end date of your project.
* **Project Status**: Select the option that best represents the current status of your project.
* **Project Funding Source**: Enter the source of your project funding (e.g., government, foundation, etc.).
* **Project Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you are seeking.
* **Project Funding Status**: Select the option that best represents the current status of your project funding.
* **Project Funding Status Details**: Provide any additional information about your project funding status.
* **Project Funding Source Notes**: Enter any notes about the source of your project funding.
* **Project Funding Allocation Notes**: Enter any notes about your project funding allocation.
* **Project Funding Additional Info**: Select any additional information that is relevant to your project funding.
* **Project Funding Notes**: Enter any additional information about your project funding.
* **Project Funding Status Notes**: Enter any additional information about your project funding status.
* **Grant Manager**: Enter the name and contact information of your grant manager.
* **Grant Manager Contact**: Enter your grant manager's contact information.

**Tips**

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your organization and project.
* Attach any supporting documents or evidence that may be required for your application.
* Review and proofread your application carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need help with the application process, contact us at [insert contact information].
