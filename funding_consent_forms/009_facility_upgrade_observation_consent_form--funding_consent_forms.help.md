# Facility Upgrade Observation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Upgrade Observation Consent Form is used to obtain consent from facility staff or stakeholders for facility upgrades or modifications. This form ensures that all necessary parties are aware of and agree to the changes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select each field as instructed below:
- Fill in the Facility Upgrade Observation Consent Form fields as follows:
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for Facility Upgrade, indicating whether the upgrade is taking place or not.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for Observation Consent, indicating whether or not you consent to the upgrade.
  - Enter the date and time of the consent.
  - Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - Enter your phone number, including any extension, for communication purposes.
  - Enter any additional notes or comments about the upgrade.
  - Select the facility where the upgrade is taking place from the provided options.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for the reason for the upgrade, indicating whether or not you agree with the reason.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for consent, indicating whether or not you give consent to the upgrade.
  - Enter your name for signature purposes.
  - Enter your title for signature purposes.
  - Select your department from the provided options, indicating which department is involved in the upgrade.
  - Enter your job title for signature purposes.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for the organization involved in the upgrade, indicating whether or not you agree with the organization.
  - Enter the location of the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for the country involved in the upgrade, indicating whether or not you agree with the country.
  - Select "Yes" or "No" for the state involved in the upgrade, indicating whether or not you agree with the state.
  - Enter the postal code of the location where the upgrade is taking place.
  - Enter the fax number of the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - Enter the phone extension for the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
  - Enter any additional notes or comments about the upgrade.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Upgrade**: This field is used to indicate whether the upgrade is taking place or not. Select "Yes" or "No" according to your observation.
  - **Facility Upgrade Observation Consent**: This field is used to indicate whether or not you consent to the upgrade. Select "Yes" or "No" according to your consent.
  - **Observation Consent Date**: Enter the date of the consent, in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
  - **Observation Consent Time**: Enter the time of the consent, in the format of 12-hour time (e.g., 2:30 PM).
  - **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, including any extension, for communication purposes.
  - **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the upgrade.
  - **Facility**: Select the facility where the upgrade is taking place from the provided options.
  - **Reason for Upgrade**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating whether or not you agree with the reason for the upgrade.
  - **Consent**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating whether or not you give consent to the upgrade.
  - **Name**: Enter your name for signature purposes.
  - **Title**: Enter your title for signature purposes.
  - **Department**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating which department is involved in the upgrade.
  - **Job Title**: Enter your job title for signature purposes.
  - **Organization**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating which organization is involved in the upgrade.
  - **Location**: Enter the location of the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - **Country**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating which country is involved in the upgrade.
  - **State**: Select "Yes" or "No" indicating which state is involved in the upgrade.
  - **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the location where the upgrade is taking place.
  - **Fax Number**: Enter the fax number of the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - **Phone Extension**: Enter the phone extension for the facility where the upgrade is taking place.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
  - **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the upgrade.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Ensure that you have selected the correct facility and organization involved in the upgrade.
- Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
