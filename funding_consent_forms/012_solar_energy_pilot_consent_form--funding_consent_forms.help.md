# Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Solar Energy Pilot Consent Form is designed to collect participant information and consent for a solar energy pilot program. This form aims to gather necessary details and ensure that participants understand their roles and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Information" section, providing the required details.
2. In the "Contact Details" section, enter your contact information.
3. In the "Participant Signature" section, sign and date the form to confirm your participation and understanding of the program.
4. Select your "Consent" option by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the corresponding field.
5. In the "Solar Pilot Program" section, enter your details about the solar pilot program you are participating in.
6. Finally, in the "Data Collection" section, provide any additional information about data collection related to the solar pilot program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (participant_info)**: This section is for entering your participant information, including any relevant details about your involvement in the solar pilot program.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Enter your contact information to ensure we can reach you for any further questions or updates about the program.
* **Participant Signature (signature)**: Please sign and date this form to confirm your participation and understanding of the program.
* **Consent (consent)**: Select "Yes" if you agree to participate and understand the program's terms and expectations; select "No" if you do not agree.
* **Solar Pilot Program (solar_pilot_program)**: Provide details about the specific solar pilot program you are participating in.
* **Data Collection (data_collection)**: Enter any additional information related to data collection for the solar pilot program.

## Tips
- Ensure you have reviewed and understood the terms and expectations of the solar pilot program before completing this form.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the program, please reach out to us for clarification.
- Complete this form carefully to ensure accurate and comparable data.
