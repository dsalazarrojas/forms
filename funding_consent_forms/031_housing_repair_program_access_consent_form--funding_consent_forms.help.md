# Housing Repair Program Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from program participants for the Housing Repair Program. It contains essential details about the user, contact, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the **User Information** page.
2. Fill in your personal details, which are marked as required.
3. Proceed to the **Contact Information** page, if you wish to provide an alternative contact.
4. On the **Resident Information** page, enter your details, if necessary.
5. On the **Form Submission** page, select a date and time for form submission.
6. Review the **Program Agreement**, signifying that you understand and agree with the program's terms.
7. Enter details about your emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact** page.
8. Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact.
9. Complete the **Emergency Contact Phone**, **Email**, and **Address** fields if applicable.
10. Review and agree to the **Emergency Contact Agreement**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`): This is where you provide your personal details, including name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): If you want to provide an alternative contact, fill in their details here.
* **Resident Information** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): Enter your resident information if applicable.
* **Form Submission** (`name`, `type`: `date`, `required`: `false`): Choose the date and time for form submission.
* **Program Agreement** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `true`): This field requires you to sign an agreement to understand and respect the program's terms.
* **Emergency Contact** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): Enter your emergency contact's details if you wish to provide them.
* **Relationship** (`name`, `type`: `select_one`, `required`: `false`): Choose your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): Provide your emergency contact's phone number, if applicable.
* **Email** (`name`, `type`: `email`, `required`: `false`): Enter your emergency contact's email, if applicable.
* **Address** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `false`): Provide your emergency contact's address, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Agreement** (`name`, `type`: `text`, `required`: `true`): Review and agree to the emergency contact agreement.

## Tips

- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up to date.
- Review each page thoroughly before proceeding to ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the above explanations or contact a program representative.
- Complete this form in one instance, and only once, to avoid redundancy.
