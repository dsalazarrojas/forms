# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about the respondent's perception of climate policy and its importance. It is used to assess the respondent's understanding of climate policy, their willingness to participate in climate policy events, and the factors that influence their views on climate policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Please select one of the options for each of the questions that ask for a choice (e.g., category, climate policy importance, climate policy knowledge, etc.).
*   If you select "None" or "Don't Know" in any of the questions, please select the corresponding option.
*   Please provide your name, email, and phone number if you want to get updates or follow-up information on climate policy.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" section.
*   Finally, review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **1. Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey**: This is a title field that does not require an answer.
*   **2. category**: Please select one of the categories that best fits your affiliation (NGO, School, Advocacy Group, etc.).
*   **3. description**: This is an optional field where you can provide a brief description of your affiliation.
*   **4. how_important_is_climate_policy_to_you**: Please select how important climate policy is to you (Very Important, Somewhat Important, Not Important).
*   **5. what_do_you_know_about_climate_policy**: Select what you know about climate policy (A lot, Some, A little, None).
*   **6. how_well_do_you_feel_influenced_by_climate_policy**: Please select how well you feel influenced by climate policy (Very Influenced, Somewhat Influenced, Not Influenced, Very little).
*   **7. would_you_participate_in_climate_policy_events**: Please select if you would participate in climate policy events (True, False).
*   **8. name**: If you want to get updates or follow-up information on climate policy, please enter your name.
*   **9. email**: If you want to get updates or follow-up information on climate policy, please enter your email.
*   **10. phone**: If you want to get updates or follow-up information on climate policy, please enter your phone number.
*   **11. organization**: If you want to get updates or follow-up information on climate policy, please enter your organization's name.
*   **12. message**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
*   **13. additional_comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
*   **14. feedback**: If you have any feedback or suggestions, please enter them here.
*   **15. what_do_you_know_about_climate_change**: Please select what you know about climate change (A lot, Some, A little, None).
*   **16. what_is_your_attitude_towards_climate_change**: Please select your attitude towards climate change (Positive, Negative, Neutral).
*   **17. how_much_support_would_you_give_to_climate_change**: Please select how much support you would give to climate change (Very High, Somewhat High, Low, None).
*   **18. what_is_your_source_of_info_about_climate_policy**: Please select where you get your information about climate policy (Social Media, Family, Friends, School, Online Research, Newspaper, TV, Internet, Book, Other).
*   **19. how_much_do_you_agree_with_the_statement**: Please select how much you agree with the statement "Climate Change is a very serious issue that requires immediate action" (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither, Somewhat Disagree, Strongly Disagree).
*   **20. do_you_know_where_to_find_more_info_on_climate_policy**: Please select if you know where to find more information on climate policy (True, False, Don’t Know).
*   **21. what_sources_have_influenced_your_climate_policy_views**: Please select the sources that have influenced your views on climate policy (Social Media, Family, Friends, School, Online Research, Newspaper, TV, Internet, Book, Other).
*   **22. how_much_have_social_media_influenced_your_climate_policy_views**: Please select how much social media has influenced your climate policy views (Very High, Somewhat High, Low, None).
*   **23. who_have_you_consulted_for_climate_info**: Please select who you have consulted for climate information (Social Media, Family, Friends, School, Online Research, Newspaper, TV, Internet, Book, Other).
*   **24. how_much_support_would_you_give_to_climate_change**: Please select how much support you would give to climate change (Very High, Somewhat High, Low, None).
*   **25. what_would_influence_your_climate_policy_participation**: Please select what would influence your participation in climate policy (Social Media, Family, Friends, School, Online Research, Newspaper, TV, Internet, Book, Other).

## Tips

*   If you select "None" or "Don't Know" in any of the questions, please select the corresponding option.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" section.
*   Please review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate and complete.
