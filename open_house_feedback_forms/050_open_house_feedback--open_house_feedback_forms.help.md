# open_house_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about an open house event. To provide your feedback, please fill out this form to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Answer all the required fields (marked as "required") to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the provided fields with your feedback and comments.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Open House Info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the open house event.
* **Open House Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional details about your experience with the open house event.
* **Comments** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the open house event.
* **Score** (`4`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the open house event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating** (`5`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of the open house event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommend** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Do you recommend this open house event? (Yes/No)
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the open house event you attended.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the open house event you attended.
* **Contact** (`9`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address or contact information.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments 2** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Comments 3** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in the fields.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the additional comments fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email/phone number].
