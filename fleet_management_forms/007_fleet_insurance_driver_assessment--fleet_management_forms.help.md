# Fleet Insurance Driver Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about drivers in a fleet management context to assess their risk profiles and determine their suitability for driving company vehicles or other fleet-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide the number of years the driver has been driving.
3. Describe the driver's driving history, including any accidents or incidents they have been involved in.
4. Provide information about the driver's incident history, including any accidents or incidents they have been involved in.
5. Describe the vehicle(s) the driver owns or has access to.
6. Indicate how often the driver uses their vehicle.
7. Enter the make and model of the vehicle(s) the driver owns or has access to.
8. Provide the driver's license number.
9. Enter the vehicle identification number (VIN) if applicable.
10. Answer the questions about the driver's vehicle license type and state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Driver Details
• **Driver Details**: Enter a brief description of the driver, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.

### Years Of Experience
• **Years Of Experience** (`number`, optional): Enter the number of years the driver has been driving.

### Driving History
• **Driving History** (`text`, optional): Describe the driver's driving history, including any accidents or incidents they have been involved in.

### Incident History
• **Incident History** (`text`, optional): Provide information about the driver's incident history, including any accidents or incidents they have been involved in.

### Vehicle Ownership
• **Vehicle Ownership** (`text`, optional): Describe the vehicle(s) the driver owns or has access to.

### Vehicle Use
• **Vehicle Use** (`text`, optional): Indicate how often the driver uses their vehicle, e.g., daily, weekly, or rarely.

### Vehicle Make And Model
• **Vehicle Make And Model** (`text`, optional): Enter the make and model of the vehicle(s) the driver owns or has access to.

### Driver License Number
• **Driver License Number** (`text`, optional): Enter the driver's license number.

### Vehicle Identification Number
• **Vehicle Identification Number** (`text`, optional): Enter the vehicle identification number (VIN) if applicable.

### Driver Vehicle License Type
• **Driver Vehicle License Type** (select one, optional): Answer if the driver has a commercial or private vehicle license.
• **Yes** or **No**

### Driver Vehicle License State
• **Driver Vehicle License State** (select one, optional): Answer if the driver's license is from a different state or not.
• **Yes** or **No**

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to ensure the assessment is thorough and fair.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, please consult the driver's records or ask the fleet manager for clarification.
- Remember to review and update the form regularly to ensure it remains accurate and up-to-date.
