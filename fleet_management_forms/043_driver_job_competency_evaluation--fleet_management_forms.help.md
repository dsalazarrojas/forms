# Driver Job Competency Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Job Competency Evaluation form is designed to assess the skills and knowledge of a driver in a fleet management context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a brief description of the driver's job competency evaluation in the **Driver Job Competency** field.
2. Select or enter the fleet management strategies or best practices in place to evaluate or improve driver performance in the **Fleet Management** field.
3. If you want to standardize evaluations, check 'Yes' in the **Standardize Evaluations** field. If not, check 'No'.
4. Enter any feedback or comments on documentation related to driver performance in the **Document Feedback** field.
5. Enter any comments or actions taken on managing driver performance records in the **Manage Submission Records** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Driver Job Competency** (Note, text, required): Enter a brief description of the driver's job competency evaluation.
* **Fleet Management** (Text, required): Select or enter the fleet management strategies or best practices in place to evaluate or improve driver performance.
* **Standardize Evaluations** (Select Multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if you want to standardize evaluations or 'No' otherwise.
* **Document Feedback** (Text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments on documentation related to driver performance.
* **Manage Submission Records** (Text, optional): Enter any comments or actions taken on managing driver performance records.
