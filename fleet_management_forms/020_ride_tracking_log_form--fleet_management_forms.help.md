# Ride Tracking Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ride Tracking Log Form is a crucial tool for tracking vehicle trips and rides, ensuring that fleet managers have accurate and up-to-date information on vehicle use, fuel consumption, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: `driver_name`, `start_location`, `end_location`, `ride_start_time`, and `ride_end_time`.
2. Provide trip details: `trip_date`, `trip_time`, `trip_distance`, and `trip_duration`.
3. Enter vehicle information: `vehicle_type`, `vehicle_number`, `vehicle_condition`, and `fuel_consumption_rate`.
4. Update ride status: `ride_status`.
5. Add any additional notes: `trip_notes`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info**: Enter the vehicle's information, including its type, number, and condition.
* **Trip Details**: Provide information about the trip, including the date, time, distance, and duration.
* **Route Map**: Select the route taken during the trip.
* **Driver Name**: Enter the name of the driver who took the trip.
* **Trip Date**: Enter the date of the trip.
* **Trip Time**: Enter the time of the trip.
* **Trip Distance**: Enter the total distance covered during the trip.
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the duration of the trip.
* **Total Fuel Used**: Enter the total fuel used during the trip.
* **Fuel Consumption Rate**: Enter the fuel consumption rate of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Select the condition of the vehicle after the trip.
* **Ride Location**: Select the locations visited during the trip.
* **Start Location**: Enter the starting point of the trip.
* **End Location**: Enter the ending point of the trip.
* **Ride Start Time**: Enter the time the trip started.
* **Ride End Time**: Enter the time the trip ended.
* **Total Miles Covered**: Enter the total miles covered during the trip.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the trip.
* **Ride Status**: Update the ride status to Active or Inactive.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to save the form data.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by drivers or fleet managers to provide accurate and up-to-date information on vehicle trips and rides.
