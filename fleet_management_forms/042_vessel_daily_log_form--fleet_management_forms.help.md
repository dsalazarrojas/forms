# Vessel Daily Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vessel Daily Log Form is a tool used to record and report information about the daily operations and activities of a vessel. It helps in monitoring and tracking the state of the vessel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the correct information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a field has options, select the most appropriate one from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vessel Operations** (`vessel_operations`, text, Optional): Enter a brief description of any notable events or activities that occurred during the day, such as repairs, maintenance, or any other notable occurrences.
* **Crew List** (`crew_list`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate if there are any crew members on board. Choose 'Yes' to confirm crew presence.
* **Vessel Name** (`vessel_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the vessel.
* **Port Arrival** (`port_arrival`, date, Optional): Record the date and time of the vessel's arrival at a port.
* **Port Departure** (`port_departure`, date, Optional): Record the date and time of the vessel's departure from a port.
* **Vessel Location** (`vessel_location`, text, Optional): Describe the current location of the vessel.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the vessel's state or activities.
* **Vessel Type** (`vessel_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of vessel (e.g., Vessel Name, Vessel Number, Ship, or Boat).
* **Crew Members** (`crew_members`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate if there are any crew members on board. Choose 'Yes' to confirm crew presence.
* **Vessel Speed** (`vessel_speed`, number, Optional): Enter the current speed of the vessel.
* **Vessel Course** (`vessel_course`, number, Optional): Enter the current course of the vessel.
* **Vessel Heading** (`vessel_heading`, text, Optional): Enter the direction the vessel is heading.
* **Time In** (`time_in`, time, Optional): Record the time the vessel arrived at a location.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, Optional): Record the time the vessel departed from a location.
* **Weather** (`weather`, select_one, Optional): Describe the current weather conditions (e.g., Fine, Windy, Rainy, Stormy, Unknown).
