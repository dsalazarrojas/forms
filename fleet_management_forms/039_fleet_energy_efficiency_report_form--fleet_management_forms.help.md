# Fleet Energy Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Energy Efficiency Report Form is designed to collect data on the energy efficiency of a vehicle or vehicle fleet. This data will be used to analyze and improve the energy efficiency of the vehicle or fleet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Vehicle Details section with the relevant information about the vehicle, including its type, fuel type, and other necessary details.
2. Enter the amount of fuel used for the vehicle or fleet in the Fuel Used field.
3. Record the distance traveled by the vehicle or fleet in the Distance Traveled field.
4. Select the energy efficiency metric for the vehicle or fleet from the Efficiency Metric field.
5. Enter the date and time of the vehicle's or fleet's usage in the Date and Time fields.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about the vehicle or fleet in the Notes field.
7. Enter the email address of the contact person for the vehicle or fleet in the Email field.
8. Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vehicle or fleet in the Phone field.
9. Select the type of vehicle from the Vehicle Type field.
10. Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle or fleet from the Fuel Type field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details**: Enter the name and model of the vehicle, as well as its year and any other relevant details.
* **Fuel Used**: Enter the amount of fuel used for the vehicle or fleet in the specified unit (e.g., liters or gallons).
* **Distance Traveled**: Enter the total distance traveled by the vehicle or fleet in the specified unit (e.g., kilometers or miles).
* **Efficiency Metric**: Select the level of energy efficiency for the vehicle or fleet (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Date**: Enter the date of the vehicle's or fleet's usage in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the vehicle's or fleet's usage in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the vehicle or fleet.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the vehicle or fleet.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the vehicle or fleet.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle from the list (e.g., Car, Truck, Bus, Bike).
* **Fuel Type**: Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle or fleet from the list (e.g., Gasoline, Diesel, Electric).
