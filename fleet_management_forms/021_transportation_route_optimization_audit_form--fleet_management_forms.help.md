# Transportation Route Optimization Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the characteristics of a transportation route, including its description, name, length, distance, type, frequency, and status. The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized way to collect and store information about each route in the fleet management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the description of the route in the "Route Description" field.
2. Enter the name of the route in the "Route Name" field.
3. Enter the length of the route in the "Route Length" field.
4. Enter the distance of the route in the "Route Distance" field.
5. Select the type of route from the options in the "Route Type" field.
6. Select the frequency of the route from the options in the "Route Frequency" field.
7. Enter the start time of the route in the "Start Time" field.
8. Enter the end time of the route in the "End Time" field.
9. Select the status of the route from the options in the "Route Status" field.
10. Enter any comments about the route in the "Route Comments" field.
11. Enter the vehicles used on the route in the "Route Vehicles" field.
12. Enter the drivers assigned to the route in the "Route Drivers" field.
13. Enter any additional notes about the route frequency in the "Route Frequency Notes" field.
14. Enter any comments about the route status in the "Route Status Comments" field.
15. Enter any notes about the route optimization in the "Route Optimization Notes" field.
16. Enter any issues with the route in the "Route Issues" field.
17. Enter any improvement plan for the route in the "Route Improvement Plan" field.
18. Enter any review comments about the route in the "Route Review" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Description**: Enter a brief description of the route. This will help other users understand the purpose and scope of the route.
* **Route Name**: Enter the name of the route as it is commonly known in your system.
* **Route Length**: Enter the total length of the route in kilometers.
* **Route Distance**: Enter the total distance traveled on the route in kilometers.
* **Route Type**: Select the type of route from the options provided:
	+ Less Than 50 km
	+ 50-100 km
	+ 100-200 km
	+ More than 200 km
* **Route Frequency**: Select the frequency of the route from the options provided:
	+ Less Than 1 time per month
	+ 1 time per month
	+ 2-3 times per month
	+ 4-6 times per month
	+ More than 6 times per month
* **Optimal Route**: Enter the optimal route schedule date.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the route.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the route.
* **Route Status**: Select the status of the route from the options provided:
	+ Inactive
	+ Active
	+ Cancelled
* **Route Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the route.
* **Route Vehicles**: Enter the vehicles assigned to the route.
* **Route Drivers**: Enter the drivers assigned to the route.
* **Route Frequency Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the route frequency.
* **Route Status Comments**: Enter any comments about the route status.
* **Route Optimization Notes**: Enter any notes about the route optimization.
* **Route Issues**: Enter any issues with the route.
* **Route Improvement Plan**: Enter any improvement plan for the route.
* **Route Review**: Enter any review comments about the route.
