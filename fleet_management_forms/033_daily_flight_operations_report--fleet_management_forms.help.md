This form is designed to capture daily flight operations data, including report details, flight details, actual flight time, scheduled arrival time, delay reasons, comments, and other relevant information. The purpose of this form is to provide an efficient way for users to report and track daily flights, including any delays, cancellations, or other notable events.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Report Details
- Enter your report title and any other relevant details about the report in the **Report Details** field.

### Step 2: Flight Details
- Enter the flight number, aircraft registration, and other flight details in the **Flight Details** field.

### Step 3: Actual Flight Time and Scheduled Arrival Time
- Enter the actual flight time and scheduled arrival time for the flight in the **Actual Flight Time** and **Scheduled Arrival Time** fields, respectively.

### Step 4: Delay Reason and Comments
- Choose the reason for delay from the drop-down menu in the **Delay Reason** field and enter any comments or notes about the delay in the **Comments** field.

### Step 5: Additional Information
- If applicable, select the type of delay and any other flight details from the drop-down menus in the **Scheduled Arrival Time Difference** and **Flight Details Difference** fields.

### Step 6: Contact Information
- Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, respectively.

### Step 7: Submission
- Enter your name and role for submission purposes in the **Assigned To** and **Submitted By** fields, respectively.
- Select the report type (e.g., Daily, Weekly, etc.) and the flight number from the drop-down menus in the **Report Type** and **Flight Number** fields, respectively.
