# Transit Temperature Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transit Temperature Control Checklist is a form used to monitor and report on temperature conditions during shipping to ensure that perishable items or sensitive materials are handled and transported under suitable conditions to prevent damage, spoilage, or contamination.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter all relevant information about the shipment, including the shipment details.
2. Specify the type of container used for the shipment.
3. Record the temperature control measures taken during transit.
4. Check the documents required for the shipment.
5. Document any corrective actions taken in case of temperature deviations.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipment Details** (`Shipment Details`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the shipment, including date, time, and any relevant notes or comments.
* **Transit Container** (`Transit Container`, `text`, required): Specify the type of container used for the shipment (e.g., refrigerated, insulated, etc.).
* **Temperature Control** (`Temperature Control`, `text`, required): Record the temperature control measures taken during transit, such as temperature sensors, coolants, or insulation.
* **Document Check** (`Document Check`, `text`, required): Check the documents required for the shipment, such as receipts, manifests, or permits.
* **Corrective Action** (`Corrective Action`, `text`, required): Document any corrective actions taken in case of temperature deviations, such as adjusting packaging, rerouting, or re-palletizing.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date this form to confirm that it has been completed accurately.

## Tips
This form is meant for one-time use. If you need to complete it multiple times, please make sure to review the previous submission for any necessary changes or updates before making a new one.
