# Logistics Transportation Safety Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and report on the safety and status of vehicles in the fleet, including their current status, inspection results, and any necessary notes or comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Fleet Manager's name and the Vehicle Number of the vehicle being audited.
2. Select the Vehicle Type (Truck, Tractor, or Tractor Trailer).
3. Enter the Safety Check Date.
4. Enter the Driver's Name.
5. Select the Vehicle Status (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter any relevant Safety Check Notes.
7. Check if the Vehicle Inspection was completed (Yes or No).
8. Check if the Driver's Signature is present (Yes or No).
9. Select the Carrier Info (e.g., company or agency).
10. Enter the Driver's Phone Number.
11. Check if the Carrier Signature is present (Yes or No).
12. Enter the name of the Fleet Inspector.
13. Check if the Inspector's Signature is present (Yes or No).
14. Enter the Vehicle Year.
15. Enter the Vehicle Make.
16. Enter the Vehicle Model.
17. Enter the current Odometer Reading.
18. Enter the Fuel Level (e.g., percentage).
19. Select the Safety Check Status (Active or Inactive).
20. Enter any notes regarding the Safety Check Status.
21. Check if the form was submitted by the Driver (Yes or No).
