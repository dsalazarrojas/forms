# Daily Shuttle Maintenance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Shuttle Maintenance Checklist is a form used to evaluate the status of the vehicle's components and systems. It helps ensure that the vehicle is in good condition and ready for use. This form is typically completed by the vehicle operator or maintenance personnel on a daily basis to track the vehicle's condition and identify potential issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's condition and assess the status of each component.
2. Select the status for each component from the options provided (Good, Not good, or Not sure).
3. Enter the number of miles or date for odometer and tires fields.
4. Enter any comments or issues that require attention.
5. Set the next maintenance date for the vehicle.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, select_one): Select the overall status of the vehicle.
	+ Good: The vehicle is in good condition and ready for use.
	+ Not good: The vehicle has issues and requires maintenance.
	+ Not sure: The vehicle's condition is unknown.
* **Brakes** (`brakes`, select_multiple): Check the condition of the vehicle's brakes.
	+ Good: The brakes are functioning properly.
	+ Not good: The brakes are damaged or not functioning properly.
	+ Not sure: The condition of the brakes is unknown.
* **Lights** (`lights`, number): Check the condition of the vehicle's lights (e.g., turn signals, headlights, brake lights).
	+ Enter the number of lights that are functioning properly.
* **Mirrors** (`mirrors`, select_one): Check the condition of the vehicle's mirrors.
	+ Good: The mirrors are clean and adjustable.
	+ Not good: The mirrors are dirty or not functioning properly.
	+ Not sure: The condition of the mirrors is unknown.
* **Seatbelts** (`seatbelts`, select_multiple): Check the condition of the vehicle's seatbelts.
	+ Good: The seatbelts are functioning properly.
	+ Not good: The seatbelts are damaged or not functioning properly.
	+ Not sure: The condition of the seatbelts is unknown.
* **Tires** (`tires`, number): Check the tread depth and condition of the tires.
	+ Enter the number of tires that need replacement or repair.
* **Odometer** (`odometer`, number): Record the current odometer reading.
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, select_one): Check the fuel level in the tank.
	+ High: The fuel level is adequate.
	+ Low: The fuel level is low.
	+ Empty: The fuel tank is empty.
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_multiple): Check the type of fuel used in the vehicle.
	+ Gasoline: The vehicle is fueled with gasoline.
	+ Diesel: The vehicle is fueled with diesel.
	+ Electric: The vehicle is electric or battery-powered.
* **Maintenance** (`maintenance`, text): Enter any maintenance notes or issues that require attention.
* **Issues** (`issues`, select_multiple): Check for any issues or problems with the vehicle.
	+ Issue 1: There is an issue with the vehicle.
	+ Issue 2: There is another issue with the vehicle.
* **Next Maintenance** (`next_maintenance`, date): Set the next maintenance date for the vehicle.
