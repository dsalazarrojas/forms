# Vehicle Deactivation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Deactivation Form is a tool for documenting the deactivation of vehicles within a fleet. It helps in keeping the vehicle management system up-to-date and accurate. Please fill out this form to provide essential information about the vehicle being deactivated.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  If a field is marked as required, ensure that you provide the necessary information.
3.  If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact your supervisor or the vehicle management team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Deactivation Form**: This is the title of the form, which cannot be edited. It is for reference purposes only.
*   **Vehicle Information**: This field is used to describe the vehicle being deactivated. Please enter a brief description or details about the vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Number**: Enter the unique identification number of the vehicle being deactivated. This number is usually found on the vehicle's registration document.
*   **Reason**: Provide a clear and specific reason for deactivating the vehicle. Examples may include:
    \- Vehicle sale or trade-in
    \- Vehicle retirement or replacement
    \- Vehicle damage or malfunction
    \- Other (please specify)
*   **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or details about the deactivation process, please enter them in this field.
*   **Signature**: Date and sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
*   If the vehicle is being transferred to another unit or organization, please ensure that the correct contact details are updated in the vehicle management system.
*   If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to your supervisor or the vehicle management team.
