# Vehicle Speed Limiter Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering or reporting information about a vehicle's speed limiter installation. The form will help the company track and manage the speed limiter details for their vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Vehicle Owner Details page with the relevant information.
2. Move on to the Vehicle Details page and provide information about the vehicle.
3. Complete the Speed Limiter Details page with the necessary information.
4. Provide any relevant Installation Info on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vehicle Owner Details
* **Vehicle Owner Details** (text): This page is where you will enter the owner's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Owner Name** (text): Enter the name of the vehicle's owner or the person responsible for the vehicle.
* **Contact Information** (text): Provide the contact details of the owner or the person responsible for the vehicle.

### Vehicle Details
* **Vehicle Details** (text): This page is where you will enter the vehicle's details.
* **Vehicle Year and Model** (text): Enter the year and model of the vehicle.
* **VIN or Serial Number** (text): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) or serial number of the vehicle.

### Speed Limiter Details
* **Speed Limiter Details** (text): This page is where you will enter the speed limiter details.
* **Serial Number** (text): Enter the serial number of the speed limiter.
* **Installation Date** (text): Enter the date of the speed limiter installation.
* **Installed By** (text): Enter the person or organization that installed the speed limiter.

### Installation Info
* **Installation Info** (text): This page is where you will enter any additional installation information.
* **Installation Location** (text): Enter the location where the speed limiter was installed.
* **Notes** (text): Enter any additional notes or comments about the installation.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all the information entered for accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the company's support team for assistance.
* This form is meant for registration and reporting purposes only.
