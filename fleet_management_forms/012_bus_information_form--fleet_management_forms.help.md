# Bus Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information about bus routes, enabling efficient scheduling, route planning, and service optimization.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the "Route Number" field and entering the unique identification number of the bus route.
2.  Enter the "Route Name" field with the name or description of the route.
3.  Choose the "Route Type" from the provided options (e.g., School Route, City Transit, Shuttle Service, etc.).
4.  Enter the "Start Location" field with the starting point of the route.
5.  Enter the "End Location" field with the final destination of the route.
6.  In the "Schedule Information" field, provide any relevant timing details.
7.  Enter the "Departure Time" field with the first departure time of the bus.
8.  Enter the "Arrival Time" field with the final arrival time of the bus.
9.  Choose the "Service Frequency" from the options (e.g., Every 15 Minutes, Every 30 Minutes, etc.).
10.  Select the days of operation for the route using the "Days of Operation" field.
11.  Enter the "Bus Number" field with the vehicle's identification number.
12.  Enter the "Bus Model" field with the make and model of the bus.
13.  Enter the "Bus Year" field with the year of manufacture.
14.  Enter the "Seating Capacity" field with the number of seats available.
15.  Select whether the bus is wheelchair accessible or not using the "Wheelchair Accessible" field.
16.  Enter the "Primary Driver" field with the assigned driver's name.
17.  Enter the "Driver ID" field with the driver's employee ID.
18.  Enter the "Driver Phone" field with the contact number of the driver.
19.  (Optional) Enter any additional information about the bus or driver in the "Additional Information" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Route Number** (2): The unique identification number of the bus route.
* **Route Name** (3): The name or description of the route.
* **Route Type** (4): The type of route service (e.g., School Route, City Transit, Shuttle Service, etc.).
* **Start Location** (5): The starting point of the route.
* **End Location** (6): The final destination of the route.
* **Schedule Information** (7): Provides timing details for the bus service.
* **Departure Time** (8): The first departure time of the bus.
* **Arrival Time** (9): The final arrival time of the bus.
* **Service Frequency** (10): The frequency of the bus service (e.g., Every 15 Minutes, Every 30 Minutes, etc.).
* **Days of Operation** (11): Select the days of the week the bus operates.
* **Bus Number** (13): The vehicle's identification number.
* **Bus Model** (14): The make and model of the bus.
* **Bus Year** (15): The year of manufacture of the bus.
* **Seating Capacity** (16): The number of seats available on the bus.
* **Wheelchair Accessible** (17): Whether the bus is wheelchair accessible or not.
* **Primary Driver** (19): The name of the assigned driver.
* **Driver ID** (20): The driver's employee ID.
* **Driver Phone** (21): The contact number of the driver.
* **Backup Driver** (22): The name of the backup driver (optional).
* **Additional Information** (23): Any other relevant details about the bus or driver.
