# Vehicle Deactivation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Deactivation Form is used to document the deactivation status of a vehicle in the fleet. This form helps to accurately track and update the status of vehicles that are no longer active in the fleet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's make in the "vehicle_make" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's model in the "vehicle_model" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's VIN number in the "vin_number" field.
4. Select the reason for removal (deactivation) from the "reason_for_removal" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date the vehicle was removed from the fleet in the "date_removed" field.
6. Enter the time the vehicle was removed from the fleet in the "time_removed" field.
7. Indicate whether the vehicle was assigned to someone (e.g. driver, employee) in the "assigned_to" field.
8. Select the current vehicle status (Active/Inactive) in the "vehicle_status" field.
9. Indicate whether the vehicle was submitted by you (the user) in the "submitted_by" field.
10. Select the vehicle type (e.g. truck, trailer) in the "vehicle_type" field.
11. Select the vehicle class (e.g. standard, specialty) in the "vehicle_class" field.
12. Select the vehicle subclass (e.g. small, large) in the "vehicle_subclass" field.
13. Select the vehicle category (e.g. passenger, commercial) in the "vehicle_category" field.
14. Select the vehicle body type (e.g. pickup, van) in the "vehicle_body_type" field.
15. Select the vehicle engine type (e.g. diesel, gasoline) in the "vehicle_engine_type" field.
16. Select the vehicle trailer type (e.g. trailer, non-trailer) in the "vehicle_trailer_type" field.
17. Enter the vehicle's weight in the "vehicle_weight" field.
18. Enter the vehicle's length in the "vehicle_length" field.
19. Enter the vehicle's height in the "vehicle_height" field.
20. Enter the vehicle's width in the "vehicle_width" field.
21. Enter the vehicle's height including cab in the "vehicle_height_with_cab" field.
22. Enter the vehicle's length including cab in the "vehicle_length_with_cab" field.
23. Select the "vehicle_category" field.
24. Enter the date the form was submitted in the "submitted_on" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Deactivation Form 1**: Enter the title of the form as it appears on the page.
* **vehicle_make_label**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g. Ford, Toyota).
* **vehicle_model_label**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g. F-150, Corolla).
* **vin_number_label**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **reason_for_removal_label**: Select the reason for deactivating the vehicle (e.g. sold, decommissioned, stolen).
* **comments_label**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's deactivation status (optional).
* **date_removed_label**: Enter the date the vehicle was removed from the fleet.
* **time_removed_label**: Enter the time the vehicle was removed from the fleet.
* **assigned_to_label**: Indicate whether the vehicle was assigned to someone (e.g. driver, employee).
* **vehicle_status_label**: Select the current status of the vehicle (Active/Inactive).
* **submitted_by_label**: Indicate whether you (the user) submitted the vehicle for deactivation.
* **vehicle_type_label**: Select the type of vehicle (e.g. truck, trailer).
* **vehicle_class_label**: Select the class of vehicle (e.g. standard, specialty).
* **vehicle_subclass_label**: Select the subclass of vehicle (e.g. small, large).
* **vehicle_category_label**: Select the category of vehicle (e.g. passenger, commercial).
* **vehicle_body_type_label**: Select the body type of vehicle (e.g. pickup, van).
* **vehicle_engine_type_label**: Select the engine type of vehicle (e.g. diesel, gasoline).
* **vehicle_trailer_type_label**: Select the trailer type of vehicle (e.g. trailer, non-trailer).
* **vehicle_weight_label**: Enter the weight of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_length_label**: Enter the length of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_height_label**: Enter the height of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_width_label**: Enter the width of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_height_with_cab_label**: Enter the height of the vehicle including cab.
* **vehicle_length_with_cab_label**: Enter the length of the vehicle including cab.
* **submitted_on_label**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

This guide should help you complete the Vehicle Deactivation Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
