# Logistics Vehicle Roadworthiness Inspection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inspect and evaluate the roadworthiness of vehicles used in logistics operations. It helps to identify potential safety issues and ensure that vehicles meet specific safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Vehicle Number in the first field. This is the unique identifier for the vehicle being inspected.
2. Provide Driver Details in the second field. This includes any relevant information about the driver, such as their name and contact information.
3. Select the Vehicle Type from the options provided in the third field. This helps to determine the specific safety standards that apply to the vehicle.
4. Enter the Vehicle Mileage in the fourth field. This is the total mileage on the vehicle.
5. Enter the Last Service Date in the fifth field. This is the date the vehicle was last serviced.
6. Select the Next Service Date from the calendar options in the sixth field. This is the date the vehicle is due for its next service.
7. Choose whether the vehicle has passed or partially passed the Safety Check in the seventh field.
8. Enter any additional Notes about the vehicle's condition in the eighth field.
9. Upload a photo of the vehicle's current condition in the ninth field (Vehicle Image).
10. Upload a copy of the driver's signature in the tenth field (Driver Signature).
11. Upload a copy of the manager's signature in the eleventh field (Manager Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the vehicle being inspected.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant information about the driver, such as their name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of vehicle from the options provided (Car, Bus, Lorry, Van).
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, required: false): Enter the total mileage on the vehicle.
* **Last Service Date** (`last_service_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the vehicle was last serviced.
* **Next Service Date** (`next_service_date`, date, required: false): Select the date the vehicle is due for its next service.
* **Safety Check** (`safety_check`, select_one, required: false): Select whether the vehicle has passed (True), partially passed (Partially), or failed (False) the safety check.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations about the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Image** (`vehicle_image`, note, required: false): Upload a photo of the vehicle's current condition.
* **Driver Signature** (`driver_signature`, note, required: false): Upload a copy of the driver's signature.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required: false): Upload a copy of the manager's signature.

## Tips
When completing this form, ensure that you have the correct information and documentation for the vehicle being inspected. This includes the vehicle number, driver details, and any relevant service records.
