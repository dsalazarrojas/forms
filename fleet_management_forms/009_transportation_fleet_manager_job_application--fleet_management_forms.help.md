# Transportation Fleet Manager Job Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates applying for the Fleet Manager position. It helps us to evaluate their qualifications and suitability for the job.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the personal details section accurately and completely, including first name, last name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your relevant job role from the "Job Role" field.
3. Indicate your level of experience for each field using the "Yes" or "No" options, including work experience, education, skills, references, and emergency contact.
4. If you have a resume that you have previously submitted for this job application, indicate that in the "Resume" field.
5. Fill out the emergency contact section with your contact information and relationship details.
6. Submit the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required: False): This is the first name of the candidate.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required: False): This is the last name of the candidate.
* **Email** (Email, Required: False): This is the email address of the candidate.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: False): This is the phone number of the candidate.
* **Job Role** (Select One, Required: True): This is the job role that the candidate is applying for.
* **Years of Experience** (Number, Required: False): This is the number of years the candidate has experience in the selected job role.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required: False): This is the date of birth of the candidate.
* **Time of Birth** (Time, Required: False): This is the time of birth of the candidate.
* **Additional Information** (Note, Required: False): This is an open field for any additional information the candidate wants to include.
* **Resume** (Select Multiple, Required: False): If the candidate has previously submitted a resume for this job application, indicate that here.
* **Job Application Form** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you are applying for this job.
* **Job Description** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you are familiar with the job description.
* **Work Experience** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you have work experience in the selected job role.
* **Education** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you have completed education relevant to the job role.
* **Skills** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you have the necessary skills for the job role.
* **References** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you have any relevant references.
* **Emergency Contact** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Indicate if you have an emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (Select Multiple, Required: False): If you have an emergency contact, indicate your relationship with them.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Required: False): This is the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Required: False): This is the address of the emergency contact.
