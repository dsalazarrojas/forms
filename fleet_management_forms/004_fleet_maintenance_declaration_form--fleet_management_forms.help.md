# Fleet Management Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare and record maintenance activities related to a vehicle within a fleet management system. It helps track and document the vehicle's maintenance status, condition, and other relevant details. The form should only be completed if the vehicle is not already listed as maintained in the system.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's unique ID.
2. Select the correct vehicle maintenance status from the options provided.
3. Indicate the vehicle's condition using the provided options.
4. Choose the type of maintenance performed (routine or emergency).
5. Select the person assigned to perform the maintenance.
6. Select the maintenance status of the vehicle after the maintenance activity.
7. Choose the vehicle's mileage reading unit (miles or kilometers).
8. Enter the current odometer reading.
9. Indicate the current fuel level of the vehicle.
10. Select who submitted the form and enter the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Declaration Form** (`maintenance_declaration_form`): Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity performed.
* **Vehicle ID** (`vehicle_id`): Enter the vehicle's unique ID (e.g., 1234ABCD).
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`): Select 'Yes' if the vehicle requires maintenance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Maintenance Date** (`maintenance_date`): Enter the date of the maintenance activity (MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Description** (`maintenance_description`): Enter a brief description of the maintenance activity.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the tool assigned to perform the maintenance (e.g., chatjimmy or chatjane).
* **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`): Select the current status of the maintenance activity (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`): Select the current condition of the vehicle (Excellent, Fair, or Poor).
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`): Select the type of maintenance performed (Routine or Emergency).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Select the person who submitted the form (e.g., John, Janes, or Jimy).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Date & Time** (`submitted_date_time`): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, or Honda).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Mileage Reading** (`mileage_reading`): Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Mileage Unit** (`mileage_reading_unit`): Select the unit of the mileage reading (Miles or Kilometers).
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`): Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`): Select the current fuel level of the vehicle (Full, Empty, or Three-quarters).

Note: This form should only be completed if the vehicle is not already listed as maintained in the system.
