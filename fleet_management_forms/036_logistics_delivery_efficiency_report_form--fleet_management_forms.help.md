# Logistics Delivery Efficiency Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about delivery efficiency for logistics operations. It helps to track performance metrics such as on-time delivery, delayed or cancelled deliveries, and any documents or communications that were delayed. The data collected will be used to analyze and improve the logistics delivery process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  If you are reporting on a delivery, enter your name and select the route ID associated with the delivery.
2.  Enter the start and end times of the delivery.
3.  Select the on-time performance of the delivery (On Time, Delayed, or Cancelled).
4.  If any documents or communications were delayed, select the corresponding option(s).
5.  Enter any additional notes about the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your name
*   **What is your name**: This field is optional, but it helps identify your name for reporting and tracking purposes.
*   Your name should be a text string, ideally your last name followed by your first name and any middle initial(s).

### Route ID
*   **route_id**: This is a unique identifier for the route associated with the delivery. Enter the exact route ID from your records or system.
*   It helps in tracking and analyzing delivery performance across different routes.

### Start Time
*   **start_time**: Enter the time the delivery started. This field is optional.
*   Use the standard time format for your region (e.g., 12:00 PM for 12:00).

### End Time
*   **end_time**: Enter the time the delivery ended. This field is optional.
*   Use the standard time format for your region (e.g., 12:00 PM for 12:00).

### On-time Performance
*   **on_time_performance**: Select the on-time status of the delivery (On Time, Delayed, or Cancelled).
*   This field is optional but helps in tracking on-time delivery performance.

### Document Delay
*   **document_delay**: Select if any documents or communications were delayed.
*   This field is optional but helps in identifying areas where document delivery efficiency can be improved.

### Any Additional Notes
*   **any_additional_notes**: Enter any additional information about the delivery.
*   This field is optional and provides a space to record any comments or issues related to the delivery.

### Contact Email
*   **contact_email**: Enter your contact email, which helps in case of any further inquiries or clarification.
*   This field is optional but recommended to ensure prompt response.
