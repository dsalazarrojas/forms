# Transportation Vehicle Maintenance Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a basic audit tool for transportation vehicle maintenance. It collects general information, inspection details, driver information, and notes to help with vehicle maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the general information section, providing the necessary details about the vehicle.
2. In the inspection information section, provide details about the recent inspections conducted on the vehicle.
3. In the driver information section, provide information about the driver who will be operating the vehicle.
4. In the notes section, include any additional comments or observations about the vehicle's condition.
5. Finally, review your answers and submit the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (ID: 1)
* **General Information**: This section is used to collect basic information about the vehicle, such as its make, model, and year.
* Required: No

### Inspection Information (ID: 2)
* **Inspection Information**: This section is used to record details about the recent inspections conducted on the vehicle. Provide a brief description of the inspections, including any issues or concerns found.
* Required: No

### Driver Information (ID: 3)
* **Driver Information**: This section is used to collect information about the driver who will be operating the vehicle. Provide their name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* Required: No

### Notes (ID: 4)
* **Notes**: This section is used to include any additional comments or observations about the vehicle's condition. Be as detailed as possible to help with maintenance planning.
* Required: Yes

### Submit (ID: 5)
* **Submit**: This is the final step to confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete. Review your answers carefully before submitting.
* Required: Yes

## Tips
* Be sure to complete all required fields (Notes) before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
