# Driver Route Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Route Check In Form is a key component in tracking and managing drivers' routes. This form gathers essential information about a driver's route, including start and end times, distance, route type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field (optional but recommended).
4. Select your Driver Status (Active or Inactive) in the Driver Status field.
5. Enter any additional Notes about the route in the Notes field (optional).
6. Enter Route Details in the Route Details field (optional).
7. Enter the Route Start Time in the route start time field.
8. Enter the Route End Time in the Route End Time field.
9. Enter the Route Distance (if known) in the Route Distance field.
10. Enter the number of Route Hours driven in the Route Hours field.
11. Enter a Driver Rating (if applicable) in the Driver Rating field (optional).
12. Enter the Route Start Location in the Route Start Location field.
13. Enter the Route End Location in the Route End Location field.
14. Enter the Vehicle Number in the Vehicle Number field.
15. Select the Vehicle Type from the available options.
16. Enter the Route Type in the Route Type field.
17. Enter the Driver ID in the Driver ID field.
18. Enter the Driver Location in the Driver Location field (optional).
19. Enter the Vehicle ID in the Vehicle ID field (optional).
20. Enter the Route Check In Date in the Route Check In Date field.
21. Enter the Route Check In Time in the Route Check In Time field.
22. Select the Route Check In Status (Active or Inactive) in the Route Check In Status field.
23. Enter the Vehicle Odometer Reading (if known) in the Vehicle Odometer Reading field (optional).
24. Enter any additional comments in the Route Check In Status field (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about this route.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted about this route.
* **Driver Status** (select_one, required): Select Active if you are currently driving, Inactive if you are not.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about this route.
* **Route Details** (note, optional): Enter any additional details about this route.
* **Route Start Time** (time, required): Enter the time you started your route.
* **Route End Time** (time, required): Enter the time you ended your route.
* **Route Distance** (text, optional): Enter the distance you traveled on this route.
* **Route Hours** (text, optional): Enter the number of hours you spent driving on this route.
* **Driver Rating** (text, optional): Enter a rating of your performance on this route.
* **Route Start Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you started your route.
* **Route End Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you ended your route.
* **Vehicle Number** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle number of the vehicle you drove on this route.
* **Vehicle Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of vehicle you drove on this route.
* **Route Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of route you took on this route.
* **Driver ID** (text, required): Enter your driver ID number.
* **Driver Location** (text, optional): Enter your current driver location.
* **Vehicle ID** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle ID number.
* **Route Check In Date** (date, required): Enter the date you checked in on this route.
* **Route Check In Time** (time, required): Enter the time you checked in on this route.
* **Vehicle Odometer Reading** (text, optional): Enter the odometer reading of the vehicle you drove on this route.
* **Route Check In Status** (select_one, required): Select Active if you are currently checked in, Inactive if you are not.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when entering data in this form to ensure accurate route tracking and management.
* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing record when checking in on a new route.
* If unsure about any field, please contact your fleet manager or administrator for assistance.
