# Cheer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through how to complete the Cheer Camp Registration Form. The form is divided into the following sections:

### Camper Information

*   **Camper's First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's full first name.
*   **Camper's Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, required): Enter the camper's full last name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the camper's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the camper's current age.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school the camper attends.
*   **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the camper's current grade level.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the camper's gender.
*   **Camper's Cheer Experience** (`cheer_experience`, select_one, required): Select the number of years of cheer experience.

### Medical and Health Information

*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies the camper has.
*   **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications the camper is currently taking.
*   **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the camper's primary care physician.
*   **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the camper's primary care physician.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the camper's insurance provider.
*   **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, optional): Enter the camper's insurance policy number or member ID.

### Contact and Emergency Information

*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for parent or guardian communications.
*   **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid contact phone number for parent or guardian.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter a valid contact phone number for the emergency contact.

### Payment and Session Information

*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (Credit card, Debit card, Check, Bank transfer, Scholarship).
*   **Camp Session** (`camp_session`, select_one, required): Select the desired camp session.

### Special Needs and Additional Information

*   **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Enter any special needs or accommodations the camper might require.
*   **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select how the parent or guardian heard about the camp.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

### Parent or Guardian Confirmation

*   **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, select_one, required): Confirm that all information is accurate.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.

Please fill out the form carefully, and do not hesitate to ask for assistance if you need help with any of the fields.
