# Cheerleader Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from cheerleaders about their experience with their team. It aims to gather information on their opinions and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and team details.
2. Select the season you are providing feedback for.
3. Answer the questions about your experience with the team, including communication, practice quality, practice frequency, skill development, teamwork, competition preparation, choreography quality, music selection, uniform quality, facility quality, safety measures, parental involvement, time commitment, cost value, and overall satisfaction.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields for that purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please fill in your full name. (Optional)
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team. (Required)
* **Season Year**: Select the season you are providing feedback for. (Required)
* **Years on Team**: Select how long you have been on this team. (Required)
* **Coach Communication**: Rate how well your coach communicated with you. (Required)
* **Practice Quality**: Rate the quality of practice sessions. (Required)
* **Practice Frequency**: Rate if you feel practices are frequent enough. (Required)
* **Skill Development**: Rate how much your skills improved under this team. (Required)
* **Team Cohesion**: Rate the teamwork and collaboration among team members. (Required)
* **Competition Preparation**: Rate how well you feel prepared for competitions. (Required)
* **Choreography Quality**: Rate the quality of routines and choreography. (Required)
* **Music Selection**: Rate the quality of music choices. (Required)
* **Uniform Quality**: Rate the quality of team uniforms. (Required)
* **Facility Quality**: Rate the quality of practice facilities. (Required)
* **Safety Measures**: Rate the adequacy of safety protocols. (Required)
* **Parental Involvement**: Rate the level of parental involvement. (Optional)
* **Time Commitment**: Rate if the time commitment is reasonable. (Required)
* **Cost vs Value**: Rate if the program's value is worth the cost. (Required)
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend this team to others? (Required)
* **Will Return Next Season**: Do you plan to return next season? (Required)
* **Favorite Part of the Season**: If you have a favorite part of the season, please describe it. (Optional)
* **Least Favorite Part of the Season**: If you have a least favorite part of the season, please describe it. (Optional)
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments, please share them. (Optional)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the team. (Required)

## Tips
* Answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the most appropriate answer based on your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback!
