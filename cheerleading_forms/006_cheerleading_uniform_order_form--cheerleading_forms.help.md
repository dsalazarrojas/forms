# Cheerleading Uniform Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for team managers or administrators to collect information for custom-made cheerleading uniform orders. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure timely and correct delivery of uniforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required information:
   - First name
   - Last name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - School or organization name
   - Team name
   - Uniform style
   - Primary color
   - Secondary color
   - Shipping address
   - City
   - State
   - Zip code
   - Delivery date needed
   - Special requests or additional notes (optional)
2. If you have a logo for your team, you can upload it for logo embroidery. Please use a valid file type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter the first name of the person placing the order.
* **Last name**: Enter the last name of the person placing the order.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address to receive order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number to contact you with order updates.
* **School or organization name**: Enter the name of the school or organization the uniform is for.
* **Team name**: Enter the name of the team the uniform is for.
* **Uniform style**: Choose the style of the uniform from the provided options.
* **Primary color**: Choose the primary color of the uniform from the provided options.
* **Secondary color**: Choose the secondary color of the uniform from the provided options.
* **Trim placement**: Choose where the trim will be placed on the uniform (Collar, Sleeves, Waistband, Bottom Hem, or None).
* **Do you want logo embroidery?**: Check yes or no if you want the team logo to be embroidered on the uniform.
* **Upload your logo file**: Upload a file for your team logo if you want it to be embroidered.
* **Athlete name**: Enter the name of the athlete this uniform is for (optional).
* **Number for uniform**: Enter the athlete's number for the uniform (optional).
* **Uniform size**: Choose the size of the uniform from the provided options.
* **Number of uniforms**: Enter the total number of uniforms ordered.
* **Price per uniform**: Enter the price per uniform (this will be calculated automatically based on the options chosen).
* **Shipping address**: Enter the address where the uniforms should be shipped to.
* **City**: Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **State**: Enter the state for the shipping address.
* **Zip code**: Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Delivery date needed**: Enter the date you need the uniforms delivered by.
* **Special requests or additional notes**: Enter any special requests or additional comments for the order.
