# Cheer Event Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cheer Event Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a cheer event. The form helps to gather information on the attendee's experience, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights for future event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Your Name: Enter your full name as an attendee of the event.
	* Email Address: Enter your email address for follow-up communications.
	* Attendee Type: Select your role at the event (e.g., Cheerleader, Coach, Parent or Family, Judge, Spectator, Event Staff).
	* Event Name: Enter the name of the event you attended.
	* Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
	* Overall Satisfaction: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
3. Answer the rating questions:
	* Venue Rating: Rate the venue's quality.
	* Event Organization Rating: Rate the event's organization.
	* Schedule Rating: Rate the event's schedule.
	* Staff Friendliness: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the staff.
	* Facility Cleanliness: Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
	* Competition Quality: Rate the quality of the competition performances.
	* Judging Fairness: Rate the fairness and consistency of the judging.
	* Value for Money: Rate whether the event was worth the cost.
4. Provide additional feedback:
	* Best Part of the Event: Share what you enjoyed most about the event.
	* Areas for Improvement: List any areas that could be improved.
	* Overall Comments: Share any additional comments or feedback.

* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`): Your full name as a attendee of the event.

* **Event Organization Rating** (`event_organization_rating`): Rate the event's organization.

* **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`): Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
* **Competition Quality** (`competition_quality`): Rate the quality of the competition performances.

* **Best Part of the Event** (`best_part`): Share what you enjoyed most about the event.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): List any areas that could be improved.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Share any additional comments or feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.

## Tips
* Please answer all the required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use the options provided to rate your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
