# Cheerleading Conduct Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cheerleading Conduct Review Form" is a tool used to evaluate the behavior, conduct, and performance of a cheerleader within a team or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and answers for each field before starting.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner.
3. For select_one fields, choose the option that best matches the situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Cheerleader Name**: Full name of the cheerleader being reviewed.
* **2. Team Name**: Name of the team the cheerleader is a part of.
* **3. Review Date**: Date of this conduct review.
* **4. Reviewer Name**: Name of the person conducting the review.
* **5. Reviewer Role**: Select one of the following:
	+ Head Coach
	+ Assistant Coach
	+ Program Director
	+ School Administrator
	+ Team Captain
	+ Other
* **6. Review Type**: Select one of the following:
	+ Routine evaluation
	+ Incident response
	+ Performance concern
	+ Behavioral issue
	+ Recognition of excellence
* **7. Attendance and Punctuality**: Rate attendance and punctuality:
	+ Excellent - Always on time
	+ Good - Rarely late
	+ Average - Occasionally late
	+ Poor - Frequently late or absent
	+ Very Poor - Chronic issues
* **8. Attitude and Behavior**: Rate overall attitude and behavior:
	+ Excellent - Always positive
	+ Good - Generally positive
	+ Average - Mixed
	+ Poor - Often negative
	+ Very Poor - Consistently negative
* **9. Teamwork and Cooperation**: Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent team player
	+ Good team player
	+ Average team player
	+ Poor team player
	+ Very poor team player
* **10. Respect for Coaches**: Rate level of respect shown to coaches:
	+ Excellent - Always respectful
	+ Good - Generally respectful
	+ Average - Sometimes disrespectful
	+ Poor - Often disrespectful
	+ Very Poor - Consistently disrespectful
* **11. Respect for Teammates**: Rate level of respect shown to teammates:
	+ Excellent - Always respectful
	+ Good - Generally respectful
	+ Average - Sometimes respectful
	+ Poor - Often disrespectful
	+ Very Poor - Consistently disrespectful
* **12. Adherence to Rules**: Rate adherence to team rules and policies:
	+ Excellent - Always follows rules
	+ Good - Usually follows rules
	+ Average - Sometimes breaks rules
	+ Poor - Often breaks rules
	+ Very Poor - Consistently breaks rules
* **13. Sportsmanship**: Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **14. Academic Performance**: Rate academic standing:
	+ (only visible if the cheerleader is a student)
* **15. Social Media Conduct**: Rate social media behavior:
	+ Excellent - Always appropriate
	+ Good - Generally appropriate
	+ Average - Some concerns
	+ Poor - Frequent concerns
	+ Not aware
* **16. Uniform and Appearance**: Rate uniform and appearance:
	+ Excellent - Always compliant
	+ Good - Usually compliant
	+ Average - Sometimes non-compliant
	+ Poor - Often non-compliant
	+ Very Poor - Consistently non-compliant
* **17. Effort Level**: Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent - Maximum effort always
	+ Good - Strong effort
	+ Average - Moderate effort
	+ Poor - Minimal effort
	+ Very Poor - No effort
* **18. Leadership Qualities**: Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent leader
	+ Good leadership potential
	+ Average leadership
	+ Developing leadership
	+ No leadership shown
* **19. Incidents Reported**: List any conduct incidents, if applicable.
* **20. Incident Details**: Provide details of any incidents, if applicable.
* **21. Corrective Actions Taken**: Provide any actions taken to address concerns, if applicable.
* **22. Improvement Plan**: Create a plan for conduct improvement, if applicable.
* **23. Follow-up Required**: Select one of the following:
	+ Yes, scheduled
	+ Yes, pending
	+ No follow-up needed
* **24. Follow-up Date**: Date for follow-up review (only visible if "Follow-up Required" is yes).
* **25. Overall Conduct Rating**: Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Unsatisfactory
* **26. Cheerleader Response**: Write a response from the cheerleader, if applicable.
* **27. Reviewer Confirmation**: Select one of the following:
	+ Yes, I confirm
	+ 'False'
* **28. Cheerleader Acknowledgment**: Select one of the following:
	+ Yes, I acknowledge
	+ 'False'
	+ Not available

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand all fields before submitting.
* Use the space provided for each field to provide detailed explanations or examples.
* Be honest and fair in your evaluation.
* Ensure consistency in your ratings and comments.
* If unsure or need help, consult with your supervisor or team leader.
* Keep a copy of this form for future reference and follow-up.
