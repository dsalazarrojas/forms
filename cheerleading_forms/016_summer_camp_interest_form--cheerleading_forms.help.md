# Summer Camp Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Summer Camp Interest Form" is a tool to help us understand your interest in the summer camp program. This form collects information about your preferences and needs, which will help us to better plan and organize the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the option that best describes your interest in the summer camp program from the "summer_camp_interest" field.
- Next, select all the activities you are interested in from the "summer_camp_activity" field.
- If you are registering a group, enter the anticipated number of people in the "number_of_people_anticipated" field.
- Finally, select the status of your registration from the "summer_camp_registration_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **summer_camp_interest** (`summer_camp_interest_form`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your interest in the summer camp program. This field is required.
* **summer_camp_activity** (`summer_camp_activity`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the activities you are interested in. This field is optional, but selecting activities will help us to better plan the program.
* **number_of_people_anticipated** (`number_of_people_anticipated`, number, optional): If you are registering a group, enter the anticipated number of people attending. This field is optional and only applies to group registrations.
* **summer_camp_registration_status** (`summer_camp_registration_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your registration. This field is optional but helps us to track the status of your interest.
