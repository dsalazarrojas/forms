# Cheerleading Demerit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record and track demerits or penalties given to cheerleaders in your team. It helps ensure that you keep accurate records of incidents, assign demerit points, and document actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the cheerleader's information: 
   - **Cheerleader Name** (`cheerleader_name`): Enter the full name of the cheerleader.
   - **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of the cheer team.
   - **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the birth date of the cheerleader.
   - **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): Enter the date the incident occurred.
   - **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred (if applicable).
   - **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident took place.

2. Fill in the incident details: 
   - **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): Enter a detailed description of what occurred.
   - **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): Verify if the time of the incident has been entered correctly.

3. Determine the demerit details: 
   - **Demerit Category** (`demerit_category`): Select the category of the violation (e.g., Attendance violation, Behavioral issue, etc.).
   - **Demerit Severity** (`demerit_severity`): Select the severity level of the violation (e.g., Minor, Moderate, Serious, etc.).
   - **Demerit Points Assigned** (`demerit_points`): Enter the number of demerit points to be assigned.
   - **Previous Violations** (`previous_violations`): Check if this is not the first violation.

4. Determine the action taken: 
   - **Action Taken** (`action_taken`): Select the action taken (e.g., Verbal warning, Written warning, etc.).
   - **Action Details** (`action_details`): Enter any additional details about the action taken.
   - **Parent or Guardian Notified** (`parent_notified`): Check if the parent or guardian has been notified.
   - **Parent or Guardian Response** (`parent_response`): Enter the response from the parent or guardian (if applicable).
   - **Cheerleader Response** (`cheerleader_response`): Enter the response from the cheerleader (if applicable).
   - **Improvement Plan** (`improvement_plan`): Enter a plan for correcting the behavior.

5. Finalize the form: 
   - **Total Demerit Points** (`total_demerit_points`): Verify the total accumulated demerit points.
   - **Consequences of Further Violations** (`consequences_of_further_violations`): Enter any consequences that will be enforced if further violations occur.
   - **Coach Signature** (`coach_signature`): Confirm that this demerit is accurate.
   - **Cheerleader Acknowledgment** (`cheerleader_signature`): Confirm that the cheerleader acknowledges receipt of this demerit.
   - **Parent or Guardian Acknowledgment** (`parent_signature`): Confirm that the parent or guardian acknowledges receipt of this demerit (if applicable).
