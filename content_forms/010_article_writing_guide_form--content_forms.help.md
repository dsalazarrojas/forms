# Article Writing Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help writers create articles according to specific guidelines and requirements. It ensures that writers understand the project details, including the article's purpose, target audience, and key points to cover, before starting their writing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, one by one. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information for each field to help the writer create a well-structured and effective article.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Writer Name** (`writer_name`, **text**, required): This field is for the writer's name, which will be used to identify the content creator.
* **Writer Email** (`writer_email`, **email**, required): Enter your primary contact email, which will be used for communication and feedback.
* **Assignment Date** (`assignment_date`, **date**, required): Specify the date when the article was assigned, to keep track of project timelines.
* **Deadline Date** (`deadline_date`, **date**, required): Set the due date for the article submission, to ensure timely completion.
* **Article Title** (`article_title`, **text**, required): Enter the proposed or confirmed title of the article.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, **text**, required): Identify the intended audience for the article.
* **Purpose of the Article** (`article_purpose`, **select_one**, required): Choose the purpose of the article from the options provided (Inform, Educate, Persuade/Advocate, Entertain, Instruct/How-to, Report/News, Analysis/Opinion, Promotional).
* **Main Topic or Thesis** (`main_topic`, **text**, required): Enter the core idea or argument of the article.
* **Key Points to Cover** (`key_points`, **text**, required): List the main ideas or points to include in the article, separated by commas.
* **Tone and Style** (`tone_style`, **select_one**, required): Select the desired tone and style for the article from the options provided (Formal and Academic, Professional and Business, Conversational and Friendly, Humorous and Casual, Technical and Detailed, Inspirational).
* **Word Count Target** (`length_guidelines`, **select_one**, required): Choose the desired word count for the article from the options provided (500-1000 words, 1000-1500 words, 1500-2000 words, 2000-3000 words, 3000+ words, Flexible).
* **Research Requirements** (`research_requirements`, **text**, optional): If required, specify the research or sources to be used in the article.
* **Required Sections or Headings** (`required_sections`, **text**, optional): If necessary, list the required sections or headings for the article.
* **Include Visual Elements?** (`visual_elements`, **select_one**, optional): Select whether to include images, charts, or graphics in the article (Yes, images required, Yes, suggested but not required, No visuals needed).
* **Citation Style Required** (`citation_style`, **select_one**, optional): Choose the citation style to be used in the article from the options provided (APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, None).
* **SEO Keywords or Focus Terms** (`seo_keywords`, **text**, optional): If necessary, enter the primary keywords or focus terms for the article.
* **Brand or Style Guide to Follow** (`brand_guidelines`, **text**, optional): If required, link to or describe the brand guidelines to follow.
* **Reference Materials or Examples** (`reference_materials`, **text**, optional): If helpful, link to articles to emulate or avoid.
* **Special Instructions or Notes** (`special_instructions`, **text**, optional): If necessary, enter any additional requirements or preferences.
* **Editor Contact Name** (`editor_contact`, **text**, optional): If required, specify the editor's name to contact with questions.
