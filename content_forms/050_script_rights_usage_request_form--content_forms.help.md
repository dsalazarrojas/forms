# Script Rights Usage Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request script rights for a specific script version. To use this form, please fill out the fields below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the company.
* **Script Name** (`script_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the script you are requesting rights for.
* **Script Version** (`script_version`, text, required): Enter the version of the script you are requesting rights for.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required): Enter the purpose of your script usage.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started using the script.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you ended using the script.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of license you are requesting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your script usage.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you to use the script.
