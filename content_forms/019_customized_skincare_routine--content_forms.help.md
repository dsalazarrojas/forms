# customized_skincare_routine - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Customized Skincare Routine" form is designed to help users create a personalized skincare routine based on their skin type and concerns. This form will guide users through a series of questions to gather information and provide tailored product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "User Name" field with your name as it should be displayed in your routine.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to receive updates on your routine.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field if you prefer to receive calls or messages about your routine.
4. Select your birthdate in the "Birthdate" field for accurate recommendations.
5. Choose your skin type from the "Skin Type" field (Normal, Dry, Combination, Sensitive).
6. Select your skin concerns from the "Skin Concerns" field (Acne, Hyper-pigmentation, Fine lines & wrinkles, Dullness, Pores).
7. Choose your routine goals from the "Routine Goals" field (Moisturizing, Brightening, Hydrating, Protecting).
8. Select the products you currently use from the "Products Used" field (Cleansers, Exfoliants, Serums, Moisturizers, Masks).
9. Choose the products you are looking for recommendations from the "Product Recommendations" field (Cleanser, Exfoliant, Serum, Moisturizer, Mask, Sunscreen).
10. Add any additional notes or comments about your skin or preferences in the "Notes" field.
11. Select the tool assigned to your routine from the "Assigned Tool" field (ChatJim, ChatJane, ChatBob).
12. Finally, choose where you would like the output to be saved to in the "Output File" field (019 Customized Skincare Routine—Content Forms.yaml, or Other).
