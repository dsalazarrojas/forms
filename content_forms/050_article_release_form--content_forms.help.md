# Article Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Article Release Form is designed to collect information about article releases, including publication details, author credentials, and rights agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, carefully review the provided instructions and fill out the required fields accurately. Ensure that you have reviewed the release terms carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Date** (Release Date, date, required): Enter the date of the article's release or publication in a standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Author Name** (Author Name, text, required): Provide the full name of the author.
* **Author Email** (Author Email, email, required): Enter the author's email address.
* **Author Phone** (Author Phone, text, optional): Enter the author's phone number (if applicable).
* **Author Address** (Author Address, text, optional): Provide the author's mailing address (if applicable).
* **Article Title** (Article Title, text, required): Enter a brief title of the article.
* **Article Description** (Article Description, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the article content.
* **Article Word Count** (Article Word Count, number, optional): Enter an approximate word count of the article.
* **Publication Name** (Publication Name, text, required): Enter the name of the publication or platform where the article will be released.
* **Publisher Name** (Publisher Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the publisher or editor handling the publication process.
* **Release Type** (Release Type, select_one, required): Determine the type of release (First Publication Rights, One-Time Publication, etc.).
* **Territory** (Territory, select_one, required): Specify the geographic territory where the article will be published.
* **Language Rights** (Language Rights, select_one, required): Select the languages included in the release.
* **Duration** (Duration, select_one, required): Set the time frame for the release (Perpetual, 1 Year, etc.).
* **Compensation Type** (Compensation Type, select_one, required): Determine the type of compensation (One-Time Payment, Royalty Based, etc.).
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount if applicable.
* **Royalty Percentage** (Royalty Percentage, number, optional): Enter the royalty percentage if applicable.
* **Byline Credit** (Byline Credit, select_one, required): Specify how the author should be credited.
* **Credit Format** (Credit Format, text, optional): Enter the preferred credit format.
* **Moral Rights** (Moral Rights, select_one, required): Ensure the author retains or waives moral rights.
* **Editing Rights** (Editing Rights, select_one, required): Determine if the publisher can edit the work.
* **Derivative Works** (Derivative Works, select_one, required): Allow or disallow derivative works to be created.
* **Digital Rights** (Digital Rights, select_one, required): Include or exclude digital rights.
* **Reprint Rights** (Reprint Rights, select_one, required): Specify if the article can be reprinted.
* **Syndication Rights** (Syndication Rights, select_one, required): Include or exclude syndication rights.
* **Withdrawal Terms** (Withdrawal Terms, select_one, required): Specify the conditions for withdrawal.
* **Original Work** (Original Work, select_one, required): Certify the article is an original work.
* **Not Previously Published** (Not Previously Published, select_one, required): Indicate if the article was previously published.
* **Previous Publication Details** (Previous Publication Details, text, optional): Provide further information about previous publications (if applicable).
* **No Infringement** (No Infringement, select_one, required): Certify there is no infringement on others' rights.
* **Indemnification** (Indemnification, select_one, required): Agree to indemnify against claims.
* **Additional Terms** (Additional Terms, text, optional): Provide any further terms or conditions (if applicable).
* **Author Signature** (Author Signature, text, required): Sign with full name.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Witness Name** (Witness Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness (if applicable).
* **Witness Signature** (Witness Signature, text, optional): Enter the witness signature (if applicable).

## Tips
Ensure that you have reviewed the release terms carefully before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your publisher or a relevant expert.
