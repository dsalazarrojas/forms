# AI Ebook Creation Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Ebook Creation Guide is a form designed to guide authors and creators in outlining their ebook project's scope, objectives, and content structure. It helps ensure that the ebook meets the desired tone, format, and completion date. This form is a crucial step in the ebook creation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the basic project details: Creator/Lead Author Name, Proposed Ebook Title, and Primary Topic/Niche.
2. Determine the target audience profile and select the relevant options.
3. Define the ebook's objectives and select the desired export format.
4. Outline the ebook's structure and content.
5. Specify the AI tools and visual elements you plan to use.
6. Set the estimated word count and target completion date.
7. Fill in any additional comments or requirements you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Creator / Lead Author Name** (`project_overview_header`, `creator_full_name`, text, required)
The name of the person creating or leading the ebook project. This information will be used as the author's credential.

### 2. **Proposed Ebook Title** (`ebook_title`, text, required)
A brief title for the ebook that captures the project's concept and focus.

### 3. **Primary Topic / Niche** (`primary_topic`, text, required)
The main subject area or focus of the ebook.

### 4. **--- Audience and Strategy ---** ( `target_audience_header`, note, required)
This section introduces the target audience profile and strategy.

### 5. **Target Audience Profile** (`target_audience_profile`, text, required)
Describe the audience the ebook is intended for.

### 6. **Reader Expertise Level** (`reader_expertise_level`, select_one, required)
Select the level of expertise of the ebook's target audience:

*   Absolute Beginner
*   Intermediate / Enthusiast
*   Professional / Expert

### 7. **Primary Objectives** (`ebook_objectives`, select_multiple, required)
Choose all the objectives for the ebook:

*   Lead Generation (List building)
*   Educational (Course companion)
*   Authority Building (Thought leadership)
*   Direct Sales (Revenue generation)

### 8. **--- Content and Format ---** (`content_details_header`, note, required)
This section guides the ebook's content structure and style.

### 9. **Estimated Word Count** (`estimated_word_count`, number, required)
Approximate the number of words in the final ebook version.

### 10. **Desired Tone of Voice** (`brand_tone_voice`, select_one, required)
Choose the tone you would like the ebook to convey:

*   Formal & Academic
*   Conversational & Casual
*   Inspirational & Bold
*   Technical & Precise

### 11. **Proposed Chapter Outline** (`outline_summary`, text, required)
Provide a brief summary of the ebook's structure and content.

### 12. **AI Tools to be Employed** (`ai_tools_to_use`, select_multiple, required)
Select the AI tools you plan to use for ebook generation and editing:

*   OpenAI (ChatGPT/GPT-4)
*   Anthropic (Claude)
*   Midjourney (Illustrations)
*   Jasper / Copy.ai
*   Grammarly / ProWritingAid

### 13. **Required Visual Elements** (`visual_elements`, select_multiple, required)
Choose the visual elements you plan to use in the ebook:

*   AI-Generated Illustrations
*   Stock Photography
*   Data Charts & Infographics
*   Screenshots / Tutorials

### 14. **Preferred Export Format** (`export_format_preference`, select_one, required)
Select the preferred format for exporting the ebook:

*   PDF (Standard Layout)
*   EPUB (Reflowable text)
*   MOBI/Kindle
*   Google Doc / MS Word

### 15. **Target Completion Date** (`project_deadline`, date, required)
Specify the intended completion date for the ebook project.

### 16. **Additional Requirements or Notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
Fill in any extra comments or specific requirements for the project.
