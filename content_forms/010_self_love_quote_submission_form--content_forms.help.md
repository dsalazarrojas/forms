# self_love_quote_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit inspirational or motivational quotes related to self-love and self-empowerment. The goal is to collect quotes that promote positive and uplifting content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (Quote, Name, and Email).
2. Provide your Email address to allow us to contact you if needed.
3. If you have a website, you can share it with us.
4. Indicate if the quote is from a known author or not.
5. If you have an image related to the quote, you can share it with us.
6. Specify the date and time you submitted the quote.
7. Check if the quote has been tagged as inspirational or not (if you're unsure, we'll do it for you).
8. Repeat the same for the second quote tag option (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quote**: A field for the quote itself, a short message that captures the essence of your inspiration. 
* **Name**: A field where you can provide your name, so we can credit you as the author of the quote.
* **Email**: A field for your Email address, which is used to contact you if needed.
* **Website**: A field where you can share your personal or professional website, if applicable.
* **Quote Author**: A field indicating if the quote is from a known author or not.
* **Quote Image**: A field where you can upload an image related to the quote, if applicable.
* **Date Submitted**: A field to record the date and time you submitted the quote.
* **Time Submitted**: A field to record the exact time you submitted the quote.
* **Quote Tagged**: A field to indicate if the quote has been tagged as inspirational or not. 
* **Quote Tagged 2**: A field to repeat the same as the previous field and allow for additional quote tags (if applicable). 

## Tips
* Please only submit original content, do not copy quotes from existing sources.
* Be honest about the quote authorship and ownership.
* If you have a quote image, make sure it's relevant to the quote and not misleading. 
* Use the "Quote Tagged" and "Quote Tagged 2" fields to provide additional context or tags for the quote, if needed.
