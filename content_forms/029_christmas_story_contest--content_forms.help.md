# Christmas Story Contest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Story Contest is a competition for original Christmas-themed stories. Submissions will be judged based on creativity, writing quality, and overall appeal. The contest is a chance for writers to showcase their talents and win publication in a future issue of our publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as required.
2. Make sure to enter your original work, as stated in the original work field.
3. If you have previously published this work, select the correct option in the previous publication field.
4. Fill out the rest of the form accordingly, providing accurate and honest information.
5. Finally, review the contest rules and agree to them before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1/author_name): Enter your first and last name.
* **Pen Name** (2/pen_name): If you want to remain anonymous, enter your pen name here. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **Email Address** (3/email_address): Enter your email address for contest updates and winner notification.
* **Phone Number** (4/phone_number): Enter your phone number for winner notification.
* **Date of Birth** (5/date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth to meet age requirements.
* **-- Author Information --** (6/section_author_info): This section is for any additional information about you as the author.
* **Street Address** (7/address_line1): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (8/city): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (9/state_province): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code** (10/postal_code): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (11/country): Select your country of residence.
* **Story Title** (13/story_title): Enter the title of your story.
* **Story Category** (14/story_category): Select the category of your story (Childrens, Young Adult, Adult Fiction, Non-Fiction, etc.).
* **Word Count** (15/word_count): Enter the total number of words in your story.
* **Story Theme** (16/story_theme): Select the main theme of your story (Family and Togetherness, Magic and Wonder, etc.).
* **-- Story Details --** (17/section_story_details): This section is for additional information about your story.
* **Story Synopsis** (18/story_synopsis): Enter a brief summary of your story.
* **Your Story** (19/story_text): Paste your complete story here.
* **Original Work** (20/original_work): Check if your story is your original creation.
* **Previous Publication** (21/previous_publication): Check if your story has been published before.
* **-- Originality --** (22/section_originality): This section is for any additional information about your originality.
* **Writing Experience** (23/writing_experience): Select your writing experience level (First-time writer, Hobby writer, etc.).
* **Age Category** (24/age_category): Select your age category.
* **-- Writer Information --** (25/section_writer_info): This section is for any additional information about you as the author.
* **How Did You Hear About Contest** (26/how_heard): Select how you heard about the contest (Social Media, Writing Website, etc.).
* **Agree to Contest Rules** (27/agree_rules): Check if you agree to all contest rules.
* **Publishing Rights** (28/publishing_rights): Check if you grant rights to publish your story.
* **Publicity Consent** (29/publicity_consent): Check if you allow use of your name if you win.
* **Author Bio** (30/author_bio): Enter a brief bio about yourself as the author.

Note: Please review and agree to the contest rules before submitting.
