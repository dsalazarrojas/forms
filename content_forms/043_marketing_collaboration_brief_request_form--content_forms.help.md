# Marketing Collaboration Brief Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request marketing collaboration for specific projects, allowing you to get the necessary help and resources for your marketing initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (*) in each section.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, following the hints provided.
3. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
