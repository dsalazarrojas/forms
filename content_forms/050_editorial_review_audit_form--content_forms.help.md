# Editorial Review Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editorial Review Audit Form is used to collect information on the editorial review process for a manuscript. It helps gather data on the manuscript's status, editorial comments, and reviewer details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the manuscript status from the options provided (In Review, Ready to Publish, or Rejected).
2. Provide detailed feedback on the manuscript in the Editorial Comments field.
3. Select any editorial recommendations for the manuscript from the options provided (Insignificant Changes, Major Revisions Required, or Reject Manuscript).
4. Enter the review date and time in the Review Date and Review Time fields.
5. Enter the reviewer's name and their department in the Reviewer Name and Reviewer Department fields.
6. Add any additional comments for the editorial board in the Editorial Board Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Manuscript** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A text area where you can enter the manuscript's details.
* **Manuscript Status** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of the manuscript from the options (In Review, Ready to Publish, or Rejected).
* **Editorial Comments** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Provide detailed feedback on the manuscript.
* **Editorial Recommendations** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any editorial recommendations for the manuscript from the options (Insignificant Changes, Major Revisions Required, or Reject Manuscript).
* **Review Date** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time** (`6`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the review.
* **Reviewer Name** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Role** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's role.
* **Reviewer Department** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter the reviewer's department.
* **Review Comments** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments for the review.
* **Editorial Board Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments for the editorial board.
* **Date Received** (`12`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the manuscript was received.
