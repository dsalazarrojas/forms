# Character Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help writers and creators develop a well-rounded character profile for their creative work. By providing the necessary information, you can gain a deeper understanding of your character's personality, motivations, and development throughout the story.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start with the basic information about your character, such as their name, age, and background.
2. Describe your character's physical appearance, including any key attributes that shape their identity.
3. Identify your character's primary goal, which drives their actions and decisions throughout the story.
4. Outline their main conflict, either internal or external, that influences their journey.
5. List up to five personality traits that shape their behavior and interactions with others.
6. Balance their strengths and weaknesses to create a realistic and relatable character.
7. Consider their character arc, thinking about how they change and grow throughout the story.
8. Finally, include any additional notes or reminders for the writer to keep in mind while developing the character.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **character_name** (`character_name`, text, required): Enter a working name for your character.
* **age_and_background** (`age_and_background`, text, optional): Describe your character's age and brief background summary.
* **physical_description** (`physical_description`, text, optional): Describe your character's key physical attributes.
* **primary_goal** (`primary_goal`, text, required): Identify your character's primary goal that drives their actions and decisions.
* **main_conflict** (`main_conflict`, text, required): Describe the internal or external conflict that influences your character's journey.
* **personality_traits** (`personality_traits`, text, optional): List up to five personality traits that shape your character's behavior and interactions.
* **strengths_and_weaknesses** (`strengths_and_weaknesses`, text, optional): Balance your character's strengths and weaknesses to create a realistic and relatable character.
* **character_arc** (`character_arc`, text, optional): Think about your character's development and growth throughout the story.
* **notes_for_writer** (`notes_for_writer`, text, optional): Add any additional prompts or reminders for the writer to consider while developing the character.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when completing each field to create a rich and believable character profile.
* Don't hesitate to add or modify fields as needed to suit your creative vision.
* Remember, this form is a tool to help you develop your character, not a rigid structure to constrain your creativity.
