# Birth Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Birth Plan Form to document your preferences and wishes for your childbirth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide detailed information for each field, including your name, due date, preferred healthcare provider, and birth location.
3. Answer the questions regarding your labor and delivery preferences, such as your preferred position during labor, pain relief, and environmental preferences.
4. Consider the newborn care options and select the ones that best fit your needs.
5. Answer the postpartum care options, including your interest in early discharge and postpartum support needs.
6. Document any special requests or considerations you may have.
7. Finally, confirm that you understand that this birth plan is a preference guide and not a contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mother's Name (id: 4)**: Enter your full name.
* **Due Date (id: 5)**: Enter the date you expect your baby to be born.
* **Birth Partner's Name (id: 6)**: Enter the name of your support person.
* **Primary Obstetrician or Midwife (id: 7)**: Enter the name of your main healthcare provider.
* **Hospital or Birth Center Name (id: 8)**: Enter the name of the hospital or birth center where you plan to give birth.
* **Activities and Movement During Labor (id: 10)**: Describe the activities you want to do during labor, such as walking or swimming.
* **Preference for Continuous Monitoring (id: 11)**: Select whether you prefer to be mobile, have intermittent monitoring, or continuous monitoring.
* **Pain Relief Preferences (id: 12)**: Describe your comfort level with pain relief options, such as epidural or natural methods.
* **Labor Support People (id: 13)**: List the people you want to have present during labor.
* **Preferred Positions for Labor and Delivery (id: 14)**: Describe the positions you prefer during labor and delivery, such as upright or squatting.
* **Environmental Preferences (id: 15)**: Describe your preferences for the environment, such as lighting or music.
* **Preferences for Tearing Prevention (id: 17)**: Describe your preferences for tearing prevention, such as perineal support or massage.
* **Episiotomy Preference (id: 18)**: Select whether you prefer to avoid, only have it if medically necessary, or have no preference.
* **Cord Management and Clamping (id: 19)**: Describe how you want the cord to be handled.
* **Placenta Delivery Preferences (id: 20)**: Describe your preferences for placenta delivery, such as natural delivery or active management.
* **Immediate Skin-to-Skin Contact (id: 22)**: Select how important it is to have immediate skin-to-skin contact with your newborn.
* **Delayed Cord Clamping (id: 23)**: Describe how long you want to wait before clamping.
* **Newborn Screening and Testing (id: 24)**: Describe the tests and procedures you want to have for your newborn.
* **Newborn Vitamins and Medicines (id: 25)**: Describe the vitamins and medicines you want to have for your newborn.
* **Feeding Plan (id: 26)**: Select your preferred feeding plan, such as breastfeeding or bottle-feeding.
* **Circumcision Preference (id: 27)**: Select whether you prefer to circumcise your male infant.
* **Rooming-In Preference (id: 28)**: Select whether you prefer to have your baby room-in with you or in a nursery.
* **Visitor Policy Preferences (id: 29)**: Describe when you want family and friends to visit.
* **If Induction Becomes Necessary (id: 31)**: Describe your preferences and concerns if induction becomes necessary.
* **If Cesarean Becomes Necessary (id: 32)**: Describe your preferences and requests if cesarean becomes necessary.
* **Medical Interventions (id: 33)**: Describe your preferences and concerns for medical interventions.
* **Interest in Early Discharge (id: 35)**: Select whether you are interested in early discharge after birth.
* **Postpartum Support Needs (id: 36)**: Describe your postpartum support needs, such as breastfeeding help or newborn care.
* **Postpartum Recovery Priorities (id: 37)**: Describe your priorities for postpartum recovery.
* **Special Requests or Considerations (id: 39)**: Describe any special requests or concerns you have.
* **Communication Preferences (id: 40)**: Describe how you want to be kept informed during the pregnancy and postpartum care.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
* Keep in mind that your healthcare provider may need to deviate from your preferences if necessary.
* This form is not a contract, but a guide to help your healthcare provider understand your needs and wishes.
* Review this form with your healthcare provider before your due date.
* Update this form as needed during your pregnancy and childbirth experience.
