# Marketing And Public Relations Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing And Public Relations Request Form is a tool for external contacts to submit their marketing and public relations requests. It gathers essential information such as first name, last name, job title, company, company website, and message to facilitate the creation of a formal record in our Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "true" in the form.
2. Enter your correct first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your correct last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your official email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter your company's name in the "Company" field.
7. Enter your company's official website in the "Company Website" field.
8. Enter the name of the contact person in the "Contact Person" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if applicable).
10. Type your message in the "Message" field and clearly state your marketing and public relations request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<id: 1, name: first_name>)**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents or communication with your organization.
* **Last Name (<id: 2, name: last_name>)**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents or communication with your organization.
* **Email (<id: 3, name: email>)**: Enter your official email address that is actively monitored and checked by you.
* **Job Title (<id: 4, name: job_title>)**: Enter your job title as it is displayed on official documents or your company's website.
* **Company (<id: 5, name: company>)**: Enter your company's name as it is displayed on official documents or your company's website.
* **Company Website (<id: 6, name: company_website>)**: Enter your company's official website URL.
* **Contact Person (<id: 7, name: contact_person>)**: Enter the name of the contact person within your organization, if applicable.
* **Phone (<id: 8, name: phone>)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Message (<id: 9, name: message>)**: Clear and concise message about your marketing and public relations request, including any specific details or requirements.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you enter in this form before submitting it to avoid errors.
* Make sure to enter the correct and up-to-date information about your company and contact details.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us directly for assistance.
