## Book Cover Design Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Book Cover Design Approval Form is used to gather feedback and approval from reviewers on book cover designs. It is an essential step in the book design and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before starting, make sure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. **Fill out the required fields**: Ensure you complete all required fields (marked as "required") with accurate information.
3. **Upload relevant files**: Attach design files where required (e.g., cover design, spine, and back cover).
4. **Provide clear feedback**: Be specific and clear in your feedback, especially in the "Revision Notes or Feedback" and "Specific Changes Required" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the full title of the book.
* **Author** (`book_author`, `text`, `required`): Enter the author's name.
* **Submitter Full Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name as the person submitting for approval.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Your Role** (`submitter_role`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your role in the project (Author, Publisher, Designer, Editor, Marketing Team, Other).
* **Design Version Number** (`design_version`, `text`, `required`): Enter the version number of the design.
* **Design Concept Description** (`design_concept`, `text`, `required`): Describe the design concept and inspiration behind the cover.
* **Cover Design File** (`cover_file_upload`, `text`, `required`): Upload the cover design file.
* **Spine Design File** (`spine_file_upload`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, upload the spine design file.
* **Back Cover Design File** (`back_cover_file_upload`, `text`, `optional`): If applicable, upload the back cover design file.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the color palette used in the design.
* **Typography Details** (`typography_details`, `text`, `optional`): Describe the font and typography choices.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, `required`): Select who the cover design is intended to attract (Young Adults, Adults 18-35, Adults 35-50, Adults 50 Plus, Children, Academic Readers, General Fiction Readers).
* **Does the Cover Match the Genre** (`genre_alignment`, `select_one`, `required`): Select if the cover design aligns with the genre expectations (Yes Perfectly, Mostly Yes, Somewhat, Not Really, False).
* **Approval Decision** (`approval_decision`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your decision on the design (Approved as Is, Approved with Minor Revisions, Needs Major Revisions, Rejected New Design Needed).
* **Revision Notes or Feedback** (`revision_notes`, `text`, `optional`): Provide specific feedback or revisions needed.
* **Specific Changes Required** (`specific_changes`, `text`, `optional`): List any specific changes required.
* **Deadline for Revisions** (`deadline_for_revisions`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the deadline for revisions, if applicable.
* **Budget Approved** (`budget_approval`, `select_one`, `optional`): Indicate if the design is within budget (True, False, Not Applicable).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any other feedback or notes.
* **Reviewer Full Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, `required`): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the review.

## Tips

* Be specific and clear in your feedback and answers to questions.
* Ensure you complete all required fields to provide accurate and comprehensive feedback.
* If you're unsure about a field, contact the submitter or reviewer for clarification.
* Proofread your answers carefully to prevent errors.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Book Cover Design Approval Form effectively and provide valuable feedback and approval on book cover designs.
