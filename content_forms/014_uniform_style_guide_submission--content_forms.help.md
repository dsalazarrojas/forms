# Uniform Style Guide Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Style Guide Submission form is designed to collect information from users about uniform styles they would like to submit for approval. The primary purpose of this form is to gather feedback and ideas for uniform styles that meet the institution's standards and branding guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Uniform Style Guide Submission form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any) with the requested information.
3. Review and submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school or educational institution.
* **Purpose**: Describe the purpose of the uniform style submission. This could include a brief explanation of the design concept, inspiration, or any other relevant information.
* **Style**: Enter the uniform style you would like to submit. This could include details about the color scheme, fabric, or design elements you would like to feature in the uniform.
* **Colors**: Specify the colors you would like to use in the uniform design.
* **Branding Guidelines**: If there are any specific branding guidelines you would like to follow, please enter them here.
* **Sizing Guidelines**: Enter any additional information about the size or fit of the uniform you would like to propose.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your submission.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any supporting images or documents, consider attaching them to the form.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact us at form@gic.mx.
