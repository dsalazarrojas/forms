# Product Video Localization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for requesting product video localization. It will help us understand your video needs and requirements for your target market.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your video details in the first field.
2. Choose the target language for your video localization from the dropdown list.
3. If you have any specific requirements for the video, enter them in the "Requirements" field.
4. Provide the deadline for your video localization project.
5. Upload your file (if required) and enter the file name and description.
6. Click submit to send us your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Details**: This is where you enter your video details. Please provide a brief description of your video.
* **Target Language**: Choose the language you want your video to be localized in from the list: English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, or Korean.
* **Requirements**: If you have specific requirements or instructions for the video, enter them in this field. This is optional.
* **Deadlines**: Enter the deadline for your video localization project. This field is optional.
* **File Upload**: If you need to upload a file, enter the file name and description in this field.
* **File**: This is where you upload your file. Please note that this field is optional.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* You can choose only one target language for your video localization.
* If you need to upload a file, please use the correct file type and size as required by the system.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
