# Documentary Narration Revision Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a revision for a documentary narration. It is intended for revisions that require specific updates or changes to the existing content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your revision details in the "Revision Details" field.
2. Choose the urgency level for your revision request (if applicable).
3. Select the timecodes relevant to your revision request (if applicable).
4. Add any reference files related to your revision request (if applicable).
5. Attach any additional files or notes related to your revision request (if applicable).
6. Enter the date and time of your request.
7. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
8. Choose the option that best describes your revision request.
9. Select any other options that apply to your revision request.
10. Enter any additional text comments you have about your revision request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Revision Details (Revision Details)**: This field is for providing a brief description of the changes you want to make to the documentary narration.
* **Urgency Level (Urgency Level)**: This field allows you to select the level of urgency for your revision request. Choose "Low" if your request is not time-sensitive, "Medium" if it's moderately urgent, or "High" if it's very urgent.
* **Timecodes (Timecodes)**: This field is for selecting specific timecodes relevant to your revision request. Choose one or more of the available timecodes to help the revision team focus on the right part of the documentary.
* **Reference Files (Reference Files)**: This field is for adding any reference files that support your revision request. You can choose one or more of the available files.
* **File Upload (File Upload)**: If you have any additional files related to your revision request, you can attach them here.
* **Notes (Notes)**: This field is for providing any additional comments or information about your revision request.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date you are making this revision request.
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time you are making this revision request.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your revision request.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your revision request (if applicable).
* **Select One (Select One)**: This field is for choosing the option that best describes your revision request.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple)**: This field is for selecting any additional options that apply to your revision request.
* **Decimal (Decimal)**: This field is for entering any decimal values related to your revision request.
* **Text (Text)**: This field is for providing any additional text comments about your revision request.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each field to help the revision team understand your request.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact us for clarification.
* Please ensure that your revision request is not already in progress or has already been submitted.
