# Press Release Writing Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Press Release Writing Checklist form is designed to collect all the necessary information for creating a press release. This form guides users through a series of fields that help in organizing and presenting the details of the press release effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the company name and contact person's name to establish the identity of the press release.
2.  Enter the date the press release will be released to the public.
3.  Write a concise headline that captures the essence of the press release.
4.  Summarize the press release in one paragraph in the summary section.
5.  Include essential details of the press release in the main body section.
6.  Choose relevant subheadings from the options provided to organize the content.
7.  Provide a description of any images included with the press release.
8.  Credit the source of any images used.
9.  Add an image caption to provide further context.
10.  Link to the company's website or social media channel to provide easy access to more information.
11.  Add any attachments relevant to the press release.
12.  Select the language in which the press release will be released.
13.  Include relevant category/Tags for better searchability.
14.  Enter a unique identifier for the press release.
15.  Enter the publication date for the press release.
16.  Enter the publication time for the press release.
17.  Specify the location of the press release's publication.
18.  Enter relevant keywords for search engine optimization.
19.  Write a description of the press release.
20.  Enter the author's name and title.
21.  Provide the contact email address.
22.  Enter the contact phone number.
23.  Enter the company name as it appears on the press release.
24.  Enter the date of submission of the press release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (Company Name, Text, Optional): Enter the full name of the company issuing the press release.
    *   Example: "ABC Corporation"
*   **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Text, Optional): Enter the full name of the contact person.
    *   Example: "John Doe, Marketing Manager"
*   **Date Released** (Date Released, Date, Optional): Enter the date the press release will be released.
    *   Example: "2024-03-01"
*   **Headline** (Headline, Text, Optional): Write a concise headline that captures the essence of the press release.
    *   Example: "Company ABC Announces New Product Launch"
*   **Summary** (Summary, Text, Optional): Summarize the press release in one paragraph.
    *   Example: "Company ABC is proud to announce the launch of its new product, XYZ. This product is the result of years of research and development and is designed to revolutionize the industry."
*   **Main Body** (Main Body, Text, Optional): Include essential details of the press release.
    *   Example: "The new product is a game-changer in the industry, offering features that were previously unheard of."
*   **Subheadings** (Subheadings, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose relevant subheadings to organize the content.
    *   Example: "News, Feature, Press Release"
*   **Image Description** (Image Description, Text, Optional): Provide a description of any images included with the press release.
    *   Example: "Image of the new product"
*   **Image Credit** (Image Credit, Text, Optional): Credit the source of any images used.
    *   Example: "Image credit: Company ABC"
*   **Image Caption** (Image Caption, Text, Optional): Add an image caption to provide further context.
    *   Example: "Image of the new product in action"
*   **Links** (Links, Select One, Optional): Link to the company website or social media channel.
    *   Example: "Company Website"
*   **Attachments** (Attachments, Select Multiple, Optional): Add any attachments relevant to the press release.
    *   Example: "Product manual, Press Release PDF"
*   **Language** (Language, Select One, Optional): Select the language in which the press release will be released.
    *   Example: "English"
*   **Category/Tags** (Category/Tags, Text, Optional): Include relevant category/Tags for better searchability.
    *   Example: "New Product, Industry-Leading"
*   **Press Release ID** (Press Release ID, Text, Optional): Enter a unique identifier for the press release.
    *   Example: "PR001"
*   **Publication Date** (Publication Date, Date, Optional): Enter the publication date for the press release.
    *   Example: "2024-03-01"
*   **Publication Time** (Publication Time, Time, Optional): Enter the publication time for the press release.
    *   Example: "10:00 AM"
*   **Publication Location** (Publication Location, Text, Optional): Specify the location of the press release's publication.
    *   Example: "New York, USA"
*   **Keywords** (Keywords, Text, Optional): Enter relevant keywords for search engine optimization.
    *   Example: "New product, industry-leading, innovative"
*   **Description** (Description, Text, Optional): Write a description of the press release.
    *   Example: "Company ABC is proud to announce the launch of its new product, XYZ. This product is the result of years of research and development and is designed to revolutionize the industry."
*   **Author** (Author, Text, Optional): Enter the author's name and title.
    *   Example: "John Doe, Marketing Manager"
*   **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Provide the contact email address.
    *   Example: "john.doe@companyabc.com"
*   **Phone** (Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the contact phone number.
    *   Example: "123-456-7890"
*   **Company Name** (Company Name, Text, Optional): Enter the company name as it appears on the press release.
    *   Example: "Company ABC"
*   **Submission Date** (Submission Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of submission of the press release.
    *   Example: "2024-03-01"

**Tips:**

1.  Be concise in your answers to ensure easy reading and understanding of the press release.
2.  Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3.  Use proper formatting and grammar throughout the press release.
4.  Include relevant images and attachments to support the press release.
5.  Proofread the press release carefully before submission.
6.  Ensure that the press release is consistent with the company's branding and tone.
