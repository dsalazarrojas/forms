# Script Adaptation Permission Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Script Adaptation Permission Request form is a tool used to obtain permission to adapt or reuse content from the GIC platform. This form is designed to ensure that users comply with copyright and usage guidelines while providing a clear and transparent process for obtaining permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email" field.
3. Describe the **project title** you want to adapt or reuse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Provide your first name to be contacted regarding your permission request.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding your permission request and provide updates.
* **Project Title**: Describe the project you want to adapt or reuse, including its title. This will help us understand the scope of your request and ensure that it aligns with our copyright and usage guidelines.
