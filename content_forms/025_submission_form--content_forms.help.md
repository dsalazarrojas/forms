# Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for submitting new content to be reviewed and approved. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your submission is handled correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions carefully before starting to fill out the form.
- Fill out each field with the required information:
  - The "Upload File" field is for uploading a file related to your submission.
  - The "Name" field is for providing your name.
  - The "Email" field is for providing your email address.
  - The "Phone" field is for providing your phone number.
  - The "Date" field is for selecting a date.
  - The "Time" field is for selecting a time.
  - The "Note" field is for providing any additional comments or notes.
  - You can upload another file in the "Upload Another File" field.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Upload File** (file_upload, text, required: false): Upload a file related to your submission.
  - This field is optional, but recommended for submitting supporting documents.
  - Please make sure to upload a relevant file that complements your submission.
- **Name** (name_field, text, required: false): Enter your name.
  - This field is optional, but required if you want to be credited as the submitter.
- **Email** (email_field, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
  - This field is optional, but recommended for any communication purposes.
- **Phone** (phone_field, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
  - This field is optional, but recommended for communication purposes.
- **Date** (date_picker, date, required: false): Select a date related to your submission.
  - Please use the provided date picker to select a valid date.
- **Time** (time_picker, time, required: false): Select a time related to your submission.
  - Please use the provided time picker to select a valid time.
- **Note** (note_field, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
  - This field is optional, but recommended for providing more context.
- **Upload Another File** (file_2, text, required: false): Upload another file related to your submission.
  - This field is optional, but recommended for submitting supporting documents.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any) before submitting the form.
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
- You can upload multiple files if necessary, but please ensure they are relevant to your submission.
