# Article Reposting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from authors for the purpose of reposting their articles on various platforms. It aims to ensure that authors understand their rights and responsibilities while sharing their work with others.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Please fill in your personal details**:
    *   **Author Name**: Enter your full legal name.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email.
    *   **Contact Phone Number**: Optionally, provide a contact method for backup purposes.
2.  **Article Information**:
    *   **Article Title**: Enter the official title of your article.
    *   **Original Article URL**: Provide the link to the original published article.
    *   **Original Publication Date**: Enter the date when your article was first published.
    *   **Approximate Article Length**: Choose the word count or length category of your article.
3.  **Reposting Details**:
    *   **Where Will The Article Be Reposted?**: Specify the platform or publication where you plan to republish.
    *   **Planned Reposting Date**: Enter the date you plan to republish the article.
    *   **Will The Article Be Modified?**: Choose the level of modification you intend to make.
    *   **Describe Proposed Modifications**: If modifications are planned, describe them in detail.
4.  **Consent and Terms**:
    *   **Will You Provide Author Credit?**: Decide whether you want to be credited as the original author.
    *   **Will You Link Back To The Original Article?**: Determine if you want to include a link to the original article.
    *   **Are You Requesting Exclusive Rights?**: Choose the exclusive rights duration you need.
5.  **Permissions and Restrictions**:
    *   **Permitted Platforms**: Select all platforms where you authorize republishing.
    *   **May The Republisher Monitize This Content?**: Decide on monetization options, including full monetization or no commercial use.
6.  **Final Confirmation**:
    *   **I Confirm I Own The Rights To This Article**: Verify that you own the rights to the article.
    *   **I Consent To The Reposting Of This Article**: Confirm your agreement to the reposting conditions.

Note: This form requires you to confirm that you have the rights to republish the article. Please fill out the form honestly and accurately.
