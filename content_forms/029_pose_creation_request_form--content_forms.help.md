The Pose Creation Request Form is a form used for users to request the creation of a pose, which is a specific pose or position in a 3D model. This form is used to gather information from the user about the requested pose, including details about the pose brief, reference uploads, deadlines, and contact information. The form is designed to be filled out by a user to submit a request to create a new pose.

To submit a request to create a new pose, follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `pose_creation_request_form` field with a brief description of the requested pose. This will be used as a starting point for creating the pose.
2. In the `pose_briefs` field, provide a detailed description of the desired pose, including any relevant details or references.
3. In the `pose_reference_uploads` field, upload any relevant references or images to help the creator of the pose understand the desired outcome.
4. Set a deadline for the creation of the pose in the `deadlines` field.
5. Provide your contact information, including your name and email, in the `contact_details` and `contact_email` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pose Creation Request Form** (`pose_creation_request_form`, `text`, required): A brief description of the desired pose.
* **Pose Briefs** (`pose_briefs`, `text`, required): A detailed description of the desired pose, including any relevant details or references.
* **Pose Reference Uploads** (`pose_reference_uploads`, `select_multiple`, required): Upload any relevant references or images to help the creator of the pose understand the desired outcome.
* **Deadlines** (`deadlines`, `date`, required): The deadline for the creation of the pose.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required): Your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the `pose_briefs` field to ensure the creator of the pose understands the desired outcome.
* Use the `pose_reference_uploads` field to upload relevant references or images to help the creator of the pose.
* Set a realistic deadline in the `deadlines` field to ensure the pose can be created on time.
* Double-check your contact information in the `contact_details` and `contact_email` fields to ensure the creator of the pose can contact you with any questions or updates.
