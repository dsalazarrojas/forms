# Content Library Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Library Access Form is a crucial part of our content library management process. It helps us to understand your needs and provide you with the right access level to our content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Requester Email**: Enter your institutional or work email address that we can use to verify your identity.
* **Organization Name**: Enter your school or organization's name as it is recognized in official records.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you are with (e.g., Educational Institution, Corporate, Nonprofit, etc.).
* **Department**: Enter your department or division within your organization, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter your official job title or position within your organization.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting access to the content library.
* **Access Type**: Select the level of access you need (e.g., View Only, Download Access, Edit Access, etc.).
* **Access Duration**: Choose how long you need access to the content library (e.g., One Day, One Week, etc.).
* **Library Sections Needed**: Select the specific sections of the content library you need access to.
* **Purpose of Access**: Select the reason for your access request (e.g., Research, Education, etc.).
* **Purpose Details**: Please provide a brief explanation of your purpose, if necessary.
* **Specific Resources Needed**: If there are specific resources you need, please list them here.
* **Supervisor Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your supervisor or manager.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of your supervisor or manager, if applicable.
* **Terms of Use Accepted**: Agree to the terms of use of our content library by selecting "Yes, I Accept."
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: Agree to maintain confidentiality of our content by selecting "Yes, I Agree."
* **Intended Use**: Explain how you plan to use the content library resources.
* **Sharing Permissions**: Decide who you will share the content with (e.g., personal use only, with team, clients, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Our Library?**: Select how you learned about our content library.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful for our team to understand your needs.

Please note that all fields are required to ensure we can provide you with the correct access level to our content library. If you have any questions or need help with any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.
