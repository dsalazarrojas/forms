# ประกipie_sharing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ประกipie_sharing_form" is a form designed to collect and share recipes with others. It's intended to gather essential information about the recipe, including ingredients, cooking methods, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct category for your recipe from the "Category" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your recipe's name in the "Recipe Name" field.
4. Add the ingredients and their quantities in the "Ingredients" field.
5. Describe the ingredients in more detail in the "Ingredients Description" field.
6. Provide the instructions for cooking the recipe in the "Instructions" field.
7. Choose the correct cooking method for your recipe from the "Cooking Method" field.
8. Enter the serving size and unit (e.g., cups, tablespoons, etc.) in the "Serving Size" field.
9. Upload a relevant image for your recipe (if applicable).
10. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sharing Form (Page 1)
* **Title** (`recipe_sharing`, text, required): Enter the title of your recipe.
* **Ingredients** (text, optional): List the ingredients required for the recipe.
* **Instructions** (text, optional): Provide step-by-step instructions for cooking the recipe.
* **Recipe Name** (text, optional): Enter a name for your recipe.
* **Serving Size** (text, optional): Enter the serving size and unit for your recipe.

### Page 2
* **Category** (select_one, optional): Select the category for your recipe (e.g., Baking, Grilling, etc.).
* **Ingredients List** (text, optional): List the ingredients used in your recipe in more detail.
* **Ingredients Description** (text, optional): Describe the ingredients used in your recipe.
* **Cooking Method** (text, optional): Choose the cooking method for your recipe.
* **Serving Size Unit** (select_one, optional): Select the unit for your serving size (e.g., cups, tablespoons, etc.).

### Recipe Image (Page 3)
* **Recipe Image** (text, optional): Upload a relevant image for your recipe.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields (title, category, and serving size).
- Use commas to separate multiple ingredients in the "Ingredients" field.
- Be as descriptive as possible in the "Ingredients Description" field.
- Proofread your information before submitting to avoid errors.
- Use the correct unit for your serving size.
