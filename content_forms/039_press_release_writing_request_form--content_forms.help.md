# Press Release Writing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Press Release Writing Request Form is designed to collect information for a press release writing service. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences for a press release that meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your company details, including your company name and any other relevant information.
2.  Provide your contact person's name and email address.
3.  Choose the type of press release you need (News Release, Booth Release, or Other).
4.  Enter the release date and due date for the finished press release.
5.  Add any supporting materials that will help us understand your request.
6.  Upload any images that you want to include in the press release.
7.  Finally, upload any attachments that support your press release request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Company Details**: Enter the name and description of your company.
*  **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person we should contact about the press release.
*  **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*  **Press Release Type**: Choose the type of press release you need (News Release, Booth Release, or Other).
*  **Release Date**: Enter the date you would like the press release to be released on.
*  **Due Date**: Enter the date by which you would like the press release to be completed.
*  **Supporting Materials**: Enter any additional information or supporting materials that will help us understand your request.
*  **Press Release Body**: Enter the content of the press release in the text box.
*  **Press Release Image**: Enter any relevant image URL or upload an image that you want to include in the press release.
*  **Press Release Attachment**: Upload any supporting attachments that are relevant to the press release request.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that we can create a press release that meets your expectations.
*   Avoid submitting multiple requests for the same press release topic to avoid duplication.
*   If you have any specific preferences or requirements for the press release, please let us know in the field for supporting materials.
