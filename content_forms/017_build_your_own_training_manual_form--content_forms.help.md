# Build Your Own Training Manual Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a comprehensive training manual tailored to your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To create a well-structured and effective manual, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information and preferences.
2. Select the relevant options for "Target Audience", "Training Category", and "Topics to Include" based on the intended user group and content of your manual.
3. Provide a brief description for each section title and content.
4. Choose the desired format and estimated page count for your manual.
5. Decide on any additional features, such as including assessments, checklists, and references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Manual Creator Name (id: 1)
* **Manual Creator Name** (`creator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the creator of this manual.

### Department (id: 2)
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Select your department or team name.

### Email Address (id: 3)
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.

### Manual Title (id: 4)
* **Manual Title** (`manual_title`, `text`, required): Name of your training manual.

### Manual Version (id: 5)
* **Manual Version** (`manual_version`, `text`, required): Version number of your manual.

### Creation Date (id: 6)
* **Creation Date** (`creation_date`, `date`, required): Date of creation.

### Target Audience (id: 7)
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `select_one`, required): Select who this manual is for (e.g. New Employees, Existing Staff, Management, etc.).

### Training Category (id: 8)
* **Training Category** (`training_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary category of your manual (e.g. Onboarding, Technical Skills, Soft Skills, etc.).

### Topics to Include (id: 9)
* **Topics to Include** (`topics_included`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all topics that apply to your manual.

### Section 1 Title (id: 10)
* **Section 1 Title** (`section_1_title`, `text`, required): First section name.

### Section 1 Content Summary (id: 11)
* **Section 1 Content Summary** (`section_1_content`, `text`, required): Brief description of content.

### Section 2 Title (id: 12)
* **Section 2 Title** (`section_2_title`, `text`, optional): Second section name. (This field is optional)

### Section 2 Content Summary (id: 13)
* **Section 2 Content Summary** (`section_2_content`, `text`, optional): Brief description of content. (This field is optional)

### Section 3 Title (id: 14)
* **Section 3 Title** (`section_3_title`, `text`, optional): Third section name. (This field is optional)

### Section 3 Content Summary (id: 15)
* **Section 3 Content Summary** (`section_3_content`, `text`, optional): Brief description of content. (This field is optional)

### Include Assessments (id: 16)
* **Include Assessments** (`include_assessments`, `select_one`, required): Should the manual include quizzes? (Yes - After Each Section, Yes - At End Only, or No).

### Include Checklists (id: 17)
* **Include Checklists** (`include_checklists`, `select_one`, required): Should checklists be included in the manual? (Yes or No).

### Include References (id: 18)
* **Include References** (`include_references`, `select_one`, required): Should an additional resources section be included? (Yes or No).

### Format Preference (id: 19)
* **Format Preference** (`format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Preferred format of the manual (PDF Document, Interactive Web, Printable Workbook, Slide Deck, or All Formats).

### Estimated Page Count (id: 20)
* **Estimated Page Count** (`estimated_pages`, `select_one`, optional): Approximate length of the manual. (This field is optional)

### Additional Notes (id: 21)
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Any other requirements or special notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your manual carefully before submitting to ensure it meets your needs.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Consider consulting with experts or colleagues for guidance and feedback.
* Use this form as a starting point and customize as needed to create a comprehensive and effective manual.
* Save your manual as a draft and update it regularly to reflect changes and updates.
