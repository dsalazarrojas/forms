# submit_your_fca_toronto_profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
The submit_your_fca_toronto_profile form is used to collect detailed information for an FCA Toronto profile. This form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of the user's background and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required fields, including contact information (email, phone number, and website).
2. Select up to 6 images for your profile.
3. Optionally, fill in additional documents such as resume and cover letter.
4. Confirm your social media links.
5. Review and submit the form for completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Profile Details (1)
* **Profile Details** (`profile_details`, text, required: false): Fill in your profile details, such as your bio and any other relevant information.

### Select Images (2)
* **Select Images** (`select_images`, select_multiple, required: false): Select up to 6 images for your profile by choosing from the options provided.

### Upload Image (3)
* **Upload Image** (`upload_image`, text, required: false): Upload an image for your profile.

### Bio (4)
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, required: false): Enter your bio, which should provide a brief overview of your background and expertise.

### Contact Info (5)
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your email or phone number for contact purposes.

### Website (6)
* **Website** (`website`, text, required: false): Enter your website URL for easy access to your online presence.

### Social Media (7)
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, required: false): Choose the social media link you wish to share (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube).

### Confirm Email (8)
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Confirm your email address.

### Confirm Phone (9)
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number.

### Confirm Website (10)
* **Confirm Website** (`confirm_website`, text, required: false): Confirm your website URL.

### Confirm Social Media (11)
* **Confirm Social Media** (`confirm_social_media`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm the social media link you wish to share.

### Resume (12)
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: false): Upload your resume for your profile.

### Cover Letter (13)
* **Cover Letter** (`cover_letter`, text, required: false): Upload your cover letter for your profile.

### Additional Documents (14)
* **Additional Documents** (`additional_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any additional documents.

### Submit (15)
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click to submit the form.

### Confirm Submit (16)
* **Confirm Submit** (`confirm_submit`, text, required: false): Confirm that the form has been submitted successfully.

### Confirm Profile Completion (17)
* **Confirm Profile Completion** (`confirm_profile_completion`, text, required: false): Confirm that the profile has been completed.

### Confirm Image Upload (18)
* **Confirm Image Upload** (`confirm_image_upload`, text, required: false): Confirm that the images have been uploaded.

### Confirm Profile Completion Success (19)
* **Confirm Profile Completion Success** (`confirm_profile_completion_success`, text, required: false): Confirm that the profile completion was successful.

### Confirm Profile Completion Failure (20)
* **Confirm Profile Completion Failure** (`confirm_profile_completion_failure`, text, required: false): Confirm that the profile completion was not successful.

### Confirm Form Completion (21)
* **Confirm Form Completion** (`confirm_form_completion`, text, required: false): Confirm that the form has been completed.

### Confirm Upload Image (22)
* **Confirm Upload Image** (`confirm_upload_image`, text, required: false): Confirm that an image has been uploaded.

### Confirm Upload Additional Docs (23)
* **Confirm Upload Additional Docs** (`confirm_upload_additional_docs`, text, required: false): Confirm that additional documents have been uploaded.

### Confirm Cover Letter Upload (24)
* **Confirm Cover Letter Upload** (`confirm_cover_letter_upload`, text, required: false): Confirm that a cover letter has been uploaded.

### Confirm Resume Upload (25)
* **Confirm Resume Upload** (`confirm_resume_upload`, text, required: false): Confirm that a resume has been uploaded.
