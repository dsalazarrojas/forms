# Student Artwork Submission Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Artwork Submission Form Template is designed to collect information about student artworks submitted for assessment or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and artwork title.
2. Select the category of the artwork.
3. Enter the date and time of the submission.
4. Provide a brief description of the artwork.
5. Specify the type of submission (online or physical).
6. Enter the school name, teacher name, and student grade level.
7. Enter any additional details, such as student ID or teacher email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (id: 2)**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their artwork submission.
* **Artwork Title (id: 3)**: Enter a brief title of the artwork being submitted.
* **Category (id: 4)**: Select the category of the artwork (e.g., Art 1, Art 2, or Art 3).
* **Date (id: 5)**: Enter the date the artwork was created or is being submitted.
* **Time (id: 6)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Artwork Description (id: 7)**: Provide a brief description of the artwork.
* **Image (id: 8)**: Enter any relevant image or file information.
* **Submission Type (id: 9)**: Specify if the submission is online or physical.
* **Submission Date (id: 10)**: Enter the date the submission was made.
* **Submission Time (id: 11)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **School Name (id: 12)**: Enter the name of the school where the student is enrolled.
* **Teacher Name (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the student's teacher.
* **Student Grade (id: 14)**: Select the student's grade level (6th-8th or 9th-12th).
* **Teacher Email (id: 18)**: Enter the email of the student's teacher.
