# Editor Article Submission Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Editor Article Submission Request form is a tool for editors to submit their work for internal review and evaluation. It collects relevant information about the article, such as the title, submission date and time, and publication details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and title in the relevant fields (editor_1_name, editor_2_name, etc.).
2. Provide the article title, subtitle, and URL in the respective fields (article_title, article_subtitle, article_url).
3. Select the submission date and time for the article in the submission_date and submission_time fields.
4. Indicate whether the article is published in any of the following:
	* Yes, I have submitted the article for publication.
	* No, I have not submitted the article for publication.
5. Specify whether you are the editor and have assigned the article to a user or if it's a general submission.
6. If you are the editor, select the user you have assigned the article to.
7. Enter your email address and phone number in the email and phone fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Article Submission Request**: This is the main field for the submission request.
* **Editor 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first editor who submitted the article.
* **Editor 2 Name**: Enter the name of the second editor who submitted the article.
* **Article Title**: Enter the title of the article.
* **Article Subtitle**: Enter the subtitle of the article.
* **Article URL**: Enter the URL of the article.
* **Submission Date**: Select the date when the article was submitted for review.
* **Submission Time**: Select the time when the article was submitted for review.
* **Published in Publications**: Indicate whether the article is published or not.
* **Editor Assigned**: Indicate whether the article is assigned to a user or a general submission.
* **Assigned To**: Select the user who the article is assigned to (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Yes/No**: Select 'Yes' for articles that are submitted for publication and 'No' for articles that are not.
* **Publication Type**: Select the type of publication the article belongs to.
* **Article Type**: Select the type of article (e.g., news, feature, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the article.
* **Editors**: Indicate whether the article is edited by one or more editors.
* **Publications**: Indicate whether the article is published in any of the listed publications.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required information, click the submit button to send your submission for review.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete and can be reviewed.
* Double-check your submission date and time to ensure accuracy.
* If you are not sure about the publication status of the article, select 'No' to indicate that it is not published.
* Enter your email and phone number to ensure that you can be contacted for further information.
* If you are the editor, select the user you have assigned the article to to ensure that it is handled correctly.
