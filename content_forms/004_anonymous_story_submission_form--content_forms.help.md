# Anonymous Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect anonymous stories from users, allowing them to share their personal experiences, community stories, historical accounts, and other types of submissions. Please ensure that you are submitting original, unpublished content to respect the integrity of the submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your story from the provided options (Personal Experience, Community Story, Historical Account, Fiction, Poetry, News Tip, or Other).
2. Provide a title for your story that accurately represents its content.
3. Write or paste your story in the space provided.
4. Estimate the word count of your story.
5. Indicate if your story contains any sensitive content (e.g., violence, substance abuse, mental health topics, discrimination, or other sensitive topics).
6. Identify the target audience for your story (General public, Adults only, Young adults, Children, or Specific community).
7. Decide whether you are allowing us to publish your story and under what conditions (Yes, publish anonymously, Yes, with edits, or For review only, not for publication).
8. Specify the format of your submission (Written text, Audio recording, Video, or Mixed media).
9. Choose the language of your story (English, Spanish, French, German, Mandarin, or Other).
10. Confirm if this story has been published before (No, this is original, Yes, previously published, or Partially published).
11. Provide a brief description of yourself as the author (optional).
12. Indicate the purpose of sharing your story (To inform, To entertain, To inspire, To raise awareness, To document, or Other).
13. Decide if you are open to being contacted about this submission (Yes, I am open to contact, or No, leave anonymous).
14. If you are open to contact, provide your email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story Category**: Select the category that best describes your story type (e.g., Personal Experience, Community Story, Historical Account).
* **Story Title**: Enter a title that accurately represents the content of your story.
* **Your Story**: Write or paste your story in this space, ensuring it aligns with the selected category and title.
* **Approximate Word Count**: Estimate the length of your story.
* **Content Warnings**: Indicate if your story contains sensitive content that may be deemed unacceptable for publication.
* **Target Audience**: Select who the story is intended for (General public, Adults only, Young adults, Children, or Specific community).
* **Publication Consent**: Decide if you allow us to publish your story and under what conditions.
* **Story Format**: Specify the format of your submission (Written text, Audio recording, Video, or Mixed media).
* **Language**: Choose the language of your story.
* **Has this Story Been Published Before**: Confirm if this is an original, unpublished work.
* **Author Background**: Provide a brief description of yourself as the author (optional).
* **Purpose of Submission**: Indicate why you are sharing your story (To inform, To entertain, To inspire, To raise awareness, To document, or Other).
* **Would you Like to be Contacted**: Decide if you are open to being contacted about this submission.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your email address if you are open to contact (optional).
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the submission terms.

By following these instructions, you will be able to complete the form successfully and submit your anonymous story for consideration. Please review each field carefully to ensure that your submission meets the necessary guidelines and criteria.
