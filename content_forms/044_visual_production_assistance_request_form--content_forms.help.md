# Visual Production Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Production Assistance Request Form is a tool for requesting visual production assistance. This form will gather necessary information to help production teams make informed decisions and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the project title and project details in the respective fields.
2.  Select the type of asset required (still image, video, motion graphic, or animation).
3.  Provide a detailed description of the asset needed.
4.  Specify the type of production status (pre-production, in production, post-production, or finalized).
5.  Share your contact information.
6.  Add any relevant production notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, **required**): Enter a brief title for the project.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, **optional**): Describe the project in a few sentences, including its context and goals.
* **Production Details** (`production_details`, text, **optional**): Provide additional details about the production process, including any specific requirements or constraints.
* **Production Budget** (`production_budget`, number, **optional**): Enter an approximate budget for the production, if known.
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select the type of asset required (still image, video, motion graphic, or animation). This will help the production team tailor their assistance accordingly.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`, text, **optional**): Give a brief description of the asset needed, including its intended use.
* **Asset Details** (`asset_details`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional details about the asset, such as its size or format requirements.
* **Production Status** (`production_status`, select_multiple, **optional**): Indicate the current production status (pre-production, in production, post-production, or finalized). This will help the production team prioritize requests based on urgency.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, **optional**): Share your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so the production team can reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, note, **optional**): Add any relevant notes or comments about the production, such as specific requirements or constraints.
