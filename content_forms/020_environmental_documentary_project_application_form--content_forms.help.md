# Environmental Documentary Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Documentary Project Application Form is a collection of questions that help gather information about environmental projects, events, and grants. Please ensure that you are applying for a relevant category and have not duplicated your project's submission in the past.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category for your project:
	* Festival: Choose 'Festival' if your project is related to an environmental festival or event.
	* School: Select 'School' if your project is an educational initiative for a school or university.
	* NGO: Choose 'NGO' if your project is related to a non-governmental organization.
	* Grant: Select 'Grant' if your project is seeking funding from a grant.
2. Provide the necessary information for your selected category:
	* **Festival Information** (Select one): Select the type of festival or event (e.g. Festival name, Location, Project duration, or Project budget).
	* **School Information** (Select multiple): Choose one or more Grade levels, Student number, and Teacher number relevant to your project.
	* **NGO Information** (Select one): Select the type of Organization (e.g., Environmental organization, Community organization, etc.) and Location of your NGO.
	* **Grant Information** (Select one): Select the type of Grant (e.g., Research grant, Project grant, etc.) and Grant amount.
3. Add a brief description of your project in the **Project Details** field.
4. If applicable, provide a specific **Project Budget**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Festival Information (Select one)**: Choose the type of festival or event related to your project.
* **School Information (Select multiple)**: Select one or more Grade levels, Student number, and Teacher number relevant to your project.
* **NGO Information (Select one)**: Select the type of Organization and Location of your NGO.
* **Grant Information (Select one)**: Select the type of Grant and Grant amount.
* **Project Details**: Briefly describe your project in 100 characters or less.
* **Project Budget**: Provide the specific project budget, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have not duplicated your project's submission in the past.
* If you need help or clarification, feel free to contact our support team.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
