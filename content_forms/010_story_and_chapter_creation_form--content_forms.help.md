# Story And Chapter Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for creating stories and chapters. It helps users to provide a title, genre, and other metadata for a story, as well as the content and tags for a chapter. The form is meant for users to create and manage their stories in an efficient and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your story.
2. Choose the genre of your story.
3. Select the chapter(s) number(s).
4. Choose the type(s) of your story.
5. Add tags to your story.
6. Enter the content of your chapter.
7. Optionally, add tags to your chapter.
8. Enter the URL or upload an image for your chapter (not shown in this guide).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Story Title** (`story_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your story.
* **Story Genre** (`story_genre`, select_one, optional): Choose the genre of your story from the provided options.
* **Chapter** (`chapter_number`, number, optional): Enter the chapter number(s) you want to add to your story.
* **Story Type** (`story_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type(s) of your story from the provided options.
* **Story Tags** (`story_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Add tags to your story to make it easier to find and categorize.
* **Chapter Content** (`chapter_content`, text, optional): Enter the content of your chapter.
* **Tags** (`story_tags_2`, select_multiple, optional): Add tags to your chapter to make it easier to find and categorize.
* **Chapter Image** (`chapter_image`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the URL or upload an image for your chapter.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for your story.
* Be specific when selecting the genre and story type.
* Use relevant tags to categorize your story and chapter.
* Enter the correct chapter number if multiple chapters are added. 

Note: The field explanations are based on the provided YAML fields. If any field label is weak or unclear, the explanations provided are the best-effort intent without inventing unsupported business logic. 

Form fields and labels are based on the following:
- **Story Title**: The name of the story.
- **Story Genre**: The type of story (e.g., romance, sci-fi, fantasy).
- **Chapter**: A section or part of a larger story.
- **Story Type**: The category or genre of the story (e.g., novel, short story, comic book).
- **Story Tags**: Keywords or keywords that describe the story.
- **Chapter Content**: The text or description of the chapter.
- **Tags**: Keywords or keywords that describe the chapter.
- **Chapter Image**: A visual representation of the chapter (e.g., a screenshot or a drawing).
- **Chapter Number**: The number of the chapter in the story.
