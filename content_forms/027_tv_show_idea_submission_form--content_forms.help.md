# TV Show Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Show Idea Submission Form is a tool for submitting TV show ideas to our company. Please use this form to share your creative ideas with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your TV show idea.
2. Choose the correct genre and target audience for your show.
3. Write a brief logline and synopsis to summarize your show's concept.
4. If desired, upload a supporting file to help us better understand your idea.
5. Provide your submitters information so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **TV Show Title** (`tv_show_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your TV show idea. This will help us understand the main concept of your show.
* **TV Show Genre** (`genre`, select_one, required): Choose the genre that best fits your TV show idea. Select one of the following options: Horror, Comedy, or Drama.
* **Log Line** (`logline`, text, optional): Write a brief log line (1-2 sentences) that summarizes your show's concept. This should give us a quick idea of what your show is about.
* **Synopsis** (`synopsis`, text, optional): Provide a more detailed synopsis (1-2 paragraphs) of your show's concept. This should give us a better understanding of your show's story and characters.
* **Main Character** (`main_character`, text, optional): Tell us about the main character(s) in your show. Who is the protagonist or main character of your show?
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, required): Choose the audience that your show is intended for. Select one or more of the following options: Adult, Teen, or Family.
* **Optional File Upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): If you have any supporting files (e.g. script, pitch document, or image) that can help us better understand your show idea, upload them here.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Provide your name, email, and any additional contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields (genre and target audience) before submitting.
* Keep your log line and synopsis brief and focused on your show's main concept.
* If you have any supporting files, please make sure they are in a compatible format (e.g. PDF, DOCX, or JPEG).
* We appreciate any additional information you provide, but keep in mind that our goal is to gather a brief and clear idea of your show concept.
