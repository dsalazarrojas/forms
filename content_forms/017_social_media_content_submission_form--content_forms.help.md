# social_media_content_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "social_media_content_submission_form" is used to collect and review content that users wish to share on social media platforms. This form ensures that all content is properly reviewed and moderated before it is released.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  Select the correct options from the dropdown menu for "Media Type".
3.  Enter the desired content in the "Content" field.
4.  Provide the URL of the media you wish to share in the "Media URL" field.
5.  Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant contact information so we can get in touch with you if needed.
- **Media Type** (`media_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of media you wish to share, such as image, video, or text post.
- **Content** (`content`, text, optional): Enter the content you wish to share on social media, including any relevant details or context.
- **Media URL** (`media_url`, text, optional): If sharing a URL, enter the URL of the media you wish to share.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently.
- Avoid resubmitting content that has already been submitted through this form.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
