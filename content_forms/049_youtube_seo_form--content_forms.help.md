# YouTube SEO Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to configure and optimize video content for YouTube search results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
	* Video Title: Provide a descriptive title for your video.
	* Video Description: Describe your video in a few words.
2. Choose options for the following fields:
	* Video Thumbnails: Choose whether to add images to show in search results.
	* Video Tags: Input relevant keywords for your video.
	* Music License: Decide whether your video includes copyrighted music.
	* Image License: Decide whether your video includes copyrighted images.
	* Blog Links: Add any blog posts that link to your video.
	* Social Media Links: Add links to your social media profiles.
3. Fill in the optional fields:
	* Twitter Post: Choose whether to post to Twitter when submitting this form.
	* Airtable Base, Airtable API Key, Google Sheets, Google Sheets URL, Zapier Account ID, and Zapier API Key: These fields are for integrations with external services, but are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Video Title** (text, required): Provide a descriptive title for your video.
* **Video Description** (text, required): Describe your video in a few words.
* **Video Thumbnails** (select_multiple, optional): Choose whether to add images to show in search results.
	+ 'Yes' - Add images to show in search results.
	+ 'No' - Do not add images.
* **Video Tags** (text, optional): Input relevant keywords for your video.
* **Music License** (select_one, optional): Decide whether your video includes copyrighted music.
	+ 'Yes' - Your video includes copyrighted music.
	+ 'No' - Your video does not include copyrighted music.
* **Image License** (select_one, optional): Decide whether your video includes copyrighted images.
	+ 'Yes' - Your video includes copyrighted images.
	+ 'No' - Your video does not include copyrighted images.
* **Blog Links** (select_multiple, optional): Add any blog posts that link to your video.
	+ 'Yes' - Add blog links.
	+ 'No' - Do not add blog links.
* **Social Media Links** (select_multiple, optional): Add links to your social media profiles.
	+ 'Yes' - Add social media links.
	+ 'No' - Do not add social media links.
* **Twitter Post** (text, optional): Choose whether to post to Twitter when submitting this form.
* **Airtable Base** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Airtable, but is not required.
* **Airtable API Key** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Airtable, but is not required.
* **Google Sheets** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Google Sheets, but is not required.
* **Google Sheets URL** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Google Sheets, but is not required.
* **Zapier Account ID** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Zapier, but is not required.
* **Zapier API Key** (text, optional): This field is for integration with Zapier, but is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Choose options carefully, as they will affect how your video is displayed in YouTube search results.
* If you're not sure about a particular field, you can leave it blank or contact us for help.
