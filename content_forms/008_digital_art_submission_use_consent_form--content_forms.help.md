# Digital Art Submission Use Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from artists submitting their digital artwork for review, exhibition, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information as the artist in the "Artist Information" field.
2. Provide details about the artwork, including its title, medium, size, and any other relevant information in the "Artwork Details" field.
3. Read and agree to the "Usage Consent" statement.
4. Sign and date your agreement in the "Signature" field.
5. Upload your artwork as described in the "Upload Artwork" field.
6. Finally, click "Review and Submit" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information (Text)**: This field is where you should fill in your name, address, and contact information as the artist.
* **Artwork Details (Text)**: Provide a detailed description of your artwork, including its title, medium, size, and any other relevant information.
* **Usage Consent (Text)**: Read this statement carefully and agree or disagree. If you agree, please type 'YES' in this field.
* **Signature (Text)**: Sign and date your agreement. Your signature is a confirmation that you have read and agree to the terms in the usage consent statement.
* **Artist Details (Text)**: You may optionally enter more information about yourself as an artist in this field.
* **Artist Signature (Text)**: Your signature is required in this field to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms in the usage consent statement.
* **Upload Artwork (Text)**: Upload your digital artwork as described in this field. Please follow the required format for file types and size.
* **Review and Submit (Text)**: Once all fields have been filled out, click this button to submit your application.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the artwork you upload is in the correct format and meets the required size criteria.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, you can contact us at <form@gic.mx>
- This form is designed to be filled out by the artist, and we will respond promptly to your submissions.
