# Vendor Content Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vendor Content Release Form" is used to collect information from vendors about their content and its intended use or distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor name and contact information, including email, phone number, and address.
2. Select the type of content you are sharing (e.g. article, image, video).
3. Specify where you would like your content to be distributed (e.g. online, offline, print).
4. Choose the level of permission you are granting for your content (e.g. public, private, confidential).
5. Date and time of signature.
6. Sign the form digitally to acknowledge your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Please enter your vendor name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your business address or the address where you are based.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the name of your city or location.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or region.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, text, required): Specify the type of content you are sharing (e.g. article, image, video).
* **Content Location** (`content_location`, text, required): Specify where your content is located (e.g. on the web, in a file, on a social media platform).
* **Content Description** (`content_description`, text, required): Describe your content in a few words.
* **Permission Level** (`permission_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of permission you grant for your content (e.g. public, private, confidential).
	+ Public: Anyone can view and use your content.
	+ Private: Only authorized personnel can view and use your content.
	+ Confidential: Only a select few can view and use your content.
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the channels through which you want your content to be distributed (e.g. online, offline, print, email, phone, social media).
	+ Online: Publish on the web or online platforms.
	+ Offline: Distribute through print or physical channels.
	+ Print: Publish in print media like newspapers or magazines.
	+ Email: Send via email.
	+ Phone: Distribute via phone calls.
	+ Social Media: Publish on social media platforms.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date on which you sign this form.
* **Time** (`signature_time`, time, required): Time on which you sign this form.
* **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, note, required): Sign this form digitally to acknowledge your agreement and grant permission for your content to be used or distributed as described above.
