# Magazine Content Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for magazine content contributors to express their interest in specific content areas, such as contributor pitches or story ideas. Contributors can indicate their interest in one or more of these areas and provide any relevant materials for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided options for contributor pitches, story ideas, and other content areas.
2. Select the areas in which you are interested in contributing.
3. Provide any relevant materials for review, if applicable.
4. Submit the form to express your interest in contributing to the magazine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contributor Pitches
* **contributor_pitches** (Select One):
	+ This field asks whether you are interested in pitching your ideas for contributor articles.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in pitching your ideas.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not interested in pitching.

### Story Ideas
* **story_ideas** (Select Multiple):
	+ This field asks whether you have story ideas for the magazine.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have story ideas.
	+ Choose 'No' if you do not have story ideas.

### Review Materials
* **review_materials** (Text):
	+ This field is for you to provide any relevant materials, such as manuscripts or research, that you would like the magazine to review.
	+ Please enter the file URL or description of the materials.

### Data Collection
* **data_collection** (Select One):
	+ This field asks whether you are interested in helping with data collection for the magazine.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in data collection.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not interested in data collection.

### Editorial Teams
* **editorial_teams** (Select One):
	+ This field asks whether you are interested in joining an editorial team for the magazine.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in joining an editorial team.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not interested in joining an editorial team.

### Collection Organization
* **collection_organization** (Note):
	+ This field is for you to provide any additional information about your collection and organization experience.
	+ Please enter any relevant details or descriptions.

### Contributor Pitches 2
* **contributor_pitches_2** (Select One):
	+ This field asks whether you are interested in pitching your ideas for contributor articles.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are interested in pitching your ideas.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not interested in pitching.

### Story Ideas 2
* **story_ideas_2** (Select Multiple):
	+ This field asks whether you have story ideas for the magazine.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you have story ideas.
	+ Choose 'No' if you do not have story ideas.

Note: This form does not require any personal contact information, and the submitted information will be reviewed by the editorial team for consideration. Please ensure that you have read and agree to the magazine's content guidelines before submitting.
