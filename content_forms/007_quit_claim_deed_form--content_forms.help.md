# Quit Claim Deed Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quit Claim Deed Form is a document used to transfer ownership of a property without the need for a court-supervised process. It is a legal document that conveys the interest of a property from one person (Grantor) to another (Grantee).

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Grantor Name**: Enter the name of the person currently owning the property.
2. **Grantee Name**: Enter the name of the person or entity that will take ownership of the property.
3. **County**: Select the county in which the property is located from the provided options (Albany, Erie, Monroe, or Orleans).
4. **Date**: Enter the date on which the quit claim deed is being executed.
5. **Time**: (Optional) Enter the time of day the quit claim deed is being executed.
6. **Notary**: Enter the name of the notary public who will witness the signing of the deed.
7. **Property Description**: Provide a brief description of the property being transferred.
8. **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property being transferred.
9. **Grantor Signature**: Sign the document as the Grantor.
10. **Grantee Signature**: Sign the document as the Grantee.
11. **Notary Seal**: (Optional) Enter the seal of the notary public.
12. **Notary Signature**: Sign the document as the notary public.
13. **Witness 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first witness.
14. **Witness 1 Signature**: Sign the document as Witness 1.
15. **Witness 2 Name**: Enter the name of the second witness.
16. **Witness 2 Signature**: (Optional) Sign the document as Witness 2.
17. **County Recorder**: Enter the name of the county recorder.
18. **Document Number**: Enter the document number assigned by the county recorder.
19. **Document Date**: Enter the date the document was recorded.
20. **County Filing Fee**: (Optional) Enter the filing fee paid to the county recorder.
21. **County Filing Fee Currency**: (Optional) Enter the currency of the filing fee.
22. **Recording Fees**: (Optional) Enter any additional recording fees paid.
23. **Recording Fees Currency**: (Optional) Enter the currency of the recording fees.
24. **Tax 1**: (Optional) Enter any tax information related to the property.
25. **Tax 2**: (Optional) Enter any additional tax information related to the property.
