# WBBP Intake Form 2021 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WBBP Intake Form 2021 is a medical intake form designed to collect essential information from patients and visitors for medical attention. This form helps our medical team to gather the required details needed for a smooth and efficient care process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
- Fill in the form with the requested information.
- Ensure that all fields with the "required" attribute are completed before proceeding to the next field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that we can contact you on in case of emergencies or for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you during your visit or for scheduling purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your visit or the date you are requesting medical attention.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your visit or the time you are requesting medical attention.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location or department where you are seeking medical attention.
* **Reason for Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, `text`, required): Describe the reason for your visit, including any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your attending doctor or the doctor you are requesting to see.
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`, required): If you have any existing medical records, please attach them or provide the relevant information for our medical team to access.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask for assistance from our medical staff.
