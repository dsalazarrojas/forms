# lifestyle_content_preference_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "lifestyle_content_preference_survey" form is designed to collect your lifestyle, content preferences, and consumption habits to help us suggest relevant content and tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read this guide carefully before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure you select all relevant options for each question.
4. Enter your email address and phone number accurately.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.
6. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly introduce yourself and explain how you found our website.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required/true): Please enter your age.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `select_one`, required/true): Select your occupation (Artist, Influencer, Entrepreneur, Student, Other).
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the level of education you have completed (High School, College, University, Master's, Doctorate, Other).
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, optional/false): If applicable, please enter your income (this field is optional).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional/false): Enter your location (city, state, or region).
* **Interests** (`interests`, `text`, optional/false): Describe your interests.
* **Social Media Platforms** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Select the social media platforms you use (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Other).
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the type of content you consume (Lifestyle, Food, Travel, Health, Fashion, Beauty, Technology, Finance, Other).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/true): Enter your phone number.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, optional/false): Select your ideal time of day.
* **Time of Week** (`time_of_week`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose when you prefer to consume content (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_of_use`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select how often you consume content (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, optional/false): If applicable, please enter the time you spend on content.
* **Devices Used** (`devices_used`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Select the devices you use to consume content (Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, Television, Radio).
* **Social Media Platforms Used** (`social_media_platforms`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Select the social media platforms you use (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Other).
* **Content Consumed** (`content_consumed`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the types of content you consume (Blog, Podcast, Video, Social Media, Book, Other).
* **Frequency of Content Consumption** (`frequency_of_content_consumption`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select how often you consume content (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Time Spent on Content** (`time_spent_on_content`, `number`, optional/false): If applicable, please enter the time you spend on content.
* **Devices Used for Content Consumption** (`devices_used_for_content_consumption`, `select_multiple`, optional/false): Select the devices you use to consume content (Smartphone, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, Television).
* **Content Format** (`content_format`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose the content formats you prefer (Text, Image, Video, Audio).
* **Ideal Time of Day** (`time_of_day_ideal`, `time`, optional/false): Select your ideal time of day.
* **Ideal Time of Week** (`time_of_week_ideal`, `select_one`, optional/false): Choose when you prefer to consume content (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, optional/false): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all required questions accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed only once.
