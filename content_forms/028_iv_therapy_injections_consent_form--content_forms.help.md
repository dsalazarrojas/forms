# IV Therapy Injections Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients or their legal representatives for IV therapy injections. It is a critical document that outlines the patient's understanding and agreement to receive IV therapy injections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the patient's **Patient Name** in the first field.
2. If applicable, enter the patient's **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Enter the patient's **Contact Number** and **Contact Number Type** in the fields provided.
4. In the **Medical History** field, provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
5. Enter the **Date of Consent** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. The **Physician Signature** field is for the doctor or healthcare provider to sign and date.
7. Enter the **Physician Name** in the field provided.
8. If applicable, enter the **Facility Name** where the treatment will take place.
9. Enter the **Injection Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` if the injection is not on the same day as the consent form is signed.
10. Select the **Consent Status** from the provided options (Yes, No, Unknown).
11. If the consent status is **No**, enter a brief reason for not providing consent in the **Reason for No** field.
12. Sign and date the **Physician Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth, or leave this field blank if not applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number, where the patient or their legal representative can be reached.
* **Contact Number Type**: Select the type of contact number (e.g., home, work, cell, etc.).
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date the consent form is signed.
* **Physician Signature**: The doctor or healthcare provider must sign and date this field.
* **Physician Name**: Enter the name of the doctor or healthcare provider signing the consent form.
* **Facility Name**: If the treatment is not performed at a hospital or other facility, leave this field blank.
* **Injection Date**: Enter the date of the injection, if different from the consent date.
* **Injection Site**: Describe the location of the injection.
* **Consent Status**: Select one of the provided options (Yes, No, Unknown).
* **Reason for No**: If consent status is No, enter a brief reason for not providing consent.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign and date the physician's signature field to confirm consent has been obtained. 

Note: The physician signature is a crucial step to confirm that the patient or their legal representative has provided informed consent for the IV therapy injection.
