# Group Chat Logo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a group chat logo, including its name, logo file location, contact details, and usage permission. It is intended for voluntary submission and serves as a means for users to provide their logo for inclusion in a platform or system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions to submit this form.
2. Fill out the fields as required, including:
	* `Logo File`: Upload your group chat logo file.
	* `Contact Details`: Enter the contact information for the logo owner.
	* `Usage Permission`: Indicate whether the logo can be used for public display, is pending review, or not used at all.
	* `Submitter Details`: Enter any additional information about the submitter, if necessary.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logo File** (`logo_file`, text, required): Upload the image file for your group chat logo.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter the contact name, email, and/or other relevant information for the logo owner.
* **Usage Permission** (`usage_permission`, select_one, required): Select the usage permission for the logo:
	+ `True`: The logo can be used for public display.
	+ `False`: The logo cannot be used for public display.
	+ `Pending`: The logo is pending review for usage permission.
* **Submitter Details** (`submitter_details`, text, required): Enter any additional information about yourself as the submitter, if necessary.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit this form.
- Review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
- If you have previously submitted a similar logo or form, do not submit this form again.
