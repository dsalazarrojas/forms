# Media Content Distribution Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Content Distribution Audit Form is designed to help assess and analyze the distribution of media content across various channels. This form will help you gather information about the performance of your media content on different platforms, allowing you to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Channel name** (Field 1) where the media content was published. This is the name of the channel where the content was distributed.
2. Enter the **Campaign name** (Field 2) associated with the media content, if applicable.
3. Enter the **Published at** (Field 3) date and time when the media content was published.
4. Select the **Media type** (Field 4) of the content (e.g. video, image, article).
5. Enter the **Media link** (Field 5) where the content can be found.
6. Identify the **Target audience** (Field 6) of the media content.
7. Select the **Distribution channels** (Field 7) where the content was distributed (e.g. Social media, Email, Website, Mobile app, Other).
8. Enter **Assessment notes** (Field 8) about the performance of the content on each channel.
9. Rate the **Effectiveness rating** (Field 9) of the content on each channel (High, Medium, Low).
10. Enter the **Channel published by** (Field 10) if the content was published by someone else.
11. Enter the **Published by** (Field 11) who published the content.
12. Enter the **Campaign goals** (Field 12) of the content.
13. Enter the **Channel goals** (Field 13) of the content.
14. Enter the **Campaign budget** (Field 14) spent on the content.
15. Enter the **Channel budget** (Field 15) spent on the content.
16. Enter the **Campaign duration** (Field 16) of the content.
17. Enter the **Channel duration** (Field 17) of the content.
18. Enter the **Media content distribution** (Field 18) description.
19. Add any additional **Notes** (Field 19) about the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Channel name** (Field 1): Enter the name of the channel where the media content was published.
* **Campaign name** (Field 2): Enter the name of the campaign associated with the media content, if applicable.
* **Published at** (Field 3): Enter the date and time when the media content was published.
* **Media type** (Field 4): Select the type of media content (e.g. video, image, article).
* **Media link** (Field 5): Enter the link where the media content can be found.
* **Target audience** (Field 6): Identify the audience the media content is intended for.
* **Distribution channels** (Field 7): Select the channels where the content was distributed (e.g. Social media, Email, Website, Mobile app, Other).
* **Assessment notes** (Field 8): Enter notes about the performance of the content on each channel.
* **Effectiveness rating** (Field 9): Rate the performance of the content on each channel (High, Medium, Low).
* **Channel published by** (Field 10): If the content was published by someone else, enter their name or organization.
* **Published by** (Field 11): Enter the name of the person or organization that published the content.
* **Campaign goals** (Field 12): Enter the goals of the campaign associated with the content.
* **Channel goals** (Field 13): Enter the goals of the channel where the content was published.
* **Campaign budget** (Field 14): Enter the budget spent on the campaign.
* **Channel budget** (Field 15): Enter the budget spent on the channel.
* **Campaign duration** (Field 16): Enter the duration of the campaign.
* **Channel duration** (Field 17): Enter the duration of the channel.
* **Media content distribution** (Field 18): Enter a description of how the content was distributed.
* **Notes** (Field 19): Add any additional notes about the content.

Note that this form is designed to be a tool to gather information, not to be filled out by a machine or automatically generated. Please fill it out manually to ensure accurate and relevant data.
