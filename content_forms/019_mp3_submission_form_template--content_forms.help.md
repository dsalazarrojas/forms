# mp3_submission_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed for users to create and submit mp3 files, including music or podcast content, with relevant information such as category, description, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your submission in the "Form Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your submission in the "Form Description" field.
3. Select the category for your submission from the "Form Category" field (e.g., Content Forms, Entertainment Forms).
4. Upload your mp3 file in the "Form File" field.
5. Specify the type of your submission (e.g., Music, Podcast, Project).
6. Enter the length of your audio file in the "Audio Length" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your submission.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a short description of your submission.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for your submission from the options (Content Forms, Entertainment Forms).
* **Form File** (`form_file`, text, optional): Upload your mp3 file for submission.
* **Audio File** (`form_audio_file`, text, optional): This field is likely for uploading additional audio files, but its purpose is unclear.
* **Audio Type** (`form_audio_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your submission (e.g., Music, Podcast, Project).
* **Audio Length** (`form_audio_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of your audio file in [unit of measurement].
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Review and submit your form.
