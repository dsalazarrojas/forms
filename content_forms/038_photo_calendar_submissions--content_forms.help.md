# photo_calendar_submissions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_calendar_submissions form is a user-facing form designed to collect photos and submitter information for a photo calendar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Upload your photo in the "Upload Photo" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your photo in the "description" field.
3. Select the category that best fits your photo from the "Category" field.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Submitter", "Submitter Email", and "Submitter Phone" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Upload Photo** (`upload_photo`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo to be displayed in the photo calendar.
* **description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your photo (e.g., location, date, occasion).
* **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the category that best fits your photo from the options provided (e.g., farming, agriculture).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the submitter of the photo.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address as the submitter of the photo.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number as the submitter of the photo.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
* Upload a high-quality photo that is suitable for public display.
* Double-check your information before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
