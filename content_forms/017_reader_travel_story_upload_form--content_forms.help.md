# Reader Travel Story Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Reader Travel Story Upload Form is designed to collect user-submitted travel stories with various details such as title, story, location, and other relevant information. This form helps users to share their travel experiences with the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your travel story in the "Travel Story" field.
2. Write your travel story in the "Story" field.
3. Enter the date of your travel in the "Date" field (if applicable).
4. Select the category of your travel story from the "Category" field.
5. Enter the location of your travel in the "Location" field.
6. Enter any additional details such as "Publisher", "Review", "Email", "Phone", "Website", and "Social Media" fields.
7. Select any relevant "Tags" for your travel story.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your travel story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Story (Required)**: Enter a brief title for your travel story.
* **Story (Required)**: Write a detailed account of your travel experience.
* **Date**: If you're submitting a story about a specific travel date, enter it in this field.
* **Time**: If you'd like to specify the time of your travel, enter it here.
* **Location (Required)**: Enter the location of your travel.
* **Country**: If you'd like to specify the country where you traveled, enter it here.
* **City**: If you'd like to specify the city where you traveled, enter it here.
* **State (Required)**: Enter the state where you traveled.
* **Travel Story Photo**: If you have a photo related to your travel story, you can upload it here.
* **Publisher**: If you'd like to specify the publisher of your travel story, enter it here.
* **Category (Optional)**: Select the category of your travel story from the options provided.
* **Review**: Write a review of your travel experience.
* **Email**: If you'd like to share your email address with the community, enter it here.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to share your phone number with the community, enter it here.
* **Website**: If you have a website related to your travel story, enter the URL here.
* **Social Media**: If you have a social media link related to your travel story, enter it here.
* **Social Media URL**: If you have a social media URL, enter it here.
* **Tags (Optional)**: Select any relevant tags for your travel story.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your travel story.
* **Submit (Required)**: Click this button to complete and submit your travel story.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel the form and start again.
* **Back**: This button is not used in this form.
* **Next**: This button is not used in this form.
* **Previous**: This button is not used in this form.
* **Save**: This button is not used in this form.
