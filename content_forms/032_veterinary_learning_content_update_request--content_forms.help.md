**veterinary_learning_content_update_request - Help Guide**
====================================================

## Purpose

This form is designed to allow users to request updates to veterinary learning content. It collects information about the user, their contact details, and the specific updates they need for veterinary learning content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information: Provide your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your contact information: Include your phone number and email address.
3. Specify your service request: Describe the type of service you need for your veterinary learning content.
4. Select the file type: Choose the type of file you are uploading (e.g., docx, pdf, mp3, mp4, jpg, png).
5. Choose the veterinary team: Select the team member you would like to assist you.
6. Enter any additional details: Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
7. Set the request date and time: Enter the date and time you would like the service to be completed.
8. Set the request time (24h format): Enter the 24-hour format of the request time.
9. Set the service request status: Describe the status of your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your phone number and email address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Service Request**: Describe the type of service you need for your veterinary learning content.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you need (e.g., video, text, image, audio).
* **Service Type Other**: If you have selected "Service Type" as "Other", provide details about the type of service you need.
* **Content ID**: Enter the ID of the content you are updating.
* **Content Version**: Enter the version of the content you are updating.
* **File**: Enter the file name or description of the file you are uploading.
* **Upload File**: Enter the file you would like to upload.
* **File Type**: Choose the type of file you are uploading (e.g., docx, pdf, mp3, mp4, jpg, png).
* **Veterinary Team**: Choose the team member you would like to assist you.
* **Veterinarian**: Enter the name of the veterinarian who will be working on your request.
* **Team Leader**: Enter the name of the team leader who will be overseeing your request.
* **Team Member**: Enter the name of the team member who will be assisting you.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the team member who will be contacting you.
* **Department**: Enter the department or section you are requesting assistance from.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like the service to be completed.
* **Request Time**: Enter the time you would like the service to be completed.
* **Request Time (24h format)**: Enter the 24-hour format of the request time.
* **Request Time Other**: If you have selected "Request Time" as "Other", provide details about the time you need.
* **Request Time Other 24**: If you have selected "Request Time" as "Other 24", provide details about the time you need.
* **Service Request Status**: Describe the status of your service request.
