# Outline Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Outline Creation Survey" form is designed to collect structured data from end-users for the purpose of creating an outline.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Main Form" section, which is the first page of the form.
2. In the "Form Data" section, enter any additional information that is relevant to the outline you are creating.
3. In the "Section 1" to "Section 23" sections, select the relevant options that best describe your outline's characteristics.
4. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields, indicated by a *.
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it for processing.
