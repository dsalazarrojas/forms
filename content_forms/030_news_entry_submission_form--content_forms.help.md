# News Entry Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about news entries. Please ensure that you have the necessary information and that you're not duplicating any existing news entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully to understand what information is required.
* Fill in the form fields as instructed below.
* Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Headline** (2): Enter a brief and accurate headline for the news entry.
* **Summary** (3): Provide a short summary of the news entry, approximately 150-200 words.
* **Content** (4): Enter the main content of the news entry. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **Image** (5): If applicable, provide a relevant image for the news entry.
* **Attachment** (6): If applicable, provide any attachments or supporting documents for the news entry.
* **Category** (7): Select the category or tags that best describe the news entry. Multiple categories can be selected.
* **Tags** (8): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the news entry.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, please ask for assistance.
