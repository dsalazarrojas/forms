# Animation Scene Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Scene Revision Request Form is designed to collect information from users who need to request revisions to animation scenes. This form helps the animation team understand the context and urgency of the request, ensuring a smooth and efficient workflow.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the request information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the project and scene details, including the project name, scene number, and scene title.
3. Specify the type of revision needed and the priority level.
4. Describe the current issue and the desired change or outcome.
5. Add any specific instructions or references that are relevant to the request.
6. If necessary, mention any other scenes or elements affected by this change.
7. Provide the estimated duration of the change and the requested completion date.
8. Specify the approval authority (supervisor or director's name, if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name (1)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your email address for request status updates.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your contact number for any additional inquiries or follow-ups.
* **Project Name (7)**: Enter the name of the animation project.
* **Scene Number or Code (8)**: Enter the scene's identifier.
* **Scene Title (9)**: Enter a brief title of the scene.
* **Timecode in Project (10)**: Enter start and end timecodes if applicable.
* **Type of Revision Needed (12)**: Select one or multiple options from Character Movement, Facial Expression, Timing Adjustment, Color or Lighting, Layout or Composition, Visual Effects, Camera Movement, Background Elements, Sound Sync, or Overall Style.
* **Priority Level (13)**: Select the urgency of the revision request: Critical, High, Medium, Low, or Future.
* **Current Issue (15)**: Describe the problem or issue with the scene.
* **Desired Change or Outcome (16)**: Describe exactly what you want to see changed or achieved.
* **Specific Instructions or Reference (17)**: Enter any technical details or style guidelines relevant to the request.
* **Reference Materials (18)**: Provide URLs or descriptions of references that support your request.
* **Does this Scene Depend on Other Scenes? (20)**: Select one option from No dependencies, Depends on previous scene, Depends on following scene, or Multiple dependencies.
* **Other Elements Affected by this Change (21)**: Describe any other scenes or elements that will be affected by this change.
* **Requested Completion Date (23)**: Enter the date by which you need this revision completed.
* **Estimated Duration of Changes (24)**: Select the estimated time it will take to complete the change: Less than 1 hour, 1-4 hours, 4-8 hours, 1-2 days, 2-5 days, Over 1 week, or Unknown.
* **Who Approved this Revision Request (26)**: Enter the name of the supervisor or director who approved this request.
* **Additional Notes or Comments (27)**: Provide any other relevant information or comments about your request.
