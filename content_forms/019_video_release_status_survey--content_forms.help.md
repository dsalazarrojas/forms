# video release status survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document the status of video releases on various platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required in the form.
2. Enter the video title in the "Video Title" field.
3. Select the current release status from the "Release Status" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the release date in the "Release Date" field.
5. Optionally, enter a secondary release date in the "Release Date 2" field if applicable.
6. Select the platforms where the video has been released or will be released.
7. Enter the contact email and phone number for further communication, if necessary.
8. Provide any additional contact details in the "Contact Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Title** (`video_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the video being tracked.
* **Release Status** (`release_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the video's release, which can be "Released", "Unreleased", or "Removed".
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date the video was released or scheduled to be released.
* **Release Date 2** (`release_date2`, date, optional): Enter a secondary release date if applicable.
* **Platforms** (`platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platforms where the video has been released or will be released. Options include Facebook, Vimeo, YouTube, and Instagram.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for further communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for further communication.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the relevant team for clarification or assistance.
