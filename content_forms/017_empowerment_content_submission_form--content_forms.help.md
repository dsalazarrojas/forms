# Empowerment Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect content submissions from users. The content can be in the form of stories, articles, or media. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your original story or article in the **original story** field.
2. Provide the name of the author in the **author** field.
3. Indicate whether the content is an **article or story** by selecting the corresponding option.
4. Select the type of **media** (Yes/No) if it's a media content.
5. Enter the **media URL** if the content is a media.
6. Provide a brief description of the content in the **media description** field.
7. Enter the length of the content in the **article length** or **story length** fields, depending on the type selected.
8. Select the **date posted** if the content is already published online.
9. Enter the **location** where the content was created or posted.
10. Enter the name of the **organization** behind the content, if applicable.
11. Choose between **Coaches**, **Nonprofits**, or **Both** for the **coach or nonprofit** field, depending on the context.
12. Select the type of content in the **content type** field (Stories, Articles, Media).
13. Enter your **email** contact information.
14. Enter your **phone** number.
15. Finally, click **Submit** to complete the form or **Cancel** to discard your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **original story**: A field to enter your original story or article. This field is not required, but it's an optional field for users to provide additional context.
* **author**: The field to enter the name of the author of the content.
* **article or story**: A field to select between article and story. This selection will affect the **article length** and **story length** fields.
* **media type**: A field to select whether the content is a media.
* **media url**: The field to enter the URL of the media content. This field is not required if the content is not media.
* **media description**: A field for a brief description of the media content.
* **article length**: The field to enter the length of the article. This field is not required.
* **story length**: The field to enter the length of the story. This field is not required.
* **date posted**: A field to select the date the content was posted. This field is not required.
* **location**: The field to enter the location where the content was created or posted.
* **organization**: The field to enter the name of the organization behind the content, if applicable.
* **coach or nonprofit**: A field to select between **Coaches**, **Nonprofits**, or **Both** for the context of the content.
* **content type**: A field to select the type of content (Stories, Articles, Media).
* **email**: A field to enter your email contact information.
* **phone**: The field to enter your phone number.
* **submit**: A field to complete and submit the form or **Cancel** to discard your entries.
