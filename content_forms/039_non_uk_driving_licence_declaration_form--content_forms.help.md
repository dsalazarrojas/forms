# Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for declaration of non-UK driving licence information. It is intended for individuals who do not possess a UK driving licence and need to declare their driving licence details for various reasons such as international travel, non-UK driving, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your non-UK driving licence number in the "driving_licence_number" field.
2.  Provide the expiry date of your non-UK driving licence in the "expiry_date" field.
3.  Enter the date your non-UK driving licence was issued in the "issue_date" field.
4.  Select the country where your non-UK driving licence was issued in the "issue_country" field.
5.  Enter your name as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_name" field.
6.  Enter your address as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_address" field.
7.  Enter your postcode as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_postcode" field.
8.  Enter your date of birth as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_dob" field.
9.  Select your gender as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_gender" field.
10. Select your nationality as it appears on your non-UK driving licence in the "driver_nationality" field.
11. Select whether the non-UK driving licence you declare is in type "Yes" or "No" in the "driver_licence_type" field.
12. Enter your passport number as it appears on your non-UK passport in the "driver_passport_number" field.
13. Select the country your non-UK passport was issued in the "driver_passport_country" field.
14. Select whether your non-UK passport has expired in the "driver_passport_expires" field.
15. Enter the date your non-UK passport was issued in the "driver_passport_issue_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Non UK Driving Licence Declaration Form** (`text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
*  **Driving Licence Number** (`driving_licence_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your non-UK driving licence number.
*  **Expiry Date** (`expiry_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the expiry date of your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your non-UK driving licence was issued.
*  **Issue Country** (`issue_country`, select_one, required/optional): Select the country where your non-UK driving licence was issued.
*  **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Driver Address** (`driver_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Driver Postcode** (`driver_postcode`, text, required/optional): Enter your postcode as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Driver Date of Birth** (`driver_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Gender** (`driver_gender`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your gender as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Nationality** (`driver_nationality`, text, required/optional): Select your nationality as it appears on your non-UK driving licence.
*  **Driving Licence Type** (`driver_licence_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the non-UK driving licence you declare is in type "Yes" or "No".
*  **Passport Number** (`driver_passport_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your non-UK passport number.
*  **Passport Country** (`driver_passport_country`, select_one, required/optional): Select the country your non-UK passport was issued in.
*  **Passport Expiry** (`driver_passport_expires`, date, required/optional): Select whether your non-UK passport has expired.
*  **Passport Issue Date** (`driver_passport_issue_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your non-UK passport was issued.
