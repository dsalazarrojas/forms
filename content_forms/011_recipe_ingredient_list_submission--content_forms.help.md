# Recipe Ingredient List Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help contributors provide information about recipe ingredients to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Ingredient List" option from the drop-down menu.
2. Choose the relevant contributor details such as name, email, phone, and location.
3. Provide the name of the recipe and the name of the ingredient(s) used in it.
4. Enter the quantity and unit of the ingredient(s).
5. Provide any additional notes about the contribution.
6. Choose whether each ingredient list is involved in the recipe or not.
7. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ingredient List**: A drop-down menu where you can select the list(s) of ingredients involved in the recipe.
* **Contributor Name**: A text field to enter the name of the contributor.
* **Contribution Date**: A date field where you can enter the date of contribution.
* **Contribution Time**: A time field where you can enter the time of contribution.
* **Contribution Note**: A text area where you can enter notes about the contribution.
* **Contributor Email**: A text field to enter the email of the contributor.
* **Contributor Phone**: A text field to enter the phone number of the contributor.
* **Contributor Location**: A text field to enter the location of the contributor.
* **Recipe Name**: A text field to enter the name of the recipe.
* **Ingredient Name**: A text field to enter the name of the ingredient.
* **Quantity**: A number field to enter the quantity of the ingredient.
* **Unit**: A text field to enter the unit of the ingredient.
* **Notes**: A text area where you can enter notes about the ingredient.
* **Ingredient List 2 - 13**: Select multiple fields where you can choose whether each ingredient list is involved in the recipe or not.
