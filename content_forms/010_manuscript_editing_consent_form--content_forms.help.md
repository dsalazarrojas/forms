# Manuscript Editing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manuscript Editing Consent Form is designed to capture information about a manuscript's editing process and ensure that the author understands and consents to the level of editing involvement, manuscript status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a manuscript from the list provided, and choose the option that best describes its current state.
2. Fill in the author's information, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Choose the manuscript type to accurately reflect its nature and scope.
4. Indicate the author's consent level for editing involvement, from no involvement to significant involvement.
5. Select the manuscript status to update its current progress.
6. Choose the level of editor involvement, from no involvement to significant involvement.
7. Enter the submission and review dates for the manuscript.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manuscript Editing Consent Form (select_one)** (`manuscript_editing_consent_form`, not required): Select the correct option that describes the manuscript's editing consent.
* **Author Information (text)** (`author_information`, not required): Enter the author's name and any other relevant information.
* **Manuscript Type (select_one)** (`manuscript_type`, not required): Choose the correct option that describes the manuscript's type, such as Article, Book, Thesis, or Research Report.
* **Author Consent (select_multiple)** (`author_consent`, not required): Select the level of editing consent from Explicit, Implied, or other options that apply to the author.
* **Manuscript Details (text)** (`manuscript_details`, not required): Enter any additional details about the manuscript.
* **Project Details (text)** (`project_details`, not required): Enter any project details related to the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Status (select_one)** (`manuscript_status`, not required): Choose the correct option that describes the manuscript's current status, such as In progress, Published, Pending, or Completed.
* **Editor Involvement (select_one)** (`editor_involvement`, not required): Choose the level of editor involvement, from no involvement to significant involvement.
* **Submission Date (date)** (`submission_date`, not required): Enter the date the manuscript was submitted.
* **Review Date (date)** (`review_date`, not required): Enter the date the manuscript was reviewed.

## Tips

* Ensure that you select the correct manuscript from the list provided.
* Be accurate when filling out the author's information and manuscript details.
* Choose the correct manuscript type and status to reflect the manuscript's true nature and progress.
* Select the correct level of editing involvement and consent from the author.
* Enter the correct submission and review dates for the manuscript.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
