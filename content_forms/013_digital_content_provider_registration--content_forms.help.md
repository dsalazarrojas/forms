# Digital Content Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a digital content provider, gathering essential information for verification and contact.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the requested information, starting with the first page.
2. Enter your **Creator Name** in the text field. This is the name that will be associated with your digital content.
3. Enter your **Email** in the text field. This will be your primary contact address.
4. If you have a **Portfolio URL**, enter it in the text field. This will be a reference to your digital content collection.
5. You may upload a **Portfolio Sample** and an **Alternate Portfolio Sample** in separate text fields. These are examples of your digital content.
6. If you provide **Digital Content**, select the type of content from the dropdown list (e.g., **Video, Audio, Image, Other**).
7. If your content has a **Content Length**, enter the length in the number field.
8. Enter a **Content Description** in the text field. This should describe your digital content.
9. You may provide an **Alternate Content Sample** in a separate text field.
10. Enter your **Contact Name**, which is the name of the person you would like to be contacted.
11. Enter your **Contact Email**, which is your email address for contact purposes.
12. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** in the text field. This is your phone number for direct contact.
13. Enter your **Contact Address** in the text field. This should be the physical address you would like to be associated with your digital content.
14. If you have any other contact information, you may enter it in the **Other Contact Info** field.
15. Finally, enter any social media information related to your contact in the **Social Media** text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name you would like to be associated with your digital content.
* **Email** (`creator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, optional): If you have a portfolio URL, enter it here.
* **Portfolio Sample** (`portfolio_sample`, `text`, optional): Upload a sample of your digital content.
* **Alternate Portfolio Sample** (`portfolio_sample_alt`, `text`, optional): Upload an alternative sample of your digital content.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of digital content you are creating (e.g., **Video, Audio, Image, Other**).
* **Content Length** (`content_length`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of your digital content.
* **Content Description** (`content_description`, `text`, optional): Describe your digital content.
* **Alternate Content Sample** (`content_sample_alt`, `text`, optional): Upload an alternative sample of your digital content.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for direct contact.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the physical address you would like to be associated with your digital content.
* **Social Media** (`contact_social_media`, `text`, optional): Enter any social media information related to your contact.
* **Other Contact Info** (`contact_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other contact information.
