# Illustrated Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit illustrated stories for review and potential publication. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to help the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
2. Be concise and keep your responses under 200 words.
3. Select the correct submission type and permission settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Story Submission**: Enter a brief title for your illustrated story.
* **Artwork**: Upload or describe your illustrated artwork.
* **Contributor Name**: Enter your name as the creator or contributor of the story.
* **Description**: Describe the artwork in 200 words or less.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number for contact purposes.
* **Permission**: Choose whether you grant permission for your work to be published.
* **Submission Type**: Select the type of submission you are making.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the submission was made.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time the submission was made.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your submission.
* **Reviewer**: Choose whether you want the reviewer to be assigned to review your submission.

Please fill out the form carefully, and do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
