# share_story_contest_form--content_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to share their story with us. It requires users to provide some personal contact information and agree to our terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Share Your Story" field to enter a brief description of your story.
2. In "What Does It Mean To You?", you can optionally provide any additional context or meaning behind your story.
3. In "Why Share Your Story?", you can optionally provide reasons why you'd like to share your story with us.
4. To upload your story, simply type it in the "Upload Your Story" field.
5. In "Share Story Submitter", enter your name and contact information.
6. Enter your email address in the "Share Story Email" field.
7. Choose whether you agree to our terms of service in the "Share Story Agree" field.
8. Confirm your agreement to our terms of service in the "Share Story Terms Agree" field.
9. Check whether you agree to our terms of service in the "Share Story Terms of Service" field.
10. Confirm your agreement to our data usage in "Share Story Data Agree" field.
11. Confirm your agreement to our IP usage in "Share Story Our IP Agree" field.
12. Agree to our agreement process in "Share Story Agreement" field.
13. Confirm your agreement to our submitter agreement in "Share Story Submitter Agreement" field.
14. Confirm your agreement to our terms of service in "Share Story Our Terms Agree" field.
15. Confirm your agreement to our terms of service in "Share Story Our Terms of Service" field.
15. Choose whether you agree to our privacy policy in "Share Story Privacy" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Share Your Story** (`form_1`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your story.
* **What Does It Mean To You?** (`form_2`, `text`, optional): You can optionally provide any additional context or meaning behind your story.
* **Why Share Your Story?** (`form_3`, `text`, optional): You can optionally provide reasons why you'd like to share your story with us.
* **Upload Your Story** (`form_5`, `text`, optional): Simply type in your story here.
* **Share Story Submitter** (`form_6`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Share Story Email** (`form_7`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Share Story Agree** (`form_9`, `select_one`, required): Agree to our terms of service.
* **Share Story Terms Agree** (`form_11`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our terms of service.
* **Share Story Terms of Service** (`form_13`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our terms of service.
* **Share Story Data Agree** (`form_19`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our data usage.
* **Share Story Our IP Agree** (`form_22`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our IP usage.
* **Share Story Agreement** (`form_23`, `select_multiple`, required): Agree to our agreement process.
* **Share Story Submitter Agreement** (`form_24`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our submitter agreement.
* **Share Story Terms Agreement** (`form_25`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our terms of service.
* **Share Story Privacy** (`form_17`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you agree to our privacy policy.

## Tips
* Make sure to agree to our terms of service and other conditions before submitting your story.
* Ensure that your email address is correct and valid.
* Confirm your agreement to all conditions before submitting your story.
