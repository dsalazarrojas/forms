# TV Series Catalog Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The TV Series Catalog Form is a structured document used to collect metadata and details about TV series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Provide the first name of the person or organization associated with the TV series.
2. Enter the last name of the person or organization associated with the TV series (if applicable).
3. Enter the title of the TV series.
4. Input the release date of the TV series in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
5. Select the genre of the TV series from the provided options.
6. Enter the number of seasons in the series (if applicable).
7. Enter the number of episodes in the series (if applicable).
8. Enter the rating of the TV series (if applicable).
9. Select the status of the TV series from the provided options.
10. Enter a detailed description of the TV series.

16. Enter the name of the creator associated with the TV series (if applicable).

18. Enter the names of the crew members associated with the TV series (if applicable).
19. Enter any awards won by the TV series.
20. Enter any additional notes about the TV series.
21. Select any tags associated with the TV series.
