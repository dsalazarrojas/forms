# website_content_submission_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect and store information about website content submissions. It captures relevant details from both the submitter and the reviewer, making it easier to track and manage submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Email: Enter the submitter's email address.
	* Phone: Enter the submitter's phone number.
	* Submission Date: Select the date the content was submitted.
	* Status: Choose the current status of the submission (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
	* Submitter Name: Enter the name of the submitter.
	* Submitter Address: Enter the submitter's address.
	* Submitter Company: Enter the submitter's company name.
	* Submitter Job Title: Enter the submitter's job title.
	* Submitter Agreement: Choose whether the submitter has agreed to the submission terms.
	* Type: Select the type of content submitted (Text, Image, or Video).
2. Fill in any additional fields as applicable:
	* Website: Enter the website associated with the submission (if applicable).
	* Review Date: Select the date the content was reviewed.
	* Review Status: Choose the current status of the review (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
	* Publish Date: Select the date the content was published (if applicable).
	* Reviewer: Enter any notes from the reviewer.
	* Comments: Enter any comments from the submitter or reviewer.
	* Content: Enter a description of the content submitted.
	* Content URL: Enter the URL of the content submitted (if applicable).
