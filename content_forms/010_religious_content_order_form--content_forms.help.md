# Religious Content Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to order religious content. It is intended for users who need to provide their personal or organizational details to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  On the first page, enter your name, email address, and the name of your organization.
2.  On the second page, select your preferred method of delivery (digital, physical, or both) and choose your payment terms (net 30, 60, or pay in full).
3.  Optionally, you can provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your order.

    *   **Payment Terms** (`form_second_page_payment_terms`, select multiple, Required): Choose your payment terms: net 30, 60, or pay in full.
    *   **Additional Information** (`form_second_page_additional_info`, text, Optional): You can provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your order.
