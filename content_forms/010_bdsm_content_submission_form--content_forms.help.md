# BDSM Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BDSM Content Submission Form is used to collect information from creators about their content for review and publication on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and pen name (if applicable) in the "Creator Name or Pen Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for submission confirmation and further communication.
3. Provide a brief description of yourself in the "Creator Bio or Background" field (optional).
4. Enter the title of your submission in the "Content Title" field.
5. Select the type of content you are submitting (e.g., written story, poetry, educational article, etc.) in the "Content Type" field.
6. Provide a brief description of your content in the "Content Description" field.
7. If applicable, enter the word count or duration of your content in the "Content Length" field.
8. Describe the main themes or topics covered in your content in the "Themes and Topics Covered" field.
9. Select the intended audience level of your content (e.g., beginners, intermediate, advanced, all levels) in the "Content Audience Level" field.
10. Describe the intensity or explicit content level in the "Content Rating or Intensity Level" field.
11. Confirm whether your content emphasizes consent and safety in the "Consent and Safety" field.
12. Indicate whether this has been previously published (e.g., no, yes with permission, yes elsewhere) in the "Has this been previously published?" field.
13. Choose a date for your submission in the "Submission Date" field.
14. Confirm that you grant rights for this content to be published in the "I grant rights for this content to be published" field.
15. Confirm that you have the right to submit this content in the "I have the right to submit this content" field.
16. Provide any additional notes or special requests in the "Additional Notes or Special Requests" field (optional).
