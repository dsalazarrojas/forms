# personal_financial_statement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about personal financial status, assets, and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your current financial status.
2. Select all assets you own, such as Cash, Investments, Real estate, Vehicles, and Other.
3. If you have any liabilities, such as Credit card debt, Mortgages, Loans, Alimony, or Other, select them.
4. Enter the value of your assets and liabilities.
5. Specify the currency of your assets.
6. Enter the date of your financial statement.
7. Select the financial statement period.
8. Enter the total value of your financial statement.
9. Provide any additional notes about your financial status, assets, and liabilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one): Select your current financial status from the options provided: Current, Potential, or Other.
* **Financial Status Note** (`financial_status_note`, note): Provide any additional notes about your financial status.
* **Assets** (`assets`, select_multiple): Select all the assets you own, such as Cash, Investments, Real estate, Vehicles, and Other.
* **Assets Value** (`assets_value`, number): Enter the value of your assets.
* **Asset Currency** (`assets_currency`, text): Enter the currency of your assets.
* **Assets Notes** (`assets_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your assets.
* **Liabilities** (`liabilities`, select_multiple): Select all the liabilities you have, such as Credit card debt, Mortgages, Loans, Alimony, or Other.
* **Liabilities Value** (`liabilities_value`, number): Enter the value of your liabilities.
* **Liabilities Notes** (`liabilities_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your liabilities.
* **Other Assets** (`other_assets`, select_multiple): Select any other assets you own.
* **Other Liabilities** (`other_liabilities`, select_multiple): Select any other liabilities you have.
* **Other Assets Notes** (`other_assets_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your other assets.
* **Other Liabilities Notes** (`other_liabilities_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your other liabilities.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your financial status, assets, and liabilities.
* **Other** (`other`, text): Enter any other information that is relevant to your financial statement.
* **Asset Currency Other** (`asset_currency_other`, text): Enter the currency of your other assets.
* **Financial Statement Other** (`financial_statement_other`, text): Enter any other information that is relevant to your financial statement.
* **Financial Statement Other Notes** (`financial_statement_other_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your financial statement.
* **Financial Statement Other Assets Notes** (`financial_statement_other_assets_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your other assets.
* **Financial Statement Other Liabilities Notes** (`financial_statement_other_liabilities_notes`, note): Provide any additional notes about your other liabilities.
* **Financial Statement Date** (`financial_statement_date`, date): Enter the date of your financial statement.
* **Financial Statement Period** (`financial_statement_period`, text): Select the financial statement period.
* **Financial Statement Total Value** (`financial_statement_total_value`, number): Enter the total value of your financial statement.
