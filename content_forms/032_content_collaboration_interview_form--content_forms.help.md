# Content Collaboration Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Collaboration Interview Form is designed to gather insights and ratings from a content collaboration interview. It helps assess a candidate's collaboration skills, content creation expertise, adaptability, and overall fit for a content collaboration role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the candidate's details, including their full name and identification number.
2. Move on to the interview and collaboration insights section, where you can rate the candidate's collaboration skills, content creation expertise, and adaptability.
3. Share specific interview questions and answers, such as describing a time you collaborated on a complex content project or how you handle feedback from collaborators.
4. In the evaluator details section, fill in your full name and identification number.
5. Rate your overall recommendation for the candidate.
6. Confirm that the form is completed accurately.
7. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name (Required)**: The candidate's full name as per their official identification or records.
* **Candidate Identification Number (Required)**: The candidate's unique identification number as per their official records.
* **Collaboration Skills Rating (Required)**: Rate the candidate's collaboration skills from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Content Creation Expertise Rating (Required)**: Rate the candidate's content creation expertise from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Adaptability and Problem-Solving Rating (Required)**: Rate the candidate's adaptability and problem-solving skills from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Specific Interview Question 1 (Required)**: Answer the first specific interview question, such as "Describe a time you collaborated on a complex content project."
* **Specific Interview Question 2 (Required)**: Answer the second specific interview question, such as "How do you handle feedback from collaborators?"
* **Evaluator Full Name (Required)**: Your full name as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Identification Number (Required)**: Your unique identification number as per your official records.
* **Interview Date (Required)**: The date of the interview.
* **Evaluator Recommendation (Required)**: Rate your overall recommendation for the candidate as Highly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Re-evaluate.
* **Interview Form Completed (Required)**: Confirm that the form is completed accurately.
* **Signature Confirmation (Required)**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
