# Funeral Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help funeral homes and their staff plan and organize the funeral process. It covers essential tasks, such as collecting contact information, coordinating services with providers, and tracking details related to the funeral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the contact information page, which helps us get in touch with you for any questions or concerns.
2. Provide information about your service provider, such as the name and contact details of the funeral home or organization handling the funeral arrangements.
3. Specify the services required for the funeral, including any specific details or instructions for the funeral director.
4. Enter information about the deceased, including their name, date of death, and any relevant background details.
5. Arrange for the burial site, selecting an option from the provided list or entering a custom choice.
6. Plan for the funeral home, specifying the type of arrangements needed and any other details.
7. Determine the burial site arrangements, specifying the location, date, and time.
8. Complete the burial site page 2, which might be used to capture any additional information or arrangements.
9. Specify the burial and cremation options, if applicable.
10. Create a death notice, which is sent out to family and friends.
11. Arrange for the headstone, specifying the inscription and any other details.
