# Client Asset Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Asset Upload Form is a tool used by clients to upload their assets, such as images, documents, and videos, for internal project management and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, clients must provide the necessary information about themselves and the project they are working on.
*   Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary rights to upload the assets and authorize their use for the specified project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Your legal first name. (Required)
*   **Last Name**: Your legal last name. (Required)
*   **Email Address**: We will confirm receipt via email. (Required)
*   **Phone Number**: For urgent questions about your assets. (Required)
*   **Project Name**: Name or reference for this project. (Required)
*   **Project Type**: Select the type of project (e.g., Website Design, Marketing Campaign). (Required)
*   **Asset Category**: Select the type of asset you are uploading (e.g., Images and Photos, Documents and PDFs). (Required)
*   **Asset Description**: Briefly describe what you are uploading. (Required)
*   **Approximate Number of Files**: Estimated count of files. (Required)
*   **Total File Size Estimate**: Select the approximate combined size of the files. (Required)
*   **Upload Purpose**: Briefly describe why you are submitting these assets. (Required)
*   **Do You Have Rights to These Assets?**: Confirm that you own or have permission to use the assets. (Required)
*   **Third Party Credits Required**: If required, list any necessary attributions. (Optional)
*   **Project Deadline**: When do you need this completed? (Required)
*   **Priority Level**: Select the urgency level of the request (e.g., Low - No Rush, Medium - Within 2 Weeks). (Required)
*   **Special Instructions**: Any specific requirements or notes. (Optional)
*   **Team Members to CC**: Email addresses of colleagues to include. (Optional)
*   **I Authorize the Use of These Assets for the Specified Project**: Confirm your authorization. (Required)
*   **Submission Date**: The date you are uploading the assets. (Required)

Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely to facilitate a smooth upload process.
