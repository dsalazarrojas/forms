# resolution_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The resolution submission form is a tool used by members to submit resolutions to the organization. It is designed to collect and store information about the resolutions submitted, including the member's name, resolution title, resolution content, submission date, submission status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Member Name** (Member Name, text, required): Enter the member's name who submitted the resolution.
2. Complete the **Resolution Title** (Resolution Title, text, required): Enter a brief title for the resolution.
3. Enter the **Resolution Content** (Resolution Content, text, required): A detailed description of the resolution.
4. Select the **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): The date when the resolution was submitted.
5. Select the **Submission Status** (Submission Status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the resolution from the dropdown list (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
6. You can optionally fill in **Email** (Email, email, optional): The member's email.
7. You can optionally fill in **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): The member's phone number.
8. Select the **Submission Status 2** (Submission Status 2, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the second co-author from the dropdown list (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
9. You can optionally fill in **Co-Author Name** (Co-Author Name, text, optional): The name of the second co-author.
10. You can optionally fill in **Co-Author Email** (Co-Author Email, email, optional): The email of the second co-author.
11. You can optionally fill in **Co-Author Phone** (Co-Author Phone, text, optional): The phone number of the second co-author.
12. You can optionally fill in **Co-Author Submission Date** (Co-Author Submission Date, date, optional): The submission date of the second co-author.
