# Anthology Submission Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect submission information from authors for the anthology. It ensures that authors provide their personal details, submission title, and type of work, allowing the editorial process to proceed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in your full name as it should appear in the anthology.
2. Enter your contact email address for submission correspondence.
3. Provide a title for your submission.
4. Select the type of work you are submitting (e.g., Poem, Short story, Essay, Artwork, or Other).
5. If required, confirm your age if you are under 18 years old.
6. Upload a link to your manuscript or paste a text link.
7. Add any additional comments or cover letter if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Author full name** (`Author Name`, `text`, required/optional): Your name as it should appear in the anthology.
* **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Used for submission correspondence and communication with the editorial team.
* **Submission title** (`Submission Title`, `text`, required/optional): Title of the piece being submitted.
* **Type of submission** (`Type of submission`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the category that best suits your submission (e.g., Poem, Short story, Essay, Artwork, or Other).
* **Age confirmation** (`Confirm age`, `select_one`, optional/required): Confirm your age if it's required by submission guidelines.
* **Cover letter or notes** (`Cover letter or notes`, `text`, optional/optional): Add an optional message to the editors with any additional information or thoughts.
* **Upload file or paste text link** (`Upload file or paste text link`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a link to your manuscript or paste the text if it's easier for you.
