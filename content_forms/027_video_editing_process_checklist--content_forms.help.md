# Video Editing Process Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track and document the video editing process. It guides you through the different steps involved in reviewing and editing video content, from reviewing the initial content to finalizing the edited version.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the video content and answer the questions on page 2 (review_steps) to determine what steps are needed to review before editing.
2. Enter the status for each step in the review_steps section.
3. Record the date and time when reviewing each step.
4. Perform the necessary editing tasks and answer the questions on page 6 (edit_steps) to determine what steps are needed to perform before editing.
5. Enter the status for each step in edit_steps section.
6. Record the date and time when editing each step.
7. Perform the necessary approval tasks and answer the questions on page 10 (approval_steps) to determine what steps are needed to perform for approval.
8. Enter the status for each step in approval_steps section.
9. Record the date and time when approving each step.
10. Finalize the edited video and answer the questions on page 14 (final_steps) to determine what steps are needed to perform before finalizing.
11. Enter the status for each step in final_steps section.
12. Record the date and time when finalizing each step.
13. Enter the final status and date for each deliverable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **review_steps** (`review_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field asks you to determine what steps are needed to review before editing. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' to indicate the steps that require review.
- **status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Enter the status for each step you reviewed. You can choose from 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started' to indicate the status.
- **date** (`review_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Record the date when you reviewed each step.
- **time** (`review_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Record the time when you reviewed each step.
- **edit_steps** (`edit_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field asks you to determine what steps are needed to perform before editing. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' to indicate the steps that require editing.
- **edit_status** (`edit_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Enter the status for each step you edited. You can choose from 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started' to indicate the status.
- **date** (`edit_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Record the date when you edited each step.
- **time** (`edit_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Record the time when you edited each step.
- **approval_steps** (`approval_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field asks you to determine what steps are needed to perform for approval. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' to indicate the steps that require approval.
- **approval_status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Enter the status for each step you approved. You can choose from 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started' to indicate the status.
- **date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Record the date when you approved each step.
- **time** (`approval_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Record the time when you approved each step.
- **deliverable** (`deliverable`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field asks you to determine what deliverables you need to perform on. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' to indicate the deliverables.
- **status** (`deliverable_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Enter the status for each deliverable you performed. You can choose from 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started' to indicate the status.
- **date** (`deliverable_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Record the date when you delivered each step.
- **time** (`deliverable_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Record the time when you delivered each step.
- **review_notes** (`review_notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any notes you have regarding the review process.
- **finalize_notes** (`finalize_notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any notes you have regarding the finalization process.
- **review_comment** (`review_comment`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any comments you have regarding the review process.
- **finalize_comment** (`finalize_comment`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any comments you have regarding the finalization process.
- **final_steps** (`final_steps`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field asks you to determine what steps are needed to perform before finalizing. You can choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' to indicate the steps that require finalization.
- **finalize_status** (`finalize_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Enter the status for each step you finalized. You can choose from 'Completed', 'In Progress', or 'Not Started' to indicate the status.
- **date** (`finalize_date`, `date`, required: `false`): Record the date when you finalized each step.
- **time** (`finalize_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Record the time when you finalized each step.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to review each step carefully and document the status, date, and time accurately.
*   Make sure to perform the necessary steps before editing, approving, or finalizing.
*   Review and finalize each step before proceeding to the next.
*   Use the notes and comments fields to document any relevant information about the review or finalization process.
*   Enter the correct status, date, and time for each step.
*   Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM:SS).
*   Be accurate and consistent in your documentation.
*   Refer to the help guide if you are unsure about any field or section.
