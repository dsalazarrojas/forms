# Business Communication Phrase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from you about a business communication phrase that meets your specific requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Request Information** (`request_information`): This section is a note field where you can provide any additional information about your request.
2. **Requester Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your name so we can address the request with the correct person.
3. **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Provide your business email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
4. **Department** (`requester_department`): Specify your department or team so we can understand your request context.
5. **Request Date** (`request_date`): Enter the date when you need the phrase.
6. **Communication Context** (`communication_context`): This section is a note field where you can provide any additional context about your request.
7. **Type of Communication** (`communication_type`): Select the type of communication (e.g., Email, Meeting, Presentation, etc.).
8. **Purpose of Communication** (`communication_purpose`): Choose the main objective of the communication (e.g., Inform, Persuade, Apologize, etc.).
9. **Target Audience** (`target_audience`): Select who will receive the communication (e.g., Internal Employees, Clients/Customers, etc.).
10. **Tone Preference** (`tone_preference`): Choose the desired tone of the communication (e.g., Professional Formal, Professional Casual, etc.).
11. **Current Wording or Context** (`current_wording`): Provide a brief description of what needs to be communicated.
12. **Key Message to Convey** (`key_message`): Enter the core message or takeaway of the communication.
13. **Target Language** (`target_language`): Select the language for the phrase (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
14. **Preferred Length** (`phrase_length`): Choose the desired length of the phrase (e.g., Very Short, Short, Medium, Long, etc.).
15. **Style Guide or Brand Voice** (`style_guide`): If necessary, provide any specific branding guidelines.
16. **Usage Context** (`usage_context`): This section is a note field where you can provide any additional context about your request.
17. **Cultural or Regional Sensitivity** (`cultural_sensitivity`): If necessary, provide any cultural considerations for the communication.
18. **Phrases or Terms to Avoid** (`avoid_phrases`): If necessary, provide any phrases or terms that should be avoided.
19. **Elements to Include** (`include_elements`): If necessary, provide any specific points or information to include in the phrase.
20. **Include Call to Action** (`call_to_action`): Check if you want a call to action included in the phrase.
21. **Preferred Outcome** (`preferred_outcome`): If necessary, specify the desired outcome from the recipient.
22. **Needed By Date** (`deadline`): Enter the date by which you need the phrase.
23. **Additional Notes or Context** (`additional_notes`): Provide any other relevant information about your request.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter your name and email address to ensure proper communication.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the purpose and key message of the communication.
* If you have any specific branding guidelines, please provide them in the Style Guide or Brand Voice field.
* If you have any cultural considerations, please provide them in the Cultural or Regional Sensitivity field.
