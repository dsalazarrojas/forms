# Infographic Content Collection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information and project details from users. The goal is to gather data for further analysis and to ensure that the collected content aligns with our project's requirements and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address to ensure you receive further updates and communications from us.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
5. Enter your company or institution name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your project name in the "Project Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Company** (text, required/optional): Enter your company or institution name for context.
* **Project Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your project name to help us understand the scope of your work.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us get the most out of your data.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us, and we'll be happy to assist you.
* You can save and resume your progress by clicking the "Save" button. You can also clear the form and restart if needed.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
