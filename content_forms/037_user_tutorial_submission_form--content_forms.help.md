# User Tutorial Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user tutorials from users. It's a simple and structured way to gather information about user tutorials, including name, email address, preferred category, favorite tags, preferred date, preferred time, and any additional notes. Please use this form to submit your user tutorial and help us improve our product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and provide as much information as you feel is relevant. The form is divided into the following sections:

*   **Name**: Enter your name in this field. This will help us identify you as the author of the tutorial.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your email address in this format: `username@example.com`. This will be used to contact you in case of any questions or concerns.
*   **Category**: Select your preferred category from the dropdown list. This will help us understand the topic of your tutorial.
*   **Tags**: Select one or more of your favorite tags from the dropdown list. These will help us categorize and search for your tutorial.
*   **Date**: Enter your preferred date for the tutorial. Please use the standard date format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **Time**: Enter your preferred time for the tutorial. Please use the 24-hour clock format `HH:MM`.
*   **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments you have about your tutorial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **What is your name?** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the author of the tutorial.
*   **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in the format `username@example.com`. This will be used to contact you in case of any questions or concerns.
*   **What is your favorite category?** (`category`, select_one, required): Select one of the three available categories to help us understand the topic of your tutorial.
*   **What are your favorite tags?** (`tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the three available tags to help us categorize and search for your tutorial.
*   **What is your preferred date?** (`date`, date, optional): Enter your preferred date for the tutorial. Please use the standard date format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **What time do you prefer?** (`time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred time for the tutorial. Please use the 24-hour clock format `HH:MM`.
*   **Do you have any additional notes?** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you have about your tutorial.

## Tips
*   Please ensure your submission is accurate and complete before proceeding.
*   You can only submit this form once a day.
*   We appreciate your time and effort in providing a high-quality tutorial.
