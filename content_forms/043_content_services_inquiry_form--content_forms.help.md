# Content Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Services Inquiry Form is a tool for users to provide information about their content needs and preferences. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide a clear understanding of your requirements and goals
* Allow us to tailor our response to your specific needs
* Get in touch with you to discuss further

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out your contact information

* Enter your name in the Contact Name field (required)
* Enter your email address in the Contact Email field (required)
* Enter your phone number in the Contact Phone field (required)

### Step 2: Provide your company details

* Enter your company's name in the Company Name field (required)
* Enter your company's website URL in the Company Website field (optional)

### Step 3: Select your industry and company size

* Select your industry from the dropdown menu (required)
* Select your company size from the dropdown menu (required)

### Step 4: Choose your services and timeline

* Select the services you are interested in (required)
* Choose your desired timeline for starting your content project (required)

### Step 5: Provide your goals and challenges

* Describe your primary goal (required)
* Describe your current content challenges (required)
* Identify your target audience (required)

### Step 6: Provide additional information (optional)

* If you have worked with content agencies before, select the option that applies to you (required)
* If you have any questions or concerns, describe them in the Questions field (optional)
* If you have a referral source, provide their name or contact information (optional)
* If you have any additional comments or preferences, enter them in the Additional Comments field (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Enter your full name (required)
* **Contact Email**: Enter your best email address for us to contact you (required)
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you (required)
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name (required)
* **Company Website**: Enter your company's website URL (optional)
* **Industry**: Select your industry from the dropdown menu (required)
* **Company Size**: Select your company size from the dropdown menu (required)
* **Services Interested In**: Select the services you are interested in (required)
* **Inquiry Date**: Enter the date of your inquiry (required)
* **Primary Goal**: Select your primary goal (required)
* **Current Challenges**: Describe your current content challenges (required)
* **Target Audience**: Describe your target audience (required)
* **Current Content Efforts**: Describe your current content marketing efforts (optional)
* **Previous Agencies**: If you have worked with content agencies before, select the option that applies to you (required)
* **What Did not Work Well**: If you have previously worked with a content agency, describe what did not work well (optional)
* **Monthly Budget**: Select your content marketing budget (required)
* **Timeline**: Choose your desired timeline for starting your content project (required)
* **Decision Makers**: If there are other people involved in the decision-making process, describe them (optional)
* **Main Competitors**: If you have main competitors, describe them (optional)
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Select how you found our agency (required)
* **Referral Source**: If you were referred to us, provide their name or contact information (optional)
* **Questions**: If you have any questions or concerns, describe them (optional)
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact (required)
* **Best Time to Contact**: Select the best time to contact you (optional)
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or preferences (optional)
