# Player Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Profile Form is a tool designed to collect essential information about players in a school setting. This form will help gather details such as coach affiliation, emergency contacts, parental contact information, and player's physical characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the correct option for "Are you a coach at a school?" This field is required and determines your role in the player's profile.
2. If you are a coach at a school, skip to "Do you need physical information of players?" and proceed to the next page.
3. If you are not a coach at a school, answer the "In case of emergency situations" field with a brief description of any emergency situations that may arise.
4. Enter the first parent's phone number in the "Parent phone number 1" field and the second parent's phone number in the "Parent phone number 2" field. Both fields are optional.
5. If required, enter the physical measurement 1 and 2 in the respective fields.
6. Answer "Do you need physical information of players?" with "True" or "False" depending on your needs.
7. If you selected "True" in the previous step, enter a description of the physical measures in the "Player physical measures" field.
8. Finally, provide a description of how to arrange clothes for the players in the "In order to arrange clothes for players" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Are you a coach at a school? (coach_at_school)**: Answer "True" if you are a coach at a school; otherwise, answer "False".
* **In case of emergency situations**: Enter a brief description of any emergency situations that may arise, if you are not a coach at a school.
* **Parent phone number 1 (parent_phone_number1)**: Enter the first parent's phone number.
* **Parent phone number 2 (parent_phone_number2)**: Enter the second parent's phone number, if available.
* **Physical measurement 1 (physical_measurement1)**: Enter the first physical measurement, if required.
* **Physical measurement 2 (physical_measurement2)**: Enter the second physical measurement, if required.
* **Do you need physical information of players? (need_physical_info)**: Answer "True" if you need physical information; otherwise, answer "False".
* **Player physical measures (player_physical_measures)**: Enter a description of the physical measures, if required.
* **In order to arrange clothes for players (clothes_arrange)**: Enter a description of how to arrange clothes for the players.
