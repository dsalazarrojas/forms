# Content Creation Permission Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Content Creation Permission Request Form is used to request permission for the creation of content within the organization. This form ensures that all necessary permissions and approvals are in place before proceeding with content creation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, identification number, and email address.
2. Provide a description of the content you wish to create, including its purpose and planned usage.
3. Select the channels through which the content will be distributed (e.g., social media, email, website, SEO, paid search, referral, or other).
4. Confirm that you have secured all necessary copyright permissions.
5. Select your current security clearance level.
6. Confirm whether permission has been granted or is pending.
7. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
8. Choose the date of the permission request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Details** (`section_requester_info`): 
  * **Requester Full Name** (`requester_name`): Enter your full name.
  * **Requester Identification Number** (`requester_id`): Enter your identification number.
  * **Email Address** (`requester_email`): Enter your email address.
* **-- Permission Request Details --** (`section_permission_request`): 
  * **Description of the Content to be Created** (`content_description`): Describe the content you wish to create.
  * **Justification for Content Creation** (`usage_justification`): Explain the purpose and planned usage of the content.
  * **Planned Content Distribution Channels** (`planned_distribution_channels`): Select all applicable channels for distributing the content.
* **-- Security and Verification --** (`section_security_verification`): 
  * **Your Current Security Clearance Level** (`security_clearance_level`): Select your current security clearance level.
  * **Permission Granted?** (`permission_granted`): Confirm whether permission has been granted or is pending.
* **-- Confirmation --** (`section_confirmation`): 
  * **Permission Request Completed?** (`permission_request_completed`): Confirm whether the request is complete.
  * **I confirm that the information provided is accurate** (`signature_confirmation`): Choose 'I Agree' to confirm accuracy.
* **Date of Permission Request** (`date_of_request`): Enter the date of the request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* Use the correct channels for distributing the content.
* Secure all necessary copyright permissions before proceeding.
* Double-check your current security clearance level.
* Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* Enter the correct date of the permission request.
