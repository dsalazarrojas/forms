# Share A Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Share A Testimonial Form is a tool used to collect customer feedback and success stories about our products and services. This form is designed to showcase the impact and effectiveness of our offerings, and to build trust and credibility with our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required fields.
2. Enter a name for the testimonial.
3. Share your story or feedback about the product/service.
4. Provide your email address or phone number so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Testimonial Name** (`testimonial_name`, text, optional): Enter a name to be associated with your testimonial. This will be displayed publicly, so feel free to use your name, nickname, or a pseudonym.
* **Testimonial Content** (`testimonial_content`, text, optional): Share your story or feedback about the product/service. Be as detailed or brief as you like, but make sure it's genuine and relevant.
* **Testimonial Email** (`testimonial_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Testimonial Phone** (`testimonial_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you. This is an alternative contact method, and your number will not be displayed publicly.
