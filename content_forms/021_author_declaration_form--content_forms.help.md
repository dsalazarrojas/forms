# Author Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Author Declaration Form is used to declare author involvement in a project or publication. Fill this form accurately to ensure that your declaration is legitimate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1.  **Fill in the declaration date** on page 1.
2.  **Enter the project title** on page 2.
3.  **Select the project type** from the options provided.
4.  **Fill in the primary author's details** on pages 3-5, including name, affiliation, email, contribution, and percentage of contribution.
5.  **Add any additional authors** (optional), including their details on pages 6-8.
6.  **Enter the total number of authors** on page 9.
7.  **Select the corresponding author** on page 10.
8.  **Enter the corresponding author's email** on page 11.
9.  **List funding sources** (if any) on page 12.
10. **Declare any conflicts of interest** on page 13.
11. **List people or organizations to acknowledge** on page 14.
12. **Check the authorship agreement** on page 15.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Declaration Date** (Page 1, `declaration_date`): Enter the date of this declaration.
*   **Project Title** (Page 2, `project_title`): Enter the full title of the project or publication.
*   **Project Type** (Page 2, `project_type`): Select the type of project or publication from the options provided.
*   **Primary Author Name** (Page 3, `author_one_name`): Enter the full name of the primary author.
*   **Primary Author Affiliation** (Page 4, `author_one_affiliation`): Enter the institution or organization of the primary author.
*   **Primary Author Email** (Page 5, `author_one_email`): Enter the email of the primary author.
*   **Primary Author Contribution** (Page 5, `author_one_contribution`): Select the contributions of the primary author from the options provided.
*   **Percentage of Contribution - Author 1** (Page 5, `author_one_percentage`): Enter the estimated percentage of contribution of the primary author.
*   **Second Author Name** (Page 6, `author_two_name`): Enter the full name of the second author (if applicable).
*   **Second Author Affiliation** (Page 7, `author_two_affiliation`): Enter the institution or organization of the second author (if applicable).
*   **Second Author Email** (Page 8, `author_two_email`): Enter the email of the second author (if applicable).
*   **Second Author Contribution** (Page 8, `author_two_contribution`): Select the contributions of the second author from the options provided (if applicable).
*   **Percentage of Contribution - Author 2** (Page 8, `author_two_percentage`): Enter the estimated percentage of contribution of the second author (if applicable).
*   **Total Number of Authors** (Page 9, `total_authors`): Enter the total number of authors.
*   **Corresponding Author Name** (Page 10, `corresponding_author`): Select the point of contact.
*   **Corresponding Author Email** (Page 11, `corresponding_author_email`): Enter the email for correspondence.
*   **Funding Sources** (Page 12, `funding_sources`): List any grants, sponsors, or funding bodies.
*   **Conflicts of Interest** (Page 13, `conflict_of_interest`): Declare any financial or personal interests.
*   **Acknowledgments** (Page 14, `acknowledgments`): List people or organizations to acknowledge.
*   **All Authors Agree to Submission** (Page 15, `authorship_agreement`): Check that all authors agree to submission.
