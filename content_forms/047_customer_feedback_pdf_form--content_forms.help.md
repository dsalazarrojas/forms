# Customer Feedback PDF Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather customer feedback, allowing us to better understand your experiences and opinions. This form will collect information about your visit, including your date and time of visit, your contact information, your rating, and your comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user input (name or alias).
2. Enter the date of your visit.
3. Enter the time of your visit (optional).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Select your rating of our service from 1 to 5.
6. Provide your comments about your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (`user_input`)
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required): Enter your name or a nickname you'd like to be known by in our records.

### Date (`date`)
* **Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your visit to us.

### Time (`time`)
* **Time** (`time`, optional): If you'd like to provide more specific information about your visit, enter the time of your visit (AM/PM is not required).

### Email (`email`)
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about your feedback.

### Rating (`rating`)
* **Rating** (`select_one`, required): Select one of the following ratings to tell us about your experience:
	+ 1 (Very Dissatisfied)
	+ 2 (Somewhat Dissatisfied)
	+ 3 (Neutral)
	+ 4 (Somewhat Satisfied)
	+ 5 (Very Satisfied)

### Comments (`comments`)
* **Comments** (`text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* We appreciate your time and input.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
