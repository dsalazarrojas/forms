# Branch Recognition Video Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Branch Recognition Video Request form is used to submit a request for a video recognition to be created for an individual or group within the company. The video can be used for various purposes, such as employee recognition, celebration of achievements, or milestone announcements. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide all the necessary information for the production team to create a well-suited video for the occasion.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your information as requested in the form.
2. Choose the purpose of the video from the provided options.
3. Specify the branch and subject of the video.
4. Select the desired video length.
5. Choose the style and music preference for the video.
6. Provide key messages and people to feature in the video.
7. Specify the distribution channels for the video.
8. Attach any relevant files or notes for the production team.
9. Submit the form for review and production.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: This field is required to provide your name and identity for the request.
* **Email Address**: This field is required for video delivery and contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This field is optional and used for additional contact information.
* **Branch Name**: This field is required to specify which branch is being recognized.
* **Video Purpose**: Choose the purpose of the video from the provided options to ensure the production team creates the right content.
* **Video Subject or Focus**: This field is required to specify who or what will be featured in the video.
* **Desired Video Length**: Choose the approximate duration of the video to ensure it fits the desired use case.
* **Video Style Preference**: Select from the provided options to ensure the video style fits the occasion.
* **Music Preference**: Choose the background music style for the video.
* **Key Messages to Convey**: This field is required to provide the main points for the video content.
* **People to Feature**: This field is optional and used to list the names and roles of people to feature in the video.
* **Delivery Due Date**: This field is required to specify when the video is needed.
* **Distribution Channels**: Choose from the provided options where the video will be shared.
* **Attached Files or Resources**: This field is optional and used to attach photos, logos, or scripts for the production team.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: This field is optional and used to note any specific requirements or additional information for the production team.
* **Distribution Channels**: This field is optional and used to specify where the video will be shared.
