# Change Of Address Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change of Address Form Template is used to update an individual's or organization's address in our records. This form is used to submit a change of address request for a customer's address to be updated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Form Date" field with the date you are submitting this form.
2. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the customer's ID number in the "Customer ID Number" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the old address details in the "Old Street Address", "Old City", "Old State", and "Old ZIP Code" fields.
7. Enter the new address details in the "New Street Address", "New City", "New State", and "New ZIP Code" fields.
8. Select the "Address Type" field to indicate the type of new address (e.g. residential, business, etc.).
9. Choose how you would like to be notified of the address change in the "Notification Preference" field.
10. Select the "Effective Date" field to indicate when the address change should take effect.
11. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).
12. Sign your name in the "Customer Signature" field with your full legal name.
13. Enter the date of signing in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Date
* **Form Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full legal name.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter the customer's email address for confirmation.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter the customer's contact phone number.

### Customer ID Number
* **Customer ID Number**: Enter the customer's ID number (if applicable).

### Old Address
* **Old Street Address**: Enter the previous street address.
* **Old City**: Enter the previous city.
* **Old State**: Enter the previous state.
* **Old ZIP Code**: Enter the previous postal code.
* **Old Country**: Enter the previous country (if applicable).

### New Address
* **New Street Address**: Enter the new street address.
* **New City**: Enter the new city.
* **New State**: Enter the new state.
* **New ZIP Code**: Enter the new postal code.
* **New Country**: Enter the new country (if applicable).

### Address Type
* **Address Type**: Select the type of new address (e.g. residential, business, etc.).

### Mail Forwarding Request
* **Mail Forwarding Request**: Choose how you would like to be notified of the address change:
	+ Yes - Forward all mail
	+ Yes - Forward for 6 months
	+ Yes - Forward for 1 year
	+ No - Do not forward

### Effective Date
* **Effective Date**: Select the date when the address change should take effect.

### Notification Preference
* **Notification Preference**: Choose how you would like to be notified of the address change:
	+ Email
	+ Postal mail
	+ Phone call
	+ No notification needed

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any special instructions or notes.

### Customer Signature
* **Customer Signature**: Sign your name with your full legal name.

### Signature Date
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
