# Post Creation Guidelines Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Creation Guidelines Submission form is designed to gather information from users about their post creation process. This form is meant to gather feedback and ensure that users are following the established guidelines for creating posts. It is an internal tool used by the organization to monitor and improve post creation processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Draft Post" field with a brief description of your post.
2. If applicable, upload a file related to your post in the "File" field.
3. Confirm whether you have followed the guidelines for creating the post by selecting "Guidelines" in the "Confirm Guidelines" field.
4. Select all files related to your post from the "Confirm Files" field.
5. Finally, confirm your guidelines confirmation by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm Guidelines Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Draft Post** (`draft_post`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your post in this field.
* **File** (`file`, text, required: false): If you have a file related to your post, please upload it here. This can be any type of file (image, video, document, etc.).
* **Confirm Guidelines** (`confirm_guidelines`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether you have followed the guidelines for creating the post by selecting "Guidelines".
* **Confirm Files** (`confirm_files`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all files related to your post that you have confirmed.
* **Confirm Guidelines Confirmation** (`confirm_guidelines_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether your guidelines are correct or not by selecting "True" or "False".

## Tips
* Make sure to follow the guidelines for creating posts to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
