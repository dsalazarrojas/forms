# Formulario De Comisiones [ENG] - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for recording commission-related information. Please ensure that you are filling this form for a valid purpose and not duplicating efforts already completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields as indicated in the field-by-field explanation section.
2. For non-required fields, you can leave them blank or fill them in as needed.
3. Ensure that you are filling the form correctly before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Commission Name** (`commission_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the commission.
* **Draw Number** (`draw_number`, `number`, **required**): Enter the draw number related to the commission.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the artist related to the commission.
* **Created** (`created_at`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date the commission was created.
* **Created Time** (`created_time`, `time`, **optional**): Enter the time the commission was created.
* **Lineart Name** (`lineart_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the line art related to the commission.
* **Color Name** (`color_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the color related to the commission.
* **Color Name 2** (`color_name_2`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the color 2 related to the commission.
* **Color Type** (`color_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the available color types (Color 1, Color 2, or Color 3).
* **Color Type 2** (`color_type_2`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select multiple options from the available color types (Option 1, Option 2).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the person who created the commission.
* **Color Type 3** (`color_type_3`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the available color types (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

## Tips
* Be careful when selecting multiple options for Color Type 2, as selecting too many options may affect the outcome of the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field label or contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
