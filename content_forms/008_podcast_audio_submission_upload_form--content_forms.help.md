# Podcast Audio Submission Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit and manage podcast audio content. It's intended for users who want to upload and share their podcast episodes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the episode title to describe the content of your podcast.
2. Upload your podcast audio file using the upload button.
3. Add a description of your podcast episode, if you want to.
4. Add relevant tags to help others find your podcast.
5. Choose the categories for your podcast.
6. Add contributors if you want to.
7. Set the upload status to reflect the current status of your file (e.g., 'Draft', 'Pending Review', etc.).
8. Set the permissions for who can access your podcast episode (Everyone, Team, or Hosts).
9. If you want to share your podcast episode with others, enter their email addresses.
10. Optionally, add a URL for the host's website, their name, and their phone number.
11. Set the host permissions to reflect who can access the host's podcast episode (Everyone, Team, or Hosts).
12. If you want to open the host's URL in a new tab, select 'New Tab' from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **episode_title** (Required): Enter a brief description of your podcast episode.
* **audio_file** (Required): Upload your podcast audio file using the upload button.
* **description** (Optional): Add a detailed description of your podcast episode.
* **tags** (Optional): Enter relevant tags to help others find your podcast episode.
* **categories** (Optional): Choose the categories for your podcast episode.
* **contributors** (Optional): Enter the names of people involved in creating the podcast episode.
* **upload_status** (Required): Set the upload status to reflect the current status of your file (e.g., 'Draft', 'Pending Review', etc.).
* **permissions** (Required): Set the permissions for who can access your podcast episode (Everyone, Team, or Hosts).
* **email** (Optional): Enter email addresses of people you want to share your podcast episode with.
* **host_email** (Optional): Enter the host's email address.
* **host_phone** (Optional): Enter the host's phone number.
* **host_name** (Optional): Enter the host's name.
* **host_permissions** (Required): Set the permissions for who can access the host's podcast episode (Everyone, Team, or Hosts).
* **host_url** (Optional): Enter the host's website URL.
* **host_url_label** (Optional): Enter a label for the host's URL.
* **host_url_link** (Optional): Enter the link text for the host's URL.
* **host_url_target** (Required): Set the target for opening the host's URL (New Tab or Current Tab).
* **host_url_blurb** (Optional): Enter a description for the host's URL.

Note: The required fields are marked with (Required) in the field-by-field explanation.
