# Voice Dubbing Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Dubbing Revision Request Form is designed for users to request revisions to voice dubbing files. It allows you to provide detailed information about the revisions needed and upload relevant files to support your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what is being asked for.
2. Select the revision details that apply to your request.
3. Choose the relevant files to upload as supporting documentation.
4. Add any additional notes that may be helpful for the reviewer.
5. Review your submission for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Revision Details
* **Revision Details** (`revision_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the revision needed for your voice dubbing file. Please be specific about the changes required.

### Revision Files
* **Revision Files** (`revision_files`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select up to three files that support your revision request. These files will be reviewed as part of the revision process.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments that may be helpful for the reviewer to consider during the revision process.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise explanations for the revisions required.
* Ensure that the supporting files are accurate and relevant to the revision request.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to avoid errors or missing information.
