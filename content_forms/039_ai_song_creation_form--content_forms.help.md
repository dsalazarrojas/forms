# AI Song Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the creation of a custom AI-generated song.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Project Brief**: Provide a brief description of your project, which will serve as a starting point for the AI to generate the song. This field is optional, so feel free to keep it blank if you don't have any specific ideas.
2. **Requester name**: Enter your name and surname.
3. **Song title or working title**: Give a title for your song, which can be a working title or a more formal one.
4. **Genre**: Select the genre of music you are looking for (e.g., Pop, Rock, Folk, etc.).
5. **Mood or tone**: Choose the mood or tone you want the song to convey (e.g., Upbeat, Melancholic, etc.).
6. **Approximate tempo in BPM**: Provide the approximate tempo of the song you are looking for, if you know it.
7. **Lyrical themes or keywords**: Enter a few keywords that will guide the lyrics of the song.
8. **Instrumentation preferences**: Select all the instruments you would like to feature in the song (e.g., Acoustic guitar, Piano, Synthesizer, etc.).
9. **Vocal style preference**: If you have a preferred vocal style, provide it here.
10. **Additional notes**: Add any additional information you think might be helpful for the AI to generate the song.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Brief (Optional)**: A brief description of your project. This will help the AI to get a sense of the song's context and tone.
* **Requester name**: Your name and surname.
* **Song title or working title**: A title for the song.
* **Genre**: The genre of music you are looking for.
* **Mood or tone**: The mood or tone you want the song to convey.
* **Approximate tempo in BPM**: The approximate tempo of the song, if you know it.
* **Lyrical themes or keywords**: A few keywords that will guide the lyrics of the song.
* **Instrumentation preferences**: The instruments you would like to feature in the song.
* **Vocal style preference**: Your preferred vocal style.
* **Additional notes**: Any additional information you think might be helpful for the AI to generate the song.
