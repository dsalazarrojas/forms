# digital_resource_co_creation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **digital_resource_co_creation_consent_form** is a form designed to gather information about contributors to a digital project or resource. This includes details about the contributor, project information, and permissions required to access or use the project resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the **Contributor Details** section.
2. Enter project information, such as the project name, description, and other relevant details, in the **Project Information** section.
3. Specify your permissions and access levels in the **Permissions** section.
4. Review and agree to the project terms and conditions in the **Contributor Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### <details> (1, contributor_details)
* **Contributor Details**: This is a text field where you can enter your name, email address, and other relevant contact information. You will be asked to agree to the project terms and conditions later in the form.
* **Project Information**: This section is for entering project-specific information, such as project name, description, and other details related to the project.
* **Permissions**: Here you can specify what level of access you need to the project resources, such as read-only, edit, or admin access.

Please note that some fields in this form are optional and can be filled in later if needed.
