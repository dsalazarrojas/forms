# Digital Media Viewer Content Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your digital media viewer content inquiry. Please use this form to provide details about the content you are inquiring about, and we'll do our best to address your questions and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields as prompted, providing as much detail as possible in each field.
2. Review the form for completeness before submitting.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Viewer Question** (`viewer_question`, text, required/optional): Enter a clear and concise question or issue you have about the digital media viewer content. Be as specific as possible to help us address your concern.
* **Request** (`request`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information or context about your request. This can include any relevant background or details that will help us better understand your inquiry.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): If you have any feedback or suggestions for improvement about the digital media viewer content, please share them with us. We value your input and use it to make informed decisions.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the following categories to help us direct your inquiry to the correct team or department:
	+ Option 1: General Inquiry
	+ Option 2: Technical Support
	+ Option 3: Content Feedback
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information or to follow up on your inquiry.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number. We'll do our best to reach out to you as soon as possible.

## Tips
Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness. If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to contact us directly.
