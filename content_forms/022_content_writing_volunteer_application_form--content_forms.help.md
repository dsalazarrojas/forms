# Content Writing Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to volunteer their writing skills to the organization. It helps us understand your writing background, experience, and preferences to match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your contact information, including your full name and email address.
2. Share your writing experience and skills by answering questions about your years of writing experience and the areas you are interested in writing about.
3. Showcase your portfolio by providing a link to your writing samples or website.
4. Explain why you want to volunteer with us and how many hours per week you can dedicate to writing.
5. Optionally, select your preferred days to write and provide your application date.
6. Finally, confirm that the information you provided is accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **City and State**: Enter the city and state where you reside.
* **Years of Writing Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been writing.
* **Areas of Interest/Expertise**: Select all the areas you have experience or interest in writing about.
* **Link to Writing Portfolio or Samples**: Provide a URL to your writing portfolio or samples.
* **Why do you want to volunteer?**: Explain why you want to volunteer with us and what motivates you.
* **Weekly Commitment (Hours)**: Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to writing per week.
* **Preferred Days to Write**: Select all the days you prefer to write.
* **Application Date**: Enter the date you submit this application.
* **I Certify**: Confirm that the information you provided is accurate and true.

Note: Fields marked as required must be completed in order to submit the form.
