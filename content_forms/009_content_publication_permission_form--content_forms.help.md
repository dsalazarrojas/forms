# Content Publication Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Publication Permission Form is a tool for content creators to grant permission to others to publish, edit, or use their content. It helps ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms of the content use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name of Creator** in the "Full Name of Creator" field.
2. Enter your **Creator Email Address** in the "Creator Email Address" field.
3. Enter the **Title of Publication or Website** where the content will be featured in the "Title of Publication or Website" field.
4. Describe the **Description of Content to be Published** in the "Description of Content to be Published" field.
5. Choose the **Rights Granted to Publisher** from the options listed in the "Rights Granted to Publisher" field.
6. Select the **Preferred Credit Line Format** in the "Preferred Credit Line Format" field.
7. Decide if the publisher is allowed to **edit the content** in the "Is the publisher allowed to edit the content" field.
8. Choose the **Duration of Permission** from the options listed in the "Duration of Permission" field.
9. Sign and date the form with your **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Creator**: Enter your full name as the content creator.
* **Creator Email Address**: Enter your email address to be used for contact purposes.
* **Title of Publication or Website**: Enter the title of the publication or website where the content will be featured.
* **Description of Content to be Published**: Include a brief description of the content, such as title, file name, or summary.
* **Rights Granted to Publisher**: Choose the level of access you grant to the publisher, such as one-time non-exclusive print rights, worldwide digital and social media rights, full transfer of copyright, or educational use only.
* **Preferred Credit Line Format**: Select how you want your name to be displayed, such as "Photo by Name".
* **Is the publisher allowed to edit the content**: Decide if the publisher can make minor or substantial edits to the content.
* **Duration of Permission**: Choose the duration of time you grant permission for the publisher to use the content.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: Sign and date the form with your digital signature, including your full name.

## Tips
* Make sure to understand the terms of permission before granting them.
* Ensure that you have the necessary rights to grant permission for the content.
* Review the form carefully before signing to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
* Keep a record of the signed form for future reference.
