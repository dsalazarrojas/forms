# E Book Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Book Distribution Consent Form is used to collect consent for the distribution of e-books to various platforms. This form ensures that the e-book author or content creator agrees to the terms of distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the required information for each field:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **e book distribution consent form**: Enter a brief description of the e-book, including its title and a short summary.
* **e book**: Enter the title of the e-book.
* **digital publication approval**: Indicate your approval status for digital publication. Please select 'yes' to approve, 'no' to decline, or 'maybe' if you're unsure.
* **platform**: Select the platforms where you want your e-book to be distributed. Currently, the options are platform1 and platform2.
* **distribution preferences**: Choose how you want your e-book to be distributed. Please select one of the available options.
* **e book information**: Enter any additional information about your e-book, such as its content or formatting.
* **signature**: Provide your digital signature as proof of consent.
* **date**: Enter the date when you provided your consent.
* **note 1**: Additional comments or explanations about your consent.
* **email**: Enter your email address to receive notifications about the distribution of your e-book.

Note that fields with 'note' type are intended for additional comments or explanations. 

## Tips
Before submitting the form, please review all fields carefully and ensure that all required fields are completed accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, contact [support email or contact person].
