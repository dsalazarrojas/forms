# Manga Application Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for manga creators to submit their work for review. It gathers information about the creator, their contact details, and the manga submission, including title, genre, description, and images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your creator name in the "Creator Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email in the "Creator Email" field.
3. Enter your website in the "Creator Website" field (if applicable).
4. For the "Manga Title" field, enter the title of the manga you are submitting.
5. Choose the correct genre for your manga from the "Manga Genre" dropdown list.
6. Enter a detailed description of your manga in the "Manga Description" field.
7. If you have cover art for your manga, upload it in the "Cover Art Upload" field.
8. If you have sample pages from your manga, upload them in the "Sample Pages Upload" field.
9. Enter a brief pitch for your manga in the "Manga Pitch" field.
10. Provide any review comments for the submission in the "Review Comments" field.
11. Enter your name as the submitter in the "Submitter Name" field.
12. Provide your email as the submitter in the "Submitter Email" field.
13. Enter your website as the submitter in the "Submitter Website" field (if applicable).
14. Enter any comments for the submitter in the "Submitter Comments" field.
15. Finally, select the review date from the calendar in the "Review Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the creator of the manga.
* **Creator Email** (`creator_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Creator Website** (`creator_contact_website`, text, optional): Enter your website (if you have one).
* **Manga Title** (`manga_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your manga.
* **Manga Genre** (`manga_genre`, select_one, required): Choose the correct genre for your manga from the dropdown list.
* **Manga Description** (`manga_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your manga.
* **Cover Art Upload** (`upload_cover_art`, text, optional): Upload your cover art for the manga.
* **Sample Pages Upload** (`upload_sample_pages`, text, optional): Upload sample pages from your manga.
* **Manga Pitch** (`manga_pitch`, text, optional): Enter a brief pitch for your manga.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Provide any review comments for the submission.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Website** (`submitter_contact_website`, text, optional): Enter your website (if you have one) as the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments for the submitter.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Select the review date from the calendar.
