# Printed Materials Development Brief - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document essential details for printed materials development projects. It is used by project managers and stakeholders to ensure that all necessary project information is gathered and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your project. If a field is marked as optional, you can skip it if it's not relevant to your project. Please ensure that you enter valid data for each field, as this will help to avoid errors and ensure that your project is accurately represented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Printed Materials Development Brief** (Text)
The title of your project, briefly describing the purpose and scope of the printed materials development project.
### 2. **Client Name** (Text)
The name of the client for whom you are developing printed materials.
### 3. **Client Contact Information** (Text)
Provide the relevant contact details for your client, including name, email, and phone number.
### 4. **Project Description** (Text)
A detailed description of the project, including its objectives, deliverables, and any specific requirements or constraints.
### 5. **Project Manager** (Text)
The name and contact information of the project manager responsible for the project.
### 6. **Project Status** (Select One)
The current status of your project, which can be Active, In Progress, or Completed.
### 7. **Project Start Date** (Date)
The date when the project started.
### 8. **Project End Date** (Date)
The date when the project is expected to end.
### 9. **Project Budget** (Number)
The budget allocated for the project, in the format of a number.
### 10. **Project Location** (Text)
The location where the project will be executed or where the printed materials will be used.
### 11. **Project Notes** (Note)
Any additional information or notes about the project that you would like to record.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use valid contact information for your client.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your project manager or the development team for assistance.
* This form should not be used for projects without a valid project ID or code.
