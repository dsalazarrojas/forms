# Series Creation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a series of ideas, including its title, description, target audience, and production status. The survey aims to gather data from creators about their series, which can be used to improve our understanding of the market and inform our business decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the series title and description on pages 1 and 2, respectively.
2. Select the target audience and idea stage from the corresponding drop-down menus on pages 3 and 4.
3. Choose the series format from the drop-down menu on page 5.
4. Select the genre of the series on page 6.
5. Enter the series title, tagline, number of show ideas, total number of episodes, and episode length on pages 7-11, respectively.
6. Select the production status of the series from the drop-down menu on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **series_ideas** (`series_creation_survey`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your series ideas.
* **idea_description** (`idea_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of your series, including its concept and plot.
* **target_audience** (`target_audience`, select_one, required): Choose the type of audience you intend to target with your series (General, Youth, Niche, or Specific).
* **idea_stage** (`idea_stage`, select_one, optional): Select the stage of development your series is currently in (Series development, Show development, or Pitch development).
* **series_format** (`series_format`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the formats of your series (Serialized, Non-serialized, or Both).
* **genre** (`genre`, select_one, optional): Select the genre of your series (Action, Comedy, Drama, or Thriller).
* **series_title** (`series_title`, text, required): Enter a short title for your series.
* **tagline** (`tagline`, text, optional): Enter a catchy tagline for your series.
* **show_ideas** (`show_ideas`, number, required): Enter the number of show ideas you plan to produce.
* **total_episodes** (`total_episodes`, text, required): Enter the total number of episodes in your series.
* **episode_length** (`episode_length`, text, required): Enter the length of each episode.
* **series_status** (`series_status`, select_one, optional): Select the production status of your series (In development, In production, In post-production, On hold, or Cancelled).
* **show_format** (`show_format`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the format of your series (Serialized, Non-serialized, or Both).
