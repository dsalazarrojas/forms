# Buter Family Contact Info - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Buter Family Contact Info form is designed to collect essential contact information from family members. This form helps the Buter family maintain a centralized and accurate record of their relationships, emails, phone numbers, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your relationship to the family. Choose how you fit into the Buter family dynamics.
2. Enter your primary and secondary email addresses, if available.
3. Add your primary and secondary phone numbers, including the type of phone number (e.g., mobile, home, work).
4. Provide your street address, city, state or province, and postal code.
5. If applicable, enter your workplace name and occupation or job title.
6. If you have a work phone number, add it.
7. Enter your social media handles (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).
8. Indicate your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone call, text message, social media).
9. Choose whether you're an emergency contact for the family or not.
10. Decide whether you want to receive family updates or newsletters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Member Name (<member_name>):** Enter the name of your family member. This will be the first and last name (e.g., John Smith).
* **Relationship to Family (<relationship>):** Select how you're related to the family, such as Parent or Guardian, Child, Spouse or Partner, Sibling, etc.
* **Primary Email Address (<primary_email>):** Enter your main email address.
* **Secondary Email Address (optional) (<secondary_email>):** Enter an alternative email address, if you have one.
* **Primary Phone Number (<primary_phone>):** Add your main phone number.
* **Secondary Phone Number (optional) (<secondary_phone>):** Enter an alternate phone number, if you have one.
* **Phone Type (<phone_type>):** Select the type of phone number (e.g., mobile, home, work).
* **Street Address (<street_address>):** Enter the address where you reside.
* **City (<city>):** Enter the city where you live.
* **State or Province (<state_province>):** Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code (<postal_code>):** Enter your ZIP code.
* **Date of Birth (<date_of_birth>):** Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Occupation or Job Title (<occupation>):** Enter your current job title or occupation, if applicable.
* **Workplace or Employer Name (<workplace_name>):** Enter the name of your workplace or employer, if applicable.
* **Work Phone Number (<work_phone>):** Enter your work phone number, if applicable.
* **Social Media Handles (<social_media>):** Enter your social media handles (e.g., Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn).
* **Preferred Contact Method (<prefer_contact_method>):** Select your preferred way to be contacted.
* **Is this person an emergency contact for the family? (<emergency_contact>):** Indicate whether you're an emergency contact for the family.
* **Receive Family Updates or Newsletter (<newsletter_preference>):** Decide whether to receive updates or newsletters from the family.
* **Additional Information or Notes (<additional_notes>):** Enter any additional information or notes that you'd like to share.
