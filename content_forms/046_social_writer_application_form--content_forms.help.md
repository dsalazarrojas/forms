# social_writer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Writer Application Form is used to gather information from individuals who are interested in writing for the platform. This information is necessary for the platform to review and consider your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Be as accurate as possible with your contact information and experience.
3. Select the correct options for your experience, education, and other fields.
4. Attach your resume and make sure to include all relevant information.
5. Make sure to include a valid email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact`, `text`, required):
	+ Please enter your name, email, and phone number. This information will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Writing Experience** (`experience`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your level of writing experience from the options provided.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the platforms or publications where you have written for.
* **Education** (`education`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the languages you are proficient in.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the social media platforms where you have a presence.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `note`, required):
	+ Attach your resume and make sure to include all relevant information.
* **Background Check** (`background_check`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide any information related to background checks or security screenings.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the companies or organizations you have worked for.
* **Referees** (`referees`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the people who can vouch for your character and work ethic.
* **References** (`references`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the people who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the people who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the times you are available to work.
* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your native language.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your nationality.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Select your gender.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter your age.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
	+ Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter a valid phone number.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, required):
