# online-magazine-content-writer-interview-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential writers for an online magazine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the editor-name field.
2. Enter your job title in the job-title field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the contact-phone field.
5. Upload your resume in the resume field.
6. Enter a sample article in the sample-article field.
7. Describe your writing experience in the work-experience field.
8. Enter your education background in the education-background field.
9. Select your availability frequency in the availability-frequency field.
10. Choose your work status in the work-status field.
11. Select your preferred communication channels in the preferred-communication-channel field.
12. Enter your social media links in the social-media-link field.
13. Enter your portfolio site in the portfolio-site field.
14. Enter your Twitter handle in the twitter-handle field.
15. Enter your YouTube channel in the youtube-channel field.
16. Enter your LinkedIn profile in the linkedin-profile field.
17. Describe your work experience in the work-experience-description field.
18. Enter your work experience company in the work-experience-company field.
19. Select your education level in the education-level field.
20. Select your preferred locations in the preferred-location field.
21. Select the channels you are available to receive communication from in the availability-notice field.
22. Select the channels you prefer to receive communication from in the preferred-communication-channel field.
23. Enter your availability in the availability field.
24. Select your writing experience years in the work-experience-years field.
25. Select your work experience in the work-experience field.
