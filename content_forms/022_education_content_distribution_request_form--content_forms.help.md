# Education Content Distribution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their education content distribution requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your school information, including any relevant details about your institution.
2. Enter your contact information, which will help us reach out to you if we need more details.
3. Describe the type of content you are requesting.
4. Choose the distribution channels where you would like to receive the content.
5. Select the target audience for the content.
6. Choose the type of content you would like to receive.
7. Enter a desired submission deadline, if applicable.
8. Optionally, provide additional details about your form submission.
9. Enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Info** (`school_info`, text, required: false): Enter your school's name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, or phone number so we can contact you about your request.
* **Content Request** (`content_request`, note, required: false): Describe the type of content you are requesting, such as a video, document, or image.
* **Distribution Channels** (`distribution_channels`, select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more options where you would like to receive the content, such as email, website, or social media.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select one, required: false): Select the primary audience for the content, such as students, teachers, or administrators.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, select multiple, required: false): Choose the type of content you would like to receive, such as text, image, or video.
* **Submission Deadline** (`submission_deadline`, date, required: false): Enter a specific date by which you would like to receive the content, if applicable.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, required: false): Optionally, provide additional details about your form submission, such as any specific instructions or requirements.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your request.
