# email_campaign_content_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit content and settings for an email campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields with the requested information for the email campaign.
2. Make sure to enter valid email addresses in the email fields.
3. Check the box next to 'Yes' for the 'email_addresses' field to indicate that the form is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **content_submission** (`content_submission`, text, optional): Enter the content of the email campaign.
* **email_addresses** (`email_addresses`, select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you want to include email addresses with the campaign.
* **email_subject** (`email_subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject line of the email campaign.
* **email_body** (`email_body`, text, optional): Enter the body text of the email campaign.
* **email_from_name** (`email_from_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the sender of the email campaign.
* **email_from_email** (`email_from_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the sender of the email campaign.
* **email_to_name** (`email_to_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the recipient of the email campaign.
* **email_to_email** (`email_to_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient of the email campaign.
* **campaign_subject** (`campaign_subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject of the campaign.
* **campaign_body** (`campaign_body`, text, optional): Enter the body text of the campaign.
* **send_at** (`send_at`, date, optional): Select the date to send the campaign.

## Tips
* Ensure that the content and settings are correct before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, double-check the form instructions and the system's help guide for more information.
