# Field Notes Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information and specific details related to field observations. It is an original adaptation generated by AI, meant to be used for data collection purposes. The form is not affiliated with any form creation or data collection platform. You are free to use, modify, and share it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (marked as "required").
2. For each field, enter the requested information in the format specified.
3. Review your entries before submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This field is meant for entering any general information you want to record. You can enter a brief description of the observation or any relevant details. It's an optional field, so you can skip it if not necessary.
* **Date And Time** (`2`, `date`, required: true): Please select the date and time of the observation from the calendar picker or enter it manually using the format: YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Location** (`3`, `text`, required: true): Enter the location where the field observation was made, such as a specific address or a geographical point.
* **Field Observation** (`4`, `text`, required: true): Describe the field observation in detail, including any relevant information about what was observed.
* **Weather** (`5`, `text`, required: false): If the weather was relevant to the observation, you can enter it here. This field is optional, but it can provide valuable context.
* **Notes** (`6`, `text`, required: true): Enter any additional notes or comments about the field observation. This field is required for thorough documentation.
