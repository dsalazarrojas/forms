# Guide To Choosing Writing Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help customers find the right writing services for their specific needs. It collects information to determine the type of writing service required, the scope of the project, the deadline, and the budget. This information will be used to match customers with suitable writing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Your User Type**: Choose one of the three options - Students, Professionals, or Agencies. This will help us understand your level of expertise and the type of writing services you need.
2. **Specify What You Are Looking For**: Select all that apply from the list of writing needs. This will help us determine the type of writing services required.
3. **Set Your Deadline**: Enter a date for the deadline of your writing project. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. **Set Your Budget**: Enter the budget for your writing project. This will help us understand how much you are willing to spend on the project.
5. **Add Any Other Details**: If there are any additional details you would like to provide, please enter them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Type**:
	+ Select one of the three options: 
		- **Students**: For academic writing projects, such as research papers, essays, and term papers.
		- **Professionals**: For business writing projects, such as company blogs, website content, and marketing materials.
		- **Agencies**: For content creation projects, such as social media management, content marketing, and website development.
* **What are you looking for?**:
	+ Select all that apply:
		- **Blog posts**: For articles to be published on your blog or website.
		- **Articles**: For in-depth articles on a particular topic.
		- **Web pages**: For website content, such as product descriptions, about pages, and FAQs.
		- **Social media posts**: For social media content, such as Facebook posts, Twitter posts, and Instagram posts.
		- **Other**: For any other type of writing project not listed above.
* **Deadline**:
	+ Enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Budget**:
	+ Enter the budget for your writing project.
* **Other Details**:
	+ If there are any additional details you would like to provide, please enter them here.
