# hagadone_newspaper_wedding_announcement_form_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a couple's wedding details, including the bride and groom's names, wedding date and time, locations, and other relevant information to share with guests and loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Bride Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the **Groom Name** in the field.
3. If applicable, select a **Wedding Date** from the calendar.
4. Choose the **Wedding Time** and **Wedding Time Zone** if desired.
5. Enter the **Wedding Location**.
6. Select the **Wedding Time Zone Description** if applicable.
7. Enter the **Officiant Name** if applicable.
8. Enter the **Wedding Color Scheme** if applicable.
9. Choose the **Invitation Type** (with or without photo).
10. Select the **Invitation Design** (Formal or Informal).
11. Choose the **Invitation Text Color** and **Invitation Background Color**.
12. Choose the **Invitation Border Color**.
13. Enter the **Reception Location** and **Reception Description**.
14. Select the **Reception Date** and **Reception Time** if applicable.
15. Enter the **Reception Time Zone** if applicable.
16. Enter the **Reception Venue** if applicable.
17. Enter the **Reception Note** if applicable.
18. Enter the **Ceremony Location**.
19. Enter the **Ceremony Description**.
20. Enter the **Wedding Theme** if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride Name** (`bride_name`, `text`, required): Enter the bride's name.
* **Groom Name** (`groom_name`, `text`, required): Enter the groom's name.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, `date`, optional): Select the wedding date from the calendar.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, `time`, optional): Choose the time of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time Zone** (`wedding_time_zone`, `text`, optional): Choose the time zone for the wedding.

* **Wedding Color Scheme** (`wedding_color_scheme`, `text`, optional): Enter the color scheme for the wedding.
* **Invitation Type** (`invitation_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of invitation (with or without photo).
* **Invitation Design** (`invitation_design`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the invitation design (Formal or Informal).
* **Invitation Text Color** (`invitation_text_color`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the text color for the invitation.
* **Invitation Background Color** (`invitation_background_color`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the background color for the invitation.
* **Invitation Border Color** (`invitation_border_color`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the border color for the invitation.
* **Reception Location** (`reception_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the reception.
* **Reception Description** (`reception_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a description of the reception.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, `date`, optional): Select the reception date.
* **Reception Time** (`reception_time`, `time`, optional): Choose the reception time.
* **Reception Time Zone** (`reception_time_zone`, `text`, optional): Choose the time zone for the reception.
* **Reception Venue** (`reception_venue`, `text`, optional): Enter the reception venue.
* **Reception Note** (`reception_note`, `text`, optional): Enter any reception notes.
* **Ceremony Location** (`ceremony_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the ceremony.
