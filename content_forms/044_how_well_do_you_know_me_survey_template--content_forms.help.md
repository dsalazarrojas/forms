# how_well_do_you_know_me_survey_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form template is designed to assess how well someone knows you. It consists of a series of questions and prompts that will help you gauge the depth of your relationship with someone. The form is meant to be completed by someone who has had interactions with you, and their answers will provide insight into your personality, habits, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your knowledge of the person.
2. Fill in your answers in the corresponding text fields.
3. Make sure to be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you're unsure about any question, try to recall specific events or memories related to your interactions with the person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How well do you know me?** (`get_to_know_me`, `text`, required: false): This question is meant to be a starting point for the form. Answer in a few sentences, describing your level of familiarity with the person.
* **Best friend's name** (`best_friend_name`, `text`, required: false): If the person is a close friend, type their name in this field.
* **How many years have you known them?** (`how_many_years`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the number of years you've known the person.
* **What is your favorite hobby?** (`what_is_your_favorite_hobby`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose one of the options that best describes your favorite hobby.
* **What do you think is my favorite food?** (`what_do_you_think_is_my_favourite_food`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the options that you think might be the person's favorite food.
* **How much money did you spend on this quiz?** (`how_much_money`, `number`, required: false): This question is for our internal records, so feel free to answer any amount you like.
* **Date of last encounter** (`date_of_last_encounter`, `date`, required: false): Try to recall the date of your last interaction with the person.
* **Time of last encounter** (`time_of_last_encounter`, `time`, required: false): Try to recall the time of your last interaction with the person.
* **Note to the host** (`note_to_the_host`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional comments or notes for the host.
* **What's your email?** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Type your email address if you'd like to be in touch with the person.
* **What's your phone number?** (`what_is_your_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be in touch with the person.
* **How much money did you spend on this quiz?** (`how_much_money_spent_on_quiz`, `number`, required: false): This is another question for our internal records, so feel free to answer any amount you like.
* **What do you know about me?** (`what_do_you_know_about_me`, `text`, required: false): Describe any interesting facts or characteristics that you're aware of about the person.
* **Favorite memory?** (`favorite_memory`, `text`, required: false): Share a specific memory that you cherish about your interactions with the person.
* **Favorite place?** (`favorite_place`, `text`, required: false): Type the name of the place that holds special meaning to your relationship with the person.
* **Best time spent together?** (`best_time_spent_together`, `text`, required: false): Describe the best time you've spent with the person.
* **What was your first impression of me?** (`what_was_your_first_impression`, `text`, required: false): Share your initial impression of the person.
* **Most interesting habit?** (`most_interesting_habit`, `text`, required: false): Describe any unique habit or characteristic of the person that you find interesting.
