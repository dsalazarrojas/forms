# Responsive Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Share Your Story Form is designed to collect information from customers, clients, or other stakeholders about their stories, business, and experiences. This form helps in providing a platform for users to share their stories and engage with others in a responsive and engaging way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the "Share Your Story" field with your story.
2. Proceed to the "About Your Business" field and select the options that best describe your business.
3. Finally, in the "Share Your Experience" field, type out your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Share Your Story** (text): This is the main section of the form where you can share your story in detail.
  * Please provide a brief and compelling story that showcases your personality, experience, and expertise.
  * Keep your story clear, concise, and within the allowed character limit.
* **About Your Business** (select multiple): This section allows you to select the options that best describe your business.
  * Choose the options that accurately represent your business or service.
  * Select all relevant options, as multiple selections are allowed.
* **Share Your Experience** (text): This section is for you to type out your experience.
  * Share your expertise, skills, or any relevant experience related to the services you offer.
  * Keep your response clear, concise, and within the allowed character limit.

## Tips
* Be as creative and engaging as possible in your story and experience sections.
* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully to ensure accuracy and coherence.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
