# Course Listing Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store course information for display on a platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course title in the "Course Title" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the course in the "Course Description" field.
3. Select one or multiple tags that best categorize the course from the options provided in the "Course Tags" field.
4. Enter the instructor's name in the "Instructor Name" field.
5. Enter the instructor's title in the "Instructor Title" field.
6. Enter the start date of the course in the "Course Start Date" field.
7. Enter the end date of the course in the "Course End Date" field.
8. Select the current status of the course from the options provided in the "Course Status" field.
9. Enter the course location in the "Course Location" field.
10. Enter the instructor's phone number in the "Instructor Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the course, e.g., "Introduction to Programming".
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the course, including its objectives and content.
* **Course Tags** (`course_tags`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple tags that best categorize the course, such as "Computer Science", "Programming", "Education", "Art", or "Business".
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the instructor or course administrator.
* **Instructor Title** (`instructor_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title or position of the instructor, e.g., "Professor", "Lecturer", or "Instructor".
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the course in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the course in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Course Status** (`course_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the course from "Active", "In Progress", or "Completed".
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the course is held, e.g., "Online", "University of [Name]", or "City Hall".
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the instructor or course administrator.
