# opinion-article-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The opinion-article-assessment-form is designed to gather feedback and assessments from users on a given article.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Article URL field with the article's URL.
2. Enter the Author's name in the Author field.
3. Provide the Article Title in the Article Title field.
4. Enter the Article Date in the Article Date field. Please use the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Rate the article's quality on a scale of 1-5 in the Rating field.
6. Choose your Recommendation from the dropdown list in the Recommendation field.
7. If you have any additional Comments, please enter them in the Comments field.
8. If you would like to provide your Email address, please enter it in the Email field.
9. You can also add a Phone number in the Phone field, although this field is not required.
10. Finally, if you have any additional notes, please use the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Article URL** (`article_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the article you are assessing.
* **Author** (`author`, text, required: false): Enter the author's name.
* **Article Title** (`article_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the article.
* **Article Date** (`article_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the article was published.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate the article's quality on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, required: false): Choose your recommendation from the dropdown list.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have on the article.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address (this will help us contact you if needed).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (this is optional, but can be helpful for follow-up questions).
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or thoughts about the article.
