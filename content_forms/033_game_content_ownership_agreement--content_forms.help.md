# Game Content Ownership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Content Ownership Agreement form is a crucial document that outlines the ownership and licensing terms for game content. It ensures that both the creator and the studio have a clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities regarding the game content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information as the creator of the game content, including your name, address, and contact details.
2. Specify the game title, content type, and ownership type.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the agreement.
4. Provide the game version and agreement terms.
5. Sign the form electronically using the E Signature field.
6. Submit the form to complete the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the creator of the game content.
* **Studio Name** (`studio_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the studio associated with the game content.
* **Game Title** (`game_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the game.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of content being agreed upon (Games, Assets, Music, Sound, Art, or Other).
* **Ownership Type** (`ownership_type`, `select_one`, required): Specify the type of ownership (Exclusive, Non-Exclusive, Royalty, or Joint).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Game Version** (`game_version`, `text`, required): Enter the version of the game.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, `note`, required): Provide a brief description of the agreement terms.
* **E Signature** (`e_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form electronically.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email as the submitter.
* **Studio Address** (`studio_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the studio.
* **Creator Address** (`creator_address`, `text`, required): Enter your address as the creator.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company as the submitter.
* **Creator Company** (`creator_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company as the creator.
* **Creator Title** (`creator_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title as the creator.
* **Studio Title** (`studio_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the studio.
* **Content License** (`content_license`, `text`, required): Specify the content license.
* **License Terms** (`license_terms`, `text`, required): Enter the license terms.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the agreement (Active, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Game Status** (`game_status`, `select_one`, required): Specify the status of the game (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the review status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure accurate completion.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form guidelines or seek assistance from the relevant authorities.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submission.
