# Music Rights Management Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Rights Management Application Form is designed to collect information about music works, their ownership status, and application details from artists, labels, or other music right holders. This form helps manage music rights by providing a centralized platform for submitting and tracking applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the artist information, including the artist's name.
2. Select whether the music works are owned by the artist or not.
3. Enter the application details, which will determine the status of the application.
4. Enter the contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
5. Select whether the music rights agreement is in place or not.
6. Choose the application date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Information (artist_info)**: Enter the name of the artist whose music work is being managed.
* **Music Works (music_works)**: Select "Yes" if the music works are owned by the artist, otherwise select "No".
* **Music Works Ownership (music_works_ownership)**: Select "Yes" if the music works are owned by the artist, otherwise select "No".
* **Application Details (application_details)**: Enter a numerical value representing the application details.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the application.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: Enter a valid email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Application Status (application_status)**: Select "Active" to indicate the application is active, otherwise select "Inactive".
* **Notes (notes)**: This field is optional, use it to add any additional information about the application.
* **Additional Info (additional_info)**: This field is optional, use it to add any additional information about the application.
* **Music Right Holder (music_right_holder)**: Enter the name of the music right holder.
* **Music Rights Agreement (music_rights_agreement)**: Select "Yes" if the music rights agreement is in place, otherwise select "No".
* **Application Date (application_date)**: Enter the date of the application.
* **Application Time (application_time)**: Enter the time of the application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Review and update your application status regularly.
* Use the "Additional Info" field for any additional details about the application.
