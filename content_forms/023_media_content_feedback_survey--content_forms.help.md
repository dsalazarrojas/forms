# Media Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Media Content Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect opinions and suggestions from users about the quality and relevance of media content provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions carefully and confirm that this form is not a duplicate or redundant effort.
2. Select whether the content is relevant to your interests.
3. Provide your honest opinion of the content's title.
4. Give a detailed description of the content.
5. Share your thoughts and suggestions about the content.
6. Rate the content's quality as 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
7. Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
8. Provide your contact phone number (optional, but helps us get in touch with you).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title to identify the content you are providing feedback on.
* **Is the content relevant to your interests?** (`media`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply to describe how relevant the content is to you.
* **Content Title** (`title`, text, required): Provide a detailed and accurate description of the content's title.
* **Content Description** (`content`, text, required): Write a detailed description of the content itself.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts and suggestions about the content.
* **Content Quality** (`rating`, select_one, required): Rate the content's quality as 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (optional, but helps us get in touch with you).
