# Note Taking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about meetings and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Meeting Notes field with a brief summary of the meeting.
2. Select the Project Name field to choose the relevant project from the options provided.
3. Choose the Project Lead from the options provided.
4. Enter the Meeting Date and Meeting Time fields to record the date and time of the meeting.
5. Enter the Meeting Location field to record the location of the meeting.
6. Select the Project Status field to indicate the status of the project.
7. Enter the Project Notes field with any additional project information.
8. Choose the Project Tags field to select any relevant tags or labels for the project.
9. Enter the Meeting Duration field to record the length of the meeting in minutes.
10. Enter the Meeting Attendees field to record who attended the meeting.
11. (Optional) Enter a Meeting File field to attach relevant files or documents from the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Notes** (meeting_notes, text, required/optional): Provide a brief summary of the meeting, highlighting key discussions, decisions, or action items.
* **Project Name** (project_name, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the relevant project from the options provided. This will help in organizing and categorizing meeting data.
* **Project Lead** (project_lead, select_one, required/optional): Select the person in charge of the project. This will help in tracking project ownership and responsibilities.
* **Meeting Date** (meeting_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the meeting in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Meeting Time** (meeting_time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the meeting in 24-hour format.
* **Meeting Location** (meeting_location, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the meeting took place.
* **Project Status** (project_status, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate the status of the project, such as Active, Inactive, or On Hold.
* **Project Notes** (project_notes, text, required/optional): Enter any additional project information, such as goals, objectives, or next steps.
* **Project Tags** (project_tags, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any relevant tags or labels for the project, such as Yes, No, or other custom tags.
* **Meeting Duration** (meeting_duration, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the meeting in minutes.
* **Meeting Attendees** (meeting_attendees, text, required/optional): Record who attended the meeting.
* **Meeting File** (meeting_file, text, required/optional): (Optional) Attach relevant files or documents from the meeting, such as meeting minutes or action items.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review and edit your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct format for dates and times.
* Use the correct tags or labels for projects.
* Attach relevant files or documents to support meeting minutes or action items.

This help guide is a starting point, and you may need to adapt it based on your specific use case or requirements.
