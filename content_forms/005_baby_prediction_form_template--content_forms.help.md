# Baby Prediction Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Baby Prediction Form Template is designed to collect predictions and fun facts from friends and family about a new baby, including expected date and time of birth, weight, length, eye and hair color, and other exciting characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field as you would like it to be displayed in the new baby book.
2. Choose between "Boy!" or "Girl!" for the baby's predicted sex.
3. Select a date of birth for your prediction.
4. Choose a time of birth for your prediction (optional).
5. Predict the baby's weight in pounds (if you're feeling generous with your estimate!).
6. Predict the baby's length in inches.
7. Select an eye color for your prediction.
8. Select a hair color for your prediction.
9. Choose who the baby will look like most.
10. Write a piece of advice for the new parents.
11. Share your heart's wish for the baby.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Baby Shower Predictions --**: This is just a friendly introduction to get you started on your predictions!
* **Your Name**: This is where you enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the new baby book.
* **What will it be?**: Choose between "Boy!" or "Girl!" for the baby's predicted sex.
* **Predicted Date of Birth**: Enter a date for your prediction. Keep in mind that due date is usually just a suggestion!
* **Predicted Time of Birth**: Enter a time of birth for your prediction (if you're feeling ambitious!).
* **Predicted Weight (Pounds)**: Estimate the baby's weight in pounds.
* **Predicted Weight (Ounces)**: Estimate the baby's weight in ounces (not required).
* **Predicted Length (Inches)**: Estimate the baby's length in inches.
* **Likely Eye Color**: Choose an eye color for your prediction.
* **Likely Hair Color**: Choose a hair color for your prediction.
* **Who will the baby look like most?**: Select who the baby will look like most, be it mom, dad, or a mix.
* **A Piece of Advice for the New Parents**: Write a heartfelt message for the new parents-to-be.
* **My Wish for the Baby**: Share your heart's wish for the baby.
