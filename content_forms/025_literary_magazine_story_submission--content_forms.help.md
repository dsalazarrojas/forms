# Literary Magazine Story Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting literary magazine stories to our publication. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth submission process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For fields with options, select the relevant value from the dropdown menu.
4. Upload any required files in the designated fields.
5. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story** (\*: text): Enter the title of your submitted story.
* **Author** (text): Provide your name as the author of the story.
* **Manuscript** (\*: text): Upload the manuscript of your submitted story.
* **Publication History** (select_multiple): Select the types of publication where your story has been published or is going to be published (Fiction, Non-Fiction, Poetry, or None).
* **File Uploads** (text): This field is for uploading any additional files related to your submission.
* **Submission Date** (\*: date): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (\*: time): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission Hour** (\*: number): Enter the hour of submission.
* **Submission Minute** (\*: number): Enter the minute of submission.
* **Submission Second** (\*: number): Enter the second of submission.
* **Submission Date Time** (text): This field is optional and for additional notes about the submission date and time.
* **Email** (\*: email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (\*: text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Manuscript Type** (\*: select_one): Select the type of manuscript you are submitting (Short Story, Poem, or other).
* **Word Count** (number): Enter the word count of your submitted story.
* **Submission Status** (\*: select_one): Select the status of your submission (Under Review, Accepted, or Rejected).
* **Submission Note 1-3** (note): These fields are for additional notes about your submission.
* **Email Uploads** (text): This field is for uploading any additional emails related to your submission.
* **Manuscript Uploads** (text): This field is for uploading any additional manuscripts related to your submission.
* **Story Uploads** (text): This field is for uploading any additional stories related to your submission.
* **Submission Date Time Uploads** (text): This field is for uploading any additional notes about the submission date and time.
* **Submission Note 1-3 Uploads** (note): These fields are for uploading any additional notes about your submission.
