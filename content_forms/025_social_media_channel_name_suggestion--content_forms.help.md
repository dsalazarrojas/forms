# Social Media Channel Name Suggestion - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you suggest a name for a social media channel. It collects information about your brand, target audience, and channel preferences to create a comprehensive name suggestion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your brand's name in the "Brand Name" field.
* Describe your brand's tagline or unique value proposition in the "Tagline" field.
* Specify your target audience, such as "18-30", "21-35", or "50-65", in the "Age Range" field.
* Choose the type of content your channel will feature, such as video, image, live, or blog posts.
* Select a color scheme for your channel, either dark or light.
* Provide a name for your social media channel, ensuring it's unique and descriptive.
* (Optional) Enter a username for your channel, which can be used as a handle.
* (Optional) Write a brief description of your channel in the "Channel Description" field.
* (Optional) Choose one or more style tags that best describe your content style, such as funny, serious, informative, or entertaining.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Brand Name** (`Brand Name`, `text`, required): Enter your brand's name.
* **Tagline** (`Tagline`, `text`, required): Describe your brand's unique value proposition or tagline.
* **Target Audience** (`Target Audience`, `text`, required): Specify your target audience's age range.
* **Audience Description** (`Audience Description`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of your target audience.
* **Age Range** (`Age Range`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the age range of your target audience.
	+ Options: 18, 21, 30, 35, 50, 65.
* **Style Name** (`Style Name`, `text`, optional): Enter a name for your style.
* **Channel Type** (`Channel Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of content your channel will feature.
	+ Options: Video, Image, Live, Blog.
* **Color Scheme** (`Color Scheme`, `select_one`, required): Select a color scheme for your channel.
	+ Options: Dark, Light.
* **Channel Name** (`Channel Name`, `text`, required): Enter a unique and descriptive name for your channel.
* **Channel Username** (`Channel Username`, `text`, optional): Enter a username for your channel.
* **Channel Description** (`Channel Description`, `text`, optional): Write a brief description of your channel.
* **Style Tags** (`Style Tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more style tags that best describe your content style.
	+ Options: Funny, Serious, Informative, Entertaining.
