# Feature Recipe Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feature Recipe Release Form is used to collect information about new recipes to be published in the system. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the recipe name in the "Recipe Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the recipe in the "Recipe Description" field.
3. Enter the URL of the featured recipe in the "Recipe URL" field, if applicable.
4. Choose the publishing permission for the recipe from the "Publishing Permission" field.
5. Add any additional publishing notes in the "Publishing Notes" field, if needed.
6. Choose the category of the recipe from the "Category" field.
7. Select relevant tags for the recipe from the "Tags" field.
8. If you have a image to upload, enter the URL in the "Image URL" field.
9. Choose whether the recipe is featured or not from the "Featured" field.
10. Click "Submit" to publish the recipe or "Save Draft" to save the recipe as a draft.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feature Recipe Release Form First Page (1)** (`feature_recipe_release_form_first_page`, `text`, required/optional): This field is the first page of the form and serves as a starting point for filling out the form.
* **User Name (2)** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name to associate it with the published recipe.
* **User Email (3)** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address to associate it with the published recipe.
* **Confirm Email (4)** (`user_email_confirmation`, `email`, required/optional): Confirm your email address to ensure it matches the one entered in the "User Email" field.
* **Recipe Name (5)** (`recipe_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the recipe you are publishing.
* **Recipe Description (6)** (`recipe_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the recipe.
* **Recipe Image (7)** (`recipe_image`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the image related to the recipe.
* **Recipe Ingredients (8)** (`recipe_ingredients`, `text`, required/optional): List the ingredients used in the recipe.
* **Recipe Instructions (9)** (`recipe_instructions`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the steps to make the recipe.
* **Image URL (10)** (`recipe_image_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the URL of the image related to the recipe.
* **Publishing Permission (11)** (`publishing_permission`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether the recipe is public or private.
* **Publishing Notes (12)** (`publishing_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the recipe's publishing.
