# Sound Design Enhancement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to submit requests for enhancements or changes to sound design features or assets in a project or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of enhancement or change you are requesting (in the "Sound Design Enhancement Request" field).
2. Check whether you need to provide audio files to support your request in the "Audio Files" field.
3. Select the priority of your request (in the "Priority" field).
4. Enter a brief description of your request in the "Brief Description" field.
5. Provide any relevant references or information about the project or product (in the "Brief References" field).
6. Enter the expected delivery date and time for your request (in the "Expected Delivery Date" and "Expected Delivery Time" fields).
7. Enter any additional comments or information about your request (in the "Additional Comments" field).
8. If necessary, provide a reference to a client or stakeholder (in the "Client Reference" field), their email (in the "Client Email" field), and their phone number (in the "Client Phone" field).
9. If you are referencing an internal or external resource, provide the relevant information (in the "Internal Reference", "External Reference", "External Reference Url", "Internal Reference Url", "External Service", and "External Service Url" fields).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sound Design Enhancement Request** (Required/Optional): 
  - A brief description of the enhancement or change you are requesting. For example, "Improve audio fidelity on page X" or "Add a sound effect to button X".

* **Audio Files** (Optional): 
  - If your enhancement request requires audio files to support it, upload them here. For example, audio files for a sound effect or a new audio track.

* **Priority** (Required/Optional): 
  - Select the priority of your request from the provided options: High, Medium, or Low. This will help us understand the urgency and importance of your request.

* **Brief Description** (Required/Optional): 
  - A brief description of your request, including any relevant background or context. For example, "We need to improve the audio quality on page X to match the client's expectation".

* **Brief References** (Required/Optional): 
  - Provide any relevant references or information about the project or product, such as a project name, product version, or relevant documentation.

* **Expected Delivery Date** (Required/Optional): 
  - Enter the expected date when you need the enhancement or change delivered.

* **Expected Delivery Time** (Required/Optional): 
  - Enter the expected time when you need the enhancement or change delivered.

* **Additional Comments** (Required/Optional): 
  - Enter any additional information or comments about your request.

* **Client Reference** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing a client or stakeholder, provide their name here.

* **Client Email** (Required/Optional): 
  - If the client has a specific email address associated with their request, provide it here.

* **Client Phone** (Required/Optional): 
  - If the client has a specific phone number associated with their request, provide it here.

* **Internal Reference** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing an internal resource, provide the relevant information here.

* **External Reference** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing an external resource, provide the relevant information here.

* **External Reference Url** (Required/Optional): 
  - Provide the URL of the external reference.

* **External Service** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing an external service, provide the relevant information here.

* **External Service Url** (Required/Optional): 
  - Provide the URL of the external service.

* **Internal Reference Url** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing an internal resource, provide the relevant information here.

* **Internal Reference** (Required/Optional): 
  - If you are referencing an internal resource, provide the relevant information here.
