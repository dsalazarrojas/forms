# sports_media_content_partnership_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for partnership applications for sports media content partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Choose a category that best represents your sports media content (Football, Basketball, or Baseball).
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for further communication.
5. Select a date and time for your partnership meeting or meeting (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes about your partnership opportunity.
7. Choose one option from the "Select One" field for your partnership type.
8. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field for your partnership type (if applicable).
9. Enter a decimal value for your partnership requirements (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Application** (text, text, required): This is the name of your sports media content partnership.
* **Category** (select multiple, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple categories that best represent your sports media content (e.g., Football, Basketball, Baseball).
* **Name** (text, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (text, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Select a date for your partnership meeting or meeting (if applicable).
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Select a time for your partnership meeting or meeting (if applicable).
* **Notes** (text, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your partnership opportunity.
* **Select One** (select one, select one, optional): Choose one option from the "Select One" field for your partnership type.
* **Select Multiple** (select multiple, select multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field for your partnership type (if applicable).
* **Decimal** (number, number, optional): Enter a decimal value for your partnership requirements (if applicable).
* **Time Zone** (time, time, optional): Select a time zone for your partnership meeting or meeting (if applicable).
