# outreach-guest-post-submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit guest posts for publication on our platform. It collects information about the author, article, and categories of the guest post.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to provide a brief description of the guest post.
3. Upload any required files (if applicable).
4. Select the relevant categories for the guest post.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **title (Text)** (`guest-post-submission`, **Required**): Enter a title for the guest post that accurately represents its content.
* **description (Text)** (`description`, **Required**): Provide a brief description of the guest post, summarizing its main points.
* **author-bio (Text)** (`author-bio`, **Required**): Enter a brief bio of the author, including their name, title, and any relevant credentials.
* **author-blog (Text)** (`author-blog`, **Optional**): If applicable, provide the URL of the author's blog or website.
* **email (Email)** (`email`, **Required**): Enter the author's email address for contact purposes.
* **phone (Text)** (`phone`, **Optional**): Provide the author's phone number, if applicable.
* **article-link (Text)** (`article-link`, **Required**): Enter a link to the guest post article.
* **categories (Select Multiple)** (`categories_selected`, **Required**): Choose the relevant categories for the guest post from the list provided.
* **tags (Text)** (`tags`, **Optional**): Provide additional keywords or tags related to the guest post, separated by commas.
* **file (Text)** (`file`, **Optional**): If applicable, upload any supporting files or resources for the guest post.
* **draft (Text)** (`draft`, **Optional**): If you want to draft the article before final submission, enter a draft title and description.
* **bio-image (Text)** (`bio-image`, **Optional**): Provide a URL or link to the author's profile image.
