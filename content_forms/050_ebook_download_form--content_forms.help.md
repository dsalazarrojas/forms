# Ebook Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users download e-books by providing a secure and easy-to-use interface to gather necessary information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select whether you want to download the e-book ("Yes" or "No").
2. Provide your ebook ID number.
3. Enter your email address for e-book delivery and future updates.
4. Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
5. Enter the payment amount for the e-book download.
6. Select whether you want to make a payment ("Yes" or "No").
7. Add any additional notes for your order (optional).
8. Select the type of e-book you are downloading ("Yes" or "No").



## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ebook_download_form_page**: This field allows you to select whether you want to download the e-book. Choose "Yes" to initiate the download process.
* **ebookid**: This field is used to enter your ebook ID number, which we need to verify your e-book download request.
* **user-email**: This field is for your email address where we will send the e-book and any updates about it.
* **userphone**: This field is for your phone number, which we may use to contact you if there's an issue with your order.
* **payment-amount**: This field is for entering the payment amount for the e-book download. Please note that the payment amount is not required for download.
* **paymentmethod**: This field is to select whether you want to make a payment ("Yes" or "No"). If you choose "Yes", we'll guide you through the payment process.
* **order-notes**: This field is for any additional notes about your order. You can use this space to let us know about any specific requirements or preferences you may have.
* **ebooktype**: This field is to select the type of e-book you are downloading ("Yes" or "No"). This helps us ensure you receive the correct e-book.
