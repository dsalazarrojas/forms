# Digital Advertising Display Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Advertising Display Consent Form is designed to collect user information for digital advertising display purposes. This form is intended to gather personal details that will be used to display targeted advertisements on various digital platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For select_one fields, choose the correct option from the dropdown list.
3. Use the correct format for date fields (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Ensure that you are providing a valid email address for the email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1**: This is the form title and identifier.
* **First Name (Required)**: Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Street Address (Required)**: Please enter your street address in this field.
* **City (Required)**: Please enter the city where you reside in this field.
* **State Province (Required)**: Please choose the correct province or state from the dropdown list. 
    - Select One Option
    - Alberta
    - British Columbia
    - Manitoba
    - New Brunswick
    - Newfoundland and Labrador
    - Northwest Territories
    - Nova Scotia
    - Nunavut
    - Ontario
    - Prince Edward Island
    - Quebec
    - Saskatchewan
* **Post Office Box (Optional)**: This field is used for individuals who have a post office box. Enter your post office box number in this field.
* **Country (Required)**: Please enter the country where you reside in this field.
* **Email (Required)**: Please enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Phone (Required)**: Please enter your phone number in this field.
* **Date of Birth (Required)**: Please enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g. 01/01/1990).
* **Date of Death (Optional)**: Please enter your date of death if applicable in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g. 01/01/1990).
* **Time (Optional)**: This field is not applicable for most users. Please do not fill out this field unless specified otherwise.
* **Notes (Optional)**: This field is for any additional information you may want to provide.
