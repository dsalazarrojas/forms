# Couples Anthology Submission Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is intended for couples submitting their work to the anthology. It is designed to collect information necessary for processing and reviewing the submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter a valid phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the type of entry you are submitting (fiction or nonfiction).
5. Choose the genres of your work (select all that apply).
6. Enter the date of submission using the "Submission Date" field.
7. Choose the time of submission using the "Submission Time" field.
8. Enter the name of the anthology you are submitting to.
9. Provide the URL of the anthology (if applicable).
10. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, required): Select the type of entry you are submitting (fiction or nonfiction).
* **Genre** (`genre`, required): Choose all that apply from the provided options (Romance, Horror, Science Fiction, Mystery, Fantasy, Historical Fiction).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting your work.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, required): Choose the time of submission.
* **Anthology Name** (`anthology_name`, required): Enter the name of the anthology you are submitting to.
* **Anthology URL** (`anthology_url`, optional): Provide the URL of the anthology if applicable.
