# document-formatting-checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The document-formatting-checklist form is designed to help users evaluate and ensure that their content meets specific formatting and submission guidelines. This form guides the user through various stages of the review process, from style and branding guidelines to layout and submission requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the guidelines and requirements for your content.
2. Select the applicable options for each page according to your content's formatting and submission status.
3. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Make sure to review the submission guidelines carefully to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
