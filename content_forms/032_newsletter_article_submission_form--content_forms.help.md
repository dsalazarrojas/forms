# newsletter-article-submission-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect articles and associated information from users for a newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Article Title, Article Content, Submitter Name, and Submitter Email).
2. Provide accurate and original information for the article and submitter details.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
4. Note that some fields are optional, so you can leave them blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Article**: Enter a clear and concise title for the article you wish to submit.
* **Article Author**: Enter your name as the author of the submitted article.
* **Article Content**: Provide the main content of the article in a clear and concise manner.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the article.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Article Title** (optional): Enter a brief title for the submitted article.
* **Article Author** (optional): Enter your name as the author of the submitted article.
* **Article Content** (optional): Provide additional information or context about the submitted article.
