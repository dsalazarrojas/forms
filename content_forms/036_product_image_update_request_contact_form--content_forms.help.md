# product_image_update_request_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to update information related to a product image. Users fill out this form to provide details about a product image they would like to update or modify.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your contact information.
2. Provide your **Phone** number (optional).
3. Describe the reason for the **Image Change Request**.
4. Select the **Image Type** from the dropdown menu.
5. Upload a new **File** for the image (optional).
6. Enter the **Image URL** where the image can be found.
7. Provide a brief **Image Title** and **Image Description**.
8. Add a **Image Alt** text for accessibility purposes.
9. Enter the **Image Order**, **Image Width**, **Image Height**, **Image Source**, and **Image Tag**.
10. Select the **Image Category** and **Image Subcategory** (optional).
11. Enter the **Date Taken** and **Time Taken** for the image.
12. Enter the **Date Added** and **Time Added** when the image was added.
13. Identify the **Image User** who uploaded the image.
14. Select the **Image Status** as **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Pending**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
- **Image Change Request** (`image_change_request`, text, required): Describe the reason for the image update or modification.
- **Image Type** (`image_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of image being updated (e.g., Image 1, Image 2, Image 3).
- **File** (`file`, text, optional): Upload a new file for the image (optional).
- **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, required): Enter the URL where the image can be found.
- **Image Title** (`image_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the image.
- **Image Description** (`image_description`, text, required): Describe the image in a few words.
- **Image Alt** (`image_alt`, text, required): Add a text description for accessibility purposes.
- **Image Order** (`image_order`, number, required): Enter the order of the image.
- **Image Width** (`image_width`, number, required): Enter the width of the image.
- **Image Height** (`image_height`, number, required): Enter the height of the image.
- **Image Source** (`image_src`, text, required): Enter the source of the image.
- **Image Tag** (`image_tag`, text, required): Enter a tag for the image.
- **Image Category** (`image_category`, text, optional): Select the category of the image (optional).
- **Image Subcategory** (`image_subcategory`, text, optional): Select the subcategory of the image (optional).
- **Date Taken** (`image_date_taken`, date, required): Enter the date when the image was taken.
- **Time Taken** (`image_time_taken`, time, required): Enter the time when the image was taken.
- **Date Added** (`image_date_added`, date, required): Enter the date when the image was added.
- **Time Added** (`image_time_added`, time, required): Enter the time when the image was added.
- **Image User** (`image_user`, text, required): Identify the user who uploaded the image.
- **Image Status** (`image_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the image (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Make sure to select the correct **Image Type** from the dropdown menu.
- For **Date Taken** and **Time Taken**, use the correct date and time format.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the product team.
