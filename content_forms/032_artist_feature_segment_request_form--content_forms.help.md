# Artist Feature Segment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Feature Segment Request Form is a tool for artists to submit their information and preferences for feature requests in various media outlets.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself as an artist.
2. Choose the type of feature you are interested in.
3. Share your current project and its details.
4. Explain what makes your story unique.
5. Confirm your willingness to participate in the feature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): Enter your professional name as an artist or band.
* **Email Address** (`artist_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`artist_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Website or Portfolio URL** (`artist_website`, `text`, optional): Provide a link to your website or portfolio.
* **Genre or Artistic Style** (`genre_style`, `text`, required): Describe the type of art you create.
* **Brief Artist Biography** (`artist_bio`, `text`, required): Provide a 100-200 word description of your work and background.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have been creating art.
* **Notable Achievements or Recognition** (`notable_achievements`, `text`, optional): List any notable achievements or recognitions you have received.
* **Type of Feature Requested** (`feature_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of feature you are interested in (e.g., magazine article, podcast interview, video interview, etc.).
* **Suggested Feature Topic or Angle** (`feature_topic`, `text`, required): Explain the specific topic or angle you would like the feature to focus on.
* **Current Project or Recent Work** (`current_project`, `text`, required): Describe your current project or recent work.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, `text`, required): Provide details about your current project.
* **What Makes Your Story Unique?** (`unique_story`, `text`, required): Explain what makes your story unique and interesting.
* **Do You Have Photos or Media?** (`media_availability`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you have high-quality media available for the feature.
* **Availability for Interview or Shoot** (`access_flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Choose how flexible you are regarding availability for the feature.
* **Why Are You Ideal for a Feature?** (`why_feature_worthy`, `text`, required): Make a compelling case for why you are ideal for a feature.
* **Target Audience or Publication Type** (`target_audience`, `text`, optional): Suggest who would be the target audience or publication type for your feature.
* **I Agree to Participate in This Feature** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your willingness to participate in the feature.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information or questions you have.
