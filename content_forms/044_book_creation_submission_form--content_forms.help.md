# Book Creation Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Book Creation Submission Form is a tool for authors or creators to submit their book proposals to the editorial team. It allows users to provide detailed information about their book, including author details, book title, genre, synopsis, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the submission details.
2. Enter your author name and email address in the designated fields.
3. Provide the proposed book title, genre, and estimated word count.
4. Write a short summary of your book (synopsis) in the designated field.
5. Optionally, specify the target audience for your book.
6. Indicate the publication status of your book.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Author name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for editorial contact purposes.
* **Book title**: Enter the title of your book as you would like it to appear on the cover.
* **Genre**: Select the primary genre of your book from the options provided.
* **Word count estimate**: Enter an approximate total word count for your book.
* **Synopsis**: Write a short summary of your book (approx. 100-200 words).
* **Target audience**: If applicable, specify who your book is intended for (e.g. children, adults, etc.).
* **Publication status**: Indicate whether your book is self-published or has been previously published.
