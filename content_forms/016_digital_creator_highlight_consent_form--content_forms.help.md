# digital_creator_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for creators who want to share their digital content on our platform and participate in studies and research initiatives.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and purpose of the form carefully.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, making sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to this form** (1, text, optional): This field is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Creator Information** (2, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Do you consent to participate in this study** (3, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are willing to participate in studies and research initiatives. Select "No" if you are not.
* **Creator Consent** (4, select_multiple, required): Select all the consents you are willing to grant to us. Typically, this includes agreeing to share your work, participate in studies, and allow us to contact you.
* **I agree to participate** (5, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are willing to participate in studies and research initiatives. Select "No" if you are not.
* **Have you read the terms and conditions** (6, text, required): Enter "Yes" if you have read and understood the terms and conditions. This field is not meant to be filled out if you haven't read the terms and conditions, but rather to confirm your agreement to them.
* **Terms and Conditions agreement** (7, text, optional): This field is not meant to be filled out if you've already confirmed reading the terms and conditions. It's a redundant field to ensure that creators understand the purpose of this field. 

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, read the instructions carefully or contact our support team for assistance.
* Remember that your participation in studies and research initiatives will help us improve our platform and provide better experiences for all users.
