# Spell Card Creation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spell Card Creation Form is used to create new "Spell Card" entries within the system. This form allows users to provide details about a specific magical ability or power that can be used by players.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure that the system has not flagged any duplication or incoherent entries.
2.  Select the correct category for the new spell card.
3.  Provide a valid and descriptive title for the spell card in the "Name" field.
4.  Describe the spell card's effect in the "Effect" field.
5.  Specify the requirements needed to cast the spell in the "Casting Requirements" field.
6.  Choose the correct rarity for the spell card from the four options: "Rare", "Common", "Uncommon", or "Legendary" in the "Rarity" field.
7.  Upload or provide the image associated with the spell card in the "Image" field.
8.  If applicable, specify the time required to cast the spell in the "Casting Time" field.
9.  Enter the casting cost for the spell card in the "Casting Cost" field.
10.  Enter the mana cost for the spell card in the "Mana Cost" field.
11.  Add any additional information about the casting requirements in the "Casting Requirements Note" field.
12.  Include any player notes about the spell card in the "Player Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (text, required)
    - A descriptive title for the spell card, which should reflect its nature and properties.
*   **Effect** (text, required)
    - A description of the spell card's effect or outcome when used.
*   **Casting Requirements** (text, required)
    - The requirements that must be met to successfully cast the spell card.
*   **Rarity** (select_one, required)
    - The rarity level of the spell card, which can be: "Rare", "Common", "Uncommon", or "Legendary".
*   **Image** (text, required)
    - A link or upload to an image associated with the spell card.
*   **Casting Time** (time, optional)
    - The time required to cast the spell card, expressed in hours and minutes.
*   **Casting Cost** (number, required)
    - The cost to cast the spell card, which should be a numeric value.
*   **Mana Cost** (number, required)
    - The mana cost for the spell card, which should be a numeric value.
*   **Casting Requirements Note** (note, optional)
    - Additional information about the casting requirements of the spell card.
*   **Player Notes** (note, optional)
    - Additional information or notes from players about the spell card.
