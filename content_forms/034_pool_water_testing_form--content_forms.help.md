# Pool Water Testing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and record data on pool water testing. The form gathers information on pool type, water testing date, sample location, and other relevant details to help track and analyze pool water quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct pool type for your pool (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
2. Enter the date on which you performed the water testing (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Provide the location where the sample was taken.
4. Enter the time when the sample was collected (HH:MM:SS, 24-hour format).
5. Enter the size of the pool (number).
6. Indicate the water testing frequency (text input).
7. Enter the Chlorine Level reading.
8. Enter the PH Level reading.
9. Enter the Alkalinity Level reading.
10. Enter the Hardness Level reading.
11. Enter the Stabilizer Level reading.
12. Enter the Total Bacteria Count.
13. Enter the E. coli Count.
14. Indicate whether or not there are Cryptosporidium Parasites present.
15. List any other contaminants that may be present in the water.
16. Provide any additional comments.
17. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
18. Enter the date on which the form was submitted (MM/DD/YYYY).
19. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
20. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
21. Select the department that submitted the form (Management, Maintenance, or Other).
22. Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
23. Enter the ID number of the person who submitted the form.
24. Enter the location where the form was submitted from.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pool Type** (`pool_type`, select_one): Select the type of pool you are testing (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
* **Water Testing Date** (`water_testing_date`, date): Enter the date on which you performed the water testing (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Sample Location** (`sample_location`, text): Provide the location where the sample was taken.
* **Sample Time** (`sample_time`, time): Enter the time when the sample was collected (HH:MM:SS, 24-hour format).
* **Pool Size** (`pool_size`, number): Enter the size of the pool.
* **Water Testing Frequency** (`water_testing_frequency`, text): Indicate the water testing frequency.
* **Chlorine Level** (`chlorine_level`, number): Enter the Chlorine Level reading.
* **PH Level** (`ph_level`, number): Enter the PH Level reading.
* **Alkalinity Level** (`alkalinity_level`, number): Enter the Alkalinity Level reading.
* **Hardness Level** (`hardness_level`, number): Enter the Hardness Level reading.
* **Stabilizer Level** (`stabilizer_level`, number): Enter the Stabilizer Level reading.
* **Total Bacteria Count** (`total_bacteria_count`, number): Enter the Total Bacteria Count.
* **E. coli Count** (`e_coli_count`, number): Enter the E. coli Count.
* **Cryptosporidium Parasites** (`cryptosporidium_parasites`, select_one): Indicate whether or not there are Cryptosporidium Parasites present.
* **Other Contaminants** (`other_contaminants`, text): List any other contaminants that may be present in the water.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Provide any additional comments.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date): Enter the date on which the form was submitted (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Form Submitted By Email** (`form_submitted_by_email`, email): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By Phone** (`form_submitted_by_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By Department** (`form_submitted_by_department`, select_one): Select the department that submitted the form (Management, Maintenance, or Other).
* **Submitted By Title** (`submitted_by_title`, text): Enter the title of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted By ID Number** (`form_submitted_by_id_number`, text): Enter the ID number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Location** (`submitted_by_location`, text): Enter the location where the form was submitted from.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the provided explanations above.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department (Management or Maintenance).
