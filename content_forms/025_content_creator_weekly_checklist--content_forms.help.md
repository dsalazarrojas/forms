# Content Creator Weekly Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Creator Weekly Checklist form is designed to help content creators set goals, track their progress, and reflect on their performance at the end of each week. This form serves as a tool for creators to stay organized and focused on their content creation and planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Ensure that all fields marked as "Required" are filled in correctly. These fields include:
	* Creator Name
	* Week Start Date
	* Week End Date
	* Content Goal for Week
	* Target Platforms
	* Total Content Pieces Planned
	* Total Content Pieces Completed
	* Weekly Reflection
	* Next Week Priorities
2. **Track progress**: Update the status of each day's content creation and completion using the "Completed", "In Progress", "Not Started", or "Skipped" options.
3. **Provide details**: Write brief descriptions for each day's content plan and weekly reflection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Name**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Full name of content creator.
* **Week Start Date** (Week End Date): <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) The Monday (Sunday) of this week.
* **Content Goal for Week**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) A short description of your main goal for this week.
* **Target Platforms**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) A list of platforms where you plan to post content (Select multiple options).
* **Total Content Pieces Planned**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) The total number of content pieces planned for this week.
* **Total Content Pieces Completed**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) The total number of content pieces completed this week.
* **Monday Content Plan**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Brief description of content to be created on Monday.
* **Monday Status**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Status of Monday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Tuesday Content Plan**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Brief description of content to be created on Tuesday.
* **Tuesday Status**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Status of Tuesday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Wednesday Content Plan**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Brief description of content to be created on Wednesday.
* **Wednesday Status**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Status of Wednesday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Thursday Content Plan**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Brief description of content to be created on Thursday.
* **Thursday Status**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Status of Thursday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Friday Content Plan**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Brief description of content to be created on Friday.
* **Friday Status**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Status of Friday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Saturday Content Plan**: Optional. Brief description of content to be created on Saturday.
* **Saturday Status**: Optional. Status of Saturday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Sunday Content Plan**: Optional. Brief description of content to be created on Sunday.
* **Sunday Status**: Optional. Status of Sunday's content creation (Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Skipped).
* **Engagement Goals**: Optional. Description of engagement metrics you aim to achieve this week.
* **Collaborations This Week**: Optional. Description of any collaborations planned this week.
* **Challenges Faced**: Optional. Description of any challenges encountered this week.
* **Weekly Reflection**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Reflection on what went well and what could improve next week.
* **Next Week Priorities**: <i class="fas fa-asterisk"></i>(Required) Description of your priorities for next week.

## Tips
* Use this form to help you stay organized and focused on your content creation and planning goals.
* Review and update your progress regularly to ensure you meet your goals.
* Reflect on your performance and identify areas for improvement.
* Use the provided fields to track and adjust your strategies as needed.
* Consider using this form as a starting point and customizing it to fit your specific needs and preferences.
