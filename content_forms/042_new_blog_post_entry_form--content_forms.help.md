# new_blog_post_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to create new blog posts. It gathers information about the blog post's title, categories, description, author, and tags. The form is intended to be a simple and straightforward way to submit blog posts for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `first_name` field with your first name.
2. Enter a valid `email` address.
3. Enter the `blog_post_title` of your new blog post.
4. Select one or more categories for your blog post from the `blog_post_categories` dropdown menu.
5. Write a brief `blog_post_description` of your new blog post.
6. Choose a date for the post's `publish_date` using the calendar icon.
7. Select the `author` of your blog post from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose one or more tags for your blog post from the `blog_post_tags` dropdown menu.
9. Click the `submit` button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **first_name** (Text, Optional)
A field to enter your first name.

### **email** (Email, Optional)
Enter a valid email address associated with your account.

### **blog_post_title** (Text, Optional)
Enter a title for your new blog post.

### **blog_post_categories** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Select one or more categories for your blog post from the available options.

### **blog_post_description** (Text, Optional)
Write a brief description of your new blog post.

### **publish_date** (Date, Optional)
Choose a date for your blog post using the calendar icon.

### **author** (Select One, Optional)
Select the author of your blog post from the available options.

### **blog_post_tags** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose one or more tags for your blog post from the available options.

### **submit** (Time, Optional)
Not applicable in this context. This field was likely incorrectly labeled as "Time", but appears to be a submit button. 

Please select the submit button to complete and submit your form.
