# News Article Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit news articles with a set of structured information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields: * Category: Select a category for your news article. * Article Checkbox Group: Select one or more of the options provided. * Article Dropdown: Select one of the options provided.
2. Fill in non-required fields as necessary: * Article Title: Enter a brief title for your news article. * Article Content: Enter a brief description of your news article. * Article Author: Enter your name as the author of the news article. * Article Email: Enter your email address. * Article Phone: Enter your phone number. * Article Url: Enter a URL for the news article. * Article Image Url: Enter a URL for the article image. * Attachment: Enter a file to attach with your news article. * Article Video Url: Enter a URL for the article video. * Article Audio Url: Enter a URL for the article audio. * Article Gallery: Select one or more of the options provided. * Article Note: Enter a note for your news article. * Article Text Area: Enter a longer description of your news article. * Article Date Picker: Select a date for your news article. * Article Time Picker: Select a time for your news article. * Article Checkbox Group: Select one or more of the options provided.
3. Review and submit the form.
