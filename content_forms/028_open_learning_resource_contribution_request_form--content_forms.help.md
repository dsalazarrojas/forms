# Open Learning Resource Contribution Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Learning Resource Contribution Request Form is used to collect information about open learning resources that you want to contribute. Please fill in the form with the required details about your resource, and we will review and process it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit Form" button to start filling in the form.
2. Enter the title of your open learning resource in the "Title" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of your resource in the "Description" field.
4. Select the license type of your resource from the list of options in the "License" field.
5. Choose the categories that best match your resource from the list in the "Categories" field.
6. Enter relevant keywords for your resource in the "Keywords" field.
7. Upload your resource file in the "File" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, optional): This is the start of the form where you can begin filling in the details.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your open learning resource.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): This is a detailed description of your resource, helping us understand its content.
* **License** (`license`, select_one, optional): Choose the license type of your resource from the list of options (e.g., License, CC0, CC-BY, etc.).
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select the categories that best match your resource (e.g., Mathematics, Science, Technology, Arts, etc.).
* **Keywords** (`keywords`, text, optional): Enter relevant keywords for your resource.
* **File** (`fileupload`, text, optional): Upload your resource file.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Ensure that the license and categories match the actual license and categories of your resource.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
