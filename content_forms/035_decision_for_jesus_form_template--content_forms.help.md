# Decision For Jesus Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect information in various fields related to a "Decision For Jesus" context, which can be used for personal or business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering a title for your form, if desired.
2. Select a category (e.g., Christian, Catholic, Protestant) to associate with the form.
3. Enter a description of the decision-making process or context.
4. If necessary, enter the date and time of the decision.
5. Enter the name(s) involved in the decision.
6. Enter the email address(es) associated with the decision.
7. If necessary, enter phone number(s) related to the decision.
8. Provide any additional notes about the decision.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Decision For Jesus Form Template** (`form_title`, note, optional): Enter a title for your form, if desired. This is a free-text field to provide a brief description of the form's purpose or context.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category to associate with the form (e.g., Christian, Catholic, Protestant).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the decision-making process or context.
* **Date** (`date_1`, date, optional): Enter the date of the decision.
* **Time** (`time_1`, time, optional): Enter the time of the decision.
* **Name** (`name_1`, text, optional): Enter the name(s) involved in the decision.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple names involved in the decision (e.g., John, Jane, Bob).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address(es) associated with the decision.
* **Email 1** (`email_1`, select_one, optional): Select the email address(es) associated with the decision from a list of options (e.g., example@gmail.com, example2@gmail.com, example3@gmail.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number(s) related to the decision.
* **Phone 1** (`phone_1`, select_one, optional): Select the phone number related to the decision (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', 'Other').
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about the decision.
