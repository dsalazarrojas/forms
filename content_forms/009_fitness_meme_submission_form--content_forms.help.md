## Fitness Meme Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Provide a title for the meme.
3. Enter the URL of the meme image.
4. Describe the meme in detail.
5. Fill in the other fields as requested, providing any additional information about the creator and event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meme Title** (`meme_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the meme.
* **Meme Image URL** (`meme_image_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the meme image.
* **Meme Description** (`meme_description`, text, required): Describe the meme in detail.
* **Creator Name** (`creator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the creator of the meme.
* **Creator Email** (`creator_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the creator.
* **Gym Name** (`gym_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the gym where the meme is relevant.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the event related to the meme.
* **Event End Date** (`event_ending_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the event related to the meme.
* **Submission Note** (`submission_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional note about the submission.

Note: Fields not marked as required may be left blank if not applicable.
