# Article Submission Tool EntranceUniversity - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a tool for students to submit their articles to EntranceUniversity. The form collects necessary information about the student and their article, including submission details, article content, and author information. It ensures that each submission meets the required standards and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself and your article.
2. Upload your article in DOC, DOCX, or PDF format.
3. Review and confirm that all information is accurate and complete before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter your university email address.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your university student ID number.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field`, `text`, required): Enter your academic discipline.
* **Article Title** (`article_title`, `text`, required): Enter the complete title of your article.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_title`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary topic or discipline of your article.
* **Word Count** (`article_length`, `number`, required): Enter the approximate length of your article.
* **Article Summary** (`article_summary`, `text`, required): Provide a brief overview of your article (100-200 words).
* **Main Arguments or Contributions** (`main_arguments`, `text`, required): Describe the key points of your article.
* **Research Methods Used** (`research_methods`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the research methods used in your article (if applicable).
* **Upload Article Document** (`article_document`, `text`, required): Upload your article in DOC, DOCX, or PDF format.
* **References and Bibliography Included** (`references_included`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your article includes citations and references (if applicable).
* **Faculty Advisor Name** (`faculty_advisor`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your supervising professor or advisor.
* **Faculty Advisor Approval** (`advisor_approval`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm that your advisor has reviewed your article.
* **This is original work not previously published** (`original_work`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that this is your original writing and not previously published.
* **I consent to publication in EntranceUniversity** (`publication_consent`, `select_one`, required): Grant permission to publish your article in EntranceUniversity.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other information for the editorial team (if necessary).
* **I confirm all information is accurate and complete** (`submission_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Final confirmation that all information is accurate and complete before submitting.
