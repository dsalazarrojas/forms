# Content Model Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for content modeling work. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to increase the chances of being considered for modeling work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, mailing address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Provide your modeling experience and interest in different content types.
3. Share your portfolio URL and Instagram handle.
4. Specify your height, weight, eye color, hair color, and clothing and shoe size.
5. Mention any special skills you have.
6. Choose your availability for work and willingness to travel.
7. If you have worked with other agencies, mention them.
8. Tell us about your interest in working with our agency.
9. Specify the date of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, **Required**): Fill in your legal first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Type your best email for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**): Use MM/DD/YYYY format to specify your date of birth.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, **Required**): Enter your street address where you live.
* **City** (`city`, text, **Required**): Fill in your city name.
* **State** (`state`, text, **Required**): Enter your state name.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, **Required**): Type in your zip code.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, **Required**): Mention the name of the modeling agency you have worked with.
* **Modeling Experience** (`modeling_experience`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your experience level in modeling work.
* **Content Type Interest** (`content_type_interest`, select_one, **Required**): Select the type of content you are interested in creating.
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, **Required**): Link to your portfolio or social media.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, text, **Optional**): Enter your Instagram username.
* **Instagram Followers** (`instagram_followers`, select_one, **Optional**): Approximate your follower count.
* **Height** (`height`, text, **Required**): Enter your height in feet and inches or cm.
* **Weight** (`weight`, text, **Optional**): Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
* **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your eye color.
* **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, select_one, **Required**): Select your hair color.
* **Clothing Size** (`clothing_size`, text, **Optional**): Mention your typical clothing size.
* **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, text, **Optional**): Enter your shoe size.
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, text, **Optional**): Mention any special talents or skills you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, **Required**): Choose your availability for work.
* **Willing to Travel** (`willing_to_travel`, select_one, **Required**): Select your willingness to travel for work.
* **Previous Agencies** (`previous_agencies`, text, **Optional**): If you have worked with other agencies, mention them.
* **Why Do You Want to Work With Us?** (`why_work_with_us`, text, **Required**): Tell us about your interest in working with our agency.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, **Required**): Specify the date of your application.
