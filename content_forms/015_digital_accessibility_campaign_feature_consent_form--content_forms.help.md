# digital_accessibility_campaign_feature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the digital accessibility campaign feature. Please fill out this form to participate in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Check the "Featured" section to select the feature you are interested in.
3. Provide a brief description of your participation in the "Participant Details" field.
4. Choose your consent in the "Consent" section.
5. List your campaign contributions in the "Campaign Contributions" field.
6. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field (optional).
7. Select the tools you are interested in using for the campaign feature in the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. Provide the date of your participation in the "Date" field (YYYY-MM-DD format).
9. If desired, add notes in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the categories and tags relevant to your participation in the "Categories" and "Tags" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, number, required): Enter your phone number (10 digits).
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe your participation in the campaign.
- **Featured** (`featured`, select_one, required): Select the feature you are interested in.
- **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate your consent.
- **Campaign Contributions** (`campaign_contributions`, text, required): List your campaign contributions.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, required): Select the tools you are interested in using for the campaign feature.
- **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your participation in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your participation (format: HH:MM).
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about your participation.
- **Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, optional): Select the categories relevant to your participation.
