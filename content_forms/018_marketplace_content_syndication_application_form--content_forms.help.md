# Marketplace Content Syndication Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for content providers to submit their content for syndication on our marketplace. It helps us gather necessary information about the content and the provider. Please provide accurate and complete information to avoid delays or rejections.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully, ensuring you have provided accurate information for each field.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have not skipped any essential details.
3. Submit the form only when you have completed all required fields (indicated by the `*` symbol next to the label).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Content Provider** (`content_provider`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your company or organization name.
	+ This field is used to identify your content provider entity.
* **Marketplace Info** (`marketplace_info`, select_one, optional)
	+ Select one of the following marketplaces:
		+ Amazon
		+ Apple
		+ Google
		+ Facebook
* **Content Details** (`content_details`, text, optional)
	+ Provide a brief description of the content you want to syndicate.
* **Requirements** (`requirements`, text, optional)
	+ List any specific requirements for the content syndication process.
* **Content Requirements** (`content_requirements`, text, optional)
	+ List any specific requirements or restrictions for the content.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional)
	+ Provide any additional information about the content or content provider.
* **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text, optional)
	+ Provide information about your partnership with the content provider.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your contact information for communication and follow-up.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		+ Submit
		+ Other (Please provide a reason for non-standard submission)

## Tips

* Please ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* We will review and contact you within 24-48 hours after submission.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
