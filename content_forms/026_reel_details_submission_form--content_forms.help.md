# Reel Details Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reel Details Submission Form is used to provide additional information about a video or image reel. This form helps in maintaining data accuracy and integrity by ensuring that all required details are submitted correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Reel Title field with the title of the reel.
2. Provide a descriptive Reel Description.
3. If applicable, enter the Reel Link.
4. Select the Reel Tags as per your preference.
5. Enter the Reel Image URL if the reel is an image.
6. Enter the Reel Video URL if the reel is a video.
7. Choose the Reel Video Type (e.g., Video, Image, Live, Live Stream, or Carousel).
8. For Reel Video Length, select the length of the video.
9. For Reel Video Format, select the format of the video (e.g., mp4, mpeg, flv, webm, avi).
10. For Reel Audio Format, select the audio format of the video (e.g., mp3, flac, m4a, m4b).
11. Enter the Reel Image Width and Height.
12. Select the Reel Image Type (e.g., JPEG, GIF, PNG, JPG).
13. Choose the Reel Image Size (e.g., small, medium, large).
14. Select the Reel Video Bitrate (e.g., high, medium, low).
15. Select the Reel Video Frame Rate (e.g., high, medium, low).
16. Select the Reel Audio Bitrate (e.g., high, medium, low).
17. Choose the Reel Video Codec (e.g., h.264, mpeg4, x264, xvid).
18. Select the Reel Audio Codec (e.g., mp3, m4a, aac, wav).
19. Enter the Reel Capture Width.
20. Enter the Reel Capture Height.
21. Select the Reel Capture Duration (e.g., short, medium, long).
22. Enter the Reel Capture Date.
23. Enter the Reel Capture Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reel Details**: A text field for a brief description of the reel.
* **Reel Title**: A text field for the title of the reel.
* **Reel Link**: A text field for the link to the reel.
* **Reel Tags**: A multiple select field for selecting relevant tags for the reel.
* **Reel Image URL**: A text field for the URL of the reel image.
* **Reel Video URL**: A text field for the URL of the reel video.
* **Reel Video Type**: A select-one field for selecting the type of reel (e.g., Video, Image, Live, Live Stream, Carousel).
* **Reel Video Length**: A number field for selecting the length of the video.
* **Reel Video Format**: A select-one field for selecting the format of the video (e.g., mp4, mpeg, flv, webm, avi).
* **Reel Audio Format**: A select-one field for selecting the audio format of the video (e.g., mp3, flac, m4a, m4b).
* **Reel Image Width**: A number field for entering the width of the reel image.
* **Reel Image Height**: A number field for entering the height of the reel image.
* **Reel Image Type**: A select-one field for selecting the type of reel image (e.g., JPEG, GIF, PNG, JPG).
* **Reel Image Size**: A select-one field for selecting the size of the reel image (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **Reel Video Bitrate**: A select-one field for selecting the bitrate of the video (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Reel Video Frame Rate**: A select-one field for selecting the frame rate of the video (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Reel Audio Bitrate**: A select-one field for selecting the bitrate of the audio (e.g., high, medium, low).
* **Reel Video Codec**: A select-one field for selecting the video codec (e.g., h.264, mpeg4, x264, xvid).
* **Reel Audio Codec**: A select-one field for selecting the audio codec (e.g., mp3, m4a, aac, wav).
* **Reel Capture Width**: A number field for entering the capture width of the reel.
* **Reel Capture Height**: A number field for entering the capture height of the reel.
* **Reel Capture Duration**: A select-one field for selecting the duration of the capture (e.g., short, medium, long).
* **Reel Capture Date**: A date field for entering the capture date of the reel.
* **Reel Capture Time**: A time field for entering the capture time of the reel.
