# Funniest Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Funniest Content Submission Form is designed for users to share their humorous creations, such as jokes, stories, or videos. The form is meant to be a fun and engaging way to showcase user-generated content on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter a funny joke or story**: In the first text field, enter a joke or story that you think is hilarious. Be creative and have fun with it!
2. **Add a funny picture or video**: In the second text field, provide a URL or upload a funny picture or video that complements your joke or story.
3. **Select content type**: In the third field, choose the type of content you're submitting: Joke, Story, Video, or Picture.
4. **Submit your form**: In the fourth text field, enter a brief description of your submission, such as a title or a tagline.
5. **Enter your contact information**: In the last three fields, provide your email address, phone number, and a date and time you are available to discuss your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter a funny joke or story**: This field is for you to share your original humor content. Try to keep it concise and clear.
* **Add a funny picture or video**: In this field, provide a URL or upload a file of a funny picture or video that complements your joke or story.
* **Select content type**: Choose the type of content you're submitting: Joke, Story, Video, or Picture.
* **Submit your form**: In this field, enter a brief description of your submission, such as a title or a tagline.
* **Email**: This field is for you to enter your email address so we can contact you about your submission.
* **Phone**: This field is for you to enter your phone number so we can reach out to you about your submission.
* **Date**: In this field, choose a date and time you are available to discuss your submission.
* **Time**: In this field, choose a specific time you are available to discuss your submission.
