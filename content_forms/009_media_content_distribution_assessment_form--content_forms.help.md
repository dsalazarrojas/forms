# Media Content Distribution Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Content Distribution Assessment Form is used to evaluate the media content distribution channels used in a media campaign. This includes assessing the performance of television, radio, print, digital, and online channels.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the media content distribution channels that were used in your campaign. 
    * Media Content Channels: Choose all the channels used, such as TV, Radio, Print, Digital, or Online.
2. Select the media performance metric channels used in your campaign. 
    * Media Performance Metric Channels: Choose all the metrics relevant to your campaign, such as Engagement, Reach, Frequency, or Cost per mille.
3. Select the audience targeting channels used in your campaign. 
    * Audience Targeting Channels: Choose all the channels used, such as Demographics, Interests, Behaviors, or Lookalike audiences.
4. Enter the performance metrics for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Performance: Enter the performance metrics for each channel, such as Engagement, Reach, Frequency, Cost per mille.
5. Enter the cost for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Cost: Enter the cost for each channel.
6. Enter the frequency for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Frequency: Enter the frequency for each channel.
7. Enter the engagement for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Engagement: Enter the engagement for each channel.
8. Enter the impressions for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Impressions: Enter the impressions for each channel.
9. Enter the reach for each channel. 
    * Media Content Distribution Channels Reach: Enter the reach for each channel.
10. Enter the total media impressions, reach, views, engagement, and frequencies. 
    * Total Media Impressions, Reach, Views, Engagement, and Frequencies: Enter the total numbers for each metric.
11. Enter the average media engagement rate. 
    * Average Media Engagement Rate: Enter the average engagement rate for your campaign.
11. Enter any additional notes. 
    * Notes: Enter any additional notes or comments about your campaign.
