# Creative Play Idea Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your creative play idea and help us review and consider it for potential inclusion in our creative play offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any other details we need to know.
2. Provide a detailed description of your creative play idea, including any relevant context or background information.
3. Choose the category that best fits your idea (art, music, or drama).
4. Select up to two activity types that your idea falls under (art, music, or dance).
5. (Optional) If you have any materials or resources needed to implement your idea, describe them in the text box.
6. (Optional) If you have any specific location or time requirements, describe them in the text box.
7. Choose whether you have any files to upload with your submission (Yes or No).
8. Finally, ensure you have selected the correct category and activity types for your idea, and that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info**: This is where you will input your name and any other identifying information we need to know.
* **play_idea**: Provide a detailed description of your creative play idea, including any relevant context or background information.
* **category**: Choose the category that best fits your idea (art, music, or drama).
* **activity_type**: Select up to two activity types that your idea falls under (art, music, or dance).
* **description**: (Optional) If you have any materials or resources needed to implement your idea, describe them here.
* **materials**: (Optional) If you have any specific location or time requirements, describe them here.
* **location**: (Optional) This field is only required if you have specific location requirements.
* **time**: (Optional) This field is only required if you have specific time requirements.
* **date**: Please select the date on which you'd like to submit this play idea. This is a required field.
* **uploaded_files**: (Optional) If you have files to share with your submission, select 'Yes' here, and we'll review them for further consideration.
