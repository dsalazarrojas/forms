# AI Assistant Press Release Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Assistant Press Release Request Form is used to request a press release for a company announcement, product launch, partnership, research, award, or executive announcement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name and email address in the "Full name" and "Email address" fields, respectively.
2.  Provide your phone number (optional) in the "Phone number" field.
3.  Enter your department or organization name in the "Department or organization" field.
4.  Enter a concise headline (up to 20 words) for the press release in the "Press release headline" field.
5.  Write a one-paragraph executive summary of the announcement in the "Executive summary" field.
6.  Choose the type of announcement (e.g., product launch, partnership, research, etc.) in the "Type of announcement" field.
7.  Select the target audience for the press release (e.g., media, customers, partners, etc.) in the "Target audience" field.
8.  Choose the key messages you want to include in the press release (up to 5) in the "Key messages" field.
9.  If the content is under embargo, select the embargo status and provide the embargo date.
10.  If you need to attach any supporting assets (e.g., high-res images, logos, etc.), select them in the "Supporting assets" field.
11.  If a draft press release is available, indicate that it's "Yes, attached" or provide it later.
12.  List the filenames of the attachments if any in the "Attachments included" field.
13.  If an official quote or spokesperson statement is required, select "True" in the "Spokesperson required" field.
14.  If an official quote or spokesperson statement is required, provide the spokesperson's name and title in the "Suggested spokesperson" field.
15.  Select the preferred distribution channels for the press release (e.g., company newsroom, wire service, etc.) in the "Distribution channels" field.
16.  Select all the team members required for approval before publishing the press release in the "Required approvals" field.
17.  Enter the required by date (deadline) for the press release in the "Required by" field.
18.  Enter the estimated budget for the distribution or assets in the "Estimated budget" field (optional).
19.  Mention any risks, compliance notes, or constraints related to the press release in the "Risks or constraints" field.
20.  Provide specific media outlets or journalists to target in the "Media list targeting" field (optional).
21.  Add any additional comments or background information in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (id: 2, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
*   **Email address** (id: 3, required): Enter your work email address for approval and follow-up communications.
*   **Phone number** (id: 4, optional): Enter your work phone number for follow-up calls.
*   **Department or organization** (id: 5, required): Enter your department or organization name.
*   **Press release headline** (id: 6, required): Enter a concise headline (up to 20 words) for the press release.
*   **Executive summary** (id: 7, required): Write a one-paragraph executive summary of the announcement.
*   **Type of announcement** (id: 8, required): Choose the type of announcement (e.g., product launch, partnership, research, etc.).
*   **Target audience** (id: 9, required): Choose the primary audience for the press release (e.g., media, customers, partners, etc.).
*   **Key messages** (id: 10, required): Select up to 5 key messages you want to include in the press release.
*   **Embargo status** (id: 11, required): Select if content is under embargo.
*   **Embargo date** (id: 12, optional): Leave blank if not under embargo.
*   **Preferred publish time** (id: 13, optional): Optional time for embargo or scheduling.
*   **Supporting assets** (id: 14, optional): Indicate which assets will be attached or supplied.
*   **Draft included** (id: 15, required): Indicate if a draft press release is available or if it will be provided later.
*   **Attachments included** (id: 16, optional): List filenames of attachments if any.
*   **Spokesperson required** (id: 17, required): Select if an official quote or spokesperson statement is required.
*   **Suggested spokesperson** (id: 18, optional): If an official quote or spokesperson statement is required, provide the spokesperson's name and title.
*   **Distribution channels** (id: 19, optional): Select all distribution channels to be used (e.g., company newsroom, wire service, etc.).
*   **Required approvals** (id: 20, required): Select all team members required for approval before publishing the press release.
*   **Required by** (id: 21, required): Enter the deadline for the press release.
*   **Estimated budget** (id: 22, optional): Enter the estimated budget for distribution or assets in USD.
*   **Risks or constraints** (id: 23, optional): Mention any risks, compliance notes, or constraints related to the press release.
*   **Media list targeting** (id: 24, optional): Provide specific media outlets or journalists to target.
*   **Additional comments** (id: 25, optional): Add any additional comments or background information.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
*   The communications team will review and approve the press release before publishing it.
*   The required team members will be notified for approval before the press release is published.
*   The press release will be distributed through the selected channels.
