# Training Request Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit a training request for approval. This form will be reviewed and approved by the designated approval manager, and once approved, it will be scheduled for the requested training date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and email address in the **Submitter Name** and **Submitter Email** fields.
* Provide your phone number in the **Submitter Phone** field, if it is applicable.
* Enter the date you would like to schedule the training in the **Training Date** field.
* Specify the time you would like to have the training in the **Training Time** field.
* Enter the duration of the training in the **Training Duration** field.
* Enter a brief description of the training topic in the **Training Subject** field.
* Select your department from the **Submitter Department** field.
* Select your manager from the **Submitter Manager** field.
* Select the person who will be responsible for approving the training from the **Approval Manager** field.
* Enter the date you would like the approval to be made on in the **Approval Date** field.
* Select the time you would like the approval to be made on in the **Approval Time** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Request Approval Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your request.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you have one, to facilitate communication.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the training.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time you would like to have the training.
* **Training Duration**: Enter the duration of the training in number of minutes.
* **Training Subject**: Enter a brief description of the training topic.
* **Submitter Department**: Select your department from the list to help us route your request.
* **Submitter Manager**: Select your manager from the list to facilitate communication.
* **Approval Manager**: Select the person who will be responsible for approving the request.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date you would like the approval to be made on.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the time you would like the approval to be made on.
