# Material Specification Approval Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Specification Approval Request form is used by customers to submit their material specification requests to the approver for approval. The form collects necessary information about the customer and the material specification, allowing the approver to make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a detailed description of the material specification in the Material Description field.
3. Select the Material Type from the options provided.
4. Upload a material image if required.
5. Attach any relevant material files.
6. Provide your signature in the Customer Signature field.
7. Fill in the Approver's Name, Email, and Phone number.
8. Provide your signature in the Approver Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Material Specification Approval Request Title (1)**: A title for the material specification approval request.
* **Customer Name (2)**: The name of the customer submitting the request.
* **Customer Email (3)**: The customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone (4)**: The customer's phone number.
* **Material Description (5)**: A detailed description of the material specification.
* **Material Specification (6)**: The material specification details.
* **Material Type (7)**: The type of material being requested.
* **Request Date (8)**: The date the request was made.
* **Request Time (9)**: The time the request was made.
* **Material Image (10)**: An optional material image.
* **Material File (11)**: An optional material file.
* **Customer Signature (12)**: The customer's signature, indicating their confirmation of the request.
* **Approver Name (13)**: The name of the person reviewing the request.
* **Approver Email (14)**: The email address of the person reviewing the request.
* **Approver Phone (15)**: The phone number of the person reviewing the request.
* **Approver Signature (16)**: The signature of the person reviewing the request, indicating their approval or decline of the request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the material specification details for accuracy.
* Ensure the material image and file are attached if required.
* Sign the customer signature field to confirm the request.
* Sign the approver signature field to indicate approval or decline of the request.
* Be sure to keep the approver informed of any changes or updates to the material specification.
