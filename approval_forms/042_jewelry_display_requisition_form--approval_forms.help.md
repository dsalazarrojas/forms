# Jewelry Display Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a jewelry display for an event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help us process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, but you can skip fields if they are not applicable to your event.
2. Review the fields below for specific instructions on what each field is for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **Jewelry Type** (`jewelry_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of jewelry you are requesting to be displayed. Options are:
	+ Ceramic Jewelry
	+ Fashion Jewelry
	+ Precious Jewelry
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of jewelry you are requesting to be displayed.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, optional): Enter the unit price of the jewelry you are requesting to be displayed.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, optional): Enter the total price of the jewelry you are requesting to be displayed.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event you are requesting a jewelry display for.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event type (e.g. exhibition, sale, etc.).
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of the event (e.g. active, cancelled, etc.).
