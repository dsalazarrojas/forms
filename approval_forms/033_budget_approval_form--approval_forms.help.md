# Budget Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Approval Form is used to request and track budget approval for new requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2.  Enter the budget code or number of the request.
3.  Provide your full name and email as the submitter.
4.  Enter the submission date of the request.
5.  Specify the fiscal year of the request.
6.  Select the department that this request is for.
7.  Enter the budget amount requested.
8.  Describe the item or expense being requested.
9.  Explain why this request is necessary and its impact.
10. Select the department head and finance director as approvers for each field.
11. Enter the department head's name and their approval or rejection of the request.
12. Enter the finance director's name and their approval or rejection of the request.
13. Enter the final approved amount (if approved).
14. Enter any additional conditions or notes (if applicable).
15. Select the final approval status of the request.
16. Enter the date the request was authorized (if approved).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Budget Code or Number (budget_code)**: Enter the budget code or number associated with this request.
* **Submitted By (submitter_name)**: Enter your full name as the submitter of this request.
* **Submitter Email (submitter_email)**: Enter your email address as the submitter of this request.
* **Submission Date (submission_date)**: Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Fiscal Year (fiscal_year)**: Enter the fiscal year of the request.
* **Department (department)**: Select the department this request is for.
* **Budget Amount Requested (budget_amount)**: Enter the amount of the request.
* **Item or Expense Description (item_description)**: Describe the item or expense being requested.
* **Justification for Request (justification)**: Explain why this request is necessary and its impact.
* **Department Head Name (department_head_name)**: Enter the name of the department head approving or rejecting this request.
* **Department Head Approval (department_head_approval)**: Select whether the department head approves or rejects this request.
* **Finance Director Name (finance_director_name)**: Enter the name of the finance director approving or rejecting this request.
* **Finance Director Approval (finance_director_approval)**: Select whether the finance director approves or rejects this request.
* **Final Approved Amount (final_budget_amount)**: Enter the final approved amount (if approved).
* **Conditions or Special Notes (conditions_or_notes)**: Enter any additional conditions or notes for this request (if applicable).
* **Final Approval Status (approval_status)**: Select the final approval status of the request.
* **Authorization Date (authorized_signature_date)**: Enter the date this request was authorized (if approved).
