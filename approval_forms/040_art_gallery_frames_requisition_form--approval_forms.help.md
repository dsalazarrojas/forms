## Art Gallery Frames Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Frames Requisition Form is used by departments to request new art frames for exhibitions or educational purposes. The form helps the art gallery's procurement team to track and process these requests in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a unique internal tracking number for the requisition.
2.  Select the department requesting the frames.
3.  Provide a detailed description of the items needed, including specifications and quantity.
4.  Explain the justification for the requisition.
5.  Estimate the cost of the frames.
6.  Select the budget allocation code for the frames.
7.  Obtain manager approval by selecting 'Approved' in the manager approval field.
8.  Optionally, add any additional comments for the procurement team.
9.  Select a delivery date for the frames (if known).
10. Wait for final approval from the director or finance department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requisition number** (1, text, **required**): A unique internal tracking number for the requisition.
*   **Department requesting frames** (2, text, **required**): The department responsible for requesting the frames, such as Curatorial Education or Exhibitions.
*   **Item description** (3, text, **required**): A detailed description of the frames needed, including specifications and quantity.
*   **Justification for requisition** (4, text, **required**): A clear explanation of why the frames are being requisitioned.
*   **Estimated cost** (5, number, **required**): The projected expense for the frames.
*   **Account code** (6, text, **required**): The budget allocation code for the frames.
*   **Manager approval** (7, select_one, **required**): Select 'Approved' to indicate that the manager has given their approval.
*   **Procurement comments** (8, text, optional): Any additional comments or information for the procurement team.
*   **Delivery ETA** (9, date, optional): The expected arrival date for the frames.
*   **Final approval** (10, select_one, **required**): Select 'Approved' to indicate that the director or finance department has given their final approval.
