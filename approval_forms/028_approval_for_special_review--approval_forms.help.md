# Approval For Special Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for special reviews, allowing reviewers to evaluate specific projects or items within the organization. This form is a critical component in the review process, ensuring that necessary details are collected and submitted properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Submitter Name** field with the name of the individual submitting the review request.
2. Enter the **Submission Date** in the required date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Select the **Department or Division** relevant to the review request, if applicable.
4. Provide the **Email Address** of the person submitting the review request.
5. Choose the type of review requested from the **Type of Review Requested** dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Policy Review
	* Compliance Review
	* Quality Review
	* Safety Review
	* Other
6. Enter the **Subject Area or Item for Review** that requires evaluation.
7. Provide a clear and concise **Reason for Special Review** to support the review request.
8. Select the **Priority Level** of the review request from the dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Urgent
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
9. If applicable, enter the **Needed By Date** for the review completion.
10. Check if **Supporting Documentation Provided** is available.
11. Enter any additional **Additional Context or Information** relevant to the review request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submission Information**: This field provides a brief overview of the submission, allowing submitters to give context to their review request.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the individual submitting the review request.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date the submission was made.
* **Department or Division**: Select the department or division related to the review request, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the review request.
* **Type of Review Requested**: Choose the type of review requested from the dropdown menu.
* **Subject Area or Item for Review**: Enter the specific area or item requiring review.
* **Reason for Special Review**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the review request.
* **Priority Level**: Select the priority level of the review request from the dropdown menu.
* **Needed By Date**: Enter the date by which the review is required to be completed.
* **Supporting Documentation Provided**: Check if supporting documentation is available.
* **Additional Context or Information**: Enter any additional context or information relevant to the review request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth review process.
* If supporting documentation is not available, please indicate so to facilitate the reviewer's understanding.
* Enter dates in the required date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
