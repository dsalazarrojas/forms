# Collections Approval Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collections Approval Request form is used to request approval for collections actions on outstanding debts. This form captures essential information about the debtor's account and the collection process. Fill out this form to submit a collection request to the necessary approval authority.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields**: Each field with the label "(required)" is required to be filled out.
2. **Enter correct contact information**: Ensure the requester's and customer's contact information is accurate.
3. **Select relevant options**: Choose the correct options from select fields such as account type, delinquency status, and collection action.
4. **Attach supporting documents**: Select all applicable supporting documents that are required for the collection process.
5. **Provide detailed justification**: Explain the reason for the collection request.
6. **Submit the form**: Once all fields are filled out, click "Submit" to submit the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester Full Name**: <code>requester_name</code> - Your full name as the person submitting the request.
* **Email Address**: <code>requester_email</code> - Your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: <code>requester_phone</code> - Your work phone number.
* **Department**: <code>department</code> - The department you belong to.
* **Request Date**: <code>request_date</code> - The date of submission.
* **Customer Name**: <code>customer_name</code> - The customer's name.
* **Customer ID Number**: <code>customer_id</code> - The customer's account identifier.
* **Customer Address**: <code>customer_address</code> - The customer's last known address.
* **Customer Phone**: <code>customer_phone</code> - The customer's contact number. (Optional)
* **Customer Email**: <code>customer_email</code> - The customer's email address. (Optional)
* **Account Type**: <code>account_type</code> - The type of debt the customer has (e.g., Credit Card, Personal Loan, etc.).
* **Original Creditor**: <code>original_creditor</code> - Who was originally owed the debt.
* **Account Opened Date**: <code>account_opened_date</code> - When the account was first opened.
* **Last Payment Date**: <code>last_payment_date</code> - The last payment date.
* **Original Balance**: <code>original_balance</code> - The initial balance of the debt.
* **Current Balance Due**: <code>current_balance</code> - The total balance due currently.
* **Principal Amount**: <code>principal_amount</code> - The base debt amount.
* **Interest Amount**: <code>interest_amount</code> - Any accrued interest. (Optional)
* **Fees and Penalties**: <code>fees_amount</code> - Any additional charges. (Optional)
* **Days Past Due**: <code>days_past_due</code> - How many days past due.
* **Delinquency Status**: <code>delinquency_status</code> - The current delinquency status.
* **Previous Collection Attempts**: <code>previous_collection_attempts</code> - What collection actions have been taken previously.
* **Last Contact Date**: <code>last_contact_date</code> - The most recent contact date.
* **Last Contact Method**: <code>last_contact_method</code> - The method of last contact (e.g., phone, email, etc.).
* **Payment Promises Made**: <code>payment_promises</code> - If the customer has promised to pay. (Optional)
* **Any Disputes**: <code>disputes</code> - If the customer disputes the debt.
* **Dispute Details**: <code>dispute_details</code> - Describe any disputes. (Optional)
* **Proposed Collection Action**: <code>proposed_action</code> - The collection action to be taken (e.g., Send Demand Letter, etc.).
* **Proposed Settlement Amount**: <code>settlement_amount</code> - If offering a settlement. (Optional)
* **Proposed Payment Plan Terms**: <code>payment_plan_terms</code> - If a payment plan is proposed. (Optional)
* **Legal Action Recommended**: <code>legal_action_recommended</code> - If legal action is recommended. (Optional)
* **Statute of Limitations Status**: <code>statute_of_limitations</code> - The status of the statute of limitations.
* **Supporting Documents**: <code>supporting_documents</code> - Select all applicable supporting documents.
* **Justification for Action**: <code>justification</code> - Explain the reason for the collection request.
* **Approval Level Needed**: <code>approval_level_needed</code> - Who is required to approve the request.
* **Urgency Level**: <code>urgency</code> - The urgency level of the collection action.
* **Additional Comments**: <code>additional_comments</code> - Any additional information. (Optional)

## Tips
* Review all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Attach all necessary supporting documents.
* Clearly explain the justification for the collection request.
* Ensure the requested approval level is accurate.
* If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or relevant department.
