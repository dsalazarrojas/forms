# Operator Training Sign Off - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operator Training Sign Off form is used to record the completion of operator training sessions. It helps to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to guarantee the operator's understanding of the training material and to track training completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the category for which the training was conducted.
2. Provide a brief description of the training session.
3. Indicate whether the supervisor reviewed the training.
4. Enter your Employee ID.
5. Enter the date of the training session.
6. Enter the duration of the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select 'Yes' if the training was conducted under this category.
* **Description**: Enter a brief summary of the training session.
* **Supervisor Review**: Select 'Yes' if the supervisor reviewed the training session. This ensures that the supervisor has verified that you have understood the training material.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training session in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Training Time**: Enter the duration of the training session in hours and minutes (e.g., 01:30).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your training completion is recorded correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or training coordinator.
