# Proof Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proof Approval Form is used to gather information related to proof approval from various teams within an organization. It's used to ensure the approval process is efficient and secure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and documents before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **proof_approval_form** (proof_approval_form, number, required: false)
This field is not required but is part of the form title for identification purposes.

### 2. **first_name** (first_name, text, required: false)
Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### 3. **last_name** (last_name, text, required: false)
Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### 4. **email** (email, email, required: false)
Enter your email for communication purposes.

### 5. **phone** (phone, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 6. **proof_id** (proof_id, select_one, required: false)
Select 'Yes' if the proof is approved, 'No' otherwise.

### 7. **proof_type** (proof_type, select_one, required: false)
Select 'Yes' if the proof type is approved, 'No' otherwise.

### 8. **approval_date** (approval_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date of approval in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 2023-02-20).

### 9. **approval_time** (approval_time, time, required: false)
Enter the time of approval in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).

### 10. **approval_status** (approval_status, select_one, required: false)
Select 'Active' if the proof is active, 'Inactive' otherwise.

### 11. **comments** (comments, note, required: false)
Enter any comments or notes for the proof approval.

# Tips

* Ensure accuracy and truthfulness in your input to maintain the approval process's efficiency and security.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
