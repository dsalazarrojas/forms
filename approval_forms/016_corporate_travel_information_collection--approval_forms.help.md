# corporate_travel_information_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about corporate travel, allowing us to track employee travel and ensure that all necessary approvals and expenses are accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the traveler's name in the `Traveler Name` field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the `Employee ID` field.
3. Select the travel dates in the `Travel Dates` field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. List the cities, countries, and airports you will visit in the `Travel Locations` field.
5. Select the purpose of your travel (Business or Personal) in the `Travel Purpose` field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the `Manager Name` field.
7. Enter your manager's email address in the `Manager Email` field.
8. Enter your manager's phone number in the `Manager Phone` field.
9. Enter the name of the travel coordinator in the `Travel Coordinator` field.
10. Select the return date in the `Return Date` field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
11. Select the flight details you will be taking (Domestic, International, Flight Number, Flight Time, or Flight Duration) in the `Flight Details` field.
12. Provide a detailed expense report in the `Expense Report` field.
13. Select the travel status of this trip (Pending, Approved, or Denied) in the `Travel Status` field.
14. Enter the total travel cost in the `Travel Cost` field.
15. Select whether your manager has approved this travel (True or False) in the `Manager Approved` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Name** (`Traveler Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person traveling.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, number, optional): Enter the employee ID number.
* **Travel Dates** (`Travel Dates`, date, optional): Select the travel dates in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Travel Locations** (`Travel Locations`, select_multiple, optional): List the cities, countries, and airports you will visit.
* **Travel Purpose** (`Travel Purpose`, select_one, optional): Select the purpose of your travel (Business or Personal).
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Travel Coordinator** (`Travel Coordinator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel coordinator.
* **Return Date** (`Return Date`, date, optional): Select the return date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Flight Details** (`Flight Details`, select_multiple, optional): Select the flight details you will be taking (Domestic, International, Flight Number, Flight Time, or Flight Duration).
* **Expense Report** (`Expense Report`, note, optional): Provide a detailed expense report of your travel.
* **Travel Status** (`Travel Status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the travel status of this trip (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Travel Cost** (`Travel Cost`, number, optional): Enter the total travel cost.
* **Manager Approved** (`Manager Approved`, select_one, optional): Select whether your manager has approved this travel (True or False).
