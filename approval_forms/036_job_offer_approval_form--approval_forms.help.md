# Job Offer Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather approval from approvers at various levels, ensuring that all parties are in agreement with the terms of the job offer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Job Offer ID" field and enter the unique identifier for the job offer.
2. Provide the name of the employee to whom the job is being offered.
3. Enter the job title, which should match the job title specified in the employee's job description.
4. Enter the start date of the job offer.
5. Enter the end date of the job offer.
6. Specify the reason for departure, if applicable.
7. Select the "Approval Level" dropdown to choose from Manager, Director, Prophets, Department Head, or CEO.
8. Choose the "Job Approval Status" dropdown to select Approved, Approved with Conditions, or Denied.
9. Enter the approval reason, if applicable.
10. Enter the Approver Name.
11. Optionally, include comments.
12. Choose the approvers' approvals for each level by selecting multiple options.
13. Enter the email of the person being offered the job.
14. Enter the phone number of the person being offered the job.
15. Select the type of job, which can be Full time, Contract, or Part-time.
16. Enter the location of the job.
17. Choose the approvers' approvals for each level by selecting multiple options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Offer ID** (1, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the job offer.
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the employee to whom the job is being offered.
* **Job Title** (3, text, required): Enter the job title, matching the job title in the employee's job description.
* **Job Start Date** (4, date, required): Enter the start date of the job offer.
* **Job End Date** (5, date, required): Enter the end date of the job offer.
* **Reason For Departure** (6, text, required): Specify the reason for the employee's departure, if applicable.
* **Approval Level** (7, select_one, required): Choose from Manager, Director, Prophets, Department Head, or CEO.
* **Approval Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date of approval.
* **Comments** (9, text, optional): Optionally, enter comments about the job offer.
* **Job Approval Status** (10, select_one, required): Choose from Approved, Approved with Conditions, or Denied.
* **Approval Reason** (11, text, optional): Enter the reason for the approval or denial, if applicable.
* **Approrer Name** (12, text, optional): Enter the name of the approver.
* **Signature** (13, note, optional): Optionally, include a digital signature.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional): Optionally, enter any additional notes about the job offer.
* **Email** (15, email, required): Enter the email of the person being offered the job.
* **Phone** (16, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person being offered the job.
* **Department** (17, text, required): Enter the department of the job.
* **Job Type** (18, select_one, required): Choose from Full time, Contract, or Part-time.
* **Job Location** (19, text, required): Enter the location of the job.
* **Manager Approvals** (20, select_multiple, required): Select multiple approvers at the Manager level.
* **Prophets Approvals** (21, select_multiple, required): Select multiple approvers at the Prophets level.
* **Department Head Approvals** (22, select_multiple, required): Select multiple approvers at the Department Head level.
* **CEO Approvals** (23, select_multiple, required): Select multiple approvers at the CEO level.
* **Comments** (24, text, optional): Optionally, enter comments about the job offer.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Select the correct approval level and job type.
* Ensure that the job end date is after the job start date.
* Verify that all approvers have provided their approval.
