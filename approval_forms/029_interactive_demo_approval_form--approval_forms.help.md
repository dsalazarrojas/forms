# interactive_demo_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interactive demo approval form is used to gather information for interactive demos with customers or partners. This form is meant to help demo teams quickly and efficiently request and track demo information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Start by filling out the demo request form, providing any necessary information about the demo you are requesting.
- Select your name from the options to provide your contact information.
- Enter your company name for your business identity.
- Provide your title or position to ensure we understand your role.
- Enter your email address so we can contact you about the demo.
- Provide your phone number for any follow-up calls.
- Choose the status of your demo request, such as "Approved" or "Rejected".
- Select the date of the demo for our scheduling purposes.
- Choose the time of the demo to ensure we can accommodate it.
- Enter the location of the demo site for our planning purposes.
- Specify the demo duration for our scheduling purposes.
- Add any additional notes for our team to understand your requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Demo Request** (`demo_request`, text, optional): A brief description of the demo you are requesting.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name and contact information.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Your company name for our records.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Your title or position to ensure we understand your role.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address for us to contact you about the demo.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for any follow-up calls.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your demo request.
* **Demo Date** (`demo_date`, date, optional): The date of the demo for our scheduling purposes.
* **Demo Time** (`demo_time`, time, optional): The time of the demo to ensure we can accommodate it.
* **Demo Location** (`demo_location`, text, optional): The location of the demo site for our planning purposes.
* **Demo Duration** (`demo_duration`, number, optional): The duration of the demo.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Any additional information you'd like to provide for our team to understand your requirements.

**Tips**
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your demo request, so we can best accommodate your needs.
- If you need to make changes to your demo request, please contact us to ensure we can update your information accordingly.
- We will follow up with you regarding the status of your demo request after you submit this form.
