# IT License Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT License Purchase Request Form is a tool used to request the purchase of IT licenses for various software and services. Please use this form to provide essential contact information and detailed software requirements to ensure a smooth license procurement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on "Contact Information" section.
2.  Enter your name and title or position.
3.  Enter your email address.
4.  Click on "Software Details" section.
5.  Enter a detailed description of the software or service you are requesting.
6.  Click on "Approval" section.
7.  Select the approver of the purchase request from the dropdown menu.
8.  Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, **Required**):
    This is where you should enter your personal or work-related contact information. 
    Enter your name and title or position, and your email address.
*   **Software Details** (Software Details, Text, **Required**): 
    Provide a detailed description of the software or service you are requesting. This includes the name or title, version, and any relevant details about its use.
*   **Approval** (Approval, Select One, **Required**): 
    Select the approver of the purchase request from the dropdown menu. This will ensure the form is routed to the correct person for further evaluation or approval.

## Tips
Please ensure that all the required fields are completed accurately, as this will aid in the swift and efficient processing of your request. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult with the help desk or relevant IT department.
