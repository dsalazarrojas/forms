# Sustaining Capex Expression Of Interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustaining Capex Expression Of Interest form is used to gather project information for consideration by the organization's management team. The form helps ensure that all proposed projects align with the company's strategic goals and priorities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out each page carefully, making sure to provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Start by filling out the "Project Ideas" page, providing a clear and concise description of your project proposal.
* On the "Project Budgets" page, enter the estimated budget for your project.
* On the "Project Justifications" page, provide a detailed explanation of why your project is necessary and beneficial to the organization.
* For "Departmentalization", select all applicable departments or teams that will be impacted by the project.
* In "Prioritization", select the most important option that best reflects your project's priority level.
* On the "Review Comments" page, provide any additional comments or feedback from reviewers or project stakeholders.
* If applicable, enter the "Project Owner" and "Project Status" information to provide context and tracking details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Ideas (project_ideas)**: A text field where you can describe your project proposal. Be clear and concise, explaining the project's objectives, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Project Budgets (project_budgets)**: Enter the estimated budget for your project. This will help us to evaluate the project's financial feasibility.
* **Project Justifications (project_justifications)**: Provide a detailed explanation of why your project is necessary and beneficial to the organization. This is your opportunity to highlight the project's value and justify its approval.
* **Departmentalization (departmentalization)**: Select all the departments or teams that will be impacted by your project. This will help us to understand the project's scope and relevance.
* **Prioritization (prioritization)**: Select the most important option that best reflects your project's priority level. This will help us to prioritize projects and allocate resources accordingly.
* **Review Comments (review_comments)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback from reviewers or project stakeholders. This will help us to improve the project's quality and ensure its successful execution.
* **Project Owner (project_owner)**: If applicable, enter the name of the project owner or the person responsible for the project's progress.
* **Project Status (project_status)**: If applicable, select the project's status, such as "Active", "In Progress", or "Completed". This will help us to track the project's progress and ensure it is up-to-date.

## Tips
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
* Ensure that you're not duplicating a project that has already been submitted or is already underway.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your project owner or the organization's management team for guidance.
