# Expense Approval Workflow Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to survey the approval workflow process for expenses within your organization. It gathers information about the expense approval process, including the approver's name, title, and email, as well as details about the expense amount, date, type, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for "Approver name" to indicate if the expense has been approved or not.
2. If "Yes", please enter the title of the approver who approved the expense.
3. If "Yes", enter the email address of the approver who approved the expense.
4. Enter the amount of the expense.
5. Select the date the expense was made.
6. Choose the type of expense (Expense Type 1, Expense Type 2, or Expense Type 3).
7. Add any additional notes about the expense (optional).
8. Select who submitted the expense (Submitted By 1, Submitted By 2, or Submitted By 3).
9. Select who approved the expense (Approved By 1, Approved By 2, or Approved By 3).
10. Select who rejected the expense (Rejected By 1, Rejected By 2, or Rejected By 3).
11. Enter the date the expense was approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Workflow**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional information about the expense workflow.
* **Approver Name**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the expense has been approved. If "Yes", enter the name of the approver who approved the expense.
* **Approver Title**: If "Yes" for Approver Name, enter the title of the approver who approved the expense.
* **Approver Email**: If "Yes" for Approver Name, enter the email address of the approver who approved the expense.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the amount of the expense.
* **Expense Date**: Select the date the expense was made.
* **Expense Type**: Choose the type of expense (Expense Type 1, Expense Type 2, or Expense Type 3).
* **Expense Note**: Add any additional notes about the expense.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the expense (Submitted By 1, Submitted By 2, or Submitted By 3).
* **Approved By**: Select who approved the expense (Approved By 1, Approved By 2, or Approved By 3).
* **Rejected By**: Select who rejected the expense (Rejected By 1, Rejected By 2, or Rejected By 3).
* **Approved Date**: Enter the date the expense was approved.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have selected "Yes" for "Approver Name" before entering the approver's details.
* Make sure to select the correct expense type and submitted by and approved by fields accurately.
* Enter the correct email address for the approver.
* Enter the correct date for the expense and approval dates.
* Use the notes field to add any additional context about the expense.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.
