# Workflow Configuration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workflow Configuration Form is used to set up and configure workflow details for various processes or projects. This form provides a structured way to input information about the workflow's initial details, step-by-step progress, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the initial workflow details in Step 1: Initial Workflow Details.
2. Proceed to Step 2: Configuration, and enter the necessary information.
3. Continue to Step 3: Review, and enter any additional details.
4. Enter the final details in Step 5: Final Details.
5. If necessary, enter contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
7. Enter the date and time for the workflow in the Date and Time fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initial Workflow Details** (`workflow_configuration_form_1`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the workflow, such as its name or purpose.
* **Step 1 - Configuration** (`configuration_form_step_1`, text, required/optional): Enter any necessary configuration details for the workflow.
* **Step 2 - Approval** (`configuration_form_step_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the approval details for the workflow.
* **Step 3 - Review** (`configuration_form_step_3`, text, required/optional): Enter any review details or notes for the workflow.
* **Final Details** (`workflow_configuration_form_5`, text, required/optional): Enter the final details or results of the workflow.
* **Email** (`configuration_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or responsible party.
* **Phone** (`configuration_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or responsible party.
* **Note** (`configuration_form_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the workflow.
* **Date** (`configuration_form_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the workflow.
* **Time** (`configuration_form_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the workflow.
