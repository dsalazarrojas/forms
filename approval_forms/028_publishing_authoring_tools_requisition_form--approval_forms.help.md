# Publishing Authoring Tools Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Publishing Authoring Tools Requisition Form is a form used to collect information from submitters regarding their requests for publishing authoring tools. This form helps the submitting team to gather all the necessary details about the tools requested and the submitter's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `form_title` field with a brief title of your request.
2. Select the type of request you are making in the `request_type` field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the software you are requesting in the `software_request` field.
4. Choose the priority level of your request in the `priority` field (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Optionally, add any additional information in the `form_submission` field.
6. Enter your email address in the `submitter_email` field.
7. Provide your phone number in the `submitter_phone` field.
8. Enter your name in the `submitter_name` field.
9. Enter your location in the `submitter_location` field.
10. Enter your team name in the `submitter_team` field.
11. Select the time zone in which you are submitting the form in the `form_submission_time_zone` field.
12. Choose the tool assigned to you from the list in the `assigned_tool` field (Jotform, Other).
13. Optionally, add any additional notes in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text): Enter a brief title of your request.
* **request_type** (select one): Select the type of request you are making (Approval Forms, Request Forms).
* **software_request** (text): Provide a detailed description of the software you are requesting.
* **priority** (select one): Choose the priority level of your request (High, Medium, Low).
* **form_submission** (note): Optionally, add any additional information.
* **submitter_email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **submitter_phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **submitter_name** (text): Enter your name.
* **submitter_location** (text): Enter your location.
* **submitter_team** (text): Enter your team name.
* **form_submission_date** (date): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **form_submission_time** (time): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **form_submission_time_zone** (text): Enter the time zone in which you are submitting the form.
* **assigned_tool** (select one): Choose the tool assigned to you (Jotform, Other).
* **notes** (note): Optionally, add any additional notes.
