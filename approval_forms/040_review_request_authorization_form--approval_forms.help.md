# Review Request Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request review and approval for a review request. It helps to gather information and track the status of the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your review request in the "Review Request" field.
* Set the deadline for the review in the "Deadline" field.
* Enter the review requestor's email in the "Review Requestor Email" field.
* Select "Approved" or "Denied" in the "Approval" field.
* Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
* Select the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.
* Choose the submission status in the "Submission Tracking" field.
* Enter the review requestor's name and department in the "Review Requestor Name" and "Review Requestor Department" fields.
* Enter the asset's ID, version, and URL in the "Asset ID", "Asset Version", and "Asset URL" fields.
* Select the document type and version in the "Document Type" and "Document Version" fields.
* Enter the document's title, URL, and date in the "Document Title", "Document URL", and "Document Date" fields.
