# Review Request Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request review and approval for a review request. It helps to gather information and track the status of the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your review request in the "Review Request" field.
* Set the deadline for the review in the "Deadline" field.
* Enter the review requestor's email in the "Review Requestor Email" field.
* Select "Approved" or "Denied" in the "Approval" field.
* Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
* Select the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.
* Choose the submission status in the "Submission Tracking" field.
* Enter the review requestor's name and department in the "Review Requestor Name" and "Review Requestor Department" fields.
* Enter the asset's ID, version, and URL in the "Asset ID", "Asset Version", and "Asset URL" fields.
* Select the document type and version in the "Document Type" and "Document Version" fields.
* Enter the document's title, URL, and date in the "Document Title", "Document URL", and "Document Date" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Review Request** (1) (`text`, required): Enter the review request details.
* **Deadline** (2) (`date`, optional): Set the deadline for the review.
* **Review Requestor Email** (3) (`email`, optional): Enter the review requestor's email.
* **Approval** (4) (`select_one`, optional): Request approval or denial of the review request.
* **Comments** (5) (`text`, optional): Add additional comments.
* **Review Date** (6) (`date`, optional): Track the review date.
* **Review Time** (7) (`time`, optional): Track the review time.
* **Submission Tracking** (8) (`select_multiple`, optional): Track submission status.
* **Review Requestor Name** (9) (`text`, optional): Enter the review requestor's name.
* **Asset ID** (10) (`text`, optional): Enter the asset ID.
* **Asset Version** (11) (`text`, optional): Enter the asset version.
* **Review Type** (12) (`select_one`, optional): Select the review type (Document or Asset).
* **Document Type** (13) (`select_multiple`, optional): Select the document type.
* **Document Title** (14) (`text`, optional): Enter the document title.
* **Document URL** (15) (`text`, optional): Enter the document URL.
* **Document Version** (16) (`text`, optional): Enter the document version.
* **Document Date** (17) (`date`, optional): Enter the document date.
* **Document Time** (18) (`time`, optional): Enter the document time.
* **Asset Title** (19) (`text`, optional): Enter the asset title.
* **Asset URL** (20) (`text`, optional): Enter the asset URL.
* **Asset Version** (21) (`text`, optional): Enter the asset version.
* **Approval Date** (22) (`date`, optional): Track the approval date.
* **Approval Time** (23) (`time`, optional): Track the approval time.
* **Submission Comments** (24) (`text`, optional): Enter submission comments.
* **Review Requestor Department** (25) (`text`, optional): Enter the review requestor department.
