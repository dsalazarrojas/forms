# Partnership Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Agreement Approval Form is used to collect and approve information related to partnership agreements between your organization and external partners. This form helps to streamline the approval process and ensures that all necessary details are captured for record-keeping and reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Partner Name: The name of the partner company or individual.
	* Partner Address: The address of the partner company or individual.
	* Agreement Type: The type of partnership agreement (e.g., Contract, Partnership, Joint Venture).
	* Start Date: The date when the agreement starts.
	* End Date: The date when the agreement ends.
	* Partner Email: The contact email of the partner company or individual.
2. Ensure that all required information is provided for successful submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): A text field for additional comments or notes about the partnership agreement.

### Partner Name
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): The name of the partner company or individual.

### Partner Address
* **Partner Address** (`partner_address`, text, optional): The address of the partner company or individual.

### Agreement Type
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, optional): The type of partnership agreement (e.g., Contract, Partnership, Joint Venture).

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, date, optional): The date when the agreement starts.

### End Date
* **End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, date, optional): The date when the agreement ends.

### Partner Email
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): The contact email of the partner company or individual.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Verify the accuracy of the information provided before submitting the form.
* Save a copy of the submitted form for future reference.
