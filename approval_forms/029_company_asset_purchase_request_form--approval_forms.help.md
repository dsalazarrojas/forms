# Company Asset Purchase Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Company Asset Purchase Request Form is used for requesting company assets, such as office supplies, equipment, and software, from specific departments. This form helps to track and manage requests efficiently, ensuring that the required assets are purchased and distributed effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and the department requesting the asset.
2. Provide a detailed description of the asset to be purchased.
3. Explain the business reason or justification for the asset purchase.
4. Specify the estimated cost of the asset, including the currency.
5. Choose a priority level (Low, Medium, or High) if necessary.
6. If required, specify the name and title of the manager or finance approver needed for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester full name** (`requester_name`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Enter your full name as the employee requesting the asset.
* **Department** (`department`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Select the department that will be using the asset.
* **Asset description** (`asset_description`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Provide a detailed description of the asset to be purchased.
* **Business justification** (`justification`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Explain the business reason or justification for the asset purchase.
* **Estimated cost** (`estimated_cost`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Include the currency symbol (e.g., $, €, etc.) and the amount.
* **Vendor preference** (`vendor_preference`):
	+ Optional.
	+ If you have a preferred supplier for the asset, enter their name here.
* **Approval required from** (`approval_required_from`):
	+ This field is required.
	+ Enter the name and title of the manager or finance approver needed for approval.
* **Priority level** (`priority_level`):
	+ Optional.
	+ Choose the level of priority: Low, Medium, or High. This may be required for urgent or high-value requests.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the asset to be purchased.
* Ensure the estimated cost includes the currency.
* If the asset requires approval from a specific manager or finance approver, include their name and title.
* Prioritization is only necessary if the request is urgent or high-value.
* Avoid submitting duplicate requests to prevent delays in approval processes.
