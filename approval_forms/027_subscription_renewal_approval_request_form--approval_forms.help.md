# Subscription Renewal Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request approval for subscription renewals. It helps track the status of these requests, ensuring they are properly reviewed and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **Subscription Details** section with your subscription information, including relevant details about your subscription.
* In the **Business Justification** section, provide a clear explanation of why you need to renew your subscription.
* Select your answer in the **Approval** section, which will determine whether your request is approved or not.
* Choose one or more managers to approve your request in the **Manager Approval** section.
* If you wish, add additional comments about your request in the **Comments** section.
* Fill out your submission and renewal dates in the **Submission Date** and **Renewal Date** sections.
* Select the **Renewal Status** of your request from the provided options.
* Add any additional comments or notes from your manager in the **Manager Comments** and **Manager Notes** sections, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscription Details** (`Subscription Details`, text, required):
  + This section is used to collect information about the subscription you are requesting to renew.
  + Fill out your subscription title, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Business Justification** (`Business Justification`, text, required):
  + Provide a clear and concise explanation of why you need to renew your subscription.
  + Explain how the subscription benefits your work or organization.
* **Approval** (`Approval`, select_one, required):
  + Select 'Yes' if you are requesting approval for your subscription renewal.
  + Select 'No' if you do not want to renew your subscription.
* **Manager Approval** (`Manager Approval`, select_multiple, required):
  + Select one or more managers to approve your request.
  + Make sure to choose only managers who are relevant to your request.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional):
  + Add any additional comments about your request, if necessary.
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, text, optional):
  + If you have received comments from your manager, add them here.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, date, required):
  + Fill out the date you submitted your request.
* **Renewal Date** (`Renewal Date`, date, required):
  + Fill out the date you are requesting to renew your subscription.
* **Renewal Status** (`Renewal Status`, select_one, required):
  + Select the status of your request from the provided options.
  + Choose 'Pending' if your request is waiting approval.
  + Choose 'Approved' if your request has been approved.
  + Choose 'Rejected' if your request has been rejected.
* **Manager Comments** (`Manager Comments`, text, optional):
  + Add any comments from your manager about your request.
* **Manager Notes** (`Manager Notes`, text, optional):
  + Add any notes from your manager about your request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be clear and concise in your business justification and comments.
* Choose the correct renewal status for your request.
* If you have additional comments or notes from your manager, include them for reference.
