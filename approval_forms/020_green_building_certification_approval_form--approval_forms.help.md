# Green Building Certification Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Building Certification Approval Form is used to seek approval for a project to be certified as a green building. This form captures essential project details and green building certification information, enabling team members to review and verify the project's status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide a brief **Project Description** in the text field.
3. Select the **Project Status** from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the **Project Location** in the text field.
5. Upload the **Project Certification Document** in the text field.
6. Enter the **Project Certification Date** using the calendar tool.
7. Provide the **Certifying Entity** details, including name and address in the text fields.
8. Enter the **Contact Name**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone** in the text, email, and text fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Project Date Started** (`project_date_started`, date, required): Enter the date the project started.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the project from the provided options (Active or Inactive).
* **Status** (`project_status_label`, text, required): Enter a brief explanation for the project status.
* **Green Building Certification** (`green_building_certification`, note, required): This field is for internal use and not meant to be filled by the user.
* **Project Certification Document** (`project_certification_document`, text, required): Upload the project certification document.
* **Project Certification Date** (`project_certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of certification.
* **Certifying Entity** (`certifying_entity`, text, required): Enter the name of the certifying entity.
* **Certifying Entity Address** (`certifying_entity_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the certifying entity.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
