# Fireworks Display Approval Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit an application for a fireworks display. To fill out this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fireworks Display Approval Application**: This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank or enter a title for your event.
2. **Event Organizer Name**: Please enter the name of the event organizer.
3. **Contact Information**: Enter any additional contact information that you would like to provide.
4. **Location**: Enter the location of the fireworks display.
5. **Date and Time**: Choose the date and time of the event.
6. **Firework Safety Plan**: Explain your safety measures and procedures for the fireworks display.
7. **Firework Safety Measures**: Provide any additional safety measures you will take.
8. **Firework Location**: Enter the exact location of the fireworks display.
9. **Event Details**: Provide any additional details about the event.
10. **Declaration**: Enter any additional declaration or comments about the event.
11. **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
12. **Venue Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the venue organizer.
13. **Venue Organizer Email**: Enter the email of the venue organizer.
14. **Venue Organizer Name**: Enter the name of the venue organizer.
15. **Venue Organizer Address**: Enter the address of the venue organizer.
16. **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue.
17. **Venue Address**: Enter the address of the venue.
18. **Venue Road**: Enter the road of the venue.
19. **Venue City**: Enter the city of the venue.
20. **Venue State**: Enter the state of the venue.
21. **Venue Zip**: Enter the zip code of the venue.
22. **Event Start Time**: Choose the start time of the event.
23. **Event Duration**: Choose the duration of the event.
24. **Firework Safety Plan 2**: Explain any additional safety measures you will take.
25. **Submission**: Choose the status of your application.
