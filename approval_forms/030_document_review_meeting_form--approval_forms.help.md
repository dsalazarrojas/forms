# Document Review Meeting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Document Review Meeting Form is designed to collect information about document reviews. This includes details about the schedule of the meeting, feedback on the document, and decisions made during the meeting. It also captures any follow-up tasks that need to be completed and notes from the review session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Meeting Schedule** field with the date and time of the document review meeting.
2. Provide your **Document Review Feedback** on the document in the text field below.
3. Indicate whether any **Decisions and Actions** were made during the meeting by selecting one or more options from the list.
4. Choose whether **Follow-up Tasks** need to be completed by selecting from the available options.
5. Provide any additional **Review Session Notes** in the text field.
6. List the **Meeting Attendees** who participated in the meeting.
7. Enter the **Document Reviewer**'s email address, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Schedule (Page 1)**: Enter the date and time of the document review meeting.
* **Document Review Feedback (Page 2)**: Provide your feedback on the document, including any comments or suggestions.
* **Decisions and Actions (Page 3)**: Select all that apply from the list of options regarding decisions made during the meeting.
	+ **Yes**: Indicates that a decision was made during the meeting.
	+ **No**: Indicates no decisions were made during the meeting.
* **Follow-up Tasks (Page 4)**: Select whether follow-up tasks are needed after the meeting.
	+ **Yes**: Indicates that follow-up tasks are needed.
	+ **No**: Indicates no follow-up tasks are needed.
* **Review Session Notes (Page 5)**: Provide any additional notes from the review session.
* **Meeting Attendees (Page 6)**: List the names of all meeting attendees.
* **Document Reviewer (Page 7)**: Enter the email address of the document reviewer, if applicable.
