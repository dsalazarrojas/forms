# End Of Service Benefit Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and track employee benefits after their employment has ended. It is a formal record of the approval or rejection process, ensuring all parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's details in the "employee_details" field.
2. Select whether the employee is eligible for benefits by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "select_benefit" field.
3. If the employee is eligible, enter a brief description of the benefits they are entitled to in the "benefit_details" field.
4. Enter the date of the approval or rejection in the "approval_date" field.
5. Enter the details of the person approving the benefits in the "approver_details" field.
6. Select the status of the approval or rejection by choosing "Active" or "Inactive" in the "approval_status" field.
7. If the approval is rejected, enter a reason for rejection in the "reason_for_rejection" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_details** (text): Enter the employee's name, job title, and contact information.
* **select_benefit** (select_one): Choose "Yes" if the employee is eligible for benefits, "No" otherwise.
* **benefit_details** (text): Enter a brief description of the benefits the employee is entitled to.
* **approval_date** (date): Enter the date of the approval or rejection.
* **approver_details** (text): Enter the name, title, and contact information of the person approving the benefits.
* **approval_status** (select_multiple): Choose "Active" if the approval is granted, "Inactive" if rejected.
* **reason_for_rejection** (text): Enter a reason for rejection if applicable.
