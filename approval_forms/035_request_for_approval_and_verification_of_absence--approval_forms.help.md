# Request For Approval And Verification Of Absence - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized process for requesting and verifying an employee's absence from work. This form allows you to easily communicate approval or denial of the request, specify the dates and times of the absence, and provide a reason for the absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether to **Approve** or **Deny** the request by selecting the appropriate option from the "Approve or Deny Request" dropdown menu.
2. If approving the request, enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the absence in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the absence in the corresponding fields.
4. Provide a **Reason for Absence** in the text field, explaining the reason for the absence.
5. If necessary, enter an **Approval Reason** in the text field, providing a brief explanation for the approval decision.
6. Enter the **Approver Name**, which is the name of the person approving or denying the request.
7. You may optionally add any additional **Comments** about the absence request in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Approve or Deny Request** (page 1, 1) : Select one of the two options to indicate whether the request should be approved or denied.
* **Start Date** (page 1, 2): Enter the date when the absence starts.
* **End Date** (page 1, 3): Enter the date when the absence ends.
* **Start Time** (page 1, 4): Enter the time when the absence starts.
* **End Time** (page 1, 5): Enter the time when the absence ends.
* **Reason for Absence** (page 1, 6): Provide a brief explanation for the absence.
* **Approval Reason** (page 1, 7): Provide a brief explanation for the approval decision, if applicable.
* **Approver Name** (page 1, 8): Enter the name of the person approving or denying the request.
* **Comments** (page 1, 9): Add any additional comments or details about the absence request, if necessary.

## Tips
* Use this form for each separate absence event to ensure accurate tracking and management of absences.
* Only fill out this form once per absence event.
* Be sure to accurately complete each field to ensure a smooth and efficient approval process.
