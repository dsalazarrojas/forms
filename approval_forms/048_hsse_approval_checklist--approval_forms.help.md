# HSSE Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HSSE Approval Checklist form is designed to facilitate the approval process for various projects, tasks, or proposals within an organization. It collects essential information from the contact person, organization, and approval details to ensure a smooth and efficient approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Organization" field with your organization's name.
2. Enter the contact person's name and phone number in the "contact_person" and "contact_phone" fields.
3. Enter the contact person's email in the "contact_email" field.
4. Select the relevant department and job title from the "department" and "job_title" fields.
5. Enter the date and time of the approval in the "date" and "time" fields.
6. Describe the approval process in the "approval_process" field.
7. Choose the approval status from the "approval_status" field (e.g., Approved or Rejected).
8. If rejected, provide a reason for rejection in the "reason_for_rejection" field.
9. Select the person who approved or rejected the proposal from the "approved_by" field.
10. Enter the date and time the proposal was approved or rejected in the "approved_date" and "approved_time" fields.
11. Optionally, provide any additional comments or attachments for the reviewer's reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter your organization's full name.
* **contact_person**: Enter the person to contact for this approval request.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **department**: Enter the department or team involved in this approval request.
* **job_title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **location**: Enter the location where the work will be done or related to this proposal.
* **date**: Enter the date of the approval request.
* **time**: Enter the time of the approval request.
* **approval_process**: Describe the steps or process involved in the approval process.
* **approval_status**: Select the approval status of the proposal (e.g., Approved or Rejected).
* **reason_for_rejection**: Provide a reason for rejecting the proposal (if applicable).
* **approved_by**: Select the person who approved or rejected the proposal.
* **approved_date**: Enter the date the proposal was approved or rejected.
* **approved_time**: Enter the time the proposal was approved or rejected.
* **reviewer_comments**: Enter any comments or feedback for the reviewer's reference.
* **attachments**: Optionally, attach any relevant documents or files to support the proposal.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the reviewer's reference.
