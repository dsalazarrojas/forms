# policy_amendment_compliance_verification_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The policy amendment compliance verification form is used to confirm that all required approvers have reviewed and approved a policy amendment for an organization. This form ensures that the amendment complies with the organization's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the **Form Title** field with a brief description of the policy amendment.
2. Enter the **Organization** field with the name of the organization related to the policy amendment.
3. Enter the **Policy Version** field with the version number of the policy amendment.
4. Enter the **Effective Date** field with the date when the policy amendment takes effect.
5. Enter the **Review Date** field with the date when the policy was last reviewed.
6. In the **Comments** field, provide any additional comments or notes about the policy amendment.
7. For the approver fields, select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment. Some approver fields are single-select, while others are multi-select.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the policy amendment.
* **Organization** (text, optional): Enter the name of the organization related to the policy amendment.
* **Policy Version** (number, optional): Enter the version number of the policy amendment.
* **Effective Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the policy amendment takes effect.
* **Review Date** (date, optional): Enter the date when the policy was last reviewed.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the policy amendment.
* **Approver 1** (select_one, optional): Select the approver who has reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 2** (select_one, optional): Select the approver who has reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 3** (select_one, optional): Select the approver who has reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 5 - Yes/No** (select_one, optional): Select whether the policy amendment has been approved or not.
* **Approver 4 - Approver1, Approver2, or Approver3** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 6 - Yes/No** (select_one, optional): Select whether the policy amendment has been approved or not.
* **Approver 7 - Approver1 or Approver2** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 8 - Approver1, Approver2, or Approver3** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 9 - Approver1 or Approver3** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 10 - Approver1 or Approver2** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
* **Approver 11 - Approver1 or Approver2 or Approver3** (select_multiple, optional): Select the approvers who have reviewed and approved the policy amendment.
