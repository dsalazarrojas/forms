# Administrative Selection Results Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Administrative Selection Results Form is used to document the selection process and decision made by a selection committee, which is responsible for choosing a candidate for a vacant position within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "How to Complete This Form" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Selection Committee Member**: Enter the name of the committee member who made the selection decision.
* **Selection Date**: Select the date of the selection decision made.
* **Position Title**: Choose the title of the position being filled.
* **Department**: Select the department where the position is located from the dropdown menu.
* **Selection Type**: Choose the type of selection made (e.g. New Hire, Promotion, Transfer, etc.).
* **Selected Candidate Name**: Enter the full name of the selected candidate.
* **Candidate ID**: Enter the ID number of the selected candidate.
* **Selection Decision**: Choose the final selection decision made.
* **Selection Ranking**: Select the candidate's ranking among applicants.
* **Selection Criteria Met**: Check if the candidate met all required criteria.
* **Interview Score**: Enter the numerical score from the interview process.
* **Skills Assessment Score**: Enter the numerical score from skills assessments.
* **Education Verification**: Confirm if the candidate's education has been verified or if it is not required.
* **Experience Verification**: Confirm if the candidate's experience has been verified or if it is not required.
* **Reference Checks Completed**: Choose the status of reference checks.
* **Background Check Status**: Select the status of the background check.
* **Proposed Start Date**: Enter the proposed start date for the candidate.
* **Salary Offered**: Enter the salary offered to the candidate.
* **Benefits Package**: Describe the benefits package offered to the candidate.
* **Selection Justification**: Enter a brief justification for the selection decision.
* **Concerns or Conditions**: List any concerns or conditions related to the selection.
* **Committee Member Signature**: Sign the form electronically with the committee member's signature.
* **Approval Authority**: Enter the name of the person with final approval authority.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and comprehensively to maintain transparency and fairness in the selection process.
* Complete the form in a timely manner to ensure that the selection process is documented and recorded correctly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required information is included.
