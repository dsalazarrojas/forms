# Commission Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for commission calculation and approval purposes. It is designed to be used by salespeople, managers, and HR/payroll personnel to track and verify commission payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Employee Identification Number** and **Full Name of Salesperson** fields to identify the salesperson.
2. Select the **Sales Department** where the sale was made.
3. Enter the **Customer or Company Name** who made the purchase.
4. Enter the **Reference Invoice Number** related to the sale.
5. Enter the **Total Sale Value** (excluding taxes and shipping).
6. Choose the **Product or Service Category** that was sold.
7. Enter the **Date of Sale** when the sale was completed.
8. If applicable, enter the **Additional Bonuses Included**.
9. Choose the **Applied Commission Rate (%)** for this calculation.
10. Enter the **Total Commission Amount to be Paid**.
11. Select the **Approving Manager Name**, whose employee ID is also required.
12. Choose the **Commission Approval Status** (Approved, Pending Further Review, or Rejected).
13. If rejected, enter the **Reason for Rejection or Review**.
14. Enter the **Date of Manager Approval**.
15. Enter any **Additional Internal Notes** for HR or Payroll use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Identification Number (Employee ID)**: Enter the unique ID assigned to the salesperson.
* **Full Name of Salesperson**: Enter the full name of the salesperson.
* **Sales Department**: Select the department where the sale was made.
* **Customer or Company Name**: Enter the name of the client who made the purchase.
* **Reference Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number related to the sale.
* **Total Sale Value**: Enter the total sale value, excluding taxes and shipping.
* **Product or Service Category**: Select the category of the product or service sold.
* **Date of Sale**: Enter the date when the sale was completed.
* **Additional Bonuses Included**: If applicable, enter the amount of bonuses included.
* **Applied Commission Rate (%)**: Enter the commission rate applied for this calculation.
* **Total Commission Amount to be Paid**: Enter the total commission amount to be paid.
* **Additional Internal Notes**: Enter any additional internal notes for HR or Payroll use.
* **Approving Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who is approving this commission.
* **Manager Employee ID**: Enter the ID of the manager who is approving this commission.
* **Commission Approval Status**: Choose the status of the commission (Approved, Pending Further Review, or Rejected).
* **Reason for Rejection or Review**: If the commission is rejected, enter the reason for rejection or review.
* **Date of Manager Approval**: Enter the date when the manager approved the commission.
* **Additional Internal Notes**: Enter any additional internal notes for HR or Payroll use.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* Double-check that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are a manager, review the form carefully and choose the correct commission approval status.
* If rejected, provide a clear reason for rejection or review.
