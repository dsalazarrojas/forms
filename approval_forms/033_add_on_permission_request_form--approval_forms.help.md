# Add-On Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Add-On Permission Request Form is used to request new software extensions, plugins, or tool add-ons that require internal approval. The form will be reviewed and processed internally to ensure that the requested add-on aligns with company policies and standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and department in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your manager's email address for initial approval.
3. Describe the add-on or tool you are requesting, including its name and product URL.
4. Specify the integration platform for the add-on.
5. Explain why this add-on is needed and what problem it solves.
6. Select the urgency level of the request.
7. Indicate whether the add-on requires access to sensitive company data.
8. Estimate the annual cost of the add-on (if any).
9. If applicable, mention any alternatives you've considered.
10. Date and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: Enter your name and title.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the provided options (IT, Marketing, Sales, Human Resources, Finance, Operations).
* **Manager's Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Name of Add-On / Tool**: Enter the name of the add-on or tool you are requesting.
* **Product URL / Documentation Link**: Enter the product URL or documentation link for the add-on.
* **Integration Platform**: Select the platform that the add-on integrates with (Browser Extension, CRM, Productivity, Communication, CMS, Other).
* **Business Justification**: Explain why this add-on is needed and what problem it solves.
* **Urgency / Priority**: Select the urgency level of the request (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
* **Does this add-on require access to sensitive company data?**: Indicate whether the add-on requires access to sensitive company data (No Access Required, Read-Only Access, Read and Write Access, Administrative Access).
* **Estimated Annual Cost**: Enter the estimated annual cost of the add-on (if any).
* **Alternatives Considered**: If applicable, mention any alternatives you've considered for the add-on.
* **Request Date**: Date and submit your request.

Note: This form requires all fields to be filled out to ensure a thorough review and approval process.
