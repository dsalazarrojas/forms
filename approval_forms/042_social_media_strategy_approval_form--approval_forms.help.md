# social_media_strategy_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media strategy approval form is used to submit social media strategies for approval. It ensures that all social media efforts are aligned with company goals and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a social media strategy for approval, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Approval Forms)**: Select the category for your social media strategy from the given options.
* **Description**: Enter a detailed description of your social media strategy, including the goals and objectives.
* **Title**: Provide a clear and concise title for your social media strategy.
* **Date**: Set the date for your social media strategy.
* **Time**: Specify the time frame for your social media strategy.
* **Assigned Tool**: Assign a tool to be used for your social media strategy. This could be a specific software or platform.
* **Output File**: Specify the output file for your social media strategy. This could be a URL or a file name.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for your social media strategy. This will help track and reference your strategy.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the company's social media strategy guidelines before filling out this form.
* Avoid duplicating efforts by checking that your social media strategy is not already submitted.
* Ensure that your social media strategy is aligned with company goals and policies.
* Use the correct format for the output file.
