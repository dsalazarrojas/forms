**employee_assistance_program_approval_form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The "employee_assistance_program_approval_form" is used to request and approve employee assistance, providing a structured and organized way to track and manage requests for support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the necessary fields, such as the Employee ID, employee_name, manager_id, and manager_name. These fields are required for the form to be saved.
2.  Select the Request Type to specify the type of assistance needed.
3.  Choose the date of the request.
4.  Enter the Reason for Request and any additional comments or notes.
5.  If the request has been approved, select Yes for Approved by Manager and enter the Approved by Manager Time and Date.
6.  Finally, choose the approval status of the request and the employee status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee ID** (number, required): This is the unique identifier of the employee requesting assistance.
*   **Employee Name** (text, required): Please enter the name of the employee requesting assistance.
*   **Manager ID** (number, required): This is the unique identifier of the manager of the employee requesting assistance.
*   **Manager Name** (text, required): Please enter the name of the manager of the employee requesting assistance.
*   **Request Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of assistance needed. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
*   **Reason for Request** (text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the request.
*   **Date of Request** (date, optional): The date the request was made.
*   **Approved by Manager** (select_one, optional): Select Yes if the request has been approved by the manager.
*   **Approved by Manager Time** (time, optional): The time the request was approved by the manager.
*   **Approved by Manager Date** (date, optional): The date the request was approved by the manager.
*   **Notes** (note, optional): Additional comments or notes related to the request.
*   **Approval Status** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the current approval status of the request. Options are Active and Inactive.
*   **Employee Status** (select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the employee requesting assistance. Options are Active and Inactive.
*   **Form submitted** (date, optional): The date the form was submitted.
*   **Submission Date** (date, optional): The date the form was submitted.

Please note that while the form fields are labeled as optional, completing this form requires filling out the required fields accurately. This ensures that the form is saved and that the request is processed correctly.
