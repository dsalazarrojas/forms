# Compliance Policy Approval Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees to acknowledge that they have read and comply with a specific compliance policy. It helps the compliance team track employee acknowledgements and provides necessary information for record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the linked policy before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields: Employee name, Department, Work email address, Policy version reviewed, Acknowledgement statement, and Date signed.
3. Enter any relevant comments or questions for the compliance team's reference.
4. Submit the form to complete the acknowledgement process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name**: Enter your full name.
* **Employee id**: Enter your company-assigned identifier. If you're unsure about your ID, contact your HR department.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team name.
* **Work email address**: Enter your work email address for record-keeping purposes.
* **Policy version reviewed**: Reference the policy version or date you have reviewed.
* **Acknowledgement statement**: Select "I acknowledge" to confirm that you have read and agree to comply with the policy.
* **Date signed**: Enter the date of your acknowledgement.
* **Comments**: Provide any feedback or questions for the compliance team's reference.

## Tips

* Make sure to read the linked policy before proceeding to ensure you understand the compliance policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, enter them in the Comments field.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approval from your manager or supervisor before submitting this form.
* Keep your email address up-to-date to ensure you receive any follow-up notifications or reminders from the compliance team.
