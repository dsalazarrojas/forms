# Recruitment Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for approving recruitment requests. It gathers information about the job title, job description, job type, reporting manager, job status, and any comments related to the recruitment process. This form is intended for internal use only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the main page (page 1) and fill in the required information.
2. On page 2, select the approval status and job type from the respective dropdown menus.
3. On page 3, fill in the job title and description.
4. On page 4, select the reporting manager(s) from the list of options.
5. On page 5, choose the job status.
6. On page 6, add any comments about the recruitment process.
7. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main Page (Page 1)
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job you are requesting approval for.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job.
* **Email** (`email_field`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Approval (Page 2)
* **Approval** (`approval_section`, select_one, optional): Choose whether to approve or reject the job request.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).

### Reporting Manager (Page 3)
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reporting manager(s) for the job.

### Job Status (Page 4)
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the job (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).

### Comments (Page 5)
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the job request.

### Submit (Page 6)
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click to submit the form for approval.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified. Please fill in the required information accurately and completely to ensure smooth processing of the recruitment request.
