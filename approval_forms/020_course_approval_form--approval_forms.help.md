# Course Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track course approvals and rejections. Fill out the form to notify the approver of the course status, provide additional comments or reasons for approval or rejection, and keep track of the course's current status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Approver" who is responsible for the approval or rejection of the course.
2. Choose the "Course Status" to indicate whether the course is approved, not approved, or under review.
3. Fill in the "Date" and "Time" fields to record the approval or rejection date and time.
4. Provide any additional "Comments" to explain the reason for approval or rejection.
5. Enter any relevant "Notes" for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name**
  * **Course Name** (Text): This is the name of the course being approved or rejected.
* **Approver**
  * **Approver** (Select One): Select the person responsible for approving or rejecting the course.
* **Date** 
  * **Date** (Date): Record the date of approval or rejection.
* **Time** 
  * **Time** (Time): Record the time of approval or rejection.
* **Course Status**
  * **Course Status** (Select Multiple): Choose whether the course is approved, not approved, or under review.
* **Comments**
  * **Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or reasons for approval or rejection.
* **Email**
  * **Email** (Email): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Phone**
  * **Phone** (Text): Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Notes**
  * **Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or details about the course.
