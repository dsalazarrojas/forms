# New Product Category Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Product Category Approval Request Form is used to request approval for a new product category in the product catalog. This form is meant to be completed by team members when they need to add a new category to the catalog.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Go to the first page and fill in the category details. This includes the category name, description, and any other relevant information.
2. Click "Next" to go to the second page.
3. On the second page, add any additional information regarding the approval process.
4. On the third page, select the approval status of the new product category.
5. Click "Submit" to send the request for approval.
6. Review the form's status on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Category Details (Page 1)**: Enter the name, description, and other relevant information about the new product category. This field is required.
* **Approval Information (Page 2)**: Enter any additional information regarding the approval process. This field is not required.
* **Approval Status (Page 3)**: Select the approval status of the new product category. This is a required field, and you must choose one of the following options:
	+ Approve
	+ Reject
	+ Not Applicable
* **Additional Information (Page 4)**: Enter any additional information related to the approval process. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the form is complete.
* If you select "Not Applicable", a human will review the form and make a final decision.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your team lead or manager for assistance.
