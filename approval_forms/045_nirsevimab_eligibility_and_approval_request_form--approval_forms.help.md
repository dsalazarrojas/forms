# Nirsevimab Eligibility And Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine the eligibility and approval of patients for the Nirsevimab treatment. It is designed to gather essential information about the patient and their medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
2. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and date of last examination in the relevant fields.
3. Provide their patient address, phone number, and email address if applicable.
4. If the patient has had any previous reactions, symptoms, or medical history, please provide this information in the "rsv_symptoms" and "medical_history" fields.
5. If you are a healthcare provider, select the name of your institution from the "healthcare_provider" dropdown list and ensure that you have entered your contact number in "provider_contact_number".
6. If the patient has received any RSV vaccinations, please select the type and number of doses administered from the "rsv_vaccine_type" and "rsv_dose_number" fields.
7. Enter the date of the last administered RSV vaccination, if any.
8. Finally, enter any additional comments or information in the "doctor_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nirsevimab Eligibility** (`nirsevimab_eligibility`): This field is not applicable as it is a page label, not a field.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **date_of_last_exam** (`date_of_last_exam`): Enter the date of the last examination or medical consultation with the patient.
* **patient_address** (`patient_address`): Enter the patient's home or residential address.
* **patient_phone_number** (`patient_phone_number`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **patient_email** (`patient_email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **rsv_symptoms** (`rsv_symptoms`): If the patient has had any previous reactions or symptoms, provide this information in the text box.
* **medical_history** (`medical_history`): Provide any relevant medical information or history for the patient.
* **patient_id** (`patient_id`): Enter the patient's ID number, if applicable.
* **healthcare_provider** (`healthcare_provider`): Select the name of the healthcare provider who will be administering or has administered the treatment to the patient.
* **provider_contact_number** (`provider_contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the healthcare provider.
* **rsv_vaccine_type** (`rsv_vaccine_type`): Select the type of RSV vaccination administered to the patient.
* **rsv_dose_number** (`rsv_dose_number`): Enter the number of RSV vaccine doses administered to the patient.
* **rsv_last_administered_date** (`rsv_last_administered_date`): Enter the date of the last administered RSV vaccine, if any.
* **doctor_name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the name of the doctor or healthcare provider who will be administering the treatment.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, refer to the relevant sections above for clarification.
- This form should be used for a single patient at a time to avoid confusion and errors.
