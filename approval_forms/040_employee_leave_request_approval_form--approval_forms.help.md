# Employee Leave Request Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Leave Request Approval Form is a tool for employees to submit leave requests to their supervisors or HR department for approval. It allows approvers to review and update the approval status of leaves in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Employee**: Enter your unique employee ID to associate your leave request with your personnel file.
2.  **Start Date**: Select the date from which you are taking leave. This is when your absence starts.
3.  **End Date**: Enter the date your leave ends.
4.  **Reason for Leave**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave. This could be a vacation, illness, family leave, etc.
5.  **Leave Duration**: Optionally, provide the duration of your leave in days or hours.
6.  **Approval Status**: Select the current status of your leave request (Active or Inactive).
7.  **Approval Note**: Add any notes or comments that the approver might need to understand the context of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Employee**: <u>Employee</u> is your unique identifier that helps link your leave request to your personnel records.
*  **Start Date**: <u>Start Date</u> is the date when you will be taking leave, which will be recorded for HR and management purposes.
*  **End Date**: <u>End Date</u> is the date when your leave will end, ensuring all relevant dates are captured.
*  **Reason for Leave**: <u>Reason for Leave</u> should be a clear and concise statement explaining why you are taking leave, which helps in processing your request.
*  **Leave Duration**: <u>Leave Duration</u> is an optional field where you can specify how many days or hours you will be away, if applicable.
*  **Approval Status**: <u>Approval Status</u> indicates whether your leave request is approved (Active) or not (Inactive), which updates your leave records accordingly.
*  **Approval Note**: <u>Approval Note</u> is a space for any comments or notes that approvers might need to understand the context of your request.
