# International Training Leave Of Absence Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request international training leave of absence for employees to participate in approved training programs or activities outside the country or region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name, Employee ID, and Department.
2. Enter the Training Location where the training will take place.
3. Select the Dates Of Absence Start and Dates Of Absence End for your leave.
4. Get the approval from your Supervisor, as a supervisor signature is required for approval.
5. Sign the form with your own Employee Signature once you have the supervisor's approval.
6. Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (Employee Name)** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID (Employee ID)** (`employee_id`, required): Enter your employee ID as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Training Location (Training Location)** (`training_location`, required): Enter the location where the training will take place.
* **Dates Of Absence Start (Dates Of Absence Start)** (`dates_of_absence_start`, required): Enter the date you will start your leave.
* **Dates Of Absence End (Dates Of Absence End)** (`dates_of_absence_end`, required): Enter the date you will end your leave.
* **Supervisor Approval (Supervisor Approval)** (`supervisor_approval`, required): Get approval from your supervisor by selecting "Yes" and have them sign the form.
* **Employee Approval (Employee Approval)** (`employee_approval`, required): Check the box to indicate that you have been approved for the leave.
* **Leave Planner Approval (Leave Planner Approval)** (`leave_planner_approval`, required): Get approval from your leave planner by selecting "Yes" and have them sign the form.
* **Department (Department)** (`department`, required): Enter the department that you belong to.
* **Supervisor Signature (Supervisor Signature)** (`supervisor_signature`, required): Get a signature from your supervisor after approval is given.
* **Employee Signature (Employee Signature)** (`employee_signature`, required): Sign the form once you have received approval from your supervisor and leave planner.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary approvals before signing the form.
* Make sure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
