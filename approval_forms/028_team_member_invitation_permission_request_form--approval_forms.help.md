# Team Member Invitation Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a team member invitation for a team lead and approver. Fill out the form to provide all necessary information for the team lead and approver to make an informed decision.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the project lead from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose whether team members are invited or not.
3. Input the date of the invitation.
4. Choose the invitation status.
5. (Optional) Add any additional comments for the team lead and approver.
6. Input the approval date and time.
7. Provide the team lead's name, email, phone number, and department.
8. Choose the invitation type.
9. Select the team member status.
10. Input the approver's name, email, and phone number.
11. Input the team name, email, and phone number.
12. Input the team lead's signature.
13. Input the approver's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Lead**: Select the project lead who will be involved in this request. This field is required.
* **Team Members**: Choose whether team members are invited or not. This field is required.
* **Invitation Date**: Input the date of the invitation. This field is required.
* **Invitation Status**: Choose the status of the invitation. This field is required.
* **Approval Date**: Input the date of approval. This field is required.
* **Approval Time**: Input the time of approval. This field is required.
* **Team Lead**: Input the team lead's name. This field is required.
* **Team Lead Email**: Input the team lead's email. This field is required.
* **Team Lead Phone**: Input the team lead's phone number. This field is required.
* **Approver**: Input the approver's name. This field is required.
* **Approver Email**: Input the approver's email. This field is required.
* **Approver Phone**: Input the approver's phone number. This field is required.
* **Team Name**: Input the team name. This field is required.
* **Team Email**: Input the team email. This field is required.
* **Team Phone**: Input the team phone number. This field is required.
* **Team Lead Department**: Input the team lead's department. This field is required.
* **Invitation Type**: Choose whether the invitation is for a new team member or not. This field is required.
* **Team Member Status**: Choose whether the team member is active or inactive. This field is required.
* **Team Lead Comment**: Add any comments for the team lead. This field is optional.
* **Approver Comment**: Add any comments for the approver. This field is optional.
* **Submission Status**: Choose whether the submission is active or inactive. This field is required.
* **Team Lead Signature**: Input the team lead's signature. This field is required.
* **Approver Signature**: Input the approver's signature. This field is required.
