# vip_guest_list_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vip_guest_list_approval_form is used to collect information for VIP guest list approvals. This form is meant to be completed by internal users to review and manage the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the vip_guest_list_approval_form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form information page.
2. Enter the full name of the VIP guest.
3. Enter the email of the VIP guest.
4. Enter the contact number of the VIP guest.
5. Provide any additional event details as needed.
6. Select the approval status (Approved, Denied, In Progress, or Rejected).
7. Enter any special requests for the VIP guest.
8. Leave comments for internal review.
9. Optionally, enter the form submitted by the internal user.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Information** (`form_information`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief overview of the form and is a placeholder for any relevant information.
- **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the full name of the VIP guest.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the VIP guest.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the VIP guest.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional event details as needed.
- **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the approval status (Approved, Denied, In Progress, or Rejected).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special requests for the VIP guest.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Leave comments for internal review.
- **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): Enter the internal user who submitted the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully and fill out all required fields before submitting.
- Verify the accuracy of the information provided.
- Use discretion when selecting the approval status.
- Use this form for internal purposes only.
- This form is meant to be a tool for internal management and review, not for external communication with the VIP guest.
