# Architectural Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Approval Request Form is designed to collect information from property owners or managers to request architectural approval for proposed projects. This form is used to gather essential details about the property, project, and approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Property Owner Information" section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the property address, parcel number, and type of property in the "Property Information" section.
3. Describe the proposed project, including the type of project, scope, and estimated cost, in the "Project Details" section.
4. If applicable, upload design plans or drawings, and describe the building materials to be used.
5. Specify the project timeline, including the proposed start and completion dates.
6. Finally, indicate the date when approval is requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Property Owner Information
* **Property Owner Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 2. Property Information
* **Property Address**: Enter the full street address of the property.
* **Parcel Number or Property ID**: If applicable, enter the parcel number or property ID.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property, such as single-family residence, multi-family dwelling, commercial, or mixed use.

### 3. Project Details
* **Description of Proposed Work**: Describe the work being proposed, including the type of project, scope, and estimated cost.
* **Type of Project**: Select the type of project, such as addition, renovation, exterior modification, landscaping, etc.
* **Project Scope**: Specify the scope of the project.
* **Estimated Cost**: Enter the estimated cost of the project.

### 4. Design Submission
* **Architect or Designer Name**: Enter the name of the architect or designer responsible for the project.
* **Design Plans or Drawings Attached**: Indicate if design plans or drawings are attached.
* **Exterior Rendering or Elevation**: Indicate if an exterior rendering or elevation is attached.
* **Building Materials Description**: Describe the building materials to be used.

### 5. Timeline
* **Proposed Start Date**: Enter the proposed start date of the project.
* **Estimated Completion Date**: Enter the estimated completion date of the project.
* **Date Approval Requested**: Enter the date when approval is requested.
