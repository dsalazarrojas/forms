# Whole Packet Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for whole packet approval. It is used to collect information from team members who will review a packet. This form is typically used for internal approval purposes, but it's not clear if it's being used for duplicate submissions. Please ensure that you are not duplicating efforts and that your submission is relevant to your team's approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all team members who will review the packet.
2. Enter any additional comments or notes about the packet.
3. Choose your approval decision (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
4. Enter the review date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`): Select all team members who will review this packet. This includes team members who will be directly involved in the packet review process. For example, "Yes" indicates that the person has agreed to review the packet, while "No" indicates that they have not.
* **Packet Review** (`packet_review`, `text`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the packet, if necessary. This can include details about the review process, concerns, or observations.
* **Approval Decision** (`approval_decision`, `select_one`): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Approved: Indicate that the packet is approved as is.
	+ Denied: Indicate that the packet is not approved and requires revisions.
	+ Pending: Indicate that the packet is pending further review.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the packet, if necessary.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`): Enter the date on which the packet was reviewed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, `time`): Enter the time on which the packet was reviewed.
