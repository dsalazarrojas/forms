# Faculty Led Workshop Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Led Workshop Approval Request Form is designed to collect information from faculty members to propose and plan a workshop, and to seek approval from authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Faculty Led Workshop Proposal" page and fill in the workshop proposal details, including expected and actual outcomes.
2. Add faculty members involved in the workshop by selecting from the options provided.
3. Choose the dates and location for the workshop.
4. Select the sponsor information, including the sponsor's department and email.
5. Fill in the remaining fields with the required information, including the number of participants, budget, and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Led Workshop Proposal** (`1`, `number`, required/optional): This field is used to enter the proposal details for the workshop, including the expected and actual outcomes.
* **Faculty Members** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the faculty members involved in the workshop.
* **Dates** (`3`, `date`, optional): Enter the start and end dates for the workshop.
* **Location** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location for the workshop.
* **Expected Outcome** (`5`, `note`, optional): Enter the expected outcome for the workshop.
* **Actual Outcome** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Enter the actual outcome for the workshop.
* **Comments** (`7`, `text`, optional): Add any comments or additional information about the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Submitter** (`8`, `email`, optional): Enter your name and email address.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Requester** (`9`, `email`, optional): Enter the name and email address of the requestor.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Department** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the department for the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Departmental Email** (`11`, `email`, optional): Enter the departmental email address.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Departmental Phone** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the departmental phone number.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form College** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Select the college for the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Faculty Contact** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the faculty contact person's name.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Faculty Email** (`15`, `email`, optional): Enter the faculty contact person's email address.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Collaborators** (`16`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any collaborators involved in the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Number of Participants** (`17`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected number of participants.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Funding Sought** (`18`, `number`, optional): Enter the funding sought for the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Budget** (`19`, `number`, optional): Enter the budget for the workshop.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Sponsor ID** (`20`, `text`, optional): Enter the sponsor's ID.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Sponsor Name** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter the sponsor's name.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Sponsor Department** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the sponsor's department.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Sponsor Email** (`23`, `email`, optional): Enter the sponsor's email address.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Sponsor Phone** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Faculty-Led Workshop Approval Request Form Comments** (`25`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the workshop.
