## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather information about a student's work study plan, which includes details about their program, course title, study hours, and progress status. This information will help the student's supervisor and administrators track their progress and provide necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the student's name
2.  Select the student's program
3.  Enter the course title
4.  Record the start and end dates of the student's work study plan
5.  Enter the total number of days and hours the student will be studying
6.  Record the location where the student will be studying
7.  Add any comments from the supervisor or student
8.  Select the current status of the student's work study plan
9.  Record the date when the form was submitted
10.  Enter the version of the form
11.  Enter the person assigned to the student's work study plan
12.  Enter the person who approved the student's work study plan
13.  Record the date when the student's work study plan was approved
14.  Enter any reason for rejection
15.  Enter the person who updated the form
16.  Record the date when the form was updated
17.  Enter the person who submitted the form
18.  Record the date when the form was submitted
19.  Enter the tool assigned to the student
20.  Add any additional notes
21.  Add further additional notes
