# Expense Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Approval Checklist is a form used to track and manage employee expenses within your organization. It serves as a centralized tool for employees to submit expenses for approval, allowing management to monitor and approve or reject requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all necessary information before starting. Review the form fields below to understand what you need to provide.
* Select the category that best describes your expense (e.g., Travel, Office Supplies).
* Fill in your expense amount, date, and description accurately.
* Choose the correct approved person from the list of available options.
* Select the status of your expense (Approved or Rejected).
* If necessary, choose the category and people involved (Submitter, Approver, Reviewer).
* Enter any additional notes or comments for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Form (Expense Form, text, required)**: Please fill in this field with a brief description of your expense.
* **Expense Type (Expense Type, select_one, required)**: Select the category that best describes your expense, such as "Travel" for a trip-related expense or "Office Supplies" for office equipment purchases.
* **Expense Amount (Expense Amount, number, required)**: Enter the exact cost of your expense, including any additional fees or taxes.
* **Expense Date (Expense Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of your expense, ensuring it aligns with company policies on expense submission and approval.
* **Expense Description (Expense Description, text, required)**: Provide a detailed description of your expense, including any relevant details.
* **Approved By (Approved By, select_one, required)**: Choose the person or team member responsible for approving your expense.
* **Expense Status (Expense Status, select_one, required)**: Select "Approved" if your expense has been verified and validated; "Rejected" if your expense requires further review or adjustment.
* **Expense Category (Expense Category, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the categories relevant to your expense (e.g., Travel, Office Supplies, Entertainment).
* **Submitted By (Submitted By, select_one, required)**: Choose the person who submitted the expense (usually the employee submitting it).
* **Review Date (Review Date, date, required)**: Enter the date your expense is reviewed by the designated reviewer.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required)**: Add any additional comments or information that could be helpful for the reviewers or approvers.
* **Reviewed By (Reviewed By, select_one, required)**: Confirm if your expense was reviewed and verified by the designated reviewer.
* **Approval Status (Approval Status, select_one, required)**: Select the final status of your expense, which should match the "Expense Status" selected earlier.
