# Sticker Usage Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain agreement on the usage of stickers in the workplace. It helps to document and clarify the expectations and guidelines for sticker placement, ensuring a smooth and respectful work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your personal information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. In the "Sticker Placement Requests" field, describe the specific requests you have regarding sticker placement, such as where on the computer or office area you would like to place your stickers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to include.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Sticker Placement Requests** (`sticker_placement_requests`, text, required: false): Describe the specific requests you have regarding sticker placement, such as where on the computer or office area you would like to place your stickers.
