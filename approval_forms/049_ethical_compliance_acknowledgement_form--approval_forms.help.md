# Ethical Compliance Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to ensure that employees acknowledge and commit to our company's Code of Conduct and Ethical Compliance Standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page:
- Click on each page to navigate through the sections.
- Fill in the required fields with your relevant information.
- Ensure you select the correct options in the acknowledgement section.
- Finally, submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (1, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel record.
* **Department** (2, text, optional): If applicable, please enter your department name or team name where you work.
* **Job Title** (3, text, optional): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Report Date** (4, date, optional): If you are reporting an incident, enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Review Date** (5, date, optional): If you are reviewing an incident, enter the date you reviewed it.
* **Acknowledgement** (6, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options:
	+ Compliance with our Code of Conduct is mandatory.
	+ I am aware and understand the Code of Conduct.
* **Other (please specify)** (7, text, optional): If you have any other relevant information or comments, please provide them here.
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (8, date, optional): Enter the date you acknowledge this document.

## Tips
- Please make sure you understand and commit to our Code of Conduct before signing this form.
- Ensure your acknowledgement is accurate and truthful.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or contact our Compliance Officer.
