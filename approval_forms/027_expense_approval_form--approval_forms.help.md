# Expense Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee expenses for approval. This form is used to track employee expenses, allowing for efficient approval and reimbursement process.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in the employee's name from the list of options provided under "Employee".
*   Enter the date of the expense in the "Expense Date" field. This date will be used to process the expense.
*   Select the relevant category of the expense from the list of options provided under "Category". This can be "Food", "Travel", or "Other".
*   Enter the amount of the expense in the "Expense Amount" field. This should be an accurate representation of the expense.
*   Provide a brief description of the expense in the "Description" field.
*   Select the name of the approver from the list of options provided under "Approver". This should match the employee's direct supervisor.
*   Optionally, upload any supporting documents or files in the "Attachments" field.
*   Provide any additional comments on the expense in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee**: <name> (<type>, required): Select the employee who incurred the expense from the provided options. This field is required to ensure accurate expense tracking.
*   **Expense Date**: <date> (<type>, required): Enter the date of the expense.
*   **Category**: <select_multiple> (<type>, required): Select the category of the expense from the provided options. This field is optional.
*   **Expense Amount**: <number> (<type>, required): Enter the accurate amount of the expense.
*   **Description**: <text> (<type>, required): Provide a brief description of the expense.
*   **Approver**: <select_one> (<type>, required): Select the name of the approver from the provided options. This field should match the employee's direct supervisor.
*   **Attachments**: <text> (<type>, optional): Optionally, upload any supporting documents or files related to the expense.
*   **Comments**: <text> (<type>, optional): Provide any additional comments on the submitted expense report.
