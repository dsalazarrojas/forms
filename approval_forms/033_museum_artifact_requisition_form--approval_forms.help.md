# Museum Artifact Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used for requesting the loan of a museum artifact to another institution or individual for a specified period of time. The form helps us track and manage the loan process, ensuring that artifacts are properly handled, stored, and returned.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately to ensure that your loan request is considered complete and properly processed.
- Make sure to provide all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (*), as they are necessary for form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **first_page** (Text, Optional): This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information or comments about your loan request.
- **artifact_details** (Number, Optional): Enter the details of the artifact you are requesting, such as its title, description, or object number.
- **loan_requestor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and title of the person or institution requesting the loan.
- **contact_details** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted about the loan:
  - **email**: We will contact you by email.
  - **phone**: We will contact you by phone.
  - **other**: You can enter your preferred contact method in the comments section.
- **delivery_instructions** (Text, Optional): Provide instructions on how you would like us to deliver the artifact (e.g., UPS, special handling requirements).
- **artifact_location** (Text, Optional): Specify where the artifact is currently being stored in our collections.
- **return_instructions** (Text, Optional): Provide instructions on how you would like the artifact returned (e.g., same way as delivery, special packaging requirements).
- **storage_location** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose where the artifact would be stored during the loan period:
  - **a**: Option A
  - **b**: Option B
  - **c**: Option C
- **loan_terms** (Number, Optional): Enter any special terms or conditions for the loan (e.g., duration, fees).
- **end_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you anticipate returning the artifact.
- **start_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you anticipate receiving the loaned artifact.
- **duration_of_loan** (Text, Optional): Enter the duration of the loan period.
- **return_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you anticipate returning the loaned artifact.
- **comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or remarks about your loan request.
- **approver** (Select One, Optional): Choose who should review and approve your loan request:
  - **curator**: Our curator will review and approve the loan request.
  - **manager**: Our collections manager will review and approve the loan request.
  - **other**: Choose "other" and enter the name of another approver in the comments section.
- **status** (Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your loan request:
  - **pending**: The loan request is pending approval.
  - **approved**: The loan request is approved.
  - **rejected**: The loan request is rejected.
- **comments_by_approver** (Text, Optional): If your loan request is rejected or approved, provide comments from the approver.
- **approved_by** (Select One, Optional): Choose who approved your loan request:
  - **curator**: Our curator approved the loan request.
  - **manager**: Our collections manager approved the loan request.
  - **other**: Choose "other" and enter the name of the approver in the comments section.
- **approved_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the loan request was approved.
- **rejected_by** (Select One, Optional): Choose who rejected your loan request:
  - **curator**: Our curator rejected the loan request.
  - **manager**: Our collections manager rejected the loan request.
  - **other**: Choose "other" and enter the name of the rejecter in the comments section.
- **rejected_reason** (Text, Optional): Enter the reason your loan request was rejected.
- **storage_condition** (Text, Optional): Provide information about the storage condition of the loaned artifact.
- **storage_duration** (Text, Optional): Enter the storage duration of the loaned artifact.
- **artifact_condition** (Text, Optional): Provide information about the condition of the loaned artifact.

Note: Some fields are optional and not marked as required, but we recommend filling them out to ensure your loan request is complete and accurately processed.
