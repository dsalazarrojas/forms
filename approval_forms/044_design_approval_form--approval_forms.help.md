# Design Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Approval Form is a tool used to track the status of designs and gather feedback from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  The design form is divided into several sections.
2.  Each section is designed to gather specific information from the designer and client.
3.  Please review each section carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Designs** (design_approval_form): This field is where you can enter a brief description of the design you are submitting.
* **Review Designs** (design_approvals): In this field, select all the designs that you want to review and provide feedback on.
* **Designer Info** (designer_info): This field is for the designer to add any relevant information about their design.
* **Client Response** (client_response): Choose how the client responded to the design, whether with a simple yes or no.
* **Upload Designs** (design_files): Here, you can upload files related to the design.
* **Client Feedback** (client_feedback): Select all the feedback you want to provide to the client.
* **Designer Notes** (designer_notes): Add any additional notes about the design.
* **Client Notes** (client_notes): Add any notes from the client regarding the design.
* **Design Status** (design_status): Set the design status to active or inactive to indicate its readiness for further work.
* **Submit** (submit): Click this button to submit the form after filling out all the necessary fields.
* **Share Link** (share_link): Enter a share link for the design.
* **Custom Design** (custom_design): Select whether the design is custom-made or not.
