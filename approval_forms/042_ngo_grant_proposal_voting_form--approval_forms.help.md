# NGO Grant Proposal Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Grant Proposal Voting Form is designed to collect evaluations and voting results for NGO grant proposals. Reviewers and submitters will use this form to provide feedback on proposal submissions, including grant amounts, review committee details, and submission dates. The form is crucial for ensuring that the evaluation process is thorough and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Grant Proposal Evaluation**: Please answer the questions on the first page to evaluate the grant proposal.
2. Select your response from the provided options or type in your answer.
3. For the grant amount fields, enter the start and end amounts of the proposed grant.
4. Choose your response to the "Review Committee" question, and optionally select a review committee from the options.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number (if applicable).
6. Add a note to the "Review Committee Note" field, if desired.
7. Enter the submission date and time on the second page.
8. Fill out the remaining fields with your contact information and submission details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grant Proposal Evaluation**: This section is for evaluating the grant proposal. Please answer the questions according to the provided options.
* **Grant Amount**: Enter the start and end amounts of the proposed grant. This field is required.
* **Grant Amount Range**: This field is optional and only required if you need to specify a range.
* **Review Committee**: Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are part of the review committee.
* **Review Committee Select**: Select a committee to review the grant proposal (if applicable).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (if applicable).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the review committee.
* **Review Committee Name**: Enter the name of the review committee (if applicable).
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the review committee.
* **Review Committee Email**: Enter the email address of the review committee (if applicable).
* **Review Committee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the review committee (if applicable).
* **Review Committee Note**: Add any additional notes about the review committee.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Review Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer (if applicable).
* **Reviewer Note**: Add any additional notes about the reviewer.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Submission Summary**: Provide a brief summary of the grant proposal submission.
