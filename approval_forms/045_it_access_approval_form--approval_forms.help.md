# it_access_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The it_access_approval_form is used to collect information for internal access approval requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out the "Requestor Info" section with your name and contact information.
* Enter a valid "User ID" associated with your account.
* Select the correct "Resource Name" for the system or resource you are requesting access to.
* Choose the appropriate "Access Level" required for the resource.
* If necessary, enter the date of the request and the date the request was approved or denied.
* The "Approver Name" and "Approver Department" fields should be filled out by the person reviewing the request.
* Select the "Approval Status" of the request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* Add any additional comments or information in the "Comments" field.
* Finally, enter your "Email" address (if required by the organization).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requestor Info**:
	+ (Requestor Info, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **User Id**:
	+ (User Id, number, required): Enter a valid user ID associated with your account.
* **Resource Name**:
	+ (Resource Name, text, required): Select the correct resource name for the system or resource you are requesting access to.
* **Access Level**:
	+ (Access Level, select_one, required): Choose the appropriate access level required for the resource.
* **Request Date**:
	+ (Request Date, date, required): If necessary, enter the date of the request.
* **Approval Date**:
	+ (Approval Date, date, required): If necessary, enter the date the request was approved or denied.
* **Approver Name**:
	+ (Approver Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person reviewing the request.
* **Approver Department**:
	+ (Approver Department, text, required): Enter the department of the person reviewing the request.
* **Approval Status**:
	+ (Approval Status, select_one, required): Select the approval status of the request (Approved, Denied, or Pending).
* **Comments**:
	+ (Comments, note, required): Add any additional comments or information about the request.
* **Email**:
	+ (Email, email, required): Enter your email address (if required by the organization).

Note: Fields marked as "required" are needed for the form to be submitted.
