# Authorized User Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to systems, resources, and services within an organization. It captures essential information needed for approval and access management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Request Date** field with the date you are requesting access to be granted.
2. Enter your **Employee Full Name**, **Employee ID**, **Employee Email**, and **Department Name** to identify yourself and your department.
3. Provide your **Current Job Title** and **Manager or Supervisor Name** to establish your job role and direct manager.
4. Select the **Type of Access Requested** (e.g., Software Application, Database Access, etc.) and specify the **System or Resource Name** that requires access.
5. Choose the **Access Level Required** (e.g., Read Only, View and Download, etc.).
6. Provide a brief **Business Justification** for requesting access.
7. If this is a temporary access, enter the **Date Access Needed** and **End Date (if temporary)**. Otherwise, select **Permanent**.
8. Choose the **Access Duration Type** (e.g., Temporary - Specific Period, Until Project Completion, etc.).
9. If this access is related to a specific project, enter the **Project Name**.
10. Confirm that you have signed a **Confidentiality Agreement** and have completed relevant **Security Training**.
11. Select the **Manager Approval Status** and enter any **Manager Comments**.
12. If your manager has already reviewed and approved this request, select **Approved**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are requesting access to be granted.
* **Employee Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee badge or ID card.
* **Employee ID**: Your employee identification number or badge number.
* **Employee Email**: Your work email address.
* **Department Name**: The name of your department.
* **Current Job Title**: Your current job title or position.
* **Manager or Supervisor Name**: The name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Manager Email**: Your manager's email address.
* **Type of Access Requested**: Select the type of access you are requesting (e.g., Software Application, Database Access, etc.).
* **System or Resource Name**: Enter the name of the system or resource requiring access.
* **Access Level Required**: Choose the level of access you need (e.g., Read Only, View and Download, etc.).
* **Business Justification**: Provide a brief explanation of why you need access to this system or resource.
* **Date Access Needed**: Enter the date you need access to start, if this access is temporary.
* **End Date (if temporary)**: Enter the date this access can be removed, if temporary.
* **Access Duration Type**: Choose the duration of access you need (e.g., Temporary - Specific Period, Until Project Completion, etc.).
* **Project Name**: If this access is related to a specific project, enter the project name.
* **Confidentiality Agreement Signed**: Confirm if you have signed a confidentiality agreement.
* **Security Training Completed**: Confirm if you have completed relevant security training.
* **Manager Approval Status**: Select the status of your manager's approval (e.g., Pending Approval, Approved, etc.).
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from your manager regarding this access request.
* **IT Department Processing Status**: Select the status of IT department's processing (e.g., Awaiting IT Review, In Progress, etc.).
* **Additional Notes or Information**: Enter any other details or comments about this access request.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have provided all required information.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult your manager or the IT department for guidance.
* This form is for internal use only; do not share it with external parties.
