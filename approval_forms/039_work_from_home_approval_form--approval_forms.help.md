# Work From Home Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request approval for work from home arrangements. It's used to propose flexible work arrangements that allow employees to work from home.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose the "Manager Approval" option in the "Manager Approval" section.
2. Fill out the remaining fields according to your needs, including "Remote Work Arrangement Request", "Start Date", "End Date", "Work Hours", "Work Frequency", "Work Location", "Work Status", and "Comments".
3. Ensure your manager reviews and signs the "Manager Approval" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Work Arrangement Request** (Text, Required): This field is used to briefly describe your work from home arrangement. Include details about your work hours and location if necessary.
* **Manager Approval** (Select One, Required): This field is for your manager to approve or decline your request. Please have your manager choose "Yes" or "No".
* **Start Date** (Date, Required): This field is used to specify the start date of your arrangement. Please use the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **End Date** (Date, Required): This field is used to specify the end date of your arrangement. Please use the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Work Hours** (Number, Required): This field is used to specify how many hours you plan to work per day. This can be a helpful detail for your manager to consider.
* **Work Frequency** (Select Multiple, Required): This field is used to specify if you plan to work full time, part time, or have a variable schedule.
* **Work Location** (Text, Required): This field is used to specify where you'll be working from. This can include your home address, coffee shop, or any other location that suits your needs.
* **Work Status** (Select One, Required): This field is used to specify if you're active, inactive, or pending on the arrangement. This is important for your manager to understand your current status.
* **Comments** (Note, Required): This field is a text area where you can add any additional comments or details about your arrangement. This can be helpful for your manager to understand any specific requirements or needs you may have.
* **Email** (Email, Required): This field is for your contact information, which your manager may use to reach out to you about your arrangement.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): This field is for your phone number, which your manager may use to reach out to you about your arrangement.

Note: The fields marked as "Required" are mandatory and must be filled out before submitting the form.
