# Risk Assessment Approval Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Approval Application Form is a crucial tool for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks within your team or department. This form ensures that all relevant information is captured and reviewed, making it easier to evaluate and address potential risks to projects, assets, or operations. 

## How to Complete This Form
1. **Review the Risk Assessment**: Fill in the risk assessment section to describe the identified risk, providing as much detail as possible.
2. **Choose the Severity Level**: Select one or more severity levels to categorize the risk's impact.
3. **Provide Mitigation Plan**: Briefly describe the steps you will take to mitigate the identified risk.
4. **Risk Owners and Approvals**:
   - Select the risk owner(s) involved.
   - Choose the internal approval date and who approved it.
5. **Risk Status and Comments**:
   - Select the current status of the risk (Active, Resolved, Closed).
   - Add any additional comments relevant to the risk.
6. **Assign and Follow-up**:
   - Select the team or individual responsible for managing the risk.
   - Enter the email address and phone number of the person to contact for further information or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment (1)**: Describe the risk in detail, including its causes, effects, and any relevant facts.
* **Severity (2)**: Choose one or more severity levels to categorize the risk's potential impact:
  - **Low**: Minor and easily managed risks.
  - **Medium**: Significant but not catastrophic risks.
  - **High**: Major or potentially catastrophic risks.
* **Mitigation Plan (3)**: Outline the steps to take to mitigate or address the risk, keeping it concise but detailed enough to be actionable.
* **Risk Owners (4)**: Select all individuals or teams directly involved with managing the risk.
* **Internal Approval Date (5)**: Record the date when the internal approval was granted or received.
* **Internal Approved By (6)**: Select the person or group that granted internal approval.
* **Risk Status (7)**: Choose the current status of the risk:
  - **Active**: Still being managed or under review.
  - **Resolved**: Successfully managed or resolved.
  - **Closed**: Fully closed without ongoing action required.
* **Additional Comments (8)**: Add any supplementary information relevant to the risk's assessment or progress.
* **Comments (9)**: Enter any further comments or observations about the risk or process.
* **Assigned To (10)**: Select the team or individual accountable for managing the risk.
* **Email To (11)**: Enter the contact email address for further updates or information.
* **Phone Number (12)**: Provide the contact phone number for immediate or urgent matters related to the risk.
