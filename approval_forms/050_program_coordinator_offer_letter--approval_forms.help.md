# Program Coordinator Offer Letter - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create an Offer Letter for a Program Coordinator position. It is a standard template that outlines the terms of employment, including the job title, job description, salary, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Program Coordinator Name** in the first field.
2. Select the **Offer Letter Date** using the date picker calendar.
3. Enter the **Manager Signature Date** using the date picker calendar.
4. Enter the **Manager Signature** in the signature field.
5. Enter the **Program Coordinator Designation** in the brief text field.
6. Enter the **Offer Letter Details** in the brief text field.
7. Enter the **Approved By** in the brief text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Coordinator Name**: Enter the name of the Program Coordinator being offered the position.
* **Offer Letter Date**: Enter the date the offer letter is being issued.
* **Manager Signature Date**: Enter the date the manager signs the offer letter.
* **Manager Signature**: Enter the manager's signature.
* **Program Coordinator Designation**: Enter the title or role of the Program Coordinator being offered.
* **Offer Letter Details**: Enter any additional details or conditions of the offer.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person or department that approved the offer.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Use the date picker calendar to select the dates accurately.
* Double-check the spelling and grammar of the text fields before submitting the form.
