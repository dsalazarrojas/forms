# Global Signatory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide their contact information and sign their name for global signatory purposes. It's intended for corporate or business settings where users need to share their details for approval or verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Name** in the provided text field.
2. Enter your **email address** in the email field, ensuring it's a valid email format.
3. Select your **Role** from the text field. You can enter a brief description of your role.
4. Enter your **Company** in the text field.
5. Enter your **Department** in the text field.
6. Enter your **Location** in the text field.
7. Finally, sign your name in the **Signature** field using your preferred method (e.g., handwritten or digital).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Role**: A brief description of your role or position.
* **Company**: Enter your company's name.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team name.
* **Location**: Enter your office or work location.
* **Signature**: Sign your name using your preferred method.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the form administrators at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* This form is for personal or business use, not for mass signatory. If you need to sign multiple instances, please contact the form administrators for guidance.
