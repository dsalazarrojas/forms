# Seminar Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request permission for a student to attend a seminar. Staff will review and process the request to determine whether the seminar is approved or denied.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the seminar you wish to attend from the seminar permission request dropdown list.
2. Enter the student's name in the Student Name field.
3. Enter the student's email address in the Student Email field.
4. Enter the name of the course the student is attending in the Course Name field.
5. Enter the date of the seminar in the Seminar Date field.
6. Enter the start time of the seminar in the Seminar Start Time field.
7. Enter the duration of the seminar in the Seminar Duration field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Permission Request** (`seminar_permission_request_form`): Select the seminar you wish to attend from the dropdown list.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name as they are enrolled in the course.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the student's email address where they can be reached.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Enter the name of the course the student is attending.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`): Enter the date of the seminar in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Seminar Start Time** (`seminar_start_time`): Enter the start time of the seminar in the 12-hour clock format (e.g., 2:00 PM).
* **Seminar Duration** (`seminar_duration`): Enter the length of the seminar in minutes.
