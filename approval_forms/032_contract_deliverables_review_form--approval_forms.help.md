# Contract Deliverables Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from reviewers regarding the review of contract deliverables. Please ensure that all necessary information is collected to help with the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
* Enter your name, job title, and department to ensure that your review is properly attributed and accounted for.
* Select the date of your review to ensure that you are reviewing the correct deliverables.
* Provide the contract number for easy identification.
* Select the title of the contract being reviewed.
* Identify the vendor or contractor that provided the deliverables.
* Enter the name of the deliverable being reviewed.
* Choose the category of the deliverable.
* Enter the date the deliverable was submitted.
* Enter the date the deliverable was due.
* Select the current status of the deliverable.
* Rate the quality of the deliverable.
* Rate the completeness of the deliverable.
* Answer questions about whether all requirements were met and whether the deliverable is compliant with specifications.
* Enter the number of defects found in the deliverable.
* Provide a description of the defects found.
* Summarize the testing results.
* Make a final decision about whether to accept or reject the deliverable.
* If necessary, provide a revision deadline and additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, which will be used to attribute the review.
* **Reviewer Title** (`reviewer_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title to ensure that your review is properly accounted for.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Enter your department to ensure that your review is attributed to the correct team.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your review to ensure that you are reviewing the correct deliverables.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contract reference for easy identification.
* **Contract Title** (`contract_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the contract being reviewed.
* **Vendor or Contractor** (`vendor_or_contractor`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the vendor or contractor that provided the deliverables.
* **Deliverable Name** (`deliverable_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the deliverable being reviewed.
* **Deliverable Type** (`deliverable_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of the deliverable (e.g. Report, Software or application, etc.).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the deliverable was submitted.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the deliverable was due. Note: This field is optional, but it may be necessary for future reference.
* **Deliverable Status** (`deliverable_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the deliverable (e.g. On time, Late, etc.).
* **Quality Rating** (`quality_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the deliverable (e.g. Excellent, Very good, etc.).
* **Completeness Rating** (`completeness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the completeness of the deliverable (e.g. Complete, Mostly complete, etc.).
* **Requirements Met** (`requirements_met`, `select_one`, required): Answer questions about whether all requirements were met.
* **Specifications Compliance** (`specifications_compliance`, `select_one`, required): Answer questions about whether the deliverable is compliant with specifications.
* **Defects Found** (`defects_found`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of defects found in the deliverable.
* **Defects Description** (`defects_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a description of the defects found.
* **Testing Results** (`testing_results`, `text`, optional): Summarize the testing results.
* **Acceptance Decision** (`acceptance_decision`, `select_one`, required): Make a final decision about whether to accept or reject the deliverable.
* **Revision Deadline** (`revision_deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter a revision deadline if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, `text`, required): Type your name as a signature.
