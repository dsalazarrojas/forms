# sku_deactivation_approval_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sku_deactivation_approval_request_form is used to request deactivation of a stock-keeping unit (SKU). This form helps track and record the approval process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and reviewed by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Request Details page with the relevant information about the deactivation request.
2.  Select the SKU for which you are requesting deactivation from the list.
3.  Provide the reason for deactivation.
4.  Enter any additional comments or notes.
5.  Choose the status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
6.  If rejected, provide comments for rejection.
7.  Add any additional comments.
8.  If approved, select the approved person and enter the approved date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Request Details
*   **Request Details** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief summary of the deactivation request.

### Select sku
*   **Select sku** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the SKU for which you are requesting deactivation from the provided list.

### Reason for Deactivation
*   **Reason for Deactivation** (text, required/optional): Enter the reason for deactivating the SKU.

### Comments
*   **Comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the deactivation request.

### Approval Request Date
*   **Approval Request Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was made.

### Approved By
*   **Approved By** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the person who approved the request.

### Approved Date
*   **Approved Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was approved.

### Request Status
*   **Request Status** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).

### Comments for Rejection
*   **Comments for Rejection** (text, required/optional): Enter comments for the reason of rejection if the request is rejected.

### Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the deactivation request.

### Reason for Rejection
*   **Reason for Rejection** (text, required/optional): Enter the reason for rejecting the deactivation request if the request is rejected.

### SKU Deactivation Date
*   **SKU Deactivation Date** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the SKU was deactivated.

### Approved By Email
*   **Approved By Email** (text, required/optional): Enter the email of the person who approved the request.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth deactivation process.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your supervisor or team lead for assistance.
*   Review the request carefully before submitting to ensure that all necessary information is provided.
*   If you are rejecting the request, be sure to provide a clear and concise reason for rejection.
