# Purchase Request Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and process purchase requests in a streamlined manner. It helps the approver review and verify the purchase request before making a decision.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. On the first page, provide a brief description of the purchase request. This will help the approver understand the context and purpose of the request.
2. On the second page, enter a detailed description of the purchase request, including any relevant information such as costs, benefits, and justifications.
3. On the third and final page, upload any supporting documentation or attachments related to the purchase request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **purchase_request_approval** (Text field, Optional):
	+ Briefly describe the purchase request, stating the purpose and context.
* **purchase_request_details** (Text field, Optional):
	+ Enter a detailed description of the purchase request, including costs, benefits, and justifications.
* **purchase_request_attachment** (Text field, Optional):
	+ Upload any supporting documentation or attachments related to the purchase request, such as receipts, quotes, or invoices.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise descriptions in the "purchase_request_approval" and "purchase_request_details" fields.
* Use the "purchase_request_attachment" field to attach relevant supporting documentation, such as receipts or quotes, to facilitate quick review and approval.
* If unsure about any part of the form, ask your supervisor or approver for clarification.
