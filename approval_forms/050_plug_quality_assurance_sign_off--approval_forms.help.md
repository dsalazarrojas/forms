# Plug Quality Assurance Sign Off - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the quality assurance sign off for a specific plug. It's meant to capture the details of the inspection process, including the date, inspector's name, and plug's ID. This form is not mandatory, but it's a good practice to use it for tracking and maintaining consistency in quality assurance processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the inspection in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Enter your name as the inspector in the "Inspector" field.
3. Enter the ID of the plug being inspected in the "Plug ID" field.
4. Select the status of the inspection (Pass or Fail) in the "Status" field.
5. Provide any additional comments about the inspection in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter your name as the approver in the "Approver" field (note: this field is not required).
7. (Optional) Enter the date of your approval in the "Date of Approvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Plug Quality Assurance Sign Off Form** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form that you are about to fill out.
* **Inspection Date** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspector** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_inspector`, text, optional): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Plug ID** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_plug_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the plug being inspected.
* **Status** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
* **Comments** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about the inspection.
* **Approver** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_approver`, text, optional): Enter your name as the approver.
* **Date of Approvement** (`plug_quality_assurance_sign_off_approvement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your approval (note: this field is not required).
