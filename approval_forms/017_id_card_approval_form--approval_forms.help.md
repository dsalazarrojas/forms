# ID Card Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ID Card Approval Form is used to request and approve ID cards for employees. It is a crucial tool for ensuring that all employees' ID cards are issued after receiving approval from their supervisors and department members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the relevant fields.
2. Select your Department and Job Title from the dropdown menus.
3. If applicable, fill in your Date of Birth and Employee ID numbers.
4. Select your supervisor's approval status from the dropdown menu.
5. Add any additional comments or notes as required.
6. Finally, submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your ID card.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your ID card.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the given options (Finance, Marketing, HR, IT).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required): Select your job title from the given options (Analyst, Associate, Manager, Specialist).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth if required.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Supervisor Approvals** (`supervisor_approvals`, select_multiple, required): Select the approval status from the dropdown menu (Approve, Reject).

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your department and job title before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary approvals from your supervisor and department members before submitting the form.
* Keep your comments and notes concise and clear.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.
