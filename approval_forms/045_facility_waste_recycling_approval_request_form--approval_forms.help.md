# Facility Waste Recycling Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Waste Recycling Approval Request Form is used by facilities to request approval for their waste recycling programs. This form is used to gather information about the facility's waste management practices and ensure compliance with internal guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your User Name to identify yourself and the facility requesting approval.
2.  Enter your Facility ID, which is the unique identifier for your facility.
3.  Select the types of waste you are recycling (e.g., Paper, Plastic, Glass, Metal, or Organic).
4.  Provide details about your recycling program, including the start and end dates, review date, and any relevant documentation.
5.  Upload any supporting documentation for your program.
6.  Enter the dates for your program's start and end dates.
7.  Select the review date for your program.
8.  List any personnel involved in the program, including their names, titles, and contact information.
9.  Enter the facility's address, contact information, and website.
10. Add any notes about the facility's waste management practices.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the form.
12. Add any notes about the submission.
13. Enter the facility's signature to indicate approval.
14. Enter the submitter's signature to confirm submission.
15. Add any notes from the approver.
16. Enter the approver's signature to confirm approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Facility ID**: Enter the unique identifier for your facility.
* **Waste Types**: Select the types of waste you are recycling (e.g., Paper, Plastic, Glass, Metal, Organic).
* **Recycling Program Details**: Enter the details about your recycling program, including the start and end dates, and review date.
* **Documentation Upload**: Upload any supporting documentation for your program.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of your recycling program.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of your recycling program.
* **Review Date**: Enter the review date for your program.
* **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person approving your program.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of your facility.
* **Facility Contact**: Enter the contact information for your facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of your facility.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of your facility.
* **Facility Website**: Enter the website of your facility.
* **Facility Notes**: Add any notes about your facility's waste management practices.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Title**: Enter the title of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Add any notes about the submission.
* **Facility Signature**: Enter the facility's signature to indicate approval.
* **Submitter Signature**: Enter the submitter's signature to confirm submission.
* **Approver Notes**: Add any notes from the approver.
* **Approver Signature**: Enter the approver's signature to confirm approval.
* **Facility Waste Stream**: Select the type of waste stream your facility is involved in (e.g., Single Stream, Multiple Streams, Both).
