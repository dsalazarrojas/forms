# Vendor Due Diligence Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Due Diligence Checklist is a form used to collect and document information about potential vendors for due diligence purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the form fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible, ensuring that all relevant information is provided.
2. Review each field carefully and provide any additional notes that may be necessary for the reviewer's understanding.
3. If a field does not apply to your situation, please leave it blank or provide an explanation for why it does not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Required: No)
	+ This field is to gather general information about the vendor, such as their name, address, and description.
* **Contact Information** (Required: No)
	+ In this field, you can enter any contact information of the vendor, including names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
* **Business Information** (Required: No)
	+ In this field, enter information about the vendor's company, such as its name, description, and date of inception.
* **Financial Information** (Required: No)
	+ This field is to capture any relevant financial information about the vendor, including income, expenses, and assets.
* **Security Information** (Required: No)
	+ In this field, you can provide any security-related information about the vendor, such as their security policies and procedures.
* **Operational Information** (Required: No)
	+ This field is for operational information about the vendor, such as their business hours, location, and management structure.
* **Review Information** (Required: No)
	+ In this field, you can enter any relevant review information about the vendor, including any reports or evaluations.
* **Additional Notes** (Required: No)
	+ Use this field to provide any additional notes or comments that may be helpful for the reviewer's understanding.
