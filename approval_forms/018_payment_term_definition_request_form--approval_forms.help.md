# Payment Term Definition Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payment Term Definition Request Form is used to request changes to payment terms. Payment terms are rules governing payment schedules, frequencies, or conditions. This form helps ensure that changes to payment terms are properly documented, reviewed, and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the three options for the "Payment Term Request" field, which indicates the type of request you're making:
	* "Request for payment term change"
	* "No payment term change request"
	* "Payment term change request"
2. If you've selected "Request for payment term change", fill out the "Reason for payment term change" field with a clear explanation of why the change is needed.
3. In the "New Payment Term" field, enter or select the new payment term value, if applicable.
4. Choose the "Payment Date" field is not required for this form.
5. Select at least one "Review Status" option for the request.
6. In the "Review Comments" field, add any relevant comments or explanations for the reviewer.
7. Select the "Payment Term Status" field, which indicates the current status of the request:
	* "Approved"
	* "Rejected"
	* "Pending"
8. If applicable, add a comment for the "Review Status" field.
9. Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to handle the request.
10. Enter the "Review Date" field is not required for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Payment Term Request (1)
* **Payment Term Request** (required/optional): This field indicates the type of payment term change request. Select one of the three options:
	+ "Request for payment term change" (e.g., change payment term from net-30 to net-60)
	+ "No payment term change request" (e.g., to decline or cancel an existing request)
	+ "Payment term change request" (e.g., to request a change to an existing payment term)
### Reason for Payment Term Change (2)
* **Reason for payment term change** (optional): If you've selected "Request for payment term change", fill out this field with a clear explanation of why the change is needed.
### New Payment Term (3)
* **New Payment Term** (optional/number): If the request is to change an existing payment term, enter the new payment term value here.
### Payment Date (4)
* **Payment Date** (optional/date): This field is not required for this form.
### Review Status (5)
* **Review Status** (select_multiple): Select at least one review status:
	+ "Active"
	+ "Inactive"
### Review Comments (6)
* **Review Comments** (optional/text): Add any relevant comments or explanations for the reviewer.
### Payment Term Status (7)
* **Payment Term Status** (select_one): Select the current status of the request:
	+ "Approved"
	+ "Rejected"
	+ "Pending"
### Department (8)
* **Department** (optional/text): Add any additional comments or explanations for the department.
### Reviewer Name (9)
* **Review Name** (optional/text): Enter the name of the reviewer assigned to handle the request.
### Review Date (10)
* **Review Date** (optional/time): This field is not required for this form.
### Payment Term Definition (11)
* **Payment Term Definition** (optional/text): Add any additional comments or explanations for the payment term definition.
### Review Status Note (12)
* **Review status note** (optional/text): Add any additional comments or explanations for the review status.
### Review Status Reviewer (13)
* **Review status reviewer** (optional/text): Enter the name of the reviewer that handled the review status.
### Payment Term Definition Note (14)
* **Payment term definition note** (optional/text): Add any additional comments or explanations for the payment term definition.
