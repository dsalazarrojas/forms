# employee_training_budget_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Budget Approval Form is used to request approval for employee training needs. It is a tool to help employees submit their requests and approvers review and manage these requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, including your name, department, position, and reason for the training request.
2. Provide detailed information about the training course, including the name of the course, start and end dates, duration, location, and cost.
3. Select the approver's name, department, and position from the available options.
4. Specify the approval status of the request.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
6. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Requester** (`training_requester_name`): Enter the name of the person requesting the training.
- **Department** (`training_requester_department`): Select the department of the person requesting the training.
- **Position** (`training_requester_position`): Enter the position of the person requesting the training.
- **Course** (`training_course_name`): Enter the name of the training course requested.
- **Start Date** (`training_course_start_date`): Enter the start date of the training course.
- **End Date** (`training_course_end_date`): Enter the end date of the training course.
- **Duration** (`training_course_duration`): Enter the duration of the training course in hours or days.
- **Location** (`training_course_venue`): Enter the location where the training course will be held.
- **Cost** (`training_course_cost`): Enter the cost of the training course.
- **Reason** (`training_request_reason`): Enter a brief explanation of why the training is needed.
- **Approver** (`training_request_approver_name`): Select the name of the approver for the training request.
- **Approver Department** (`training_request_approver_department`): Select the department of the approver for the training request.
- **Approver Position** (`training_request_approver_position`): Enter the position of the approver for the training request.
- **Approval Status** (`training_approval_status`): Select the current status of the training request (Active or Inactive).
- **Comments** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the request.
- **Contact Information** (`email`, `phone`): Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that your training request is properly reviewed and approved.
