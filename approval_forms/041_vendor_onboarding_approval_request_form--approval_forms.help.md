# Vendor Onboarding Approval Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vendor Onboarding Approval Request Form is used to collect information about a new vendor and request approval for onboarding into the system. This form is used by vendors to provide their details and approval requests for access to the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Information section with your company's details.
2. Provide your Contact Information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your address and phone number for your business.
4. Enter your email address and website (if applicable).
5. Select the type of vendor you are (Retail, Wholesale, or E-commerce).
6. Provide your Tax ID and Bank Account details.
7. Enter your Bank Address and Routing Number.
8. Enter your Account Number and Account Holder details.
9. Provide any additional notes and comments about your business.
10. Choose whether to submit or cancel your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information** (`1`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your company's name and any other details you'd like to provide.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about your application.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your business address.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your business phone number.
* **Email** (`5`, `email`, `required false`):
  Enter your business email address.
* **Website** (`6`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your business website (if applicable).
* **Vendor Type** (`7`, `select_one`, `required false`):
  Select the type of vendor you are (Retail, Wholesale, or E-commerce).
* **Tax ID** (`8`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your Tax ID.
* **Bank Account** (`9`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your bank account number.
* **Routing Number** (`10`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your bank's routing number.
* **Account Number** (`11`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your account number.
* **Account Holder** (`12`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Bank Address** (`13`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your bank's address.
* **Bank Phone** (`14`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your bank's phone number.
* **Account Manager** (`15`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter the name of your account manager.
* **Account Manager Phone** (`16`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter your account manager's phone number.
* **Vendor Status** (`17`, `select_multiple`, `required false`):
  Select the status of your vendor (Pending, Active, or Inactive).
* **Vendor Notes** (`18`, `note`, `required false`):
  Enter any additional notes about your business.
* **Approval Requester** (`19`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter the name of the person requesting approval.
* **Approved By** (`20`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter the name of the person approving your application.
* **Date Approved** (`21`, `date`, `required false`):
  Enter the date your application was approved.
* **Time Approved** (`22`, `time`, `required false`):
  Enter the time your application was approved.
* **Comments** (`23`, `text`, `required false`):
  Enter any comments or notes about your application.
* **Submit** (`24`, `select_one`, `required false`):
  Choose to submit or cancel your application.
* **Notes** (`25`, `note`, `required false`):
  Enter any additional notes about your application.
