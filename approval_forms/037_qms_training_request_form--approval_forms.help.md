# QMS Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The QMS Training Request Form is used to request training on Quality Management System (QMS) topics. It helps the training team to better understand your requirements and provide the necessary training to improve your knowledge and skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Requester Name" and "Requester E-mail" fields, respectively.
2. Choose the topic(s) you are interested in learning about from the "Training Topic" dropdown menu.
3. Select a date and time for the training, taking into account the available slots.
4. Indicate the duration of the training by entering a numerical value in the "Training Duration" field.
5. Choose whether you want the training to be held at the office or remotely in the "Training Location" dropdown menu.
6. Specify the resources you require for the training by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Requested Resources" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name** (`requester_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name so that the training team can address you correctly.
* **Requester E-mail** (`requester_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your team lead's name so that we can inform them of your request.
* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more topics you are interested in learning about from the dropdown menu.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for the training. Note: Please select a date from the calendar when prompted.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available for the training. Note: Please select a time from the calendar when prompted.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of time you need for the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you want the training to be held at the office or remotely.
* **Requested Resources** (`requested_resources`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you need any special resources for the training.

## Tips

* Please fill in your name and email address accurately to ensure proper communication and follow-up.
* Choose a training date and time that works for you and your team.
* Be specific when requesting resources to help us better prepare for your needs.
* If you have any other requirements or preferences, please include them in the comments or additional information section.
