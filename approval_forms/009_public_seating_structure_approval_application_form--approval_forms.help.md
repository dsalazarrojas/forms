# public_seating_structure_approval_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public_seating_structure_approval_application_form is used to collect information from applicants for the public seating structure.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Navigate through each page of the form and fill in the required fields.
2.  For the second page, select the appropriate option from the dropdown menu.
3.  Ensure you provide accurate information for all required fields, as indicated by the red asterisks (*).
4.  For the fields with dropdown menus, select only one option that best suits your situation.
5.  Review your entries before submitting the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, not required): Please fill in this page with your details.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your situation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Middle Name** (`middle_name`, text, optional): If you have a middle name, enter it here.
* **Suffix** (`suffix`, text, optional): If you have a suffix, enter it here.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Direct Superior** (`direct_superior`, text, optional): Enter your direct superior's name.
* **Reporting Line** (`reporting_line`, text, optional): Enter your reporting line.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, text, required): List the team members you are working with.
* **Seating Plan** (`seating_plan`, text, required): Enter your seating plan.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your submission.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your approval.
* **Submission Team Members** (`team_members`, text, optional): List the team members who submitted the form.
* **Seating Plan** (`seating_plan`, text, optional): Enter your seating plan.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information for all required fields.
* Review your entries before submitting the form for approval.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or the relevant department.
