# Tornado Shelter Installation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tornado Shelter Installation Agreement form is used to collect information for installation scheduling and access purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the "General Info" section.
2. Provide your name, address, and contact details in the "Name", "Address", and "Phone" fields.
3. Specify the contact person and their role in the "Contact Person" field.
4. Enter the scheduled start and end dates and times in the "Scheduling" and "Start Date", "End Date", and "Start Time", "End Time" fields.
5. Indicate the access type and person in charge of access in the "Access Type" and "Access Person" fields.
6. Provide additional access information in the "Site Access" field.
7. Enter any necessary notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter a brief description of the installation.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization requesting the installation.
* **Address**: Enter the address where the installation will take place.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for contact and communication.
* **Scheduling**: Enter the scheduled start and end dates and times of the installation.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the installation.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the installation.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the installation.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the installation.
* **Site Access**: Enter any additional information about site access, such as restricted areas or specific instructions.
* **Access Type**: Select the type of access required for the installation (Manager, Employee, or Other).
* **Access Person**: Enter the name of the person with access authority.
* **Access Time**: Enter the time when access is allowed.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the installation.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (note: this field is identical to the previous "Phone" field, please ensure consistency).

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check that the form is correctly filled out and that all necessary information is provided.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, please contact the relevant personnel for clarification.
