# Pay Structure Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on the approval process of a pay structure, including any changes made to the structure, the effective date of the changes, justifications for the changes, and the approvals required for the changes. The form helps ensure that the pay structure is approved by the necessary personnel, such as HR, Finance, or Manager, and provides a clear record of the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Template" page to access the form's template.
2. Fill in the "Structure Changes" field with a brief description of the changes made to the pay structure.
3. Select the "Effective Date" of the changes in the date field.
4. Provide justifications for the changes in the "Justifications" field.
5. Choose the necessary approvals required for the changes from the "Approvals Required" select-one field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Structure Changes
* **Structure Changes** (`Structure Changes`, text, required: false): Describe the changes made to the pay structure, including any updates or modifications.

### Effective Date
* **Effective Date** (`Effective Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the changes to the pay structure will take effect.

### Justifications
* **Justifications** (`Justifications`, text, required: false): Explain the reasons or justifications for the changes made to the pay structure.

### Approvals Required
* **Approvals Required** (`Approvals Required`, select_one, required: false): Choose the necessary approvals required for the changes, such as HR, Finance, or Manager.
