# Academic Advisor Research Leave Of Absance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Advisor Research Leave Of Absance Form is used to request and track research leaves of absence for academic employees. This form helps to ensure that necessary permissions and arrangements are made for the leave, and that the employee's department and university have a clear understanding of their absence.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your details as an academic advisor, including your name, email address, employee ID, department, and current position.
2. Provide a brief description of your years of service at the institution.
3. Specify the requested leave start and end dates.
4. Select the type of research leave you are requesting (e.g. sabbatical, research fellowship, visiting researcher position, etc.).
5. Describe the title and objectives of your research project.
6. Explain where the research will be conducted.
7. If applicable, specify the funding source for the research.
8. Indicate the number of students currently in your advising caseload.
9. Propose a plan for advising coverage during your absence.
10. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Please enter your complete name for identification purposes.
* **Email address**: Enter your university email address for contact and communication.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your university employee ID number for HR purposes.
* **Department**: Select your department for the leave to be approved by the relevant department head.
* **Current position or title**: Indicate your current job title or position.
* **Years of service**: Enter the number of years you have served at the institution.
* **Requested leave start date**: Enter the date you would like to take leave from.
* **Requested leave end date**: Enter the date you expect to return from leave.
* **Type of research leave**: Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g. sabbatical, research fellowship, visiting researcher position, etc.).
* **Title of research project**: Enter a brief title for your research project.
* **Description of research objectives and activities**: Describe the goals, methodology, and expected outcomes of your research project.
* **Where will the research be conducted**: Specify the location or institution where the research will take place.
* **Funding source**: Indicate the funding source for the research project, if applicable.
* **Number of students currently in your advising caseload**: Report the number of students you are currently advising.
* **Proposed plan for advising coverage**: Suggest a plan for ensuring your students are covered during your absence.
* **Department chair or head approval status**: Select the status of approval from the department head (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Dean approval status**: Select the status of approval from the Dean (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
* **Additional notes or comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes you would like to include.
