# Corporate Policy Review Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Policy Review Voting Form is a tool for employees to provide their opinions and feedback on corporate policies. This form helps the organization gather insights and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Policy Title** field with the name of the policy being reviewed.
2. Enter your **Employee Name** in the designated field.
3. Select one of the options under **Vote** to indicate your opinion on the policy.
4. Provide any additional **Feedback** you have on the policy.
5. Enter the **Date** you reviewed the policy in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6. If necessary, enter the **Time** you spent reviewing the policy.
7. If you have any additional notes, enter them in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Title** (`policy_title`, text, required): Enter the name of the policy being reviewed.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Vote** (`vote`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you approve of the policy, 'No' if you do not approve, or 'Abstain' if you have no opinion.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions on the policy.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you reviewed the policy in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the amount of time you spent reviewing the policy.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments on the policy.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise when providing feedback.
* Only complete the form once and not duplicate it.
* Ensure the date and time you enter are accurate.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR department.
