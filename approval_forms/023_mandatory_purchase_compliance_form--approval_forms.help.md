# mandatory_purchase_compliance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mandatory Purchase Compliance Form is a tool used to record and manage mandatory purchases for a company.

## How To Complete This Form

### Required Purchases
*   **Required Purchases** (`1`): Select all purchases that are mandatory. You can choose from the options: "Yes", "No". 

### Purchase Amount
*   **Purchase Amount** (`2`): Enter the total cost of the purchase.

### Purchase Date
*   **Purchase Date** (`3`): Enter the date the purchase was made.

### Purchase Details
*   **Purchase Details** (`4`): Enter any additional information about the purchase.

### Approver Name
*   **Approver Name** (`5`): Enter the name of the person approving the purchase.

### Approver Role
*   **Approver Role** (`6`): Select the role of the person approving the purchase. You can choose from the options: "Yes", "No". 

### Acknowledgement
*   **Acknowledgement** (`7`): Enter a brief acknowledgement of the purchase.

### Approval Status
*   **Approval Status** (`8`): Select the current status of the approval. You can choose from the options: "Active", "Inactive". 

### Comments
*   **Comments** (`9`): Enter any additional comments about the purchase.

### Attachment
*   **Attachment** (`10`): Attach any relevant documents or images to the purchase.

### Department
*   **Department** (`11`): Enter the department associated with the purchase.

### Team
*   **Team** (`12`): Enter the team associated with the purchase.

### Manager
*   **Manager** (`13`): Enter the manager associated with the purchase.

### Approval Date
*   **Approval Date** (`14`): Enter the date the purchase was approved.

### Submission Status
*   **Submission Status** (`15`): Select the status of the submission. You can choose from the options: "Active", "Inactive". 

### Submission Note
*   **Submission Note** (`16`): Enter any additional information about the submission.

### Form Version
*   **Form Version** (`17`): Enter the version of the form submitted.

### Form Submitted By
*   **Form Submitted By** (`18`): Enter the person who submitted the form.

### Form Submitted On
*   **Form Submitted On** (`19`): Enter the date the form was submitted.

### Form Approved By
*   **Form Approved By** (`20`): Enter the person who approved the form.

### Form Approved On
*   **Form Approved On** (`21`): Enter the date the form was approved.

### Form Modified By
*   **Form Modified By** (`22`): Enter the person who modified the form.

### Form Modified On
*   **Form Modified On** (`23`): Enter the date the form was modified.

### Form Created By
*   **Form Created By** (`24`): Enter the person who created the form.

### Form Created On
*   **Form Created On** (`25`): Enter the date the form was created.

## Tips

*   Make sure to select all mandatory purchases.
*   Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Attach any relevant documents or images to support your purchase.
*   Enter the correct submission and approval status.
*   Keep the form up to date with the latest version.
*   Record the correct date and time of form submission and approval.
