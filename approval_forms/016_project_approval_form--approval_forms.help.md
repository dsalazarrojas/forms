# Project Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Project Approval Form is designed to collect information from project stakeholders to facilitate the approval process. This form is used to gather relevant details such as project fee, contact information, and consent to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the project fee (if applicable).
2. Enter your contact name.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. If required, provide your mailing address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Fee**: Enter the fee associated with the project. This is an optional field, so you can leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Contact Name**: Please enter your name as the contact person for this project.
* **Phone**: Provide your contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Consent to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions. This field is required.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your physical address or email address for contact purposes. Please ensure this information is accurate and up-to-date. 

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Make sure to review the terms and conditions before agreeing to them.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact the project administrators for assistance.
