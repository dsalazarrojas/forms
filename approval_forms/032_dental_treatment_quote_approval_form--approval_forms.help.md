# Dental Treatment Quote Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Treatment Quote Approval Form is used to request approval for a dental treatment plan. It gathers information from the patient, the treatment plan, quote details, and the submitter's information for the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before starting.
2.  Fill out the Patient Information section with the patient's details.
3.  Provide the Treatment Plan in the corresponding section.
4.  Complete the Quote Information section with the relevant details.
5.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Approval section, or choose 'Other' if the approval is not a simple 'Yes' or 'No'.
6.  Add any comments or remarks in the Comments section.
7.  Enter your information in the Submitter Information section, including your signature and date.
8.  Optionally, add any additional comments or remarks in the Comments 2 section.
9.  Fill out the Submitter Notes section with any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Information** (1, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, contact information, and other relevant details. This section should be filled out accurately to ensure the correct patient data is associated with the treatment plan.

- **Treatement Plan** (2, text, optional): Enter the details of the proposed treatment, including the procedure and expected outcomes.

- **Quote Information** (3, text, optional): Enter any quote details, such as costs and payment options.

- **Approval** (4, select one: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other', required): Select whether to approve or deny the proposed treatment plan. If 'Other' is chosen, please include a brief explanation for the approval status.

- **Comments** (5, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks to provide context or clarify the approval status.

- **Submitter Information** (6, text, optional): Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as the person submitting the form.

- **Submitter Signature** (7, text, optional): Provide your signature as the submitter, confirming that the information provided is accurate.

- **Submitter Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date you're submitting the form, ensuring the approval process is timestamped.

- **Comments 2** (9, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks for the reviewer's reference.

- **Submitter Notes** (10, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the reviewer to consider during the approval process.
