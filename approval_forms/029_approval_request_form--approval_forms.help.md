# Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for requesting various types of approvals within an organization. It should only be used for requests that require approval, such as budget approvals, travel approvals, purchase approvals, personnel approvals, leave approvals, or other types of requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Requester Information section with your details.
2. Select the Type of Request from the options provided.
3. Enter a brief Description of the request.
4. If applicable, specify the Amount or Cost.
5. Provide a clear and concise justification for the request.
6. Specify the Required Timeline.
7. Choose the approver to whom you want to submit the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requester Information** (Requester Name, Requester Title, Requester Email, Requester Phone): This section is for the contact information of the person making the request.
  * **Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on the approval request.
  * **Position or Title**: Enter your job title or position.
  * **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your official phone number.
- **Type of Request**: Select the type of request you are making (e.g. Budget Approval, Travel Approval, etc.).
- **Detailed Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of the request.
- **Amount or Cost**: If applicable, enter the amount or cost associated with the request.
- **Business Justification**: Provide a clear and concise justification for the request.
- **Required Timeline**: Specify the timeline in which the request needs to be fulfilled.
- **Submit to Approver**: Choose the person to whom you want to submit the request.
- **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level of the request (Routine, Timely, or Urgent).
  *   *Routine*: The request can be processed normally.
  *   *Timely*: The request needs to be processed in a timely manner.
  *   *Urgent*: The request needs to be processed urgently.
