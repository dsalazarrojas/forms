# Property Maintenance Owner Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for property maintenance owners to provide approval for maintenance tasks related to their properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the properties and maintenance tasks listed on the form.
2. Select the maintenance tasks for which you approve.
3. Enter your name and contact information (email address and phone number).
4. Add any additional comments, if needed.
5. Submit the form to complete your approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **properties**: This section contains a note about the properties related to the maintenance tasks.
* **maintenance tasks**: Describe the specific tasks that need to be performed for the property.
* **approvals**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your approval for each task. 
    - **approval_1**: Approve or reject task 1.
    - **approval_2**: Approve or reject task 2.
    - **approval_3**: Approve or reject task 3.
    - **approval_4**: Approve or reject task 4.
* **submit**: Enter your approval decision after selecting the tasks.
* **date**: Enter the date the form is being submitted.
* **time**: Enter the time the form is being submitted.
* **email address**: Enter your email address.
* **phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or information regarding the maintenance tasks.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select all necessary approvals for each task.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities.
