# Doctorate Committee Member Approval - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain approval from a member of the graduate committee on a doctorate dissertation. The committee member will be asked to review and decide on the dissertation quality, provide feedback, and make an approval or rejection decision.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter the committee member's name**: In the "Committee Member Name" field, enter the name of the graduate committee member who is reviewing your dissertation.
2. **Enter the committee member's email**: In the "Committee Member Email" field, enter the email address of the graduate committee member, if available. This is optional.
3. **Choose a department**: In the "Department" field, select the department of the graduate school the committee member belongs to (e.g., Department of Computer Science, Department of Mathematics, etc.). This is optional.
4. **Enter the approval date**: In the "Approval Date" field, select the date when the committee member made their approval or rejection decision.
5. **Make an approval decision**: In the "Approval Decision" field, select one of the following options:
	* True: The committee member approves the dissertation.
	* False: The committee member rejects the dissertation.
	* Pending: The committee member is still reviewing the dissertation and has not made a decision yet.
6. **Enter evaluation comments**: In the "Evaluation Comments" field, enter any comments or feedback the committee member has on the dissertation quality.
7. **Enter evaluation score**: In the "Evaluation Score" field, enter a score between 1 and 5 based on the evaluation criteria.
8. **Add committee member signature**: In the "Committee Member Signature" field, enter the committee member's signature.
9. **Enter graduate school**: In the "Graduate School" field, select the name of the graduate school the committee member belongs to (e.g., University of California, Santa Cruz, University of California, Santa Barbara, etc.). This is optional, but recommended for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Committee Member Name**: Enter the name of the graduate committee member who is reviewing your dissertation. This field is required.
* **Committee Member Email**: Enter the email address of the graduate committee member, if available. This field is optional.
* **Approval Date**: Select the date when the committee member made their approval or rejection decision. This field is optional.
* **Approval Decision**: Select one of the following options:
	+ True: Approves the dissertation
	+ False: Rejects the dissertation
	+ Pending: Still reviewing the dissertation
* **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback the committee member has on the dissertation quality. This field is optional.
* **Evaluation Score**: Enter a score between 1 and 5 based on the evaluation criteria. This field is optional.
* **Committee Member Signature**: Enter the committee member's signature. This field is required.
* **Department**: Select the department of the graduate school the committee member belongs to (e.g., Department of Computer Science, Department of Mathematics, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Committee Member Title**: Enter the title of the committee member (e.g., Professor, Chairperson, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Graduate School**: Select the name of the graduate school the committee member belongs to (e.g., University of California, Santa Cruz, University of California, Santa Barbara, etc.). This field is optional and recommended for tracking purposes.
* **Committee Member Title**: Enter the name of the committee member (e.g., Professor of Computer Science, Chairperson, etc.). This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
* Use clear and concise language when entering evaluation comments and evaluation score.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your graduate school administrators for assistance.
