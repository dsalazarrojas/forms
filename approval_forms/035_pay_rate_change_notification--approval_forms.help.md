# Pay Rate Change Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pay Rate Change Notification" form is designed to notify selected managers about pay rate changes for a specified employee. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the employee's ID for whom the pay rate change is applicable.
2. Choose up to three managers who will receive notifications about this change. Selecting multiple managers will ensure more comprehensive review and approval from various departments.
3. Provide the Pay Rate Change Date, the date when the new pay rate will take effect.
4. Indicate the Pay Rate Effective Date, the date when the new rate will start to be applied. This date should not be before the pay rate change date.
5. Enter the Old Pay Rate, which is the current pay rate of the employee.
6. Enter the New Pay Rate, the rate that will replace the old one.
7. If necessary, provide a Reason for Change, a brief explanation of why the pay rate change is happening.
8. Decide on a Notification Method to send the notification to the selected managers. You can choose from Email, Phone, or In-person.
9. Add any additional Notes, if there is any information that requires further explanation or context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the ID of the employee whose pay rate is changing. This is a unique identifier that helps track the employee's information.
* **Manager Approvals (2)**: Select up to 3 managers who will receive notifications about this change. Choose individuals with relevant authority or interest in the change.
* **Pay Rate Change Date (3)**: Enter the date when the pay rate change is effective.
* **Pay Rate Effective Date (4)**: Enter the date when the new pay rate will be applied.
* **Old Pay Rate (5)**: Enter the current pay rate of the employee.
* **New Pay Rate (6)**: Enter the rate that will replace the old one.
* **Reason for Change (7)**: Provide a brief explanation of why the pay rate change is happening.
* **Notification Method (8)**: Choose how you want to send the notification to the selected managers. 
* **Notes (9)**: Add any additional information or context about the pay rate change that requires further explanation.

## Tips
- Make sure to select managers who are authorized or interested in reviewing and approving the pay rate change.
- Enter accurate dates for Pay Rate Change Date and Pay Rate Effective Date to prevent any confusion.
- Be clear and concise in your Reason for Change explanation to help managers understand the context of the change.
- Choose a suitable Notification Method to reach the selected managers effectively.
- Add relevant context or notes to help managers review and approve the change without hesitation.
