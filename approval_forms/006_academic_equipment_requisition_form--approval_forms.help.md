# Academic Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Equipment Requisition Form is used to request academic equipment for departmental use. This form helps to streamline the process of acquiring and managing equipment, ensuring that requests are properly evaluated and approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact email, which will be used for approval updates.
2. Identify the department or unit requesting the equipment.
3. Describe the equipment needed, including make and model if known.
4. Specify the quantity of equipment required.
5. Provide a justification for the request, explaining how the equipment supports academic activities.
6. (Optional) If known, provide the estimated total cost of the equipment.
7. If known, specify the date by which the equipment is needed.
8. Enter your approver's name for departmental approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name** (`requester_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Department or unit** (`department`, text, required): Type in the name of the department or unit requesting the equipment.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your institutional email for approval updates.
* **Equipment needed** (`equipment_needed`, text, required): Describe the equipment being requested, including make and model if known.
* **Quantity requested** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of units required.
* **Justification for request** (`justification`, text, required): Clearly explain how the requested equipment supports academic activities.
* **Estimated total cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, optional): Provide the estimated total cost of the equipment, including currency if available.
* **Required by date** (`required_by_date`, date, optional): If known, specify the date by which the equipment is needed.
* **Approver name and confirmation** (`approver_signature`, text, optional): Enter your approver's name for departmental approval.
