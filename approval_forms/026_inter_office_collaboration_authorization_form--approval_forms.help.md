# Inter Office Collaboration Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for internal collaboration and approval processes, ensuring that all relevant parties are informed and involved in project-related decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are provided.
2. Review the information carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. If applicable, select the correct collaboration approver and project manager approver from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name and any relevant information about your collaboration request.
* **Project Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the project, including any necessary context or background.
* **Approver Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the individual who will be handling your collaboration request.
* **Collaboration Approver** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the name of the person who will be collaborating with you on this project.
* **Collaboration Approver Phone** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the collaboration approver for easy contact.
* **Collaboration Approver Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the collaboration approver for direct communication.
* **Project Manager Approver** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the project manager.
* **User Request Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date you are requesting collaboration.
* **User Request Time** (`9`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you are requesting collaboration.
* **Approver Comment** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or information about your collaboration request.
* **Approver Status** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the status of your collaboration request, as per the options provided.
