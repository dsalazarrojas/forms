# Salary Advance Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salary Advance Approval Form is used to request and approve salary advances for employees. It ensures that the advance is for a valid reason and aligns with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name as the employee requesting the advance.
2. Enter your manager's name as the one approving the advance.
3. Provide the start and end dates for the advance period.
4. Enter the reason for the advance.
5. Select the tool or group assigned to you for the advance.
6. Review and update the form as necessary.
7. Submit the form to complete the request.
