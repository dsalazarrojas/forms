# School Resource Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Resource Upgrade Request Form is designed to help school administrators collect information from teachers and staff about the resources they need for their classrooms. The form is used to submit requests for upgrades, additions, or modifications of school resources, which are then reviewed and processed by the school administration team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title/position at the school.
2. Provide a brief description of the resource you need to upgrade or modify.
3. Specify the reason for the upgrade or modification.
4. Describe the expected outcome or benefit of the upgrade or modification.
5. If applicable, attach any supporting documents or images.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_1`): Enter the name of your school. (Required)
* **Resource Description** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_8`): Describe the resource you need to upgrade or modify. (Required)
* **Reason for Upgrade** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_9`): Explain the reason for requesting the upgrade or modification. (Required)
* **Expected Outcome** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_11`): Describe the expected outcome or benefit of the upgrade or modification. (Required)
* **Supporting Documents** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_10`): If applicable, attach any supporting documents or images to aid in the review process. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_12`): Provide any additional information or comments about your request. (Optional)
* **Contact Person** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_14`): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of further questions or clarification. (Optional)
* **Resource Type** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_15`): Select the type of resource you are requesting to be upgraded or modified. (Optional)
* **Location** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_16`): Specify the location where the resource is used. (Optional)
* **Date Needed by** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_17`): Provide the deadline or earliest date by which you need the resource upgraded or modified. (Optional)
* **Additional Information** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_18`): Provide any additional information that might be helpful in processing your request. (Optional)
* **Request Type** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_19`): Select the type of request (e.g., upgrade, addition, modification). (Optional)
* **Attachments** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_20`): Attach any supporting documents or images that demonstrate your need. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_21`): Provide any additional comments or information about your request. (Optional)
* **Confirmation** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_22`): Confirm that you have read and understood the school's resource upgrade request process. (Optional)
* **Signature** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_24`): Sign and date your request, confirming that the information provided is accurate and complete. (Required)
* **School Administrator's Signature** (`school_resource_upgrade_request_form_25`): The school administration team will sign and date the request, confirming that it has been received and is being processed. (Required)
