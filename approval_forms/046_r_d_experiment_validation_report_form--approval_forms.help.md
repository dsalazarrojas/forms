# r_d_experiment_validation_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The r_d_experiment_validation_report_form is a form used to report and validate the status of experiments. It helps users to track and document the progress of experiments, providing a clear and concise way to record and share information about experiments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Experiment ID, which is a unique identifier for the experiment.
2. Select the Validation Status from the dropdown menu.
3. Fill in any Experiment Results or findings.
4. Enter any Validation Comments about the experiment's validation process.
5. Enter the Experiment Conclusion.
6. Enter the Next Steps or actions to be taken regarding the experiment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Experiment ID
* **Experiment ID** (`Experiment ID`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the experiment.
### Validation Status
* **Validation Status** (`Validation Status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the experiment from the dropdown menu: Approved, In-Progress, Not Approved, or Not Started.
### Experiment Results
* **Experiment Results** (`Experiment Results`, text, optional): Enter any relevant experiment results or findings.
### Validation Comments
* **Validation Comments** (`Validation Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the experiment's validation process.
### Experiment Conclusion
* **Experiment Conclusion** (`Experiment Conclusion`, text, optional): Enter the conclusion of the experiment.
### Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, text, optional): Enter the next steps or actions to be taken regarding the experiment.
