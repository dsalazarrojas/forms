# Maintenance Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Approval Form is used to submit a maintenance request for equipment or machinery within the organization. It is used to collect relevant information about the maintenance request, including the description of the work required, start date and time, status, and comments from the requestor or their supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your contact information and maintenance details.
2. Provide a detailed description of the maintenance required, including any relevant dates and times.
3. Choose the status of the maintenance request (Open, Closed, or Pending).
4. Select the priority level of the maintenance request (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Add any additional comments or comments from the supervisor if needed.
6. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Requester** (id: 2): Enter your name and contact information.
	+ Please enter your name and any relevant contact details.
* **Maintenance Description** (id: 3): Describe the maintenance required.
	+ Please provide a detailed description of the maintenance, including any relevant dates and times.
* **Start Date** (id: 4): Enter the start date of the maintenance.
	+ Please select the date the maintenance is scheduled to start.
* **Start Time** (id: 5): Enter the start time of the maintenance.
	+ Please select the time the maintenance is scheduled to start (optional).
* **Status** (id: 6): Choose the status of the maintenance request.
	+ Select Open, Closed, or Pending from the options.
* **Priority** (id: 7): Select the priority level of the maintenance request.
	+ Choose High, Medium, or Low from the options.
* **Maintenance Requestor** (id: 8): Enter your name and contact information.
	+ This is a duplicate field, please enter your information again.
* **Maintenance Comment** (id: 9): Add any additional comments about the maintenance request.
	+ Please provide any comments or comments from the supervisor if needed.
* **Maintenance Approval** (id: 10): Choose the maintenance approval status.
	+ Select Approved, Rejected, or Pending from the options.
* **Status Update** (id: 11): Add an optional status update.
	+ Please provide any updated information about the maintenance request.
* **Requester Status Update** (id: 12): Add an optional status update from the maintenance requestor.
	+ Please provide any updated information from the requestor.
* **Requester Email** (id: 13), **Requester Phone** (id: 14), **Requester Comments** (id: 15), **Requester Address** (id: 16), **Requester Organization** (id: 17), **Requester Job Title** (id: 18), **Requester Department** (id: 19), **Requester Supervisor** (id: 20), **Requester Supervisor Phone** (id: 21), **Requester Supervisor Email** (id: 22), **Requester Supervisor Job Title** (id: 23), **Requester Supervisor Department** (id: 24), **Requester Supervisor Notes** (id: 25): Enter the relevant contact information and comments from the requestor or their supervisor.
	+ Please enter the relevant information as needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have discussed the request with your supervisor or the facilities team before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to the facilities team or your supervisor.
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing comments or status updates.
