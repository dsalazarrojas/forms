# Business Activity Transfer Proposal - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for proposing a transfer of business activity or responsibility between departments or entities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the following information:
	* Your personal details, including name, position, department, email, and phone number.
	* The name and description of the business activity being transferred.
	* The current and proposed owner of the activity.
	* The type and reason for the transfer.
	* The impact assessment and transition plan.
	* The proposed timeline for the transfer.
	* The potential risks associated with the transfer and the risk mitigation strategy.
	* The required approvals needed for the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Proposer Information** (`proposer_info`, note, optional): This section provides a brief description of the proposer's details and background.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name in this field.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter your current job title in this field.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Transfer Details** (`transfer_details`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the proposed transfer.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business activity being transferred.
* **Activity Description** (`activity_description`, text, required): Describe the activity in detail.
* **Current Owner** (`current_owner`, text, required): Enter the current department or entity responsible for the activity.
* **Proposed Owner** (`proposed_owner`, text, required): Enter the department or entity to receive the transfer.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of transfer (Full Transfer, Partial Transfer, Temporary Transfer, Shared Responsibility, or Other).
* **Reason for Transfer** (`transfer_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for the transfer (Organizational Restructuring, Efficiency Improvement, Cost Reduction, Strategic Realignment, Resource Optimization, Compliance Requirement, or Other).
* **Transfer Justification** (`transfer_justification`, text, required): Provide a detailed justification for the transfer.
* **Impact Assessment** (`impact_assessment`, note, optional): Assess the impact of the transfer on the organization.
* **Operational Impact** (`operational_impact`, select_one, required): Select the operational impact of the transfer (No Impact, Minor Impact, Moderate Impact, Significant Impact, Major Disruption).
* **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, select_one, required): Select the financial impact of the transfer (Cost Savings, No Financial Impact, Minor Cost Increase, Moderate Cost Increase, Significant Cost Increase).
* **Employee Impact** (`employee_impact`, select_one, required): Select the employee impact of the transfer (No Impact, Reporting Change Only, Role Modification, Position Elimination, New Positions Created).
* **Number of Affected Employees** (`affected_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees affected by the transfer.
* **Proposed Timeline** (`timeline`, note, optional): Enter the proposed timeline for the transfer.
* **Proposed Start Date** (`proposed_start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed start date for the transfer.
* **Proposed Completion Date** (`proposed_completion_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed completion date for the transfer.
* **Transition Plan** (`transition_plan`, text, required): Describe the transition approach.
* **Potential Risks** (`risks`, text, optional): List any potential risks associated with the transfer.
* **Risk Mitigation Strategy** (`mitigation_strategy`, text, optional): Describe how risks will be addressed.
* **Required Approvals** (`approvals_needed`, select_multiple, required): Select all required approvals (Department Head, Finance Approval, HR Approval, Legal Review, Executive Approval, Board Approval, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to support your proposal.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your department head or relevant authorities.
