# customer_feedback_survey_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer feedback survey approval form is designed to collect customer feedback and approval from customers. It's a survey that aims to improve customer satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide detailed and constructive feedback in the "Feedback Survey" field.
3. If applicable, add any additional comments or explanations in the "Comments" field.
4. Select the desired outcome for the submission in the "Approval" field. You can choose from the following options:
	* Approved
	* Rejected
	* Pending
5. If necessary, add any comments for the approval outcome in the "Comments Approval" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your public records or documents.
* **Feedback Survey**: Provide a detailed and constructive feedback about your experience with our organization. Be as specific as possible to help us improve our services.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or explanations about your feedback or submission.
* **Approval**: Select the desired outcome for your submission:
	+ **Approved**: Indicate that your feedback is accurate and valid.
	+ **Rejected**: Indicate that your feedback is not accurate or valid.
	+ **Pending**: Indicate that your feedback requires further review or clarification.
* **Comments Approval**: Add any comments or explanations about your approval outcome selection.
