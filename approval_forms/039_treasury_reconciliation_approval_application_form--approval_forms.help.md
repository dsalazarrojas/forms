# Treasury Reconciliation Approval Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assist users in submitting a treasury reconciliation application for review and approval. The form aims to gather relevant information from applicants to facilitate a quick and efficient review process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a new browser tab or window.
2. Start with the first page, "Reconciliation Application," where you can provide a detailed description of the reconciliation you are applying for.
3. Proceed to the second page, "Supporting Documentation," where you can attach supporting documents or files related to your application.
4. On the third and final page, "Reviewer Details," please provide your contact information for the reviewer who will be handling your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reconciliation Application** (`reconciliation_application`, text, required): A detailed description of the reconciliation you are applying for. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the reconciliation process.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, text, optional): Supporting documents or files related to your application. You can attach files such as invoices, receipts, or other relevant documents that will help the reviewer understand your application.
* **Reviewer Details** (`reviewer_details`, text, required): Please provide your contact information for the reviewer who will be handling your application. This will ensure that the reviewer can reach out to you for any additional information or clarification needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to help the reviewer understand your application quickly and efficiently.
* Use supporting documentation to demonstrate your points and provide evidence where possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
