# Sponsorship Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool used to gather information and facilitate the process of requesting and managing sponsorships for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the fields for the Event Organizers, including the name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the Sponsor Name and their contact information, including their email and phone number.
3. Enter the Sponsorship Date when the sponsorship agreement was made.
4. Enter the Agreement Date when the sponsorship agreement was signed.
5. Provide the Event Name and Event Date for the sponsored event.
6. Enter the Location of the sponsored event.
7. Add a message from the sponsor, if any.
8. Select the Sponsor Type of the sponsorship (Small, Medium, or Large).
9. Enter any additional details about the sponsor.
10. Choose the approval status of the sponsorship (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
11. If the sponsorship was denied, enter the reason for denial.
12. Add any notes about the sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizers** (Text): Please enter the name of the event organizers, who should be the people responsible for managing the event.
* **Sponsor Name** (Text): Please enter the name of the sponsor, who should be the person or organization providing the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Email** (Email): Please enter the email address of the sponsor, so we can contact them about the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Phone** (Text): Please enter the phone number of the sponsor, so we can contact them about the sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Date** (Date): Please enter the date when the sponsorship agreement was made.
* **Agreement Date** (Date): Please enter the date when the sponsorship agreement was signed.
* **Event Name** (Text): Please enter the name of the event being sponsored.
* **Event Date** (Date): Please enter the date of the event being sponsored.
* **Location** (Text): Please enter the location of the event being sponsored.
* **Sponsor Message** (Note): Please enter any message from the sponsor, if any.
* **Sponsor Type** (Select Multiple): Please select the type of sponsor (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Sponsor Details** (Text): Please enter any additional details about the sponsor.
* **Approval** (Select One): Please choose the status of the sponsorship (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Reason Denied** (Text): If the sponsorship was denied, please enter the reason for denial.
* **Notes** (Text): Please add any notes about the sponsorship.
