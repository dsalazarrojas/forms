# Proposal Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proposal Approval Form is designed to collect necessary information and approvals for proposals submitted to the organization. This form helps to track and process proposals efficiently, ensuring that all required details are captured and reviewed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Proposal Details page (Page 1) with a brief summary of your proposal.
2. Choose your project manager from the list on Page 1.
3. Select your team leader from the dropdown list on Page 1.
4. Enter the project status and approval date in the respective fields on Page 1.
5. Record the time spent approving the proposal on Page 1.
6. Add any comments or feedback on Page 1.
7. Verify your proposal number on Page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposal Details**: Enter a brief summary of your proposal, describing the project, its objectives, and any notable features.
* **Project Manager**: Select the manager responsible for the project from the dropdown list.
* **Team Leader**: Choose the team leader who will be working on the project from the dropdown list.
* **Project Status**: Enter the current status of the project (e.g., approved, pending, cancelled).
* **Approval Date**: Enter the date you approve the proposal.
* **Approval Time**: Record the time you spent approving the proposal.
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you'd like to provide with the proposal.
* **Proposal Number**: Verify the unique number assigned to your proposal.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your proposal carefully before submitting it for approval.
* Check that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for dates and times to ensure easy processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Save a copy of your completed proposal for your records.
