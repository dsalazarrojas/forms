# Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Reporting Approval Application Form is a structured way for finance team members to submit reports to the treasury department for review and approval.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you are authorized to submit reports.
3. Review the report details carefully to ensure accuracy.
4. Attach any necessary supporting documents.
5. Select the correct report type and submitter department and location.
6. Fill out the approver comments and attachment details if necessary.
7. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name, which will appear on the report.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name, which will appear on the report.
* **Email** (3): Enter your official email address.
* **Department and Location** (4): Enter your department and location.
* **Report Date** (5): Enter the date of the report, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Report Type** (6): Select the type of report you are submitting (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Submitter** (7): Select the person submitting the report (Finance Team Leader, Treasury Manager, or Financial Analyst).
* **Report Details** (8): Enter a brief description of the report.
* **Supporting Documents** (9): Attach any necessary supporting documents to the report.
* **Submitted By** (10): Enter the name of the person submitting the report.
* **Submit Date** (11): Enter the date and time of the submission.
* **Approved By** (12): Select the person or group that will approve the report (Finance Team Leader, Treasury Manager, or Financial Analyst).
* **Reason for Approval** (13): Enter a brief reason for approval.
* **Approved Status** (14): Select the status of the report (Approved or Rejected).
* **Comments** (15): Enter any additional comments for the approver.
* **Submitter Department** (16): Select the department submitting the report (Finance or Treasury).
* **Submitter Location** (17): Select the location of the submission (North, South, East, or West).
* **Attachments** (18): Attach any relevant attachments to the report.
* **Submitter Comments** (19): Enter comments from the submitter.
* **Approver Comments** (20): Enter comments from the approver.
* **Attachment Name** (21): Enter the name of the attachment.
* **Attachment Description** (22): Enter a brief description of the attachment.
* **Attachment Size** (23): Enter the size of the attachment.
* **Attachment Type** (24): Enter the type of attachment.
* **Submitted For** (25): Enter the report or project being submitted for.

**Tips**

* Make sure to attach any necessary supporting documents to your report.
* Ensure that you are submitting the correct report type.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the finance team leader or treasury manager.
* Do not submit duplicate reports or reports that are incomplete.
