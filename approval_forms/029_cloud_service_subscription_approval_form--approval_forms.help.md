# Cloud Service Subscription Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and manage cloud service subscriptions for various services such as collaboration tools, project management, data analytics, development tools, productivity apps, and security and storage services. It helps ensure that all requested services align with the company's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Requester Information section with your details.
2. In the Service Details section, provide the name of the service, its category, provider, and subscription plan.
3. In the Subscription Details section, specify the number of users, subscription duration, and monthly and annual costs.
4. In the Business Justification section, explain why this service is needed and what benefits it will bring to the organization.
5. In the Approval Status section, select the status of your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester Name**: Your name and title.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for quick contact.
* **Department**: The department you belong to.
* **Manager Name - Approval**: The name of your direct supervisor for approval purposes.
* **Request Date**: The date you submitted your request.
* **Cloud Service Name**: The name of the cloud service you are requesting.
* **Service Category**: The category of the service (e.g., collaboration tools, project management).
* **Service Provider**: The vendor or company offering the service.
* **Subscription Plan**: The plan you are requesting (e.g., free, basic, professional).
* **Number of Users - Seats**: The number of users you need the service for.
* **Subscription Duration**: The duration of the subscription (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Monthly Cost - USD**: The monthly cost of the subscription.
* **Annual Cost - USD**: The annual cost of the subscription.
* **Business Purpose and Justification**: Explain why you need this service and what benefits it will bring to the organization.
* **Expected Benefits or ROI**: The expected benefits or return on investment from this service.
* **Approval Status**: Select the status of your request (e.g., pending, approved, rejected).
* **Approver Name**: If your request has already been approved or rejected, enter the name of the approver.
* **Approval Date**: The date the request was approved or rejected.
* **Approval Comments**: Any comments from the approver.
