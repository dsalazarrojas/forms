# Professional Sports Trade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Sports Trade Request Form is designed to facilitate the trade request process in professional sports organizations. It collects essential information about player trades, including player details, trade conditions, and review and approval stages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that you've filled in all the required fields to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player ID** (1): Enter the unique identification number of the player involved in the trade.
* **Team Requesting** (3): Select 'Yes' to confirm that your team is requesting the trade or 'No' if it's not.
* **Player Name** (4): Enter the name of the player participating in the trade.
* **Position** (5): Select the specific position of the player involved in the trade.
* **Trade Type** (6): Indicate the type of trade you're requesting (e.g., 'Yes' for a trade request).
* **Trade Status** (7): Select 'Active' if the trade is currently active or 'Inactive' if it's not.
* **Date of Trade** (8): Provide the date when the trade took place.
* **Time of Trade** (9): Enter the time of the trade.
* **Trade Details** (10): Describe the trade details and conditions.
* **League Staff** (11): Enter the email of the league staff member handling the trade.
* **Trade Comments** (12): Add any additional comments about the trade.
* **Approval Status** (13): Select the status of the trade approval (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Trade Request Date** (14): Enter the date when the trade request was made.
* **Trade Request Time** (15): Enter the time of the trade request.
* **Trade Requester** (16): Enter the name of the person or team requesting the trade.
* **Trade Requester Phone** (17): Enter the phone number of the person or team requesting the trade.
* **Review Notes** (18): Add any notes for the review process.
* **Review Status** (19): Select the status of the review process (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Review Date** (20): Enter the date when the review took place.
* **Review Time** (21): Enter the time of the review.
* **Review Comment** (22): Add any comments about the review process.
* **Review Status Details** (23): Provide additional details about the review status.
* **Approval Comments** (24): Enter any comments about the trade approval.
* **Approval Date** (25): Enter the date when the trade was approved.
