# Mortgage Pre Approval Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Pre Approval Checklist is a form used by lenders to gather essential information about you and your property to pre-approve a loan. This information helps assess your creditworthiness and loan eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each section of the form, starting from the first page.
2.  Ensure you fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Be as accurate as possible when filling in the fields.
4.  Review each field for any errors or issues before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached for communication and loan documents.
*   **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number for identity and credit checks.
*   **Employment Status**: Select how you are employed:
    *   Employed
    *   Self-Employed
    *   Retired
    *   Unemployed
*   **Income Source**: Select the sources of your income (select multiple for multiple sources):
    *   Wages
    *   Self-Employment Income
    *   Investments
    *   Pensions
    *   Other
*   **Co-Borrower Information (Optional)**: Enter any information about your co-borrower. This is an optional field.
*   **Loan Amount**: Enter the amount of loan you are seeking.
*   **Loan Term**: Select the term of your loan:
    *   15-30 Years
    *   30-40 Years
    *   More than 40 Years
*   **Loan Type**: Select the type of loan you are seeking (select multiple for multiple types):
    *   Fixed Rate
    *   Adjustable Rate
    *   Balloon
*   **Interest Rate**: Enter the interest rate for your loan.
*   **Credit Score (Optional)**: Enter your credit score for credit checks.
*   **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are purchasing:
    *   Primary Residence
    *   Secondary Residence
    *   Investment
*   **Property Value**: Enter the value of the property.
*   **Down Payment**: Enter the down payment percentage.
*   **Mortgage Insurer (Optional)**: Select the type of mortgage insurer:
    *   VA
    *   FHA
    *   USDA
    *   Conventional
    *   Other
*   **Property Location**: Enter the location of your property.
*   **Property Age**: Enter the age of your property.
*   **Property Condition**: Select the condition of your property:
    *   New
    *   Old
    *   Poor
*   **Property Use**: Select the purpose of your property:
    *   Primary
    *   Secondary
    *   Investment
*   **Property Purpose**: Select the purpose of your property:
    *   Residential
    *   Commercial
    *   Other
*   **Co-Borrower Income (Optional)**: Enter your co-borrower's income.
*   **Co-Borrower Employment (Optional)**: Enter any information about your co-borrower's employment.
*   **Co-Borrower Education (Optional)**: Enter any information about your co-borrower's education.
*   **Co-Borrower Income Source (Optional)**: Select the sources of your co-borrower's income:
    *   Wages
    *   Self-Employment Income
    *   Investments
    *   Pensions
    *   Other
*   **Co-Borrower Liability (Optional)**: Enter your co-borrower's liability.
*   **Other Documents (Optional)**: Enter any other documents required for your loan.

Please review each field carefully and fill it in accurately to ensure a smooth pre-approval process.
