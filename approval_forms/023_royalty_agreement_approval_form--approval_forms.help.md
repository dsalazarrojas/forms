# Royalty Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for obtaining approval of a royalty agreement from an approver. The purpose of this form is to facilitate the review and approval process of a royalty agreement, ensuring that it aligns with organizational standards and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contract Review" page where you can upload and review the royalty agreement document. This is an important step to thoroughly examine the contract before proceeding.
2. On the "Agreement Details" page, provide a brief description of the agreement, such as its purpose, scope, and any relevant context.
3. On the "Approval Status" page, select one of the three options: "Approved", "Pending", or "Rejected". This indicates the current status of the agreement.
4. If the agreement is approved, enter the "Date Approved" on which the agreement was approved.
5. If the agreement is rejected, enter the "Date Rejected" on which the agreement was rejected.
6. Optionally, provide the name, email address, and phone number of the person who approved the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Review** (`contract_review`, text, required): This is the page where you can upload and review the royalty agreement document. Please ensure that you have thoroughly examined the contract before proceeding.
* **Agreement Details** (`agreement_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the agreement, such as its purpose, scope, and any relevant context.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options: "Approved", "Pending", or "Rejected" to indicate the current status of the agreement.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, optional): If the agreement is approved, enter the date on which it was approved.
* **Date Rejected** (`date_rejected`, date, optional): If the agreement is rejected, enter the date on which it was rejected.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the name of the person who approved the agreement.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, optional): Optionally, enter the email address of the person who approved the agreement.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, optional): Optionally, enter the phone number of the person who approved the agreement.
