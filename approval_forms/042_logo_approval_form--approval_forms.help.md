# Logo Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Logo Approval Form" is used to gather input from design approvers and approvers to determine whether a logo is approved or not. The form consists of several fields that guide the approver through the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the "Logo Review" page, briefly describe the logo and its intended use.
2. On the "Designer" page, select the designer who created the logo.
3. On the "Approver" page, select all approvers who have reviewed the logo.
4. On the "Comments" page, provide any additional comments or feedback about the logo.
5. On the "Approver Comments" page, add any comments or feedback from approvers.
6. On the "Approval Decision" page, select whether the logo is approved or not.
7. On the "Logo Approved" page, select all approvers who have approved the logo.
8. On the "Design Approver Phone" page, enter the phone number of the design approver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logo Review (Logo Review)** (`logo_review`, text, optional): Briefly describe the logo and its intended use.
* **Designer (design_approver)** (`designer`, select_one, optional): Select the designer who created the logo. This field is used to identify the person responsible for creating the logo.
* **Approver (Approver)** (`approver`, select_multiple, optional): Select all approvers who have reviewed the logo. This field is used to identify all individuals who have reviewed the logo.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the logo. This field is used to gather feedback from approvers.
* **Approver Comments (Approver Comments)** (`approver_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback from approvers. This field is used to document feedback from approvers.
* **Approval Decision (Approval Decision)** (`approval_decision`, select_one, optional): Select whether the logo is approved or not. This field is used to make an informed decision about the logo.
* **Logo Approved (Logo Approved)** (`logo_approved`, select_multiple, optional): Select all approvers who have approved the logo. This field is used to identify all approvers who have approved the logo.
* **Design Approver Phone (Design Approver Phone)** (`design_approver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the design approver. This field is used to contact the design approver for further information.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use the "Comments" and "Approver Comments" fields to provide detailed feedback about the logo.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accurate information is gathered.
* Contact the design approver if you are unsure about the logo or need further clarification.
