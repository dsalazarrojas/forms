# lease_extension_amendment_approval_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Lease Extension Amendment Approval Form is used to process and document the approval of a lease extension request from a tenant. This form is a crucial document that ensures a smooth and transparent process of lease extension and amendment approval.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Review and confirm the accuracy of the provided details.
3. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Landlord Details** (`landlord_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the landlord.
* **Tenant Details** (`tenant_details`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the tenant.
* **Lease Extension Request** (`extension_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the lease extension request.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Reason For Extension** (`reason_for_extension`, text, optional): Elaborate on the reason for lease extension.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, select one, optional): Select the person approving the lease extension (Administrator, Manager, or Landlord).
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of approval.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the lease extension.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the landlord or tenant.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the landlord or tenant.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the landlord or tenant.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the landlord or tenant.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the landlord or tenant.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the landlord or tenant.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the landlord or tenant.
* **Lease Length** (`lease_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lease.
* **Extension Length** (`extension_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the lease extension.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Enter the rent amount.
* **Approved By Title** (`approved_by_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the person approving the lease extension.
* **Approval Date Title** (`approval_date_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the approval date.
* **Reason For Extension Title** (`reason_for_extension_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the reason for lease extension.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the signature of the tenant.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, optional): Obtain the signature of the landlord.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select one, optional): Set the status of approval (Approved, Pending, or Denied).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
* Make sure to keep a record of the lease extension agreement for future reference.
