# Public Safety Equipment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request public safety equipment for use in performing job duties.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For fields marked as optional, you can choose to provide as much or as little information as needed.
3. Make sure to submit the form after you have reviewed all the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **equipment_requested** (required): Please enter the type of equipment you are requesting. For example, "fire extinguisher" or "first aid kit".
* **department** (required): Choose the department for which you are requesting the equipment. This will help us understand who is requesting the equipment and who will be using it.
* **reason_for_request** (required): Please provide a brief explanation of why this equipment is needed. This will help us to better understand the request and prioritize the equipment request.
* **priority** (required): Choose the priority level for your request. This will help us to understand the urgency of the request.
* **contact_person** (required): Please enter your name or the name of the person who will be using the equipment.
* **contact_email** (required): Please enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **departmental_approvals** (optional): If you would like this request to go through both Departmental Manager and Departmental Director, please select this option. Otherwise, please select the Departmental Manager.
* **date_received** (optional): Please enter the date when you received the information or became aware of the need for the equipment.
* **time_received** (optional): Enter the time when you received the information or became aware of the need for the equipment.
* **submitted_by** (required): Please enter your name.
* **date_submitted** (required): Please enter the date you submitted this form.
* **time_submitted** (required): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **additional_info** (optional): If you have any extra information that would be helpful for us to know, please enter it here.
* **notes** (optional): If you have any additional comments or remarks about the request, please enter them here.
