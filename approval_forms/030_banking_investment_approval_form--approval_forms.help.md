# Banking Investment Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Banking Investment Approval Form is a document used to gather information from applicants seeking to make investments through the bank. This form helps the bank's risk management and compliance team to evaluate the applicant's risk tolerance, financial situation, and investment goals, ensuring that the investment process is carried out efficiently and safely.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please ensure you have the required documents and information ready. You can fill out the form in two main sections:

1.  **Applicant Information**: This section gathers basic information about you such as your name, contact details, and account details.
2.  **Investment Details**: Here, you will be asked about your investment goals, financial situation, and risk tolerance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required)
Your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required)
Enter your contact email address where you can be reached for any updates or inquiries.

### 3. **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required)
Provide your contact phone number for easy communication.

### 4. **Mailing Address** (`applicant_address`, `text`, required)
Enter your street address, city, state, and ZIP code for mail correspondence.

### 5. **Account Number** (`account_number`, `text`, required)
Enter your existing bank account number for easy tracking and management.

### 6. **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, `select_one`, required)
Select from the options provided to indicate the type of investment you are seeking (e.g. Stocks, Bonds, etc.).

### 7. **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, `number`, required)
Enter the amount you intend to invest to help us assess your investment capacity.

### 8. **Investment Term** (`investment_term`, `select_one`, required)
Choose the duration of your investment (e.g. Short-Term, Medium-Term, etc.).

### 9. **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, `select_one`, required)
Select your risk tolerance level (e.g. Conservative, Moderate, etc.).

### 10. **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your main investment goal (e.g. Capital Preservation, Income Generation, etc.).

### 11. **Source of Funds** (`source_of_funds`, `select_one`, required)
Choose where the funds for the investment come from (e.g. Salary, Business Income, etc.).

### 12. **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, `select_one`, required)
Select your annual income level as per your financial situation.

### 13. **Net Worth** (`net_worth`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your current net worth to help us assess your financial standing.

### 14. **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, `select_one`, required)
Select your level of investment experience (e.g. Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).

### 15. **Liquidity Needs** (`liquidity_needs`, `select_one`, required)
Indicate when you will need access to the funds (e.g. Within 1 year, 1-3 years, etc.).

### 16. **Tax Considerations** (`tax_considerations`, `select_one`, optional)
If applicable, choose your tax preference for the investment.

### 17. **Existing Investment Portfolio** (`existing_portfolio`, `select_one`, required)
State your current status of investment portfolio.

### 18. **Financial Advisor** (`financial_advisor`, `select_one`, required)
Indicate whether you have a financial advisor or need one.

### 19. **Advisor Name (if applicable)** (`advisor_name`, `text`, optional)
If you have a financial advisor, provide their name.

### 20. **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, optional)
Add any special instructions or comments about your investment.

### 21. **Terms Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required)
Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

### 22. **Risk Disclosure Acknowledgment** (`risk_disclosure_ack`, `select_one`, required)
Confirm that you have read and understood the risk disclosure.

### 23. **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required)
Date and time of application submission.

After carefully filling out the form, review and submit it for review and processing. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our team for assistance.
