## Board Resolution Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## 
This form is used for proposing and approving resolutions. Resolutions are formal decisions made by a board or committee.
## How To Complete This Form
### 
Fill out this form to propose a resolution to the board or committee. This form guides you through the necessary steps to capture all relevant information and ensure that your proposal is properly documented.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resolution reference** (Resolution reference, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for the resolution, so it can be easily tracked and referenced later.
* **Resolution summary** (Resolution summary, text, required): Provide a brief description of the decision or proposal, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Proposer** (Proposer name, text, required): Enter the name of the board member or committee proposing the resolution.
* **Vote deadline** (Vote deadline, date, required): Set the latest date by which responses are due. This helps ensure that decisions are made in a timely manner.
* **Member vote** (Member vote, select_one, required): Select your vote: Approve, Reject, or Abstain from the options provided.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any remarks or conditions that may be relevant to the decision.
* **Signer name** (Signer name, text, required): Enter the name of the board member signing the resolution.
