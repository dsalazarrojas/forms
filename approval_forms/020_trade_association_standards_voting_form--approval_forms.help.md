# Trade Association Standards Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect ratings and reviews from committee members on trade association standards. It helps the committee gather feedback and make informed decisions about standards adoption or modification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the committee members on each page and select either "Yes" or "No" for each.
* Rate the standards on the "Standards Rating" page using the provided options: Good, Fair, or other (if available).
* Enter your contact information, including Committee Name, Committee Email, Committee Phone, Committee Address, Committee City, and Committee Country, on the "Committee Information" page.
* Provide any additional notes on the "Committee Rating Note", "Committee Review Note", and "Committee Review Rating Note" pages.
* Confirm your vote on the "Committee Review Voting Form" page by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Committee Review (1)
* **Committee Review**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the committee members have reviewed the standards.
* **Committee Review Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes on the review process.
* **Committee Review Voting Form**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your vote on the review.

### Standards Rating (3)
* **Standards Rating**: Rate the standards as Good, Fair, or other (if available).

### Committee Information (6)
* **Committee Name**: Enter the name of the committee.
* **Committee Email**: Enter the email address of the committee.
* **Committee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the committee.
* **Committee Address**: Enter the address of the committee.
* **Committee City**: Enter the city of the committee.
* **Committee Country**: Enter the country of the committee.
* **Committee Zipcode**: Enter the ZIP code of the committee.

### Committee Rating (5)
* **Committee Rating**: Rate the committee members' performance on this standard.
* **Committee Rating Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes on the rating process.

### Committee Review Rating (16)
* **Committee Review Rating Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes on the review process.

### Committee Voting Form (18)
* **Committee Review Voting Form**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your vote on the review.

### Tips
* Ensure that you are submitting your own vote and not duplicating a previous vote.
* Review the instructions carefully before filling out the form.
* Provide any additional comments or notes if needed.
