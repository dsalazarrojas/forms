# Decision Review Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Decision Review Application Form is used to collect information and manage the decision review process for requests or applications submitted to an organization. This form helps track the status of decisions, assignments, and reviews, ensuring transparency and consistency in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as such in each section.
2. Use the select options for Decision Reviewer, Route to Reviewer, and Review Status as needed.
3. Enter comments for any specific reasons or explanations for the decision.
4. Review your form before submitting it for final review and submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Application form** (application_form, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of the application or request being reviewed.

### 2. **Decision Requester** (decision_requester, text, optional)
Enter the name or ID of the person or entity making the decision request.

### 3. **Decision Review Committee** (decision_review_committee, text, optional)
Enter the name or ID of the committee or team responsible for reviewing the decision.

### 4. **Decision Reviewer** (decision_reviewer, text, optional)
Choose a reviewer from the available options, such as Chat Jimmy, John, or Jenny, for the review process.

### 5. **Date of Request** (date_of_request, date, optional)
Enter the date the decision request was made.

### 6. **Decision Date** (decision_date, date, optional)
Enter the date the decision was made or will be made.

### 7. **Review Status** (review_status, select_one, optional)
Choose the current status of the review: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.

### 8. **Comments** (comments, note, optional)
Enter any comments or reasons for the decision made.

### 9. **Assigned Reviewer** (assigned_reviewer, select_one, optional)
Choose a reviewer from the available options, such as Chat Jimmy, John, or Jenny, to assign the review.

### 10. **Route to Reviewer** (route_to_reviewer, select_multiple, optional)
Select one or more reviewers from the available options to route the review to.

Please submit your completed form for final review and submission.
