# Accessory Approval Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request the approval for accessories needed by employees. The form asks for basic employee information, request details, and additional context to ensure that the request is legitimate and meets the company's approval and procurement processes.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Make sure you have all necessary information available, including:
    + Your own employee ID and department
    + Your manager's name and email address
*   Fill in the form accurately, ensuring all fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   If you're requesting an urgent accessory, indicate the correct urgency level.
*   Be as specific as possible about your request, including the vendor you prefer and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name**: Your full name as an employee.
*   **Employee ID**: Your unique employee identification number.
*   **Department**: The department you belong to.
*   **Manager Name**: Your direct manager's name.
*   **Manager Email**: Your direct manager's email address.
*   **Request Date**: Date of the request.
*   **Accessory Category**: The type of accessory you're requesting (Computer Hardware, Software, Office Supplies, etc.).
*   **Accessory Name**: The name of the specific accessory you're requesting.
*   **Brand and Model**: The brand and model of the accessory.
*   **Quantity Requested**: How many units of the accessory you need.
*   **Estimated Cost**: Estimated cost per unit of the accessory.
*   **Total Cost**: Total cost for all requested accessories.
*   **Business Justification**: Briefly explain why you need this accessory.
*   **Urgency Level**: Indicate the urgency level of your request (Critical, High, Medium, or Low).
*   **Expected Delivery Date**: Date when you need the accessory delivered.
*   **Vendor Name**: Name of the vendor you'd like to purchase from.
*   **Vendor Contact Info**: Contact information for the vendor (if applicable).
*   **Approval Required Level**: Level of approval needed for this request (Manager Only, Department Head, Finance Approval, Executive Approval, Procurement Review).
*   **Asset Tracking Required**: Indicate whether this accessory needs to be tracked as an asset.
*   **Additional Comments**: Any other information about your request.

Note: This form is designed to gather all necessary information for the approval process, ensuring that your request is legitimate and aligns with company policies. Please complete it accurately and thoroughly.
