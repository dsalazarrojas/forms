# ethics_committee_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the Ethics Committee to review and approve research projects, ensuring that they adhere to ethical standards and guidelines. It collects information about the research project, including its purpose, duration, and involvement of human or animal subjects. The form also captures the committee's comments, recommendations, and approval decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the research project in the "Research Project" field.
2. Describe the purpose of the research in the "Purpose of Research" field.
3. List any human subjects involved in the research in the "Human Subjects" field.
4. List any animal subjects involved in the research in the "Animal Subjects" field.
5. Enter the expected duration of the research project in the "Research Duration" field.
6. Select the committee members who have reviewed and approved the project in the "Approved By" field.
7. Enter the date of approval in the "Date Approved" field.
8. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the principal investigator and their institution in the "Principal Investigator Name", "Principal Investigator Title", and "Principal Investigator Institution" fields.
10. Enter the names and titles of team members in the "Research Team" field.
11. Record the comments and recommendations of institutional reviewers in the "Institutional Reviewer Comments" and "Institutional Reviewer Recommendation" fields.
12. Obtain the institutional reviewer's signature in the "Institutional Reviewer Signature" field.
13. Enter the date of institutional review in the "Date Institutional Review" field.
14. Select the ethics committee's approval decision in the "Ethics Committee Approval" field.
15. Enter the approval date and time in the "Approval Date" and "Approval Time" fields.
16. Enter any additional approval notes in the "Approval Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Project**: Enter the title of the research project.
* **Purpose of Research**: Describe the purpose of the research project.
* **Human Subjects**: List any human subjects involved in the research project.
* **Animal Subjects**: List any animal subjects involved in the research project.
* **Research Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the research project.
* **Approved By**: Select the committee members who have reviewed and approved the project.
* **Date Approved**: Enter the date of approval.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes.
* **Principal Investigator Name**: Enter the name of the principal investigator.
* **Principal Investigator Title**: Enter the title of the principal investigator.
* **Principal Investigator Institution**: Enter the institution of the principal investigator.
* **Research Team**: Enter the names and titles of team members.
* **Institutional Reviewer Comments**: Record the comments of the institutional reviewers.
* **Institutional Reviewer Recommendation**: Record the recommendations of the institutional reviewers.
* **Institutional Reviewer Signature**: Obtain the institutional reviewer's signature.
* **Date Institutional Review**: Enter the date of institutional review.
* **Ethics Committee Approval**: Select the ethics committee's approval decision.
* **Approval Date**: Enter the approval date.
* **Approval Time**: Enter the approval time.
* **Approval Note**: Enter any additional approval notes.
