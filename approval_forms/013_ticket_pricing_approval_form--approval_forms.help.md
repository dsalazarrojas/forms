# Ticket Pricing Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for reviewing and approving or rejecting ticket pricing proposals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the ticket pricing proposals you agree with by checking the "Ticket Pricings" field.
2. Enter any ticket price changes in the "Ticket Price" field, if necessary.
3. Provide any additional ticket information in the "Ticket" field.
4. Enter any comments in the "Comments" field, if needed.
5. Choose your approval decision for the ticket pricing proposal in the "Approve" field.
6. Indicate whether the ticket pricing proposal is a proposal in the "Ticket Pricing Proposal" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ticket Pricings** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select the ticket pricing proposals you agree with. Check all that apply.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_2`, number, optional): Enter any changes to the ticket price, if necessary.
* **Ticket** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_4`, text, optional): Provide any additional ticket information.
* **Price** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_5`, text, optional): Enter any additional price information.
* **Approve** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_6`, select_one, optional): Choose your approval decision for the ticket pricing proposal.
	+ Select "Approved" to approve the proposal.
	+ Select "Not Approved" to reject the proposal.
* **Comments** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_7`, note, optional): Enter any comments or explanations for your approval decision.
* **Ticket Pricing Proposal** (`ticket_pricing_approval_form_8`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the ticket pricing proposal is a proposal.
	+ Select "True" to indicate it is a proposal.
	+ Select "False" to indicate it is not a proposal.
