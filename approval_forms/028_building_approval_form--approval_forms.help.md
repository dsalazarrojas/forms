# Building Approval Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect detailed information about a construction project for approval purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Use the "Submitted Documents" field to select all relevant documents that have been submitted.
4. Choose the correct "Approval Type" and "Construction Type" for your project.
5. Enter the correct "Application Date" and "Expected Completion Date" for your project.
6. Enter your "Applicant Name", "Company Name", and other relevant contact information.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" for questions about zoning compliance, environmental review, and other relevant questions.
8. Provide a detailed "Project Description" of your construction project.
9. Enter any additional "Submitted Documents" that are relevant to your project.
10. Finally, certify that the information provided is accurate by selecting "Yes - I Certify" in the "Applicant Certification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (1): Enter your full name as the project applicant.
* **Company Name** (2): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title** (3): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Application Date** (6): Enter the date of application.
* **Project Name** (7): Enter the name of the project.
* **Property Address** (8): Enter the full address of the property.
* **Parcel Number** (9): Enter the tax parcel ID number.
* **Approval Type** (10): Select the type of approval needed for your project.
* **Project Description** (11): Provide a detailed description of the project.
* **Construction Type** (12): Select the type of construction (e.g. residential, commercial, etc.).
* **Estimated Project Cost** (13): Enter the total cost of the project.
* **Total Square Footage** (14): Enter the total square footage of the project.
* **Proposed Start Date** (15): Enter the date the project is expected to begin.
* **Expected Completion Date** (16): Enter the date the project is expected to be completed.
* **Architect Name** (17): Enter the name of the project architect.
* **Architect License Number** (18): Enter the license number of the project architect.
* **Contractor Name** (19): Enter the name of the general contractor.
* **Contractor License Number** (20): Enter the license number of the general contractor.
* **Zoning Compliance** (21): Select "Yes" or "No" if the project is zoning compliant.
* **Environmental Review** (22): Select "Yes", "No", or "Unknown" if the project requires environmental review.
* **Submitted Documents** (23): Select all documents that have been submitted.
* **Previous Applications** (24): Select "Yes" or "No" if you have applied for approval before.
* **Neighbor Notification** (25): Select "Yes" or "No" if neighbors have been notified.
* **Applicant Certification** (26): Select "Yes - I Certify" to confirm that all information is accurate.
