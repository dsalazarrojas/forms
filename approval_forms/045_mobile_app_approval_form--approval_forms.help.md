# mobile_app_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about mobile apps for approval and review purposes. It gathers details such as app metadata, status, and other relevant information to facilitate the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the mobile app.
2. Fill out the form fields as follows:
	* Mobile App Info: Enter the name and other relevant details about the app.
	* Contact Info: Enter the contact information for the app's developer or distributor.
	* App Version: Enter the current version number of the app.
	* App Build Number: Enter the build number of the app.
	* App Upload Date: Select the date the app was uploaded.
	* App Status: Select the current status of the app (Apple Review Queue, Apple Review in Progress, Approved, Not Approved, or Not Reviewed).
	* App Rejection Reason: If the app is not approved, enter the reason for rejection.
	* Developer Info: Enter any additional information about the developer or distributor.
	* App Store: Select the store where the app is or will be published (Apple App Store or Google Play Store).
	* Submission Type: Select the type of submission (Apple, Google, or Both).
	* Apple App ID: Enter the app's Apple ID.
	* Apple App Status: Select the current status of the app on Apple App Store (Pending, Approved, Removed, or Unknown).
	* Google App ID: Enter the app's Google ID.
	* Google App Status: Select the current status of the app on Google Play Store (Pending, Approved, Removed, or Unknown).
	* Notes: Enter any additional notes about the app or approval process.
	* Approval Notes: Enter any notes about the approval process.
	* Rejection Notes: Enter any notes about the rejection process.
	* Approval Date: Select the date the app was approved.
	* Rejection Date: Select the date the app was rejected.
	* Approval Time: Enter the time the app was approved.
	* Rejection Time: Enter the time the app was rejected.
	* App Type: Select the type of app (Game or Non Game).
	* App Category: Select the category of the app (Game or Non Game).
	* App Rating: Select the rating of the app (4+, 3+, 2+, or 1+).
