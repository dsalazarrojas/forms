The purpose of the "hr_approval_process_form" is to facilitate the approval process for employee requests, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and processed efficiently. This form is designed to gather details from employees, such as their name, job title, department, request date, approver's name, email, and phone number, as well as any additional notes. ## hr_approval_process_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "hr_approval_process_form" is a crucial document used for employee requests, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the required fields, marked as such.
* Enter your employee name and job title.
* Select your department from the available options.
* Enter the date of your request.
* Provide the name and email of the person you have designated to approve your request.
* Enter your approver's phone number.
* If you have any additional notes or comments, you can include them in the "Notes" field, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as assigned by your department or manager.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the available options:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Support
	+ Finance
	+ Human Resources
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your request.
* **Approver Name** (`approver_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person designated to approve your request.
* **Approver Email** (`approver_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the approver.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date the approval was given or expected to be given.
* **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the approver.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.

## Tips
* Double-check the department and approver's details to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to include any necessary dates, including the request date and approval date.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact your HR department or manager.

This form is designed to provide clear and concise information for both employees and approvers, ensuring that the approval process is efficient and well-organized.
