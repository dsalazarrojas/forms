# Proof Approval - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proof Approval form is used to obtain approval for artworks from approvers. It gathers information about the artwork, such as title, date, and notes, as well as the approver's signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Artwork Title** in the text field. This is the title of the artwork you are seeking approval for.
2. Enter the **Artwork Date** in the date field. This is the date the artwork was created or last updated.
3. Provide any additional **Artwork Notes** in the text area. This can include context or details about the artwork.
4. Select the **Approver** from the dropdown list. Choose the approver who will be reviewing and approving your artwork.
5. Enter any **Comments** you have in relation to the artwork. This can include any relevant information or questions you have for the approver.
6. Sign your **Signature** to confirm you have submitted the form. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artwork Title** (`proof_approval_form_artwork_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the artwork you are seeking approval for. This field is not required, but it's helpful for the approver to know what the artwork is.
* **Artwork Date** (`proof_approval_form_artwork_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the artwork was created or last updated. This field is not required, but it's useful for the approver to know the timeline of the artwork.
* **Artwork Notes** (`proof_approval_form_artwork_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or context about the artwork. This field is not required, but it's helpful for the approver to have more information.
* **Approver** (`proof_approval_form_approver`, select_one, required/optional): Select the approver who will be reviewing and approving your artwork. Choose one of the listed approvers from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments** (`proof_approval_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or questions you have about the artwork. This field is not required, but it's helpful for the approver to know your thoughts.
* **Signature** (`proof_approval_form_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm you have submitted the form. This field is required to ensure the approver knows who submitted the form.
