# Mental Health Program Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Program Approval Form is used to collect information necessary for the approval process of mental health programs. This form serves as a central location for collecting all relevant details related to a mental health program, including its status, approval date, and other supporting information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "mental_health_program_info" field with a brief description of the mental health program.
3. Provide information about the approver, including their name, position, and email address in the "approver_info" field.
4. Select the current status of the mental health program ("Open" or "Closed") in the "program_status" field.
5. Enter the date of approval in the "approval_date" field.
6. Enter the approver's name in the "approver_name" field.
7. Enter the approver's position in the "approver_position" field.
8. Enter the approver's email address in the "approval_email" field.
9. Enter the approver's phone number in the "approver_phone" field.
10. Indicate if the mental health program has undergone a clinical review in the "mental_health_clinical_review" field (True/False).
11. Add any relevant notes about the mental health program in the "mental_health_program_notes" field.
12. Add any comments about the mental health program in the "mental_health_program_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **mental_health_program_info** (`mental health program info`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter a brief description of the mental health program.
* **approver_info** (`approver info`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter information about the approver, including their name, position, and email address.
* **program status** (`program status`, select one, required: true): This field lets you select the current status of the mental health program, which is either "Open" or "Closed".
* **approval date** (`approval date`, date, required: false): This field is where you enter the date of approval for the mental health program.
* **approver name** (`approver name`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the name of the approver.
* **approver position** (`approver position`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the position of the approver.
* **approver email** (`approver email`, email, required: false): This field is where you enter the email address of the approver.
* **approver phone** (`approver phone`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the phone number of the approver.
* **mental health clinical review** (`mental health clinical review`, select multiple, required: false): This field indicates if the mental health program has undergone a clinical review (True/False).
* **mental health program notes** (`mental health program notes`, note, required: false): This field is where you enter any relevant notes about the mental health program.
* **mental health program comments** (`mental health program comments`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter any comments about the mental health program.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required.
