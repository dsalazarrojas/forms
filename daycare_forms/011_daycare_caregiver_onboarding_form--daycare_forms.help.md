# Daycare Caregiver Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from daycare caregivers during onboarding, ensuring a smooth and efficient integration into our daycare system. The form is meant to capture essential details about the caregiver, their availability, acknowledgement of our policies, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your details as a daycare caregiver in the "Daycare Caregiver Details" field.
2. Select the dates and times you are available to work in the "Availability" field.
3. Acknowledge our policies and terms in the "Acknowledgement Form" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field for easy communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daycare Caregiver Details** (`daycare_caregiver_details`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a brief description of your role as a daycare caregiver, including your experience and qualifications.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required: false):
	+ Select the dates and times you are available to work, including weekends and holidays.
* **Acknowledgement Form** (`acknowledgement_form`, text, required: false):
	+ Read and acknowledge our policies and terms of service.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for easy communication and scheduling.
	+ Please ensure to enter your phone number in the format of `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
