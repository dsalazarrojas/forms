# toilet_training_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The toilet training checklist is a form used to track a child's progress in toilet training. It captures essential information on the child's name, start and completed dates, frequency and number of accidents, successful flushes, pull-ups, and other observations and notes.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Guidelines

- Ensure you have all the required information before starting to fill out the form.
- For fields with dates, use the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).
- For fields that require a number, enter a whole number (e.g., 5, 10, or 20).
- For fields that require a text response, provide a clear and concise answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name (1)** (`child_name`, required): Enter the child's name as it appears on their file or identification.
* **Start Date (2)** (`start_date`, required): Enter the date when the child started toilet training.
* **Completed Date (3)** (`completed_date`, required): Enter the date when the child completed toilet training.
* **Diaper Frequency (4)** (`diaper_frequency`, required): Record the frequency (number) of diaper changes per day.
* **Accidents (5)** (`accidents`, required): Record the number of accidents (if any) per day.
* **Successful Flushes (6)** (`successful_flushes`, required): Record the number of successful flushes per day.
* **Pull-Ups (7)** (`pull-ups`, required): Record the number of pull-ups per day.
* **Wipes (8)** (`wipes`, required): Record the number of wipes used per day.
* **Training Stages (9)** (`training_stages`, required): Select all the training stages the child has completed (e.g., "Option 1," "Option 2," and so on).
* **Observation Notes (10)** (`observation_notes`, optional): Record any important observations or notes about the child's progress.
* **Parent Comments (11)** (`parent_comments`, optional): Provide any comments or feedback from the parent regarding the child's progress.
* **Daycare Staff Notes (12)** (`daycare_staff_notes`, optional): Record any notes from the daycare staff regarding the child's progress.
* **Completed (13)** (`completed`, required): Mark the form as completed.
* **Reason for Not Completing (14)** (`reason_for_not_completing`, optional): If the form is not completed, enter the reason.
* **Date Not Completed (15)** (`date_not_completed`, optional): Record the date when the form was not completed.
* **Completed By (16)** (`completed_by`, required): Enter the name of the staff member who completed the form.
* **Comments (17)** (`comments`, optional): Record any additional comments about the child's progress.
* **Observations (18)** (`observations`, optional): Record any observations about the child's progress.
* **Completed Date/Time (19)** (`completed_date_time`, optional): Record the date and time when the form was completed.
* **Completed By User (20)** (`completed_by_user`, optional): Enter the name of the user who completed the form.
* **Training Stages Notes (21)** (`training_stages_notes`, optional): Record any notes about the training stages.
* **Completed By Time (22)** (`completed_by_time`, optional): Record the time when the form was completed.
* **Completed Date/Time (23)** (`completed_date_time`, optional): Record the date and time when the form was completed.
* **Completed By User (24)** (`completed_by_user`, optional): Enter the name of the user who completed the form.
* **Training Status (25)** (`training_status`, required): Record the child's current training status.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are filled out completely and accurately.
- Review the form for any necessary corrections or updates.
- Store the form in a secure and organized location for easy access.
