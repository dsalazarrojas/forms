# Daycare Child Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Child Arrival Check In form is used to track a child's arrival details when they are dropped off at the childcare center or daycare facility. This form helps staff to ensure that all relevant information is recorded correctly and that children are accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the child's arrival.
2. Enter the child's name and caregiver's name, ensuring accuracy.
3. Select the time the child arrived and the time they will be picked up.
4. Enter your contact number so that the daycare staff can reach out to you if needed.
5. Provide any additional pick up details if necessary.
6. Sign the form to confirm your identity and acknowledge that you are the caregiver.
7. Optionally, add any comments about the child's arrival.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Form**: This is the main form that contains all the other fields. Fill out this section completely and accurately.
* **Time Arrived**: Enter the exact time the child arrived at the daycare facility. This is a required field to ensure accurate record keeping.
* **Caregiver Name**: Enter your name, the person responsible for dropping off and picking up the child.
* **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child being dropped off.
* **Date Arrived**: Enter the date the child arrived at the daycare facility.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time the child will be picked up from the daycare facility.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number so that the daycare staff can reach out to you if necessary.
* **Pick up Details**: If you have any specific instructions or requirements for the pickup, enter them here. This is an optional field.
* **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm that you are the authorized caregiver for the child.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional information about the child's arrival, enter it here. This is an optional field.
