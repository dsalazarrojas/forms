# Dog Daycare Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reserving a spot for your furry friend at our dog daycare. We require some essential information from you to ensure a comfortable and safe experience for your dog. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately, and we'll take care of the rest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the services your dog will be enjoying during their stay.
3. Provide any special requests or instructions we should know about.
4. Choose a date and time for the reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reservation Form** (`reservation_form`, text, optional): Enter your reservation request or any special comments.
* **Dog Details** (`dog_details`, text, optional): Provide your dog's characteristics, such as breed, age, and any medical conditions.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Share your contact information and any relevant health insurance details.
* **Services Selected** (`services_selected`, select multiple, optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for services like food, toys, and playtime.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Inform us of any special requirements or requests for your dog's stay.
* **Date Of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Select the date for your dog's visit.
* **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): Choose the time for your dog's visit.

## Tips
- Please be accurate when filling out the form, as it helps us provide the best possible care for your dog.
- If you're unsure about any information, don't hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
- We appreciate your attention to detail and cooperation in helping us provide a great experience for your dog.
