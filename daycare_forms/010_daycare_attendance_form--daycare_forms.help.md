# Daycare Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Attendance Form is designed to track attendance of children at daycare centers during normal working hours. It helps caregivers and staff members to report attendance data accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form for each day of attendance.
- Ensure that you have the necessary information and permissions to fill out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Child's Name** (`child_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the child being reported for attendance.
- **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of attendance (e.g., 2024-02-20).
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the start time of attendance (e.g., 08:00).
- **Finish Time** (`finish_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the finish time of attendance (e.g., 17:00).

## Tips
- If a child's attendance is not recorded on a particular day, you can leave the relevant fields blank.
- If a child is absent, please indicate the reason for absence in a separate document or note.
- Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and consistently to maintain accurate records.
