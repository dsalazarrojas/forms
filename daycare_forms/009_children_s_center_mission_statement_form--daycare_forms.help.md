# Children Center Mission Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children Center Mission Statement Form is designed to gather information about childcare centers and daycares, including their mission, target age range, and contact details. This form will help you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Organization Name field with the name of your childcare center or daycare.
2. In the Mission Statement field, type a brief description of your center's mission statement.
3. If applicable, list up to 5 core values that your center stands for in the Core Values field.
4. Select the target age range that your center serves in the Target Age Range field.
5. Describe any outreach or partnership activities in the Community Involvement field.
6. Provide a short description of your center's approach to care and learning in the Operating Philosophy field.
7. Enter the name and email of the person responsible for mission inquiries in the Contact Person and Contact Email fields, respectively.
8. If necessary, add any additional notes about your center in the Additional Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required): The name of your childcare center or daycare.
* **Mission Statement** (`mission_statement`, required): A brief description of your center's mission statement.
* **Core Values** (`core_values`, optional): A list of up to 5 core values that your center stands for.
* **Target Age Range** (`target_age_range`, required): The age group served by your center.
* **Community Involvement** (`community_involvement`, optional): Describe any outreach or partnership activities.
* **Operating Philosophy** (`operating_philosophy`, optional): A short description of your center's approach to care and learning.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required): The name of the person responsible for mission inquiries.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required): The email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, optional): Any extra information about your center.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the person responsible for mission inquiries.
* This form is a public document, and the information collected will be publicly accessible.
