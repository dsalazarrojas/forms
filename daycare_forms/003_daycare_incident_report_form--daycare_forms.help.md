# Daycare Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Incident Report Form is used to document incidents that have occurred in a daycare setting, such as accidents, illnesses, or other events that require attention or investigation. This form helps staff and parents to report and respond to these incidents in a standardized and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2.  Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
3.  Describe the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
4.  Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Description of the Incident" field.
5.  Indicate if a staff member was involved in the incident and if so, choose the "Yes" option in the "Was a staff member involved?" field.
6.  Indicate if a parent was involved in the incident and if so, choose the "Yes" option in the "Was a parent involved?" field.
7.  Choose the "Yes" option in the "Report by" field if a staff member reported the incident.
8.  Choose the "Yes" option in the "Reported by" field if a parent reported the incident.
9.  Select the type of incident from the options provided in the "Type of Incident" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child who was involved in the incident.
*   **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
*   **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Description of the Incident** (`incident_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Was a staff member involved?** (`staff_involvement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if a staff member was involved in the incident.
*   **Was a parent involved?** (`parent_involvement`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if a parent was involved in the incident.
*   **Report by** (`report_by_staff`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if a staff member reported the incident.
*   **Reported by** (`report_by_parent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if a parent reported the incident.
*   **Type of Incident** (`incident_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of incident from the provided options (Accident, Illness, Other).

## Tips
*   Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Description of the Incident" field.
*   If an incident requires immediate attention, report it as soon as possible.
*   If an incident is a recurrence of a previous incident, please mention the previous incident number or description.
*   Review and update your answers carefully before submitting the form.
