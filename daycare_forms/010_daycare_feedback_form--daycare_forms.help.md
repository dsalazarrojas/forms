# Daycare Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for parents or caregivers to provide feedback on their daycare experience. Please fill out this form to help us understand your thoughts and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form only once per family or child.
- If you have multiple children, you may need to fill out the form multiple times, but please do not submit duplicate or redundant feedback.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability, and don't hesitate to add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daycare Form** (`daycare_form`): Enter your thoughts, comments, or feedback about your child's daycare experience. This is your opportunity to share your opinions and suggestions.
* **Parents** (`parent`): Please enter your name or the names of the individuals responsible for submitting this feedback (e.g., both parents, guardian, or another authorized caregiver).
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`): Answer the question "What did we do well?" with a brief description or example of an accomplishment or achievement.
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`): Provide any additional comments, thoughts, or suggestions you have about your child's daycare experience.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): Please enter the date of your child's most recent visit or the date you are providing feedback on.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`): Enter the approximate time of your child's most recent visit or the time you are providing feedback on (e.g., morning, afternoon, or full day).
* **Rating Question** (`rating_question`): Answer the question "How well did we do?" with one of the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Select one of the following options: Good, Fair. Please note that this rating refers to your overall satisfaction with your child's daycare experience.
* **Rating 1-5** (`rating_1`, `rating_2`, `rating_3`, `rating_4`, `rating_5`): These fields are not currently used and may be removed or repurposed in the future. Please do not fill them out.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`): Please provide a brief explanation or example of your ratings, if needed.
* **Rating Note** (`rating_note`): Additional comments or suggestions related to your ratings.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name or the name of the person submitting this feedback.
* **Note** (`note`): Any additional comments or suggestions you have about your child's daycare experience.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`): This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
