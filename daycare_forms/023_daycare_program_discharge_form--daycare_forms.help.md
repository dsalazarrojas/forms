# Daycare Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the discharge process of a child from a daycare program.
## How To Complete This Form

- Please start by selecting the date of discharge using the calendar feature.
- Choose the reason for discharge from the available options.
- Provide contact information for the caregiver, including their name, email, and phone number.
- Enter the child's name, date of birth, and gender.
- Enter the caregiver's name and phone number.
- Indicate the discharge status of the child.
- Enter the daycare name and phone number.
- Optionally, add any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Discharge Date** (1, date, required): Select the date the child is being discharged using the calendar feature.
*   **Reason For Discharge** (2, text, optional): Please explain why the child is being discharged.
*   **Caregiver Contact Information** (3, note, optional): Enter the contact information for the person responsible for the child after discharge. This can include their name, email, and phone number.
*   **Comments** (4, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the child's discharge.
*   **Discharge Signature** (5, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the child has been discharged completely (True) or not (False).
*   **Child Name** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of the child being discharged.
*   **Child Date of Birth** (7, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the child being discharged.
*   **Child Gender** (8, select_multiple, optional): Choose the gender of the child being discharged.
*   **Caregiver Name** (9, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the child after discharge.
*   **Caregiver Email** (10, email, optional): Enter the email of the person responsible for the child after discharge.
*   **Caregiver Phone Number** (11, text, optional): Enter the 10-digit phone number of the person responsible for the child after discharge.
*   **Discharge Status** (12, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the child has been fully discharged (Complete) or not (Incomplete).
*   **Daycare Name** (13, text, optional): Enter the name of the daycare program the child was in.
*   **Daycare Address** (14, text, optional): Enter the address of the daycare program.
*   **Daycare Phone Number** (15, text, optional): Enter the 10-digit phone number of the daycare program.
*   **Notes** (16, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the child's discharge.
