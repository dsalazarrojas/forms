# Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Boarding Waiver And Consent Form is a document that allows pet owners to provide essential information about their pet, including medical conditions, contact information, and emergency contact details. This form is used to ensure that the pet is properly cared for and that the owner's wishes are respected in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the pet's name and type/breed in the relevant fields.
2. Enter the owner's contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Provide emergency contact details, including name, phone number, and relation.
4. Indicate the date and time you would like the waiver to be effective.
5. Sign the waiver, selecting the correct option from the dropdown menu.
6. Sign the terms and conditions, providing a written agreement to the terms outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_boarding_waiver_and_consent_form**: This is the main title of the form. Please do not fill in this field.
* **pet_boarding_waiver_and_consent_form_name**: This is the pet's name. Please enter the name of your pet.
* **contact_email**: This is the owner's email address. Please enter your email address.
* **contact_phone**: This is the owner's phone number. Please enter your phone number.
* **pet_name**: This is the name of your pet.
* **pet_breed**: This is the breed of your pet. Please select from the dropdown menu.
* **pet_type**: This is the type of your pet (e.g. dog, cat, etc.). Please select all that apply from the dropdown menu.
* **owner_sign_date**: This is the date that you, the owner, agree to the terms of this waiver.
* **owner_sign_time**: This is the time that you, the owner, agree to the terms of this waiver.
* **owner_sign_am_pm**: This is the AM/PM selection for when you sign the waiver. Please select "AM" or "PM".
* **waiver**: This is the waiver that you, the owner, agree to the terms of this document. Please select from the dropdown menu.
* **owner_signature**: This is where you, the owner, sign the document. Please select "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **terms_and_conditions**: This is where you, the owner, agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this document. Please read carefully and sign with your signature.
* **date_of_admission**: This is the date that your pet will be admitted to the boarding facility. Please enter the date.
* **owner_signature_date**: This is the date that you, the owner, sign this document. Please enter the date.
* **emergency_contact**: This is the name and phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_relation**: This is the relation of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_address**: This is the address of the emergency contact person.
* **owner_address**: This is your address.
* **owner_phone**: This is your phone number.
* **emergency_phone**: This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **pet_medical**: This is any medical information about your pet that is essential for its care.
* **owner_medical**: This is any medical information about you, the owner, that is essential for your pet's care.
* **owner_address**: This is your address.
* **owner_phone**: This is your phone number.
