# kidz_inc_childcare_job_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kidz_inc_childcare_job_application form is a job application form for potential caregivers at Kidz Inc. childcare center. It's designed to collect your personal details, job preferences, and experience to help us determine whether you're a good fit for our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Enter your Contact Phone number.
3. Provide your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Choose your Job Status (Available, Unavailable, or Other).
6. Select your Job Shift (AM, PM, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend).
7. Enter your Available Start and End dates.
8. Choose how many days per week you are available to work.
9. Select your Level of Job Education (High School Diploma, Some College, Bachelor's, or Master's).
10. Enter the field of job education (if applicable).
11. Choose your level of Job Experience (None, Some, or More).
12. Enter your Salary Request (optional).
13. Choose your Availability (Full-time, Part-time, or Flexible).
14. Select your Salary Range (optional).
15. Choose your Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, Temp, or Freelance).
16. Enter a brief Job Description.
17. List any relevant Job Skills.
18. Choose your level of Job Requirements (High, Medium, or Low).
19. Enter any additional Job Requirement Details.
20. List any Job References (optional).
21. Enter a brief Job Agreement.
22. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Application (1)**: This is the title of your job application form.
* **First Name (2)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth (6)**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Job Title (7)**: Enter the job title you are applying for (if any).
* **Job Status (8)**: Choose whether you are Available, Unavailable, or Other.
* **Job Shift (9)**: Choose your preferred job shift (AM, PM, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend).
* **Available Start (10)**: Enter the start date you are available to begin working.
* **Available End (11)**: Enter the end date you are available to work.
* **Available Days (12)**: Choose how many days per week you are available to work.
* **Job Education (13)**: Choose your highest level of education completed (High School Diploma, Some College, Bachelor's, or Master's).
* **Job Education Field (14)**: Enter the field of education relevant to the job you are applying for (if applicable).
* **Job Experience (15)**: Choose whether you have No experience, Some experience, or More experience.
* **Salary Request (16)**: Enter your desired salary range (optional).
* **Availability (17)**: Choose whether you are Full-time, Part-time, or Flexible.
* **Salary Range (18)**: Choose your salary range (optional).
* **Job Type (19)**: Choose whether you are looking for Full-time, Part-time, Temp, or Freelance work.
* **Job Description (20)**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for (if any).
* **Job Skills (21)**: List any relevant skills you have that match the job requirements.
* **Job Requirements (22)**: Choose whether your level of Job Requirements is High, Medium, or Low.
* **Job Requirement Details (23)**: Enter any additional details about your job requirements.
* **Job References (24)**: List any job references (optional).
* **Job Agreement (25)**: Enter a brief agreement you have with the job you are applying for.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that your application is complete and accurate before submitting.
* You can save your application and come back later to complete it if you need more time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact email/phone number].
