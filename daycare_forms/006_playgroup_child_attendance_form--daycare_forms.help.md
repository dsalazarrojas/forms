# Playgroup Child Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the attendance of children at a playgroup session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's name under the "Child Name" field.
2. Enter the guardian's name under the "Guardian Name" field.
3. Record the child's date of birth under the "Child Dob" field.
4. Enter the time the child is dropped off under the "Drop Off Time" field.
5. Enter the time the child is picked up under the "Pick Up Time" field.
6. Record the date of attendance under the "Attendance Date" field.
7. Select the attendance status of the child under the "Prophets Attendance Status" field (Note: This field is likely intended to be the "Attendance Status" field).
8. Enter any drop off details under the "Drop Off Details" field.
9. Enter any pick up details under the "Pick Up Details" field.
10. Indicate whether the child was dropped off by an authorized person under the "Drop Off Person" field.
11. Indicate whether the child was picked up by an authorized person under the "Pick Up Person" field.
12. Add any comments under the "Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name (Child Name)**: The name of the child.
* **Guardian Name (Guardian Name)**: The name of the child's guardian.
* **Child Dob (Child Dob)**: The date of birth of the child.
* **Drop Off Time (Drop Off Time)**: The time the child is dropped off at the playgroup.
* **Pick Up Time (Pick Up Time)**: The time the child is picked up from the playgroup.
* **Attendance Date (Attendance Date)**: The date the child attended the playgroup.
* **Attendance Status (Prophets Attendance Status)**: The status of the child's attendance at the playgroup. Please note that this field is a number field, suggesting that it may have specific status codes.
* **Drop Off Details (Drop Off Details)**: Any details about the child's drop off, such as any special instructions or requirements.
* **Pick Up Details (Pick Up Details)**: Any details about the child's pick up, such as any special instructions or requirements.
* **Drop Off Person (Drop Off Person)**: Whether the child was dropped off by an authorized person.
* **Pick Up Person (Pick Up Person)**: Whether the child was picked up by an authorized person.
* **Comments (Comments)**: Any additional comments or information about the child's attendance.

## Tips
* Be accurate and consistent when filling in the child's and guardian's names.
* Ensure the dates and times entered are correct and easy to read.
* Use the correct attendance status for the child's attendance. If unsure, check with the playgroup staff.
* Enter any additional comments or details when necessary.
