# Daycare Service Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Service Booking Form is designed to enable parents to book a daycare service for their children. This form helps the childcare staff to gather essential details about the child and the requested service.

## How To Complete This Form

To book a daycare service, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the booking details, including the start date and start time of the service.
2. Provide your child's details, including any medical or dietary requirements.
3. Specify the type of care required, such as babysitting, child care, or other.
4. Indicate the duration of care needed.
5. Add any additional care notes if required.
6. Review your form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details** (`daycare_service_booking_form_1`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to enter the start date and start time of the service.
* **Parent Details** (`parent_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your child's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you'd like to start the service.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you'd like to start the service.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the service will end.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the service will end.
* **Care Notes** (`care_notes`, `note`, required: false): Please add any additional information about your child's needs or requirements.
* **Care Type** (`care_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of care required, such as Babysitting, Child care, or Other.
* **Care Duration** (`care_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours or days of care needed.
