# Daycare Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Emergency Contact Form is a crucial document that helps daycare providers gather essential contact information in case of an emergency. This form ensures that the provider has the necessary details to contact you or your authorized representative in case of an unexpected event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the emergency contact option that applies to you. If you have more than one emergency contact, you can select multiple options.
2. Enter the name of your emergency contact.
3. Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
4. Specify the type of relation this person has with the child (e.g., father, mother, brother, etc.).
5. Enter an alternative contact number and name in case the primary contact is unavailable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`): Select one or more emergency contacts from the dropdown menu. This is a critical step in ensuring the daycare provider has the right contact information in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of your emergency contact. This should match the name used by your emergency contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relation** (`relation`): Specify the type of relation this person has with the child (e.g., father, mother, brother, sister).
* **Relation Type** (`relation_type`): This field is used to describe the type of relation between the emergency contact and the child.
* **Alternative Contact** (`emergency_contact_alternative`): If the primary contact is unavailable, enter an alternative contact name.
* **Alternative Phone Number** (`alternative_phone`): Enter the phone number of the alternative contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have informed your emergency contact about their role and the importance of being reachable in case of an emergency.
* Review your submission to ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
