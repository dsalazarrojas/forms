# Daycare Staff Shift Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Staff Shift Scheduling Form is used to schedule shifts for daycare staff, ensuring that staff are assigned to shifts based on their availability and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the shifts you are available to work (`Staff Availability`).
2. Choose the type of shift you are available for (`Shift Types`).
3. Enter the date of the shift (`Schedule Date`).
4. Enter the start and end times of the shift (`Start Time` and `End Time`).
5. Select the staff member assigned to the shift (`Staff Member`).
6. Choose the tools that will be assigned to the staff member (`Assigned Tool`).
7. Add any notes or comments about the shift (`Notes`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Shifts**: Please enter the shifts you are willing to work. You can enter multiple shifts, if needed.
* **Staff Availability**: Select 'Yes' if you are available to work, 'No' otherwise.
* **Shift Types**: Choose the type of shift you are available for, such as Shift A, Shift B, or Shift C.
* **Schedule Date**: Enter the date of the shift. Please use the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the shift. Please use the format 24-hour clock (e.g., 08:00 for 8am).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the shift. Please use the format 24-hour clock (e.g., 17:00 for 5pm).
* **Staff Member**: Select 'Yes' if you are available to be assigned to this shift. Please note that this field may not be applicable in all cases.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tools that will be assigned to the staff member, such as chatjimmy or Other.
* **Notes**: Add any notes or comments about the shift, if needed.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the `Submit` button to submit your schedule.
