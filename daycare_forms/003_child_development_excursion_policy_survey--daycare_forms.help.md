# Child Development Excursion Policy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Child Development Excursion Policy Survey is a form designed for parents or guardians to provide information about their child's participation in excursions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the childcare center's excursion policy.
2. Select the survey date from the calendar prompt.
3. Fill out the child's details: first name, last name, date of birth, and emergency contact information.
4. Confirm that you have reviewed the excursion policy and understand the procedures.
5. Provide your consent for local and distant excursions.
6. Indicate any medical conditions that may be relevant during excursions.
7. Specify any mobility accommodations needed.
8. Consent to transportation by approved childcare vehicles.
9. Provide any additional comments or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Date of survey completion.
* **Parent First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Legal first name of parent or guardian.
* **Parent Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Legal last name of parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Primary email address for contact.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Primary phone number for contact.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, text, required): Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, text, required): Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, required): Please provide the child's birth date.
* **Excursion Policy Review Confirmation** (`policy_reviewed`, select_one, required): Have you reviewed the childcare center's excursion policy? (Yes, I have reviewed the policy; No, I need to review the policy; I need a copy of the policy.)
* **Understanding of Excursion Procedures** (`understand_excursion_procedures`, select_one, required): Do you understand the procedures for child excursions? (Yes, completely; Mostly; Somewhat; No, I need clarification)
* **Consent for Local Excursions** (`consent_for_local_excursions`, select_one, required): Do you consent to your child participating in local excursions within the neighborhood? (Yes, I consent; No, I do not consent; I need more information)
* **Consent for Distant Excursions** (`consent_for_distant_excursions`, select_one, required): Do you consent to your child participating in excursions outside the local area? (Yes, I consent; No, I do not consent; Case by case basis)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Full name of emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Phone number for emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions Relevant to Excursions** (`medical_conditions_during_excursions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions staff should be aware of during excursions.
* **Mobility Accommodations Needed** (`mobility_accommodations`, select_one, required): Does your child require any mobility accommodations during excursions? (No accommodations needed; Stroller required; Wheelchair accessible transport; Frequent rest breaks; Other)
* **Transportation Consent** (`transportation_consent`, select_one, required): Do you consent to your child being transported by approved childcare vehicles? (Yes, I consent; No, I do not consent; Only for distant excursions)
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or concerns regarding excursions.
