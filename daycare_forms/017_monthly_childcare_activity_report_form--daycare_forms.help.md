# Monthly Childcare Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about childcare activities for a specific month and year.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the month you want to report on.
2. If you are a provider, enter your name in the "Provider Name" field. If you are not a provider, this field can be left blank.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Provider Phone" field, if you have one.
4. Enter the name of the activity or service provided in the "Childcare Activity" field.
5. Add any relevant notes about the activity in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By" field.
7. Enter the email of the person submitting the form in the "Submitted By Email" field.
8. Select the date the form was submitted in the "Submit Date" field.
9. Select the rating for the provider's service in the "Provider Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monthly** (`month`, `select_one`, not required): Select the month for which you are submitting this report.
* **Year** (`year`, `select_one`, not required): Select the year for which you are submitting this report.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the provider.
* **Provider Phone** (`provider_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the provider, if you have one.
* **Childcare Activity** (`activity_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the activity or service provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Add any relevant notes about the activity.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, `date`, not required): Select the date the form was submitted.
* **Provider Rating** (`provider_rating`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the rating for the provider's service.
