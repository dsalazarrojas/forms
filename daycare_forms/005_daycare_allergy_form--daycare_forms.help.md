# Daycare Allergy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any allergies, medical conditions, or emergency contact information for a child in our daycare program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields with your child's information, such as their name, date of birth, and emergency contact details.
2. If your child has any allergies, select all that apply from the "Food Allergies" and "Medication Allergies" dropdown menus.
3. Provide any additional medical information, such as medical documentation, notes from your doctor, and any relevant comments.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click "submit" to save your child's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name**: Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Allergy Type**: Select all the food allergies that your child has, such as Peanut, Tree Nut, Fish, Shellfish, Milk, Eggs, Wheat, Soy, or Sesame.
* **Allergy Description**: Provide any additional information about your child's allergies, such as any triggers or reactions.
* **Medical Documentation**: Attach any relevant medical documentation or reports from your child's doctor.
* **Medical Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of your child's primary medical contact.
* **Medical Notes**: Provide any additional medical notes or information about your child's health.
* **Food Allergies**: Select all the food allergies that your child has from the list.
* **Medication Allergies**: Select any medication allergies that your child has from the list.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact and your child (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Daycare Name**: Enter the name of the daycare your child will be attending.
* **Daycare Address**: Enter the address of the daycare.
* **Daycare Contact**: Enter the contact information for the daycare.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of your child's doctor.
* **Doctor Notes**: Provide any notes from your child's doctor.
* **Medication**: Enter any medication that your child is currently taking.
* **Medication Notes**: Provide any notes about your child's medication.
* **Medical History**: Provide any medical history or conditions that your child has.
* **Medical Comments**: Provide any additional medical comments or information about your child.

Note: This form is intended for daycare staff use only, and the information provided will be kept confidential and used only for your child's care.
