# Daycare Video Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by daycare or childcare institutions to obtain consent from parents or guardians for video monitoring in the classroom. It is intended to be used for monitoring purposes only and not for any other use, such as recording or sharing of the video footage without explicit consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the purpose and implications of video monitoring.
2. Select the correct option for "Give Us Permission" from the drop-down menu. Options include "Parent or Guardian", "Parent", or "Guardian". This will indicate who is giving the consent.
3. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Do You Consent" to video monitoring in the classroom.
4. If you have given consent, you can optionally select a date for "Date" and a time for "Time" to record the specific consent date and time.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can provide them in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Give Us Permission** (`select_multiple`, required): This field asks you to specify who you are giving consent for. Options are "Parent or Guardian", "Parent", or "Guardian". Please select one or more of these options.
* **Do You Consent** (`select_one`, required): This field asks if you give consent to video monitoring in the classroom. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): If you have given consent, you can optionally select a specific date for the consent to be recorded.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): If you have given consent, you can optionally select a specific time for the consent to be recorded.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you want to provide regarding your consent.
