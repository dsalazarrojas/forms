# Pediatric Medical Daycare Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a daycare center during a pediatric medical visit. The information collected will be used to assess the daycare center's compliance with health and safety standards. Please fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the daycare center you are visiting.
2.  Enter the address of the daycare center.
3.  Enter the city of the daycare center.
4.  Enter the state of the daycare center.
5.  Enter the zip code of the daycare center.
6.  Enter the date of your visit to the daycare center.
7.  Enter the time of your visit to the daycare center.
8.  Enter the name of the physician who performed the inspection.
9.  Enter the address of the physician.
10. Enter the physician's license number.
11. Enter the physician's phone number.
12. Enter the physician's email.
13. Enter the name of the pediatrician (if not the same as the physician).
14. Enter the address of the pediatrician.
15. Enter the pediatrician's phone number.
16. Enter the pediatrician's email.
17. Enter the pediatrician's license number.
18. Select all pediatrician specialties (e.g., pediatrics, internal medicine, etc.).
19. Select all insurance types (e.g., private, public, etc.).
20. Select the type of license held by the pediatrician.
21. Enter the pediatrician's experience (e.g., number of years in practice).
22. Enter the pediatrician's phone number.
23. Enter the pediatrician's email.
24. Enter the pediatrician's license number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Daycare Center** (`daycare_center`, text, required): Enter the name of the daycare center you are visiting.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the daycare center.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of the daycare center.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state of the daycare center.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the daycare center.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit to the daycare center.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit to the daycare center.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the physician who performed the inspection.
* **Physician Address** (`physician_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the physician.
* **Physician License** (`physician_license`, text, required): Enter the physician's license number.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Physician Email** (`physician_email`, email, required): Enter the physician's email address.
* **Pedictrian Name** (`pediatrician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pediatrician (if not the same as the physician).
* **Pediatrician Address** (`pediatrician_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the pediatrician.
* **Pediatrician Phone** (`pediatrician_phone`, text, required): Enter the pediatrician's phone number.
* **Pediatrician Email** (`pediatrician_email`, email, required): Enter the pediatrician's email address.
* **Pediatrician License** (`pediatrician_license`, text, required): Enter the pediatrician's license number.
* **Pediatrician License Number** (`pediatrician_license_number`, text, required): Enter the pediatrician's license number.
* **Pediatrician Specialty** (`pediatrician_specialty`, select_multiple, false): Select all pediatrician specialties (e.g., pediatrics, internal medicine, etc.).
* **Pediatrician Insurance** (`pediatrician_insurance`, select_multiple, false): Select all insurance types (e.g., private, public, etc.).
* **Pediatrician License Type** (`pediatrician_license_type`, select_multiple, false): Select the type of license held by the pediatrician.
* **Pediatrician Experience** (`pediatrician_experience`, text, required): Enter the pediatrician's experience (e.g., number of years in practice).
* **Pediatrician Phone** (`pediatrician_phone`, text, required): Enter the pediatrician's phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check all entered data for accuracy before submission.
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly to avoid missing information.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or contact our support team.
