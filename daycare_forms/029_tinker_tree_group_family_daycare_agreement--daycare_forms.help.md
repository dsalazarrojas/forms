# tinker_tree_group_family_daycare_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for daycare settings to gather essential data about the child's family, including their name, address, contact information, and relationship details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Address" fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your "Phone Number" and "Email" to facilitate communication.
3. Provide your "Date of Birth" and "Start Date" for the child, as well as the "End Date".
4. Select the correct "Relationship" type (Mother, Father, or Guardian) to indicate your relationship to the child.
5. Enter the "Emergency Contact Name" and their "Relationship" type.
6. Provide the "Emergency Contact Phone Number".
7. If applicable, fill in the "Assigned By" field with the name of the user who assigned the form.
8. Finally, add any "Comments" you might have about the child or their care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter the child's last name.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Enter the child's home address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter the child's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, text, required: false): Enter the child's contact email.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required: false): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the child's care.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the child's care.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select your relationship to the child (Mother, Father, or Guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, required: false): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person to the child.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`Emergency Contact Phone Number`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Assigned By** (`Assigned By`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the user who assigned the form (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required: true): Add any comments or remarks about the child or their care (required field).

## Tips
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Ensure that each field is completed correctly to avoid duplication.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask for help or clarification before proceeding.
* Fill in the form in a way that's easy to read and understand for others who might need to access the information.
