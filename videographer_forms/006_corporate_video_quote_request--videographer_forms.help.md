# corporate_video_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate video quote request form is designed to gather essential details for video production projects. It helps internal teams or external vendors to understand project requirements in a structured and standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the "Video Quote Request" in the first field.
2. Fill out the "Company Name", "Contact Person", and "Email" fields with the relevant information.
3. Provide your "Phone" number and the "Project Location" where the video production will take place.
4. Specify the "Project Type" and include any additional "Project Details" in the text field.
5. Enter the expected "Start Date" and "End Date" of the project.
6. Provide an estimate of the "Budgeted Time" required for the project.
7. Enter the "Estimated Budget" for the project.
8. Enter the actual "Actual Budget" if the project has already been budgeted.
9. Add any "Project Notes" that may be relevant to the project.
10. Finally, select "Select Submit" to submit the form, or choose "Cancel" to discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Quote Request**: A text field where you can enter the title of the video project.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company or organisation.
* **Contact Person**: The person responsible for the project.
* **Email**: The contact email of the person responsible for the project.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number of the person responsible for the project.
* **Project Location**: The location where the video production will take place.
* **Project Type**: A field to specify the type of project.
* **Project Details**: A text field where you can provide additional details about the project.
* **Start Date**: The date when the project is expected to start.
* **End Date**: The date when the project is expected to end.
* **Budgeted Time**: The estimated time required for the project.
* **Estimated Budget**: The estimated budget for the project.
* **Actual Budget**: The actual budget if the project has already been budgeted.
* **Project Notes**: Any additional notes about the project.
* **Form Submit**: Select "Select Submit" to submit the form.
