# Music Video Direction Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Video Direction Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in applying for music video direction work. This form allows applicants to provide their contact details, portfolio, experience, reel information, and submit their application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Fill in the form with the requested information, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Attach any additional relevant files, such as your portfolio or reel, to support your application.
4. Submit the form to initiate your music video direction application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): A brief introduction to your application.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact details, including email and phone number.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, optional): Share your portfolio or a link to your online portfolio to showcase your work and experience.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information that you feel is relevant to your application.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Briefly describe your experience and qualifications for music video direction work.
* **Reel Info** (`reel_info`, text, optional): Share information about your reel, including the platform it was published on and the date it was published.
* **Reel Files** (`reel_files`, text, optional): Attach any relevant files related to your reel, such as a link to the video itself or a screenshot.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your application once you have completed all the required and optional fields.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to ensure a successful submission.
* Attach relevant files to support your application.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.
* Contact us with any questions or concerns regarding the form.
