# Music Video Collaboration Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Video Collaboration Agreement is a form used to outline the terms and conditions of music video collaborations between clients and videographers. This guide will help you complete the form accurately and ensure a smooth collaboration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the Project Lead field with the name and contact information of the project lead.
2. Enter the Project Details in the next field.
3. Select the Collaboration Terms that apply to your project.
4. Choose the Roles involved in the collaboration.
5. Fill in the Timelines for the project.
6. Enter the Credit Terms for the collaborators.
7. Specify the Compensation Terms for the project.
8. Add any Other Notes about the project.
9. Enter the Music Video Title.
10. Provide Client Information.
11. Enter the Client's Contact Email.
12. Enter the Client's Contact Phone number.
13. Describe the Music Video in the Music Video Description field.
14. Select the Music Video Genre.
15. Enter the Music Video Release Date.
16. Specify the Music Video Duration.
17. Choose the Music Video Format.
18. Select the Music Video Resolution.
19. Enter the Client's Approvals.
20. Specify the Client Review.
21. Choose the Client Confirmation option.
22. Enter the Client Confirmation Date.
23. This field is for the Music Video Collaboration Agreement itself.
24. Enter the version of the Music Video Collaboration Agreement.
25. If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Lead**: Enter the name and contact information of the project lead.
* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project.
* **Collaboration Terms**: Select from the options provided to outline the terms of the collaboration.
* **Roles**: Select the roles involved in the collaboration, such as Client, Director, Videographer, or Other.
* **Timelines**: Enter the timelines for the project, including start and end dates.
* **Credit Terms**: Enter the terms for crediting the collaborators.
* **Compensation Terms**: Enter the compensation terms for the collaborators.
* **Other Notes**: Add any additional notes about the project. (Optional)
* **Music Video Title**: Enter the title of the music video.
* **Client Information**: Enter the client's information.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Music Video Description**: Briefly describe the music video.
* **Music Video Genre**: Select the genre of the music video.
* **Music Video Release Date**: Enter the release date of the music video.
* **Music Video Duration**: Enter the duration of the music video.
* **Music Video Format**: Select the format of the music video.
* **Music Video Resolution**: Select the resolution of the music video.
* **Client Approvals**: Enter the client's approval status.
* **Client Review**: Enter the client's review of the video.
* **Client Confirmation**: Select the client's confirmation option.
* **Client Confirmation Date**: Enter the date of the client's confirmation.
* **Music Video Collaboration Agreement**: This field is for the Music Video Collaboration Agreement itself.
* **Music Video Collaboration Agreement Version**: Enter the version of the Music Video Collaboration Agreement.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the project.

Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to maintain a smooth and successful music video collaboration.
