# videographer_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The videographer_information_form is a form designed to collect essential information from videographers, including their name, contact details, and professional contact information. This form helps ensure that videographers can be contacted and communicated with effectively, facilitating a smooth experience for both the videographer and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. (Optional) Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. (Optional) Enter your Address in the Address field.
6. (Optional) Enter your Company name in the Company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your personal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address where you can be contacted. This field is optional, but it helps us ensure we can reach you quickly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached. This field is optional, but it helps us contact you directly if needed.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your office or studio address where you can be reached. This field is optional, but it helps us send physical communications.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your professional company name if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to contact us for further assistance.

This help guide has been created to make it easy for users to understand how to complete the form efficiently and effectively.
