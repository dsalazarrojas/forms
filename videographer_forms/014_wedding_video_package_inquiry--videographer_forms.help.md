# Wedding Video Package Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Video Package Inquiry form is a tool used to gather essential information about your event to provide a tailored quote for our video services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Get Started" to begin the inquiry process.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Event Category" to indicate the type of event you are planning.
3. Enter the "Event Date" in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. Select the "Event Time" of your event (HH:MM).
5. Enter the "Venue" where the event will take place.
6. Describe the "Event Type" in the text field.
7. Provide detailed information about the "Event Details".
8. Enter a "Message" about your event (optional).
9. Enter your "Contact Number".
10. Enter your "Contact E-mail".
11. Enter your "Name".
12. Enter your "Phone".
13. Enter your "Address".
14. Enter a "Message" for us to contact you.
15. If applicable, select "Yes" and enter the "Event Budget Amount" in the specified currency.
16. Select the "Event Video Package" you are interested in (Yes/No).
17. Enter detailed information about the "Event Video Package Details".
18. Select the "Payment Method" (Yes/No) and provide "Payment Details".
19. Select the "Payment Terms" (Yes/No) and provide "Payment Details".
20. Enter the "Client Reference" (optional).
21. Enter the "Event Video Package Price" (in the specified currency).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Get Started**: Select "Yes" to begin the inquiry process.
* **Event Category**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate the type of event you are planning.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event in the DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of your event in the HH:MM format.
* **Venue**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Event Type**: Describe the type of event in the text field.
* **Event Details**: Provide detailed information about the event in the text field.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information about your event (optional).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact E-mail**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Message**: Enter a message for us to contact you about your inquiry.
* **Event Budget Amount**: If applicable, enter the budget for your event in the specified currency.
* **Event Video Package**: Select "Yes" to indicate interest in our video services.
* **Event Video Package Details**: Enter detailed information about the video package you are interested in.
* **Payment Method**: Select "Yes" to indicate your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Terms**: Select "Yes" to indicate your preferred payment terms.
* **Payment Details**: Enter payment details related to your selection.
* **Client Reference**: Enter a reference number or ID (optional).
* **Event Video Package Price**: Enter the price of the video package you are interested in (in the specified currency).
* **Form Submitted**: This field will be populated automatically when you submit the form.
