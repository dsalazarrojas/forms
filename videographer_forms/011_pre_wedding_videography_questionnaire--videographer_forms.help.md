# Pre-Wedding Videography Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for the videography services for your upcoming wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter your contact information in the contact fields.
3. Choose your preferred contact method for communication.
4. Select the videographer and their experience level.
5. Specify the equipment required for the videography service.
6. Choose the format and resolution for the final video product.
7. Select the desired delivery time frame for the final video.
8. Review and provide any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Videography Information** (`wedding_videography_information`): This field is where you can provide a brief description of the wedding event.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`): Describe the wedding event, including the location, date, and time.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your contact name, phone number, and email address.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Describe the event, including the location, date, and time.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Specify the event venue.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the event date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the event time.
* **Videographer Name** (`videographer_details`): Choose the name of the videographer you would like to work with (Videographer Name, Videographer 1, Videographer 2, etc.).
* **Videographer Experience** (`videographer_experience`): Optional field for specifying the experience level of the chosen videographer.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Provide any additional equipment requirements for the videography service.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Choose the equipment types required (Camera, Lens, Drone, Sound, Other).
* **Special Request** (`special_request`): Enter any special requests or requirements for the videography service.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional information or comments about the event.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`): Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text).
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Enter your preferred contact method.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Wedding Video Length** (`wedding_video_length`): Optional field for specifying the desired video length.
* **Video Format** (`video_format`): Choose the video format (4K, 6K, HD, Full HD).
* **Video Resolution** (`video_resolution`): Choose the video resolution (1080p, 2K, 4K).
* **Final Delivery** (`final_delivery`): Choose the desired delivery time frame for the final video (1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks, 4 weeks, 5 weeks, 6 weeks).
