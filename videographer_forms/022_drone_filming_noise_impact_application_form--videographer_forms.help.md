# Drone Filming Noise Impact Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help operators report their drone filming operations and provide information about the noise impact of their activities. This form will help in assessing and managing noise levels to ensure that operations do not exceed limits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the operator's contact information in the "operator_info" section.
2. Enter the location where the drone filming operation took place in the "location" section.
3. Provide the start and end times of the operation in the "start_time" and "end_time" sections.
4. Rate the noise level of the operation in the "noise_level" section.
5. Indicate if the operation is a priority in the "priority" section.
6. Select if the operation is assigned to a team member in the "assigned_to" section.
7. Choose the status of the form in the "form_status" section.
8. Provide any comments about the operation in the "comments" section.
9. If necessary, provide comments from the operator in the "operator_comments" section.
10. Select who submitted the form in the "submitted_by" section.
11. Finally, select the status of the form in the "form_status_2" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **What is the operator's contact information?** (`operator_info`, `text`, `true`)
Please provide the operator's name and contact details.

### 2. **What is the location of the drone filming operation?** (`location`, `text`, `true`)
Please enter the location where the operation took place.

### 3. **What is the start time of the drone filming operation?** (`start_time`, `time`, `true`)
Please enter the start time of the operation.

### 4. **What is the end time of the drone filming operation?** (`end_time`, `time`, `true`)
Please enter the end time of the operation.

### 5. **What is the noise level of the drone filming operation?** (`noise_level`, `number`, `true`)
Please rate the noise level of the operation using the provided scale.

### 6. **Is the drone filming operation a priority for noise impact?** (`priority`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select 'Yes' if the operation is a priority for noise impact.

### 7. **Is the drone filming operation assigned to a team member?** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select 'Yes' if the operation is assigned to a team member.

### 8. **What is the status of the form?** (`form_status`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select the status of the form as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### 9. **Any comments about the drone filming operation?** (`comments`, `note`, `true`)
Please provide any comments about the operation.

### 10. **Any comments from the operator about the drone filming operation?** (`operator_comments`, `note`, `true`)
Please provide any comments from the operator.

### 11. **Who submitted the form?** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select who submitted the form.

### 12. **What is the status of the form?** (`form_status_2`, `select_one`, `true`)
Please select the status of the form as either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have conducted a drone filming operation before filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for guidance.
* Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure that your operation is properly assessed.
