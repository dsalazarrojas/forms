# video_order_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The video_order_form_template is designed to collect video order information from clients. This form is meant to be completed by clients to provide their details and sign off on orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client details, including any necessary information for the order.
2. Fill in your order details, including the date.
3. Enter your timeline for the order.
4. Sign with your electronic signature.
5. If applicable, fill in any additional client details.
6. Select the delivery date.
7. Add any relevant contact information, such as your email and phone number.
8. Sign with your electronic signature.
9. Add any additional reports as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (1): This field is for your information, such as your name and contact details.
- **Order Details** (2): Enter the details of the order, including any relevant dates.
- **Timeline** (3): Provide your desired timeline for the order.
- **E-Signature** (4): Sign with your electronic signature to confirm your order.
- **Client Details 2** (5): If you have more than one client, you can fill in their details here.
- **Delivery Date** (6): Select the date you would like the order to be delivered.
- **Email** (7): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (8): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **E-Signature 2** (9): Sign with your electronic signature to confirm your order details.
- **Report 1** (10), **Report 2** (11), **Report 3** (12), **Report 4** (13), **Report 5** (14), **Report 6** (15), **Report 7** (16): These fields are for adding any additional reports or comments as needed.
