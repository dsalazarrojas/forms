# Travel Blog Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Blog Video Upload Form is designed to allow users to share their travel experiences with the blog's audience through video content. This form will help us collect relevant and high-quality videos that can be showcased on the blog.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your video in the "Video Title" field. This will be displayed as the title of your video on the blog.
2. Write a brief description of your video in the "Video Description" field. This will provide context for your video and help users understand what they can expect to see.
3. Add relevant tags to your video in the "Video Tag" field. Tags will help users find your video when searching for content related to your travel experiences.
4. Upload your video by clicking on the "Upload Video" button.
5. Select a category for your video in the "Category" field. This will help us categorize your video for easier navigation on the blog.
6. Optionally, enter a status for your video in the "Status" field. This will help us understand the status of your video.
7. Choose a date for your video in the "Date" field. This will help us schedule your video for publication on the blog.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Title** (`video_title`, required: false): Enter a brief title for your video that accurately describes its content.
* **Video Description** (`video_description`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your video to give users a sense of what they can expect to see.
* **Video Tag** (`video_tag`, required: false): Add relevant tags to your video to help users find it when searching for content related to your travel experiences.
* **Upload Video** (`upload_video`, required: false): Click on this button to upload your video to the blog.
* **Category** (`category`, required: false): Select a category for your video to help us categorize it for easier navigation on the blog.
* **Status** (`status`, required: false): Optionally, enter a status for your video to help us understand its progress.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Choose a date for your video to help us schedule it for publication on the blog.
