# Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Release Form is designed to obtain consent from individuals or entities to use their footage or images in a commercial or non-commercial capacity. This form ensures that all necessary parties are informed and agree to the terms of use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the requested information, including name, address, contact number, and email.
2. Select the purpose for which the footage or image is being released (commercial, non-commercial, media, documentaries, or news).
3. If applicable, indicate any limitations or restrictions on the use of the footage or image.
4. Confirm that the producer has been informed and has signed the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Please enter the name of the individual or entity releasing the footage or image.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the individual or entity releasing the footage or image.
* **Contact Number** (text, required): Provide a contact number where the individual or entity can be reached.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the individual or entity releasing the footage or image.
* **Date Signed** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Time Signed** (time, required): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Purpose Released** (select multiple, required): Select the purpose for which the footage or image is being released.
* **Limitations** (text, optional): If there are any limitations or restrictions on the use of the footage or image, please enter them here.
* **Producer Name** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the producer who was informed and signed the agreement.
* **Producer Email** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the email address of the producer who was informed and signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed** (select one, required): Confirm that the producer has signed the agreement.
* **Date Agreement Signed** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Time Agreement Signed** (time, optional): If applicable, enter the time the agreement was signed.
