# visual_art_creation_footage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Art Creation Footage Consent form is designed to capture consent from individuals whose visual art or footage is being used for various purposes, such as educational, commercial, or promotional activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand its purpose and the questions it asks.
2. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to answer all questions truthfully.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): This is a brief description of the form's purpose and the context in which it is being used. Please provide a short paragraph explaining what you are consenting to.
- **Permission** (`page_2`, `text`, optional): Please indicate whether you grant permission for your visual art or footage to be used.
- **Art Making Details** (`page_3`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are granting permission for your art to be used, or "No" if you are not granting permission.
- **Art Type** (`page_4`, `select_one`, optional): This field is likely related to the type of art being used. Select "Yes" if you are granting permission for your art to be used, or "No" if you are not granting permission.
- **Consent** (`page_5`, `date`, optional): This field may be used to record the date of consent. However, it's unclear without further information on the actual use case.
- **Artwork Details** (`page_6`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional details about your art or footage that might be relevant to its use.
- **Submission** (`page_7`, `text`, optional): This field may be used to record any additional information about the submission or usage of your art or footage.
- **Artist Agreement** (`page_8`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are granting permission for your art to be used, or "No" if you are not granting permission.
- **Artwork Permission** (`page_9`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information related to the permission or usage of your art or footage.
- **Form Submission** (`page_10`, `text`, optional): This field is likely for any additional comments or notes about the form submission.
