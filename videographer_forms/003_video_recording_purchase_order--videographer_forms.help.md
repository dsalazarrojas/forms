# Video Recording Purchase Order - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for video recording services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your customer name and contact email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the date and time of your event.
3. Choose the recording techniques and camera positions that suit your event's requirements.
4. Select the recording equipment and video format that you need.
5. Choose the video resolution and event type that best describes your event.
6. Provide any additional event details and recording location.
7. Select the event size and client company (if applicable).
8. Enter your client name and contact number.
9. Choose the order status and payment method you prefer.
10. Fill in the invoice date, time, and total cost of your order.
11. Add any order notes if you have any specific requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as the event organizer or representative.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, **required**): Select the date of your event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, **required**): Choose the start time of your event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, **required**): Select the end time of your event.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, **optional**): Provide any additional details about your event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, **optional**): Enter the location of your event.
* **Recording Techniques** (`recording_techniques`, select_one, **required**): Choose the recording technique best suited for your event.
* **Camera Positions** (`camera_positions`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the camera positions needed for your event.
* **Recording Equipment** (`recording_equipment`, select_one, **required**): Select the recording equipment you need.
* **Video Format** (`video_format`, select_one, **required**): Choose the video format for your recording.
* **Video Resolution** (`video_resolution`, select_one, **required**): Select the video resolution for your recording.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, **optional**): Enter the location where your recording will take place.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose the type of your event.
* **Event Size** (`event_size`, select_one, **required**): Select the size of your event.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as the client.
* **Client Company** (`client_company`, text, **optional**): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, **required**): Enter your contact number.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, **required**): Choose the status of your order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **required**): Select the payment method you prefer.
* **Invoice Date** (`invoice_date`, date, **required**): Fill in the date of the invoice.
* **Invoice Time** (`invoice_time`, time, **required**): Select the time of the invoice.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, **required**): Fill in the total cost of your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, note, **optional**): Add any additional notes about your order.

Note: If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
