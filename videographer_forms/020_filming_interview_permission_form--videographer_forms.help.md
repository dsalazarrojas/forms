# filming_interview_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Filming Interview Permission Form is a document used to obtain permission from the subject to conduct an interview. It ensures that both the subject and the videographer are aware of their responsibilities and rights during the filming process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the subject's information on Page 1.
2. Provide details about the filming on Page 2.
3. Select the filming location on Page 3.
4. Enter your contact information on Page 4.
5. Sign the release agreement on Page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Subject Information (Page 1)
* **Subject Information (subject_info)**: Provide your name, title, and any other identifying details that we should know about you. This information will be used to address you correctly and ensure that we have a clear understanding of your identity.

### Filming Details (Page 2)
* **Filming Details (filming_details)**: Please describe the filming project, including the topic, duration, and expected outcome. This will help us understand the purpose and scope of the filming.

### Filming Location (Page 3)
* **Filming Location (location)**: Choose one or more locations where we will be conducting the interview. Please select from the options below:
	+ Alley
	+ Beach
	+ City
	+ Forest
	+ Mountain
	+ Park

### Contact Information (Page 4)
* **Subject Contact Information (contact_info)**: Please provide your email, phone number, and any other contact method we should use to reach you. This will ensure that we can communicate with you efficiently.

### Release (Page 5)
* **Release (release)**: Please indicate your consent to be filmed by selecting "Yes" or "No". If you select "Yes", you are giving us permission to film you. If you select "No", we will not proceed with the filming.

### Signature (Page 6)
* **Signature (signature)**: Please sign and date this form to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided. Your signature indicates your agreement to be filmed and confirms that you are aware of your responsibilities as a subject.
