# Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from both the videographer and the subject of the video shoot, ensuring that all parties are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials before starting the video shoot.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Review and agree to the terms of the video release agreement, and sign it if applicable.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Videographer Details (1)** (`videographer_details`, required): Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as the videographer.
* **Video Details (2)** (`video_details`, required): Describe the video shoot, including its purpose, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Consent (3)** (`consent`, required): Select one of the three options to indicate your consent status:
	+ I am 18 or older
	+ I am under 18 but I am a parent or guardian and I give consent
	+ I am under 18 and I give consent
* **Contact Information (4)** (`contact_info`, required): Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Agreement (5)** (`agreement`, required): Review and agree to the terms of this video release agreement. This is an important part of the form, as it outlines the rights and responsibilities of all parties involved.
* **Signature (6)** (`signature`, optional): If you agree to the terms, sign this section to confirm your consent.
* **Additional Comments (7)** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, use this field to provide them.
