# Location Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a location release from a property owner or manager, typically for commercial, film, or video productions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your location name, contact name, and organization name.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter the address of the location.
5. Choose the type of release you are requesting (Commercial, Film, TV Show, or Video).
6. Enter the duration of the release.
7. Provide a description of the release.
8. Sign and date the form.
9. Provide your signature contact details (email, phone, and address).
10. Add any additional notes about the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Location Name** (`location_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the location where filming will take place.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the location.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the organization or company associated with the location.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the location.
* **Date** (`location_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the release.
* **Time** (`location_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the release.
* **Location** (`location_location`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about the location.
* **Location Type** (`location_type`, text, not required): Choose the type of location (e.g. residential, commercial, public).
* **Release Type** (`release_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of release you are requesting (Commercial, Film, TV Show, Video).
* **Release Duration** (`release_duration`, number, not required): Enter the duration of the release.
* **Release Description** (`release_description`, text, required): Provide a description of the release.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Sign and date the form.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed** (`signature_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Contact** (`signature_contact`, text, not required): Enter your contact information.
* **Signature Email** (`signature_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Signature Phone** (`signature_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signature Address** (`signature_address`, text, required): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`signature_note`, text, not required): Add any additional notes about the location.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Ensure that the contact information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
