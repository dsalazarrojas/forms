# photo_video_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo_video_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback on photo and video content, allowing content creators to understand what viewers like or dislike about their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name (or nickname) in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field (if you want to receive a response).
4. Write your thoughts and comments in the "Message" field, describing what you liked or disliked about the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name or nickname for reference, so we can better understand who's providing feedback.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Optional): Provide your last name, which will help us understand your identity (if you feel comfortable sharing).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to receive a response or follow-up questions about your feedback.
* **Message** (`message`, Note, Optional): Share your thoughts, likes, dislikes, or suggestions about the photo or video content. Be specific and detailed for the content creator's benefit.

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive with your feedback, as it will help the content creator improve their work.
- Use a clear and respectful tone to maintain a positive dialogue.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
