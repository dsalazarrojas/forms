# Gym Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering users at the gym, collecting information such as first name, last name, email, phone, date, time, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form and begin filling in your first name.
2. Follow by entering your last name.
3. Input your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select a date you are available to visit the gym.
6. Choose a time that you would like to visit the gym.
7. Add any additional notes about yourself that you'd like to share with the gym staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address that you check regularly. This will be used by the gym staff to contact you.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for the gym staff to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Date (date)**: Select a date you are available to visit the gym.
* **Time (time)**: Choose a time that you would like to visit the gym.
* **Notes (note)**: Add any additional information about yourself that you'd like to share with the gym staff.
