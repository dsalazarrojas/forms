# Fitness Boot Camp Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals who want to participate in a fitness boot camp program. It will help the organizers understand the individual's details and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Specify your level of experience in the fitness boot camp.
4. If you have an emergency contact, provide their details.
5. Choose your payment terms, including frequency and method.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Provide any other relevant notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Level of Experience** (`level_of_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): Agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ I agree to the terms and conditions
	+ I do not agree to the terms and conditions
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your payment terms:
	+ I pay per month
	+ I pay per year
	+ I pay per session
* **Membership Terms** (`membership_terms`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your membership terms:
	+ I agree to the membership terms and conditions
	+ I do not agree to the membership terms and conditions
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any other notes about your participation in the boot camp.
* **First Name of Sponsor** (`first_name_sponsor`, `text`, optional): Enter the first name of your sponsor.
* **Last Name of Sponsor** (`last_name_sponsor`, `text`, optional): Enter the last name of your sponsor.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your sponsor.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your membership type:
	+ Month-to-Month
	+ Month-to-Year
	+ Per Session
* **Membership Period** (`membership_period`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your membership period:
	+ 1-3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your payment frequency:
	+ Monthly
	+ Yearly
	+ Per Session
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your payment method:
	+ Online Payment
	+ Offline Payment
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any other notes about your participation in the boot camp.
