# Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football League Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from users who are interested in joining a football league. This form will help the league administrators to get to know the users better and ensure that all necessary information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Information on the first page.
2. Fill out the League Information on the second page.
3. If you have any team details to share, you can do so on the third page.
4. Click the "Submit" button on the fourth page to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information (1)
• **User Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
• **User Email**: Enter your email address that you use regularly.
• **User Phone**: Enter your phone number that you can be reached at.

### League Information (2)
• **League Name**: Enter the name of the league you want to register for.
• **League Season**: Choose the season you want to register for.

### Team Details (3) (Optional)
• **Team Name**: If you are registering as a team, enter your team's name.
• **Team Members**: List the names of all team members who will be participating in the league.

### Submit (4)
• **Submit**: Click this button to complete your registration.
