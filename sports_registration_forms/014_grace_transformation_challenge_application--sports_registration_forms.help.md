# Grace Transformation Challenge Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grace transformation challenge application form is designed to gather personal, contact, and medical information from individuals interested in participating in a transformational challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" (Fields 5: goals, 6: motivation, and 8: consent).
2. Provide as much detail as possible for the non-required fields (Fields 1: personal_info, 2: contact_info, 3: emergency_contact, 4: physical_details, and 7: medical_info).
3. Ensure that you provide a clear and concise answer for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (Field 1): A field to provide a brief description of yourself.
- **Contact Info** (Field 2): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
- **Emergency Contact** (Field 3): Provide information about someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
- **Physical Details** (Field 4): Describe any physical characteristics that are relevant to the challenge.
- **Goals** (Field 5): Briefly describe what you want to achieve through the transformation challenge. Make sure to be specific about your goals.
- **Motivation** (Field 6): Explain why you are interested in the transformation challenge. Be as detailed as possible.
- **Medical Info** (Field 7): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that might impact your participation, please describe them in this field.
- **Consent** (Field 8): This is a note field for you to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of the challenge. Please check the box to confirm your consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- You are free to use and modify this form as you see fit, just give credit.
