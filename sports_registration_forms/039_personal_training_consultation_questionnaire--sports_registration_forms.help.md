# Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Personal Training Consultation Questionnaire is designed to gather information from individuals who are interested in or are currently undergoing personal training. This form helps trainers and consultants to understand your goals, medical history, and emergency contact information to provide a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Select all that apply for the "training consultation questions" field.
3. Enter your exercise goals in the "exercise goals" field.
4. If you have any injuries, please list them in the "injuries" field.
5. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the "emergency contact" field.
6. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "emergency contact phone number" field.
7. Write any relevant medical notes in the "medical notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Consultation Questions** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose all that apply to help us tailor our consultation to your specific needs.
* **Exercise Goals** (Text):
	+ Describe your fitness goals, such as running a marathon, improving flexibility, or building muscle.
* **Injuries** (Text):
	+ List any injuries you've had in the past 6 months, including but not limited to sprains, strains, or medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact** (Email):
	+ Enter your emergency contact's email address so we can reach out to them in case of any issues during your training.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text):
	+ Enter your emergency contact's phone number so we can reach out to them quickly in case of any issues during your training.
* **Medical Notes** (Note):
	+ Write any relevant medical information that may impact your training, such as allergies or medical conditions.
