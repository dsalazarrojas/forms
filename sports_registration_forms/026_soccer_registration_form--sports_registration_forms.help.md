# Soccer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering players for soccer leagues, teams, or other soccer-related activities. It's meant to collect essential information from participants to create a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Please fill out the form carefully, providing as much detail as possible in each field.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in, as they are marked as "required".
* If you need help or have questions, you can contact us at <form@gic.mx>.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information (main_page)** 
  - This field allows you to provide your name, which will be displayed in our records and for team management purposes.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** 
  - Enter your email, phone number, or other preferred contact method for us to reach you regarding your registration.
* **Registration Details (registration)** 
  - This field is required, so please provide the specific details of your registration, which will be used to track your participation in the league or activity.

## Tips
* If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask, and we'll do our best to clarify.
* Remember to double-check your submissions for accuracy to avoid errors.
* Once you submit your form, we'll review and process your registration promptly.
