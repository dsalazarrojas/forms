# Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Registration Form is designed to collect essential details from teams and players for the smooth organization and running of the sports tournament. This form helps the tournament staff to create a comprehensive plan, including scheduling and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all the required fields (those marked as "Required" in the form) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Fill in the non-required fields with any relevant details that can help the tournament staff understand your team's or player's profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. *Tournament name*
	* (id: 1, name: tournament_name, label: Tournament name, type: text, required: false)

    Enter the official name of the tournament in this field. This can be the name given to the tournament by the organizers or the name used for communication and branding purposes.

### 2. *Team name*
	* (id: 2, name: team_name, label: Team name, type: text, required: false)

    Type the name of your team here. This will help the tournament staff to identify and communicate with your team throughout the event.

### 3. *Player name*
	* (id: 3, name: player_name, label: Player name, type: text, required: false)

    Enter the names of all players participating in the tournament.

### 4. *Date*
	* (id: 4, name: date, label: Date, type: date, required: false)

    Provide the date of the tournament or the date by which you need to confirm your participation. Ensure the date is in a format that is easily understandable by the organizers.

### 5. *Time*
	* (id: 5, name: time, label: Time, type: time, required: false)

    If applicable, provide the scheduled time of the tournament or the expected time by which you will be participating. This can help with coordination and planning.

### 6. *Email*
	* (id: 6, name: email, label: Email, type: email, required: false)

    Enter your email address or that of your team manager. This will serve as a contact point for any important communication from the tournament staff.

### 7. *Phone*
	* (id: 7, name: phone, label: Phone, type: text, required: false)

    Provide your contact phone number or that of your team manager. This can be used as a backup contact method in case of emails going astray or for urgent matters.

### 8. *Team Manager*
	* (id: 8, name: team_manager, label: Team manager, type: text, required: false)

    If your team has a manager, enter their name here. This is useful for the tournament staff to know who to contact regarding team matters.

### 9. *Player position*
	* (id: 9, name: player_position, label: Player position, type: text, required: false)

    If your team has any specific positions (like team captain, coach, etc.), mention it here. This can help the tournament staff in organizing teams and scheduling.

### 10. *Tournament Manager*
	* (id: 10, name: tournament_manager, label: Tournament manager, type: text, required: false)

    If your team is participating in a specific tournament managed by a particular person, enter their name here. This can aid in communication and coordination.

### 11. *Team Manager Email*
	* (id: 11, name: team_manager_email, label: Team manager email, type: email, required: false)

    Enter the email address of your team's manager. This can be used as a contact method for all team-related communications from the tournament staff.

### 12. *Team Manager Phone*
	* (id: 12, name: team_manager_phone, label: Team manager phone, type: text, required: false)

    If applicable, provide the phone number of your team manager. This can serve as an alternative contact method in case emails are not replied.

### 13. *Player Manager Email*
	* (id: 13, name: player_manager_email, label: Player manager email, type: email, required: false)

    Enter the email address of each player's manager. This can help in direct communication with the players and their team.

### 14. *Player Manager Phone*
	* (id: 14, name: player_manager_phone, label: Player manager phone, type: text, required: false)

    If a player has a personal manager, enter their phone number here. This can be used as a backup contact method for player-related communication.

## Tips
- Double-check all provided details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Use the provided hints for guidance where fields are left blank.
- If you're still unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask the tournament staff for clarification. 

This guide is designed to walk users through the necessary steps and provide clear explanations for each field in the tournament registration form to ensure a smooth and efficient experience for all participants and organizers.
