# Gym Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for sports registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** (page 1) - Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Provide your **Email** (page 1) - Enter a valid email address.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** (page 1) - Enter your phone number.
4. Enter your **Gym Name** (page 1) - The name of the gym or sports center you are registering with.
5. Fill in **Sports Center** (page 1) - Enter the sports center you are associated with.
6. Enter **Address** (page 2) - Your address.
7. Enter **City** (page 2) - The city of your location.
8. Enter **State** (page 2) - Your state or province of residence.
9. Enter **Country** (page 2) - Your country of residence.
10. Enter **Zip Code** (page 2) - Your zip code or postal code.
11. Enter your **Date Of Birth** (page 2) - Date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Gym Name** (text, required: false): The name of the gym or sports center you are registering with.
* **Sports Center** (text, required: false): Enter the sports center you are associated with.
* **Address** (text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **City** (text, required: false): The city of your location.
* **State** (text, required: false): Your state or province of residence.
* **Country** (text, required: false): Your country of residence.
* **Zip Code** (text, required: false): Your zip code or postal code.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, required: false): Date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips
* Ensure the email address is correct and valid.
* Use the correct format for the date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD). 
* If any field is required but not filled, the form might not be able to be submitted. Please fill in all required fields.
