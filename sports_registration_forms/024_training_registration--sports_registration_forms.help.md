# Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Registration" form is used to collect information about a training event, including its details such as report name, location, date, time, provider, contact information, and status. This form is used to gather data for internal record-keeping and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the report name as it will appear on the registration form.
2. Enter the training ID, which is a unique identifier for the training session.
3. Select the training date using the date picker.
4. Select the training time using the time picker.
5. Enter the training location, e.g., office or classroom.
6. Select the training provider or organization offering the training.
7. Enter the contact name and email of the person responsible for the training.
8. Select the training status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Canceled.
9. Enter any additional notes about the training session.
10. Enter the training venue, e.g., office or classroom.
11. Select the training start date using the date picker.
12. Select the training end date using the date picker.
13. Enter the training duration, which is the number of days or hours the training will last.
14. Enter the training capacity, which is the maximum number of attendees allowed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Name** (`report_name`, text, required): Enter the report name as it will appear on the registration form.
* **Training ID** (`training_id`, number, required): Enter the training ID, which is a unique identifier for the training session.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select the training date using the date picker.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Select the training time using the time picker.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the training location, e.g., office or classroom.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required): Select the training provider or organization offering the training.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the contact name of the person responsible for the training.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the person responsible for the training.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, required): Select the training status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Canceled.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the training session.
* **Training Venue** (`training_venue`, text, required): Enter the training venue, e.g., office or classroom.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, required): Select the training start date using the date picker.
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, required): Select the training end date using the date picker.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required): Enter the training duration, which is the number of days or hours the training will last.
* **Training Capacity** (`training_capacity`, number, required): Enter the training capacity, which is the maximum number of attendees allowed.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure correct processing and communication.
