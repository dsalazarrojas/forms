## Personal Trainer Client Intake Form - Help Guide
### Purpose

The Personal Trainer Client Intake Form is designed to collect relevant information about the client from a personal trainer to help establish a relationship, assess their training needs, and identify any potential health risks or allergies. This form helps trainers understand the client's goals, availability, and emergency contact details.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your personal details: 
   - **Client Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
   - **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
   - **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
   - **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
   - **Available Days**: Enter the days you are available for training.
   - **Available Time**: Enter the time you are available for training.

2. Provide emergency contact information:
   - **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
   - **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
   - **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
   - **Alternative Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of an alternative emergency contact person.
   - **Alternative Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the alternative emergency contact person.
   - **Alternative Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the alternative emergency contact person.

3. Share your health details:
   - **Medical Conditions**: List any existing medical conditions or concerns.
   - **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities.
   - **Medical Notes**: Enter any additional medical information.

4. Set your training goals and frequency:
   - **Training Goals**: Describe your fitness goals.
   - **Exercise Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your current exercise routine.
   - **Training Frequency**: Enter your desired training frequency.
   - **Training Frequency 2**: Enter any additional training frequency information.
