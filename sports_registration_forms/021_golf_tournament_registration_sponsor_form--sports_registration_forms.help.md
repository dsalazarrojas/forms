# golf_tournament_registration_sponsor_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from golf tournament participants who have sponsors for their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant details section with the relevant information about the participant.
2. In the sponsor details section, provide details about the sponsor.
3. Answer the question about whether the participant has a sponsor in the participant sponsor section.
4. If the participant has a sponsor, fill in their email in the sponsor email section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This section is where you enter details about the participant, such as their name, phone number, or other relevant contact information.
  • **Sponsor Details** (`sponsor_details`): Here, you enter information about the sponsor, like their name, company, or other relevant details.
  • **Participant Sponsor Details** (`participant_sponsor_details`): Enter additional details about the participant's sponsor relationship, such as how they're sponsored or how long they've been sponsored.
  • **Has Sponsor?** (`participant_sponsor`): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has a sponsor.
  • **Sponsor Email** (`participant_sponsor_email`): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, enter the email of the participant's sponsor here.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in each section to ensure efficient processing of your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the tournament organizers or administrators for clarification.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
