# Football Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from football players, including team and player details, contact information, and player's medical status.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3.  Leave blank any fields that are not applicable.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant person or entity for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the football team you will be representing.
*   **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, false): Enter the player's name.
*   **Player Email** (`player_email`, email, false): Enter the player's email address.
*   **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, false): Enter the player's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, false): Enter the contact number for the player or team.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, false): Enter the player's address.
*   **Position** (`player_position`, text, false): Enter the player's position on the team.
*   **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, text, false): Enter the player's shirt size.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, false): Enter the emergency contact number.
*   **Player Education** (`player_education`, text, false): Enter the player's education details.
*   **Player Experience** (`player_experience`, text, false): Enter the player's experience in football.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, false): Enter the relationship between the player and the emergency contact.
*   **Player Medical Condition** (`player_medical_condition`, text, false): Enter any medical condition that the player may have.
*   **Player Medical Notes** (`player_medical_notes`, text, false): Enter any additional medical notes about the player.
*   **Player Medical Authorization** (`player_medical_authorization`, text, false): Enter the authorization for the player's medical information.

Note: The fields marked as `false` are not required, but it is highly recommended to fill them out for better clarity and communication.
