# Youth Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information from parents and guardians of students participating in youth sports programs. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary contact, medical, and payment information to ensure a safe and successful experience for the student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, please select "Parent Information", "Student Information", or "Submit" to move to the relevant section of the form.
2. Fill out each section as instructed, using the space provided for each field.
3. When finished, review your answers and select "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide basic contact information about themselves.
	+ **Name (Text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	+ **Email (Text)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Student Information (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide basic contact information about the student.
	+ **Student Name (Text)**: Enter the student's full name.
	+ **Age (Text)**: Enter the student's age.
	+ **Grade Level (Text)**: Enter the student's grade level or school year.
* **Contact Preferences (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide additional contact information.
	+ **Contact Method (Text)**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, text).
	+ **Contact Time (Text)**: Select how often you would like to be contacted (e.g. daily, weekly, monthly).
* **Emergency Contacts (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide emergency contact information.
	+ **Contact Name (Text)**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
	+ **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
	+ **Relationship (Text)**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the student (e.g. parent, guardian, teacher).
* **Medical Information (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide any medical information that may be relevant to the student's participation.
	+ **Medical Condition (Text)**: Describe any medical conditions that may affect the student's participation.
	+ **Medication (Text)**: List any medications the student takes.
* **Payment Preferences (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to select payment preferences.
	+ **Payment Method (Text)**: Select how you would like to pay for the program (e.g. credit card, bank transfer).
* **Payment Details (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to provide payment details.
	+ **Card Number (Text)**: Enter the credit card number.
	+ **Expiry Date (Text)**: Enter the credit card's expiry date.
* **Terms and Conditions (Optional)**: This section is for parents or guardians to agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
	+ **Agree (Text)**: Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Once all sections are filled out, select "Submit" to submit the form.
* **Save Draft**: Select "Save Draft" to save any unsaved changes and continue filling out the form later.
* **Delete Draft**: Select "Delete Draft" to discard any unsaved changes.

Please review each section carefully and complete the form in a sequential manner. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
