# sports_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in sports-related activities or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your player information in the "Player Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. If you need to, enter emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
4. Fill in your registration information in the "Registration Information" section.
5. Enter any additional player information in the "Player Information 3" section, if necessary.
6. If you have an emergency contact, provide their information in the "Emergency Contact 2" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information (Player Information, text, required/optional)**: This section is for you to enter your name, age, and other relevant player details.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required/optional)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email to ensure we can reach you.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact, text, required/optional)**: If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Registration Information (Registration Information, text, required/optional)**: Enter your registration details, such as team name, jersey number, and other relevant information.
* **Player Information 3 (Player Information 3, text, required/optional)**: This section is for any additional player information you want to provide.
* **Emergency Contact 2 (Emergency Contact 2, text, required/optional)**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their information here.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
