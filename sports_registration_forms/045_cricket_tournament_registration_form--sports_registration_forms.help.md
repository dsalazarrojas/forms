# Cricket Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your player information, such as name, contact details, and player position.
3. Select your emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about the tournament.
* **Player Address**: Enter your residential address.
* **Player Contact Number**: Enter your primary contact number.
* **Player Contact Number 2**: Enter your secondary contact number.
* **Player Position**: Select your position in the team:
	+ Cricket
	+ Bowler
	+ Batter
	+ All-rounder
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (dd/mm/yyyy).
	+ Note: This field is optional but recommended.
* **Date of Birth Date**: Enter your birth date.
	+ Note: This field is optional but recommended.
* **Date of Birth Year**: Enter the year you were born.
	+ Note: This field is optional but recommended.
* **Date of Birth Month**: Select your birth month:
	+ January
	+ February
	+ March

	+ May
	+ June
	+ July
	+ August
	+ September
	+ October
	+ November
	+ December

	+ Note: This field is optional but recommended.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your T-shirt size:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Extra Large
	+ Extra Extra Large
	+ X-Large
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship of your emergency contact:
	+ Father
	+ Mother
	+ Brother
	+ Sister
	+ Friend
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Emergency Contact Name 2**: Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number 2**: Enter the contact number of your secondary emergency contact.

	+ Other (please specify)

## Tips

* Please ensure that your emergency contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are under 18 years old, please get your parent or guardian's approval before filling out this form.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please contact the tournament organizers directly.
