# Fitness Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Fitness Client Intake Form is a comprehensive questionnaire designed to collect essential information from clients interested in fitness services. It helps our team understand their contact details, medical background, emergency contacts, and medical insurance details, ensuring a safe and informed experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Use the provided fields as guidelines for what information is expected from you.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (Contact Details, text, required: false)**: Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Client Details (Client Details, text, required: false)**: Briefly describe yourself, including your age, height, weight, and any other relevant medical information.
* **Availability Status (Availability Status, select_one, required: false)**: Indicate your availability for training, choosing between "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Training Goals (Training Goals, select_multiple, required: false)**: Specify your training goals, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Health History (Health History, text, required: false)**: Share any medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns that may impact your participation in fitness services.
* **Acknowledgement (Acknowledgement, select_one, required: false)**: Confirm that you understand and agree with our terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions (Terms and Conditions, select_one, required: false)**: Confirm that you have read and understood our terms and conditions.
* **Health Insurance (Health Insurance, select_multiple, required: false)**: Disclose your health insurance information, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact, select_multiple, required: false)**: Indicate if you have an emergency contact, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Emergency Contact Details (Emergency Contact Details, text, required: false)**: Provide the details of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and other relevant information.
* **Relationship (Relationship, select_one, required: false)**: Specify your relationship with your emergency contact, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Provide the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: Provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Other (Other, text, required: false)**: Provide any other relevant information about your emergency contact.
* **Details (Details, text, required: false)**: Provide more information about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship, select_one, required: false)**: Specify your relationship with your emergency contact, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Notes (Notes, text, required: false)**: Share any additional comments or notes about your emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance Name (Medical Insurance Name, text, required: false)**: Provide your medical insurance name.
* **Policy (Policy, text, required: false)**: Provide your medical insurance policy number.
* **Group (Group, text, required: false)**: Provide your medical insurance group number.
* **Start Date (Start Date, date, required: false)**: Provide the start date of your medical insurance coverage.
* **End Date (End Date, date, required: false)**: Provide the end date of your medical insurance coverage.
* **Provider (Provider, text, required: false)**: Provide your medical insurance provider name.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" but are not actually required to be filled out. This is to ensure that the form is still navigable and fillable even if the user chooses not to answer them.
