# COVID 19 Self Quarantine Employee Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Self Quarantine Employee Leave Form is designed to gather information from employees who are required to self-quarantine due to COVID-19 exposure or infection. This form will be used by HR and management to process and manage employee absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Employee Name** and **Job Title** fields with your full name and job title.
2. Provide the **Name of Advising Medical Provider** and their **Healthcare Provider Contact Phone** number.
3. Enter a brief **Brief Summary of Quarantine Advice** explaining why you are being advised to self-quarantine.
4. Select the correct **Type of Leave to be Applied** from the provided options.
5. Check the box confirming that you have been advised by a healthcare provider to self-quarantine.
6. Sign and date your **Employee Acknowledgment and Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Your full name as per your personnel records.
* **Job Title**: Your job title as per your personnel records.
* **Name of Advising Medical Provider**: The name of the healthcare provider who advised you to self-quarantine.
* **Medical Provider License Number**: [Optional] The license number of the healthcare provider who advised you.
* **Brief Summary of Quarantine Advice**: A concise explanation of the medical advice received.
* **Leave Start Date**: The date you are expected to start your leave.
* **Leave End Date**: The date you expect to end your leave.
* **Type of Leave to be Applied**: Select the type of leave that best suits your situation.
* **I certify that I have been advised by a healthcare provider to self-quarantine**: Check the box if you have received medical advice.
* **Healthcare Provider Contact Phone**: The contact phone number of your healthcare provider.
* **HR Authorization Name**: [Optional] To be completed by HR department.
* **Employee Acknowledgment and Signature**: Sign your full name to acknowledge that you are submitting this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
- Complete this form carefully to ensure it is processed correctly.
