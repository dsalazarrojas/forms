# Payroll System Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Payroll System Access Form is used to manage employee information, including access to the payroll system. This form will help ensure that employees have the necessary permissions to perform tasks within the system. 

This form is not intended for general employee information updates but rather for specific payroll system access purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields.
2. Review each field carefully.
3. Double-check that all information is accurate.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name in the format "Last Name, First Name".
* **Email** (2, email, required/optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Job Title** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your job title within the company.
* **Manager Id** (4, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have a manager, otherwise select "No".
* **Manager Name** (5, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name if you have a manager.
* **Department** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the departments that you have access to.
* **Employee Type** (7, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have access to employee type, otherwise select "No".
* **Date Hired** (8, date, required/optional): Enter the date you were hired.
* **Date Last Access** (9, date, required/optional): Enter the date you last accessed the system.
* **Pay Rate** (10, number, required/optional): Enter your pay rate.
* **Pay Type** (11, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have pay type, otherwise select "No".
* **Phone** (12, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (13, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your account.
* **Assigned Tool** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the tools you have been assigned.
* **Category** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the category that you belong to.

## Tips

- Be honest about your manager status and access to tools and departments to ensure that you have the necessary permissions to perform tasks in the system.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor for guidance.
