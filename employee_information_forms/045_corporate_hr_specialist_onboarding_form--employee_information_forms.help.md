# corporate_hr_specialist_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate HR specialist onboarding form is designed to collect essential information about new employees, enabling the HR team to onboard them efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all the necessary information about the new employee before starting the form.
* Select the "Employee Details" page and enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
* On the "Employee Information" page, enter the employee's name, title, department, and other relevant details.
* On the "Emergency Contact Details" page, provide the name and contact information of the employee's emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details (<type: text>
* **Employee ID** (`employee_details`, text, required): This is an internal employee ID number that is usually assigned to employees for tracking and identification purposes.
### Employee Information (<type: text>
* **Name** (`employee_info`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee.
* **Title** (`employee_info`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title or position.
* **Department** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department or team.
### Emergency Contact Details (<type: text>
* **Name** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please double-check the information entered to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to save the form before proceeding to the next page.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the HR team for assistance.
