# Screening Checklist For Visitors And Employees - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from visitors and employees to identify potential health risks and ensure a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Contact Information**: Provide your name, contact number, and email address.
2. **Health Declaration**: Answer "yes" or "no" to questions about potential health risks, such as medical conditions or recent contact with someone with a medical condition.
3. **Medical History**: List any medical conditions or treatments you have received.
4. **Screening Results**: Answer questions about your medical screening results, including any reasons for non-screening.
5. **Screening Checklist Results**: Indicate the status of your screening results.
6. **Contact History**: List any recent contacts with medical conditions, people, or places.
7. **Visitor History**: List any recent visits to medical facilities or hospitals.
8. **Medical History**: List any medical conditions or treatments you have received.
9. **Contact History List**: Select one or more options for recent contacts, such as "Name", "Contact Location", "Date of Contact", or "Contact Type".
10. **Medical History List**: Select one or more options for medical conditions, such as "Previously Treated Doctor", "Medical Conditions", or "Other Medical Condition".
11. **Screening Results List**: Select the status of your screening results, such as "Result" or "Reason".
12. **Screening Checklist Results**: Select one or both options for your screening results, such as "Screening Results" or "Other".
13. **Contact Information List**: Select one or more options for your contact information, such as "Name", "Phone", or "Email".
14. **Medical Declaration List**: Select one or more medical conditions, such as "Blood Pressure", "Diabetes", or "Heart Disease".
15. **Employee Checklist List**: Select one or more options for your employee information, such as "Name", "Employee ID", or "Email".
16. **Visitor Checklist List**: Select one or more options for visitor information, such as "Name", "Contact Number", or "Email".
17. **Visitor History List**: Select one or more options for visitor history, such as "Contact Person", "Medical Condition", or "Recently Treated Doctor".
