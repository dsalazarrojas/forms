# Paternity Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paternity Leave Application Form is a tool for employees to submit a formal application for paternity leave. This form is designed to gather the necessary information required to process your leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Employee Information**: Provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information as prompted.
2. **Enter Paternity Information**: Provide details about your paternity leave, including dates and any other relevant information.
3. **Select Leave Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your requested leave period.
4. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your supervisor or manager's contact details, if applicable.
5. **Add Comments (optional)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please add them here.
6. **Confirm**: Confirm that you're applying for paternity leave.
7. **Select Submit Option**: Confirm that you've reviewed and submitted your application.
8. **Review and Approve**: Review and approve your application, if approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (required): Enter your name and any other relevant information to help us process your application.
- **Paternity Information** (required): Enter details about your paternity leave, including dates and any other relevant information.
- **Leave Dates** (required): Enter the start and end dates of your requested leave period.
- **Contact Information** (required): Enter your supervisor or manager's contact details, if applicable.
- **Comments (optional)**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please add them here.
- **Confirm** (required): Confirm that you're applying for paternity leave.
- **Submit** (required): Confirm that you've reviewed and submitted your application.
- **Review and Approve** (not required, but recommended for approval): Review and approve your application, if approved.
