# New Agent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Agent Information Form is designed to collect and store information about new or updated agents in the organization. This form is meant to be used once for each new or updated agent, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data is recorded for reference and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, ensure you have all the necessary information ready to fill in the form fields.
2. Start by filling in the first name and last name fields with the new agent's personal details.
3. Proceed to the job title fields, where you can enter the agent's current or previous job title(s).
4. For historical work information, you can select the option that best describes the agent's work history or fill in the corresponding text fields.
5. Enter the date and time of joining or leaving (if applicable) for the agent's employment history.
6. Provide the agent's contact details, including their email, phone, and work phone (if applicable).
7. Lastly, input the agent's address and any additional job title(s) as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the first name of the new agent.
* **last_name** (Text, Optional): Enter the last name of the new agent.
* **job_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the current or previous job title of the new agent.
* **work_history** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the new agent's work history.
* **interests** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about the new agent's interests.
* **job_history_1** (Select One, Optional): Choose the best description of the new agent's job history (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **work_history_2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose multiple descriptions of the new agent's job history (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): Enter the new agent's date of birth.
* **time_of_birth** (Time, Optional): Enter the new agent's time of birth.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter the new agent's email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the new agent's phone number.
* **work_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the new agent's work phone number.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter the new agent's work address.
* **job_title_1** (Text, Optional): Enter an additional job title of the new agent.
* **job_title_2** (Text, Optional): Enter another additional job title of the new agent.
* **job_title_3** (Text, Optional): Enter yet another additional job title of the new agent.
* **date_of_joining** (Date, Optional): Enter the new agent's date of joining.
* **time_of_joining** (Time, Optional): Enter the new agent's time of joining.
* **job_title_4** (Text, Optional): Enter another additional job title of the new agent.
* **job_title_5** (Text, Optional): Enter yet another additional job title of the new agent.
* **date_of_leave** (Date, Optional): Enter the new agent's date of leaving.
* **time_of_leave** (Time, Optional): Enter the new agent's time of leaving.
* **job_title_6** (Text, Optional): Enter an additional job title of the new agent.
* **job_title_7** (Text, Optional): Enter the last additional job title of the new agent.
