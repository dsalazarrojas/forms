# Staff Induction - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from new staff members as part of the onboarding process. It's a mandatory form that helps ensure all necessary details are captured accurately and securely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting.
2. Select the relevant sections for your emergency contact details.
3. Fill in the form accurately and honestly.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your work email address.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your personal phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Emergency Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant emergency contact details.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact number for your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact address for your emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any other emergency contact details.
- **Other Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): Enter your relationship with your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact number for your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact address for your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Other Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any other emergency contact details.
- **Emergency Contact Other Relationship Employer** (Text, Optional): Enter your relationship with your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Employer Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact address for your other emergency contact's employer.
- **Emergency Contact Other Phone 2** (Text, Optional): Enter another contact number for your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Other Email 2** (Email, Optional): Enter another email address for your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Other Address 2** (Text, Optional): Enter another contact address for your emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Other Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any further emergency contact details.
- **Emergency Contact Other Relationship Employer 2** (Text, Optional): Enter your relationship with your other emergency contact.
- **Other Emergency Contact Employer Address 2** (Text, Optional): Enter another contact address for your other emergency contact's employer.

Note: The above list of fields is exhaustive and includes all the options provided in the input schema. However, for actual usage, you can filter out unnecessary fields according to the specific use cases and requirements.
