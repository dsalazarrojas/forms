# Acknowledgment Of Policy Handbook - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by employees to acknowledge that they have received and understood the company's policy handbook. By completing this form, employees are confirming that they are aware of their responsibilities and expectations as per the company's policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Full Name in the space provided.
2. Select your Department from the options provided.
3. Enter the Date Handbook Received.
4. Select the Handbook Version you are acknowledging.
5. In the "Ack Acknowledgment Statements" field, please check each item below.
6. Confirm that you have read and understood the policies.
7. Confirm that you understand the at-will nature of your employment.
8. Indicate your agreement to maintain company confidentiality.
9. Sign your name electronically by typing it as per the "Employee Electronic Signature" field.
10. Enter the Date Signed.
11. Enter your Employee Email for confirmation purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Date Handbook Received** (`date_handbook_received`, `date`, required): Enter the date you received the policy handbook.
* **Handbook Version** (`handbook_version`, `select_one`, required): Select the version of the policy handbook you are acknowledging.
* **Ack Acknowledgment Statements** (`ack_header`, `note`, required): Please check each item below as it applies to you.
*   *   Please confirm that you have read and understood the policies outlined in the handbook.
*   *   Please indicate that you understand the at-will nature of your employment.
*   *   Please confirm that you are aware that this acknowledgment is a condition of continued employment.
*   *   Please confirm that you understand that any disputes regarding this acknowledgment will be resolved through established company procedures.
* **I confirm I have read and understood the policies** (`read_and_understood`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understood the policies.
* **I understand the at-will nature of my employment** (`at_will_employment_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the at-will nature of your employment.
* **I agree to maintain company confidentiality** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your agreement to maintain company confidentiality.
* **Employee Electronic Signature** (`employee_electronic_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as per your electronic signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are signing this document.
* **Employee Email for Confirmation** (`employee_email_for_confirmation`, `email`, required): Enter your employee email for confirmation purposes.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have read and understood the policy handbook before completing this form. This form is a legal document and requires careful attention to ensure accuracy. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
