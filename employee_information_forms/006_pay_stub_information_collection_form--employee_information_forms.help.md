# Pay Stub Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect accurate and complete information about your pay stubs. The information collected will help ensure that your payments are processed correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out each field with the relevant information, making sure to provide accurate and complete details.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your HR or payroll representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Unit** (`business_unit`, text, optional): Enter the current business unit you are a part of.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, optional): Enter the department name you are currently working in.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your current job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your current job (if applicable).
* **Job Title Change** (`job_title_change`, text, optional): If you have changed your job title, enter the new title.
* **Job Change Date** (`job_change_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you changed your job title (if applicable).
* **Business Unit Change** (`business_unit_change`, text, optional): If you have changed your business unit, enter the new unit.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your current manager's name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, date, optional): Enter the pay period for which you are providing information.
* **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of your pay (e.g., bi-weekly, semi-monthly, etc.).
* **Pay Method** (`pay_method`, text, optional): Enter the method by which you receive payment (e.g., direct deposit, check, etc.).

Note: Some fields may not be required, but it's best to fill them out if you have the relevant information available to ensure accurate and complete processing of your pay stub. If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your HR or payroll representative for clarification.
