# New Hire Employment Package Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Employment Package Form is used to collect and document essential information about a new employee's employment status, job title, supervisor, and other relevant details. This form helps to ensure that all necessary records are accurately and thoroughly completed, providing a comprehensive new hire package for the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of when the employee's employment began.
2. Enter the employee's job title.
3. Choose their employment status (Full-Time or Part-Time).
4. Enter the date of when the employee's employment will end.
5. Enter the date of when the employee will start working.
6. Provide the name of their supervisor.
7. Enter their supervisor's title.
8. Enter their supervisor's department.
9. Briefly describe their job duties and responsibilities.
10. Specify their job category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of when the employee's employment began.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's job title.
* **employment_status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose their employment status (Full-Time or Part-Time).
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of when the employee's employment will end.
* **work_start_date** (`work_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of when the employee will start working.
* **supervisor** (`supervisor`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of their supervisor.
* **supervisor_title** (`supervisor_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter their supervisor's title.
* **supervisor_department** (`supervisor_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter their supervisor's department.
* **job_description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe their job duties and responsibilities.
* **job_category** (`job_category`, `text`, required: false): Specify their job category.
