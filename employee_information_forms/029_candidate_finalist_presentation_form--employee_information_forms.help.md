# Candidate Finalist Presentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candidate Finalist Presentation Form is used by the recruitment team to evaluate and present the top candidates to the hiring organization. This form is a comprehensive guide to provide an in-depth analysis of the candidate's qualifications, fit, and potential as a future team member. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the client receives a well-rounded understanding of the candidate's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Introduce the top candidate in the Finalist Overview section.
2. Fill out the candidate's information in the Candidate Full Name, Job Title for Presentation, and Client Name fields.
3. Provide a summary of the candidate's relevant experience in the Summary of Relevant Experience field.
4. List the key technical and soft skills in the Key Technical Competencies and Key Soft Skills fields.
5. Summarize the candidate's educational background in the Educational Background Summary field.
6. Present the business case for the candidate in the Unique Value Proposition field.
7. Assess the candidate's alignment with the client's culture in the Alignment with Client Culture field.
8. Determine the candidate's expected salary and the client's budgeted salary in the Candidate's Expected Salary and Client's Budgeted Salary fields.
9. Explain any salary variance in the Rationale for Salary Variance field.
10. Confirm the candidate's relocation status and notice period in the Relocation Required and Notice Period fields.
11. Provide a final recommendation for the candidate in the Recruiter's Final Assessment field.
12. Determine the priority level of the candidate's presentation in the Priority Level field.
13. Set the submission confirmation date in the Submission Confirmation Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Finalist Overview**: A brief introduction to the candidate.
* **Candidate Full Name**: The candidate's full name.
* **Job Title for Presentation**: The job title being filled by the candidate.
* **Client Name**: The name of the hiring organization.
* **Presenting Recruiter or Agent Name**: The name of the person presenting the candidate.
* **Date of Presentation**: The date of the presentation.
* **Summary of Relevant Experience**: A brief summary of the candidate's relevant experience.
* **Key Technical Competencies**: The most important technical skills and qualifications.
* **Key Soft Skills**: The most important soft skills and qualifications.
* **Educational Background Summary**: A summary of the candidate's educational background.
* **Unique Value Proposition**: A compelling business case for the candidate.
* **Alignment with Client Culture**: How well the candidate fits with the client's culture.
* **Candidate's Expected Salary**: The candidate's expected salary.
* **Client's Budgeted Salary**: The client's budgeted salary.
* **Rationale for Salary Variance**: A justification for any salary variance.
* **Relocation Required**: Whether the candidate requires relocation.
* **Notice Period**: The notice period for the candidate.
* **Recruiter's Final Assessment**: A final recommendation for the candidate.
* **Priority Level**: The priority level of the candidate's presentation.
* **Submission Confirmation Date**: The date of submission.
