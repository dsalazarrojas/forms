# Parental Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parental Leave Time Off Form" is used to request and manage parental leave for employees. It is designed to collect necessary information from both the employee and their manager for internal HR or departmental use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all required information, including date of birth, job title, and department ID.
2. Select the expected start and end dates for the leave.
3. Provide a reason for the leave.
4. Choose the manager approval status.
5. Enter your employee and manager details.
6. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requesting Parental Leave** (`parental_leave_time_off_form`): This is the form title.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`): Enter the date you plan to start the leave in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Expected End Date** (`expected_end_date`): Enter the date you plan to end the leave in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`): Provide a brief explanation for the leave request.
- **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`): Select the approval status from Approved, Denied, or Pending.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name.
- **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email.
- **Department Head** (`department_head`): Enter the name of your department head.
- **Department** (`department`): Enter your department name.
- **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter your manager's phone number.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name.
- **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter your email.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your job title.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): Enter your reporting manager's name.
- **Department ID** (`department_id`): Enter your department ID.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID.
- **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`): If applicable, enter the duration of your leave in the format mm/dd/yyyy - mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Leave Type** (`leave_type`): Select the type of leave you are applying for.
- **Leave Frequency** (`leave_frequency`): If applicable, enter the frequency of your leave (e.g., weekly, biweekly, etc.).
- **Leave Reason** (`leave_reason`): Provide a detailed explanation for the leave request.
- **Employee Notes** (`employee_notes`): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your leave request.
- **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`): Enter any notes or comments from your manager regarding the leave request.
- **Approval Status** (`approval_status`): Select the approval status from Approved, Denied, or Pending.
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: This guide is for internal use and should not be shared with external parties.
