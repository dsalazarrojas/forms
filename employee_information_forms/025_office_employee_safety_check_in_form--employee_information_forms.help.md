# Office Employee Safety Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from office employees to track their presence and provide emergency contact information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, choose your onsite status, enter any comments or notes about your current status, enter the date and time of your check-in, and provide your work email and phone number.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Safety Check In**: Enter any general comments or notes about your current status.
* **Onsite Status**: Choose if you are physically present in the office or not.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your current status.
* **Date of Check-in**: Enter the date you are physically present in the office (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time of Check-in**: Enter the time you are physically present in the office (Format: 12-hour clock, HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Email**: Enter your work email address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number for emergency contact purposes.
