# Education Verification Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's educational background and verify certain aspects of their credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
*   Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
*   Enter your graduation date in the "Graduation Date" field in the format "Month/Day/Year".
*   Select your degree earned from the options in the "Degree" field.
*   Enter your field of study in the "Field of Study" field.
*   Enter your GPA in the "GPA" field.
*   If you are requesting a transcript, check the "Transcript Request" field.
*   If you have sent a transcript, check the "Transcript Sent" field.
*   If you are requesting a diploma, select all relevant options from the "Diploma Requested" field.
*   If you have sent a diploma, select all relevant options from the "Diploma Sent" field.
*   Select the status of your degree verification from the options in the "Degree Verification" field.
*   If applicable, enter the name of the entity that verified your degree in the "Verified by" field.
*   Finally, enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
*   Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
*   **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your graduation.
*   **Degree** (`degree`, select_one, required): Select your degree from the options.
*   **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, text, optional): Enter your field of study.
*   **GPA** (`gpa`, number, required): Enter your GPA.
*   **Transcript Request** (`transcript_request`, note, optional): If you need a transcript, indicate this here.
*   **Transcript Sent** (`transcript_sent`, select_one, required): Check if you have sent a transcript.
*   **Diploma Requested** (`diploma_request`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options.
*   **Diploma Sent** (`diploma_sent`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant options.
*   **Degree Verification** (`degree_verification`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your degree verification.
*   **Verified by** (`verified_by`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the entity that verified your degree.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to indicate completion.
