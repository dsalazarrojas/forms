# Health Check Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Check Absence Form is used to provide information about an employee's absence from work due to health reasons. It helps the management team keep track of absences and facilitate the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your absence details.
- Select the correct absence status (Sick, Other, etc.).
- Provide a short description of your illness or absence reason.
- Enter the start and end dates of your absence.
- Enter the duration of your absence.
- Choose your department and team leader from the options provided.
- Ensure that the manager approval status is set to the correct level of approval required.
- Fill out any additional comments or comments from the manager.
- Ensure that the form is submitted with the correct submitted date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (1)**: Enter your employee ID and other relevant details.
* **Absence Status (2)**: Select the reason for your absence (Sick, Other, etc.).
* **Illness Details (3)**: Provide a short description of your illness or absence reason.
* **Start Date (4)**: Enter the date your absence started.
* **End Date (5)**: Enter the date your absence ended.
* **Absence Duration (6)**: Enter the number of days you were absent.
* **Reason For Absence (7)**: Enter a detailed reason for your absence.
* **Employee ID (8)**: Select your employee ID.
* **Contact Number (9)**: Enter your contact number.
* **Absence Details (10)**: Provide any additional details about your absence.
* **Manager Approval (11)**: Select the level of manager approval required (Not Required, Required, Recommended).
* **Approval Comments (12)**: Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Form Submitted Date (13)**: The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (14)**: The person who submitted the form.
* **Submission Status (15)**: The current status of your submission (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Form Status (16)**: The status of your form (In Progress, Completed, Pending).
* **Comments (17)**: Enter any additional comments.
* **Employee Name (18)**: Enter your name.
* **Department (19)**: Select your department.
* **Manager Name (20)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Team Leader (21)**: Select your team leader.
* **Location (22)**: Enter your location.
* **Reporting Manager (23)**: Enter your reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Email (24)**: Enter your reporting manager's email.
* **Reporting Manager Phone (25)**: Enter your reporting manager's phone number.
