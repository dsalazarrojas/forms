# Employment Verification Form For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify employment information for a mortgage application. It is used by mortgage lenders to confirm an applicant's employment status with a specific employer and to verify details about their job and employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. Enter the employer's name as it is spelled on their business documents.
3. Enter the job title and start and end dates of employment.
4. Enter the employer's address, phone number, and email address.
5. Review and ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Employers Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the employer's name as it is spelled on their business documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the job title and a brief description of the role.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee started working with the employer.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee ended their employment with the employer (if applicable).
* **Length Of Service** (`length_of_service`, text, optional): Enter the employee's length of service with the employer (e.g., "X years").
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, text, optional): Enter the number of years the employee has worked with the employer.
* **Employers Address** (`employer_address`, text, required): Enter the employer's business address.
* **Employers Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, required): Enter the employer's phone number.
* **Employers Email** (`employer_email`, text, required): Enter the employer's email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If the employee has changed employers, please provide details about their employment history.
* If the employee has any gaps in employment, please provide an explanation.
