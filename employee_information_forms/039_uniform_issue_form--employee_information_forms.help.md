# Uniform Issue Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any issues related to employee uniforms, such as missing or damaged items, discrepancies with the uniform type or quantity, or other problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the field provided.
2. Specify your position in the company.
3. Enter the date of the issue.
4. Report the number of uniforms or equipment affected by the issue.
5. Select the type of uniform or equipment that is causing the problem.
6. If multiple types of equipment are involved, select all relevant options.
7. Enter the serial number of the affected equipment (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or in our records.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your current position within the company.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you first noticed the issue or when it occurred.
* **Issue Quantity** (`issue_quantity`, number, optional): Report the number of uniforms or equipment that are affected by the issue.
* **Uniform Type** (`uniform_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of uniform that is causing the problem from the options provided.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, optional): If multiple types of equipment are involved, select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the affected equipment (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide as much detail as possible to help us understand the issue.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is for reporting issues related to uniforms and equipment only. If you have a concern about another company resource, please use the appropriate form for that specific item.
