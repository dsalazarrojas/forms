# Weekly Timesheet Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to your timesheet entries for a specific period. Please submit this form if you need to make any adjustments to your hours worked or leave taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your correct employee name from the employee information field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the period you're requesting adjustments for.
3. Enter the number of hours you're requesting to adjust.
4. Optionally, provide a brief reason for the adjustment.
5. Optionally, add any additional comments about the submission.
6. Select the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, select_one, required): Select your correct name from the list to associate this submission with your timesheet.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the period you're requesting adjustments for.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the period you're requesting adjustments for.
* **Hours Adjustment** (`hours_adjustment`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you're requesting to adjust.
* **Reason for Adjustment** (`reason_for_adjustment`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the adjustment request, if necessary.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or context about your submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select the person submitting the form on your behalf, if applicable.
