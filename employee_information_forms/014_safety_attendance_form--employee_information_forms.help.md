# Safety Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendance information for employee safety meetings. It helps supervisors to verify that employees have received critical safety information and are aware of any hazards in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide meeting information in the "Meeting Info" page.
2. Indicate whether you attended the safety meeting in the "Safety Meeting" field.
3. Specify if you attended other safety meetings in the "Attend" field.
4. Sign the form with your "Employee Signature" and "Supervisor Signature" if required.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Info** (`meeting_info`, note, not required): Enter a brief description of the meeting, including the date, time, and location.
* **Safety Meeting** (`safety_meeting`, select_one, not required): Choose "Yes" to indicate that you attended the safety meeting.
* **Attend** (`attended`, select_multiple, not required): Select all safety meetings you have attended, separated by commas (e.g., "Yes, Yes, No").
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, not required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the safety information provided.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, not required): Sign your name as the supervisor to verify that you have delivered the safety information to the employee.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the meeting in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the meeting in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Be sure to complete the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact your supervisor.
