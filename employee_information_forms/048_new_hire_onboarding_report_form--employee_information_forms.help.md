# New Hire Onboarding Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Report Form is a tool used to collect information about new employees and help them get started with their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager" field.
5. Enter your date of hire in the "Date Hired" field.
6. Select your job title from the "Job Title" field.
7. Select your department from the "Department" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Select your job location from the "Job Location" field.
11. Select your shift schedule from the "Shift" field.
12. If applicable, enter your termination date in the "Termination Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Department**: Select your department (e.g. HR, Finance, Sales, IT).
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title (e.g. Account Executive, Account Manager, Account Specialist).
* **Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Date Hired**: Enter your date of hire.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the list of options.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Job Location**: Select your job location (e.g. Remote, Onsite, Hybrid).
* **Shift**: Select your shift schedule (e.g. Yes, No).
* **Termination Date**: If you are leaving the company, enter your termination date.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask your HR representative for clarification.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
