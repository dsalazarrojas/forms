# Maternity Leave Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maternity Leave Absence Form is used to notify the company of an employee's maternity leave. This form requires information from employees, managers, and supervisors to ensure a smooth process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee start date.
2. Enter the date you expect to return to work.
3. Enter your employee ID.
4. Provide your manager's name and contact information.
5. Select whether your manager has approved your maternity leave.
6. Provide your supervisor's name and contact information.
7. Select whether your supervisor has approved your maternity leave.
8. Enter the reason for your leave.
9. Enter your company address and your personal address.
10. Enter your contact number and email address.
11. Enter your company name.
12. Enter your name and department ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Start Date**: Enter the date you started working for the company.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you expect to return to work after your maternity leave.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct manager.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's contact number.
* **Manager Approval**: Select 'Yes' if your manager has approved your maternity leave.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's contact number.
* **Supervisor Approval**: Select 'Yes' if your supervisor has approved your maternity leave.
* **Leave Reason**: Enter the reason for your maternity leave.
* **Company Address**: Enter the company's address.
* **Employee Address**: Enter your personal address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Company Name**: Enter the company's name.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name.
* **Department ID**: Enter your department ID.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Contact Number**: Enter your manager's contact number.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Contact Number**: Enter your supervisor's contact number.

Note: Ensure you have all the necessary approvals before submitting this form.
