# News Anchor Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The News Anchor Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information about new hires, including their job titles, departments, and contact details. This information is crucial for creating employee profiles, assigning tasks, and facilitating communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **New Hire Details** section with the relevant information about the new hire.
2. Enter the employee's **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** in the corresponding fields.
3. Select the **Department** and **Job Title** that match the new hire's role.
4. Add any relevant **Manager Details**, such as their name and title.
5. Select whether the new hire has an **Emergency Contact** and provide their contact information.
6. Enter the **Job Description** and **Company Location** to help with communication and collaboration.
7. Add any additional **Employee Files**, such as documents or attachments, that are relevant to the new hire.
8. Capture the new hire's **Employee Profile Picture** and **Signature**.
9. Finally, review the form and ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**: Enter a brief description of the new hire's position and role in the organization.
* **employee_name**: Enter the new hire's name.
* **employee_email**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **employee_phone**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **hire_date**: Enter the date the new hire started working.
* **employee_files**: Attach any relevant employee files or attachments.
* **e_signature**: Capture the new hire's signature.
* **New Hire Details Files**: Attach any additional information about the new hire, such as ID documents.
* **employee_profile_picture**: Upload a profile picture of the new hire.
* **emergency_contact**: Select whether the new hire has an emergency contact person.
* **job_description**: Enter a brief job description of the new hire's role.
* **company_location**: Enter the location of the company the new hire is working for.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the new hire's manager.
* **manager_title**: Enter the title of the new hire's manager.
* **department**: Select whether the new hire is part of a team or department.
* **team_name**: Enter the name of the team the new hire is part of.
* **employee_id**: Enter the new hire's employee ID number.
* **job_id**: Enter the new hire's job ID number.
* **employee_type**: Select the type of employee (e.g., full-time, part-time).
* **job_title**: Enter the new hire's job title.

## Tips

* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields to ensure easy understanding.
* Attach relevant employee files to help with onboarding and communication.
