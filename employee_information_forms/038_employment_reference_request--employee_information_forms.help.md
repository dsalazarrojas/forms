# employment_reference_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from employees for internal reference purposes. It collects essential details about an employee's employment status, job title, and manager's contact information. The collected data will be used to facilitate internal processes and communications within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Provide your company name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Select your current employment status from the "Employment Status" dropdown menu.
5. If applicable, enter your job start date in the "Job Start Date" field.
6. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
7. Enter your manager's title in the "Manager Title" field.
8. Enter your manager's phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
9. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
10. Enter your manager's address in the "Manager Address" field.
11. Provide a brief reason for the request in the "Request Reason" field (if required).
12. Select the manager who will be handling your reference in the "Manager Reference" dropdown menu.
13. If applicable, select any additional references who will be involved in the process (in the "Other Reference" field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as described in your job description.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your current employment status from the available options:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive
	+ Terminated
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's job title as described in their job description.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's address as it appears on official documents.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for the reference request (if required).
* **Manager Reference** (`manager_reference`, select_one, required): Select the manager who will be handling your reference:
	+ Manager 1
	+ Manager 2
	+ Manager 3
* **Other Reference** (`other_reference`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional references who will be involved in the process:
	+ Reference 1
	+ Reference 2
	+ Reference 3

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to facilitate efficient processing of your reference request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR department for assistance.
* This form is intended for internal use only; do not share it with external parties.
