# Government Employee Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Employee Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information about new government employees to facilitate their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Provide accurate and complete information about yourself and your emergency contacts.
3. Select the relevant options for your department, position, job title, job type, job status, work location, and manager details.
4. Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address.
5. Review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**: Enter your name, department, and position.
	+ This field is used to gather general information about the new hire.
* **Hire Name**: Enter the new hire's name.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's name for official purposes.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list.
	+ This field helps identify the department the new hire will be working in.
* **Position**: Select your position from the list.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's job title.
* **Date of Hire**: Enter the date you started working.
	+ This field is used to track the date of hire for official purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's job title.
* **Manager**: Select your manager from the list.
	+ This field helps identify the new hire's manager.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you hold (Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
	+ This field is used to categorize the new hire's job type.
* **Job Status**: Select the status of your job (Active, Inactive).
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's job status.
* **Work Location**: Select the location where you work (Remote, On-site).
	+ This field helps identify the new hire's work location.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact (Spouse, Parent, Child).
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact details.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the type of relationship you have with the emergency contact (Spouse, Parent, Child).
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email Address**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Note**: Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
	+ This field is used to record any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Manager Contact**: Select the type of contact you have with the manager (Primary, Secondary).
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's manager's contact details.
* **Manager Contact Name**: Enter the manager's name.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's manager's name.
* **Manager Contact Phone**: Enter the manager's phone number.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's manager's phone number.
* **Manager Contact Email**: Enter the manager's email address.
	+ This field is used to record the new hire's manager's email address.
* **Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes about the manager.
	+ This field is used to record any additional information about the manager.
* **Department Head**: Select the department head from the list.
	+ This field helps identify the new hire's department head.
