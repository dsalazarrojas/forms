# New Hire Worker Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and employment-related information from new hires to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "new-hire-first-name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "new-hire-last-name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "new-hire-email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "new-hire-phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "new-hire-date-of-birth" field.
6. Enter your work address in the "new-hire-work-address" field.
7. Select your employment type in the "new-hire-employment-type" field.
8. If applicable, enter your termination date in the "new-hire-termination-date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Worker Liability Waiver Form**: This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page.
* **new-hire-first-name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **new-hire-last-name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **new-hire-email**: Enter your email address that you will use for company communications.
* **new-hire-phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **new-hire-date-of-birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **new-hire-work-address**: Enter your work address where you will be working.
* **new-hire-employment-type**: Select your employment type (e.g. full-time, part-time, contract, etc.).
* **new-hire-termination-date**: Enter the date of your termination (if applicable). If not applicable, leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* This form should only be filled out once when the worker is initially onboarded.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
