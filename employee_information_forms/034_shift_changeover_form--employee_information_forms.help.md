# Shift Changeover Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shift Changeover Form is used to record the shift changeover details for employees. This form is used to update the shift schedule and ensure a smooth transition of shifts between employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the correct shift to swap to from the "Confirm Shift" dropdown menu.
3. Confirm the shift changeover by selecting "Yes" from the "Confirm Swap" dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter the email and phone number of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
6. Enter the start and end times of the shift to be handed over.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the shift to be handed over.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (id: 1, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee making the shift changeover.
* **Confirm Shift** (id: 2, select_one, optional): Select the shift you want to swap to from the dropdown menu. Options: Shift 1, Shift 2, Shift 3.
* **Swap To Shift** (id: 3, select_one, optional): Select the shift you want to swap to from the dropdown menu. Options: Shift 1, Shift 2, Shift 3.
* **Confirm Swap** (id: 4, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm the shift changeover, or "No" to cancel.
* **Notes** (id: 5, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the shift changeover.
* **Email** (id: 6, email, optional): Enter the email of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
* **Phone** (id: 7, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
* **Start Time** (id: 8, time, optional): Enter the start time of the shift to be handed over.
* **End Time** (id: 9, time, optional): Enter the end time of the shift to be handed over.
* **Start Time 2** (id: 10, time, optional): Enter the start time of the shift to be handed over (if applicable).
* **End Time 2** (id: 11, time, optional): Enter the end time of the shift to be handed over (if applicable).
* **Start Time 3** (id: 12, time, optional): Enter the start time of the shift to be handed over (if applicable).
* **End Time 3** (id: 13, time, optional): Enter the end time of the shift to be handed over (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (id: 14, date, optional): Enter the start date of the shift to be handed over.
* **End Date** (id: 15, date, optional): Enter the end date of the shift to be handed over.
* **Assigned Employee** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
* **Assigned Employee Email** (id: 17, email, optional): Enter the email of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
* **Assigned Employee Phone** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee assigned to take over the shift.
* **Assigned Employee Start Time** (id: 19, time, optional): Enter the start time of the shift for the assigned employee.
* **Assigned Employee End Time** (id: 20, time, optional): Enter the end time of the shift for the assigned employee.
* **Assigned Employee Start Date** (id: 21, date, optional): Enter the start date of the shift for the assigned employee.
* **Assigned Employee End Date** (id: 22, date, optional): Enter the end date of the shift for the assigned employee.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are not required fields, but it's good practice to fill them in whenever possible to ensure accurate and complete information.
