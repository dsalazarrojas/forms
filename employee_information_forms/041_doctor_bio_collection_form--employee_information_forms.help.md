# Doctor Bio Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a doctor's personal details, medical credentials, and professional history for internal use.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information (1)**: Enter your personal details.
* **Bio (2)**: Write a short description of your medical background and experience.
* **Specialties (3)**: Select the medical specialties you are certified in.
* **Contact Information (4)**: Enter your contact details, such as email and phone number.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth (7)**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth (8)**: Enter your time of birth.
* **Medical School (9)**: Enter the name of your medical school.
* **Graduation Year (10)**: Enter the year you graduated from medical school.
* **Specialty (11)**: Select your primary specialty.
* **Medical License (12)**: Select your medical license status.
* **Medical Board (13)**: Select the board that issued your medical license.
* **Medical School Attended (14)**: Indicate if you have attended multiple medical schools.
* **Medical License State (15)**: Enter the state where your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Number (16)**: Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Issued (17)**: Enter when your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Expires (18)**: Enter when your medical license expires.
* **Medical License Renewed (19)**: Enter when your medical license was renewed.
* **Medical License Status (20)**: Enter your medical license status.
* **Medical License Issued By (21)**: Enter who issued your medical license.
* **Medical License Issued State (22)**: Enter the state where your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Issued Date (23)**: Enter when your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Issued Country (24)**: Enter the country where your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Renewal Info (25)**: Enter any renewal information related to your medical license.
* **Address (26)**: Enter your address.
* **Address State (27)**: Enter the state of your address.
* **Address City (28)**: Enter the city of your address.
* **Address Country (29)**: Enter the country of your address.
