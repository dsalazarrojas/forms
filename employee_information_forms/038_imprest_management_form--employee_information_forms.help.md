# imprest_management_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information related to account management, including account details, status, and approval processes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the provided guidelines and instructions before starting to fill out the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to access and modify account information.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely, paying attention to required fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Number** (`account_number`, `number`, required): Enter the unique account number assigned to the account.
* **Account Name** (`account_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name associated with the account.
* **Account Status** (`account_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the account (active, inactive, or pending).
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of account (checking, savings, or cash).
* **Account Balance** (`account_balance`, `number`, required): Enter the current balance of the account.
* **Account Currency** (`account_currency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the currency associated with the account (USD, CAD, or EUR).
* **Account Notes** (`account_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the account.
* **Account Date** (`account_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date associated with the account.
* **Account Time** (`account_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time associated with the account (if applicable).
* **Account Email** (`account_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with the account.
* **Account Phone** (`account_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number associated with the account.
* **Account Comments** (`account_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or notes related to the account.
* **Account Manager** (`account_manager`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the account has a manager (yes or no).
* **Account Approver** (`account_approver`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the account has an approver (yes or no).
* **Account Approver Email** (`account_approver_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the account approver.
* **Account Approver Phone** (`account_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the account approver.
* **Account Comments** (`account_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes related to the account approver.
* **Account Approver Comments** (`account_approver_comments`, `text`, required): Enter comments or notes from the account approver.
* **Account Approval Status** (`account_approval_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current approval status of the account (approved, denied, or pending).
* **Account Remarks** (`account_remarks`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments related to the account.
* **Account Remarks Comments** (`account_remarks_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter comments or notes from the account approver for the remarks.
* **Account Remarks Approval Status** (`account_remarks_approval_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current approval status of the remarks (approved, denied, or pending).
* **Account Remarks Approver** (`account_remarks_approver`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the account remarks have an approver (yes or no).
* **Account Remarks Approver Phone** (`account_remarks_approver_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the account remarks approver.
* **Account Remarks Approver Email** (`account_remarks_approver_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the account remarks approver.
