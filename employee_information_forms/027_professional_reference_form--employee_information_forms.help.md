# professional_reference_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The professional reference form is used to gather information about an employee's professional experience and references. This form will help us to verify the employee's work history and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Make sure to include your current job title and contact information.
3. If you have a professional reference, please indicate whether you have one (yes) or not (no).
4. If you have a professional reference, provide their contact information and type of reference.
5. If you have worked with a professional reference before, please provide their years of service and a brief description of your experience with them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title or most recent job title.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your up-to-date contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Professional Reference**: Check 'Yes' if you have a professional reference, 'No' otherwise.
* **Type of Professional Reference**: Select the type of professional reference you have (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4).
* **Years of Service**: Enter the years you have worked with your professional reference. If you have worked with them for less than a year, enter 0.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your experience with your professional reference.
* **Reference Link**: Enter any online link to your professional reference's website or profile.
* **Professional Reference Link**: Enter any link associated with your professional reference (e.g. LinkedIn profile).
* **Professional Reference Email**: Enter the email address of your professional reference.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
