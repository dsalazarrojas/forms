# Trainee Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from trainees for the purpose of establishing communication and sending relevant updates or notifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information from the "contact_information" dropdown menu to confirm if the trainee would like to receive contact information.
2. Fill in the trainee's contact details in the "contact_details" field.
3. Enter the trainee's address in the "address" field.
4. Provide the trainee's city in the "city" field.
5. Enter the trainee's country in the "country" field.
6. Enter the trainee's zip code in the "zip" field.
7. Enter the trainee's state in the "state" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to confirm if the trainee would like to receive contact information.
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate that you would like to receive contact information, or "No" if you do not.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter the trainee's contact details, such as their email address, phone number, or other relevant information.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the trainee's physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter the city where the trainee is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter the trainee's country of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter the trainee's zip code.
* **State** (`state`, text, required: true):
	+ Enter the trainee's state of residence.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the trainee.
