# Employee Raise Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Raise Form is used to request a salary increase for an employee. This form helps gather the necessary information for the review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your job title.
4. Enter your department.
5. Enter the amount of the salary increase you are requesting.
6. Provide reasons for the raise.
7. Enter the name of your manager.
8. Enter the name of your supervisor.
9. Enter the date of the request.
10. Choose how your manager will respond to the request (Approve, Decline, or Hold).
11. Enter any additional comments if necessary.
12. Have your manager, supervisor, or department head sign below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Salary Amount** (`salary_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are requesting as a salary increase.
* **Reasons for Raise** (`reasons_for_raise`, note, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the raise.
* **Manager's Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Supervisor's Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor's name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Date of Request** (`date_of_request`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Manager's Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): Choose how your manager will respond to the request.
* **Reason for Denial** (`reason_for_denial`, text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for denial.
* **Employee Response** (`employee_response`, text, required): Enter a response to the manager's decision.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, required): Sign below indicating you have read and agreed with the decision.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): Sign below indicating you have reviewed and approved or declined the request.
* **Department Head Signature** (`department_head_signature`, note, required): Sign below indicating you have reviewed and approved or declined the request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign below.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you sign below.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name_2`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Job Title** (`job_title_2`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Reason** (`reason`, note, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the raise.
* **Department** (`department_2`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, required): Enter your current salary.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you are requesting as a salary increase.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments.

Note: Some fields marked as optional may be required depending on the context.
