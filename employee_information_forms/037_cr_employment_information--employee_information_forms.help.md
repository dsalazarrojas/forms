# CR Employment Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CR Employment Information form is used to collect accurate and up-to-date employment information about employees in the organization. It's essential to complete this form for all current employees to ensure consistent and accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Full Name**: Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification.
2. **Employee ID Number**: Enter the employee's unique ID number assigned by the organization.
3. **Work Email Address**: Enter the employee's work email address.
4. **Work Phone Number**: Enter the employee's work phone number.
5. **Department**: Enter the employee's department or team they belong to.
6. **Job Title or Position**: Enter the employee's current job title or position.
7. **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date the employee started their employment with the organization.
8. **Direct Manager or Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the employee's direct manager or supervisor.
9. **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Temporary, or Seasonal).
10. **Primary Work Location**: Enter where the employee primarily works (Office, site, or remote).
11. **Salary Range**: Select the employee's salary level (Entry level, Mid-level, Senior level, or Executive).
12. **Benefits Enrollment Status**: Select whether the employee is enrolled in benefits (Enrolled, Not enrolled, or Pending enrollment).
13. **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the employee's emergency contact person.
14. **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee's emergency contact person.
15. **Street Address**: Enter the employee's current residential street address.
16. **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter the employee's city, state, and ZIP code.
17. **Social Security Number - Last Four Digits**: Enter the last four digits of the employee's social security number.
18. **Date of Birth**: Enter the employee's date of birth.
19. **Employment Status Notes or Comments**: Enter any relevant comments or notes about the employee's employment status.
20. **Date Information Last Updated**: This field is auto-generated and should not be manually edited.
