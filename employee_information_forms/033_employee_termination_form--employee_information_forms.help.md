# Employee Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information related to employee termination. This form is used to record details about the reasons for termination, dates, and other relevant details that will be used for administrative and HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's ID and name in the respective fields.
2. Provide a brief reason for termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
3. Specify the effective date and termination date, which should include the time of termination if necessary.
4. Calculate and enter the separation allowance and outplacement allowance, if applicable.
5. Enter the benefits end date and time if applicable.
6. Record the last day the employee worked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the terminated employee.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, required): Provide a brief reason for termination, e.g., "Employee resigned", "Terminated for performance", etc.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the termination became effective.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date of termination.
* **Termination Time** (`termination_date_time`, time, required): Enter the time of termination.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for termination, e.g., "Employee resigned", "Terminated for performance", etc.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required): Enter the number of days or weeks given as notice before termination.
* **Separation Allowance** (`separation_allowance`, number, required): Enter the separation allowance amount.
* **Outplacement Allowance** (`outplacement_allowance`, number, required): Enter the outplacement allowance amount.
* **Benefits End Date** (`benefits_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date benefits ended.
* **Benefits End Time** (`benefits_end_time`, time, required): Enter the time benefits ended.
* **Last Day Worked** (`last_day_worked`, date, required): Record the last day the employee worked.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, text, required): Enter a brief reason for termination. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the dates and times for accuracy.
* If applicable, enter the separation and outplacement allowances carefully.
* Ensure that the termination reason is clear and concise.
