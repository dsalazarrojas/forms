# Daily Construction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Construction Report form is used to record the progress and status of a construction project on a daily basis. It's an essential tool for tracking project completion, identifying areas of improvement, and facilitating communication among team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project_id, which is the unique identifier for the project being reported on.
2. Fill in the start_date and end_date fields with the dates of the day's work.
3. Provide a brief description of the location where the work was done.
4. Enter your progress report in the progress field.
5. Add any additional notes about the day's work in the notes field.
6. Enter your name and title/position in the employee_name, employee_role, and employee_phone fields.
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. If assigned a specific tool for the task, enter it in the assigned_tool field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_information_prophets** (`employee_information_prophets`, text, false): This field is not clearly labeled, but it appears to be a placeholder for employee information. Please enter any relevant details about your work, such as your role or department, if applicable.
* **project_id** (`project_id`, number, false): Enter the unique identifier for the project being reported on.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, date, false): Fill in the date you started working on the project.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, date, false): Fill in the date you finished working on the project.
* **location** (`location`, text, false): Enter a brief description of the location where the work was done.
* **progress** (`progress`, text, false): Provide a concise report of your progress on the project.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, false): Add any additional notes or comments about the day's work.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **employee_role** (`employee_role`, text, false): Enter your official job title or role.
* **employee_phone** (`employee_phone`, text, false): Enter your work phone number.
* **email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your work email address.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, false): If you were assigned a specific tool for the task, enter it here.

## Tips

* Be concise and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure you're using the correct project_id for the project being reported on.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or team lead.
