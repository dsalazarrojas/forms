# onboarding_signature_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onboarding signature collection form is used to collect information about an employee's employment details, emergency contacts, and acknowledgments. It's essential to understand the company's policies and procedures during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, gather all necessary documents and information. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (1)
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### Job Title (2)
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title as assigned by the company.

### Email (3)
* **Email**: Enter your company email address (if applicable) or your personal email address used for work-related communications.

### Job Start Date (4)
* **Job Start Date**: The date you started your job at the company.

### Job End Date (5)
* Optional field. Enter the date you expect to leave the company, if applicable.

### Manager Name (6)
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.

### Company Location (7)
* **Company Location**: Enter the address of the company's main office or location where you'll be working.

### Emergency Contact Name (8)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### Relationship (9)
* **Relationship**: Choose the relationship between you and the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone (10)
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Email (11)
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Address (12)
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.

### Relationship (13) (if not Emergency Contact)
* **Relationship**: If you do not have an emergency contact, select the relationship between you and the other contact (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Sponsor, or No Contact).

### Emergency Contact Phone (14)
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the other contact (if not an emergency contact).

### Emergency Contact Email (15)
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the other contact (if not an emergency contact).

### Policy Acknowledgments (16)
* **Policy Acknowledgments**: Read and confirm that you understand the company's policies.

### Onboarding Acknowledgments (17)
* **Onboarding Acknowledgments**: Read and confirm that you understand the onboarding process and company procedures.

### Signature (18)
* **Signature**: Confirm that you have read and understand the company's policies and onboarding documents.
 
### Onboarding Agreement (19)
* **Onboarding Agreement**: Confirm your agreement to follow company policies and onboarding procedures.
