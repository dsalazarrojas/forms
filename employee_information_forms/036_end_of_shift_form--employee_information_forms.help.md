# End Of Shift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Form is designed to gather essential information about an employee's work completed during their shift. It helps in tracking tasks, recording comments and notes, and provides a clear record of employee and supervisor details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Employee Name** in the text field provided.
2.  Provide a brief report of your activities and tasks completed during the shift in the **End Of Shift Report** text field.
3.  Indicate whether you **Completed Tasks** by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
4.  Add any additional **Comments** or **Notes** about your shift in the text area provided.
5.  Enter the **Shift Date** of the shift, using the date picker or manually entering in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6.  If you were working on **Employee Shift**, indicate whether you were on shift or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
7.  Enter the **Supervisor Name** and **Supervisor Phone** numbers if applicable.
8.  Record the **End Of Shift Time**, using the time format.
9.  Enter any additional **Notes** you'd like to share with your supervisor or management.
10. Finally, enter your **Supervisor Email** if you've been in touch with them during the shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Text)** (`employee_name`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **End Of Shift Report (Text)** (`end_of_shift_report`, optional): Provide a concise report of your activities and tasks completed during the shift.
* **Tasks Completed (Select Multiple)** (`employee_tasks_completed`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you've completed tasks, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments (Note)** (`comments`, optional): Add any comments or notes about your shift.
* **Shift Date (Date)** (`shift_date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your shift, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employee Shift (Select One)** (`employee_shift`, required/optional): Indicate whether you were on shift or not, by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options.
* **Supervisor Name (Text)** (`supervisor_name`, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor if you've been in touch with them during the shift.
* **Supervisor Phone (Text)** (`supervisor_phone`, optional): Enter the contact number of your supervisor.
* **End Of Shift Time (Time)** (`end_of_shift_time`, required/optional): Record the time you finished your shift, using the time format.
* **Notes (Note)** (`notes`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your shift.
* **Supervisor Email (Email)** (`supervisor_email`, optional): Enter the email of your supervisor if you've been in touch with them during the shift.
