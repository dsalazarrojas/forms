# Shadowing Sign Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shadowing Sign Off Form is a form used to document shadowing experiences, providing essential information such as the title, location, date, and duration of the shadowing activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have actually participated in a shadowing activity and are submitting this form for that specific experience.
2. Enter the title of the shadowing activity in the "Shadowing Experience Title" field.
3. Enter the location where the shadowing took place in the "Shadowing Location" field.
4. Enter the date of the shadowing activity in the "Shadowing Date" field. This field accepts dates in a standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. If applicable, enter the start and end times of the shadowing activity in the "Shadowing Start Time" and "Shadowing End Time" fields, respectively. These fields accept time values in a 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Enter the site or facility visited during the shadowing activity in the "Shadowing Site" field.
7. Enter the name of your supervisor or the person you shadowed in the "Shadowing Supervisor Name" field.
8. If your supervisor is in a title-bearing position, enter their title in the "Shadowing Supervisor Title" field.
9. If applicable, describe your supervisor's specialty or area of expertise in the "Shadowing Supervisor Specialty" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shadowing Experience Title** (`shadowing_experience_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description or title of the shadowing activity, for example, "Shadowing Orthopedic Surgery".
* **Shadowing Location** (`shadowing_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the shadowing activity took place, such as "ABC Hospital".
* **Shadowing Date** (`shadowing_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the shadowing activity in a standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD), such as "2022-07-01".
* **Shadowing Start Time** (`shadowing_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the shadowing activity in a 24-hour format (HH:MM), such as "08:00".
* **Shadowing End Time** (`shadowing_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the shadowing activity in a 24-hour format (HH:MM), such as "16:00".
* **Shadowing Site** (`shadowing_site`, text, required: false): Enter the site or facility visited during the shadowing activity, such as "Orthopedic Surgery Department".
* **Shadowing Supervisor Name** (`shadowing_supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your supervisor or the person you shadowed, such as "Dr. Smith".
* **Shadowing Supervisor Title** (`shadowing_supervisor_title`, text, required: false): If your supervisor is in a title-bearing position, enter their title here, such as "Orthopedic Surgeon".
* **Shadowing Supervisor Specialty** (`shadowing_shadowing_supervisor_specialty`, text, required: false): Describe your supervisor's specialty or area of expertise, such as "Pediatric Orthopedic Surgery".
