This form is used for correcting errors or discrepancies in employee time clock records. It's an internal form used to review and validate changes made to time clock entries. The form is intended to be filled out by an employee who has noticed an error in their time clock record and is requesting to have it corrected. The form is used to collect information about the original time clock entry, the corrections made, and the reason for the correction.

This guide will walk you through how to fill out the form correctly and explain each field in detail.

# Time Clock Correction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Time Clock Correction Form is used for employees to correct errors or discrepancies in their time clock records. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the corrections are properly recorded and approved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2.  Make sure to include the correct date and time for the corrected entry.
3.  Explain the reason for the correction in the "Reason for Correction" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Date** (`date`, required/optional): Please enter the date for the time clock entry you're correcting.
*   **Original Time In** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the original time you clocked in.
*   **Original Time Out** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the original time you clocked out.
*   **Corrected Time In** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the corrected time you actually clocked in.
*   **Corrected Time Out** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the corrected time you actually clocked out.
*   **Reason** (`text`, required/optional): Briefly explain the reason for the correction in this field.
*   **Employee Signature** (`note`, required/optional): Please sign and date this form to confirm that you're requesting the correction.
*   **Manager Signature** (`note`, required/optional): A manager or supervisor must sign and date this form to approve or deny the correction.
*   **Approved by Manager** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if the correction has been approved, 'No' if it has not.
*   **Time Clock Correction Form Status** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Active' to mark the form as active, 'Inactive' to mark it as inactive.
*   **Employee ID** (`number`, required/optional): Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Corrected Date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date the corrected time clock entry was made.
*   **Time Worked** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the total time you worked during the corrected entry.
*   **Corrected Time In Hours** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the corrected hours you actually worked.
*   **Corrected Time Out Hours** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the corrected hours you actually worked.
*   **Reason for Correction** (`text`, required/optional): Briefly explain the reason for the correction in this field.
*   **Corrected Hours** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the total corrected hours you actually worked.

Remember to review and double-check your filled-out form before submitting it for approval.
