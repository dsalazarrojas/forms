# staff_information_collection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "staff_information_collection_form" is used to collect and document information about staff members for HR-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields, such as employee name, job title, and supervisor name.
3. Select relevant options for status attributes, like job status and employee status.
4. Enter any other relevant details, such as email, phone number, and job family.
5. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's full name.
* **job_title** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's job title or role.
* **department** (select_one, required/optional): Select the employee's department from the options provided.
* **date_hired** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **supervisor_name** (text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's name.
* **job_title_supervisor** (text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's job title or role.
* **department_supervisor** (text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's department.
* **date_hired_supervisor** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the supervisor was hired.
* **supervisor_phone** (text, required/optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **staff_id** (number, required/optional): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **job_classification** (select_one, required/optional): Select the employee's job classification from the options provided.
* **job_classification_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom job classification if not provided.
* **department_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom department name if not provided.
* **location** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's location or office.
* **employee_status** (select_one, required/optional): Select the employee's status as Active or Inactive.
* **employee_status_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom employee status if not provided.
* **job_status** (select_one, required/optional): Select the employee's job status as Active or Inactive.
* **job_status_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom job status if not provided.
* **job_family** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's job family.
* **job_family_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom job family name if not provided.
* **employee_code** (text, required/optional): Enter the employee's code or ID.
* **employee_code_other** (text, required/optional): Enter a custom employee code name if not provided.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or access to enter sensitive data.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, consult with HR or the system administrators.
