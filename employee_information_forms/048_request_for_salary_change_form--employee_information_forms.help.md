# Request For Salary Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Request For Salary Change Form is used to request a salary change for an employee. This form is to be filled out by authorized employees to submit a request for a salary change to the department head or manager in charge of the employee's department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized employee and not an external user.
2. Fill out your employee ID, job title, and department.
3. Provide your manager's name, phone number, and email address.
4. If you are requesting a salary change, fill out the requested salary and previous salary fields with your desired salary and current salary respectively.
5. Provide a brief reason for the change in the reason for change field.
6. If you are requesting a change for any other reason, provide that reason in the other reason for change field.
7. Sign and date the form, and have it reviewed and signed by your manager and department head.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): This is your employee ID number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Department Head** (`department_head`, text, required): Enter the name of your department head.
* **Department Head Phone** (`department_head_phone`, text, required): Enter the department head's phone number.
* **Department Head Email** (`department_head_email`, email, required): Enter the department head's email address.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, required): Enter the name of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, required): Enter the team lead's phone number.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, required): Enter the team lead's email address.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required): Enter your manager's title.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required): Enter the user assigned to you.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Sign your name to confirm the request.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
* **Requested Salary** (`requested_salary`, number, required): Enter your requested salary.
* **Previous Salary** (`previous_salary`, number, required): Enter your previous salary.
* **Impact On Work** (`impact_on_work`, text, optional): If the salary change has any impact on your work, describe it here.
* **Other Reason For Change** (`reason_for_change_other`, text, optional): If the salary change is for any other reason, provide that reason here.
* **Reason For Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for the salary change.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to you, so only fill out the fields that are relevant to your situation.
