# military_leave_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture employee information for military leave purposes. It includes details such as employee ID, name, address, military status, and leave duration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Employee Name" field.
4. Select the correct "Date of Leave" and "Expected Return Date" for your military leave.
5. Enter the reason for your leave in the "Reason for Leave" field.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Choose your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
8. Select your available shifts in the "Shift" dropdown menu.
9. Choose your military leave status from the "Military Leave Status" dropdown menu.
10. Select the military branch you are associated with from the "Military Leave Category" dropdown menu.
11. Enter the duration of your military leave in the "Military Leave Duration" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your full name.
* **Date of Leave (3)**: Select the date you will be leaving for military duty.
* **Expected Return Date (4)**: Select the date you expect to return to work.
* **Reason for Leave (5)**: Provide a brief explanation for your military leave.
* **Address (6)**: Enter your physical address.
* **Department (7)**: Select the department you are associated with (Active Duty, Reserve Duty, or Inactive Duty).
* **Shift (8)**: Select the shifts you will be absent from (Morning, Evening, or Night).
* **Military Leave Status (9)**: Choose your military leave status (Active, Reserve, or Inactive).
* **Military Leave Category (10)**: Select the branch you are associated with (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, or Coast Guard).
* **Military Leave Duration (11)**: Enter the duration of your military leave in days.
