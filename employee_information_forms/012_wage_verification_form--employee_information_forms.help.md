# Wage Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wage Verification Form is a document used to confirm an employee's wage information, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records. This form helps employers validate an employee's wage data for official purposes, such as tax reporting or benefit calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Wage Verification Form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the employee's information** in the "employee_information" field. This includes providing details such as their name, date of birth, and ID number.
2. **Enter the wage verification details** in the "wage_verification" field. This section is crucial for confirming the employee's current wage or salary.
3. **Add your employee's signature** (if required) in the "employee_signature" field. This is a personal touch to verify the employee's identity.
4. **Add your employer's signature** (if required) in the "employer_signature" field. This step confirms the employer's acknowledgment of the employee's wage information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_information** (`employee_information`, text, required): Fill in the employee's basic details such as name, date of birth, and ID number to ensure accurate identification.
* **wage_verification** (`wage_verification`, text, required): Enter the employee's current wage or salary information for official purposes.
* **employee_signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): The employee's signature confirms their identity and acknowledges the wage information provided.
* **employer_signature** (`employer_signature`, text, optional): The employer's signature verifies their acknowledgment of the employee's wage information, ensuring accuracy and authenticity.

## Tips
- Double-check the employee's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure the employee's signature is authentic and legible.
- Verify the employer's signature before finalizing the form.
- Keep the form up-to-date with the latest wage information.
- Store the form securely to maintain confidentiality and prevent tampering.
