# film crew self assessment covid screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool for film crew members to report their COVID-19-related health information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form fields with your relevant information. All fields are optional.
2. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "medical condition" field.
8. If you have a travel history, enter it in the "travel history" field.
9. If you have an exposure history, enter it in the "exposure history" field.
10. If you are experiencing any symptoms, enter them in the "symptoms" field.
11. If you have received any test results, enter them in the "test results" field.
12. Select your vaccination status from the options provided.
13. If you have traveled recently, select your travel destinations from the options provided.
14. If applicable, enter your travel dates and duration in the format DD/MM/YY and number, respectively.
15. If applicable, select how frequently you have traveled in the last 14 days.
16. Select your exposure risk level from the options provided.
17. If you have received medical attention or have a high risk level, select the corresponding option.
18. Select the severity of your symptoms from the options provided.
19. Select the type of test you took.
20. If applicable, enter the date you received your test results.
21. Select the type of vaccine you received.
22. Select the location where you received your vaccination.
