# publicUtilityWorkerExitOffboardingForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is an exit offboarding form for workers who are leaving the company. It collects personal and work-related information necessary for maintaining accurate records and ensuring a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What is the worker's last day of employment?" under the "exit_date" field.
2. Choose a reason for the worker's exit from the "exit_reason" field.
3. If you have any feedback or comments about your experience at the company, type it in the "feedback" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Identify your supervisor under the "supervisor_name" field.
7. Select the department you are leaving under the "department" field.
8. If you have any supervisor comments about your exit, type them in the "supervisor_comments" field.
9. Enter your ID number under the "employee_id" field.
10. If you have any manager comments about your exit, type them in the "manager_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **exit_date** (date): This field is for the worker's last day of employment.
* **exit_reason** (select_one): This field is for choosing the reason for the worker's exit.
* **feedback** (note): This field is for providing feedback or comments about your experience at the company.
* **phone_number** (text): This field is for entering your phone number.
* **email** (email): This field is for entering your email address.
* **supervisor_name** (text): This field is for identifying your supervisor.
* **department** (select_one): This field is for selecting the department you are leaving.
* **supervisor_comments** (note): This field is for providing supervisor comments about your exit.
* **employee_id** (number): This field is for entering your ID number.
* **manager_comments** (note): This field is for providing manager comments about your exit.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your HR or personnel department.
* If you are having trouble accessing or filling out the form, contact the IT department for help.
