# Flexiforce PEP Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flexiforce PEP Registration Form is designed to collect information from new candidates registering for the Flexiforce PEP (Performance Excellence Program). This form is used to gather essential data about the employee, including their basic details, job title, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Employee Name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your Contact Number.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Enter a description of your Job Title.
6. Select your Job Category from the provided options.
7. Enter your Job Location.
8. Select your Candidate Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee registering for the PEP.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the employee.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job the employee is applying for.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the job title.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the job from the options provided.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job.
* **Candidate Status** (`candidate_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the candidate from the options provided.
