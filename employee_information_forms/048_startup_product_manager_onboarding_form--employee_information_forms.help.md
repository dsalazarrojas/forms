# Startup Product Manager Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Product Manager Onboarding Form is used to collect essential information for new startup product managers onboarding into the company. This form helps to standardize the onboarding process and ensure that all necessary details are collected in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for any necessary follow-up communication.
4. Enter your **Job Title** to help us understand your role within the company.
5. Select your **Department** to help us identify your team.
6. If applicable, enter your **Manager's Name** for supervision and reporting purposes.
7. Choose your **Onboarding Preferences** from the available options to specify how you would like to receive onboarding updates.
8. Select your preferred **Route Updates** option to determine how you would like to receive updates on your submissions.
9. Enter the **Date Submitted** when you completed the form.
10. Enter the **Time Submitted** to specify when you completed the form.
11. Optionally, add any additional **Notes** that may be relevant to your onboarding.
12. If applicable, select the **Assigned Tool** that you have been assigned to use.
13. Finally, choose a **Category** for your onboarding information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to collect your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter the email address associated with your company-provided email or a personal email that you use for work-related purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your work-related phone number that we can use for follow-up communication.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to collect your job title within the company. Please enter your official job title as it appears in your employee profile.
* **Department**: This field is used to collect your department within the company. Please enter the department where you work, such as Engineering, Marketing, or Sales.
* **Manager**: This field is used to collect the name of your manager. If you are not sure who your manager is, please leave this field blank.
* **Onboarding Preferences**: This field allows you to choose how you would like to receive onboarding updates. Please select one or more of the available options to specify your preferences.
* **Submission Updates**: This field is used to collect any updates related to your submissions. Please enter any relevant details about your submissions.
* **Route Updates**: This field is used to determine how you would like to receive updates on your submissions. Please select one of the available options to specify your preferred route for updates.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is used to collect the date you completed the form. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Submitted**: This field is used to collect the time you completed the form. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Notes**: This field is used to collect any additional notes about your onboarding. Please enter any relevant details that may be helpful.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is used to collect the assigned tool for your onboarding. Please select the tool that you have been assigned to use.
* **Category**: This field is used to collect the category for your onboarding information. Please select a category that best describes your onboarding information.

Note: All fields marked as required are mandatory, while fields marked as optional can be left blank.
