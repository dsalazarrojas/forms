# daily_sign_in_log_validation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to validate daily sign-in logs for employees. It gathers essential details about employee attendance and work-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Employee ID**: Enter your unique Employee ID in the first field.
2. Enter your **Date** in the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Enter your **In Time** and **Out Time** if applicable.
4. Choose your **Status** (Present, Late, or Absent) and provide any additional **Manager Comments** if necessary.
5. Enter your **Employee Name**.
6. Select your **Employee Type** (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
7. Fill in your **Location** if applicable.
8. Enter your **IP Address** and **User Agent** if necessary.
9. Provide your **OS Version**, **Browser**, and **Operating System** details.
10. Enter your **Device** information.
11. Enter your **Browser Version** and **User ID** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID (1)**: A unique identifier for you as an employee. Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Date (2)**: The date of your sign-in (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **In Time (3)**: The time you started work in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Out Time (4)**: The time you finished work in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Status (6)**: Choose your attendance status: Present, Late, or Absent.
* **Manager Comments (7)**: Any comments or feedback from your manager.
* **Employee Name (8)**: Your name as an employee.
* **Department (9)**: The department you belong to.
* **Employee Type (10)**: Your employment type: Full-time, Part-time, or Contract.
* **Location (11)**: The location of your work.
* **IP Address (12)**: The IP address of the device you're using.
* **User Agent (13)**: The user agent of your browser.
* **OS Version (14)**: The version of your operating system.
* **Browser (15)**: The browser you're using.
* **Operating System (16)**: The operating system of your device.
* **Device (17)**: The device you're using.
* **Browser Version (18)**: The version of your browser.
* **User ID (19)**: A unique identifier for you as an employee (this is not the same as your Employee ID).
 
## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all mandatory fields accurately.
- Enter dates and times in the correct format.
- Choose the correct status and provide manager comments if necessary.
- Be precise with your employee type and department.
