# Workplace Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Accommodation Request Form is a tool used by employees to request workplace accommodations that are reasonable and necessary for them to perform their job duties. This form helps employees to submit their accommodation requests, providing the necessary information to support their requests, and allowing managers and HR representatives to review and approve or reject them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Select your workplace location from the "Workplace Location" dropdown menu.
* If applicable, enter the date of your accommodation request in the "Accommodation Request Date" field.
* If applicable, describe your accommodation requests in the "Accommodation Details" field.
* Specify the type of accommodation you require in the "Accommodation Needs" field.
* If you are not requesting an accommodation from your department, specify the accommodation providing party (e.g. facilities team, manager, etc.) in the "Accmodation Providing Party" field.
* You may attach any supporting documentation for your accommodation request in the "Supporting Document" field.
* Finally, click "Submit" to submit your accommodation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **Required**): This is your employee name, which is mandatory to be filled in order to process your request.
* **Email** (`email`, text, **Required**): Enter your email address in order for the accommodation providing party to get in contact with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number in order for the accommodation providing party to get in touch with you.
* **Workplace Location** (`workplace_location`, select_one, **Required**): Select your workplace location, which will allow the accommodation providing party to get in touch with the right person to process your request.
* **Accommodation Request Date** (`accommodation_request_date`, date, **Optional**): If applicable, enter the date of your accommodation request, in case you need your accommodation by a specific date.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, text, **Optional**): If applicable, describe your accommodation requests in more detail here, e.g. if you have a disability or medical needs.
* **Accommodation Needs** (`accommodation_needs`, text, **Required**): Specify the type of accommodation you require, e.g. wheelchair, ergonomic chair, etc.
* **Accommodation Providing Party** (`accommodation_providing_party`, text, **Optional**): If you are not requesting an accommodation from your department, specify the accommodation providing party here, e.g. facilities team, manager, etc.
* **Supporting Document** (`supporting_document`, note, **Optional**): If you have any supporting documentation for your accommodation request, attach it here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, **Optional**): Click "Submit" to submit your accommodation request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all mandatory fields before submitting your request.
* If you are not sure about your workplace location, you can contact your HR representative for help.
* If you need help with any other field, you can contact the person processing your request.
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your accommodation needs, in order for the accommodation providing party to get a better understanding of your request.
