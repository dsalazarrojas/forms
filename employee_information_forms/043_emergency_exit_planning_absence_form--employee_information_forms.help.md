# Emergency Exit Planning Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a crucial part of our emergency exit planning process, ensuring that employees' emergency contacts are informed and able to assist during an emergency. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to help us respond promptly and effectively in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
2.  Enter your employee's name, ID number, and emergency exit date accurately.
3.  Provide your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to you.
4.  If you have two emergency exit times, provide both and ensure they are accurate.
5.  If you have two emergency exit locations, provide both and ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the emergency exit time?** (<em>emergency_exit_time</em>): This field is required. Enter the time you plan to leave the premises.
*   **What is the emergency exit location?** (<em>emergency_exit_location</em>): This field is required. Enter a specific location or address where you can be reached.
*   **Who is the contact person's name?** (<em>emergency_contact_name</em>): This field is required. Enter the name and contact information of the person to be contacted in an emergency.
*   **What is the contact person's phone number?** (<em>emergency_contact_phone</em>): This field is required. Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **What is the contact person's relationship to the employee?** (<em>emergency_contact_relationship</em>): This field is required. Enter the contact person's relationship to you (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
*   **What is the employee's name?** (<em>employee_name</em>): This field is required. Enter your name.
*   **What is the employee's ID number?** (<em>employee_id</em>): This field is required. Enter your employee ID number.
*   **What is the emergency exit date?** (<em>emergency_exit_date</em>): This field is required. Enter the date of the planned emergency exit.
*   **What is the emergency exit time 2?** (<em>exit_time_2</em>): This field is required. Enter the time of your second emergency exit, if applicable.
*   **What is the emergency exit location 2?** (<em>exit_location_2</em>): This field is required. Enter the location of your second emergency exit, if applicable.
*   **Who is the contact person's name 2?** (<em>contact_name_2</em>): This field is required. Enter the contact person's name for the second emergency exit.
*   **What is the contact person's phone number 2?** (<em>contact_phone_2</em>): This field is required. Enter the contact person's phone number for the second emergency exit.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact your HR or management representative.
*   This form is confidential and will only be shared with authorized personnel in case of an emergency.
