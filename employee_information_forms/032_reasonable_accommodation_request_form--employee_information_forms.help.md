# Reasonable Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reasonable Accommodation Request Form is a tool used to document and track requests for reasonable accommodations in the workplace. It is designed to help ensure that such requests are processed and addressed in a fair and timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you are submitting a valid and genuine reason for your request.
*   Provide detailed and clear information about your request, including any relevant dates.
*   Ensure your request is reasonable and does not pose an undue hardship on the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Request (<name>, <type>, required/optional: employee_request, text, optional)
This field is the first section of the form where you can type in your request for a reasonable accommodation. Please provide a clear and concise description of the accommodation you are seeking.

### Employee Department (<name>, <type>, required/optional: employee_department, text, optional)
This field is used to identify which department you belong to. Please type in your department name, e.g., HR, Marketing, Sales, etc.

### Accommodation Requested (<name>, <type>, required/optional: accommodation_requested, text, optional)
This field is where you can describe the specific accommodation you are requesting. Be as detailed as possible to help us process your request efficiently.

### Accommodation Description (<name>, <type>, required/optional: accommodation_description, text, optional)
In this field, provide a brief description of the accommodation you need. This can include any relevant background information or context that will help us understand your request.

### Accommodation Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional: accommodation_date, date, optional)
This field is used to record the date of your request. Please enter the date you are submitting this form in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
