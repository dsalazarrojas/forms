# film_production_family_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a family-related leave of absence from work. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your leave of absence is properly approved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on page 1.
2. Enter your employee information on page 2.
3. Provide your leave start date on page 3.
4. Specify the duration of your leave on page 4.
5. Choose the type of leave you are requesting on page 5.
6. Enter any additional leave details on page 6.
7. Select the manager's approval on page 7.
8. Enter any manager comments on page 8.
9. Confirm your information on page 9.
10. Confirm the production manager's information on page 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Fill in your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **employee** (`employee`, text, required: false): Enter your employee ID or name to ensure accurate processing of your request.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like your leave to start.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required: false): Specify the number of days you would like your leave to last.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of leave you are requesting (e.g., Sick Leave, Maternity Leave, etc.).
* **Leave Details** (`leave_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information regarding your leave.
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required: false): Select whether your manager has approved or rejected your request.
* **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, required: false): Enter any comments or feedback from your manager regarding your leave request.
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, text, required: false): Confirm that you have submitted this form accurately.
* **Production Manager Confirmation** (`production_manager_confirmation`, text, required: false): Confirm that your production manager has reviewed and approved this form.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary approvals from your manager before submitting this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR representative.
