# Artist Information Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Information Registration Form is a tool for artists and bands to provide information about themselves and their performance capabilities. This form is used to gather essential details about the artist, such as their contact information, performance style, and availability for gigs. By filling out this form, artists can increase their chances of getting booked for events and performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Artist or Band Name" field with your professional name or the name of your band.
2. Next, provide the "Band Leader or Contact Name" field with the name of the primary contact person for your group.
3. Enter your "Email Address" in the "Primary contact email" field.
4. Fill out the "Phone Number" field with your contact number.
5. Select your "Primary Genre" from the list of options provided.
6. Write a brief "Artist or Band Biography" in the provided text area.
7. If applicable, provide your "Years Active" in the number field.
8. Enter your "Website or Social Media URL" if you have an online presence.
9. Indicate how many "Band Members" are in your group in the number field.
10. List the "Band Members and Instruments" in the text field.
11. Choose the "Type of Performance Offered" from the list of options provided.
12. Select the "Typical Set Length" from the list of options.
13. If necessary, describe any "Equipment or Technical Requirements" needed for your performance.
14. Choose the days and times you are available for gigs from the list of options.
15. Select how much "Advance Notice Needed" for booking from the list of options.
16. If you wish to include an optional "Performance Fee or Rate," enter it in the text field.
17. If you'd like to suggest an optional "Donation Amount," enter it in the number field.
18. Verify that all information is accurate by selecting "Yes, I Confirm" from the list of options.
19. Finally, add any "Additional Comments or Information" in the text field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name** (Artist or Band Name, text, required): Your professional name or the name of your band.
* **Band Leader or Contact Name** (Band Leader or Contact Name, text, required): The name of the primary contact person for your group.
* **Email Address** (Primary contact email, email, required): Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Your contact number.
* **Primary Genre** (Primary Genre, text, required): Select your main music or art genre.
* **Artist or Band Biography** (Artist or Band Biography, text, required): A brief professional bio (100-150 words).
* **Years Active** (Years Active, number, optional): If your group has been active for more than a year.
* **Website or Social Media URL** (Website or Social Media URL, text, optional): Link to your online presence.
* **Number of Band Members** (Number of Band Members, number, required): How many performers are in your group.
* **List of Band Members and Instruments** (List of Band Members and Instruments, text, required): Names and instruments of all performers.
* **Type of Performance Offered** (Type of Performance Offered, select_one, required): What type of performance do you offer (Live Music, DJ, Spoken Word, Comedy, Theater, Dance, Other).
* **Typical Set Length** (Typical Set Length, select_one, required): How long your typical performance is.
* **Equipment or Technical Requirements** (Equipment or Technical Requirements, text, optional): Special equipment needed for your performance.
* **Availability for Gigs** (Availability for Gigs, select_multiple, required): When you are available to perform (Weekdays, Weekends, Mornings, Afternoons, Evenings, Nights, Flexible).
* **Advance Notice Needed** (Advance Notice Needed, select_one, optional): How much advance notice do you require (1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 6 weeks, 2 months, Flexible).
* **Performance Fee or Rate** (Performance Fee or Rate, text, optional): Your typical rate.
* **Donation Amount (Optional)** (Donation Amount (Optional), number, optional): Suggested donation amount.
* **I Confirm All Information is Accurate** (I Confirm All Information is Accurate, select_one, required): Verify the accuracy of this registration.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (Additional Comments or Information, text, optional): Any other details we should know.
