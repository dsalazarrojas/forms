# intern_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "intern_information_form" is used to collect information about interns, including personal details, contact information, and emergency contact details. This form helps the organization to keep accurate and up-to-date records about their interns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name of the intern in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the intern in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the intern's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the intern's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the intern's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Enter the start and end dates of the intern's contract or agreement in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
7. Enter the name of the company the intern is associated with in the "Company" field.
8. Enter the intern's job title and role in the "Job Title" and "Role" fields.
9. Enter a brief description of the intern's job in the "Job Description" field.
10. Enter the first name of the intern's manager in the "Manager First Name" field.
11. Enter the last name of the intern's manager in the "Manager Last Name" field.
12. Enter the emergency contact information for the intern in the "Emergency Contact" fields, including name, phone number, email address, and address.
13. Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the intern in the "Relationship" field.
14. Select the type of phone number the emergency contact uses in the "Emergency Phone Type" field.
15. Select the language spoken by the emergency contact in the "Emergency Language" field.
16. Select the country of the emergency contact's location in the "Emergency Country" field.
17. Select the time zone of the emergency contact's location in the "Emergency Timezone" field.
18. Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact in the "Emergency Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the intern.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the intern.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the intern's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the intern's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the intern's date of birth.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the intern's contract or agreement.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the intern's contract or agreement.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company the intern is associated with.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Enter the intern's role.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the intern's job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the intern's job.
* **Manager First Name** (`manager_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the intern's manager.
* **Manager Last Name** (`manager_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the intern's manager.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the intern.
* **Emergency Phone Type** (`emergency_phone_type`, text, required): Select the type of phone number the emergency contact uses.
* **Emergency Language** (`emergency_language`, text, required): Select the language spoken by the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Country** (`emergency_country`, text, required): Select the country of the emergency contact's location.
* **Emergency Timezone** (`emergency_timezone`, text, required): Select the time zone of the emergency contact's location.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship Notes** (`emergency_relationship_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the relationship between the emergency contact and the intern.
