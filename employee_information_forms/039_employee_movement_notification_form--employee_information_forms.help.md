# Employee Movement Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Movement Notification Form is used to document an employee's change in status, including transfers, promotions, or separations. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are recorded and communicated to relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the employee's name.
2. Enter the employee's ID number.
3. Select the reason for the transfer, promotion, or separation.
4. Enter the date of the transfer.
5. Select the new office the employee will be moving to.
6. Select the new manager the employee will be reporting to.
7. Enter the new position the employee will be taking.
8. Enter the effective date of the change.
9. If applicable, enter the previous manager's name and position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Employee Name
*   **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their record.
*   **Required**.

### 2. Employee ID
*   **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's unique ID number.
*   **Required**.

### 3. Reason for Transfer/Promotion/Separation
*   **Reason for transfer/Promotion/Separation**: Select the reason for the employee's change in status.
*   This field is used to categorize the reason for the change.

### 4. Date of Transfer
*   **Date of transfer**: Enter the date the employee's status changed.
*   This field is used to record the date the change took effect.

### 5. New Office
*   **New office**: Select the new office the employee will be moving to.
*   This field is used to record the new location of the employee.

### 6. New Manager
*   **New manager**: Select the new manager the employee will be reporting to.
*   This field is used to record the new supervisor.

### 7. New Position
*   **New position**: Enter the new job title or position the employee will be taking.
*   This field is used to record the employee's new role.

### 8. Effective Date
*   **Effective date**: Enter the date the change took effect.
*   This field is used to record the date the change occurred.

### 9. Previous Manager
*   **Previous manager**: Enter the name of the previous manager.
*   This field is used to record the previous supervisor.

### 10. Previous Position
*   **Previous position**: Enter the previous job title or position.
*   This field is used to record the employee's previous role.

### 11. Reason for Separation
*   **Reason for separation**: Select the reason for the separation.
*   This field is used to categorize the reason for the separation.

### 12. Reason for Promotion
*   **Reason for promotion**: Select the reason for the promotion.
*   This field is used to categorize the reason for the promotion.

### 13. Signature
*   **Signature**: This is a note field for the employee or HR representative to sign and date the form.
*   This field is used for authentication and record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the form is complete and accurate before submission.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR or IT personnel for guidance.
