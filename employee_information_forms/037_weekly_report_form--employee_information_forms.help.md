# Weekly Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information on a weekly basis from employees to track their progress, notes, and other relevant details. This form is used to gather information for various employees, and the data collected will be used to monitor and improve employee performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the Employee**: Choose the employee whose information you are reporting on from the list of available options.
2. **Enter Progress**: Report the progress you made this week on the `Progress Form` field.
3. **Add Notes**: Enter any notable events or updates on the `Notes Form` field.
4. **Enter Email**: Provide your email address on the `Email Form` field.
5. **Enter Phone Number**: Input your phone number on the `Phone Form` field.
6. **Select Date**: Enter the date of the report on the `Date Form` field.
7. **Select Time**: Choose the time of the report on the `Time Form` field.
