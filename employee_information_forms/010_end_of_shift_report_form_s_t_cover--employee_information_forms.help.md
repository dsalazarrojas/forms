# End Of Shift Report Form S&T Cover - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Report Form S&T Cover is used to report the details of an employee's shift, including work summary, hours worked, overtime status, and other relevant information. This form is typically completed by the employee at the end of their shift and is used to ensure accurate record-keeping and reporting of work hours and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your employee ID and name in the "Employee ID" and "Employee Name" fields.
2. Select the shift type (Regular, Overtime, Double Overtime, or Call In) and provide the shift start and end dates and times.
3. Enter the hours worked and overtime status.
4. Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
5. Sign and date the form with your supervisor's signature and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift ID** (`shift_id`, Number, required): Enter your shift ID number.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, Number, required): Enter the total hours worked during the shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, Time, required): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, Time, required): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Overtime Status** (`overtime_status`, Select One, required): Select whether you worked overtime or not.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, Date, required): Date of signature.
* **Time** (`signature_time`, Time, required): Time of signature.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, Select One, required): Select your supervisor from the list.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, Text, required): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, required): Enter your name.
* **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, Select One, required): Select the type of shift (Regular, Overtime, Double Overtime, or Call In).
* **Shift Start Date** (`shift_start_date`, Date, required): Enter the start date of your shift.
* **Shift Start Time** (`shift_start_time`, Time, required): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Date** (`shift_end_date`, Date, required): Enter the end date of your shift.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, Time, required): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Break Hours** (`hours_break`, Number, optional): Enter the total break hours taken during the shift.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, Time, optional): Enter the break time.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, Select One, required): Select your employee type (Manager, Employee, Temp).
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, Select One, required): Select your employee status (Active, Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
