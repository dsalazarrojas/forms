# Employee End Of Day Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee End Of Day Report form is used to report your end-of-day activities and progress. It is used by management to keep track of your work hours and performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field. This field is required.
2. Select the date of your workday from the "Date" field. This field is required.
3. If you worked in during your workday, enter the time you clocked in.
4. If you worked out during your workday, enter the time you clocked out.
5. Select your progress for the day from the options provided in the "Employee Progress" field. This field is required.
6. Enter any relevant notes about your workday in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field. This field is required.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Add any comments from your manager in the "Manager Comments" field.
10. Sign and date your form with your manager's signature in the "Manager Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
- **Date**: Enter the date of your workday.
- **Time In**: Enter the time you clocked in during your workday.
- **Time Out**: Enter the time you clocked out during your workday.
- **Employee Progress**: Select one or more of the options provided:
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Bad
- **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about your workday.
- **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, such as your phone number or extension.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments from your manager.
- **Manager Signature**: Sign and date your form with your manager's signature.
  - A signature block may be provided for this field.
