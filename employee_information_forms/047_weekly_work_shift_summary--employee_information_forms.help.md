# Weekly Work Shift Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to track and document the work shifts of employees for a specific week.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the week you are reporting on (Week 1-5).
2. Enter the start and end date of your shift for each week.
3. Enter the start and end time of your shift for each week.
4. Enter the name of the employees working each shift.
5. Enter any additional details about each shift.
6. Enter the total hours worked for each week.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week 1** (`1`): Enter the week number you are reporting on.
* **Week 2** (`2`): Enter the week number you are reporting on.
* **shiftStart date 1** (`shift_start_date_1`): Enter the date when your shift started for Week 1.
* **shiftStart time 1** (`shift_start_time_1`): Enter the time when your shift started for Week 1.
* **shiftEnd date 1** (`shift_end_date_1`): Enter the date when your shift ended for Week 1.
* **shiftEnd time 1** (`shift_end_time_1`): Enter the time when your shift ended for Week 1.
* **Employee Name 1** (`employee_name_1`): Enter the name of the employee working this shift.
* **shiftDetails 1** (`shift_details_1`): Enter any additional details about this shift.
* **Work Hours 1** (`work_hours_1`): Enter the total hours worked for Week 1.
* **Week Hours Total 1** (`week_hours_total_1`): This field is automatically calculated and displays the total hours worked for Week 1.
* **shiftStart time 2** (`shift_start_time_2`): Enter the time when your shift started for Week 2.
* **shiftEnd date 2** (`shift_end_date_2`): Enter the date when your shift ended for Week 2.
* **Employee Name 2** (`employee_name_2`): Enter the name of the employee working this shift.
* **shiftDetails 2** (`shift_details_2`): Enter any additional details about this shift.
* **shiftEnd time 2** (`shift_end_time_2`): Enter the time when your shift ended for Week 2.
* **Week 3** (`3`): Enter the week number you are reporting on.
* **shiftStart date 3** (`shift_start_date_2`): Enter the date when your shift started for Week 3.
* **shiftDetails 3** (`shift_details_3`): Enter any additional details about this shift.
* **Week 4** (`4`): Enter the week number you are reporting on.
* **Employee Name 3** (`employee_name_3`): Enter the name of the employee working this shift.
* **shiftStart time 3** (`shift_start_time_3`): Enter the time when your shift started for Week 3.
* **Week 5** (`5`): Enter the week number you are reporting on.
* **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`): Enter the total hours worked for all shifts in Week 5.

### Tips

* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each shift. This will help ensure accurate reporting and tracking of work shifts.
* If you have any conflicts or issues with your shifts, please report them to your supervisor or HR representative immediately.
