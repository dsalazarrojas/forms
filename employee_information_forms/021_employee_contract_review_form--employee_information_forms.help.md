# employee_contract_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee contract review form is used to gather and document information about employee contracts. This form helps managers or HR representatives review and update employee contract details, ensuring that all necessary information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name.
2. Provide the job title of the employee.
3. Enter the start date of the contract.
4. Enter the end date of the contract.
5. Enter the manager's name.
6. Select the employee's manager(s) who reviewed the contract.
7. Enter the date the contract was reviewed.
8. Choose the status of the contract (Approved, Pending, or In Progress).
9. Provide any comments or feedback on the contract (optional).
9. Enter the manager's email address.
10. Enter the manager's phone number.
11. Indicate if the employee has a contract (True or False).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter the job title of the employee.
* **Contract Start Date** (Contract Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the contract in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contract End Date** (Contract End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the contract in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter the manager's name who is responsible for the contract.
* **Manager(s) Who Reviewed** (Reviewed By, select_multiple, required): Select the manager(s) who reviewed the contract. This field has options for multiple managers.
* **Review Date** (Reviewed Date, date, required): Enter the date the contract was reviewed in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Status** (Status, select_one, required): Choose the status of the contract: Approved, Pending, or In Progress.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the contract.
* **Manager Email** (Manager Email, email, required): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Manager Phone, text, required): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **Has Contract** (Has Contract, select_one, required): Indicate if the employee has a contract (True or False).

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate information about the employee's name, job title, contract dates, and manager's details.
* Review the contract carefully and choose the correct status.
* Make sure to enter the correct manager's email and phone number.
* Confirm that the employee has a contract or not.
