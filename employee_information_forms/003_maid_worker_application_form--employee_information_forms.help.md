# Maid Worker Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maid Worker Application Form is designed to collect information from maid workers who are applying for employment. This form is intended to be completed by the applicant and will be used for internal purposes, such as processing the application and communicating with the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employer Name (Employer Name)** (`employer_name`, `text`, Required): Please enter the name of the company or person who is employing you as a maid worker.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`contact_number`, `text`, Optional): If you want to be contacted for further information, please enter a valid phone number. This is not required, but it will help us reach out to you more easily.
* **Email Address (Email Address)** (`email_address`, `email`, Optional): If you have an email address, please enter it so we can communicate with you more efficiently. This is not required, but it will help us send you updates and information about your application.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding in this process.
