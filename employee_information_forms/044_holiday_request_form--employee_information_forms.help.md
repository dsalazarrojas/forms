# Holiday Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Holiday Request Form. This form is used to submit holiday requests, including start and end dates, employee details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name and job title.
2. Select the start and end dates of your holiday.
3. Choose the type of holiday you are requesting (e.g., Business, Personal, Annual Leave, or Other).
4. Select the reason for your request (e.g., Personal or family issues, Annual leave, or Other).
5. If required, provide additional holiday start and end dates.
6. Enter your Employee ID and Manager ID (if applicable).
7. Specify the length of your holiday in days.
8. Enter the number of days you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start receiving holiday enquiries with Jotform** (User Input): Enter a brief introduction or message to begin your holiday request.
* **Holiday start date** (Date): Select the date your holiday will start.
* **End date** (Date): Select the date your holiday will end.
* **Employee name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your employee card or ID.
* **Job title** (Text): Enter your official job title as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Department** (Text): Enter your department as it appears in the company's organizational structure.
* **Team** (Text): Enter your team as it appears in the company's organizational structure.
* **Holiday type** (Select One): Choose the type of holiday you are requesting (e.g., Business, Personal, Annual Leave, or Other).
* **Reason for request** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable reasons for your holiday request (e.g., Personal or family issues, Annual leave, or Other).
* **Holiday start date 2** (Date): If necessary, select an additional start date for your holiday.
* **End date 2** (Date): If necessary, select an additional end date for your holiday.
* **Employee ID** (Number): Enter your Employee ID as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Manager ID** (Number): Enter your Manager ID as it appears in the company's HR system.
* **Holiday length** (Number): Enter the length of your holiday in days.
* **Days requested** (Number): Enter the number of days you are requesting for your holiday.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are submitting a valid holiday request form to avoid duplication or confusion.
* Double-check your holiday start and end dates for accuracy.
* Select the correct holiday type and reason for request to ensure proper processing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative for assistance.
