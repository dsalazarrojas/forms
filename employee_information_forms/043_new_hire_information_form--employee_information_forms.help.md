# New Hire Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Information Form is used to provide essential details about a new employee's job, responsibilities, and work environment. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately for HR and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information about the new hire.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you have provided accurate information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Information** (`new_hire_info_form`): This field is not a field to fill in, but rather a header for the form. Please proceed to the next section.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the official job title of the new hire.
* **Other (if applicable)** (`job_title_other`): If the new hire has a different job title, enter it in this field.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`): Briefly describe the new hire's job and its responsibilities.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of the company the new hire will be working for.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the manager the new hire will be reporting to.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email of the manager the new hire will be reporting to.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter the phone number of the manager the new hire will be reporting to.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date the new hire will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the date the new hire will end.
* **Salary** (`salary`): Enter the salary of the new hire.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`): Enter any benefits the new hire is entitled to.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the new hire.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the status of the job (Active or Inactive).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`): Select the type of job (Sales, Marketing, Development, Finance, IT).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`): Select the category of the job (Executive, Management, Non-Executive, Non-Management).
* **Job Title Category** (`job_title_category`): Select the category of the job title (Executive, Manager, Non-Executive).
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department of the new hire.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`): Select the level of the job (Entry Level, Mid-level, Senior).
* **Job Family** (`job_family`): Select the family of the job (Corporate, Business, Operations).
* **Job Function** (`job_function`): Enter the function of the new hire's job.
* **Job Function Category** (`job_function_category`): Enter the category of the new hire's job function.
* **Job Grade** (`job_grade`): Enter the grade of the new hire's job.
* **Job Grade Category** (`job_grade_category`): Enter the category of the new hire's job grade.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the new hire's job.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the New Hire Information Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to HR or your supervisor.
