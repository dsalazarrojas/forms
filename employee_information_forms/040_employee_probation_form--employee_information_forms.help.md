# Employee Probation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Probation Form is used to document the employee probation process, tracking and evaluating an employee's performance during their trial or test period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each new hire entering a probationary period.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback and performance ratings.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Name (Page 1)**: Enter the name of the new hire.
* **Job Position (Page 1)**: Indicate whether the new hire has been given a job position during their probationary period. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Performance Rating (Page 1)**: Provide a numerical rating of the new hire's performance, out of a scale (to be determined by your organization).
* **Probation Period (Page 1)**: Enter the duration of the new hire's probationary period.
* **Feedback Notes (Page 1)**: Add any feedback or comments about the new hire's performance.
* **Date of Last Evaluation (Page 1)**: Record the date of the last evaluation of the new hire's performance.
* **Time of Last Evaluation (Page 1)**: Record the time of the last evaluation of the new hire's performance.
* **Evaluation By (Page 1)**: Record who conducted the last evaluation of the new hire's performance.
* **Evaluation Comments (Page 1)**: Add any comments or observations from the last evaluation.
* **Overall Performance (Page 1)**: Provide a numerical rating of the new hire's overall performance.
* **Areas of Improvement (Page 1)**: Identify areas where the new hire needs improvement. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Recommended Training (Page 1)**: Identify any recommended training for the new hire. Options are "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Comments (Page 1)**: Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Be honest and accurate in your feedback and ratings.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
