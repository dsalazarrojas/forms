# RBT/BCBA Information Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The RBT/BCBA Information Submission form is used to collect and verify employee information for RBT/BCBA (Registered Behavior Analyst or Board Certified Behavioral Analyst) certification and registration purposes. This form requires submission of personal details and professional credentials to facilitate a smooth and efficient certification process. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information as it is crucial for the certification and registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Rbt/Bcba Information Submission" page.
2. Fill in the required fields, paying close attention to each field's label and instructions.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next step.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting it for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`Title`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you provide a brief title or name that will be used to identify you in the certification process.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as per your personal records.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Provide your current address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Rbt Certificate Status** (`Rbt Certificate Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Active" if your RBT certificate is currently active, or "Inactive" if it has expired or is not valid.
* **Bcba Certification** (`Bcba Certification`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Active" if you have an active BCBA certification, or "Inactive" if it is not active.
* **Expiration Date** (`Expiration Date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, provide the date of expiration for your BCBA certification.
    Please note that all fields with "required: false" can be omitted if the field's value is not applicable to your situation.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and truthfully to avoid any delays or issues with your certification process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification before submitting your form.
* Please review your submission thoroughly before submitting it for review and approval.
