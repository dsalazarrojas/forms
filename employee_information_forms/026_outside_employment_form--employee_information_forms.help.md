# Outside Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's external employment details, helping to maintain accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. If applicable, enter the employee's job position and title in the respective fields.
3. Select the employee's employment type from the options (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
4. If necessary, select the employee's employment status (Active, Terminated, or Retired).
5. Enter the date of employment if known.
6. Provide a brief reason for the employment (optional).
7. Enter the supervisor's name and other relevant contact information (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's full name, as it appears on their official documents.
* **Job Position**: Enter the employee's job position or title, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of employment (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
* **Employment Status**: Select the current employment status (Active, Terminated, or Retired).
* **Date of Employment**: Enter the date of employment, if known.
* **Reason for Employment**: Provide a brief explanation for the employment (optional).
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the supervisor's name, if applicable.
* **Employee Number**: Enter the employee's ID number, if applicable.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the employee's contact number, if applicable.
* **Department**: Enter the department the employee is affiliated with, if applicable.
* **Branch**: Enter the branch the employee is working in, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure that the employee's employment status and details are relevant to the current situation.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult the relevant authority or HR department.
