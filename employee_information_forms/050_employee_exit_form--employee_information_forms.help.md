# employee_exit_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The employee_exit_form is a data collection tool used to gather information from employees who are leaving their job. This form helps track relevant details about the employee's departure, including the reason for leaving and last day of work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the employee has left their job with their current employer.
2. Enter the name of the employee's current or previous employer.
3. Enter the employee's job title.
4. Choose a reason for the employee's departure from the list of options, or provide a custom reason if "Other" is selected.
5. Enter the last day of work for the employee.
6. Enter the exit date for the employee.
7. Provide a more detailed reason for the departure, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name**: Select "Yes" if the employee has left their job with their current employer, or "No" otherwise. This will help to identify the correct employer.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is leaving their job. This is the name that will be associated with the exit data.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title that the employee held while working with the current employer.
* **Reason for Departure**: Choose a reason from the list of options or provide a custom reason if "Other" is selected. This will help to track the reason for the employee's departure.
* **Last Day of Work**: Enter the last day that the employee worked with the current employer. This is typically the last day of work before the employee's departure.
* **Exit Date**: Enter the date of the employee's exit from the company. This date should match the last day of work for the employee.
* **Reason for Departure (Other)**: If "Other" is selected as the reason for departure, enter a more detailed reason for the employee's departure. This should be a brief but clear explanation of the reason why the employee is leaving.
