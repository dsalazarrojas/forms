# Workers Profile Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workers Profile Form is used to collect and manage employee information, including their personal details and job-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing the correct type of information.
3. Use the drop-down menus to select relevant options for fields like supervisor and job status.
4. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the employee, such as their job role or responsibilities.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, date, optional): Enter the employee's start date in the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's work phone number.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the employee has a supervisor, 'No' otherwise.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable departments the employee is part of.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the employee's job responsibilities.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, optional): Enter the employee's unique identification number.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select 'Active' if the employee is currently active, 'Inactive' otherwise.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter the employee's termination date.
* **Job Title Change Date** (`job_title_change_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee's job title was last changed.
* **Supervisor Change Date** (`supervisor_change_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee's supervisor was last changed.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* If an employee's status changes, update the relevant fields accordingly.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with HR or the relevant department for guidance.
