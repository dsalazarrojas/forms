# Workplace Inclusion Program Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Inclusion Program Absence Form is a document used to report the absence of an employee from work. This form will help management and HR track and manage the reason for the absence and ensure the employee's status while away.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "employee_name". This field is required.
2. Enter the date of your absence in the field labeled "date_of_absence". This field is required and should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Briefly describe the reason for your absence in the field labeled "absence_reason". This field is required.
4. Select your current employee status in the field labeled "employee_status". This field is required.
5. Enter your direct manager's name in the field labeled "manager_name". This field is required.
6. Enter your manager's email address in the field labeled "manager_email". This field is required and should be in the format name@domain.com.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required): This field is for your name as an employee.
* **date_of_absence** (date, required): This field is for the date you will be absent from work. Please enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **absence_reason** (text, required): This field is for a brief explanation of the reason for your absence.
* **employee_status** (text, required): This field is for your current status as an employee. Please select one of the following options: [insert options here]
* **manager_name** (text, required): This field is for your direct manager's name.
* **manager_email** (email, required): This field is for your direct manager's email address. Please enter it in the format name@domain.com.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and honestly, and that you have obtained approval from your manager before submitting this form.
