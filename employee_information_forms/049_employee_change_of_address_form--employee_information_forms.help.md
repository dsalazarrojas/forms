# Employee Change Of Address Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Change Of Address Form is used to update your address information with the company. This form is typically required when you have a change of address, such as moving to a new home or office.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID number in the "Employee Id" field.
2. Provide the old address information in the "Address Line 1", "Address Line 2", "City", and "Zip" fields.
3. Enter the "Date Of Move" when you moved to the new address.
4. Enter the new address information in the "New Address Line 1", "New Address Line 2", "New City", and "New Zip" fields.
5. Explain the reason for your move in the "Reason For Move" field.
6. Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id**: Enter your unique Employee ID number assigned to you by the company. This is used to identify you in our system.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter the old building or apartment number where you lived before moving. Please include the house or building number, street address, and any apartment or suite number.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter any additional address information that is not included in the first line, such as floor or room numbers.
* **City**: Enter the city where you used to live.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your old address.
* **Date Of Move**: Enter the date you moved to your new address.
* **New Address Line 1**: Enter the building or apartment number where you currently live.
* **New Address Line 2**: Enter any additional address information that is not included in the first line, such as floor or room numbers.
* **New City**: Enter the city where you currently live.
* **New State**: Select the state where you currently live from the list.
* **New Zip**: Enter the zip code of your new address.
* **Reason For Move**: Explain the reason for your change of address in a few sentences. This can include moving for a new job, family, education, or other personal reasons.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
