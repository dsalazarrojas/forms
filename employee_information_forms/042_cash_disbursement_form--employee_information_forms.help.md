# Cash Disbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cash Disbursement Form is used to record and authorize cash disbursements, ensuring accurate and efficient processing and accounting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the disbursement ID number in the "Disbursement ID Number" field.
2. Select the date of the disbursement in the "Disbursement Date" field.
3. Provide the payee's information in the "Payee Name", "Payee Type", and "Payee Address" fields.
4. Enter the payment amount and select the currency in the "Payment Amount" and "Currency" fields, respectively.
5. Choose the payment method and reason for the payment in the "Payment Method" and "Reason for Payment" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the detailed description of the payment in the "Detailed Description" field.
7. If applicable, enter the invoice number, invoice date, purchase order number, and account information in their respective fields.
8. Select the budget category and tax information (if applicable) in the "Budget Category" and "Tax Information" fields.
9. Enter the number of documents attached, if any.
10. If applicable, enter the approval level and processor name.
11. Finally, enter the requester's name, request date, and approver's name, and approval date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disbursement ID Number (2)** (Required/Not Required): Disbursement ID number.
* **Disbursement Date (3)** (Required/True): Date of disbursement.
* **Payee Information (4)** (Required/Not Required): Information about the person or entity receiving payment.
*   *Payee Name (5)* (Required/True): Full name or company of the payee.
*   *Payee Type (6)* (Required/True): Category of the payee (Employee, Vendor or supplier, etc.).
*   *Payee Address (7)* (Required/Not Required): Mailing address of the payee.
*   *Payee Email (8)* (Required/Not Required): Contact email of the payee.
*   *Payee Phone (9)* (Required/Not Required): Contact phone number of the payee.
*   *Payee ID Number (10)* (Required/Not Required): ID number of the payee (if applicable).
*   *Payment Details (11)* (Required/Not Required): Payment specifics.
*   *Payment Amount (12)* (Required/True): Total dollar amount of payment.
*   *Currency (13)* (Required/True): Currency type (USD, EUR, etc.).
*   *Payment Method (14)* (Required/True): How payment is made (Cash from petty cash, Check, etc.).
*   *Reason for Payment (15)* (Required/True): Purpose of payment.
*   *Detailed Description (16)* (Required/True): More information about the payment.
*   *Invoice Number (17)* (Required/Not Required): If applicable, enter invoice number.
*   *Invoice Date (18)* (Required/Not Required): If applicable, enter invoice date.
*   *Purchase Order Number (19)* (Required/Not Required): If applicable, enter purchase order number.
*   *Account Information (20)* (Required/Not Required): Charging details.
*   *General Ledger Code (21)* (Required/True): GL account.
*   *Department Code (22)* (Required/Not Required): Cost center.
*   *Project Code (23)* (Required/Not Required): If applicable, enter project code.
*   *Budget Category (24)* (Required/Not Required): Expense category.
*   *Tax Information (25)* (Required/Not Required): Tax details.
*   *Is Tax Applicable (26)* (Required/False): Tax status.
*   *Tax Amount (27)* (Required/False): Tax value.
*   *Tax Rate (28)* (Required/False): Tax percentage.
*   *Supporting Documents (29)* (Required/Not Required): Attached documentation.
*   *Documents Attached (30)* (Required/Not Required): What is included.
*   *Number of Documents (31)* (Required/Not Required): Document count.
*   *Authorization (32)* (Required/Not Required): Approvals required.
*   *Requested By (33)* (Required/True): Name of requester.
*   *Request Date (34)* (Required/True): Date of request.
*   *Approved By (35)* (Required/Not Required): Name of approver.
*   *Approval Date (36)* (Required/Not Required): Date of approval.
*   *Approval Level Required (37)* (Required/Not Required): Authority level.
*   *Processed By (38)* (Required/Not Required): Name of processor.
*   *Process Date (39)* (Required/Not Required): Date of processing.
*   *Check Details (40)* (Required/Not Required): If paid by check, enter check details.
*   *Check Number (41)* (Required/Not Required): Check number.
*   *Bank Account (42)* (Required/Not Required): Account used.
*   *Cash Balance Before Disbursement (43)* (Required/Not Required): Starting balance before disbursement.
*   *Cash Balance After Disbursement (44)* (Required/Not Required): Ending balance after disbursement.
*   *Notes (45)* (Required/Not Required): Additional notes.
*   *Payee Signature Date (46)* (Required/False): Date of payee signature.
*   *Preparer Signature Date (47)* (Required/False): Date prepared by preparer.
*   *Approver Signature Date (48)* (Required/False): Date of approval by approver.
