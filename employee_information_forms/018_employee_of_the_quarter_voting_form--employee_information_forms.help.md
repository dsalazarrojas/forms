# Employee Of The Quarter Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Of The Quarter Voting Form is a democratic process that allows team members to nominate and vote for the Employee of the Quarter. This form is designed to be completed by authorized team members to cast their votes without influence or coercion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and ensure you have read and understood each field's label and purpose.
2.  On the first page, select "Yes" or "No" for each of the four options: employee_of_the_quarter_voting_form, outstanding\_employee_first_name, outstanding\_employee_last_name, outstanding\_employee\_department, and outstanding\_employee\_position.
3.  On the second page, enter the first name and last name of the employee you wish to nominate.
4.  On the third page, select "Yes" or "No" for the employee\_number field, and enter the employee's employee number.
5.  On the fourth page, select "Yes" or "No" for the department field, and enter the department of the employee you wish to nominate.
6.  On the fifth page, select "Yes" or "No" for the position field, and enter the position of the employee you wish to nominate.
7.  On the sixth page, enter the job title of the employee you wish to nominate.
8.  On the seventh page, enter the reason why you are nominating this employee.
9.  On the eighth page, enter the name of the nominator.
10. On the ninth page, enter the department of the nominator.
11. On the tenth page, enter the job title of the nominator.
