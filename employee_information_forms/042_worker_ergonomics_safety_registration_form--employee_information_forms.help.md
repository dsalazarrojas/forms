# Worker Ergonomics Safety Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from workers to ensure their safety and well-being in the workplace. The goal is to gather essential details about the worker's job, supervisor, and emergency contact information to help maintain a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your job title and category.
2.  Provide your job type and work location.
3.  Enter your supervisor's name, email, and phone number.
4.  Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
5.  Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email.
6.  Provide details about your relationship with your emergency contact.
7.  Enter the duration of your relationship with your emergency contact.
8.  Enter any additional worker ergonomics information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Job Title** (required): Enter your official job title.
  - **Job Category** (required): Enter your job category.
  - **Job Type** (required): Enter your job type.
  - **Work Location** (required): Enter your work location.
  - **Supervisor Name** (required): Enter your supervisor's name.
  - **Supervisor Email** (required): Enter your supervisor's email address.
  - **Supervisor Phone** (required): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
  - **Emergency Contact** (required): Select your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Emergency Contact Person, Family Member, Spouse, Child, Parent).
  - **Name** (required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
  - **Phone** (required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
  - **Email** (required): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
  - **Relationship Details** (required): Provide details about your relationship with your emergency contact.
  - **Relationship Since** (required): Enter the duration of your relationship with your emergency contact.
  - **Worker Ergonomics Information 1-5** (optional): Enter any additional worker ergonomics information if required.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- Review and understand the relevant policies and procedures related to worker ergonomics safety in your organization.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
- Do not complete this form multiple times or with incomplete information.
