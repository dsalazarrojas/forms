# Religious Exemption For Covid Vaccine Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a religious exemption from the COVID-19 vaccine for an employee. It is designed to gather essential information for processing the exemption request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your employee ID.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
3. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your place of birth.
5. Select your religion from the list provided.
6. Explain the reason for your exemption request.
7. Indicate your current exemption status.
8. Fill out any additional comments or notes if required.
9. Specify your current employee status (Active, Inactive, or Resigned).
10. Provide the date and time you are requesting the exemption.
11. Enter your manager's name.
12. Provide your manager's email address.
13. Enter your manager's phone number.
14. Enter your manager's department.
15. Enter your supervisor's name (if applicable).
16. Enter your supervisor's phone number (if applicable).
17. Enter your supervisor's email address (if applicable).
18. Enter the company address.
19. Sign your name as an employee.
20. Sign your manager's name (if required).
21. Enter the date and time of submission.
