# Employee Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Transfer Form is used to initiate an employee's transfer to a new department within the organization. This form gathers essential details about the employee's current and new departments, transfer date, reason, and other relevant information to facilitate a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the employee's data, including their ID, current department, and reason for transfer.
2. Select the new department the employee will be transferring to.
3. Enter the effective date of the transfer.
4. Specify the reason for the transfer, if applicable.
5. Confirm the new department and old department of the employee.
6. Enter the old department's location (if different from the new department's location).
7. Add any additional comments about the transfer.
8. Review and submit the form once all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Data** (`employee_data`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the employee's details, including their ID, current department, and reason for transfer.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the new department the employee will be transferring to.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the transfer is expected to take place.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`reason_for_transfer`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the reason for the transfer, if applicable.
* **New Department** (`new_department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the new department of the employee.
* **Old Department** (`old_department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm the old department of the employee.
* **Old Department Location** (`old_department_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the old department, if different from the new department's location.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments about the transfer.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the employee's ID.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check the employee's details and department assignments to avoid errors.
- If the transfer is for an unknown or exceptional reason, please provide a clear explanation in the "Reason for Transfer" field.
