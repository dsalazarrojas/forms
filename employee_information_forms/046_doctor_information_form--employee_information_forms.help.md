# doctor_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The doctor_information_form is used to collect and store information about doctors working within our organization or medical practice. This includes their personal details, contact information, professional credentials, and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Optionally, provide your **Nickname** if applicable.
4. Enter your **Address** where you can receive mail and other correspondence.
5. Enter your **Contact Number** where you can be reached for official communications.
6. Enter your **Email** address for secure and efficient communication.
7. Select your **Specialty** as "Yes" or "No", depending on your area of expertise.
8. Enter your **License Number** for identification and credential purposes.
9. Select your **License Expiration Date** from the provided calendar.
10. Select whether you have **Board Certification** as "Yes" or "No", depending on your qualifications.
11. Choose your level of **Degree** as "Yes" or "No", indicating your educational attainment.
12. Select whether you have worked in **Worked At** any previous medical institutions or practices as "Yes" or "No".
13. Choose which **Languages Spoken** you are proficient in, selecting all that apply.
14. Finally, provide a brief description of your **Education**, highlighting your relevant studies and training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (doctor_first_name)**: A required field to collect your first name.
- **Last Name (doctor_last_name)**: A required field to collect your last name.
- **Nickname (doctor_nickname)**: An optional field for any additional names by which you are known.
- **Address (doctor_address)**: Enter the address where you can receive mail and other correspondence.
- **Contact Number (doctor_contact_number)**: Enter a number where you can be reached for official communications.
- **Email (doctor_email)**: Enter your email address for secure and efficient communication.
- **Specialty (doctor_specialty)**: Select whether you have this specialty, answering "Yes" or "No".
- **License Number (doctor_license_number)**: Enter a license number for identification and credential purposes.
- **License Expiration Date (doctor_license_expiration_date)**: Select your license expiration date from the provided calendar.
- **Board Certification (doctor_board_certification)**: Select whether you have this certification, answering "Yes" or "No".
- **Degree (doctor_degree)**: Choose your level of degree, selecting "Yes" or "No".
- **Worked At (doctor_worked_at)**: Select whether you have worked in other medical institutions or practices, answering "Yes" or "No".
- **Languages Spoken (doctor_languages_spoken)**: Choose all languages you are proficient in, selecting multiple options.
- **Education (doctor_education)**: Provide a brief description of your education, highlighting relevant studies and training.
