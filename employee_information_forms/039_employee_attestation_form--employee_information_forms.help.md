# Employee Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Attestation Form is used for employees to update their personal and employment details for payroll and HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is designed to gather essential information about your employment status. Please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary details before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and employment information.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information (Page 1)**:
    * **Employee ID**: Please enter your unique employee ID number.
    * **Reporting Manager**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
    * **Department**: Type in your department name or code.
    * **Location**: Enter the name of your work location.
    * **Job Grade**: Enter your job grade or level.
    * **Job Code**: Enter your job code or title.
* **Date Of Hire**: Enter the date you were hired.
* **Date Of Last Promotion**: Enter the date of your last promotion.
* **Employee ID**: This is a verification field to match your employee ID.
* **Date Of Last Payroll Run**: Enter the date of the last payroll run.
* **Last Payroll Run Number**: Enter the last payroll run number.
* **Next Payroll Run Date**: Enter the expected date of the next payroll run.
* **Reporting Manager 2**: Enter the name of your secondary reporting manager (if applicable).
* **Department 1**: Enter your department name or code (if different from Department).
* **Location 1**: Enter the name of your secondary work location (if applicable).
* **Reporting Manager 1**: Enter the name of your primary reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager 2 2**: This field is likely an error in the provided schema and should be omitted.

Please note that the provided schema has multiple identical fields for "Reporting Manager 2" and "Reporting Manager 2 2", which should not be filled out as it is a duplicate field.

## Tips

* Ensure your information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays or errors in payroll processing.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Complete the form carefully, as it may require multiple attempts to fill out correctly.
