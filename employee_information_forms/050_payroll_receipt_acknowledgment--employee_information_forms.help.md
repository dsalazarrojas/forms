# Payroll Receipt Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Payroll Receipt Acknowledgment" form is used to confirm that an employee has received their payroll payment. This form ensures that employees acknowledge receipt of their pay and helps the payroll team track and verify payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the pay period in which the payment was received in the "Pay Period" field.
4. Enter the date the payment was received in the "Pay Date" field.
5. Enter the amount of the payment in the "Pay Amount" field.
6. Enter the check number in the "Check Number" field.
7. Enter the bank account number in the "Bank Account" field.
8. Enter the date the check was issued in the "Check Date" field.
9. Enter the name of the bank in the "Bank Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Pay Period**: Select the pay period in which you received the payment.
* **Pay Date**: Enter the date you received the payment.
* **Pay Amount**: Enter the total amount of the payment you received.
* **Check Number**: Enter the check number or payment reference number on the payment slip.
* **Bank Account**: Enter your bank account number or routing number.
* **Check Date**: Enter the date the check was issued by the bank.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy to ensure that your information is correct.
* If you have any issues or discrepancies with your payment, contact your HR or payroll representative.
* If you are unsure of any field, consult with your HR or payroll representative for clarification.
