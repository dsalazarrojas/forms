# Physical Therapist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from physical therapists during their onboarding process. The form includes fields for identifying the individual, their professional credentials, and contact information. It will help in maintaining accurate records and facilitating a smooth transition for the therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the label next to each field.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid errors and inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Physical Therapist Info** (physical_therapist_info, text, optional): This field is for entering a brief description or notes about the physical therapist.
- **Credential Verification** (credential_verification, text, optional): Check with your local medical board to verify your credentials.
- **Scheduling Preferences** (scheduling_preferences, select_multiple, required): Select the preferred scheduling options for your work. (Note: Options 1, 2, and 3 are available)
- **Contact Info** (contact_info, select_one, required): Select the preferred contact method for communication.
- **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Date of Graduation** (date_of_graduation, date, optional): Enter your date of graduation.
- **License Number** (license_number, text, optional): Enter your physical therapy license number.
- **License State** (license_state, text, optional): Enter your physical therapy license state.
- **Clinic Info** (clinic_info, text, optional): Enter any additional clinic information.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
- Double-check for errors and inconsistencies before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
