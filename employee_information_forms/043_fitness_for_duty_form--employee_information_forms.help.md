# Fitness For Duty Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's fitness for duty. It helps managers and administrators to track employee assignments and approvals.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing the form

*   Fill in your name, status (active or inactive), return date (if applicable), and other relevant fields as prompted.

### Submitting the form

*   Once you have filled out the form, select the "Submit" button to send the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Information

*   **Employee Information**: Select 'Yes' if the employee is assigned to the task, 'No' otherwise.

### 2. Employee Status

*   **Employee Status**: Select 'Active' if the employee is currently active, 'Inactive' if the employee is not active.

### 3. Return Date

*   **Return Date**: If the employee is on a return date, enter the date they are expected to return.

### 4. Comments

*   **Comments**: Add any additional information or comments about the employee's assignment.

### 5. Assigned To

*   **Assigned To**: Select 'Yes' if the employee is assigned to the task, 'No' otherwise.

### 6. Submitted By

*   **Submitted By**: Select 'Yes' if the form is submitted by the employee, 'No' otherwise.

### 7. Submitted On

*   **Submitted On**: Enter the date the form was submitted.

### 8. Reviewed By

*   **Reviewed By**: Select 'Yes' if the form has been reviewed, 'No' otherwise.

### 9. Reviewed On

*   **Reviewed On**: Enter the date the form was reviewed.

### 10. Approved By

*   **Approved By**: Select 'Yes' if the form has been approved, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Select 'No' if the employee has not been assigned to the task or is not actively working.
