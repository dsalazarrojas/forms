# Daily Company Covid 19 Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about employees' potential exposure to COVID-19, including symptoms and travel history. The data collected will help the company take necessary precautions to maintain a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company name and your name in the respective fields.
2. Select all the symptoms you have been experiencing in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Answer the "Travel History" field as instructed.
4. In the "Close Contacts" field, list any close contacts you've had recently.
5. For "Recent Symptoms", select any new or worsening symptoms you've experienced.
6. If you've undergone any tests, enter the results in the "Test Results" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (Text)**: Your company name, where you work or have recently worked.
* **Employee Name (Text)**: Your full name, as per your ID badge or personnel records.
* **Department (Text)**: Your department or team you belong to at your company.
* **Symptoms (Select Multiple)**: Select all the symptoms you have been experiencing, such as Fever, Cough, Shortness of breath, or Chest pain or discomfort, or Muscle aches.
* **Travel History (Text)**: Describe your recent travel history, including any international or domestic travel, and dates of return.
* **Close Contacts (Text)**: List any close contacts, such as family members, coworkers, or clients, you've had in the past 14 days.
* **Recent Symptoms (Select Multiple)**: Select any new or worsening symptoms you've experienced in the past 14 days.
* **Test Results (Text)**: Enter any test results you've received, such as a positive or negative COVID-19 test result.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your HR department for assistance.
* Keep the form up to date with any changes to your status.
