# Feature Artist Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feature artist registration information. Please complete this form to register your artist's details, including publicist or manager's contact information. Your data will be used internally for feature management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your artist's information** on the Artist Info page.
2. **Add your artist's artwork or feature image** on the Artist Image page.
3. **Enter your artist's social media profiles** on the Artist Social Media page.
4. **Add your publicist or manager's contact information** on the subsequent pages, including their email, phone number, and address.
5. **Submit your completed form** once you have filled out all the required fields.
