The purpose of this form is to collect essential information about new employees, which will be used for employee access and management purposes.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you are completing this form for a new employee. If you have already completed this form for this employee, please do not submit the form again. This form is designed to be completed once during the onboarding process.

This form contains the following fields to ensure accurate and efficient processing of employee information:

### new_employee_access_form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The new employee access form is used to collect essential information about new employees for employee access and management purposes.

#### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* employee_name: Enter the employee's full name.
	* department: Select the employee's department (e.g. Accounting, HR, Marketing, Sales).
	* supervisor_name: Enter the employee's supervisor's name.
	* employee_email: Enter the employee's email address.
	* employee_phone: Enter the employee's phone number.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* date_of_birth: Enter the employee's date of birth.
	* job_title: Enter the employee's job title.
	* job_description: Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
	* job_start_date: Enter the date the employee started their job.
	* job_end_date: Enter the date the employee's job ended (if applicable).
	* manager_approvals: Select whether the employee has manager approvals.
	* job_level: Select the employee's job level (e.g. Entry Level, Mid-level, Senior Level).
	* department_approvals: Select whether the employee has department approvals.
	* emergency_contact_name: Enter the emergency contact's name.
	* emergency_contact_phone: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
	* emergency_contact_email: Enter the emergency contact's email address.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name (Text, required)**: Enter the employee's full name.
* **date_of_birth (Date, optional)**: Enter the employee's date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **job_title (Text, optional)**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **job_description (Text, optional)**: Enter a brief description of the employee's job.
* **department (Select One, optional)**: Select the employee's department (e.g. Accounting, HR, Marketing, Sales).
* **supervisor_name (Text, optional)**: Enter the supervisor's name.
* **employee_email (Email, required)**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **employee_phone (Text, optional)**: Enter the employee's phone number.
* **job_start_date (Date, optional)**: Enter the date the employee started their job.
* **job_end_date (Date, optional)**: Enter the date the employee's job ended (if applicable).
* **manager_approvals (Select Multiple, optional)**: Select whether the employee has manager approvals.
* **job_level (Select One, optional)**: Select the employee's job level (e.g. Entry Level, Mid-level, Senior Level).
* **department_approvals (Select Multiple, optional)**: Select whether the employee has department approvals.
* **emergency_contact_name (Text, optional)**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **emergency_contact_phone (Text, optional)**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_contact_email (Email, optional)**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
