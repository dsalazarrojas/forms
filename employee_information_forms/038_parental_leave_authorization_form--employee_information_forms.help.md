# parental_leave_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Leave Authorization Form is used to collect and manage information related to an employee's parental leave request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant employee information from the options provided.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the leave period.
3. Provide the employee's name and job title.
4. Choose the reason for the leave and the department the employee is part of.
5. Enter the manager's email and phone number.
6. Select the employee's status (active or inactive).
7. Choose the approval status (approved or declined).
8. Add any comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information**: This section is used to select the relevant employee details. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Start Date**: This field is used to enter the start date of the leave period. Please use the date format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **End Date**: This field is used to enter the end date of the leave period. Please use the date format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Employee Name**: This field is used to enter the employee's name.
* **Manager Name**: This field is used to enter the manager's name.
* **Reason for Leave**: This field is used to enter the reason for the employee's leave.
* **Department**: This field is used to select the department the employee is part of.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to enter the employee's job title.
* **Employee Status**: This field is used to select the employee's status (active or inactive).
* **Manager Email**: This field is used to enter the manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: This field is used to enter the manager's phone number.
* **Approval Date**: This field is used to enter the date and time of approval.
* **Approval Time**: This field is used to enter the time of approval.
* **Approval Status**: This field is used to select the approval status (approved or declined).
* **Comments**: This field is used to add any additional comments about the leave request.
* **Form ID**: This field is used to enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is used to enter the tool assigned for the leave request.
