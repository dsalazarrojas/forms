# Injury Recovery Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to document an employee's injury recovery absence, providing essential information about their medical condition, recovery status, and related details. It helps management and HR to track and manage injury-related absences efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a legitimate reason for submitting this form, as stated above.
* Carefully fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Email** (employee_email, email, required): Enter your official work email address.
* **Injury Details** (injury_details, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your injury or illness, including any relevant medical information.
* **Recovery Start Date** (recovery_start_date, date, optional): Enter the date you began your recovery period.
* **Recovery End Date** (recovery_end_date, date, optional): Enter the expected date you will return to work.
* **Reason for Recovery** (reason_for_recovery, text, optional): Briefly explain the reason for your recovery absence.
* **Physician Name** (physician_name, text, optional): Enter the name of your attending physician.
* **Physician Phone** (physician_phone, text, optional): Enter the contact number of your attending physician.
* **Recovery Status** (recovery_status, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your recovery:
	+ Pending: Your recovery status is still pending approval.
	+ Approved: Your recovery status has been approved.
	+ Denied: Your recovery status has been denied.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your recovery absence.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are using this form for a legitimate reason and not for other purposes.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
