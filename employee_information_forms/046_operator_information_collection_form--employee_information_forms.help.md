# Operator Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store operator information, including their name, department, job title, contact details, and language spoken. The collected data will be used for administrative purposes and to facilitate communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Operator Name in the first field.
2. Select your department from the drop-down menu.
3. Enter your job title in the corresponding field.
4. Provide your manager's name in the next field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field, using the correct format (e.g., `operator@example.com`).
6. Fill in your phone number in the phone field.
7. Provide the date you were on-boarded in the Onboarding Date field.
8. Enter any additional notes about yourself in the Operator Notes field.
9. If applicable, enter any department notes in the Department Notes field.
10. Enter your Employee ID in the Employee ID field.
11. Fill in your address in the Operator Address field.
12. Enter your zip code in the Operator Zip field.
13. Enter the city where you are located in the City field.
14. Enter the state where you are located in the State field.
15. Select the country where you are located from the dropdown menu.
16. Select your time zone from the dropdown menu.
17. Select the languages you speak from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Operator Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your ID or as you prefer to be called.
* **Department**: Select the department you work in from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title within the organization.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Operator Email**: Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `operator@example.com`).
* **Operator Phone**: Enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date you were on-boarded in the correct format (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD`).
* **Operator Notes**: Enter any notes about yourself that you would like to share with others.
* **Department Notes**: Enter any department-specific notes that you would like to share with others.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Operator Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Operator Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **City**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **State**: Enter the state where you are located.
* **Country**: Select the country where you are located from the dropdown menu.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone from the dropdown menu.
* **Languages Spoken**: Select all languages you speak from the dropdown menu.
