# Lab Data Analyst Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a required part of the onboarding process for new employees joining the lab as data analysts. It helps us understand their role, lab-related details, and any necessary internal acknowledgments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely.
2. Select your role from the options provided.
3. Enter your internal acknowledgment, if applicable.
4. Fill out the lab details section with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Lab Data Analyst Details** (`new_lab_data_analyst_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter a brief description of your new role as a Lab Data Analyst.
* **Role Setup** (`role_setup`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select one or more of the following roles that apply to you:
		- Lab Data Analyst
		- Data Coordinator
		- Researcher
* **Internal Acknowledgment** (`internal_acknowledgement`, note, optional):
	+ If you have any internal acknowledgment or clearance, please note it here.
* **Acknowledge Details** (`acknowledgement_details`, text, optional):
	+ Please provide any additional details regarding your internal acknowledgment.
* **Lab Details** (`lab_details`, text, required):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the lab you will be working in, such as lab number, location, or contact person.
