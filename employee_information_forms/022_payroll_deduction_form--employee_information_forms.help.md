# Payroll Deduction Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track payroll deductions for employees. It is typically completed by employees to submit their payroll deductions and managed by HR or finance teams for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill out the form. 
2. Fill in the employee's details in the "Employee" field, including their full name. 
3. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field. 
4. Enter the deduction amount and select the "Payment Frequency" from the dropdown menu. This will determine how often the deduction is paid. 
5. List the deductions you would like to make in the "Deductions List" field. 
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field, if necessary. 
7. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`payroll_deduction_form_employee`, text, not required): Enter the employee's full name in this field.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, not required): Enter your unique employee ID in this field.
* **Deduction Amount** (`deduction_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of the deduction in this field.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to make the deduction (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, etc.).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the deduction in this field.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date of the deduction in this field.
* **Deductions List** (`deductions_list`, select_multiple, required): Select all deductions you would like to make from the list.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your deduction in this field.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, not required): Enter your name as the submitter of this form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, not required): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time you submit this form.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed your deductions in this field.
* **Reviewed On** (`reviewed_on`, date, not required): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of the review.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who approved your deductions in this field.
* **Approved On** (`approved_on`, date, not required): This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of the approval.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary approval and submission authority before completing this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR or finance representative for clarification.
