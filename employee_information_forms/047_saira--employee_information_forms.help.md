# Artist Advance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from artists for event planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to review and complete each section thoroughly.
3. If any field is not applicable, leave it blank or fill it with "N/A" (not applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your name and contact details, including email and phone number.
### Credentials (Page 1)
* **Credentials**: Share your professional credentials, such as your resume, portfolio, or any other relevant documents.
### Vehicles (Page 1)
* **Vehicles**: If you have any vehicles that you will be bringing to the event, please list them here.
### Catering (Page 1)
* **Catering**: If you have any specific catering requirements, please specify them here.
### Hospitality Details (Page 1)
* **Hospitality Details**: Provide any special hospitality requirements you may have, such as accommodation or meal requests.
### Artist (Page 2)
* **Artist**: Share your artistic credentials, including your name, style, and experience.
### Transportation (Page 2)
* **Transportation**: If you have any transportation requirements, please specify them here.
### Arrival and Departure (Page 2)
* **Arrival and Departure**: Provide your expected arrival and departure time from the venue.
### On-Site Media (Page 2)
* **On-Site Media**: If you have any specific media requirements, such as electricity or internet, please specify them here.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for your convenience, and we appreciate your cooperation in providing us with accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
* Please fill in the form in a legible and readable format.

Note: The following fields are not required, so you can leave them blank if they do not apply to you.
