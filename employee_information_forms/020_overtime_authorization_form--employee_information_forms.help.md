# Overtime Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Overtime Authorization Form is a document used to request approval for employees to work beyond their regular working hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand that it is for new overtime requests and ensure you are not revising an existing one.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* The form section that requests the number of overtime hours
	* The start and end dates of the overtime work period
	* The employee's name
	* The department they belong to
	* The job title and type of work
3. Submit the form for approval to your manager or team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overtime Hours**: This is a numerical field where you should input the total hours you are requesting for overtime work. This will help managers track and manage overtime hours across the team.
* **Start Date**: This date field is where you should enter the start date of the overtime work period.
* **End Date**: This date field is where you should enter the end date of the overtime work period.
* **Employee Name**: This field is for the employee's name who is requesting overtime.
* **Department**: This field is to select the department the employee belongs to. Please choose the correct department from the options provided.
* **Job Title**: This field is for the employee's job title.
* **Job Type**: This field is for selecting the type of job or task the employee will be performing during overtime. Please choose the correct type from the options provided.
* **Department** (again, for select_one): This field is similar to the previous "Department" field but with a select_one type. Please select the correct department from the options.
* **Job Type Options**: This field is not used in the form submission but is likely used internally for tracking and reporting purposes. Please do not fill this field.
