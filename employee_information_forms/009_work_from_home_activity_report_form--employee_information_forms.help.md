# Work From Home Activity Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about employee activities while working from home. It is intended for employees to report their work hours, work location, and other relevant details. Please ensure that you have permission to work from home before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and date of work in the appropriate fields.
2. Provide your start and end time of work.
3. Specify your work location, if applicable.
4. Select your current status from the options provided (e.g., "Available to work", "On a break", etc.).
5. Enter the number of hours you worked.
6. Optionally, provide any comments or notes about your work experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel records.
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you worked from home in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY HH:MM:SS.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you started working from home in the format HH:MM.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you ended your work from home in the format HH:MM.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required: false): If you worked from a different location, enter the name or address of the location where you worked from home.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your current status from the provided options:
	+ Option 1: Available to work
	+ Option 2: On a break
	+ Option 3: On a leave
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total number of hours you worked from home.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Optionally, enter any comments or notes about your work experience.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have permission to work from home before completing this form.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out this form, as it will be used to track your work activities.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to your manager or HR representative for assistance.
