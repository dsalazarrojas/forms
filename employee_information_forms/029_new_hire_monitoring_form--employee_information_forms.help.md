# new_hire_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about new hires, including their job details, employment status, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields according to the explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Details (1)**: Enter the new hire's name and any other relevant details.
* **Hire Date (2)**: Enter the date the new hire was hired.
* **Job Title (3)**: Enter the new hire's job title.
* **Department (4)**: Select the department the new hire is working in.
* **Job Status (5)**: Select the new hire's job status (Active, Resigned, or Retired).
* **Job Grade (6)**: Select all applicable job grades (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4) that apply to the new hire.
* **Contact Email (7)**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **Contact Phone (8)**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **New Hire Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes about the new hire.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure the form is complete.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for official documentation.
