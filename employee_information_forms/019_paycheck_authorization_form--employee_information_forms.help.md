# Paycheck Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paycheck Authorization Form is designed to provide an official authorization for an employee's paychecks, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for proper processing and handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "First Name", "Last Name", and "Date" fields with your correct information.
2. Provide the "Pick up name" and "Authorized Name" fields if necessary.
3. Check the "Relationship" field and select 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
4. If applicable, enter the relationship type in the "Relationship Type" field.
5. Fill out the "Pick up Date" field with the date you would like to pick up your paycheck.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date**: Enter your date of birth or a specific date relevant to your employment or identity.
* **Pick up name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to pick up your paycheck if it is not you.
* **Authorized Name**: Enter the name of the person authorized to pick up your paycheck.
* **Authorized Date**: Enter the specific date of authorization.
* **Relationship**: Check "Yes" if you have a relationship with the payee, otherwise check "No".
* **Relationship Type**: If you have a relationship with the payee, enter the type of relationship (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Pick up Date**: Enter the date you would like to pick up your paycheck.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact your HR department for assistance.
- This form is for internal use only; please do not share it with others.
- The information provided on this form will be used for official payroll purposes only.
