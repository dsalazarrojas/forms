# Daycare Staff Shift Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Staff Shift Scheduling Form is used to collect information from staff members about their availability and schedule preferences for daycare shifts. This form helps the management to plan and schedule shifts effectively, ensuring that the right staff members are assigned to the right shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the staff scheduling information section. This section requires providing basic information about yourself, such as staff scheduling info.
2. In the availability and schedule preferences section, indicate your availability by selecting the shifts you are available to work. You can select multiple shifts if applicable.
3. In the shift details section, provide any additional information about your shifts, such as start and end times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_scheduling_form** (`staff_scheduling_form`, text, optional): This field is for providing staff scheduling information.
* **staff_scheduling_info** (`staff_scheduling_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing more detailed information about your staff scheduling.
* **schedule_shifts** (`schedule_shifts`, text, optional): This field is for indicating which shifts you are available to work. Select multiple shifts if applicable.
* **select_shifts** (`select_shifts`, select multiple, optional): This field is for selecting which shifts you are available to work.
* **available_time** (`available_time`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date you are available to work.
* **selected_date** (`selected_date`, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date you are available to work.
* **start_time** (`start_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the start time of your shift.
* **end_time** (`end_time`, time, optional): This field is for selecting the end time of your shift.
* **shift_details** (`shift_details`, note, optional): This field is for providing additional information about your shifts.
* **select_shift_time** (`select_shift_time`, select one, optional): This field is for selecting whether you are available to work at the selected time.
* **select_shift_day** (`select_shift_day`, select one, optional): This field is for selecting whether you are available to work on the selected day.
* **select_shift_start** (`select_shift_start`, select one, optional): This field is for selecting whether you are available to work at the selected start time.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and truthful information about your availability and schedule preferences.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant staff member or management.
