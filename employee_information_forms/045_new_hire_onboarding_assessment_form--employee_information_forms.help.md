# New Hire Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Assessment Form is designed to capture essential information about a new hire during the onboarding process. This form helps HR or administrative staff assess and onboard new employees efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the New Hire Onboarding Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's **First Name** in the "first_name" field.
2. Select the employee's **Hire Date** from the calendar.
3. Enter the employee's **Job Title** in the "job_title" field.
4. Enter the employee's **Department** in the "department" field.
5. If applicable, enter the employee's **Employee ID** in the "employee_id" field. Note that this field might be specific to your organization's ID system.
6. Enter the employee's **Manager's Name** in the "manager_name" field.
7. Enter the employee's **Manager's Email** in the "manager_email" field. This will help the manager be contacted for any important updates or notifications.
8. Enter the employee's **Phone Number** in the "phone_number" field.
9. Select the employee's **Onboarding Assessment** from the options provided in the "new_hire_onboarding_assessment_form_2" field. This is a multiple-choice question to gauge the employee's onboarding experience.
10. Select the employee's **Onboarding Preferences** from the options provided in the "new_hire_onboarding_assessment_form_3" field. This is a multiple-choice question to understand the employee's onboarding needs.
11. Enter the manager's **Phone Number** in the "manager_phone_number" field. This is not explicitly necessary, but it might be useful for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Hire Date** (date, required): Select the date the employee was hired.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (text, required): Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): If applicable, enter the employee's ID number. (Note: This field might be specific to your organization's ID system.)
* **Manager Name** (text, required): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (email, optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Onboarding Assessment** (select one, optional): Select how well the employee felt about the onboarding process. (Note: Options depend on the specific choices provided.)
* **Onboarding Preferences** (select multiple, optional): Select the onboarding preferences for the employee. (Note: Options depend on the specific choices provided.)
* **Manager Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the manager's phone number. (Note: This field is not explicitly necessary, but it might be useful for communication purposes.)
