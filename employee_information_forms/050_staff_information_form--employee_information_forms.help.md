# Staff Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Information Form is used to collect basic staff information for organizational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, job title, email, and phone numbers.
2. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Check the "Employment Status" field to confirm your current employment status.
4. If you have an alternate phone number, please provide it in the "Alternate Phone" field.
5. Select your department from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your primary email address associated with your role.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number associated with your role.
* **Alternate Phone** (`contact_phone_alternate`, text, optional): Enter any other phone number that may be used for contact purposes.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, text, optional): Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department from the options provided.
