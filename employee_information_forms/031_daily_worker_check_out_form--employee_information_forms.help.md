# Daily Worker Check-Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Worker Check-Out Form is designed to record the date and time worked by employees on a daily basis. This form helps ensure accurate reporting and tracking of work hours, which is vital for payroll processing, time tracking, and attendance management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's information, including the date worked.
2. Enter the time worked, in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
3. Select the check-out time when the employee finished their workday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_information** (Required/Optional: Not Required): This field is intended for employees to provide their personal details, but it is not a mandatory field. However, if you choose to include this field, please fill in the employee's name and ID for accurate tracking.
* **Date Worked** (`date`): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY, or MM/DD/YY (e.g., 09/02/2022).
* **Time Worked** (`time`): Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM or 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Check Out Time** (`time`): Enter the time when the employee finished their workday, in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).

## Tips
* Double-check the date and time fields to ensure accuracy.
* If you need to report work hours for multiple employees, repeat the form for each employee.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared or used for external purposes without permission from your organization.
