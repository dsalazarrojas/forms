# Bonus Pay Tax Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect bonus pay tax withholding elections from employees. Employees must complete this form to elect their tax withholding options for bonus pay. The information collected will be used for payroll processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the employee's name and ID in the first two fields.
2. Enter the employee's email address to ensure that they receive any necessary notifications or confirmations.
3. Select the department the employee belongs to.
4. Enter the pay period date for the bonus.
5. Enter the gross bonus amount.
6. Select the federal tax filing status and bonus tax withholding method.
7. If selecting the aggregate method, select the aggregate method details.
8. If selecting the separate method, enter the withholding rate.
9. If selecting the custom amount method, enter the dollar amount to withhold.
10. Select the federal income tax withholding method.
11. If selecting the extra federal withholding method, enter the per-paycheck amount.
12. Select the state income tax withholding method.
13. If selecting the extra state withholding method, enter the per-paycheck amount.
14. Select the local tax withholding method.
15. Select the Medicare withholding method.
16. Select the Social Security withholding method.
17. If selecting a non-standard FICA withholding rate, enter the rate.
18. Select the 401(k) contribution election.
19. If selecting a custom 401(k) contribution amount, enter the amount.
20. List any other deductions or elections.
21. Confirm that the elections are accurate.
22. Select the date of the election.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the employee's name as it is spelled out on their identification.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number as issued by the company or government.
* **Email Address**: Enter the employee's email address to ensure that they receive any necessary notifications or confirmations.
* **Department**: Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Pay Period for Bonus**: Enter the month and year for the pay period in which the bonus is being paid.
* **Gross Bonus Amount**: Enter the total bonus amount being paid to the employee.
* **Federal Tax Filing Status**: Select the tax filing status that applies to the employee.
* **Bonus Tax Withholding Method**: Select the method to withhold taxes from the bonus.
* **Aggregate Method Details**: If selected, select the method to aggregate withhold from the bonus.
* **Separate Method Withholding Rate**: If selected, enter the percentage to withhold.
* **Custom Withholding Amount**: If selected, enter the dollar amount to withhold.
* **Federal Income Tax Withholding**: Select the method to withhold federal income tax.
* **Federal Extra Withholding**: If selected, enter the per-paycheck amount to withhold extra federal tax.
* **State Income Tax Withholding**: Select the method to withhold state income tax.
* **State Name**: If selecting extra state withholding, enter the state's name.
* **State Extra Withholding**: If selected, enter the per-paycheck amount to withhold extra state tax.
* **Local Tax Withholding**: Select the method to withhold local tax.
* **Medicare Withholding**: Select the method to withhold Medicare tax.
* **Social Security Withholding**: Select the method to withhold Social Security tax.
* **FICA Combined Rate**: If selecting a non-standard FICA withholding rate, enter the rate.
* **401(k) Contribution Election**: Select the 401(k) contribution election.
* **401(K) Contribution Amount**: If selected a custom 401(k) contribution amount, enter the amount.
* **Other Deductions or Elections**: List any additional deductions or elections.
* **I Confirm the Above Elections are Accurate**: Confirm that the elections are accurate.
* **Date of Election**: Select the date of the election.
