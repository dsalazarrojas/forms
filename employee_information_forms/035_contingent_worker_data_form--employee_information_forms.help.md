# Contingent Worker Data Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information about contingent workers, including their identity, contact details, employment status, and onboarding requirements. Ensure you provide accurate and complete data to enable a smooth onboarding process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the below steps:

1. Enter your full name.
2. Select your date of birth.
3. Enter your personal email address.
4. Enter your mobile phone number.
5. Select your staffing agency or enter 'Independent' if self-employed.
6. Enter your job title.
7. Choose a targeted start date for your assignment.
8. Optionally, enter your estimated end date.
9. Select all equipment and access requirements you need for the assignment.
10. Enter the name of your supervisor or hiring manager.
11. Confirm whether tax forms have been submitted (W-9 or W-8BEN).
12. Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
13. Select your relationship with the emergency contact.
14. Sign and confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
15. Date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Text): Enter your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Select your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Personal Email Address** (Email): Enter your personal email address for communication.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (Text): Enter your mobile phone number for communication.
* **Staffing Agency Name** (Text): Enter the name of your staffing agency or select 'Independent' if self-employed.
* **Job Title** (Text): Enter your assignment job title.
* **Targeted Start Date** (Date): Select the date you're expected to start the assignment.
* **Estimated End Date** (Date): Optionally, enter the estimated end date of your assignment. Leave blank if indefinite or ongoing.
* **Equipment and Access Required** (Select Multiple): Select all equipment and access requirements you need for the assignment.
	+ Company Laptop
	+ VPN/Remote Access
	+ Physical Building Badge
	+ Corporate Email Account
	+ Slack/Teams Access
	+ Specialized Software License
* **Hiring Manager/Supervisor Name** (Text): Enter the name of your supervisor or hiring manager.
* **Tax Forms (W-9 or W-8BEN) Submitted** (Select One): Confirm whether tax forms have been submitted (W-9 or W-8BEN).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (Select One): Select your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **I confirm the information provided is accurate** (Text): Sign and confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
* **Today's Date** (Date): Date and submit the form.

Note: Optional fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
