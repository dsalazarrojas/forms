# Administrative Assistant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an administrative assistant, gathering feedback on their attendance, punctuality, communication skills, organizational abilities, computer proficiency, and other critical aspects of their role. The evaluation will help managers and supervisors assess their administrative assistants' strengths and weaknesses, provide guidance for improvement, and make informed decisions about their development and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Provide your name and title in the "Evaluator Name" and "Evaluator Position" fields.
4. Specify the evaluation period in the "Evaluation Period" field.
5. Select the department the employee works in from the "Department" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date the employee started in their position in the "Position Start Date" field.
7. Rate the employee's attendance and punctuality in the "Attendance and Punctuality" field.
8. Evaluate their communication skills in the "Communication Skills" field.
9. Assess their organizational skills in the "Organizational Skills" field.
10. Rate their computer proficiency in the "Computer Proficiency" field.
11. Evaluate their multitasking ability in the "Multitasking Ability" field.
12. Rate their problem-solving skills in the "Problem-Solving Skills" field.
13. Assess their ability to maintain confidentiality in the "Confidentiality" field.
14. Evaluate their teamwork skills in the "Teamwork" field.
15. Rate their initiative and proactivity in the "Initiative" field.
16. Rate the quality of their work in the "Quality of Work" field.
17. Assess their productivity in the "Productivity" field.
18. Evaluate their customer service skills in the "Customer Service" field.
19. List the employee's key strengths in the "Key Strengths" field (optional).
20. Identify areas for improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field (optional).
21. Specify goals for the next evaluation period in the "Goals for Next Period" field (optional).
22. Provide your overall performance rating of the employee in the "Overall Performance Rating" field.
23. Complete the form by entering the date you completed it in the "Evaluation Date" field.
24. Sign and date the form with your signature in the "Evaluator Signature" field.
