# Work Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Release Form is used to document the official separation of an employee from their work. This form is used to collect information on the employee's name, ID, job title, date of separation, reason for separation, and expected return date. It also requires a signature from the employee, confirming their understanding of their separation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's **Employee Name** and **Employee ID**.
2. Enter the **Employer Name** if applicable.
3. Provide the **Job Title** of the employee.
4. Enter the **Date of Separation** in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Select the **Reason for Separation** from the provided options or describe a brief reason.
6. Enter the **Expected Return Date** in the format HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 02:30 PM).
7. Sign and date the **Signature of Employee** section, confirming that you understand the terms of your separation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification or personnel records.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter the employee's ID number as assigned by HR or the company.
- **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's current or former employer (if applicable).
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title as it appears in their personnel records.
- **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, date, required): Enter the date the employee will be separated from work (MM/DD/YYYY format).
- **Reason for Separation** (`reason_for_separation`, text, required): Select the reason for the employee's separation from the provided options or describe a brief reason.
- **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, time, required): Enter the expected date and time the employee will return to work (HH:MM AM/PM format).
- **Signature of Employee** (`signature`, note, required): Sign and date the signature section, confirming that you understand the terms of your separation.
