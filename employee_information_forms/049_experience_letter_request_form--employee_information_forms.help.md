# Experience Letter Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request an experience letter, a document that confirms your work history and experience in a specific job position within a company. The experience letter serves as a professional reference, often required for job applications, promotions, or other employment-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Employee Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the number of years you worked in the Experience Years field.
3. Provide your Job Title and any other relevant job details in the Job Title field.
4. Enter the name of the company you worked for in the Company Name field.
5. Select the start date of your employment in the Job Start Date field.
6. If applicable, select the end date of your employment in the Job End Date field.
7. Enter the department and job function you worked in during your employment.
8. Enter the name of your manager at the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, employee ID, or any other relevant details that identify you as the employee.
- **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the total number of years you worked in your job position.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position held during your employment.
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company you worked for.
- **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your employment.
- **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your employment if applicable.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team you worked in during your employment.
- **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job function or responsibilities.
- **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager at the company.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and truthfully to ensure the experience letter is issued correctly.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR department for guidance.
