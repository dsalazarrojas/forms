# Teacher Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record a teacher's personal and professional information for internal reference and use.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your teacher name in the "Teacher Name" field. This field is required and should contain your full name as it appears on official documents.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This field is also required for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
4. Select your department affiliation from the options provided in the "Department" field. This field is required to determine your subject area.
5. Enter your school name in the "School" field. If you are associated with multiple schools, list all relevant institutions.
6. Select your job role(s) from the options provided in the "Teacher Role" field. Multiple roles can be selected, as applicable.
7. Enter the date you were hired in the "Date Hired" field, following the date format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy, depending on your location.
8. Provide the date you are available to work in the "Date Available" field, following the date format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
9. (Optional) Add a brief bio in the "Bio" field, describing your teaching experience, qualifications, or any relevant information.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Department** (Select One, Required): Select your department affiliation from the provided options (e.g., Math, Science, English).
* **School** (Text, Required): Enter your school name(s) if associated with multiple institutions.
* **Teacher Role** (Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple roles that describe your job title or responsibilities (e.g., Class Teacher, Subject Teacher, Teacher Assistant).
* **Date Hired** (Date, Required): Enter the date you were hired using the mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Date Available** (Date, Required): Enter the date you are available to work using the mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy format.
* **Bio** (Text, Optional): Add a brief bio describing your teaching experience, qualifications, or relevant information.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments for reference.
