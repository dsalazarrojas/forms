# Employee Self Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Employee Self Declaration Form" is a document used by employees to provide accurate and up-to-date information about themselves. This form is essential for maintaining a precise record of employee details, which is vital for employee management, reporting, and other administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each section.
2.  Fill in your name and contact information in the "Name And Contact Information" section.
3.  Enter your employee information in the "Employee Information" section.
4.  Provide a detailed description of your work history in the "Work History" section.
5.  Verify that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name and Contact Information** (`Name and Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your full name and contact details (email, phone number, or address) as accurately as possible.
* **Employee Information** (text, required): Provide an overview of your employee status, job title, job responsibilities, or any other relevant details.
* **Work History** (text, required): Give a brief account of your work history, including past employers, job roles, and the dates of your employment.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting it.
* Make sure to keep the form up-to-date as your circumstances change.
* Review and update your form periodically to reflect any changes in your work history.
