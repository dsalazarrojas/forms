# subcontractor_information_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about subcontractors working with the organization, including their contact details, employment history, and contract terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the subcontractor's name in the **Subcontractor Name** field.
2. Enter the subcontractor's company name in the **Company Name** field (optional).
3. Enter the subcontractor's email address in the **Email** field (optional).
4. Enter the subcontractor's phone number in the **Phone** field (optional).
5. Enter the subcontractor's company website in the **Company Website** field (optional).
6. Enter the subcontractor's job title in the **Job Title** field (optional).
7. Enter the subcontractor's address in the **Subcontractor Address** field (optional).
8. Enter the subcontractor's city in the **Subcontractor City** field (optional).
9. Enter the subcontractor's state in the **Subcontractor State** field (optional).
10. Enter the subcontractor's zip code in the **Subcontractor Zip** field (optional).
11. Enter the subcontractor's start date of work in the **Subcontractor Date** field (optional).
12. Enter the subcontractor's work hours in the **Subcontractor Time** field (optional).
13. Enter any notes about the subcontractor in the **Subcontractor Note** field (optional).
14. Enter the subcontractor's phone number in the **Subcontractor Phone** field (optional).
15. Enter the subcontractor's fax number in the **Subcontractor Fax** field (optional).
16. Enter the company's fax number in the **Company Fax** field (optional).
17. Enter the contract start date in the **Contract Date** field (optional).
18. Enter the contract end date in the **Contract End Date** field (optional).
19. Enter the contract start time in the **Contract Start Time** field (optional).
20. Enter the contract end time in the **Contract End Time** field (optional).
21. Enter the subcontractor's rate of pay in the **Subcontractor Rate** field (optional).
22. Select the subcontractor's agreement type from the **Subcontractor Agreement** field (optional).
23. Select the subcontractor's payment terms from the **Subcontractor Payment Terms** field (optional).
24. Enter any terms and conditions for the subcontractor in the **Subcontractor Terms and Conditions** field (optional).
25. Select a reference for the subcontractor from the **Subcontractor Reference** field (optional).
