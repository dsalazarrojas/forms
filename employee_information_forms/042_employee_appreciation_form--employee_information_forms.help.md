# Employee Appreciation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Appreciation Form is used to show appreciation for an employee's hard work and dedication. It allows managers and supervisors to recognize and reward employees for their efforts. This form helps to boost employee morale, motivation, and engagement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Choose whether the employee is in the department by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Department" field.
5. Enter the employee's manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
6. Enter the employee's employee number in the "Employee Number" field.
7. Select the appreciation level for the employee (High or Low) in the "Appreciation Level" field.
8. Provide a reason for the appreciation in the "Reason for Appreciation" field.
9. Enter a message expressing appreciation in the "Appreciation Message" field.
10. Select the date of the appreciation in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Select the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the employee is in the department by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's manager's name.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, number, optional): Enter the employee's employee number.
* **Appreciation Level** (`appreciation_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select the appreciation level for the employee (High or Low).
* **Reason for Appreciation** (`appreciation_reason`, note, optional): Provide a reason for the appreciation.
* **Appreciation Message** (`appreciation_message`, text, optional): Enter a message expressing appreciation for the employee.
* **Date** (`appreciation_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the appreciation.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide a specific reason for the appreciation to help the employee understand why they are being appreciated.
* Keep the appreciation message concise and sincere.
* Use the correct appreciation level to accurately reflect the employee's performance.
