# Employment Verification Form For Child Care - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to verify the employment status of existing employees, ensuring accuracy and consistency in child care services. It confirms the current employment status, employer details, and verification method for a particular employee, helping child care providers to provide accurate and secure services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employment status of the employee from the options provided.
2. Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their job card or ID card.
3. Enter the employer's name as it appears on their ID or business card.
4. Enter the job start date in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
5. Enter the job end date (if applicable) in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
6. Enter the employer's address as it appears on their ID or business card.
7. Select the current job status of the employee.
8. Enter the date of the employment verification.
9. Specify the verification method used to confirm the employment status.
10. Describe the source of the verification information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employment_status**: Select the current employment status of the employee:
	+ None: Not Applicable (used for non-employees)
	+ Other: Choose this option for any other employment status not listed
* **job_title**: Enter the employee's current job title.
* **employer_name**: Enter the name of the employee's current or previous employer.
* **job_start_date**: Enter the date the employee started their job in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **job_end_date**: Enter the date the employee ended their job (if applicable) in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **employer_address**: Enter the address of the employee's current or previous employer.
* **job_status**: Select the current job status of the employee:
	+ active
	+ inactive
	+ terminated
	+ retired
* **employment_verification_date**: Enter the date of the employment verification.
* **verification_method**: Specify the method used to confirm the employment status.
* **verification_source**: Describe the source of the verification information.
* **employee_confirmation**: Provide a statement confirming that the information provided is accurate.
* **employer_confirmation**: Provide a statement confirming that the employment information is accurate.
* **verification_note**: Additional comments or notes about the employment verification (optional).
* **form_submitter**: Enter your name and title for verification purposes.
* **employee_id**: Enter the employee ID number (if applicable).
* **employer_id**: Enter the employer ID number (if applicable).
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee being verified.
* **employer_name_verification**: Enter the name of the employer verification.
* **job_title_verification**: Enter the job title verification (if applicable).
* **job_start_date_verification**: Enter the start date verification (if applicable).
* **job_end_date_verification**: Enter the end date verification (if applicable).
* **job_status_verification**: Enter the job status verification (if applicable).
* **employment_verification_date_verification**: Enter the verification date verification (if applicable).
* **verification_method_verification**: Enter the verification method verification (if applicable).
* **verification_source_verification**: Enter the source of verification verification (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* This form is for employment verification purposes only, so only enter information related to employment.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your HR or supervisor for clarification.
* Make sure to keep a record of the employment verification form for future reference.
