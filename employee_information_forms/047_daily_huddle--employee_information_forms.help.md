# Daily Huddle - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Huddle form is used to collect and record employee information for daily operations. This form gathers essential details such as employee name, contact information, work schedule, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your employee details as required.
2. Ensure you fill out all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your official email address used for official communication.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of your daily activities.
* **Time** (5, time, required): Enter the time of your daily activities.
* **Note** (6, note, required): Enter a brief description or note about your daily activities.
* **Another Note** (7, note, required): Enter another brief description or note about your daily activities.
* **Category** (8, select_one, required): Select a category or label for your daily activities.
* **Category Selected** (9, text, required): Select a value from the category options you chose.
* **Select Multiple** (10, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list.
* **Boolean** (11, text, required): Enter true if the option is true or false if the option is false.
* **Decimal** (12, number, required): Enter a decimal value, e.g., 1.1.
* **Integer** (13, number, required): Enter an integer value, e.g., 1.
* **Another Integer** (14, number, required): Enter another integer value, e.g., 2.
* **Date Range** (15, text, required): Enter a date range in the format 'from' to 'to', e.g., 01-01-2020 to 01-31-2020.
* **Date Range Selected** (16, text, required): Enter the date range you selected.
* **Date To** (17, date, required): Enter the date you are working up to.
* **Time From** (18, time, required): Enter the time you start your activities.
* **Time To** (19, time, required): Enter the time you finish your activities.
* **Date Time** (20, text, required): Enter a date and time in the format '01-01-2020 01:00'.
* **Date Time From** (21, text, required): Enter the date and time you start your activities.
* **Date Time To** (22, text, required): Enter the date and time you finish your activities.
* **Date Time Range Selected** (23, text, required): Enter the date and time range you selected.
* **Another Time** (24, time, required): Enter another time value, e.g., 01:00.
* **Another Integer** (25, number, required): Enter another integer value, e.g., 3.
