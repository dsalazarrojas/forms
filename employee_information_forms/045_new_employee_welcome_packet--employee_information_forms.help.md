# New Employee Welcome Packet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Employee Welcome Packet form is designed to collect essential information about new employees, ensuring a seamless onboarding process and facilitating effective communication with HR and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, using your real name and employee information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Employee Welcome Packet** (`new_employee_welcome_packet_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form. You can leave this field blank as it's not required.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): If you have been assigned an employee ID, please enter it. This will be used for internal tracking purposes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): If you are aware of your start date, please enter it. This will help HR schedule necessary onboarding activities.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Please enter the department you will be working in, as assigned by your supervisor.
* **Handbook Acknowledgments** (`handbook_acknowledgments`, note, optional): This section is for acknowledging that you have reviewed and understand the company handbook. Please sign and date it when you've completed the review.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their name and contact information (e.g., phone number and address).

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with HR or your supervisor.
* This form will be kept on file for internal use only; it's not intended for external sharing or use.
