# ICT New User Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICT New User Form is designed to collect information about new employees joining the organization for internal purposes such as personnel administration, communication, and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Employee Details section with your personal information, including name, job title, department, and reporting relationships.
2. In the Emergency Contact section, provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, relationship, and ID.
3. If applicable, select the tool you have been assigned from the list provided.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Employee Details
* **Employee Details** (`new_user_form`, text, required/optional): This section includes general employee information, such as name, job title, department, and reporting relationships.
* **Role and Position** (`role_and_position`, select_one, required/optional): Select your job role and position from the list provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, required/optional): Enter your reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Team** (`reporting_team`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your reporting team from the list provided.
* **Reporting Level** (`reporting_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your reporting level from the list provided.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your job end date.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter your work location.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Work Phone** (`manager_work_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's work phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required/optional): Select your emergency contact from the list provided.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **Emergency Contact ID** (`emergency_contact_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's ID.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool you have been assigned from the list provided.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your HR or IT representative.
* This form is for internal use only, and any information collected through this form will be used for personnel administration and reporting purposes only.
