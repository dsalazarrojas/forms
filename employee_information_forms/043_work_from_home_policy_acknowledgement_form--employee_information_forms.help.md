# Work From Home Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather acknowledgement from employees when they are working remotely or from home. This form is used to confirm that employees have received and understood the work from home policy and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you are filling it out for the correct purpose.
2. If you are working from home, please sign the form to acknowledge that you have received and understood the work from home policy.
3. If you have any comments or questions about the policy, please add them to the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (text, required/optional: false, field 1):
This field is not a required field but can be filled out for your identification purposes.
* **Acknowledgement** (select multiple, required: true, field 2):
Select one or more of the following options to acknowledge that you have received and understood the work from home policy:
	+ Option 1: Yes, I have received and understood the work from home policy.
	+ Option 2: No, I have not received and understood the work from home policy.
	+ Option 3: I'm still reading through the policy.
* **Comments** (note, required: false, field 3):
This field is for any comments or questions you may have about the policy.
* **Email** (email, required: false, field 4):
Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false, field 5):
Enter your work phone number.
* **Agreement** (note, required: false, field 6):
This field is left empty intentionally and should not be filled out.
* **Date Signed** (date, required: false, field 7):
Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date Received** (date, required: false, field 8):
Enter the date you received this form.
* **Date Returned** (date, required: false, field 9):
Enter the date you returned this form.
* **Signature** (text, required: false, field 10):
This field is for your signature.
* **Manager Signature** (text, required: false, field 11):
This field is for your manager's signature.
* **Employee Signature** (text, required: false, field 12):
This field is for your employee signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out this form for the correct purpose only.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
