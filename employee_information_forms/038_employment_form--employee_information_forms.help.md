# Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Form is a form for employees to provide information about their employment details for internal record-keeping and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form sections and fields carefully.
2.  Ensure that each required field is filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: The name of this field is self-explanatory - it is used for the name of your job or position title.
 * employment_form_employee_details**: This section is used for providing your employee details such as name, address, and other relevant details.

* **Workplace Location**: Enter the address or location of your workplace where you perform your duties.
 * employment_form_work_location: This is the location where you work.

* **Work Date**: Select the date you started working at your current position.
 * employment_form_work_date: This date should be the start date of your current employment.

* **Work Time**: Enter the time you started working at your current position.
 * employment_form_work_time: This time should be the start time of your current employment.

* **Job Title**: Enter the title of your job or position.
 * employment_form_job_title: This is the name of your job or position.

* **Employer**: Enter your employer's name or company name.
 * employment_form_employer: This is your employer's name or company.

* **Employment Type**: Select the type of employment you are under (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, etc).
 * employment_form_employment_type: Choose from one of the provided options for employment type.

* **Employment Start Date**: Enter the start date of your employment.
 * employment_form_employment_start_date: This is the date when you started employed.

* **Employment End Date**: Enter the end date of your employment (if applicable).
 * employment_form_employment_end_date: This date is for employment that has ended.

* **Employment Duration**: Enter the duration of your employment in months or years.
 * employment_form_employment_duration: This is the length of time you have been employed.

* **Employment Status**: Select the status of your employment (e.g. Active, Inactive, etc).
 * employment_form_employment_status: Choose from one of the provided options for employment status.

* **Employer Address**: Enter the address of your employer (if applicable).
 * employment_form_employer_address: This is the address of your employer.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness of information.
