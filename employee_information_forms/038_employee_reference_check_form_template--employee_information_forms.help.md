# Employee Reference Check Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Reference Check Form Template is used to gather honest opinions from a reference about an employee's performance, behavior, and other relevant details. This helps in verifying the accuracy of the information provided by the employee during the hiring process or performance evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Select the appropriate questions relevant to the employee and fill out the contact details of the person providing the reference.
*   Answer the questions honestly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
*   If you need to provide uploaded documents, select the "Yes" option.
*   Sign and date the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, required: false)
    This field is for the contact information of the person providing the reference. Please fill in their name, title, company, and contact number.
*   **Questions** (`Questions`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select all the questions that apply to the employee you are referencing. You can choose multiple options if necessary.
*   **Questions 2** (`Questions 2`, text, required: false)
    This field is for any additional comments or questions that you may have about the employee that are not listed above.
*   **Questions 3** (`Questions 3`, text, required: false)
    This field is also for any additional comments or questions that you may have about the employee that are not listed above.
*   **Questions 4** (`Questions 4`, select_one, required: true)
    Answer this question based on your honest opinion. Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad?
*   **Questions 5** (`Questions 5`, select_one, required: true)
    Answer this question based on your honest opinion. Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad?
*   **Comments** (`Comments`, note, required: false)
    This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the employee.
*   **Signature** (`Signature`, date, required: false)
    Sign and date this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
*   **Uploaded Documents** (`Uploaded Documents`, select_multiple, required: false)
    If you have any relevant documents, please select "Yes" to indicate that they have been uploaded or will be uploaded.
