# employee_availability_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee availability information for work purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the start date of your availability.
3. Select the end date of your availability.
4. Choose which days you are available during your selected start and end dates.
5. Enter your start time.
6. Enter your end time.
7. Add any additional notes if needed.
8. Provide your email address.
9. Provide your phone number.
10. Enter the tool that this availability is for.
11. Enter the person or team this availability is assigned to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to work.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the date you are no longer available to work.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, select_multiple, required): Choose which days of the week you are available to work.
*   *Monday*
*   *Tuesday*
*   *Wednesday*
*   *Thursday*
*   *Friday*
*   *Saturday*
*   *Sunday*
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the time you start work on your selected date.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the time you end work on your selected date.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional information or comments about your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Enter the tool or project this availability is for.
* **To** (`assigned_to`, text, required): Enter the person or team this availability is assigned to.
* 

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it's recommended to fill out all fields for a more comprehensive submission.
