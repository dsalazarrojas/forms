# Reimbursement Form Team - Help Guide
## Purpose
This reimbursement form is designed for team members to submit reimbursement requests for expenses incurred during work-related activities. Please ensure that you have all necessary documents and receipts attached before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form in a private setting, such as your home or office, as it is not recommended to do so in public areas where you may be overheard or observed.
2. Be honest and accurate in filling out the information. Inaccurate or misleading information may lead to form rejection or further investigation.
3. Use this form for legitimate work-related expenses only. Personal expenses, non-work-related activities, or unauthorized expenses will not be reimbursed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documentation (e.g., ID, passport, etc.)
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documentation
* **Email** (3): Enter your company email address or any other email that can be verified by our team. This helps us reach out to you for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Phone** (4): Enter your work or a verified phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date** (5): Enter the date of the expense in the format of MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/15/2024). This is the date when you actually incurred the expense.
* **Time** (6): Enter the time of the day when you incurred the expense in the format of HH:MM AM/PM (e.g., 08:00 AM).
* **Note** (7): Enter a brief description of the expense, including any necessary details or context.
* **Select One** (8): Choose one of the provided options to describe the type of expense you incurred (e.g., meals, transportation, etc.).
