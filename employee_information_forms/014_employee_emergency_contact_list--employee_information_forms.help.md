# Employee Emergency Contact List - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Emergency Contact List form is used to collect information about an employee's emergency contacts, including their availability to be contacted in case of an emergency, phone numbers, email, and other contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your emergency contacts, such as their availability, phone numbers, and email addresses.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can contact the right people in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_ecl_info_1**: This is a note field that likely contains some general information or instructions about the emergency contact list. Please provide any necessary details or guidance.
* **employee_ecl_info_2**: This is a text field where you can enter any relevant information about your emergency contacts.
* **employee_ecl_info_3**: This field asks whether you are available to be contacted in case of an emergency. Please answer "Yes" if you are available.
* **employee_ecl_info_4**: This field is similar to employee_ecl_info_3, but allows you to select multiple options. Please answer "Yes" for any contacts that you are available to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **employee_ecl_info_5**: This is an email field where you can enter the email addresses of your emergency contacts.
* **employee_ecl_info_6**: This is a number field where you can enter any relevant phone numbers of your emergency contacts.
* **employee_ecl_info_7**: This is another number field, possibly for alternative phone numbers or additional contact details.
* **employee_ecl_info_8**: This is a date field, possibly for recording the date of last contact or emergency contact.
* **employee_ecl_info_9**: This is a time field, possibly for recording the time of last contact or emergency contact.

Note: This form is designed for optional completion, and the fields are not marked as required. Please only complete this form if you feel it's necessary or helpful for your emergency contact information.
