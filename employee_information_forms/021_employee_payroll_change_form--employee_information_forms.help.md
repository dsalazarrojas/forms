# Employee Payroll Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Payroll Change Form is used to make changes to an employee's payroll information. This form is used to update an employee's pay rate, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form.
2. Enter the Employee ID of the employee whose payroll information is to be changed.
3. Enter the new Employee Pay Rate.
4. Optionally, select the Payroll Change Date for tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID**: Enter the unique identifier for the employee whose payroll information is to be changed.
* **Employee Pay Rate**: Enter the new pay rate for the employee. This is a required field.
* **Payroll Change Date**: Select the date when the change is to be applied to the employee's payroll. This field is optional but recommended for tracking purposes.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the employee's original payroll information before making any changes.
* Check with HR/Finance teams before completing this form to ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals.
* This form will be reviewed and validated by HR/Finance teams before being implemented.
