# Recruitment Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a job requisition for recruitment purposes, capturing essential information about a job opening in the company. It is used to provide accurate and detailed information about the job requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the job title and position in the "Job Position" field.
2. Choose a department from the available options in the "Department" field.
3. Select the job category from the available options in the "Job Category" field.
4. Enter the salary range in the "Salary Range" field.
5. Select the location where the job is based in the "Location" field.
6. Enter the end date of the job posting in the "Job Posting End Date" field.
7. Choose whether the job is "Yes" or "No" as per the job type in the "Job Type" field.
8. Select the status of the job as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "Job Status" field.
9. Choose the job level as "High" or "Low" in the "Job Level" field.
10. Select whether the job has a "Yes" or "No" family in the "Job Family" field.
11. Select whether the job has a "Yes" or "No" function in the "Job Function" field.
12. Choose the grade of the job as "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Grade" field.
13. Enter the reporting line in the "Reporting Line" field.
14. Choose the classification of the job as "Yes" or "No" in the "Job Classification" field.
15. Enter the job family description in the "Job Family Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Position**: Enter the name of the job position you are trying to fill.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job position.
* **Department**: Select the department that the job belongs to.
* **Job Category**: Select the category of the job.
* **Salary Range**: Enter the salary range for the job.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the job is based.
* **Job Posting End Date**: Enter the end date of the job posting.
* **Job Type**: Choose whether the job is "Yes" or "No".
* **Job Status**: Select the status of the job as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Job Level**: Choose the level of the job as "High" or "Low".
* **Job Family**: Select whether the job has a "Yes" or "No" family.
* **Job Function**: Select whether the job has a "Yes" or "No" function.
* **Job Grade**: Choose the grade of the job as "Yes" or "No".
* **Reporting Line**: Enter the reporting line for the job.
* **Job Classification**: Choose the classification of the job as "Yes" or "No".
* **Job Family Description**: Enter the description of the job family.
