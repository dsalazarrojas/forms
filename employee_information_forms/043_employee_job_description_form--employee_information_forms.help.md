# employee_job_description_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_job_description_form is a tool used to document an employee's job title, department, and working hours. This form is used to ensure that all employees have a clear understanding of their roles and responsibilities, which is vital for employee development, performance assessment, and organizational planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "employee_job_description_form" template from the available options.
2. Fill out each field as follows:
	* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the title of the job or position you hold.
	* **Department** (`department`): Type in the department where you work.
	* **Photo** (`photo`): If you have a photo ID or any other image, please upload it. This field is optional.
	* **Working Hours** (`working_hours`): Describe your normal working hours, including start and end time, and any breaks or exceptions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter a brief description of your job title, such as "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".
* **Department** (`department`): Type in the department where you work, such as "IT" or "Finance".
* **Photo** (`photo`): If you have a photo ID or any other image that can help identify you, please upload it. This field is optional.
* **Working Hours** (`working_hours`): Describe your normal working hours, including start and end time, and any breaks or exceptions. For example, "9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday".

## Tips
* Be as accurate and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the support team.
* This form is an essential tool for employee development and performance assessment, so please fill it out thoroughly and accurately.
