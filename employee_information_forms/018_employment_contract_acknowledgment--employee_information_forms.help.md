# Employment Contract Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Contract Acknowledgment form is used to verify that an employee has received and acknowledged an employment contract, ensuring they understand and agree to its terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by your HR department.
2. In the "Contract Terms" field, provide a brief summary of the employment contract you have received, explaining that you have reviewed and understand its terms.
3. Enter the date you received the employment contract in the "Received Contract Date" field.
4. Enter the date you are acknowledging receipt of the contract in the "Acknowledgment Date" field.
5. In the "Acknowledgement Form Submitted By" field, enter the name of the person submitting the form (usually the employee).
6. Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who verified that you have received the contract and its terms in the "Submitted To Manager" field.
7. Enter the date the manager verified that you have received the contract and its terms in the "Manager Verification Date" field.
8. Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who verified that you have received the contract and its terms in the "Manager Verification" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employment Contract Acknowledgment** (`employment_contract_acknowledgment`, text, required): This is the main section where you provide a brief summary of the contract you have received.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID number assigned by your HR department.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required): Describe the terms of the contract you have reviewed and understand.
* **Received Contract Date** (`received_contract_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the employment contract.
* **Acknowledgment Date** (`acknowledgment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are acknowledging receipt of the contract.
* **Acknowledgement Form Submitted By** (`acknowledgement_form_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted To Manager** (`submitted_to_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who verified that you have received the contract and its terms.
* **Manager Verification** (`manager_verification`, text, required): Enter the name of the manager or supervisor who verified that you have received the contract and its terms.
* **Manager Verification Date** (`manager_verification_date`, date, required): Enter the date the manager verified that you have received the contract and its terms.
* **Acknowledgement Form Verification** (`acknowledgement_form_verification`, text, required): This is not a field that actually exists in the form.
