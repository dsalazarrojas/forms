# labour_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The labour registration form is used to gather and store important employee information for official records and purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)** (`text`, required: false): This field is used to enter the employee's full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Employee Number (2)** (`number`, required: false): This field is used to enter your unique employee identification number. If you don't have an employee number, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
* **Prophet's Name (3)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether the employee is a prophet. Please select 'Yes' if applicable.
* **Employment Status (4)** (`select_one`, required: false): This field indicates the employee's current employment status. Please select 'active' if the employee is currently employed, 'inactive' if they are not, or 'unknown' if you are unsure.
* **Job Title (5)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the employee's job title. Please choose from the available options.
* **Department (6)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the employee's department. Please choose from the available options.
* **Branch (7)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the employee's branch. Please choose from the available options.
* **Job Type (8)** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field is used to select the employee's job type. Please choose from the available options.
* **Start Date (9)** (`date`, required: false): This field is used to enter the employee's start date of employment. Please enter in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date (10)** (`date`, required: false): This field is used to enter the employee's end date of employment. Please enter in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Work Hours (11)** (`number`, required: false): This field is used to enter the employee's expected work hours per day. Please enter a numerical value.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to your HR representative or manager for assistance.
* Please note that some fields may be mandatory, depending on company policies and procedures.
