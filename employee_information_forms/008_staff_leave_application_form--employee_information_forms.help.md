# Staff Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by employees to apply for leave from work. It helps the HR department process leave requests efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your employee name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your official employee ID or personnel file.
2. **Enter your job title**: Please enter your current job title or position within the organization.
3. **Choose leave start date**: Select the date you would like to start your leave from.
4. **Choose leave end date**: Select the date you would like to end your leave by.
5. **Select leave type**: Choose the type of leave you are applying for (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, or Medical Leave).
6. **Select leave duration**: Choose how long you would like your leave to be (Single Day, Multiple Days, or Leave Duration).
7. **Enter reason for leave**: Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
8. **Add comments**: You can add any additional comments or notes regarding your leave request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Your official name as it appears on your employee ID or personnel file.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your current job title or position within the organization.
* **Leave Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The date you would like to start your leave from.
* **Leave End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): The date you would like to end your leave by.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of leave you are applying for (Annual Leave, Sick Leave, or Medical Leave).
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select how long you would like your leave to be (Single Day, Multiple Days, or Leave Duration).
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation for your leave request.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): You can add any additional comments or notes regarding your leave request.
