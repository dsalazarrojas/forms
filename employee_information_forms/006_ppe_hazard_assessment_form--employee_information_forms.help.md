# ppe_hazard_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is to assist users in filling out the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Hazard Assessment Form, a document used to record and manage potential hazards and risks associated with personal protective equipment in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Employee Information**: Provide your basic information, including employee name and ID.
2.  **Equipment Location**: Select the equipment location from the provided options.
3.  **PPE Type**: Choose all relevant PPE types that apply to the hazard.
4.  **PPE Inspection Details**: Enter the frequency of inspections, last inspection date, and next inspection date for the PPE.
5.  **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the PPE or hazard.
6.  **Submission and Assignment**: Select the status of the form and add any comments or assigned tool.
7.  **Hazard Assessment**: Evaluate the hazard level and provide a description of the hazard.
8.  **Recommendations**: Specify the recommended and prohibited PPE.
9.  **Signatures**: Sign and date to confirm submission and completion of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name (1)**: Enter your name for record-keeping purposes.
    *   **Employee ID (2)**: Provide your employee ID number for identification.
    *   **Supervisor Name (3)**: Enter the name of your immediate supervisor.
    *   **Supervisor ID (4)**: Provide the supervisor's ID number for identification.
*   **Equipment Location (5)**: Select the location where the PPE is being used.
*   **PPE Type (6)**: Choose all relevant PPE types that apply to the hazard.
*   **PPE Inspection Frequency (7)**: Enter the frequency of PPE inspections (e.g., daily, weekly).
*   **PPE Inspection Last Date (8)**: Record the last date of PPE inspection.
*   **PPE Inspection Next Date (9)**: Enter the next scheduled inspection date for the PPE.
*   **Notes (10)**: Add any additional comments or information about the PPE or hazard.
*   **Submitted By (11)**: Enter your name for submission and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Submitted Date (12)**: Record the date of submission.
*   **Assigned To (13)**: Enter the name of the person assigned to review or action the form.
*   **Assigned Date (14)**: Record the date of assignment.
*   **Status (15)**: Select the current status of the form (e.g., new, pending review, completed).
*   **Comments (16)**: Add any comments or feedback about the form.
*   **Hazard Level (17)**: Evaluate the hazard level as low, moderate, high, or critical.
*   **Hazard Description (18)**: Provide a description of the hazard.
*   **Recommended PPE (19)**: Specify the recommended PPE for the hazard.
*   **PPE Prohibited (20)**: Indicate if any PPE is prohibited.
*   **Supervisor Signature (21)**: Sign and date to confirm review and approval.
*   **Supervisor Date (22)**: Record the date of review and approval.
*   **Employee Signature (23)**: Sign and date to confirm completion of the form.
*   **Employee Date (24)**: Record the date of completion.
*   **Assigned Tool (25)**: Enter the name of the tool assigned for review or action.

## Tips

*   Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate and complete hazard assessment.
*   Use the provided options for fields to ensure consistency and accuracy.
*   Review and submit the form according to the established process and deadlines.
