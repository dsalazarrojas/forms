# Emergency Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Absence Form is used by employees to report their absences due to emergency or other unforeseen circumstances. This form allows employees to provide essential information about their absence, enabling their organization to take necessary steps to ensure their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are reporting your absence accurately and completely.
2. Provide your Employee ID, which can be found on your ID badge or in your personnel file.
3. Enter a clear and concise reason for your absence in the "Reason for Absence" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of your absence in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Indicate the duration of your absence in the "Duration of Absence" field.
6. If applicable, provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
7. Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person to you.
8. Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
9. Enter the emergency contact person's email address.
10. Enter the emergency contact person's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1, Number, Required):
	+ This field is used to identify you as an employee. Please enter your employee ID number.
* **Reason for Absence** (2, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to explain the reason for your absence. Please enter a clear and concise description of the reason.
* **Start Date** (3, Date, Optional):
	+ This field is used to specify the start date of your absence.
* **End Date** (4, Date, Optional):
	+ This field is used to specify the end date of your absence.
* **Duration of Absence** (5, Number, Optional):
	+ This field is used to indicate the duration of your absence in number of days.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (6, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to provide the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to the Employee** (7, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to specify the relationship of the emergency contact person to you (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (8, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (9, Email, Optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (10, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about a field, please contact your HR or supervisor for assistance.
* Please note that this form is confidential, and the information will only be used for official purposes.
* If you have any concerns or need clarification, feel free to reach out to your HR representative.
