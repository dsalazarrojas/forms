# Team Member Payment Update Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Payment Update Form is used to update the payment details of a team member. This form is intended for use by authorized personnel within the organization and is an essential tool for maintaining accurate financial records.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team member's name in the **team_member_name** field.
2. Input the new pay rate of the team member in the **new_pay_rate** field. This is where you'll enter the updated remuneration details.
3. Select the effective date of the change in the **effective_date** field. This will be the date when the updated pay rate will take effect.
4. Provide a reason for the update in the **reason_for_update** field. This will help in understanding the context of the change.
5. Enter the old pay rate of the team member in the **old_pay_rate** field. If the previous rate is not applicable, enter "Not Applicable".
6. Select the new pay status of the team member in the **new_pay_status** field. This field may be used for specific organizational reasons.
7. Enter your signature as the manager in the **manager_signature** field. This is a required step to authenticate the update.
8. Date and sign the form with the current date in the **date_signed** field.
9. Enter an additional reason for the update, if necessary, in the **reason_for_update_2** field.
10. Enter the team member's email address in the **employee_email** field.
11. Enter the team member's phone number in the **employee_phone** field.
12. Enter the team member's unique ID in the **team_member_id** field. This may be an employee ID or other unique identifier.
13. Enter your name as the manager in the **manager_name** field.
14. Enter your email address as the manager in the **manager_email** field.
15. Enter your phone number as the manager in the **manager_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_member_name** (team_member_name, text, required): Enter the name of the team member whose payment details you are updating.
* **new_pay_rate** (new_pay_rate, number, required): Enter the updated pay rate of the team member.
* **effective_date** (effective_date, date, required): Enter the effective date of the pay update, i.e., the date when the updated rate takes effect.
* **reason_for_update** (reason_for_update, text, required): Provide a reason for the update in this field.
* **old_pay_rate** (old_pay_rate, text, required): If the previous rate is not applicable, enter "Not Applicable".
* **new_pay_status** (new_pay_status, text, required): Select the new pay status of the team member. 
* **manager_signature** (manager_signature, text, required): Enter your signature as the manager.
* **date_signed** (date_signed, date, required): Date and sign the form with the current date.
* **reason_for_update_2** (reason_for_update_2, text, optional): Enter an additional reason for the update if necessary.
* **employee_email** (employee_email, email, optional): Enter the team member's email address.
* **employee_phone** (employee_phone, text, optional): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **team_member_id** (team_member_id, text, required): Enter the team member's unique ID.
* **manager_name** (manager_name, text, required): Enter your name as the manager.
* **manager_email** (manager_email, email, optional): Enter your email address as the manager.
* **manager_phone** (manager_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the manager.

## Tips
Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it, ensuring all required fields are filled out correctly. Also, double-check the team member's details before proceeding with the update.
