# Hotel Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from new staff members to facilitate their onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email, and job title.
2. Provide your availability for shifts, selecting the days and times you are available to work.
3. Enter your emergency contact information, including their name and contact details.
4. Enter your manager's details, including their name and contact information.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title, such as Front Desk Staff or Housekeeper.
* **Department** (`Department`, `text`, required): Enter the department you will be working in, such as Front Desk or Housekeeping.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Start Date** (`Job Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Job Start Time** (`Job Start Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you started your job.
* **Job Duration** (`Job Duration`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you are expected to work.
* **Shift Pattern** (`Shift Pattern`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your regular shift pattern, which is the default times you are expected to work during the week. You can select from the following options:
	+ 'Mon - Thu: 08:00 - 16:00'
	+ 'Fri - Mon: 10:00 - 18:00'
	+ 'Sat - Sun: 10:00 - 18:00'
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days and times you are available to work. This field is different from the Shift Pattern field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Manager Name** (`Manager Name`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`Manager Email`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`Manager Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your onboarding process.
* **Job Notice** (`Job Notice`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your job notice or termination.
* **Shift End Time** (`Shift End Time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Shift Duration** (`Shift Duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your shift.
* **Manager Note** (`Manager Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments from your manager.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, `text`, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Department Manager** (`Department Manager`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your department manager.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, `date`, optional): This field is automatically generated and not editable.
