# Resume Information Gathering Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resume Information Gathering Form is used to collect detailed information about an employee's work history, job title, job preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Please fill in your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone number field.
5. Select your job title from the list of available options.
6. Enter your current company in the company field.
7. If you have a resume, upload it in the resume attachment field.
8. If you have not been with your current employer, you can select the length of time you have worked from the options.
9. If you have not been with your current employer, you can also explain the reason for your departure in the work end reason field.
10. Select your work experience from the options.
11. If you have any other relevant work experience, you can describe it in the work experience other field.
12. Select your area of education in the education field.
13. Select your skills from the list of available options.
14. Select your job preferences from the options.
15. Select your employment status from the options.
16. Select your job status from the options.
17. Enter your work location in the work location field.
18. Enter the date you started working in the work start date field.
19. Enter the date you stopped working in the work end date field.
20. Select the reason for your departure from the options.
21. If you have any other job title, enter it in the job title other field.
22. If you have a resume URL, enter it in the resume URL field.
23. Select your current work status from the options.
24. Select your job type from the options.
25. Select your job level from the options.
25. Select your job category from the options.
25. Select your job function from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the list of available options.
* **Company**: Enter the company you are currently working for.
* **Resume Attachment**: If you have a resume, upload it in this field.
* **Work Experience**: Select the length of time you have worked from the options. If you have not worked for your current employer, you can select "Less than 1 year", "1-2 years", "2-5 years", "5-10 years", or "More than 10 years".
* **Work Experience Other**: If you have any other relevant work experience, you can describe it in this field.
* **Education**: Select your area of education from the options.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you have from the list of available options.
* **Job Preferences**: Select your job preferences from the options.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status from the options.
* **Job Status**: Select your job status from the options.
* **Work Location**: Enter your work location.
* **Work Start Date**: Enter the date you started working.
* **Work End Date**: Enter the date you stopped working.
* **Work End Reason**: Select the reason for your departure from the options.
* **Job Title Other**: If you have any other job title, enter it in this field.
* **Resume URL**: If you have a resume URL, enter it in this field.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status from the options.
* **Job Type**: Select your job type from the options.
* **Job Level**: Select your job level from the options.
* **Job Category**: Select your job category from the options.
* **Job Function**: Select your job function from the options.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
* This form is intended to be a single source of truth for employee information. Please ensure that only one form is completed per employee.
