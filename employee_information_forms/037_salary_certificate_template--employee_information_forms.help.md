# Salary Certificate Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This salary certificate template is designed to provide a standardized way of recording employee information, including their salary details, employee status, and other relevant details. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary information is captured correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, please start by entering the employee's name, which should match the name on their identification documents.
2.  Next, provide the company's name where the employee is employed.
3.  Enter the job title of the employee.
4.  Record the date the employee joined the company.
5.  If the employee is no longer with the company, provide the date they left.
6.  Specify the employee's status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Left.
7.  If the employee left the company, provide the reason for their departure.
8.  Enter the employee's salary details, including the salary amount.
9.  If necessary, provide the notice period given to the employee before their departure.
10.  Enter the date of the last drawal (if applicable).
11.  If applicable, enter the last drawal amount.
12.  Enter the total drawal amount.
13.  Enter the employee's ID number.
14.  Provide the tax payer's name and ID number (if applicable).
15.  Enter the tax ID number (if applicable).
16.  Enter the bank account number.
17.  Enter the bank name.
18.  Enter the branch name.
19.  Enter the bank address.
20.  Enter the account number.
21.  Enter the SWIFT code (if applicable).
22.  Enter the branch code (if applicable).
23.  Specify the account status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Closed.
24.  Enter the account type.
25.  Enter the bank phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Please enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company the employee is employed with.
* **Job Title**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Date of Joining**: Record the date the employee joined the company.
* **Date of Leaving**: If the employee is no longer with the company, enter the date they left.
* **Employee Status**: Select the employee's status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Left.
* **Reason for Leaving**: If the employee left the company, provide the reason for their departure.
* **Salary**: Enter the employee's salary amount.
* **Notice Period**: If necessary, provide the notice period given to the employee before their departure.
* **Last Drawal Date**: Enter the date of the last drawal (if applicable).
* **Last Drawal Amount**: If applicable, enter the last drawal amount.
* **Total Drawal Amount**: Enter the total drawal amount.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Tax Payer**: Provide the tax payer's name and ID number (if applicable).
* **Tax ID**: Enter the tax ID number (if applicable).
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter the bank account number.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of the bank.
* **Branch Name**: Enter the name of the branch.
* **Bank Address**: Enter the bank's address.
* **Account Number**: Enter the account number.
* **SWIFT Code**: Enter the SWIFT code (if applicable).
* **Branch Code**: Enter the branch code (if applicable).
* **Account Status**: Specify the account status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Closed.
* **Account Type**: Enter the account type.
* **Bank Phone**: Enter the bank phone number.

This guide should provide a clear understanding of the form's purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means.
