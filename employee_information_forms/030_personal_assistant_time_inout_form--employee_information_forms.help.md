# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information about personal assistant time-in and time-out activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

- Fill out the form with all required fields. Some fields are optional, but they help us better understand your activities.
- Enter your time-in and time-out activities for each shift.
- Use the rendered hours field to report the number of hours rendered for each shift.
- Use the comments fields to leave any relevant comments about your activities.
- Select the date for the corresponding time-in and time-out activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time In** (`personalassistant_timein`, time, required): Enter the start time of your personal assistant shift.
* **Time Out** (`personalassistant_timeout`, time, required): Enter the end time of your personal assistant shift.
* **Rendered Hours** (`personalassistant_renderedhours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours rendered for the shift.
* **Comments** (`personalassistant_comments`, note, optional): Leave any comments about your activities.
* **Date** (`personalassistant_date`, date, required): Select the date for the corresponding time-in and time-out activities.
* **Personal Assistant Timein 2** (`personalassistant_timein_2`, time, optional): Enter the start time of an additional personal assistant shift (if applicable).
* **Personal Assistant Timeout 2** (`personalassistant_timeout_2`, time, optional): Enter the end time of an additional personal assistant shift (if applicable).
* **Personal Assistant Comments 2** (`personalassistant_comments_2`, note, optional): Leave any comments about the additional personal assistant shift (if applicable).
* **Employee Rendered Hours** (`employee_renderedhours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours rendered for the shift (if not already reported).
* **Note** (`personalassistant_note`, note, optional): Leave any general comments about your activities.
* **Personal Assistant Comments 3** (`personalassistant_comments_3`, note, optional): Leave any comments about your activities.
* **Employee Note** (`employee_note`, note, optional): Leave any comments about the employee's activities.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
