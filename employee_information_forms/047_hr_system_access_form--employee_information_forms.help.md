# HR System Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new hires within an organization, including their job title, department, and date of hire.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the "New Hire" page.
* Fill out the "Job Title" field with the new employee's job title.
* Enter the "Hire Date" using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Select the "Department" field and enter the new employee's department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (New Hire, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the new employee's job title, such as "Software Engineer" or "Marketing Manager".
* **Hire Date** (Hire Date, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date the new employee was hired, in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2023).
* **Department** (Department, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the new employee's department, such as "IT" or "Sales".
