# Employee Direct Deposit Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Direct Deposit Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from employees for direct deposit purposes. This guide will walk you through each page of the form to ensure a smooth and accurate completion process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Details (Page 1)**:
	* Enter your employee name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* Enter your employee ID as provided by your HR department.
2. **Direct Deposit Frequency and Fund Type (Page 2)**:
	* Select your preferred direct deposit frequency from the options provided:
		+ Every Pay Period
		+ Bi-Weekly
		+ Semi-Monthly
		+ Monthly
	* Choose your fund type:
		+ Cash
		+ Checking
		+ Savings
3. **Bank Account Information (Page 3)**:
	* Enter your bank account number as it appears on your check or bank statement.
	* Enter your routing number as provided by your bank.
4. **Additional Fund Details (Page 4-6)**:
	* Enter the start date for your direct deposit funds.
	* Enter the end date for your direct deposit funds, if applicable.
	* Enter any additional funds for direct deposit, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as provided by your HR department.
* **Direct Deposit Frequency**: Choose your preferred direct deposit frequency to receive your paychecks on a regular schedule.
* **Fund Type**: Select the type of fund you wish to use for direct deposit (Cash, Checking, or Savings).
* **Bank Account Number**: Enter your bank account number as it appears on your check or bank statement.
* **Routing Number**: Enter your routing number as provided by your bank.
* **Fund Start Date**: Enter the start date for your direct deposit funds.
* **Fund End Date**: Enter the end date for your direct deposit funds, if applicable.
* **Additional Funds**: Enter any additional funds for direct deposit, if applicable.
* **Additional Fund Start Date**: Enter the start date for any additional direct deposit funds.
* **Additional Fund End Date**: Enter the end date for any additional direct deposit funds, if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your bank account information is correct to avoid any issues with direct deposit.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department for assistance.
