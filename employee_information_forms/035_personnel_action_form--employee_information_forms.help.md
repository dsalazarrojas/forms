# Personnel Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee information and details. It is intended for use in HR-related purposes such as employee records, performance evaluations, and other personnel actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Open the form and start by entering the employee information.
2. Click on the "job details" page and enter the relevant details about the employee's job.
3. Finally, enter the manager's information.
4. Once you have filled out all the required fields, submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee info (employee_info)**: This field is used to enter the employee's basic details such as name, employee ID, and other relevant information.
* **job details (job_details)**: In this field, enter the employee's job title, department, and other job-related details.
* **manager info (manager_info)**: Enter the manager's name and other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact HR for clarification.
* This form is for HR-related purposes only. If you need to use it for other reasons, please consult with HR first.
