# Unpaid Leave Of Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting an unpaid leave of absence from work. Only fill out this form for non-routine absences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's information in the "Employee Information" page.
2. Select a reason for the leave of absence in the "Reason" page.
3. Enter the start and end dates for the leave of absence in the "Start Date" and "End Date" pages.
4. Optionally, provide a detailed explanation for the leave of absence on the "Reason For Leave" page.
5. Sign and date the form as the employee in the "Employee Signature" page.
6. If the manager agrees, sign and date the form as the manager in the "Manager Signature" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Information** (`Employee Information`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your employee information here, including any necessary details as prompted.
- **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Select the reason for the unpaid leave of absence.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the start date of the leave of absence. Please note that the start date must be on or after the current date.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the end date of the leave of absence.
- **Reason For Leave** (`Reason For Leave`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ Optionally, provide a more detailed explanation for the leave of absence.
- **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Sign and date this form as the employee to validate the leave of absence request.
- **Manager Signature** (`Manager Signature`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ If the manager approves, sign and date this form to validate the leave of absence request.
- **Date Manager Signed** (`Date Manager Signed`, `date`, **optional**):
	+ If the manager approves, enter the date they signed and validated the leave of absence request.

## Tips

Please note that this form requires both the employee and manager's signature and date to be completed before sending it for approval and processing. Make sure to sign and date both pages accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with the leave of absence request.
