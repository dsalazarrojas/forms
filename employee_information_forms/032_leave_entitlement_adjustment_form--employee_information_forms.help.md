# Leave Entitlement Adjustment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leave Entitlement Adjustment Form is a form used by employees to request changes to their leave entitlements. It is intended for adjustments such as adding or removing leave entitlements due to various reasons like leave adjustment, leave cancellation or leave carry forward.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct employee ID from the dropdown list.
2. Enter your employee ID (if not already selected).
3. Enter your current leave balance.
4. Enter the leave balance after adjustment.
5. Select the date of adjustment.
6. Provide a reason for the adjustment (optional).
7. Enter the adjustment amount.
8. Enter the total leave and remaining leave balance after the adjustment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information**: Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee ID (Select One)**: Select your employee ID from the list of options.
* **Leave Balance**: Enter your current leave balance.
* **Leave Balance After Adjustment**: Enter your leave balance after adjustment.
* **Date of Adjustment**: Enter the date of adjustment.
* **Reason for Adjustment**: Provide a brief reason for the adjustment (optional).
* **Adjustment Amount**: Enter the adjustment amount.
* **Total Leave**: Enter the total leave balance after adjustment.
* **Remaining Leave**: Enter the remaining leave balance after adjustment.
