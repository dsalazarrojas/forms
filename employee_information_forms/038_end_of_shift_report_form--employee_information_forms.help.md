# End Of Shift Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Shift Report Form is used to document the end of a shift for employees. This form captures essential information about the employee's shift, including their name, shift duration, employee ID, shift end time, and any comments about their shift. The form helps ensure that accurate records are kept and that necessary information is reported.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are in the correct location and time zone for your shift.
2. Enter your employee name in the Employee Name field.
3. Enter the duration of your shift in hours and minutes in the Shift Duration field.
4. Enter your Employee ID to help us identify you.
5. Enter the time when your shift ended in the Shift End Time field.
6. Provide any comments or notes about your shift in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your shift in hours and minutes (e.g., 8:30).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Shift End Time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when your shift ended (e.g., 17:00).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your shift, such as any issues or concerns that need to be addressed.
