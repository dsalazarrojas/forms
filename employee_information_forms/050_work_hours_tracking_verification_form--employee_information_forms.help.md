# work_hours_tracking_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's work hours to track and verify their work periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and job title.
2. Enter the start and end dates of the work period.
3. Enter the start and end times of the work period.
4. Enter the number of hours worked.
5. If applicable, add a note about any breaks taken.
6. Finally, add any comments or notes about the work period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee whose work hours are being tracked.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the employee whose work hours are being tracked.
* **Date Start** (`Date Start`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee's work period started.
* **Time Start** (`Time Start`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the employee's work period.
* **Date End** (`Date End`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the employee's work period ended.
* **Time End** (`Time End`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the employee's work period.
* **Hours Worked** (`Hours Worked`, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of hours worked during the period.
* **Break Duration** (`Break Duration`, text, required/optional): Enter a note about any breaks taken during the work period.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or notes about the work period.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
