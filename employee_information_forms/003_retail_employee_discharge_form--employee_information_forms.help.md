# Retail Employee Discharge Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Retail Employee Discharge Form is used to document the termination of an employee's employment with the retail store, including the reason for discharge and any necessary details. This form helps ensure that the termination process is conducted efficiently and fairly, and provides a clear record of the discharge for both the employee and the store's administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Complete all the fields marked as "required" (e.g. "Reason for Discharge Select" and others marked as such).
2. **Provide specific details**: In fields where a specific answer is requested (e.g. "Employee Id", "Date Of Discharge", "Notice Period Days" and others), enter the specific details as accurately and concisely as possible.
3. **Check for consistency**: Before submitting the form, review your answers to ensure they are consistent and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Store Number
* **Store Number**: Enter the store number where the employee was working.

### Employee Id
* **Employee Id**: Enter the employee ID of the person being discharged.

### Reason for Discharge
* **Reason For Discharge Select**: Select 'Yes' to indicate the discharge is for a reason other than misconduct or performance issues, or 'No' if it's due to misconduct or performance issues. If other is selected, provide additional details in the "Reason for Discharge Other" field.

### Date Of Discharge
* **Date Of Discharge**: Enter the date when the employee's employment will be terminated.

### Notice Period Days
* **Notice Period Days**: Enter the number of days the employee will be given to return company property (e.g. uniform, equipment, company car).

### Return Property
* **Return Property**: Select 'Yes' if you need the employee to return company property, or 'No' if it's not necessary.

### Employee Name
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the person being discharged (if not already known).

### Reason for Discharge Other
* **Reason For Discharge Other**: If you selected 'Yes' for "Reason For Discharge Select" in the previous field, use this field to provide additional details on the reason for discharge (e.g. "Employee was transferred to another location due to company restructuring", etc.).

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurately completed and up-to-date to facilitate a smooth transition and for easy reference in the future.
* If you have questions or need assistance, please reach out to the administrative department.
