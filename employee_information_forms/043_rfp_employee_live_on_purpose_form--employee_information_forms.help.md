# RFP Employee Live On Purpose Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RFP Employee Live On Purpose Form is designed to collect information about an employee's live on purpose, including their job title, department, job start date, job status, and various other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct page to start with. The first page is the "Welcome to RFP" page.
2. Fill out each field in the form, following the instructions and explanations provided for each field.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: This field is for your first name.
- **Last name**: This field is for your last name.
- **Email**: This field is for your work email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Job title**: This field is for your current job title. Select from the options provided.
- **Department**: This field is for your department. Select multiple departments that apply to you.
- **Job start date**: This field is for the date you started your job.
- **Job start time**: This field is for the time you started your job.
- **Job status**: This field is for your current job status. Select from the options provided.
- **Note**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about your job status.
- **Email verified**: This field is for indicating whether your email has been verified.
- **Phone**: This field is for your work phone number.
- **Manager**: This field is for your manager's name. Select from the options provided.
- **Team**: This field is for the team you belong to. Select multiple teams that apply to you.
- **Location**: This field is for the location you work from. Select multiple locations that apply to you.
- **Job Title Manager**: This field is for your job title manager. Select from the options provided.
- **Department Manager**: This field is for your department manager. Select from the options provided.
- **Employee status**: This field is for your current employee status. Select multiple statuses that apply to you.
- **Reporting manager**: This field is for your reporting manager's name. Select from the options provided.
- **Reporting department**: This field is for your reporting department. Select multiple departments that apply to you.
- **Reporting team**: This field is for your reporting team. Select multiple teams that apply to you.
- **Reporting level**: This field is for your reporting level. Select multiple levels that apply to you.
- **Reporting date**: This field is for the date you started reporting to your manager.
- **Reporting time**: This field is for the time you started reporting to your manager.
- **Note 2**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about your reporting status.
- **Phone**: This field is for your work phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information required before filling out the form.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or questions, please ask your HR representative or manager for clarification.
