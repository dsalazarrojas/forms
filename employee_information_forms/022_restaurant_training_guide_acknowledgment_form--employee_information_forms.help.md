# Restaurant Training Guide Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge that an employee has read and understood the restaurant training guide, and to provide their name and title for record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter your Title in the text field provided.
3. Select the Date you have read and understood the restaurant training guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your title or position in the restaurant (e.g., Server, Manager, Chef).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you have read and understood the restaurant training guide. This will be stored for record-keeping purposes. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have actually read and understood the restaurant training guide before acknowledging it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or training department.
* Make sure to enter your information accurately, as this form is for record-keeping purposes.
* If you are completing this form for someone else, please ensure that they are aware that they are acknowledging the guide and that the information provided is accurate.
