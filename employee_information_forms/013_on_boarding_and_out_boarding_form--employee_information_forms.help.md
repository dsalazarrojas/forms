# On Boarding And Out Boarding Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The On Boarding And Out Boarding Form is designed to collect essential information for both onboarding (welcoming new employees) and offboarding (bidding farewell to departing employees) processes. This form helps HR departments or relevant personnel manage the necessary paperwork and details for smooth transitions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the following sections:
	* Employee Information (page 1)
	* On Boarding Date (page 2)
	* On Boarding Time (page 3)
	* Out Boarding Date (page 4)
	* Out Boarding Time (page 5)
2. For each section, provide accurate and relevant information as prompted.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name, ID number, or any other relevant identifier to help track employee details.
* **On Boarding Date** (`on_boarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the employee's onboarding or joining the company.
* **On Boarding Time** (`on_boarding_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the employee's onboarding or joining the company.
* **Out Boarding Date** (`out_boarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the employee's offboarding or leaving the company.
* **Out Boarding Time** (`out_boarding_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the employee's offboarding or leaving the company.

## Tips
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Review all pages carefully and make sure to complete all required fields.
- Ensure that all information is up-to-date and relevant to the employee's onboarding or offboarding process.
