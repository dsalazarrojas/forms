# Tip Income Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to report their shift and workday information, as well as their tip income, for a given pay period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the fields on each page as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct shift and workday information for the pay period.
3. Report your tip income for the pay period, including any tips received during your shifts.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift and Workday (1)**: Please enter the number of the shift and workday you are reporting for.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Shift Start Time (3)**: Enter the start time of your shift, using the `HH:MM` format.
* **Shift End Time (4)**: Enter the end time of your shift, using the `HH:MM` format.
* **Tip Income (5)**: Report the total amount of tips you received during your shifts, in dollars.
* **Total Tips Received (6)**: Report the total amount of tips you received during your shifts, in dollars.
* **Employee Signature (7)**: Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Employee Date (8)**: Enter the date you are submitting this form, using the `MM/DD/YY` format.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are reporting only the shifts and workdays you actually worked during the pay period.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Remember to submit the form accurately and completely, as it is used to calculate your tip income for the pay period.
