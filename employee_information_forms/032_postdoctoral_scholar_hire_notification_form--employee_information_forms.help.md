# Postdoctoral Scholar Hire Notification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to notify administrators or HR about new postdoctoral scholars and their employment details. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that their information is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you've provided information for all required fields.
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Scholar Information" field, as this field will help administrators or HR understand the scholar's qualifications and background.
3. Select the correct "employment status" of the scholar from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Scholar**: Enter the scholar's name and other relevant details in this text field.
* **Scholar Information**: Provide a brief description of the scholar, including their qualifications, research interests, and any other relevant information.
* **Employment Status**: Select 'Active' to indicate that the scholar is currently employed, 'Inactive' to indicate that they are not currently employed.
* **Job Application ID**: Select 'Yes' if the scholar has applied for a job through the university's internal job board, 'No' otherwise.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date the scholar is expected to start their employment.
* **Start Month**: Select 'Yes' if the scholar is expected to start in the current month, 'No' otherwise.
* **End Date**: Enter the date the scholar's employment is expected to end.
* **Reason for Leaving**: Provide a brief explanation for why the scholar is leaving or terminating their employment.
* **Job Opening Number**: Enter the job opening number associated with the scholar's employment.
* **Job Opening ID**: Select 'Yes' if the job opening is related to the scholar's employment, 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Title**: Select 'Yes' if the scholar has the job title associated with their employment, 'No' otherwise.
* **Department**: Select 'Yes' if the scholar is part of the department related to their employment, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately to avoid any confusion or errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or HR for guidance.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.
