# return_to_work_notification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The return to work notification form is used to notify management and HR of an employee's status and reason for returning to work after a leave of absence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the date the employee is returning to work in the "Return To Work Date" field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the number of hours the employee will be working in the "Work Hours" field.
5. Select the employee's shift in the "Shift" field.
6. Enter the department the employee belongs to in the "Department" field.
7. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
8. Enter the supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
9. Enter the employee's ID in the "Employee ID" field.
10. Provide a reason for returning to work in the "Return To Work Reason" field.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
12. Select the manager's approval status in the "Manager Approval" field.
13. Select the supervisor's approval status in the "Supervisor Approval" field.
14. Select the departmental approval status in the "Departmental Approval" field.
15. Enter the manager's note in the "Manager Note" field.
16. Enter the supervisor's note in the "Supervisor Note" field.
17. Enter the departmental note in the "Departmental Note" field.
18. Select the final approval status in the "Approval Status" field.
19. Enter the date and time of completion in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
20. Enter the name of the person completing this form in the "Completed By" field.
21. Enter the employee ID of the person completing this form in the "Completed By Employee ID" field.
22. Enter the status of the return to work notification in the "Status" field.
23. Enter the name of the person updating this form in the "Updated By" field.
24. Enter the employee ID of the person updating this form in the "Updated By Employee ID" field.

* **Supervisor Approval** (15): Select the supervisor's approval status.
* **Departmental Approval** (16): Select the departmental approval status.
* **Manager Note** (17): Enter the manager's note.
* **Supervisor Note** (18): Enter the supervisor's note.
* **Departmental Note** (19): Enter the departmental note.
* **Approval Status** (20): Select the final approval status.
* **Date Completed** (21): Enter the date of completion.

* **Completed By** (23): Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Completed By Employee ID** (24): Enter the employee ID of the person completing this form.
