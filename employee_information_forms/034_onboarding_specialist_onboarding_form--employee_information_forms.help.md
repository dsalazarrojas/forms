# Onboarding Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Specialist Onboarding Form is a comprehensive form used to collect and store employee information for onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all required information and documents before proceeding.
2.  Start with the employee's **Employee Information** page.
3.  On the **Employee Information** page, provide the employee's details as requested.
4.  Proceed to the next page for further employment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's name as it appears on their ID or government-issued ID.
*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's name in the format used on their ID or government-issued ID.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's email address.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's job title as it appears in the company's records or as provided by HR.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, not required)
    *   Select the department where the employee is currently working or the department they are being onboarded to.
*   **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, `date`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's hire date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Manager** (`manager`, `select_one`, not required)
    *   Confirm whether the employee has a manager.
*   **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, not required)
    *   Confirm the current status of the employee's job.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter a brief description of the employee's job responsibilities.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the employee's contact number or work phone.
*   **Other Job Title** (`job_title_other`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter any other job titles the employee may have.
*   **Manager's Manager** (`manager_manager`, `select_one`, not required)
    *   Confirm whether the employee's manager also has a manager.
*   **Other Job Status** (`job_status_other`, `select_one`, not required)
    *   Enter any other status of the employee's job.
*   **Date of Termination** (`date_of_termination`, `date`, not required)
    *   Enter the date the employee's contract or position was terminated.
*   **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `text`, not required)
    *   Briefly describe the reason for the employee's termination.
*   **Other Department** (`department_other`, `select_multiple`, not required)
    *   Select other departments the employee may be involved with or have contact with.
*   **Other Contact Number** (`contact_number_other`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter any other contact numbers or phones the employee may have.
*   **Other Manager** (`manager_other`, `select_one`, not required)
    *   Confirm whether the employee has an other manager.
*   **Job Title ID** (`job_title_id`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the job title ID as assigned by HR or the system.
*   **Date of Hire** (`date_of_hire`, `date`, not required)
    *   Enter the date the employee was hired in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Department ID** (`department_id`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the department ID as assigned by HR or the system.
*   **Job Description ID** (`job_description_id`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the job description ID as assigned by HR or the system.
*   **Contact Number ID** (`contact_number_id`, `text`, not required)
    *   Enter the contact number ID as assigned by HR or the system.
*   **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `date`, not required)
    *   Enter the date the employee's contract or position was terminated.
*   **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `text`, not required)
    *   Briefly describe the reason for the employee's termination.

Please ensure to fill all required fields accurately and consistently to maintain reliable data and avoid errors.
