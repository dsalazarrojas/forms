# dental_professional_education_leave_of_absence_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and record leave of absence for dental professional's educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you are taking a leave of absence for educational purposes and select the relevant options in the "Dental Program" field.
2. Enter the start and end dates of your education leave in the "Education Start Date" and "Education End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Select the reason for your education leave in the "Reason for Leave" field. Options include "Medical", "Non-Medical", and "Other".
4. Choose the type of leave you are taking in the "Leave Type" field. Options include "Annual Leave", "Sick Leave", and "Other Leave".
5. If necessary, select the current approval status of your leave in the "Approval Status" field. Options include "Pending", "Approved", and "Rejected".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dental Program** (`dental_program`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  This field allows you to select the dental program you are taking leave for. Select all relevant programs that apply.
* **Education Start Date** (`education_start_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the start date of your education leave.
* **Education End Date** (`education_end_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the end date of your education leave.
* **Reason for Leave** (`reason_for_leave`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose the reason for your education leave from the options provided.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Select the current approval status of your education leave. This will be updated by an administrator when they review your request.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Choose the type of leave you are taking for your education purposes. This will help us track your leave history.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
- Make sure to select all relevant options in the "Dental Program" field if applicable.
- If you are unsure about the approval status of your leave, please contact your administrator for assistance.
- Keep a record of your leave history for future reference.
- This form is for internal use only and is subject to change.
