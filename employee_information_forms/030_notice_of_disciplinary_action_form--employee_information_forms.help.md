# Notice Of Disciplinary Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notice Of Disciplinary Action Form is used to record instances of employee misconduct and document the disciplinary actions taken. This form helps to maintain accurate records and ensures that all disciplinary actions are properly documented and communicated with the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason for filling out this form. Double-check your company's policies to avoid duplication.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Use the available options for Disciplinary Action Taken wisely, as this will determine the severity of the action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID (1)**: Enter the unique ID number of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Action Date (2)**: Record the date of the incident or the date the disciplinary action was taken.
* **Incident Summary (3)**: Provide a brief description of the incident that led to the disciplinary action.
* **Action Summary (4)**: Summarize the disciplinary action taken against the employee.
* **Disciplinary Action Taken (5)**: Select one or more of the available options to reflect the disciplinary action taken. These options are:
	+ Verbal Warning: A verbal warning issued to the employee, usually for minor issues.
	+ Written Warning: A written warning issued to the employee, usually for more serious issues.
	+ Final Warning: A final warning, often issued before termination, indicating a more severe disciplinary action.
	+ Suspension: A temporary suspension of the employee's duties or privileges.
	+ Termination: The permanent end of the employee's employment.

## Tips
- Always use accurate and honest information when filling out this form.
- Consult with your supervisor if you're unsure about any field or the disciplinary action taken.
- Review your company's policies before filling out this form.
- Keep the incident summary and action summary brief and to the point.
- Be cautious when selecting the disciplinary action taken, as it will determine the severity of the action.
