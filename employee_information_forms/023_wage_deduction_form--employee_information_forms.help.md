# Wage Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wage Deduction Form is used to collect information for wage deductions, which may be necessary for tax purposes, benefits, or other financial calculations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that the form is not being filled out multiple times. If you are completing this form for the same purpose, use a single submission.
2. Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Deduction Information** (`wage_deduction_form_1`, text, optional): This is a free-text field where you can provide any relevant information about the wage deduction, such as the reason for the deduction or any other details.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee who is being deducted. This field is used to identify the employee.
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee ID number. This field is used to track the employee's information.
- **Amount** (`amount`, text, optional): Enter the amount of the wage deduction. This field should be a numerical value.
- **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the wage deduction, such as the purpose or details.
- **Type** (`type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options: Fixed or Percentage. This field determines the type of wage deduction.
  - **Fixed**: A fixed amount will be deducted from the employee's pay.
  - **Percentage**: A percentage of the employee's pay will be deducted.
- **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple of the following options: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. This field determines how often the wage deduction will be applied.
  - **Daily**: The wage deduction will be applied daily.
  - **Weekly**: The wage deduction will be applied weekly.
  - **Monthly**: The wage deduction will be applied monthly.
- **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the wage deduction will start. This field should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the wage deduction will end. This field should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authorization to complete this form.
