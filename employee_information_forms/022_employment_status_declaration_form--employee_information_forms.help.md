# Employment Status Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Status Declaration Form is a form used to declare one's employment status, including details such as first name, last name, email, phone number, job title, employment status (active, resigned, or retired), start date, end date (if applicable), manager's name, manager's title, and manager's email (if applicable).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the corresponding information. Please make sure to:

- Enter your first and last names in the `First Name` and `Last Name` fields.
- Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
- Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
- If you have a job title, enter it in the `Job Title` field (this field is optional).
- Select your current employment status from the options in the `Employment Status` field.
- Enter your start date in the `Start Date` field.
- If you have an end date, enter it in the `End Date` field.
- If you have a manager, enter their name in the `Manager Name` field (this field is optional).
- If you have a manager's title, enter it in the `Manager Title` field (this field is optional).
- If you have a manager's email, enter it in the `Manager Email` field (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: If you have a job title, enter it here (this field is optional).
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status: Active, Resigned, or Retired.
* **Start Date**: Enter your start date.
* **End Date**: If you have an end date, enter it here (this field is optional).
* **Manager Name**: If you have a manager, enter their name here (this field is optional).
* **Manager Title**: If you have a manager's title, enter it here (this field is optional).
* **Manager Email**: If you have a manager's email, enter it here (this field is optional).
