# Employment Income Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify employee income information for HR purposes, such as payroll processing, benefits administration, or other related tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the field below.
2. Provide the employee's salary.
3. Choose the verification date from the calendar.
4. Select the verification period from the options provided (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months).
5. Choose the method of verification (In-person, Phone, or Video).
6. Select who is performing the verification (HR Manager, Accountant, or HR Representative).
7. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or notes about the verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being verified.
* **employee Salary**: Enter the current salary of the employee.
* **verification Date**: Enter the date on which the verification was completed.
* **verification Period**: Select the duration of the verification period (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months). 
* **verification_method**: Choose the method of verification (In-person, Phone, or Video).
* **verification_by_whom**: Select who performed the verification (HR Manager, Accountant, or HR Representative).
* **employee_position**: Enter the employee's position or role within the company.
* **verification_note** and **verification_note2**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the verification process. 

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to provide reliable verification results.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant stakeholders or HR department. 
* If you need to verify income for multiple employees, please fill out a new form for each employee.
