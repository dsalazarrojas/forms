# prophets_title - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document employee information, including personal details, employment history, and security clearance levels. It is intended for use by internal staff to update or verify employee records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field that has a dropdown or select multiple option.
3. If a field has a "hint", use it as a guide to help you fill in the field correctly.
4. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have entered the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment History** (`employment_history`, text, optional): Briefly describe your work history, including job titles, departments, and dates of employment.
* **Security Clearance Level** (`security_clearance_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of security clearance you currently hold.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more departments where you currently or previously worked.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or previous manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your current or previous manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your current or previous manager.
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check_status`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the current status of your background check.
* **Security Clearance Notes** (`security_clearance_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your security clearance.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager Clearance Level** (`manager_clearance_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of security clearance of your manager.
* **Departmental Clearance Level** (`departmental_clearance_level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of security clearance for your department.
* **Date of Last Hire** (`date_of_last_hire`, date, optional): Enter the date you were last hired.
* **Last Position** (`last_position`, text, optional): Briefly describe your last job title and department.
* **Last Salary** (`last_salary`, number, optional): Enter your last salary amount.
* **Date of Last Salary** (`date_of_last_salary`, date, optional): Enter the date you received your last salary.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Date of Last Hire 2** (`date_of_last_hire_2`, date, optional): Enter the date you were last hired (if applicable).
* **Last Department** (`last_department`, text, optional): Briefly describe the department you last worked in.
* **Last Manager** (`last_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of your last manager.
* **Last Manager Title** (`last_manager_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of your last manager.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional job title you may have.
