# Workplace Safety Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Program Intake Form is designed to collect information from employees about their workplace safety concerns and incidents. This form helps the workplace safety team respond promptly and effectively to potential safety issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and job title as an employee or as the emergency contact person.
2.  Provide your email address for contact purposes.
3.  Describe the incident you are reporting, including the date, time, location, and any injuries or hazards involved.
4.  Indicate the medical condition of the person involved (if applicable).
5.  Describe the first aid procedure administered (if applicable).
6.  Provide any additional safety information or concerns you would like to share with the safety team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the employee to provide their personal details for contact purposes.
    *   Enter your name and job title.
*   **Name (`name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the employee or emergency contact person to enter their name.
*   **Email (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide a valid email address for the employee or emergency contact person.
*   **Job Title (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title as an employee or emergency contact person.
*   **Department (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department where the incident occurred.
*   **Location (`location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
*   **Emergency Contact Information (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is an open field for you to provide any additional emergency contact information.
*   **Contact Name (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact person's name.
*   **Relationship (`relationship`, `select_one`, required: false):
    +   Mother
    +   Father
    +   Spouse
    +   Child
    +   Other
*   **Phone (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Provide the emergency contact person's phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false):
    +   Mother
    +   Father
    +   Spouse
    +   Child
    +   Other
*   **Emergency Contact Job Title (`emergency_contact_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact person's job title.
*   **Emergency Contact Department (`emergency_contact_department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact person's department.
*   **Emergency Contact Location (`emergency_contact_location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location where the emergency contact person is based.
*   **Other (`emergency_contact_other`, `text`, required: false): Provide any other relevant emergency contact information.
*   **Employee Safety Information (`employee_safety_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional safety information or concerns.
*   **Hazards (`hazards`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    +   Slip, Trip, or Fall
    +   Physical Assault
    +   Chemicals
    +   Other
*   **Date of Incident (`date_of_incident`, `date`, required: false): Describe the date of the incident.
*   **Time of Incident (`time_of_incident`, `time`, required: false): Describe the time of the incident.
*   **Location of Incident (`location_of_incident`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location of the incident.
*   **Description of Incident (`description_of_incident`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Medical Condition (`medical_condition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the medical condition of the person involved (if applicable).
*   **First Aid Procedure (`first_aid_procedure`, `text`, required: false): Describe the first aid procedure administered (if applicable).
*   **Employee Safety Information 2 (`employee_safety_info_2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide any further safety information or concerns.
