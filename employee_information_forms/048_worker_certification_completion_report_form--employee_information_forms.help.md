# Worker Certification Completion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Certification Completion Report Form is used to record an employee's status on required training and certification programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select the department the employee belongs to from the **department** dropdown menu.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the **job_title** field.
4. Indicate whether the employee has completed training by checking **Yes** or **No** in the **Has Training Completed** field.
5. Indicate whether the employee has completed certification by checking **Yes** or **No** in the **Has Certification Completed** field.
6. Enter the date the employee completed training in the **Completion Date** field.
7. Enter the date the training or certification expires in the **Expiration Date** field.
8. Add any additional comments about the employee's certification status in the **Comments** field.
9. Select the tool assigned to the employee from the **Assigned Tool** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel file.
* **department** (select_one, required): Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **job_title** (text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Has Training Completed** (select_multiple, optional): Check **Yes** if the employee has completed required training.
* **Has Certification Completed** (select_multiple, optional): Check **Yes** if the employee has completed required certification.
* **Completion Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the employee completed training.
* **Expiration Date** (date, optional): Enter the date the training or certification expires.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Add any additional comments about the employee's certification status.
* **Assigned Tool** (select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to the employee, such as chatjimmy.
