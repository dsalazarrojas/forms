# Pay Stub Printing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay Stub Printing Request Form is used to request pay stub printing for employees. The form is used to collect necessary information to fulfill this request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee information in the `employee_info` field.
2. Select your preferred delivery method by answering the `delivery_method` question.
3. If you selected a delivery method, provide the `delivery_address` field.
4. Specify when you would like to receive your pay stub by selecting the `pay_period` and `pay_frequency` fields.
5. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
6. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`): Enter your employee identification number, name, or any other relevant information you'd like to provide.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`): Select "Yes" if you want your pay stub printed and delivered, or "No" if you don't want it printed or want to pick it up in-person.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`): If you selected a delivery method, enter your address for the pay stub to be delivered to.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): If you selected a delivery method, select the date when you would like to receive your pay stub.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`): Select "Yes" if you want to receive pay stubs on a regular basis, or "No" if you only need them occasionally.
* **Pay Frequency** (`pay_frequency`): Select "Yes" for each pay period you want to receive pay stubs, or "No" if you only need them occasionally.
* **Pay Amount** (`pay_amount`): Enter the amount you would like to pay for pay stub printing.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your work email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your work phone number where you can be reached.
* **Notes** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
