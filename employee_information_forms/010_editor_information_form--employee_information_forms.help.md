# editor_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store editor information for various purposes, such as personnel management, record-keeping, and other internal needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. If applicable, provide your job title in the **job_title** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **email** field, ensuring it follows the standard email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
5. Provide your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
6. If applicable, specify your department in the **department** field.
7. Describe your job responsibilities in the **job_description** field.
8. Enter your work location in the **location** field.
9. Specify your work hours in the **work_hours** field in 24-hour format (e.g., `08:00-16:00`).
10. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field in `dd/mm/yyyy` format.
11. Enter your start date of employment in the **start_date** field in `dd/mm/yyyy` format.
12. If applicable, enter your end date of employment or contract in the **end_date** field in `dd/mm/yyyy` format.
13. Add any additional notes about your editor role or job responsibilities in the **notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name here.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name here.
* **job_title**: If you have a specific job title, enter it here. This field is not required but can be useful for internal management.
* **email**: Enter your email address following the standard format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number.
* **department**: If you belong to a specific department, enter it here. This field is not required but can help with internal management.
* **job_description**: Provide a brief description of your job responsibilities, which can be helpful for internal reference.
* **location**: Enter your work location.
* **work_hours**: Enter your work hours in 24-hour format, including start and end times (e.g., `08:00-16:00`).
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in `dd/mm/yyyy` format.
* **start_date**: Enter your start date of employment in `dd/mm/yyyy` format.
* **end_date**: If you have an end date or contract end date, enter it here in `dd/mm/yyyy` format. This field is not required.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or information about your editor role or job responsibilities.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on a specific field, contact your internal support team for assistance.
