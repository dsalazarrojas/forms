# Payroll Adjustment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request adjustments to an employee's payroll. It helps the payroll team to track and process the changes effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct pay period you are requesting the adjustment for.
2. Choose the type of adjustment you are requesting (e.g., Raise, Salary, or Bonus).
3. Enter the reason for the adjustment.
4. Enter the amount of the adjustment.
5. Add any additional comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information (1)**: Enter your employee ID number or name as it appears on your pay stub.
* **Pay Period (2)**: Select the pay period you are requesting the adjustment for from the dropdown list.
* **Adjustment Type (3)**: Choose the type of adjustment you want to request (e.g., Raise, Salary, or Bonus).
* **Reason (4)**: Briefly explain the reason for the adjustment.
* **Requested Amount (5)**: Enter the amount of the adjustment in the currency of your salary (e.g., $).
* **Comments (6)**: Add any additional comments or details about the adjustment, such as supporting documentation or evidence.
