# Team Member Discovery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Discovery Form is designed to collect information about team members' personal details, team affiliation, department, job title, and manager relationships. This form helps facilitate the discovery of team members' data for various HR-related purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after each field label.
2. Enter your team member type (individual or team) in the "Team Member Type" field.
3. Select your department(s) from the list of options.
4. Choose your job title from the list of options.
5. Enter your manager's details, including their name, email, phone number, and job title.
6. Determine your manager's team member type (individual or team).
7. Select your manager's reporting level (direct report, team manager, or no reports).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Team Member Type**: Indicate whether you are an individual or team member.
* **Team Member Type Options**: Select your team member type from the list of options.
* **Department**: Select your department from the list of options.
* **Department Options**: Select all applicable departments.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Job Title Options**: Select your job title from the list of options.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Job Title**: Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Team Member Type**: Indicate whether your manager is an individual or team member.
* **Manager Team Member Type Options**: Select your manager's team member type from the list of options.
* **Reporting Manager Name**: Enter your reporting manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager Email**: Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Phone**: Enter your reporting manager's phone number.
* **Reporting Manager Job Title**: Enter your reporting manager's job title.
* **Reporting Manager Team Member Type**: Indicate whether your reporting manager is an individual or team member.
* **Reporting Manager Team Member Type Options**: Select your reporting manager's team member type from the list of options.
* **Reporting Level**: Select your reporting level (direct report, team manager, or no reports).
* **Manager Reporting Level Options**: Select your manager's reporting level from the list of options.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your manager's role or relationship.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after each field label.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your HR department or manager.
* If you are a team member, make sure to select the correct team member type for yourself and your manager.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the HR department.
