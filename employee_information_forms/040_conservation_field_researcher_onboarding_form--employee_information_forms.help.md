# Conservation Field Researcher Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the onboarding process of conservation field researchers. It collects necessary information to ensure a smooth transition to the research position, including contact details, work history, and health and safety requirements. Please complete this form carefully to ensure you are properly prepared for your new role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the requested information in the provided fields.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
3. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
4. Review your completed form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Date (1)**: Date of onboarding. This should be the date you started working at the organization. (Type: date, required)
* **Researcher Name (2)**: Your legal name, as it appears on your identification documents. (Type: text, required)
* **Preferred Name (3)**: The name you would like to go by in the office. This can be your nickname or a shortened version of your full name. (Type: text, optional)
* **Email Address (4)**: Your contact email address. We will use this to send you important updates and notifications. (Type: email, required)
* **Phone Number (5)**: Your contact phone number. We will use this to reach you in case of emergencies. (Type: text, required)
* **Address (6)**: Your mailing address, including the street address. (Type: text, required)
* **City (7)**: The city where you live or work. (Type: text, required)
* **State (8)**: The state or province where you live or work. (Type: text, required)
* **Postal Code (9)**: The ZIP or postal code of your location. (Type: text, required)
* **Date of Birth (10)**: Your date of birth. This is for record-keeping purposes. (Type: date, required)
* **Position Type (11)**: The type of role you will be performing. This can be Full-time employee, Part-time employee, Seasonal, Contractor, Volunteer, or Intern. (Type: select_one, required)
* **Position Title (12)**: Your job title. This will be used for administrative purposes. (Type: text, required)
* **Department (13)**: The research department or team you will be working with. (Type: text, required)
* **Supervisor Name (14)**: The name of your direct supervisor. (Type: text, required)
* **Start Date (15)**: The date you started working. This should be the same as the onboarding date. (Type: date, required)
* **End Date (16)**: If you are a seasonal or temporary employee, enter your expected end date. If not, leave this field blank. (Type: date, optional)
* **Highest Education Level (17)**: Your highest level of education, such as High school, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Master degree, Doctorate, or Other. (Type: select_one, required)
* **Field of Study (18)**: Your major or area of study. This can be relevant to your research position. (Type: text, required)
* **Relevant Experience (19)**: Your level of experience in the field, measured in years. (Type: select_one, required)
* **Research Specialties (20)**: The areas of expertise you have. Select multiple options as needed. (Type: select_multiple, required)
* **Relevant Certifications (21)**: Any relevant certifications you have. This can include academic or professional certifications. (Type: text, optional)
* **Can You Meet Physical Requirements (22)**: Do you have any physical limitations that may affect your ability to perform your duties? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Physical Limitations (23)**: If you have any physical limitations, describe them here. (Type: text, optional)
* **Outdoor Experience Level (24)**: How comfortable are you in outdoor conditions? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Do You Have Vehicle Access (25)**: Do you have access to a vehicle? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Do You Have Valid Drivers License (26)**: Is your driver's license valid? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Emergency Contact Name (27)**: The name of your emergency contact. (Type: text, required)
* **Emergency Contact Phone (28)**: The phone number of your emergency contact. (Type: text, required)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (29)**: Your relationship to your emergency contact. (Type: text, required)
* **Tetanus Vaccination (30)**: Is your tetanus vaccination up to date? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Relevant Medical Conditions (31)**: Any medical conditions we should know about. (Type: text, optional)
* **Current Medications (32)**: Any medications we should know about. (Type: text, optional)
* **Allergies (33)**: Any allergies we should know about. (Type: text, required)
* **Clothing Sizes (34)**: Your clothing sizes for personal protective equipment and uniforms. (Type: text, optional)
* **Equipment Needed (35)**: Any equipment you will need to perform your duties. (Type: text, optional)
* **Background Check Consent (36)**: Do you consent to a background check? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Confidentiality Agreement (37)**: Have you accepted the confidentiality agreement? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Safety Training Required (38)**: Have you acknowledged the safety training requirements? (Type: select_one, required)
* **Additional Information (39)**: Any other information we should know about you. (Type: text, optional)
* **Your Signature (40)**: Your signature, with your full name. (Type: text, required)
* **Supervisor Signature (41)**: Your supervisor's signature, for office use. (Type: text, optional)
* **Onboarding Status (42)**: The status of your onboarding process. (Type: select_one, required)

Please note that some fields may have specific formatting requirements or additional explanations that will be provided in a separate document or onboarding process. Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
