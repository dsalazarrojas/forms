# Maternity Leave Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide employees with a template to apply for maternity leave. It helps to streamline the process of submitting leave requests and ensures that all necessary information is provided for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, including your employee information form.
2. Provide leave details, such as the expected start date, leave period, and leave type.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Enter the date of expected start date and return to work date.
5. Select the leave duration and type (if not already selected).
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information Form** (`employee_information_form`, text, required: false): 
  This section captures your basic contact information, but in this template, it's optional. Please fill in your details as instructed by your HR or management team.
* **Leave Details** (`leave_details`, text, required: false): 
  Enter a brief description of your leave plans, including the date you intend to take leave and any other relevant details.
* **Date of Birth Form** (`date_of_birth_form`, number, required: false): 
  Enter your date of birth to help your HR department keep track of important employee dates.
* **Expected Start Date Form** (`expected_start_date_form`, date, required: false): 
  Enter the date you anticipate taking maternity leave.
* **Expected Start Date** (`expected_start_date`, date, required: false): 
  Confirm the date you intend to start your maternity leave.
* **Return to Work Date Form** (`return_to_work_date_form`, date, required: false): 
  Enter the date you expect to return to work after maternity leave.
* **Return to Work Date** (`return_to_work_date`, date, required: false): 
  Confirm the date you intend to return to work after maternity leave.
* **Leave Period** (`leave_period`, select_one, required: false): 
  Choose from 1, 2, or 3 weeks for the leave duration.
* **Leave Type** (`leave_type`, select_one, required: false): 
  Select Maternity Leave or other approved leave types.
* **Leave Duration** (`leave_duration`, number, required: false): 
  If not already selected, choose the duration of your maternity leave.
* **Leave Start Date** (`leave_start_date`, date, required: false): 
  Confirm the date your leave starts.
