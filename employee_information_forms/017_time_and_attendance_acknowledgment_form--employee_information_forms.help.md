# Time And Attendance Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time And Attendance Acknowledgment Form is used to confirm an employee's working hours, location, and other relevant details for payroll and HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information before starting the form.
* Fill in your employee name and ID in the respective fields.
* Select your department and location from the options provided.
* Enter the date and time you started and ended work.
* If you need to make an amendment, provide a reason for the change.
* Review and confirm your submission status.
* Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as issued by HR.
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **Location**: Select the location where you worked from the options provided.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your work hours in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time In**: Enter the time you started work in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Time Out**: Enter the time you ended work in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Amendment**: If you need to make an amendment, enter the reason for the change.
* **Submission Status**: Select your submission status as 'Active' or 'Inactive' based on your current work status.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your time and attendance information.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your work hours or location.

Note: The fields marked as required must be completed for the form to be considered valid.
