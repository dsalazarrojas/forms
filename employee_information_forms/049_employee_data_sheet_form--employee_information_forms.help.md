# employee_data_sheet_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_data_sheet_form is designed to collect information from employees in an organization. It is intended to capture details about the employee's company affiliation, name, title, department, start and end dates, and job position. This form is useful for HR management, employee onboarding, and other related administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the employee_data_sheet_form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name, if applicable.
2. Fill in the employee's name as it appears on their official documents.
3. Provide the employee's title or position within the company.
4. Enter the department the employee is affiliated with.
5. Select the start date of the employee's employment.
6. Select the end date of the employee's employment, if applicable.
7. Provide the department name, if applicable.
8. Enter the employee's job position or role within the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company the employee is affiliated with.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee as it appears on their official documents.
* **Employee Title**: Enter the title or position of the employee within the company.
* **Department**: Enter the department the employee is affiliated with.
* **Start Date**: Select the date the employee started their employment.
* **End Date**: Select the date the employee's employment ended, if applicable.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department the employee is affiliated with, if applicable.
* **Job Position**: Enter the job position or role of the employee within the company.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete employee data.
- If the employee's employment has not ended, you may leave the "End Date" field blank. 
- The "Department Name" field might be optional but provide valuable context for employee tracking.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors or mistakes. 
- If you're unsure about any information, it's recommended to double-check with the relevant departments or HR.
