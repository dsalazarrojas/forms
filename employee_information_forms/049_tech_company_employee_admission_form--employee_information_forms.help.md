# Tech Company Employee Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about new hires within a tech company. It covers personal and job-related details to facilitate the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the new hire's personal details in the "New Hire Details" page.
2. Enter the personal information in the "Personal Info" page.
3. Provide job-related details in the "Job Details" page.
4. Describe the onboarding process in the "Onboarding Process" page.
5. Select the tech company the new hire belongs to from the "Tech Company" dropdown.
6. Choose the new hire's manager(s) from the "New Hire Manager" dropdown.
7. Enter the new hire's job title, department, and job description.
8. Specify the new hire's job type and status.
9. Set the onboarding date and time.
10. Add any new hire comments or notes.
11. Enter the new hire's contact information (email and phone number).
12. Add any manager's notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details**: Enter the new hire's name, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
* **Personal Info**: Enter the new hire's personal details, such as their address and phone number.
* **Job Details**: Enter the new hire's job title, department, and job description.
* **Onboarding Process**: Describe the onboarding process for the new hire.
* **Tech Company**: Select the tech company the new hire belongs to from the dropdown menu.
* **New Hire Manager**: Choose the new hire's manager(s) from the dropdown menu.
* **Job Title**: Enter the new hire's job title.
* **Department**: Enter the new hire's department.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of the new hire's job.
* **Job Type**: Specify the new hire's job type (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date the new hire will start working.
* **Onboarding Time**: Enter the time the new hire will start working.
* **New Hire Comments**: Add any comments or notes about the new hire.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email**: Enter the new hire's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter the new hire's address.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the new hire's employee ID.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the new hire's manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the new hire's manager's email address.
* **Manager Notes**: Add any notes or comments from the manager.
* **Tech Company Location**: Enter the location of the tech company the new hire belongs to.
* **Job Status**: Specify the new hire's job status (Active, Inactive, or On Leave).
* **Job Type Status**: Specify the new hire's job type status (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Manager Approval**: Select whether the manager has approved the new hire.

## Tips

* Double-check the information entered for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the new hire's personal and job-related details are up-to-date.
* Review the form for any missing or incomplete information.
* Save a copy of the completed form for reference purposes.
