# Talent Pool Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Pool Registration Form is a tool for collecting and managing job information in the organization's talent pool. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data is up-to-date and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job opening in the "Job Title" field. This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide a clear and concise title that accurately represents the position.
2. **Email**: Enter the email address associated with the job opening in the "Email" field. This field is not required, but it's essential for contact and communication purposes.
3. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number (phone or phone extension) associated with the job opening in the "Contact Number" field. This field is not required, but it's helpful for quick contact.
4. **Candidate Name**: Enter the name of the candidate associated with the job opening in the "Candidate Name" field. This field is not required, but it's useful for tracking candidate progress.
5. **Job Status**: Select the current job status in the "Job Status" field from the options:
	* Active: The job is currently open and available for selection.
	* Inactive: The job is closed or not available for selection.
6. **Department**: Select the department associated with the job opening in the "Department" field from the options:
	* Finance
	* Marketing
	* Sales
7. **Job Type**: Select the type of job in the "Job Type" field from the options:
	* Full-time
	* Part-time
	* Contract
8. **Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience required for the job opening in the "Experience" field. This field is not required, but it's helpful for filtering and sorting job openings.
9. **Education**: Enter any relevant education information in the "Education" field. This field is not required, but it's useful for additional context.
10. **Job Level**: Select the job level in the "Job Level" field from the options:
	* Entry-level
	* Mid-level
	* Senior-level
11. **Job Category**: Select the categories associated with the job opening in the "Job Category" field from the options:
	* IT
	* HR
	* Finance

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the job opening.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the email address associated with the job opening.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter the contact number (phone or phone extension) associated with the job opening.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the candidate associated with the job opening.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, not required): Select the current job status from the options:
	* Active: The job is currently open and available for selection.
	* Inactive: The job is closed or not available for selection.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, not required): Select the department associated with the job opening.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, not required): Select the type of job from the options:
	* Full-time
	* Part-time
	* Contract
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, not required): Enter the number of years of experience required for the job opening.
* **Education** (`education`, text, not required): Enter any relevant education information.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, not required): Select the job level from the options:
	* Entry-level
	* Mid-level
	* Senior-level
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, not required): Select the categories associated with the job opening.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with your HR department or supervisor for guidance.
* This form is not intended to replace other HR processes or systems, but rather to supplement them.
* If you're duplicating information that already exists in other systems, please correct it to avoid confusion.
