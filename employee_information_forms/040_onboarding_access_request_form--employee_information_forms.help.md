# Onboarding Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Access Request Form is used to grant access to new employees for their new role. It requests information about the employee, their job title, department, employee ID, manager's name, manager's title, manager's department, access level, resources, date, time, and reason for request.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your employee name in the first field, "employee_name".
2.  Enter your job title in the second field, "job_title".
3.  Enter your department in the third field, "department".
4.  Enter your employee ID in the fourth field, "employee_id".
5.  Enter your manager's name in the fifth field, "manager_name".
6.  Enter your manager's title in the sixth field, "manager_title".
7.  Enter your manager's department in the seventh field, "manager_department".
8.  Select your access level in the eighth field, "access_level".
9.  Select the resources you need access to in the ninth field, "resources".
10. Enter the date you would like to start using the resources in the tenth field, "date".
11. Enter the time you would like to start using the resources in the eleventh field, "time".
11. Enter a brief reason for your access request in the twelfth field, "reason".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (text, Optional): Enter your name as an employee.
* **job_title** (text, Optional): Enter your job title as an employee.
* **department** (text, Optional): Enter your department as an employee.
* **employee_id** (number, Optional): Enter your employee ID.
* **manager_name** (text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **manager_title** (text, Optional): Enter your manager's title.
* **manager_department** (text, Optional): Enter your manager's department.
* **access_level** (select_one, Optional): Select the appropriate access level for your new role. Options are: Level 1, Level 2, Level 3.
* **resources** (select_multiple, Optional): Select the resources you need access to. Options are: Resource 1, Resource 2, Resource 3.
* **date** (date, Optional): Enter the date you would like to start using the resources.
* **time** (time, Optional): Enter the time you would like to start using the resources.
* **reason** (text, Optional): Enter a brief reason for your access request. 

Note: All fields are optional, but please fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate processing of your onboarding request.
