# direct_deposit_account_selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The direct_deposit_account_selection form is used to select a direct deposit account for an employee. It captures information related to the employee's direct deposit account, including their ID, account details, and authorization status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary information to complete the form.
2. Enter the Employee ID in the corresponding field.
3. Select the direct deposit account from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the bank account number and routing number.
5. Choose whether direct deposit is authorized.
6. Enter the date and time for direct deposit (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Direct Deposit Account Selection Form**: This is the main section where you can select the direct deposit account for the employee.
* **Employee ID** (ID: 2, type: number, required: false): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Employee ID** (ID: 3, type: number, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the previous one, but with a different ID. This field is likely a duplicate and should not be filled out.
* **Direct Deposit Account Selection** (ID: 4, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the direct deposit account for the employee from the dropdown list.
* **Bank Account Number** (ID: 5, type: text, required: false): Enter the bank account number for the selected direct deposit account.
* **Routing Number** (ID: 6, type: text, required: false): Enter the routing number for the selected direct deposit account.
* **Direct Deposit Authorization** (ID: 7, type: select_one, required: false): Choose whether direct deposit is authorized for the employee.
* **Date** (ID: 8, type: date, required: false): Enter the date for direct deposit (if applicable).
* **Time** (ID: 9, type: time, required: false): Enter the time for direct deposit (if applicable).
* **Notes** (ID: 10, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the direct deposit account.
* **Email** (ID: 11, type: email, required: false): Enter the employee's email address (if applicable).

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct direct deposit account for the employee.
* Double-check the bank account number and routing number for accuracy.
* Ensure direct deposit is authorized for the employee.
* Use the notes field to add any additional comments or information about the direct deposit account.
