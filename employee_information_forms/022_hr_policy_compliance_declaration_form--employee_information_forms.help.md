# HR Policy Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Compliance Declaration Form is used for employees to declare their understanding and compliance with HR policies and procedures. This form ensures that employees are informed about and agree to abide by these policies, preventing misunderstandings and disputes in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your information on the **Employee Information** page.
2. Read and confirm your understanding of **Policy Understanding**.
3. Select the relevant compliance options for **Compliance Declaration**.
4. Enter the date of any policy changes on the **Policy Changes** page.
5. Provide the duration of policy reviews on the **Policy Reviews** page.
6. Confirm your declaration on the **Declaration Confirmation** page.
7. Note any policy consequences on the **Policy Consequences** page.
8. Enter the number of policy implementations on the **Policy Implementation** page.
9. Sign the form with your **Employee Signature**.
10. Finally, enter the date you signed the form on the **Date Signed** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Information**
* **Employee Information**: Enter your personal details, such as name, employee ID, and department (text)
### 2. **Policy Understanding**
* **Policy Understanding**: Confirm your understanding of the HR policies by writing your thoughts in the text field.
### 3. **Compliance Declaration**
* **Compliance Declaration**: Select all the options that apply to you (select multiple)
### 4. **Policy Changes**
* **Policy Changes**: If any policy changes have occurred, enter the date they took effect (date)
### 5. **Policy Reviews**
* **Policy Reviews**: Enter the duration of policy reviews (time)
### 6. **Declaration Confirmation**
* **Declaration Confirmation**: Confirm your declaration by selecting one of the options (select one)
### 7. **Policy Consequences**
* **Policy Consequences**: If there are any consequences for non-compliance, note them here (note)
### 8. **Policy Implementation**
* **Policy Implementation**: Enter the number of policy implementations (number)
### 9. **Employee Signature**
* **Employee Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your declaration (text)
### 10. **Date Signed**
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form (date)

Note: Ensure you fill out all required fields to complete the form correctly.
