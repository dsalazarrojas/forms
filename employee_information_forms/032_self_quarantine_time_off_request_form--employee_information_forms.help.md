# Self Quarantine Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for employees who need to request time off due to self-quarantine. It is used to record the employee's details, including employee name, ID, and dates of self-quarantine, as well as provide a medical certificate and the doctor's details for verification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee name and ID.
2. Provide the date when you first started self-quarantine.
3. Enter the date when you expect to end your self-quarantine.
4. Choose and upload a medical certificate from one of the provided options, or select "Other" if you have a different type of medical certificate.
5. If you selected "Other", provide the name and phone number of your doctor.
6. Review and submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Quarantine Time Off Request Form**: This is the form title.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Date of Offense** (`date_of_offense_start`, required): Enter the date when you first started self-quarantine. This is the start date of your self-quarantine period.
* **Date of Offense End** (`date_of_offense_end`, required): Enter the date when you expect to end your self-quarantine period.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certificate_attachment`, required): Choose one of the provided medical certificate options, or select "Other" and provide the name and phone number of your doctor. This is required to verify your self-quarantine.
* **Doctor's Name** (`doctor_name`, required): If you selected "Other" for the medical certificate, enter the name of your doctor.
* **Doctor's Phone** (`doctor_phone`, optional): If you selected "Other" for the medical certificate, enter your doctor's phone number.
