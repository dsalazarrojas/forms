# Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Handbook Acknowledgement Form" is a form that requires employees to acknowledge that they have read and understood the company's employee handbook. This form is used to verify that employees have received and understood the handbook before starting work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your employee ID in the "employee_id" field.
3. Choose the date you received the handbook in the "acknowledgement_date" field.
4. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "employee_signature" field to indicate that you have read and understood the handbook.
5. Enter your manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
6. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "manager_signature" field to indicate that they have reviewed and acknowledged your understanding of the handbook.
7. Choose whether your manager has read and understood the handbook by checking "Yes" or "No" in the "manager_title" field.
8. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "employee_supervisor_signature" field to indicate that your supervisor has reviewed and acknowledged your understanding of the handbook.
9. Enter the date and time the form was completed in the "date" and "time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Name (employee_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel record.
- **Employee ID (employee_id)**: Enter your employee ID number as assigned by the company.
- **Acknowledge Date (acknowledgement_date)**: Choose the date you received the employee handbook.
- **Employee Signature (employee_signature)**: Check "Yes" if you have read and understood the employee handbook, otherwise check "No".
- **Manager Name (manager_name)**: Enter your manager's name.
- **Manager Signature (manager_signature)**: Check "Yes" if your manager has reviewed and acknowledged your understanding of the handbook, otherwise check "No".
- **Manager Title (manager_title)**: Choose "Yes" if your manager has read and understood the handbook, otherwise choose "No".
- **Employee Supervisor Signature (employee_supervisor_signature)**: Check "Yes" if your supervisor has reviewed and acknowledged your understanding of the handbook, otherwise check "No".
- **Completed Date and Time (date, time)**: Enter the date and time the form was completed.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand the employee handbook before completing this form.
- Verify with your manager and supervisor that they have reviewed and acknowledged your understanding of the handbook before checking their respective fields.
- Double-check the form for any errors before submitting it.
