# Employee Pay Period Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Pay Period Submission" form is used to provide details about an employee's work hours, pay cycle, and other relevant information for payroll processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the relevant employee records to ensure that the information is up-to-date and accurate.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields, taking into account the specific instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Regular Hours** (`regular_hours`, number, optional): Enter the regular hours worked by the employee during the pay period. This field is used to track hours worked outside of overtime hours.
* **Overtime Hours** (`overtime_hours`, number, optional): Enter the overtime hours worked by the employee during the pay period. This field is used to track hours worked beyond regular hours.
* **Pay Cycle** (`pay_cycle`, date, optional): Select the pay cycle for which this form is being submitted. This field determines when the employee will be paid.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the pay period. This field helps to identify the pay period's start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the pay period. This field helps to identify the pay period's end date.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_multiple, optional): Select the pay period (e.g., Pay Period 1, Pay Period 2, etc.) for which this form is being submitted.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee's ID number. This field helps to identify the employee's personnel file.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their ID card or HR records. This field helps to confirm the employee's identity.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title. This field helps to confirm the employee's role within the company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the employee worked during the pay period. This field helps to track employee movements and assignments.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the pay period. This field is for free-text entry.

Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently with the employee's personnel file. If you're unsure about any field, review the relevant employee records or consult with HR for guidance.
