# Part-Time Staff Schedule Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect schedule preferences from part-time staff members. The purpose of this form is to gather information on their work arrangement, start and end times, available days, preferred start time, work week, notice period, and potential secondary work arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your preferred work arrangement from the options provided.
2.  Enter your desired start time.
3.  Enter your desired end time.
4.  Choose all days of the week that you are available to work.
5.  If you have a preferred start time that is different from your start time, enter it in the Preferred Start Time field.
6.  Select your preferred work week type (Full-time, Part-time, Variable).
7.  Choose your desired notice period in advance of a scheduled shift.
8.  If you have a secondary work arrangement, select all options that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Work Arrangement (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)**:
    +   This field allows you to select the times of day you are available to work. Choose all options that apply to you.

*   **Start Time**:
    +   Enter your desired start time in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8am).

*   **End Time**:
    +   Enter your desired end time in 24-hour format (e.g., 16:00 for 4pm).

*   **Available Days (Select all days of week)**:
    +   Choose all days of the week that you are available to work.

*   **Preferred Start Time**:
    +   Enter your desired start time, if different from your start time entered in the Start Time field.

*   **Work Week (Full-time, Part-time, Variable)**:
    +   Select your preferred work week type.

*   **Notice Period (1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours)**:
    +   Choose your desired notice period in advance of a scheduled shift.

*   **Work Arrangement 2 (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)**:
    +   If you have a secondary work arrangement, select all options that apply to you.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when entering your desired start and end times.
*   Ensure you select all days of the week that you are available to work.
*   If you have a preferred start time that is different from your start time, enter it to ensure accurate scheduling.
