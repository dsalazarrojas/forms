# Food Delivery Personnel Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Delivery Personnel Onboarding Form is used to collect and document essential information on new employees, ensuring a smooth integration process and providing necessary details for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your general information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact details, such as phone number and email.
3. If you have any medical information or licenses, please fill in the relevant fields.
4. If you own a vehicle, please provide information on your vehicle, including type, color, year, license plate, and mileage.
5. If you have any medical specialties, select all that apply.
6. Select your medical insurance provider from the list.
7. If you have any vehicle-specific information, such as make, model, and year, please fill in the relevant fields.
8. Enter the delivery zone, address, and time for your scheduled delivery.
9. Enter any additional delivery notes or information.
10. If you have any emergency contacts, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (1)**: This section collects your basic contact information, which is essential for communication and administrative purposes.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Please enter your phone number and email for communication with our team.
* **Delivery Information (3)**: This section captures essential details about your delivery operations, including zones, addresses, and schedules.
* **Vehicle Information (4)**: Please enter your vehicle's color, year, license plate, and mileage for operational purposes.
* **Vehicle Type (5)**: Select the type of vehicle you own, such as car, truck, or motorcycle.
* **Vehicle Color (6)**: Select the color of your vehicle from the list.
* **Vehicle License (7)**: Please enter your vehicle's license number for verification purposes.
* **Emergency Contacts (8)**: List any emergency contacts with their name, phone number, and relationship for emergency situations.
* **Emergency Notes (9)**: Add any additional notes or information about your emergency contacts.
* **Vehicle Make (10)**: Select the make of your vehicle from the list.
* **Vehicle Model (11)**: Please enter your vehicle's model for reference purposes.
* **Vehicle Year (12)**: Select the year of your vehicle from the list.
* **Delivery Location (13)**: Enter the location for your delivery operations.
* **Delivery Notes (14)**: Add any additional notes or information about your delivery operations.
* **Vehicle Mileage (15)**: Please enter the mileage of your vehicle for operational purposes.
* **Medical Information (16)**: This section captures any relevant medical information or licenses you may hold.
* **Medical Specialties (17)**: Select any medical specialties you have, such as cardiology or neurology.
* **Medical Insurance (18)**: Select your medical insurance provider from the list.
* **Vehicle License Plate (19)**: Please enter your vehicle's license plate for verification purposes.
* **Delivery Zone (20)**: Enter the zone for your delivery operations.
* **Delivery Address (21)**: Enter the address for your delivery operations.
* **Delivery Time (22)**: Select the time for your delivery operations.
* **Vehicle Model (23)**: Please enter your vehicle's model for reference purposes.
* **Delivery Location (24)**: Enter the location for your delivery operations.
* **Delivery Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes or information about your delivery operations.

## Tips
- Please fill the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth integration process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
- This form is for administrative and operational purposes only; it is not intended for public disclosure.
