# Sales Staff Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Staff Onboarding Form is used to gather information about new sales staff members for the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category: Choose whether the staff member is a "New Hire", "Sales Representative", or "Sales Team" member.
2. Enter the sales representative's email address.
3. Provide the sales representative's name.
4. Enter the sales representative's title or job position.
5. Select the sales representative's manager's title or position.
6. Enter the sales representative's phone number.
7. Enter the sales representative's cell phone number.
8. Enter the sales representative's external email address, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Employees** (`new_employees`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category that best describes the type of sales staff member being onboarded.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the sales representative's email address for internal and external communication.
* **Sales Rep Name** (`sales_rep_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the sales representative being onboarded.
* **Sales Rep Title** (`sales_rep_title`, text, optional): Enter the sales representative's title or job position.
* **Sales Rep Manager** (`sales_rep_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the sales representative's manager's title or position, which could be "Sales Manager", "Sales Director", or "CEO".
* **Sales Rep Phone** (`sales_rep_phone`, text, optional): Enter the sales representative's phone number.
* **Sales Rep Cell** (`sales_rep_cell`, text, optional): Enter the sales representative's cell phone number.
* **Sales Rep Ext Email** (`sales_rep_ext_email`, email, optional): Enter the sales representative's external email address, if applicable.
