# knowledge_transfer_offboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The knowledge transfer offboarding form is used to document the details of an employee's departure from the company, including their reasons for leaving, the knowledge transfer date, and the status of the knowledge transfer process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the employee's information, including their name, email, and job title.
2. Select the reason for the employee's departure from the provided options.
3. Enter the date of departure and the date of the knowledge transfer.
4. Identify the areas of knowledge to be transferred to the new employee using the provided options.
5. Select the status of the knowledge transfer process.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the employee's departure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (`id: 1`): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Email** (`id: 3`): Enter the employee's work email address.
* **Job Title** (`id: 4`): Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their personnel record.
* **Manager ID** (`id: 5`): Enter the manager's ID number or other identifier.
* **Date of Departure** (`id: 6`): Enter the date the employee will depart from the company.
* **Reason for Leaving** (`id: 7`): Select the reason for the employee's departure from the provided options.
* **Notes** (`id: 8`): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the employee's departure.
* **Knowledge Transfer Date** (`id: 9`): Enter the date the knowledge transfer process will be completed.
* **Knowledge Transfer** (`id: 10`): Identify the areas of knowledge to be transferred to the new employee using the provided options.
* **Contact** (`id: 11`): Enter any contact information for the employee, such as their phone number or personal email.
* **Knowledge Transfer Status** (`id: 12`): Select the status of the knowledge transfer process from the provided options.
* **Completed** (`id: 13`): Enter the name of the person who completed the knowledge transfer process.
* **Completed by** (`id: 14`): Select the person who completed the knowledge transfer process from the provided options.
