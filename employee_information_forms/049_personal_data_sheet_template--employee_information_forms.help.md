# Personal Data Sheet Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal data from employees for HR or administrative purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
- Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to check the checkboxes correctly for department and manager details.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if it's not applicable.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name as you would like to be known in the company.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as per your personal identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official company email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for work-related matters.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your work address for delivery purposes or other work-related matters.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth as per your personal identification documents.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Check if you belong to a department. If yes, select 'Yes' from the dropdown list.
* **Position** (`position`, text, optional): Enter your official position in the company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title in the company.
* **Manager** (`manager`, select_one, required): Check if you have a manager. If yes, select 'Yes' from the dropdown list.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address for communication.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's contact phone number for work-related matters.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and should not be filled in.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and should not be filled in.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and should not be filled in.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for your profile.
* **Employee Code** (`employee_code`, text, optional): Enter your employee code for internal reference.
* **Department IDs** (`department_ids`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you belong to multiple departments. If yes, select 'Yes' from the dropdown list for each department.
* **Manager Phone Number** is not a required field, it's just for informational purposes only.

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields are self-explanatory, but if you're unsure about any field, please refer to your HR department or the company's policies and procedures.
