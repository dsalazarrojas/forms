# Salary Slip Overview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide an overview of an employee's salary slip, including their personal details, job title, department, pay period, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "Employee Job Title" field.
3. Select the employee's department from the "Department" dropdown list.
4. Choose the pay period for which the salary slip is being viewed or created from the "Pay Period" dropdown list.
5. Add any relevant salary documentation in the "Salary Documentation" field.
6. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the salary slip in the "Reviewed By" field.
7. Fill in the date and time of the review in the "Date of Review" and "Time of Review" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title or position in the company.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select the department where the employee works. Options: Sales, Marketing, IT.
* **Pay Period** (`pay_period`, select_one, optional): Select the pay period for which the salary slip is being viewed or created. Options: Month, Quarter, Year.
* **Salary Documentation** (`salary_slip_overview_form_salary_documentation`, note, optional): Add any relevant salary-related documentation or comments.
* **Reviewed By** (`salary_slip_reviewed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the salary slip.
* **Date of Review** (`date_of_review`, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Time of Review** (`time_of_review`, time, optional): Enter the time of the review.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary information and authorization to complete this form accurately.
* Consult with your HR or payroll department if you are unsure about any field or requirement.
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the information entered before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes to this form, please refer to your organization's change management process.
