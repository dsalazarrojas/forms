# Employee Emergency Contact Amendment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update or add new emergency contacts for an employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the employee's details, such as their Employee ID.
2. Choose the correct relationship type for the emergency contact from the provided options.
3. Fill out the emergency contact's details, including their name, address, and contact information.
4. Select the correct contact type from the options provided.
5. Date the form accurately.
6. Sign the form to confirm you have filled it out correctly.
7. Have the employee sign the form as well, and ensure it is dated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (1, number, required/optional): Enter the employee's ID number. This is used to identify the employee in the system.
* **Title** (2, text, required/optional): This is a text field for entering the title.
* **First Name** (3, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Last Name** (4, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Relationship** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select the correct relationship type for the emergency contact, such as Spouse, Parent, Child, or Sibling.
* **Email** (6, email, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Address** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (9, select_one, required/optional): Select the correct relationship type from the provided options.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (10, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (11, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (12, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (14, email, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Employee Signature Date** (15, date, required/optional): Date the employee's signature.
* **Manager Signature Date** (16, date, required/optional): Date the manager's signature.
* **Manager Signature** (17, note, required/optional): Sign the form to confirm the manager's approval.
* **Reason** (18, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for updating the emergency contact information.
* **Manager Notes** (19, text, required/optional): Enter any notes from the manager.
* **Manager Comments** (20, text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the manager.
* **Employee Signature** (21, note, required/optional): Sign the form to confirm the employee's approval.
* **Date** (22, date, required/optional): Date the form accurately.
* **Employee Comments** (23, text, required/optional): Enter any comments from the employee.
* **Other** (24, text, required/optional): Enter any other relevant details.
* **First Name** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Last Name** (26, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's last name.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use the correct relationship type for the emergency contact.
* If the employee already has an existing record, review and update the information instead of filling out this form.
* Date all fields accurately.
