# Missed Punch Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missed Punch Form is used to report when an employee has missed a punch-in time and to notify their manager of the missed punch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that you have already reported the missed punch-in time on a separate form.
2. Select "Yes" for "Missed Punch Form Submitted" to confirm that you have already submitted a form reporting the missed punch.
3. Enter your name to confirm that you are the one submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Missed Punches** (`missed_punches`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the reason for missing a punch-in time.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, note, optional): This field is used to confirm that your manager has been informed about the missed punch-in time.
* **Missed Punch Form Submitted** (`missed_punch_form_submitted`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm that you have already submitted a separate form reporting the missed punch-in time.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select one, optional): This field is used to confirm that you are the one submitting this form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): This field is used to record the time when the form was submitted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): This field is used to add any additional comments or information about the missed punch.
