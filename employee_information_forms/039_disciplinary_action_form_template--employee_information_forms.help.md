# Disciplinary Action Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Form Template is a document used to record and manage disciplinary actions taken against employees within the organization. It's designed to provide a structured approach to documenting incidents, actions taken, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the provided fields with the relevant details about the incident.
2. Ensure you've reviewed the organization's policies and procedures before proceeding.
3. Complete all required fields, marked as "required."
4. Sign the form, ensuring that both employee and manager signatures are included.
5. Keep a record of the completed form, along with any supporting documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, `note`, not required): Provide a brief description of the incident, including details about what happened and any relevant context.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, number, not required): Enter the employee's ID number (if applicable).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, not required): Enter the employee's name.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, not required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time Of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, not required): Enter the time of the incident (if available).
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Describe the incident and its impact on the employee and the organization.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, note, not required): Describe the action taken as a result of the incident.
* **Disciplinary Action Date** (`disciplinary_action_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the disciplinary action.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, not required): This signature is required to confirm employee acknowledgment of the disciplinary action.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, not required): This signature is required to confirm manager acknowledgement of the disciplinary action and approval.
* **Reason For Disciplinary Action** (`reason_for_disciplinary_action`, note, not required): State the reason for the disciplinary action, taking into account the incident and relevant policies.
