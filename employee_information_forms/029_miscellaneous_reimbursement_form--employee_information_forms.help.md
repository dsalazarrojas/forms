# Miscellaneous Reimbursement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about miscellaneous reimbursements for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form title.
2. Select the category of the expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
3. Provide a brief description of the expense.
4. Select whether the employer is responsible for the expense.
5. Select whether the insurance covers the expense.
6. Choose the types of expenses that this form covers (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', multiple selections possible).
7. Enter the date when the expense was incurred.
8. Enter the total amount of the expense.
9. Provide details about the expense (e.g., receipts, notes).
10. Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
11. Select the date when the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: A brief title of the reimbursement form. This is the name of the form that will help track and verify the expense.
* **Form Category**: Select the category of the expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No'). This helps to group and identify the type of expense.
* **Form Description**: A brief description of the expense, describing what was purchased or what the expense was for.
* **Form Employer**: Select whether the employer is responsible for the expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No'). This determines who covers the expense.
* **Form Insurance**: Select whether the insurance covers the expense (e.g., 'Yes', 'No'). This indicates whether the insurance company covers the expense.
* **Form Expense Type**: Choose the types of expenses that this form covers (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', multiple selections possible). This helps to narrow down the types of expenses that are being reported.
* **Form Expense Date**: Enter the date when the expense was incurred. This is the date when the expense was incurred.
* **Form Expense Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense. This is the amount of the expense.
* **Form Expense Details**: Provide details about the expense (e.g., receipts, notes). This provides additional context about the expense.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form. This is who is submitting the form for review.
* **Form Submission Date**: Select the date when the form was submitted. This is the date when the form was submitted for review.
