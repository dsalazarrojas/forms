# Work Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect work-related information from employees for administrative and HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into three sections. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on the first page (Employee Information).
2. On the second page (Job Information), enter your job title, job description, and other related details.
3. On the third page (Onboarding Information), provide any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`work_information_collection_form_1_page`): This section requires your name, employee ID, job title, and other personal details. Ensure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Job Information** (`work_information_collection_form_2_page`): This section is about your job title, job description, and any other relevant details about your role. Please be specific and clear.
* **Onboarding Information** (`work_information_collection_form_3_page`): This section is for any comments or notes about your onboarding process. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
