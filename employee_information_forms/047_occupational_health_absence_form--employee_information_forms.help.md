# Occupational Health Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to report and document their absences from work due to health reasons, ensuring accurate and systematic tracking of employee health status and potential absences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your employee identifier in the first field.
2. Select the date of your absence in the second field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Briefly describe the reason for your absence in the third field.
4. If applicable, select the date you plan to return to work in the fourth field.
5. If you have attached a medical certificate, add it in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Identifier** (Text): Enter your unique employee identifier, as assigned by your HR department.
* **Date of Absence** (Date): Select the date when you took your absence from work. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Reason for Absence** (Text): Describe the reason for your absence, including any necessary medical details.
* **Return to Work Date** (Date): If you know when you plan to return to work, select this date. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Medical Certificate** (Note): Attach any relevant medical certificates or documents that support your absence.

## Tips
Remember to complete this form accurately and truthfully, as it is a record of your employee status. If you have any doubts or questions, contact your HR department for assistance.
