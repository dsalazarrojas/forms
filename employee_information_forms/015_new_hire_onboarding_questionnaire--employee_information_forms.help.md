# New Hire Onboarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about new employees, ensuring a seamless integration into the company's system and team. This guide will walk you through each section, explaining what each field represents and how to fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the new hire's personal information on **New Hire Information (Page 1)**.
2. Provide contact details on **Contact Details (Page 2)**.
3. Identify the new hire's emergency contacts on **Emergency Contact (Page 3)**.
4. Enter job-specific information on **Job Details (Page 4)**.
5. Select the onboarding process for the new hire on **Onboarding Process (Page 5)**.
6. Enter the new hire's job title, department, and manager's details on **Job Title, Department, and Manager (Pages 6-8)**.
7. Provide the new hire's job description and job status on **Job Description and Status (Pages 9-10)**.
8. Set the onboarding date and time on **Onboarding Date and Time (Pages 11-12)**.
9. Select the new hire's job type and status on **Job Type and Status (Pages 13-14)**.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments on **Notes (Page 15)**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Information (Page 1):** This section gathers the new hire's personal information, including their name, address, and other contact details.
* **Contact Details (Page 2):** Enter the new hire's contact information, such as phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 3):** Identify the new hire's emergency contacts, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Job Details (Page 4):** Enter the new hire's job title, department, and job description.
* **Onboarding Process (Page 5):** Select the onboarding process that best suits the new hire's needs.
* **Job Title (Page 6):** Enter the new hire's job title.
* **Department (Page 7):** Enter the new hire's department.
* **Manager Name (Page 8):** Enter the new hire's manager's name.
* **Manager Phone (Page 9):** Enter the new hire's manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email (Page 10):** Enter the new hire's manager's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Page 11):** Enter the new hire's emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Page 12):** Enter the new hire's emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Page 13):** Enter the new hire's emergency contact's email address.
* **Onboarding Date (Page 14):** Enter the date the new hire will start onboarding.
* **Onboarding Time (Page 15):** Enter the time the new hire will start onboarding.
* **Job Type (Page 16):** Select the new hire's job type (Full-time, Part-time, or Contract).
* **Job Status (Page 17):** Select the new hire's job status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Location (Page 18):** Enter the new hire's work location.
* **Notes (Page 19):** Enter any additional notes or comments about the new hire.
* **Job Description (Page 20):** Enter a detailed description of the new hire's job responsibilities.
* **Job Start Date (Page 21):** Enter the date the new hire will start working.
* **Job End Date (Page 22):** Enter the date the new hire's job will end.
* **Job Duration (Page 23):** Enter the length of time the new hire will work for the company.
* **Employee Status (Page 24):** Select the new hire's employee status (Active, Inactive, or Resigned).
* **Employee Number (Page 25):** Enter the new hire's employee number.

Note: This form requires you to fill out a total of 25 fields. Please review each section carefully and ensure you've provided accurate information for each field. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to reach out to your HR or IT department for assistance.
