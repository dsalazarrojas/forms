# Veterans Self Identify Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterans Self Identify Form is used to collect information from employees about their veteran status and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully and fill in your employee information accurately.
2. Select your veteran status from the provided options.
3. Enter your date of birth and phone number (if applicable).
4. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, required): This field is used to collect your employee information, which will be used to identify you in the system.
* **Veteran ID** (`veteran_id`, number, optional): This field is used to collect your veteran ID, if you have one.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is used to collect your name.
* **Service Number** (`service_number`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your service number, if you have one.
* **Service Number 2** (`service_number_2`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your secondary service number, if you have one.
* **Service Number 3** (`service_number_3`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your tertiary service number, if you have one.
* **Veteran Status** (`veteran_status`, select_one, optional): Please select your current veteran status from the provided options (Active, Reservist, Retired).
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): This field is used to collect your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your HR representative for assistance.
* If you have previously submitted this form, you will be prompted to update your information, not submit new information.
