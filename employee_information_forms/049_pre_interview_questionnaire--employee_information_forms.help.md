# pre-interview-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a job or a position, with the aim of helping them prepare for a pre-interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
2. Choose your employment status, which will help us understand your work situation.
3. Describe your work experience, focusing on the relevant skills and qualifications you have acquired.
4. List the languages you speak fluently.
5. Choose your previous work experience in terms of duration.
6. Indicate your work type, which will help us determine your work style.
7. Select your availability period, if applicable.
8. List your technical skills and expertise.
9. Choose your work experience industry, which will help us understand your background.
10. Provide your job title, job description, and job location, if applicable.
11. Enter your contact email and phone number.
12. Enter your contact address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): This is the section where you will provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `note`, required/optional): Describe your work experience, focusing on the relevant skills and qualifications you have acquired.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required/optional): If applicable, choose your availability period.
* **Skills and Interests** (`skills_and_interests`, `text`, required/optional): List your relevant skills and interests.
* **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant educational background information.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the languages you speak fluently from the options provided.
* **Previous Work Experience** (`previous_work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose your previous work experience in terms of duration.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate your work type, which will help us determine your work style.
* **Availability Period** (`availability_period`, `time`, required/optional): If applicable, choose your availability period.
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, `text`, required/optional): List any technical skills and expertise you have.
* **Work Experience Industry** (`work_experience_industry`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your work experience industry, which will help us understand your background.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your job title, if applicable.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job description, if applicable.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job location, if applicable.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact address.

Note: The fields marked as required/optional may or may not be required depending on the specific situation.
