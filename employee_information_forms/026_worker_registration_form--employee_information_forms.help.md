# Worker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Registration Form is a tool used for collecting information about new employees. It is a critical part of the hiring process and helps in maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of employees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the job title and job description in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the job status from the options provided.
3. Answer the questions regarding the supervisor, job category, job type, job level, job family, job group, work location, job hours, job date, job time, and job note.
4. Enter the email and phone number of the employee.
5. Fill in the job address.
6. Select the work status from the options provided.
7. Add any additional notes regarding job level, job category, supervisor, job type, job group, job family, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the job position being created.
* **Job Description** (2, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job position.
* **Job Status** (4, select_one, required): Select the current status of the job position from the options provided.
* **Supervisor** (5, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the supervisor will be available for the job position.
* **Job Category** (6, select_one, required): Determine if the job category is Yes or No.
* **Job Type** (7, select_one, required): Determine if the job type is Yes or No.
* **Job Level** (8, select_one, required): Choose the level of the job from the options provided.
* **Job Family** (9, select_one, required): Determine if the job family is Yes or No.
* **Job Group** (10, select_one, required): Determine if the job group is Yes or No.
* **Work Location** (11, select_one, required): Determine if the work location is Yes or No.
* **Job Hours** (12, select_one, required): Determine if the job hours are Yes or No.
* **Job Date** (13, date, required): Enter the date of the job posting.
* **Job Time** (14, time, required): Enter the time of the job posting.
* **Job Note** (15, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job position.
* **Email** (16, email, required): Enter the email address of the employee.
* **Phone** (17, text, required): Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Job Address** (18, text, required): Enter the address of the job location.
* **Work Status** (19, select_one, required): Select the current work status of the employee from the options provided.
* **Job Level Note** (20, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job level.
* **Job Category Note** (21, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job category.
* **Supervisor Note** (22, note, required): Add any notes regarding the supervisor.
* **Job Type Note** (23, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job type.
* **Job Group Note** (24, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job group.
* **Job Family Note** (25, note, required): Add any notes regarding the job family.
