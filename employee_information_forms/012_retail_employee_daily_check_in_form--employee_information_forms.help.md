# Retail Employee Daily Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track employee daily attendance and activity for retail stores.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your employee information, including your name and ID.
2. Enter the date and time of your shift start and end.
3. Enter your in and out locations.
4. Record your in-time arrival and out-time departure.
5. Enter any break start and end times, as well as break duration.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (14)**: Enter your name as it appears in your employee ID.
* **Date (2)**: Enter the date of your shift, in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time (3)**: Enter the start and end times of your shift, in 12-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
* **In Location (4)**: Enter the location where you started your shift.
* **Out Location (5)**: Enter the location where you finished your shift.
* **In Time (Arrival) (6)**: Enter the time you arrived at the location.
* **Shift Start (7)**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Break Start (8)**: Enter the start time of your break.
* **Break End (9)**: Enter the end time of your break.
* **Shift End (10)**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Break Duration (11)**: Enter the duration of your break in minutes.
* **Notes (12)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Shift Notes (13)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Employee ID (15)**: Enter your employee ID.
* **Location (16)**: Enter the location where you worked.
* **Manager Name (17)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Phone (18)**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Email (19)**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Retail Store (20)**: Enter the name of the retail store where you worked.
* **Store Phone (21)**: Enter the phone number of the retail store.
* **Store Email (22)**: Enter the email address of the retail store.
* **Supervisor Name (23)**: Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Store Manager (24)**: Enter the name of the store manager.
* **Comments (25)**: Add any additional comments about your shift.
