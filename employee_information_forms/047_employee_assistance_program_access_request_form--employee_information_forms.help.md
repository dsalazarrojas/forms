# Employee Assistance Program Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) Access Request Form is designed to help employees request access to confidential support services, such as counseling, financial planning, and legal assistance. The form gathers necessary information to ensure that the employee's request is processed efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select the department that you are currently working in or have affiliation with from the "Department" field.
3. If applicable, select "Yes" in the "Manager ID" field to indicate that your manager is aware of your request.
4. Enter the date you are requesting EAP services for in the "EAP Request Date" field.
5. Choose the type of EAP service you are requesting from the "EAP Type" field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments related to your request in the "Additional Notes" field.
7. Select your current employment status from the "Employee Status" field.
8. Finally, select the status of your department head's response from the "Department Head Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (Text, Required: No)
	+ Enter your unique employee ID number to associate your request with your employee record.
* **Department** (Select Multiple, Required: No)
	+ Select the department you are currently working in or have affiliation with.
* **Manager ID** (Select Multiple, Required: No)
	+ If your manager is aware of your request, select "Yes" to confirm.
* **EAP Request Date** (Date, Required: No)
	+ Enter the date you are requesting EAP services for.
* **EAP Type** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ Choose the type of EAP service you are requesting (e.g., counseling, financial planning, legal support).
* **Additional Notes** (Note, Required: No)
	+ Add any additional information or comments related to your request.
* **Employee Status** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ Select your current employment status (e.g., active, leave of absence, retired).
* **Department Head Status** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ Select the status of your department head's response to your request (e.g., approve, reject, in progress, not applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to your HR representative for guidance.
* The EAP services are confidential and available to all employees; however, please note that some services may have limited availability.
* The department head's response will determine the processing of your request, so please allow sufficient time for processing.
