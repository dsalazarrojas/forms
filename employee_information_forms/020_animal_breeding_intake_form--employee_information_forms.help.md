# Animal Breeding Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about your animal and your breeding goals. It will help us understand your animal's history, health, and needs, ensuring a smooth and successful integration into our breeding program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Owner Information**: 
   - Please fill in your full name and email address. We'll use these details to contact you about your animal's breeding progress and any necessary updates.
   - Enter your phone number so we can reach you for any further questions or concerns.
   - Provide your address for our records and any potential visits or pickups.

2. **Animal Information**:
   - Enter your animal's registered name, which is the name given to them by their breeder or original owner.
   - Give us your animal's everyday name, if different from their registered name.
   - Choose the species of your animal from the provided options.
   - Enter your animal's breed, as specified on their registration papers or documentation.

3. **Animal History**:
   - Provide your animal's date of birth, which is their date of registration or documentation.
   - Choose your animal's sex from male or female.
   - If your animal has microchipping, enter their microchip number for our records.
   - Describe their color and any distinctive markings for identification.

4. **Breeding History and Goals**:
   - If you have breeding history, describe any previous breedings, including number and outcomes.
   - Specify the type of breeding program you're enrolling your animal in.
   - Describe any desired traits or characteristics for their offspring.

5. **Program and Contract**:
   - Confirm whether you have a microchip contract or other breed-specific documentation.
   - If you're enrolling in a stud program, choose the type of contract you need.
   - Specify the disposition plan for any offspring, which may include showing, breeding, or companionship.

6. **Agreement and Signature**:
   - Confirm your agreement to comply with the breeding program's terms and conditions.
   - Sign your name, and type it as per your full name.
   - Enter today's date for the signature.

7. **Program Representative**:
   - Sign with your breeder or program representative's name.
   - This is to ensure you've been authorized to enroll your animal in the program.
