# lcc_department_semester_schedule_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request a semester schedule for the LCC (Lincoln County College) departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your department from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the semester you would like to schedule for.
3. Specify your schedule request.
4. Select your schedule type (Regular or Part-time).
5. Choose the dates you are available to work.
6. Choose the times you are available to work.
7. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).
8. (Optional) Enter any additional contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (ID: 1, Required): Select your department from the dropdown list. This field is used to determine which department you are requesting a schedule for.
* **Semester** (ID: 2, Required): Choose the semester you would like to schedule for. This field is used to determine which semester you are requesting a schedule for.
* **Schedule Request** (ID: 3, Optional): Please specify any additional information about your schedule request.
* **Schedule Type** (ID: 4, Required): Choose your schedule type (Regular or Part-time). This field is used to determine the type of schedule you need.
* **Available Dates** (ID: 5, Required): Choose the dates you are available to work. You can select multiple dates from the list.
* **Available Times** (ID: 6, Required): Choose the times you are available to work. You can select multiple times from the list.
* **Note** (ID: 7, Optional): Please enter any additional comments about your schedule request.
* **Email** (ID: 8, Required): Enter your email address. This is your primary contact information.
* **Phone** (ID: 9, Optional): Enter your phone number. This is your secondary contact information.
* **Fax** (ID: 10, Optional): Enter your fax number. This is your tertiary contact information.
* **Address** (ID: 11, Optional): Enter your address. This is your physical address.
* **Name** (ID: 12, Required): Enter your name.
* **Email 1** (ID: 13, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Email 2** (ID: 14, Optional): Enter an additional email address.
* **Phone 1** (ID: 15, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Phone 2** (ID: 16, Optional): Enter an additional phone number.
* **Address 1** (ID: 17, Optional): Enter your address.
* **Address 2** (ID: 18, Optional): Enter an additional address.
* **Department 1** (ID: 19, Optional): Enter your department name.
* **Department 2** (ID: 20, Optional): Enter an additional department name.
