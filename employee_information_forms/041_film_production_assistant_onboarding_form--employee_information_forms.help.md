# Film Production Assistant Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for the onboarding of a Film Production Assistant. It is designed to collect essential information about the individual's production assistant details, availability, acknowledgments, contact details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in all required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary documents and information ready before filling out the form.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Assistant Details** (`production_assistant_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about your production assistant details.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Enter your availability for the production schedule, including your start and end dates.
* **Acknowledgments** (`acknowledgments`, note, optional): If you have any acknowledgments or certifications relevant to your work as a production assistant, please list them here.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Production Notes** (`production_notes`, note, optional): List any relevant production notes or information that you would like to share with the production team.
* **Production Schedule** (`production_schedule`, time, optional): Enter your availability for the production schedule, including start and end times.
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your availability, such as days off or conflicting schedules.
* **Emergency Details** (`emergency_details`, text, optional): If you have any emergency contacts or details that the production team should know about, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns that the production team should be aware of, please list them here.
* **Emergency Medical Contacts** (`emergency_medical_contacts`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of your emergency medical contacts.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional): If you have any special equipment or requirements for your work, please list them here.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional): If you will be using a vehicle for work, please provide details about your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Enter any personal details that you would like to share with the production team.
* **Availability Period** (`availability_period`, date, optional): Enter your availability for the production period, including start and end dates.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): List the names and contact information of your emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contacts.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, text, optional): If you have vehicle insurance, please provide details about your policy.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): If you have a vehicle registration, please provide the relevant details.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, text, optional): If you have a vehicle license, please provide the relevant details.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_vehicle_type`, text, optional): If you drive a company vehicle, please select the type of vehicle.
* **License Status** (`vehicle_license_status`, text, optional): If you have a driver's license, please indicate its status.
* **Driver License Status** (`vehicle_driver_license_status`, text, optional): If you have a driver's license, please indicate its status.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that you have all necessary information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the production team.
