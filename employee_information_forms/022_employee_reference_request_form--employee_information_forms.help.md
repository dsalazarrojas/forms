# Employee Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reference Request Form is used to collect information from the employee's current supervisor or HR representative to confirm employment details and facilitate a smooth transition during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required information accurately.
2. Make sure to provide complete and correct details about the employee's employment history.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (<employee_name>):**
  Please enter the name of the employee being referenced.
* **Job Title (<job_title>):**
  Enter the employee's job title during their employment.
* **Company Name (<company_name>):**
  Enter the name of the employee's current company.
* **Supervisor's Name (<supervisor_name>):**
  Enter the name of the employee's current supervisor.
* **Start Date (<start_date>):**
  Enter the date the employee started working with the company.
* **End Date (<end_date>):**
  Enter the date the employee left or is expected to leave the company.
* **Work Status (<work_status>):**
  Briefly describe the employee's work status during their employment (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
* **Work Address (<work_address>):**
  Enter the address of the employee's work location during their employment.
* **Employee Phone (<employee_phone>):**
  Enter the employee's contact phone number.

Note: All fields are optional, but it is essential to fill in the required information accurately to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
