# Employee Change Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Change Form is used to record changes made to an employee's status within the company, such as updates to their employment status or any reason for the change. This form helps the HR department to keep track of changes in a structured and organized manner, facilitating better decision-making and record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, select the employee's ID number.
2. Choose the old status of the employee (Active, Inactive, or Fired).
3. Select the new status of the employee (Active, Inactive, or Fired).
4. Optional: provide a reason for the change, if necessary.
5. Optional: add any comments or additional information about the change.
6. Click submit to save the changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_id**: Enter the unique identifier of the employee whose status is being changed.
* **Old Status**: Select the employee's current status (Active, Inactive, or Fired) from the dropdown list.
* **New Status**: Choose the new status of the employee (Active, Inactive, or Fired) from the dropdown list.
* **Reason**: Provide a detailed explanation of why the employee's status was changed, if applicable.
* **Comments**: Add any additional information or context about the change that may be helpful for future reference.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is reserved for future use and currently does not have any functionality. Please do not complete this field for now.
