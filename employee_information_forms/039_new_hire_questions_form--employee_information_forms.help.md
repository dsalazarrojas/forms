# new_hire_questions_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Questions Form is designed to collect essential information about new employees for onboarding and benefits enrollment purposes. It is crucial to complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth transition for the employee and to meet company requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email Address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Phone Number for contact and emergency contact purposes.
4. Enter your Date of Birth for age verification and benefits enrollment.
5. Enter your Hire Date for onboarding and benefits enrollment.
6. Select your Job Title and Job Type from the provided options.
7. Describe your Job Title and Job Responsibilities in a brief summary.
8. Enter your Salary and Salary Range for financial planning and benefits enrollment.
9. Enter the number of Work Hours you expect to work per week.
10. Add any benefits you receive, such as health insurance, 401(k), etc.
11. Provide contact information for your emergency contact, including their Name, Relationship, Phone Number, Email, and Address.
12. Confirm your emergency contact information for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, Select Multiple, required): Select one or more of the available job titles to describe your role, such as Senior Software Engineer or Team Lead.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address for communication and benefits enrollment.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number for contact and emergency contact purposes.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification and benefits enrollment.
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter your start date with the company for onboarding and benefits enrollment.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as described in the job description.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required): Enter the type of job you hold, such as software engineer or team lead.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, required): Describe your job responsibilities in a brief summary.
* **Salary** (`salary`, number, required): Enter your annual salary.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, number, required): Enter the range of your expected salary per year.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you expect to work per week.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, note, required): List any benefits you receive, such as health insurance or 401(k).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, required): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) and are essential for completing the form accurately.
