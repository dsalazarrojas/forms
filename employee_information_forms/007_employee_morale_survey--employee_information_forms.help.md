# employee_morale_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_morale_survey" form is designed to collect subjective feedback from employees regarding various aspects of the workplace. It is meant to provide insights to HR teams and managers to identify areas of improvement and enhance employee morale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant answer for each question and provide your feedback in the corresponding text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Satisfaction** (`<name>`, `text`, **required**):
    This field asks for your level of satisfaction with your current job. Please provide a brief description or explanation of your job satisfaction.
* **Communication** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is meant to understand how well you feel you are being communicated with by your colleagues and superiors. Please provide an answer that describes your experience.
* **Teamwork** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about how well you feel your colleagues work together as a team. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Work-Life Balance** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about how well you feel you are able to balance your work and personal life. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Company Culture** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is meant to understand how well the company culture aligns with your values and expectations. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Job Security** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about how secure you feel with your job. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about how satisfied you are with being an employee. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Company Image** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about how you perceive the image of the company. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Leadership** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is meant to understand how well you feel you are being led by your superiors. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Workplace Experience** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is about your overall experience in the workplace. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`<name>`, `text`, **not required**):
    This field is meant to understand your overall satisfaction with being an employee. Please provide a brief description or answer that describes your experience.

## Tips
- Please be as honest as possible when providing your feedback.
- Provide a brief description or answer that is specific to your experience.
- Do not repeat the same feedback for multiple questions.
- You can provide suggestions or ideas for improvement.
- You can also ask HR to provide further guidance or clarification if needed.
