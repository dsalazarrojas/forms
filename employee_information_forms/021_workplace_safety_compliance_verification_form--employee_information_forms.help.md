# Workplace Safety Compliance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about employee safety compliance in the workplace. It ensures that employees have the necessary knowledge, training, and resources to perform their job safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2.  Make sure you have the necessary information and resources to answer each question.
3.  If you're unsure about any question, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information Form** (`employee_info_form`, text, optional): This section is meant to collect general information about the employee. Please fill in your name, job title, and other relevant details.
* **Safety Standards Form** (`safety_standards_form`, text, optional): In this section, please describe the safety standards and procedures that apply to your job. This includes any relevant codes, regulations, or guidelines that you need to follow.
* **Safety Practices Form** (`safety_practices_form`, text, optional): Here, please list any safety practices or procedures that you perform regularly. This includes any specific protocols or processes that help you complete your job safely.
* **Training Record Form** (`training_record_form`, text, optional): This section is meant to record any safety training or certifications you have received. Please list the training you've received, when it was completed, and any certification numbers or dates.
* **Emergency Contact Form** (`emergency_contact_form`, text, optional): In this section, please provide emergency contact information, such as a contact person's name, phone number, and address.
* **Employee Certification Form** (`employee_certification_form`, text, optional): Here, please list any safety certifications, licenses, or qualifications that you possess. This includes any relevant credentials or qualifications that help you complete your job safely.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any question, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Please save and submit the form once you have completed all sections.
