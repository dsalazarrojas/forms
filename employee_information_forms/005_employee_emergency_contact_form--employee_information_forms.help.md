# Employee Emergency Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect emergency contact information from employees, which will be used to stay in touch with you and your loved ones in case of an emergency or critical situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your emergency contact's name in the **Employee Name** field.
2. Select the relationship between your emergency contact and you (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, etc.) from the **Relation** dropdown menu.
3. If your emergency contact is someone with a different relationship from the provided options, please describe the relationship in the **Other Relation** field.
4. Enter your emergency contact's email address in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your own name as it should be listed on any important documents.
* **Relation**: Select the relationship between your emergency contact and you from the provided list. For example, if your emergency contact is your mother, select "Mother".
* **Other Relation**: If your emergency contact has a relationship that is not on the list, describe it briefly in this field. For example, if your emergency contact is your neighbor, enter "Neighbor".
* **Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact. This will be used as a primary contact method.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact. This will be used as a secondary contact method.
* **Phone**: This field is optional and is used to store the phone number of your emergency contact. 

Note: Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that we can reach your emergency contacts in case of an emergency.
