# Form I 9 Employment Eligibility Verification - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to verify employment information about an employee, especially regarding their eligibility to work in the United States.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. If applicable, enter your Date of Hire in the "Date of Hire" field.
4. If applicable, enter your Social Security Number in the "Social Security Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
6. Enter the date you completed the I-9 form in the "I-9 Form Date Completed" field.
7. If applicable, enter the Certification Date in the "Certification Date" field.
8. If applicable, enter your I-9 Document Number in the "I-9 Document Number" field.
9. Enter the name of the Certifying Officer in the "Certifying Officer" field.
10. If applicable, enter your Employer Name in the "Employer Name" field.
11. If applicable, enter your full address in the "Address" field.
12. If applicable, enter the city for your address in the "City" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Last Name** (Last Name): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **First Name** (First Name): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Hire** (Date of Hire): Enter the date you started working for this employer (if applicable).
* **Social Security Number** (Social Security Number): Enter your social security number (if you have one).
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID): Enter your employee ID (if you have one).
* **I-9 Form Date Completed** (I-9 Form Date Completed): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Certification Date** (Certification Date): Enter the date you were certified (if applicable).
* **I-9 Document Number** (I-9 Document Number): Enter the document number (if applicable).
* **Certifying Officer** (Certifying Officer): Enter the name of the person who certified your status.
* **Employer Name** (Employer Name): Enter your employer's name (if you have one).
* **Address** (Address): Enter your full address (if you have one).
* **City** (City): Enter the city for your address (if you have one).

Note: Some fields might not be required, but they can be useful for additional context or administrative purposes. Make sure to check with your HR representative or supervisor for any specific requirements.
