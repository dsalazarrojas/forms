# Union Leader Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about union leaders, including their personal details, union affiliation, work location, and other relevant information. The purpose of this form is to provide an accurate and up-to-date record of union leaders, which can be used for various administrative and analytical purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your work email address in the "Email" field. This should be the email address associated with your work account.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
5. Select the type of email address you use (work or personal).
6. Choose the department you are associated with.
7. Enter your role/position in the union.
8. Select your work location (local, remote, or hybrid).
9. Enter the number of hours you have worked for the union.
10. Enter the start date of your union leadership position.
11. Enter the date you stopped leading the union (if applicable).
12. Select the type of union you are associated with (local, national, or international).
13. (Optional) Enter your lead position and years in the union.
14. Select the types of union members you are supervising (local, national, or international).
15. Enter the name of your union.
16. Enter the union number associated with your union.
17. Enter the type of work you do in the union.
18. (Optional) Enter the termination date of your union leadership position.
19. (Optional) Select the types of union leaders you supervise.
20. (Optional) Enter the number of employees you supervise.
21. (Optional) Enter the reason for termination.
22. (Optional) Select the type of termination (if applicable).
23. (Optional) Select the status of your union leadership position (active, inactive, or pending).
24. (Optional) Enter any additional comments about your union leadership role.
