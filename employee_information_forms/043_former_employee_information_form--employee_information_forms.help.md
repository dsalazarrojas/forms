# Former Employee Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Former Employee Information Form is designed for former employees to provide their information in a structured format, making it easier for the company to keep track of their details and ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Former Employee's Name**, which is the name of the person who is providing the information.
2. In the **Reason for Leaving** field, enter a brief explanation of why the employee left the company.
3. In the **Company Name** field, enter the name of the company where the employee was working.
4. In the **Department** field, enter the department where the employee was working.
5. In the **Job Title** field, enter the employee's job title.
6. In the **Date of Leaving** field, enter the date the employee left the company.
7. In the **Reason for Leaving Details** field, provide a more detailed explanation of why the employee left the company.
8. In the **Supervisor's Name** field, enter the name of the employee's supervisor or the person who was responsible for their work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Former Employee's Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the former employee.
* **Reason for Leaving** (text, optional): Enter a brief explanation of why the employee left the company.
* **Company Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the company where the employee was working.
* **Department** (text, optional): Enter the department where the employee was working.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Date of Leaving** (date, optional): Enter the date the employee left the company.
* **Reason for Leaving Details** (text, optional): Provide a more detailed explanation of why the employee left the company.
* **Supervisor's Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor or the person who was responsible for their work.
