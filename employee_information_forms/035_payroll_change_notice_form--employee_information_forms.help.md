# Payroll Change Notice Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Payroll Change Notice Form is used to report changes to an employee's job title, job classification, or other relevant information that may affect their pay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. If the employee's job title has changed, select 'Yes' in the "job_title_change" field.
3. If the employee's job classification has changed, select 'Yes' in the "job_classification_change" field.
4. If applicable, enter the new job title in the "job_title" field.
5. If applicable, enter the new job classification in the "job_classification" field.
6. Enter the manager's email address in the "manager_email" field.
7. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee (1)**: Enter the employee's name as it appears in our HR system.
* **Employee Name (2)**: Enter the employee's full name, as it appears on their ID card or personnel file.
* **Job Title (3)**: Enter the new job title, if applicable.
* **Manager Email (4)**: Enter the email address of the employee's manager.
* **Job Title Change (5)**: Select 'Yes' if the employee's job title has changed, 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Title Level Change (6)**: Select 'High', 'Low', or leave blank if the employee's job title level has not changed.
* **Job Classification (7)**: Select 'Yes' if the employee's job classification has changed, 'No' otherwise.
* **Job Classification Change (8)**: Select 'Yes', 'High', or 'Low' if the employee's job classification has changed, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and submitted only when a change has actually occurred.
* Submit the form promptly to ensure accurate and timely updates to the employee's pay.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact HR or payroll personnel for guidance.
