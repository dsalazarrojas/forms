# employment_information_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment information from employees, managers, or other relevant parties for employment-related purposes, such as HR or administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Employee Information Request Form** - The first page of this form contains the main information requested.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date details.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employment Information Request Form (Page 1)
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the company name where you work or are employed with.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Provide your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the following options: Employed, Unemployed, or Retired.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started working with your current company.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date you ended your employment with your current company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your current job title or position.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and role.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your professional phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your work or home address.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from the following options: Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern, or Freelance.
* **Manager Information** - This section collects information about your manager's details.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's job title or position.
* **Manager Contact Email** (`manager_contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's work email address.
* **Manager Contact Phone** (`manager_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's work phone number.
* **Manager Contact Address** (`manager_contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's work or home address.
* **Manager Employment Type** (`manager_employment_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose from the following options: Employed, Unemployed, or Retired.
* **Manager Employment Start Date** (`manager_employment_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your manager's employment start date.
* **Manager Employment End Date** (`manager_employment_end_date`, date, required/optional): If applicable, enter the date your manager ended their employment.
* **Other Employment Status** (`other_employment_status`, text, required/optional): If not listed above, enter your manager's other employment status.
* **Other Employment Details** (`other_employment_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about your manager's employment status.
* **Request Reason** (`request_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter your reason for requesting this form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required/optional): The date you submit this form.

Note: The fields marked "required/optional" are either required or optional depending on your specific situation. Please refer to the form's specific requirements for accurate completion.
