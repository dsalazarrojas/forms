# Shift Bid - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees about their shift preferences, allowing for easier shift assignment and management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Step 1: Input Your Information**
   * Fill out the form with your name and available start and end times.
   * If you have applied for shifts, select the ones you are interested in.
   * Indicate whether you are available to work shifts by checking "Yes" or "No" on the "Available Shifts" field.
   * Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Shift Bid" field to indicate your bid on a shift.

2. **Step 2: Review and Submit**
   * Carefully review your input to ensure accuracy.
   * Make sure to submit your form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or in your personnel file.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Shifts Applied For** (`shifts_applied`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have applied for shifts, select the ones you are interested in.
* **Available Shifts** (`available_shifts`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you are available to work shifts; check "No" if you are not.
* **Shift Bid** (`shift_bid`, select_one, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you are interested in bidding on a shift; check "No" if you are not.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your shift bid.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately input your start and end times to ensure you are correctly scheduled.
* Be honest about your availability to avoid conflicts with your shift schedule.
* Clearly indicate your interest in bidding on a shift to ensure management can review your application correctly.
