# Direct Deposit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Direct Deposit Form Template is a tool used to collect information from employees about their direct deposit settings. This form is meant for employees who wish to set up or modify their direct deposit arrangements. It is an essential tool for HR administrators to process and manage direct deposit requests accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Direct Deposit Form Template, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Enter Your Information
- Please enter your name and title in the designated fields. This information will help us process your direct deposit request correctly.

### Step 2: Select Your Bank Account Information
- Choose whether you have a bank account (`bank_account` field).
- If you have a bank account, enter the account number in the `account_number` field.
- Select your bank routing numbers from the list (`bank_routing_numbers` field).
- Choose your account type from the list (`account_type` field). This will help us identify the type of account you have.

### Step 3: Check Your Account Types
- Select the types of accounts you have from the list (`account_type_0`, `account_type_1`, `account_type_2`, `account_type_3`, `account_type_4`, `account_type_5` fields). For each type, choose 'Yes' if you have that type of account.

### Step 4: Review and Submit
- Carefully review your information for accuracy and completeness.
- Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your bank account and payroll records.
* **bank_account** (text, optional): Enter your bank account number for direct deposit processing.
* **account_number** (text, optional): Enter the bank account number you want to use for direct deposit.
* **direct_deposit_form** (text, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the form title. We will disregard its value.
* **bank_routing_numbers** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a bank account with routing numbers.
* **routing_numbers** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have bank routing numbers.
* **account_type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of account you have from the available options. For example, 'Yes' for checking/savings, 'No' for another type.
* **account_type_0** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a checking or savings account.
* **account_type_1** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a credit account.
* **account_type_2** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a savings account.
* **account_type_3** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a money market account.
* **account_type_4** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have a credit card account.
* **account_type_5** (select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you have an other account.

## Tips
- Double-check your account information and account types to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any of the options, please contact your HR representative for assistance.
- This form will help us process your direct deposit request efficiently and accurately.
