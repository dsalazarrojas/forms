# timesheet_submission_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Timesheet Submission Verification Form is designed to verify employee submitted timesheets. This form helps to ensure that submitted timesheets are accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your employee details.
2. Enter your job information in the Job Info section.
3. Provide the necessary information about your timesheet in the Timesheet Info section.
4. Select the date of submission from the calendar in the Submission Date field.
5. Choose the submission time from the clock in the Submission Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (text)**:
	+ Label: User Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Use this section to enter your employee details such as your name, employee ID, and any other relevant information.
* **Job Info (text)**:
	+ Label: Job Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Enter your job title, department, or any other relevant job-related information.
* **Timesheet Info (text)**:
	+ Label: Timesheet Info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Describe your work hours, tasks, or any other information related to your timesheet.
* **Submission Date (date)**:
	+ Label: Submission Date
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: false
	+ Select the date you submitted your timesheet using the calendar.
* **Submission Time (time)**:
	+ Label: Submission Time
	+ Type: time
	+ Required: false
	+ Choose the time you submitted your timesheet using the clock.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your submission date and time for accuracy.
* Verify that all required fields are filled out completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Review and submit the form carefully before sending it in.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact your HR representative or IT department.
