# Payroll System Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for access to the payroll system. It gathers essential information to determine the level of access each employee requires.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and requirements.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the payroll system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name and any other relevant information.
* **Payroll System Approvals** (`payroll_system_approvals`, text, optional): Enter any relevant approval information, such as approval dates and times.
* **Access Levels** (`access_levels`, select_one, optional): Select the level of access the employee requires, from Level 1 to Level 3.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the employee's department.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job description.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the employee's date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter the employee's time of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Employee Manager** (`employee_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select the employee's manager(s) from the available options.
* **Job Manager** (`job_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select the employee's job manager(s) from the available options.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of access the employee requires, from Level 1 to Level 3.
* **Department Head** (`department_head`, select_one, optional): Select the department head from the available options.
* **Payroll Manager** (`payroll_manager`, select_one, optional): Select the payroll manager from the available options.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, optional): Select the approval status, from Approved, Pending, or Rejected.
* **Access Level 2** (`access_level2`, select_one, optional): Select the level of access the employee requires, from Level 1 to Level 3.
* **Access Level 3** (`access_level3`, select_one, optional): Select the level of access the employee requires, from Level 1 to Level 3.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): Enter the approval date.
* **Approval Time** (`approval_time`, time, optional): Enter the approval time.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select the submission status, from Approved, Pending, or Rejected.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the submission time.

Note: All fields are optional unless specifically marked as required. Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the payroll system before filling out this form.
