# Employee Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Travel Authorization Form is a request to authorize employee travel. This form is used to track and manage travel approvals for employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the first question, "Travel to conference?" by selecting "Yes" or "No". This will determine the type of travel authorization required.
2. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Indicate the reason for the travel in the "Traveling For" field by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
4. Enter your department name in the "Employee Department" field.
5. Select a date for the travel in the "Travel Dates" field.
6. Choose a start and end time for the travel in the "Travel Start" and "Travel End" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel to Conference?** (id: 1, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ This field is used to determine the type of travel authorization required. Select "Yes" if you are traveling for a conference, "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (id: 2, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Traveling For** (id: 3, type: select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all applicable reasons for the travel. This field is used to track the purpose of the travel.
* **Employee Department** (id: 4, type: select_one, required: false)
	+ Select your department name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Employee Location** (id: 5, type: text, required: false)
	+ Enter your current location, which could be a city or a building.
* **Travel Dates** (id: 6, type: date, required: false)
	+ Select the date(s) you plan to travel. You can enter a single date or a range of dates.
* **Travel Start** (id: 7, type: time, required: false)
	+ Enter the start time of your travel.
* **Travel End** (id: 8, type: time, required: false)
	+ Enter the end time of your travel.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about what type of travel authorization is required, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* Please ensure that your department is properly updated in the form.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and approval of your travel.
