# Visitor Request Form BlinkNow - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by visitors to submit their requests to BlinkNow staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to review the field explanations below for any specific guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (1)
 * **Name**: This field is for you to enter your name, which will be used to identify you as the visitor.
### Visitor Title (2)
 * **Visitor Title**: Please enter a brief title or description of who you are visiting, e.g., "John Doe, Customer Support".
### Department (3)
 * **Department**: Select the department you are visiting, which will help us direct your request to the right person. Options: IT, Facilities, Management, Accounting.
### Purpose of Visit (4)
 * **Purpose of Visit**: Describe the reason for your visit in a few words, e.g., "To discuss new project requirements".
### Arrival Date (5)
 * **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at BlinkNow.
### Arrival Time (6)
 * **Arrival Time**: Enter the time you plan to arrive at BlinkNow.
### Contact Phone (7)
 * **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, which we can use to reach out to you if needed.
### Contact Email (8)
 * **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email, which we can use to reach out to you if needed.
### Notes (9)
 * **Notes**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your visit.
### Assigned to (10)
 * **Assigned to**: Select the person you would like to be assigned to handle your request. Options: chatjimmy, Other (if you have a different person in mind).
### Status (11)
 * **Status**: Please select the current status of your request. Options: New, In Progress, Done.

Tips:
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to reach out to us directly.
