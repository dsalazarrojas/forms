# home_care_support_worker_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The home care support worker intake form is used to collect relevant information from home care support workers. This form will be reviewed and stored for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately.
* Select the relevant category or categories that best describe your work experience or qualifications.
* Provide a detailed description of your relevant skills or experience.
* Fill in your first and last name as they appear on your identification documents.
* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* Provide the number of years you have been working as a home care support worker.
* If applicable, list your relevant qualifications or certifications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, **required: false**): Select the categories that best describe your experience or qualifications as a home care support worker.
* **Description** (`description`, text, **required: false**): Provide a detailed description of your relevant skills or experience.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required: false**): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required: false**): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required: false**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required: false**): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, **required: false**): Enter the number of years you have been working as a home care support worker.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, text, **required: false**): If applicable, list your relevant qualifications or certifications.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
* The form will be reviewed and stored for future reference.
