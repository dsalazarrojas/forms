# Employee Refusal Of Medical Treatment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to formally document an employee's refusal of medical treatment. It's essential to understand that refusing medical treatment can lead to health risks and potential complications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions and purpose carefully.
2. Complete each field thoroughly and accurately.
3. Select the correct option for each field as instructed.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your refusal of medical treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, employee ID, and contact information so we can reach you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Refuse Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_refusal`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate your refusal of medical treatment:
	+ Option 1: Refuse all medical treatment
	+ Option 2: Refuse specific medical treatment (please specify in the "Medical Condition" field)
* **Acknowledge Medical Risks** (`acknowledge`, select_one, optional): Please indicate your understanding of the potential risks associated with refusing medical treatment:
	+ Option 1: I understand the potential risks and consequences of refusing medical treatment.
	+ Option 2: I do not fully understand the potential risks and consequences of refusing medical treatment.
* **Consent to Treatment** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to indicate your consent to treatment:
	+ Option 1: Consent to medical treatment
	+ Option 2: Do not consent to medical treatment
* **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, text, optional): If you have a medical contact (e.g., doctor, nurse), please provide their name and contact information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have a medical condition that relates to your refusal of medical treatment, please describe it briefly.
* **Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, text, optional): If you have a specific medical treatment you refuse, please describe it briefly.
* **Medical Risks** (`medical_risks`, text, optional): Please describe the potential risks associated with refusing medical treatment and how it may impact your health and well-being.

## Tips
- Please be aware that refusing medical treatment can lead to serious health consequences.
- Make sure to consult with your supervisor or HR representative if you're unsure about any part of this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
