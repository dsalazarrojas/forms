# Short Term Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Absence Form is used to report employee absences that last less than 5 days. This form is typically used for short-term absences such as vacation, personal, or family-related reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name.
2. Enter the Start Date of your absence.
3. Enter the End Date of your absence.
4. Provide a Reason for your absence.
5. Write a Manager Note to keep your manager informed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Name (`employee_name`, Text, Optional)
* Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record or ID badge.

### Start Date (`start_date`, Date, Optional)
* Enter the date you start your absence, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### End Date (`end_date`, Date, Optional)
* Enter the date you expect to return to work, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### Reason (`reason`, Text, Optional)
* Please provide a brief explanation for your absence.

### Manager Note (`manager_note`, Note, Optional)
* Write a short note to keep your manager informed about your absence and any important details.

## Tips
* If your absence is due to a personal or family-related reason, please be as detailed as possible in the "Reason" field.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact your HR department or the absent employee's manager.
* Please submit the completed form at least 24 hours before your expected absence date to ensure timely processing.
