# Daily Health Monitoring Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about an employee's health status on a daily basis. This includes their current health conditions, medications, medical history, and other relevant details. The form is used to keep track of employee health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for "Health Conditions" as required.
2. If taking medication, enter the name of the medication in the "Medication" field.
3. In the "Medical History" field, provide a brief description of your medical history.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the timezone where you are submitting the form from in the "Submitted By Time Zone" field.
7. Select the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted By" field.
8. Select the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To" field.
9. Select the department who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Department" field.
10. Select the role of the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Role" field.
11. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Email" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Phone" field.
13. Select the department of the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Department" field.
14. Select the role of the person who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To Role" field.
15. Select the user who submitted the form to in the "Submitted To User" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, required): Enter your employee ID or username.
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select any health conditions you are currently experiencing.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If you are taking any medication, enter the name of the medication.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your medical history.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, required): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Time Zone** (`submitted_by_time_zone`, select_one, optional): Select the timezone where you are submitting the form from.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Department** (`submitted_to_department`, select_one, optional): Select the department who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Role** (`submitted_to_role`, select_one, optional): Select the role of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Email** (`submitted_to_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Phone** (`submitted_to_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Department 1** (`submitted_to_department_1`, select_one, optional): Select the department of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Role 1** (`submitted_to_role_1`, select_one, optional): Select the role of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To User** (`submitted_to_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who submitted the form to.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Submitted To Email** (`submitted_to_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Phone** (`submitted_to_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Department** (`submitted_to_department`, select_one, optional): Select the department of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To Role** (`submitted_to_role`, select_one, optional): Select the role of the person who submitted the form to.
* **Submitted To User** (`submitted_to_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who submitted the form to.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your health status.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Please select the correct timezone where you are submitting the form from.
