# Remote Employee Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for remote employees who are terminating their employment with our company. The goal is to gather necessary details to ensure a smooth offboarding process and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required and optional information according to your circumstances.
2. Be as detailed and accurate as possible when providing information, especially for fields like email, phone number, last day of work, hours worked, and reason for departure.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the most relevant or accurate choice.
4. If you're unsure about any field, you can ask your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Provide your current contact information, including your email address, phone number, and name.

### Company Details (Page 1)
* **Company (company)**: Enter the name of the company you are associated with.
* **Job Title (job_title)**: Enter your job title while working with us.

### Departure Details (Page 1)
* **Departure Details (departure_info)**: Provide a brief description of your reason for leaving the company.
* **Reason for Departure (reason_for_departure)**: Select all that apply to your reason for leaving. You can choose from the following options: poor performance, job offer, company change, personal, or other (please specify in the text field).
* **Last Day of Work (last_day_of_work)**: Enter the date when you last worked with the company.
* **Hours Worked (hours_worked)**: If possible, provide the number of hours you worked while with the company.
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any comments or notes about your experience with the company.
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.

### Status Change (Page 2)
* **Job Status Change (job_status)**: Select whether your job is active or inactive.
* **Company Change (company_change)**: Select whether you are changing companies or not.
* **Termination (termination)**: Select whether you are terminating your employment with the company.
* **Termination Date (termination_date)**: Enter the date of your termination.
* **Termination Time (termination_time)**: Enter the time of your termination.
* **Reason for Returning (reason_for_returning)**: Select whether you are returning to work or not.
* **Return Date (return_date)**: Enter the date you plan to return to work.
* **Return Time (return_time)**: Enter the time you plan to return to work.
* **Other Return Information (other_return_info)**: If applicable, provide any additional information about your return to work.
