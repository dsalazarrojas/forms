# Employee Handover Documentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Handover Documentation form is designed to capture information about employee handovers, ensuring that all relevant details are documented and communicated effectively during the handover process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the Employee Handover Documentation field with a brief description of the job or task being handed over.
2. Enter employee information (ID number, name, etc.) in the Employee Information field.
3. If your manager's name is provided, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Manager Approval field.
4. Enter the employee's name in the Employee Name field.
5. Enter the manager's name in the Manager Name field.
6. Enter the transition date in the Transition Date field.
7. Enter the transition time in the Transition Time field.
8. Provide a brief reason for the handover in the Reason for Handover field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Employee Information** (`1`, `number`, required: `false`)
The Employee Information field captures the employee's ID number and name. Enter the employee's ID number and name, if applicable.

### **Manager Approval** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
This field is required if your manager's name is provided. Select 'Yes' if the manager approves the handover, or 'No' if they do not.

### **Employee Name** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter the employee's name in this field.

### **Manager Name** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter the manager's name in this field.

### **Transition Date** (`6`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter the date of the transition in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### **Transition Time** (`7`, `time`, required: `false`)
Enter the time of the transition in the format `HH:MM:SS`.

### **Reason for Handover** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`)
Provide a brief reason for the handover, explaining why the employee is transitioning out of the role or task.

### **Manager Name** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter the manager's name in this field.

### **Employee Handover Documentation** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter a brief description of the job or task being handed over.

Note: Please ensure that you have all necessary approvals and information before completing this form. If you are unsure about any aspect of the handover process, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
