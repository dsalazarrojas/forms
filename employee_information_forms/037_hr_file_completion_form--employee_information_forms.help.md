# HR File Completion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR File Completion Form is a document used to collect and store employee information and ensure compliance with company policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the employee's first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the "Email" field.
4. If applicable, enter the emergency contact's first and last name in the "Emergency Contact First Name" and "Emergency Contact Last Name" fields, respectively.
5. Select the employee's department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
6. Select the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact from the "Relationship" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the employee's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. If applicable, select the employee's start and end dates and times from the "Start Date and Time" and "End Date and Time" fields, respectively.
9. Review and acknowledge the relevant company policies by selecting the appropriate options from the "Policy Acknowledgment" fields.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`employee_first_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (`employee_last_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the emergency contact's last name.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, select_multiple, required): Select the employee's department from the dropdown menu.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the employee and the emergency contact from the dropdown menu.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Extension** (`employee_extension`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the employee's extension number.
* **Start Date and Time** (`employee_start_date_time`, time, required): Select the employee's start date and time.
* **End Date and Time** (`employee_end_date_time`, time, optional): If applicable, select the employee's end date and time.
* **Employee Documents** (`employee_document_1`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any relevant employee documents.
* **Document 2** (`employee_document_2`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any additional documents.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 1** (`policy_acknowledgment_1`, select_multiple, required): Review and acknowledge the first policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 2** (`policy_acknowledgment_2`, select_multiple, required): Review and acknowledge the second policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 3** (`policy_acknowledgment_3`, select_multiple, required): Review and acknowledge the third policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 4** (`policy_acknowledgment_4`, select_multiple, optional): Review and acknowledge the fourth policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 5** (`policy_acknowledgment_5`, select_multiple, optional): Review and acknowledge the fifth policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 6** (`policy_acknowledgment_6`, select_multiple, optional): Review and acknowledge the sixth policy.
* **Policy Acknowledgment 7** (`policy_acknowledgment_7`, select_multiple, optional): Review and acknowledge the seventh policy.
* **Note** (`employee_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments, if necessary.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Review each policy carefully before acknowledging it.
* If you're unsure about any field or policy, please contact your HR representative for clarification.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* The form is designed to be completed in conjunction with company policies and procedures.
