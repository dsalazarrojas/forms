# Workplace Readiness Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Readiness Declaration Form is a document used to collect information from employees about their work status, contact details, and other relevant information before returning to work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Enter your emergency contact information in the designated fields.
4. Indicate your work location, schedule, and any travel restrictions.
5. Provide any medical clearance information, if applicable.
6. Enter your return to work status and provide the reason, if applicable.
7. Enter any other work location information, if applicable.
8. Select your current work status and provide the reason, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Location** (1): Select 'Yes' if you are currently working from your usual work location, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Work Schedule** (2): Select 'Yes' if you are currently working your regular schedule, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Work From Home** (3): Select 'Yes' if you are currently working from home, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (4): Enter the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (5): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (6): Select 'Yes' if the emergency contact is a family member or friend, or 'No' if it is a colleague or other.
* **Workplace Location** (7): Enter the address or name of your current workplace.
* **Workplace Address** (8): Enter the address of your current workplace.
* **Return to Work Date** (9): Enter the date you are expected to return to work.
* **Return to Work Time** (10): Enter the time you are expected to return to work.
* **Return to Work Location** (11): Enter the location where you will be returning to work.
* **Health Declaration** (12): Enter any health issues that may affect your work.
* **Travel Restrictions** (13): Select 'Yes' if you have travel restrictions, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Travel Restriction Details** (14): Enter any details about your travel restrictions.
* **Medical Clearance** (15): Select 'Yes' if you have received medical clearance, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Medical Clearance Date** (16): Enter the date you received medical clearance.
* **Medical Clearance Provider** (17): Enter the name of the medical clearance provider.
* **Return to Work Status** (18): Select your current work status.
* **Return to Work Status Reason** (19): Enter the reason for your current work status.
* **Other Work Location** (20): Enter any other work location information.
* **Other Work Location Address** (21): Enter the address of the other work location.
* **Other Work Location Type** (22): Select 'Yes' if the other work location is your usual work location, or 'No' if it is not.
* **Other Work Location Details** (23): Enter any other work location details.
* **Work Status** (24): Select your current work status.
* **Work Status Reason** (25): Enter the reason for your current work status.
