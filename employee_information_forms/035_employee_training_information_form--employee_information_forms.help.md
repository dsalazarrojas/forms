# Employee Training Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Training Information Form is used to collect and store information about employee training programs. This form helps HR or management staff track, report, and manage employee training activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, which are highlighted in bold in the field-by-field explanation section.
2. Enter information in the corresponding fields, using the format specified for each field type (e.g., date, text, email).
3. Review and submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Training (id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of the training program.
* **Training Program (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the training program.
* **Start Date (id: 3)**: Enter the start date of the training in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Training Duration (id: 4)**: Enter the duration of the training in hours.
* **Training Hours (id: 5)**: Enter the total hours of training.
* **Training Location (id: 6)**: Enter the location of the training.
* **Training Status (id: 7)**: Select the current status of the training (Complete, In Progress, Pending, or Cancelled).
* **Trainee Name (id: 8)**: Enter the name of the trainee.
* **Trainee Email (id: 9)**: Enter the email address of the trainee.
* **Trainee Phone (id: 10)**: Enter the phone number of the trainee.
* **Training Date (id: 11)**: Enter the date of the training in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Training Time (id: 12)**: Enter the time of the training.
* **Supervisor Name (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email (id: 14)**: Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone (id: 15)**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
* **Assigned Manager (id: 16)**: Enter the name of the assigned manager.
* **Notes (id: 17)**: Enter any additional notes about the training.
* **Trainee Notes (id: 18)**: Enter any additional notes from the trainee.
* **Agreed (id: 19)**: Enter any agreement or acknowledgement from the trainee.

Note: Some fields may be optional, indicated by a blank or a faint gray background.
