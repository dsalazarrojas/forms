# Project Manager Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about project managers' personal details, work history, and project-related information. It appears to be used for internal purposes, such as HR-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your project manager's personal details in the "Personal Details" field.
2. Provide your work history in the "Work History" field.
3. Describe your project experience in the "Experience" field.
4. Enter your job title and work status in the "Job Title" and "Work Status" fields, respectively.
5. Select your work status and manager status in the "Work Status", "Manager Status", and "Project Manager Status" fields.
6. Choose your department and role from the "Department" and "Role" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the project name and start date in the "Project Name" and "Project Start Date" fields, respectively.
8. Provide the project finish date and reason in the "Project Finish Date" and "Project Finish Reason" fields, respectively.
9. Enter your manager's contact information in the "Manager Contact" field.
10. Describe your project manager role and project description in the "Project Manager Role" and "Project Description" fields.
11. Finally, enter your job position and employee number in the "Job Position" and "Employee Number" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details
* **Personal Details**: Enter your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information.
* **Manager**: Enter your manager's name and contact information.

### Work History
* **Work History**: Describe your work history, including your previous projects and job experiences.

### Project Details
* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are currently managing.
* **Project Start Date**: Enter the start date of the project.
* **Project Finish Date**: Enter the finish date of the project.
* **Project Finish Reason**: Describe the reason for project finish.
* **Project Manager**: Enter your name as the project manager.
* **Project Description**: Describe the project description.
* **Project Manager Description**: Describe your role as project manager.

### Status
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Work Status**: Select your current work status.
* **Manager Status**: Select your manager's status.
* **Project Manager Status**: Select your project manager status.

### Department and Role
* **Department**: Select your department.
* **Role**: Select your role.

### Additional Information
* **Project Manager Role**: Select your project manager role.
* **Project Manager**: Enter your name as project manager.
* **Job Position**: Enter your job position.
* **Employee Number**: Enter your employee number.
