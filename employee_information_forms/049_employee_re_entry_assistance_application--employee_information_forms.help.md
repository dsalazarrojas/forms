# Employee Re Entry Assistance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Re Entry Assistance Application form is a crucial tool for HR departments to collect and process employee re-entry requests. This form helps to gather essential information about the employee's return date, reason for return, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name in the `Employee Name` field.
2. Enter the `Return Date` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the `Reason for Return`.
4. Select the `Employee Status` from the dropdown list.
5. Select the `Return Status` from the dropdown list.
6. Enter the `Manager Approval` comments.
7. Enter the `Approval Date` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. Enter the `Submission Status` and confirm it is accurate.
9. Fill in the `Submitted By` details, including the username, department, job title, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
    *   **Return Date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the return date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
    *   **Reason for Return** (`reason_for_return`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the return.
    *   **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`, date, required): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the employee.
        +   **Active**: The employee is currently employed.
        +   **In Active**: The employee is not currently employed but was previously active.
        +   **In Leave**: The employee is on leave.
        +   **Resigned**: The employee has resigned from the company.
        +   **Terminated**: The employee's employment has been terminated.
        +   **Probationary Leave**: The employee is on a probationary leave.
        +   **Probationary Resigned**: The employee is on a probationary resigned status.
        +   **Probationary Terminated**: The employee's employment has been terminated due to probationary reasons.
    *   **Return Status** (`return_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the return.
        +   **Active**: The employee is returning to work.
        +   **In Active**: The employee is not returning to work but was previously active.
        +   **In Leave**: The employee is on leave and will return.
        +   **Resigned**: The employee is returning after resignation.
        +   **Terminated**: The employee's employment will be reinstated after termination.
        +   **Probationary Leave**: The employee is on a probationary leave status.
        +   **Probationary Resigned**: The employee is on a probationary resigned status.
        +   **Probationary Terminated**: The employee's employment will be reinstated after termination due to probationary reasons.
        +   **On Probation**: The employee is on probation.
        +   **No Status**: The employee's status is unknown.
    *   **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, text, required): Enter the manager's approval comments.
    *   **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the manager's approval in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
    *   **Manager Comment** (`manager_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments from the manager.
    *   **Employee Comment** (`employee_comment`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments from the employee.
    *   **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, text, required): Confirm the submission status.
    *   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Fill in the details of the person submitting the form.
    *   **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
    *   **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by username** (`submitted_by_username`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by user id** (`submitted_by_userid`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by department** (`submitted_by_department`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by job title** (`submitted_by_job_title`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by email** (`submitted_by_email`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by department 2** (`submitted_by_department_2`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by username 2** (`submitted_by_username_2`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
    *   **Submitted by username 3** (`submitted_by_username_3`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.
