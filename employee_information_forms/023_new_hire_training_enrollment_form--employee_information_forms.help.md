# new_hire_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Training Enrollment Form is used to collect information about a new hire, including their name, contact details, training programs, and job-related information. This form is required for all new hires and must be completed in full to ensure accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an *asterisk*. These include:
	* `new_hire_name` (new hire's name)
	* `new_hire_email` (new hire's email address)
	* `new_hire_phone` (new hire's phone number)
	* `new_hire_training_programs` (select multiple training programs)
	* `new_hire_category` (new hire's category)
	* `new_hire_department` (new hire's department)
	* `new_hire_job_title` (new hire's job title)
	* `new_hire_manager` (new hire's supervisor)
	* `new_hire_start_date` (new hire's start date)
	* `new_hire_status` (new hire's status)
	* `new_hire_supervisor` (new hire's supervisor)
	* `new_hire_terminates` (new hire's termination status)
	* `new_hire_termination_date` (new hire's termination date)
	* `new_hire_termination_status` (new hire's termination status)
	* `new_hire_rehire` (whether the new hire has a rehire option)
	* `new_hire_review` (new hire's review options)

Note: All fields with a *asterisk* next to them are required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_hire_name**: *new hire's name* - Enter the new hire's full name.
* **new_hire_email**: *new hire's email* - Enter the new hire's email address.
* **new_hire_phone**: *new hire's phone* - Enter the new hire's phone number.
* **new_hire_training_programs**: *select multiple training programs* - Select the training programs the new hire will be enrolling in.
* **new_hire_category**: *new hire's category* - Select the new hire's category from the options provided. This field is required.
* **new_hire_department**: *new hire's department* - Select the new hire's department from the options provided. This field is required.
* **new_hire_job_title**: *new hire's job title* - Enter the new hire's job title.
* **new_hire_manager**: *new hire's supervisor* - Select the new hire's supervisor from the options provided. This field is required.
* **new_hire_start_date**: *new hire's start date* - Enter the new hire's start date.
* **new_hire_end_date**: *new hire's end date* - Enter the new hire's end date. Note: This field is optional.
* **new_hire_status**: *new hire's status* - Select the new hire's status from the options provided. This field is required.
* **new_hire_supervisor**: *new hire's supervisor* - Select the new hire's supervisor from the options provided. This field is required.
* **new_hire_job_description**: *new hire's job description* - Enter the new hire's job description. Note: This field is optional.
* **new_hire_terminates**: *new hire's termination status* - Select the new hire's termination status. This field is required.
* **new_hire_termination_date**: *new hire's termination date* - Enter the new hire's termination date. Note: This field is optional.
* **new_hire_terminates_reason**: *new hire's termination reason* - Enter the reason for the new hire's termination. Note: This field is optional.
* **new_hire_termination_status**: *new hire's termination status* - Select the new hire's termination status. This field is required.
* **new_hire_termination_note**: *new hire's termination note* - Enter any additional notes related to the new hire's termination. Note: This field is optional.
* **new_hire_rehire**: *whether the new hire has a rehire option* - Select whether the new hire has a rehire option. This field is required.
* **new_hire_review**: *new hire's review options* - Select the new hire's review options. This field is required.
