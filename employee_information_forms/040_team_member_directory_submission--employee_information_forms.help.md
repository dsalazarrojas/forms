# Team Member Directory Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store employee information for the team directory.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your correct information.
2. Upload your profile picture.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address (optional).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the dropdown menu (required).
* **Department**: Enter your department name (required).
* **Position**: Enter your position title (required).
* **Location**: Enter your working location (optional).
* **Profile Picture**: Upload a photo to represent yourself in the team directory.
* **Bio**: Write a brief bio about yourself (optional).
* **Job Description**: Write a brief description of your job (optional).
* **LinkedIn**: Enter your LinkedIn profile URL (optional).
* **Resume**: Upload your resume or CV (optional).
* **Photo Description**: Add a description of your profile picture (optional).
* **Team Lead**: Select whether you are a team lead (optional).
* **Manager**: Select whether you have a manager (optional).
* **Director**: Select whether you are a director (optional).
* **Director Title**: Enter your director title (optional).
* **Director Bio**: Write a brief bio about yourself as a director (optional).
* **Team Member**: Select whether you are a team member (optional).
* **Reporting Manager**: Select your reporting manager (optional).
* **Reporting Director**: Select your reporting director (optional).
* **Created By**: This field will be populated automatically (optional).
* **Created At**: This field will be populated automatically (optional).
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person who submitted you (optional).
* **Created At**: This field will be populated automatically (optional).

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in before submission.
