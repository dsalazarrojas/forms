# employee_benefits_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_benefits_request_form is a tool for employees to request benefits such as paid time off, training, or other forms of support. It is intended to be used by employees to submit their requests in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an employee of the company and have not already submitted a benefits request in the past 30 days.
2. Select the benefits you would like to request from the options provided.
3. Fill in your job title, department, and location as relevant.
4. Select your department from the options provided.
5. Enter your employee name and number if requested.
6. Enter your date of birth and job start date if requested.
7. Fill in the date you are requesting the benefits.

## Tips
- Be specific when requesting benefits to ensure your needs are met.
- Review the available options carefully before making your selection.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to your HR representative.
