# Employee Withholding Allowance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture information regarding an employee's withholding allowance claims. It helps to track and manage employee allowance claims, including the type of allowance claimed, the start and end dates of the allowance, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form purpose and fields to understand the required information.
2. Select the "employee_details" page to enter the employee's personal information.
3. On the "allowance_claimed" page, choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the employee claims an allowance.
4. On the "allowance_type" page, select the type of allowance the employee is claiming (if applicable).
5. Enter the tax rate as a numeric value.
6. Enter the allowance amount as a numeric value.
7. Enter the employee ID as a numeric value.
8. Select the "allowance_start_date" and "allowance_end_date" pages to enter the start and end dates of the allowance period.
9. On the "tax_authority" page, choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the tax authority has been notified.
10. On the "account_number" page, enter the account number associated with the allowance claim (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details** (Employee Details, text, Optional): Enter the employee's name and any other personal information.
* **Allowance Claimed** (Allowance Claimed, select_one, Optional): Check 'Yes' to indicate that an allowance is claimed.
* **Allowance Type** (Allowance Type, select_multiple, Optional): Select the type of allowance being claimed (if applicable).
* **Tax Rate** (Tax Rate, number, Optional): Enter the tax rate as a numeric value.
* **Allowance Amount** (Allowance Amount, number, Optional): Enter the allowance amount as a numeric value.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, Optional): Enter the employee ID as a numeric value.
* **Allowance Start Date** (Allowance Start Date, date, Optional): Enter the start date of the allowance period.
* **Allowance End Date** (Allowance End Date, date, Optional): Enter the end date of the allowance period.
* **Tax Authority** (Tax Authority, select_one, Optional): Check 'Yes' to indicate that the tax authority has been notified.
* **Account Number** (Account Number, text, Optional): Enter the account number associated with the allowance claim (if applicable).
