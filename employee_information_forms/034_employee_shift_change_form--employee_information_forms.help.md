# employee_shift_change_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_shift_change_form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their shift changes. It is intended for use by employees to notify their employer of their shift changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the `User Information` section.
2. In the `Shift Change` section, describe the reason for your shift change.
3. In the `Shift Start Date` section, enter the date you want to start your new shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your name and any other relevant personal information.
* **Shift Change** (`shift_change`, `text`, optional):
	+ Describe the reason for your shift change, such as a vacation request or a schedule conflict.
* **Shift Start Date** (`shift_start_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date you want to start your new shift. This should be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
