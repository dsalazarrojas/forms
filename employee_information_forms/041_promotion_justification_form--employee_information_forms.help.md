# Promotion Justification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Justification Form is used to document and justify promotions within the organization. It provides a standardized structure for employees to provide information about their promotions, ensuring consistency and accuracy in tracking and reviewing promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee details, including your name and ID (fields 1 and 6).
2. Select the promotion date (field 2) and promotion type (field 8).
3. Provide a clear justification for your promotion (field 4).
4. Attach any relevant evidence or supporting documents (field 5).
5. Specify your current manager and department (fields 12 and 10).
6. Confirm your status as active or inactive within the organization (fields 14 and 15).
7. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee**: Enter your name and employee ID for reference and tracking (field 1).
* **Promotion Date**: Select the date of your promotion (field 2).
* **Justification**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for your promotion, including any supporting evidence (field 3).
* **Evidence**: Attach any supporting documents or records that justify your promotion (field 4).
* **Manager**: Specify your current manager (field 6).
* **Employee Name**: Verify your name and ID for accuracy (field 7).
* **Promotion Reason**: Enter a brief reason for your promotion (field 8).
* **Promotion Type**: Select whether your promotion is active or inactive (field 9).
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for tracking and reference (field 10).
* **Department**: Specify your department (field 11).
* **Team**: Select the teams you belong to (field 12).
* **Reporting Manager**: Specify your reporting manager (field 13).
* **Promotion Effective Date**: Select the date your promotion took effect (field 14).
* **Promotion Status**: Confirm your status as active or inactive within the organization (field 15).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have attached all required supporting evidence or documents.
* Double-check your entry accuracy, especially your employee details and promotion date.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
