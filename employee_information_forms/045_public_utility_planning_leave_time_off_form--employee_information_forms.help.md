# Public Utility Planning Leave Time Off Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a time off from work for public utility planning. This includes requesting a leave of absence for various reasons such as vacations, sick leave, or other personal events. The form is designed to capture all necessary information for the employee's leave request and the approval process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID or name (Employee Request) in the Employee Request field.
2. Select the date you are requesting the leave (Date Requesting).
3. Check the box for Time Off Type if you are requesting a leave.
4. Fill in the Start Date and End Date of your leave.
5. Provide a reason for your leave in the Reason For Leave field.
6. If you wish to add any additional comments, you can do so in the Comment field.
7. Sign and date the form as an employee and have it approved by your supervisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Request**: Please enter your employee ID or name to associate this leave request with your HR record.
* **Date Requesting**: Enter the date you are requesting the leave to start.
* **Time Off Type**: Check the box if you are requesting a leave.
*   *Yes* indicates you are requesting a leave, *No* indicates you are not.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your leave.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your leave.
* **Reason For Leave**: Provide a clear explanation for your leave request.
* **Comment**: Optional field for any additional comments.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your leave request.
* **Approver Signature**: Sign and date this form to approve the leave request.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the name of the person approving the leave.
* **Approver Title**: Enter the title of the person approving the leave.
* **Approver Date**: Enter the date of approval.
* **Approver Comment**: Optional field for any comments from the approver.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it for approval.
* Make sure to sign and date all relevant fields.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
