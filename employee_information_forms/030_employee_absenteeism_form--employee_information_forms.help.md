# Employee Absenteeism Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Absenteeism Form is used to report an employee's absence from work due to a specific reason.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the personal details of the employee, including their First Name, Last Name, Employee ID, and Work Location.
2. Select the correct Absence Reason from the provided options (Sick leave, Vacation, Personal reasons, Doctor’s visit).
3. Enter the date of absence start and end dates (if applicable).
4. Enter your supervisor's name and contact details (Name, Email, Phone).
5. Enter a detailed Note about the absence (optional).
6. Select the assigned tool or equipment used during absence (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter the employee's last name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Date of Absence Start**: Enter the start date of the absence period.
* **Absence Reason**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Sick leave: If the employee is absent due to illness or injury.
	+ Vacation: If the employee is absent due to a pre-approved vacation or leave.
	+ Personal reasons: If the employee is absent due to personal reasons (e.g., family emergencies, personal appointments).
	+ Doctor’s visit: If the employee is absent due to a scheduled doctor's visit.
* **Work Location**: Enter the location where the employee was supposed to work during the absence period.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor who will be handling the absence.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
* **Note**: Provide a detailed note about the absence. This is optional but highly recommended for further clarification.
* **Assigned tool**: If applicable, select the tool or equipment assigned to the employee during the absence period.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
* This form will help us keep track of employee absences and improve our workplace attendance record.
