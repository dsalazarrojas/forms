# Shift Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's shift preference, including start and end dates, times, and break periods. It also requires manager-level information such as manager's name, title, contact details, and their notes. The form is intended to ensure that employees and managers have all necessary information to effectively manage shifts and communicate with each other.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the start date of your shift from the calendar picker.
2. Enter the shift start time in the "Shift start time" field. Please note that this field is required.
3. Enter the shift end time in the "Shift end time" field. This field is also required.
4. Enter the break start time in the "Break start time" field. This field is required.
5. Enter the break end time in the "Break end time" field. This field is required.
6. Select your preferred shift from the options provided in the "Preferred shift" field. Please note that this field is required.
7. Enter any additional information or notes about your shift in the "Shift notes" field. This field is optional.
8. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field. This field is optional.
9. Enter your contact email in the "Contact email" field. This field is optional.
10. Enter any additional information or notes about your manager in the "Manager notes" field. This field is required.
11. Sign and date your form in the "Employee signature" field. This field is required.
12. Sign and date your form in the "Manager signature" field. This field is required.
13. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager name" field. This field is required.
14. Enter your manager's title in the "Manager title" field. This field is required.
15. Enter your manager's contact phone number in the "Manager contact phone" field. This field is required.
16. Enter your manager's contact email in the "Manager contact email" field. This field is required.
17. Enter any additional information or notes from your manager in the "Manager notes" field. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start date**: Enter the date you would like to start your shift.
* **Shift start time**: Enter the time you would like to start your shift. This field is required.
* **Shift end time**: Enter the time you would like to end your shift. This field is required.
* **Break start time**: Enter the time you would like to start your break. This field is required.
* **Break end time**: Enter the time you would like to end your break. This field is required.
* **Preferred shift**: Select your preferred shift from the options provided.
* **Shift notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about your shift.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Manager notes**: Enter any additional information or notes from your manager.
* **Employee signature**: Sign and date your form.
* **Manager signature**: Sign and date your form.
* **Manager name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager title**: Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager contact phone**: Enter your manager's contact phone number.
* **Manager contact email**: Enter your manager's contact email.
* **Manager notes**: Enter any additional information or notes from your manager.
