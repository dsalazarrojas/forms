# employee_instructional_hours_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employee instructional hours, which are hours worked by employees outside of their regular work hours for training, professional development, or other work-related purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form in the following steps:
*   Review each page and field carefully.
*   Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Information:** Please enter your name and any other relevant employee information.
*   **Instruction Hours:** Enter the total hours worked outside of regular work hours for training or professional development.
*   **Reporting Start:** Enter the start date of the reporting period.
*   **Reporting End:** Enter the end date of the reporting period.
*   **Reporting Method:** Select the method used for reporting (Manual or Automated).
*   **Notes:** Enter any additional comments or explanations about your instructional hours.
*   **Email:** Enter your company email address.
*   **Phone:** Enter your company phone number.
*   **Other (Reporting Method):** Enter any other relevant information about the reporting method used.
*   **Other:** Enter any other relevant information about your instructional hours.
## Tips
*   Save a copy of the form before submitting it for future reference.
*   Review the form's description for guidance on any unsure fields.
