# Company Directory Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Company Directory Update Request Form" is a tool used to update an employee's information in the company directory. Use this form to provide new email addresses, phone numbers, job titles, or other details about your colleagues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully to understand what needs to be updated.
2. Fill in the employee's name as it appears in the directory.
3. Enter the employee's internal identifier (if available).
4. If updating an email address, enter the new address in the "New email address" field.
5. If updating a phone number, enter the new number in the "New phone number" field.
6. If updating a job title, enter the new title in the "New job title" field.
7. If updating a location, enter the new address in the "New office or location" field.
8. If an update requires a reason, provide a brief explanation in the "Reason for update" field (optional).
9. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee name**: Enter the employee's full name as it appears in the directory.
* **Employee ID**: If available, enter the internal identifier for the employee. This is not required, but it helps us update the correct record.
* **New email address**: Enter the new email address to update in the employee directory.
* **New phone number**: Enter the new phone number to update in the employee directory.
* **New job title**: Enter the new job title to update in the employee directory.
* **New office or location**: Enter the new office address or remote location to update in the employee directory.
* **Reason for update**: This field is optional, but provides context for the change.
