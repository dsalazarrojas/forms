# Corrective Action Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track corrective actions taken when an employee has been involved in an incident that requires disciplinary action.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Employee Information" section with the employee's basic details and contact information.
2. Proceed to the "Incident Details" section and describe the incident, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
3. In the "Corrective Action Taken" section, describe the actions that have been taken or are planned to be taken regarding the incident.
4. Select the root cause of the incident from the options provided.
5. In the "Corrective Action Plan" section, provide a detailed plan for addressing the incident.
6. In the "Effectiveness of Corrective Action" section, evaluate the effectiveness of the actions taken and suggest areas for improvement.
7. Select the employee's conduct in relation to the incident.
8. If necessary, schedule a follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up Date" field.
9. Finally, confirm the completion of the corrective action by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (Employee Information, text, required): Enter the employee's name, job title, department, and contact information in this field.
* **Incident Details** (Incident Details, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Corrective Action Taken** (Corrective Action Taken, text, required): Describe the actions that have been taken or are planned to be taken regarding the incident.
* **Root Cause** (Root Cause, select_one, required): Select the root cause of the incident from the options provided.
* **Corrective Action Plan** (Corrective Action Plan, text, required): Provide a detailed plan for addressing the incident.
* **Effectiveness of Corrective Action** (Effectiveness of Corrective Action, text, required): Evaluate the effectiveness of the actions taken and suggest areas for improvement.
* **Employee Conduct** (Employee Conduct, select_one, required): Select the employee's conduct in relation to the incident.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date, date, required): Schedule a follow-up date and time, if necessary.
* **Corrective Action Follow-up** (Corrective Action Follow-up, date, required): Enter the follow-up date.
* **Completed** (Completed, select_one, required): Confirm the completion of the corrective action.

Note: The required fields are marked as required. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
