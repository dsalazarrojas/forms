# Employee Reprimand Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Reprimand Form is used to document and process instances of employee reprimand, which is an action taken by management to address misconduct or unsatisfactory performance by an employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the employee's name and any other required fields.
2. Provide a clear reason for the reprimand.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident.
4. Describe the incident in detail.
5. Check if employee actions were taken to address the issue.
6. Check if supervisor actions were taken to address the issue.
7. Sign the form to confirm completion.
8. Enter the date and time of signing.
9. Provide a reason for signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee being reprimanded.
* **Reason for Reprimand** (`reason_for_reprimand`, text, required): Enter a clear and specific reason for the reprimand.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required): Enter the date of the incident that led to the reprimand.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required): Enter the time of the incident that led to the reprimand.
* **Description of Incident** (`description_of_incident`, text, required): Describe the details of the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Employee Actions Taken** (`employee_actions_taken`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply to indicate if employee actions were taken to address the issue:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Supervisor Actions Taken** (`supervisor_actions_taken`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to indicate if supervisor actions were taken to address the issue:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, note, required): A space for the manager's signature.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): A space for the employee's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Reason Signed** (`reason_signed`, text, required): A space for the reason for signing the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
