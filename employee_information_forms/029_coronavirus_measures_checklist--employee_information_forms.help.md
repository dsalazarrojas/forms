# Coronavirus Measures Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee information related to COVID-19 measures in the workplace, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Employee Temperature** (`employee_temperature`, number, Optional): Please take a temperature reading and enter it here. If your body temperature is normal, enter 0. If it's above normal, enter the actual temperature reading. (Note: Normal body temperature is around 37°C or 98.6°F)
3. **Employee Symptoms** (`employee_symptoms`, Select Multiple, Optional): Please indicate if you are experiencing any COVID-19 related symptoms, such as cough, fever, etc. Choose all that apply.
	* Yes
	* No
4. **Employee Travel History** (`employee_travel_history`, Select One, Optional): Are you experiencing any travel history that may be relevant for contact tracing?
	* Yes
	* No
5. **Travel Country** (`travel_country`, Select One, Optional): If you answered "Yes" to the previous question, please select the country you traveled to. 
	* Yes (Note: This field is not applicable if you selected "No" in the previous question)
6. **Temperature/Fever** (`temperature_fever`, Time, Optional): Please take a temperature reading (if you have a fever). If not, please leave this field blank.
7. **Health Status** (`health_status`, Select One, Optional): Please indicate your current health status:
	* Active
	* Inactive
8. **Contact Tracing Info** (`contact_tracing_info`, Text, Optional): If applicable, please provide any relevant contact tracing information.
9. **Next Checkup Date** (`next_checkup_date`, Date, Optional): If recommended, please select a date for your next checkup.
10. **Last Checkup Date** (`last_checked_date`, Date, Optional): If previously checked, please select the date of your last checkup.
11. **Next Checkup Frequency** (`next_checkup_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): If recommended, please select how frequently you will be checked:
	* Yes
	* No
12. **Employee Name** (`employee_name2`, Text, Required): Confirm your name.
