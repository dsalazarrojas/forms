# Employees Health Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect employee health information, including personal details and health status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason to fill out this form, as it is used by HR or management for tracking employee health and well-being.
2. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
	* **Employee Email**: Enter your work email address.
	* **Employee Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
	* **Employee Address**: Enter your official work address or home address.
3. Provide your health information:
	* **Employee Birthdate**: Enter your birthdate in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
	* **Employment Status**: Enter your current employment status (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
4. Complete the health declaration:
	* **Health Declaration Date**: Enter the date you are signing this declaration.
	* **Health Status Description**: Describe your current health status (e.g., fit, unfit, medical condition).
5. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have completed and reviewed the information accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, required): Enter your official work address or home address.
* **Employee Birthdate** (`employee_birthdate`, date, optional): Enter your birthdate in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Employment Status** (`employee_employment_status`, text, optional): Enter your current employment status (e.g., full-time, part-time, contract).
* **Health Declaration Date** (`health_declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are signing this declaration.
* **Health Status Description** (`health_status_description`, text, optional): Describe your current health status (e.g., fit, unfit, medical condition).
* **Health Declaration Form Signature** (`health_declaration_form_signature`, note, required): Sign to acknowledge that you have completed and reviewed the information accurately.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
* Be honest and thorough in describing your health status.
* Review your information before signing the declaration.
* If you have any health concerns, discuss them with your HR or management team.
