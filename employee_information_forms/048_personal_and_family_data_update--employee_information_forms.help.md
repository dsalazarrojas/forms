# Personal And Family Data Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Personal And Family Data Update" form is used to update employee and family details, including contact information, address, and other essential data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields (if any).
2. Fill out the non-required fields as needed.
3. Review each field below to ensure you understand its intent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal And Family Data Update** (`personal_and_family_data_update`, text, required/optional): This is the main title of the form, it's not a field but a section.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter the employee's contact details, such as their address or phone number.
* **Family Member Details** (`family_member_details`, text, optional): Enter details about the family members of the employee, such as their names and relationships.
* **Family Member Details Form** (`family_member_details_form`, text, optional): Similar to the previous field, enter more details about family members, if needed.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the employee, such as their work experience or education.
* **Employee Details Form** (`employee_details_form`, text, optional): Enter more details about the employee, if needed.
* **Personal Contact** (`personal_contact`, text, optional): Enter the employee's personal contact information.
* **Email** (`personal_email`, email, optional): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Phone** (`personal_phone`, text, optional): Enter the employee's phone number.
* **Address** (`personal_address`, text, optional): Enter the employee's address.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before filling it out.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
