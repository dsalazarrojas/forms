# Employee Records Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect employee information to provide a centralized platform for accessing and updating employee records efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above in the "How To Complete This Form" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

Here is a detailed explanation of each field:

*   **Employee Name (Text):** Enter the full name of the employee as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Employee Email (Email):** Enter the employee's official email address for work purposes.
*   **Employee Phone (Text):** Enter the employee's contact phone number.
*   **Department (Select Multiple):** Select the departments that the employee belongs to.
*   **Status (Select One):** Choose from the options: Active, Inactive, or Left.
*   **Job Title (Text):** Enter the employee's job title if required.
*   **Job Description (Text):** Enter the employee's job description.
*   **Manager Name (Text):** Enter the employee's manager's name.
*   **Manager Email (Email):** Enter the manager's official email address.
*   **Manager Phone (Text):** Enter the manager's phone number.
*   **Date of Birth (Date):** Enter the employee's date of birth.
*   **Gender (Select One):** Choose from the options: Male, Female, or Other.
*   **Marital Status (Select One):** Choose from the options: Single, Married, or Divorced.
*   **Anniversary Date (Date):** Enter the employee's anniversary date.
*   **Notes (Text):** Enter any additional notes about the employee.
