# Security Clearance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's security clearance status and background check. It is typically filled out by employees who need to be cleared for security purposes, such as those working with sensitive information or in a high-security facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Background Check" option for your background check status.
2. Choose the correct "Clearance Level" based on your required clearance level.
3. Enter your "Social Security Number" in the required format (XXX-XX-XXXX).
4. Enter your "Address" in the format of "Street Address", "City, State, ZIP".
5. Select your "Date of Birth" in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY".
6. Choose the correct "Clearance Type" option.
7. Enter the name of the "Security Clearance Issuing Agency" (if applicable).
8. If applicable, enter the "Clearance Level Expiration Date" in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Check** (Background Check, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the background check option that best describes your background check status.
* **Clearance Level** (Clearance Level, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the clearance level that aligns with your required security clearance level.
* **Social Security Number** (Social Security Number, text, optional):
	+ Enter your Social Security Number in the format of "XXX-XX-XXXX".
* **Address** (Address, text, optional):
	+ Enter your address in the format of "Street Address", "City, State, ZIP".
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional):
	+ Select your date of birth in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Clearance Type** (Clearance Type, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the clearance type that aligns with your required clearance type.
* **Security Clearance Issuing Agency** (Security Clearance Issuing Agency, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the agency that issued your security clearance (if applicable).
* **Clearance Level Expiration Date** (Clearance Level Expiration Date, date, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter the expiration date of your clearance level in the format of "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Security Clearance Status** (Security Clearance Status, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the status of your security clearance (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
* Ensure that your background check information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to enter your clearance level and type accurately based on your required clearance status.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
