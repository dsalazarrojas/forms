# Covid 19 Customer Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers visiting the workplace during a pandemic. The data collected will help the management track vaccination status and other relevant details for customer safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Customer ID.
2. If applicable, enter the date and time you visited the workplace.
3. Fill in your Customer Name.
4. If you received a vaccine, select the vaccination site where you received it.
5. Enter the time you received the vaccine (if you received one).
6. Choose your vaccination status from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter any comments or additional information about your visit.
8. Provide any allergy information or medical conditions that may be relevant.
9. Enter the details of your emergency contact person.
10. If applicable, select your next vaccination date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, Text, Required): Enter your unique customer ID to help us identify you.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, Date, Optional): If you visited the workplace, enter the date of your visit in MM/dd/yy format.
* **Checkin Time** (`checkin_time`, Time, Optional): If you visited the workplace, enter the time of your visit.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Vaccination Site** (`vaccination_site`, Text, Optional): If you received a vaccine, enter the name of the vaccination site where you received it.
* **Vaccination Time** (`vaccination_time`, Time, Optional): If you received a vaccine, enter the time you received it.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, Select One, Required): Choose your vaccination status from the dropdown menu:
	+ Completed
	+ Pending
	+ Failed
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Enter any comments or additional information about your visit.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`, Text, Optional): If you have any allergies, enter the details here.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, Optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter the details here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter the details of your emergency contact person.
* **Next Vaccination Date** (`next_vaccination_date`, Date, Optional): If you have a scheduled next vaccination date, enter it in MM/dd/yy format. 

# Tips
* Always double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the designated authorities for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, and the data will not be shared publicly.
* Ensure you have the necessary permission to access and use the collected data.
