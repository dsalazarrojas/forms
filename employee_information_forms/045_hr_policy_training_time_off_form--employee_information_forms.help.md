# HR Policy Training Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Training Time Off Form is a document used by employees to request time off for training purposes. This form is used to collect information about the employee, the training details, and the status of the time off request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (Page 1):
	+ This section is where you enter your employee information.
	+ Please fill in your name, employee ID, and other relevant details.
* **Time Off Request** (Page 1):
	+ Describe the reason for your time off request, including the dates you need off and the type of training you will be attending.
* **Training Details** (Page 2):
	+ Provide detailed information about the training you will be attending, including the topic, location, and duration.
* **Time Off Status** (Page 2):
	+ Indicate the status of your time off request, whether it is Active or Inactive.
* **Comments** (Page 2):
	+ Add any additional comments or explanations related to your time off request.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have completed all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, you can contact your HR department for assistance.
