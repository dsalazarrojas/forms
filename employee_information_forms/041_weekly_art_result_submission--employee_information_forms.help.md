# Weekly ART Result Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to submit the results of the weekly ART test. This form is required for all employees who are tested.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the required information and documentation.
2. Select the outcome of your ART test result (Positive, Negative, or Unknown).
3. If required, upload a photo proof.
4. Enter your photo proof details.
5. Select the date you submitted your result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information Form**: This form is used to collect your personal details. Please fill in your information accurately.
* **Weekly ART Result**: This field is used to select the outcome of your ART test. Please choose from the options: Positive, Negative, or Unknown.
* **Upload Photo Proof**: This field is used to upload a photo proof of your test result. If required, please attach a photo of your test result.
* **Photo Proof**: This field is used to enter any additional details about your photo proof. Please fill in this field if you have uploaded a photo proof.
* **Submitted Date**: This field is used to enter the date you submitted your result. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
