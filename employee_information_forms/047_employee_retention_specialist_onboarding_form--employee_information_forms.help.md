# Employee Retention Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Specialist Onboarding Form is designed to collect information about an employee's job, performance, and onboarding status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below. Please fill in all required fields, and if unsure about any field, contact your HR or People Operations department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, Optional):
	+ Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, Optional):
	+ Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, Optional):
	+ Choose the department where you are currently working.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your current job title.
* **Hire Date** (Hire Date, date, Optional):
	+ Enter your date of hire.
* **Email** (Email, email, Optional):
	+ Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your office phone number.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, number, Optional):
	+ Enter your employee ID number as assigned by HR.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your immediate manager's name.
* **Reporting Manager** (Reporting Manager, select_one, Optional):
	+ Choose your reporting manager from the list.
* **Reporting Manager Name** (Reporting Manager Name, text, Optional):
	+ Enter the name of the manager you have selected for the "Reporting Manager".
* **Job Description** (Job Description, text, Optional):
	+ Briefly describe your job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Notes** (Job Notes, text, Optional):
	+ Any additional information about your job.
* **Employee Status** (Employee Status, select_one, Optional):
	+ Choose your current status within the organization.
* **Manager Notes** (Manager Notes, text, Optional):
	+ Leave notes for your manager or other relevant parties.
* **Onboarding Status** (Onboarding Status, select_one, Optional):
	+ Choose your onboarding status, which helps us track your progress.
* **Employee Retention Specialist ID** (Employee Retention Specialist ID, number, Optional):
	+ Enter your Employee Retention Specialist ID number (if applicable).
* **Retention Manager ID** (Retention Manager ID, number, Optional):
	+ Enter your Retention Manager ID number (if applicable).
* **Onboarding Start Date** (Onboarding Start Date, date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date when your onboarding process started.
* **Onboarding End Date** (Onboarding End Date, date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date when your onboarding process ended.
* **Onboarding Duration** (Onboarding Duration, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the number of days it took to complete your onboarding.
* **Onboarding Days** (Onboarding Days, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the number of days in your onboarding process.
* **Job Performance** (Job Performance, text, Optional):
	+ Describe your current job performance.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, Optional):
	+ Any feedback you have about your onboarding process or experience.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, Optional):
	+ Additional comments or suggestions about your onboarding process.
