# PTO Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and manage employee requests for paid time off (PTO) donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`id: 1`): This section asks for your name and contact information.
* **Donation Details** (`id: 2`): This section asks for specific details about the PTO donation, including the amount, date, and recipient.
* **Recipient Information** (`id: 3`): This section is optional and asks for the recipient's details if you are donating PTO to another employee within the company.
* **Submission** (`id: 4`): This section is where you submit your request for PTO donation.
* **Notes** (`id: 5`): This section is for any additional comments or information you wish to provide.
