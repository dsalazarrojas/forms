# PTO Reimbursement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PTO Reimbursement Form is used to submit and manage PTO requests for employees, including the date of the request, the reason for the leave, and the total amount reimbursed. This form serves as a single point of entry for all relevant information, streamlining the process for employees, managers, and administrators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the employee information section** (`Employee Name`, `Manager Name`, `Supervisor Name`, `Admin Name`).
2. **Submit the request by selecting the "Submitted To" fields** (`Submitted To`, `Submitted To ID`, `Submitted To Dept`, `Submitted To Email`, `Submitted To Phone`, `Submitted To Note`).
3. **Enter the reimbursement details** (`Reimbursement Date`, `Start Date`, `End Date`, `Reason`, `Number of Days`, `Rate`, `Total Reimbursed`).
4. **Add any additional comments or notes** (`Comments`, `Notes 2`).
5. **Submit the request for approval** by selecting the appropriate approval fields (`Approved By`, `Approved On`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Information** (`Employee Name`, `Manager Name`, `Supervisor Name`, `Admin Name`): Enter your name and the names of your manager, supervisor, and administrator.
* **Reimbursement Date** (`Reimbursement Date`): The date you are requesting reimbursement for.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`): The start date of your leave.
* **End Date** (`End Date`): The end date of your leave.
* **Reason** (`Reason`): Briefly explain the reason for your leave.
* **Number of Days** (`Number of Days`): Enter the number of days you are taking off.
* **Rate** (`Rate`): The rate at which you are entitled to reimbursement.
* **Total Reimbursed** (`Total Reimbursed`): The total amount reimbursed for your leave.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`): Select who submitted the request (Employee, Manager, Supervisor, Admin).
* **Submitted On** (`Submitted On`): The date you submitted the request.
* **Approved By** (`Approved By`): Select who approved the request (Employee, Manager, Supervisor, Admin).
* **Approved On** (`Approved On`): The date the request was approved.
* **Notes** (`Comments`, `Notes 2`): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Submitted To** (`Submitted To`, `Submitted To ID`, `Submitted To Dept`, `Submitted To Email`, `Submitted To Phone`, `Submitted To Note`): Enter the details of who the request was submitted to.
* **Notes 2** (`Notes 2`): Additional notes about your request.
