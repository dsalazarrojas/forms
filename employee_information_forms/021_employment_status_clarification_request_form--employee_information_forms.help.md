# employment_status_clarification_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help employees request clarification on their employment status in a clear and concise manner. It is intended for employees who have been reported as having a specific employment status but are unsure of their actual status or have reason to believe it is incorrect.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your employee ID to confirm your identity.
2. Provide your date of birth to verify your employment details.
3. Enter the date of your last pay period to confirm your employment duration.
4. Enter the name and contact information of your manager to ensure they are aware of your request.
5. Explain the reason for your request and provide any additional comments or information that may support your case.
6. Select your employment status from the provided options.
7. Sign the form to confirm your request.
8. Sign the form again for your manager to confirm they have reviewed and approved your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission** (form_submission): This is the title of the form, it does not require any input.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Enter your unique employee ID to confirm your identity.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth to verify your employment details.
* **Date of Last Pay Period** (date_of_last_pay): Enter the date of your last pay period to confirm your employment duration.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name): Enter your manager's name to ensure they are aware of your request.
* **Manager Email** (manager_email): Enter your manager's email address to ensure they can be reached for review and approval.
* **Manager Phone** (manager_phone): Enter your manager's phone number to ensure they can be reached for any follow-up questions.
* **Reason for Request** (reason_for_request): Explain the reason why you are requesting clarification on your employment status.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status): Select your employment status from the provided options.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Provide any additional comments or information that may support your case.
* **Signature** (signature): Sign the form to confirm your request.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature): Sign the form again for your manager to confirm they have reviewed and approved your request.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you are submitting this form (optional).
* **Reason for Leave** (reason_for_leave): Explain the reason for your leave (if applicable).
* **Paystub** (paystub): Attach a copy of your paystub to confirm your pay history (if applicable).
* **Pay Period** (pay_period): Enter the pay period you are referring to (if applicable).
* **Last Pay Period Start** (last_pay_period_start): Enter the start date of your last pay period (if applicable).
* **Last Pay Period End** (last_pay_period_end): Enter the end date of your last pay period (if applicable).
* **Last Pay Date** (last_pay_date): Enter the date of your last pay (if applicable).
* **Last Pay Amount** (last_pay_amount): Enter the amount of your last pay (if applicable).
* **Leave Start Date** (leave_start_date): Enter the start date of your leave (if applicable).
* **Leave End Date** (leave_end_date): Enter the end date of your leave (if applicable).
* **Leave Duration** (leave_duration): Enter the duration of your leave (if applicable).
* **Leave Reason** (leave_reason): Enter the reason for your leave (if applicable).
