# Hot Work Permit Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is used to declare hot work activities on-site and ensure the necessary safety measures are taken before, during, and after the work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Declare whether you are performing or supervising hot work.
3. Indicate whether you have reviewed the safety measures and internal and external safety measures that are in place.
4. Select the duration of the permit.
5. Enter the date and time the permit is issued and the date and time the permit is to be reviewed.
6. Add any necessary notes or comments.
7. List any equipment and materials used during the hot work operation.
8. Select areas where the hot work will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or badge number.
* **Employee Schedule** (`employee_schedule`, number, optional): If you are performing the hot work, enter your 4-digit work schedule number.
* **Work Permit** (`work_permit`, select_one, optional): Check the box to declare that you are performing hot work.
* **Work Assignment** (`work_assignment`, select_multiple, optional): If you are supervising hot work, select the relevant activities.
* **Hot Work Activity** (`hot_work_activity`, select_one, optional): Check the box to declare that the hot work involves sparks or flames.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Check the location where the hot work will take place.
* **Permit Duration** (`permit_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the permit.
* **Permit Duration Unit** (`permit_duration_unit`, select_one, optional): Check the box to select the unit of time (e.g. minutes, hours).
* **Permit Issue Date** (`permit_issue_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the permit is issued.
* **Permit Issue Time** (`permit_issue_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the permit is issued.
* **Permit Status** (`permit_status`, select_one, optional): Check the box to declare the status of the permit (Active or Inactive).
* **Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, note, optional): Review and add any safety measures that have been put in place.
* **Internal Safety Measures** (`safety_measures_internal`, text, optional): Enter any internal safety measures you are required to take.
* **External Safety Measures** (`external_safety_measures`, text, optional): Enter any external safety measures you are required to take.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supervisor who is supervising you.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the supervisor.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`contact_person_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Permit Sign Off** (`permit_sign_off`, select_one, optional): Check the box to declare that the hot work is completed and the permit is signed off.
* **Work Start Time** (`work_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the hot work operation.
* **Work End Time** (`work_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the hot work operation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments about the hot work operation.
* **Work Areas** (`work_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select the areas where the hot work will take place.
* **Equipment and Materials** (`equipment_and_materials`, text, optional): Enter any equipment and materials used during the hot work operation.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the permit is to be reviewed.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" are not required and you can skip them if they do not apply to your situation.
