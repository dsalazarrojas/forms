# Health Compliance Employment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects employment information from employees for administrative or regulatory purposes, such as health compliance or benefits tracking. Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employer name field with your company's name.
2. Enter your employee ID or equivalent identifier.
3. Provide your department and job title.
4. Specify your work type (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, etc.).
5. Select your work status (e.g., Active, Inactive, On Leave, etc.).
6. Choose the days you work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
7. Enter the number of hours you work per shift.
8. If you do not see your work type listed, please specify in the text field.
9. Enter your manager's name and contact information (email, phone number, etc.).
10. Sign and date the form with your manager's signature and title.
11. Specify your manager's relationship to you (e.g., Manager, Supervisor, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID or equivalent identifier.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Shift Start** (`shift_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End** (`shift_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Shift Length** (`shift_length`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per shift.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, select_one, optional): Choose your work type (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, etc.).
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your work status (e.g., Active, Inactive, On Leave, etc.).
* **Shift Days** (`shift_days`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the days you work (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per shift.
* **Work Type Other** (`work_type_other`, text, optional): If you do not see your work type listed, please specify in this text field.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter your job location.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Contact** (`manager_contact`, text, optional): Enter additional contact information for your manager.
* **Employee Attestation Declaration** (`employee_attestation_declaration`, note, optional): I, the undersigned employee, hereby attest that all information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Manager Attestation Declaration** (`manager_attestation_declaration`, note, optional): I, the undersigned manager, hereby attest that all information provided is accurate and truthful.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, optional): Enter your manager's signature.
* **Manager Date** (`manager_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your manager signed this form.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, optional): Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager Relationship** (`manager_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose your manager's relationship to you (e.g., Manager, Supervisor, etc.).
* **Manager Email Other** (`manager_email_other`, text, optional): If your manager's email is not listed, please enter it in this field.
* **Manager Phone Other** (`manager_phone_other`, text, optional): If your manager's phone number is not listed, please enter it in this field.
