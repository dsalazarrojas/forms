# Safety Meeting Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Meeting Checklist is a form used to document the details of a safety meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the meeting date from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
2. Select the start and end times of the meeting.
3. Enter the location where the meeting took place.
4. Describe the incident that was discussed during the meeting.
5. Identify the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Equipment, Equipment failure, Human error, Other).
6. List any safety measures that were put in place as a result of the incident.
7. If necessary, choose multiple safety measures that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Date (`meeting_date`)

* Meeting Date (`meeting_date`, date, required: false):
This field is used to record the date the meeting occurred. Please enter the date in the correct format.

### Start Time (`start_time`)

* Start Time (`start_time`, time, required: false):
Enter the start time of the meeting in the correct format.

### End Time (`end_time`)

* End Time (`end_time`, time, required: false):
Enter the end time of the meeting in the correct format.

### Incident Location (`incident_location`)

* Incident Location (`incident_location`, text, required: false):
Provide the location where the incident took place.

### Incident Description (`incident_description`)

* Incident Description (`incident_description`, text, required: false):
Describe the incident that was discussed during the meeting.

### Incident Type (`incident_type`)

* Incident Type (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false):
Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., Equipment, Equipment failure, Human error, Other).

### Safety Measure 1 (`safety_measure_1`)

* Safety Measure 1 (`safety_measure_1`, text, required: false):
Enter any safety measures that were implemented as a result of the incident.

### Safety Measure 2 (`safety_measure_2`)

* Safety Measure 2 (`safety_measure_2`, text, required: false):
Enter any additional safety measures that were implemented as a result of the incident.

### Safety Measures (`safety_measures`)

* Safety Measures (`safety_measures`, select_multiple, required: false):
Choose multiple safety measures that apply to the incident (e.g., Hard hat, Safety glasses, Safety harness).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your meeting notes or discuss with your team lead.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all necessary information is included.
