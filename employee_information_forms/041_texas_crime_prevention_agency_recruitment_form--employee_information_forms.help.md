# Texas Crime Prevention Agency Recruitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Texas Crime Prevention Agency Recruitment Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals interested in working for our agency. This form is used to gather contact and employment details, which will be used to evaluate applicants for potential positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" with asterisk (*).
3. Enter any necessary attachments or supporting documents in the designated fields.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<name>, text, required: false)**: This field collects your name.
* **Last Name (<name>, text, required: false)**: This field is for your surname.
* **Date Of Birth (<name>, date, required: false)**: Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Information (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Phone Number (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool (<name>, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select from the options to indicate if you are assigned to any specific tools or equipment.
* **Job Title (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the title of your desired job position.
* **Department (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the department you are interested in working in.
* **Work Shift (<name>, select_one, required: false)**: Select the shift you are available to work (Police Officer, Detective, Sergeant).
* **Hours Worked (<name>, number, required: false)**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Salary (<name>, number, required: false)**: Enter your expected salary range.
* **Start Date (<name>, date, required: false)**: Enter your start date of employment (if applicable).
* **End Date (<name>, date, required: false)**: Enter your end date of employment (if applicable).
* **Note (<name>, note, required: false)**: Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Address (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter your address.
* **Job Description (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Job Requirements (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter any specific requirements for the job you are applying for.
* **Qualifications (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter any relevant qualifications or skills.
* **Emergency Contact (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Job Title ID (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the ID for your job title.
* **Job Description ID (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the ID for your job description.
* **Department ID (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the ID for the department you are interested in.
* **Work Shift ID (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the ID for the work shift you are interested in.
* **Salary ID (<name>, text, required: false)**: Enter the ID for your expected salary.

## Tips

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Attach any necessary supporting documents or attachments as required.
