# Pay Rate Change Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify employees of pay rate changes, and to track changes to their hourly rate, monthly rate, or salary.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager who is responsible for the pay rate change.
2. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee whose pay rate is changing.
3. **Pay Rate Amount**: Enter the new pay rate amount for the employee.
4. **Pay Rate Frequency**: Select the frequency at which the pay rate change is effective (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
5. **Pay Rate Start Date**: Enter the date when the pay rate change is effective.
6. **Pay Rate End Date**: Enter the date when the pay rate change will end (leave blank for no end date).
7. **Pay Rate Reason**: Enter a brief explanation for the pay rate change.
8. **Manager Acknowledge**: Indicate if the manager has acknowledged the pay rate change.
9. **Payroll Acknowledge**: Check if the payroll department has acknowledged the pay rate change.
10. **HR Acknowledge**: Check if the HR department has acknowledged the pay rate change.
11. **Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the manager.
12. **Manager Suggestion**: Enter any suggestions or recommendations from the manager.
13. **Pay Rate Status**: Select the status of the pay rate change (Active or Inactive).
14. **Manager Status**: Select the status of the manager's approval (Active or Inactive).
15. **Pay Rate Type**: Select the type of pay rate (Hourly, Monthly, or Salary).
16. **Manager Approvals**: Select the type of manager approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).
17. **Payroll Approvals**: Select the type of payroll approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).
18. **HR Approvals**: Select the type of HR approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the manager responsible for the pay rate change.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee whose pay rate is changing.
* **Pay Rate Amount** (`pay_rate_amount`, number, optional): Enter the new pay rate amount for the employee.
* **Pay Rate Frequency** (`pay_rate_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the frequency at which the pay rate change is effective (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Pay Rate Start Date** (`pay_rate_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the pay rate change is effective.
* **Pay Rate End Date** (`pay_rate_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the pay rate change will end (leave blank for no end date).
* **Pay Rate Reason** (`pay_rate_reason`, note, optional): Enter a brief explanation for the pay rate change.
* **Manager Acknowledge** (`manager_acknowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the manager has acknowledged the pay rate change.
* **Payroll Acknowledge** (`payroll_acknowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the payroll department has acknowledged the pay rate change.
* **HR Acknowledge** (`hr_acknowledge`, select_multiple, optional): Check if the HR department has acknowledged the pay rate change.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the manager.
* **Manager Suggestion** (`manager_suggestion`, note, optional): Enter any suggestions or recommendations from the manager.
* **Pay Rate Status** (`pay_rate_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the pay rate change (Active or Inactive).
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the manager's approval (Active or Inactive).
* **Pay Rate Type** (`pay_rate_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of pay rate (Hourly, Monthly, or Salary).
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of manager approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).
* **Payroll Approvals** (`payroll_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of payroll approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).
* **HR Approvals** (`hr_approvals`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of HR approval (Approve, Approve and Sign, or Approve and Email).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure the form is accurate and complete.
- Check with the manager and payroll department to confirm that they have acknowledged and approved the pay rate change.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it for final approval.
