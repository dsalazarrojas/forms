# Employee Physical Examination Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an employee's physical health status, medical history, and other relevant health-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Read each field carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
* Fill in the required fields (check the required/optional labels for each field).
* Use the provided options for select_multiple and select_one fields.
* Ensure that you answer accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Medical Status
* **Medical Status**: A brief description of your current medical status.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Recent Injuries or Illnesses
* **Recent Injuries or Illnesses**: Describe any recent injuries or illnesses you have experienced.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Height (in cm)
* **Height (in cm)**: Your height in centimeters.
  (Required: False, Type: number)

### Weight (in kg)
* **Weight (in kg)**: Your weight in kilograms.
  (Required: False, Type: number)

### BMI
* **BMI**: Your Body Mass Index, calculated based on your height and weight.
  (Required: False, Type: number)

### Blood Pressure (mmHg)
* **Blood Pressure (mmHg)**: Your current blood pressure in mmHg.
  (Required: False, Type: number)

### Heart Rate (bpm)
* **Heart Rate (bpm)**: Your current heart rate in beats per minute.
  (Required: False, Type: number)

### Medical History
* **Medical History**: A brief description of any medical conditions or health issues you have experienced.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Medication History
* **Medication History**: A list of any medications you are currently taking.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Allergies
* **Allergies**: A list of any allergies you have.
  (Required: False, Type: select_multiple, Options: Active, Inactive)

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: A statement indicating whether you have any known medical conditions.
  (Required: False, Type: select_one, Options: True, False)

### Chronic Conditions
* **Chronic Conditions**: A list of any chronic conditions you have.
  (Required: False, Type: select_multiple, Options: True, False)

### Smoking or Tobacco Use
* **Smoking or Tobacco Use**: A statement indicating whether you use tobacco or smoke.
  (Required: False, Type: select_one, Options: True, False)

### Medical Devices or Equipment
* **Medical Devices or Equipment**: A list of any medical devices or equipment you use.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Implants or Prosthetics
* **Implants or Prosthetics**: A list of any implants or prosthetics you have.
  (Required: False, Type: text)

### Regular Medications
* **Regular Medications**: A list of any medications you take regularly.
  (Required: False, Type: select_multiple, Options: True, False)

### Medical Procedures or Surgeries
* **Medical Procedures or Surgeries**: A list of any medical procedures or surgeries you have undergone.
  (Required: False, Type: select_multiple, Options: True, False)

### Medical Insurance
* **Medical Insurance**: A statement indicating whether you have any medical insurance.
  (Required: False, Type: select_multiple, Options: True, False)
