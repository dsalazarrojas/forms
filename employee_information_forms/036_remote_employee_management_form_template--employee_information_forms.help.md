# Remote Employee Management Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Employee Management Form Template is a tool used to gather and maintain information about remote employees. It helps managers and administrators keep track of employee remote work status, contact details, work schedule, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each field. 
2. Be accurate and complete in your responses. 
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your manager or HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Remote Employee Form** (`remote_employee_management_form_template`, text, optional):
	+ This is the main section of the form where you will enter your remote employee details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your current work status, e.g., full-time, part-time, leave, or off-duty.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, number, optional):
	+ Enter your employment status (e.g., 1 for full-time, 2 for part-time).
* **Remote Work Location** (`remote_work_location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location where you will be working remotely (e.g., home, office, or co-working space).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your job title or role.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the department you belong to.
* **Remote Work Schedule** (`remote_work_schedule`, date, optional):
	+ Enter your remote work schedule (e.g., Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
* **Remote Work Time** (`remote_work_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter your remote work hours (e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
* **Remote Work Days** (`remote_work_days`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the days you will be working remotely.
* **Remote Work Start Date** (`remote_work_start_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when your remote work started.
* **Remote Work End Date** (`remote_work_end_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when your remote work will end.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, text, optional):
	+ Describe your preferred communication channels (e.g., email, phone, chat).
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the channels you prefer to use for communication (e.g., email, phone, chat).
* **Remote Work Location Address** (`remote_work_location_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of your remote work location.
* **Remote Work Location Country** (`remote_work_location_country`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the country of your remote work location.
* **Remote Work Location State** (`remote_work_location_state`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the state of your remote work location.
* **Remote Work Location City** (`remote_work_location_city`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the city of your remote work location.
* **Remote Work Location Zip** (`remote_work_location_zip`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the zip code of your remote work location.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your manager's name.
* **Employee** (`employee`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Remote Work Location Notes** (`remote_work_location_notes`, text, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes about your remote work location.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your manager or HR department.
* This form is for management and administrative purposes only.
