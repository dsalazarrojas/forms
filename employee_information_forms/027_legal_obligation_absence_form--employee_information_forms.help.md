# Legal Obligation Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Obligation Absence Form is a formal template for employees to report and document their absences due to legal obligations, such as family or court obligations. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and reviewed by HR and management for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Employee ID to identify yourself.
2.  Provide a clear Reason for Absence, indicating the specific legal reason for your absence.
3.  Enter the Start and End Dates of your absence.
4.  Specify the number of Hours Absence you expect to take.
5.  If applicable, add a Comment to provide additional context.
6.  Select the Legal Basis for your absence (Family Obligation, Court Obligation, or Other).
7.  If required, upload a Legal Document supporting your claim.
8.  Enter the Document Number if a document is attached.
9.  Select who submitted the form (Employee, Manager, or HR).
10. Enter the date you Submitted the form.
11. Select who approved your request (Manager, HR, or None).
12. Enter the date you were Approved.
13. Select the Approval Status (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
14. If denied, provide a Reason for Denial.
15. Add any comments from your Manager.
16. Add any comments from HR.
17. Add any comments from yourself as the employee.
18. Add any comments from the Manager.
19. Add any comments from HR.
20. Select the Submission Status (Submitted, Approved, or Rejected).
21. Enter the date of Submission.
22. Select who last updated the form (Employee, Manager).
23. Enter the last update date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID**: <number> - Your unique employee ID to identify yourself in the system.
- **Reason for Absence**: <text> - Clearly state the reason for your absence, explaining the legal obligation.
- **Start Date**: <date> - Enter the start date of your absence.
- **End Date**: <date> - Enter the end date of your absence.
- **Hours Absence**: <number> - Specify the number of hours you expect to be absent.
- **Comment**: <text> - Add any additional context or details about your absence.
- **Legal Basis**: <select one> - Select the legal reason for your absence (Family Obligation, Court Obligation, or Other).
- **Legal Document**: <text> - Attach a supporting document if required.
- **Document Number**: <text> - Enter the document number if a document is attached.
- **Submitted By**: <select one> - Select who submitted the form (Employee, Manager, or HR).
- **Submitted On**: <date> - Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Approved By**: <select one> - Select who approved your request (Manager, HR, or None).
- **Approved On**: <date> - Enter the date you were approved.
- **Approval Status**: <select one> - Select the approval status (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
- **Reason for Denial**: <text> - Provide a reason for denial if your request is denied.
- **Comment Manager**: <text> - Add any comments from your Manager.
- **Comments Hr**: <text> - Add any comments from HR.
- **Comments Employee**: <text> - Add any comments from yourself as the employee.
- **Comments Manager**: <text> - Add any comments from the Manager.
- **Comments Hr**: <text> - Add any comments from HR.
- **Submission Status**: <select one> - Select the submission status (Submitted, Approved, or Rejected).
- **Submission Date**: <date> - Enter the date you submitted the form.
- **Updated By**: <select one> - Select who last updated the form (Employee, Manager).
- **Updated On**: <date> - Enter the last update date.

This guide provides step-by-step instructions and field-by-field explanations for completing the Legal Obligation Absence Form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please refer to your HR or management department.
