# Temporary Employee Payroll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for temporary employees who are being paid on a non-standard schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Fill out each field as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire** (`New Hire`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the new hire, such as their job title, hire date, or reason for hire.
* **Tax Information** (`Tax Information`, text, optional): Enter any relevant tax information, such as tax filing status or tax identification number.
* **Wages Info** (`Wages Info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the employee's wages, such as hourly wage or annual salary.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, optional): Enter the employee's contact details, such as phone number or email address.
* **Tax Filing Status** (`Tax Filing Status`, select_one, optional): Select the employee's tax filing status from the options provided.
* **Other Tax Filing Status** (`Other Tax Filing Status`, text, optional): If the employee's tax filing status is not listed, enter the correct status here.
* **Tax Filing Status 2** (`Tax Filing Status 2`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Social Security Number** (`Social Security Number`, number, optional): Enter the employee's Social Security number. This should be a 9-digit number in the format XXX-XX-XXXX.
* **Wages** (`Wages`, number, optional): Enter the employee's hourly wage or annual salary.
* **Pay Frequency** (`Pay Frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the employee's pay frequency from the options provided.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee started working in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the employee ended working in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Hours Worked** (`Hours Worked`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the employee worked.
* **Pay Rate** (`Pay Rate`, number, optional): Enter the employee's pay rate.
* **Pay Frequency 2** (`Pay Frequency 2`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Pay Schedule** (`Pay Schedule`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the employee's pay schedule.
* **Pay Rate 2** (`Pay Rate 2`, number, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Benefits** (`Benefits`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the employee's benefits.
* **Deductions** (`Deductions`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the employee's deductions.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Enter the employee's address.
* **Pay Frequency 3** (`Pay Frequency 3`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Pay Period** (`Pay Period`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Pay Frequency 4** (`Pay Frequency 4`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Pay Rate 3** (`Pay Rate 3`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.
* **Pay Rate 4** (`Pay Rate 4`, text, optional): This field is not currently used; please do not complete it.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the HR or payroll administrator.
* This form should only be used for temporary employees who are being paid on a non-standard schedule.
