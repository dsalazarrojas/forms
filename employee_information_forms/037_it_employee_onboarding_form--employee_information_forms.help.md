# it_employee_onboarding_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The it\_employee\_onboarding\_form is designed to help track and manage employee onboarding progress. This form collects information about employee email, onboarding status, tasks completed, notes, phone number, onboarding date and time, time of completion, and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the first field, "IT Employee Email".
2. Select your current onboarding status from the "Onboarding Status" field, which is a dropdown menu with three options: "Completed", "Not Started", or "In Progress".
3. If required, indicate if you have completed any tasks by selecting the "Completed Tasks" field and choosing the relevant option(s).
4. If needed, add any notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "IT Employee Phone" field, if applicable.
6. Provide the date and time of your onboarding in the "IT Employee Onboarding Date" and "IT Employee Onboarding Time" fields.
7. Optionally, select the time of completion in the "IT Employee Completion Time" field.
8. Finally, add any comments or information in the "IT Employee Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **IT Employee Email (Email)**: Enter your email address associated with your employment.
* **Onboarding Status (Select One)**: Select the status of your onboarding, which can be "Completed", "Not Started", or "In Progress".
* **Completed Tasks (Select Multiple)**: If applicable, select the tasks you have completed during onboarding.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your onboarding process.
* **IT Employee Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **IT Employee Onboarding Date (Date)**: Provide the date of your onboarding.
* **IT Employee Onboarding Time (Time)**: Provide the time of your onboarding.
* **IT Employee Completion Time (Time)**: Optionally, select the time of completion.
* **IT Employee Comments (Text)**: Add any comments or information related to your onboarding.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you're unsure about a specific field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- Review and update your onboarding status regularly to reflect changes.
