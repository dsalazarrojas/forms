# Professional Development Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new hire professional development specialists to ensure they are properly on-boarded into the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each section accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name, job title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select the training programs that you require to perform your job duties effectively.
* **New Hire Information** (`new_hire_information`, `number`, required: false): Please enter your date of hire or other relevant information about your new hire status.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the training programs that you require to perform your job duties effectively.
* **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your training goals or objectives.
* **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your work environment to ensure that the necessary resources and equipment are provided.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job title.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your reporting manager.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your job classification.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your job level.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your work status.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your job category.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job role.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your job location.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your work email.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your work phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter the email of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Job** (`emergency_contact_job`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the job of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Job Title** (`emergency_contact_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the job title of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Work Location** (`emergency_contact_work_location`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the work location of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Work Email** (`emergency_contact_work_email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter the work email of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Work Phone** (`emergency_contact_work_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the work phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_contact_notes`, `text`, required: false): Please enter any additional information about your emergency contact person.
* **New Hire Date** (`new_hire_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter your date of hire or other relevant information about your new hire status.
