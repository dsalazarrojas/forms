# Work Permit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information for a work permit application. It collects details about the employee, their job title, reporting manager, department, work location, start and end dates of work, employee type (full-time, part-time, or contract), hours worked, pay rate, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter the employee's job title in the "job_title" field.
3. Enter the name of the employee's reporting manager in the "reporting_manager" field.
4. Select the employee's department from the available options.
5. Enter the employee's work location in the "work_location" field.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the employee's work in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively.
7. Select the employee's type of employment (full-time, part-time, or contract) in the "employee_type" field.
8. Enter the number of hours worked by the employee in the "hours_worked" field.
9. Enter the employee's pay rate in the "pay_rate" field.
10. (Optional) Provide emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact" and "Emergency Alternate Contact 1" fields.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the work permit in the "work_permit_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the employee's name as it appears on their official documentation.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the employee's job title as it appears on their official documentation.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee's reporting manager.
- **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the department where the employee works.
- **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the employee works.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the employee's work.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the employee's work.
- **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of employment (full-time, part-time, or contract).
- **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the number of hours worked by the employee.
- **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, number, required): Enter the employee's pay rate.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
- **Emergency Alternate Contact 1** (`emergency_alternate_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of an alternate emergency contact.
- **Notes** (`work_permit_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the work permit.
