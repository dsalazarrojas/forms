# Workforce Planning Tool Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Planning Tool Requisition Form is designed to collect and document information for employee requests for job changes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review all fields carefully and fill out the form with the required information.
2. For fields with options, select the most relevant and accurate option from the provided list.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below or contact your HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information Form
* **Employee Information Form** (employee_information_form): This section is meant for the employee making the job change request. Please fill out the form with your personal details and job requirements.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (job_title): Select the current or desired job title you are applying for.

### Requisition Date
* **Requisition Date** (requisition_date): Enter the date you are submitting your job change request.

### Requisitioned By
* **Requisitioned By** (requisitioned_by): Enter your name or employee ID.

### Manager Review
* **Manager Review** (manager_review): Select the manager's name or ID who will review your job change request.

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (job_description): Provide a brief description of the job you are applying for.

### Job Classification
* **Job Classification** (job_classification): Select the job classification that best fits your new role.

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (job_type): Select the job type that best suits your new role.

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (job_status): Select the status of your new job request.

### Requisition Details
* **Requisition Details** (requisition_details): Provide any additional details or comments about your job change request.

### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Select the employee ID of the person making the job change request.

### Manager ID
* **Manager ID** (manager_id): Select the manager's ID who will review your job change request.

### Requisitioned By Manager
* **Requisitioned By Manager** (requisitioned_by_manager): Enter the name or ID of the manager who will review your job change request.

### Manager Review Status
* **Manager Review Status** (manager_review_status): Select the status of the manager's review.

### Notes
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or notes about your job change request.

### Job Title 2
* **Job Title 2** (job_title_2): Select the second job title you are applying for.

### Job Classification 2
* **Job Classification 2** (job_classification_2): Select the second job classification that best fits your new role.

### Job Type 2
* **Job Type 2** (job_type_2): Select the second job type that best suits your new role.

### Job Status 2
* **Job Status 2** (job_status_2): Select the status of your second job request.

### Requisition Details 2
* **Requisition Details 2** (requisition_details_2): Provide any additional details or comments about your second job change request.

### Employee ID 2
* **Employee ID 2** (employee_id_2): Select the employee ID of the person making the second job change request.

### Manager ID 2
* **Manager ID 2** (manager_id_2): Select the manager's ID who will review your second job change request.

### Requisitioned By Manager 2
* **Requisitioned By Manager 2** (requisitioned_by_manager_2): Enter the name or ID of the manager who will review your second job change request.

### Manager Review Status 2
* **Manager Review Status 2** (manager_review_status_2): Select the status of the manager's review.

### Notes 2
* **Notes 2** (notes_2): Add any additional comments or notes about your second job change request.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting your job change request.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out as per your current job status and requirements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, refer to your HR representative for guidance.
