# Time Off Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Time Off Request Form is a document used to record an employee's time off requests, allowing them to inform the management and HR of their absence.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal contact information, including your contact number and email address.
2. Select the date you will be taking off and the date you will be returning to work.
3. Choose the time interval you will be taking off (e.g., 1 day, 1 week, etc.).
4. Choose the time unit for your time off (e.g., AM/PM).
5. Provide a brief reason for your time off.
6. Add any additional comments or notes regarding your time off.
7. Fill out your external contact email address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, optional): Fill out this field with your personal contact information.
* **Leave Date** (Leave Date, date, optional): Enter the date you will be taking off.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the date you will be returning to work.
* **Time Interval** (Time Interval, number, optional): Choose the number of days or hours you will be taking off.
* **Time Interval Unit** (Time Interval Unit, select_one, required): Choose the time unit for your time off (e.g., AM/PM).
* **Time Off Reason** (Time Off Reason, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your time off.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your time off.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, optional): Enter your internal contact email address.
* **External Contact Email** (External Contact Email, email, optional): Enter your external contact email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, as it will be used to document your time off.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, contact your HR representative.
* If you need to add additional time off days or units, please fill out this form for each day/unit separately.
