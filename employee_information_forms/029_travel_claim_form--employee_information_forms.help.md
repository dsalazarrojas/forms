# Travel Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Claim Form is used by employees to submit their travel expenses for reimbursement. To ensure accurate processing, please fill out the form with all necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you should:

* Review all fields carefully before submitting your claim.
* Ensure that you have attached a receipt for the submitted expense (if required).
* Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details (`employee_details`)**: Enter your employee ID, name, and any other identifying information as required. This will help us to process your claim accurately.
* **Expense Type (`expense_type`)**: Select the type of expense you are claiming reimbursement for (e.g., Airfare, Hotel, Restaurants, or Rental Car).
* **Expense Amount (`expense_amount`)**: Enter the exact amount of the expense, including any taxes or fees associated with it.
* **Expense Date (`expense_date`)**: Enter the date of the expense or the date you incurred the expense.
* **Expense Description (`expense_description`)**: Provide a brief description of the expense, including any relevant details that would help us process your claim.
* **Receipt Upload (`receipt_upload`)**: If required, please attach a receipt for the expense as proof of purchase.
* **Submit (`submit`)**: Once you have completed all fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your claim for review.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your submission before finalizing it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your manager or HR representative for assistance.
