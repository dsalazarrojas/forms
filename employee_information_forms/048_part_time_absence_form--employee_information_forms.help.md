# Part Time Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to report a part-time absence by an employee, providing details about the absence duration and reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee number (if required for internal use).
2. Fill in your name and job title as they appear on your personnel file.
3. Enter the date you will start being absent and the date you will end being absent.
4. Enter a brief description of your absence reason.
5. Enter the total number of days you will be absent.
6. Choose the status of your absence (Approved, Pending, or Denied).
7. Add any additional comments or evidence related to your absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id**: This field is for internal use only. Please don't fill out this field, unless you are authorized to do so.
* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel file.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your personnel file.
* **absence_start_date**: Enter the date from which you will be absent.
* **absence_end_date**: Enter the date until which you will be absent.
* **reason_for_absence**: Enter a brief description of your absence reason, e.g., sick leave, family leave, etc.
* **days_absence**: Enter the total number of days you will be absent.
* **approval_date**: This field is for future use and is not required at the moment. If you have any supporting documents or evidence, attach it to this field using the `attachment` field.
* **approval_status**: Choose one of three options: Approved, Pending, or Denied.
* **submission_note**: Enter any additional comments you have regarding your absence.
* **attachment**: Attach any supporting evidence or documents related to your absence.
