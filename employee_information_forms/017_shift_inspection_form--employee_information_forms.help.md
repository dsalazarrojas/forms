# shift_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shift_inspection_form is used by management or HR personnel to record and document an employee's attendance and performance during their shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions on this form as accurately as possible.
- You may choose multiple options for each field where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Shift** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether or not you worked your first shift. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Second Shift** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether or not you worked your second shift. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Performance** (`note`, required/optional): Describe your performance during your shifts in the note field.
- **Attendance** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select whether or not you attended your shifts. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you worked a shift but are unable to choose 'Yes', please explain in the note field.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to management or HR.
