# Massage Health History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Massage Health History Form is designed to gather information about a client's medical history, medications, and allergies to ensure their safety and well-being during massage therapy sessions. This form helps identify any potential risks or concerns that may affect the massage treatment and provides valuable information for the massage therapist to make informed decisions about the client's care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Details**: Enter the client's name and contact information for our records.
2. **Medical History**: Check the relevant boxes for any medical conditions or allergies you have. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide more details in the text fields.
3. **Allergies and Medications**: Select all the medications you are currently taking and any allergies you have.
4. **Medications Side Effects**: Check if you experience any side effects from your medications.
5. **Massage Therapist**: Confirm if you have had a massage therapist before.
6. **Last Massage**: Enter the date of your last massage session.
7. **Next Massage**: Enter the date of your next massage session.
8. **Medical Conditions**: Check if you have any medical conditions.
9. **Allergies Details**: Provide more information about any allergies you have.
10. **Medications Details**: Provide more information about any medications you are taking.
11. **Medical Conditions Details**: Provide more information about any medical conditions you have.
12. **Medications Side Effects Details**: Provide more information about any side effects from your medications.
13. **Massage Therapist Details**: Provide more information about your massage therapist experience.
14. **Last Massage Details**: Provide more information about your last massage session.
15. **Next Massage Details**: Provide more information about your next massage session.
16. **Emergency Contacts**: Enter any emergency contacts.
17. **Other Health Info**: Provide any additional health information that may be relevant to your massage therapy.
18. **Health Info Comments**: Add any comments or concerns you have about your health.
19. **Emergency Contacts Details**: Provide more information about your emergency contacts.
20. **Other Health Info Details**: Provide more information about any other health information.
21. **Health Info Comments Details**: Add any additional comments or concerns about your health.
22. **Medical History Comments**: Add any comments or concerns about your medical history.
23. **Allergies Comments**: Add any comments or concerns about your allergies.
24. **Medications Comments**: Add any comments or concerns about your medications.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please ask your massage therapist for clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for medical advice or diagnosis. If you have any health concerns, please consult a medical professional.
* Please keep in mind that the information collected in this form will be used to provide the best possible care for you during massage therapy sessions.
