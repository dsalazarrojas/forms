# Lip Blush Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and medical history for a lip blush consultation. It's intended to help beauty professionals assess the client's suitability for certain treatments or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Client Information** (page 1) by entering your name, contact name, contact number, and email address.
2. On **Medical Information** (page 2), select any applicable medical conditions you have from the list.
3. For **Allergies** (page 3), select any allergies you have from the list.
4. On **Medical History** (page 4), enter any relevant medical history or notes about your condition.
5. If you take any medications, enter the details in the **Medications** field.
6. On **Allergies** (page 5), indicate if you have any allergies to medications (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
7. On **Medical Condition** (page 6), indicate if you have any existing medical conditions (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
8. Enter any additional medical notes or concerns in the **Medical Notes** field (page 7).
9. On **Contact Information** (page 8), enter the contact details of the person you are consenting to have this information shared with.
10. Sign the **Client Signature** field to confirm your consent.
11. Enter your **Esthetician Signature** (page 9) to acknowledge you've reviewed the form and understand the information collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (page 1, required): Enter your personal details such as name, contact name, contact number, and email address.
* **Medical Information** (page 2, required): Select any medical conditions you have from the list.
* **Allergies** (page 3, required): Select any allergies you have from the list.
* **Medications** (page 4, optional): Enter any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (page 5, optional): Indicate if you have any allergies to medications (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Medical Condition** (page 6, optional): Indicate if you have any existing medical conditions (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Medical Notes** (page 7, optional): Enter any additional medical notes or concerns.
* **Contact Information** (page 8, optional): Enter the contact details of the person you are consenting to have this information shared with.
* **Client Signature** (page 9, required): Sign to confirm your consent.
* **Esthetician Signature** (page 10, required): Enter your signature to acknowledge you've reviewed the form and understand the information collected.

Note: Some fields have been removed or merged to improve clarity.
