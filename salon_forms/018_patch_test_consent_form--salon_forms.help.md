# Patch Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patch Test Consent Form is a form used to collect necessary information and consent from clients before undergoing a patch test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your salutation (Mr, Mrs, Dr, Ms, Other)
* Enter your name
* Enter your contact phone number
* Enter your email address
* Choose your date of birth
* Select your gender
* Choose your relationship with the client (Spouse, Child, Friend, Other)
* Provide consent for patch testing
* Provide a signature for consent

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Salutation (Mr, Mrs, Dr, Ms, Other):**
	+ Choose the correct salutation that best describes you.
* **Client Name:**
	+ Enter your full name.
* **Phone:**
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email:**
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth:**
	+ Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender:**
	+ Choose your gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary).
* **Relationship:**
	+ Choose your relationship with the client (Spouse, Child, Friend, Other).
* **Consent for Patch Test:**
	+ Indicate whether you give consent for patch testing or not.
* **Patch Test Consent Date:**
	+ Enter the date you provided consent for patch testing.
* **Patch Test Result:**
	+ Choose the result of your patch test (Negative, Positive).
* **Consent Date 2:**
	+ This field is used to record the date of consent again.
* **Test Result 2:**
	+ Choose the result of your second patch test (Negative, Positive).
* **Type of Consent:**
	+ Choose the type of consent you provided (Patch Test, Allergic Test, Other).
* **Consent Notes:**
	+ Enter any additional notes related to your consent.
* **Treatment Date:**
	+ Enter the date of treatment.
* **Treatment Time:**
	+ Enter the time of treatment.
* **Treatment Provider:**
	+ Enter the name of the treatment provider.
* **Treatment Details:**
	+ Enter any additional details about your treatment.
* **Consent:**
	+ Indicate whether you give consent for patch testing or not.
* **Patch Test Date:**
	+ Enter the date of the patch test.
* **Type:**
	+ Enter the type of patch test.
* **Result:**
	+ Enter the result of your patch test.
 
Note: This form is designed to be completed with the guidance of a healthcare professional. If you are unsure of any of the information or have questions, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
