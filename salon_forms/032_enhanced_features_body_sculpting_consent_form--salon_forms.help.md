# Enhanced Features Body Sculpting Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients regarding their consent to undergo body sculpting procedures. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the client's preferences and medical history to ensure a safe and successful treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer for "Body Sculpting" by choosing one or more of the options below:
	* Yes
	* No
2. Choose your salutation (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.):
	* Mr.
	* Mrs.
	* Ms.
	* Dr.
3. Enter your first name in the "FirstName" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "LastName" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "DateOfBirth" field.
8. If you have any medical history or concerns, please provide this information in the "MedicalHistory" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Body Sculpting**: This field allows you to indicate your interest in body sculpting procedures. Choose "Yes" if you are interested, and "No" if you are not.
* **Salutation**: This field is used to indicate your marital status (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.). Choose the option that best represents your relationship status.
* **FirstName**: This field is used to collect your first name. Please enter your first name in this field.
* **LastName**: This field is used to collect your last name. Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone**: This field is used to collect your phone number. Please enter your phone number in this field.
* **DateOfBirth**: This field is used to collect your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in this format: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **MedicalHistory**: This field is used to provide any relevant medical history or concerns. Please enter any relevant information in this field.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
