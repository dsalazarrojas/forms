# kathryns_color_match_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kathryns_color_match_form is a form designed to help users identify their perfect color match. It consists of a series of questions and fields that guide the user through the process of determining their ideal color scheme.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Match Color Form" button to initiate the process.
2. Select your color match from the options provided in the "What color is your match?" field.
3. Confirm your color match selection by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Your color match is" field.
4. Provide a brief description of your match in the "Description" field.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your color match in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Match Color Form** (`1`, `text`, optional): This is the first step in the color matching process. It is a text field where you can enter any relevant information about your color preferences.
* **What color is your match?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field presents you with a list of options to choose from. Select the color that best matches your preferences.
* **Your color match is** (`3`, `select_one`, optional): This field asks you to confirm your color match selection. Choose "Yes" to confirm or "No" to indicate that the provided options do not match your preferences.
* **Description** (`4`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of your color match in this field. This can be a helpful way to clarify your thoughts and preferences.
* **Note** (`5`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your color match in this field. This is a free-text field where you can write your thoughts and ideas.

## Tips
- Make sure to select a color that you truly feel matches your preferences.
- Be as specific as possible when describing your color match in the "Description" field.
- Use the "Note" field to record any additional information or insights you'd like to capture about your color match.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if you feel that they are not relevant to your color matching process.
