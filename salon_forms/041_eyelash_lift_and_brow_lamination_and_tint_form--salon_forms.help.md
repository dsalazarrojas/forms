# Eyelash Lift And Brow Lamination And Tint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for eyelash lift, brow lamination, and tinting services at the salon. Please complete this form to provide essential details for your procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the procedure you want to book: Choose from "Eyelash Lift", "Brow Lamination", or "Tint" to indicate the service you wish to book.
2. Provide your name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your age: Please provide your age for administrative purposes.
4. Enter your phone number: Provide your contact phone number for communication and scheduling purposes.
5. Enter your email: Provide your email address for communication and scheduling purposes.
6. Provide medical history: Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or contraindications that may affect your treatment.
7. Provide medical history contraindications: If there are any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please describe them in this field.
8. List any allergies: If you have any allergies, please list them in this field.
9. Informed consent: Please confirm that you have understood the procedures and risks involved.
10. Signature: Please sign and date this form to confirm your consent.
11. Date: Enter the date you sign this form.
12. Note: Add any additional comments or notes about your procedure.
13. Medical history medication: Describe any medications you are currently taking.
14. Medical history medication allergies: If you have any allergies to medications, please list them in this field.
15. Medical history medical conditions: Describe any medical conditions you may have.
16. Medical history medical conditions allergies: If you have any allergies related to medical conditions, please list them in this field.
17. Medical history previous procedures: Describe any previous procedures you have undergone.
18. Medical history previous procedures allergies: If you have any allergies related to previous procedures, please list them in this field.
19. Medical history surgeries: Describe any surgeries you have undergone.
20. Medical history surgeries allergies: If you have any allergies related to surgeries, please list them in this field.
21. Consent: Confirm that you have read and understood the procedures and risks involved.
22. Signature date: Enter the date you sign this form.
23. Payment: Please provide payment information for your procedure.
