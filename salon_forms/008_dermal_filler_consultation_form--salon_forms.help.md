## dermal_filler_consultation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to provide information regarding their dermal filler consultation. It aims to collect general medical history, medical conditions, allergies, medications, dermal filler use, medical history, general questions, consent, and additional comments. This form is intended to gather crucial information for healthcare professionals to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) and provide any additional comments or details as needed.
3. Ensure you have the consent form signed and dated before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Medical History** (`general_medical_history`, text, required: false): This field is meant to collect any general medical history relevant to your treatment. Please provide any relevant medical information, such as previous surgeries, medical conditions, or allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): In this field, list any existing medical conditions you may have, including but not limited to, diabetes, high blood pressure, or allergies.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): Report any allergies you may have, including medications, foods, or environmental factors.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosage and frequency.
* **Dermal Filler Use** (`dermal_filler_use`, text, required: false): If you have used dermal fillers before, please provide details about your past experiences, including when and why you used them.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any significant medical history you think might be relevant to your treatment.
* **General Questions** (`general_questions`, text, required: false): Answer any general questions or concerns you may have about dermal fillers.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, required: false): Review and sign this consent form, indicating you understand and agree to the treatment.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information you think are relevant to your treatment.
* **Dermal Filler Product Used** (`dermal_filler_product_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the dermal filler product(s) you have used or would like to use. The options are Juveda, Restylane, Revanesse, Belotero, and Collagen.
