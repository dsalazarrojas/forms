# Salon Equipment Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Equipment Entry Form is a data collection tool used to record information about salon equipment, such as hairdryers, towel warmers, and hair trimmers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of equipment from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the brand and model of the equipment.
3. Specify the condition of the equipment (New, Old, or Refurbished).
4. Enter the serial number of the equipment.
5. Enter the last inspection date of the equipment.
6. Enter the price of the equipment.
7. Specify the status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, or Sold).
8. Enter the last updated time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Type
* **Type**: Select the type of equipment from the dropdown menu to indicate what kind of equipment it is (e.g., Hairdryer, Towel warmer, Hair trimmer).
### Brand
* **Brand**: Enter the brand of the equipment.
### Model
* **Model**: Enter the model number or version of the equipment.
### Condition
* **Condition**: Specify the condition of the equipment (New, Old, or Refurbished).
### Serial Number
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the equipment.
### Last Inspection
* **Last Inspection**: Enter the date of the last inspection of the equipment.
### Price
* **Price**: Enter the price of the equipment.
### Status
* **Status**: Specify the status of the equipment (Active, Inactive, or Sold).
### Last Updated
* **Last Updated**: Enter the last time the equipment was updated.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the equipment information is up-to-date and accurate.
* Use the dropdown menu for the Type field to select the correct type of equipment.
* If the equipment is not a new item, select the correct condition (Old, Refurbished) from the dropdown menu.
* The serial number, last inspection date, price, and last updated time fields should be entered in their respective formats.
* The status field should be filled out based on the current state of the equipment.
