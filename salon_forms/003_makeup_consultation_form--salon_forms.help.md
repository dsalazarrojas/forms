# Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Consultation Form is a guide to help clients fill out the form correctly.
## How To Complete This Form

*   **Form Title**: Enter any name or a brief description of your needs. (`form-title`, text, optional)
*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification. (`full-name`, text, optional)
*   **Age**: Enter your age. (`age`, number, optional)
*   **Email**: Enter your email address. (`email`, email, optional)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (`phone`, text, optional)
*   **Makeup Type**: Select the type of makeup service you are interested in. (`makeup-type`, select_one, optional)
	+ Foundation
	+ Concealer
	+ Blush
	+ Eyeshadow
	+ Eyeliner
	+ Mascara
	+ Lipstick
*   **Color Preferences**: Select the colors you prefer for your makeup. (`color-prefs`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Blue
	+ Red
	+ Pink
	+ Purple
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
*   **Hair Type**: Select the type of hair you have. (`hair-type`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Long
	+ Short
*   **Face Shape**: Select the shape of your face. (`face-shape`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Oval
	+ Round
	+ Heart
	+ Diamond
*   **Hair Color**: Select the color of your hair. (`hair-color`, select_one, optional)
	+ Blonde
	+ Brunett
	+ Red
	+ Black
*   **Skin Type**: Select the type of skin you have. (`skin-type`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Normal
	+ Sensitive
	+ Oily
	+ Combination
*   **Product Interested**: Select the products you are interested in. (`product-interested`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Foundation
	+ Concealer
	+ Eyeshadow
	+ Eyeliner
	+ Mascara
	+ Lipstick
*   **Note**: Add any additional comments or questions. (`note`, note, optional)
*   **Confirm Appointment**: Confirm that you have an appointment scheduled. (`confirm-appointment`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ True
	+ False
*   **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of your appointment. (`appointment-date`, date, optional)
*   **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of your appointment. (`appointment-time`, time, optional)
*   **Follow-up Question**: Ask any follow-up question. (`followup-question`, text, optional)
*   **Follow-up Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication. (`followup-email`, text, optional)
*   **Follow-up Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication. (`followup-phone`, text, optional)
*   **Note 2**: Add any additional comments or questions. (`note-2`, note, optional)
*   **Follow-up Question 2**: Ask any additional follow-up question. (`followup-question-2`, text, optional)
*   **Follow-up Email 2**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication. (`followup-email-2`, text, optional)
*   **Follow-up Phone 2**: Enter your phone number for follow-up communication. (`followup-phone-2`, text, optional)

## Tips

*   Be accurate when filling out the form to ensure that the makeup artist has the best possible information to provide you with the best service.
*   Review your information carefully before submitting the form to catch any errors.
*   If you need to make any changes, you can edit the form before submitting it.
