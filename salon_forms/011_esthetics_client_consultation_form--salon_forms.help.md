# esthetics_client_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The esthetics_client_consultation_form is a form used to gather information from clients about their skincare needs and preferences. This form is intended to be completed by clients before or during an esthetician consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Please provide accurate and honest information about your skincare needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (1): 
	+ Label: Client Information
	+ Description: This section is where you can provide general information about yourself. Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (2): 
	+ Label: Medical History
	+ Description: This section is for any medical conditions or allergies you may have that could affect your skincare routine. Please select all that apply.
* **Skin Assessment** (3): 
	+ Label: Skin Assessment
	+ Description: Describe your current skin issues, such as acne, rosacea, or aging concerns.
* **Skincare Preferences** (4): 
	+ Label: Skincare Preferences
	+ Description: Share your skincare preferences, including your favorite products, routines, and concerns.
* **Skincare Needs** (5): 
	+ Label: Skincare Needs
	+ Description: Describe your current skin needs, including areas of concern and desired outcomes.
* **Medical Conditions** (6): 
	+ Label: Medical Conditions
	+ Description: Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your skincare routine. Select all that apply.
* **Skincare Products** (7): 
	+ Label: Skincare Products
	+ Description: List the products you currently use and are comfortable with.
* **Skincare Procedures** (8): 
	+ Label: Skincare Procedures
	+ Description: Describe any skincare procedures or treatments you have tried before.
* **Skincare Goals** (9): 
	+ Label: Skincare Goals
	+ Description: Share your skincare goals, such as reducing acne, improving skin texture, or reducing wrinkles.
* **Skincare Products Used** (10): 
	+ Label: Skincare Products Used
	+ Description: List the products you currently use and are comfortable with.
* **Skincare Procedures Used** (11): 
	+ Label: Skincare Procedures Used
	+ Description: Describe any skincare procedures or treatments you have tried before.
* **Skincare Goals Used** (12): 
	+ Label: Skincare Goals
	+ Description: Share your skincare goals, such as reducing acne, improving skin texture, or reducing wrinkles.
* **Skincare Recommendations** (13): 
	+ Label: Skincare Recommendations
	+ Description: This section is for the aesthetician to take notes on your skincare recommendations.
* **Skincare Product Recommendations** (14): 
	+ Label: Skincare Product Recommendations
	+ Description: This section is for the aesthetician to take notes on product recommendations for you.
* **Medical History Other** (15): 
	+ Label: Medical History Other
	+ Description: If you have any other medical history not listed above, please enter it here.
* **Client Notes** (16): 
	+ Label: Client Notes
	+ Description: This section is for you to take notes on any concerns or questions you have.
* **Aesthetician Notes** (17): 
	+ Label: Aesthetician Notes
	+ Description: This section is for the aesthetician to take notes on your consultation.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your aesthetician.
* This form is designed to help the aesthetician understand your needs and provide the best possible service for you.
