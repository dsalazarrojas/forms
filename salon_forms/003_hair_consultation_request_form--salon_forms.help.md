## Purpose
The Hair Consultation Request Form is intended to gather information from customers who are interested in receiving a hair consultation at a salon. This form helps the salon to understand the customer's preferences, requirements, and availability, allowing them to provide a tailored experience for the customer.

Before completing this form, please ensure that you are not duplicating a request for a consultation that has already been made. If you have previously submitted a consultation request, you may contact the salon directly to inquire about the status of your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Select the type of service you are interested in (Cut, Color, Style).
3. Choose the length of your hair (Short, Medium, Long).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Provide your phone number.
6. Select a date and time for your consultation.
7. Select a time for your consultation (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
• **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required)
This field is where you should enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Hair Type
• **Hair Type** (`consultation_type`, Select, Optional)
Select the type of service you are interested in (Cut, Color, Style).

### Hair Length
• **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose the length of your hair (Short, Medium, Long).

### Customer Email
• **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Optional)
Enter your email address. This will be used by the salon to communicate with you about your consultation.

### Customer Phone
• **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, Optional)
Provide your phone number. This will be used by the salon to contact you about your consultation.

### Date of Visit
• **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, Date, Optional)
Select a date for your consultation.

### Time of Visit
• **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, Time, Optional)
Select a time for your consultation. Please note that this is not required.
