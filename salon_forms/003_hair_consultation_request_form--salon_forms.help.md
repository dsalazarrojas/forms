# Hair Consultation Request Form - Help Guide

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
• **Hair Type**: Select the type of service you are interested in (Cut, Color, Style).
• **Hair Length**: Choose the length of your hair (Short, Medium, Long).
• **Customer Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used by the salon to communicate with you about your consultation.
• **Customer Phone**: Provide your phone number. This will be used by the salon to contact you about your consultation.
• **Date of Visit**: Select a date for your consultation.
• **Time of Visit**: Select a time for your consultation.
