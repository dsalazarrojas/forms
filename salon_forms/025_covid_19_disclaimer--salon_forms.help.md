# COVID 19 Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Disclaimer form is a mandatory health screening tool used to assess a client's health status, especially concerning COVID-19 symptoms and vaccination. This form helps the salon take necessary safety measures to prevent the transmission of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Read each question carefully**: Answer each question honestly and accurately.
2.  **Select the correct answer**: Choose the option that best describes your current situation.
3.  **Fill out the form completely**: Ensure you answer all required questions to ensure accurate assessment.
4.  **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to finalize your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **-- COVID-19 Health Disclaimer --**: This section explains the importance of the form and the salon's safety protocols.
*  **Client Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued identification.
*  **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
*  **Service Requested**: Describe the service you're interested in receiving (e.g., haircut, coloring, etc.).
*  **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
*  **Email Address**: Optional: enter your email address for salon communications.
*  **-- Health Screening Questions --**: Answer the following questions regarding your health status.
    +  **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 10 days?**: Answer 'Yes' if you've been diagnosed with COVID-19 within the past 10 days. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Do you currently have COVID-19 symptoms?**: Answer 'Yes' if you're experiencing symptoms. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **If yes, describe symptoms**: If you answered 'Yes' to the previous question, describe your symptoms in the text box below.
    +  **Do you have a fever?**: Answer 'Yes' if you currently have a fever. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Do you have a cough?**: Answer 'Yes' if you're experiencing a cough. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Difficulty breathing?**: Answer 'Yes' if you're having difficulty breathing. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Loss of taste or smell?**: Answer 'Yes' if you're experiencing loss of taste or smell. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Sore Throat?**: Answer 'Yes' if you have a sore throat. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Close contact with confirmed COVID-19 case in past 14 days?**: Answer 'Yes' if you've had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case. Answer 'No' if not.
    +  **Anyone in your household has COVID-19 symptoms?**: Answer 'Yes' if someone in your household is experiencing symptoms. Answer 'No' if not.
*  **-- Vaccination Status --**: Answer the following question regarding your vaccination status.
    +  **Vaccination Status**: Choose from the options to select your vaccination status (Fully vaccinated with booster, fully vaccinated without booster, Partially vaccinated, Not vaccinated, or Prefer not to disclose).
*  **-- Acknowledgment and Agreement --**: Read and agree to the following statements.
    +  **The health information above is truthful and accurate**: Answer 'Yes' if your answers are accurate.
    +  **I understand the risks of COVID-19 transmission**: Answer 'Yes' if you understand the risks.
    +  **I acknowledge the salon safety measures**: Answer 'Yes' if you acknowledge the safety measures.
    +  **I release the salon from liability for COVID-19 exposure**: Answer 'Yes' if you release the salon from liability.
    +  **I agree to this disclaimer and release of liability**: Answer 'Yes' if you agree to the disclaimer.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to answer all required fields accurately to ensure proper assessment.
*   If you're unsure about any question, answer 'Unsure' and we'll discuss further with you at the salon.
*   Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
