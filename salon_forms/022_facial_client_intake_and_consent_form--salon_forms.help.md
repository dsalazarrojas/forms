# Facial Client Intake And Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from clients prior to receiving treatment at a salon or spa facility. It includes sections for client details, medical history, skin health, allergies, contact information, medical notes, and a signature confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their name, contact details, and any relevant medical history.
2. Provide information about their skin health and any medical conditions that may affect their treatment.
3. List any allergies they may have.
4. Add any relevant medical notes or comments.
5. Finally, select one of the options for the signature field to confirm that they have read and understood the provided information and will provide accurate and truthful answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, text, optional)**: This section is for clients to provide their name, contact information, and any other relevant details that the salon or spa may need to know.
* **Medical History (Medical History, text, optional)**: Please provide any previous medical history that may be relevant to their treatment. This can include allergies, previous surgeries, or health conditions.
* **Skin Health (Skin Health, text, optional)**: Describe the current state of their skin, including any conditions such as acne, eczema, or other skin irritations.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions, text, optional)**: List any medical conditions that may impact their treatment, such as pregnancy, epilepsy, or other conditions that require special care.
* **Allergies (Allergies, text, optional)**: If the client has any allergies or sensitivities, please list them here.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, text, optional)**: Enter the client's contact information, such as their phone number and email address.
* **Medical Notes (Medical Notes, text, optional)**: Add any additional medical notes or comments that may be relevant to their treatment.
* **Signature (Signature, select_one, optional)**: Select one of the options to confirm that they have read and understood the provided information and will provide accurate and truthful answers. 

## Tips
Please ensure that clients complete this form accurately and honestly before proceeding with treatment. If there are any concerns or questions, please ask the client to clarify their response.
