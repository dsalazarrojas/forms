# Dermal Filler Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dermal Filler Client Intake Form is designed to collect client information for dermal filler procedures. This form helps to gather essential details about the client, including their name, contact information, and appointment schedule, to prepare for their appointment and facilitate effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that the client's details are not already stored in the system to avoid duplication.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the client's full name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Notes** (`note`, text, required/optional: false): Provide any additional information about the client, if necessary.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional: false): Enter the time of the appointment.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the entered information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you encounter any errors while filling the form, please review the form carefully and try again.
