# Maskcara Color Match - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maskcara Color Match form is designed to gather information from customers about their color preferences and needs. This form helps the business understand the customer's color type (natural or unnatural) and the type of color match they are looking for (exact or similar). The form also captures the customer's name, color name, and other relevant details to facilitate effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **customer name** in the "customer name" field.
2. Provide the **color name** you are interested in matching.
3. Choose whether the color is **natural** or **unnatural** from the options provided.
4. Select the **color match type** you are looking for: **exact** or **similar**.
5. Enter the **date of interest**, if applicable.
6. Enter the **time of interest**, if applicable.
7. Add any additional **note** about your color needs.
8. Enter your **email address** (not shown, but assumed to be a placeholder).
9. Enter your **phone number**, if you prefer to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Color Name**: Enter the name of the color you are interested in matching.
* **Customer Color Type**: Choose whether the color is natural (based on organic pigments) or unnatural (based on synthetic pigments).
* **Color Match Type**: Select whether you are looking for an exact match or a similar match.
* **Date of Interest**: Enter the date when you would like the color matching service to be performed, if applicable.
* **Time of Interest**: Enter the time when you would like the color matching service to be performed, if applicable.
* **Note**: Add any additional information about your color needs or preferences.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your color match.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly, if you prefer.
