# Hydromassage Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydromassage Liability Waiver form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a hydromassage treatment. It ensures that both the client and the provider are aware of their responsibilities and liabilities before, during, and after the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Waiver Details field with a brief description of the treatment and any relevant details.
2. Select the date of the treatment from the calendar.
3. If you have a provider, enter their name and other relevant information.
4. Select the massage treatment(s) you will receive.
5. Enter your name and contact information (phone number and email).
6. Sign the waiver to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Sign the provider's signature to confirm that they have read and understood the terms and conditions.
8. Enter the date of the massage treatment.
9. Enter your email address and client address information (city, state, and ZIP).
10. Enter any other relevant information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waiver Details**: A brief description of the treatment and any relevant details.
* **Date**: The date of the treatment. Select a date from the calendar.
* **Provider Name**: If you have a provider, enter their name.
* **Massage Treatment**: Select the massage treatment(s) you will receive.
* **Client Name**: Enter your name.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Waiver Signature**: Sign to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Provider Signature**: Sign to confirm that the provider has read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Massage**: Enter the date of the massage treatment.
* **Client E-Mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Client Address**: Enter your city, state, and ZIP.
* **Client City**: Enter the city where you will receive the massage.
* **Client State**: Enter the state where you will receive the massage.
* **Client ZIP**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Provider Address**: Enter the provider's address.
* **Provider City**: Enter the city where the provider's business is located.
* **Provider State**: Enter the state where the provider's business is located.
* **Provider ZIP**: Enter the provider's ZIP code.
* **Terms and Conditions**: A description of the terms and conditions of the treatment.
* **Client DOB**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Provider License**: If you have a provider, enter their license number.
* **Client Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions you have that may affect the treatment.
* **Provider License Number**: If you have a provider, enter their license number.
* **Client Medical Insurance**: If you have medical insurance, enter the name and number.
* **Client Other Info**: Enter any other relevant information you would like to provide.

Note: This form is intended to be a general guide and should be reviewed and updated as necessary to ensure it accurately reflects the specific needs and requirements of your business.
