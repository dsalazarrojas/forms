# thrivers_society_digital_consultation_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients about their experience at the salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide a rating for your overall experience.
4. Write a review of your experience.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Information** (`salon_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about the salon that you visited. This can include the name of the salon, the address, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required/optional): This field is for you to select the time of your appointment.
* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide any additional feedback about your experience.
* **How did you feel about the service?** (`feedback_question1`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for you to select how you felt about the service you received. Please choose from the options: very good, good, neutral, bad.
* **Was the service delivered on time?** (`feedback_question2`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for you to select whether the service was delivered on time. Please choose from the options: yes, no, unknown.
* **Would you come back to this salon?** (`feedback_question3`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for you to select whether you would come back to this salon. Please choose from the options: yes, no, maybe.
* **How likely are you to recommend this salon to a friend?** (`feedback_question4`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for you to select how likely you are to recommend this salon to a friend. Please choose from the options: very likely, likely, not likely, not at all likely.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a rating of your experience.
* **Review** (`review`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to write a review of your experience.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your phone number.
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which services you received at the salon.
* **Service 1** (`service1`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 1 you received at the salon.
* **Service 2** (`service2`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 2 you received at the salon.
* **Service 3** (`service3`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 3 you received at the salon.
* **Service 4** (`service4`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 4 you received at the salon.
* **Service 5** (`service5`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 5 you received at the salon.
* **Service 6** (`service6`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 6 you received at the salon.
* **Service 7** (`service7`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 7 you received at the salon.
* **Service 8** (`service8`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select which service 8 you received at the salon.
