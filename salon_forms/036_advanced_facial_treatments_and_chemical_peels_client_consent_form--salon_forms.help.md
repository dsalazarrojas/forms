# Advanced Facial Treatments And Chemical Peels Client Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain client consent for advanced facial treatments and chemical peels. It gathers information about the client's name, contact phone number, treatment selection, allergies and sensitivities, medications, review of contraindications, expected results and risks, and aftercare instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2.  Enter your contact phone number for appointment confirmation.
3.  Specify the treatment or peel type you have selected.
4.  List any known allergies or skin sensitivities.
5.  List any current medications (topical or oral).
6.  Confirm if you have reviewed the contraindications.
7.  Confirm if the expected results and risks have been explained to you.
8.  Confirm if you understand and will follow the aftercare instructions.
9.  Sign your name as a digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: text
*   **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for appointment confirmation.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: text
*   **Treatment selected**: Specify the advanced facial treatment or chemical peel type you have chosen.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: text
*   **Allergies and skin sensitivities**: List any known allergies or skin sensitivities.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: text
*   **Current medications**: List any current topical or oral medications.
    *   Required: False
    *   Type: text
*   **Contrainications reviewed**: Confirm if you have reviewed the contraindications with your provider.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: select_one
*   **Expected results explained**: Confirm if you have been explained the expected results and risks.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: select_one
*   **Aftercare instructions acknowledged**: Confirm if you will follow the aftercare instructions provided.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: select_one
*   **Client signature name**: Enter your name as a digital signature.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: text
*   **Date of consent**: Date of signing this form.
    *   Required: Yes
    *   Type: date
