# makeup_pre_procedure_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information for a makeup-related procedure. It includes fields for first name, last name, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number. 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your correct email address to receive any follow-up communication related to your procedure.
- If you need to update your information, please inform the staff at your next visit.
