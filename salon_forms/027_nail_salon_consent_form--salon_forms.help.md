# Nail Salon Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nail Salon Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from clients visiting a nail salon. This includes contact details, treatment preferences, and consent for the treatment process. The form ensures that clients are informed about the treatment and any potential risks or side effects.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by filling in the required fields.
2.  Begin with the **Client Info** section on Page 1.
3.  Enter your name, contact information, and any other requested details.
4.  Proceed to the **Treatment Details** section.
5.  Provide your preferred treatment details or any specific requirements.
6.  In the **Treatment Tips** section, you can share any important information about your treatment, such as allergies or sensitivities.
7.  Move on to the **Contact Info** section.
8.  Enter the necessary contact information, such as your name, number, and email.
9.  In the **Contact Preferences** section, you can specify your preferred contact method (e-mail, phone, or in-person).
10. Lastly, sign your name in the **Client Signature** field to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info (salon_info)**: This is the first section where you'll enter your personal details. Please fill in your name and contact information accurately.
* **Treatment Details (treatment_details)**: Here, you can provide any additional treatment details or requirements you may have.
* **Treatment Tips (treatment_tips)**: Share any allergies, sensitivities, or important information about your treatment.
* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Preferences (contact_preferences)**: Specify your preferred contact method (e-mail, phone, or in-person).
* **Client Signature (client_signature)**: Sign your name to indicate your consent for the treatment process.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete in filling out the form to ensure a smooth and informed treatment process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your salon staff for assistance.
* Make sure to review your filled-in form before submitting it to the salon for review.
