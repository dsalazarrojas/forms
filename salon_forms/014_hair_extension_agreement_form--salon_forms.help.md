# Hair Extension Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Extension Agreement Form is a document designed to collect information from clients who are interested in hair extension services at the salon. This form helps the salon staff to prepare for the appointment and provide the best possible service to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the fourth field.
5. Choose the **Service type** you are interested in from the drop-down menu.
6. Select a **Service date** for your appointment.
7. Choose a **Service time** for your appointment.
8. Specify the **Service duration** in hours.
9. Describe the **Hair type** you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Service type**: Choose the type of hair service you are interested in, such as extensions, styling, or maintenance.
* **Service date**: Select a date for your hair service appointment.
* **Service time**: Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Service duration**: Specify the duration of your service in hours.
* **Hair type**: Describe the type of hair service you are interested in, such as color, trim, or style.
