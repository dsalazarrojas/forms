# Skin Care Acknowledgement And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain client acknowledgement and consent for skin care treatments provided at the salon.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately, including the client's name, date, and any relevant treatment information.
- Select the correct treatment from the provided list.
- Sign and date the form to indicate that you have read and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Care Acknowledgement And Consent Form (1)**: This is the title of the form, which should be filled out by the client.
* **Acknowledgement Date (2)**: This field should be completed with the date the client is acknowledging the information.
* **Salon Name (3)**: This field is where the salon name is entered.
* **Treatment Name (4)**: This field is where the client selects the specific treatment they are receiving.
* **Treatment Procedure Description (5)**: This field is where the client can provide any additional information or comments about the treatment.
* **Client Name (6)**: This field is where the client enters their name.
* **Client Signature (7)**: This field is where the client signs to confirm that they have read and understand the information provided. Please select one of the provided options for the client's signature.
* **Salon Signature (8)**: This field is where the salon representative signs to confirm that the client has received and understood the information provided. Please select one of the provided options for the salon signature.
* **Treatment Area (9)**: This field is where the client can enter any relevant information about the treatment area.
* **Allergy Information (10)**: This field is where the client can enter any allergy information related to the treatment.
* **Medical Information (11)**: This field is where the client can enter any medical information related to the treatment.
* **Consent (12)**: This field is where the client selects their level of understanding of the information provided. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Signature Date (13)**: This field is where the client enters the date they signed the form.
* **Notes (14)**: This field is where the client or salon representative can enter any additional comments or notes about the treatment.
* **Form Completion (15)**: This field is where the client or salon representative can enter any information about the form completion status.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before signing and dating it.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask a salon representative for clarification.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
