# Covid 19 Salon Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your recent travel history, contact with individuals with COVID-19, and the type of services you wish to receive at the salon. This information helps us ensure your safety and provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information:
    *   Enter your name.
    *   Provide your phone number.
    *   Enter your email address.
2.  Answer our questions:
    *   Indicate if you have traveled recently.
    *   Tell us if you have visited a high-risk location.
    *   Answer if you have been in close contact with someone.
    *   Answer if you have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19 symptoms.
    *   Answer if you have been in contact with someone with COVID-19.
    *   Answer if you have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19 symptoms.
    *   Answer if you have been in contact with someone with COVID-19.
3.  Select the salon service(s) you wish to receive:
    *   Choose one or more of the following services: Haircut, Color, Chemical Service, or Waxing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
*   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
*   **Have you traveled recently?**: Check "Yes" if you have traveled internationally or domestically in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you visited a high-risk location?**: Check "Yes" if you have visited a high-risk location in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you been in close contact with someone?**: Check "Yes" if you have been in close contact with someone in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you been in close contact with someone with COVID-19 symptoms?**: Check "Yes" if you have been in close contact with someone who has COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you been in contact with someone with COVID-19?**: Check "Yes" if you have been in contact with someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you been in contact with someone with COVID-19?**: This is a duplicate question, please see "Have you been in contact with someone with COVID-19?" above.
*   **Have you been in close contact with someone with COVID-19 symptoms?**: This is a duplicate question, please see "Have you been in close contact with someone with COVID-19 symptoms?" above.
*   **Have you been in contact with someone with COVID-19?**: This is a duplicate question, please see "Have you been in contact with someone with COVID-19?" above.
*   **Have you been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19?**: Check "Yes" if you have been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Have you been in contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19?**: Check "Yes" if you have been in contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days. Check "No" if you have not.
*   **Service Type**: Check one or more of the following services you wish to receive:
    +   Haircut
    +   Color
    +   Chemical Service
    +   Waxing
*   **Recent Travel History 2**: If you have traveled recently, please enter the date of your return or arrival.

## Tips
*   Please answer all questions truthfully to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.
*   We will use the information collected on this form to ensure your safety and provide you with the best possible experience.
