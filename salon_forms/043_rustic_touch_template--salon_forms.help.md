# Rustic Touch Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rustic Touch Template form is designed to collect salonn-related information from customers. This form serves as a tool for salonn services, such as appointment scheduling or product orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow the instructions below for each field.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Review and submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salonn Form Data** (`salonn_form_data`, `text`, optional): Enter any salonn-related data that you'd like to provide about your experience or preferences.
* **Salonn Date** (`salonn_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are interested in booking an appointment or would like to schedule a service.
* **Salonn Time** (`salonn_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you would prefer for your appointment or service.
* **Salonn Phone** (`salonn_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your appointment or service.
* **Salonn Email** (`salonn_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive communications about your appointment or service.
* **Salonn Notes** (`salonn_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or information about your experience or preferences.
* **Salonn Notes 2** (`salonn_notes_2`, `note`, optional): Add any further notes or information about your experience or preferences.
* **Salonn Notes 3** (`salonn_notes_3`, `note`, optional): Add any additional details or notes about your experience or preferences.

## Tips

- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Review and confirm the details entered in the form before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the salon staff directly.

Note: The form fields are labeled with "Salonn" as it appears to be a prefix used consistently throughout the form. If you have a different understanding or context for this prefix, please adjust the explanation accordingly.
