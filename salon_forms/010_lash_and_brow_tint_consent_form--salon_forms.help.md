# Lash And Brow Tint Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for clients undergoing lash and brow tinting services. It ensures that clients understand the process and risks involved with the treatment, as well as any allergies or medical conditions that may impact their safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm you have read and understood the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lash and Brow Tint Consent Form** (`title`, required/optional): This is the title of the form. You do not need to answer this question.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the provided options to indicate your understanding of the service.
	+ Client understands that the service will involve the administration of tint to their lashes and/or brows.
	+ Client understands that the client will be provided with a list of the ingredients and their effects and that they can choose not to use any products or chemicals that contain the ingredient(s) that they are allergic or sensitive to.
	+ Client understands that the service will involve chemical processing and that the client will be advised not to touch, rub or manipulate their lashes and brows for 48 hours after treatment.
* **Ingredients** (`ingredients`, text, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, list the ingredients you are allergic or sensitive to.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`, text, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please provide more information about them.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your safety, please provide more information.
* **Previous Allergies** (`previous_allergies`, text, optional): If you have had any previous allergies or reactions to treatments, please provide more information.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): If you are currently taking any medications, please list them.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign and date the form to confirm you have read and understood the information.
* **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`, note, required/optional): The technician will sign and date the form to confirm they have provided you with the necessary information and that you have read and understood it.

## Tips
- Please be honest about your allergies and medical history to ensure your safety.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your technician for clarification.
- Make sure to sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the information.
