# permanent_makeup_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help permanent makeup clients provide information about their desired services and medical history, as well as their skin tone and allergies, to ensure safe and effective treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your category from the provided options.
2. Briefly describe the services you are interested in.
3. Describe the procedure you are interested in.
4. Share your medical history.
5. Choose your skin tone.
6. List any medical conditions that may affect your skin.
7. If applicable, list any allergies you have.
8. Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
9. Check if you have obtained medical clearance, if not, you will be required to sign a waiver.
10. Finally, provide your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client's Category**: Please select one of the provided options that best describes your interest.
  - Yes: You are interested in permanent makeup services.
  - No: You are not interested in permanent makeup services.

* **Permanent Makeup Title**: Briefly describe the services you are interested in, for example, "Eyeliner, Brows, or Lip Blush".

* **Description of Services**: Describe the services you are interested in, for example, "I want to enhance my eyebrows, eyeliner, and lip line."

* **Description of Procedure**: Describe the procedure you are interested in, for example, "I want to get a lip liner procedure to correct uneven brows."

* **Client's Medical History**: Please list any medical conditions or health issues that you are aware of, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.

* **Skin Tone**: Choose your skin tone from the provided options:
  - Light: You have light skin.
  - Medium: You have medium skin.
  - Dark: You have dark skin.

* **Skin Conditions**: Please list any skin conditions that may affect your skin tone, such as vitiligo or psoriasis.

* **Allergies**: Please list any allergies you have, for example, "I am allergic to shellfish, latex, or nickel."

* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address, for follow-up purposes.

* **Medical Clearance**: If you have medical clearance, select "Yes". If not, a waiver will be provided for your signature.
  - Yes: You have obtained medical clearance.
  - No: You do not have medical clearance and need to sign a waiver.

* **Consent and Waiver**: If you do not have medical clearance, a waiver will be provided for your electronic signature.
  - Please read and sign the waiver, indicating that you understand the risks and procedures involved in the treatment.

* **Signature**: Please enter your electronic signature for final confirmation.
