# Color Street Current Inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about the current stock of Color Street nail polish shades. This includes dates of inventory counts, collection counts, and notes about low and out-of-stock items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the inventory count.
2. Enter the name of the person conducting the inventory count.
3. Enter the storage location where the inventory is stored.
4. Enter the counts of each collection (Classic, Glitter, Matte, and Shimmer).
5. Identify popular shades (top 2) and enter their names and quantities.
6. List items that need to be re-stocked.
7. List items that are completely out of stock.
8. Enter the estimated retail value of the current inventory.
9. Enter any additional notes or observations about the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inventory Date** (`inventory_date`, date, required): Date when the inventory is being counted.
* **Stylist Name** (`stylist_name`, text, required): Name of the person conducting the inventory count.
* **Storage Location** (`location`, text, required): Where the inventory is stored.
* **Classic Collection Count** (`classic_collection_count`, number, required): Number of Classic Collection strips in stock.
* **Glitter Collection Count** (`glitter_collection_count`, number, required): Number of Glitter Collection strips in stock.
* **Matte Collection Count** (`matte_collection_count`, number, required): Number of Matte Collection strips in stock.
* **Shimmer Collection Count** (`shimmer_collection_count`, number, required): Number of Shimmer Collection strips in stock.
* **Popular Shade 1 Name** (`popular_shade_1`, text, optional): Name of the most popular shade.
* **Popular Shade 1 Quantity** (`popular_shade_1_quantity`, number, optional): Current stock quantity.
* **Popular Shade 2 Name** (`popular_shade_2`, text, optional): Name of the second most popular shade.
* **Popular Shade 2 Quantity** (`popular_shade_2_quantity`, number, optional): Current stock quantity.
* **Items Needing Restock** (`low_stock_items`, text, required): List of shades that are running low.
* **Out of Stock Items** (`out_of_stock_items`, text, required): List of shades that are completely out of stock.
* **Total Inventory Value** (`total_inventory_value`, number, optional): Estimated retail value of current inventory.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Any other inventory observations.
