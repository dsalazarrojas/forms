# Beauty Consultation And Health Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their health history, skin type, and beauty goals. It helps us understand their current medical conditions, previous treatments, and preferred treatments, ensuring that our beauty professionals can provide the best possible services and advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out your name, date of birth, email, and phone number in the client information section.
* Provide your current medical conditions, medications, and known allergies in the health history section.
* Answer the question about previous adverse reactions to beauty products and describe the details.
* Describe your skin type and any current skin conditions.
* Mention your primary skin concerns and any previous treatments you have received.
* Finally, provide information about your treatment goals and preferred treatment types.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it is written on your government-issued ID.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Current Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Mention any current medical conditions you are experiencing.
- **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): Include allergies, including environmental, food, and product allergies.
- **Skin Product Allergies** (`skin_allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies related to skin products.
- **Previous Reactions** (`previous_reactions`, `select_one`, required): Check if you have had previous adverse reactions to beauty products.
- **Describe Previous Reactions** (`reaction_details`, `text`, optional): Describe the details of your previous reactions.
- **Previous Surgeries or Procedures** (`surgeries`, `text`, optional): Mention any recent surgeries or procedures within the last 3 months.
- **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your skin type as dry, normal, oily, combination, or sensitive.
- **Primary Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, `text`, required): Identify your primary skin concerns, such as acne, aging, or sensitivity.
- **Current Skin Conditions** (`skin_conditions_current`, `text`, optional): List any current skin conditions you are experiencing.
- **Treatment Goals** (`treatment_goals`, `text`, required): Specify what you would like to achieve through the treatment.
- **Previous Salon or Spa Treatments** (`previous_treatments`, `text`, optional): Mention any previous salon or spa treatments you have received.
- **Preferred Treatment Types** (`preferred_treatments`, `text`, optional): List your preferred treatment types.
- **Consent and Acknowledgment** (`consent_header`, `note`, optional): Read and agree to our terms of service.
- **Information Accuracy** (`information_accuracy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the above information is accurate.
- **Consent to Treatment** (`consent_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the recommended treatments.
- **Privacy Agreement** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to our privacy and data handling terms.

## Tips
Please be honest and detailed in your responses to help us provide the best possible service. If you need help or clarification on any question, feel free to ask.
