# custom_nail_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The custom_nail_order_form is a one-page form used by customers to place custom nail orders in a salon setting. This form helps the salon staff create a unique nail design tailored to each customer's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the desired color name for your nails in the "Color Name" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Customer Name" field.
3. Indicate whether you want your nails to be of a specific type in the "Nail Type" field.
4. Choose your preferred nail color in the "Nail Color" field.
5. Select the size of your nails in the "Nail Size" field.
6. Enter the number of nails you'd like to have the custom design applied to in the "Quantity" field.
7. Set the price for your custom nail design in the "Price" field.
8. If you have any additional information or notes for the salon staff, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Color Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the color you'd like to use for your nails. This could be a name of a specific color or a custom color.
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or records. This is a required field to ensure your order is accurately linked to your account.
* **Nail Type** (select multiple, required): Indicate whether you'd like your nails to have a specific type. Select all applicable options if you'd like to have multiple types.
* **Nail Color** (select multiple, required): Choose your preferred nail color from the available options.
* **Nail Size** (select one, required): Select the size of your nails from the available options.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the number of nails you'd like to have the custom design applied to.
* **Price** (number, required): Set the price for your custom nail design.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes you'd like to provide to the salon staff for a more accurate design.
