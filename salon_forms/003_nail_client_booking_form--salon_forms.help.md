# Nail Client Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from clients for their nail services appointment bookings.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please provide your First Name and Last Name as per your identity.
*   Enter your Email and Phone number for our salon to contact you.
*   Select a convenient Appointment Time for your visit, or choose to leave it open if you don't have a specific time in mind.
*   Choose the type of service you are interested in from the options provided (e.g., Nail Trim, Toenail Care, Manicure).
*   If you have any additional comments or requests, type them in the Note field.
*   Specify the estimated Service Duration required for the service.
*   Select the equipment or tool you would like us to use during the service from the options provided.
*   Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
*   **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, optional): Select a time that suits you for your appointment, or leave it open if you're flexible.
*   **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Nail Trim, Toenail Care, Manicure).
*   **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select a date for your appointment that suits you.
*   **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Type any additional comments or requests for your appointment.
*   **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, `number`, required): Estimate the time needed for the service.
*   **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool or equipment you prefer for your service.
*   **Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
