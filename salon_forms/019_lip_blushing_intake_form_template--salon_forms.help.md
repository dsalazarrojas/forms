# Lip Blushing Intake Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lip Blushing Intake Form Template is a comprehensive form designed to collect necessary information from clients before undergoing a lip flushing treatment. This form helps treatment providers to gather client details, medical history, treatment information, and consent, ensuring a thorough understanding of the client's situation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out each section carefully**: Please complete each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. **Provide clear and concise answers**: Use simple language and avoid unnecessary details.
3. **Review and submit the form**: Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Enter your personal and contact information, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, including allergies, conditions, or surgeries.
* **Lip Blushing Treatment**: Enter the details of your lip flushing treatment, including the date and location of the treatment.
* **Consent**: Check this box to indicate that you understand and agree to the treatment process.
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional information that may be helpful for your treatment provider to know.
* **Client Agreement**: Check this box to confirm that you agree to the treatment terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Be truthful and accurate in your answers.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask your treatment provider for clarification.
* Take a few minutes to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
