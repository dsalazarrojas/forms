# Skincare Client Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect client information and health profile for providing personalized skincare services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) as accurately as possible. This includes:
	* Client Name
	* Contact Number
	* Date of Birth
	* Medical License Number
	* Medical Board License Status
2. You may choose to fill in non-required fields if you feel necessary.
3. Select the correct option for Medical Board License from the dropdown menu.
4. Fill in the Emergency Contact details if you have any.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, *): Provide your full name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, *): Provide your contact number (e.g. phone number or email).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you wish to be contacted via email.
* **Address** (`address`, text, *): Provide your home address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, *): Provide your date of birth in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, note, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name of the person to contact in case of emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Provide the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose how you are related to the emergency contact (Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend, Spouse, Other).
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, select_one, optional): Choose your medical insurance provider (Blue Cross, United Health, Aetna, Cigna, Humana, Other).
* **Medical Insurance Plan** (`medical_insurance_plan`, text, optional): If you have any medical insurance, provide the plan details.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): If you have the address of the emergency contact, please provide it.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please describe them.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical history, please describe them.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them.
* **Medical Conditions History** (`medical_conditions_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions history, please describe them.
* **Medical Test Results** (`medical_test_results`, text, optional): If you have any medical test results, please provide them.
* **Medical Treatments** (`medical_treatments`, text, optional): If you have any medical treatments, please describe them.
* **Medical Procedures** (`medical_procedures`, text, optional): If you have any medical procedures, please describe them.
* **Medical Specialties** (`medical_specialties`, text, optional): If you have any medical specialties, please describe them.
* **Medical License Info** (`medical_license_info`, text, optional): If you have any medical license info, please provide it.
* **Medical Board Certifications** (`medical_board_certifications`, text, optional): If you have any medical board certifications, please describe them.
* **Medical Board License** (`medical_board_license`, select_one, *): Choose the status of your medical board license (Active, Inactive, Expired).
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, *): Provide your medical license number.
