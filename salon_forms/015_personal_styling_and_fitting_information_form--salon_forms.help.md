# Personal Styling And Fitting Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal styling and fitting information from clients. Please fill out this form to provide our stylists and tailors with the necessary details to create a custom fit just for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. **Contact Number**: If you have a contact number where you can be reached, please enter it so we can contact you with any questions or concerns.
3. **Email**: If you have an email address where you can be reached, please enter it so we can send you updates on your order and any future promotions.
4. **Measurement Unit**: Please select the unit of measurement you prefer to use for your body measurements (e.g., inches, centimeters, etc.).
5. **Body Measurement Location**: Please select the body locations where you would like to be measured (e.g., chest, waist, bust, etc.).
6. **Height**: Enter your height in your preferred unit of measurement.
7. **Weight**: Enter your weight in your preferred unit of measurement.
8. **Body Type**: Please select the general shape of your body type (e.g., hourglass, rectangular, etc.).
9. **Fit Preference**: Please select the fit you prefer for your clothing (e.g., relaxed, fitted, etc.).
10. **Style Preference**: Please select the style you prefer for your clothing (e.g., formal, casual, etc.).
11. **Occasion**: Please select the occasion for which you are looking for clothing (e.g., formal, informal, etc.).
12. **Clothing Type**: Please select the type of clothing you are looking for (e.g., top, bottom, both).
13. **Clothing Size**: Please select your clothing size (e.g., plus, regular, junior, etc.).
14. **Fit Adjustment**: If you need any adjustments made to your clothing fit, please select the size of the adjustment (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
15. **Style Adjustment**: If you need any style adjustments made to your clothing, please select the size of the adjustment (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
16. **Color**: Enter any specific colors you prefer for your clothing.
17. **Fabric Type**: Enter any specific fabric types you prefer for your clothing.
18. **Style Influence**: Enter any style influences you have (e.g., celebrity, designer, etc.).
19. **Favorite Color**: Enter any favorite colors you have.
20. **Hair Color**: Enter your hair color.
21. **Skin Tone**: Enter your skin tone.
22. **Face Shape**: Enter your face shape.
23. **Neck Size**: Enter your neck size.
24. **Sleeve Length**: Select the length of your sleeve (e.g., short, long, etc.).
25. **Notes**: Please enter any additional notes or comments you have for our stylists and tailors.
