# Hairdressers Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow clients to request appointments at a hairdresser's salon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, text): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to let us know about your appointment request.
2. **Service** (`services_form`, select_one): Choose the service you would like to have during your appointment. Options include Service 1, Service 2, and Service 3.
3. **Stylist** (`stylist_form`, select_one): Select your preferred stylist. Options include Stylist 1, Stylist 2, and Stylist 3.
4. **Date** (`date_form`, date): Choose a date for your appointment.
5. **Time** (`time_form`, time): Select a time for your appointment.
6. **Service** (`service_form`, select_multiple): If you are requesting multiple services, check the boxes for each one you would like to have during your appointment. You can select up to three services.
7. **Appointment** (`appointment_form`, text): If you have any special requests or instructions for your appointment, type them here.
8. **Message** (`message_form`, note): If you have any further comments or questions, you can type them here.
9. **Email** (`email_form`, email): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your appointment.
10. **Phone** (`phone_form`, text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (email and phone).
- If you selected multiple services, make sure to include all of them.
- If you have any special requests, type them in the "Appointment" field.
- Double-check that your email and phone number are correct before submitting the form.
