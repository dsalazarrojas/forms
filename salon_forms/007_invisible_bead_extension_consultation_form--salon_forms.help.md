# invisible_bead_extension_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The invisible_bead_extension_consultation_form is a consultation form used to gather information from clients about their hair extension needs. This form helps the salon's staff to understand the client's preferences and make informed decisions about their extensions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, such as name, phone number, and email address. Please note that these fields are optional.
2. Answer the questions regarding the client's hair history, including their hair color and length.
3. Select the desired hair extension style and color.
4. Write any additional comments that might be helpful for the client's hair extension needs.
5. Choose if the client would like to include their photo for further consultation.
6. Suggest a staff member to assist the client with their hair extension needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Salon Form):
	+ This field is for the client's basic contact information.
* **Hair History** (Salon Form 2):
	+ Describe the client's hair history, including their current hair color and length.
* **Hair Color** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the client's current hair color.
* **Hair Length** (Select One):
	+ Select the client's current hair length.
* **Extension Style** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose the desired hair extension style for the client.
* **Extension Length**:
	+ Enter the desired length of the hair extension.
* **Extension Color** (Select One):
	+ Select the desired hair extension color.
* **Client Comments** (Text):
	+ Write any additional comments that might be helpful for the client's hair extension needs.
* **Client Image** (Select One):
	+ Choose if the client would like to include their photo for further consultation.
* **Suggested Staff Member** (Select One):
	+ Suggest a staff member to assist the client with their hair extension needs.
* **Client Phone Number**:
	+ Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email**:
	+ Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Address**:
	+ Enter the client's address.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all the required fields and avoid duplicating information. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the salon staff for assistance.
