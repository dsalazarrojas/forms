# Waxing Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waxing Waiver Form is a document intended to collect essential information from clients about their waxing experience. This form is used to ensure a safe and pleasant experience for both the client and the salon staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Provide Client Information**: Enter your name and contact details in the `client_info` field.
2.  **Describe Your Waxing Experience**: In the `service_info` field, provide a brief description of your previous waxing experience, including any relevant details such as the type of waxing services you've received, any issues you've encountered, and what services you'd like to receive this time.
3.  **Sign the Waiver**: Read and agree to the terms of the waiver in the `service_waiver` field.
4.  **E-Sign the Form**: Enter your digital signature in the `e_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **client_info** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name and contact details. Please provide your full name, phone number, and email address.
*   **service_info** (`text`, required/optional): Describe your previous waxing experience, including the type of waxing services you've received, any issues you've encountered, and what services you'd like to receive this time.
*   **service_waiver** (`note`, required/true): This field is the waiver that you need to read and agree to. Please indicate your understanding and acceptance of the waiver terms.
*   **e_signature** (`text`, required/true): Enter your digital signature to confirm that you've completed this form and agree to the waiver terms.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review and understand the waiver terms before signing.
*   Ensure that your contact details are accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you have any concerns or questions about the form, do not hesitate to ask the salon staff for assistance.
