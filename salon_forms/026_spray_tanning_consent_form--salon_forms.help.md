# Spray Tanning Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spray Tanning Consent Form is a document used to gather information from clients before undergoing a spray tanning session. This form informs clients of potential side effects and risks and obtains their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your contact information: Enter your name and email address to contact you about any questions or concerns.
2. Acknowledge potential side effects: Review the list of potential side effects and risks associated with spray tanning, and check the box if you are aware and willing to accept these risks.
3. Choose your consent: Select the option that best reflects your understanding and agreement to undergo the spray tanning session.
4. Review your session details: Enter your session date and any relevant comments about your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Enter your name and email address where the staff can contact you about your appointment.
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your session records.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address where the staff can contact you about your appointment.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number where the staff can contact you about your appointment.
* **Potential Side Effects**: Review the list of potential side effects and risks associated with spray tanning, and check the box if you are aware and willing to accept these risks.
* **Consent**: Select the option that best reflects your understanding and agreement to undergo the spray tanning session. Note: This field is required.
* **Review**: Enter the date and time of your session for reference.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Spray Tanner ID**: Enter the ID number of the staff member performing the spray tanning session.
* **Client ID**: Enter your internal ID number for our records.
* **Session ID**: Enter the ID number of your session.
* **Consent Timestamp**: This field is automatically generated and is not editable.
* **Tanning Product**: Select the product used for your session. If none were used, select "None".
* **Tanning Product Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the product used.
* **Client Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your session.
* **Staff Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your session.
* **Tanning Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your session.
* **Product Used**: Select the product(s) used for your session.
* **Reported**: Select any reported side effects or issues during your session.
* **Tanning Machine**: Select the machine used for your session.
* **Tanning Solution**: Select the solution used for your session.
* **Tanning Machine Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the machine used.
* **Tanning Solution Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the solution used.
* **Client Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your session. Note: This field is required for client feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to thoroughly review all fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are aware of and willing to accept the potential side effects and risks associated with spray tanning.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the staff member performing the session or the salon administration.
