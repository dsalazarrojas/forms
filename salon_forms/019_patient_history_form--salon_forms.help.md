# Patient History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient History Form is designed to collect comprehensive information about a patient's medical history. This form is meant to be completed by healthcare professionals to document a patient's background, including their demographics, medical conditions, medications, and relevant medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form carefully to understand what information is being asked and why.
2. Complete each page of the form in order, using the text boxes provided to record the patient's answers.
3. Be accurate and thorough in your responses, as this information will be used to inform the patient's medical care.
4. If you're unsure about any question or have a concern, please consult with the patient or consult relevant medical resources.
