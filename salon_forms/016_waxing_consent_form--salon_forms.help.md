# Waxing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a waxing service at a beauty salon. It typically includes the customer's name, email, phone number, and the salon's information such as name, address, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) in the corresponding fields.
2. Fill in the details of the beauty salon where you are receiving the service (name, address, and contact information).
3. Answer the Pros and Cons section, if applicable.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name, which will be used to associate you with your appointment and any communication with the salon.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure that the salon can contact you for any updates or questions.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number so that the salon can reach out to you if needed.
* **Saloon Name** (`saloon_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the beauty salon where you are receiving the service.
* **Saloon Address** (`saloon_address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the beauty salon.
* **Saloon Phone** (`saloon_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the beauty salon.
* **Saloon Email** (`saloon_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the beauty salon.
* **Pros and Cons** (`pros_cons`, text, optional): If you have any comments or concerns about the service, please share them here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign this form to confirm your consent for the waxing service.

Please note that some fields are optional, but it's recommended to fill them in for a smooth and efficient communication with the salon.
