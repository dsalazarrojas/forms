# Hair History Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a person's hair history, including past treatments, health, concerns, medical conditions, medical tests, hair dye use, hair styling products, relaxers, and hair extensions. This information will help our team better understand your hair journey and provide more personalized advice and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each field in the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing information about your hair history.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Past Treatments
* **Past Treatments** (`past_treatments`, text, optional): Describe any hair treatments you have received in the past, such as coloring, cutting, or straightening.

### Hair Health
* **Hair Health** (`hair_health`, text, optional): Report any issues or conditions that affect your hair's overall health, such as dandruff, itchiness, or dryness.

### Concerns
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, text, optional): Share any concerns you have about your hair, such as thinning, breakage, or color fading.

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions that may affect your hair, such as thyroid issues, hormonal imbalances, or autoimmune disorders.

### Medical Tests
* **Medical Tests** (`medical_tests`, text, optional): Report any recent medical tests related to your hair, such as blood tests or scalp examinations.

### Hair Dye
* **Hair Dye** (`hair_dye`, select_one, optional): Do you currently use hair dye?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have used hair dye in the past month.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not used hair dye in the past month.

### Hair Styling Products
* **Hair Styling Products** (`hair_styling_products`, select_multiple, optional): Do you currently use any hair styling products?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you use hair products such as hairspray, gel, or mousse.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not use hair products.

### Relaxers
* **Relaxers** (`relaxers`, select_multiple, optional): Do you currently use relaxers?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you use relaxers to straighten your hair.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not use relaxers.

### Hair Extensions
* **Hair Extensions** (`hair_extensions`, select_multiple, optional): Do you currently use hair extensions?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have added hair extensions to enhance your hair.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not use hair extensions.
