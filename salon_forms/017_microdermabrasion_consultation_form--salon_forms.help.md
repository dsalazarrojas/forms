# Microdermabrasion Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a client's medical background, history, and preferences to provide safe and effective microdermabrasion treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing your personal details as requested.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and if you have any medical concerns or conditions, please list them under the "Medical History" field.
3. If you have allergies, please specify them under the "Allergies" field.
4. Choose the correct answer for each question regarding microdermabrasion treatment, area, and frequency.
5. Enter your medical insurance information under the "Medical Insurance" field, if applicable.
6. Choose your preferred payment method and status under "Payment Method" and "Payment Status" fields respectively.
7. Fill out the "Client Signature" and "Staff Signature" fields with your name after completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): List any known medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required): Specify any allergies you have, e.g., latex, penicillin, etc.
* **Treatment** (`microdermabrasion_treatment`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are interested in microdermabrasion treatment.
* **Area** (`microdermabrasion_area`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas of your body you are interested in for the treatment.
* **Frequency** (`microdermabrasion_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how frequently you would like to receive the treatment.
* **Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required): Mention any underlying medical conditions you have.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or concerns you have.
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, `text`, required): Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, `text`, required): Specify your medical school name.
* **Medical Certificate** (`medical_certification`, `text`, required): Upload your medical certificate.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required): Provide your medical insurance information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred payment method, e.g., bank transfer, credit card, etc.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your payment status, e.g., active, inactive, etc.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as proof of your consent.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as staff verification.

Please ensure you complete the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
