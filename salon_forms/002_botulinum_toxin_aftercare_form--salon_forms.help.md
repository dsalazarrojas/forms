# Botulinum Toxin Aftercare Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to ensure that clients understand and commit to following post-treatment care instructions to achieve optimal results and minimize complications from botulinum toxin injections.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each field carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Answer all required fields as they are marked.
3. Fill out the form completely before submitting it to your provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Treatment Date** (`treatment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your botulinum toxin injection.
* **-- Aftercare Instructions --** (`section_header_instructions`, `note`, required): Review and acknowledge the post-treatment care instructions provided.
* **Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 hours** (`avoid_exercise`, `select_one`, required): Select "Understood" to confirm you will avoid strenuous exercise for the recommended 24 hours.
* **Avoid alcohol and blood thinners for 24 hours** (`avoid_alcohol`, `select_one`, required): Select "Understood" to confirm you will avoid alcohol and blood thinners for the recommended 24 hours.
* **Do not touch or massage treated areas** (`avoid_touching`, `select_one`, required): Select "Understood" to confirm you will not touch or massage treated areas for the recommended 24 hours.
* **Stay upright for at least 4 hours** (`upright_position`, `select_one`, required): Select "Understood" to confirm you will stay upright for at least 4 hours.
* **Minor swelling or redness expected** (`minor_swelling`, `select_one`, required): Select "Acknowledged" to confirm you understand that minor swelling or redness is expected.
* **Slight bruising may occur** (`bruising_possible`, `select_one`, required): Select "Acknowledged" to confirm you understand that slight bruising may occur.
* **Full results appear within** (`results_timeline`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided timeframes for when you expect full results to appear.
* **Use gentle moisturizer regularly** (`moisturizer_use`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options regarding your use of moisturizer.
* **Apply SPF 30+ sunscreen daily** (`sunscreen_use`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options regarding your sunscreen use.
* **Avoid harsh skincare products for 24 hours** (`avoid_products`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the provided options regarding your use of harsh skincare products.
* **Best Contact Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number for follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for aftercare reminders.
* **Recommended Follow-up Visit Date** (`follow_up_date`, `date`, optional): Select a date for your recommended follow-up visit, usually 2 weeks post-treatment.
* **Client understands aftercare instructions** (`client_signature`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree and understand" to confirm you have read and committed to following the post-treatment care instructions.

Note: Please answer all required fields. If you are unsure about any information, discuss with your provider before submitting this form.
