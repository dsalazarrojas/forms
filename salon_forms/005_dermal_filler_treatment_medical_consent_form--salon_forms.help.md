# Dermal Filler Treatment Medical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather medical consent and information from patients before undergoing dermal filler treatments. It will guide you through the necessary questions and fields to provide your consent and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Patient Information**: In the "Patient Information" section, provide your basic details such as your name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
2. **Check Medical History**: In the "Medical History" section, describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may impact your treatment.
3. **Confirm Consent**: In the "Consent to Treatment" section, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to undergoing the dermal filler treatment.
4. **Check Medical Consents**: In the "Medical Consents" section, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to specific medical procedures (e.g., sedation, numbing, etc.).
5. **List Medical Conditions**: In the "Medical Conditions" section, use the space provided to list any medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
6. **List Allergies**: In the "Allergies" section, list any allergies you may have.
7. **Provide Contact Information**: In the "Contact Information" section, provide your contact information so that we can get in touch with you before, during, and after your treatment.
8. **Provide Emergency Contact Information**: In the "Emergency Contact Information" section, provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (`patient_information`, text, required: false)**: This is your basic contact information that we will use to get in touch with you before, during, and after your treatment.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`, text, required: false)**: Please describe any medical conditions, allergies, or previous treatments that may impact your treatment.
* **Consent to Treatment (`consent_to_treatment`, select_one, required: false)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you understand and consent to undergoing the dermal filler treatment. Select "No" to indicate that you do not wish to proceed with treatment.
* **Medical Consents (`medical_consents`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select "Yes" for each medical procedure you consent to, such as sedation or numbing. Select "No" to indicate that you do not consent to a specific procedure.
* **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, note, required: false)**: Use this space to list any medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
* **Allergies (`allergies`, text, required: false)**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, required: false)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact Information (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false)**: Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
