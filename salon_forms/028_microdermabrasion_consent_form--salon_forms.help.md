# Microdermabrasion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microdermabrasion Consent Form is a document that requires a patient's consent before performing a microdermabrasion procedure. This form is used to ensure that the patient understands the treatment and its risks, and to obtain their consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (if applicable, as this field is not required).
4. Share your medical history in the "Medical History" field.
5. Confirm that you understand that the microdermabrasion treatment will be performed in the office of [salon_name] by signing and dating the "Signature" field.
6. Enter the date you are completing the form in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time you are completing the form in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter a phone number where you can be reached for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required):
	+ Describe any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you have.
* **I understand that the Microdermabrasion treatment will be performed in the office of [salon_name]** (`consent`, text, required):
	+ Confirm that you understand where the treatment will be performed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required):
	+ Sign your name to indicate your consent for the procedure.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you are completing the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time you are completing the form.
* **Procedure Description** (`procedure_description`, text, optional):
	+ Provide any additional information about the procedure you would like to include.
* **I consent to the procedure** (`client_consent`, select_one, required):
	+ Select "True" to confirm your consent for the procedure.
