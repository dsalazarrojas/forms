# Salon Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking appointments at the salon for existing clients. If you're a new client, please contact the salon directly to inquire about services and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields (Client Name and Client Phone).
2. Select a date and time for your appointment.
3. Choose the service(s) you would like to book.
4. Provide any additional message for the salon staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**
	+ <label>Client Name</label>
		- This is the name of the client booking the appointment.
* **Select Date**
	+ <label>Select Date</label>
		- Choose the date for your appointment. Please select a date that is available according to the salon's availability.
* **Select Time**
	+ <label>Select Time</label>
		- Choose the time slot for your appointment. Please select a time that is available according to the salon's availability.
* **Select Service**
	+ <label>Haircut</label>
	+ <label>Coloring</label>
	+ <label>Massage</label>
		- Choose one or more services from the list above to book with your appointment.
* **Client Name**
	+ <label>Client Name</label>
		- Enter the name of the person booking the appointment.
* **Client Email**
	+ <label>Client Email</label>
		- Enter the email address of the person booking the appointment.
* **Client Phone**
	+ <label>Client Phone</label>
		- Enter the phone number of the person booking the appointment.
* **Message**
	+ <label>Client Message</label>
		- Add any additional comments or messages for the salon staff.
* **Client Message**
	+ <label>Client Message</label>
		- Enter any additional comments or messages for the salon staff.
