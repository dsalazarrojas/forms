# Essential Oil And Wellness Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Essential Oil And Wellness Quiz is a form designed to collect information about your essential oil usage and preferences. This data will help us understand your interests and behaviors related to essential oils.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions as honestly as possible.
2. Select all applicable answers for select_multiple questions.
3. Choose the best option for select_one and select_multiple questions.
4. Enter your answers in the text fields.
5. Submit the form when you have completed all questions.
