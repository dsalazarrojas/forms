# Barbershop Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Barbershop Service Request form is designed for clients to submit their service requests efficiently. It will help the barbershop staff plan and prepare for your visit, ensuring a smooth experience for you. Please fill out this form accurately to help us get you into the chair faster.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the required fields with your information.
2.  Select your preferred services and add any additional services you'd like.
3.  If you have a scheduled appointment, indicate it in the "Do you have a scheduled appointment?" field.
4.  Choose your preferred barber or select "Next Available" for the most efficient experience.
5.  Provide your social media handle if you're willing to be tagged on our social media posts.
6.  Confirm your estimated wait time and photo consent.
7.  Finally, select your desired date and arrival time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.
*   **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`, required/optional): Enter your mobile number so we can text you when your barber is ready.
*   **Do you have a scheduled appointment?** (`walk_in_status`, required/optional): If you have a scheduled appointment, select "Yes - Scheduled for today".
*   **Haircut Services** (`main_service`, required/optional): Choose from a variety of haircut services, including Standard Haircut, Skin Fade, Buzz Cut, and more.
*   **Beard and Shave Services** (`beard_shave_service`, required/optional): Select from a range of beard and shave services, such as Beard Trim and Line-up, Full Beard Sculpting, and Hot Towel Shave.
*   **Service Add-ons** (`add_on_services`, required/optional): Choose from a list of additional services, such as Shampoo and Conditioning, Charcoal Face Mask, and more.
*   **Barber Preference** (`preference_header`, required/optional): Select whether you'd like a specific barber or prefer the next available.
*   **Name of Requested Barber** (`requested_barber_name`, required/optional): If you selected "Request Specific Barber," enter the name of the barber you prefer.
*   **Current Wait Time Acknowledgement** (`wait_time_ack`, required/optional): Confirm that you understand the estimated wait time is subject to change.
*   **Portfolio Consent** (`photo_consent_header`, required/optional): Understand that by checking this field, we may take photos of your finished cut for our social media.
*   **May we take a photo of your finished cut for our social media?** (`photo_consent`, required/optional): Choose whether to allow a photo opportunity.
*   **Social Media Handle (Instagram)** (`social_handle`, required/optional): Enter your social media handle, e.g., `@yourname`.
*   **Date** (`entry_date`, required/optional): Select the date for your appointment.
*   **Arrival Time** (`entry_time`, required/optional): Enter your expected arrival time.

Tips:

*   Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any special requests or instructions, please mention them in the "Notes" section.
*   Our barbershop staff will review and confirm your appointment upon receiving your form submission. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
