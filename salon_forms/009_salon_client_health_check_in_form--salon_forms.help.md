# Salon Client Health Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's health status, medical history, and other relevant details to ensure their safety and well-being during salon treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the spaces provided, and make sure to fill out all required fields (if applicable).
2. Use the space provided on each page to write your answer.
3. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Overall Health Status** (`overall_health_status`): Please describe your overall health status, including any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns you may have.

### 2. **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.

### 3. **Allergies** (`allergies`): List any allergies you have, including medication allergies, food allergies, or environmental allergies.

### 4. **Current Medications** (`current_medications`): If you are currently taking any medications or supplements, please list them here, along with their dosages and any relevant instructions or restrictions.

Note: Allergies and medical history can be described in brief and concise terms. If the answer requires more space or detail, please use additional space provided on the page.
