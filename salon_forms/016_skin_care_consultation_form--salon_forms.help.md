# skin_care_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a skin care consultation with a medical professional. It is intended for individuals who want to schedule a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about your appointment.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can reach you in case of any questions or concerns.
4. Select your Skin Type (Normal, Oily, Dry, Combination, Acne, Rosacea, or Other).
5. Choose your Skin Concerns (Pore Size, Pore Spacing, Fine Lines, Puffiness, Skin Discoloration, Acne, Rosacea, or Other).
6. If you have any Health Conditions, please list them.
7. If you are taking any Medications, please list them.
8. If you have any Medical Conditions, please list them.
9. If you have any Allergies, please list them.
10. Provide your Medical History.
11. In the Appointment Date field, choose a date and time for your consultation.
12. In the Follow-Up Appointment field, select whether you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment.
13. If you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment, provide a date and time in the Follow-Up Date field.
14. If you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment, provide the time in the Follow-Up Time field.
15. In the Follow-Up Notes field, provide any additional information or questions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Skin Type** (`Skin Type`, select_multiple, required): Choose your skin type (Normal, Oily, Dry, Combination, Acne, Rosacea, or Other).
* **Skin Concerns** (`Skin Concerns`, select_one, required): Choose your skin concerns (Pore Size, Pore Spacing, Fine Lines, Puffiness, Skin Discoloration, Acne, Rosacea, or Other).
* **Health Conditions** (`Health Conditions`, text, required): If you have any health conditions, list them here.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, text, required): If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, text, required): If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, required): If you have any allergies, list them here.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Provide your medical history.
* **Doctor Info** (`Doctor Info`, text, required): Provide any information about your doctor or medical professional you are consulting.
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, date, required): Choose a date and time for your consultation.
* **Appointment Time** (`Appointment Time`, time, required): Choose a time for your consultation.
* **Follow-Up Appointment** (`Follow-Up Appointment`, select_one, required): Select whether you would like to schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-Up Date** (`Follow-Up Date`, date, optional): If you selected to schedule a follow-up appointment, provide a date and time.
* **Follow-Up Time** (`Follow-Up Time`, time, optional): If you selected to schedule a follow-up appointment, provide the time.
* **Follow-Up Notes** (`Follow-Up Notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional information or questions you have for your follow-up appointment.
