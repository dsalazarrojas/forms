# Chemical Peel Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help an esthetician provide a personalized chemical peel consultation for a client. It asks for information about the client's skin type, medical history, and treatment goals to make informed decisions about the type of peel to recommend and the number of sessions required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Choose your skin type from the options provided.
3. Select your main skin concerns (e.g. fine lines and wrinkles, sun damage, etc.).
4. Describe your treatment goals and what you hope to achieve.
5. Answer questions about your medical history, including previous skin treatments, medical conditions, and allergies.
6. Answer questions about your skincare routine, including sunscreen use and sun exposure habits.
7. Answer questions about your history with Accutane and keloid scarring tendency.
8. Finally, answer questions about your current esthetician's recommendations and your readiness to proceed with treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Client Name, required): Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, required): Enter your date of birth to help the esthetician understand your skin type and age.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, required): Enter your contact number in case the esthetician needs to contact you.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, required): Enter your email address in case the esthetician needs to contact you.
* **Address** (Address, required): Enter your address to provide the esthetician with your location information.
* **Consultation Date** (Consultation Date, required): Enter the date of this consultation.
* **Skin Type** (Skin Type, required): Choose your skin type from the options: Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, or Sensitive.
* **Skin Concerns** (Skin Concerns, required): Select all of the main skin concerns you are experiencing (e.g. fine lines and wrinkles, sun damage, etc.).
* **Treatment Goals** (Treatment Goals, required): Describe what you hope to achieve with the chemical peel treatment.
* **Previous Treatments** (Previous Treatments, required): Select all of the previous skin treatments you have undergone (e.g. chemical peels, microdermabrasion, etc.).
* **Previous Peel Details** (Previous Peel Details, optional): If you have had previous chemical peels, describe your experiences in detail.
* **Current Skincare Routine** (Current Skincare Routine, required): List the products you currently use in your skincare routine.
* **Retinol Use** (Retinol Use, required): Answer whether you currently use retinol products.
* **Accutane History** (Accutane History, required): Answer whether you have taken Accutane in the past year.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, required): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, required): List any allergies you have.
* **Cold Sore History** (Cold Sore History, required): Answer whether you get cold sores.
* **Pregnancy or Nursing Status** (Pregnancy or Nursing Status, required): Answer whether you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Sun Exposure Habits** (Sun Exposure Habits, required): Answer how much sun exposure you get (Minimal, Moderate, High, or Use tanning bed).
* **Sunscreen Use** (Sunscreen Use, required): Answer how often you use sunscreen.
* **Keloid Scarring Tendency** (Keloid Scarring Tendency, required): Answer whether you tend to scar easily.
* **Recommended Peel Type** (Recommended Peel Type, required): This field will be filled in by the esthetician after reviewing your information.
* **Recommended Number of Sessions** (Recommended Number of Sessions, required): This field will be filled in by the esthetician after reviewing your information.
* **Estimated Cost** (Estimated Cost, optional): This field will be filled in by the esthetician after reviewing your information.
* **Client Questions** (Client Questions, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please list them here.
* **Esthetician Name** (Esthetician Name, required): This field will be filled in by the esthetician after reviewing your information.
* **Esthetician Notes** (Esthetician Notes, optional): This field will be filled in by the esthetician after reviewing your information.
* **Ready to Proceed** (Ready to Proceed, required): Answer whether you are ready to proceed with treatment (Yes, schedule appointment, Need more time to decide, or No, not interested).
