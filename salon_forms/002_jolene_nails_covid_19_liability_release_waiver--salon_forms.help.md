# jolene_nails_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Liability Release and Waiver for Jolene Nails, a business that provides medical or beauty services. It is designed to collect information from clients to ensure they understand their rights and responsibilities before undergoing any treatment or service. This form may be required for clients who have received or are about to receive treatments related to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
2. **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
3. **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it is written on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
4. **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship you have with the person providing this information (e.g., John, Mom, Dad, Friend, Other).
5. **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have that could affect treatment or service.
6. **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you have that could affect treatment or service.
7. **Number of Vaccine Doses Administered** (`Number of Vaccine Doses Administered`, number, optional): If you have received any COVID-19 vaccines, enter the number of doses you have received.
8. **Last Vaccine Dose Date** (`Last Vaccine Dose Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your last COVID-19 vaccine dose.
9. **Treatment Location** (`Treatment Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you received any treatments or services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This field is for your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
* **Name**: This field is for your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or as you prefer it to be written.
* **Relationship**: Please select the relationship you have with the person providing this information. This helps us understand who you are providing this information on behalf of.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have that could affect treatment or service. This will help us take necessary precautions to ensure your safety.
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical conditions you have that could affect treatment or service. This will help us take necessary precautions to ensure your safety.
* **Number of Vaccine Doses Administered**: If you have received any COVID-19 vaccines, enter the number of doses you have received.
* **Last Vaccine Dose Date**: Enter the date of your last COVID-19 vaccine dose.
* **Treatment Location**: Enter the location where you received any treatments or services.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields (Relationship, Allergies, Medical Condition, Number of Vaccine Doses Administered, Last Vaccine Dose Date) to ensure we can provide safe and effective service.
* If you are unsure about any information, please provide as much detail as possible or contact the salon for guidance. 
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to Jolene Nails for clarification or assistance.
