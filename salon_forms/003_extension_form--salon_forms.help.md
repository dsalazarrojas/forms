# Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extension Form is used to collect information from clients for salon extensions or appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the **salon_name** field. This is the name of the salon where the extension is taking place.
2. Enter your name in the **client_name** field. This is your personal name.
3. Select the type(s) of extension you would like to have from the **extension_type** options. You can choose one or multiple types.
4. Enter the start date of the extension in the **start_date** field.
5. Enter the end date of the extension in the **end_date** field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the **notes** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
8. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **salon_name** (text): Enter the name of the salon where the extension is taking place.
* **client_name** (text): Enter your personal name.
* **extension_type** (select multiple): Select the type(s) of extension you would like to have.
* **start_date** (date): Enter the start date of the extension.
* **end_date** (date): Enter the end date of the extension.
* **notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
