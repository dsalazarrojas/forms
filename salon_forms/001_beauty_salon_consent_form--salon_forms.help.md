# Beauty Salon Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial part of the beauty salon's process, serving as a legal and ethical foundation for providing services. It helps ensure that clients understand their rights, responsibilities, and the potential risks involved with certain treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** accurately.
2. If you have a **Date of Birth**, please enter it. This information is only required if you wish to share it.
3. Select your current hair color from the options provided.
4. Choose your desired hair color from the options.
5. Select your preferred hair cut style from the options.
6. If you have any **Allergies or Sensitivities**, list them in the field provided.
7. Answer whether you have had **Previous Chemical Treatments**, and if so, what type.
8. Select your **Scalp Condition** from the options.
9. If you have any **Medical Conditions Affecting Hair**, list them in the field provided.
10. Choose to **Agree or Not Agree** to the beauty services and understand the risks involved.
11. Confirm you have received and understood the **Aftercare Instructions** provided.
12. Finally, sign with the current **Date of Consent**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: We will contact you with updates and information related to your appointment.
* **Phone Number**: Include your area code for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth**: If you choose to share this information, it helps us tailor services to your needs.
* **Current Hair Color**: Select the color of your natural hair.
* **Desired Hair Color**: Choose from the options to get the best results.
* **Preferred Hair Cut Style**: Select from the common styles or describe your preference.
* **Hair Length Preference**: Choose from the options to get the desired length.
* **Allergies or Sensitivities**: List any allergies or sensitivities you may have to hair products or treatments.
* **Previous Chemical Treatments**: Answer truthfully about previous chemical treatments you've received.
* **Scalp Condition**: Select how healthy your scalp is.
* **Medical Conditions Affecting Hair**: List any medical conditions affecting your hair, such as thyroid issues or alopecia.
* **I Consent to the Beauty Services and Understand the Risks Involved**: Agree to proceed with the services and understand the potential risks.
* **I Have Received and Understood Aftercare Instructions**: Confirm you have received and understood the aftercare instructions.
* **Date of Consent**: Sign with the current date to confirm your consent.
