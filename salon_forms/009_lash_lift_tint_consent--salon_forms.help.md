# Lash Lift & Tint Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Lift & Tint Consent form is designed to gather information from clients about their interest in receiving a Lash Lift & Tint treatment. This form helps us understand their preferences and expectations for the treatment, ensuring that we can provide the best possible experience for them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone number, and Date of Birth in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your level of interest in a Lash Lift & Tint treatment from the "Interested" dropdown menu.
3. Provide any additional details about the treatment in the "Treatment Details" field.
4. Select your preferred appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.
5. Choose your preferred appointment date in the "Appointment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be used by our staff.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be used by our staff.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth to confirm your identity.
* **Interested** (select one, required): Select whether you are interested or not interested in a Lash Lift & Tint treatment.
* **Treatment Details** (note, optional): Provide any additional details about the treatment you are interested in, such as specific requests or concerns.
* **Appointment Time** (time, required): Select your preferred appointment time for the treatment.
* **Appointment Date** (date, required): Select your preferred appointment date for the treatment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the treatment, please provide them in the "Treatment Details" field.
* Our staff will review your form and contact you to confirm your appointment details.
