# Client Consultation Form For Nail Tech - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from clients about their nail service preferences and history. This information helps our nail technicians better understand the client's needs and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client full name** in the "Client full name" field.
2. Provide your **contact phone number** in the "Contact phone" field (optional, but recommended for communication).
3. Enter your **email address** in the "Email address" field (optional, but recommended for booking confirmations).
4. Select your **primary service requested** from the dropdown menu for "Treatments requested" (this is required).
5. Choose your **nail shape preference** from the dropdown menu for "Nail shape preference" (optional).
6. If you have any **allergies or skin conditions**, enter them in the "Allergies or skin conditions" field.
7. List your **current nail products used** in the "Current nail products used" field (optional).
8. If you have a specific **date of last nail service**, enter it in the "Date of last nail service" field (optional).
9. Finally, if you have any **special instructions or preferences**, enter them in the "Special instructions or preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client full name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Your best contact number for our salon to reach you.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for booking confirmations and updates.
* **Treatments requested** (`treatment_requested`, select_one, required): Select one of the following services: 
  + Manicure
  + Pedicure
  + Gel service
  + Acrylics
  + Nail repair
* **Nail shape preference** (`nail_shape_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following styles: 
  + Square
  + Round
  + Almond
  + Coffin
  + Stiletto
* **Allergies or skin conditions** (`allergies_or_conditions`, text, optional): List any allergies, skin conditions, or sensitivities that our technicians should know about.
* **Current nail products used** (`current_products`, text, optional): List your current nail products, including brands and methods of application (e.g., gel, dip).
* **Date of last nail service** (`last_service_date`, date, optional): If you have a record of your last nail service, enter the date for better service planning.
* **Special instructions or preferences** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or notes for our technicians.
