# Lash Extension Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Extension Intake Form is used to collect information from clients for lash extension services. It helps the lash technician to understand the client's requirements and preferences for their appointment, including services, application type, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (Client Name, Lash Application Type, Lash Materials) to ensure that the lash technician has enough information to provide the best service for the client.
* Select the desired Lash Service (Lash Lift, Lash Half-Set, or Full Lash Application) from the options provided.
* Select the Lash Application Type (Hybrid, Volume, Mega Volume, or Natural) that the client is interested in.
* Indicate whether the client is interested in using lash materials (Yes or No).
* If applicable, enter the appointment details (date and time).
* Enter the client's contact information (phone number and email).
* Add any additional notes about the client's appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: A required field to input the client's name.
* **Lash Technician**: A field to input the name of the lash technician assigned to the client.
* **Lash Service**: A field to select the type of lash service desired (Lash Lift, Lash Half-Set, or Full Lash Application).
* **Lash Application Type**: A field to select the type of lash application desired (Hybrid, Volume, Mega Volume, or Natural).
* **Lash Materials**: A field to indicate whether the client is interested in using lash materials (Yes or No).
* **Appointment Date**: A field to input the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: A field to input the time of the appointment.
* **Contact Phone**: A field to input the client's contact phone number.
* **Contact E-mail**: A field to input the client's contact email.
* **Notes**: A field to input any additional notes about the client's appointment.
* **Contact Address**: A field to input the client's contact address.

Note: Some fields are not required, but filling them in can provide the lash technician with more information to better serve the client.
