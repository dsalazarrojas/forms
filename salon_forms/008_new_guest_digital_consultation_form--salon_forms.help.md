# New Guest Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Guest Digital Consultation Form is designed to collect information from new guests for digital consultations. This form helps the salon to understand the guest's preferences, schedule appointments, and provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your first name in the text field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_first_name'.
2. Fill in your last name in the text field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_last_name'.
3. Enter your email address in the email field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_email'.
4. Provide your phone number in the text field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_phone'.
5. Select whether you have a preferred service (Yes/No) in the select-one field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service'.
6. Provide details about your desired service in the text field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service_details'.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes about your consultation in the note field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_notes'.
8. Choose a date for your consultation in the date field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_date'.
9. Choose a time for your consultation in the time field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_time'.
10. Select your desired services categories (Yes/No) in the select multiple field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_category'.
11. Select your desired service types (Yes/No) in the select multiple field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service_type'.
12. Select whether you agree with the terms and conditions (Yes/No) in the select-one field marked 'new_guest_digital_consultation_form_agree'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_first_name**: Enter your first name to help us know you better.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_last_name**: Enter your last name to help us know you better.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_phone**: Provide your phone number so we can reach you to confirm your appointment.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service**: Select 'Yes' if you have a preferred service, or 'No' if you are open to suggestions.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service_details**: Provide details about your desired service so we can tailor our approach.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_notes**: Add any additional notes about your consultation.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_date**: Choose a date for your consultation from the calendar.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_time**: Choose a time for your consultation from the time picker.
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_category**: Select all the desired services categories (e.g., haircut, coloring, etc.).
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_service_type**: Select all the desired service types (e.g., hair treatment, makeup, etc.).
* **new_guest_digital_consultation_form_agree**: Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have read and agree with the terms and conditions.
