# sarah_rose_therapies_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sarah_rose_therapies_client_intake_form is a form designed to collect essential information from clients for the purpose of their therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a Date for your therapy session in the Date field.
6. Select a Time for your therapy session in the Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: false): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional: false): Select a date for your therapy session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional: false): Select a time for your therapy session.
