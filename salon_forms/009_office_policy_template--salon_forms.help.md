# office_policy_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **office_policy_template** form is used to record essential information from clients, including their agreements, financial responsibilities, and contact details. This helps salon staff ensure accurate and up-to-date client information, promoting seamless service delivery and effective communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct client and their appointment details.
2. Select all services that you have agreed to provide to the client from the **services_agreed** field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the salon's cancellation policy in the **cancellation_policy** field.
4. Choose the relevant financial responsibility from the options for the client.
5. Enter the client's electronic signature in the **e_signature** field.
6. Check the box to indicate if the client is in agreement with the salon's terms in the **client_agreement** field.
7. Enter the client's name in the **client_name** field.
8. Enter the client's email address in the **client_email** field (optional).
9. Enter the client's phone number in the **client_phone** field (optional).
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the **note** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **services_agreed**: This is a multiple-choice field where you select all the services you have agreed to provide to the client.
* **cancellation_policy**: This is a text field where you must describe the salon's cancellation policy.
* **financial_responsibilities**: This is a select-one field where you choose the relevant financial responsibility for the client.
* **e_signature**: This is a date field where you enter the client's electronic signature.
* **client_agreement**: This is a multiple-choice field where you check the box to indicate if the client is in agreement with the salon's terms.
* **client_name**: This is a text field where you enter the client's name.
* **client_email**: This is an optional email field where you can enter the client's email address.
* **client_phone**: This is an optional text field where you can enter the client's phone number.
* **note**: This is an optional note field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the client.
