# dermal_filler_treatment_medical_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dermal_filler_treatment_medical_consent_form is a medical consent form for dermal filler treatments. This form is used to gather information about the client's medical history, allergies, and current medications before undergoing a dermal filler treatment. The form helps the healthcare provider to assess the client's suitability for the treatment and ensure their safety during the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" for the questions that apply to you. Some questions are multiple-choice questions with two options: "Yes" and "No". 
2.  Answer any text fields (e.g., current medications, medical tests results) in as much detail as possible. 
3.  Be sure to answer all required fields, even if the answer is "None" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): This section is used to determine if you have any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant information that may impact your dermal filler treatment.
* **Medical History** (text): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries. This information will help the healthcare provider understand your overall health status.
* **Current Medications** (text): List all your current medications, including vitamins and supplements. This is important so that the healthcare provider can assess potential interactions with the dermal filler treatment.
* **Allergies** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): Indicate if you have any known allergies, including food or environmental allergies.
* **Surgical History** (text): Describe any previous surgeries you have had, including the reason for the surgery and the date it was performed.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your treatment, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Medical Tests** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): Indicate if you have undergone any medical tests, such as blood work or imaging studies.
* **Medical Test Results** (text): Provide the results of any medical tests you have mentioned in the previous question.
* **Current Vitamins** (text): List any vitamins or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Medical Procedures** (text): Describe any previous medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medications Taken** (text): List any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Conditions List** (`Yes/No`, select_multiple): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your treatment. 

## Tips
* Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Be sure to review the entire form carefully before submitting it, as some questions are required and must be completed for your safety.
