# Spa Service Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from clients or customers after a spa service appointment. It helps us to understand our services better and improve our performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the purpose of the form.
2. Fill out all the fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form to provide your feedback and ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Spa Service Survey Form** (spa_service_survey_form, text, required: no):
	+ This is the title of the form that appears in the header.
* **What did you rate?** (rating, number, required: no):
	+ Use a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall satisfaction with the service you received.
* **Any comments?** (comments, note, required: no):
	+ Share any thoughts or feedback you have about your experience with us.
* **Email** (email, email, required: no):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, required: no):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you if we need more information.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know in the "Any Comments?" field.
* If you have any other questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
