# ipl_consent_amp_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ipl_consent_amp_waiver" form is designed to collect client information and IP consent for specific projects. This includes gathering client name, contact information, IP lawyer details, and IP consent. The form is crucial for ensuring accurate record-keeping and maintaining clear communication with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
3. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field (optional).
4. Select the IP lawyer's name from the drop-down list in the "IP Lawyer Name" field.
5. If applicable, enter the date and time of signing for the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields.
6. Enter any additional notes for the "IP Consent" field, if necessary.
7. Enter any additional notes for the "Waiver" field, if applicable.
8. Finally, add your signature to the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the client being served.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `number`, **Optional**): Provide the client's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter the client's email address for communication.
* **IP Lawyer Name** (`ip_lawyer_name`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the IP lawyer's name responsible for the project.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the date the contract or agreement was signed, if applicable.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, `time`, **Optional**): Record the time of signing.
* **IP Consent** (`ip_consent`, `note`, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes for IP consent, if necessary.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, `note`, **Optional**): Record any waivers or additional notes, if applicable.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, **Required**): Add your signature as the representative of the company or law firm.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and legible writing in the text fields.
- Double-check the IP lawyer's name and date of signing for correctness.
- Keep the IP consent and waiver fields up to date and relevant to the project.
- Sign the form with a clear and legible signature.
