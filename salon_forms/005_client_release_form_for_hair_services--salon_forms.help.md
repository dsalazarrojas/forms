# Client Release Form For Hair Services - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather vital information from clients, ensuring their safety and satisfaction with the hair services provided by the salon. It helps us understand their medical history, service preferences, and contact information, which enables us to tailor the service according to their needs and comfort level.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.
2. Be as specific as possible when describing your desired result or style.
3. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, list them in the "Allergies" field.
4. Confirm your understanding of potential risks associated with hair services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name, as you would like to be referred to in the salon.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): This is your contact number where we can reach you for any updates or concerns.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This is your email address where we can send you updates or notifications about your service.
* **Do you have any known allergies to hair products or chemicals?** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies or sensitivities, list them here.
* **Do you have any scalp conditions or sensitivities?** (`scalp_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any scalp conditions or sensitivities, list them here, such as dandruff or eczema.
* **Have you had previous chemical treatments to your hair?** (`previous_treatments`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have had previous chemical treatments.
* **What services are you requesting today?** (`services_requested`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the services you would like to receive today, such as a hair cut, color, or perm.
* **Will you be using color or chemical processing?** (`color_processing`, `select_one`, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you will be using color or chemical processing.
* **Describe the desired result or style you are requesting** (`desired_result`, `text`, required): Be as specific as possible when describing the style or result you desire.
* **Have you completed a patch test for color or chemical treatments?** (`patch_test`, `select_one`, optional): If you have completed a patch test, please select 'Yes', otherwise select 'No' or 'Not Applicable'.
* **I understand that hair services may carry risks including breakage, color results variation, or scalp sensitivity** (`understand_risks`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm you understand the potential risks.
* **I hereby release the salon and stylists from liability related to hair services performed** (`release_liability`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to release the salon and stylists from liability.
* **I trust the professional judgment of my stylist to recommend what is best for my hair health** (`professional_judgment`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm you trust the stylist's professional judgment.
* **I agree to follow professional aftercare instructions** (`aftercare_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm you will follow aftercare instructions.
* **May we take photos of your hair for salon portfolio or marketing purposes?** (`photo_consent`, `select_one`, optional): Please select 'Yes' if you are willing to be photographed.
* **Your assigned stylist** (`stylist_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name of your assigned stylist.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, `date`, required): Please select the date of your service.
