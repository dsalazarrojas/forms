# Hair Salon New Client Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hair Salon New Client Consent Form is a document used to gather essential information from new clients, including their contact details, appointment preferences, and medical information. This form helps the salon understand the client's needs and ensures a safe and comfortable experience for both the client and the salon staff.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Client Information" section, which includes your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. In the "Appointment Details" section, provide the date and time you would like to book your appointment, as well as any specific services you would like to receive during your visit.
3. The "Consent" section asks you to agree to our Terms and Conditions. Please select the option that best suits your needs.
4. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5. In the "Medical Conditions" section, list any medical conditions that may affect your appointment, such as any allergies or sensitivities you have.
6. The "Medical Information" section is where you can provide more detailed information about any medical conditions you may have.
7. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please list them in the "Allergies" section.
8. In the "Meds" section, list any medications you are currently taking.
9. The "Medical History" section is where you can provide information about your medical history.
10. Finally, please sign the "Client Signature" section to confirm you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Please provide your name, address, and other contact details.
* **Appointment Details**: Choose the date and time you would like to book your appointment, as well as the services you would like to receive during your visit.
* **Consent**: Select "Client Information", "Terms and Conditions", or "Both" to agree to our terms.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Phone Number**: Provide your phone number.
* **Email**: Provide your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their contact information.
* **Emergency Phone Number**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their phone number.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect your appointment, such as allergies or sensitivities.
* **Medical Information**: Provide more detailed information about any medical conditions you may have.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Meds**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History**: Provide information about your medical history.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign this section to confirm you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Date**: Please enter the date of this agreement.
* **Signature**: Please sign this section to confirm you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.

Note: The required fields are marked as "required: false" but are still necessary for the client to complete the form.
