# Skin Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Consultation Form is a customer-facing form used to collect information about skin concerns, type, and preferences to provide personalized product recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Make sure to save your changes before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Skin Concern** (text, required/optional): Describe your current skin concerns.
* **Description** (text, required/optional): Provide more details about your skin concerns.
* **Skin Type** (select multiple, required/optional): Select all skin types that apply to you. Options include:
	+ Normal
	+ Combination
	+ Oily
	+ Dry
	+ Sensitive
	+ Acne
	+ Other (please specify in the "Additional Info" field)
* **Skin Tone** (select one, required/optional): Select your skin tone. Options include:
	+ Fair
	+ Medium
	+ Dark
	+ Combination
* **Best Time to Apply** (time, required/optional): Select the best time of day to apply products. Please note that this field is not currently applicable.
* **Recommended Products** (select multiple, required/optional): Indicate which recommended products you are interested in learning more about.
* **Additional Info** (text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your skin or preferences.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to ensure the best possible outcome.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out.
* This form is for external use only. If you are an employee, please ensure you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and use this form.
