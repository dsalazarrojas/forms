# spa_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and authorization for services offered by the spa.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (Page 1)
* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required: false): This section is for collecting general information about the client, such as name, address, and contact details.

### Service Details (Page 2)
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required: false): Here, please provide details about the services you require, such as the type of treatment, time, and date.

### Authorization (Page 3)
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `text`, required: false): In this section, please indicate which services you are authorizing, such as which treatments or procedures you are agreeing to.

### Procedure Details (Page 4)
* **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for providing more detailed information about the procedures, such as the duration and any special instructions.

### Signature (Page 5)
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided. Your signature serves as authorization for the services you have agreed to.
