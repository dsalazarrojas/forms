# Laser Covid 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Covid 19 Liability Release Waiver form is designed to ensure that clients understand their rights and responsibilities when receiving laser treatments at the salon. It covers medical history, contact information, and consent for treatment, photography, and sharing of client information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your client information, such as first name, last name, and email (if applicable).
2. Choose your contact preference (phone or email) for communication.
3. List any medical conditions or allergies that we should know about.
4. Provide your emergency contact information.
5. Confirm your consent for treatment.
6. Sign the form as a client, confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
7. Sign the form as a representative of the salon, confirming that they have read and understood the terms and conditions.
8. If applicable, select whether you allow photography or not.
9. Confirm whether you allow the sharing of your information.
10. If applicable, confirm whether you allow the sharing of your image.
