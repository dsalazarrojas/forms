# Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Consultation Form is a necessary step in preparing for your visit to our salon. It allows us to gather essential information about you, which helps us tailor our services to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Enter your First Name and Last Name in the provided fields.
* Provide your Email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* Enter your Phone number, in case we need to contact you directly.
* Choose your preferred time for your massage appointment, using the time format (e.g., 10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, etc.). Please note that we will contact you to confirm the appointment schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for appointment reminders, special instructions, or any other important information.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you directly.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose the time slot that suits you best for your massage appointment. Please be aware that we will contact you to confirm the appointment schedule.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any special requests or allergies, feel free to mention them in the notes section (not included in this form, but you can provide this information in person or over the phone).
* Our salon staff will review your form and contact you shortly to confirm your preferred time and discuss any further details.
* Please arrive at least 15 minutes prior to your appointment time to allow for check-in and preparation.
