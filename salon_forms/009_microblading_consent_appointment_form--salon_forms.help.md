# microblading_consent_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by salons to collect information from clients for microblading services. It is designed to gather customer details, appointment requests, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Information** section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Appointment Request** section, specify the type of service you are requesting (e.g., Microblading, Eyeliner, etc.).
3. In the **Contact Info** section, provide your contact information so the salon can get in touch with you.
4. If you are requesting a specific service, select it from the **Services** dropdown menu.
5. In the **Appointment Details** section, provide any additional details about your appointment, such as your preferred dates and times.
6. Read and agree to the **Consent** terms.
7. Finally, sign the **Signature** section to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): This is the first section of the form where you provide your personal details such as name, phone number, and email address.
* **Appointment Request** (`appointment_request`): This section is used to specify the type of service you are requesting (e.g., Microblading, Eyeliner, etc.).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): This section is used to provide your contact information so the salon can get in touch with you.
* **Services** (`services`): This is a dropdown menu where you select the type of service you are requesting. The options are:
	+ Microblading
	+ Eyeliner
	+ Eyeliner Touch-up
	+ Microblading Touch-up
	+ Eyelashes
	+ Eyelash Lift
	+ Eyelash Fill
	+ Microblading Fill
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`): This section is used to provide any additional details about your appointment, such as your preferred dates and times.
* **Consent** (`consent`): This section is used to indicate your consent to the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This is the final section where you sign the form to complete it.

## Tips
Please make sure to carefully review each section and fill in all required fields before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the salon directly.
