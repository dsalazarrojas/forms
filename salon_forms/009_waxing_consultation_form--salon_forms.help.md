# Waxing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waxing Consultation Form is used to gather information from customers for waxing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the "Customer Name" and "Phone Number" fields.
2. Select the type of waxing service you are interested in from the "Service Type" field.
3. Enter your hair length and type in the respective fields.
4. Specify the area you would like to be treated in the "Area to be treated" field.
5. If you have any medical conditions or previous treatments, provide more information in the "Medical Conditions" and "Previous Treatment" fields.
6. If you have any allergies, select them from the "Allergy" field.
7. Provide any additional medical notes or concerns you have in the "Medical Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Required: No): Please enter your name.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required: No): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, Select, Required: No):
	+ Waxing: Hot or cold waxing service
	+ Sugaring: Sugar-based waxing service
	+ Shaving: Shaving service
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, Number, Required: No): Enter the length of your hair in inches.
* **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, Select, Required: No):
	+ Coarse
	+ Fine
	+ Medium
	+ Coarse and Medium
* **Area to be treated** (`area_to_be_treated`, Text, Required: No): Specify the area you would like to be treated.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, Text, Required: No): If you have any medical conditions, please provide more information.
* **Previous Treatment** (`previous_treatment`, Text, Required: No): If you have had previous waxing treatments, please describe them.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, Select Multiple, Required: No):
	+ Allergy1
	+ Allergy2
	+ Allergy3
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, Note, Required: No): Provide any additional medical notes or concerns you have.
