# Barber Shop Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new and existing clients to book an appointment with a barber and provide consent for services. It collects personal and contact information, appointment preferences, and consent for services and photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (Client Full Name, Contact Phone Number, and Email Address).
2. Select your preferred appointment date and time.
3. Choose the service you want to book (e.g., Standard Haircut, Haircut and Beard Trim).
4. Select your preferred barber or choose "No preference".
5. If you requested a specific barber, enter their name.
6. Check if this is your first visit to our shop.
7. If you have any medical conditions or skin issues, please let us know.
8. Provide consent for the services and photos.
9. Confirm that you understand our cancellation policy.
10. Accept or decline our liability waiver.
11. If you're a minor, provide your parent or guardian's name and signature.
12. Add any special requests or notes for your appointment.
13. Choose how you'd like to be reminded of your appointment.
14. Confirm that you'd like to receive marketing communications from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Phone Number** (2, text, required): This is your primary contact phone number for appointment reminders.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): We'll use this to send you appointment confirmations.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (4, date, required): Select your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (5, time, required): Choose your preferred time slot.
* **Service Requested** (6, select_one, required): Choose the service you want to book (e.g., Standard Haircut, Haircut and Beard Trim).
* **Barber Preference** (7, select_one, required): Select your preferred barber or choose "No preference".
* **Specific Barber Name** (8, text, not required): If you requested a specific barber, enter their name.
* **Is This Your First Visit** (9, select_one, required): Check if this is your first visit to our shop.
* **Medical Conditions or Skin Issues** (10, text, not required): If you have any medical conditions or skin issues, please let us know.
* **Service Consent Agreement** (11, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the services.
* **Photo Consent** (12, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to photos or not.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (13, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand our cancellation policy.
* **Liability Waiver** (14, select_one, required): Accept or decline our liability waiver.
* **Is the Client Under 18** (15, select_one, required): If the client is under 18, provide parental consent and contact information.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (16, text, not required): If the client is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (17, text, not required): If the client is under 18, type the parent or guardian's signature.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (18, text, not required): Add any special requests or notes for your appointment.
* **Appointment Reminder Preference** (19, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be reminded of your appointment (e.g., Text message, Email, Phone call).
* **Marketing Communications** (20, select_one, required): Confirm that you'd like to receive marketing communications from us.
