# Makeup Appointment Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Makeup Appointment Request" form is used to collect information from clients for a makeup session. This form is used to gather the client's name, email, phone number, and other relevant details to provide personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
4. Choose a date for your appointment in the "Date" field.
5. Select a time for your appointment in the "Time" field.
6. Choose the duration of your appointment in the "Duration" field.
7. Select the location for your appointment in the "Location" field.
8. Select the makeup services you would like to book (Makeup Session, Touch-up, or Makeup Lesson).
9. Choose the artist assigned to you from the provided options.
10. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Requests" field.
11. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "Terms and Conditions" field.
12. Click "Submit" to complete and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Duration** (`duration`, text, optional): Choose the duration of your appointment.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Select the location for your appointment.
* **Makeup Services** (`makeup_services`, select_multiple, required): Choose the makeup services you would like to book (Makeup Session, Touch-up, or Makeup Lesson).
* **Makeup Artist** (`makeup_artist`, select_one, required): Choose the artist assigned to you from the provided options.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests, type them here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click "Submit" to complete and submit your request.
* **Back** (`back`, text, optional): Click "Back" to go back to the previous page.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click "Cancel" to cancel your request.
