This help guide is intended to support end-users in completing the Brow Tint Waiver form, which is used to gather information about a client's personal and medical history before performing a brow tinting procedure. This form is designed to ensure that the client is adequately informed about the risks and benefits of the procedure, and to provide a legal waiver of liability in the event of any adverse reactions or complications.

The form will be displayed in multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. This guide will walk the user through each page, explaining the purpose and requirements of each field.

## Brow Tint Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brow Tint Waiver form is a critical document that must be completed before any brow tinting procedure can take place. It is used to gather essential information about the client's medical history, skin and eye conditions, and previous experiences with brow tinting. This form is used to ensure the client's safety and well-being, as well as to protect the salon and its staff from any potential liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each page and answering the questions accordingly.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing information about your medical history, skin and eye conditions, and previous experiences with brow tinting.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not sure" or ask your technician for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any questions or concerns.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for any updates or notifications.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your appointment for the brow tinting procedure.
* **Have you had brow tinting before?** (`previous_tinting`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "True" if you have had brow tinting before, "False" if not, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.
* **Preferred or previous tint brand** (`tint_brand`, `text`, `optional`): If you have had brow tinting before, enter the brand of the product used.
* **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your skin type from the options: Oily, Dry, Combination, Sensitive, or Normal.
* **Known allergies to dyes or chemicals** (`known_allergies`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any known allergies to dyes or chemicals, enter the details here.
* **Have you had allergic reactions to dyes?** (`allergic_reactions`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "True" if you have had allergic reactions to dyes, "False" if not, or "Not sure" if you are unsure.
* **Current skin conditions around brows** (`skin_conditions`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any current skin conditions around your brows, such as rash, eczema, or psoriasis, enter the details here.
* **Any eye conditions or sensitivities** (`eye_conditions`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any eye conditions or sensitivities, such as dry eyes or sensitivity to products, enter the details here.
* **Current medications affecting skin** (`current_medications`, `text`, `optional`): If you are currently taking any medications that may affect your skin, enter the details here.
* **Recent procedures on face or eyes** (`recent_procedures`, `text`, `optional`): If you have had any recent procedures on your face or eyes, enter the details here, especially if they are within the last 2 weeks.
* **Pregnancy or nursing status** (`pregnancy_status`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your current pregnancy or nursing status from the options: Pregnant, Nursing, Neither, or Prefer not to answer.
* **A patch test was performed** (`patch_test`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "Yes, passed" if a patch test was performed and you did not have a reaction, "Yes, minor reaction" if a minor reaction occurred, or "Not yet completed" if the patch test has not been done.
* **I understand the risks of brow tinting** (`acknowledge_risks`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "True" if you have read and understand the risks of brow tinting, or "False" if you have not.
* **I waive all liability and consent to tinting** (`sign_waiver`, `select_one`, `required`): Select "Yes, I waive liability" to confirm your consent to the procedure, or "False" if you do not.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you are signing the waiver.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when providing information about your medical history, skin and eye conditions, and previous experiences with brow tinting.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not sure" or ask your technician for guidance.
* Please review each page carefully before submitting the form.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Brow Tint Waiver form accurately and confidently, ensuring a safe and successful brow tinting experience.
