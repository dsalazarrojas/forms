# Salon Chemical Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about chemical exposure incidents, ensure compliance with safety regulations, and track chemical usage in the salon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Provide all required information, as indicated by the required label next to each field.
3. If you are unsure about any question, consult with your supervisor or a relevant authority.
4. Use the provided fields to answer the questions. If a field has a hint, use it as guidance to provide the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a general introduction to the form.
* **Salon Information**: Please provide your salon's information, including the name and address.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Role in the Salon**: Select your role in the salon (Owner/Manager, Staff Member, Client).
* **Chemical Information**: Provide the chemical being used, as well as any other relevant details.
* **Chemical Name**: Enter the name of the chemical being used.
* **Concentration**: Enter the concentration of the chemical being used.
* **Date of Release**: Enter the date you released the chemical.
* **Exposure Duration**: Enter the duration of your exposure to the chemical.
* **Previous Chemical Exposure Incidents**: Check the box if you have had any previous chemical exposure incidents.
* **Occupation**: Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies that may be affected by chemical exposure.
* **Emergency Contact Information**: Provide the contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Last Chemical Release Date**: Enter the date of your last chemical release.
* **Chemical Release Frequency**: Enter the frequency of chemical releases.
* **Chemical Safety Procedures**: Check the box if you have received training on chemical safety procedures.
* **Chemical Spill Response Plan**: Check the box if the salon has a chemical spill response plan.
* **Chemical Spill Response Plan Location**: Provide the location of the chemical spill response plan, if applicable.
* **Chemical Safety Officer**: Provide the name and contact information of the chemical safety officer.
* **Chemical Inventory**: Provide a list of chemicals used in the salon.
* **Chemical Inventory Storage**: Describe how you store the chemical inventory.
* **Chemical Labeling and Locking**: Check the box if the chemicals are labeled and locked.
* **Chemical Access**: Provide a list of people who have access to the chemicals.
* **Chemical Usage**: Check the box if the chemicals are used properly.

Please take a few minutes to review the form carefully and complete it as instructed. If you have any questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or the chemical safety officer for assistance.
