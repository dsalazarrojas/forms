# Skincare Facial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by salons to collect information from clients before providing a facial treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the date of your visit from the calendar and enter the time of your visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.
3. Check the "Services Offered" field to confirm the treatments you will be receiving.
4. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
5. If you have any medical history, please provide it in the "Medical History" field.
6. Read and agree to the "Consent Statement".
7. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Select the date you plan to visit the salon.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Select the time you plan to visit the salon.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, text, required): Check the services you will be receiving during your visit.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact information for communication and follow-up.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies, please list them here.
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms of service, including any potential risks and side effects of the treatments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional information or concerns you'd like to share with the salon staff.
