# cranial_prothesis_client_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about clients who are undergoing a cranial prosthesis procedure. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary data is collected to provide the best possible care and treatment for the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's **Salon Form Data** on Page 1.
2. Enter the client's **Client Name**.
3. Provide their **Email** address.
4. Enter their **Phone Number**.
5. Select the **Appointment Date** and **Appointment Time** for the procedure.
6. Enter any **Notes** about the client's condition or procedure.
7. Indicate if an **Assigned Technician** will be available to work with the client.
8. Select the **Selected Style** of prosthesis to be performed.
9. Provide **Service Details** about the client's procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Form Data** (Page 1, text, required: false): Enter any relevant information about the client's visit, such as their medical history or allergies.
* **Client Name** (Page 2, text, required: false): Enter the client's name.
* **Email** (Page 3, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 4, text, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Appointment Date** (Page 5, date, required: false): Select the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (Page 6, time, required: false): Select the time of the appointment.
* **Notes** (Page 7, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the client's condition or procedure.
* **Assigned Technician** (Page 8, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if an assigned technician will be available to work with the client.
* **Selected Style** (Page 9, select_one, required: false): Select the style of prosthesis to be performed.
* **Service Details** (Page 10, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the client's procedure.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant medical professional.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact the medical team for assistance.
