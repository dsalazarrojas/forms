# Nail Technician Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information to ensure the best possible service experience during their nail technician visit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the fields as accurately as possible.
- Make sure to select all relevant options for your preferred services.
- Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for any updates or notifications.
- Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
- Provide any additional comments or questions you may have in the Client Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (Text Field):
  The Client Details field is used to collect basic client information such as name and other relevant details.
- **Nail Type** (Select One):
  This field is used to select the type of nail service you require, such as Nail Polish, Gel, Acrylic, or other options.
- **Preferred Service** (Select Multiple):
  This field allows you to select multiple services you prefer, such as Filing, Buffing, Shaping, or Polishing.
- **Client Comments** (Text Area):
  Use this field to provide any additional comments or questions you may have regarding your service.
- **Email** (Email Address):
  If you'd like to be contacted via email for any updates or notifications, please enter your email address here.
- **Phone** (Text Field):
  If you'd like to be contacted via phone for any updates or notifications, please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Ensure that all services selected are relevant to your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
