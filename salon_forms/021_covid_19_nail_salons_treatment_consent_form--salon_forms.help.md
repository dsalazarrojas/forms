# COVID 19 Nail Salons Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from clients before providing nail treatments during the COVID-19 pandemic. It covers personal details, health screening, and consent to receive treatments, aiming to create a safe and healthy environment for both clients and salon staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Ensure you answer 'True' or 'False' for questions about COVID-19 symptoms and medical history truthfully.
3.  Choose the correct options for your allergies or sensitivities.
4.  Read the consent statement carefully and sign it if you agree to the terms.
5.  Date the form to confirm when you gave consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required):
    This is your complete name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required):
    Please enter your active email address for any follow-up contact or communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required):
    Your phone number where you can be contacted for any urgent matters.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required):
    This is your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required):
    The date on which you intend to visit or have visited the salon for your treatment.
*   **Type of Service** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Choose the specific service you're interested in, such as manicure or pedicure, or multiple services if needed.
*   **Do you have any COVID-19 symptoms?** (`covid_symptoms`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' if you're currently experiencing symptoms, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if uncertain.
*   **If yes, describe symptoms** (`symptom_list`, `text`, optional):
    Describe the symptoms you're experiencing, if any.
*   **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (`diagnosed_covid`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' if you've been diagnosed, 'False' if not, or 'Unsure' if unsure.
*   **Have you been in close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 case?** (`close_contact_covid`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' if you've had close contact, 'False' if not, or 'Unsure' if uncertain.
*   **Do you have a fever or elevated temperature?** (`temperature`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' if you have a fever, 'False' if not, or 'Do not know' if you're unsure.
*   **Do you have allergies to nail products?** (`nail_polish_allergy`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' if you're allergic, 'False' if not.
*   **Describe allergies** (`allergy_details`, `text`, optional):
    If you have allergies, describe them briefly.
*   **Do you have skin conditions affecting nails or hands?** (`skin_conditions`, `select_one`, optional):
    Answer 'True' if you have skin conditions affecting your nails or hands, 'False' if not.
*   **I understand the salon safety measures during COVID-19** (`understand_safety_measures`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'True' to confirm you understand and agree to the measures.
*   **I consent to receive the requested nail treatment** (`consent_treatment`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'I consent' if you agree to the treatment, 'I do not consent' if you don't.
*   **I accept the risks of COVID-19 transmission** (`accept_risk`, `select_one`, required):
    Answer 'I accept' if you agree to the risks, 'I do not accept' if you don't.
*   **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, `date`, required):
    Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask salon staff for clarification.
*   The form is a part of the salon's protocol for your safety and ours.
