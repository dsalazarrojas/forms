# Botulinum Toxin Injection Site Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for medical professionals to record and document botulinum toxin injection procedures. It helps to track treatment information, product details, and patient reactions, ensuring accurate reporting and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the patient's name under "Patient Name" (ID 2).
2. Enter the patient's ID number under "Patient ID Number" (ID 3).
3. Record the date of the treatment under "Treatment Date" (ID 4).
4. Enter the time of the treatment under "Treatment Time" (ID 5), if applicable.
5. Select the product brand used under "Product Brand" (ID 8) from the provided options (e.g., Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, or "Other").
6. Enter the product batch number (ID 9) for traceability purposes.
7. Record the product expiration date (ID 10) for verification of shelf life.
8. Enter the number of units injected into the forehead area under "Forehead Units Injected" (ID 12).
9. Enter the number of units injected into the glabella area (between the eyebrows) under "Glabella (Between Brows) Units" (ID 13).
10. Enter the number of units injected into the crow's feet area under "Crow Feet Units" (ID 14), or indicate the combined total if applicable.
11. Provide any additional treatment areas under "Other Treatment Areas" (ID 15), if necessary.
12. Enter the total number of units administered under "Total Units Administered" (ID 16).
13. Record any immediate reactions experienced by the patient under "Any immediate reactions" (ID 18).
14. Rate the patient's comfort level during the procedure under "Patient Comfort Level" (ID 19), using the provided scale (e.g., Very comfortable, Comfortable, Slightly uncomfortable, Uncomfortable, Very uncomfortable).
15. Enter any additional provider notes under "Additional Provider Notes" (ID 20).
16. Schedule a recommended follow-up date under "Recommended Follow-up Date" (ID 21), if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (ID 2)**: Enter the full legal name of the patient.
* **Patient ID Number (ID 3)**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Treatment Date (ID 4)**: Record the date of the injection procedure.
* **Treatment Time (ID 5)**: Enter the time of the injection procedure, if applicable.
* **Provider Name (ID 6)**: Enter the name of the administering professional.
* **Product Brand (ID 8)**: Select the brand of the product used (e.g., Botox, Dysport, Xeomin, Jeuveau, or "Other").
* **Product Batch Number (ID 9)**: Enter the batch number of the product for traceability purposes.
* **Product Expiration Date (ID 10)**: Record the expiration date of the product for verification of shelf life.
* **Forehead Units Injected (ID 12)**: Enter the total number of units injected into the forehead area.
* **Glabella (Between Brows) Units (ID 13)**: Enter the total number of units injected into the glabella area (between the eyebrows).
* **Crow Feet Units (ID 14)**: Enter the total number of units injected into the crow's feet area or indicate combined total if applicable.
* **Other Treatment Areas (ID 15)**: Provide any additional treatment areas, if necessary.
* **Total Units Administered (ID 16)**: Enter the total number of units administered.
* **Any immediate reactions (ID 18)**: Record any immediate reactions experienced by the patient (e.g., redness, swelling, discomfort).
* **Patient Comfort Level (ID 19)**: Rate the patient's comfort level during the procedure using the provided scale (e.g., Very comfortable, Comfortable, Slightly uncomfortable, Uncomfortable, Very uncomfortable).
* **Additional Provider Notes (ID 20)**: Enter any additional provider notes or observations.
* **Recommended Follow-up Date (ID 21)**: Schedule a recommended follow-up date for results assessment, if applicable.
