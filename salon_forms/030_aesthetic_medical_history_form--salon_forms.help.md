# Aesthetic Medical History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about your medical history to ensure your safety during aesthetic treatments. Please answer all required fields honestly and to the best of your knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer all questions truthfully and as accurately as possible.
3. If you are unsure about any question, contact the healthcare professional responsible for your treatment.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Are you pregnant or breastfeeding?** (Required): Select 'True' if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, 'False' if you are not, and 'N/A' if you are not sure.
* **Current Medications** (Optional): List all your current prescription and over-the-counter medications.
* **Allergies** (Optional): List any drug or latex allergies you may have.
* **Skin Conditions** (Required): Select all skin conditions you have from the options provided (e.g., Eczema, Psoriasis, etc.).
* **Medical Conditions** (Optional): Select all medical conditions you have from the options provided (e.g., Diabetes, Heart Condition, etc.).
* **Previous Aesthetic Procedures** (Optional): Select all aesthetic procedures you have had before (e.g., Botox, Dermal Fillers, etc.).
* **History of Adverse Reactions** (Required): Select 'False' if you have never had a reaction, or 'Yes (Please describe)' if you have had a reaction.
* **Confirmation of Accuracy** (Required): Confirm that the above information is true and complete.
* **Date Signed** (Required): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Signature**: This section is for the signature and date of the patient.
