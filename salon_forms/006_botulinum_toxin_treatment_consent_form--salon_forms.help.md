# Botulinum Toxin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their understanding and consent for botulinum toxin treatment. It ensures that clients are aware of the procedure, risks, and benefits associated with the treatment and voluntarily consent to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your **Client Name**, which is your full legal name.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth**, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide the **Date of Treatment**, when the procedure is scheduled.
4. Enter the **Name of the Treating Provider**, who will be conducting the treatment.
5. Check the boxes to confirm that the **Procedure has been explained** to you, you **understand the risks and benefits**, and **alternative treatment options were discussed**.
6. Check the boxes to acknowledge the **risks of bruising and swelling**, **asymmetry or unevenness**, **temporary drooping**, and **allergic reaction**.
7. Confirm that you **understand the results are temporary and require maintenance**, and **full results appear within 7-14 days**.
8. Check the box to **release the provider from liability for expected risks**.
9. Indicate your consent to take **before-and-after photographs for records**.
10. Confirm your **voluntary consent to this treatment**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your full legal name.
	+ Required field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ Required field.
* **Date of Treatment**: The scheduled date of the procedure.
	+ Required field.
* **Treating Provider**: The name of the medical professional who will be conducting the treatment.
	+ Required field.
* **Procedure Explained**: Confirm that the procedure has been explained to you.
	+ Required field.
* **Understand Risks and Benefits**: Confirm that you understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment.
	+ Required field.
* **Alternative Treatment Options**: Confirm that alternative treatment options were discussed with you.
	+ Required field.
* **Risks of Bruising and Swelling**: Acknowledge the risk of bruising and swelling.
	+ Required field.
* **Asymmetry or Unevenness**: Acknowledge the risk of asymmetry or unevenness.
	+ Required field.
* **Temporary Drooping**: Acknowledge the risk of temporary drooping.
	+ Required field.
* **Allergic Reaction**: Acknowledge the risk of allergic reaction.
	+ Required field.
* **Temporary Results**: Confirm that you understand that results are temporary and require maintenance.
	+ Required field.
* **Full Results**: Confirm that you understand full results appear within 7-14 days.
	+ Required field.
* **Release and Consent**: Confirm that you release the provider from liability for expected risks.
	+ Required field.
* **Before-and-After Photographs**: Indicate your consent to take before-and-after photographs for records.
	+ Optional field.
* **Signature and Consent**: Confirm your voluntary consent to this treatment.
	+ Required field.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have carefully read and understood all sections before signing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your treating provider before proceeding with the treatment.
