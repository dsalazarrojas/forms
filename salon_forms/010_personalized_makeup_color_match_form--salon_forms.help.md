# Personalized Makeup Color Match Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to match your personal makeup color preferences with a personalized color palette.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your skin tone from the options provided.
2. Choose your preferred style.
3. Select your color preferences.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter your email address and/or phone number (optional).
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Tone (`Porcelain`, `Olive`, `Fair`, `Medium`, `Tanned`, select_one):** Select your natural skin tone from the provided options.
* **Style (`Natural`, `Dramatic`, `Soft`, `Bold`, `Neutral`, select_one):** Choose the style that reflects your makeup style, whether it's natural and subtle or dramatic and bold.
* **Color Preferences (`Red`, `Pink`, `Peach`, `Beige`, `Neutral`, select_multiple):** Select the colors you prefer for your makeup, and we will match them with a personalized palette.
* **Skin Tone (number, number):** Select your skin tone number (this field is not currently used in the form but is included for future reference).
* **Match Color (`Peach`, `Pink`, `Beige`, `Red`, `Neutral`, select_one):** Select a color that you like to match your skin tone.
* **Match Style (`Natural`, `Dramatic`, `Soft`, `Bold`, `Neutral`, select_one):** Select a style that you like to match your skin tone.
* **Match Tone (`Porcelain`, `Olive`, `Fair`, `Medium`, `Tanned`, select_one):** Select a skin tone to match your color match.
* **Matched Preferences (`Red`, `Pink`, `Peach`, `Beige`, `Neutral`, select_multiple):** Select the preferences that match your skin tone.
* **Additional Notes (text, note):** Add any additional notes or comments about your skin tone or color preferences.
* **Email (text, email):** Enter your email address if you want to be contacted with your personalized color palette.
* **Phone (text, text):** Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted with your personalized color palette.
* **Submit (text, text):** Click this button to complete the form and submit your information.

Note: Some fields are not currently used in the form but are included for future reference.
