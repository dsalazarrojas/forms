# Makeup-Appointment-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup-Appointment-Form is a form used for customers to schedule appointments at a salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Schedule Appointment" field with your desired appointment date.
2. Select the type of service you would like to book in the "Service Type" field.
3. Choose a date for your appointment in the "Availability" field.
4. Select a start and end time for your appointment in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
5. Provide any additional notes in the "Note" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, so the salon can contact you with appointment reminders.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you have one.
8. Provide the name of the salon where you would like to book your appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Appointment**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you would like to book, such as "Makeup Application" or "Makeup Touch-up".
* **Availability**: Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time for your appointment.
* **End Time**: Select the end time for your appointment.
* **Note**: Provide any additional notes or requests for your appointment.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the salon can contact you with appointment reminders.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Salon Name**: Enter the name of the salon where you would like to book your appointment.
