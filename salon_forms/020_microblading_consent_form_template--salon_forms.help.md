# Microblading Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microblading Consent Form Template is a form used to obtain client consent for microblading services. It is intended to be used by salons to ensure clients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the microblading process. This form is not meant to be a substitute for a thorough consultation with a medical professional, but rather a way to document client information and consent in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Client Reservation** field with your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your **Medical History** in the next field.
3. Select your **Consent** option from the dropdown menu, indicating that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the microblading process.
4. Choose the applicable **Salon Information** options, which include the type of microblading service you are receiving and any other relevant information about your visit.
5. Sign your name in the **Signature** field, confirming that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the microblading process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Reservation**: This field is used to collect your contact information so that we can reach out to you with any questions or concerns before your appointment.
	+ Label: Client Reservation
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Medical History**: Please provide any relevant medical history that may impact your microblading procedure. This will help us better understand your needs and ensure your safety.
	+ Label: Medical History
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
* **Consent**: This field is used to obtain your consent for the microblading process. Please select the option that best describes your understanding of the process.
	+ Label: Consent
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Required: true
* **Salon Information**: This field is used to collect information about your visit, including the type of microblading service you are receiving and any other relevant details.
	+ Label: Salon Information
	+ Type: select_multiple
	+ Required: true
* **Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the microblading process.
	+ Label: Signature
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough when completing this form, as this information will be used to ensure your safety and well-being.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us before or after your appointment.
* We will review this form with you before proceeding with the microblading process to ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
