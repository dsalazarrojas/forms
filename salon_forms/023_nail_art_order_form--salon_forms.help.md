# Nail Art Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nail Art Order Form is used to collect information from clients for nail art services. This form helps to record and confirm the details of the order for the service technician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the services you want to order from the list of options.
2. Choose the nail design and type of nail art you desire.
3. Specify the type of nail service you want to book.
4. Choose the nail color and size you prefer.
5. Select the shape and length of the nails you want to be done.
6. Choose the technician you want to book the appointment with.
7. Confirm the appointment date and time.
8. Add any special requests or comments.
9. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select the services you want to order from the list of options such as Manicure, Pedicure, Nail Art, and Nail Polish.
* **Services**
* **Nail Design** (`nail_design`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the nail design you prefer from the options such as Abstract, Color Block, Floral, Geometric, Minimalist, Ombre, Rhinestones, and Watercolor.
* **Nail Art Type** (`nail_art_type`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of nail art you want from the options such as Artistic, Color Blocking, French Tips, Nail Stamp, and Nail Sticker.
* **Nail Type** (`nail_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the type of nail you want to get done from the options such as Fingernails and Toenails.
* **Nail Service** (`nail_service`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the nail service you want to book from the options such as Manicure, Pedicure, Nail Art, and Nail Polish.
* **Nail Color** (`nail_color`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the nail color you prefer from the options such as Pink, Purple, Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Black, and White.
* **Nail Size** (`nail_size`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the size of the nail you want from the options such as Small, Medium, and Large.
* **Nail Shape** (`nail_shape`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the shape of the nail you prefer from the options such as Oval, Round, Square, Heart-Shaped, and Almond.
* **Nail Length** (`nail_length`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the length of the nail you want from the options such as 1-10 cm, 10-15 cm, and 15-20 cm.
* **Nail Width** (`nail_width`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose the width of the nail you prefer from the options such as 1-5 cm, 5-10 cm, and 10-15 cm.
* **Nail Height** (`nail_height`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the height of the nail you want from the options such as 1-20 cm, 20-25 cm, and 25-30 cm.
* **Nail Pattern** (`nail_pattern`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the nail pattern you prefer from the options such as Striped, Solid, Ombre, and Gradient.
* **Nail Color Scheme** (`nail_color_scheme`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the nail color scheme you want from the options such as Single, Double, Triple, and Multi.
* **Special Request** (`nail_special_request`, text, optional):
	+ Add any special requests you have for your nail art service.
* **Notes** (`nail_notes`, note, optional):
	+ Add any notes or comments about your order.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name for the order.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email for the order.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for the order.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your address for the order.
* **Technician** (`technician`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Choose the technician you want to book the appointment with from the options such as John, Jane, and Jack.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date for the appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, optional):
	+ Enter the time for the appointment.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
	+ Add any comments about your appointment.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, select_one, required):
	+ Confirm the order status as Confirmed, Cancelled, or Pending.

# Tips
* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure accuracy.
* Review and submit the form carefully before confirming the order.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please add them in the Notes field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact our service technician directly.
