## Botulinum Toxin Medical History Form - Help Guide
### Purpose

This form is designed to collect medical history information from patients before undergoing botulinum toxin injections. The form is intended to gather essential details about the patient's health, medical conditions, and treatment history, which will help our medical staff in providing the best possible care and minimize potential risks.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read this form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2.  Provide all necessary information to the best of your abilities.
3.  If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
4.  Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly.
5.  Review the form before submitting for accuracy.
6.  Sign and date the form to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (patient_first_name): This is your legal first name.
    *   **Last Name** (patient_last_name): This is your legal last name.
    *   **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Please enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
    *   **Gender** (gender): Select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say).
    *   **Phone Number** (phone): This is your primary contact number for appointment communications.
    *   **Email Address** (email): This is your email address for appointment communications.
    *   **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name): This is the full name of your emergency contact person.
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): This is the phone number of your emergency contact person.
    *   **Primary Physician Name** (primary_physician): This is the name of your primary care doctor.
    *   **Primary Physician's Phone** (physician_phone): This is your primary physician's contact number.
    *   **Reason for Seeking Treatment** (reason_for_treatment): Describe the concerns that brought you to this clinic.
    *   **Neuromuscular Disorders** (neuromuscular_disorders): Do you have any of the listed neuromuscular conditions or other conditions not listed?
    *   **Bleeding or Clotting Disorders** (bleeding_disorders): Do you have any of the listed bleeding or clotting disorders or other conditions not listed?
    *   **Known Allergies** (allergies): List all known allergies, including medications.
    *   **Allergy to Botulinum Toxin** (allergy_to_botulinum): Have you ever had an allergic reaction to botulinum toxin or similar?
    *   **Pregnancy Status** (pregnancy_status): Are you currently pregnant or breastfeeding?
    *   **Current Medications** (current_medications): List all prescription medications you are currently taking.
    *   **Blood Thinner Use** (blood_thinners): Are you taking any blood thinner medications?
    *   **Supplements and Vitamins** (supplements): List any supplements you take regularly.
    *   **Recent Antibiotic Use** (antibiotics): Have you taken antibiotics in the past 2 weeks?
    *   **Muscle Relaxant Use** (muscle_relaxants): Do you take muscle relaxant medications?
    *   **Sleep Aid Use** (sleep_aids): Do you take sleep aids or sedatives?
    *   **Previous Cosmetic Procedures** (previous_cosmetic_procedures): Have you had any of the listed cosmetic procedures?
    *   **Previous Botox Complications** (previous_botox_complications): Have you ever had complications from Botox?
    *   **Complications Description** (complications_description): Describe any previous complications.
    *   **Facial Surgery History** (facial_surgery_history): Have you had any facial surgeries?
    *   **Surgery Details** (surgery_details): Describe any facial surgeries.
    *   **Skin Conditions** (skin_conditions): Do you have any of the listed skin conditions or other conditions not listed?
    *   **Other Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions_other): List any other medical conditions you may have.
    *   **Tobacco Use** (tobacco_use): Do you use tobacco products?
    *   **Alcohol Consumption** (alcohol_consumption): How often do you consume alcohol?
    *   **Information Accuracy** (information_accuracy): I certify that all information provided is accurate.
    *   **Patient Signature** (patient_signature): Type your full legal name as signature.
    *   **Signature Date** (signature_date): Date of signing.

This guide is designed to provide a clear understanding of the form and its requirements. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions accurately. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
