# Hair Salon Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Client Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients before or during their visit to the salon. This form helps the salon staff to better understand the client's needs and preferences, ensuring a more personalized and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information section with your personal details.
2. Select your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Text Message) in the Contact Preference section.
3. If you would like to schedule an appointment, choose a date and time in the Schedule Appointment section.
4. Enter the desired appointment time in the Appointment Time section.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note section.
6. Enter your email address in the Email section, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information)**: This field is intended for you to provide your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Contact Preference (Contact Preference)**: Please select how you would like to be contacted by the salon. Options include:
	+ Phone: The salon will contact you via phone.
	+ Email: The salon will contact you via email.
	+ Text Message: The salon will contact you via text message.
* **Schedule Appointment (Schedule Appointment)**: If you want to schedule an appointment, choose a date from the calendar.
* **Appointment Time (Appointment Time)**: Select the desired time for your appointment.
* **Note (Note)**: Leave any additional notes or comments for the salon staff in this section.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted via email.
