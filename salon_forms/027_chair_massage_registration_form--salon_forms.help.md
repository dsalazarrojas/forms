# Chair Massage Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Chair Massage Registration Form is used to register clients for a chair massage session. It collects information about the client, their preferences, and the details of the massage appointment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to complete it.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. For fields with multiple options, select the option that best suits your needs.
4. For fields that require a date, select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. For fields that require a phone number, enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to register for a massage appointment.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be written.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Preferred Therapist** (`preferred_therapist`, `text`, required): If you have a preferred massage therapist, enter their name.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, `date`, required): Select a date for your massage appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, required): Select a time slot for your massage appointment.
* **Massage Duration** (`massage_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the length of your massage session from 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes.
* **Massage Type** (`massage_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of massage you would like, such as relaxation or deep tissue.
* **Pressure Preference** (`pressure_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of pressure you prefer for your massage, such as light, medium, or firm.
* **First Time Client** (`first_time_client`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes - First time" if this is your first chair massage with us.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select "No medical conditions" if you have no medical conditions, or "Yes - Will specify in comments" if you do have conditions.
* **Current Injuries or Pain Areas** (`current_injuries`, `text`, required): If you have any current pain or injuries, list them here.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your pregnancy status, such as "False" or "Yes - First trimester".
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required): Select where you heard about us, such as online search or social media.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required): If you have any special requests, list them here.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will pay for your massage, such as credit card or cash.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes - I understand" to acknowledge the cancellation policy.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as your signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of registration in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

Note: Please review the cancellation policy carefully before selecting "Yes - I understand".
