# Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Consultation Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before their massage therapy session. This information will help the massage therapist create a tailored treatment plan that meets the client's specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth.
3. Select your Medical Condition from the provided options (High Blood Pressure, Pregnancy, or Other).
4. If applicable, provide your Medical History in the text field.
5. Choose your Massage Therapy Goal (Pain Relief, Relaxation, Improved Flexibility, or Other).
6. Select your Massage Frequency (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
7. Choose the areas you would like to focus on during your massage (Back, Neck, Head, Shoulders, Hips).
8. If you have additional goals for your massage, select from the provided options (Pain Relief, Relaxation, Improved Flexibility, Stress Reduction).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your given name as it is written on your identification.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Enter your given name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it is written on your identification.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Enter your surname.
- **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Medical Condition**: Select your current medical condition from the options provided.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Select one of the available medical conditions.
- **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history.
  - Type: Note
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Provide any relevant medical history for the massage therapist's awareness.
- **Massage Therapy Goal**: Select the primary goal for your massage therapy session.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Select one of the available goals.
- **Massage Frequency**: Select your current level of massage frequency.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Select one of the available frequencies.
- **Massage Area**: Select the areas you would like to focus on during your massage.
  - Type: Select Multiple
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Select one or more of the available areas.
- **Additional Goals**: Select any additional goals for your massage therapy session.
  - Type: Select Multiple
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Select any additional goals for your massage.
