# Dermal Filler Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps healthcare professionals collect and store client information and treatment records for dermal filler treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter the client's last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Enter the client's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field.
4. Enter the date of the treatment in the `treatment_date` field.
5. Choose the type of dermal treatment administered from the dropdown menu in the `dermal_treatment` field.
6. Enter any additional notes about the treatment in the `treatment_notes` field.
7. Enter the date of the next scheduled visit in the `next_visit` field.
8. If necessary, enter the follow-up time in the `follow_up` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter the client's first name.
* **last_name**: Enter the client's last name.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter the client's date of birth.
* **treatment_date**: Enter the date of the treatment.
* **dermal_treatment**: Choose the type of dermal filler used from the dropdown menu:
	+ Dermal Filler
	+ Chemical Peel
	+ Facial Filler
	+ Skin Resurfacing
* **treatment_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the treatment.
* **next_visit**: Enter the date of the next scheduled visit.
* **follow_up**: If necessary, enter the time of the next scheduled follow-up appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the client's data.
- Use this form to improve your record-keeping and treatment planning.
- Customize the form as needed to fit your specific use case.
