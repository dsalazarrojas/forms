# Reflexology Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by clients or patients to schedule an appointment for reflexology treatment. The goal is to collect the necessary information for the practitioner to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all the required fields marked as **required**.
- Enter your name, email (if you want to be contacted by us), and reason for the visit.
- Select a date and time for your appointment.
- If you have a health insurance, please provide it, but it's not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule An Appointment**: Enter a brief description of your desired appointment date and time.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your appointment card.
* **Phone**: Optional contact information, enter your phone number if you'd like to be reached back.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you with appointment reminders or updates. Please note that your email will not be shared with anyone.
* **Select Date**: Choose a date for your appointment. Please ensure it's not too far in the future.
* **Select Time**: Choose a time for your appointment. Please consider our available time slots.
* **Reason for Visit**: Enter a brief description of the reason you're visiting us. This will help us to provide the best possible service.
* **Health Insurance**: If you have a health insurance, please provide it for our records.
* **Medical History**: Enter any relevant medical history you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when selecting your appointment date and time.
- If you need to cancel or change your appointment, please let us know as soon as possible.
- We'll do our best to accommodate your requests, but availability is subject to change.
