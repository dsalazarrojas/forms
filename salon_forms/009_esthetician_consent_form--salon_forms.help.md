# Esthetician Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Esthetician Consent Form is a document used to gather information and confirmations about the esthetician's identity, services, and equipment usage prior to the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and thorough information about the esthetician.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Details** (`salondetails`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is not required but allows the esthetician to provide a brief description of the salon where they work.
* **Esthetician Name** (`estheticianname`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your name as it appears on your professional documents and identification.
* **Esthetician Email** (`estheticianeid`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address associated with your professional account or identification.
* **Esthetician ID** (`estheticianeid`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter your professional identification number, if applicable.
* **Salon Equipment** (`salondequipment`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is not required but allows the esthetician to list any equipment they will be using during the appointment.
* **Salon Services** (`salonservices`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Choose 'Yes' if you will be providing services, 'No' if you will not be providing services.
* **Salon Dates** (`salondates`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of your appointment, if known.
* **Salon Comments** (`saloncomments`, `note`, required: `false`): Provide any additional comments or information about your appointment, if needed.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and thorough completion of all fields.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If any fields are unclear or you need assistance, contact your designated contact person.
