# Halloween Nail Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Nail Pre Order Form is a tool used by salons or nail technicians to collect information from clients interested in pre-ordering nail designs for the Halloween holiday. This form helps to gauge interest and collect contact information for follow-up marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Client Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person interested in pre-ordering a nail design for Halloween.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so the salon or nail technician can follow up with you about your interests and offer special promotions.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address to ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
* Make sure to enter your name and email address clearly and accurately.
* If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact the salon or nail technician using the provided contact information at the top of the page.
