# Mary Kay Beauty Profile By Julie Potts - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect your beauty profile information from clients. It helps us understand your preferences and needs to provide you with the best service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select whether you want to fill out a salon form.
2. If you want to fill out a salon form, provide your name.
3. If you want to provide an email address, enter it in the Email field.
4. If you want to provide a phone number, enter it in the Phone field.
5. If you want to add a note, enter it in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Forms** (`Salon Forms`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you want to fill out a salon form. This is a selection menu to choose if you want to provide additional information.
* **Salon Form** (`Salon Form`, `text`, required: false): If you selected to fill out a salon form, provide your name in this field.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, which will be used to address you personally.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): If you want to be contacted by email, enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): If you want to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional note or comments about your visit or preferences.
