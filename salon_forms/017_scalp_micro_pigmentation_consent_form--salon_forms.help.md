# Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect a client's informed consent for the Scalp Micro Pigmentation treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your client's information on Page 1 (Client Information).
2. Select all relevant points of understanding related to the treatment on Page 2 (Treatment Information).
3. Sign the form as either a client, parent/guardian, or practitioner on Page 3 (Consent).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information (Page 1)** (`client_info`, text, optional):
This field collects the client's personal details for their scalp micro-pigmentation treatment.
- **Treatment Information (Page 2)** (`treatment_info`, text, optional):
This section is where you should indicate your understanding of the treatment's potential effects and aftercare instructions.
- **Consent (Page 3)** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional):
Select all that apply to demonstrate your informed understanding of the treatment's possible outcomes and risks. Please note that this includes the risks of skin irritation and the necessity of post-treatment medication and care.
  • I understand that the Scalp Micro-Pigmentation treatment is a medical treatment that may cause skin irritation.
  • I understand that I will need to take certain medications after treatment.
  • I will follow the post-treatment care instructions as provided by the practitioner.
  • I understand the risks and side effects of the Scalp Micro-Pigmentation treatment.
- **Signature (Page 3)** (`signature`, select_multiple, optional):
Indicate your role by selecting client, parent/guardian, or practitioner.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the Scalp Micro-Pigmentation treatment before completing this form.
* If you are a parent/guardian, please ensure that the minor has understood the treatment's risks and side effects.
* This form must be completed before the commencement of the treatment.
