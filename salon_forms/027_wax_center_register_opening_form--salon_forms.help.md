# Wax Center Register Opening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wax Center Register Opening Form is used to gather information about the opening and closing times, total transactions, total revenue, and other relevant details of a salon.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the salons for which you are reporting data (Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each salon).
2. Enter the opening and closing times of the salon (in 24-hour format).
3. Enter the total number of transactions, total revenue, cash in hand, and cash out for the relevant salons.
4. Enter any notes, email address, phone number, and comments for the salons.
5. Enter any salons and employee observations.
6. Enter the total services offered, total services revenue, total transactions, salons total revenue, total transactions value, cash in hand value, cash out value, total services revenue value, salons total revenue value, and salons total value for the salons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salons** (`salons`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the salons for which you are reporting data (Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each salon).
* **Employees** (`employees`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the employees working at the salons (Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each employee).
* **Opening Time** (`opening_time`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the opening time of the salon in 24-hour format.
* **Closing Time** (`closing_time`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the closing time of the salon in 24-hour format.
* **Total Transactions** (`total_transactions`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total number of transactions.
* **Total Revenue** (`total_revenue`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total revenue.
* **Cash In Hand** (`cash_in_hand`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the cash in hand value.
* **Cash Out** (`cash_out`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the cash out value.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **Optional**): Enter any additional notes or comments about the salons.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the email address of the salons.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the salons.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **Required**): Enter any comments or observations about the salons.
* **Salons Observations** (`salons_observations`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any observations about the salons.
* **Employee Observations** (`employee_observations`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any observations about the employees.
* **Total Services Offered** (`total_services_offered`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total services offered.
* **Total Services Revenue** (`total_services_revenue`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total services revenue.
* **Total Transactions** (`total_transactions`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total transactions.
* **Salons Total Revenue** (`salons_total_revenue`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total revenue of the salons.
* **Total Transactions Value** (`total_transactions_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total transactions value.
* **Cash In Hand Value** (`cash_in_hand_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the cash in hand value.
* **Cash Out Value** (`cash_out_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the cash out value.
* **Total Services Revenue Value** (`total_services_revenue_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total services revenue value.
* **Salons Total Revenue Value** (`salons_total_revenue_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total revenue of the salons.
* **Salons Total Value** (`salons_total_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the total value of the salons.
* **Salons Average Value** (`salons_average_value`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the average value of the salons.
