# Hair Salon Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience at our salon. Your feedback will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve to provide the best service for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience.
2. Select the services you received at the salon.
3. Rate your stylist's performance.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback.
5. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback**: Select how satisfied you are with your overall experience at our salon. Choose from the following options:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Highly Dissatisfied
* **What Services Did You Receive?**: Select all the services you received at our salon. You can choose multiple services.
	+ Hair Cut
	+ Hair Style
	+ Colouring
	+ Highlights
	+ Chemical Treatments
	+ Hair Removal
	+ Nail Services
	+ Waxing
* **How Would You Rate Your Stylist?**: Rate your stylist's performance. Choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will allow us to contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you if we need to discuss your feedback.
* **Rate Your Experience**: Rate your experience at our salon. Choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Form End**: This is the final section of the form. Please review all your answers before submitting.
