# Permanent Makeup Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients about their preferences, medical history, and contact details before scheduling a permanent makeup session. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide personalized services based on your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately, including all relevant fields.
2. Please select the options that best fit your preferences.
3. If you have any special requests or notes, feel free to add them in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact number, where we can reach you for further questions or appointment scheduling.
* **Medical History**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may affect the services we provide, please list them here.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Permanent Makeup Experience**: If you have had any permanent makeup services before, please describe your experience.
* **Previous Permanent Makeup Artist**: If you have had permanent makeup services with another artist, please provide their name.
* **Expected Services**: Select all the services you would like to have during your session (Brows, Lips, Eyes, Lash, Tattoo, Nail).
* **Skin Type**: Please select your skin type (Fair, Light, Medium, Tan, Dark).
* **Skin Condition**: If you have any skin conditions that may affect the services we provide, please describe them here.
* **Hair Type**: Please select your hair type (Blonde, Brown, Red, Black).
* **Hair Condition**: If you have any hair conditions that may affect the services we provide, please describe them here.
* **Desired Color Scheme**: If you have any specific color preferences for your session, please describe them here.
* **Skin Tone**: Select your skin tone (Fair, Light, Medium, Tan, Dark).
* **Hair Dye**: If you dye your hair, please select all applicable colors (None, Blonde, Brown, Red, Black).
* **Previous Makeup Style**: If you have had any previous makeup services, please select the style you have had (Natural, Glam, Contour, Caricature, None).
* **Preferred Makeup Style**: Select your preferred makeup style (Natural, Glam, Contour, Caricature, None).
* **Face Shape**: Select your face shape (Round, Oval, Heart, Square, Diamond).
* **Hair Growth**: Select your hair growth rate (Slow, Medium, Fast, Very Fast, None).
* **Brow Style**: Select your brow style (Soft, Hard, Natural, Angled, None).
* **Expected Length**: Select the length of time you expect your session to take (Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4 hours or more).
* **Available Days**: Select all the days you are available for your session (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Preferred Time**: Select your preferred time slot for your session.
* **Available Time**: Select all the time slots you are available for your session.
* **Notes**: Any additional information or requests you would like to share with us.
