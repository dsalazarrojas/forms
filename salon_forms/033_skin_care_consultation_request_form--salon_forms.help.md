# Skin Care Consultation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users who are requesting a skin care consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field to ensure you provide the correct information.
3. Do not leave any required fields blank.
4. For select fields, choose the most relevant option from the list.
5. If you need help, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about your skin care concerns.
* **Skin Conditions** (`skin_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the skin conditions you are experiencing, such as acne, psoriasis, or rosacea.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Briefly describe your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or surgeries.
* **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, select_one, optional): If you are allergic to any medications, select them from the list.
* **Treatment Preferences** (`treatment_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of treatment you are interested in, such as chemical peels or microdermabrasion.
* **Appointment Scheduling** (`appointment_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Select a scheduling option for your consultation, such as same day or next week.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number so we can reach out to you about your consultation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your consultation.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes, contact us directly.
* We will respond to your consultation request as soon as possible.
