# Dermal Filler Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be completed by individuals seeking dermal filler procedures. It collects information on their preferences, procedure details, and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred dermal filler type from the options provided.
2. Enter your preferred procedure location in the text field.
3. Indicate your consent status by selecting "True" or "False".
4. Enter the date of consent in the "Consent Date" field using the date format.
5. Confirm if your consent has been submitted.
6. Answer additional general questions for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salongeneralquestions**: This field is for general questions related to your dermal filler procedure. Please provide your thoughts or concerns.
* **Dermalfillertype**: Choose your preferred type of dermal filler from the provided options.
* **Procedurelocation**: Enter the location where you would like to have the procedure.
* **Consentstatus**: Indicate your status of consent by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Consentdate**: Enter the date of your consent using the date format.
* **Consentsubmitted**: Confirm if your consent has been submitted.
* **Salongeneralquestions2**: This field is for additional general questions related to your dermal filler procedure. Please provide your thoughts or concerns.
* **Dermalfillertype2**: Choose your preferred type of dermal filler from the provided options (this field may not be necessary depending on your selection in the previous field).
* **Procedurelocation2**: Enter the location where you would like to have the procedure (this field may not be necessary depending on your selection in the previous field).

## Tips
* Please answer all required questions to ensure your consent is properly documented.
* Be as detailed as possible when answering general questions to provide the best possible care.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
