# ewc_time_card_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ewc_time_card_amendment_form" is a form used for amending time cards in the salon management system. It's designed to collect accurate and up-to-date information from authorized personnel, typically the submitter, to update or correct time card entries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are an authorized submitter before proceeding.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Review and verify your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Time Card Date**: Enter the date of the time card entry you want to amend.
* **Salutation**: Select your salutation (Mr, Mrs, Ms, Dr, Prof).
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Salutation Value**: Select your salutation value (Mr, Mrs, Ms, Dr, Prof).
* **EMail**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the time card entry.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form (chatjimmy, Other).
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Name**: Enter the name of who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By ID**: Enter the ID of who submitted the form.
* **Submit User**: Enter your name.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter ID**: Enter the ID of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes from the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Salutation**: Select the salutation of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Salutation Value**: Select the salutation value of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Enter the department of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitter Comments**: Leave any additional comments or notes from the person who submitted the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and verify all entries before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or system administrator for guidance.
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Do not submit incomplete or inaccurate information.
