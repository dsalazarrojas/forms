# Permanent Makeup Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Release Form is a document that helps you, the client, to decide how your photographs and videos taken by our salon can be used. This form is essential for us to understand your preferences and ensure that we respect your wishes regarding the use of your images.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and date of birth in the "Client Name" field.
2. Choose the "Release Date" when you are comfortable with the images being used.
3. If you are a client, select "Client" as the "Contact Person". If you are not a client, select "Salon" or "Other (please specify)".
4. Choose how you want to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail, or in person).
5. Select the specific use of your photographs and videos (e.g., online advertising, in-studio advertising, print advertising, or educational purposes).
6. If you have any other preferences or specifications, you can enter them in the "Other (specify)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (Client Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This is the primary field for identifying you as a client.
  - Your name will be used to verify your identity and associate you with the images.
- **Release Date** (Release Date, Date, Optional): Select the date when you are comfortable with us using your images.
  - This date will serve as a reference point for when you agreed to the use of your images.
- **Photographer Name** (Photographer Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the photographer who captured your images.
  - This field is for internal use and helps us track who took the images.
- **Photograph Release** (Photograph Release, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you want us to use your photographs:
  - All photographs taken at our studio
  - All photographs taken at your home or other location
  - All photographs taken at other studio or location
- **Video Release** (Video Release, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you want us to use your videos:
  - All videos recorded at our studio
  - All videos recorded at your home or other location
  - All videos recorded at other studio or location
- **Photo or Video Use** (Photo or Video Use, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the specific use of your images:
  - Online advertising (social media, etc.)
  - In studio advertising (e.g., website, brochures, etc.)
  - Print advertising (magazines, newspapers, etc.)
  - Educational or training purposes
  - All of the above
- **Contact Method** (Contact Method, Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred method of contact:
  - Email
  - Phone
  - Mail
  - Online messaging (e.g., text, chat)
  - In person
- **Contact Person** (Contact Person, Select One, Optional): Choose who will be your primary contact person:
  - Client (you)
  - Salon (our representative)
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other (specify)** (Other (specify), Text, Optional): If you have any other preferences or specifications, please enter them here.
  - This field is for any additional comments or requests you may have.
- **Other (phone)** (Other (phone), Text, Optional): Enter your preferred phone number.
  - This field is for any additional phone number you want to use for contact.
