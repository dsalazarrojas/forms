# Reflexology And Massage Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for reflexology and massage consultations. It helps the practitioner to understand the client's needs, preferences, and medical history to provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, and any other relevant details you would like to share with the practitioner.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history or conditions that may affect your experience during the session.
* **Massage Preferences** (`massage_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options from Massage, Reflexology, or Other to indicate your preferred type of service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the practitioner.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can send you updates or information about your session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Share any medical conditions you would like to disclose.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies you have.
* **Meditations** (`medications`, text, optional): Share any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, optional): Share any additional medical history or information.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' to indicate your consent to proceed with the session, or 'False' to indicate you would like to reschedule or cancel.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly and thoroughly to help us provide the best possible service.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* If you would like to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please let us know at least 24 hours in advance.
