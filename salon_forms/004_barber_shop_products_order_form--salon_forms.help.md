# Barber Shop Products Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to process orders for products from a barber shop. Fill out the form with the required information to ensure timely and accurate order processing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Order Date" field with the date you would like to order the products.
2. Enter the "Shop Name" of the barber shop where the order is being placed.
3. Provide the "Contact Person" name who is placing the order.
4. Enter the "Phone Number" of the contact person in case of any questions or inquiries.
5. Input the "Email Address" for order confirmation and tracking.
6. Enter the "Shipping Address" where the products will be delivered.
7. Enter the "City" where the shipping address is located.
8. Enter the "State" where the shipping address is located.
9. Enter the "ZIP Code" for the shipping address.
10. Select the "Shipping Method" from the available options.
11. Select the "Payment Method" from the available options.
12. If applicable, enter the "Promo Code" for any discounts.
13. Finally, enter "Special Instructions" or any additional information.
