# Hair Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Hair Quiz" form is used to collect information about a client's hair and skin conditions, schedule hair services, and store contact information for follow-up appointments.

## How To Complete This Form

### Client Information

* Please provide your name.
* You can enter any other contact information (phone number, email) to help us stay in touch.

### Hair Information

* Please select the condition of your hair (Baldness, Dandruff, Dryness, etc.).
* Please select the type of your hair (Afro, Bald, Curly, etc.).

### Skin Condition

* Please select the condition of your skin (Acne, Allergies, Eczema, etc.).

### Client Satisfaction

* Please select your level of satisfaction with our services (Dissatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied).

### Service

* Please select the service you would like to schedule (Chemical Treatment, Color, Cut, etc.).

### Contact Information

* Please select the type of contact information you would like to provide (Business, Home, None, Other).
* Please enter your phone number.
* You can also enter your email address (optional).

### Follow-up

* Please select the follow-up date for your scheduled appointment.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can provide you with the best services.
* If you have any additional comments or requests, please use the "Notes" field.
* You can also add a follow-up date if you would like to schedule another appointment.
