# YoniVaginal Steam Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The YoniVaginal Steam Intake Form is designed to capture essential information from our customers to provide a personalized vaginal steam experience.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Name** in the text box labeled "name". This will help us get to know you better.
2. Enter your **email** address in the email field. We'll use this to contact you with any updates or instructions.
3. Select one or more **Contact** method(s) from the dropdown list. This will help us reach out to you if we need more information.
4. Enter your **Arrival Time** in the time field. Please format it in 24-hour time (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
5. You may choose to report your **Arrival Time** again if your arrival time changes.
6. Choose the type of service you would like: **Haircut**, **Color**, **Trim**, or **Style**.
7. Enter the **length** of your appointment in number format (e.g., 30 for 30 minutes).
8. Provide a brief description of your **duration** of visit in the text box.
9. If you have any **Medical History** that may be relevant to your visit, please include it in the text box.
