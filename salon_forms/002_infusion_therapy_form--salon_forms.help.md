# infusion_therapy_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The infusion_therapy_form is a tool to collect patient information and infusion therapy details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Collect the patient's demographic information, including their name.
2. Provide the patient's contact information, including their contact number and email.
3. Enter any relevant medical information.
4. Specify the infusion details, including the infusion type and frequency.
5. Enter the contact information for the medical provider.
6. Complete the facility address field if applicable.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact number and email.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Infusion Details** (`infusion_details`, text, required: false): Specify the infusion details.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Infusion** (`date_of_infusion`, date, required: false): Enter the date of infusion.
* **Infusion Type** (`infusion_type`, select_one, required: true): Specify whether the patient receives infusions or not.
* **Infusion Frequency** (`infusion_frequency`, number, required: false): Enter the infusion frequency (if applicable).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the patient's contact email.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the medical provider.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required: false): Enter the facility address.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If a field is not relevant, you can leave it blank or enter 'N/A' (not applicable).
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact your supervisor or support team.
