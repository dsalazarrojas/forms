# Skin Care Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Consent Form is a critical document that requires customers to provide their consent for each skin care procedure they wish to undergo. It helps to ensure that customers understand the treatment and its potential risks and side effects, thereby providing informed consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Enter a brief description of the skin care procedure**: In this text field, please type in a short description of the skin care procedure you are requesting. This will help us understand the scope of the treatment.
2. **Choose your consent for the procedure**: Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu to indicate your consent for the procedure.
3. **Sign and date the form**: Please provide your signature and date to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Care Consent (1)** (`skin_care_consent`, `text`, required): Enter your consent for the skin care procedure. Select "Yes" to indicate that you understand and consent to the procedure.
* **Procedure Name (2)** (`procedure_name`, `text`, required): Please enter a brief description of the skin care procedure.
* **Consent Given (3)** (`consent_given`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu to indicate your consent for the procedure.
* **Signature (4)** (`signature`, `text`, required): Please provide your signature to confirm your consent. This is a mandatory field to ensure your consent is binding and valid.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you carefully read and understand each question before providing your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the skin care procedure, please do not hesitate to ask your practitioner for clarification.
* This form should be completed once for each skin care procedure. If you need to make changes or corrections, please inform your practitioner before submitting the form.
