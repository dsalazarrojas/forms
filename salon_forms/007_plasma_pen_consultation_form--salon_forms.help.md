# plasma_pen_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plasma Pen Consultation Form is used to collect information from clients to better understand their needs and schedule for plasma pen treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Client Name.
2. Select your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Contact Number.
4. Enter your Email Address.
5. Provide a brief description of your Medical History.
6. Describe your Treatment Details.
7. Select your preferred schedule.
8. Select the next steps involved in the treatment process.
9. Indicate any Medical Conditions or Medical Treatments that you have or need.
10. Indicate any other Medical Treatments or Medical Conditions that you have or need.
11. Select the area of treatment.
12. Indicate whether you have had a Client Consultation with the clinic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number for the clinic to contact you.
- **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for the clinic to contact you.
- **Medical History** (Medical History, note, required/optional): Provide a brief description of any medical history that you have or need.
- **Treatment Details** (Treatment Details, text, required/optional): Describe your skin concerns and goals.
- **Preferred Schedule** (Preferred Schedule, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the available times you are available for treatment.
  - Available: Available for treatment at any time.
  - Unavailable: Unavailable for treatment at all times.
  - Preferential: Preferable time for treatment.
  - Morning: Available for treatment in the morning.
  - Afternoon: Available for treatment in the afternoon.
- **Next Steps** (Next Steps, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the next steps involved in the treatment process.
  - Consultation: Consultation with a medical professional.
  - Appointment: Scheduled appointment for treatment.
  - Both: Both consultation and appointment.
  - None: No next steps involved.
- **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have any medical conditions.
  - Yes: Yes, you have a medical condition.
  - No: No, you do not have a medical condition.
- **Medical Treatment** (Medical Treatment, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have undergone medical treatment.
  - Yes: Yes, you have undergone medical treatment.
  - No: No, you have not undergone medical treatment.
- **Medical Treatment 2** (Medical Treatment 2, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have undergone another medical treatment.
  - Yes: Yes, you have undergone another medical treatment.
  - No: No, you have not undergone another medical treatment.
- **Medical History 2** (Medical History 2, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether you have any other medical history.
  - Yes: Yes, you have another medical history.
  - No: No, you do not have another medical history.
- **Medical Treatment 3** (Medical Treatment 3, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have undergone another medical treatment.
  - Yes: Yes, you have undergone another medical treatment.
  - No: No, you have not undergone another medical treatment.
- **Treatment Area** (Treatment Area, select_one, required/optional): Select the area of treatment.
  - Yes: Yes, you need treatment in this area.
  - No: No, you do not need treatment in this area.
- **Client Consultation** (Client Consultation, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have had a client consultation with the clinic.
  - Yes: Yes, you have had a client consultation.
  - No: No, you have not had a client consultation.
