# Permanent Make Up Consent Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a signed consent from the client for a permanent make-up procedure. It collects personal information and ensures that the client understands and agrees to the terms of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and date.
2. Select the procedure you are consenting to from the drop-down menu.
3. Sign the "Consent" field to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
4. Read and sign the "Terms and Conditions" field to confirm that you have read and understand them.
5. Sign the form to indicate your agreement to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please fill out your first name. This is the first name by which you are known.
* **Last Name**: Please fill out your last name. This is the last name by which you are known.
* **Email**: Please fill out your email address.
* **Phone**: Please fill out your phone number.
* **Procedure**: Please select the permanent make-up procedure for which you are consenting.
* **Artist**: Please fill out the name of the artist performing the procedure.
* **Date**: Please select the date of the procedure.
* **Signature**: Please sign below to indicate that you understand and agree to the procedure.
* **Consent**: Please select the option that indicates you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and sign below to confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign below to indicate your agreement to the procedure.
* **Artist Signature**: Please sign below to indicate your agreement to the procedure.
* **Date Signed**: Please select the date on which you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**: Please select the time on which you signed the form.
* **Client Notes**: Please add any additional comments or information as needed.
