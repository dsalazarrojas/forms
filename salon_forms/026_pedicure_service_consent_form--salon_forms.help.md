# pedicure_service_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a pedicure service consent form used in salons to gather information from clients and ensure they understand the service being performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the client.
2. Select the correct category of service (Pedicure Service).
3. Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting (optional).
4. Enter your contact information (Phone and Email).
5. Select the date and time of the service (DD/MM/YY).
6. Indicate whether you have agreed to the service (True/False).
7. Add any additional notes about the service.
8. Enter the salon name and the technician performing the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pedicure Service Consent Form** (1, text, required/optional):
	* This is the title of the form.
* **Category** (2, select_one, required/optional):
	* Select the correct category for the pedicure service. Options are:
		+ Salon Forms
		+ Nail Forms
		+ Other
* **Description** (3, text, required/optional):
	* Enter a brief description of the service you are requesting.
* **Client Name** (4, text, required/optional):
	* Enter your name as the client.
* **Phone** (5, text, required/optional):
	* Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email** (6, email, required/optional):
	* Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Service** (7, date, required/optional):
	* Select the date of the service.
* **Time of Service** (8, time, required/optional):
	* Select the time of the service.
* **Service Agreed** (9, select_one, required/optional):
	* Indicate whether you have agreed to the service. Options are:
		+ True
		+ False
* **Notes** (10, note, required/optional):
	* Add any additional notes about the service.
* **Salon Name** (11, text, required/optional):
	* Enter the name of the salon.
* **Technician** (12, text, required/optional):
	* Enter the name of the technician performing the service.
* **Service Details** (13, text, required/optional):
	* Enter any other details about the service.
