# Hair Salon New Client Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from new clients about their hair salon preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all required fields.
2. Fill in additional information where applicable.
3. Confirm that the provided information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Style Preferences (2)**: Select any hair styles you are interested in. (Optional)
* **Hair Type (3)**: Select the type of hair you have (e.g. Dry, Oily, Curly, Straight).
* **Hair Texture (4)**: Select the texture of your hair (e.g. Fine, Coarse, Medium).
* **Face Shape (5)**: Select the shape of your face (e.g. Oval, Round, Square, Heart-Shaped).
* **Hair Length (6)**: Enter your hair length in inches or centimeters. (Optional)
* **Hair Color (7)**: Enter your hair color.
* **Preferred Color Scheme (8)**: Select the color scheme you prefer (e.g. Neutral, Bright, Vibrant).
* **Hair Styling Frequency (9)**: Select how often you get your hair styled (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Notes (10)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about your visit.
* **Email (11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments (13)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your visit.
* **Preferred Language (14)**: Select the language you prefer to communicate in (e.g. English, Spanish, French).
* **Preferred Time (15)**: Select your preferred time for the appointment. (Optional)
* **Preferred Date (16)**: Select your preferred date for the appointment. (Optional)
* **Stylist Name (17)**: Enter the name of your preferred stylist. (Optional)
* **Stylist Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of your preferred stylist. (Optional)
* **Stylist Email (19)**: Enter the email of your preferred stylist. (Optional)
* **Appointment Duration (20)**: Enter the length of your appointment in minutes. (Optional)
* **Additional Staff (21)**: Select any additional staff members you would like to see. (e.g. Stylist, Barber)
* **Special Requests (22)**: Select any special requests you have for your visit. (e.g. Color, Cut, Style)
* **Special Services (23)**: Select any special services you require. (e.g. Haircut, Coloring, Highlights)
* **Services (24)**: Select the services you require. (e.g. Haircut, Coloring, Highlights)
* **Confirm (25)**: Confirm that the provided information is correct.

Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the salon staff.
