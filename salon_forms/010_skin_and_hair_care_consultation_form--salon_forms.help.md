# Skin And Hair Care Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients about their skin and hair care needs and preferences. This information will help practitioners provide more effective and personalized care to the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your skin type from the dropdown menu.
2. Describe your skin condition in the text field.
3. Select your hair condition from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide your treatment history in the text field.
5. List any medical conditions you may have.
6. List any medications you are currently taking.
7. Select any allergies you may have.
8. Select your current hair treatment in the dropdown menu.
9. Describe your hair care plan.
10. Select your skin care frequency in the dropdown menu.
11. Select your hair care frequency in the dropdown menu.
12. Select a follow-up date and time.
13. Select how you would like to be followed up with (phone, email, or text).
14. Enter your client name.
15. Enter your client phone number.
16. Enter your client email.
17. Enter your client address.
18. Enter your practitioner name.
19. Enter your practitioner phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Type** (1): Please select your skin type from the dropdown menu.
* **Skin Condition** (2): Describe your skin condition in as much detail as possible.
* **Hair Condition** (3): Select your hair condition from the dropdown menu.
* **Treatment History** (4): Please provide a detailed account of your treatment history, including any previous treatments and conditions.
* **Medical Conditions** (5): List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Medications** (6): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Allergies** (7): Select any allergies you may have.
* **Hair Treatment** (8): Select your current hair treatment from the dropdown menu.
* **Skin Care Plan** (9): Describe your skin care plan, including any products or routines you follow.
* **Hair Care Plan** (10): Describe your hair care plan, including any products or routines you follow.
* **Follow Up** (11): Select a date and time to follow up with the practitioner.
* **Follow Up Method** (12): Select how you would like to be followed up with (phone, email, or text).
* **Client Name** (13): Please enter your name.
* **Client Phone Number** (14): Please enter your phone number.
* **Client Email** (15): Please enter your email.
* **Client Address** (16): Please enter your address.
* **Practitioner Name** (17): Please enter the name of your practitioner.
* **Practitioner Phone Number** (18): Please enter the phone number of your practitioner.
