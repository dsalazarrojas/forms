# Hair Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Consultation Form is designed to gather essential information from clients before and after their hair consultation appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Make sure to select the correct hair type from the options provided.
3. Choose the desired services from the options.
4. Enter your current hair color and length.
5. Select your hair style preference.
6. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
7. Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hair Type**: Select the type of hair you have from the options provided.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`): Enter your first name, last name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Services**: Choose the services you are interested in or would like to discuss during your consultation.
* **Hair Color**: Enter your current hair color.
* **Hair Length**: Enter your current hair length.
* **Hair Style**: Select the style you prefer from the options provided.
* **Appointment Date**: Select a date and time for your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Select a time slot for your appointment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach out.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred contact method.
* **Preferred Contact Time**: Select the time of day you prefer to be contacted.

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your salon staff for assistance.
