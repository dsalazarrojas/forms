# Pedicure Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Pedicure Consultation Form, its sections, and fields. It helps clients understand how to complete the form correctly and efficiently, ensuring their needs are met during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Client Information** (page 1): Enter your name and contact details in this section. This is optional but helps us keep in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information (page 1)
* **Client Information**: This section is where you provide your personal details such as your name and contact information. Please enter your name, email, and phone number accurately so we can get in touch with you for any further consultation or appointment updates.

### nail_condition (page 2)
* **Nail Condition**: This section is concerned with the current condition of your nails. Please select one or more of the options that best describe your nail condition from the options: **Nails are healthy and well-maintained, Nails have ridges, Nails have chips or cracks, Nails have fungal infection, Other (please specify)**. This will help us understand your nail needs better.

### nail_shape (page 2)
* **Nail Shape**: Choose the shape you desire for your nails from the options: **Oval, Square, Round, Other (please specify)**. This will help us understand what shape you prefer for your nail treatment.

### nail_color (page 2)
* **Nail Color**: This is where you select your preferred nail color from the options: **Red, Pink, Purple, Blue, Green, Yellow, Other (please specify)**. Please choose one or more colors that appeal to you.

### Service Request (page 3)
* **Service Request**: Describe in the text field what nail services you would like. This could be a pedicure, a nail repair, or any other specific service you need.

### nail_length (page 3)
* **Nail Length**: If applicable, please enter the length of your nails or the desired length if you've had them cut recently.

### Follow-up (page 3)
* **Follow-up**: This is the date you would like to schedule for your next pedicure appointment.

### Appointment Time (page 4)
* **Appointment Time**: Choose the time of day you prefer for your appointment from the options provided.

### Notes (page 4)
* **Notes**: This is an optional section for any additional comments or special requests you may have. Please feel free to write any notes that are essential for your pedicure.

### Email (page 4)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep in touch with you.

### Phone (page 4)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for any further clarification or updates.

### Follow-up Appointment Date (page 4)
* **Follow-up Appointment Date**: If you would like to book a follow-up appointment, enter the date you would prefer.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form as accurately and completely as possible so we can provide you with the best service.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact us and we will guide you through the process.
- The salon staff will review your form and get back to you as soon as possible to finalize your pedicure appointment.
