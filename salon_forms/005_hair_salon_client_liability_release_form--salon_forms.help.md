# Hair Salon Client Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Client Liability Release Form is a document that you need to complete before undergoing any treatment or service at our salon. It's essential to read and understand the contents of this form carefully before signing it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and provide accurate information where required.
2. Complete all the fields provided in the form.
3. Sign the form once you have read and understood all the contents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Accepts Risk**: Please indicate that you understand and accept the risks associated with the treatments and services provided by our salon.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect your health or safety during the treatment or service.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have that may be relevant to the treatment or service.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking that may be relevant to the treatment or service.
* **Emergency Medical Authorization**: Please indicate whether you agree to accept all risk or not. If you do not agree, please provide a reason.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their contact information.
* **Insurance Info**: If you have insurance, please provide your insurance provider's name, policy number, and contact information.
* **Insurance Policy**: If you have an insurance policy, please provide the policy number.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign this form to confirm you have read and understood all the contents.
* **Date**: Please date and sign the form below.
* **Client Signature**: Please sign this form below.
* **Staff Signature**: Please sign this form below as a staff member of the salon.
* **Date**: Please date and sign the form below.

Remember to review and sign the form carefully before submitting it to the salon staff. Your cooperation and understanding are greatly appreciated.
