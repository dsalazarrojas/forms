# Microdermabrasion Treatment Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record information about a microdermabrasion treatment given to a client. The form is used to document the treatment type, date administered, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Select the type of treatment given from the "Treatment Type" dropdown list.
3.  Enter the date the treatment was administered.
4.  Enter any notes about the treatment in the "Notes" field.
5.  Select the client's salutation (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr., or Prof.).
6.  Enter the client's phone number.
7.  Enter the client's email address.
8.  Enter the contact person's name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name.
*   **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of treatment given (Microdermabrasion, Chemical Peel, or Laser Treatment).
*   **Date Administered** (`date_administered`, date, optional): Enter the date the treatment was administered.
*   **Prophets Used** (`prophets_used`, text, optional): Note any prosthesis used during the treatment.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the treatment.
*   **Salutation** (`salutation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the client's salutation (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr., or Prof.).
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure accurate records.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to your clinic's policies and procedures.
*   This form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel only.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
