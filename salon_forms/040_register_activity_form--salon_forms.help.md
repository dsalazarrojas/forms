# Register Activity Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Register Activity Form" is a tool used by salon staff to inform the management team about various activities, events, or updates. It is essential to keep the management team informed and up-to-date with current information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Select One" field to indicate the type of activity or event you are registering (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
2.  Choose a date and time for the activity or event in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
3.  Add a note about the activity or event in the "Note" field.
4.  Enter an email address for contact purposes in the "Email" field.
5.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field for easy communication.
6.  Indicate whether the activity involves a drop or not in the "Without or With Drop" field.
7.  Fill in the "Register Closing Form" field with any additional information or comments.
8.  Add any necessary notes or information in the "Register Closing Form Hint" field.
9.  Enter any details about shift changes in the "Shift Change Form" field.
10.  Provide any additional information or notes in the "Shift Change Form Hint" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Register Opening Form** (`register_opening_form`, text, not required): This field is the opening section of the form where you can add a brief description or note about the activity or event.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you are registering a specific activity or event, or choose "No" to indicate that it is not a priority.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, not required): This field allows you to register multiple activities or events at once by selecting "Yes" for each one.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the activity or event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the activity or event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Provide a brief note or comment about the activity or event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Without or With Drop** (`without_drop`, text, not required): Indicate whether the activity involves a drop or not.
* **Register Closing Form** (`register_closing_form`, text, not required): Fill in any additional information or comments about the activity or event.
* **Register Closing Form Hint** (`register_closing_form_hint`, text, not required): Add any necessary notes or information about the activity or event.
* **Shift Change Form** (`shift_change_form`, text, not required): Enter any details about shift changes.
* **Shift Change Form Hint** (`shift_change_form_hint`, text, not required): Add any additional information or notes about shift changes.

**Tips**

* Make sure to complete all fields clearly and concisely to ensure that the management team has all the necessary information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or omissions.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the management team for guidance.
