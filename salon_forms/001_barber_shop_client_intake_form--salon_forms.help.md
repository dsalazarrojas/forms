# Barber Shop Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their personal and grooming preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your full name, phone number, and email address (if you provide an email address).
2.  Select your hair type (Straight, Wavy, Curly, Coily, or Combination).
3.  Choose your preferred hair length (Short, Medium, Long, or Very Short).
4.  Select your preferred fade style (High Fade, Mid Fade, Low Fade, No Fade, or Skin Fade).
5.  Choose your preferred beard style (Clean Shaven, Trimmed Beard, Full Beard, Stubble, or Shaped).
6.  Answer whether you had a haircut in the last 3 months.
7.  If necessary, list the hair care products you use at home.
8.  Indicate if you have any product allergies or sensitivities.
9.  Provide information about any scalp conditions or allergies.
10.  If this is your first visit, select True. Otherwise, select False or Regular Customer.
11.  If you prefer a specific barber, please specify their name.
12.  Choose your preferred appointment time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, or Flexible).
13.  Add any special requests or notes.
14.  Review the consent section and select I Agree.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for appointment confirmations.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for appointment reminders (optional).
* **Hair Type**: Select your hair texture.
* **Hair Length Preference**: Choose your preferred hair length.
* **Fade Style Preference**: Select your preferred fade style.
* **Beard Styling Preference**: Choose your preferred beard style.
* **Had a Haircut in the Last 3 Months**: Indicate if you've had a haircut recently.
* **Hair Care Products**: List any hair care products you use at home (if necessary).
* **Product Allergies or Sensitivities**: Indicate any product allergies or sensitivities.
* **Scalp Condition**: Provide information about any scalp conditions or allergies.
* **Is This Your First Visit?**: Indicate if this is your first visit.
* **Preferred Barber**: Specify your preferred barber's name (if applicable).
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Choose your preferred appointment time.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Add any additional information or requests.
* **Consent**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form to ensure the best experience and service.
* Provide any necessary information for your comfort and safety.
* If you're unsure about a field, please ask a staff member for clarification.
* The barbers will use this information to tailor your visit to your preferences and needs.
