# Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Consultation Form is a tool used by salon staff to collect information from clients and schedule appointments. This form is used to capture client details, service preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's contact information, including their name, contact number, and email address (if applicable).
2. Select the service type and details you require from the available options.
3. Choose your hair type and condition.
4. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
5. Leave any additional notes or messages for the staff.
6. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: The client's name.
* **Contact Number**: The client's phone number.
* **Email**: The client's email address (if applicable).
* **Service Type**: The type of service the client is interested in (e.g., haircut, coloring, etc.).
* **Service Details**: A description of the service the client requires (e.g., "cut and style").
* **Hair Type**: The type of hair the client has (e.g., blonde, brunette, etc.).
* **Hair Condition**: The condition of the client's hair (e.g., dry, curly, etc.).
* **Appointment Date**: The date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: The time of the appointment.
* **Consultation Note**: Notes from the consultation with the client.
* **Message**: A message for the client to leave for the staff.
* **Service Price**: The price of the service.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Client Feedback**: Feedback from the client after the service.
* **Service Staff**: The staff member assigned to the client's appointment.
* **Client Address**: The client's address (if applicable).
* **Service Notes**: Notes for the service performed.
* **Service Time**: The time it takes to perform the service.
* **Staff Note**: Notes from the staff member.
* **Email Staff**: The email address of the staff member.
* **Client Signature**: A signature for the client.
* **Staff Signature**: A signature for the staff member.
* **Payment Status**: The status of the payment.
* **Payment Date**: The date of the payment.
* **Payment Method Used**: The method of payment used for the service.

## Tips
Make sure to review and confirm your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and ensure that your appointment is scheduled correctly.
