# Digital Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Consultation Form is a tool used by salon staff to collect information from clients and schedule appointments. This form is used to capture client details, service preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's contact information, including their name, contact number, and email address (if applicable).
2. Select the service type and details you require from the available options.
3. Choose your hair type and condition.
4. Choose a date and time for your appointment.
5. Leave any additional notes or messages for the staff.
6. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.
