# Cosmetology Client Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a cosmetology service consultation. It gathers essential details such as client contact information, service preferences, and payment method to ensure a smooth and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **Client Name**, **Client Email**, **Client Phone**, **Service Price**, **Stylist Name**, **Stylist Phone**, **Salon Address**, and **Payment Method**. These fields are marked as required and must be completed.
2. Select the **Service Needed** option that best suits your needs.
3. Choose the **Hair Length** and **Hair Type** that describe your hair.
4. Select your preferred **Hair Color**.
5. Enter your **Service Start Date** and **Service End Date** (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional **Service Start Time** and **Service End Time** (if applicable).
7. Enter any relevant **Notes**, **Client Note**, and **Service Notes**.
8. Finally, review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text): Enter your name for our records.
* **Client Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Client Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Service Needed** (Select One): Choose the type of service you require, such as cutting, coloring, chemical treatment, makeup, waxing, nail care, or facial.
* **Hair Length** (Select Multiple): Select the length of your hair, which can be short, medium, or long.
* **Hair Type** (Select One): Choose the type of hair you have, which can be dry, curly, wavy, straight, or other.
* **Hair Color** (Select Multiple): Select your hair color, which can be brown, black, blond, red, or other.
* **Prophet** (Text): This field is not currently used, so you can leave it blank.
* **Service Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to start the service.
* **Service Start Time** (Time): Enter the time you would like to start the service.
* **Service End Date** (Date): Enter the date you would like to end the service.
* **Service End Time** (Time): Enter the time you would like to end the service.
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your service.
* **Client Note** (Note): Enter any comments or notes from the client.
* **Service Notes** (Note): Enter any service notes or comments.
* **Salon ID** (Text): This field is not used for client input, so you can leave it blank.
* **Stylist Name** (Text): Enter the name of the stylist providing the service.
* **Stylist Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the stylist providing the service.
* **Salon Address** (Text): Enter the address of the salon where the service will be provided.
* **Payment Method** (Text): Choose the method of payment you prefer.
* **Service Price** (Number): Enter the price of the service.
* **Stylist Note** (Note): This field is not used for client input, so you can leave it blank.
* **Client Note** (Note): This field is not used for client input, so you can leave it blank.
* **Service Notes** (Note): This field is not used for client input, so you can leave it blank.
