# Client Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Disclosure Form is a necessary step before receiving services. It outlines important information, including our liability for clothing damage, 24-hour cancellation policy, and photo release consent. This form ensures that both you and us are on the same page, protecting you and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Client Full Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your service in the "Date of Service" field. This is the date you will be receiving the service.
3. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, select "I acknowledge" in the "Allergy Disclosure Acknowledgement" field.
4. If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please list them in the free-text field "Medical Conditions and Concerns" (not present in this YAML but implied in the actual form).
5. Check "I understand" in the "Clothing Damage Acknowledgement" field to confirm you are aware of our liability for clothing damage.
6. Check "I agree" in the "Cancellation Policy Acknowledgement" field to confirm you are aware of our 24-hour cancellation policy and any associated fees.
7. Check "I consent" in the "Photo Release" field to confirm you are okay with before and after photos being taken for professional use and social media.
8. If you wish to be contacted in case of an emergency, enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
9. Sign your name in the "Client Signature" field. This is your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date you will be receiving the service.
* **Service Disclosure and Liability**: This is a section that clarifies our liability for clothing damage. Please take a moment to read this section carefully.
* **Clothing Damage Acknowledgement**: This confirms that you are aware of our liability for clothing damage. 
* **Allergy Disclosure Acknowledgement**: This confirms that you will inform your stylist about any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgement**: This confirms that you are aware of our 24-hour cancellation policy and any associated fees. 
* **Photo Release**: This confirms that you are okay with before and after photos being taken for professional use and social media.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you want us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Client Signature**: This is your digital signature. Please type your full name as it appears on your identification.
