# skin_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from clients or patients for the purpose of obtaining their consent for a skin treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Enter a valid email address
4. Provide your Date of Birth
5. Share your Medical History
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for the consent
7. Sign your name in the Signature field

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your personal documents. Please fill it in carefully.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your personal documents. Please fill it in carefully.
* **Email**: A valid email address that can be used to contact you. Please make sure to enter it correctly.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of your birth in the format of day/month/year (DD/MM/YYYY). Please enter it correctly.
* **Medical History**: A brief summary of your medical history, including any allergies, previous treatments, or medical conditions. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Consent**: Please select "Yes" to give your consent for the treatment, or "No" if you do not wish to proceed. This is a required field.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name in this field to confirm your consent. This is a required field.
