# Salon Pre Appointment Health Check - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from clients before their appointment at a salon. This information will be used to contact the client and confirm their appointment, as well as to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have completed the "Client Registration Form" (not included here) to be eligible to book an appointment.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Double-check the information you provide to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional: false)
This is the field to provide your name, which will be used to identify you in our system. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required/optional: false)
This is the field to provide your contact phone number, which will be used to contact you to confirm your appointment and for any last-minute changes or notifications.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional: false)
This is the field to provide your email address, which will be used to contact you and confirm your appointment. Please ensure that your email is up to date and correct.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional: false)
This is the field to provide your date of birth, which will be used to ensure that we have up-to-date and accurate information about you in our system.
* **Client Contact Details** (`salon_pre_appointment_health_check_page`, text, required/optional: false)
Please fill in this field with any additional contact details that we should know before your appointment, such as your address or any medical conditions that may affect your appointment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may affect your appointment, please list them in the "Client Contact Details" field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
