# Microneedling Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Microneedling Consultation Form is designed to gather information from clients for a microneedling consultation. It is a crucial step in the process of determining the best course of treatment for a client's skin condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info** (Client Info, text, required): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Skin Type** (Skin Type, select_one, required): Please select your skin type from the provided options (Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive, Acne Prone).
* **Skin Condition** (Skin Condition, text, required): Describe your current skin condition (e.g., rosacea, eczema, etc.). Be as detailed as possible so that the practitioner can understand your situation.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required): List any medical conditions that may impact treatment, such as allergies or sensitivities.
* **Treatment Goals** (Treatment Goals, text, required): Specify your goals for microneedling treatment.
* **Contrainications** (Contrainications, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you are taking any medications that may impact treatment.
* **Num Microneedling Sessions 1-23** (Num Microneedling Sessions, select_one, optional): These fields are used to determine the number of sessions needed for your treatment. Please select the number of sessions that best suits your needs or choose "Not Sure" if you are unsure.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions carefully and honestly to ensure the practitioner can provide the best possible treatment.
* If you are unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask the practitioner for clarification.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the practitioner can provide accurate information.
* If you have any medical concerns or questions before starting treatment, please address them with the practitioner.
* The number of sessions required may change based on your specific situation. If you select "Not Sure" and later determine that more or fewer sessions are needed, let the practitioner know so they can adjust your treatment plan accordingly.
