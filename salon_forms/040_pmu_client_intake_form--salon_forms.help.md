# PMU Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PMU Client Intake Form is a crucial tool for our salon staff to gather essential information from clients. This form helps us understand clients' needs, preferences, and contact details, ensuring we provide personalized services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the correct format (e.g., user@example.com).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
5. Select a date from the calendar for your appointment.
6. Choose a time for your appointment.
7. Enter any additional **Notes** you'd like to share with our salon staff.
8. Select all the **Categories** that apply to your appointment (e.g., Hair, Nail, Skincare).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Date**: Select a date from the calendar for your appointment.
* **Time**: Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments you'd like to share with our staff.
* **Category**: A **Select One** field where you can choose only one category that applies to your appointment (e.g., Yes for Hair).
*   **Category (Select Multiple)**: A field where you can choose multiple categories that apply to your appointment (e.g., Hair, Skincare).

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields (indicated by \*).
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our staff for assistance.
- For appointments, select a date and time that suits you best.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in the **Notes** field.
- If you have any concerns or requests, please don't hesitate to contact us.

Note: This help guide assumes that the form will not be used for other purposes. If there are any changes to the form or its purpose, this guide will be updated accordingly.
