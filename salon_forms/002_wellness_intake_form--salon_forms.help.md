# Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wellness Intake Form is a required form to be completed by individuals seeking care or services at our salon. The form will help us understand your overall wellness, including your medical history and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
*   Ensure you complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`name`, `text`, \*):
    Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`,):
    Enter your primary contact number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`,):
    Enter your email address.
*   **Health Condition** (`health_condition`, `text`,):
    List any known health conditions or medical concerns you have.
*   **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, `text`,):
    Provide details about your medical history, including any previous conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`,):
    List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
*   **Medication** (`medication`, `text`,):
    List any prescribed medications, including their dosage and frequency.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`,):
    Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`,):
    Enter the contact number for your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`,):
    Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`,):
    Enter the name of your primary doctor or healthcare provider.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and thorough when completing this form.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
*   The information collected on this form will be used to provide you with personalized care and services.
