# Skin Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Care Survey is a set of questions designed to collect information about your skin care habits, concerns, and preferences. Please take a few minutes to answer these questions to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your skin care routine, treatment frequency, and goals.
2. For the "Concerns" section, select all the issues that apply to you.
3. In the "Perceptions" section, choose how you would describe your skin.
4. Select one option for "Skin Treatment Behavior" and "Skin Treatment Clinics" if applicable.
5. Choose all relevant skin treatment products for your answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Care Routine** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the options that describe your current skin care routine (e.g., washing your face twice a day, using a moisturizer, etc.).
* **Skin Treatment Frequency** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please choose how often you receive skin treatments (e.g., every 4 weeks, every 6 months, etc.).
* **Skin Care Goals** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the goals you have for your skin care (e.g., reduce acne, improve texture, etc.).
* **Concerns** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the concerns you have about your skin (e.g., aging, sensitivity, etc.).
* **Perceptions** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Describe your skin by selecting all the adjectives that apply to you (e.g., sensitive, hydrated, etc.).
* **Skin Treatment Behavior** (`6`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select one option for how often you change your skin treatment behavior (e.g., often, rarely, etc.).
* **Skin Treatment Clinics** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the skin treatment clinics you have visited (e.g., dermatologists, spas, etc.).
* **Skin Treatment Products** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the skin treatment products you use (e.g., creams, serums, etc.).

Note: You can select all that apply for the multiple-choice questions, and choose one option for the single-choice questions.
