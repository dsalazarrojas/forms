# Massage Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Intake Form is a tool used to collect necessary information from patients before their massage appointments. This information includes contact details, appointment date, and massage preferences to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Be accurate and thorough when providing information, as this will help us provide the best possible service for you.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (text): A field where you should enter your name.
* **Appointment Date** (date): The date of your scheduled massage appointment.
* **Symptoms** (text): Describe your symptoms or areas of tension.
* **Contact Info** (text): Your contact information, such as your address or any other relevant details.
* **Massage Type** (select one): Choose the type of massage you would like to receive:
	+ Massaging oil
	+ Hot stone
	+ Swedish
	+ Deep Tissue
	+ Aromatherapy
* **Massage Duration** (number): The duration of your desired massage appointment in minutes.
* **Notes** (note): Leave any additional comments or questions you have for the massage therapist.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Confirm Email** (email): Confirm the email address you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Phone** (text): Confirm the phone number you entered earlier.

Note: Please be sure to confirm your contact information to ensure we can reach you for any updates or concerns related to your appointment.
