# Slicks_beauty_consultation_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Slicks_beauty_consultation_form_template is used to collect information from clients for beauty salon services. It serves as a template for staff to take notes and assign tasks while interacting with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the client's name in the `client_name` field.
2. Enter the client's email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter the client's phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Select the type of service the client would like to receive from the `service_type` dropdown menu.
5. Choose a date for the appointment in the `date` field.
6. Choose a time for the appointment in the `time` field.
7. Enter any additional comments in the `comments` field, if necessary.
8. Select the staff member assigned to the client in the `assigned_to` dropdown menu.
9. Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the client in the `assigned_tool` field, if necessary.
10. Enter the service price in the `service_price` field.
11. Select the payment method in the `payment_method` dropdown menu.
12. Select the payment status in the `payment_status` dropdown menu.
13. Enter the payment amount in the `payment_amount` field.
14. Enter any notes for the client in the `client_notes` field.
15. Enter any notes for the staff member in the `staff_notes` field, if necessary.
16. Select whether to follow up with the client after the service in the `follow_up` dropdown menu.
17. Enter any follow-up notes in the `follow_up_notes` field.
18. Select whether a photo was taken during the service in the `photo_taken` dropdown menu.
19. Enter any photo taken notes in the `photo_taken_notes` field, if necessary.
20. Enter any additional notes in the `notes` field.
21. Enter a signature from the client, if possible.
22. Enter any additional service notes in the `service_notes` field.
23. Enter any additional service details in the `service_details` field.
24. Enter any additional service details 2 in the `service_details2` field.
25. Enter any additional service details 3 in the `service_details3` field.

* **payment_status** (12, select_one, required): Select the payment status.
* **payment_amount** (13, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **client_notes** (14, text, required): Enter any notes for the client.
* **staff_notes** (15, text, optional): Enter any notes for the staff member.
* **follow_up** (16, select_one, required): Select whether to follow up with the client.
* **follow_up_notes** (17, text, required): Enter any follow-up notes.
* **photo_taken** (18, select_one, required): Select whether a photo was taken during the service.
* **photo_taken_notes** (19, text, optional): Enter any photo taken notes.
* **notes** (20, text, required): Enter any additional notes.
