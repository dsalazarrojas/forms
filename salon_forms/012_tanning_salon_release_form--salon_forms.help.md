# Tanning Salon Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tanning Salon Release Form is used to capture information from clients before or after a tanning session, allowing the salon to keep records and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Salon Address field with your tanning salon's address.
2. Enter your salon's phone number in the Salon Phone Number field.
3. Sign the Client Signature field, indicating your consent to the tanning session.
4. Choose a date in the Date Signed field to confirm when you signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Address** (`salon_address`, `text`, required/optional): The address of the tanning salon where you were serviced.
* **Salon Phone Number** (`salon_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): The phone number of the tanning salon.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent to the tanning session. Please sign in print.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required/optional): The date when you signed this form.

## Tips
* If you are completing this form as a salon staff member, please ensure that you are representing the client in signing this form.
* This form should not be used for multiple clients; each client must complete a separate form.
* Keep a record of this form for future reference and compliance.
