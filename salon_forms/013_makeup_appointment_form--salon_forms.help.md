# makeup_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The makeup_appointment_form is used to create a makeup appointment in a salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of makeup service you require from the "Makeup Type" field.
2. Choose the time slot for your appointment from the "Time" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Makeup Type**: Selects the type of makeup service required. You can choose from:
	+ Eyeshadow
	+ Eyeshadow Eyeliner
	+ Eyeshadow Highlight
* **Time**: Selects the time slot for the appointment.
	+ 10:00-12:00
	+ 13:00-15:00
	+ 16:00-18:00
* **Client Name**: Requests the client's name.
* **Contact Number**: Requests the client's contact number.
* **Email**: Requests the client's email address.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled in correctly to avoid errors.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Makeup Type" field.
- If you have any further questions, please contact the salon directly.
