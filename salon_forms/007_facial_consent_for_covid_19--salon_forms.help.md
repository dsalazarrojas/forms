# Facial Consent For COVID 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent for services provided at a salon, taking into account the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please fill out the fields as prompted.
2. Answer the questions truthfully.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **consent_form** (`consent_form`, text, required: false): A text field for the client to confirm their consent for the services provided.
	+ Please provide your consent by typing in the box.
* **salon_info** (`salon_info`, text, required: false): A text field for the salon to provide additional information about the services offered.
	+ This is where the salon can provide details about the services provided.
* **client_name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): A text field for the client to enter their name.
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **service_info** (`service_info`, text, required: false): A text field for the salon to provide more information about the services provided.
	+ This field is for the salon to describe the services in more detail.
* **signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): A text field for the client to sign their name.
	+ Please print your name in the field as a confirmation of your consent.
* **submit_button** (`submit_button`, select_multiple, required: false): A dropdown menu to confirm submission.
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm submission.
* **confirm_consent** (`confirm_consent`, select_one, required: false): A dropdown menu to confirm or deny consent.
	+ Select 'Client understands and confirms that the above information is correct' to confirm consent.
	+ Select 'I do not confirm the above information' if you do not wish to proceed.
* **reason** (`reason`, text, required: false): A text field for the client to provide a reason if they do not confirm consent.
	+ If you select 'I do not confirm the above information', please provide a reason why.

## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and accurately.
* Review your answers before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your salon representative for assistance.
