# Hyaluron Pen Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hyaluron Pen Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from clients for a hyaluronic acid treatment. This form is used to gather information about the client, their medical history, and treatment details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter the client's date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** field.
3. Enter the client's contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4. Select the type of treatment the client is undergoing (Medical, Surgical, Cosmetic, or Other) in the **Medical** field.
5. If the client has a medical history, enter it in the **Medical History** field.
6. Check if the client consents to treatment in the **Treatment Consent** field.
7. Enter any treatment details in the **Treatment Details** field.
8. If the client has an emergency contact, enter their name in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
9. If the client has an emergency contact number, enter it in the **Emergency Contact Number** field.
10. If the client has any allergies, select them in the **Allergies** field.
11. If the client is taking any medication, enter it in the **Medication** field.
12. If the client has a medical condition, enter it in the **Medical Condition** field.
13. Enter the treatment area in the **Treatment Area** field.
14. Enter the expected length of the treatment in the **Treatment Length** field.
15. Enter the total session cost in the **Total Session Cost** field.
16. If you have any notes about the client, enter them in the **Notes** field.
17. Sign the form in the **Signature** field.
18. If you are using an alternative contact method, select it in the **Contact Method** field.
19. If you need to use an alternative contact method, enter the details in the **Contact Method Details** field.
20. If the client is receiving treatment at a different medical facility, enter it in the **Medical Facility** field.
21. If the client is receiving a specific treatment procedure, enter it in the **Treatment Procedure** field.
22. If the client's treatment area is not listed, enter it in the **Treatment Area Other** field.
23. If you have any additional notes about the treatment, enter them in the **Treatment Notes** field.
24. If you are using an alternative contact method, select it in the **Other Contact Method** field.
25. If you need to sign the form with an alternative signature, enter it in the **Other Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the client's contact number.
* **Medical**: Select the type of treatment the client is undergoing (Medical, Surgical, Cosmetic, or Other).
* **Medical History**: If the client has a medical history, enter it here.
* **Treatment Consent**: Check if the client consents to treatment.
* **Treatment Details**: Enter any treatment details.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the client's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the number of the client's emergency contact.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies the client has.
* **Medication**: If the client is taking any medication, enter it here.
* **Medical Condition**: If the client has a medical condition, enter it here.
* **Treatment Area**: Enter the treatment area.
* **Treatment Length**: Enter the expected length of the treatment.
* **Total Session Cost**: Enter the total session cost.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the client.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm consent.
* **Contact Method**: Select the contact method to use.
* **Contact Method Details**: Enter any additional contact method details.
* **Medical Facility**: If the client is receiving treatment at a different facility, enter it here.
* **Treatment Procedure**: If the client is receiving a specific treatment procedure, enter it here.
* **Treatment Area Other**: If the client's treatment area is not listed, enter it here.
* **Treatment Notes**: Enter any additional treatment notes.
* **Other Contact Method**: Select an alternative contact method.
