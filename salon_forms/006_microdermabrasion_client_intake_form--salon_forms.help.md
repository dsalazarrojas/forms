# Microdermabrasion Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients who are interested in or have scheduled a microdermabrasion procedure. The collected information will be used to create a tailored treatment plan and assess the client's suitability for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:
*   Complete all the required fields. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the practitioner.
*   Enter your client name, phone number, and email address in their respective fields.
*   In the "Information about client?" field, provide any relevant medical history, skin concerns, or other information that may affect the treatment plan.
*   Choose your skin type and select all concerns that apply to your skin.
*   In the "Practice Goals" field, describe what you hope to achieve with the microdermabrasion procedure.
*   Answer the "Medical Procedure" field questions to provide information about any medical conditions or procedures that may affect the treatment plan.
*   In the "Microdermabrasion Procedure" field, provide any relevant information about the microdermabrasion procedure.
*   Choose your next visit date and time in the "Next Visit" and "Appointment Time" fields.
*   Sign and date the "Client Signature" and "Practitioner Signature" fields.
*   Select your date of birth, medical condition, medication, allergies, medical procedure, microdermabrasion procedure, number of sessions, number of procedures, and total price in their respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, \*): Enter your name.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, \*): Enter your phone number.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, \*): Enter your email address.
*   **Information about client?** (`client_info`, `note`, \*): Provide any relevant medical history, skin concerns, or other information that may affect the treatment plan.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, \*): Provide any relevant medical history that may affect the treatment plan.
*   **Skin Type** (`skin_type`, `select_one`, \*): Choose your skin type (Acne, Normal, Other).
*   **Skin Concerns** (`skin_concerns`, `select_multiple`, \*): Choose all concerns that apply to your skin (Fine Lines, Pores, Acne, Hyper-pigmentation, Aging, Rosacea).
*   **Practice Goals** (`practice_goals`, `text`, \*): Describe what you hope to achieve with the microdermabrasion procedure.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `note`, \*): Provide any relevant information about any medical conditions or procedures that may affect the treatment plan.
*   **Microdermabrasion Procedure** (`microdermabrasion_procedure`, `note`, \*): Provide any relevant information about the microdermabrasion procedure.
*   **Next Visit** (`next_visit`, `date`, \*): Choose your next visit date.
*   **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, `time`, \*): Choose your appointment time.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `select_one`, \*): Sign this field to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
*   **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, `select_one`, \*): Sign this field to confirm that the practitioner has reviewed and agreed on the treatment plan.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `select_one`, \*): Choose whether you are true, false, or unknown.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `select_one`, \*): Choose Other.
*   **Medication** (`medication`, `select_one`, \*): Choose True or False.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_one`, \*): Choose True or False.
*   **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `select_one`, \*): Choose True or False.
*   **Microdermabrasion Procedure** (`microdermabrasion_procedure`, `select_one`, \*): Choose True or False.
*   **Number of Sessions** (`num_sessions`, `number`, \*): Enter the number of sessions.
*   **Number of Procedures** (`num_procedures`, `number`, \*): Enter the number of procedures.
*   **Total Price** (`total_price`, `number`, \*): Enter the total price.

## Tips
*   Please provide accurate and complete information.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the practitioner.
*   The information collected will be used to create a tailored treatment plan and assess your suitability for the microdermabrasion procedure.
*   Please sign both the "Client Signature" and "Practitioner Signature" fields to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
