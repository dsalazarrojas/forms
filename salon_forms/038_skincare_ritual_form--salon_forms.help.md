# Skincare Ritual Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skincare Ritual Form is designed to collect in-depth information about a client's skin type, concerns, and preferences to help professionals provide personalized skincare advice and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Skincare Ritual Form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field. 
2.  Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This will be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
3.  Enter your **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field. This will be used for scheduling and confirmation.
4.  Select your **Skin Type** from the dropdown menu in the "Skin Type" field. Choose from Normal, Oily, Sensitive, Combination, or Dry skin.
5.  Select all applicable **Skin Concerns** from the dropdown menu in the "Skin Concerns" field. Choose from Acne, Aging, Dullness, Hyper-Pigmentation, Rosacea, Redness, or Sallowness.
6.  Select all applicable **Product Preferences** from the dropdown menu in the "Product Preferences" field. Choose from Moisturiser, Serum, Sunscreen, or Cleanser.
7.  Select all applicable **Product Sensitivities** from the dropdown menu in the "Product Sensitivity" field. Choose from Fragrance, Color, pH Level, or Moisture Level.
8.  Select all applicable **Skin Conditions** from the dropdown menu in the "Skin Conditions" field. Choose from Hyper-Pigmentation, Aging, Dullness, or Redness.
9.  Select all applicable **Product Recommendations** from the dropdown menu in the "Product Recommendations" field. Choose from Moisturiser, Serum, Sunscreen, or Cleanser.
10. Enter your **Skincare Routine** in the "Skincare Routine" field. Provide detailed information about your skincare routine, including products used, frequencies, and any concerns.
11. Enter any additional **Notes** in the "Notes" field. This is an optional field for providing additional comments or concerns.
12. Set a **Follow-up** date in the "Follow-up" field if you have any scheduled appointments or follow-up sessions.
13. Select all applicable **Skincare Specialties** from the dropdown menu in the "Skincare Specialties" field. Choose from Chemical Peeling, Microdermabrasion, Micro-needling, or Chemical Exfoliation.
14. Select all applicable **Recommended Products** from the dropdown menu in the "Recommended Products" field. Choose from Moisturiser, Serum, Sunscreen, or Cleanser.
15. Select all applicable **Skin Treatments** from the dropdown menu in the "Skin Treatments" field. Choose from Chemical Peeling, Microdermabrasion, Micro-needling, or Chemical Exfoliation.
16. Select all applicable **Recommended Professionals** from the dropdown menu in the "Recommended Professionals" field. Choose from Dermatologist, Esthetician, or Cosmetic Chemist.
17. Enter your **Skincare Plan** in the "Skincare Plan" field. This is an optional field for providing detailed information about your skincare plan.
18. Enter your **Skincare Routine Frequency** in the "Skincare Routine Frequency" field. Provide information about how often you perform your skincare routine.
19. Select your **Skincare Routine Goals** from the dropdown menu in the "Skincare Routine Goals" field. Choose from Hydration, Anti-Aging, Anti-Acne, or Anti-Cellulite.
20. Enter any **Skincare Routine Advice** in the "Skincare Routine Advice" field. This is an optional field for providing additional information or concerns.
21. Enter any **Skincare Routine Reminders** in the "Skincare Routine Reminders" field. This is an optional field for providing reminders or scheduling information.
22. Enter any additional **Skincare Routine Comments** in the "Skincare Routine Comments" field. This is an optional field for providing additional comments or concerns.
23. Set a **Skincare Routine Follow-up** date in the "Skincare Routine Follow-up" field if you have any scheduled appointments or follow-up sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be recorded.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address to be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter a phone number to be used for scheduling and confirmation.
* **Skin Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose from Normal, Oily, Sensitive, Combination, or Dry skin.
*   * **Skin Concerns** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable skin concerns from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Product Preferences** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable product preferences from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Product Sensitivities** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable product sensitivities from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Skin Conditions** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable skin conditions from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Product Recommendations** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable product recommendations from the dropdown menu.
* **Skincare Routine** (Text, Optional): Provide detailed information about your skincare routine, including products used, frequencies, and any concerns.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **Follow-up** (Date, Optional): Set a follow-up date if you have any scheduled appointments or follow-up sessions.
* **Skincare Specialties** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable skincare specialties from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Recommended Products** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable recommended products from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Skin Treatments** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable skin treatments from the dropdown menu.
*   * **Recommended Professionals** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable recommended professionals from the dropdown menu.
* **Skincare Plan** (Note, Optional): Enter your skincare plan, including any detailed information about your skin conditions and concerns.
* **Skincare Routine Frequency** (Text, Optional): Provide information about how often you perform your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Goals** (Select One, Optional): Choose from Hydration, Anti-Aging, Anti-Acne, or Anti-Cellulite.
* **Skincare Routine Advice** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or concerns about your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Reminders** (Text, Optional): Enter any reminders or scheduling information for your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or concerns about your skincare routine.
* **Skincare Routine Follow-up** (Date, Optional): Set a follow-up date if you have any scheduled appointments or follow-up sessions.
