# Henna Brow Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from patients for henna brow procedures. It's a mandatory step to ensure that patients understand and agree to the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information on the first page, including their name and contact details.
2. On the second page, provide details about the procedure, including what is involved and what the patient can expect.
3. Answer the consent question, "Do you agree to proceed with the henna brow procedure?" with either "Yes" or "No".
4. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
5. Fill in the date and time of the procedure.
6. Enter your email and phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_info**: Provide your name and any other relevant contact information. This is a required field for us to contact you for any questions or updates.
* **procedure_info**: This field is where you can provide any additional details about the procedure, such as any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **consent**: Please answer "Yes" if you agree to proceed with the henna brow procedure, and "No" if you do not. This is a crucial field that we need you to fill in to confirm your agreement.
* **signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you have read and understood the procedure and have given your consent.
* **date**: Please enter the date of the procedure, in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **time**: Enter the scheduled time of the procedure.
* **email**: Provide your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you before and after the procedure.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number, which we'll use to contact you in case of any last-minute changes or updates.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the procedure and its risks before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask us.
* We'll be in touch with you before and after the procedure to ensure everything goes smoothly.
