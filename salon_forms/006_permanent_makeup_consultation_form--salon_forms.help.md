# Permanent Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Consultation Form is designed to help the permanent makeup artist understand the client's medical history, appointment preferences, and payment details. This information will be used to create a personalized consultation plan and schedule the appointment accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the permanent makeup artist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (form_data, text, required): Please enter your name and any other identifying information that you would like to share with the permanent makeup artist.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries that may impact your permanent makeup procedure, please describe them in this field.
* **Medical History Questions** (Medical History Questions, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **Are you allergic to any medications?**: If yes, please specify the medications.
	+ **Have you experienced any previous surgeries?**: If yes, please describe the surgeries.
	+ **Have you had a permanent makeup tattoo in the past?**: If yes, please describe the procedures.
	+ **Is there a history of skin cancer in your family?**: If yes, please describe the situation.
	+ **Previous permanent makeup procedure(s)**: If yes, please specify the procedures.
* **Appointment Details** (Appointment Details, text, required): Please provide the permanent makeup artist with your preferred appointment date and time.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, required): Please indicate how you would like to pay for the permanent makeup procedure (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
