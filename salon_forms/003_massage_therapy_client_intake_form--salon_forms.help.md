# Massage Therapy Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Client Intake Form is a comprehensive form used to gather information from massage therapy clients before their appointment. This form helps the massage therapist understand the client's needs, medical history, and preferences, ensuring that they can provide the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

1. **Client Information**: Enter your name and contact details, including your phone number and email address.
2. **Contact Information**: Provide your current contact information, including your street address and city.
3. **Medical History**: Briefly describe your medical history, including any allergies, conditions, or procedures you have had in the past.
4. **Allergies**: Select all the allergies that apply to you.
5. **Conditions**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to you.
6. **Procedures**: Select all the medical procedures you have had in the past.
7. **Massage Preferences**: Select your preferred massage style and length.
8. **Appointment Preferences**: Select your preferred appointment time and date.
9. **Appointment Time**: Select a time for your appointment that works best for you.
10. **Appointment Date**: Select a date for your appointment that works best for you.
11. **Massage Length**: Enter the length of time you would like your massage to be.
12. **Massage Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your massage appointments (e.g., one-time, weekly, bi-weekly).
13. **Client Goals**: Briefly describe your goals for your massage therapy session.
14. **Massage Style**: Select your preferred massage style (e.g., Swedish, deep tissue, etc.).
15. **Massage Duration**: Enter the duration of your massage session (e.g., 30 minutes, 60 minutes).
16. **Massage Fees**: Enter the fees you are willing to pay for your massage appointments.
17. **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, cash, etc.).
18. **Payment Information**: If you have any special payment instructions, please provide them here.
19. **Insurance Information**: If you have insurance coverage for massage therapy, please provide your insurance information.
20. **Client Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm that you have provided accurate information.
