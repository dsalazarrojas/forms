# Brow Lamination Tint Information And Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to be a comprehensive guide for clients to provide necessary information about their brow lamination and tint procedure. It is meant to ensure a clear understanding of the process, any medical history that may impact the procedure, and to gather contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the guidelines below:

1. Please fill out all required fields. Fields marked as "required" are essential for the procedure and our staff to provide the best service.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and in as much detail as possible.
3. Review all fields carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Name

* **Client name**: Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification or government-issued ID.
* This field is required and will be used for communication purposes.

### Contact Number

* **Contact number**: Please provide your phone number to ensure we can reach you for follow-up or scheduling purposes.
* This field is required and will be used for scheduling and follow-up.

### Medical History

* **Medical history**: Please list any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities that may affect your procedure or treatment.
* This field is optional but recommended, as it helps us provide the best service possible.
* Please be as detailed as possible, as this information helps us understand any potential risks or complications.

### Procedure Understanding

* **Procedure understanding**: Please select whether you understand the lamination and tint process.
* This field is required and will help us ensure that you are fully aware of what to expect.
* Please select "I understand" to confirm your understanding.

### Prior Treatments

* **Prior treatments**: If you have received any chemical or cosmetic treatments recently, please list them here.
* This field is optional but recommended, as it helps us understand your current situation.
* Please be as detailed as possible, including dates and procedures.

### Consent Confirmation Email

* **Consent signature email**: Please enter your email to confirm that you have signed the waiver.
* This field is required and will be used to send you a copy of the signed waiver.
* Please enter your email address carefully, as this is where the confirmation will be sent.

### Aftercare Instructions

* **Aftercare instructions**: Please read this section carefully after completing the form, as it outlines the necessary steps to follow after your procedure.
* This field is optional but recommended, as it helps you understand what to do after your treatment.

### Additional Notes

* **Additional notes**: Please use this field to add any remarks or comments that you think are relevant to your treatment.
* This field is optional and can be used to add any additional information or questions you may have.
* Please be as detailed as possible, but concise.
