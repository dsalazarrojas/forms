# Hair Color Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for a hair color consultation. The information collected will be used by the stylists to provide personalized hair color recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the relevant options from the dropdown menus or fill in the text fields as follows:

1. **Select Hair Color Options**: Choose one or more hair colors that appeal to you. This will help us understand your preferences.
	* Select from the available options: Blonde, Red, Brown, Black, Gray, Gray-Black, Platinum, Silver, Auburn, Beige.
2. **Select Hair Length**: Choose the length of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Short, Long, Medium, Pixie, Bob, Bobbed.
3. **Select Hair Type**: Choose the type of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Fine, Medium, Coarse, Thick, Thin, Very.
4. **Select Hair Condition**: Choose the condition of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Normal, Dry, Oily, Damaged, Curly, Wiry, Curled.
5. **Select Hair Texture**: Choose the texture of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Fine, Medium, Coarse, Normal, Very.
6. **Select Hair Density**: Choose the density of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Fine, Medium, Coarse, Normal, Very.
7. **Select Face Shape**: Choose the shape of your face.
	* Select from the available options: Oval, Round, Square, Heart, Triangular.
8. **Select Skin Tone**: Choose your skin tone.
	* Select from the available options: Fair, Medium, Tan, Dark, Very.
9. **Select Your Desired Hair Hue**: Choose the desired hue of your hair.
	* Select from the available options: Natural, Warm, Cool, Neutral, Deep, Very.
10. **Additional Notes**: If you have any special requests or notes, feel free to add them in this field.
11. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
12. **Email**: Enter your email address.
13. **Address**: Enter your address.
14. **City**: Enter your city (optional).
15. **State**: Enter your state (optional).
16. **ZIP**: Enter your ZIP code.
17. **Select Appointment Time**: Choose the time of your appointment.
18. **Select Appointment Date**: Choose the date of your appointment.
19. **Select Your Stylist**: Choose your preferred stylist.
	* Select from the available options: Jimmy, Sally, Jane, John, Doe.
20. **Select Services**: Choose the service you would like to get.
	* Select from the available options: Consultation, Cut, Color, Trim, Style.
21. **Hair Length 2**: Choose the length of your hair for this service (if different from your main hair length).
	* Select from the available options: Short, Long, Medium, Pixie, Bob, Bobbed.
22. **Hair Condition 2**: Choose the condition of your hair for this service (if different from your main hair condition).
	* Select from the available options: Normal, Dry, Oily, Damaged, Curly, Wiry, Curled.

## Tips

* Please select the relevant options for each field to ensure that we have enough information to provide personalized recommendations.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please feel free to add notes or ask us to clarify.
* Please enter your phone number and email address accurately, as we will use them to contact you for appointment confirmations and follow-ups.
