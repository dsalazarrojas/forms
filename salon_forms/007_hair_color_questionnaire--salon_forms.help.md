# Hair Color Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the best hair color that suits your face shape, length, and color preferences. Please answer the questions honestly, and the form will provide you with personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
2. Select the options that best match your face shape and hair color preferences from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter the numerical values for face length, hair length, and width as accurately as possible.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, click submit to get your personalized hair color recommendations.
