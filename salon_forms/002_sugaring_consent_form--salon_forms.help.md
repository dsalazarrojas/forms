# sugaring_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Consent Form is used to gather essential information and consent from clients for sugaring services, including their contact details, preferred contact method, and consent type. This form is required for all clients who receive sugaring services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the client's information, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the client's contact number and email address in the specified fields.
3. Select the type of sugaring consent the client has given.
4. Fill in the service details if applicable.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm the client's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the client's full name as they would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Number (Text, Required)**: Enter the client's primary phone number where they can be reached.
* **Email (Email, Optional)**: Enter the client's email address where they can be contacted.
* **Address (Text, Optional)**: Enter the client's address if they have a different address than their contact number.
* **Service Details (Text, Required)**: List any specific services the client is requesting, such as eyelash sugaring.
* **Consent Type (Select One, Required)**: Select the type of sugaring consent the client has given, such as General or Specialized.
* **Consent Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the date the client provided consent for sugaring services.
* **Signature (Note, Required)**: Sign and date the form to confirm the client's consent.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the client's contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
- This form is required for all clients who receive sugaring services.
- Do not fill out this form multiple times for the same client.
