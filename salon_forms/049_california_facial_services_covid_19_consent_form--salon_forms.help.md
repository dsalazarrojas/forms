# California Facial Services COVID 19 Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their health status, travel history, and understanding of the salon's COVID-19 policies to ensure a safe and healthy environment for all clients and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Client Name** (required)
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** (required)
3. Provide your **Email Address** for confirmation (required)
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for contact (required)
5. Choose your **Appointment Date** for the scheduled service (required)
6. Select the type of **Service** you are receiving (required)
7. Check if you are currently experiencing any **COVID-19 Symptoms** (required)
8. If you are experiencing symptoms, list them in the **Symptoms List** (optional)
9. Confirm if you have been **Exposed to COVID-19** (required)
10. If yes, state how long ago it was (optional)
11. Confirm your **Vaccination Status** (optional)
12. Check your **Recent Travel History** (optional)
13. Agree to wear a **Mask** when not receiving service (required)
14. Consent to **Temperature Screening** (required)
15. Agree to **Sanitize Hands** upon arrival (required)
16. Agree to follow **Social Distancing Guidelines** (required)
17. Understand that no **Guests are Allowed** (required)
18. Understand the **Cancellation Policy** (required)
19. Accept the **Risk of Exposure** (required)
20. Agree to hold the establishment **Harmless** (required)
21. Provide your **Health Insurance Provider** (optional)
22. Enter the name and phone number of your **Emergency Contact** (optional)
23. Enter your **Client Signature** (required)
24. Enter the **Date of Signature** (required)
25. Enter the name of your **Esthetician Provider** (optional)
26. Enter any **Additional Notes** about your health information (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for communication.
* **Appointment Date**: Choose the date of your scheduled service.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you are receiving from the options provided.
* **COVID-19 Symptoms Check**: Check if you are currently experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Symptoms List**: If you are experiencing symptoms, list them from the options provided.
* **Exposure Check**: Confirm if you have been exposed to anyone with COVID-19.
* **Positive Test Check**: Confirm if you have tested positive for COVID-19.
* **Positive Test Date**: If yes, enter the date you tested positive.
* **Vaccination Status**: Confirm your vaccination status.
* **Recent Travel History**: Check if you have traveled outside California in the last 14 days.
* **Mask Policy Acknowledgment**: Agree to wear a mask when not receiving service.
* **Temperature Screening**: Consent to temperature screening.
* **Hand Sanitizing Agreement**: Agree to sanitize hands upon arrival.
* **Social Distancing Agreement**: Agree to follow social distancing guidelines.
* **Guest Policy Acknowledgment**: Understand no guests are allowed.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Understand the COVID-19-related cancellation policy.
* **Risk Acknowledgment**: Accept the risk of exposure.
* **Liability Waiver**: Agree to hold the establishment harmless.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider (optional).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact (optional).
* **Client Signature**: Enter your digital signature or typed name (required).
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature (required).
* **Esthetician Name**: Enter the name of your esthetician provider (optional).
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any other health information you wish to share (optional).
