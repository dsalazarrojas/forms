# Hair Salon Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Salon Review Form is a tool for clients to share their feedback and ratings about their experience at a salon. This form helps the salon management track customer reviews, identify areas of improvement, and provide quality service to their customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the salon where you received service.
2.  Rate your experience (1-5 stars).
3.  Share your review about your visit (e.g., what you liked, disliked, or would improve).
4.  If you had a favorite staff member, let us know their name.
5.  Enter the name of the person you contacted to schedule or inquire about your appointment (if different from the staff member).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Salon Name**: Enter the name of the salon where you received service. For example, "ABC Hair Salon" or "John's Barber Shop".
*   **Rating**: Rate your overall experience at the salon, using the stars as a guide (1-5 stars).
*   **Review**: Share a brief description of your visit, including what you enjoyed or disliked about your experience.
*   **Rating Score**: Enter a numerical value for your rating (e.g., 4 out of 5).
*   **Salon Staff Name**: If you had a favorite staff member, enter their name (e.g., "John" or "Mary").
*   **Contact Person**: If you contacted someone to schedule or inquire about your appointment, enter their name (e.g., "Jane" or "Bob").
