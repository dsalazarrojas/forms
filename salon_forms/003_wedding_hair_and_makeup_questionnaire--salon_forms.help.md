# Wedding Hair And Makeup Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects details about the wedding party to provide an exceptional hair and makeup experience. Please fill out the form below to help us prepare for your special day.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Enter any additional information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Hair Questions** (`wedding_hair_questions`, text, optional): A text field for any hair-related questions or concerns.
  - This field allows you to provide any additional hair-related questions or comments that will help us understand your preferences.
- **Hair Style** (`hair_style`, select_one, optional): Select the type of hair style you desire.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate your preferred hair style, such as a simple updo or a more elaborate design.
- **Makeup Questions** (`makeup_questions`, text, optional): A text field for any makeup-related questions or concerns.
  - This field is for any makeup-related questions or comments that will help us understand your preferences.
- **Eye Type** (`eye_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of eye style you desire.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate your preferred eye style, such as a subtle or dramatic look.
- **Lip Color** (`lip_color`, select_multiple, optional): Select the desired lip color(s).
  - Choose from the available options to indicate your preferred lip color, such as a simple neutral tone or bold bright shade.
- **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, optional): Enter the wedding date.
  - Enter the date of your wedding ceremony.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your event.
  - Enter the start time of your wedding ceremony.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your event.
  - Enter the end time of your wedding ceremony.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): A text field for any additional information.
  - This field is for any additional information or comments that will help us prepare for your wedding.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  - Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any further questions or concerns.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
  - Enter your name so we can address you properly in our communication.
- **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
  - Enter your address so we can ensure we are delivering your materials correctly.
- **Wedding Party Size** (`wedding_party_size`, number, optional): Enter the number of people in your party.
  - Enter the number of people that will be attending your wedding ceremony.
- **Wedding Party Type** (`wedding_party_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of wedding party.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate the type of wedding party (e.g. bride and groom, bridesmaids and groomsmen, etc.).
- **Wedding Theme** (`wedding_theme`, select_multiple, optional): Select the theme of your wedding.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate the theme of your wedding (e.g. vintage, beach, etc.).
- **Hair Accessories** (`hair_accessories`, select_multiple, optional): Select any hair accessories you desire.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate any hair accessories you would like to use (e.g. hair clips, headpieces, etc.).
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): A text field for any special requests.
  - This field is for any special requests you may have.
- **Wedding Attire** (`wedding_attire`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of wedding attire you desire.
  - Choose from the available options to indicate your preferred wedding attire (e.g. formal, informal, etc.).
