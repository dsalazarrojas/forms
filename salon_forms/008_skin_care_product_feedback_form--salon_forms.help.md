# skin_care_product_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin care product feedback form is designed to collect information from customers about their experiences with our skin care products. This form will help us understand how our products are performing in the field and provide better products and services to our customers.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. In the "skin care product" field, enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the product by selecting a number from 0-5 in the "Rating" field.
4. You can also rate your experience in multiple categories by selecting multiple options in the "Ratings 5" field.
5. In the "Feedback" field, enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the product.
6. If you would like to be contacted to follow up on your feedback, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Select the category of the product from the options in the "Category" field.
9. If you would like to select multiple subcategories, use the "Subcategory" field.
10. Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on in the "Product" field.
11. Enter the email address of the person who will be responding to your feedback in the "Response Email" field.
12. Enter the phone number of the person who will be responding to your feedback in the "Response Phone" field.
13. If you would like to confirm that you are the correct contact person for your feedback, enter the same phone number again in the "Response Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
* **Skin Care Product**: Enter the name of the skin care product you are providing feedback on.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the product by selecting a number from 0-5.
* **Ratings 5**: Rate your experience in multiple categories if applicable.
* **Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the product.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Category**: Select the category of the product from the options.
* **Subcategory**: Select multiple subcategories for the product if applicable.
* **Product**: Enter the name of the product.
* **Response Email**: Enter the email address of the person who will be responding to your feedback.
* **Response Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who will be responding to your feedback.
* **Response Phone Number**: Confirm that the phone number entered is correct.
