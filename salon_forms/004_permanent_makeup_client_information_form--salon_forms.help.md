# Permanent Makeup Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Makeup Client Information Form is used to collect essential details about your clients, including their personal and medical information, appointment scheduling, and payment arrangements. This form ensures that the salon has accurate and complete information to provide the best possible service and to establish a professional relationship with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your address to help the salon contact you.
3. Enter your email address so the salon can communicate with you.
4. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
5. If applicable, provide your date of birth.
6. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them.
7. Let us know about any medications you are currently taking.
8. If you have any medical history, please describe it briefly.
9. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship.
10. If you have a medical insurance, please let us know about it.
11. Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time.
12. Choose a payment method (if not specified, payment will be made through other means).
13. Read and agree to the client terms, which outline the salon's policies and procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Provide your mailing address to help us contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): If applicable, please provide your date of birth.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): If you have any medications, let us know about them.
* **Allergy** (`allergy`, text, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical history, describe it briefly.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): If you want to provide their address.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): If you have medical insurance, let us know about it.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your insurance provider.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Schedule your appointment by selecting a date.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Schedule your appointment by selecting a time.
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, text, optional): If you have any specific requests about your appointment duration.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): If applicable, please provide your total cost.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, required): If you have a deposit to make, enter it.
* **Remaining Balance** (`remaining_balance`, number, required): If applicable, provide your remaining balance.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose a payment method (Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card, or Cash).
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, note, optional): Read and agree to the client terms.
* **Salon Terms** (`salon_terms`, note, optional): Read and agree to the client terms, which outline the salon's policies and procedures.

Please complete this form accurately and fully, and our team will be happy to assist you with any questions or concerns.
