# Makeup Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients for their upcoming makeup services. It collects personal details, service preferences, and other relevant information to ensure that the service provider has a clear understanding of the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details such as your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the type of service you are interested in from the provided options.
3. Choose your hair type and preferred makeup style.
4. Select your color scheme and brow style preferences.
5. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with the service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Optional)
This field is used to collect your name, which will be used to identify you in our records.

### 2. **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, Text, Optional)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment or send reminders.

### 3. **Client Email** (`client_email`, Email, Optional)
Enter your email address so we can send you notifications and confirmations.

### 4. **Service Type** (`service_type`, Select One, Optional)
Choose the type of service you are interested in from the provided options.

### 5. **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Select your hair type to help us understand your needs better.

### 6. **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose your preferred color scheme to ensure we get it right.

### 7. **Eye Color** (`eye_color`, Select One, Optional)
Select your eye color to help us match it with the rest of your look.

### 8. **Lip Color** (`lip_color`, Select One, Optional)
Select your lip color to ensure we match it with your eye color.

### 9. **Eyebrow Color** (`eye_brow_color`, Select One, Optional)
Select your brow color to ensure a harmonious look.

### 10. **Brow Type** (`brow_type`, Select One, Optional)
Choose your brow style to complete your look.

### 11. **Brow Style** (`brow_style`, Select One, Optional)
Select your preferred brow style to ensure we get it right.

### 12. **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, Time, Optional)
If you know how long you plan to stay for your service, please enter the duration.

### 13. **Makeup Style** (`makeup_style`, Select One, Optional)
Choose your preferred makeup style to ensure we deliver what you want.

### 14. **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, Note, Optional)
Add any additional comments or notes you would like to share with us.

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure we provide the best possible experience for you. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
