# Hair Salon Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Hair Salon Client Questionnaire" is a form used to collect information from clients visiting the salon. It is intended to be completed by the client to provide their personal details, service preferences, and any special requests or concerns they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred date and time of visit.
3. Choose the service you would like to book.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the service provided.
6. Select any follow-up options if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (`id: 1`, `text`, optional): Provide your personal details, including your name and contact information.
* **Name** (`id: 2`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`id: 3`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Visit** (`id: 5`, `date`, optional): Select the date you would like to visit the salon.
* **Time of Visit** (`id: 6`, `time`, optional): Select the time you would like to visit the salon.
* **Service** (`id: 7`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of service you would like to book (e.g. Haircut, Shampoo, Color, etc.).
* **Note** (`id: 8`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Service Details** (`id: 9`, `text`, optional): If you have specific requests or requirements for your service, enter them here.
* **Satisfaction** (`id: 10`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the service provided (1-5).
* **Follow Up** (`id: 11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any follow-up options you would like to schedule.
