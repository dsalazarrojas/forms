# Makeup Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Consultation Form is a form used to collect information from customers for makeup services. It is used to ensure that the necessary details are gathered for the service to be provided effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the customer's input in the "Customer Input" field.
2. Select the products you would like to use for your service in the "Product Selection" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the service you would like to receive in the "Service Details" field.
4. Choose a date and time for the service to start in the "Expected Service Start" field.
5. Choose a time for the service to end in the "Expected Service End" field.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field (optional).
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
8. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Confirm if you would like to be assigned to a specific personnel in the "Assigned To" field.
10. Confirm if the service will be assigned to a specific personnel in the "Assigned By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Input** (Customer Input, text, required): Enter your personal details for the service.
* **Product Selection** (Product Selection, select multiple, required): Select the products you would like to use for your service.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, optional): Describe the service you would like to receive.
* **Expected Service Start** (Expected Service Start, date, required): Choose a date for the service to start.
* **Expected Service End** (Expected Service End, time, required): Choose a time for the service to end.
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the service.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you choose to provide one).
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Provide your phone number.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select one, required): Confirm if you want to be assigned to a specific personnel.
* **Assigned By** (Assigned By, select one, required): Confirm if the service will be assigned to a specific personnel.
