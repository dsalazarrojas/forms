# Eyelash Training Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyelash Training Registration Form Template is designed to register students for eyelash training sessions. This form helps capture necessary information, including personal and training-related details, to ensure efficient registration and organization of training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Eyelash Training Registration Form Template, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields with the label "Required" are filled in to prevent submission errors.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student Name (Text, Required)**
Student Name: Enter your full name as you would like to be identified.

### 2. **Email (Email, Required)**
Email: Enter your email address for correspondence and updates.

### 3. **Phone (Text, Required)**
Phone: Provide your contact number for any further communication.

### 4. **Training Date (Date, Required)**
Training Date: Select the date of your eyelash training session.

### 5. **Course Start Date (Date, Required)**
Course Start Date: Select the start date of the training course.

### 6. **Course End Date (Date, Required)**
Course End Date: Select the end date of the training course.

### 7. **Training Location (Text, Required)**
Training Location: Provide the location of your eyelash training session.

### 8. **Instructor Name (Text, Required)**
Instructor Name: Enter the name of the instructor leading the training session.

### 9. **Course Name (Text, Required)**
Course Name: Enter the name of the course being offered.

### 10. **Number of Students (Number, Required)**
Number of Students: Enter the total number of students enrolled in the training session.

### 11. **Student Notes (Text, Optional)**
Student Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes regarding the training session.

### 12. **Form Submitter (Text, Optional)**
Form Submitter: Enter your name and contact information for any follow-up communication.

### 13. **Form Submitted By (Text, Optional)**
Form Submitted By: Enter your name and contact information.

### 14. **Assigned Tool (Text, Optional)**
Assigned Tool: Enter any tools assigned to the training session.

### 15. **Training Status (Select One, Optional)**
Training Status: Select the current status of the training session from:
  - Waiting List
  - In Progress
  - Completed

### 16. **Payment Status (Select One, Optional)**
Payment Status: Select the current payment status from:
  - Paid
  - Unpaid

### 17. **Student Address (Text, Required)**
Student Address: Enter your home address for correspondence and updates.

### 18. **Student Address 2 (Text, Optional)**
Student Address 2: Enter any additional address information as needed. 

## Tips
- Ensure all "Required" fields are filled in to avoid submission errors.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact your training administrator.
