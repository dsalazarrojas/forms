# Body Contouring Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Body Contouring Consultation Form is used to gather information about the client's body contouring concerns and preferences for treatment. This form helps the client communicate their needs and expectations to the medical staff and helps in creating a personalized treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Enter your full name and email address to contact you.
*   Provide your phone number for further communication.
*   Enter your date of birth.
*   Enter your address.
*   Select how you heard about the clinic.
*   Describe your primary body contouring concern.
*   Select the areas of your body that you are concerned about.
*   Choose how long your concern has been a problem.
*   Describe any previous body contouring treatments.
*   Select how satisfied you were with previous treatments.
*   Choose which body contouring procedures you are interested in.
*   Select how much downtime you can tolerate.
*   Provide any relevant medical history.
*   List any current medications.
*   List any known allergies.
*   Select your smoking status.
*   Select your current pregnancy status.
*   Choose the goal of this consultation.
*   List any questions or concerns you may have.
*   Select your preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (required): Please enter your phone number for further communication.
*   **Date of Birth** (required): Enter your date of birth.
*   **How did you hear about us** (optional): Select how you heard about the clinic.
    +   Friend or Family
    +   Online Search
    +   Social Media
    +   Advertisement
    +   Previous Patient
    +   Medical Professional
    +   Other
*   **Primary Body Contouring Concern** (required): Describe your primary body contouring concern.
*   **Problem Areas** (required): Select all the areas of your body that you are concerned about.
    +   Face
    +   Chin
    +   Neck
    +   Chest
    +   Arms
    +   Abdomen
    +   Flanks
    +   Waist
    +   Hips
    +   Buttocks
    +   Thighs
    +   Calves
    +   Legs
    +   Back
    +   Multiple Areas
*   **How long has this been a concern** (required): Choose how long your concern has been a problem.
    +   Less than 6 months
    +   6 months - 1 year
    +   1-2 years
    +   2-5 years
    +   More than 5 years
*   **Previous Body Contouring Treatments** (optional): Describe any previous body contouring treatments.
*   **Satisfaction with Previous Treatments** (optional): Select how satisfied you were with previous treatments.
    +   Very Satisfied
    +   Satisfied
    +   Neutral
    +   Dissatisfied
    +   Very Dissatisfied
    +   Not Applicable
*   **Interested In Which Procedures** (required): Choose which body contouring procedures you are interested in.
    +   Non-Surgical Sculpting
    +   Laser Body Contouring
    +   Radiofrequency
    +   Ultrasound Therapy
    +   Cryotherapy
    +   Chemical Peels
    +   Surgical Contouring
    +   Combination Approach
    +   Unsure
*   **Do you have realistic expectations** (required): Select whether you have realistic expectations about what procedures can achieve.
    +   True
    +   Mostly
    +   Somewhat
    +   False
    +   Unsure
*   **Ideal Outcome** (required): Describe your ideal outcome from this consultation.
*   **Desired Timeline for Results** (required): Choose your desired timeline for results.
    +   Immediate
    +   1-4 weeks
    +   1-3 months
    +   3-6 months
    +   6-12 months
    +   Flexible - No Timeline
*   **Downtime Tolerance** (required): Select how much downtime you can tolerate.
    +   None - Need to return immediately
    +   Minimal - Few days
    +   Some - Up to 1 week
    +   Flexible - Can tolerate 2+ weeks
*   **Relevant Medical History** (optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
*   **Current Medications** (optional): List any current medications.
*   **Known Allergies** (optional): List any known allergies.
*   **Smoking Status** (required): Select your smoking status.
    +   Non-smoker
    +   Former Smoker
    +   Current Smoker
    +   Occasional Smoker
*   **Pregnancy Status** (required): Select whether you are pregnant or nursing.
    +   Yes - Pregnant
    +   Yes - Nursing
    +   False
    +   Unsure
*   **Goal of This Consultation** (required): Choose the goal of this consultation.
    +   Get Information
    +   Explore Options
    +   Decide on Procedure
    +   Get Treatment Plan
    +   All of the Above
*   **Questions or Concerns** (optional): List any questions or concerns.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (required): Select your preferred contact method.
    +   Phone
    +   Email
    +   Text Message
    +   In Person
