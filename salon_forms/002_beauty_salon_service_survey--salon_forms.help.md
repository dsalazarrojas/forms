# Beauty Salon Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback about your recent visit to our beauty salon. Your input will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your recent visit in the "Date of Your Visit" field.
2. Select the primary service you received, such as a hair cut, color, or treatment, from the "What Service Did You Receive" dropdown list.
3. If you want to provide your stylist's name, enter it in the "Stylist Name" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, communication with the stylist, and several other aspects of your visit using the corresponding dropdown lists.
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field. (Note: This field is optional.)
6. Indicate whether you would like us to contact you about your feedback, and whether you would recommend our salon to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Your Visit**: Enter the date of your recent visit to the salon. This will help us track the date of your experience.
* **What Service Did You Receive**: Select the primary service you received during your visit. This will help us understand the type of service you had, such as a haircut, color, or massage.
* **Stylist Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of your stylist, if you want to give us feedback about them specifically.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Service**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service you received, using the dropdown list provided.
* **How Professional Was Your Stylist**: Rate the professionalism of your stylist, using the dropdown list.
* **Salon Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the salon, using the dropdown list.
* **Salon Ambiance and Comfort**: Rate the atmosphere and comfort level of the salon, using the dropdown list.
* **Quality of Products Used**: (Optional) Rate the quality of the products used during your visit. This will help us understand the effectiveness of our products.
* **Value for Money**: Rate whether the price you paid was fair for the service you received, using the dropdown list.
* **How Long Did You Wait**: Select how long you waited for your service, using the dropdown list.
* **Stylist Communication about Your Needs**: Rate how well your stylist listened and understood your needs, using the dropdown list.
* **Results Met Your Expectations**: Rate whether your expectations were met, using the dropdown list.
* **Would You Recommend This Salon to Others**: Indicate whether you would recommend our salon to others.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: (Optional) Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your visit.
* **May We Contact You About Your Feedback**: Indicate whether you would like us to contact you about your feedback.
