**Lash Extension Client Liability Waiver - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Lash Extension Client Liability Waiver is a document that acknowledges a client's understanding and acceptance of the terms and conditions of your salon's services. This waiver is a legal requirement to ensure that both you and your clients are protected. Please complete this form carefully to ensure a smooth and secure experience for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the provided field.
2. If you have a contact number, please enter it in the **Contact Number** field. This is optional.
3. If applicable, select a date for the **Service Date**.
4. Enter your **Client Email** if you have one.
5. For the **Waiver Signed** field, select one of the options to indicate whether you have read and understand the waiver or not.
6. If you selected "I have read and understand the waiver", enter the date you signed the waiver in the **Waiver Signed Date** field.
7. Select your role in the salon from the options in the **Salon Staff** field.
8. Finally, sign the **Client Signature** and **Salon Staff Signature** fields with your name as a verification of your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who is signing this waiver.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number to ensure we can reach you in case of any questions or concerns.
* **Service Date**: If applicable, select the date you plan to receive service from us.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address if you have one, this will help us keep you informed about your appointment and any important updates.
* **Waiver Signed**: Select one of the options to indicate your understanding of the waiver. If you select "I have read and understand the waiver", it is expected that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
* **Waiver Signed Date**: If you selected "I have read and understand the waiver", enter the date you signed the waiver.
* **Technician**: This field is currently not required but will be used to identify the technician who will be working with you.
* **Technician Signature**: Currently not required, this field will be used for record-keeping purposes.
* **Salon Staff**: Select one of the options to indicate your role in the salon (Owner, Manager, or Technician).
* **Client Signature**: Please sign below to verify that you have read and understand the waiver.
* **Salon Staff Signature**: Please sign below as a verification of your identity as salon staff.
* **Salon Staff Signature (2)**: This is not required, but for record-keeping purposes.
* 
Please note that some fields are required and others are optional, please fill in the required fields carefully to avoid any issues.
