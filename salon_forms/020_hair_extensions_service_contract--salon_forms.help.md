# Hair Extensions Service Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a service contract for hair extensions services, capturing essential details for both the salon and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the service provider from the provided options (Salon, Stylist, Both).
2. Choose the client(s) who will be receiving the services (Client, Client's Representative).
3. Enter the date of service.
4. Enter detailed information about the services to be rendered.
5. Calculate the total labor and equipment costs.
6. Enter the total cost of the services.
7. Select the payment method (Check, Bank Transfer, Credit Card).
8. Choose the payment schedule (On Signing of Contract, On Completion of Service).
9. Review and sign the service contract, and have both the client and provider sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provider** (Select One: Salon, Stylist, Both): 
  - This field determines who will be providing the services, whether it is the salon as a whole or an individual stylist.
* **Client** (Select Multiple: Client, Client's Representative): 
  - Choose the client(s) who will be receiving the services. This could be the individual or their representative.
* **Date**:
  - Enter the date when the services will be performed.
* **Service Details**:
  - Provide detailed information about the services to be rendered, such as type of hair extensions, color, and style.
* **Services Rendered**:
  - Describe the services that were actually performed during the appointment.
* **Total Hours**:
  - Calculate the total hours spent on the service.
* **Labor Cost**:
  - Calculate the labor cost of the services based on the total hours.
* **Equipment Cost**:
  - Calculate the equipment cost associated with the services.
* **Total Cost**:
  - Calculate the total cost of the services, including labor and equipment costs.
* **Payment Method** (Select One: Check, Bank Transfer, Credit Card): 
  - Choose the method of payment for the services.
* **Payment Schedule** (Select One: On Signing of Contract, On Completion of Service): 
  - Select when the payment is due, whether at the time of signing or after the services are completed.
* **Service Contract** (Note): 
  - Review and sign the service contract, which outlines the terms of the service.
* **Client Signature**:
  - Sign the contract as proof of agreement.
* **Provider Signature**:
  - Sign the contract as proof of acceptance of the terms of service.
* **Payment Terms**:
  - Review and discuss payment terms with the client before signing the contract.
* **Service Terms**:
  - Review and discuss service terms with the client before signing the contract.

Note that some fields may not be required, depending on the specific situation or service provider. Always ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
