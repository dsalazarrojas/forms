# Wax Room Check In Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the condition of wax equipment, chemicals, and other relevant materials at a salon. It helps ensure that staff can track and maintain inventory, identify potential issues, and maintain quality standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the date of the client's visit.
2. Enter the client's name as it appears on their record.
3. Choose the condition of the equipment using the provided options:
	* Clean
	* Dirty
	* In need of repair
	* Missing
	* Unknown
4. Select the condition of the chemicals using the provided options:
	* Good
	* Poor
	* Unknown
5. Choose the condition of the wax using the provided options:
	* Good
	* Poor
	* Unknown
6. Add any additional notes about the client's visit or equipment condition.
7. Sign the form with your name.
8. Indicate if the equipment is in good condition (True) or not (False).
9. Select the condition of the second set of chemicals using the provided options:
	* Good
	* Poor
	* Unknown
10. Select the condition of the second set of equipment using the provided options:
	* True
	* False
11. Add any additional notes about the second set of equipment condition.
12. Sign the form with your name again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client's Date of Visit**: The date of the client's visit, which helps track the timing of the assessment.
* **Client Name**: The client's name as it appears on their record.
* **Equipment Condition**: The condition of the equipment, with options for clean, dirty, in need of repair, missing, or unknown.
* **Chemicals Condition**: The condition of the chemicals, with options for good, poor, or unknown.
* **Wax Condition**: The condition of the wax, with options for good, poor, or unknown.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional information about the client's visit or equipment condition.
* **Technician's Signature**: Your name, indicating that you completed the form.
* **Equipment Condition (Yes/No)**: A yes or no answer to indicate if the equipment is in good condition.
* **Chemicals Condition 2**: The condition of the second set of chemicals, with options for good, poor, or unknown.
* **Chemicals Condition 2 2**: The condition of the second set of chemicals, with options for good, poor, or unknown.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about the second set of equipment condition.
* **Yes No Equipment Condition 2**: A yes or no answer to indicate if the second set of equipment is in good condition.
* **Technician's Name**: Your name, indicating that you completed the form.
* **Additional Notes (2)**: Any additional information about the second set of equipment condition.

**Tips**

* Be accurate and thorough when selecting the conditions of the equipment and chemicals.
* If unsure, choose "Unknown" for fields where you're not certain.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
