# Botulinum Toxin Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Botulinum Toxin Medical History Form is a questionnaire designed to collect information about a person's medical history, specifically regarding botulinum toxin treatments. It is intended to be completed by patients or their representatives before receiving botulinum toxin treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each page carefully and complete the sections in order.
- Fill out the personal information page (pages 1-4) with your name, date of birth, contact phone number, and any other relevant details.
- Answer the questions on the medical history page (pages 5-8) about any existing medical conditions, allergies, and medications you are taking.
- On the neuromuscular conditions page (pages 9-11), indicate whether you have any known conditions, such as myasthenia gravis or Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome.
- On the reproductive health page (pages 12-13), indicate your current pregnancy status and whether you are breastfeeding.
- On the previous botulinum toxin treatments page (page 14), confirm whether you have had previous botulinum toxin treatments.
- Finally, confirm that all information is accurate by checking the "I agree" box on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Personal Information -- (page 1)**: This is the first section of the form, where you will enter your personal information.
* **Full Name (page 2)**: Your legal name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (page 2)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number (page 3)**: Enter your best contact phone number so we can reach you for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **-- Medical History -- (page 5)**: This section collects information about any existing medical conditions, such as diabetes, hypertension, or heart disease.
* **Do you have any existing medical conditions (page 6)**: If you have any medical conditions, list them here.
* **Known Drug Allergies (page 7)**: If you have any allergies, list them here, including any reactions you have experienced.
* **Food Allergies (page 7)**: If you have any food sensitivities or allergies, list them here.
* **History of bleeding disorders (page 8)**: Indicate whether you have a history of bleeding disorders, such as hemophilia or platelet disorders.
* **-- Current Medications and Supplements -- (page 10)**: This section collects information about any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Current Medications (page 11)**: List the names and dosages of any medications you are currently taking.
* **Taking blood thinners or anticoagulants (page 11)**: Indicate whether you are taking blood thinners or anticoagulants, such as warfarin, aspirin, or ibuprofen.
* **Dietary Supplements (page 12)**: If you are taking any dietary supplements, such as vitamins or herbs, list them here.
* **-- Neuromuscular Conditions -- (page 13)**: This section collects information about any known neuromuscular conditions.
* **Myasthenia Gravis or similar condition (page 14)**: Indicate whether you have any known myasthenia gravis or similar conditions.
* **Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome (page 14)**: Indicate whether you have Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome.
* **-- Reproductive Health -- (page 15)**: This section collects information about your reproductive health.
* **Are you pregnant (page 16)**: Indicate whether you are currently pregnant.
* **Are you breastfeeding (page 16)**: Indicate whether you are currently breastfeeding.
* **Previous Botox or similar treatments (page 17)**: Confirm whether you have had previous botulinum toxin treatments.
* **I confirm all information is accurate (page 18)**: Check this box to confirm that all information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, choose the "Unsure" option.
- If you have any medical questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider before filling out this form.
- If you have any questions about this form, please contact us for assistance.
