# Hairdressing Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for booking a hairdressing appointment and providing personal information to ensure that our stylists can provide the best service for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details.
2. Select your preferred appointment date.
3. Choose the type of service you would like (e.g., haircut, color, style, chemical treatment).
4. Enter the duration of your appointment.
5. Select your preferred appointment time.
6. Choose the services you would like from the list of options.
7. Describe your current hair condition.
8. Briefly provide any relevant hair history.
9. Enter your phone number for communication.
10. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, text, required):
  Please enter your name, address, and other relevant personal details.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required):
  Enter the date you would like to book your appointment in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required):
  Choose the services you would like to receive during your appointment (e.g., haircut, color, style, chemical treatment).
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, number, required):
  Enter the length of your appointment in minutes.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required):
  Select the time you would like to book your appointment in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, select_multiple, required):
  Choose the services you would like from the list of options (e.g., wash, cut, dry, style).
* **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, select_one, required):
  Rate your current hair condition (good, fair, poor).
* **Hair History** (`hair_history`, note, required):
  Briefly describe any previous hair treatments or conditions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required):
  Enter a phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
  Enter your email address for communication and appointment reminders.

Please fill out the form accurately and in the correct format to ensure that our stylists can provide the best service for you.
