# Makeup, Lashes And Brow Services COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a COVID-19 liability waiver for clients undergoing makeup, lashes, and brow services at our salon. It is essential to read and understand the terms before signing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the salon in the `Salon Name` field.
2.  If you are receiving makeup services, select `Yes` in the `Makeup Services` field.
3.  Choose one or more services you would like to receive from the options in the `Select Services` field.
4.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
6.  Select the date of service in the `Date of Service` field.
7.  Select the time of service in the `Time of Service` field.
8.  Enter the estimated service time in minutes in the `Service Time` field.
9.  Enter the number of services you would like to receive in the `Number of Services` field.
10. Enter the price for the selected services in the `Price` field.
11. Sign the `Signature` and `Client Signature` fields to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
12. Enter the date of signature in the `Date of Signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Name** (`salon_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the salon where you will receive the services.
* **Makeup Services** (`makeup_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you are receiving makeup services, select `Yes`.
* **Select Services** (`services_selected`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one or more services you would like to receive from the options:
	+ Lash Extensions
	+ Brow Services
	+ Makeup
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required/optional): Select the date of service.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, required/optional): Select the time of service.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the estimated service time in minutes.
* **Number of Services** (`number_of_services`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of services you would like to receive.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price for the selected services.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Sign to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of signature.
