# Laser Hair Removal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Hair Removal Consent Form is a document that you, as a patient, will be required to sign before undergoing laser hair removal treatment. This form is essential to ensure you understand the treatment process, potential risks, and your role in the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully to understand the information required.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional)
Enter your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information (e.g., phone number and email).

### 2. **Procedure Information** (`procedure_info`, text, optional)
Describe the type of laser hair removal procedure you will undergo, including the location and any specific details about the treatment.

### 3. **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional)
This is the section where you will provide your informed consent for the laser hair removal treatment. Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately, and sign the form to confirm your consent.

### 4. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional)
If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries that may impact your treatment, please describe them in this section.

### 5. **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional)
List any allergies you have that may be relevant to the treatment. This will help your healthcare provider take necessary precautions.

### 6. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email, so your healthcare provider can reach you after the treatment.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If you have any concerns or questions, ask your healthcare provider before signing the form.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
 
Note: Please consult with a healthcare provider if you have any doubts or concerns about the treatment or form.
