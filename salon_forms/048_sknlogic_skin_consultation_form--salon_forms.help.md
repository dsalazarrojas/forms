# Sknlogic Skin Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sknlogic Skin Consultation Form is designed to collect information from clients or patients for skin consultation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Ensure that you answer all required fields truthfully.
3. If a field is optional, you can skip it if you feel that the information is not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **skin_concerns** (text, required/optional): Describe your skin concerns or issues you are experiencing.
* **age** (number, required/optional): Enter your age in numerical format.
* **phone_number** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **medical_conditions** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you have any medical conditions that might affect your skin.
* **medications** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you are taking any medications.
* **allergies** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you have any allergies.
* **medical_history** (text, required/optional): Describe your medical history, including any previous skin issues or conditions.
* **current_medications** (select_multiple, required/optional): Check if you are currently taking any medications.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed when filling out this form to ensure accurate consultation results.
- If you have any questions or concerns about a particular field, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- This form is for skin consultation purposes only, so please do not fill it out unless it is relevant to your skin concerns.
