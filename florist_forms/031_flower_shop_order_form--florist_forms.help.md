# Flower Shop Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for flowers at our flower shop. Please fill out the form with your details to place your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. You can skip any fields that you don't have information for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Please enter your delivery address where you would like to receive your flowers. This can be a street address or a post office box.
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would like to pick up your order at the shop or have it delivered. Please select either "Pick-up" or "Delivery".
* **Flower Type** (`flower_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of flower you would like to order. Please select one or more of the following options: "Roses", "Daisies", "Lilies", and "Tulips".
* **Number of Flowers** (`number_of_flowers`, number, optional): Enter the number of flowers you would like to order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up or receive your order. Please use the date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up or receive your order. Please use the 24-hour clock format "HH:MM:SS".
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you to confirm your order.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address where we can send you a confirmation of your order.
