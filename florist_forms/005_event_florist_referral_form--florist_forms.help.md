# Event Florist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to refer event-related services to potential vendors. It gathers information about the event type, date, and other relevant details to help vendors tailor their services accordingly. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with the required information about the event and vendor.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it for follow-up action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the vendor you are referring. This can be a personal name or a business name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): 
  Enter the email address associated with the vendor.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the phone number associated with the vendor.
* **Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the address of the vendor's business or location.
* **Venue Preference** (`vendor_venue`, `text`, required: false): 
  Describe the type of venue where the event will be held.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `text`, required: false): 
  Specify the type of event this referral is for, e.g., wedding, corporate event, etc.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the end time of the event.
* **Vendor Experience (years)** (`vendor_experience`, `number`, required: false): 
  Estimate the number of years the vendor has experience in this industry.
* **Services Offered** (`vendor_services`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Choose the types of services offered by the vendor, e.g., wedding and reception.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name of the person to contact for follow-up.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): 
  Enter the email address to contact for follow-up.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the phone number to contact for follow-up.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the address to contact for follow-up.
* **Additional Event Details** (`event_details`, `note`, required: false): 
  Add any additional information about the event that may help the vendor prepare.
* **Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_method`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Choose the method of follow-up, e.g., phone call, email, text.
* **Follow Up Message** (`follow_up_message`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter a brief message to send to the vendor for follow-up.
* **Event Florist Referral** (`event_florist_referral`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Indicate whether this vendor referral is for an event florist.
* **Follow Up Status** (`follow_up_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Track the follow-up status, e.g., sent, in progress, not followed up.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, `text`, required: false): 
  Add any comments about the follow-up actions taken.
* **Next Follow Up Date** (`follow_up_next_follow_up`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the next follow-up date.
* **Next Follow Up Time** (`follow_up_next_follow_up_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the next follow-up time.
* **Next Follow Up Call** (`follow_up_next_call`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the next follow-up call details.
* **Next Follow Up Email** (`follow_up_next_email`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the next follow-up email details.
* **Next Follow Up Email Sent** (`follow_up_next_email_sent`, `date`, required: false): 
  Enter the date of the next follow-up email sent.
* **Next Follow Up Email Time** (`follow_up_next_email_time`, `time`, required: false): 
  Enter the time of the next follow-up email sent.
* **Follow Up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, `note`, required: false): 
  Add any additional follow-up notes.
* **Vendor Referral Status** (`vendor_referral_status`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Track the status of the vendor referral, e.g., new, existing, followed.
* **Vendor Referral Date** (`vendor_referral_date`, `date`, required: false): 
  Track the date of the vendor referral. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and consistently.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting it for follow-up action.
* Do not submit this form multiple times for the same vendor.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance. 

Note: This help guide is a general outline of the form fields and their meanings. The specific fields and their meanings may be subject to change as your organization's needs and processes evolve.
