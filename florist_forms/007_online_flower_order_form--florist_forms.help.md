# Online Flower Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to order flowers online, providing details for a successful and accurate flower delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the types of flowers you want to buy.
2. Indicate the quantity of each selected flower.
3. Choose the desired delivery date for your order.
4. Specify the time you would like to receive your order.
5. Enter your address where the flowers will be delivered.
6. Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any order updates.
7. Enter your email address so we can send you order confirmations and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Flower Selection**: 
    Select one or more flowers from the provided list to include in your order.
* **Quantity**: Indicate the number of each selected flower you would like to receive.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when you would like to receive your order.
* **Time**: Choose the time you would like to receive your order.
* **Address**: Enter your address where the flowers will be delivered to.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any order updates.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you order confirmations and updates.

## Tips
* Ensure you select the correct types of flowers and their quantities to avoid any confusion with your order.
* Double-check your delivery date and time to ensure it aligns with your preferences.
* Enter your address accurately to ensure the correct delivery location.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at the phone number or email address provided.
