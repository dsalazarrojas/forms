# Wedding Flower Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for a wedding flower consultation. It will help wedding planners or brides-to-be to gather information about their flower preferences, including favorite flower types, colors, and shapes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required or have an asterisk (*) after the label.
3. Select the options for each field according to your preferences.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wedding Flower Consultation Form** (text): This field is for a brief description of your wedding flower consultation request.
* **Flower Types Liked** (text): List your favorite flower types, such as Roses, Lilies, Carnations, Orchids, or Sunflowers.
* **Wedding Theme** (select_one): Choose a theme that best describes your wedding style, such as Modern, Classic, Rustic, Coastal, or Bohemian.
* **Color Scheme** (select_one): Select the primary color scheme for your wedding flowers, such as White, Pink, Yellow, Red, or Purple.
* **Flower Budget** (number): Enter your estimated flower budget for the consultation.
* **Special Requests** (text): Describe any special requests or instructions for your wedding flowers.
* **Flowers Ordered** (select_multiple): Select the types of flowers you would like to order, such as Roses, Lilies, Carnations, Orchids, or Sunflowers.
* **Flower Color** (select_multiple): Select the colors of flowers you would like to order, such as Pink, White, Red, Yellow, or Purple.
* **Flower Shape** (select_multiple): Select the shapes of flowers you would like to order, such as Round, Square, Triangular, Heart, or Elliptical.
* **Wedding Date** (date): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of your wedding.
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time of your wedding.
* **Venue** (text): Describe the venue of your wedding.
* **Contact Person** (text): Provide the name of the person you would like to contact for the consultation.

Tips:

* Be as specific as possible when answering each field to ensure accurate consultation results.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, do not hesitate to describe them in the "Special Requests" field.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
