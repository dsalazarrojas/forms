# Floral Arrangement Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Floral Arrangement Billing Form is used by the florist to create an invoice for a floral arrangement order. This form collects information related to the order details, customer, billing, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **order_details** field with information about the order, such as the order number or description.
2. Complete the **customer_info** field with the customer's name, address, and contact information.
3. Enter the **billing_info** field with the billing address and contact information.
4. Calculate the **total_cost** of the order, including the cost of the flowers, vase, and any additional services.
5. Calculate the **service_charges**, if applicable.
6. Calculate the **subtotal**, which is the total cost plus service charges.
7. Select the **payment_method** from the dropdown list (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
8. Enter the **payment_date** when the payment was made or is due.
9. Calculate the **tax_rate** as a percentage (e.g., 8% for local tax).
10. Calculate the **tax_amount** by multiplying the subtotal by the tax rate.
11. Calculate the **total_amount** by adding the subtotal and tax amount.
12. Add any **notes** about the order in the free-text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details**: Enter a brief description of the order, such as the type of flowers, container, and any special requests.
* **customer_info**: Enter the customer's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **billing_info**: Enter the billing address and contact information for the customer.
* **total_cost**: The total cost of the order, including the cost of the flowers, vase, and any additional services.
* **service_charges**: If applicable, enter any service charges, such as delivery or setup fees.
* **subtotal**: The total cost plus service charges.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used for the transaction (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **payment_date**: Enter the date the payment was made or is due.
* **tax_rate**: Enter the tax rate as a percentage (e.g., 8% for local tax).
* **tax_amount**: The total tax amount calculated by multiplying the subtotal by the tax rate.
* **total_amount**: The total amount due, including the subtotal, tax amount, and any other charges.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
