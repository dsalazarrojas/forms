# House Plant Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Plant Delivery Form is designed to collect all necessary information from customers for the smooth execution of their house plant delivery order. This includes order details, customer information, payment options, delivery details, and other relevant information to ensure a successful delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section with the relevant information about your order.
2. In the **Customer Info** section, provide your contact information, including phone and email.
3. In the **Order Payment** section, select your preferred payment method.
4. In the **Delivery Details** section, enter your delivery location and choose the delivery time and date.
5. In the **Order Notes** section, add any additional notes about your order.
6. In the **Customer Phone** and **Customer Email** sections, enter your phone number and email address respectively.
7. In the **Delivery Location** section, enter the address where you want your order to be delivered.
8. In the **Plant Choice** section, select the type of plant you want to receive.
9. In the **Delivery Time** section, choose the time you want your order to be delivered.
10. In the **Delivery Date** section, choose the date you want your order to be delivered.
11. In the **Delivery Notes** section, add any additional delivery instructions.
12. In the **Order Total** section, enter the final amount for your order.
13. In the **Payment Gateway** section, select how you want to pay for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: This is the section where you can enter all the details of your order, such as the type of plant and the quantity you want to purchase.
* **Customer Info**: This section collects your contact information, including phone number and email address, to ensure we can contact you about your order.
* **Order Payment**: This section allows you to choose how you want to pay for your order.
* **Delivery Details**: In this section, you can enter the address where you want your order to be delivered, choose the delivery time, and date.
* **Order Notes**: Here, you can add any additional notes or special requests about your order.
* **Customer Phone**: Please enter your phone number in the E.164 format (e.g., +1-555-1234567).
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address here.
* **Delivery Location**: Enter the address where you want your order to be delivered.
* **Plant Choice**: Select the type of plant you want to receive.
* **Delivery Time**: Choose the time you want your order to be delivered.
* **Delivery Date**: Choose the date you want your order to be delivered.
* **Delivery Notes**: Add any additional delivery instructions here.
* **Order Total**: Enter the final amount for your order.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select how you want to pay for your order.
