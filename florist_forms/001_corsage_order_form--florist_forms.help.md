# Corsage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Corsage Order Form" is a tool for florists to collect information from customers about their corsage orders. This form helps florists gather essential details such as customer name, order notes, and payment information to process orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and phone number in the "customer_name" and "customer_phone" fields, respectively. These details help us contact you with any questions or updates about your order.
2. Select a delivery date and time that suits you in the "delivery_date" and "delivery_time" fields.
3. Choose the type of corsage you would like to order from the "corsage_type" dropdown menu. Our florists will prepare your chosen corsage according to your preferences.
4. Enter any special message or message for the recipient of the corsage in the "message" field.
5. Select the type of flower(s) you would like to include in your corsage from the "flower_type" dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Enter the price of the corsage in the "price" field. Please note that this is the price you are willing to pay, and our florists will review and adjust if necessary.
7. If you have any additional notes or instructions for your order, use the "notes" field.
8. Choose your preferred payment method from the "payment_method" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field. This is the address we will use to communicate with you about your order.
10. Enter the email address of the florist responsible for your order in the "florist_email" field.
11. Enter the phone number of the florist responsible for your order in the "florist_phone" field.
12. Finally, select the payment status of your order from the "payment_status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed for communication about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
* **Delivery Date** (optional): Select the date you would like your corsage delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (optional): Select the time you would like your corsage delivered.
* **Corsage Type** (optional): Choose the type of corsage you would like to order from our available options.
* **Message** (required): Enter a special message or message for the recipient of the corsage.
* **Flower Type** (optional): Select the type of flower(s) you would like to include in your corsage.
* **Price** (required): Enter the price you are willing to pay for your corsage.
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Payment Method** (optional): Choose your preferred payment method from our available options.
* **Customer Email** (required): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your order.
* **Florist Email** (required): Enter the email address of the florist responsible for your order.
* **Florist Phone** (required): Enter the phone number of the florist responsible for your order.
* **Payment Status** (optional): Select the status of your payment (e.g., paid, pending, etc.).
