# flower_seeds_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for flower seeds. It collects information about the user's flower seed preferences and shipping options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before submitting.
2.  Fill in the fields as prompted and choose your options carefully.
3.  Submit the form once you have finished filling it in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Main Info (main_info)**: A text field for entering any additional information about your order.
*   **Seeds Selection (seeds_selection)**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to select seeds, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Shipping Details (shipping_details)**: Choose 'Yes' if you want to receive shipping information, and 'No' otherwise.
*   **Order Details (order_details)**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
*   **Order Comments (order_comments)**: Enter any comments about your order in this text field.

## Tips
*   Ensure you fill in all required fields correctly.
*   Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
