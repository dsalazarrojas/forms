# Online Plant Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to place online orders for plants. It collects necessary information for successful order processing, including plant selection, payment details, and shipping information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to complete your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of plant you wish to order from the "Plants" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the quantity of the selected plant.
3. Choose your preferred payment gateway from the "Payment gateway" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your shipping address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter your customer name, email, and phone number.
6. If desired, add any additional customer notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First page**: Please enter your order information in this text box. You can type any additional comments or instructions that will help us process your order efficiently.
- **Plants**: Select the type of plant you wish to order from the dropdown menu. 
    - **Type**: Select One (`select_one`, `options`: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the selected plant.
    - **Type**: Number (`number`, `options`: None)
- **Payment info**: Select your preferred payment gateway from the dropdown menu.
    - **Type**: Select One (`select_one`, `options`: gateway1, gateway2, gateway3)
- **Payment gateway**: Select your preferred payment gateway from the dropdown menu.
    - **Type**: Select One (`select_one`, `options`: gateway1, gateway2, gateway3)
- **Shipping address**: Enter your shipping address in this text box.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **City**: Enter the city where you would like to have the plant shipped.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **State**: Enter the state where you would like to have the plant shipped.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Zip**: Enter the zip code where you would like to have the plant shipped.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Order date**: Enter the date you would like to receive your order.
    - **Type**: Date (`date`, `options`: None)
- **Order time**: Enter the time you would like to receive your order.
    - **Type**: Time (`time`, `options`: None)
- **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about your order.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Customer name**: Enter your name as it appears on your order.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Customer email**: Enter your email address.
    - **Type**: Email (`email`, `options`: None)
- **Customer phone**: Enter your phone number.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Customer notes**: Enter any additional customer notes.
    - **Type**: Text (`text`, `options`: None)
- **Order submitted**: Choose whether or not your order has been submitted.
    - **Type**: Select One (`select_one`, `options`: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
