# Holiday Wreath Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers to provide details about their holiday wreath order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of wreath you want to order (Holiday Wreath or Custom Wreath).
2. Enter your first name and last name in the Customer Details field.
3. Enter your address, city, and zip code in the Address field.
4. Select your email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
6. Specify the quantity of wreaths you want to order in the Quantity field.
7. Choose the date and time you want your order to be fulfilled in the Date Ordered and Time Ordered fields.
8. Add any additional notes or instructions in the Note field.
9. Finally, review your order details to ensure accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details (Select One)** (`customer_details`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the name and last name of the person who is making the order. You can choose either First Name or Last Name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's address where they want the wreath to be delivered.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where the customer's address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required: false): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Order Details (Select Multiple)** (`order_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type of wreath you want to order (Holiday Wreath or Custom Wreath).
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of wreaths you want to order.
* **Date Ordered** (`date_ordered`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when you want your order to be fulfilled.
* **Time Ordered** (`time_ordered`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time when you want your order to be fulfilled.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or instructions for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Double-check your order details to ensure accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any special instructions or requests, add them in the Note field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the florist or flower shop directly.
* You can also order a custom wreath and specify any special requests in the Order Details field.
