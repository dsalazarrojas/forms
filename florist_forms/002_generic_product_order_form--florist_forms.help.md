# Generic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic Product Order Form is a customer-facing form designed to allow customers to place orders for products in a simple and easy-to-use interface.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the product you wish to order using the "Product Select" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the quantity and price of the product.
4. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
5. Enter your shipping address.
6. Provide any additional comments or special requests in the "Order Comments" field.
7. Review your information and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you wish to order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of units of the product you wish to purchase.
* **Price**: Enter the price of the product you wish to purchase.
* **Product Select**: Select the product you wish to order from the dropdown menu.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any special requests or comments about your order.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional information or notes about your order.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct product from the "Product Select" dropdown menu.
* Double-check your email address to ensure it is correct.
* Double-check your phone number to ensure it is correct.
* Enter your shipping address accurately to ensure timely delivery. 
* If you have any special requests or comments, enter them in the "Order Comments" field. 
* If you have any additional information or notes, enter them in the "Customer Note" field.
