# Christmas Tree Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Christmas Tree Order Form is designed for customers to provide their information and preferences for a customized Christmas tree order. This form collects essential details about the customer, their tree selection, delivery, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** in the **Full Name** field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to receive order confirmation and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** for delivery coordination purposes.
4. If you are ordering for a company, enter the **Company Name**.
5. Select the **Tree Species** you desire (e.g., Fraser Fir, Balsam Fir, etc.).
6. Choose the **Tree Height** you prefer (e.g., 4-5 feet, 6-7 feet, etc.).
7. Select the **Tree Grade** of your preference (e.g., Premium Grade A, Standard Grade B, etc.).
8. Choose the quantity of trees you need.
9. Select the type of **Tree Stand** you require (e.g., No, Yes basic stand, etc.).
10. If needed, select the **Stand Size** to match your tree trunk size.
11. Choose any **Additional Products** you would like to include with your order (e.g., tree netting, etc.).
12. Select your **Delivery Method** (e.g., Pickup at farm, Local delivery, etc.).
13. Choose your preferred **Delivery Date** for pickup or delivery.
14. If you have a specific delivery address, enter it in the **Delivery Address** field.
15. Provide any **Delivery Instructions** (e.g., gate codes, special directions, etc.).
16. Choose your **Payment Method** (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
17. Confirm if you have made a **Deposit Payment**.
18. If this is a gift, enter a **Gift Message** for the recipient.
19. If you have any specific **Special Requests**, enter them in the **Special Requests** field.
20. Finally, let us know how you **Hear About Us** (e.g., Website, Social Media, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as required for order processing and delivery purposes.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for order confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for delivery coordination and any necessary contact.
* **Company Name**: If ordering for a business, enter your company name for billing purposes.
* **Tree Species**: Choose the type of tree you desire from the available options.
* **Tree Height**: Select the height of the tree you prefer from the available options.
* **Tree Grade**: Choose the quality grade of the tree you want.
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of trees you need.
* **Tree Stand Needed**: Select if you require a tree stand.
* **Stand Size**: If you need a tree stand, select the size that matches your tree trunk size.
* **Additional Products**: Choose any additional products you want to include with your order.
* **Delivery Method**: Select how you want to receive your tree (e.g., pickup, local delivery, etc.).
* **Delivery Date**: Choose your preferred delivery date for pickup or delivery.
* **Delivery Address**: If you have a specific delivery address, enter it here.
* **Delivery Instructions**: Provide any necessary delivery instructions (e.g., gate codes, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, etc.).
* **Deposit Paid**: Confirm if you have made a deposit payment.
* **Gift Message**: If this is a gift, enter a message for the recipient.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requirements, enter them here.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Let us know how you heard about our farm.

Note that some fields are not required but are optional for providing additional information or preferences.
