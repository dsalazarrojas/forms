# Live Plant Delivery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Plant Delivery Order Form is used to gather information for live plant delivery orders. Fill out this form to place an order for live plants to be delivered to your location.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the delivery date when you would like to receive your live plants.
2. Select a delivery time that suits you best.
3. Choose your preferred payment method for the order.
4. Select the type of plant you would like to order.
5. Specify the quantity of plants you would like to receive.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you about the order.
7. Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
8. Enter the amount you will pay for the order.
9. Write a note with any additional shipping address information.
10. Fill in your name so we can confirm the order recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive your live plants.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required: false): Select a delivery time that suits you best for live plant delivery.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to pay for your order. Options are likely for credit card, PayPal, etc.
*   **Plant Type** (`plant_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type(s) of plants you would like to order. Examples are likely provided for selection.
*   **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Specify the number of plants you would like to receive.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the order.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you will pay for the order.
*   **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, note, required: false): Write a note with any additional shipping address information.
*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your name so we can confirm the order recipient.
