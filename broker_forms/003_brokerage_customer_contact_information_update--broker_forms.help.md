# Brokerage Customer Contact Information Update - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update a client's contact information, including their address, phone number, and email address. It's essential to fill this out only when your contact information has changed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with the most up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Make sure to re-enter your phone number and email address in the confirmation fields (Phone Number and Confirm New Phone Number, Email Address and Confirm New Email Address) to ensure accuracy.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail, or Text Message).
4. Decide if you would like to opt-out of marketing communications.
5. Choose the date when your contact information changed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Client ID Number** (`client_id`, text, required): Enter your unique account identifier.
* **Previous Phone Number on File** (`previous_phone`, text, optional): If your phone number has changed, enter your previous phone number.
* **Current Phone Number** (`current_phone`, text, required): Enter your current phone number.
* **Previous Email on File** (`previous_email`, email, optional): If your email address has changed, enter your previous email address.
* **Current Email Address** (`current_email`, email, required): Enter your current email address.
* **Previous Mailing Address** (`previous_address`, text, optional): If your address has changed, enter your previous address.
* **Current Mailing Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`current_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`current_state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`current_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`phone_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Mail, or Text Message).
* **Opt-out of Marketing Communications** (`do_not_contact`, select_one, required): Check if you prefer not to receive marketing communications.
* **Date of Change** (`update_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your contact information changed.
* **Confirm New Phone Number** (`confirmation_phone`, text, required): Re-enter your current phone number to confirm.
* **Confirm New Email Address** (`confirmation_email`, email, required): Re-enter your current email address to confirm.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is designed to ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate. If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know.
