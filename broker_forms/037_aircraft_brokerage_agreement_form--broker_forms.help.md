# Aircraft Brokerage Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document and outline the terms and conditions of the aircraft brokerage agreement between the broker and the client.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each section accurately and thoroughly, making sure to include all required fields.
2.  Ensure that all necessary information is provided, and any missing information is addressed.
3.  Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that all terms and conditions are understood and agreed upon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`): The date on which the agreement is made.
*   **Agreement Number** (`agreement_number`): A reference number for the agreement.
*   **Broker Name** (`broker_name`): The full name of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Broker License Number** (`broker_license`): The license or certification number of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Broker Address** (`broker_address`): The business address of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Broker Phone** (`broker_phone`): The contact phone number of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Broker Email** (`broker_email`): The contact email of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`): The full name of the client.
*   **Client Type** (`client_type`): The type of client (Buyer or Seller, or Both).
*   **Client Address** (`client_address`): The mailing address of the client.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`): The contact phone number of the client.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`): The contact email of the client.
*   **Aircraft Make** (`aircraft_make`): The manufacturer of the aircraft.
*   **Aircraft Model** (`aircraft_model`): The model of the aircraft.
*   **Aircraft Year** (`aircraft_year`): The year of manufacture of the aircraft.
*   **Aircraft Registration** (`aircraft_registration`): The N-number or tail number of the aircraft.
*   **Aircraft Serial Number** (`aircraft_serial`): The manufacturer's serial number of the aircraft.
*   **Listing Price (USD)** (`listing_price`): The asking price of the aircraft.
*   **Minimum Acceptable Price (USD)** (`minimum_acceptable_price`): The lowest price the client is willing to pay.
*   **Commission Rate (%)** (`commission_rate`): The commission percentage paid to the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Commission Structure** (`commission_structure`): How the commission is calculated (Percentage of sale price, Fixed fee, Tiered structure, or Other).
*   **Exclusive Agreement** (`exclusive_agreement`): Whether this agreement is exclusive or not.
*   **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_duration`): The length of the agreement.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`): The date when the agreement begins.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`): The date when the agreement expires.
*   **Marketing Plan** (`marketing_plan`): A description of the marketing approach.
*   **Advertising Budget (USD)** (`advertising_budget`): The budget for marketing.
*   **Showing Procedures** (`showing_procedures`): How showings will be handled.
*   **Disclosure Requirements** (`disclosure_requirements`): Required disclosures.
*   **Earnest Money Requirements** (`earnest_money`): The deposit amount required.
*   **Closing Costs Responsibility** (`closing_costs`): Who pays closing costs.
*   **Termination Conditions** (`termination_clause`): How the agreement can be terminated.
*   **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`): Any other terms.
*   **Broker Signature** (`broker_signature`): A digital signature of the broker or brokerage firm.
*   **Client Signature** (`client_signature`): A digital signature of the client.
*   **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): An optional witness signature.

**Tips:**

*   Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that all terms and conditions are understood and agreed upon.
*   This form is intended to document and outline the terms and conditions of the agreement. Please review it carefully to ensure that all necessary information is included.
