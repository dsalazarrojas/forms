# real_estate_transaction_summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "real_estate_transaction_summary" form is designed to collect detailed information on real estate sales and purchases. It's used to track and document transactional data, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of real estate deals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the **Transaction Details** page.
2. Fill in **transaction_details**, providing a brief description of the sale or purchase.
3. Move on to **buyer_info**, entering the buyer's details, including their name, contact, and address.
4. Enter the **property_details**, providing a description of the property, including its type and address.
5. In **sale_info**, enter the sale's details, such as sale date and price.
6. Select the sale type from **sale_type** and choose the type of property from **property_type**.
7. Add any additional sale terms in **sale_terms**.
8. Enter the buyer's name in **buyer_name** and the sale status in **sale_status**.
9. Provide the property address in **property_address** and the agent's details in **agent_info**, including their name, email, and phone number.
10. Enter the agency's details, such as their email and name.
11. Complete the transaction date in **transaction_date**, the purchase price and sale price currencies in **purchase_price_currency** and **sale_price_currency**, respectively.
12. Enter the total value of the transaction in **total_value**.
13. Finally, enter the buyer's address in **buyer_address** and the transaction number in **transaction_number**.

* **Transaction Details (transaction_details)**: A brief description of the transaction.
* **Buyer Info (buyer_info)**: The buyer's name, contact, and address.

* **Sale Info (sale_info)**: Sale date, price, and other sale details.
* **Sale Type (sale_type)**: Choose the type of sale (Sale or Lease).
* **Property Type (property_type)**: Choose the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).

* **Agent Email (agent_email)**: The agent's email address.
* **Agency Info (agency_info)**: The agency's email address.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: The buyer or agent's phone number.
* **Transaction Number (transaction_number)**: The unique number assigned to the transaction.
* **Sale Date (sale_date)**: The date of the sale.
* **Purchase Price Currency (purchase_price_currency)**: The currency of the purchase price.
