# Agent Agreement And Compensation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the terms and conditions of an agent's agreement with a brokerage firm, including compensation structures, payment schedules, and termination clauses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2.  Enter your full legal name as the agent.
3.  Enter the name of the employing broker.
4.  Describe your role and responsibilities in the role description field.
5.  Enter your compensation structure, including commission splits and fees.
6.  Specify the payment frequency and terms in the payment schedule field.
7.  Enter the effective date of the agreement.
8.  Summarize the termination clause, if applicable.
9.  Sign the form electronically with your full name as the agent.
10. Sign the form electronically with the name of the broker or authorized representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agent name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as the agent.
*   **Broker firm** (`broker_firm`, text, required): Enter the name of the employing broker.
*   **Role description** (`role_description`, text, required): Describe your duties and expectations as an agent.
*   **Compensation structure** (`compensation_structure`, text, required): Enter your commission split and fees.
*   **Payment schedule** (`payment_schedule`, text, required): Specify the frequency and payment terms.
*   **Effective date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date the agreement begins.
*   **Termination clause summary** (`termination_clause`, text, optional): Summarize the conditions for termination, if applicable.
*   **Agent signature name** (`agent_signature_name`, text, required): Sign the form electronically with your full name.
*   **Broker signature name** (`broker_signature_name`, text, required): Sign the form electronically with the name of the broker or authorized representative.
