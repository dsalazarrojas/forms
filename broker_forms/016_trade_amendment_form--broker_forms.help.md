# Trade Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trade Amendment Form is used to record and document trade amendments, which are changes made to existing trade agreements or contracts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Trade Amendment Form page with the required information.
2. Enter the date of the amendment in the "Date Of Amendment" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the amendment in the "Description" field.
4. Select the countries involved in the "Countries Involved" field.
5. Select the parties involved in the "Parties Involved" field.
6. Enter a summary of the amendment in the "Amendment Summary" field.
7. Check if there are any changes to the terms in the "Changes To Terms" field.
8. Enter the reason for the amendment in the "Reason For Amendment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trade Amendment Form** (`trade_amendment_form`, text, required): This field is the main title of the form and is required.
* **Date Of Amendment** (`date_of_amendment`, date, required): Enter the date on which the amendment was made.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the amendment.
* **Countries Involved** (`countries_involved`, select_multiple, required): Select all the countries involved in the amendment.
* **Parties Involved** (`parties_involved`, select_multiple, required): Select all the parties involved in the amendment.
* **Amendment Summary** (`amendment_summary`, text, optional): Enter a summary of the amendment.
* **Changes To Terms** (`changes_to_terms`, select_one, required): Check if there are any changes to the terms of the agreement.
* **Reason For Amendment** (`reason_for_amendment`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the amendment.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
