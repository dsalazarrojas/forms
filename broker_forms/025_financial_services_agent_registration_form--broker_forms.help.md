# financial_services_agent_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial services agent registration form is designed to gather essential information from agents who want to be recognized and authorized as financial service providers. This guide will walk you through the form fields and their explanations to ensure accurate completion.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the 'Submit' button at the end of the form to initiate the registration process.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information:
    - **Agent Info**: Provide a brief description about yourself as an agent.
    - **Agent Phone**: Enter your phone number.
    - **Agent Email**: Enter your work email address.
    - **Agent Address**: Enter your work address.
    - **Agent Zip**: Enter your zip code for the agent address.
    - **Agent Country**: Select your country from the dropdown list (optional but recommended).
    - **Agent State**: Select your state from the dropdown list (optional but recommended).
    - **Agent City**: Enter your work city.
    - **Agent Company**: Enter your work company name (if applicable).
    - **Agent Position**: Enter your position or title within the company (if applicable).
    - **Agent Licensing Info**: Enter any necessary licensing information (if applicable).
    - **Agent Licensing Type**: Select whether you have any licensing information (optional).
    - **Agent Agency**: Select whether you have an agency affiliation (optional).
    - **Agent License Number**: Enter any license number associated with your work (if applicable).
    - **Agent National ID**: Enter your national ID number (if applicable).
    - **Agent Business Name**: Enter the name of your business (if applicable).
    - **Agent Business Address**: Enter the address of your business (if applicable).
    - **Agent Business Phone**: Enter the phone number of your business (if applicable).
    - **Agent Business Email**: Enter the email address of your business (if applicable).
    - **Agent Business Zip**: Enter the zip code of your business (if applicable).
    - **Agent Fax**: Enter the fax number of your business (if applicable).
    - **Date Registered**: Enter the date you registered as a financial services agent (if applicable).
