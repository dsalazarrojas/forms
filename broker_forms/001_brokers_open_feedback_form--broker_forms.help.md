# Brokers Open Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brokers Open Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from real estate agents on their experience with a specific property during an open house event. The form helps identify areas of improvement and provides valuable information to the listing agent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Navigate through each page and fill in the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance if you are unsure about any question or field.
3. Ensure that you are completing the form for the correct property and that you have not already submitted feedback for the same property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your license.
* **Brokerage Company** (`brokerage`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your real estate brokerage.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your best contact number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property you viewed.
* **Open House Date** (`open_house_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the open house event.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall impression of the property. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
* **Property Condition Rating** (`property_condition`, `select_one`, required): Rate the property condition. Choose from:
	+ Move-in Ready
	+ Minor Repairs Needed
	+ Moderate Repairs Needed
	+ Major Repairs Needed
	+ Fixer Upper
* **Pricing Feedback** (`pricing_feedback`, `select_one`, required): Choose your opinion on the listing price. Select from:
	+ Priced Too High
	+ Priced Slightly High
	+ Priced Appropriately
	+ Priced Slightly Low
	+ Priced Too Low
* **Showing Experience** (`showing_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate the showing process. Choose from:
	+ Very Smooth
	+ Smooth
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Would You Show This to Clients** (`client_suitability`, `select_one`, required): Is this property suitable for your clients? Choose from:
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
* **Best Features** (`best_features`, `text`, optional): Mention what stood out positively about the property.
* **Concerns or Issues** (`concerns`, `text`, optional): Mention any concerns or issues you noticed.
* **Recommended Improvements** (`recommended_improvements`, `text`, optional): Suggest improvements for the seller.
* **Estimated Showings Interest** (`estimated_showings`, `select_one`, optional): Estimate the number of showings you expect. Choose from:
	+ 0-2
	+ 3-5
	+ 6-10
	+ 11-15
	+ 15+
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any other feedback.
* **Would You Recommend This Listing** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, optional): Would you recommend this listing to other agents? Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
