# Permission To Trade Vehicle Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to trade a vehicle with a customer. It's designed to gather essential information about the vehicle and the customer's request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "owner" field with the customer's name and contact information.
2. Enter the "vehicle" field with the vehicle's make, model, and registration number.
3. Provide details about the vehicle in the "vehicle details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **owner** (`owner`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **vehicle** (`vehicle`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and registration number.
* **vehicle details** (`vehicle details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the vehicle, such as its condition or any relevant features.
