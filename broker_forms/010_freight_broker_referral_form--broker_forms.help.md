# Freight Broker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Broker Referral Form is designed to gather referral information from partners and customers. This form is typically used as a follow-up or as part of a recurring process to maintain relationships with existing clients or partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form sections carefully** to ensure you are providing all necessary information.
2. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**.
3. **Submit the form** once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Referral Info 
* **Referral Info** (`main_page_referral_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the referral, including the name, business or organization being referred, and any relevant details.
### Business Details
* **Business Details** (`main_page_business_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant business details, such as industry, type, or specific services offered by the referred business.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`main_page_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for the referred business or individual, including name, job title, phone number, and email address.
### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`main_page_additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional information you think is relevant to the referral, such as a brief description, website, or social media handles.
### Signature
* **Signature** (`main_page_signature`, `text`, required: false): Please sign and date your name and title to confirm you have reviewed and agreed on the referral information provided.
### Review
* **Review** (`main_page_review`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments or feedback on the referral, such as successes or areas of improvement.
### Referral Source
* **Referral Source** (`main_page_referral_source`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the source of the referral, such as a networking event, advertisement, or personal recommendation.

## Tips
* Double-check the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it.
* Ensure you have permission to collect and share referral information.
* Review and discuss the referral with the referred party before submitting the form.
* Consider adding a note or comment section for future reference or follow-up.
