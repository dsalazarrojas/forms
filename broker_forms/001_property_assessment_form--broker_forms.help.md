# Property Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Assessment Form is a self-reporting form for property owners to provide detailed information about their properties. This form helps us update property records and ensure accuracy in our assessment data. Please fill out this form carefully, and avoid duplicating entries that are already in the database.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Determine the Type of Property

* Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are reporting a property type that already exists in our database.
* If you answer "Yes", please continue to fill out the form to provide additional information about the property.
* If you answer "No", please proceed to step 2.

### Step 2: Provide Property Details

* Enter the address of the property.
* Ensure the address matches the property you are reporting.
* This field is required.

### Step 3: Add Property Value and Year Built Information

* Enter the current value of the property.
* Choose the year the property was built from the date options provided.
* Both fields are optional, but they will help us better assess your property.

### Step 4: Add Assessment Information

* Enter the date of the property assessment.
* This field is optional, but it will help us keep track of changes.
* Enter the name of the property assessor who made the assessment.
* This field is optional, but it will help us verify the accuracy of the information provided.

### Step 5: Additional Contact Information

* Enter the property owner's email address.
* Enter the property owner's phone number.
* Both fields are optional, but they will help us contact you with any questions or updates about your property.

### Tips

* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
* Avoid duplicating entries by checking our database before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
* If you have already submitted a form for this property, please do not fill out this form again.
