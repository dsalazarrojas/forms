# Investment Management Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Management Quote Form is designed to collect detailed information about investments and contact details from end-users. This form will help you understand their investment preferences, risk tolerance, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the type of investment you are interested in (e.g., Stocks, Bonds, Mutual Funds, etc.).
2. Input your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide information about the investment type, frequency, amount, and return expectations.
4. Choose the risk level and asset allocation that best suits your investment goals.
5. Input additional information and comments as necessary.
6. Finally, submit your completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Investment Management** (`investment_management_quote_form`, select_multiple): Choose the type of investment management you require (Investment Management, Financial Planning, or Wealth Management).
- **Name** (`contact_name`, text): Enter your full name for contact purposes.
- **Email** (`contact_email`, text): Input your email address so that we can contact you regarding your investment.
- **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Input your phone number so that we can contact you efficiently.
- **Investment Type** (`investment_type`, select_one): Select the type of investment you are interested in (e.g., Stocks, Bonds, Mutual Funds, etc.).
- **Investment Invested** (`investment_invested`, number): Input the amount invested in the investment type selected.
- **Investment Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, select_one): Choose how often you invest (One-time or Regular).
- **Investment Amount** (`investment_amount`, number): Input the amount invested in the investment type selected.
- **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, number): Estimate the expected return on investment.
- **Investment Duration** (`investment_duration`, number): Input the duration of the investment.
- **Investment Frequency 2** (`investment_frequency_2`, select_one): Choose how often you invest in the second investment type (One-time or Regular).
- **Investment Amount 2** (`investment_amount_2`, number): Input the amount invested in the second investment type selected.
- **Investment Return 2** (`investment_return_2`, number): Estimate the expected return on the second investment type.
- **Investment Duration 2** (`investment_duration_2`, number): Input the duration of the second investment type.
- **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one): Choose your risk level (High, Medium, or Low).
- **Asset Allocation** (`asset_allocation`, select_multiple): Select the asset classes you want to allocate to (e.g., Cash, Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate).
- **Investment Objective** (`investment_objective`, select_one): Choose your investment objective (Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term).
- **Asset Allocation 2** (`asset_allocation_2`, select_multiple): Select the asset classes for the second investment type (e.g., Cash, Stocks, Bonds, Real Estate).
- **Investment Objective 2** (`investment_objective_2`, select_one): Choose the investment objective for the second investment type (Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term).
- **Contact Email 2** (`contact_email_2`, text): Input the email address of the secondary contact person.
- **Contact Phone 2** (`contact_phone_2`, text): Input the phone number of the secondary contact person.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text): Input any additional information relevant to your investment.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text): Input any comments or notes about your investment.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text): Click the submit button to submit your form for review.
