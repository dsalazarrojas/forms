# Insurance Binder Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Binder Request Form is used to collect information for new insurance policies or updates to existing policies. It helps gather details such as policy types, contract numbers, and dates associated with the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Policy Type that best describes the policy you are requesting.
2. Fill in the dates associated with the policy, including Contract Start Date, Contract End Date, Policy Start Date, and Policy End Date.
3. Provide the Contract Number and Policy Number associated with the policy.
4. Enter the number of Policy Periods.
5. Ensure all required and optional fields are filled in accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Type** (`policy_type`, select_one): Select the type of policy you are requesting. This will help us understand the policy better.
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Policy Start Date** (`policy_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of the policy.
* **Policy End Date** (`policy_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of the policy.
* **Policy Period** (`policy_period`, number): Enter the number of policy periods.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, number): Enter the contract number.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number): Enter the policy number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date): Enter the start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date): Enter the end date.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date): Enter the contract date.
* **Policy Date** (`policy_date`, date): Enter the policy date.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date): Enter the effective date.
* **Coverage Date** (`coverage_date`, date): Enter the coverage date.
* **Policy Period 2** (`policy_period_2`, number): Enter the policy period 2.
* **Contract Period** (`contract_period`, number): Enter the contract period.
* **Policy Contract Date** (`policy_contract_date`, date): Enter the policy contract date.
* **Coverage Policy Date** (`coverage_policy_date`, date): Enter the coverage policy date.
* **Contract Coverage Date** (`contract_coverage_date`, date): Enter the contract coverage date.
* **Policy Effective Date** (`policy_effective_date`, date): Enter the policy effective date.

Note: All dates should be entered in the format YYYY-MM-DD. All numbers should be whole numbers.
