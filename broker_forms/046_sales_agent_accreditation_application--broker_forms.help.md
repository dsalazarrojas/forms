# Sales Agent Accreditation Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for sales agents to be accredited to represent and work on behalf of a company. The form gathers information about the sales agent, their company, and the documents uploaded.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Ensure that you upload all the necessary documents.
3. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Agent Accreditation Application Form** (`sales_agent_accreditation_application_form`, text, required):
This is the main application form where you will be filling out your details.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required):
Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Company Representative Name** (`company_representative_name`, text, required):
Enter the name of the person who will be your representative.
* **Company Representative Title** (`company_representative_title`, text, required):
Enter the title of the company representative.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required):
Enter the address of your company.
* **City** (`city`, text, required):
Enter the city of your company.
* **State** (`state`, text, required):
Enter the state of your company.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required):
Enter the zip code of your company.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required):
Enter the country of your company.
* **Documents Uploaded** (`documents_uploaded`, note, false):
This field is for any additional documents you would like to upload.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required):
Upload any required documents in the allowed formats: pdf, doc, docx, xls, xlsx, ppt, pptx, jpg, jpeg, png, txt, csv.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required):
Select 'Yes' to submit your application.
* **Decline** (`decline`, select_one, false):
Select 'Yes' to decline submitting your application.
* **Pending** (`pending`, select_multiple, false):
Select 'Yes' to mark your application as pending.
Note: This field is not required but will help in tracking the status of your application.
