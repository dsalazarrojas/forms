# Annual Compliance Review For Brokers - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Compliance Review For Brokers form is designed to help firms and brokers demonstrate their commitment to regulatory compliance and industry standards. It is essential to complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure your firm's compliance status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Broker Information**:
	* Please enter your full name as the broker (Page 1).
	* Provide your firm's name and any relevant license or ID number (Page 4).
2. **Compliance Period**:
	* Select the year of the compliance review (Page 8).
	* Enter the date you submit this review (Page 9).
3. **Regulatory Compliance**:
	* Indicate your compliance status with fiduciary duties (Page 11).
	* Confirm your compliance with conduct rules (Page 12).
	* Confirm your compliance with disclosure requirements (Page 13).
4. **Client Protection and Security**:
	* Verify that you handle client funds securely (Page 15).
	* Confirm your commitment to protecting client data (Page 16).
	* Manage conflicts of interest (Page 17).
5. **Violations and Incidents**:
	* Report any regulatory violations that occurred this year (Page 19).
	* Provide details on any reported violations (Page 20).
6. **Training and Education**:
	* Indicate the status of compliance training completed (Page 23).
	* Select relevant training topics completed (Page 24).
7. **Attestation**:
	* Review and confirm your compliance status for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Broker Name**: Enter your full name as the broker.
* **Broker ID or License Number**: Provide your firm's license or ID number.
* **Brokerage Firm Name**: Enter your firm's name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your firm's contact phone number (optional).
* **Review Year**: Select the year of the compliance review.
* **Review Submission Date**: Enter the date you submit this review.
* **Fiduciary Duty Compliance**: Indicate your firm's status with fiduciary duties.
* **Code of Conduct and Rules**: Confirm your compliance with conduct rules.
* **Disclosure Requirements**: Confirm your compliance with disclosure requirements.
* **Client Funds Handling**: Verify that you handle client funds securely.
* **Privacy and Data Protection**: Confirm your commitment to protecting client data.
* **Conflict of Interest Management**: Manage conflicts of interest.
* **Regulatory Violations This Year**: Report any regulatory violations that occurred this year.
* **Violation Details**: Provide details on any reported violations.
* **Corrective Actions Taken**: Describe how violations were addressed.
* **Compliance Training Completed**: Indicate the status of compliance training completed.
* **Training Topics Completed**: Select relevant training topics completed.
* **All Other Topics**: Select any other topics relevant to the training completed.
* **Attestation**: Review and confirm your compliance status for each section.

## Tips
* Double-check each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting.
* Verify that you've answered all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Take care to complete the form according to the instructions and guidelines provided.
