# Homeownership Readiness Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's or household's readiness to purchase a home, providing a comprehensive evaluation of their financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions accurately and thoroughly, as your responses will determine the assessment results.
2. Enter numerical values when prompted, and select options from the provided lists.
3. Ensure you provide honest and realistic answers, as they will influence the outcome.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Income** (Page 1): Enter the total household income.
	+ This value will be used to evaluate your current income and debt-to-income ratio.
* **Household Size** (Page 1): Select the number of household members.
	+ This will affect the assessment of your household's overall financial stability.
* **Net Worth** (Page 2): Enter the total value of your household's assets and savings.
	+ This will be used to measure your financial health and ability to cover a home's price.
* **Mortgage History** (Page 2): Select the number of months you have been paying off your mortgage.
	+ This will help evaluate your mortgage repayment history.
* **Employment Status** (Page 3): Select your current employment status.
	+ This will assess your income stability and employment history.
* **Income Source** (Page 3): Select the sources of your income (salary, self-employed, or other).
	+ This will help understand your income stability.
* **Credit Score** (Page 4): Enter your current credit score (if known).
	+ This will measure your credit health.
* **Debt Obligations** (Page 4): Enter the total debt balance.
	+ This will affect your overall debt-to-income ratio.
* **Assets Owned** (Page 5): Enter the total value of your assets (savings, investments, etc.).
	+ This will measure your overall financial health.
* **Assets Value** (Page 5): Enter the total value of your assets.
	+ This will assess your asset base.
* **Financial Goals** (Page 6): Select your primary financial goal (own home, rent, or invest).
	+ This will guide the assessment of your homebuying readiness.
* **Home Ownership Goals** (Page 6): Select your desired homebuying goals (own home, rent, or invest).
	+ This will help evaluate your homebuying intentions.
* **Mortgage Funding** (Page 7): Select the type of mortgage funding you desire (conventional, FHA, VA, or other).
	+ This will affect your mortgage options.
* **Home Price** (Page 8): Enter the desired home price.
	+ This will measure your affordability.
* **Credit History** (Page 8): Select your credit history (no banks, 3 banks, or 5 banks).
	+ This will assess your credit health.
* **Loan Amount** (Page 9): Enter the desired loan amount.
	+ This will measure your borrowing capacity.
* **Down Payment** (Page 9): Enter the desired down payment percentage.
	+ This will affect your overall loan-to-value ratio.
* **Credit Inquiries** (Page 10): Select the number of credit inquiries you have made.
	+ This will evaluate the risk associated with your credit history.
* **Employment History** (Page 10): Select your employment history (0, 1, or 2 years).
	+ This will measure your employment stability.
* **Employment Verification** (Page 10): Select your employment verification status (verified or not verified).
	+ This will assess your employment verification.
* **Income Verification** (Page 10): Select your income verification status (verified or not verified).
	+ This will measure your income verification.
* **Property Type** (Page 11): Select the type of property you desire (single family home, single family home with mortgage, townhome, condominium, or other).
	+ This will guide the assessment of your property type.
* **Property Location** (Page 11): Select your desired property location (single family neighborhood, single family community, or other).
	+ This will measure your property location preferences.
* **Property Size** (Page 11): Select the property size you desire (1-100, 101-200, 201-300, or 301-400).
	+ This will evaluate your property size preferences.
* **Closing Process** (Page 12): Select the type of closing process you desire (preapproval, preapproval with credit report).
	+ This will affect your closing process.

Please note that this form is intended for initial consultations only. Additional forms, like the mortgage application form, will be used for more detailed analysis.
