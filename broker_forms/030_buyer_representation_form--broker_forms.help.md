# Buyer Representation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Buyer Representation Form is designed to collect information from buyers in order to facilitate communication and organization in real estate transactions. It should only be used by buyers who have a legitimate need to provide this information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name, contact information, and representation preferences.
3. Review and agree to the terms of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Current Address** (`buyer_address`, text, required): Enter your current residence address.
* **City** (`buyer_city`, text, required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (`buyer_state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`buyer_zip`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`buyer_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`buyer_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Co-Buyer Information** (`cobuyer_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide details about any co-buyer information.
* **Real Estate Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your real estate agent.
* **Brokerage Company** (`agent_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the brokerage company representing you.
* **Agent Phone Number** (`agent_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of your real estate agent.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your real estate agent.
* **Type of Representation** (`representation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of representation you require: Exclusive Buyer Agent, Limited Buyer Agent, Transaction Broker, or Other.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of your representation agreement.
* **Duration of Agreement** (`agreement_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration of your representation agreement: 30 days, 60 days, 90 days, 6 months, 1 year, or Other.
* **Target Property Details** (`property_target`, text, optional): Enter specific property or area details.
* **Exclusive Agent Clause** (`exclusive_agent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your agent is an exclusive representative.
* **I Consent to Agent Representation** (`consent_to_represent`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to representation by the agent.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the agreement.

Note: Please fill out this form completely and accurately. If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your real estate agent or seek clarification before submitting the form.
