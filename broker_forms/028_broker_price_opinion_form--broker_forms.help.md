# Broker Price Opinion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Broker Price Opinion Form is used to collect information about a specific property, including its address, type, comparable sales data, estimated value, condition notes, date of the report, and contact information for the broker. This form is typically used by real estate brokers to provide their professional opinion of the property's value.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Property address** of the property you are providing an opinion on.
2. Select the **Property type** from the options provided (Single family, Multi family, Commercial, or Land).
3. In the **Comparable sales summary** field, list recent comparable sales used to support your opinion.
4. Enter your **Estimated market value** of the property.
5. Add any **Condition notes** regarding the property's condition.
6. Select the **Date of report** when the opinion was prepared.
7. Enter your **Broker name** (your name as the reporting broker).
8. Provide your **Broker contact email** for any clarifications or follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property address
* **Property address**: Enter the full address of the property you are providing an opinion on.

### Property type
* **Property type**: Select the type of real estate from the options provided (Single family, Multi family, Commercial, or Land).

### Comparable sales summary
* **Comparable sales summary**: List recent comparable sales used to support your opinion. This is optional, but helps to provide context to your opinion.

### Estimated market value
* **Estimated market value**: Enter your broker's opinion of the property's value.

### Condition notes
* **Condition notes**: Enter any notes about the property's condition that may affect its value.

### Date of report
* **Date of report**: Enter the date when the opinion was prepared.

### Broker name
* **Broker name**: Enter your name as the reporting broker.

### Broker contact email
* **Broker contact email**: Provide your email address for any clarifications or follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your broker team or supervisor for guidance.
