# Water Trailer Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Trailer Rental Application Form is designed to collect information from potential renters about their contact details, equipment requirements, and other relevant information to facilitate the rental process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and other contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
2. Describe the equipment you need to rent and any specific details about your rental needs in the "Equipment Details" field.
3. Review and agree to the rental terms and conditions in the "Rental Terms" field.
4. If applicable, provide any additional information about your rental request in the "Additional Information" field.
5. Verify your identity in the "User Verification" field (this is not required).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): This is the field where you can enter your name and contact information such as email and phone number.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to describe the equipment you need to rent, including any specific details or requirements.
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`, text, required/optional): Here, you are expected to review and agree to the rental terms and conditions. Please carefully read this section before submitting your application.
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, text, required/optional): This field is not explicitly described in the form YAML, so it's not included in the explanation.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional information about your rental request, you can provide it in this field.
* **User Verification** (`user_verification`, text, required/optional): This field is not explicitly described in the form YAML, so it's not included in the explanation.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to send your application for review.
