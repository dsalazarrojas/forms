# rent_schedule_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for creating a rent schedule. It is intended for individuals seeking to create a rental agreement with a landlord. The purpose of this form is to gather the necessary details for the rental arrangement, including the tenant's name, email, and contact information, as well as the dates and financial terms of the rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the first field, "Name". This is a required field.
2. Enter your email address in the second field, "Email". This is also a required field.
3. Select the start date of the rental in the third field, "Start Date". This is a required field.
4. Select the end date of the rental in the fourth field, "End Date". This is also a required field.
5. Enter the frequency of rent payments in the fifth field, "Rent Frequency". This is a required field and should be a number.
6. Enter the total amount of rent to be paid in the sixth field, "Rent Amount". This is also a required field and should be a number.
7. Select the landlord's confirmation status for the rental in the seventh field, "Landlord Confirmation". This is a required field with three options: "Confirmed", "Declined", and "Pending".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for the rental agreement.
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Select the start date of the rental.
- **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Select the end date of the rental.
- **Rent Frequency** (`Rent Frequency`, `number`, required): Enter the frequency of rent payments (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
- **Rent Amount** (`Rent Amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of rent to be paid.
- **Landlord Confirmation** (`Landlord Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select the landlord's confirmation status for the rental ("Confirmed", "Declined", "Pending").

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- Ensure that you enter the correct start and end dates.
- Be specific and accurate with the rent payment frequency and amount.
- Select the correct confirmation status from the options provided.
