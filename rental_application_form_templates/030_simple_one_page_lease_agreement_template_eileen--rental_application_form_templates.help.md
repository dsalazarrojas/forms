# Simple One Page Lease Agreement Template Eileen - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a one-page template for rent applications, designed to collect basic contact and identification information from potential tenants for assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary contact information ready.
2. Fill out the relevant fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Be prepared to provide additional information if requested by Eileen's team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Please provide your name as you would like it to be referred to by Eileen's team.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, **required**): Provide your phone number for Eileen's team to contact you.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* Fill out only the necessary fields based on your situation.
* If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to Eileen's team.
