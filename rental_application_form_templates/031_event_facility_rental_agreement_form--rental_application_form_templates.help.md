# Event Facility Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to streamline the rental application process for event facilities. It gathers essential information from customers to ensure a smooth and secure rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information and event facility details.
2. Fill out the event details, including dates and expected number of attendees.
3. Review and agree to the rental terms and conditions.
4. Read and sign the rental agreement.
5. Submit the completed form to the facility owner or manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Application Form** (`rental_application_form`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your rental application.
* **Facility Info** (`facility_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide details about the facility you are interested in renting, including address and contact information.
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the rental terms and conditions, including payment terms and any other relevant details.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide details about your event, including dates, expected number of attendees, and any specific requirements.
* **Rental Agreement Terms** (`rental_agreement_terms`, `text`, required: false): Review and agree to the rental agreement terms, including cancellation policies and payment terms.
* **Rental Agreement Review** (`rental_agreement_review`, `text`, required: false): Carefully review the rental agreement to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
* **Rental Agreement Approval** (`rental_agreement_approval`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date the rental agreement to confirm your acceptance of the terms.
* **Rental Agreement Signature** (`rental_agreement_signature`, `text`, required: false): Provide your signature and print your name as a formal acceptance of the rental agreement.
* **Facility Owner Info** (`facility_owner_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for the facility owner or manager in case of any questions or issues.
* **Rental Application Submission** (`rental_application_submission`, `text`, required: false): Upload any supporting documents or materials related to your rental application.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling out all sections will help ensure a complete and accurate rental application.
