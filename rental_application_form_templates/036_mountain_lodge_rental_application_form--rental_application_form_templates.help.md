# Mountain Lodge Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for guests who wish to rent a room at the Mountain Lodge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Make sure to select the correct contact method (Phone, Email, or In-person).
3. Enter your email address and phone number accurately.
4. Choose the date and time of your planned arrival.
5. Indicate the number of guests and the length of your stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Method (Contact Method, select_one, required)**: This is where you choose how you would like to be contacted by the Mountain Lodge staff. You can select between Phone, Email, or In-person.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Please enter your email address accurately and correctly formatted. This is how the Mountain Lodge will contact you about your stay.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number accurately. This is how the Mountain Lodge will contact you about your stay.
* **Date of Arrival (Date of Arrival, date, required)**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at the Mountain Lodge. Please use the format mm/dd/yy.
* **Time of Arrival (Time of Arrival, time, required)**: Enter the time you plan to arrive at the Mountain Lodge. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Number of Guests (Number of Guests, number, required)**: Indicate the number of guests that will be staying at the Mountain Lodge.
* **Length of Stay (Length of Stay, number, required)**: Indicate the number of nights you plan to stay at the Mountain Lodge.
* **Special Requests (Special Requests, text, required)**: If there's anything special you would like the Mountain Lodge to arrange for you, please write it down here.

## Tips

* Please fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting.
* The form is only intended for renting a room at the Mountain Lodge.
* If you have any further questions, please contact the Mountain Lodge staff.
