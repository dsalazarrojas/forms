# Rental Application Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and rental preferences from users who are interested in renting a property.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your email address in the "User email" field. This will be your primary contact method for communication.
2. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone" field. This will allow us to reach out to you with further questions or to discuss your interest in renting.
3. Enter your current street address in the "Address" field. This will help us understand your current location.
4. Enter the date you plan to move in to the property in the "Move in Date" field. This will help us schedule viewings and showings.
5. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This will help us identify you and address any follow-up communications.
6. Choose the type of rental you are interested in from the "Rental Type" dropdown menu. Select one of the options that best fits your needs.
7. Enter the name of the neighborhood or area you currently live in in the "Move in Location" field. This will help us understand your current location.
8. Select one or more types of properties you are interested in renting from the "Property Type" dropdown menu. You can select multiple options.
9. Choose the rent range you are interested in from the "Rent Range" dropdown menu. Select one or more options that best fit your budget.
10. Enter any additional message or information you would like to share with us in the "Message" field.
11. If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian in the "Name of Parent or Guardian" field. This is for your protection and to ensure we can follow up with the correct authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User email**: Enter your email address to receive further information and updates about your rental interest.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you with questions or discuss your interest in renting.
* **Address**: Enter the street address where you currently live.
* **Move in Date**: Enter the date you plan to move into the property.
* **Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Rental Type**: Choose the type of rental you are interested in (e.g., Single Family, Townhouse, etc.).
* **Move in Location**: Enter the neighborhood or area you currently live in.
* **Property Type**: Choose one or more types of properties you are interested in renting (e.g., House, Apartment, etc.).
* **Rent Range**: Choose one or more rent ranges that fit your budget.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or questions you have about renting a property.
* **Name of Parent or Guardian**: If you are under 18, enter the name of your parent or guardian for further contact and identification purposes.
