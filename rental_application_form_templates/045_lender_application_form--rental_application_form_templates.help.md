# Lender Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lender Application Form is used to capture information about a property lender, including contact and address details, loan terms, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a clear and easy-to-read format.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (denoted by an asterisk *).
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information.
4. If unsure about any field, please contact your administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (id: 3, email, required): Enter your email address in this format: `john.doe@example.com`.
* **Phone** (id: 4, text, required): Enter your phone number in this format: `123-456-7890`.
* **Property address** (id: 5, text, required): Enter the address of the property you are applying for.
* **Property type** (id: 6, select_one, required): Select whether the property is a:
	+ **Yes** (default option): Select this option if the property is the type you are applying for.
	+ **No**: Select this option if you are not applying for a specific type of property.
* **Property types** (id: 7, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple types of properties you are interested in applying for (if applicable).
* **Loan amount** (id: 8, number, required): Enter the loan amount you are interested in applying for.
* **Loan term** (id: 9, number, required): Enter the loan term you are interested in applying for.
* **Interest rate** (id: 10, number, required): Enter the interest rate you are interested in applying for.
* **Lender name** (id: 11, text, required): Enter your name as the lender.
* **Lender address** (id: 12, text, required): Enter your address as the lender.
* **Lender phone** (id: 13, text, required): Enter your phone number as the lender.
* **Lender email** (id: 14, email, required): Enter your email address as the lender.

## Tips
1. Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
2. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
3. Be honest and provide accurate information to ensure the best possible experience.
