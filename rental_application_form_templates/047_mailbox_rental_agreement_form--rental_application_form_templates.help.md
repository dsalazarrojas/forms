# Mailbox Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mailbox Rental Agreement Form is designed to collect essential information from users who are interested in renting a mailbox, whether for personal or business use. This form will help property managers or landlords gather necessary details about the tenant and the rental conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Start with User Information**:
	* Fill in your name and email address to ensure we can contact you with updates or queries about your mailbox rental.
	* Provide your phone number to facilitate communication.
2. **Specify Rental Information**:
	* Share the address where you'd like to rent the mailbox (or the location for the mailbox service).
	* Describe the type of service you need (e.g., mailbox, mail forwarding, etc.).
3. **Review and Agree to Rental Terms**:
	* Carefully read and agree to the rental terms and conditions (if provided).
	* This is a crucial step to ensure you understand your responsibilities and obligations as a tenant.
4. **Add Your Signature**:
	* Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agreed to the rental terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **User Name** (`name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ **Email Address** (`email`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your email address to receive updates and communication from us.
	+ **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **Required**): Provide your contact number to facilitate communication and emergency contact.
* **Rental Information (Page 2)**:
	+ **Address** (`address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the address where you'd like to rent the mailbox (or the location for the mailbox service).
	+ **Service Type** (`service`, `text`, **Required**): Describe the type of service you need (e.g., mailbox, mail forwarding, etc.).
* **Rental Agreement (Page 3)**:
	+ **Rental Agreement** (`agreement`, `text`, **Required**): Carefully read and agree to the rental terms and conditions provided. This is a sample text for demonstrating the field.
* **Signature (Page 4)**:
	+ **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, **Required**): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agreed to the rental terms.

## Tips
Before submitting the form, ensure you have carefully read and understood the rental terms and conditions. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
