# Tractor Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering information from customers who want to rent a tractor. Please fill out this form with your details to ensure we have all necessary information for the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your general information (Page 1)
2. Provide details about the tractor you want to rent (Page 2)
3. Fill in your user information (Page 3)
4. Review the rental terms (Page 4)
5. Enter your payment information (Page 5)
6. Specify if you want to rent the equipment and if it's available (Page 6)
7. Set your desired rental start and end dates (Page 7)
8. Select the brand and model of the tractor you want to rent (Page 8)
9. Choose the rental duration (Page 9)
10. Fill in your contact information (Page 10)
11. Add any notes or comments (Page 11)
12. Enter the equipment ID (Page 12)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`title`, `text`, optional): This page is for collecting general information about you. Please fill in your name, address, or any other relevant details.
* **Tractor Info** (`label`, `text`, optional): Please provide details about the tractor you want to rent, such as its brand and model.
* **User Info** (`label`, `text`, optional): This page is for collecting information about you. Please fill in your contact details for the rental service to get in touch with you.
* **Rental Terms** (`label`, `text`, optional): Review the rental terms and agreements before proceeding with the rental.
* **Payment Info** (`label`, `text`, optional): Please enter your payment information so we can process the rental fee.
* **Equipment List** (`label`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if you want to rent any equipment along with the tractor. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for each item.
* **Rental Start Date** (`label`, `date`, optional): Enter the start date of the rental period in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Rental End Date** (`label`, `date`, optional): Enter the end date of the rental period in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Tractor Brand** (`label`, `select_one`, optional): Select the brand of the tractor you want to rent. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Tractor Model** (`label`, `select_one`, optional): Select the model of the tractor you want to rent. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Rental Duration** (`label`, `number`, optional): Enter the length of the rental period in days.
* **Contact Info** (`label`, `text`, optional): Please fill in your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Notes** (`label`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the rental.
* **Equipment ID** (`label`, `number`, optional): Please enter the ID of the equipment you want to rent.

Tips: 

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
