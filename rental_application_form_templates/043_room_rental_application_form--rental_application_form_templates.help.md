# Room Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Room Rental Application Form is a digital document used to collect information from potential renters. This form helps landlords or property managers to gather essential details about the applicant and make informed decisions about their rental applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the desired move-in date using the date field.
6. Choose the desired lease length from the options provided (6 months, 1 year, or 2 years).
7. Enter your available move-out date (if applicable).
8. Enter your available move-in date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Desired Move-in Date** (`desired_move_in_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want to move in.
- **Rental Agreement Length** (`rental_agreement_length`, select_one, optional): Choose the desired lease length from the options provided (6 months, 1 year, or 2 years).
- **Available Move-out Date** (`available_move_out_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you expect to move out of the rental unit.
- **Available Move-in Date** (`available_move_in_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date you will move in after the current rental term ends. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your property manager for clarification.
- The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes; please take your time and ensure accuracy.
