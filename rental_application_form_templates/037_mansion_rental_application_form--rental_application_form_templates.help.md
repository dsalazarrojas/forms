# Mansion Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mansion Rental Application Form is designed to collect essential information from potential renters applying to rent a property. This form helps property managers and landlords gather necessary data to evaluate applicant suitability and assess renter qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete the form, please provide the following:
    *   Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
    *   Be as concise as possible while providing sufficient details for a thorough assessment.
    *   Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
2.  Ensure to fill in all required fields (none are marked as required in this form, but some fields are mandatory due to their inherent nature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info**: 
    *   Please provide your contact information (name, phone number, and email address) to enable property managers to reach out to you for further discussion.
*   **Property Search**: 
    *   Specify the type of property you are interested in renting and its location.
*   **Tenant Info**: 
    *   Provide details about your identity and your experience as a tenant.
*   **Renter Agreement**: 
    *   Describe your understanding and expectations of the rental agreement, including any terms and conditions you are willing to accept.
*   **Payment Info**: 
    *   Mention any financial information related to your renter status (e.g. previous rental history, credit score, or payment history with previous landlords).

Note: As this form does not have any required fields, all fields should be filled in to ensure a complete and thorough assessment of your rental application.
