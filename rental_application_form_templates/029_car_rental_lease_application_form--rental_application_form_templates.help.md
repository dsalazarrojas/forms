# Car Rental & Lease Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from applicants to determine their eligibility and preferences for renting or leasing a vehicle. This information will be used to process their application for vehicle rental or lease.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal information section, which includes your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. Proceed to the rental details section, where you will select your preferred pickup and return dates, choose the type of vehicle you wish to rent, and specify the primary purpose of the rental.
3. If you have active personal car insurance, indicate this in the insurance information section.
4. Confirm your understanding of the rental application terms by selecting "Yes, I agree".
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name_rental`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_dob_rental`): Enter your date of birth to verify age and eligibility.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email_rental`): Enter a valid email address where you can receive rental status updates.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_rental`): Enter your primary contact number for the rental.
* **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license_number_rental`): Enter your valid state driver's license number.
* **Preferred Rental Pickup Date** (`rental_pickup_date`): Select the date you would like to pick up the vehicle.
* **Preferred Rental Return Date** (`rental_return_date`): Select the date you would like to return the vehicle.
* **Preferred Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type_rental`): Choose the type of vehicle you would like to rent (e.g., Economy Sedan, Luxury SUV, etc.).
* **Primary Purpose of Rental** (`primary_rental_use`): Indicate how you plan to use the vehicle (Personal/Vacation, Business, etc.).
* **Estimated Mileage Needed** (`rental_mileage_estimate`): Enter an estimate of the total mileage you will need for the rental (if known).
* **Do you have active personal car insurance?** (`personal_insurance_active`): Indicate if you have active personal car insurance.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (`insurance_provider_rental`): If applicable, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **I acknowledge the car rental and lease application terms** (`rental_acknowledgement_lease`): Confirm your understanding of the rental application terms by selecting "Yes, I agree".
* **Type your name for rental application confirmation** (`signature_confirmation_rental`): Enter your full name to confirm your rental application.
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (`additional_rental_comments`): Provide any additional information or requests you may have.
* **Are you interested in a long-term lease option?** (`lease_interest_status`): Indicate if you are interested in a long-term lease option.
* **Preferred Lease Term (Months)** (`lease_term_preference`): If applicable, select the preferred lease term (12, 24, 36, or 48 months).
