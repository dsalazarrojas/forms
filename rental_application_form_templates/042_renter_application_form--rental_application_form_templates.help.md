# Renter Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Renter Application Form is a critical document that helps property managers or landlords assess the suitability of potential renters for a rental property. It is designed to collect relevant information about the applicant, including contact details, rental history, employment verification, and agreement on rental terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Be truthful and honest in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your full name, address, phone number, and email address.

### 2. Rental History
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe your rental history, including any previous rental properties and dates of tenancy.

### 3. Employment Verification
* **Employment Verification** (`employment_verification`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Currently employed
	+ Option 2: Self-employed

### 4. Rental Agreement
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the following options for the rental agreement:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Tips
* Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the property manager or landlord for clarification.
* Be clear and concise in your answers.
* Ensure that the form is signed and dated before submission.

This guide is intended to be a starting point, and the actual form fields and their labels should be used as the final authority.
