# rental_credit_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental credit application form is designed to collect information from potential renters to assess their eligibility for rental credit or to gather necessary data for the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Tenant Details**: Enter your personal details in the "Tenant Details" section.
2. **Address**: Provide your address in the "Address" section.
3. **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number in the "Social Security Number" section.
4. **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status from the options provided in the "Employment Status" section.
5. **Income**: Enter your income in the "Income" section.
6. **Employment History**: For your employment history, select the relevant options for "Employment Status 1", "Employment Status 2", and "Employment History" sections if applicable.
7. **Rental History**: Provide any relevant information about your rental history in the "Rental History" section.
8. **Eviction History**: If you have a history of evictions, provide details in the "Eviction History" section.
9. **Rental Property**: List any rental properties you have a history with in the "Rental Property" section.
10. **Credit Report**: Provide any relevant information about your credit report in the "Credit Report" section.
11. **Criminal Record**: If you have a criminal record, provide details in the "Criminal Record" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Details**: This section is for you to provide your personal details. Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Social Security Number**: This is a required field. Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your government-issued identification.
* **Employment Status**: This field helps us understand your employment status. Select the correct option from the dropdown menu.
* **Employment Income**: This field is optional. If you have any employment income, please enter it.
* **Employment History**: This section is for you to provide details about your employment history, if applicable.
* **Income**: Enter your current income.
* **Previous Employment**: This field is optional. If you've had previous employment, describe it here.
* **Rental History**: Please provide any information about your rental history, including the properties you've rented.
* **Eviction History**: If you have a history of evictions, provide details in this section.
* **Rental Property**: List any rental properties you have a history with.
* **Credit Report**: Provide any relevant information about your credit report.
* **Criminal Record**: If you have a criminal record, provide details in this section.
* **Rental Property 1** to **Rental Property 8**: These fields are for listing rental properties you have a history with.
* **Tips**: Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML file and assumes that the form is used for evaluating renter's creditworthiness or gathering data for the rental process.
