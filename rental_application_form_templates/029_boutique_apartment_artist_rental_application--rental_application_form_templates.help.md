# Boutique Apartment Artist Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to apply for a residency at a boutique apartment. The form gathers essential details about the artist, their artistic practice, and rental requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with your name, email address, phone number, and portfolio URL.
2. In the Artistic Practice section, choose your primary medium and write a brief statement about yourself as an artist.
3. In the Rental and Housing section, select your desired residency duration and preferred move-in date. Choose if you need a separate studio space and indicate the number of occupants.
4. In the References section, provide the names and contact information of your professional or artistic references.
5. In the Portfolio Submission section, link to your high-resolution portfolio images.
6. In the Submission Details section, select the date of application.

* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your online portfolio.
