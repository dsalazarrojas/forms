# Boutique Apartment Artist Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for artists to apply for a residency at a boutique apartment. The form gathers essential details about the artist, their artistic practice, and rental requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

*   Fill out the fields as they appear on each page.
*   Each section is labeled with a clear header and a brief description for better organization and clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Artist Name** (`artist_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`artist_email_addr`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`artist_phone_no`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your online portfolio.
*   **Primary Medium** (`med_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary medium you work with from the options provided.
*   **Brief Artist Statement** (`statement_text`, `text`, required): Write a brief statement about yourself as an artist.
*   **Desired Residency Duration** (`duration_select`, `select_one`, required): Choose your desired residency duration from the options provided.
*   **Preferred Move-in Date** (`move_in_dt`, `date`, required): Enter your preferred move-in date.
*   **Studio Requirement** (`studio_req`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you require a separate studio space or not.
*   **Number of Occupants** (`occupants_val`, `number`, required): Enter the number of occupants.
*   **Professional/Artistic Reference Name and Contact** (`prof_ref`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of your professional or artistic reference.
*   **Previous Landlord Reference** (`land_ref`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and contact information of your previous landlord (if applicable).
*   **Link to High-Resolution Portfolio Images** (`portfolio_link_val`, `text`, required): Enter the URL of your high-resolution portfolio images.
*   **Date of Application** (`submission_dt`, `date`, required): Enter the date of application.
