# Rent Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rent Certificate Form" is a document used to record and document rental payments between a landlord and a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tenant's name in the "Tenant Name" field.
2. Select the date of the rental payment in the "Rent Date" field.
3. Enter the amount received in the "Amount Received" field.
4. Enter the property owner's name in the "Property Owner" field.
5. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
6. Enter the landlord's name in the "Landlord Name" field.
7. Enter the landlord's phone number in the "Landlord Phone" field.
8. Enter the landlord's email address in the "Landlord Email" field.
9. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
10. Select the payment status from the "Payment Status" field.
11. Enter the amount paid in the "Amount Paid" field.
12. Enter the total amount paid in the "Total Amount Paid" field.
13. Enter the amount remaining in the "Amount Remaining" field.
14. Enter the last payment date in the "Last Payment Date" field.
15. Enter the next payment date in the "Next Payment Date" field.
16. Enter the total amount in the "Total Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person renting the property.
* **Rent Date** (`rent_date`, date, required): Select the date of the rental payment.
* **Amount Received** (`amount_received`, number, required): Enter the amount received from the tenant.
* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property being rented.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the landlord.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the date of the signature.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the landlord.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the tenant.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Select the method of payment used.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the payment.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, number, required): Enter the amount paid by the tenant.
* **Total Amount Paid** (`total_amount_paid`, number, required): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Amount Remaining** (`amount_remaining`, number, required): Enter the amount remaining to be paid.
* **Last Payment Date** (`last_payment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the last payment.
* **Next Payment Date** (`next_payment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the next payment.
* **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, number, required): Enter the total amount due for the rental.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of payments.

* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the payment.
