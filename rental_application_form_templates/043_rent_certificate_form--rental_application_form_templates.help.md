# Rent Certificate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rent Certificate Form" is a document used to record and document rental payments between a landlord and a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the tenant's name in the "Tenant Name" field.
2. Select the date of the rental payment in the "Rent Date" field.
3. Enter the amount received in the "Amount Received" field.
4. Enter the property owner's name in the "Property Owner" field.
5. Enter the property address in the "Property Address" field.
6. Enter the landlord's name in the "Landlord Name" field.
7. Enter the landlord's phone number in the "Landlord Phone" field.
8. Enter the landlord's email address in the "Landlord Email" field.
9. Select the payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
10. Select the payment status from the "Payment Status" field.
11. Enter the amount paid in the "Amount Paid" field.
12. Enter the total amount paid in the "Total Amount Paid" field.
13. Enter the amount remaining in the "Amount Remaining" field.
14. Enter the last payment date in the "Last Payment Date" field.
15. Enter the next payment date in the "Next Payment Date" field.
16. Enter the total amount in the "Total Amount" field.
