# Beachfront Property Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beachfront Property Rental Application form is designed to collect essential information from potential renters about themselves, their rental preferences, and additional requirements. This information helps property managers or landlords make informed decisions about potential renters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, **Phone Number**, and **Current Address** in the respective fields.
2. Select the **Property Name or Address** you are interested in renting.
3. Enter your **Desired Arrival Date** and **Desired Departure Date**.
4. Specify the **Total Number of Occupants**, including children.
5. Indicate whether you will bring **Pets** and provide a brief description of your pet(s).
6. If you have **Pets**, you can provide additional details about them in the **Pet Description** field.
7. In the **Additional Information** section, provide any **Additional Comments** or questions you have about the rental.
8. If you have an **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone**, please provide their details.
9. You can also mention any **Personal or Professional References** you would like to include.
10. Finally, enter your **Primary Occupation** and **Annual Income Range** if applicable.
11. Review your form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Applicant Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Current Address**: Enter your current address for contact and verification purposes.
### Rental Details
* **Property Name or Address**: Choose the property you are interested in renting.
* **Desired Arrival Date**: Select the date you plan to move in.
* **Desired Departure Date**: Select the date you plan to move out.
* **Total Number of Occupants**: Include all individuals who will be occupying the rental, including children.
* **Will there be pets?**: Indicate if you plan to bring pets.
* **Pet Description**: Briefly describe your pet(s) if applicable.
### Additional Information
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: Share any specific needs or questions you have about the rental.
* **Personal or Professional References**: Include any relevant references you would like to share.
* **Primary Occupation**: Enter your primary occupation or type of work.
* **Annual Income Range**: Select your estimated annual income range.
* **Occupation**: This field appears to be an optional field for additional details about your occupation.
* **References**: This field appears to be optional for any additional personal or professional references.
* **Comments**: This field is for any further comments or questions you have about the rental.
