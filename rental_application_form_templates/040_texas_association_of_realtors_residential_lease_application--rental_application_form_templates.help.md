# Texas Association Of Realtors Residential Lease Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Texas Association Of Realtors Residential Lease Application form is designed to collect and store information from renters and landlords for residential lease applications. This form aims to streamline the rental process by providing a standardized and organized way to gather necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Renter Information
* **Renter Information** (`Renter Information`, `text`, required/optional): Fill out this section with your personal details as the renter. Provide your name, address, and contact information accurately.

### Step 2: Property Information
* **Property Information** (`Property Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about the rental property, including the address, square footage, and any other relevant information.

### Step 3: Lease Terms
* **Lease Terms** (`Lease Terms`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the terms of the rental agreement, including rent, lease duration, and any other conditions.

### Step 4: Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide contact information for communication, such as email, phone number, or address.

### Step 5: Rental Agreement
* **Rental Agreement** (`Rental Agreement`, `text`, required/optional): Outline the terms and conditions of the rental agreement.

### Step 6: Renter Documents
* **Renter Documents** (`Renter Documents`, `text`, required/optional): Upload or provide any required documents.

### Step 7: Landlord Information
* **Landlord Information** (`Landlord Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about the property owner or manager.

### Step 8: Lease Signature
* **Lease Signature** (`Lease Signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and agree to the terms of the rental agreement.

### Step 9: Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional details or information.

### Step 10: Renter Signature
* **Renter Signature** (`Renter Signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and agree to the terms of the rental agreement.

### Step 11: Lease Terms 2
* **Lease Terms 2** (`Lease Terms 2`, `text`, required/optional): Outline any additional terms or conditions of the rental agreement.

### Step 12: Lease Signature 2
* **Lease Signature 2** (`Lease Signature 2`, `text`, required/optional): Sign and agree to the terms of the rental agreement.

### Step 13: Additional Terms
* **Additional Terms** (`Additional Terms`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional terms or conditions.

### Step 14: Renter Info 2
* **Renter Info 2** (`Renter Info 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the renter.

### Step 15: Contact Info 2
* **Contact Info 2** (`Contact Info 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter contact information for communication.

### Step 16: Property Info 2
* **Property Info 2** (`Property Info 2`, `text`, required/optional): Enter details about the rental property.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is designed to be completed efficiently, but please take your time to ensure accuracy.
* Review the terms of the rental agreement carefully before signing.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
* The form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to understand, but if you need further clarification, please ask.
