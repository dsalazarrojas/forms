This form serves as a comprehensive guide for landlords to gather essential information from potential tenants. The form helps landlords assess the suitability of potential tenants, ensuring they understand the tenant's rental history, employment status, financial stability, and other relevant factors. The form's purpose is to streamline the rental application process, ensuring landlords make informed decisions and maintain accurate records. Before proceeding, review the following steps to complete the form:

# Landlord Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Landlord Rental Application Form is designed for potential tenants to provide necessary information for a smooth and successful tenancy process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Information**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email in the Contact Information section. This allows you to communicate effectively with the landlord and property manager.
2. **Applicant Details**: Provide your name, email, and phone number in the Applicant Details section.
3. **Rental Agreement**: If you're looking for a rental property, indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the rental in the Rental Agreement section.
4. **Property Information**: Describe the property you're interested in, including its type and location, in the Property Information section.
5. **References**: List any references you'd like to provide, such as previous landlords or property managers, in the References section.
6. **Employment Verification**: Confirm your employment status and employment start date in the Employment Verification section.
7. **Lease Details**: Specify the rental period, lease start date, and lease end date in the Lease Details section.
8. **Rental Period**: Choose the duration of your desired rental period from the options provided.
9. **Rental Unit**: Select the type of rental unit you're interested in, such as 1 or 2-bedroom.
10. **Renter Identity**: Confirm your identity and whether you'll be the primary renter.
11. **Renter Income**: Indicate your financial stability and income in the Renter Income section.
12. **Renter Employment**: Confirm your employment status and employment start date.
13. **Renter Financial**: Confirm your financial stability and financial status.
14. **Property Type**: Choose the type of property you're looking for, such as an apartment or house.
15. **Property Status**: Confirm the property's current status, such as for rent or leased.
16. **Property Location**: Confirm whether the property is located within a desirable location.
17. **Property Address**: Provide the property's address.
18. **Renter Address**: Provide your current address.
19. **Renter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
20. **Renter Email**: Enter your email address.
21. **Renter Identification Number**: Enter your identification number (if applicable).
22. **Renter Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number (if applicable).
23. **Renter Bank Name**: Enter your bank name and account number (if applicable).
24. **Renter Account Number**: Enter your account number (if applicable).
25. **Renter ROUT Number**: Enter your ROUT number (if applicable).
26. **Applicant Name**: Enter your name.
27. **Applicant Email**: Enter your email address.
28. **Applicant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
29. **Landlord Name**: Enter your name as the landlord.
30. **Landlord Address**: Enter your address.
31. **Landlord Phone**: Enter your phone number.
32. **Landlord Email**: Enter your email address.
33. **Applicant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
34. **Applicant Signature**: Sign below to confirm you've completed this application form.
35. **Landlord Signature**: Sign below to confirm you've reviewed and accepted the application.
36. **Property Manager Name**: Enter the property manager's name.
37. **Property Manager Email**: Enter the property manager's email address.
38. **Property Manager Phone**: Enter the property manager's phone number.
39. **Property Manager Address**: Enter the property manager's address.
40. **Applicant SSN**: Enter your Social Security number.
41. **Renter SSN**: Enter the renter's Social Security number.
42. **Renter DOB**: Enter the renter's date of birth.
43. **Renter Employment Start Date**: Enter the renter's employment start date.
44. **Renter Employment End Date**: Enter the renter's employment end date.
45. **Renter Previous Employment Start**: Enter the renter's previous employment start date.
46. **Renter Residence Duration**: Enter the duration of the renter's residence.
