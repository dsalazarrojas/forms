# Yacht Club Dock Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yacht Club Dock Rental Application Form is a form designed to help users apply for a rental at the Yacht Club's dock.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Information**: Please fill in the following details about yourself:
    * **User Name**: Your name.
    * **Email**: Your email address.
    * **Phone**: Your contact phone number.
    * **Title**: Your work or professional title.
    * **Company**: The company you represent.
    * **Address**: Your workplace address.
    * **State**: Your state (province or region).
    * **Zip**: Your postal zip code.
    * **City**: Your city or town.
    * **Country**: Your country.
2. **Rental Request**: Please specify the dates you need the dock for:
    * **Rental Request Dates**: The dates you require the dock for.
3. **Payment Method**: Please select your preferred payment method:
    * **Payment Method**: Select from the options provided (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal).
4. **Rental Details**: Please fill in the following:
    * **Start Date**: The start date of your rental request.
    * **End Date**: The end date of your rental request.
    * **End Date Note**: Any additional notes for the end date.
    * **Total Nights**: The total number of nights you'll be occupying the dock.
    * **Payment Terms**: Any special payment terms.
    * **Additional Requests**: Any additional requests.
    * **Comments**: Any comments or remarks.
5. **Signature**: Please sign below to confirm you've read and understood the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to increase the chances of getting approval for your rental request.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the form description above.
* If you have any questions, contact the Yacht Club staff for further assistance.
* Please note that all fields are case sensitive for email, phone, and address.
