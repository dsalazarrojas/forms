# Farm Land Lease Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farm Land Lease Form is designed to collect necessary information for a land lease agreement between a landowner and a tenant. This form will guide you through the required and optional fields to ensure a comprehensive lease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the basic information about the lease, including the lease term (e.g., Tenure, Lease, or Rent) and the land use (e.g., Agricultural, Recreational, or Commercial).
2. Provide the start and end dates for the lease. These dates are optional, so feel free to adjust according to your specific situation.
3. If necessary, include contact information for both the tenant and the landowner, such as names, email, and phone numbers.
4. Optionally, you can include additional details about the division of land and the size of the division, which may be relevant depending on your specific lease needs.
5. Finally, use the text fields for any additional lease agreement details or notes you need to include, and remember that this is where you can add any specific terms or conditions of the lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Land Owner Size (id: 2)**: Enter the size of the land in a numerical value.
* **Land Area Size (id: 3)**: Similar to "Land Owner Size", enter the area or size of the land.
* **Lease Term (id: 4)**: Choose the type of lease term you want to apply. This could be Tenure, Lease, or Rent.
* **Land Use (id: 5)**: Select the primary use of the land, which could be for Agricultural, Recreational, or Commercial purposes.
* **Lease Start Date (id: 6)**: Provide the start date of the lease in the standard date format (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **Lease End Date (id: 7)**: Enter the end date of the lease, if applicable. Again, in the standard date format.
* **Tenant Info (id: 8)**: Select or list the names of the tenants involved in the lease.
* **Land Owner Info (id: 9)**: Similarly, list or select the names of the landowners involved in the lease.
* **Division of Land (id: 10)**: If the land is divided or shared among several individuals or entities, provide details about this division.
* **Division Size (id: 11)**: Enter the size of the land being divided or shared.
* **Farm Land Lease Agreement (id: 12)**: This is where you can add any specific agreement terms or conditions related to the lease.
* **Lease Agreement Details (id: 13)**: Use this field for any additional lease details or terms you want to include.
* **Farm Land Lease Form Notes (id: 14)**: Any notes or comments about the lease or the form itself can be added here.
* **Farm Land Lease Form Comments (id: 15)**: More comments or feedback about the lease or the form can be added here.
* **Farm Land Lease Form Attachments (id: 16)**: Attach any relevant documents or files to the lease form if necessary.
* **Farm Land Lease Form Email (id: 17)**: Enter the email of the person or entity responsible for the lease.
* **Farm Land Lease Form Phone (id: 18)**: Provide the phone number of the person or entity responsible for the lease.

Note that some fields may have optional status but are still necessary for a comprehensive understanding or execution of a lease agreement. Always review the entire form carefully and ensure all necessary fields are filled out before submission.
