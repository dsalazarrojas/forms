# cultural_center_residency_rental_application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect rental application information from users. It includes fields for user information, contact information, property information, rental details, payment information, additional information, review, agreement, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide your contact information, including any relevant contact details.
3. Enter property information, including any relevant details about the property you are renting.
4. Provide rental details, including any relevant information about your rental request.
5. Enter payment information, including any relevant payment details.
6. Add any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
7. Review your information and make sure everything is accurate.
8. Agree to the terms and conditions of the rental agreement.
9. Sign the agreement to confirm your application.

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.

* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter payment details, including methods and amounts.
* **Payment Info 2** (`payment_info_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional payment details, if applicable.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Add any additional information that may be relevant to your application.
