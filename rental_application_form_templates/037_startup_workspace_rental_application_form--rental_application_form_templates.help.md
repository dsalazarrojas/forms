# Startup Workspace Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from startups and businesses that are interested in renting a workspace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the User Information page with your company's details.
2. Provide information about the workspace you are interested in.
3. Share your contact information and application details.
4. Upload any required documents.
5. Review the terms and conditions.
6. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`user_info`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input your company's details, including name, company size, and industry.
  - Please select your company name from the options provided.
  - Choose the size of your company from Small, Medium, or Large.
  - Select the industries that apply to your company (e.g., Tech, Finance, Marketing).

- **Workspace Information** (`workspace_info`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input information about the type of workspace you are interested in renting.
  - Choose the type of workspace that suits your company's needs (e.g., Coworking Space, Incubator, Flexible Office).

- **Application Information** (`application_info`, text, **required**): This page is where you will provide a brief description about your company and your workspace needs.
  - Enter a concise description about your company and what you are looking for in a workspace.

- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input your contact information so we can get back to you.
  - Enter your email address.
  - Enter your phone number.

- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input payment details for your workspace rental.
  - Please provide your payment method and any other payment information required.

- **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, **required**): This page is where you will upload any required documents or files.
  - Please upload any relevant documents that will help us evaluate your application.

- **Review** (`review`, text, **not required**): This page is where you can add any additional comments or information about your application.
  - Use this space to add any additional details or information that you think will help us better understand your application.

- **Date** (`date`, date, **required**): This page is where you will input the date for your application.
  - Choose the date you would like to start your workspace rental.

- **Time** (`time`, time, **required**): This page is where you will input the time for your application.
  - Choose the time you would like to start your workspace rental.

- **Company Name** (`company_name`, select_one, **required**): This page is where you will select your company's name.
  - Please choose your company's name from the options provided.

- **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, **required**): This page is where you will choose your company's size.
  - Choose the size of your company from Small, Medium, or Large.

- **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, **required**): This page is where you will select your company's industry.
  - Choose the industries that apply to your company (e.g., Tech, Finance, Marketing).

- **Location** (`location`, select_one, **required**): This page is where you will select your company's location.
  - Choose the location of your company (e.g., US, Europe, Asia).

- **Type** (`type`, select_multiple, **required**): This page is where you will select the type of workspace you are interested in.
  - Choose the type of workspace that suits your company's needs (e.g., Coworking Space, Incubator, Flexible Office).

- **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): This page is where you will input your email address.
  - Enter your email address.

- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input your phone number.
  - Enter your phone number.

- **Address** (`address`, text, **required**): This page is where you will input your company's address.
  - Enter your company's address.

- **Description** (`description`, text, **not required**): This page is where you can add any additional comments or information about your company.
  - Use this space to add any additional details or information that you think will help us better understand your application.

- **Note** (`note`, note, **not required**): This page is where you can add any additional notes or comments about your application.
  - Use this space to add any additional information that you think will help us evaluate your application.

- **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text, **not required**): This page is where you can upload any additional files or documents.
  - Please upload any additional files that will help us evaluate your application.

- **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, **not required**): This page is where you will confirm if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
  - Please select 'True' if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.

- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, **required**): This page is where you will confirm if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
  - Please select 'I have read and agree to the terms and conditions' if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.

- **Submit Button** (`submit`, text, **not required**): This page is where you will submit your application.
  - Please click the 'Submit' button to complete your application.

- **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, **required**): This page is where you will confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
  - Please select 'True' if you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
