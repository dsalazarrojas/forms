# Rental Agreement Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the Rental Agreement Access Form, used to collect information from potential tenants to assess their eligibility for a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information and details about the rental property you're interested in.
2. Provide a unique ID for the rental application.
3. Enter your name and contact information.
4. Enter your contact number.
5. Provide the address of the rental property.
6. Enter the start and end dates of the rental period.
7. Specify the duration of the rental.
8. Enter the security deposit amount.
9. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Agreement Access Form**: This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page.
* **application_form_id**: This is a unique ID for the rental application. Please enter a number that can be used to identify your application.
* **landlord_name**: This field is for the name of the person or company providing the rental property. Please enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **tenant_name**: This field is for your name as the potential tenant. Please enter your full name.
* **rental_address**: This is the address of the rental property you're interested in. Please enter the exact address where you'll be renting.
* **rental_start_date**: This is the start date of the rental period. Please enter the date you'd like the rental to begin.
* **rental_end_date**: This is the end date of the rental period. Please enter the date you'd like the rental to end.
* **rental_duration**: This field specifies the length of the rental period. Please enter a number of days, weeks, or months.
* **rental_security_deposit**: This field is for the security deposit amount. Please enter a dollar amount.
* **tenant_signature**: This field is for your signature as the potential tenant. Please enter your signature to confirm your agreement.
* **tenant_contact_number**: This field is for your phone number or contact information. Please enter your number in the correct format (e.g., ###-####-####).

Note that some fields may be optional, but it's essential to fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate rental application.
