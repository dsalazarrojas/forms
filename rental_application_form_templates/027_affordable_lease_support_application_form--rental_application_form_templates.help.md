# Affordable Lease Support Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from applicants for rental assistance programs, including demographics, household composition, income, and housing preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your demographic information, including full name, date of birth, social security number, email address, phone number, and current address.
2. Describe your current housing situation, including whether you are renting, staying with relatives/friends, temporarily housed, homeless, or have other arrangements.
3. Provide information about your household composition, including the number of people in your household and their relationships.
4. Report your income information, including primary source of income, monthly gross income, and other household income.
5. Provide details about your current monthly rent and utilities costs.
6. Specify your desired rent amount and preferred housing location.
7. Choose the type of housing unit you are seeking.
8. If applicable, indicate any accessibility needs and provide more details.
9. Describe any special circumstances affecting your housing needs.
10. Report your history of emergency assistance, eviction, and criminal background.
11. Certify that you have provided accurate information and sign the form to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your birth.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, `text`, required): Enter your social security number for verification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication about your application.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **Current Housing Situation** (`current_housing_situation`, `select_one`, required): Describe your current housing arrangement, choosing from the options provided.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of people in your household.
* **Dependent Children Count** (`dependent_children_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of dependent children in your household.
* **Household Member Details** (`household_member_details`, `text`, required): Describe the ages and relationships of all household members.
* **Primary Income Source** (`primary_income_source`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your primary source of income from the options provided.
* **Monthly Gross Income** (`monthly_gross_income`, `number`, required): Enter your total monthly income before taxes and deductions.
* **Other Household Income** (`other_household_income`, `number`, required): Enter the monthly income of other household members.
* **Total Monthly Household Income** (`total_household_income`, `number`, required): Enter the combined monthly income of all household members.
* **Income Verification Documents** (`income_verification_documents`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the documents you can provide to verify income, selecting from the options provided.
* **Current Monthly Rent** (`current_monthly_rent`, `number`, required): Enter how much you currently pay for rent.
* **Current Monthly Utilities** (`current_monthly_utilities`, `number`, required): Enter how much you currently pay for utilities.
* **Housing Expense Hardship** (`housing_expense_hardship`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether your current housing expenses are causing financial hardship, choosing from the options provided.
* **Desired Rent Amount** (`desired_rent_amount`, `number`, required): Specify the maximum rent you can afford.
* **Preferred Housing Location** (`preferred_housing_location`, `text`, required): Enter the area or neighborhood you are looking for housing in.
* **Housing Unit Preference** (`housing_unit_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of housing unit you are seeking from the options provided.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have any accessibility requirements, choosing from the options provided.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, `text`, optional): Provide more details about your accessibility needs if applicable.
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, `text`, optional): Describe any special circumstances affecting your housing needs.
* **Emergency Assistance History** (`emergency_assistance_history`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have received emergency rental assistance before, choosing from the options provided.
* **Eviction History** (`eviction_history`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have been evicted from a rental property, choosing from the options provided.
* **Criminal Background** (`criminal_background`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have any criminal convictions that might affect rental eligibility, choosing from the options provided.
* **Income Limit Compliance** (`income_limit_compliance`, `select_one`, required): Certify that your household income meets the program's income limits, choosing from the options provided.
* **Information Accuracy Certification** (`information_accuracy_certification`, `select_one`, required): Certify that all information provided is true and accurate, choosing from the options provided.
* **Electronic Signature** (`authorization_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your application electronically to submit it.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields marked as required are filled out accurately and completely.
* You can select multiple documents to verify income from the "Income Verification Documents" field.
* If you have any accessibility needs, please provide more details to help us understand your requirements.
* Remember to certify that all information provided is true and accurate.
