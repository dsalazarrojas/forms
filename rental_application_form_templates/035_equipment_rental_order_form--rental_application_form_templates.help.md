# equipment_rental_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the equipment rental order form, used to collect and process rental requests for equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Review and verify the accuracy of your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (Text Field, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the contact information of the person or organization making the request. This includes name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Equipment Information** (Text Field, Text, Required/Optional): Describe the equipment you are looking to rent, including any special requests or requirements.
* **Rental Period** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the rental period for the equipment, which is 1 Day, 2 Days, or 3 Days.
* **Start Date** (Date Field, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the start date of the rental period, ensuring it is a valid date.
* **End Date** (Date Field, Date, Required/Optional): Enter the end date of the rental period, ensuring it is a valid date and is after the start date.
* **Equipment Return** (Text Field, Text, Required/Optional): Provide any specific instructions for the return of the equipment, such as pickup location or special care requirements.
* **Delivery Information** (Text Field, Text, Required/Optional): Enter the delivery information, including any specific details or special instructions for the equipment delivery.
* **Payment Information** (Text Field, Text, Required/Optional): Enter any relevant payment information or special requests.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure a successful rental application.
* Double-check the accuracy of your entries, especially for dates and equipment requests.
* For specific or urgent requests, provide as much detail as possible to facilitate a smooth rental process.
