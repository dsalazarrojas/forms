# real_estate_agent_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_agent_contact_form is designed to collect contact information and property details from real estate agents. It helps track and manage leads from agents, providing a structured way to collect and analyze data for real estate business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the fields as required and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of property (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial) to categorize the property.
* **Property Status** (`Property Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the current status of the property (Available, Rented, or Sold) to indicate its availability.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how the agent would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Message).
* **Lead Source** (`Lead Source`, `select_one`, required): Specify the source of the lead (Agent, Online, Referral, Print, or Social).
* **Realty Company** (`Realty Company`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the realty company the agent works for (Realty Company 1, Realty Company 2, or Realty Company 3).
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Property Location** (`Property Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the property.
* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `text`, required): Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
* **Property Description** (`Property Description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the property.
* **Lead Status** (`Lead Status`, `select_one`, required): Specify the current status of the lead (New, Follow-up, Converted, or Expired).
* **Follow-up Date** (`Follow-up Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for the next follow-up action.
* **Follow-up Time** (`Follow-up Time`, `time`, required): Enter the time for the next follow-up action.
* **Agent Name** (`Agent Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Phone** (`Agent Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the real estate agent.
* **Agent Email** (`Agent Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the real estate agent.
* **Contact Request** (`Contact Request`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional contact requests.
* **Additional Contact Request** (`Additional Contact Request`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional contact requests.
* **Rental Information** (`Rental Information`, `text`, optional): Enter any rental information.
* **Agent Information** (`Agent Information`, `text`, optional): Enter any agent information.
* **Property Info** (`Property Info`, `text`, optional): Enter any property information.
* **Property Details** (`Property Details`, `text`, optional): Enter any property details.
