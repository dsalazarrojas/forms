# Scooter Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scooter Rental Application Form is a document that collects essential information from users interested in renting a scooter. This form helps the rental service to verify the user's identity, assess their rental needs, and tailor the scooter rental experience according to their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your First Name in the designated text field.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the designated text field.
3.  Enter your Date Of Birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
4.  Enter your Phone Number in the format of ###-###-####.
5.  Enter your Email address.
6.  Choose one or more scooters to rent by selecting multiple options from the Scooter Type dropdown menu.
7.  Enter the Start Date of your rental in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
8.  Enter the End Date of your rental in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
9.  Enter the Total Rental Hours for which you need the scooter in the designated text field.
10. If you have any additional comments, please provide them in the Comments text field.
11. Check the box to agree to the Rental Agreement.
12. Enter your Rider ID in the designated text field.
13. Enter the Vehicle Number of your vehicle.
14. Enter the Make of your vehicle.
15. Enter the Model of your vehicle.
16. Enter the Year of your vehicle.
17. Enter the Color of your vehicle.
18. Enter the Vehicle Plate number.
19. Enter the Type of vehicle.
20. Enter the Vehicle Registration number.
21. Enter the Vehicle Insurance number.
22. Enter the Vehicle License Number.
23. Enter the Vehicle License Plate number.
24. Enter the Type Of Use of your vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date Of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format of ###-###-####.
* **Email** (5, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Scooter Type** (6, select multiple, required): Choose the type of scooter you want to rent.
* **Start Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date you want to start renting the scooter in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **End Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date you want to end renting the scooter in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Total Rental Hours** (9, text, required): Enter the total hours you need to rent the scooter.
* **Comments** (10, text, required): If you have any additional comments or requests, please provide them in this field.
* **Rental Agreement** (11, text, required): Check this box to agree to the rental agreement terms.
* **Rider ID** (13, text, required): Enter your rider ID.
* **Vehicle Number** (14, text, required): Enter your vehicle number.
* **Vehicle Make** (15, text, required): Enter your vehicle make.
* **Vehicle Model** (16, text, required): Enter your vehicle model.
* **Vehicle Year** (17, text, required): Enter your vehicle year.
* **Vehicle Color** (18, text, required): Enter your vehicle color.
* **Vehicle Plate** (19, text, required): Enter your vehicle plate number.
* **Vehicle Type** (20, text, required): Enter the type of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration** (21, text, required): Enter your vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (22, text, required): Enter your vehicle insurance number.
* **Vehicle License Number** (23, text, required): Enter your vehicle license number.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (24, text, required): Enter your vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Type Of Use** (25, text, required): Enter the type of use for your vehicle.

Note: All fields are required for the form to be valid. Please ensure you fill out all fields accurately.
