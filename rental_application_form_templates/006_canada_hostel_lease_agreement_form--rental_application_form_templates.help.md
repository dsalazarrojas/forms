# Canada Hostel Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canada Hostel Lease Agreement Form is used to create a lease agreement between the hostel and the tenant. This form gathers essential information to establish a clear understanding of the terms of the rental.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your personal details in the Tenant Information section.
2. Select your preferred accommodation type and lease duration in the Lease Terms section.
3. Confirm your agreement with the hostel's rules and policies by selecting the relevant checkboxes.
4. Fill out the emergency contact details in the Emergency Contact section.
5. Date and sign the agreement in the Agreement Date section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Passport Number or ID**: Enter your ID number for verification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Permanent Home Address**: Enter your primary residence outside the hostel.
* **Check-in Date**: Enter the expected date of your arrival at the hostel.
* **Check-out Date**: Enter the expected date of your departure from the hostel.
* **Lease Duration**: Select the duration of your stay from the options provided.
* **Room Type**: Select your preferred accommodation type.
* **Monthly Rent Amount**: Enter the agreed-upon rent amount in Canadian dollars.
* **Security Deposit**: Enter the deposit amount you are willing to pay.
* **House Rules Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the hostel's community guidelines.
* **Damage Policy Agreement**: Confirm that you are responsible for any damages caused during your stay.
* **Smoking Policy Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand the smoking policy.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Date of Agreement**: Date and sign the agreement.
