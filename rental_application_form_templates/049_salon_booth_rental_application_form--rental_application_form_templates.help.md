# Salon Booth Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Booth Rental Application Form is designed to gather essential information from stylists who wish to rent a booth or workstation in a salon or spa. This form will help salon administrators and rental coordinators assess the stylist's qualifications, availability, and rental preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Read the entire form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting**. If you need to provide additional information or clarification, please contact the rental coordinator or salon administrator.
2.  **Provide accurate and honest answers in each field**. Incomplete or false information may lead to your rental application being rejected or delayed.
3.  **Submit the form only when you are ready**. Do not submit incomplete applications, as this may cause delays in processing your rental request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Booth Details (<name>: Booth Details, <type>: text, required)**: 
    Enter a detailed description of the type and size of booth you are interested in renting. Please include the number of stations you will need and any specific features you require (e.g., electrical outlets, mirrors, etc.).

    - **Stylist Details (<name>: Stylist Details, <type>: text, optional)**: 
        Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a stylist. This information will be used to verify your identity and assess your qualifications.

    - **Review Details (<name>: Review Details, <type>: text, optional)**:
        If you have any previous reviews or ratings from clients, please share them here. This will help us assess your reputation and work history as a stylist.

## Tips

*   Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient rental process.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the rental coordinator or salon administrator.
*   We strive to process rental applications as quickly as possible, but please allow up to 3 business days for us to review and respond to your application.
