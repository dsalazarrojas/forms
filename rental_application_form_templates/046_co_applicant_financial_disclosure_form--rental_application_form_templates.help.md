# Co Applicant Financial Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Co Applicant Financial Disclosure Form is used to gather financial information about the co-applicant in a rental application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name as the co-applicant.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact.
3. Provide the date of your disclosure.
4. Enter your Social Security Number (last 4 digits only).
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Enter your gross annual income from all sources.
7. Select your current employment status (Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Retired, Unemployed, or Student).
8. If applicable, enter your current employer's name and job title.
9. Enter your total assets (savings, investments, property).
10. Enter your cash reserves or savings.
11. If applicable, enter the value of any real property you own.
12. If applicable, enter the value of any vehicles you own.
13. Enter your total liabilities (debts and obligations).
14. If applicable, enter the amount of mortgage debt.
15. If applicable, enter the amount of credit card debt.
16. If applicable, enter the amount of other debt (student loans, car loans, etc.).
17. If known, enter your approximate credit score.
18. Indicate if you have any bankruptcy history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Co-Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as the co-applicant.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Disclosure** (`disclosure_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date you are providing this financial disclosure.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security`, `text`, **required**): Enter your Social Security Number (last 4 digits only).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **required**): Enter your date of birth.
* **Gross Annual Income** (`gross_annual_income`, `number`, **required**): Enter your income from all sources.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your current employment status (Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Retired, Unemployed, or Student).
* **Current Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your current employer's name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter your job title.
* **Total Assets** (`total_assets`, `number`, **required**): Enter your total assets (savings, investments, property).
* **Cash Reserves or Savings** (`cash_reserves`, `number`, **optional**): Enter your cash reserves or savings.
* **Real Property Value** (`property_value`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the value of any real property you own.
* **Vehicle Value** (`vehicle_value`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the value of any vehicles you own.
* **Total Liabilities** (`total_liabilities`, `number`, **required**): Enter your total liabilities (debts and obligations).
* **Mortgage Debt** (`mortgage_debt`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the amount of mortgage debt.
* **Credit Card Debt** (`credit_card_debt`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the amount of credit card debt.
* **Other Debt** (`other_debt`, `number`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the amount of other debt (student loans, car loans, etc.).
* **Approximate Credit Score** (`credit_score`, `number`, **optional**): If known, enter your approximate credit score.
* **Any Bankruptcy History** (`bankruptcy_history`, `select_one`, **required**): Indicate if you have any bankruptcy history.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Make sure to review and verify the information provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your landlord or property manager.
* This form is part of the rental application process and is used to assess your creditworthiness.
* Failure to provide accurate information may affect your rental application.
