# Windsurf Equipment Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to apply for windsurf equipment rental.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide details about the windsurf equipment you want to rent in the "Windsurf Equipment Details" section.
3. Select the rental agreement that suits your needs.
4. Add any additional comments or information about your rental request.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Choose whether you want to submit your form.
8. Select the category of equipment you want to rent.
9. Choose a date and time for your rental.
10. Select the equipment you want to rent.
11. Select whether you want to rent at a specific location.
12. Select whether you want to contact a person directly.
13. Select the agreement term that applies to your rental.
14. Enter the start date of your rental.
15. Enter the end date of your rental.
16. Enter the total cost of your rental.
17. Enter any deposit required.
18. Enter any late fee.
19. Enter any other charges.
20. Select your payment term.
21. Select your payment method.
22. Check the status of your payment.
23. Confirm your rental request.
24. Review your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required): This is where you provide your personal details such as name and contact information.
* **Windsurf Equipment Details** (`Windsurf Equipment Details`, text, required): This is where you provide details about the windsurf equipment you want to rent.
* **Rental Agreement** (`Rental Agreement`, select_one, required): This is where you select the rental agreement that suits your needs.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, required): This is where you can add any additional comments or information about your rental request.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This is where you enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): This is where you enter your phone number.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required): This is where you choose whether you want to submit your form.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_multiple, required): This is where you select the category of equipment you want to rent.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): This is where you choose a date for your rental.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): This is where you choose a time for your rental.
* **Equipment** (`Equipment`, select_multiple, required): This is where you select the equipment you want to rent.
* **Location** (`Location`, select_one, required): This is where you select whether you want to rent at a specific location.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, select_one, required): This is where you select whether you want to contact a person directly.
* **Agreement Term** (`Agreement Term`, select_multiple, required): This is where you select the agreement term that applies to your rental.
* **Rental Start Date** (`Rental Start Date`, date, required): This is where you enter the start date of your rental.
* **Rental End Date** (`Rental End Date`, date, required): This is where you enter the end date of your rental.
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, number, required): This is where you enter the total cost of your rental.
* **Deposit** (`Deposit`, number, required): This is where you enter any deposit required.
* **Late Fee** (`Late Fee`, number, required): This is where you enter any late fee.
* **Other Charges** (`Other Charges`, number, required): This is where you enter any other charges.
* **Payment Term** (`Payment Term`, number, required): This is where you select your payment term.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, required): This is where you select your payment method.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, select_one, required): This is where you check the status of your payment.
* **Confirmation** (`Confirmation`, select_one, required): This is where you confirm your rental request.
* **Review** (`Review`, select_multiple, required): This is where you review your application.
