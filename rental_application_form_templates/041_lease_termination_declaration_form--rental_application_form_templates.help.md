# Lease Termination Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for declaring the termination of a lease agreement between a landlord and a tenant. It is designed to gather information and provide a formal declaration of the lease termination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form.
2. Fill out the form by providing the required information for both the landlord and tenant sections.
3. Enter the lease termination details in the corresponding fields.
4. Sign and date the form with your signature.
5. Verify that the submitted information is correct before sending it to the intended recipient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Information** (optional):
	+ This field is for providing information about the landlord.
* **Tenant Information** (optional):
	+ This field is for providing information about the tenant.
* **Lease Details** (optional):
	+ This field is for entering lease termination details.
* **Declaration** (optional):
	+ This is a text field for any additional declaration or information related to the lease termination.
* **Tenant Signature** (required):
	+ This field is for obtaining the tenant's signature, indicating their agreement to terminate the lease.
* **Landlord Signature** (required):
	+ This field is for obtaining the landlord's signature, indicating their agreement to terminate the lease.
* **Date** (required):
	+ Enter the date of lease termination in the corresponding format.
* **Time** (required):
	+ Enter the time of lease termination in the corresponding format.
* **Submitted** (required):
	+ This field is for providing information about when the form was submitted.
* **Email Sent** (optional):
	+ This field is for indicating whether an email has been sent to the tenant or landlord.
		+ Note: This field is not required as it is intended for administrative purposes.

Please note that this guide assumes that you have all necessary permissions and approvals to complete this form. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any potential issues.
