# eco_friendly_home_rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from applicants regarding their rental application for an eco-friendly home. This information will help us understand the applicant's needs and preferences for a suitable rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information by filling out the "Applicant Info" page.
2. Enter your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
3. Select your preferred rental type (Rent, Rent and Buy, or Other) on the "Rental Preferences" page.
4. If you have selected "Other (Specify)" as your preferred rental type, please provide additional information on the "Other Rent Type" page.
5. Select a move-in date and time on the "Move In Date" page.
6. Enter the length and frequency of your rent payment on the "Lease Length" and "Rent Frequency" pages.
7. Specify the currency and unit of your rent payment on the "Rent Frequency Currency" and "Rent Frequency Unit" pages.
8. Enter any additional rent frequency information on the "Rent Frequency Additional Info" page.
9. Enter any security deposit information on the "Deposit" page.
10. List any utilities you would like to have included in your rent on the "Utilities" page.
11. If you have selected "Other" as a utility type, please provide additional information on the "Utilities Other Info" page.
12. Specify your preferred property type (House, Condo, Townhouse, or Other) on the "Property Preferences" page.
13. If you have selected "Other" as a property type, please provide additional information on the "Property Type Other" page.
14. Select a move-in condition (Move In Condition 1, Move In Condition 2, or Other) on the "Move In Condition" page.
15. If you have selected "Other" as a move-in condition, please provide additional information on the "Move In Condition Other" page.
16. Provide any relevant background information about yourself on the "Renter Background" page.
17. If you have additional information about your background, please provide it on the "Renter Background Other" page.
18. Describe your relevant experience on the "Renter Experience" page.
19. If you have additional experience to share, please provide it on the "Renter Experience Other" page.
20. Enter any additional information on the "Additional Info" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, `text`, optional): This page is where you will enter your general information, including your name, contact information, and any other details you would like to share.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): This page is where you will enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, and address.
* **Rental Preferences** (`rental_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): This page allows you to select your preferred rental type. Please choose from Rent, Rent and Buy, or Other (Specify).
* **Other Rent Type** (`other_rent_type`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information if you have selected "Other (Specify)" as your preferred rental type.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, optional): This page is where you will select a date for your move-in date.
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_date_time`, `time`, optional): This page is where you will select a time for your move-in date.
* **Lease Length** (`lease_length`, `number`, optional): This page is where you will enter the length of your lease.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, `number`, optional): This page is where you will enter the frequency of your rent payments.
* **Rent Frequency Unit** (`rent_frequency_unit`, `select_one`, optional): This page allows you to select a unit for your rent payments (Dollar, Euro, or Pound).
* **Rent Frequency Currency** (`rent_frequency_currency`, `select_one`, optional): This page allows you to select a currency for your rent payments (USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Rent Frequency Additional Info** (`rent_frequency_additional_info`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information about your rent frequency.
* **Deposit** (`deposit`, `number`, optional): This page is where you will enter any additional deposit information.
* **Utilities** (`utilities`, `text`, optional): This page is where you will list any utilities you would like to have included in your rent.
* **Utilities Other Info** (`utilities_other_info`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information about your utilities.
* **Property Preferences** (`property_preferences`, `text`, optional): This page is where you will enter any property preferences you have.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, optional): This page allows you to select a property type (House, Condo, Townhouse, or Other).
* **Property Type Other** (`property_type_other`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information about your property type if you select "Other".
* **Move In Condition** (`move_in_condition`, `select_one`, optional): This page allows you to select a move-in condition (Move In Condition 1, Move In Condition 2, or Other).
* **Move In Condition Other** (`move_in_condition_other`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information about your move-in condition if you select "Other".
* **Renter Background** (`renter_background`, `text`, optional): This page is where you will enter any relevant background information about yourself.
* **Renter Background Other** (`renter_background_other`, `text`, optional): This page is for providing additional information about your background if you have selected "Other".
