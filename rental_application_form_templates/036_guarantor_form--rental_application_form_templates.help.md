# Guarantor Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the guarantor of a loan or rental agreement. Please complete this form to provide your details and ensure the agreement can proceed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If a field is left blank or marked as 'No', it may delay the processing of your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guarantor Name** (`guarantor_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Details** (`guarantor_contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Employment Status** (`guarantor_employment_status`, select multiple, required: false): Select the status of your employment, which may be 'Employed', 'Self-employed', 'Retired', or 'Student'.
* **Employment Details** (`guarantor_employment_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your employment, such as your job title or industry.
* **Income** (`guarantor_income`, number, required: false): Enter your annual income.
* **Income Frequency** (`guarantor_income_frequency`, number, required: false): Indicate how often you receive income, e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.
* **Employment Verification** (`guarantor_employment_verification`, select one, required: false): Check if you have employment verification, which means your employer will confirm your employment status.
* **Income Verification** (`guarantor_income_verification`, select one, required: false): Check if you have income verification, which means you can provide proof of income.
* **Education Level** (`guarantor_education`, select multiple, required: false): Choose your highest education level, which may be 'High School Diploma', 'Some College', 'College', or 'University'.
* **Employment Type** (`guarantor_employment_type`, select multiple, required: false): Indicate the type of employment you have, which may be 'Full-time', 'Part-time', 'Contract', or 'Freelance'.
* **Occupation** (`guarantor_occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your current occupation.
* **Income Source** (`guarantor_income_source`, text, required: false): Describe the source of your income.
* **Employment Length** (`guarantor_employment_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Education Level** (`guarantor_education_level`, select multiple, required: false): Choose your highest education level.
* **Employment Status Change** (`guarantor_employment_status_change`, select multiple, required: false): Check if your employment status has changed.
* **Occupation Change** (`guarantor_occupation_change`, select multiple, required: false): Check if your occupation has changed.
* **Income Source Change** (`guarantor_income_source_change`, select multiple, required: false): Check if your income source has changed.
* **Employment Occupation** (`guarantor_employment_occupation`, select multiple, required: false): Check if you have employment occupation.
* **Employment Employment Occupation** (`guarantor_employment_employment_occupation`, select multiple, required: false): Check if you have employment employment occupation.
* **Employment Details Change** (`guarantor_employment_details_change`, text, required: false): Enter any changes in your employment details.
* **Income Employment Details** (`guarantor_income_employment_details`, text, required: false): Enter any changes in your income employment details.
* **Occupation Employment Details** (`guarantor_occupation_employment_details`, text, required: false): Enter any changes in your occupation employment details.
* **Employment Occupation Change** (`guarantor_employment_occupation_change`, text, required: false): Enter any changes in your employment occupation.
* **Form Signature** (`guarantor_form_signature`, date, required: false): Date and sign the form to confirm you have completed it accurately.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents ready to provide proof of your identity and employment status.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
