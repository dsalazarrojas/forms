# Equestrian Facility Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applying to rent an equestrian facility. It will help us understand your rental needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all relevant questions to the best of your ability.
2. If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "special_requests" field.
3. Make sure to provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equestrian Facility Rental Application** (`equestrian_facility_rental_application_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`): Choose 'Yes' if you are the applicant, otherwise choose 'No'. This field is required.
* **Rental Request** (`rental_request`): Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting a rental, otherwise choose 'No'. This field is required.
* **Facility Info** (`facility_info`): Please provide any relevant facility-related information.
* **Rental Details** (`rental_details`): Provide any additional information about your rental.
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`): Please review and agree to the rental terms and conditions.
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`): Please select the start date of your rental. This field is required.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`): Please select the end date of your rental. This field is required.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`): Please enter the duration of your rental. This field is required.
* **Equestrian Activities** (`equestrian_activities`): Please specify the type of equestrian activities that will take place on the facility.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): Please provide any additional requests or requirements.
* **Rental Status** (`rental_status`): Please choose the status of your rental. Choose 'Active' if you are currently renting, otherwise choose 'Inactive'. This field is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Please provide any additional information that you think is relevant to your rental.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Please enter your phone number.
