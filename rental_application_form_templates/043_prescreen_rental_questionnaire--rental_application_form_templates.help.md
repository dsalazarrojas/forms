# Prescreen Rental Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prescreen Rental Questionnaire is a pre-screening tool used to collect personal and contact information from potential renters. It helps us verify the accuracy of the information provided in the rental application and assess eligibility for renting.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read the instructions and explanations for each field to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
- Fill in your first name and last name to help us identify you.
- Provide a valid email address to enable us to contact you regarding your application.
- Enter your phone number to facilitate communication.
- Describe your rental history, including any past rental experiences or issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name, as identified by the applicant.
  This field is required to help us identify you.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name, as identified by the applicant.
  This field is required to help us identify you.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address where we can contact you.
  This field is required to ensure we can reach you for further communication.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number for contact.
  This field is required for us to reach you.
- **Rental History** (`rental_history`, text, optional): Describe your past rental experiences, including any issues or concerns.
  This field is optional, but providing accurate information will help us better assess your eligibility.
