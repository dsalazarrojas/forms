# Rental Background Check Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Background Check Authorization Form is a crucial document that confirms the tenant's and property manager's understanding and consent for a background check to be conducted. This form is used to ensure that both parties are aware of the background check's purpose and scope.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  The tenant fills out the form by providing their information and making selections.
2.  The tenant must sign the form to confirm that they understand and authorize the background check.
3.  The property manager reviews the form, signs it, and ensures that they have made the selections and understand the scope of the background check.
4.  Both the tenant and property manager must sign the form, indicating their agreement and approval of the background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Tenant Name** (Tenant Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tenant who will be subject to the background check.
- **Authorization Date** (Authorization Date, date, required): Enter the date of the authorization, which is the date when the form was signed by both the tenant and the property manager.
- **Rental Unit Number** (Rental Unit Number, number, optional): Enter the number of the rental unit that the tenant is renting.
- **Property Manager Name** (Property Manager Name, text, required): Enter the name of the property manager who is authorizing the background check.
- **Authorized By** (Authorized By, select one, required): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the background check is authorized.
- **Background Check Results** (Background Check Results, select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the background check results are available or 'No' to indicate that they are not.
- **Signature** (Signature, note, required): Sign the form to confirm that the tenant understands and authorizes the background check.
- **Property Manager Signature** (Property Manager Signature, note, required): Sign the form to confirm that the property manager authorizes and approves of the background check.

## Tips

*   Always make sure to sign the form completely and accurately.
*   Review the background check process and scope with the tenant and property manager before filling out the form.
*   Ensure that both the tenant and property manager sign the form before proceeding with the background check.
