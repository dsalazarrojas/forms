# suburban_townhouse_family_rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for families applying to rent a suburban townhouse. It gathers information about the applicant's personal details, rental history, landlord references, pets, move-in and move-out dates, rent frequency, payment method, move-in status, rent amount, move-in and move-out fees, lease term, property type, and move-out status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete each section of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. If a field is optional, use it only if the information is relevant and applicable to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your social security number for identification purposes.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status:
	+ Option 1: Employed
	+ Option 2: Self-Employed
	+ Option 3: Unemployed
* **Rental History**: Select your rental history:
	+ Option 1: Good Rental History
	+ Option 2: Fair Rental History
	+ Option 3: Poor Rental History
* **Landlord References**: Select your landlord references:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **Pets**: Select if you have pets:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you plan to move in.
* **Move In Time**: Enter the time you plan to move in.
* **Move In Date Note**: Add any notes or additional information about your move-in date.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date you plan to move out.
* **Move Out Date Time**: Enter the time you plan to move out.
* **Move Out Date Note**: Add any notes or additional information about your move-out date.
* **Rent Frequency**: Select your rent frequency:
	+ Option 1: Monthly
	+ Option 2: Bi-Monthly
	+ Option 3: Quarterly
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method:
	+ Option 1: Bank Transfer
	+ Option 2: Cash
	+ Option 3: Check
* **Move In Status**: Select your move-in status:
	+ Option 1: Available to move in immediately
	+ Option 2: Will be available to move in within 30 days
	+ Option 3: Unable to move in immediately
* **Rent Amount**: Enter the rent amount (in dollars).
* **Move In Fee**: Enter the move-in fee (in dollars).
* **Move Out Fee**: Enter the move-out fee (in dollars).
* **Lease Term**: Select your lease term:
	+ Option 1: 6 months
	+ Option 2: 12 months
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property you are interested in:
	+ Option 1: Townhouse
	+ Option 2: Condo
	+ Option 3: Apartment
* **Move Out Status**: Select your move-out status:
	+ Option 1: Will move out immediately
	+ Option 2: Will move out within 30 days
	+ Option 3: Unable to move out immediately

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If a field is optional, use it only if the information is relevant and applicable to your situation.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
