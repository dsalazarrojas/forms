# Apartment Leasing License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help applicants complete the Apartment Leasing License Application Form accurately and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by providing the application date**. This is the date when you submitted your application.
2. **Enter your full name and email address**. This is your contact information.
3. **Provide your phone number**. This is how we will contact you with any updates or questions.
4. **Enter your date of birth**. This is your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
5. **Enter your Social Security Number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN)**. This is your identification number.
6. **Enter your driver's license number**. This is your driver's license number.
7. **Enter your driver's license state**. This is the state where your driver's license was issued.
8. **Enter your current street address**. This is your current residence address.
9. **Enter your current city**. This is the city where your current residence is located.
10. **Enter the current state where you live**. This is the state where your current address is located.
11. **Enter your current zip code**. This is the zip code of your current address.
12. **Select the current housing status**. Choose "Renting", "Owning", "Living with family", "Living with roommates", or "Other".
13. **If applicable, enter your current landlord's name**. This is your current landlord's name.
14. **If applicable, enter your current landlord's phone number**. This is your current landlord's contact number.
15. **If applicable, enter your current landlord's email address**. This is your current landlord's contact email.
16. **Select the length of time you have been at your current address**. Choose from less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 2 years, 2 to 5 years, or more than 5 years.
17. **Enter the reason for moving**. This is the explanation for why you are applying for a new apartment.
18. **Enter the address or name of the property you are applying for**. This is the address or name of the apartment you are interested in.
19. **Enter the desired unit number if known**. This is the unit number you are interested in.
20. **Enter the desired move-in date**. This is the date you would like to move in.
21. **Select the preferred lease term**. Choose from 6 months, 9 months, 12 months, 18 months, or 24 months.
22. **Please note that this section is for employment information**.
23. **Select your current employment status**. Choose "Employed full-time", "Employed part-time", "Self-employed", "Unemployed", "Retired", "Student", "Disabled", or "Other".
24. **Enter your employer's name**. This is your current or most recent employer's name.
25. **Enter your employer's address**. This is your employer's address.
26. **Enter your job title**. This is your job title or position.
27. **Select the length of time you have been employed**. Choose from less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 2 years, 2 to 5 years, or more than 5 years.
28. **Enter your supervisor's name**. This is your current or most recent supervisor's name.
29. **Enter your supervisor's phone number**. This is your supervisor's contact number.
30. **Enter your monthly gross income**. This is your monthly income before taxes.
31. **If applicable, enter any additional income sources**. This is any income you earn from other sources.
32. **If applicable, enter the source of the additional income**. This is the source of the additional income.
33. **Please note that this section is for financial information**.
34. **Enter the name of your bank**. This is your bank's name.
35. **Select the type of account you have**. Choose from "Checking", "Savings", "Both", or "Other".
36. **Enter your approximate credit score range**. This is your credit score range.
37. **Select whether you have ever filed bankruptcy**. Choose "False" or "True".
38. **If applicable, enter details about your bankruptcy**. This is any additional information about your bankruptcy.
39. **Select whether you have ever been evicted**. Choose "False" or "True".
40. **If applicable, enter details about your eviction history**. This is any additional information about your eviction history.
41. **Select whether you have any criminal history**. Choose "False" or "True".
42. **If applicable, enter details about your criminal history**. This is any additional information about your criminal history.
43. **Please note that this section is for additional occupants**.
44. **Enter the total number of occupants**. This is the number of people who will live with you at the apartment.
45. **Enter the number of adults (18+)**. This is the number of adults living with you.
46. **Enter the number of children under 18**. This is the number of children living with you.
47. **Enter the ages of your children**. This is the age of each child.
48. **Please note that this section is for pet information**.
49. **Select whether you have any pets**. Choose "False" or "True".
50. **If applicable, enter the type of pet**. This is the type of pet you have.
51. **If applicable, enter the breed of your pet**. This is the breed of your pet.
52. **If applicable, enter the size of your pet**. This is the weight of your pet in pounds.
53. **If applicable, enter the age of your pet**. This is the age of your pet.
54. **Please note that this section is for vehicle information**.
55. **Enter any vehicle information**. This is any information about your vehicle.
56. **Select whether parking is required**. Choose "True" or "False".
57. **Please note that this section is for personal references**.
58. **Enter the name of the first reference**. This is the full name of your first reference.
59. **Enter the phone number of the first reference**. This is the contact number of your first reference.
60. **Enter the email address of the first reference**. This is the contact email of your first reference.
61. **Select the relationship with the first reference**. Choose how you know them.
62. **Enter the name of the second reference**. This is the full name of your second reference.
63. **Enter the phone number of the second reference**. This is the contact number of your second reference.
64. **Enter the email address of the second reference**. This is the contact email of your second reference.
65. **Select the relationship with the second reference**. Choose how you know them.
66. **Enter any additional comments**. This is any additional information you would like to share.
67. **Enter your signature**. This is your digital signature.
68. **Enter the date signed**. This is the date you signed the application.
69. **Select whether an application fee has been paid**. Choose "True", "False", or "Waived".
70. **Select the status of your application**. Choose from "Submitted", "Under review", "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending additional information".
