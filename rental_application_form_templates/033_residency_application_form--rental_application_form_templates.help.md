# Residency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residency Application Form is used to gather information about a tenant's rental agreement, including their contact details, address, and lease terms. This form helps landlords or property managers to quickly and accurately collect the necessary data for managing tenancy records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Enter your contact number for easy communication.
3. Provide your address, which is the address of the property you are renting.
4. Select the start and end dates of your tenancy from the calendar.
5. If you have a reason for your tenancy, you can optionally provide this information.
6. If applicable, provide the name of your landlord or property manager.
7. Choose the nature of your relationship with the landlord from the provided options.
8. If you have lease start and end dates, you can optionally provide these.
9. Finally, if your lease has a specific length, you can enter this information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name**: Enter your full name as the tenant.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your telephone number for easy communication with the landlord or property manager.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the property you are renting.
* **Start Date**: Select the date you started your tenancy.
* **End Date**: Select the date you plan to end your tenancy.
* **Reason**: If you have a reason for your tenancy, provide a brief explanation.
* **Landlord Name**: If you are renting from a specific landlord, provide their name.
* **Tenant Relationship**: Choose how you are related to the landlord (e.g. tenant, family member, business partner).
* **Lease Start Date**: Select the start date of your lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Select the end date of your lease.
* **Lease Length**: If your lease has a specific length, enter this information.
* **Rent**: Not applicable in this form (this field is not currently used).
* **Rent** was removed because it was marked as optional and there was no corresponding information for rent in the yaml file. If you are interested in adding this information, please let me know so I can update this guide.
