# Vacuum Lifter Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in renting a vacuum lifter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information page with your name and contact details.
2. Provide your User Details in the User Details page.
3. Enter Equipment Details in the Equipment Details page.
4. Specify Rental Details in the Rental Details page.
5. If you have any special requests, enter them in the Special Requests page.
6. Add your contact information in the Contact Information page.
7. Finally, confirm your submission by typing "I confirm my submission" in the Confirm Submission field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This is the first page where you'll enter your name and contact details. 
* **User Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Equipment Details**: Provide details about the equipment you need for rental.
* **Rental Details**: Specify the details of the rental you're requesting.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as specific dates or time windows, enter them here.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read through the terms and conditions of the rental agreement before submitting your form.
* **Confirm Submission**: This is a confirmation field that asks you to confirm your submission. Type "I confirm my submission" to complete the form.
* **Confirm Submission 2-18**: These fields are redundant and can be ignored.
