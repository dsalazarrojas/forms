# Health Retreat Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow individuals to submit a rental application for a health retreat, providing necessary details for a smooth and successful rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully before proceeding.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid delays in processing your application.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retreat Request (Retreat Request, text, required/optional):** This field is used to describe the retreat type and preferences you are looking for. Please specify your preferred retreat type, and provide any additional details about the retreat you are interested in.
* **Group Details (Group Details, select_one, required/optional):** Select the group type you are interested in (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3). This helps us understand your specific needs and provide the most suitable retreat environment.
* **Date Range (Date Range, date, required/optional):** Specify your preferred date range for the retreat, using the standard date format (mm/dd/yy - mm/dd/yy). If you're flexible with dates, please provide a range of dates.
* **Time Slots (Time Slots, select_multiple, required/optional):** Select one or multiple time slots for the retreat (09:00-12:00, 12:00-15:00, 15:00-18:00). These will be used to schedule your retreat sessions.
* **Venue Preferences (Venue Preferences, note, required/optional):** This field is for any special requests or preferences you may have for the retreat venue. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, email, required/optional):** Enter your email address to enable us to contact you regarding your application and any further details.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required/optional):** If you'd like to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number. Please ensure it's up-to-date and correct.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, required/optional):** If there's anything else you'd like to share about your retreat preferences or requirements, please provide additional comments here.
