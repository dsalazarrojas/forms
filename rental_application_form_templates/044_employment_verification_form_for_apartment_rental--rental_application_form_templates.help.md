# Employment Verification Form For Apartment Rental - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Verification Form is designed to gather information about your current or past employment to help us assess your eligibility for an apartment rental. This form is a mandatory requirement for all rental applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name exactly as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your current or previous employer's name and contact information.
3. Enter your job title or role at the time of employment.
4. Enter your date of hire or start date of employment.
5. Provide your current salary or annual income.
6. Confirm your current or past employment status with your employer.
7. Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
8. Date the form with the date you complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name**: 
  • Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employer Name**: 
  • Enter your current or previous employer's name, including their company or organization.
* **Job Title**: 
  • Enter your job title or role at the time of employment.
* **Date of Hire**: 
  • Enter your date of hire or start date of employment in the MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 01/01/20).
* **Salary**: 
  • Enter your current or annual salary.
* **Employer Address**: 
  • Enter your current or previous employer's address.
* **Employer Phone**: 
  • Enter your current or previous employer's phone number.
* **Confirm Employment**: 
  • Select "Employer Confirmed" if your employer has confirmed your employment status, or "Employer Not Confirmed" if they have not.
* **Employment Verification Signature**: 
  • Sign here to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Applicant Signature**: 
  • Sign here to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
* **Date of Signature**: 
  • Date the form with the date you complete it.
* **Employer Signature**: 
  • Sign here if you are the employer or authorized representative.
* **Employment Verification Form Signature Date**: 
  • Date the form with the date you complete it as the employer or authorized representative.
