# Rental Reference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Reference Form is used to gather information about a tenant's rental history. This form helps landlords and property managers to verify the identity and employment status of potential tenants, ensuring they have a good understanding of a tenant's ability to pay rent and fulfill their responsibilities. This form is designed to be completed by a reference, typically a friend, family member, or colleague, who can vouch for the tenant's character and rental history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct property type from the dropdown menu to select the type of property you are renting.
2. Enter the applicant's email address in the "Applicant email" field. This is the email address you will use to contact the applicant.
3. Enter the email addresses of up to four potential landlords or property managers who are aware of the applicant's rental history.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the rental agreement, if applicable.
5. Enter the length of tenancy, in months.
6. Select the reason for leaving, if the applicant is no longer renting.
7. Enter the amount of rent paid, if applicable.
8. Enter the amount of rent arrears, if applicable.
9. Select the employment status of the reference and the landlord.
10. Enter the reference's phone number and address.
11. Select the reference's employment status and income level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property type**: Select the type of property you are renting, such as "House", "Flat", "Apartment", "Townhouse", or "Condo".
* **Applicant email**: Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Landlord 1 email**, **Landlord 2 email**, **Landlord 3 email**, **Landlord 4 email**: Enter the email addresses of up to four potential landlords or property managers who are aware of the applicant's rental history.
* **Start date**: Enter the start date of the rental agreement.
* **End date**: Enter the end date of the rental agreement, if applicable.
* **Length of tenancy**: Enter the length of tenancy, in months.
* **Reason for leaving**: Select the reason the applicant left the property, if applicable.
* **Rent paid**: Select if the applicant has paid rent.
* **Rent paid amount**: Enter the amount of rent paid.
* **Rent arrears**: Select if the applicant has rent arrears.
* **Rent arrears amount**: Enter the amount of rent arrears.
* **Reference name**: Enter the reference's name.
* **Reference relationship**: Select the relationship between the reference and the applicant.
* **Reference phone**: Enter the reference's phone number.
* **Reference address**: Enter the reference's address.
* **Reference employment**: Select the reference's employment status.
* **Reference income**: Select the reference's income level.
* **Reference employed since**: Enter the time the reference has been employed.
* **Reference employed at**: Enter the location where the reference is employed.
* **Landlord employment**: Select the employment status of the landlord.
* **Landlord income**: Select the income level of the landlord.
