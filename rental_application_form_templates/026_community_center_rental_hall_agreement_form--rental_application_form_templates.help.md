# Community Center Rental Hall Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from renters to reserve a rental space within a community center. The form ensures that the community center has the necessary details to manage the rental process efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) in the field label.
2. Ensure that the email address you provide is valid and active.
3. Choose the correct type of event or activity from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date and time of the event accurately.
5. Provide a valid payment method and estimated number of attendees.
6. Carefully review and sign the rental agreement and terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Renter or Organization** (`renter_full_name_agr`): Enter the full name of the renter or organization.
* **Email Address** (`renter_email_agr`): Enter a valid email address that the renter can be reached at.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`renter_phone_agr`): Enter the primary contact phone number of the renter.
* **Mailing Address** (`renter_address_agr`): Enter the street address, city, state, and zip code of the renter's address.
* **Type of Event or Activity** (`hall_event_type_agr`): Choose the correct type of event or activity from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of the Event** (`rental_date_requested_agr`): Enter the date of the event accurately.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time_agr`): Enter the start time of the event accurately.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time_agr`): Enter the end time of the event accurately.
* **Estimated Number of Attendees** (`estimated_attendees_agr`): Enter the estimated number of attendees.
* **Which space is being rented** (`rental_space_requested_agr`): Choose the correct space from the dropdown menu.
* **Total Rental Fee** (`total_rental_fee_agr`): Enter the total rental fee in USD.
* **Security Deposit Amount** (`security_deposit_amount_agr`): Enter the security deposit amount that will be refunded after the event.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_agr`): Choose the preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Rental Terms Acknowledgment** (`rental_terms_acknowledgment_agr`): Choose to agree to the rental terms and policies.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release_agr`): Choose to assume liability for any damage or injury that occurs during the event.
* **Date of Agreement** (`date_of_agreement_agr`): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Digital Signature** (`renter_signature_digital_agr`): Sign and type your full name in this field.
