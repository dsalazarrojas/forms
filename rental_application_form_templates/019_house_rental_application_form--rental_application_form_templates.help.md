# House Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals applying to rent a house. It's designed to provide a template for you to fill out your details and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form with your information, making sure to provide accurate details about yourself and the property you are interested in renting.
- Be sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) in the field label.
- You can skip fields that are not required, but be aware that missing required fields may delay or impact the processing of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, `text`, Optional): Please provide your full name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, `text`, Optional): Describe the type of property you are interested in renting, including any specific features or amenities you need.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact information, including any preferred email and phone numbers for the landlord or property manager.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your phone number to enable quick communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, Optional): Enter your email address to stay updated about your application status.
* **Rental Period** (`rental_period`, `date`, Optional): If you're not planning to stay long-term, please specify the exact dates you plan to rent the property.
* **Move-in Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, Optional): If you're planning to move in immediately or in the near future, please enter the expected move-in date.
* **Move-out Date** (`move_out_date`, `date`, Optional): If you're planning to move out, please specify the expected move-out date.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `select_one`, Optional): Please choose the type of property you're interested in (e.g., single-family home, apartment, etc.).
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, Optional): If you have a preferred rent amount, please enter it here. This will help the landlord or property manager understand your budget and expectations.
