# Lease Renewal Instructions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information required for lease renewal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Ensure you have the required information before submitting the form.
3. Enter your lease start date in the `Lease Start Date` field.
4. Enter your lease end date in the `Lease End Date` field.
5. Select the rental frequency from the options provided in the `Rent Frequency` field.
6. If you select `Other` in the `Rent Frequency` field, enter the frequency in the `Other (if other, specify)` field.
7. Enter the lease renewal deadline in the `Lease Renewal Deadline` field.
8. Enter the notice period in the `Notice Period` field.
9. Select the unit of time for the notice period from the options provided in the `Notice Period Unit` field.
10. If you select `Other` in the `Notice Period Unit` field, enter the unit in the `Other (if other, specify)` field.
11. Select the lease renewal option from the options provided in the `Lease Renewal Option` field.
12. If you select `Other` in the `Lease Renewal Option` field, enter the option in the `Other (if other, specify)` field.
13. Enter the name of the landlord in the `Landlord Name` field.
14. Enter the name of the tenant in the `Tenant Name` field.
15. Enter the email of the tenant in the `Tenant Email` field.
16. Enter the phone number of the tenant in the `Tenant Phone` field.
17. Enter the phone number of the landlord in the `Landlord Phone` field.
18. Enter the property address in the `Property Address` field.
19. Enter the landlord signature in the `Landlord Signature` field.
20. Enter the tenant signature in the `Tenant Signature` field.
21. Enter the lease terms and conditions in the `Lease Terms and Conditions` field.
22. Enter the lease renewal instructions in the `Lease Renewal Instructions` field.
23. Enter the date of signature in the `Date` field.
24. Enter your signature in the `Signature` field.
25. Enter any additional notes in the `Notes` field.
26. Finally, click the `Submit` button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is this for**: This field is intended to prompt the user to understand the purpose of the form.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the date when the lease started.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the date when the lease will end.
* **Rent Frequency**: Select how often rent is paid, such as weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
* **Other (if other, specify)**: If your rent frequency is not listed, enter the frequency here.
* **Lease Renewal Deadline**: Enter the date when the lease needs to be renewed.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the number of days, weeks, months, or years before the lease ends.
* **Notice Period Unit**: Select the unit of time for the notice period, such as days, weeks, months, or years.
* **Other (if other, specify)**: If you select `Other` in the `Notice Period Unit` field, enter the unit here.
* **Lease Renewal Option**: Select whether the lease renewal will be automatic, manual, or other.
* **Other (if other, specify)**: If you select `Other` in the `Lease Renewal Option` field, enter the option here.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email**: Enter the email of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the tenant.
* **Landlord Phone**: Enter the phone number of the landlord.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Landlord Signature**: Enter the landlord's signature.
* **Tenant Signature**: Enter the tenant's signature.
* **Lease Terms and Conditions**: Enter any lease terms and conditions.
* **Lease Renewal Instructions**: Enter any instructions for lease renewal.
* **Date**: Enter the date of signature.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes.
* **Submit**: Click the button to complete the form and submit the data.
