# watersports_equipment_hire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The watersports equipment hire form is used to collect information for hiring or renting watersports equipment for a specific duration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of watersports equipment you wish to hire.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Specify the date you wish to hire the equipment.
5. Enter the date when the hired equipment is expected to be returned.
6. Choose the type of watersports equipment you wish to hire.
7. Select if you wish to hire more than one piece of equipment.
8. Provide a brief description of the equipment.
9. Indicate the location where the equipment will be used.
10. Specify if there are any special instructions for the equipment hire.
11. Add any comments or remarks regarding the equipment hire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Watersports Equipment** (`watersports_equipment`): Choose the type of equipment you wish to hire.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you wish to hire the equipment.
* **Equipment Hire Start Date** (`equipment_hire_start_date`): Enter the start date of the hire period.
* **Equipment Hire End Date** (`equipment_hire_end_date`): Enter the end date of the hire period.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Choose the type of watersports equipment you wish to hire.
* **Equipment Quantity** (`equipment_quantity`): Choose if you wish to hire more than one piece of equipment.
* **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`): Provide a brief description of the equipment.
* **Rental Location** (`rental_location`): Indicate the location where the equipment will be used.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`): Provide any special instructions for the equipment hire.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments or remarks regarding the equipment hire.
* **Additional Equipment** (`additional_equipment`): Add any additional equipment you wish to hire (if applicable).
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`): Add any rental terms and conditions (if applicable).
* **Equipment Hire Status** (`equipment_hire_status`): Choose the status of the hired equipment (Active or Inactive).
* **Hire Status** (`hire_status`): Choose the status of the hire request (Active or Inactive).
* **Status** (`status`): Choose the status of the hire request (Active or Inactive).
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`): Enter the date you wish to hire the equipment (not required).
* **Hire Time** (`hire_time`): Enter the time you wish to hire the equipment (not required).
* **Return Date** (`return_date`): Enter the date when the hired equipment is expected to be returned (not required).
* **Return Time** (`return_time`): Enter the time when the hired equipment is expected to be returned (not required).
