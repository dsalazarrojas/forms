# Short Term Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document a short-term rental agreement between a renter and a landlord. It collects essential information about the renter, contact details, property specifics, and payment terms. This form helps establish clear communication and prevents misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Review and Understand the Form:** Before filling out the form, make sure you understand its purpose and the required information. This form is divided into sections for renter details, contact information, property details, and payment and rental terms.

2.  **Gather Necessary Information:** Before proceeding, collect all necessary documents and details such as government-issued ID, proof of address, and payment information.

3.  **Fill Out the Form:** Start by filling out the form section by section, providing accurate and complete information for each field as you go.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Renter Details**: This section is for you to provide your personal details as the renter.
    *   **Renter Info**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
    *   **Renter Address**: Enter your address where you'll be staying.
- **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information for easy communication.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your email address for official communication.
- **Property Details**: Describe the property you wish to rent.
    *   **Property Name or Address**: Specify the name or address of the property you wish to rent.
    *   **Rent Period**: Specify the duration of your rental period.
- **Payment Information**: This section is for financial details regarding your rental.
    *   **Payment Method**: Describe the method you will use for payment (e.g., bank transfer, check, etc.).
    *   **Payment Information**: Specify any additional payment details if necessary.
- **Terms and Conditions**: Read through this section carefully, it outlines the terms and conditions of your rental.
    *   **Rental Fees**: Specify the fees you are willing to pay for the rental.
    *   **Rental Fees Details**: Clarify any additional details or conditions for your rental fee.
- **Renter Address**: This field is to confirm the address of the renter.
    *   **Renter Address**: Confirm your address where you'll be staying.
- **Renter Signature**: This is your agreement signature.
    *   **Renter Signature**: Confirm your agreement by signing this section.
- **Landlord Signature**: This is the signature of the landlord or property owner.
    *   **Landlord Signature**: Confirm your agreement by signing this section.

## Tips

- Always double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all fields are filled out completely for a smooth and successful rental agreement process.
- Read through the terms and conditions section to understand your responsibilities and expectations.
- Sign both the renter and landlord sections to finalize your agreement.
