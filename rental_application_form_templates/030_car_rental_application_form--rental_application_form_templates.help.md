# Car Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from customers for a car rental service. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the pickup location and date for when you would like to pick up the vehicle.
2. Select the preferred vehicle class from the options provided.
3. If you have any specific vehicle requests, enter them in the "Specific Vehicle Request" field.
4. Provide your driver information, including your full name, age, email address, and phone number.
5. If you need any additional services, select them from the options provided.
6. Acknowledge that you have read and agree with the car rental application terms.
7. Sign the form to confirm your application.
8. Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to pick up the vehicle.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Select the date for pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required): Select the time for pickup.
* **Drop-off Location** (`dropoff_location`, text, required): Enter the location where you would like to return the vehicle.
* **Drop-off Date** (`dropoff_date`, date, required): Select the date for return.
* **Drop-off Time** (`dropoff_time`, time, required): Select the time for return.
* **Preferred Vehicle Class** (`vehicle_type_preference`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle from the options provided (e.g., Economy, Compact, Mid-size, Full-size, SUV, Luxury, Minivan).
* **Specific Vehicle Request** (`specific_vehicle_request`, text, required): If you have any specific vehicle requests, enter them here (e.g., Ford Mustang, Toyota Camry).
* **Driver Full Name** (`driver_full_name_basic`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Driver Age** (`driver_age_basic`, number, required): Enter your age to verify age and eligibility.
* **Email Address** (`driver_email_basic`, email, required): Enter your email address for rental status updates.
* **Phone Number** (`driver_phone_basic`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number for the rental.
* **Additional Services Needed** (`additional_services_requested`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional services you need, such as GPS Navigation, Child Seat, Additional Driver, Satellite Radio, Full Insurance Coverage.
* **Acknowledgement** (`rental_acknowledgement_basic`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree with the car rental application terms.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation_basic`, text, required): Type your name to confirm the rental application.
* **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_rental_comments_basic`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or requests you may have for the rental.

Remember to review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting your application.
