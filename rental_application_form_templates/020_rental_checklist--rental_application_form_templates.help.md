# Rental Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a rental property or agreement. It gathers details such as contact information, rental terms, and lease dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Rental Application Form" section with the relevant details about the rental property or agreement.
2.  Provide contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3.  Specify the rental terms in the "Rental Terms" section.
4.  Select relevant tenant references from the "Tenant References" section.
5.  Enter landlord information in the "Landlord Information" section.
6.  Choose landlord references from the "Landlord References" section.
7.  Specify lease terms in the "Lease Terms" section.
8.  Enter lease dates in the "Lease Dates" section.
9.  Update the "Rental Notes" section with any additional information.
10. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required and optional fields have been completed.
