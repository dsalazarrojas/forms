# Golf Cart Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Cart Rental Application Form is a simple form designed to gather essential information from clients who are interested in renting a golf cart for an event. This form is used to collect contact information, event details, and rental requirements from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
4. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
5. Choose the date of your **Event Date**.
6. Choose the time of your **Event Time**.
7. Select the type of cart you would like to rent (**Golf Cart** or **Electric Cart**).
8. Indicate the number of carts you need (**Cart Quantity**).
9. Choose the duration for which you would like to rent the cart (**Rental Duration**).
10. Add any additional information or requests for the rental in the **Notes** field (optional).
11. Click **Submit** to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name so we can contact you regarding the rental.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you regarding the rental.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Address**: Enter your address to ensure we can deliver the cart to your location.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of your event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of your event.
* **Cart Type**: Choose between **Golf Cart** or **Electric Cart** based on your preference.
* **Cart Quantity**: Indicate how many carts you need for your event.
* **Rental Duration**: Choose the duration for which you would like to rent the cart (1, 2, or 3 hours).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or requests for the rental (optional).
* **Submit**: Click to send the form and complete the rental application.
