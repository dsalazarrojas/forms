# extended_stay_suite_rental_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The extended_stay_suite_rental_application form is used to collect information from individuals seeking to rent a suite for an extended stay. It is designed to ensure accurate communication and streamline the rental application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, starting with the "Contact Info" page.
2. On the "Property Details" page, provide the suite rental property details.
3. On the "Rental Period" page, select the desired rental period (Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term).
4. On the "Move In Date" page, select the desired move-in date.
5. On the "Move Out Date" page, select the desired move-out date.
6. On the "Rental Amount" page, enter the rental amount (if applicable).
7. On the "Payment Terms" page, select the desired payment term (Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Contact Info (1)**: text (optional)
*   This page requires the applicant's contact information, which will be used for communication.
### **Property Details (2)**: text (optional)
*   Here, you provide the suite rental property details that will help the rental company assess the property's condition and suitability for the applicant.
### **Email (4)**: email (optional)
*   Please enter a valid email address that the rental company will use for communication.
### **Phone Number (5)**: text (optional)
*   Enter your phone number for the rental company to contact you.
### **Rental Period (6)**: Select One (optional)
*   Choose a rental period: Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term.
### **Move In Date (7)**: date (optional)
*   Select the desired move-in date for the suite rental.
### **Move Out Date (8)**: date (optional)
*   Select the desired move-out date for the suite rental.
### **Rental Amount (9)**: number (optional)
*   Enter the rental amount if you've discussed it with the rental company.
### **Payment Terms (10)**: Select Multiple (optional)
*   Choose a payment term: Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
