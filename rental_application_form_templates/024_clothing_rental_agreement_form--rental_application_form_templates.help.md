# Clothing Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a comprehensive clothing rental agreement between the renter and the rental company. It collects information about the renter, the rental item, and the terms of the rental, including pricing and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your address.
3. Fill out the rental details, including the description, size, and color of the garment.
4. Choose the rental start and end dates.
5. Set the rental duration in days.
6. Agree on the pricing and deposit terms.
7. Read and agree to the rental terms and conditions.
8. Sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter Name** (`renter_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`renter_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`renter_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Renter Address** (`renter_address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
* **-- Rental Details --** (`section_rental`, note, required): This section contains information about the rental item.
* **Item Description** (`item_description`, text, required): Describe the type, style, and brand of the garment.
* **Item Size** (`item_size`, text, required): Enter the size of the rental item.
* **Colour** (`item_colour`, text, required): Enter the color of the rental item.
* **Condition Upon Rental** (`item_condition`, select_one, required): Choose the initial condition of the garment.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, date, required): Choose the date you pick up the item.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, date, required): Choose the date you return the item.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days renting.
* **-- Pricing and Deposit --** (`section_pricing`, note, required): This section contains pricing and deposit details.
* **Daily Rental Rate** (`daily_rental_rate`, number, required): Enter the price per day.
* **Total Rental Cost** (`total_rental_cost`, number, required): The total cost is calculated based on the daily rate and duration.
* **Security Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, required): Enter the deposit amount.
* **Total Amount Due** (`total_due`, number, required): The total amount due is calculated based on the total cost and deposit.
* **-- Rental Terms and Conditions --** (`section_terms`, note, required): This section contains important terms and conditions.
* **Damage Assessment** (`damage_assessment`, select_one, required): Agree to return the item in the same condition.
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ False
* **Late Return Policy** (`late_return_policy`, number, optional): Enter the late return charge per day.
* **Who is Responsible for Cleaning** (`cleaning_responsibility`, select_one, required): Choose who is responsible for cleaning the garment.
	+ Renter responsible
	+ Rental company responsible
	+ Included in rental
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required): Choose the cancellation policy.
	+ Non-refundable
	+ Full refund if cancelled 7+ days before
	+ 50 percent refund if cancelled within 7 days
	+ Full refund anytime
* **-- Agreement Signature --** (`section_signature`, note, required): This section contains important legal information.
* **Renter Signature** (`renter_signature`, text, required): Sign the agreement with your printed name.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing.

Note: Make sure to read each section carefully and agree to the terms before signing.
