# Vineyard Estate Rental Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential renters to evaluate their suitability for renting a vineyard estate. The form asks for details about the applicant's payment method, rental preferences, and additional information that may be relevant to the rental process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your application information on page 1.
2. Enter your payment details on page 2.
3. Specify the rental type and duration on page 3.
4. Enter your address details on page 4.
5. Provide any additional information relevant to your application on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Info (Page 1, **application_info**) (Optional): This field is for providing your name, contact information, and other relevant details about your application. 
* **Payment Info (Page 2, **payment_info**) (Optional): This field is for entering your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.) and any relevant payment terms.
* **Rental Info (Page 3, **rental_info**) (Optional): This field is for specifying the type of accommodation you are looking for (e.g., house, apartment, cabin, etc.) and the duration of your stay. 
* **Address Info (Page 4, **address_info**) (Optional): This field is for providing your current address and any other relevant contact information. 
* **Additional Info (Page 5, **additional_info**) (Optional): This field is for providing any additional information that may be relevant to your application, such as references or special requests.
