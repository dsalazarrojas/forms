# Comfort Care Product Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from renters about their rental agreement for a Comfort Care product. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that you understand the terms and conditions of the rental.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, please ensure that you have all the necessary information about the rental period, product usage, and terms ready.
2.  Complete each section carefully, paying attention to the required fields.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Renter name**: (`renter_name`, Text, Required) Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Renter contact information**: (`renter_contact`, Text, Required) Provide your preferred contact email or phone number. This will help us reach you in case of any questions or issues.
*   **Product rented**: (`product_rented`, Text, Required) Describe the product you are renting, including its model or any other relevant details.
*   **Rental start date**: (`rental_start_date`, Date, Required) Enter the date when the rental period begins in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Rental end date**: (`rental_end_date`, Date, Optional) Enter the date when the rental period ends, or specify an estimated duration if unknown.
*   **Deposit amount**: (`deposit_amount`, Number, Optional) If applicable, enter the deposit amount. Please use decimal values.
*   **Terms and conditions**: (`terms_agreed`, Select One, Required) Choose whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the rental. Please ensure you understand what these terms mean before making your choice.
*   **Delivery or pickup option**: (`delivery_option`, Select One, Optional) Indicate how you would like to receive the product - either through delivery or pickup.
*   **Additional notes**: (`additional_notes`, Text, Optional) If there are any special arrangements or requirements that you need to discuss, please include them here. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for us to know about any specific needs you may have.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
