The Renter Questionnaire form is a comprehensive tool used to gather information from renters to evaluate their eligibility for rental properties. This form helps rental property managers and agents to assess the renter's background, financial stability, and rental history, making it an essential part of the rental application process. 

The form consists of various sections that collect data on the renter's personal and financial situation. Each field is designed to gather specific information that will be reviewed to determine the rental status.

Below is the detailed help guide for filling out the form:

# Renter Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Renter Questionnaire is used to gather relevant information from renters to help make informed decisions about their rental applications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Renter Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the **Report Name** field (Field 1).
2.  Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** field (Field 2).
3.  Answer "Yes" or "No" in the **Rental Application** field (Field 3) if you are currently applying for a rental.
4.  Describe your current goals in the **goals** field (Field 4).
5.  Enter your current address in the **Current Address** field (Field 5).
6.  Select your employment status in the **Employment Status** field (Field 6).
7.  Enter your current employment income in the **Employment Income** field (Field 7).
8.  Indicate if you have rental history in the **Rental History** field (Field 8).
9.  Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have personal references in the **Personal References** field (Field 9).
10.  Answer "Yes" or "No" if you have landlord references in the **Landlord References** field (Field 10).
11.  Indicate if you have a criminal record in the **Criminal Record** field (Field 11).
12.  Enter the rental application fee in the **Rental Application Fee** field (Field 12).
13.  Enter the rent amount in the **Rent Amount** field (Field 13).
14.  Enter the lease duration in the **Lease Duration** field (Field 14).
15.  Enter the security deposit in the **Security Deposit** field (Field 15).
16.  Enter your credit score in the **Credit Score** field (Field 16).
17.  Select the rental application status in the **Rental Application Status** field (Field 17).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Report Name (Field 1)** (`title`, `text`, `false`): Enter a brief name for this rental application.
*   **Contact Information (Field 2)** (`contact_information`, `text`, `false`): Enter your contact information.
*   **Rental Application (Field 3)** (`rental_application`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Answer "Yes" if you are currently applying for a rental.
*   **goals (Field 4)** (`goals`, `text`, `false`): Describe your current goals.
*   **Current Address (Field 5)** (`current_address`, `text`, `false`): Enter your current address.
*   **Employment Status (Field 6)** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select your employment status.
*   **Employment Income (Field 7)** (`employment_income`, `number`, `false`): Enter your current employment income.
*   **Rental History (Field 8)** (`rental_history`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Indicate if you have rental history.
*   **Personal References (Field 9)** (`personal_references`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Answer "Yes" if you have personal references.
*   **Landlord References (Field 10)** (`landlord_references`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Answer "Yes" if you have landlord references.
*   **Criminal Record (Field 11)** (`criminal_record`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Indicate if you have a criminal record.
*   **Rental Application Fee (Field 12)** (`rental_application_fee`, `number`, `false`): Enter the rental application fee.
*   **Rent Amount (Field 13)** (`rent_amount`, `number`, `false`): Enter the rent amount.
*   **Lease Duration (Field 14)** (`lease_duration`, `number`, `false`): Enter the lease duration.
*   **Security Deposit (Field 15)** (`security_deposit`, `number`, `false`): Enter the security deposit.
*   **Credit Score (Field 16)** (`credit_score`, `number`, `false`): Enter your credit score.
*   **Rental Application Status (Field 17)** (`rental_application_status`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the rental application status.

## Tips
