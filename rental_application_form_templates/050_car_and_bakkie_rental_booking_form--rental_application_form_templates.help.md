# Car And Bakkie Rental Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car And Bakkie Rental Booking Form is designed to collect information from customers to reserve a car or bakkie rental. Fill out this form to book your desired vehicle, specify your details, and confirm your booking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled before submitting.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Driver's License Number accurately.
3. Select your preferred Vehicle Type (Sedan, SUV, etc.) and Fuel Type Preference (Petrol, Diesel, etc.).
4. Choose your Passenger/Cargo Capacity Needed.
5. Enter your Pickup Date, Pickup Time, Pickup Location, Return Date, and Return Time.
6. Select your preferred Insurance Coverage (Basic, Standard, etc.) and if you have Additional Drivers.
7. Choose any Additional Equipment Needed (GPS/Navigation, Child seat, etc.).
8. If applicable, select if you are a Loyalty Program Member and if you have any Special Requests or Preferences.
9. Select your Preferred Payment Method (Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
10. Confirm your booking details and agree to rental terms by selecting "Yes, confirm booking".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Driver's License Number**: Enter your driver's license number for verification purposes.
* **License Expiry Date**: Enter the date your license expires.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City**: Enter the city where you can be reached.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code where you can be reached.
* **Vehicle Type**: Choose the type of vehicle you prefer (Sedan, SUV, etc.).
* **Fuel Type Preference**: Choose your preferred fuel type (Petrol, Diesel, etc.).
* **Passenger/Cargo Capacity Needed**: Choose the capacity of your vehicle needs (2-4 passengers, 5 passengers, etc.).
* **Pickup Date**: Enter the date you want to pick up the vehicle.
* **Pickup Time**: Enter the time you want to pick up the vehicle.
* **Pickup Location**: Enter the location where you want to pick up the vehicle.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you want to return the vehicle.
* **Return Time**: Enter the time you want to return the vehicle.
* **Return Location**: Enter the location where you want to return the vehicle.
* **Insurance Coverage**: Choose your preferred insurance coverage (Basic, Standard, etc.).
* **Additional Drivers**: If there are additional drivers, select "Yes" or specify "No".
* **Additional Equipment Needed**: Choose any additional equipment you need (GPS/Navigation, Child seat, etc.).
* **Loyalty Program Member**: If you are a loyalty program member, select "Yes" or "Interested in joining".
* **Special Requests or Preferences**: Enter any special requests or preferences you may have.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
* **Booking Confirmation**: Confirm your booking details by selecting "Yes, confirm booking" to agree to rental terms.</thinking>
