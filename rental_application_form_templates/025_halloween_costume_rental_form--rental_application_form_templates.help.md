# Halloween Costumer Rental Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Customer Rental Form is a tool used to collect customer information and preferences for renting costumes during the Halloween season.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Information**: Please fill out the customer information section, providing your name, address, and any other relevant details. (customer_information, text, required: true)
2. **Price**: Select your budget for the costume rental. (price, number, required: false)
3. **Preferred Costume**: Choose your preferred costume from the options provided (Pumpkin, Ghost, Zombie, Vampire, or Superman). (preferred_costume, select_one, required: false)
4. **Group Affiliation**: Select your group affiliation (True, False, or Maybe). (group_affiliation, select_multiple, required: false)
5. **Date**: Select your preferred date for the rental. This field is currently not applicable as the options are empty. (date, date, required: false)
6. **Time**: Select your preferred time for the rental. This field is currently not applicable as the options are empty. (time, time, required: false)
7. **Note**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us. (note, text, required: false)
8. **Contact Information**: Please provide your contact details, as this information is mandatory. (contact_information, text, required: true)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information**: This is the first section of the form where you will be asked to provide your personal details. Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information.
- **Price**: This field allows you to select your budget for the costume rental. Please be aware that a higher budget will provide you with more options. 
- **Preferred Costume**: In this field, you will be able to select one of the available costumes. Be sure to choose a costume that you feel comfortable wearing and will make you happy!
- **Group Affiliation**: This is a selection where you will choose how you would like to be associated with other customers. You can select one or multiple options.
- **Date**: As this field is currently not applicable, please disregard this field.
- **Time**: Similar to the date field, the time field is currently not applicable. 
- **Note**: This is where you can provide any additional information that you would like to share with us. This could be anything from special requests to allergies you may have.
- **Contact Information**: This field is mandatory, please provide accurate information. This will help us communicate with you and provide updates about your rental. 
- **Preferred Costume**: In this field, you will be able to select one of the available costumes.
