# Rental Assistance Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Assistance Program Application Form is a tool to help individuals apply for the Rental Assistance Program, which provides financial assistance to individuals or families in need. This form gathers necessary information to ensure eligibility and process the application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please ensure you have all required information and documents before proceeding.
2. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.
3. Provide your contact information by filling in Phone and Email.
4. Enter your address and current living situation details.
5. Fill in your income and household income details.
6. Select your Rental Agreement type from the provided options.
7. If applicable, select the status of your current Application.
8. Provide any Additional Notes or Comments as required or desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Please enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Please enter your current address where you reside.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your current email address.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Income** (Income, number, required): Please enter your individual income.
* **Household Income** (Household Income, number, required): Please enter your household income.
* **Family Size** (Family Size, number, required): Please enter the number of people residing in your household.
* **Rental Agreement** (Rental Agreement, select_one, optional): Please choose one of the provided options for your rental agreement.
* **Rent Amount** (Rent Amount, number, required): Please enter the amount of rent you pay per month.
* **Application Status** (Application Status, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or multiple options for your current application status.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, optional): Please provide any additional information that might be helpful in processing your application.
* **Comments** (Comments, select_multiple, optional): Please select one or multiple options to provide comments on your application.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled correctly before submitting the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the Rental Assistance Program staff for guidance.
