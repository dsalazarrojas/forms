# Flight Time Rental Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a Flight Time Rental Agreement, which is a formal document that outlines the terms and conditions of renting an aircraft for a specific period of time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number, and confirm that you are willing to receive communication related to this rental.
3. Select the type of aircraft you wish to rent.
4. Choose the date and start and end times of the rental period.
5. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the rental.
6. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Aircraft Type** (Required): Select the type of aircraft you wish to rent.
* **Rental Date** (Required): Choose the date of the rental period.
* **Rental Start Time** (Required): Choose the start time of the rental period.
* **Rental End Time** (Required): Choose the end time of the rental period.
* **Terms & Conditions** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the rental.
* **Signed At** (Required): Please sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **User Name** (Required): Please enter your name.
* **Aircraft ID** (Optional): Please enter the ID of the aircraft.
* **Owner ID** (Optional): Please enter the ID of the owner.
* **Owner** (Optional): Please enter the name of the owner.
* **Type ID** (Optional): Please enter the type ID of the aircraft.
* **Status** (Required): Please select the status of the aircraft.
* **Remarks** (Optional): Please add any remarks about the rental.
* **Comments** (Optional): Please add any comments about the rental.
* **Signature** (Required): Please sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **User Signature** (Required): Please sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **User Name 2** (Optional): Please enter your name.
* **Date Signed** (Required): Please enter the date you signed the form.
* **User Email 2** (Optional): Please enter your email address.
* **User Phone 2** (Optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (Optional): Please add any notes about the rental.
