# NYC Apartment Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for renters to provide their personal and rental details to the property management team. The information collected will be used to evaluate and process the rental application.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to facilitate communication and updates.
3. Provide your **Phone** number to ensure easy contactability.
4. For **Rental Date**, select the date of your desired rental start date.
5. Choose your preferred **Rental Time** for a more comfortable rental period.
6. Select the **Rental Duration** in terms of months or years.
7. Choose your preferred **Move In Date** for a smooth transition.
8. Select a suitable **Move In Time** for an easy moving-in process.
9. Enter the **Rent Per Month** for your desired rent amount.
10. Select the **Rent Start Date** for your preferred rent start date.
11. Select the **Rent End Date** for your rent termination date.
12. Choose your preferred **Rent Start Time** for an optimal rent period.
13. Select the **Rent End Time** for your rent termination date.
14. For **Rent Frequency**, choose one or more of the available options to indicate your rent payment frequency.
15. Click **Submit** to complete and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for updates and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, date, required): Select the date you wish to start your rental.
* **Rental Time** (`rental_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred rental time for a comfortable period.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your rental in terms of months or years.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to move in.
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred move-in time.
* **Rent Per Month** (`rent_per_month`, number, required): Enter the desired rent amount per month.
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, date, required): Select the date you want to start renting.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, date, required): Select the date you want to end your rent.
* **Rent Start Time** (`rent_start_time`, time, required): Choose your preferred rent start time.
* **Rent End Time** (`rent_end_time`, time, required): Select your preferred rent end time.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more rent payment frequencies.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to complete and submit your application.
