# innovation_lab_rental_application_form--rental_application_form_templates - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Innovation Lab rental application process. It gathers essential details from applicants to facilitate a smooth and efficient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Review and proofread your submission for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you wish to rent the lab equipment.
* **Rental Duration** (`rental_duration`, number, optional): Specify the number of days you plan to rent the lab equipment for.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional): Describe the purpose and scope of your project.
* **Lab Name** (`lab_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lab you are applying to rent.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, optional): Specify the specific equipment you need to rent.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, optional): Describe the purpose of your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select one, optional): Select the status of your project.
* **Lab Contact Name** (`lab_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the lab contact person.
* **Lab Contact Email** (`lab_contact_email`, email, optional): Provide the email address of the lab contact person.
* **Lab Contact Phone** (`lab_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the lab contact person.
* **Lab Contact Address** (`lab_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the lab contact person.
* **User Comment** (`user_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **Lab Comment** (`lab_comment`, text, optional): Add any comments or remarks from the lab.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Specify the time you plan to use the lab.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your schedule.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select multiple, optional): Select the submitter(s) for your application.
* **Submitter 2** (`submitter_2`, select one, optional): Select the submitter 2 for your application.
* **Submitter 1** (`submitter_1`, text, optional): Enter the submitter 1 for your application.
