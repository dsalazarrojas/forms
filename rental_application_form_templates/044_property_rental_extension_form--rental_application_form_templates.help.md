# Property Rental Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Rental Extension Form is designed to capture information and decisions related to requesting an extension of lease terms for a rental property.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you fill out all relevant information accurately.
2. Complete all required fields before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details**: Please provide any relevant details about the property you wish to extend or modify.
* **Rent Extension**: Select the desired rent extension period from the options provided.
* **Reason for Extension**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the requested extension.
* **Tenant Signature**: Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Landlord Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your understanding of the extension terms.
* **Property Manager Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your support for the extension request.
* **Comments**: Include any additional comments or notes about the extension request.
* **Date Extension Requested**: Select the date when you would like the extension to take effect.
* **Date Extension Granted**: Select the date when the extension was granted or denied.
* **Extension Status**: Select the current status of the extension request (pending, granted, or denied).
* **Comments Extension Status**: Include any additional comments or notes about the extension status.
* **Property Details 2**: Please provide any additional property details that are relevant to the extension request.
## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to avoid delays or errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
