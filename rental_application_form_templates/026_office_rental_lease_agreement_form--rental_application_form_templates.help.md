# Office Rental Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Rental Lease Agreement Form is a document used to establish a formal agreement between a landlord and a tenant regarding the rental of a property. This form helps to clarify the terms of the lease, including rent details, lease duration, and other essential conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand each section and its purpose.
2. Fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Make sure to sign and date the form where indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Office Lease Terms** (1): This is a note section for any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
* **Rent Details** (2): Enter the details of the rent, if required.
* **Rent Amount** (3): Enter the amount of rent to be paid.
* **Lease Duration** (4): Select whether this lease is for a fixed term or open-ended. Please choose 'Yes' for a fixed term and 'No' for an open-ended lease.
* **Start Date** (5): Enter the date the lease is scheduled to begin.
* **End Date** (6): Enter the date the lease is scheduled to end.
* **Lease Terms** (7): This is a note section for any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
* **Tenant Sign Date** (8): Enter the date the tenant signs the agreement.
* **Landlord Sign Date** (9): Enter the date the landlord signs the agreement.
* **Lease Agreement Form Signed** (10): Select whether the agreement form has been signed or not. Please choose 'Yes' if signed and 'No' if not.

## Tips
- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records.
- Review the form carefully before signing it.
- Ensure that both parties agree to the terms before signing the form.
