## Purpose
The duplex rental application form is designed to collect essential information from applicants to assess their eligibility and suitability for renting a duplex.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Use a pen or keyboard to fill out the form, making sure to write clearly and legibly.
3. If you need help with any section, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info**: Please enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Applicant Info**: Provide your details, including your employment status.
* **Rental Preferences**: Describe your rental preferences, including the length and frequency of the rental.
* **Property Details**: Enter the property details, including the status of the property.
* **Income Proof**: Provide income proof, including the amount and frequency of your income.
* **Employment Verification**: Verify your employment status and length of employment.
* **Rental History**: Describe your rental history, including previous rentals and any issues you have had.
* **References**: Provide references from previous landlords or property managers.
* **Rental Agreement**: Review and sign the rental agreement, which outlines the terms of the rental.
* **Signature**: Sign the form, indicating your agreement to the rental terms.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the application.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting the application.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status (Part-time, Full-time, Freelance, Self-Employed, Retired, or Other).
* **Income Frequency**: Select how frequently you receive income (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Income Amount**: Enter your monthly income amount.
* **Employment Length**: Enter the length of your employment.
* **Rental Length**: Enter the length of the rental you are applying for.
* **Rental Frequency**: Select how frequently you rent (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Rental Status**: Select the rental status you are applying for (Leasing, Renting, Occupied, Vacant, Other).
* **Property Status**: Select the status of the property you are applying to rent (Leased, Owned, Vacant, For Sale, Other).
* **Lease Terms**: Select the lease terms you are applying for (Month-to-Month, 6-Month, 12-Month).
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign the form, indicating your agreement to the rental terms.

Please ensure you have all necessary documents and information before submitting the form.
