# Water Sports Equipment Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Sports Equipment Rental Application Form is designed to help us understand your requirements for renting water sports equipment. This form will help us process your request and provide you with the necessary information for a successful rental experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information in the **User Information** section.
2.  Select the type of water sports equipment you wish to rent in the **Equipment Requested** section.
3.  Choose the rental period you are interested in from the available options.
4.  If applicable, enter the start and end dates of your rental period.
5.  Indicate if the equipment is available or not.
6.  Add any additional comments or notes in the **Notes** section.
7.  Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): This section is used to collect your contact information. Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your rental request.
* **Equipment Requested** (Equipment Requested, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of water sports equipment you are interested in renting. You can select multiple options if you want to rent multiple types of equipment.
*   **Rental Period** (Rental Period, date, optional): Choose a date for the rental period you are interested in.
*   **Start Date** (Start Date, date, optional): If you have selected a specific rental period, this field will be used to select the start date of your rental.
*   **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): If you have selected a specific rental period, this field will be used to select the end date of your rental.
*   **Equipment Availability** (Equipment Availability, select one, optional): Indicate if the equipment you have selected is available for rental or not.
*   **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you may want to provide about your rental request.
*   **Contact Info** (Contact Info, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your rental request.
*   **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your rental request.
