# Mobile Home Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for applicants to provide their information for the rental application process of a mobile home. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your application is complete.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section of the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are filled out completely.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
3. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review your application for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (Page 1)**: This section is for you to provide basic information about yourself. 
* **Personal Details (Page 2)**: Here, you can provide any additional details about yourself that are relevant to the rental application.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)**: This section is required and asks for your contact information, such as your email and phone number. Please ensure you have provided accurate and up-to-date contact details so that we can contact you efficiently.
* **Rental Details (Page 4)**: In this section, you can provide information about the mobile home you are interested in renting, such as its address and desired start date.
* **Additional Information (Page 5)**: This final section is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about your application.
