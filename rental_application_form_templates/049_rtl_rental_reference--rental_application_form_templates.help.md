# RTL Rental Reference - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RTL Rental Reference form is a data collection tool used to gather demographic information from renters. This information will be used for reference purposes only and will not be used to verify creditworthiness or make any decisions regarding the rental application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your Date Of Birth using the date picker or enter it manually in the "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your primary contact phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately to ensure the rental application process is smooth.
* If you have any issues with completing this form, please contact the rental office.
