# Container Home Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Container Home Rental Application Form is designed for potential renters to apply for container home rentals. To ensure a smooth and efficient application process, please carefully review the form below and fill it out accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling out the form with all required information. Each field is marked as "Required" in the form.
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your legal first and last name as they appear on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, `text`, Required): Enter your Social Security Number for credit and background checks.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, Required): Enter your primary email address for communication with us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Required): Enter your best contact number for us to reach you.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, Required): Enter the street address where you currently live.
* **Current City** (`current_city`, `text`, Required): Enter the city where you currently live.
* **Current State** (`current_state`, `text`, Required): Enter the state where you currently live.
* **Current ZIP Code** (`current_zip`, `text`, Required): Enter the ZIP code of your current address.
* **Current Monthly Rent** (`current_rent_amount`, `number`, Required): Enter the amount of your current monthly rent payment.
* **Current Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of your current landlord or property manager.
* **Landlord Phone Number** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, Required): Enter the contact number for your current landlord.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, Required): Select your current employment situation.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, Optional): If you're employed, enter the name of your current employer.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, Optional): If you're employed, enter your job title.
* **Gross Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, Required): Enter your total monthly income before taxes.
* **Length of Employment** (`length_of_employment`, `select_one`, Required): Select how long you've worked at your current job.
* **Number of Occupants** (`number_of_occupants`, `number`, Required): Enter the number of people who will live in the container home.
* **Do You Have Pets?** (`pets`, `select_one`, Required): Select whether you have any pets.
* **Pet Details** (`pet_details`, `text`, Optional): If you have pets, describe them in detail.
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, `select_one`, Required): Select your smoking status.
* **Desired Move-In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date you would like to move in.
* **Preferred Lease Term** (`lease_term_preference`, `select_one`, Required): Select the length of the lease you're seeking.
* **Have You Ever Been Evicted?** (`previous_evictions`, `select_one`, Required): Select whether you've ever been evicted.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, Optional): If you have any other information you'd like to share, use this field.
