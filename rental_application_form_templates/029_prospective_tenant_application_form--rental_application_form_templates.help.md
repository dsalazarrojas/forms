# Prospective Tenant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from prospective tenants who are interested in renting a property. It serves as a starting point for evaluation and verification by the landlord or property manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review each field explanation for specific guidance on what information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your general information, including any additional comments or details you would like to share with the landlord or property manager.

### Contact Information (2)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.

### Rental History (3)
* **Rental History** (`rental_history`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your previous rental history, including the addresses and dates of your previous rentals.

### Employment Information (4)
* **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your employment status, including your job title, employer, and income.

### Income Information (5)
* **Income Information** (`income_info`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your income, including your annual income and source of income.

### Employment Verification (6)
* **Employment Verification** (`employment_verification`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information to verify your employment status, including your employer's name and contact information.

### Rental Verification (7)
* **Rental Verification** (`rental_verification`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information to verify your rental history, including the addresses and dates of your previous rentals.

### References (8)
* **References** (`references`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your references, including their names and contact information.

### Rental Preferences (9)
* **Rental Preferences** (`rental_preferences`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your rental preferences, including your desired move-in date and move-out date.

### Move In Date (10)
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to collect your desired move-in date.

### Move Out Date (11)
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to collect your desired move-out date.

### Move In Time (12)
* **Move In Time** (`move_in_time`, time, required/optional): This field is used to collect your desired move-in time.

### Move In Date Range (13)
* **Move In Date Range** (`move_in_date_range`, select multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select a range of dates for your desired move-in date.

### Move Out Date Range (14)
* **Move Out Date Range** (`move_out_date_range`, select multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select a range of dates for your desired move-out date.

### Phone (15)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your phone number.

### Email (16)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect your email address.

### Address (17)
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your address.

### City (18)
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your city.

### State (19)
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your state.

### Zip Code (20)
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your zip code.

### Country (21)
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect your country.

### Rental Agreement (22)
* **Rental Agreement** (`rental_agreement`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your rental agreement.

### Rent (23)
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect your expected rent amount.

### Security Deposit (24)
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, required/optional): This field is used to collect your expected security deposit amount.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Incomplete or inaccurate information may result in delays or even rejection of the application.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the landlord or property manager.
