# Rental Application With Background Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal, address, background, and rental preference information from applicants. It also includes fields for a signature and terms and conditions acknowledgment, as well as an option to upload a file for supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your personal information on page 1 (Personal Information).
2. Move on to page 2 to enter your address information.
3. On page 3, please provide any background information that you would like to share with us.
4. If applicable, enter the emergency contact person's name and relationship to you on page 4.
5. On page 5, specify your rental preferences.
6. Sign and date the declaration on page 6.
7. If necessary, upload any supporting files on page 7.
8. Finally, review and agree to our terms and conditions on page 8, then select the "Submit" option to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name, email, and phone number on this page.
* **Address Information** (`Address Information`, `text`, required): Enter your current address and any other relevant details about your address.
* **Background Information** (`Background Information`, `text`, required): Share any additional background information that you feel would be helpful to us.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): If you have someone we should contact in case of an emergency, enter their name and relationship to you.
* **Rental Preferences** (`Rental Preferences`, `text`, required): Clearly state your preferred rental options and any special requirements.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date this page to confirm your identity and agreement to our terms.
* **Upload File** (`Upload File`, `text`, optional): If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `text`, required): Carefully review and agree to our terms and conditions before submitting your application.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options: "Submit Form", "Save for Later", or "Cancel".
