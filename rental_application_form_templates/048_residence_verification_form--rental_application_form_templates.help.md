# Residence Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Residence Verification Form is designed to collect information about a tenant's stay in a residence. This form is used to verify the tenant's details and ensure accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and correct details for each field.
3. If you are unsure about any field, you can refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who is the tenant.
* **Tenant ID Number** (`tenant_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the tenant's identification number or ID.
* **Date Moved In** (`date_moved_in`, date, required): Enter the date the tenant moved into the residence.
* **Current Rent** (`current_rent`, text, optional): Enter the current rent amount, if applicable.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Rent Due Date** (`rent_due_date`, date, optional): Enter the date rent is due or was due.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease agreement, if applicable.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease agreement, if applicable.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date the tenant moved into the residence.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, optional): Enter the current address of the residence.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the tenant moved out of the residence.

## Tips
