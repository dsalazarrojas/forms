# historic_property_rental_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential renters for historic properties. It's suitable for various types of rentals, including apartments, houses, and commercial properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user information in the "User Information" field (Page 1).
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Information" field (Page 2).
3. Enter the rental specifics in the "Rental Details" field (Page 3).
4. Add any relevant review notes in the "Review Notes" field (Page 4).
5. Read and agree to the rental terms, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any relevant identification information.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Rental Details (Page 3)** (`rental_details`, text, optional): Enter the property address, desired move-in date, and rental period.
* **Rental Agreement (Page 4)** (`rental_agreement`, text, optional): Review and agree to the rental terms and conditions.
* **Review Notes (Page 5)** (`review_notes`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about the property or rental.
* **Submit (Page 6)** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click to submit your application.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely. You can save your progress and come back later if you need more time. If you have any issues, please contact the property manager or owner.
