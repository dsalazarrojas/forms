# Rental Reference Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a rental reference from a referee who can vouch for your rental history and creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide the name of the person you would like to be contacted for reference in the "Contact" field.
6. Select your relationship with the person you would like to be contacted (e.g. "Parent", "Spouse", "Friend", etc.) in the "Relationship" field.
7. Enter the title of the person you would like to be contacted (e.g. "Mr.", "Mrs.", etc.) in the "Title" field.
8. Enter the phone number of the person you would like to be contacted in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter the email address of the person you would like to be contacted in the "Email" field.
10. Enter the number of years you have known the person you would like to be contacted in the "Years Known" field.
11. Enter any additional information about your relationship with the person you would like to be contacted in the "Reference Notes" field.
12. If you have a signature from the person you would like to be contacted, select the corresponding option in the "Signature" field.
13. Enter the date of the reference check in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email**: Your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Contact**: The name of the person you would like to be contacted for reference.
* **Relationship**: Select your relationship with the person you would like to be contacted (e.g. "Parent", "Spouse", "Friend", etc.).
* **Title**: The title of the person you would like to be contacted (e.g. "Mr.", "Mrs.", etc.).
* **Phone**: The phone number of the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Email**: The email address of the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Years Known**: The number of years you have known the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Reference Notes**: Any additional information about your relationship with the person you would like to be contacted.
* **Signature**: Select the option indicating whether a signature from the person you would like to be contacted has been provided.
* **Date**: The date of the reference check.
