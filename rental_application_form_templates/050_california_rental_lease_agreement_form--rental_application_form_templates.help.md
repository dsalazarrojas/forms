# California Rental Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The California Rental Lease Agreement Form is a comprehensive document that outlines the terms and conditions of a rental property in California. This form is designed to facilitate the rental process, ensuring that both landlords and tenants understand their responsibilities and rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately:

1.  Ensure all required fields (marked as "required" in bold) are filled in completely and correctly.
2.  Review each field description carefully to understand what information is required.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the property manager or landlord for clarification.
4.  Ensure that all fields are filled in legibly and correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (`1`, `text`, **required**): Enter the full legal name or company name of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Address** (`2`, `text`, **required**): Enter the mailing address of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Email** (`3`, `email`, **required**): Enter the contact email of the landlord or property owner.
* **Landlord Phone** (`4`, `text`, **required**): Enter the contact number of the landlord or property owner.
* **Tenant Name** (`5`, `text`, **required**): Enter the full legal name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email** (`6`, `email`, **required**): Enter the contact email of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone** (`7`, `text`, **required**): Enter the contact number of the tenant.
* **Property Address** (`8`, `text`, **required**): Enter the full address of the rental property.
* **City** (`9`, `text`, **required**): Enter the city of the rental property.
* **State** (`10`, `text`, **required**): Enter the state of the rental property (California).
* **ZIP Code** (`11`, `text`, **required**): Enter the ZIP code of the rental property.
* **Property Type** (`12`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of rental property from the given options.
* **Lease Start Date** (`13`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when the lease begins.
* **Lease End Date** (`14`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date when the lease ends.
* **Lease Type** (`15`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the type of lease agreement from the given options.
* **Monthly Rent Amount** (`16`, `number`, **required**): Enter the rent amount in dollars.
* **Rent Due Date** (`17`, `number`, **required**): Enter the date rent is due.
* **Security Deposit** (`18`, `number`, **required**): Enter the security deposit amount in dollars.
* **Accepted Payment Methods** (`19`, `select_multiple`, not **required**): Select the methods by which tenant can pay rent.
* **Late Fee** (`20`, `number`, not **required**): Enter the late fee amount.
* **Grace Period** (`21`, `number`, not **required**): Enter the number of days before late fee applies.
* **Number of Occupants** (`22`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of people living in the unit.
* **Occupant Names** (`23`, `text`, not **required**): Enter the names of all occupants.
* **Pets Allowed** (`24`, `select_one`, **required**): Select if pets are permitted.
* **Pet Deposit** (`25`, `number`, not **required**): Enter the additional pet deposit if applicable.
* **Parking Included** (`26`, `select_one`, not **required**): Select if parking is included.
* **Utilities Included** (`27`, `select_multiple`, not **required**): Select which utilities are included.
* **Smoking Policy** (`28`, `select_one`, **required**): Select if smoking is permitted.
* **Lease Terms Acknowledgment** (`29`, `select_one`, **required**): Select if you agree to all lease terms.
* **Landlord Signature** (`30`, `text`, **required**): Enter the digital signature or typed name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Signature Date** (`31`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Tenant Signature** (`32`, `text`, **required**): Enter the digital signature or typed name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Signature Date** (`33`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Additional Terms** (`34`, `text`, not **required**): Enter any special conditions or addendums.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the property manager or landlord.
* Ensure all fields are filled in legibly and correctly.
* This form must be signed by both the landlord and tenant.
