# Swimming Pool Rental Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Swimming Pool Rental Form is a form designed to collect essential information from customers for swimming pool rental purposes. This form is used to gather contact details, booking information, and preferences for a smooth and enjoyable rental experience. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your contact information on the "Customer Information" page.
2. Fill in your name, email, and phone number accurately.
3. Proceed to the "Booking Information" page.
4. Select your preferred check-in and check-out dates.
5. Choose the type of rental you're interested in (Day, Week, or Month).
6. Finally, review and agree to the terms and conditions to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (Page 1)**: 
    - **Name** (contact_info_1): Enter your name accurately. This field is required.
    - **Email** (contact_info_2): Enter your valid email address. This field is required.
    - **Phone** (contact_info_3): Provide your phone number for contact purposes. (optional)

* **Booking Information (Page 2)**:
    - **Check In Date** (contact_booking_1): Select the date you would like to check in.
    - **Check Out Date** (contact_booking_2): Select the date you would like to check out.
    - **Booking Type** (contact_booking_3): Choose between Day, Week, or Month rental options. (optional)

* **Terms And Conditions (Page 3)**: 
    - **Terms And Conditions** (contact_terms): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental. This field is provided for your review. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure a smooth rental process.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
