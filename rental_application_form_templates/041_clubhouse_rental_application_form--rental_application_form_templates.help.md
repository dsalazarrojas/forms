# Clubhouse Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clubhouse Rental Application Form is designed to collect information for a clubhouse rental application. It is used by individuals or groups planning events or gatherings at the clubhouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **Applicant Full Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
4.  Select your residency status in the **Residency Status** field. Choose between "Yes, HOA Resident", "No, Non-Resident", or "Guest of Resident".
5.  Enter your unit or property address in the **Unit or Property Address** field.
6.  Enter the name of your event or gathering in the **Event Name** field.
7.  Enter the date of your event in the **Event Date** field. Use the date format "MM/DD/YYYY".
8.  Enter the time you will arrive to set up in the **Setup Time** field. Use the time format "HH:MM AM/PM".
9.  Enter the start time of your event in the **Event Start Time** field. Use the time format "HH:MM AM/PM".
10. Enter the end time of your event in the **Event End Time** field. Use the time format "HH:MM AM/PM".
11.  Enter the estimated time of teardown in the **Estimated Teardown Completion** field. Use the time format "HH:MM AM/PM".
12. Enter the number of guests expected in the **Expected Number of Guests** field.
13.  Select the type of event in the **Type of Event** field. Choose between "Birthday Party", "Anniversary", "Wedding", "Family Gathering", "Community Event", "Business Event", "Other".
14.  Select all the amenities needed in the **Amenities Needed** field. Choose between "Tables", "Chairs", "Kitchen Access", "Bar Service", "Audio System", "Projector", "Parking Availability".
15.  Select whether you will bring food or cater in the **Catering or Food Plans** field. Choose between "Professional Catering", "Potluck Style", "Bring Your Own", "No Food Service".
16.  Select whether alcohol will be served in the **Will Alcohol Be Served** field. Choose between "True" and "False".
17.  Select whether you have liability insurance in the **Have Liability Insurance** field. Choose between "Yes, Certificate Attached", "Yes, Can Obtain", "False".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your personal or business email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Residency Status**: Indicate your residency status for access to the clubhouse facilities.
* **Unit or Property Address**: Enter your unit or property address associated with the clubhouse.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of your event or gathering.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of your event in "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **Setup Time**: Enter the time you will arrive to set up in "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of your event in "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of your event in "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Estimated Teardown Completion**: Enter the estimated time of teardown in "HH:MM AM/PM" format.
* **Expected Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests expected for your event.
* **Type of Event**: Select the type of event you are hosting.
* **Amenities Needed**: Select all the amenities required for your event.
* **Catering or Food Plans**: Choose whether you will bring food or cater for your event.
* **Will Alcohol Be Served**: Indicate whether alcohol will be served.
* **Have Liability Insurance**: Indicate whether you have liability insurance.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* Duplicate applications should be avoided.
* Complete applications will be processed promptly.
