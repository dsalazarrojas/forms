# Wagon Rental Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users to facilitate the rental process of waggons from the company. The form is used to collect contact details, rental preferences, and any special requests the user may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Information** on page 1 of the form. This includes any relevant details about you as a user.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** on page 2 of the form, including any phone number and email address.
3. On page 3, choose one or more **Vehicle Requests** that you are interested in renting. Please select from the available options: Wagon, Tractor, or U-Haul.
4. On page 4, select your desired **Rent Period**, which can be Hour, Half-Day, Day, or Month.
5. On page 5, choose your preferred **Rental Location**, which can be On-site, Off-site, or Delivery.
6. On page 6, provide your **Contact Name**.
7. Enter your **Phone Number** on page 7.
8. Enter your **Email Address** on page 8.
9. If you have any **Special Requests**, please enter them on page 9.
10. Sign the **Signature** page 10 to confirm your information is correct.
11. Finally, on page 11, enter the **Date Signed** and **Time Signed** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**:
  • A text field to provide any relevant information about you as a user.
* **Contact Info**:
  • Enter your contact details to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Vehicle Requests**:
  • Select one or more vehicles you would like to rent from the available options.
* **Rent Period**:
  • Select your desired rental duration: Hour, Half-Day, Day, or Month.
* **Rental Location**:
  • Choose where you would like to rent the vehicle, On-site, Off-site, or Delivery.
* **Contact Name**:
  • Enter your name to be associated with your rental.
* **Phone Number**:
  • Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Email Address**:
  • Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Special Requests**:
  • Enter any special requests you may have for the rental process.
* **Signature**:
  • Sign this field to confirm your information is correct and complete.
* **Date Signed**:
  • Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**:
  • Enter the time you signed the form.
