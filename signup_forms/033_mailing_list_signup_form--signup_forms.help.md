# Mailing List Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals who wish to be added to a mailing list or newsletter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and choose any optional fields you'd like to provide. Here are the required fields:

1. **Name**: Enter your name.
2. **Email**: Enter your email address. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Address**: Enter your address to help us contact you.
4. **Date**: Choose a date for your signup (e.g., when you'd like to be added to the mailing list).
5. **Address Format**: Select the format of your address (e.g., Street, PO Box, etc.).

For each field, make sure to fill it out accurately and completely. If you're unsure about any of the optional fields, you can skip them or leave them blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`required_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
- **Email** (`required_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
- **Phone** (`optional_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, enter it here.
- **Inquiries** (`optional_inquiry`, note, optional): This field is not clearly defined, but it seems to be for any additional comments or questions you have.
- **Date** (`required_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your signup.
- **Time** (`optional_time`, time, optional): If you'd like to provide a time, select a time zone and enter a time (e.g., 10:00 AM).
- **Language** (`optional_language`, text, optional): Select your native language to help us tailor our communication.
- **Time Zone** (`optional_time_zone`, text, optional): Select your time zone to ensure accurate scheduling.
- **Email Format** (`optional_email_format`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined, but it seems to be for email format (e.g., HTML, plain text, etc.). If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
- **Phone Format** (`optional_phone_format`, text, optional): This field is not clearly defined, but it seems to be for phone format (e.g., E. 123-456-7890, etc.). If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
- **Address Format** (`required_address_format`, text, required): Select the format of your address (e.g., Street, PO Box, etc.).

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any of the optional fields, you can skip or leave them blank.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
