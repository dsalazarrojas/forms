# Submit Your Music - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Submit Your Music" form is designed to collect your information and details about the music you are submitting. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your submission is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal contact information in the First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number fields.
2. Enter the title, description, and URL of the music you are submitting.
3. Upload or enter the URL of the music file.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address in this format: `yourname@example.com`.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Music Title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title for the music you are submitting.
- **Music Description** (text, optional): Describe the music you are submitting in a few sentences.
- **Music URL** (text, optional): If you have a website or platform where the music can be found, enter the URL.
- **Music File** (text, optional): Upload a file containing the music, or enter the URL of a file hosting platform.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name, Last Name, and Email).
- If you have a file to upload, ensure it is in a supported format.
- If you are submitting music to a specific platform or website, enter the correct URL in the "Music URL" field.
