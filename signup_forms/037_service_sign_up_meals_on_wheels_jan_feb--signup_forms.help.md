# Service Sign Up: Meals On Wheels (JAN/FEB) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up services for Meals On Wheels program during the months of January and February. It helps the program administrators to track and manage services offered by various providers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct category for your service from the dropdown list.
2. Provide a brief description of your service.
3. Enter your name and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Add your email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct category for your service from the dropdown list. This is crucial as it will help the program administrators to categorize your service accurately.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your service that will help the program administrators to understand what you are offering.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to help the program administrators contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Add your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose the correct category for your service to avoid any confusion.
* Provide a clear and concise description of your service.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information for contact purposes.
