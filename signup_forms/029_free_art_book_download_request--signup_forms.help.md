# free-art-book-download-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting a free art book download. Fill in your details to download a free art book for personal or educational use. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the second field.
3. Confirm that you consent to download the art book.
4. Enter the title of the art book you are interested in downloading.
5. Enter the author of the art book you are interested in downloading.
6. Enter the publication date of the art book you are interested in downloading (in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`).
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`form1name`, text, required): Enter your name to receive the art book.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive the download link.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to download the art book.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter a brief message if you have any specific requests or questions.
* **Artbook Title** (`artbook_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the art book you are interested in downloading.
* **Artbook Author** (`artbook_author`, text, required): Enter the author of the art book you are interested in downloading.
* **Artbook Pub Date** (`artbook_pub_date`, date, required): Enter the publication date of the art book in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
