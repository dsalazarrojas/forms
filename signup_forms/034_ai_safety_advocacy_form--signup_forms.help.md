# AI Safety Advocacy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Safety Advocacy Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to volunteer their time and resources to support the AI Safety Advocacy Group. This form helps the group understand who is interested in advocating for AI safety and how they can contribute.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill in your **Full name** in the "Full name" field. This is your first and last name that you want to be referred to by.
2.  Enter your **Email address** in the "Email address" field. This will be used for communications and updates from the group.
3.  Select your **Availability to volunteer** in the "Availability to volunteer" dropdown. Choose how often you can volunteer your time with the group.
4.  Select all the **Areas of expertise** you have in the "Areas of expertise" field. Choose one or more of the options that best describe your skills and experience.
5.  If you are available, choose your **Advocacy interests** in the "Advocacy interests" field. Write a brief description of what you are interested in or what you would like to focus on.
6.  Select whether you **Consent to share contact details** with partners in the "Consent to share contact details with partners" field. Choose whether you are comfortable sharing your contact information with other organizations or partners.
7.  If you'd like to mention how you **heard about this advocacy group**, feel free to provide that information in the "How did you hear about this advocacy group" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
*  **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached.
*  **City and Country** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the city and country where you are located.
*  **Areas of expertise** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply - Policy and governance, Technical AI safety, Community organizing, Education and outreach, Research.
*  **Availability to volunteer** (`availability_to_volunteer`, select_one, required): Select one - Weekly, Monthly, Occasional events, Not available.
*  **Advocacy interests** (`advocacy_interests`, text, optional): If you are available, write a brief description of what you are interested in or what you would like to focus on.
*  **Consent to share contact details with partners** (`consent_to_share_details`, select_one, required): Select one - True, False.
*  **How did you hear about this advocacy group** (`referral_source`, text, optional): If you'd like to mention how you heard about this advocacy group, feel free to provide that information.
