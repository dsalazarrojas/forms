# Vaping Cessation Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in quitting vaping or seeking support for their vaping cessation journey. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your experiences, challenges, and progress in quitting vaping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information (Page 1) - This section is where you provide basic details about yourself, such as your name, email, and contact number.
2. Share your Smokey Details (Page 2) - This section is where you provide information about your vaping habits, including the type of products you use, how often you vape, and any challenges you are facing.
3. Provide your Contact Information (Page 3) - This section is where you provide your contact information, such as your email address, phone number, and address.
4. Share your Smoking Habits (Page 4) - This section is where you provide information about your smoking habits, including when you started smoking, how often you smoke, and any challenges you are facing.
5. Describe your Cessation Support (Page 5) - This section is where you provide information about your plans for quitting vaping, including any support systems you have in place, such as counseling or support groups.
6. Provide Participant Information (Page 6) - This section is where you provide information about your participation in any related studies or research projects.
7. Disclose any Medical Conditions (Page 7) - This section is where you provide information about any medical conditions that may be relevant to your vaping habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section asks for your basic details, such as name, email, and contact number. Please fill in the information accurately and completely.
* **Smokey Details** (`smoking_info`, text, optional): This section is where you provide information about your vaping habits, including the type of products you use, how often you vape, and any challenges you are facing. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is where you provide your contact information, such as email address, phone number, and address. Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
* **Smoking Habits** (`smoking_habits`, text, optional): This section is where you provide information about your smoking habits, including when you started smoking, how often you smoke, and any challenges you are facing. Please be honest and detailed in your responses.
* **Cessation Support** (`cessation_support`, text, optional): This section is where you provide information about your plans for quitting vaping, including any support systems you have in place, such as counseling or support groups. Please be specific about your goals and any challenges you are facing.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This section is where you provide information about any related studies or research projects you are participating in. Please note that this is an optional section and only if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): This section is where you provide information about any medical conditions that may be relevant to your vaping habits. Please be honest and detailed in your responses.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
* Please note that your responses will be kept confidential and used solely for research purposes.
* If you have already filled out a similar form with us, please do not proceed with this form.
