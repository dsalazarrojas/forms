# signup_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up a new user. Please fill out the form with the required and optional information to create a new account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `single_group_form` field with your group name.
2. Choose the size of your group in the `group_size` field. Please select a number to indicate the size of your group.
3. Select the type of group you are creating in the `group_type` field. This could be either "Group" or "Single".
4. Enter your name in the `name` field.
5. Provide your email address in the `email` field. Please note that this is a required field for verification purposes.
6. Fill out your phone number in the `phone` field, if you wish to provide this information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **single_group_form** (`single_group_form`, text, required/optional): This is the name of your group. Please fill out this field with a descriptive name of your group.
- **group_size** (`group_size`, number, required/optional): This field is used to indicate the size of your group. Please select a number to represent the number of people in your group.
- **group_type** (`group_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select the type of group you are creating. Please choose between "Group" or "Single" to indicate the type of group you are forming.
- **name** (`name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to input your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **email** (`email`, email, required): This field is required for verification purposes. Please enter a valid email address that you use to receive communications.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used to input your phone number. Please note that this field is not required, but providing your phone number can enhance communication with our team.
