# icssc_rcf_student - Help Guide
## Purpose
The icssc_rcf_student form is used to collect registration information from students for the icssc_rcf_student program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your registration information in the "registration_form" field.
2. Fill in your student information in the "student_info" field.
3. Enter your contact details in the "contact_details" field.
4. Select your course(s) of interest from the "course_selection" dropdown list.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Enter your date of interest in the "date" field.
8. Select your preferred time slot in the "time" field.
9. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **registration_form** (id: 1, text, optional): This field is a general registration form where you can enter any information you'd like to be considered for the icssc_rcf_student program.
* **student_info** (id: 2, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself as a student.
* **contact_details** (id: 3, text, optional): Enter your contact details such as address, phone number, or other relevant contact information.
* **course_selection** (id: 4, number, required): Select the course(s) you are interested in. This is a required field.
* **email** (id: 5, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **phone** (id: 6, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **date** (id: 7, date, optional): Enter the date you are interested in.
* **time** (id: 8, time, optional): Select your preferred time slot.
* **notes** (id: 9, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
