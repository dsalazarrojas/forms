# Winery Customer Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers visiting a winery for marketing, sales, and customer relationship management (CRM) purposes. It is designed to be completed by customers who are interested in learning more about the winery's products, events, and promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field is not required, but it will help us to contact you with updates and promotions.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your wine preferences from the "Wine Preference" field. You can select multiple options.
6. Select your preferred wine type from the "Preferred Wine Type" field.
7. Choose the wine region that you are interested in from the "Preferred Wine Region" field.
8. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. Enter the time of your visit in the "Time of Visit" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
11. Select the assigned agent who will be handling your case from the "Assigned Agent" field.
12. Enter the address of the winery in the "Winery Address" field.
13. Add any additional notes or comments about the winery in the "Winery Notes" field.
14. Select your customer type from the "Customer Type" field.
15. Enter the number of guests attending from the "Number of Guests" field.
16. Enter the number of children attending from the "Number of Children" field.
17. Select whether you are a wine member or not from the "Is Wine Member" field.
18. Select the type of membership from the "Membership Type" field.
19. Enter the start date of your membership from the "Membership Start Date" field.
20. Enter the end date of your membership from the "Membership End Date" field.
21. Select whether you are the responsible drinker from the "Is Responsible Drinker" field.
22. Select whether you are the responsible parent from the "Is Responsible Parent" field.
23. Select whether you are the responsible guardian from the "Is Responsible Guardian" field.
24. Select whether you are the responsible guardian 2 from the "Is Responsible Guardian 2" field.
25. Select whether you are the responsible guardian 3 from the "Is Responsible Guardian 3" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates and promotions from us.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Wine Preference** (5, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of wine you are interested in learning more about.
* **Preferred Wine Type** (6, select_one, optional): Select your preferred type of wine.
* **Preferred Wine Region** (7, select_one, optional): Select the wine region that interests you.
* **Date of Birth** (8, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Visit** (9, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Notes** (10, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Agent** (11, select_one, optional): Select the assigned agent who will be handling your case.
* **Winery Address** (12, text, optional): Enter the address of the winery.
* **Winery Notes** (13, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the winery.
* **Customer Type** (14, select_one, optional): Select your customer type.
* **Number of Guests** (15, number, optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
* **Number of Children** (16, number, optional): Enter the number of children attending.
* **Is Wine Member** (17, select_one, optional): Select whether you are a wine member or not.
* **Membership Type** (18, select_one, optional): Select the type of membership you have.
* **Membership Start Date** (19, date, optional): Enter the start date of your membership.
* **Membership End Date** (20, date, optional): Enter the end date of your membership.
* **Is Responsible Drinker** (21, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the responsible drinker.
* **Is Responsible Parent** (22, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the responsible parent.
* **Is Responsible Guardian** (23, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the responsible guardian.
* **Is Responsible Guardian 2** (24, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the responsible guardian 2.
* **Is Responsible Guardian 3** (25, select_one, optional): Select whether you are the responsible guardian 3.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* You can select multiple options in the "Wine Preference" field.
* You can add multiple lines of comments in the "Notes" field.
* You can select only one option in the "Preferred Wine Region" and "Preferred Wine Type" fields.
* You can enter a date in the "Date of Birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* You can enter a time in the "Time of Visit" field in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* You can enter a number of guests or children as a numerical value.
* You can select only one option in the "Customer Type" field.
* You can select only one option in the "Membership Type" field.
