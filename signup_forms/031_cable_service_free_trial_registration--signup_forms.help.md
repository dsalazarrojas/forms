# Cable Service Free Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to sign up for a free trial of the cable service. Please fill out the form with the required information to ensure your free trial is activated correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out your **Full name** in the "Full name" field. This will be used to identify you as a customer.
- Enter your **Email address** in the "Email address" field. This is the email address where the trial activation link will be sent.
- Provide your **Service address** in the "Service address" field. This is where the trial service will be provided.
- (Optional) Select a **Preferred start date** for your trial in the "Preferred start date" field. If you do not select a date, the trial will be activated immediately.
- (Optional) Select if any **Equipment required** for the trial. Choose one or more options from "Modem", "Router", or "Professional installation".
- Select if you **Accept the trial terms** in the "Acceptance of trial terms" field. Please note that agreeing to the terms is a required field.
- (Optional) Enter any **Referral code** you may have in the "Referral code" field.
- Enter any **Comments or questions** you may have in the "Comments or questions" field. This is a field for any additional information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address where you want to receive the trial activation link.
* **Service address**: Enter the physical address where the trial service will be provided.
* **Preferred start date**: Choose the date you would like the trial to begin. If no date is selected, the trial will begin immediately.
* **Equipment required**: Select one or more of the options for any equipment you may need for the trial (Modem, Router, or Professional installation).
* **Acceptance of trial terms**: Please select "I agree" to indicate that you agree to the trial terms and conditions.
* **Referral code**: If you have a referral code, enter it here. This is optional.
* **Comments or questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the trial.
