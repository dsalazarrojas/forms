# Mailing List Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mailing List Registration Form is a simple form designed to collect user information for marketing or promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you with updates and newsletters.
3. Select one of the three options in the "Join Mailing List" field to choose which mailing list you would like to join.
4. If you have any comments or additional information you'd like to share, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
5. If you have any specific interests you'd like to declare, you can enter them in the "Interests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`user_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home or business address. Please include your city, state, and zip code for accurate delivery of our emails and packages.
* **Email** (`user_email`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with updates and newsletters.
* **Phone Number** (`user_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any urgent matters.
* **Join Mailing List** (`join_mailing_list`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the three options to join our mailing list. Please note that you can only join one list.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any comments or feedback, you can enter them in this field.
* **Interests** (`user_interests`, text, required/optional): If you have any interests or hobbies you'd like to declare, you can enter them in this field.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure that we can contact you properly.
* If you're having trouble completing the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* You can modify or withdraw your subscription at any time by contacting us.
