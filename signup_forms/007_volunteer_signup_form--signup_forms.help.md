# volunteer_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer_signup_form is a registration form designed for users to sign up as volunteers for a project or task.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in all required fields.
- Enter a valid project name for projects you are volunteering for.
- Choose your role from Admin or Volunteer.
- Select valid start and end dates for your volunteering period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (text, project_name, optional): Enter the name of the project you are volunteering for.
* **Task Name** (text, task_name, optional): Enter the name of the task you are volunteering for.
* **Participant Name** (text, participant_name, optional): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Volunteer Name** (text, volunteer_name, optional): Enter your name as the volunteer.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication.
* **Phone** (text, phone, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (select_one, role, required): Choose your role as Admin or Volunteer.
* **Start Date** (date, start_date, optional): Select the start date of your volunteering period.
* **End Date** (date, end_date, optional): Select the end date of your volunteering period.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Double-check your project and task details to ensure correct alignment with project requirements.
- Be clear about your role and dates to facilitate smooth communication and planning.
