## Daily Inspirational Quote Signup - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Inspirational Quote Signup form is designed to collect user information for motivational and inspirational purposes. This form helps to sign up users for daily inspirational quotes and messages. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the First Name field with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Fill in the Last Name field with your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure it's a valid email address where you can receive daily inspirational quotes and messages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your official documents. This is a text field where you can enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your official documents. This is a text field where you can enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Please enter a valid Email address where you can receive daily inspirational quotes and messages. This will be used as the contact address where we'll send motivational content.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter a valid Email address as this is required to receive the daily inspirational quotes and messages.
* If you experience any difficulties filling out the form or need further assistance, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
