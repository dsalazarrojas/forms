# Santa Claus Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information and additional comments from users who want to interact with Santa Claus. It's designed to help manage contacts and gather feedback for Santa's gift-giving operations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the first field to identify yourself.
2. Provide your **Email** address so Santa can get in touch with you.
3. Fill in your **Phone** number if you want Santa to be able to contact you directly.
4. Select a **Category** to sort your form response (if applicable).
5. Type any **Additional Comments** you have, such as a message or a note.
6. Select who the **Assigned To** field is directed at (e.g., Santa's elves, a specific department, or another team member).
7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 for the additional **Comment** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name to be identified by Santa.
* **Email**: Your email address for Santa to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want Santa to be able to contact you directly.
* **Category**: Choose a category to sort your form response (e.g., "Signup Forms").
* **Comment**: Type any additional comments or messages you have for Santa.
* **Assigned To**: Select who the form response is directed at (e.g., Santa's elves or a specific team member).
* **Additional Comments 2-9**: Repeat the process for each additional comment field.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure Santa receives your contact information.
* Use the **Category** field to help Santa sort and manage form responses.
* Use the **Assigned To** field to direct your form response to the right team member or department.
* Be concise in your comments, as there are multiple fields for additional comments.
