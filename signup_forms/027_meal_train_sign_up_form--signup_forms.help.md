# Meal Train Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user information and meal planning preferences for meal train services. This information will be used to provide personalized meal planning and communication with users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select your preferred meal plan option from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date and time for your meal train event.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the note field.
5. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
6. Enter your email address for communication.
7. Click on the submit button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant information to help us better understand your needs.
* **Meal Plan** (select_one, required: false): Choose your preferred meal plan option from the dropdown menu:
	+ Breakfast
	+ Lunch
	+ Dinner
* **Date** (date, required: false): Select a date for your meal train event.
* **Time** (time, required: false): Select a time for your meal train event.
* **Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location for your meal train event.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your meal train event.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Submit** (text, required: false): Click this button to send your information to us.
* **Cancel** (text, required: false): Click this button to cancel and exit the form.
