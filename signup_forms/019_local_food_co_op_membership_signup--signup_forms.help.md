# local_food_co_op_membership_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up as a member of the local food co-op.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
*   Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
*   Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
*   Select the membership type ("Yes" or "No") in the "Membership Type" field.
*   Select the membership length ("Yes", "No") for each option in the "Membership Length" field.
*   Select a payment method ("Yes" or "No") in the "Payment Method" field.
*   Enter any additional payment details in the "Payment Details" field (if applicable).
*   Enter the start date of your membership in the "Membership Start Date" field (if applicable).
*   Confirm your membership length in the "Confirm Membership Length" field (if applicable).
*   Confirm your payment method in the "Confirm Payment Method" field (if applicable).
*   Enter any comments as a member in the "Member Comments" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Membership Type** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you want to sign up as a member, "No" otherwise.
*   **Membership Length** (select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" for each membership length option you want to sign up for.
*   **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you have a payment method, "No" otherwise.
*   **Payment Details** (text, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
*   **Membership Start Date** (date, optional): Enter the start date of your membership.
*   **Confirm Membership Length** (text, optional): Confirm your membership length.
*   **Confirm Payment Method** (text, optional): Confirm your payment method.
*   **Confirm Membership Start Date** (date, optional): Confirm the start date of your membership.
*   **Confirm Membership Length** (select_multiple, optional): Confirm your membership length.
*   **Confirm Payment Method** (select_one, optional): Confirm your payment method.
*   **Member Comments** (text, optional): Enter any comments as a member.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Double-check your information before submitting.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to our support team.
