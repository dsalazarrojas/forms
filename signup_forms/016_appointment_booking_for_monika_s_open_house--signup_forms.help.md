# Appointment Booking For Monika's Open House - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the "Appointment Booking For Monika's Open House" form. The form is designed to collect information from potential attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the open house visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Booking Date" field and choose the date you would like to visit Monika's Open House. Please make sure the date is not too far in the future, as we want to ensure we can accommodate your request.
2. Enter your "Visitor Name" and "Visitor Email" to ensure we can contact you with updates and confirmations.
3. Provide your "Visitor Phone" number so we can reach you if needed.
4. In the "Number of Guests" field, enter the total number of people attending the open house visit with you.
5. If you have guests with you, please list their names in the "Guest Names" field.
6. Choose your preferred "Preferred Date" for the visit from the available dates.
7. Select your preferred "Preferred Time" slot from the available options.
8. Choose the type of property you are interested in from the "Property Interest" options.
9. Select your budget range for the purchase from the "Budget Range" options (if applicable).
10. If you currently own a home, please select "True" in the "Currently Own Home" field.
11. If you have financing in place, please select the corresponding option in the "Financing Status" field.
12. If you are working with a real estate agent, please enter their name in the "Agent Name" field.
13. If you have any special requirements or accessibility needs, please describe them in the "Special Requirements" field.
14. Please select how you heard about this open house from the "How Heard" options.
15. If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
16. Finally, confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes - Confirmed" in the "Confirm Attendance" field.
