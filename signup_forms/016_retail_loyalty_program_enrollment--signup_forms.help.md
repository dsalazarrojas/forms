# Retail Loyalty Program Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Loyalty Program Enrollment form is designed to enroll customers in the retail loyalty program. This program is meant to provide customers with exclusive rewards and benefits for their continued loyalty to our store.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, please fill in your name under the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address under the "Customer Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number under the "Customer Phone" field.
4. Enter your current address under the "Customer Address" field.
5. Select your city under the "Customer City" field.
6. Choose your state under the "Customer State" field. Please note that this field is a dropdown menu.
7. Enter your zip code under the "Customer Zip" field.
8. Choose your country under the "Customer Country" field. This field is also a dropdown menu.
9. Select your preferred language under the "Customer Language" field. You can choose more than one language if needed.
10. Choose the customer's timezone under the "Customer Timezone" field. This field allows multiple selections.
11-13 are additional fields that may be used for advanced purposes, please do not fill these in unless directed otherwise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your current address.
* **Customer City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **Customer State**: Select the state where you reside.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Customer Country**: Select the country where you reside.
* **Customer Language**: Select the languages you are proficient in. You may choose more than one language.
* **Customer Timezone**: Select the timezones you are available in. You may choose more than one timezone.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact our customer support team.
* Note that this form is for new customer enrollments, if you have already enrolled in the program, do not use this form.
