# simple_email_capture_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **simple_email_capture_form** is a basic contact information capture form designed to gather an email address, name, and phone number from users for follow-up on sales leads in a B2B context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. This is a required field and must be a valid email address.
2. Enter your name in the **Name** field. This field is optional, but providing your name will help us get in touch with you more effectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field. This field is optional, but providing your phone number will allow us to contact you more easily.
4. Enter your second email address in the **Email 2** field. This field is not required, but if you have multiple email addresses associated with your company, please provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, **required**): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Name (Name)** (`text`, **optional**): This field is intended to capture your name, which will help us get in touch with you more effectively.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`number`, **optional**): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you via phone.
* **Email 2 (Email 2)** (`email`, **optional**): If you have multiple email addresses associated with your company, please enter them here.
