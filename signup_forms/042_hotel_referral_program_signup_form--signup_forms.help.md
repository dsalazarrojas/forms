# Hotel Referral Program Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a part of the Hotel Referral Program, designed for users who want to refer their friends and family to our hotel. By filling out this form, you'll be able to track and confirm referrals, helping us keep our referral system up-to-date and secure.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First name, Last name, Email).
2. If you have a friend or family member who is visiting our hotel, provide their Referrer name and email (if available).
3. Confirm that you've obtained their consent for this referral.
4. Choose whether you want to confirm the referral (or not).
5. If you'd like to send them an email with the referral information, select "Confirm email".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (<type: text, required: true>):** 
    Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name (<type: text, required: true>):** 
    Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (<type: email, required: true>):** 
    Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about your referral.
* **Phone (<type: text, required: false>):** 
    Provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your referral.
* **Referral name (<type: text, required: false>):** 
    If you're referring someone, enter their name as you know it.
* **Referrer name (<type: text, required: true>):** 
    Enter the name of the person you're referring (as known to you).
* **Referrer email (<type: email, required: false>):** 
    Enter the email address of the person you're referring (if available).
* **Referrer phone (<type: text, required: false>):** 
    Provide the phone number of the person you're referring (if available).
* **Referral consent (<type: text, required: true>):** 
    Confirm that you've obtained the person's consent for this referral.
* **Referral date (<type: date, required: false>):** 
    Enter the date of the referral, if you're able to recall it.
* **Referrer time (<type: time, required: false>):** 
    Enter the time of the referral, if you're able to recall it.
* **Confirm referral (<type: select_one, required: true>):** 
    Choose "True" to confirm this referral and "False" to cancel.
* **Confirm email (<type: select_one, required: true>):** 
    Select "True" to send a confirmation email to the person being referred and "False" to keep it private.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and consents from the people you're referring to participate in our referral program.
- If you're unsure about any of the details, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
