# Browngates Dog And Cat Boarding Contract 2019 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the Browngates Dog And Cat Boarding Contract 2019. It is used to collect information from the owner about their pet's details, boarding dates, and other relevant information for the pet's stay at Browngates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the owner of the pet.
2. Enter your home address.
3. Enter the city where your home is located.
4. Enter the state where your home is located.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter the name of the pet you are boarding.
8. Select the type of pet you are boarding (Dog, Cat, or Both).
9. Enter the breed of the pet.
10. Enter the age of the pet (in years).
11. Enter the weight of the pet (in pounds).
12. Enter the color or any distinctive markings of the pet.
13. Enter the date you plan to check-in your pet.
14. Enter the date you plan to pick up your pet after boarding.
15. Confirm that your pet is currently on all vaccinations required (Rabies and DHPP).
16. If your pet has any special dietary or medical needs, enter them here.
17. Provide any behavioral notes or temperaments about your pet.
18. Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
19. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
20. Enter the name of your veterinarian.
21. Enter the phone number of your veterinarian.
22. Enter the daily boarding rate for your pet.
23. Finally, select "Yes, I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the boarding contract terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Full Name** (1, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the owner of the pet.
* **Home Address** (2, `text`, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (3, `text`, required): Enter the city where your home is located.
* **State** (4, `text`, required): Enter the state where your home is located.
* **Phone Number** (5, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (6, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Pet Name** (7, `text`, required): Enter the name of the pet you are boarding.
* **Pet Type** (8, `select_one`, required): Select the type of pet you are boarding (Dog, Cat, or Both).
* **Breed** (9, `text`, required): Enter the breed of the pet.
* **Age** (10, `number`, required): Enter the age of the pet (in years).
* **Weight** (11, `number`, required): Enter the weight of the pet (in pounds).
* **Color or Markings** (12, `text`, optional): Enter any distinctive markings or colors of the pet.
* **Boarding Check-In Date** (13, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to check-in your pet.
* **Boarding Check-Out Date** (14, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to pick up your pet after boarding.
* **Current on All Vaccinations** (15, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your pet is currently on all required vaccinations (Rabies and DHPP).
* **Special Diet or Medical Needs** (16, `text`, optional): If your pet has any special dietary or medical needs, enter them here.
* **Behavioral Notes or Temperament** (17, `text`, optional): Provide any behavioral notes or temperaments about your pet.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (18, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (19, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Veterinarian Name** (20, `text`, required): Enter the name of your veterinarian.
* **Veterinarian Phone** (21, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your veterinarian.
* **Daily Boarding Rate** (22, `number`, required): Enter the daily boarding rate for your pet.
* **I agree to the boarding contract terms** (23, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the boarding contract terms.
