# Referee Availability Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the availability of a referee for a specific week.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your availability for each day of the week by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' for each day.
2. Enter your available time for morning, afternoon, and evening periods, if applicable.
3. Provide a start and end time for your available periods, if applicable.
4. Add any additional comments if you need to provide further information.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monday** (`week_0`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Monday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Tuesday** (`week_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Tuesday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Wednesday** (`week_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Wednesday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Thursday** (`week_3`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Thursday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Friday** (`week_4`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Friday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Saturday** (`week_5`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Saturday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Sunday** (`week_6`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select your availability for Sunday. Please choose 'Yes' if you are available, 'No' otherwise.
* **Morning** (`morning`, `time`, required: false): This field allows you to enter your available time for morning periods.
* **Afternoon** (`afternoon`, `time`, required: false): This field allows you to enter your available time for afternoon periods.
* **Evening** (`evening`, `time`, required: false): This field allows you to enter your available time for evening periods.
* **End Time** (`morning_end`, `date`, required: false): This field allows you to enter the end time of your available periods.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `date`, required: false): This field allows you to enter the start time of your available periods.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to enter any additional information about your available periods.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, required: false): This field allows you to add any additional comments about your availability.

* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your phone number, which will be used for contact purposes.
