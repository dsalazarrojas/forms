# media_accreditation_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from media representatives for accreditation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information, including First Name, Last Name, Organization, Job Title, Email, and Contact Number.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted by selecting one or multiple options from the "How can we contact you?" field.
3. Provide your address information, including Address Line 1, Address Line 2, City, State or Province, and Postal Code.
4. Select your Media Type from the available options.
5. Enter your Media Contact information.
6. Enter the Event Name and Role you will be attending.
7. Fill out any additional fields as necessary, such as Media Outlet and Media Accreditation ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your organization or publication.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or role within your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, `text`, optional): Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2** (`address_line_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the second line of your address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city where you live or work.
* **State or Province** (`state_or_province`, `text`, optional): Enter the state or province where you live or work.
* **Post Office Box** (`post_office_box`, `text`, optional): Enter your post office box number (if applicable).
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, optional): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **How can we contact you?** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Select one or multiple ways that we can contact you.
* **How can we contact you?** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple ways that we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time (if applicable).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your accreditation.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of media you represent.
* **Media Contact** (`media_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for your media.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Enter your role at the event.
* **Media Outlet** (`media_outlet`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your media outlet.
* **Media Accreditation ID** (`media_accreditation_id`, `text`, optional): Enter your media accreditation ID.
* **Media Accreditation Status** (`media_accreditation_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your media accreditation.
* **Media Accreditation Note** (`media_accreditation_note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your media accreditation.
