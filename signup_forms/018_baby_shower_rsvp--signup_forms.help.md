# Baby Shower RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baby Shower RSVP form is a simple form designed to collect information from guests attending a baby shower event. This form is intended to gather details such as guest names, email addresses, and meal preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

### Completing this form is straightforward. Simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional information you'd like to share with the host.
1. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents or as you'd like to be listed for the event.
2. If you have a confirmed email address, enter it so that the host can contact you if needed.
3. Select your attendance status:
	* True: You are attending the baby shower.
	* False: You are not attending the baby shower.
	* Maybe: You are uncertain if you will attend.
4. If you will be attending, enter the number of guests (including yourself) that will be coming with you.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the meal preference that best suits your needs.
6. If you'd like to make a song request, enter your song title and artist.
7. If you have any transportation information (parking or carpooling) that the host may find useful, enter it here.
8. Enter the date you are submitting your RSVP response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name**: This field is used to store your full name.
* **Email address**: This field is used to store your email address for the host to contact you.
* **Attending**: Select your attendance status: True if you are attending, False if you are not, and Maybe if unsure.
* **Number of guests**: If attending, enter the number of guests (including yourself) coming with you.
* **Meal preference**: Select from the options provided to indicate your meal preference:
	+ None: No meal preference.
	+ Vegetarian: A vegetarian meal.
	+ Vegan: A vegan meal.
	+ Gluten free: A gluten-free meal.
	+ Other: Specify a different meal option.
* **Song request**: If you'd like to make a song request, enter your song title and artist.
* **Transportation notes**: If you have any transportation information that may be useful, enter it here.
* **Gift selected**: If you've chosen a gift from the host's registry, enter the item name or link.
* **Expected arrival time**: If you're attending, indicate your expected arrival time to help with coordination.
* **RSVP submission date**: Enter the date you're submitting this form.
