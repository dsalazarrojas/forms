## Lead Magnet Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lead Magnet Checklist Form is used to collect essential details and preferences from individuals interested in learning more about a particular lead magnet or resource. This form helps the lead generation team understand the level of interest, contact information, and additional preferences from potential leads, which enables the team to tailor the lead magnet and follow-up process to better serve their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Confirm your email address by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number (if applicable) in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred option from the "Select One" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
7. Optionally, provide a date and time in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
8. Add a note about your interest or any additional details in the "Note" field.
9. Describe the lead magnet you are interested in learning more about in the "Lead Magnet" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (Text): Provide the email address you check most frequently.
* **Confirm Email** (Select One): Confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
* **Phone** (Text): If you have a phone number, enter it here (optional).
* **Select One** (Select One): Choose the option that best represents your preference or interest.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple): Select all that apply from the list of options.
* **Date** (Date): Enter a date in MM/DD/YYYY format (optional).
* **Time** (Time): Enter a time in 12-hour format (AM/PM) (optional).
* **Note** (Note): Add any additional details or notes about your interest.
* **Lead Magnet** (Text): Briefly describe the lead magnet you are interested in learning more about.
