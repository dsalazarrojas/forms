# virtual_library_sign_up_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your interests, preferences, and reading habits. By filling out this form, you'll be able to:
* Sign up for our virtual library service
* Receive personalized book recommendations based on your interests
* Get updates on new book releases and events

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information and select your preferences from the options provided. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for us to address you by.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for us to address you by.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates from us.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter a password to secure your account. (Note: This field is for account creation purposes only. Do not enter an actual password here. If you already have an account, please log in instead.)
* **Address Line 1** (`address_line_1`, text, optional): Enter your mailing address line 1 if you want to receive physical mail from us.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city for us to better serve you.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state for us to better serve you.
* **Zip** (`zip`, number, required): Enter your zip code to ensure we can deliver mail to you.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence for us to better serve you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Book Preferences** (`book_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of books you're interested in (e.g., Fiction, Non-Fiction, Biography, etc.).
* **Genre** (`genre`, select_one, optional): Select the genre you're interested in (e.g., Fiction, Non-Fiction, Biography, etc.).
* **Reading Habits** (`reading_habits`, select_multiple, optional): Select the times you read (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, Evening, etc.).
* **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_multiple, optional): Select the format you prefer to read in (e.g., E-book, Paperback, Hardcover, etc.).
* **Notification** (`notification`, select_one, optional): Select how often you'd like to receive updates from us (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Read and agree to our terms and conditions to complete your sign-up.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to you.
