# Camp Fun Quest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Fun Quest Registration Form is used to gather information about each camper's personal details, health, and preferences for the camp session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the Camper Information section with the camper's first name, last name, date of birth, age, and email address (if provided).
2. In the Parent/Guardian Information section, provide the guardian's first name, last name, relationship to the camper, phone number, and email address.
3. In the Emergency Contact Information section, provide the emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship to the camper.
4. The Health and Medical Information section requires you to indicate if the camper has any allergies, specify allergy details, provide any significant medical conditions, list current medications, and mention any dietary restrictions or special food needs.
5. Finally, select the preferred camp session from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Camper Information, required): Enter the camper's first name.
* **Last Name** (Camper Information, required): Enter the camper's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Camper Information, required): Enter the camper's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Age** (Camper Information, required): Enter the camper's age.
* **Email Address** (Camper Information, optional): Enter the camper's email address (if available).
* **Phone Number** (Camper Information, optional): Enter the camper's phone number (if available).
* **Guardian First Name** (Parent/Guardian Information, required): Enter the guardian's first name.
* **Guardian Last Name** (Parent/Guardian Information, required): Enter the guardian's last name.
* **Relationship to Camper** (Parent/Guardian Information, required): Select the relationship between the guardian and the camper (Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Guardian Phone Number** (Parent/Guardian Information, required): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email Address** (Parent/Guardian Information, required): Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Information, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Emergency Contact Information, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship to Camper** (Emergency Contact Information, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the camper.
* **Does the camper have any allergies** (Health and Medical Information, required): Select "True" if the camper has any allergies, "False" otherwise.
* **Allergy Details** (Health and Medical Information, optional): If the camper has allergies, provide more details in this field.
* **Any significant medical conditions** (Health and Medical Information, optional): List any significant medical conditions the camper has.
* **Current Medications** (Health and Medical Information, optional): List the camper's current medications.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Special Food Needs** (Health and Medical Information, optional): Mention any dietary restrictions or special food needs the camper has.
* **Preferred Camp Session** (Camp Preferences, required): Select the preferred camp session from the options provided.
