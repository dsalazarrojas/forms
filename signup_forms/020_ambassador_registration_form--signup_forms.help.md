# Ambassador Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Ambassador Registration Form is designed for individuals who want to join our ambassador program as an event host, social media representative, or outreach representative. This form helps us understand your availability and interests, ensuring a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the registration section.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Provide your email address for updates.
4. Choose your region.
5. Select your role of interest (event host, social media, or outreach).
6. (Optional) Specify your available start date for participating.
7. (Optional) Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Add any additional comments or relevant information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Full name
* **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first and last name for proper identification. Please use the format "John Doe" or "Jane Doe."

### 3. Email address
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you use for updates and communications. This is used for notifications and event invitations.

### 4. Region
* **Region** (`region`, `select_one`, required): Select your geographical region from the options provided (North, South, East, West).

### 5. Role of interest
* **Role of interest** (`role_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred ambassador role from the options (Event host, Social media, Outreach).

### 6. Available start date
* **Available start date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific date when you can begin participating, please enter it. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### 7. Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact purposes. This is optional and not required for the registration process.

### 8. Additional comments
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Share any relevant information or details that you think would be helpful for us to know. This is not required, but it will aid in your onboarding process.
