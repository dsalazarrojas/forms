# Talent Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from talents who are interested in working with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the First Page section.
2. Upload your Resume in the Resume field.
3. Add a photo of yourself in the Image field.
4. Write a brief Profile in the Profile field.
5. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
6. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
7. Add any additional details about yourself in the Details field.
8. Provide any other information you feel is relevant to your talents in the Other Info field (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Page**: This is the first section of the form where you will provide your contact information. Please fill in the required fields to ensure you are registered correctly.
  - **Resume**: This is where you will upload your resume, which should be in a PDF or Word document format.
  - **Image**: Please upload a photo of yourself. This will help us get to know you better.
  - **Profile**: Write a brief description about yourself and your skills. This will help us understand your talents better.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
  - **Details**: Add any additional details about yourself that you think are relevant to your talents.
  - **Other Info**: This is an optional field where you can provide any other information you feel is important about yourself.
