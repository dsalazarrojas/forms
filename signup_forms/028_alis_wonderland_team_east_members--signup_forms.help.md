# Alis Wonderland Team East Members - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from prospective team members of Alis Wonderland Team East, a group focused on community service, environmental projects, technology, arts, sports, and education. The form is intended for students and their parents or guardians to provide details about themselves, their interests, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked as "true" or "required".
3. For optional fields, answer any information you feel is relevant or useful for the team.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your school or personal email.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, select_one, required): Select your school from the provided options.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select your preferred t-shirt size.
* **Area of Interest** (`team_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas that interest you.
* **Available Days** (`availability_days`, select_multiple, required): Select all days you are available.
* **Available Times** (`availability_time`, select_multiple, required): Select all times you are available.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation`, select_one, required): Select how you plan to arrive at events.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, text, false): This field is optional; enter any previous volunteer experience.
* **Skills and Talents** (`skills_and_talents`, text, false): This field is optional; list your skills and talents.
* **Why Join?** (`why_join`, text, required): Explain why you want to join the team.
* **Goals for Team** (`goals_for_team`, text, required): State your goals for being part of the team.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, false): This field is optional; enter an emergency contact name if different from the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Medical Conditions/Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, false): This field is optional; enter any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, false): This field is optional; enter any dietary restrictions.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Decide whether you allow photos to be taken.
* **Media Release** (`media_release`, select_one, required): Decide whether you allow your name and image to be used in social media.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Agree to the team's code of conduct.
* **Commitment Understanding** (`commitment_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of the team's commitment expectations.
* **How Heard About Us** (`how_hear_about_program`, select_one, false): This field is optional; select how you heard about the team.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, false): This field is optional; add any additional comments or information.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and fill out all required fields carefully.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask for clarification before proceeding.
- The team will use this information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all members.
