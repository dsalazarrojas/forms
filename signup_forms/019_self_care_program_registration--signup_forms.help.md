# Self Care Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in a Self Care Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter the program's information, including the program name, start date and time, location, and notes.
3. Enter the contact person's information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
4. If applicable, select the organization type from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 4`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Program Name** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the program name.
* **Program Date** (`id: 6`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the program start date.
* **Program Time** (`id: 7`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the program start time.
* **Participant Phone** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Phone Extension** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant's phone extension.
* **Program Location** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the program location.
* **Program Notes** (`id: 11`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the program.
* **Organization** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the organization type (Wellness Provider, Community Organization, or Workplace Wellbeing Team).
* **Participant Notes** (`id: 13`, `type: note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
* **Contact Name** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 16`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Participant Address** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Contact Address** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's address.
* **Program ID** (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the program ID.
* **Participant ID** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the participant ID.
* **Status** (`id: 21`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the status of the program.
* **Created By** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the person who created the program.
* **Created At** (`id: 23`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date the program was created.
* **Updated At** (`id: 24`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date the program was last updated.
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter a comma-separated list of tools used for this program.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about a field, contact the contact person listed above.
* If you need to edit or update a program, contact the contact person listed above.
