# Exclusive Sale Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exclusive Sale Signup Form is designed for sales representatives and business professionals to capture information about their sales activities, sales performance, and related details. This form is likely used to track and record sales data for analysis, reporting, and possibly for promotional or incentive purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Input your **Company** name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your **Department** name in the "Department" field.
6. Select your **Job Title** from the options provided in the "Job Title" field.
7. Enter your **Job Position** in the "Job Position" field.
8. Input your **Job Function** in the "Job Function" field.
9. Choose the type of **Sale** you are associated with from the "Sale Type" options.
10. Enter the **Promotion Date** of your sale in the "Promotion Date" field.
11. Enter the **Sales Amount** (in the currency of your choice) in the "Sales Amount" field.
12. Input your **Discount** details, if applicable, in the "Discount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Input your company name.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, required): Enter your job position.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Input your job function or role.
* **Sale Type** (`sale_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of sale you are associated with.
* **Promotion Date** (`promotion_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your sale promotion or offer.
* **Sales Amount** (`sales_amount`, number, required): Enter the sales amount.
* **Discount** (`discount`, text, optional): Input any applicable discount details.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* This form is designed to capture data for sales performance tracking; if you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or support team.
