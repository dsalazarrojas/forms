# Douro River Exploration Signup - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Douro River Exploration Signup form is designed to collect information from participants who want to sign up for a river exploration experience or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Douro River Exploration Signup form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and contact information.
2. Provide any additional details you think are relevant to your participation in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering your details, but it's not clear what type of details are expected. Please fill in any information you feel is relevant to your participation.
* **participant_details_2** (`text`, optional): Another field for entering more details. Similar to the first field, please provide any information you feel is useful.
* **Safety Acknowledgement** (`text`, optional): This field is for acknowledging your understanding of safety guidelines. Please type in a brief statement or message to confirm you've read and understood the safety information.
* **participant_details_3-10** (`text`, optional): Repeat fields for entering more details. Please fill in any information you think is relevant to your participation. 

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields as clearly and concisely as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
* Remember to review all fields before submitting the form to ensure you've filled in all necessary information.
