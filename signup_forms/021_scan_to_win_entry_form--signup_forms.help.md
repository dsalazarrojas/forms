# Scan To Win Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect entry information for the Scan to Win promotion. Users will need to provide personal details, select options, and optionally note any additional information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields marked with an *.
2. Select one or more options from the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
3. Enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.
4. Fill out the "Scan to Win" field with any number from 0 to 9999.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Zip** (number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter a date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (e.g. 2022-01-01).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter a time in the format "HH:MM:SS" (e.g. 12:00:00).
* **Select One** (select_one, optional): Choose one of the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the available options.
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about your entry.
* **Scan to Win** (number, optional): Enter any number from 0 to 9999.
* **Scan to Win** (number, optional): This field is not required, but if filled out, will be used to determine the prize to be won.
