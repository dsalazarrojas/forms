# Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a sign-up form used for users to provide their personal and subscription information for account creation or subscription purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field.
4. Select your preferred Subscription option from the Subscription field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field (if applicable).
6. You may add any additional information or notes about yourself in the Note field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Subscription** (`subscription`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available subscription options (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or notes you want to provide.
