# Customer Retention Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Retention Pilot Registration Form is designed to collect information from customers who will participate in a customer retention pilot program. This form will help us understand your background, role, and preferences to ensure that you receive the most relevant support and resources for your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your name, company, job title, and contact information.
2. Provide your company information, including team name and job function.
3. Indicate your role and department within the company.
4. Specify your location.
5. Confirm your participation by selecting 'Yes' from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)**:
	+ This is where you'll enter your participant information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Participant Name (2)**:
	+ Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Participant Email (3)**:
	+ Enter the email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Participant Company (4)**:
	+ Type in your company's name.
* **Participant Job Title (5)**:
	+ Enter your job title within your company.
* **Participant Phone (6)**:
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Company (7)**:
	+ Duplicate of Participant Company (4). This field is intended for consistency and ease of data management.
* **Participant Team (8)**:
	+ Enter your team name.
* **Participant Role (9)**:
	+ Describe your role within your company.
* **Participant Job Function (10)**:
	+ Enter your job function within your company.
* **Participant Department (11)**:
	+ Specify your department.
* **Participant Job Title Other (13)**:
	+ Enter any additional job titles you hold within your company.
* **Participant Contact Title (14)**:
	+ Enter a title for your contact information.
* **Contact Email (15)**:
	+ Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Contact Phone (16)**:
	+ Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Contact (17)**:
	+ Enter the name of your contact person.
* **Team (18)**:
	+ Enter the name of your team.
* **Role (19)**:
	+ Duplicate of Participant Role (9). This field is intended for consistency and ease of data management.
* **Job Function (20)**:
	+ Duplicate of Participant Job Function (10). This field is intended for consistency and ease of data management.
* **Department (21)**:
	+ Duplicate of Participant Department (11). This field is intended for consistency and ease of data management.
* **Location (22)**:
	+ Duplicate of Participant Location (12). This field is intended for consistency and ease of data management.
* **Date (23)**:
	+ Enter the date of your participation in the pilot program.
* **Time (24)**:
	+ Enter the time of your participation in the pilot program.
* **Notes (25)**:
	+ Enter any additional information or comments about your participation in the pilot program.
* **Confirm (26)**:
	+ Select 'Yes' to confirm your participation in the pilot program.
