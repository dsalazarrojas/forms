## Summer Camp Registration Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a summer camp lead with your information for follow-up and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Review the form carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
* Enter your name and email address in the `Parent Name` and `Parent Email` fields, respectively.
* Select the type of summer program you are interested in from the `Program Type` dropdown menu.
* Choose the date, time, and duration of the program in the `Program Date`, `Program Time`, and `Program Session Length` fields, respectively.
* Select the location of the program in the `Camp Location` field.
* Enter the session time in the `Session Time` field.
* If you would like to be contacted through any specific method, select it from the `Follow-up Method` dropdown menu.
* Enter any additional notes or comments in the `Notes` field.
* If you have a preferred tool for communication, choose it from the `Assigned Tool` dropdown menu.
* Submit the form to register your interest in our summer programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of summer program you are interested in.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the program.
* **Program Time** (`program_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the program.
* **Camp Location** (`camp_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the camp.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, text, required): Enter the time of the session.
* **Program Session Length** (`program_session_length`, text, required): Enter the length of the program session in minutes.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select the methods through which you would like to be contacted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred tool for communication.
