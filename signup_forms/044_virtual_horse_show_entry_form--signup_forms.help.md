# Virtual Horse Show Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Horse Show Entry Form is designed to collect information from horse owners and competitors to facilitate the registration process for online virtual horse shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the horse and owner.
2. Select the correct breed, class, horse type, sex, and division for the horse.
3. Enter the horse's height, sire, dam, and owner information.
4. Choose the payment method for the entry fee.
5. Set the show date and time.
6. Review the entry for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Horse Name (id: 1)**: Enter the name of your horse.
* **Breed (id: 2)**: Select the correct breed of your horse.
* **Class (id: 3)**: Select the class for your horse (Amateur, Amateur/Professional, Am/Pro, etc.).
* **Height (id: 4)**: Enter the height of your horse.
* **Horse Breed (id: 5)**: Select multiple breeds for your horse (e.g., Quarter Horse, Pleasure Horse, etc.).
* **Horse Type (id: 6)**: Select the type of horse (Halter, Spotted, etc.).
* **Sex (id: 7)**: Select the sex of your horse (Male, Female, etc.).
* **Age (id: 8)**: Optional - enter the age of your horse.
* **Sire (id: 9)**: Enter the sire's name.
* **Dam (id: 10)**: Enter the dam's name.
* **Breeder (id: 11)**: Optional - enter the breeder's name.
* **Owner (id: 12)**: Enter the owner's name.
* **Trainer (id: 13)**: Enter the trainer's name.
* **Trainer's Address (id: 14)**: Enter the trainer's address.
* **Contact Person (id: 15)**: Enter a contact person for the trainer.
* **Contact Email (id: 16)**: Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone (id: 17)**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Entry Fees (id: 18)**: Enter the total entry fee.
* **Payment Method (id: 19)**: Choose how you'd like to pay (PayPal, Check, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Other Payment Method (id: 20)**: Optional - enter other payment method if not listed above.
* **Show (id: 21)**: Enter the name of the show.
* **Date (id: 22)**: Enter the date of the show.
* **Time (id: 23)**: Enter the time of the show.
* **Division (id: 24)**: Select the division for your horse.
* **Entry Date (id: 25)**: Enter the date you are submitting your entry.
