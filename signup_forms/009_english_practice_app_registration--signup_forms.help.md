# English Practice App Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for the English Practice App. The form collects information about the student, including name, contact details, school details, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your birthdate in the "Birthdate" field.
5. Choose your preferred language from the "Language" dropdown menu.
6. Select your practice level from the "Practice Level" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
8. Select your school grade from the "School Grade" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your guardian's name in the "Guardian Name" field.
10. Enter your guardian's phone number in the "Guardian Phone" field.
11. Enter your guardian's email address in the "Guardian Email" field.
12. Enter your school address in the "School Address" field.
13. Select whether your parent has given you permission to use the app in the "Parent Permission" dropdown menu.
14. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
15. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
16. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
17. (Optional) If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Conditions" field.
18. (Optional) If you take any medication, enter it in the "Medication" field.
19. (Optional) If you have any medical notes, enter them in the "Medical Notes" field.
20. (Optional) If you have any allergies, enter them in the "Allergy" field.
21. (Optional) If you have a list of medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Condition List" field.
22. (Optional) If you have any further information about your emergency contact, enter it in the "Emergency Contact Notes" field.
23. (Optional) If you have any other information, enter it in the "Other" field.
24. Review all the information you have entered and submit the form to complete your registration.
