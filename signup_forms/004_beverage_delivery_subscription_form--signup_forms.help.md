# Beverage Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beverage Delivery Subscription Form is designed to create a custom delivery subscription for users. Fill out this form to receive regular deliveries of your favorite beverages, tailored to your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Create Your Account" to start the form.
2. Fill in your personal details including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Street Address, City, State/Province, and Postal Code.
3. Choose your preferred delivery schedule, including the frequency and time of delivery.
4. Select the type of beverages you want to receive.
5. Specify your average weekly consumption and any special preferences or dietary restrictions.
6. Select your preferred payment method and whether or not to auto-renew your subscription.
7. Optionally, enter a Referral Code or Promo Code.
8. Finally, agree to the subscription terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Create Your Account**: Click on this section to begin the subscription process.
  * **First Name**: Enter your given name.
  * **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
  * **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you want to receive subscription updates.
  * **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for delivery notifications.
  * **Street Address**: Enter your delivery address including the street name and number.
  * **City**: Enter the city where you want to receive deliveries.
  * **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where you want to receive deliveries.
  * **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code or zip code where you want to receive deliveries.
- **Delivery Address**: This section is for delivery purposes only.
- **Subscription Preferences**:
  * **Choose Your Subscription Plan**: Select from Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or Custom schedule.
  * **Preferred Delivery Day**: Choose a day of the week for delivery.
  * **Preferred Delivery Time Window**: Choose a time slot for delivery.
  * **Beverage Types**: Select all the types of beverages you want to receive.
  * **Average Weekly Consumption (units)**: Enter the average number of units you consume per week.
  * **Special Preferences or Dietary Restrictions**: Enter any allergies or special preferences.
- **Payment Information**:
  * **Payment Method**: Select from Credit card, Debit card, Bank transfer, or Payment plan.
  * **Is Billing Address Same as Delivery Address**: If yes, then your billing address will be the same as your delivery address.
  * **Auto-Renewal Preference**: Choose whether to auto-renew your subscription.
  * **Referral Code or Promo Code**: Enter any referral or promo code you may have.
  * **I Agree to Subscription Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement to the subscription terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) to ensure your account creation is successful.
* Review your choices carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
