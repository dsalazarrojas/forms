# Developer Meetup Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Meetup Signup Form is a tool used to collect information from developers who are interested in attending meetups. It helps the organizers to keep track of attendees, their details, and other essential meetup information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Meetup details** section with the basic information about the meetup, such as location and date.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** details to register yourself as an attendee.
3. Specify the **Meetup type** as General, Tech talk, or Workshop.
4. Confirm your **Signup confirmation** by selecting one of the options (Yes, I'm coming!, No thanks!, or Not sure yet!).
5. Enter the **Attendee details**, including attendee name, email, and phone number.
6. Add any **Additional info** about the meetup if needed.
7. Upload any relevant **Meetup image** or **Meetup file** if required.
8. Finally, click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meetup details**: Enter the basic information about the meetup, such as location and date.
* **Name**: Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Email**: Enter your email address as an attendee.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number as an attendee.
* **Meetup type**: Choose the type of meetup (General, Tech talk, or Workshop).
* **Signup confirmation**: Confirm your attendance by selecting one of the options (Yes, I'm coming!, No thanks!, or Not sure yet!).
* **Attendee details**: Enter the name, email, and phone number of each attendee.
* **Meetup image**: Upload a relevant image about the meetup.
* **Meetup file**: Upload a relevant file about the meetup.
* **Additional info**: Add any additional information about the meetup.
* **Meetup address**: Enter the meetup address.
* **Attendee name**: Enter the name of each attendee.
* **Signup date**: Enter the date you are signing up for the meetup.
* **Attendee email**: Enter the email address of each attendee.
* **Attendee phone**: Enter the phone number of each attendee.
* **Meetup description**: Enter a short description of the meetup.
* **Meetup tags**: Enter any relevant tags about the meetup.
* **Meetup URL**: Enter the URL of the meetup.
* **Meetup image**: Upload a relevant image about the meetup.
* **Meetup file**: Upload a relevant file about the meetup.
* **Additional info 2**: Add any additional information about the meetup.
* **Note**: Add any notes about the meetup.

Note: Some fields may not be required, so make sure to fill out only the required fields as per the form instructions.
