# data_entry_with_easy_search - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows you to search through your data using a simple and easy-to-use interface.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by entering the search term you are looking for in the "Search Term" field. This field is a required field, meaning you must enter a value in it to submit the form.
2. Once you have entered your search term, click the "Search" button to initiate the search.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Search Form** (`search_form`, `text`, required/optional): This is the main form where you can enter your search query. It is a text field where you can type in your search term. You can enter any combination of characters, including spaces.
* **Search Term** (`search_term`, `text`, **required**): This field is where you enter the term you want to search for. Please make sure to enter a value in this field to submit the form.
* **Search** (`search_button`, `text`, required/optional): This is the button that initiates the search. Clicking this button will process your search query and display the results.
