# Health Product Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Product Raffle Entry form is used to collect information for a raffle entry related to health products. This form will help us understand who is interested in participating in the raffle and their preferences regarding health products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the fields as requested.
2. Provide your email address.
3. Enter your phone number (if available).
4. Choose the name of the health product that interests you most.
5. Select the quantity you would like to enter.
6. Choose the date you would like to be considered for entry.
7. Select the time of your entry.
8. Add any additional information in the "Note" field.
9. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If available, enter your phone number.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, select_one, not required): Choose the name of the health product that interests you.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, not required): If applicable, select the quantity of the health product you are interested in.
* **Entry date** (`date`, date, not required): Select a date you are interested in being considered for entry.
* **Entry time** (`time`, time, not required): Choose a time for your entry.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional information you think is important for us to know.
* **Agree to terms and conditions** (`agree_terms`, select_multiple, not required): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the appropriate option.
