# email_sign_up_form_4 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for general email sign-up purposes, collecting basic contact and personal details from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Type in your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your desired password in the "Password" field.
4. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email" field to ensure it's accurate.
5. Verify your password by re-entering it in the "Confirm password" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name, which can be any name you'd like to be known by.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
- **Password** (`password`, Text, Optional): Type in the password for your new email account.
- **Confirm email** (`confirm_email`, Text, Optional): Re-enter your email address for verification.
- **Confirm password** (`confirm_password`, Text, Optional): Re-enter your chosen password for verification purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
- If you have any issues with the form or its functionality, please contact our support team.
- You can always come back and edit your details if you need to make changes after submitting.
