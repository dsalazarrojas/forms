# Play Reading Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Play Reading Signup Form" is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in play readings. This form helps to gather essential details about the participants, including their role and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **username** in the first field. This is a general identifier for you in the system. 
2. Enter the **play** you want to participate in. Please type the name of the play you are interested in.
3. Select your **role** in the play. This could be Actor, Director, or Both, depending on your involvement.
4. If you have a specific **availability** date and time, please enter it in the date field. This is optional but helps the organizers plan the play reading.
5. Enter your **email** for contact purposes. This will be used to communicate with you about the play reading.
6. You can provide any additional **contact** information if you have any specific requirements or needs.
7. Briefly describe your **role description** in the play, if you have any specific skills or experiences relevant to your chosen role.
8. If you have any **notes** that you would like to add, feel free to share them in the text box below.
9. Finally, confirm your role by selecting **true** or **false** in the last field. This ensures that we have accurate information about your role and participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **username** (text, optional)
A unique identifier for you in the system.
### 2. **play** (text, optional)
Type the name of the play you're interested in participating in.
### 3. **role** (select one, required)
Select your role in the play as Actor, Director, or Both.
### 4. **availability** (date, optional)
If you have a specific date and time you're available, please enter it here. This helps the organizers plan accordingly.
### 5. **email** (email, optional)
Enter your contact email for communication about the play reading.
### 6. **contact** (text, optional)
Provide any additional contact information if you have specific requirements or needs.
### 7. **role_description** (text, optional)
Describe your role in the play, including any specific skills or experiences relevant to your chosen role.
### 8. **notes** (note, optional)
Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
### 9. **confirm_role** (select one, required)
Confirm your role by selecting True or False. This ensures accurate information about your participation.
