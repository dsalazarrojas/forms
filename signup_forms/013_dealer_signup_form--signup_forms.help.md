# Dealer Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Signup Form is designed to collect information from dealers who want to sign up for our services. This form is used for new dealer onboarding and should only be used for this specific purpose to avoid confusion and data duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the required fields.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "dealer_email" field.
3. Fill out the "dealer_phone" field with your phone number.
4. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **dealer_signupsubmit** (Text, required): Enter a short description of why you're signing up as a dealer.
* **dealer_name** (Text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business documents.
* **dealer_email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **dealer_phone** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **dealer_signup** (Text, required): Re-enter your dealer name for confirmation.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
