# Digital Marketing Webinar Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information and preferences from attendees for a digital marketing webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate registration details and preferences.
2. Ensure that you fill in all the required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any field, you can refer to this guide for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your registration details in this field.
* **Webinar Date** (`2`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you plan to attend the webinar.
* **Webinar Time** (`3`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you plan to attend the webinar.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Attendee Interests** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your interests from the list provided.
* **Attendee Company** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Attendee Job Title** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Webinar Link** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any other relevant link, enter it here.
* **Confirmation** (`10`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or confirmations for your registration.
* **Notes** (`11`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Attendee Email** (`12`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirmation Date** (`13`, `date`, required/optional): Select the confirmation date for your attendance.
* **Attendee Address** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all the required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
