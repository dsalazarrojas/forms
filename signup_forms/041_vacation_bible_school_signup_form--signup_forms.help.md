# Vacation Bible School Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for the Vacation Bible School. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your child's information is up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information for each field, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, *): This field is for you, the parent, to provide your name and contact details.
* **Child Info** (`child_info`, `text`, *): Please enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Group Info** (`group_info`, `text`, *): Enter any relevant information about your child's group (e.g., class, team).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, *): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with updates.
* **Date** (`date`, *): Select the date you want to register your child for the Vacation Bible School.
* **Time** (`time`, *): Choose the time slot you prefer for the Vacation Bible School.
* **Group** (`group`, `select_one`, *): Please indicate if your child will be part of a group or not.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, *): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with important updates.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `text`, *): Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about the Vacation Bible School.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, *): Any additional information you'd like to provide about your child.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, *): Once you've completed all required fields, click the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.

## Tips
* Double-check your information carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select a date and time that works for you and your child.
