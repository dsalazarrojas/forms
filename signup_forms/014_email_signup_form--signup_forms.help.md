# email-signup-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for email signup and confirmation purposes. It collects user information and confirmations for multiple stages of the process.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **Email** address.
2. Fill out **Name**.
3. Enter your **Phone** (optional).
4. Select your **Email Type** (e.g., choice1, choice2, or choice3).
5. Indicate your **Lead Source** (e.g., Yes or No).
6. Enter a **Date** for your signup (optional).
7. Enter a **Time** for your signup (optional).
8. Enter a **Note** for your signup (optional).
9. Click **Submit** to save your progress.
10. Confirm your **Email**, **Email Type**, **Lead Source**, **Date**, **Time**, and **Note** (optional).
