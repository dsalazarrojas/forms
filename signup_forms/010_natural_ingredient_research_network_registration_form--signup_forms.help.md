# Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration Form is designed to gather information from researchers interested in joining the network. By filling out this form, you will be providing the network administrators with essential details about yourself, such as your first and last name, contact information, research experience, and interests. This information will help the network administrators verify your identity and ensure a smooth integration into the research community. Please note that some fields are optional, and you can skip them if you're not comfortable providing that information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you enter your correct information, especially your email address and phone number.
3. If you're unsure about any field, try to provide a brief explanation or an approximation.
4. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form before leaving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (email, Required): Enter your active email address that you use to receive notifications.
* **Phone Number** (text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (text, Optional): Enter your mailing address or location.
* **Research Experience** (text, Optional): Briefly describe your research experience or relevant training.
* **Research Interests** (select_multiple, Optional): Indicate your interests in natural ingredient research by selecting "Yes" if you're interested in any of the areas listed.
* **Research Areas of Interest** (text, Optional): Provide a list of your research interests, if you have any.
* **Research Goals** (select_one, Optional): Answer "Yes" if you're interested in joining a research network.
* **Research Network ID** (number, Optional): Enter your unique Research Network ID, if you have one.
* **Institution** (text, Optional): Name your institution if you're affiliated with one.
* **Role** (select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" if you're currently involved with a research institution or organization.
* **Research Network** (text, Optional): Enter the name of your research network.
* **Join Date** (date, Optional): Enter the date you wish to join the network.
* **Time** (time, Optional): If you're available, select a time that suits your schedule for a meeting or discussion.
* **Notes** (note, Optional): Leave any additional comments or questions you have for the network administrators.
* **Confirm Email** (email, Optional): Enter your email address again to confirm it.

Please remember that some fields are optional, so you can skip them if you're not comfortable sharing that information. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us for assistance.
