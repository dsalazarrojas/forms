# Email Newsletter Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a simple email newsletter subscription form designed to collect users' contact information for the newsletter mailing list.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Signup Form" field to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
4. Confirm your email address by entering it again in the "Confirm Email" field.
5. Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Signup Form**: 
  * (No description, this is the main form section)
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you want to use to subscribe to the newsletter. Please make sure it is a valid and active email address.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the newsletter.
* **Confirm Email**: Enter your email address again to confirm that it is correct and to prevent spam and fake subscriptions.
