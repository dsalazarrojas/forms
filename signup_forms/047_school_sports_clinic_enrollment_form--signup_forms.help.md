# school_sports_clinic_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The school sports clinic enrollment form is used to enroll students in sports clinics provided by the school. This form is used to collect necessary information from students and parents to process enrollment requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the student's name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the student's date of birth.
3. Select the sports clinics the student wants to enroll in.
4. Enter the enrollment date.
5. Confirm the payment status.
6. Enter the school's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student's Name** (`Student's Name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Parent's Name** (`Parent's Name`, text, optional): Enter the parent/guardian's full name (if applicable).
* **Student's Email** (`Student's Email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, number, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Sports Clinics** (`Sports Clinics`, select_multiple, required): Select the sports clinics the student wants to enroll in. Choose all applicable options (e.g., Baseball, Basketball, Volleyball).
* **Enrollment Date** (`Enrollment Date`, date, required): Enter the date the student is enrolling in the sports clinic.
* **Student's Fee** (`Student's Fee`, number, required): Enter the student's fee for the sports clinic.
* **Fee Paid Status** (`Fee Paid Status`, select_one, required): Confirm if the fee has been paid or not.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, optional): Enter the school's name (if applicable).
* **School Email** (`School Email`, email, optional): Enter the school's email address (if applicable).
* **School Phone** (`School Phone`, number, optional): Enter the school's phone number (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_one, optional): Select the payment method used (Online Payment or Offline Payment).

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid any errors or missing information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the school for assistance.
