# Doula Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Intime Form is designed to collect user information and medical history from individuals seeking doula services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including any relevant details you are comfortable sharing.
2. Provide your birthdate.
3. Share your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
4. List your emergency contacts, including their names and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, or any other relevant information you are comfortable sharing with us.
* **Birthdate (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical History (note)**: Share any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or other health information that may impact your care.
* **Emergency Contacts (text)**: List the names and contact information (phone numbers and/or email) of individuals who can be reached in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have permission from your emergency contacts before sharing their information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is intended to be completed by individuals seeking doula services; please do not use it for any other purpose.
