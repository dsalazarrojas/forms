# Hi Fi Music Service Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hi Fi Music Service Membership Form is designed to collect your basic contact information and music service preferences to create a secure and personalized account for you to enjoy our music services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required fields.
2. Double-check that your email address is accurate and active.
3. Enter a valid password and confirm it for security purposes.
4. Select your preferred music service from the list provided.
5. Read and agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name 
- **First Name** (text, required)
  Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Last Name 
- **Last Name** (text, required)
  Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Email 
- **Email** (email, required)
  Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
### Confirm Email 
- **Confirm Email** (email, required)
  Re-enter your email address to verify its accuracy.
### Password 
- **Password** (text, required)
  Enter a strong password to secure your account.
### Confirm Password 
- **Confirm Password** (text, required)
  Re-enter your password to verify its accuracy.
### Phone Number 
- **Phone Number** (text, required)
  Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
### Music Service 
- **Music Service** (select_one, required)
  Select your preferred music service from the list provided:
  - Spotify
  - Apple Music
  - Google Play Music
### Other Music Service 
- **Other Music Service** (text, optional)
  If your preferred music service is not listed, please specify it here.
### Agree to Terms 
- **Agree to terms** (text, required)
  Read and agree to our terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Use a valid email address and a strong password.
- Check that your selected music service is correct.
- Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
- If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
