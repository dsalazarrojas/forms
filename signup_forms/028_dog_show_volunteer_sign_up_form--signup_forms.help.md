# Dog Show Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Show Volunteer Sign Up Form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to volunteer at a dog show event. This form is intended for external participants and volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the **full name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Provide your phone number in the **phone** field.
4. Select the days you are available to volunteer from the **availability** field.
5. Select the time of your availability in the **time** field.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can provide them in the **note** field.
7. Enter the name of the event you are interested in volunteering for in the **event name** field.
8. Enter the ID of the event you are interested in volunteering for in the **event ID** field.
9. Select the type of event you are interested in volunteering for in the **event type** field.
10. Click the **submit** button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **availability**: Select the days you are available to volunteer.
* **time**: Select the time of your availability.
* **note**: If you have any additional notes or comments, provide them here.
* **event name**: Enter the name of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **event ID**: Enter the ID of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **event type**: Select the type of event you are interested in volunteering for.
* **submit**: Click this button to submit your application and become a volunteer.
