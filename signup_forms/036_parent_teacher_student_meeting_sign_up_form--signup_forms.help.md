This form is designed to allow parents to sign up for parent-teacher-student meetings. The purpose of this form is to gather information from parents about their availability for meetings, which will be scheduled with teachers and students. This form is used by parents to communicate their availability and preferences for meetings, and it will be used by the school administration to schedule meetings that work for all parties involved.

# Parent Teacher Student Meeting Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps parents sign up for parent-teacher-student meetings with their child's teachers. Please complete the form below to provide your availability and preferences for these meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and your child's name.
2. Select a date for the meeting that works for you.
3. Choose a start and end time for the meeting.
4. Provide your contact email and phone number so we can reach you with meeting details.
5. Enter the teacher's name (if known).
6. Enter the meeting topic (if known).
7. Enter the meeting location (if known).
8. Add any additional notes about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name and your relationship to the student (e.g. mother, father, guardian).
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of your child who will be attending the meeting.
* **Meeting Date**: Select the date for the meeting using the calendar picker. This is the date you are available to meet with the teacher and student.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time for the meeting. You can select a start time using the clock picker.
* **End Time**: Choose the end time for the meeting. You can select an end time using the clock picker.
* **Parent Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with meeting details.
* **Parent Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with meeting details.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your child's teacher (if known).
* **Meeting Topic**: Enter a brief description of the meeting topic (if known).
* **Location**: Enter the location of the meeting (if known).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or requests about the meeting.

## Tips
* Please ensure you select a date and time that works for you and your child.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us and we'll be happy to assist you.
