# Student Ministry Retreat Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Ministry Retreat Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from students who are planning to attend a retreat. This form helps the organizers to gather essential details about each student, their parents' contact information, and any other relevant data required for a successful and enjoyable retreat experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Provide your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Enter the date of arrival and departure for the retreat in the `Date of Arrival` and `Date of Departure` fields, respectively.
5. Choose your grade level and grade section from the dropdown options in the `Grade` field.
6. Select the grade level and year you're currently in from the `Grade Level` and `Grade Year` fields.
7. Enter your grade class and teacher's name in the `Grade Class` and `Grade Teacher` fields.
8. List any relevant subjects you're currently taking in the `Grade Subjects` field.
9. Provide your parent's name, contact information, and relationship with you in the `Parent's Name`, `Parent's Name and Contact`, and `Parent's Relationship` fields.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the `Note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student. This is the name that will be used to address you during the retreat.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you during the retreat.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach out to you during the retreat.
* **Date of Arrival**: Enter the date you plan to arrive for the retreat.
* **Date of Departure**: Enter the date you plan to leave the retreat.
* **Grade**: Select your current grade level from the dropdown options (6th, 7th, 8th).
* **Grade Level**: Choose the grade level and year you're currently in from the dropdown options (6-8, 9-12, 9th, 10-12).
* **Grade Year**: Enter the current year you're in for your grade level.
* **Grade Class**: Enter your current grade class.
* **Grade Teacher**: Enter your grade teacher's name.
* **Grade Subjects**: List any relevant subjects you're currently taking.
* **Parent's Name**: Enter your parent's full name.
* **Parent's Name and Contact**: Provide your parent's name and contact information.
* **Parent's Relationship**: Explain how you're related to your parent (e.g., son, daughter).
* **Parent's Contact Phone**: Enter your parent's contact phone number.
* **Parent's Contact Email**: Enter your parent's email address.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the retreat.

Note: Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form, as some fields are required and cannot be left blank.
