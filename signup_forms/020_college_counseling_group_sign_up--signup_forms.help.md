# College Counseling Group Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in participating in college counseling groups.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Select your preferences for group counseling, including the type of groups you are interested in and your availability.
3. Provide any additional information or comments that you think would be helpful for the college counseling team to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (student_first_name): Your first name as it appears on your student ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (student_last_name): Your last name as it appears on your student ID or passport.
* **Student ID** (student_id): Your student ID number.
* **Email Address** (student_email): Your email address as it appears on your student email account.
* **Phone Number** (student_phone): Your phone number.
* **Class Year or Grade Level** (class_year): Select your current class year or grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate).
* **Interested in Group Counseling** (group_counseling_interest): Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in group counseling.
* **Type of Group Counseling of Interest** (group_preference): Select your primary interest for group counseling (Stress and Anxiety Management, Depression Support, Social Skills and Relationships, etc.).
* **Other Groups Interested In** (other_groups_interested): Select any additional groups you are interested in from the options provided.
* **Have you received counseling before** (previous_counseling): Select "Yes" if you have participated in individual or group counseling in the past.
* **Have you participated in group counseling before** (previous_group_experience): Select "Yes" if you have participated in group counseling in the past.
* **Preferred Meeting Days and Times** (schedule_preference): Select your preferred meeting days and times for group counseling.
* **Schedule Constraints or Limitations** (availability_constraints): Provide any schedule constraints or limitations that may affect your ability to attend group counseling.
* **Expected Commitment Level** (commitment_level): Select the length of time you are willing to commit to group counseling (4 weeks, 8 weeks, 12 weeks, etc.).
* **Primary Mental Health Concerns or Challenges** (mental_health_concerns): Provide a brief description of the primary mental health concerns or challenges that bring you to group counseling.
* **Goals for Group Counseling** (goals_for_counseling): Describe the goals you hope to achieve through group counseling.
* **Communication Preferences** (communication_preferences): Select how you would prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, Text Message, etc.).
* **Understand Group Confidentiality Guidelines** (confidentiality_understanding): Select "Yes" if you understand and agree to the group confidentiality guidelines.
* **Consent to Participate in Group Counseling** (consent_to_participate): Select "Yes" if you consent to participate in group counseling.
