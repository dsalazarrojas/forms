# Signup Form With Image Slider - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signup Form With Image Slider is a comprehensive form designed to collect information from users for sign-up purposes. This form helps you collect a variety of data such as name, email, password, confirm password, and other details like slider and date/time selections. By filling out this form, you'll be able to create a user profile.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the **Name** field with your full name as it appears on your documents. (Required)
2. Enter your **Email** address in the designated field. This must be a valid email address you own. (Required)
3. In the **Password** field, create a strong and unique password. This password will be used to secure your account. (Required)
4. Confirm your **Password** by re-entering it in the **Confirm** field to ensure accuracy.
5. The **Slider** field allows you to select one or more options from the available choices.
6. Choose the correct **Date** from the list of options (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday) for the purpose of this form.
7. Select the desired **Time** for your activity.
8. Optionally, you can add a brief **Note** about your sign-up purpose or any additional details.
9. If you have a specific **Email Option**, provide it in the designated field.
10. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field, if required.
11. For **Select One**, choose one option from the list.
12. In **Select Multiple**, choose multiple options from the available list.
13. Enter a number in the **Integer** field, if required.
14. Enter a number with decimal places in the **Decimal** field.
15. Enter the **Date/Time** according to your preference.
16. You have the option to **Upload** files from your device.
17. Select one or more files to upload from the available options.
18. Enter a range of dates in the **Date Range** field.
19. Enter a range of integers in the **Integer Range** field.
20. Lastly, select one or more dates from the list for the **Select One Date** field.
21. For **Select Multiple Dates**, choose multiple dates from the available list.
22. Enter a date and time in the **Date/Time** field.
