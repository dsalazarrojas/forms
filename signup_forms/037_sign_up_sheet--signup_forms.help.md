# Sign Up Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect volunteer sign-ups for an event or opportunity. Please complete the form to express your interest and provide the necessary information for us to consider your sign-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Choose your **Volunteer Role** from the available options or type it in if it's not listed.
4. Select your **Start Date** for the volunteer opportunity.
5. Choose your **End Date** for the volunteer opportunity.
6. Enter your **Time** availability for the volunteer opportunity.
7. Type in your **Location** if applicable.
8. Select your **Shift** (if applicable).
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your volunteer experience or any relevant information.
10. If you have a preferred **Assigned Tool**, choose one from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your complete name as it should be recorded.
* **Email**: Your email address for us to contact you.
* **Volunteer Role**: Choose your role or position within the organization or event (e.g., Team Leader, Team Member, etc.). If your role is not listed, please add it in the text field.
* **Start Date**: The date you can start volunteering.
* **End Date**: The date you plan to end your volunteering.
* **Time**: The time of day or shift you are available to volunteer.
* **Location**: If you are volunteering at a specific location, please type it in.
* **Shift**: If you are assigned a specific shift, choose it here.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about yourself or your experience that we should know.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose a tool that you have experience with or prefer to use during your volunteer opportunity.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *) to ensure your sign-up is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help, please do not hesitate to contact us.
