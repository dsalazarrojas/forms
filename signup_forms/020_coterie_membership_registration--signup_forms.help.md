# Coterie Membership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a member of Coterie. To ensure accurate and complete registration, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to provide all the required fields.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Type your email address correctly, following the standard email format (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
4. Select your date of birth in the correct mm/dd/yyyy format.
5. Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-1234.
6. Type your address in the text format.
7. Choose your preferred membership level from the options.
8. Select your membership status from the options.
9. Enter your application date in the correct mm/dd/yyyy format.
10. Choose your application type from the options.
11. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the standard email format (e.g., `username@domain.com`).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-1234.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Type your address in the text format.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred membership level from the options.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_multiple, required): Select your membership status from the options.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter your application date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Choose your application type from the options.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): If necessary, add any additional notes about your application.
