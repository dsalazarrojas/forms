# Military Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in military programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Be accurate and truthful when providing your contact information.
3. If you are representing an organization, ensure you have permission to provide contact information on behalf of the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Military ID** (`military_id`, number, required): Please enter your military ID number.
* **Branch of Military Service** (`branch_of_military_service`, select_one, optional): Please select the branch of military service you are interested in. Options include Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Other.
* **Date of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, date, required): Please enter the date you are interested in the military program.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Please select how you would like to be contacted. Options include Phone, Email, or In-person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Please enter your preferred phone number for contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Contact In Person** (`contact_in_person`, text, optional): Please enter your preferred contact method for in-person interactions. (Note: This field is optional and only required if you selected In-person as your contact method.)
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your school or organization.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of your school or organization.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Please enter the address of your school or organization.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of your school or organization.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of your school or organization.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): Please enter the website of your school or organization.
* **Training Provider Name** (`training_provider_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Location** (`training_provider_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Phone** (`training_provider_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Email** (`training_provider_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the training provider.
* **Outreach Team Name** (`outreach_team_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the outreach team.
* **Outreach Team Location** (`outreach_team_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of the outreach team.
* **Outreach Team Phone** (`outreach_team_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of the outreach team.
* **Outreach Team Email** (`outreach_team_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of the outreach team.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information that may be helpful for us to know.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Please click the submit button to complete the form.
