# WordPress Website Maintenance Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you subscribe to our website maintenance services. Please fill in the form with your information and preferences to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Choose your Payment Method (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
3. Select your Payment Frequency (Weekly, Monthly, or Annually).
4. Choose the Plan Duration (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, 24 Months, or Custom).
5. Select the Maintenance Services you require (Website Updates, Backups, Website Security, Website Speed, Performance, SEO, Content Delivery Network (CDN), or Website Monitoring).
6. Fill in the Agency Name and Agency Name 2 (if applicable).
7. Choose your Contact Method (Phone, Email, or Live Chat).
8. Fill in the Contact Value.
9. Fill in the Website URL and Website URL 2 (if applicable).
10. Choose the Website Type (WordPress, HTML, PHP, or Other).
11. Fill in the Plan Price.
12. Choose the Subscription Status (Active, Trial, Inactive, or Expired).
13. Add any additional Notes.
14. (Optional) Fill in your Payment Frequency 2, Custom Plan Duration, and Payment Plan Start and End Dates (if applicable).
15. (Optional) Fill in the Agency Name 2 and Website Type 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (3): Fill in your valid email address.
* **Payment Method** (4): Choose how you want to pay for the maintenance services: Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Frequency** (5): Select how often you want to pay for the maintenance services: Weekly, Monthly, or Annually.
* **Plan Duration** (6): Choose the duration of your maintenance plan: 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, 24 Months, or Custom.
* **Maintenance Services** (7): Select the maintenance services you require: Website Updates, Backups, Website Security, Website Speed, Performance, SEO, Content Delivery Network (CDN), or Website Monitoring.
* **Agency Name** (8): Fill in the name of the agency handling your website's maintenance.
* **Agency Name 2** (22): Fill in the name of the agency handling your website's maintenance (if applicable).
* **Contact Method** (9): Choose how you want to contact the agency: Phone, Email, or Live Chat.
* **Contact Value** (10): Fill in the contact value.
* **Website URL** (11): Fill in the URL of your website.
* **Website URL 2** (24): Fill in the URL of your second website (if applicable).
* **Website Type** (12): Choose the type of your website: WordPress, HTML, PHP, or Other.
* **Website Type 2** (25): Choose the type of your second website (if applicable).
* **Plan Price** (13): Fill in the price of your maintenance plan.
* **Subscription Status** (14): Choose the status of your subscription: Active, Trial, Inactive, or Expired.
* **Notes** (15): Add any additional notes.
* **Payment Frequency 2** (16): (Optional) Select how often you want to pay for the maintenance services: Weekly, Monthly, or Annually.
* **Custom Plan Duration** (17): (Optional) Choose the duration of your maintenance plan: 1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 12 Months, 24 Months.
* **Payment Plan Start** (18): (Optional) Fill in the start date of your payment plan.
* **Payment Plan End** (19): (Optional) Fill in the end date of your payment plan.
* **Contact Method 2** (23): (Optional) Choose how you want to contact the agency: Phone, Email, or Live Chat.
* **Contact Value 2** (24): (Optional) Fill in the contact value.
* **Notes** (17): Add any additional notes.

Note: Some fields have been marked as optional. If you're unsure or need more information about any field, please don't hesitate to contact the agency for assistance.
