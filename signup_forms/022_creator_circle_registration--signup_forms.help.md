# creator_circle_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from creators who want to join a creator circle, a community of creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your creator details such as your name, email, and description.
2. Upload any necessary attachments, videos, or documents.
3. Select your creator type and category.
4. Choose your keywords and social media links.
5. Add any review notes and notes for onboard process.
6. Upload your profile picture and cover picture.
7. Select the background picture and video.
8. Upload audio files and documents for onboard process.
9. Choose the onboard date and time.
10. Finally, submit the form to complete the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creator Details (1)**: Enter your creator name, email, and description to introduce yourself.
* **Review Application (2)**: Provide any review application or notes related to your creator profile.
* **Onboard Member (3)**: Enter any notes or information related to the onboard process.
* **Creator Program (4)**: Enter any information related to your creator program or affiliation.
* **Contact Info (5)**: Enter your contact information such as phone number and address.
* **Review Status (6)**: Enter your review status or any notes related to it.
* **Onboarding Status (7)**: Enter your onboard status or any notes related to it.
* **Creator Type (8)**: Select your creator type, e.g., artist, writer, musician, etc.
* **Creator Category (9)**: Select your creator category, e.g., fiction, non-fiction, comedy, etc.
* **Creator Keywords (10)**: Enter any keywords related to your creators work or profile.
* **Website (11)**: Enter your website link.
* **Social Media (12)**: Enter your social media links.
* **Review Notes (13)**: Enter any review notes or feedback.
* **Onboard Notes (14)**: Enter any onboard notes or instructions.
* **Profile Picture (15)**: Upload your profile picture.
* **Cover Picture (16)**: Upload your cover picture.
* **Background Picture (17)**: Upload your background picture.
* **Video (18)**: Upload a video related to your creator profile.
* **Audio File (19)**: Upload an audio file related to your creator profile.
* **Document Upload (20)**: Upload any documents related to your creator profile.
* **Attachment (21)**: Upload any attachment related to your creator profile.
* **Onboard Attachment (22)**: Upload any attachment related to the onboard process.
* **Video Transcription (23)**: Upload a transcription of your video.
* **Onboard Date (24)**: Enter the date you want to onboard.
* **Onboard Time (25)**: Enter the time you want to onboard.
