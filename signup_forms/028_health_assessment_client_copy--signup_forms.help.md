# Health Assessment Client Copy - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Assessment Client Copy form is used to collect information about a client's health status, including medical history, allergies, medications, and other relevant health information. This form is designed to be completed by the client themselves or by a healthcare professional on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the client's health status.
2. For select_multiple fields, please select all applicable options that apply to the client's situation.
3. If a field is not relevant to the client's situation, please leave it blank or indicate that it does not apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (id: 1, type: text): Please provide the client's name, date of birth, and other relevant demographic information.
* **Medical History** (id: 2, type: text): Please provide a brief description of the client's medical history, including any significant medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations.
* **Medications** (id: 3, type: select_multiple): Please select all medications the client is currently taking, including prescribed medications, over-the-counter medications, and supplements.
* **Medical Conditions** (id: 4, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical conditions the client has been diagnosed with, such as diabetes, hypertension, etc.
* **Allergies** (id: 5, type: text): Please provide a list of the client's known allergies, including any food, medication, or environmental allergies.
* **Medical Procedures** (id: 6, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical procedures the client has undergone, such as surgeries, hospitalizations, etc.
* **Medical Tests** (id: 7, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical tests the client has undergone, such as blood work, imaging studies, etc.
* **Medical Notes** (id: 8, type: text): Please provide any additional medical notes or comments about the client's health status.
* **Family History** (id: 9, type: text): Please provide information about the client's family medical history, including any family members with significant medical conditions.
* **Health Assess** (id: 10, type: text): Please provide a brief description of the client's current health status.
* **Health Assesment** (id: 11, type: text): This field is a duplicate of "Health Assess" and can be skipped.
* **Risk Factors** (id: 12, type: select_multiple): Please select all risk factors that apply to the client's health status, such as smoking, obesity, etc.
* **Client Goals** (id: 13, type: text): Please provide a brief description of the client's goals and aspirations related to their health.
* **Mental Health History** (id: 14, type: text): Please provide information about the client's mental health history, including any diagnoses or treatment.
* **Mental Health Notes** (id: 15, type: text): Please provide any additional mental health notes or comments about the client's mental health status.
* **Treatment Plans** (id: 16, type: text): Please provide any information about the client's current treatment plans, including medication regimens and therapy schedules.
* **Client Caregiver Info** (id: 17, type: text): Please provide information about the client's caregiver or family members who may be involved in their care.
* **Medical Team** (id: 18, type: text): Please provide a list of the client's healthcare providers, including physicians, specialists, and therapists.
* **Medical History Review** (id: 19, type: text): This field is for the healthcare provider's review of the client's medical history.
* **Medication Side Effects** (id: 20, type: text): Please provide information about any side effects of the client's medications.
* **Medical Condition List** (id: 21, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical conditions that apply to the client's health status.
* **Allergies List** (id: 22, type: select_multiple): Please select all allergies that apply to the client's health status.
* **Medical Procedures List** (id: 23, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical procedures that apply to the client's health status.
* **Medical Tests List** (id: 24, type: select_multiple): Please select all medical tests that apply to the client's health status.
* **Client Caregiver Info List** (id: 25, type: select_multiple): Please select all caregivers or family members who may be involved in the client's care.

## Tips
* Please fill in all fields that apply to the client's health status.
* If a field is not relevant, please leave it blank or indicate that it does not apply.
* Please ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
