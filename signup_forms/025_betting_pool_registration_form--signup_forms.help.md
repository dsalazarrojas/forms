# Betting Pool Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants to join a betting pool and provide necessary information to facilitate their involvement in the pool.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. We will use this email for pool updates.
3.  If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and phone number.
4.  Choose the type of betting pool you are joining from the "Pool Type" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the name of the betting pool you are joining in the "Betting Pool Name" field.
6.  Select your payment method and status for the entry fee in the "Payment Method" and "Payment Status" fields.
7.  Select the team or player you are betting on in the "Team or Player Selection" field.
8.  If applicable, upload or describe your bracket picks in the "Bracket Submission" field.
9.  Agree to follow the pool rules and terms in the "I Have Read and Accept the Pool Rules" field.
10.  If you are participating in a liability waiver, please select "Yes, I Agree" in the "Liability Waiver" field.
11.  Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (participant_name)** (Required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be recognized in the pool.
* **Email Address (participant_email)** (Required): Enter your email address where you will receive pool updates and other notifications.
* **Phone Number (participant_phone)** (Optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their phone number.
* **Betting Pool Name (pool_name)** (Required): Enter the name of the betting pool you are joining.
* **Pool Type (pool_type)** (Required): Choose the type of betting pool you are joining from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Name (event_name)** (Required): Enter the name of the event this pool is for.
* **Event Date (event_date)** (Required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Entry Fee Amount (entry_fee)** (Required): Enter the amount you are paying to enter the pool.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)** (Required): Choose how you are paying the entry fee from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Status (payment_status)** (Required): Select the status of your payment.
* **Team or Player Selection (team_selection)** (Required): Select the team or player you are betting on.
* **Predicted Score (predicted_score)** (Optional): If you wish to predict a score, enter it here.
* **Bracket Submission (bracket_submission)** (Optional): If applicable, upload or describe your bracket picks here.
* **I Have Read and Accept the Pool Rules (pool_rules_accepted)** (Required): Agree to follow the pool rules and terms.
* **Liability Waiver (liability_waiver)** (Required): Select "Yes, I Agree" to participate in liability waiver.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact)** (Optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)** (Optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their phone number.
* **How Did You Hear About This Pool (how_heard_about)** (Optional): Select where you heard about this pool.
* **T-Shirt Size (t_shirt_size)** (Optional): If prizes include apparel, select your T-shirt size.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or information, if necessary.
