# Holiday Discount Challenge Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Discount Challenge Sign Up" form is designed to help users sign up for a holiday-specific discount or promotion. This form collects information from users, including their email address, phone number, and other details, to help them participate in the holiday season activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to participate in the "Yes" or "No" option.
2. Enter your email address in the required email format.
3. Enter your phone number in the required format (numbers only, no symbols or special characters).
4. Enter a unique form ID, which is a numeric value.
5. Choose a category for your sign up (e.g., "Signup Forms", "Other").
6. Provide a description of your sign up, if desired.
7. Select an assigned tool for your sign up, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Discount Challenge Sign Up**: This is the title of the form, which indicates the purpose of the form.
* **Yes/No**: This field asks whether you want to participate in the holiday discount challenge. Select "Yes" if you are interested, and "No" otherwise.
* **Email Address**: This field is for your email address, which is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which is required for contact purposes.
* **Form ID**: This field is a unique identifier for your sign up, which is required for tracking purposes.
* **Category**: This field asks you to choose a category for your sign up (e.g., "Signup Forms", "Other").
* **Description**: This field is optional and allows you to provide a brief description of your sign up.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is optional and allows you to select an assigned tool for your sign up.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your sign up is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the support team for assistance.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
