# Arts And Craft Fair Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you and your business better, so we can better understand your needs and provide the best service to you. Please provide accurate and complete information so we can process your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Vendor or Business Name**, the **Primary Contact Person**, and **Email Address**. Ensure that these details are accurate and up-to-date.
2. Choose your **Booth Size Preference** and indicate if you require electricity or not. Please note that an additional fee may apply if you need electricity.
3. Select your **Primary Craft Category** and **Secondary Categories** (if applicable) from the provided options. Be sure to choose the most relevant category for your business.
4. Provide the **Years in Business** to give us an idea of your level of experience in the craft industry.
5. **Upload Product Photos** of your products to showcase what you offer.
6. Choose the **Fair Dates** you are available to participate in.
7. Briefly describe your **Previous Fair Experience** (if applicable).
8. Indicate if you **Have Liability Insurance** or not.
9. Enter any **Special Requests or Comments** you have, if any.
10. Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Terms of Agreement** by selecting "Yes".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor or Business Name** (id: 1): Enter your business name or your artist name as it is registered. This will be the name that appears on all marketing materials and promotional items.
* **Primary Contact Person** (id: 2): Enter the name of the person we should contact regarding your participation.
* **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you regarding your application and fair arrangements.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Business Address** (id: 5): Enter your full mailing address.
* **Primary Craft Category** (id: 6): Select the main category that your business falls under (e.g., Jewelry, Pottery, etc.). This will help us understand what you sell.
* **Secondary Categories** (id: 7): Select any other categories that your business also falls under (if applicable).
* **Years in Business** (id: 8): Indicate how many years you have been selling your crafts.
* **Booth Size Preference** (id: 9): Choose the preferred size of your booth.
* **Do You Need Electricity** (id: 10): Indicate if you require electricity for your display or activities.
* **Website or Online Shop URL** (id: 11): Enter your website or online shop URL so we can see your products online.
* **Upload Product Photos** (id: 12): Upload images of your products to showcase what you offer.
* **Which Fair Dates Interest You** (id: 13): Select the fair dates you are available to participate in.
* **Have You Participated in Craft Fairs Before** (id: 14): Briefly describe your experience in participating in craft fairs (if applicable).
* **Do You Have Liability Insurance** (id: 15): Indicate if you have liability insurance, and be prepared to provide proof upon request.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (id: 16): Enter any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Application Date** (id: 17): Enter the date you are submitting this application.
* **I Agree to Fair Rules and Regulations** (id: 18): Confirm that you have read and agree to all terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".
