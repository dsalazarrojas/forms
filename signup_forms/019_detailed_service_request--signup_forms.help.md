# detailed-service-request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "detailed-service-request" form is designed to gather comprehensive information from users for their service requests. This form helps us understand their needs and preferences, which enables us to provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of service you are requesting (Service Type).
2. Enter your name (Name) for reference purposes.
3. Choose a date for the service (Service Date).
4. Specify the time for the service (Service Time).
5. Provide a detailed description of your service request (Service Description).
6. Enter your contact number (Contact Number) so we can get back to you.
7. Enter your email address (Email) for communication purposes.
8. Mention the service provider or details (Service Provider) if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one): Select the type of service you are requesting. This field has the following options:
	+ Haircut
	+ Car fix
	+ Other
* **Name** (`name`, text): Enter your name for reference purposes. This field is not mandatory but helps us know who is making the request.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date): Choose a date for the service. This date will be used to schedule the service if necessary.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time): Specify the time for the service. This field can be left blank if your service does not have specific timing requirements.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text): Give us a detailed description of your service request so we can better understand your needs.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your phone number or contact information so we can get back to you for further details or to confirm the service.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you regarding your service request.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text): Mention any specific service provider or details if applicable.

## Tips
Please ensure you enter accurate and non-duplicate information in this form. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us for assistance.
