# 2021 2023 NPHC LC Officers Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 2021 2023 NPHC LC Officers Nomination Form is designed to collect information for nominating individuals for leadership positions within the NPHC (National Panhellenic Greek Council) local chapter. This form is used to gather details about the nominee, their experience, and the nominator's endorsement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Nominee Information section**: Enter the full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable) of the individual being nominated.
2. **Select the Nominee's Current Status**: Choose the category that best describes the nominee's affiliation with the NPHC local chapter.
3. **Specify the Position Nominated For**: Select the leadership position for which the nominee is being considered, or choose "Other" if a different office is intended and provide the exact title.
4. **Enter Leadership Experience and Previous Offices Held**: Provide a brief summary of the nominee's leadership experience and any previous positions they have held within the NPHC local chapter.
5. **Write a Supporting Statement**: Submit a supporting statement from the nominator or nominee, explaining why they are suitable for the position (recommended 150-250 words).
6. **Nominate Information**: Enter the full name, email address, and phone number (if applicable) of the person submitting the nomination.
7. **Confirm the Nominee's Consent**: Select whether the nominee has consented to be nominated.
8. **Additional Comments or Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`): Enter the full legal name as it should appear on records.
* **Email address** (`email_address`): Enter the primary contact email for nomination communications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): Include country code if outside the U.S.
* **Current status** (`current_status`): Choose the category that best describes the nominee's affiliation with the NPHC local chapter.
* **Position nominated for** (`position_nominated`): Select one from the list, or choose "Other" if a different office is intended and provide the exact title.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the nominee.
* **Membership years** (`membership_years`): Enter whole years of affiliation with the NPHC local chapter.
* **Supporting statement** (`supporting_statement`): Submit a supporting statement from the nominator or nominee, explaining why they are suitable for the position (recommended 150-250 words).
* **Nominator full name** (`nominator_full_name`): Enter the full name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator email address** (`nominator_email`): Enter the email address of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Nominator phone number** (`nominator_phone`): Optional secondary contact.
* **Nomination date** (`nomination_date`): Enter the date of the nomination submission.
* **Availability conflicts** (`availability_conflicts`): Select if the nominee has time conflicts or restrictions.
* **Nominee consent** (`consent_for_nomination`): Confirm whether the nominee has consented to be nominated.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`): Optional comments or notes, if applicable.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
