# PDF Download Newsletter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PDF Download Newsletter Form is a form used to collect contact information and newsletter preferences from users to create a mailing list for our newsletters.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Fill in your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use the format `example@email.com`.
4. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field, if applicable (not required).
5. Select your preferred newsletter by clicking on the **Newsletter** field.
6. Enter the date for which you would like to receive the newsletter in the **Date** field.
7. Optionally, select the time you would like to receive the newsletter in the **Time** field.
8. You may add any notes or comments in the **Note** field, if needed.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field, if needed.
10. Fill in your second email address in the **Email2** field, if you have multiple email addresses.
11. Fill in your second phone number in the **Phone2** field, if applicable.
12. Repeat steps 10 and 11 for **Email3**, **Phone3**, **Email4**, **Phone4**, **Email5**, **Phone5**, **Email6**, **Phone6**, **Email7**, **Phone7**, **Email8**, **Phone8**, **Email9**, and **Phone9** fields.
