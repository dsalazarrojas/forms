# Holiday Gift Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Holiday Gift Sign Up Form" is a tool used to collect information from individuals who wish to sign up for a holiday gift, allowing organizers to track and manage the gift list. This form helps to gather details such as category preferences, contact information, and gift choices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the "Category" you prefer for your gift (e.g., yes or no).
2. Enter your "Name" and "Email" in the respective fields.
3. Add any additional "Message" or notes you'd like to include with your sign-up.
4. Specify the "Number Of Guests" you're signing up for.
5. Select the "Gift" you'd like to receive (e.g., Apple, Orange, or Grapes).
6. Enter the "Date" you'd like to receive your gift.
7. Choose the "Time" you'd like to receive your gift.
8. Add any additional "Note" or comments.
9. Enter your "Phone" number for contact purposes.
10. Indicate if you've been "Assigned" a gift (yes or no).
11. Select the "Assigned Tool" you'd like to be assigned (yes or no).
12. Click "Submit" to complete your sign-up.
13. Optionally, you can add a "Form ID" for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Yes/No):** Choose whether you'd like to sign up in a specific category or not. This helps the organizer track gift distribution.
* **Name:** Enter your name as you'd like to be listed in the gift registry.
* **Email:** Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **Message (optional):** Add any additional details or notes about your sign-up.
* **Number Of Guests:** Specify the number of guests you're signing up for the holiday gift.
* **Gift (Apple/Orange/Grapes):** Select the gift you'd prefer to receive.
* **Date:** Enter the date you'd like to receive your gift.
* **Time:** Choose the time you'd like to receive your gift.
* **Note (optional):** Add any additional comments or details about your sign-up.
* **Phone:** Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned (Yes/No):** Indicate if you've been assigned a gift already.
* **Assigned Tool (Yes/No):** Select if you'd like to be assigned a specific tool.
* **Submit:** Click to complete your sign-up and submit your request.
* **Form ID (optional):** Enter a reference ID for your sign-up, if desired.
