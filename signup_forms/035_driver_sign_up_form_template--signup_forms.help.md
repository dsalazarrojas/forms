# Driver Sign Up Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect information from drivers for sign-up purposes. It can be used in various industries, such as transportation, delivery services, or ride-sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page of the form, which includes **Driver Info**, **Contact Preferences**, **Address**, and **Date and Time**.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided in the **Contact Preferences** page.
4. Fill in your address in the **Address** page.
5. Enter your availability in the **Date and Time** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Driver Info
* **Driver Info** (driver_info, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your driver information, including your name, license number, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Preferences
* **Contact Method* (contact_preferences, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to choose your preferred contact method. You can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Email
	+ Phone

### Address
* **Address** (address, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your address, which will be used for communication and other purposes.

### Date and Time
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time, text, required/optional): This field is for you to indicate your availability, including your preferred date and time for work or service.
