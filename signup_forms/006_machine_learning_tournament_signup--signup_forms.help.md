# Machine Learning Tournament Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Machine Learning Tournament Signup form.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Fill in Tournament Details
1. The first step is to fill in the **Tournament Name** field, which is the name of the tournament you are signing up for.
2. Next, fill in the **Participant Name**, which is your name as a participant in the tournament.
3. Enter your **Participant Email**, which is your contact email address.
4. Fill in your **Participant Phone** number, which is your contact phone number.

### Step 2: Team Details
1. If you are signing up as a team, fill in the **Team Name** field with your team's name.
2. If you are signing up with a team, list the team members in the **Team Members** field. You can select up to 4 team members.
3. Fill in the **Team Member 1**, **Team Member 2**, **Team Member 3**, and **Team Member 4** fields with the names of your team members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, required)
* This field is used to identify the tournament you are signing up for.
### 2. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required)
* This field is used to store your name as a participant in the tournament.
### 3. **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required)
* This is your contact email address.
### 4. **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required)
* This is your contact phone number.
### 5. **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required for teams)
* This field is used to store your team's name, if you are signing up as a team.
### 6. **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required for teams)
* This field allows you to select up to 4 team members. Select "Yes" for each member who is part of your team.
### 7. **Team Member 1** (`team_member_1`, text, required for teams)
* This field is used to store the name of your first team member.
### 8. **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, text, required for teams)
* This field is used to store the name of your second team member.
### 9. **Team Member 3** (`team_member_3`, text, required for teams)
* This field is used to store the name of your third team member.
### 10. **Team Member 4** (`team_member_4`, text, required for teams)
* This field is used to store the name of your fourth team member.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, including Participant Name, Participant Email, and Participant Phone.
* If you are signing up as a team, make sure to fill in the Team Name and Team Members fields correctly.
* Double-check your team composition and member details before submitting the form.
