# Mobile Experience Waitlist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us determine your interest in getting early access to our mobile experience. By filling out this form, you'll be added to our waitlist and notified when it becomes available.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the basic information on Page 1:
	* **Email** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_5): Enter your email address to get notified when our mobile experience becomes available.
	* **Phone** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_6): Enter your phone number for further contact.
	* **Device Type** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_7): Select your device type (Apple, Samsung, or Google).
2. On Page 2, select your preferences:
	* **Early Access** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_3): Check "Yes" if you're interested in getting early access to our mobile experience.
	* **Waitlist** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_4): Select all the options that apply to you.
	* **Preferred Platform** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_8): Choose your preferred platform (Android, iOS, or Desktop).
3. Fill out the required fields to confirm your interest:
	* **Terms of Agreement** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_10): Read and agree to our terms of agreement.
	* **Privacy Policy** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_11): Read and agree to our privacy policy.
4. Finally, provide some additional details:
	* **Early Access List** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_12): Indicate when you'd like to be added to the early access list.
	* **Early Access Quantity** (mobile_experience_waitlist_form_13): Enter the quantity you're interested in for early access.
