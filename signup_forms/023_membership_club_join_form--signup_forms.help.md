# membership_club_join_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The membership club join form is used to collect information from users interested in joining the membership club. It helps to create a new member account and provides necessary information for the user to become a member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, and **Phone Number** on the first page.
2. Select your **Payment Method** (Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal) on the second page.
3. If you selected **Card** as your payment method, fill in your **Card Brand** (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex) and **Card Number** on the third page.
4. On the fourth page, select your **Amount** to be paid.
5. On the fifth page, **Read and accept the Terms of Service** by filling in the text field.
6. On the last page, fill in your **Date of Birth**, **Time of Birth**, **Address**, **Zip Code**, **Country of Residence**, and **State of Residence**.
7. Select your **City of Residence** from the options provided.
8. Fill in your **Zip Code 2** and select your **Country of Residence 2**, **State of Residence 2**, and **City of Residence 2**.
9. Finally, click on the **Submit** button to complete your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for your membership (Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Card Brand**: If you selected **Card** as your payment method, select the brand of your card (Visa, Mastercard, or Amex).
* **Card Number**: If you selected **Card** as your payment method, enter your card number.
* **Amount**: Select the amount you would like to pay for your membership.
* **Terms of Service**: Read and accept the terms of service by filling in the text field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 02/12/1990).
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the format of 12:00 AM.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address where you reside.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code where you reside.
* **Country of Residence**: Select your country of residence (United States, Canada, or Mexico).
* **State of Residence**: Select your state of residence (Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, etc.).
* **City of Residence**: Select your city of residence (New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, etc.).
* **Zip Code 2**: Enter your zip code 2.
* **Country of Residence 2**: Select your country of residence 2 (Yes or No).
* **State of Residence 2**: Select your state of residence 2 (Yes or No).
* **City of Residence 2**: Select your city of residence 2 (Yes or No).
