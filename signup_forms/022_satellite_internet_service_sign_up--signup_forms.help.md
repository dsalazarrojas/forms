# Satellite Internet Service Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to sign up for satellite internet service. Please fill out the form to express your interest and we will follow up with you to confirm your details and schedule an installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields. The form is divided into sections with clear labels for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Please enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Please enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Please enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): Please enter your zip code.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Please select the type of service you are interested in, options are Satellite, Fiber, and Wireless.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, date, required/optional): Please select the date you would like to have the installation scheduled.
* **Installation Time** (`installation_time`, time, required/optional): Please select the time you would like to have the installation scheduled.
* **Installation Address** (`installation_address`, text, required/optional): Please enter the address where you would like the installation to be done.
* **Installer Name** (`installer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the installer who will be doing the installation.
* **Installer Phone** (`installer_phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter the phone number of the installer.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the status of your sign up, options are Pending, Approved, and Rejected.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Please enter any comments you may have.

Note: Some fields are optional, please only fill out the required fields.
