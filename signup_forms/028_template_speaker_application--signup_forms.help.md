# Template Speaker Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who wish to become a template speaker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the User Name field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the User Email field.
3. Enter your speaker name in the Speaker Name field.
4. Enter a brief description of yourself in the Speaker Bio field.
5. Enter a URL or image name for your Speaker Image field.
6. Enter your Speaker Social Link.
7. Select your Speaker Category from the list of options.
8. Enter your Speaker Website, if applicable.
9. Enter your phone number in the Speaker Phone field.
10. Enter a valid email address for your Speaker Email.
11. Enter a brief description of yourself in the Speaker Bio field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Control** (`form_control`, `text`, required: false): This field is a placeholder text field used for form control. You can ignore this field for now.
* **User Name** (`name_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **User Email** (`email_input`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for your user account.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter your speaker name.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself as a speaker.
* **Speaker Image** (`speaker_image_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL or image name for your speaker image.
* **Speaker Social Link** (`speaker_social_link_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter a URL for your social link.
* **Speaker Category** (`speaker_category_input`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your speaker category from the list of options.
* **Speaker Website** (`speaker_website_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter your speaker website, if applicable.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter your speaker phone number.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid email address for your speaker account.
* **Speaker Bio** (`speaker_bio_input`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself as a speaker.
