# Athletic Signing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Signing Form is used to gather essential information from athletes and their parents or guardians to ensure a smooth and safe participation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") as completely as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your parent or guardian for help.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level from the options provided.
* **Sport** (`sport`, select_one, required): Select your primary sport from the options provided.
* **Team Level** (`team_level`, select_one, required): Select your competition level from the options provided.
* **Season** (`season`, select_one, required): Select which season you plan to participate in.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, text, required): Enter your current academic year.
* **Jersey Number Preference** (`jersey_number_preference`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your preferred jersey number.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter your health insurance provider's name.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, text, required): Enter your insurance policy or member number.
* **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your regular doctor.
* **Primary Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter your doctor's contact phone number.
* **Physical Examination Date** (`physical_exam_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your sports physical examination.
* **Medical Clearance Status** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required): Select the status of your medical clearance.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to follow the athletic code of conduct.
* **Sportsmanship Agreement** (`sportsmanship_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to demonstrate good sportsmanship.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept the risks of athletic participation.
* **Medical Treatment Permission** (`medical_treatment_permission`, select_one, required): Grant permission for emergency medical treatment.
* **Media Release** (`media_release`, select_one, required): Grant permission for photos and media coverage.
* **Commitment Statement** (`commitment_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment to giving your best effort throughout the season.
* **Athlete Signature** (`athlete_signature`, text, required): Sign your name with your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as your parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your signature.
