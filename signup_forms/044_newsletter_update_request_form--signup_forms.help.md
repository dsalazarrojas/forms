# Newsletter Update Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Newsletter Update Request Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your subscriber email address in the "Subscriber Email" field, which is a required field.
2. In the "Name" field, enter your full name, if desired.
3. In the "Newsletter" field, select the newsletter option you would like to receive updates about.
4. In the "Unsubscribe" field, select the reason(s) you would like to unsubscribe from the newsletter.
5. In the "Unsubscribed Comment" field, enter a brief reason for unsubscribing, if desired.
6. In the "Subscribe" field, select the newsletter option(s) you would like to subscribe to.
7. In the "Subscribe Comment" field, enter a brief reason for subscribing, if desired.
8. In the "Email" field, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subscriber Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that you used to sign up for our newsletter.
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Newsletter** (select_one, optional): Select the newsletter you would like to receive updates about.
* **Unsubscribe** (select_multiple, optional): Select the reason(s) you would like to unsubscribe from the newsletter.
* **Unsubscribed Comment** (text, optional): Enter a brief reason for unsubscribing.
* **Subscribe** (select_multiple, optional): Select the newsletter option(s) you would like to subscribe to.
* **Subscribe Comment** (text, optional): Enter a brief reason for subscribing.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that you would like to associate with your request.
