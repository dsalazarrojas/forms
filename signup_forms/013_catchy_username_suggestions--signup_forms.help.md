# Catchy Username Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your preferences for catchy username suggestions. Please fill out the form below to provide us with the necessary information to create a username that fits your style and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. For each field, provide as much information as you feel necessary.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your preferences to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`user_name`): Your real name or nickname.
* **Email Address** (`email`): The email address where you want to receive the suggestions.
* **Platform or Use Case** (`platform`): Select the platform or use case where you plan to use the username (e.g., Gaming, Social Media, Professional, etc.).
* **Preferred Style** (`preferred_style`): Select the style you prefer for your username (e.g., Cool and Edgy, Fun and Playful, Professional, etc.).
* **Favorite Words or Themes** (`favorite_words`): Enter any words or themes that you are interested in or that are related to your hobbies or interests.
* **Favorite Numbers** (`favorite_numbers`): Enter any numbers that hold significance to you or that you like.
* **Include Numbers in Username** (`include_numbers`): Select whether you want numbers included in your username.
* **Include Underscores or Special Characters** (`include_underscores`): Select whether you want underscores or special characters in your username.
* **Preferred Username Length** (`username_length`): Select the length of your preferred username (e.g., Short, Medium, Long, etc.).
* **Alliteration Preference** (`alliteration_preference`): Select whether you like repeating sounds in your username.
* **Favorite Animal** (`animal_preference`): Enter your favorite animal (if applicable).
* **Favorite Color** (`color_preference`): Enter your favorite color (if applicable).
* **Usernames You Already Tried** (`existing_usernames`): Enter any usernames that have already been taken.
* **How Many Suggestions Needed** (`quantity_needed`): Select the number of suggestions you want.
* **Preferred Tone** (`tone`): Select the tone you prefer for your username (e.g., Serious, Funny, Mysterious, etc.).
* **Words to Avoid** (`avoid_words`): Enter any words that you do not want in your username.
* **Inspiration Sources** (`inspiration_sources`): Enter any movies, books, games, or other sources that inspire your username.
* **Additional Preferences** (`additional_preferences`): Enter any other requirements or preferences you have for your username.

Tips:

* Please provide as much information as you can to help us create a username that suits your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will do our best to create a username that meets your requirements.
