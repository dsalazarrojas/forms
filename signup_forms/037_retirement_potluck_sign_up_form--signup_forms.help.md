# Retirement Potluck Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who plan to attend a retirement potluck event. By filling out this form, you will help the organizers plan a successful and enjoyable event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the **Name** and **Email** fields, respectively.
2. Select the type of potluck you plan to attend (e.g. Potluck, BBQ, Party).
3. Indicate how many guests you will be bringing with you in the **Number of Guests** field.
4. Choose the **Potluck Location** (e.g. Office, Home).
5. Provide a specific date and time for the event in the **Date** field.
6. Enter the quantity of the dish you plan to bring in the **Quantity** field.
7. Choose the dishes you plan to bring (e.g. Salad, Fruits, Sandwiches) in the **Potluck Dishes** field.
8. Indicate how you plan to pay for your contribution in the **Payment Method** field (e.g. Check, Credit Card, Cash).
9. Enter the address where the potluck will take place.
10. Enter the time of the event in the **Time** field.
11. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
12. Select the currency you plan to pay in (e.g. Dollars, Euros, Pounds) in the **Budget** field.
13. Enter the amount you plan to contribute in the **Budget Amount** field.
14. Enter your contact information for budget purposes in the **Budget Contact** field.
15. Finally, select how you plan to pay for your contribution in the **Payment Method** field (e.g. Check, Credit Card, Cash).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it will be displayed in the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the organizers can contact you.
* **Contact**: Enter any additional contact information, if desired.
* **Date**: The date for the event.
* **Dishes**: The type of dish you plan to bring.
* **Message**: Optional field for leaving a message.
* **Potluck Type**: Select the type of potluck you plan to attend.
* **Potluck Dishes**: Choose the dishes you plan to bring.
* **Quantity**: Indicate the quantity of the dish you plan to bring.
* **Potluck Location**: The location of the event (e.g. Office, Home).
* **Time**: The time for the event.
* **Message**: Optional field for leaving a message.
* **RSVP**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for your contribution (e.g. Check, Credit Card, Cash).
* **Description**: Optional field for adding a description.
* **Guests**: Number of guests attending the event.
* **Number of Guests**: Indicate how many guests are coming with you.
* **Budget**: Select the currency for the budget (e.g. Dollars, Euros, Pounds).
* **Budget Amount**: Enter the budget amount.
* **Budget Contact**: Enter your email address for budget contact.
* **Budget Method**: Enter how you plan to pay for your contribution.
