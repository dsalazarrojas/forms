# Microchanneling Service Waitlist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Microchanneling Service Waitlist form collects information from clients who wish to receive microchanneling services. This form helps our team keep track of client requests and ensures that we follow up with them according to their preferred method and frequency. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the client's name, email address, and phone number accurately.
3. Select the services you are interested in and any other services you may need.
4. Choose your preferred follow-up method and frequency.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes for our staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address where we can contact them.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time you would like to schedule your appointment.
* **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services you are interested in (e.g., 'Yes' for each service).
* **Services Details** (`services_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the services you selected.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., 'Yes' for phone call or email).
* **Follow up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g., 'Yes' for daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **Follow up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_one, optional): Choose the preferred method for follow-up (e.g., 'Yes' for phone, email, or text).
* **Follow up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, required): Enter any notes about your follow-up preferences.
* **Follow up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of your follow-up (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Follow up Next** (`follow_up_next`, date, required): Select the next date for follow-up.
* **Other Services** (`services_other`, text, optional): Enter any other services you may need.
* **Other Services** (`services_other_services`, text, optional): Enter a description of the other services you selected.
* **Follow up Frequency Notes** (`follow_up_frequency_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your follow-up frequency.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your request.
