# signature_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The signature verification form is designed to collect and document the signature of an individual or organization. This form ensures that all relevant information is collected, including user input, selection options, date and time of signing, and verification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the corresponding field.
2. Select one of the options provided for the "Option" field.
3. Enter the date and time of signing in the "Date Signed" and "Time Signed" fields respectively.
4. Select the "Verification Status" of the signature from the provided options.
5. Choose the "Signature Type" from the options provided.
6. If applicable, enter the reason for rejection in the "Reason for Rejection" field.
7. Add any additional notes regarding the signature in the "Notes" field.
8. Enter the email address of the signatory in the "Email" field (if required).
9. Enter the phone number of the signatory in the "Phone" field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (User Input, text, required): Enter the relevant information regarding the signatory, such as their name, title, or other identifying details.
* **Option** (Option, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the options provided, which may include different types of signatures, agreements, or certifications.
* **Date Signed** (Date Signed, date, required): Enter the date on which the signature was made.
* **Time Signed** (Time Signed, time, required): Enter the time at which the signature was made.
* **Verification Status** (Verification Status, select_one, required): Select the status of the verification, which may be active or inactive.
* **Signature Type** (Signature Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of signature, which may be "Yes" or "No".
* **Reason for Rejection** (Reason for Rejection, text, optional): Enter the reason for rejection, if applicable.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes regarding the signature.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the signatory (if required).
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the signatory (if required).
