# Heritage Site Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heritage Site Membership Registration Form is a tool for potential members to provide necessary information to join the heritage site. By filling out this form, members will be able to select their membership type and provide any required payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what information is required and how to fill it out.
2. Enter your **User Details** (page 1) with the required information.
3. Provide your **Contact Info** (page 1) with the relevant contact details.
4. Fill in your **Site Information** (page 1) with any relevant site-related information.
5. Select your desired **Membership Type** (page 2) from the available options.
6. Enter your **Payment Information** (page 2) to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details (Page 1)
* **User Details** (text): Enter your name, username, and any other relevant personal information.

### Contact Info (Page 1)
* **Contact Info** (text): Provide your email address, phone number, or any other contact information to help us get in touch with you.

### Site Information (Page 1)
* **Site Information** (text): Enter any relevant information about your site, such as your website or social media handles.

### Membership Type (Page 2)
* **Membership Type** (select_multiple):
	+ Membership Individual: Select this option if you want to join as an individual member.
	+ Membership Family: Select this option if you want to join with your family.
	+ Membership Group: Select this option if you want to join as a group or organization.

### Payment Information (Page 2)
* **Payment Information** (text): Enter your payment details, including your bank account or credit card information.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* You will receive a confirmation email after submitting the form, please check your email inbox for details.
