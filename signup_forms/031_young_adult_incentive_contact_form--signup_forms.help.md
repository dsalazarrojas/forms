# young_adult_incentive_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and program interests from young adults for incentives and program participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the program of interest you are interested in from the options provided.
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your contact phone number.
6. Enter the name of your school (if applicable).
7. If you have any other program interests not listed, please specify them in the "Other Program Interest" field.
8. Enter any additional message or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Youth Incentive Program of Interest** (`youth_incentive_program_of_interest`, select one, required): Select the specific program you are interested in, such as "Program of Interest Option 1" or "Program of Interest Option 2".
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): If you are currently enrolled in school, enter the name of the school.
* **Other Program Interest** (`youth_incentive_program_of_interest_other`, text, optional): If you have any other program interests not listed, please specify them here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your submission.
