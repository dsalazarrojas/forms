# Best Sellers Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Best Sellers Club Membership Form is used to collect information from customers who wish to become Best Sellers Club members. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that we can tailor our services to meet your requirements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review the form carefully before submitting it.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" on the form.
3. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Include your country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, `date`, required): Select the relevant date for your membership application.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best applies to your membership status (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
* **Quantity or rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, `number`, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional relevant information about your membership application.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, `number`, optional): Use decimal format when applicable.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, `text`, optional): Optional closing remarks.
>
Note: The form fields are labeled with their corresponding "id" values for easy reference.
