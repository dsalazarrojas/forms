# ATV Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for ATV event participants to provide their personal information, medical history, and acknowledgment of the risks associated with ATV riding. It helps organizers collect necessary details for participant safety and liability protection.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Participant Information**: This is a brief section to introduce yourself as a participant in the event.
2.  **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
3.  **Date of Birth**: Provide your birth date in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
4.  **Address**: Enter your current residential address.
5.  **City, State, ZIP Code**: Enter your city, state, and postal code of your address.
6.  **Phone Number**: Provide your primary contact phone number.
7.  **Email Address**: Enter your current email for event communications.
8.  **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
9.  **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
10.  **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact person.
11.  **Medical Conditions or Physical Limitations**: If you have any medical conditions or physical limitations that may affect your participation, list them here.
12.  **Medications**: If you're taking any medications, list them.
13.  **Allergies**: List any severe allergies you have.
14.  **Previous ATV Experience**: Describe your previous experience with ATVs.
15.  **Risk Acknowledgment**: Answer "Yes" to acknowledge that ATV riding involves inherent risks.
16.  **Liability Waiver Agreement**: Answer "Yes" to agree to release the organizers from liability.
17.  **Photography Consent**: Answer "Yes" to allow photography during the event.
18.  **Signature**: Sign your name electronically to acknowledge agreement to the terms.
19.  **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you signed the waiver.

## Tips

- Always enter accurate and complete information to ensure your safety and the organizers' liability protection.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
- Ensure you complete the form carefully and honestly.
