# Beauty Product Launch Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential customers about their interest in a beauty product launch. By filling out this form, you will be contacted about exclusive offers and early access to our products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. Review each field to understand its purpose and relevance to our lead generation efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter your city and state or country.
* **Product Categories of Interest** (`product_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant product categories (Skincare, Makeup, Haircare, Fragrance, Nail Care, Body Care, Wellness).
* **Specific Product Interest** (`specific_interest`, `text`, required): Describe the specific product you're interested in.
* **I Am A** (`customer_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Existing Customer, New Customer, Beauty Professional, Influencer, Retailer.
* **Likelihood to Purchase This Product** (`purchase_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, Very Likely.
* **When Would You Purchase** (`purchase_timeframe`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Pre-launch, Launch Week, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, Later, Unsure.
* **Interest in Early Access or Exclusive Offers** (`early_access_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Email, SMS, Phone Call, Social Media.
* **Opt In to Newsletter** (`newsletter_opt_in`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Interested in Promotional Offers** (`promotional_offers`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes, No.
* **Most Important Product Features** (`important_features`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all relevant product features (Natural Ingredients, Cruelty-free, Sustainable Packaging, Dermatologist-tested, Affordable, Premium Quality).
* **Price Sensitivity** (`price_sensitivity`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Very Price-sensitive, Moderate, Price Not Primary Factor, Premium Price Acceptable.
* **Follow Us on Social Media** (`social_media_follower`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Yes - Already Follow, Yes - Will Follow, No.
* **Would You Refer Friends or Family** (`referral_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose from: Definitely Not, Probably Not, Unsure, Probably Yes, Definitely Yes.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
