# Theatre Festival Play Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a play that will be performed at the theatre festival. It helps festival organizers keep track of the play's details and ensures that the play is included in the festival's program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Play Title** (`play_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the play you are submitting for the festival.
* **Play Year** (`play_year`, number, required): Enter the year the play was performed or produced.
* **Play Type** (`play_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the play is a full-length performance, 'No' otherwise.
* **Play Length** (`play_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the play in minutes. This field is optional and only needed for internal planning purposes.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue where the play was performed or will be performed. This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event. This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event. This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Play Description** (`play_description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the play. This field is optional and can be left blank.
* **Play URL** (`play_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL where the play can be accessed or previewed. This field is optional and can be left blank.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as 'required') before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the festival organizers for guidance.
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
