# Jackpot Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by riders to enter their information for jackpot competitions. It allows users to provide their contact details, guesses, and other relevant information for the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your rider contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Indicate whether you have made guesses for the competition (Yes/No).
3. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (Rider Name).
4. Enter your email address (Rider Email).
5. Enter your phone number (Rider Phone).
6. Enter your address (Rider Address).
7. Indicate whether the competition you are entering is related to another race (Yes/No).
8. Enter the date of the related race (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rider Contact Information** (rider_contact): This field is for entering your name, email, and phone number.
* **Jackpot Date** (jackpot_date): Enter the date of the jackpot competition.
* **Guesses** (guesses): Select whether you have made guesses for the competition (Yes/No).
* **Rider Name** (rider_name): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Rider Email** (rider_email): Enter your email address.
* **Rider Phone** (rider_phone): Enter your phone number.
* **Rider Address** (rider_address): Enter your address.
* **Related Race Guess** (related_race_guess): Select whether the competition you are entering is related to another race (Yes/No).
* **Related Race Date** (related_race_date): Enter the date of the related race (if applicable).
