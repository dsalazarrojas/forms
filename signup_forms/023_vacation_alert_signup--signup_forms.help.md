# Vacation Alert Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your travel preferences and contact information so we can provide you with relevant vacation updates, offers, and news. Please fill out the form to create your account and stay informed about exciting travel opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select your travel preferences from the options provided.
3. Enter your email address and verify it to ensure we can contact you.
4. Optionally, you can choose to unsubscribe from email updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agencies**: Select all the travel agencies you are interested in learning about.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Tour Operators**: Select all the tour operators you'd like to hear from.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Travel Brands**: Select all the travel brands you are interested in.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Subscriber Preferences**: Select whether you want to receive updates from us.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'
* **Email**: Enter the email address you want to use to receive updates from us.
	+ Format: [your email address]
* **Email Address**: Enter your preferred email address (not required for signing up, but recommended for easier contact).
* **Name**: Enter your name (optional, but helps us personalize updates).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us contact you directly).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us contact you directly).
* **Unsubscribe from Email**: Select whether you want to receive email updates.
	+ Options: 'Yes' or 'No'

## Tips
- Make sure to select all relevant travel options to receive tailored updates.
- Enter accurate contact information to ensure we can reach you directly.
- Consider unsubscribing from email updates if you're not interested.
