# join_the_solopreneur_waitlist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "join_the_solopreneur_waitlist" form is designed for users to join the Solopreneur waitlist. This form collects information to add or manage your business on the waitlist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category that best describes your business. This will help us better understand your business needs and provide you with more relevant information and support.
2. Enter your name and email address to be contacted for further instructions.
3. Enter your phone number for easier communication.
4. Confirm your consent to manage data collection for your business.
5. Confirm your understanding of the payment terms.
6. If you have an IBAN, BIC, or other account details, enter them to be able to receive payments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: waitlist_signup
* **Join The Solopreneur Waitlist**: Enter your choice of category for your business. This field is required and will help us tailor our support and resources to your business needs. Options include:
	+ Creators
	+ Freelancers
	+ Founders
* **Name**: Enter your name or business name for identification and contact purposes. This field is not required, but it will help us personalize our communication and offer targeted support.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you with further instructions and updates. This field is not required, but it is recommended for effective communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for faster communication and easier updates. This field is not required, but it is recommended for timely responses and support.

### Page 2: 
* **Manage data collection for**: Confirm your understanding and consent for data collection for your business. This field is required and will determine how we collect and use information about your business. Options include:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Payment terms**: Confirm your understanding and agreement with the payment terms. This field is required and will help us ensure clear communication and expectations. Options include:
	+ I understand and agree
	+ 'False'
* **Payment terms**: Enter any additional comments or questions about the payment terms. This field is not required, but it is recommended to ensure clarity and understanding.
* **IBAN**: Enter your International Bank Account Number for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for smoother payment processing.
* **Account**: Enter your account details for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for faster payment processing.
* **BIC**: Enter your Bank Identifier Code for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for efficient payment processing.
* **IBAN/BIC**: Enter your International Bank Account Number and Bank Identifier Code for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for streamlined payment processing.
* **IBAN/BIC/IBAN**: Enter your International Bank Account Number, Bank Identifier Code, and International Bank Account Number for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for comprehensive payment processing.
* **IBAN/BIC/IBAN/IBAN**: Enter all the necessary information for receiving payments. This field is not required, but it is recommended for optimal payment processing.
