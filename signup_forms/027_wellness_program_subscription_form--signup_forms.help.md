# Wellness Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Program Subscription Form is designed to help users sign up for the Wellness Program. This form gathers essential information from users to create a successful and personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
3. Select a time of day for your sessions (e.g., 9am, 10am, etc.).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Provide your phone number.
6. Enter your company (if applicable).
7. Enter your address (if applicable).
8. Choose your program start and end numbers (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
9. Select the duration of your program (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
10. Choose how many days per week you wish to participate.
11. Choose the time per session (e.g., 30, 45, 60, etc.).
12. Select the type of program (Wellness Program or Other).
13. If you selected "Other," provide more information in the "Program Type Other" field.
14. Add any additional comments about your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Date of Birth** (3): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY` (e.g., 12/12/1990).
* **Time** (4): Select a time of day for your sessions (e.g., 9am, 10am, etc.).
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (6): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (7): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (8): If applicable, enter your address.
* **Program Start** (9): Choose your program start number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Program End** (10): Choose your program end number (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Duration** (11): Choose the duration of your program (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Days per Week** (12): Choose how many days per week you wish to participate.
* **Time per Session** (13): Choose the time per session (e.g., 30, 45, 60, etc.).
* **Program Type** (14): Select the type of program (Wellness Program or Other).
* **Program Type Other** (15): If you selected "Other," provide more information about your program.
* **Additional Comments** (16): Add any additional comments about your subscription.

# Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to us for support.
* This form is for signing up for the Wellness Program. If you need to cancel or update your current subscription, please contact us separately.
