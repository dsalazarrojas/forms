# Dating App Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from users who refer new friends to our dating app. The form helps us understand the source of the referral, the friend's contact information, and any additional context about the referral. By filling out this form, you will help us grow our community by connecting people who are interested in meeting new friends and potential matches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct "Referral Source" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your friend's email address and name in the "Friend Email" and "Friend Name" fields.
3. Enter your own name and email address in the "Referrer Name" and "Referrer Email" fields.
4. Describe the reason for the referral in the "Referral Context" field.
5. If applicable, enter the date of the referral in the "Date of Referral" field.
6. Select whether the referral is made with "Explicit" or "Implied" consent.
7. Indicate whether you would like to follow up with your friend in the "Follow up" field.
8. Enter a message to send to your friend in the "Referrer Comment" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose the source of the referral, such as "Referral from Website", "Referral from Social Media", or "Referral from Event".
* **Friend Email** (`friend_email`, text, required): Enter your friend's email address. This is the email address of the person who will be invited to the app.
* **Friend Name** (`friend_name`, text, required): Enter your friend's name. This will help us personalize the invite to your friend.
* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Enter your own name. This is the name of the person who is inviting their friend to the app.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`, text, required): Enter your email address. This is how we will contact you to let you know about the status of your referral.
* **Referral Context** (`referral_context`, text, required): Describe the reason for the referral. This helps us understand how your friend was interested in our app.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, optional): If you know the exact date when your friend showed interest in our app, please enter it here.
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This is an additional way for us to contact you to confirm the referral.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the referral is made with "Explicit" or "Implied" consent. This ensures we follow your friend's expectations and boundaries.
* **Follow up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you would like us to follow up with your friend after the referral.
* **Referrer Comment** (`referrer_comment`, text, optional): Enter a personal message to send to your friend after the referral. This is an opportunity to add a personal touch to the referral.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information for your friend's email and phone number.
- Be honest about the source of the referral and the context of the referral.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can always contact us for assistance.
