# Gardening Workshop Series Lead Generation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gardening Workshop Series Lead Generation form is designed to collect leads for a multi-page form series. This form captures user feedback, comments, or testimonials for the Gardening Workshop Series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information by typing in your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
2. Answer the questions and provide your feedback in the "Lead Generation Page One", "Lead Generation Page Two", "Lead Generation Page Three", and so on fields, up to "Lead Generation Page Twelve".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to get in touch with you.
* **Lead Generation Page One** (`lead_generation_page_one`, `text`, required: false): Please share your thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Two** (`lead_generation_page_two`, `text`, required: false): Continue sharing your feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Three** (`lead_generation_page_three`, `text`, required: false): Provide more of your thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Four** (`lead_generation_page_four`, `text`, required: false): Keep sharing your feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Five** (`lead_generation_page_five`, `text`, required: false): More thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Six** (`lead_generation_page_six`, `text`, required: false): Keep going with your feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Seven** (`lead_generation_page_seven`, `text`, required: false): Additional thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Eight** (`lead_generation_page_eight`, `text`, required: false): Final thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Nine** (`lead_generation_page_nine`, `text`, required: false): Last part of your feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Ten** (`lead_generation_page_ten`, `text`, required: false): Final feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Eleven** (`lead_generation_page_eleven`, `text`, required: false): Final thoughts on the Gardening Workshop Series.
* **Lead Generation Page Twelve** (`lead_generation_page_twelve`, `text`, required: false): Final feedback on the Gardening Workshop Series.
