# Vape Support Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a participant in the Vape Support Network. This form gathers essential participant information to provide better support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Participant Information" page if you are already a participant in the support network.
2. Provide your support needs in the text field on the "Support Needs" page.
3. Choose your location from the available options on the "Location" page.
4. Enter your email address on the "Email" page.
5. Enter your phone number on the "Phone" page.
6. Input your date of birth on the "Date of Birth" page in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
7. Choose your time zone from the available options on the "Time Zone" page.
8. Finally, describe how you heard about the Vape Support Network on the "How did you hear about us?" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This is a selection of "Yes" or "No" indicating if you are already a participant in the support network.
* **Support Needs**: This is a text field where you can describe your specific support needs.
* **Location**: This is a selection of available locations, please choose where you would like to receive support from.
* **Email**: This is a field for your email address.
* **Phone**: This is a field for your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: This is a field for your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Zone**: This is a selection of available time zones.
* **How did you hear about us?**: This is a text field where you can describe how you heard about the Vape Support Network.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the support team for assistance.
