# Dinner Prepartion Team Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dinner Prepartion Team Signup form is a tool to collect information from individuals who wish to join a dinner preparation team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review and understand the form's purpose and requirements.
2. Select the relevant options for "Volunteer Details" by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field, following the date format (yyyy-mm-dd).
4. Choose the role(s) you are interested in from the "Roles" dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief description of your role in the "Role Description" field.
6. Enter a title for your role in the "Role Title" field.
7. Enter your name and email address in the "Volunteer Name" and "Volunteer Email" fields, respectively.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Volunteer Phone" field.
9. Enter the name of your role and email address in the "Role Name" and "Role Email" fields, respectively.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Details (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Select one or multiple options to indicate your availability to join the team.
* **Availability (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your availability in the format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Roles (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Choose the role(s) you are interested in.
* **Role Description (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Provide a short description of your role.
* **Role Title (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter a title for your role.
* **Volunteer Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your name.
* **Volunteer Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Role Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the name of your role.
* **Role Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Enter the email address of your role.

Please note that the fields marked as required/optional have a specific requirement or suggestion to ensure accurate and consistent data collection.
