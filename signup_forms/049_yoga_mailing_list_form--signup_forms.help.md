# Yoga Mailing List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for targeted marketing materials. Please fill out this form carefully and truthfully.
## How To Complete This Form
Fill out the form by following these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure this is your correct email address.
3. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you accept the terms and conditions, select "I accept the terms and conditions" from the "Accept Terms" dropdown menu.
6. Select whether you want to accept newsletters from the "Accept Newsletter" dropdown menu. Please note that you must accept the newsletter to proceed.
7. Select all applicable countries from the "Country" dropdown menu. This will help us tailor our marketing materials to your location.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <ul>
* **Full Name** (Name, Text, Required: False): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required: False): Enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Confirm Email** (Email, Text, Required: False): Confirm your email address to help us verify your identity.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required: False): Enter your phone number. We will use this to contact you if needed.
* **Accept Terms** (Select One, Select, Required: False): Select "I accept the terms and conditions" if you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Accept Newsletter** (Select, Select, Required: True): Select "Yes" to accept our newsletter.
* **Country** (Select Multiple, Select, Required: False): Select all countries that apply to you.
* **City** (Text, Text, Required: False): Enter your city for targeted marketing materials.
</ul>
