# Hearing Test Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hearing Test Sign Up Form is a simple form designed for users to sign up for a hearing test. This form collects essential information from the users to schedule and prepare for the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3.  Select a date for the test in the "date" field.
4.  Choose the type of test you need ("Hearing Test", "Other Test", "Additional Test") in the "test_type" field.
5.  Enter the time for the test in the "test_time" field.
6.  Select a date for the test in the "test_date" field.
7.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
8.  Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9.  Confirm your email address in the "confirm_email" field.
10.  Confirm your phone number in the "confirm_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your first name.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your last name.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): This is where you select a date for the test.
* **test_type** (`test_type`, select_multiple, required): This is where you choose the type of test you need.
* **test_time** (`test_time`, time, required): This is where you enter the time for the test.
* **test_date** (`test_date`, date, required): This is where you select a date for the test.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): This is where you enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is where you enter your phone number.
* **confirm_email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): This is where you confirm your email address.
* **confirm_phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required): This is where you confirm your phone number.
