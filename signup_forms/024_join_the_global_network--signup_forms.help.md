# Join The Global Network - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Join The Global Network" form is a user-facing form designed for individuals who want to join the global network. It is not intended for internal users or existing network members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Email in the third field (ensure it's a valid email address).
4. Enter your Phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select your Interest Areas in the fifth field (you can choose one or multiple options).
6. Enter your Contact Number in the sixth field.
7. Provide a brief Description of yourself in the seventh field.
8. Select the Assigned Tool that you want to use (if you have a preference).
9. Finally, save your Output File in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications from the global network.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple areas of interest that you want to be part of the global network.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number for verification purposes.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of yourself to help others get to know you better.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool that you want to use within the global network.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Save your work or output in this field for future reference.
