# Join The Housing Co Operative Mailing List - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us collect information from individuals interested in joining The Housing Co Operative Mailing List. We will use this information to tailor our content and communication with you.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) below.
2. Choose your interests from the list provided.
3. Select how you prefer to be communicated with and how often you would like to be communicated with.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can provide them in the "Other Comments" field.
5. Finally, provide any additional contact information you feel is necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.

### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.

### Email (3)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. We will use this as a unique identifier.

### Interests (4)
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the interests that apply to you.
	+ Community housing
	+ Cooperative living
	+ Community events
	+ Other

### Phone (5)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. (Not required, but recommended for easier contact)

### Notes (6)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional comments or notes you have.

### Date of Birth (7)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (optional).

### Time (8)
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to be contacted (optional).

### How did you hear about us? (9)
* **How did you hear about us?** (`join_method`, select_one, optional): How did you find out about The Housing Co Operative Mailing List?
	+ Social media
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Online Search
	+ Friend/Neighbor

### How would you like us to communicate with you? (10)
* **How would you like us to communicate with you?** (`how_do_we_communicate`, select_one, required): How would you like us to communicate with you?
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Mail
	+ Never

### How often would you like us to communicate with you? (11)
* **How often would you like us to communicate with you?** (`how_often_to_communicate`, select_multiple, required): How often would you like us to communicate with you?
	+ As needed
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Never

### How do you prefer to contact us? (12)
* **How do you prefer to contact us?** (`how_do_you_prefer`, select_one, optional): How would you like to contact us?
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
	+ None

### Other comments or notes (13)
* **Other comments or notes** (`other_notes`, text, optional): Any other comments or notes you have.

### Other contact information (14)
* **Other contact information** (`other_contact_info`, text, optional): Any other contact information you feel is necessary.

Note: All fields with an asterisk (\*) are required.
