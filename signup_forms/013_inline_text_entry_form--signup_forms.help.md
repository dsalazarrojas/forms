# Inline Text Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inline Text Entry Form is a form designed to collect participant information, including their name, email address, and phone number, as well as a short description of their profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name in the "Participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant email" field.
3. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant phone" field.
4. Add a short description of the participant's profile in the "Participant profile" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: This field is for entering the participant's name. It is not required but is recommended for identification purposes.
* **Participant email**: This field is for entering the participant's email address. It is an optional field but is required for communication and verification purposes. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Participant phone**: This field is for entering the participant's phone number. It is an optional field but is recommended for contact purposes.
* **Participant profile**: This field is for adding a short description of the participant's profile. It is not required but is recommended for providing more information about the participant.
