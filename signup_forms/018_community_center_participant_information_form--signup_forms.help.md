# Community Center Participant Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about participants in a community center program or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Participant name" field.
2. If applicable, provide your date of birth for program eligibility purposes.
3. Enter your primary contact phone number or email in the "Primary contact" field.
4. Provide the name and phone number of the emergency contact person.
5. Enter the name of the program or class you are enrolled in.
6. If necessary, list any medical conditions or allergies in the "Medical conditions or allergies" field.
7. Choose whether to allow your photo to be used for promotional materials in the "Photo consent" field.
8. If you require special accommodations, list them in the "Special accommodations" field.
