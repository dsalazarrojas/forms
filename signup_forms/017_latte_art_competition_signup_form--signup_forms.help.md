# Latte Art Competition Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect participant information and details for the Latte Art Competition. It is used to gather necessary information to register participants for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Participant Details section with your name, email address, and other relevant details.
2. Enter your Contact Details, including your phone number and other contact information.
3. Provide your Payment Details if you have made a payment for the competition.
4. Enter your Order Details to confirm your order.
5. Add any Additional Information if needed.
6. Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
7. Choose the correct Event Status and Participant Status.
8. Select the correct Event Privacy and Participant Privacy.
9. Confirm that you have submitted your entry correctly.
10. Review and agree to the Participant Agreement Terms.
