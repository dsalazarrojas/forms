# Player Eligibility Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about players to assess their eligibility to participate in certain activities or events. This information is used to ensure players' safety and compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. Please ensure the email address is valid and active.
4. If applicable, fill in your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Fill in your **Address** in the "Address" field.
6. Answer the question "Have Health Problems?" by selecting either "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
7. Answer the question "Have Sports History?" by selecting either "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
8. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** by selecting "Agree" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid and active email address to which we will send important updates and information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by us.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for our records, if applicable.
* **Have Health Problems?** (`has_health_problems`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have any health problems or concerns that we should be aware of.
* **Have Sports History?** (`has_sports_history`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have any sports-related medical history or concerns.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions_form`, select_one, required): Read and agree to our terms and conditions before proceeding.
