# Survivor Pool Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to sign up for a survivor pool. Please fill out this form to provide your information and make your picks for the pool.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant info in the "Participant Info" section.
2. Select your weekly picks in the "Weekly Picks" section. You can choose one pick from the available options.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
5. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" section by re-entering the email address you provided in the "Email" section.
6. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" section by re-entering the phone number you provided in the "Phone" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`Participant Info`, text, optional): Enter your name and other identifying information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Weekly Picks** (`Weekly Picks`, select_one, required): Select one pick from the available options to make your choice for the week.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, note, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone** (`Confirm Phone`, note, required): Re-enter your phone number to confirm its accuracy.
