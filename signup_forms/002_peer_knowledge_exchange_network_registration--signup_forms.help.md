# Peer Knowledge Exchange Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Knowledge Exchange Network (PKEN) is a platform for users to share knowledge, ideas, and expertise with peers from various fields. By filling out this form, you will be registering for the PKEN and contributing to a community of professionals who are passionate about exchanging knowledge and ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field with your relevant information.
2. Make sure to review and submit your form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. user_input (Text)
*   **user_input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): A field where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your registration.

### 2. Name (Text)
*   **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.

### 3. email (Text)
*   **email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used as your username for the PKEN platform.

### 4. phone (Text)
*   **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you in case of any important notifications.

### 5. password (Text)
*   **password** (`password`, `text`, required: false): Create a password to secure your account. Please make sure to choose a strong and unique password.

### 6. confirm_password (Text)
*   **confirm_password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required: false): Confirm your password to ensure it matches the one you created earlier.

### 7. expertise (Text)
*   **expertise** (`expertise`, `text`, required: false): Describe your area of expertise or interest. This will help us understand your preferences and match you with peers who share similar interests.

### 8. learning_goals (Text)
*   **learning_goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required: false): Specify your learning goals or areas you are interested in learning more about. This will help us tailor the content and recommendations for you.

### 9. member_type (Select One)
*   **member_type** (`member_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose if you are a member of a community organization.

### 10. community_organizations (Select Multiple)
*   **community_organizations** (`community_organizations`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If you are a member of a community organization, select all that apply.

### 11. agree (Note)
*   **agree** (`agree`, `note`, required: false): Check this box to agree to the terms and conditions of the PKEN.

### 12. confirm_agree (Note)
*   **confirm_agree** (`confirm_agree`, `note`, required: false): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the PKEN.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is successful.
*   Use a strong and unique password to secure your account.
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out your information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
