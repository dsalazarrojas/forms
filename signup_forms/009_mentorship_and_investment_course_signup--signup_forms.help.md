# Mentorship And Investment Course Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for the Mentorship and Investment Course. It helps the course administrators to understand participant interests, course preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details in the Participant Details section.
2. Indicate your interests in the Participant Interests section.
3. Select your consent option in the Participant Consent section.
4. Provide the course details in the Course Details section.
5. Enter your phone number in the Participant Phone section.
6. Enter your email address in the Participant Email section.
7. Add any additional participant notes in the Participant Notes section.
8. Choose the course instructors in the Course Instructors section.
9. Select the course start and end dates in the Course Start Date and Course End Date sections.
10. Enter the course location in the Course Location section.
11. Enter the course investment amount in the Course Investment section.
12. Enter the course description in the Course Description section.
13. Enter the course price in the Course Price section.
14. Enter your participant investment amount in the Participant Investment section.
15. Select your participant investment type in the Participant Investment Type section.
16. Choose your participant programs of interest in the Participant Programs section.
17. Select your participant programs interests in the Participant Programs Interests section.
18. Enter your participant programs investment amount in the Participant Programs Investment section.
19. Select your participant programs investment type in the Participant Programs Investment Type section.
20. Enter the participant programs location in the Participant Programs Location section.
21. Enter the course programs price in the Course Programs Price section.
22. Select your participant programs investment status in the Participant Programs Investment Status section.
23. Select the participant programs start and end dates in the Participant Programs Start Date and Participant Programs End Date sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, Text, Required): Enter your name, title, or any other details you would like to share about yourself.
* **Participant Interests** (`Participant Interests`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all interests that apply to you.
* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, Select One, Required): Indicate your consent to participate in the course.
* **Course Details** (`Course Details`, Text, Required): Enter the course title, description, and any other details about the course.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for any follow-up communication.
* **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, Note, Required): Add any additional participant notes.
* **Course Instructors** (`Course Instructors`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all course instructors that will be teaching the course.
* **Course Start Date** (`Course Start Date`, Date, Required): Select the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`Course End Date`, Date, Required): Select the end date of the course.
* **Course Location** (`Course Location`, Text, Required): Enter the location of the course.
* **Course Investment** (`Course Investment`, Number, Required): Enter the investment amount required for the course.
* **Course Description** (`Course Description`, Text, Required): Enter a detailed description of the course.
* **Course Price** (`Course Price`, Text, Required): Enter the price of the course.
* **Participant Investment** (`Participant Investment`, Number, Required): Enter the investment amount you are willing to invest.
* **Participant Investment Type** (`Participant Investment Type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of investment you are willing to make.
* **Participant Programs** (`Participant Programs`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all programs that you are interested in.
* **Participant Programs Interests** (`Participant Programs Interests`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all interests related to the programs.
* **Participant Programs Investment** (`Participant Programs Investment`, Number, Required): Enter the investment amount for the programs.
* **Participant Programs Investment Type** (`Participant Programs Investment Type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of investment for the programs.
* **Participant Programs Location** (`Participant Programs Location`, Text, Required): Enter the location of the programs.
* **Course Programs Price** (`Course Programs Price`, Text, Required): Enter the price of the programs.
* **Participant Programs Investment Status** (`Participant Programs Investment Status`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the status of your investment for the programs.
* **Participant Programs Start Date** (`Participant Programs Start Date`, Date, Required): Select the start date of the programs.
* **Participant Programs End Date** (`Participant Programs End Date`, Date, Required): Select the end date of the programs.
