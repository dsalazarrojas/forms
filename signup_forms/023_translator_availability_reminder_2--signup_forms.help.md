# Translator Availability Reminder 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Translator Availability Reminder 2 form is used to track and manage the availability of translators for future projects. This form helps to keep track of a translator's current status, availability, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the relevant fields that apply to your current status and availability.
2. Choose "Available" if you are able to take on projects and "Not Available" or "Busy" otherwise.
3. Enter your availability start and end dates, if applicable.
4. Enter the number of words you are available to work on, if you have a specific limit.
5. Select your current status for the second availability field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Translator Availability** (`translator_availability`, note, required: false): A note field for any additional information about your translator availability.
* **Availability 1** (`availability_1`, select_one, required: false): Select your current status, whether you are Available, Not Available, or Busy.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of your current availability period, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of your current availability period, if applicable.
* **Number of Words** (`number_of_words`, number, required: false): Enter the number of words you are currently available to work on, if you have a specific limit.
* **Availability 2** (`availability_2`, select_one, required: false): Select your current status for your second availability field, if applicable.
