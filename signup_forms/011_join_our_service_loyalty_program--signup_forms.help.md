# Join Our Service Loyalty Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Join Our Service Loyalty Program" form is designed to help new customers join the service loyalty program. This program offers rewards and benefits to customers who consistently use our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are not duplicating your sign-up effort or attempting to join a program you're already part of.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely with the required information.
3. If you have any issues or need assistance, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Join Our Loyalty Program** (`form_header`, note, **not required**): This is an introductory note providing a brief explanation of the service loyalty program and its benefits.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, **not required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **not required**): Enter the email address associated with your account or preferred contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **not required**): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone calls or messages.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select one, **not required**): Select the type of service you use. Our services are:
	+ Service A
	+ Service B
	+ Service C
* **Location** (`location`, select multiple, **not required**): Choose the locations you have used our service at:
	+ Option A
	+ Option B
	+ Option C
* **Loyalty Points** (`loyalty_points`, number, **not required**): Enter the number of loyalty points you have earned or would like to earn.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, select one, **not required**): Select the follow-up option that best suits your needs. Our options are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2

## Tips
- Ensure that you are not already part of the loyalty program before signing up.
- Double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team.
