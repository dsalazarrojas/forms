# Subscribe To Mental Health Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to subscribe to mental health updates, enabling them to stay informed about relevant news, events, or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text box provided.
2. Enter your Last Name in the text box provided.
3. Enter your Email address in the format of `example@example.com`. This will be used to send you updates.
4. Enter your Phone number in the text box provided (optional).
5. Enter your Organization name in the text box provided (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates, such as `example@example.com`. This will be used to send you updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to receive updates via phone call (optional).
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with, if applicable (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* If you're having trouble with your subscription, please contact us at [support email] for assistance.
* You can expect to receive mental health related updates, news, and resources.
