# Bloxburg Variety Show Talk Show Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bloxburg Variety Show Talk Show is a platform that features a diverse range of content creators with varying experiences and skills. By signing up, creators can showcase their content to a broader audience and connect with other creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To sign up for the Bloxburg Variety Show Talk Show, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the purpose of the form and the show's guidelines.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information and preferences.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator Username** (`creator_username`, Text, Required): Enter your unique username or handle on the platform you use to create content.
* **Email Address** (`creator_email`, Email, Required): Provide your email address to help us communicate with you about your sign-up and performance details.
* **Phone Number** (`creator_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with important updates and reminders.
* **Primary Content Platform** (`creator_platform`, Select One, Required): Choose the platform where you primarily create content (e.g., YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter/X, Other).
* **Type of Content You Create** (`content_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type(s) of content you create (e.g., Comedy, Gaming, Music, Drama, Educational, Lifestyle, Other).
* **Approximate Follower Count** (`follower_count`, Select One, Optional): Estimate your approximate follower count on your primary platform.
* **Bloxburg Experience Level** (`bloxburg_experience`, Select One, Required): Choose your level of experience with the Bloxburg platform (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert creator).
* **Interested Show Segments** (`show_segment_interest`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the types of show segments you are interested in (e.g., Comedy sketches, Talent showcase, Interview segment, Gaming demo, Music performance, Interactive games).
* **Preferred Performance Length** (`performance_length`, Select One, Required): Choose the length of your preferred performance (e.g., 5 minutes, 10 minutes, 15 minutes, Flexible).
* **Any special props or setup needed?** (`special_props_needed`, Text, Optional): If you need any special props or setup for your performance, describe them here.
* **Any co-creators joining?** (`co_creators`, Text, Optional): If you are performing with co-creators, list their names and platforms.
* **Available Performance Dates** (`available_dates`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the dates when you are available to perform (e.g., This weekend, Next weekend, Next week, Flexible/TBD).
* **Technical Requirements** (`technical_requirements`, Text, Optional): Describe any technical requirements for your performance (e.g., Microphone, special effects, etc.).
* **Social Media Links** (`social_links`, Text, Optional): Share your social media links for promotion and networking.
* **Have you performed in shows before?** (`previous_shows`, Select One, Optional): If you have prior performing experience, select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you are signing up for the show.

Note: All required fields must be completed before submitting the form.
