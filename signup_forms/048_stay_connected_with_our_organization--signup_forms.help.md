# Stay Connected With Our Organization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the "Stay Connected With Our Organization" form, which is meant to help users connect with our organization and provide their contact details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" page.
2. Choose your preferred methods of communication on the "Communication Preferences" page.
3. Indicate how you would like to be followed up with on the "Follow Up" page.
4. Review and submit your form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details**: This is the first page of the form where you can enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information. This will help our organization get in touch with you.
* **Communication Preferences**: On this page, you can select which methods you would like to be contacted through, such as email, phone, or mail. You can choose one or more of these options to suit your preferences.
* **Follow Up**: In this page, you can indicate how you would like to be followed up with, such as in person, via email, or via phone. This will help our organization tailor its communication with you.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
* Use the hints and instructions provided to guide you through the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
