# Gaming Community Member Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new members in the gaming community. It helps us keep track of our members and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review the form before submitting it to make sure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Submit the form when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **username** (Text, Optional)
Enter a unique username that you'd like to use for your community profile.
### 2. **member_name** (Text, Optional)
Enter your real name or the name you'd like to be known by in the community.
### 3. **discord_username** (Text, Optional)
Enter your Discord username (if you have one).
### 4. **email** (Email, Optional)
Enter your email address (if you have one).
### 5. **password** (Text, Optional)
Enter a password for your community account (if you have one).
### 6. **confirm_email** (Email, Optional)
Confirm your email address to make sure it's correct.
### 7. **phone** (Text, Optional)
Enter your phone number (if you have one).
### 8. **join_date** (Date, Optional)
Enter the date you joined the community (if you know it).
### 9. **gaming_group** (Select One, Optional)
Choose the type of gaming group you're a part of (Discord, Clan, Group).
### 10. **gaming_server** (Select One, Optional)
Choose whether your gaming server is Public or Private.
### 11. **gaming_group_size** (Select One, Optional)
Choose the size of your gaming group (Small, Medium, Large).
### 12. **member_level** (Select One, Optional)
Choose your member level (Newbie, Member, Moderator, Admin).
### 13. **game** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Select the games you're interested in or play (e.g. League of Legends, Fortnite, Minecraft).
### 14. **playtime** (Number, Optional)
Enter the amount of time you spend playing games per week.
### 15. **bio** (Note, Optional)
Enter a brief bio about yourself.
### 16. **accept_terms** (Text, Optional)
Check this box to confirm you've read and accepted the terms and conditions.
### 17. **terms_and_conditions** (Note, Optional)
Read and accept the terms and conditions of the community.
### 18. **member_image** (Text, Optional)
Upload a profile picture (if you have one).
### 19. **member_file** (Text, Optional)
Upload a file related to your gaming profile (if you have one).
### 20. **member_image_alt** (Text, Optional)
Enter an alternative profile picture (if you have one).
### 21. **member_file_alt** (Text, Optional)
Enter an alternative file related to your gaming profile (if you have one).
### 22. **member_notes** (Note, Optional)
Enter any additional notes about yourself.
### 23. **custom_notes** (Note, Optional)
Enter any custom notes about yourself.
### 24. **submission_notes** (Note, Optional)
Enter any submission notes about your registration.

Note: Some fields are marked as "optional", which means you can skip them if you don't have the information or don't want to fill it out.
