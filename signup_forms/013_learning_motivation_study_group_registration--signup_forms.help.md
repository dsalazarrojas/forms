# Learning Motivation Study Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register an interest in joining or creating a study group for motivation and learning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select a Schedule Date in the "Schedule Date" field.
6. Choose a Schedule Time in the "Schedule Time" field.
7. Enter the Group Name in the "Group Name" field.
8. If applicable, provide a Description of your study group in the "Description" field.
9. Select how often you would like to meet in the "How often would you like to meet?" field.
10. Choose the day of the week you would like to meet in the "Which day of the week?" field.
11. Select the Start Time for your meetings in the "Start Time" field.
12. Choose the End Time for your meetings in the "End Time" field.
13. Enter the number of participants you plan to bring in the "How many participants do you plan to bring?" field.
14. Confirm if you are a coach in the "Are you a coach?" field.
15. If applicable, provide a Session Topic in the "Session Topic" field.
16. If the topic is not on the list, provide a "Session Topic other" in the "Session Topic other" field.
17. Enter the location of your meetings in the "Location" field.
18. If the location is not on the list, provide a "Location other" in the "Location other" field.
19. Enter your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
20. Enter your Contact Email in the "Contact Email" field.
21. If applicable, provide any Additional Info in the "Additional Info" field.
22. Confirm that you have read and agree to the Terms of Service in the "I have read and agree to the Terms of Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Schedule Date**: Select a date you are available to meet.
* **Schedule Time**: Choose a time slot for your meetings.
* **Group Name**: Enter a unique name for your study group.
* **Description**: A brief description of your study group, if applicable.
* **How often would you like to meet?**: Select how frequently you'd like to meet, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Which day of the week?**: Choose a day of the week you'd like to meet.
* **Start Time**: Select the start time for your meetings.
* **End Time**: Choose the end time for your meetings.
* **How many participants do you plan to bring?**: Enter the number of participants you plan to bring.
* **Are you a coach?**: Confirm if you are a coach for the group.
* **Session Topic**: A description of the topic you will be covering in your sessions, if applicable.
* **Session Topic other**: If the topic is not listed, provide your own topic in this field.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your meetings.
* **Location other**: If the location is not on the list, provide your own location in this field.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your study group, if applicable.
* **I have read and agree to the Terms of Service**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
