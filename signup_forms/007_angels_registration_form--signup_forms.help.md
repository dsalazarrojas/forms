# Angels Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Start by filling in your basic contact information in fields 1-3 (Applicant Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
* In field 4 (Date of Birth), enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* In field 5 (Registration Intro), read the welcome note and acknowledge that you are interested in becoming an Angel mentor.
* In field 6 (Program Type), choose the program that you are interested in, selecting all that apply.
* In field 7 (Motivation), describe what motivates you to become an Angel mentor.
* Fields 8-12 are related to your availability and experience:
	+ Field 8 (Availability) and 9 (Hours per Week): indicate when you are available to mentor and how many hours per week you can commit.
	+ Field 10 (Mentoring Experience): select your level of experience, and field 11 (Experience Details) is an optional field for further explanation of your experience.
* Fields 13-15 are related to your background:
	+ Field 13 (Skills): select all the skills you can offer.
	+ Field 14 (Preferred Age Group): indicate the age range you prefer to mentor, if any.
	+ Field 15 (Education Level): select your highest level of education attained.
* Fields 16-18 are related to your background and preferences:
	+ Field 16 (Occupation): describe your current occupation.
	+ Field 17 (Background Check): acknowledge your willingness to undergo a background check.
	+ Field 18 (References): indicate your willingness to provide references.
* Field 19 (How Heard) is optional, and you can select where you heard about the program.
* Field 20 (Additional Comments or Questions): provide any additional information or questions you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Registration Intro (5)**: This field is a welcome note to acknowledge your interest in becoming an Angel mentor.
* **Which Angel Program Are You Interested In (6)**: Select all the programs that interest you from the list.
* **What motivates you to become an Angel mentor (7)**: Describe your reasons for becoming an Angel mentor.
* **Availability (8)**: Select your availability to mentor (Weekdays - mornings, Weekdays - afternoons, Weekdays - evenings, Weekends, or Flexible schedule).
* **How many hours per week can you commit (9)**: Select the number of hours per week you can dedicate to mentoring.
* **Do you have previous mentoring or volunteering experience (10)**: Select your level of experience.
* **Please describe your experience (11)**: Provide details about your experience, if any.
* **What skills or talents can you offer (12)**: Select all the skills or talents you can offer.
* **Preferred Age Group to Mentor (13)**: Indicate the age range you prefer to mentor, if any.
* **Highest Level of Education (14)**: Select your highest level of education attained.
* **Current Occupation (15)**: Describe your current occupation.
* **Are you willing to undergo a background check (16)**: Acknowledge your willingness to undergo a background check.
* **Can you provide professional references (17)**: Indicate your willingness to provide references.
* **How did you hear about this program (18)**: Select where you heard about the program.
* **Additional Comments or Questions (19)**: Provide any additional information or questions you might have.
* **I agree to abide by the program guidelines and policies (20)**: Confirm your agreement to abide by the program guidelines and policies.

Please note that some fields are required, marked as (required: true) in the form schema. Ensure you complete all required fields to submit your registration form.
