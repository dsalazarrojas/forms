This help guide is intended to be a reference for users who are interested in signing up for our academic support program. The purpose of this form is to gather information about users' interests and needs, so we can provide tailored support. Please note that this form is not a formal application for services, but rather a way for us to understand what kind of support users are looking for.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

## Purpose
The form is used to express your interest in our academic support services. It will help us understand your needs so that we can follow up with available options.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by providing your personal details such as your first name, last name, email address, and student ID number.
2.  Next, choose your academic level (freshman, sophomore, junior, senior, or graduate).
3.  Select your major or program of study.
4.  Choose the type of academic support services that you are interested in (one-on-one tutoring, group tutoring, writing center assistance, etc.).
5.  List the subject areas that you are struggling with or need help with.
6.  Indicate how urgently you need academic support.
7.  Select the times of the day and week when you are available for support sessions.
8.  Choose your preferred format for support sessions (in-person, online, or either).
9.  If you have used academic support services before, indicate whether they were helpful or not.
10.  Provide any additional information about your academic needs that may help us tailor support to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
*   **Last name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
*   **Email address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter your institutional email address.
*   **Phone number** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred phone number for us to contact you, enter it here.
*   **Student ID number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter your student ID number.
*   **Academic level** (`academic_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your academic level (freshman, sophomore, junior, senior, or graduate).
*   **Major or program** (`major`, `text`, required): Enter your major or program of study.
*   **Which academic support services are you interested in** (`services_interested`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the services that you are interested in (one-on-one tutoring, group tutoring, writing center assistance, etc.).
*   **What subject areas do you need help with** (`subject_areas`, `text`, required): List specific courses or topics that you are struggling with or need help with.
*   **How urgently do you need academic support** (`urgency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how urgently you need academic support (immediately, within the next few weeks, before midterms or finals, or general ongoing support).
*   **When are you available for support sessions** (`preferred_times`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the times of the day and week when you are available for support sessions.
*   **Preferred format for support sessions** (`format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred format for support sessions (in-person, online, or either).
*   **Have you used academic support services before** (`previous_support`, `select_one`, required): If you have used academic support services before, indicate whether they were helpful or not.
*   **Any additional information about your academic needs** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your academic needs that may help us tailor support to your needs.

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
*   We will follow up with you after you submit this form to discuss your options for academic support.
