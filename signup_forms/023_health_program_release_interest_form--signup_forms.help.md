**Health Program Release Interest Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential participants about their interest in participating in a health program. The information collected will help the relevant team to identify potential participants who may be suitable for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions on this page, and select the options that apply to you.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to determine if you are interested in participating in the health program. Please select "Yes" if you are interested and "No" otherwise.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to enter your email address. Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number in the correct format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): This field is used to select a date. Please select a date from the calendar or enter a date in the correct format (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): This field is used to select a time. Please select a time from the time picker or enter a time in the correct format (e.g., `10:00 AM`).
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required/optional): This field is used to add any additional information you feel is relevant to your application. Please enter any comments or additional information you would like to provide.
* **Participant Interests** (`Participant Interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is used to select your interests. Please select the options that apply to you.

Note: Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
