# Registration Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Sign Up Form is designed for individuals to provide their personal information for a registration process. This form helps to gather necessary details to facilitate the registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "email" field, using the correct format (e.g., [your email address]).
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
6. Enter your address in the "address" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. Enter the time of signing in the "time of signing" field, using the format HH:MM AM/PM.
9. Select your preference from the "what do you want?" dropdown menu.
10. Select any additional options from the "what else?" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., [your email address]).
* **Phone Number (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (address)**: Enter your address.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Signing (time_of_signing)**: Enter the time of signing in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **What Do You Want? (select_1)**: Select your preference from the dropdown menu.
* **What Else? (select_2)**: Select any additional options from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure that you've not already registered before completing this form.
* If you have any doubts or issues, feel free to ask for assistance.
