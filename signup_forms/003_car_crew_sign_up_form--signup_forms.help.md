# Car Crew Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Car Crew Sign Up Form. Please follow these steps to ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. Please complete each section according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant name
* **Participant name** (Text, Required): This is where you should enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Contact email
* **Contact email** (Email, Required): This is the email address where you can be reached for event communications.

### Contact phone
* **Contact phone** (Text, Optional): This is your mobile phone number where you can be reached for event communications.

### Role interest
* **Role interest** (Select One, Required): Please select one of the available roles that best suits your skills and interests:
	+ Driver
	+ Mechanic
	+ Marshal
	+ Support
	+ Media

### Availability
* **Availability** (Select Multiple, Required): Please choose the days you are available to participate in events:
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
	+ Weekdays

### Prior experience details
* **Prior experience details** (Text, Optional): If you have any previous experience that is relevant to the role you selected, please provide a brief summary.

### Vehicle details
* **Vehicle details** (Text, Optional): If you are bringing your vehicle to an event, please provide its make, model, and year.

### Emergency contact information
* **Emergency contact information** (Text, Required): Please provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any special requirements or equipment needs, please let us know in the "Prior experience details" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for clarification on our website or social media channels.
* Once you submit the form, you will receive further instructions on how to proceed and what to expect next.
