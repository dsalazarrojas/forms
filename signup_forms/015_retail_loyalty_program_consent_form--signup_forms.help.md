# Retail Loyalty Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and consent from customers to participate in a retail loyalty program. This program aims to provide customers with exclusive rewards and promotions tailored to their preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Provide your personal details on page 1.
2. Choose how you would like to be contacted on page 2.
3. Enter your preferred location (if applicable) on page 3.
4. Select how often you would like to receive communications from us on page 4.
5. Confirm your enrollment in the program and how you would like to receive rewards on page 5.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Customer Information
* **Customer Information (Name, Phone Number, etc.)**: This section collects your basic contact information. Please enter your full name and any other relevant details you'd like us to have.

### Page 2: Contact Preferences
* **Contact Preferences (Email, Phone, SMS, Mail, In-app)**: Here, you can choose how you'd like to be contacted. Select all the channels you're comfortable with, and we'll use those methods to reach you.

### Page 3: Location
* **Location**: If you'd like to provide your location, please enter the name of your nearest store or a nearby city/town. This helps us tailor rewards and communications to your area.

### Page 4: Communication Preferences
* **How often would you like to be contacted? (Once, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly)**: Choose how often you'd like to receive communications from us, and we'll respect your preference.

### Page 5: Enrollment Preferences
* **How do you want to enroll in the loyalty program? (True, False)**: Please confirm whether you'd like to join our loyalty program and how you'd like to receive rewards.
