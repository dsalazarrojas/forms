# Bed And Breakfast Hosting Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us gather essential information about your bed and breakfast property, including its name, address, and details about your hosting participation. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as the information will be used to create a listing for guests to book stays.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your primary host name and email address in fields **Primary Host Name** and **Email Address**, respectively.
2. Provide your phone number in **Phone Number** field.
3. Choose the **Type of Property** from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the **Total Number of Guest Rooms**, the **Amenities Offered** (if applicable), and the **Maximum Total Guest Capacity** for your property.
5. Select the **Current Listing Availability** of your property.
6. Check the **I agree to the platform hosting standards and commission terms** box.
7. Indicate whether you have **short-term rental or commercial liability insurance** for your property.
8. Write a short **Short Property Description for Guests** (max 200 words).
9. Choose the **Date** you are submitting this form.
10. Ensure you have selected all applicable **Amenities Offered** and entered the **Maximum Total Guest Capacity** accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Host Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for hosting the bed and breakfast.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the host.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for guests to contact you.
* **Type of Property**: Choose the type of property you're hosting (e.g., Historic Home, Modern Villa, Farmhouse / Cottage, etc.).
* **Total Number of Guest Rooms**: Enter the total number of guest rooms available for booking.
* **Amenities Offered**: Select all applicable amenities offered at your property (e.g., Free Wi-Fi, Full Breakfast Included, etc.).
* **Maximum Total Guest Capacity**: Enter the maximum number of guests that can stay at your property.
* **Current Listing Availability**: Choose the current listing availability of your property (e.g., Year-Round, Seasonal Only, etc.).
* **Hosting Participation Terms**: This section explains the terms and conditions of hosting on our platform.
* **I agree to the platform hosting standards and commission terms**: Check this box to confirm you've read and agree to our hosting standards and commission terms.
* **Do you have short-term rental or commercial liability insurance?**: Indicate whether you have insurance for your property.
* **Short Property Description for Guests**: Write a short description of your property for guests to read.
* **Date**: Choose the date you're submitting this form.

By following these steps, you'll be able to provide us with the necessary information to list your bed and breakfast property on our platform. If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
