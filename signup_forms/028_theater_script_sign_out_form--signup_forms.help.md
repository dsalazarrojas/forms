# Theater Script Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Script Sign Out Form is used to sign out a theater script from the system. It captures user and script details for accurate tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the required field.
2. Enter your Email for contact purposes.
3. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
4. Enter the Time In for when you started working on the script.
5. Select the Script Name to associate with your work.
6. Choose the Script Type (Stage Play, Movie, or TV Show).
7. Select the Script Status (In Progress, Complete, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Enter the Script Out Time when you finished working on the script.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes for the user and script.
10. If you have assigned tools, enter their respective details for easy reference.

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your Email for contact purposes.

* **Script Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): Select the Script Name to associate with your work.

* **Script Status** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the Script Status (In Progress, Complete, Approved, or Rejected).

* **Script Out Date** (`17`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you finished working on the script.
