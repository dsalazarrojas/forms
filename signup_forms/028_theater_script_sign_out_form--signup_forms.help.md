# Theater Script Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Script Sign Out Form is used to sign out a theater script from the system. It captures user and script details for accurate tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the required field.
2. Enter your Email for contact purposes.
3. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
4. Enter the Time In for when you started working on the script.
5. Select the Script Name to associate with your work.
6. Choose the Script Type (Stage Play, Movie, or TV Show).
7. Select the Script Status (In Progress, Complete, Approved, or Rejected).
8. Enter the Script Out Time when you finished working on the script.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes for the user and script.
10. If you have assigned tools, enter their respective details for easy reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your Email for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`4`, `date`, required): Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
* **Time In** (`5`, `time`, required): Enter the Time In for when you started working on the script.
* **Script Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): Select the Script Name to associate with your work.
* **Script Type** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose the Script Type (Stage Play, Movie, or TV Show).
* **Script Status** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the Script Status (In Progress, Complete, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Script Out Time** (`16`, `time`, required): Enter the Script Out Time when you finished working on the script.
* **Script Out Date** (`17`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you finished working on the script.
* **Script Out User** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the user name of the person who signed out the script.
* **Script Out Note** (`19`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the script sign out.
* **User ID** (`10`, `number`, optional): Enter your User ID for tracking purposes.
* **User Name** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **User Email** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter your Email for contact purposes.
* **User Phone** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **User Note** (`14`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the user.
* **Assigned Tool ID** (`20`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`21`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool Email** (`22`, `text`, optional): Enter the Email of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool Phone** (`23`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool Note** (`24`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the assigned tool.
* **Form ID** (`25`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of the form for tracking purposes.
