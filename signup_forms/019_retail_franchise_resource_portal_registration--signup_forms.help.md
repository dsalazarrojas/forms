# Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration form is designed to collect information from retail franchisees to be listed in the Retail Franchise Resource Portal. This form is intended to create a comprehensive directory of retail franchises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your retail franchisee information, including your name, email, phone number, and store details.
2. Provide the name of your franchisor (the company that owns your franchise).
3. Enter your store location and store number (if applicable).
4. Choose a date and time for your registration.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your franchise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retail Franchise Resource Portal
* **Retail Franchise Resource Portal** (`retail_franchise_resource_portal`, text, required: false): Enter your retail franchise resource portal name.
### Franchise Staff
* **Franchise Staff** (`franchise_staff`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the staff member responsible for this franchise.
### Affiliated Franchisee
* **Affiliated Franchisee** (`affiliated_franchisee`, text, required: false): If you are affiliated with another franchisee, enter their name or company name.
### Email
* **Email** (`franchisee_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`franchisee_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
### Franchisor Name
* **Franchisor Name** (`franchisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the franchisor company.
### Other Franchisor Name
* **Other Franchisor Name** (`franchisor_name_other`, text, required: false): If the franchisor name is not listed above, enter the name of the franchisor company.
### Franchisor Franchise
* **Franchisor Franchise** (`franchisor_name_franchise`, text, required: false): Enter the franchisor franchise name (e.g. "McDonald's" for McDonald's franchise).
### Other Franchisor Franchise
* **Other Franchisor Franchise** (`franchisor_name_franchise_other`, text, required: false): If the franchisor franchise is not listed above, enter the franchise name.
### Store Number
* **Store Number** (`store_number`, text, required: false): Enter your store number.
### Store Location
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, required: false): Enter your store location (e.g. "123 Main St, Anytown, USA").
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to be listed in the portal.
### Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to be listed in the portal.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your franchise.
### Other Store Location
* **Other Store Location** (`store_location_other`, text, required: false): If your store location is not listed above, enter the location.
### Other Store Number
* **Other Store Number** (`store_number_other`, text, required: false): If your store number is not listed above, enter the number.

## Tips
