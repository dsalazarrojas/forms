# Support Code Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Support Code Giveaway Registration form. It's a simple form designed to collect basic participant information for support code giveaways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name.
2. Provide your Participant Email address.
3. Enter your Participant Phone number.
4. If applicable, specify your Participant Company.
5. If applicable, specify your Participant Department.
6. Enter your Participant Job Title.
7. Enter the Support Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a department within your company, enter the department name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Support Code** (`support_code`, text, optional): Enter the support code.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or need clarification, contact form@gic.mx.
