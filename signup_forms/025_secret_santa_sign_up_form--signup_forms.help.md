# Secret Santa Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Secret Santa Sign Up Form is used to sign up for a Secret Santa gift exchange. Fill out this form to participate in the exchange and provide your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is the name that will be used to identify you in the exchange.
2. Select your preferred name from the "Name" dropdown list. Please choose a name from the list, or suggest another name if you don't see yours listed.
3. Select "Submit" to complete your sign-up. If you'd like to cancel your sign-up, select "Cancel".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your own name as you'd like it to be referred to in the exchange.
* **Name**: Select a name from the list of participants to receive a gift from. If you don't see your name, suggest a friend's name.
* **Name**: This field is not used, but is a duplicate label.
* **Email**: This field is not used, as it's a hint and not a required field.
* **Name**: This is the list of participants to choose from for the Secret Santa gift exchange.
* **Submit**: Select this option to complete your sign-up. If you'd like to cancel, select "Cancel". 

Note: The field "Name Hint" and "Email Hint" are hints for the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively, and are not actually used in the form. The label "Name" on page 3 is a duplicate. The "Name" field on page 3 and 4 are the same field.
