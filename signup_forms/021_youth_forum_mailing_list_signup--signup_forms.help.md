# Youth Forum Mailing List Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Forum Mailing List Signup form is designed for individuals to join the youth forum mailing list. It collects information about the users to facilitate communication and engagement within the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. Please use a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your interests from the Interests field. You can choose multiple options.
6. If applicable, enter a date in the Date field.
7. If applicable, enter a time in the Time field.
8. Add a note in the Note field, if you have any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name you go by.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the last name you go by.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the interests that align with your profile.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you'd like to be included in future events or activities.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you'd like to be included in future events or activities.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* Be accurate when selecting your interests to ensure you receive relevant information.
* Keep your note field concise and relevant to your interests and activities.
