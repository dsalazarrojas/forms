# Arts & Crafts Fair Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from artisans and crafters who want to participate in the Arts & Crafts Fair. The information collected will be used to determine the suitability of your work for the fair and to ensure a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review your application carefully before submitting it.
3. Make sure you have read and agree to the terms and conditions before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Business or Studio Name** (3, text, required): If you have a business or studio name, please enter it here.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Please enter your active email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Please enter your active phone number where we can contact you.
* **Street Address** (6, text, required): Please enter your street address.
* **City** (7, text, required): Please enter your city or town.
* **State or Province** (8, text, required): Please select your state or province.
* **Zip or Postal Code** (9, text, required): Please enter your zip or postal code.
* **Primary Craft or Art Category** (10, select_one, required): Please select the primary category for your craft or art.
* **Description of Your Crafts or Arts** (11, text, required): Please describe your crafts or art in detail.
* **Years in Business** (12, number, optional): If applicable, please enter the number of years you have been in business.
* **Booth Size Required** (13, select_one, required): Please select the booth size you require.
* **Booth Fee or Rental Cost** (14, number, optional): If applicable, please enter the booth fee or rental cost.
* **Electricity Required** (15, select_one, optional): If you require electricity, please select 'Yes'.
* **Special Setup or Equipment Needs** (16, text, optional): If you have any special setup or equipment needs, please describe them.
* **Event Date Applying For** (17, date, required): Please select the date you are applying for.
* **Prior Participation in This Fair** (18, select_one, optional): If you have participated in this fair before, please select 'Yes'.
* **Insurance Certificate** (19, select_one, optional): If you have an insurance certificate, please select 'Yes'.
* **I Agree to Fair Terms and Conditions** (20, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Information** (21, text, optional): If you have any additional information to provide, please enter it here.
