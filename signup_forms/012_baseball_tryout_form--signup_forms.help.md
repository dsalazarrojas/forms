# Baseball Tryout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Tryout Form is designed to collect essential information from baseball players who are interested in trying out for a team. This form is used to streamline the tryout process and ensure that all necessary information is gathered efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, and phone number.
2. If you are under 18, please provide your parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number.
3. Indicate the date and location of the tryout.
4. Select your primary and secondary baseball positions you are interested in trying out for.
5. If you have prior baseball experience, please indicate the years of experience you have.
6. If you have previously played for a team, please provide the team's name or league.
7. Confirm whether you have medical clearance to play and if you have any current injuries or medical conditions.
8. Finally, agree to the liability waiver and team terms, and confirm that all information provided is accurate and truthful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter a valid phone number that can be used for communication.
* **Parent/Guardian name**: If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent/Guardian email**: If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent/Guardian phone**: If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Tryout date**: Enter the date you are attending the tryout.
* **Tryout location**: Enter the location of the tryout.
* **Primary position**: Select the primary position you are interested in trying out for.
* **Secondary position**: Select the secondary position you are interested in trying out for (if applicable).
* **Years of experience**: Enter the number of years you have played baseball.
* **Previous team**: If you have previously played for a team, enter the team's name or league.
* **Medical clearance**: Confirm whether you have medical clearance to play.
* **Injuries or conditions**: If you have any current injuries or medical conditions, describe them in detail.
* **Liability waiver**: Agree to the liability waiver and team terms.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate and truthful.

Note: Please ensure that all fields marked as "required" are completed accurately and truthfully.
