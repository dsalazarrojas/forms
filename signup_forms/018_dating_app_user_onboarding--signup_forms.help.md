# Dating App User Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dating App User Onboarding form is designed to help new users create a comprehensive profile, providing essential information to help potential matches understand their interests, preferences, and characteristics. This form collects information on various aspects of a user's life, such as their relationship status, education, occupation, language skills, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field, ensuring you provide accurate and detailed answers.
2. Fill in the "Signup Info" page with your basic details, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
3. Move on to the "User Profile" page, where you can describe yourself and your preferences in more depth.
4. On the "Relationship Status" page, choose your current relationship status.
5. In the "User Photos" section, select which photos you'd like to share with other users.
6. Provide your email address and other contact information on the "Email" and "Phone" pages.
7. Input your date of birth and time zone on the "Date of Birth" and "Time" pages.
8. Write down any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
9. Specify your location and whether you have any language skills on the "Location" and "Languages" pages.
10. Indicate your level of education and whether you have any special education type or work experience on the "Education Level" and "Work Experience" pages.
11. List your hobbies and interests on the "Hobbies" and "Interests" pages.
12. Provide details about your family status and number of siblings on the "Family Status" and "Siblings Number" pages.
13. Finally, review and submit your completed profile.
