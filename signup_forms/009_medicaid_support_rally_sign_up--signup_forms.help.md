# medicaid_support_rally_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users about a medicaid support rally sign-up event. It gathers basic details about the user and the event they are interested in or attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the user information section with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide event information, including the date and time of the event.
3. Enter your contact information, including address and phone number.
4. Select your attendance status for the event (Yes, No, or Maybe).
5. Add any additional event details in the note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text)**: Fill out your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Event Information (text)**: Enter the date and time of the event you are interested in or attending.
* **Contact Info (text)**: Enter your address and phone number.
* **Event Date (date)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (time)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location (text)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Attendance Status (select-one)**: Select your attendance status for the event. You can choose from Yes, No, or Maybe.
* **Event Details (note)**: Add any additional details about the event.
* **Submit (text)**: Click this button to submit your form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizers or administrators for clarification.
