# Kids Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kids Summer Camp Registration Form is a document used to collect information from parents or guardians about their children who are interested in attending the summer camp. This form helps the camp staff gather the necessary details to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Please fill out the personal information section with the child's details.
2. Provide contact information for the child's parent or guardian.
3. If the child has a medical condition, please provide any relevant information in the Medical Info section.
4. If necessary, provide medical notes for the camp staff to be aware of.
5. Authorize the camp staff to contact you or the child in case of an emergency.
6. Finally, provide any additional contact phone number for the camp staff to reach out to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Info
• **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, text, required: false): This is the section where you will provide the child's full name, age, and any other personal details.

### Parents
• **Parents** (`Parents`, text, required: false): Please list the names of the child's parents or guardians who are responsible for their care.

### Contact Info
• **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Childs History
• **Childs History** (`Childs History`, text, required: false): If the child has a relevant medical history, please provide any important information that the camp staff should be aware of.

### Medical Info
• **Medical Info** (`Medical Info`, text, required: false): Please provide any medical information about the child, such as allergies, conditions, or previous injuries.

### Medical Notes
• **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, text, required: false): This is the place to provide any additional medical notes or information that the camp staff should be aware of.

### Authorization
• **Authorization** (`Authorization`, select_one, required: false): Please choose "Yes" if you give the camp staff permission to contact you or the child in case of an emergency.

### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required: false): Provide an additional contact phone number for the camp staff to reach out to you in case of an emergency.

### Email
• **Email** (`Email`, text, required: false): This is the email address of the parent or guardian who will be responsible for the child's care.

### Parent Name
• **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required: false): Please provide your name as a parent or guardian of the child.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to the camp staff for clarification.
* You can always contact the camp staff if you have any questions or need further assistance.
