# Fitness Program Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help our fitness program team create a customized fitness program that meets your specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by following these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Details**, including your preferred **Location** where you would like to work out.
2. Choose a **Fitness Program** that suits your goals and interests (e.g., Weight Lifting, Yoga, etc.).
3. Select your **Preferred Time** of day when you would like to work out (e.g., Mornings, Afternoons, etc.).
4. Indicate how often you would like to work out (e.g., Daily, 3x per week, etc.).
5. Select your **Preferred Trainer** who you would like to work with.
6. Fill in your **Email** and **Phone Number** so we can contact you.
7. Choose your **Language** of communication (e.g., English, Spanish, etc.).
8. Provide **Emergency Contact Information**, including the **Relationship**, **Phone Number**, **Email**, and **Address** of your emergency contact.
9. Check if you have any **Medical Conditions** or **Medication** that we should be aware of.
10. Indicate if you have **Medical Authorization**, and if so, what type of authorization it is.
11. Finally, fill in any **Additional Comments** or **Emergency Contact Notes** that would help us better understand your needs.
