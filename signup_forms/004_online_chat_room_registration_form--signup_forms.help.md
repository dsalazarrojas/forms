# Online Chat Room Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Chat Room Registration Form is designed to collect information from users to verify their identity and assign them a role in the chat room. This form helps us understand who is joining the chat room and what role they will be playing in it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
4. Choose your **Role** from the dropdown menu:
	* **Admin**: Grants you access to admin-only features.
	* **Moderator**: Allows you to help manage the chat room.
	* **Participant**: Enables you to participate in discussions.
5. Enter your **Date** of birth or registration (MM/DD/YYYY) in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., driver's license, passport, or birth certificate). This will help us verify your identity.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role**: Choose your role in the chat room from the dropdown menu. This will determine what features you have access to.
* **Date**: Enter your date of birth or registration. This will help us verify your age or eligibility to join the chat room.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If you have any issues with the form or need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for registration purposes only and is not intended for general contact or feedback.
