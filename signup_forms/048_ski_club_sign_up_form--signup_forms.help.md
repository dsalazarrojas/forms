# ski_club_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ski_club_sign_up_form is a registration form designed for users who wish to join the ski club. This form gathers essential personal data, emergency contact information, and ski level preference to facilitate smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Be aware that fields marked as optional are not required but will aid in the registration process.
3. For fields like "Register Date" and "Confirm Register Date", ensure that the dates match each other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the ski club records.
* **Email**: <Name> (<type: email, required: false>): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Age**: <Name> (<type: number, required: false>): Enter your age, if applicable.
* **Ski Level**: <Name> (<type: select_one, required: false>): Choose your skiing level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Register Date**: <Name> (<type: date, required: false>): Enter the date you wish to register with the ski club.
* **Confirm Register Date**: <Name> (<type: date, required: false>): Confirm the date entered in the previous field.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Describe the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: <Name> (<type: email, required: false>): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Describe the type of relationship between you and the emergency contact.
* **Guardian Name**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter the name of the guardian person (if applicable).
* **Guardian Relationship**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Describe the relationship between you and the guardian person.
* **Guardian Phone**: <Name> (<type: text, required: false>): Enter the phone number of the guardian person (if applicable).

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling in the form to ensure smooth and efficient processing.
- If you encounter any issues or have doubts, contact the ski club administration for assistance.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in processing your registration.
