# Mindfulness Programs Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective leads interested in our Mindfulness Programs. Please complete it to help us better understand your needs and generate relevant leads for our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. Each page is separate and self-explanatory. You can navigate through the pages using the buttons at the top of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mindfulness Programs Lead Generation Form** (`lead_generation_form_page_1`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is not a field to fill out but rather the heading of the form.
* **Program Details** (`lead_generation_form_page_2`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the program you are interested in. This will help us understand your interests.
* **Additional Information** (`lead_generation_form_page_3`, text, required): Please share any additional information about yourself or your program needs that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Contact Information** (`lead_generation_form_page_4`, text, required): Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you about our programs.
* **Submission Information** (`lead_generation_form_page_5`, text, required): Please provide any submission information related to your program interest (e.g., previous programs you've attended).
* **Lead-Generation-Form-Page-6** (`lead_generation_form_page_6`, text, required): This field is currently empty and should be ignored.
