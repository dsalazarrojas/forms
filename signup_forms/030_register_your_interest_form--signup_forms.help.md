# Register Your Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Register Your Interest Form is used to provide basic contact information and indicate areas of interest for an individual or company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please ensure you enter a valid email address that you can be contacted at.
3. If you represent a company, fill in the Company field with your company name.
4. Indicate your areas of interest by selecting from the list of options provided in the Interests field. You can select multiple interests.
5. Add any additional comments or notes to the Message field if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your personal identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your personal identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you can be contacted at.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): If you are representing a company, enter your company name.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the provided interests to indicate your areas of interest.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your interest.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
- Ensure your Email address is valid and up-to-date.
- Be specific and clear in your interests and message to help us better understand your needs.
