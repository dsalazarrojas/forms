# FMLA Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FMLA Policy Form is designed to collect and store company information and policies in a centralized location. This form is used to gather essential details about the company, including its policies, contact information, and policy revision dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name, which is a required field.
2. Enter the effective date of the policy, which is also a required field.
3. Provide the company's contact person's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the company's phone number and email.
5. Fill in the contact person's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Policies** (`company_policies`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the company's policies.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
* **Policy Details** (`policy_details`, note, required): Enter a brief description of the policy.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, required): Enter the effective date of the policy in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, optional): Enter the last updated date of the policy.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, required): Enter the company's email address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter the contact person's address.
* **Policy Revision Date** (`policy_revision_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the policy revision.
