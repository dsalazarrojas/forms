# Mobile App Download Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mobile App Download Guide form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in downloading our mobile app. The form helps us to better understand the user's preferences and contact method, as well as their device information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields (if any) and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Select the correct contact method from the dropdown list.
4. Enter any additional information about your device (if necessary).
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information you'd like us to know about you, such as your username or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted: through email or phone.
* **Device Info** (`device_info`, Text, Optional): If you want to provide more information about your device, such as its make, model, or operating system, this is the place to do so.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure that we can contact you as desired.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll review your information and get back to you shortly after receiving your submission.
