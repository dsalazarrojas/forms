# Mobile App Launch Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mobile App Launch Lead Generation Form is designed to collect data from potential leads interested in launching their own mobile app within the next 6 weeks. This form will help our sales team to schedule follow-up meetings with the leads to discuss further details about the mobile app launch.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review each field carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
4. If you are filling out the form on behalf of someone else, make sure they have given you permission to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your interest in our mobile app launch?**: This field is used to identify if you are interested in launching a mobile app.
	+ Please answer with a brief description of your interest in the mobile app launch.
* **name**: This field is used to collect your name for communication purposes.
	+ Please enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **email**: This field is used to collect your email address for follow-up communication.
	+ Please enter a valid email address that you check frequently.
* **company**: This field is used to identify your company (optional).
	+ If you are not affiliated with a company, please enter "None" or leave blank.
* **What is your time zone?**: This field is used to schedule follow-up meetings.
	+ Please select your time zone from the provided options.
* **When are you available for a pre-launch meeting?**: This field is used to schedule a pre-launch meeting.
	+ Please enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **What time are you available?**: This field is used to schedule the pre-launch meeting time.
	+ Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **What language would you like to communicate in?**: This field is used to ensure that our communication is in your preferred language.
	+ Please select the language you would like to communicate in.
* **How would you like to be followed up?**: This field is used to determine the follow-up method.
	+ Please select one or more of the provided options.
* **Do you confirm your email address is correct?**: This field is used for email validation.
	+ Please confirm that the email address entered is correct.
* **Is the interest in the mobile app launch?**: This field is used to validate your interest in the mobile app launch.
	+ Please answer with "True" if you are interested, otherwise "False".
* **Is this information correct?**: This field is used for validation purposes.
	+ Please review all the entered information and confirm if it is correct.
* **What is your job title?**: This field is used to identify your job title (optional).
	+ If you don't have a job title, please enter "None" or leave blank.
* **Company 2**: This field is used to identify a second company (optional).
	+ If you are not affiliated with a second company, please enter "None" or leave blank.
* **What time are you available? (Second Company)**: This field is used to schedule a meeting time for the second company.
	+ Please enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **What timezone are you in? (Second Company)**: This field is used to schedule the second company's follow-up meeting in their time zone.
	+ Please select their time zone from the provided options.
* **What language would you like to communicate in? (Second Company)**: This field is used to determine the language for communication with the second company.
	+ Please select the language you would like to communicate in.
* **How would you like to be followed up? (Second Company)**: This field is used to determine the follow-up method for the second company.
	+ Please select one or more of the provided options.
* **Email 2**: This field is used to collect the second email address for the second company (optional).
	+ If you are not entering an email for the second company, please leave blank.
* **Phone 2**: This field is used to collect the phone number of the second company (optional).
	+ If you are not entering a phone number for the second company, please leave blank.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help with the form, please contact us at [your contact information].
* If you are filling out the form on behalf of someone else, please ensure they have given you permission to do so.
