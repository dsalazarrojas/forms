# Newsletter Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to sign up for a newsletter. By filling out this form, you'll be able to choose your preferred newsletter topics and frequency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to use a valid email address that you have access to.
4. Select your preferred **Email Opt-in** option (True or False).
5. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select whether you want to **Confirm Phone** (Yes or No).
7. Choose your preferred **Newsletter Frequency** (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
8. Select the topics you're interested in from the **Newsletter Topics** list (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name will be used for personalized content recommendations. Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for personalized content recommendations.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Confirm Email**: Please confirm your email address by selecting "Yes" if it matches your previous entry.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Confirm Phone**: Select "Yes" if your phone number matches your previous entry.
* **Email Opt-in**: Select "True" if you want to receive emails from us.
* **Newsletter Frequency**: Choose how often you want to receive newsletters (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Newsletter Topics**: Select the topics you're interested in for more personalized content.
