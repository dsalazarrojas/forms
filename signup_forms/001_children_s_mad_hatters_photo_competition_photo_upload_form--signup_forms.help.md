# CHILDREN'S MAD HATTERS PHOTO COMPETITION Photo Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CHILDREN'S MAD HATTERS PHOTO COMPETITION Photo Upload Form is designed to collect information and a photo from children participating in the "Children's Mad Hatters" competition. This form is used to gather details about the child's entry, including their name, school name, and photo.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label. This includes the child's first name, last name, age, school name, parent or guardian name, contact email, and contact phone number.
2. Provide a link to a photo of the child wearing their hat design.
3. Optionally, you can upload a photo or provide other relevant details in the "Entry Details" section.
4. Read and agree to the competition terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child First Name**: \* Enter the first name of the child entering the competition.
* **Child Last Name**: \* Enter the last name of the child entering the competition.
* **Child Age**: \* Enter the child's age. Please note that the competition is open to children of all school ages.
* **School Name**: \* Enter the name of the child's school.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: \* Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is submitting this entry.
* **Contact Email**: \* Enter a valid email address to which we can send notifications and updates.
* **Contact Phone Number**: \* Enter a valid phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Hat Design Description**: \* Tell us about your child's outrageous hat design.
* **Photo Upload Instructions**: \* Please ensure the photo clearly shows the child wearing the hat.
* **Link to Photo**: \* Provide a link to the photo you have uploaded or would like to use.
* **Terms Agreement**: \* Confirm that you have read and agree to the competition terms and conditions.
