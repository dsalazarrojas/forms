# Webinar Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Subscription Form is used to collect information from users who want to subscribe to our webinars. To use this form, please make sure you are actually interested in subscribing to our webinars.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the webinars you are interested in by choosing one or multiple options from the "Subscribe To Our Webinars" field.
2. Fill out your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Optionally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date in the "Date" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscribe To Our Webinars**: Select the webinars you are interested in. This field allows you to choose from multiple options.
* **Name**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Optionally, enter your phone number in this field. If you don't want to provide your phone number, you can leave this field blank.
* **Date**: This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have a specific date in mind for the webinar you want to attend.
