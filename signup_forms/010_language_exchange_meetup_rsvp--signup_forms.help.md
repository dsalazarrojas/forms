# Language Exchange Meetup RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to RSVP to a language exchange meetup, providing the necessary information to organizers to plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, enter your name in the "Name" field.
- Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
- In the "RSVP" field, select "True" to indicate that you will attend the meetup or "False" to indicate that you won't.
- List your spoken and written languages in the "Language Preferences" field, using the provided options.
- Select the start date and end date of the meetup using the respective fields.
- Choose the start and end times of the event.
- Enter the location of the meetup in the "Location" field.
- Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
- Finally, select your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the meetup.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, Select One, False): Select "True" to RSVP to the meetup or "False" to decline.
* **Language Preferences** (`Language Preferences`, Select Multiple, False): Choose your spoken and written languages.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, Date, False): Select the start date of the meetup.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, Date, False): Select the end date of the meetup.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, Time, False): Choose the start time of the meetup.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, Time, False): Choose the end time of the meetup.
* **Location** (`Location`, Text, False): Enter the location of the meetup.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, Note, False): Add any additional comments about the meetup.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, Select One, False): Choose your preferred language from the options.
