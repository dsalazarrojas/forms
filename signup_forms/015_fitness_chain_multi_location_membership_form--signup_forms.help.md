# Fitness Chain Multi Location Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a member's information and emergency contact details for a fitness chain with multiple locations. It includes fields for personal information, emergency contact, medical conditions, and billing details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the member's first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the member's email address and phone number.
3. Select the membership plan chosen by the member.
4. Choose the location of the fitness chain's branch where the member would like to visit.
5. If the member has an emergency contact, enter their first and last name, email address, and phone number. You can also select the relationship between the member and the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Sibling, Spouse, Friend, or Other) and add any additional notes.
6. Enter any medical conditions the member has.
7. Select a date and time for the member's visit.
8. Add any notes related to the member's membership plan.
9. Enter the billing name and details, including the address, city, state, zip, country, card type, and card number.
