# email_sign_up_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for user sign-up or onboarding, allowing users to provide necessary information to complete their registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "user_name" field.
3. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. If applicable, select your preference from the "user_name" field with a dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that will be used for communication and login purposes. Please use a valid email address that you have access to.
* **user_name** (user_name, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it would be displayed on our system. This field is not required but recommended for identification purposes.
* **phone** (phone_number, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted through phone calls or SMS. This field is not required.
* **user_name (Yes/No)** (user_name, `select_one`, optional): If you want to be contacted through phone or email, select "Yes" to be on our contact list. If not, select "No". This field is optional.
* **user_name (Yes/No)** (user_name, `select_multiple`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of the previous one, but it's possible that it's intended for a different purpose. If you select "Yes" here, you will be contacted through both phone and email. Select "No" if you don't want to be contacted. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* This form is for sign-up purposes only; do not use it for other purposes.
