The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants in a shadowing experience, where they are paired with a medical professional for a short period to gain insight into the professional's work. This form is meant to help assess the participant's satisfaction and engagement with the experience, as well as gather contact information for the supervisor who managed the shadowing session.

The form will be filled out by the participant, and will require information such as their name, email, and contact information for their supervisor. They will also be asked to rate their satisfaction with the experience and provide comments about the session.

To fill out this form, the participant will need to navigate through the pages of the form and enter the requested information. Each field is designed to capture specific details about the shadowing experience.

## Purpose
The Shadowing Sign Off Form is used to gather feedback from participants in a shadowing experience, to improve the quality of the experience and enhance the skills of future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Medical Professional** field.
2. Describe your **Shadowing Experience** in the provided text box.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your shadowing experience in the **Shadowing Start Date** and **Shadowing End Date** fields, respectively.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the experience in the **Shadowing Satisfaction** field.
5. Provide any additional comments about the experience in the **Shadowing Comments** field.
6. Enter your signature, as proof of your completion of the form.
7. Enter your supervisor's contact information, including their title, name, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shadowing Sign Off Form** (Page 1): This is the header of the form, indicating that this is the Shadowing Sign Off Form.
* **Medical Professional** (Required): Enter your name, as the participant who took part in the shadowing experience.
* **Shadowing Experience** (Required): Describe your experience with the medical professional, including any notable events or observations.
* **Shadowing Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you started the shadowing experience.
* **Shadowing End Date** (Date): Enter the date the shadowing experience ended.
* **Shadowing Duration** (Text): Describe the length of time you shadowed the medical professional.
* **Shadowing Satisfaction** (Select One): Rate your satisfaction with the experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Shadowing Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments about the experience.
* **Shadowing Signature** (Note): This is where you sign and date the form, as proof of your completion.
* **Shadowing Agreement** (Email): Enter your email address, as a form of agreement to the terms of the shadowing experience.
* **Shadowing Supervisor Title** (Text): Enter your supervisor's title or role.
* **Shadowing Supervisor Name** (Text): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Shadowing Supervisor Email** (Email): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Shadowing Supervisor Phone** (Text): Enter your supervisor's phone number.

Note: The required fields are marked with "(Required)" next to their label.
