# 2021 Swfc Unit Master Prize Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who wish to participate in the 2021 Swfc Unit Master Prize Worksheet. Please complete the form with accurate and detailed information to ensure your entry is valid.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Personal Information section with your details.
2. Complete the Order Details section with your order preferences.
3. If applicable, add any additional comments or notes in the Additional Comments section.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you use to receive notifications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, optional): If you wish to provide your phone number, include it with the country code.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Provide your date of birth for age verification, if you choose to do so.
* **Age** (`age`, `integer`, optional): If you provided your date of birth, this field will be pre-populated. Otherwise, enter your age.
* **Mailing address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address for delivery purposes, if you choose to provide it.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number below.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, including the country code.
* **--- Order Details ---**: This is the section where you can select your order preferences.
* **Number of popcorn bags** (`popcorn_quantity`, `integer`, required): Enter the quantity of popcorn bags you wish to order.
* **Preferred popcorn flavor** (`popcorn_flavor`, `select_one`, required): Choose one from the available flavors: Butter, Caramel, Cheese, Sweet Corn, Sea Salt, or Mixed.
* **Special requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, such as gluten-free packaging or extra seasoning, enter them here.
* **Desired delivery date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you wish to receive your popcorn.
* **Is delivery address the same as mailing address?** (`delivery_address_same_as_mail`, `select_one`, required): Select yes if the delivery address is the same as your mailing address.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, optional): If you selected yes for the previous question, this field will be ignored. Otherwise, enter the delivery address where you wish to receive your order.
* **--- Experience & Referral ---**: This section is for additional details.
* **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience level with ordering popcorn: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): Select the primary source where you heard about us: Social media, Friend/Family, Website, Flyer/Poster, or Other.
* **Any special requirements or accommodations?** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, such as dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **--- Additional Comments ---**: This is an optional section to add any additional comments or notes.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* We appreciate your participation in the 2021 Swfc Unit Master Prize Worksheet.
