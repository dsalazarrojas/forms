# Business Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a necessary step for businesses to sign up for your services. Please complete this form to provide us with the necessary information about your business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Business Name**, which is the legal name of your business.
2. Enter the **Contact Person Name**, which is the primary contact person's name for your business.
3. Enter the **Email Address**, which is the primary email address associated with your business.
4. Enter the **Phone Number**, which is the primary phone number associated with your business.
5. Enter the **Business Address**, which is the street address of your business.
6. Enter the **City**, which is the city name where your business is located.
7. Enter the **State or Province**, which is the state or province where your business is located.
8. Enter the **Postal Code**, which is the ZIP or postal code associated with your business location.
9. Enter the **Country**, which is the country of operation for your business.
10. Select the **Type of Business**, which is the nature of your business (e.g., Retail, Services, Manufacturing, etc.).
11. (Optional) Enter the **Industry Sector**, which is the primary industry associated with your business.
12. (Optional) Enter the **Number of Employees**, which is the number of people employed by your business.
13. (Optional) Enter the **Annual Revenue**, which is an approximate annual revenue of your business.
14. (Optional) Enter the **Business Website**, which is the URL of your business website.
15. (Optional) Enter the **Years in Operation**, which is the number of years your business has existed.
16. (Optional) Enter the **Interest in Our Services**, which is what services you are interested in.
17. Select your preferred **Preferred Contact Method**, which is how you prefer to be contacted.
18. (Optional) If you wish to receive updates from us, check **Subscribe to Newsletter**.
19. Check **Agree to Terms and Conditions**, which is a necessary step before signing up.
20. Enter the **Sign Up Date**, which is the date you are signing up for our services.
