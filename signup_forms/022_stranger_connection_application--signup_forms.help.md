# Stranger Connection Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stranger Connection Application form is designed to gather basic contact information and preferences from users for the purpose of establishing a connection with a stranger. This form is typically used for social or professional networking, and the collected data will be used to facilitate communication and interaction between users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is valid and up-to-date.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Specify your location in the "Location" field.
6. List your interests in the "Interests" field.
7. Select your preferred communication method(s) in the "How you prefer to communicate" field. You can choose one or more options from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is commonly known or professionally known.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is commonly known or professionally known.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached, if applicable.
* **Location**: Enter your city, state, or zip code to help identify your location.
* **Interests**: List any hobbies, industries, or areas of expertise that you are interested in.
* **How you prefer to communicate**: Select one or more of the following options:
	+ **Email**: Send messages via email.
	+ **Text Message**: Send messages via text message.
	+ **Phone Call**: Have a voice conversation via phone call.
	+ **Video Chat**: Have a visual conversation via video call.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing your contact information and preferences.
* If you are experiencing technical difficulties or have questions, feel free to ask your support team for assistance.
* Remember to keep your communication preferences private and secure.
