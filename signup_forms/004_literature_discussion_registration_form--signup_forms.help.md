# Literature Discussion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a Literature Discussion Registration Form to help literature discussion groups plan and manage their sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the Session Details section to provide information about the session date, time, duration, and location.
2.  In the Participant Information section, enter the number of participants and select the participant age range and gender.
3.  Enter the contact email, phone number, and social media outreach method to reach out to the group.
4.  In the Group Details section, choose the group type, select the session frequency, and enter the group leaders' information.
5.  Fill out the Session Logistics section to provide more detailed information about the session duration, start and end date, and session notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Session Date** (id: 1, type: date, required: false): Enter the date of the session.
*   **Session Time** (id: 2, type: time, required: false): Enter the time of the session.
*   **Participants** (id: 3, type: number, required: false): Enter the number of participants.
*   **Contact Email** (id: 4, type: text, required: false): Enter the email of the contact person.
*   **Session Duration** (id: 5, type: time, required: false): Enter the duration of the session.
*   **Location** (id: 6, type: text, required: false): Enter the location of the session.
*   **Session Note** (id: 7, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the session.
*   **Group Name** (id: 8, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the group.
*   **Group Description** (id: 9, type: text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the group.
*   **Contact Phone** (id: 10, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Group Type** (id: 11, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the type of group (Book Club, Literature Discussion Group, or Book Study Group).
*   **Session Availability** (id: 12, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the days the session is available (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun, or Any).
*   **Participant Age Range** (id: 13, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select the age range of the participants (0-10, 11-20, 21-30, 31-40, 41-50, 51+).
*   **Outreach Method** (id: 14, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the method of outreach (Social Media, Email, or Phone).
*   **Participant Gender** (id: 15, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the gender of the participants (Male, Female).
*   **Session Frequency** (id: 16, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the frequency of the sessions (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
*   **Group Leaders** (id: 17, type: text, required: false): Enter the name(s) of the group leaders.
*   **Group Leaders Phone** (id: 18, type: text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the group leaders.
*   **Group Leaders Email** (id: 19, type: email, required: false): Enter the email of the group leaders.
*   **Library Outreach** (id: 20, type: select_one, required: false): Choose the library outreach method (School, Library, Community, Public Space).
*   **Session Capacity** (id: 21, type: number, required: false): Enter the maximum capacity of the session.
*   **Session Start Date** (id: 22, type: date, required: false): Enter the start date of the session.
*   **Session End Date** (id: 23, type: date, required: false): Enter the end date of the session.
*   **Session Notes** (id: 24, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the session.
*   **Library Outreach Note** (id: 25, type: note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the library outreach.

**Tips**

*   Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid any confusion.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the form is complete.
*   If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
