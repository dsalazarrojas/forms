# Recruitment Platform Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recruitment Platform Subscription Form is a tool to help job posters create a detailed job posting that attracts the right candidates. This form guides users through the process of creating a comprehensive job posting that meets the needs of the platform and its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Enter the location of the job in the "Location" field.
7. Select the type of job you are looking for in the "Job Type" field.
8. Select the level of the job in the "Job Level" field.
9. Choose the job category in the "Job Category" field.
10. Select the industries related to the job in the "Job Industries" field.
11. (Optional) Enter a description of the job type in the "Job Type Description" field.
12. Enter the required skills and qualifications for the job in the "Job Requirements" field.
13. Select relevant tags for the job in the "Job Tags" field.
14. Select the benefits of the job in the "Job Benefits" field.
15. Choose the job status in the "Job Status" field.
16. Enter a detailed description of the job in the "Job Description" field.
17. Enter the URL of the job posting.
18. (Optional) Enter a description of the job location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.
* **Job Title**: Enter a brief and descriptive title of the job posting.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Location**: Enter the city or region of the job posting.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job you are looking for (e.g. Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Intern).
* **Job Level**: Select the level of the job you are looking for (e.g. Junior, Mid-level, Senior).
* **Job Category**: Choose the category of the job you are looking for (e.g. Sales, Marketing, Customer Service).
* **Job Industries**: Select the industries related to the job you are looking for (e.g. Finance, Technology, Healthcare).
* **Job Type Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job type.
* **Job Requirements**: Enter the required skills and qualifications for the job.
* **Job Tags**: Select relevant tags for the job posting.
* **Job Benefits**: Select the benefits of the job posting.
* **Job Status**: Choose the status of the job posting (e.g. Active, Inactive).
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of the job posting.
* **Job Location**: Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Job URL**: Enter the URL of the job posting.
* **Job Category Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job category.
* **Job Industries Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job industries.
* **Job Requirements Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job requirements.
* **Job Tags Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job tags.
* **Job Benefits Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job benefits.
* **Job Location Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job location.
* **Job URL Description**: (Optional) Enter a description of the job URL.

Note: Fields with a "(Optional)" label are not required to be filled out.
