# Loyalty Rewards Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for the loyalty rewards program. By completing this form, you will be enrolled in the program and receive exclusive benefits and offers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred email format in the "Preferred Email Format" field. You can choose between HTML and Plain Text.
5. Select your preferred communication frequency in the "Preferred Communication Frequency" field. You can choose to receive daily, weekly, or monthly updates.
6. Read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" by checking the box.
7. Finally, check the "Agreement" box to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter a title for your subscription, if desired.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted for loyalty rewards program updates and offers.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed in loyalty rewards program communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted by the loyalty rewards program.
* **Preferred Email Format** (`preferred_email_format`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to receive email updates: in HTML format or plain text.
* **Preferred Communication Frequency** (`preferred_communication_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of loyalty rewards program updates you would like to receive: daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Read the terms and conditions of the loyalty rewards program by clicking on the "Terms and Conditions" link.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, checkbox, required): Check this box to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
