# Donor Drive Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Drive Signup Form is a crucial tool for our organization to gather information from potential donors. Completing this form will help us understand your interest in supporting our campaigns and tailor our outreach efforts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your accurate information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number. This is a required field.
* **Address**: Enter your residential or business address. This is a required field.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code. This is a required field.
* **Donor Type**: Select your type of donor from the options provided. This is a required field.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your donations. This is a required field.
* **Outreach**: Select the method(s) you would like to use for outreach. This is a required field.
* **Campaign**: Select the campaign you would like to support. This is a required field.
* **Message**: Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share. This is a required field.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the information you provided is accurate. This is a required field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share (optional).
