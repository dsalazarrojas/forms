# Gym Membership Tier Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Tier Selection Form is designed to help you choose the most suitable membership plan for your fitness goals and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your desired membership tier.
2. Enter your first and last name as required.
3. Choose your membership type (yes or no).
4. Select your desired plan (yes or no).
5. Enter your membership start date.
6. Enter your membership end date (note that you can only enter one date).
7. Enter your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Select your membership type plan (optional).
10. Confirm your understanding of our terms and conditions.
11. Select your desired plan 2 (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tier Selection** (`Tier Selection`, `select_one`, required): This is the initial membership type selection. Please choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to select your desired membership tier.
- **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as required for membership registration.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as required for membership registration.
- **Membership Type** (`Membership Type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between 'Yes' and 'No' to select your desired membership type.
- **Plan Selection** (`Plan Selection`, `select_one`, required): Select between 'Yes' and 'No' to select your desired plan for your membership.
- **Membership Start Date** (`Membership Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like your membership to start.
- **Membership End Date** (`Membership End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date your membership will end (note that you can only select one date).
- **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for membership registration.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for membership registration.
- **Membership Type Plan** (`Membership Type Plan`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for providing additional information about your membership type plan.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your understanding of our terms and conditions by selecting between 'Yes' and 'No'.
- **Plan Selection 2** (`Plan Selection 2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select between 'Yes' and 'No' to confirm your desired plan 2 for your membership.
