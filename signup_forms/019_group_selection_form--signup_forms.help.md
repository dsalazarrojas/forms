# Group Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Selection Form is a form designed to collect relevant information from users about a group, including email addresses, preferences, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Group Selection Form Page 1" as the starting page.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* "Group Email" and "Group Email 2" are both required email fields.
3. You can choose your group's presentation preferences by selecting one of the options in the "Group Presentation Preferences" field.
4. You can also choose multiple options for "Group Meeting Preferences".
5. You can add notes about your group in the "Group Notes" field.
6. If necessary, fill out "Group Availability" with a date.
7. Select "Group Selection Form Page 2" to proceed.
8. On the second page, you can again choose your group's presentation preferences by selecting one of the options in the "Group Presentation Preferences 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Selection Form Page 1** (1): This field is not directly editable, but it's the first page of the form.
* **Group Email** (6): This is a required field for entering a group's email address.
* **Group Phone** (7): This field is not required and can be used to enter any other contact information for the group, but it's not a critical piece of data.
* **Group Email 2** (8): This is another required field for entering an email address for the same group.
* **Group Presentation Preferences** (2): This field allows you to choose whether your group prefers to be presented in a certain way.
* **Group Meeting Preferences** (3): This field allows you to choose multiple options for how your group prefers to be presented during meetings.
* **Group Availability** (4): This field is not required, but it can be used to enter a date for when your group is available.
* **Group Notes** (5): This field is for adding any additional notes about your group.
* **Group Selection Form Page 2** (9): This field is not directly editable, but it's the second page of the form.
* **Group Presentation Preferences 2** (10): This field allows you to choose whether your group prefers to be presented in a certain way on the second page of the form.
