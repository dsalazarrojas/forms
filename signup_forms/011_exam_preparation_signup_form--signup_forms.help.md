# Exam Preparation Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to sign up for exam preparation services. It collects personal information about students and their exam schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name and last name fields with your personal information.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select your exam date and schedule time.
4. Choose your enrollment type (e.g., Exam Prep or Other).
5. Fill out any additional fields as necessary, such as school name, student schedule, and exam date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): Select the date of your exam.
- **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, required): Select the time of your exam.
- **Schedule Duration** (`schedule_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your exam preparation schedule.
- **Enrollment Type** (`enrollment_type`, select_one, required): Select Exam Prep or Other as your enrollment type.
- **School** (`school`, text, required): Enter your school name.
- **Tutor** (`tutor`, text, required): Enter the name of your tutor.
- **Enrolled Students** (`enrolled_students`, select_multiple, required): Select the students you are enrolling in the exam preparation service.
- **Enrolled Schedule** (`enrolled_schedule`, time, required): Select the schedule of the enrolled students.
- **Enrolled Students' Schedule** (`enrolled_students_schedule`, time, required): Select the schedule of the enrolled students.
- **Enrolled Students' Tutor** (`enrolled_students_tutor`, text, required): Enter the name of the tutor for the enrolled students.
- **Enrolled Students' Enrollment Type** (`enrolled_students_enrollment_type`, select_one, required): Select Exam Prep or Other as the enrollment type of the enrolled students.
- **Enrolled Students' Exam Date** (`enrolled_students_exam_date`, date, required): Select the exam date of the enrolled students.
- **Enrolled Students' Exam Time** (`enrolled_students_exam_time`, time, required): Select the exam time of the enrolled students.
- **Student Details** (`student_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the details of the students (e.g., Student 1, Student 2, Student 3).
- **Exam Preparer** (`exam_preparer`, text, required): Enter the name of the exam preparer.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
- **Student Schedule** (`student_schedule`, time, required): Select the schedule of the student.
- **Student Exam Date** (`student_exam_date`, date, required): Select the exam date of the student.
- **Student Exam Time** (`student_exam_time`, time, required): Select the exam time of the student.
- **Student Exam Preparer** (`student_exam_preparer`, text, required): Enter the name of the exam preparer for the student.
- **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid errors.
* If you're unsure about a field, ask your school or exam preparer for clarification.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

This help guide should provide a clear and concise explanation of each field's purpose and how to fill it out correctly. It's designed to assist users in completing the form efficiently and accurately.
