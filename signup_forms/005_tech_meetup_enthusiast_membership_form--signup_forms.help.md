## Tech Meetup Enthusiast Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from Tech Meetup Enthusiast community members, enabling us to create a more personalized and connected experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Choose a strong password and confirm it for verification.
3. Select your interests and agree to our terms and conditions.
4. (Optional) Add your phone number, address, city, state, zip code, join date, join time, and meetup days.
