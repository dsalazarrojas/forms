# Catalog Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your personal information and preferences for requesting a catalog from the company. Please complete this form to receive the latest catalog offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as required.
2. Select the preferred delivery method for the catalog.
3. Choose the areas of interest for the catalog.
4. Select your industry type.
5. Indicate how you heard about the company.
6. Agree to receive marketing updates from the company, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): If you are a business or representative of a business, enter your company name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address where you would like to receive the catalog.
* **City, State, Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Enter your city, state, and zip code to ensure accurate delivery.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Select your country of residence.
* **Which catalog version would you like to receive** (`catalog_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the options provided (Spring/Summer, Fall/Winter, Full Annual, Digital Only).
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Select from the options provided (Physical Copy by Mail, Digital Copy by Email, Both).
* **Areas of Interest** (`interest_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all areas of interest (Outdoor Furniture, Interior Decor, Kitchenware, Gardening Tools, Holiday Items).
* **Your Industry** (`industry_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your industry type (Interior Design, Real Estate, Hospitality, Retailer, Homeowner/Consumer).
* **How did you hear about our products** (`hearing_source`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how you heard about the company (Social Media, Google Search, Magazine Ad, Friend Recommendation, Trade Show).
* **I agree to receive marketing updates from the company** (`submission_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to receive marketing updates from the company ('Yes', 'No').
