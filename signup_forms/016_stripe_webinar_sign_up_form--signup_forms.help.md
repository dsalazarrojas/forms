# Stripe Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to sign up for a Stripe webinar and receive updates and notifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately, including your first and last name, email address, and company affiliation.
* Make sure your email address is entered correctly and is the same for both the email and confirm email fields.
* Submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (email): Confirm your email address by re-entering it.
* **Company** (text): Enter your company name (optional).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive updates and notifications.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the webinar organizers for assistance.
