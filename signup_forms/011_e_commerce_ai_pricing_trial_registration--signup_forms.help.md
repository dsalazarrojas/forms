# E Commerce AI Pricing Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce AI Pricing Trial Registration form is designed to help merchants sign up for the AI pricing trial for their e-commerce business. This form collects necessary information to determine eligibility and provide access to the trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as follows:

1. **Select Trial Signup**: Choose 'Yes' to express your interest in signing up for the trial.
2. **Merchant Info**: Enter your business name, e.g. 'Acme Inc.'.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address. Make sure it's your business email address and not personal.
4. **Merchant Name**: Enter your personal name (if different from your business name).
5. **Website**: Enter your website URL.
6. **Business Type**: Select the type of business you have:
	* SaaS: Software as a Service
	* Pricing provider: A provider of pricing services
	* Other: Other types of businesses
7. **SaaS Details**: If you selected 'SaaS', enter your SaaS details.
8. **Review Status**: Select the current status of your review:
	* Not started
	* In progress
	* Completed
