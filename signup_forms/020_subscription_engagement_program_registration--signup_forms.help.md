# Subscription Engagement Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Engagement Program Registration form is used to collect information from users for the program. This information will be used to personalize and tailor content and promotions based on the user's preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your user details, such as name and contact information.
3. Choose your subscription preferences (e.g. Email Newsletter, SMS, Push Notifications).
4. Enter your email address and phone number (if applicable).
5. Choose your start and end date for the program (if applicable).
6. Choose your time of day for program engagement (if applicable).
7. Indicate your consent for data use.
8. Add any additional notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (User Details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Subscription Preferences** (Subscription Preferences, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose how you want to be engaged with us, such as through Email Newsletter, SMS, or Push Notifications.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact and program updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required/optional): Choose your start date for the program (if applicable).
* **End Date** (End Date, date, required/optional): Choose your end date for the program (if applicable).
* **Time of Day** (Time of Day, time, required/optional): Choose your time of day for program engagement (if applicable).
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required/optional): Indicate your consent for data use.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about yourself or your engagement preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure you receive the most relevant content and promotions.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the form, please contact us.
* We'll use the information you provide to personalize and tailor content and promotions to your preferences.
* We'll only use your data for the purpose of the Subscription Engagement Program.
