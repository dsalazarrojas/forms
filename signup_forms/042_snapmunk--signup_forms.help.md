# Snapmunk - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration data for employees or team members of an organization. Fill out this form to provide your contact and job title information to Snapmunk.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type in your company name in the "Company" field.
6. Provide your job title in the corresponding "Job Title" field. Note that there are multiple job title fields, so fill in the one that best describes your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title_1`, text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* (Repeat for **Job Title 2**, **Job Title 3**, ..., **Job Title 19** fields)

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you're unsure which job title field to use, you can always contact the form administrator for guidance.
* Make sure to provide a valid phone number if applicable.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact the form administrator for assistance.
