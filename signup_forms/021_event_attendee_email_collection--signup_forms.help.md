# Event Attendee Email Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Attendee Email Collection form is designed to gather essential information from event attendees, including their name, email, event details, and communication preferences. This data is collected to enhance the event experience and ensure attendees receive relevant updates and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the first field. This is a required field to identify you as an attendee.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field. This will be used for communication and updates. Please ensure the format is correct (e.g., `example@email.com`).
3. Select the **Event Name** that corresponds to the event you are attending. Choose the correct event from the list to ensure we accurately track your attendance.
4. Enter the **Event Date** of the event you are attending. Use the correct date format (e.g., `dd/mm/yy` or `dd/mm/yyyy`).
5. If applicable, enter the **Event Time** of the event. This is an optional field, but it helps us track your specific attendance times.
6. Choose your **Communication Preferences**. Select all channels you are comfortable receiving updates from us, such as Email, SMS, or Phone.
7. If you have any **Additional Info** you'd like to share, please type it in the provided space. This is an optional field, but it helps us provide a better experience for you.
8. Confirm you **Agree to Terms** by selecting the correct option. This ensures you understand our terms and conditions for event attendance.
9. Finally, select whether you would like to **Opt out of email updates**. Choose this option if you don't want to receive email updates from us, but please note that this may impact your experience.

* **Name**: Type your full name as it appears on your identification. This is a required field.
