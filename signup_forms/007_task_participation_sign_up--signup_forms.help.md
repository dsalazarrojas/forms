# Task Participation Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for a specific task.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Name** field with your name.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the email field.
3. Select your **Task Preferences** to indicate your interest in participating in the task.
4. Choose your **Availability** to indicate when you are available to work on the task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, optional): Enter your name for task assignment and communication purposes.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive task updates and instructions.
* **Task Preferences** (select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate your interest in participating in the task.
* **Availability** (select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' when you are available to work on the task; 'No' otherwise.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in the form accurately to avoid errors and duplication of efforts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the task owner for assistance.
* This form is designed for task participation, do not use it for other purposes.
