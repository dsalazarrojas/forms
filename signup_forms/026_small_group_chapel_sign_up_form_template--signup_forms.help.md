# Small Group Chapel Sign Up Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Small Group Chapel Sign Up Form Template" is designed to collect information from students about their small group meetings. This form is intended to gather details about the meetings, such as meeting days and times, meeting place, and leader contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Student ID** (`student_id`): This is an optional field, but it's meant for students to identify themselves.
	* **Group Name** (`group_name`): Enter the name of the group you're signing up for.
	* **Leader Name** (`leader_name`): Enter the name of the leader of the group.
	* **Meeting Days** (`meeting_days`): Select the days of the week you meet.
	* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`): Select the time of your meeting.
	* **Leader Phone** (`leader_phone`): Enter the phone number of the leader.
	* **Leader Email** (`leader_email`): Enter the email address of the leader.
	* **Leader Contact** (`leader_contact`): Enter any additional contact information for the leader.
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date you start meeting.
	* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the date you end meeting.
	* **Max Size** (`max_size`): Enter the maximum number of people that can join the group.
	* **Min Size** (`min_size`): Enter the minimum number of people that need to join the group.
	* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your group.
	* **Meeting Place** (`meeting_place`): Enter the location of your meeting.
	* **Group Type** (`group_type`): Select the type of group you're joining.
	* **Meeting Days 2** (`meeting_days_2`): Select the second set of meeting days.
	* **Meeting Time 2** (`meeting_time_2`): Select the meeting time for the second set of days.
	* **Leader Status** (`leader_status`): Select the status of the leader.
