# Golf Team Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Team Registration form is designed to collect information from golf teams about their players and guests. This form helps us gather necessary details for event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely to provide all required information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if your team has players or guests.
3. For each player, enter their name and handicap level.
4. For each player, select the number of guest tickets you'd like to purchase.
5. You can add additional guest tickets in the "Additional Tickets" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: A text field to provide any additional comments or information about your team.
* **Players**: Select "Yes" to indicate if your team has players, then enter their names and handicap levels below.
* **Player 1 Name**: Enter the name of your first player.
* **Player 1 Handicap**: Enter the handicap level of your first player.
* **Player 1 Guest Tickets**: Select the number of guest tickets you'd like to purchase for your first player.
* **Player 2 Name**: Enter the name of your second player.
* **Player 2 Handicap**: Enter the handicap level of your second player.
* **Player 2 Guest Tickets**: Select the number of guest tickets you'd like to purchase for your second player.
* **Player 3 Name**: Enter the name of your third player.
* **Player 3 Handicap**: Enter the handicap level of your third player.
* **Player 3 Guest Tickets**: Select the number of guest tickets you'd like to purchase for your third player.
* **Player 4 Name**: Enter the name of your fourth player (if applicable).
* **Player 4 Handicap**: Enter the handicap level of your fourth player (if applicable).
* **Player 4 Guest Tickets**: Select the number of guest tickets you'd like to purchase for your fourth player (if applicable).
* **Additional Tickets**: Enter the number of additional guest tickets you'd like to purchase.
* **Banking Details**: Enter any banking information necessary for processing fees or payments.
