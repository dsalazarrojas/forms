# Cultural Night Performer Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Night Performer Signup form is designed to collect information about performers who will be participating in a cultural night event. This includes details such as performer name, contact information, performance dates and time, venue preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Ensure that the performer's name and contact information are correct.
3. Choose the correct category and venue for the performance.
4. Select the follow-up method that you prefer.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Name** (`performer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the performer, such as Jane Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the performer's email address, such as johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the performer's phone number, such as 123-456-7890.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description about the performer, such as "Jazz singer and pianist."
* **Category** (`category`, select one, required/optional): Choose the type of performance (Drama, Music, Dance, or Other).
* **Performer Experience** (`performer_experience`, text, required/optional): Enter the performer's experience, such as "10 years of professional experience in performing."
* **Performance Dates** (`performance_dates`, date, required/optional): Select the performance dates in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`, time, required/optional): Select the performance time in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Venue** (`venue`, select one, required/optional): Choose the venue for the performance (School, Community Venue, or Public Stage).
* **Organizer** (`organizer`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the organizers of the performance (School A, School B, Community Organizer, or Public Organizer).
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select one, required/optional): Choose the follow-up method (Email, Phone, or In-person).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the performance.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the information.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person submitting the information.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the information.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, required/optional): Enter the organization of the person submitting the information.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate coordination of the cultural night event.
