# Trivia Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from teams that are signing up for a Trivia Night event. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure that your team's details are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Enter the number of players in your team in the "Number of Players" field.
3. Check if any of your team members are seniors in the "Is Senior" field.
4. If you are a senior team, enter the number of senior players in the "Number of Senior Players" field.
5. Check if you are a first-time team in the "Is First Time" field.
6. Enter your team's contact information, including email and phone number.
7. Enter your team's team rep's name and whether they are a senior team rep.
8. Provide a team description to help us get a better understanding of your team.
9. Enter your team's arrival time and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name (Text)**: Enter your team's name.
* **Number of Players (Number)**: Enter the number of players in your team.
* **Is Senior (Select One)**: Check if any of your team members are seniors.
* **Is First Time (Select One)**: Check if this is your team's first time participating in the event.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your team's email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your team's phone number.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your team's name.
* **Team Rep (Text)**: Enter your team's team rep's name.
* **Is Senior Team Rep (Select One)**: Check if your team's rep is a senior.
* **Team Description (Text)**: Provide a brief description of your team.
* **Special Instructions (Text)**: Enter any special instructions or requests.
* **Team Name (Alias) (Text)**: Enter an alternative name for your team.
* **Team Name (Short) (Text)**: Enter a short name for your team.
* **Team Name (Long) (Text)**: Enter a long name for your team.
* **Is Senior Team (Select One)**: Check if you are a senior team.
* **Number of Senior Players (Number)**: Enter the number of senior players in your team.
* **Number of Non-Senior Players (Number)**: Enter the number of non-senior players in your team.
* **Arrive (Select One)**: Check if you will arrive on time.
* **Arrive Time (Time)**: Enter the time you will arrive.
* **Arrive Date (Date)**: Enter the date you will arrive.
* **Arrive By (Date)**: Enter the date you must arrive by.
* **Arrive At (Time)**: Enter the time you will arrive at.
* **Team Name (Alias) (Text)**: Enter an alternative name for your team.
* **Team Name (Short) (Text)**: Enter a short name for your team.
* **Team Name (Long) (Text)**: Enter a long name for your team.
