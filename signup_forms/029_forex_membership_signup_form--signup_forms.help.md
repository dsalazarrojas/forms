# Forex Membership Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forex Membership Signup Form is designed to collect user information for a membership program related to forex trading. This form is used to gather personal and contact details from users who are interested in joining the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your forex signup data on **Forex Signup Data** page 1.
2. Provide your personal information on **Member Information** page 2.
3. Add your contact details on **Contact Information** page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Forex Signup Data** (`forex_signup_form_data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your forex trading interests or goals.
* **Member Information** (`forex_member_info`, text, optional): Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email address, and other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`forex_member_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your phone number and any other contact information you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your membership application is complete and processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
