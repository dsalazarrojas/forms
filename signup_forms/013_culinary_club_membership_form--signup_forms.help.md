# Culinary Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the Culinary Club Membership Form, a registration form for joining the Culinary Club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Indicate your interest in activities by selecting the relevant options in the "activities" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Provide your address in the "address" field.
6. Specify your preferences for club activities by selecting the relevant options in the "preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name.
* **Activities** (`activities`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select the activities you're interested in participating in (e.g., cooking classes, potluck events).
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your valid email address (e.g., [your.name@example.com](mailto:your.name@example.com)).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Address** (`address`, text, **Optional**): Enter your address (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Select the activities you'd like to participate in (e.g., baking, grilling, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Name, Email).
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* For activities and preferences, select all options that apply.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the club administrators for assistance.
