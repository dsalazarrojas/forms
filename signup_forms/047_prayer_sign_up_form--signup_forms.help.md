# Prayer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prayer Sign Up Form is a data collection tool used for users to provide information 
for a prayer request process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out correctly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`signup_form`, text, required/optional): This field is for your first name. 
Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your last name. 
Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **email** (email, email, required/optional): This field is for your email address. 
Please enter the email address you use to communicate. 
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This field is for your date of birth. 
Please enter it in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Prayer Request** (`prayer_request`, text, required/optional): This field is for your prayer request. 
Please enter a brief description of your prayer request.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select multiple, required/optional): This field is for how you would like to be contacted. 
You can select one or more of the following options: Phone, Email, Text.
* **Language of Prayer** (`prayer_language`, select one, required/optional): This field is for the language you prefer for your prayer. 
You can select one of the following options: English, Spanish, Other.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): This field is for your location. 
Please enter your city, state or province, and country in the format `City, State/Province, Country`.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): This field is unclear in purpose. 
It seems to be a placeholder for a specific tool or application assigned to you. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): This field is for additional comments or notes. 
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required/optional): This field is not used in the form logic. 
It seems to be a placeholder for a unique form identifier.

## Tips
- Be clear and concise in your prayer request description.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
