# Coming Soon - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to express their interest in a upcoming workbook or service. It collects essential information to tailor communications and follow-up accordingly. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your first and last name**: Provide your full name as you would like to be addressed.
2. **Enter your email address**: Provide a valid email address that you check regularly for updates.
3. **Optionally, enter your phone number**: If you prefer, provide your phone number for SMS reminders.
4. **Enter your organization or company (if applicable)**: If you are representing an organization, provide its name.
5. **Select how you heard about us**: Choose the most relevant source from the provided options.
6. **Choose your interest level in the workbook**: Select the best match from the provided options.
7. **Add any additional comments or questions**: If you have any notes or queries, type them here.
8. **Consent to be contacted**: Confirm whether you agree to receive updates and follow-ups.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name to be addressed correctly.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide a valid email address for important updates and notifications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to receive SMS reminders, enter your phone number here.
* **Organization or company** (`organization`, text, optional): If you represent an organization, please provide its name.
* **How you heard about us** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Social media, Friend or colleague, Search engine, Newsletter, Other.
* **Interest level in the workbook** (`interest_level`, select_one, required): Select: Very interested, Interested, Maybe later, Not interested.
* **Additional comments or questions** (`comments`, text, optional): Type any notes or queries you have.
* **Consent to be contacted** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required): Confirm: Yes I consent, No I do not consent.
