# Master Class Tour Email Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Master Class Tour Email Signup form is used to sign up for a Master Class tour. To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information. Start by entering your First Name and Last Name on the first two fields. Then, enter your email address in the Email field and confirm it in the Email Confirmation field to ensure accuracy. Select the type of event you would like to attend by choosing from the Event Type options. If you would like to receive a phone call, enter your Phone number. Finally, indicate if you are available to attend the tour on the specified Tour Dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional): Retype your email address to confirm its accuracy.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of event you would like to attend, such as Master Class, Master Class Series, or Workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to attend the tour.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to attend the tour.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Tour Dates** (`tour_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you are available to attend the tour on the specified dates.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before confirming it.
- Be aware that if you select multiple event types, you will receive information for each type.
- Selecting a date and time that is not available will not allow you to sign up for the tour.
