# Contact Lens Comfort Study Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a contact lens comfort clinical study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll to the "Contact Lens Comfort Clinical Study" page and provide your name, age, and email address.
2.  Select your age group from the drop down menu.
3.  Enter your years of experience with contact lenses.
4.  Choose your current contact lens type from the dropdown menu.
5.  Select any discomfort issues you experience while wearing lenses.
6.  Check if you have any known eye conditions.
7.  Choose your general availability for clinic visits.
8.  Confirm if you have participated in a clinical study before.
9.  Select your consent to a screening call by a study coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Lens Comfort Clinical Study** (study_header): This page header provides an introduction to the study and its purpose.
*   **Full Name** (full_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Age** (age): Select your age group from the provided options.
*   **Email Address** (email): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (phone): Enter your phone number, including area code.
*   **What type of lenses do you currently wear?** (current_lens_type): Choose your current contact lens type from the provided options.
*   **Years of contact lens wear experience** (years_wearing_lenses): Enter the number of years you have been wearing contact lenses.
*   **Do you experience any of the following?** (discomfort_issues): Select all the discomfort issues you experience while wearing contact lenses.
*   **Have you participated in a clinical study before?** (study_participation_history): Check if you have participated in a clinical study before.
*   **Any known eye conditions?** (medical_conditions): List any known eye conditions you have.
*   **General availability for clinic visits** (availability): Choose your general availability for clinic visits from the provided options.
*   **I consent to a screening call by a study coordinator** (consent_for_screening): Confirm your consent to a screening call by selecting "I consent" from the dropdown menu.
