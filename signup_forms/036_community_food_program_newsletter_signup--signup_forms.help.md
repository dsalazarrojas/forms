# Community Food Program Newsletter Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the Community Food Program understand your needs and interests so we can provide you with relevant information and services. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly, as it will help us tailor our programs and communications to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, as this is how we will contact you for newsletter updates.
4.  Enter your ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
5.  If you have a household, enter the number of people in your household in the "Household Size" field.
6.  If you have children under 18 in your household, enter the number of children in the "Number of Children" field.
7.  Choose your reason for signing up in the "Reason for Signup" field.
8.  Select the programs you're interested in learning more about in the "Programs of Interest" field.
9.  Choose how often you'd like to receive updates from us in the "Preferred Newsletter Frequency" field.
10. If you're interested in volunteering, choose your availability in the "Volunteer Availability" field.
11. If you're interested in donating, choose your preferred donation type in the "Preferred Donation Type" field.
12. If you have any dietary needs or restrictions, enter them in the "Dietary Needs or Restrictions" field.
13. If you have any accessibility needs, enter them in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
14. If you have any other information to share, enter it in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you want us to use for newsletter delivery.
* **ZIP Code** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Household Size** (`5`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Number of Children** (`6`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of children under 18 in your household.
* **Reason for Signup** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Choose why you're signing up for the program.
	+ **Interested in Food Programs**: You're interested in learning more about our programs.
	+ **Need Food Assistance**: You're in need of food assistance.
	+ **Want to Volunteer**: You're interested in volunteering with us.
	+ **Want to Donate**: You'd like to donate to the program.
	+ **General Interest**: You just want to know more about us.
	+ **Community Partner**: You're a representative of a community organization.
* **Programs of Interest** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the programs you'd like to learn more about.
	+ **Food Pantry**: Food distribution and pantry management.
	+ **Community Fridge**: Food storage and distribution.
	+ **Meal Delivery**: Meal delivery services.
	+ **Senior Food Program**: Food assistance for seniors.
	+ **School Breakfast Program**: Food assistance for schools.
	+ **Summer Food Program**: Summer food assistance.
	+ **Cooking Classes**: Nutrition education and cooking classes.
	+ **Nutrition Education**: Nutrition education and workshops.
* **Preferred Newsletter Frequency** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you'd like to receive updates from us.
	+ **Weekly**: Regular updates.
	+ **Bi-Weekly**: Less frequent updates.
	+ **Monthly**: Monthly updates.
	+ **Quarterly**: Quarterly updates.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Are you interested in volunteering with us?
	+ **Yes, Definitely**: Yes, I'd like to volunteer.
	+ **Maybe**: I'm not sure yet.
	+ **False**: No, I'm not interested.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your availability for volunteering.
	+ **Weekday Mornings**: Monday to Friday, 8-12 am.
	+ **Weekday Afternoons**: Monday to Friday, 1-5 pm.
	+ **Weekday Evenings**: Monday to Friday, 6-9 pm.
	+ **Weekend Mornings**: Saturday and Sunday, 8-12 am.
	+ **Weekend Afternoons**: Saturday and Sunday, 1-5 pm.
	+ **Weekend Evenings**: Saturday and Sunday, 6-9 pm.
* **Interested in Donating** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Are you interested in donating to the program?
	+ **Yes, Regularly**: Yes, I'd like to donate regularly.
	+ **Yes, Occasionally**: Yes, I'd like to donate occasionally.
	+ **Maybe**: I'm not sure yet.
	+ **False**: No, I'm not interested.
* **Preferred Donation Type** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose how you'd like to contribute.
	+ **Monetary Donation**: Financial contribution.
	+ **Food Donation**: Food and grocery donation.
	+ **Time Donation**: Volunteer your time.
	+ **In-Kind Services**: Other services, such as expertise or skills.
* **Dietary Needs or Restrictions** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter any dietary requirements or restrictions.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter any accessibility needs or requirements.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`16`, `select_one`, optional): How did you learn about us?
	+ **Website**: Our website.
	+ **Social Media**: Social media platforms.
	+ **Friend or Family**: A friend or family member.
	+ **Community Organization**: A community organization.
	+ **School**: A school or school district.
	+ **Healthcare Provider**: A healthcare provider.
	+ **Other**: Other sources.
* **Additional Comments** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information you'd like to share.
