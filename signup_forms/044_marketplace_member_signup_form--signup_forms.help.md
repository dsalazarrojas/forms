# Marketplace Member Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Marketplace Member Signup Form is designed to collect information from potential marketplace members, enabling them to create a comprehensive profile and connect with other users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the email field. This will be used as your login ID and for communication purposes.
3. Enter your company and position in the respective fields.
4. Select your role from the provided options (Staff, Vendor, or Partner).
5. Fill in your address in the address fields (address 1, address 2, zip code).
6. Enter your contact number and website (if applicable).
7. Write a brief bio to describe yourself.
8. Add your image URL and profile URL (if applicable).
9. Enter your social media handles (Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube).
10. Add your profile name, tagline, headline, and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used as your login ID and for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Enter your position or job title.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role from the options (Staff, Vendor, or Partner).
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, required): Enter your apartment number or building name (if applicable).
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number (e.g., mobile or landline).
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter your website URL (if applicable).
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, required): Write a brief description about yourself.
* **Image URL** (`image_url`, text, required): Enter your image URL (if applicable).
* **Profile URL** (`profile_url`, text, required): Enter your profile URL (if applicable).
* **Twitter Handle** (`twitter_handle`, text, required): Enter your Twitter handle.
* **LinkedIn Handle** (`linkedin_handle`, text, required): Enter your LinkedIn handle.
* **Facebook Handle** (`facebook_handle`, text, required): Enter your Facebook handle.
* **Instagram Handle** (`instagram_handle`, text, required): Enter your Instagram handle.
* **YouTube Handle** (`youtube_handle`, text, required): Enter your YouTube handle.
* **Profile Name** (`profile_name`, text, required): Enter your profile name.
* **Profile Tagline** (`profile_tagline`, text, required): Enter your profile tagline.
* **Profile Headline** (`profile_headline`, text, required): Enter your profile headline.
* **Profile Description** (`profile_description`, text, required): Enter your profile description.
* **Profile Email** (`profile_email`, email, required): Enter your profile email.
