# Toolbox Talks Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toolbox Talks Sign In Form is designed to collect information from users for a Toolbox Talks event or service. This form is meant for individuals who want to sign in for the event or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
4. Select a Date for the event or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Date**: This field is for the date you plan to attend the event or service. Please select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and correct information for each field.
