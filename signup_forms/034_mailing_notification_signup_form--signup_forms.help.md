# mailing_notification_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to enable them to receive notifications via preferred channels. Please fill out the form with your details to complete your signup.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Select one or more preferred notification channels from the "Preferred Notification Channel" dropdown menu.
4. Select all relevant notification channels from the "Notification Channels" dropdown menu (at least one is required).
5. Enter a name in the "User Name" field.
6. Enter a password in the "User Password" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`<input type="email">`, required/optional): This is the primary email address associated with your account. Enter the address you use to check your inbox.
* **Phone Number** (`<input type="text">`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to receive notifications via SMS or voice call.
* **Preferred Notification Channel** (`<select>`, required/optional): Choose how you want to receive notifications: via email, text, or both.
* **Notification Channels** (`<select multiple>`, required): Select all the channels where you want to receive notifications (at least one is required). You can choose to receive notifications via email, text, phone, or other (if available).
* **User Name** (`<input type="text">`, required/optional): Enter a name to be associated with your account.
* **User Password** (`<input type="text">`, required/optional): Enter a password to secure your account.
