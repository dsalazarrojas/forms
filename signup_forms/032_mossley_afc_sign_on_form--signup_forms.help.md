# Mossley AfC Sign On Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mossley AfC Sign On Form is a sign-up form for a program or service. It collects personal and player information to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the format of [example@domain.com](mailto:example@domain.com) in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If applicable, enter your Parent Name in the "Parent Name" field.
6. Enter your Player Age in the "Player Age" field.
7. Indicate your Player Grade by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Player Grade" field.
8. Select your Player Position by checking the relevant options in the "Player Position" field.
9. Provide any additional information about the player in the "Additional Player Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number to enable communication.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are registering as a parent or guardian, enter the player's name here.
* **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter the player's age to verify eligibility.
* **Player Grade** (`player_grade`, select_one, required): Indicate if the player has a grade to register for.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select all positions that apply to the player.
* **Additional Player Info** (`additional_player_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the player.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
