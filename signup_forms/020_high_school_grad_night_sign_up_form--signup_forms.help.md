# High School Grad Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a High School Grad Night event. This includes details about parents and students, event specifics, and confirmations. By filling out this form, you'll help ensure that all parties are informed and on board.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Parent Information**: Fill in the Parent Name, Parent Email, Parent Phone, and School Name fields with the relevant details.
2. **Enter Student Information**: Fill in the Student Name, Student Grade, and Student Year fields with the relevant details.
3. **Select Event Details**: Choose the Event Date, Event Time, and Event Status from the respective dropdown menus.
4. **Confirm**: Select "Confirm" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`): Enter the parent's contact information.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): Enter a valid email address for the parent.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`): Choose the student's grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Student Year** (`student_year`): Choose the student's year from the dropdown menu (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Select the date of the High School Grad Night event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`): Choose the status of the event from the dropdown menu (Active, Completed, or Cancelled).
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`): Choose whether the student will attend the event.
* **Teacher Attendance** (`teacher_attendance`): Choose whether the teacher will attend the event.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`): Choose whether the parent has given permission for the student to attend.
* **Teacher Permission** (`teacher_permission`): Choose whether the teacher has given permission for the student to attend.
* **Student Comments** (`student_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback from the student.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback from the teacher.
* **Event Comments** (`event_comments`): Enter any comments or feedback about the event.
* **Student Attendance Confirmation** (`student_attendance`): Confirm the student's attendance.
* **Teacher Attendance Confirmation** (`teacher_attendance`): Confirm the teacher's attendance.
* **Parent Permission Confirmation** (`parent_permission`): Confirm the parent's permission.
* **Teacher Permission Confirmation** (`teacher_permission`): Confirm the teacher's permission.
* **Event Status Confirmation** (`event_status`): Confirm the event status.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the school's email address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): Enter the school's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`): Select "Confirm" to submit the form. 

This help guide should provide a clear understanding of how to complete the form and what each field represents, helping users efficiently and accurately fill out the form.
