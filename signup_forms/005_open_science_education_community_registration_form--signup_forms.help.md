# Open Science Education Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register interested individuals in the Open Science Education Community. To register, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Member Details form.
2. Select your interests from the Member Interests field.
3. Choose your consent status.
4. Add any additional comments if needed.
5. Provide your contact information.
6. Select your science focus area.
7. Select your educational background.
8. Select your research interests.
9. Select your skills.
10. Select your language spoken.
11. Agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, required): This is a text field where you can enter your name, affiliation, or any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Member Interests** (`member_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of options (Science, Education, Open Science, Community Development, Research) to indicate your areas of interest within the Open Science Education Community.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to agree to the terms and conditions of the Open Science Education Community or 'No' if you do not agree.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, note, optional): You can enter your contact information, such as phone number or email, to be contacted by community members or administrators.
* **Science Focus Area** (`science_focus_area`, select_one, required): Select your area of focus within the scientific disciplines (Astrophysics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Earth and Planetary Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Medicine, Other).
* **Educational Background** (`educational_background`, select_one, optional): Select your highest level of education completed (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD).
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of options (Artificial Intelligence, Biomechanics, Computational Biology, Data Science, Machine Learning, Neuroscience, Quantum Mechanics, Robotics, Sustainability).
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of options (Communication, Leadership, Project Management, Time Management, Team Management).
* **Language Spoken** (`language_spoken`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of options (English, Mandarin, Spanish, French, Japanese, German, Portuguese, Italian).
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to agree to the terms and conditions of the Open Science Education Community.
