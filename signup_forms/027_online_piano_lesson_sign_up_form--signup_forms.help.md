# Online Piano Lesson Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for online piano lessons with a choice of instructors and lesson types. Please fill out the form to complete your sign-up process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Sign Up" to initiate the sign-up process.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Select your preferred instructor and lesson type from the respective dropdown menus.
4. Provide a phone number and email address for contact purposes.
5. Schedule your preferred lesson time.
6. Optionally, you can enter a message for the instructor.
7. Review and submit the form to complete your sign-up process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sign Up Details** (`sign_up_details`, text, optional): This field is a general description of why you are signing up for piano lessons.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed by the instructor.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number for the instructor to contact you.
- **Lesson Scheduling** (`lesson_scheduling`, time, optional): Select a time for your lesson, if you prefer to schedule it immediately.
- **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your lesson, if you prefer to schedule it later.
- **Select Instructor** (`select_instructor`, select_one, required): Choose the instructor you would like to work with. Currently, you can select from Mr. Smith, Ms. Johnson, or Mr. Johnson.
- **Select Lesson Type** (`select_lesson_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of lesson you would like to take, such as Private Lessons, Group Lessons, or Trial Lessons.
- **Message to Instructor** (`message`, text, optional): Optionally enter a message to provide to the instructor with your sign-up.
- **Sign Up Confirmation** (`sign_up_confirmation`, text, optional): This field is automatically populated after submitting the form. Review it before proceeding.

## Tips
* Ensure you have chosen your preferred instructor and lesson type before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, for accuracy.
* If you need to cancel or change your lesson, contact the instructor directly.
* Review your lesson schedule and preferred time carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or questions, include them in your message to the instructor.
