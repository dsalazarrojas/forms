# Talent Show Auditions Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for performers to sign up for a talent show audition. It is used to collect information about the performer, their contact details, and their performance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide your personal details in the "Performer Details" section.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
4. Describe your performance in the "Performance Details" section.
5. Upload a video or image files as required for each performance.
6. Choose your talent type (e.g., Singer, Dancer, Actor, Musician).
7. Add any additional information if needed.
8. Select a date and time for your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Talent Type**: Select one of the following options: Singer, Dancer, Actor, Musician.
	+ This field helps us understand the type of talent you are showcasing.
* **Performer Name**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed on the talent show.
* **Performer Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Performer Contact**: Enter your phone number or any other contact information for us to reach you.
* **Performance Name**: Enter a name for your performance (e.g., a song title, a dance routine name).
* **Performance Description**: Describe your performance in a few words (e.g., a short description of your song or dance routine).
* **Upload Performer Image**: Upload an image of yourself for promotional purposes.
* **Upload Performance Image**: Upload an image of your performance (e.g., a photo of your costume or setup).
* **Video URL**: If you have a video of your performance, provide a URL or upload it directly.
* **Video Embed**: If you have a video platform link, you can paste it here.
* **Image URL**: If you have an image of your performance, provide a URL or upload it directly.
* **Image Embed**: If you have an image platform link, you can paste it here.
* **Upload Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your performance that we should know.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your application.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
