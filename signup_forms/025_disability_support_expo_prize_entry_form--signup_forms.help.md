# Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disability Support Expo Prize Entry Form is used to collect and store details about a prize and the person winning it. This form helps us to efficiently gather essential information from prize winners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Prize Details` section with information about the prize you're submitting.
2. Provide details about the winner in the `Winner Details` section.
3. Enter your contact information in the `Contact Details` section.
4. If you have any special preferences or requests for the winner, you can mention them in the `Winner Preferences` section.
5. Finally, enter the winner's contact details in the `Winner Contact Details` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prize Details**: This section allows you to describe the prize you're submitting. Enter a brief description of the prize to help us understand what it is.
* **Winner Details**: Here, you can provide any relevant information about the winner, such as their name, age, or any notable accomplishments.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you if we need more information or clarification about the entry.
* **Winner Preferences**: If you have any specific requests or preferences for the winner, you can mention them here.
* **Winner Contact Details**: Enter the winner's contact information so we can easily get in touch with them to notify them about their prize win. 

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- Ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, don't hesitate to ask for help.

Note: The form can be completed by anyone, but please ensure that the prize winner is aware that they will be contacted and that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
