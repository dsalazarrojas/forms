# Habit Formation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Habit Formation Challenge Registration form collects essential information from participants to facilitate their involvement in the habit formation program. Please complete this form accurately to ensure a smooth and effective registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Provide your organizational details, such as organization name, organization type, and job title.
3. Specify the number of participants and the duration of the program.
4. Choose your preferred frequency for the program and goal frequencies.
5. Select the start and end dates of the program.
6. Provide additional program details and the type of program (in-person or virtual).
7. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (ID: 1): Enter your name, ensuring it's accurate and consistent with your identity.
* **Email** (ID: 2): Input your email address, which will be used for future communications.
* **Phone** (ID: 3): Add your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Signup Date** (ID: 4): Enter the date you signed up for the program.
* **Goals** (ID: 5): Select one or more of your program goals.
* **Program Info** (ID: 6): Provide any additional information about your program.
* **Name** (ID: 7): Enter your name, if different from Participant Info.
* **Org Info** (ID: 8): Input your organization's name.
* **Contact Person** (ID: 9): Provide the name of the contact person.
* **Organization** (ID: 10): Enter your organization's name.
* **Role** (ID: 11): Enter your job title or role.
* **Department** (ID: 12): Enter your department within the organization.
* **Job Title** (ID: 13): Enter your job title.
* **Location** (ID: 14): Enter the location of your organization.
* **Org Type** (ID: 15): Select the type of your organization (company, non-profit, or government).
* **Org Size** (ID: 16): Enter the size of your organization.
* **Num Participants** (ID: 17): Specify the number of participants in your program.
* **Duration** (ID: 18): Enter the duration of your program.
* **Frequency** (ID: 19): Select the frequency of your program (e.g., once, weekly, monthly).
* **Goals Frequency** (ID: 20): Choose how often you plan to work towards your goals.
* **Start Date** (ID: 21): Enter the start date of your program.
* **End Date** (ID: 22): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Program Details** (ID: 23): Provide any extra details about your program.
* **Program Type** (ID: 24): Select the type of program (in-person or virtual).
* **Submit** (ID: 25): Confirm your submission to complete the registration process.

By following this guide, you'll be able to accurately and efficiently complete the Habit Formation Challenge Registration form.
