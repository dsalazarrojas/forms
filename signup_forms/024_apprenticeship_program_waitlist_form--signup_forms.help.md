# Apprenticeship Program Waitlist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Apprenticeship Program Waitlist Form is designed to collect information from interested individuals who want to participate in the apprenticeship program. This form helps us understand your background, motivation, and availability to complete the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your personal and professional information.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (marked as `required` in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address that you access regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Education and Background** (`education_info`, `note`, `optional`): Provide a brief overview of your educational background and any relevant work experience.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the highest level of education you have completed from the provided options.
* **Prior Apprenticeship Experience** (`prior_apprenticeship`, `select_one`, `optional`): Indicate if you have prior experience in an apprenticeship program.
* **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, `note`, `optional`): List the specific apprenticeship program you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Apprenticeship Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the apprenticeship program you are interested in.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date_preference`, `date`, `optional`): If applicable, enter your preferred start date for the apprenticeship program.
* **Current Employment Status** (`availability`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Why are you interested in this apprenticeship** (`motivation`, `text`, `required`): Briefly describe why you are interested in this apprenticeship program.
* **Commitment to Completing Program** (`commitment`, `select_one`, `required`): Indicate your level of commitment to completing the apprenticeship program.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Completing this form will help us gauge your eligibility and prepare for the program.
