# widows_and_widowers_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The widows and widowers registration form is used to collect information from potential participants about their registration for support services. Please fill out this form to provide your details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **participant name** in the "participant_name" field.
2.  Enter your **organization** (if applicable) in the "organization" field.
3.  Enter your **phone number** in the "phone_number" field (optional).
4.  Enter your **email** in the "email" field (required).
5.  Select the **date of birth** in the "date_of_birth" field (optional).
6.  Select the **time** of your preference (optional).
7.  Enter any additional **participant notes** in the "participant_notes" field (optional).
8.  Select the **support services wanted** from the list (required).
9.  If you want to add additional **support services**, please enter them in the "support_services_other" field (optional).
10.  Enter any other ways you **heared about us** from the "how_did_you_hear_about_us" list (optional).
11.  Enter any **additional comments** in the "additional_comments" field (optional).
12.  Finally, click the "submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **widows_and_widowers_registration**: This is the title of the form.
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as it is written on your identification.
* **organization**: If you are part of an organization or community group, enter it here.
* **phone_number**: Enter your contact phone number (if you have one).
* **email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **date_of_birth**: Select your date of birth (if applicable).
* **time**: Select your preferred time of day for support services.
* **participant_notes**: Enter any additional information about yourself or your situation.
* **support_services_wanted**: Select the support services you need or want.
* **support_services_other**: Enter any other support services you may want or need (if not listed).
* **how_did_you_hear_about_us**: Select how you heard about our support services.
* **how_did_you_hear_about_us_other**: Enter any other ways you heard about us.
* **additional_comments**: Enter any other comments or suggestions you may have.
* **submit**: Click this button to complete your registration.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask our staff for help.
* We appreciate your honesty and transparency when filling out this form.
