# CSDOT Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CSDOT Application Form is a comprehensive tool used to gather information from potential candidates for various positions within the CSDOT organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary documents and information required for the application.
2. Select the correct program or department applying for from the list provided.
3. Provide accurate and clear answers to all questions and fields.
4. Ensure you have checked the correct options for each select type field.
5. Sign the application form electronically by typing your full name in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Applicant Name, text, required):
	+ This field is used to capture the applicant's full name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the applicant's date of birth.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the applicant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the applicant's phone number.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required):
	+ This field is used to capture the applicant's street address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (City, State, ZIP Code, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the applicant's city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Highest Level of Education** (Highest Level of Education, select one, required):
	+ Please select your highest level of education from the provided options.
* **Current Employment Status** (Current Employment Status, select one, required):
	+ Please select your current employment status from the provided options.
* **Years of Relevant Experience** (Years of Relevant Experience, number, optional):
	+ This field is used to collect the number of years of relevant experience the applicant has.
* **Key Skills or Certifications** (Key Skills or Certifications, text, required):
	+ Please provide a list of relevant skills or certifications held by the applicant.
* **Program or Department Applying For** (Program or Department Applying For, select one, required):
	+ Please select the program or department the applicant is applying for from the list provided.
* **Position or Role Sought** (Position or Role Sought, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the specific position or role the applicant is seeking.
* **Career Goals and Aspirations** (Career Goals and Aspirations, text, required):
	+ This field is used to collect the applicant's career goals and aspirations.
* **Why Are You Interested in This Program** (Why Are You Interested in This Program, text, required):
	+ This field is used to capture the applicant's motivation for applying to the program.
* **Availability to Start** (Availability to Start, select one, required):
	+ Please select the availability to start from the provided options.
* **Professional References** (Professional References, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to collect the names and contact information of professional references.
* **Willing to Undergo Background Check** (Willing to Undergo Background Check, select one, required):
	+ Please select whether the applicant is willing to undergo a background check.
* **I Certify That Information Provided Is True** (I Certify That Information Provided Is True, select one, required):
	+ Please select whether the applicant certifies that the information provided is true.
* **Electronic Signature** (Electronic Signature, text, required):
	+ Please type your full name as electronic signature.

Please note that all fields with asterisk (\*) are required fields. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the application. 

This form is designed to guide you through the process, and completing it thoroughly and accurately will help the CSDOT organization assess your qualifications for the available roles.
