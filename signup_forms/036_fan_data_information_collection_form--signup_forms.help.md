This form is designed to collect information about fans, including their data, details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the pages of the form, which are separated into multiple sections for easy reference.
2. For each page, read the label and hint text (if provided) to understand the type of information required.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields, taking into account the type of field (e.g., text, email, select_one).
