This guide is intended to help users understand the purpose and usage of the Evolve Challenge Interest Survey form. The form is designed to collect information from participants about their interests and preferences regarding a challenge. The purpose of this form is to gauge the level of interest from potential participants in the challenge, and to collect additional details that will help in organizing and communicating with the participants.

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

## Purpose
The Evolve Challenge Interest Survey is designed to help us understand which challenges are of interest to you, our participant, and to gather additional details to communicate with you more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. Provide your answers to each question based on your preferences and interests.
3. Be as brief and clear as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (`participant_information`, text, optional)**: This section is for you to provide your name and any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **What challenge are you interested in? (`challenge_interest`, select_one, optional)**: Choose the challenge that you are interested in participating in.
* **Availability (`availability`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all the days of the week when you are available to participate in the challenge.
* **What do you prefer? (`preferences`, select_one, optional)**: Choose your preferred option from the available choices.
* **Participant Details (`participant_details`, text, optional)**: This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional details about yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Notes (`participant_notes`, note, optional)**: This is a space for you to add any notes or comments about your participation.
* **How do you want to be communicated with? (`challenge_communications`, select_one, optional)**: Choose your preferred method of communication.
* **Participant Status (`participant_status`, select_one, optional)**: Choose your current participant status.

## Tips

* Be honest and clear in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to communicating with you about the challenge.
