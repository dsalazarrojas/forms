# Education Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Class Registration Form is designed to collect information from students to register them for an education class. This form is used to gather contact, personal, and educational details to ensure that students can be properly registered and accommodated in the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your email address (this will be used for communication) and phone number (in case we need to reach you).
2. Provide your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant personal information.
3. Select the courses you are interested in (English, Math, Science, History, or Arts).
4. Enter your class schedule details, including the date and time you are available (if any).
5. Share your student preferences, such as your interests and goals.
6. If applicable, enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
7. Choose your preferred schedule (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
8. Confirm your availability by selecting whether you prefer to be contacted by email or phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to allow us to contact you about your registration and any further communication.
* **Personal Information**: Provide any additional personal details that may help us understand your background and interests.
* **Which Coursework**: Select the courses you are interested in taking (English, Math, Science, History, or Arts).
* **Class Information**: Enter any relevant class information you think we should know about (e.g., specific topics or levels of expertise).
* **Class Schedule**: Enter the date and time you are available for the class.
* **Student Preferences**: Share your interests and goals related to the class (e.g., what you hope to achieve).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Class Schedule Time**: If you have a preferred time for the class, enter it here (if any).
* **Student Interest**: Confirm whether you have interest in participating in class activities or events.
* **Class Schedule Date**: Enter the date you are available for the class (if any).
* **Email/Phone**: Choose whether you prefer to be contacted by email or phone.
* **Address**: Enter your address for in-person contact (if applicable).
* **Schedule**: Select your preferred schedule for the class (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).

**Tips**

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any registration issues.
* Double-check your contact and schedule details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
