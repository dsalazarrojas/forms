# Sign Up For Seasonal Collection Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to sign up for seasonal collection updates, allowing users to provide their preferences for notifications about new and upcoming collections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field.
2. Select your preferred season for updates (Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter).
3. Choose your preferred marketing channels (Email, SMS, Push, In-app).
4. Optionally, provide your name and phone number.
5. Confirm your email address and phone number.
6. Agree to the terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign Up for Seasonal Collection Updates** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter your sign up message.
* **Email** (`2`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates.
* **Name** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Seasonal Collection Updates** (`5`, `select_one`, optional): Select the season for which you would like to receive updates (Spring, Summer, Autumn, or Winter).
* **Marketing Channels** (`6`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the marketing channels you would like to receive updates through (Email, SMS, Push, In-app).
* **Time of Year** (`7`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you would like to receive updates.
* **Time of Day** (`8`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of day when you would like to receive updates.
* **Note** (`9`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional note for the business.
* **Confirm Email** (`10`, `email`, optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`11`, `text`, optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Agree to Terms** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Agree or disagree to the terms of service ('Yes' or 'No').
