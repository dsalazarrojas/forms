# Creative STEM Outreach Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals with the Creative STEM Outreach Network. Please fill out this form to connect with like-minded professionals, educators, and enthusiasts in the STEM community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Email address.
2. Briefly describe your User Experience.
3. Check if you select "Yes" or "No" for Select School, indicating if you are affiliated with a school or organization.
4. Check the organizations you are a part of by selecting "Yes" or "No" for Select Organization.
5. Provide a brief description of your organization(s) in the Organization Description field.
6. Select the interests that best align with your goals and activities by choosing "Yes" or "No" for Select Interests.
7. Optionally, describe future activities you plan to pursue in the Future Activities field (optional).
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, **required**): A valid email address is required to create your account.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, note, **optional**): Briefly describe your background and experience in the STEM field.
* **Select School** (`select_school`, select_one, **optional**): Check "Yes" if you are affiliated with a school or organization.
* **Select Organization** (`select_organization`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose all the organizations you are a part of.
* **Prophets Organization** (`prophets_organization`, note, **optional**): If not applicable, please leave this field blank.
* **Organization Description** (`organization_description`, text, **optional**): Provide a brief description of your organization(s).
* **Select Interests** (`select_interests`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose the interests that align with your goals and activities.
* **Future Activities** (`future_activities`, note, **optional**): Optionally, describe future activities or projects you plan to pursue.

**Tips**

* Be accurate when filling out your User Email address to ensure you can access your account.
* If you have multiple affiliations with organizations, select all the relevant options in Select Organization.
* Choose the interests that best match your goals and activities in Select Interests.
* Keep the Organization Description field brief, as it is not required to be filled out.
