# Sign Up Form | Email Widget|Wordpress - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users to create an account or sign up for a WordPress email service. This form is a user-friendly way to gather essential details to facilitate the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields. While some fields are optional, please ensure you've filled in all the necessary ones to avoid missing any important information.
2. Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form submission process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Sign Up Form** (`signup_form_email_widget_wordpress_1`, text, optional)
	* This field allows you to type in a name or a phrase that you want to be associated with your account or service sign up. It is an optional field, so feel free to leave it blank if you don't want to provide a name.
### 2. **Email Address** (`signup_form_email_widget_wordpress_2`, email, optional)
	* Enter your email address to associate with your account or service sign up. This is an optional field, but please note that it is essential for you to be able to receive notifications and updates related to your account or service.
### 3. **Password** (`signup_form_email_widget_wordpress_3`, text, optional)
	* This field is for entering a password. Please ensure you enter a strong and secure password that is not easily guessable. It is an optional field, but please note that it is essential to have a password to protect your account or service information.

## Tips
* Please be cautious when entering sensitive information, such as your email address and password.
* Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
