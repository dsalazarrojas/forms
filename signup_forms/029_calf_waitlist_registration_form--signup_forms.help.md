# Calf Waitlist Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Calf Waitlist Registration Form is designed to gather information about your preferences and requirements for purchasing calves. This information will help us tailor our calf selection to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Fill in your contact information (full name, email address, phone number, and farm or business name).
2. Select your preferred breed and gender of the calf.
3. Choose your preferred age at purchase and number of calves needed.
4. Select your preferred delivery date and indicate your level of flexibility on timing.
5. Choose the purpose for purchasing the calf (breeding stock, beef production, etc.).
6. If applicable, indicate if you require health certificates.
7. Select whether you will arrange transport or if it will be shared responsibility.
8. If you have any special requests or requirements, please list them in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on any communication or documentation related to your account.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with any updates or changes to our calf selection.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Farm or Business Name** (`farm_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your farm or business, as it will be used to communicate with your account.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your farm or business address for logistical purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city for logistical purposes.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state for logistical purposes.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for logistical purposes.
* **Preferred Calf Gender** (`calf_preferences_header`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred gender of calf, whether Male (Bull), Female (Heifer), or No preference.
* **Preferred Breed** (`breed`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred breed for the calf, from our available options: Angus, Hereford, Simmental, Charolais, Brahman, Mixed, or Other.
* **Preferred Age at Purchase** (`age_at_purchase`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred age at purchase for the calf, from Newborn, Weaned (under 1 year), Yearling (1-2 years), or No preference.
* **Number of Calves Needed** (`quantity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of calves you need to purchase.
* **Desired Delivery Date** (`desired_delivery_date`, `date`, required): Enter your preferred delivery date for the calf.
* **How Flexible Are You on Timing?** (`flexibility`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your level of flexibility on timing, whether Very flexible, Somewhat flexible, or Not flexible - exact date needed.
* **Purpose for Calf Purchase** (`usage_purpose_header`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the purpose for which you are purchasing the calf, from Breeding stock, Beef production, Dairy production, Show animal, Pet/hobby farm, or Other.
* **Do You Require Health Certificates?** (`health_certificates`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you require health certificates for the calf.
* **Transport Arrangement** (`transport_arrangement`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you will arrange transport for the calf or if it will be shared responsibility.
* **Special Requests or Requirements** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): List any special requests or requirements you have for the calf purchase.

Note that this form may not be suitable for all users. Please let us know if you have any issues or concerns during the form completion process.
