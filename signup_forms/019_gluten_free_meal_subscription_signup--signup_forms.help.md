# Gluten Free Meal Subscription Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up for a gluten-free meal subscription service, allowing users to select their preferred meal plan, delivery method, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your **Delivery Preferences** from the options provided.
2. Choose your **Meal Plan** that suits your dietary needs.
3. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
4. Enter your **Name** and **Email** to create your account.
5. Enter your **Phone Number** (optional).
6. Choose your **Subscription Plan** that fits your needs.
7. Select your **Delivery Frequency**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Preferences** (`delivery_preferences`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select one or more options that suit your delivery preferences (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Meal Plan** (`meal_plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred meal plan to accommodate your dietary needs (e.g., vegan, vegetarian, omnivore).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select a payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for further communication (optional).
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, `select_one`, required): Choose a subscription plan that fits your needs (e.g., basic, premium, deluxe).
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you would like to receive your meal deliveries (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).

## Tips

- Please ensure that your email address is valid and working.
- Select a payment method that you are comfortable with.
- Choose a meal plan that suits your dietary needs.
- Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
- Be aware that some fields are required to proceed with your subscription.
