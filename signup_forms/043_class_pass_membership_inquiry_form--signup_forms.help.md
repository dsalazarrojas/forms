# Class Pass Membership Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Pass Membership Inquiry Form is designed to collect information from potential members about their fitness goals, preferences, and eligibility for membership. This form helps our team assess suitability and provide guidance on the next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred membership type (e.g., Monthly Unlimited, Monthly 10 Classes).
3. Choose your fitness goals (e.g., weight loss, build muscle).
4. Select the classes you are interested in (e.g., Yoga, Pilates).
5. Indicate your fitness experience level (e.g., beginner, intermediate).
6. Choose your preferred class schedule (e.g., early morning, late evening).
7. Select your preferred location (e.g., downtown, uptown).
8. Confirm if you have a current or previous gym membership.
9. Indicate how you heard about us (e.g., social media, friend referral).
10. If applicable, provide the name of the person who referred you.
11. If you have any medical conditions or injuries, please disclose this information.
12. Indicate your budget range for membership.
13. Choose when you would like to start your membership.
14. Decide if you would like a free trial class.
15. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name (first and last name).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for membership information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for membership eligibility verification.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your street, city, state, and ZIP code for address verification.
* **Membership Type Interested In**: Choose the membership type that suits your needs (e.g., Monthly Unlimited, Monthly 10 Classes).
* *Weight loss*: Select if you are interested in weight loss as a fitness goal.
* *Build muscle*: Select if you are interested in building muscle as a fitness goal.
* *Improve flexibility*: Select if you are interested in improving flexibility as a fitness goal.
* *Increase stamina*: Select if you are interested in increasing stamina as a fitness goal.
* *Stress relief*: Select if you are interested in stress relief as a fitness goal.
* *General fitness*: Select if you are interested in general fitness as a goal.
* *Athletic training*: Select if you are interested in athletic training as a goal.
* *Rehabilitation*: Select if you are interested in rehabilitation as a goal.
* *Social connection*: Select if you are interested in social connection as a goal.
* **Which Classes Interest You**: Select the classes you are interested in (e.g., Yoga, Pilates).
* **Fitness Experience Level**: Indicate your level of fitness experience (e.g., beginner, intermediate).
* **Preferred Class Schedule**: Choose the time slots you prefer for classes (e.g., early morning, late evening).
* **Preferred Location**: Select your preferred studio location (e.g., downtown, uptown).
* **Do You Currently Have a Gym Membership**: Indicate if you have a current or previous gym membership.
* **Previous Gym or Studio**: If applicable, enter the name of the previous gym or studio.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about us (e.g., social media, friend referral).
* **Who Referred You**: If you were referred, enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **Do You Have Any Medical Conditions**: Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect workouts.
* **Please Describe Medical Conditions**: If you have medical conditions, describe them briefly.
* **Do You Have Any Current or Past Injuries**: Indicate if you have current or past injuries that may affect workouts.
* **What Is Your Monthly Budget**: Choose your budget range for membership (e.g., under 50 dollars, 150-200 dollars).
* **When Would You Like to Start**: Choose when you would like to start your membership (e.g., immediately, within a week).
* **Would You Like a Free Trial Class**: Decide if you would like a free trial class.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest when answering the medical conditions and injuries questions.
* Make sure to select all relevant classes and preferences.
* If you are unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask our team for clarification.
* Provide any additional comments or questions to help us better serve you.
