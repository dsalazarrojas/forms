# Multi Email Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect multiple email addresses from users for registration purposes. It is intended for users to input their email addresses and provide additional information such as their name and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email addresses in the designated fields. You can input up to two email addresses.
2. Provide your first and last name (if applicable).
3. If you want to add any comments, you can do so in the designated field.
4. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email 1**: Enter your first email address. This field is required.
* **Email 2**: Enter your second email address. This field is required.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name if you wish to provide it. This field is optional.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name if you wish to provide it. This field is optional.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email addresses correctly to ensure they are recognized as valid.
* If you experience any issues with submitting the form, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
