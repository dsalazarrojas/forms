### Amazon Gift Card Drawing Entry - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is for entering a drawing or contest to win an Amazon gift card. To be eligible, you must be 18 years or older and agree to the official rules and terms.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.
2. Be accurate and honest when filling out your personal information.
3. Make sure you have read and agree to the official rules, terms, and privacy policy.
4. Confirm your eligibility to participate.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter a valid email address that we can use to notify you if you win.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number for winner verification.
* **Date of Birth** (5): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street Address** (6): Enter your street address for prize delivery.
* **Apartment/Suite Number** (7): Enter your apartment or suite number if applicable.
* **City** (8): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (9): Select your state from the list.
* **ZIP Code** (10): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Drawing/Contest Name** (11): Enter the name of the drawing or contest you're entering.
* **How did you hear about this drawing** (12): Select how you heard about this drawing.
* **Have you entered this drawing before** (13): Indicate if this is your first or subsequent entry.
* **Sign up for our newsletter** (14): Select if you want to receive updates on future promotions.
* **Receive promotional emails from partners** (15): Select if you agree to receive promotional emails from partners.
* **Receive text message notifications** (16): Select if you agree to receive text message notifications.
* **Do you follow us on social media** (17): Indicate if you follow us on social media.
* **Which platforms do you use** (18): Select all social media platforms you use.
* **Age Verification** (19): Confirm you're 18 years or older.
* **Terms and Conditions** (20): Select if you agree to the official rules and terms.
* **Privacy Policy Acknowledgment** (21): Select if you agree to the privacy policy.
* **Eligibility Confirmation** (22): Confirm your eligibility to participate.
* **Do you have an Amazon account** (23): Indicate if you have an Amazon account.
* **How often do you shop on Amazon** (24): Select how often you shop on Amazon.
* **Favorite Shopping Categories** (25): Select all categories you're interested in.
* **Referral Code** (26): If you were referred by someone, enter their code.
* **Additional Comments** (27): Optional comments or notes.
* **Entry Date** (28): Enter the date you're submitting this entry.
* **Entry Time** (29): Optional entry time (not required).

Note: This form is for entering a drawing or contest to win an Amazon gift card. All fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required.
