# Sports Registration Form. - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Registration Form is designed for athletes to register for a sports program. This form collects information about the athlete, their preferred position, and their guardian's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: position_preferred and athlete_position.
2. Fill in any optional fields: program_information, athlete_details, guardian_contact, terms_and_conditions, consent, athlete_height, athlete_weight, and guardian_relationship.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Position Preferred** (position_preferred, select_one, required)
Enter your preferred position. Select one of the following options: Catcher, Pitcher, Shortstop, Second base, Third base, First base, or Outfielder.

### 2. **Athlete Position(s)** (athlete_position, select_multiple, required)
Select the position(s) you are interested in playing. Multiple positions can be selected. Options: Pitcher, Catcher, Outfielder, Shortstop, First base, Second base, and Third base.

### 3. **Program Information** (program_information, text, optional)
Enter any additional program information.

### 4. **Athlete Details** (athlete_details, number, optional)
Enter the athlete's details. (Please note: This field is not currently used for any programmatic purposes.)

### 5. **Guardian Contact Information** (guardian_contact, text, optional)
Enter the guardian's contact information.

### 6. **Terms and Conditions** (terms_and_conditions, note, optional)
Read and understand the terms and conditions of the program.

### 7. **Consent** (consent, text, optional)
Read and understand the consent for the athlete's registration.

### 8. **Athlete Height** (athlete_height, number, optional)
Enter the athlete's height.

### 9. **Athlete Weight** (athlete_weight, number, optional)
Enter the athlete's weight.

### 10. **Guardian Relationship** (guardian_relationship, text, optional)
Enter the relationship between the guardian and the athlete.
