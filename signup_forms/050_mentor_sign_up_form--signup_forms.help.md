# Mentor Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to sign up as a mentor for a program or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (email, role, and any other required fields).
2. Fill out any optional fields that you feel are relevant to your sign up (first name, last name, phone number, experience years).
3. Choose your role from the dropdown menu (Coach, Mentor, Buddy).
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose your role from the dropdown menu (Coach, Mentor, Buddy). Please note that if you are signing up as a Coach, you should have experience in the field.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter your number of experience years in the field, if relevant.
 

 ## Tips
* Please fill out as much or as little information as you want, but make sure not to fill out duplicate or incomplete information as it might lead to issues with your sign up process.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please feel free to reach out to the administrators.
