# sba_intake - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SBA Intake form is designed to collect information from Small Business Owners/Operators (SBOs) for the SBA's assessment and guidance on potential funding opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Select your SBA Funding Type (Yes or No).
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. Enter your SSN (Social Security Number).
5. Provide your Business Name and Address.
6. Enter your Business Phone number and Email.
7. Enter your Business License number and Website (if applicable).
8. Select your Business Type (Yes or No).
9. Enter a brief Business Description.
10. Select your SBA Program interest (Yes or No).
11. Enter your SBA Location.
12. Enter the Funding Amount required for your business.
13. Specify the terms of your funding (e.g., loan terms, interest rate, repayment schedule).
14. Repeat the SBA Location field to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
* **SBA Funding Type**: Choose whether your business is eligible for SBA funding.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **SSN**: Enter your Social Security Number.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business's street address.
* **Business Phone**: Enter your business's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your business's email address.
* **Business License**: Enter your business's license number.
* **Business Website**: Enter your business's website URL (if applicable).
* **Business Type**: Choose whether your business is eligible for SBA funding.
* **Business Description**: Provide a brief description of your business.
* **SBA Program**: Choose whether your business is interested in the SBA Program.
* **SBA Location**: Enter the location where your business operates.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding your business requires.
* **Funding Terms**: Specify the terms of your funding (e.g., loan terms, interest rate, repayment schedule).
* **SBA Location (2)**: Repeat the SBA Location field to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information before completing this form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the SBA representative for assistance.
