# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signup Form is designed to collect user information for signup purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
- Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
- Provide your email address in the "email" field.
- Enter a password in the "password" field for account creation or confirmation.
- Confirm your email address in the "confirm email" field.
- Select your signup status in the "signup" field.
- Read and agree to the "terms of service" conditions.
- Add any additional notes about your signup in the "notes" field.
- Enter the form ID, created at, updated at, phone number, creator, created time, updated reported time, updated time, signup date, form version, user ID, user, password hash, user email, creator 2, and selected terms of service options as required and/or specified by the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your first name as it appears on your documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required: false): Create a password for your account.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Confirm your email address.
* **Signup** (`signup`, select_one, required: false): Select your signup status.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms`, select_multiple, required: false): Read and agree to the terms of service conditions.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your signup.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter the form ID as specified by the system.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, required: false): Enter the created at date as specified by the system.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, required: false): Enter the updated at date as specified by the system.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Creator** (`created_by`, select_one, required: false): Select the creator of your signup.
* **Created Time** (`created_at_2`, time, required: false): Enter the created time as specified by the system.
* **Updated Reported Time** (`updated_reported_at`, time, required: false): Enter the updated reported time as specified by the system.
* **Updated Time** (`updated_at_2`, time, required: false): Enter the updated time as specified by the system.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, time, required: false): Enter the signup date as specified by the system.
* **Form Version** (`form`, number, required: false): Enter the form version as specified by the system.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required: false): Enter the user ID as specified by the system.
* **User** (`user`, number, required: false): Enter the user as specified by the system.
* **Password Hash** (`password_hash`, number, required: false): Enter the password hash as specified by the system.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter the user email as specified by the system.
* **Creator 2** (`created_by_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the creator 2 as specified by the system.
* **Selected Terms of Service** (`terms`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the terms of service options as specified by the system.
