# Meeting Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Sign In Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to the meeting. This form helps to ensure accurate records and facilitates efficient communication within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Meeting Sign In Form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already signed in for this meeting. If you've previously submitted your information, do not attempt to sign in again.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information:
	* Meeting Title (Meeting Title): Provide the title of the meeting you are attending.
	* Email (Email): Enter your official email address. This will be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
	* Attendee Name (Attendee Name): Enter your name as it appears on your company badge or ID card.
	* Start Time (Meeting Start Time): Enter the start time of the meeting.
3. You may optionally provide additional information:
	* Attendee Notes (Attendee Notes): If you have any relevant comments or notes related to the meeting, you can include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Title (Meeting Title)** (`meeting_title`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Enter the title of the meeting you are attending.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: required): Enter your official email address. This will be used for communication and follow-up purposes.
* **Attendee Name (Attendee Name)** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: required): Enter your name as it appears on your company badge or ID card.
* **Attendee Phone (Attendee Phone)** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional: optional): You can optionally enter your phone number if it's relevant to the meeting or follow-up.
* **Meeting Date (Meeting Date)** (`date`, `date`, required/optional: optional): If this field is available, enter the date of the meeting.
* **Start Time (Meeting Start Time)** (`time`, `time`, required/optional: required): Enter the start time of the meeting.
* **Attendee Notes (Attendee Notes)** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional: optional): If you have any relevant comments or notes related to the meeting, you can include them here.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out the required fields accurately before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, it's best to leave it blank or consult with the meeting organizer.
* Ensure that your email address is up-to-date and accurate.
* This form is intended for internal use within the organization, so avoid sharing this information outside of the organization.
