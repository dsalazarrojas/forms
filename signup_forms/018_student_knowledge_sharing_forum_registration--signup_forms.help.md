# Student Knowledge Sharing Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from students who want to join the knowledge sharing forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

* **Student Info** (Page 1, Student Info, text, required: false): Enter your student's name, age, and other relevant details.
* **Parent Info** (Page 2, Parent Info, text, required: false): Enter your parent's name, phone number, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Info** (Page 3, Contact Info, text, required: false): Enter your address, phone number, and emergency contact details.

* **Academic Info** (Page 6, Academic Info, text, required: false): Enter your grade level or degree.
