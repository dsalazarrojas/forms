# Youth Coat Pick Up RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the youth coat pick up event. It helps us ensure that we have accurate pickup details for each youth participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for confirmation.
3. Enter your email address for confirmation.
4. Choose a pickup time that suits you.
5. Select the size of the youth coat you are picking up.
6. Choose a pickup date for the coat.
7. Select how you would like to be confirmed (text, email, or phone).
8. Add any comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, Required): Please fill in your name so we can address you correctly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter your contact phone number for confirmation purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, Optional): Enter your email address for confirmation via email.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, `time`, Optional): Select a time for coat pickup that works for you.
* **Coat Size** (`coat_size`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose the size of the coat you are picking up (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, `date`, Optional): Select a date for coat pickup.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, `time`, Optional): Select a time to RSVP.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, Optional): Add any additional comments about your coat pickup.
* **Confirmation Method** (`confirmation_method`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose how you would like to be confirmed (text, email, or phone).
* **Confirmation Sent** (`confirmation_sent`, `text`, Optional): This field is not used for the end-user to fill out; it's a field used to track confirmation status.
