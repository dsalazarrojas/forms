# visitor_sign_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information, including name, company, position, and other details related to their visit, such as entry mode and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. Please note that the form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of fields. Please scroll through the pages to ensure you fill in all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the visitor, as they would like to be known during their visit.
* **Visitor Company** (`visitor_company`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the company the visitor belongs to.
* **Visitor Position** (`visitor_position`, Text, Required): Enter the position or title of the visitor.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the company the visitor is visiting, if different from their own company.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Text, Not Required): Enter the ID number of the employee the visitor is representing.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, Not Required): Select the department the visitor is visiting (Security, Management, IT, Finance).
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Not Required): Enter the date of the visit.
* **Time In** (`time_in`, Time, Not Required): Enter the time of entry.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, Time, Not Required): Enter the time of exit.
* **Mode of Entry** (`mode_of_entry`, Select One, Not Required): Select the mode of entry (Foot, Vehicle, Other).
* **Mode of Entry Vehicle Number** (`mode_of_entry_vehicle_number`, Text, Not Required): Enter the number of the vehicle, if applicable.
* **Visitor Signature** (`visitor_signature`, Note, Not Required): This field is reserved for the visitor's signature.
* **Guard Signature** (`guard_signature`, Note, Not Required): This field is reserved for the guard's signature.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, Text, Not Required): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Not Required): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the visit.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure accurate visitor information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the visitor or the guard on duty.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only.
