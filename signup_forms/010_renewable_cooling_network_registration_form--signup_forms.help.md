# renewable_cooling_network_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Renewable Cooling Network Registration Form is a collection of fields that gather information about your organization and its job roles within the renewable cooling industry. This form aims to collect detailed contact information, job details, and organization information for networking and collaboration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your primary interests within the renewable cooling industry.
3. Add any additional information that you think would be relevant to the network.
4. Enter your job title, organization, department, location, and job start and end dates.
5. Enter your organization's address, website, and contact person's name.
6. Select your organization's job titles and departments.
7. Enter your organization's start and end dates for its job roles.
8. Enter the duration of each job role.
