# Player Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect player information for registration, team affiliation, and other relevant details for sports-related activities. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure correct processing and validation of your player data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Player First Name and Player Last Name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your Email and Player Phone for contact purposes.
3. Select your Team affiliation from the given options.
4. Provide your Player Position, number, height, and weight for identification.
5. Specify your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
6. Answer the Language Proficiency and Player English Proficiency questions, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each.
7. Select your Player Country of origin.
8. Fill out the Player Language and Player City fields if applicable.
9. Provide any additional notes about yourself in the Player Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Player Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Player Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Team** (6, select_multiple, required): Select the team you are affiliated with from the given options.
* **Player Position** (7, text, required): Enter your position or role on the team.
* **Player Number** (8, number, required): Enter your player number as assigned by the team.
* **Player Date of Birth** (9, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Player Proficiency (English)** (10, select_multiple, required): Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your English proficiency level.
* **Player Language (English)** (11, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your language proficiency in English.
* **Player Country** (12, select_one, required): Select your country of origin.
* **Player City** (13, text, required): Enter your current city of residence.
* **Player State** (14, text, optional): Enter your state/province of residence.
* **Player Zip** (15, text, optional): Enter your postal/zip code.
* **Player Shoe Size** (16, number, optional): Enter your shoe size.
* **Player Shoe Size Unit** (17, select_one, required): Select the unit for your shoe size (e.g., US or UK).
* **Player Notes** (18, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about yourself.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid form rejection.
* Use the correct format for date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY).
* Be aware that some fields are required for player registration and validation purposes.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling the form, please contact our support team.
