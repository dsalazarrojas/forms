# yoga_class_signup_schedule_preference_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information and schedule preferences for a yoga class. Users can sign up for a class by providing their details, including their schedule preferences and class selection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Information fields.
2. Choose your preferred schedule slot (morning, afternoon, or evening).
3. Select the class you would like to sign up for from the available options.
4. Add any additional contact information if needed.
5. Confirm your email address for communication purposes.
6. Add any phone number for further contact.
7. Enter any relevant notes about your preferences or requirements.
8. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`text`, required: false): This is a general field for users to provide any relevant information about themselves.
* **Schedule Preference** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more time slots to prefer for your yoga class. Options are:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **Select Class** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose the class you would like to sign up for from the available options. Options are:
	+ Class 1
	+ Class 2
	+ Class 3
* **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter a phone number for further contact.
* **Note** (`note`, required: false): Add any relevant notes about your preferences or requirements.
* **Submit** (`text`, required: false): Submit the form to complete your registration.
