# Knitting Circle Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and find the best fit for you within our knitting circle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred meeting time in the "Preferred Meeting Time" field.
6. If applicable, choose when you joined the knitting circle in the "Member Since" field.
7. Choose how often you'd like to meet with the group in the "Frequency of Meetings" field.
8. Select which day of the week you prefer to meet in the "Meeting Day" field.
9. Indicate how often you knit in the "Frequency of Knitting" field.
10. Select your primary knitting tool in the "Primary Knitting Tool" field.
11. Choose the type of yarn you prefer in the "Primary Knitting Material" field.
12. If you have any other knitting tools, enter them in the "Other Knitting Tools" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name, as you would like to be known within the circle.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you for meetings and other important updates.
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (time, required): Choose when you are available to meet.
* **Member Since** (date, not required): If you have joined the circle before, please let us know when.
* **Frequency of Meetings** (select one, required): How often would you like to meet with the group?
	+ Meeting once a week
	+ Meeting every two weeks
	+ Meeting once a month
	+ Meeting less than once a month
* **Meeting Day** (select one, required): Which day of the week would you prefer to meet?
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Frequency of Knitting** (select one, required): How often do you typically knit?
	+ Almost every day
	+ Several times a week
	+ Once a week
	+ Less than once a week
* **Primary Knitting Tool** (select one, required): What is your go-to tool for knitting?
	+ Knitting needles
	+ Knitting needles with cable
	+ Knitting needles with lace
	+ Other
* **Primary Knitting Material** (select one, required): What type of yarn do you prefer?
	+ Wool
	+ Acrylic
	+ Cotton
	+ Other
* **Other Knitting Tools** (text, not required): Do you have any other tools or materials that you enjoy using?
* **Primary Knitting Tool** is not "Other Knitting Tool".
