# Community Defense Signal Group Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who wish to join a community defense signal group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name or preferred alias in the "Name or Alias" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for initial contact and verification.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This number should be associated with your Signal account.
4. Select the neighborhood or area you represent in the "Neighborhood or Area" field.
5. Choose a reason for joining the group in the "Reason for Joining" field.
6. Indicate your availability for response in the "Availability for Response" field.
7. Confirm your identity verification status in the "Have You Verified Your Identity" field.
8. Confirm whether you have Signal installed on your device in the "Do You Have Signal Installed" field.
9. Select your preferred communication methods in the "Preferred Communication Methods" field.
10. Confirm that you agree to follow the group's guidelines and protocols in the "Agree to Group Guidelines" field.
11. Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality in the "Confidentiality Agreement" field.
12. Optionally, provide additional comments or information in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name or Alias** (Participant Name, Text, Required): Enter your name or preferred alias.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your email address for initial contact and verification.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter the phone number associated with your Signal account.
* **Neighborhood or Area** (Neighborhood or Area, Text, Required): Select the neighborhood or area you represent.
* **Reason for Joining** (Reason for Joining, Select One, Required): Choose a reason for joining the group from the options provided.
* **Availability for Response** (Availability for Response, Select Multiple, Required): Select when you are available to respond to the group.
* **Have You Verified Your Identity** (Have You Verified Your Identity, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have verified your identity.
* **Do You Have Signal Installed** (Do You Have Signal Installed, Select One, Required): Confirm that you have Signal installed on your device.
* **Preferred Communication Methods** (Preferred Communication Methods, Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred communication methods.
* **Agree to Group Guidelines** (Agree to Group Guidelines, Select One, Required): Confirm that you agree to follow the group's guidelines and protocols.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (Confidentiality Agreement, Select One, Required): Confirm that you agree to maintain confidentiality.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, Text, Optional): Provide the name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, Text, Optional): Provide the phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about yourself or your circumstances.
