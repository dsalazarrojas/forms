# Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register your business with us. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the basic information about your business, such as its name, type, and address.
2. Provide details about your business's owner, including their name, email, phone number, and start date.
3. Answer questions about the number of employees and your business's website (if available).
4. Finally, give a brief description of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, Limited Liability Company, Nonprofit Organization).
* **Owner First Name** (`owner_first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the business owner.
* **Owner Last Name** (`owner_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the business owner.
* **Owner Email Address** (`owner_email`, email, required): Enter the primary contact email of the business owner.
* **Owner Phone Number** (`owner_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the business owner.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the street address or physical location of your business.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of the area where your business is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where your business is located.
* **Business Phone Number** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Enter the main business contact number (leave this blank if not applicable).
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your business website (if available).
* **Business Start Date** (`startup_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you started your business.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required): Enter the number of people working for your business.
* **Brief Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): Describe your business in a few sentences, providing context about what you do.
