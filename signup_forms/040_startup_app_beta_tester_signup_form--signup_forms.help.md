# Startup App Beta Tester Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup App Beta Tester Signup Form is designed to collect information from potential beta testers and gather feedback on the startup app. By filling out this form, you will be joining the beta testing program and providing valuable insights that will help us improve the app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* First Name: Enter your first name.
	* Last Name: Enter your last name.
2. Enter your contact information:
	* Email: Enter your email address. We'll use this to communicate with you about the beta testing program.
	* Phone (optional): If you'd like to provide a phone number, you can do so. This is not required.
3. Set up your account security:
	* Email Confirmation: Enter your email address again to confirm it's correct.
	* Password: Enter a password of your choice.
	* Confirm Password: Enter your password again to confirm it's correct.
4. Choose your beta program preferences:
	* Beta Program: Select the beta program you're interested in (A, B, or C).
5. Confirm your terms of service:
	* I accept the terms of service: Check the box to agree to our terms of service.
6. Share how you heard about the beta program:
	* How did you hear about the beta program? (Optional): Select how you learned about the beta program.
7. Add any comments:
	* Comments (optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the beta program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address. This is how we'll contact you about the beta testing program.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, `email`, required): Enter your email address again to confirm it's correct.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide a phone number, you can do so.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, required): Enter a password of your choice.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required): Enter your password again to confirm it's correct.
* **Beta Program** (`beta_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the beta program you're interested in (A, B, or C).
* **I accept the terms of service** (`terms_of_service`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the box to agree to our terms of service.
* **How did you hear about the beta program?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you learned about the beta program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the beta program.
