# Referral Code Submission For Credit Monitoring Service - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect referral information from a referrer, enabling the credit monitoring service to track and analyze referral activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your referrer details, including your name and any other required information in the **Referrer Details** page (page 1).
2. Provide your contact information in the **Referrer Contact** page (page 2).
3. Enter your referral code in the **Referral Code** page (page 3).
4. If you have any credit card information, fill it out in the **Referrer Credit Card** page (page 4).
5. Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions in the **Referrer Acceptance** page (page 5).
6. Record the date and time of form submission in the **Form Submission** page (page 6).
7. Review and confirm your acceptance agreement in the **Referrer Acceptance Agreement** page (page 7).
8. Select the current status of your referral in the **Referral Status** page (page 8).
9. Choose whether to submit your referral or not in the **Submit Referral** page (page 9).
10. Review and confirm your submission in the **Submit Referral Confirmation** page (page 10).
11. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Referrer Note** page (page 11).
12. Review and confirm your submission agreement in the **Submit Referral Confirmation Agreement** page (page 12).
13. Add any additional notes or comments for the referral in the **Submit Referral Note** page (page 13).
14. Record any status updates for your referral in the **Submit Referral Status Update** page (page 14).
15-20. Add any additional notes or comments for referrer in **Referrer Note 2-5** pages (pages 15-19).
