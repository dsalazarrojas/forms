# Referral Program Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referral Program Form is designed to help clients provide information about their referrals. This form allows clients to input details about their referral, including the referral's name, email, number, and any additional comments they would like to provide. The form also asks clients to input their own name and email address, which will be used to associate the referral information with their account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referral's name in the "Referral Name" field.
2. Enter the referral's email address in the "Referral Email" field.
3. Enter the referral's number in the "Referral Number" field.
4. Optionally, you can provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
5. Enter your own name in the "Clients Name" field.
6. Enter your own email address in the "Clients Email" field.
7. Enter your own referral number in the "Clients Number" field.
8. Select the "Submit" option to confirm the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referral Name**: A text field where you can enter the name of the person you are referring.
- **Referral Email**: A text field where you can enter the email address of the person you are referring.
- **Referral Number**: A number field where you can enter the phone number of the person you are referring.
- **Comments**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments about your referral.
- **Clients Name**: A text field where you can enter your own name.
- **Clients Email**: A text field where you can enter your email address.
- **Clients Number**: A number field where you can enter your phone number.
- **Submit**: A select_one field with two options: "Submit" and "Other".
