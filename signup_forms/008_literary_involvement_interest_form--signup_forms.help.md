# Literary Involvement Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literary Involvement Interest Form is designed to gather your preferences for participating in literary events or programs. By filling out this form, you will help us better understand your interests and tailor our events to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Provide a title for the event or program you're interested in.
2.  Fill in your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
3.  Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
4.  Provide your phone number (optional).
5.  Choose the type of event you're interested in (e.g. Book Club, Workshop, Festival, or Lecture Series).
6.  Enter the name of the organization or group you're affiliated with (optional).
7.  Specify the location of the event (optional).
8.  Select a date for the event (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
9.  Click submit to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): A brief title for the event or program you're interested in. This will help us identify your preferences.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Your name as you would like it to appear on our records. Please use your real name or a name you prefer to be known by.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address where we can contact you about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted by phone).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of event you're interested in: Book Club, Workshop, Festival, or Lecture Series.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): The name of the organization or group you're affiliated with (if any).
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location of the event (if any).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): The date of the event (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
