# Gym Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Sign Up Form is designed to collect information for new gym members. It helps us understand your preferences and needs so we can provide the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information to avoid any issues with your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard format of mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive updates and notifications from us.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by our staff.
- **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, select one, optional): Choose from the options: "Month to Month" or "Ongoing".
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select multiple, optional): Select one or more payment methods from: "Credit Card", "Bank Transfer", or "Check".
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select one, optional): Select from the options: "Parent", "Sibling", "Spouse", or "Other".
- **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select one, optional): Select from the options: "Active", "Inactive", "Pending", or "Cancelled".
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This is a reference number for our staff, please do not fill it out.
