# Anxiety Support Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experiences with anxiety and what you hope to achieve through joining our support group. The information collected will assist us in creating a safe and supportive environment for all members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (indicated by *).
2. For questions with multiple options, select the answer that best describes your situation.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses, as the information will help us tailor the group to meet your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Age group**: Select the age range you belong to for better grouping.
* **How long have you been experiencing anxiety?**: Choose the duration of your anxiety experience.
* **Severity of anxiety symptoms**: Indicate the level of your anxiety symptoms' impact on your life.
* **Primary anxiety concerns**: Select all the areas that best describe your anxiety concerns.
* **Have you participated in support groups before?**: Choose how you've previously sought support.
* **Are you currently in treatment for anxiety?**: Select your current treatment status.
* **Preferred meeting format**: Choose the type of meeting format you prefer.
* **Preferred meeting day**: Select the day of the week you prefer for meetings.
* **Preferred meeting time**: Choose the time of day you prefer for meetings.
* **How often can you commit to attend?**: Select your available frequency for attending meetings.
* **Preferred group size**: Choose the size of the group you feel comfortable with.
* **Preference for group facilitator**: Select the type of facilitator you prefer.
* **What are your goals for attending? (Optional)**: If you have goals for attending the support group, describe them briefly.
* **Comfort level sharing in a group setting**: Indicate your comfort level in sharing personal matters in a group setting.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you have any accessibility or accommodation needs, describe them.
* **Emergency contact name (Optional)**: If you'd like to have a contact person in case of an emergency, enter their name and contact information.
* **I agree to group confidentiality and code of conduct**: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm your understanding and commitment to the group's rules.
