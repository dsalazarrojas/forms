# house_association_direct_deposit_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "house_association_direct_deposit_form" is used to collect information for a direct deposit to an individual or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Address for direct deposit purposes.
3. Enter the Bank Name and Routing Number for the bank account you're using for direct deposit.
4. Enter the Account Number and Account Holder Name for the account you're depositing into.
5. Enter your Email and Phone number for contact purposes.
6. If applicable, select a Category for the deposit from the provided options.
7. Enter any additional Notes about the deposit.
8. Finally, enter the Date and Time of the deposit.
