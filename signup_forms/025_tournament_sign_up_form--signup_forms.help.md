# tournament_sign_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from teams and their players to register for a tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Team Name field with your team's name.
2. Enter your Player Name and Player Comments in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Email address to help us get in touch with you.
4. Enter your Phone number in case we need to reach you about your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter your team's name in this field.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a player on the team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Player Comments** (`player_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your team.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you about your team.
