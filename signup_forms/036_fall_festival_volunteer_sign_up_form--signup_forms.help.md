# Fall Festival Volunteer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to sign up volunteers for the Fall Festival event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter a valid Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select your availability for the event by choosing one or more shifts in the Availability field.
5. Choose your role from the Volunteer Role field, this will determine how you will be assigned to work during the event. Please note that this field is required.
6. If you have any time constraints, you can input your available time in the Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more shifts that you are available to work during the event. Options include Shift 1, Shift 2, and Shift 3.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, required): Choose the role you would like to take during the event. Options include Event Volunteer, Event Staff, and Festival Coordinator. This field is required.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you have any time constraints, you can input your available time in this field.
