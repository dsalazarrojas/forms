# Queue Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for signing up for a queue, where you will be assigned a time slot based on your selected preferences. Please ensure that you complete the form accurately to avoid any issues with your queue assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your preferred queue from the list of available queues on page 2.
2. Select your preferred service for the chosen queue.
3. Choose a date and time slot for the selected service.
4. Enter your name (optional but recommended).
5. Enter your email address (optional but recommended).
6. Enter your phone number (optional but recommended).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: 
* **Select a queue** (`select_queue`, optional): Select a queue for which you want to sign up.
* **Select a service** (`select_service`, optional): Select a service for the chosen queue.
* **Select a time** (`select_time`, optional): Select a time slot for the selected service.
* **Select a date** (`select_date`, optional): Select a date for the selected service.
### Page 2:
* **Select a queue** (`select_queue_name`, optional): Select a queue name.
* **Select a service** (`select_service`, optional): Select a service for the chosen queue.
* **Select a time** (`select_time`, optional): Select a time slot for the selected service.
* **Select a date** (`select_date`, optional): Select a date for the selected service.
* **Name** (`name`, optional): Enter your name (recommended for queue management purposes).
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address (recommended for queue management purposes).
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number (recommended for queue management purposes).

## Tips
* Make sure to select a queue with available time slots.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with your queue assignment, contact the support team.

Please ensure to complete this form accurately for correct queue assignment.
