# App Beta Tester Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you, our potential beta tester, to determine your suitability for our app beta testing program. We are looking for individuals who are interested in testing our app and providing us with valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your real name and contact information.
2. Select your application date (date you are submitting your application).
3. Provide your age group.
4. Choose your preferred contact method (email or phone number).
5. Describe your previous beta testing experience.
6. Indicate your interests in testing iOS and Android apps.
7. Specify your primary device for testing.
8. List specific device models you own (if any).
9. Mention the operating system versions you have access to.
10. Indicate your average daily app usage time.
11. Select the app categories you are interested in testing.
12. Choose your testing commitment level.
13. Describe your comfort level with reporting detailed bug reports.
14. Indicate your willingness to provide constructive feedback.
15. Confirm your willingness to sign a confidentiality agreement.
16. Tell us how you found out about this opportunity.
17. Add any additional information about yourself (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application date**: The date you are submitting your application.
* **First name**: Your first name.
* **Last name**: Your last name.
* **Email address**: Your email address.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number (if you wish to be contacted).
* **Age group**: Your age group (choose from 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, 65+).
* **Location**: Your city or region.
* **Previous beta testing experience**: Your experience with beta testing (choose from Extensive, Some, Limited, No experience).
* **Interest in testing iOS app**: How interested are you in testing iOS apps (choose from Primary, Secondary, Maybe, No interest, Not applicable).
* **Interest in testing Android app**: How interested are you in testing Android apps (choose from Primary, Secondary, Maybe, No interest, Not applicable).
* **Primary device for testing**: Choose the primary device you will use for testing (choose from iPhone, Android phone, iPad, Android tablet, Multiple devices, No preference).
* **Specific device models**: If you own a specific device model, list it here.
* **Operating system versions**: The operating system versions you have access to (e.g., iOS 15, Android 12).
* **Daily app usage time**: How many hours per day you spend using apps (choose from Less than 1 hour, 1-3 hours, 3-5 hours, 5-8 hours, 8+ hours).
* **App categories you are interested in testing**: Choose all the app categories you are interested in testing (e.g., Social media, Productivity, Gaming).
* **Testing commitment level**: How much time are you willing to dedicate to testing (choose from Flexible and available, Few hours per week, Regular, Limited time available).
* **Comfortable reporting detailed bug reports**: How comfortable are you with reporting detailed bug reports (choose from Very comfortable, Comfortable, Somewhat comfortable, Not comfortable).
* **Willing to sign confidentiality agreement**: Are you willing to sign a confidentiality agreement (choose from Yes, Yes, but have concerns, Need more information, False).
* **Referral source**: How did you find out about this opportunity (choose from Social media, Word of mouth, Website, App store, Forum or community, Email).
* **Additional information about yourself**: Add any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant (optional).
