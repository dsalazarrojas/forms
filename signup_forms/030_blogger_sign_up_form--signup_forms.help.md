# Blogger Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blogger Sign Up Form is designed to collect data from potential bloggers for collaboration and business development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your blog, such as its name, URL, and category.
3. Select your blog's category from the given options.
4. Share your blog's traffic statistics and social media followers.
5. Specify your experience in blogging, the type of content you create, and your collaboration interests.
6. Provide links to your rate card and portfolio samples.
7. Choose your availability to start collaborating and list any previous brand collaborations.
8. Explain why you want to join our network.
9. Agree to our terms and conditions.
10. Add any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Blogger Sign Up Form**: This is the header for the form, providing an introduction to the form's purpose.
* **2. Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): This field is for your legal name, which is essential for our records.
* **3. Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is your primary contact email, where we'll communicate with you about collaboration opportunities.
* **4. Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number, which we may use for further discussions or clarifications.
* **5. Blog Name** (`blog_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your blog, which we'll use to identify your presence in our network.
* **6. Blog URL** (`blog_url`, `text`, required): Provide the URL of your blog, so we can check it for legitimacy and relevance.
* **7. Blog Category** (`blog_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose a category that best describes the type of content you create, such as Lifestyle, Travel, or Technology.
* **8. Monthly Page Views** (`monthly_traffic`, `select_one`, required): Select an approximate traffic range for your blog, which helps us gauge its popularity.
* **9. Social Media Followers** (`social_followers`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of social media followers you have across all platforms.
* **10. Years Blogging** (`years_active`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you've been actively blogging.
* **11. Content Types Created** (`content_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the types of content you've created, such as Written Articles, Photo Essays, or Podcasts.
* **12. Collaboration Interests** (`collaboration_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of collaboration opportunities you're interested in, such as Sponsored Content, Product Reviews, or Social Media Campaigns.
* **13. Rate Card** (`rate_card`, `text`, optional): Provide a link to your rate card or pricing information, which helps us understand your services.
* **14. Portfolio Samples** (`portfolio_samples`, `text`, required): Share links to your best work or portfolio samples, showcasing your expertise.
* **15. Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you can start collaborating, such as Immediately or Within 1 Month.
* **16. Previous Brand Collaborations** (`previous_collaborations`, `text`, optional): List any notable brands you've worked with in the past.
* **17. Why Do You Want to Join** (`why_join`, `text`, required): Explain why you want to join our network and become part of our community.
* **18. Terms Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **19. Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Share any other relevant details about yourself that you'd like to mention.
