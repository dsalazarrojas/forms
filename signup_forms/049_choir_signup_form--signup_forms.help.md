# Choir Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who wish to join the choir. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill out the form.
2. Complete each field as instructed in the following sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (4, number, not required): If you are over 18, enter your age.
* **Email Address** (7, email, required): Enter your valid email address that the choir management can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (8, text, required): Enter your phone number for the choir management to get in touch with you.
* **Voice Type or Vocal Range** (14, select_one, required): Choose your voice type or vocal range from the options provided.
* **Singing Experience Level** (15, select_one, required): Indicate your level of singing experience from the options provided.
* **Previous Choir Experience** (16, select_one, required): Select any previous choir experience you have from the options provided.
* **Can You Read Music** (17, select_one, required): Choose how proficient you are in reading music.
* **Why Are You Interested in Joining the Choir** (19, text, not required): Share your reasons for joining the choir.
* **Availability** (20, select_multiple, required): Select the days you are available for rehearsals.
* **Can You Attend Regular Rehearsals** (21, select_one, required): Indicate if you can attend regular rehearsals.
* **Are You Willing to Perform in Concerts** (22, select_one, required): Confirm if you are willing to perform in concerts.
* **Do You Have Transportation to Rehearsals** (23, select_one, not required): Indicate if you have transportation to rehearsals, even if it's not necessary.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (25, text, not required): If you have any further comments or questions, please include them here.

Please ensure you've filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
