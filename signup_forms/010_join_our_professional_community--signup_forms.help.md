# Join Our Professional Community - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Join Our Professional Community" form is designed to help us understand more about you and tailor the community experience to best suit your needs. By filling out this form, you will be giving us valuable information about yourself which will allow us to make informed decisions about your membership. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name
2. Enter a valid Email Address
3. Enter a valid Phone Number
4. Select your Job Title
5. Choose your Role (Administrator, Member, or Guest)
6. Enter the Date and Time you plan to join
7. Check the box to Agree to Terms and Conditions
8. Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions
9. Check the box to Agree to Privacy Policy
10. Review and agree to the Privacy Policy
11. Check the box to Agree to Our Community Code of Conduct
12. Review and agree to the Code of Conduct
13. Enter how much time you plan to commit to the community
14. Enter your Expected Commitment Frequency
15. Enter what you hope to gain from joining the community
16. Enter why you want to join the community
17. Enter how you heard about us
18. Enter any other comments you may have
19. Enter the Date and Time you plan to join (if different from the previous field)
20. Check the box to Agree to Receive Emails
21. Check the box to Agree to Receive Updates
22. Check the box to Agree to Receive Notifications

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, `Optional`): Your first name. Please enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, `Required`): Your last name. Please enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, `Required`): Enter a valid email address to receive communications from us. Please ensure it is a real email address you access regularly.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you if we need to.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter your job title. This will help us understand your role and tailor the community experience to best suit your needs.
* **Role** (`Role`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Select the role you wish to play in the community (Administrator, Member, or Guest).
* **Join Date** (`Join Date`, `date`, `Required`): Enter the date you plan to join the community. This will help us plan events and activities for the community.
* **Join Time** (`Join Time`, `time`, `Required`): Enter the time you plan to join the community. This will help us ensure we are prepared for your arrival.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`Agree to Terms and Conditions`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to the community's terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `note`, `Optional`): Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Agree to Privacy Policy** (`Agree to Privacy Policy`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to our privacy policy.
* **Privacy Policy** (`Privacy Policy`, `note`, `Optional`): Review and agree to our privacy policy.
* **Agree to Our Community Code of Conduct** (`Agree to Our Community Code of Conduct`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to our community code of conduct.
* **Code of Conduct** (`Code of Conduct`, `note`, `Optional`): Review and agree to our code of conduct.
* **How much time do you plan to commit** (`How much time do you plan to commit`, `number`, `Optional`): Enter a number representing how much time you plan to commit to the community.
* **Expected Commitment Frequency** (`Expected Commitment Frequency`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter how often you plan to commit to the community.
* **What do you hope to gain from joining** (`What do you hope to gain from joining`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter what you hope to gain from joining the community.
* **Why do you want to join** (`Why do you want to join`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter why you want to join the community.
* **How did you hear about us** (`How did you hear about us`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter how you heard about the community.
* **Any other comments** (`Any other comments`, `text`, `Optional`): Enter any other comments you may have.
* **Join Date and Time** (`Join Date and Time`, `date`, `Optional`): Enter the date and time you plan to join the community (if different from the previous field).
* **Agree to Receive Emails** (`Agree to Receive Emails`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to receive emails from the community.
* **Agree to Receive Updates** (`Agree to Receive Updates`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to receive updates from the community.
* **Agree to Receive Notifications** (`Agree to Receive Notifications`, `select_one`, `Required`): Check this box to agree to receive notifications from the community.
