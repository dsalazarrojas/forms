# Dance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a form for registering for a dance course. Users are required to provide their name, email, course details (name, acronym, start and end date, location), and other relevant information. The form is used to gather necessary information for course registration and administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Registrant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Choose your dance category (optional).
5. Add any comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).
6. Enter the course name.
7. Enter the course acronym.
8. Select the course start date.
9. Select the course end date.
10. Enter the course location.
11. Enter the name of the academy where the course will be held.
12. Enter the academy acronym.
13. Enter the name of the contest or festival associated with the course.
14. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication and account creation.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information (not required for account creation).
* **Dance Category** (select one, optional): Choose the category of dance you will be participating in (e.g., ballet, hip hop, contemporary).
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration or course participation.
* **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Acronym** (text, required): Enter the acronym for the course name (e.g., "Dance101").
* **Course Start Date** (date, required): Select the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (date, required): Select the end date of the course.
* **Course Location** (text, required): Enter the location where the course will be held.
* **Academy Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the academy where the course will be held.
* **Academy Acronym** (text, required): Enter the acronym for the academy name.
* **Contest Festival Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the contest or festival associated with the course.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your course registration or participation.
