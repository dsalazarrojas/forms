# 2019 AFLAC Open Enrollment Meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the 2019 AFLAC Open Enrollment Meeting. It is designed to gather information from employees about their current employment status, health coverage, and other relevant details. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your enrollment process is smooth and efficient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Provide your personal details, such as name, employee ID, and email address.
3. Select your department and employment status from the provided options.
4. Answer questions about your health coverage, dependents, and spouse information (if applicable).
5. Indicate your interest in supplemental coverage and approximate budget for insurance premiums.
6. Provide emergency contact information and any special needs or requirements.
7. Choose how you heard about this meeting and any additional comments or notes.
8. Confirm your meeting attendance and agree to electronic signature consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`Full name`, `text`, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Employee ID Number** (`Employee ID Number`, `text`, required): Enter your 6-digit identifier from your employee badge.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, required): We'll send confirmation to this email address.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `phone`, optional): Include area code for contact purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, `date`, required): Used for policy verification.
* **Age** (`Age`, `integer`, optional): Calculated from date of birth.
* **Home address** (`Home address`, `text`, optional): Current residential address.
* **Department** (`Department`, `select_one`, required): Select your current department.
* **Employment status** (`Employment status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current employment classification.
* **Years with company** (`Years with company`, `integer`, optional): Total years of service.
* **Current health coverage** (`Current health coverage`, `select_one`, required): Select your current insurance coverage.
* **Number of dependents** (`Number of dependents`, `integer`, optional): How many family members will be covered?
* **Spouse name (if applicable)** (`Spouse name (if applicable)`, `text`, optional): Enter if spouse will be included in coverage.
* **Number of children** (`Number of children`, `integer`, optional): How many children will be covered?
* **Previous AFLAC experience** (`Previous AFLAC experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you had AFLAC coverage before?
* **Interested in supplemental coverage** (`Interested in supplemental coverage`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check all that apply (Accident insurance, Critical illness, Hospital indemnity, Life insurance, Disability insurance, Dental/vision).
* **Budget for insurance premiums** (`Budget for insurance premiums`, `decimal`, optional): Approximate amount you can allocate.
* **Emergency contact name** (`Emergency contact name`, `text`, optional): Person to contact in case of emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`Emergency contact phone`, `phone`, optional): Include area code.
* **Any special needs** (`Any special needs`, `text`, optional): Please specify any special needs or requirements.
* **How did you hear about this meeting?** (`How did you hear about this meeting?`, `select_one`, optional): How were you informed about this enrollment meeting?
* **Additional comments or notes** (`Additional comments or notes`, `text`, optional): Any other information about your coverage needs.
* **Will you attend the full meeting?** (`Will you attend the full meeting?`, `select_one`, required): We recommend staying for the entire presentation.
* **Electronic signature consent** (`Electronic signature consent`, `select_one`, required): By checking this box, you consent to electronic processing.

## Tips
* Please answer truthfully and accurately to ensure that your enrollment process is smooth and efficient.
* If you have any special needs or requirements, please mention them in the "Any special needs" field.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact your HR representative.
