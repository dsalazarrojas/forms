# Food Bank Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the food bank program, providing us with the necessary information to deliver meals and services to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Please refer to the field-by-field explanations for help with any unclear fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, not required): This field is intended to capture basic user information, such as name, address, etc.
* **Meal Pickup** (`meal_pickup`, select_one, not required): Please select one of the meal options provided. This will help us understand your meal preferences.
* **Meal Pickup Date** (`meal_pickup_date`, date, not required): Please enter the date you would like to pick up your meal.
* **Meal Pickup Time** (`meal_pickup_time`, time, not required): Please select the time you would like to pick up your meal.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): This field is for you to enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address for us to contact you with updates.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, not required): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, not required): This field is for any other comments or feedback you may have.
* **Food Bank Comments** (`food_bank_comments`, text, not required): If you have any feedback or comments about the food bank program, please enter them here.
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, select_one, not required): Please indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the food bank program.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, not required): If you agree to the terms and conditions, please enter the date you are giving consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, not required): If you agree to the terms and conditions, please select the time you are giving consent.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanations above.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
