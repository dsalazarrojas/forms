# Winter Wellness Bundle Giveaway Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to enter a giveaway for a wellness bundle. Participants can submit their name and email address to be entered into the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Giveaway Entry** (form_entry, text, optional):
	+ This is the main entry field where you can write your message or answer the question about why you'd love to receive the wellness bundle. Please be creative and brief!
* **Name** (name, text, required):
	+ Enter your full name as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (email, email, required):
	+ Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.

## Tips
Make sure to provide a valid email address so we can contact you if you win the giveaway. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
