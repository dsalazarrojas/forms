# Coaching Program Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your coaching interests and preferences to provide you with the best possible coaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Primary Contact Email** in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your **Contact Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
4. (Optional) If applicable, fill in your **Company Name** and your **Job Title**.
5. Select your **Industry** from the list.
6. Choose your **Coaching Interest** from the list.
7. Explain your **Coaching Goals** in the "Coaching Goals" field.
8. Describe the **Challenges** you are facing in the "What Challenges Are You Facing" field.
9. Answer whether you have had **Previous Coaching** experience, and if so, describe it in the "Previous Experience Details" field.
10. Choose your **Preferred Coaching Format**, **Delivery Method**, and **Session Frequency**.
11. Select a time frame for when you want to start coaching sessions in the "When Do You Want to Start" field.
12. (Optional) Set a **Budget Range**.
13. Confirm that you are the **Decision Maker** and select whether you can decide to purchase coaching services on your own or if you need approval from someone else.
14. If applicable, choose the number of people who need coaching in the "How Many People Need Coaching" field.
15. Indicate if you are interested in a **Discovery Call** with one of our coaches.
16. Select a preferred time for a **Discovery Call**.
17. Choose how you would like to be contacted for further questions or follow-ups.
18. (Optional) Provide the name of the person who referred you.
19. If you have any **Additional Questions or Comments**, feel free to add them in the "Additional Questions or Comments" field.
20. Finally, decide if you would like to join our **Newsletter** and receive coaching tips and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your legal full name that we will use to address you in our communication.
* **Email Address**: Primary contact email that we will use to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number**: Contact phone number we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Company Name**: Optional field for you to provide your company name (e.g., for billing purposes).
* **Job Title**: Optional field for you to provide your job title.
* **Industry**: Please select from the given options (e.g., Technology, Healthcare, etc.).
* **Coaching Interest**: Please choose what type of coaching you are interested in (e.g., Executive Coaching, Leadership Coaching, etc.).
* **Coaching Goals**: Describe what you want to achieve through coaching (e.g., improve leadership skills, overcome personal challenges, etc.).
* **What Challenges Are You Facing**: Explain the specific issues you want to address through coaching.
* **Previous Coaching Experience**: Select whether you have had coaching before and describe your experience.
* **Previous Experience Details**: If you have had coaching before, describe your experience (e.g., how many sessions, results, etc.).
* **Preferred Coaching Format**: Select how you would like to receive coaching (e.g., one-on-one, group coaching, etc.).
* **Delivery Method**: Select how you would like coaching sessions to be delivered (e.g., in person, via video call, phone call, etc.).
* **Session Frequency**: Select how often you would like coaching sessions to take place (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **When Do You Want to Start**: Choose a start time for coaching sessions.
* **Budget Range**: (Optional) Select your estimated budget for coaching sessions.
* **Decision Maker**: Confirm that you can decide to purchase coaching services on your own or if you need approval from someone else.
* **How Many People Need Coaching**: If you are buying for a team, select the number of people that need coaching.
* **Interested in Discovery Call**: Check if you are interested in a free consultation with one of our coaches.
* **Discovery Call Time**: Select a preferred time for the discovery call.
* **Best Time to Contact You**: Select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select how you heard about us (e.g., online search, referral, etc.).
* **Referral Name**: If a referral brought you to us, provide the name of the referrer.
* **Additional Questions or Comments**: Use this field to add any other questions, comments, or concerns.
* **Join Our Newsletter**: Decide if you want to join our newsletter to receive coaching tips and updates.
