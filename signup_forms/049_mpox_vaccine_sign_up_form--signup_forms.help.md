# Mpox Vaccine Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to sign up for the Mpox vaccine. It is intended for individuals who are eligible to receive the Mpox vaccine. Please ensure you are eligible before completing this form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the "user_info" field.
2. Select the Mpox vaccine type you wish to receive by choosing from the "Choose Mpox Vaccine" dropdown menu.
3. Schedule your vaccine appointment by entering the desired date in the "schedule_vaccine" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **user_info** (Text, required): This field collects your basic information for contact purposes.
  - Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details as required.
  
- **Choose Mpox Vaccine** (Select One, required): Choose the Mpox vaccine you would like to receive:
  - J&J (Johnson & Johnson)
  - Pfizer
  - Moderna

- **schedule_vaccine** (Date, required): Select the date you would like to schedule your vaccine appointment.
  - Use the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 02/15/2024) to enter your desired appointment date.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy to ensure you receive notifications and confirmations.
- Ensure you have the necessary documents and identification to complete the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your local health department or healthcare provider for assistance.
