# Joining Form Mutual Attraction 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Joining Form Mutual Attraction 2" is designed to collect basic information from individuals interested in joining a mutual attraction platform. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have not used this form previously. If you have already joined, you cannot rejoin.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
3. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Desired Partner Gender** (`desired_partner_gender`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the gender you are interested in as a partner (Female, Male, Both).
* **Desired Attraction Gender** (`desired_attraction_gender`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the gender you are interested in as an attraction (Female, Male, Both).
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your current relationship status (Single, In a relationship, Married).
* **Desired Attraction Orientation** (`desired_attraction_orientation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the attraction orientation you prefer (Straight, Gay, Bi, Other).
* **Match or Partner You Are Searching** (`match_or_partners`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the type of match or partner you are searching for.
* **I Agree** (`signup_form_agree`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select "I Agree" to confirm you agree to the terms of the platform.

## Tips

1. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
2. Ensure your email address and phone number are accessible and accurate.
3. Double-check your choices for desired partner and attraction gender and orientation.
4. Review the terms of the platform before confirming your agreement.
