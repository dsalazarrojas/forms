# Professional Networking Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the professional networking platform. It requires users to provide their personal and professional information to create a profile on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the format of "example@domain.com" in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
5. Enter your company name (if different from your own name) in the "Company" field.
6. Enter your position or job title in the "Position" field.
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter your LinkedIn profile URL in the "LinkedIn" field (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field to collect your first name.
* **Last Name**: A required field to collect your last name.
* **Email**: A field to collect your email address, which will be used for login and communication purposes. Please use a format like "example@domain.com".
* **Company Name**: A field to collect your company's name.
* **Company**: A field to collect your company name (if it's different from your personal name).
* **Position**: A field to collect your position or job title in your current company.
* **Job Title**: A field to collect your job title.
* **Location**: A field to collect your current location.
* **Phone**: A field to collect your phone number.
* **LinkedIn**: A field to collect your LinkedIn profile URL, if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address is formatted correctly before submitting the form.
* If your company name is different from your personal name, you can enter it in both the "Company Name" and "Company" fields.
* If you don't have a LinkedIn profile, you can leave this field blank.
* You can use this form multiple times, but please note that each submission will create a new profile.
