# Petition Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Petition Signup Form is used to collect information from individuals who want to sign up for a petition. This form helps to keep track of signers' details and their preferences for which petition they want to sign up for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the third field. Please make sure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the fourth field.
5. Enter your **Address** in the fifth field.
6. Select the **Petition** you want to sign up for from the sixth field.
7. If you are representing an **Organization**, enter its name in the seventh field.
8. If you have a specific **Position** within the organization, enter it in the eighth field.
9. Write a brief **Signature** (e.g., a short message) in the ninth field.
10. Choose whether you want to **Submit** your information (yes) or **Not Submit** (no) in the tenth field.
11. Confirm your **Email** address by re-entering it in the eleventh field.
12. Confirm your **Contact Phone** number by re-entering it in the twelfth field.
13. Enter the date you signed up in the thirteenth field (using the date format MM/DD/YYYY).
14. Enter the time you signed up in the fourteenth field (using the 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or how you prefer to be called.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or how you prefer to be called.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter a valid email address you check regularly.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **Sign up for** (`sign_up_for`, select_one, not required): Select which petition you want to sign up for.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Position** (`position`, text, not required): If you have a specific position within the organization, enter it here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, not required): Write a brief message, e.g., a short statement of support.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, not required): Choose to submit your information (yes) or not (no).
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, not required): Re-enter your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Phone** (`phone_confirmation`, text, not required): Re-enter your contact phone number to confirm.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you signed up (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`signature_time`, time, not required): Enter the time you signed up (HH:MM).
