# Gaming Community Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you sign up for a membership in the Gaming Community Network. It collects necessary information about you, such as your name, email, gaming experience, and preferences, to create your account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter a password in the "Password" field. 
   Make sure your password is at least 8 characters long and contains a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. 
4. Confirm your password in the "Confirm Password" field.
5. Describe your gaming experience in the "Gaming Experience" field.
6. Choose your time zone from the "Time Zone" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your time zone offset in the "Time Zone Offset" field.
8. Select your role in the "Role" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your time zone offset units in the "Time Zone Offset Units" field (e.g., hours, minutes, etc.).
10. Enter the number of minutes your time zone offset is from UTC in the "Time Zone Offset Minutes" field.
11. Select the types of games you are interested in playing from the "What type of games would you play?" dropdown menu (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your real name as you would like to be known in the community.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for receiving community updates and notifications.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, required): Enter a strong password of at least 8 characters with a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required): Confirm your password to ensure its accuracy.
* **Gaming Experience** (`gaming_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your experience with gaming, including games you've played or played competitively.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, `select_one`, required): Choose your time zone from the provided options.
* **Time Zone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, `number`, required): Enter your time zone offset from UTC.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the community, such as player, moderator, or admin.
* **Time Zone Offset Units** (`timezone_offset_units`, `text`, required): Choose the unit of your time zone offset (e.g., hours, minutes, etc.).
* **Time Zone Offset Minutes** (`timezone_offset_minutes`, `number`, required): Enter the offset in minutes.
* **What type of games would you play?** (`game_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of games you are interested in playing, such as multiplayer, single-player, or co-op.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for your time zone offset and offset units to ensure correct calculations.
* Be honest and accurate when describing your gaming experience.
* Choose your role carefully, as it affects your permissions and responsibilities within the community.
* You can select multiple types of games you are interested in playing.
