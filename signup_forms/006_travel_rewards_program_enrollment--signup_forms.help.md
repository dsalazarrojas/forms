# Travel Rewards Program Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Rewards Program Enrollment form is designed to help users enroll into the travel rewards program. This form is intended for users who wish to participate in the program and start earning rewards for their travel-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information and select your travel preferences. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment** (`enrollment`, text, optional): Enter your enrollment reason here. This field is not required, but helps us understand your motivation for joining the program.
* **Member Info** (`member_info`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant information about yourself as a member, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* **Travel Preferences** (`travel_preferences`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify your travel preferences. You can enter any details about your travel habits, such as destinations, frequency, or type of travel.
* **Segment** (`segment`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are a frequent traveler or 'No' if you are a occasional traveler.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Here, you can select multiple travel preferences from the dropdown list. This will help us better understand your preferences and tailor our rewards to your needs.
