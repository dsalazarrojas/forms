# religious_study_group_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "religious_study_group_application_form" is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in joining a religious study group. The form is used to gather details about the group's meeting time, name, size, and frequency, as well as the submitted by and submitted on information. This form is essential for creating a successful and organized study group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting day and time that suits your study group's schedule.
2. Enter the name of your study group.
3. Choose the size of your study group (Small, Medium, or Large).
4. Select the meeting frequency (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
5. Choose the type of study group (Bible Study, Prayer Group, or Book Club).
6. Enter the preferred meeting time.
7. Add any additional information about your study group.
8. Enter the name and email address of the person submitting the form.
9. Select the date of submission.
10. Choose the meeting time for your study group.
