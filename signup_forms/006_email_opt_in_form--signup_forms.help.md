# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from visitors interested in receiving email marketing materials from us. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide your email address for us to contact you
* Share your name and job title for our records
* Indicate your department and company information
* Provide your company website for verification purposes
* Choose whether you'd like to opt-in to receive email marketing materials
* Optionally provide a message for email marketing materials

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details
2. Select whether you'd like to opt-in to email marketing materials

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`1`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from us.
* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide your name and job title for our records.
* **Job Title** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your business card or company profile.
* **Department** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Company** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your company name or organization.
* **Company Website** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your company website URL.
* **Email Marketing Opt-in** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you'd like to opt-in to email marketing materials (`Yes` or `No`).
* **Email Marketing Opt-in Message** (`8`, `text`, optional): Provide a message for email marketing materials (if opted-in).
* **Alternate Job Title** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter an alternate job title for your professional profile.
* **Job Title Alternative** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter an alternative job title for your professional profile.
* **Job Title Variation** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter a variation of your job title.
* **Another Job Title** (`12`, `text`, required): Enter another job title for your professional profile.
* **Job Title with Hint** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter a job title with a hint (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [contact email].
