# grand_jury_district_court_juror_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The grand jury district court juror questionnaire is a form designed to gather information from jurors serving in a district court. This form helps to ensure that all jurors have accurate and up-to-date information, which is vital for the efficient functioning of the jury system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information in the "Jury Information" section. This includes your contact number and email address. You can type in your contact number and email address as they appear on your official records.
2. Select your juror duty from the options provided in the "Juror Duty" section. If you do not see your duty listed, you can specify it in the "Other (please specify)" text field.
3. If you selected "Other" in the "Juror Duty" section, provide a detailed explanation in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Juror Information** (Jury Information, text, optional): This section is for entering your personal details, such as your contact number and email address.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, number, optional): Enter your contact number as it appears on your official records.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): You can enter any additional phone number you'd like to provide for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your official records.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, number, optional): Enter your zip code as it appears on your official records.
* **Juror Duty** (Juror Duty, select_one, optional): Select your juror duty from the options provided. If you don't see your duty listed, you can choose "Other" and provide a detailed explanation in the text field below.
* **Juror Duty Other** (Juror Duty Other, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "Juror Duty" section, provide a detailed explanation of your duty here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your court administrator or support team.
* This form is for your personal and official records, so please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
