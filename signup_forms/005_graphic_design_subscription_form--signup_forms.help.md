# graphic_design_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by the business to collect information from clients for graphic design services. Fill out the form to provide essential details about your design needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete details.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your business email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Design Service** (`design_service`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you need graphic design services.
* **Design Service Type** (`design_service_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the types of graphic design services you need (e.g., logo, brochures, posters).
* **Design File** (`design_file`, `note`, optional): Provide any relevant information about your design file.
* **Upload Design File** (`upload_design_file`, `text`, optional): If you have a design file, attach it here.
* **Project Deadline** (`project_deadline`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when you need the project to be completed by.
* **Project Start Time** (`project_start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when you want the project to start.
