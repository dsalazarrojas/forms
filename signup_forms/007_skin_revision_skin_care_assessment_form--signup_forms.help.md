# Skin Revision Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your skin care assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all the answers that apply to you from the "signup_form" question.
2. In the "assessment" section, provide detailed information about your skin care needs and concerns.
3. Choose whether you have "consent" or not.
4. If required, enter your contact information (phone number, email, or both) to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **signup_form** (Select Multiple, required): Indicate whether you are signing up for our skin care services, signing off, or both.
- **assessment** (Text, optional): Enter your skin concerns, skin type, or any other information that you think is relevant to your skin care needs. This can include any medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities you have.
- **consent** (Select One, required): Choose whether you give us permission to contact you for follow-up.
- **terms and conditions** (Text, optional): This is a text field for any additional information related to your skin care needs.
- **notes** (Note, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations you have about your skin care needs.
- **phone number** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.
- **email** (Email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
- **date** (Date, optional): Enter the date of your skin care assessment.
- **time** (Time, optional): Enter the time of your skin care assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* Our team will use the information provided to create a personalized skin care plan tailored to your needs.
