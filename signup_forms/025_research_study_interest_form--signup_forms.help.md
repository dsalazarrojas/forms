# Research Study Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from researchers and participants interested in potential research studies. It allows them to provide details about their interest in studies, contact preferences, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and thorough information.
2. Select your preferred contact method from the options provided.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes that will help study administrators understand your interest and needs.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Studies Interest** (`research_studies_interest`, text, required)
  * This field is where you can provide details about your interest in potential research studies.

* **Contact Requests** (`contact_requests`, select_one, required)
  * Please select how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Other).

* **Follow Up Requests** (`follow_up_requests`, select_multiple, required)
  * Please select which follow-up methods you would prefer (Phone call, Email, or Mail).

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
  * This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your interest or specific study preferences.

* **Study Area** (`study_area`, text, required)
  * Please specify the area of study you are interested in.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required)
  * Enter your full name.

* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required)
  * Enter your email address where you can be reached.

* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional)
  * This is an optional field for your phone number.

* **Study Institution** (`study_institution`, select_one, required)
  * Please select the type of institution (Academic, Non-academic, or Both).

* **Research Team** (`research_team`, select_one, required)
  * Please select your role in the research team (Lead Researcher, Research Assistant, or Both).

* **Study Status** (`study_status`, select_one, required)
  * Please select the status of the study (Active, Recruiting, or Completed).

* **Follow-Up Timeframe** (`follow_up_timeframe`, select_one, required)
  * Please select how soon you would like to be contacted for follow up (Within a week, Within two weeks, or More than two weeks).

* **Contact Request Timeframe** (`contact_request_timeframe`, select_one, required)
  * Please select how soon you would like to be contacted after submitting the form (Immediately, Within a week, or More than a week).

* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional)
  * This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information that will be shared with the participant.
