## Purpose
The form is for users to enter themselves into a giveaway contest for a free sweatshirt.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email.
2. Select your preferred size and style of sweatshirt.
3. Choose your favorite colors.
4. Enter your shipping information, including your zip code, address, city, and state.
