# Consignment Sale Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for consignors (vendors) to sign up for a consignment sale event. It is used to collect information about the consignor, their sale event preferences, and the items they will be consigning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the sign-up date for your participation in the sale event.
2. Provide your full name and contact information, including email address, phone number, and mailing address.
3. Indicate your preferred sale event and date.
4. Select the categories of items you will be consigning (e.g., Clothing - Women, Shoes, etc.).
5. Specify whether you will be donating unsold items to charity.
6. Choose how you would like to be paid for your participation in the sale event.
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the sale event.
8. Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign-Up Date** (`signup_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your registration for the sale event.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`vendor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`vendor_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`vendor_address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`vendor_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your mailing address.
* **State/Province** (`vendor_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your mailing address.
* **Postal Code** (`vendor_postal_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your mailing address.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you have a business name, enter it here (if applicable).
* **Sale Event** (`sale_event`, text, required): Select the sale event you are joining.
* **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the sale event.
* **Sale Location** (`sale_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the sale event.
* **Item Categories** (`item_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select the categories of items you will be consigning (e.g., Clothing - Women, Shoes, etc.).
* **Other Categories** (`item_categories_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, specify the categories of items you will be consigning.
* **Estimated Number of Items** (`estimated_items`, select_one, required): Select the estimated number of items you will be consigning.
* **Estimated Total Value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter the approximate retail value of the items you will be consigning.
* **Item Condition** (`item_condition`, select_one, required): Select the quality of the items you will be consigning (e.g., New with tags, Like new, etc.).
* **Pricing Responsibility** (`pricing_responsibility`, select_one, required): Select who will be responsible for pricing the items (e.g., Consignor sets prices, Sale organizer sets prices, etc.).
* **Commission Rate** (`commission_rate`, select_one, required): Select the commission rate you are willing to pay (e.g., 30 percent, 35 percent, etc.).
* **Other Commission Rate** (`commission_rate_other`, number, optional): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, enter the commission rate you are willing to pay.
* **Drop-Off Date** (`drop_off_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you will deliver the items to be consigned.
* **Drop-Off Time** (`drop_off_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred drop-off time.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you will collect unsold items.
* **Are You Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in volunteering during the sale event.
* **Volunteer Shifts Available** (`volunteer_shifts`, select_multiple, optional): Select the volunteer shifts you are available to work during the sale event.
* **Volunteer Benefit Understanding** (`volunteer_benefit`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the volunteer benefits.
* **Payment Preference** (`payment_preference`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be paid (e.g., Check, Cash, etc.).
* **Charity Donation** (`charity_donation`, text, optional): If you selected "Donate to charity" in the previous field, specify the charity you are donating to.
* **Unsold Items Plan** (`unsold_items`, select_one, required): Select what to do with unsold items (e.g., Pick up after sale, Donate to charity, etc.).
* **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the sale event.
* **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your full name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth process for both you and the sale event organizers.
* Double-check your contact information and ensure it is up to date.
* Select the correct category and estimated number of items to be consigned accurately.
* Be clear about your pricing responsibility and commission rate.
* Confirm your volunteer interest and shift availability.
* Read and agree to the terms and conditions before signing the form.
