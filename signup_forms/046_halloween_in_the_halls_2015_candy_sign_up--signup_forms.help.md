# Halloween In The Halls 2015 Candy Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for signing up students for the "Halloween In The Halls 2015" event at your school or educational institution. Please fill out this form to provide the required information for your student's registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Make sure you have all the necessary information about the student.
2. Fill out each field with the correct information. You can refer to the field explanations below if you are unsure about any of the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sign up sheet**: This field is a text area where you can add any additional information about the student or any other relevant details.
* **First name**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Last name**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Phone number**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address. Please note that this field is expecting a valid email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Student id**: Enter the student's ID number.
* **Year**: Enter the student's year (likely their grade level or class).
* **Grade**: Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **Student status**: Select the student's status (Active, Non-resident, or Resident).
* **Parent name**: Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all the required fields and double-check your information before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your school or event organizers for help.
