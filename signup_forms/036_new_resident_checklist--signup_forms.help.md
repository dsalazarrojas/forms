# new_resident_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_resident_checklist form is used to collect information about a new resident's details for the resident's records and to ensure a smooth transition into the building or community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *) and the optional fields with relevant information.
2. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Resident Checklist 1** (New Resident Checklist, text, optional): This field allows you to provide a brief description of the resident's information.
* **New Resident Checklist 2** (New Resident Checklist 2, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the resident.
* **Unit Number** (Unit Number, text, optional): This field is used to record the resident's unit or apartment number.
* **Move-in Date** (Move-in Date, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the resident moved into the building or community.
* **Resident Name** (Resident Name, text, optional): This field is used to record the resident's name.
* **Resident Email** (Resident Email, email, optional): This field is used to record the resident's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): This field is used to record the resident's phone number.
* **Move-in Date 2** (Move-in Date 2, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the resident moved into the building or community (this field seems to be a duplicate of "Move-in Date").

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
- This form is not intended for duplicate or outdated records; ensure you only submit one form per new resident.
