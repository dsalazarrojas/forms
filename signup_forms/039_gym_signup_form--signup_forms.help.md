# Gym Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Signup Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to join the gym. This form is used to create a new member profile, which will be used to manage their membership and account information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication and account management.
3. Choose a unique username that will be used for internal system access.
4. Create a strong password for your account.
5. Confirm your password to ensure it is accurate.
6. Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., 1990-02-12).
7. Enter your phone number for communication and emergency contact purposes.
8. Select the type of gym that best suits your needs (e.g., minimalist, commercial, fitness, yoga, strength, cardio, functional).
9. Choose the type of membership you prefer (e.g., monthly, annual, one time).
10. Select the membership plan that best suits your needs (e.g., monthly, annual, one time).
11. Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
12. Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, guardian, other).
13. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
14. Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
15. If you have any medical conditions, enter a brief description in the text field.
16. Enter the location of your preferred gym.
17. Enter the address of the gym.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Last name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for communication and account management.
* **Username** (Text, Optional): Choose a unique username that will be used for internal system access.
* **Password** (Text, Required): Create a strong password for your account.
* **Confirm password** (Text, Required): Confirm your password to ensure it is accurate.
* **Date of birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (e.g., 1990-02-12).
* **Phone number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for communication and emergency contact purposes.
* **Type of gym** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of gym that best suits your needs (e.g., minimalist, commercial, fitness, yoga, strength, cardio, functional).
* **Membership type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of membership you prefer (e.g., monthly, annual, one time).
* **Membership plan** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the membership plan that best suits your needs (e.g., monthly, annual, one time).
* **Emergency contact name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (Select One, Optional): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, guardian, other).
* **Emergency contact phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical condition** (Text, Optional): If you have any medical conditions, enter a brief description in the text field.
* **Medical condition details** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional information about your medical condition.
* **Gym location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your preferred gym.
* **Gym address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the gym.
