# Wedding RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for couples or individuals to RSVP to a wedding event. It will help the wedding host gather necessary information about the guest's presence, contact details, and special requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your guest's name in the **Guest Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, so the host can contact you with updates.
3. Provide your phone number in the **Phone** field, if you'd like to be reached by phone.
4. Select your attendance status in the **Attending** field:
	* True: You will definitely be attending the wedding.
	* False: You will not be attending the wedding.
	* Maybe: You might attend, but are not sure yet.
5. If you're attending, choose a date for your RSVP in the **RSVP Date** field.
6. If you have any special requests, such as seating or transportation, write them in the **Message** field.
7. If you're attending with other guests, enter the number in the **Guest Count** field.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them in the **Dietary Restrictions** field:
	* Gluten Free
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* None
9. If you have any meal preferences, select them in the **Meal Preferences** field:
	* Grilled Steak
	* Baked Salmon
	* Vegetarian Option
	* None
10. If you'd like to provide a specific time of arrival, use the **Time of Arrival** field.
11. If you have any additional comments or requests, use the **Message 2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your guest's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so the host can contact you with updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number if you'd like to be reached by phone.
* **Attending** (`attending`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your attendance status.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for your RSVP.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required/optional): Write any special requests or comments.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, required/optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Meal Preferences** (`meal_preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any meal preferences.
* **Time of Arrival** (`time_arrival`, time, required/optional): Choose a specific time of arrival.
* **Message 2** (`message2`, note, required/optional): Write any additional comments or requests.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help the host plan for the wedding.
* If you have any special requests, such as seating or transportation, be sure to mention them in the **Message** field.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please select them in the **Dietary Restrictions** field to ensure you receive suitable options.
