# Book Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Book Club Registration Form is designed to gather information from potential members to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone. It helps us understand your preferences, interests, and availability to match you with the right group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you understand the form's purpose and guidelines.
2. Fill out the form carefully, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting your application.
4. Once submitted, you'll receive a confirmation email with further instructions on the next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (3)** (Required): Enter the email address you want us to use for meeting updates.
* **Phone Number (4)** (Optional): Enter your phone number for urgent communications.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (Optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address Line 1 (6)** (Optional): Enter your street address.
* **City (7)** (Optional): Enter your city.
* **State (8)** (Optional): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code (9)** (Optional): Enter your ZIP code.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (10)** (Optional): Choose how you found out about this book club (e.g., Friend or Family Referral, Social Media, etc.).
* **Favorite Reading Genres (11)** (Required): Select all the genres you enjoy reading. For example, Fiction, Mystery, Romance, etc.
* **What Are You Currently Reading (12)** (Optional): List any books you're currently reading.
* **Reading Goal (13)** (Required): Choose your approximate reading rate per year.
* **Preferred Book Format (14)** (Required): Select how you prefer to read (e.g., Physical, eBook, Audiobook).
* **Preferred Meeting Type (15)** (Required): Choose how you'd like to meet (e.g., In Person Only, Virtual Only, Hybrid Either Works).
* **Your Availability (16)** (Required): Select all the days and times you're available to attend meetings.
* **Comfort Level Sharing in Groups (17)** (Optional): Choose how comfortable you are sharing opinions in groups.
* **Previous Book Club Experience (18)** (Optional): Select if you've been in a book club before.
* **Interested in Volunteering (19)** (Required): Choose if you're interested in helping with organizing or facilitating.
* **Accessibility Needs or Accommodations (20)** (Optional): Enter any accommodations we should provide for you.
* **Emergency Contact Name (21)** (Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (22)** (Optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **I Agree to Book Club Guidelines (23)** (Required): Select "Yes I agree" to confirm you'll participate respectfully.
* **Registration Date (24)** (Required): Enter the date you're submitting your application.

Please review each field carefully and ask for guidance if you're unsure. Your answers will help us tailor the book club experience to suit your needs.
