# customer_relationship_management_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create or manage customer subscriptions for relationship management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information, including their name and email address.
2. Select the subscription type and date.
3. Choose a subscription duration, such as month, month to month, or year.
4. Add any notes about the subscription.
5. Select the assigned tool(s) for the customer.
6. Finally, confirm the form by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer** (`id: 1`): Enter the customer's name.
* **First name** (`id: 2`): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last name** (`id: 3`): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`id: 4`): Enter the customer's email address. Please note that this field is expected to be an email address.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Subscription type** (`id: 6`): Select the type of subscription for the customer.
* **Subscription date** (`id: 7`): Select the date of the subscription.
* **Subscription duration** (`id: 8`): Select the duration of the subscription, e.g., month, month to month, or year.
* **Notes** (`id: 9`): Add any additional notes about the subscription.
* **Assigned tool** (`id: 10`): Select the tool assigned to the customer, such as chatjimmy.
* **Created by** (`id: 11`): Enter the person who created the subscription.
* **Updated by** (`id: 12`): Enter the person who updated the subscription.

## Tips
Make sure to fill in all required fields and double-check the information before submitting the form.
