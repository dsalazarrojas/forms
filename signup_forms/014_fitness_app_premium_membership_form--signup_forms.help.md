# Fitness App Premium Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to sign up for a premium membership to the fitness app. It will help you create or update your premium membership plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Email Address.
3. Provide your Password (8-12 characters).
4. Confirm if you would like a premium membership plan.
5. Confirm your Payment Method.
6. Enter your Card Number.
7. Enter your Expiration Date.
8. Enter your Start Date.
9. Enter your Plan Price.
10. Confirm your Billing Cycle.
11. Confirm your Billing Frequency.
12. Confirm your Payment Terms.
13. Enter your Onboarding Details (optional).
14. Confirm your Terms and Conditions.
15. Enter your Plan Start Date.
16. Enter your Billing Date.
17. Confirm your Billing Status.
18. Confirm your Membership Status.
19. Enter your Membership End Date.
20. Enter your Payment Method Details (optional).
21. Enter your Billing Address (optional).
22. Enter your Billing City (optional).
23. Enter your Billing State (optional).
24. Enter your Billing Zip (optional).
25. Enter your Billing Country (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, **required**): Enter a strong password between 8-12 characters.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm if you would like a premium membership plan.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your payment method.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your card number.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your expiration date.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your start date.
* **Plan Price** (`plan_price`, `number`, **required**): Enter your plan price.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing_cycle`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your billing cycle.
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing_frequency`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your billing frequency.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your payment terms.
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, `note`, **optional**): Enter your onboarding details.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Confirm your terms and conditions.
* **Plan Start Date** (`plan_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your plan start date.
* **Billing Date** (`billing_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your billing date.
* **Billing Status** (`billing_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your billing status.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm your membership status.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your membership end date.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your payment method details.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing address.
* **Billing City** (`billing_city`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing city.
* **Billing State** (`billing_state`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing state.
* **Billing Zip** (`billing_zip`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing zip.
* **Billing Country** (`billing_country`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your billing country.
