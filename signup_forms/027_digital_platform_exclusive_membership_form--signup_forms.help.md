# Digital Platform Exclusive Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to onboard new members to the platform and collect relevant information for their management and review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in all required fields (marked as "Required" in each field's description).
2.  Select the correct platform type from the options in the "Platform Type" field.
3.  Fill in your correct email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields respectively.
4.  Confirm your email and phone number in the "Confirm Email" and "Confirm Phone" fields.
5.  Enter your member since information, if applicable, in the "Member Since" field.
6.  Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
7.  Select the correct review status and onboarding status for your membership in the "Review Status" and "Onboarding Status" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information** (`member_info`, text, required): This field is used to enter your name and other basic information.
* **Platform Type** (`platform_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of platform you represent (Website, Mobile App, or Other).
* **Platform URL** (`platform_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your platform.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_one, optional): Select your membership level (Free, Premium, or VIP).
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, optional): Enter the date you became a member.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the email you entered is correct.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, select_one, optional): Confirm that the phone number you entered is correct.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your membership.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to be reviewed.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your review (In Progress, Approved, or Rejected).
* **Onboarding Status** (`onboarding_status`, select_one, optional): Select the onboarding status of your membership (Onboarding, Not Onboarding, or Pending).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* Enter the correct review status and onboarding status for your membership.
