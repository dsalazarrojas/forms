# Join The Wealth Building Group - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Join The Wealth Building Group form is a one-page form designed to gather essential information from individuals who are interested in achieving financial independence and becoming part of the Wealth Building Group program. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide your personal and professional details, which will help the program's administrators tailor their support to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have not previously signed up for the Wealth Building Group program, as this form will only be displayed to new sign-ups.
2. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated text fields.
3. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
4. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Describe your Goal in the Goal field, being as specific and concise as possible.
6. Select your consent by choosing either "Yes" or "No" from the Consent field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be referred to by our team.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be referred to by our team.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
- **Goal** (`Goal`, text, required): Describe your financial goal, being as specific and concise as possible. This will help us understand how to support you best.
- **Consent** (`Consent`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you agree to receive updates and support from the Wealth Building Group, or "No" if you do not.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, as they are marked with an asterisk (*) in the form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact our support team.
* Once you submit the form, you will be contacted by our team to discuss your goals and provide further support.
