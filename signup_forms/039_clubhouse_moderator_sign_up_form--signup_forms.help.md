# Clubhouse Moderator Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for signing up as a moderator for a Clubhouse session. It collects information about your qualifications, preferences, and commitments as a moderator.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed the session details and are familiar with the expected responsibilities of a moderator.
2. Fill in your full name, email address, phone number, and Clubhouse username.
3. Provide the date and time you plan to conduct the session.
4. Select your area of expertise and experience level as a moderator.
5. Indicate your commitment level and how involved you will be during the session.
6. Optionally, provide any special requests or requirements you may have.
7. Confirm your acceptance of moderator responsibilities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Full Name (Moderator Name, Required)**: Enter your complete name as you would like it to appear on session materials.
  - This will be used to address you as a moderator and in session communications.
- **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for session-related communications.
  - This will be used for session updates and notifications.
- **Phone Number (Optional)**: Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
  - This will be used for last-minute session updates or coordination.
- **Clubhouse Username (Required)**: Enter your Clubhouse username as it appears on our platform.
  - This will be used to identify you as a moderator within the Clubhouse platform.
- **Sign-Up Date (Required)**: Choose the date you plan to conduct the session.
  - This will be used to schedule session reminders and communications.
- **Session Date (Required)**: Choose the date you plan to conduct the session.
  - This will be used to schedule session reminders and communications.
- **Session Start Time (Required)**: Choose the time you plan to start the session.
  - This will be used to schedule session reminders and communications.
- **Expected Number of Attendees (Optional)**: Indicate the expected number of attendees for your session.
  - This will be used to plan session logistics and materials.
- **Moderation Experience Level (Required)**: Select your level of moderation experience.
  - This will help us understand your qualifications and assign you to suitable sessions.
- **How Will You Contribute to the Session (Required)**: Select all the ways you will contribute to the session.
  - This will help us understand your role and responsibilities as a moderator.
- **Your Area of Expertise (Required)**: Describe your area of expertise and specialization.
  - This will help us understand your qualifications and assign you to suitable sessions.
- **Commitment Level (Required)**: Select your commitment level to the session.
  - This will help us understand your availability and involvement.
- **Special Requests or Requirements (Optional)**: Describe any specific needs or requirements you may have.
  - This will help us accommodate your needs and provide a smooth session experience.
- **I Accept Moderator Responsibilities (Required)**: Confirm your acceptance of moderator responsibilities.
  - This is a critical step to ensure you understand and will uphold session guidelines and expectations.
