# Mini Subscribe Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mini Subscribe Form is a simple form designed to collect user's name and email address for subscription purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
3. Confirm your email address in the "confirm_email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, text, required/optional): Confirm your email address by re-entering it.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "email_address" field.
- Double-check that you entered your email address correctly in the "confirm_email" field.
