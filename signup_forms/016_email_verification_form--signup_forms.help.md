# email_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify a user's email address for account setup purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and country of residence.
2. Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
3. Confirm your phone number if you have provided it.
4. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for account creation and future communication.
* **Confirm email** (Confirm email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address again to confirm accuracy.
* **Phone number** (Phone number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you have one. This will be used for account verification purposes.
* **Country** (Country, select_one, required/optional): Select your country of residence from the list of options.
* **Confirm phone number** (Confirm phone number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number again to confirm accuracy. This field is only required if you have provided a phone number.
* **Verify email** (Verify email, note, required/optional): This field is for administrative purposes only and is not a required field.
 

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy before confirming it.
* If you have provided a phone number, double-check it for accuracy before confirming it.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, contact our support team for assistance.
