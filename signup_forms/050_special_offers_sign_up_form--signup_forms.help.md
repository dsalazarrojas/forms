# Special Offers Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to provide them with special offers and promotions. Please fill in the required fields to register for our special offers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. If you want to be contacted via phone, fill in your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If you're interested in a specific offer, select it from the "special offer" field.
6. Choose which newsletter you'd like to subscribe to from the "newsletter" field.
7. Confirm your email address in the "confirm email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **last name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.
* **special offer** (text, optional): Select the special offer you're interested in. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **sign up** (select multiple, optional): Choose the special offers you're interested in receiving.
* **newsletter** (select one, optional): Select the newsletter you'd like to subscribe to.
* **confirm email** (email, optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm it's correct.
