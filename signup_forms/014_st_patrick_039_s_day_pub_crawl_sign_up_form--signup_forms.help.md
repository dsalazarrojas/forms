# St. Patricks Day Pub Crawl Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The St. Patricks Day Pub Crawl Sign Up Form is designed to gather information from participants in the event. This form requires accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the first page of the form, which includes your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. On the second page, select your T Shirt Size, Pub Crawl, T Shirt Design, T Shirt Color, and T Shirt Sleeve Length.
3. On the third page, indicate your Pub Crawl Experience, Shirt Size Fits, and select the Emergency Contact information, including Name, Phone, Email, Relationship, and Address.
4. Finally, indicate the T Shirt Quantity and provide any additional Emergency Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **T Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select your T-shirt size: XS, Small, Medium, Large, XL, XXL, or None.
* **Pub Crawls** (`pub_crawls`, select_multiple, required): Select the Pub Crawl(s) you will be attending: Crawl 1, Crawl 2, Crawl 3.
* **T Shirt Design** (`t_shirt_design`, select_one, required): Select your preferred T-shirt design: Design 1, Design 2, Design 3, Design 4.
* **T Shirt Color** (`t_shirt_color`, select_one, required): Select your preferred T-shirt color: Red, Green, Blue, Yellow, None.
* **T Shirt Sleeve Length** (`t_shirt_sleeve_length`, select_one, required): Select your preferred T-shirt sleeve length: Short, Long, None.
* **Pub Crawl Experience** (`pub_crawl_experience`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have experience in pub crawls: True, False.
* **Shirt Size Fits** (`shirt_size_fits`, select_one, required): Select how your shirt size fits: Tight, Loose, Perfect.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, text, required): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Select your relationship type with your emergency contact: Relative, Friend, Other.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional emergency notes.
* **T Shirt Quantity** (`t_shirt_quantity`, number, required): Select the quantity of T-shirts you need: .
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship Type** (`emergency_relationship_type`, select_one, required): Select your emergency contact's relationship type: Relative, Friend, Other.
* **Relationship Status** (`emergency_relationship_status`, select_one, required): Select your emergency contact's relationship status: Single, Married, In a relationship, It's complicated, Other.
* **Emergency Note** (`emergency_note`, text, required): Enter any additional emergency notes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth (hh:mmam).
