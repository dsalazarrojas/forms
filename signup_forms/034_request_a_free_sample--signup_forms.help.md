# Request A Free Sample! - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your contact information and preferences to receive free samples.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in your first name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Fill in your last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. **Enter your email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further correspondence.
4. **Enter your phone number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
5. **Write a note**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
6. **Select a date**: Choose the date you'd like to receive the sample on (if applicable).
7. **Enter your address**: Enter your address for shipping purposes.
8. **Enter your street**: Enter the street address for shipping purposes.
9. **Enter your city**: Enter the city for shipping purposes.
10. **Enter your state**: Enter the state for shipping purposes.
11. **Enter your zip code**: Enter the zip code for shipping purposes.
12. **Enter your zip code (alternative)**: This field is intended for users who have a zip + 4 or zip + 2 code. Please enter the alternative zip code.
13. **Enter your country**: Enter your country for international shipping purposes.
14. **Enter your phone number 2 (optional)**: If you have a second phone number, please enter it here.
