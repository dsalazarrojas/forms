# Beta Product Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Beta Product Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential information from potential beta testers about their background, interest in the beta product, and technical details to help us better understand your qualifications and suitability for our beta program. Your responses will help us tailor the product development process to your needs and preferences. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, and don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Describe your occupation and industry.
3. Choose the beta program you are interested in.
4. Explain why you are interested in the beta program.
5. Indicate how you heard about us.
6. Share your experience with similar products.
7. Specify the primary device you will use for testing.
8. Enter the operating system version you are using.
9. Rate your tech-savviness level.
10. Approximate the number of hours you can dedicate to testing each week.
11. Confirm that you are willing to provide detailed bug reports, including logs and screenshots.
12. Agree to the Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
13. Add any questions or comments you have for the beta team.
14. Finally, confirm the date and time you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (`inquiry_user_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (`inquiry_user_email`): Enter your professional email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Your Occupation** (`inquiry_user_occupation`): Describe your job title, position, or area of expertise.
* **Your Industry** (`inquiry_user_industry`): Select the industry that best describes your work or field of expertise.
* **Beta Program Interest** (`program_interest_reason`): Explain why you are interested in participating in the beta program.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source_select`): Indicate how you first learned about our company or product.
* **Similar Product Experience** (`similar_product_experience`): Share any experience you have with similar products or beta testing.
* **Primary Device for Testing** (`testing_device_choice`): Choose the device you will primarily use for testing our product.
* **Operating System Version** (`os_version_input`): Enter the version of the operating system you are using.
* **Tech-Savviness Level** (`tech_savviness_rating`): Rate your technical expertise level, from 1 (low) to 5 (expert).
* **Hours for Testing** (`testing_hours_weekly`): Approximate the number of hours you can dedicate to testing each week.
* **Detailed Bug Reports** (`bug_report_willingness`): Confirm that you are willing to provide detailed bug reports.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`nda_agreement_status`): Agree to keep the product confidential.
* **Questions for Beta Team** (`beta_team_questions`): Add any questions or comments for the beta team.
* **Date of Inquiry** (`inquiry_date_stamp`): Confirm the date and time you submitted the form.
