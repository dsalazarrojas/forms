# Watagans Abseiling Camp 25 26th July 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from attendees to register for the abseiling camp event on 25-26 July.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth check-in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Church Activity** (church_activity, text, optional): Please describe any previous church-related activities you have participated in or would like to participate in for this event.
* **Camping Details** (camping_details, text, optional): Share any relevant camping experience or information about your experience and needs for this event.
* **Name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for camp communication.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address for camp communication and updates.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Provide a phone number where the camp organizers can reach you for any reason.

## Tips
* Please double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Make sure to enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for camp activities.
* If you need any special assistance, mention it in the "Camping Details" field.
* This form is for event registration and is not a public form, so be mindful of sharing your personal info.
