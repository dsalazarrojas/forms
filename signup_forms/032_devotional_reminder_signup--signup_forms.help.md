## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name, Text, Optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (last_name, Text, Optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (email, Email, Optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Preferred Language** (preferred_language, Select One, Optional): Select your preferred language for the reminders.
* **Preferred Email** (preferred_email, Email, Optional): Enter a preferred email address for receiving reminders.
* **Schedule** (schedule, Date, Optional): Select a date for the reminder.
* **Schedule Time** (schedule_time, Time, Optional): Select a time for the reminder.
* **Reminder Type** (reminder_type, Select One, Optional): Select the type of reminder you want, such as "Devotional", "Sermon", "Blog Post", or "Podcast".
* **Reminder Frequency** (reminder_frequency, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how often you want to receive reminders, options are "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly" or "Never".
* **Timezone** (timezone, Select One, Optional): Select your time zone to adjust the reminder time accordingly.
* **Timezone Offset** (timezone_offset, Select One, Optional): Select your time zone offset to further adjust the reminder time.
* **Note** (note, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes for the reminder.
* **Preferences** (preferences, Note, Optional): Enter any preferences for the reminder.
* **Terms and Conditions** (terms_and_conditions, Note, Optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service.
