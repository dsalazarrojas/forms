# Cardio Drumming Class Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential participants of a cardio drumming class, ensuring that the instructor can tailor the experience to their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to receive updates and notifications about the class.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** so we can contact you with class schedule updates.
4. Select your **Age Group**, which will help us determine the class level best suited for you.
5. Choose your **Fitness Level** from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced, as this will affect the class pace and intensity.
6. Select your **Session Type** from Drop-in Class, 4-Week Package, 8-Week Package, 12-Week Package, or Unlimited Monthly.
7. Choose your preferred **Class Date** and **Class Time** for the class session.
8. Enter your **Class Fee** as per the pricing and payment details provided.
9. If you have any medical conditions, please list them in the **Medical Conditions** field.
10. Indicate if you have any **Current Injuries or Limitations** that may impact your participation.
11. If you have any medications affecting exercise, select **Yes** or **No**.
12. Confirm that you have doctor clearance for aerobic exercise.
13. Select **Yes** or **No** if you have read and agree to the waiver and consent agreement.
14. Finally, choose your **Drumming Experience** level and select your motivations for taking the class.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address where you can be reached for class updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your active phone number where we can contact you for class schedule updates.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided.
* **Fitness Level**: Choose your level of fitness from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Session Type**: Select the session type that suits your needs and schedule.
* **Class Date**: Choose your preferred class date from the provided options.
* **Class Time**: Choose your preferred class time from the options available.
* **Class Fee**: Enter the class fee as per the pricing and payment details provided.
* **Drumming Experience**: Select your level of drumming experience from No Experience, Some Background, or Experienced Drummer.
* **Motivation**: Choose your motivation for taking the class from Fitness and Cardio, Fun and Music, Stress Relief, Community, or Rhythm Coordination and Weight Management.

## Tips

* Be honest when answering the medical conditions and medical history questions, as this will help us provide a safe environment for your participation.
* If you have any injuries or limitations, please inform us so we can provide accommodations and adjustments as needed.
* Please review the waiver and consent agreement carefully before selecting **Yes** or **No**.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

This help guide is designed to be comprehensive and easy to follow, ensuring that participants can complete the form accurately and efficiently.
