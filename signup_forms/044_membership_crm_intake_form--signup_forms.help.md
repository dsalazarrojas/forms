# Membership CRM Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Membership CRM Intake Form is a critical tool used to gather necessary details about new members, ensuring a smooth onboarding process and accurate database entry. This comprehensive form helps administrators quickly and easily capture the required information, which is essential for various administrative tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing **New Member Information** on Page 1. This section is optional, but it's recommended to be completed by the member or their representative.
2. Move to **Contact Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** fields, where you can enter or update the member's contact details.
3. Under **Membership Type**, select the correct category for the new member (Individual or Organization).
4. Enter the **Join Date** in the format MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD to record the date the member joined.
5. For **Join Time**, use 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Enter the number of years the member has been a member in the **Member Since** field.
7. In the **Member Status** section, choose one or multiple statuses that apply to the new member (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
8. Provide **Address 1** details to ensure accurate address records.
9. Enter the city name.
10. Select the state from the available options.
11. Enter the zip code of the member's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Member Information**: This field is optional and serves as an introduction or a personal note about the new member. It helps in capturing any specific details or remarks that are not covered by other fields.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to be contacted in case of any updates or issues.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person. This could be the member or their representative.
* **Phone**: Provide the contact phone number of the person or organization.
* **Membership Type**: Choose between Individual or Organization to classify the new member.
* **Join Date**: Enter the date the member joined in MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Join Time**: Record the time of the member's join in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Member Since**: Enter the number of years the member has been a member of the organization.
* **Member Status**: Choose all the statuses that apply to the new member (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Address 1**: Enter the detailed address of the new member for accurate address records.
* **City**: Provide the city name where the member's address is located.
* **State**: Select the state from the available options for accurate location mapping.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the member's address.

## Tips
- Ensure that each field is completed accurately to prevent duplication and ensure accurate records.
- If a member is already registered, do not submit a duplicate entry.
- Review the form carefully before submission to guarantee that all necessary details are captured.
- In case of any discrepancies, contact the contact person or organization for clarification.
