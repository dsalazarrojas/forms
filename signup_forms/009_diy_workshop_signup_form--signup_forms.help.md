# DIY Workshop Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about DIY workshops, including details about the workshop itself, participants, and instructors. Fill out this form to register your workshop and manage its associated data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Workshop Details** section, which includes general information about the workshop.
2. Move on to the **Participant Details** section, where you can input participant information.
3. Select your preferred **Registration Date** for the workshop.
4. Enter **Studio Details**, if necessary.
5. Choose your **Instructor** (or select multiple).
6. Select the **Instructors** who will lead the workshop (if multiple).
7. Choose your **Workshop Topics** (or select multiple).
8. Select the **Participant Topics** that will be covered in the workshop (if multiple).
9. Select the **Participants** who will attend the workshop (if multiple).
10. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the workshop.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, optional): Enter participant information.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you want to schedule the workshop.
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, `text`, optional): Enter studio location information.
* **Instructor** (`instructors`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the instructor leading the workshop.
* **Instructors** (`participant_instructors`, `select_one`, optional): Select the instructor leading the workshop.
* **Instructors** (`participant_instructors`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple instructors leading the workshop.
* **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, `text`, optional): Enter workshop topic information.
* **Workshop Topics Multiple** (`workshop_topics_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple workshop topics.
* **Participant Topics** (`participant_topics`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select participant topics for the workshop.
* **Participant Topics Multiple** (`participant_topics_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple participant topics.
* **Participants** (`participants`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select participants attending the workshop.
* **Participants Multiple** (`participants_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple participants attending the workshop.

## Tips
