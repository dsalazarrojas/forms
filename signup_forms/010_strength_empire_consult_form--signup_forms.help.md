# Strength Empire Consult Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users who seek guidance or advice on achieving a personal goal. Users can fill out the form to provide their contact information, preferences, and details about their goals, in order to receive a consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Specify your Goal for the consultation (e.g., Health, Wealth, Relationships, Career, or Personal Growth).
5. Choose your Preferred Time for the consultation (though this field is currently not implemented).
6. Enter the Start Date and End Date for the consultation.
7. Write any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
8. Confirm your notes in the Confirm Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the first name of the person filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This is the last name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication.
* **Goal** (`goal`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your goal for the consultation. Options: Health, Wealth, Relationships, Career, Personal Growth.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, `time`, required: false): This field is currently not implemented, please disregard.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the consultation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the consultation.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Write any additional notes or comments for the consultation.
* **Confirm Note** (`confirm_note`, `note`, required: false): Confirm your notes for the consultation.
