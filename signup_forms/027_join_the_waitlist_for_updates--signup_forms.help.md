# Join The Waitlist For Updates - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Join The Waitlist For Updates" form is designed to allow users to sign up for updates on specific topics, such as new product releases, company news, and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
4. Select your preferences from the "Preferences" dropdown menu.
5. Select all applicable consents from the "Consent" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any additional information to share, enter it in the "Notes" field (optional).
7. Finally, click "Form Submit" to submit your information.

* **Waitlist Form (1)** (`waitlist_form`, text, optional): This is the main field where you can enter your interest in joining the waitlist.
### Name (2)
* **Name (2)** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in future communications.
### Email (3)
* **Email (3)** (`email`, text, optional): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly to receive updates.
### Phone (4)
* **Phone (4)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone for updates (not required).
