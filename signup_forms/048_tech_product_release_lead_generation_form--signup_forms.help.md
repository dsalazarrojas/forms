# tech_product_release_lead_generation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tech product release lead generation form is a tool used to collect information from job seekers interested in tech-related positions. It helps us understand the job seeker's profile and preferences, enabling us to provide tailored support and guidance throughout the recruitment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information and job search preferences.
2. Ensure you meet the requirements mentioned above to be considered for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (2, Email, Required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Job Title** (3, Text, Optional): If you'd like to specify your current job title, please enter it here. This will help us understand your background and experience.
* **Company Name** (4, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company you're currently working with or the one you're interested in joining.
* **Job Function** (5, Select One, Optional): Choose the job function that best describes your role or the one you're interested in.
* **Location** (6, Text, Optional): Enter your location or the one you'd like to work in.
* **Job Level** (7, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the level of job you're looking for, such as Entry Level, Mid Level, or Senior.
* **Company Size** (8, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the company size that best fits your needs, ranging from 1-10 employees to 1001+.
* **Industry** (9, Select One, Optional): Select the industry you're interested in or have experience in.
* **Job Type** (10, Select One, Optional): Choose whether you're looking for a Full-time, Part-time, or Contract job.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (11, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your preferred contact method, such as Email or Phone.
* **Job Status** (12, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the status of the job you're looking for, such as Open or Closed.
* **Job Posting ID** (13, Number, Required): Enter the job posting ID provided by the company or platform.
* **Job Opened** (14, Date, Required): Enter the date the job was opened.
* **Job Closing** (15, Date, Required): Enter the date the job is scheduled to close.
* **Lead Source** (16, Select One, Required): Choose the source where you found the job posting, such as Social Media or Referral.
* **Company Website** (17, Text, Optional): Enter the company's website for more information.
* **LinkedIn** (18, Select One, Optional): Check whether you have a LinkedIn profile.
* **GitHub** (19, Select One, Optional): Check whether you have a GitHub profile.
* **Twitter** (20, Select One, Optional): Check whether you have a Twitter profile.
* **Company Size 2** (21, Select One, Optional): Choose the approximate company size for better matching.
* **Job Posting ID 2** (22, Text, Optional): Enter an additional job posting ID if needed.
* **Lead Status** (23, Select One, Required): Choose the current status of the lead.
* **Source** (24, Text, Optional): Enter the source where you found the job posting.
* **Follow-up** (25, Note, Optional): If you have any further comments or questions, please include them here.
