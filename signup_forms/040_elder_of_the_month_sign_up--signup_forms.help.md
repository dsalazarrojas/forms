# Elder Of The Month Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Elder Of The Month Sign Up" form is designed to collect information for a recognition program that aims to honor a nominee who has demonstrated exceptional qualities, skills, or characteristics. The form is used to submit nominations for individuals who deserve recognition and appreciation within the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Nominee Name field with the name of the individual you are nominating.
2. Enter the Nominee E-mail field with the nominee's email address, if available.
3. In the Nominee Story field, describe the reasons why you are nominating this individual, highlighting their achievements, skills, or qualities that make them deserving of the recognition.
4. In the Review Candidate field, select 'Yes' if you would like to review the nominee's eligibility before submitting their nomination, or 'No' if you are confident in their qualifications.
5. Finally, select the Submit button to finalize your nomination. If you want to submit the form without reviewing the nominee, you can select 'Other' as a backup option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Nominee Name** (`Nominee Name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of the individual you are nominating. This field is not required, but it is essential to provide a clear and accurate name for the nominee.

### **Nominee E-mail** (`Nominee E-mail`, `email`, optional)
Enter the email address of the nominee, if available. This field is not required but can be helpful for communication purposes.

### **Nominee Story** (`Nominee Story`, `text`, optional)
In this field, describe the reasons why you are nominating this individual. Be as detailed as possible, highlighting their achievements, skills, or qualities that make them deserving of the recognition.

### **Review Candidate** (`Review Candidate`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select 'Yes' if you would like to review the nominee's eligibility before submitting their nomination, or 'No' if you are confident in their qualifications. This option is optional, but it's crucial to ensure the nomination process is thorough and accurate.

### **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the 'Submit' button to finalize your nomination. If you're unsure about the nomination process, you can select 'Other' as a backup option.
