# Lunch Trade Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lunch Trade Participation Form is used to collect information from participants about their lunch trade participation preferences and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Participant Name** in the Participant Name field.
2. Select your **Preferred Meal** by choosing one of the options provided.
3. Enter your **Available Date** in the **Available Date** field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Enter your **Available Time** in the **Available Time** field in the format `HH:MM (24-hour clock)`.
5. Enter your **Preferred Time** in the **Preferred Time** field in the format `HH:MM (24-hour clock)`.
6. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
8. In the **Participant Preferences** field, provide any additional notes or preferences you have regarding your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Preferred Meal**: Choose your preferred meal option. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Available Date**: Enter the date(s) you are available to participate in lunch trades. Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Available Time**: Enter the time(s) you are available to participate in lunch trades. Use the format `HH:MM (24-hour clock)`.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter the time(s) you prefer to participate in lunch trades. Use the format `HH:MM (24-hour clock)`.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Preferences**: This field is for any additional notes or preferences you have regarding your participation.
