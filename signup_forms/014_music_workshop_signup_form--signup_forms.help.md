# Music Workshop Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Workshop Signup Form is used to collect information from participants who are interested in attending a music workshop. This form helps the organizers to gather essential details about the participants, which is crucial for planning and executing the workshop smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Music Workshop Signup Form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session option that best suits your needs.
2. Enter your participant name.
3. Enter your participant email.
4. Enter your participant phone number.
5. Write any additional notes about yourself as a participant.
6. If applicable, enter the session type, date, and time.
7. Write any further notes about the session.
8. Optionally, you can add participant details in the participant details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session** (`session`, select_one, required): Select the session you are interested in. This is a one-time selection.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, text, required): Enter your email address to be contacted by the organizers.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Participant note** (`participant_note`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant. This can be used to share more information with the organizers.
* **Session type** (`session_type`, text, optional): If you know which session type you want to attend, enter it here. This will help the organizers to prepare accordingly.
* **Session date** (`session_date`, text, optional): If you know the session date you want to attend, enter it here. This will help the organizers to prepare the schedule.
* **Session time** (`session_time`, text, optional): If you know the session time you want to attend, enter it here. This will help the organizers to plan the logistics.
* **Session note** (`session_note`, text, optional): Add any further notes about the session. This can be used by the organizers to tailor the session according to your needs.
* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, note, optional): Add any additional participant details. This can be used by the organizers to prepare the session.
* **Form submission id** (`form_submission_id`, number, required): This is a unique identifier for the form submission. You don't need to fill this field.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and honestly.
* You can add any additional notes or details that you think will help the organizers to prepare the session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organizers directly.
