# embedded_opt_in_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for user account setup and email subscription management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested. Please make sure to provide accurate and correct information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address here. This will be used for communication and subscription management.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm it's correct.
* **Confirm First Name** (`confirm_first_name`, text, optional): Re-enter your first name to confirm it's correct.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number here. This is not required for account setup, but it's useful for communication.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number to confirm it's correct.
* **Email Subscription** (`email_subscription`, number, optional): Please select your email subscription preference:
	+ 1: Yes, subscribe to emails
	+ 2: No, do not subscribe to emails
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please select your preferred time slot for email delivery:
	+ 9:00 AM
	+ 12:00 PM
	+ 3:00 PM
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Please enter any additional note or message here.
* **Confirm Note** (`confirm_note`, note, optional): Re-enter your note to confirm it's correct.
