# cruise_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from passengers registering for a cruise. This information will help us verify the identity of each passenger and ensure a smooth check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each field**:
   - Ensure that all required fields are completed.
   - For optional fields, provide any relevant information if you wish to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Details**
   *   *Passenger Name* (`_passenger_name_`, text, required): Enter the name(s) of the passenger(s) registering for the cruise.
* **Passport Details**
   *   *Passport Number* (`_passport_number_`, text, required): Enter the passport number for each passenger.
* **Contact Details**
   *   *Contact Name* (`_contact_name_`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization handling the passenger's contact information.
   *   *Phone Number* (`_phone_number_`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
   *   *Email Address* (`_email_address_`, text, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   If any passenger has a different passport number than their name, please provide an explanation in the `contact_details` field.
*   Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form.
