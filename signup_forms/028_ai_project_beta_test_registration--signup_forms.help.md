# AI Project Beta Test Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Project Beta Test Registration form is designed to gather information from interested parties who want to test an AI project in a beta environment. This form will help the project team assess potential participants' qualifications and suitability for the beta test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **full name** (required). This is the name that will be displayed for other participants and project team members.
2. Enter your **company or organization** (optional, but if you are participating on behalf of a company, provide the name of the company).
3. Provide your **email address** (required). This will be used for invitations and communication purposes.
4. Select your **role at organization** (required). This will help us understand your qualifications and suitability for the beta test.
5. Briefly describe your **intended use case** (required). This will help us understand how you plan to use the project.
6. Select your **technical readiness to integrate beta** (required). This will help us assess your ability to integrate the project into your system.
7. Specify your **availability window for testing** (optional, but if you have specific dates or timeframe in mind, please provide them).
8. Select whether an **NDA is required for participation** (required). This will help us understand any potential restrictions on your behalf.
9. If applicable, add any **additional requirements or notes** (optional). This will provide us with any further information we might need to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`): This field requires your first and last name.
* **Company or organization** (`company`): If you are participating on behalf of a company, enter the name of the company. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email address** (`email`): Use your personal or work email address. This will be used for invitations and communication purposes.
* **Role at organization** (`role`): Choose a suitable role that reflects your position or qualifications within the organization.
* **Description of intended use case** (`use_case_description`): Briefly describe how you plan to use the project.
* **Technical readiness to integrate beta** (`technical_readiness`): Select one of the options that best reflects your ability to integrate the project.
* **Availability window for testing** (`availability_window`): If you have specific dates or a timeframe in mind, enter them.
* **NDA required for participation** (`nda_required`): Select whether an NDA is required for your participation in the beta test.
* **Additional requirements or notes** (`additional_requirements`): If you have any further information to share, please provide it here.
