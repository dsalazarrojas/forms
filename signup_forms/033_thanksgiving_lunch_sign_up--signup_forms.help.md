## Purpose
The "Thanksgiving_Lunch_Sign_Up" form is designed to gather essential information for organizing a successful and enjoyable Thanksgiving lunch event. By filling out this form, attendees can provide details about their attendance, meal preferences, and any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the text field provided.
2. Specify the **Guest Count**, which is the number of people attending with you.
3. Choose the **Date** on which you would like to attend the event.
4. Select the **Time** of the event you would like to attend.
5. Check if you would like to have a **Meal Type**, which can be either "Yes" or "No".
6. Indicate your preference for the **Main Course**, which can be either "Yes" or "No".
7. Select the **Side Course** options you would like to have, which can be either "Yes" or "No".
8. Enter any **Dessert** preferences in the text field.
9. Provide any **Special Requests** in the text field.
10. Enter your **Contact Phone** number.
11. Provide your **Email** address.
12. Leave any additional comments or notes in the **Comment** field.
13. Finally, select your **Meal Preference**, which can be "Meat", "Veggie", or "Vegan".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required/optional): Specify the number of people attending with you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date on which you would like to attend the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the event you would like to attend.
* **Meal Type** (`meal_type`, select_one, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you would like to have a meal.
* **Main Course** (`main_course`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the main courses you would like to have, which can be either "Yes" or "No".
* **Side Course** (`side_course`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the side courses you would like to have, which can be either "Yes" or "No".
* **Dessert** (`dessert`, text, required/optional): Enter any dessert preferences.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Provide any special requests.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, required/optional): Leave any additional comments or notes.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Select your meal preference, which can be "Meat", "Veggie", or "Vegan".
