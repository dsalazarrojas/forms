# Unsubscribe Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to process a user's request to unsubscribe from a mailing list. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to help us understand why you want to unsubscribe.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is the address you used when you signed up for the list.
2. In the "confirm_email" field, enter the same email address to confirm your identity.
3. In the "reason" field, select the reason why you want to unsubscribe from one of the provided options or choose "Other" to provide a custom reason.
4. If you selected "Other" in the "reason" field, provide a brief description of your reason in the "other_reason" field.
5. In the "unsubscribe" field, confirm your intention to unsubscribe by selecting "Yes, remove me" from the dropdown list.
6. Finally, select the type of account removal you prefer in the "remove_account" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a short title to identify yourself.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your reason for unsubscribing.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to verify your identity.
* **Reason** (`reason`, select multiple, optional): Select the reason for unsubscribing from one of the provided options or choose "Other" to provide a custom reason.
* **Additional Reason** (`other_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "reason" field, provide a brief description of your reason.
* **Confirm Unsubscribe** (`confirm_unsubscribe`, select one, optional): Confirm your intention to unsubscribe by selecting "Yes, remove me" from the dropdown list.
* **Account Removal** (`remove_account`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of account removal you prefer.
