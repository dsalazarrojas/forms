## Academic Impact Study Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of an academic study on the effects and outcomes of various interventions or actions. It collects your informed consent to participate in the study, ensuring you understand the purpose, potential risks, and benefits involved.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, carefully read this guide to understand each field's purpose and requirements.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Ensure you understand the study's purpose, risks, and benefits before providing your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (Required): Type your full legal name as per the field.
* **Contact email** (Required): Enter your email address for study communications and updates.
* **Study description** (Required): Briefly describe the purpose of the study in your own words.
* **Risks and benefits** (Required): Summarize the potential risks and benefits of participating in the study.
* **How data will be used** (Required): Explain how your data will be analyzed, reported, and shared as part of the study.
* **Consent to participate** (Required): Select "I consent" to indicate your agreement to participate in the study.
* **Withdrawal rights** (Optional): You can choose to withdraw from the study if you no longer wish to participate.
* **Signature confirmation** (Required): Type your full name as a signature confirmation.
* **Date of consent** (Required): Enter the date when you gave your consent.

## Tips
- Ensure you understand the study's purpose and procedures before providing consent.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the study team for clarification.
- Keep a record of your consent date for your personal reference.
- Contact form@gic.mx for any further assistance or questions.
