# educational-material-translation-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational material translation consent form is used to gather consent and information from participants and translators for the translation of educational materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information on page 2.
2. Indicate your consent for translation on page 3.
3. Enter your translator information on page 4 (if applicable).
4. Confirm your translation consent on page 5.
5. Enter the translation date on page 6.
6. Enter the translation time (if applicable) on page 7.
7. Add any additional notes on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (text, required): Fill in your participant information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Participant Translation Consent** (select multiple, required): Select all the translations you consent to. You can choose 'Yes' for each translation you are comfortable with.
* **Translator Information** (text, optional): If you are a translator, enter your information here. This field is not required but is useful for tracking and record-keeping.
* **Translator Translation Consent** (select one, required): Confirm that you are the translator and that you agree to the terms of the translation process. Choose 'Yes' to indicate your consent.
* **Translation Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the translation.
* **Translation Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the translation, if applicable.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the translation process.
