# Personalized Health Plan Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of patients for personalized health plans. It collects patient information, contact details, and consent for various health services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the "Patient Information" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Select the health services you are consenting to in the "Health Plan" field.
4. Check the box next to "Services" to indicate the health services you are consenting to.
5. Select your preferred date and time for the consent in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Enter the name of your healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider" field.
10. Enter the name of the healthcare organization in the "Healthcare Organization" field.
11. Enter the name of the healthcare service in the "Healthcare Service" field.
12. Sign the form with your signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
13. Sign the form with the healthcare provider's signature in the "Healthcare Provider Signature" field.
14. Enter the date signed in the "Date Signed" field.
15. Enter the name of the healthcare provider in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.
16. Enter the name of the healthcare organization in the "Healthcare Organization Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and phone number as the healthcare provider or representative.
* **Health Plan** (`health_plan`, text, required): Select the health plan that applies to you.
* **Services** (`services`, select_one, required): Check the box next to the health services you are consenting to.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select your preferred option for consent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the consent.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about the consent.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Organization** (`healthcare_organization`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare organization.
* **Healthcare Service** (`healthcare_service`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare service.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`healthcare_provider_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with the healthcare provider's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date signed.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Organization Name** (`healthcare_organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the healthcare organization.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required", this means that you are not able to leave these fields empty, and they are necessary for your consent form.
