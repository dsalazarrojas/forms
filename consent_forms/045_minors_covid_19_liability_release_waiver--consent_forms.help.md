# minors_covid_19_liability_release_waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about a minor's COVID-19 vaccination status and related contact information. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the minor's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the minor's COVID-19 vaccination status and the number of doses they have received.
2. Provide the required contact information for the minor's parents or guardians, including their names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
3. Enter any relevant medical information, such as any medical conditions or allergies the minor may have.
4. Answer the question about whether the minor has received the COVID-19 vaccine.
5. Enter the number of doses of the COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
6. Enter the type of COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
7. Enter the dosing information for the COVID-19 vaccine, including the number of doses administered and the dosage amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **minor_covid_19_liability** (1, text, required): This field is for the minor's COVID-19 vaccination status.
* **consent** (2, text, required): This field requires the parent or guardian's consent for the minor's vaccination information.
* **contact_information** (3, select_multiple, required): Select the contact information for the minor's parents or guardians, including their names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
* **medical_conditions** (4, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions or allergies the minor may have.
* **insurance** (5, text, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information for the minor.
* **vaccine_info** (6, text, required): Enter any relevant information about the minor's COVID-19 vaccination, such as any reactions or side effects.
* **emergency_contact** (7, text, optional): Enter any emergency contact information for the minor.
* **emergency_contact_info** (8, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **medical_info** (9, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical information for the minor.
* **insurance_info** (10, text, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information for the minor.
* **contact_info** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional contact information for the minor, such as their parent's name, email address, and phone number.
* **minor_covid_status** (12, select_one, optional): Select whether the minor has received the COVID-19 vaccine, is unknown, or has not received it.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (13, text, optional): Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the minor.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (14, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_emergency_contact_name** (15, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **vaccine_side_effects** (16, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about any side effects from the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **emergency_contact_address** (17, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_relationship_name** (18, text, optional): Enter the name of the relationship between the emergency contact and the minor.
* **minor_covid_19_vaccine** (19, text, required): Enter the name of the COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
* **minor_covid_19_vaccine_dose** (20, text, required): Enter the number of doses of the COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
* **vaccine_info_2** (21, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the minor's COVID-19 vaccination.
* **minor_covid_vaccine_doses** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the minor has received multiple doses of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **minor_covid_19_dose_number** (23, text, required): Enter the number of doses of the COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
* **minor_covid_19_vaccine_type** (24, text, required): Enter the type of COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
* **minor_covid_19_dosage** (25, number, required): Enter the dosage amount of the COVID-19 vaccine the minor has received.
