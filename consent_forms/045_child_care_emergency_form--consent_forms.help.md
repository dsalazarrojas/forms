# Child Care Emergency Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Care Emergency Form is a crucial document that provides essential information in case of a child's medical emergency. This form is completed by the parent or guardian to inform care providers about the child's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the child's basic information: Enter the child's name, date of birth, and form completion date.
2. Provide contact information: Enter your name, phone number, and email address (if provided).
3. List medical contacts: Enter the names, relationships, and contact information for up to two emergency contacts.
4. Medical information: List any known medical conditions, current medications, and allergies the child may have.
5. Emergency procedures: Describe any necessary medical emergency procedures, such as seizure or asthma action plans.
6. EpiPen authorization: Select whether the child is authorized to use an EpiPen in case of an emergency.
7. Medical treatment authorization: Select whether you authorize emergency medical treatment if you cannot be reached.
8. Optional information: Provide the name and contact information for a preferred hospital or emergency facility and health insurance carrier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Child Care Emergency Form --**: This is the title of the form.
* **Child's name**: Enter the child's full name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Form completion date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Parent or guardian name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Child care facility name**: Enter the name of the child care facility where the child is currently enrolled.
* **Primary emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the primary emergency contact.
* **Relationship to child**: Describe your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Emergency Contact 1 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the primary emergency contact.
* **Secondary emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the secondary emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact 2 Relationship**: Describe the relationship to the child (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone**: Enter the phone number of the secondary emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Child's physician name**: Enter the name of the child's physician.
* **Physician phone number**: Enter the physician's phone number.
* **Known medical conditions**: List any known medical conditions the child has.
* **Current medications**: List any medications the child is currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies the child has (e.g., food, medication, environmental).
* **Instructions for medical emergency**: Describe any necessary medical emergency procedures.
* **Seizure action plan (if applicable)**: Describe any specific procedures for seizure emergencies.
* **Asthma action plan (if applicable)**: Describe any specific procedures for asthma emergencies.
* **EpiPen authorization**: Select whether the child is authorized to use an EpiPen in case of an emergency.
* **I authorize emergency medical treatment**: Select whether you authorize emergency medical treatment if you cannot be reached.
* **Preferred hospital or emergency facility**: Enter the name and contact information for a preferred hospital or emergency facility (if applicable).
* **Health insurance carrier and policy number**: Enter your health insurance carrier and policy number (if applicable).
