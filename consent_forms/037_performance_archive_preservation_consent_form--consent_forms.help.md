# Performance Archive Preservation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the consent of a performer to preserve and archive their performance data. The purpose of this form is to ensure that the performer is aware of and agrees to the preservation and archiving of their performance data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (Name, Consent, and Consent Date).
2. Provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Specify the performance date, time, and location (if applicable).
4. Choose whether you consent to the preservation and archiving of your performance data.
5. Add any additional comments or information about the performance (optional).
6. Enter the Performance Archive ID and Archivist ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Performance Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Performance Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the performance (if applicable).
* **Location** (Optional): Enter the location of the performance (if applicable).
* **Consent** (Required): Choose whether you consent to the preservation and archiving of your performance data (options: True, False, or No Response).
* **Consent Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your consent (automatically calculated by the system).
* **Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the performance.
* **Performance Archive ID** (Optional): Enter the Performance Archive ID (if applicable).
* **Archivist ID** (Optional): Enter the Archivist ID (if applicable).
