# Workplace Child Visitor Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain parental or guardian consent before allowing children to visit a workplace. Fill out the form to record and document the visit details and obtain necessary permissions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the necessary permissions are granted.
2. Answer all required fields truthfully.
3. For fields marked as `true`, ensure that you fill them out before submitting the form.

* **Workplace Child Visitor Consent** (`workplace_child_visitor_consent_form`): A text field where you can enter a brief note or description for the visit.
* **Parent or Guardian Permission** (`parent_or_guardian_permission`): Choose whether you grant or deny permission for your child to visit the workplace. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
	+ If 'No' is selected, do not proceed with the rest of the form.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`): Enter your child's name accurately.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter your contact details in case we need to reach out to you for any reason.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): Enter the date of the visit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): Enter the start time of the visit in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): Enter the end time of the visit in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_children`): Enter the number of children visiting.
* **Number of Adults** (`number_of_adults`): Enter the number of adults accompanying the children.
* **Workplace Contact** (`workplace_contact`): Enter the contact person or contact information for the workplace, in case we need to reach out for any reason.
