# Talent Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Release Form is used to collect information from a talent to release their work for commercial use, allowing production companies to obtain the necessary permissions to use the talent's likeness and work in a project. This form is crucial for ensuring that the talent is aware of and agrees to the usage of their material in the production.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Talent Name** of the individual who will be appearing in the project. This field is not required.
2. Enter the **Project Title** of the production that the talent will be participating in. This field is not required.
3. Provide a brief description of the **Material Description** to be used in the project. This field is not required.
4. Enter any relevant **Project Information** that is necessary for the production. This field is not required.
5. Provide your **Contact Number** to ensure that the talent can be reached with any questions or concerns. This field is not required.
6. Enter your **Email Address** to receive updates and notifications regarding the project. This field is not required.
7. Enter the **Production Company** that is producing the project. This field is not required.
8. Select the **Project Date** when the project will take place. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Talent Name** (`talent_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the talent who will be participating in the project.
* **Project Title** (`project_title`, Text, Not Required): Enter the title of the production that the talent will be appearing in.
* **Material Description** (`material_description`, Text, Not Required): Provide a brief description of the material that will be used in the project.
* **Project Information** (`project_info`, Text, Not Required): Enter any additional information that is necessary for the production.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, Text, Not Required): Enter your contact number to ensure that the talent can be reached with any questions or concerns.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Not Required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications regarding the project.
* **Production Company** (`production_company`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the production company that is producing the project.
* **Project Date** (`project_date`, Date, Not Required): Select the date when the project will take place.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the talent is properly informed and agrees to the use of their likeness and work in the project.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the production team for clarification.
* This form is for the benefit of the talent, and it is essential to get their consent before proceeding with the project.
