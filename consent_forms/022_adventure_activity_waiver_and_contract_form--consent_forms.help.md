# Adventure Activity Waiver And Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to inform participants about the inherent risks involved in adventure activities and to obtain their explicit consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as your full name, date of birth, address, contact phone number, and email address in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter the activity details such as the activity name, scheduled date, and location in the "Activity Details" section.
3. Review and acknowledge the risks involved in the activity in the "Risk Acknowledgment" section.
4. Agree to release the activity provider from liability in the "Liability Release" section.
5. Agree to indemnify the provider against claims in the "Indemnification Agreement" section.
6. Consent to being photographed during the activity in the "Photography Consent" section.
7. Agree to all terms and conditions of the contract in the "Contract Terms Agreement" section.
8. Sign and date the form on the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the adventure activity.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, required): Enter the scheduled date of the activity in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Activity Location** (`activity_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the activity will take place.
* **Inherent Risks Acknowledged** (`inherent_risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to acknowledge that adventure activities have inherent risks.
* **Injury Assumption** (`injury_assumption`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to understand that injuries may occur during the activity.
* **Equipment Use Acknowledgment** (`equipment_use_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to use provided safety equipment properly.
* **Liability Release** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to release the activity provider from liability for injuries or damages.
* **Indemnification Agreement** (`indemnification_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to indemnify the provider against claims.
* **Photography Consent** (`photography_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to consent to being photographed during the activity.
* **Contract Terms Agreement** (`contract_terms_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to agree to all terms and conditions of the contract.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you sign this waiver and contract.
