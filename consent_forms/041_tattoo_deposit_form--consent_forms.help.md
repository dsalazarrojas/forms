# Tattoo Deposit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Deposit Form is designed to collect client information and details necessary for the deposit process. This form is used to gather essential data, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for both clients and staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Client Details** on the first page. This is where you will enter your name, email, and other identifying information.
2. Enter your **Deposit Details** on the second page. Here, you will provide information about the tattoo deposit, including deposit amount and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your **Appointment Details** on the third page. This is where you will schedule your appointment and provide any additional information about your booking.
4. Finally, enter your **Contact Details** on the fourth page. This is the last step, where you will provide your contact information and any other relevant contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details (client_details)**: This is the first field on the form, where you will enter your name and contact information. Please ensure that the email address provided is accurate, as it will be used for communication.
* **Deposit Details (deposit_details)**: On this page, you will enter the deposit amount and any other relevant details related to the deposit.
* **Appointment Details (appointment_details)**: Here, you will schedule your appointment and provide any additional information about your booking.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)**: This is the last field, where you will enter your contact information and any other relevant contact details.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
* If you encounter any issues while filling out the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* If you are an existing client, please do not use this form to avoid duplication of records.
* This form is intended for new clients only, so please do not submit it if you have already registered with us.
