# AI Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in AI research studies. Its purpose is to ensure that participants understand the scope of the study, the potential risks and benefits involved, and their rights as participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately.
2. Review each section and ensure you understand the information provided.
3. Answer the questions truthfully.
4. Sign and date the form after you have read and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Study and Researcher Information (Page 1)
* **Research Study Title**: Provide the title of the study.
* **Principal Investigator (PI) Name**: Enter the name of the principal investigator or researcher.
* **Institutional Affiliation**: Enter the institutional affiliation of the researcher.

### Participant Information (Page 1)
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address.

### Study Disclosure and Purpose (Page 2)
* **Purpose of Research**: Read the study's purpose and ensure you understand its scope.
* **Understanding of Risks & Benefits**: Select "I understand and acknowledge" if you understand the potential risks and benefits.

### Informed Consent
* **Informed Consent for Participation**: Select "Yes, I consent to participate" if you agree to participate in the study.
* **Data Sharing & Publication Consent**: Select "Yes, I consent to anonymized data sharing" if you agree to have your anonymized data used in scientific publications and shared in open research repositories.
* **Consent to Audio/Video Recording**: Select "Yes, I consent to being recorded" if you agree to audio/video recording for the study.

### Withdrawal Rights Acknowledgment
* **Right to Withdraw**: Select "I understand I can stop participating at any time without penalty or loss of compensation" if you acknowledge your right to withdraw from the study.

### Signature Confirmation
* **Participant Digital Signature**: Select "I sign and confirm my agreement to the terms above" if you agree to the terms.

### Researcher Contact Information
* **Questions or Concerns?**: If you have any questions or concerns, contact the PI at the email listed above.
