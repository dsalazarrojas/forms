# Medical Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Authorization Form is used to collect medical authorization and information related to a medical professional's qualifications and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter the required information in the User Input field.
2. Provide a brief Medical History in the Medical History field.
3. Enter the Doctor's Name in the Doctor Name field.
4. Enter the Doctor's Contact Information in the Doctor Contact Info field.
5. Enter the Patient's Name in the Patient Name field.
6. Select the authorization status in the Authorization field.
7. Enter the Date of the authorization in the Date field.
8. Enter the Time of the authorization in the Time field.
9. Enter any relevant notes in the Note field.
10. Enter the Doctor's Email in the Email field.
11. Enter the Doctor's Phone number in the Phone field.
12. Select the Medical Facility from the dropdown menu in the Facility field.
13. Select the Medical Board from the dropdown menu in the Medical Board field.
14. Select the Medical Board Date in the Medical Board Date field.
15. Enter the Medical License Number in the Medical License Number field.
16. Enter the Medical License Expiration Date in the Medical License Expiration Date field.
17. Select the Medical License Status from the dropdown menu in the Medical License Status field.
18. Select the Medical License Type from the dropdown menu in the Medical License Type field.
19. Enter the Medical License Expiration Date in the Medical License Expiration Date field.
20. Enter the Medical License Status Change Date in the Medical License Status Change Date field.
21. Select the Medical License Status Change Reason from the dropdown menu in the Medical License Status Change Reason field.
22. Enter any other reason for the Medical License Status Change in the Reason Other field.
23. Enter the Medical Board Expiration Date in the Medical Board Expiration Date field.
24. Enter any other reason for the Medical License Status Change in the Reason Other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Required): Enter a brief description of the reason for the authorization.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the authorized doctor.
* **Doctor Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the authorized doctor.
* **Patient Name** (Required): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Authorization** (Required): Select 'True' to authorize the medical treatment or procedure.
* **Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the authorization.
* **Time** (Optional): Enter the time of the authorization.
* **Note** (Optional): Enter any relevant notes about the authorization.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the authorized doctor.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of the authorized doctor.
* **Facility** (Required): Select the authorized medical facility from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Board** (Required): Select the authorized medical board from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Board Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the medical board.
* **Medical License Number** (Optional): Enter the medical license number of the authorized doctor.
* **Medical License Expiration Date** (Optional): Enter the expiration date of the medical license.
* **Medical License Status** (Required): Select the status of the medical license from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical License Type** (Required): Select the type of medical license from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical License Expiration Date** (Optional): Enter the expiration date of the medical license.
* **Medical License Status Change Date** (Optional): Enter the date of the medical license status change.
* **Medical License Status Change Reason** (Required): Select the reason for the medical license status change from the dropdown menu.
* **Reason Other** (Optional): Enter any other reason for the medical license status change.
* **Medical Board Expiration Date** (Optional): Enter the expiration date of the medical board.
* **Reason Other** (Optional): Enter any other reason for the medical license status change.
