# Club Highlight Reel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from participants for various club-related media, including video recordings, photos, and audio content.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply go through each section, read the explanations, and make informed decisions about how you'd like your participation and likeness to be used. Be sure to answer all required fields accurately to ensure your consent is properly recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you about your participation and media usage.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you about your participation and media usage.
* **Age or Date of Birth** (`participant_age`, text, required): If you're 18 or older, enter your age or date of birth. If not, you can confirm you're 18 or older.
* **Club or Team Affiliation** (`club_affiliation`, text, required): Enter your club or team affiliation, if applicable.
* **Permission to Video Record** (`video_permission`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow video recording for club highlight reel and promotional videos.
* **Permission to Take Photos** (`photo_permission`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow photos to be taken for club highlight reel and promotional use.
* **Permission to Record Audio** (`audio_permission`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow audio recording, including voice and commentary, for club highlight reel and promotional videos.
* **Okay to Share on Social Media** (`social_media_use`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow sharing on social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Facebook.
* **Okay to Use on Club Website** (`website_use`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow use on the club website.
* **Okay for Marketing and Promotional Materials** (`promotional_use`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow use in marketing and promotional materials like flyers, videos, and advertisements.
* **Okay for Commercial or Paid Use** (`commercial_use`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow use for commercial or paid purposes, such as selling content or products.
* **Specific Activity or Event Being Recorded** (`specific_activity`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the specific activity or event being recorded.
* **Dates of Recording** (`recording_dates`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the dates of recording.
* **Permission to Use Your Name** (`name_usage`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes, use my name" to allow use of your name in credits or attributions.
* **I understand the club may use these materials indefinitely** (`rights_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I agree" to understand that the club may use the recorded materials indefinitely.
* **I transfer all rights to the club for this content** (`rights_transfer`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I transfer rights" to transfer all rights to the club for this content.
* **I consent to participate and be recorded** (`final_consent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I fully consent" to participate and be recorded, acknowledging all terms above.
* **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the current date to sign off on your consent.
