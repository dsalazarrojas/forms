# Parental Authorization Form For Minors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's medical history and authorization for treatment. The form is typically used by medical providers to ensure that parents or guardians have provided consent for their child's medical treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are a parent or guardian with the authority to give consent for the child's medical treatment.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you are authorizing the child's medical treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Minor ID**: A unique identifier for the child. This field is required.
* **Parent Name**: The name of the parent or guardian giving consent. This field is required.
* **Relationship**: The relationship between the parent or guardian and the child (e.g., Parent, Guardian, Other). This field is required.
* **Address**: The address of the parent or guardian. This field is not required.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the parent or guardian. This field is not required.
* **Email**: The email of the parent or guardian. This field is not required.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child. This field is required.
* **Date of Treatment**: The date of the medical treatment or procedure. This field is required.
* **Medical Condition**: A list of the child's medical conditions (e.g., Asthma, Allergies, Anaphylaxis, Autism). This field is required.
* **Medical Procedure**: A description of the medical procedure or treatment. This field is required.
* **Signature Date**: The date of the signature. This field is required.
* **Signature**: A signature of the parent or guardian. This field is required.
* **Signature Name**: The name of the person signing the form. This field is required.
