# Analytics Data Tracking Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Analytics Data Tracking Consent Form is a one-time request for users to provide their consent for data tracking on the platform. This form is used to understand user behavior and improve services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Full Name and User Email Address in the required fields.
2. Select the Types of Data Collected that you agree to share.
3. Choose the Primary Purpose of Tracking for the data collected.
4. Choose whether you allow third-party tracking cookies and consent to the collection and processing of your personal data.
5. Confirm your consent by providing a digital signature or a confirmation code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Full Name** (`User Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **User Email Address** (`User Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Types of Data Collected** (`Types of Data Collected`, select_multiple, required): Select the data types you agree to share. Options include:
	+ Page Views
	+ Device Information
	+ Geographic Location
	+ Referral Source
* **Primary Purpose of Tracking** (`Primary Purpose of Tracking`, select_one, required): Choose the primary purpose of tracking for the data collected. Options include:
	+ Product Improvement
	+ Marketing Personalization
	+ Security Monitoring
	+ Research
* **Do you allow third-party tracking cookies?** (`Do you allow third-party tracking cookies?`, select_one, required): Choose whether you allow third-party tracking cookies. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **I consent to the collection and processing of my personal data?** (`I consent to the collection and processing of my personal data?`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to the collection and processing of your personal data. Options are:
	+ I Consent
	+ I Do Not Consent
* **Digital Signature or Confirmation Code** (`Digital Signature or Confirmation Code`, text, required): Enter your full name to confirm your consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the platform administrator.
- If you have already filled this form previously, there is no need to fill it again.
- Once you submit the form, your consent is recorded and you will not be able to edit it later.
