# Texas Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Texas Telemedicine Consent Form is designed to collect information from both the patient and medical provider for telemedicine consultations. This form helps ensure that both parties are aware of the necessary information and understand their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each field carefully and ensure you have the required information before filling out the form.
2. Begin by filling out the patient information page.
3. Next, fill out the contact information page.
4. Then, move on to the medical history and telemedicine information page.
5. After that, fill out the insurance information page.
6. Next, sign the consent page, indicating your understanding of the telemedicine services.
7. Finally, complete the emergency contact and signature page, including your date and time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Information**
- **Patient Name**, **Patient Type**: Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (month/day/year).
- **Address**: Enter your mailing address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Medical Provider**: Please enter the name of your primary care physician or doctor.

### 2. **Contact Information**
- **Name**: Enter the name of the person completing the form.
- **Address**: Enter the person's mailing address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter the person's phone number where they can be reached.
- **Email**: Enter the person's email address where they can be reached.

### 3. **Medical History**
- **Medical History**: Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any allergies, medications, and previous medical conditions.
- **Telemedicine Info**: Provide information about your telemedicine experience, including any previous telemedicine consultations.

### 4. **Insurance Information**
- **Insurance Provider**: Please enter the name of your insurance provider.
- **Group Number**: Enter your insurance group number.

### 5. **Consent**
- **Consent**: Please sign and date this section, indicating your understanding of the telemedicine services.

### 6. **Emergency Contact**
- **Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Relationship**: Enter the relationship between you and the emergency contact.
- **Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number.
- **Address**: Enter the emergency contact person's address.

### 7. **Signature**
- **Signature**: Sign and date this section, indicating your understanding and consent to the telemedicine services.

### 8. **Date Signed**
- **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed this form.

### 9. **Time Signed**
- **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed this form.

### 10. **Signature**
- **Signature**: Sign your name, indicating your understanding and consent to the telemedicine services.

Note: This document is for information purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment.
