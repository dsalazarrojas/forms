# Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo And Video Release Form is designed to collect information related to the release of photo and video content. This form helps to gather details about the content, such as descriptions, credits, and usage permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. Each field is designed to collect a specific type of information. Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure accurate record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter the name of the client or individual associated with the photo or video.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Required: false): Provide the contact information for the client or individual, such as email or phone number.
- **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, Text, Required: false): Briefly describe the photo or video content being released.
- **Photo Credit** (`photo_credit`, Text, Required: false): Specify the credit information for the photo, such as the photographer's name or copyright holder.
- **Photo Use** (`photo_use`, Text, Required: false): Specify the intended use of the photo, such as "social media" or "marketing materials".
- **Photo Sharing** (`photo_sharing`, Text, Required: false): Specify if the photo will be shared online or through other channels.
- **Video Description** (`video_description`, Text, Required: false): Briefly describe the video content being released.
- **Video Credit** (`video_credit`, Text, Required: false): Specify the credit information for the video, such as the director's name or copyright holder.
- **Video Use** (`video_use`, Text, Required: false): Specify the intended use of the video, such as "social media" or "marketing materials".
- **Video Sharing** (`video_sharing`, Text, Required: false): Specify if the video will be shared online or through other channels.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter the name of the project related to the photo or video.
- **Project Description** (`project_description`, Text, Required: false): Provide a brief description of the project.
- **Project Use** (`project_use`, Text, Required: false): Specify the intended use of the project.
- **Project Sharing** (`project_sharing`, Text, Required: false): Specify if the project will be shared online or through other channels.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
