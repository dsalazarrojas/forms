# Hotel Policy And Deposit Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Policy And Deposit Consent Form is used to ensure that customers understand and agree to the hotel's policies and procedures, particularly regarding deposit payments and room usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please read each section carefully to understand the hotel's policies and procedures.
2. Check all boxes for the policies you agree to.
3. Fill out your contact information (if applicable).
4. Submit the form to acknowledge your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hotel Policy** (`hotel_policy`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can describe any special requests or concerns you have about your stay.
* **Deposit Agreement** (`deposit_agreement`, text, required): Please type out your agreement to the hotel's deposit policy, including understanding that the deposit will be returned after your stay if there are no damages or issues.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, select_multiple, required): Select all the policies listed below that you acknowledge and agree to:
	+ Yes, I have read and agree to the hotel policies and procedures.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted by hotel staff, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by hotel staff, please provide your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth check-in process.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the hotel policies, please reach out to hotel staff.
* This form should only be filled out once per stay.
* The hotel's policies and procedures are subject to change, so please review them regularly.
