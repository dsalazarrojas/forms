# street_art_project_photography_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining consent from participants for a street art project photography project. It is intended to gather essential contact information and consent from individuals who have been photographed as part of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have the permission to access this form. Only authorized users have access to this form.
2. Fill out the form with the necessary information, making sure to complete all required fields.
3. Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant
* **Participant** (`participant`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name or the name of the person being photographed.
### Artist
* **Artist** (`artist`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person taking the photograph or responsible for the photography project.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `note`, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional contact information or notes about the participant or the photography project.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you wish to provide a phone number for the participant, enter it here.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
