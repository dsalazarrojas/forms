# Passenger Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects essential information from passengers for their waiver, ensuring a smooth and secure travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Please enter the passenger's name as it appears on their official ID or travel documents.
- Enter the date of birth for the passenger.
- Provide the emergency contact's name and their relationship to the passenger.
- Sign the waiver form to acknowledge your understanding and consent.
- Enter the date of signing.
- Confirm the location where the form was signed.
- Sign on behalf of the airline.
- Enter the flight number.
- Record the travel date.
- Enter the passenger's contact information, including email and phone number.
- Provide the airline's contact phone number.
- Enter the passenger's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Waiver Form** (`passenger_waiver_form`): Fill out this field with your name as it appears on your official ID or travel documents.
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`): Enter the passenger's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the passenger's date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Describe the relationship between the passenger and their emergency contact.
* **Passenger Signature** (`passenger_signature`): Sign on behalf of the passenger to acknowledge understanding and consent.
* **Passenger Signature Date** (`passenger_signature_date`): Enter the date of signing in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Passenger Signature Location** (`passenger_signature_location`): Confirm the location where the form was signed.
* **Airline Signature** (`airline_signature`): Sign on behalf of the airline to acknowledge their acceptance of the waiver.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`): Enter the flight number.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`): Record the date of travel in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Passenger Email** (`passenger_email`): Enter the passenger's email address.
* **Passenger Phone** (`passenger_phone`): Enter the passenger's phone number.
* **Airline Phone** (`airline_phone`): Enter the airline's contact phone number.
* **Passenger Address** (`passenger_address`): Enter the passenger's address.

## Tips
- Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness before proceeding.
- Ensure you have the necessary authority to fill out this form on behalf of the passenger.
- Review and understand the waiver terms before signing.
