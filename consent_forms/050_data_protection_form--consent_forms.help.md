# Data Protection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Protection Form is a document used to collect and record sensitive information about individuals. This form is used to ensure that we handle personal data responsibly and in accordance with our internal policies and external regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the required information, making sure to enter accurate details about the individual.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (form_data, text, optional): This field is used to describe the purpose or context of the data being collected.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual or organization that this data is about.
* **Contact Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the individual or organization that this data is about.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the individual.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual or organization that this data is about.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter the address of the individual or organization that this data is about.
* **Social Security Number** (social_security_number, text, optional): Enter the social security number of the individual.
* **Consent Date** (consent_date, date, optional): Enter the date of consent for the data collection.
* **Form Signature** (form_signature, note, optional): This field is reserved for a signature from the individual or their authorized representative.
* **Form Footer** (form_footer, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the form completion.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation above.
- If you are collecting sensitive information about minors, please ensure that you have the necessary authorities and permissions to do so.
- If you are filling out this form for an individual, please ensure that you have their consent before collecting and storing their data.
