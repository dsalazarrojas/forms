# Metabolic Bodybuilding Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Bodybuilding Intake Form is designed to gather crucial information about a patient's metabolic bodybuilding journey. This form is meant for patients who are or have been involved in bodybuilding activities. The data collected will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's past and current experiences with bodybuilding, which can inform their treatment plan and improve outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Complete the form in its entirety, as some questions are required to be answered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `note`, required):
This section is where you provide consent for the form to be filled out and used for the intended purpose.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required):
Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as required.
* **Metabolic Bodybuilding Background** (`metabolic_bodybuilding_background`, `note`, required):
Please provide a brief description of your background and history with metabolic bodybuilding, including any relevant experiences or concerns you have.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- If you need to update any information, please let your healthcare provider know and they will guide you through the process.
