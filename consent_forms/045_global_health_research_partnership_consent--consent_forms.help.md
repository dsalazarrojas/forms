# global_health_research_partnership_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a partnership between an individual or institution and a global health research collaboration. It is essential to fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the partnership.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Make sure to sign the form to confirm your consent to the partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Please fill out your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Please provide a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required/optional): Please provide a valid phone number where you can be contacted.
- **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, required/optional): Please provide a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Department** (`Department`, text, required/optional): Please provide the department or institution you are affiliated with.
- **Research Institution** (`Research Institution`, text, required/optional): Please provide the name of the research institution you are collaborating with.
- **Contact Institution** (`Contact Institution`, text, required/optional): Please provide the name of the institution you are affiliated with.
- **Address** (`Address`, text, required/optional): Please provide the physical address of your institution.
- **Research Project** (`Research Project`, text, required/optional): Please provide the name of the research project you are collaborating on.
- **Consent** (`Consent`, select multiple, required): Please select the options that apply to your partnership. Options may include, but are not limited to:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, date, required/optional): Please provide the date you signed the form.
- **Signature** (`Signature`, time, required/optional): Please sign the form to confirm your consent to the partnership.
- **Uploaded Documents** (`Uploaded Documents`, note, required/optional): Please attach any relevant documents related to the partnership.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any confusion or misunderstandings.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date and correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the partnership, please contact the relevant institution or party involved.
