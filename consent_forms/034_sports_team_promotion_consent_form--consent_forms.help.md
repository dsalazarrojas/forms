# sports_team_promotion_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child to participate in sports team promotions, including the use of their name, image, and likenesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You may submit the form without filling in all the fields, but it is recommended to fill in as much information as possible to ensure accurate records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Please enter your name, your child's name, or any other relevant details that you want to provide.

### Parent Permission
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select one, optional): Select "Parent permission granted" to indicate that you are granting permission for your child to participate in the sports team promotion.

### Names Images and Likenesses
* **Names Images and Likenesses** (`names_images_likenesses`, select multiple, optional): Choose from "Names", "Images", or "Likenesses" to indicate what aspects of your child's identity you are granting permission for. Select all that apply.

### Marketing and Publicity
* **Marketing and Publicity** (`marketing_publicity`, text, optional): Please enter any specific details or restrictions you have regarding the use of your child's image or likeness for marketing and publicity purposes.

### School Club or League
* **School Club or League** (`school_club_league`, select one, optional): Select "School", "Club", or "League" to indicate the specific sports team your child is participating in.

### Athlete or Parent
* **Athlete or Parent** (`athlete_parent`, select one, optional): Select "Athlete" or "Parent" to indicate your relationship to the child participating in the sports team promotion.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you are granting permission for your child to participate in the sports team promotion.

### Coach or Staff
* **Coach or Staff** (`coach_staff`, select one, optional): Select "Coach" or "Staff" to indicate your affiliation with the sports team.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please enter any additional comments or notes you have regarding your child's participation in the sports team promotion.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully review all fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or the sports team management.
