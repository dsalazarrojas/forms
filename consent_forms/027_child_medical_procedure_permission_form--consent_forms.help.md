# Child Medical Procedure Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Medical Procedure Permission Form is a crucial document that grants medical staff permission to perform a specific medical procedure on a child. This form ensures that the child's parents or guardians are aware of and agree to the procedure, and that they have been informed of the associated risks and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill in the child's name, date of birth, and the name of the medical procedure you are requesting.
* Select the correct relationship to the minor (parent, legal guardian, or other authorized representative).
* Indicate whether the child has recently been ill or has fasted as required.
* Confirm that you have been informed of the risks and benefits associated with the procedure.
* Finally, select the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Medical Procedure** (`procedure_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the medical procedure being performed (e.g., Tonsillectomy, Dental Filling).
* **Scheduled Procedure Date** (`scheduled_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled procedure (date of appointment).
* **Minor Full Name** (`minor_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child.
* **Date of Birth** (`minor_dob`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth to verify identity.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person granting permission (parent or guardian).
* **Relationship to Minor** (`relationship_to_minor`, select_one, required): Select the relationship to the child (Parent, Legal Guardian, or Other Authorized Representative).
* **Any recent illnesses or fever?** (`recent_illness`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any recent illnesses or fever in the child within the last 48 hours.
* **Has the child fasted as required?** (`fasting_status`, select_one, required): Select whether the child has fasted as required or has not (Yes, No, or Not Applicable).
* **I have been informed of the risks and benefits** (`risk_informed`, select_one, required): Select whether you have been informed of the risks and benefits (Yes, I Understand, or No, I Need More Information).
* **Today Date** (`today_date`, date, required): Enter the current date.

Tips:

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask for clarification or assistance from medical staff.
* This form is an essential part of the child's medical record, so please ensure it is completed carefully and honestly.
