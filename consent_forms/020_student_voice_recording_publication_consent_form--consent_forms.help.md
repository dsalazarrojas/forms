# student_voice_recording_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for the publication of their child's voice recordings.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide all required information.
2. If you are not sure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
3. Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Info** (`id: 1`, `label: Parent Info`, `type: note`): This is a brief description of the parent's information. Please provide any relevant details that may be helpful in identifying the parent.
* **Parent Name** (`id: 2`, `label: Parent Name`, `type: text`): This is the name of the parent or guardian who is providing consent for the publication of the recording.
* **Parent Contact** (`id: 3`, `label: Parent Contact`, `type: text`): This is the contact information of the parent or guardian, including their phone number, email, or any other relevant contact details.
* **School Info** (`id: 4`, `label: School Info`, `type: text`): This is a brief description of the school or educational institution that the recording belongs to.
* **Publication Type** (`id: 5`, `label: Publication Type`, `type: select_one`): Select the type of publication that the recording will be published under. Please choose from:
	+ Student
	+ School
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Publication Purpose** (`id: 6`, `label: Publication Purpose`, `type: text`): This is a brief description of the purpose of the publication.
* **Other (Please specify)** (`id: 7`, `label: Other (Please specify)`, `type: text`): If you selected "Other" in the Publication Type field, please provide a brief explanation of the publication purpose.
* **Student Informed Consent** (`id: 8`, `label: Student Informed Consent`, `type: select_one`): Check if the student has given informed consent for the publication of their recording. Please select:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Consent Status** (`id: 9`, `label: Consent Status`, `type: select_multiple`): Select the consent status of the publication. Please choose from:
	+ Approved
	+ Pending
	+ Denied
* **Notes** (`id: 10`, `label: Notes`, `type: note`): This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes regarding the publication.
* **Publication Location** (`id: 11`, `label: Publication Location`, `type: text`): This is the location where the recording will be published (e.g., website, social media, etc.).
* **Publication Date** (`id: 12`, `label: Publication Date`, `type: date`): This is the date when the publication will take place.
* **Publication Time** (`id: 13`, `label: Publication Time`, `type: time`): This is the time when the publication will take place.
* **Publication Duration** (`id: 14`, `label: Publication Duration`, `type: number`): This is the duration of the publication (in hours, minutes, or seconds).
* **Publication Date Range** (`id: 15`, `label: Publication Date Range`, `type: select_multiple`): Select the date range of the publication. Please choose from:
	+ From
	+ To
* **Recording File Name** (`id: 16`, `label: Recording File Name`, `type: text`): This is the name of the recording file.
* **Recording File** (`id: 17`, `label: Recording File`, `type: text`): This is the location of the recording file.
* **Publication URL** (`id: 18`, `label: Publication URL`, `type: text`): This is the URL where the publication can be accessed.
* **Consent to Publish** (`id: 19`, `label: Consent to Publish`, `type: select_one`): Check if you consent to publish the recording. Please select:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Consent Reason** (`id: 20`, `label: Consent Reason (Optional)`, `type: text`): If you selected 'False' in the Consent to Publish field, please provide a brief explanation of the reason for not consenting to publish.
* **Parent Sign Date** (`id: 21`, `label: Parent Sign Date`, `type: date`): This is the date when the parent or guardian signed this form.
* **Parent Sign Time** (`id: 22`, `label: Parent Sign Time`, `type: time`): This is the time when the parent or guardian signed this form.
* **Parent Sign Location** (`id: 23`, `label: Parent Sign Location`, `type: text`): This is the location where the parent or guardian signed this form.
* **School Sign Date** (`id: 24`, `label: School Sign Date`, `type: date`): This is the date when the school or educational institution signed this form.
* **School Sign Time** (`id: 25`, `label: School Sign Time`, `type: time`): This is the time when the school or educational institution signed this form.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth submission process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Once you have filled out the form, review it carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
* Make sure to keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
