# Rock Chip Repair Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise way for customers to acknowledge their appointment schedule and confirm their repair details with the rock chip repair service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Step 1: Fill out the Customer Information section**
	* Provide your name and contact information so that we can contact you with updates and confirmations.
2. **Step 2: Provide Repair Information**
	* Give us details about the rock chip you would like us to repair, including any relevant descriptions or photos.
3. **Step 3: Select an Appointment Schedule**
	* Choose a preferred date and time for your appointment from the provided options.
4. **Step 4: Confirm and Acknowledge**
	* Review your information and acknowledge that it is accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required): Please enter your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you with updates and confirmations.
* **Repair Information** (`repair_info`, text, required): Describe the rock chip you would like us to repair, including any relevant details or photos.
* **Appointment Schedule** (`appointment_schedule`, text, required): Select a date and time for your appointment from the provided options.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, text, required): Please review and confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
* **Customer Confirmation** (`customer_confirmation`, number, optional): This field is not currently used in the form, but it is here for future reference.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will contact you with updates and confirmations once we receive your form submission.
