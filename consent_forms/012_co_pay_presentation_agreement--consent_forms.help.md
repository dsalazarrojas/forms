# Co Pay Presentation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Co-Pay Presentation Agreement, used to collect information from participants for a presentation or program. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number**.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** for identification purposes.
3. Enter the **Name of the Presentation or Program** you are attending.
4. Select the correct **Payment Due Date Understanding**.
5. Choose your preferred **Payment Method**.
6. Review and confirm your **Refund Policy Acknowledgment** and **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**.
7. Indicate if you **Consent to Participate** in the presentation.
8. Sign the **Date of Agreement** and provide the **Witness Name** if applicable.
9. Add any **Additional Comments** if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name (1)**: Please enter your complete name, as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, passport, or driver's license). (Required)
* **Email Address (2)**: Enter your primary email address where we can contact you. (Required)
* **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your contact phone number. (Required)
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your Date of Birth for identification purposes. (Optional)
* **Presentation or Program Name (6)**: Type in the name of the presentation or program you are attending. (Required)
* **Presentation Date (7)**: Select the date of the scheduled presentation. (Required)
* **Organization Name (8)**: Enter the name of the organizing entity. (Required)
* **Co-Pay Amount (9)**: Choose one of the pre-defined amounts or select "Other Amount" if it's not listed. (Required)
* **Payment Responsibility Acknowledgment (10)**: Confirm you understand your payment responsibility. (Required)
* **Payment Due Date Understanding (11)**: Select when you expect to pay. (Required)
* **Will Insurance Be Billed (12)**: Indicate if your insurance will cover this expense. (Optional)
* **Insurance Provider (13)**: Enter your insurance company's name, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Policy Number (14)**: Enter your insurance policy number, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Experiencing Financial Hardship (15)**: If you're facing financial difficulties, please indicate. (Optional)
* **Request Payment Plan (16)**: If you need to set up a payment plan, select "True". (Optional)
* **Preferred Payment Method (17)**: Choose your preferred method of payment. (Required)
* **Refund Policy Acknowledgment (18)**: Confirm you understand the refund policy. (Required)
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment (19)**: Confirm you understand the cancellation terms. (Required)
* **Consent to Participate (20)**: Agree to participate in the presentation. (Required)
* **Date of Agreement (21)**: Sign with the current date. (Required)
* **Witness Name (22)**: Enter the name of the witness, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Additional Comments (23)**: Add any additional information or questions. (Optional)
