# Cadet Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cadet Program Registration form is used to collect information from applicants to the Cadet Program. This form is required for all applicants to verify their eligibility and provide necessary details for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the form with your information.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in.
3.  Provide any additional information that is relevant to your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Cadet name** (`cadet_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy to verify your age eligibility for the program.
*   **Parent or guardian name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, not required): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's name.
*   **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter a phone number for emergency contact purposes.
*   **Medical concerns or allergies** (`medical_concerns`, text, not required): If you have any medical concerns or allergies, enter them in this field to ensure program safety.
*   **Consent for participation** (`consent_for_participation`, select_one, required): Select "I consent" if you agree to participate in the Cadet Program. If you are a minor, a parent or guardian must consent on your behalf.
*   **Uniform size** (`uniform_size`, text, not required): If you are interested in purchasing a uniform, enter your uniform size.
*   **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, not required): Provide any additional information that is relevant to your application.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
*   Use the correct format for date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
*   If you are a minor, a parent or guardian must consent on your behalf for participation in the program.
*   Enter any relevant medical concerns or allergies to ensure program safety.
*   If you are interested in purchasing a uniform, enter your uniform size.
*   Provide any additional information that is relevant to your application.
