# Liability Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's declaration of liability. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully to ensure that the individual is aware of and accepts the risks associated with their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
2. Fill out the form with your name and address as it appears on your official documents (e.g., ID, driver's license, or passport).
3. Provide the date and time of the event or activity you are participating in.
4. Specify the location where the event or activity will take place.
5. If your supervisor or organization is involved, please provide their name and phone number.
6. If an emergency contact is required, please provide their name, phone number, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Street** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your street address as it appears on your official documents.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Please enter the city of your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Please enter the state of your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Please enter the zip code of your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Please enter the country of your address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the event or activity you are participating in.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, optional): Please enter the time of the event or activity you are participating in.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Please enter the location where the event or activity will take place.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your organization or company if applicable.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): If you have a supervisor or organization involved, please enter their name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): If you have a supervisor or organization involved, please enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): If an emergency contact is required, please enter their first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): If an emergency contact is required, please enter their last name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If an emergency contact is required, please enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): If an emergency contact is required, please enter their address.

## Tips

* Always double-check the accuracy of the information you provide.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities or contacts listed above.
