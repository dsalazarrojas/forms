# Body Modification Indemnity Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients for a body modification procedure. It is essential to carefully review and understand the form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you understand each field and its implications.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Type of Body Modification** (`modification_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of body modification you are undergoing, such as tattoo, piercing, or scarification.
* **Specific Modification Details** (`specific_modification`, text, required): Describe the specific modification you are requesting.
* **Body Location** (`modification_location`, text, required): Specify the location on your body where the modification will be applied.
* **Artist or Technician Name** (`artist_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the artist or technician performing the modification.
* **Date of Modification** (`modification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the modification.
* **I understand the procedure and its risks** (`procedure_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the procedure and its risks.
* **I acknowledge the risks involved** (`risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the potential risks involved.
* **I understand infection risks** (`infection_risks_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risk of infection.
* **I have disclosed all allergies** (`allergies_disclosed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all allergies.
* **I have disclosed medical conditions** (`medical_conditions_disclosed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have disclosed any medical conditions.
* **I have disclosed all medications** (`medications_disclosed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have disclosed all medications.
* **I am not pregnant or nursing** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are not pregnant or nursing.
* **I understand aftercare instructions** (`aftercare_instructions_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the aftercare instructions.
* **I understand potential pain levels** (`pain_level_expectation`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the potential pain levels involved.
* **I understand healing time** (`healing_time_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the healing time involved.
* **I am voluntarily choosing this procedure** (`voluntarily_choosing`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are voluntarily choosing this procedure.
* **I consent to this body modification** (`consent_to_procedure`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the modification.
* **I release and waive liability** (`release_and_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm that you release and waive liability for any complications.
* **I indemnify the artist against liability** (`indemnity_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you indemnify the artist against liability.

## Tips
- Double-check that you have filled out the form accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure you understand each field and its implications.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
