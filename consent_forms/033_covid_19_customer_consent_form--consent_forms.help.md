# COVID 19 Customer Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 Customer Consent Form is a crucial document that requires customers to acknowledge their understanding and acceptance of the risks associated with receiving services at our facility during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps ensure the customer's safety and our facility's compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Provide your personal details**:
    *   **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
2.  **Provide your health details**:
    *   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to help us assess your risk level.
    *   **Service Date**: Enter the date you are receiving services at our facility.
    *   **Type of Service**: Select the type of service you are receiving (e.g., medical, fitness, salon, etc.).
3.  **Acknowledge COVID-19 risks**:
    *   **I acknowledge that COVID-19 is a serious respiratory illness**: Confirm your understanding of the risks associated with COVID-19.
    *   **I understand that COVID-19 can spread through close contact**: Acknowledge that COVID-19 can spread through close contact.
    *   **I understand that I may be exposed to COVID-19**: Confirm your understanding of the risks of exposure to COVID-19.
4.  **Understand facility protocols**:
    *   **I understand the safety precautions in place**: Confirm your understanding of our safety protocols (masking, distancing, cleaning, testing, etc.).
    *   **I release the facility from liability related to COVID-19**: Release our facility from liability related to COVID-19 exposure.
    *   **I agree to follow all facility guidelines and protocols**: Confirm your agreement to follow our guidelines and protocols.
5.  **Provide health declarations**:
    *   **I attest that I am not experiencing COVID-19 symptoms**: Confirm that you are not experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
    *   **I have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days**: Confirm that you have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
    *   **I am not currently under quarantine or isolation**: Confirm that you are not under quarantine or isolation.
    *   **I have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days**: Confirm that you have not tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 10 days.
6.  **Consent and signature**:
    *   **Consent Date**: Enter the date you are confirming your consent to receive services at our facility during the COVID-19 pandemic.
    *   **Consent Confirmation**: Confirm that you fully consent to receiving services at our facility, understanding all terms and conditions.
