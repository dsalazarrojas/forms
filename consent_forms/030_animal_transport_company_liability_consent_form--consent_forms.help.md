# Animal Transport Company Liability Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the risks associated with animal transport, and to establish their understanding of liability and emergency procedures. Completing this form is essential before transport services can be provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Ensure that you understand the risks and liabilities associated with animal transport before signing.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to our transport team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Legal Name** (`client_legal_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Animal Name and Species** (`animal_transport_details`, `text`, required): Please provide the full name of the animal and its species.
* **Pick-up Location Address** (`pickup_location_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the animal will be picked up from.
* **Drop-off Location Address** (`dropoff_location_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the animal will be dropped off.
* **Scheduled Transport Date** (`scheduled_transport_date`, `date`, required): Select the date for the transport service.
* **Acknowledgment of Inherent Risks** (`risk_acknowledgment_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select `I understand and accept the risks` to acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with animal transport.
* **Liability Release for Accidents** (`accident_liability_release`, `select_one`, required): Please select `I release the company from liability` to acknowledge that you understand that the company is not liable for accidents beyond its control.
* **Emergency Medical Authorization** (`emergency_med_auth`, `select_one`, required): Please select `I authorize emergency care` to grant permission for emergency medical treatment if required during transport.
* **Maximum Veterinary Expenditure Limit** (`max_vet_expenditure_limit`, `number`, required): Enter the maximum amount you are willing to spend on veterinary care for the animal.
* **Health Certificate Confirmation** (`health_cert_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Please select `Yes - Certificate provided` to confirm that a valid health certificate is provided for the animal.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_primary`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact phone number in case of emergencies.
* **Client Digital Signature** (`client_signature_acknowledgment`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of this form.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have a valid health certificate for your animal before transport.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to our transport team for clarification.
* Please complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure that we can provide safe and reliable transport services for your animal.
