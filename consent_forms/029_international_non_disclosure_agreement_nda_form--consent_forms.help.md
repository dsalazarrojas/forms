# International Non Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Non Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Form is used to document and track non-disclosure agreements made with clients, contractors, or vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required and optional information as requested.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
3. Signature section requires an actual signature, not just a typing or digital mark.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Prophets Are Forbidden** (`non_disclosure_agreement`, text): Please enter a short description of the non-disclosure agreement, if necessary.
* **Non Discloses** (`non_disclosures`, select_multiple): Select all applicable non-disclosures (Confidential, Internal Use, External Use, Non-disclose, Disclose).
* **Term of Agreement** (`term_agreement`, date): Enter the date the agreement was made.
* **Non Disclosure Period** (`non_disclosure_period`, time): Enter the time the agreement was made.
* **Confidentiality** (`confidentiality`, select_one): Select the level of confidentiality (Confidential, Internal, External).
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, text): Please enter any additional confidentiality agreement details, if necessary.
* **Non Disclosure Terms** (`non_disclosure_terms`, text): Please enter any non-disclosure terms, if necessary.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note): Please sign and date this section.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Date** (`non_disclosure_agreement_date`, date): Enter the date the agreement was made.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term** (`non_disclosure_agreement_term`, text): Please enter any additional non-disclosure agreement details, if necessary.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signer** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signer`, select_one): Select who signed the agreement (Client, Contractor, Vendors).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed By** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_by`, text): Please enter the name(s) of who signed the agreement.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed On** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_on`, date): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed At** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_at`, time): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed Time Zone** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_by_time_zone`, select_one): Select the time zone where the agreement was signed (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaskan, UTC).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed Time** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_time`, time): Enter the time the agreement was signed.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Signed On 2** (`non_disclosure_agreement_signed_on_2`, date): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Agreement Term** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term`, select_multiple): Select all applicable agreement terms (30 days, 90 days, 180 days, 1 month, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, 4 years, 5 years).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term In Days** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term_days`, select_multiple): Select all applicable agreement terms in days (I agree, I do not agree).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term In Months** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term_months`, select_multiple): Select all applicable agreement terms in months (I agree, I do not agree).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term In Years** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term_years`, select_multiple): Select all applicable agreement terms in years (I agree, I do not agree).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Agreement Term Years** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term_years_years`, select_multiple): Select all applicable agreement terms in years (I agree, I do not agree).
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Agreement Term 2** (`non_disclosure_agreement_agreement_term_2`, text): Please enter any additional agreement term details, if necessary.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term Date** (`non_disclosure_agreement_term_date`, date): Enter the date of the agreement term.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement Term Time** (`non_disclosure_agreement_term_time`, time): Enter the time of the agreement term.

## Tips

* This form is used to document and track non-disclosure agreements made with clients, contractors, or vendors.
* Please review each section carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* Signature section requires an actual signature, not just a typing or digital mark.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the person who initiated the agreement.
