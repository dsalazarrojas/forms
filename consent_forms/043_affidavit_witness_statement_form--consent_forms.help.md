# Affidavit Witness Statement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Affidavit Witness Statement Form is used to gather information from witnesses who have witnessed an incident or event. This form is used to collect their statements, which can be used as evidence in court proceedings or legal matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

- Carefully read each field and its label.
- Answer each question accurately and truthfully.
- Save and return to the form if you need more time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Witness Full Name
* **Witness Full Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to record your full name as a witness.
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`witness_dob`, `date`, required): This field is used to record your date of birth.
### Occupation
* **Occupation** (`witness_occupation`, `text`, required): This field is used to record your occupation.
### Address
* **Address** (`witness_address`, `text`, required): This field is used to record your address.
### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`witness_contact`, `text`, required): This field is used to record your contact number.
### Plaintiff / Claimant
* **Plaintiff / Claimant** (`plaintiff`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record the name of the party bringing the case, if applicable.
### Defendant / Respondent
* **Defendant / Respondent** (`defendant`, `text`, optional): This field is used to record the name of the party defending, if applicable.
### Date of Incident
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when the event occurred.
### Location of Incident
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): This field is used to record where the incident occurred.
### Statement of Events
* **Statement of Events** (`statement_content`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe exactly what you saw or heard.
### Relationship to Parties
* **Relationship to Parties** (`relationship`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe your relationship to the parties involved.
### Declaration
* **Declaration** (`truth_declaration`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to declare that the facts stated in this witness statement are true. Choose "I Believe" if you believe the statement to be true.
### Date Signed
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date when you signed the form.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and truthful information.
* You may save and return to the form if you need more time.
* If you are unsure about any field, you may seek guidance from the authority administering this form.
