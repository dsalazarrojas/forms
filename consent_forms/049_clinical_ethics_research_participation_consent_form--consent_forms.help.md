# Clinical Ethics Research Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from potential participants about their interest in participating in a clinical research study. It covers important details about the study, the participant's personal information, potential risks and benefits, participation requirements, and confidentiality and privacy concerns. By completing this form, participants will be providing informed consent to participate in the study.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided study information, including the study title, description, and procedures.
2. Provide your personal information, such as your name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and time commitment required.
3. Consider potential risks and benefits associated with participation.
4. Choose how you would like your data to be used.
5. Confirm your understanding of your rights as a participant, including the right to withdraw at any time and the understanding that participation is voluntary.
6. Finally, confirm your agreement to participate and your understanding of the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the study. This will help you understand what the research study is about.
* **Principal Investigator** (`principal_investigator`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the principal investigator or researcher in charge of the study.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, `text`, required): Answer the question "What is the study about?" to give a brief overview of the study.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be referred to in the study.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Description of Potential Risks** (`risks_description`, `text`, required): Consider the potential risks and discomforts associated with participation.
* **Severity of Potential Risks** (`severity_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the severity level of the potential risks.
* **General Benefits to Society** (`general_benefits`, `text`, required): Consider the general benefits this study may bring to society.
* **Time Commitment Required** (`time_commitment`, `text`, required): Enter the hours or visits per week/month required for participation.
* **Study Procedures** (`procedures`, `text`, required): Describe what you will be asked to do as a participant.
* **How Data Will Be Protected and Stored** (`data_protection`, `text`, required): Explain how your data will be protected and stored.
* **How Data Will Be Used** (`data_usage`, `text`, required): Explain how your data will be used in the study.
* **Voluntary Participation** (`voluntary_participation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that your participation is voluntary.
* **Right to Withdraw** (`withdraw_rights`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand you can withdraw at any time.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and understand this consent form.
* **Participation Agreement** (`participation_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to participate in this research study.
