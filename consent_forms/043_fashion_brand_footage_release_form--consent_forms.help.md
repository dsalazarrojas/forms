To provide a clear understanding of the Fashion Brand Footage Release Form, here's an overview of its purpose and usage guidelines:

This form serves as a legal agreement between the fashion brand and the submitter, allowing them to release footage of their brand for use in marketing campaigns and social media content. The form ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions under which the footage will be used. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and truthfully, as it will be used to establish a binding contract.

Before filling out this form, please ensure:

* You're aware of the purpose and scope of the agreement.
* You're authorized to represent the brand or company.
* You're not duplicating or submitting the same footage multiple times.

### Fashion Brand Footage Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain your consent for using footage of your fashion brand in various marketing campaigns and social media content.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you're submitting truthful information about your brand and company.
3. Sign and date the form to acknowledge your agreement.
4. Submit the completed form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input** (User Input, text, required): A brief description of the footage being submitted.
* **fashion_brand_name** (Fashion Brand Name, text, optional): The name of your fashion brand.
* **campaign_type** (Campaign Type, select_one, required): Choose whether the footage is for a:
	+ 'Yes': Marketing campaign
	+ 'No': Social content
* **campaign_platforms** (Campaign Platforms, select_multiple, required): Select the platforms where the footage will be used (e.g., 'Yes' for Instagram, 'Yes' for Facebook, etc.).
* **consent_for_campaigns** (Consent for Campaigns, select_multiple, required): Grant permission for the footage to be used in marketing campaigns:
	+ 'Yes' for marketing campaigns
	+ 'No' for social content
* **consent_for_social_content** (Consent for Social Content, select_multiple, optional): Grant permission for the footage to be used in social content (optional):
	+ 'Yes' for Instagram
	+ 'Yes' for Facebook
	+ 'No' for other platforms
* **brand_promotions** (Brand Promotions, select_multiple, optional): Grant permission for the brand to promote your products or services:
	+ 'Yes' for email marketing
	+ 'Yes' for influencer partnerships
	+ 'No' for other channels
* **recorded_on** (Recorded On, date, optional): The date the footage was recorded.
* **duration_of_recording** (Duration of Recording, number, optional): The length of the recording in minutes.
* **file_types** (File Types, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of files associated with the footage:
	+ 'Yes' for images
	+ 'Yes' for videos
	+ 'No' for other file types
* **submitter_info** (Submitter Information, text, required): A brief description of the submitter and their role in the brand.
* **submitter_email** (Submitter Email, email, required): The submitter's email address.
* **submitter_phone** (Submitter Phone, text, optional): The submitter's phone number.
* **submitter_signature** (Submitter Signature, text, required): The submitter's handwritten or digital signature.
* **submitter_agreement** (Submitter Agreement, note, required): A statement of agreement to the terms of the contract.
* **company_name** (Company Name, text, required): The name of the company or entity submitting the footage.
* **job_title** (Job Title, text, required): The job title of the representative submitting the form.
* **company_address** (Company Address, text, required): The address of the company or entity submitting the form.
* **company_phone** (Company Phone, text, required): The phone number of the company or entity submitting the form.
* **company_email** (Company Email, email, required): The email address of the company or entity submitting the form.
* **company_tax_id** (Company Tax ID, text, optional): The company's tax ID number (if applicable).
* **representative_name** (Representative Name, text, required): The name of the representative submitting the form.
* **representative_job_title** (Representative Job Title, text, required): The job title of the representative submitting the form.
* **representative_email** (Representative Email, email, required): The email address of the representative submitting the form.
* **representative_phone** (Representative Phone, text, optional): The phone number of the representative submitting the form.
