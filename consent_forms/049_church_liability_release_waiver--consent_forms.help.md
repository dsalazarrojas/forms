# Church Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Liability Release Waiver is a document that protects the church and its staff from potential legal liabilities in case of accidents or injuries during church activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Make sure to sign the form with your full legal name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name (participant_name_waiver)** (*required*): Please fill out your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Date of Birth (participant_dob_waiver)** (*required*): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Parent/Guardian Name (parent_name_if_minor)**: If you are a minor, please fill out the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Activity or Event (waiver_activity_event)** (*required*): Describe the activity or event for which you are participating.
* **Assumption of Risk (assumption_of_risk_agree)** (*required*): Please select "I Acknowledge and Agree" to acknowledge the risks of the activity.
* **Release of Liability (release_of_liability_agree)** (*required*): Please select "I Agree" to release the church from liability for accidental injuries or damages.
* **Emergency Medical Treatment (medical_treatment_consent_waiver)** (*required*): Please select "I Consent" to consent to emergency medical treatment if necessary.
* **Photography (photo_release_agree_waiver)** (*required*): Please select "True" to consent to photography for church use.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name_waiver)** (*required*): Fill out the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone_waiver)** (*required*): Fill out the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Severe Allergies or Medical Conditions (allergies_medical_notes_waiver)**: Please list any severe allergies or medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Health Insurance Provider and Policy Number (insurance_provider_policy)**: If you have health insurance, please list your provider and policy number.
* **Signature (legal_signature_name_waiver)** (*required*): Please sign the form with your full legal name.
* **Date Signed (date_signed_waiver)** (*required*): Enter the date of signing in YYYY-MM-DD format.
