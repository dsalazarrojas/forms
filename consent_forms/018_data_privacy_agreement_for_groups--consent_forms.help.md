# Data Privacy Agreement For Groups - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a group's data privacy agreement, including their data handling practices, data protection regulations, and contact information. The form is designed to ensure that groups have a clear understanding of their data handling responsibilities and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Group Name in the designated field.
2. Check the boxes for Data Handling Practices, Personal Information, Data Protection Regulations, Group Members, Data Collection Methods, Data Storage Security, Data Disposal, Data Breach Notification, Data Protection Officer, Data Breach Response, Data Protection Training, and Signature to indicate your group's practices.
3. Enter the contact person's details in the Contact Person, Contact E-mail, Contact Phone, and Contact Address fields.
4. Enter the Group Size and Age Range (if applicable).
5. Enter the Date Signed, Date Effective, and Date Reviewed fields to indicate the agreement's start and end dates.
6. Sign the agreement by selecting "Yes" for Signature and Terms And Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Group Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the group.
* **Data Handling Practices** (2, note, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has a clear understanding of their data handling practices.
* **Personal Information** (3, note, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group handles personal information.
* **Data Protection Regulations** (4, note, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group follows data protection regulations.
* **Group Members** (5, select_multiple, required): Check this box to indicate that your group has members.
* **Data Collection Methods** (6, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the methods your group uses to collect data.
* **Data Storage Security** (7, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has secure data storage in place.
* **Data Disposal** (8, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has procedures for disposing of data.
* **Data Breach Notification** (9, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has procedures for notifying of data breaches.
* **Data Protection Officer** (10, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has a data protection officer.
* **Data Breach Response** (11, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has procedures for responding to data breaches.
* **Data Protection Training** (12, select_multiple, optional): Check this box to indicate that your group has provided data protection training.
* **Contact Person** (13, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact E-mail** (14, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (15, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address** (16, text, required): Enter the contact person's address.
* **Group Size** (17, number, required): Enter the number of people in the group.
* **Age Range** (18, number, optional): Enter the age range of the group members.
* **Data Privacy Agreement Version** (19, text, optional): Enter the version of the data privacy agreement.
* **Date Signed** (20, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Date Effective** (21, date, required): Enter the date the agreement becomes effective.
* **Date Reviewed** (22, date, required): Enter the date the agreement was reviewed.
* **Signature** (23, select_one, required): Check this box to indicate that the contact person has signed the agreement.
* **Terms And Conditions** (24, select_one, required): Check this box to indicate that the contact person has read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Notes** (25, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the group's data privacy agreement.
