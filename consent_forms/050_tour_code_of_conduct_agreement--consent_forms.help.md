# Tour Code Of Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Code Of Conduct Agreement form is a document that participants must complete to acknowledge and agree to the rules and regulations of a tour or event. This form helps ensure that all participants are aware of their responsibilities and expectations during the tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the information required.
2. Fill in all the necessary fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Consent
• **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required: false): This field is where the participant confirms their understanding and agreement to the code of conduct. Please fill in your response to indicate your understanding and acceptance.

### Emergency Contact Name
• **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Relationship To Participant
• **Relationship To Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or more relationships that the emergency contact has to the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Other).

### Contact Phone
• **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Contact Email
• **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.

### Participant Signature
• **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required: false): This field is intended for the participant's signature, but it seems to be a note field. It's likely not necessary for participants to sign, and this field might be for staff/staff's use.

### Staff/Operator Signature
• **Staff/Operator Signature** (`staff_signature`, note, required: false): This field is intended for staff or operators to sign, confirming they've received the participant's consent.

### Date
• **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which this form is submitted.

### Time
• **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time on which this form is submitted.

### Form Submitted By
• **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, required: true): Select the tool used to generate this form (e.g., Assigned Tool, Other).

### Participant Name
• **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: true): Fill in the name of the participant.

Please note that the form's required fields are **Participant Consent** and **Form Submitted By**. Review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
