# Tooth Gem Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect informed consent from patients regarding the "Tooth Gem" procedure. It's essential to review and understand the information presented here before proceeding with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and provide detailed answers. The form is divided into several sections, each asking about specific aspects of the "Tooth Gem" procedure. Please fill out all required fields, but you can skip optional ones if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tooth Gem Consent** (`tooth_gem_consent`): This is the primary field for the patient's consent statement. Please provide a detailed written statement indicating your understanding and agreement to the procedure.
* **Risks and Contraindications** (`risks_and_contraindications`): Describe any medical conditions or concerns you have that may affect your suitability for the "Tooth Gem" procedure.
* **Procedure** (`procedure`): Outline the specific steps and details of the "Tooth Gem" procedure, as explained by your doctor or dentist.
* **Aftercare Requirements** (`aftercare_requirements`): Indicate any specific aftercare instructions or follow-up visits you need to keep in mind after the procedure.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`): Confirm that you've received and understand the information provided about the "Tooth Gem" procedure and its risks.
* **Legal Protection** (`legal_protection`): Acknowledge that you've been informed about the potential risks and benefits of the procedure and that you're aware of the patient's rights.
* **Risks and Side Effects** (`risks_and_side_effects`): Describe any concerns or risks you're aware of regarding the "Tooth Gem" procedure.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Sign and date this form to confirm that you've read and understand the information provided.
* **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`): The healthcare provider will sign this field to confirm that they've explained the procedure and risks to you, and that you've provided informed consent.
