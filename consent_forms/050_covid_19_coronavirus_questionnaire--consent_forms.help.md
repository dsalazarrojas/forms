# Covid 19 (CoronaVirus) Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals about their recent experiences with COVID-19, including their current health condition, recent travel history, and contact history. This questionnaire is designed to help us better understand the spread and impact of the virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully**: Take a few minutes to read through each page to understand what information is being requested.
2. **Fill out each field carefully**: Make sure to answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. **Submit your response**: Once you have completed all the fields, click submit to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (Page 1)**:
	+ **Contact Details (contact_details)**: Please enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Current Health Condition (Page 2)**:
	+ **Current Health Condition (current_health_condition)**: Describe your current health condition, including any symptoms or illnesses you are experiencing.
* **Recent Travel History (Page 3)**:
	+ **Recent Travel (recent_travel)**: Check the box (or boxes) that best describe your recent travel history, including any international travel or exposure to others who have been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Recent Contact History (Page 4)**:
	+ **Recent Contact History (recent_contact_history)**: Describe any recent close contact with individuals who have been diagnosed with COVID-19 or have been in contact with someone who has been diagnosed.

## Tips
- Please take your time to answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
- If you have any concerns or questions after completing the form, please contact us.
