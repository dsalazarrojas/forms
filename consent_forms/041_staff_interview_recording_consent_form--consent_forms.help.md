# Staff Interview Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Interview Recording Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from staff members for recording interviews, meetings, or discussions in a professional setting. This form helps ensure that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the recording process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you're filling it out for a relevant purpose.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Ensure you've answered all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Interview Recording Consent Form 1** (`staff_interview_recording_consent_form_1`, text, required): *Enter a brief description of the interview or discussion you want to record, including the date, time, and location.*
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required): *Select the type of recording you want to make: Audio, Video, or Both.*
* **Recording Method** (`recording_method`, select_one, required): *Choose how the recording will be done: Live or Pre-Recorded.*
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, select_multiple, required): *Specify where the recording will take place: On-site or Off-site, or Both.*
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): *Enter the date you're requesting consent for the recording.*
* **Recorded By** (`recorded_by`, email, required): *Enter your email address associated with your account.*
* **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required): *Choose whether you want to seek manager approval for the recording: Approval or No Approval.*
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, select_one, required): *Select whether the staff member consents to the recording: Consent or No Consent.*
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, text, required): *Add any additional notes or comments from the manager.*
* **Recording Consent Date** (`recording_consent_date`, date, required): *Enter the date of consent for the recording.*
* **Recorded On** (`recorded_on`, date, required): *Enter the date the recording actually took place.*
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`, text, required): *Enter the name of the staff member being recorded.*
* **Recording Consent Note** (`recording_consent_note`, text, required): *Add any additional notes or comments for the recording consent.*
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): *Enter the name of the manager associated with the recording.*
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): *Enter the department associated with the recording.*
* **Staff Department** (`staff_department`, text, required): *Enter the department of the staff member being recorded.*
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required): *Enter the email of the manager associated with the recording.*
* **Recording Consent Email** (`recording_consent_email`, email, required): *Enter the email associated with the recording consent.*
* **Recorded By Email** (`recorded_by_email`, email, required): *Enter the email associated with the recording.*
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required): *Enter the email of the staff member being recorded.*
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, required): *Enter the phone number of the staff member being recorded.*
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required): *Enter the phone number of the manager associated with the recording.*
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`, text, required): *Enter the phone number associated with the recording department.*
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): *Enter the date you're requesting consent for the recording.*
* **Recording Method** (`recording_method`, select_one, required): *Choose how the recording will be done: Live, Pre-Recorded, or Both.*
