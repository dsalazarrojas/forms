# Press Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in the authorization of press releases by collecting relevant information for verification and publication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. If you are releasing an image, provide it in the `image_release` field.
5. Sign your name in the `signature` field.
6. Enter the date and time of the press event in the `press_date` and `press_time` fields.
7. Enter the publish date and time of the press release in the `publish_date` and `publish_time` fields.
8. Enter the name of the person or organization publishing the press release in the `published_by` field.
9. Select the date of publication for the press release in the `published_at` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information (general_info)** (`text`, optional): This field is for providing any additional general information about the press release.
- **Name (name)** (`text`, optional): Enter your name to be associated with this press release.
- **Email (email)** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone (phone)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for any further inquiries.
- **Image Release (image_release)** (`text`, optional): If this press release includes an image, please provide it in this field.
- **Signature (signature)** (`note`, optional): Sign your name to authenticate this press release.
- **Press Date (press_date)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of the press event or conference.
- **Press Time (press_time)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of the press event or conference.
- **Publish Date (publish_date)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of publication for the press release.
- **Publish Time (publish_time)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of publication for the press release.
- **Published By (published_by)** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization publishing this press release.
- **Published At (published_at)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of publication for this press release.
