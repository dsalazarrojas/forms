# Covid Questionnaire For Clients - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Questionnaire For Clients form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their exposure to COVID-19, their symptoms, contact tracing, travel history, exposure risk, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each question and select the relevant options for the client's situation.
2.  For each field, choose one of the options that best matches the client's information.
3.  Fill out the text fields as required and enter the date for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Has the client been exposed to COVID-19
*   **Has the client been exposed to COVID-19** (`exposure_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This field asks whether the client has been exposed to COVID-19. Select 'Yes' if the client has been exposed, and 'No' otherwise.

### Has the client experienced COVID-19 symptoms
*   **Has the client experienced COVID-19 symptoms** (`symptoms_exposure`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This field asks whether the client has experienced symptoms of COVID-19. Select 'Yes' if the client has experienced symptoms, and 'No' otherwise.

### Has the client been in contact with someone with COVID-19
*   **Has the client been in contact with someone with COVID-19** (`contact_tracing`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This field asks whether the client has been in contact with someone who has been exposed to COVID-19. Select 'Yes' if the client has been in contact, and 'No' otherwise.

### Has the client traveled recently
*   **Has the client traveled recently** (`travel_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This field asks whether the client has traveled recently. Select 'Yes' if the client has traveled recently, and 'No' otherwise.

### What is the client's exposure risk
*   **What is the client's exposure risk** (`exposure_risk`, `select_one`, optional): 
    This field asks about the client's exposure risk level. Select 'Low', 'Medium', or 'High' based on the client's situation.

### Client response
*   **Client response** (`client_response`, `text`, optional): 
    This field is for the client to provide any additional comments or information they want to share with the organization.

### Business response
*   **Business response** (`business_response`, `text`, optional): 
    This field is for the organization to provide any additional comments or information related to the client's situation.

### Follow up date
*   **Follow up date** (`follow_up`, `date`, optional): 
    This field is for the organization to schedule a follow-up date with the client.

### Contact number
*   **Contact number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): 
    This field is for the organization to enter their contact number for follow-up purposes.
