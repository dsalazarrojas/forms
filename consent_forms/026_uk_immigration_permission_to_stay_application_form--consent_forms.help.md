# UK Immigration Permission To Stay Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals wishing to apply for permission to stay in the UK. It is essential to ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Please enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide any necessary documentation (e.g., ID, passport, etc.) in the 'Document 1' field.
4. Enter the information for 'Document 2' if required.
5. If applicable, please sign and date the form to confirm your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Document 1** (`document1`, text, required): Upload or enter details of the required document (e.g., ID, passport, etc.). Ensure the document is clear and legible.
* **Document 2** (`document2`, text, required): If required, enter details of the second document needed for the application.
* **Signature 1** (`signature_1`, text, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your identity.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Attach clear and legible copies of any documents required.
- Use your valid email address for communication purposes.
- If you are unsure about any field or have further questions, please contact our support team.
