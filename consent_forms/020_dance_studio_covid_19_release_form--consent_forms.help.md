# Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Studio COVID 19 Release Form is a document that collects personal information and waiver/consent from students, parents, or guardians before participating in dance studio activities. It's designed to ensure that individuals understand the risks associated with COVID-19 and take necessary precautions to minimize the risk of infection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, parent's name (if applicable), and contact information.
2. Enter your date of birth.
3. Select the terms and conditions you agree to, including understanding COVID-19 risks and taking necessary precautions.
4. Provide emergency contact and phone number information.
5. Waive the dance studio's liability by selecting the correct option in the waiver section.
6. Declare your awareness and agreement to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Parent Name (Parent Name)**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian if you are under 18.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)**: Enter your contact number for emergency purposes.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **I consent to the following terms and conditions (Consent)**: Select all statements that apply to you. You must agree to all statements for this form to be considered valid.
  - I understand that COVID-19 is a highly contagious and potentially deadly virus and that it is possible to contract it through human interaction
  - I will take all necessary precautions to reduce the risk of infection
  - I will be on time for my scheduled classes
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone Number (Emergency Phone Number)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Waiver (Waiver)**: This is a declaration that you waive the dance studio's liability. Select "I am aware of and agree to the terms and conditions" to waive liability.
* **Declaration (Declaration)**: Declare your awareness and agreement to the terms and conditions. Select "I am aware of and agree to the terms and conditions" to continue.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, ask the dance studio staff for assistance.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before selecting the statements that apply to you.
