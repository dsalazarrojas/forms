# new_guest_consent_form_salon - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for guests to provide their consent to receive promotional and marketing materials from the salon. The form is designed to be completed by new guests who visit the salon. It is not intended for existing clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill in your **Guest Name** in the Guest Name field.
2. Enter your **Address** in the address field.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the phone_number field.
4. Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions in the **Policies Agreement** field.
5. Enter your **Email** in the email field.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address, including street number and postcode.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Policies Agreement** (note, optional): This is an acknowledgement that you have read and understand our policies.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you for updates and promotions.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth as per the format mm/dd/yyyy.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any).
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
