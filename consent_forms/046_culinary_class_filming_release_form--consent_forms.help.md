# Culinary Class Filming Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Class Filming Release Form is a document used to obtain consent from participants in a culinary class for filming purposes. This form is essential to ensure that all participants have given their consent for being filmed and recorded during the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the first name field with your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
3. Provide your email address for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Indicate whether or not you grant permission for filming and recording during the class.
6. If you grant permission, select the date and time of the filming.
7. Enter the location where the filming will take place.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
9. If you are representing a school, fill in the school name, email, and phone number.
10. Enter the name of the event organizer, their email, and phone number.
11. Provide the date and time of the event.
12. Enter the location of the event.
13. Describe the event in a few sentences.
14. Indicate whether or not you grant consent for the participant to be filmed.
15. Select the date and time of the participant's consent.
16. Add any notes or comments about the participant's consent.
17. If you are a chef, fill in your bio, email, and phone number.
18. Enter your name, email, and phone number for the chef.
