# Pet Dental Anesthetic Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Dental Anesthetic Consent Form is used to obtain the necessary consent from pet owners for anesthetic procedures to be performed on their pets. This form ensures that the owner has given their consent for the treatment and provides essential details about the pet and the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information and answers ready before starting the form.
* Read each field carefully and fill it in accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Dental Anesthetic Consent Form** (`pet_dental_anesthetic_consent_form`, text, required)
This field is the title of the form that is used to identify the purpose of the form.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional)
This field is used to record the owner's consent for the anesthetic procedure. Select "Yes" if you give your consent, "No" if you do not, and "Unknown" if you are unsure or have questions.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, text, required)
The owner's signature is required to confirm that they have read and understood the form and are giving their consent.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required)
Enter the date on which the owner is giving their consent.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`vet_signature`, text, required)
The veterinarian's signature is required to confirm that they have obtained the owner's consent and will perform the anesthetic procedure.
* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the clinic where the procedure will be performed.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required)
Enter the physical address of the clinic where the procedure will be performed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required)
Enter the phone number of the clinic.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
Enter the email address of the clinic.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a pen or keyboard ready to fill in the form.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask the veterinarian or clinic staff.
