# Credit Check Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from individuals for a credit check. It is typically required for employment, loan, or credit application purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your form title in the **Form Title** field.
2.  Provide your **Client Information**.
3.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
4.  Enter your **Social Security Number** in the format `XXX-XX-XXXX`.
5.  Enter your **Address**.
6.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
7.  Enter your **Email** address.
8.  Select your **Consent** to a credit check by choosing either 'Yes' or 'No'.
9.  Select the **Credit Bureau Agency** you want to use for the credit check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`Form Title`, text, required): Enter the title of this form.
* **Client Information** (`Client Information`, text, required): Provide your information for identity verification.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Social Security Number** (`Social Security Number`, text, optional): Enter your social security number in the format `XXX-XX-XXXX`.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, optional): Provide your address for identity verification.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Consent** (`I consent to a credit check`, select_multiple, true): Choose 'Yes' if you consent to a credit check, 'No' otherwise.
* **Credit Bureau Agency** (`Credit Bureau Agency`, select_one, false): Select the credit bureau agency you want to use for the credit check.
