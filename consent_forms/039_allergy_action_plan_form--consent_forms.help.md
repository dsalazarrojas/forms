# Allergy Action Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an allergy action plan, which is a crucial document for individuals with allergies to provide guidance on how to manage and respond to allergic reactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information, including the patient's medical history, allergies, and emergency contact details.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, taking care to include all relevant information.
3. Review the form with a medical professional before using it in emergency situations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent/Guardian 1 Name** (`parent_guardian_1_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian who will be responsible for the patient.
* **Parent/Guardian 1 Phone** (`parent_guardian_1_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the patient's full home address.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the treating physician.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the physician.
* **Specific Allergies** (`specific_allergies`, text, required): List all the patient's specific allergies.
* **Allergy Triggers** (`allergy_triggers`, select_multiple, required): Select all the triggers that cause the patient's allergic reactions (e.g., food ingestion, food contact, insect stings, etc.).
* **Mild Symptoms** (`typical_symptoms_mild`, text, required): Describe the patient's typical mild symptoms of allergic reactions.
* **Severe Symptoms** (`typical_symptoms_severe`, text, required): Describe the patient's typical severe symptoms of allergic reactions.
* **History of Anaphylaxis** (`anaphylaxis_history`, select_one, required): Indicate if the patient has experienced anaphylaxis before.
* **Prescribed Medications** (`prescribed_medications`, text, required): List all medications prescribed to the patient.
* **Epinephrine Auto-Injector** (`epinephrine_auto_injector`, text, required): Describe the type and dosage of epinephrine auto-injector used.
* **Epinephrine Dose** (`epinephrine_dose`, text, required): Enter the amount of epinephrine used.
* **Number of Injectors Available** (`number_of_injectors`, number, required): Enter the number of epinephrine auto-injectors available.
* **Injector Locations** (`injector_locations`, text, required): Describe where the epinephrine auto-injectors are kept.
* **Antihistamine Dosage** (`antihistamine_dosage`, text, required): Describe the type and amount of antihistamine used.
* **Asthma Medications** (`asthma_medications`, text, optional): If applicable, list the patient's asthma medications.
* **Treatment for Mild Reactions** (`mild_reaction_treatment`, text, required): Describe the treatment for mild allergic reactions.
* **Treatment for Severe Reactions** (`severe_reaction_treatment`, text, required): Describe the treatment for severe allergic reactions.
* **When to Administer Epinephrine** (`when_to_administer_epinephrine`, text, required): Describe when to administer epinephrine.
* **Emergency Instructions** (`emergency_instructions`, text, required): Describe what to do after epinephrine administration.
* **Call 911 Instructions** (`call_911`, select_one, required): Select when to call 911 (always, if symptoms persist, or based on severity).
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_1`, text, required): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact 1.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of the emergency contact 2.
* **School/Workplace Instructions** (`school_workplace_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any special instructions for school or workplace.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, required): List any dietary restrictions or food allergies.
* **Cross-Contamination Concerns** (`cross_contamination_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any cross-contamination concerns.
* **Can Patient Self-Administer?** (`patient_can_self_administer`, select_one, optional): Indicate if the patient can self-administer epinephrine.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`, select_one, required): Sign and date the form indicating authorization.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, select_one, required): Sign and date the form indicating authorization.
* **Physician Signature Date** (`physician_signature_date`, date, required): Date signed by the physician.
* **Parent Signature Date** (`parent_signature_date`, date, required): Date signed by the parent or guardian.

Note: Please review and understand each field before completing the form. If unsure about any field, consult a medical professional for guidance.
