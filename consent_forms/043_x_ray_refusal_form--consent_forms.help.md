# x_ray_refusal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients or individuals to refuse X-ray exams or procedures. It collects information about the patient's identity, medical condition, and reasons for refusal.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Make sure to enter all required and optional fields.
2. Review the options in the "Consent Given" field carefully and select the option that best applies to you.
3. If you have any issues or need help while filling out the form, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or medical records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Medical Condition**: Briefly describe your current medical condition, if any.
* **Reason for Refusal**: Explain why you are refusing the X-ray exam or procedure.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date on which you are signing this form (in the standard date format, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Signature**: Enter your signature in this field.
* **Consent Given**: Review the provided options and select the one that best applies to you:
	+ I have read and understand the terms
	+ I have not read and do not understand the terms
	+ I have read but do not consent
	+ I do not understand the terms

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team. We are here to help.
