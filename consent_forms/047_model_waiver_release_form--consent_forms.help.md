# Model Waiver & Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Model Waiver & Release Form is a document used to obtain consent from models to use their images in photographs. This form helps ensure that models are aware of how their images will be used and are comfortable with it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the model waiver and release form in the "Model Waiver & Release Form" field.
2. Select "Release to use image" from the dropdown menu to indicate the reason for using the model's image.
3. Provide the photographer's information in the "Photographer Information" field. This could include details such as their name and contact number.
4. Enter the photographer's name in the "Photographer name" field.
5. Provide the photographer's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter the model's email address in the "Email" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Model Release" field to indicate if the model has given their consent to use their image.
8. Enter the date of when the consent was given in the "Date" field.
9. Enter the time of when the consent was given in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Model Waiver & Release Form** (`title`, text, required):
	+ Enter the title of the waiver and release form.
* **Release to use image** (`release_to_use_image`, select_one, required):
	+ Select "Release to use image" from the dropdown menu to indicate the reason for using the model's image.
* **Photographer Information** (`photographer_information`, text, required):
	+ Enter information about the photographer, such as their name and contact number.
* **Photographer name** (`photographer_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter the contact number of the photographer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter the email address of the model or photographer.
* **Model Release** (`model_release`, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select "Yes" if the model has given their consent to use their image, otherwise select "No".
* **Date** (`date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of when the consent was given.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required):
	+ Enter the time of when the consent was given.
