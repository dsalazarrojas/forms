# Hormone Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's hormone therapy treatment and to obtain their consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information (email and phone number).
2. Provide information about your medical record number and any medical condition you are being treated for.
3. Identify your hormone therapy provider and the type of hormone therapy you are receiving.
4. Specify the dosage and start date of your treatment.
5. Provide the expected end date of your treatment.
6. Sign and date the consent section.
7. Include your doctor's name and the name of the hospital where you are receiving treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe your medical condition.
* **Hormone Therapy Provider** (`hormone_therapy_provider`, text, optional): Identify the healthcare provider who will be administering your hormone therapy treatment.
* **Hormone Type** (`hormone_type`, text, optional): Specify the type of hormone therapy you are receiving.
* **Dosage** (`dosage`, text, optional): Enter the dosage of your hormone therapy treatment.
* **Treatment Start Date** (`treatment_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your hormone therapy treatment started.
* **Treatment End Date** (`treatment_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the expected end date of your hormone therapy treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are giving consent for your hormone therapy treatment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where you are receiving treatment.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
