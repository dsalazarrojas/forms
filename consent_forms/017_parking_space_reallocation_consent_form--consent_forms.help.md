# Parking Space Reallocation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain reallocation consent from users for parking spaces within a parking lot.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and ensure you have the authority to fill it out and that the information provided is accurate.
2. Fill out the form with your details and consent to the reallocation of a parking space.
3. Complete all required fields (marked as "required") as they are necessary for the reallocation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Consent to Parking Space Reallocation** (`consent_to_parking_space_reallocation`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate your consent to the parking space reallocation. This field is required and must be completed in order to proceed.
- **Parking Lot Name** (`parking_lot_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the parking lot where the space is located. This field is not required but provides additional context.
- **Parking Space Number** (`parking_space_number`, number, not required): Enter the number of the parking space being reallocated. This field is not required but helps track the specific space.
- **Parking Space Type** (`parking_space_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of parking space from the provided options. This field is not required but provides additional details.
- **Parking Lot Address** (`parking_lot_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the parking lot where the space is located. This field is not required but provides additional context.
- **Parking Space Description** (`parking_space_description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of the parking space being reallocated. This field is not required but helps with future reference.
- **Parking Space Size** (`parking_space_size`, number, not required): Enter the size of the parking space. This field is not required but helps track the space dimensions.
- **Parking Space Location** (`parking_space_location`, select_one, not required): Select the location of the parking space from the provided options. This field is not required but provides additional details.
- **Parking Space Status** (`parking_space_status`, select_multiple, not required): Select the status of the parking space from the provided options. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Parking Space Image** (`parking_space_image`, text, not required): You can upload an image of the parking space. This field is not required but provides additional visual context.
- **Parking Space Notes** (`parking_space_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the parking space. This field is not required but helps with future reference.
- **Parking Space Phone** (`parking_space_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number associated with the parking space. This field is not required but provides additional contact information.
- **Parking Space Email** (`parking_space_email`, email, not required): Enter the email associated with the parking space. This field is not required but provides additional contact information.
- **Parking Space Date** (`parking_space_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the parking space reallocation. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Parking Space Time** (`parking_space_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the parking space reallocation. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Parking Space Location 2** (`parking_space_location_2`, select_one, not required): Select the location of the second parking space. This field is not required but provides additional details.
- **Parking Space Status 2** (`parking_space_status_2`, select_multiple, not required): Select the status of the second parking space from the provided options. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Parking Space Image 2** (`parking_space_image_2`, text, not required): You can upload an image of the second parking space. This field is not required but provides additional visual context.
- **Parking Space Notes 2** (`parking_space_notes_2`, note, not required): Add any additional notes about the second parking space. This field is not required but helps with future reference.
- **Parking Space Phone 2** (`parking_space_phone_2`, text, not required): Enter the phone number associated with the second parking space. This field is not required but provides additional contact information.
- **Parking Space Email 2** (`parking_space_email_2`, email, not required): Enter the email associated with the second parking space. This field is not required but provides additional contact information.
- **Parking Space Date 2** (`parking_space_date_2`, date, not required): Enter the date of the second parking space reallocation. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Parking Space Time 2** (`parking_space_time_2`, time, not required): Enter the time of the second parking space reallocation. This field is not required but helps with tracking.
- **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, not required): This field is not required but provides additional information about who submitted the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the authority to fill out this form and that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review all fields carefully and provide detailed answers to help with the reallocation process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator for assistance.
