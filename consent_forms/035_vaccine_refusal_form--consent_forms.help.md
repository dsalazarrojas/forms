# Vaccine Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document a patient's refusal to receive a vaccine, taking into account their medical history, allergies, and other health factors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each section accurately and completely.
2. Enter the patient's basic contact information and demographic details in the "Patient Information" section.
3. Select any medical conditions the patient has been diagnosed with in the "Medical History" sections.
4. Select any medications or tests the patient is currently undergoing or has undergone in the "Medications" and "Medical Tests" sections.
5. Indicate any allergies the patient has in the "Allergies" section.
6. Select the person who should be contacted in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact" section.
7. Sign the form by selecting "True" or "False" and entering the date of signing in the "Signature" and "Signature Date" sections.
