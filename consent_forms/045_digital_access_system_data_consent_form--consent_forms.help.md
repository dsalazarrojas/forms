# digital_access_system_data_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user consent for the collection, processing, and storage of data. It is intended to ensure that users understand how their data will be used and that they are willing to provide their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Fill in your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. For the `consent_given` field, select all applicable options to indicate your consent. You may select one or more of the options, depending on whether you consent to the collection, processing, or storage of your data.
4. For the `data_processing` field, select all applicable options to indicate how your data will be processed. You may select one or more of the options, depending on your understanding of data processing.
5. For the `data_storage` field, select all applicable options to indicate where your data will be stored. You may select one or more of the options, depending on your understanding of data storage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name.
- **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name.
- **consent_given** (`consent_given`, select_multiple, required): Indicate your consent for data collection, processing, or storage by selecting all applicable options. You may select one or more of the options: True, False, or Maybe.
  - True: I consent to the collection, processing, and storage of my data.
  - False: I do not consent to the collection, processing, or storage of my data.
  - Maybe: I have some reservations or concerns about data collection, processing, or storage.
- **data_processing** (`data_processing`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how your data will be processed by selecting all applicable options. You may select one or more of the options: Explicitly, Implicitly, or Both.
  - Explicitly: My data will be processed with a clear and transparent method.
  - Implicitly: My data will be processed without a clear and transparent method.
  - Both: My data will be processed with both clear and transparent, and without clear and transparent methods.
- **data_storage** (`data_storage`, select_multiple, required): Indicate where your data will be stored by selecting all applicable options. You may select one or more of the options: True, False.
  - True: My data will be stored on a specific server or location.
  - False: My data will not be stored on a specific server or location.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have understood the purpose and context of this form before completing it.
- Be honest and accurate when indicating your consent and understanding of data processing and storage.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please seek clarification from the relevant authority.
