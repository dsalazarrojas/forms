# Faith Based Counseling Session Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faith Based Counseling Session Consent Form is a document used to gather information and obtain consent from patients before a counseling session. This form helps establish a clear understanding of the patient's personal details, contact information, and consent for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Patient Name** in the "Patient Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Contact Number** in the "Contact Number" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field. This is a required field.
5. If applicable, enter the **Parent Name** in the "Parent Name (if applicable)" field.
6. If applicable, enter the **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Number**, and **Relationship** in the "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Number", and "Relationship" fields respectively.
7. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Consent to Share**, indicating whether you consent to share your information with third parties.
8. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **Consent to Track**, indicating whether you consent to have your progress tracked during the session.
9. Choose the type of **Session Type** (Individual, Couple, Family, or Group) that best describes your counseling needs.
10. In the **Session Details** field, provide a brief description of your goals and expectations for the session.
11. In the **Notes** field, add any additional comments or notes about your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to send you updates and reminders.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Parent Name (if applicable)** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to allow us to share your information with third parties.
* **Consent to Track** (`consent_to_track`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to allow us to track your progress during the session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session that best describes your needs (Individual, Couple, Family, Group).
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your session goals and expectations.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
