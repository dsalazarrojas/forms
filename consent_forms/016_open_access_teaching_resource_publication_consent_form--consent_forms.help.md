# Open Access Teaching Resource Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect consent from the creators of a teaching resource, such as course packs, syllabi, or other educational materials, for publication through open access channels. This ensures that the creators understand and agree to the terms and conditions of publication, including copyright and sharing agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Name**: This field requires your name to be associated with the consent form submission.
2. **Role**: Select your role related to the resource, such as Instructor, Department Member, Department Chair, or Other (please specify).
3. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
4. **Phone Number**: This field is optional but can be used for contact purposes.
5. **Department**: If applicable, enter your department affiliation.
6. **Permission**: Select whether permission is granted for publication ('True'), not granted ('False'), or if permission is conditional ('Conditional'). Please provide details if permission is conditional.
7. **Conditional Details**: If permission is conditional, provide a brief explanation.
8. **Resource Name**: Enter a name for the resource to be published.
9. **Resource Description**: Provide a brief description of the resource to be published.
10. **Resource Type**: Select the type of resource, such as Course Pack, Syllabus, Lecture Notes, Presentation, Dataset, or Other (please specify).
11. **Resource Version**: Enter the version of the resource to be published.
12. **Publication Date**: Select the date the resource was published or will be published.
13. **Publication Status**: Choose the status of the resource, such as Published, In Review, Under Review, In Progress, or Not Applicable.
14. **Resource URL**: This field is optional but can be used to provide a link to the resource.
15. **Resource License**: Choose the license under which the resource will be published, such as CC BY, CC BY-NC, CC BY-ND, CC BY-ND-NC, CC BY-SA, CC BY-ND-SA, CC0, or Public Domain.
16. **License Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes related to the license.
17. **Copyright Holders**: This field is optional but can be used to list individuals or organizations holding the copyright.
18. **Copyright Years**: Enter the years the copyright is valid.
19. **Copyright Note**: Provide any additional notes about the copyright.
20. **Sharing Agreement**: This field is optional but can be used to specify any sharing agreements related to the resource.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have obtained necessary permissions and understand the terms and conditions of the resource's publication before submitting this form.
* If permission is granted, ensure that you understand and agree to the terms of the resource's publication.
* If permission is conditional, provide clear details about the condition and any additional requirements.
* Review this form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
