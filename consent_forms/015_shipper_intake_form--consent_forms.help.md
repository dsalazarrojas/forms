# Shipper Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from shippers who wish to transport goods on our network. The form captures the essential details of the shipper, their company, address, and the goods they are transporting. This information will help us to efficiently handle shipments and provide accurate quotes for freight services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and clear information.
2. For any field, if you're unsure of the required format or details, use the available hints or contact our support team for assistance.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipper Information** (`shipper_intake_form`, text, required): Enter the shipper's name and any other relevant details.
* **Shipper Company** (`shipper_company`, text, optional): If the shipper is associated with a company, enter the company name. This can help us to provide more accurate quotes for freight services.
* **Company Contact** (`company_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name, phone number, and/or email of the shipper's company.
* **Shipper Address** (`shipper_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipper's address, which will help us to determine the most efficient route for your shipment.
* **Freight Description** (`freight_description`, text, optional): Describe the goods you are transporting, including their dimensions, weight, and any special handling requirements.
* **Shipping Weight** (`shipping_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the shipment, which will help us to provide accurate quotes for freight services.
* **Package Size** (`package_size`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of the package, such as "Length x Width x Height" (in inches). This will help us to determine the best shipping solution for your needs.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any additional instructions or requirements for handling your shipment, such as refrigeration or special equipment needs.
