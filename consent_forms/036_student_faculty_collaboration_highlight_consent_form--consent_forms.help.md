# Student Faculty Collaboration Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from students and faculty members to collaborate on a project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and other required information as a **Student**:
	* **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
	* **Faculty Name**: Enter the name of the faculty member you are collaborating with.
	* **Project Details**: Briefly describe the project you are working on.
	* **Student Signature**: Sign the form as a student.
2. Fill out the form with your name and other required information as a **Faculty Member**:
	* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student you are collaborating with.
	* **Faculty Name**: Enter your name as a faculty member.
	* **Project Details**: Briefly describe the project you are working on.
	* **Faculty Signature**: Sign the form as a faculty member.
3. Select the **Project Start Date** when the project will begin.
4. Select the **Date** when the consent form is being completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your full name as a student.
* **Faculty Name**: Enter the full name of the faculty member you are collaborating with.
* **Project Details**: Describe the project in a brief and clear manner, including its objectives and goals.
* **Student Signature**: Sign the form as a student, indicating your consent to collaborate on the project.
* **Faculty Signature**: Sign the form as a faculty member, indicating their consent to collaborate on the project.
* **Project Start Date**: Select the date when the project will begin.
* **Date**: Select the date when the consent form is being completed.
* **Project Start Date** (optional field): This field is not required but can be useful for tracking the project timeline.

Note: If a field is required, it is marked as such in the form. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
