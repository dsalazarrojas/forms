# Uniform Design Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Uniform Design Release Form is designed to collect uniform design permissions and information from team members. This form helps in maintaining a clear understanding of who has access to specific uniform designs and ensures that team members are aware of their responsibilities and permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill in the required fields only if you are authorized to do so.
3. Upload any supporting documentation if applicable.
4. Provide your team member name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Collect uniform design permissions**: This field is for team members to indicate their permissions for uniform design access. Please fill in the relevant permissions you have or do not have.
* **Design signature**: This is where you can add your digital signature for verification purposes.
* **Collect supporting documentation**: This field is for team members to provide any relevant documentation related to the uniform design.
* **Supporting documentation uploads**: Upload any supporting documentation here. This can be in various file formats (e.g., PDF, image, etc.).
* **Team member name**: Please fill in your name as a team member.
* **Team member title**: Fill in your title as a team member.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are only filling in this form if you are authorized to do so.
* Be accurate when filling in your permissions and documentation.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the form.
* Upload supporting documentation in relevant formats only.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
