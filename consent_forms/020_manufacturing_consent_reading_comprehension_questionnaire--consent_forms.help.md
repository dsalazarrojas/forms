# Manufacturing Consent Reading Comprehension Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a tool for assessing manufacturing consent and reading comprehension among team members. It aims to evaluate the team's ability to understand and agree on specific class concepts and reading group requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each page carefully and answer each question accordingly.
2.  Select the correct response for each question, based on your understanding.
3.  Enter any additional comments or details if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Consent Statement** (`Consent Statement`, `note`, required: true): Please read and agree to the consent statement carefully. This statement outlines the importance of honest and accurate responses.
*   **Class Concepts** (`Class Concepts`, `select_multiple`, required: true): This section asks you to confirm your understanding of specific class concepts. Select all the concepts you are familiar with or have experience with.
*   **Reading Group** (`Reading Group`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the type of reading group you are familiar with or have experience with.
*   **Class Concepts Details** (`Class Concepts Details`, `text`, required: false): If you have experience with class concepts, please provide more details about them.
*   **Reading Group Details** (`Reading Group Details`, `text`, required: false): If you have experience with reading groups, please provide more details about them.
*   **Other (please specify)** (`Other (please specify)`, `text`, required: true): If you have experience with other class concepts or reading groups not listed, please specify them in this field.
*   **Training Session** (`Training Session`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section asks you to confirm your experience with training sessions, if any.
*   **Training Session Details** (`Training Session Details`, `text`, required: false): If you have experience with training sessions, please provide more details about them.
*   **Other (please specify)** (`Other (please specify)`, `text`, required: false): If you have additional comments or details regarding your experience with class concepts, training sessions, or reading groups, please provide them in this field.
*   **Class Concepts Comments** (`Class Concepts Comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or concerns regarding class concepts, please provide them in this field.
*   **Reading Group Comments** (`Reading Group Comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or concerns regarding reading groups, please provide them in this field.
*   **Training Session Comments** (`Training Session Comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or concerns regarding training sessions, please provide them in this field.
*   **Other Comments** (`Other Comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or concerns that do not fit in any of the above categories, please provide them in this field.
