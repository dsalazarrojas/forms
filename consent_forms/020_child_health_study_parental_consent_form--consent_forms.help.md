# Child Health Study Parental Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is to obtain consent from the legal guardian or parent of a child participant in a clinical study. It is used to verify the guardian's consent for their child's participation in the study, including clinical assessments and potential biological sample collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all required fields.
2. Enter the child's name as listed in the recruitment material.
3. Provide your date of birth to verify age eligibility.
4. Enter your name as the person giving consent.
5. Provide your primary phone number for contact.
6. Answer the questions regarding consent for clinical assessments and biological sample collection.
7. Confirm you have read and understood the risk disclosure information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Health Study** (`health_study_name`, required): Enter the name of the study as listed in the recruitment material.
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, required): Enter the full name of the child participant.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_dob`, required): Enter the child's date of birth to verify age eligibility.
* **Guardian Full Name** (`guardian_name`, required): Enter your full name as the guardian or parent giving consent.
* **Guardian Phone Number** (`guardian_phone`, required): Enter your primary phone number for contact.
* **I consent to my child participating in clinical assessments** (`clinical_consent`, required): Select "Yes - I Consent" if you agree to your child participating in clinical assessments.
* **I consent to the collection of biological samples** (`biological_consent`, required): Select "Yes - Consent to samples" if you agree to the collection of biological samples.
* **I have read and understood the risk disclosure** (`risk_understanding`, required): Select "Yes - Read and Understood" to confirm you have read and understood the risk disclosure information.
* **Date of Electronic Signature** (`signature_date`, required): Enter the current date as the date of electronic signature.
