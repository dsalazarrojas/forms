# COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in activities or events where COVID-19 liability is a concern. It ensures that all relevant details are collected in a single, concise format to inform decision-making and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the name of the activity or event you are participating in from the dropdown options in the "Activity or Event Name" field.
7. Enter the date of the activity or event from the "Activity Date" field.
8. Enter the location of the activity or event in the "Activity Location" field.
9. Select the name of the activity or event provider or organization from the "Activity Provider or Organization" field.
10. Answer the questions related to your current health status, COVID-19 symptoms, and recent exposure to COVID-19 in the "Current Health", "Any COVID-19 Symptoms", "Temperature above 100.4F in past 24 hours", "Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days", and "Tested positive for COVID-19 recently" fields, selecting the options that best describe your situation.
11. Select your vaccination status from the "Vaccination Status" field.
12. Answer the questions related to your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the activity or event, including acknowledging the risks of COVID-19, participating voluntarily, and releasing the organization from liability in the "I acknowledge the risks of COVID-19", "I voluntarily participate despite these risks", "I release the organization from liability", and "I agree to indemnify and defend the organization" fields.
13. Finally, select the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address where you can be reached.
* **Activity or Event Name**: Select the name of the activity or event you are participating in from the dropdown options.
* **Activity Date**: Enter the date of the activity or event.
* **Activity Location**: Enter the location of the activity or event.
* **Activity Provider or Organization**: Select the name of the activity or event provider or organization from the options.
* **Current Health**: Select your current health status:
	+ Good Health: You are currently in good health.
	+ Fair Health: You are experiencing some health issues, but not severe.
	+ Poor Health: You are experiencing severe health issues.
* **Any COVID-19 Symptoms**: Select "True" if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, "False" if you are not experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Temperature above 100.4F in past 24 hours**: Select "True" if your temperature has been above 100.4F in the past 24 hours, "False" if not.
* **Exposed to COVID-19 in past 14 days**: Select "True" if you have been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days, "False" if not, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
* **Tested positive for COVID-19 recently**: Select "True" if you have recently tested positive for COVID-19, "False" if not.
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status:
	+ Fully Vaccinated: You have completed the full vaccination series.
	+ Partially Vaccinated: You have not completed the full vaccination series.
	+ Not Vaccinated: You have not received any COVID-19 vaccinations.
* **I acknowledge the risks of COVID-19**: Select "Yes, I Acknowledge" to indicate that you understand and acknowledge the risks of COVID-19.
* **I voluntarily participate despite these risks**: Select "Yes, Voluntary" to indicate that you participate in the activity or event voluntarily.
* **I release the organization from liability**: Select "I Release Them" to indicate that you release the organization from liability.
* **I agree to indemnify and defend the organization**: Select "I Agree" to indicate that you agree to indemnify and defend the organization.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date on which you sign this form.

# Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.
* Make sure to review and understand the terms and conditions related to COVID-19 liability before signing the form.
