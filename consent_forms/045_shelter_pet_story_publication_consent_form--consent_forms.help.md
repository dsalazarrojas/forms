# Shelter Pet Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shelter Pet Story Publication Consent Form is a form used to collect user consent for publishing their shelter pet stories, images, and other media on our platform. It's a crucial step in ensuring that users have control over how their content is shared and used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **User Name** in the first field. This is your identity for our records.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is where we'll contact you for any updates or further requests.
3.  Provide your **Phone** in the third field. This is an optional field for us to contact you in case we need more information or clarification.
4.  Select the **Media Types** you're willing to share. Choose one or more of the options: Image, Video, or Audio.
5.  Choose the **Media Permissions** you're granting. Select one of the options: Upload, Publish, or Share.
6.  Enter a brief **Story Title** that summarizes your pet's story.
7.  Write a more detailed **Story Description** about your pet's life, including their experiences in the shelter.
8.  Enter the **Story Date** when your pet's story took place.
9.  Select the **Story Time** when your pet's story took place.
10. Select the end time of the **Story Time**.
11. Enter the end date of the **Story Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport. This helps us verify your identity.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with updates or requests.
* **Phone**: If you'd like us to contact you for any further information, provide your phone number.
* **Media Types**: Choose one or more of the following: Image, Video, or Audio. This is what type of media you're willing to share about your pet.
* **Media Permissions**: Select one of the following: Upload, Publish, or Share. This determines how your media is used and shared on our platform.
* **Story Title**: Enter a brief and descriptive title for your pet's story.
* **Story Description**: Write a more detailed account of your pet's life, including their experiences in the shelter.
* **Story Date**: Enter the date when your pet's story took place.
* **Story Time**: Select the time when your pet's story took place.
* **Story Time End**: Select the end time of your pet's story.
* **Story Date End**: Enter the end date of your pet's story.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is processed correctly.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to reach out to us for clarification.
* We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in sharing your pet's story with us.
