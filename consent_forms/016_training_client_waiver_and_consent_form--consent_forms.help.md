# Training Client Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent for training purposes. It ensures the client acknowledges and understands their responsibilities and risks associated with training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Client Name** (<code>client_name</code>) with your legal name.
2.  Provide your **Contact Info** (<code>contact_info</code>) including your address, phone number, and email for communication.
3.  Select the type of **Training Type** (<code>training_type</code>) you will be undergoing (Private Session, Small Group Session, Large Group Session, or Seminar).
4.  Answer the question about **Risk Assessment** (<code>risk_assessment</code>) by selecting "Yes" if you have assessed the risks involved, "No" if you have not, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
5.  Fill in your **Emergency Contact** (<code>emergency_contact</code>) information including their name, relationship with you, and contact information (phone, address).
6.  Select the date of your **Consent Date** (<code>consent_date</code>) for this form.
7.  Sign and date your **Signature Date** (<code>signature_date</code>) to confirm your consent.
8.  Answer the question about your **Client Waiver** (<code>client_waiver</code>) by selecting "Yes" if you agree to the terms, "No" if you do not.
9.  Provide a brief explanation for why you are not signing the waiver if you selected "No" (<code>client_waiver_reason</code>).
10.  Fill in your **Medical Info** (<code>medical_info</code>) if you have any medical conditions or concerns.
11.  Answer the question about **Emergency Medical** (<code>emergency_medical</code>) by providing any relevant medical information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (<code>client_name</code>): Fill in your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Contact Info** (<code>contact_info</code>): Provide your address, phone number, and email for communication purposes.
*   **Emergency Contact** (<code>emergency_contact</code>): Select the person to contact in case of an emergency. Provide their name, your relationship with them, and their contact information.
*   **Consent Date** (<code>consent_date</code>): Select the date you have agreed to the terms and conditions of this form.
*   **Signature Date** (<code>signature_date</code>): Sign and date your agreement to the terms and conditions of this form.
*   **Type of Training** (<code>training_type</code>): Select the type of training you will be undergoing (Private Session, Small Group Session, Large Group Session, or Seminar).
*   **Risk Assessment** (<code>risk_assessment</code>): Answer "Yes" if you have assessed the risks involved, "No" if you have not, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
*   **Emergency Contact Info** (<code>emergency_contact_info</code>): Provide the contact information for your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (<code>emergency_contact_relationship</code>): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (<code>emergency_contact_phone</code>): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (<code>emergency_contact_phone_2</code>): Provide the secondary phone number of your emergency contact (if applicable).
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (<code>emergency_contact_address</code>): Provide the address of your emergency contact.
*   **Client Waiver** (<code>client_waiver</code>): Answer "Yes" if you agree to the terms, "No" if you do not.
*   **Client Waiver Reason** (<code>client_waiver_reason</code>): Provide a brief explanation for why you are not signing the waiver if you selected "No".
*   **Medical Info** (<code>medical_info</code>): Provide any relevant medical information.
*   **Medical Info 2** (<code>medical_info2</code>): Provide additional medical information if necessary.
*   **Training Location** (<code>training_location</code>): Provide the location where the training will take place.
*   **Training Time** (<code>training_time</code>): Provide the time of the training session.
*   **Emergency Medical** (<code>emergency_medical</code>): Provide any emergency medical information.
*   **Medical Condition** (<code>medical_condition</code>): Report any medical conditions that may affect your training.
*   **Client Info 1** (<code>client_info_1</code>): Provide any additional information about yourself.
*   **Client Info 2** (<code>client_info_2</code>): Provide additional information about yourself.
*   **Medical Info 3** (<code>medical_info_3</code>): Provide any additional medical information.
*   **Medical Info 4** (<code>medical_info_4</code>): Provide any additional medical information.
*   **Medical Info 5** (<code>medical_info_5</code>): Provide any additional medical information.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions before signing this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your training provider.
*   Please fill in all fields accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Please sign and date the form before submitting it.
