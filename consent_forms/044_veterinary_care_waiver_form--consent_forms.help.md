# Veterinary Care Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Care Waiver Form is designed to collect information about a pet's health and treatment history, enabling veterinarians to provide informed care and attention to the pet's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about your pet, including their name, species, age, and breed.
2. Fill in the "Owner Information" section with your name, contact details, and any relevant medical history for yourself.
3. Enter any contact details, such as phone numbers and email addresses, in the "Contact Details" field.
4. Describe the pet's medical history and any current conditions in the "Medical History" field.
5. Outline the pet's treatment details, including any medications or therapies currently being administered.
6. Enter any relevant veterinary information, such as dates of visits or procedures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Information** (`Pet Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your pet, including their name, species, age, and breed.
* **Owner Information** (`Owner Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, contact details, and any relevant medical history for yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any contact information that may be necessary for follow-up communication or emergencies, such as phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Waiver** (`Waiver`, `text`, required/optional): This field may be used to provide additional information or consent for specific treatments or services.
* **Pet Condition** (`Pet Condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any current medical conditions or issues affecting your pet.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your pet's medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or treatments.
* **Treatment Details** (`Treatment Details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about the treatment or medication currently being administered to your pet.
* **Veterinary Information** (`Veterinary Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any information about recent veterinary visits or procedures, including dates and descriptions.

## Tips
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use clear and concise language to describe your pet's medical history and treatment details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant veterinary professionals.
