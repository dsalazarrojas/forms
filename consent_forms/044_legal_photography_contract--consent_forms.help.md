# Legal Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Photography Contract form is used to create or modify contracts with photography clients. It captures essential details such as session time, payment methods, and terms of cancellation. The form helps ensure clear communication and agreement with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the form with the necessary details for the photography contract.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Photography Contract** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is the title of the contract.
* **Session Details** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the session time: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Arrival Time** (`3`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the expected arrival time for the photography session.
* **Purpose of this Agreement** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of the contract.
* **Payment Method** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the payment method: Check, Cash, or Online Payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`6`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the total payment amount for the photography services.
* **Cancellation** (`7`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the cancellation terms: No Cancellation, 15 minutes prior, or 30 minutes prior.
* **Late Fees** (`8`, `number`, required/optional): Enter any late fees for the photography services.
* **No-show** (`9`, `note`, required/optional): Leave any additional notes for no-show clients.
* **Client Name** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Photographer** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the photographer.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before finalizing the form.
* Ensure you have captured all necessary details for the photography contract.
* This form can be used for both creating new contracts and modifying existing ones.
