# ice_skating_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ice skating waiver form is a mandatory form for individuals participating in ice skating activities. It helps us gather essential information to ensure your safety and the safety of others during the event. Completing this form helps us respond effectively in case of an emergency and maintain a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details, including first and last name.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. If applicable, list your emergency contact information, including name, relationship, phone, and email.
4. If you have any known medical conditions, please list them.
5. Check the "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" option.
6. Sign the declaration by selecting "Sign Here".
7. If you have any further emergency contacts, please select "Emergency Contact" and list their name.
8. Date and time of your participation (optional).
9. Sign the form with your name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any known medical conditions you may have.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Name** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_2_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between you and your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Phone** (`emergency_contact_2_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Email** (`emergency_contact_2_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): List any relevant medical history you may have.
* **Medical History Detail** (`medical_history_detail`, text, optional): Provide further details about your medical history, if necessary.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select the option that indicates you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Sign Here** (`sign_here`, select_one, optional): Sign the declaration by selecting "Sign Here".
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Select the option to include another emergency contact or list "Not Listed".
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your participation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your participation.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form with your name.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
