# Healthcare Robotics Research Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Robotics Research Filming Consent Form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients participating in healthcare robotics research filming activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's information on page 1, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
2. On page 2, indicate your filming consent by selecting "Yes" or "No" for filming frequency and location.
3. On page 3, provide details about the filming duration and equipment used.
4. On page 4, add any additional filming consent notes.
5. On page 5, sign and date the filming consent with your signature and name.
6. On page 6, sign and date the consent form with the signature and name of the individual giving consent.
7. Finally, add any signature notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: This is where you will enter the patient's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Contact Details**: This is where you will enter your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Filming Consent**: This is where you will indicate your consent to filming. Select "Yes" if you are willing to be filmed, or "No" if you are not.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Research Information**: This is where you will provide details about the research study, including its purpose and goals.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Date**: This is where you will enter the date of the filming consent.
  - Required: No
  - Type: date

* **Time**: This is where you will enter the time of the filming consent.
  - Required: No
  - Type: time

* **Researcher Name**: This is where you will enter the name of the researcher or person leading the study.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Filming Duration**: This is where you will enter the duration of the filming.
  - Required: No
  - Type: number

* **Filming Frequency**: This is where you will indicate how frequently the filming will take place. Select "Yes" or "No".
  - Required: No
  - Type: select_one

* **Filming Location**: This is where you will indicate where the filming will take place. Select "Yes" or "No".
  - Required: No
  - Type: select_multiple

* **Filming Equipment**: This is where you will enter any special equipment used during filming.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Filming Consent Notes**: This is where you can add any additional notes about filming consent.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Filming Consent Signature**: This is where you will sign and date the filming consent.
  - Required: No
  - Type: date

* **Signature Date**: This is where you will enter the date of the signature.
  - Required: No
  - Type: date

* **Signature Time**: This is where you will enter the time of the signature.
  - Required: No
  - Type: time

* **Signature Notes**: This is where you can add any additional notes about the signature.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

* **Signature Name**: This is where you will enter the name of the individual giving consent.
  - Required: No
  - Type: text

## Tips
* Please take the time to read and understand each field before proceeding.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the research team.
* This form is designed to be completed by the patient or their legal representative.
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and honestly.
* If the form is incomplete or unclear, do not proceed with filming.
