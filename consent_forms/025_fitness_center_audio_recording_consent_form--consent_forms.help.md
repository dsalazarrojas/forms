# Fitness Center Audio Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from members for audio recordings made at the fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
2. Review and understand the purpose and context of this form before completing it.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to all questions, especially for fields like consent reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Information** (`Member Information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect basic information about the member, such as their name and contact details. This information is not directly related to the audio recording consent.
* **Consent Date** (`Consent Date`, `date`, required): The date on which the member provided consent for the audio recording.
* **Recording Date** (`Recording Date`, `date`, required): The date on which the audio recording was made.
* **Recording Time** (`Recording Time`, `time`, required/optional): The time at which the audio recording was made.
* **Recording Duration** (`Recording Duration`, `number`, required/optional): The length of the audio recording. This field is not directly related to the consent process, but rather to the recording details.
* **Audio Recording** (`Audio Recording`, `text`, required/optional): Notes about the audio recording, such as the format or medium used.
* **Recording Device** (`Recording Device`, `text`, required): The device used to make the audio recording.
* **Recording Location** (`Recording Location`, `text`, required/optional): The location where the audio recording was made.
* **Consent Given** (`Consent Given`, `select_one`, required): A yes or no answer to indicate if the member has given consent for the audio recording.
* **Consent Reason** (`Consent Reason`, `text`, required/optional): The reason why the member gave or did not give consent for the audio recording. This field is optional, but it can help understand the member's perspective.
* **Recording Permission** (`Recording Permission`, `select_multiple`, required): A selection of options to indicate the type of recording permission given, such as yes or no.

Remember to review and submit the form accurately and truthfully, and to consult with fitness center staff if you have any questions or concerns.
