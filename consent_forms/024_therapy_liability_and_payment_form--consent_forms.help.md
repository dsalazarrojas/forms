# Therapy Liability And Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients for therapy services. It includes details such as client name, contact information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select a start date for the therapy services in the "Start Date" field.
3. Select an end date for the therapy services in the "End Date" field.
4. Enter the client's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the client's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter the client's address in the "Address" field.
7. Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's full name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date for the therapy services.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date for the therapy services.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact.
 
## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate processing.
- Review the form carefully before submission to prevent errors.
