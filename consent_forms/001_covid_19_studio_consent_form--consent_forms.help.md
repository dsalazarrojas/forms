# COVID 19 Studio Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from clients before providing services, including appointment scheduling, contact details, and health protocols for COVID-19 precautions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your **Client full name** in the space provided to help us verify your identity.
2. Enter a valid **Client email** to receive confirmation and updates about your appointments.
3. Select the scheduled **Appointment date** for your service.
4. Check yes or no to indicate if you have experienced any **COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days**. If you have experienced symptoms, please provide additional notes if necessary.
5. If available, input your **Temperature reading in celsius** to help us monitor your health.
6. Choose **Consent to receive services under studio policies** by selecting "I consent" or "I do not consent".
7. Provide the name and phone number of an **Emergency contact** in case of emergencies.
8. Share any **Additional notes or health considerations** in the space provided to ensure your comfort and safety during the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client full name**: This is your full name, including your title and suffixes if applicable.
* **Client email**: A valid email address where we will send confirmation and updates about your appointments.
* **Appointment date**: The date scheduled for your service. Make sure to select the correct date to avoid conflicts.
* **COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days**: Check "Yes" if you have experienced any symptoms, or "No" if not. If yes, provide further details in the adjacent notes field.
* **Temperature reading in celsius**: If measured, report your current body temperature for our records.
* **Consent to receive services under studio policies**: Choose "I consent" to agree to our health protocols, which are designed to keep you and our staff safe and healthy.
* **Emergency contact**: Name and contact number of someone we can reach in case of emergency or if you need assistance during the service.
* **Additional notes or health considerations**: Share any allergies, medical conditions, or special instructions we should be aware of for your comfort and safety.
