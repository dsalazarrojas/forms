## Skincare New Customer Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skincare New Customer Consultation Form is designed to collect essential information from potential customers to provide personalized skincare services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and ensure you provide accurate and detailed information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **new_customer_info (Text, Optional)**: Enter your basic contact information.
* **health_info (Text, Optional)**: Share any health-related concerns or medical conditions you have.
* **skin_details (Text, Optional)**: Describe your skin conditions, concerns, or any previous treatments you've had.
* **skincare_goals (Text, Optional)**: Specify your skincare goals and objectives.
* **availability (Text, Optional)**: Indicate your availability for treatment and follow-up appointments.
* **treatment_terms_and_conditions (Text, Optional)**: Carefully read and agree to the terms of treatment, including cancellation policies and payment terms.
* **consent_to_treatment (Text, Optional)**: Confirm your consent to receive treatment and agree to the terms outlined in the previous field.
* **customer_notes (Text, Optional)**: Include any additional comments or notes you think would be helpful for the skincare professional.

## Tips
Remember, the information gathered from this form will be used to provide tailored services and advice. Please provide detailed and accurate information in each field to ensure the best possible experience. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
