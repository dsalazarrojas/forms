# Fashion International Campaign Image Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion International Campaign Image Consent Form is used to collect information and consent from individuals whose images are being captured for promotional and editorial purposes. This form helps to ensure that users understand how their images will be used and agree to the usage terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the `image_url` field with the URL of the image you wish to use.
2. Select the type of usage your image will be used for by choosing one or more of the options in the `usage_type` field.
3. Choose the resolution of the image you wish to use from the `image_resolution` field.
4. Enter the date and time the image was captured in the `image_capture_date` and `image_capture_time` fields.
5. Check the boxes to agree to the usage terms in the `image_usage_agreement`, `user_agreement`, and `fashion_agreement` fields.
6. Enter your email address in the `user_email` field.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the `note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **image_url**: Enter the URL of the image you wish to use in this campaign. This can be a URL from a website, social media, or other online platform.
* **usage_type**: Choose the types of usage your image will be used for. This can be one or more of the following:
	+ Promotional
	+ Editorial
	+ Both
	+ None
* **image_resolution**: Select the resolution of the image you wish to use. This can be one of the following:
	+ 4x5
	+ 6x7
	+ 7x8
	+ 8x10
	+ 10x12
	+ Other (please specify)
* **image_capture_date**: Enter the date the image was captured.
* **image_capture_time**: Enter the time the image was captured.
* **consent**: Provide a brief statement of consent for the usage of your image.
* **note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the image or its usage.
* **user_email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the image usage.
* **user_phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **image_usage_agreement**: Check the box to agree to the usage terms of the image.
* **fashion_brand**: Enter the name of the fashion brand or company associated with the image.
* **user_agreement**: Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **fashion_agreement**: Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions for the fashion brand.
* **company_name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **company_address**: Enter the address of your company or organization (optional).
* **image_usage_terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions for the image usage (optional).
* **image_usage_url**: Enter a URL for more information about the image usage terms (optional).
* **image_usage_note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the image usage (optional).
* **image_resolution_other**: If you selected "Other" as the image resolution, enter the resolution here.
* **user_note**: Add any additional comments or notes about the image or its usage.
* **fashion_image_url**: Enter a URL for more information about the fashion brand or company.
* **fashion_agreement_url**: Enter a URL for more information about the fashion brand or company's terms and conditions.
* **image_capture_date_time**: Enter the date and time the image was captured.

Note: This form is for collecting consent and information from individuals whose images are being used in a campaign. Please make sure to review and understand the form carefully before filling it out. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
