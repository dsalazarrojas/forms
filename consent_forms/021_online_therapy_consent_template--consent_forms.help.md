# online_therapy_consent_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain online therapy consent from individuals, typically clients or patients, before starting online therapy sessions. It helps ensure that all parties involved have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the online therapy service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the "Terms and Conditions" field carefully.
2. Check the box next to "I agree to the terms and conditions" if you understand and agree to the terms.
3. Provide a brief description of your online therapy service in the "Description" field.
4. In the "Agreement" field, type "I agree" if you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
5. Optionally, you can provide any changes or updates in the "Changes" field.
6. If your online therapy service is provided by a third party (e.g., a therapist), list their name(s) in the "Providers" field.
7. Type "I agree" in the "Provider Agreement" field to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions provided by your online therapy service provider.
8. Select "I agree to the terms and conditions" from the dropdown menu in the "Consent Form Completion" field.
9. Enter the date of completion in the "Date of Completion" field (if required by your online therapy service).
10. Fill in the "Date of Signature" field with the date you sign this form.
11. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
12. Enter your printed name in the "Printed Name" field.
13. Enter your printed title (e.g., Dr., Mr., Ms., etc.) in the "Printed Title" field.
14. Enter your printed address in the "Printed Address" field.
15. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
16. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
17. If you have any contact information for an emergency contact, enter it in the "Contact Information" field (not required).
18. Enter the name of the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact" field.
19. Select the relationship between you and the emergency contact in the "Relationship" field.
20. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
21. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact Email" field.
22. Optionally, you can add any emergency contact note in the "Emergency Contact Note" field (not required).
23. Type "I agree" in the "Emergency Contact Agreement" field to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions provided by your emergency contact.
24. Check the box next to "Terms and Conditions Read" to confirm you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
