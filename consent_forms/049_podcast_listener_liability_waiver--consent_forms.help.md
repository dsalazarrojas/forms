# Podcast Listener Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather listener's consent for the terms and conditions of the podcast. This form ensures that listeners understand their liability in participating in or contributing to the podcast.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field, if you wish to be contacted by the podcast host.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone_number" field, if you wish to be contacted by the podcast host.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
5. Provide a signature in the "signature" field, as a proof of your consent.
6. Select the date you submitted the form in the "Date submitted" field.
7. Answer the question of whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "accept_terms" field.
8. Select whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "terms_and_conditions" field.
9. Answer the question of whether you are a listener in the "listener_agreement" field.
10. Select whether you agree to the podcast agreement in the "podcast_agreement" field.
11. Select whether there are any other agreements you have with the podcast host in the "other_podcast_agreement" field.
12. Provide emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact" field.
13. Enter the address of the emergency contact in the "emergency_address" field.
14. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the "emergency_relationship" field.
15. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the "emergency_phone" field.
16. Provide any other emergency contact information in the "emergency_other" field.
17. Select the date of the consent in the "consent" field.
18. Answer the question of whether you have read and agree to the emergency agreement in the "emergency_agreement" field.
19. Select whether you have read and agree to the emergency agreement in the "emergency_agreement_read" field.
20. Answer the question of whether you have any other agreements with the podcast host in the "other_agreement" field.
21. Select whether you agree to the emergency agreement in the "agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **email_address**: Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted by the podcast host.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by the podcast host.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **signature**: Please provide a signature as a proof of your consent.
* **Date submitted**: Select the date you submitted the form.
* **accept_terms**: Answer "Yes" if you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **terms_and_conditions**: Select whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **listener_agreement**: Answer "Yes" if you are a listener.
* **podcast_agreement**: Select whether you agree to the podcast agreement.
* **other_podcast_agreement**: Select whether there are any other agreements you have with the podcast host.
* **emergency_contact**: Provide the emergency contact information.
* **emergency_address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **emergency_phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_other**: Provide any other emergency contact information.
* **consent**: Select the date of your consent.
* **emergency_agreement**: Answer "Yes" if you have read and agree to the emergency agreement.
* **terms_and_conditions_read**: Select whether you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **emergency_agreement_read**: Select whether you have read and agree to the emergency agreement.
* **other_agreement**: Answer "Yes" if you have any other agreements with the podcast host.
* **agreement**: Select whether you agree to the emergency agreement.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to review all the options carefully before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please contact the podcast host.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for listeners who participate in or contribute to the podcast.
