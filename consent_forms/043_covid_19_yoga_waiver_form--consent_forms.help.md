# Covid 19 Yoga Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Covid 19 Yoga Waiver Form is a mandatory document that must be completed by all participants before attending yoga classes. The form aims to inform and obtain consent from participants regarding the risks associated with yoga practices, especially in the context of Covid 19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant name** in the first field. This is the name of the person who will be attending the yoga classes.
2. Provide your **Contact information** in the second field. This is the contact information of the participant, which will be used in case of emergencies.
3. If applicable, fill in the **Emergency contact name** and **Emergency contact phone** fields. These fields are used to provide contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
4. List any **Health conditions** that you have, which may affect your ability to participate in yoga practices. This is crucial to inform the instructors and ensure your safety.
5. Choose your answer for the **Waiver signature** field. You must choose either 'Yes' or 'No' to signify your understanding and acceptance of the waiver of liability.
6. Finally, select the option for the **Instructor signature** field. This is a confirmation that the instructor has read and understood the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: This is the name of the person who will be attending the yoga classes. Please fill in your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact information**: This field is used to provide your contact details, which will be used in case of emergencies. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Emergency contact name**: If you have an emergency contact, please fill in their name. This is the person who will be contacted in case you are unable to attend a class.
* **Emergency contact phone**: If you have an emergency contact, please fill in their phone number. This is the contact number that will be used to reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact address**: If you have an emergency contact, please fill in their address. This is the address that will be used to contact them in case of an emergency.
* **Health conditions**: List any health conditions that you have, which may affect your ability to participate in yoga practices. This is crucial to inform the instructors and ensure your safety.
* **Waiver signature**: Please choose 'Yes' to signify that you have read and understood the waiver of liability. This is a mandatory field, and you cannot proceed without selecting an option.
* **Instructor signature**: The instructor will select an option from the provided list. This is a confirmation that they have read and understood the waiver.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please ask your instructor or contact the yoga studio administration.
* Ensure that all fields are completed before attending the yoga classes.
