## Robot Activity Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Robot Activity Liability Waiver is a form designed to obtain consent from a participant's guardian or parent for a robot activity. This form is used to ensure that the participant's legal guardians are aware of and consent to the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the participant's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
2. Enter the name of the participant's legal guardian or parent.
3. Specify the relationship of the guardian or parent to the participant.
4. Enter the participant's age.
5. Enter the name of the participant's school (if applicable).
6. Enter the guardian's address (if applicable).
7. Indicate whether the waiver has been signed or not.
8. Sign the form as the participant.
9. Sign the form as the guardian or parent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partners Participant Name** (`prophets_participant_name`): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Guardians Name** (`guardians_name`): Enter the name of the participant's legal guardian or parent.
* **Guardians Relationship** (`guardians_relationship`): Specify the relationship of the guardian or parent to the participant (e.g., mother, father, custodial guardian).
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participants School** (`participant_school`): Enter the name of the participant's school (if applicable).
* **Prophets School** (`prophets_school`): Enter the name of the participant's school (if applicable).
* **Participants Address** (`participant_address`): Enter the participant's address (if applicable).
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`): Indicate whether the waiver has been signed or not.
	+ Select "True" if the waiver has been signed.
	+ Select "False" if the waiver has not been signed.
* **Participants Signature** (`participants_signature`): Sign the form as the participant.
* **Guardians Signature** (`guardians_signature`): Sign the form as the guardian or parent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Ensure that the waiver has been signed by the guardian or parent before proceeding with the robot activity.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for reference and future reference.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with the relevant personnel or authorities.
