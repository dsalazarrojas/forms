# Public Art Mural Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather consent from participants regarding the inclusion of a public art mural in a specified location. The form helps to ensure that all participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the mural project, including their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete each field carefully**:
	* Ensure that all required fields (marked as required) are filled out accurately and truthfully.
	* Do not leave any required fields blank.
2. **Use correct contact information**:
	* Enter your name and contact details (including email address and phone number) for us to contact you.
3. **Choose correct mural participation options**:
	* Select the option that best describes your participation in the mural project.
4. **Sign the form**:
	* Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the mural project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details (1)**:
	+ Enter your name, email address, and phone number for us to contact you regarding the mural project.
* **Consent Details (2)**:
	+ Provide a brief statement explaining why you are participating in the mural project and what you are consenting to.
* **Mural Participation (3)**:
	+ Select all options that apply to your participation in the mural project:
		- Option 1: Artist or Artisan
		- Option 2: Participant or Collaborator
		- Option 3: Other (please specify)
* **Mural Location (4)**:
	+ Enter the location where the mural will be installed.
* **Participant Signature (5)**:
	+ Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the mural project.
* **Artist Statement (6)**:
	+ Provide an optional statement about your artistic experience, skills, and qualifications.
* **Artist Statement Agreement (7)**:
	+ Select the option that best describes your agreement with the artist statement:
		- Option 1: I have read and agree to the terms of the artist statement.
		- Option 2: I have not read and do not agree to the terms of the artist statement.
* **Participant Permission (8)**:
	+ Select all options that apply to your consent to the terms of the mural project:
		- Option 1: I grant permission for the mural to be installed in the specified location.
		- Option 2: I do not grant permission for the mural to be installed in the specified location.
* **Participant Permission Date (9)**:
	+ Enter the date you are granting permission for the mural to be installed.
* **Participant Permission Time (10)**:
	+ Enter the time you are granting permission for the mural to be installed.

## Tips

* Ensure that you understand all the terms and conditions of the mural project before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or options, do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it to us.
