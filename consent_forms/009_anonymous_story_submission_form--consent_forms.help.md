# Anonymous Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anonymous Story Submission Form is designed for users to share their stories anonymously, with the option to consent to publication. This form collects basic information about the story, such as title and text, along with some additional details like age range and trigger warnings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the required fields, including the Story title and Story text.
3. Choose your preferred age range (if applicable).
4. Select the trigger warnings that apply to your story (if applicable).
5. Indicate your consent to publish your story anonymously.
6. Add any additional notes for the editors (if applicable).
7. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Story Submission --** (section_submission): This is the starting point of the form. It's just a section header, and you don't need to input anything here.
* **Story title** (story_title, required): Enter a short descriptive title for your story. This will help our editors understand the content and context of your submission.
* **Story text** (story_text, required): Paste or type the full text of your story here. Please be as detailed and honest as you can be.
* **Consent to publish anonymously** (consent_to_publish, required): Choose "Yes I consent to anonymous publication" if you're okay with your story being published without your name or identifying information.
* **Age range of author** (age_range, optional): Select your age range from the options provided. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your demographic.
* **Does your story contain trigger warnings** (trigger_warnings, optional): If your story deals with sensitive topics like violence, abuse, self-harm, or substance misuse, select all that apply.
* **Additional notes for editors** (additional_notes, optional): If you have any additional context or requests for our editors, you can add them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and respectful when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our team for clarification.
* Remember that we're committed to protecting your anonymity, but we can't guarantee complete confidentiality.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
