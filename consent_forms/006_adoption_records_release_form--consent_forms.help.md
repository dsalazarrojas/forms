# Adoption Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adoption Records Release Form is designed to collect information from a person requesting access to their adoption records. The form helps the requester provide essential details about themselves and the adoptee, ensuring a smooth and secure release process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information, including your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Enter the adoptee's name, date of birth, and other relevant details.
3. Specify what type of records you are requesting and whether you need any additional information.
4. Accept the terms and conditions of the release, including a certified copy of the records if required.
5. Confirm that you understand and agree to any processing fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Requester)**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Relationship to Adoptee (Requester)**: Select your connection to the adopted person, such as Adoptee (Adult), Biological Family Member, or Adoptive Family Member.
* **Email Address (Requester)**: Enter a valid email address for communication about your request.
* **Phone Number (Requester)**: Enter a valid phone number for contact.
* **Mailing Address (Requester)**: Enter your mailing address where you want the records to be sent.
* **Adoptee Full Name**: Enter the adoptee's legal name.
* **Adoptee Date of Birth**: Enter the adoptee's birth date.
* **Adoptee Current Age**: Enter the adoptee's current age.
* **Date of Adoption**: Enter the date the adoption was finalized (if known).
* **Original Adoption Agency**: Enter the name of the agency handling the adoption (if known).
* **Adoption Court Location**: Enter the court where the adoption was finalized (if known).
* **Type of Records Needed**: Select all applicable records, such as Birth Certificate, Medical Records, etc.
* **Medical History Information**: Indicate whether you need access to medical history information.
* **Biological Family Contact Information**: Indicate whether you need access to biological family information.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the confidentiality requirements and terms of the release.
* **Certified Copy Required**: Indicate if you need a certified copy of the records.
* **Accept Processing Fee**: Confirm that you understand and accept any processing fees associated with the release.
* **Additional Information or Notes**: Enter any additional information or special requests.
