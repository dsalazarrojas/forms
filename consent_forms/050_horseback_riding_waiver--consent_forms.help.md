# Horseback Riding Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Horseback Riding Waiver form is designed to collect information from riders to ensure their safety and the safety of others during horseback riding activities. Completing this form will help us understand your personal details, emergency contact information, and any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your personal details, including first name, last name, address, city, date of birth, and emergency contact information.
2. Select the correct option for your emergency contact relation from the list.
3. Sign the form above the signature line to indicate your acceptance of the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address (3)**: Enter your current address where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **City (4)**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State (5)**: Select your state of residence from the list.
* **Date of Birth (6)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Rider Info 2 (7)**: Select how you plan to ride (Horseback or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Name (8)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone (9)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation (10)**: Select the relation of your emergency contact from the list.
* **Signature (11)**: Sign your name above the signature line to indicate your acceptance of this waiver.
* **Permission to contact emergency contact (12)**: Check if you grant permission to contact your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions (13)**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this field.
* **Insurance Provider (14)**: Enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Number (15)**: Enter your insurance number.
* **Insurance Provider Phone (16)**: Enter the phone number of your insurance provider.
* **Rider Info 3 (17)**: Select how you plan to ride (Horseback or Other).

Tips:
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you have any medical conditions, please be honest about them to ensure your safety and the safety of others.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
