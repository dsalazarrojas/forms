### Live Audience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to obtain consent from participants to record audio during an interview. The form is not affiliated with or endorsed by any form platform, and its creation was inspired by public survey topic metadata. As a form designed to collect participant details and consent, it does not collect any proprietary content from other platforms. The form's purpose is to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the recording of their conversations.

Please note that this form is an original adaptation and can be used, modified, and even sold - just give credit.

To complete this form, gather participant information, select their consent to record audio, have them sign their name if they consent, and fill out the date the interview was conducted.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Provide any relevant details about the participant, such as their name, contact information, and other identifying features.
* **Consent to Record Audio**: Select "Yes" if you have obtained consent from the participant to record audio during the interview. If not, select "No".
* **Participant Signature**: If the participant has given their consent, have them sign their name on this field. This serves as their official agreement.
* **Date Interview Conducted**: Fill out the date the interview was conducted.
