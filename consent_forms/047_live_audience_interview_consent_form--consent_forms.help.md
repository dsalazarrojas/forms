## Live Audience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Live Audience Interview Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from participants to record audio during an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Gather Participant Information
- To start, fill out the participant's details on the first page of the form. This includes any relevant information the participant has shared with you.
- On the second page, select the option that best represents your response to recording audio during the interview. If you are unsure, ask your participant if they are comfortable with the audio recording.
- If you have obtained consent, the participant will sign their name on the third page to confirm their agreement.
- On the final page, fill out the date the interview was conducted. This can be done in any format accepted by your device's date picker.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about the participant, such as their name, contact information, and any other identifying features.
* **Consent to Record Audio** (`consent_to_record_audio`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you have obtained consent from the participant to record audio during the interview. If not, select "No". If unsure, you may want to ask for clarification.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional): If the participant has given their consent, have them sign their name on this field. This serves as their official agreement.
* **Date Interview Conducted** (`date_interview_conducted`, date, optional): Fill out the date the interview was conducted. This can be done in any format accepted by your device's date picker.

## Tips
- Always ensure that you clearly explain the purpose and implications of recording audio to the participant before obtaining consent.
- If the participant declines to record audio, respect their decision and refrain from recording.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, refer to the participant's answers for clarification.
