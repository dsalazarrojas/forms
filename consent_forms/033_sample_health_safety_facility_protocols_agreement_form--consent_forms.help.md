# Sample Health Safety Facility Protocols Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and confirm agreements related to health and safety protocols in a facility. It gathers user, facility, and employee information, as well as facility capabilities and protocols. The form is intended to be a starting point for facility safety protocols and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully** to understand the purpose and sections.
2. **Gather information** about yourself and the facility, such as your name, job title, and contact information.
3. **Fill out the form** by providing the required and optional information in each section.
4. **Submit the form** once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): This section is for providing your name, title, and contact information.
* **Facility Information** (`facility_info`, Text, Optional): Here, you can enter details about the facility, such as its name and address.
* **Facility Contacts** (`facility_contacts`, Text, Optional): Enter the contact information for the facility's administrators, owners, or managers.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, Text, Optional): If you are an employee at the facility, this is the section to provide your name, title, and contact information.
* **Facility Capabilities** (`facility_capabilities`, Text, Optional): Describe the capabilities and services offered by the facility, such as training or safety equipment.
* **Protocols** (`protocols`, Text, Optional): Outline the health and safety protocols in place at the facility, including procedures and guidelines.
* **Signature Info** (`signature_info`, Text, Optional): This section is for capturing any additional signature information or agreements related to the facility's protocols.

## Tips
