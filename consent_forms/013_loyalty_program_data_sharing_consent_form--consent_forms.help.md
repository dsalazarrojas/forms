# Loyalty Program Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loyalty Program Data Sharing Consent Form is used to collect participant consent for sharing data with third parties. This form is essential for ensuring that participants are aware of and agree to the sharing of their data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for question 1.
3. For question 2, select all the data types you agree to share (Email, Phone, or both).
4. Optionally, add any additional comments.
5. Enter your agreement to share your data in the loyalty program.
6. If you have any contact information for a person to contact, provide it.
7. Add any other relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Am I a program member?** (`i_am_a_program_member`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate if you are a participant in the loyalty program.
* **I Agree to sharing my data** (`i_agree_to_sharing_my_data`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the data types you agree to share (Email, Phone, or both).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you give consent to share your data.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or reasons for your consent.
* **I agree to sharing my data in the loyalty program** (`i_agree_to_sharing_my_data_in_the_loyalty_program`, text, optional): Enter your agreement to share your data in the program.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): If you have a contact person, enter their name here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you have any other contact information, enter it here.
