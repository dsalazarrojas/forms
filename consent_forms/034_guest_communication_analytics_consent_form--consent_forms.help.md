# Guest Communication Analytics Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a guest's communication preferences and consent for data use and storage. This form is intended for guests visiting a specific location or engaging with an organization's services. It collects contact details, communication preferences, and data handling settings to enable personalized and secure interactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
4. Select your preferred contact method(s) in the "Contact Preference" field. You can choose to be contacted via email, phone, or both.
5. Indicate your consent for how you would like your data to be used in the "How would you like us to use your data?" field. You can choose for analytics, marketing, or both.
6. Choose your data storage preference in the "How would you like your data to be stored?" field. Options include storage locally, securely, or both.
7. Decide whether you would like your data to be anonymized in the "Would you like your data to be anonymized?" field. You can choose to have your data anonymized or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Provide your mailing address.
* **Contact Preference** (`Contact Preference`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred contact methods. You can choose email, phone, or both.
* **How would you like us to use your data?** (`data_use`, select_one, required): Indicate how you would like us to use your data. You can choose analytics, marketing, or both.
* **How would you like your data to be stored?** (`data_storage`, select_one, required): Decide how you want us to store your data. You can choose locally, securely, or both.
* **Would you like your data to be anonymized?** (`data_anonymization`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want your data to be anonymized or not.
