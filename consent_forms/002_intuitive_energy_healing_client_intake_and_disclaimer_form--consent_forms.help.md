# Intuitive Energy Healing Client Intake And Disclaimer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients seeking intuitive energy healing services, ensuring that both the client and the energy healer understand each other's expectations and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the client.
2. Enter your email address (if you have one).
3. Provide your phone number (if you have one).
4. Select the disclaimer agreement that best represents your understanding.
5. Choose the healing services you are interested in receiving.
6. If applicable, list any medical conditions you would like to share with the energy healer.
7. Describe your goals for the session.
8. Indicate the frequency of sessions you would like to schedule.
9. Note down any additional comments or information you'd like to share with the energy healer (optional).
10. Record the date and time of form completion (not visible to the client).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (text, optional): Enter your full name as the client.
* **Client Email** (email, optional): If you have an email address, enter it here. This will help us contact you for further information or to confirm our appointment.
* **Client Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number if you have one. This will allow us to contact you for any last-minute changes or to confirm our appointment.
* **Disclaimer Agreement** (select_one, required): Please select one of the options provided, indicating your understanding of the disclaimer.
* **Healing Services** (select_multiple, required): Choose the services you are interested in receiving during the session. Select all that apply.
	+ Intuitive: A session that uses your intuition to help you find solutions.
	+ Reiki: A session that helps balance your energy.
	+ Energy Healing: A session that targets your energy flow.
	+ Meditation: A session that helps you relax and focus.
	+ Chakra Balancing: A session that balances your body's energy centers.
	+ Aromatherapy: A session that combines aromas with energy healing.
	+ Sound Healing: A session that utilizes sound to balance your energy.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): If you have medical conditions, please list them here so that we can take necessary precautions.
* **Client Goals** (note, optional): Briefly describe your goals for the session, so that we can tailor the session to your needs.
* **Session Frequency** (number, optional): If you would like to schedule multiple sessions, indicate how frequently you would like to have them (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Intake Form Completed** (time, optional): This will automatically record the date and time of form completion.
