# minor_travel_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information about minor travelers. It is used to understand their travel plans and ensure their safety and well-being during the journey.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the following fields accurately and truthfully to ensure that we have the necessary information to keep your child safe during travel.
2.  The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the minor's travel.
3.  Please answer all questions truthfully and completely.
4.  If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **travel_details** (`travel_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the travel, including any relevant details.
*   **parent_info** (`parent_info`, text, required): Provide your contact information and any relevant medical information about your child.
*   **minor_consent** (`minor_consent`, text, required): Please sign below to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and will follow the rules and regulations.
*   **travel_dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): Enter the start and end dates of the travel.
*   **travel_destination** (`travel_destination`, text, required): Enter the destination of the travel.
*   **travel_time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the expected travel time.
*   **return_date** (`return_date`, date, required): Enter the expected return date.
*   **travel_duration** (`travel_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the travel.
*   **travel_frequency** (`travel_frequency`, text, required): Indicate how often the minor travels with you/ alone.
*   **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Provide a contact number for an emergency contact.
*   **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure the safety and well-being of the minor during the journey. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
