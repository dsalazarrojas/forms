# Wildlife Veterinary Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent for a wildlife veterinary interview, ensuring that participants understand the purpose and scope of the interview. It helps to establish clear expectations and ensure that all parties involved are aware of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Participant Name field, where you can enter your name as it appears on any official identification.
- Next, enter your Email to provide a means of contact.
- The Contact Number field allows you to provide an additional contact method if needed.
- Provide your address for further correspondence in the Address field.
- Select your birthdate for identification purposes in the Birthdate field.
- Choose from the options in the Consent To Participate field to indicate your agreement to participate.
- If you have any additional details to provide for your participation, enter them in the Other (please specify) field.
- Provide details of the Wildlife Veterinary Interview you will be participating in in the Wildlife Veterinary Interview field.
- Specify the location of the interview in the Interview Location field.
- Identify who will be conducting the interview with the Interview Facilitator field.
- Enter the date and time of the interview in the Date of Interview and Time of Interview fields.
- Indicate the expected duration of the interview in the Expected Duration field.
- List the participants of the interview in the Interview Participants field, selecting from the options provided.
- If there are any other participants involved, describe them in the Interview Participants Other field.
- Confirm if the interview will be conducted with a wildlife interviewer in the Interview Conducted By field.
- Finally, select the interviewers from the Wildlife Interviewers field, choosing from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on any official identification.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email for communication purposes.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide an additional contact method, if needed.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address for further communication.

### Birthdate
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, required): Provide your birthdate for identification purposes.

### Consent To Participate
* **Consent To Participate** (`consent_to_participate`, select_multiple, required): Choose to confirm participation in the interview.

### Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)** (`consent_to_participate_other`, text, required): Explain why you want to participate, if not listed.

### Wildlife Veterinary Interview
* **Wildlife Veterinary Interview** (`wildlife_veterinary_interview`, text, required): Describe the wildlife veterinary interview you will be participating in.

### Interview Location
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, required): Specify the location of the interview.

### Interview Facilitator
* **Interview Facilitator** (`interview_facilitator`, text, required): Identify who will conduct the interview.

### Date of Interview
* **Date of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.

### Time of Interview
* **Time of Interview** (`time_of_interview`, time, required): Enter the time of the interview.

### Expected Duration
* **Expected Duration** (`expected_duration`, time, required): Specify the expected duration of the interview.

### Interview Participants
* **Interview Participants** (`interview_participants`, select_multiple, required): List the participants of the interview.

### Interview Participants Other
* **Interview Participants Other** (`interview_participants_other`, text, required): Provide details of other participants if necessary.

### Interview Facilitators
* **Interview Facilitators** (`interview_facilitators`, select_multiple, required): Confirm the facilitators of the interview.

### Interview Facilitators Other
* **Interview Facilitators Other** (`interview_facilitators_other`, text, required): Provide additional details about the facilitators if necessary.

### Interview Notes
* **Interview Notes** (`interview_notes`, text, required): Take notes during the interview.

### Interview Notes Other
* **Interview Notes Other** (`interview_notes_other`, text, required): Provide any additional notes about the interview.

### Interview Conducted By
* **Interview Conducted By** (`interview_conducted_by`, text, required): Confirm the person conducting the interview.

### Wildlife Interviewers
* **Wildlife Interviewers** (`wildlife_interviewers`, select_multiple, required): Select who will be conducting the interview.

### Wildlife Interviewers Other
* **Wildlife Interviewers Other** (`wildlife_interviewers_other`, text, required): Provide additional details about the wildlife interviewers if necessary.
