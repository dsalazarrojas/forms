### Bridal Makeup Consent Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to gather information about the bride's preferences, needs, and requirements for the makeup services. It helps the makeup artist understand the bride's expectations and ensures a seamless experience on the day of the wedding.

### How To Complete This Form

1. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Enter your information in the provided fields.
3. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bride Name** (`Bride Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Provide your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Wedding Date** (`Wedding Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Makeup Artist Name** (`Makeup Artist Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your makeup artist.
* **Makeup Service Date** (`Makeup Service Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the makeup service.
* **Service Start Time** (`Service Start Time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of the service.
* **Service Location** (`Service Location`, `select_one`, required): Select where the makeup service will be performed:
	+ Makeup artist salon
	+ Your home
	+ Wedding venue
	+ Hotel
	+ Other location
* **Number of People in Bridal Party** (`Number of People in Bridal Party`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of people in your bridal party that will be receiving makeup services.
* **Makeup Style Preference** (`Makeup Style Preference`, `text`, required): Describe your desired look in a few words.
* **Inspiration or Reference Images** (`Inspiration or Reference Images`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to provide inspiration or reference images:
	+ Yes, bringing references
	+ No references
	+ Will discuss with artist
* **Your Skin Type** (`Your Skin Type`, `select_one`, required): Select your skin type:
	+ Dry
	+ Oily
	+ Combination
	+ Sensitive
	+ Acne-prone
* **Skin Concerns or Conditions** (`Skin Concerns or Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any skin concerns or conditions:
	+ Sensitive skin
	+ Acne or breakouts
	+ Rosacea
	+ Eczema
	+ Dermatitis
	+ Allergy prone
	+ None
* **Product Allergies or Sensitivities** (`Product Allergies or Sensitivities`, `text`, required): Enter any product allergies or sensitivities.
* **Preferred Makeup Brands** (`Preferred Makeup Brands`, `text`, required): Enter any preferred makeup brands.
* **Makeup Coverage Preference** (`Makeup Coverage Preference`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of coverage you desire:
	+ Light and natural
	+ Medium coverage
	+ Full coverage
	+ Very heavy coverage
* **Will You Have a Trial Session** (`Will You Have a Trial Session`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want a trial session:
	+ Yes, scheduled
	+ Yes, to be scheduled
	+ No trial needed
	+ Unsure
* **Touchup Service** (`Touchup Service`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want touchup services during the event:
	+ Yes, included
	+ Yes, additional fee
	+ No touchups needed
* **Service Package Selected** (`Service Package Selected`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service package:
	+ Bride makeup only
	+ Bride and bridal party
	+ Full day coverage
	+ Custom package
* **Total Service Cost** (`Total Service Cost`, `number`, required): Enter the total service cost agreed upon.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment terms:
	+ Full payment upfront
	+ 50% deposit, 50% day of
	+ Payment due day of service
	+ Other arrangement
* **I understand the Cancellation Policy** (`I understand the Cancellation Policy`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you understand the cancellation policy:
	+ Yes, I understand
	+ Need clarification
* **Consent to Photography** (`Consent to Photography`, `select_one`, required): Select your consent for photography:
	+ Yes, you may photograph
	+ No photography
	+ Ask before using
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`Special Requests or Notes`, `text`, required): Enter any special requests or notes.
* **Consent and Agreement** (`Consent and Agreement`, `note`, required): This section is for your review and understanding of the terms and conditions of service.
* **Consent Confirmation** (`Consent Confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Select your consent to the makeup services as described above:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ I do not consent
