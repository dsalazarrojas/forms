# Hospice Care Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospice Care Authorization Form is a document used to obtain the consent of a patient or their authorized representative for hospice care. This form is used to ensure that the patient or their representative has given their consent for the provision of hospice care services. The form is typically used by healthcare providers to verify that the patient or their representative has agreed to receive hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the `patient_name` field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the `patient_date_of_birth` field.
3. Identify the primary caregiver's name in the `primary_caregiver_name` field.
4. Select the relationship of the primary caregiver in the `primary_caregiver_relationship` field (e.g., Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, etc.).
5. Provide the patient's address in the `patient_address` field.
6. Share any relevant medical history in the `medical_history` field.
7. Select the type of hospice service requested in the `hospice_service_requested` field (e.g., Inpatient Hospice Care or Home Hospice Care).
8. Indicate whether or not the consent is signed in the `consent_signed` field.
9. Sign and date the form in the `signature_date` field.
10. Sign the form in the `signature` field.
11. Sign the form as a medical provider in the `medical_provider_signature` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name**: The name of the patient. Enter the patient's full name.
* **patient_date_of_birth**: The date of birth of the patient. Enter the date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **primary_caregiver_name**: The name of the primary caregiver. Enter the caregiver's name.
* **primary_caregiver_relationship**: The relationship of the primary caregiver to the patient. Select one or more of the following options: Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Partner, or Friend.
* **patient_address**: The address of the patient. Enter the patient's full address.
* **medical_history**: Any relevant medical history or conditions of the patient. Enter a brief description or history.
* **hospice_service_requested**: The type of hospice care service requested. Select one or both of the following options: Inpatient Hospice Care or Home Hospice Care.
* **consent_signed**: The consent status of the patient or their representative. Select True or False.
* **signature_date**: The date the form was signed. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **signature**: The signature of the patient or their representative. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **medical_provider_signature**: The signature of the healthcare provider. Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **date**: The date and time the form was completed. Enter the date and time in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM AM/PM.
* **time**: The time the form was completed. Enter the time in the 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).
