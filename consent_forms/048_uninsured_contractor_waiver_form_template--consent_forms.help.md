# Uninsured Contract Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver from an uninsured contractor or business for a specific contract term. It collects general information about the contractor, contract terms, and contact details to ensure a smooth and secure business relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, address, phone number, and email on the **General Information** page.
2. Fill in the **Contractor Details** page with the name of the contractor and any relevant details about the contract terms.
3. Enter the **Contract Terms** on the page with the contract start and end dates.
4. Sign and date the **Signature** page to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
5. Fill in the **Contractor Address**, **Contractor Phone**, and **Contractor Email** fields with the relevant information.
6. Sign and date the **Contractor Signature** page to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
7. Provide the **Business Information** page with any relevant details about your business.
8. Fill in the **Business Name**, **Business Address**, **Business Phone**, and **Business Email** fields with the relevant information.
9. Sign and date the **Business Signature** page to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1): Enter your personal information to be contacted by the contractor or business.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Contractor Details** (2): Enter the name and any relevant details about the contract.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Contract Terms** (3): Enter the terms of the contract.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Signature** (4): Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Contractor Address** (5): Enter the address of the contractor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Contractor Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the contractor.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Contractor Email** (9): Enter the email address of the contractor.
  * Type: email
  * Required: No
* **Contractor Signature** (10): Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Business Information** (11): Enter any relevant details about your business.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Business Name** (12): Enter the name of your business.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Business Address** (13): Enter the address of your business.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Business Phone** (14): Enter the phone number of your business.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No
* **Business Email** (15): Enter the email address of your business.
  * Type: email
  * Required: No
* **Business Signature** (16): Sign and date to confirm your agreement to the contract terms.
  * Type: text
  * Required: No

Note: All fields are optional, but it's best to fill them in to ensure a smooth and secure business relationship.
