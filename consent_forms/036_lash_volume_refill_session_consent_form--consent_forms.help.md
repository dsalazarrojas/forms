# Lash Volume Refill Session Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather client information and consent for lash refill sessions. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure a safe and effective lash refill session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your basic client information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3. Confirm that you have received and understood the lash technician's information.
4. Select whether the lash technician is present in the "Lash Technician" section.
5. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
6. Sign the form to confirm the lash technician's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required)**: This field is used to collect your basic information, including [insert any relevant fields such as name, date of birth, or address].
* **Medical History (Medical History, text, required)**: This field is used to gather any medical history that may affect the lash refill session. Please provide any relevant medical information, such as [insert any relevant medical conditions or allergies].
* **Client Consent (Client Consent, text, required)**: This field is used to confirm that you have received and understood the lash technician's information. Please read and sign this field to acknowledge your understanding.
* **Lash Technician (Lash Technician, select_one, optional)**: This field is used to indicate whether the lash technician is present during the session. Please select 'Yes' if the technician will be present, or 'No' if they will not.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature, text, required)**: This field is used to sign the form, confirming your consent for the lash refill session. Please sign your name to acknowledge your agreement to the terms.
* **Lash Technician Signature (Lash Technician Signature, text, required)**: This field is used to sign the form, confirming the lash technician's consent and agreement to the terms. Please sign your name to acknowledge your understanding and agreement to the terms.

## Tips
- Please take the time to read and understand the information provided before signing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask the lash technician or the administrator.
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any errors.
