# Nonprofit Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Participation Waiver Form is a document used to collect consent and other necessary information from participants in nonprofit events or activities. It's crucial to complete this form accurately and honestly to ensure transparency and fairness in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you're completing this form for yourself and not on behalf of someone else.
2. Fill out the form with your actual information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents or as you would like to be referred to during the event.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that you regularly use and check. This will help us contact you with any updates or notifications about the event.
* **Waiver date** (`waiver_date`, date, required): Choose the date on which you're signing this waiver form. This will ensure that your waiver is valid for the selected event period.
* **Participant signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Provide your genuine signature. You can use this space to sign your name manually or paste a digital signature if you have one. This is a mandatory field to confirm your commitment to the waiver terms.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure that your email address is active and can receive notifications.
- Use your actual name as it appears on your identification documents.
- Review the waiver terms and conditions carefully before signing.
- Sign with your genuine signature to confirm your commitment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
