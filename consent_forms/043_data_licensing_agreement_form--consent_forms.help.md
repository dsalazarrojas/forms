# Data Licensing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Licensing Agreement Form is a tool to ensure that both data providers and licensees understand and agree on the terms of data usage. It is used to grant or deny licensing agreements for data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the User Input section with your details.
2. Enter the name of the data provider in the Data Provider field.
3. Select the permitted use of the data from the Permitted Use dropdown menu.
4. Select the signature type from the Signatures dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date you signed the form in the Date field.
6. Enter your name in the Name Signed By field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (User Input, text, required: false): Please enter your details in this field. This is an optional field, but it is required for us to have a record of your input for future reference.
* **Data Provider** (Data Provider, text, required: false): Enter the name of the data provider you are obtaining data from. This is an optional field, but it helps us to keep a record of who provided the data.
* **Permitted Use** (Permitted Use, select_one, required: false): Select the type of use you plan to make of the data. You can choose from three options: Commercial and non-commercial use, Commercial use, or Non-commercial use.
* **Signatures** (Signatures, select_one, required: false): Select the type of signature you are providing. You can choose from three options: Both, Data provider, or Licensee.
* **Date** (Date, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the form. This is an optional field, but it helps us to keep a record of when you signed the form.
* **Name Signed By** (Name Signed By, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field. This is an optional field, but it helps us to keep a record of who signed the form.
