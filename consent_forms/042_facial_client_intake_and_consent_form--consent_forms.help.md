# Facial Client Intake And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects essential information from clients to help the practitioner prepare for their visit and obtain their consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information on the **Client Information** page.
2. On the **Consent** page, select the option that indicates you have read and understand the form.
3. Choose the services you would like to receive during your visit from the **Services** dropdown list.
4. Select your **Skin Type** from the available options.
5. Select any **Skin Concerns** you have from the list.
6. Select your **Hair Type** from the available options.
7. If you have any additional notes or information to share with the practitioner, you can do so in the **Note** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**:
	+ This is where you can enter your name, address, and contact information.
	+ Please fill in your contact details accurately so that the practitioner can reach out to you for any further instructions or to confirm your appointment.
* **Consent**:
	+ This field is essential to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
* **Services**:
	+ Choose the services you would like to receive during your visit, such as Massage, Exfoliation, or Chemical Peel.
* **Skin Type**:
	+ Select your skin type from the options: Dry, Oily, or Combination.
* **Skin Concerns**:
	+ Select any skin concerns you have, such as Acne, Aging, or Hyper-pigmentation.
* **Hair Type**:
	+ Select your hair type from the options: Fine or Coarse.
* **Note**:
	+ This is an optional field where you can add any additional information or comments for the practitioner.
* **Note 2**:
	+ This is another optional field for any further comments or information.
* **Note 3** (Note 2 in the original YAML):
	+ This is an optional field for any additional information or comments for the practitioner. 

Note: If you have any questions or concerns while filling out this form, please don't hesitate to reach out.
