# pet_spa_media_publication_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from pet owners or businesses for the publication of their pet's images and videos in various media channels. The form is required for the legitimate use and publication of media related to a pet, and should only be used for the purpose of publishing such media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure that you are a legitimate representative of the pet or business owner.
3. Fill in your name and contact information in the form accurately.
4. Select the correct options for Photo Release, Video Release, Publication Channel, and Media Channels.
5. Sign the form with your signature.
6. Submit the form to the relevant authorities for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name**: <name> - This is the name of the pet owner who is providing consent for the publication of their pet's images and videos. 
* **Owner Email**: <email> - This is the email address of the pet owner who is providing consent for the publication of their pet's images and videos.
* **Pet Name**: <name> - This is the name of the pet whose images and videos are being published.
* **Photo Release**: <option> - This selects whether or not the pet's photos can be published. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Yes, I give permission for the photo to be published.
	+ Option 2: No, I do not give permission for the photo to be published.
	+ Option 3: Only with written consent from me.
	+ Option 4: Only with written consent from me, and only after 6 months.
	+ Option 5: Only with written consent from me, and only after 1 year.
* **Video Release**: <option> - This selects whether or not the pet's videos can be published. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Yes, I give permission for the video to be published.
	+ Option 2: No, I do not give permission for the video to be published.
	+ Option 3: Only with written consent from me.
	+ Option 4: Only with written consent from me, and only after 6 months.
	+ Option 5: Only with written consent from me, and only after 1 year.
* **Consent Date**: <date> - This is the date on which the consent was provided.
* **Publication Channel**: <option> - This selects which channel(s) the published images and videos will be on. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Online (social media, websites, etc.)
	+ Option 2: Offline (printed media, newspapers, etc.)
	+ Option 3: Both online and offline.
	+ Option 4: Only online (social media, websites, etc.)
	+ Option 5: Only offline (printed media, newspapers, etc.)
* **Media Channels**: <option> - This selects which media channels the published images and videos will be on. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Social media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.)
	+ Option 2: Online news websites
	+ Option 3: Printed magazines
	+ Option 4: Online blogs
	+ Option 5: Other (please specify in the notes field)
	+ Option 6: TV or video channels
	+ Option 7: Print ads (billboards, flyers, etc.)
* **Notes**: <note> - This is a field where additional information or comments can be added.
* **Owner Signature**: <signature> - This is where the pet owner or business owner signs the form to indicate their consent.
* **Business Signature**: <signature> - This is where the business representative signs the form to indicate their consent.
* **Business Name**: <name> - This is the name of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Business Address**: <address> - This is the address of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Contact Number**: <number> - This is the contact number of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Contact Email**: <email> - This is the email address of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Fax Number**: <number> - This is the fax number of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Business Fax**: <number> - This is the fax number of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Business Website**: <url> - This is the website of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Business Contact**: <name> - This is the name of the business contact providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Business License**: <license number> - This is the license number of the business providing their consent for the publication of the pet's images and videos.
* **Media Channels**: <option> - This selects which media channels the published images and videos will be on. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Social media (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc.)
	+ Option 2: Online news websites
	+ Option 3: Printed magazines
	+ Option 4: Online blogs
	+ Option 5: Other (please specify in the notes field)
	+ Option 6: TV or video channels
	+ Option 7: Print ads (billboards, flyers, etc.)
* **Publication Date**: <date> - This is the date on which the images and videos will be published.
* **Consent Type**: <option> - This selects the type of consent provided. Options include:
	+ Option 1: Limited consent: images and videos can be published.
	+ Option 2: Exclusive consent: images and videos can only be published by us.
	+ Option 3: Non-exclusive consent: images and videos can be published by others.
	+ Option 4: Exclusive for a limited time: images and videos can only be published for a specific time period.
	+ Option 5: Non-exclusive for a limited time: images and videos can be published by others for a specific time period.
* **Consent Version**: <version> - This is the version of the consent provided.
* **Notes**: <note> - This is a field where additional information or comments can be added.
