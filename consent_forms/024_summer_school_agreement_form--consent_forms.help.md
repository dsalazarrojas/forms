# Summer School Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Agreement Form is used to collect information from students and authorized persons involved in the summer school program. It helps to keep records and track progress.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

* Start on the "Terms and Conditions" page (page1) and read the terms carefully.
* On each subsequent page, fill in the required fields:
	+ Page 2: Fill in the student's name and email address.
	+ Page 3: Enter the student's phone number.
	+ Page 4: Select the start date of the program.
	+ Page 5: Choose the start time of the program.
	+ Page 6: Fill in the authorized person's name.
	+ Page 7: (Optional) Enter any additional information about the authorized person.
	+ Page 8: Sign the authorized person's signature.
	+ Page 9: Sign the submitter's signature.
	+ Page 10: Fill in the submitter's name.
* On the last page (page 20), select who the form is for: "Authorized Person" or "Submitter".
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Terms and Conditions** (page1, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the summer school program.
* **Name (Student)** (page2, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Email (Student)** (page2, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone (Student)** (page3, text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Start Date** (page4, date, required): Select the start date of the program.
* **Start Time** (page5, time, required): Choose the start time of the program.
* **Authorized Person** (page6, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the authorized person.
* **Authorized Person Signature** (page7, text, required): Sign the authorized person's signature.
* **Submitter Signature** (page8, text, required): Sign the submitter's signature.
* **Name (Submitter)** (page9, text, optional): Fill in the submitter's name.
* **Title (Submitter)** (page10, text, optional): Enter the submitter's title.
* **Phone (Submitter)** (page11, text, optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Email (Submitter)** (page12, text, optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Date (Submitter)** (page13, date, optional): Select the submitter's date.
* **Time (Submitter)** (page14, time, optional): Choose the submitter's time.
* **Notes** (page15, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (page17, select_multiple, required): Check the "Yes" box to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Authorized Person Notes** (page18, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the authorized person.
* **Additional Notes** (page19, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations.
* **Page20** (page20, select_one, required): Select who the form is for: "Authorized Person" or "Submitter".
