# Fire Drill Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals participating in a fire drill event, ensuring they are aware of the risks and procedures involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Fire Drill Participation Consent" field with a brief statement expressing your understanding and consent to participate in the fire drill event.
2. Enter the details of your Emergency Contact 1, including their name and any additional information as required.
3. Enter your Phone Number 1 for emergency contact purposes.
4. Enter your Phone Number 2 (if applicable).
5. Enter your Email 1 for emergency contact purposes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Drill Participation Consent** (`fire_drill_participation_consent_form_1`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief statement expressing your understanding and consent to participate in the fire drill event.
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_1`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person, who will be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Phone Number 1** (`phone_number_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that will be used for emergency contact purposes.
* **Phone Number 2** (`phone_number_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional phone number that may be used for emergency contact purposes (if applicable).
* **Email 1** (`email_1`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that will be used for emergency contact purposes (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person, who will be notified in case of an emergency (if applicable).
