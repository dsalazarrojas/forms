# Release Of Body To Funeral Home Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize the release of a body to a funeral home, providing essential information for the proper handling and care of the deceased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Funeral Home** where the deceased will be taken for processing.
2. Enter the **Facility** where the deceased passed away (e.g., hospital, home, etc.).
3. Provide the **Authorized Person** who has the authority to sign on behalf of the deceased's family or estate.
4. Enter the **Date Of Death** and **Time Of Death** of the deceased.
5. Enter the **Cause Of Death** as accurately as possible.
6. Enter the **Name Of Deceased**, **Address Of Deceased**, **Phone Of Deceased**, and **Email Of Deceased** for contact purposes.
7. Enter the **Funeral Director Name**, **Funeral Director Phone**, and **Funeral Director Email** for contact purposes.
8. Enter a brief **Reason For Release** for the release of the body.
9. Sign the **Release By** section with the name and title of the person authorizing the release.
10. Sign the **Signature Of Release** with a physical signature.
11. Enter the **Date Of Release** and **Time Of Release** for the release authorization.
12. Enter the **Time Signed** and **Date Signed** for the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Funeral Home** (funeral_home, text, required): Enter the name of the funeral home where the deceased will be taken for processing.
* **Facility** (facility, text, required): Enter the location where the deceased passed away (e.g., hospital, home, etc.).
* **Authorized Person** (authorized_person, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person authorizing the release on behalf of the deceased's family or estate.
* **Date Of Death** (date_of_death, date, required): Enter the date of the deceased's passing.
* **Time Of Death** (time_of_death, time, required): Enter the time of the deceased's passing.
* **Cause Of Death** (cause_of_death, text, required): Enter the reason for the deceased's passing.
* **Name Of Deceased** (name_of_deceased, text, required): Enter the name of the person who passed away.
* **Address Of Deceased** (address_of_deceased, text, required): Enter the address of the person who passed away.
* **Phone Of Deceased** (phone_of_deceased, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person who passed away.
* **Email Of Deceased** (email_of_deceased, email, required): Enter the email of the person who passed away.
* **Funeral Director Name** (funeral_director_name, text, required): Enter the name of the funeral director handling the deceased's arrangements.
* **Funeral Director Phone** (funeral_director_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the funeral director.
* **Funeral Director Email** (funeral_director_email, email, required): Enter the email of the funeral director.
* **Release Authorization** (release_authorization, text, required): Enter a brief statement authorizing the release of the body.
* **Release By** (release_by, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person authorizing the release.
* **Signature Of Release** (signature_of_release, text, required): Enter a physical signature of the release authorization.
* **Date Of Release** (date_of_release, date, required): Enter the date of the release authorization.
* **Time Of Release** (time_of_release, time, required): Enter the time of the release authorization.
* **Reason For Release** (reason_for_release, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining the reason for the release.
* **Signed By** (signed_by, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person signing the release.
* **Date Signed** (date_signed, date, required): Enter the date of signature.
* **Time Signed** (time_signed, time, required): Enter the time of signature.

Note: Each field is required for proper completion of the form, and missing information may cause delays in processing. Please ensure that you fill out all necessary fields accurately and completely.
