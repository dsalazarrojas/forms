# youth_sports_safety_workshop_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians regarding their child's participation in a youth sports workshop or similar activity. It's essential for providing informed consent and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2.  Answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3.  Ensure that you've provided all necessary details and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required=false): Please select "Yes" if you give your child permission to participate in the youth sports workshop, or "No" if you do not. This confirms that you are aware of the risks involved.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required=false): If your child has any medical conditions, please list them in this field. This information will be kept confidential but will help the instructors and coaches be aware of any potential needs or requirements.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required=false): Please select "Yes" if you'd like someone to contact you or another specified contact person in case of an emergency, or "No" otherwise.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required=false): If you selected "Yes" for the emergency contact, please provide the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required=false): If you selected "Yes" for the emergency contact, please provide the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, select_one, required=false): If you selected "Yes" for the emergency contact, please indicate the relationship of the contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, family friend, etc.).
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required=false): If your child has any medical notes or concerns, please provide them in this field. This will help the instructors and coaches be aware of any specific needs.
*   **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, required=false): If your child has any medical details or requirements, please list them in this field. This will help the instructors and coaches be aware of any specific needs.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required=false): If your child has any allergies, please select "Yes" next to each relevant allergy from the list.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, required=false): If your child is taking any medications, please select "Yes" next to each relevant medication from the list.
*   **Medical Conditions List** (`medical_conditions_list`, text, required=false): If your child has any medical conditions, please list them in this field. This information will be kept confidential but will help the instructors and coaches be aware of any potential needs or requirements.
*   **Emergency Instructions** (`emergency_instructions`, text, required=false): If you'd like to provide any specific emergency instructions or information, please do so in this field.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required=false): Please provide your name as the parent or guardian of the child participating.
*   **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, text, required=false): Please provide your contact phone number in case we need to reach out to you.
*   **Medical Emergency Contact** (`medical_emergency_contact`, select_one, required=false): Please select "Yes" if you'd like to be contacted in case of a medical emergency, or "No" otherwise.
*   **Medical Contact Relationship** (`medical_contact_relationship`, select_one, required=false): If you selected "Yes" for the medical emergency contact, please indicate the relationship of the contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, family friend, etc.).
*   **Medical Instructions** (`medical_instructions`, text, required=false): If you'd like to provide any additional medical instructions or information, please do so in this field.
*   **Medical Details 1** (`medical_details_1`, text, required=false): If you have any further medical details or information, please provide them in this field.
*   **Parent Permission List** (`parent_permission_list`, text, required=false): Please list any additional permissions or concerns you have regarding your child's participation.
*   **Parent Medications List** (`parent_medication_list`, text, required=false): If your child is taking any medications, please list them in this field.
