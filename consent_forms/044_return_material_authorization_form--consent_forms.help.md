# Return Material Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Return Material Authorization Form is a document used to facilitate the return of materials from customers to the supplier or manufacturer. This form is a critical part of the return process, ensuring that the customer's and supplier's interests are protected and that the return is processed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information, including name, company, address, and contact details.
2. Select the material type and quantity to be returned.
3. Choose the contact method and person responsible for handling the return.
4. Provide the authorization date and time for the return.
5. Add any additional remarks or notes about the return.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your return request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_information)**: This field is used to collect the customer's personal and contact details.
  * Customer Name: The name of the customer who is returning the material.
  * Company: The company where the customer is based.
  * Address: The address of the customer's location.
  * Phone: The customer's contact phone number.
  * Email: The customer's email address.
* **Contact Information (contact_information)**: This field is used to collect the contact details of the person responsible for handling the return.
  * Name: The name of the person responsible for handling the return.
  * Phone: The contact phone number of the person responsible for handling the return.
