# Gender Identification Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gender Identification Declaration Form is a vital document that allows users to express their genuine identity and preferences. This form is designed to help us better understand and respect our users' needs and boundaries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select your gender identity**: Choose from the list of options provided on the first page. If you identify as non-binary, you may be asked to provide a description of what that means to you.
2. **Choose your preferred pronouns**: Select the pronouns that feel most comfortable for you from the options provided on the second page.
3. **Enter your preferred name**: On the third page, type your preferred name as you would like it to be used.
4. **Provide your date of birth**: Enter your date of birth on the fourth page.
5. **Add any additional information**: If you have any other details you'd like to share, please use the text field on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your gender identity?**: Select the option that best reflects how you identify as a person. This may include:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Genderqueer
	+ Agender
	+ Androgyne
	+ Bigender
	+ Gender Fluid
	+ Gender Non-Binary
	+ Gender variant
	+ Neutrois
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What pronouns do you prefer?**: Choose the pronouns that are most comfortable for you, such as:
	+ they
	+ he
	+ she
* **What is your preferred name?**: Enter the name you would like to be used for you, as you would like it to be used.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your birthdate in the format of month/day/year.
* **If non-binary, what does that mean to you?**: If you identify as non-binary, please provide a description of what that means to you.
* **Other gender identity**: If none of the above options apply to you, select "None of the above" or "Other (please specify)".
* **Additional information**: Please use this field to share any additional information you'd like to provide about your identity.
