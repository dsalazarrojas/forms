# Child Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization for a minor to travel with a specific destination and purpose. It is typically completed by the minor's guardian or adult responsible for them.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
- Ensure that you have the permission granted box checked according to your authorization decision.
- Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Minor Full Name**: The name of the child traveling.
- **Minor Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the child.
- **Authorizing Guardian Name**: The full legal name of the guardian or adult authorizing the travel.
- **Guardian Contact Phone**: The best number for the guardian or adult responsible to be reached.
- **Destination and Purpose of Travel**: The specific destination and reason for the travel.
- **Departure Date**: The scheduled start date of the travel.
- **Return Date**: The scheduled end date of the travel.
- **Name and ID of Accompanying Adult**: The name and identification of the adult accompanying the minor during travel.
- **I Grant Permission**: A confirmation that the guardian or adult has granted permission for the minor to travel as described.

## Tips
- Make sure to have a pen or digital signature to confirm authorization.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it's accurate.
- If permission is withheld, do not submit the form, as it will be considered incomplete.
