# Financial Performance Benchmark Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Performance Benchmark Consent Form is designed to collect user information and consent for participating in benchmarking projects. This form is intended for individuals to provide demographic data and agree to participate in benchmarking projects with various organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First name and Last name.
2. Enter your Email address and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide the Company name (if applicable) and Company address.
4. Select your Date of birth and Time of birth.
5. Provide a Note (if any).
6. Choose whether to participate in benchmarking projects (Do you agree to participate in benchmarking projects).
7. If yes, choose which projects you agree to participate in (Which benchmarking projects do you agree to participate in).
8. Select the organizations with which you have a relationship.
9. If you have multiple relationships, select multiple options (With which organizations do you have a relationship).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Company address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company address (if applicable).
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth.
* **Time of birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Select your time of birth.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Do you agree to participate in benchmarking projects?** (`select_one_benchmarking`, select one, optional):
	+ Select "Benchmarking projects" to indicate your agreement to participate in projects.
	+ Select "Do not participate" to indicate your refusal to participate.
	+ Select "Do not answer" to indicate you do not wish to answer.
* **Which benchmarking projects do you agree to participate in?** (`select_multiple_benchmarking`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple projects you agree to participate in.
* **With which organizations do you have a relationship?** (`select_one_organizations`, select one, optional): Select "Company" or "Association".
* **Which organizations do you have a relationship with?** (`select_multiple_organizations`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple organizations with which you have a relationship.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Select the correct options for your relationship with organizations.
- If you have multiple relationships, select all applicable options.
- If you do not agree to participate in benchmarking projects, select "Do not participate" or "Do not answer" for the respective fields.
- If you have any additional comments or notes, provide them in the "Note" field.
