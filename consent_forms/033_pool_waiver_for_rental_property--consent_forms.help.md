# Pool Waiver For Rental Property - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for users who wish to use the rental property's pool facilities. It is designed to collect essential information about the user, their emergency contact, and their pool usage habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the provided fields for any additional information or guidance.
3. Make sure to sign the relevant sections with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pool User Information** (Page 1): Provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
- **Waiver Agreement** (Page 1): Read and agree to the terms of the pool usage agreement. This is a critical section that outlines your responsibility and liability for pool usage.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (Page 1): Provide information about the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool. This could include phone numbers, email addresses, or other contact information.
- **Medical Information** (Page 1): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please disclose them for safety purposes. This information will help the rental property's management ensure your safety while using the pool.
- **Pool Usage Information** (Page 1): Indicate your pool usage habits and preferences, such as whether you plan to swim or engage in other activities like diving or playing.
- **Emergency Procedures** (Page 2): Familiarize yourself with the emergency procedures outlined in this section, which will guide you on what to do in case of an emergency.
- **Additional Information** (Page 2): If you have any additional information or concerns that you'd like to share, please enter them here.
- **Confirmation** (Page 2): Confirm that you have read and understood all the information on this form.
- **Signature** (Page 2): Sign this section to indicate that you have read and agreed to all the terms outlined in the form.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (Page 3): Provide contact information for the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Emergency Contact Signature** (Page 3): Sign this section to confirm that you have authorized the emergency contact to handle any emergency situation on your behalf.
  - **Emergency Contact Name** (Page 3): Enter the name of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Page 3): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Emergency Contact Email** (Page 3): Enter the email address of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Medical Conditions** (Page 3): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please disclose them for safety purposes.
  - **Prescription Medications** (Page 3): Indicate if you are taking any prescription medications that may affect your pool usage.
  - **Number of People** (Page 3): If there are other people in your party using the pool, indicate the number of people.
  - **Activities** (Page 3): Indicate your planned activities while using the pool, such as swimming, diving, or playing.
  - **Waiver Agreement 2** (Page 4): Read and agree to the terms of the pool usage agreement.
  - **Confirmation 2** (Page 4): Confirm that you have read and understood all the information on this form.
  - **Signature 2** (Page 4): Sign this section to indicate that you have read and agreed to all the terms outlined in the form.
  - **Emergency Contact Signature 2** (Page 4): Sign this section to confirm that you have authorized the emergency contact to handle any emergency situation on your behalf.
  - **Emergency Contact Name 2** (Page 4): Enter the name of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Emergency Contact Phone Number 2** (Page 4): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
  - **Emergency Contact Email 2** (Page 4): Enter the email address of the person responsible for handling any emergency situation that may arise while you're using the pool.
