# Community Health Story Archive Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Health Story Archive Consent Form is designed to collect stories and experiences of individuals related to their health journey. It is meant to be filled out by individuals who are willing to share their story to help others who may be going through similar experiences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the storyteller.
2. Provide a brief title for your story.
3. Share your health story in the text field.
4. Check the boxes indicating your consent to archive, use for research, outreach and marketing, and edit for clarity purposes.
5. Select your anonymity preference (real name, pseudonym, or anonymous).
6. Sign the form by typing your name.
7. Finally, provide the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storyteller Full Name** (`storyteller_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as the individual sharing the story.
* **Title of Your Story** (`story_title`, `text`, required): Provide a brief and descriptive title for your story.
* **Your Health Story** (`story_content`, `text`, required): Share your personal and honest experience or journey related to your health.
* **Consent to Archive** (`archive_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if you permit your story to be stored in our permanent digital archive.
* **Consent to Use for Research** (`research_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if you allow researchers to use your story for public health studies.
* **Consent to Use for Outreach and Marketing** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if you permit us to use excerpts from your story for newsletters or social media.
* **Permission to Edit for Clarity** (`edit_permission`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if you grant permission to edit your story for grammar or spelling corrections.
* **Anonymity Preference** (`anonymity_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preference for how you would like to be credited (real name, pseudonym, or anonymous).
* **Participant Electronic Signature** (`signature_confirmation`, `text`, required): Type your name to sign the form.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Permission to Edit for Clarity** (`edit_permission`, `select_one`, required): Check 'True' if you grant permission to edit your story for clarity. 

Note: This permission to edit is meant to ensure that your story can be easily understood by others.
