# Personal Data Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal data from users to facilitate communication and collaboration with our team. Please ensure that you only fill out this form if you intend to provide consent for your personal data to be processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply scroll through the pages and enter your information in each field as prompted. You don't need to worry about saving or submitting the form until you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required/optional: false): This is the first page of the form where you'll provide some basic information about yourself. Please fill out this field if you want to be contacted by our team for any reason related to your data. 
* **Contact Person** (`contact_pers`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is where you can specify the name of the person our team should contact in case we need more information or clarification.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `note`, required/optional: false): This is an additional notes field where you can provide any relevant contact information that our team needs to get in touch with you.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required/optional: false): This is where you'll sign to confirm your consent for our team to process your personal data.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your form can be processed correctly.
* If you're unsure about what to fill out, feel free to ask your contact person for clarification.
* Once completed, submit the form to finalize your consent. 

Please note that the form is optional, but you'll only be able to provide consent for your personal data to be processed if you fill out the "User Information" field. If you're unsure or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out to our team!
