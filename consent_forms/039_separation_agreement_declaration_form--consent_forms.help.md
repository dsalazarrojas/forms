# Separation Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Separation Agreement Declaration Form is a document used to outline the terms of a separation or divorce agreement between two individuals. It is essential to ensure a smooth and amicable separation, detailing the division of assets, financial support, and other crucial aspects of the separation process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the relevant details about the separation agreement, including the terms of the agreement, asset division, and financial support.
2. Provide accurate and complete information about the assets being divided, including their values.
3. Sign the form, including the sign date and time, location, and the names and titles of the signers.
4. Have the document notarized, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Separation Agreement Declaration Form Details** (Page 1)
	+ This section is a general description of the separation agreement and its terms.
* **Agreement Terms** (Page 2)
	+ Describe the terms of the separation agreement, including any specific conditions or requirements.
* **Asset Division** (Page 3)
	+ Enter the number of assets being divided.
* **Child Custody** (Page 4)
	+ Describe the arrangements for child custody.
* **Asset Description** (Page 5)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the assets being divided.
* **Asset Values** (Page 6)
	+ Enter the values of the assets being divided.
* **Payment Terms** (Page 7)
	+ Describe the payment terms of the separation agreement.
* **Financial Support** (Page 8)
	+ Describe any financial support arrangements.
* **Property Division** (Page 9)
	+ Enter the number of properties being divided.
* **Property Description** (Page 10)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the properties being divided.
* **Property Values** (Page 11)
	+ Enter the values of the properties being divided.
* **Other Assets** (Page 12)
	+ Describe any other assets being divided.
* **Other Assets Description** (Page 13)
	+ Provide a detailed description of the other assets being divided.
* **Other Assets Values** (Page 14)
	+ Enter the values of the other assets being divided.
* **Sign Date** (Page 15)
	+ Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Sign Time** (Page 16)
	+ Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Sign Location** (Page 17)
	+ Enter the location where the form was signed.
* **Sign By** (Page 18)
	+ Enter the names and titles of the signers.
* **Signers List** (Page 19)
	+ Enter a list of the signers.
* **Signers Title** (Page 20)
	+ Enter the title of the signers.
* **Notarized** (Page 21)
	+ Indicate if the document has been notarized.
* **Witness 1 Name** (Page 22)
	+ Enter the name of the first witness.
* **Witness 1 Title** (Page 23)
	+ Enter the title of the first witness.
* **Witness 2 Name** (Page 24)
	+ Enter the name of the second witness.
* **Witness 2 Title** (Page 25)
	+ Enter the title of the second witness.

Please note that this form is a general guide and may require additional information or modifications based on specific circumstances. It is essential to consult a legal professional for personalized advice.
