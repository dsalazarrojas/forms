# Psychiatric Treatment Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psychiatric Treatment Permission Form is a document that confirms a patient's consent for psychiatric treatment. It ensures that you have obtained informed consent from the patient or their authorized representative before providing any treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required patient information fields.
2. Select the treatment type, treatment duration, and treatment frequency.
3. Enter the medication dosage (if applicable).
4. Specify the treatment location.
5. Enter the date the consent is given.
6. Sign and date your consent.
7. Enter your name and title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient.
* **Treatment Goal** (`treatment_goal`, text, optional): Specify the treatment goal for the patient.
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of treatment the patient is receiving. Options include:
	+ Medication
	+ Therapy
	+ Hospitalization
* **Treatment Duration** (`treatment_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of the treatment.
* **Treatment Frequency** (`treatment_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Specify how often the treatment will be administered. Options include:
	+ Once a day
	+ Twice a day
	+ Three times a day
* **Medication Dosage** (`medication_dosage`, number, optional): Enter the medication dosage (if applicable).
* **Treatment Location** (`treatment_location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the treatment.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the consent is given.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the consent is signed.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date the patient's consent.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's name and title (if applicable).
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the healthcare provider's name.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable for your specific treatment plan. Ensure you have reviewed the form with the patient or their representative before submitting.
