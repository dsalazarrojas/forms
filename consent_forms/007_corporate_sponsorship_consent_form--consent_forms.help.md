# Corporate Sponsorship Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Corporate Sponsorship Consent Form is a crucial document used to collect information from companies that wish to sponsor events, products, or services offered by the organization. This form ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the sponsorship, including payment schedules and agreement statuses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields with the company's details, such as company name, contact information, and the type of sponsorship being sought.
2. Select the type of sponsorship the company is interested in, which can be one-time, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
3. Provide a specific payment schedule, including the amount to be paid and whether it has been paid or not.
4. Specify the agreement status, such as active, inactive, or cancelled.
5. Include any relevant notes or comments regarding the agreement.
6. Complete the fields for the agreement signed by, signed date, signed time, signed IP address, signed location, and signed browser.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (corporate_sponsorship_consent_form): Enter the name of the company sponsoring the event, product, or service.
* **Contact First Name** (contact_first_name): Enter the first name of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Contact Last Name** (contact_last_name): Enter the last name of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Company Address** (company_address): Enter the address of the company sponsoring the event, product, or service.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Type** (sponsorship_type): Select the type of sponsorship being sought, which can be Sponsorship, Grant, Partnership, or other.
* **Agreement Term** (agreement_term): Enter the term of the agreement, usually in months or years.
* **Agreement Start Date** (agreement_start_date): Select the start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Date** (agreement_end_date): Select the end date of the agreement.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Enter the payment terms, usually in dollars.
* **Payment Schedule** (payment_schedule): Select the payment schedule, which can be one-time, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount): Enter the amount to be paid.
* **Payment Status** (payment_status): Select the payment status, which can be Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* **Agreement Status** (agreement_status): Select the status of the agreement, which can be Active, Inactive, or Cancelled.
* **Agreement Notes** (agreement_notes): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed By** (agreement_signed_by): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (agreement_signed_date): Select the date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed Time** (agreement_signed_time): Select the time the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed IP** (agreement_signed_ip): Enter the IP address used to sign the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed Location** (agreement_signed_location): Enter the location where the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Signed Browser** (agreement_signed_browser): Enter the browser used to sign the agreement.
* **Company Representative** (company_representative): Enter the name of the company representative.
* **Company Representative Email** (company_representative_email): Enter the email address of the company representative.
