# public_advocacy_program_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Advocacy Program Waiver Form is used to obtain participant consent and agreement for the program's activities. This form ensures that participants understand their rights and responsibilities and provides a clear understanding of the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your participant details (page 1).
2. Answer the program details questions (page 2).
3. Indicate your agreement or disagreement with the risks associated with the program (page 3).
4. Select your agreement or disagreement with the legal protections (page 4).
5. Answer the advocacy agreement questions (page 5).
6. Provide your email address (page 6).
7. Enter your phone number (page 7).
8. Provide a waiver statement (page 8).
9. Enter the date and time of your participation (page 9).
10. Enter any additional notes or comments (page 10).
11. Select your agreement or disagreement with the program's terms (page 11).
12. Select your agreement or disagreement with the program's risks (page 12).
13. Enter the date and time of the program manager's signature (page 13).
14. Enter the program manager's name (page 14).
15. Enter the program manager's email address (page 15).
16. Enter the program manager's phone number (page 16).
17. Enter the date of the program manager's signature (page 17).
18. Enter the date of your participant agreement (page 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to identify yourself as a participant.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, number, required/optional): Enter the details of the program you are participating in.
* **Risks** (`risks`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your agreement or disagreement with the risks associated with the program.
* **Legal Protections** (`legal_protections`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your agreement or disagreement with the legal protections.
* **Advocacy Agreement** (`advocacy_agreement`, number, required/optional): Answer the questions related to the advocacy agreement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, required/optional): Provide a waiver statement for your participation in the program.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your participation in the program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your participation in the program.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your participation.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Select your agreement or disagreement with the program's terms.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your agreement or disagreement with the program's risks.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, text, required/optional): Enter the date and time of your participation in the program.
* **Program/Particpant Agreement** (`program_particpant_agreement`, number, required/optional): Answer the questions related to the program and participant agreement.
* **Advocacy Agreement Date** (`advocacy_agreement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the advocacy agreement.
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, text, required/optional): Enter your declaration as a participant.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature as a participant.
* **Program Manager Signature** (`program_manager_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the program manager's signature.
* **Date Program Manager Signature** (`date_program_manager_signature`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the program manager's signature.
* **Program Manager Name** (`program_manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the program manager's name.
* **Program Manager Email** (`program_manager_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the program manager's email address.
* **Program Manager Phone** (`program_manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the program manager's phone number.
* **Date Program Manager Phone** (`date_program_manager_phone`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the program manager's phone number.
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participant_agreement_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your participant agreement.
