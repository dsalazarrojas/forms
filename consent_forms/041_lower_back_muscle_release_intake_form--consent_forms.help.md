# Lower Back Muscle Release Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lower Back Muscle Release Intake Form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their lower back muscle release experience. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the client's current medical status and provide informed consent for further treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please fill in the **General Information** section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the **Symptoms** section, select all the symptoms that you are currently experiencing related to your lower back muscle release.
3. In the **Medical History** section, provide details about any medical conditions or injuries that you have.
4. In the **Medical Procedures** section, select any medical procedures you have undergone.
5. In the **Current Medications** section, list all the medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
6. In the **Allergies** section, list any allergies you have, including medications, foods, etc.
7. In the **Emergency Contacts** section, provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
8. In the **Insurance Info** section, select your insurance coverage type.
9. In the **Medical Professional** section, select the type of medical professional you are seeing.
10. In the **Medical Specialty** section, select the medical specialty of your medical professional.
11. In the **Client Statement** and **Provider Statement** sections, provide any additional comments or information related to your lower back muscle release experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)**: 
    * **Name** (`<name>`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name.
    * **Email** (`<email>`, `text`, `required`): Enter your email address.
    * **Phone Number** (`<phone_number>`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number.
* **Symptoms (Page 2)**: 
    * **Muscle pain or strain in lower back** (`<symptom>`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select this option if you are experiencing muscle pain or strain in your lower back.
    * **Sciatica pain in lower back and leg** (`<symptom>`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select this option if you are experiencing sciatica pain in your lower back and leg.
    * **Numbness or tingling in leg** (`<symptom>`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select this option if you are experiencing numbness or tingling in your leg.
    * **Weakness in legs** (`<symptom>`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select this option if you are experiencing weakness in your legs.
* **Medical History (Page 3)**: 
    * **Medical History** (`<medical_history>`, `note`, `required`): Provide details about any medical conditions or injuries you have.
* **Medical Procedures (Page 4)**: 
    * **Surgery** (`<surgery>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select this option if you have undergone surgery.
    * **Hospitalization** (`<hospitalization>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select this option if you have been hospitalized.
    * **Infection** (`<infection>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select this option if you have an infection.
    * **Other** (`<other>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select this option if you have any other medical procedures.
* **Current Medications (Page 5)**: 
    * **Current Medications** (`<current_medications>`, `text`, `required`): List all the medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequency.
* **Allergies (Page 6)**: 
    * **Allergies** (`<allergies>`, `text`, `required`): List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, etc.
* **Emergency Contacts (Page 7)**: 
    * **Emergency Contacts** (`<emergency_contacts>`, `text`, `required`): Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Info (Page 8)**: 
    * **Insurance Coverage Type** (`<insurance_info>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your insurance coverage type, such as Private Pay, Medicare, Medicaid, or Other.
* **Medical Professional (Page 9)**: 
    * **Medical Professional** (`<medical_professional>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of medical professional you are seeing, such as MD, NP, PA, or Other.
* **Medical Specialty (Page 10)**: 
    * **Medical Specialty** (`<medical_specialty>`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the medical specialty of your medical professional, such as Chiropractor, Massage Therapist, Physical Therapist, or Other.
* **Client Statement (Page 11)**: 
    * **Client Statement** (`<client_statement>`, `note`, `required`): Provide any additional comments or information related to your lower back muscle release experience.
* **Provider Statement (Page 12)**: 
    * **Provider Statement** (`<provider_statement>`, `note`, `required`): Provide any additional comments or information from your medical professional related to your lower back muscle release experience.
