# Mpox Vaccine Screening And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for screening and obtaining consent from individuals regarding Mpox vaccine administration. It collects patient information, vaccine details, and consent status to ensure proper vaccination history and follow-up care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on the "Patient Information" page.
2. Select the date of vaccination on the "Vaccination Date" page.
3. Provide any relevant medical history on the "Medical History" page.
4. On the "Consent Status" page, select one of the options:
	* 'yes' for consent to vaccination
	* 'no' to indicate no consent
5. On the "Vaccination Status" page, select all applicable options for active or inactive vaccination status.
6. Enter the dose number on the "Vaccine Dose" page.
7. Provide any reported side effects on the "Side Effects" page.
8. Schedule the next appointment on the "Next Appointment" page.
9. Enter the doctor's information on the "Doctor Information" page.
10. Provide the contact information of the emergency contact person on the "Emergency Contact" page.
11. Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person on the "Emergency Contact Relationship" page.
12. Enter the contact information of the emergency contact person on the "Contact Information" page.
13. Provide the contact address of the emergency contact person on the "Contact Address" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Vaccination Date**: Select the date of vaccination.
* **Medical History**: Describe any relevant medical conditions or allergies the patient may have.
* **Consent Status**: Indicate whether the patient consents to vaccination.
* **Vaccination Status**: Indicate whether the patient is active or inactive in receiving vaccination.
* **Vaccine Dose**: Enter the number of vaccination doses administered.
* **Side Effects**: Report any side effects experienced after vaccination.
* **Next Appointment**: Schedule the next appointment for vaccination follow-up.
* **Doctor Information**: Enter the doctor's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship of the emergency contact person (e.g., family member, friend).
* **Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
