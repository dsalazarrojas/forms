# Pediatric Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Consent Form is used to obtain consent from the parent or guardian of a child for medical treatment or procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the consent date, which is the date on which the consent was given.
2. Enter the consent time, which is the time on which the consent was given.
3. Enter the patient's name.
4. Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
5. If applicable, select the 'Signature' field to confirm that the parent or guardian has given consent.
6. Enter the consent reason, a brief description of the medical treatment or procedure that will be provided.
7. Enter the contact number, which is the phone number of the parent or guardian.
8. Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
9. Enter the emergency contact's name, which is a contact person in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the consent was given.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, optional): Enter the time on which the consent was given.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' to confirm that the parent or guardian has given consent.
* **Consent Reason** (`consent_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the medical treatment or procedure.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
