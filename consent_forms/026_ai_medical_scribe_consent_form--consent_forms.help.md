# AI Medical Scribe Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Medical Scribe Consent Form is designed to obtain your consent for the use of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) medical scribe during your visit. The AI medical scribe is a tool that records the audio of your visit and converts it into a structured medical note for your physician to review, edit, and finalize.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose of the AI medical scribe.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Read and agree to the terms of the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Clinic / Hospital Name**: Please enter the name of the clinic or hospital where you are visiting.
* **Attending Physician / Provider Name**: Please enter the name of your attending physician or healthcare provider.
* **Consent to Audio Recording**: Please select 'I Consent to Audio Recording' to allow the recording of your visit.
* **Human Review Acknowledgment**: Please select 'I understand' to acknowledge that a human healthcare provider will review and verify the AI-generated notes.
* **Data Privacy & HIPAA Acknowledgment**: Please select 'I understand the privacy terms' to acknowledge the handling of your medical data.
* **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Please select 'I understand' to acknowledge your right to withdraw consent at any time.
* **Would you like a patient-friendly summary of today's visit notes?**: Select 'Yes, please email it to me' if you would like a summary of your visit notes to be emailed to you.
* **Patient Email Address**: Enter your email address if you would like a digital copy of this consent or a visit summary to be sent to you.
* **Digital Signature**: Select 'I agree and provide my digital signature' to confirm that you have read and understood this consent form.
* **Date of Signature**: Please enter the date you are signing this consent form.
* **Parent / Legal Guardian Name (if applicable)**: If the patient is a minor or unable to provide consent, please enter the name of the parent or legal guardian.
* **Internal Use: Staff/Witness Name**: This field is not intended for public use.

Note: All fields marked as 'required' are mandatory and must be filled out before submission.
