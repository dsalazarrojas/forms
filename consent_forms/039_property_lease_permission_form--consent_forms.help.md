# Property Lease Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect permission from tenants to lease properties. It's a crucial document for property management, as it helps keep track of who is allowed to access a property and for how long.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as the tenant.
2. Provide the number and duration of guest visits.
3. Select the subleasing status (Allowed, Not Allowed, or Pending).
4. Provide any additional subleasing details.
5. Sign the form as the tenant.
6. If the landlord needs to sign, enter their date of signature.
7. Enter the date the form was submitted.
8. Select the start date of subleasing (if applicable).
9. Select the end date of subleasing (if applicable).
10. Enter any subleasing status updates (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Lease Permission Form** (`property_lease_permission_form`): This is the main title of the form.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`): Enter the tenant's name.
* **Guest Visits** (`guest_visit_details`): Enter the number and duration of guest visits.
* **Subleasing Status** (`subleasing_status`): Select whether subleasing is Allowed, Not Allowed, or Pending.
* **Subleasing Details** (`subleasing_details`): Provide any additional information about subleasing.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`): Sign the form as the tenant (date is automatically filled in).
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`): Sign the form as the landlord (date is automatically filled in).
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Subleasing Start Date** (`subleasing_start_date`): Select the start date of subleasing (if applicable).
* **Subleasing End Date** (`subleasing_end_date`): Select the end date of subleasing (if applicable).
* **Subleasing Status Update** (`subleasing_status_update`): Enter any updates to the subleasing status (if necessary).

## Tips
Please ensure that you're filling in all required fields. If you're unsure about any field, contact the property management team for assistance.
