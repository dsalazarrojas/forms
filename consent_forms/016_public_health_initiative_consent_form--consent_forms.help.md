# public_health_initiative_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Initiative Consent Form is a document that requires participants to provide information about themselves and their interest in the program. The form is used to ensure that participants understand the initiative's purpose, rules, and guidelines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as required.
3. Optional fields can be filled out if relevant to your participation in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Information** (`study_information`, note, required: false): This section is a general note about the study. You can use it to provide any additional information you think is necessary.
* **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for your contact information, such as phone number and email. Please enter your contact details so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your informed consent to participate in the program. Please indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the researcher to provide their information. Please do not fill this out unless you are the researcher.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, text, required: false): This field is for you to indicate the location of the study. Please enter the location where the study will take place.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, number, required: false): This field is for you to provide the duration of the program. Please enter the number of days or weeks the program will last.
* **Study Status** (`study_status`, select_one, required: false): This field is for you to indicate the status of the study. Please select one of the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **study_status_2** (`study_status_2`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is similar to the previous field, but it allows you to select multiple options. Please choose one or more of the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, required: false): This field is for you to provide the date of completion of the program. Please enter the date in the correct format.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, required: false): This field is for you to provide the time of completion of the program. Please enter the time in the correct format.
* **Researcher Notes** (`researcher_notes`, text, required: false): This field is for the researcher to provide notes about the program. Please do not fill this out unless you are the researcher.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before filling it out.
* Only fill out the required fields.
* Optional fields can be filled out if relevant to your participation in the program.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the researcher or the program administrators for clarification.
