# Audio Engineering Session Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and consent from performers and artists for audio engineering sessions. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured and agreements are made for the recording process, including consent to be recorded, grant of rights, and financial agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the relevant information.
2. Select the appropriate options for the type of recording and grant of rights.
3. If the participant is a minor, enter their parent or guardian's name and signature.
4. Review and sign the form to agree to the cancellation policy and the terms of the recording session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Overview
* **-- Session Overview --** (`section_session_info`, note, not required):
	+ This section provides a brief summary of the recording session, including the date, location, and details of the recording.

### Performer/Artist Full Name
* **Performer/Artist Full Name** (`performer_name`, text, required):
	+ Enter the full name of the performer or artist, as it should appear in the credits.

### Band/Group Name (if applicable)
* **Band/Group Name (if applicable)** (`band_name`, text, not required):
	+ If the recording involves a band or group, enter their name here.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of the recording session.

### Lead Recording Engineer
* **Lead Recording Engineer** (`recording_engineer`, text, required):
	+ Enter the name of the lead recording engineer.

### Recording Details
* **-- Recording Details --** (`section_recording_details`, note, not required):
	+ This section provides more details about the recording, including the type and tracks involved.

### Type of Recording
* **Type of Recording** (`type_of_recording`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of recording from the options: Music - Full Band, Music - Solo Vocalist, Podcast Episode, Voiceover/Commercial, Audio Book, or Other.

### Working Titles of Tracks/Episodes
* **Working Titles of Tracks/Episodes** (`track_titles`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the working titles of the tracks or episodes being recorded.

### Legal Consent and Releases
* **-- Legal Consent and Releases --** (`section_consent`, note, not required):
	+ This section outlines the legal consent and releases for the recording.

### I Consent to Being Recorded (Audio and Video)
* **I Consent to Being Recorded (Audio and Video)** (`recording_release`, select_one, required):
	+ Select whether you consent to being recorded, either I Consent or I Do Not Consent.

### Grant of Rights for Distribution
* **Grant of Rights for Distribution** (`grant_of_rights`, select_one, required):
	+ Select whether you authorize the studio to use excerpts for promotion, either I Authorize or I Do Not Authorize.

### Preferred Name for Credits
* **Preferred Name for Credits** (`credit_attribution`, text, required):
	+ Enter your preferred name to be listed in the credits.

### Is the Participant a Minor?
* **Is the Participant a Minor?** (`minor_participant`, select_one, required):
	+ Select whether the participant is under 18 years of age, either True or False.

### Parent or Guardian Signature (Type Name)
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (Type Name)** (`guardian_name`, text, not required):
	+ If the participant is a minor, enter the parent or guardian's name and signature.

### Session Financials
* **-- Session Financials --** (`section_financial`, note, not required):
	+ This section outlines the financial terms and agreements.

### Agreed Session Fee
* **Agreed Session Fee** (`session_fee_agreed`, number, required):
	+ Enter the agreed session fee.

### I Have Read and Agree to the Cancellation Policy
* **I Have Read and Agree to the Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one, required):
	+ Select whether you agree to the cancellation policy, either I Agree or I Disagree.

### Date of Agreement
* **Date of Agreement** (`agreement_date`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date of this agreement.

### Artist Digital Signature (Type Name)
* **Artist Digital Signature (Type Name)** (`artist_signature`, text, required):
	+ Enter your digital signature, as your type name.
