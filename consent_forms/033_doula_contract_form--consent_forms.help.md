# Doula Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Contract Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect critical information between doulas and their clients. It captures essential details about contract agreements, client information, and other relevant data to ensure a clear understanding between both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review each field carefully**, paying attention to the required fields indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
2. **Enter all required fields**. Failure to do so will prevent you from submitting the form.
3. **Select the correct options** from the dropdown menus as requested, ensuring you choose the correct client, doula, contract type, and status.
4. **Write clear and concise text** in the text fields, providing detailed information about the contract and any other relevant notes.
5. **Review and sign the contract**, ensuring you agree to the terms and conditions outlined.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Details** (Required): Enter a brief description of the contract, detailing the services and expectations.
* **Client Info* (Required): Select all the relevant client information you wish to capture, such as Name, Address, and Phone Number.
* **Doula Info (Required): Select the doula you are working with** to ensure a clear understanding of who is handling the contract.
* **Contract Start Date (Optional): Enter the start date of the contract**, ensuring a clear understanding of when services are to begin.
* **Contract End Date (Optional): Enter the end date of the contract**, marking the end of the services.
* **Contract Notes (Optional): Provide any additional notes or details about the contract**, such as meeting times or dates.
* **Client Agreement (Required): Confirm the client's agreement to the contract terms and conditions**. This is a critical step to ensure both parties are on the same page.
* **Doulas Agreement (Required): Confirm the doulas' agreement to the contract terms and conditions**. This is essential for both parties to understand their roles and responsibilities.
* **Contract Type (Required): Choose the contract type** that best describes the services to be provided.
* **Contract Status (Required): Select the status of the contract**, whether it's active, inactive, or a draft.
* **Agreement Terms (Optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions** that are relevant to the contract.
* **Contract Terms (Optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions** that are specific to the contract.
* **Contract Review (Optional): Set a date for contract review**, ensuring both parties can revisit and update the contract.
* **Doula Signature (Optional): Provide a space for the doula to sign the contract**, marking their agreement.
