# COVID Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to capture a participant's personal and health-related information to inform COVID-19 risk assessment and mitigation. It is essential that this form is completed accurately and truthfully to ensure that participants are aware of and acknowledge the risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
2.  Enter your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3.  Provide the name of the event or activity you plan to participate in, the date of the event, and the location.
4.  Indicate your understanding of the risks associated with COVID-19, including your willingness to assume full responsibility for your health.
5.  Confirm that you have not been exposed to COVID-19 recently and are not under a public health quarantine order.
6.  Declare your COVID-19 vaccination status (if applicable).
7.  Review and agree to follow all COVID-19 safety protocols.
8.  Confirm that all information provided is truthful and accurate.
9.  Finally, read and understand the disclaimer carefully and select the correct options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`disclaimer_name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`disclaimer_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`disclaimer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`disclaimer_phone`, phone, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Activity or Event Name** (`disclaimer_activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event or activity you plan to participate in.
* **Activity Date** (`disclaimer_activity_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Activity Location** (`disclaimer_activity_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event or activity.
* **I Understand the Risks of COVID-19** (`disclaimer_understand_risks`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I understand the risks" to acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19.
* **I Acknowledge Risk of Virus Exposure** (`disclaimer_acknowledge_virus_exposure`, select_one, required): Select "I acknowledge the risk" to understand that you may be exposed to COVID-19.
* **I Assume Full Responsibility for My Health** (`disclaimer_assume_responsibility`, select_one, required): Select "I assume responsibility" to confirm that you will take care of your health.
* **I Agree the Organization is Not Liable** (`disclaimer_agree_no_liability`, select_one, required): Select "I agree, no liability" to acknowledge that the organization is not liable for any COVID-19-related incidents.
* **I Have No COVID-19 Symptoms** (`disclaimer_no_symptoms`, select_one, required): Select "No symptoms" to confirm that you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **I Have Not Been Exposed Recently** (`disclaimer_not_exposed`, select_one, required): Select "Not exposed" to confirm that you have not been exposed to COVID-19 recently.
* **I Am Not Under Quarantine Order** (`disclaimer_not_quarantined`, select_one, required): Select "Not quarantined" to confirm that you are not under a public health quarantine order.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`disclaimer_vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current vaccination status.
* **I Will Follow All COVID-19 Safety Protocols** (`disclaimer_follow_protocols`, select_one, required): Select "I will follow protocols" to agree to follow all COVID-19 safety protocols.
* **All Information I Provided is Truthful** (`disclaimer_truthful_information`, select_one, required): Select "Information is truthful" to confirm that all information provided is accurate.
* **I Have Read and Understand This Disclaimer** (`disclaimer_read_understand`, select_one, required): Select "I have read and understand" to confirm that you have read and understand the disclaimer carefully.

## Tips

*   Please review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any question or have concerns, please discuss with the organizer or authority.
*   This form is essential for risk assessment and mitigation purposes, so please complete it carefully.
