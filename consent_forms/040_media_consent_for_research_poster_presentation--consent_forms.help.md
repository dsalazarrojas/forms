# Media Consent For Research Poster Presentation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Consent For Research Poster Presentation form is used to collect consent from individuals regarding their media usage in a research poster presentation. This form ensures that individuals are aware of and agree to the usage of their media in the presentation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and instructions.
2. Fill out all required fields, indicated by the required label.
3. Select the appropriate options for each field as instructed.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Consent Form** (`media_consent_form`, text, required): This is the main form where you will provide your consent.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the media presentation.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide your contact information so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Project Info** (`project_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional project information, please provide it here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the presentation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the presentation.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you agree to the media usage terms and conditions.
* **Signed Media Permissions** (`signed_media_permissions`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" for any media items you wish to give permission for, e.g., photo, video, etc.
* **Researcher** (`researcher`, select_one, optional): Check "Yes" if you are a researcher for this presentation.
* **Research Group** (`research_group`, select_multiple, optional): Check "Yes" if you are a member of a research group for this presentation.
Please ensure you carefully fill out all the fields as instructed to ensure accurate and up-to-date records. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.

## Tips
* Make sure to only fill out this form once for each instance of media usage in the presentation.
* If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
