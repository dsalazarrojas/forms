# Donor Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Feature Consent Form is a tool for donors to consent to share their information with your organization. This form asks donors to confirm what type of information they are comfortable sharing, ensuring their data privacy and security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being asked.
2. For each page, answer the questions according to your preferences.
3. Submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Feature Consent** (`donor_feature_consent_form_1`, `text`, optional): This is a text field where you can provide your consent statement for what type of information you are comfortable sharing. Please enter a clear and concise statement explaining what you are willing to share.
* **Contact Information** (`donor_feature_consent_form_2`, `text`, optional): If you wish to be contacted, please provide your contact information here.
* **How Do You Want to be Contacted?** (`donor_feature_consent_form_3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the communication methods you are comfortable with to be contacted by our organization. You can choose multiple options.
	+ **Email**: We will contact you via email.
	+ **Phone**: We will contact you via phone.
	+ **Mail**: We will contact you via mail.
* **What Information to Share** (`donor_feature_consent_form_4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of information you are comfortable sharing with us.
	+ **Address**: Share your address with us.
	+ **Contact Information**: Share your contact information.
	+ **Donations History**: Share your donation history with us.
	+ **Personal Bio**: Share your personal bio with us.

## Tips
- Take your time to carefully review each field before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
