# creditReportAuthorizationForm - Help Guide

## Purpose
The credit report authorization form is used to obtain user consent for access to credit reports.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reading this guide carefully**.
2. Complete all required fields marked as `required` true. This form has no required fields, so no action is necessary.
3. Fill in your Individual Details (Page 1).
4. Provide details about your employment history (Page 2).
5. Select your current or past employer from the options provided (Page 3).
6. Enter your employer's address and contact information (Page 4).
7. Choose the type of credit report you are authorizing (Page 5).
8. Select whether you grant or deny access for your credit report (Page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Individual Details (Page 1)
* **Individual Details** (`individualdetails`, text, optional): Enter your name, surname, and any other relevant details you want to share about yourself.

### Employment History (Page 2)
* **employment history** (employmenthistory, text, optional): Describe your employment history, including any relevant job titles, dates, and reasons for leaving.

### Current Employer (Page 3)
* **currentEmployer** (currentEmployer, select_one, optional): Choose your current or past employer from the options provided. If you are not employed, select "Landlord" or "Lender" depending on your situation.

### Employer Address (Page 4)
* **employerAddress** (employmenthistoryemployeraddress, text, optional): Enter your employer's address if different from your personal address.

### Employer Contact (Page 4)
* **employerContact** (employmenthistoryemployercontact, text, optional): Enter your employer's contact information if different from your personal contact information.

### Credit Report Type (Page 5)
* **creditReportType** (creditreporttype, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of credit report you are authorizing. Options include Landlord Report, Employment Verification Report, and Credit Inquiry Report.

### Consent (Page 6)
* **consent** (consent, select_one, optional): Select whether you grant or deny access for your credit report.
