# Global Wellness Research Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent from participants and researchers for a global wellness research project.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Make sure to sign and date each section as required.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the form author.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `note`, required: false): This section is for providing participant demographic information. Please fill out this section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Researcher Information** (`researcher_information`, `note`, required: false): This section is for providing researcher demographic information. Please fill out this section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Consent to Record** (`consent_to_record`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select 'Participant' if you consent to record your information, or 'Researcher' if you do not.
* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select 'Yes' if you consent to share your information, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all applicable options to indicate your consent to participate in the project.
* **Researcher Consent** (`researcher_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all applicable options to indicate your consent to participate in the project.
* **Participant Contact Information** (`participant_contact`, `date`, required: false): This section is for providing participant contact information. Please fill out this section with your contact details.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `select_one`, required: true): Please sign and date this section to indicate your consent.
* **Researcher Contact Information** (`researcher_contact`, `date`, required: false): This section is for providing researcher contact information. Please fill out this section with your contact details.
* **Researcher Signature** (`researcher_signature`, `select_one`, required: true): Please sign and date this section to indicate your consent.
* **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, `date`, required: false): This section is for indicating the date of your signature.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for any additional notes from the participant.
* **Researcher Notes** (`researcher_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for any additional notes from the researcher.
* **Form Notes** (`form_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for any additional notes about the form.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the version of the form you are using.
* **Form Revision** (`form_revision`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the revision of the form you are using.
* **Date of Revision** (`date_of_revision`, `date`, required: false): This section is for indicating the date of the last revision of the form.
* **Version Notes** (`version_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for any notes about the version of the form.
* **Revision Notes** (`revision_notes`, `text`, required: false): This section is for any notes about the revision of the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to the form author for clarification.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* This form is subject to change as the research project progresses. Please check back regularly for updates. 

Note: The form's version and revision numbers will indicate when changes have been made. Please refer to these numbers to ensure you are using the latest version of the form. 

Please submit this form after reviewing and understanding the content carefully. 

By signing and submitting this form, you are indicating your consent to participate in the research project.
