# Educational Game Beta Trial Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants in an educational game beta trial, ensuring they are aware of the terms and conditions of the trial and have provided their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Educational Game Beta Trial Consent Form**: This is the title of the form, which will not require any input from the participant.
2. **Participant Details**: Enter your name, age, and gender in the text field.
3. **Participant Age**: Provide your age to confirm you are eligible to participate.
4. **Contact Details**: Select one or more contact methods (email, phone, or other) for communication during the trial.
5. **Participant Experience**: Optionally, provide any relevant experience with similar educational games or research trials.
6. **Informed Consent**: Read and acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of the trial.
7. **Terms and Conditions**: Select "Yes" to indicate you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
8. **Research Participant**: Confirm whether you are participating in this research or not.
9. **Contact Method**: Optionally, choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or other) for communication during the trial.
10. **Emergency Contacts**: Provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact person.
11. **Medical Condition**: Optionally, indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation.
12. **Health Insurance**: Optionally, provide your health insurance information.
13. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Confirm the relationship with the emergency contact person.
14. **Participant Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your consent to participate.
15. **Researcher Signature**: Sign this form to confirm you have obtained consent to conduct the research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Fill in your full name and any other relevant details for identification purposes.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter your age to confirm you are eligible to participate.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of your preferred contact methods (email, phone, or other) for communication during the trial.
* **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, text, optional): Provide any relevant experience with similar educational games or research trials.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, note, required): Read and acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of the trial.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Research Participant** (`research_participant`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are participating in this research or not.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, optional): Optionally, choose your preferred method of contact (email, phone, or other) for communication during the trial.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): Provide the name and contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact information (phone number or email) of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, text, optional): Optionally, provide your health insurance information.

* **Research Team** (`research_team`, text, optional): Optionally, provide the name of the research team.
