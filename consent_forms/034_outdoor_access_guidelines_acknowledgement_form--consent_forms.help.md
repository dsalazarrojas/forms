# outdoor_access_guidelines_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record the user's acknowledgement of outdoor access guidelines. It's used to ensure that users have read and understood the guidelines for accessing outdoor areas. Please ensure that you have filled out this form correctly before submitting it. This form should only be filled out once and by the same user. If you've already filled out this form, please do not repeat it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information: Enter your name and any other relevant user information in the User Information field.
2. Add your signature: Add your electronic or handwritten signature to confirm that you've read and understood the guidelines.
3. Provide Contact Details: Enter any contact details you'd like us to have in case we need to reach you while you're accessing the outdoor areas.
4. Select your Access Date: Choose the date you plan to access the outdoor areas.
5. Choose your Access Time: Select the time you plan to access the outdoor areas.
6. Mention the Duration of Visit: Enter the length of time you plan to spend accessing the outdoor areas.
7. Contact Phone: Enter your contact phone number.
8. Contact Email: Enter your contact email.
9. Other Details: Add any other relevant information that might be useful to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This is where you enter your personal details to help us identify you. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **User Signature**: This is where you add your electronic or handwritten signature to confirm that you've read and understood the guidelines. Please make sure that your signature is clear and legible.
* **Contact Details**: This is where you enter any contact details we might need to reach you while you're accessing the outdoor areas. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Access Date**: This is where you select the date you plan to access the outdoor areas. Please choose a date when you'll be able to access the areas without interruptions.
* **Access Time**: This is where you select the time you plan to access the outdoor areas. Please choose a time that suits you best.
* **Duration of Visit**: This is where you enter the length of time you plan to spend accessing the outdoor areas. Please be realistic about the time you'll need.
* **Contact Phone**: This is where you enter your contact phone number. We might need to reach you if we have any questions or concerns.
* **Contact Email**: This is where you enter your contact email. We might need to reach you if we have any further questions or need to send you information.
* **Other Details**: This is where you can add any other relevant information that might be helpful to us. Please be as specific as possible.
