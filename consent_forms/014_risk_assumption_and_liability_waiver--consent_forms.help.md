# Risk Assumption And Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants about their understanding and acceptance of risks associated with a specific activity or project. It helps in ensuring that participants are aware of their responsibilities and potential liabilities, and it serves as a legal document to protect both the participant and the organization involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review each question carefully and select the correct answer for each field.
2. Select "Yes" if you understand and agree with the statement, and "No" if you do not.
3. Fill in any additional information if required in the text fields.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
