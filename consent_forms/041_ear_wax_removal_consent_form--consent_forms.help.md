# Ear Wax Removal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ear Wax Removal Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide their medical history, consent for the ear wax removal procedure, and contact information for their medical contact.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, not required): Provide your personal details such as name, email, etc.
2. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, not required): Describe your medical history, including any previous surgeries, allergies, or conditions that may affect this procedure.
3. **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, true): Choose whether you have any other medical conditions that would affect this procedure.
4. **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, true): List any medical conditions you have that may be a contraindication for this procedure, such as asthma or high blood pressure.
5. **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, true): List any allergies you have, such as allergic to latex or antibiotics.
6. **Medications** (`medications`, select_multiple, true): List any medications you are currently taking, such as blood thinners or diabetes medications.
7. **Medical Contact** (`medical_contact`, select_one, true): Choose the type of medical contact that will be present during the procedure, such as a family doctor or surgeon.
8. **Medical Contact Name** (`medical_contact_name`, text, not required): Provide the name of your medical contact.
9. **Medical Contact Phone** (`medical_contact_phone`, text, not required): Provide the phone number of your medical contact.
10. **Relationship** (`medical_contact_relationship`, select_one, true): Choose how you are related to your medical contact, such as brother or mother.
11. **Procedure Details** (`procedure_details`, text, not required): Provide any additional information about the procedure, such as previous experiences or concerns.
12. **Risks** (`risks`, text, not required): List any potential risks associated with the procedure, such as bleeding or infection.
13. **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Sign below to confirm you have reviewed and understand the form.
14. **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Date you completed the form.
15. **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, not required): Signature of the healthcare provider who will be performing the procedure.
16. **Date/Time** (`date_time`, time, not required): Date and time you completed the form.
17. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, not required): Your full name.
18. **Email** (`patient_email`, email, not required): Your email address.
19. **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, not required): Name of the healthcare provider who will be performing the procedure.
