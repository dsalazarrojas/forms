# Accessibility Evaluation Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from participants before they begin the accessibility evaluation. It includes sections for participant details, study specifics, required accommodations, consent statements, and participant rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your full name**: In the "Full Name" field, enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Enter your email address**: In the "Email Address" field, provide your primary email address where you can be reached for communication about the evaluation.
3. **Enter your phone number**: In the "Phone Number" field, provide your mobile phone number for any additional communication needs.
4. **Select your study details**: In the "Title of the Study" field, select the title of the study you are participating in.
5. **Choose your participation date**: In the "Date of Participation" field, select the date you plan to participate in the evaluation.
6. **Check for accommodations**: If you require any accommodations during the evaluation, select them in the "Do you require any of the following for the session?" field.
7. **Specify any other needs**: If you have any additional needs or requirements not listed, describe them in the "Any other specific needs or requirements" field.
8. **Consent to participate**: In the "I voluntarily agree to participate in this accessibility evaluation" field, select "I Consent" to confirm your willingness to participate.
9. **Consent to recording**: In the "I consent to being video or audio recorded for research analysis" field, select "I Consent" to agree to recording.
10. **Confirm data confidentiality**: In the "I understand my personal data will be kept confidential" field, select "I Understand" to confirm your understanding.
11. **Right to withdraw**: In the "I understand I can stop the session at any time for any reason" field, select "I Understand" to confirm your understanding.
11. **Sign and date**: Finally, sign the form with your full name and date to digitally validate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Provide your primary email address where you can be reached for communication about the evaluation.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number for any additional communication needs.
* **Title of the Study** (`study_title`, text, required): Select the title of the study you are participating in.
* **Date of Participation** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to participate in the evaluation.
* **Do you require any of the following for the session?** (`required_accommodations`, select_multiple, required): If you require any accommodations during the evaluation, select them to ensure we are prepared.
* **Any other specific needs or requirements** (`other_needs`, text, required): If you have any additional needs or requirements not listed, describe them here.
* **I voluntarily agree to participate in this accessibility evaluation** (`participation_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to confirm your willingness to participate.
* **I consent to being video or audio recorded for research analysis** (`recording_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to agree to recording.
* **I understand my personal data will be kept confidential** (`data_confidentiality`, select_one, required): Select "I Understand" to confirm your understanding.
* **I understand I can stop the session at any time for any reason** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required): Select "I Understand" to confirm your understanding.
* **Signature - Type Full Name** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your full name digitally to validate your consent.
* **Date of consent** (`signing_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your consent digitally.
