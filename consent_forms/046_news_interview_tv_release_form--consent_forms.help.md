# News Interview TV Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the participant's consent for a TV interview and gather essential information for the shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the participant's consent by selecting "Yes" in the "participant_consent" field.
2. Fill in the participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
3. Enter the participant's email in the "participant_email" field.
4. Provide the participant's phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
5. Select the date and time of the shoot in the "shoot_date" and "shoot_time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the location of the shoot in the "shoot_location" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the shoot in the "notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tv_release_form**: This is the page heading, indicating the form's purpose.
* **participant_name**: This field is for the participant's name.
* **participant_email**: This is the participant's email address.
* **participant_phone**: This is the participant's phone number.
* **participant_consent**: Select "Yes" to indicate the participant's consent for the TV interview.
* **shoot_date**: This is the date of the TV shoot.
* **shoot_time**: This is the time of the TV shoot.
* **shoot_location**: This is the location of the TV shoot.
* **notes**: This is an optional field for any additional notes about the shoot.

## Tips
* Make sure to get the participant's consent before proceeding with the shoot.
* Ensure accurate contact information (email and phone number) are collected to facilitate communication and coordination.
* Double-check the shoot date, time, and location to avoid confusion.
