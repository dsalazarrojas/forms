# Spray Tanning Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and consent from clients before they receive spray tanning services. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure a safe and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, contact number, and email address on pages 1-3.
2. Provide emergency contact information on page 4.
3. Read and acknowledge the risks associated with spray tanning on page 6.
4. Report any allergy information, medical conditions, or medical history on page 7.
5. Choose your preferred spray tan products on page 10.
6. Sign and date the form on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number where we can reach you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you about your appointment.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **Risk Acknowledge** (`risk_acknowledge`, `note`, required): Read and acknowledge the risks associated with spray tanning.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_information`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical history, please list it here.
* **Spray Tan Products** (`spray_tan_products`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred spray tan products from the list.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `note`, required): Read and sign the consent form to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.
