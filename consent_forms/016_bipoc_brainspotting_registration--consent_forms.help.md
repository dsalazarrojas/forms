# BIPOC Brainspotting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BIPOC Brainspotting Registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants who are interested in registering for the Brainspotting program. This form will help the program staff understand your preferences, needs, and experiences to provide a safe and inclusive environment for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to receive registration confirmation.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** (if you have one).
4. Answer the question about your **Racial or Ethnic Identity**.
5. Choose your **Experience with Brainspotting**.
6. Select your **Preferred Session Time** (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or No preference).
7. If applicable, select your **Preferred Session Date**.
8. If you have a specific area you'd like to work on, provide more details in the **Specific Area You Want to Work On** field.
9. If you have any **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**, let us know.
10. If you have any **Health Conditions** that may impact your participation, please provide them.
11. If you are **Currently working with a Therapist or Counselor**, indicate this.
12. Finally, confirm your **Consent to Participate** and **Confidentiality Policy Acceptance**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant Full Name (required)
**Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.

### 2. Email Address (required)
**Email Address**: Enter the email address you want to receive registration confirmation to.

### 3. Phone Number (optional)
**Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, you can provide it for contact purposes.

### 4. Racial or Ethnic Identity (required)
**Racial or Ethnic Identity**: Select your racial or ethnic identity as described in the options.

### 5. Have You Experienced Brainspotting Before? (required)
**Have you experienced brainspotting before?**: Choose your experience level with brainspotting from the options.

### 6. Preferred Session Time (required)
**Preferred Session Time**: Select your preferred time for sessions from the options provided.

### 7. Preferred Session Date (optional)
**Preferred Session Date**: If you have a preferred date, select it from the calendar.

### 8. Specific Area You Want to Work On (optional)
**Specific Area You Want to Work On**: Describe the area you'd like to focus on during the sessions.

### 9. Accessibility or Accommodation Needs (optional)
**Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: If you have any needs, let us know to ensure your comfort.

### 10. Any Health Conditions (optional)
**Any Health Conditions**: If you have health conditions that may impact your participation, provide more details.

### 11. Currently Working with a Therapist or Counselor (optional)
**Currently working with a Therapist or Counselor**: Indicate your current counseling status.

### 12. I Consent to Participate (required)
**I consent to participate in brainspotting sessions**: Confirm your consent to participate.

### 13. I Understand and Accept Confidentiality Policies (required)
**I understand and accept confidentiality policies**: Accept the confidentiality policies.

### 14. I Acknowledge Potential Emotional Intensity of Sessions (required)
**I acknowledge potential emotional intensity of sessions**: Confirm your understanding of the sessions' intensity.

### 15. How Did You Hear About This Program? (optional)
**How did you hear about this program?**: Select how you found out about the program.

### 16. Additional Information or Concerns (optional)
**Additional Information or Concerns**: If you have any further details or concerns, provide them here.
