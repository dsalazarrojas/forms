# International NGO Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International NGO Waiver Form is designed to obtain waiver and acknowledgment from participants or individuals involved in specific activities or events related to international NGOs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for "participant information" on page 1. This field is not required, but it's an opportunity to provide more information about the participant.
2. Provide a brief description of the waiver on page 2. This field is also not required, but it's an important section to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved.
3. Enter a number to indicate the level of risk involvement on page 3.
4. Describe any potential liability on page 4.
5. Select one of the options for acknowledgment on page 5. This field is not required, but it's a confirmation that you understand the risks and liability.
6. Write a short description of any risk and liability on page 6.
7. Finally, provide your signature on page 7, which is not required but a formal confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant information (page 1)**: Choose 'Yes' if you are a participant or have any involvement in the activity or event. This field is not required.
* **waiver (page 2)**: Briefly describe any waiver or agreement you have regarding the risks involved in the activity or event.
* **risk involvement (page 3)**: Enter a number to indicate the level of risk involved in the activity or event. For example, 1-5.
* **liability (page 4)**: Describe any potential liability you may have regarding the activity or event.
* **acknowledgment (page 5)**: Choose 'Yes' if you understand and acknowledge the risks and liability involved in the activity or event.
* **risk and liability (page 6)**: Briefly describe any risk and liability you are aware of regarding the activity or event.
* **signature (page 7)**: Provide your signature as a formal confirmation of your understanding and agreement with the waiver and acknowledgment.
