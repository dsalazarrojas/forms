# medical_equipment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain medical equipment authorization for use in healthcare settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct Medical Equipment Type from the list of options.
2. Enter the name of the medical equipment being authorized.
3. Briefly describe the medical equipment.
4. Specify the location where the equipment will be used.
5. Choose the frequency of usage from the list of options.
6. Select the medical staff involved in the usage of the equipment.
7. Enter the start date when the equipment will be used.
8. Enter the end date when the equipment will be used.
9. Sign the form with your official signature.
10. Select the authorized person from the list of options.
11. Provide any additional comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Equipment Type** (`Medical Equipment Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct type of medical equipment from the list of options.
- **Name of Medical Equipment** (`Name of Medical Equipment`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the medical equipment being authorized.
- **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the medical equipment.
- **Location of Equipment** (`Location of Equipment`, `text`, required): Specify the location where the equipment will be used.
- **Usage Frequency** (`Usage Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the frequency of usage from the list of options.
- **Medical Staff Involvement** (`Medical Staff Involvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the medical staff involved in the usage of the equipment.
- **Usage Start Date** (`Usage Start Date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date when the equipment will be used.
- **Usage End Date** (`Usage End Date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date when the equipment will be used.
- **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form with your official signature.
- **Authorized Person** (`Authorized Person`, `select_one`, required): Select the authorized person from the list of options.
- **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the equipment.
