# donation_pickup_gps_tracking_consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request consent from donors for GPS tracking during the pickup of their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select "Yes" or "No" for each question. If you are a donor, select "Yes" to allow us to track your donation, or "No" if you do not want to be tracked. Fill in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, etc.) if they are applicable and relevant to your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor** (`donation_pickup_gps_tracking_consent_page`, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are a donor and if you would like to be tracked.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Street Number** (`street_number`, text): Enter the number of the street where your donation was dropped off.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, text): Enter the apartment or unit number where your donation was dropped off (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text): Enter the zip code of the location where your donation was dropped off.
* **Pickup Address** (`pickup_address`, text): Enter the address where you dropped off your donation.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date): Enter the date you dropped off your donation.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time): Enter the time you dropped off your donation.
* **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text): Enter a brief description of the location where you dropped off your donation.
* **Reason for pickup** (`reason`, text): Enter a brief description of why you are donating.
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, text): Enter any additional information about your donation.
* **Consent to GPS tracking** (`consent`, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you consent to GPS tracking.
* **GPS Data** (`tracking_data`, select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to be tracked. 

Note: Please select "Yes" or "No" for the consent fields, even if it's not required to indicate your preference for GPS tracking.
