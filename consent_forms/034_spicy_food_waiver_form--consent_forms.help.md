# Spicy Food Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spicy Food Waiver Form is a document that requires information from individuals, likely for liability or identification purposes. The form collects general contact and demographic information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Spicy Food Waiver Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Street address in the Street field.
4. Enter your City in the City field.
5. Enter your State in the State field.
6. Enter your Zip code in the Zip field.
7. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
8. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
9. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
10. Enter your Time (if applicable) in the Time field.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Note field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Street** (`form_street`, text, required/optional): Enter your street address where you reside.
* **City** (`form_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (`form_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the two-letter state abbreviation of your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`form_zip`, number, required/optional): Enter your zip code as a five-digit number.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`form_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of birth, if applicable (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the correct format for Date of Birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* Use the correct format for Time (HH:MM AM/PM).
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
