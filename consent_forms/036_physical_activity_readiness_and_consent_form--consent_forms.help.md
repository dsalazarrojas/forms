# Physical Activity Readiness And Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Physical Activity Readiness And Consent Form is a critical document designed to evaluate an individual's physical readiness for exercise and physical activity. It aims to identify potential risks and limitations associated with physical activity and ensures that the individual is aware of their capabilities and limitations. This form is a requirement for anyone planning to engage in physical activity, such as exercise or sports, to ensure their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
2.  Fill out all required fields, marked as required.
3.  Provide accurate and honest information about your medical history, physical activity level, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **A thorough medical history**: A medical history form will help identify any pre-existing conditions that may affect the physical activity. Please fill out this section with your medical history, including any chronic illnesses, injuries, or allergies.
    *   Please answer this question thoroughly to provide a comprehensive medical history.
*   **What is your current level of physical activity?**: Select one of the options provided to describe your current level of physical activity. This will help us understand your current physical capabilities and limitations.
    *   Choose the option that best describes your daily physical activity level.
*   **Do you have any of the following medical conditions?**: Select all applicable medical conditions from the list provided. This will help us identify potential risks associated with physical activity.
    *   Be honest and thorough when selecting your medical conditions.
*   **Please list your current medications**: List all your current medications, including dosages and frequencies. This is crucial for ensuring your safety during physical activity.
    *   Provide accurate information about your current medications.
*   **What are your physical activity limitations?**: Describe your physical activity limitations, including any physical constraints or difficulties you may experience during exercise.
    *   Be honest about your physical limitations to ensure your safety.
*   **Who is your emergency contact?**: Provide contact information for your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
    *   Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
*   **Describe your plan for physical activity**: Select one of the options provided to describe your plan for physical activity. This will help us understand your goals and expectations.
    *   Choose the option that best describes your plan for physical activity.
*   **Do you have any physical activity risks?**: Select all applicable risks associated with physical activity from the list provided. This will help us identify potential hazards.
    *   Be honest and thorough when selecting your risks.
*   **Do you have any physical activity precautions?**: Select all applicable precautions associated with physical activity from the list provided. This will help us understand your safety needs.
    *   Be honest and thorough when selecting your precautions.
*   **What are your physical activity goals?**: Describe your physical activity goals, including what you hope to achieve through exercise.
    *   Be specific and clear about your goals.
*   **What is your physical activity schedule?**: Provide the date for your physical activity schedule, including the start and end dates.
    *   Provide accurate information about your physical activity schedule.
*   **How will you track your physical activity progress?**: Select one of the options provided to describe how you will track your progress.
    *   Choose the option that best describes how you will track your progress.

Note: The fields and their explanations are based on the provided YAML schema. The form is designed to be completed with accurate and honest information to ensure the individual's safety and well-being during physical activity.
