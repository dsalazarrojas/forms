# Student Ministry Event Medical Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Ministry Event Medical Release Form is designed to collect medical information and event details for students participating in a ministry event. This form is required to ensure the student's safety and well-being during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `student_name` field.
2. Provide your contact phone number in the `contact_phone` field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
4. Describe your relationship with the student in the `student_relationship` field.
5. Provide any relevant medical information in the `medical_info` field.
6. List any medications the student is currently taking in the `medication` field.
7. Describe any allergies the student has in the `allergies` field.
8. Provide emergency contact information in the `emergency_contact_info` field.
9. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the `emergency_contact_relationship` field.
10. Provide a brief description of the event in the `event_info` field.
11. Select the type of event the student will be participating in from the `event_type` field.
12. Enter the date of the event in the `event_date` field.
13. Enter the time of the event in the `event_time` field.
14. Provide the location of the event in the `event_location` field.
15. Enter a brief description of the event in the `event_description` field.
16. Answer the question regarding medical supervision in the `medical_supervision` field.
17. If necessary, provide additional information regarding medical supervision in the `medical_supervision_explain` field.
18. If necessary, provide any additional notes regarding medical supervision in the `medical_supervision_note` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Student Relationship** (`student_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the student.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical information.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications the student is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): Describe any allergies the student has.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, text, optional): Select the type of event the student will be participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Medical Supervision** (`medical_supervision`, select_one, required): Answer the question regarding medical supervision.
* **Medical Supervision Explain** (`medical_supervision_explain`, text, optional): If necessary, provide additional information regarding medical supervision.
* **Medical Supervision Note** (`medical_supervision_note`, text, optional): If necessary, provide any additional notes regarding medical supervision.
