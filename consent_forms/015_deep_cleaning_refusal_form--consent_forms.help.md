# Deep Cleaning Refusal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deep Cleaning Refusal Form is a template designed to collect information from clients who refuse deep cleaning services. Its purpose is to gather details about the client's decision and the reason for refusal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client details (name, phone number, and email) on page 1.
2. List the services declined on page 2.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of refusal on page 3.
4. Sign the form on page 5 to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information.
5. Sign the form on page 6 as the service provider to confirm the refusal.
