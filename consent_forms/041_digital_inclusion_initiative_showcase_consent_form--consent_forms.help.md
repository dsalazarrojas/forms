# Digital Inclusion Initiative Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Inclusion Initiative Showcase Consent Form is designed to gather participant information and consent for sharing stories related to digital inclusion initiatives. This form helps us understand your interest in sharing your stories and ensures we have your consent to do so.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant information in the "Participant Information" field.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the stories you would like to share in the "Stories Shared" field.
4. If you select "Other" in the "Stories Shared" field, please specify the stories you would like to share in the "Other (Please specify)" field.
5. Make your selection in the "Consent" field regarding sharing your stories.
6. Enter the number of data points you would like to share in the "Data Shared" field (if applicable).
7. Enter your contact phone number and address in the respective fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your name, organization, and title (if applicable).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address, which we will use to contact you.
* **Stories Shared**: Select the type of stories you would like to share, or specify "Other" to enter your own description.
* **Other (Please specify)**: If you selected "Other" in the "Stories Shared" field, enter a brief description of the stories you would like to share.
* **Consent**: Select "I consent to sharing my stories" if you agree to share your stories, or "I do not consent to sharing my stories" if you do not.
* **Data Shared**: (If applicable) Enter the number of data points you would like to share, up to 10. This field is optional, but please be accurate for successful data synchronization.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive correspondence from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Address**: Enter your contact address (if applicable).
