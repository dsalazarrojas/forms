# social_media_profile_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent for sharing your social media profiles and contact information. It is essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that your privacy and preferences are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, take a moment to review each field and understand what information is being requested.
2. **Answer each field**: Fill out each field accurately and completely. If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
3. **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Social Media Profile Sharing Consent
* **Social Media Profile Sharing Consent** (`social_media_profile_sharing_consent`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can give your consent for sharing your social media profiles. If you select "Yes", you are giving consent for sharing your profiles. If you select "No", you are not giving consent.

### Share My Profile
* **Share My Profile** (`sharing_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to confirm whether you want to share your profile with others. Select "Yes" if you want to share your profile, and "No" if you don't want to share it.
* Options: "Yes" and "No" are the available options for this field.

### Email
* **Email** (`email_consent`, email, required/optional): This field is for your email address. You can enter your email address if you want to share it with others.
* Note: If you enter an email address, you are giving consent for it to be shared.

### Profile Sharing
* **Profile Sharing** (`profile_sharing`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can specify the level of profile sharing you are comfortable with (e.g., "Public", "Private", etc.) or leave it blank if you don't want to share your profile.

### Campaign Details
* **Campaign Details** (`campaign_details`, text, required/optional): This field is for describing the campaign or purpose for which you are sharing your social media profiles and contact information. If you are sharing your profile for a specific campaign, enter the campaign details here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Leave blank any fields that don't apply to you.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
