# Virtual Academic Panel Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Academic Panel Participation Consent Form is designed to collect participant information and consent for virtual academic panel participation. This form ensures that participants are aware of the event purpose, format, and expected outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their purpose of participation, the format of the event, and their expected outcome.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you.
3. Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
4. Check the "Participant Consent" field to indicate your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Purpose of the Event** (`purpose_of_event`, `text`, required): Describe the purpose of your participation in the virtual academic panel.
* **Format of the Event** (`format_of_event`, `select_one`, required): Select the format of the event from the available options.
* **Expected Outcome** (`expected_outcome`, `number`, required): Indicate your expected outcome or result from participating in the virtual academic panel.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `text`, required): Confirm your understanding and agreement to participate in the virtual academic panel.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Provide any necessary contact information, such as your name and email.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your participation.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Use a format that is easy to understand and fill in.
- Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure your understanding and agreement with the terms.
