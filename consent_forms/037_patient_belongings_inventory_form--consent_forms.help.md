# patient belongings inventory form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and manage the personal belongings of patients, including their contact information, medical conditions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **Name** in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the relevant **Item Category** from the provided options (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
3. Provide a brief **Description** of the item in the "Description" field.
4. Select the **Status** of the item from the provided options (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
5. Enter the **Quantity** of the item in the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter the **Value** of the item in the "Value" field.
7. Specify the **Date Brought** to the hospital in the "Date Brought" field.
8. Specify the **Time Brought** to the hospital in the "Time Brought" field.
9. Provide the **Contact Person** responsible for the item in the "Contact Person" field.
10. Enter the **Contact Number** of the contact person in the "Contact Number" field.
11. Provide the email address of the contact person in the "Email Contact" field.
12. Enter the patient's **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
13. Provide the patient's **Address** in the "Address" field.
14. Enter the patient's **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
15. Enter the patient's **Email** address in the "Email" field.
16. Select the patient's **Gender** from the provided options (e.g., Male, Female, Other).
17. Select the patient's **Status** from the provided options (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
18. Enter the patient's **Medical Condition** in the "Medical Condition" field.
19. Enter the patient's **Medical History** in the "Medical History" field.
20. Specify the **Item Location** where the item is currently stored in the "Item Location" field.
21. Enter the patient's **Weight** in the "Weight" field.
22. Enter the patient's **Height** in the "Height" field.
23. Enter the patient's **Allergies** in the "Allergies" field.

* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Email Contact**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender from the provided options (e.g., Male, Female, Other).
* **Status**: Select the patient's status from the provided options (e.g., Status 1, Status 2, Status 3).
* **Medical Condition**: Enter the patient's medical condition.
* **Medical History**: Enter the patient's medical history.

* **Weight**: Enter the patient's weight.
