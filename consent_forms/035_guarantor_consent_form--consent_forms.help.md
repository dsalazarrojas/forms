# Guarantor Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guarantor Consent Form is used by business entities (e.g., property owners) to obtain the consent of a guarantor to enter into a rental agreement with a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the tenant's information on Page 1: Tenant Information.
2. Fill in the guarantor's information on Page 2: Guarantor Information.
3. Provide the guarantor's consent on Page 3: Guarantor Consent.
4. Enter the contact information for the tenant and guarantor on Page 4: Contact Information.
5. Add any additional notes on Page 5: Notes.
6. Enter the tenant's email on Page 6: Tenant Email.
7. Enter the guarantor's email on Page 7: Guarantor Email.
8. Enter the guarantor's phone number on Page 8: Guarantor Phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tenant Information
* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the tenant's details.

### Guarantor Information
* **Guarantor Information** (`guarantor_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the guarantor's details.

### Guarantor Consent
* **Guarantor Consent** (`guarantor_consent`, `text`, required: false): In this section, the guarantor should provide their consent to be the guarantor for the tenant.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the contact information for the tenant and guarantor.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required: false): This is a space for any additional notes or comments about the rental agreement.

### Tenant Email
* **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, `email`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the tenant's email address.

### Guarantor Email
* **Guarantor Email** (`guarantor_email`, `email`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the guarantor's email address.

### Guarantor Phone
* **Guarantor Phone** (`guarantor_phone`, `text`, required: false): This is the section where the business entity (e.g., property owner) should enter the guarantor's phone number.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* Save the completed form for future reference.
