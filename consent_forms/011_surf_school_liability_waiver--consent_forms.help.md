# Surf School Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surf School Liability Waiver form is designed to gather information about a participant's liability and medical status before engaging in a surf school activity. By filling out this form, participants acknowledge that they understand the risks associated with surfing and take responsibility for their own safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the form carefully, including all the sections and questions.
2. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information presented.
4. Make sure to keep the completed form handy in case of any emergency situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Surf School Liability Waiver** (`form_title`, not applicable, optional): This is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): Please provide your name, age, and other relevant contact information.
* **Parent Contact Info** (`parent_contact_info`, text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, please provide your parent or guardian's contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, please provide an emergency contact person's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Please provide a phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, please get a signed permission slip from your parent or guardian.
* **Conducted By** (`minor_conducted_by`, text, not applicable): If you are under 18 years old, please confirm that you are accompanied by a parent or guardian during the activity.
* **Participant Conducted By** (`participant_conducted_by`, text, not applicable): Please confirm that you are conducting the activity by yourself.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, text, not applicable): Please acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with surfing.
* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, text, not applicable): Please explain your awareness of the risks associated with surfing.
* **Surfing Equipment** (`surfing_equipment`, text, optional): Please indicate if you have any surf equipment experience or need help with rentals.
* **Surfing Environment** (`surfing_environment`, text, optional): Please describe the surfing environment you are familiar with (e.g., ocean, lake, etc.).
* **Emergency Release** (`emergency_release`, text, not applicable): Please acknowledge that you understand the emergency procedures in case of an injury.
* **Medical Declaration** (`medical_declaration`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please inform us.
* **Other Risks** (`other_risks`, text, optional): If you are aware of any other risks associated with surfing, please specify.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, optional): Please provide your medical insurance information (if applicable).
* **Emergency Procedure** (`emergency_procedure`, text, not applicable): Please acknowledge that you understand the emergency procedures in case of an injury.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, text, required): Please acknowledge that you are signing this form freely and voluntarily.
* **Form Signature** (`form_signature`, text, required): Please sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understood the information presented.

## Tips
- Make sure to read and understand each section before signing the form.
- If you are under 18 years old, please get a signed permission slip from your parent or guardian.
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your instructor or surf school staff.
