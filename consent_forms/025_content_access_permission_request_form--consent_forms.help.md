# Content Access Permission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request access permission to specific content. Fill out this form with your user details and provide a clear reason for your access request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your user details in the first section, providing your name, identification number, and email address.
2. In the second section, specify the content you need access to and choose the duration of access.
3. Provide a clear justification for your access request in the third section.
4. Select your current security clearance level and confirm if access permission is granted or not.
5. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **User Identification Number**: Enter your unique identification number as required.
- **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Content Requested for Access**: List the titles or links of the specific materials you need access to.
- **Requested Access Duration**: Choose how long you need access to the content. Options are:
	+ One-time (24 hours)
	+ Short-term (1 week)
	+ Medium-term (1 month)
	+ Long-term (1 year)
	+ Perpetual
- **Justification for Access Request**: Explain why you need access to this content.
- **Current Security Clearance Level**: Select your current clearance level:
	+ Standard (Public)
	+ Low (Staff)
	+ Medium (Manager)
	+ High (Director)
	+ Top Secret (Admin)
- **Access Permission Granted?**: Confirm if you've been granted access or not.
- **Request Completed?**: Confirm if the request is completed.
- **I confirm that the information provided is accurate**: Select to confirm the accuracy of your information.
- **I Agree/ I Disagree**: Select to confirm or disagree that the information provided is accurate.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your access request is processed efficiently.
