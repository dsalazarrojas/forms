# Online Discussion Archiving Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online discussion archiving consent form is a crucial document that provides consent for the archiving of discussions. It ensures that all relevant information about the discussion, including its title, location, and participants, is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Describe the discussion details, including the title, date, time, and location (if applicable).
3. Choose how you prefer the discussion to be archived (online or offline) and whether you agree to the archiving of your discussion (yes or no).
4. Indicate your preference for data collection (once, ongoing, or periodically) and the method of archiving (automatic or manual).
5. Confirm that you have completed the form correctly (complete or partial) and whether the archiving is complete (yes or no).
6. Provide the time of submission.
7. Verify the form completion and archiving completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submission Details** (`form_submission`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your submission.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Discussion Details** (`discussion_details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the discussion, including its title, date, time, and location (if applicable).
* **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose "I agree to the archiving of our discussion" to confirm your consent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about the discussion.
* **Discussion Title** (`discussion_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the discussion.
* **Discussion ID** (`discussion_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the discussion ID.
* **Discussion Type** (`discussion_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether the discussion was online, offline, or in-person.
* **Discussion Date** (`discussion_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the discussion.
* **Discussion Time** (`discussion_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the discussion.
* **Discussion Location** (`discussion_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the discussion.
* **Discussion Attendees** (`discussion_attendees`, `text`, required/optional): List the attendees of the discussion.
* **Archiving Method** (`archiving_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the archiving method (automatic or manual).
* **Data Collection Period** (`data_collection_period`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the data collection period (once, ongoing, or periodically).
* **Consent Form Type** (`consent_form_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the consent form type (online or offline).
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether the form is complete or partial.
* **Archiving Completion** (`archiving_completion`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether the archiving is complete (yes or no).
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person signing the form.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the submission details.
* **Discussion URL** (`discussion_url`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the discussion URL.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time of submission.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the form ID.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before completing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
