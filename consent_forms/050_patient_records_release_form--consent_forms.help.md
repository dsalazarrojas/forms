# Patient Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Records Release Form is used to obtain consent from patients or their representatives to access their medical records. This form ensures that patients' medical information is only accessed by authorized individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Navigate through each page, filling out the required fields in the correct order.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields, as indicated by the "required" label next to each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Patient Records** (`patient_records`, `number`, required: false): Enter your patient ID number or a brief description of the records you are requesting.
* **2. Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date on which you are authorizing access to your records.
* **3. Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Select `Yes` if you are authorizing your healthcare provider to access your records, or `No` otherwise.
* **4. Release Type** (`release_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of records you want to access. You can choose `All`, `Summary only`, or `Partial`.
* **5. Records Destination** (`records_destination`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where you would like your records to be sent or stored.
* **6. Authorization Method** (`authorization_method`, `text`, required: false): Enter the method you used to authorize access to your records.
