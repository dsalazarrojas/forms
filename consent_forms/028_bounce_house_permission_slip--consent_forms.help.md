# Bounce House Permission Slip - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bounce House Permission Slip is a form designed to obtain permission from parents or guardians for their children to participate in activities involving bounce houses. This form ensures that parents or guardians provide necessary information about their child's health and consent for their child's participation in these activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the "Event Information" section with the name and date of the event.
2.  Enter the child's name and age in the "Participant Details" section.
3.  Provide your name and contact information in the "Parent / Guardian Information" section.
4.  Indicate your child's health status and any medical conditions or allergies in the "Safety and Waiver" section.
5.  Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Host / Event Name** (`host_event_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the event or program where your child will participate.
* **Event Date** (`date_of_event`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the event or program.
* **Child Name** (`child_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your child's full name.
* **Child Age** (`child_age_val`, Number, Required): Enter your child's age.
* **Parent / Guardian Name** (`guardian_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_phone_no`, Text, Required): Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **My child is in good health and has no physical conditions that would restrict use** (`health_status`, Select One, Required): Select "True" if your child is in good health and does not have any physical conditions that would restrict their participation.
* **Any allergies or medical conditions we should be aware of?** (`medical_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any allergies or medical conditions you would like to disclose.
* **I hereby give permission for my child to participate in activities involving the bounce house** (`consent_statement`, Note, Optional): This is a statement confirming your consent for your child's participation.
* **I agree to waive all liability for any injuries that may occur** (`liability_agree`, Select One, Required): Select "I Agree" to waive liability for any injuries that may occur.
* **Parent / Guardian Signature - Type Full Name** (`guardian_sign`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Date Signed** (`sign_dt`, Date, Required): Enter the date you signed this form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by parents or guardians, so it's essential to have the necessary information and consent before allowing your child to participate in activities involving bounce houses.
