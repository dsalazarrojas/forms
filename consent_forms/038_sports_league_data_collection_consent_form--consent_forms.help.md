# Sports League Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and participant information for sports leagues to ensure accurate and secure data management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select "How to Complete This Form" below for step-by-step instructions.
2. Carefully read through each field on the form, making sure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. Enter your name in the "League Details" field, which is the name or title of the sports league you are participating in or working with.
4. In the "Participant Details" field, enter your own details, such as your name and contact information (if applicable).
5. In the "Emergency Contacts" field, provide the contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
6. In the "Contact Person" field, enter the name and contact information of the person managing the sports league (if applicable).
7. In the "E-mail" field, enter your email address for official communication from the sports league.
8. In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number for easy contact.
9. Select your choice in the "Consent" field to indicate your permission for data collection and management.
10. If you are a league administrator, select "League Admin 1" or "League Admin 2" in the "League Admin" field.
11. In the "emergency_name", "emergency_email", and "emergency_phone" fields, enter the contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **League Details**: Enter the name or title of the sports league you are participating in or working with.
* **Participant Details**: Enter your own details, such as your name and contact information (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contacts**: Provide the contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the person managing the sports league (if applicable).
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address for official communication from the sports league.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Consent**: Select "I consent to the collection of my data" to indicate your permission for data collection and management.
* **League Admin**: If you are a league administrator, select "League Admin 1" or "League Admin 2" to indicate your role.
* **emergency_name**: Enter the name of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_email**: Enter the email of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_phone**: Enter the phone number of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Date**: Enter the date (not currently required but may be used as a timestamp for data management).
* **Signature**: This is a placeholder for your digital signature (not currently functional but may be used in the future).

## Tips
- Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the emergency contact information provided is up-to-date and accurate.
- If you are a league administrator, choose the correct role in the "League Admin" field.
- Read and understand the consent terms before making your selection.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the sports league management for further clarification.
