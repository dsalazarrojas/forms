> # Medical Study Publication Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Study Publication Release Form is a standard template used by researchers to release their published medical studies for review. This form helps verify the study's publication status and ensures the accuracy and credibility of the publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Study Title** field with the title of the published medical study.
2. Select the **Publication Status** to indicate whether the study is active or inactive.
3. Enter the **Publication Date** of the study.
4. Fill in the **Study ID** field with the unique identifier of the study.
5. Provide the **Researcher Name** who conducted the study.
6. Enter the **Institution Name** where the study was conducted.
7. Enter the **Department Name** where the study was conducted.
8. Enter your contact **Email** and **Phone Number** for further communication.
9. Sign the **Signature Date** with the date you are signing the form.
10. Provide your **Signature** to confirm your approval of the study.
11. Indicate whether you **E-Signatures** the form with 'Yes' or 'No'.
12. Fill in the **Approved By** field with the name of the person or organization approving the study.
13. Enter the **Approval Date** when the study was approved.
14. Enter the **Reviewer Name** who reviewed the study.
15. Enter the **Reviewer Email** and **Reviewer Phone Number** for further communication.
16. Sign the **Review Date** with the date you finished reviewing the study.
17. Provide your **Comment** about the study (optional).
18. Indicate the **Publication Status Approved** to confirm your approval or rejection.
19. Sign the **Review Signature Date** with the date you finished reviewing the study.
20. Provide your **Reviewer Signature** to confirm your review.
21. Indicate whether you **E-Reviewer Signatures** the form with 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Study Publication Release** (Field 1): Enter the name of the form to indicate you are releasing this medical study for review.
* **Study Title** (Field 2): A unique title of the published medical study.
* **Publication Status** (Field 3): Select whether the study is active or inactive.
* **Publication Date** (Field 4): The date when the study was published.
* **Study ID** (Field 5): A unique identifier of the published medical study.
* **Researcher Name** (Field 6): The name of the researcher who conducted the study.
* **Institution Name** (Field 7): The name of the institution where the study was conducted.
* **Department Name** (Field 8): The name of the department where the study was conducted.
* **Email** (Field 9): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (Field 10): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Signature Date** (Field 11): Sign the date when you sign the form.
* **Signature** (Field 12): Provide your signature to confirm your approval.
* **E-Signatures** (Field 13): Indicate whether you sign the form with 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Approved By** (Field 14): Enter the name of the person or organization who approved the study.
* **Approval Date** (Field 15): Enter the date when the study was approved.
* **Reviewer Name** (Field 16): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (Field 17): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone Number** (Field 18): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Review Date** (Field 19): Enter the date when the review was finished.
* **Comments** (Field 20): Provide any comments about the study (optional).
* **Publication Status Approved** (Field 21): Confirm your approval or rejection of the study.
* **Review Signature Date** (Field 22): Sign the date when the review was finished.
* **Reviewer Signature** (Field 23): Provide your reviewer's signature to confirm the review.
* **E-Reviewer Signatures** (Field 24): Indicate whether you sign the form with 'Yes' or 'No'.

Note: All fields with a label ending in "date" are date fields where you can select or input a date. Fields with a label ending in "signature" are text fields where you can sign your name. Fields with a label ending in "signatures" are select one or multiple fields where you can select 'Yes' or 'No'.
