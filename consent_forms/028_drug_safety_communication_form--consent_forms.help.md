# Drug Safety Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's reaction to a drug, which is essential for assessing the drug's safety and efficacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the "Patient Age" field.
3. Enter the drug name in the "Drug Name" field.
4. If the patient had a reaction to the drug, enter it in the "Reaction" field (if not, leave this field blank).
5. If the reaction had a specific severity, describe it in the "Reaction Description" field.
6. If the patient had a pre-existing medical condition, enter it in the "Patient Condition" field.
7. If the patient received a specific dose of the medication, enter it in the "Medication Dose" field.
8. Enter any additional information about the patient's reaction in the "Reaction Description" field.
9. If the patient needs to follow-up with the doctor, enter the follow-up details in the "Follow-up" field.
10. Enter the name of the doctor who is reporting the reaction in the "Reporting Doctor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (1, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Age** (2, number, required): Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Drug Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the drug the patient took.
* **Reaction** (4, text, optional): Enter any reaction or side effect the patient had after taking the drug.
* **Severity** (5, text, optional): Enter the severity of the reaction (if any).
* **Patient Condition** (6, text, optional): Enter any pre-existing medical condition that may be relevant to the reaction.
* **Medication Dose** (7, text, optional): Enter the specific dose of the medication taken.
* **Reaction Description** (8, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the patient's reaction to the drug.
* **Follow-up** (9, text, optional): Enter any follow-up information, such as a phone number or contact method.
* **Reporting Doctor** (10, text, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who is reporting the reaction.
