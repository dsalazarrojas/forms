# Temporary Account Information Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about temporary accounts from users who have been assigned to work on these accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "Title" field.
2. Select the category of the temporary account from the "Category" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the account in the "Description" field.
4. Choose the tools used to access the account in the "Assigned Tool" field.
5. Enter the name of the form in the "Form ID" field.
6. Select the category of the form from the "Category" field.
7. Enter a brief description of the form in the "Description" field.
8. Enter the title of the form in the "Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form, which will be used to identify the form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the temporary account. This field will help us understand the context of the account.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the temporary account.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the tools used to access the account. This will help us understand how the account is accessed.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter the name of the form, which will be used as a unique identifier.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Select the category of the form. This will help us understand the purpose of the form.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the form.
* **Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form, which will be used to identify the form.
