# Academic Study Video Diary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Academic Study Video Diary Consent Form is a crucial document designed to obtain informed consent from participants in an academic study that involves video diary recording. It outlines the scope and limitations of the study, ensures participants understand their rights and responsibilities, and guarantees the confidentiality and anonymity of their data.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, participants must carefully read and understand each section:

*   Start by filling out the contact details: `Participant Name`, `Email Address`, and `Institution`.
*   Provide the `Study Title` and `Principal Investigator` name.
*   Answer the question regarding the `Study Duration`.
*   Provide consent for various aspects of the study: `Consent to Participate`, `Consent to Video Recording`, `Consent to Audio Recording`, `Consent to Image Use`, `Consent to Data Analysis`, `Consent to Publication`, and `Consent to Data Retention Period`.
*   Optionally, mention any `Special Conditions or Restrictions`.
*   Sign the form with the `Participant Signature`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `Text`, Required): Please enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `Email`, Required): Enter your contact email for this study.
*   **Institution** (`participant_institution`, `Text`, Required): Provide your university or organization affiliation.
*   **Study Title** (`study_title`, `Text`, Required): Enter the title of the academic study involving video diaries.
*   **Principal Investigator** (`study_principal_investigator`, `Text`, Required): Provide the name of the lead researcher for the study.
*   **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, `Text`, Required): Answer the question about the study duration.
*   **Consent to Participate** (`consent_to_participate`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to participate.
*   **Consent to Video Recording** (`consent_to_video_recording`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to video recording.
*   **Consent to Audio Recording** (`consent_to_audio_recording`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to audio recording.
*   **Consent to Image Use** (`consent_to_image_use`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to image use.
*   **Consent to Data Analysis** (`consent_to_data_analysis`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to data analysis.
*   **Consent to Publication** (`consent_to_publication`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I consent" if you agree to publication.
*   **Data Retention Period** (`data_retention_period`, `Select One`, Required): Answer the question regarding data retention.
*   **Data Deletion Rights** (`data_deletion_rights`, `Select One`, Required): Select "Yes, I understand" to acknowledge your right to data deletion.
*   **Special Conditions or Restrictions** (`special_conditions_restrictions`, `Text`, Optional): Provide any special conditions or restrictions.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `Text`, Required): Sign the form with your full name as an electronic signature.

## Tips

*   Read each field carefully before answering.
*   Ensure you understand your rights and responsibilities as a participant.
*   Sign the form only if you are comfortable with the study details and conditions.
*   Review the study title and principal investigator's name to understand the study's scope and context.
*   Be aware that data retention and deletion are crucial aspects of this study.
