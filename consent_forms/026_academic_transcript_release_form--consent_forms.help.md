# Academic Transcript Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Transcript Release Form is a document that authorizes the release of your academic transcript to a specified recipient. This form is used to obtain an official or unofficial transcript, depending on your needs, and is typically used for employment verification, graduate school applications, or other purposes that require verification of your academic credentials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your student full legal name, date of birth, student ID number, email address, and current mailing address.
2. Specify the dates you attended the institution.
3. Choose the type of transcript you wish to release (official or unofficial).
4. Select the purpose of the release and the number of copies you want.
5. Check the box authorizing the release of your transcript in compliance with FERPA regulations.
6. Date and sign the form to confirm your authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full legal name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your academic records.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student ID number**: Enter your student ID number as issued by the institution.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for any further information.
* **Current mailing address**: Enter your current mailing address where the transcript will be sent.
* **Dates of attendance**: Specify the dates you attended the institution, e.g., Fall 2020 - Spring 2024.
* **Degree awarded (if applicable)**: If you have earned a degree, enter the degree name and date awarded.
* **Name of recipient (person or organization)**: Enter the name of the person or organization you are authorizing to receive your transcript.
* **Recipient mailing address or email**: Enter the address or email of the recipient where the transcript will be sent.
* **Purpose of transcript release**: Choose the reason for the transcript release from the options provided (e.g., employment verification, graduate school application).
* **Type of transcript requested**: Choose whether you want an official or unofficial transcript.
* **Number of copies**: Specify the number of transcripts you want to release.
* **I authorize the release of my academic transcript in compliance with FERPA regulations**: Check this box to confirm your authorization.
* **Date of authorization**: Date and sign the form to confirm your authorization.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in the form accurately to avoid any issues with the transcript release process.
- Double-check the recipient's address and purpose of release to ensure it's correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your institution's administration or registrar's office for assistance.
