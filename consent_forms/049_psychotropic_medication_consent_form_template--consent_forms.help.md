# Psychotropic Medication Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from patients for the use of psychotropic medication. It is essential to understand the form's purpose and its implications on the patient's well-being and treatment. Please ensure that this form is only used for patients who are eligible to receive psychotropic medication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format (month/day/year).
3. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient's Address" field.
4. Enter the prescriber's name in the "Prescriber Name" field.
5. Enter the prescriber's contact information in the "Prescriber Contact" field.
6. Select "True" or "False" in the "Consent" field to indicate the patient's consent.
7. Enter the name of the medication in the "Medication Name" field.
8. Enter the dose and strength of the medication in the "Dose Strength" field.
9. Enter the start date of the medication in the "Start Date" field in the format (month/day/year).
10. Enter the stop date of the medication in the "Stop Date" field in the format (month/day/year).
11. Enter any reason for using the medication in the "Reason For Use" field.
12. Enter any potential side effects of the medication in the "Potential Side Effects" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What Is This?
*   **What Is This?** (`medication_consent_form_template`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the medication and its purpose.
### 2. Patient Name
*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name.
### 3. Date of Birth
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format (month/day/year).
### 4. Patient's Address
*   **Patient's Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address for communication and delivery purposes.
### 5. Prescriber Name
*   **Prescriber Name** (`prescriber_name`, text, optional): Enter the prescriber's name.
### 6. Prescriber Contact
*   **Prescriber Contact** (`prescriber_contact`, text, optional): Enter the prescriber's contact information.
### 7. Consent
*   **Consent** (`consent`, select one, required): Select "True" to indicate the patient's consent.
### 8. Medication Name
*   **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the medication.
### 9. Dose Strength
*   **Dose Strength** (`dose_strength`, text, optional): Enter the dose and strength of the medication.
### 10. Start Date
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the medication in the format (month/day/year).
### 11. Stop Date
*   **Stop Date** (`stop_date`, date, optional): Enter the stop date of the medication in the format (month/day/year).
### 12. Reason For Use
*   **Reason For Use** (`reason_for_use`, text, optional): Enter any reason for using the medication.
### 13. Potential Side Effects
*   **Potential Side Effects** (`potential_side_effects`, text, optional): Enter any potential side effects of the medication.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that the patient's consent is voluntary and informed.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary fields are completed.
*   Verify the patient's address for accurate delivery and communication.
*   Enter the prescriber's contact information for easy communication.
*   Select "True" to indicate the patient's consent.
*   Enter the medication's name and dose strength accurately.
*   Enter the start and stop dates of the medication in the correct format.
*   Provide a clear reason for using the medication.
*   Document any potential side effects of the medication.
