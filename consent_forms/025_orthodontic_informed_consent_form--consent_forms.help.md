The form is designed to collect information from patients for informed consent related to orthodontic treatment. This form is intended to be used by orthodontic practices to gather necessary information from patients before starting treatment.

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients for orthodontic treatment. This form is used to ensure that patients understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment and to gather necessary information for their medical history, contact details, and treatment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information on page 1: "patient_info".
2. Provide contact information on page 2: "contact_info".
3. Disclose any medical history on page 3: "medical_history".
4. List any allergies on page 4: "allergies".
5. Enter insurance information on page 5: "insurance".
6. Read and sign the consent page (6): "consent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **patient_info**
(patient_info, text, optional)

This is the first page of the form where you will enter the patient's information. Enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details as instructed.

### 2. **contact_info** (contact_info, text, optional)

On this page, please enter your contact details such as your address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information for communication purposes.

### 3. **treatment_terms**
(treatment_terms, text, optional)

Here, you will find detailed information about the treatment, including risks and benefits. Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any of the information.

### 4. **emergency_contact**
(emergency_contact, text, optional)

In case of an emergency, who should we contact? Please provide the name and contact information of the emergency contact.

### 5. **medical_history**
(medical_history, text, optional)

Please disclose any medical conditions, surgeries, or health issues that may affect your treatment.

### 6. **allergies**
(allergies, text, optional)

List any allergies or sensitivities you may have that could impact your treatment.

### 7. **insurance**
(insurance, text, optional)

Provide your insurance information to help us process payments and coverage.

### 8. **consent**
(consent, text, optional)

Read and sign this page to confirm you have read and understood the information provided and to grant consent for treatment. 

## Tips
