# Art Exhibit Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Art Exhibit Waiver Form is a contract between an artist and a gallery for the exhibition of artwork. It ensures that both parties understand their rights, responsibilities, and compensation terms for the exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your artist information: 
   - **Artist Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
   - **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
   - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
   - **Waiver Date**: Enter the date of the exhibition.

2. Enter exhibit information: 
   - **Exhibit Name**: Enter the title of the artwork or exhibit being displayed.
   - **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the exhibition venue.
   - **Exhibit Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the exhibition.

3. Add artwork details: 
   - **Artwork Title**: Enter the title of the artwork.
   - **Medium or Material**: Enter the material or medium used to create the artwork.
   - **Artwork Dimensions**: Enter the height, width, and depth of the artwork.
   - **Artwork Estimated Value**: Enter the estimated value of the artwork for insurance purposes.

4. Select your terms and conditions: 
   - **Liability Waiver**: Select 'Yes, I agree' if you agree to waive liability for damage, theft, or loss of the artwork.
   - **Display Permission**: Select 'Yes, I agree' if you grant permission to display the artwork as agreed.
   - **Photography Permission**: Select 'Yes, I agree' if you grant permission for photography and documentation of the artwork.
   - **Publicity Rights**: Select 'Yes, I agree' if you grant permission for use of images in publicity materials.
   - **Insurance Coverage**: Select 'Yes, I agree' to the type of insurance coverage provided.
   - **Commission or Fee Percentage**: Enter the percentage of the commission or fee taken by the gallery.
   - **Acknowledge and Accept**: Select 'Yes, I acknowledge and accept' all terms in this waiver.
