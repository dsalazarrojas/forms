# Parental Consent Form Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent Form Upload is designed to collect essential information from parents for consent purposes in various settings such as educational institutions or medical facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the parent's name.
2.  Enter the consent date.
3.  Capture a digital signature.
4.  (Optional) Upload relevant files or documents.
5.  (Optional) Upload a digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the consent was given.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Capture the parent's digital signature.
*   **Signature Upload** (`uploaded_signature`, text, optional): Upload a digital signature if not captured via the signature field.
*   **Files** (`uploaded_files`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files or documents.
*   **Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, text, optional): Upload additional documents supporting the consent.
*   **Videos** (`uploaded_videos`, text, optional): Upload videos for further evidence.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter the parent's name correctly.
*   Ensure the consent date is accurately recorded.
*   Capture a clear digital signature.
*   Only upload relevant files or documents if necessary.
*   Review all uploaded materials before submission.
