# estheticianConsentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information from clients or customers to facilitate efficient communication and scheduling with the salon or aesthetician service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter Salon Information**: Fill in the required details about the salon, such as its name and contact information.
2. **Enter Esthetician Information**: Provide the esthetician's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
3. **Enter Appointment Details**: Select the date and time of the appointment, and make sure to fill in the required information for the appointment.
4. **Enter Contact Information**: Fill in your contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
5. **Sign and Submit**: Sign the form to confirm your consent, and click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Salon Information (Salon Information, text, required)**: Please fill in the name of the salon, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Esthetician Information (Esthetician Information, text, required)**: Enter the name and contact information of the esthetician providing the service.
* **Appointment Details (Appointment Details, date, required)**: Select the date and time of the appointment.
* **Appointment Date (Appointment Date, time, optional)**: If you'd like to specify a particular time for the appointment, enter it here.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information, text, required)**: Fill in your phone number and email address (if applicable) so that the salon can get in touch with you.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, required)**: Enter your phone number for the salon to contact you.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: If you have an email address, enter it here so the salon can contact you via email.
* **Signature (Signature, text, required)**: Sign the form to confirm your consent for the appointment.
