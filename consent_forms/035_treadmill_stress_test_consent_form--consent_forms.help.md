# Treadmill Stress Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treadmill Stress Test Consent Form is used to document a patient's medical history, allergies, and medications, as well as their consent to participate in a treadmill stress test. The form helps to ensure that the patient's medical history and consent are thoroughly documented before undergoing the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Enter the patient's information**: Fill in the patient's basic information, including their name, contact information, and emergency contact details.
2. **Provide medical information**: Share the patient's medical history, including allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
3. **List medical procedures and tests**: Record any recent medical procedures or tests the patient has undergone.
4. **List medical equipment and therapy**: Document any medical equipment or therapy the patient is currently using.
5. **Provide medical diagnosis and prescription**: Share any current medical diagnosis and prescription information.
6. **Add medical notes**: Record any additional medical notes relevant to the patient's condition.
7. **Select submitter details**: Choose the submitter's department and role.
8. **Submit the form**: Click the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Patient Info)**: Enter the patient's name and basic contact details.
* **Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name and contact details.
* **Medical Information**: Document the patient's medical history, including allergies, medications, and medical conditions.
* **Medical Procedures**: List any recent medical procedures or tests undergone.
* **Medical Equipment**: Document any medical equipment the patient is currently using.
* **Medical Therapy**: Document any medical therapy the patient is currently receiving.
* **Medical Diagnosis**: Share any current medical diagnosis information.
* **Prescription**: Share any current medical prescription information.
* **Medical Notes**: Record any additional medical notes relevant to the patient's condition.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of consent for the treadmill stress test.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time of consent for the treadmill stress test.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date of submission of the form.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time of submission of the form.
* **Submitter**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Department**: Choose the department of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Role**: Choose the role of the person submitting the form.
