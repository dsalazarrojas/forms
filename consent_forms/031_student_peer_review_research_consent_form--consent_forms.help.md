# Student Peer Review Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to give their informed consent and provide information about themselves and the research project. It will help the researcher understand students' demographics and ensure they have given their consent to participate in the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Researcher Name** field with the name of the person leading the research project.
2. In the **Research Description** field, provide a brief description of the research project and the purpose of the survey.
3. Choose whether you have given your **Informed Consent** to participate in the project.
4. In the **Research Purpose** field, describe the purpose of the research project.
5. In the **Participant Information** field, provide a brief description of your demographic information such as age, gender, etc.
6. Enter your **Student Information** in the corresponding field, including your name, email, and any other relevant details.
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by **Email**, **Phone**, or **Other** (if Other, provide a specific method in the **Contact Method Other** field).
8. Finally, click **Submit** to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Informed Consent**: Check the box to give your informed consent to participate in the research project.
* **Researcher Name**: Enter the name of the researcher leading the project.
* **Research Description**: Provide a brief description of the research project and its purpose.
* **Research Purpose**: Describe the purpose of the research project.
* **Participant Information**: Provide a brief description of your demographic information, such as age, gender, etc.
* **Student Information**: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as by Email, Phone, or Other.
* **Contact Method Other**: If you chose Other, provide a specific method for contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to help the researcher understand your demographics and ensure your informed consent.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the research project, contact the researcher.
