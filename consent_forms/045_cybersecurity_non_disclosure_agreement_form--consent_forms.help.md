# Cybersecurity Non-Disclosure Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a cybersecurity non-disclosure agreement between a company and an individual or organization. It captures the date and details of the agreement, acknowledgement, and other relevant information. This form is used for internal record-keeping and to ensure that all necessary steps are taken to comply with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review and sign the agreement section, indicating your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
2. Provide your acknowledgement, including the date you acknowledged the agreement.
3. Indicate whether you have signed the acknowledgement.
4. If you have signed the acknowledgement, provide the date you signed it.
5. Provide any additional information or comments relevant to the agreement.
6. Review and sign the acknowledgement section, indicating your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
7. Provide the date you reviewed the acknowledgement.
8. Provide any additional information or comments relevant to the acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agreement** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This is the text of the agreement that you need to review and sign. You must read and understand the terms and conditions before signing.
* **Agreement Text** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is not used in this form.

* **Agreement Date** (`5`, `date`, required/optional): This is the date when the agreement was made.
