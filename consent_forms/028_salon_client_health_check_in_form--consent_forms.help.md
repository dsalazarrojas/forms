# salon_client_health_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for salons or spas to gather information about a client's health status and medical history before providing services. The goal is to ensure client safety and provide relevant information for service provision considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any relevant medical history, allergies, or current medications.
2. Provide the date and time of your last appointment with the salon or spa.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Check In** (`Health Check In`, text, optional): This field is a general description of the form's purpose.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Please describe any significant medical events or conditions that may affect your health status.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities you have, including medications or substances that may cause adverse reactions.
* **Current Medications** (`Current Medications`, text, optional): Provide a list of any medications or treatments you are currently taking.
* **Date and Time of Last Appointment** (`Date and Time of Last Appointment`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your previous visit to the salon or spa.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or doubts, don't hesitate to ask the salon or spa staff for assistance.
* This form is for the client's safety and benefit, and providing accurate information helps the service providers to provide the best possible experience for you.
