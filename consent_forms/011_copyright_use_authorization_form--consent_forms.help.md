# copyright_use_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The copyright_use_authorization_form is used to collect information about the use of copyrighted materials within an organization. This form is likely used to obtain consent from employees or contractors to use copyrighted materials for specific purposes, such as for work projects or research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "user name" field.
2. Enter the name of the organization or company you are representing.
3. Select the start and end dates of the period during which you plan to use the copyrighted material.
4. Enter the name of the copyright holder.
5. Choose the type of use you plan to make of the material (e.g., for work projects, research, etc.).
6. If applicable, upload a copy of the license agreement.
7. Sign the form to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: This field is used to collect your name and contact information.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or company you are representing.
* **Start Date**: This field is used to select the start date of the period during which you plan to use the copyrighted material.
* **End Date**: This field is used to select the end date of the period during which you plan to use the copyrighted material.
* **Copyright Holder**: Enter the name of the copyright holder.
* **Type of Use**: This field allows you to choose the type of use you plan to make of the material (e.g., for work projects, research, etc.). Select all applicable options.
* **License Agreement**: If applicable, upload a copy of the license agreement that you have signed.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to acknowledge your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "submit" button to submit your request.
