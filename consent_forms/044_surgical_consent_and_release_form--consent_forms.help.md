# Surgical Consent And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Surgical Consent And Release Form is a document that aims to inform and obtain patient consent for a surgical procedure. This form is a crucial part of the healthcare process to ensure that patients are aware of the risks and benefits of the surgery and to obtain their formal agreement to proceed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please navigate through the pages of the form, reading the provided instructions and questions carefully.
2. For each page, provide the required and optional information in the corresponding fields.
3. Review each field below for a detailed explanation of its purpose and how it is used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to provide their name, contact number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to provide contact information for their emergency contact, including name, phone number, and address.
* **Consent Information** (`consent_info`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to provide their consent for the surgery, including the type of procedure, the risks and benefits, and any pre- and post-operative instructions.
* **Release Information** (`release_info`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to provide their consent for the release of their medical records and any other relevant information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to sign and date their consent, indicating that they have read and understood the information provided.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
* Remember that obtaining patient consent is an essential part of the healthcare process, ensuring that patients are informed and agree to their treatment plan.
