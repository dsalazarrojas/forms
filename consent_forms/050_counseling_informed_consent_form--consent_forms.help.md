# Counseling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselings Informed Consent Form is a tool used by counseling services to gather essential information from clients before beginning treatment. This form helps ensure that clients understand their rights, the services they will receive, and any applicable terms.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:
  1. **Client Information**: Please provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
  2. **Contact Information**: Provide the contact information of a person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  3. **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your treatment, please describe them in this section.
  4. **Consent**: Select all the services you consent to receive (Counselor, Therapist, Psychologist, or Coach).
  5. **Service Details**: Describe the specific services you are requesting.
  6. **Billing Information**: Provide any necessary billing information (e.g., insurance details).
  7. **Insurance**: Indicate whether you have insurance or not.
  8. **Client Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
  9. **Date Signed**: Date and time of signing.

- **Client Information**: This section gathers your basic contact information.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Review each field to ensure you understand its purpose and the implications of providing information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your counselor or contact us.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- The services you choose to consent to are subject to change as per your needs and circumstances.
- Please inform us of any changes to your contact information or medical conditions.
