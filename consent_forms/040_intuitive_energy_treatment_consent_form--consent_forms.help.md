**Intuitive Energy Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Intuitive Energy Treatment Consent Form is a document used by medical professionals to obtain informed consent from patients for energy treatments. This form gathers crucial information about the patient's medical condition, treatment details, and provider involved in the treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you understand the form's purpose and requirements.
2.  Review each field carefully and answer truthfully to the best of your ability.
3.  Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
4.  Review the form for completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information**: This section gathers essential details about the patient, such as their name, medical condition, and treatment start and end dates. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for this field.
*   **Treatments Details**: This field captures specific details about the energy treatment, including the treatment duration and frequency. Please be as detailed as possible when providing information for this field.
*   **Medical Condition**: This field asks for a description of the patient's medical condition. Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can about the patient's condition.
*   **Treatment Provider**: Please select the treatment provider from the list provided. This will ensure that the patient receives the care of a qualified professional.
*   **Treatment Frequency**: Indicate how often the patient will receive the energy treatment, whether daily, weekly, or monthly. Please be accurate in your selection.
*   **Treatment Start Date**: Enter the date on which the treatment will begin. This should be the first day the patient will receive treatment.
*   **Treatment End Date**: Enter the date on which the treatment will end. If this is unknown, please provide an estimated date.
*   **Treatment Notes**: Please add any additional comments or instructions about the treatment that may be relevant to the patient's care.
*   **Patient Signature**: Please indicate whether the patient has read and understood the form and the treatment details. Please sign and date this field if you indicate "Yes".
*   **Treatment Provider Signature**: Please indicate that you, as the treatment provider, have reviewed and understood the form and the treatment details. Please sign and date this field if you indicate "Yes".

Note: The form requires the patient's and provider's signature to indicate agreement and understanding of the treatment details.

**Tips**

*   Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
*   Be honest and provide as much detail as possible when completing each field.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your medical professional or provider for guidance.
*   Ensure the patient and treatment provider have signed and dated their respective fields indicating their understanding and agreement with the treatment details.
