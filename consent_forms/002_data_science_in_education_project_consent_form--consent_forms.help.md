# Data Science In Education Project Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a consent form for the Data Science in Education project. It collects information about participants and their projects for data collection and analysis. This information will be used to improve education and research in the field of data science.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in your project information on page 1.
2.  On page 2, provide your participant information.
3.  On page 3, input the project lead information.
4.  On page 4, select your participant consent options.
5.  On page 5, select your contact information (email, phone number, or address).
6.  On page 6, input your email address.
7.  On page 7, input your address.
8.  On page 8, input your cell phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Information** (`project_info`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your project title and a brief description.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your name and other relevant information about yourself.
* **Project Lead Information** (`project_lead_info`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter the name of the project lead and other relevant information about them.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, required/optional: false):
	+ Select one or more of the following options to indicate your consent:
		+ I am at least 18 years old.
		+ I am 17 years old or younger.
		+ I am not sure.
		+ I am unable to consent.
		+ Other (please provide an explanation).
* **Contact Information** (`participant_contact`, select_one, required/optional: false):
	+ Select how you would like to be contacted:
		+ Email Address
		+ Cell Phone Number
		+ Address
* **E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your address.
* **Cell Phone Number** (`participant_cellphone`, text, required/optional: false):
	+ Enter your cell phone number.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to us.
- We will review this form to ensure that it is not a duplicate submission.
