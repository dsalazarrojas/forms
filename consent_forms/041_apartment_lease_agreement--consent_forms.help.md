# Apartment Lease Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apartment Lease Agreement is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a rental property, including rent, security deposit, utilities, and other essential agreements between the landlord and tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information for the landlord and tenant, including their names, email addresses, phone numbers, and addresses.
2. Provide the rental property address, unit number, and a brief description of the property.
3. Choose the lease type (fixed-term, month-to-month, or year-to-year) and select the renewal terms.
4. Specify the monthly rent amount, rent due date, and security deposit amount.
5. Choose the accepted payment methods and late fee amount.
6. Select the utilities included in the rental property (e.g., water, electricity, gas, internet).
7. Indicate which utilities the tenant is responsible for paying.
8. Specify the maximum number of occupants allowed and whether pets are allowed.
9. Choose the pet policy details and whether smoking is allowed.
10. Specify the maintenance request procedure and emergency contact number.
11. List the landlord and tenant's maintenance responsibilities.
12. Choose the notice-to-vacate required for termination.
13. Specify the early termination fee (if applicable).
14. Sign the document digitally with your name and date.
