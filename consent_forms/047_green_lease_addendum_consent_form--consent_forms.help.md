## Purpose
The green lease addendum consent form is designed to obtain tenant consent for green lease agreements that include additional terms and conditions specific to the rental property. These agreements may focus on sustainability, energy efficiency, or other environmental considerations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the green lease terms on page 1 of the form.
2. Provide your tenant signature and date on page 2, if required.
3. Provide your landlord signature and date on page 3, if required.
4. Select your commitments to sustainability on page 4 (if multiple commitments are listed).
5. Choose your consent to the green lease terms on page 5.
6. If desired, add any additional comments on page 6.
