# Health Research Collaboration Case Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and consent status for health research collaborations. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to help researchers collaborate effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the first page.
2. Provide your contact details, including your email address.
3. If you have a contact number, enter it in the "Contact Number" field. This is optional.
4. Specify your research institution.
5. If your project is related to health research, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Research Project" field.
6. Finally, indicate your consent status, which can be "True", "False", or "Refused".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`id: 1`, text, required): This field is where you enter your basic participant information.
* **Contact Details** (`id: 2`, email, required): Enter your email address so that researchers can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`id: 3`, text, optional): Enter your 10-digit mobile number for additional contact.
* **Research Institution** (`id: 4`, text, required): Specify the institution associated with your research project.
* **Research Project** (`id: 5`, select_one, optional): Indicate if your research project is related to health research (Yes/No).
* **Consent Status** (`id: 6`, select_one, optional): Select your consent status, which can be True, False, or Refused.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your research institution or the project lead.
- You can only submit the form once for each participant.
