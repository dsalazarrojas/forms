# House Rental Lease Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The House Rental Lease Agreement Template is a standard form used to document the terms of a house rental lease between a tenant and a property owner. It outlines the essential details of the lease, ensuring a clear understanding between both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for "Tenant Information" if you are the tenant or not.
2. Enter the property owner's name in the "Property Owner" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the property in the "Property Details" field.
4. Enter the start date of the lease in the "Lease Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of the lease in the "Lease End Date" field.
6. Enter the monthly rent amount in the "Rent" field.
7. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for "Payment Method" if you will be paying rent or not.
8. Sign with your signature in the "Tenant Signature" field, indicating your acceptance of the lease terms.
9. Sign with the property manager's signature in the "Property Manager Signature" field, indicating their acceptance of your lease application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Information**: Select 'Yes' if you are the tenant or 'No' if not.
* **Property Owner**: Enter the name of the person or entity that owns the property.
* **Property Details**: Provide a brief description of the property, including address, size, and any other relevant details.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the date when the lease is scheduled to begin.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the date when the lease is scheduled to end.
* **Rent**: Enter the amount of rent you will pay each month.
* **Payment Method**: Select 'Yes' if you will be paying rent and 'No' if not.
* **Tenant Signature**: Sign with your signature, indicating your acceptance of the lease terms.
* **Property Manager Signature**: Sign with the property manager's signature, indicating their acceptance of your lease application.

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully and fill out all necessary fields to ensure accurate documentation of the lease.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the property owner or manager before proceeding.
* Ensure you save a copy of the signed lease for your records.
* Keep a copy of the signed lease with the property owner or manager, as needed.
