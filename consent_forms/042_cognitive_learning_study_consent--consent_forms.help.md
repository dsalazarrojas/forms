# Cognitive Learning Study Consent - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a cognitive learning study. It is crucial that you understand the purpose of the study, procedures involved, risks and benefits associated with it, privacy and confidentiality measures, and your voluntary participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please review each field carefully and answer accordingly. Ensure you understand the purpose of each section before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Name (2)**: This is the title of the research study.
* **Principal Investigator Name (3)**: This is the name of the lead researcher.
* **Institution (4)**: This is the university or organization conducting the study.
* **Study Description (5)**: This is a brief overview of the research.
* **Participant Name (7)**: Please enter your full name.
* **Age (8)**: Enter your age.
* **Email Address (9)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (10)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **I understand the purpose of this study (12)**: Confirm that you understand the purpose of the study.
* **I understand the procedures involved (13)**: Confirm that you understand the procedures involved.
* **I understand potential risks (15)**: Confirm that you understand the potential risks associated with the study.
* **I understand potential benefits (16)**: Confirm that you understand the potential benefits associated with the study.
* **I understand how my privacy will be protected (18)**: Confirm that you understand how your privacy will be protected.
* **I understand how my data will be used (19)**: Confirm that you understand how your data will be used.
* **I understand how long my data will be kept (20)**: Confirm that you understand how long your data will be kept.
* **I understand my participation is voluntary (22)**: Confirm that you understand that your participation is voluntary.
* **I understand I can withdraw at any time (23)**: Confirm that you understand you can withdraw from the study at any time.
* **I understand there is no penalty for withdrawal (24)**: Confirm that you understand that there is no penalty for withdrawal.
* **I agree to participate in this study (26)**: Confirm that you agree to participate in the study.
* **Do you have questions about this study (27)**: If you have any questions, please answer "Yes" and provide the contact person's name and email.
* **Contact Person for Questions (28)**: If you have any questions, please provide the name and email of the contact person.
* **Date of Consent (30)**: Enter the date you are providing consent to participate in the study.
