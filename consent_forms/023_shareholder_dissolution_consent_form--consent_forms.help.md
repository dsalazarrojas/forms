# Shareholder Dissolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to obtain consent from shareholders for the dissolution of a company. It is used to collect information about the reason for dissolution, date and time of dissolution, and other relevant details. This form is essential for the company to formally record the shareholder's consent and ensure a smooth transition process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the company's name in the **Company Name** field.
2.  Select the reason for dissolution from the list provided in the **Reason for Dissolution** field.
3.  Enter the date of dissolution in the **Date of Dissolution** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  Enter the time of dissolution in the **Time of Dissolution** field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
5.  Sign the **Shareholder Signature** field with your signature.
6.  Enter the date of signing in the **Signature Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7.  If necessary, provide a notarized signature in the **Notarized Signature** field.
8.  If necessary, enter the date of notarization in the **Notarized Signature Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9.  In the **Reason** field, provide a brief explanation for the dissolution.
10. Enter the date of dissolution in the **Date** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
11. Enter the time of dissolution in the **Time** field in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
12. Enter the company address in the **Company Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
*   **Reason for Dissolution** (`reason_for_dissolution`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided reasons for dissolution.
*   **Date of Dissolution** (`date_of_dissolution`, date, required): Enter the date of dissolution in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of Dissolution** (`time_of_dissolution`, time, required): Enter the time of dissolution in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Shareholder Signature** (`shareholder_signature`, text, required): Sign this field with your signature.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Notarized Signature** (`notarized_signature`, text, required): Provide a notarized signature if necessary.
*   **Notarized Signature Date** (`notarized_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of notarization in the format MM/DD/YYYY if necessary.
*   **Reason** (`reason`, text, required): Provide a brief explanation for the dissolution.
*   **Date** (`dissolution_date`, date, required): Enter the date of dissolution in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time** (`dissolution_time`, time, required): Enter the time of dissolution in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the company address if necessary.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the provided options or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
*   This form is essential for the company's records and should be completed seriously and carefully.
