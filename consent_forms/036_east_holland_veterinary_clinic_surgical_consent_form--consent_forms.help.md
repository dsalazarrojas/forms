# East Holland Veterinary Clinic Surgical Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients and pet owners prior to surgery at East Holland Veterinary Clinic. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured, which will be used by the surgeon and medical team to prepare for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient's details (Name, etc.) in the "Patient Information" field.
* Provide information about the pet undergoing surgery.
* Specify the procedure to be performed.
* Enter the date and time of admission.
* Mention the name(s) of the surgeon(s) involved with the patient's care.
* Specify the name and role of the assistant nurse.
* Confirm whether or not you have conversed with the surgeon regarding your pet's condition.
* Add any additional comments regarding the patient's condition or procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (patient_info): Enter the patient's name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Pet Information** (pet_info): Provide the pet's name, species, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Procedure** (procedure): Specify the specific procedure(s) to be performed.
* **Date of Admission** (date_of_admission): Enter the date and year of admission.
* **Time of Admission** (time_of_admission): Enter the time of admission.
* **Surgeon Name** (surgeon_name): Mention the name of the surgeon(s) involved with the patient's care.
* **Assistant Nurse** (assistant_nurse): Specify the name and role of the assistant nurse.
* **Have you conversed with the surgeon?** (consent_given): Choose the response that best matches your situation.
* **Comments** (comments): Add any additional comments or details about the patient's condition or procedure.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out to ensure proper patient care.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered, especially regarding the procedure and date/time of admission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask for clarification from the medical staff at East Holland Veterinary Clinic.
