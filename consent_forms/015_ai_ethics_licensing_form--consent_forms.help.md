# AI Ethics Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Ethics Licensing Form is a mandatory document that must be completed by developers or organizations requesting access to our company's AI technology. The form ensures that all parties involved are aware of and agree to our company's AI ethics and licensing policies, which are designed to protect our intellectual property and promote responsible AI development and deployment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary information about the entity requesting the license.
2. Specify the details of the AI project or model for which you are requesting a license.
3. Describe the intended use case of the AI technology.
4. Choose the license type that best suits your needs.
5. Acknowledge any restricted use categories that do not apply to your project.
6. Confirm compliance with relevant data privacy standards.
7. Select the level of model transparency you are willing to provide.
8. Specify the preferred audit/monitoring frequency.
9. Provide contact information for your compliance officer.
10. Identify the jurisdiction under which the law governing your use of the AI technology applies.
11. Accept or decline the liability and indemnification terms of the license.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Licensee Information - Organization / Entity Name**: This is the name of the organization or entity requesting the license. Please enter the name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Licensee / Authorized Representative**: This is the name of the person completing the form. This could be you or someone authorized to act on your behalf.
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent. This could be a For-Profit Corporation, Non-Profit / NGO, Academic / Research Institution, Individual Developer, or Government Agency.
* **AI Product / Model Name**: Enter the name of the AI project or model for which you are requesting a license.
* **Detailed Description of Intended Use**: Describe how and where you plan to deploy the AI technology. This could include details about your specific use case, such as which industry or sector you operate in.
* **License Type Requested**: Choose the type of license you require. This could be a Commercial Enterprise License, Research and Development License, Educational / Non-Commercial License, or Evaluation / Trial License.
* **Restricted Use Acknowledgment**: Check all categories for which you do not intend to use the AI technology. This helps ensure that you are aware of and will not infringe on our company's restrictions on certain uses.
* **Data Privacy Standards Compliance**: Select the data privacy standards your implementation adheres to. This could include GDPR (EU), CCPA/CPRA (California), HIPAA (Healthcare US), SOC 2 Type II, or local privacy laws.
* **Model Transparency Level**: Choose the level of transparency you are willing to provide for the AI model. This could be Fully Transparent (Open Weights/Source), Partially Transparent (API access + Documentation), or Proprietary / Black Box.
* **Requested License Effective Date**: Enter the date on which you would like the license to become effective. Please ensure this date is in the future.
* **Preferred Audit / Monitoring Frequency**: Select the frequency with which you would like our company to audit or monitor your use of the AI technology. This could be Quarterly Self-Assessment, Annual External Audit, or Continuous Monitoring (API logs).
* **Compliance Officer Email**: Provide the email address of your compliance officer. This is the contact information we will use to communicate with you regarding ethics and licensing follow-up.
* **Governing Law Jurisdiction**: Enter the jurisdiction under which the law governing your use of the AI technology applies. This could be a specific country or state.
* **I accept the liability and indemnification terms of this license**: Confirm that you understand and accept our liability and indemnification terms.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely. Failure to do so may result in delayed or denied license approval.
