# Electrolysis Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrolysis Consent Form is a document that collects informed consent from a client for an electrolysis procedure. It is used to ensure that the client is aware of the risks and benefits associated with the procedure and to obtain their agreement to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Answer the questions related to the electrology procedure, including any risk factors, contraindications, medical history, allergies, previous injuries, medications, and electrologist information.
4. Sign and date the form, indicating your informed consent to participate in the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`electrology_consent_form`, `text`, required): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the document.
* **What is the electrology procedure?** (`introduction`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide a brief description of the electrology procedure and its purpose.
* **What are the risk factors I should be aware of?** (`risk_factors`, `text`, required): This field is used to list any risks associated with the procedure that the client should be aware of.
* **Do you have any contraindications?** (`contraindications`, `text`, required): This field is used to ask if the client has any contraindications that may impact their participation in the procedure.
* **Please list your medical history** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect any relevant medical history from the client.
* **Do you have any allergies?** (`allergy`, `text`, required): This field is used to ask if the client has any allergies that may impact their participation in the procedure.
* **Have you had any injuries or past medical conditions?** (`previous_injuries`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect any information about previous injuries or medical conditions from the client.
* **Are you taking any medications?** (`medications`, `text`, required): This field is used to ask if the client is taking any medications that may impact their participation in the procedure.
* **What is your electrologist?** (`electrologist`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect information about the electrologist who will be performing the procedure.
* **Provide informed consent** (`informed_consent`, `text`, required): This field is used to obtain the client's informed consent to participate in the procedure.
* **Client signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): This field is used to obtain the client's signature, indicating their agreement to participate in the procedure.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): This field is used to record the date the form is signed.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): This field is used to record the time the form is signed.
* **Electrologist Signature** (`electrologist_signature`, `text`, required): This field is used to obtain the electrologist's signature, indicating their agreement to perform the procedure.
* **Additional notes** (`note`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect any additional notes or comments from the client or electrologist.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to ask your electrologist.
* This form is a legal document and should be kept confidential.
* Do not submit the form until you have read and understood all of the fields and questions.
* If you need assistance with completing this form, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
