# Car Accident Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Accident Release Form is a document that acknowledges the resolution of all claims related to a car accident. It's a legal agreement between the parties involved, releasing each other from any further claims or actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to understand its terms and conditions.
2. Ensure you have the authority to sign the form on behalf of the releasing party.
3. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, providing all required information.
4. Date and sign the form in the presence of a witness (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Releasing Party Name**: Enter the full name of the party releasing their claims.
* **Address**: Enter the full address of the releasing party.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact number of the releasing party.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the releasing party.
* **Released Party Name**: Enter the full name of the party being released from claims.
* **Released Party Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company covering the released party.
* **Released Party Policy Number**: Enter the policy number of the released party's insurance coverage.
* **Date of Accident**: Enter the date the accident occurred.
* **Location of Accident**: Enter the street address where the accident took place.
* **Brief Description of Accident**: Provide a brief description of what happened during the accident.
* **Settlement Amount**: Enter the dollar amount agreed upon for the settlement.
* **Settlement Type**: Select the type of settlement agreed upon (Full settlement, Partial settlement, Payment plan, Insurance only, Other).
* **Payment Terms**: Describe how payment will be made (e.g., cash, check, etc.).
* **Expected Payment Date**: Enter the date payment is expected to be made.
* **Damages Covered**: Select all damages covered by the settlement (Vehicle damage repair, Property damage, Medical expenses, Lost wages, Pain and suffering, Other).
* **Excluded Damages**: If applicable, enter any damages not covered by the settlement.
* **Release Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you release all claims against the other party (Yes, I release all claims, No).
* **Future Claims Waiver**: Confirm that you waive the right to file future claims (Yes, I waive future claims, No).
* **Legal Advice**: Confirm that you have reviewed this form with legal counsel (Yes, No, Not applicable).
* **Understand Terms**: Confirm that you understand all terms of this release (Yes, I understand, No).
* **Voluntary Agreement**: Confirm that you enter into this agreement voluntarily (Yes, voluntary, No).
* **Date of Signature**: Date and sign the form.
* **Witness Required**: Confirm if a witness is present (Yes, No).
* **Witness Information**: If a witness is present, enter their name and contact information.
* **Signature Date**: Date and sign the form in the presence of a witness (if required).
