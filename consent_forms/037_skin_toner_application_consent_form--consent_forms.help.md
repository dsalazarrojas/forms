> 
> # skin_toner_application_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin toner application consent form is used to record the client's consent for a skin toner application treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the required information as prompted.
2. Provide your consent to receive the skin toner application treatment.
3. Fill out the date and time of the treatment.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Skin Toner Application** (`skin_toner_application`, `text`, required): Enter the type of skin toner application treatment that you are receiving.
* **Esthetician Name** (`esthetician_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the esthetician providing the treatment.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the client receiving the treatment.
* **Skin Toner Product** (`skin_toner_product`, `text`, required): Enter the product being used for the skin toner application treatment.
* **Allergy Declaration** (`allergy_declaration`, `text`, required): Declare any allergies you have that may be relevant to the treatment.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may be relevant to the treatment, please specify.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): Provide your consent to receive the skin toner application treatment.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Fill in the date of the treatment.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Fill in the time of the treatment.
* **Esthetician Signature** (`esthetician_signature`, `note`, required): Sign below to confirm the treatment has been provided.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required): Sign below to confirm that you, the client, have received the treatment.
* **Esthetician Notes** (`esthetician_notes`, `text`, optional): The esthetician providing the treatment may have additional comments.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `text`, optional): You, the client, may have additional comments or questions.
