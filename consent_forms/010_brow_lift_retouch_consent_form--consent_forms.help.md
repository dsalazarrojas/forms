# Brow Lift Retouch Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide essential information about the client's medical history, consent, and contact details for the brow lift retouch procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the appointment date and time for the retouch procedure from the provided date and time options.
3. If you have any concerns or issues with your previous brow lift procedure, please describe them in the "Current Concerns or Issues" field.
4. List any known allergies or medications that may affect the healing process.
5. Indicate whether you have any active skin conditions or are currently pregnant/nursing.
6. Confirm that you have had the risks and benefits of the procedure explained to you.
7. Confirm that you consent to the brow lift retouch procedure.
8. Acknowledge that you understand the risks and waive liability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Full Name
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Address
* **Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Enter your street address where the original procedure was performed or where you would like to receive communication about your retouch procedure.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates or questions.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates or questions.

### Appointment Date for Retouch
* **Appointment Date for Retouch** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the retouch procedure from the provided options.

### Appointment Time for Retouch
* **Appointment Time for Retouch** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time of the retouch procedure from the provided options.

### Date of Original Brow Lift Procedure
* **Date of Original Brow Lift Procedure** (`original_procedure_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the original procedure, if you know it.

### Where was the Original Procedure performed
* **Where was the Original Procedure performed** (`procedure_location`, text, optional): Enter the name of the clinic or facility where the original procedure was performed, if you know it.

### Have your brows healed properly since last procedure
* **Have your brows healed properly since last procedure** (`healing_status`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options: Yes, completely; Mostly yes; Some concerns; No, has not healed well.

### Current concerns or issues with brows
* **Current concerns or issues with brows** (`current_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any concerns or issues you have with your brows, if you have any.

### Known Allergies or Numb Agents
* **Known allergies or numbing agents** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies or medications that may affect the healing process.

### Current Medications Affecting Healing
* **Current medications affecting healing** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications that may affect the healing process.

### Do you have any active skin conditions?
* **Do you have any active skin conditions?** (`skin_conditions`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options: True; False.

### Are you currently pregnant or nursing?
* **Are you currently pregnant or nursing?** (`pregnancy`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options: Yes, pregnant; Yes, nursing; False.

### Risks Explained
* **Risks Explained** (`risks_explained`, select_one, required): Confirm that the risks and benefits of the procedure have been explained to you.

### I Consent to the Brow Lift Retouch Procedure
* **I Consent to the Brow Lift Retouch Procedure** (`consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to the brow lift retouch procedure.

### I assume all risks and waive liability
* **I assume all risks and waive liability** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risks and waive liability.

### Signature Date
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this consent form.
