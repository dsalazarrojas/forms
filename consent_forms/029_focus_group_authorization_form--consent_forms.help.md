# Focus Group Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Focus Group Authorization Form" is used to onboard new participants or members to a research study or project, ensuring they are aware of and agree to its terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the participant's responsibilities and commitments.
2.  Answer the questions according to your role and experience with the research study or project.
3.  Make sure to read and agree to the terms outlined in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Consent**: A text field where you can provide your consent to participate in the study or project.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: true
*   **Participant Information**: A text field to provide demographic information about yourself as a participant.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Study Information**: A text field to provide context about the study or project you are participating in.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Consent Details**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about your consent.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: true
*   **Study Lead**: A select one field to determine who is responsible for leading the study or project.
    *   Options: Participant Lead, Researcher Lead, Project Lead, Other
    *   Required: true
*   **Participant Comments**: A text field for any comments or feedback about your experience.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Other Study Lead**: A text field for additional information about the study or project lead.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false
*   **Participant Consent**: A select one field to confirm your consent for the study or project.
    *   Options: True, False
    *   Required: true
*   **Participant Agreement**: A select multiple field to confirm your understanding and agreement to the study or project's terms.
    *   Options: I understand and agree, I do not understand, Unsure
    *   Required: true
*   **Participant Notes**: A text field for any additional notes or comments.
    *   Type: text
    *   Required: false

## Tips
*   Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask your study lead or project manager for clarification.
*   Complete the form only if you are certain about your consent and agreement.
