# Franchise Seller Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Franchise Seller Disclosure Form is a document that provides information about the terms and conditions of a franchise agreement. It is used to ensure that franchisees are fully informed about their obligations and responsibilities before entering into a franchise agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information.
3. Once you have completed the form, review it for completeness and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Franchise Seller Name**: <franchise_seller_name> (Required) - This is the name of the person or company selling the franchise.
* **Address**: <address> (Required) - This is the physical address of the franchise seller.
* **City**: <city> (Optional) - This is the city where the franchise seller is located.
* **State**: <state> (Optional) - This is the state where the franchise seller is located.
* **Zip Code**: <zip_code> (Required) - This is the zip code of the franchise seller's address.
* **Phone Number**: <phone_number> (Required) - This is the phone number of the franchise seller.
* **Email**: <email> (Optional) - This is the email address of the franchise seller.
* **Business Description**: <business_description> (Optional) - This is a brief description of the business.
* **Franchise Fees**: <franchise_fees> (Optional) - This is the fee structure of the franchise.
* **Terms And Conditions**: <terms_and_conditions> (Required) - This is a detailed outline of the terms and conditions of the franchise agreement.
* **Agreement Start Date**: <agreement_start_date> (Required) - This is the start date of the franchise agreement.
* **Contract End Date**: <contract_end_date> (Required) - This is the end date of the franchise agreement.
* **Payment Terms**: <payment_terms> (Optional) - This is the payment structure of the franchise.
* **Cancellation Term**: <cancellation_term> (Optional) - This is the term for cancelling the franchise agreement.
* **Contract Term**: <contract_term> (Optional) - This is the term for the contract.
* **Termination Term**: <termination_term> (Optional) - This is the term for terminating the franchise agreement.
* **Franchise Liquidation**: <franchise_liquidation> (Optional) - This is the process for liquidating the franchise.
* **Contract Renewal**: <contract_renewal> (Optional) - This is the term for renewing the franchise contract.
* **Dispute Resolution**: <dispute_resolution> (Optional) - This is the process for resolving disputes.
* **Termination Procedures**: <termination_procedures> (Optional) - This is the process for terminating the franchise agreement.
* **Franchisor**: <franchisor> (Required) - This is the name of the franchisor.
* **Franchisee**: <franchisee> (Required) - This is the name of the franchisee.
* **Franchise Number**: <franchise_number> (Required) - This is the unique number of the franchise.
* **Territory**: <territory> (Required) - This is the geographic area covered by the franchise.
* **Franchisee Agreement**: <franchisee_agreement> (Required) - This is the agreement between the franchisor and franchisee.
