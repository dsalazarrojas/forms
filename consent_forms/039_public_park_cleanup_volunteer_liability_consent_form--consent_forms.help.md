# public_park_cleanup_volunteer_liability_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers who wish to participate in park cleanup events. It captures contact information and liability consent, ensuring that volunteers are properly registered and their emergency contact information is recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and any other details as requested in the "Volunteer Details" section.
2. Select the waiver options that apply to you, indicating your understanding and consent.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section, including your phone number and email.
4. Provide your emergency contact's name, relationship to you, phone number, and email in the "Emergency Contact" section.
5. Optionally, enter a relationship description in the "Relationship" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Volunteer Details
- **Volunteer Details** (`Volunteer Details`, text, required/optional): Please provide any relevant details about yourself as a volunteer, such as your name, address, and contact number.

### Waiver
- **Waiver** (`Waiver`, select_multiple, required): Please select all the options that apply to you:
  - Volunteer agrees to participate in park cleanup events
  - Volunteer understands that participating in park cleanup events involves risks
  - Volunteer acknowledges that participating in park cleanup events is voluntary

### Contact Information
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Please enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.

### Emergency Contact
- **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.

### Relationship
- **Relationship** (`Relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact.

### Phone
- **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Email
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.

### Signature
- **Signature** (`Signature`, text, required): Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver and consent.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the park administration office.
- Please note that participating in park cleanup events is voluntary and involves risks.
