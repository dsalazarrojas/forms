# film_trailer_distribution_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals or organizations interested in distributing a film trailer. It's a legal requirement in many jurisdictions to ensure that all parties involved in the distribution process have given their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including your general details (page 1), distribution details (page 2), and any other relevant information.
3. Make your selection from the distribution authorization options (page 3) to indicate who is authorized to distribute the trailer.
4. Select the relevant distribution channels for the trailer (page 4).
5. Provide any additional trailer details (page 5), if necessary.
6. Sign and date the form (page 6).
7. Enter your contact information, including your name and email address (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Page 1)**:
	+ **General Information**: This section is for you to provide your general details, such as your name, contact information, and other relevant contact details.
* **Distribution Details (Page 2)**:
	+ **Distribution Details**: Please provide any relevant details about the distribution you are requesting, such as the trailer type, duration, and format.
* **Distribution Authorization (Page 3)**:
	+ **Distribution Authorization**: Select who is authorized to distribute the trailer. This could be a person, organization, or company.
* **Distribution Channels (Page 4)**:
	+ **Distribution Authorized**: Select the distribution channels you intend to use for the trailer distribution, such as websites, social media, or streaming platforms.
* **Trailer Details (Page 5)**:
	+ **Trailer Details**: You can provide any additional details about the trailer, such as its content, target audience, or other relevant information.
* **Date Signature (Page 6)**:
	+ **Date Signature**: Please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Partner Distributor (Page 7)**:
	+ **Partner Distributor**: This field is for you to provide the name of the partner or organization you are distributing the trailer with.
* **Email Address (Page 8)**:
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further information or questions about the trailer distribution.
