# Travel Document Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from parents or guardians to authorize travel for minors. It collects details about the child's travel plans, including travel documents, destination, and authorization information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information:
	* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
	* **Parent Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
2. Provide minor's information:
	* **Minor's Date of Birth (DOB)**: Enter the child's date of birth.
3. Specify travel details:
	* **Travel Details**: Enter a brief description of the travel plans, including destination and duration.
4. Upload travel documents:
	* **Travel Documents**: Attach any relevant documents, such as ID cards or passport numbers.
5. Select authorized documents:
	* **Authorized Documents**: Choose which travel documents you are authorizing for the minor.
6. Set authorization details:
	* **Authorization Date**: Enter the date of authorization.
	* **Authorized By**: Select the person authorizing the travel (Parent, Guardian, or Other).
7. Finalize travel details:
	* **Travel Destination**: Enter the travel destination (e.g., country, city, or resort).
	* **Travel Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of the trip.
