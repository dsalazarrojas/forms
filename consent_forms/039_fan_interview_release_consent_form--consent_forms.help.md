# Fan Interview Release Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fan Interview Release Consent Form is a form used to obtain consent from a participant (a fan) and their guardian to record a conversation with them. This form helps ensure that both the participant and their guardian are aware of and agree to the recording.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with the required information, including:
	* Participant Name: Enter the name of the participant.
	* Guardian Name: Enter the name of the participant's guardian.
	* Participant Age: Enter the participant's age.
	* Participant Date of Birth: Enter the participant's date of birth.
	* Guardian Consent: Sign and date to indicate that you are giving consent.
	* Participant Consent: Sign and date to indicate that you are giving permission.
2. Review the completed form for accuracy.
3. Sign and date all applicable signature fields (Guardian Signature, Participant Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.

### Guardian Name
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant's guardian.

### Participant Age
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter the participant's age.

### Participant Date of Birth
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the participant's date of birth.

### Guardian Consent
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, text, required): Sign and date to indicate that you are giving consent.

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, required): Sign and date to indicate that you are giving permission.

### Interviewer ID
* **Interviewer ID** (`interviewer_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the interviewer.

### Participant Signature
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, optional): A signature field for the participant.

### Guardian Signature
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, note, optional): A signature field for the guardian.

### Participant Phone Number
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.

### Interviewer Phone Number
* **Interviewer Phone Number** (`interviewer_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the interviewer's phone number.

### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.

### Participant Parent Email
* **Participant Parent Email** (`participant_parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's parent or guardian's email address.

### Interview Details
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the interview.

### Release Consent
* **Release Consent** (`release_consent`, text, required): This field is not applicable for this form as it is not required.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (`participant_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.

### Guardian Additional Notes
* **Guardian Additional Notes** (`guardian_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes from the guardian.

### Participant Address
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address.

### Participant City
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, optional): Enter the participant's city.

### Participant State
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, optional): Enter the participant's state.

### Participant Postal Code
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter the participant's postal code.

Note: This form is designed to collect the necessary information from both the participant and their guardian to ensure that all parties are aware and consent to the recording of the conversation.
