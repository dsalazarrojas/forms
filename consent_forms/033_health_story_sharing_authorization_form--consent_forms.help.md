# Health Story Sharing Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain your consent for sharing your health-related data. By completing this form, you are authorizing the sharing of your health information with relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your willingness to share your health story.
2. Enter your name in the "Enter your name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Your email" field.
4. Leave the "Phone" field blank if you don't want to provide your phone number.
5. Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your consent for sharing your health story.
6. Optionally, provide additional comments in the "Additional comments" field.
7. Select "Yes" or "No" to determine the output type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you are willing to share your health story with relevant parties. If you select "No", your health story will not be shared.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `select_one`, required): This field is not currently used in the form, so please leave it blank.
* **Enter your name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Your email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Your phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number you would like to provide, enter it here.
* **I Agree** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to confirm you are willing to share your health story. If you select "No", your health story will not be shared.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to add.
* **Output** (`output`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you want the output to be shared as is. Otherwise, select "No" to indicate that the output should be processed or modified before sharing.
