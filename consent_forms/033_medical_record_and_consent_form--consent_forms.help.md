# Medical Record And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Record And Consent Form is a document used to capture a patient's medical record and obtain their consent for medical treatments or procedures. It is essential for medical professionals to have accurate and up-to-date patient information to provide proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the Medical Record section with any relevant medical history, allergies, or other important information about the patient.
* In the Consent Form section, provide a clear and concise statement of the treatment or procedure, and have the patient sign it to indicate their consent.
* Enter the patient's name and date of birth in the Patient Name and Date Of Birth fields.
* Enter the patient's address in the Address field.
* Enter the patient's medical record ID number, if applicable.
* Enter the consent date and time in the Consent Date field.
* In the Signature field, have the patient sign to indicate their consent.
* Enter any additional contact information in the Contact Info field.
* Enter the patient's email address in the Email field.
* Have the patient sign the Medical Record And Consent Form Signature field to confirm their consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Record** (`medical_record`, text, optional): Enter any relevant medical history, allergies, or other important information about the patient.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise statement of the treatment or procedure, and have the patient sign it to indicate their consent.
* **Medical Record And Consent Form Details** (`medical_record_and_consent_form_details`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about the treatment or procedure.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Record ID** (`medical_record_id`, text, optional): Enter the patient's medical record ID number, if applicable.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Enter the consent date and time.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Have the patient sign to indicate their consent.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Record And Consent Form Signature** (`medical_record_and_consent_form_signature`, text, optional): Have the patient sign to confirm their consent.

## Tips
Please make sure to have the patient read and understand each field before submitting the form. Ensure that the form is completed accurately and completely for medical professionals to make informed decisions.
