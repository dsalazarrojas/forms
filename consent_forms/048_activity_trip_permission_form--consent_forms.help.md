# Activity Trip Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain permission from a parent or guardian for a participant to attend an activity trip. It collects information about the participant's personal details, trip specifics, and any potential medical conditions that may affect their attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide the participant's date of birth in the "Participant Date of Birth" field in the format dd/mm/yy.
3. Enter the parent or guardian's name in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
4. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent or Guardian Phone" field.
5. Enter the parent or guardian's email address in the "Parent or Guardian Email" field.
6. Enter the trip destination in the "Trip Destination" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the trip in the "Trip Dates" field.
8. Enter the purpose of the trip in the "Purpose of Trip" field.
9. If the participant has any medical conditions, select "Yes" in the "Medical Conditions" field and provide further details in the "Details of Medical Conditions" field.
10. List any medications the participant is currently taking in the "Current Medications" field.
11. Finally, select "Yes, I grant permission" or "No, I do not grant permission" in the "Permission Granted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the participant going on the trip.
- **Participant Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the date of birth of the participant in the format dd/mm/yy.
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (text, required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian providing consent.
- **Parent or Guardian Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
- **Parent or Guardian Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
- **Trip Destination** (text, required): Enter where the trip is going.
- **Trip Dates** (text, required): Enter the start and end dates of the trip.
- **Purpose of Trip** (text, required): Enter the purpose of this trip.
- **Medical Conditions** (select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the participant has any medical conditions, or "No" otherwise.
  *If "Yes" is selected, you will need to provide further details in the "Details of Medical Conditions" field.
- **Details of Medical Conditions** (text, optional): Provide details of any medical conditions the participant has.
- **Current Medications** (text, optional): List any medications the participant is currently taking.
- **Permission Granted** (select_one, required): Select "Yes, I grant permission" to allow the participant to attend the trip, or "No, I do not grant permission" otherwise.
- **Signature Date** (date, required): Enter the date of consent and signature.
