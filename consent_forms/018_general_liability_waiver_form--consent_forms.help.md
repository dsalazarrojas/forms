# General Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a participant's liability for general liability purposes. It is intended to be used in a business or professional setting to ensure that the participant is aware of and accepts the risks associated with participating in certain activities or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information, including their name, email, phone number, and date and time of participation.
2. If you are representing a business, fill out the business information, including the business name, address, city, state, and zip code.
3. Review and acknowledge the waiver text, ensuring you understand and accept the terms.
4. Sign and date the form, using either the printed version or the screen version of the signature.
5. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and honest information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)** (<type:text>): This is a text field where you will provide the participant's details. Please include your name and any other relevant information.
* **Participant Signature** (<type:select_one>): Choose how you would like to sign the document: either on the document or on the screen.
* **Waiver Text** (<type:note>): This is a text field where you will provide a brief explanation of the waiver. Please review and understand the terms before proceeding.
* **Participant Email** (<type:email>): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone Number** (<type:text>): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Business Name** (<type:text>): If you are representing a business, enter the business name.
* **Business Address** (<type:text>): If you are representing a business, enter the business address.
* **Business City** (<type:text>): If you are representing a business, enter the business city.
* **Business State** (<type:text>): If you are representing a business, enter the business state.
* **Business Zip** (<type:text>): If you are representing a business, enter the business zip code.
* **Participant Date of Participation** (<type:date>): Enter the date you will participate in the activity.
* **Participant Time of Participation** (<type:time>): Enter the time you will participate in the activity.
* **Participant Signature Date** (<type:date>): This is the date you sign the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct date.
* **Participant Signature Time** (<type:time>): This is the time you sign the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct time.
* **Business Signature Date** (<type:date>): This is the date the business representative signs the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct date.
* **Business Signature Time** (<type:time>): This is the time the business representative signs the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct time.
* **Participant Name** (<type:text>): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Age** (<type:number>): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant Height** (<type:number>): Enter the participant's height.
* **Participant Gender** (<type:select_one>): Choose the participant's gender: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Participant Injury** (<type:select_one>): Check if the participant has any injury or not.
* **Business Signature Date** (<type:date>): This is the date the business representative signs the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct date.
* **Business Signature Time** (<type:time>): This is the time the business representative signs the form. Please make sure to sign it with the correct time.

## Tips

* Please review the form carefully before signing it.
* Make sure to sign the form with the correct date and time.
* Ensure that you are providing accurate and honest information.
* If you are representing a business, make sure to sign the form as the authorized representative.
* If you are participating in an activity with a minor, please ensure that you have obtained their guardian's consent first.
