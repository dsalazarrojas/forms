# Lithium Ion Battery Warnings And Usage Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to identify potential risks or concerns related to the use of lithium ion batteries and to gather information that can be used to provide guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question.
3. Enter any relevant information in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lithium Ion Battery Warnings And Usage Waiver Form** (1): This is the main heading for the form.
* **Have you ever had an allergic reaction to a lithium ion battery** (2): Check 'Yes' if you have had an allergic reaction to a lithium ion battery in the past. This is an important question as it can indicate a potential risk factor for using these batteries.
* **Have you had any other lithium ion batteries in the past year** (3): Check 'Yes' if you have had any other lithium ion batteries in the past year. This question helps to gather information about your history with lithium ion batteries.
* **Do you have any health insurance** (4): Check 'Yes' if you have any health insurance. This information can be useful for future reference.
* **Do you have any medical conditions or disabilities** (5): Check 'Yes' if you have any medical conditions or disabilities. This question helps to identify potential risks or concerns related to your health.
* **Medical Condition Description** (6): Enter any relevant information about your medical condition or disability. Please be as descriptive as possible.
* **Lithium Ion Battery Warning** (7): Enter the date you received a warning about a potential risk or concern related to the use of lithium ion batteries.
* **Date of last allergic reaction** (8): Enter the date of your last allergic reaction to a lithium ion battery. If you have not had an allergic reaction, leave this field blank.
* **Medical Condition Treatment** (9): Enter any relevant information about your medical condition or disability treatment.
* **Medical Condition Symptoms** (10): Enter any symptoms you are experiencing related to your medical condition or disability.
* **Medical Device Used** (11): Enter any medical devices you are using that may be related to your medical condition or disability.
* **Medical Device Description** (12): Enter any relevant information about the medical device you are using.
