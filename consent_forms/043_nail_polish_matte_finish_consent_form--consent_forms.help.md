# Nail Polish Matte Finish Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients about their consent and health status before undergoing a nail service with a matte finish.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Client agrees to undergo a nail service" from the dropdown menu to indicate your consent.
2. Fill in any health details you think are relevant to the nail service.
3. List any health issues you have that may impact the nail service.
4. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Consent** (Client agrees to undergo a nail service):
	+ This field is a dropdown menu where you must select one of the two options: "Client agrees to undergo a nail service" or "Client does not consent".
* **Health Details**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can fill in any relevant health information you think may impact the nail service.
* **Health Issues**:
	+ This field is a text box where you can list any health issues you have that may impact the nail service.
* **Signature**:
	+ This field is a signature box where you can sign to acknowledge that you have read and understood the content of the form.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields carefully and accurately.
- If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, please ask the nail technician for assistance.
- Make sure to review the content of the form carefully before signing it.
