# Trademark Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trademark Release Form is used to obtain permission from the trademark owner to use their trademark. This form provides details of the agreement, including the date the permission was issued, the term of the agreement, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the trademark name.
2.  Enter the business name.
3.  Enter the date the trademark was first used, if applicable.
4.  Enter the date the trademark was last used, if applicable.
5.  Specify the trademark owner.
6.  Select whether the permission to use the trademark has been issued or not.
7.  Enter the date the permission to use the trademark was issued, if applicable.
8.  Enter the date of the agreement.
9.  Enter the term of the agreement.
10.  Enter your signature.
11.  Specify the type of copy of the agreement, if provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trademark Name** (`enter_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the trademark.
* **Business Name** (`enter_business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business or entity using the trademark.
* **Date First Used** (`date_first_use`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the trademark was first used, if applicable.
* **Date Last Used** (`date_last_use`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the trademark was last used, if applicable.
* **Trademark Owner** (`trademark_owner`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the trademark owner.
* **Permission Issued** (`permission_issued`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if permission to use the trademark has been issued, otherwise select 'No'.
* **Date Permission Issued** (`date_permission_issued`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the permission to use the trademark was issued, if applicable.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Term** (`agreement_term`, `text`, required): Enter the term of the agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Enter your signature.
* **Copy of Agreement** (`notarized_copy`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Notarized Copy' or 'Other' to specify the type of copy of the agreement provided.
