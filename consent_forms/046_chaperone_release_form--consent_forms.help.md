# Chaperone Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from chaperones to ensure their safety and supervision during events or trips.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please fill in all required fields. For each field, please provide accurate and complete information as requested.
*   The form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of fields.
*   Please review each field carefully and ask your supervisor or administrator if you have any questions or doubts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): The date of submission of this form.
	+ Please enter the current date to indicate when this form was completed.
* **Chaperone Full Name** (`chaperone_name`, text, required): The legal name of the chaperone.
	+ Please enter your full name as per your government-issued identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): The date of birth of the chaperone for background check purposes.
	+ Enter your date of birth to help facilitate background checks.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): The contact email of the chaperone.
	+ Enter your email address for easy communication during the event or trip.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): The contact phone number of the chaperone.
	+ Please enter your phone number for quick communication during the event or trip.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): The current address of the chaperone.
	+ Enter your current address for easy contact and coordination.
* **Event or Trip Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of the event or trip the chaperone will be supervising.
	+ Enter the name of the event or trip as per your supervisor's instructions.
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, text, required): The start and end dates of the event or trip.
	+ Enter the dates of the event or trip for accurate planning and coordination.
* **Trip Destination** (`destination`, text, required): The location of the event or trip.
	+ Enter the destination of the event or trip as per your supervisor's instructions.
* **Group Being Supervised** (`group_supervised`, number, optional): The number of people the chaperone will be supervising.
	+ Please enter the number of participants if it's relevant to the event or trip.
* **Age Group of Participants** (`age_group`, text, required): The age group of the participants.
	+ Please enter the age group as per your supervisor's instructions.
* **Experience Supervising Youth** (`supervision_experience`, select one, optional):
	+ Select the level of your supervision experience from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	+ Please enter the name of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): The contact phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	+ Please enter the phone number of the person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Any important health information about the chaperone.
	+ If applicable, please enter any medical conditions or allergies relevant to the event or trip.
* **Acknowledgment of Liability Risks** (`liability_acknowledgment`, select one, required): Please acknowledge that you understand the risks involved with the activity.
	+ Please select the option to acknowledge the risks of the event or trip.
* **Acceptance of Supervision Responsibility** (`responsibility_acceptance`, select one, required): Please accept the responsibility of ensuring the safety and well-being of the participants.
	+ Please select the option to accept the responsibility of supervision.
* **Consent to Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, select one, required): Please grant permission for background checks.
	+ Select the option to consent to background checks.
* **Agreement to Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct_agreement`, select one, required): Please agree to abide by the behavioral standards of the event or trip.
	+ Select the option to agree to the code of conduct.
* **I Have Read and Agree to Terms** (`signature_confirmation`, select one, required): Please confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the event or trip.
	+ Select the option to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.

## Tips

*   Please review each field carefully and ask your supervisor or administrator if you have any questions or doubts.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
*   If you need to make any changes, please contact your supervisor or administrator for approval.
