# Guest Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and medical information from guests for various medical purposes. This information helps the healthcare provider to prepare for the visit and understand the guest's needs better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as name, phone number, email address, address, and date of birth.
2. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name and phone number.
3. Enter your doctor's name and phone number, if applicable.
4. Share any medical condition you are experiencing.
5. Select your treatment options.
6. Enter your insurance information, if applicable.
7. Provide your medical record number, if applicable.
8. Enter the date and time of your doctor visit, if applicable.
9. Select the location of your doctor visit.
10. Share any medication allergies or medical history.
11. Choose the doctor's specialties and office hours.
12. Select the payment options.
13. Enter any additional comments or information.
14. Sign the form to confirm.
