# delivery_service_feedback_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their delivery experiences and gather their consent for data use. It is used to improve the delivery service and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including email and phone number if available.
2. Answer the question about your delivery experiences. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you had a positive or negative experience.
3. Share any feedback or suggestions about the delivery service.
4. Answer the question about consenting to data use. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your consent.
5. Optionally, indicate whether you would like to submit your feedback anonymously.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information in this field. This will help us contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Delivery Experiences** (`delivery_experiences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Answer 'Yes' if you had a positive experience with our delivery service, 'No' if you had a negative experience.
* **Feedback and Suggestions** (`feedback_and_suggestions`, `text`, required/optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about our delivery service. This will help us improve our service.
* **Consent for Data Use** (`consent_for_data_use`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you consent to our data use, 'No' if you do not.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Delivery Service Improvement** (`delivery_service_improvement`, `note`, required/optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about the delivery service.
* **Customer Experience Improvement** (`customer_experience_improvement`, `note`, required/optional): Share any additional feedback or comments about your customer experience.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any questions or need to contact us, enter your message in this field.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are ready to submit your feedback, 'No' if you are not.
