### crisis_hotline_participation_consent_form - Help Guide

### Purpose

The crisis hotline participation consent form is a document used to obtain consent from individuals to record and share their personal information with crisis hotline staff, emergency services providers, or other organizations. This form is crucial in ensuring that individuals understand and agree to the terms of their data sharing.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  To start the form, please fill in the participant's information.
2.  Enter the participant's phone number in the <u>participant_phone</u> field.
3.  Enter the participant's email address in the <u>participant_email</u> field.
4.  Select one or both of the options for <u>consent_to_record</u>: Yes or No.
5.  Select one or more options for <u>consent_to_share</u>: With Crisis Hotline staff, With emergency services providers, or With other organizations.
6.  Add any additional comments or notes in the <u>additional_notes</u> field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Info**: This page is a note section for the participant to provide any information they want to share about themselves.
    \*\* Required: No
*   **participant_phone**: Enter the participant's phone number for communication purposes.
    \*\* Type: text \*\* Required: No
*   **participant_email**: Enter the participant's email address for contact and communication purposes.
    \*\* Type: email \*\* Required: No
*   **consent_to_record**: Select "Yes" if you agree to have your information recorded.
    \*\* Type: select\_multiple \*\* Options: Yes, No \*\* Required: No
*   **consent_to_share**: Select one or more options to decide with whom you want your information to be shared.
    \*\* Type: select\_multiple \*\* Options: With Crisis Hotline staff, With emergency services providers, With other organizations \*\* Required: No
*   **additional_notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you want to share with the Crisis Hotline staff.
    \*\* Type: note \*\* Required: No

### Tips

*   Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
*   Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
