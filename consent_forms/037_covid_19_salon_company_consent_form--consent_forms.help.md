# COVID 19 Salon Company Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is intended to ensure that clients understand and agree to the COVID-19 safety protocols of the salon. By completing this form, clients confirm their identity, provide necessary contact information, and agree to participate in temperature checks and contact tracing if required.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Choose the date of your scheduled appointment.
4. Select the type of service you have booked.
5. Enter the name of the stylist or technician assigned to your appointment.
6. Indicate whether you have any symptoms associated with COVID-19 or have been in contact with anyone positive in 14 days.
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the salon's safety terms and conditions.
8. If necessary, provide a contact phone number and email address for contact tracing purposes.
9. Confirm that you agree to wear a mask at all times during your service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Appointment Date**: Select the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Service Type**: Choose the type of service you have booked (Haircut/Style, Color Treatment, Facial, Massage, or Waxing).
* **Stylist or Technician Name**: Enter the name of the stylist or technician assigned to your appointment.
* **I certify that I do not have a fever, cough, or shortness of breath**: Confirm that you do not have any symptoms associated with COVID-19.
* **I certify that I have not been in contact with anyone positive for COVID-19 in 14 days**: Confirm that you have not been in contact with anyone positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
* **I waive and release the Salon from any liability related to COVID-19**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the salon's liability waiver related to COVID-19.
* **I agree to wear a mask at all times during my service**: Confirm that you agree to wear a mask at all times during your appointment.
* **Temperature Record (F)**: This field will be filled out by salon staff.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter a contact phone number for contact tracing purposes.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter a contact email address for contact tracing purposes.
* **I have read and agree to the salon safety terms and conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the salon's safety terms and conditions.
