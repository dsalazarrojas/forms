# Educational Technology Pilot Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from the school administration for filming activities on their premises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide information about the school and location where filming will take place.
2. Enter the date and time of the filming.
3. Specify the duration of the filming.
4. Enter the contact information of the school (email and phone number).
5. Choose whether filming consent is granted, pending, or denied.
6. (Optional) Add any additional filming consent notes.
7. If filming consent is granted, indicate the type of equipment to be used.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Info**: Enter the name of the school where filming will take place. This could be a physical address or school name.
* **Filming Location**: Enter a brief description of the location within the school where filming will take place.
* **Filming Date**: Enter the date when the filming will take place.
* **Filming Time**: Enter the time of the filming event.
* **Filming Duration**: Enter the approximate duration of the filming event in minutes or hours.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school contact person.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school contact person.
* **Filming Consent**: Select one of the three options:
	+ Consent: The school grants filming consent.
	+ No Consent: The school denies filming consent.
	+ Pending: The school requires further review or discussion before making a decision.
* **Filming Consent Note**: (Optional) Enter any additional information about filming consent, such as specific rules or restrictions.
* **Filming Equipment**: (Optional) If filming consent is granted, list the type of equipment to be used during filming.
