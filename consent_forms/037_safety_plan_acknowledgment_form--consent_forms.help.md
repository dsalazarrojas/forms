# Safety Plan Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Plan Acknowledgment Form is a document that aims to acknowledge the understanding and acceptance of a safety plan by employees. This form is used to gather information about the employee's awareness and commitment to the safety measures outlined in the plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the safety plan to understand the measures and guidelines outlined.
2. Select the answer to the "Acknowledge The Safety Plan" question on Page 1. Indicate whether you fully understand and agree to the safety plan by selecting the appropriate option.
3. Enter your employee information in the "Employee Information" field on Page 2. This includes your employee ID and any other relevant details.
4. Provide your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field on Page 2. This will help ensure that someone can contact you in case of an emergency.
5. Sign the form on Page 4, indicating your acknowledgment and agreement to the safety plan.
6. Date and date stamp the form on Page 5 to confirm when you signed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Acknowledge The Safety Plan** (`safety_plan_acknowledgment_form`, select multiple, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have read and understand the safety plan, or "No" if you have not.
* **Employee Information** (`employee_information`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Sign Below** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name to acknowledge and agree to the safety plan.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Date and date stamp the form to confirm when you signed it.

## Tips
* Please take a few minutes to read and review the safety plan before completing this form.
* Ensure you understand the safety measures and guidelines outlined in the safety plan.
* If you are unsure about any part of the safety plan, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Remember to date and sign the form to confirm your acknowledgment.
