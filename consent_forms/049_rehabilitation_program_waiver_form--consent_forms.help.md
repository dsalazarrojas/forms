# Rehabilitation Program Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehab Program Waiver Form is used to collect participant's details for the rehabilitation program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rehab Program Waiver Form**: This is the title of the form. It is a header and does not require any input.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name as it is written on their ID or as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number**: Enter the participant's contact number (e.g., phone number or alternate phone number).
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Medical History**: If the participant has a medical condition that may affect their participation in the program, mark 'True'. Otherwise, mark 'False'.
* **Program Services**: Select the program services the participant is interested in (Inpatient, Outpatient, or Both).
* **Program Terms**: This is a text field for the participant to acknowledge the program terms. It is not a place to enter information but rather to sign and date it.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm participation.
