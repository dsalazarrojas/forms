# Waiver Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver Submission Form is designed to collect information about waivers submitted by participants in activities or events. This form gathers data on the activity name, participant's details, and acknowledgement of risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Activity Name** of the activity or event for which the waiver is being submitted.
2. Provide your **Date Of Activity**, the date on which the activity took place.
3. Enter your **Participant Name** and **Participant Email** for identification purposes.
4. Enter your **Participant Phone** number.
5. Check the **Risk Acknowledgement** field to indicate whether you understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the activity.
6. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **Waiver Signed** field to indicate whether you have signed the waiver.
7. Enter the date on which you **Waiver Signed** in the **Date Signed** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the activity or event for which the waiver is being submitted.
* **Date Of Activity** (`date_of_activity`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date on which the activity took place.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter your email address for identification purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Check the box to indicate whether you understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the activity.
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, **Optional**): Select **Yes** to indicate that you have signed the waiver, or **No** if you have not.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, **Optional**): Enter the date on which you signed the waiver.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
* Review the risk acknowledgement carefully before selecting an option.
* Ensure that you sign the waiver if you have completed the activity.
