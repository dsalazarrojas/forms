# Fan Submission Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from fans for the publication of their submissions. It ensures that fans understand and agree to the terms of publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the purpose of each field.
2. Fill in the required fields marked as "required".
3. Choose the correct options for the "Publication Type" field, if applicable.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if applicable.
5. Fill in the "Fan Submitter Info" field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
6. Review your submission carefully and ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fan Submission Publication** (`publication_consent_form`): This field is the main title of the form and serves as a header.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`): Enter the date you are giving consent for the publication of your work. Use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`): Enter the time you are giving consent for the publication of your work. Use the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Publication Name** (`publication_name`): Enter the name of the publication you are consenting to.
* **Publication Type** (`publication_type`): Select the type of publication you are consenting to. Choose from the options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Fan Submitter Info** (`fan_submitter_info`): Enter any additional information about yourself as the fan submitter.
* **Fan Submission Consent** (`fan_submission_consent`): Select the types of consent you are giving. Choose from the options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Other** (`other_publication_type_other`): If you selected "Other" as your publication type, enter a brief description of what it means.
* **Other Publication Type** (`other_publication_type_other_details`): Enter any additional details about your "Other" publication type.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for communication purposes. This field is only required if specified by the publisher.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for communication purposes. This field is only required if specified by the publisher.
