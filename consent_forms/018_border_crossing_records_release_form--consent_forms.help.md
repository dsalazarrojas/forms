# Border Crossing Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request the release of border crossing records to a specific agency or individual. By completing this form, you are authorizing the release of your records to the recipient listed below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Provide your subject information**: Enter your full legal name in the "Full Legal Name" field.
2. Choose the time period for which you want to release your records in the "Time Period for Records Release" field.
3. Enter your passport number in the "Passport Number" field.
4. Choose your nationality from the list.
5. Enter the name of the agency or individual you are authorizing to receive your records in the "Name of Recipient Agency or Individual" field.
6. Enter the address of the recipient agency or individual in the "Recipient Address" field.
7. Select "Yes - I understand and agree" in the "I understand that this authorization is voluntary" field.
8. Enter your digital signature in the "Digital Signature - Type Full Name" field.
9. Enter the date of your authorization in the "Date of Authorization" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Legal Name** (`legal_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name, as it appears on your passport.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, required): This field is not used in this form, but is part of your personal information.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_no`, text, required): Enter your passport number.
* **Nationality** (`person_nationality`, text, required): Select your nationality from the list.
* **Time Period for Records Release** (`records_period_select`, select_one, required): Choose the time period for which you want to release your records. Options include "Past 12 Months", "Past 5 Years", "All Available Records", and "Specific Date Range".
* **If Specific Date Range - please list** (`custom_date_range`, text, required): If you chose "Specific Date Range" in the previous field, enter the date range for which you want to release your records.
* **Name of Recipient Agency or Individual** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency or individual you are authorizing to receive your records.
* **Recipient Address** (`agency_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the recipient agency or individual.
* **I understand that this authorization is voluntary** (`understanding_ack`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - I understand and agree" to confirm your understanding.
* **Digital Signature - Type Full Name** (`digital_sign`, text, required): Enter your digital signature, including your full name.
* **Date of Authorization** (`sign_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you are authorizing the release of your records.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that your records are released correctly.
* If you are unsure of any field, please contact the relevant authority for assistance.
* By completing this form, you are authorizing the release of your border crossing records to the recipient agency or individual listed above.
