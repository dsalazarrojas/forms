# Newcomer Family Retreat Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Newcomer Family Retreat Agreement" form is designed to collect essential information about the newcomer's family, including their contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contact relationships. This information is crucial for the well-being and safety of the individual.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the designated field. This is your personal identifier.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the corresponding field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. If applicable, enter your **Emergency Contact** name in the designated field.
4. Select the relationship between you and your **Emergency Contact** from the drop-down menu.
5. If necessary, provide a brief description of any **Special Needs** you may have.
6. Check the **Guideline Consent** box to confirm that you have read and understand the guidelines.
7. Enter the **Emergency Contact Number** of your primary emergency contact.
8. Select the relationship between you and your secondary **Emergency Contact** from the drop-down menu.
9. Provide the **Emergency Contact Email** of your secondary emergency contact.
10. Enter the **Emergency Contact Address** of your secondary emergency contact.
11. Enter the **Emergency Contact Number 2** of your tertiary emergency contact.
12. If you have a **Medical Condition**, provide a brief description.
13. List any **Allergies** you may have.
14. Repeat steps 3-13 for additional emergency contacts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name**: Enter your personal identifier.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Special Needs**: Provide a brief description of any special needs you may have.
* **Guideline Consent**: Check this box to confirm that you have read and understand the guidelines.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of your primary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2**: Select the relationship between you and your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your secondary emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number 2**: Enter the contact number of your tertiary emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition**: Describe any medical conditions you may have.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you may have.
