# Athlete Livestream Appearance Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by athletes before a livestream event where they will be appearing in a production or event. The form is used to obtain consent for the athlete's appearance, image, and likeness to be used in the livestream.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1.  Ensure you have all the required information and documents at hand.
2.  Fill out the form fields with the required information:
   *   Event Title
   *   Scheduled Livestream Date
   *   Production Company or Organization
   *   Athlete Full Name
   *   Team or Club Name
   *   Email Address
   *   Phone Number
3.  Select your consent options for the following:
   *   I consent to my appearance in the scheduled livestream
   *   I consent to the use of my name, image, and likeness (includes graphics, overlays, and commentary)
   *   I consent to participate in live in-game or post-game interviews
   *   I consent to the use of recorded footage for replays and archives
4.  Confirm that you agree to not receive any financial compensation for this appearance.
5.  Confirm that you acknowledge the Production owns all rights to the production.
6.  Confirm that you have read and agree to the Production Privacy Policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Title (ID: 2)
*   **Event Title** (`production_title`, required): The title of the production or event you will be participating in.

### Scheduled Livestream Date (ID: 3)
*   **Scheduled Livestream Date** (`livestream_date`, required): The date of the livestream event.

### Production Company or Organization (ID: 4)
*   **Production Company or Organization** (`production_org`, required): The company or organization responsible for the production.

### Athlete Full Name (ID: 6)
*   **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, required): Your full name as an athlete.

### Team or Club Name (ID: 7)
*   **Team or Club Name** (`athlete_team`, required): The name of your team or club.

### Email Address (ID: 8)
*   **Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): Your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number (ID: 9)
*   **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.

### I consent to my appearance in the scheduled livestream (ID: 11)
*   **I consent to my appearance in the scheduled livestream** (`livestream_consent`, required): Check "Yes, I consent" to indicate that you agree to appear in the livestream.

### I consent to the use of my name, image, and likeness (ID: 12)
*   **I consent to the use of my name, image, and likeness** (`image_name_consent`, required): Check "Yes, I consent" to indicate that you agree to the use of your name, image, and likeness for the production.

### I consent to participate in live in-game or post-game interviews (ID: 13)
*   **I consent to participate in live in-game or post-game interviews** (`interview_consent`, required): Check "Yes, I consent" to indicate that you agree to participate in live in-game or post-game interviews.

### I consent to the use of recorded footage for replays and archives (ID: 14)
*   **I consent to the use of recorded footage for replays and archives** (`archival_consent`, required): Check "Yes, I consent" to indicate that you agree to the use of recorded footage for replays and archives.

### I agree that I will receive no financial compensation for this appearance (ID: 16)
*   **I agree that I will receive no financial compensation for this appearance** (`compensation_waiver`, required): Check "I agree" to confirm that you will not receive financial compensation for your appearance.

### I acknowledge that the Production owns all rights to the production (ID: 17)
*   **I acknowledge that the Production owns all rights to the production** (`rights_ownership`, required): Check "I acknowledge" to confirm that the Production owns all rights to the production.

### I have read and agree to the Production Privacy Policy (ID: 18)
*   **I have read and agree to the Production Privacy Policy** (`privacy_agreement`, required): Check "I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the Production Privacy Policy.

### Athlete Electronic Signature (ID: 20)
*   **Athlete Electronic Signature** (`athlete_signature`, required): Type your full name as an athlete.

### Date of Signature (ID: 22)
*   **Date of Signature** (`signing_date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
