# online_assessment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online assessment consent form is used to collect informed consent from users before conducting an online assessment. It is a crucial step to ensure users understand the purpose, process, and implications of the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form and start filling out the required information.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your name in the "trainer_name" field.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the school or clinic team that you are part of.
5. If you're participating in a remote evaluation, provide the details in the "remote_evaluation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address to help us keep you updated with assessment details.
* **trainer_name** (`trainer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or documentation.
* **school_clinic_team** (`school_clinic_team`, text, required/optional): If you are a member of a school or clinic team, enter the name of your team.
* **remote_evaluation** (`remote_evaluation`, text, required/optional): If you are participating in a remote evaluation, describe the evaluation details (e.g., location, date, time).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in this form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, ask your trainer or contact person for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've filled out all necessary fields.
