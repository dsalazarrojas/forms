# Bank Public Injunctive Relief Waiver Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bank Public Injunctive Relief Waiver Acknowledgement form is used to obtain a waiver from customers to prevent them from seeking legal remedies that have the primary purpose of preventing harm to the general public. This waiver is typically used in banking or financial contexts where such relief is not allowed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information:
	* **Customer Legal Name**: Enter your full name as listed on your account agreement.
	* **Account Number or Unique ID**: Enter your account number or unique identifier.
	* **Current Mailing Address**: Enter your current mailing address.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
2. Read and understand the waiver:
	* **Disclosure Statement**: Read the disclosure statement carefully. This statement explains the waiver of public injunctive relief.
3. Confirm your agreement:
	* **I acknowledge that my account is subject to an Arbitration Provision**: Confirm that you understand your account is subject to an arbitration provision.
	* **I understand and agree to the waiver of public injunctive relief**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the waiver of public injunctive relief.
	* **I agree to bring claims only in my individual capacity**: Confirm that you will bring claims only in your individual capacity, not as part of a class action or representative proceeding.
4. Confirm your legal representation:
	* **Did you consult with legal counsel before signing this waiver?**: Confirm whether you consulted with an attorney or are signing on your own free will.
5. Sign and confirm:
	* **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)**: Enter your full name as a digital signature.
	* **Date of Acknowledgement**: Enter the date of your acknowledgement.
6. Review and confirm:
	* **Security Note**: Review and confirm that you understand that your IP address and timestamp will be logged with this submission for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Legal Name** (`customer_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as listed on your account agreement.
* **Account Number or Unique ID** (`account_identifier`, text, required): Enter your account number or unique identifier.
* **Current Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Enter your current mailing address.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Disclosure Statement** (`waiver_disclosure_text`, note, required): Read the waiver disclosure statement carefully.
* **I acknowledge that my account is subject to an Arbitration Provision** (`arbitration_provision_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your account is subject to an arbitration provision.
* **I understand and agree to the waiver of public injunctive relief** (`waiver_scope_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the waiver of public injunctive relief.
* **I agree to bring claims only in my individual capacity** (`individual_capacity_check`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will bring claims only in your individual capacity, not as part of a class action or representative proceeding.
* **Did you consult with legal counsel before signing this waiver?** (`counsel_consultation`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you consulted with an attorney or are signing on your own free will.
* **I sign this agreement voluntarily and without duress** (`voluntary_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are signing this agreement voluntarily and without duress.
* **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)** (`signature_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Acknowledgement** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your acknowledgement.
* **Security Note** (`ip_log_note`, note, required): Review and confirm that you understand that your IP address and timestamp will be logged with this submission for verification.
