This form is used to grant authorization to represent or act on behalf of another person or entity in legal matters.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the authorization type from the "Authorization" field.
3. Sign the form by filling in the "Signature" field with your actual signature.
4. Fill in your title and position if you're signing on behalf of an organization.
5. Confirm your authority by filling in the "Confirm" field.
