# New Policy Implementation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Policy Implementation Consent Form is used to obtain consent from stakeholders for new policy implementations. This form ensures that all relevant stakeholders have provided their consent before proceeding with the new policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions asked.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### New Policy Implementation Consent Form (page 1)
* **New Policy Implementation Consent Form** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This is the title of the form. You are expected to confirm that this is the correct form for the new policy implementation.
### Organization (page 2)
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If your organization has a presence in the relevant area or industry, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
### Stakeholders (page 3)
* **Stakeholders** (`stakeholder`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): If you or your organization will be affected by the new policy, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
### Signature (page 4)
* **Signature** (`signature`, `time`, required/optional): Sign and date this section to confirm your consent.
### Date (page 5)
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Date of your signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out this form for the correct purpose and not for any other reason.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the responsible party.
* This form is a legal document and should be treated as such.
