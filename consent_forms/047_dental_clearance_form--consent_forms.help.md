# Dental Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Clearance Form is used to gather information from patients about their medical history and current treatment needs. This form is typically completed by patients before undergoing dental procedures to ensure that the dentist has a clear understanding of their medical status and can provide the best possible care. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the field marked "Patient Name". This is a required field, so be sure to fill it out accurately.
2. Enter the date of the patient's last visit in the field marked "Date of Last Visit". If the patient has never visited the dentist before, leave this field blank.
3. Provide details about the patient's disease process in the field marked "Disease Process". This could include any medical conditions or allergies the patient has that may impact their dental care.
4. Enter the patient's medical history in the field marked "Medical History". This could include any previous surgeries, hospitalizations, or other medical conditions that may affect their dental care.
5. If necessary, indicate if the patient requires additional treatment by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the field marked "Treatment Needed".
6. If necessary, indicate if the patient requires additional treatments not listed in the field marked "Additional Treatment". Select "Yes" or "No" for each option.
7. Enter any comments from the doctor or other dental team members in the field marked "Doctor Comments". This could include any notes or concerns they may have about the patient's care.
8. Enter your phone number in the field marked "Phone". This will help the dentist contact you with any updates or questions they may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text, required)**: Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Date of Last Visit (date, required)**: The date of the last time the patient visited the dentist.
* **Disease Process (text, required)**: Provide details about any medical conditions or allergies that may impact the patient's dental care.
* **Medical History (text, optional)**: Enter any medical history that may affect the patient's dental care, such as previous surgeries or hospitalizations.
* **Treatment Needed (select_one, optional)**: If the patient requires additional treatment, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
* **Additional Treatment (select_multiple, optional)**: If the patient requires additional treatments not listed, select "Yes" for each option.
* **Doctor Comments (note, optional)**: Enter any comments or notes from the dentist or other dental team members about the patient's care.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for the dentist to contact you with any updates or questions.
