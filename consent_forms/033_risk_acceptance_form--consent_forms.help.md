# Risk Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Acceptance Form is a project management tool used to identify and document potential risks related to a specific project. It helps users acknowledge and manage risks effectively, ensuring that all stakeholders are aware of the risks and can take necessary actions to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your user input, if required.
3. Select the relevant date and time for the risk description.
4. Provide a clear and concise risk description.
5. Choose your level of risk acceptance from the options provided.
6. Add any comments or further explanations related to the risk.
7. Select who the risk is assigned to.
8. Choose the current status of the risk.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: A field to collect any additional information from the user, if required.
* **Date**: The date when the risk was identified or last updated.
* **Time**: The time when the risk was identified or last updated.
* **Risk Description**: A field to describe the potential risk in detail.
* **User Risk Acceptance**: A field to choose your level of risk acceptance.
*   + Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: A field to acknowledge that you have understood and accepted the risk.
*   + Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Comments**: A field to add any further comments or explanations related to the risk.
* **Assigned To**: A field to select who is responsible for managing or mitigating the risk.
*   + Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Status**: A field to update the status of the risk.
*   + Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
