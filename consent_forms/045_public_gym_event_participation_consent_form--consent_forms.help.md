# Public Gym Event Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Public Gym Event Participation Consent Form, designed to collect contact information and health status of participants for the purpose of ensuring their well-being and safety during public gym events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form instructions and guidelines before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required and optional fields.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant contact information, such as email or phone number, to help us reach you if needed.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, text, optional): Please describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or disabilities you may have.
* **Signed Consent** (`signed_consent`, text, optional): This is a field to sign and date your consent to participate in the event. Please type your signature and the date you are signing this form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask the event organizers for clarification.
- Remember to sign and date your consent to complete the form.
