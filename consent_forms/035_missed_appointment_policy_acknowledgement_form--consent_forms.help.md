# Missed Appointment Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a missed appointment in accordance with the organization's missed appointment policy. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the missed appointment, including the patient's name, contact number, email, appointment date and time, and the reason for missing the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient name" field.
2. Enter the patient's contact number in the "phone number" field.
3. Enter the patient's email address in the "email" field.
4. Select the date of the missed appointment in the "appointment date" field.
5. Select the time of the missed appointment in the "appointment time" field.
6. Enter the reason for missing the appointment in the "reason for missed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient name** (text, required): Enter the name of the patient who missed the appointment.
* **phone number** (text, optional): Enter the patient's contact number, if available.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the patient's email address, if available.
* **appointment date** (date, optional): Select the date of the missed appointment.
* **appointment time** (time, optional): Select the time of the missed appointment.
* **reason for missed** (note, optional): Enter the reason why the appointment was missed.
