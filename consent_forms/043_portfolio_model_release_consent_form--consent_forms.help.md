# Portfolio Model Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from models or individuals whose images are being used in a portfolio or similar visual collection. The form ensures that models are aware of and agree to the terms of how their images will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully and understand the terms and conditions of image usage.
2.  Ensure that you have all necessary information and documents before proceeding.
3.  Fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
4.  Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
5.  Submit the form and keep a record of the signed version for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Portfolio Model Release Consent Form**:
    *   This is the form title and heading.
*   **Model Name**: 
    *   Please enter your name as the model or individual whose image is being used.
*   **Photographer**: 
    *   Please enter the name of the photographer or individual capturing your image.
*   **Date of Signature**: 
    *   Enter the date you sign this form.
*   **Model Signature**: 
    *   Select one of the options to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms.
*   **Photographer Signature**: 
    *   Select one of the options to confirm that they have read and agree to the terms.
*   **Model Consent**: 
    *   Select one of the options to indicate your consent to the usage of your images.
*   **Photographer Consent**: 
    *   Select one of the options to indicate your consent to the usage of your images.
*   **Additional Comments**: 
    *   If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
*   **Email**: 
    *   Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: 
    *   Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date**: 
    *   Enter the date of the event or shoot.
*   **Time**: 
    *   Enter the time of the event or shoot.
