# Hair Salon COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers or clients about their COVID-19 liability release waiver for the hair salon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the available fields to provide any additional comments or information about your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`): This is the first field in the form. Please provide your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Risks** (`risks`): This field is where you should list any known or suspected risks you have regarding COVID-19. This may include any underlying health conditions or other factors that could impact your visit to the hair salon.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`): This field asks about your current health status. Please select "Active" if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms or have been diagnosed with COVID-19. Select "Inactive" if you are not experiencing any symptoms and have not been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): This field is where you can list any medical conditions you have that may impact your visit to the hair salon.
* **Have You Had the COVID-19 Vaccine?** (`have_covid_vaccine`): This field asks about your vaccination status. Please select "Yes" if you have received the COVID-19 vaccine, and "No" if you have not.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`): This field is where you can describe any recent travel or exposure to COVID-19. Please be as detailed as possible, including dates and locations.
* **Have You Been in Contact with Someone with COVID-19?** (`been_in_contact`): This field asks about your exposure to someone with COVID-19. Please select "Yes" if you have been in contact with someone with COVID-19, and "No" if you have not.
* **Have You Experienced Symptoms?** (`have_symptoms`): This field asks about your current symptoms. Please select "Yes" if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms, and "No" if you are not.
* **Recent Travel Date** (`recent_travel`): This field is where you can enter the date of your most recent travel.
* **Last Day in Contact** (`last_day_in_contact`): This field is where you can enter the date you last came into contact with someone with COVID-19.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): This field is where you can enter the name and contact information of the person you came into contact with who has COVID-19.
* **Signature Date and Time** (`signature_date` and `signature_time`): These fields are where you can sign and date the form, indicating that you have read and understood the terms of the liability release waiver.
* **Salutation** (`salutation`): This field asks if you agree to the terms of the liability release waiver. Please select "Yes" if you agree, and "No" if you do not.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): This field is where you can enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): This field is where you can enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is where you can enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): This field is where you can enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`): This field is where you can enter your city.
* **State** (`state`): This field is where you can enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`zip`): This field is where you can enter your zip code.
