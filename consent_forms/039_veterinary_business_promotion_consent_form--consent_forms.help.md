# Veterinary Business Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent from clients of veterinary businesses for promotion, marketing, and sharing of business information. It helps veterinary businesses to obtain permission from their clients to share their data, promotional emails, and marketing messages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name on the "client_name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address on the "contact_email" field.
3. Provide your phone number on the "contact_phone" field.
4. Enter your business address on the "address" field.
5. Choose how you want to share your data with the "consent_to_share_data" field. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
6. Choose how you want to receive promotional emails with the "consent_to_receive_promos" field. You can select 'email', 'text', or 'both'.
7. Choose how you want to share promotional messages with the "consent_to_share_promos" field. You can select 'email', 'text', or 'both'.
8. Select the marketing channel you prefer with the "marketing_channel" field. You can select 'social media', 'email', or 'both' or 'text'.
9. Type the promotional message you want to share with the "marketing_message" field.
10. Choose how you want to share the promotional message with the "consent_to_share_marketing_message" field. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
11. Choose whether to use images for promotion with the "consent_to_use_image" field. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
12. Choose how you want to share your phone number with the "consent_to_share_phone" field. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
13. Choose how you want to share your business address with the "consent_to_share_address" field. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
14. Enter your business promotional email address on the "veterinary_business_promo_email" field.
15. Enter your business promotional phone number on the "veterinary_business_promo_phone" field.
16. Enter your business promotional address on the "veterinary_business_promo_address" field.
17. Type the promotional message you want to share on the "veterinary_business_promo_message" field.
18. Upload an image for promotion on the "veterinary_business_promo_image" field.
19. Enter the URL of the promotional image on the "veterinary_business_promo_image_url" field.
20. Enter the ID of the promotional image on the "veterinary_business_promo_image_id" field.
21. Enter the width of the promotional image on the "veterinary_business_promo_image_width" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client you are representing the veterinary business.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the veterinary business.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the veterinary business.
* **Address**: Enter the business address of the veterinary business.
* **Veterinary Business Name**: Enter the name of the veterinary business.
* **Veterinary Business Address**: Enter the address of the veterinary business.
* **Consent to Share Data**: Choose whether to share your client data with others. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
* **Consent to Receive Promos**: Choose how you want to receive promotional emails. You can select 'email', 'text', or 'both'.
* **Consent to Share Promos**: Choose how you want to share promotional messages. You can select 'email', 'text', or 'both'.
* **Marketing Channel**: Select the marketing channel you prefer. You can select 'social media', 'email', or 'both' or 'text'.
* **Marketing Message**: Type the promotional message you want to share.
* **Consent to Share Marketing Message**: Choose whether to share the marketing message with others. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
* **Consent to Use Image**: Choose whether to use images for promotion. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
* **Consent to Share Phone**: Choose how you want to share your phone number with others. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
* **Consent to Share Address**: Choose how you want to share your business address with others. You can select 'yes' or 'no'.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Email**: Enter the email address of the promotional message.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Phone**: Enter the phone number of the promotional message.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Address**: Enter the promotional address of the veterinary business.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Message**: Type the promotional message you want to share.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Image**: Upload an image for promotion.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Image URL**: Enter the URL of the promotional image.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Image ID**: Enter the ID of the promotional image.
* **Veterinary Business Promotional Image Width**: Enter the width of the promotional image.
