# Nasal Flu Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent and other relevant information from patients and their guardians regarding the nasal flu vaccine. It is intended to ensure that all necessary permissions and details are captured before administering the vaccination.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information as a patient or guardian. This includes filling out the fields under the "Patient Information" page.
2. If applicable, provide the required information for your guardian.
3. Select the correct "Flu Type" and enter the date and time of vaccination.
4. Enter the details of the vaccination, including who administered it and the "Vaccine Lot Number".
5. Confirm your "Consent" by selecting the appropriate option from the dropdown list.
6. Sign and date the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (page 1, text): Enter your personal contact information and demographic details.
* **Guardian Information** (page 2, text): Enter your guardian's contact information and demographic details, if applicable.
* **Vaccination Date** (page 3, date): Enter the date you will receive the nasal flu vaccine.
* **Vaccination Time** (page 3, time): Enter the time you will receive the vaccine.
* **Flu Type** (page 4, select_one): Select the type of flu vaccine you are receiving (Influenza A, Influenza B, Both, or Unknown).
* **Consent** (page 4, select_multiple): Confirm your consent for the vaccination. Select "True" to indicate you consent, "False" to decline, or "Pending" if you are unsure.
* **Patient Signature** (page 5, note): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Guardian Signature** (page 5, note): Sign your name if you are a guardian providing consent.
* **Healthcare Provider** (page 6, text): Enter your healthcare provider's contact information and details.
* **Nasal Flu Vaccine Info** (page 6, text): Enter any additional information about the nasal flu vaccine you received.
* **Vaccination Administered** (page 7, text): Enter the details of who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccination Administered By** (page 7, text): Enter the details of who administered the vaccine.
* **Vaccine Lot Number** (page 8, text): Enter the lot number of the nasal flu vaccine administered.
* **Expiration Date** (page 8, date): Enter the expiration date of the vaccine.
* **Expiration Time** (page 8, time): Enter the expiration time of the vaccine.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields and that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
