# Return Authorization Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form serves as a legal document to obtain the customer's consent for returning an item. It ensures that the customer understands and agrees to the return policy and procedures, which are essential for a smooth return process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately, paying attention to the "customer_info_form" (Customer Information), "return_reason" (Reason for Return), "product_info_form" (Product Information), "return_policy_form" (Return Policy), and "order_details_form" (Order Details).
* For "contact_info_form" (Contact Information), select "Yes" if you would like to receive contact from the retailer regarding your return, or "No" if you do not want to be contacted.
* Enter your shipping address in "shipping_return_address_form" (Shipping Return Address).
* If required, select the reason for return from "return_reason_reason_code" (Return Reason Code).
* Sign the form with your actual signature at "signature_form" (Signature) or "signature_form_2" (Signature 2). Please note that the signature date is also required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (customer-info-form-label)**: 
  Enter your name and contact information to help the retailer contact you regarding your return.
* **Reason for Return (return-reason-label)**: 
  Explain the reason you are returning the item. This will help the retailer understand the reason for the return and process it accordingly.
* **Product Information (product-info-form-label)**: 
  Provide a detailed description of the product you are returning, including the product name, model number, and any other relevant details.
* **Return Policy (return-policy-form-label)**: 
  Confirm that you have read and understood the return policy. This is a critical part of the return process and ensures that you are aware of the expectations and procedures.
* **Order Details (order-details-form-label)**: 
  Enter the order number and date of the purchase to help the retailer locate the order in their system.
* **Contact Information (contact-info-form-label)**: 
  Select "Yes" if you would like the retailer to contact you regarding your return, or "No" if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Shipping Return Address (shipping-return-address-form-label)**: 
  Enter your shipping address where the returned item will be shipped back to you.
* **Return Reason Code (return-reason-reason-code-label)**: 
  Select the reason for return from the provided options if required.
* **Signature (signature-form-label)**: 
  Sign the form with your actual signature, ensuring that you are the person returning the item.
* **Signature 2 (signature-form-2-label)**: 
  If required, sign the form again with your actual signature, ensuring that you are the person returning the item.
* **Signature Date (signature-form-date-label)**: 
  Enter the date of your actual signature, ensuring that it matches the date of your actual signature.
