# Childcare Sleep Policy Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help ensure that you, as a parent or guardian, are aware of and understand the childcare sleep policy. It will ask for information about your child's sleep habits, any sleep difficulties they may have, and your own comfort items and preferences for their sleep.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as instructed, providing as much detail as possible. Please note that some fields are required for us to provide safe and quality care for your child. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`): <u>Date of policy acknowledgement</u>
Please provide the date you received and read this policy.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`): <u>Legal first name of parent or guardian</u>
Please type your legal first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`): <u>Legal last name of parent or guardian</u>
Please type your legal last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`): <u>Primary email address for contact</u>
Please provide your primary email address where we can reach you.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`): <u>Primary phone number for contact</u>
Please provide your primary phone number where we can reach you.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`): <u>Legal first name of the child</u>
Please type your child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`): <u>Legal last name of the child</u>
Please type your child's legal last name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`): <u>Please provide the child's birth date</u>
Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`): <u>Current age of the child in years</u>
Please enter your child's current age in years.
* **Child Room or Class** (`child_room_or_class`): <u>Assigned room or class</u>
Please indicate the assigned room or class for your child.
* **Sleep Policy Received** (`policy_received`): <u>Have you received a copy of the sleep policy?</u>
Please select whether you have received a copy of the sleep policy.
* **Policy Understanding** (`policy_understanding`): <u>Do you understand the sleep policy requirements?</u>
Please select whether you understand the sleep policy requirements.
* **Safe Sleep Practices** (`safe_sleep_practices`): <u>Do you understand safe sleep practices for infants?</u>
Please select whether you understand safe sleep practices for your child.
* **Nap Schedule Understanding** (`nap_schedule_understanding`): <u>Do you understand the center's nap schedule?</u>
Please select whether you understand the center's nap schedule.
* **Child Sleep Routine** (`child_sleep_routine`): <u>Description of your child's typical sleep routine at home</u>
Please describe your child's typical sleep routine at home.
* **Child Nap Schedule** (`child_nap_schedule`): <u>What is your child's typical nap schedule?</u>
Please select your child's typical nap schedule.
* **Typical Nap Duration** (`typical_nap_duration`): <u>How long does your child typically nap?</u>
Please select the length of time your child typically naps.
* **Sleep Comfort Items** (`sleep_comfort_items`): <u>Does your child need any comfort items for sleep?</u>
Please select any comfort items your child may need for sleep.
* **Sleep Difficulties** (`sleep_difficulties`): <u>Does your child have any sleep difficulties?</u>
Please select whether your child has any sleep difficulties.
* **Soothing Techniques** (`soothing_techniques`): <u>What techniques work to soothe your child to sleep?</u>
Please describe any techniques that help soothe your child to sleep.
* **Sleep Position Preference** (`sleep_position_preference`): <u>Does your child have a preferred sleep position?</u>
Please select your child's preferred sleep position.
* **Medical Sleep Concerns** (`medical_sleep_concerns`): <u>Are there any medical concerns related to sleep?</u>
Please describe any medical concerns related to sleep.
* **Sleep Apnea or Breathing Issues** (`sleep_apnea_or_breathing`): <u>Does your child have any breathing issues during sleep?</u>
Please select whether your child has any breathing issues during sleep.
* **Monitoring Preferences** (`monitoring_preferences`): <u>How often should staff check on your child during sleep?</u>
Please select the frequency of staff checks on your child during sleep.
* **Wake-Up Instructions** (`wake_up_instructions`): <u>Any special instructions for waking your child?</u>
Please describe any special instructions for waking your child.
* **Policy Acknowledgement** (`policy_acknowledgement`): <u>Have you acknowledged that you have read and understand the sleep policy?</u>
Please select whether you acknowledge understanding the sleep policy.
* **Policy Compliance Agreement** (`policy_compliance_agreement`): <u>Do you agree to comply with the sleep policy?</u>
Please select whether you agree to comply with the sleep policy.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): <u>Any additional notes about your child's sleep needs</u>
Please provide any additional notes about your child's sleep needs.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`): <u>Type your full name as your electronic signature</u>
Please type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): <u>Date of electronic signature</u>
Please enter the date of your electronic signature.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible in each section to ensure we have a clear understanding of your child's sleep needs.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form is designed to ensure your child receives safe and quality care while at our center.
