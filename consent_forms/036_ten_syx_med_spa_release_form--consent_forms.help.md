## Purpose
The "ten_syx_med_spa_release_form--consent_forms" form is designed to capture patient information and consent for medical release purposes. It is essential to ensure that the patient's personal and medical information is accurately recorded and that their consent is properly documented. 
The form should not be used for any other purpose and should only be filled out by patients for medical release purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your personal and medical information accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
3. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to it.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Medical Group** (`Medical Group`, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the list of medical groups.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Physician** (`Physician`, text, required): Enter your current physician's name.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, text, required): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to this form.
* **Date Of Last Release Form Signing** (`Date Of Last Release Form Signing`, date, required): Enter the date of the last time you signed a release form.
* **Medical Group (2)** (`Medical Group (2)`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical group information if necessary.
* **Physician Name** (`Physician Name`, text, optional): Enter your physician's name if you have one.
* **Physician Signature** (`Physician Signature`, text, optional): Enter your physician's signature if applicable.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the form, if applicable.
Please note that some fields are required, while others are optional, and you should only fill out those that apply to your situation.
