# Tenant Directory Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tenant Directory Publication Consent Form is used to obtain consent from tenants to publish their directory listing in the building's tenant directory. The form ensures that tenants are aware of and agree to their information being shared with other tenants and the building management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select a date and time for your directory listing in the Date and Time fields.
6. Select the type of tenant you are (Commercial, Residential, or Mixed Use).
7. Choose which directory type you would like your listing to be (Public, Private, or Both).
8. If you have any additional details, enter them in the Additional Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date** (Required): Select a date for your directory listing to be published.
* **Time** (Required): Select a time for your directory listing to be published.
* **Tenant Type** (Required): Select the type of tenant you are (Commercial, Residential, or Mixed Use).
* **Directory Type** (Optional): Choose which directory type you would like your listing to be (Public, Private, or Both).
* **Additional Details** (Optional): Enter any additional information about your directory listing.
