# Audio Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture participant consent for audio recordings, ensuring a clear understanding of the recording's purpose, usage, and distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your full name, email address, phone number, and date of birth as requested.
2. Choose your age group (18 or older) for age verification.
3. If you are a minor, provide your parent or guardian's name and email address.
4. Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., podcast, interview, voice over).
5. Enter the project name, recording date, location, and method.
6. Consent to being recorded and agree to the usage, duration, and distribution of the recording.
7. Choose your preferred anonymity level and whether you will receive compensation.
8. Understand that you may withdraw your consent before publication.
9. Sign the form as your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, required): Choose your age group (18 or older).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, optional): If you are a minor, enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Recording Purpose** (`recording_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the recording (e.g., podcast, interview, voice over).
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name or title of the project.
* **Recording Date** (`recording_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the recording session.
* **Recording Location** (`recording_location`, text, required): Enter where the recording will take place.
* **Recording Method** (`recording_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of recording (e.g., in-person studio, remote recording).
* **Consent Granted** (`consent_granted`, select_one, required): Consent to being recorded and agree to the usage, duration, and distribution of the recording.
* **Usage Rights** (`usage_rights`, select_multiple, required): Choose how the recording may be used (e.g., public distribution, internal use only).
* **Usage Duration** (`usage_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long the recording may be used.
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, select_one, required): Choose where the recording may be distributed.
* **Compensation** (`compensation`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will receive compensation.
* **Anonymity Request** (`anonymity_request`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred anonymity level.
* **Withdrawal Rights** (`withdrawal_rights`, select_one, required): Understand that you may withdraw your consent before publication.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign the form as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.

Please take a moment to review this form carefully and fill it out accurately to ensure a smooth and compliant audio recording process.
