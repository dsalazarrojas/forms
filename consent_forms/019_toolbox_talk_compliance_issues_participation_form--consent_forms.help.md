# Toolbox Talk Compliance Issues Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants regarding compliance issues that may have arisen during a toolbox talk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You do not need to be an expert in compliance issues to participate. If you are unsure about any of the questions, ask your supervisor for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in the format of "name@example.com" (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
- **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your role or position within the company.
- **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your supervisor, as it appears on their ID badge or personnel file.
- **Compliance Issue Type** (`compliance_issue_type`, select multiple, required/optional): Select one or more compliance issues that you believe occurred during the toolbox talk. The options are:
  - Other
  - Workplace violence
  - Harassment
  - Workplace injury
  - Other (please specify)
- **Other Compliance Issue** (`compliance_issue_other`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other" as the compliance issue type, describe the issue in the space provided.
- **Did the participant attend the compliance talk?** (`participant_attended`, select one, required/optional): If you were present during the toolbox talk, choose "True". If you were not present, choose "False".
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the toolbox talk.
- **Supervisor's Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, text, required/optional): The supervisor's signature is required to validate your submission.
- **Participant's Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required/optional): Your signature is required to confirm that you have provided accurate and honest information.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and honest information when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor.
- This form is not intended to be a comprehensive or exhaustive list of compliance issues. It is meant to be a starting point for discussion and follow-up action.
