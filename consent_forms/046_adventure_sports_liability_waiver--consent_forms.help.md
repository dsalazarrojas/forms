# Adventure Sports Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for participants in adventure sports activities. It's designed to collect necessary information to ensure the participant's safety and the liability of the organizers in case of accidents or injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full legal name as prompted in the "Participant name" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of the activity you will be participating in in the "Activity description" field.
3.  Select "I acknowledge" in the "Risk acknowledgement" field to confirm that you understand and accept the risks associated with the activity.
4.  If you have any medical conditions or restrictions, provide details in the "Medical conditions or restrictions" field (optional).
5.  Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency contact" field.
6.  Choose "I agree" in the "Indemnity agreement" field to confirm that you understand and agree to indemnify the organizers.
7.  Type your name as it appears on your identification in the "Signature name" field.
8.  Select the date you sign the form in the "Signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Liability Waiver --**: This is the title of the form.
- **Participant name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
  *   Full legal name (e.g., John Doe)
- **Activity description**: Briefly describe the activity you will be participating in.
  *   Example: "Participating in an extreme sports competition"
- **Risk acknowledgement**: Check "I acknowledge" to confirm that you understand the risks associated with the activity.
  *   You are acknowledging that you understand the risks involved in the activity.
- **Medical conditions or restrictions**: If you have any medical conditions or restrictions, provide details.
  *   Example: "I have a heart condition and need to take regular breaks"
- **Emergency contact**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
  *   Example: "John Smith, 555-123-4567"
- **Indemnity agreement**: Check "I agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to indemnify the organizers.
  *   You are confirming that you understand and agree to hold the organizers harmless.
- **Signature name**: Type your name as it appears on your identification.
  *   Your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Signature date**: Select the date you sign the form.
  *   Date signed (mm/dd/yyyy)
