# Vehicle Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Release Form is used to document vehicle releases and obtain information about the vehicle being released. This form is required for all vehicles being released from our inventory.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct vehicle make and model from the options provided.
3. Enter the correct vehicle registration number.
4. Provide a valid owner's name and address.
5. Enter any additional details about the vehicle, such as its year and color.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner** (`vehicle_owner`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vehicle's original owner.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the vehicle's original owner.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number as displayed on its license plate.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one, optional): Select the make of the vehicle from the provided options: Ford, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, or Other.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Mustang, Corolla, Civic, etc.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle, e.g., 2018, 2020, etc.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the vehicle, e.g., Red, Blue, Black, etc.
* **Buyer's Full Name** (`buyer_full_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person buying the vehicle.
* **Buyer's Address** (`buyer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person buying the vehicle.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to avoid any issues with the vehicle release process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance before proceeding.
* Please review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
