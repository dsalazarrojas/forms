# Education Technology Product Demo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the consent of users before providing a product demo.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, ensure you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the demo.

1. Enter your user name in the `User Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email_address` field.
3. Enter the product version you will be demoing.
4. Enter the duration of the demo in hours or minutes.
5. Enter the date and time of the demo.
6. Select your role during the demo (User or Administrator).
7. Enter your organization name.
8. Enter your contact phone number.
9. Enter your contact email address.
10. If applicable, enter the product URL.
11. Enter any demo notes.
12. Select the consent status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
13. Enter any participant details.
14. Select the approval status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
15. Enter any approval notes.
16. Enter the participant's name.
17. Enter the participant's email address.
18. Enter the participant's phone number.
19. Enter the participant's organization name.
20. If applicable, enter the participant's product URL.
21. Enter the participant's notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter your name as it will appear on the demo report.
* **email_address**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Product Version**: Enter the version of the product you will be demoing.
* **Demo Duration**: Enter the duration of the demo in hours or minutes.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the demo in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the demo in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Role**: Select your role during the demo (User or Administrator).
* **Organization**: Enter your organization name.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Product URL**: If applicable, enter the product URL.
* **Demo Notes**: Enter any additional information about the demo.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date of the consent in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time of the consent in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Consent Status**: Select the consent status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
* **Participant Details**: Enter any details about the participant.
* **Approval Status**: Select the approval status (Approved, Declined, or Pending).
* **Approval Notes**: Enter any additional information about the approval.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Organization**: If applicable, enter the participant's organization name.
* **Participant Product URL**: If applicable, enter the participant's product URL.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional information about the participant.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
* Review the terms and conditions of the demo before providing consent.
