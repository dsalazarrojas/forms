# School Volunteer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to provide consent for their child to participate in school volunteer activities. It collects personal and contact information, emergency contact details, and medical information, as well as information on the volunteer role and event they will be participating in.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your child's parent or guardian information, including your name, contact info, and any medical information that may be relevant.
2. Select the school event and volunteer role your child will be participating in.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and relationship to your child.
4. Review and confirm your child's medical information, including any allergies or medical conditions.
5. Review and confirm your emergency contact information.
6. Indicate your permission for your child to participate in the selected event and role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the volunteer activity.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name as it will be displayed on the volunteer activity roster.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your phone number and email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **School Event** (`school_event`, select_one, required): Select the specific event your child will be participating in (e.g. Event 1, Event 2, Event 3).
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, required): Select the volunteer role your child will be playing in the event (e.g. Parent, Teacher, Volunteer).
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, note, required): Enter any relevant medical information about your child, including allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your child's medical information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Indicate your relationship to the emergency contact person (e.g. Spouse, Friend, Family Member).
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, text, required): Indicate your permission for your child to participate in the selected event and role.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`, text, optional): Indicate your child's permission to participate in the selected event and role (if applicable).
* **Volunteer Permission** (`volunteer_permission`, text, optional): Indicate your permission for your child to participate in the selected event and role (if applicable).
* **Background Check** (`background_check`, text, optional): Check the box if you want a background check to be performed on your child.

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the school administration or volunteer coordinator.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
