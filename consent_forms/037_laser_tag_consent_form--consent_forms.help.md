# Laser Tag Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Laser Tag Consent Form is used to capture essential information from customers or their parents/guardians. This form is a crucial document that helps us understand the individual's preferences and consent for our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Double-check your information to ensure it is correct.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask your parent/guardian or contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer, as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Age**: Enter the customer's age.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's name (if applicable).
* **Contact Number**: Enter a valid phone number or other contact method where we can reach them.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where we can send important updates and notifications.

Note: This form has no required fields. Please fill in all fields that are relevant to your situation.
 
Tips:
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

This form serves as a consent document for our services. Please take the time to review and fill it out carefully. We appreciate your cooperation and respect your privacy.
