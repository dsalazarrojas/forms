# Field Trip Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Authorization Form is used by students to request permission for a field trip. This form helps school administrators gather necessary information and make informed decisions about trip approvals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the trip option(s) you wish to attend from the dropdown list.
2. Enter the date of the trip using the date picker.
3. Enter the time of the trip using the time picker.
4. Enter the location of the trip.
5. Enter your parent's name.
6. Enter your name as a student.
7. If applicable, enter your email address (for optional use).
8. Select your school administrator from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Field Trip Authorization** (`field_trip_authorization`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type(s) of field trip you wish to attend by selecting one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the trip using the time picker.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the trip.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your parent's name.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one. Please note that this is optional and not required for approval.
* **School Admin** (`school_admin`, select_one, optional): Select the school administrator who will be responsible for your trip.
