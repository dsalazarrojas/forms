# Community Radio Song Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to grant consent for the community radio station to feature and broadcast your music.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Artist or Band Name**: Enter the name of your artist or band.
2. **Primary Contact Full Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the artist or band.
3. **Artist or Band Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
4. **Artist or Band Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
5. **Song Title(s) for Consent**: List each song title that you are granting consent for, separated by commas.
6. **Album or EP Title**: Enter the title of the album or EP that the song(s) is/are from, if applicable.
7. **Date of Original Recording**: Enter the date the song(s) was originally recorded.
8. **Do you own the full copyright for this recording?**: Select the correct answer based on your copyright ownership.
9. **If no, what is the name of the external copyright holder?**: If you selected "No" above, enter the name of the external copyright holder.
10. **Broadcast Permission**: Select the level of broadcast permission you are granting (full or no permission).
11. **Station Recording Permission**: Select the level of recording permission you are granting (full or no permission).
12. **Social Media and Website Permission**: Select whether you are granting permission for the station to use clips on their digital platforms.
13. **General Promotional Use Permission**: Select whether you are granting permission for the station to use your music in station promos.
14. **How should we credit you during the broadcast?**: Enter the format you would like to be credited in during the broadcast.
15. **Artist Agreement Statement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.
16. **Artist or Authorized Representative Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
17. **Today's Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Band Name**: Enter the name of your artist or band as it appears on your releases.
* **Primary Contact Full Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be handling communications for the artist or band.
* **Artist or Band Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Artist or Band Contact Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Song Title(s) for Consent**: List each song title that you are granting consent for, separated by commas.
* **Album or EP Title**: Enter the title of the album or EP that the song(s) is/are from, if applicable.
* **Date of Original Recording**: Enter the date the song(s) was originally recorded.
* **Do you own the full copyright for this recording?**: Select "Yes" if you own the copyright, "Yes - My band owns the rights" if your band owns the copyright, or "No" if someone else owns the rights.
* **If no, what is the name of the external copyright holder?**: If you selected "No" above, enter the name of the external copyright holder.
* **Broadcast Permission**: Select "I grant full permission" if you are granting permission for the station to broadcast your music, or "I do not grant permission" if you are not.
* **Station Recording Permission**: Select "I grant full permission" if you are granting permission for the station to record a live performance, or "I do not grant permission" if you are not.
* **Social Media and Website Permission**: Select "True" if you are granting permission for the station to use clips on their digital platforms, or "False" if you are not.
* **General Promotional Use Permission**: Select "True" if you are granting permission for the station to use your music in promos, or "False" if you are not.
* **How should we credit you during the broadcast?**: Enter the format you would like to be credited in during the broadcast (e.g. "Song Name - Artist Name").
* **Artist Agreement Statement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this form.
* **Artist or Authorized Representative Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Today's Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to list each song title that you are granting consent for, separated by commas.
* If you selected "No" for "Do you own the full copyright for this recording?", make sure to enter the name of the external copyright holder.
* Use the correct format for crediting you during the broadcast.
