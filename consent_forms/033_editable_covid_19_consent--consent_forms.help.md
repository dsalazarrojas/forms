# Editable Covid-19 Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and usage of the Editable Covid-19 Consent form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your client/patient information under Client Information.
2. Answer the Covid-19-related questions under Covid-19 Information.
3. Enter your Esthetician information under Esthetician Information.
4. Sign the form under Signature.
5. Enter your contact information under Phone and Email.
6. Provide the date and time of the form completion under Date and Time.
7. Select your preference under Select One and Select Multiple.
8. Finally, review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Information**: Enter your personal details such as name, address, and contact information. This information will help us understand who you are and provide us with a way to contact you.
- **Covid-19 Information**: Answer the questions related to Covid-19 under this section, providing us with essential details about your Covid-19 status.
- **Esthetician Information**: Enter the details of the Esthetician, if applicable, under this section.
- **Signature**: Sign this form to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
- **Date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
- **Time**: Enter the time you completed this form.
- **Select One**: Choose one of the options to select your preference.
- **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options to select your preference.
- **Selectone 2**: Choose one of the options to select your preference.
- **Selectmultiple 2**: Choose multiple options to select your preference.
