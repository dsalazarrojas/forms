# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Laptop Agreement Form is used to obtain formal agreement from employees before issuing or returning a company-owned laptop. This form ensures that employees understand their obligations and responsibilities related to the use of company property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter the required information.
2. Ensure that you have answered all questions accurately and honestly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your supervisor or the IT department for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form** (`employee_laptop_agreement_form`, note, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you will find the other fields. Please note that this field is not a field to be filled but rather a heading to identify the form.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee-signature`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and understand the agreement, or 'No' if you have not.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager-signature`, select_one, required: false): Please select 'Yes' to confirm that you have obtained your manager's approval, or 'No' if not.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which you received the laptop.
* **Date of Issue** (`date-of-issue`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the laptop was issued to you.
* **Issue Date** (`issue-date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the laptop will be returned or become due for return. 
* **Date of Return** (`date-of-return`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which you expect to return the laptop.

**Tips**:

* Make sure to enter the correct dates accurately.
* Review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your supervisor or the IT department for assistance.
