# Chronic Illness Experience Podcast Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather consent and information from guests who will be participating in a podcast about chronic illnesses. The form is intended to ensure that guests understand the purpose and scope of the podcast, as well as their rights and responsibilities as participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the relevant fields.
2. Select your preferred name for use on the podcast.
3. Provide information about your chronic condition and how long you have been diagnosed.
4. Choose your comfort level with sharing your story.
5. Select the recording date and format (in-person studio, video call remote, phone call, or pre-recorded audio).
6. Choose the expected duration of the episode.
7. Provide consent to be recorded and to have your voice broadcast.
8. Choose how you want to be referred to on the podcast (real name, first name only, pseudonym, or remain anonymous).
9. Select how you want to be compensated for your appearance.
10. Share your social media handles or website or blog link if you want to be tagged.
11. Review and agree to the rights and liabilities section.
12. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for podcast coordination.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for recording day contact.
* **Preferred Name**: Enter the name you would like to be referred to on the podcast.
* **Chronic Condition**: Describe your chronic condition in detail.
* **How Long Diagnosed**: Select how long you have been diagnosed with your chronic condition.
* **Comfort Level Sharing**: Choose how comfortable you are sharing your story.
* **Podcast Name**: Enter the name of the podcast you are participating in.
* **Recording Date**: Select the date of the recording.
* **Recording Format**: Choose the recording format (in-person studio, video call remote, phone call, or pre-recorded audio).
* **Expected Duration**: Select the expected duration of the episode.
* **Consent to Record**: Give consent to be recorded.
* **Consent to Broadcast**: Give consent to have your voice broadcast.
* **Consent to Use Name**: Choose how you want to be referred to on the podcast.
* **Consent to Use Name for Promotion**: Choose how you want to be promoted on social media and online platforms.
* **Compensation**: Choose if you expect any compensation for your appearance.
* **Social Media Handles**: Share your social media handles if you want to be tagged.
* **Website or Blog**: Share your website or blog link if you want to be linked.
* **Rights Acknowledgment**: Review and agree to the rights and liabilities section.
* **Revocation Understanding**: Understand that consent can be revoked at any time.
* **Guest Acknowledgment**: Certify that you are 18 or older.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date and time you are signing this document.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure you understand the information being requested.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the podcast host.
* This form is a requirement for participation in the podcast, and all fields must be completed before your appearance can be confirmed.
