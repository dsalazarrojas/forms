# Teledentistry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from a patient before providing teledentistry services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Ask the patient to review and fill out the form.
3. Ensure the patient's contact information (phone and email) is up to date.
4. Have the patient sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. This is where we will contact you with any updates or concerns.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): List your emergency contact(s) information (name, phone number, address).
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, required/optional): If applicable, list your medical insurance information.
* **Teledentistry Agreement** (`teledentistry_agreement`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the teledentistry agreement by signing below.

Note: The patient is expected to sign and date the form after reviewing and agreeing to the terms.
