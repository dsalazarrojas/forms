# Patient Experience Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Patient Experience Interview Consent Form" is a tool designed to collect important contact information and emergency contact information from patients. This information is crucial for healthcare providers to reach out to patients in case of any medical issues or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
   * Carefully read each field description to understand what is required.
   * Fill in the required fields, making sure to enter accurate and complete information.
   * Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
