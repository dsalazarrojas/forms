# Covid 19 Informed Consent For In Person Physical Therapy Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking in-person physical therapy services. It helps healthcare professionals gather necessary details for patient care and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **General Information**: Fill in the basic contact information, including your name and contact details.
2. Complete the **Medical History** section, where you can provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your treatment.
3. Specify your **Physical Therapy Goals**, such as what you hope to achieve through physical therapy.
4. Provide the **Emergency Contact** information, including the name and contact details of a family member or friend you would like to have notified in case of an emergency.
5. Select from the **Covid 19 Symptoms** options to indicate any Covid-19 symptoms you are experiencing.
6. Answer the **Medical Conditions** question, if applicable.
7. List your **Current Medications**, if any.
8. Describe any **Allergies** you may have.
9. Provide information on **Past Surgeries**, if applicable.
10. Answer the **Medical Tests** question, if applicable.
11. Select your **Covid 19 Vaccination Status**.
12. Fill in the **Physical Therapy Start Date** and **Physical Therapy End Date**, if applicable.
13. Choose your preferred **Physical Therapy Frequency** from the options provided.
14. Provide any additional details about **Other Physical Therapy** in the text field.
15. Grant **Consent to Treatment** and **Consent for Treatment** by signing the relevant fields.
16. Fill in the **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Phone**, and **Emergency Contact Relationship** fields.
17. Provide an **Emergency Contact Email** and **Emergency Contact Alternate Email** (if applicable).
18. Add any **Notes** about your condition or treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: Provide your basic contact information, such as name and contact details.
* **Medical History**: List any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your treatment.
* **Physical Therapy Goals**: Specify what you hope to achieve through physical therapy.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact details of a family member or friend you would like to have notified in case of an emergency.
* **Covid 19 Symptoms**: Select from the options to indicate any Covid-19 symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Medical Conditions**: Answer this question if you have any underlying medical conditions.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: Describe any allergies you may have.
* **Past Surgeries**: Provide information on any past surgeries you have undergone.
* **Medical Tests**: Answer this question if you have had any recent medical tests.
* **Covid 19 Vaccination Status**: Select your vaccination status from the options provided.
* **Physical Therapy Start Date**: Fill in the start date of your physical therapy sessions, if applicable.
* **Physical Therapy End Date**: Fill in the end date of your physical therapy sessions, if applicable.
* **Physical Therapy Frequency**: Choose your preferred frequency of physical therapy sessions from the options provided.
* **Other Physical Therapy**: Provide any additional details about your physical therapy plan.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Grant consent to treatment by signing this field.
* **Consent for Treatment**: Grant consent for treatment by signing this field.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Provide the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Alternate**: Provide an alternate phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Provide the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Alternate Email**: Provide an alternate email address for the emergency contact.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your condition or treatment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is intended to gather necessary information for patient care and treatment planning. Please do not hesitate to ask questions or seek clarification if you are unsure about any field.
