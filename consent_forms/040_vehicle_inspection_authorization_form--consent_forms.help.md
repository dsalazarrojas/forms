# vehicle_inspection_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vehicle Inspection Authorization Form is a document used to gather essential details about the vehicle and its owner to facilitate a smooth and efficient vehicle inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Double-check the information entered before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner Info** (`vehicle_owner_info`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle owner's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.).
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Inspection Type** (`vehicle_inspection_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of inspection (e.g., Safety Inspection, Mechanical Inspection, etc.).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the owner's contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Vehicle Notes** (`vehicle_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the vehicle for the inspector's reference.

## Tips

* Ensure the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you need to make any changes to the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
