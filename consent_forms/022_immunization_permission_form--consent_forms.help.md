# immunization_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The immunization_permission_form is used to obtain the necessary information to grant or deny immunization permission to a parent/guardian on behalf of a child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
3. Enter the school's name in the "School Name" field.
4. Select a valid date for the consent in the "Consent Date" field. The date should be a real date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. If applicable, select the vaccination schedule for the child.
6. Enter any additional notes about the immunization permission in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is granting permission for the child's immunization.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school the child is attending.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional): Enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY for the consent to be granted.
* **Vaccination Schedule** (`vaccination_schedule`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the vaccination schedule for the child. Options include "None" and "Other" (if "Other" is selected, you will be prompted to enter additional information).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the child's immunization permission.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
