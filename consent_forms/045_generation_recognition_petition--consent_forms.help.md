# Generation Recognition Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generation Recognition Petition is a formal document used to demonstrate public acknowledgment and support for a particular cause or issue. It is a crucial tool for gathering endorsements and testimonials from supporters. By completing this form, you will be able to provide your name, email, phone number, and comments in support of the cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the **Form Title** field with a brief title that describes the petition you are supporting.
2. Provide a **Form Description** that outlines the petition's purpose and goals.
3. Fill in your **Supporter Name** to associate your name with your submission.
4. Enter your **Supporter E-mail** address to ensure that we can contact you for further information.
5. Optionally, you can provide your **Supporter Phone** number for additional contact information.
6. In the **Form Signed By** field, select the option that best reflects your stance on the petition's conditions.
7. Finally, provide any **Supporter Comments** you have regarding the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (text, optional): A brief title that describes the petition you are supporting.
* **Form Description** (text, optional): A description of the petition's purpose and goals.
* **Supporter Name** (text, optional): The name of the supporter.
* **Supporter E-mail** (email, optional): The email of the supporter.
* **Supporter Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the supporter.
* **Form Signed By** (select_one, optional): Select the option that best reflects your stance on the petition's conditions.
  * **I agree and accept the conditions**: Indicates that you agree with the conditions outlined in the petition.
  * **I disagree with the conditions**: Indicates that you do not agree with the conditions.
  * **Decline to answer**: Indicates that you choose not to respond to the question.
* **Supporter Comments** (text, optional): Additional comments from the supporter.
