# Seminar Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for seminar participants to provide their consent and share additional information about themselves. It helps seminar organizers to obtain necessary details and ensure that participants understand the seminar's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your correct information.
2. Answer the consent question by selecting either "Yes, I consent to participating in this seminar." or "No, I do not consent to participating in this seminar."
3. If necessary, provide any additional information in the "Additional Info" field.
4. Select one of the options in the "Agreement" field to acknowledge that you have read and understood the seminar's terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`seminar_participation_release_form_participant_info`): This field is for you to enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Seminar Details** (`seminar_participation_release_form_seminar_info`): Please enter a brief description of the seminar you are participating in.
* **Consent** (`seminar_participation_release_form_consent`): This is a multiple-choice question where you can select whether you consent to participating in the seminar. If you answer "Yes," it means you agree to participate, and if you answer "No," it means you decline participation.
* **Additional Info** (`seminar_participation_release_form_additional_info`): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the seminar organizers, you can enter it in this field.
* **Agreement** (`seminar_participation_release_form_agreement`): Select one of the options to acknowledge that you have read and understood the seminar's terms and conditions.
* **Organizer Information** (`seminar_participation_release_form_organizer_info`): This field is not required, but if you want to provide more details about the seminar organizer, you can do so here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you need more space for any field, you can use multiple lines or attach additional pages.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the seminar organizers for assistance.
