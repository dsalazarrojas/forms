# Emergency Evacuation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Evacuation Authorization Form is designed to collect information from employees for emergency evacuation procedures. It is used to gather necessary details for evacuation protocols and emergency response plans in the event of a crisis or emergency situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the form as it will appear on the emergency plan documents.
2. Provide your name as an employee.
3. Select or input the date of the emergency situation.
4. Enter the time of the emergency situation.
5. Specify the location where you will be during the emergency.
6. Enter your employer's name.
7. Enter your employer's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Emergency Evacuation Authorization Form** (title, text, optional)
* This field is a title for the form. It should briefly describe the nature of the emergency.

### **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional)
* This field is for your name, which will help identify you as an employee during the emergency response process.

### **Date** (emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_date, date, optional)
* Enter the date of the emergency situation in mm/dd/yyyy format. This is used to track when the emergency occurred.

### **Time** (emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_time, time, optional)
* Enter the time of the emergency situation in 12-hour format. This helps to track the exact timing of the emergency.

### **Location** (emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_location, text, optional)
* Specify the location where you will be during the emergency, such as a floor number, building, or specific area. This is crucial for evacuation planning and response.

### **Employer's Name** (emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_employer_name, text, optional)
* Enter your employer's name as it will be used to identify your workplace and emergency response protocols.

### **Employer's Address** (emergency_evacuation_authorization_form_employer_address, text, optional)
* Enter your employer's physical address to help with emergency response and evacuation planning.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out the form accurately and truthfully, as the information collected will be used for emergency response and planning purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your emergency response team or supervisor for assistance.
