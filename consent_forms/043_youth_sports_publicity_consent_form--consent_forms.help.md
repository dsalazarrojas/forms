# Youth Sports Publicity Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child's participation in youth sports publicity, which includes photos, videos, and media representation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information: 
   - `parent_name`: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the youth athlete.
   - `email`: Enter your email address.
   - `contact_number`: Enter your contact number.
2. Choose the sport your child participates in:
   - `sport`: Select the sport your child is involved in (e.g., Basketball, Football, etc.).
3. Choose your consent for media use:
   - `photo_release`: Decide if you agree to allow photos of your child to be used in media.
   - `photo_use`: Select which media outlets you agree to use photos of your child in (e.g., Social Media, Website, etc.).
   - `photo_credit`: Enter a credit line for the photos of your child (e.g., "Photo taken by John Doe").
   - `media`: Decide if you agree to allow your child to be featured in media.
   - `media_description`: Enter a description of the media your child will be featured in.
   - `other_media`: If your answer to `media` is yes, select where the media will be featured (e.g., Social Media, Website, etc.).
   - `other_media_description`: Enter a description for other media outlets.
   - `consent2`: Decide if you agree to allow your child's likeness to be used in media (e.g., videos, interviews).
   - `consent3`: Decide if you agree to allow your child's image to be used in media for promotional purposes (e.g., brochures).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **`parent_name`**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the youth athlete.
* **`email`**: Enter your email address.
* **`contact_number`**: Enter your contact number.
* **`sport`**: Select the sport your child participates in.
* **`photo_release`**: Decide if you agree to allow photos of your child to be used in media.
* **`photo_use`**: Select which media outlets you agree to use photos of your child in.
* **`photo_credit`**: Enter a credit line for the photos of your child.
* **`media`**: Decide if you agree to allow your child to be featured in media.
* **`media_description`**: Enter a description of the media your child will be featured in.
* **`other_media`**: If your answer to `media` is yes, select where the media will be featured.
* **`other_media_description`**: Enter a description for other media outlets.
* **`consent2`**: Decide if you agree to allow your child's likeness to be used in media.
* **`consent3`**: Decide if you agree to allow your child's image to be used in media for promotional purposes.
* **`consent`**: Decide if you agree that the youth athlete may be featured in media, photographs, and videos.
