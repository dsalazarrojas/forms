# pop_up_event_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pop-up_event_declaration_form is a form designed to collect information about participants in events. It gathers details about the participant, including their contact information and event details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information in each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information for each field.
3. Review your entries for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of the participant as they will be known at the event.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, **required**): Provide the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number (optional).
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter the participant's date of birth in the standard date format.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address (optional).
* **Event Information** (`event_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the event (optional).

### Event Information

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, **required**): Enter the start date of the event in the standard date format.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, **required**): Enter the end date of the event in the standard date format.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, **required**): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of event (Pop-up event or Non-pop-up event).
* **Participant Declaration** (`participant_declaration`, text, **required**): Enter a brief statement declaring your participation in the event.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the terms and conditions you agree to (I have read and agree to the terms and conditions or Other).

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review your entries for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the provided hints or contact the event organizer for clarification.
