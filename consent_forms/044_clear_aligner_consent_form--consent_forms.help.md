# Clear Aligner Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Clear Aligner Consent Form is a document that ensures patients understand the clear aligner orthodontic treatment process, risks, and expectations. It also protects the provider from potential liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Ensure that you have not filled this form out previously, and if you have, use the submitted data to fill it out automatically.
3. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Patient Full Name** (`patient_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

- **Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

- **Email Address** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be contacted.

- **Phone Number** (`patient_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.

- **Home Address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.

- **Orthodontist/Dentist Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your treatment provider.

- **Practice Name** (`practice_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the dental/orthodontic practice.

- **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your initial consultation.

- **Clear Aligner Product Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of clear aligner system you would like to use. Options include Brand A Clear Aligners, Brand B Clear Aligners, Brand C Clear Aligners, Generic Clear Aligners, or Not Yet Determined.

- **Estimated Treatment Duration** (`estimated_duration`, text, required): Enter the expected length of treatment.

- **Primary Treatment Goal** (`treatment_goal`, text, required): Specify the main objective of your treatment.

- **I Understand the Clear Aligner Treatment Process** (`understand_process`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you understand the treatment process.

- **Clear Aligners Must Be Worn 20-22 Hours Daily for Optimal Results** (`aligner_compliance`, select_one, required): Acknowledge that you understand the wearing requirements for clear aligners.

- **I Understand the Maintenance and Care Requirements** (`maintenance_understanding`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you understand how to properly care for and maintain your clear aligners.

- **Possible Risks and Side Effects** (`possible_side_effects`, select_multiple, required): Select all potential risks and side effects that you are aware of.

- **I Have Been Informed of Potential Risks Associated with Clear Aligner Treatment** (`understand_risks`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you have been informed of potential risks.

- **I Understand Who to Contact in Case of Emergency or Complications** (`emergency_contact`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you understand who to contact in case of emergencies or complications.

- **Treatment Cost Has Been Discussed and Explained** (`treatment_cost_discussed`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that the treatment cost has been discussed and explained.

- **Payment Plan or Financing Terms Have Been Reviewed** (`payment_plan_accepted`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you have reviewed the payment plan or financing terms.

- **I Consent to Clear Aligner Orthodontic Treatment** (`consent_treatment`, select_one, required): Please indicate that you consent to clear aligner orthodontic treatment.

- **I Acknowledge I Have Received and Read All Treatment Information** (`acknowledge_information`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you have received and read all treatment information.

- **All My Questions Have Been Answered to My Satisfaction** (`questions_answered`, select_one, required): Please confirm that all your questions have been answered to your satisfaction.

- **I Voluntarily Enter into This Treatment with Clear Understanding of Risks** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required): Please indicate that you understand the risks and enter into this treatment voluntarily.

- **I Agree to Release and Hold Harmless the Provider from Any Complications or Dissatisfaction** (`liability_release`, select_one, required): Please agree to release and hold harmless the provider from any complications or dissatisfaction.

- **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you signed this consent form.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Review each section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any information.
- Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
