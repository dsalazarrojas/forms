# digital_media_content_creator_award_nomination - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for nominating a digital media content creator for an award. The collected information will be used to evaluate their eligibility for the award.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the first name of the digital media content creator being nominated.
2. Provide the last name of the digital media content creator being nominated.
3. Enter the email address of the digital media content creator.
4. Enter the phone number of the digital media content creator.
5. Select the category that best describes the work of the digital media content creator.
6. Optionally, provide a brief description of the work of the digital media content creator.
7. Enter the date when the nomination is being made.
8. Enter the time when the nomination is being made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter the first name of the digital media content creator being nominated.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter the last name of the digital media content creator being nominated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter the email address of the digital media content creator. This will be used as contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the digital media content creator.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category that best describes the work of the digital media content creator from the list:
	+ Award Forms
	+ Consent Forms
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the work of the digital media content creator. This is an optional field.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`, date, required): Please enter the date when the nomination is being made.
* **Nomination Time** (`nomination_time`, time, required): Please enter the time when the nomination is being made.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the digital media content creator.
* Double-check the category selection to ensure it is correct.
* The nomination date and time should be in the correct format.
* If you have any questions or need help with the nomination process, feel free to contact us.
