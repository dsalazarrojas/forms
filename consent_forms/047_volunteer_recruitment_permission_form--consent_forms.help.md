# Volunteer Recruitment Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Recruitment Permission Form is a crucial document that collects information from potential volunteers about their availability, role, and other relevant details. This information will be used to match volunteers with suitable roles and shifts within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and details about your availability and preferences.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing all requested information.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): This field is an optional field where you can provide the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select whether you are a Volunteer or Non-Volunteer. Please choose the option that best describes your role.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your role or the position you are applying for.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your email address or phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name of an emergency contact person, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person you provided in the previous field.
* **Relation to Volunteer** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Describe your relation to the emergency contact person, if applicable.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, optional): List your availability for volunteering, including your preferred days and times.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to end volunteering.
* **Shift Preferences** (`shift_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your shift preferences, such as morning, afternoon, or evening shifts.
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, optional): Enter your preferred location for volunteering.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, text, optional): Provide your available work hours per week.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you work or volunteer.
* **Shift** (`shift`, text, optional): Specify the shift you are applying for.
* **Work Hours/Week** (`work_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, text, optional): Describe your time commitment level, such as full-time, part-time, or casual.
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, text, optional): Enter your volunteer position or role.
* **Work Location 2** (`work_location_2`, text, optional): Enter a secondary work location, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Provide additional emergency contact information, if needed.
* **Shift Type** (`shift_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether you are available for full-time, part-time, or casual shifts.
* **Volunteer Type 2** (`volunteer_type_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you are a yes or no for this role.
* **Emergency Contact Relation 2** (`emergency_contact_relation_2`, text, optional): Describe your relation to the emergency contact person 2.
* **Assigned** (`assigned`, text, optional): Check if you have been assigned to a volunteer position.

Note: The fields marked as `optional` can be skipped if you do not have the necessary information or preferences.
