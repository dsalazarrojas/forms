# leadership_assessment_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant consent for a leadership assessment or evaluation. It captures essential information about the participant, including their contact details and role, to facilitate communication and follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your Participant Name, Organization Name, and Contact Name.
2. Enter your Contact Email and Contact Phone number to enable easy communication.
3. Provide your Participant Title and Participant Role to help us understand your position and responsibilities.
4. Check the Participant Agreement checkbox to confirm your consent to participate in the assessment process.
5. Once you have filled all the required fields, click the submission button to send your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
- **Organization Name** (text): Enter the name of the organization you belong to or work with.
- **Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the contact person for you in this assessment process.
- **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address where we can reach you for further communication.
- **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
- **Participant Title** (text): Enter your official title in the organization (e.g. Manager, Team Lead, etc.).
- **Participant Role** (text): Describe your role and responsibilities within the organization.
- **Participant Agreement** (checkbox): Check this box to confirm your agreement to participate in the assessment process.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough when filling the form.
- Review your submission before clicking the submission button.
- In case of any issues or questions, contact the provided contact email or phone number.
