# Advanced Facial Treatments And Chemical Peels Client Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for clients to provide consent for advanced facial treatments and chemical peels. The form aims to gather information about the client's medical history, skin concerns, and consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, **Contact Number**, and **Email Address**.
2. Select all applicable skin concerns from the list (e.g. Acne, Hyperpigmentation).
3. Answer whether you have a **History of Cold Sores** (True or False).
4. Answer whether you are **Currently Using Retinoids or Accutane** (True or False).
5. List any **Known Allergies to Skincare Ingredients** (if applicable).
6. Select the **Treatment Type** being performed (e.g. Chemical Peel, Microdermabrasion).
7. Read and agree with the **Consent Statement**, which informs you of potential side effects.
8. Choose whether you agree to the **Informed Consent Agreement**, which gives permission for the clinician to perform the treatment.
9. Sign your name in the **Client Signature** field.
10. Date the form in the **Consent Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Current Skin Concerns** (`current_skin_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select all skin concerns that apply (e.g. Acne, Hyperpigmentation).
* **History of Cold Sores** (`history_of_cold_sores`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have a history of cold sores (True or False).
* **Currently Using Retinoids or Accutane** (`currently_using_retinoids_or_accutane`, select_one, required): Answer whether you are currently using retinoids or accutane (True or False).
* **Known Allergies to Skincare Ingredients** (`known_allergies_to_skincare_ingredients`, text, required): List any known allergies to skincare ingredients (e.g. aspirin, salicylic acid).
* **Treatment Type** (`treatment_type`, select_one, required): Select the service being performed (e.g. Chemical Peel, Microdermabrasion).
* **Consent Statement** (`consent_statement`, note, required): Read and agree with the potential side effects of the treatment.
* **Informed Consent Agreement** (`informed_consent_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you agree to the treatment.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, required): Sign your name.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Date the form.
