# Fashion Week Backstage Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Fashion Week Backstage Release Form" is a document that captures your consent and details regarding your participation in a fashion event. It is essential to review and understand the information below to ensure you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Ensure you answer each question accurately and truthfully.
3. Once you have completed the form, review the information for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fashion Week Backstage Release Form** (`text`, required): This is the form title and identifier. Please leave this field blank.
* **participant_details** (`text`, required): This field is for you to provide any additional details about your participation in the event. Please be brief but include any relevant information.
* **participant_name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **participant_email** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **participant_phone** (`text`, optional): If you wish to be contacted, enter your phone number. This is not required.
* **show_day** (`select_one`, optional): Indicate if you will be participating on a particular day. Options: Yes, No.
* **fashion_week_backstage_area** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any areas you will be involved in during the event. Options: Yes.
* **participantistency** (`date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date you will be participating in the event.
* **participant_time** (`time`, optional): If applicable, enter the time you will be participating in the event.
* **consent_given** (`text`, optional): Check the box if you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **comments** (`note`, optional): If you have any comments or questions, please include them here.
* **participant_photo_credit** (`text`, optional): If you have provided a photo, enter your name as it appears on the photo credit.
* **participant_photo_credit_url** (`text`, optional): If you have provided a photo, enter the URL where the photo can be found.
* **resource** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select any resources you are affiliated with or represent during the event. Options: resource 1, resource 2, resource 3.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure you answer all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* This form is for participants who have been invited to the event. If you are not a participant, please do not submit this form.
