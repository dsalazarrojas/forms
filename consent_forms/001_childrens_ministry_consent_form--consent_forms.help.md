# Childrens Ministry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians to obtain their consent for their child's participation in the Children's Ministry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Parent/Guardian Information section with your contact details.
2. Provide your child's information in the Child Information section.
3. Review and agree to the Participation Consents section.
4. Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Full Name (`parent_full_name`):** Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address (`parent_email`):** We will use this for session invitations and other communications.
* **Phone Number (`parent_phone`):** Your phone number will be used to contact you in case of an emergency or for session reminders.
* **Child Name (`child_name`):** Enter your child's name.
* **Child Age (`child_age`):** Enter your child's age.
* **Online Session Consent (`zoom_session_consent`):** Select "I give my consent" if you agree to your child participating in the online CCN Kids Zoom sessions.
* **Video Contribution Consent (`video_content_consent`):** Select "I give my consent" if you agree to your child contributing content to pre-recorded videos.
* **Media Release Terms (`media_release_notes`):** Review the terms for video sharing.
* **Digital Signature (`digital_signature`):** Type your full name to sign this form.
* **Date of Consent (`consent_date`):** This is automatically populated based on the date you submit this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Children's Ministry.
