# Field Trip Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Waiver Form is a document that collects essential information from participants in a field trip. It is a crucial document that ensures participants are aware of and agree to the trip's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permission from your guardian or parent to participate in the trip.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required):
	+ This section is for providing basic details about yourself, such as your name, age, and contact information.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, not required):
	+ If you want to provide additional information about the trip, you can do so in this section.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required):
	+ This is where you provide your electronic or written signature, indicating that you have read and agreed to the trip's terms and conditions.
* **Guardian Contact** (`guardian_contact`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is the contact information for your guardian or parent. Only fill this out if your guardian or parent needs to be contacted.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_multiple`, required):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your consent.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the organization related to the trip you are participating in.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `note`, required):
	+ Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the trip.
* **Trip Coordinator** (`trip_coordinator`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ This is the contact information for the trip organizer.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required):
	+ If you have any additional comments or notes about the trip, you can put them here.
* **Trip Coordinator Phone** (`trip_coordinator_phone`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is the phone number for the trip organizer.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is your phone number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, not required):
	+ This is your email address.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is your name.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, `date`, required):
	+ Enter the date of the trip.
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, `text`, required):
	+ Provide the location of the trip.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter the duration of the trip in hours.
* **Participant Info 2** (`participant_info_2`, `note`, not required):
	+ Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to the trip.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter your address.
* **Participant Relationship** (`participant_relationship`, `text`, not required):
	+ This is the relationship between you and the trip organizer.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required):
	+ Enter your age.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ Choose your gender.
* **Participant School** (`participant_school`, `select_one`, required):
	+ Choose the school you attend.
* **Participant Permission** (`participant_permission`, `select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your permission.
* **Participant Employment** (`participant_employment`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ This is your employment status.
* **Participant Medical Info** (`participant_medical_info`, `note`, required):
	+ Provide any medical information that is relevant to the trip.
* **Participant Employment** (`participant_employment`, `select_one`, not required):
	+ This is your employment status.
