# Radiology Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radiology Consent Form is a critical document that confirms a patient's understanding and agreement to undergo a radiology procedure. It ensures that the patient is aware of the risks and benefits associated with the procedure and provides informed consent before the procedure is conducted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2.  Provide the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3.  Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4.  Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Describe the patient's medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
6.  Read and understand the "Risk Benefit Disclosure" section, which outlines the potential risks and benefits of the procedure.
7.  Select the status of your informed consent in the "Informed Consent" field.
8.  Choose the type of procedure being conducted in the "Procedure Details" field.
9.  Enter the facility's name in the "Facility Name" field.
10. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
11. Enter the doctor's title in the "Doctor Title" field.
12. Select the status of the patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
13. Select the status of the doctor's signature in the "Doctor Signature" field.
14. Select the status of the facility's signature in the "Facility Signature" field.
15. Enter the date and time the procedure was conducted in the "Date of Procedure Conducted" and "Time of Procedure Conducted" fields.
16. Enter the type of procedure conducted in the "Procedure Type" field.
17. Enter any comments from the doctor in the "Doctor Comments" field.
18. Enter any comments from the facility in the "Facility Comments" field.
19. Enter any procedure-related comments in the "Procedure Comments" field.
