# Theater Performance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Performance Consent Form is a critical document that protects the rights and safety of participants in a theater performance. By completing this form, participants provide essential information about themselves and their involvement in the performance, which helps ensure their safety and well-being during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Information section with your details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.
2. In the Guardian Information section, provide the contact details of your legal guardian or emergency contact person.
3. In the Performance Details section, provide information about the performance, including the schedule and location.
4. In the Participant Declaration section, confirm that you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of your participation.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This section gathers personal details about you, the participant. Please ensure that you fill out this section accurately and completely.
* **Guardian Information**: If you are under the age of 18 or have a guardian, provide their contact details here. This is for their information and emergency contact purposes.
* **Performance Details**: Provide the details about the performance, including the schedule and location. This will help the organizers ensure your safety and well-being during the event.
* **Emergency Contact**: In case of an emergency, please provide the contact details of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Please specify your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Participant Declaration**: This section confirms that you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of your participation. Please read this section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your consent.
* **Guardian Declaration**: If you have a guardian, please sign and date this section to confirm their consent.
* **Guardian Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your guardian's consent.
* **Emergency Contact Details**: This section gathers additional details about your emergency contact.
* **Participant Permission**: This section is for the participant to confirm their permission for the performance.
* **Participant Permission Date**: Record the date of the participant's permission.
* **Performance Schedule**: Provide the schedule of the performance, including start and end times.
* **Performance Location**: Provide the location of the performance.
* **Performance Duration**: Provide the duration of the performance.
* **Participant Role**: Specify your role in the performance (e.g., actor, dancer, etc.).
* **Participant Contact**: Provide your contact information for communication purposes during the event.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your safety and well-being during the performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask the organizers or your guardian for clarification.
* This form should be signed and dated by both the participant and their guardian (if applicable) to confirm consent.
