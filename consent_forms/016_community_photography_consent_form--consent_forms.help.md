# Community Photography Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals for photography and other media recording purposes. It helps ensure that participants understand what kind of photos, videos, and recordings will be taken and how they will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the content.
2. Fill out all the required fields with accurate information.
3. Choose the consent options that apply to your circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person being photographed.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the person being photographed.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the person being photographed.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person being photographed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number where the person being photographed can be contacted.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the street address of the person being photographed.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the person being photographed resides.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where the person being photographed resides.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the ZIP code of the person being photographed.
* **Event or Location Details** (`event_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about the event or location where photography will occur.
* **Event or Location Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event or location where photography will occur.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or location where photography will occur.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the full address of the event or location where photography will occur.
* **Organization Requesting Consent** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization requesting consent.
* **Consent Options** (`consent_options`, note, optional): Choose from the list of options what you allow regarding photography, video, and audio recording.
* **I Consent to Photography** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you give permission for still photography. Options: Yes - I Give Permission, No - I Do Not Consent.
* **I Consent to Video Recording** (`video_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you give permission for video recording. Options: Yes - I Give Permission, No - I Do Not Consent.
* **I Consent to Audio Recording** (`audio_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you give permission for audio recording. Options: Yes - I Give Permission, No - I Do Not Consent.
* **I Consent to Digital Media Use** (`digital_media_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you give permission for digital media use. Options: Yes - I Give Permission, No - I Do Not Consent.
* **Approved Purposes** (`usage_purposes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the purposes that apply for the consent.
* **May We Use Your Name and Likeness** (`name_likeness`, select_one, required): Choose whether your name and likeness can be used in conjunction with images. Options: True, False.
* **Consent Duration** (`time_limit`, select_one, required): Choose how long the consent lasts. Options: One Time Use Only, One Year, Three Years, Five Years, Indefinitely.
* **Any Restrictions or Conditions** (`restrictions`, text, optional): Specify any limitations or conditions regarding the consent.
* **Parent or Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`, note, optional): If the participant is under 18, provide information about the parent or guardian.
* **Is Participant Under 18** (`is_minor`, select_one, required): Choose whether the participant is under 18. Options: True, False.
* **Guardian First Name** (`guardian_first_name`, text, optional): If the participant is under 18, enter the first name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Last Name** (`guardian_last_name`, text, optional): If the participant is under 18, enter the last name of the guardian.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, optional): If the participant is under 18, choose the relationship between the guardian and the participant. Options: Parent, Legal Guardian, Other.
* **Date of Consent** (`guardian_signature_date`, date, required): If the participant is under 18, enter the date of consent signed by the guardian.
