# Drone Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The drone permission form is designed to collect information about the event or activity where a drone will be used, including details about the location, pilot, and drone. This information is necessary for safety and regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event or activity where the drone will be used.
2. Provide the location where the event or activity will take place, including any relevant notes.
3. Enter the date and start and end times of the event or activity.
4. Enter the details of the pilot, including their name and license number.
5. Enter the details of the drone, including its name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (id: 1, required: false): Enter any additional information about the event or activity. This field is optional and can be used to add any relevant details or notes.
* **Drone Location** (id: 2, required: false): Enter the exact location where the event or activity will take place.
* **Location Address** (id: 3, required: false): Enter the address of the location where the event or activity will take place.
* **Location Notes** (id: 4, required: false): Enter any additional notes or details about the location.
* **Event Name** (id: 5, required: false): Enter the name of the event or activity.
* **Event Description** (id: 6, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event or activity.
* **Date of Event** (id: 7, required: false): Enter the date of the event or activity.
* **Start Time of Event** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the start time of the event or activity.
* **End Time of Event** (id: 9, required: false): Enter the end time of the event or activity.
* **Pilot Details** (id: 10, required: false): Enter any additional details about the pilot, such as their experience or qualifications.
* **Pilot Name** (id: 11, required: false): Enter the name of the pilot.
* **Pilot License Number** (id: 12, required: false): Enter the pilot's license number.
* **Drone Details** (id: 13, required: false): Enter any additional details about the drone, such as its model or specifications.
* **Drone Name** (id: 14, required: false): Enter the name of the drone.
