# Broker Of Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Broker Of Record Form is a document used to change the broker of record for a policy. This form is completed by the policyholder to provide the necessary information for the change of record, including the new broker's details and the effective date of the change.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the policyholder's personal information, including their full name, date of birth, and policy number.
2. Select the type of insurance policy they are changing.
3. Provide the new broker's information, including their name, company, address, phone number, and email address.
4. Check the boxes to authorize the release of information to the new broker and to consent to cancel any existing broker agreement.
5. Sign and date the form, and if applicable, have a witness or notary sign and date as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policyholder Full Name**: Enter the full legal name of the policyholder.
* **Policyholder Date of Birth**: Enter the policyholder's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number associated with the policy.
* **Policy Type**: Select the type of insurance policy being changed.
* **Current Broker Name**: Enter the name of the current broker, if applicable.
* **Current Broker Company**: Enter the name of the current broker's company, if applicable.
* **Current Broker Phone**: Enter the phone number of the current broker, if applicable.
* **New Broker Full Name**: Enter the full legal name of the new broker.
* **New Broker License Number**: Enter the license number of the new broker.
* **New Broker Company**: Enter the name of the new broker's company.
* **New Broker Business Address**: Enter the business address of the new broker.
* **New Broker Phone**: Enter the phone number of the new broker.
* **New Broker Email**: Enter the email address of the new broker.
* **Authorization Statement**: Check the box to authorize the release of information to the new broker.
* **Consent to Cancel**: Check the box to consent to cancel any existing broker agreement.
* **I Understand**: Check the box to acknowledge that you understand the authorization.
* **Policyholder Signature**: Sign your full legal name.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign the witness's name if applicable.
* **Notary Name**: Enter the name of the notary, if applicable.
* **Notary Commission Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the notary's commission, if applicable.
* **Notary Signature**: Sign the notary's name and affix their seal, if applicable.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please contact your broker or agent for assistance.
* This form is only for changing the broker of record, if you need to make any other changes to your policy, you may need to complete additional forms.
