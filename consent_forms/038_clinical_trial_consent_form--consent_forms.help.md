# Clinical Trial Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from participants in a clinical trial. It is intended to be completed by the participant or their legal representative to ensure that they understand the study's purpose, risks, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Enter the title of the clinical study in the "Clinical Study Title" field.
3. Enter the name of the lead researcher in the "Lead Researcher Name" field.
4. Read and understand the study's information sheet, and indicate your acknowledgement by selecting "I have read and understood the study information sheet".
5. Confirm that you have had the opportunity to ask questions about the study by selecting "I have had the opportunity to ask questions about the study".
6. Confirm that your participation is voluntary by selecting "I understand that my participation is voluntary".
7. Confirm that your data will be kept confidential and anonymized by selecting "I understand that my data will be kept confidential and anonymized".
8. Confirm that you understand the potential risks and benefits of the study by selecting "I understand the potential risks and benefits of the study".
9. If applicable, consent to the collection of biological samples by selecting "I consent to the collection of blood samples as described".
10. If applicable, consent to the storage of your biological samples for future research by selecting "I consent to the storage of my samples for future research".
11. Indicate if you wish to receive a summary of the study results by selecting "I wish to receive a summary of the study results when completed".
12. Sign and date the form by providing your digital signature and the date of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Clinical Study Title** (`study_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the study you are participating in.
* **Lead Researcher Name** (`lead_researcher`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the lead researcher conducting the study.
* **-- Consent Acknowledgements --** (`section_acknowledgements`, `note`, `false`): This section acknowledges that you have received and understood the study's information sheet.
*  I have read and understood the study information sheet: Indicate your understanding of the study's information sheet.
* **I have had the opportunity to ask questions about the study** (`opportunity_to_ask_questions`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm that you have had the opportunity to ask questions about the study.
* **I understand that my participation is voluntary** (`voluntary_participation_ack`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm that your participation is voluntary.
* **I understand that my data will be kept confidential and anonymized** (`data_confidentiality_ack`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm that you understand how your data will be handled.
* **I understand the potential risks and benefits of the study** (`risks_benefits_ack`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm that you understand the potential risks and benefits of the study.
* **-- Biological Samples --** (`section_samples`, `note`, `false`): This section is for consent related to biological sample collection.
* **I consent to the collection of blood samples as described** (`sample_collection_consent`, `select_one`, `false`): Confirm that you consent to the collection of blood samples as described.
* **I consent to the storage of my samples for future research** (`sample_storage_consent`, `select_one`, `false`): Confirm that you consent to the storage of your samples for future research.
* **-- Communication --** (`section_communication`, `note`, `false`): This section is for consent related to communication.
* **I wish to receive a summary of the study results when completed** (`result_summary_request`, `select_one`, `false`): Indicate if you wish to receive a summary of the study results.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, `date`, `true`): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Participant Digital Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, `true`): Sign the form to confirm your consent.

Please note that this help guide is intended to assist in the completion of the form, and it is the participant's or their legal representative's responsibility to ensure that they understand the study's information sheet and the form's content before signing. If any doubts or questions arise during the completion of this form, please do not hesitate to contact the study team.
