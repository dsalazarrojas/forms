# Theater Cast Media Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from the legal guardian or parent of a minor for the use of their images or videos captured during a performance or event at a school or theater.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of media that was captured (e.g. Photo, Video, Audio).
2. Indicate whether consent has been given for the media capture (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
3. If "No" was selected in the previous step, provide information about the guardian or parent's contact information (e.g. guardian_info).
4. If necessary, provide any additional performer or parent information (e.g. performer_info).
5. Enter the date of birth of the minor.
6. Enter the date and time of media capture (e.g. 01/01/2022 12:00 PM).
7. Provide a description of the media capture.
8. Indicate whether the media can be shared (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
9. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select media type** (`media_type`): Please select the type of media that was captured (e.g. Photo, Video, Audio).
* **Consent given** (`consent_given`): Indicate whether consent has been given for the media capture. Select "Yes" if consent has been given, and "No" if it has not. If "No" was selected, please proceed to the next step.
* **Guardian information** (`guardian_info`): If consent was not given, please provide the contact information of the guardian or parent (e.g. name, relationship, email, phone number).
* **Performer information** (`performer_info`): If necessary, provide any additional information about the performer or minor (e.g. name, school affiliation).
* **Parent information** (`parent_info`): If necessary, provide any additional information about the parent or guardian (e.g. name, relationship).
* **School information** (`school_info`): If necessary, provide any additional information about the school or event (e.g. name, date).
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the date of birth of the minor (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of media capture** (`date_of_media_capture`): Enter the date and time of media capture (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY 12:00 PM).
* **Time of media capture** (`time_of_media_capture`): Enter the time of media capture (e.g. 12:00 PM).
* **Location of media capture** (`location_of_media_capture`): Provide a description of the location where the media was captured (e.g. school auditorium).
* **Description** (`description`): Describe the media capture (e.g. a photo of John Doe performing in the school play).
* **Permission to share** (`permission_to_share`): Indicate whether the media can be shared (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
