# Customer Data Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to customer data by a business representative. It is essential to understand that completing this form does not automatically grant access to customer data. The customer data access process involves multiple steps, including approval from the business. This form is used to initiate the request, not to grant access. Please review the following instructions carefully to ensure you are using the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are requesting access to the customer's data.** If you select "Yes", proceed to the next section to provide more details about your request.
2. **Enter the customer's email address**, if you wish to request access to their data.
3. **Enter your phone number**, if you wish to receive updates and notifications about the status of your data access request.
4. **Enter the customer's date of birth**, if you are requesting access to data related to their age.
5. **Select the type of data you are requesting access to.** This can be "Request personal data", "Request data held on our systems", or "Request data held by third party".
6. **Describe the frequency of your data access request.** This can be a one-time request or an ongoing request.
7. **Describe the specific service you are requesting.** This can include information about the type of data you are seeking access to and the purpose of the request.
8. **Enter your signature** by typing it in the provided text field. This confirms that you have read and understood the terms of the form.
9. **Enter the business representative's signature** by typing it in the provided text field. This confirms that the business has reviewed and approved the request.
10. **Provide any additional notes or comments** about the data access request.
11. **Upload an image of your signature** if required by the business.
12. **Upload an image of the business representative's signature** if required by the business.
13. **Enter the date and time you are requesting access to the customer's data.**
14. **Enter the date and time of the agreement to access the customer's data.**

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Data Request**: 
  **customer_data_request** (Select "Yes" or "No"): 
    - This field is used to indicate whether you are requesting access to the customer's data.
* **Email Address**: 
  **email_address** (Email): 
    - Enter the email address of the customer you are requesting data access for.
* **Phone Number**: 
  **phone_number** (Text):

* **Date of Birth**: 
  **date_of_birth** (Date): 
    - Enter the customer's date of birth if you are requesting access to data related to their age.
* **Data Request Type**:

* **Customer Service Request**: 
  **customer_service_request** (Text): 
    - Describe the specific service you are requesting. This can include information about the type of data you are seeking access to and the purpose of the request.
* **Customer Signature**: 
  **customer_signature** (Text): 
    - Enter your signature by typing it in the provided text field. This confirms that you have read and understood the terms of the form.
* **Business Signature**: 
  **business_signature** (Text): 
    - Enter the business representative's signature by typing it in the provided text field. This confirms that the business has reviewed and approved the request.
* **Next Steps**: 
  **next_steps** (Text): 
    - Provide any additional notes or comments about the data access request.
* **Data Access Request Notes**: 
  **data_access_request_notes** (Note): 
    - Provide any additional notes or comments about the data access request.
* **Customer Signature Image**: 
  **customer_signature_image** (Text):

## Tips
This form is a crucial part of the data access process. Please review the form carefully to ensure you are using it correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the business representative responsible for data access.
