# Waiver Of Lien Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver Of Lien Form is a document that waives certain rights or claims related to a property or vehicle. It's typically used by landlords or lenders to give up their lien on a property or vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the renter (person or entity waiving the lien) in the following fields:
	* Renter Name: Enter the name of the person or entity waiving the lien.
	* Renter Address: Enter the address of the person or entity waiving the lien.
	* Renter City: Enter the city where the renter is located.
	* Renter State: Enter the state where the renter is located.
	* Renter Zip: Enter the zip code of the renter's location.
2. Fill in the relevant details for the property or vehicle:
	* Date of Lien: Enter the date when the lien was created.
	* Property Type: Enter the type of property or vehicle being waived.
	* Property Address: Enter the address of the property or vehicle being waived.
	* Property Location: Enter the location of the property or vehicle being waived.
3. Fill in the vehicle details (if applicable):
	* Vehicle Make: Enter the make of the vehicle being waived.
	* Vehicle Model: Enter the model of the vehicle being waived.
	* Vehicle Year: Enter the year of the vehicle being waived.
	* Vehicle License: Enter the license plate number of the vehicle being waived.
4. Sign the form to confirm:
	* Renter Authorized Signer: Enter the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the renter.
	* Renter Signature: Sign with a handwritten signature to confirm.
	* Authorized Signer: Enter the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the authorized signer.
	* Authorized Signer Signature: Sign with a handwritten signature to confirm.
	* Lien Date: Enter the date when the lien is being waived.
	* Lien Description: Enter a brief description of the lien being waived.
	* Signature Date: Enter the date when the waiver is being signed.
	* Signature Location: Enter the location where the waiver is being signed.

* **Date of Lien** (`date_of_lien`): Enter the date when the lien was created.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Enter the type of property or vehicle being waived.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter the address of the property or vehicle being waived.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`): Enter the location of the property or vehicle being waived.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of the vehicle being waived.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle being waived.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle being waived.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle being waived.
* **Renter Authorized Signer** (`renter_authorized_signer`): Enter the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the renter.
* **Renter Signature** (`renter_signature`): Sign with a handwritten signature to confirm.
* **Authorized Signer** (`authorized_signer`): Enter the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the authorized signer.
* **Authorized Signer Signature** (`authorized_signer_signature`): Sign with a handwritten signature to confirm.
* **Lien Date** (`lien_date`): Enter the date when the lien is being waived.
* **Lien Description** (`lien_description`): Enter a brief description of the lien being waived.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date when the waiver is being signed.
* **Signature Location** (`signature_location`): Enter the location where the waiver is being signed.
* **Renter Notes** (`renter_notes`): Enter any additional information or notes from the renter.
