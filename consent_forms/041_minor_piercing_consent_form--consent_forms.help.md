# Minor Piercing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Piercing Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from minors for piercing procedures, outlining their consent and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and ensure it matches your desired format.
2. Select the correct category for the form.
3. If necessary, add a description to provide additional context.
4. Enter the version number and author of the form.
5. Choose the date of consent.
6. Sign the form to indicate acceptance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Minor Piercing Consent Form** (1, text, required): This is the title of the form. Please fill in your desired title.
* **Category** (2, select_one, required): Choose the category for this form from the provided options. This will help categorize the form for future reference.
* **Description** (3, text, optional): If necessary, provide a brief description of the piercing procedure to clarify the purpose of the consent form.
* **Version** (4, text, optional): Enter the version number of the form to track updates and changes.
* **Author** (5, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Date** (6, date, required): Select the date on which the consent was given.
* **Signature** (7, date, required): Sign the form to indicate acceptance of the consent form.
