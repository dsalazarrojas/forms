# Case Study Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Study Release Form is used to obtain consent and authorization from participants to publish their case study. It ensures that participants' rights and privacy are respected and that they are aware of how their case study will be used and distributed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer all questions truthfully.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the organization or institution responsible for publishing the case study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Release Authorization** (`release_date`): Enter the date you are giving permission for the case study to be published.
* **Case Study Title** (`case_study_title`): Enter the title of the case study.
* **Organization or Institution Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of the entity seeking permission to publish.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`participant_contact`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Type of Publication** (`publication_type`): Select the type of publication you are giving permission for (e.g., Academic Journal Article, Case Study Report, etc.).
* **Description of the Case Study** (`publication_description`): Describe what the case study is about and its purpose.
* **Intended Audience or Publication Venue** (`target_audience`): Describe who will read or access this publication.
* **Will Identifying Information Be Included?** (`identifying_information`): Select whether identifying information will be included or not.
* **Which Personal Details May Be Included?** (`personal_details_included`): Select all personal details that you are willing to be included.
* **How Will Sensitive Information Be Handled?** (`sensitive_information`): Describe how sensitive information will be handled.
* **I Grant Permission to Publish This Case Study** (`grant_permission`): Select whether you grant permission or not.
* **Permitted Uses of the Case Study** (`usage_rights`): Select all permitted uses of the case study.
* **May Organization Use This Case in Future Publications?** (`future_permissions`): Select whether the organization can use the case study in future publications.
* **May I Review the Published Case Before Release?** (`review_opportunity`): Select whether you want to review the published case before release.
* **Can This Permission Be Withdrawn Later?** (`withdrawal_clause`): Select whether the permission can be withdrawn later.
* **Additional Terms or Conditions** (`additional_terms`): Enter any special requests or requirements.
