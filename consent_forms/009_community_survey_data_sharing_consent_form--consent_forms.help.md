# Community Survey Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide their consent for sharing their survey responses with various entities, including academic researchers, government agencies, nonprofit organizations, and others. It ensures that participants understand how their data will be used and that their rights and privacy are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number (if you have one).
2. Select the survey you participated in from the list.
3. Choose the date you completed the survey.
4. Read the information on data sharing purposes and understand that your responses may be shared for research, policy development, community planning, grant applications, program evaluation, public reports, and statistical analysis.
5. Confirm that you understand that your personal information will be removed from shared data and that your responses will be combined with others in reports.
6. Select the level of data sharing you consent to: full consent, limited consent, minimal consent, or no consent.
7. Confirm that you consent to sharing your data with academic researchers, government agencies, nonprofit organizations, and including it in public reports.
8. Confirm that you understand your participation is voluntary and that you can withdraw consent at any time.
9. Confirm that you understand you will not be compensated for data sharing.
10. If needed, contact us with any questions about data sharing.
11. Sign the form electronically to confirm your understanding and consent.
12. Date and time of consent.
13. If applicable, provide the name of the person who witnessed your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes, if you have one.
* **Survey Name**: Select the survey you participated in from the list.
* **Date Completed**: Choose the date you completed the survey.
* **Data Sharing Purposes**: Read and understand the information on how your responses may be used for research, policy development, community planning, grant applications, program evaluation, public reports, and statistical analysis.
* **Consent Level**: Select the level of data sharing you consent to: full, limited, minimal, or no consent.
* **Data Sharing Entities**: Confirm that you consent to sharing your data with academic researchers, government agencies, nonprofit organizations, and including it in public reports.
* **Voluntary Participation**: Understand that your participation is voluntary and that you can withdraw consent at any time.
* **No Compensation**: Understand that you will not be compensated for data sharing.
* **Contact Us**: If you have any questions about data sharing, contact us.
* **Explain Consent Process**: Confirm that someone explained the consent process to you.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign the form electronically to confirm your understanding and consent.
* **Date and Time of Consent**: Enter the date and time of consent.
* **Witness Name**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who witnessed your consent.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time of consent.

This guide will help you complete the Community Survey Data Sharing Consent Form efficiently and effectively. If you have any questions or doubts, please contact us.
