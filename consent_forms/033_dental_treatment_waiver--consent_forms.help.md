# Dental Treatment Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain the patient's consent for dental treatment and to gather relevant information for their medical history, allergies, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information (1) accurately and completely.
2. Describe the patient's medical history (2) if they have any pre-existing conditions or allergies.
3. Indicate any allergies (4) the patient has, if any.
4. List any medications (5) the patient is currently taking.
5. Provide emergency contact details (6, 7, 8) in case of an emergency.
6. Fill out the alternate emergency contact details (9, 10, 11) if applicable.
7. Sign the patient signature (13) section, indicating that they have read and understood the waiver.
8. Date and time the signature (14, 15) by filling out the signature date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (1): Enter the patient's name, address, and phone number.
* **Medical History** (2): Describe any medical conditions or allergies the patient has, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.
* **Dental Procedure** (3): Describe the dental procedure to be performed.
* **Allergies** (4): Select 'Yes' if the patient has any allergies.
* **Medications** (5): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (6): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (7): Describe the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, parent, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (8): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact** (9): Enter the name of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship** (10): Describe the relationship between the patient and the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Phone** (11): Enter the phone number of the alternate emergency contact person.
* **Patient Signature** (13): Sign and date the waiver, indicating that the patient has read and understood it.
* **Date** (14): Date and time of the signature.
* **Time** (15): Time of the signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
- If the patient has any medical history or allergies, describe them in detail.
- Ensure that the emergency contact details are up-to-date and accurate.
- Sign and date the waiver after reading and understanding it.
