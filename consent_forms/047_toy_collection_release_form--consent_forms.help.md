# Toy Collection Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toy Collection Release Form is a tool used to document the release of a toy from a collector to a donor. This form ensures that both the collector and donor agree on the terms of the transfer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide Donor Information: Enter the donor's name and any other relevant contact details in the "Donor Information" field.
2. Describe the Toy: Enter a detailed description of the toy to be transferred, including its name, condition, and any other relevant details, in the "Toy Details" field.
3. Enter Transfer Information: Enter the date and time of the transfer, as well as any other relevant transfer details, in the "Transfer Information" field.
4. Provide Collector Information: Enter your name and any other relevant contact details, as the collector, in the "Collector Information" field.
5. Enter Collection Group Information: If the toy is part of a collection, enter the collection group details in the "Collection Group Information" field.
6. Choose the Assigned Tool: This field is currently not in use.
7. Select the Transfer Date: Enter the date when the toy will be transferred to the donor.
8. Select the Transfer Time: Enter the time when the toy will be transferred to the donor.
9. Complete any other required fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (donor_info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the donor's name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Toy Details (toy_details)** (`text`, required: false): Describe the toy to be transferred, including its name, condition, and any other relevant details.
* **Transfer Information (transfer_info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter the date and time of the transfer, as well as any other relevant transfer details.
* **Collection Group Information (collection_group_info)** (`text`, required: false): If the toy is part of a collection, enter the collection group details.
* **Collector Information (collector_info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant contact details, as the collector.
* **Transfer Date (transfer_date)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date when the toy will be transferred to the donor.
* **Transfer Time (transfer_time)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time when the toy will be transferred to the donor.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)** (`text`, required: false): Not in use at this time.
* **Transfer Time (again, this field should be removed)** : This field has been removed as it is not being used.

## Tips
* Ensure that both the collector and donor have agreed on the transfer terms before filling out this form.
* Double-check the transfer date and time to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
