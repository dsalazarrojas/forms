# privacy-policy-and-conditions-agreement-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' consent to the terms and conditions of a service, product, or website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the content of the form carefully.
2. Fill in the "privacy-policy-and-conditions-agreement" field with your response to the privacy policy and conditions of use.
3. Choose your answer to the "conditions-of-use" field, which is not required.
4. Finally, select whether you confirm your subscription by choosing "True" or "False" in the "confirm-subscription" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **privacy-policy-and-conditions-agreement** (Text, Required): This field asks you to provide your response to the privacy policy and conditions of use. Please type your answer here.
* **conditions-of-use** (Text, Optional): This field is optional and asks you to provide any additional comments or questions you may have about the conditions of use.
* **confirm-subscription** (Select One, Optional): This field is a yes or no question that asks if you confirm your subscription to the service, product, or website. Select "True" if you confirm your subscription, or "False" if you do not.
