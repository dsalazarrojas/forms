# Permanent Guardianship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permanent Guardianship Form is used to establish a permanent guardianship relationship between an adult and a minor child. This form is used to provide legal documentation for courts, social services, and other organizations to verify the guardianship status of a child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, `date`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone** (4, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Email** (5, `email`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's email address.
* **Relationship** (6, `select_one`, Required: False): Indicate whether the guardian has a relationship with the child (Yes/No).
* **Address** (7, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the guardian's address.
* **Date Executed** (8, `date`, Required: False): Please enter the date the guardianship was executed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Name of Guardian** (9, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the name of the person who is the guardian.
* **Name of Child** (10, `text`, Required: False): Please enter the name of the child.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with a lawyer or other relevant authorities.
* This form is intended for permanent guardianship relationships. If you are seeking temporary guardianship, please use the Temporary Guardianship Form.
