# Dissertation Data Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect informed consent from participants in a research study. It ensures that participants understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of the study, and that they have provided their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the **Consent Statement** section and ensure you understand the purpose, risks, and benefits of the research study.
2. Provide your information in the **Participant Info** field, including your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
3. Sign and date the **Informed Consent** section, indicating that you understand the consent statement.
4. Sign and date the **Participant Declaration** section, declaring that you understand the purpose of the study and agree to participate.
5. Have your research supervisor sign and date the **Research Supervisor Signature** section, if applicable.
6. Sign and date the **Participant Signature** section, indicating that you have read and understand the consent statement.
7. Enter the date of the informed consent in the **Date of Informed Consent** and **Date of Signature** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Study Details**: This field is a free-text area where you can provide a brief overview of the research study, including its purpose, procedures, and expected outcomes.
* **Participant Info**: This field collects your name, contact information, and other relevant details that will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of the research study.
* **Informed Consent**: This field is where you sign and date to indicate that you understand the consent statement.
* **Consent Statement**: This is the text that you are being asked to consent to, which explains the purpose, risks, and benefits of the research study.
* **Participant Declaration**: This field is where you declare that you understand the purpose of the study and agree to participate.
* **Date of Informed Consent**: This field is used to record the date when you signed the informed consent.
* **Date Informed Consent Given**: This field is used to record the date when the informed consent was given.
* **Research Supervisor Signature**: This field is optional and only required if your research supervisor needs to sign off on your participation.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is where you sign and date to indicate that you have read and understand the consent statement.
* **Participant Signature Date**: This field is used to record the date when you signed the participant signature.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out for the form to be complete.
