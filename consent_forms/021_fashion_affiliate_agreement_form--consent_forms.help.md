# Fashion Affiliate Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for fashion affiliate partners to document their affiliation with us. It outlines the agreement details for the partnership, including the term start and end dates, affiliate contact information, and any relevant terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "New Affiliate" option to start the process.
2. Enter your Affiliate Name in the text field provided.
3. Provide your Company Name in the text field.
4. Choose the Agreement Start Date and Agreement End Date using the date input field.
5. Add any additional Agreement Terms that apply to your partnership with us.
6. Enter your Affiliate Terms, any specific conditions or requirements for our partnership.
7. Enter your Affiliate Email address.
8. Enter your Affiliate Phone number.
9. Enter your Affiliate Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Affiliate Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your fashion affiliate business or organization.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Agreement Start Date** (date, optional): Select the start date of the agreement, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Agreement End Date** (date, optional): Select the end date of the agreement, or leave blank if not applicable.
* **Agreement Terms** (text, optional): Add any additional terms or conditions that apply to our partnership.
* **Affiliate Terms** (text, optional): Enter any specific conditions or requirements for our partnership with you.
* **Affiliate Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Affiliate Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Affiliate Address** (text, optional): Enter your business or organization's address.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting before submitting. 
* Ensure your phone number is formatted correctly to ensure we can contact you easily. 
* Review your Agreement Terms and Affiliate Terms for accuracy and compliance with our partnership agreement. 

Please submit the completed form to initiate the fashion affiliate partnership with us.
