# Medical Treatment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about medical treatments, including the treatment name, description, date, and cost, as well as the consent given by the subject. It is intended to be used in a medical setting to document and authorize medical treatments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and date of birth or ID number, as well as the name of the subject receiving treatment.
2. Check the box for "Consent Given" to indicate that the subject has given consent for the treatment.
3. Enter the name and description of the treatment, including any relevant medical treatment notes.
4. Enter the date and time of the treatment, as well as the name of the medical provider and facility where the treatment was received.
5. Enter the cost of the treatment and the method of payment.
6. Enter the date and time of payment.
7. Finally, enter any additional notes about the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Medical Treatment Authorization**: Enter a brief description of the medical treatment being authorized.
- **Subject Name**: Enter the name of the subject receiving the medical treatment.
- **Consent Given**: Check the box to indicate that the subject has given consent for the treatment. This should only be checked if the subject has given consent.
- **Treatment Name**: Enter the name of the medical treatment being authorized.
- **Treatment Description**: Enter a brief description of the medical treatment.
- **Date of Treatment**: Enter the date on which the medical treatment was received.
- **Time of Treatment**: Enter the time of day when the medical treatment was received.
- **Medical Provider**: Enter the name of the medical provider who administered the treatment.
- **Medical Treatment Facility**: Enter the name of the medical treatment facility where the treatment was received.
- **Medical Treatment Room**: Enter the name of the room where the treatment was received.
- **Treatment Cost**: Enter the total cost of the medical treatment.
- **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment used for the treatment (Cash, Credit Card, or Other).
- **Payment Date**: Enter the date on which payment was made.
- **Payment Time**: Enter the time of payment.
- **Medical Treatment ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the medical treatment (e.g. an insurance claim number).
- **Medical Treatment Notes**: Enter any additional information about the medical treatment, such as any allergies, medications, or other relevant details.
