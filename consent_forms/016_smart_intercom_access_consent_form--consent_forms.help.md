# Smart Intercom Access Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Smart Intercom Access Consent Form" is used to collect information from residents regarding their access to Smart Intercom, a communication system that enables residents to request assistance from building management or maintenance teams. This form helps ensure that residents' contact details and consent are recorded accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Smart Intercom Access Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
2. Choose "Yes" for Resident Permission if you agree to be contacted through the Smart Intercom system.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for Contact Details, based on your preference for how often you want to receive communications.
4. If you choose to provide contact details, fill in your name, email, and phone number.
5. Write any additional comments or notes in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Permission** (Resident Permission, select_one, required): This field asks whether you agree to be contacted through the Smart Intercom system. Choose "Yes" to grant permission and "No" to decline.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, select_multiple, required): This field asks how often you want to receive communications from the Smart Intercom system. Choose "Yes" to request frequent updates, "No" to decline updates, or any other option to specify your preference.
* **Name** (Name, text, optional): If you have selected "Yes" for Contact Details, provide your name here.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): If you have selected "Yes" for Contact Details, provide your email address here. Please use the format `user@example.com`.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you have selected "Yes" for Contact Details, provide your phone number here.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Write any additional comments or notes about your contact preferences here.
* **Consent** (Consent, select_one, required): This field asks whether you consent to provide your contact details to the Smart Intercom system. Choose "Yes" to grant consent and "No" to decline.
