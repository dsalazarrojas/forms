# legal_documentation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Documentation Consent Form is used to collect user information and consent for the collection, use, and sharing of their data. This form is an essential part of our organization's legal compliance and helps ensure that users understand their data rights and how it will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select the type of consent you are giving, such as "Data Usage Consent" or "Legal Process Consent".
4. If you are giving data usage consent, provide details about how you will use the data.
5. Indicate whether you agree or disagree to share your data with others.
6. Choose whether you will be subject to any legal processes or not.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, you can add them in the "Note" section.
8. Enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to collect your basic user information, such as your name and title.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to collect your contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Type of Consent** (`Type of Consent`, `select_one`, required): This field asks you to select the type of consent you are giving, such as "Data Usage Consent" or "Legal Process Consent". This is an essential part of the form and must be completed to proceed.
* **Data Usage** (`Data Usage`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide details about how you will use the data, if you are giving data usage consent.
* **Data Sharing** (`Data Sharing`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to indicate whether you agree or disagree to share your data with others.
* **Legal Process** (`Legal Process`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field asks you whether you will be subject to any legal processes or not.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional): This is a text field where you can add any additional information or comments.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): This is a field where you can enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you understand the implications of the consent you are giving.
* Review the information provided carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact our support team.
