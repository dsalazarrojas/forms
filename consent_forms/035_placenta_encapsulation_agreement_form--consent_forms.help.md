# placenta_encapsulation_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The placenta encapsulation agreement form is a medical registration form that requires users to provide their basic information and agreement to the placenta encapsulation service. It is essential to complete this form accurately and correctly to ensure proper processing and to avoid any errors or discrepancies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

* Fill out the form with your information, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, address, date of birth, time of birth, and date of encapsulation.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and correctly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address for shipping purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 1999-01-01).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (e.g. 12:00).
* **Date of Encapsulation** (`date_encapsulation`, date, required): Enter the date when your placenta encapsulation service will be performed.
 
## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and correctness before submission.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the administration for guidance.
