# tablet_contract_4 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for a tablet contract, ensuring they are aware of and agree to the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read through the **Terms and Conditions** section to understand the agreement.
2. Fill out the **Name** field with your legal name.
3. Enter the **Date** you are agreeing to the terms.
4. Select your response to **Customer Agreement** (agree or decline).
5. If you have any additional comments, provide them in the **Comments** field.
6. Specify the **Assigned Tool** (if applicable).
7. Sign and date the **Customer Signature** field.
8. Finally, enter the **Date Signed** field with the date you signed the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Terms and Conditions**: A text field where you can review the terms of the agreement.
* **Name**: Enter your legal name.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are agreeing to the terms.
* **Customer Agreement**: Select "agree" to indicate your acceptance of the terms or "decline" to indicate your rejection.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the agreement.
* **Assigned Tool**: Specify the tool assigned to you, if applicable.
* **Customer Signature**: A field for your signature.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the contract.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your representative.
* Ensure you have signed the contract before submitting the form.
