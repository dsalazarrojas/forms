# Medical Aesthetic Procedures Delegation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Aesthetic Procedures Delegation Form is designed to capture the essential information related to a medical aesthetic procedure, including the provider's and patient's details, procedure specifics, and facility information. This form serves as a standardized document to ensure accuracy and consistency across different forms and contexts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the provider's information, including their name, date of service, procedure details, and provider notes.
2. Next, enter the patient's information, such as their name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email.
3. Proceed to enter the medical record number, medical record type, and other procedure-specific information like procedure type, procedure description, number of units used, total units used, and total cost.
4. Finally, include the patient's signature, provider's signature, date signed, time signed, facility name, facility address, and facility phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the medical aesthetic procedure.
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date the procedure was performed.
* **Procedure Details**: Describe the specific procedure performed, including any relevant details.
* **Provider Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the provider.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient receiving the procedure.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Type**: Enter the type of medical record (e.g., chart, note, etc.).
* **Consent Signed By**: Enter the name of the individual who signed the consent form.
* **Date of Consent**: Enter the date the consent was signed.
* **Procedure Type**: Select the type of procedure performed (e.g., Botox, chemical peel, etc.).
* **Procedure Description**: Describe the procedure performed.
* **Number of Units Used**: Enter the number of units used during the procedure.
* **Total Units Used**: Enter the total number of units used.
* **Total Cost**: Enter the total cost of the procedure.
* **Patient Signature**: Have the patient sign the form to confirm they received the procedure.
* **Provider Signature**: Have the provider sign the form to confirm they performed the procedure.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the procedure was performed.
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where the procedure was performed.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility where the procedure was performed.

Note: This form is a critical document in the medical aesthetic industry, and it's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and industry standards.
