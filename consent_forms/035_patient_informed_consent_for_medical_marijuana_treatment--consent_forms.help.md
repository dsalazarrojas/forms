# Patient Informed Consent For Medical Marijuana Treatment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients for medical marijuana treatment, ensuring they understand the risks and benefits associated with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient information section (page 1) with your identifying details.
2. Provide information about your medical history (page 2).
3. Describe your current medication use (page 3).
4. Outline the risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment (page 4).
5. Confirm that you have received medical counseling (page 5).
6. Indicate your consent to participate in the medical marijuana program (page 6).
7. Provide your informed consent date (page 7).
8. Confirm if your healthcare provider is involved in your treatment (page 8).
9. Sign the form to confirm your agreement (page 9).
10. Have your healthcare provider sign the form to acknowledge their involvement (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (Page 1)** (`Patient Information`, text, optional): Enter your name, date of birth, and any other identifying details as required.
* **Medical History (Page 2)** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Describe your medical history, including any conditions, allergies, or previous treatments.
* **Medication Use (Page 3)** (`Medication Use`, text, optional): List your current medications, dosages, and frequency of use.
* **Risks and Benefits (Page 4)** (`Risks and Benefits`, text, optional): Outline the potential risks and benefits of medical marijuana treatment for your condition.
* **Medical Counseling (Page 5)** (`Medical Counseling`, text, optional): Confirm that you have received counseling on the treatment and its implications.
* **Consent to Participate (Page 6)** (`Consent to Participate`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you agree or disagree to participate in the medical marijuana program.
* **Informed Consent Date (Page 7)** (`Informed Consent Date`, date, optional): Enter the date you provided informed consent.
* **Healthcare Provider (Page 8)** (`Healthcare Provider`, select one, optional): Confirm if your healthcare provider is involved in your treatment.
* **Patient Signature (Page 9)** (`Patient Signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature (Page 10)** (`Healthcare Provider Signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to acknowledge your healthcare provider's involvement.

## Tips
Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any potential issues with your treatment. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
