# Health Insurance Renewal Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for clients to provide their consent for health insurance renewal, ensuring that their insurance policy is up-to-date and compliant with regulatory requirements. It is a crucial document that requires accurate and informed client input.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter your **Client Email** in the "Client Email" field (this will help us contact you if we need more information).
3. If your client has a health insurance policy, fill in your policy's **Name** in the "Health Insurance Name" field.
4. If your client has a policy number, provide it in the "Policy Number" field.
5. If you are providing consent for the renewal of your client's health insurance policy, select the "Renewal Date" field and choose the relevant date.
6. Provide the **Name** of the agent who is providing the renewal information in the "Agent Name" field.
7. If you are providing information for a specific **Provider**, enter their name in the "Provider Name" field.
8. For the **Policy Type**, select the type of health insurance policy you are renewing (e.g. Health, Dental, Vision, Prescription, or Other).
9. Read the **Consent** carefully and select all applicable options:
	* I understand and accept that my health insurance policy will be renewed.
	* I understand and accept that my health insurance policy will not be renewed.
10. Optionally, provide a brief **Reason for Renewal** if you want to explain why the policy is being renewed.
11. Leave any additional **Notes** regarding the renewal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (ID 1)**: This is the name of the client whose health insurance policy is being renewed.
* **Client Email (ID 2)**: This is the email address of the client, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Health Insurance Name (ID 3)**: If the client has an existing health insurance policy, enter the name of the policy here.
* **Policy Number (ID 4)**: If the client has a policy number, provide it here.
* **Renewal Date (ID 5)**: This is the date the client wants their policy to be renewed.
* **Agent Name (ID 6)**: Enter the name of the agent who is providing the renewal information.
* **Provider Name (ID 7)**: If the client is seeing a specific provider, enter their name here.
* **Policy Type (ID 8)**: Select the type of health insurance policy being renewed (e.g. Health, Dental, Vision, Prescription, or Other).
* **Consent (ID 9)**: Carefully read the consent options and select all applicable:
	+ I understand and accept that my health insurance policy will be renewed.
	+ I understand and accept that my health insurance policy will not be renewed.
* **Reason for Renewal (ID 10)**: Provide a brief explanation for why the policy is being renewed.
* **Notes (ID 11)**: Leave any additional comments or information about the renewal process.
* **Signature (ID 12)**: Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
