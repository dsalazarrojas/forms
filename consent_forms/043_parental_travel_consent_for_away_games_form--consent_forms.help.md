# Parental Travel Consent For Away Games Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Parental Travel Consent Form is designed to gather information from parents regarding their child's travel plans for away games. This form helps ensure that parents provide necessary details about their child's travel, ensuring their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you are a parent or guardian of the child traveling.
2.  Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much information as required.
3.  If a field is not applicable, please select "N/A" or provide a clear explanation for your choice.
4.  Review and submit your answers carefully to ensure all necessary information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Permission** (Parent Permission): 
    *   This field determines if you, as a parent or guardian, consent to your child's travel.
    *   Select "Yes" if you are aware of and consent to your child's travel.
    *   If you are not aware or do not consent, select "No".

* **Emergency Contacts** (Emergency Contacts):
    *   Please provide the names and contact information of family members or emergency contacts who should be notified in the event of an emergency.
    *   You may also provide any additional contact information or instructions for these individuals.

* **Travel Details** (Travel Details):
    *   Indicate if your child will be traveling on this trip (Yes/No).
    *   This information will help the team and chaperones understand your child's travel status.

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details):
    *   Please provide your name and any other contact information that may be relevant to your child's travel.

* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates):
    *   Enter the date your child will travel.
    *   If multiple dates apply, please provide each date separately.

* **Travel Locations** (Travel Locations):
    *   Enter the location your child will be traveling to.
    *   Provide as much detail as possible, including addresses or GPS coordinates.

* **Travel Method** (Travel Method):
    *   Indicate if you will be accompanying your child on their trip (Yes/No).
    *   This information will help the team and chaperones plan for your child's transportation.

* **Travel Distance** (Travel Distance):
    *   Enter an estimate of the distance your child will be traveling, in kilometers or miles.
    *   This helps the team and chaperones plan for your child's transportation needs.

*   **Travel Vehicle** (Travel Vehicle):
    *   Please provide details about the vehicle your child will be traveling in (e.g., car, bus, plane).
    *   If applicable, provide the license plate number or vehicle make and model.

*   **Return Time** (Return Time):
    *   Enter the estimated time your child will return from their travel.
    *   If multiple return times apply, provide each time separately.

*   **Travel Time** (Travel Time):
    *   Enter the time your child will begin their travel.
    *   If multiple travel times apply, provide each time separately.

*   **Travel Cost** (Travel Cost):
    *   Estimate the cost of your child's travel, if applicable.
    *   You may also provide receipts or proof of payment for this field.

*   **Travel Notes** (Travel Notes):
    *   Provide any additional notes or details about your child's travel that you feel is essential.
    *   This could be anything from allergies to specific medication instructions.

*   **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone):
    *   Provide your phone number.
    *   This is crucial for the team and chaperones to contact you in case of an emergency.

*   **Contact Email** (Contact Email):
    *   Provide your email address.
    *   This allows for easy communication with the team and chaperones.

*   **Contact Notes** (Contact Notes):
    *   Enter any additional notes or details that you feel is relevant to your contact information.
    *   This could be anything from your work schedule to your availability for communication.

*   **Travel Frequency** (Travel Frequency):
    *   Indicate if your child will be traveling to this location frequently (Yes/No).
    *   This information helps the team and chaperones plan for your child's regular travel.

*   **School Name** (School Name):
    *   Enter your child's school name.
    *   This helps the team and chaperones understand your child's academic affiliation.

*   **Team Name** (Team Name):
    *   Enter your child's sports team name.
    *   This helps the team and chaperones track your child's team affiliation.

*   **Team Name 2** (Team Name 2):
    *   Enter your child's second team name (if applicable).
    *   This helps the team and chaperones track your child's team affiliations.

*   **Travel Frequency 2** (Travel Frequency 2):
    *   Indicate if your child will be traveling for this team frequently (Yes/No).
    *   This information helps the team and chaperones plan for your child's regular travel.

*   **School Name 2** (School Name 2):
    *   Enter your child's second school name (if applicable).
    *   This helps the team and chaperones understand your child's academic affiliation.

*   **Team Name 3** (Team Name 3):
    *   Enter your child's third team name (if applicable).
    *   This helps the team and chaperones track your child's team affiliations.

*   **Travel Frequency 3** (Travel Frequency 3):
    *   Indicate if your child will be traveling for this team frequently (Yes/No).
    *   This information helps the team and chaperones plan for your child's regular travel.

*   **School Name 3** (School Name 3):
    *   Enter your child's third school name (if applicable).
    *   This helps the team and chaperones understand your child's academic affiliation.

## Tips
Please review your answers carefully before submitting them to ensure you have provided all necessary information.
If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
