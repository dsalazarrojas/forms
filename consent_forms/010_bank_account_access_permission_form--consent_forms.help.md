# Bank Account Access Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to grant access to a bank account for an authorized representative. It requires information about the primary account holder and the representative to be granted access.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the following information:
	* **Account Holder Full Name** (`holder_full_name`): Enter the full name of the primary account holder as it appears on the bank statements.
	* **Bank Account Number** (`account_number`): Enter the full account number for authorization.
	* **Account Type** (`account_type`): Select the type of account you want to grant access to (e.g., Personal Checking, Personal Savings, Business Checking, Business Savings, or Money Market).
	* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number for verification purposes.
	* **Representative Full Name** (`rep_full_name`): Enter the full name of the authorized representative.
	* **Relationship to Account Holder** (`rep_relationship`): Specify the relationship between the account holder and the representative (e.g., Spouse, Attorney, Accountant, Business Partner).
	* **Representative ID Type** (`rep_id_type`): Select the type of identification to be presented (e.g., State Driver's License, Passport, Government ID).
	* **Permitted Actions** (`access_type`): Select the level of authority granted (e.g., View Balances Only, View Transaction History, Make Deposits, Execute Withdrawals (Limited), Execute Withdrawals (Unlimited), Open/Close Sub-Accounts).
	* **Duration of Access** (`access_duration`): Select the duration of access granted (e.g., Permanent (Until Revoked), One-Time Access, Temporary - Specified Date Range).
	* **Access Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`): Enter the date when the access will expire (only required if Temporary access is selected).
	* **Liability Acknowledgment** (`liability_waiver`): Select whether you understand that you are responsible for all actions taken by the authorized representative or wish to restrict liability.
	* **Revocation Notice** (`revocation_notice`): Agree that you may revoke this access at any time in writing.
	* **Date of Authorization** (`digital_signature_date`): Enter the date of authorization.
