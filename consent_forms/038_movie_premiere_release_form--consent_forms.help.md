# Movie Premiere Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending a movie premiere or sharing their thoughts on a film. It is essential to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure that you can be contacted about the event or receive updates on the movie.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Confirm your email address to ensure it is accurate.
3. Select your preferred time slot for the premiere.
4. Choose the options that best describe your interests or preferences.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can provide them in the dedicated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm its accuracy.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for potential follow-up contacts.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or details you'd like to share with us.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to attend the premiere.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time slot that suits you best for the premiere.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Confirm the start time of the selected time slot.
* **Interests** (`what_do_you_like`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your interest in the movie.
* **Multiple Interests** (`what_do_you_like-2`, select_multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of options.
* **Notes 2** (`notes-2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your interests.
* **Notes 3** (`notes-3`, note, optional): Share any further comments or details.
