# Satellite TV Local Channel Waiver Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for a waiver for a local channel on the satellite TV system. A waiver is a written permission to temporarily waive a rule or regulation, allowing for access to a channel that would otherwise be restricted. This form is used to document and track requests for waivers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the customer name in the "customer name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer phone" field.
4. Enter the reason for the waiver request in the "reason for request" field.
5. Enter the local channel number that the customer is requesting a waiver for in the "local channel number" field.
6. Sign and date the "signature date" field.
7. Sign and time the "signature time" field.
8. Provide a brief description of the waiver request in the "waiver request" field.
9. Indicate whether the customer has accepted the waiver terms in the "waiver terms accepted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satellite TV Local Channel Waiver Request Form**: This is the title of the form, which serves as a header and introduction to the form.
* **Customer Name**: The customer's name is required to be entered here. This will help us identify the customer making the request.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address so we can contact them if needed.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Reason for Request**: Briefly explain the reason for the waiver request. This will help us understand the context of the request.
* **Local Channel Number**: Enter the specific local channel number for which the waiver request is being made.
* **Signature Date**: Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the waiver request.
* **Signature Time**: Sign and time the form to indicate the exact time of signature.
* **Waiver Request**: Describe the request in a few words, including any supporting details.
* **Waiver Terms Accepted**: Check "True" to indicate that you have read and accepted the waiver terms.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the request can be processed.
* If you are unable to access the channel due to technical issues, do not submit a waiver request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
