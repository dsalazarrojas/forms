# Fitness Studio Social Post Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture informed consent from individuals who are being featured in the Fitness Studio's social media posts. By completing this form, individuals are giving their consent for the Fitness Studio to post their likeness, voice, and other personal data on various social media platforms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of social post you are comfortable with:
	* Social post on your personal social media profiles
	* Post on other social media platforms
	* Post in our fitness studio social media channels
	* Post with your photo
	* Post with your testimonial
	* Post with your video
	* Do not post
2. Enter your name and email address:
	* First Name: _______________________________________
	* Last Name: _______________________________________
	* Email: _______________________________________
3. Select the type of consent you are giving:
	* Informed Consent
	* Express Consent
4. Enter the date and time of your service:
	* Date of Service: ___________________________
	* Time of Service: ___________________________
5. Choose how you want to be shared on social media:
	* Share on Facebook
	* Share on Twitter
	* Share on Instagram
	* Share on YouTube
	* Share on Other Platforms
	* Do not share
6. Provide photo and video consent:
	* Photo Consent: Photo
	* Video Consent: Video
7. Sign and date the form:
	* Signature: _______________________________________
	* Date Signed: _______________________________________
8. Specify the type of release form attached:
	* Photo Release: Signed photo release form attached
	* Video Release: Signed video release form attached

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Post Consent** (Select one):
	+ Social post on your personal social media profiles
	+ Post on other social media platforms
	+ Post in our fitness studio social media channels
	+ Post with your photo
	+ Post with your testimonial
	+ Post with your video
	+ Do not post
* **First Name:** Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name:** Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email:** Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Photo Consent:** Choose the type of photo consent you are giving.
* **Video Consent:** Choose the type of video consent you are giving.
* **Testimonial Consent:** Check all that apply.
* **Share on Facebook:** Check if you want to be shared on Facebook.
* **Share on Twitter:** Check if you want to be shared on Twitter.
* **Share on Instagram:** Check if you want to be shared on Instagram.
* **Share on YouTube:** Check if you want to be shared on YouTube.
* **Share on Other Platforms:** Check if you want to be shared on other platforms.
* **Photo Release:** Specify if a photo release form is attached.
* **Video Release:** Specify if a video release form is attached.
* **Date of Service:** Enter the date of your service.
* **Time of Service:** Enter the time of your service.
* **Type of Consent:** Choose the type of consent you are giving.
* **Signed By:** Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date Signed:** Enter the date you signed this form.
* **Time Signed:** Enter the time you signed this form.
* **Signature:** Sign this form to indicate your consent.
* **Form Version:** Enter the version number of this form.
* **Assigned Tool:** Enter the tool used to create this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand each section before completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the Fitness Studio administration.
* This form is a legal document and should be completed carefully and accurately.
* Make sure to sign and date the form when you have finished.
