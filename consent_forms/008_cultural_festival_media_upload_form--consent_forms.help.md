# Cultural Festival Media Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect media and information about cultural festivals, including participant details, festival dates and times, location, and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Festival Name** field with the name of the cultural festival.
2. In the **Participant Media** field, describe any media that will be used during the festival, such as photos, videos, or performances.
3. For **Participant Photo**, select the number of photos to be uploaded.
4. Answer **Participant Video** by selecting whether or not video will be part of the festival program.
5. Answer **Participant Audio** by selecting whether or not audio will be part of the festival program.
6. For **Upload Files**, select whether or not files will be uploaded as part of the festival media.
7. Enter the **Festival Date** and **Festival Time** fields with the actual dates and times of the festival.
8. Select the correct **Festival Start Time** and **Festival Finish Time** from the time options.
9. For **Festival Organizer**, select whether or not the festival has an organizer.
10. Enter the **Festival Email** address of the festival organizer.
11. In the **Festival Website** field, provide the URL of the festival website (if applicable).
12. Describe the **Festival Description** in the text box (if required).
13. Select the **Festival Location** by choosing the correct options from the dropdown menu.
14. Select the **Festival Venue** by choosing the correct options from the dropdown menu.
