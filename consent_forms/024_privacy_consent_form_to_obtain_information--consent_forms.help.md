# Privacy Consent Form To Obtain Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for privacy purposes, such as data collection, research, or other similar activities. It is essential to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure that your data is handled responsibly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
*   Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
*   Enter your Birth Date in the "Birth Date" field. This field is designed to accept date information.
*   Fill in your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
*   **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth here, in the format of a standard date (e.g., 01/01/1990).
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number here, which can be a phone number or any other contact method. 

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy.
*   Provide truthful information to the best of your ability.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support team.
