# Employee Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Employee Confidentiality Form** is a document for collecting employee-related information, likely for internal purposes, such as HR record-keeping or data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Employee Name** field with your name.
2.  Enter your **Job Title** in the corresponding field.
3.  Type your **Department** in the field provided.
4.  Select the date of the confidentiality agreement in the **Date** field.
5.  Write your **Manager's Name** in the field provided.
6.  Enter the company policy that applies to you in the **Company Policy** field.
7.  Write your acknowledgement of understanding in the **Acknowledgement** field.
8.  Sign and date the **Confidentiality Agreement** field to confirm your understanding.
9.  In the **Acknowledgement 2** field, provide a statement of your understanding of the confidentiality agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (1)**: Type your name in this field. This field is optional.
* **Job Title (3)**: Enter your job title in this field. This field is optional.
* **Department (4)**: Type your department in this field. This field is optional.
* **Date (5)**: Select the date of the confidentiality agreement in this field.
* **Manager's Name (6)**: Write your manager's name in this field. This field is optional.
* **Company Policy (7)**: Enter the company policy that applies to you in this field. This field is optional.
* **Acknowledgement (8)**: Write your acknowledgement of understanding in this field.
* **Confidentiality (9)**: Sign and date the confidentiality agreement in this field. This field is optional.
* **Acknowledgement 2 (10)**: Provide a statement of your understanding of the confidentiality agreement in this field. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete records.
* Double-check the company policy and date before signing the confidentiality agreement.
* Review and understand the confidentiality agreement before signing it.
* Keep a copy of the signed confidentiality agreement for your records.
