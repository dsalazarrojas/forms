# Youth Team Travel Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Team Travel Release Consent Form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child's travel to events or activities associated with the team. This form helps to ensure that parents/guardians are aware of and approve the travel arrangements and dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct option for "Parent Consent" on Page 1.
2. Enter the travel start and end dates in the "travel_dates_start" and "travel_dates_end" fields, respectively.
3. Fill out the remaining fields as required for your child's travel, such as contact information, team details, and travel specifics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent Consent** (`Parent 1/ Parent 2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the correct option indicating whether both or one parent has given consent for your child's travel. This field is required.
  - If both parents have given consent, select "Both Parents."
  - If only one parent has given consent, select "Parent 1" or "Parent 2".
- **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your child's travel. This field is optional.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person. This field is optional.
- **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, `text`, optional): Describe the destination or location of the travel. This field is optional.
- **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your child's team. This field is optional.
- **Team Contact** (`team_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for the team. This field is optional.
- **Travel Type** (`Team Event/Tournament`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the type of travel event your child will be attending (Team Event or Tournament). This field is required.
- **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for the travel purpose or event your child will be attending. This field is optional.
- **Team League** (`team_league`, `text`, optional): Enter the league or affiliation of your child's team. This field is optional.
- **Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`, `number`, optional): Enter the distance or miles traveled for the trip. This field is optional.
- **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the trip in hours or days. This field is optional.
- **Travel Cost** (`travel_cost`, `number`, optional): Enter an estimate of the travel cost or expenses. This field is optional.
- **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the travel or event. This field is optional.
- **Team Sponsor** (`team_sponsor`, `text`, optional): Enter the team sponsor or organization associated with your child's team. This field is optional.
- **Travel Frequency** (`travel_frequency`, `text`, optional): Describe how often your child will be traveling with the team. This field is optional.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of a parent or guardian. This field is optional.
- **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes for the travel. This field is optional.
- **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm your consent.
- **Travel Date Signature** (`travel_date_signature`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the form.
- **Travel Time Signature** (`travel_time_signature`, `time`, required): Enter the time you signed the form.
- **Team Manager Signature** (`team_manager_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form as the team manager.
- **Team Travel Coordinator** (`team_travel_coordinator`, `text`, optional): Enter the contact information for the team travel coordinator. This field is optional.
- **Team Travel Coordinator Phone** (`team_travel_coordinator_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the team travel coordinator. This field is optional.

Tips:
- Please ensure that both parents/guardians have given consent before submitting the form.
- Double-check the travel dates and details before submitting the form.
- Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
