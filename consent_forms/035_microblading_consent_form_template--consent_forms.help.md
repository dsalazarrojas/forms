# Microblading Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that clients understand and agree to the microblading procedure before it is performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:
* Review the form carefully to understand all the questions and instructions.
* Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
* Sign and date the form as a record of your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Reservations
* **Client Reservations** (`Client Reservations`, text, optional): This section is meant to capture any information that may be relevant to your reservations or concerns about the procedure.
* Please share any reservations or concerns you have about the procedure.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): This section will collect any relevant medical history that may be important for the practitioner to consider before performing the procedure.
* Share any relevant medical history that may impact the procedure or your comfort level with it.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, optional): This will capture your contact information for communication purposes.
* Please share your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

### Signature
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, optional): This is where you will record your consent by signing your name.
* Please sign your name to indicate your understanding and agreement to proceed with the procedure.

### Date/Time
* **Date/Time** (`Date/Time`, date, optional): This field will record the date and time of when the form was completed.
* This field will be filled out automatically when the form is completed.

# Tips
* Please take your time to review the form carefully before signing.
* Make sure to answer all questions thoroughly and accurately.
* If you have any concerns or reservations, please share them in the Client Reservations section.
* If you have any medical history that may impact the procedure, please share it in the Medical History section.
* Sign and date the form as a record of your consent.
* Contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
