# Foot Spa Paraffin Wax Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foot Spa Paraffin Wax Consent Form is a document used to obtain a client's consent for the application of paraffin wax in our spa's foot treatment services. This form is essential for us to ensure that clients are aware of and agree to the procedures involved in our treatment, including the use of paraffin wax.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Provide any relevant health information in the "Health Information" section, if applicable.
3. Indicate whether you have signed the consent by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Signed Consent" section.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: This is the section where you will provide your personal details, including your name and contact information.
* **Health Information**: Please provide any health information that may be relevant to your treatment, such as allergies or medical conditions.
* **Signed Consent**: Please select "True" to indicate that you have read and understood the procedures involved in our treatment and consent to the use of paraffin wax, or "False" if you do not.
* **Contact Info**: This is where you will enter your contact information, which will be used to reach out to you with any questions or concerns.
