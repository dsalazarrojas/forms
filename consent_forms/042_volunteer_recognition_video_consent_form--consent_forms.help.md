# Volunteer Recognition Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from volunteers to be featured in a video recognition. The video will acknowledge and thank the volunteers for their contributions or achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your title.
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Provide your email address.
5. Choose whether you consent to being featured in the video by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. If you select "Yes", you may add any additional comments about your experience or contributions.
7. If you select "Yes", you may also enter your organization, department, role, job title, and volunteer type.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (text): Enter your title for the video recognition.
* **First name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address that you can be contacted at.
* **Video Consent** (select_one): Choose whether you consent to being featured in the video by selecting "Yes" or "No".
	+ Select "Yes" if you agree to be featured in the video.
	+ Select "No" if you do not consent.
* **Additional Comments** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, you may add any additional comments or information about your experience or contributions.
* **Organization** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your organization's name.
* **Department** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your department name.
* **Role** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your role in the organization.
* **Job Title** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your job title.
* **Volunteer Type** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your type of volunteer work (e.g., event volunteer, regular volunteer, etc.).
* **Video Consent Organization** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your organization's name.
* **Video Consent Department** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your department name.
* **Video Consent Role** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your role in the organization.
* **Video Consent Job Title** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your job title.
* **Video Consent Volunteer Type** (text): If you select "Yes" to being featured, enter your type of volunteer work (e.g., event volunteer, regular volunteer, etc.).
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to submit your response.
* **Back** (text): Click this button to go back to the previous page.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you select "Yes" to being featured, make sure to check your email for further instructions on the video creation process.
* If you select "No" to being featured, your response will still be received and appreciated, but you will not be included in the video.
