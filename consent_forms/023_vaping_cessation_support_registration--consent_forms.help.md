# Vaping Cessation Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in quitting or have quit vaping. It will be used by support staff to provide personalized support and follow-up services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`User Info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Please enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, optional): Share any relevant medical history that may impact your vaping cessation journey.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, text, optional): Please indicate your consent to participate in the support program.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, text, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (e.g. Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Follow Up Info** (`Follow Up Info`, text, optional): Provide any additional follow-up information we should know.
* **Date Of Registration** (`Date Of Registration`, date, optional): Enter the date you registered for this program.
* **Date Of Cessation** (`Date Of Cessation`, date, optional): Enter the date you quit vaping.
* **Participant Info** (`Participant Info`, text, optional): Share any relevant participant information.
* **Medical History Details** (`Medical History Details`, note, optional): Provide detailed information about your medical history.
* **Follow Up Method** (`Follow Up Method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be followed up (e.g. Phone, Email, In Person, Mail, Other).
* **Program Type** (`Program Type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of program you are interested in (e.g. Nicotine Replacement Therapy, Behavioral Counseling, Medication, Both).
* **None** (`None`, text, optional): If you don't have any information to provide for this field, just leave it blank.
* **Program Location** (`Program Location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the program you are interested in.
