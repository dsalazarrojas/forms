# Liability Waiver For Youth Activities - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liability Waiver For Youth Activities is a form used to obtain parental consent for youth activities. It is used to ensure that parents or guardians have given permission for their child to participate in a program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

*   Fill out the Consent Form, selecting the appropriate options for your child.
*   Sign the Guardian Consent, indicating your permission for your child to participate.
*   List all emergency contact information in the Emergency Contacts field.
*   Sign and date the Parent Signature field.
*   Enter your name in the Parent Name field.
*   Enter your child's name in the Child Name field.
*   Select the date of the program or activity.
*   Enter the name of the program or activity in the Program Name field.
*   Enter the location of the program or activity in the Program Location field.
*   Select the start and end times of the program or activity.
*   Enter the name of the staff member in charge of the program or activity in the Staff Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Consent Form**:
    *   This field allows you to select which type of activity your child will be participating in (e.g., sports, arts, etc.).
*   **Guardian Consent**:
    *   This field is used to obtain your consent for your child to participate in the activity.
*   **Emergency Contacts**:
    *   List all emergency contact information, including names and phone numbers of people who can be reached in case of an emergency.
*   **Parent Signature**:
    *   This field is for you to sign and date, indicating that you have read and understand the waiver.
*   **Parent Name**:
    *   Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
*   **Child Name**:
    *   Enter your child's name.
*   **Date**:
    *   Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
*   **Program Name**:
    *   Enter the name of the program or activity.
*   **Program Location**:
    *   Enter the location where the activity will take place.
*   **Program Start Time**:
    *   Select the start time of the program or activity.
*   **Program End Time**:
    *   Select the end time of the program or activity.
*   **Staff Name**:
    *   Enter the name of the staff member in charge of the program or activity.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the staff or organization in charge of the program or activity.
*   This form is a requirement for your child to participate in the activity.
