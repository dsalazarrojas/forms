# Social Media Policy Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect acknowledgment from employees regarding the company's social media policy. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that employees understand and agree to the policy's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your work Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Department in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
7. If applicable, sign and date the "Date Signed" field with the current date.
8. Provide a signature in the "Signature" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "I acknowledge the social media policy" question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your work email address to ensure that you can be contacted regarding social media policy updates and inquiries.
* **Department (4)**: Enter the department you belong to within the company.
* **Job Title (5)**: Enter your official job title within the company.
* **Date Signed (6)**: If applicable, sign and date this field with the current date to confirm that you have read and understood the social media policy. 
* **Signature (7)**: Provide a handwritten or digital signature to confirm that you have read and understood the social media policy.
* **I acknowledge the social media policy (8)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the social media policy. If you are unsure, select "No" and consult your supervisor or HR department for clarification.
