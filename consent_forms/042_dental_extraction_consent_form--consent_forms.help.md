# dental_extraction_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental extraction consent form is a crucial document that ensures the patient is informed and agrees to the dental extraction procedure. This form helps to establish a clear understanding between the patient and the dentist, outlining the risks, complications, and responsibilities involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
2. **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address as it appears on their identification documents.
3. **Dentist Name**: Enter the name of the dentist performing the procedure.
4. **Procedure Description**: Describe the dental extraction procedure that will be performed, including any relevant medical history or previous treatments.
5. **Risks And Complications**: List any potential risks or complications associated with the procedure.
6. **Patient Signature**: The patient must sign the form to confirm their understanding and agreement to the procedure.
7. **Dentist Signature**: The dentist must sign the form to confirm they have obtained the patient's consent.
8. **Consent Date**: Enter the date when the patient provided their consent.
9. **Consent Time**: Enter the time when the patient provided their consent.
10. **Patient Date**: Enter the date of the dental extraction procedure (optional, but recommended for record-keeping purposes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (1)**: A text field for entering the patient's name.
* **Patient Address (2)**: A text field for entering the patient's address.
* **Dentist Name (3)**: A text field for entering the dentist's name.
* **Procedure Description (4)**: A note field for describing the dental extraction procedure.
* **Risks And Complications (5)**: A text field for listing potential risks or complications.
* **Patient Signature (6)**: A text field for the patient's signature.
* **Dentist Signature (7)**: A text field for the dentist's signature.
* **Consent Date (8)**: A date field for entering the date when the patient provided their consent.
* **Consent Time (9)**: A time field for entering the time when the patient provided their consent.
* **Patient Date (10)**: A text field for entering the date of the dental extraction procedure.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Ensure the patient has fully understood and agreed to the procedure.
* Store the completed form in a secure location, such as a patient's file or electronic record.
* Update the form as needed to reflect any changes to the procedure or patient information.
