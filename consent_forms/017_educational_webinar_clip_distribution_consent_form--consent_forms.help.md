# Educational Webinar Clip Distribution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in the educational webinar clip distribution program. It helps us understand their consent and preferences for the distribution of webinar clips, which will be used for educational purposes. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form and explain each field in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information:
	* Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
	* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
	* Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This will be the email address we use to contact you.
2. Choose your instructor:
	* Select one (or multiple) instructor(s) you are interested in from the "Select One Instructor" and "Select Multiple Instructors" fields.
3. Confirm your agreements:
	* Select one (or multiple) agreement(s) you agree to from the "Select One Agreement" and "Select Multiple Agreements" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Your email address. This will be the address we use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Your phone number. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Date Of Birth (Date)**: Your date of birth. This will help us understand your age range for demographic purposes.
* **Select One Instructor (Select One)**: Choose one instructor you are interested in. This will help us determine which clips you are interested in.
* **Select Multiple Instructors (Select Multiple)**: Choose multiple instructors you are interested in. This will help us determine which clips you are interested in.
* **Note (Note)**: Leave a note about your preferences or any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **Time (Time)**: Your preferred time for webinar clips distribution. This will help us schedule the clips according to your availability.
* **Select One Agreement (Select One)**: Choose one agreement you agree to. This will help us understand your consent for the clip distribution.
* **Select Multiple Agreements (Select Multiple)**: Choose multiple agreements you agree to. This will help us understand your consent for the clip distribution.
