# Smart Key Integration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Smart Key Integration Consent Form is a crucial document that helps us collect essential information from you for integration with our smart device or system. This form is designed to ensure that we have all necessary details to provide you with the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Enter your user name in the "User Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. If required, enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field (optional).
5. Enter your key name in the "Key Name" field.
6. Select your key type from the options provided in the "Key Type" field (optional).
7. Enter your key details in the "Key Details" field.
8. Enter the date for your key in the "Key Date" field.
9. Enter your smart name in the "Smart Name" field.
10. Select your smart type from the options provided in the "Smart Type" field (optional).
11. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Smart Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your user name to identify yourself in our system.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding the integration process.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it to enable us to contact you more effectively.
* **Note** (`user_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes that might be helpful for the integration process.
* **Key Name** (`key_name`, `text`, required): Enter your key name to associate it with your integration details.
* **Key Type** (`key_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of key you are integrating (Key 1, Key 2, or Key 3).
* **Key Details** (`key_details`, `text`, required): Enter key details to provide us with more information about your key.
* **Key Date** (`key_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date for your key.
* **Smart Name** (`smart_name`, `text`, required): Enter your smart name to associate it with your integration details.
* **Smart Type** (`smart_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of smart device or system you are integrating (Smart 1, Smart 2, or Smart 3).
* **Smart Note** (`smart_note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding your smart integration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use the options provided for fields where applicable.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team for clarification.
* Once you've completed the form, you'll be able to proceed with the integration process.
