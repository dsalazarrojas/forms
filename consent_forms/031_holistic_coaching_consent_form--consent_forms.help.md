# holistic_coaching_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from clients or individuals participating in the holistic coaching program, ensuring both parties are aligned on the coaching goals, consent status, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Fill in the fields accurately, providing your coaching goals, consent status, and other relevant details.
3.  Ensure all required fields are filled in, as indicated by the "required" flag in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, `text`, required: `false`): This field is for the client to describe their coaching goals. Please be specific and clear about what you hope to achieve from the coaching process.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for the date you consent to participate in the coaching program. Please enter the date you are willing to start the coaching process.
* **Coaching Approach** (`coaching_approach`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the provided options to indicate the coaching approach you prefer (e.g., phone calls, emails, or in-person meetings).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address for communication with the coach.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for the date you plan to start the coaching process.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the consent types that apply to you (e.g., I agree, I do not agree).
* **Onboarding Details** (`onboarding_details`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any additional details about your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Time** (`onboarding_time`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you plan to have your onboarding.
* **Consent Confirmation** (`consent_confirmation`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is for the coach to confirm your consent.
* **Onboarding Method** (`onboarding_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the provided options to indicate the method of onboarding you prefer (e.g., phone calls, emails, or in-person meetings).
* **Onboarding Location** (`onboarding_location`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the location for onboarding (e.g., home, office, or online).
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is for the client to provide their signature.
* **Coach Signature** (`coach_signature`, `note`, required: `false`): This field is for the coach to provide their signature.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address for communication with the coach.
* **Onboarding Time 2** (`onboarding_time_2`, `time`, required: `false`): Enter the time you plan to have your second onboarding.
* **Onboarding Date 2** (`onboarding_date_2`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for the date you plan to have your second onboarding.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact 2.
* **Onboarding Method 2** (`onboarding_method_2`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select one of the provided options to indicate the method of onboarding you prefer (e.g., phone calls, emails, or in-person meetings).
* **Onboarding Location 2** (`onboarding_location_2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the location for onboarding 2 (e.g., home, office, or online).
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the consent types that apply to you (e.g., I agree, I do not agree).
* **Consent Date 2** (`consent_date_2`, `date`, required: `false`): This field is for the date you agree to participate in the coaching program 2.

## Tips
- Please review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- Be specific and clear about your coaching goals and onboarding preferences.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your coach for clarification.
- This form is a supplementary tool to facilitate smooth communication and ensure both parties are on the same page.
