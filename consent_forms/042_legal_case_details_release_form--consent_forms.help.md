# Legal Case Details Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Case Details Release Form is used to collect information about a legal case from a respondent, typically in the context of a release or settlement agreement. This form helps to gather essential details about the case, including the respondent's contact information and any relevant context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `First name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `Last name` field.
3.  Provide your email address in the `Email` field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including the area code and any extension (optional).

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities or seek legal counsel.
*   Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
*   This form is for official use only and is not intended for solicitation or advertising purposes.
