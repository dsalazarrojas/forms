# Client Testimonial Advertising Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Testimonial Advertising Release Form is used to collect client testimonials and advertising releases from customers who have used the company's service. This form is intended to gather authentic and honest feedback from clients, which can be used to build credibility and trust with potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the required fields with your information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Share your experience with our service by writing a testimonial or quote in the "Your Testimonial or Quote" field.
3. Describe the impact of our service in the "Before and After Description" field (optional).
4. If you have specific benefits or results achieved from using our service, please describe them in the "Specific Benefits or Results Achieved" field (optional).
5. Please select how long you have used our service from the "How Long Have You Used Our Service?" field (optional).
6. Review and agree to the terms of use for your testimonial.
7. Confirm that your testimonial is accurate and true.
8. Release the company from any liability regarding the use of your testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Your Testimonial or Quote**: Share your experience with our service. This can be a brief description of your experience or a quote.
* **Before and After Description**: Describe the impact of our service on you or your business.
* **Specific Benefits or Results Achieved**: Describe specific benefits or results achieved from using our service.
* **How Long Have You Used Our Service?**: Select how long you have used our service.
* **I Grant Permission to Publish**: Select whether you grant permission for your testimonial to be published in marketing materials.
* **My Name May Be Used with the Testimonial**: Select whether you agree to have your name used with the testimonial.
* **A Photo of Me May Be Used**: Select whether you agree to have a photo of yourself used with the testimonial.
* **Approved Use of Testimonial**: Select where you are willing for your testimonial to be used (optional).
* **Duration of Permission**: Select for how long you grant permission for your testimonial to be used.
* **I Understand This Is Provided Without Compensation**: Select whether you understand that you are not being compensated for your testimonial.
* **I Confirm the Testimonial Is Accurate and True**: Confirm that your testimonial is accurate and true.
* **I Release the Company From Any Liability**: Select whether you release the company from any liability regarding the use of your testimonial.
* **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date on which you are signing this agreement.
