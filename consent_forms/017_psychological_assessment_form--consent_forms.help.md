# Psychological Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychological Assessment Form is used to collect information about a client's psychological health and assessment details from mental health professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand what each field is asking for.
2. Enter your information into the relevant fields, such as your name, phone number, email, and assessment details.
3. Select the appropriate options for each field according to your circumstances.
4. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant to your assessment.
* **Psychological Health** (`psychological_health`, number, required: false): Enter your psychological health score or rating (e.g., 1-10).
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your assessment.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your assessment or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, select_one, required: false): Select "I agree" if you consent to the assessment and its terms.
* **Mental Health Professional** (`mental_health_professional`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of mental health professional you see (e.g., Psychiatrist, Therapist).
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of client you are (e.g., Adult, Child, Senior).
* **Assessment Location** (`assessment_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the location of your assessment (e.g., Office, Home, Clinic).
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your assessment.
* **Assessment Duration** (`assessment_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the duration of your assessment.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about your assessment.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (`follow_up_schedule`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your follow-up schedule (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Client Status** (`client_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your current client status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Assessment Frequency** (`assessment_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select the frequency of your assessments (e.g., One Time, Ongoing).

Please note that the fields that are required: false can be left empty if not applicable.
