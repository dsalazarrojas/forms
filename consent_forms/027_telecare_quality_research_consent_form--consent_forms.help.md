# Telecare Quality Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Telecare Quality Research Consent Form is to gather essential information for a specific study or research project. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected to provide an overview of the project, its scope, and the participant's involvement. It is used to record participant information, study details, and any other relevant data that may affect the study's progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the form carefully and making sure you have a clear understanding of the project details and requirements.
2. Answer all the sections as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Enter the participant's information, such as their name, contact details, and emergency contact information.
4. Provide details about the study, including its name, start date, and status.
5. Select the relevant options for each field based on your specific situation.
6. Once all the required fields are filled out, review the form to ensure everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Details** (`study_details`, text, optional): This section captures the essential details of the study, including its purpose, scope, and objectives. Please provide a concise description of the study to help us understand its nature.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other contact information relevant to the study. This will help us communicate with you effectively and keep you updated on the project's progress.
* **Study Involvement** (`study_involvement`, text, optional): Describe the specific activities or tasks you will be involved in during the study. This will help us ensure that you understand your role and responsibilities within the project.
* **Telehealth Involvement** (`telehealth_involvement`, text, optional): If applicable, mention any telehealth-related activities or tools you will use during the study. This will help us monitor any potential technical issues and provide necessary support.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided options to signify your consent to participate in the study. This ensures we have a record of your informed consent.
* **Data Protection** (`data_protection`, note, optional): Please provide any information about how your data will be handled and protected during the study. This will help us ensure the confidentiality and security of your data.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, number, optional): Enter the names and contact information of at least two emergency contacts, in case we need to reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information that may be useful for communication purposes, such as a phone number, email, or address.
* **Study Status** (`study_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the statuses that apply to your participation in the study, such as 'Active', 'Paused', or 'Completed'.
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the study, or the date you started participating in it. This will help us track the study's timeline and progress.
