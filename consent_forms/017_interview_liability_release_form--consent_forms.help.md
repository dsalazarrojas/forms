# Interview Liability Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Interview Liability Release Form is a crucial document used to capture essential details of an interview, including liability release information, interviewee and journalist particulars, and media organization details. This form serves as a safeguard to ensure that all parties involved in the interview understand their responsibilities and liabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before You Start

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and clearance to access and complete this form.
2. Review the form carefully and fill it out accurately.
3. Fill out all required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (*) symbol.

### Completing the Form

1. **Interview Liability Release Details**: This section captures the details of the interview, including the date and time of the interview.
2. **Interview Consent**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the interviewee has given their consent.
3. **Interviewee Details**: Enter the interviewee's name, date of birth, email, phone number, and address.
4. **Journalist Details**: Enter the journalist's name, email, and phone number.
5. **Media Organization Details**: Enter the media organization's name and website.
6. **Media Organization Address**: Enter the media organization's address.
7. **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
8. **Interview Time**: Enter the time of the interview.
9. **Release Date**: Enter the date of release.
10. **Release Time**: Enter the time of release.
11. **Consent Signature**: Enter the date of consent signing.
12. **Release Signature**: Enter the date of release signing.
13. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
14. **Signature Time**: Enter the time of signing.
15. **Media Organization Name**: Enter the name of the media organization.
16. **Media Organization Website**: Enter the website of the media organization.
17. **Media Organization Address**: Enter the address of the media organization.
18. **Interviewee Name**: Enter the name of the interviewee.
19. **Interviewee Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the interviewee.
20. **Interviewee Email**: Enter the email of the interviewee.
21. **Interviewee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the interviewee.
22. **Interviewee Address**: Enter the address of the interviewee.
23. **Journalist Name**: Enter the name of the journalist.
24. **Journalist Email**: Enter the email of the journalist.
25. **Journalist Phone**: Enter the phone number of the journalist.
