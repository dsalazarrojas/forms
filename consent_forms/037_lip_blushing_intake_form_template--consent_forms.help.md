# Lip Blushing Intake Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who are undergoing lip blushing treatment. It helps the treatment provider to understand the client's needs and preferences, which is essential for providing the best possible treatment outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete the contact information section**: Please fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Provide your medical history**: Share any relevant medical information that may affect your treatment.
3. **Select your treatment details**: Choose the type of treatment you are undergoing (Lip Blushing, Enhancements, or Revisions).
4. **Specify your lip blushing details**: If you selected Lip Blushing, choose the location of the blushing, the size of the area, the type of blushing desired (Natural, Enhanced, etc.), and the color of the blushing desired.
5. **Share your allergy information**: If you have any allergies, please provide details.
6. **Mention any medical conditions**: If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please inform the treatment provider.
7. **Provide any additional medical history information**: If there's anything else you'd like to add, please write it down.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to get in touch with you efficiently.
* **Client Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical information that may affect your treatment.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_details`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of treatment you are undergoing (Lip Blushing, Enhancements, or Revisions).
* **Lip Blushing Details** (`lip_blushing_details`, select_multiple, required: false): If you selected Lip Blushing, choose the location of the blushing, size of the area, type of blushing desired, and color of the blushing desired.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`, text, required: false): If you have any allergies, please provide details.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, please inform the treatment provider.
* **Medical History Information** (`medical_history_info`, text, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to add, please write it down.

## Tips

* Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best treatment outcome.
* If you have already completed this form, please do not submit it again as it may cause duplication of effort and data loss.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the treatment provider.
