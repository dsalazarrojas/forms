# yoga_class_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoga_class_release_form is a document designed to collect information from participants in a yoga class. It includes sections for participant details, liability consent, emergency contact, and signatures from both the participant and instructor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Participant Information**: Enter your name, contact details, and any relevant medical conditions or allergies in the "participant information" section.
2. **Acknowledge Liability Consent**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your understanding of the liability consent. If you select "Yes", you will be considered to have acknowledged the risks and responsibilities associated with participating in the yoga class.
3. **Specify Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the name and relationship of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency, and provide their contact details.
4. **Sign the Form**: Print your name and sign the "participant_signature" section to confirm your acknowledgment of the liability consent.
5. **Instructor Signature**: Print the instructor's name and sign their "instructor_signature" section to confirm their acknowledgment of your liability consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant information`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name and contact details in this section.
* **Liability Consent** (`liability_consent`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select "Yes" to acknowledge that you understand the risks and responsibilities associated with participating in the yoga class. Select "No" to indicate that you do not understand or wish to decline.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name and relationship of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false)
	+ Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false)
	+ Enter the date you are participating in the yoga class.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required: false)
	+ Print your name and sign this section to confirm your acknowledgment of the liability consent.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, note, required: false)
	+ Print the instructor's name and sign this section to confirm their acknowledgment of your liability consent.
