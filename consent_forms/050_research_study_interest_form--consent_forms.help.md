# Research Study Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Research Study Interest Form is designed to help us understand your interest in participating in research studies. This form is not required for actual participation, but it helps us gauge potential participants' interest and eligibility for studies in our organization. Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and does not guarantee participation in any study. We will review the information provided and contact potential participants if we have a study that matches their interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required information for your demographic details, study preferences, and contact information. You can select multiple options for study and research interest, and enter your contact email and phone number as we need to reach out to you for any follow-up questions or further discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Study Interest Form** (`participant_information`, text, optional): This field is for your general contact information. Please provide a brief description of your background and interests.
* **Research Study** (`research_study`, select_multiple, optional): Here, you can select which research studies you are interested in. Please choose all the studies that apply to your interests.
* **Interested** (`interested`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the research topics that you are interested in.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): This is your email address where we can contact you. Please ensure it is accurate and current.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): This is your phone number where we can contact you. Please provide a working number.
* **Date of Interest** (`date_of_interest`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind when you would like to participate in a research study, please provide it here.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or updates, please provide any additional information that would help us get in touch with you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Here, you can provide any additional comments or questions you have about the research studies or our organization. This will help us better understand your interests and concerns.

## Tips
If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us directly. We will review your submitted form and contact you if we have a study that matches your interests and qualifications.
