# Creative Direction Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Direction Interview Consent Form is a crucial document that helps ensure participants in a creative direction interview understand the terms of the project, including the use of recorded audio/video, participant agreement, and project use. This form helps maintain transparency, trust, and respect for all parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your project name in the "project name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "participant name" field.
3. Select the date and time of the interview in the "date" and "time" fields respectively.
4. Choose how you would like your recorded audio/video to be used in the "use of recorded audio/video" field.
5. Write a brief agreement in the "participant agreement" field, outlining your understanding of the project's terms and conditions.
6. Sign your name in the "participant signature" field, indicating your agreement to the project's terms.
7. Choose how you would like your recorded data to be used in the project in the "project use" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional information about the recording device used in the "recording device" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
* **participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the interview.
* **time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the interview.
* **use of recorded audio/video** (`use_of_recorded_audio_video`, select one, optional): Choose how you would like your recorded audio/video to be used. You can select "recorded audio/video", "audio only", "video only", or "none".
* **participant agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Write a brief agreement outlining your understanding of the project's terms and conditions.
* **participant signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Sign your name indicating your agreement to the project's terms.
* **project use** (`project_use`, select multiple, optional): Choose how you would like your recorded data to be used. You can select "all", "project use", "no commercial use", or "no project use".
* **recording device** (`recording_device`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the recording device used.
* **other project name** (`project_name_other`, text, optional): If your project is part of a larger project, provide the name of that project.
