# employment-history-release-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employment history information from users. It's intended for employment purposes such as background checks, job applications, or employee onboarding processes.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, start by filling in your employment history in reverse chronological order, from the most recent job to the least recent.
- Review each section carefully to ensure you fill in the correct information.
- If you're unsure about any section, please ask for clarification.
