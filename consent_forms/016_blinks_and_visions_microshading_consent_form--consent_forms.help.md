# Blinks And Visions Microshading Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blinks And Visions Microshading Consent Form is a document that gathers essential information from clients prior to undergoing the microshading procedure. The form helps to ensure that clients are informed and aware of the process and potential outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the information provided carefully.
2. Fill out your personal details (Client name, Date of Birth, Emergency Contact).
3. Provide a brief description of the procedure you are undergoing.
4. Confirm that you have understood and acknowledged the potential risks and complications.
5. Give your permission for before and after photos to be taken for medical and portfolio purposes.
6. List any known allergies.
7. Provide your emergency contact information.
8. Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client name**: Please enter your full legal name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth for record keeping purposes.
* **Procedure description**: Briefly describe the microshading procedure you are undergoing.
* **Risks and complications acknowledged**: Confirm that you have understood and acknowledged the potential risks and complications associated with the procedure.
	+ Choose 'True' if you have read and understood the risks.
	+ Choose 'False' if you have not read and understood the risks.
* **Permission to take before and after photos**: Grant permission for before and after photos to be taken for medical and portfolio purposes.
	+ Choose 'True' if you grant permission.
	+ Choose 'False' if you do not grant permission.
* **Allergies**: List any known allergies to topical agents.
* **Emergency contact**: Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Consent signature name**: Enter your full name to confirm that you have read and understood the information provided.
* **Consent signature**: Sign your name to confirm that you have given your consent to undergo the microshading procedure.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood the information provided before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your technician or contact us directly.
* Keep in mind that your consent is required for the procedure to proceed.
