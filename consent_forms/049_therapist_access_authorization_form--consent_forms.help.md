# Therapist Access Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapist Access Authorization Form is a document that grants therapists temporary access to client information for the purpose of providing therapy services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Enter your therapist's name in the **Therapist Name** field.
3. Enter your contact email in the **Contact Email** field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field (if applicable).
5. Enter any additional authorization details in the **Authorization Details** field.
6. Confirm the date and time of your consent in the **Consent Date** and **Consent Time** fields.
7. Sign the **Client Signature** field to confirm your consent.
8. Sign the **Therapist Signature** field to confirm the therapist's access authorization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client for whom you are granting access to their information.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of the therapist who will have access to the client's information.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can reach you if needed.
* **Contact Phone**: If you would like to be contacted about your therapy schedule or other matters, enter your phone number here.
* **Authorization Details**: Enter any additional information that is necessary for the therapist to have access to your information (e.g., medical conditions, therapy goals).
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date on which you gave your consent for the therapist to access your information.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time at which you gave your consent for the therapist to access your information.
* **Client Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you have given your consent for the therapist to access your information.
* **Therapist Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you have authorized the therapist to access the client's information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you need to change any information, a new form should be submitted.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, do not hesitate to contact us.
