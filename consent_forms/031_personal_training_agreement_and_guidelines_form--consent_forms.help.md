# Personal Training Agreement And Guidelines Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Agreement And Guidelines Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of personal training sessions. It's designed to ensure that both the client and the trainer are on the same page regarding the training process, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Personal Training Agreement And Guidelines Form** (Page 1)
    * This is the introductory page of the form, which summarizes the purpose of the form and provides an overview of the training agreement.
2.  **Client Information** (Page 2)
    * Enter your name and other relevant details as required.
3.  **Client Contact Information** (Page 3)
    * Enter your contact information, including email address and phone number.
4.  **Training Agreement** (Page 4)
    * Select either "I have read and agreed to the terms of the personal training agreement" or "I have not read and agreed to the terms of the personal training agreement."
5.  **Emergency Contact Information** (Page 5)
    * Enter the contact information for an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, email address, and address.
6.  **Alternative Emergency Contact Information** (Page 6)
    * Enter additional emergency contact information in case the primary contact is unavailable.
