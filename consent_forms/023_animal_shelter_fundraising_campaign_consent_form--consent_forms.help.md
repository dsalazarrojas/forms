# Animal Shelter Fundraising Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and information from participants in an animal shelter fundraising campaign. It is essential to ensure that your data is protected and that you are aware of how it will be used in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
2. Select the campaign name and date you are participating in.
3. Choose your participation type (e.g., Event Participant, Volunteer, Team Captain, Sponsor, Donor, Social Media Ambassador, or Other).
4. If participating on a team, enter the team name.
5. Set your personal fundraising goal (if applicable).
6. Choose whether you consent to the use of your photos, videos, and name in campaign materials.
7. Select whether you consent to the sharing of your testimonial or story.
8. If you consent to social media tagging, provide your social media handle.
9. Choose whether you consent to the use of your data for campaign purposes.
10. Select whether you agree to share your information with campaign partners.
11. Indicate whether you consent to future contact from the campaign.
12. Accept the liability waiver.
13. Confirm your age and provide your guardian's name (if under 18).
14. Sign electronically.
15. Date and sign the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city of residence.
* **State or Province**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Campaign Name**: Select the campaign you are participating in.
* **Campaign Date**: Select the date of the campaign you are participating in.
* **Type of Participation**: Choose how you will participate in the campaign (e.g., Event Participant, Volunteer, Team Captain, Sponsor, Donor, Social Media Ambassador, or Other).
* **Team Name**: If participating on a team, enter the team name.
* **Personal Fundraising Goal**: Set your personal fundraising goal (if applicable).
* **May We Use Photos of You for Campaign Promotion**: Choose whether you consent to the use of your photos in campaign materials.
* **May We Use Video Footage of You**: Choose whether you consent to the use of your videos in campaign materials.
* **May We Use Your Name in Campaign Materials**: Choose whether you consent to the use of your name in campaign materials (donor lists, participant recognition, etc.).
* **May We Share Your Testimonial or Story**: Choose whether you consent to sharing your testimonial or story.
* **May We Tag or Mention You on Social Media**: Choose whether you consent to social media tagging.
* **Social Media Handle**: If you consent to social media tagging, provide your social media handle.
* **Data Usage Consent**: Choose whether you consent to the use of your data for campaign purposes.
* **May We Share Your Information with Campaign Partners**: Choose whether you agree to share your information with campaign partners.
* **May We Contact You About Future Campaigns**: Choose whether you consent to future contact from the campaign.
* **Liability Waiver**: Accept the liability waiver to release the shelter from liability during campaign activities.
* **Age Confirmation**: Confirm that you are 18 years or older.
* **Guardian Name**: If under 18, enter the name of your guardian.
* **Guardian Signature**: If under 18, enter your guardian's full name.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign electronically.
* **Date of Consent**: Date and sign the consent form.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* Ensure that you understand how your data will be used in the campaign.
* If you are under 18, ensure that a parent or guardian signs and dates the consent form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the campaign team.
