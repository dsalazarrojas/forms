# Tennis Tournament Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tennis Tournament Release Form is a crucial document that ensures the safety and well-being of all participants in the tennis tournament. It collects essential information about each participant, including their name, contact details, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

* Carefully read the form instructions and guidelines.
* Fill out the form completely and truthfully.
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a tournament staff member for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.

### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact phone number, enter it here.

### Risk Acknowledgement
* **Risk Acknowledgement (Risk Acknowledgement)** (`risk_acknowledgement`, text, required): By signing this form, you acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with participating in the tennis tournament. Please check the box to confirm your understanding.

### Parental Consent
* **Parental Consent (Parental Consent)** (`parental_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Parental Consent
	+ No Parental Consent
	+ Other (please specify)

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation in the tournament, list them here.

### Medication History
* **Medication History (Medication History)** (`medication_history`, text, optional): If you have any medical history or are taking any medications that may impact your participation in the tournament, list them here.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

### Emergency Contact Relationship
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship)** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any concerns or questions, ask a tournament staff member for assistance.
* This form is a mandatory requirement for all participants in the tennis tournament.
