# telehealth_consent_form_colorado - Help Guide
## Purpose
The telehealth_consent_form_colorado form is a collection of fields designed to gather user information and consent for telehealth services specifically for users located in the state of Colorado.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as required. You can refer to the field-by-field explanations below for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **telehealth_consent_form_colorado** (text)
This field is the title of the form, which should remain unchanged.

### 2. **user_name** (text, Optional)
Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.

### 3. **user_email** (email, Optional)
Enter your email address where you can be contacted for telehealth services.

### 4. **user_phone** (text, Optional)
Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for telehealth services.

### 5. **state** (select_one, Required)
Select your state of residence. Please choose "Colorado" to continue.

### 6. **services** (select_multiple, Optional)
Select the telehealth services you are interested in. Choose from "telehealth services" or "in-person services" options.

### 7. **user_terms** (select_one, Optional)
Please select "accept" to acknowledge the terms and conditions of telehealth services.

### 8. **terms_agreed** (note, Optional)
This field is for your confirmation of the terms and conditions.

### 9. **user_signature** (text, Optional)
Enter your signature as proof of consent.
