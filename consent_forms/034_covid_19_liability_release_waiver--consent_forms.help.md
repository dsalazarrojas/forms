# COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

The COVID 19 Liability Release Waiver form is a legal document that protects the participant's and the organization's rights and liabilities during activities or events related to COVID-19. It gathers essential information about the participant's identity, activity details, and health status to ensure a safe and secure environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Step 1:** Start by entering your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
    *   Enter your full name accurately.
    *   Select the correct date of birth.
    *   Enter your street address.
    *   Enter your city, state, and zip code.
    *   Enter your phone number.
    *   Enter a valid email address.
*   **Step 2:** Provide your emergency contact information.
    *   Enter the name of your emergency contact.
    *   Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Step 3:** Select the correct type of activity or event.
    *   Enter the date of the activity or event.
    *   Enter the location of the activity or event.
*   **Step 4:** Confirm your understanding of COVID-19 risks and transmission.
    *   Select "Yes, I Understand" if you understand the risks of COVID-19 exposure.
    *   Select "No, I Do Not Understand" if you need clarification or have concerns.
*   **Step 5:** Confirm your health status.
    *   Select "Yes, No Symptoms" if you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
    *   Select "No, I Have Symptoms" if you experience any COVID-19 symptoms.
*   **Step 6:** Confirm your COVID-19 exposure status.
    *   Select "Yes, No Exposure" if you have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
    *   Select "No, I Was Exposed" if you were exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
*   **Step 7:** Confirm your vaccination status.
    *   Select "Fully Vaccinated" if you have completed the full vaccination series.
    *   Select "Partially Vaccinated" if you have received partial vaccination.
    *   Select "Not Vaccinated" if you have not received any vaccination.
*   **Step 8:** Confirm your understanding of the liability release.
    *   Select "I Agree" to release and hold harmless the organization from liability.
    *   Select "I Do Not Agree" if you have concerns or need clarification.
*   **Step 9:** Confirm your agreement to indemnify and defend the organization.
    *   Select "I Agree" to indemnify and defend the organization.
    *   Select "I Do Not Agree" if you have concerns or need clarification.
*   **Step 10:** Finally, enter the date of the agreement.

Please review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the organization representative for clarification.
