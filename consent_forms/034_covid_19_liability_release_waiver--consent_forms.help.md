## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name accurately. This is required information for your participation in the activity or event.
*   **Date of Birth**: Select the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). This will help the organization understand your age and ensure your safety.
*   **Street Address**: Enter your full street address. This is required for contact and location information.
*   **City, State, Zip**: Enter your city, state, and zip code. This will help the organization confirm your location.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This is required for emergency contact and communication purposes.
*   **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication and notification.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
*   **Type of Activity or Event**: Select the correct type of activity or event. This will help the organization understand your participation.
*   **Activity Date**: Select the date of the activity or event. This will help the organization confirm your participation.
*   **Activity Location**: Enter the location of the activity or event. This will help the organization confirm your location.
*   **I understand risks of COVID-19 exposure**: Select "Yes, I Understand" if you understand the risks of COVID-19 exposure. Select "No, I Do Not Understand" if you need clarification or have concerns.
*   **I understand transmission and symptoms**: Select "True" if you understand the transmission and symptoms of COVID-19. Select "False" if you need clarification or have concerns.
*   **Facility takes reasonable precautions**: Select "True" if you understand that the facility takes reasonable precautions for your safety. Select "False" if you have concerns.
*   **No COVID-19 symptoms**: Select "Yes, No Symptoms" if you do not have any COVID-19 symptoms.
*   **No known COVID-19 exposure in past 14 days**: Select "Yes, No Exposure" if you have not been exposed to COVID-19 in the past 14 days.
*   **Vaccination Status**: Select "Fully Vaccinated" if you have completed the full vaccination series. Select "Partially Vaccinated" if you have received partial vaccination. Select "Not Vaccinated" if you have not received any vaccination.
*   **I release and hold harmless the organization**: Select "I Agree" to release and hold harmless the organization from liability. Select "I Do Not Agree" if you have concerns or need clarification.
*   **I agree to indemnify and defend the organization**: Select "I Agree" to indemnify and defend the organization. Select "I Do Not Agree" if you have concerns or need clarification.
*   **Date of Agreement**: Enter the date of the agreement in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
