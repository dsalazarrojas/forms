# survey_publication_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect information about surveys, focusing on authorization and participant details for publication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (those marked as "required") with the necessary information.
2. Enter the survey publication information in the corresponding fields (e.g., Survey Publication Authorization 1, Survey Publication Authorization 2, etc.).
3. Provide participant information, including their name and any other relevant details.
4. Enter the publication date and time (if applicable).
5. Indicate the publication location.
6. Specify the method of publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Publication Authorization 1**: This field is used to describe the purpose and scope of the survey publication.
  - **Survey Publication Authorization 2**: Similar to Survey Publication Authorization 1, but for further details or clarifications.
- **Participant Information**: Enter the participant's name, role, or any other relevant details.
- **Publication Date**: Enter the date when the survey was published or will be published.
- **Time**: Enter the time when the survey was published or will be published (if applicable).
- **Publication Location**: Enter the location where the survey was published or will be published.
- **Survey Publication Authorization 3**: Similar to Survey Publication Authorization 1 and 2, but for further authorization or clarification.
- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who provided the survey data or consent.
- **Publication Method**: Describe the method through which the survey was published, such as print, online, or in-person.
