# Procedure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information related to a procedure, including potential medical risks and benefits. It is designed to obtain patient consent before proceeding with the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your consent is valid.
2. Start by providing your **Patient Name**.
3. List all **Medical Risks** associated with the procedure.
4. Describe the **Benefits** of the procedure.
5. Provide your **Healthcare Provider Name**.
6. Enter the **Procedure Date** and **Procedure Time** (if applicable).
7. Enter the **Facility Name** where the procedure will take place.
8. Sign the **Physician Signature** to confirm that you have been informed about the procedure and its associated risks.
9. Finally, sign the **Patient Signature** to confirm that you understand and agree to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Procedure Consent Form** (`procedure_consent_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Medical Risks** (`medical_risks`): Please list all possible risks associated with the procedure. Be as detailed as possible to help the healthcare provider inform you about potential complications.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`): Describe the benefits of the procedure. This will help you understand what you can expect from the procedure.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare provider performing the procedure.
* **Procedure Date** (`procedure_date`): Enter the date of the procedure (if known).
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`): Enter the time of the procedure (if known).
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility where the procedure will take place.
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`): The healthcare provider must sign this field to confirm that you have been informed about the procedure and its associated risks.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): You must sign this field to confirm that you understand and agree to the procedure.
* **Procedure Time** (`procedure_time`): This field is not required but helps the healthcare provider schedule the procedure.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): This field is not required but helps the healthcare provider know the location of the procedure.

## Tips

* Please take your time to carefully read and understand the form before signing it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* Ensure that your signatures and information are accurate and complete.
