# COVID 19 Salon Venus Nails Bar Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Salon Venus Nails Bar Consent Form is a mandatory form required for all customers entering the salon during the pandemic. The form helps protect the safety of both customers and staff by gathering relevant health information and obtaining consent for treatment.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and phone number in the first field.
2. Select the type of nail service you want to receive from the options provided.
3. Answer the question about your current health status.
4. Confirm whether you or anyone in your household has been sick in the last 14 days.
5. Confirm you will follow the salon's safety protocols and take on the associated risks.
6. Select your appointment time.
7. Finally, confirm that you certify all information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Full Name** (`customer_name_full`): Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed in our records.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_no_val`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of emergencies or to confirm your appointment.
* **Nail Service Type** (`nail_service_cat`): Select the type of nail service you want to receive, such as Manicure, Pedicure, Gel/Acrylic, Nail Art, or Removal.
* **Temperature Reading (F)** (`temp_reading_val`): This field is not relevant in this context, as the form is not collecting temperature data.
* **Current Health Status** (`health_status_cat`): Answer honestly about your current health status, which can be Feeling Great, Minor cold symptoms, or Recovering from illness.
* **Recent Illness** (`recent_illness_yn`): Confirm whether you or anyone in your household has been sick in the last 14 days.
* **Venus Nails Bar Safety Protocols** (`venus_safety_agree`): Confirm you will follow the salon's safety protocols to minimize the risk of COVID-19 transmission.
* **Risk Assumption Acknowledgement** (`risk_assumption_ack`): Confirm you understand and take on the risks associated with nail treatment during the pandemic.
* **Hand Hygiene** (`wash_hands_agree`): Confirm you agree to wash or sanitize your hands before treatment begins.
* **Appointment Time** (`appt_time_val`): Enter your scheduled appointment time.
* **Certification of Accuracy** (`venus_consent_cert`): Confirm that all information provided is accurate.
