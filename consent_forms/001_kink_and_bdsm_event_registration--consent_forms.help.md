# Kink And BDSM Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants in kink and BDSM events. Event organizers use this form to gather relevant details about the event and its attendees, including payment options and agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Event Details** section with the relevant information about the event.
2.  Provide your **Participant Details** in the designated field.
3.  Select one or more **Payment Options** that apply to you.
4.  Read and agree to the **Agreement** terms and conditions.
5.  Optionally, add any additional **Notes** you'd like to share with the event organizers.
6.  Select the **Event Organizer** responsible for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Participant Details**: Provide your personal details, including name, contact information, and any relevant identifiers.
* **Payment Options**:
  + Option 1: This is an option for payment.
  + Option 2: This is another option for payment.
  + Option 3: This is the third payment option.
* **Agreement**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or messages you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Event Organizer**: Select the person or organization responsible for organizing the event.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication with the event organizers.
* Review the agreement carefully before submitting to understand your responsibilities and expectations.
