# mold_remediation_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect client consent and information for mold remediation services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name as the Client Name.
3. Enter your email as the Client Email.
4. Enter the Property Address where the remediation will take place.
5. Select the Remediation Type (Yes or No).
6. Enter the Remediation Date and Remediation Time.
7. Select the Remediation Status (Active or Inactive).
8. Enter any Comments regarding the remediation.
9. Enter the Assigned to person or team responsible for the remediation.
10. The form will automatically fill out the Created At field with the current date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter your name as the client.
* **Client Email** (Client Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Property Address** (Property Address, text, required): Enter the address of the property where the remediation will take place.
* **Remediation Type** (Remediation Type, select_one, required): Select Yes if you consent to remediation, No otherwise.
* **Remediation Date** (Remediation Date, date, required): Enter the date of the remediation.
* **Remediation Time** (Remediation Time, time, required): Enter the time of the remediation.
* **Remediation Status** (Remediation Status, select_multiple, required): Select Active or Inactive to indicate the current status of the remediation.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required): Enter any additional comments regarding the remediation.
* **Assigned to** (Assigned to, text, required): Enter the person or team responsible for the remediation.
* **Created At** (Created At, text, required): This field is automatically filled out by the system and indicates when the form was created.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Only submit the form if you are the actual client or authorized representative.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the assigned person or team.
