# Odissi Dance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Odissi Dance Registration Form is used to register individuals for the Odissi dance program. This form requires personal details, emergency contact information, and dance program preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
*   Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
*   Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
*   Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   Enter the name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
*   Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
*   Select the relationship of the emergency contact person from the "Relationship" field.
*   If the relationship is "Other," enter the specific relationship in the "Other (specify)" field.
*   Select your classes preferences from the "Classes" field.
*   If you have other classes you would like to be considered, enter them in the "Other Classes" field.
*   Select your workshop preferences from the "Workshops" field.
*   If you have other workshops you would like to be considered, enter them in the "Other Workshops" field.
*   Select your ongoing programs preferences from the "Ongoing Programs" field.
*   If you have other ongoing programs you would like to be considered, enter them in the "Other Ongoing Programs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person.
*   **Other (specify)** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, required): Enter the specific relationship if "Other" is selected.
*   **Classes** (`classes`, select_multiple, required): Select your classes preferences.
*   **Other Classes** (`classes_other`, text, required): Enter other classes you would like to be considered.
*   **Workshops** (`classes_workshops`, select_multiple, required): Select your workshop preferences.
*   **Other Workshops** (`classes_workshops_other`, text, optional): Enter other workshops you would like to be considered.
*   **Ongoing Programs** (`ongoing_programs`, select_multiple, required): Select your ongoing programs preferences.
*   **Other Ongoing Programs** (`ongoing_programs_other`, text, optional): Enter other ongoing programs you would like to be considered.
