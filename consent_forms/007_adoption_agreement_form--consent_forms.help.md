# Adoption Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Adoption Agreement Form is a legally binding document that outlines the terms and conditions of adopting an animal from a shelter. It ensures the adopter understands their responsibilities and commitment to providing proper care and treatment for the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the adoption terms**: Carefully read each term on page 1, and select "I agree" to confirm your understanding.
2. Fill in your information**: On page 2, enter your full name and date as an electronic signature.
3. Confirm your payment status**: On page 3, select the status of your adoption fee payment.
4. Provide animal details**: On page 4, enter the identifier or name of the animal you are adopting.
5. Submit the form**: Once all fields are filled out, submit the form to finalize the adoption.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Adoption Terms and Conditions --**: This is a page where you will find the terms and conditions of adopting an animal. Please read each term carefully and select "I agree" to confirm your understanding.
- **I agree to provide adequate food, water, and shelter.** (`term_1`, note, required: false): This term ensures you understand your responsibility to provide basic needs for the animal.
- **I agree to keep the animal as a household pet.** (`term_2`, note, required: false): This term confirms you will not use the animal for research or fighting, and it will be a part of your household.
- **I agree to provide regular veterinary care.** (`term_3`, note, required: false): This term highlights your commitment to regular veterinary check-ups and care for the animal's health.
- **I agree the animal will not be used for research or fighting.** (`term_4`, note, required: false): This term ensures the animal will not be used for any activities that may harm the animal.
- **I agree to return the animal to the shelter if I can no longer care for it.** (`term_5`, note, required: false): This term guarantees the animal's return to the shelter if you are unable to provide care for it.
- **Acceptance of Terms** (`acceptance_of_terms`, select_one, required: true): Select "I agree" to confirm your understanding and commitment to the terms and conditions.
- **Adopter Signature Name** (`adopter_signature_name`, text, required: true): Enter your full name as an electronic signature.
- **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the adoption agreement.
- **Animal ID or Name** (`animal_id_or_name`, text, required: true): Enter the identifier or name of the animal you are adopting.
- **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of your adoption fee payment.
