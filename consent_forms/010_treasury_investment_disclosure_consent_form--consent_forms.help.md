# Treasury Investment Disclosure Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain investment disclosure consent from investors for treasury investments. The form is used to document the investor's understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the investment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the investor's name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the correct investment type and subtype from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct form status from the options provided.
4. Sign the form digitally.
5. Select the date and time of consent.
6. Confirm that you have submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the form.
* **Investor Name** (`investor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the investor.
* **Investor Email** (`investor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the investor.
* **Investor Phone** (`investor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the investor.
* **Investor Address** (`investor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the investor.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required): Enter the date of consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required): Enter the time of consent.
* **Consent Form Type** (`consent_form_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consent form.
* **Other Consent Form Type** (`other_consent_form_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other type of consent form not listed.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign the form digitally.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required): Enter the time of signature.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, text, required): Confirm that the form has been submitted.
* **Investor Investment Type** (`investor_investment_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of investment made.
* **Investor Investment Subtype** (`investor_investment_subtype`, select_multiple, optional): Select any subtypes of the investment.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the form.
* **Other Form Status** (`other_form_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other status of the form not listed.
* **Form Submitted** (`form_submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm that the form has been submitted.
* **Investor Investment Type Other** (`investor_investment_type_other`, text, optional): Enter any other investment type not listed.
* **Investor Investment Subtype Other** (`investor_investment_subtype_other`, text, optional): Enter any other investment subtype not listed.
* **Form Note** (`form_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the form.
* **Investor Consent** (`form_investor_consent`, select_one, required): Choose the type of investor consent.
* **Other Investor Consent** (`other_investor_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select any other type of investor consent not listed.
* **Form Investor Consent Note** (`form_investor_consent_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the investor consent.
* **Other Investor Consent Note** (`other_investor_consent_note`, text, optional): Enter any other note about the investor consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* Keep a record of the signed form for future reference.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
