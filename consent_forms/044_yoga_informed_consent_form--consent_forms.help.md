# Yoga Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a medical or wellness-related program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, following the instructions provided.
2. If any field is not applicable or not relevant, mark it as "N/A" or enter "Not Applicable".
3. If you're unsure about any field, contact the organization or healthcare professional responsible for the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter the participant's contact phone number. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, select_one, optional): Check the box to indicate that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Relationship to Participant** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship to the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Emergency Declaration** (`emergency_declaration`, text, optional): Describe any emergency declarations or conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that may impact participation in the program.
* **Medical Condition History** (`medical_condition_history`, text, optional): Describe your medical condition history.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address. If not applicable, leave blank.
* **Medical Supervision** (`medical_supervision`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes indicating whether you are under medical supervision and why.
* **Medical Supervision Reason** (`medical_supervision_reason`, text, optional): Describe the reason for medical supervision.
* **Emergency Contact 1** (`emergency_contact_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the first emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 1 Number** (`emergency_contact_1_number`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the first emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`emergency_contact_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 2 Number** (`emergency_contact_2_number`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 3** (`emergency_contact_3_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the third emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact 3 Number** (`emergency_contact_3_number`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the third emergency contact person.
* **Medical Supervision Acknowledgement** (`medical_supervision_ack`, text, optional): Check this box to acknowledge medical supervision requirements.
* **Medical Supervision 2** (`medical_supervision_2`, text, optional): Describe any medical supervision requirements or conditions.

Please ensure that you carefully review and complete this form to ensure the participant's safety and well-being. If you're unsure about any field or requirement, contact the organization or healthcare professional responsible for the program.
