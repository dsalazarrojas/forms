# Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment form is used to collect acknowledgment of the terms of enrollment for users. It is a crucial part of the enrollment process and ensures that users understand the terms and conditions of the programs they are enrolling in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the course id of the course you are enrolling in.
2. Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
3. Enter the date of the enrollment.
4. Enter the program id of the program you are enrolling in.
5. Enter the name of the program you are enrolling in.
6. Read and acknowledge the enrollment terms.
7. Enter the date and time of the acknowledgment.
8. Select the status of the acknowledgment.
9. Add any additional notes for the acknowledgment.
10. Enter the course id 2 if you are enrolling in a second course.
11. Enter the name of the second course.
12. Enter the program id 2 if you are enrolling in a second program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **course id** (`course_id`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the id of the course you are enrolling in.
- **course name** (`course_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
- **date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the enrollment.
- **program id** (`program_id`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the id of the program you are enrolling in.
- **program name** (`program_name`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the program you are enrolling in.
- **enrollment terms** (`enrollment_terms`, `note`, required/optional: false): Read and acknowledge the enrollment terms.
- **acknowledgement date** (`acknowledgement_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the acknowledgment.
- **acknowledgement time** (`acknowledgement_time`, `time`, required/optional: false): Enter the time of the acknowledgment.
- **acknowledgement status** (`acknowledgement_status`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select the status of the acknowledgment.
- **acknowledgement notes** (`acknowledgement_notes`, `note`, required/optional: false): Add any additional notes for the acknowledgment.
- **course id 2** (`course_id_2`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the id of the second course.
- **course name 2** (`course_name_2`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the second course.
- **program id 2** (`program_id_2`, `number`, required/optional: false): Enter the id of the second program.
