# Media Release And Personal Injury Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Release And Personal Injury Waiver form is a form designed to collect information about an incident, including the date and time of the injury, the location where the injury occurred, and the description of the injury.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form carefully and thoroughly, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately.
2. Enter your name and contact information (phone and email) in the designated fields.
3. Provide the date and time of the injury.
4. Describe the location where the injury occurred.
5. Give a detailed description of the injury.
6. Specify how the media usage of the injury will be handled.
7. If applicable, provide information about any medical conditions related to the injury.
8. If applicable, provide details about any witnesses, including their name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Release And Personal Injury Waiver**: This is the title of the form.
* **Name**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in this field.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **Date Of Injury**: Enter the date of the injury.
* **Time Of Injury**: Enter the time of the injury.
* **Location Of Injury**: Enter the location where the injury occurred.
* **Description Of Injury**: Describe the injury in detail.
* **Media Usage**: Specify how the media usage of the injury will be handled.
* **Release Of Liability**: Choose whether the media usage is for "Media usage" or "Both".
* **Medical Condition**: Check if there are any medical conditions related to the injury.
* **Witnesses**: Provide details about any witnesses, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Witness1**: Enter the first witness's first name.
* **Witness1 Last Name**: Enter the first witness's last name.
* **Witness1 Email**: Enter the first witness's email address.
* **Witness1 Phone**: Enter the first witness's phone number.
* **Witness2**: Enter the second witness's first name.
* **Witness2 Last Name**: Enter the second witness's last name.
* **Witness2 Email**: Enter the second witness's email address.
* **Witness2 Phone**: Enter the second witness's phone number.
* **Witness3**: Enter the third witness's first name.
* **Witness3 Last Name**: Enter the third witness's last name.
* **Witness3 Email**: Enter the third witness's email address.
* **Witness3 Phone**: Enter the third witness's phone number.
* **Witness4**: Enter the fourth witness's first name.
* **Witness4 Last Name**: Enter the fourth witness's last name.
* **Witness4 Email**: Enter the fourth witness's email address.
* **Witness4 Phone**: Enter the fourth witness's phone number.
