# Artistic Workshop Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent from workshop participants for filming and photography during Artistic Workshops. It's essential to understand that their participation implies consent to be filmed and photographed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have a valid email address to submit the form.
- Carefully review each field and provide accurate information.
- Fill in all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`, Text, *)
    Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, Text, *)
    Enter your surname as it appears on official documents.
- **Email Address** (`participant_email`, Email, *)
    Enter your email address, which we'll use to communicate about workshop-related matters and consent details.
- **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, Text, Optional)
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes, but this is not required.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional)
    Enter your date of birth to verify your age. This is not required but recommended for workshop organizers.
- **Workshop Name or Title** (`workshop_name`, Text, *)
    Enter the name of the workshop where you're participating, as given by the workshop organizers.
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, Date, *)
    Enter the date when you'll be attending the workshop.
- **Type of Filming or Photography** (`filming_type`, Select Multiple, *)
    Select all types of filming or photography where you're comfortable being captured:
    - **Photography**: Taking pictures of you or your activities.
    - **Video Recording**: Recording your voice, actions, or movements.
    - **Audio Recording**: Capturing your voice or sound.
    - **Live Streaming**: Live broadcasting of the event or your activities.
    - **Documentary Style**: Recording a story or event in a more documentary-like style.
- **Filming Location Within Workshop** (`filming_location`, Text, Optional)
    Specify where during the workshop you're comfortable being filmed or photographed, such as "Full workshop" or "Certain activities only".
- **Intended Usage of Footage** (`footage_usage`, Select Multiple, *)
    Choose how you'd like your recorded material to be used:
    - **Educational Materials**: For educational or training purposes.
    - **Social Media**: For social media platforms.
    - **Marketing Promotion**: For promotional purposes.
    - **Documentary**: For documentary-style content creation.
    - **Other**: Specify in the "Additional Conditions or Restrictions" field.
- **I Consent to Image and Voice Recording** (`image_release_consent`, Select One, *)
    Choose whether you consent to be recorded:
    - **Yes**: You give your consent.
    - **No**: You do not give your consent, or you're not sure.
- **Permission to Share on Social Media** (`social_media_usage`, Select One, *)
    Choose how you'd like to be shared on social media:
    - **Yes - With Name**: Your name will be included with the posted content.
    - **Yes - Anonymous Only**: Your name won't be included.
    - **No**: Your image or voice will not be shared on social media.
- **Permission for Commercial or Promotional Use** (`commercial_usage`, Select One, *)
    Choose whether you consent to commercial use:
    - **Yes**: You give your consent for commercial use.
    - **No**: You do not give your consent.
    - **Certain Uses Only**: Specify in the "Additional Conditions or Restrictions" field.
- **Preference for Credits or Attribution** (`credit_preference`, Text, Optional)
    Enter your preference for giving credits or attribution for your image or voice.
- **I May Request a Copy of Footage** (`footage_copy`, Select One, Optional)
    Choose whether you'd like to request a copy of recorded material:
    - **Yes**: You'd like a copy of the recording.
    - **No**: You do not wish to have a copy.
- **Compensation for Image Use** (`compensation`, Select One, Optional)
    Choose whether you want compensation for image use:
    - **No Compensation**: You don't want compensation.
    - **Complimentary Workshop**: You'd like a complimentary workshop or service.
    - **Other**: Specify your preferred compensation in the "Additional Conditions or Restrictions" field.
- **I Can Withdraw Consent at Any Time** (`withdrawal_option`, Select One, *)
    Choose whether you can withdraw consent at any time:
    - **Yes**: You can withdraw your consent at any time.
    - **No**: You do not want to withdraw your consent.
- **Additional Conditions or Restrictions** (`additional_conditions`, Text, Optional)
    Enter any additional conditions or restrictions you'd like to specify.
- **Date of Consent** (`signature_date`, Date, *)
    Enter the date when you're giving consent, which will be the date of this form.
- **Parental Consent Required (if under 18)** (`parental_consent_if_minor`, Select One, *)
    Choose whether you're under 18 or your parent/guardian has given consent:
    - **Yes - Parent Signature Provided**: A parent or guardian has given their signature.
    - **No - Participant is 18+**: You are 18 or older and do not need parental consent.
- **Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required)
    Sign and date this form to confirm your consent.
