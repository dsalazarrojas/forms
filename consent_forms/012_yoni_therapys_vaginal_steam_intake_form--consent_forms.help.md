# yoni Therapys Vaginal Steam Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient information, medical history, treatment details, and consent for vaginal steam therapy. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible care and treatment from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" section.
3. Provide details about your treatment in the "Treatment Details" section.
4. Select the correct consent options in the "Consent" section.
5. Enter the date of your consent in the "Consent Date" section (required).
6. Enter any relevant information about your vaginal steam treatment in the "Steam Information" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (1, Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **Medical History** (2, Text, Optional): Describe any past medical experiences, allergies, or conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Treatment Details** (3, Text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the vaginal steam treatment you are interested in, including any specific areas of focus or concerns.
* **Consent** (4, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose one or more of the following consent options:
	+ Patient: I understand and consent to the treatment as a patient.
	+ Provider: I consent to the treatment as a healthcare provider (if applicable).
	+ Other: I consent to the treatment as a third party (if applicable).
* **Consent Date** (5, Date, Required): Enter the date you are consenting to the treatment.
* **Steam Information** (6, Text, Optional): Enter any relevant information about your vaginal steam treatment, such as the frequency or duration of treatment.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure the best possible care and treatment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for guidance.
* This form is only intended for patients who have been referred to yoni Therapys for treatment.
