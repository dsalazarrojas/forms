# skin_tightening_ultrasound_therapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The skin_tightening_ultrasound_therapy_consent_form is a consent form that helps healthcare professionals obtain informed consent from clients before performing skin tightening ultrasound therapy. It ensures that clients understand the treatment, its potential risks and benefits, and their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Health Details (page 1).
2. Select the Skin Tightening Procedure that you wish to undergo (page 2).
3. Confirm whether you understand and acknowledge the treatment (page 3).
4. Sign the form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Health Details** (`client_health_details`, `text`, required: false): This form is designed to capture any relevant medical history or information about the client's health that is relevant to the treatment. Please provide any relevant health details, such as medical conditions, allergies, or medications.
* **Skin Tightening Procedure** (`skin_tightening_procedure`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select the type of skin tightening procedure you wish to undergo. Options are:
	+ Skin Tightening
	+ Skin Reshaping
* **Treatment Acknowledgments** (`treatment_acknowledgments`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please confirm that you have understood and will adhere to the following:
	+ Yes, I have read and understood the treatment and its potential risks and benefits.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): This is a required field for the healthcare professional to obtain your signature as a form of consent. Please sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form and are willing to undergo the treatment.
