# Anti Steering Disclosure Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from borrowers about their loan options and understanding of the loan process. It helps maintain transparency and accountability in the loan process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field as instructed, providing accurate and honest information about your loan options and understanding.
2. If you have a co-borrower, please fill out their information as well.
3. Read the **Steering Disclosure Statement** and ensure you understand its contents.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm you have read and understood the disclosure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disclosure Date**: Date of disclosure, the date you received the loan options and information.
* **Borrower Full Name**: Your primary name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Co-Borrower Name** (if applicable): If you have a co-borrower, enter their name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Property Address**: Address of the secured property.
* **Loan Amount**: Principal loan amount.
* **Loan Purpose**: Select one of the options to indicate what the loan is for (e.g. Purchase, Refinance, etc.).
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g. Single family home, Condominium, etc.).
* **Occupancy Type**: Select how the property will be used (e.g. Primary residence, Secondary residence, etc.).
* **Lender Name**: Name of the lending institution.
* **Loan Officer Name**: Name of the loan officer.
* **Loan Officer NMLS ID**: NMLS identification number of the loan officer.
* **Steering Disclosure Statement**: Read and acknowledge understanding of the disclosure.
* **Number of Loan Options Presented**: Number of loan options provided.
* **Loan Options Details**: Brief description of each loan option.
* **Loan Selected by Borrower**: Select the loan you chose.
* **Reason for Selection**: Select the reason you chose this loan.
* **Other Factors** (if applicable): If you chose another reason not listed, describe it here.
* **Loan Officer Compensation Disclosure**: Select if compensation was explained.
* **Does Compensation Vary by Loan Option**: Select if compensation varies by loan option.
* **Borrower Understanding**: Confirm if you understand the disclosure.
* **Did You Ask Questions**: Select if you were able to ask questions.
* **Voluntary Selection**: Confirm if your selection was made voluntarily.
* **Additional Comments** (if applicable): Any other remarks about the process.
* **Borrower Signature**: Digital signature of the borrower.
* **Signature Date**: Date signed.
* **Co-Borrower Signature** (if applicable): Digital signature of the co-borrower.
* **Loan Officer Signature**: Digital signature of the loan officer.
* **Witness Name** (if applicable): Name of the witness.
* **Witness Signature** (if applicable): Signature of the witness.
