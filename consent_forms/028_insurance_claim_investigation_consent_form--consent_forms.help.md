## Insurance Claim Investigation Consent Form - Help Guide

**Purpose**
The "Insurance Claim Investigation Consent Form" is a document used to gather information and obtain consent from policyholders for insurance claim investigations. This form helps ensure that all parties involved in the investigation have a clear understanding of the process and their rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the policyholder's name in the "Policyholder Name" field.
2. Enter the policyholder's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Describe the claim details in the "Claim Details" field.
5. Provide any additional investigation details in the "Investigation Details" field.
6. Sign the form to confirm your agreement and date/time stamp it in the "Signature" field.
7. Select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agreement" field.
8. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the "Comments" field (optional).
9. Date and time stamp the form in the "Date" field.
10. Choose the correct category for the form (e.g., "Consent Forms", "Insurance Forms") in the "Category" field.
11. Provide the assigned tool or device used for the investigation in the "Assigned Tool" field (optional).
12. Specify the output file or document generated from this form in the "Output File" field (optional).
13. Enter the form ID or number (optional).
14. Provide a brief title for the form (optional).
15. Enter any additional category information (optional).
16. Specify the name of the insurer (optional).
17. Enter any additional insurer information (optional).
