# Emotional Resilience Program Filming Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from participants in the emotional resilience program filming process, ensuring their rights and obligations are understood.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and answer truthfully to all questions.
2.  Ensure that you understand each field's purpose and implications.
3.  If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Permission** (`participant_permission`, text, optional): This field asks for the participant's permission to be filmed. Please enter a brief statement indicating whether or not the participant has given their permission.
*   **Use of Recorded Data** (`use_of_recorded_data`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: 'Yes', indicating that the participant consents to the use of recorded data.
*   **Filming Participants** (`filming_participants`, select multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Participant, Instructor, Both. This field determines who will be filmed during the program.
*   **Filming Location** (`filming_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the filming will take place.
*   **Filming Equipment** (`filming_equipment`, text, optional): Describe the equipment that will be used during the filming process.
*   **Recording Media** (`recording_media`, text, optional): Specify the type of recording media to be used, such as video, audio, or photo.
*   **Filming Purpose** (`filming_purpose`, text, optional): Clearly state the purpose of the filming, including the program goals and objectives.
*   **Filming Duration** (`filming_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the filming process.
*   **Filming Scheduling** (`filming_scheduling`, text, optional): Provide a schedule for the filming activities.
*   **Participant Privacy** (`participant_privacy`, text, optional): Describe the measures that will be taken to protect the participant's privacy during and after filming.
*   **Participant Photo Release** (`participant_photo_release`, text, optional): Ask for the participant's consent to be photographed or have their likeness captured.
*   **Participant Video Release** (`participant_video_release`, text, optional): Ask for the participant's consent to be filmed or have their video captured.
*   **Participant Audio Release** (`participant_audio_release`, text, optional): Ask for the participant's consent to have their voice or audio recorded.
*   **Filming Facilities** (`filming_facilities`, text, optional): Specify the facilities where filming will take place.
*   **Participant Notification** (`participant_notification`, text, optional): Describe the method of participant notification for filming activities.
*   **Filming Cancellation** (`filming_cancellation`, text, optional): Provide information on what happens in case filming is cancelled.
*   **Filming Completion** (`filming_completion`, text, optional): State what participants can expect upon completion of the filming process.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Have the participant sign a statement indicating they have understood and agreed to the filming terms.
*   **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth.
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Provide the participant's phone number.
*   **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address.
*   **Participant City, State** (`participant_city_state`, text, optional): Enter the participant's city and state.
*   **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postalcode`, text, optional): Enter the participant's postal code.
*   **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the submission process.
