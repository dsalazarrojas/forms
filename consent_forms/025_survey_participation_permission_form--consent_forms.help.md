# survey_participation_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The survey_participation_permission_form is used to collect users' consent for participation in surveys and record their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey participation form. Please provide a short description of your participation in surveys.
2. **Survey Name** (`survey_name`, text, optional): If you're participating in a specific survey, please provide its name.
3. **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current survey participation status. You can choose between 'True' and 'False'.
4. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select the contact methods you'd like to be contacted through. You can choose multiple options.
	* Phone
	* Email
	* Postal
	* Social Media
5. **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact.
6. **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you're available to participate.
7. **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you're available to participate.
8. **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.

## Tips

* Make sure to review your contact information and survey participation status carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Once you've submitted the form, you'll receive an email confirming your participation status.
