# Pilot Program Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilot Program Agreement Form is a crucial document that participants must complete to provide their details and acknowledge their understanding of the program's terms and conditions. It helps to establish a clear understanding of the program's expectations and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and truthfully.
3. Select all applicable timelines that apply to your participation in the program.
4. Write the acknowledgment in the provided field, confirming your understanding and agreement to the program's terms and conditions.
5. Fill in your submission date and time in the provided fields.
6. Enter the start and end dates of the program.
7. Specify the duration of your participation in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Agreement (1)** (`participant_agreement`, text, required): This field is where you must confirm your understanding and agreement to the program's terms and conditions. Please write down your acknowledgment in this field.
* **Timelines (2)** (`timelines`, select_multiple, required): Select all the timelines that apply to your participation in the program. This field is used to determine your availability and schedule for the program.
* **Submission Date (5)** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you submit this form.
* **Submission Time (6)** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time when you submit this form.
* **Start Date (7)** (`pilot_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program.
* **End Date (8)** (`pilot_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Duration (9)** (`program_duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of your participation in the program.
* **Participant Name (10)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (11)** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (12)** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
