# Advertisement Likeness Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for obtaining consent from an individual for the use of their likeness in advertisements. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the individual and specify the conditions under which their likeness can be used.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the individual information section with your legal name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Specify the authorized party and the permitted uses of your likeness.
3. Choose the geographic scope and duration of consent.
4. Indicate whether any compensation is provided for the use of your likeness.
5. Choose the media types for which you consent to be photographed or videoed.
6. Specify whether you consent to editing or modification of your likeness.
7. Choose whether your likeness can be used for commercial and/or non-commercial purposes.
8. Understand and agree to your right to revoke the consent.
9. Provide the revocation process details if you wish to revoke the consent.
10. Sign and date the consent form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Fill out your legal full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Provide your current residential address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Authorized Party** (`authorized_party`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization or individual granted permission.
* **Permitted Uses** (`permitted_uses`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the uses for which you consent to:
	+ Print advertisements
	+ Digital advertisements
	+ Social media
	+ Television commercials
	+ Radio advertisements
	+ Billboards
	+ Website
	+ Other (specify)
* **Geographic Scope** (`geographic_scope`, `select_one`, required): Choose where the likeness can be used:
	+ Local
	+ National
	+ International
	+ Online only
* **Duration of Consent** (`duration_of_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long the consent is valid:
	+ One-time use
	+ 6 months
	+ 1 year
	+ 2 years
	+ 3 years
	+ Perpetual
	+ Until revoked
* **Compensation Details** (`compensation_details`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether any compensation is provided for the use of your likeness:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not applicable
* **Photo/Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to photography and videography:
	+ Photos only
	+ Videos only
	+ Photos and videos
	+ Neither
* **Editing/Modification Consent** (`editing_modification_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you consent to editing or modification of your likeness:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Only minor edits
* **Commercial/Non-Commercial** (`commercial_noncommercial`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether your likeness can be used for commercial and/or non-commercial purposes:
	+ Commercial use
	+ Non-commercial use
* **Right to Revoke** (`right_to_revoke`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you understand and agree to your right to revoke the consent:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Revocation Process** (`revocation_process`, `text`, required): If you wish to revoke the consent, provide the revocation process details.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Sign and date the consent form to indicate your understanding of the conditions.
