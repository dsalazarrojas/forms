# Water Conservation Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Conservation Participation Consent Form is a tool used to collect information from participants on their water usage habits and conservation participation. This form helps us understand individual and community water usage patterns, which informs our conservation efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2.  Choose the relevant options for each field based on your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Water Conservation Participation Consent Form (Page 1)
**Water Conservation Participation Consent Form** (Required/Not Required: False)
This is the title of the form, which should not be edited.

### Participant Details (Page 2)
**Participant Details** (Required/Not Required: False)
Select all applicable options to indicate your participation status:
*   Option 1
*   Option 2
*   Option 3

### Conservation Programs (Page 3)
**Conservation Programs** (Required/Not Required: False)
Choose one of the conservation programs that you are participating in:
*   Program 1
*   Program 2
*   Program 3

### Date (Page 4)
**Date** (Required/Not Required: False)
Enter the date you are completing this form, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time (Page 5)
**Time** (Required/Not Required: False)
Enter the time you are completing this form, in the format 12:HH.

### Email (Page 6)
**Email** (Required/Not Required: False)
Enter your participant email address.

### Phone Number (Page 7)
**Phone Number** (Required/Not Required: False)
Enter your phone number.

### Water Use (Page 8)
**Water Use** (Required/Not Required: False)
Provide a numerical value representing your average daily water use, for example, 20 gallons.

### Water Quality (Page 9)
**Water Quality** (Required/Not Required: False)
Enter a brief description of your water use habits and any water quality concerns.

Note: If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, please contact our water conservation team.
