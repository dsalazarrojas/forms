# Tournament Photography And Filming Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants for photography and filming during a tournament. It helps to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the filming and photography that may take place during the event. This form is a mandatory part of the tournament's documentation and helps to protect the rights and interests of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field that apply to your situation. Make sure to carefully read the field descriptions and options below to ensure you understand the purpose and implications of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Permission** (`tournament_photography_and_filming_consent_form`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select the type of photography and filming that may take place during the tournament. Please choose "None" if you do not wish to participate in any photography or filming.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Parent Consent Given** (`parent_consent_given`, select_one, optional): If you are under the age of 18, please select "True" to indicate that your parent or guardian has given consent for you to participate in photography and filming.
* **Filming On Campus** (`filming_on_campus`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will take place on campus, and "False" otherwise.
* **Filming Outside Of Campus** (`filming_outside_of_campus`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will take place outside of campus, and "False" otherwise.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if photo release is applicable, and "False" otherwise.
* **Filming Duration** (`filming_duration`, time, optional): Enter the estimated duration of filming in [HH:MM:SS] format (e.g., 01:30:00).
* **Filming Participants** (`filming_participants`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of participants that will be filmed (e.g., "None", "Other"). If other, please specify in the "Filming Location Details" field below.
* **Filming Location** (`filming_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where filming will take place (e.g., "Tournament Field", "Other").
* **Filming Type** (`filming_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of filming that will take place (e.g., "None", "Other").
* **Filming Capture** (`filming_capture`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of filming capture that will take place (e.g., "None", "Other").
* **Filming Interview** (`filming_interview`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will involve an interview, and "False" otherwise.
* **Filming Speeches** (`filming_speeches`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will involve speeches, and "False" otherwise.
* **Filming Singing** (`filming_singing`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will involve singing, and "False" otherwise.
* **Filming Dance** (`filming_dance`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if filming will involve dance, and "False" otherwise.
* **Photo Release Date** (`photo_release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of photo release.
* **Photo Taken** (`photo_taken`, time, optional): Enter the estimated time of photo taken.
* **Filming Location Details** (`filming_location_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the filming location.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation in filming and photography.

Note: Please make sure to review and sign the form carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the tournament organizers.
