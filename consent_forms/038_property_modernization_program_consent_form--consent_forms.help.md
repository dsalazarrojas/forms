# Property Modernization Program Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Modernization Program Consent Form is used to gather information from property owners regarding their consent and details about their property for some sort of program or service. This form is designed to ensure that property owners understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the program or service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
	* Enter your name: This is your full name.
	* Enter your address: This is your property address.
	* Enter your phone number: This is your contact phone number.
	* Enter your email: This is your contact email address.
2. Provide property information:
	* Enter the type of property: This refers to the type of property you own (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
	* Enter the property address: This is the address where you own the property.
3. Share improvement needs:
	* Select the improvements needed for your property: Choose from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments: You can enter any other comments or information about your property.
5. Sign the consent:
	* Sign the consent form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the program or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details**
	+ **Owner Name**: Enter your full name as the property owner.
	+ **Owner Address**: Enter the address of the property you own.
	+ **Owner Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
	+ **Owner Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Property Info**
	+ **Property Type**: Enter the type of property you own (e.g., single-family home, condo, etc.).
	+ **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property.
* **Improvement Needs**
	+ **Improvement Needed**: Select the improvements needed for your property (e.g., repairs, renovations, etc.).
* **Signed Consent**
	+ **Signed Consent**: Sign this field to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the program or service.
* **Signed Date**
	+ **Signed Date**: Date on which you signed this consent form.
* **Signed Time**
	+ **Signed Time**: Time on which you signed this consent form.
* **Assigned Tool**
	+ **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to you for the program or service.
* **Owner Comments**
	+ **Owner Comments**: Add any additional comments about your property.
* **Property Comments**
	+ **Property Comments**: Add any additional comments about your property.
* **Signed By**
	+ **Signed By**: Enter the name of the person signing this consent form.
* **Signed Title**
	+ **Signed Title**: Enter the title of the person signing this consent form.
* **Signed Name**
	+ **Signed Name**: Enter your name as the person signing this consent form.
* **Signed Position**
	+ **Signed Position**: Enter your position or role as the person signing this consent form.
* **Signed Signature**
	+ **Signed Signature**: Sign this field with your signature.
* **Notes**
	+ **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your property.
* **Property Manager Comments**
	+ **Property Manager Comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the property manager.
* **Property Status**
	+ **Property Status**: Enter the current status of your property.
