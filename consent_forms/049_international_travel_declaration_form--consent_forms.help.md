# international_travel_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "international_travel_declaration_form" is used to declare international travel. This form captures essential details about the travel, including dates, destinations, purposes, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your travel details, including the destination and any other relevant information.
2. Select the purpose of your travel (Business, Personal, or Other).
3. Choose the type of travel (Domestic or International).
4. Enter any travel documents ID.
5. Provide flight information (if applicable).
6. Indicate whether you have any health issues.
7. Provide contact information and email (if available).
8. Identify any emergency contact information (if applicable).
9. Add any travel notes.
10. Indicate whether you are assigned a travel agent (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details** (text): Enter a brief description of your travel.
* **Travel Dates** (date): Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
* **Travel Purposes** (select multiple): Select one or more reasons for your travel (Business, Personal, or Other).
* **Travel Destination** (text): Enter the name of your travel destination.
* **Travel Type** (select one): Choose the type of travel (Domestic or International).
* **Travel Documents** (text): Enter any travel documents ID.
* **Travel Document ID** (number): Enter your travel documents ID (if applicable).
* **Flight Info** (date): Enter the flight information (if applicable).
* **Health Info** (select multiple): Indicate if you have any health issues (True or False).
* **Contact Info** (text): Enter contact information for yourself.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address (if available).
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Enter any emergency contact information (if applicable).
* **Travel Notes** (note): Add any additional travel notes.
* **Assigned Traveler** (select one): Indicate if you are assigned a traveler (True or False).
* **Assigned Traveler ID** (number): Enter the ID of the assigned traveler (if applicable).
* **Travel Agent** (select one): Indicate if you are assigned a travel agent (True or False).
