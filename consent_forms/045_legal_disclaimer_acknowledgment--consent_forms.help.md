# Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment form is used to collect legal disclaimers and acknowledgments from users for regulatory or contractual purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Information (ID 1).
2. Provide the required legal text for Legal Text 1 (ID 2).
3. Provide the required legal text for Legal Text 2 (ID 3).
4. Select one option from Checkbox 1 (ID 4).
5. Select multiple options from Checkbox 2 (ID 5).
6. Enter your Date (ID 6).
7. Provide your Email (ID 9).
8. If required, enter your Phone (ID 10).
9. Provide the required legal text for Text 1 (ID 11).
10. Provide the required legal text for Text 2 (ID 12).
11. Select one option from Checkbox 3 (ID 13).
12. Select multiple options from Checkbox 4 (ID 14).
13. Select multiple options from Checkbox 5 (ID 15).
14. Enter your additional Text 3 (ID 16).
15. If required, enter a Date (ID 17).
16. If required, enter your Email (ID 18).
17. Select one option from Select 1 (ID 19).
18. If not required, select one option from Select 2 (ID 20).
19. Enter your additional Text 4 (ID 21).
20. Enter your additional Text 5 (ID 22).
21. If not required, enter a Date (ID 23).
22. Enter your additional Text 6 (ID 24).
23. Select multiple options from Checkbox 6 (ID 25).

* **Date (ID 6)** (`date`, optional/optional): Enter your date.
* **Email (ID 9)** (`email`, optional/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (ID 10)** (`text`, optional/optional): If required, enter your phone number.
* **Text 1 (ID 11)** (`text`, optional/optional): Enter the required legal text for this section.
* **Text 2 (ID 12)** (`text`, optional/optional): Enter the required legal text for this section.
* **Checkbox 3 (ID 13)** (`select_one`, optional/optional): Select one option from this checkbox.
* **Checkbox 4 (ID 14)** (`select_multiple`, optional/optional): Select multiple options from this checkbox.
* **Checkbox 5 (ID 15)** (`select_multiple`, optional/optional): Select multiple options from this checkbox.
* **Text 3 (ID 16)** (`text`, optional/optional): Enter your additional text.
* **Date (ID 17)** (`date`, optional/optional): If required, enter a date.
* **Email (ID 18)** (`email`, optional/optional): If required, enter your email address.
* **Select 1 (ID 19)** (`select_one`, optional/optional): Select one option from this select.
* **Select 2 (ID 20)** (`select_one`, optional/optional): If not required, select one option from this select.
