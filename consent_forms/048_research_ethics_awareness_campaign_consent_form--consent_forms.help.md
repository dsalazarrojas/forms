# research_ethics_awareness_campaign_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for obtaining informed consent from participants in research studies to ensure they understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the information provided.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Sign the form to indicate your consent.
4. Date and sign the form as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Provide your name, contact information, and other relevant details about yourself as a participant.
* **Research Study**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are participating in a research study.
* **Description of the Study**: Briefly describe the research study you are participating in, including its purpose and any relevant details.
* **Purpose of the Study**: Describe the main purpose of the study you are participating in.
* **Participant Contributions**: Provide any relevant information about your contributions or involvement in the study.
* **Participant Rights**: Acknowledge your rights as a participant, including any rights or privileges you may have in relation to the study.
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name and contact information of the person who is conducting or organizing the research study.
* **Research Organiser**: Provide the name and contact information of the person or organization conducting the research study.
* **Participant Signature**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and understood the information provided and agree to participate in the study.
* **Date**: Provide the date on which you are signing the form.
* **Research Organiser Signature**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you, as the person conducting the research study, have obtained informed consent from the participant.
* **Research Organisation**: Provide the name and details of the organization conducting the research study.
* **Research Funder**: Provide the name and details of the organization funding the research study.
* **Research Venue**: Provide the location or venue where the research study is being conducted.
* **Research DOI (Digital Object Identifier)**: Provide a unique identifier for the research study.

Note: The fields that are marked as required must be filled out correctly to ensure the form is valid.
