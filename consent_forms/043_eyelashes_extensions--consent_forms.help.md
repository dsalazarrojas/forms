# eyelashes_extensions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from patients for the eyelashes extension procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you are the patient or their authorized representative.
2. Complete all required fields (marked as **"required"** in the form).
3. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **consent_form** (id 1, text, required: false): This is a text field for you to write your consent statement. Please provide a clear and concise statement expressing your consent for the eyelashes extension procedure.
* **name** (id 2, text, required: true): Please provide your name as it appears on your identification or medical records.
* **date_of_birth** (id 3, date, required: true): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **contact_number** (id 4, text, required: false): If you have a contact number, please provide it so we can reach you easily.
* **email** (id 5, email, required: false): If you have an email address, please provide it so we can contact you.
* **medical_history** (id 6, note, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history or conditions that may be relevant to the procedure.
* **medication_list** (id 7, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all medications you are currently taking.
* **doctor_name** (id 8, text, required: true): Enter the name of your doctor or the doctor performing the procedure.
* **doctor_signature** (id 9, note, required: true): This is for your doctor's signature, please have them sign this form.
* **patient_signature** (id 10, note, required: true): This is for your signature, please sign this form to confirm your consent.
