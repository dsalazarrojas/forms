# Distance Learning Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about distance learning agreements, which are essential for the management and administration of online courses. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields based on your role in the distance learning process (e.g., student, instructor, or course administrator).
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.
