# Hospital Staff Innovation Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from hospital staff about their innovation stories, which are unique experiences, ideas, or approaches that have improved patient care or the hospital's operations. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and insights from staff members, allowing the hospital management to identify areas for improvement and recognize staff members who have made significant contributions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your staff title from the options provided.
2. Enter a title for your innovation story.
3. Describe your innovation story, including details about what you did, how you did it, and the impact it had.
4. Provide more information about your story, if necessary.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
6. Check the box to confirm that you consent to share your story with the hospital management.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Title** (`Manager/Director`, `Clinical Specialist`, `Nurse`, `Doctor`, `Other (please specify)`): Please select your staff title from the options provided. This will help the hospital management understand your role in the innovation story.
* **Innovation Story Title**: Enter a brief title for your innovation story that captures its essence. This will be used to identify and categorize your story.
* **Consent to Share**: Please select "Yes" if you are willing to share your innovation story with the hospital management, and "No" if you are not.
* **Innovation Story**: Describe your innovation story in detail, including what you did, how you did it, and the impact it had. This will be used to gather feedback and insights from the hospital management.
* **More about the Story**: If you have more information about your story that you would like to add, please enter it here. This is an optional field, but it will help provide more context to your innovation story.
* **Staff Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number. This will help the hospital management reach out to you with any follow-up questions or requests.
* **Consented Notes**: This is a checkbox field where you can confirm your consent to share your innovation story with the hospital management.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here. This is an optional field, and your comments will be reviewed by the hospital management.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and truthful.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
* Your innovation story will be reviewed by the hospital management, and you will be contacted with any feedback or follow-up questions.
