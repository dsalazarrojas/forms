# Skin Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for skin treatment, including the patient's name, the type of skin treatment, the consent date, the doctor's signature, and the patient's signature. The form helps in maintaining a clear and transparent medical record.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the patient's name in the designated field.
2. Specify the type of skin treatment in the corresponding field.
3. Enter the consent date in the designated field.
4. Sign as the doctor in the designated field.
5. Sign as the patient in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient-name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **skin treatment** (`skin_treatment`, text, required/optional): Describe the type of skin treatment the patient will undergo.
* **consent date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the patient's consent.
* **doctor** (`doctor_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign as the doctor providing the skin treatment.
* **patient signature** (`patient_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign as the patient receiving the skin treatment.
