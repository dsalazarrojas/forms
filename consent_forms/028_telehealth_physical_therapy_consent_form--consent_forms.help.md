> # telehealth_physical_therapy_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain telehealth physical therapy consent from patients before starting a telehealth physical therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. **Appointment Information**: Enter the appointment details, including the appointment date, time, and location (if applicable). Ensure this information is filled out accurately.
3. **Payment Details**: Select the payment option that applies to your session (Yes/No).
4. **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method for the session (Yes/No).
5. **Signature**: Sign your name to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
6. **Telehealth Information**: Provide any additional information about your telehealth session (e.g., video conferencing platform, device requirements).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Information
* **Patient Information (text)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information to identify the patient.

### Appointment Information
* **Appointment Information (text)**: Enter the appointment details, including the appointment date, time, and location (if applicable), to schedule the telehealth session accurately.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details (select)**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to pay for the telehealth session, and "No" otherwise.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method (select)**: Select your preferred payment method for the session (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).

### Signature
* **Signature (note)**: Sign your name to indicate that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the telehealth physical therapy session.

### Telehealth Information
* **Telehealth Information (text)**: Provide any additional information about your telehealth session, such as video conferencing platform and device requirements.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out this form accurately and honestly.
- This form is required to be filled out before starting a telehealth physical therapy session.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your physical therapist.
