# Lookbook Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Lookbook Release Form is to obtain permission from contributors to release their creative assets (e.g., images, videos, or other copyrighted materials) for use in a lookbook. A lookbook is a collection of creative assets used to showcase a brand's style and aesthetic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you are comfortable with the release terms and conditions.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contributor Permissions** (`contributor_permissions`): Enter any specific permissions or restrictions you have regarding the use of your creative assets. For example, if you have any usage limitations or specific contexts where your assets can be used.
- **Creative Assets** (`creative_assets`): Describe the creative assets you are releasing, including images, videos, or other copyrighted materials. Provide a brief summary or list of the assets.
- **Publication Details** (`publication_details`): Enter any details about where and how your creative assets will be used, such as the publication name, URL, or intended audience.
- **Publication Terms** (`publication_terms`): Explain any specific terms or conditions you have for the use of your creative assets, such as copyright information or usage limits.
- **Contributor Signature** (`contributor_signature`): This field is for a note-taking purpose; you can sign and date your signature here if desired.
- **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`): Enter your name as a contributor, so we can identify you correctly.
- **Brand Details** (`brand_details`): Enter any brand-specific details you want to include with your creative assets, such as brand name or logo.

## Tips
- Make sure to be as specific as possible when describing your creative assets and their usage.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to ask us for clarification.
- Remember to keep your description concise and clear, so we can easily understand your requirements.
