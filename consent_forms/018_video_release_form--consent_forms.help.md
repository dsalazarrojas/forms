# Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Release Form is designed to collect consent from individuals and agencies for the use of their images, videos, and other media. This form ensures that individuals and organizations understand their rights, responsibilities, and any limitations involved in the use of their content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Your personal details (name, email, phone number)
	* Agency information (name, address, email, phone number)
	* Agreement details (agreement date, time, purpose)
	* Video specifics (video type, purpose, presentation to)
2. Review and sign the agreement section to confirm your understanding and consent.
3. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form 1: Participant Name
* **participant_name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### Form 2: Participant Email
* **participant_email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and information.
### Form 3: Participant Phone
* **participant_phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
### Form 4: Agency Name
* **agency_name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of the agency or organization you are representing.
### Form 5: Agency Address
* **agency_address** (`text`, optional): Enter the physical address of the agency or organization.
### Form 6: Agency Phone
* **agency_phone** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the agency or organization.
### Form 7: Agency Email
* **agency_email** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address of the agency or organization.
### Form 8: Agreement Date
* **agreement_date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you agreed to the terms and conditions.
### Form 9: Agreement Time
* **agreement_time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you agreed to the terms and conditions.
### Form 10: Purpose
* **purpose** (`text`, optional): Briefly explain the purpose of the agreement.
### Form 11: Agreement Signed
* **agreement_signed** (`text`, optional): Sign and date to confirm your understanding and consent.
### Form 12: Limitations
* **limitations** (`text`, optional): If any limitations apply, please list them here.
### Form 13: Agreement Signed By
* **agreement_signed_by** (`text`, optional): Sign and date to confirm your understanding and consent.
### Form 14: Agreement Signed At
* **agreement_signed_at** (`date`, optional): Date when you signed the agreement.
### Form 15: Video Type
* **video_type** (`text`, optional): Specify the type of video (e.g., commercial, non-commercial).
### Form 16: Agreement Purpose Type
* **agreement_purpose** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the agreement.
### Form 17: Presentation To
* **presentation_to** (`select_one`, required): Select from the options:
	+ Commercial
	+ Non-Commercial
### Form 18: Agency Address 2
* **agency_address_2** (`text`, optional): Enter the second line of the agency or organization's address.
### Form 19: Agency Address 3
* **agency_address_3** (`text`, optional): Enter the third line of the agency or organization's address.
### Form 20: Agency Address 4
* **agency_address_4** (`text`, optional): Enter the fourth line of the agency or organization's address.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [contact email or phone number].
