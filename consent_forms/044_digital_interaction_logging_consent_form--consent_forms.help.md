# Digital Interaction Logging Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Interaction Logging Consent Form is used to document employee consent for digital interactions, such as monitoring or recording of work activities. This form helps ensure that employees are aware of and agree to the collection of their interaction data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your company name**: In the "Company Name" field, type your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
2. **Choose your interaction type**: Select the type of interaction you are consenting to, such as Monitoring or Recording.
3. **Choose your consent options**: Select the specific interactions you are consenting to, such as Monitoring, Recording, or both.
4. **Enter interaction details**: If required, enter the details of the interaction, such as a specific date or time.
5. **Set review frequency**: Select the frequency at which your interactions will be reviewed, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
6. **Enter email and phone contact information**: If required, enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
7. **Sign the form**: Sign the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Company Name
* **Company Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.

### 2. Interaction Type
* **Interaction Type** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of interaction you are consenting to, such as Monitoring or Recording.

### 3. Consent To Interactions
* **Consent To Interactions** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the specific interactions you are consenting to, such as Monitoring, Recording, or both.

### 4. Interaction Details
* **Interaction Details** (date, required/optional): If required, enter the details of the interaction, such as a specific date or time.

### 5. Interaction Start Time
* **Interaction Start Time** (time, required/optional): If required, enter the start time of the interaction.

### 6. Interaction End Time
* **Interaction End Time** (time, required/optional): If required, enter the end time of the interaction.

### 7. Review Frequency
* **Review Frequency** (select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency at which your interactions will be reviewed, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

### 8. Interaction Log
* **Interaction Log** (note, required/optional): If required, enter a note about the interaction, such as any relevant comments or notes.

### 9. Email
* **Email** (email, required/optional): If required, enter your email address for contact purposes.

### 10. Phone
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): If required, enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### 11. Consent Date
* **Consent Date** (date, required/optional): If required, enter the date of your consent.

### 12. Employee Signature
* **Employee Signature** (text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent.

### 13. Supervisor Signature
* **Supervisor Signature** (text, required/optional): If a supervisor is required, sign their name to confirm their consent.

Tips: Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, as it is a record of your consent for digital interactions. If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative or supervisor.
