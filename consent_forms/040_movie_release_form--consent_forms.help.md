# Movie Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Movie Release Form is designed to collect information and consent from individuals who have performed in a movie. This form is used to document the consent process and ensure that the individual's rights are respected.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your individual name in the "Individual Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the date of your performance in the "Performance Date" field.
4. Specify the duration of your performance in the "Performance Duration" field.
5. Select the type of performance you provided, such as "Music", "Dance", or "Other".
6. Select the type(s) of image rights you have granted, such as "Public Domain", "Commercial Use", or "Non-Commercial Use".
7. Select the type(s) of voice rights you have granted, such as "Public Domain", "Commercial Use", or "Non-Commercial Use".
8. Confirm that you have given your consent to the terms and conditions by selecting "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further communication.
* **Performance Date (date)**: Enter the date when you performed in the movie.
* **Performance Duration (number)**: Enter the length of time you spent performing.
* **Performance Type (select_one)**: Select the type of performance you provided, such as music, dance, or other.
* **Image Rights Granted (select_multiple)**: Select the types of image rights you have granted, such as public domain, commercial use, or non-commercial use.
* **Voice Rights Granted (select_multiple)**: Select the types of voice rights you have granted, such as public domain, commercial use, or non-commercial use.
* **Consent Given (select_one)**: Confirm that you have given your consent to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the production team or management.
* Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records, in case you need to refer back to it in the future.
* If you have already filled out this form, you may not need to fill it out again. However, please check with the production team or management to confirm.
