# Oral And Maxillofacial Surgery Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Oral And Maxillofacial Surgery Consent Form is designed to collect patient information and consent for an oral and maxillofacial surgery procedure. This form is typically completed by a patient before undergoing surgery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Patient Name** (page 1) accurately and completely.
2. Enter the **Procedure Date** and **Procedure Time** (page 1) for the upcoming surgery.
3. Write down the **Surgeon Name** (page 1) who will perform the surgery.
4. Select the **Consent Date** (page 1) and **Consent Time** (page 1) when you will be signing this form.
5. Sign your name as the **Patient Signature** (page 1) to indicate your consent for the procedure.
6. The **Doctor Signature** (page 1) should be signed by the surgeon who will perform the procedure.
7. In the **Procedure Description** (page 1) field, describe the planned procedure and its purpose.
8. List any **Risks** (page 2) associated with the procedure, including any potential complications.
9. List any **Complications** (page 2) that may occur during or after the procedure.
10. Select the type of **Anesthesia** (page 2) that will be used during the procedure.
11. Check the box to indicate whether you **Consent To Procedure** (page 2) or not.
12. Enter your **Contact Phone** (page 3), **Contact Email** (page 3), and **Contact Relationship** (page 3) information.
13. Provide your **Contact Address** (page 3) and **Contact Note** (page 3) for follow-up purposes.
14. Share your **Medical History** (page 4) with the healthcare provider, including any previous surgeries, illnesses, or allergies.
15. List any **Allergies** (page 4) you may have.
16. List any **Medications** (page 4) you are currently taking.
17. Provide your **Emergency Contact** (page 5) information, including name and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name, as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Procedure Date**: Enter the date of the planned surgery.
* **Procedure Time**: Enter the time of the planned surgery.
* **Surgeon Name**: Write the name of the surgeon who will perform the procedure.
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date you will be signing this form.
* **Consent Time**: Enter the time you will be signing this form.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign your name to indicate your consent for the procedure.
* **Doctor Signature**: The surgeon should sign this field to confirm they have reviewed and discussed the form with you.
* **Procedure Description**: Describe the planned procedure and its purpose.
* **Risks**: List any potential risks or complications associated with the procedure.
* **Complications**: List any possible complications that may occur during or after the procedure.
* **Anesthesia**: Select the type of anesthesia to be used during the procedure.
* **Consent To Procedure**: Check the box to indicate your consent for the procedure.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the contact person.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Contact Note**: Provide any additional notes about the contact person.
* **Medical History**: Share your medical history with the healthcare provider.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you may have.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact details of your emergency contact.

Please note that some fields may require specific formatting or details, such as dates and times. If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
