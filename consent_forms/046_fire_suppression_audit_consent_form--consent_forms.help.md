# fire_suppression_audit_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire suppression audit consent form is designed to collect user information for the purpose of conducting fire suppression system audits. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your name and contact information (Name, Phone Number, and Email) in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your contact address.
3. Enter the details of the auditor performing the audit.
4. Schedule a time for the audit to take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (1): This is the field where you enter your name for the audit.
* **Contact Number** (2): This is the field where you enter your contact number so we can reach you for further information or to schedule the audit.
* **Contact Email** (3): This is the field where you enter your email address so we can send you updates or reminders about the audit.
* **Contact Address** (4): This is the field where you enter your address so we can send you any necessary information or documents after the audit.
* **Auditor Details** (5): This is the field where you enter the details of the auditor performing the audit, including their name and contact information.
* **Schedule an Inspection** (6): This is the field where you enter the desired date and time for the audit to take place.
