# Glaucoma And Vision Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to screen for glaucoma and vision issues. It collects information about your eye health and vision problems, helping healthcare professionals assess your condition accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form instructions and questions carefully.
2. Answer each question in as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any issues with your vision or eye health, please describe them thoroughly.
4. Do not leave any questions blank. If unsure, write "Don't know" or "No issues" to indicate this.
