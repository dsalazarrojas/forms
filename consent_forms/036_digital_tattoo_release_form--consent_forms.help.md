# Digital Tattoo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a legal release of rights from individuals who have appeared in digital content, such as tattoos, images, or videos, that the company may use, distribute, or license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter a title for the digital content in the "Digital Tattoo Release Form" field.
* Select the type of digital content that the form is being used for (e.g., tattoo, image, video).
* Provide a description of the likeness, image, or voice used.
* Select the specific likeness, image, or voice used.
* Provide a description of the story associated with the digital content.
* Select the specific story used.
* Provide a description of the voice used.
* Select the specific voice used.
* Grant the rights to use the digital content.
* Provide a date and time of signing.
* Enter the location where the form was signed.
* Enter the individual's email address.
* Enter the individual's phone number (optional).
* Enter the individual's name.
* Enter the individual's contact information.
* Provide a signature of the individual.
* Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Digital Tattoo Release Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for the digital content.
* **Type of Digital Content** (`consent_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of digital content (e.g., tattoo, image, video).
* **Description of Likeness Used** (`likeness_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the likeness, image, or voice used.
* **Likeness Used** (`likeness_used`, select_one, required): Select the specific likeness, image, or voice used.
* **Description of Story** (`story_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the story associated with the digital content.
* **Story Used** (`story_used`, select_one, required): Select the specific story used.
* **Description of Voice** (`voice_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the voice used.
* **Voice Used** (`voice_used`, select_one, required): Select the specific voice used.
* **Release of Rights** (`release_grant`, text, required): Grant the rights to use the digital content.
* **Acknowledgement of Agreement** (`agreement_signed`, text, required): Provide a statement indicating that the individual has read and understood the agreement.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date signed by the individual, in mm/dd/yy format.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required): Enter the time signed by the individual.
* **Location Signed** (`signed_at`, text, required): Enter the location where the form was signed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the individual's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the individual's phone number (optional).
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the individual's name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the individual's contact information.
* **Signature of Individual** (`signature`, text, required): Provide a signature of the individual.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, text, required): Provide any additional notes.
* **Relationship to Individual** (`contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the individual and the contact person (e.g., Parent, Spouse, Friend, Other).
* **Role of Contact Person** (`contact_role`, select_one, required): Select the role of the contact person (e.g., Manager, Spouse, Friend, Other).
