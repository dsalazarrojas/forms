# Wheelchair Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheelchair Repair Form is used to document the repair process and details of a wheelchair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including your name, contact email, phone number, and address.
2. Provide the wheelchair serial number, which can be found on the wheelchair's frame or in the manufacturer's documentation.
3. Describe the condition of the wheelchair, using the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Choose the type of repair needed (Wheelchair Repair, Chair Repair, Both).
5. Enter the date and time of the repair, if applicable.
6. Sign the form to confirm the repair was done.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**: Your name and contact information to reach you.
	+ This field is required and should be filled out by the user.
* **Wheelchair Serial Number (3)**: The unique number assigned to the wheelchair.
	+ This field helps identify the wheelchair being repaired.
* **Wheelchair Condition (5)**: Describe the condition of the wheelchair.
	+ Choose from the options Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Repair Type (6)**: Choose the type of repair needed.
	+ Options include Wheelchair Repair, Chair Repair, or Both.
* **Date Repaired (7)**: The date the repair was done.
	+ Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Repaired (8)**: The time the repair was done.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Technician Signature (9)**: Sign to confirm the repair was done.
	+ This field is required and should be filled out by the repair technician.
* **Manager Signature (10)**: Sign to confirm the repair was authorized.
	+ This field is required and should be filled out by the person authorizing the repair.
* **Manager Name (11)**: The name of the person authorizing the repair.
* **Manager Title (12)**: The title of the person authorizing the repair.
* **Company (13)**: The company name and contact information.
* **Contact Email (14)**: The email address to contact the company.
* **Contact Phone (15)**: The phone number to contact the company.
* **Contact Fax (16)**: The fax number to contact the company.
* **Contact Address (17)**: The address of the company.
* **Contact City (18)**: The city of the company.
* **Contact State (19)**: The state of the company.
* **Contact Zip (20)**: The zip code of the company.
* **Contact Country (21)**: The country of the company.
* **Contact Name (22)**: The name of the person to contact at the company.
* **Contact Title (23)**: The title of the person to contact at the company.
* **Additional Notes (24)**: Any additional information about the repair.
* **Date Created (25)**: The date the form was created.
	+ This field is automatically generated and should not be edited.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the correct contact information for the company.
* Be thorough when describing the condition of the wheelchair.
* Sign the form to confirm the repair was done.
* Keep the form up to date with any changes or updates.
