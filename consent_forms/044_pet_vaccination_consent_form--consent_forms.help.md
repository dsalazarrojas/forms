# Pet Vaccination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from pet owners for vaccinations for their pets. This form helps ensure that pet owners are aware of the benefits and risks of vaccination and can provide their consent for the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the benefits and risks of vaccination for your pet.
2. Select the type of vaccination you are consenting to (e.g. Rabies, Flu, Parvo).
3. Choose whether the vaccination is a one-time or recurring event.
4. Provide the date and time of the vaccination.
5. Enter the name and contact information of the veterinarian performing the vaccination.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Vaccination Benefits**: This field is for you to describe the benefits of vaccination for your pet. Please provide a brief description of why you think vaccination is beneficial for your pet.
* **Pet Vaccination Risks**: This field is for you to describe any potential risks or concerns you have about vaccination for your pet. 
* **Consent to Vaccination**: Please select "Yes" if you consent to the vaccination, or "No" if you do not. 
* **Type of Vaccination**: Select the type of vaccination you are consenting to (e.g. Rabies, Flu, Parvo).
* **Frequency of Vaccination**: Choose whether the vaccination is a one-time event or a recurring event.
* **Vaccination Date**: Enter the date on which the vaccination will be administered.
* **Vaccination Time**: Enter the time of day when the vaccination will be administered.
* **Veterinarian Name**: Enter the name of the veterinarian performing the vaccination.
* **Veterinarian Contact**: Enter the contact information of the veterinarian performing the vaccination.
* **Client Signature**: This is the space for you to sign and date your consent.
* **Veterinarian Signature**: This is the space for the veterinarian to sign and date their acceptance of your consent.
* **Additional Notes**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information about your pet's vaccination.
* **Form Version**: Please leave this field blank, as it is used by the system for internal tracking.
* **Form Status**: Select "Active" if the form is currently active, or "Inactive" if it is not.
