# Patient Consent Documentation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient consent and documentation for medical procedures. It helps healthcare providers gather essential information from patients and ensures they are complying with regulatory requirements while providing excellent patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. For the "Anesthesia Consent" section, select one of the options (Anesthesia, No Anesthesia, or Other) that best fits your situation.
3. In the "Surgery Type" section, select all that apply from the list of options (Cardiac, Orthopedic, Neurosurgery, or Other).
4. For "Medical History", provide a detailed description of your medical history.
5. In the "Medication History" section, select all that apply from the list of medication options (Medication A, Medication B, Medication C).
6. In the "Allergies" section, select all that apply from the list of allergy options (Allergy A, Allergy B, Allergy C).
7. In the "Patient Signature" and "Provider Signature" sections, enter the date on which you are signing the form.
8. Enter your "Patient Name" and "Date of Birth" in the corresponding fields.
9. Select your "Provider Name" from the list of options and enter your "Provider License" number(s) if applicable.
10. Enter your "Patient ID" number if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Procedure Pre Admission** (`procedure_pre_admission`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the procedure you are preparing to undergo.
* **Anesthesia Consent** (`anesthesia_consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your preference or need for anesthesia during the procedure.
* **Surgery Type** (`surgery_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of surgery types (Cardiac, Orthopedic, Neurosurgery, or Other) to indicate the type of procedure you are having.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or previous procedures.
* **Medication History** (`medication_history`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of medication options (Medication A, Medication B, Medication C) to indicate any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of allergy options (Allergy A, Allergy B, Allergy C) to indicate any allergies you have.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date on which you are signing the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, date, optional): Enter the date on which your healthcare provider is signing the form.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, select_one, optional): Select your healthcare provider's name from the list of options.
* **Provider License** (`provider_license`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of provider license options (License 1, License 2, License 3) if applicable.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, text, optional): Enter your patient ID number if applicable.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, please consult your healthcare provider.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please reach out to the form provider or your healthcare provider.
