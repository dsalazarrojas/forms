# Shipping Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for shipper to declare shipping details to the company for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to your shipping information and details.
2. Please ensure you have provided all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_company_name** (`shipping_company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company or shipper.
* **shipper_name** (`shipper_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the shipper.
* **shipper_address** (`shipper_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the shipper.
* **shipper_phone** (`shipper_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the shipper.
* **shipper_email** (`shipper_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the shipper.
* **shipper_country** (`shipper_country`, select_one, optional): Select the country where you are shipping from, options: United Kingdom, United States.
* **package_description** (`package_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the package being shipped.
* **package_weight** (`package_weight`, number, optional): Enter the weight of the package.
* **package_dimensions** (`package_dimensions`, text, optional): Enter the dimensions of the package.
* **contents** (`contents`, text, optional): Enter the contents of the package.
* **shipper_signature** (`shipper_signature`, note, optional): Sign the form to authenticate your declaration.
* **declaration_type** (`declaration_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of declaration, options: Express, Parcel.
* **declaration_date** (`declaration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the declaration.
* **declaration_time** (`declaration_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the declaration.
* **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person responsible for reviewing your declaration, options: Jim, Other.

**Tips:**
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately for official purposes.
* Review your submission before submitting for review.
* Keep a record of your declaration submission for reference.
