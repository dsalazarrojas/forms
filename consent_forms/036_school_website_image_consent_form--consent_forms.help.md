# School Website Image Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Website Image Consent Form is a form designed for parents and administrators to provide consent for students' images to be published on the school website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the student's name and select "Yes" if you agree to publish their image on the school website.
2.  Sign your name as the parent or administrator, indicating your consent for the image to be published.
3.  If you have any restrictions on the image, select them in the "Optional Restrictions" field.
4.  Choose whether to submit a student work or video, and select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
5.  Decide if you want to publish the student's videos on the school website, and select "Yes" or "No" if necessary.
6.  Set up the form submission settings, such as submit email and date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
    *   Type: `email`
    *   Required: Optional
*   **Optional Restrictions**: Select any specific restrictions you want to apply to the image publication.
    *   Type: `select_multiple`
    *   Required: No
*   **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Parent Signature**: Sign your name as the parent or administrator, indicating your consent for the image to be published.
    *   Type: `note`
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Publish on School Website**: Select "Yes" to publish the image on the school website.
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Student Work**: Select if you want to submit a student work.
