# Animation Scene Commentary Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Scene Commentary Consent Form is a document that obtains your consent for the recording and use of your commentary for an animation project. It's essential to understand that your participation in this project involves the recording of your voice and potentially your appearance on camera.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the "Participant Information" section.
2. If you are under 18, provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. In the "Scene Information" section, provide the name of the animation project and the scene title or number you will be commenting on.
4. Select the format in which your commentary will be recorded (Audio Recording, Video Recording, Written Transcript, or Combination).
5. Choose the purpose of your commentary (Educational Behind-the-Scenes, Director's Commentary, Scene Breakdown, Animation Techniques, Production Insights, Character Development, or Other).
6. In the "Recording Permissions" section, indicate your consent to audio and video recording, as well as your name being credited in the footage.
7. In the "Usage Rights and Permissions" section, choose how you wish to distribute and use your commentary (Yes, distribute freely, Yes, for nonprofit use only, Yes, with permission for each use, or No, do not distribute).
8. Select how you wish to use social media (Yes, use freely, Yes, ask me first, or No, do not use).
9. Choose the usage rights for commercial use (Yes, allow commercial use, No, nonprofit only, or No, personal use only).
10. Indicate whether you consent to editing or modifying your commentary (Yes, edit as needed, Yes, with approval, or No, keep original only).
11. If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name, email address, and consent to the terms in the "Parental Consent" section.
12. Finally, confirm your agreement to the terms by selecting "Yes, I agree to all terms" in the "Agreement Final" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for correspondence and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Your contact number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Your date of birth if you are under 18.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): The name of the animation project.
* **Scene Title or Number** (`scene_title`, text, required): The title or number of the scene you will be commenting on.
* **Date of Commentary Recording** (`commentary_date`, date, required): The date of the commentary recording.
* **Commentary Format** (`commentary_format`, select_one, required): The format of the commentary recording (Audio Recording, Video Recording, Written Transcript, or Combination).
* **Purpose of Commentary** (`commentary_purpose`, select_one, optional): The purpose of your commentary (Educational Behind-the-Scenes, Director's Commentary, Scene Breakdown, Animation Techniques, Production Insights, Character Development, or Other).
* **I consent to audio recording of my commentary** (`audio_recording_consent`, select_one, required): Permission to record your voice.
* **I consent to video recording of my commentary** (`video_recording_consent`, select_one, required): Permission to record video with your appearance.
* **I consent to my name being credited in the footage** (`name_credit_consent`, select_one, required): Whether to credit your name in the footage.
* **Permission to distribute commentary online** (`online_distribution`, select_one, required): Whether to distribute your commentary online.
* **Permission to share on social media** (`social_media_use`, select_one, required): Whether to share your commentary on social media.
* **Permission for commercial use** (`commercial_use`, select_one, required): Whether to allow commercial use of your commentary.
* **Permission to edit or modify commentary** (`derivative_works`, select_one, required): Whether to edit or modify your commentary.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): The full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): The contact email of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`parent_consent`, select_one, optional): Whether your parent or guardian consents to the terms.
* **I have read and agree to this consent form** (`agreement_final`, select_one, required): Confirmation of your agreement to the terms.

## Tips
Please ensure that you understand and agree to all terms before signing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project team.
