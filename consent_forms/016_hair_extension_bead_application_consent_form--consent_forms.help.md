# Hair Extension Bead Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information for the hair extension bead application process, including client details, hair history, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled.
- Provide accurate and honest information for each field.

* **Client Information** (1, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Hair History** (2, note, optional): Describe your previous hair treatments, styles, and experiences, if any.
* **Signature** (3, text, optional): This field is intended for the client to sign their name, indicating they are 18 years or older.
* **Client Consent** (4, select_one, optional): Select 'True' if you are 18 years or older, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Email** (5, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (6, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Additional Information** (7, text, optional): Enter any additional details or comments that may be helpful for the application process.
