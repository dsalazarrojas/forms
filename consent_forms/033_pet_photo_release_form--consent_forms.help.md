# Pet Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Photo Release Form is used to obtain a legal release from a pet owner for photos taken of their pet. This form is used for veterinary or insurance purposes and provides proof that a photo of the pet has been taken.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Owner's Name**, which is the name of the person who owns the pet.
2. Enter a valid date in the **Photo Date**, which indicates when the photo was taken.
3. Provide a brief description of where the photo was taken in the **Photo Location** field.
4. Enter the name of the person who took the photo in the **Photographer's Name** field.
5. Provide the contact information of the person who took the photo in the **Photographer's Contact** field.
6. Describe the purpose for which the photo will be used in the **Photo Usage** field (optional).
7. If the photo will be used for a purpose not listed, describe the purpose in the **Other Photo Usage** field (optional).
8. Sign the form to confirm the photo release by providing your signature in the **Signature** field.
9. Date the form by providing the date in the **Signature Date** field.
10. Indicate whether or not you grant the permission to use the photo by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the **Photo Release** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner's Name**: This is the name of the person who owns the pet being photographed.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Required
* **Photo Date**: This is the date when the photo was taken. Please enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Required
* **Photo Location**: This is the location where the photo was taken.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
* **Photographer's Name**: This is the name of the person who took the photo.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Required
* **Photographer's Contact**: This is the contact information of the person who took the photo.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Required
* **Photo Usage**: This is a description of how the photo will be used. Please be specific and provide a brief description.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
* **Other Photo Usage**: This is a field for any additional or alternative photo usage not already listed.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Not Required
* **Signature**: This is where you, the pet owner, sign to confirm the photo release.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Required
* **Signature Date**: This is the date when you sign the photo release.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: Required
* **Photo Release**: This is where you confirm whether or not you grant permission to use the photo.
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: Required

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the person who took the photo.
* Once you have signed and dated the form, the photographer has permission to use the photo as specified.
