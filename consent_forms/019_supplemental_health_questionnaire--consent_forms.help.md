# Supplemental Health Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplemental Health Questionnaire is a form designed to gather information about a patient's medical history, current conditions, and potential risks or allergies to help medical professionals provide better care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your medical history, current conditions, and potential risks or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Screen Patient
* **Screen Patient** (`screen_patient`, text, optional): This field is for patients to provide any relevant information about their medical screening or test results.

### Patient Screening
* **Patient Screening** (`patient_screening`, text, optional): This field is for patients to provide any information about their medical screening or test results.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for patients to provide a brief summary of their medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or conditions.

### Allergies
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): This field is for patients to list any allergies or sensitivities they may have.

### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): This field is for patients to provide information about any current medical conditions or symptoms they may be experiencing.

### Current Medications
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): This field is for patients to list any current medications they are taking.

### Travel History
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, optional): This field is for patients to provide information about any recent travel history, including destinations and dates.

### Exposure to COVID
* **Exposure to COVID** (`exposure_to_covid`, text, optional): This field is for patients to report any exposure to COVID-19, including dates and circumstances.

### Fever
* **Fever** (`fever`, number, optional): This field is for patients to report their current body temperature, if applicable.

### Cough
* **Cough** (`cough`, text, optional): This field is for patients to report if they are experiencing a cough, and to provide any details about it.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure that medical professionals have a complete understanding of your medical status.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form should be filled out before or after your medical appointment to help your healthcare provider make informed decisions about your care.
