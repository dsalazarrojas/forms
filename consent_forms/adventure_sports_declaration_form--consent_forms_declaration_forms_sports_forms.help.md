# ADVENTURE SPORTS Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Adventure Sports Declaration Form. The form is designed to gather information about your personal details, adventure sports preferences, and medical clearances to assess your eligibility for various activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your personal details, including your full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and age.
2.  Select your primary sport of interest and experience level.
3.  Choose your preferred location and workout schedule.
4.  Indicate your current fitness level and goals.
5.  Provide any medical conditions or injuries that may affect your participation.
6.  Confirm that you have received medical clearance to participate.
7.  Agree to the liability waiver terms.
8.  Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (Required): Enter your legal first and last name.
*   **Email address** (Required): Enter your email address, which we'll use to send confirmations.
*   **Phone number** (Optional): Provide your preferred contact number.
*   **Date of birth** (Required): Format: `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Age** (Optional): Enter your age in years.
*   **Emergency contact name** (Optional): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency contact relationship** (Optional): Relationship to you (e.g., Parent, Friend, Spouse).
*   **Emergency contact phone** (Optional): Phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Primary sport of interest** (Required): Select one of the options: `Rock Climbing`, `Whitewater Rafting`, `Bungee Jumping`, `Mountain Biking`, `Skydiving`, or `Paragliding`.
*   **Experience level** (Required): Choose your current skill level: `Beginner`, `Intermediate`, `Advanced`, or `Expert`.
*   **Preferred location** (Optional): Enter the preferred location for your adventure sports activities.
*   **Preferred workout time** (Optional): Enter your preferred workout time.
*   **Workout sessions per week** (Optional): Indicate how many times per week you plan to train.
*   **Current fitness level** (Required): Choose your overall fitness status: `Beginner`, `Intermediate`, `Advanced`, or `Competitive`.
*   **Fitness goals** (Optional): Select all that apply: `Weight loss`, `Muscle gain`, `Endurance`, `Flexibility`, `Stress relief`, or `Competition prep`.
*   **Current injuries or limitations** (Optional): Briefly describe any ongoing medical issues.
*   **Medical conditions** (Optional): Select any that apply: `Asthma`, `Diabetes`, `Heart disease`, `Allergies`, or `None`.
*   **Medical clearance** (Required): Confirm that you have received medical clearance to participate.
*   **Liability waiver** (Required): Agree to the terms of the liability waiver.
*   **Date of declaration** (Required): Enter the date you are filling out this form.
*   **Additional comments or notes** (Optional): Provide any other information you'd like to share.
*   **Signature (print name)** (Required): Print your full name as your signature.
*   **Signature date** (Required): Date of your signature.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your personal details for accuracy.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the relevant authority.
*   Ensure that you have medical clearance to participate in adventure sports activities.
*   Review the liability waiver terms carefully before agreeing to them.
