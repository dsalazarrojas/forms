# Benefit Assignment Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Benefit Assignment Authorization Form is used to collect information from patients or beneficiaries to facilitate the assignment of benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's or beneficiary's information:
	* Enter the patient's or beneficiary's name in the "Patient Full Name" field.
	* Enter the patient's or beneficiary's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
	* Enter the patient's or beneficiary's email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter the patient's or beneficiary's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Add insurance information:
	* Enter the insurance company name in the "Insurance Company Name" field.
	* Enter the policy number in the "Policy Number" field.
	* If applicable, enter the group number in the "Group Number" field.
3. Select the type of benefit:
	* Select the type of coverage from the "Type of Coverage" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the benefit assignment details:
	* Select the type of benefit assignment from the "Type of Benefit Assignment" dropdown menu.
	* Enter the provider name in the "Healthcare Provider Name" field.
	* Enter the provider's NPI number in the "Provider NPI Number" field.
	* Enter the provider's address in the "Provider Address" field.
5. Select the preferred payment method:
	* Select the preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
6. Authorize the benefit assignment:
	* Select "I Agree" to authorize the benefit assignment.
	* Select "I Do Not Agree" to decline the benefit assignment.
7. Acknowledge the terms and conditions:
	* Select "I Agree" to acknowledge the terms and conditions.
7. Set the effective date and date of form completion:
	* Enter the effective date of assignment in the "Effective Date of Assignment" field.
	* Enter the date of form completion in the "Date of Form" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the full name of the patient or beneficiary.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's or beneficiary's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient ID Number**: Enter the patient's or beneficiary's ID number, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: Enter the patient's or beneficiary's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the patient's or beneficiary's phone number.
* **Insurance Company Name**: Enter the insurance company name.
* **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number.
* **Group Number**: Enter the group number, if applicable.
* **Type of Coverage**: Select the type of coverage from the dropdown menu.
* **Type of Benefit Assignment**: Select the type of benefit assignment from the dropdown menu.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider.
* **Provider NPI Number**: Enter the provider's NPI number.
* **Provider Address**: Enter the provider's address.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select the preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **I Authorize Benefit Assignment**: Select "I Agree" to authorize the benefit assignment.
* **I Authorize Direct Payment to Provider**: Select "I Agree" to authorize direct payment to the provider.
* **I Acknowledge Terms and Conditions**: Select "I Agree" to acknowledge the terms and conditions.
* **Effective Date of Assignment**: Enter the effective date of assignment.
* **Date of Form**: Enter the date of form completion.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our support team.
* The information collected on this form will be stored securely in accordance with our data protection policies.
