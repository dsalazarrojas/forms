# disaster_response_team_media_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The disaster response team media consent form is used to collect consent for the use of media during disaster response efforts. This form ensures that media content is collected in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the media consent options and select the applicable choices for each media type (photo, video, audio).
2. Choose the status of the consent (granted, pending, denied).
3. Add any additional comments or information as needed.
4. Enter your contact name and contact email.
5. Enter your contact phone number (optional).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Media Consent** (`media_consent`, select_multiple, required): Select which media types you consent to the use of during this disaster response effort. Choose from the options: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of media that you are consenting to. Options: photo, video, and audio.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of your consent. Options: granted, pending, and denied.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information relevant to the media consent.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the contact person (optional).

## Tips
* Ensure that you select the correct media type and consent status for each media type.
* Enter accurate contact information to facilitate communication.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors or omissions.
