# Sugaring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Consent Form is used to obtain written consent from patients before performing sugaring services. This form ensures that patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the service, including the type and frequency of the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Describe the service to be performed in the "Service Description" field.
3. Select the type of service(s) agreed to by the patient from the "Consent" field. This can be "Sugaring", "Waxing", or "Other".
4. Enter the date the patient provided consent in the "Consent Date" field.
5. Enter the technician's name in the "Technician Name" field.
6. Enter the patient's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Enter the patient's email in the "Email" field.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the type of service to be performed from the "Service Type" field. This can be "Sugaring", "Waxing", or "Other".
10. Select the frequency of the service from the "Service Frequency" field. This can be "Single", "Regular", or "Maintenance".
11. Select the consent status from the "Consent Status" field. This can be "Accepted", "Refused", or "Unknown".
12. Sign the form with the patient's signature in the "Patient Signature" field.
13. Sign the form with the technician's signature in the "Technician Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Service Description**: Describe the service to be performed, including the type and any relevant details.
* **Consent**: Select all the services the patient agrees to, separated by commas (e.g., "Sugaring, Waxing").
* **Consent Date**: Enter the date the patient provided consent, in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician performing the service.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number, including the area code.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the service.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service to be performed, such as "Sugaring" or "Waxing".
* **Service Frequency**: Select the frequency of the service, such as "Single", "Regular", or "Maintenance".
* **Consent Status**: Select the status of the consent, such as "Accepted" or "Refused".
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form with the patient's signature.
* **Technician Signature**: Sign the form with the technician's signature.
