# Theme Park Disability Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theme Park Disability Access Request Form is designed for guests with disabilities to inform the park staff about their accessibility needs. This form is used to gather essential information from the guest to provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience for everyone, including those with disabilities. To ensure a smooth experience, please fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you've already submitted this form, please do not resubmit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on "Submit a Request" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the "Contact Person" field with your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Guest" or "Jane Doe, Guest's Caregiver").
3. Enter a valid "Email" address that we can use to contact you.
4. Provide your "Phone" number if you'd like to be contacted by park staff.
5. Select a "Visit Date" when you plan to visit the theme park.
6. Describe your "Accommodation Requests" in the provided space, so we can better assist you.
7. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required):
  This field is for you to enter your name and title (e.g., "John Doe, Guest" or "Jane Doe, Guest's Caregiver").
* **Email** (Email, email, required):
  A valid email address is required to contact you for more information or to confirm your visit details.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional):
  If you'd like to be contacted by park staff, please provide your phone number.
* **Visit Date** (Visit Date, date, required):
  Select a date when you plan to visit the theme park. This will help us prepare for your visit and ensure a smooth experience.
* **Accommodation Requests** (Accommodation Requests, text, required):
  Describe any special accommodations you may need, such as wheelchair accessibility, sign language interpretation, or other assistance.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional):
  If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them here so we can provide a better experience for you.
