# integrative_health_program_evaluation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from program participants and providers for the evaluation and consent process of an integrative health program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Program Details** field with the relevant information about the program.
2. Enter the **Contact Info** of the participant and **Provider Name**, **Provider Title**, **Provider License**, and **Provider Email**.
3. Select **Consent Status** as either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the participant's agreement to participate.
4. Enter the **Participant Info**, including **Participant Age**, **Participant Gender**, and **Participant Contact Info**.
5. Fill out the **Evaluation Date** and **Evaluation Time** if necessary.
6. Sign the **Participant Signature** and **Provider Signature** fields to formalize the participant's and provider's agreement to participate.
7. Enter the **Date Signed** and **Time Signed** to record the date and time of signing.
8. Fill out the **Program Start Date** and **Program End Date**.
9. Enter the **Participant Age** and select the **Participant Gender** from the options provided.
10. Enter the **Provider Name**, **Provider Title**, **Provider License**, and **Provider Email**.
11. Enter the **Program Location**.
12. Enter the **Participant Condition**.
13. Enter any **Additional Comments** if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Details** (1): A text field for entering the program's details.
* **Contact Info** (2): A text field for entering the participant's contact information.
* **Consent Status** (3): A select_one field for indicating the participant's consent status.
* **Participant Info** (4): A text field for entering the participant's information.
* **Evaluation Date** (5): A date field for entering the evaluation date (optional).
* **Evaluation Time** (6): A time field for entering the evaluation time (optional).
* **Participant Signature** (7): A note field for the participant's signature.
* **Provider Signature** (8): A note field for the provider's signature.
* **Date Signed** (9): A date field for recording the date of signing.
* **Time Signed** (10): A time field for recording the time of signing.
* **Program Start Date** (11): A date field for entering the program's start date.
* **Program End Date** (12): A date field for entering the program's end date.
* **Participant Age** (13): A number field for entering the participant's age.
* **Participant Gender** (14): A select_one field for selecting the participant's gender.
* **Participant Contact Info** (15): A text field for entering the participant's contact information.
* **Provider Name** (16): A text field for entering the provider's name.
* **Provider Title** (17): A text field for entering the provider's title.
* **Provider License** (18): A text field for entering the provider's license.
* **Provider Email** (19): An email field for entering the provider's email address.
* **Program Location** (20): A text field for entering the program's location.
* **Participant Condition** (21): A text field for entering the participant's condition.
* **Provider Notes** (22): A text field for entering the provider's notes (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (23): A text field for entering any additional comments.
