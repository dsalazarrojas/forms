# Airflow Testing Appointment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airflow Testing Appointment Consent Form is used to collect patient information and consent for testing appointments. This form is essential for ensuring that all necessary medical history, contact information, and consent for the testing procedure are collected before proceeding with the test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's first name, last name, and date of birth.
2. Enter the patient's contact information, including email address, phone number, and street address.
3. Select the type of airflow test being performed.
4. Enter the appointment date and time.
5. List any relevant medical history, including respiratory conditions.
6. Select any current symptoms the patient is experiencing.
7. Indicate the patient's smoking history.
8. List any medications being taken for respiratory conditions.
9. Indicate if the patient has recently been ill or had surgery.
10. Describe any recent illnesses or surgeries.
11. Indicate if the patient has received a thorough explanation of the testing procedure.
12. Indicate if the patient understands the risks associated with the testing procedure.
13. Indicate if the patient's questions have been answered.
14. Indicate if the patient consents to the testing procedure.
15. Indicate if the patient consents to the use of their medical records.
16. Indicate if the patient consents to sharing their test results with their doctor.
17. Indicate if the patient has received instructions on preparation for the test.
18. Indicate if the patient has any special needs or accommodations.
19. Enter the patient's insurance provider and policy number (if applicable).
20. Sign and date the form to confirm the patient's consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`patient_first_name`): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`patient_last_name`): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`): Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`): Enter the patient's street address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the patient's city.
* **State** (`state`): Enter the patient's state or province.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`): Enter the patient's ZIP code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the full name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Referring Physician** (`referring_physician`): Enter the name of the referring physician (if applicable).
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`): Enter the contact phone number of the referring physician (if applicable).
* **Type of Airflow Test** (`test_type`): Select the type of airflow test being performed.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`): Enter the scheduled appointment date.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`): Enter the scheduled appointment time.
* **Appointment Location** (`appointment_location`): Enter the location where the test will be performed.
* **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`): List any relevant medical history, including respiratory conditions.
* **Current Symptoms** (`current_symptoms`): Select any current symptoms the patient is experiencing.
* **Smoking History** (`smoking_history`): Indicate the patient's smoking history.
* **Current Respiratory Medications** (`medications`): List any medications the patient is taking for respiratory conditions (if applicable).
* **Allergies** (`allergies`): List any allergies the patient has (if applicable).
* **Recent Illness or Surgery** (`recent_illness`): Indicate if the patient has recently been ill or had surgery.
* **Illness Details** (`illness_details`): Describe any recent illnesses or surgeries (if applicable).
* **Procedure Explanation Received** (`procedure_explanation`): Indicate if the patient has received a thorough explanation of the testing procedure.
* **Risks Understood** (`risks_understood`): Indicate if the patient understands the risks associated with the testing procedure.
* **Questions Answered** (`questions_answered`): Indicate if the patient's questions have been answered.
* **Consent for Testing** (`consent_given`): Indicate if the patient consents to the testing procedure.
* **Data Usage Consent** (`data_usage_consent`): Indicate if the patient consents to the use of their medical records.
* **Results Sharing Permission** (`results_sharing`): Indicate if the patient consents to sharing their test results with their doctor.
* **Preparation Instructions Received** (`preparation_instructions`): Indicate if the patient has received instructions on preparation for the test.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`): Indicate if the patient has any special needs or accommodations.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`): Enter the patient's insurance provider (if applicable).
* **Insurance ID Number** (`insurance_id`): Enter the patient's policy number (if applicable).
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Enter the patient's digital signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`): Enter the healthcare provider's signature (if applicable).
