# Craft Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Craft Workshop Waiver Form" is designed to collect necessary information about participants in a workshop and to gather their consent to participate. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information. 
2. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the 'Required' status in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact person. Options include:
	+ Emergency Contact Person
	+ Family Member
	+ Parent
	+ Other
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required: false): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required: false): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information above.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form to indicate that you are the emergency contact person.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, required: false): Select 'I have read and understood all of the information above, and I am willing to participate in this craft workshop.' to indicate your consent.
* **Waiver Signature** (`waiver_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form to indicate that you have read and understood the information above and are willing to participate.
