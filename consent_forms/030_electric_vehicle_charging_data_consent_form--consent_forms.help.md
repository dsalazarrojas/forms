# Electric Vehicle Charging Data Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent and data related to electric vehicle charging for various research, analysis, or business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your vehicle's identification information in the "vehicle_identification" field.
2. Select your consent preference from the "driver_consent" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Select your data collection preferences from the "data_collection" dropdown menu.
6. Select your form submission preference from the "form_submission" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional comments in the "additional_comments" field.
8. Enter your signature in the "signature_1" field.
9. Enter your signature in the "signature_2" field.
10. Enter your signature in the "signature_3" field.
11. Enter your signature in the "signature_4" field.
12. Enter your signature in the "signature_5" field.
13. Enter your form submission in the "form_submission" field.
14. Enter your signature in the "signature_6" field.
15. Enter the date of your signature in the "date_of_signature" field.
16. Enter the time of your signature in the "time_of_signature" field.
17. Enter the date of the form submission in the "date" field.
18. Enter the time of the form submission in the "time" field.
19. Enter the year of the form submission in the "year" field.
20. Enter the month of the form submission in the "month" field.
21. Enter the month number of the form submission in the "month_number" field.
22. Enter the day of the form submission in the "day" field.
23. Enter your signature in the "signature" field.
24. Enter your form ID in the "form_id_1" field.
25. Enter your form ID in the "form_id_2" field.
