> # Orthodontic Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the patient's medical history, contact information, and treatment details, as well as obtain their consent for orthodontic treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic patient information (id: 1) and contact information (id: 2).
2. Enter the treatment details (id: 3) and review the consent terms (id: 4).
3. Sign the patient signature section (id: 5) to confirm your agreement to the treatment plan.
4. Sign the doctor signature section (id: 6) to confirm the doctor's agreement to the treatment plan.
5. Complete the date and time sections (id: 7-8, 16-17) to track the signing and completion dates and times.
6. Add any additional comments or notes in the patient notes (id: 12) and doctor notes (id: 13) sections.
7. Upload a digital image of your signature (id: 14) and a digital image of yourself (id: 15).
8. Select your doctor's name (id: 17) and enter their contact information (id: 18-20).
9. Enter your contact information (id: 21-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (id: 1): Provide your basic patient information, such as name and contact information.
  - Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - Enter your address, city, state, zip, and country.
  - Enter your phone number.
- **Contact Info** (id: 2): Provide additional contact information, such as email or phone number.
  - Enter your email address or phone number.
- **Treatment Details** (id: 3): Enter the specifics of your treatment, including the type of treatment and any relevant medical history.
  - Enter the type of treatment you are undergoing.
  - Enter any relevant medical history or allergies.
- **Consent Terms** (id: 4): Review the terms of your treatment, including potential risks and benefits.
  - Read and understand the terms of your treatment.
  - Ask questions or seek clarification if you have any concerns.
- **Patient Signature** (id: 5): Sign this section to confirm your agreement to the treatment plan.
  - Use the "Signature Image" section (id: 14) to upload a digital image of your signature.
- **Doctor Signature** (id: 6): Sign this section to confirm the doctor's agreement to the treatment plan.
  - Select your doctor's name from the drop-down menu or enter their name manually.
- **Date Signed** (id: 7): Enter the date you signed this form.
  - Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time Signed** (id: 8): Enter the time you signed this form.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Date Given** (id: 10): Enter the date the form was given to you.
  - Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time Given** (id: 11): Enter the time the form was given to you.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Patient Notes** (id: 12): Add any additional comments or notes about your treatment.
  - Enter any relevant comments or notes about your treatment.
- **Doctor Notes** (id: 13): Add any additional comments or notes about your treatment.
  - Enter any relevant comments or notes about your treatment.
- **Signature Image** (id: 14): Upload a digital image of your signature.
  - Upload a clear, legible image of your signature.
- **Patient Image** (id: 15): Upload a digital image of yourself.
  - Upload a clear, legible image of yourself.
- **Date Completed** (id: 16): Enter the date this form was completed.
  - Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time Completed** (id: 17): Enter the time this form was completed.
  - Enter the time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
- **Doctor** (id: 18): Select your doctor's name from the drop-down menu.
  - Select your doctor's name as it appears on their license to practice.
- **Doctor's Phone** (id: 19): Enter your doctor's phone number.
  - Enter your doctor's phone number.
- **Doctor's Email** (id: 20): Enter your doctor's email address.
  - Enter your doctor's email address.
- **Address** (id: 21): Enter your address.
  - Enter your complete address, including street, city, state, and zip.
- **City** (id: 22): Enter the city where you live.
  - Enter the city name.
- **State** (id: 23): Enter the state where you live.
  - Enter the state abbreviation.
- **Zip** (id: 24): Enter your zip code.
  - Enter the zip code.
- **Country** (id: 25): Enter the country where you live.
  - Enter the country name.
- **Phone Number** (id: 26): Enter your phone number.
  - Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review the consent terms before signing the form.
- Use clear and legible handwriting when signing your name.
- Upload clear and legible images of your signature and yourself.
- Enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
- Review and understand all terms and conditions before signing the form.
- Ask questions or seek clarification if you have any doubts or concerns.
