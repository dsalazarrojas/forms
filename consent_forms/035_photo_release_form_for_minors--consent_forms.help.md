# Photo Release Form For Minors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent or permission from minors for the use of their photo. It helps to ensure that you are respecting their rights and the rights of their parents or guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Consent Forms" category from the "Category" field, as this is the relevant option for photo release forms.
2. Provide a description of why you need to obtain the minor's photo, as this will help the minor and their parents or guardians understand the context.
3. Specify which tool or project the photo is associated with, by filling in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the "Consent Forms" option, as this is the relevant category for photo release forms.
* **Description** (`description`, note, required): Provide a brief description of why you need to take or use the minor's photo. This helps the minor and their parents or guardians understand the context and purpose of the photo.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Indicate which tool or project the photo is related to, such as a school photo shoot or a family album.
