# Professional Counselling Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by clients before their first session with the counsellor. It outlines the client's understanding of the services they will receive, the scope of the sessions, and their expectations. This form is a requirement for the start of the counselling process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form completely and accurately.
- Ensure you understand each section and provide the required information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the counsellor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Description** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the form that will help you understand its content and purpose. Please describe the services you are expecting to receive from the counsellor.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your sessions and any important updates.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached for scheduling or emergency purposes.
* **Session Length** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the expected length of each session in minutes.
* **Number of Sessions** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Specify the number of sessions you are expecting to have with the counsellor.
* **Frequency of Sessions** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the frequency of sessions you are expecting (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, As Needed).
* **Session Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Select a date for your first session. If you don't know the exact date, please leave it blank for now.
* **Agreement** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Read the agreement carefully and indicate your understanding by signing below. This is a critical part of the consent process.
* **Client Signature** (`10`, `note`, required: false): Sign and date this form to confirm your understanding of the counselling services and their scope. Please sign with your pen in the space provided.

## Tips

- If you are unsure about any section, do not hesitate to ask the counsellor for clarification.
- Ensure you understand the agreement before signing, as it outlines the responsibilities of both you and the counsellor.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- If there are any changes to the counselling plan or session schedule, you will be contacted accordingly.
