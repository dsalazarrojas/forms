# E Visits Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients for electronic visits with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided information about E-visits, including privacy protections, data collection and security, and other important details.
2. Select the patient information that applies to you, such as name, date of birth, email, phone number, and address.
3. Choose whether you have read, understood, and consent to participate in E-visits.
4. Answer any additional questions about E-visits, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **eVisits Information (Page 1)**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to read and understand the provided information about E-visits.
* **eVisits Privacy**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to read and understand the privacy protections related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Terms Of Use**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to read and understand the terms of use for E-visits.
* **Patient Information (Page 2)**
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Purpose: Please select the relevant patient information that applies to you.
	+ Options: 
		- Patient Name
		- Patient Date of Birth
		- Patient Email
		- Patient Phone
		- Patient Address
* **eVisits Consent**
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Purpose: Choose whether you have read, understood, and consent to participate in E-visits.
	+ Options: 
		- I have read, understood, and consent to participate in E-visits
		- I have not read, understood, and do not consent to participate in E-visits
* **eVisits Terminology**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to explain any terminology you are not familiar with in relation to E-visits.
* **eVisits Risks**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to list any risks or concerns related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Consequences**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to list any potential consequences of participating in E-visits.
* **eVisits Privacy Protections**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the privacy protections related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Data Collection**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the data collection processes related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Data Security**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the data security measures related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Data Retention**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the data retention policies related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Electronic Health Records**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the electronic health records related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Informal Communications**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the informal communications related to E-visits.
* **eVisits Medical Device**
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Purpose: This field is for you to understand and confirm the medical device related to E-visits.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully read and understand each field before providing your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the E-visits team.
* This form is a requirement for participating in E-visits.
