# Parent Guardian Publicity Authorization And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain authorization and release from a parent or guardian for publicity-related activities or projects. It aims to gather necessary information from parents or guardians for official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you, as a parent or guardian, grant authorization for publicity. This decision will be binding on your child.
2.  Provide your name and contact information so the organization or entity can reach you if needed.
3.  Enter your home or work address for contact purposes.
4.  Enter the state or region where you reside for administrative purposes.
5.  Enter your postal code for precise location identification.
6.  Enter the city or town where you reside for context.
7.  If applicable, provide your date of birth for verification or other administrative reasons.
8.  Please provide a brief description of the project or publicity activity your child is involved in, including details on what you, as a parent or guardian, are aware of. This will help the organization or entity understand your involvement.
9.  Finally, select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you, as a parent or guardian, release your child for publicity. This decision will be binding on your child. Please note that this form is for publicity purposes only and does not cover other permissions or releases that may be required for different activities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Guardian Publicity Authorization**: Select 'Yes' to grant authorization for your child's publicity. This means you, as a parent or guardian, give explicit consent for your child's image, name, and/or likeness to be used for publicity purposes.
* **Name**: Provide your name so the organization or entity can contact you with updates, questions, or concerns related to your child.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number to be contacted in case of any issues related to this form or your child's publicity.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from the organization or entity.
* **Address**: Enter your home or work address so the organization or entity can contact you if needed.
* **State or Region**: Enter your state or region of residence for administrative purposes.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your postal or zip code for location identification.
* **City or Town**: Enter your city or town of residence for context.
* **Date of Birth**: If applicable, enter your date of birth for verification or other administrative reasons. (This field may not be required for every case)
* **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of the project or activity your child is involved in, including what you know about it.
* **Consent**: Select 'Yes' to indicate that you, as a parent or guardian, grant consent for your child's image, name, and/or likeness to be used in publicity. Please note that this is different from 'Release' as consent grants access for publicity purposes only, while release grants more extensive permissions.
*   **Release**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you, as a parent or guardian, release your child completely for all types of publicity and other activities. This decision will be binding on your child and grants more extensive permissions than consent.
    Please note that this form is designed for publicity purposes, and you should only release your child if you are comfortable with it.
