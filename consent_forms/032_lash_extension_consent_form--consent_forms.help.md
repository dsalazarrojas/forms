# lash_extension_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain client consent and collect relevant information for lash extension services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as indicated in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
2. Review each field to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of your service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, text, required): A statement of your agreement to the terms of your lash extension service.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, text, required): A confirmation that you have received clearance from a medical professional before receiving lash services.
* **Lash Technician Name** (`lash_technician_name`, text, optional): The name of the lash technician performing your service.
* **Allergic Reactions** (`allergic_reactions`, text, required): A statement of any allergic reactions you have experienced related to lash services.
* **Lash Extensions Previously Worn** (`lash_extensions_worn`, select multiple, required): A confirmation of any previous lash extensions you have worn.
* **Lash Extension Type Details** (`lash_extension_type_details`, text, optional): Additional information about your lash extensions.
* **Lash Technician Signature** (`lash_technician_signature`, date, required): A date and time stamp of the lash technician's agreement to perform services.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, date, required): A date and time stamp of your agreement to receive services.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, required): A confirmation of the date and time of the service.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Your name and contact information.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location where the service is being performed.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required): Emergency contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Any relevant medical information related to the service.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): A list of any allergies you have.
* **Insurance** (`insurance`, text, optional): Insurance information related to the service.
* **Client Notes** (`client_note`, text, optional): Additional comments or notes related to your service.
