# Runway Image Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Runway Image Release Form is used to obtain consent from models and individuals whose images are used in runway, fashion, or photography projects. It helps establish a record of permission and provides a clear understanding of the usage of the image.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Runway Image Release Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all the requested information.
2. Ensure that you have the required consent from the models or individuals whose images are being used.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the individual who is releasing their image.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address associated with the individual who is releasing their image.
* **Date** (`date_of_capture`, date, required): Enter the date the image was captured.
* **Time** (`time_of_capture`, time, required): Enter the time the image was captured.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required): Please enter the name of the photographer who captured the image.
* **Model** (`model_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the model or individual whose image is being used.
* **Consent** (`consent_given`, select_one, required): Indicate whether consent was given for the image to be used. Choose 'True' if consent was given, and 'False' if not.
* **Image** (`image_description`, text, required): Provide a description of the image being released.
* **Location** (`image_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the image was captured.
* **Size** (`image_size`, text, required): Provide the size of the image being released.
* **File** (`image_file`, text, required): Enter the file name or reference number of the image being released.
* **URL** (`image_url`, text, required): Enter the URL or reference number of the image being released.

## Tips
* Make sure to obtain consent from the models or individuals whose images are being used before submitting this form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the image owner or relevant parties for clarification.
