# Wildlife Photography Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from individuals or organizations for wildlife photography purposes. It gathers information about the photographer, their project, and any specific restrictions or restrictions related to the photoshoot. This form is designed for internal use and should only be used for wildlife photography projects that require permission from landowners, wildlife agencies, or other relevant parties. Before starting the form, ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals in place, as this form only serves as a placeholder for official records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine whether you are submitting as an individual or an organization.
2. Fill out the name field with your name or your organization's name.
3. Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., name@domain.com).
4. Provide your contact phone number.
5. Select the type of permission you are seeking (General Permission or Specific Permission).
6. Enter the location where the photoshoot will take place.
7. Specify the duration of the photoshoot.
8. Describe any specific restrictions or limitations that apply to the photoshoot.
9. Add any additional notes or details relevant to the photoshoot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### individual_or_organization (Individual / Organization)
* This field allows you to specify if you are submitting as an individual or an organization.
* Please select the correct option that applies to your situation.

### name
* Enter your name if you are an individual or your organization's name if you are an organization.

### email
* Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., name@domain.com).

### phone
* Provide your contact phone number.

### permission_type
* Select the type of permission you are seeking:
	+ General Permission: Permission for a broad range of photography activities.
	+ Specific Permission: Permission for a specific photography activity or project.

### location
* Enter the location where the photoshoot will take place.

### duration
* Specify the duration of the photoshoot.

### restrictions
* Describe any specific restrictions or limitations that apply to the photoshoot.

### notes
* Add any additional notes or details relevant to the photoshoot.

Note: This guide is intended to be a general reference and may need to be adapted based on specific project requirements and regulations.
