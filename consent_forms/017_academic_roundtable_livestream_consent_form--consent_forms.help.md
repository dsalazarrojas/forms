# Academic Roundtable Livestream Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Roundtable Livestream Consent Form is used to collect consent from participants for recording and livestreaming their participation in academic roundtables. This form is essential to ensure that participants' rights and interests are respected and that the recorded content can be shared and used according to their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Email address**: Enter your institutional email address associated with your role in the roundtable (e.g., your university email).
3. **Phone number**: If you have a phone number associated with your role in the roundtable, enter it. If not, leave this field blank.
4. **Your role in the roundtable**: Select your role in the roundtable from the options provided:
	* Panelist or Speaker
	* Moderator
	* Discussant
	* Audience Member
	* Organizer
5. **Institutional affiliation**: Enter your institutional affiliation (e.g., your university name).
6. **Title of the event**: Enter the title of the roundtable event.
7. **Date of the event**: Enter the date of the event.
8. **Consent to livestream**: Select "Yes - I consent" to allow your participation to be live streamed.
9. **Consent to recording**: Select "Yes - I consent" to allow your participation to be recorded for future use.
10. **Distribution platforms**: Select all the platforms where you consent to the recording being shared:
	* Institutional website
	* YouTube
	* Social media
	* Academic repositories
	* Internal use only
11. **Consent to display name and title**: Select "True" to allow your name and title to be displayed during the livestream.
12. **Understanding of right to withdraw**: Select "Yes - I understand" to acknowledge that you can request removal of your portion from the recording within 30 days of the event.
13. **Date of consent**: Enter the date you are consenting to the livestream and recording.
14. **Additional conditions or restrictions**: If you have any additional conditions or restrictions, enter them in this field.
