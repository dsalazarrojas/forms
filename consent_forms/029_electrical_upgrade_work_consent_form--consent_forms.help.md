# electrical_upgrade_work_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The electrical_upgrade_work_consent_form is used to obtain consent from the customer for electrical upgrade work. The form captures essential information about the scope of work, customer details, upgrade details, approval status, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the scope of work in the "Scope of Work" field.
2. Provide customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Upgrade Details" field to indicate whether the upgrade is approved.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for multiple approval statuses in the "Approval" field.
5. Provide your signature in the "Signature" field, indicating your acceptance of the electrical upgrade work.
6. (Optional) If required by the customer, provide their signature in the "Customer Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the electrical upgrade work to be performed.
- **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **Upgrade Details** (`upgrade_details`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that the upgrade is approved, or "No" to indicate that it is not.
- **Approval** (`approval`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple approval statuses, such as "Yes" for approval and "No" for cancellation.
- **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name to indicate your acceptance of the electrical upgrade work.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, time, optional): If required by the customer, enter their signature to confirm their acceptance of the electrical upgrade work.
