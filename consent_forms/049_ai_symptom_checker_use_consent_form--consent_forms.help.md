# AI Symptom Checker Use Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI Symptom Checker Use Consent Form is a crucial document that you must complete to access the AI symptom checker tool. This form is designed to collect your personal and health-related information for the purpose of using the symptom checker. By filling out this form, you are providing us with the necessary information to provide you with the best possible outcome for your health concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your patient information:
	*   **Full Name**: Your complete name as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
	*   **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth as it appears on your ID or other official documents.
2.  Choose your biological sex:
	*   Select your biological sex from the options provided.
3.  Enter your contact details:
	*   **Email Address**: Your active email address where we can reach you for follow-up or additional information.
	*   **Phone Number**: Your phone number where we can reach you in case of emergencies (optional).
4.  Describe your health concerns:
	*   **Primary Symptoms or Reason for Checking**: Briefly describe what you are experiencing.
5.  Specify the duration of your symptoms:
	*   **Duration of Symptoms**: Choose the duration of your current symptoms from the options provided.
6.  Indicate the severity of your discomfort:
	*   **Severity of Current Discomfort**: Choose the level of discomfort you are feeling from 1 to 10.
7.  Acknowledge important disclaimers:
	*   **NOT A MEDICAL DIAGNOSIS**: The AI symptom checker is for informational purposes only, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
	*   **NOT FOR EMERGENCY SITUATIONS**: This tool is not designed for emergency medical situations. If you are experiencing severe symptoms, call 911 or your local emergency services immediately.
8.  Understand that the AI may provide incorrect, incomplete, or biased information:
	*   Acknowledge that the AI symptom checker may not always be accurate or comprehensive.
9.  Consent to data collection and processing:
	*   **I Consent to the Collection and Processing of My Health Data**: By completing this form, you are giving us permission to collect and process your health data for the purpose of the symptom checker.
10.  Understand your data handling:
	*   **I understand that my data will be handled according to the Privacy Policy (HIPAA/GDPR compliant)**.
11.  Acknowledge emergency symptoms:
	*   **IF YOU ARE EXPERIACING CHEST PAIN, DIFFICULTY BREATHING, OR SEVERE BLEEDING, CALL 911 OR LOCAL EMERGENCY SERVICES IMMEDIATELY**.
12.  Sign the form digitally:
	*   **Type your name as a Digital Signature**. By typing your name, you are providing a legal digital signature.
