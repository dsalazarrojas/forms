# Parental Consent And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for a child's participation in a program or activity. It also includes a release of any potential liability for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Fill out the required fields, including "Child Participation" and "Parent/Guardian Permission".
3. Select the correct "Contact Info" field to indicate who will be the primary contact person.
4. Add any additional notes if necessary.
5. Sign the form to indicate your consent and acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Participation** (<type:text>): Please describe your child's planned participation in the program or activity, including the specific details such as dates and times.
* **Program Participation** (<type:text>): Describe the program or activity your child will be participating in, including the specific details such as dates and times.
* **Contact Info** (<type:select_multiple>): Choose one or more from the following options:
	+ Mom or Dad: This will be the primary contact person for your child.
	+ Child: This will be the child's primary contact person.
	+ Other: Select "Other" and specify the name and contact information of the primary contact person.
* **Guardian Permission** (<type:select_one>): Select "True" if you grant permission for your child's participation in the program or activity, or "False" otherwise.
* **Additional Notes** (<type:text>): If you need to provide any additional information or clarification, please include it here.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (<type:text>): Please sign your name to confirm your consent and acceptance of the terms.
