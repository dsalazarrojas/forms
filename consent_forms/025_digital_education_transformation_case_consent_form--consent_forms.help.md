# Digital Education Transformation Case Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Education Transformation Case Consent Form is a mandatory document that requires you to provide personal details and consent for program participation. Please fill out this form accurately and carefully, as the information collected will be used to support your case.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** with accurate information.
2. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1999-01-01).
4. Enter your **Age** (numeric value).
5. Select your **Country of Residency** from the provided options.
6. Choose your **Gender** from the options provided.
7. Select whether you **Consent** to participate in the program (True or False).
8. Enter the **Date of Submission** in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please fill in your first name as you wish to be addressed in the program.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for proper identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you frequently use for communication.
* **Phone**: Your contact number for any further communication from us.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birth date in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1999-01-01).
* **Age**: Enter your age as a numeric value (e.g., 30).
* **Country of Residency**: Choose your country of permanent residence from the options provided.
* **Gender**: Select your gender as it pertains to your identity.
* **Consent**: Select True if you agree to participate in the program, and False otherwise.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-01-01).
