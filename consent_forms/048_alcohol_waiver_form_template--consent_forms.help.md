# Alcohol Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to obtain a participant's consent and waiver for an alcohol-related event. It ensures that the participant is aware of the risks associated with alcohol consumption and releases the organizers from any liability related to injuries, property damage, or legal consequences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	* **Event Name and Location**: Enter the name and location of the event you are participating in.
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
2. Confirm your consent and responsibility:
	* **I confirm I am of legal drinking age**: Select the checkbox that confirms you are of legal drinking age.
	* **I accept full responsibility for my alcohol consumption**: Select the checkbox that confirms you accept responsibility for your alcohol consumption.
	* **I release the organizers from any liability**: Select the checkbox that releases the organizers from any liability related to injuries, property damage, or legal consequences.
	* **I agree not to operate a motor vehicle while impaired**: Select the checkbox that confirms you will not operate a motor vehicle while impaired by alcohol.
3. Provide emergency contact information:
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
4. Sign and date the form:
	* **Date Signed**: Enter the current date.
5. Final acceptance:
	* **I have read and fully understand this waiver and release**: Select the checkbox that confirms you have read and understood the waiver and release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Name and Location**: The name and location of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event.
* **I confirm I am of legal drinking age**: A checkbox confirming you are of legal drinking age. Please ensure you are actually over the age of 18 years old.
* **I accept full responsibility for my alcohol consumption**: A checkbox confirming you accept responsibility for your alcohol consumption.
* **I release the organizers from any liability**: A checkbox releasing the organizers from any liability related to injuries, property damage, or legal consequences.
* **I agree not to operate a motor vehicle while impaired**: A checkbox confirming you will not operate a motor vehicle while impaired by alcohol.
* **My plan for safe transportation after the event**: A dropdown menu with options for how you plan to get home safely.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The name of a contact person to reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Date Signed**: The current date.
* **I have read and fully understand this waiver and release**: A checkbox confirming you have read and understood the waiver and release.

## Tips

* Ensure you carefully read and understand the waiver and release before signing.
* Make sure you have a plan for safe transportation after the event.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
