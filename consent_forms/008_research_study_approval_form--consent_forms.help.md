# Research Study Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Research Study Approval Form is used to gather necessary information about a new research study, which will then be reviewed and approved by relevant authorities before the study can begin.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Research Group" field with the name of your research team or group.
2. Enter the name of the "Research Institution" where the study will take place.
3. Provide a brief "Study Description" to give context to your research project.
4. Choose the "Study Type" that best fits your project (Yes, No, or Other).
5. Select the "Start Date" and "End Date" of your study.
6. Enter the expected "Study Duration" in days.
7. Select the "Study Status" (Yes, No, or Other).
8. If applicable, enter the name of the "Contact Person" who will be leading the study.
9. If applicable, enter the name of the "Research Group 2" and "Study Location" where the study will take place.
10. If applicable, provide information on the "Study Funding" and "Research Institution 2" where the study will take place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Group** (`research_group`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your research team or group.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the institution where the study will take place.
* **Study Name** (`study_name`, `text`, required): Enter a brief name for your study.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, `text`, required): Give a brief description of your research project.
* **Study Type** (`study_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of study you are conducting (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Study Start Date** (`study_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the study will start.
* **Study End Date** (`study_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the study will end.
* **Study Duration** (`study_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the expected duration of the study in days.
* **Study Status** (`study_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of your study (Yes, No, or Other).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `note`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the contact person leading the study.
* **Research Group 2** (`research_group_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the second research group.
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the study will take place.
* **Study Funding** (`study_funding`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide information on the study funding sources.
* **Research Institution 2** (`research_institution_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the second research institution.
* **Study Name 2** (`study_name_2`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the second study.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate and complete.
