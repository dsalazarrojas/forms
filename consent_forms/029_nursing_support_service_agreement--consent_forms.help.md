# Nursing Support Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a patient's consent for nursing services, which includes information about their personal details, authorized care team members, and specific services they will receive.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Review each field's explanation for specific guidance on what to enter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nursing Support Service Agreement** (`service_agreement`, text, optional): Enter a brief statement indicating your consent to receive nursing services. This is an optional field and can be left blank if you are not comfortable providing a statement.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information, to help our team identify and contact you.
* **Authorized Care Team** (`prophets`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the following options to indicate who has authority to provide care:
	+ **Nurse**: Choose this option if you have authorized a nurse to provide care.
	+ **Other**: Choose this option if you have authorized someone else to provide care.
* **Care Team Members** (`care_team`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the nurses who will provide care for you. You can choose one or more nurses from the list:
	+ **Nurse 1**
	+ **Nurse 2**
	+ **Nurse 3**
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services you will receive:
	+ **Home care**: Choose this option if you will receive care at home.
	+ **Clinic**: Choose this option if you will receive care at a clinic.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
