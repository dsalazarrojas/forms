# Nurse Practitioner Preceptor Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your clinical rotation or placement as a Nurse Practitioner. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have the necessary details for effective communication and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by providing your name, as we need to know who is submitting this information.
2. Next, specify your role as a preceptor, as this will help us understand your responsibilities and expectations during the rotation.
3. Select the correct rotation options from the provided choices, as this will help us determine the specifics of your clinical rotation.
4. Enter the address and contact information for your clinical site, so we can stay in touch with you.
5. Provide the dates and time for your rotation, as this is crucial for planning and coordination.
6. Finally, confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and complete by checking the "I confirm" checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`form_2`): Please provide your full name as a Nurse Practitioner.
* **What is your role as a preceptor?** (`form_3`): Specify your role as a preceptor, such as primary preceptor or co-preceptor.
* **Rotation Information** (`form_4`): Select one of the following options:
	+ What is the rotation duration?
	+ What are the rotation dates?
	+ What is the rotation time?
* **Clinical Site Information** (`form_5`): Enter the name or description of your clinical site.
* **What is the clinical site address?** (`form_6`): Enter the address of your clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Contact** (`form_7`): Enter the contact name and information for your clinical site.
* **Rotation Dates and Time** (`form_8`): Select the date range for your rotation.
* **What is the rotation start date?** (`form_9`): Enter the start date of your rotation.
* **What is the rotation end date?** (`form_10`): Enter the end date of your rotation.
* **What is the rotation start time?** (`form_11`): Enter the start time of your rotation.
* **What is the rotation end time?** (`form_12`): Enter the end time of your rotation.
* **Additional Information** (`form_13`): If you have any additional information about your clinical site or rotation, please enter it here.
* **Is there any additional information you would like to provide about your clinical site or rotation?** (`form_14`): If you have any further details to share, please enter them here.
* **I confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete** (`form_15`): Please check this box to confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and reliable.
* **Signature of the preceptor** (`form_16`): Please sign your name to confirm your commitment to the clinical rotation.
