# Towing Damage Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is for collecting information and gathering signatures from customers and towing companies after a towing service has been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Customer Details**
	* Fill out your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
2. **Vehicle Details**
	* Provide the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
3. **Towing Company Details**
	* Fill out the name and address of the towing company that performed the service.
4. **Liability Release**
	* Indicate your understanding of the waiver and release of liability.
5. **Vehicle Condition**
	* Choose the condition of your vehicle before and after the towing service.
6. **Towing Services**
	* Select all the services that were provided by the towing company.
7. **Signature**
	* Sign this section to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver and release of liability.
8. **Customer Signature**
	* Sign this section to confirm that you, as the customer, have read and understood the contents of this form.
9. **Towing Company Signature**
	* Sign this section to confirm that the towing company has read and understood the contents of this form.
10. **Customer Acknowledgement**
	* Confirm that you understand that this form is a document of understanding and acknowledgment.
11. **Towing Company Acknowledgement**
	* Confirm that the towing company has read and understood the contents of this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details (1)**: Provide your personal details, such as your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Vehicle Details (2)**: Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
* **Towing Company Details (3)**: Enter the name and address of the towing company that performed the service.
* **Liability Release (4)**: Confirm that you understand the waiver and release of liability by signing this section.
* **Vehicle Condition (5)**: Choose the condition of your vehicle before and after the towing service.
* **Towing Services (6)**: Select all the services that were provided by the towing company.
* **Signature (7)**: Sign to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver and release of liability.
* **Customer Signature (8)**: Sign to confirm that you, as the customer, have read and understood the contents of this form.
* **Towing Company Signature (9)**: Sign to confirm that the towing company has read and understood the contents of this form.
* **Customer Acknowledgement (10)**: Confirm that you understand that this form is a document of understanding and acknowledgment.
* **Towing Company Acknowledgement (11)**: Confirm that the towing company has read and understood the contents of this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before signing the form.
* Ensure that you, as the customer, have read and understood the contents of this form before signing.
* Ensure that the towing company has read and understood the contents of this form before signing.
