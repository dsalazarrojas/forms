# Vaccination Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's vaccination history and contact details, to ensure a smooth delivery of vaccinations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is **patient_information**.
2. Enter any relevant vaccination dates in the **vaccination_dates** field.
3. Add any relevant contact details in the **contact_details** field.
4. If you have any medical history that is relevant to the vaccination, please enter it in the **medical_history** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_information**: A field to collect general information about the patient, such as name, address, or contact numbers.
* **vaccination_dates**: Enter any past vaccination dates that are relevant to the patient's vaccination history.
* **contact_details**: Provide contact details such as phone numbers or email addresses where you can reach the patient for follow-up.
* **medical_history**: A field to collect any relevant medical history that may impact the vaccination delivery.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and comprehensive as possible when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for help or clarification.
