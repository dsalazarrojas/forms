# Back Facial Deep Cleansing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back Facial Deep Cleansing Consent Form is a mandatory document that needs to be completed before undergoing a Back Facial Deep Cleansing treatment. This form helps clients understand the treatment process, potential risks, and post-treatment care instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please carefully read each field explanation and select the appropriate options.
2. Enter your client's information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent and acknowledgment of the treatment risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Primary Skin Concerns for your back**: Select all the skin concerns that apply to your back (e.g., Acne/Breakouts, Congestion/Blackheads, Dryness/Flakiness, etc.).
* **Current Topical Meds**: Select any topical medications or products you are currently using, including Retin-A / Tretinoin, Accutane (within last 6 months), etc.
* **Known Allergies to Skincare Ingredients**: List any known allergies to skincare ingredients, such as Salicylic acid, Aloe, Fragrance, etc.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions**: List any relevant medical conditions that may impact your skin (e.g., Eczema, Psoriasis, Keloid scarring, etc.).
* **Description of Deep Cleansing Treatment**: Please review the treatment description, which includes cleansing, exfoliation, extractions, and a customized mask.
* **Do you consent to manual extractions?**: Choose "Yes, I consent" if you are comfortable with manual extractions.
* **I understand that outcomes can vary and some risks exist**: Select "I understand and accept the risks" to acknowledge the potential risks and outcomes.
* **I agree to follow the provided post-treatment care instructions**: Choose "I agree" to commit to following the post-treatment care instructions.
* **Digital Signature Acknowledgment**: Select "Yes, this is my signature" to confirm you have read and understood the form.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Technician or Witness Name**: Enter the name of the technician or witness who will be performing the treatment.

## Tips

* Please take your time and carefully review each field before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the technician or ask for clarification.
* Remember to sign and date the form to confirm your consent and acknowledgment.
