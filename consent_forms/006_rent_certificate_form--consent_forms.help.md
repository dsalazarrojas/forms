# rent_certificate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rent_certificate_form is a document used to report the receipt of rent from a landlord for a specific period of time. This form is typically used by landlords or their representatives to certify that they have received rent from a tenant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Tenant Name** field with the name of the tenant who paid the rent.
2. Enter the **Landlord Name** field with the name of the landlord who received the rent.
3. Provide the **Address** where the rent was received.
4. Enter the **Period of Time** field with the duration for which the rent was received (e.g., 1 month, 2 months, etc.).
5. Enter the **Received Rent** field with the amount of rent received.
6. Enter the **Date Signed** field with the date the rent was signed for.
7. Enter the **Date Received** field with the date the rent was received.
8. Place your **Signature of Lawer** in the space provided.
9. Place your **Signature of Tenant** in the space provided.
10. Enter the **Date Signed by Lawer** field with the date the document was signed by the lawer.
11. Enter the **Date Signed by Tenant** field with the date the document was signed by the tenant.
12. Enter the **Certificate Number** field with a unique number assigned to the certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant who paid the rent.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the landlord who received the rent.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Provide the address where the rent was received.
* **Period of Time** (`period_of_time`, number, required): Enter the duration for which the rent was received (e.g., 1 month, 2 months, etc.).
* **Received Rent** (`received_rent`, number, required): Enter the amount of rent received.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date the rent was signed for.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required): Enter the date the rent was received.
* **Signature of Lawer** (`signature_of_lawer`, note, required): Place your signature as the lawer.
* **Signature of Tenant** (`signature_of_tenant`, note, required): Place your signature as the tenant.
* **Date Signed by Lawer** (`date_signed_lawer`, date, required): Enter the date the document was signed by the lawer.
* **Date Signed by Tenant** (`date_signed_tenant`, date, required): Enter the date the document was signed by the tenant.
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, text, required): Enter a unique number assigned to the certificate.
