# Resident Access Badge Renewal Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Access Badge Renewal Consent Form is used to request and verify the renewal of a resident's access badge. This form is required for property managers and residents to provide their signatures and contact information to confirm their identities and authorize the badge renewal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, including your contact information and any relevant notes.
2. Make sure to sign and date the form as required.
3. Have the property manager and resident sign and date the form as required.
4. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Renewal** (`Request Renewal`, `text`, required): This is where you will enter your request for badge renewal. Please provide a clear and concise description of why you need to renew your badge.
* **Resident's Signature** (`resident_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your signature to confirm that you are the resident requesting badge renewal.
* **Request Date** (`renewal_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are requesting the badge to be renewed.
* **Property Manager's Signature** (`property_manager_signature`, `text`, required): Sign and date your signature to confirm that you are the property manager authorizing the badge renewal.
* **Property Manager's Name** (`property_manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the property manager.
* **Property Manager's Title** (`property_manager_title`, `text`, required): Enter your title as the property manager (e.g. 'Building Manager', 'Property Manager', etc.).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number so that the badge renewal team can reach out to you.
* **Property Manager's Email** (`property_manager_email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address as the property manager.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the property manager.
* **Property Manager Notes** (`property_manager_notes`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for the property manager to add any additional notes about the badge renewal.
* **Resident's Notes** (`resident_notes`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you as the resident to add any additional notes about the badge renewal.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign and date all required fields to confirm your identities.
* Be clear and concise in your request and notes sections.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the badge renewal team for assistance.
