# GCIC Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The GCIC Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from individuals for various background checks. This form is typically used in conjunction with other forms and processes to verify information. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the consent is valid and applicable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the form's initial page, which is the GCIC Consent Form page.
2. On this page, you will find a section to select one of the options for background checks. Choose the option that best suits your needs.
3. If you need to select multiple background check types, use the checkboxes provided.
4. On the second page, you will be asked to enter your date of birth. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. On the second page, you will also be asked to enter your email address. Enter your email address exactly as it appears in your records.
6. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all necessary information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **GCIC Consent Form** (`gcic_consent_form_page_1`, note, required: false): This is the initial page of the form where you will find the background check options.
* **Consent To Background Check** (`consent_to_background_check`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options for background checks that you wish to consent to. This will help the GCIC process proceed with the chosen option.
* **Background Check Type** (`background_check_type`, select_multiple, required: false): If you selected the option for multiple background checks, use the checkboxes provided to select all the types you need.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address exactly as it appears in your records. 

## Tips
- Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authority to fill out the form.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or personnel for assistance.
