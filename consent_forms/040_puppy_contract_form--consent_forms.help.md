# puppy_contract_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The puppy_contract_form is a form designed to record the details of a sale between a seller and a buyer. This form ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of the terms and conditions of the sale.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Seller Name, Buyer Name, and Puppy ID fields with the relevant information.
2. Enter the Purchase Price of the puppy.
3. Select the Sale Date of the sale.
4. Sign the form as the Seller and Buyer, indicating your agreement with the terms of the sale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Name** (`seller_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the seller who is giving the puppy for sale.
* **Buyer Name** (`buyer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the buyer who is purchasing the puppy.
* **Puppy ID** (`puppy_id`, `text`, required): Enter the identification number or name of the puppy being sold.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, required): Enter the price at which the puppy is being sold.
* **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, `date`, required): Select the date on which the sale was finalized.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, `text`, required): Sign here to indicate your agreement to the sale as the seller.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, `text`, required): Sign here to indicate your agreement to the sale as the buyer.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
- Review your completed form for any errors before finalizing the sale.
- Both the seller and buyer should retain a copy of this form for their records.
