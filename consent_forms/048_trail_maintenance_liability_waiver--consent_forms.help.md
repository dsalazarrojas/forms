# Trail Maintenance Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting participant information for trail maintenance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information for the participant and their vehicle. You can access the form fields below:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`): Confirm that you have read and understand the waiver and agreement terms.
* **Trail Location** (`trail_location`): Enter the location of the trail maintenance project (optional).
* **Trail Date** (`trail_date`): Enter the date of the trail maintenance project.
* **Trail Time** (`trail_time`): Enter the time of the trail maintenance project.
* **Participant Duties** (`participant_duties`): Describe the tasks the participant will be performing during the trail maintenance project.
* **Participant Supervises** (`participant_supervises`): Select whether the participant will be supervising children or not.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact information for an emergency contact.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): Add any additional notes about the participant (optional).
* **Staff Agreement** (`staff_agreement`): Confirm that you have read and understand the waiver and agreement terms for staff members.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter the staff member's name.
* **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`): Enter the staff member's phone number.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`): Enter the staff member's email address.
* **Staff Duties** (`staff_duties`): Describe the tasks the staff member will be performing during the trail maintenance project.
* **Vehicle Make/Model** (`vehicle_make_and_model`): Enter the make and model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`): Enter the vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`): Confirm that the vehicle is insured.
* **Vehicle Plate** (`vehicle_plate`): Enter the vehicle registration plate number.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`): Enter the vehicle registration expiration date.
* **Vehicle Notes** (`vehicle_notes`): Add any additional notes about the vehicle (optional).
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`): Add any additional notes about the submission (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
