# Hybrid Learning Session Replay Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hybrid Learning Session Replay Consent Form is a tool used to obtain consent from participants for the recording and replay of hybrid learning sessions. This form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms of the recording, which is essential for building trust and maintaining participant privacy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter participant name**: Provide your name as a participant in the session.
2. **Enter participant email**: Enter your email address as a participant in the session.
3. **Select session ID**: Choose the ID of the session you are participating in (not required).
4. **Enter session title**: Enter the title of the session you are participating in (required).
5. **Select recording options**: Decide if you consent to recording the session and if you want the recording to include audio.
6. **Enter participant notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about your participation in the session (not required).
7. **Enter session notes**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about the session (not required).
8. **Provide consent**: Select the option that indicates your consent to recording the session (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name (Text)**: Enter your name as a participant in the session.
* **participant_email (Email)**: Enter your email address as a participant in the session.
* **session_id (Select One)**: Choose the ID of the session you are participating in. This field is not required, but it helps the session organizers track your participation.
* **session_title (Text)**: Enter the title of the session you are participating in. This field is required to ensure that the session is accurately represented.
* **recorded_for_replay (Select Multiple)**: Decide if you consent to recording the session and if you want the recording to include audio. Select "Yes" if you consent to recording the session, and select "No" if you do not consent.
* **reuse_with_audio (Select Multiple)**: If you select "Yes" to recording the session, decide if you want the recording to include audio. Select "Yes" if you want audio included, and select "No" if you do not want audio included.
* **participant_notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about your participation in the session. This field is not required, but it helps you provide more information about your experience.
* **session_notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about the session. This field is not required, but it helps you provide more context about the session.
* **consent (Select One)**: Select the option that indicates your consent to recording the session. Select "I consent to recording this session" if you agree to recording, "I do not consent to recording this session" if you do not agree, or "Decline" if you decline consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (participant email, session title, and consent).
* If you select "Yes" to recording the session, be aware that the recording may be made available for reuse by session organizers or participants.
* If you select "No" to recording the session, your responses will not be recorded.
* If you select "Decline" to consent, your participation will not be recorded.
* Use this form to ensure that you are aware of and agree to the terms of the recording, which is essential for building trust and maintaining participant privacy.
