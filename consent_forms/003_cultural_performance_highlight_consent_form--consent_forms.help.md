# cultural_performance_highlight_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "cultural_performance_highlight_consent_form" form is designed to collect information from participants in a cultural performance event. The form aims to gather essential details from the participants to ensure that their images or performances are used correctly in the event's marketing and promotional materials.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. Review the Agreement field carefully, as it affects the usage of your image and performance.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for the Agreement field based on your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself, if needed.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be represented in the event's materials.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you agree to the usage of your image or performance in the event's materials, or "No" if you do not. Please review the usage terms carefully.
* **Performance Info** (`performance_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide additional details about your performance or activity.
* **Performance Name** (`performance_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your performance or activity.
* **Performance Start Date** (`performance_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your performance started.
* **Performance Start Time** (`performance_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of your performance.
* **Performance End Date** (`performance_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when your performance ended.
* **Image Usage** (`image_usage`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the image(s) taken during your performance.
* **Image Name** (`image_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the image(s) taken during your performance.
* **Image Start Date** (`image_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the images were taken.
* **Image End Date** (`image_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the images were taken.
* **Image Usage Agreement** (`image_usage_agreement`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you agree to the usage of your image(s) in the event's materials, or "No" if you do not. Please review the usage terms carefully.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure correct representation in the event's materials.
* Review the Agreement fields carefully to understand the terms of usage for your image and performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for clarification.
