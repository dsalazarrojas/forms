# Rock Climbing Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a basic waiver form designed to collect essential information from participants in rock climbing activities. It includes fields for participant information, waiver details, emergency contact, and participant signature.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields (if any) are filled out.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter your basic participant information. This section includes details about yourself, such as your name, age, and any other relevant information the organizer may require.
* **Waiver Information**: In this section, you will provide details about the waiver you are submitting. This may include the reason for climbing, your physical condition, and any medical conditions you may have.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Signature**: A participant signature is required to confirm that you have read and understand the waiver terms.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have read and understood the terms of the waiver before signing.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask the organizer for clarification.
- A clear and accurate response will help to ensure the waiver process is smooth and efficient.
