# Cultural Documentary Footage Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Documentary Footage Consent Form is designed to obtain participant consent for cultural documentary footage. It is essential for participants to understand the form's purpose and content to ensure accurate and respectful completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your participant name in the **Participant Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Participant Email** field.
3.  Enter your contact number in the **Contact Number** field.
4.  Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field.
5.  Provide a description of the footage you consented to in the **Description of Footage** field.
6.  Specify the location of the footage, if applicable, in the **Location of Footage** field.
7.  Select your preferred release option in the **Release Form** field:
    *   Signed and returned
    *   Signed and not returned
    *   Other (please specify)
8.  Optionally, sign your name in the **Signed by** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
*   **Participant Name** (required): A field for the participant to enter their name for consent and identification purposes.

### Participant Email
*   **Participant Email** (required): A field for the participant to enter their contact email for communication and consent purposes.

### Contact Number
*   **Contact Number** (required): A field for the participant to enter their contact number for communication purposes.

### Address
*   **Address** (optional): A field for the participant to enter their address, if they feel it is necessary to share.

### Date of Birth
*   **Date of Birth** (required): A field for the participant to enter their date of birth for age verification purposes.

### Description of Footage
*   **Description of Footage** (required): A field for the participant to describe the cultural documentary footage they have consented to.

### Location of Footage
*   **Location of Footage** (optional): A field for the participant to specify the location where the footage was recorded, if applicable.

### Release Form
*   **Release Form** (required): A field for the participant to select their release preference:
    *   Signed and returned
    *   Signed and not returned
    *   Other (please specify)

### Signed by
*   **Signed by** (optional): A field for the participant to sign their name, if desired.
