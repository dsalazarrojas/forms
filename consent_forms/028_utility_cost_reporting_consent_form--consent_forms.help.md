# Utility Cost Reporting Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the tenant's consent to collect and report their utility cost data. It is essential to fill this form to ensure accurate and reliable data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please read the instructions carefully before filling out the form.
2. If you agree to report your utility cost data, select "Utility Cost Reporting Consent" in the dropdown list on page 2.
3. If you disagree or have other preferences, choose "Other" in the dropdown list on page 2.
4. Confirm your choice by selecting "True" on page 3, if you agree to report your costs.
5. Finally, review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Utility Cost Reporting Consent Form**: This is the main title of the form.
* **Utility Cost Reporting Consent** (on page 2): Choose this option if you give permission to report your utility cost data.
* **Other** (on page 2): Choose this option if you have other preferences or disagree with reporting your utility costs.
* **Do you give permission to collect and report your utility cost data?** (on page 3):
	+ **True**: Select this option if you give permission to report your costs.
	+ **False**: Select this option if you disagree with reporting your costs.

## Tips
- Please fill the form accurately and honestly to ensure reliable data.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact the form administrators.
- The collected data will be used for utility cost reporting purposes only.
