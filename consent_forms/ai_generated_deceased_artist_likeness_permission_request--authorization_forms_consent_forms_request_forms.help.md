# AI Generated Deceased Artist Likeness Permission Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants seeking permission to use the AI-generated likeness of a deceased artist. The form aims to determine the scope of usage, intended purpose, and terms of use for the AI-generated likeness.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Ensure that you have permission from the rights holder or estate representative to submit this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of request**: The date you are requesting permission for the usage of the AI-generated likeness.
* **Type of request**: Select the type of request you are making:
	+ New permission request
	+ Renewal of existing permission
	+ Modification to existing permission
	+ Commercial license extension
* **Full name of applicant**: Your full name as the applicant or rights holder.
* **Email address**: The email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone number**: Your phone number, including country code (optional).
* **Organization or company name**: If you are representing an organization or company, enter its name (optional).
* **Full name of deceased artist**: The full name of the deceased artist as known publicly.
* **Date of death**: The date of the artist's passing.
* **Primary profession/field of artist**: The artist's primary profession or field of work.
* **Name of estate representative or rights holder**: The name of the person or entity currently holding the rights to the artist's work.
* **Intended use**: Select all applicable reasons for using the AI-generated likeness:
	+ Digital art exhibition
	+ Documentary film
	+ Educational content
	+ Commercial advertisement
	+ Video game character
	+ Merchandise design
	+ Social media content
	+ Virtual reality experience
* **Distribution channels**: Select all applicable channels where the content will be published:
	+ Streaming platform
	+ Television broadcast
	+ Online video platforms
	+ Print publication
	+ Art gallery exhibition
	+ Mobile application
	+ Social media
	+ Physical merchandise
* **Commercial purpose**: Indicate if this is for commercial purposes (yes or no).
* **Estimated audience size**: The approximate number of viewers/users for the project.
* **Project timeline**: The deadline for the project, if applicable.
* **Compensation offered**: The amount of compensation offered to the estate, if applicable.
* **Project description**: A detailed description of the project and its goals.
* **Description of AI methodology**: A description of how the AI-generated likeness will be created.
* **Similar artists**: Names of living artists with a similar style to the deceased artist (optional).
* **Applicant signature**: Your full name as the applicant or rights holder.
* **Additional comments**: Any other relevant information about your project or request.

## Tips
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
- Be aware of any potential conflicts of interest or competing projects.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
