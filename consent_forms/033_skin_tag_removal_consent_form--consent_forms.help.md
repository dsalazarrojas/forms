# Skin Tag Removal Consent Form - Help Guide
</figure>

<figure><h2>Purpose</h2>
The skin tag removal consent form is a document that requires a client's consent before undergoing a skin tag removal procedure. This form helps to ensure that the client understands the risks and benefits of the procedure and provides essential information for the staff performing the removal.
</h2>
</figure>

<figure><h2>How To Complete This Form</h2>
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
2.  Enter your client's name and contact information (phone number and email address).
3.  Select the type of skin tag removal procedure the client will undergo.
4.  Provide any relevant medical history or allergies.
5.  List any current medications or medical conditions.
6.  Provide an emergency contact person and their contact information.
7.  Sign and date the form to confirm that the client understands and consents to the procedure.
8.  Have a staff member sign and date the form to confirm they have obtained consent.
</h2>
</figure>

<figure><h2>Field-by-Field Explanation</h2>
*   **Client Name**: The client's full name as it appears on their identification.
*   **Tag Type**: The type of skin tag removal procedure the client will undergo (tags, moles, or other).
*   **Medical History**: Any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies the client has.
*   **Allergies**: List any medications or substances the client is allergic to.
*   **Medications**: List any medications the client is currently taking.
*   **Medical Conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions the client has.
*   **Emergency Contact**: The client's emergency contact person's name and contact information.
*   **Consent Given**: Confirm that the client has provided their consent for the procedure.
*   **Date Signed**: The date the client signed the form.
*   **Time Signed**: The time the client signed the form.
*   **Location Signed**: The location where the client signed the form.
*   **Signature**: A staff member's signature to confirm they have obtained consent.
*   **Staff Name**: The staff member's name who signed the form.
*   **Staff Title**: The staff member's title.
*   **Staff Location**: The location where the staff member signed the form.
*   **Staff Email**: The staff member's email address.
*   **Client Tag Removal Consent**: Confirm that the staff member has obtained the client's consent.
*   **Client Tag Removal Consent Date**: The date the client provided their consent.
*   **Client Tag Removal Consent Time**: The time the client provided their consent.
*   **Client Tag Removal Consent Location**: The location where the client provided their consent.
*   **Client Tag Removal Consent Reason**: The reason for the tag removal procedure, if applicable.
*   **Staff Tag Removal Consent**: Confirm that the staff member has obtained the client's consent.
</h2>
</figure>

<figure><h2>tips</h2>
*   Ensure that the client reads and understands the form before signing.
*   If a client is unable to read or understand the form, consider providing a translation or assistance.
*   Ensure that the staff member signing the form is aware of the client's rights and responsibilities.
*   Consider having a witness or notary present for the staff member's signature.
*   Keep the form in a secure location for future reference.
</h2>
</figure>
