# Pet Vaccination Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your pet's vaccination status, including their vaccination history, type, and additional comments. This information helps our veterinary team provide accurate and up-to-date care for your pet.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the pet owner (step 1) and their contact details (steps 2 and 3).
2. Select the vaccination status of your pet (step 2).
3. If your pet is vaccinated, enter the date of their most recent vaccination (step 3).
4. Choose the type of vaccination your pet has received (step 4).
5. If necessary, provide any additional comments about your pet's vaccination history (step 5).
6. Select the vaccination history of your pet (step 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner (1)**: Please enter the name of the pet owner.
* **Vaccination Status (2)**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Vaccinated: Select this option if your pet is up-to-date on their vaccinations.
	+ Not vaccinated: Select this option if your pet has not received any vaccinations.
	+ Unknown: Select this option if you are unsure about your pet's vaccination status.
* **Vaccination Date (3)**: If your pet is vaccinated, enter the date of their most recent vaccination.
* **Vaccination Type (4)**: Select all the types of vaccinations your pet has received:
	+ Rabies
	+ Parvovirus
	+ Distemper
	+ Other
	+ None
* **Additional Comments (5)**: If you have any additional information about your pet's vaccination history, enter it here.
* **Vaccination History (6)**: Select all the following options that apply:
	+ Up-to-date
	+ Out-of-date
	+ Never vaccinated

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet's vaccination status.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, select the "Unknown" option.
* You can add additional comments if you have any further information about your pet's vaccination history.
