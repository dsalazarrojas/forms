# Community Service Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Community Service Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to provide consent for their participation in a community service activity. The form helps ensure that participants are aware of and agree to the risks and rules associated with the activity, including any medical treatment that may be necessary during the service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal details.
2. Provide your medical information, including any health conditions or allergies.
3. Answer questions about your participation, including whether you have health insurance and if you are willing to participate in the activity.
4. Review and agree to the waiver and release section, which includes liability release and consent for medical treatment.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, `required`): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, `required`): Enter the state where you live.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, `required`): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, `note`, `optional`): Provide information about the service activity, including what you will be doing and where it will take place.
* **Service Activity** (`service_activity`, `text`, `required`): Describe the service activity you will be participating in.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you will be participating in the service activity.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, `text`, `required`): Enter the location where the service activity will take place.
* **Health Information** (`health_info`, `note`, `optional`): Provide information about any health conditions or allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, `optional`): List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, `optional`): List any current medications you are taking.
* **Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, `text`, `optional`): List any physical limitations you have that may affect your participation.
* **Do You Have Health Insurance** (`insurance`, `select_one`, `optional`): Check if you have health insurance.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, `optional`): If you have health insurance, enter the name of your insurance provider.
* **Waiver and Release** (`waiver_section`, `note`, `optional`): Review the waiver and release section, which includes liability release and consent for medical treatment.
* **I Understand There Are Risks** (`risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you understand the potential risks associated with the activity.
* **My Participation Is Voluntary** (`voluntary_participation`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you are participating willingly.
* **I Release the Organization from Liability** (`liability_release`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you agree to release the organization from liability.
* **I Consent to Emergency Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you consent to emergency medical treatment if needed.
* **Photo and Video Release** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you consent to photo and video release.
* **I Agree to Follow All Rules** (`rules_agreement`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you agree to follow all rules associated with the service.
* **I Certify This Information Is Accurate** (`accuracy_statement`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if you certify that the information provided is accurate.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, `required`): Sign your name as the participant.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`guardian_section`, `note`, `optional`): Review and sign if you are a parent or guardian.
* **Is Participant Under 18** (`is_minor`, `select_one`, `required`): Check if the participant is under 18.
* **Guardian First Name** (`guardian_first_name`, `text`, `optional`): If the participant is under 18, enter the guardian's first name.
* **Guardian Last Name** (`guardian_last_name`, `text`, `optional`): If the participant is under 18, enter the guardian's last name.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, `text`, `optional`): If the participant is under 18, sign as the guardian.
* **Guardian Date** (`guardian_date`, `text`, `optional`): If the participant is under 18, enter the date of the guardian's signature.
