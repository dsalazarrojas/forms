# Medical Technology Innovation Showcase Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects participant information and consent for events such as Medtech Showcases and Innovation Events. It is used to follow up with participants and understand their interests and experiences in medical technology.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the `Participant Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Participant Email` field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
4. Select the events where you have attended or plan to attend from the list in the `Participant Innovation Event Attendance` field. You can select multiple events.
5. Indicate your experience with medical technology by answering the question in the `Participant Medtech Experience` field.
6. Choose your interests in medical technology from the list in the `Participant Medtech Interests` field. You can select multiple interests.
7. Select your skills in medical technology from the list in the `Participant Medtech Skills` field. You can select multiple skills.
8. Indicate your familiarity with specific medical technology tools in the `Participant Medtech Tools` field. You can select multiple tools.
9. List any medical technology innovations you have developed, invested in, or have an interest in from the `Participant Medtech Innovations` field.
10. Indicate your interests in medical technology partnerships and collaborations from the list in the `Participant Medtech Partners` field. You can select multiple partners.
11. Choose any other resources you have in medical technology, such as education or community from the list in the `Participant Medtech Resources` field. You can select multiple resources.
12. Optionally, list any funding for medical technology projects you've received or participated in.
13. Optionally, list any Medtech awards you've received.
14. Optionally, list any publications related to medical technology.
15. Optionally, list any networking opportunities or contacts you've made in the medical technology field.
16. Optionally, list any conferences or events you've participated in or attended related to medical technology.
17. Optionally, list any participation in medical technology activities or projects.
18. Optionally, list any Medtech-related recommendations or advice you'd like to provide.
19. Optionally, include any additional comments.
20. Finally, sign and date the form below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer**: Please enter the name of the person or organization organizing the event.
* **Hospital**: Please enter the name of the hospital or institution associated with the event.
* **University**: Please enter the name of the university or institution associated with the event.
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as a participant in the event.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address so that the organizers can follow up with you.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Participant Medtech Experience**: Indicate your level of experience with medical technology, whether 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Participant Medtech Interests**: Choose your interests in medical technology, such as Medtech Development, Innovation, or Investment.
* **Participant Medtech Skills**: Select your skills in medical technology, such as Medtech Research, Development, or Design.
* **Participant Medtech Tools**: Choose the tools you are familiar with, such as 3D Printing, Medtech Prototyping, or Medtech Testing.
* **Participant Medtech Innovations**: List any medical technology innovations you have developed, invested in, or have an interest in.
* **Participant Medtech Partners**: Choose any partners or collaborators you've worked with in the medical technology field.
* **Participant Medtech Resources**: Choose any resources you have, such as Medtech Education, Community, or Research.
* **Participant Medtech Funding**: Optionally, list any funding for medical technology projects you've received or participated in.
* **Participant Medtech Awards**: Optionally, list any Medtech awards you've received.
* **Participant Medtech Publications**: Optionally, list any publications related to medical technology.
* **Participant Medtech Networking**: Optionally, list any networking opportunities or contacts you've made in the medical technology field.
* **Participant Medtech Conferences**: Optionally, list any conferences or events you've participated in or attended related to medical technology.
* **Participant Medtech Participation**: Optionally, list any participation in medical technology activities or projects.
* **Participant Medtech Recommendations**: Optionally, list any Medtech-related recommendations or advice you'd like to provide.
* **Participant Medtech Comments**: Optionally, include any additional comments.
* **Participant Medtech Signature**: Please sign and date the form below.
* **Participant Medtech Date**: Date you sign this form.
* **Participant Medtech Time**: Time you sign this form. 

Note: This form is not intended for general use, and the information collected will not be shared beyond the event organizers and attendees. Please ensure you understand the purpose of this form before completing it.
