# Tooth Whitening Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tooth Whitening Consent Form is a document that requires patients to provide their consent for a tooth whitening treatment. The form is used to ensure that patients understand the risks and benefits of the treatment and agree to undergo it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the patient information section (page 1) with your personal details.
* Select your current dental health status from the options provided (page 2).
* Answer questions about any previous dental work, history of dental issues, and gum problems you may have (pages 3-5).
* Choose whether you want to sign the waiver and consent (page 6).
* Provide your signature and date/time of consent (pages 7-9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name, contact number, and email address in this section.
* **Dental Health (Page 2)**: Select "Healthy" if you do not have any dental health issues, or "Unhealthy" if you have any dental health problems.
* **Have you had any previous dental work? (Page 3)**: Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ True: If you have had any dental work done previously.
	+ False: If you have not had any dental work done previously.
* **Do you have a history of dental issues? (Page 4)**: Select "True" if you have a history of dental issues.
* **Have you experienced any gum problems? (Page 5)**: Select all the options that apply to you:
	+ True: If you have experienced any gum problems.
	+ False: If you have not experienced any gum problems.
* **Waiver and Consent (Page 6)**: Select "Sign" if you agree to the waiver and consent.
* **Signature (Page 7)**: Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Date (Page 8)**: Enter the date you signed the waiver.
* **Time (Page 9)**: Enter the time you signed the waiver.

**Tips**

* Please answer these questions honestly, as they are necessary for the dental professional to understand your medical history.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask your dental professional for clarification.
* Remember to read the waiver and consent carefully before signing it.
