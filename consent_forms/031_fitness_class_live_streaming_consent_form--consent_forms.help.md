# Fitness Class Live Streaming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from individuals who wish to participate in live streaming of fitness classes. The form is intended to gather basic contact information and emergency contact information in case of an unexpected event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review and acknowledge the consent section.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address.
3. Confirm your email address.
4. Provide your emergency contact information, including the contact's name, phone number, and address.
5. Enter your date of birth and age.
6. Add any emergency notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fitness Class Consent** (`fitness_class_consent`, note, not required): Please acknowledge that you have read and understand the consent statement, and indicate your willingness to participate in live streaming of fitness classes.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, not required): This field is used to gather your basic contact information. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, not required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy and prevent spam.
* **Email Confirmed** (`email_confirmed`, select multiple, not required): Select 'True' if you have confirmed your email address. 
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, not required): Provide the name of the emergency contact person, such as a family member or friend.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth in the format 'MM/DD/YY'.
* **Age** (`age`, number, not required): Enter your age.
* **Address** (`emergency_address`, note, not required): This field is not being used in this form.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional emergency notes, such as medical information or allergies.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any changes to your contact information, please update this form accordingly.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please provide necessary emergency notes.
