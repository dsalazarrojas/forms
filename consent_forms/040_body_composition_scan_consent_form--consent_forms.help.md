# Body Composition Scan Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a consent form for clients to provide information and consent for a body composition scan. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete all the required fields**:
	* Enter your first name in the **Client First Name** field.
	* Enter your last name in the **Client Last Name** field.
	* Enter your contact email address in the **Client Email** field.
	* Enter your contact phone number in the **Client Phone** field.
	* Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format MM/DD/YY.
	* Select your gender from the dropdown menu in the **Gender** field.
	* Enter your height in feet and inches in the **Height** field.
	* Enter your current weight in pounds in the **Current Weight** field.
	* Select the type of scan you are having from the dropdown menu in the **Type of Scan** field.
	* Select the purpose of the scan from the dropdown menu in the **Purpose of Scan** field.
	* List any medical conditions you may have in the **Medical Conditions** field.
	* Answer if you have a pacemaker or metal implant in the **Pacemaker or Metal Implants** field.
	* Answer if you are pregnant or nursing in the **Pregnancy Status** field.
	* Answer if you have eaten in the last 4 hours in the **Recent Meal** field.
	* Answer if you have exercised in the last 12 hours in the **Recent Exercise** field.
	* Answer if you are properly hydrated in the **Hydration Status** field.
	* Answer if the scan procedure has been explained to you in the **Scan Procedure Explained** field.
	* Answer if you understand any risks associated with the scan in the **Risks Understood** field.
	* Choose how you would like to receive your results in the **How to Receive Results** field.
	* Answer if you would like a follow-up consultation after the scan in the **Follow-Up Consultation** field.
	* Answer if you are aware that this is a voluntary participation in the **Voluntary Participation** field.
	* Answer if you understand your right to withdraw from the scan at any time in the **Right to Withdraw** field.
	* Sign your name in the **Client Signature** field.
	* Enter the date of consent in the **Signature Date** field.
	* If applicable, enter the name of the witness in the **Witness Name** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Client Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.

* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Current Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Type of Scan**: Select the type of scan you are having.
* **Purpose of Scan**: Select the purpose of the scan from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you may have.
* **Pacemaker or Metal Implants**: Answer if you have a pacemaker or metal implant.
* **Pregnancy Status**: Answer if you are pregnant or nursing.
* **Recent Meal**: Answer if you have eaten in the last 4 hours.
* **Recent Exercise**: Answer if you have exercised in the last 12 hours.
* **Hydration Status**: Answer if you are properly hydrated.
* **Scan Procedure Explained**: Answer if the scan procedure has been explained to you.
* **Risks Understood**: Answer if you understand any risks associated with the scan.
* **How to Receive Results**: Choose how you would like to receive your results.
* **Follow-Up Consultation**: Answer if you would like a follow-up consultation after the scan.
* **Voluntary Participation**: Answer if you are aware that this is a voluntary participation.
* **Right to Withdraw**: Answer if you understand your right to withdraw from the scan at any time.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name as a signature of consent.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of consent.
* **Witness Name**: Enter the name of the witness if applicable.
* **Tips**: Please make sure to answer all required fields truthfully and accurately.
