# Drug Test Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and consent from individuals for drug testing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact information, including name, contact number, and date of birth.
2. Choose the date of the test and the type of test that will be performed.
3. Indicate your consent status by selecting one of the three options provided.
4. Fill in your first and last name.
5. Specify your employee status and department.
6. Obtain signatures from your supervisor and yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This page provides a summary of the form's purpose and instructions on how to complete it.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Please enter your contact information, including your name, contact number, and date of birth.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of Test** (`date_of_test`): Enter the date of the test that will be administered.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`): Choose the type of test that will be performed (Urine Test, Hair Test, Blood Test, or Hair Follicle Test).
* **Other** (`test_type_other`): If the test type is not listed, you can specify it in this field.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Indicate your consent status by selecting one of the three options provided (True, False, or Maybe).
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`): Specify your current employee status.
* **Department** (`department`): Specify your department.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`): Obtain a signature from your supervisor to confirm your consent status.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`): Obtain a signature from yourself to confirm your consent status.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of signing the form.
