# Tubal Ligation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a patient's consent and gather necessary information before undergoing a tubal ligation procedure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Describe any medical history you have, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.
4. Select one of the options for consent, indicating whether you consent for the procedure or refuse it.
5. If you have a medical condition, please describe it in the space provided.
6. If a doctor's name is required, please enter their name as it appears on their identification documents.
7. Enter the address of the clinic where the procedure will be performed.
8. Select the date for the procedure from the calendar provided.
9. If additional notes or comments are necessary, please provide them below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Name (Patient Name)
* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, Text, Required): This field asks for your name as it appears on your identification documents. Please ensure the name is accurate to ensure your records are up to date.

### Birth Date (Birth Date)
* **Birth Date** (Birth Date, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Medical History (Medical History)
* **Medical History** (Medical History, Note, Required): Describe any medical history you have, including allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.

### Consent (Consent)
* **Consent** (Select One, Required): Select one of the options to indicate whether you consent to the procedure or refuse it.

### Medical Condition (Medical Condition)
* **Medical Condition** (Text, Optional): If you have a medical condition, please describe it in this space.

### Doctor Name (Doctor Name)
* **Doctor Name** (Text, Optional): If a doctor's name is required, please enter their name as it appears on their identification documents.

### Clinic Address (Clinic Address)
* **Clinic Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the clinic where the procedure will be performed.

### Procedure Date (Procedure Date)
* **Procedure Date** (Date, Required): Select the date from the calendar for the procedure.

### Notes (Notes)
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): If additional notes or comments are necessary, please provide them below.
