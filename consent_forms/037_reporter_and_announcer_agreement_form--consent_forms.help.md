# Reporter And Announcer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from reporters and announcers for event coverage. It captures details about the reporter's and announcer's details, event details, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name and title in the "Reporter Details" field.
2. Enter the "Name Of Reporter", "Name Of Announcer", and "Event Organizer" fields with the required information.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Consent For Event Coverage".
4. Enter your "Contact Number" and optional "Email Address" if applicable.
5. In the "Event Details" section, enter the "Event Title", "Event Start Date", "Event End Date", "Event Time", and "Event Venue" details.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Agreement Terms" and "Agreement Status".
7. Enter the "Agreement Date" and "Agreement Time".
8. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" for "Submit".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter And Announcer Agreement Form** (`text`, required, always displayed): This is the form title.
* **Reporter Details** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter your name and title in this field.
* **Name Of Reporter** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter your name as the reporter.
* **Name Of Announcer** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter the name of the announcer.
* **Assignment Details** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter the assignment details.
* **Consent For Event Coverage** (`select_multiple`, required, always displayed): Select "Yes" to consent to event coverage.
* **Contact Number** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required, always displayed): Enter your email address.
* **Agreement** (`note`, required, always displayed): Enter the agreement terms.
* **Event Details** (`text`, not always displayed): Enter the event details.
* **Event Title** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter the event title.
* **Event Start Date** (`date`, required, always displayed): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`date`, required, always displayed): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`time`, required, always displayed): Enter the event time.
* **Event Venue** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter the event venue.
* **Contact Person** (`text`, not always displayed): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, not always displayed): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, not always displayed): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Event Organizer** (`text`, required, always displayed): Enter the event organizer's name.
* **Agreement Terms** (`note`, required, always displayed): Enter the agreement terms.
* **Agreement Date** (`date`, required, always displayed): Enter the agreement date.
* **Agreement Time** (`time`, required, always displayed): Enter the agreement time.
* **Agreement Version** (`text`, not always displayed): Enter the agreement version.
* **Agreement Status** (`select_one`, required, always displayed): Select "Yes" for agreement status.
* **Submit** (`select_one`, required, always displayed): Select "Yes" to submit.
