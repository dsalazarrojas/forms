# Hair Salon Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be completed by clients visiting a hair salon for services. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the client understands the services being provided and the terms of use. The information collected will be used to create a client contract and to provide the best possible experience for the client.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure that the client's needs and expectations are met.
*   Please fill in your details as prompted, including your client name, address, phone number, and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Service Provided* (`service_provided`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe the services you want to receive at the salon. This will help the stylist understand your requirements and preferences.
*   *Client Name* (`client_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   *Phone* (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
*   *Email* (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you have one. This will help us contact you with any updates or changes to your appointment.
*   *Client Address* (`client_address`, Text, Optional): Enter your address for our records. This will help us locate you in case of emergencies.
*   *Client Signature* (`client_signature`, Note, Optional): Please sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms of use. This is a legal requirement.
*   *Salon Services* (`salon_services`, Text, Optional): List all the services you want to receive at the salon, including any specific details or requests you may have.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   If you have any special requests or requirements, make sure to list them in the "Salon Services" field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your stylist for assistance.
*   This form is a legal document, and it's essential to understand the terms of use before proceeding with any services.
