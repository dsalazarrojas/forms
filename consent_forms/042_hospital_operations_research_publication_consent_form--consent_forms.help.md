# Hospital Operations Research Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Operations Research Publication Consent Form is used to capture informed consent from research participants regarding the use and handling of their sensitive data. This form is designed to provide clear instructions and guidelines for research participants to understand how their data will be used, stored, and protected. It includes fields for participant contact information, consent status, data use and retention periods, and researcher affiliations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter Patient Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
2. **Publication Title**: Enter the title of the research publication that this form is associated with.
3. **Researcher Name**: Enter your name and title, as it appears on official business cards or email communications.
4. **Researcher Institution**: Enter your institutional affiliation or affiliation of your research team.
5. **Research Publication Date**: Enter the date when the research publication was published, or when the study was conducted.
6. **Participant Data Use**: Select one of the options below to indicate how you want your data to be used:
	* Data will not be shared with any 3rd party
	* Data will not be used for commercial purposes
	* Data will not be used in any other research studies
	* Data will be shared with 1st party partners
7. **Participant Data Storage**: Select all applicable options below to indicate where you want your data to be stored:
	* Data will not be stored
	* Data will be stored in participant database
	* Data will be stored on a secure server
8. **Participant Data Anonymization**: Select one of the options below to indicate how you want your data to be anonymized:
	* Data will not be anonymized
	* Data will be partially anonymized
	* Data will be fully anonymized
9. **Participant Data Destruction**: Select one of the options below to indicate when you want your data to be destroyed:
	* Data will not be destroyed
	* Data will be destroyed after 1 year
	* Data will be destroyed after 2 years
10. **Participant Data Retention**: Select all applicable options below to indicate how long you want your data to be retained:
	* Data will be retained for 1 year
	* Data will be retained for 2 years
	* Data will be retained for 5 years
11. **Participant Data Sharing**: Select all applicable options below to indicate with whom you want your data to be shared:
	* Data will not be shared
	* Data will be shared with 1st party partners
	* Data will be shared with 2nd party partners
	* Data will be shared with 3rd party partners
12. **Consent Given**: Check the box to indicate that you have given consent for your data to be used and shared.
13. **Participant Notification**: Select all applicable options below to indicate how you want to be notified about data handling:
	* No notification
	* Email notification
	* Phone call notification
14. **Participant Data Use Agreement**: If you have any specific agreements or conditions for data use, please describe them in this field.
15. **Participant Signature**: Please sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the consent form.
16. **Research Institution**: Enter the name of the research institution or organization affiliated with this study.
17. **Research Study**: Enter a brief description or title of the research study.
18. **Participant Data Destruction Method**: Select one of the options below to indicate how you want your data to be destroyed:
	* Data will not be destroyed
	* Data will be shredded
	* Data will be erased
19. **Participant Data Retention Period**: Select all applicable options below to indicate how long you want your data to be retained:
	* Data will be retained for 1 year
	* Data will be retained for 2 years
	* Data will be retained for 5 years
20. **Participant Signature Date**: Please enter the date when you signed this consent form.
21. **Researcher Name 2**: Enter the name and title of the researcher or team member associated with this study.
22. **Research Institution 2**: Enter the name of the research institution or organization affiliated with this study.

## Tips

* Please make sure to carefully read and understand each field before filling it out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your researcher or study coordinator.
* This form is designed to protect your data and ensure its secure handling. Your consent is required for its use.
