# Data Literacy Workshop Broadcast Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent from participants to broadcast their sessions online. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected from participants before broadcasting their sessions, which can include video recordings of themselves, their voices, or images.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your **Participant Name**, which should match the name you'll be using to be recognized on the broadcast or video.
2.  Enter your **Email**, which will be used to notify you about the broadcast and any related updates.
3.  Enter your **Phone** number, which can be used for further communication or reminders about the broadcast.
4.  Select your **Participant Role** (e.g., speaker, moderator, or attendee).
5.  Choose the type of **Session Type** (e.g., workshop, seminar, or presentation).
6.  Enter the **Session Date** of your broadcast, in standard date format.
7.  Provide the **Session Location** where the broadcast will take place.
8.  Check the box indicating your **Participant Consent** for the session, which is necessary for its broadcasting.
9.  Check the box indicating your **Recording Consent**, which is required for recording your voice or image during the session.
10. Check the box indicating your **Broadcasting Consent**, which is necessary for broadcasting your session online.
11. Add any additional **Session Notes** regarding your session, which can be useful for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you on the broadcast.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any additional communication.
*   **Participant Role**: Select your role in the session, such as speaker or moderator.
*   **Session Type**: Choose the type of session, e.g., workshop or seminar.
*   **Session Date**: Enter the date of your session in standard date format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Session Location**: Provide the location where your session will take place.
*   **Participant Consent**: Check this box if you give your consent for the session to be broadcast.
*   **Recording Consent**: Check this box if you agree to be recorded during the session.
*   **Broadcasting Consent**: Check this box if you consent to having your session broadcast online.
*   **Session Notes**: Add any additional notes about your session here.
