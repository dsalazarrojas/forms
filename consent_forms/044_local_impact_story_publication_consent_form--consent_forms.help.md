# Local Impact Story Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Local Impact Story Publication Consent Form is designed to collect consent from individuals whose stories or data may be published. This form helps ensure that we respect the rights and privacy of the individuals and maintain transparency in our publication process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Review each field carefully, as they are essential to the publication process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents, such as your driver's license or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Consent Given
	+ Consent Not Given
	+ Consent Pending
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **Date of Publication** (`date_of_publication`, date, optional): Enter the date you're requesting publication.
* **Time of Publication** (`time_of_publication`, time, optional): Enter the time you're requesting publication.
* **Local Impact Story Publication Consent Given** (`local_impact_story_publication_consent_given`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Consent Given
	+ Consent Not Given
	+ Consent Pending
* **Date of Consent** (`date_of_consent`, date, optional): Enter the date you provided consent.
* **Time of Consent** (`time_of_consent`, time, optional): Enter the time you provided consent.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the forms that you're consenting to:
	+ Form 1
	+ Form 2
	+ Form 3
* **Other Consent** (`other_consent`, text, optional): Enter any other consent related to the publication.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your consent.
* **Other Form** (`other_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more forms related to your consent:
	+ Form 1
	+ Form 2
	+ Form 3
* **Comments Given** (`comments_given`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to your consent.
* **Comments Received** (`comments_received`, text, optional): Enter any comments received from others about your consent.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ Pending
	+ Approved
	+ Denied
* **Status Date** (`status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the consent status change.
* **Status Time** (`status_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the consent status change.
* **Status Comments** (`status_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the consent status change.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature (name, date, and title).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you'd like to be published.
