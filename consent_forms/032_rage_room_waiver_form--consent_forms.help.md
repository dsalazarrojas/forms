# Rage Room Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rage Room Waiver Form" is a document that protects both the rage room facility and its participants by ensuring they understand the physical and mental demands of the experience and agree to participate with the rules and risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the participant's information and waiver sections.
2. Fill in the participant's information section with the required details.
3. Answer the waiver section questions honestly and carefully.
4. Sign the form to indicate your agreement and understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): Enter the participant's name, date of birth, and other relevant personal details.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): If the participant is under 18 years old, please enter their age. If they are 18 or older, this field is not applicable.
* **Participant Height** (`participant_height`, number, optional): Enter the participant's height in centimeters.
* **Participant Weight** (`participant_weight`, number, optional): Enter the participant's weight in kilograms.
* **Diet** (`participant_diet`, text, optional): Enter the participant's diet, e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.
* **Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the participant has, e.g., diabetes, asthma, etc.
* **Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, text, optional): Enter any allergies the participant has, e.g., nuts, gluten, etc.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the participant's emergency contact information, e.g., name, phone number, etc.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_information`, text, optional): If the participant is under 18 years old, enter their guardian's name and contact information.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to indicate your agreement and understanding of the terms and conditions.

### Waiver Section
* **Facility Laws** (`facility_laws`, text, optional): Read and understand the facility laws, including any rules and regulations.
* **Facility Laws Agreed** (`facility_laws_agreed`, select_multiple, optional): Select "I have read and understand the terms and conditions" and/or "I am aware that I am participating at my own risk" and/or "I agree to the terms and conditions".
* **Waiver Signed** (`waiver_signed`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate you have signed the form.
* **Facility Signature** (`facility_signature`, text, optional): Sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, text, optional): If the participant is under 18 years old, sign the form to confirm your agreement and understanding of the terms and conditions.

### Important Tips
* Always review the form carefully before signing it.
* Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required for the participant's information section.
* If the participant is under 18 years old, ensure you have their guardian's signature.
* Sign the form honestly and carefully, acknowledging the risks and rules of the rage room experience.
