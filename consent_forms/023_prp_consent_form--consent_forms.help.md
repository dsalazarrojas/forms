# prp_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **prp_consent_form** is designed to collect patient consent and medical information for treatment purposes. This form is used to ensure that patients have provided informed consent for treatment and have accurate medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **consent_form** field with your name, which is a text input field.
2. Enter your **patient_info** in the text field provided.
3. Provide details about the **treatment_info** in the text field.
4. Enter the **consent_date** and **consent_time** in the respective date and time format fields.
5. Select the **treatment_provider** from the dropdown list.
6. Choose the **treatment_location** from the dropdown list.
7. If necessary, enter any **medical_history** in the text field.
8. List any **allergies** you have in the select multiple field.
9. Enter any **medical_notes** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **consent_form** (prp_consent_form)
	* A text input field to enter your name.
### 2. **patient_info** (patient_info)
	* A text field to enter your patient information.
### 3. **treatment_info** (treatment_info)
	* A text field to enter details about your treatment.
### 4. **consent_date** (consent_date)
	* A date field to enter the date you provided consent.
### 5. **consent_time** (consent_time)
	* A time field to enter the time you provided consent.
### 6. **treatment_provider** (treatment_provider)
	* A dropdown list of possible treatment providers.
### 7. **treatment_location** (treatment_location)
	* A dropdown list of possible treatment locations.
### 8. **medical_history** (medical_history)
	* A text field for entering medical history information.
### 9. **allergies** (allergies)
	* A select multiple field to list any allergies you have.
### 10. **medical_notes** (medical_notes)
	* A text field for entering any additional medical notes.

**Tips:**

* Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form creator at form@gic.mx.
