# Athlete Bio Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about an athlete's background, achievements, and consent for the publication of their bio on various platforms. The collected data will be used for promotional materials and other official purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Athlete Information**: Provide your full name, team or club affiliation, and any relevant academic achievements.
2. **Share Your Story**: Write a brief personal introduction and list your most significant athletic and academic accomplishments.
3. **Consent for Publication**: Choose whether you consent to publication on the official website, print media, social media channels, and sharing with approved media partners.
4. **Withdrawal Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you understand you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.
5. **Sign and Date**: Sign your name and the date to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`Athlete Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Team or Club Affiliation** (`Team or Club Affiliation`, text, required): List your team or club name and affiliation (e.g., University, School, or Team Name).
* **Personal Introduction** (`Personal Introduction`, text, required): Write a short paragraph about your background and journey as an athlete.
* **Athletic Career Highlights** (`Athletic Career Highlights`, text, required): List your most significant athletic achievements and milestones.
* **Academic Achievements** (`Academic Achievements`, text, optional): Include any academic awards, GPA, or notable school involvement.
* **Hobbies and Interests** (`Hobbies and Interests`, text, optional): Share your interests and activities outside of sports.
* **Role Models and Inspiration** (`Role Models and Inspiration`, text, optional): Mention who inspires you and why.
* **Website Consent** (`I consent to publication on the official organization website`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow your bio to be published on the official website.
* **Print Consent** (`I consent to publication in print media - programs, brochures`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow your bio to be published in print media.
* **Social Media Consent** (`I consent to publication on social media channels`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I consent" to allow your bio to be published on social media channels.
* **Media Partner Consent** (`I consent to sharing my bio with approved media partners`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I consent" to allow your bio to be shared with approved media partners.
* **Withdrawal Acknowledgement** (`I acknowledge my right to withdraw this consent at any time`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand your right to withdraw your consent at any time.
* **Electronic Signature of Athlete** (`Electronic Signature of Athlete`, text, required): Type your full name to sign the form.
* **Electronic Signature of Parent or Guardian** (`Electronic Signature of Parent or Guardian`, text, optional): If you are under 18 years old, enter the signature of your parent or guardian.
* **Date of Consent** (`Date of Consent`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.
