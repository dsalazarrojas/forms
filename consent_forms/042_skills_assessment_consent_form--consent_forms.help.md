# Skills Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skills Assessment Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to facilitate a smooth and effective skills assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your participant information.
2. Describe the evaluation details.
3. Indicate your consent for the skill assessment.
4. Specify the evaluation date.
5. Provide the evaluation time.
6. Mention the evaluation location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Participant Information, text, not required)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details to help the assessors know who you are and what you can bring to the assessment.
* **Evaluation Details (Evaluation Details, text, not required)**: Explain the specific skills being assessed and the type of evaluation you will be undergoing.
* **Consent (Consent, select_one, not required)**: Indicate your consent for the skill assessment by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'. Please choose 'Yes' if you agree to be assessed.
* **Evaluation Date (Evaluation Date, date, not required)**: Enter the date when the assessment is scheduled to take place.
* **Evaluation Time (Evaluation Time, time, not required)**: Enter the time when the assessment is scheduled to take place.
* **Evaluation Location (Evaluation Location, text, not required)**: Specify the location where the assessment will occur.
