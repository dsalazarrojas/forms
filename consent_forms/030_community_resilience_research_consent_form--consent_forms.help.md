# Community Resilience Research Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather informed consent from participants in community resilience and disaster preparedness studies. It ensures that participants understand the purpose and implications of their participation, and that they are aware of their rights and responsibilities as a study participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the introductory page carefully to understand the purpose and goals of the study.
2. Fill out your name, date of birth, contact email address, and phone number on the respective fields.
3. Confirm that you understand the purpose of the study and that your participation is voluntary.
4. Choose to grant permission for researchers to use your survey responses, and to record your interview (if applicable).
5. Confirm that you understand you can withdraw from the study at any time.
6. Choose to receive a copy of the final study results (if you wish).
7. Finally, indicate your willingness to participate in future follow-up studies.
8. Sign your digital signature, and select today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`study_participant_full_name`): This is your name as it would appear on any official documents.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`): Please enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Contact Email Address** (`study_participant_email`): This is your email address where you can be reached for study updates or further communication.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`study_participant_phone`): This is your phone number where you can be reached for study updates or further communication.
* **Do you understand the purpose of this study?** (`study_purpose_confirmation`): Please confirm that you have read and understood the study's purpose.
* **I understand my participation is voluntary** (`voluntary_participation_agreement`): You have the right to withdraw from the study at any time.
* **I understand my information will be kept confidential** (`confidentiality_and_privacy_agreement`): Your personal data will be protected and kept confidential.
* **I grant permission for researchers to use my survey responses** (`research_data_use_permission`): You are giving permission for researchers to use your survey responses for study purposes.
* **I consent to being recorded for an interview** (`interview_recording_consent`): If participating in an interview, you are giving permission for recording.
* **I understand I can withdraw from the study at any time** (`withdraw_participation_right`): You have the right to withdraw from the study at any time.
* **Would you like to receive a copy of the final study results?** (`study_results_interest`): This is optional, but you can choose to receive the final study results.
* **Would you be willing to participate in future follow-up studies?** (`follow_up_study_interest`): This is optional, but you can choose to participate in future studies.
* **Participant Digital Signature** (`participant_digital_signature`): Please type your full name to sign this form.
* **Today's Date** (`today_consent_date`): Please select today's date.
* **Thank You for Your Participation** (`research_footer`): A final thank you message.

## Tips

* Make sure to read and understand each field carefully before filling it out.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the study researcher.
* Your participation is voluntary, and you can withdraw at any time.
