# ZOOM Liabiliy Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and emergency contact information for participants in the Zoom Liabiliy program. Please complete the form accurately to ensure the smooth execution of the program and to allow us to provide the necessary support in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your personal and emergency contact information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of fields. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format YYYY/MM/DD (e.g., 1995/01/01).
* **Residential address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your residential address.
* **Medical conditions** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them briefly.
* **Emergency contact first name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their first name.
* **Emergency contact last name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their last name.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend, colleague).
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please enter their phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
