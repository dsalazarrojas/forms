# Nonprofit Technology Training Recording Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide their consent to be recorded during training sessions. It captures essential information about the participant's details, consent preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Info in the text field provided.
2. Select your Recording Consent preference from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide your Contact Details, including your name and any other relevant information.
4. If applicable, enter your Contact Phone number.
5. Enter your Organization (if relevant).
6. Specify your Role (if relevant).
7. Enter your Email address (if applicable and relevant).
8. Finally, sign your name in the designated Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: This text field is where you will provide your basic participant details. Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Recording Consent**: This dropdown menu allows you to choose how you would like to be recorded during the training session. Please select either Option 1 or Option 2.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your name, to ensure that we can reach out to you with any further instructions or questions.
* **Contact Phone**: This field is optional and allows you to provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization**: This field is optional and should be filled in if you represent an organization.
* **Role**: This field is optional and should specify your role within the organization.
* **Email**: This field is optional and allows you to provide an email address for contact purposes.
* **Signature**: Sign this field to confirm your consent.

## Tips
• Ensure that you have filled out all required fields to complete the form.
• Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
• If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
