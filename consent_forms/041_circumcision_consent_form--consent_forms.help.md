# Circumcision Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to be filled out by patients who are undergoing a circumcision procedure. It gathers essential information about the patient, including contact details, medical history, and consent. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the procedure.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through the form carefully and make sure you understand what is required.
2.  Fill out the form with your contact details, medical history, and other relevant information.
3.  Select the correct answers to the questions and options presented.
4.  Sign the form to confirm your consent to the procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Full Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, `optional`): If you are a minor, enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
*   **Child's Full Name** (`child_name`, `text`, `optional`): If you are a child, enter your full name.
*   **Child's Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of birth of the child.
*   **Patient's Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of birth of the patient.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Home Address** (`address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your street, city, state, and zip code for contact purposes.
*   **Type of Procedure** (`procedure_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of circumcision procedure you are undergoing (e.g., Infant, Child, Adult, Revision).
*   **Reason for Procedure** (`medical_reason`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the reasons that apply for undergoing the procedure (e.g., Religious/cultural, Medical necessity, Recurrent infections, etc.).
*   **Relevant Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, `required`): Enter any relevant medical history, such as bleeding disorders or allergies.
*   **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, `required`): Enter any current medications or supplements you are taking.
*   **Known Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, `required`): Enter any known allergies you have.
*   **Anesthesia Preference** (`anesthesia_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your preference for anesthesia (e.g., Local, Regional, General, No preference).
*   **Understanding of Risks** (`procedure_risks`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you understand and accept the risks involved with the procedure.
*   **Risks Acknowledged** (`risks_acknowledged`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all the risks you understand and acknowledge (e.g., Bleeding, Infection, Pain, etc.).
*   **Benefits Understood** (`benefits_understood`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you understand the benefits of the procedure.
*   **Alternatives Discussed** (`alternatives_discussed`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you have discussed alternative options with your doctor.
*   **Understanding of Post-Procedure Care** (`post_care_understanding`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you understand the post-procedure care requirements.
*   **Follow-Up Appointment Scheduled** (`follow_up_appointment`, `select_one`, `required`): Select if a follow-up appointment has been scheduled.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_info`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your insurance provider's name and number.
*   **Consent to Procedure** (`consent_grant`, `select_one`, `required`): Select that you grant consent for the procedure.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, `required`): Type your full name as a signature.
*   **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date you sign the form.
*   **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the full name of the person witnessing your signature.
*   **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the witness's signature.

The form is designed to ensure the patient understands the implications of the procedure and provides a clear and concise way to gather essential information for the procedure.
