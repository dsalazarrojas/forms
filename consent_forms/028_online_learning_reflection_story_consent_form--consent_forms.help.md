# Online Learning Reflection Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain the consent of parents or guardians for a child's participation in online learning activities, such as accessing the platform, using digital tools, and sharing personal data.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form with the child's details:
  - **Student Information**: Enter a brief description of the child's online learning activity.
  - **Student Name**: Enter the child's name.
  - **Email**: Enter the child's email address (if applicable).
  - **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's or guardian's name.
  - **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent's or guardian's phone number (if applicable).
  - **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the parent's or guardian's signature.
  - **Signature**: Select one of the provided options to indicate the type of signature made.
  - **Permission**: Confirm that the parent or guardian grants permission for the child to participate in online learning activities.
  - **Other Permission**: If the child requires any additional permissions, select "Yes".
  - **Comments**: If necessary, add any additional comments or information.
  - **Form Completion**: Enter the date and time the form is completed.
  - **Student Signature**: The student will need to sign the form, if required.
  - **Parent Signature**: The parent or guardian will need to sign the form, if required.
  - **Student Signature Date**: Enter the date the student signed the form.
  - **Student Signature Time**: Enter the time the student signed the form.
  - **Student Signature Notary**: If a notary is required, confirm if the signature is notarized.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information**: A brief description of the child's online learning activity.
* **Student Name**: The name of the child participating in the online activity.
* **Email**: The child's email address, if applicable.
* **Parent Name**: The name of the parent or guardian giving consent.
* **Parent Phone**: The parent's or guardian's phone number, if applicable.
* **Signature Date**: The date the parent or guardian signed the form.
* **Signature**: The type of signature made (e.g., electronic, handwritten).
* **Permission**: Confirm that the parent or guardian grants permission for the child to participate in online learning activities.
* **Other Permission**: If additional permissions are required, select "Yes".
* **Comments**: Additional comments or information about the child's participation.
* **Form Completion**: The date and time the form is completed.
* **Student Signature**: The student's signature, if required.
* **Parent Signature**: The parent or guardian's signature, if required.
* **Student Signature Date**: The date the student signed the form.
* **Student Signature Time**: The time the student signed the form.
* **Student Signature Notary**: If a notary is required, confirm if the signature is notarized.
* **Parent Signature Notary**: If a notary is required, confirm if the parent's or guardian's signature is notarized.

## Tips

- Make sure to complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure that the parent or guardian's signature is notarized if required.
- Store the completed form securely and confidentially.
- Review and revise the form as necessary.
- Consult with a notary if unsure about notarization requirements.
