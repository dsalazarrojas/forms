# midwifery_client_intake_medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect medical history information from clients, which can help midwifery practitioners provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have filled out this form only for the midwifery client in question, and any subsequent updates or revisions should be made on a separate form.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): This is a text field for you to enter any relevant information about the client, such as their name, contact details, or medical background.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This is another text field for you to record any medical history or health conditions that the client may have.
* **Birth Preferences** (`birth_preferences`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **option 1**: Choose one of the available options.
	+ **option 2**: Choose another option.
	+ **option 3**: Choose the third available option.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history_2`, text, optional): Another text field for recording additional medical history.
* **Medical History 3** (`medical_history_3`, number, optional): Enter any numerical medical history information.
* **Medical History 4** (`medical_history_4`, text, optional): Another text field for recording further medical history.
* **Medical History 5** (`medical_history_5`, select_one, optional):
	+ **option 1**: Choose the available option.
	+ **option 2**: Choose the second available option.
* **Medical History 6** (`medical_history_6`, date, optional): Enter the date of any relevant medical history events.
* **Medical History 7** (`medical_history_7`, time, optional): Enter the time of day for any relevant medical history events.
* **Medical History 8** (`medical_history_8`, text, optional): Another text field for recording further medical history.
* **Medical History 9** (`medical_history_9`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ **option 1**: Choose one of the available options.

* **Medical History 10** (`medical_history_10`, select_multiple, optional):
