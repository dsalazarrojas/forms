# Diver Activities Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diver Activities Consent Form is used to collect information about the activities in which the diver wishes to participate and to gather their consent for those activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Activities" section, where you can select the activities you wish to participate in. This is a multiple-choice section, where you can choose from the options provided.
2. Next, select the date on which you wish to participate in the activities. You can use the calendar to pick a date.
3. Then, choose the time of day you wish to participate in the activities. Please note that this field is not necessarily related to the date field, but rather to the timing of your participation.
4. In the "Activities" section again, confirm your selection by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each activity.
5. Finally, provide the location of the activity in the "Location" field. This is an optional field, but please provide accurate information if you wish to participate in activities that take place at a specific location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activities** (`activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This section allows you to select multiple activities in which you wish to participate. Please choose each activity carefully, as this will affect the activities in which you will be allowed to participate.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): 
    This field is used to select the date on which you wish to participate in the activities. Please use the calendar to pick a date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): 
    This field is used to choose the time of day you wish to participate in the activities. Please note that this field is not necessarily related to the date field, but rather to the timing of your participation.
* **Activities** (`activities`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
    This section is a duplicate of the "Activities" section on page 1, where you confirm your selection by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each activity.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): 
    This field is used to provide the location of the activity. Please provide accurate information if you wish to participate in activities that take place at a specific location.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your activities are properly recorded and your consent is obtained. If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
