# Release Of Information Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for collecting information from non-profit organizations for release of information purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about the non-profit organization.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Release Of Information** (`release_of_information_template`, text, required): This field is used to collect the release of information from the non-profit organization.
* **Release Of Info Date** (`release_of_info_date`, date, required): Enter the release of information date.
* **Release Of Info Location** (`release_of_info_location`, text, required): Enter the release of information location.
* **Release Of Info Phone** (`release_of_info_phone`, text, required): Enter the release of information phone number.
* **Release Of Info Email** (`release_of_info_email`, email, required): Enter the non-profit organization's email address.
* **Non Profit Number** (`non_profit_number`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's number.
* **Non Profit Name** (`non_profit_name`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's name.
* **Non Profit Address** (`non_profit_address`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's address.
* **Non Profit City** (`non_profit_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit State** (`non_profit_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Zip** (`non_profit_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Phone** (`non_profit_phone`, text, required): Enter the non-profit organization's phone number.
* **Non Profit Email** (`non_profit_email`, email, required): Enter the non-profit organization's email address.
* **Another Release Of Info Date** (`release_of_info_date2`, date, required): Enter the release of information date.
* **Non Profit 501c3** (`non_profit_501c3`, select_one, required): Select whether the non-profit organization is a 501c3.
* **Non Profit NDA** (`non_profit_nda`, select_one, required): Select whether the non-profit organization has a non-disclosure agreement.
* **Non Profit Certifications** (`non_profit_certifications`, text, required): Enter any relevant certifications for the non-profit organization.
* **Non Profit Terms** (`non_profit_terms`, text, required): Enter any terms and conditions related to the non-profit organization.
* **Another Release Of Info Date** (`release_of_info_date3`, date, required): Enter the release of information date.
* **Non Profit NDA Text** (`non_profit_nda_text`, text, optional): Enter any text related to the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Non Profit Fax** (`non_profit_fax`, text, optional): Enter the non-profit organization's fax number.
* **Non Profit Website** (`non_profit_website`, text, optional): Enter the non-profit organization's website.
* **Non Profit EIN** (`non_profit_ein`, text, optional): Enter the EIN number of the non-profit organization.
