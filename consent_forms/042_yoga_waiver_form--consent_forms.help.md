# Yoga Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mandatory document that you must complete before participating in yoga activities or classes. It's a legal requirement to provide your consent and contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in the "Medical Condition" field.
7. If you have any allergies, please list them in the "Allergies" field.
8. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
9. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
10. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
11. Enter your emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
12. Enter your emergency contact's city in the "Emergency Contact City" field.
13. Enter your emergency contact's state in the "Emergency Contact State" field.
14. Enter your emergency contact's zip code in the "Emergency Contact Zip" field.
15. Choose the type of relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship Type" field.
15. Sign the "Signature" field to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address here. This will be used for communication and emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number here. This will be used for communication and emergency contact.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required/optional): If you have any allergies, please list them here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number here.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's address here.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's city here.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency_contact_state`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's state here.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`emergency_contact_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's zip code here.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (`emergency_contact_relationship_type`, text, required/optional): Choose the type of relationship with your emergency contact here.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Sign here to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can contact you or your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your instructor or staff member.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you understand the terms and conditions.
