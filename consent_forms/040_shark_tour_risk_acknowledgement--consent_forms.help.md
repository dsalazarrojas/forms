# Shark Tour Risk Acknowledgement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shark Tour Risk Acknowledgement form is a document that aims to collect important information from participants and tour operators regarding the risks associated with shark tour activities. It is a critical tool used to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all tour participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's name in the provided text field.
2. Enter the name of the tour operator responsible for the tour.
3. Fill in the risk acknowledgement text field, if you acknowledge any risks associated with the tour.
4. Provide your signature to confirm you have read and understood the risks involved in the tour.
5. Enter the date when the risk acknowledgement was made.
6. If an incident occurs during the tour, report it in the text field.
7. Provide your contact details to facilitate communication in case of an emergency.
8. Enter the name of the charter company providing the tour.
9. Enter the ownership number of the charter company.
10. Enter the license number of the charter company.
11. Enter your name and signature as the participant or authorized representative of the charter company.
12. Enter your name as the emergency contact person (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a participant.
- **Tour Operator** (`tour_operator`, text, required): Enter the name of the tour operator responsible for the tour.
- **Risk Acknowledgement** (`shark_tour_risk_acknowledgement`, text, optional): Fill in this field if you acknowledge any risks associated with the tour.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign this field to confirm you have read and understood the risks involved in the tour.
- **Date of Risk Acknowledgement** (`date_of_risk_acknowledgement`, date, required): Enter the date when you acknowledged the risks associated with the tour.
- **Reported Incident** (`reported_incident`, text, optional): If an incident occurs during the tour, report it in this field.
- **Participant Contact Details** (`participant_contact_details`, text, required): Enter your contact details for emergency situations.
- **Tour Operator Contact Details** (`tour_operator_contact_details`, text, required): Enter the contact details of the tour operator.
- **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Charter Company Name** (`charter_company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the charter company providing the tour.
- **Charter Company Ownership Number** (`charter_company_ownership_number`, text, required): Enter the ownership number of the charter company.
- **Charter Company License Number** (`charter_company_license_number`, text, required): Enter the license number of the charter company.
- **Participant Name (2)** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is the name of the participant (or authorized representative).
- **Participant Name (2)** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is another field for participant's name (no additional information provided in the yaml file, but assumed to be a duplicate entry).
