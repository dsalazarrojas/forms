# X RAY Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The X RAY Release Form is used to document patient information, X-ray details, authorization, and dates related to X-ray procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the page labeled "Patient Information" and fill in the patient's details as required.
2. Proceed to the "X RAY Details" page and provide the relevant information about the X-ray procedure.
3. Move on to the "Authorization" page and document the necessary authorization details.
4. Complete the "Date Received" and "Date Given" pages with the relevant dates.
5. Finally, fill in the "Time" page with the X-ray procedure's start and end time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, required/optional): This page is for collecting basic patient information such as name, ID, and contact details. Please fill in the required fields accurately.
* **X RAY Details** (`x_ray_details`, text, required/optional): This section is for recording the details of the X-ray procedure, including the reason for the procedure, findings, and any other relevant information.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, text, required/optional): Document the authorization details, if any, related to the X-ray procedure. This includes any signatures, stamps, or other proof of authorization.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, required/optional): Record the date the X-ray was received or taken.
* **Date Given** (`date_given`, date, required/optional): Record the date the X-ray procedure was performed.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Document the start and end time of the X-ray procedure.

## Tips
* Double-check the information you provide before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, and use the provided options when available.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the form's creators or your supervisor for assistance.
