# hospital-safety-innovation-filming-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain filming consent from individuals before recording or filming activities within a hospital setting. It is essential to ensure that all participants are aware of and agree to the filming process, which may involve capturing images or video footage of them. This form is a crucial step in maintaining the safety and privacy of patients, staff, and visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the purpose of filming within the hospital setting.
2. Answer the questions honestly and provide accurate information as requested.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title (Not Displayed on Form)** (`title`, `title`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is not displayed on the form itself.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required/optional): This field provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Filming Purpose** (`filming_purpose`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the purpose of filming, including the type of activities that will be recorded and the areas that will be filmed.
* **Filming Areas** (`filming_areas`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the locations or areas within the hospital where filming will take place.
* **Filming Equipment** (`filming_equipment`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the equipment that will be used for filming, including cameras, microphones, and other recording devices.
* **Filming Teams** (`filming_teams`, `text`, required/optional): List the individuals or teams involved in the filming, including their roles and responsibilities.
* **Filming Consent** (`filming_consent`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm that you have read and understand the filming consent form and agree to its terms.
* **Filming Declaration** (`filming_declaration`, `text`, required/optional): Make a declaration that you understand the filming will take place in the hospital setting and that you are aware of the potential risks and benefits.
* **Filming Signature** (`filming_signature`, `date`, required/true): Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.
* **Filming Review** (`filming_review`, `date`, required/false): Date the form to indicate when the filming review was completed.
* **Filming Notes** (`filming_notes`, `text`, required/false): Provide any additional notes or comments related to the filming.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`filming_reviewer_notes`, `text`, required/false): Provide any additional notes or comments from the reviewer.
* **Completion Date** (`filming_completion_date`, `date`, required/false): Date the form to indicate when the filming was completed.
* **Completion Time** (`filming_completion_time`, `time`, required/false): Specify the time when the filming was completed.
* **Completion** (`filming_completion`, `select_one`, required/true): Select whether the filming was completed or not.
* **Decline** (`filming_decline`, `select_one`, required/false): Select whether you decline to participate in the filming or not.
* **Consent Form** (`filming_consent_form`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Choose whether you agree or disagree to the filming consent form.
* **Consent Version** (`filming_consent_version`, `select_one`, required/false): Select the version of the consent form you are agreeing to.
* **Consent Date** (`filming_consent_date`, `date`, required/false): Date the form to indicate when you gave consent.
* **Consent Time** (`filming_consent_time`, `time`, required/false): Specify the time when you gave consent.
* **Reviewer** (`filming_reviewer`, `text`, required/false): Provide the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Role** (`filming_reviewer_role`, `text`, required/false): Specify the role of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`filming_reviewer_email`, `email`, required/false): Provide the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`filming_reviewer_phone`, `text`, required/false): Provide the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notice** (`filming_reviewer_notice`, `select_one`, required/false): Select whether the reviewer was notified or not.
* **Reviewer Message** (`filming_reviewer_message`, `text`, required/false): Provide any additional message from the reviewer.
