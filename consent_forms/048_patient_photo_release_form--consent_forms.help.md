## Patient Photo Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain patient consent for the release of their medical photos for various purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you need to release your photos. Select "Yes" if you are willing to release your photos for any or all of the purposes mentioned.
2. Enter your name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Enter the date and time you are releasing your photos in the "Release Date" and "Release Time" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the type of consent you are giving in the "Type of Consent" field.
5. Optionally, you can specify the location where your photos were taken in the "Release Location" field.
6. If you have any additional information or notes about the release, you can enter them in the "Release Notes" field.
7. Indicate if the photos will be assigned to anyone in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Photo Release** (`patient_photo_release_form`): This is a multiple-choice field where you can select whether you are releasing your photos for any or all of the purposes mentioned.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): This is the field to enter your full name.
* **Release Date** (`photo_release_date`): Enter the date you are releasing your photos.
* **Release Time** (`photo_release_time`): Enter the time you are releasing your photos.
* **Type of Consent** (`consent_type`): Choose the type of consent you are giving, which can be Patient Information, Medical Information, or Other.
* **Release Location** (`photo_release_location`): Enter the location where your photos were taken, if applicable.
* **Release Notes** (`photo_release_notes`): Enter any additional information you want to provide about the release.
* **Release Date** (`photo_release_date`): Enter the date you are releasing your photos again, to confirm.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Indicate if the photos will be assigned to anyone, and if so, which person or department.
