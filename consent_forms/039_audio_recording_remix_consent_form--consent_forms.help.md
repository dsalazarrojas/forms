**Audio Recording Remix Consent Form - Help Guide**

# Purpose
To obtain the consent and permission of the original artist for a remix of their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields: Original Artist Name, Remixer Full Name or Alias, Contact Email, and Date of Agreement.
2. Select the correct permission type: Type of Permission Granted that applies to your remix.
3. Specify the mandatory attribution requirements: Describe any mandatory attribution requirements for the original artist's name to be included in your remix.
4. Specify any prohibited modifications: If there are any modifications that are not allowed, please specify them.
5. Choose the revenue sharing agreement: Decide how the revenue generated by the remix will be split between you (remixer) and the original artist.
6. Sign and date the agreement: Sign and date the form to confirm that you and the original artist agree on the terms of the consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Original Artist Name** (Original Artist Name, text, required): The name of the original artist who created the work being remixed.
* **Remixer Full Name or Alias** (Remixer Full Name or Alias, text, required): Your full name or alias as the remixer.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): The email address where the original artist can contact you for any updates or inquiries.
* **Title of the Original Work** (Title of the Original Work, text, required): The title of the original work being remixed.
* **Original Release Date** (Original Release Date, date, optional): The date the original work was released.
* **Intended Scope of Remix** (Intended Scope of Remix, text, required): A brief description of the scope of your remix, e.g., "EDM Remix" or "Samples for a new track".
* **Type of Permission Granted** (Type of Permission Granted, select_one, required): Choose the type of permission granted, e.g., Non-Exclusive Commercial Use, Non-Commercial/Personal Use Only, or Master Use License required separately.
* **Mandatory Attribution Requirements** (Mandatory Attribution Requirements, text, required): Specify any mandatory attribution requirements, e.g., "(Original Artist Remix) must be in title".
* **Any Prohibited Modifications** (Any Prohibited Modifications, text, optional): Specify any modifications that are not allowed, e.g., "No use of vocals in specific genres, no political use".
* **Revenue Sharing Agreement** (Revenue Sharing Agreement, select_one, required): Choose how the revenue generated by the remix will be split between you (remixer) and the original artist, e.g., 50-50 Split or Custom agreement attached.
* **Consent Expiration Date** (Consent Expiration Date, date, optional): The date the consent will expire. Leave blank for perpetual consent.
* **Original Artist Signature (Type Name)** (Original Artist Signature (Type Name), text, required): Sign with your name as the original artist.
* **Remixer Signature (Type Name)** (Remixer Signature (Type Name), text, required): Sign with your name as the remixer.
* **Date of Agreement** (Date of Agreement, date, required): The date the agreement was signed.
