This form is a Telemedicine Services Agreement, which is a legal document that patients sign to consent to receive telemedicine services from a healthcare provider. The purpose of this form is to obtain informed consent from patients before they receive telemedicine services, ensuring that they understand the terms and conditions of their care.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide your information as a physician**:
	* Fill in the `physician_info` field with your name, address, and contact information as a healthcare provider.
2. **Provide your patient's information**:
	* Fill in the `patient_info` field with your patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. **Review the services agreement**:
	* Read the `services_agreement` field carefully and acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of telemedicine services.
4. **Sign the agreement**:
	* Sign your name in the `physician_signature` field.
5. **Enter your name**:
	* Fill in your name in the `physician_name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physicians Information**: Enter your name, address, and contact information as a healthcare provider.
* **Patient Information**: Enter your patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Services Agreement**: Review and acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of telemedicine services. This field is a note field, so you cannot enter any specific information, but you must acknowledge understanding the terms.
* **Physician Signature**: Sign your name in this field as a legal acknowledgment of your acceptance of the terms and conditions of telemedicine services.
* **Date**: This field is not used, as it is currently not required. 

## Tips

* Make sure to review the services agreement carefully before signing.
* Ensure that the patient's information is accurate.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for actual medical consultation.

Note: The fields `date` is not used in the form, so it is not necessary to fill it in. The `services_agreement` field is a note field, but it is still necessary for the user to read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of telemedicine services.
