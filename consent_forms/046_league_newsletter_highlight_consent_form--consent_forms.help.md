# League Newsletter Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent for sharing the league newsletter content with different entities. You will be asked to choose how you want to be included in the newsletter content.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Select how you want to be included in the newsletter content by choosing from the options provided for "individual_consent", "team_consent", and "both_consent".
2. If you choose to be included as part of a team, please select "Team consent" for "individual_consent".
3. If you choose to be included as part of a team, you do not need to fill out any other fields.
4. If you choose to be included as part of both teams and individuals, select "Both" for both "individual_consent" and "team_consent".
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field to provide a way to contact you.
6. You can leave the "phone" field blank if you do not have a phone number you'd like to provide.
7. You can leave the "note" field blank if you do not have any additional information you'd like to provide.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" for "both_consent" to confirm if you want to be included in the newsletter content.
9. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **individual_consent** (Select One): Choose how you want to be included in the newsletter content:
	+ Individual consent: You will be included in the newsletter content as an individual.
	+ Team consent: You will be included in the newsletter content as part of a team.
	+ Both: You will be included in the newsletter content as part of a team and as an individual.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **note** (Text): Provide any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **team_consent** (Select One): Choose how you want to be included in the newsletter content as part of a team.
* **both_consent** (Select One): Confirm if you want to be included in the newsletter content as part of a team and as an individual.
* **submit** (Submit): Submit the form to complete your request.
