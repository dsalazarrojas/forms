# HIPAA Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Authorization Form is designed to collect information necessary for the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) authorization process. This form is intended to be used by healthcare providers to collect patient information and obtain consent for treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information in the `provider_information` field.
2. Provide the date of service in the `date_of_service` field.
3. If necessary, select the type of signature in the `signature_type` field.
4. Enter the date and method of signature in the `date_signed_provider` and `date_signed_provider_method` fields.
5. If the patient has provided a signature, enter the date and method of signature in the `date_signed_patient` and `date_signed_patient_method` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Provider Information** (`provider_information`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the provider's name and contact information.

### 2. **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's name, address, and other relevant contact information.

### 3. **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date of the service or treatment provided.

### 4. **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's signature, if applicable.

### 5. **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the signature was made.

### 6. **Name on Signature** (`name_on_signature`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the name of the person making the signature.

### 7. **Signature Type** (`signature_type`, `select_one`, required: false)
Select the type of signature from the options provided.

### 8. **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the provider's signature, if applicable.

### 9. **Date Signed Provider** (`date_signed_provider`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the provider signed.

### 10. **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the patient's signature, if applicable.

### 11. **Date Signed Patient** (`date_signed_patient`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the patient signed.

### 12. **Provider Signature Method** (`provider_signature_method`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the method used by the provider to sign.

### 13. **Date Signed Provider Method** (`date_signed_provider_method`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the provider used to sign.

### 14. **Patient Signature Method** (`patient_signature_method`, `text`, required: false)
Enter the method used by the patient to sign.

### 15. **Date Signed Patient Method** (`date_signed_patient_method`, `date`, required: false)
Enter the date the patient used to sign.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are accurately filled out.
* Double-check that the patient's information is up-to-date.
* Store the completed form securely, as it contains sensitive patient information.
