# Medical Information For Air Travel - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information from passengers traveling by air. The purpose of this form is to ensure that passengers disclose any medical conditions or special needs that may impact their air travel experience. This information helps airlines to better serve passengers with medical requirements or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your passenger's name in the "Passenger Name" field.
2. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
3. If you have any flight-related concerns or questions, you can enter them in the "Flight Details" field. This is optional.
4. If you have a medical condition that may affect your air travel, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent" field.
5. If you have a medical condition, describe it in the detail in the "Medical Condition" field.
6. Provide additional medical details in the "Medical Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the passenger who is traveling.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, optional): If you have any flight-related concerns or questions, enter them here.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): If you have a medical condition that may affect your air travel, describe it here.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, text, required): Provide more details about your medical condition.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a medical condition that may affect your air travel, or "No" to indicate that you do not have any medical conditions. 

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure that we can provide the best service for your passenger.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the airline's customer service.

(Note: The "Consent" field is meant to indicate that the passenger has declared a medical condition, not necessarily that they have any specific condition that affects air travel. This field is used to trigger further investigation or support from the airline.)
