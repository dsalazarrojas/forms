# Teacher Student Co Authorship Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from both the teacher and student for a publication that they will co-author. The form ensures that both parties have agreed to the terms and conditions of the publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the publication in the "Publication Title" field.
2. Enter the teacher's first name, last name, and email in the "Teacher First Name", "Teacher Last Name", and "Teacher Email" fields respectively.
3. Enter the student's first name, last name, and email in the "Student First Name", "Student Last Name", and "Student Email" fields respectively.
4. Enter the date of publication in the "Publication Date" field.
5. Select the options for "Student Co-Authorship Consent", "Teacher Co-Authorship Consent", and "Consent Signed By Teacher" fields.
6. Select the options for "Consent Signed By Student" field.
7. Enter the date signed in the "Date Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: The title of the publication.
	+ A brief title of the publication that will be co-authored by the teacher and student.
* **Teacher First Name**: The first name of the teacher.
	+ Enter the teacher's first name.
* **Teacher Last Name**: The last name of the teacher.
	+ Enter the teacher's last name.
* **Teacher Email**: The email of the teacher.
	+ Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Student First Name**: The first name of the student.
	+ Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name**: The last name of the student.
	+ Enter the student's last name.
* **Student Email**: The email of the student.
	+ Enter the student's email address.
* **Publication Date**: The date of the publication.
	+ Enter the date of the publication.
* **Publication Title**: The title of the publication.
	+ Enter the title of the publication.
* **Publication Journal**: The journal where the publication will be published.
	+ Enter the journal where the publication will be published.
* **Student Co-Authorship Consent**:
	+ Select whether the student has given consent to co-authorship.
* **Teacher Co-Authorship Consent**:
	+ Select whether the teacher has given consent to co-authorship.
* **Consent Signed By Teacher**:
	+ Select whether the teacher has signed the consent form.
* **Consent Signed By Student**:
	+ Select whether the student has signed the consent form.
* **Date Signed**:
	+ Enter the date when the consent form was signed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
