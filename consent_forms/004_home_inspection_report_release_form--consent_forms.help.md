# Home Inspection Report Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from homeowners about their inspection report and real estate professionals involved in the inspection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the fields for the inspection report details:
	* **Inspection Report**: Enter a brief description of the inspection report.
	* **Inspection Date**: Enter the date of the inspection.
	* **Inspection Time**: Enter the time of the inspection.
	* **Inspection Location**: Enter the address of the inspected home.
2. Provide details about the real estate professional(s) involved:
	* **Real Estate Professional**: Select the type of real estate professional (e.g., Broker, Lender, Appraiser).
	* **Relationship with Real Estate Professional**: Describe the relationship between the homeowner and the real estate professional.
	* **Address of Real Estate Professional**: Enter the address of the real estate professional.
	* **Email of Real Estate Professional**: Enter the email address of the real estate professional.
	* **Phone of Real Estate Professional**: Enter the phone number of the real estate professional.
	* **Fax of Real Estate Professional**: Enter the fax number of the real estate professional.
3. Check the permission box to determine if the homeowner grants permission to share the inspection report:
	* **Homeowner Permission to Share**: Select whether the homeowner grants permission to share the report with real estate professionals.
4. If applicable, add additional notes about the inspection:
	* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
