# Blood Donation Drive Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who are interested in donating blood. It is designed to gather essential information about the donor, including their consent, medical conditions, travel history, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by reading the introduction section at the top. This will explain the purpose of the form and the information we are collecting.
2. Answer the questions as accurately as possible. Make sure to check the correct box for each question.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Blood Donation Drive Consent --** (`permission_intro`):
	+ This is an introduction section that explains the purpose of the form and what we are collecting information for.
* **First Name** (`donor_first_name`):
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`donor_last_name`):
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Age** (`donor_age`):
	+ Enter your age as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`):
	+ Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further information or follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`):
	+ Enter a phone number where we can contact you for further information or follow-up.
* **Home Address** (`donor_address`):
	+ Enter your current home address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **I consent to donate blood** (`consent_donation`):
	+ Check the box to confirm that you agree to donate blood.
* **I consent to health screening procedures** (`consent_health_screening`):
	+ Check the box to confirm that you agree to health screening procedures.
* **I consent to infectious disease testing** (`consent_infectious_testing`):
	+ Check the box to confirm that you agree to infectious disease testing.
* **I consent to use of my health data for screening** (`consent_data_use`):
	+ Check the box to confirm that you agree to the use of your health data for screening.
* **I consent to be contacted for follow-up** (`consent_contact_up`):
	+ Check the box to confirm that you agree to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Do you have any chronic medical conditions?** (`medical_conditions`):
	+ Check the box to confirm if you have any chronic medical conditions.
* **Are you currently pregnant?** (`current_pregnancy`):
	+ Check the box to confirm if you are currently pregnant.
* **Have you traveled outside the country recently?** (`recent_travel`):
	+ Check the box to confirm if you have traveled outside the country recently.
* **Have you had tattoos or piercings in the last year?** (`tattoo_piercing`):
	+ Check the box to confirm if you have had tattoos or piercings in the last year.
* **Parent/Guardian consent** (`parent_guardian_consent`):
	+ Check the box to confirm if you are under 18 and have parental or guardian consent.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`):
	+ Date and sign this form to confirm your consent.
 
Note: Please review each field carefully and answer all questions accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
