# Fertility Clinic Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's discharge from a fertility clinic. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that all relevant details are recorded and communicated effectively between healthcare providers.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all required fields marked as "required".
2.  If you are unsure about any field, please seek assistance from a healthcare professional.
3.  Complete the form in one sitting, as it may be more efficient for you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information (`page1`, Text, Optional)**: This field is intended for providing any additional information about the patient. Please enter any relevant details that may be helpful for the patient's care.
*   **Medical History (`medical_history`, Text, Optional)**: This field is for documenting any medical history that may be relevant to the patient's care. Please enter any medical conditions, allergies, or previous surgeries that may impact their treatment.
*   **Medication Information (`medication_info`, Text, Optional)**: Enter any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
*   **Allergies (`allergies`, Select multiple, Optional)**: Indicate whether the patient has any allergies. Please select all applicable options from the list provided.
*   **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, Select one, Optional)**: Identify any medical condition the patient has. Please select one option from the list provided.
*   **Patient Consent (`consent`, Select multiple, Optional)**: Indicate the type of consent the patient has given. Please select all applicable options from the list provided.
*   **Follow Up Information (`follow_up_info`, Text, Optional)**: Enter any follow-up information that may be helpful for the patient's care.
*   **Patient Information (`patient_info`, Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional information about the patient that may be useful for their care.

## Tips

*   Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in patient care.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please seek assistance from a healthcare professional.
*   Complete the form in one sitting for efficiency.
*   Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure all necessary information is included.
*   Please keep the form up-to-date, as patient information may change over time.
