# Experience Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture participant information and waiver details for events. It is intended for individuals who want to participate in events and are required to sign a waiver before doing so. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Name** field with your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your **Participant Email** in the format of `[name}@[domain]`.
3. Provide your **Participant Phone** number.
4. Enter the **Event Name** as it is advertised or known.
5. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the event.
6. For **Waiver**, select all options that apply to you.
7. Finally, type "Yes" or "No" in the **Agree** field to signify your agreement with the waiver terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is where you should enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of `[name]` at `[domain]`.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): This is your phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event as it is known or advertised.
* **Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, required): Select the date when the event starts.
* **End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, required): Select the date when the event ends.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_multiple, optional): This field offers you options to waive or not waive certain claims.
* **Agree** (`waiver_agree`, text, optional): Check if you agree to the waiver terms by typing "Yes" or "No".

## Tips
- Please double-check the accuracy of your input before submitting.
- If you have any issues or questions, reach out to our support team.
