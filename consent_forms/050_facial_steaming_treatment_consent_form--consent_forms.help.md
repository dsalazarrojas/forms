# Facial Steaming Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Facial Steaming Treatment Consent Form is used to collect client information, treatment details, and a client declaration for facial steaming treatments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately.**
   Please provide all required information to ensure that your treatment is tailored to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Client Information (page 1)

* **Client Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your phone number and email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address**: Enter your current address.

### Treatment Details (page 1)

* **Treatment Type**: Select the type of facial steaming treatment you are interested in (e.g., acne, aging, etc.).
* **Treatment Location**: Specify the location where the treatment will take place.
* **Treatment Time**: Enter the duration of the treatment.

### Client Declaration (page 2)

* **I hereby declare that I am aware that facial steaming can cause irritation or redness**: Check this box to confirm that you understand the potential risks and are willing to proceed.
* **I have no medical conditions that may affect the treatment outcome**: Check this box if you do not have any medical conditions that might impact the effectiveness of the treatment.
* **I understand that I must follow the treatment guidelines and instructions provided**: Check this box to confirm that you will follow all instructions during the treatment.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully and accurately fill out each one.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your medical professional or contact the treatment center.
* The information collected in this form will be used to tailor your treatment to your specific needs and ensure a safe and effective experience.
