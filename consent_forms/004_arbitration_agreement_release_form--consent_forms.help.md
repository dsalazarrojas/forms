## Arbitration Agreement Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the terms of an arbitration agreement between two parties involved in a dispute. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the terms of the agreement are clear and binding. By completing this form, you will be able to agree to binding arbitration, waive the right to appeal the decision, and release confidential information related to the dispute. This form is a critical step in resolving disputes in a fair and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the dispute and its nature before filling out this form.
2.  Carefully read through each section and fill out the relevant information for your dispute.
3.  Make sure to sign and date the form, as this is a legally binding agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Releasing Party Full Name** (`releasing_party_name`, text, required): This field is for the name of the individual or entity releasing their rights to participate in arbitration.
*   **Releasing Party Address** (`releasing_party_address`, text, required): This field is for the full mailing address of the individual or entity releasing their rights to participate in arbitration.
*   **Other Party Name** (`other_party_name`, text, required): This field is for the name of the individual or entity with whom you are in dispute.
*   **Nature of Dispute** (`dispute_nature`, select_one, required): Select the type or category of the dispute (e.g., Contract, Employment, Property, Personal Injury, or Commercial).
*   **Dispute Description** (`dispute_description`, text, required): This field is for a detailed explanation of the dispute.
*   **Date Dispute Arose** (`dispute_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date when the dispute began (if applicable).
*   **Agree to Binding Arbitration** (`arbitration_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Agree" to indicate that you agree to binding arbitration.
*   **Confidentiality Clause** (`release_confidentiality`, select_one, required): Select "True" to indicate that you agree to keep all proceedings confidential.
*   **Waiver of Appeal Rights** (`appeal_waiver`, select_one, required): Select "True" to waive your right to appeal the arbitration decision.
*   **Date of Release** (`release_date`, date, required): This field is for the date on which you are releasing your rights to participate in arbitration.
*   **Witness Name (if applicable)** (`witness_signature_name`, text, optional): If a witness is present, this field is for their name.
*   **Legal Counsel Attestation** (`legal_counsel_attestation`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if a legal counsel has reviewed this release; otherwise, select "False" or "Not Applicable".

## Tips

*   Make sure to sign and date the form, as this is a legally binding agreement.
*   Ensure that the form is completed accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, consult with a legal expert or a qualified mediator.
