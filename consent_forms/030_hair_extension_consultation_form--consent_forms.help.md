# Hair Extension Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers about their hair condition, style, and other relevant details. It will help our team better understand their needs and preferences for hair extensions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Choose the correct options for each field.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Hair Extension Consultation Form 1**
* Hair extension consultation form 1: This is the start of the form. You can enter any relevant information in this field.

### **Consent Date**
* Consent date: Please enter the date you consented to the hair extension consultation.

### **Customer Name**
* Customer name: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.

### **Contact Email**
* Contact email: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### **Phone Number**
* Phone number: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### **Hair Condition**
* Hair condition: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your hair is:
	+ Yes (in good condition)
	+ No (in poor condition)

### **Hair Texture**
* Hair texture: Select all that apply from the list below to describe your hair texture:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### **Hair Style**
* Hair style: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are looking for:
	+ Yes (a specific style)
	+ No (no specific style)

### **Hair Type**
* Hair type: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have:
	+ Yes (a specific hair type)
	+ No (no specific hair type)

### **Color Treatment**
* Color treatment: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are looking for:
	+ Yes (color treatment)
	+ No (no color treatment)
