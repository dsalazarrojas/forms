# Expense Dispute Resolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for expense disputes that require further investigation and resolution. It's essential to be honest and accurate when filling out this form to help resolve the dispute efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your business information.
2. Provide your employee information.
3. Describe the dispute details in the text area.
4. Attach any supporting documents related to the dispute.
5. Sign the employee signature section.
6. Get your manager's signature.
7. Get your approver's signature.
8. Enter the date when the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Info** (Business Info, text, not required): This is the field where you enter your business information, which may be required for some disputes.
* **Employee Info** (Employee Info, text, not required): This is the field where you enter your employee information to identify yourself for the dispute.
* **Dispute Details** (Dispute Details, text, not required): Please describe the dispute in detail, including what happened and the resolution you are seeking.
* **Supporting Documents** (Supporting Documents, text, not required): Attach any documents, receipts, or other evidence that support your claim or dispute.
* **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, not required): Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the company's expense policy and that the information provided is accurate.
* **Manager Signature** (Manager Signature, text, not required): Sign this form to direct the employee to complete it and confirm that you have discussed the dispute with the employee.
* **Approver Signature** (Approver Signature, text, not required): Sign this form to confirm that you have reviewed and approved the resolution to the dispute.
* **Form Submitted** (Form Submitted, number, not required): This field is for internal use and indicates when the form was submitted.
## Tips
* Make sure to attach all necessary documents to support your claim.
* Be clear and concise when describing the dispute.
* Ensure the employee signs the form to confirm they have read the policy and understand the dispute resolution.
* The manager or approver should review and sign the form to confirm they have discussed the dispute with the employee.
* The approver should sign to confirm they have reviewed and approved the resolution.
