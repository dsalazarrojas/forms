# Sugaring Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sugaring Waiver Form is designed to collect consent and information from customers or patients before undergoing sugaring activities. It typically includes personal details, treatment specifics, and consent for the service provider to administer the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Birth Date**, which is your date of birth.
3. Provide a detailed description of the **Service Agreement** you are consenting to, including any specific conditions or details about the treatment you will receive.
4. In the **Customer Acknowledgement** field, confirm that you have read and understand the service provider's policies and procedures.
5. Enter the name of the **Service Provider**, who will be administering the treatment.
6. Select a date for the **Date of Service**, when you will receive the treatment.
7. Enter the address of the **Service Provider**, where you will receive the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sugaring Waiver Form**:
	+ This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Name**:
	+ A text field to enter your name.
* **Birth Date**:
	+ A date field to enter your date of birth.
* **Service Agreement**:
	+ A text field to describe the specific service you are consenting to, including any conditions or details about the treatment.
* **Customer Acknowledgement**:
	+ A text field to confirm that you have read and understood the service provider's policies.
* **Service Provider Name**:
	+ A text field to enter the name of the service provider who will administer the treatment.
* **Date of Service**:
	+ A date field to select the date of the treatment.
* **Service Provider Address**:
	+ A text field to enter the address of the service provider.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submission.
- If you are unsure about any field or the information required, contact the service provider for clarification.
- Review the service provider's policies and procedures before signing the form.
