# Global Waiver Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Waiver Agreement form is designed to gather information and obtain consent from participants before engaging in certain activities or using specific services. This form is intended for use in a variety of settings, including but not limited to educational institutions, research centers, and organizations offering recreational activities. The form ensures that participants are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions, risks, and responsibilities associated with the activity or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your information**: Fill in your participant details in the "Participant Information" page.
2. **Acknowledge the risks**: In the "Risk Acknowledgement" page, carefully read and agree to the risks associated with the activity or service.
3. **Accept the legal terms**: In the "Legal Agreement" page, review and accept the terms and conditions of the activity or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Information**: 
    - (`Participant Information`, text, optional): This section requires you to provide your basic contact information, such as name and contact number.

- **Risk Acknowledgement**:
    - (`Risk Acknowledgement`, note, optional): In this section, you will acknowledge the potential risks associated with the activity or service. Please read this section carefully to understand the risks involved.

- **Legal Agreement**:
    - (`Legal Agreement`, text, optional): This section presents the legal terms and conditions of the activity or service. Please read and accept this agreement carefully before proceeding.

## Tips
- Always read the entire form carefully before submitting your information.
- Ensure you understand the risks and legal terms associated with the activity or service.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the form administrator for assistance.
