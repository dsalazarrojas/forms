# Broodmare Reproductive Procedure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals involved in the reproductive procedures of broodmares to collect essential information. It is meant to ensure accuracy and compliance in the process. Review the form carefully before proceeding to ensure it meets your specific needs and is not a duplicate submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and contact information in the "Owner Full Name", "Owner Phone Number", and "Owner Email Address" fields.
2. Provide the registered name or barn name of the mare in the "Mare Name" field.
3. Enter the mare's age in years in the "Mare Age" field.
4. Choose the mare's breed from the options provided in the "Mare Breed" field.
5. Select the type of procedure being performed from the options in the "Procedure Type" field.
6. Enter the scheduled date of the procedure in the "Scheduled Procedure Date" field.
7. Fill in the "Procedure Location" field with the name of the clinic, farm, or facility where the procedure will take place.
8. If applicable, enter the estimated cost of the procedure in the "Estimated Procedure Cost" field.
9. Choose how you will be paying for the procedure from the options in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Acknowledge understanding of the risks associated with the procedure by selecting "Yes, I understand" from the "I understand the risks associated with this procedure" field.
11. Confirm your consent to the procedure by selecting "Yes, I consent" from the "I authorize this procedure and accept all risks" field.
12. Date your consent by filling in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Owner Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Owner Email Address** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Mare Name** (text, required): Enter the registered name or barn name of the mare.
* **Mare Registration Number** (text, optional): If applicable, enter the breed registry number of the mare.
* **Mare Age** (number, required): Enter the age of the mare in years.
* **Mare Breed** (select_one, required): Choose the mare's breed from the options provided.
* **Breeding History** (number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of foals produced by the mare.
* **Date of Last Foal** (date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the last foal.
* **Procedure Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of procedure being performed from the options provided.
* **Scheduled Procedure Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the scheduled procedure.
* **Veterinarian Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the veterinarian conducting the procedure.
* **Procedure Location** (text, required): Enter the name of the clinic, farm, or facility where the procedure will take place.
* **Estimated Procedure Cost** (number, optional): If applicable, enter the estimated cost of the procedure.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose how you will be paying for the procedure from the options provided.
* **I understand the risks associated with this procedure** (select_one, required): Acknowledge understanding of the risks associated with the procedure.
* **I authorize this procedure and accept all risks** (select_one, required): Confirm your consent to the procedure.
* **Date** (date, required): Date your consent.
