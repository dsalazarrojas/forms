# Reentry Simulation Participant Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reentry Simulation Participant Registration form collects participant information and session preferences for Reentry Simulation events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in your participant information (name, email, phone, and address) in the respective fields.
3. Select your preferences for each session from the provided options.
4. Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
5. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): This section collects general participant information, including name, email, phone, and address.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your residential or mailing address.
* **Session 1 Preferences** (`session_preferences_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preference for Session 1 from the options provided.
* **Session 2 Preferences** (`session_preferences_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preference for Session 2 from the options provided.
* ...
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional consent or comments you may have.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are registered for the Reentry Simulation event before submitting this form.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers for help.
