# lash_and_brow_tint_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lash and brow tint consent form is a document used to obtain consent from clients for lash and brow tinting services. It ensures that clients understand the procedures, risks, and potential outcomes involved in the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully, as they are all required unless marked as optional.
2. Make sure to select all relevant services offered from the drop-down menu, as this will help us understand which services you would like to receive.
3. Provide your contact information for us to reach you if any issues arise during the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Enter your personal details, as you would typically do in a standard form submission.
* **Services Offered (2)**: Select all the services you are interested in, which may include Lash and Brow Tinting and Other Services. This will help us understand what services you would like to receive.
* **Consent (3)**: In this field, please provide your consent for the selected services. Type 'yes' or 'no' as a confirmation.
* **Contact Details (4)**: Enter your contact details, including name, address, and phone number (if available), so we can contact you if any issues arise during or after the service.
* **Emergency Contact (5)**: Please provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact, in case we need to reach them in case of an issue.
* **Signature (6)**: This is a note field where you can enter your signature as a confirmation of your consent.
* **Date (7)**: Enter the date you are providing consent for the selected services.
* **Services Conducted (8)**: Select all the services you are interested in, which may include Lash and Brow Tinting and Other Services. This will help us understand which services you would like to receive.
* **Date and Time (9)**: Enter the date and time you would like to receive the selected services.
* **Services Touched (10)**: Enter any services that will be applied during the lash and brow tinting process (if applicable).
* **Services Washed (10)**: Enter any services that will be washed during the lash and brow tinting process (if applicable).
* **Services Applied (11)**: Enter any additional services applied during the lash and brow tinting process (if applicable).

**Tips**

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure you understand what you are consenting to.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us so we can address them before proceeding with the service.
