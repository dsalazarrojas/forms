# Media Coverage Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture media coverage records release information for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Contact Person: Enter the name of the person who provided you with the media coverage records.
	* Release Date: Enter the date when you will release the media coverage records.
	* Release Time: Enter the time when you will release the media coverage records.
	* Phone Number: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
	* Email: Enter the email of the contact person.
	* Signature: Enter your signature to confirm that you are submitting the media coverage records release information accurately.
	* Date Signed: Enter the date when you signed the release.
	* Time Signed: Enter the time when you signed the release.
2. Fill in the optional fields as necessary:
	* Organization: Enter the name of the organization providing the media coverage records.
	* Media Type: Select the type of media coverage records being released.
	* Media Usage: Select the intended use of the media coverage records.
	* Contact Info 2: Enter any additional contact information for the contact person.
	* Phone Number 2: Enter any additional phone number for the contact person.
	* Email 2: Enter any additional email for the contact person.
	* Signature 2: Enter your signature for any additional release.
	* Date Signed 2: Enter the date when you signed any additional release.
	* Time Signed 2: Enter the time when you signed any additional release.
	* Date Approved 2: Enter the date when any additional release was approved.
	* Time Approved 2: Enter the time when any additional release was approved.
	* Notes 2: Enter any additional notes about the release.
3. Review and submit the form for approval.
