# Substance Abuse Counseling Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form collects consent information from individuals who are considering or are currently receiving substance abuse counseling services.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" on Page 1 to indicate your willingness to participate in counseling services.
3. Enter your contact information on Page 2, including your full name and contact details.
4. Enter your full name and date of birth on Page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: "page_1" (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to participate in counseling services, and "No" if you are not. This page helps us understand your willingness to begin the counseling process.
* **Page 2: "contact_info" (Contact Information)**: Enter your full name, phone number, and any other contact details you wish to provide. This information helps us stay in touch with you and provide updates on your counseling progress.
* **Page 3: "name" (Name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Page 3: "date_of_birth" (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format. This helps us understand your age and verify your identity.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your counselor or contact us at [insert contact information].
