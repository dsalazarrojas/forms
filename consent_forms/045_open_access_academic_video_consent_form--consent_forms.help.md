# open-access-academic-video-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for open-access academic video recording purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your details.
2. Provide your contact information for future communication.
3. Enter your participant information, including details about the participant (e.g., name, age, etc.).
4. Specify the purpose of the recording.
5. Choose one of the consent options for the participant.
6. Fill out the date and time of consent.
7. Enter the date of the recording.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)** (text, optional)
	+ This field captures your basic information.
* **Contact Information (2)** (text, optional)
	+ This field allows you to provide your contact details for future communication.
* **Phone Number (3)** (number, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for further questions.
* **Email Address (4)** (email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for further questions.
* **Participant Information (5)** (text, optional)
	+ Provide details about the participant, such as their name, age, etc.
* **Recording Video (6)** (text, optional)
	+ Specify the purpose of the recording.
* **Publication Permission (7)** (text, optional)
	+ If you have already granted publication permission, enter the details.
* **Participant Consent (8)** (select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the consent options for the participant:
		+ Option 1: Participant has given consent.
		+ Option 2: Participant has declined to give consent.
		+ Option 3: Participant is unable to give consent.
		+ Option 4: Participant is deceased.
		+ Option 5: Not applicable.
* **Date (9)** (date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of consent.
* **Time (10)** (time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of consent.
* **Recording Date (11)** (date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the recording.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid delays in processing.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you need assistance or clarification, contact your local administrator.
