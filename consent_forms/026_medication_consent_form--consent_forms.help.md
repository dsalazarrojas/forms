# Medication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medication Consent Form is used by healthcare professionals to gather information from patients about their medication use. It helps collect data about patient name, medication name, treatment details, dosage, and consent to receive treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., ID card, passport, etc.). This information will help us identify you correctly.
2. **Medication Name**: Enter the name of the medication you are currently taking or have taken. Be as specific as possible.
3. **Treatmment Details**: Provide any relevant details about your treatment, such as the reason for taking the medication, any medical conditions you are trying to address, etc.
4. **Medication Dosage**: Enter the dosage of the medication you are currently taking.
5. **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of treatment.
6. **Patient Consent**: Please select whether you consent to receive treatment from our healthcare professionals. This includes understanding the risks and benefits associated with treatment.
7. **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form. The date must be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
8. **Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign this form.
9. **Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions. This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: The patient's name as it appears on their identification documents. This is not required but will help us identify them correctly.
* **Medication Name**: The specific name of the medication being used. This helps healthcare professionals understand what you are taking.
* **Treatmment Details**: Any relevant details about the treatment or medical condition being addressed.
* **Medication Dosage**: The dosage of the medication being taken.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of treatment.
* **Patient Consent**: A select option to confirm whether you consent to receive treatment.
* **Signature Date**: The date you sign this form. The date must be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Signature Time**: The time you sign this form.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling in the form.
* If you are unsure about anything, ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
* Ensure that your date and time of signing match the format required.
