# Applicant Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Applicant Consent Form is used to obtain the necessary consents from applicants before the company conducts background checks and processes their personal data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you are completing this form for the correct purpose and with the correct link or form instance provided to you.
2. Carefully read each section of the form and provide accurate information as requested.
3. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required").
4. Select the relevant options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (full_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. passport, ID card).
- **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of username@domain.com.
- **Application Reference ID** (application_id, text, optional): If known, provide any relevant Application Reference ID for your submission.
- **Position or Program Applied For** (position_title, text, required): Select the job or program you are applying for.
- **Consent for Background Check** (background_check_consent, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to authorize the company to conduct a background check.
- **Consent for Reference Checks** (reference_check_consent, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to authorize the company to contact your previous employers and personal references.
- **Consent for Personal Data Processing** (data_processing_consent, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to agree to the collection and processing of your personal data for recruitment purposes.
- **Future Opportunities Notification** (marketing_communications_opt_in, select_one, optional): Select "Yes - Opt-in" to be notified about future job openings.
- **Accuracy of Information** (accuracy_confirmation, select_one, required): Select "I Confirm" to certify that all information provided is true and complete.
- **Electronic Signature (Type Full Name)** (electronic_signature_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g. passport, ID card).
- **Date of Signature** (signature_date, date, required): Enter the date on which you are signing this form.

By completing this form, you are providing the necessary consents for the company to proceed with your application and background checks. Please ensure that you have read and understood each section of the form before submitting.
