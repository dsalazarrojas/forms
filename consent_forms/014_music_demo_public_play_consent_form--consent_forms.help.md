# Music Demo Public Play Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music Demo Public Play Consent Form is a document that gathers information from artists and their representatives to ensure public plays of their music are done lawfully. This form helps administrators verify the artist's permission for public play, which is a necessary step in various music industry practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Artist Permission field with the appropriate option: Artist, Manager, or Record Label.
2. Enter the artist's Name in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the artist's Email address in the Artist Email field.
4. Enter the artist's Phone number in the Artist Phone field.
5. Select the Date of Public Play from the calendar provided.
6. Choose the Time of Public Play from the time selection options.
7. Enter the Recording URL where the music will be played.
8. Add any additional Recording Notes if necessary.
9. Finally, fill in the Form Submitter field with your name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Public Play Consent Form** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This is the main form title.
- **Artist Permission** (`id: 2`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose the correct option to indicate who gave you permission to play the music:
	+ Artist: The artist themselves granted permission.
	+ Manager: The artist's manager granted permission.
	+ Record Label: The record label granted permission.
- **Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the artist's full name.
- **Email** (`id: 4`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the artist's email address.
- **Phone** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the artist’s phone number.
- **Date of Public Play** (`id: 6`, `type: date`, required: false): Select the date you intend to play the music publicly.
- **Time of Public Play** (`id: 7`, `type: time`, required: false): Choose the time you plan to play the music.
- **Recording URL** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the URL where the music will be recorded and played.
- **Recording Notes** (`id: 9`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about the recording.
- **Form Submitter** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Fill in your name and contact information as the person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and truthfully.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the artist or their representative for clarification.
- If you need to make changes to the form, save your progress and revisit this guide for further instructions.
