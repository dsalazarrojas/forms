# medical_consent_withdrawal_amendment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to a patient's medical consent withdrawal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review the form to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields.
3. Leave optional fields blank unless required for specific reasons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`id: 1`): This section is for the patient's basic details such as name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Preference** (`id: 2`): Choose how you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Medical History** (`id: 3`): List any significant medical history relevant to your condition or treatment.
* **Medical Conditions** (`id: 4`): List any medical conditions that may be relevant to your treatment or condition.
* **Medication List** (`id: 5`): List any medications currently being taken, including dosage and frequency.
* **Withdrawal Reason** (`id: 6`): Explain the reason you are withdrawing your consent for medical treatment.
* **Patient Statement** (`id: 7`): Add any additional comments or information you feel is relevant to your case.
* **Healthcare Provider Comments** (`id: 8`): Comments from the healthcare provider about your case.
* **Patient Signature** (`id: 9`): Check the box to confirm that you have read and understood this form.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`id: 10`): Check the box to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the patient's information.

Note: Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and information before filling out this form.
