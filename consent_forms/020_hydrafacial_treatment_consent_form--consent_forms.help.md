# hydrafacial_treatment_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hydrafacial_treatment_consent_form is used to obtain consent from patients for treatment at your medical practice. It is essential that this form is completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the patient's rights and medical history are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
4. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Enter the name of the medical practice in the "Medical Practice" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`patient_first_name`, text, Required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last name** (`patient_last_name`, text, Required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, Optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, Optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Medical Practice** (`medical_practice`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the medical practice where the patient will be receiving treatment.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled in to successfully submit the form.
