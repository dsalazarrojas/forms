# emergency_release_authorization_intuce_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The emergency release authorization intake form is used to capture information about an individual's emergency contact details. This form is necessary to ensure that the individual's medical team has the necessary information to contact the individual's next of kin or authorized representative in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, optional): Enter the individual's first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, optional): Enter the individual's last name as it is known to you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter the individual's contact number where they can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, optional): Describe your relationship to the individual, e.g., parent, sibling, spouse, etc.
