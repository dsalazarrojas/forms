# Voice Chat Speaking Permission Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to use Voice Chat speaking permissions. By filling out this form, you are providing information about your role, planned usage, and preferred time and frequency of access. This form helps us understand your needs and requirements for Voice Chat speaking permissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out **form_2: What is your role in the community?** This field is required and helps us understand your position within the community.
2. Select one of the provided options for **form_4: Select the duration of time for your Voice Chat speaking access**.
3. Enter your expected **form_5: What is your expected time of usage for Voice Chat speaking permissions?** in the required date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Choose a preferred time of day for **form_7: Select the time of day for your Voice Chat speaking access**.
5. Select one of the provided options for **form_8: Select the frequency of usage for your Voice Chat speaking access**.
6. (Optional) If you need to, select additional information about your Voice Chat speaking access from **form_9: Select any additional information for Voice Chat speaking access**.
7. Add any additional comments or concerns you have about Voice Chat speaking permissions in **form_10: Additional comments or concerns about Voice Chat speaking permissions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_1: Voice Chat Speaking Permission Request Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **form_2: What is your role in the community?**: Please select your role in the community to help us understand your position and responsibilities.
* **form_3: How long do you plan to use Voice Chat speaking permissions for?**: This field helps us understand how long you need access to Voice Chat permissions.
* **form_4: Select the duration of time for your Voice Chat speaking access**: Select the duration of time you need access to Voice Chat permissions.
*	* 1 week: You need access to Voice Chat permissions for a week.
	* 2 weeks: You need access to Voice Chat permissions for 2 weeks.
	* 3 weeks: You need access to Voice Chat permissions for 3 weeks.
* **form_5: What is your expected time of usage for Voice Chat speaking permissions?**: Enter the date when you expect to start using Voice Chat permissions.
* **form_7: Select the time of day for your Voice Chat speaking access**: Choose a time of day when you are most active in the Voice Chat platform.
	* Morning: You are most active in the morning.
	* Afternoon: You are most active in the afternoon.
	* Evening: You are most active in the evening.
* **form_8: Select the frequency of usage for your Voice Chat speaking access**: Select how often you plan to use Voice Chat permissions.
*	* Regular: You plan to use Voice Chat permissions regularly.
	* Irregular: You plan to use Voice Chat permissions occasionally.
	* No answer: You don't have a fixed schedule for using Voice Chat permissions.
* **form_9: Select any additional information for Voice Chat speaking access**: If necessary, provide additional information about your need for Voice Chat permissions.
* **form_10: Additional comments or concerns about Voice Chat speaking permissions**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required).
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
