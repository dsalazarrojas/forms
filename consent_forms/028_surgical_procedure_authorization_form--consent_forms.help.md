# Surgical Procedure Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and obtain authorization for surgical procedures. It is used by medical professionals to collect relevant data from patients, doctors, and other healthcare providers. The form is essential for ensuring that all necessary information is collected and documented accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the procedure being performed in the "Procedure Name" field.
3. Enter the doctor's name who will be performing the surgery in the "Doctor Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the surgery in the "Date of Surgery" field.
5. Enter the time of the surgery in the "Time of Surgery" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: The patient's name is a required field. Please enter the patient's full name, including their first and last name.
* **Procedure Name**: Enter the name of the procedure being performed, such as "Knee Replacement Surgery" or "Hysterectomy".
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the name of the doctor who will be performing the surgery.
* **Date of Surgery**: Enter the date of the surgery in the format "MM/DD/YY" (e.g., 02/15/2023). Please ensure that the date is correct, as it is crucial for scheduling and record-keeping purposes.
* **Time of Surgery**: Enter the time of the surgery in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 08:00). The time is essential for ensuring that the patient is on time for the procedure.
* **Note**: This form is not a comprehensive surgical consent form and should not be used as such. It is intended to be used as a general information collection tool. Please ensure that you have a separate consent form that covers all necessary details and is signed by the patient before the procedure.
