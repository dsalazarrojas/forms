# House Of Colours Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by the company to collect relevant information from clients, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and documented accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Client Name** (Field 1) and **Email** (Field 2) accurately and completely.
2. Provide your **Contact Number** (Field 3), if applicable.
3. Enter your **Home Address** (Field 4) including your street address and house number (Field 5).
4. Enter the **Date Of Visit** (Field 6) and **Time Of Visit** (Field 7) if relevant.
5. Provide a brief **House Description** (Field 8) of your property.
6. Enter your **Job Description** (Field 9) and **Work Schedule** (Field 10) if applicable.
7. Select the **Job Start Date** (Field 11) and **Job Finish Date** (Field 12), if known.
8. Enter the expected **Job Duration** (Field 13), if applicable.
9. Enter the estimated **Job Finish Time** (Field 14), if applicable.
10. Enter the **Job Cost** (Field 15), if applicable.
11. Agree to the **Payment Terms** (Field 16), which should be read and understood carefully.
12. Sign your **Signature 2** (Field 17), if applicable.
13. Sign and date your **Client Signature** (Field 18) to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (1, required)**: Enter the full name of the client or person you are providing services for.
* **Email (2, required)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Number (3, optional)**: If you want to be contacted at a specific number, enter it here.
* **Home Address (4, required)**: Enter your home address including street number, but do not include any additional descriptions.
* **House Number (5, optional)**: Enter any house number relevant to your address.
* **Date Of Visit (6, optional)**: Enter the date you expect the job to be done.
* **Time Of Visit (7, optional)**: Enter the estimated start and finish time of the job.
* **House Description (8, required)**: Briefly describe the property you are working on.
* **Job Description (9, optional)**: Describe the specific work being done.
* **Work Schedule (10, optional)**: If you have a schedule for work, enter it here.
* **Job Start Date (11, optional)**: If known, select the start date of the job.
* **Job Finish Date (12, optional)**: If known, select the finish date of the job.
* **Job Duration (13, optional)**: If the job duration is known, enter the number of hours or days.
* **Job Finish Time (14, optional)**: If known, select the expected finish time of the job.
* **Job Cost (15, optional)**: If known, enter the cost associated with the job.
* **Payment Terms (16, required)**: Agree to and read the payment terms carefully.
* **Signature 2 (17, optional)**: If required, sign your second signature here.
* **Client Signature (18, required)**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement.
* **Job Start Time (19, optional)**: If known, select the estimated start time of the job.
* **Job Finish (20, optional)**: If completed, enter the finish status of the job.
* **Job Notes (21, optional)**: Add any additional notes related to the job.
* **Job Notes 2 (22, optional)**: Add any additional notes, if needed.
* **Client Name 2 (23, optional)**: Enter any additional client name, if applicable.
* **Client Number 2 (24, optional)**: Enter any additional client number.
* **Client Number (25, optional)**: Enter the client number, if known.
