# Eyelash Extension Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients for lash extension procedures. It is meant to ensure that clients are aware of the risks and benefits associated with the procedure and to provide a record of the client's consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Describe the lash extension procedure you are interested in, including the type and expected duration of the procedure, in the "Procedure Details" section.
3. Read and acknowledge the risks associated with the procedure in the "Risks" section.
4. Consider the benefits of the procedure and indicate your understanding in the "Benefits" section.
5. Indicate your consent to proceed with the procedure in the "Client Consent" section.
6. Provide your technician's contact information and name in the "Technician Information" section.
7. Fill in your salon's information in the "Salon Information" section.
8. Sign and date the form in the "Client Signature" and "Technician Signature" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (Customer Information): 
  This section is where you should enter your name and contact information, such as your email address and phone number.

* **Procedure Details** (Procedure Details): 
  In this section, please describe the type of lash extension procedure you are interested in, including the expected duration of the procedure.

* **Risks** (Risks): 
  Please read and acknowledge the risks associated with the procedure. If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your technician.

* **Benefits** (Benefits): 
  Consider the benefits of the procedure and indicate your understanding of them.

* **Client Consent** (Client Consent): 
  Indicate your consent to proceed with the procedure by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.

* **Technician Information** (Technician Information): 
  Please enter your technician's name and contact information.

* **Salon Information** (Salon Information): 
  Enter your salon's information, including its name and address.

* **Client Signature** (Client Signature): 
  Sign the form to indicate your understanding and consent to the procedure.

* **Technician Signature** (Technician Signature): 
  Sign the form to confirm that you, as the technician, have explained the procedure and risks to the client and that the client has signed the form.

* **Date** (Date): 
  Enter the date you signed the form.

* **Time** (Time): 
  Enter the time you signed the form.

* **Form Submitted By** (Form Submitted By): 
  Enter your name.

* **Form Submitted By Email** (Form Submitted By Email): 
  Enter your email address.

* **Form Submitted By Phone** (Form Submitted By Phone): 
  Enter your phone number.

* **Form Submitted By Extension** (Form Submitted By Extension): 
  This field is not currently used.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have read and understood all the sections before signing the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please discuss them with your technician.
