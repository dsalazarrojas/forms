# Fundraising Event Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and guardian information, and to obtain consent from them before allowing participation in a fundraising event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's information in the **participant_information** field.
2. Enter the guardian's information in the **guardian_information** field.
3. Indicate whether the participant has given consent to participate in the **participant_consent** field.
4. Enter any additional consent or comments from the participant in the **participant_consent_part_1**, **participant_consent_part_2**, and **participant_consent_part_3** fields.
5. Enter any comments or consent from the guardian in the **guardian_consent** field.
6. Enter any details about the event in the **event_details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_information** (text): Enter the participant's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **participant_guardian** (text): Enter the guardian's name and contact information.
* **participant_consent** (select multiple): Select 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has given consent to participate.
* **participant_consent_part_1**, **participant_consent_part_2**, **participant_consent_part_3** (text): Enter any additional comments or consent from the participant.
* **guardian_consent** (text): Enter any comments or consent from the guardian.
* **event_details** (text): Enter any details about the event, such as the date, time, location, and expected activities.

## Tips
* Make sure to read and review all fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that participants and their guardians understand the terms and conditions of the event before giving consent.
* Use this form for educational settings, such as schools, colleges, or universities, where students may participate in fundraising events.
* Review and modify this form according to your specific needs before using it for any other purpose.
