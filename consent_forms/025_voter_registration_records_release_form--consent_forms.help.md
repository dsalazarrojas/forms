# Voter Registration Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request access to voter registration records. A voter registration record is a document that contains information about a voter, such as their name, address, and voting history. This form is typically used by government agencies, election officials, or other authorized individuals to request access to these records for legitimate purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Title**: Enter a brief title for the request, e.g., "Request for Voter Registration Records".
2. **Choose a Category**: Select the category for which you are requesting access to the records. You can choose "Consent Forms" or "Other" depending on the purpose of your request.
3. **Provide a Description**: Briefly describe the reason for your request.
4. **Enter Requested by Information**: Provide your name and title (if applicable) so that the requester can contact you.
5. **Specify Request Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you are requesting access to the records.
6. **State Reason for Request**: Provide a brief explanation for the request.
7. **Verify Requester Information (if necessary)**: If you are requesting on behalf of someone else, provide their name and title (if applicable).
8. **Specify Second Request Date and Time (if necessary)**: If you are making a follow-up request, enter the date and time of the second request.
9. **Verify Reason for Second Request (if necessary)**: If this is a follow-up request, provide an updated explanation for the request.
