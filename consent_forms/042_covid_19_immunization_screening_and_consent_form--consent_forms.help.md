# COVID 19 Immunization Screening And Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about a patient's medical history, current health status, and informed consent for COVID-19 vaccination. It is used to ensure that patients are properly screened and informed before receiving the vaccine.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your patient's personal details such as name, date of birth, patient ID, phone number, and email address.
2. Answer questions about severe allergies, current illnesses, medications, and immunocompromised status.
3. Confirm your prior COVID-19 diagnosis and vaccination history.
4. Review and understand the benefits and risks of COVID-19 vaccination.
5. Provide consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (Patient Name)** (`patient_name`, text, Required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their government-issued ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth (patient_dob)** (date, Required): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Patient ID (patient_id)** (text, Required): Enter the patient's ID number or other identifying information.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)** (text, Required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address (email_address)** (email, Required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Vaccination Appointment Date (appointment_date)** (date, Required): Enter the date of the scheduled vaccination appointment.
* **Severe Allergies to Vaccine Components (severe_allergies)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you have any severe allergies to vaccine components.
* **Allergy Details (allergy_details)** (text, Optional): Provide more information about your allergies if needed.
* **Currently Ill with Fever or Infection (current_illness)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you are currently ill with fever or infection.
* **Currently Taking Medications (current_medications)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you are currently taking any medications.
* **List of Medications (medication_list)** (text, Optional): Provide a list of your current medications.
* **Immunocompromised or Weakened Immune System (immunocompromised)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you have an immunocompromised or weakened immune system.
* **Pregnant or Breastfeeding (pregnancy_status)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
* **Recent Vaccinations in Past Two Weeks (recent_vaccinations)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you have had any recent vaccinations in the past two weeks.
* **Prior COVID-19 Diagnosis (prior_covid_diagnosis)** (select_one, Required): Indicate if you have had a prior COVID-19 diagnosis.
* **Date of Diagnosis (covid_diagnosis_date)** (date, Optional): If you have had a prior COVID-19 diagnosis, enter the date of diagnosis.
* **I Understand the Benefits of COVID-19 Vaccination (benefits_understood)** (select_one, Required): Confirm that you understand the benefits of the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **I Understand Potential Risks and Side Effects (risks_understood)** (select_one, Required): Confirm that you understand potential risks and side effects of the vaccine.
* **My Questions Have Been Answered Satisfactorily (questions_answered)** (select_one, Required): Confirm that your questions have been answered satisfactorily.
* **I Consent to Receive the COVID-19 Vaccine (consent_to_vaccinate)** (select_one, Required): Confirm that you consent to receive the COVID-19 vaccine.
* **Date of Consent (consent_signature_date)** (date, Required): Enter the date of your consent.
