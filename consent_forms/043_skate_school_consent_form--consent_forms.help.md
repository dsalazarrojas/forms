# Skate School Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skate School Consent Form is a document designed to be completed by participants in skate schools or programs. This form helps in gathering essential information about the participant, including emergency contact details, medical clearance, and other crucial data. By filling out this form, participants acknowledge and consent to various terms and conditions related to the skate school program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Skate School Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Fill in the "Emergency Contact Information" section with the required details.
3. Provide any necessary information in the "Medical Clearance" section.
4. Enter any required Participant Information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Information (Emergency Contact, text, required)**: Please fill out this section with the name and contact details of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Details (Emergency Contact Details, text, required)**: Add any additional information about the emergency contact, such as phone numbers, email addresses, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Medical Clearance (Medical Clearance, text, required)**: Please provide any existing medical conditions or allergies that may affect the participant's safety while engaging in the skate school activities.
* **Participant Information (Participant Information, text, optional)**: If you have any other information about the participant that may be relevant for the skate school staff to know, please add it here.
