# covid-19 consentwaiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 consent waiver form is used to collect information from individuals who have tested positive for COVID-19 or have been in contact with someone who has. This form helps to track and manage cases of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully to ensure you provide the required information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Fill out the form completely and submit it once finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact info** (contact_info, Email, required):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **date-tested-positive** (date_tested_positive, Date, required):
	+ Enter the date you tested positive for COVID-19.
* **symptoms** (symptoms, Select Multiple, required):
	+ Check yes if you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **travel histories** (travel_histories, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Check yes if you have traveled recently.
* **exposure histories** (exposure_histories, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Check yes if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
* **consent date** (consent_date, Date, required):
	+ Enter the date you are giving consent to share your information.
* **consent time** (consent_time, Time, required):
	+ Enter the time you are giving consent to share your information.
* **assigned-tool** (assigned_tool, Select One, required):
	+ Select the tool you were assigned for tracking and managing COVID-19 cases.
* **other-tool** (other_tool, Text, optional):
	+ If you were assigned a different tool, enter its name here.

Note: If you are unsure about any field, please contact your organization's contact person for assistance.
