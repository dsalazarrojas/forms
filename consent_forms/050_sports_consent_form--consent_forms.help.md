# Sports Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Consent Form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for a child's participation in sports activities. This form is required for all children to ensure their safety and well-being during sports events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sports Consent Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the parent's name in the "parent_name" field.
2. Provide the parent's phone number in the "parent_phone" field (optional).
3. Enter the child's date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
4. Select the sport activity the child will be participating in in the "sport_activity" field.
5. Choose the consent status by selecting "yes" or "no" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the sports activity.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by the sports organization or coaches, please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sport Activity** (`sport_activity`, text, required): Select the specific sport activity your child will be participating in (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Swimming).
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, required): Choose "yes" to give consent for your child to participate in the sports activity, or "no" to decline.
