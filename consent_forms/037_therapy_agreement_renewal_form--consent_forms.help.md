# Therapy Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew and update the therapy agreement between a client and their therapist. It ensures both parties are on the same page and have a clear understanding of the therapy goals, boundaries, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and the fields below.
2. Complete the Client Information field with your name and contact information.
3. Describe your Agreement Details, including any changes or updates to your therapy goals and expectations.
4. Provide the updated information in the Updated Information field.
5. Check the Therapist Agreement field for any changes or updates from your therapist.
6. Describe your therapy goals and expectations in the Therapy Goals field.
7. Sign and date the Client Signature field to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your name and contact information to help your therapist get in touch with you.
* **Agreement Details** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe any changes or updates to your therapy goals and expectations.
* **Updated Information** (Text, Optional):
	+ Provide any new or updated information that affects your therapy.
* **Therapist Agreement** (Text, Optional):
	+ Check for any updates or changes from your therapist.
* **Therapy Goals** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your updated therapy goals and expectations.
* **Updated Agreement** (Text, Optional):
	+ Confirm that you've read and agreed to the updated terms.
* **Client Agreement** (Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your agreement to the updated terms.
* **Client Signature** (Text, Optional):
	+ Sign and date to confirm your agreement.
Note: All fields are optional, but it's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a successful therapy process.
