# Massage Service Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Service Release Form is a document that helps healthcare providers or spas to obtain informed consent from clients before providing massage services. It is used to ensure that clients are aware of potential risks and complications associated with the service, and to obtain their acknowledgment of these risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each page and ensure you understand the information and risks associated with the massage service.
2. Check the "Risks Disclosures" page to review and acknowledge the potential risks and complications.
3. Answer the "Service Providers" question truthfully, indicating whether you will be receiving services from a massage provider.
4. If applicable, complete the "Client Acknowledgement" and "Provider Acknowledgement" sections.
5. Select the checkboxes on the "Release Form" page to indicate your acknowledgment of the risks and your understanding of the provider's rights.
6. Sign and date the "Signature" and "Date" sections.
7. The "Provider Signature" and "Provider Name" sections are to be completed by the massage provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): This section is used to gather basic information about you, the client. Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Risks Disclosures** (`risk_disclosures`, select_multiple, optional): Please review and select the potential risks and complications associated with the massage service. This will help you understand what to expect and take necessary precautions.
* **Service Providers** (`service_providers`, select_one, optional): Check the box indicating whether you will be receiving services from a massage provider.
* **Client Acknowledgement** (`client_acknowledgement`, note, optional): This section is used to acknowledge that you have received and understand the risks associated with the massage service.
* **Provider Acknowledgement** (`provider_acknowledgement`, note, optional): This section is used to acknowledge that you have received and understand the provider's rights.
* **Release Form** (`release_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select the checkboxes to indicate your acknowledgment of the risks and your understanding of the provider's rights.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please sign this section to indicate that you have read and understand the information on this form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you signed this form.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, text, optional): The massage provider will sign this section to indicate their agreement to provide services.
* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, optional): The massage provider will enter their name in this section.
