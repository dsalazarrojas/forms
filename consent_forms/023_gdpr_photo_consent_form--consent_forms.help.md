# gdpr_photo_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent for the use and release of photos taken by our organization. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start at the first page.
2. Fill out all required fields on each page.
3. Review each field carefully before proceeding.
4. Submit the form when all fields are complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: This is a general information page, where you can enter any relevant details about the photos you are releasing.
  - **Photo Release**: Please confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of the photo release.
  - **Conditions for Consent**: Describe the conditions under which the photo will be used and released. This may include events, projects, or other relevant details.
  - **Lawful Processing**: Confirm that you understand how your photo will be handled and processed.
  - **Recipient Informations**: Enter your email address where the photo will be sent.
  - **Conditions for Release**: Select all applicable conditions for the release of the photo. This may include events, projects, or other relevant details.
  - **Release Date**: Enter the date the photo will be released.
  - **Signature**: Choose the option that indicates your signature.
  - **Date Signed**: Confirm the date and time you signed the form.
  - **Photo Release Confirmation**: A confirmation page to review and confirm your photo release before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- This form is designed to be completed individually. If you need to fill it out for others, please ensure that you have their authorization and knowledge.
