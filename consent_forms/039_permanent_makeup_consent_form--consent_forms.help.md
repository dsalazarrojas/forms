# Permanent Makeup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from clients for permanent makeup procedures. It is intended for use in a spa or clinic setting where clients will be receiving services such as eyeliner, eyebrow, liner, blush, lip, lip liner, microblading, or eyelid services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the services you wish to receive from the dropdown menu in the "Services Selected" field.
6. Choose a date for your service in the "Service Date" field.
7. Choose the time for your service in the "Service Time" field.
8. Enter the name of the technician performing your service in the "Technician" field.
9. Select your payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
10. Enter the amount paid for your service in the "Amount Paid" field.
11. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
12. Confirm your consent to the terms in the "Do you consent to these terms?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for any necessary information or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you during or after your service.
* **Services Selected**: Select the services you wish to receive, such as eyeliner, eyebrow, liner, etc.
* **Service Date**: Choose a date for your service from the calendar.
* **Service Time**: Choose a time for your service from the available options.
* **Technician**: Enter the name of the technician who will be performing your service.
* **Payment Method**: Select your payment method, such as cash, bank transfer, or credit card.
* **Amount Paid**: Enter the amount paid for your services.
* **Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your consent to these terms.
* **Do you consent to these terms?**: Confirm that you agree to the terms by selecting "True". 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information in all fields.
* Review the services selected carefully to ensure you are choosing the correct options.
* Double-check the date and time selected to ensure you are available.
* Select a payment method and enter the correct amount paid.
