# Utility Service Termination Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Utility Service Termination Consent Form is used to document a customer's consent to terminate a utility service. It gathers necessary information from the customer to facilitate a smooth transition of services and ensures that the customer is aware of their responsibilities during this process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you have actually asked to cancel your utility service.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. customer_name
 * **Customer Name** (text, optional): The customer's full name.
 * Please enter your full name as it appears on your account.

### 2. select_service
 * **Select Service Type** (select_multiple, optional): The type(s) of utility service being terminated.
 * Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Electric
	+ Gas
	+ Water
	+ Sewer

### 3. reason_for_cancellation
 * **Reason for Cancellation** (text, optional): A brief explanation for why the customer is terminating the service.
 * Enter a brief description of why you are cancelling your service.

### 4. account_details
 * **Account Details** (text, optional): Additional information about your account.
 * Provide any relevant account details that might be helpful for the transition process.

### 5. termination_date
 * **Termination Date** (date, optional): The date you would like to terminate the service.
 * Enter the date you would like to have the service terminated.

### 6. account_manager
 * **Account Manager** (text, optional): The name of your account manager.
 * Enter the name of the person who manages your account.

### 7. customer_signature
 * **Customer Signature** (select_one, optional): A confirmation of consent.
 * Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have read and understood the terms of termination.

### 8. account_manager_signature
 * **Account Manager Signature** (select_one, optional): A confirmation from the account manager.
 * Select 'Yes' to confirm that you have been informed and agree to the terms of termination.

### 9. date_completed
 * **Date Completed** (date, optional): The date the service was terminated.
 * This field will be automatically filled out when the service is terminated.

### 10. utility_service_terminated
 * **Utility Service Terminated** (text, optional): A confirmation of the service termination.
 * This field will be automatically filled out when the service is terminated.

### 11. notes
 * **Notes** (text, optional): Additional comments or notes about the termination process.
 * Enter any additional comments or notes about the termination process.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure that you have actually asked to cancel your utility service before filling out this form.
