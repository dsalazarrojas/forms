# Ethical Standards Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Standards Commitment Form is used to collect commitments and acknowledgements from staff members and stakeholders regarding the Ethical Standards of our organization. This form ensures that all staff and stakeholders are aware of and committed to upholding these standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, but only if required fields are marked as required (false for all fields in this form).
2. Enter your staff member name in the "Staff Member Name" field.
3. Enter the date of your acknowledgement in the "Date" field.
4. Provide your acknowledgement in the "Acknowledgement 1", "Acknowledgement 2", and "Acknowledgement 3" fields.
5. Enter your staff member signature in the "Staff Member Signature" field.
6. Enter the stakeholder name in the "Stakeholder Name" field.
7. Enter the stakeholder signature in the "Stakeholder Signature" field.
8. Enter the date of your second acknowledgement in the "Date 2" field.
9. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Acknowledgement 4" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member Name**: Enter your name as a staff member of our organization.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are acknowledging the Ethical Standards.
* **Acknowledgement 1**: Describe your understanding and commitment to uphold the Ethical Standards.
* **Acknowledgement 2**: Acknowledge any relevant policies or procedures related to the Ethical Standards.
* **Acknowledgement 3**: Acknowledge any further information or guidance related to the Ethical Standards.
* **Staff Member Signature**: Sign your name as a staff member to confirm your commitment.
* **Stakeholder Name**: Enter the name of the stakeholder you are acknowledging the Ethical Standards.
* **Stakeholder Signature**: Sign the stakeholder's name to confirm their commitment.
* **Date 2**: Enter the date of your second acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement 4**: Provide any additional comments or information related to the Ethical Standards.
