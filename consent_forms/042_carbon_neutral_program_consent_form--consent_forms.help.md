# Carbon Neutral Program Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Carbon Neutral Program Consent Form is a crucial step in participating in the Carbon Neutral Program. It collects information about participants, their carbon footprint, and their consent to data collection and handling practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the participant information section, which includes your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Next, provide the city and state, and zip code for your location.
3. If you are representing an organization or company, please enter their name.
4. Select the Carbon Neutral Program you are participating in and its start date.
5. Choose the program duration, which can be 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or ongoing.
6. Indicate which program goals you are interested in, such as reducing carbon footprint, carbon offset, sustainability education, behavioral change, or environmental impact measurement.
7. If applicable, provide your baseline emissions in metric tons of CO2e (mtCO2e).
8. Review and agree to the informed consent section, ensuring you understand the program's objectives and requirements.
9. Confirm your agreement to data collection for carbon tracking purposes.
10. Consent to the program's privacy policy and data handling practices.
11. Understand that participation is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time.
12. If applicable, authorize carbon offset on your behalf.
13. Agree to monitoring and reporting of your carbon activities.
14. Lastly, confirm your signature by selecting "Yes, I Consent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Street address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address for location tracking.
* **City, state, zip code** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and zip code for location tracking.
* **Organization or company (if applicable)** (`organization`, text, optional): If representing an organization or company, enter their name.
* **Carbon Neutral Program name** (`program_name`, text, required): Select the Carbon Neutral Program you are participating in.
* **Program start date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program duration** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the program duration from the available options (3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years, or ongoing).
* **Program goals** (`program_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select all program goals that apply to you, such as reducing carbon footprint, carbon offset, sustainability education, behavioral change, or environmental impact measurement.
* **Baseline emissions** (`baseline_emissions`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your baseline emissions in metric tons of CO2e (mtCO2e).
* **Informed consent** (`informed_consent`, select_one, required): Review and agree to the informed consent section, ensuring you understand the program's objectives and requirements.
* **Data usage agreement** (`data_usage_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to data collection for carbon tracking purposes.
* **Privacy consent** (`privacy_consent`, select_one, required): Consent to the program's privacy policy and data handling practices.
* **Voluntary participation** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required): Understand that participation is voluntary and you can withdraw at any time.
* **Carbon offset consent** (`carbon_offset_consent`, select_one, optional): If applicable, authorize carbon offset on your behalf.
* **Monitoring agreement** (`monitoring_agreement`, select_one, required): Agree to monitoring and reporting of your carbon activities.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, note, optional): If you wish to provide an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you provided an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): If you provided an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Signature confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your signature by selecting "Yes, I Consent".
