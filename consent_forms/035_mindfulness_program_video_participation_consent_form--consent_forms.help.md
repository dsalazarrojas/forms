# Mindfulness Program Video Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from participants to record their video for the Mindfulness Program. Participants must provide their name, email address, preferred method of contact (email or phone), and their consent to participate in video recordings. This form also allows participants to provide any additional comments or questions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field. This is the name of the person participating in the Mindfulness Program.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is the contact information we will use to reach you.
3. Select your preferred method of contact in the "contact_method" field. You can choose to be contacted by email or phone.
4. If you select "other (specify)" as your method of contact, please specify your preferred method in the "other_contact_method" field.
5. Indicate your consent to participate in video recordings in the "video_consent" field. You can choose to be recorded or not.
6. If you have any comments or questions, you can provide them in the "participant_comments" field.
7. If you have any comments or questions for the instructor, you can provide them in the "instructor_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it should be listed in the program materials.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **video_consent** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you consent to participate in video recordings or not.
* **contact_method** (select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred method of contact (email or phone).
* **other_contact_method** (text, required/optional): If you selected "other (specify)" as your method of contact, enter your preferred method here.
* **participant_comments** (text, required/optional): Provide any comments or questions you may have about the program.
* **instructor_comments** (text, required/optional): Provide any comments or questions you may have for the instructor.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your correct email address so that we can reach you with updates and information.
* If you select "other (specify)" as your method of contact, please make sure to specify your preferred method in the "other_contact_method" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact us.
