# Franchise Tax Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request an extension of the franchise tax period for an entity. It helps in tracking and maintaining the status of such requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Filing Date when the original return was due or the date you're requesting an extension.
2. Enter the Franchise Tax Year for which you're requesting the extension.
3. Provide a brief Reason for Extension, describing why you need more time.
4. Enter a valid Tax Extension Date when the extended tax return will be filed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Entity Identifier
**Entity Identifier** (`Entity Identifier`, `text`, **Required**: true)
Enter your entity's valid identifier as required by the tax authority.

### Filing Date
**Filing Date** (`Filing Date`, `date`, **Required**: true)
Enter the date when the original return was due. This date is used to track the request's status.

### Franchise Tax Year
**Franchise Tax Year** (`Franchise Tax Year`, `number`, **Required**: true)
Enter the tax year for which you're requesting the extension.

### Reason for Extension
**Reason for Extension** (`Reason for Extension`, `text`, **Required**: true)
Provide a brief description of why you're requesting more time for your tax return.

### Tax Extension Date
**Tax Extension Date** (`Tax Extension Date`, `time`, **Required**: true)
Enter a valid date when the extended tax return will be filed.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and valid information in each field.
- Review and submit the form only once you have filled out all required fields.
- If you have any issues or need help, contact your tax authority for assistance.
