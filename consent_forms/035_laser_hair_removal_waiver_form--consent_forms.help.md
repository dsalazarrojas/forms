# Laser Hair Removal Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laser Hair Removal Waiver Form is a critical document that ensures a safe and effective treatment experience for both you and your practitioner. This form gathers essential information about your medical history, contact methods, and consent to proceed with the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: In the "Client Name" field, please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Enter your email and phone number**: In the "Email" and "Phone" fields, please enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. **Select your contact method**: In the "Contact Method" field, please choose how you prefer to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-Person).
4. **Provide medical history**: In the "Medical History" field, please list any medical conditions or allergies you have that may impact your treatment.
5. **Provide allergy information**: In the "Allergy Information" field, please list any allergies or medical conditions that may affect your treatment.
6. **Consent**: In the "Consent" field, please indicate whether you agree to undergo the laser hair removal treatment. Select "Yes" to proceed with the treatment.
7. **Acknowledge risks**: In the "Risk Acknowledgement" field, please indicate whether you understand and acknowledge the potential risks associated with the treatment. Select "Yes" to acknowledge the risks.
8. **Sign and date**: In the "Signature Date" and "Signature Time" fields, please sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
9. **Select your laser technician**: In the "Laser Technician" field, please indicate if you have a preferred technician or if you are open to being assigned one.
10. **Verify technician's license**: In the "Technician License" field, please confirm that the laser technician has the necessary qualifications and certifications.
11. **Select the procedure**: In the "Procedure" field, please indicate whether you will be undergoing a laser hair removal treatment.
12. **Provide procedure information**: In the "Procedure Information" field, please provide any additional information related to your treatment.
13. **Specify the number of sessions**: In the "Number of Sessions" field, please indicate the number of sessions you will need to achieve your desired results.
14. **Add notes**: In the "Notes" field, please provide any additional information that may be helpful to your practitioner.
15. **Review and sign**: Finally, please review the form and sign it to confirm your consent and acknowledge the risks.
