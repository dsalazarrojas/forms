# Minor Consent For Body Art - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Consent For Body Art form is used to obtain consent from minors who are undergoing body art procedures, such as tattoos, piercings, or other body modifications. This form is designed to ensure that minors understand the risks and benefits of the procedure and that their parents or guardians provide consent for the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of consent (e.g. "Consent Forms").
2. Provide a description of the procedure (e.g. "tattoo" or "piercing").
3. Enter the minor's name.
4. Enter the minor's date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter the minor's parent's name.
6. Enter the minor's parent's address.
7. Provide the minor's purpose for the procedure.
8. Check the minor's parental consent box.
9. Check the minor's signature box.
10. Check the parent's signature box.
11. Record the date and time of submission.
12. Sign the form as the artist.
13. Record the studio name (if applicable).
14. Record the form version.
