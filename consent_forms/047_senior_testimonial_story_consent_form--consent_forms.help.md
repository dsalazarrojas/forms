# Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form is designed to collect the consent of seniors and their families for their story to be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as instructed.
2. Review and understand each field's purpose before proceeding.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Senior Testimonial Story Consent Form** (`senior_testimonial_story_consent_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which can be left blank.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you are providing consent for your story to be shared.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you are providing consent for your story to be shared.
* **Senior Story** (`senior_story`, text, optional): Share your senior's story.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options:
	+ Option 1: Yes, I consent for my story to be shared.
	+ Option 2: No, I do not consent for my story to be shared.
	+ Option 3: I need to discuss with my family member first.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to make about your consent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are authorized to access and use this form before proceeding.
* If you have any doubts, please reach out to the relevant authorities.
* If you have any issues while filling out this form, please contact the support team for assistance.
