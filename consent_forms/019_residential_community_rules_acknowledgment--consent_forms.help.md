# Residential Community Rules Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that verifies a resident's understanding of the community's rules, ensuring they are aware of their responsibilities and the community's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your property address for our records.
3. Provide your contact number and email address so we can reach you if needed.
4. If applicable, enter your confirmation date, which is usually the date you moved in or started living in the community.
5. Please sign your name in the box marked as "Signature of Resident" to confirm that you have read and understood the rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Name** (1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Property Address** (2): Enter your property's address for our records.
* **Contact Number** (3): Enter your contact number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Email Address** (4): Enter your email address for us to contact you if required.
* **Confirmation Date** (5): Enter the date you moved in or started living in the community, if applicable.
* **Have you read and understood the rules?** (7): Please select "Yes" if you have read and understood the community's rules. If not, you can select "No" and we will follow up with a rules briefing.
* **I acknowledge that I have read and understood the rules** (8): Please select "Agreed" if you confirm that you have read and understood the rules.
* **Signature of Resident** (9): Sign your name here to confirm that you have read and understood the rules.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you select "No" to the "Have you read and understood the rules?" question, please be prepared for a follow-up discussion about the community's rules.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the rules or this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to the community management.
