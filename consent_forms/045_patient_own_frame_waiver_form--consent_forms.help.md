# Patient Own Frame Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document patient-owned frame waivers for medical procedures that involve a patient's personal equipment or frame. It collects essential information from the patient to ensure clear and accurate documentation of consent and liability waivers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Own Frame Waiver Form** - This form serves as the main page.
2. **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name in the space provided.
3. **Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
4. **Phone**: Provide the patient's phone number for communication.
5. **Address**: Enter the patient's address.
6. **City**: Enter the city where the patient resides.
7. **State**: Select the state where the patient resides from the available options.
8. **ZIP**:
	* Select 'ZIP' if you have a postal zip code.
	* Select 'Other' if you do not have a standard zip code (e.g., PO box, international address, etc.).
9. **Date**: Enter the date of the medical procedure or examination.
10. **Time**: Enter the time of the medical procedure or examination.
11. **Note** (optional): Add any additional comments or information about the patient or procedure (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **patient_name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
- **email** (Email, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
- **phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number for communication.
- **address** (Address, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
- **city** (City, text, optional): Enter the city where the patient resides.
- **state** (State, select_one, required): Select the state where the patient resides.
- **zip** (ZIP, select_multiple, required): Select 'ZIP' if you have a standard postal zip code; otherwise, select 'Other' if you have a non-standard address (e.g., PO box, international address).
- **date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the medical procedure or examination.
- **time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the medical procedure or examination.
- **note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the patient or procedure.
