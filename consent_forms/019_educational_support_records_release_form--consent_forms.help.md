# Educational Support Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Support Records Release Form is used to obtain consent for the release of educational records from an institution. This form is used to ensure that records are accurately and lawfully transferred between institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Complete the fields for your name and institution name.
2. Select the type of records you are requesting to be released.
3. Choose the record retention period for the release of records.
4. Provide the date for the release of records.
5. Check the box indicating your consent to release the records.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
7. Have an authorized representative of the institution sign and date the form as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official record.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official record.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution providing the records.
* **Department**: Enter the department within the institution that is providing the records.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID if applicable.
* **Record Type**: Select the type of records you are requesting to be released (e.g. Record of academic performance, Record of attendance and participation).
* **Records Release**: Select the types of records you are requesting to be released.
* **Record Retention**: Choose the period for which records are to be released.
* **Release Date**: Enter the date on which records are to be released.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the release of records.
* **Consent**: Check the box indicating you have read and agreed to the terms of the record release.
* **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Date**: Enter the date you sign the form.
* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of the institution providing the records.
* **Department**: Enter the department within the institution that is providing the records.
* **Position**: Enter the position within the institution that is providing the records.
* **Email**: Enter the contact information for the institution.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact information for the institution.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the institution.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the institution.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or instructions from the institution.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have obtained the necessary consent and permissions for the release of records.
* Verify that the records are accurate and up-to-date before release.
* Keep a record of the form for your records.
