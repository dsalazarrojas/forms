# Online Training Disclaimer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online training disclaimer form is used to gather information and consent related to online training activities. This form helps users confirm their participation and agreement to the terms and conditions of the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the terms and conditions carefully.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for Terms and Conditions.
3. If you selected "No" for Terms and Conditions, explain why in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for Customer Agreement.
5. If you selected "No" for Customer Agreement, explain why in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
6. Enter your customer email address.
7. Enter your customer phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Terms and Conditions (2)** (`terms_and_conditions`, optional): Please select "Yes" if you agree to the terms and conditions. If you select "No", please provide an explanation in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
* **Customer Information (3)** (`customer_information`, optional): Please enter your customer information, including name, address, and other relevant details. 
* **Online Training Participation (4)** (`online_training_participation`, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm your online training participation. If you select "No", please explain why in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
* **Online Training Participation Other (5)** (`online_training_participation_other`, optional): If you selected "No" for Online Training Participation, please provide a detailed explanation in this field.
* **Customer Agreement (6)** (`customer_agreement`, optional): Select "Yes" to agree to the customer agreement. If you select "No", please explain why in the "Other (If applicable)" field.
* **Customer Agreement Other (7)** (`customer_agreement_other`, optional): If you selected "No" for Customer Agreement, please provide a detailed explanation in this field.
* **Customer Agreement Other Description (8)** (`customer_agreement_other_description`, optional): Please describe your reason for not agreeing to the customer agreement.
* **Customer Email (9)** (`customer_email`, optional): Enter your customer email address.
* **Customer Phone (10)** (`customer_phone`, optional): Enter your customer phone number.
