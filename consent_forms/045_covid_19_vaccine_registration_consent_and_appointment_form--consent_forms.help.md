# COVID 19 Vaccine Registration, Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vital information and consent from patients for COVID-19 vaccination. It covers various aspects of the patient's medical history, vaccine preferences, and appointment scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information Section**: This section requires your basic information such as name, email, phone number, date of birth, and address.
	* **Patient Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for vaccination appointment reminders and updates.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for vaccination appointments and questions.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
	* **Street Address**: Enter your residential address where you can be reached for vaccination appointments.
	* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
	* **State**: Enter your state or province where you reside.
	* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code or postal code where you reside.
2. **Insurance Information Section**: This section requires your insurance status and provider details.
	* **Insurance Status**: Choose your insurance status from the options provided.
	* **Note**: If you have any insurance provider details or specific requirements, please mention them here.
3. **Appointment Section**: This section requires your preferred appointment date and time.
	* **Preferred Appointment Date**: Choose a date for your vaccination appointment from the calendar provided.
	* **Preferred Appointment Time**: Choose a time slot for your vaccination appointment.
4. **Dose and Vaccine Preference Section**: This section requires information about the vaccine dose and preference.
	* **Dose Number**: Choose the number of vaccine dose you require.
	* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Choose your preferred vaccine type from the options provided.
5. **Consent and Informed Section**: This section requires your understanding of the vaccine benefits, risks, and side effects.
	* **I received and understood vaccine information**: Check if you have received and understood vaccine information.
	* **I understand the benefits of vaccination**: Check if you understand the benefits of vaccination.
	* **I understand the risks and side effects**: Check if you understand the risks and potential side effects of vaccination.
6. **Medical History Section**: This section requires your medical history disclosure.
	* **I have disclosed my complete medical history**: Check if you have disclosed your medical history accurately.
7. **Authorization and Emergency Section**: This section requires your authorization and emergency contact details.
	* **I authorize this facility to administer the vaccine**: Check if you authorize the facility to administer the vaccine.
	* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter your emergency contact name who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact phone number.
8. **Consent Date Section**: This section requires your consent date.
	* **Consent Date**: Date when you provided your consent for vaccination.
