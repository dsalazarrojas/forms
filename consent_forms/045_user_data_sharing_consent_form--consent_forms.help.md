# user_data_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **User Data Sharing Consent Form** is used to collect user data sharing consent information for internal business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to sharing your data with third-party services.
2.  Enter your email address as it appears in your records or the email address where you would like to receive communication from us.
3.  Enter your full name as it appears on your records or the name you prefer to be known by.
4.  Enter your date of birth in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
5.  Enter your phone number as it appears in your records or the number where you can be reached.
6.  Enter your Social Security Number as it appears in your records or the number used to identify you in our system.
7.  Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your consent to the sharing of your data.
8.  Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Data Sharing Consent**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to choose whether or not you wish your data to be shared with third-party services.
    \*\* Select "Yes" to consent to data sharing and "No" if you do not.
*   **Email**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter your email address as it appears in your records or the email address where you would like to receive communication from us.
*   **Name**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter your full name as it appears in your records or the name you prefer to be known by.
*   **Date of Birth**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter your date of birth in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
*   **Phone**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter your phone number as it appears in your records or the number where you can be reached.
*   **Social Security Number**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter your Social Security Number as it appears in your records or the number used to identify you in our system.
*   **Consent**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to confirm your consent to the sharing of your data with third-party services.
    \*\* Select "Yes" to consent to data sharing and "No" if you do not.
*   **Notes**: 
    \*\* This option allows you to enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide in the space provided.
*   **File Upload**: 
    \*\* This option is currently not used.
