# Virtual Care System Usability Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture user consent for the use of a virtual care system, which offers telehealth services, electronic health records, and other features. The form is used to ensure that users understand and agree to the terms of use, including data sharing, retention, security, and access policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out each section**:
   - **User Information**: Provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details as required.
   - **User Consent**: Check if you have reviewed and understand the form.
   - **Patient Information**: Enter your patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details as required.
   - **Patient Consent**: Check if you have reviewed and understand the form for your patient.
   - **Telehealth Services**: Select whether you consent to telehealth services, such as phone, video, or messaging.
   - **Electronic Health Records**: Select whether you consent to electronic health records, such as EHRs or PHRs.
   - **Payment Information**: Enter any relevant payment information as required.
   - **Payment Consent**: Check if you have reviewed and understand the form for payment purposes.
   - **Data Sharing**: Select the entities with whom you agree to share data (Insurance companies, Researchers, or Other).
   - **Data Sharing Consent**: Check if you consent to data sharing.
   - **Data Retention**: Provide the expected duration of data retention for your patient's data.
   - **Security and Privacy**: Check if you have reviewed and understand the security and privacy policies.
   - **Data Access**: Select who should have access to your patient's data (Patient, Provider, or System updates).
   - **Data Access Consent**: Check if you have reviewed and understand data access.
   - **Technical Issues**: Report any technical issues you experience.
   - **Technical Issues 2**: Report any additional technical issues.
