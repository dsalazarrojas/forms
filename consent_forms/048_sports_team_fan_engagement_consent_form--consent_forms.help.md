# Sports Team Fan Engagement Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect contact information and consent from sports team fans for engagement purposes. This allows the team to stay in touch with their fans, share updates, and offer promotions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all necessary fields with accurate and up-to-date information about the fan's contact details and preferences.
2. Select the desired marketing and communication preferences for the fan to receive updates.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments if applicable.

*   **Contact Details**:
    *   <name>fan_contact_details</name>
    *   <type>Text field for the fan to input their contact details</type>
    *   This field is optional and used to capture the fan's basic contact information.
*   **Marketing Preferences**:
    *   <name>marketing_preferences</name>
    *   <type>Select multiple options for the fan to choose their marketing preferences</type>
    *   This field offers a dropdown menu with options for the fan to choose their preferred marketing channels and frequency.
*   **Consent to Contact**:
    *   <name>consent_to_contact</name>
    *   <type>Select one option for the fan to give consent for contact</type>
    *   This field allows the fan to choose how they would like to be contacted by the team, such as by phone, email, or mail.
*   **Email Address**:
    *   <name>email_address</name>
    *   <type>Email field for the fan to input their email address</type>
    *   This field is used to capture the fan's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number**:
    *   <name>phone_number</name>
    *   <type>Text field for the fan to input their phone number</type>
    *   This field is used to capture the fan's phone number for communication purposes.
*   **Communication Preferences**:

    *   This field is used to capture the fan's preferred communication date for regular updates and promotions.
*   **Time Preference**:
    *   <name>time_preference</name>
    *   <type>Time field for the fan to select their preferred time of communication</type>
    *   This field is used to capture the fan's preferred time for receiving updates and promotions.
*   **Notes**:
    *   <name>notes</name>
    *   <type>Note field for the fan to add any additional comments or notes</type>
    *   This field is used to capture any additional comments or notes from the fan.

## Tips

*   Be sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
*   Double-check the entered information for accuracy before submission.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the team's officials.
