# Coast Guard Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for the Coast Guard to obtain medical clearance for applicants before they can participate in certain activities or programs. It gathers essential information about the applicant's medical history, health conditions, and any necessary clearances or authorizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your full name, date of birth, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. List any previous surgeries or hospitalizations in the "Previous Surgeries or Hospitalizations" field.
4. Provide any ongoing medical conditions, current medications, and known drug allergies in the respective fields.
5. Indicate if you have any vision correction requirements and any hearing issues.
6. Fill out your height and weight (in inches and pounds) in the "Height and Weight" field.
7. Answer medical questionnaire questions regarding heart disease, respiratory disease, mental health, and substance abuse.
8. Provide information about your examining physician's name, evaluation date, and medical clearance status.
9. Finally, confirm your consent to release medical information and sign the form with your clearance date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Applicant ID Number** (`applicant_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your assigned or ID number.
- **Previous Surgeries or Hospitalizations** (`previous_surgeries`, text, optional): List any significant procedures you've undergone or hospitalizations you've experienced.
- **Chronic Conditions** (`chronic_conditions`, text, optional): List any ongoing health conditions you have.
- **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List all medications you're currently taking with their respective dosages.
- **Medication Allergies** (`medication_allergies`, text, required): List any known drug allergies.
- **Vision Correction Required** (`vision_correction`, select_one, required): Indicate if you require vision correction.
- **Any Hearing Issues** (`hearing_issues`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any hearing issues.
- **Height and Weight** (`height_weight`, text, optional): Enter your height in inches and weight in pounds.
- **Heart Disease** (`heart_disease`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a history of heart disease.
- **Respiratory Disease** (`respiratory_disease`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a history of respiratory disease.
- **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have received mental health or psychological treatment.
- **Substance Abuse** (`substance_abuse`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have a history of substance abuse.
- **Examining Physician Name** (`physician_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your examining physician.
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your medical evaluation.
- **Physician Medical Clearance** (`physician_clearance`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of your medical clearance (Cleared, Cleared with restrictions, Not cleared, or Pending further evaluation).
- **Physician Notes or Recommendations** (`physician_notes`, text, optional): Enter any medical concerns or recommendations from your physician.
- **Consent and Authorization** (`consent_release`, select_one, required): Confirm your authorization to release medical information (I Consent, I Do Not Consent).
- **Clearance Date** (`clearance_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your medical clearance.
