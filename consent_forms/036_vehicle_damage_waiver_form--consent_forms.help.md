# vehicle-damage-waiver-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and obtain consent for vehicle repairs or maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle owner's name in the "vehicle owner" field.
2. Enter the year of your vehicle in the "vehicle year" field.
3. Specify the make of your vehicle in the "vehicle make" field.
4. Enter the model of your vehicle in the "vehicle model" field.
5. Provide the vehicle's identification number in the "vehicle identification number" field.
6. If your vehicle is insured, provide the insurance company's name in the "vehicle insurance company" field and the policy number in the "vehicle insurance policy number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle damage waiver form**: This is the title of the form.
* **vehicle owner**: Enter the name of the vehicle's owner or driver.
* **vehicle year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **vehicle make**: Specify the make of your vehicle (e.g. Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **vehicle model**: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g. Corolla, Mustang, Accord).
* **vehicle identification number**: This is the unique identifier for your vehicle.
* **vehicle insurance company**: If your vehicle is insured, enter the name of the insurance company (e.g. State Farm, Geico, Progressive).
* **vehicle insurance policy number**: If your vehicle is insured, enter the policy number provided by the insurance company.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure that your vehicle receives the necessary repairs or maintenance.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* This form can be used for multiple vehicles, but please do not complete it multiple times for the same vehicle.
