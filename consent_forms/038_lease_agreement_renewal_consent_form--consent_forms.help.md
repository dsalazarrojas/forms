# lease_agreement_renewal_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for landlords and tenants to provide their consent and details for lease agreement renewal. It helps in documenting the renewal process, ensuring that all parties are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully and understand the purpose of each field.
* Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, providing all requested information.
* For fields marked as required, ensure you provide the requested information.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the landlord or tenant who initiated the renewal process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Landlord Details** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter the name of the landlord who is responsible for the property.

*   This field is for the landlord's name, which helps identify the person involved in the lease agreement renewal.

### 2. **Tenant Details** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter the name of the tenant who is occupying the property.

*   This field is for the tenant's name, which helps identify the person involved in the lease agreement renewal.

### 3. **Lease Terms** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): 
Select the lease term duration (6 months, 12 months, or 24 months) that applies to the renewal agreement.

*   This field lets you choose the term length of the renewed lease, ensuring clarity on the renewal conditions.

### 4. **Renewal Date** (`date`, required/optional): 
Enter the date when the lease will be renewed.

*   This field is for the date on which the lease agreement will be renewed, ensuring all parties know when the new terms take effect.

### 5. **Landlord Signature** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter your signature as the landlord, confirming your consent for the lease agreement renewal.

*   This field requires your digital signature as the landlord, affirming your agreement to the renewal terms.

### 6. **Tenant Signature** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter your signature as the tenant, confirming your consent for the lease agreement renewal.

*   This field requires your digital signature as the tenant, confirming your agreement to the renewal terms.

### 7. **Lease Start Date** (`date`, required/optional): 
Enter the start date of the lease.

*   This field is for the date when the original lease began, helping to establish the renewal context.

### 8. **Lease End Date** (`date`, required/optional): 
Enter the end date of the lease.

*   This field is for the date when the original lease was set to end, establishing the renewal period.

### 9. **Property Location** (`text`, required/optional): 
Enter the address of the property.

*   This field is for the location of the property, ensuring clear identification of the rental unit.

### 10. **Lease Terms Notes** (`text`, required/optional): 
Add any additional notes or terms that are relevant to the lease agreement renewal.

*   This field is for any additional terms or conditions that need to be addressed in the renewal agreement.

### 11. **Tenant Authorized Agents** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): 
Select any authorized agents for the tenant.

*   This field lets you choose which agents the tenant is authorized to act on their behalf, ensuring clear communication channels are established.
