# Safari Tour Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants and operators involved in the Safari Tour, ensuring that all necessary consents and safety guidelines are met.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Safari Tour Consent Form** field with the name of the tour or activity you are undertaking.
2. Enter your **Tour Operator Name** in the respective field, if applicable.
3. Provide your **Participant Name** in the field provided.
4. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field, if required.
5. Enter the start date of the tour in the **Safari Tour Start Date** field.
6. Enter the end date of the tour in the **Safari Tour End Date** field.
7. Read and agree to the **Participant Safety Guidelines**.
8. Provide the **Emergency Contact Name**.
9. Enter the **Emergency Contact Phone Number**.
10. Enter the **Emergency Contact Email**.
11. Provide your **Informed Consent**.
12. Add any relevant **Safari Tour Notes**, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safari Tour Consent Form**: Enter the name of the tour or activity you are undertaking.
* **Tour Operator Name**: Enter the name of the tour operator, if applicable.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the tour.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth, if required.
* **Safari Tour Start Date**: Enter the start date of the tour.
* **Safari Tour End Date**: Enter the end date of the tour.
* **Participant Safety Guidelines**: Read and agree to the safety guidelines for the tour.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Informed Consent**: Provide your informed consent for the tour.
* **Safari Tour Notes**: Add any relevant notes about the tour, if required.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the tour operator or the Safari Tour team for clarification.
* Please review the participant safety guidelines carefully before proceeding with the tour.
