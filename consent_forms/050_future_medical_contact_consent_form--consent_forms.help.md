# future_medical_contact_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient contact information and consent for future medical contacts. It is used to ensure that patients have a clear understanding of how their information will be used and shared in the event of medical emergencies or other situations requiring contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and make sure to complete it accurately.
2. Answer every question that applies to you.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to collect the patient's consent for future medical contacts. You must agree or disagree with the terms of use of your contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address or other contact information for the healthcare provider to use in case of an emergency or for sending medical updates.
* **Relationship with Patient** (`contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to define your relationship with the patient. Select the option that best describes your connection, such as Contact as Parent, Contact as Spouse, or Contact as Sibling.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it is to be used for contact purposes, or that of the patient if different from yours.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number or the patient's phone number, if different from yours.
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the medical providers you have visited or are currently seeing for this patient. This may include Primary Care Physicians or Specialists.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address or the patient's address, if different from yours.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required: false): This field is used to define whether you are an emergency contact or not for the patient. Select 'True' if you are an emergency contact or 'False' if not.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's address, if different from yours.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's phone number, if different from yours.

## Tips
Please complete this form carefully and accurately. If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
