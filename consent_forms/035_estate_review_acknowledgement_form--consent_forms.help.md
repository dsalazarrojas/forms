# Estate Review Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Review Acknowledgement Form is used to capture the confirmation from team leads, estate administrators, and office staff that they have reviewed and acknowledged the estate in question.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your estate address in the "Estate Address" field.
3. Confirm your first name in the "Confirm First Name" field.
4. Confirm your last name in the "Confirm Last Name" field.
5. Confirm your estate address in the "Confirm Estate Address" field.
6. Select the Team Lead who confirmed the review in the "Team Lead Confirmation" field.
7. Select all relevant Estate Administrators who confirmed the review in the "Estate Admin Confirmation" field.
8. Select all relevant Offices that confirmed the review in the "Office Confirmation" field.
9. Enter the name of the person who confirmed the review in the "Confirmed by" field.
10. Enter the date of the confirmation in the "Date of Signature" field.
11. Enter the time of the confirmation in the "Time of Signature" field.
12. Enter a signature in the "Signature" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
14. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
15. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field (optional).
16. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field (optional).
17. Enter a comment on whether the review was confirmed or not in the "Comment" field.
18. Enter any additional comments from the team lead in the "Team Lead Comment" field.
19. Enter any additional comments from the estate administrator in the "Estate Admin Comment" field.
20. Enter any additional comments from the office in the "Office Comment" field.
21. Confirm the review in the "Confirm Date" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on the estate documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on the estate documents.
* **Estate Address** (`estate_address`, required): Enter the address of the estate you are reviewing.
* **Confirm First Name** (`confirm_first_name`, required): Confirm your first name.
* **Confirm Last Name** (`confirm_last_name`, required): Confirm your last name.
* **Confirm Estate Address** (`confirm_estate_address`, required): Confirm your estate address.
* **Date of Confirmation** (`confirm_signature_date`, required): Enter the date of the confirmation.
* **Time of Confirmation** (`confirm_signature_time`, required): Enter the time of the confirmation.
* **Team Lead Confirmation** (`team_lead_confirmation`, required): Select the team lead who confirmed the review.
* **Estate Admin Confirmation** (`estate_admin_confirmation`, required): Select all relevant estate administrators who confirmed the review.
* **Office Confirmation** (`office_confirmation`, required): Select all relevant offices that confirmed the review.
* **Confirmed by** (`team_confirmed`, required): Enter the name of the person who confirmed the review.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, required): Enter the date of the signature.
* **Time of Signature** (`signature_time`, required): Enter the time of the signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, required): Enter a signature.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, optional): Confirm your email address (optional).
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, optional): Confirm your phone number (optional).
* **Comment** (`confirm_comment`, required): Enter a comment on whether the review was confirmed or not.
* **Other (please specify)** (`confirm_comment_other`, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Team Lead Comment** (`team_lead_comment`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the team lead.
* **Estate Admin Comment** (`estate_admin_comment`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the estate administrator.
* **Office Comment** (`office_comment`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the office.
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirm_date`, optional): Confirm the review (optional).
