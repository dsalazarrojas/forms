# intellectual property release form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Intellectual Property Release Form is used to gather information from individuals who are creating intellectual property for the organization. This form helps to clarify ownership and usage rights for all parties involved in the project.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it is currently displayed on your official ID or business card.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your address, city, and state.
4. Enter your zip code.
5. Select the type of work you are releasing (e.g., Intellectual Property, Creative Work, Literary Work, Published Work).
6. Briefly describe the work you are releasing.
7. Confirm that you understand the organization's rights to the released work.
8. Confirm that you understand your own rights to the released work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intellectual Property Release Form**: This is the title of the form.
*   **Name**: Enter your name as it is displayed on your official ID or business card.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Address**: Provide your physical address.
*   **City**: Provide your city.
*   **State**: Provide your state.
*   **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
*   **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
*   **Type of Work**: Select the type of work you are releasing (e.g., Intellectual Property, Creative Work, Literary Work, Published Work).
*   **Description**: Briefly describe the work you are releasing.
*   **Our Rights**: Confirm that you understand the organization's rights to the released work.
*   **Your Rights**: Confirm that you understand your own rights to the released work.
*   **Agreement**: This field is for any additional comments or agreements related to the release of the work.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the organization.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
