# marshere_dance_studio_social_distancing_terms_of_service_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Terms of Service agreement for users participating in social distancing programs at Marshere Dance Studio. It collects essential contact information, emergency contact details, and user consent to the studio's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to select one of the options for each field that best matches your situation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`1`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, and other contact details as requested.
- **Contact Details** (`2`, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information that may be helpful for us to reach you.
- **Emergency Contact** (`3`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of a trusted emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`4`, text, optional): Explain how you know this emergency contact person (e.g., friend, family member, etc.).
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`5`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person (if applicable).
- **Emergency Contact Consent** (`6`, select_one, optional): Select the level of consent you are willing to grant to the studio (First, Second, or Third).
- **Medical Emergency** (`7`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of emergency contact involvement in case of a medical emergency (First, Second, or Third).
- **Emergency Contact Message** (`8`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions for the emergency contact.
- **Terms of Service** (`9`, select_one, optional): Acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the studio's Terms of Service (First, Second, or Third).
- **Emergency Contact Message 2** (`10`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions regarding the Terms of Service.

## Tips
- Please note that some fields are optional, but we appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
