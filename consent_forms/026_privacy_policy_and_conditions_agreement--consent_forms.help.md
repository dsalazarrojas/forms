# Privacy Policy And Conditions Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user agreement and consent on privacy policy and conditions related to a specific project or organization. It is meant to be a straightforward way to ensure users understand and agree to the terms of use before accessing certain services or resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the "Privacy Policy" and "User Conditions" sections, ensuring you understand their contents.
2. If you agree to the terms, sign in the "Signature" field.
3. Provide your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. (Optional) Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Privacy Policy** (text, optional): Please write a statement indicating your understanding of the project's or organization's privacy policy.
* **User Conditions** (text, optional): Please write a statement indicating the conditions under which you agree to abide by.
* **Signature** (note, required): Please sign your name as a digital confirmation of your agreement.
* **Name** (text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
