# medical-marijuana-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients about their medical history, conditions, and consent to use medical marijuana for treatment. It is essential for medical professionals to assess the patient's eligibility and suitability for medical marijuana treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details in the first page of the form.
2. Indicate medical conditions: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the patient has any of the listed medical conditions.
3. Provide doctor information: Enter the name and contact details of the patient's doctor in the third page of the form.
4. Share medical history: Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history in the fourth page of the form.
5. Give consent: Select all the relevant options to indicate the patient's consent for medical marijuana treatment.
6. Sign the form: Provide your signature as the patient or representative in the final page of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has any of the listed medical conditions, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Doctor Info** (`doctor_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of the patient's doctor.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed account of the patient's medical history, including previous treatments, allergies, and current medications.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the relevant options to indicate the patient's consent for medical marijuana treatment.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your signature as the patient or representative, confirming that you have read and understood the form's instructions.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood the form's instructions and explanations before filling it out.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please ask your doctor or medical professional for clarification.
- This form is only for patients who have not been previously evaluated or treated for medical marijuana.
