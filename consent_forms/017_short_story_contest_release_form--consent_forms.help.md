# short_story_contest_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect entry information and participant details for short story contests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form sections for "Entry Information", "Participant Information", "Permissions and Signatures", and "Date" according to the specific instructions and guidelines provided by the contest organizers.
*   Review each section carefully and make sure to provide accurate and truthful information.
*   Make sure to ask for a "yes" or "no" answer to the permission questions, as they are select_one fields.
*   Enter your email address correctly in the "Participant Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number correctly in the "Participant Phone" field.
*   If applicable, provide the guardian information in the "Guardian Information" field.
*   Indicate the guardian's permission in the "Guardian Permission" field.
*   Sign with your name or print your name for the "Participant Signature", "Parent Signature", and "Entry Signature" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Entry Information**: This is the first section of the form where you enter your information about the contest entry.
    *   **Entry Information** (`entry_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your information about the contest entry. This field is optional.
*   **Participant Information**: This section is for you to provide your details.
    *   **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your details in this field.
*   **Permission**: This field is for you to indicate your permission to participate in the contest.
    *   **Permission** (`permission`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you give permission to participate in the contest. Select "No" otherwise.
*   **Participant Email**: This is the email address you provide to the contest organizers.
    *   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Participant Phone**: This is the phone number you want to associate with your contest entry.
    *   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Guardian Information**: This section is for you to provide information about your guardian or parent.
    *   **Guardian Information** (`guardian_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the guardian's information.
*   **Guardian Permission**: This field is for your guardian to indicate their permission to participate.
    *   **Guardian Permission** (`guardian_permission`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if your guardian gives permission to participate in the contest. Select "No" otherwise.
*   **Participant Signature**: This is for you to sign the contest entry.
    *   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign with your name for the contest entry.
*   **Parent Signature**: This is for your parent or guardian to sign the contest entry.
    *   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign with your name or parent's name for the contest entry.
*   **Entry Signature**: This is for the contest entry itself to be signed.
    *   **Entry Signature** (`entry_signature`, note, required/optional): Sign with your name for the contest entry.
*   **Date**: This is the date you enter for the contest entry.
    *   **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the contest entry.

## Tips

*   Make sure to review each field carefully and enter accurate and truthful information.
*   Use the "Entry Information" section to provide details about your contest entry.
*   Ensure that you enter your email address correctly in the "Participant Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number correctly in the "Participant Phone" field.
*   If applicable, provide guardian information in the "Guardian Information" field.
*   Indicate the guardian's permission in the "Guardian Permission" field.
*   Sign with your name or print your name for the "Participant Signature", "Parent Signature", and "Entry Signature" fields.
*   Enter the date correctly in the "Date" field.
