# Sofly's Tooth Spa Covid 19 Services Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sofly's Tooth Spa Covid 19 Services Consent Form" is used to obtain consent from patients for services provided during the COVID-19 pandemic. This form helps ensure that patients are aware of the services they are receiving and that the spa maintains accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the services you are willing to receive**: Choose the services you are interested in from the provided options.
2. Enter your **Name**: Provide your name as it appears on your identification.
3. Enter your **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or information.
4. Enter your **Phone**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any important information.
5. Sign below to indicate your consent: By signing, you are agreeing to receive the selected services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Services Selected (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more of the services offered by Sofly's Tooth Spa that you are interested in receiving. This includes dental services such as cleanings, fillings, or other treatments.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any updates or information.
* **Phone (Text)**: Provide your phone number so we can contact you with any important information.
* **Signature (Note)**: Please sign below to indicate your consent to receive the selected services.
* **Date Of Visit (Date)**: Enter the date of your scheduled visit to Sofly's Tooth Spa.
* **Reported Date (Date)**: Enter the date you reported any COVID-19 symptoms or other relevant information.
  *Note: This field might not be required if you do not have any reported symptoms.*
