# Field Visit Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Visit Permission Form is a crucial document that helps school administrators manage and monitor student activities during field visits. It's essential to ensure student safety and prevent duplication of entries.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the field trip, including:
	* `school_id`: The unique identifier for the school.
	* `student_id`: The student's unique identifier.
	* `field_trip_date`: The date of the field trip.
	* `trip_name`: A brief description of the field trip.
2. Provide consent for your child to participate in the field trip by selecting 'True' in the `parent_consent` and `guardian_consent` fields.
3. If your child is old enough, obtain their consent by selecting 'True' in the `student_consent` field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the field trip in the `notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_id** (`school_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the school.
* **student_id** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student's unique identifier.
* **field_trip_date** (`field_trip_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the field trip.
* **trip_name** (`trip_name`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the field trip.
* **parent_consent** (`parent_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to provide your consent for your child to participate in this field trip.
* **guardian_consent** (`guardian_consent`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to provide your consent as a guardian for your child to participate in this field trip.
* **student_consent** (`student_consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box to provide your consent as a student (if old enough).
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the field trip.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your child's information is up-to-date.
* If you're having trouble finding the required information, consult with your child's teacher or school administrator.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
