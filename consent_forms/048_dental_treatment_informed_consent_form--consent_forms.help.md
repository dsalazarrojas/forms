# Dental Treatment Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather the necessary information from patients before undergoing dental treatment. It helps the dentist and the patient understand the treatment, its risks, and the patient's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient's personal information, including their name, contact number, and date of treatment.
2. Provide a detailed description of the procedure to be performed, including the expected outcome and any risks involved.
3. Sign the acknowledgment and waiver section to indicate that you understand the procedure and its risks.
4. Sign the consent section to confirm your willingness to proceed with the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **patient_information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This field is for entering the patient's basic personal information.
*   Please enter the patient's basic personal details such as address, ID number, and insurance information (if applicable).

### **patient_name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the patient's name.
*   Please enter the patient's full name as it appears on their identification document.

### **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for entering the patient's contact number.
*   Please enter the patient's phone number or any other contact information where they can be reached.

### **procedure_details** (`procedure_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a detailed description of the procedure to be performed.
*   Please enter a clear and detailed description of the procedure, including the expected outcome and any risks involved.

### **procedure_description** (`procedure_description`, text, optional): This field is for providing a detailed description of the procedure to be performed.
*   Please enter a detailed description of the procedure, including the expected outcome and any risks involved.

### **acknowledgment_waiver** (`acknowledgment_waiver`, note, optional): This field is for acknowledging the risks and details of the procedure.
*   Please read and sign this section to acknowledge that you have understood the procedure and its risks.

### **dentist_name** (`dentist_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the dentist performing the procedure.
*   Please enter the name of the dentist performing the procedure.

### **patient_signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional): This field is for the patient to sign their consent.
*   Please sign this section to confirm your willingness to proceed with the treatment.

### **date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for entering the date of the treatment.
*   Please enter the date of the treatment.
 
Tips:
*   Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
*   If you're unsure about any part of the form, please ask your dentist for clarification.
*   Please sign all sections carefully to avoid errors.
