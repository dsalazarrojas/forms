# Photography Client Agreement Form (With Deposit) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Photography Client Agreement Form, designed for clients to provide their information and agree to certain terms before a photo shoot. It's essential to complete this form carefully to ensure a smooth and successful experience for both the client and the photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the **Client Name**, **Contact Info**, and **Photographer** fields.
2. Choose your preferred **Contact Method** (Email, Phone, or Mail).
3. Select the date and time for the **Shoot Date** and **Shoot Time**.
4. Enter the location for the shoot in the **Location** field.
5. Write your **Client Agreement** and **Photographer Agreement** to confirm your understanding of the terms.
6. Provide the **Deposit Amount** and **Deposit Date** for the payment.
7. Choose the **Payment Method** (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
8. Enter the **Total Price** for the shoot.
9. Review and agree to the **Terms And Conditions**.
10. Sign and date the **Signature** field (both client and photographer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information (email, phone number, or mail address).
* **Photographer** (`photographer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail).
* **Shoot Date** (`shoot_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the photo shoot.
* **Shoot Time** (`shoot_time`, time, required): Enter the time for the photo shoot.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location for the shoot.
* **Photographer Agreement** (`photographer_agreement`, text, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the photographer's terms.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, text, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms.
* **Deposit Amount** (`deposit_amount`, number, required): Enter the deposit amount for the shoot.
* **Deposit Date** (`deposit_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the deposit payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, number, required): Enter the total price for the shoot.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, date, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Photographer Signature** (`photographer_signature`, date, required): Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Photographer Name 2** (`photographer_name_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the second photographer (if applicable).
* **Contact Info 2** (`contact_info_2`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for the second photographer (if applicable).
* **Contact Method 2** (`contact_method_2`, select_one, required): Choose how you want to be contacted (Email, Phone, or Mail) for the second photographer.
* **Shoot Date 2** (`shoot_date_2`, date, required): Enter the date for the second photographer's shoot.
* **Shoot Time 2** (`shoot_time_2`, time, required): Enter the time for the second photographer's shoot.
* **Location 2** (`location_2`, text, required): Enter the location for the second photographer's shoot.
* **Photographer Agreement 2** (`photographer_agreement_2`, text, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the second photographer's terms.
* **Client Agreement 2** (`client_agreement_2`, text, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms for the second photographer.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the photographer.
- Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or delays.
