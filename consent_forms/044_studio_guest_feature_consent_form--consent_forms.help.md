# Studio Guest Feature Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a studio guest's consent for participation in a studio setting. It captures their permission to access certain features and areas of the studio, signature, phone number, email, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct information for the studio guest.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Consent Form
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form_page_1`, text, required/optional`): This field is a text-based area where you can enter any additional comments or notes regarding the guest's consent.

### 2. Guest Permissions
* **Guest Permissions** (`guest_permissions`, select_one, required`: This field is used to confirm whether the guest has permission to access certain features of the studio. Select "Yes" to grant permission and "No" if you do not.

### 3. Guest Signature
* **Guest Signature** (`guest_signature`, note, required`): This field is used to capture the guest's signature, which serves as their acknowledgment of the consent. Please have the guest sign in this field.

### 4. Guest Phone
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required`): Enter the guest's phone number in this field.

### 5. Guest Email
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required`): Enter the guest's email address in this field.

### 6. Guest Notes
* **Guest Notes** (`guest_notes`, note, optional`): This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments about the guest's visit.

### 7. Studio Owner
* **Studio Owner** (`studio_owner`, select_one, required`): This field is used to confirm whether the studio owner is aware of the guest's visit. Select "Yes" if the studio owner is aware, and "No" otherwise.

### 8. Owner Notes
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, note, required`): This field is used to capture any additional notes or comments from the studio owner regarding the guest's visit.

### 9. Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required`): Enter the date of the guest's visit.

### 10. Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required`): Enter the time of the guest's visit.

This guide is intended to be a comprehensive resource for completing this form. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
