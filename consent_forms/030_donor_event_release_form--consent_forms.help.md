# Donor Event Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and manage donor events, including event details, donor information, and consent for fundraising campaigns. It's a crucial tool for effective communication and successful fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. The fields are organized into sections for easy reference. Start by providing event information, then move on to donor details, and finally, sign and confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (donor_event_form_1, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the event, including the name and details of the fundraising campaign.
* **Donor Consent** (donor_event_form_2, text, required/optional): Check to indicate that the donor has consented to participate in the event.
* **Signature** (donor_event_form_3, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your consent.
* **Donor Information** (donor_event_form_4, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information to facilitate smooth communication with the event organizer.
* **Event Date** (donor_event_form_5, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (donor_event_form_6, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Fundraising Goal** (donor_event_form_7, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of funds you are raising for this campaign.
* **Donor Name** (donor_event_form_8, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Donor Address** (donor_event_form_9, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (donor_event_form_10, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Province** (donor_event_form_11, text, required/optional): Enter the province where you reside.
* **Phone** (donor_event_form_12, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (donor_event_form_13, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Signature Date** (donor_event_form_14, date, required/optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Signature Notary** (donor_event_form_15, text, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier to confirm your signature.
* **Notary Signature** (donor_event_form_16, text, required/optional): Enter your signature as a notary.
* **Event Organizer** (donor_event_form_17, text, required/optional): Enter your name and contact information for communication with the event organizer.
* **Organization** (donor_event_form_18, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization or group you represent.
* **Phone Number** (donor_event_form_19, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Location** (donor_event_form_20, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Signature Type** (donor_event_form_21, select one, required/optional): Choose the type of signature you are providing (Event Organizer, Donor, Event Notary).
* **Event Type** (donor_event_form_22, select multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more types of events (Walk/Run, Ride, Event).
* **Date of Event** (donor_event_form_23, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of Event** (donor_event_form_24, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Signature Reason** (donor_event_form_25, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for your signature.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
- Ensure that you provide all necessary information to avoid delays or errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, consult the event organizer or refer to the provided instructions.
- This form is a crucial tool for effective communication and fundraising efforts; use it wisely.
