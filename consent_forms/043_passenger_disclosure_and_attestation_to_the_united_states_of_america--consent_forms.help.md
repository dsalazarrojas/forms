# Passenger Disclosure And Attestation To The United States Of America - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Disclosure And Attestation To The United States Of America form is a document used to collect information and confirm aspects of passenger travel to the United States. This form may be used by airlines, travel agencies, or passengers themselves to provide details about their flight, such as departure and arrival dates and times, flight status, and passenger type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the attester in the "Name of Attester" field.
3. Enter the airline in the "Airline" field.
4. Enter the aircraft number in the "Aircraft Number" field.
5. Enter the flight number in the "Flight Number" field.
6. Enter the departure date in the "Departure Date" field.
7. Enter the departure time in the "Departure Time" field.
8. Enter the arrival date in the "Arrival Date" field.
9. Enter the arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field.
10. Select the flight status from the "Flight Status" field.
11. Select the flight type from the "Flight Type" field.
12. Select the flight origin from the "Flight Origin" field.
13. Select the flight destination from the "Flight Destination" field.
14. Select the flight status update from the "Flight Status Update" field.
15. Select the passenger type from the "Passenger Type" field.
16. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Travel Agent" field (optional).
17. Enter your declaration or attestation in the "Passenger Attestation" field (optional).
18. Confirm your attestation by entering a statement in the "Passenger Attestation Confirmation" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Name of Attester (Text)**: Enter the name of the person attesting or confirming the information in this form.
* **Airline (Text)**: Enter the name of the airline you are flying with.
* **Aircraft Number (Text)**: Enter the number of the aircraft you are flying on.
* **Flight Number (Text)**: Enter the flight number or code.
* **Departure Date (Date)**: Enter the date of departure in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Departure Time (Time)**: Enter the time of departure in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Arrival Date (Date)**: Enter the date of arrival in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Arrival Time (Time)**: Enter the time of arrival in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Flight Status (Text)**: Select the current flight status from the options provided.
* **Flight Type (Select One)**: Select whether the flight is Scheduled or Non-Scheduled.
* **Flight Origin (Select Multiple)**: Select the city or airport where the flight is originating from.
* **Flight Destination (Select Multiple)**: Select the city or airport where the flight is headed to.
* **Flight Status Update (Select Multiple)**: Select the status update of the flight from the options provided.
* **Passenger Type (Select One)**: Select whether you are an Adult, Child, or Infant.
* **Travel Agent (Text)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your travel agent or representative.
* **Passenger Attestation (Text)**: Enter your declaration or attestation, as required.
* **Passenger Attestation Confirmation (Text)**: Confirm your attestation by entering a statement or signature.
