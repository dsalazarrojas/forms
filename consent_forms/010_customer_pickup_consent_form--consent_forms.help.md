# Customer Pickup Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Pickup Consent Form is a document that customers must complete before collecting their items or packages. It is a legally binding agreement that confirms their understanding and consent to the terms and conditions of the pickup.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
2. Enter your customer name in the required field.
3. Enter the date and time you would like to collect your items or packages.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Customer Pickup Consent Form** (`customer_pickup_consent_form`, number, required): Enter 1 to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the pickup.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to collect your items or packages.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to collect your items or packages.
## Tips
* Please ensure that you read and understand the terms and conditions before signing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the store staff for assistance.
