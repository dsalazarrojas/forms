# Video Commentary Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain the participant's consent for the publication of their video commentary. It is a critical step in ensuring that participants understand and agree to the terms of publication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Yes" to indicate your consent for the publication of your video commentary.
2. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field. This is a required field.
4. If you wish to provide your phone number, enter it in the "Participant Phone Number" field. This field is optional.
5. If you wish to provide your address, enter it in the "Participant Address" field. This field is optional.
6. Select your country of residence from the "Participant Country of Residence" dropdown menu.
7. If you have video commentary to share, enter it in the "Participant Video Commentary" field. This field is optional.
8. Select your preference for video commentary publication from the "Participant Video Commentary Options" dropdown menu. If you select "Yes, with photos", your video commentary may be published with photos.
9. Select "Yes" to indicate your agreement to the publication of your video commentary. This is a required field.
10. If you wish to select additional agreement options, choose from the "Participant Agreement Select Option 1", "Participant Agreement Select Option 2", or "Participant Agreement Options" dropdown menus. Note that these fields are optional and are meant for more specific agreements that may be required for certain types of publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Video Commentary Publication Consent**: This is a single selection field where you must choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your consent to the publication of your video commentary. This field is required.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** true

- **Participant Name**: Please enter your name to complete this field. This is a required field to ensure we have a record of your identity.
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** true

- **Participant Email**: Please enter a valid email address in this field. This is a required field.
  * **Type:** email
  * **Required:** true

- **Participant Phone Number**: If you wish to provide your phone number, please enter it here. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Address**: If you wish to provide your address, please enter it here. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Country of Residence**: Please select your country of residence from the dropdown menu.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** true

- **Participant Video Commentary**: If you have video commentary to share, enter it here. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** text
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Video Commentary Options**: Please select one option from this dropdown menu.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Agreement**: Please select "Yes" to agree to the publication of your video commentary. This field is required.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** true

- **Participant Agreement Options**: If you wish to select additional agreement options, choose from this dropdown menu. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** select_multiple
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Agreement Select Option 1**: Please select one option from this dropdown menu. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** false

- **Participant Agreement Select Option 2**: Please select one option from this dropdown menu. This field is optional.
  * **Type:** select_one
  * **Required:** false

## Tips
* Please ensure you understand what you are consenting to before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* If you wish to withdraw your consent, please contact us and we will remove your video commentary from publication.
