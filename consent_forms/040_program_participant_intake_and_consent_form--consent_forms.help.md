# Program Participant Intake And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from program participants for the initial intake process. It will be used to identify program participation, gather contact details, and establish emergency contact preferences. Completing this form accurately will help us tailor our support and communication according to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing all necessary details. Ensure all fields that are marked as 'required' are filled in as these are crucial for our program's success.
2. When filling out the 'Contact Preference' section, select all preferred methods of contact from the list.
3. Make sure to fill in any other relevant fields as well, even if they are not marked as 'required', to ensure we have a complete understanding of your preferences and needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and any other contact details you'd like to share.
* **Program Participation** (`program_participation`, text, required/optional): Describe your interest or participation in the program. Be as detailed as possible about your role or involvement.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the methods through which you'd like to be contacted:
	+ Contact via Email
	+ Contact via Phone
	+ Contact via SMS
	+ Contact via Mail
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your preferred email address. This will be our primary contact point.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact phone number for any urgent matters.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth, as this information is important for certain aspects of the program.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person or their relationship to you.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person. This will help us understand their role in your life.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact preferences for the best-suited communication approach.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
