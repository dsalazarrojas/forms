# Spray Tanning Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spray Tanning Intake Form is a collection of personal and contact information used to book and schedule a spray tanning appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by *) with your information.
2. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Enter your Email in the **Email** field, following the correct format (e.g., [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com)).
6. Enter your Contact Address in the **Contact Address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This field is required.
  - Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - This field is required.
  - Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your current phone number where you can be reached.
  - This field is required.
  - Please ensure your phone number is correct to ensure proper communication.
- **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive communications from us.
  - This field is optional, but please enter it if you wish to receive updates and confirmations.
  - Please follow the email format (e.g., [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com)).
- **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your address where you can be reached.
  - This field is optional.
  - Please enter your address if you wish to receive mail or package delivery information.
