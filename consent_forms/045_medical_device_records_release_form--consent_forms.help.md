# Medical Device Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Device Records Release Form is used to collect information about a medical device and the patient's consent to release their medical records. This form is designed to be completed by the patient or their representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Patient Information Form (page 1) with your personal and contact information.
2. On the Medical Device Form (page 2), provide details about the medical device you are releasing your records for.
3. In the Consent Form (page 3), confirm that you have read and understood the purpose and implications of releasing your medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information Form** (`patient_information_form`, text, optional): Fill out your name, contact information (address, phone number, email), and date of birth.
- **Medical Device Form** (`medical_device_form`, text, optional): Describe the medical device you are releasing your records for (e.g., device name, serial number, manufacturer, etc.).
- **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, optional): Check the box confirming that you have read and understood the purpose and implications of releasing your medical records. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any information, please contact us for assistance.
- Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting it.
