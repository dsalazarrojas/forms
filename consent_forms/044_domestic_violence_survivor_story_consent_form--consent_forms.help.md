# Domestic Violence Survivor Story Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with domestic violence. It is a confidential and safe space for you to share your story. Your responses will be handled with care and respect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, as it is your story to tell.
2. Please provide as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing.
3. If you need to skip any questions, you can. However, please note that some fields are marked as required, which means they are essential for the form's purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any general information about yourself, such as your name, contact information, or any other details you feel are relevant.
* **Survivor Details**: Please select the appropriate category that best describes your identity (Female, Male, or Other).
* **Date of Violence**: Enter the date when the domestic violence occurred.
* **Time of Violence**: Enter the time when the domestic violence occurred.
* **Location of Violence**: Provide the location where the domestic violence occurred.
* **Perpetrator Name**: Enter the name of the person who committed the domestic violence.
* **Relationship to Perpetrator**: Please describe your relationship to the perpetrator (e.g., spouse, partner, family member).
* **Number of People in Violence**: If multiple people were involved, please specify the number of individuals.
* **Type of Violence**: Describe the type of domestic violence you experienced (e.g., physical, emotional, psychological).
* **Support System Used**: Check the boxes that apply to the support systems you used (Police, Shelter, Other).
* **Perpetrator Identification**: Please provide any identification information about the perpetrator (e.g., address, description).
* **Perpetrator Address**: Enter the address where the perpetrator can be reached.
* **Survivor Support System In Place**: Check if you have a support system in place for your safety and well-being.
* **Perpetrator Harm Reports to Survivor**: Select any harm the perpetrator reports to you (Physical, Emotional, Psychological, Financial).
* **Perpetrator Harm Reports to Others**: Select any harm the perpetrator reports to others (Physical, Emotional, Psychological, Financial).
* **Perpetrator Relationship with Survivor**: Please describe your relationship with the perpetrator.
* **Perpetrator Previous Violence Against Survivor**: If the perpetrator has previously harmed you, please describe the incident.
* **Perpetrator Reports Survivor to Authorities**: Check if the perpetrator reports your experience to authorities.
* **Perpetrator Previous Violence Against Others**: Select if the perpetrator has previously harmed others.
* **Survivor Support**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about your support system.
* **Perpetrator Sustains Harm from Survivor**: Select any harm the perpetrator sustains from you (True or False).
* **Perpetrator Sustains Harm from Others**: Select if the perpetrator sustains harm from others (True or False).
* **Perpetrator Sustains Harm from Someone Else**: Select if the perpetrator sustains harm from someone else (True or False).
* **Perpetrator Violence Prevention Methods Used**: Please describe any methods the perpetrator used to prevent further violence.
* **Survivor Voluntary Statement**: This is a space for you to provide any additional information or statements you would like to make about your experience.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and carefully, as it is your story to tell.
* If you need help or support while filling out this form, please reach out to a trusted friend, family member, or professional.
* Remember, your responses are confidential and will be handled with care.
