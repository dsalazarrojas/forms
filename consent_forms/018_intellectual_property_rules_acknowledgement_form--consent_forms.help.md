# Intellectual Property Rules Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to acknowledge and record intellectual property rights of individuals or teams within the organization. It's a crucial document that helps maintain accurate records and ensures compliance with intellectual property regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, make sure you understand the purpose and the sections included.
2. **Fill out all required fields**: Make sure to complete all the fields marked as "required" to ensure accurate and complete submission.
3. **Use the correct format**: For fields like "Date" and "Time", use the correct date and time format to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Your Name**: This field is where you enter your name. Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number, including the country code.
* **IP Address**: Enter the IP address associated with your work account. This is usually the IP address of your work computer or laptop.
* **User Agreement**: This field is not applicable. Please ignore it.
* **Acknowledgement**: Select one or more of the following options to acknowledge your understanding of the intellectual property rules:
	+ Option 1: I understand and agree to the rules.
	+ Option 2: I do not understand and need clarification.
	+ Option 3: I disagree with the rules.
* **Acknowledgement2**: This field is not applicable. Please ignore it.
* **Date**: Enter the current date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" or the desired format as specified in your organization's date format.
* **Time**: Enter the current time in the format "HH:MM:SS" or the desired format as specified in your organization's time format.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you're unsure about any section or field, consult with your supervisor or the IP rights team before submission.
