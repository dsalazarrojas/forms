# IRB Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IRB Consent Form is a legal document that outlines the terms of consent for participants in a research study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Review all fields carefully to understand their relevance to the study and participant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Provide clear and concise information in the text fields.
4. Select the correct options for the select_multiple field, "Study Participants".
5. Use the format mm/dd/yyyy for the date field, "Informed Consent Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Participants** (`study_participants`, select_multiple, required: false):
  This field asks whether the study involves human participants or not. Select all applicable options to indicate who is participating in the study.
* **Research Goal** (`research_goal`, text, required: false):
  This field should be used to briefly describe the purpose and objectives of the study.
* **Informed Consent Date** (`informed_consent_date`, date, required: false):
  This field requires the date on which the participant's informed consent was obtained.
* **Medical Researcher** (`medical_researcher`, text, required: false):
  This field should be used to identify the person or team of people conducting the research.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, text, required: false):
  This field is used to provide the name and address of the organization conducting the research.
* **Study Description** (`study_description`, text, required: false):
  This field should be used to describe the research study in more detail, including the methods and procedures.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false):
  This field should be used to provide the name and contact information of the person responsible for the research study.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false):
  This field should be used to provide the email address of the contact person.
* **Research Institution Address** (`research_institution_address`, text, required: false):
  This field is used to provide the street address of the research institution.
* **Research Institution Phone** (`research_institution_phone`, text, required: false):
  This field is used to provide the phone number of the research institution.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check all fields before submission.
- If a field is unclear or not applicable, please seek clarification from the research team or the IRB committee.
- If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the research team or the IRB office for assistance.
