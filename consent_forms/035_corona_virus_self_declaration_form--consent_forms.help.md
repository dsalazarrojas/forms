# Corona Virus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-declaration form designed for individuals to report on their health status, specifically regarding the COVID-19 virus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact number in the respective fields.
2. If you have experienced any of the symptoms listed, select all that apply from the drop-down menu.
3. Indicate your travel history by selecting a date or filling in the travel location if you are unsure.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): If you have experienced any of the following symptoms over the past 14 days, select all that apply:
	+ Fever
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Chest pain or pressure
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, date, optional): Indicate the date of your last travel.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, optional): If you are unsure of the date of your last travel, fill in the location instead.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are experiencing severe symptoms, please contact medical professionals immediately.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please seek clarification from the relevant authorities.
