# Donor Data Usage Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to obtain your acknowledgement of how your data will be used by the organization. Please take a few moments to review the form and provide your consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read through the provided explanation of data usage (question 6).
2. Choose whether you consent to the usage of your data (question 2).
3. If you want to provide your contact information and mailing address (questions 3 and 4, respectively), feel free to fill those in as well.
4. If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I consent to the usage of my data** (`donor_consent_2`, select_one, **required**):
	+ Please select "True" if you give your consent to the usage of your data.
	+ Please select "False" if you do not give your consent to the usage of your data.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_3`, email, **optional**):
	+ If you want to provide your contact information (email address and phone number), please fill in this field.
	+ You can leave this field blank if you do not want to share your contact information.
* **Mailing Address** (`address_4`, text, **optional**):
	+ If you want to provide your mailing address, please fill in this field.
	+ You can leave this field blank if you do not want to share your mailing address.
* **Data Usage Explanation** (`donor_data_usage_6`, note):
	+ This is a detailed explanation of how your data will be used by the organization.
	+ Please take a few moments to read through it before making your decision.
