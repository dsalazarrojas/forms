# Final Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a waiver and release from participants in an event or activity. It captures essential information such as participant details, event information, and waiver terms. The participant must agree to the waiver terms and sign the form to confirm their agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section.
2. Provide event information.
3. Agree to the waiver terms and sign the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be listed on the form.
* **Event Info**: Enter any relevant information about the event.
* **Waiver**: Enter any notes about the waiver terms.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign your name to confirm you are the participant.
* **Participant Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Participant Time**: Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address (optional).

### Event Information
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Info**: Enter any additional information about the event.

### Waiver and Agreement
* **Waiver Terms**: Read and agree to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Agrees**: Select "I agree to the terms" to confirm you agree to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Agrees Date**: Enter the date you agree to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Agrees Time**: Enter the time you agree to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Agrees Signature**: Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Participant Agrees Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the waiver terms carefully before agreeing to them.
* Enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
* Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the waiver terms.
