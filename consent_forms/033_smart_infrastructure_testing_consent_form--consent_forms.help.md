# Smart Infrastructure Testing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Infrastructure Testing Consent Form is a tool for tracking and documenting information about smart infrastructure testing projects. This form collects details from participants, project leads, contractors, and observers to ensure that all relevant information is captured efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the Participant option that best fits your role in the project. The options are: Option 1, Option 2, and Option 3.
2.  Fill out the participant information, including your project name, contact information and project notes.
3.  Provide your project lead and observer information, if relevant.
4.  Add any additional notes about your project if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Smart Infrastructure Testing Consent** (`smart_infrastructure_testing_consent`, text, required: false): This is the title of the project, which should be filled in with the actual project name.
*   **Participant** (`participant`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options that best fits your role in the project.
*   **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select_one, required: false): This field should be filled with your role in the project, in line with your selection in the "Participant" field.
*   **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of your project.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where the project will take place.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the project.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the project.
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your participant email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your participant phone number.
*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your participant role or project.
*   **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your project lead.
*   **Project Lead Email** (`project_lead_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your project lead.
*   **Project Lead Phone** (`project_lead_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your project lead.
*   **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the project.
*   **Contractor** (`contractor`, text, required: false): Enter any information about the contractor, if applicable.
*   **Contractor Email** (`contractor_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the contractor.
*   **Contractor Phone** (`contractor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contractor.
*   **Contractor Notes** (`contractor_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the contractor.
*   **Observer** (`observer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the observer, if applicable.
*   **Observer Email** (`observer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the observer.
*   **Observer Phone** (`observer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the observer.
*   **Observer Notes** (`observer_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the observer.
*   **Test Type** (`test_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of test.
*   **Test Location** (`test_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the test.
*   **Test Status** (`test_status`, text, required: false): Enter the current test status.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all relevant fields for accurate tracking and documentation.
*   Select the correct participant and project lead options to ensure accurate project information.
