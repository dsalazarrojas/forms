# recurring_credit_card_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to obtain recurring credit card authorization from users, ensuring secure and reliable payments for subscription-based services or services that require periodic payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your credit card number in the "card number" field.
3. Provide the Card Verification Value (CVV) in the "cvv" field.
4. Enter your credit card expiration date in the "expiration date" field.
5. Select the start month of your desired payment interval in the "month start" field.
6. Select the end month of your desired payment interval in the "month end" field.
7. Choose the frequency of your payments (e.g., every 1 month, every 2 months, etc.) in the "charge interval" field.
8. Enter the amount that will be charged each interval in the "amount" field.
9. Select your preferred payment processor (Stripe or Other) in the "payment processor" field.
10. Choose your payment method (Credit Card or Direct Debit) in the "payment method" field.
11. Provide any additional notes (optional) in the "notes" field.
12. Select your credit card type (VISA, Mastercard, Amex) in the "card type" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
14. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
15. Enter the label for your credit card number in the "card number label" field.
16. Enter the label for your credit card expiration date in the "card expiry date label" field.
17. Enter the label for your Card Verification Value (CVV) in the "card cvv label" field.
18. Enter the label for your credit card start date in the "card start date label" field.
19. Enter the label for your credit card end date in the "card end date label" field.
