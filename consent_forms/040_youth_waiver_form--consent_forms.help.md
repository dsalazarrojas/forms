# Youth Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Waiver Form is used to obtain consent from a parent or guardian to allow their child to participate in a specific activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Parent Name** field with the name of the child's parent or guardian.
2. Enter the **Student Name** field with the name of the child participating in the activity.
3. Enter the **Event Date** field with the date of the activity or event.
4. Enter the **Waiver Date** field with the date the waiver was signed.
5. In the **Parent Signature** field, select 'Yes' to confirm that the parent or guardian has given their consent.
6. In the **Student Signature** field, select 'Yes' to confirm that the student has read and understands the terms of the waiver.
7. Enter the **Contact Number** field with the parent or guardian's contact number.
8. Enter the **Contact Address** field with the parent or guardian's address.
