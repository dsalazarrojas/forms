# Camp Knock Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather the necessary consents and releases from parents or guardians before their child can participate in camp activities. It ensures that the parent or guardian has provided their consent for their child to engage in various aspects of the camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_full_name_consent`): This field is for the parent or guardian's full name.
* **Camper Full Name** (`camper_full_name_consent`): This field is for the child's full name.
* **Camper Date of Birth** (`camper_birth_date`): This field is for the child's date of birth. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Medical Emergency Release** (`medical_emergency_release`): This field is to authorize Camp Knock to seek emergency medical treatment for your child. Please select "I Consent and Agree" to grant consent.
* **Media - Photo and Video Release** (`photo_video_release`): This field is to grant permission for photos of your child to be used for camp promotional materials. Please select "Yes - I Consent" to grant permission.
* **Swimming and Waterfront Participation** (`swimming_water_release`): This field is to permit your child to participate in supervised water activities. Please select "Yes - I Consent" to grant permission.
* **Sunscreen and Insect Repellent Application** (`sun_safety_consent`): This field is to permit staff to apply sunscreen and bug spray as needed. Please select "Yes - I Consent" to grant permission.
* **Transportation and Off-site Trips** (`transportation_trip_consent`): This field is to permit your child to be transported by camp vehicles for scheduled excursions. Please select "Yes - I Consent" to grant permission.
* **Release of Liability and Indemnity** (`liability_release_waiver`): This field is to understand the risks of camp activities and release Camp Knock from liability. Please select "I Accept and Agree" to grant consent.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact (Name and Phone)** (`secondary_emergency_contact_consent`): This field is for an additional emergency contact in case the parent or guardian cannot be reached. Please provide the name and phone number of the contact.
* **Essential Medical Notes** (`medical_notes_consent`): This field is for any essential medical information that staff should be aware of, such as allergies or current medications. Please provide any relevant medical notes.
* **Parent - Guardian Signature (Typed Name)** (`signature_parent_consent`): This field is for the parent or guardian's signature, which confirms their consent.
* **Date Signed** (`date_of_consent_signing`): This field is to record the date the form was signed.

Please complete this form carefully and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or concerns.
