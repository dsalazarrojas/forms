# Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form is used to collect information from patients who require a retouch of their eyeliner pigments. This form is used to assess the patient's eligibility for the procedure and obtain their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions below to provide your information.
2. Select your response for each question from the provided options.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Eyeliner Retouch Pigment Consent Form** (`eyleneretouchpigmentconsent`, text): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your condition or concerns.
* **Eyeliner Retouch Patient Info** (`eyleneretouchpatientinfo`, select_multiple, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have any of the following information:
	+ Are you a new patient?
	+ Do you have a previous medical history?
	+ Do you have any allergies or medical conditions?
* **Signature 1** (`eyleneretouchsignature1`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have read and understand the information provided in the consent section.
* **Signature 2** (`eyleneretouchsignature3`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are willing to proceed with the procedure if cleared by a medical professional.
* **Signature 3** (`eyleneretouchsignature4`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you understand that the procedure may not be successful and that further procedures may be needed.
* **Signature 4** (`eyleneretouchsignature4`, select_one, optional): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are aware of and agree to the risks associated with the procedure.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure that you receive the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask a medical professional for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, please submit it to the medical professional for review and processing.
