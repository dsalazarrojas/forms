# Influenza Vaccine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Influenza Vaccine Consent Form is designed to gather essential information from patients to inform and secure their consent for influenza vaccination. It helps healthcare professionals understand patient preferences, medical history, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your influenza vaccine interest, indicating your willingness to receive the vaccine.
2.  Provide your patient identification number, which is used to verify your identity.
3.  Enter your date of birth to confirm your age eligibility for vaccination.
4.  Enter the date when you consent to receive the influenza vaccine.
5.  Name of person providing consent, usually a healthcare provider.
6.  Sign to confirm your signature to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form.
7.  Signature of the person giving consent, often a healthcare provider or parent/guardian.
8.  Enter the name of the medical organization providing the vaccine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **influenza_vaccine_interest (Text, Optional):** Describe your interest in receiving the influenza vaccine. This section helps healthcare providers understand your preference for vaccination.
* **patient_identification (Number, Optional):** Enter your unique patient ID number to verify your identity.
* **patient_date_of_birth (Date, Optional):** Enter your date of birth to confirm your age eligibility for the vaccine.
* **consent_date (Date, Optional):** Record the date when you consent to receive the vaccine.
* **consent_given_by (Text, Optional):** Name of person providing consent, usually a healthcare provider.
* **patient_signature (Text, Optional):** Sign to acknowledge that you have read and understood the form and are willing to receive the vaccine.
* **consent_signature (Text, Optional):** Signature from the person giving consent, often a healthcare provider or parent/guardian.
* **medical_organization (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the medical organization providing the vaccine.
