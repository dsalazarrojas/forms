# Hospital Admission Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Admission Permission Form is designed for patients to provide their personal and contact information, as well as consent for their hospital admission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select the correct options for relationship status, address, and healthcare provider signature.
3. Enter the emergency contact's phone number and email.
4. Provide your medical history, including any allergies and current medications.
5. Indicate your emergency contact's phone number and email (if applicable).
6. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
7. If applicable, sign the healthcare provider signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Relationship Status**: Select how you are related to the emergency contact (e.g., Married, Single, Partnered, Other).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and relationship to you (e.g., Spouse, Parent, Partner).
* **Address**: Select the address where you can be reached (e.g., Home, Work, Other).
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Medical History**: Provide any relevant medical history, allergies, or current medications.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address (if applicable).
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your consent.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature**: Sign the form if you are a healthcare provider.
* **Date**: Enter the date of admission.
* **Time**: Enter the time of admission.
* **Patient Consent**: Indicate your consent for hospital admission (e.g., 'True' or 'False').
* **Healthcare Provider Consent**: Indicate your consent as a healthcare provider (e.g., 'True' or 'False').

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
* This form is a requirement for hospital admission, so please complete it carefully and honestly.
