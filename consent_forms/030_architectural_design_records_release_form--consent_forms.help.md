# Architectural Design Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Architectural Design Records Release Form is used to document the release of records and documents related to architectural design projects. This form ensures that the records are shared with the correct parties, while also maintaining confidentiality and adhering to relevant non-disclosure agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information section with the name and contact details of the client.
2. Enter the project name, address, and completion period.
3. Specify the records to be released, such as design drawings, specifications, and reports.
4. Choose the purpose of the release, which could be internal use, sharing with contractors, or public display.
5. Identify the recipients of the released records, including their relationship to the project.
6. Confirm that the records contain confidential information and determine if a non-disclosure agreement is required.
7. Enter the date of release authorization and the authorized signatory's name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the individual or company name of the client.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter the name or description of the project.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`): Enter the project's location or address.
* **Project Period** (`project_period`): Enter the completion period of the project.
* **Architect or Design Firm Name** (`architect_firm_name`): Enter the name of the architect or design firm involved in the project.
* **Records to be Released** (`records_to_release`): Check all the records that apply, such as design drawings, specifications, and reports.
* **Purpose of Release** (`release_purpose`): Choose how the records will be used, such as internal use, sharing with contractors, or public display.
* **Other Purpose** (`other_purpose`): If the release purpose is not listed, describe its intended use.
* **Recipient Names or Organizations** (`recipient_names`): Enter the names or organization of the recipients of the released records.
* **Relationship of Recipients to Project** (`recipient_relationship`): Choose the relationship of the recipients to the project, such as construction contractor or marketing use.
* **Records Contain Confidential Information** (`confidentiality_agreement`): Confirm if the records contain confidential information.
* **Non-disclosure Agreement Required** (`nda_required`): Determine if a non-disclosure agreement is required.
* **Date of Release Authorization** (`release_date`): Enter the date of release authorization.
* **Authorized Signatory Name** (`authorized_signatory`): Enter the name of the authorized signatory.
* **Authorized Signatory Title** (`authorized_title`): Enter the title of the authorized signatory.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure that all relevant information is included.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the form's help guide or consult with the relevant authorities.
