# Team Member Data Management Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from team members for data management purposes, such as updating employee records or for compliance with data protection regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
3. Select your role in the team from the "Role in the team" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your job type in the "Job type" field.
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
6. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
8. Enter your date of join in the "Date of join" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
9. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Read and agree to the terms in the "Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role in the team** (`Role in the team`, select_one, required/optional): Select your role in the team, which could be Manager, Team Member, Team Leader, or Team Assistant.
* **Job type** (`Job type`, text, required/optional): Enter your job type, which could include job category or function.
* **Department** (`Department`, text, required/optional): Enter your department as it appears on your official documents.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required/optional): Enter your work location.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date of join** (`Date of join`, date, required/optional): Enter your start date of employment in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field requires you to read and agree to the terms, which includes understanding that the company will handle your data securely, providing data for the form, and using the data for business purposes only.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): This is a submit button to save your changes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields correctly.
* Review the terms and conditions in the "Agreement" field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to your HR representative or team lead for clarification.
