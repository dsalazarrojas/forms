# swimming_lesson_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swimming lesson consent form is a crucial document that requires accurate and honest information from swimmers and their emergency contacts. It is used to ensure the swimmer's safety and well-being during swimming lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the swimmer's name in the "Swimmer's Name" field (Page 1, field 1).
2. Enter the parent/guardian's name in the "Parent's or Guardian's Name" field (Page 1, field 2).
3. Enter the swimmer's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field (Page 1, field 3), following the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Enter the emergency contact's name in the "Emergency Contact's Name" field (Page 1, field 4).
5. Select the relationship between the swimmer and the emergency contact from the options provided (Page 1, field 5).
6. List any medical conditions in the "Medical Conditions" field (Page 1, field 6).
7. Enter the emergency contact's address in the "Emergency Contact's Address" field (Page 1, field 7).
8. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact's Phone Number" field (Page 1, field 8).
9. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the "Emergency Contact's Email" field (Page 1, field 9).
10. Select whether the swimmer has medical clearance or not from the options provided (Page 1, field 10).
11. Enter any notes from the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact's Notes" field (Page 1, field 11).
12. Enter any notes from the swimmer in the "Swimmer's Notes" field (Page 1, field 12).
13. Have the swimmer sign the form in the "Swimmer's Signature" field (Page 1, field 13).
14. Have the emergency contact sign the form in the "Emergency Contact's Signature" field (Page 1, field 14).
15. Enter the swimmer's date of birth again in the "Swimmer Dob 2" field (Page 1, field 15).
16. Enter the swimmer's date of birth again in the "Swimmer Dob 3" field (Page 1, field 16).
17. Enter the swimmer's date of birth again in the "Swimmer Dob 4" field (Page 1, field 17).
18. Enter the swimmer's date of birth again in the "Swimmer Dob 5" field (Page 1, field 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Swimmer's Name (swimmer_id)**: Enter the swimmer's full name.
* **Parent's or Guardian's Name (parent_id)**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Birth (swimmer_dob)**: Enter the swimmer's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Emergency Contact's Name (emergency_contact_name)**: Enter the emergency contact's full name.
* **Relationship to Swimmer (emergency_contact_relationship)**: Select how the emergency contact is related to the swimmer (Mother, Father, or Guardian).
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)**: List any medical conditions the swimmer has.
* **Emergency Contact's Address (emergency_contact_address)**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Phone Number (emergency_contact_phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Email (emergency_contact_email)**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Clearance (emergency_clearance)**: Select whether the swimmer has medical clearance or not.
* **Emergency Contact's Notes (emergency_contact_notes)**: Enter any notes from the emergency contact.
* **Swimmer's Notes (swimmer_notes)**: Enter any notes from the swimmer.
* **Swimmer's Signature (swimmer_signature)**: Sign the form as the swimmer.
* **Emergency Contact's Signature (emergency_contact_signature)**: Sign the form as the emergency contact.
* **Swimmer Dob 2 (swimmer_dob_2)**: Enter the swimmer's date of birth again.
* **Swimmer Dob 3 (swimmer_dob_3)**: Enter the swimmer's date of birth again.
* **Swimmer Dob 4 (swimmer_dob_4)**: Enter the swimmer's date of birth again.
* **Swimmer Dob 5 (swimmer_dob_5)**: Enter the swimmer's date of birth again.

## Tips
- Please double-check the information entered in each field for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that the swimmer and emergency contact signs the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
