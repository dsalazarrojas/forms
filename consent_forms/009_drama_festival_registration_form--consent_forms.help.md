# drama_festival_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The drama festival registration form is a collection of fields to gather information from participants. Fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process and to help us manage the festival effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Fill in your first name and last name for identification.
2. Enter your contact email to ensure we can reach you.
3. Select your role in the festival from the options provided.
4. If applicable, fill in your group name and group type.
5. Specify your availability for the festival, including start and end times.
6. Provide a brief description of your participation, including any production details.
7. If necessary, submit your scheduling requests.
8. Enter your entry fee, if applicable.
9. Select your payment method.
10. Confirm your payment status.
11. Add any additional notes about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: A text field to collect your personal name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: A text field to collect your family name.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: A field to collect your email address so we can contact you.
* **Role (Role)**: A dropdown menu where you can select your participation role, such as Performer, Crew, or Volunteer.
* **Group Name (Group Name)**: A text field to enter your group name, if you're part of a group.
* **Group Type (Group Type)**: A dropdown menu to categorize your group type, such as Dance, Theater, or Music.
* **Start Time (Start Time)**: A field to specify your available start time for the festival.
* **End Time (End Time)**: A field to specify your available end time for the festival.
* **Description (Description)**: A text area to describe your participation, including any relevant production details.
* **Scheduling Request (Scheduling Request)**: A dropdown menu to select your scheduling preferences, such as Early, Late, or Flexible.
* **Production Details (Production Details)**: A text field to provide any additional information about your production.
* **Entry Fee (Entry Fee)**: A numeric field to submit the amount you're willing to pay to participate.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: A dropdown menu to choose your payment method, such as Credit, Debit, or Online.
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)**: A dropdown menu to confirm your payment status, such as Paid, Partial, or Not Paid.
* **Notes (Notes)**: A text area to add any additional comments about your participation.

**Tips**

* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be clear about your availability and participation details.
* Confirm your payment status to avoid any confusion.
* Add any relevant notes to help us better understand your needs.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
