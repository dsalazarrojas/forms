# Blended Learning Experience Survey Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blended Learning Experience Survey Consent Form is designed to collect informed consent from participants in a blended learning experience study. This form ensures that participants understand the nature and requirements of the study, their rights, and their responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have understood the questions and the implications of your responses.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Sign and date the form once you have completed all the required pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Blended Learning Experience Survey Consent Form** (`consent_header`, note, required: false): This is a heading that introduces the form and explains the purpose of the survey.
* **Study Title** (`study_title`, note, required: false): This is a heading that provides information about the study.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: true): Please provide your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required: true): Please enter your contact email address.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, required: false): If you were assigned an ID, please enter it.
* **Age Verification** (`age_verification`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I am 18 or older" if you are 18 or above, or "No I am under 18" if you are under 18.
* **Study Purpose Understanding** (`study_purpose`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the study purpose, or "No I Need More Information" if you need clarification.
* **Procedures Understanding** (`procedures_understanding`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood what participation involves, or "No I Need Clarification" if you need further explanation.
* **Risks Understanding** (`risks_understanding`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you are aware of any potential risks, or "No I Have Questions" if you need more information.
* **Benefits Understanding** (`benefits_understanding`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you have read and understood the potential benefits, or "No I Do Not Understand" if you have not understood.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you agree to keep your data confidential, or "No I Do Not Agree" if you do not agree.
* **Voluntary Participation** (`voluntary_participation`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you understand that participation is voluntary, or "No I Do Not Understand" if you are unsure.
* **Right to Withdraw** (`right_to_withdraw`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Understand" if you know that you can withdraw at any time, or "No I Do Not Understand" if you are unsure.
* **Data Usage Consent** (`data_usage_consent`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Consent" if you consent to your data being used for research, or "No I Do Not Consent" if you do not consent.
* **Audio Recording Consent** (`audio_recording_consent`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Consent" if you consent to audio recording, or "False" if you do not consent, or "Not Applicable" if you are not being recorded.
* **Publication Consent** (`publication_consent`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes I Consent" if you consent to anonymized data being used in publications, or "False" if you do not consent.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information_provided`, select_one, required: true): Please select "True" if you have received contact information from the researcher.
* **Questions Addressed** (`questions_addressed`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes All Answered" if all your questions were answered, "I Have No Questions" if none of your questions were unanswered, or "I Still Have Questions" if you still have questions.
* **Electronic Signature** (`consent_signature`, text, required: true): Please type your name as your electronic signature.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required: true): Please enter the current date.
* **Copy of Consent Form** (`copy_received`, select_one, required: true): Please select "True" if you have received a copy of this form, or "False" if you have not received one.
