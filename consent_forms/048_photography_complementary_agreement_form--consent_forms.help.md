# Photography Complementary Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for clients to provide their information and consent for complementary photography services. It helps ensure that we have all necessary details and permissions before providing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is required for your complementary photography services.
2. Fill in the Client Information section with your relevant details.
3. In the Service Details section, describe the services you would like to receive.
4. Carefully read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
5. Submit the form once you have reviewed and agreed to all the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information, text, required: false)**: In this section, please provide your personal details such as name, email, and contact information.
* **Service Details (Service Details, text, required: false)**: Describe the services you would like to receive, such as portrait, wedding, or event photography.
* **Terms and Conditions (Terms and Conditions, note, required: false)**: Read this section carefully and agree to the terms of service. It contains important details about the photography services, including cancellation policies and service scope.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, if applicable.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
