# mental_health_workshop_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from participants for the mental health workshop. This includes details about the workshop such as the name, description, date, and time, as well as information about the participant, like their name, phone number, and email. The form also includes an agreement section where participants must select whether they understand and agree to the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the form carefully and ensuring you understand the purpose and the information being collected.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as such in the form.
3. For fields with dropdown options, select the relevant option that best applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **workshop_name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the mental health workshop you are participating in. 
* **workshop_description** (`workshop_description`, text, required/optional): Describe the mental health workshop you are participating in.
* **workshop_date** (`workshop_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **workshop_time** (`workshop_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a participant in the workshop.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **workshop_agreement** (`workshop_agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you understand and agree to the terms and conditions. You can choose "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions." or "I do not understand and do not agree to the terms and conditions."
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact, in case of any urgent matter.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* Review your answers before submission to ensure they are complete and correct.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to seek clarification or assistance.
* Submit the form only when you are confident that the information provided is accurate and truthful.
