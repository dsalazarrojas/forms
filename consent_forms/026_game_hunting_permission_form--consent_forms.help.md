# Game Hunting Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Hunting Permission Form is used to collect and record game hunting permissions from landowners. It is essential to have this permission before hunting on private lands to ensure that both parties are aware of the terms and conditions of the permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* Hunter Name: Enter your name as the hunter.
	* Landowner Name: Enter the name of the landowner.
	* Hunter Contact: Enter your contact information as the hunter.
	* Landowner Contact: Enter the contact information of the landowner.
2. Provide landowner information:
	* Landowner Address: Enter the address of the landowner.
	* Landowner Phone: Enter the phone number of the landowner.
3. Set dates:
	* Permission Start Date: Enter the date when the permission starts.
	* Permission End Date: Enter the date when the permission ends.
	* Hunting Season Start Date: Enter the date when the hunting season starts.
	* Hunting Season End Date: Enter the date when the hunting season ends.
4. Confirm landowner permission:
	* Landowner Permission: Select 'Yes' if the landowner has given permission for hunting.
5. Add notes (optional):
	* Hunter Notes: Provide any additional notes or comments as the hunter.
	* Landowner Notes: Provide any additional notes or comments as the landowner.
