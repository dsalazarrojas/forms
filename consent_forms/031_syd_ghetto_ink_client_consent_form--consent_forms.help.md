# SYD.Ghetto.INK Client CONSENT FORM - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client consent for artistic procedures and ensure that clients are aware and agree to the work done on them, maintaining transparency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title at the top. This is a required field where you can type a brief description of the consent or the procedure you are giving consent for.
2. Select the category for your consent from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the type of procedure you are giving consent for.
3. Provide a detailed description of the procedure you are giving consent for. This is an essential part of ensuring that we understand the scope of work and what you are agreeing to.
4. Enter the name of the artist performing the procedure. This is a required field to ensure that we can contact the artist directly.
5. Enter your contact number, so we can reach out to you with any further questions or clarifications.
6. Select the date when you gave your consent. This ensures that we keep track of when you agreed to the procedure.
7. Provide the name of the procedure you are giving consent for.
8. Select the date when you will undergo the procedure. This ensures that we coordinate the schedule correctly.
9. Provide the time you will undergo the procedure. This is essential for planning and coordination.
10. Sign with your artist's signature, indicating that you understand and agree to the procedure.
11. Finally, sign with your own signature, indicating that you have read and understood the consent form and agree to the procedure.
