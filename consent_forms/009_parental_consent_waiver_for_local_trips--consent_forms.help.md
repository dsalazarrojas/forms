# Parental Consent Waiver For Local Trips - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Parental Consent Waiver For Local Trips" form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child to participate in local trips. This form is a crucial document that helps ensure the child's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the trip details in the "Trip Details" field.
2. Provide your primary contact information in the "Guardians Information" field.
3. List any medical conditions your child may have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
4. List any allergies or sensitivities your child has in the "Allergy Information" field.
5. Enter the emergency contact information and relationship in the corresponding fields.
6. List any medical attention details, trip supervisors, and trip leaders that will be present during the trip.
7. Sign and submit the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`): Enter the trip details, including the date, time, location, and other essential information.
* **Guardians Information** (`guardian_info`): Enter your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Parental Contact** (`parental_contact`): Enter your primary contact information.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`): Enter your child's name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`): Select "I grant permission for my child to participate" or "I do not grant permission for my child to participate" to indicate your consent.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`): List any allergies or sensitivities your child has.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the emergency contact person's name, contact number, and relationship.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Indicate the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Attention** (`medical_attention`): Enter any medical attention details.
* **Medical Attention Details** (`medical_attention_details`): Enter any additional medical attention details.
* **Trip Supervisors** (`trip_supervisors`): List any trip supervisors that will be present during the trip.
* **Trip Supervisors 2** (`trip_supervisors2`): List any additional trip supervisors.
* **Trip Supervisors 3** (`trip_supervisors3`): List any additional trip supervisors.
* **Emergency Supervisors** (`emergency_supervisors`): List any emergency contact supervisors.
* **Medical Supervisors** (`medical_supervisors`): List any medical supervisors.
* **Trip Leaders** (`trip_leaders`): List any trip leaders that will be present during the trip.
* **Emergency Leaders** (`emergency_leaders`): List any emergency contact leaders.
* **Medical Leaders** (`medical_leaders`): List any medical leaders.
* **Trip Coordinators** (`trip_coordinators`): List any trip coordinators.
* **Emergency Coordinators** (`emergency_coordinators`): List any emergency contact coordinators.
* **Medical Coordinators** (`medical_coordinators`): List any medical coordinators.
* **Parent Signature** (`signature`): Sign and submit the form to confirm your consent.
