# Digital Inclusion Project Video Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Inclusion Project Video Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures participants understand and agree to the terms of the video recording process. By completing this form, participants are providing their consent to be part of the project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your project name in the "Project Name" field.
2.  Fill in your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
5.  Indicate whether you have given consent in the "Consent Given" field.
6.  Enter the date you gave consent in the "Consent Date" field.
7.  Provide a link to the video recording in the "Video Link" field.
8.  Enter your location in the "Participant Location" field.
9.  Add any additional video recording details in the "Video Recorder Details" field.
10.  Upload a participant photo in the "Participant Photo" field.
11.  Provide a signature for participant verification in the "Participant Signature" field.
12.  Read and agree to the "Video Recording Terms" in the "Video Recording Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the project you are participating in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if you have given consent.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you gave your consent.
* **Video Link** (`video_link`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide a link to the video recording.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, required: false): Enter your current location.
* **Video Recorder Details** (`video_recorder_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about the video recording.
* **Participant Photo** (`participant_photo`, note, required: false): This field is for uploading a participant photo.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required: false): This field is for obtaining participant signature.
* **Video Recording Terms** (`video_recording_terms`, text, required: false): Carefully read and agree to the terms of the video recording.
