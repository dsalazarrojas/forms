# massage_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The massage_consent_form is a user-facing form used to collect information about a client's massage preferences and consent for a massage therapy session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's information (page 1) with their details.
2. Select the type of massage you would like to receive (page 1).
3. Choose the length of the massage (page 1).
4. Select the areas of the body where you would like to receive the massage (page 1).
5. Choose the pressure level you prefer (page 1).
6. Select the frequency of the massage (page 1).
7. Enter the duration of the massage (page 1).
8. Enter the cost of the massage (page 1).
9. Choose the payment method (page 1).
10. Leave your signature (page 1).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: Enter your client's information.
	+ **Label**: client_information
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: no
* **What kind of massage**: Select the type of massage you would like to receive.
	+ **Label**: what_kind_of_massage
	+ **Type**: select_one
	+ **Options**: Swedish massage, Deep Tissue massage, Sports massage
* **Massage Length**: Enter the length of the massage in minutes.
	+ **Label**: massage_length
	+ **Type**: number
	+ **Required**: no
* **Massage Area**: Select the areas of the body where you would like to receive the massage.
	+ **Label**: massage_area
	+ **Type**: select_multiple
	+ **Options**: Back, Neck, Shoulders, Legs, Arms
* **Massage Pressure**: Select the pressure level you prefer.
	+ **Label**: massage_pressure
	+ **Type**: select_multiple
	+ **Options**: Gentle, Medium, Deep
* **How Often**: Select the frequency of the massage.
	+ **Label**: how_often
	+ **Type**: select_one
	+ **Options**: Once, Twice, Three or more times
* **Massage Duration**: Enter the duration of the massage in minutes.
	+ **Label**: massage_duration
	+ **Type**: number
	+ **Required**: no
* **Massage Cost**: Enter the cost of the massage.
	+ **Label**: massage_cost
	+ **Type**: number
	+ **Required**: no
* **How Will You Pay**: Choose the payment method.
	+ **Label**: how_will_you_pay
	+ **Type**: select_one
	+ **Options**: Cash, Credit card, Other (please specify)
* **Client Signature**: Leave your signature.
	+ **Label**: client_signature
	+ **Type**: text
	+ **Required**: no

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understood the form's purpose before completing it.
* This form should only be used for massage therapy sessions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask your massage therapist.
