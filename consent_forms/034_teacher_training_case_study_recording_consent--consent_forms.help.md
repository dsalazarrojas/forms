# Teacher Training Case Study Recording Consent - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from teachers regarding the recording of their training case studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, including all the required fields.
2. Enter your teacher name in the "Teacher Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Select the date of your training session in the "Session Date" field.
5. Select the time of your training session in the "Session Time" field.
6. Enter the link to the recording of your session in the "Recording Link" field.
7. Choose your response to the question "Consent Given" in the "Consent Given" field.
8. Enter any additional notes you have in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Answer the question "Did the teacher give consent for recording?" with either "Yes" or "No" in the "Did the teacher give consent for recording?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (text, required): Enter your name as a teacher.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Session Date** (date, required): Select the date of your training session.
* **Session Time** (time, required): Select the time of your training session.
* **Recording Link** (text, optional): Enter the link to the recording of your session.
* **Consent Given** (select one, optional): Check if you gave your consent for recording.
* **Additional Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes you have regarding your session.
* **Did the teacher give consent for recording?** (select one, required): Check if the teacher gave their consent for recording.
