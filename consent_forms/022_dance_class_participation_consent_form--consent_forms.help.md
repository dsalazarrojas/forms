# Dance Class Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain parent/guardian approval for a student to participate in a dance class. It ensures that parents/guardians are informed about the class, its risks, and their rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please answer all the questions carefully.
2. Make sure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
3. For fields with options, select only the relevant options for your situation.
4. When prompted, sign your name as proof of consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Consent
* **Parent Consent** (<label>): This is where you should provide your name and signature as a parent/guardian.
*   Please ensure that the name you provide is the name of a parent or guardian who is responsible for the student.

### 2. Student Information
* **Student Information** (<label>): This is where you should provide basic information about the student, including their name, and any other relevant details.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### 3. Guardian Information
* **Guardian Information** (<label>): This is where you should provide information about yourself as a parent/guardian.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

### 4. Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (<label>): This is where you should select any medical conditions that may affect the student's participation in the dance class.
*   Please select any medical conditions that may apply, such as allergies or asthma.

### 5. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (<label>): This is where you should provide information about an emergency contact person.
*   Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about the emergency contact person.

### 6. Medical Authorization
* **Medical Authorization** (<label>): This is where you should select any medical authorization that may be relevant to the student's participation in the dance class.
*   Please select any medical authorization that may apply, such as allergies or asthma.

### 7. Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (<label>): This is where you should provide information about payment for the dance class.
*   Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about payment.

### 8. Terms of Service
* **Terms of Service** (<label>): This is a note about the terms of service.
*   Please read and agree to the terms of service before proceeding.

### 9. Parent Signature
* **Parent Signature** (<label>): This is where you should sign your name as proof of consent.
*   Please sign your name as a parent/guardian to confirm your consent.

### 10. Parent Notes
* **Parent Notes** (<label>): This is a note for any additional information you would like to provide.
*   Please use this space for any additional information that may be relevant to the student's participation in the dance class.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by a parent/guardian, not the student.
