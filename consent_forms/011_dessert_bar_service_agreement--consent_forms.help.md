# Dessert Bar Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Dessert Bar Service Agreement" form is used to collect information from customers about their event or service needs related to a dessert bar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Describe your event details in the "Event Details" field.
3. Read and agree to the "Service Terms" field.
4. Select your approval in the "Approval" field by choosing "Yes" or "No".


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: This section is where you enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
	+ Please provide your name as the customer.
* **Event Details**: In this field, describe your event or service details, such as the date, time, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Service Terms**: Read the service terms carefully and agree to them if you accept the terms of the service.
* **Approval**: Select "Yes" to approve the service terms, or "No" if you do not approve them. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please contact the form's administrators for further assistance.
