# Eyelashes Extensions Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information and consent for eyelash extensions procedures. It helps our team understand client preferences, risks, and expectations, ensuring a safe and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer every question to the best of your ability.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "consent_form" question, indicating your consent to proceed with the eyelash extension procedure.
3. In the "client_info" field, please provide your full name, contact information, and any relevant medical history.
4. Choose your preferred eyelash type (classic, cluster, hybrid, or nano) from the "lashes_type" dropdown menu.
5. For "num_rows" and "num_lashes", enter the number of rows and number of lashes you desire, respectively. Note that these fields are not required but will help us better understand your needs.
6. Select your preferred lash technique (1, 2, or 3) from the "lash_technique" dropdown menu.
7. In "notes", provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
8. Select the status of your consent ("consent pending" or "consent received") in the "consent_status" dropdown menu.
9. Finally, schedule your follow-up visit by selecting a date in the "follow_up_visit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **consent_form** (select multiple): Are you consenting to undergo an eyelash extension procedure? Please select "Yes" to indicate your consent.
* **client_info** (text): Please provide your full name, contact information (phone number, email, etc.), and any relevant medical history that may affect your lash extension procedure.
* **lashes_type** (select one): Choose from the options: classic, cluster, hybrid, or nano lash.
* **num_rows** (number): If you want, you can specify the number of rows for your eyelashes.
* **num_lashes** (number): If you want, you can specify the number of lashes you desire.
* **lash_technique** (select one): Select from the options: lash technique 1, lash technique 2, or lash technique 3.
* **lash_technique_other** (text): If none of the above techniques suit your needs, please provide your preferred lash technique.
* **notes** (note): Leave any additional comments or questions you may have for our team.
* **consent_status** (select one): Indicate your consent status by selecting "consent pending" or "consent received".
* **follow_up_visit** (date): Schedule your follow-up visit to finalize your appointment.

By completing this form, you are giving us the necessary information to ensure a smooth and safe experience for you. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
