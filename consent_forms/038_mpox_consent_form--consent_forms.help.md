# Mpox Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Consent Form is designed to collect essential information from patients for vaccination and treatment consent. This form helps healthcare professionals understand a patient's preferences and requirements for medical procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the patient's details, including their first name, last name, date of birth, email, phone number, and patient ID.
2. Select the type of consent the patient has given (e.g., to vaccination or treatment).
3. Specify the date of the consent.
4. Choose the preferred contact method (email, phone, or in-person).
5. If applicable, provide additional vaccine information and the consent status.
6. Add any relevant notes for the patient's record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of consent the patient has given (e.g., to vaccination or treatment).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the consent.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the patient's preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, required/optional): Specify the patient's consent status (e.g., informed or not informed).
* **Vaccine Information** (`vaccine_info`, note, required/optional): Add any relevant vaccine information (if applicable).
* **Consent Note** (`consent_note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes for the patient's record.

Note: Fields marked as 'required' or 'optional' are self-explanatory and indicate if the field must be filled or not. Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately to ensure correct and up-to-date patient records.
