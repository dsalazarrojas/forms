# Utility Trailer Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from renters regarding the rental of utility trailers. The form is not intended to be used for other types of rentals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details as the renter, including your name and contact information.
2. Provide trailer information, such as the type of trailer you want to rent and any special features.
3. Choose the rental dates for the trailer.
4. Enter payment information, including the rent and deposit amount.
5. Confirm your acknowledgment of the rental terms.
6. Enter your address.
7. Select the type of trailer you want to rent.
8. Enter the start and end dates of the rental.
9. Enter the rent and deposit amounts.
10. Choose your preferred payment method and schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renter Details**: Enter your name and contact information as the renter.
* **Trailer Information**: Provide details about the trailer you want to rent, including any special features.
* **Rental Dates**: Choose the dates you want to rent the trailer for.
* **Payment Info**: Enter the rent and deposit amounts for the trailer.
* **Acknoweldgment**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the rental terms.
* **Renter Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Trailer Type**: Select the type of trailer you want to rent.
* **Rental Start Date**: Enter the start date of the rental.
* **Rental End Date**: Enter the end date of the rental.
* **Trailer Rent**: Enter the rent amount for the trailer.
* **Trailer Deposit**: Enter the deposit amount for the trailer.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the rental.
* **Payment Schedule**: Choose how you would like to pay for the rental.
