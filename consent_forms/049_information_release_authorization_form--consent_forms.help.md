# information_release_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect basic contact and demographic information about an individual for the purpose of authorization and identification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Double-check your entries for any errors or inaccuracies.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, **Text**, Optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, **Text, Optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, **Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, **Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, **Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of death** (`date_of_death`, **Date, Optional): Enter your date of death in the format MM/DD/YYYY (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, **Text, Optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, **Text, Optional): Enter the city where you reside.
