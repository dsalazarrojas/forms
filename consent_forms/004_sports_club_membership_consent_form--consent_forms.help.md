# Sports Club Membership Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Membership Consent Form is a form used to collect contact information and consent from individuals wishing to join a sports club.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields (first_name, last_name, and email) to ensure you can be contacted and receive updates from the club.
2. Provide a valid email address to receive important club information and communications.
3. If you have any issues or questions, feel free to reach out to the club administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional, required): Enter a valid email address to receive important club updates and communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information, although this is not a required field.
 
 ## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive important club updates and information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club administrators.
