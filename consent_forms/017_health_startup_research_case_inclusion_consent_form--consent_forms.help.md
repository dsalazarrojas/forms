# Health Startup Research Case Inclusion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from research participants for inclusion in health startup research cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Name` field with your real name.
2. Enter your `Date of Birth` in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Enter your `Email` address in the format `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your `Phone Number` in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
5. Fill in the `Address` field with your residential or mailing address.
6. Describe your current `Medical Condition` in the space provided.
7. Select the `Research Start Date` of the research case you are participating in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Research Case** (`research_case`, text, required): The title of the research case you are participating in.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Your real name, as it should appear on the consent form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): The date you were born, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address to contact you with regarding the research case.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Your residential or mailing address.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required): A description of your current medical condition.
* **Research Start Date** (`research_start_date`, date, required): The date when the research case started.
## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling out this form for a real research case and not as a test or exercise.
* Double-check that you have filled out the form for the correct research case.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your research coordinator or team lead.
