# Tattoo Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from clients before they receive a tattoo. It helps the artist understand the client's medical history, contact preferences, and tattoo placement to ensure a safe and successful tattooing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Client Details" section.
2. Provide information about your tattoo details, including the placement and size of the design.
3. Share any medical history that may be relevant to your tattooing experience.
4. List any emergency contacts that we should know about.
5. Choose the correct blood type from the options provided.
6. Note any allergies or sensitivities you have.
7. Submit the form to complete the consent process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required: false): Please fill in your personal details, such as your name and contact information.
- **Tattoo Details** (`tattoo_details`, text, required: false): Describe the design and details of the tattoo you wish to get.
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required: false): Let us know how you would like to be contacted during and after the tattooing process.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any medical conditions or history that may affect your tattooing experience.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): Provide information about emergency contacts, such as next of kin or medical professionals.
- **Tattoo Placement** (`tattoo_placement`, select_one, required: false): Choose the placement of the tattoo on your body.
- **Tattoo Size** (`tattoo_size`, number, required: false): Enter the size of the tattoo design in inches or other units of measurement.
- **Blood Types** (`blood_types`, text, required: true): Select your blood type from the options provided.
- **Allergies** (`allergies`, note, required: false): Note any allergies or sensitivities you have, such as latex or shellfish.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields, especially blood type.
- If you have any medical history, please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have allergies or sensitivities, let us know so we can take necessary precautions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
