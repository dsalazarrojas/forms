# Mothers And Children Acceptance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain acceptance of terms or conditions from mothers and their children. It's a way to ensure that both parties understand and agree to the terms being presented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the terms or conditions being presented.
2. If you are a mother, fill out the contact details section with your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
3. If you have a child, fill out the child name section with their name.
4. Review the completed form carefully to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mothers And Children Acceptance Form** (`parent_consent`, `number`, not required):
	+ This field is the title of the form, which indicates that it is a general acceptance form for mothers and their children.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, not required):
	+ This field is used to capture the contact details of the mother, including name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, `text`, not required):
	+ This field is used to capture the name of the child being accepted.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant authorities or administrators.
* This form should only be filled out once per family unit to avoid duplication.
