# Refugee Aid Volunteer Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers who will be working with refugees and providing aid. The purpose of this form is to gather contact and emergency contact information, assess the volunteer's understanding of risk and responsibility, and obtain their medical clearance and consent for emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5.  Enter the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
6.  Enter the phone number of the person you have designated as your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
7.  Describe your relationship with the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
8.  Enter your understanding of the risks and responsibilities associated with volunteering for this cause in the "Risk Understanding" field.
9.  Enter your medical clearance information in the "Medical Clearance" field.
10. Check the type of medical clearance you have (Yes/No) in the "Medical Clearance Type" field.
11. Enter the date your medical clearance was granted in the "Medical Clearance Date" field.
12. Provide any additional medical clearance details in the "Medical Clearance Details" field.
13. Indicate if you have any emergency procedures in place in the "Emergency Procedure" field.
14. Enter the expiration date of your medical clearance, if applicable, in the "Medical Clearance Expiration" field.
15. Agree to your emergency contact and procedures in the "Emergency Contact Agreement" field.
16. Acknowledge your assumption of risks associated with volunteering for this cause in the "Risk Assumption of Responsibility" fields.
17. Review and agree to all the information provided.
