# Brow Lamination & Tint Information And Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from clients before their brow lamination and tinting appointment. It ensures that clients are aware of the potential risks and benefits of the treatment and establishes a clear understanding of what they can expect during and after the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name, Date of Birth, Phone Number, and Email Address.
2. Select the service you want to receive (Brow Lamination Only, Tint Only, or Lamination and Tint).
3. Answer truthfully to the questions regarding your previous brow treatments, skin sensitivities, medications, and health conditions.
4. Indicate your pregnancy status and whether or not you have been informed of the risks and benefits of the treatment.
5. Read and select the options regarding your consent to the treatment, waiver of liability claims, and understanding of aftercare instructions.
6. Finally, sign and date the form by selecting the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or concerns.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for any further questions or concerns.
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, select_one, required): Select the service you want to receive (Brow Lamination Only, Tint Only, or Lamination and Tint).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your appointment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Have you had brow treatments before** (`prior_treatments`, select_one, required): Indicate whether or not you have had brow treatments before (including lamination or tinting).
* **Previous reactions to brow treatments** (`prior_reactions`, text, optional): If you have had previous reactions or sensitivities to brow treatments, please describe them here.
* **Known skin sensitivities or allergies** (`skin_sensitivities`, text, optional): If you have any known skin sensitivities or allergies (including product allergies), please list them here.
* **Current medications affecting skin** (`medications`, text, optional): If you are currently taking any topical medications, please list them here.
* **Pregnancy Status** (`pregnancy_status`, select_one, required): Indicate whether or not you are currently pregnant or nursing.
* **Do you have any skin conditions affecting brows** (`health_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any skin conditions that may affect the treatment, such as eczema, psoriasis, or rosacea, please describe them here.
* **I have been informed of the risks and benefits** (`informed_risks`, select_one, required): Indicate whether or not you have been informed of the risks and benefits of the treatment.
* **I consent to the brow lamination and tint treatment** (`consent_to_treat`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you consent to the treatment.
* **I acknowledge and waive liability claims** (`waiver_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you understand and accept all risks.
* **Aftercare instructions understood** (`aftercare_understood`, select_one, required): Select whether or not you understand and will follow the aftercare instructions.
* **Date of Agreement** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the current date to sign and date the form.
