# employment_verification_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment verification release form is designed to verify employment status and gather information from job applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the fields as accurately as possible.
2.  Ensure that you have answered all required fields (indicated by a asterisk *) and provide any necessary information.
3.  Double-check that your answers are correct and complete.
4.  Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Employment Verification** (1, text, required): Enter your current or previous employer's name. This field helps us understand your employment history.
*  **Business Address** (2, text, optional): Enter your current or previous employer's business address, if applicable.
*  **Job Title** (3, text, optional): Enter your current or previous job title. This field helps us understand your role and responsibilities.
*  **Employment Start Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date you started your current or previous job.
*  **Employment End Date** (5, date, optional): Enter the date you ended your current or previous job, if applicable.
*  **Reason for Leaving** (6, text, optional): Enter a brief reason for leaving your current or previous job, if applicable.
*  **Signature** (7, text, optional): Sign this form to confirm that the information provided is accurate and true.
*  **Date** (8, date, optional): Enter the date you signed this form.
*  **Job Applicant Name** (9, text, optional): Enter your name as it should appear on the form.
*  **Job Applicant Address** (10, text, optional): Enter your address, if you'd like to include it on the form.
*  **Job Title at Company** (11, text, optional): Enter your job title at your current or previous employer.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all required fields are accurately filled out before submitting the form.
*   Use correct and standard formatting for dates.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is correct and complete.
*   If you are unsure or have any questions, please contact the relevant authorities or your employer.
