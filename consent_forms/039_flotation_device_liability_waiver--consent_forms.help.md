# Flotation Device Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flotation Device Liability Waiver is a form that must be completed by participants before engaging in activities involving flotation devices. The form aims to inform participants of the associated risks and obtain their consent to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you are a parent or guardian signing on behalf of a minor, please complete the 'Parent or Guardian' section.
3. Ensure that you have read and understood the 'Terms and Conditions' section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Parent or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are a parent or guardian signing on behalf of the participant. If 'No', proceed to the next field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address where you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Participant Weight** (`participant_weight`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your weight.
* **Participant Height** (`participant_height`, number, required): Enter your height in inches or feet.
* **Participant Height Unit** (`participant_height_unit`, select_multiple, required): Select the unit of measurement for your height (ft or in).
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant Safety Question 1** (`participant_safety_question_1`, note, required): Answer the first safety question truthfully.
* **Participant Safety Question 2** (`participant_safety_question_2`, note, required): Answer the second safety question truthfully.
* **Participant Safety Question 3** (`participant_safety_question_3`, note, required): Answer the third safety question truthfully.
* **Participant Safety Question 4** (`participant_safety_question_4`, note, required): Answer the fourth safety question truthfully.
* **Participant Safety Question 5** (`participant_safety_question_5`, note, optional): Answer the fifth safety question truthfully.
* **Participant Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, text, optional): If applicable, list any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Participant Medical Devices** (`participant_medical_devices`, text, optional): If applicable, list any medical devices you are using during the activity.
* **Participant Medical Notes** (`participant_medical_notes`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional medical notes.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name, acknowledging that you have read and understood the form.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_or_guardian_signature`, text, required): Sign your name, acknowledging that you have read and understood the form on behalf of the participant.
* **Waiver** (`waiver`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Read and understand the terms and conditions carefully.
