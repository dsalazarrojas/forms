# Digital Literacy Workshop Participation Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for the Digital Literacy Workshop, ensuring that each participant has provided their consent and relevant details for emergency contact and accessibility needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Contact Number.
5. For Emergency Contact information:
   - Enter the First Name and Last Name of your emergency contact.
   - Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact.
   - Enter the contact number.
6. If necessary, describe your accessibility needs in the Accessibility Needs field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and notifications.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number where you can be reached in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Choose the type of relationship you have with your emergency contact (e.g., Mom or Dad, Brother or Sister, Spouse or Partner, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements for the workshop, please describe them in this field. This will help us to provide an inclusive experience for you.
