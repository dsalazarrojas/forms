# Service Cancellation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users complete the Service Cancellation Consent Form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your service description in the "service_description" field.
2. Select the reason for service cancellation from the "client_consent" field.
3. Check the box in the "terms_and_conditions" field to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
4. Enter the date of service in the "date_of_service" field.
5. Enter the time of service in the "time_of_service" field.
6. Provide the reason for cancellation in the "reason_for_cancellation" field.
7. Enter your name in the "client_name" field.
8. Enter your contact information in the "contact_number" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_cancellation_consent** (`service_cancellation_consent`, text, optional): A short description of the service that is being cancelled.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, text, optional): A brief explanation of the service being cancelled.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, select_one, optional): Select the reason for service cancellation. Options are:
	+ Service Cancellation
	+ Refund
	+ Other
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box to acknowledge that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): The date on which the service was provided.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): The time at which the service was provided.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`, text, optional): A brief explanation of the reason for service cancellation.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): The name of the client.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): The phone number of the client.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): The email address of the client.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide a clear and concise service description and reason for cancellation.
* Be specific when entering the date and time of service.
* Check the box in the "terms and conditions" field to acknowledge that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* Enter accurate contact information to ensure that the client can be reached.
