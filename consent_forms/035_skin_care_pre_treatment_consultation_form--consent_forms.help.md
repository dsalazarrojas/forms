# Skin Care Pre Treatment Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's skin care pre-treatment consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2.  Select the options that apply to you from the provided lists.
3.  Enter any additional information that may be required in the text fields.
4.  Sign the form digitally to confirm your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information (1):** Enter your name, email, and contact details.
*   **Skin Condition (2):** Select all the skin conditions that apply to you. These include Acne, Aging, Eczema, Hyper-pigmentation, Rosacea, and Scars.
*   **Medical History (3):** Please provide a detailed account of your medical history, including any medical conditions you've been diagnosed with or are currently being treated for.
*   **Medical Condition (4):** Check if you have any medical conditions, including Yes or No.
*   **Treatment Goal (5):** Select all the treatment goals that apply to you. These include Improve skin appearance, Reduce skin sensitivity, and Prevent skin irritation.
*   **Skin Type (6):** Select the type of skin you have. Options include Normal, Dry, Oily, or Combination.
*   **Skin Tone (7):** Select the tone of your skin. Options include Pale, Fair, Medium, Tan, or Dark.
*   **Allergies (8):** Select all the allergies that apply to you, including None, Fragrances, Dye, and Medications.
*   **Skincare Products (9):** Select all the skincare products you use, including None, True, Cleanser, Moisturizer, Sunscreen, and Exfoliator.
*   **Sun Protection (10):** Select all the sun protection methods you use, including None, Daily, Occasional, or Always.
*   **Smoking Habits (11):** Select your smoking habits, including Never, Socially, Occasionally, or Frequently.
*   **Sun Protection Methods (12):** Select all the sun protection methods you use, including None, Clothing, Sunscreen, Hat, or Sunglasses.
*   **Hair Color (13):** Select your hair color.
*   **Hair Type (14):** Select your hair type.
*   **Eye Color (15):** Select your eye color.
*   **Skin Protection (16):** Select all the skin protection methods you use, including None, Daily, Occasional, or Always.
*   **Medical Record (17):** Check if you have a medical record.
*   **Medications (18):** Check if you are taking any medications.
*   **Other Medical History (19):** Please provide any other medical history that may be relevant.
*   **Contact Details (20):** Enter your contact details.
*   **Emergency Contact (21):** Enter the contact details of your emergency contact.
*   **Treatment Plan (22):** Select all the treatment plans that apply to you. Options include None, Short-term, Medium-term, or Long-term.
*   **Treatment Outcome (23):** Select the treatment outcome you expect, including None, Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, or Not Satisfied at all.
*   **Client Signature (24):** Sign the form digitally to confirm your information.
*   **Date (25):** Please enter the current date.

Note that this form requires a valid signature and date. Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can provide you with the best possible service.
