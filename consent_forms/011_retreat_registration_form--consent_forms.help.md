# Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retreat Registration Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential participant information, emergency contact details, and payment information for a secure and successful retreat experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the participant information section, providing your personal details such as participant name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
2.  In the emergency contact section, enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
3.  In the medical information section, disclose any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the retreat.
4.  Enter any allergy information that may impact your retreat experience.
5.  In the payment section, choose your preferred payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or check) and enter the payment amount in the respective currency (USD, EUR, or RUB).
6.  Provide any additional participant and emergency contact notes that will help us better understand your needs and requirements.
7.  Finally, review and submit your form by selecting the "Submit" option. If you need to submit via another method, choose "Other" and provide the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any known medical conditions that may impact your participation in the retreat.
*   **Allergy Information** (`allergy_info`, text, required): List any allergies or sensitivities you may have that could affect your participation.
*   **Emergency Information** (`emergency_info`, text, required): Enter any relevant emergency information that may be required.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
*   **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount in the selected currency.
*   **Payment Currency** (`payment_currency`, select_one, required): Select the currency of your payment (USD, EUR, or RUB).
*   **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation in the retreat.
*   **Emergency Contact Notes** (`emergency_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about your emergency contact person that will help us better understand their needs.
*   **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
*   **Submit Form** (`submit`, select_one, required): Review and confirm your submission by selecting the "Submit" option. If you need to submit via another method, choose "Other" and provide the necessary details.
