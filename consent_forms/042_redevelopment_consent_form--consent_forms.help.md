# Redevelopment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The redevelopment consent form is a crucial document that allows project owners to provide their consent for redevelopment projects. It ensures that all necessary information is captured accurately and securely. This form is a critical component of the project approval process and should be filled out carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, using your best judgment.
3. Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authority to fill out this form.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Owner Contact Info
* **Owner Contact Info** (`owner_contact_info`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is intended to capture the contact information of the project owner. Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number, to ensure that you can be reached for any further inquiries or updates.

### Project Details
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required/optional: false): This field is meant to capture a brief description of the project. Provide a concise and accurate summary of the project, including its purpose, scope, and any relevant background information.

### Owner Approval
* **Owner Approval** (`owner_approval`, `select_one`, required/true): This field requires the project owner to confirm their approval for the project. Select 'True' if you approve of the project, and 'False' if you do not.

### Project Manager
* **Project Manager** (`project_manager`, `text`, required/true): The project manager is an essential part of the project team. Enter the name and contact information of the project manager to ensure they are aware of the project's progress and any updates.

### Property Address
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required/true): This field is crucial in determining the location of the project. Enter the exact address of the property to ensure accurate mapping and tracking.

### Project Start Date
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): If applicable, enter the start date of the project. This will help in tracking and monitoring the project's progress.

### Project End Date
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, `date`, required/optional: false): If applicable, enter the end date of the project. This will help in determining the project's completion status.

### Project Status
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the current status of the project from the options provided: Active, On-Hold, Completed, or Cancelled. Ensure this reflects the accurate status of the project.

### Project Notes
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, required/optional: false): This field is meant for any additional comments or notes related to the project. Use it to capture any relevant information that doesn't fit in the other fields.

### Owner Signature
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, `text`, required/true): Finally, please sign and date this form to confirm your consent for the project. This is a critical step in the project approval process.
