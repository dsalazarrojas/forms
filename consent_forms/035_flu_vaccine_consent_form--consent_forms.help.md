# flu_vaccine_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flu_vaccine_consent_form is a comprehensive form used for recording consent and vaccination information for flu vaccines. It helps in tracking vaccination records and preventing potential outbreaks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, age, contact number, and email.
2. Provide your consent to receive a flu vaccine, indicating whether you are 'True', 'False', or 'Undecided'.
3. Specify the flu season you are getting vaccinated for (Current, Upcoming, or None).
4. Enter the date and time of vaccination.
5. Provide details about the vaccine you are receiving, including the type, manufacturer, lot, and expiration date.
6. Fill in the details of the healthcare provider or facility that administered the vaccine.
7. Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1, Patient Information)**: Enter the patient's name, age, and contact number. This information is essential for tracking and follow-up.
* **Patient Name (2, patient name)**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Age (3, age)**: Enter the patient's age in years.
* **Contact Number (4, contact number)**: Enter the patient's contact number.
* **Email (5, email)**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Consent (6, consent)**: Select 'True', 'False', or 'Undecided' to confirm your consent to receive a flu vaccine.
* **Flu Season (7, flu season)**: Choose the flu season you are getting vaccinated for (Current, Upcoming, or None).
* **Vaccination Date (8, vaccination date)**: Enter the date of the vaccination.
* **Doctor Name (9, doctor name)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine.
* **Doctor Contact (10, doctor contact)**: Enter the contact number or email of the healthcare provider.
* **Medical Facility (11, medical facility)**: Enter the name of the facility where the vaccine was administered.
* **Signature (12, signature)**: Sign the form to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Flu Vaccine Type (13, flu vaccine type)**: Choose the type of flu vaccine received (Influenza type A, B, or C).
* **Flu Vaccine Doses (14, flu vaccine doses)**: Choose the number of doses administered (1, 2, or 3).
* **Flu Vaccine Manufacturer (15, flu vaccine manufacturer)**: Enter the name of the vaccine manufacturer.
* **Flu Vaccine Lot (16, flu vaccine lot)**: Enter the lot number of the vaccine.
* **Flu Vaccine Expiration Date (17, flu vaccine expiration date)**: Enter the expiration date of the vaccine.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By (18, flu vaccine adminstered by)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who administered the vaccine.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By Nurse (19, flu vaccine adminstering nurse)**: Enter the name of the nurse who administered the vaccine.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered At (20, flu vaccine adminstered at)**: Choose the location where the vaccine was administered (Hospital, Clinic, or Doctor's office).
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By Doctor (21, flu vaccine adminstered by doctor)**: Enter the name of the doctor who administered the vaccine.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By Clinic (22, flu vaccine adminstered by clinic)**: Enter the name of the clinic where the vaccine was administered.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered By (23, flu vaccine adminstered by)**: This field should be used for other administration personnel not listed above.
* **Flu Vaccine Administered At Location (24, flu vaccine adminstered at location)**: Enter the location where the vaccine was administered.
* **Additional Information**: This field should be used for any additional comments or information.
