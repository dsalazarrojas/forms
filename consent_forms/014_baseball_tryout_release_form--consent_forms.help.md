# Baseball Tryout Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Tryout Release Form is a document that participants must complete before participating in a baseball tryout. This form is used to gather essential information about each participant, their emergency contacts, and medical conditions, as well as to obtain their release and waiver agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure that you answer all required questions truthfully.
3.  Review all sections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, `phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
*   **Parent/Guardian full name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian if you are under 18.
*   **Relationship to participant** (`parent_relationship`, `text`, optional): If you are a parent or guardian, describe your relationship to the participant.
*   **Parent/Guardian email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
*   **Parent/Guardian phone** (`parent_phone`, `phone`, optional): Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
*   **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
*   **Relationship to participant** (`emergency_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the participant.
*   **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_phone`, `phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
*   **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): If you have any medical conditions, please select "Yes - Please Describe" and describe the condition in the field below.
*   **If yes, describe the condition** (`condition_description`, `text`, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have, as mentioned above.
*   **Are you currently taking medications?** (`medications`, `select_one`, required): If you are currently taking medications, please select "Yes - Please Describe" and describe the medications in the field below.
*   **If yes, describe medications** (`medication_description`, `text`, optional): Describe any medications you are currently taking.
*   **I acknowledge that baseball involves risks...** (`risk_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Acknowledge that baseball involves risks.
*   **...including injuries from thrown balls, collisions, and impacts** (`risk_acknowledgment`, not applicable): This is a statement.
*   **I release the team and organization from liability for injuries during tryouts** (`release_liability`, `select_one`, required): If you are willing to release the team from liability, please select "I Agree".
*   **I assume all risks associated with participation in these tryouts** (`assumption_risk`, `select_one`, required): Assume all risks associated with participation.
*   **I confirm I have medical clearance to participate** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have medical clearance to participate.
*   **Parent/Guardian: I consent to participant's involvement and acknowledge all terms** (`parent_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): If you are a parent or guardian, confirm that you consent to the participant's involvement and acknowledge the terms.
