# AI Clinical Decision Model Testing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI Clinical Decision Model Testing Consent Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions under which you will be participating in AI clinical decision model testing. This form is used to obtain your consent to use your medical data for AI model training and to outline your rights as a participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please read this form carefully before providing your responses.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the researcher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age**: Enter your age in years.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact phone number for follow-up.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of visit**: Enter the date you plan to visit for this study.
* **Do you consent to participate in AI clinical decision model testing?**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ No, I do not consent
	+ I need more information
* **Current symptoms**: Select all symptoms that apply from the list:
	+ Fever
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Cough
	+ Pain
	+ Nausea
	+ Dizziness
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Pain level**: Rate your pain level from 0 to 10, where 0 is no pain and 10 is the worst pain imaginable.
* **Current medications**: List all medications you are currently taking, including dosages.
* **Known allergies**: List any known allergies or reactions you have to medications or materials.
* **Relevant medical history**: Describe any relevant medical conditions, previous surgeries, or medical procedures you have had.
* **Do you consent to your medical data being used for AI model training?**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I consent
	+ No, I do not consent
	+ Only for this study
* **Preferred data retention period**: Choose how long you would like your data to be stored:
	+ For duration of study only
	+ Up to 1 year after study
	+ Up to 5 years after study
	+ Indefinitely for research purposes
* **I understand I have the right to withdraw consent at any time**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes, I understand
	+ No, I need clarification
* **Would you like to be contacted about future studies?**: Choose one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Additional comments or concerns**: If you have any questions or concerns about the study, please provide them here.
* **Digital signature**: Type your full name to sign this form.
* **Date of signature**: Enter the date you are signing this form.

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for a thorough medical consultation with a healthcare professional. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact the researcher.
