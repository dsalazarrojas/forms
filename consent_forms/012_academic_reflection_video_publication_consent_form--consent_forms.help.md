# Academic Reflection Video Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to grant permission for the use and publication of your video reflections in academic settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant full name** in the text field.
2. Enter your **Email address** in the email format.
3. Enter your **Phone number** (if available).
4. Select your **Role** from the dropdown menu:
	* Student
	* Faculty
	* Researcher
	* Guest Speaker
	* Staff
5. Enter your **Institution or department** name.
6. Provide a brief **Title or topic of the video reflection**.
7. Enter the **Date of video recording** using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
8. Read and agree to the **Publication Consent** section, which outlines the terms of use and publication of your video reflection.
9. Select which platforms you **Consent to having your video published on** (select all that apply):
	* Institutional website
	* YouTube
	* Vimeo
	* Learning management system
	* Social media channels
	* Internal use only
10. Decide if you **Consent to still images from the video being used in promotional or educational materials** (Yes or No).
11. Choose if you require **Approval of the final edited video before publication** (Yes or No).
12. Select the **Duration of consent**:
	* Indefinitely
	* 1 year
	* 2 years
	* Until I revoke consent in writing
13. Confirm that you **Understand you may withdraw consent before publication** by contacting the project coordinator (Yes or No).
14. Enter the **Date of consent** using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
15. If applicable, specify any **Special conditions or restrictions** on the use of your video reflection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant full name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address as per your official contact information.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Role**: Select your role in the academic setting (e.g., Student, Faculty, Researcher, etc.).
* **Institution or department**: Enter the name of your institution or department.
* **Title or topic of the video reflection**: Enter a brief title or topic of your video reflection.
* **Date of video recording**: Enter the date of the video recording in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Publication Consent**: Read and agree to the terms of use and publication of your video reflection.
* **Consent to having your video published on**: Choose which platforms you consent to publishing on.
* **Consent to still images from the video**: Decide if you consent to still images being used in promotional or educational materials.
* **Do you require approval of the final edited video before publication**: Choose if you require approval of the final edited video before publication.
* **Duration of consent**: Choose the duration of your consent.
* **Understand you may withdraw consent before publication**: Confirm that you understand you may withdraw consent before publication.
* **Date of consent**: Enter the date of your consent in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Special conditions or restrictions**: Specify any special conditions or restrictions on the use of your video reflection.
