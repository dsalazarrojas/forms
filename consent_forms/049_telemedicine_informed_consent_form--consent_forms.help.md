# Telemedicine Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients before providing telemedicine services. It is a crucial document that ensures patients are aware of the services offered and the expectations of the healthcare provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Info**: Enter your name, address, and contact information as prompted. This information is used to identify you and ensure that you can be contacted if needed.
2. Complete the **Medical Records** field with any relevant medical history or information that is pertinent to your current health status.
3. In the **Medical Conditions** field, select all applicable conditions that apply to you. This will help us provide you with the most effective care.
4. If applicable, select the **Medications** you are currently taking.
5. If you have any **Allergies**, please list them in this field.
6. In the **Medical History** field, provide any relevant medical history that may be useful for your care.
7. For your **Emergency Contact**, please provide the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
8. Provide the contact information of your **Emergency Contact** in the field below.
9. In the **Consent Status** field, select the option that best indicates your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the telemedicine service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name and other contact information as prompted.
- **Medical Records** (`text`, required): This field is used to capture any relevant medical history or information that may affect your care.
- **Medical Conditions** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable conditions that apply to you. Examples include chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension.
- **Medications** (`select_one`, required): If you are taking any medication, please select from the list.
- **Allergies** (`text`, optional): List any allergies you may have.
- **Medical History** (`note`, optional): This field is used to capture any relevant medical history that may be useful for your care.
- **Emergency Contact** (`text`, required): Please provide the name of the person you have listed as your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (`text`, required): Please provide the contact information of your emergency contact.
- **Consent Status** (`select_one`, required): Please select the option that best indicates your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the telemedicine service.
 
 ## Tips
- Please review this form carefully before using it.
- This form is a required document for telemedicine services and must be completed before starting treatment.
- The information provided will be used to create your medical record.
- If you are unsure about any part of this form, please contact your healthcare provider.
