# Baseball Camp Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Camp Waiver Form is a crucial document that provides the camp with essential information about each participant. This form helps the camp to ensure the well-being and safety of the participant during the camp sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter the participant's full name in the "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Enter the participant's date of birth in the "Participant Date of Birth" field in MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Select the participant's gender from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter the parent or guardian's full name in the "Parent or Guardian Full Name" field.
5. Enter the parent or guardian's email address in the "Parent or Guardian Email" field.
6. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent or Guardian Phone" field.
7. Enter the participant's home address in the "Home Address" field.
8. Enter the emergency contact's full name in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
9. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
10. Specify the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
11. Select the camp session the participant is attending from the drop-down menu.
12. Enter the name of the participant's medical insurance provider in the "Medical Insurance Provider" field.
13. If applicable, enter the participant's medical policy number in the "Policy Number" field.
14. If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies the participant has in the "Medical Conditions or Allergies" field.
15. If applicable, list any current medications the participant is taking in the "Current Medications" field.
16. If applicable, specify any physical limitations the participant has in the "Physical Limitations" field.
17. If applicable, enter the name of the participant's primary care physician in the "Primary Care Physician Name" field.
18. If applicable, enter the doctor's phone number in the "Physician Phone" field.
19. Select "I understand and accept the risks" for the assumption of risk section.
20. Select "I agree to release liability" for the liability release section.
21. Select "I authorize medical treatment" for the medical treatment authorization section.
22. Select "Yes, I consent" for the photo and video consent section.
23. If applicable, specify who can pick up the participant in the "Authorized for Pickup" field.
24. Select "Yes, I give permission" for the transportation permission section.
25. Select "I agree to follow all rules" for the code of conduct section.
26. Sign your name and enter your full name as the parent or guardian in the "Parent or Guardian Signature" field.
27. Enter the date you signed the form in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: This is the field where you will enter the participant's full name.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: This is the field where you will enter the participant's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: This is the field where you will select the participant's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: This is the field where you will enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: This is the field where you will enter your email address for camp communications.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: This is the field where you will enter your phone number for the camp to contact you.
* **Home Address**: This is the field where you will enter the participant's home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the field where you will enter the emergency contact's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: This is the field where you will enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: This is the field where you will specify the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
* **Camp Session**: This is the field where you will select the camp session the participant is attending.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: This is the field where you will enter the name of the participant's medical insurance provider.
* **Policy Number**: This is the field where you will enter the participant's medical policy number if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: This is the field where you will list any medical conditions or allergies the participant has.
* **Current Medications**: This is the field where you will list any medications the participant is taking if applicable.
* **Physical Limitations**: This is the field where you will specify any physical limitations the participant has if applicable.
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: This is the field where you will enter the name of the participant's primary care physician if applicable.
* **Physician Phone**: This is the field where you will enter the doctor's phone number if applicable.
* **Assumption of Risk**: This is the field where you will select "I understand and accept the risks" to acknowledge the risks associated with baseball activities.
* **Liability Release**: This is the field where you will select "I agree to release liability" to authorize the camp to release liability for ordinary negligence.
* **Medical Treatment Authorization**: This is the field where you will select "I authorize medical treatment" to allow the camp to provide medical treatment in case of an emergency.
* **Photo and Video Consent**: This is the field where you will select "Yes, I consent" to allow the camp to use photos or videos of the participant.
* **Authorized for Pickup**: This is the field where you will specify who can pick up the participant if applicable.
* **Transportation Permission**: This is the field where you will select "Yes, I give permission" to allow the camp to transport the participant if needed.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement**: This is the field where you will select "I agree to follow all rules" to agree to the participant's code of conduct.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: This is the field where you will sign your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date**: This is the field where you will enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, please contact the camp staff for clarification.
* The camp staff will review and verify the information provided before allowing the participant to attend the camp.
