# Virtual Field Trip Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you complete the Virtual Field Trip Permission Form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name and guardian's name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the student's and guardian's email and phone numbers in the respective fields.
3. Enter the student's address and guardian's address in the respective fields.
4. Select the student's attendance status (Always, Sometimes, or Never) from the dropdown menu.
5. Enter the emergency contact name, phone number, and relationship to the student in the respective fields.
6. Enter the school name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
7. Enter the trip details, dates, time, duration, location, objectives, and supervision information in the respective fields.
8. Add any additional emergency contact message if required.
9. Finally, review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter the guardian's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, required): Enter the student's home address.
* **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`, `text`, required): Enter the guardian's home address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the guardian's phone number (if available).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the student.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the school name.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the school email.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the school phone number.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's attendance status for the trip.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, `text`, optional): Add an additional emergency contact message if required.
* **Emergency Contact Message** (`emergency_contact_message`, `text`, optional): Add an additional emergency contact message if required.
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `text`, required): Enter the trip details.
* **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, `date`, required): Enter the trip dates.
* **Trip Time** (`trip_time`, `time`, required): Enter the trip time.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the trip duration.
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, `text`, required): Enter the trip location.
* **Trip Objectives** (`trip_objectives`, `text`, required): Enter the trip objectives.
* **Trip Supervision** (`trip_supervision`, `text`, required): Enter the trip supervision information.
* **Trip Risk** (`trip_risk`, `text`, required): Enter the trip risk information.
