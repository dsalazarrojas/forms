# third-party-release-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Third Party Release Form is used to gather necessary information about a third party involved in a particular project or activity. This form ensures that you collect all relevant details, including their name, address, phone number, email, relationship to the project, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill out the Third Party Name field with the third party's name.
- Enter the third party's address in the Third Party Address field.
- Provide the third party's phone number in the Third Party Phone field.
- Enter the third party's email in the Third Party Email field.
- Select either 'Yes' or 'No' in the Third Party Relationship field to indicate their connection to the project.
- If applicable, sign and date the Third Party Signature Date field.
- Check the Third Party Agreement field for any additional information or terms related to the project.
- Sign and date the Assignee Signature Date field to confirm your acceptance of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Third Party Name** (required, text): Fill in the name of the third party involved in the project.
- **Third Party Address** (required, text): Enter the address of the third party.
- **Third Party Phone** (required, text): Enter the third party's phone number.
- **Third Party Email** (required, email): Enter the third party's email address.
- **Third Party Relationship** (required, select one): Indicate if the third party is connected to the project by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- **Third Party Signature Date** (optional, date): If applicable, sign and date this field if required by the project's terms.
- **Third Party Agreement** (required, note): Review and read any relevant terms or agreements related to the project.
- **Assignee Signature Date** (required, date): Sign and date this field to confirm your acceptance of the terms.
