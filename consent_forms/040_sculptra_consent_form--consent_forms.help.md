# Sculptra Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sculptra Consent Form is a medical consent form designed to collect essential information from patients before treatment. It helps medical professionals understand the patient's medical history, allergies, and consent to treatment, ensuring a safe and effective treatment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your patient's demographic information, including their name, date of birth, and medical history.
2. Provide information about any allergies or medications you are taking.
3. Select your consent to treatment, which may include multiple options.
4. Specify the treatment area and procedure required.
5. Enter your expected outcome and any medical/surgical history.
6. Finally, sign and date the form, and choose whether you sign as the patient, doctor, or witness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Medical Condition**: Describe your medical condition, if any.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including previous illnesses or treatments.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medications**: List the medications you are currently taking.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medications Allergies**: List any allergies you have to medications.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Medical/Surgical History**: Describe any previous medical or surgical procedures you have undergone.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Consent to Treatment**: Select all applicable options to indicate your consent to treatment.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: false

- **Treatment Area**: Specify the area of treatment required.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Procedure**: Describe the procedure required.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Expected Outcome**: Describe the expected outcome of the treatment.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Signature**: Choose whether you sign as the patient, doctor, or witness.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your doctor or medical professional.
