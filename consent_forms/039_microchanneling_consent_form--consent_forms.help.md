# Microchanneling Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Microchanneling Consent Form is a document that must be completed by clients before undergoing microchanneling procedures. It captures essential information about the client, the procedure, and contact details for aftercare instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **client_name**: Enter the client's name.
2.  **contact_info**: Provide the client's contact information.
3.  **consent_given**: Select "Consent" to confirm that the client has been informed.
4.  **procedure_details**: If applicable, provide details about the procedure.
5.  **aftercare**: Provide aftercare instructions.
6.  **signature**: Sign and date the form.
7.  **time_signed**: Enter the time signed.
8.  **location**: Enter the location where the form was signed.
9.  **contact_name**: Enter the contact person's name.
10. **contact_email**: Enter the contact person's email.
11. **contact_phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
12.  **contact_phone_type**: Select the type of phone number for the contact person.
13.  **aftercare_instructions**: Provide aftercare instructions.
14.  **contact_phone_ext_type**: Not applicable.
15.  **client_email**: Not applicable.
16.  **client_phone**: Not applicable.
17.  **client_ext_phone**: Not applicable.
