# online_peer_feedback_sharing_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_peer_feedback_sharing_consent_form is used to collect participant information and obtain their consent for sharing feedback in online research studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information in the Participant Information section.
2. Choose how you want your feedback to be shared in the Share Feedback section.
3. Provide more details about your feedback in the Feedback Details section.
4. Enter your email address in the Email Address section.
5. Select your consent preference in the Feedback Sharing Consent section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (1)**: Fill in your participant information, such as your name, contact details, etc.
* **Share Feedback (2)**: Select how you want your feedback to be shared. You can choose from the options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Feedback Details (3)**: Provide more details about the feedback you have provided, if you want to explain your answers.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback Sharing Consent (5)**: Select your preference for sharing your feedback. You can choose between 'Consent' or 'Refuse'.


## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all the required fields, marked as 'true' in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* If you would like to withdraw your consent, please contact us with your request.
