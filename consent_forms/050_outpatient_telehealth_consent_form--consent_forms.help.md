# Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outpatient Telehealth Consent Form is designed to collect necessary information from patients for outpatient telehealth services. This form helps healthcare providers gather important patient data and obtain consents for services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided options to select the correct response.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Information**
*   **Patient Information (`patient_information`, `text`, required: false)**: Please provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
### 2. **Consent to Services**
*   **Consent to Services (`consent_to_services`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Indicate your consent to participate in telehealth services.
### 3. **Telehealth Agreement**
*   **Telehealth Agreement (`telehealth_agreement`, `note`, required: false)**: This section is for any additional comments or agreements related to telehealth services.
### 4. **Contact Information**
*   **Contact Information (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your emergency contact's name, relationship, and contact information.
### 5. **Emergency Contact**
*   **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the name, relationship, and contact information of your emergency contact.
### 6. **Consent to Emergency Contact**
*   **Consent to Emergency Contact (`consent_to_emergency_contact`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Indicate your consent to share your emergency contact information with your healthcare provider.
### 7. **Telehealth Service Agreement**
*   **Telehealth Service Agreement (`telehealth_service_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select all services you are interested in or consent to participating in.
### 8. **Medical History**
*   **Medical History (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide any relevant medical history or conditions.
### 9. **Allergies**
*   **Allergies (`allergies`, `text`, required: false)**: List any allergies you have.
### 10. **Medications**
*   **Medications (`medications`, `text`, required: false)**: List any current medications you are taking.
### 11. **Medical Procedures**
*   **Medical Procedures (`medical_procedures`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide any relevant medical procedures you have undergone.
### 12. **Medical Conditions**
*   **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false)**: List any current medical conditions.
### 13. **Insurance Coverage**
*   **Insurance Coverage (`insurance_coverage`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your insurance information.
### 14. **Billing Information**
*   **Billing Information (`billing_info`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your billing information.
### 15. **Electronic Access**
*   **Electronic Access (`electronic_access`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select how you prefer to access your medical records or communicate with your healthcare provider.
### 16. **Email**
*   **Email (`email`, `email`, required: false)**: Provide your email address.
### 17. **Phone**
*   **Phone (`phone`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your phone number.
### 18. **Fax**
*   **Fax (`fax`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your fax number.
### 19. **Address**
*   **Address (`address`, `text`, required: false)**: Provide your address.
### 20. **Patient Consent**
*   **Patient Consent (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Indicate your consent to participate in telehealth services.
### 21. **Provider Consent**
*   **Provider Consent (`provider_consent`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Indicate your consent for your healthcare provider to access your medical records.
### 22. **Provider Agreement**
*   **Provider Agreement (`provider_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select all agreements you agree to with your healthcare provider.
### 23. **Patient Agreement**
*   **Patient Agreement (`patient_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select all agreements you agree to as a patient.
### 24. **Medical Facility Agreement**
*   **Medical Facility Agreement (`medical_facility_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select all agreements you agree to with the medical facility.
### 25. **Telehealth Agreement**
*   **Telehealth Agreement (`telehealth_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select all agreements you agree to with the telehealth services provider.
