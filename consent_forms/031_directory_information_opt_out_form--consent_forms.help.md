# Directory Information Opt Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Directory Information Opt Out Form is designed to collect consent from individuals to opt-out of directory information sharing. This form ensures that their personal data is not shared or used without their explicit consent.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, you will need to provide some basic information to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing. Please fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Directory Information Opt-Out** (`directory_information_opt_out`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise statement indicating your intention to opt-out of directory information sharing.
2. **Confirm Directory Information** (`confirm_directory_information`, text, required): Confirm that you have opted out of directory information by entering the same statement you entered in the first field.
3. **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`, text, required): Enter a phone number to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing.
4. **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`, text, required): Enter an address to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing.
5. **Confirm Name** (`confirm_name`, text, required): Enter your name to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing.
6-9. **Confirm Other Info** (`confirm_other`, text, required): Enter any other information that you would like to share with us to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Directory Information Opt-Out** (`directory_information_opt_out`): This field is where you enter a clear and concise statement indicating your intention to opt-out of directory information sharing. Please enter a simple 'yes' or 'no' statement.
* **Confirm Directory Information** (`confirm_directory_information`): This field is a repeat of the previous field to ensure that you have correctly understood the implications of opting out of directory information sharing.
* **Confirm Phone Number** (`confirm_phone_number`): This field is where you enter your phone number to confirm that you are the person opting out of directory information sharing.
* **Confirm Address** (`confirm_address`): This field is where you enter your address to confirm that you are the person opting out of directory information sharing.
* **Confirm Name** (`confirm_name`): This field is where you enter your name to confirm that you are the person opting out of directory information sharing.
* **Confirm Other Info** (`confirm_other`), **Confirm Other Info 2** (`confirm_other_info_2`), **Confirm Other Info 3** (`confirm_other_info_3`), **Confirm Other Info 4** (`confirm_other_info_4`): These fields are for any other information you would like to share with us to confirm that you want to opt-out of directory information sharing.
