# Fitness Program Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fitness Program Participation Consent Form is a document designed to gather essential information from individuals who wish to participate in a fitness program. The form is used to collect participant details, emergency contact information, and any medical conditions or limitations that may affect their participation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details as requested in the "Participant Information" section.
2.  Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Information" section.
3.  Describe your relationship to the participant in the "Relationship to Participant" section.
4.  Select your relationship type from the options provided in the "Relationship Type" section.
5.  Provide any emergency contact information for the alternate contact person.
6.  Report any medical conditions or illnesses that may affect your participation in the "Medical Condition or Illness" section.
7.  List any medications you are taking in the "Medications" section.
8.  Provide any medical supplement information in the "Medical Supplements" section.
9.  Report any recent injuries or surgeries in the "Injury or Surgery" section.
10.  If applicable, provide medical clearance information in the "Medical Clearance" section.
11.  Finally, provide your signature in the "Participant Signature" section to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Information**: This section is where you can enter your personal details such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information.
*   **Emergency Contact Information**: Please provide the name and contact information of a person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
*   **Relationship to Participant**: Describe your relationship to the participant, such as a parent, spouse, child, or other.
*   **Relationship Type**: Select the type of relationship you have with the participant from the provided options.
*   **Alternate Contact Information**: Provide any additional contact information for the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Condition or Illness**: Report any medical conditions or illnesses that may affect your participation in the fitness program.
*   **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Medical Supplements**: Provide any information about medical supplements you are taking.
*   **Injury or Surgery**: Report any recent injuries or surgeries that may impact your participation in the fitness program.
*   **Medical Clearance**: If applicable, provide medical clearance information that has been issued by a medical professional.
*   **Participant Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your consent to participate in the fitness program.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
*   If you have any medical conditions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask questions or seek clarification before participating in the fitness program.
*   Ensure that the emergency contact person is aware of the participant's participation in the fitness program.
