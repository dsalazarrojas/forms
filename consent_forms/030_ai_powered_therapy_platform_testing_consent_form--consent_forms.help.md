# AI Powered Therapy Platform Testing Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a consent form for participants who will be testing the AI powered therapy platform. It is designed to collect information about the participant and the testing process, and to obtain their consent for the collection of data related to their interactions with the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information, starting from the top down.
2. Make sure to review each section carefully and ask questions if you are unsure about any part of the form.
3. Once you have completed the form, sign and date it to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`Participant Full Name`, `text`, required): This section asks for your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): This section asks for your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): This section asks for your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Overseeing Researcher / Clinician** (`Overseeing Researcher / Clinician`, `text`, required): This section asks for the name of the researcher or clinician who will be overseeing your participation in the study.
* **Crisis Intervention Disclaimer** (`MANDATORY: Crisis Intervention Disclaimer`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the two options provided. If you are in immediate danger, please call emergency services such as 911.
* **Understanding AI Limitations** (`Understanding AI Limitations`, `note`, required): This section explains that the platform utilizes Large Language Models which may occasionally generate incorrect, biased, or inappropriate responses. This is an experimental tool and not a substitute for human professional judgment.
* **Consent to Session Recording** (`Consent to Session Recording`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you consent to the recording and storage of your interactions (text/audio) with the AI platform for analysis.
* **Usage of Data for Model Improvement** (`Usage of Data for Model Improvement`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you consent to your anonymized data being used to train and improve future versions of the AI model.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`Emergency Contact Information`, `text`, required): This section asks for the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of distress.
* **Right to Withdraw** (`Right to Withdraw`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you understand that you can stop using the platform and withdraw your consent at any time without penalty.
* **Participant Signature Acknowledgment** (`Participant Signature Acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have read this form and voluntarily agree to participate in the platform.
* **Date of Signature** (`Date of Signature`, `date`, required): This section asks for the date you sign this form.
* **Primary Device Used for Testing** (`Primary device used for testing`, `select_one`, required): Please select the primary device you will use for testing the platform.
* **Participant ID / Group** (`Internal Use: Participant ID / Group`, `text`, not required): This section is for internal use and not intended for participant completion.

This form is designed to ensure that you, as a participant, are aware of the nature of the platform, the data collection process, and your rights and responsibilities. By completing this form, you are giving your consent for the collection and use of your data for research and testing purposes only. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
