# Emergency Contact Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from you about your emergency contact. This information will be kept confidential and will only be used in case of an emergency or other crisis. Please note that you should only submit this form once for each emergency contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below with your emergency contact's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Form** (`emergency_contact_form`, text, optional): This field is not visible to the end-user, as it is a placeholder for the form's name. You can ignore it.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose how you are related to your emergency contact (e.g. Parent, Stepparent, Guardian, Sibling, etc.).
* **Relationship to the Emergency Contact** (`contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Choose your relationship to the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency (e.g. Mother, Stepmother, Father, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for your emergency contact.
- This form should only be submitted once for each emergency contact.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your [support contact].
