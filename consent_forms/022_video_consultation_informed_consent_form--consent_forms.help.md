# Video Consultation Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect informed consent from patients before engaging in a video consultation. This form ensures that patients understand their rights, responsibilities, and data protection related to the consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details: Enter your name, date of birth, and other required information.
2. Select your consent preferences: Choose your preferred method of video consultation (video, phone, or in-person).
3. Provide contact information: Enter your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
4. Complete the clinician details: Enter the clinician's name, title, organisation, and contact information.
5. Confirm video platforms: Select the video platforms you are willing to use for the consultation.
6. Sign the form: Verify your signature to confirm that you have read and understood the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Patient Details
#### **Video Consultation Informed Consent Form** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the form title.
#### **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
#### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
#### **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
### Page 2:
#### **Video Consultation Consent** (`video_consent`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of video consultation (Video Consultation, Phone Consultation, In-person Consultation).
#### **Email Consent** (`email_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to receive emails from the clinician (Yes/No).
#### **Phone Consent** (`phone_consent`, text, required): Enter your phone number (123-456-7890).
#### **Contact Relationship** (`contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
### Page 3:
#### **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number (123-456-7890).
#### **Contact Emergency Email** (`contact_emergency_email`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email (if applicable).
#### **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number (123-456-7890).
### Page 4:
#### **Clinician Name** (`clinician_name`, text, required): Enter the clinician's name.
#### **Clinician Title** (`clinician_title`, text, required): Enter the clinician's title.
#### **Organisation Name** (`organisation_name`, text, required): Enter the organisation's name.
### Page 5:
#### **Clinician Address** (`clinician_address`, text, optional): Enter the clinician's address.
#### **Clinician Phone** (`clinician_phone`, text, optional): Enter the clinician's phone number (123-456-7890).
#### **Clinician Email** (`clinician_email`, text, optional): Enter the clinician's email.
#### **Clinician Specialisation** (`clinician_specialisation`, text, optional): Describe the clinician's specialisation.
#### **Clinician Qualifications** (`clinician_qualifications`, text, optional): List the clinician's qualifications.
### Page 6:
#### **Video Platforms** (`video_platforms`, select_multiple, required): Select the video platforms you are willing to use for the consultation (Skype, Zoom, Google Meet).
#### **Other Video Platforms** (`other_video_platforms`, text, optional): If you have any other video platforms, please specify.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have read and understood the form before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your clinician.
- This form is for informational purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose without consulting the clinician.
