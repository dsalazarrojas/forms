# Informed Consent Documentation Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an informed consent process for clients, ensuring that they understand the details of the treatment or research study they are participating in. It helps healthcare providers or researchers to acknowledge and record the client's consent, which is a crucial aspect of their profession.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a thorough understanding of the informed consent process and the treatment or research study you are participating in.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information for each section.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the client or participant. If you are unsure of their preferred name format, it is best to use their official ID or a unique identifier.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider involved in the treatment or research study.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which the informed consent was given. Use the date format as shown in the example.
* **Informed Consent** (`informed_consent`, text, optional): Describe the details of the informed consent process, including any relevant information about the treatment or research study.
* **Research Study** (`research_study`, text, optional): If applicable, provide a brief description of the research study being conducted.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline the treatment plan or procedure being used.
* **Research Informed Consent** (`research_informed_consent`, text, optional): Describe the specific informed consent process related to the research study.
* **Signature** (`consent_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that the informed consent process has been completed.
* **Research Consent Date** (`research_consent_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which the research informed consent was given.
* **Treatment Consent Date** (`treatment_consent_date`, date, optional): Record the date on which the treatment informed consent was given.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider who gave the consent.
* **Research Study Title** (`research_study_title`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the title of the research study being conducted.

**Tips**

* Make sure to use the exact date format for dates.
* Be specific and detailed when describing the informed consent process.
* Ensure that you have the consent of the client or participant before proceeding with the treatment or research study.
* Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.

Note: The form fields that are labeled as "optional" can be omitted if not relevant to the specific treatment or research study. However, it's essential to include all required fields to ensure accurate and complete documentation.
