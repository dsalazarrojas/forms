# COVID 19 Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your COVID-19 treatment options, medical history, and consent to treatment. It is essential to carefully read and understand all the questions and answers before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: Provide your consent date, name, date of birth, contact information, and emergency contact details.
2. Report your symptoms: Select all the symptoms you are currently experiencing due to COVID-19.
3. Disclose your medical conditions: List any underlying medical conditions, such as heart disease, lung disease, or diabetes.
4. List your medications: Report all your current medications, including any allergies.
5. Treatment options: Select the treatments you are interested in and have them explained to you by your healthcare provider.
6. Consent to treatment: Answer questions about your understanding of treatment risks and benefits, alternatives, and your right to refuse treatment.
7. Sign and acknowledge: Sign and date the form, acknowledging your consent to treatment and receipt of the HIPAA Privacy Notice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Date**: The date on which you provided your consent for COVID-19 treatment. 
* **Patient First Name**: Your legal first name as per identification documents.
* **Patient Last Name**: Your legal last name as per identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender**: Choose the correct gender option from Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, or Other.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email.
* **Home Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact**: Choose how you are related to your emergency contact from Spouse, Parent, Child, Sibling, Friend, or Other.
* **Insurance Provider**: If applicable, enter your insurance company name.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: If applicable, enter your member ID.
* **Primary Care Physician Name**: Name of your primary care physician.
* **Physician Phone**: Phone number of your primary care physician.
* **COVID-19 Diagnosis Date**: Date you were diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Current Symptoms**: Select all the symptoms you are experiencing due to COVID-19.
* **Symptom Severity**: Choose the severity level of your symptoms from Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Very Severe.
* **Underlying Conditions**: Select any underlying medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications**: List all your current medications, including any allergies.
* **Medication Allergies**: List any medication allergies you have.
* **Treatments Options Reviewed**: Have you had treatment options explained to you?
* **Treatment Type Consent**: Choose the treatment options you are consenting to.
* **Understanding of Risks and Benefits**: Answer if you understand the potential risks and benefits of treatment.
* **Understanding of Alternatives**: Answer if alternative treatments have been discussed with you.
* **Voluntary Consent**: Answer if your consent is fully voluntary.
* **Right to Refuse Treatment**: Answer if you understand your right to refuse treatment.
* **Questions Opportunity**: Answer if you had the opportunity to ask questions.
* **Treatment Consent Agreement**: Answer if you consent to receive COVID-19 treatment as recommended by your healthcare provider.
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment**: Answer if you have received and acknowledged the HIPAA Privacy Notice.
* **Patient Signature Date**: Date you signed the form.
* **Healthcare Provider Name**: Name of your healthcare provider.
* **Provider Signature Date**: Date your healthcare provider signed the form.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you are unsure about any question, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Ensure you have had all treatment options explained to you before giving consent.
- Review and understand all the questions and answers before submitting the form.
