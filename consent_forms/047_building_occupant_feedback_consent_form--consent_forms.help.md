# Building Occupant Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from building occupants on various aspects of the building, including overall satisfaction, space utilization, and facility maintenance. Your input will help the building management team make informed decisions to improve the building's operations and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Identify the building or area you occupy in the "Building or area you occupy" field.
5. Select the topic of your feedback in the "Feedback topic or survey topic" field.
6. Choose the frequency of surveys you are willing to participate in from the "Frequency of surveys" field.
7. Indicate how your feedback will be used in the "How will feedback be used" field.
8. Select the methods you prefer to be contacted for feedback in the "Methods I can be contacted for feedback" field.
9. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred contact method" field.
10. Indicate how often you are willing to be contacted in the "How often are you willing to be contacted" field.
11. Select the ideal time for contact or surveys in the "Ideal time for contact or surveys" field.
12. Indicate whether your feedback will involve health or medical information in the "Will feedback involve health or medical information" field.
13. Indicate whether you are willing to share personal information in the "Willing to share personal information" field.
14. Confirm your understanding of the purpose of feedback collection in the "I understand the purpose of feedback collection" field.
15. Confirm your understanding of how your feedback will be used in the "I understand how my feedback will be used" field.
16. Give your consent to provide feedback and participate in surveys in the "I consent to provide feedback and participate in surveys" field.
17. If you have any limitations or conditions for your consent, enter them in the "Limitations on my consent" field.
18. Confirm that you understand you can withdraw your consent at any time in the "I understand I can withdraw consent at any time" field.
