# Social Media Feature Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain user consent for social media features.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Social media feature consent form
*   **Social media feature consent form**: Enter a brief description of the social media feature you are consenting to.

### Step 2: Feature content
*   **Feature content**: Select the content related to the social media feature you are consenting to.

### Step 3: Social media purposes
*   **Social media purposes**: Choose the purpose for which you are consenting to use the social media feature. This may include marketing, promotional, or informational purposes.

### Step 4: Social media channels
*   **Social media channels**: Select the social media channels through which you will be using the feature, such as Facebook, Twitter, or Instagram.

### Step 5: Email
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Step 6: Phone number
*   **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Step 7: Consent date
*   **Consent date**: Enter the date on which you are providing consent.

### Step 8: Note
*   **Note**: Enter any additional information about your consent.

### Step 9: Assigned to
*   **Assigned to**: Select who the social media feature is assigned to.

### Step 10: Note (optional)
*   **Note**: Enter any additional information about the assignment.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Be specific and clear in your answers.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR department.
