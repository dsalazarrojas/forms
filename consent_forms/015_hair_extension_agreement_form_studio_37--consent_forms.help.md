# Hair Extension Agreement Form Studio 37 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a client's agreement to the terms and conditions of the hair extension service provided by Studio 37. The form helps to ensure a clear understanding between the client and the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Client Details" section, providing your name and any other relevant contact information.
2. Provide the details of the service you are requesting, including any specific requirements or requests.
3. Review the "Pricing Details" section to understand the costs associated with the service.
4. Review the "Payment Terms" section to understand the payment schedule and any applicable late payment fees.
5. Review the "Cancellation Terms" section to understand the policies for cancelling or rescheduling the service.
6. Read through the "Terms and Conditions" section to understand the general policies and rules governing the service.
7. Sign the "Agreement Confirmation" section by selecting "Client Signature".
8. Leave a "None" option if you do not wish to sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Please provide your name and any other relevant contact information to enable Studio 37 to communicate with you effectively.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): Describe the specific service you are requesting, including any specific requirements or requests.
* **Pricing Details** (`pricing_details`, text, required): Review this section to understand the costs associated with the service.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Review this section to understand the payment schedule and any applicable late payment fees.
* **Cancellation Terms** (`cancellation_terms`, text, required): Review this section to understand the policies for cancelling or rescheduling the service.
* **Agreement Confirmation** (`agreement_confirmation`, text, required): Select "Client Signature" to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the service. If you do not wish to sign, select "None".
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, not required): Read through this section to understand the general policies and rules governing the service.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, select_one, required): This field is for your signature to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the service. Please select "Client Signature" to confirm your agreement. If you do not wish to sign, select "None".
