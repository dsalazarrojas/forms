# Safety Equipment Demonstration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect the necessary information for a participant and a demonstrator to demonstrate safety equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant details in the first section.
2. Provide your signature and date in the second section.
3. Enter the demonstrator's name and contact information in the third section.
4. Enter the demonstrator's email address in the fourth section.
5. Enter the organization's name and address in the fifth section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): Enter your name, job title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and understood the safety equipment demonstration.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you signed the participant signature.
* **Demonstration Date** (`demonstration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the safety equipment demonstration.
* **Demonstrator Name** (`demonstrator_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or organization demonstrating the safety equipment.
* **Demonstrator Contact Number** (`demonstrator_contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the person or organization demonstrating the safety equipment.
* **Demonstrator Email** (`demonstrator_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person or organization demonstrating the safety equipment.
* **Organisation Name** (`organisation_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization or company demonstrating the safety equipment.
* **Organisation Address** (`organisation_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the organization or company demonstrating the safety equipment.
