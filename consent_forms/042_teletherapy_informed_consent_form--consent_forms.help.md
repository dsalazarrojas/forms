# Teletherapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent from patients for teletherapy services. It gathers information about the patient, their treatment plan, and other relevant details, including their consent to receive therapy sessions. The form also requires signatures from both the patient and the practitioner to confirm their understanding and agreement to the terms.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
2. Review and understand the treatment plan, which outlines the therapy sessions and services you will receive.
3. Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the teletherapy informed consent.
4. Sign the form to confirm your consent.
5. Sign and date the form as the practitioner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): Please fill in your name, email address, and phone number. This information will help us contact you with any updates or instructions.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Read and review the treatment plan carefully to understand what therapy sessions and services you will receive. This includes the frequency, duration, and type of sessions.
* **Teletherapy Informed Consent** (`teletherapy_informed_consent`, text, optional): Check the box to indicate that you have read and understood the informed consent. This is a crucial step to ensure you understand the terms and conditions of the therapy sessions.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Choose "Consent Given" to confirm that you have read and understood the informed consent. This is the final step to confirm your consent.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, please add your notes here.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This is your email address where we can contact you with any updates or instructions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This is your phone number where we can contact you with any updates or instructions.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you would like to list an emergency contact, please add their name and contact information.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.
* **Practitioner Signature** (`practitioner_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm that you have reviewed and explained the informed consent to the patient.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): This is the date you received therapy services.
