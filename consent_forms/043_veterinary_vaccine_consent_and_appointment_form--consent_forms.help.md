# Veterinary Vaccine Consent And Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from pet owners for veterinary vaccination appointments. It helps veterinarians and their staff to understand the pet's medical history, vaccination schedule, and any allergies or medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your pet's information, including its name and any relevant details.
2. Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
3. Select the vaccination schedule that applies to your pet.
4. Indicate if your pet has any allergies or medical conditions.
5. Provide any medical history for your pet.
6. Consent to the vaccination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Information** (`pet_information`, text, required: false): Enter your pet's name and any relevant details.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required: false): Select the appointment time that works best for you.
* **Vaccination Schedule** (`vaccination_schedule`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the vaccination schedule that applies to your pet.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for updates and reminders.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of consent you are giving for the vaccination process.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant medical history for your pet.
* **Allergy List** (`allergy_list`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if your pet has any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if your pet has any medical conditions.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the previous Consent field. Please ignore this field.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This field is redundant, please ignore.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is redundant, please ignore.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your pet.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
