# Consent For Data Protection - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your consent for data protection, which is essential for our organization to process your personal data in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each section carefully to ensure you understand what each field represents.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as 'true' in the required column).
3. Choose your preferences for each section.
4. Sign this form digitally to provide your explicit consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Data Subject**: This field is your name as it appears on official records and documents. Please enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents. (Name, required)
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your primary email address that you use for official communications. (Email, required)
* **Which types of data do you consent for us to process?**: Select all the types of data that you consent for us to process. Please be aware that selecting all types does not necessarily imply consent for all purposes. (Select Multiple, required)
	+ Contact Details (Email, Phone)
	+ Identity Information (Name, DOB)
	+ Behavioral/Usage Data
	+ Financial/Transaction History
	+ Marketing Preferences
* **Purpose of Data Processing**: Select the purposes for which you consent to process your data. This will help us ensure we handle your data correctly. (Select Multiple, required)
	+ Service Provision and Management
	+ Customer Support and Communication
	+ Direct Marketing and Newsletters
	+ Statistical Analysis
	+ Legal and Regulatory Compliance
* **I understand my data will be stored as per the Data Retention Policy**: Please acknowledge that you understand how we will store your data. (Select One, required)
* **I consent to my data being shared with necessary third-party partners**: This allows us to share your data with partners that help us provide our services. (Select One, required)
* **I am aware of my right to withdraw consent at any time**: Please understand that you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. (Select One, required)
* **I am aware of my right to access and rectify my personal data**: This is your right to access and correct your data. (Select One, required)
* **Do you provide explicit consent for processing sensitive data?**: If you select 'Yes', it means you are aware of the sensitive nature of the data and consent to it being processed. (Select One, required)
* **Digital Signature (Type Name)**: This is where you will digitally sign this consent form. Please use your name as it appears on your identification documents. (Text, required)
* **Date of Consent**: This is the date on which you are giving your consent. (Date, required)

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when filling this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* Once you have completed this form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
* You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.
* You can also contact us to access and rectify your personal data.
