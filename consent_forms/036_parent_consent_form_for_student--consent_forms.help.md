# Parent Consent Form For Student - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain consent from a parent or guardian for their child's participation in school-related activities. The form asks for essential information about the student and the parent/guardian, as well as permission for the student to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the student's unique identifier (ID) in the **Student Id** field.
2. Provide the student's first name in the **Student First Name** field.
3. Enter the student's last name in the **Student Last Name** field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** field.
5. Enter the parent/guardian's first name in the **Parent First Name** field.
6. Enter the parent/guardian's last name in the **Parent Last Name** field.
7. Enter the parent/guardian's email address in the **Parent Email** field.
8. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number in the **Parent Phone** field.
9. Enter the school's name in the **School Name** field.
10. Enter the school's address in the **School Address** field.
11. Enter the school's city in the **School City** field.
12. Enter the school's state in the **School State** field.
13. Enter the school's zip code in the **School Zip** field.
14. Enter the emergency contact's name in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
15. Select the emergency contact's relationship to the student in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
16. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
17. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.
18. Enter the emergency contact's address in the **Emergency Contact Address** field.
19. Enter the emergency contact's city in the **Emergency Contact City** field.
20. Enter the emergency contact's state in the **Emergency Contact State** field.
21. Enter the emergency contact's zip code in the **Emergency Contact Zip** field.
22. Select the student's permission to participate in activities in the **Student Permission** field.
23. Select the school's permission to provide information to the student in the **School Permission** field.
24. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Id (1)**: Enter the student's unique identifier.
* **Student First Name (2)**: Enter the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name (3)**: Enter the student's last name.
* **Date Of Birth (4)**: Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Parent First Name (5)**: Enter the parent/guardian's first name.
* **Parent Last Name (6)**: Enter the parent/guardian's last name.
* **Parent Id (7)**: Enter the parent/guardian's ID (usually a phone number or email address).
* **Parent Email (8)**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone (9)**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **School Name (10)**: Enter the school's name.
* **School Address (11)**: Enter the school's address.
* **School City (12)**: Enter the school's city.
* **School State (13)**: Enter the school's state.
* **School Zip (14)**: Enter the school's zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Name (15)**: Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (16)**: Select the emergency contact's relationship to the student.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (17)**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email (18)**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address (19)**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact City (20)**: Enter the emergency contact's city.
* **Emergency Contact State (21)**: Enter the emergency contact's state.
* **Emergency Contact Zip (22)**: Enter the emergency contact's zip code.
* **Student Permission (23)**: Select the student's permission to participate in activities.
* **School Permission (24)**: Select the school's permission to provide information to the student.
* **Notes (25)**: Add any additional notes or comments.
