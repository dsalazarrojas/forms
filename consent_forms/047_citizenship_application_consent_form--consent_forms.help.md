# Citizenship Application Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is part of the citizenship application process. It is used to collect necessary information from the applicant to verify their identity, address, and background. The information provided will be used to process your application for citizenship.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure all fields are properly filled, especially required fields.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult the field-specific explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Legal Name** (`applicant_name`, Text, Required): As it appears on official documents. Please enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Current Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your current street address, city, state, and zip.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Country of Birth** (`country_of_birth`, Text, Required): Enter the country where you were born.
* **Current Nationality** (`current_nationality`, Text, Required): Select your current nationality.
* **Current Residency Status** (`residency_status`, Select One, Required): Indicate your current residency status. Options include Permanent resident, Temporary resident, Work visa holder, Student visa holder, Asylum seeker, or Other.
* **Residency Start Date** (`residency_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you became a resident of the country.
* **Type of Application** (`application_type`, Select One, Required): Indicate the type of citizenship application you're submitting. Options include Naturalization, Citizenship by descent, Citizenship by marriage, Restoration of citizenship, or Other.
* **Consent to Background Check** (`consent_background_check`, Select One, Required): Indicate your consent to a background check. Options include I consent, I do not consent.
* **Consent to Information Verification** (`consent_verification`, Select One, Required): Indicate your consent to verification of all provided information. Options include I consent, I do not consent.
* **Information Accuracy Statement** (`accuracy_statement`, Select One, Required): Confirm that all information provided is true and accurate. Options include I confirm accuracy, I need to make corrections.
* **Have You Previously Applied for Citizenship** (`previous_applications`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have previously applied for citizenship. Options include True, False.
* **Please Provide Details of Previous Application** (`previous_application_detail`, Text, Optional): If you have previously applied for citizenship, provide a brief explanation of your application.
* **Do You Have Any Criminal History?** (`criminal_history`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have any criminal history. Options include True, False.
* **Please Provide Details** (`criminal_history_detail`, Text, Optional): If you have a criminal history, provide a brief explanation.
* **Have You Traveled Outside the Country?** (`travel_history`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have traveled outside the country. Options include True, False.
* **Language Proficiency** (`language_proficiency`, Select One, Required): Indicate your language proficiency level. Options include Fluent, Intermediate, Basic, or None.
* **Have You Prepared for the Citizenship Test?** (`test_preparation`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have prepared for the citizenship test. Options include Yes, completed preparation, Yes, currently preparing, No, not yet.
* **Do You Have Legal Representation?** (`legal_representation`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you have legal representation. Options include Yes, attorney, Yes, accredited representative, False.
* **Privacy Policy Consent** (`privacy_consent`, Select One, Required): Indicate your consent to data processing according to privacy policy. Options include I consent, I do not consent.
* **Applicant Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of signature.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, Text, Optional): If required, enter the name of the witness.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, Text, Optional): If required, enter the signature of the witness.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled accurately.
- If you're unsure, please consult the field-specific explanations above.
- Please sign and date the form before submission.
