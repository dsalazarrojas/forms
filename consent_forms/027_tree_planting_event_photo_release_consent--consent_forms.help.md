# Tree Planting Event Photo Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tree Planting Event Photo Release form is a consent form that allows us to share photos and videos taken during the event. By completing this form, you are giving us permission to use your name, photo, and video for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose your preferred option for sharing your information in section 2.
3. Provide your name, email, phone number, and address (if required).
4. Select your date of birth and the date of the event (if required).
5. Confirm your photo release by providing a brief statement or signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **I Am** (`tree_planting_event_photo_release`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Give us** (`photo_video_permission`): Choose one of the three options:
	+ Consent to share my name, photo, and video publicly
	+ Consent to share my name, but not my photo or video
	+ Do Not Share
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **E-mail** (`email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`): Enter your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`): Please provide a brief statement confirming that you give permission to share your photo. You can do this by typing a short sentence, such as "I give permission for my photo and video to be shared." or by providing a signature.
