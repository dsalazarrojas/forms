# Student Parent Handbook Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Parent Handbook Acknowledgment Form is a form that allows parents or guardians to acknowledge receipt and understanding of the student handbook. This form is intended to ensure that parents/guardians are aware of the policies, procedures, and expectations outlined in the handbook.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please review the student handbook and ensure you have a thorough understanding of its contents.
2. Check the relevant boxes in the form that apply to you and your family.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_1`, Note, not required): This field is where you can enter your acknowledgment of receipt of the student handbook. Please write your thoughts or comments about the handbook.
* **Handbook Attachments** (`handbook_attach`, Text, not required): In this field, you can attach any relevant documents or files related to the handbook that you would like to keep on record.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 2** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_2`, Select Multiple, not required): Please select all the statements that apply to your understanding of the handbook. For example, if the handbook mentions "cell phone policies" and you think your child should not have a cell phone, you can select "No".
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 3** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_3`, Select One, not required): Select one of the options that best represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 4** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_4`, Select One, not required): This field is similar to field 3. Select one of the options that best represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 5** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_5`, Email, not required): You can enter your email address or contact information in case the school needs to communicate with you about the handbook.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 6** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_6`, Select Multiple, not required): Please select all the statements that apply to your understanding of the handbook.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 7** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_7`, Select One, not required): Select one of the options that best represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 8** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_8`, Select Multiple, not required): Please select all the statements that apply to your understanding of the handbook.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 9** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_9`, Select One, not required): Select one of the options that best represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 10** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_10`, Time, not required): You can enter a specific time that represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Student Handbook Acknowledgment 11** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_11`, Note, not required): This field is similar to field 1, where you can enter your thoughts or comments about the handbook.
* **Number of Students** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_12`, Number, not required): You can enter the number of students for whom you are acknowledging the handbook.
* **Handbook Acknowledgment 12** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_13`, Number, not required): You can enter a number that represents your understanding of the handbook's content.
* **Additional Comments** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_14`, Text, not required): In this field, you can enter any additional comments or thoughts about the handbook.
* **Handbook Acknowledgment Date** (`student_parent_handbook_acknowledgment_form_15`, Date, not required): You can enter the date of your acknowledgment of the handbook.

## Tips
- Please review the handbook carefully before completing this form to ensure you understand its contents.
- If you are unsure about any part of the handbook, please reach out to your child's school or teacher for clarification.
- Remember to keep a copy of the form for your records.
