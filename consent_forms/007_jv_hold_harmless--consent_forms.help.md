# jv_harmless - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "jv_harmless" form is designed to collect basic patient information for non-clinical purposes. Please fill out this form if you are a patient seeking medical services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Select the type of service you are seeking (Medical Exam, Treatment, Test, or Test Result).
3. Provide your contact phone number and email address.
4. Enter your medical record number (if applicable).
5. Enter your doctor's name and title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): Enter the time of the service you are seeking.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more services you are seeking (Medical Exam, Treatment, Test, or Test Result).
* **Contact Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical record number (if applicable).
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's name.
* **Doctor Title** (`doctor_title`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's title.

## Tips
Please fill out the form completely and accurately. If you are unsure about any information, please contact the medical staff for assistance.
