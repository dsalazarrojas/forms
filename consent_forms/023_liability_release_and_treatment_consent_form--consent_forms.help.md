# Liability Release And Treatment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect liability release and treatment consent from patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient information, including your date of birth and address.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including name, phone number, relationship, and address.
3. Describe your medical condition and treatment information.
4. Acknowledge the risk of participation and provide your consent.
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your personal and contact information, including your date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_information`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your medical condition.
* **Medical Condition Details** (`medical_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any relevant medical conditions related to the treatment or procedure.
* **Treatment Details** (`treatment_information`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the treatment or procedure you are participating in.
* **Risk of Participation** (`risk_of_participation`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the risk of participation in the treatment or procedure.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Consent" to indicate that you understand and agree to the treatment or procedure.
* **Provider Consent** (`provider_consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Agree" to indicate that your healthcare provider understands and agrees to the treatment or procedure.
* **Acknowledgement of Risk** (`acknowledgement_of_risk`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any risks or concerns you have about the treatment or procedure.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Sign and date the form to indicate that your provider has reviewed and understood the treatment or procedure.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Sign and date the form to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the treatment or procedure.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Provider** (`provider`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider.
* **Patient** (`patient`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.

## Tips
Make sure to carefully read and understand each field before answering. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
