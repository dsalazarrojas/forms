# Telemedicine Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telemedicine Consent Form is a document that patients must sign or provide consent for the use of telemedicine services. This form aims to inform patients about their rights and responsibilities, the boundaries of the telemedicine relationship, and how their data will be shared.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as accurately as possible based on your understanding of the provided options.

### Page 1: Telemedicine Consent
1. **Telemedicine Consent**: This is the main agreement for telemedicine services. Please indicate your acceptance of the terms.

### Page 2: Patient Consent
1. **Patient Consent** (`Required: false`): This field is currently optional but may be required in certain situations. Please select the appropriate option to indicate your understanding of how your telemedicine data will be used and shared. 
    * If you select `1`, please explain in the notes that this number represents your understanding of how your data will be used.
2. **Note**: This is a field for additional comments or clarifications related to your understanding of your telemedicine data.

### Page 3: Telemedicine Privacy and Security
1. **Telemedicine Privacy**: 
    * Select `public` if you're willing to share your telemedicine data publicly.
    * Select `private` if you're not willing to share your data publicly.
3. **Telemedicine Security**: This field is not currently being used for data collection. 
4. **Data Sharing**: 
    * Select `True` if you're willing to share your data with others.
    * Select `False` if you're not willing to share your data with others.
5. **Data Security** (`Required: false`): This field is currently optional but may be required in the future. 
    * Select `strong` if you believe your data is highly secure.
    * Select `weak` if you believe your data is not secure.
6. **Electronic Medical Record**: This field is not currently being used for data collection. 
7. **Telemedicine Equipment**: 
    * Please describe any medical equipment you're using for telemedicine services.
8. **Data Security** (`Required: false`): This field is currently optional but may be required in the future. 
    * Select `strong` if you believe your data is highly secure.
    * Select `weak` if you believe your data is not secure.

### Tips
- Please answer each question carefully to ensure your data is collected and shared accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
- This form is a record-keeping tool; please keep it for future reference.
- If your situation changes or you have new information, please update your responses accordingly.
- If you're unsure about any question or option, please consult with your healthcare provider for clarification.
