# Permission To Walk Home Unattended Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from parents for their child to walk home unattended after an event at school. It helps to gather necessary information from parents, school staff, and the child, ensuring that all necessary permissions are given before allowing the child to walk home alone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields, including the child's and parent's names, phone numbers, email addresses, and event dates and times. You will also need to provide the school's and event locations. If you are a school staff member, you will need to sign the form to indicate that you have reviewed and verified the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone_number`, text, required): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Enter the child's name.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to grant permission or 'No' to deny.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the school's name.
* **School Event Name** (`school_event_name`, text, optional): Enter the school event name.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): Please have the parent sign this section to indicate that they have read and understood the form.
* **School Staff Name** (`school_staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the school staff name.
* **School Staff Phone** (`school_staff_phone`, text, optional): Enter the school staff's phone number.
* **School Staff Email** (`school_staff_email`, email, optional): Enter the school staff's email address.
* **School Permission** (`school_permission`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to grant permission or 'No' to deny.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Permission Given By** (`permission_given_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person giving permission.
* **Permission Date** (`permission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date permission was given.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Form Submitted Date** (`form_submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Parent Address** (`parent_address`, text, optional): Enter the parent's address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): Enter the school's address.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes from the parent.
* **School Notes** (`school_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes from the school staff.
