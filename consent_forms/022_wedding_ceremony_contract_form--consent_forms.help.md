# wedding_ceremony_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the wedding ceremony, including details about the ceremony's time, location, language, and other essential aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with `required: true`.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Venue** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_venue`): Enter the venue where the ceremony will take place. This can be a specific location or a general description.
* **Date** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_date`): Select the date of the wedding ceremony. Make sure to choose the correct date in the correct format.
* **Officiant Fee** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant_fee`): This field is optional and is used to indicate if the officiant has a fee for their services. If you choose to fill this in, provide the amount of the fee.
* **Payment Terms** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_payment_terms`): This field is optional and is used to describe any terms related to payment. If you choose to fill this in, provide a clear description.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_cancellation_policy`): This field is optional and is used to describe any cancellation policies related to the ceremony. If you choose to fill this in, provide a clear description.
* **Officiant** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant`): Select whether you have an officiant or not. This field is required.
* **Cancellation Fees** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_cancellation_fees`): This field is optional and is used to indicate if there are any cancellation fees. If you choose to fill this in, provide the amount of the fee.
* **Ceremony Time** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_time`): Select the time of the wedding ceremony. You can choose from 2:30 pm or 4:00 pm.
* **Ceremony Location** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_location`): Enter the location of the wedding ceremony. This can be a specific address or a general description.
* **Ceremony Language** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_language`): Select the language of the ceremony. You can choose from English or Spanish.
* **Officiant Signature** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant_signature`): This field is required and is used to obtain the signature of the officiant.
* **Client Signature** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_client_signature`): This field is required and is used to obtain the signature of the client.
* **Officiant Name** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant_name`): Enter the name of the officiant.
* **Ceremony Type** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_type`): Select the type of ceremony. This field is required.
* **Ceremony Place** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_place`): Enter the place where the ceremony will take place.
* **Cancellation Reason** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_cancellation_reason`): This field is optional and is used to describe the reason for cancellation. If you choose to fill this in, provide a clear description.
* **Officiant Agreement** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant_agreement`): This field is optional and is used to describe any agreement between the officiant and the client.
* **Payment Terms Agreement** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_payment_terms_agreement`): This field is optional and is used to describe any agreement related to payment terms.
* **Venue Terms** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_venue_terms`): This field is optional and is used to describe any terms related to the venue.
* **Officiant Service** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_officiant_service`): Enter the officiant's services offered. This field is required.
* **Ceremony Length** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_length`): Enter the length of the ceremony. This field is required.
* **Ceremony Type Venue** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_type_venue`): Enter the venue where the ceremony will take place.
* **Ceremony Language English** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_language_english`): Select the language of the ceremony in English. You can choose from English or French.
* **Ceremony Language French** (`wedding_ceremony_contract_form_ceremony_language_french`): Select the language of the ceremony in French. You can choose from French or English.
