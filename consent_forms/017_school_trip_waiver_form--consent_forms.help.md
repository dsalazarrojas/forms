# School Trip Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to gather information about students going on a school trip. The school staff uses this form to ensure the student's safety and well-being during the trip.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date details.
2. Complete the Student Name field with the student's name.
3. Provide the parent/guardian's name and their relationship to the student.
4. Enter the Emergency Contact's phone number and address.
5. Add any additional information about the emergency contact if necessary.
6. Specify the trip details, including the date and time of the trip.
7. Choose the school name and teacher name (if applicable).
8. Choose the student's grade level.
9. Select the student's attendance status (e.g., "6" for "6th grade" or "10" for "10th grade").
10. Add any additional comments about the student's attendance.
11. Read and sign the waiver agreement, agreeing to the terms and conditions.
12. Sign off with your official title and position at the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their school record.
* **Parent Name** (Parent Name, text, required): Enter the parent/guardian's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact, if not a parent/guardian.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Trip Details** (Trip Details, text, required): Provide a brief description of the trip.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the trip.
* **Destination** (Destination, text, optional): Enter the destination of the trip, if applicable.
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Teacher Name** (Teacher Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the teacher in charge of the trip.
* **Student Grade** (Student Grade, number, required): Choose the student's grade level (e.g., "6" for 6th grade).
* **Student Attendance** (Student Attendance, select_one, required): Select the student's attendance status (e.g., "6" for 6th grade).
* **Student Attendance Comment** (Student Attendance Comment, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the student's attendance.
* **Waiver Agreement** (Waiver Agreement, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the trip.
* **Sign Officer** (Sign Officer, select_multiple, required): Choose your official title and position at the school.
