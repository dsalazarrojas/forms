# Human Library Participation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers participating in Human Library events. It gathers contact details, event information, and confirmations of participation and data handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your event name in the "Event Name" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select the date of the event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
6. Choose the time of the event from the list in the "Event Time" field.
7. Provide session details in the "Session Details" field.
8. Confirm your participation in the events by selecting "Yes" in the "I Confirm" field.
9. Confirm that you understand and agree to share your data with event organizers by selecting "Yes" in the "Confirm Privacy" field.
10. Add any session notes in the "Session Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the Human Library event you are participating in.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the event from the list.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about the session, including any specific information you think the organizers should know about you or the session.
* **I Confirm** (`confirmation1`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you are participating in the event by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirmation2** (`confirmation2`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Confirmation3** (`confirmation3`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Confirmation4** (`confirmation4`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Confirm Session** (`confirm_session`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you have attended the session.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about yourself as a volunteer.
* **Form Submission 2** (`form_submission_2`, select_one, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.
* **Confirm Agreement** (`confirm_agreement`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you agree to share your data with event organizers.
* **Confirm Privacy** (`confirm_privacy`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm that you understand and agree to the data handling policy.
* **Session Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any notes about the session.
* **Human Library** (`form_title2`, text, required/optional): This field is not used in the form.

Note: Please fill out all fields with the required information, except where noted as optional.
