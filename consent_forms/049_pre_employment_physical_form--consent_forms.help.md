# Pre Employment Physical Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Employment Physical Form is designed to collect personal and medical information from applicants or new hires to assess their ability to perform job requirements. This form is typically completed by the individual before starting work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by *).
3. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`): Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`): List any known medical conditions you have or have had in the past, including any ongoing or past injuries.
* **Medications** (`Medications`): List any prescription medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`): List any known allergies or sensitivities you have, including food allergies.
* **Surgical History** (`Surgical History`): List any previous surgeries or medical procedures you have undergone.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`): Provide a brief description of any past medical conditions or treatments you have received.
* **Family History** (`Family History`): List any known medical conditions or concerns in your family history.
* **Medical Certs** (`Medical Certs`): Upload any relevant medical certificates, test results, or documents.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`): Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Signature** (`Signature`): Sign and date this form to confirm your understanding and agreement with the submitted information. 

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with the designated representative before completing this form.
* This form will be used for internal purposes only, and its content will not be shared with anyone without your consent. 

This form is designed to be completed by the individual and not duplicated. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the contact provided.
