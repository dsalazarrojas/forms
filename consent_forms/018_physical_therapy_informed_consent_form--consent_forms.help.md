# Physical Therapy Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Therapy Informed Consent Form is a document that patients need to fill out before starting physical therapy sessions. It is designed to ensure that you understand the treatment plan, the risks and benefits, and your rights as a patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. For fields marked as required, please provide a full explanation or answer.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your physical therapist or nurse for clarification.
4. Once you have filled out all the necessary fields, review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This field is a yes/no question to confirm if you are filling out this form for yourself or on behalf of someone else.
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., 05/12/1990).
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of your physical therapy appointment.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Insurance Info**: If you have health insurance, please provide the necessary information.
* **Patient Address**: Enter your full address, including street address, apartment number, and city.
* **Medical History**: Please provide a brief summary of your medical history, including any allergies, medications, or previous surgeries.
* **Physical Therapy Goals**: Describe your goals for the physical therapy sessions, including what you hope to achieve and any limitations or restrictions.
* **Patient Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm you have read and understood the form.
* **Physical Therapist Signature**: Sign your name as the physical therapist or healthcare provider.
* **Physical Therapy Agreement**: Please review and sign this agreement to confirm you understand the terms of the physical therapy.
* **Consent to Treatment**: Please indicate if you consent to physical therapy treatment.
* **Consent to Treatment Agreement**: Please review and sign this agreement to confirm you understand the terms of treatment consent.
* **Informed Consent**: Please indicate if you have received informed consent from your healthcare provider.
* **Consent to Video Taping**: Please indicate if you consent to video recording during physical therapy sessions.
* **Consent to Video Taping Agreement**: Please review and sign this agreement to confirm you understand the terms of video taping consent.

## Tips
* Please take your time to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to ask your physical therapist or nurse.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
