# Beauty Salon COVID 19 Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a COVID-19 liability waiver for clients visiting a beauty salon. It aims to inform clients about the risks associated with COVID-19 and to have them acknowledge and agree to certain conditions to minimize those risks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the Client Information section.
2. Select your COVID-19 vaccination status.
3. Answer questions about your health status.
4. Acknowledge the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
5. Confirm that you will follow all salon safety measures.
6. Sign the waiver by typing your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`client_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Fully Vaccinated: You have received both the initial doses of COVID-19 vaccine and have had time to build immunity.
	+ Partially Vaccinated: You have received one dose of the COVID-19 vaccine and are waiting for the next dose.
	+ Not Vaccinated: You have not received any COVID-19 vaccine doses yet.
	+ Prefer Not to Say: You do not wish to disclose your vaccination status.
* **Booster Shot Status** (`booster_status`, select_one, **Optional**):
	+ Yes - Up to Date: You have received a COVID-19 booster shot and are up to date on your vaccinations.
	+ No - Not Yet: You have not received a COVID-19 booster shot yet.
	+ Not Applicable: You are not eligible for a COVID-19 booster shot.
	+ Prefer Not to Say: You do not wish to disclose your booster shot status.
* **Have You Had COVID-19 Symptoms in Past 14 Days** (`covid_symptoms`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You have had COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
	+ No: You have not had COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
* **Describe Symptoms** (`symptom_details`, text, **Optional**): If you have had COVID-19 symptoms, describe them in detail.
* **Exposure to Known COVID-19 Cases in Past 14 Days** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You have been exposed to a known COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
	+ No: You have not been exposed to a known COVID-19 case in the past 14 days.
* **Currently Under Quarantine or Isolation** (`quarantine_requirement`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You are currently under quarantine or isolation due to COVID-19 exposure.
	+ No: You are not under quarantine or isolation.
* **I Understand COVID-19 Transmission Risks** (`understood_transmission_risk`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
	+ No: You do not understand the risks associated with COVID-19 transmission.
* **I Acknowledge Salon Safety Measures** (`acknowledged_salon_measures`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You acknowledge that you will follow all salon safety measures.
	+ No: You do not acknowledge that you will follow all salon safety measures.
* **I Assume the Risk of COVID-19 Exposure** (`assumed_risk`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure while visiting the salon.
	+ No: You do not assume the risk of COVID-19 exposure while visiting the salon.
* **I Release the Salon from Liability** (`release_salon`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You release the salon from liability for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
	+ No: You do not release the salon from liability for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
* **I Agree Not to Sue the Salon** (`not_sue_salon`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You agree not to sue the salon for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
	+ No: You do not agree not to sue the salon for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
* **I Will Indemnify and Hold Harmless the Salon** (`indemnify_salon`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You will indemnify and hold harmless the salon for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
	+ No: You will not indemnify and hold harmless the salon for any COVID-19 related illness or complications.
* **I Will Follow All Salon Safety Measures** (`safety_measures`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You will follow all salon safety measures.
	+ No: You will not follow all salon safety measures.
* **All Information Provided Is Truthful** (`honesty_declaration`, select_one, **Required**):
	+ Yes: You have provided truthful information.
	+ No: You have not provided truthful information.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, text, **Required**): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver.

Note: Please review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
