The Legal Guardian Release Form is a document that allows a minor to release their legal guardian or parent from their responsibilities. This form is typically used in situations where a minor is seeking to live independently or is moving away from their legal guardian's supervision. The form serves as a formal agreement between the minor and their guardian, outlining the terms of the release. 

The form is usually required for minors who wish to live away from their guardians' supervision, often for reasons such as going to college, joining the military, or getting married. It's essential for both the minor and their guardian to understand the terms and conditions of the release to avoid any potential disputes or conflicts in the future.

To complete this form, make sure to provide accurate and truthful information. Ensure all required fields are filled out completely, as incomplete or inaccurate information may delay or prevent the release from being processed.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Minor Name** (`minor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the minor releasing their guardian.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the guardian being released, if different from the minor's name.
* **Date of Birth** (`birthdate`, date, optional): Enter the minor's date of birth for identification purposes.
* **Permission** (`guardian_permission`, select_one, optional): Select "Permission Granted" or "Permission Denied" as appropriate.
* **Guardian Signature** (`signature_guardian`, text, optional): The guardian's signature, if applicable.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed_guardian`, date, optional): The date the guardian signed the release, if applicable.
* **Minor Signature** (`signature_minor`, text, optional): The minor's signature, if applicable.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed_minor`, date, optional): The date the minor signed the release, if applicable.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the witness's name who signed the release.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, text, optional): Enter the witness's signature.
* **Witness Title** (`witness_title`, text, optional): Enter the witness's title or position.
* **Witness Organization** (`witness_organization`, text, optional): Enter the witness's organization or affiliation.
* **Witness Contact** (`witness_contact`, text, optional): Enter the witness's contact information.
* **Guardian Address** (`guardian_address`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's address, if different from the minor's address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, optional): Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Minor Address** (`minor_address`, text, optional): Enter the minor's address, if different from the guardian's address.
* **Minor Phone** (`minor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the minor's phone number.
* **Minor Email** (`minor_email`, email, optional): Enter the minor's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between the minor and the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Guardian Signature Date** (`guardian_signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the guardian signed the release, if applicable.

Note: Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid delays or issues with the release process. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant documentation or seek guidance from a legal professional.
