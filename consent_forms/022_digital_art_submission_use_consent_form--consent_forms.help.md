# Digital Art Submission Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from artists before submitting their digital art to a platform or organization. It helps artists understand their rights and responsibilities when granting or denying consent for their digital art to be used in specific ways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and understand its purpose and each field's explanation.
2.  Answer each field according to your preference and consent.
3.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Artist Information** (`digital_art_submission_use_consent_form_page`): This field is optional and allows you to provide additional information about yourself as an artist.
    *   Please enter your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself as an artist.
*   **Use Consent** (`digital_art_submission_use_consent_form_use_consent`): This field is a select-one field that asks you to choose how you want to grant consent for your digital art to be used.
    *   Choose one of the three options:
        +   **Allow**: This option means you grant unlimited consent for your digital art to be used as intended by the platform or organization.
        +   **Deny**: This option means you deny consent for your digital art to be used at all.
        +   **Request**: This option means you want to receive more information or clarification on how your digital art will be used before granting or denying consent.
*   **Consent Details** (`digital_art_submission_use_consent_form_use_consent_details`): This field is optional and allows you to provide more information about your consent choices.
    *   If you chose **Request** in the previous field, you can provide more details about what you are requesting.
*   **Artist Signature** (`digital_art_submission_use_consent_form_signature`): This field is optional and is used to capture your signature as an artist.
    *   If you are granting consent, please sign this field with your signature or provide a handwritten signature if possible.

## Tips

*   Please note that the form is designed to be used by artists, and it is recommended to read it carefully before submitting your digital art.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact the platform or organization submitting your digital art to.
*   Remember to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you understand the terms of use.
