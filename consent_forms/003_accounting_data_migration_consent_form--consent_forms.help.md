# Accounting Data Migration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain consent from clients for the migration of their accounting data to a new system. Ensure to have a clear understanding of the client's authorization and agreement on the process before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the client's full name or company name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Provide the current accounting system being used in the "Current System Name" field (e.g., QuickBooks Desktop).
3.  Enter the new system the data is being migrated to in the "New System Name" field (e.g., Xero Cloud).
4.  Select all the categories of data to be transferred in the "Data Types to Migrate" field.
5.  Enter the proposed date to begin the data transfer in the "Migration Start Date" field.
6.  Read and acknowledge the risks associated with data migration in the "Authorization and Risks --" note field.
7.  Agree or decline the data migration process in the "Data Integrity Acknowledgment" field.
8.  Provide a person to contact in case of issues in the "Emergency Contact During Migration" field.
9.  Enter a direct line for emergency contact in the "Emergency Phone" field.
10.  Enter the email address to receive status updates in the "Notification Email" field.
11.  Enter the date of authorization in the "Authorization Date" field.
12.  Type the name to authorize the migration in the "Authorized Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's full name or company name.
*   **Current System Name** (`current_system_name`, text, required): Provide the current accounting system being used (e.g., QuickBooks Desktop).
*   **New System Name** (`new_system_name`, text, required): Enter the new system the data is being migrated to (e.g., Xero Cloud).
*   **Data Types to Migrate** (`data_types_to_migrate`, select_multiple, required): Select all categories of data to be transferred (e.g., General Ledger, Accounts Payable).
*   **Migration Start Date** (`migration_start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed date to begin the data transfer.
*   **Authorization and Risks** (`section_authorization`, note, required): Acknowledge the risks associated with data migration.
*   **Data Integrity Acknowledgment** (`data_integrity_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Agree or decline the data migration process.
*   **Emergency Contact During Migration** (`emergency_contact_during_migration`, text, required): Provide a person to contact in case of issues.
*   **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter a direct line for emergency contact.
*   **Notification Email** (`notification_email`, email, required): Enter the email address to receive status updates.
*   **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required): Enter the date of authorization.
*   **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, text, required): Type the name to authorize the migration.

## Tips

*   Ensure to have a clear understanding of the client's authorization and agreement on the process.
*   Review the form fields carefully before submitting.
