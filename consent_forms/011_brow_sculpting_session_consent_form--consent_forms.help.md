# Brow Sculpting Session Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from clients before a brow sculpting session, ensuring that they are aware of the procedure's risks, requirements, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and reminders.
5. Select the date of your scheduled appointment from the calendar.
6. Choose the type of brow sculpting you are interested in (Threading, Waxing, Microblading, or Combination).
7. Indicate whether you have had brow sculpting before or not (True, False, or Not sure).
8. Select your skin type (Oily, Dry, Combination, Sensitive, or Normal).
9. If applicable, list any known skin allergies or sensitivities.
10. If applicable, list any current medications that affect your skin.
11. If applicable, list any current skin conditions (rash, acne, psoriasis, etc.).
12. Indicate your pregnancy status (Yes, pregnant, Yes, nursing, or False).
13. Select your pain sensitivity level (Low sensitivity, Normal sensitivity, or High sensitivity).
14. If applicable, list any recent facial procedures (Botox, fillers, chemical peels, etc.).
15. Describe your desired brow sculpting look or goal.
16. Acknowledge the potential risks associated with the procedure.
17. Confirm your consent to the brow sculpting procedure.
18. Date the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Client Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and reminders.
* **Appointment Date (5)**: Select the date of your scheduled appointment from the calendar.
* **Type of Brow Sculpting Desired (6)**: Choose from Threading, Waxing, Microblading, or Combination.
* **Have you had brow sculpting before? (7)**: Indicate whether you have had brow sculpting or not.
* **Skin Type (8)**: Select your skin type (Oily, Dry, Combination, Sensitive, or Normal).
* **Known skin allergies or sensitivities (9)**: If applicable, list any known skin allergies or sensitivities.
* **Current medications that affect skin (10)**: If applicable, list any current medications that affect your skin.
* **Current skin conditions (11)**: If applicable, list any current skin conditions (rash, acne, psoriasis, etc.).
* **Pregnancy Status (12)**: Indicate your pregnancy status (Yes, pregnant, Yes, nursing, or False).
* **Pain Sensitivity Level (13)**: Select your pain sensitivity level (Low sensitivity, Normal sensitivity, or High sensitivity).
* **Recent facial procedures (14)**: If applicable, list any recent facial procedures (Botox, fillers, chemical peels, etc.).
* **Brow goals (15)**: Describe your desired brow sculpting look or goal.
* **I acknowledge all potential risks (16)**: Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the potential risks associated with the procedure.
* **I consent to the brow sculpting procedure (17)**: Confirm that you consent to the procedure.
* **Date of Consent (18)**: Date the form with the current date.
