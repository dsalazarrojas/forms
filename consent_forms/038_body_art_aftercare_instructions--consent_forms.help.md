# Body Art Aftercare Instructions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from clients about their experience after a body art procedure to ensure they understand and follow aftercare instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please answer each question accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the studio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Procedure Date**: Enter the date of your body art procedure.
* **Procedure Type**: Select the type of procedure you received (Tattoo, Body Piercing, Microblading, Permanent Makeup, or Other).
* **Procedure Location on Body**: Describe the location on your body where the procedure was done.
* **Artist Name**: Enter the name of the artist who performed the procedure.
* **Studio Name**: Enter the name of the studio where the procedure was done. (Optional)
* **Aftercare Instructions Provided**: Indicate if you received aftercare instructions, either verbally or in writing.
* **Instructions Understood**: Indicate if you understand the aftercare instructions provided.
* **Cleaning Products Provided**: Describe any cleaning products given to you for aftercare.
* **Cleaning Frequency**: Indicate how often you were instructed to clean the area.
* **Ointment Application**: Indicate how often you were instructed to apply ointment to the area.
* **Activities to Avoid**: Select all activities that may affect the healing process.
* **Expected Healing Time**: Indicate how long it is expected to take for the area to heal completely.
* **Follow-Up Appointment**: Indicate if you have a follow-up appointment scheduled.
* **Emergency Contact Provided**: Indicate if you have emergency contact information.
* **Warning Signs Understood**: Indicate if you understand the warning signs of potential complications.
* **Signs of Infection to Watch**: Select all signs that may indicate an infection.
* **Questions About Aftercare**: If you have any questions or concerns about aftercare, type them here.
* **Client Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand and will follow aftercare instructions.
* **Client Signature**: Sign your name with your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your acknowledgment.

Note: This form is only to be filled out by clients who have received a body art procedure at the studio.
