# Body Contouring Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from clients interested in body contouring services. It helps us understand their goals, medical history, and expectations, ensuring a smooth and effective treatment experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct options for your gender, treatment areas, and program expectations.
3. Review the medical conditions, medications, and contraindications carefully.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent and understanding of the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the following options:
	+ Female
	+ Male
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your mailing address.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your mailing address.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter the ZIP code of your mailing address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Height** (`height`, `text`, required): Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Current Weight** (`current_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **Goal Weight** (`goal_weight`, `number`, optional): Enter your target weight.
* **Body Contouring Program Selected** (`program_selected`, `select_one`, required): Select the program that suits your needs:
	+ Basic Package
	+ Standard Package
	+ Premium Package
	+ Custom Program
	+ Consultation Pending
* **Treatment Areas** (`treatment_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas you want to treat:
	+ Abdomen
	+ Flanks
	+ Thighs
	+ Buttocks
	+ Arms
	+ Back
	+ Multiple Areas
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, list them.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): If you are taking any medications, list them.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): If you have any allergies, list them.
* **Contrainications** (`contraindications`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any contraindications that apply:
	+ Pregnancy
	+ Nursing
	+ Pacemaker
	+ Metal Implants
	+ Liver Disease
	+ Kidney Disease
	+ Heart Disease
	+ Bleeding Disorders
	+ Active Cancer
	+ None Apply
* **Previous Body Contouring** (`previous_treatments`, `text`, optional): If you have undergone any previous body contouring treatments, list them.
* **Lifestyle Commitment** (`lifestyle_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of commitment to diet and exercise:
	+ Yes, Fully Committed
	+ Working on It
	+ Need Guidance
* **Daily Water Intake** (`water_intake`, `select_one`, optional): How much water do you drink daily?
	+ Less than 4 glasses
	+ 4-6 glasses
	+ 7-9 glasses
	+ 10+ glasses
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): How often do you exercise?
	+ Never
	+ 1-2 times per week
	+ 3-4 times per week
	+ 5+ times per week
* **Program Expectations** (`program_expectations`, `text`, required): Describe your expectations from the program.
* **Financial Agreement** (`financial_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the payment terms:
	+ Yes, I Agree
	+ False
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy:
	+ Yes, I Understand
	+ False
* **HIPAA Acknowledgment** (`hipaa_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of your HIPAA rights:
	+ Yes, I Acknowledge
	+ False
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as your signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of intake.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by clients prior to their first consultation or treatment. It helps us understand their needs and requirements for a personalized and effective body contouring experience.
