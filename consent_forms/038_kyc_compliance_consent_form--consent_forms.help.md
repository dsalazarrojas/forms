# KYC Compliance Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects user information for Know Your Customer (KCY) compliance, a process to verify user identities to prevent illicit activities such as money laundering and terrorist financing.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields marked with <required> in the form.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone field.
4. Select your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field using the calendar picker.
5. Enter your Address, City, Country, State, and Zip in the corresponding fields.
6. Once all fields are filled out accurately, submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, <required>): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, <required>): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, <required>): Enter your valid email address that you use for official purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, <required>): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, <required>): Select your date of birth using the calendar picker.
* **Address** (6, text, <optional>): Enter your current address for identity verification.
* **City** (7, text, <optional>): Enter your city name for identity verification.
* **Country** (8, text, <optional>): Enter your country of residence for identity verification.
* **State** (9, text, <optional>): Enter your state or province of residence for identity verification.
* **Zip** (10, text, <optional>): Enter your zip code for identity verification.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth onboarding process.
* Required fields are marked with <required> in the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
