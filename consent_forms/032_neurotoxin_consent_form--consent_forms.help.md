# Neurotoxin Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from individuals who have been exposed to a neurotoxin. This information will be used to assess potential risks and provide guidance on next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Provide your age in the "age" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone number" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "contact information" field.
7. Provide your consent by typing in the "consent" field.
8. If you have been exposed to a neurotoxin, enter the date of exposure in the "exposure to neurotoxin" field.
9. If applicable, enter the time you started experiencing symptoms related to the neurotoxin exposure in the "start exposure time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required: false): Provide your age in years for assessment of potential risks.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Contact information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter any additional contact information (e.g., address, emergency contact).
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required: false): This is where you provide your consent for us to use the information you've provided.
* **Exposure to neurotoxin** (`exposure_to_neurotoxin`, date, required: false): If you've been exposed to a neurotoxin, enter the date of exposure.
* **Start exposure time** (`start_exposure_time`, time, required: false): If applicable, enter the time you started experiencing symptoms related to the neurotoxin exposure.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure we have the necessary information to proceed.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact us at the provided email address.
