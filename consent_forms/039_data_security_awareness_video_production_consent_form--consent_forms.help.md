# data_security_awareness_video_production_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "data_security_awareness_video_production_consent_form" is used to collect information and consent for video production. This form is intended to ensure that all necessary information is collected for video production, and to obtain consent from the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "role" field to specify your role in the video production (e.g., IT, HR, or Other).
2. Enter your "name" and "email" in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your "phone" number.
4. Select the type of video you are requesting (e.g., Awareness or Training).
5. Choose the location where the video will be shot (e.g., Local or Remote).
6. Provide a brief description of the video content.
7. Indicate your consent for the video production (e.g., "True" or "False").
8. Leave your "video_location" field empty if you do not have a specific location in mind.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the video production. For example, you might choose "IT" if the video is about a technical topic, or "HR" if it's about company policies.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the video credits.
* **email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your work email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **video_type** (`video_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of video you are requesting (e.g., Awareness or Training).
* **video_location** (`video_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose the location where the video will be shot (e.g., Local or Remote).
* **video_description** (`video_description`, `note`, required): Provide a brief description of the video content.
* **consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent for the video production (e.g., "True" or "False").
* **date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like the video to be shot on.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you would like the video to be shot on.
* **department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **video_location** (`video_location`, `select_one`, optional): (Note: This is the same field as above, please disregard this entry)

Tips: 

* Make sure to enter your correct information to ensure accurate video production.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the IT department.
* This form is meant to be used for internal purposes only.
