# Church Transportation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Transportation Consent Form is a mandatory document that needs to be filled out by parents or guardians before their child can participate in church transportation services. This form ensures that we have all the necessary information to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If a field is not applicable, please write "N/A" in the corresponding field.
4. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child's Full Name** (1, text, required): This field is for your child's name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (2, date, required): This field is for your child's date of birth.
* **Parent/Guardian Full Name** (3, text, required): This field is for your name or the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (4, email, required): This field is for your email address or the email address of the person filling out this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone Number** (5, text, required): This field is for your phone number or the phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Home Address** (6, text, required): This field is for your home address or the address of the person filling out this form.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (7, text, required): This field is for the name and phone number of someone we can contact in case you are unavailable.
* **Event or Activity Name** (8, text, required): This field is for the name of the event or activity your child will be attending.
* **Event Date** (9, date, required): This field is for the date of the event or activity.
* **Departure Location** (10, text, required): This field is for where your child will be picked up from.
* **Departure Time** (11, time, required): This field is for when your child will be picked up.
* **Return Location** (12, text, required): This field is for where your child will be returned to.
* **Expected Return Time** (13, time, required): This field is for when you expect your child to be returned.
* **Type of Transportation** (14, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the type of transportation your child will use (e.g., Church Van, Church Bus, etc.).
* **Does Your Child Have Any Medical Conditions?** (15, select_one, required): This field is for indicating whether your child has any medical conditions.
* **Please Describe Medical Conditions** (16, text, required if medical conditions exist): This field is for providing more details about your child's medical conditions.
* **Does Your Child Have Any Special Needs?** (17, select_one, required): This field is for indicating whether your child has any special needs.
* **Please Describe Special Needs** (18, text, required if special needs exist): This field is for providing more details about your child's special needs.
* **Is Your Child Prone to Car Sickness?** (19, select_one, required): This field is for indicating whether your child is prone to car sickness.
* **Behavior Expectations Acknowledgment** (20, select_one, required): This field is for acknowledging that your child will follow the safety rules during transportation.
* **Who Is Authorized to Pick Up Your Child?** (21, text, required): This field is for listing the names of people authorized to pick up your child.
* **Transportation Consent** (22, select_one, required): This field is for granting permission for your child to use the transportation service.
* **Liability Understanding** (23, select_one, required): This field is for acknowledging that you understand the liability terms of the transportation service.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (24, text, required): This field is for signing with your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Date of Signature** (25, date, required): This field is for signing and dating the form with the current date.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and carefully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* This form is a mandatory document for participating in church transportation services.
* Once you have filled out this form, please return it to the transportation service coordinator.
* Please note that this form will be kept confidential and will not be shared with anyone else.
* In case of emergencies, we will make every effort to contact the contact person listed in the Emergency Contact Name and Phone field.
