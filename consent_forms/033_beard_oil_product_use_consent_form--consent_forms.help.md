# Beard Oil Product Use Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beard Oil Product Use Consent Form is a necessary document for clients to fill out before using a particular beard oil product. It helps to gather essential information about the client's health, allergies, and personal preferences, ensuring a safe and successful product usage.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details and medical history.
2. Choose the correct answer for each field based on your knowledge and understanding.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to use the product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you want us to use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of application.
* **Grooming Professional Name** (`grooming_professional`, `text`, required): If you are visiting a service provider, enter their name.
* **Beard Oil Brand** (`oil_brand`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the beard oil product you are using.
* **Key Ingredients** (`oil_ingredients`, `text`, required): List the main ingredients of the product.
* **Nut Allergies** (`nut_allergies`, `select_one`, required): If you have a tree nut allergy or peanut allergy, select "Yes" next to the relevant option.
* **Skin Allergies** (`skin_allergies`, `text`, required): If you have any skin allergies, list them here.
* **Sensitive Skin** (`sensitive_skin`, `select_one`, required): If you have sensitive skin, select "True" if you are unsure or if you think you might.
* **Previous Reactions** (`previous_reactions`, `text`, optional): If you have had any bad reactions to oils, describe them here.
* **Product Patch Tested** (`product_tested`, `select_one`, required): If you have been tested for product reactions, select the relevant option.
* **Usage Instructions** (`usage_instructions`, `select_one`, required): If you understand how to use the product, select "True".
* **Potential Reactions** (`potential_reactions`, `select_one`, required): If you were informed about potential side effects, select "True".
* **Consent to Use** (`consent_to_use`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you consent to use this product.
* **Purchasing Take Home Product** (`take_home_product`, `select_one`, required): If you will purchase the product to take home, select "True".
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your full name in this field.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date and sign this field after completing the form.
