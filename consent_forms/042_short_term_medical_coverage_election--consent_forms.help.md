# Short Term Medical Coverage Election - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for short-term medical coverage from users. The goal is to collect information about the user's medical needs, including the type of coverage they require, the start and end dates of their coverage, the medical group they belong to, their coverage choices, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name (user_input_8) and phone number (user_input_9) to identify yourself.
2. Select the type of medical coverage (user_input_2) you require.
3. Select the start and end dates (user_input_3 and user_input_4) of your coverage.
4. Select the medical group (user_input_5) you belong to.
5. Choose the coverage options (user_input_6) that apply to you.
6. Enter any additional notes (user_input_7) regarding your medical coverage.
7. Agree (user_input_13) to the terms of submission.
8. Select the user who submitted (user_input_14) this form.
9. Select the IP address (user_input_15) of the user who submitted this form.
10. Confirm that the user agreed to the terms (user_input_16) of submission.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes (user_input_7, user_input_17, user_input_18, user_input_19, user_input_20, user_input_21, user_input_22, user_input_23, user_input_24, user_input_25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input_1 (user_input_8)**: Enter your name.
* **user_input_2 (medical_coverage_type)**: Select the type of medical coverage you require.
* **user_input_3 (start_date)**: Enter the start date of your coverage.
* **user_input_4 (end_date)**: Enter the end date of your coverage.
* **user_input_5 (medical_group)**: Select the medical group you belong to.
* **user_input_6 (coverage_choice)**: Choose the coverage options that apply to you.
* **user_input_7 (notes)**: Enter any additional notes regarding your medical coverage.
* **user_input_8 (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **user_input_9 (phone)**: Enter your phone number.
* **user_input_10 (submission_date)**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **user_input_11 (submission_time)**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **user_input_12 (user_notes)**: Enter any notes you have about your submission.
* **user_input_13 (user_agreed)**: Agree or disagree to the terms of submission.
* **user_input_14 (submission_user)**: Select the user who submitted this form.
* **user_input_15 (submission_ip)**: Select the IP address of the user who submitted this form.
* **user_input_16 (user_ip_agreed)**: Confirm that the user agreed to the terms of submission.
* **user_input_17** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **user_input_18**, **user_input_19**, **user_input_20**, **user_input_21**, **user_input_22**, **user_input_23**, **user_input_24**, **user_input_25** (text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
