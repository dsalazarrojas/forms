# Heritage Archive Photography Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heritage Archive Photography Consent Form is used to collect information from organizations submitting images to the Heritage Archive for publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form header section.
2. Enter the name of the subject in the "Subject Name" field.
3. Enter the number of images being submitted in the "Subject Image Count" field.
4. Provide the name of the photographer in the "Photographer Credit" field (optional).
5. Provide the contact information of the photographer in the "Photographer Contact" field (optional).
6. Enter the name of the organization submitting the images in the "Organization Name" field.
7. Enter the address of the organization in the "Organization Address" field.
8. (Optional) Enter the contact phone number of the organization in the "Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: This is a placeholder section for any additional information or comments.
* **Subject Name**: The name of the subject(s) in the image(s).
* **Subject Image Count**: The number of images being submitted for publication.
* **Photographer Credit**: The name of the photographer(s) who took the image(s).
* **Photographer Contact**: The contact information of the photographer(s) (e.g. email, phone number, etc.).
* **Organization Name**: The name of the organization submitting the image(s).
* **Organization Address**: The address of the organization submitting the image(s).
* **Contact Phone**: The contact phone number of the organization submitting the image(s).

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions from the image subjects before submitting the form.
* Use a high-quality image that accurately represents the heritage subject.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the Heritage Archive team for guidance.
