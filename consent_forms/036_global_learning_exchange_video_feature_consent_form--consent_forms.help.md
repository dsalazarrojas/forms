# global_learning_exchange_video_feature_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Global Learning Exchange Video Feature Consent Form** is used to collect user consent for accessing exclusive video content. This form is used only once per user and is essential for users to provide details about their video usage and agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out all the fields that are marked as **required**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Consent
* **User Consent** (`user_consent`, text, required): Please describe your consent for accessing the Global Learning Exchange video feature. This is the most important part of the form, as it ensures you're eligible to access the feature.

### Video Usage
* **Video Usage** (`video_usage`, text, optional): Describe how you plan to use the video content. This helps us understand your needs and improve our services.

### User Agreement
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, this is the place to express them. Please be clear and concise.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This is how we'll contact you about your video feature usage and any updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number. This is not required, but it helps us reach you more efficiently.
