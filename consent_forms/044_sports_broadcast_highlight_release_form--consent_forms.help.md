# Sports Broadcast Highlight Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain release permissions from participants or organizations to use their names or likenesses in sports broadcasts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the participant's name.
2. Enter the participant's email address.
3. Enter the organization's name.
4. Enter a contact number for the participant or organization.
5. Choose the participant's role.
6. Select the participant's likenesses.
7. Choose the release type.
8. Select the media type.
9. Enter the release date.
10. Enter the expiration date.
11. Select the consent options.
12. Enter a signature.
13. Enter the organization's address.
14. Enter the organization's city.
15. Enter the organization's state.
16. Enter the organization's postal code.
17. Enter the participant's address.
18. Enter the participant's city.
19. Enter the participant's state.
20. Enter the participant's postal code.
21. Select the consent to use name options.
22. Select the consent to use likeness options.
23. Enter any additional notes.
24. Enter the submitted by name.
25. Enter the submitted date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant or individual who will be featured in the sports broadcast.
- **Email**: Enter the email address of the participant or organization. This will be used to contact them about the release permission.
- **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization that the participant or participant is associated with.
- **Contact Number**: Enter a contact number for the participant or organization that can be reached for further information.
- **Role**: Choose the role of the participant in the sports broadcast.
- **Participant Likeness**: Select the participant's likenesses that will be used in the sports broadcast.
- **Release Type**: Choose the type of release permission required for the sports broadcast.
- **Media Type**: Select the type of media that will be used in the sports broadcast.
- **Release Date**: Enter the date when the release permission begins.
- **Expiration Date**: Enter the date when the release permission expires.
- **Consent**: Select the consent options for the participant or organization.
- **Signature**: Enter a signature of the participant or organization to confirm the release permission.
- **Organization Address**: Enter the address of the organization.
- **Organization City**: Enter the city of the organization.
- **Organization State**: Enter the state of the organization.
- **Organization Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the organization.
- **Participant Address**: Enter the address of the participant.
- **Participant City**: Enter the city of the participant.
- **Participant State**: Enter the state of the participant.
- **Participant Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the participant.
- **Consent to Use Name**: Select the consent options for the participant's name.
- **Consent to Use Likeness**: Select the consent options for the participant's likeness.
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the release permission.
- **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
- **Submitted**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
