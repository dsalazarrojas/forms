# Treasury Account Monitoring Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Account Monitoring Consent Form is a document that collects essential information from clients and their representatives regarding the monitoring of their treasury accounts. This form helps to obtain written consent for the monitoring of the client's account and ensures that all parties involved are aware of the scope of work and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill in the **Client Name**, which should be the name of the individual or entity that you represent.
2. Enter the **Client Address**, which should be the physical or postal address of the client.
3. Enter the **Date**, which should be the current date for which the consent is being given.
4. Describe the **Scope of Work**, which should outline the specific tasks or activities that will be monitored or reviewed.
5. Sign and date the **Signed** field, indicating that you have read and understand the contents of this form.
6. Enter the **Signature Date**, which should be the date you sign the form.
7. Enter the **Bank Name**, which is the name of the bank associated with the client's account.
8. Enter the **Bank Address**, which is the physical or postal address of the bank.
9. Enter the **Advisor Name**, which is the name of the individual or entity advising on the account.
10. Enter the **Advisor Address**, which is the physical or postal address of the advisor.
11. Enter the **Authorized By**, which should be the name of the individual or entity that is authorizing the monitoring.
12. Enter the **Authorized Date**, which should be the date the client's account is authorized to be monitored.
