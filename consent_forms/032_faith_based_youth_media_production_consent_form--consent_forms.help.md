# Faith Based Youth Media Production Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faith Based Youth Media Production Consent Form is designed to gather necessary information and consent from participants in youth media production projects. This form ensures that both participants and their parents or guardians understand their roles and responsibilities in the project, as well as provide consent for their media representation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Participant Information**: Enter the participant's name, age, and other relevant details on the "Participant Information" page. 
2. **Share Parent or Guardian Information**: On the "Parent or Guardian Information" page, provide the parent or guardian's name, contact details, and any other necessary information. 
3. **Project Details**: In the "Project Information" page, describe the media production project, including the project name, location, dates, and any other relevant details. 
4. **Media Release**: On the "Media Release" page, provide your consent for your media representation in the project. 
5. **Agreement**: Finally, review and agree to the terms and conditions of the project on the "Agreement" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Page 1)**: This page is used to collect basic participant information, such as name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent or Guardian Information (Page 2)**: This page is used to collect parent or guardian information, such as name, contact details, and any other necessary information.
* **Project Information (Page 3)**: This page is used to describe the media production project, including the project name, location, dates, and any other relevant details.
* **Media Release (Page 4)**: This page is used to obtain participant consent for media representation in the project.
* **Agreement (Page 5)**: This page is used to review and agree to the terms and conditions of the project.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information or have questions, please contact the form administrators.
* Ensure that you have obtained the necessary consent from your parent or guardian before proceeding.
* Review the project details carefully to ensure you understand your role and responsibilities in the project.
