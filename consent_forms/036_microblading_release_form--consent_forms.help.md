# Microblading Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Microblading Release Form is used to capture client information and signatures for microblading services. It is essential to complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and successful microblading experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your basic information in the Client Information section.
2. Select the date of your microblading procedure in the Microblading Date field.
3. Enter your technician's name in the Technician Name field.
4. Sign and date the Technician Signature field to confirm that you have been informed about the risks and expectations of the microblading procedure.
5. Finally, sign and date the Client Signature field to confirm that you have received and understood the information about the microblading procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: (`client_information`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field collects general information about you, including your name and contact details.
* **Microblading Date**: (`microblading_date`, `date`, optional)
	+ This field is used to record the date of the microblading procedure.
* **Technician Name**: (`technician_name`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is used to record the name of the technician performing the microblading procedure.
* **Technician Signature**: (`technician_signature`, `time`, optional)
	+ This field is used to record the signature of the technician, confirming that they have informed you about the risks and expectations of the microblading procedure.
* **Client Signature**: (`client_signature`, `note`, optional)
	+ This field is used to record your signature, confirming that you have received and understood the information about the microblading procedure.

## Tips

* Please fill in your information accurately and carefully.
* Make sure to review the form carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, ask your technician before signing the form.
* Keep a copy of the form for your records.
