# Vaccination Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients who have received a vaccination. It helps healthcare professionals to track and monitor vaccination records accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Enter details about the vaccine administered in the "Vaccine Information" section.
3. Provide contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
4. If necessary, provide any relevant medical history or allergies in the "Medical History" section.
5. List any allergies or medical conditions that the patient has in the "Allergies" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (patient_info)**: This is where you will enter the patient's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
* **Vaccine Information (vaccine_info)**: In this section, please enter details about the vaccine administered, including the name of the vaccine, the date administered, and any dose details.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Provide the patient's contact details so that we can follow up with them if needed.
* **Medical History (medical_history)**: If you have any relevant medical history about the patient, please provide it in this section.
* **Allergies (allergies)**: List any allergies or medical conditions that the patient has. This is essential for us to provide proper care and caution.
## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is for internal use only and will be reviewed by authorized personnel.
