# Car Model Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Model Release Form is used to obtain consent from the owner of a car model for the use of images of the car in various media channels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required information.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "Full Name" field (page 2).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (page 3).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (page 4).
5. Enter your mailing address in the "Mailing Address" field (page 5).
6. Describe your car model in the "Car Make and Model" field (page 7).
7. Enter the year of manufacture of your car model in the "Vehicle Year" field (page 8).
8. If applicable, enter your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the "Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)" field (page 9).
9. Describe how the image of your car model will be used in the "Description of Image Usage" field (page 11).
10. Select the media outlets where your car model image will be used (page 12).
11. Choose the duration of the release (page 13).
12. Confirm your acknowledgement of the terms of service (page 15).
13. Type your name for release confirmation (page 16).
14. If applicable, enter the name of a witness who has witnessed your agreement (page 17).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (page 2): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (page 3): Enter your email address for digital copy of the release.
* **Phone Number** (page 4): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (page 5): Enter your home or business address.
* **Car Make and Model** (page 7): Describe your car model in detail.
* **Vehicle Year** (page 8): Enter the year of manufacture of your car model.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (page 9): If applicable, enter your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) code.
* **Description of Image Usage** (page 11): Describe how the image of your car model will be used.
* **Media Outlets** (page 12): Select the media channels where your car model image will be used.
* **Duration of Release** (page 13): Choose the duration of the release.
* **I Acknowledge the car model release terms** (page 15): Confirm your agreement with the terms of service.
* **Type your name for release confirmation** (page 16): Type your name to confirm the release.
* **Witness Full Name** (page 17): If applicable, enter the name of a witness who has witnessed your agreement.
* **Any additional comments or requests?** (page 18): Provide any additional comments or requests for the release.
