# Infectious Disease Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver that participants must complete before taking part in a project with potential exposure to infectious diseases. It's essential to understand the risks involved and acknowledge your willingness to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the potential risks involved.
2. Provide your participant information, including your contact details.
3. Answer the screening questions truthfully to ensure you are eligible to participate.
4. Acknowledge that you have read and understand the form.
5. Select your contact information and address options.
